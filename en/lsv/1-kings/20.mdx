<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 1 Kings 15:20; 1 Kings 22:31"> And Ben-Hadad king of Aram has gathered all his force, and thirty-two kings &#91;are&#93; with him, and horse and chariot, and he goes up and lays siege against Samaria, and fights with it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:2–22; 2 Kings 19:9; Isaiah 37:9–10"> and sends messengers to Ahab king of Israel, to the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Isaiah 10:13–14"> and says to him, “Thus said Ben-Hadad: Your silver and your gold are mine, and your wives and your sons—the best—are mine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 15:11–13; Deuteronomy 28:48; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> And the king of Israel answers and says, “According to your word, my lord, O king: I &#91;am&#93; yours, and all that I have.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the messengers turn back and say, “Thus spoke Ben-Hadad, saying, Surely I sent to you, saying, Your silver, and your gold, and your wives, and your sons, you give to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:34; Joel 3:5; 2 Kings 18:31–32"> for if, at this time tomorrow, I send my servants to you, then they have searched your house, and the houses of your servants, and it has been &#91;that&#93; every desirable thing of your eyes they place in their hand, and have taken &#91;them&#93; away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:7; Proverbs 6:14; 1 Kings 8:1"> And the king of Israel calls to all &#91;the&#93; elderly of the land and says, “Please know and see that this &#91;one&#93; is seeking evil, for he sent to me for my wives, and for my sons, and for my silver, and for my gold, and I did not withhold from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And all the elderly and all the people say to him, “Do not listen, nor consent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he says to the messengers of Ben-Hadad, “Say to my lord the king, All that you sent to your servant for at the first I do, and this thing I am not able to do”; and the messengers go and take him back word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 19:2; Exodus 11:8; 2 Kings 19:23–24"> And Ben-Hadad sends to him and says, “Thus the gods do to me, and thus do they add, if the dust of Samaria suffice for handfuls for all the people who &#91;are&#93; at my feet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Isaiah 10:15–16; 1 Samuel 17:44–47"> And the king of Israel answers and says, “Speak: Do not let him who is girding on boast himself as him who is loosing &#91;his armor&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 16:9; 1 Kings 20:16; Proverbs 31:4–5"> And it comes to pass at the hearing of this word—and he is drinking, he and the kings, in the shelters—that he says to his servants, “Set yourselves”; and they set themselves against the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 20:28; Ezekiel 20:22; Isaiah 7:1–9"> And behold, a certain prophet has come near to Ahab king of Israel and says, “Thus said YHWH: Have you seen all this great multitude? Behold, I am giving it into your hand today, and you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:50; Genesis 14:14–16; 1 Kings 18:44"> And Ahab says, “By whom?” And he says, “Thus said YHWH: By the young men of the heads of the provinces”; and he says, “Who directs the battle?” And he says, “You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 7:16; 1 Samuel 14:2; Judges 7:7"> And he inspects the young men of the heads of the provinces, and they are two hundred and thirty-two, and after them he has mustered all the people, all the sons of Israel—seven thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16–17; Hosea 4:11; 1 Kings 16:9"> and they go out at noon, and Ben-Hadad is drinking—drunk in the shelters, he and the kings, the thirty-two kings helping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:14–15; 1 Kings 20:19"> And the young men of the heads of the provinces go out at the first, and Ben-Hadad sends, and they declare to him, saying, “Men have come out of Samaria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 14:8–12; 1 Samuel 14:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> And he says, “If they have come out for peace, catch them alive; and if they have come out for battle, catch them alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And these have gone out of the city—the young men of the heads of the provinces—and the force that &#91;is&#93; after them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:16–17; 2 Kings 19:36; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> and each strikes his man, and Aram flees, and Israel pursues them, and Ben-Hadad king of Aram escapes on a horse—and the horsemen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 3:18; Judges 3:28; 2 Kings 3:24"> and the king of Israel goes out, and strikes the horses, and the charioteers, and has struck among the Arameans a great striking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Kings 20:26; 1 Kings 20:13"> And the prophet comes near to the king of Israel and says to him, “Go, strengthen yourself, and know and see that which you do, for at the turn of the year the king of Aram is coming up against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 1 Samuel 4:8; Psalms 50:21–22"> And the servants of the king of Aram said to him, “Their gods &#91;are&#93; gods of hills, therefore they were stronger than us; and yet, we fight with them in the plain—are we not stronger than they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 22:31; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Kings 20:16"> And this thing you do: turn aside each of the kings out of his place, and set captains in their stead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 10:3"> and you, number for yourself a force as the force that is fallen from you, and horse for horse, and chariot for chariot, and we fight with them in the plain; are we not stronger than they?” And he listens to their voice, and does so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; 1 Kings 20:22; 1 Samuel 4:1"> And it comes to pass at the turn of the year, that Ben-Hadad inspects the Arameans, and goes up to Aphek, to battle with Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:5; 1 Samuel 13:5–8; 1 Samuel 14:2"> and the sons of Israel have been inspected, and supported, and go to meet them, and the sons of Israel encamp before them, like two flocks of goats, and the Arameans have filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 20:13; 1 Kings 17:18; Ezekiel 12:16"> And a man of God comes near, and speaks to the king of Israel and says, “Thus said YHWH: Because that the Arameans have said, YHWH &#91;is&#93; God of &#91;the&#93; hills, but He &#91;is&#93; not God of &#91;the&#93; valleys—I have given the whole of this great multitude into your hand, and you have known that I &#91;am&#93; YHWH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:17; 2 Chronicles 28:6; Psalms 10:16"> And they encamp opposite one another &#91;for&#93; seven days, and it comes to pass on the seventh day, that the battle draws near, and the sons of Israel strike one hundred thousand footmen of Aram in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 22:25; 2 Chronicles 18:24; 1 Kings 20:26"> And those left flee to Aphek, into the city, and the wall falls on twenty-seven chief men who are left, and Ben-Hadad has fled, and comes into the city, into the innermost part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:31; Genesis 37:34; 1 Kings 20:23"> And his servants say to him, “Now behold, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel—that they are kind kings; please let us put sackcloth on our loins and ropes on our heads, and we go out to the king of Israel; it may be he keeps you alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 20:3–6; Job 12:17–18; Daniel 5:20–23"> And they gird sackcloth on their loins, and ropes &#91;are&#93; on their heads, and they come to the king of Israel, and say, “Your servant Ben-Hadad has said, Please let me live”; and he says, “Is he still alive? He &#91;is&#93; my brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 16:8; 2 Kings 10:15; Proverbs 25:13"> And the men observe diligently, and hurry, and catch &#91;the word&#93; from him, and say, “Your brother Ben-Hadad”; and he says, “Go in, bring him”; and Ben-Hadad comes out to him, and he causes him to come up on the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 15:20; Isaiah 26:10; 1 Kings 20:42"> And he says to him, “The cities that my father took from your father, I give back, and you make streets for yourself in Damascus, as my father did in Samaria; and I, with a covenant, send you away”; and he makes a covenant with him, and sends him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 13:17–18; 2 Kings 4:1"> And a certain man of the sons of the prophets said to his neighbor by the word of YHWH, “Please strike me”; and the man refuses to strike him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 13:21–24; 1 Kings 13:26; 1 Samuel 15:22–23"> and he says to him, “Because that you have not listened to the voice of YHWH, behold, you are going from me, and the lion has struck you”; and he goes from him, and the lion finds him, and strikes him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 Kings 20:35"> And he finds another man and says, “Please strike me”; and the man strikes him, striking and wounding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 14:2; Matthew 6:16; 1 Kings 22:30"> and the prophet goes and stands for the king on the way, and disguises himself with ashes on his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Kings 10:24; Job 36:18; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> And it comes to pass—the king is passing by—that he has cried to the king and says, “Your servant went out into the midst of the battle, and behold, a man has turned aside and brings a man to me, and says, Keep this man; if he is at all missing, then your life has been for his life, or you weigh out a talent of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:5–7; Matthew 25:24–27; Job 15:6"> and it comes to pass, your servant is working here and there, and he is not!” And the king of Israel says to him, “Your judgment &#91;is&#93; right; you have determined &#91;it&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 13:19; Job 2:8"> And he hurries and turns aside the ashes from off his eyes, and the king of Israel discerns him, that he &#91;is&#93; of the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 22:31–37; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; 1 Kings 20:39"> and he says to him, “Thus said YHWH: Because you have sent away the man I devoted, out of &#91;your&#93; hand, even your life has been for his life, and your people for his people”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 21:4; Esther 5:13; Esther 6:12–13"> and the king of Israel goes to his house, sulky and angry, and comes to Samaria.</span>
