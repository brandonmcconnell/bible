<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Proverbs 6:24; Nehemiah 13:23–27"> And King Solomon has loved many strange women, and the daughter of Pharaoh, females of Moab, Ammon, Edom, Sidon, &#91;and&#93; of the Hittites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:16; Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Joshua 23:12–13"> of the nations of which YHWH said to the sons of Israel, “You do not go in to them, and they do not go in to you; surely they turn aside your heart after their gods”; Solomon has cleaved to them for love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Samuel 5:13–16; 2 Chronicles 11:21"> And he has seven hundred wives, princesses, and three hundred concubines; and his wives turn aside his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 11:6"> And it comes to pass, at the time of Solomon’s old age, his wives have turned aside his heart after other gods, and his heart has not been perfect with his God YHWH, like the heart of his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 2:13; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And Solomon goes after Ashtoreth goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:8; Joshua 14:14"> and Solomon does evil in the eyes of YHWH, and has not been fully after YHWH, like his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Judges 11:24; Acts 7:43"> Then Solomon builds a high place for Chemosh the abomination of Moab, in the hill that &#91;is&#93; on the front of Jerusalem, and for Molech the abomination of the sons of Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11–12; 1 Kings 11:1; Ezekiel 16:22–29"> and so he has done for all his strange women, who are perfuming and sacrificing to their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:2; 1 Kings 3:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> And YHWH shows Himself angry with Solomon, for his heart has turned aside from YHWH, God of Israel, who had appeared to him twice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 7:17–22; 1 Kings 9:4–7"> and given a charge to him concerning this thing, not to go after other gods; and he has not kept that which YHWH commanded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15–16; 1 Kings 11:31; 2 Kings 17:21"> and YHWH says to Solomon, “Because that this has been with you, and you have not kept My covenant and My statutes that I charged on you, I surely tear the kingdom from you, and have given it to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 20:19; 1 Samuel 9:4–5; 2 Kings 22:19–20"> Only, I do not do it in your days, for the sake of your father David; I tear it out of the hand of your son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; Deuteronomy 12:11; 1 Kings 11:32"> only, I do not tear away all of the kingdom; I give one tribe to your son, for the sake of My servant David, and for the sake of Jerusalem that I have chosen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Psalms 89:30–34; Isaiah 13:17"> And YHWH raises up an adversary against Solomon, Hadad the Edomite; he &#91;is&#93; of the seed of the king in Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Chronicles 18:12–13; Deuteronomy 20:13"> and it comes to pass, in David’s being with Edom, in the going up of Joab head of the host to bury the slain, that he strikes every male in Edom—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> for six months Joab abided there, and all Israel, until the cutting off of every male in Edom—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 2:13–14; Exodus 2:1–10; 2 Kings 11:2"> and Hadad flees, he and certain Edomites, of the servants of his father, with him, to go to Egypt, and Hadad &#91;is&#93; a little youth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:12; Deuteronomy 33:2; Deuteronomy 1:1"> and they rise out of Midian, and come into Paran, and take men with them out of Paran, and come into Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he gives a house to him, and has commanded bread for him, and has given land to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:4; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Genesis 39:21"> And Hadad finds grace in the eyes of Pharaoh exceedingly, and he gives to him a wife, the sister of his own wife, sister of Tahpenes the mistress;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; 1 Samuel 1:24"> and the sister of Tahpenes bears to him his son Genubath, and Tahpenes weans him within the house of Pharaoh, and Genubath is in the house of Pharaoh in the midst of the sons of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Exodus 4:19; Matthew 2:20"> And Hadad has heard in Egypt that David has lain with his fathers, and that Joab head of the host is dead, and Hadad says to Pharaoh, “Send me away, and I go to my land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22–23; Luke 22:35; Mark 14:31"> And Pharaoh says to him, “But what are you lacking with me, that behold, you are seeking to go to your own land?” And he says, “Nothing, but you certainly send me away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 11:14; 2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 10:15–18"> And God raises an adversary against him, Rezon son of Eliadah, who has fled from Hadadezer king of Zobah, his lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:8; 2 Samuel 10:18; 1 Kings 20:34"> and gathers men to himself, and is head of a troop in David’s slaying them, and they go to Damascus, and dwell in it, and reign in Damascus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 106:40; 1 Kings 5:4"> and he is an adversary against Israel all the days of Solomon (besides the evil that Hadad &#91;did&#93;), and he cuts off in Israel, and reigns over Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:6; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> And Jeroboam son of Nebat, an Ephraimite of Zereda—the name of whose mother &#91;is&#93; Zeruah, a widow woman—servant to Solomon, he also lifts up a hand against the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 9:15; Proverbs 30:32"> and this &#91;is&#93; the thing &#91;for&#93; which he lifted up a hand against the king: Solomon built Millo—he shut up the breach of the city of his father David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; 2 Samuel 19:20; Judges 1:22–23"> and the man Jeroboam &#91;is&#93; mighty in valor, and Solomon sees that the young man is doing business, and appoints him over all the burden of the house of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 1 Kings 14:2; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> And it comes to pass, at that time, that Jeroboam has gone out from Jerusalem, and Ahijah the Shilonite, the prophet, finds him in the way, and he is covering himself with a new garment; and both of them &#91;are&#93; by themselves in a field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–28; 1 Samuel 24:4–5"> and Ahijah lays hold on the new garment that &#91;is&#93; on him, and tears it &#91;into&#93; twelve pieces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11–12"> and says to Jeroboam, “Take ten pieces for yourself, for thus said YHWH, God of Israel: Behold, I am tearing the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and have given the ten tribes to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 1 Kings 14:21"> but he has the one tribe for My servant David’s sake, and for Jerusalem’s sake, the city which I have fixed on, out of all the tribes of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 11:9; 1 Kings 11:5–7; 1 Kings 9:5–7"> because they have forsaken Me, and bow themselves to Ashtoreth, goddess of the Zidonians, to Chemosh god of Moab, and to Milcom god of the sons of Ammon, and have not walked in My ways, to do that which &#91;is&#93; right in My eyes, and My statutes and My judgments, like his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 1 Kings 11:31; Job 11:6"> And I do not take the whole of the kingdom out of his hand, for I make him prince all &#91;the&#93; days of his life, for the sake of My servant David whom I chose, who kept My commands and My statutes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:12; Exodus 20:5–6"> and I have taken the kingdom out of the hand of his son, and given it to you—the ten tribes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 8:19; 1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 15:4"> and to his son I give one tribe, for there being a lamp before Me for My servant David in Jerusalem &#91;for&#93; all the days, the city that I have chosen for Myself to put My Name there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:26; 1 Kings 11:26; 2 Samuel 3:21"> And I take you, and you have reigned over all that your soul desires, and you have been king over Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:8"> and it has been, if you hear all that I command you, and have walked in My ways, and done that which is right in My eyes, to keep My statutes and My commands, as My servant David did, that I have been with you, and have built a steadfast house for you, as I built for David, and have given Israel to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Luke 2:11; Isaiah 11:1–10"> and I humble the seed of David for this; only, not &#91;for&#93; all the days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:2–9; Isaiah 14:24–27; Lamentations 3:37"> And Solomon seeks to put Jeroboam to death, and Jeroboam rises and flees to Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, and he is in Egypt until the death of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29–31"> And the rest of the matters of Solomon, and all that he did, and his wisdom, are they not written on the scroll of the matters of Solomon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11"> And the days that Solomon has reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel &#91;are&#93; forty years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; 1 Kings 2:10; Matthew 1:7"> and Solomon lies with his fathers, and is buried in the city of his father David, and his son Rehoboam reigns in his stead.</span>
