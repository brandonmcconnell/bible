<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> And it comes to pass, in the four hundred and eightieth year of the going out of the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, in the fourth year—in the month of Ziv, it &#91;is&#93; the second month—of the reigning of Solomon over Israel, that he builds the house for YHWH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> As for the house that King Solomon has built for YHWH, its length &#91;is&#93; sixty cubits, and its breadth twenty, and its height thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> As for the porch on the front of the temple of the house, its length &#91;is&#93; twenty cubits on the front of the breadth of the house; its breadth &#91;is&#93; ten by the cubit on the front of the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> and he makes frames of narrowing windows for the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> And he builds a couch against the wall of the house all around, &#91;even&#93; the walls of the house all around, of the temple and of the oracle, and makes sides all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lowest couch, its breadth &#91;is&#93; five by the cubit; and the middle, its breadth &#91;is&#93; six by the cubit; and the third, its breadth &#91;is&#93; seven by the cubit, for he has put withdrawings of the house all around outside—not to lay hold on the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> And the house, in its being built, has been built of perfect stone &#91;that was&#93; brought; and hammer and the axe—any instrument of iron—was not heard in the house in its being built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The opening of the middle side &#91;is&#93; at the right shoulder of the house, and with windings they go up on the middle one, and from the middle one to the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> And he builds the house, and completes it, and covers the house &#91;with&#93; beams and rows of cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he builds the couch against all the house, its breadth &#91;is&#93; five cubits, and it takes hold of the house by cedar-wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of YHWH is to Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “This house that you are building—if you walk in My statutes, and do My judgments, indeed, have done all My commands, to walk in them, then I have established My word with you, which I spoke to your father David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> and have dwelt in the midst of the sons of Israel, and do not forsake My people Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> And Solomon builds the house and completes it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> and he builds the walls of the house within with beams of cedar, from the floor of the house to the walls of the ceiling; he has overlaid the inside with wood, and covers the floor of the house with ribs of fir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> And he builds the twenty cubits on the sides of the house with ribs of cedar, from the floor to the walls; and he builds for it within, for the oracle, for the Holy of Holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And &#91;this area of&#93; the house was forty by the cubit; it &#91;is the area of&#93; the temple before My face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> And the cedar for the house within &#91;is&#93; carvings of knobs and openings of flowers; the whole &#91;is&#93; cedar, there is not a stone seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> And he has prepared the oracle in the midst of the house within, to put the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> And before the oracle &#91;is&#93; twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in breadth, and its height &#91;is&#93; twenty cubits; and he overlays it with gold refined, and overlays the altar with cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> And Solomon overlays the house within with gold refined, and causes &#91;it&#93; to pass over in chains of gold before the oracle, and overlays it with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> And he has overlaid the whole of the house with gold, until the completion of all the house; and the whole of the altar that the oracle has, he has overlaid with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> And he makes two cherubim of the oil-tree within the oracle; their height &#91;is&#93; ten cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and the first wing of the cherub &#91;is&#93; five cubits, and the second wing of the cherub five cubits—ten cubits from the ends of his wings even to the ends of his wings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and the second cherub &#91;is&#93; ten by the cubit; one measure and one form &#91;are&#93; of the two cherubim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> the height of the first cherub &#91;is&#93; ten by the cubit, and so &#91;is&#93; the second cherub;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> and he sets the cherubim in the midst of the inner house, and they spread out the wings of the cherubim, and a wing of the first comes against the wall, and a wing of the second cherub is coming against the second wall, and their wings &#91;stretched&#93; into the midst of the house, coming wing against wing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and he overlays the cherubim with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> and he has carved all around the walls of the house with engravings of carvings, cherubim, and palm trees, and openings of flowers, inside and outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> And he has overlaid the floor of the house with gold, inside and outside;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> as for the opening of the oracle, he made doors of the oil-tree; the lintel &#91;and&#93; doorposts &#91;are&#93; a fifth &#91;of the wall&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> And the two doors &#91;are&#93; of the oil-tree, and he has carved on them carvings of cherubim, and palm-trees, and openings of flowers, and overlaid with gold, and he causes the gold to go down on the cherubim and on the palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And so he has made for the opening of the temple, doorposts of the oil-tree, from the fourth &#91;of the wall&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> And the two doors &#91;are&#93; of fir-tree, the two sides of the first door are revolving, and the two hangings of the second door are revolving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And he has carved cherubim, and palms, and openings of flowers, and overlaid the carved work with straightened gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> And he builds the inner court, three rows of hewn work, and a row of beams of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> In the fourth year the house of YHWH has been founded, in the month of Ziv,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> and in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul (the eighth month), the house has been finished in all its matters, and in all its ordinances, and he builds it &#91;in&#93; seven years.</span>
