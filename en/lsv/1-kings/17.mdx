<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25–26; James 5:17; Luke 1:17"> And Elijah the Tishbite, of the inhabitants of Gilead, says to Ahab, “YHWH, God of Israel, lives, before whom I have stood, there is not dew and rain these years, except according to my word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:1; Jeremiah 7:1; Hosea 1:1–2"> And the word of YHWH is to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Jeremiah 36:26; Psalms 83:3"> “Go from this &#91;place&#93;; and you have turned for yourself eastward, and been hidden by the Brook of Cherith that &#91;is&#93; on the front of the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> and it has been &#91;that&#93; you drink from the brook, and I have commanded the ravens to sustain you there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Matthew 16:24; John 15:14"> And he goes and does according to the word of YHWH, indeed, he goes and dwells by the Brook of Cherith, that &#91;is&#93; on the front of the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:23–24; Matthew 6:31–33; Jeremiah 37:21"> and the ravens are bringing bread and flesh to him in the morning, and bread and flesh in the evening, and he drinks from the brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 54:10; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And it comes to pass, at the end of &#91;the&#93; days, that the brook dries up, for there has been no rain in the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; 1 Kings 17:2; Hebrews 13:6"> and the word of YHWH is to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:26; Obadiah 1:20; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> “Rise, go to Zarephath, that &#91;belongs&#93; to Sidon, and you have dwelt there; behold, I have commanded a widow woman to sustain you there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:7; Genesis 24:17; Genesis 21:15"> And he rises, and goes to Zarephath, and comes to the opening of the city, and behold, there &#91;is&#93; a widow woman gathering sticks, and he calls to her and says, “Please bring a little water to me in a vessel, and I drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–40; 1 Kings 18:4; Hebrews 13:2"> And she goes to bring &#91;it&#93;, and he calls to her and says, “Please bring a morsel of bread to me in your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; 1 Kings 17:1; Genesis 21:16"> And she says, “Your God YHWH lives, I do not have a cake, but the fullness of the hand of meal in a pitcher, and a little oil in a dish; and behold, I am gathering two sticks, and have gone in and prepared it for myself, and for my son, and we have eaten it—and died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Malachi 3:10; Genesis 22:1–2"> And Elijah says to her, “Do not fear, go, do according to your word, only make a little cake for me there, in the first place, and you have brought &#91;it&#93; out to me; and for you and for your son make &#91;it&#93; last;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:36–38; Matthew 14:17–20; 2 Kings 4:42–44"> for thus said YHWH, God of Israel: The pitcher of meal is not consumed, and the dish of oil is not lacking, until the day of YHWH’s giving a shower on the face of the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; Mark 12:43; Matthew 15:28"> And she goes, and does according to the word of Elijah, and she eats, she and he and her household &#91;for many&#93; days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:28–30; John 4:50–51; Luke 1:37"> the pitcher of meal was not consumed, and the dish of oil did not lack, according to the word of YHWH that He spoke by the hand of Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; Daniel 5:23; John 11:14"> And it comes to pass, after these things, the son of the woman, mistress of the house, has been sick, and his sickness is very severe until no breath has been left in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 5:8; John 2:4; 2 Kings 3:13"> And she says to Elijah, “What &#91;is this&#93; to me and to you, O man of God? You have come to me to cause my iniquity to be remembered, and to put my son to death!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; Acts 9:37"> And he says to her, “Give your son to me”; and he takes him out of her bosom, and takes him up to the upper chamber where he is abiding, and lays him on his own bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 5:15–18; Matthew 21:22; Joshua 7:8–9"> and cries to YHWH and says, “My God YHWH, have You also brought calamity on the widow with whom I am sojourning, to put her son to death?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:10; Acts 9:40; 2 Kings 4:33–35"> And he stretches himself out on the boy three times, and calls to YHWH and says, “O YHWH my God, please let the soul of this boy return into his midst”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 11:11; Hebrews 11:35"> and YHWH listens to the voice of Elijah, and the soul of the boy turns back into his midst, and he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 4:36–37; Acts 9:41; Luke 7:15"> And Elijah takes the boy, and brings him down from the upper chamber of the house, and gives him to his mother, and Elijah says, “See, your son lives!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:30; John 2:11; John 3:2"> And the woman says to Elijah, “Now &#91;by&#93; this I have known that you &#91;are&#93; a man of God, and the word of YHWH in your mouth &#91;is&#93; truth.”</span>
