## Moses’ Staff

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:14; Jeremiah 1:6"> Then Moses answered, “What if they do not believe me or listen to my voice? For they may say, ‘The LORD has not appeared to you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Exodus 4:20; Isaiah 11:4"> And the LORD asked him, “What is that in your hand?”<br />“A staff,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:10–15; Amos 5:19; Exodus 4:17"> “Throw it on the ground,” said the LORD. So Moses threw it on the ground, and it became a snake,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew nachash, in contrast to Aaron’s staff, which became a tannin in Exodus 7:10">a</a></sup>and he ran from it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Genesis 22:1–2; Psalms 91:13"> “Stretch out your hand and grab it by the tail,” the LORD said to Moses, who reached out his hand and caught the snake, and it turned back into a staff in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Genesis 48:3; Exodus 3:15"> “This is so that they may believe that the LORD, the God of their fathers—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob—has appeared to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses’ Hand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 5:27; Numbers 12:10"> Furthermore, the LORD said to Moses, “Put your hand inside your cloak.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew into your bosom; twice in this verse and twice in verse 7">b</a></sup>” So he put his hand inside his cloak, and when he took it out, his hand was leprous,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew word traditionally translated as leprous was used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.">c</a></sup>white as snow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:39; Matthew 8:3"> “Put your hand back inside your cloak,” said the LORD.<br />So Moses put his hand back inside his cloak, and when he took it out, it was restored, like the rest of his skin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:10; Deuteronomy 32:39; Exodus 4:30–31"> And the LORD said, “If they refuse to believe you or heed the witness of the first sign, they may believe that of the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–25; Exodus 1:22; Matthew 7:2"> But if they do not believe even these two signs or listen to your voice, take some water from the Nile and pour it on the dry ground. Then the water you take from the Nile will become blood on the ground.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Appointment of Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:12; Acts 7:22"> “Please, Lord,” Moses replied, “I have never been eloquent, neither in the past nor since You have spoken to Your servant, for I am slow of speech and tongue.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Psalms 146:8; Ezekiel 3:26–27"> And the LORD said to him, “Who gave man his mouth? Or who makes the mute or the deaf, the sighted or the blind? Is it not I, the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20"> Now go! I will help you as you speak, and I will teach you what to say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Exodus 4:1; Matthew 13:41"> But Moses replied, “Please, Lord, send someone else.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 14:13–15; Exodus 4:27; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> Then the anger of the LORD burned against Moses, and He said, “Is not Aaron the Levite your brother? I know that he can speak well, and he is now on his way to meet you. When he sees you, he will be glad in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:16; Deuteronomy 18:18; Luke 21:15"> You are to speak to him and put the words in his mouth. I will help both of you to speak, and I will teach you what to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:1–2; Psalms 82:6; John 10:34–35"> He will speak to the people for you. He will be your spokesman, and it will be as if you were God to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Exodus 7:9–20"> But take this staff in your hand so you can perform signs with it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses Leaves for Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Timothy 6:1"> Then Moses went back to his father-in-law Jethro<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Moses’ father-in-law Jethro was also called Reuel; see Exodus 2:18.">d</a></sup>and said to him, “Please let me return to my brothers in Egypt to see if they are still alive.”<br />“Go in peace,” Jethro replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 2:20; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 2:15"> Now the LORD had said to Moses in Midian, “Go back to Egypt, for all the men who sought to kill you are dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:9; Numbers 20:8–9; Exodus 18:3–4"> So Moses took his wife and sons, put them on a donkey, and headed back to Egypt. And he took the staff of God in his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Isaiah 63:17; Exodus 14:8"> The LORD instructed Moses, “When you go back to Egypt, see that you perform before Pharaoh all the wonders that I have put within your power. But I will harden<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or stiffen or strengthen">e</a></sup>his heart so that he will not let the people go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Romans 9:4; Jeremiah 31:9"> Then tell Pharaoh that this is what the LORD says: ‘Israel is My firstborn son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Exodus 12:29; Psalms 135:8"> and I told you to let My son go so that he may worship Me. But since you have refused to let him go, behold, I will kill your firstborn son!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 22:22–23; Hosea 13:8"> Now at a lodging place along the way, the LORD met Moses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew him">f</a></sup>and was about to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 5:2–3; Genesis 17:14; 2 Samuel 16:7"> But Zipporah took a flint knife, cut off her son’s foreskin, and touched it to Moses’ feet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew his feet">g</a></sup>“Surely you are a bridegroom of blood to me,” she said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So the LORD let him alone. (When she said, “bridegroom of blood,” she was referring to the circumcision.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## The People Believe Moses and Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 4:14–16; 1 Kings 19:8"> Meanwhile, the LORD had said to Aaron, “Go and meet Moses in the wilderness.” So he went and met Moses at the mountain of God and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 4:8–9; Exodus 4:15–16; Matthew 21:29"> And Moses told Aaron everything the LORD had sent him to say, and all the signs He had commanded him to perform.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 3:16; Exodus 24:11; Exodus 24:1"> Then Moses and Aaron went and assembled all the elders of the Israelites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 4:16"> and Aaron relayed everything the LORD had said to Moses.<br />And Moses performed the signs before the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:25; Psalms 106:12–13"> and they believed. And when they heard that the LORD had attended to the Israelites and had seen their affliction, they bowed down and worshiped.</span>
</div>
</div>
