## The Bronze Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> “You are to build an altar of acacia wood. The altar must be square, five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The altar was approximately 7.5 feet in length and width, and 4.5 feet high (2.3 meters in length and width, and 1.4 meters high).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> Make a horn on each of its four corners, so that the horns are of one piece, and overlay it with bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> Make all its utensils of bronze—its pots for removing ashes, its shovels, its sprinkling bowls, its meat forks, and its firepans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> Construct for it a grate of bronze mesh, and make a bronze ring at each of the four corners of the mesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> Set the grate beneath the ledge of the altar, so that the mesh comes halfway up the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> Additionally, make poles of acacia wood for the altar and overlay them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> The poles are to be inserted into the rings so that the poles are on two sides of the altar when it is carried.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Construct the altar with boards so that it is hollow. It is to be made just as you were shown on the mountain.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> You are also to make a courtyard for the tabernacle. On the south side of the courtyard make curtains of finely spun linen, a hundred cubits long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="100 cubits is approximately 150 feet or 45.7 meters; also in verse 11.">b</a></sup>on one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands on the posts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Likewise there are to be curtains on the north side, a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, and with silver hooks and bands on the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The curtains on the west side of the courtyard shall be fifty cubits wide,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="50 cubits is approximately 75 feet or 22.9 meters; also in verse 13.">c</a></sup>with ten posts and ten bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The east side of the courtyard, toward the sunrise, is to be fifty cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> Make the curtains on one side fifteen cubits long,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15 cubits is approximately 22.5 feet or 6.9 meters; also in verse 15.">d</a></sup>with three posts and three bases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the curtains on the other side fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> The gate of the courtyard shall be twenty cubits long,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="20 cubits is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters.">e</a></sup>with a curtain embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. It shall have four posts and four bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the posts around the courtyard shall have silver bands, silver hooks, and bronze bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The entire courtyard shall be a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The courtyard was approximately 150 feet long and 75 feet wide (45.7 meters long and 22.9 meters wide).">f</a></sup>with curtains of finely spun linen five cubits high,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5 cubits is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters.">g</a></sup>and with bronze bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the utensils of the tabernacle for every use, including all its tent pegs and the tent pegs of the courtyard, shall be made of bronze.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Oil for the Lamps

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And you are to command the Israelites to bring you pure oil of pressed olives for the light, to keep the lamps burning continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil that is in front of the Testimony,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The Testimony refers to the stone tablets in the ark of the covenant inscribed with the Ten Commandments.">h</a></sup>Aaron and his sons are to tend the lamps before the LORD from evening until morning. This is to be a permanent statute for the Israelites for the generations to come.</span>
</div>
</div>
