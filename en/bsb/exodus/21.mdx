## Hebrew Servants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> “These are the ordinances that you are to set before them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> If you buy a Hebrew servant, he is to serve you for six years. But in the seventh year, he shall go free without paying anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he arrived alone, he is to leave alone; if he arrived with a wife, she is to leave with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master gives him a wife and she bears him sons or daughters, the woman and her children shall belong to her master, and only the man shall go free.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> But if the servant declares, ‘I love my master and my wife and children; I do not want to go free,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then his master is to bring him before the judges.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or before God">a</a></sup>And he shall take him to the door or doorpost and pierce his ear with an awl. Then he shall serve his master for life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> And if a man sells his daughter as a servant, she is not to go free as the menservants do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If she is displeasing in the eyes of her master who had designated her for himself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or so that he does not designate her for himself">b</a></sup>he must allow her to be redeemed. He has no right to sell her to foreigners, since he has broken faith with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he chooses her for his son, he must deal with her as with a daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he takes another wife, he must not reduce the food, clothing, or marital rights of his first wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If, however, he does not provide her with these three things, she is free to go without monetary payment.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Personal Injury Laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> Whoever strikes and kills a man must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> If, however, he did not lie in wait, but God allowed it to happen, then I will appoint for you a place where he may flee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> But if a man schemes and acts willfully against his neighbor to kill him, you must take him away from My altar to be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> Whoever strikes his father or mother must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> Whoever kidnaps another man must be put to death, whether he sells him or the man is found in his possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> Anyone who curses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or dishonors or reviles">c</a></sup>his father or mother must surely be put to death.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 15:4 and Mark 7:10">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> If men are quarreling and one strikes the other with a stone or a fist, and he does not die but is confined to bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> then the one who struck him shall go unpunished, as long as the other can get up and walk around outside with his staff. Nevertheless, he must compensate the man for his lost work and see that he is completely healed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> If a man strikes his manservant or maidservant with a rod, and the servant dies by his hand, he shall surely be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> However, if the servant gets up after a day or two, the owner shall not be punished, since the servant is his property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> If men who are fighting strike a pregnant woman and her child is born prematurely,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally and her children come out">e</a></sup>but there is no further injury, he shall surely be fined as the woman’s husband demands and as the court allows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> But if a serious injury results, then you must require a life for a life—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> eye for eye, tooth for tooth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 5:38">f</a></sup>hand for hand, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> burn for burn, wound for wound, and stripe for stripe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> If a man strikes and blinds the eye of his manservant or maidservant, he must let the servant go free as compensation for the eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if he knocks out the tooth of his manservant or maidservant, he must let the servant go free as compensation for the tooth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> If an ox<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or a bull; also in verses 29–36">g</a></sup>gores a man or woman to death, the ox must surely be stoned, and its meat must not be eaten. But the owner of the ox shall not be held responsible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the ox has a habit of goring, and its owner has been warned yet does not restrain it, and it kills a man or woman, then the ox must be stoned and its owner must also be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If payment is demanded of him instead, he may redeem his life by paying the full amount demanded of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If the ox gores a son or a daughter, it shall be done to him according to the same rule.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox gores a manservant or maidservant, the owner must pay thirty shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30 shekels is approximately 12 ounces or 342 grams of silver.">h</a></sup>to the master of that servant, and the ox must be stoned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> If a man opens or digs a pit and fails to cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit shall make restitution; he must pay its owner, and the dead animal will be his.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> If a man’s ox injures his neighbor’s ox and it dies, they must sell the live one and divide the proceeds; they also must divide the dead animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> But if it was known that the ox had a habit of goring, yet its owner failed to restrain it, he shall pay full compensation, ox for ox, and the dead animal will be his.</span>
</div>
</div>
