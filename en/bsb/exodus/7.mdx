## God Commands Moses and Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:15–16; Psalms 82:6; John 10:35–36"> The LORD answered Moses, “See, I have made you like God to Pharaoh, and your brother Aaron will be your prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:15; Exodus 6:29; Deuteronomy 4:2"> You are to speak all that I command you, and your brother Aaron is to tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites go out of his land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:9; Psalms 105:27–36"> But I will harden Pharaoh’s heart, and though I will multiply My signs and wonders in the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 11:9; Exodus 6:6; Exodus 12:51"> Pharaoh will not listen to you.<br />Then I will lay My hand on Egypt, and by mighty acts of judgment I will bring the divisions of My people the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:18; Exodus 7:17; Exodus 8:22"> And the Egyptians will know that I am the LORD, when I stretch out My hand against Egypt and bring the Israelites out from among them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 7:2; Genesis 6:22; Exodus 7:10"> So Moses and Aaron did just as the LORD had commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Acts 7:30; Deuteronomy 31:2"> Moses was eighty years old and Aaron was eighty-three when they spoke to Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Aaron’s Staff

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; John 2:18; Isaiah 7:11"> “When Pharaoh tells you, ‘Perform a miracle,’ you are to say to Aaron, ‘Take your staff and throw it down before Pharaoh,’ and it will become a serpent.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew tannin; here and in verse 10, in contrast to Moses’ staff, which became a nachash in Exodus 4:3 and was noted again in Exodus 7:15">a</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:3; Luke 10:19; Amos 9:3"> So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and did just as the LORD had commanded. Aaron threw his staff down before Pharaoh and his officials, and it became a serpent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:22; 2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 8:18"> But Pharaoh called the wise men and sorcerers and magicians of Egypt, and they also did the same things by their magic arts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Exodus 9:11; Exodus 8:18–19"> Each one threw down his staff, and it became a serpent. But Aaron’s staff swallowed up the other staffs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 8:15; Zechariah 7:11–12"> Still, Pharaoh’s heart was hardened,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or stiffened or strengthened; also in verse 22">b</a></sup>and he would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The First Plague: Blood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 8:15; Exodus 10:1"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Pharaoh’s heart is unyielding;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or heavy or stubborn">c</a></sup>he refuses to let the people go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 2:5; Exodus 7:10"> Go to Pharaoh in the morning as you see him walking out to the water. Wait on the bank of the Nile to meet him, and take in your hand the staff that was changed into a snake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 9:1; Exodus 9:13"> Then say to him, ‘The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to tell you: Let My people go, so that they may worship Me in the wilderness. But until now you have not listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:9; Exodus 5:2; Exodus 7:5"> This is what the LORD says: By this you will know that I am the LORD. Behold, with the staff in my hand I will strike the water of the Nile, and it will turn to blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:24; Exodus 7:21; Numbers 11:20"> The fish in the Nile will die, the river will stink, and the Egyptians will be unable to drink its water.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 10:12; Exodus 8:5–6"> And the LORD said to Moses, “Tell Aaron, ‘Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt—over their rivers and canals and ponds and all the reservoirs—that they may become blood.’ There will be blood throughout the land of Egypt, even in the vessels of wood and stone.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 78:44; Psalms 105:29; Exodus 7:17–18"> Moses and Aaron did just as the LORD had commanded; in the presence of Pharaoh and his officials, Aaron raised the staff<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew he raised the staff; see verse 19; some translators Moses raised the staff.">d</a></sup>and struck the water of the Nile, and all the water was turned to blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 7:18; Revelation 8:9"> The fish in the Nile died, and the river smelled so bad that the Egyptians could not drink its water. And there was blood throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; 2 Timothy 3:8; Jeremiah 27:18"> But the magicians of Egypt did the same things by their magic arts. So Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he would not listen to Moses and Aaron, just as the LORD had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; Ezekiel 40:4; Malachi 2:2"> Instead, Pharaoh turned around, went into his palace, and did not take any of this to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So all the Egyptians dug around the Nile for water to drink, because they could not drink the water from the river.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:9–10; 2 Samuel 24:13"> And seven full days passed after the LORD had struck the Nile.</span>
</div>
</div>
