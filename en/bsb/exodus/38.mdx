## The Bronze Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> Bezalel constructed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally He constructed">a</a></sup>the altar of burnt offering from acacia wood. It was square, five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The altar was approximately 7.5 feet in length and width, and 4.5 feet high (2.3 meters in length and width, and 1.4 meters high).">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> He made a horn at each of its four corners, so that the horns and altar were of one piece, and he overlaid the altar with bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> He made all the altar’s utensils of bronze—its pots, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He made a grate of bronze mesh for the altar under its ledge, halfway up from the bottom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> At the four corners of the bronze grate he cast four rings as holders for the poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> And he made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> Then he inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar for carrying it. He made the altar with boards so that it was hollow.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> Then he constructed the courtyard. The south side of the courtyard was a hundred cubits long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="100 cubits is approximately 150 feet or 45.7 meters.">c</a></sup>and had curtains of finely spun linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, and with silver hooks and bands on the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The north side was also a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The hooks and bands of the posts were silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The west side was fifty cubits long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="50 cubits is approximately 75 feet or 22.9 meters.">d</a></sup>and had curtains, with ten posts and ten bases. The hooks and bands of the posts were silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the east side, toward the sunrise, was also fifty cubits long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> The curtains on one side of the entrance were fifteen cubits long,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15 cubits is approximately 22.5 feet or 6.9 meters.">e</a></sup>with three posts and three bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the curtains on the other side were also fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the curtains around the courtyard were made of finely spun linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The bases for the posts were bronze, the hooks and bands were silver, and the plating for the tops of the posts was silver. So all the posts of the courtyard were banded with silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> The curtain for the entrance to the courtyard was embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. It was twenty cubits long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="20 cubits is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters.">f</a></sup>and, like the curtains of the courtyard, five cubits high,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5 cubits is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> with four posts and four bronze bases. Their hooks were silver, as well as the bands and the plating of their tops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> All the tent pegs for the tabernacle and for the surrounding courtyard were bronze.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Inventory of Materials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> This is the inventory for the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the Testimony, as recorded at Moses’ command by the Levites under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> Bezalel son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made everything that the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> With him was Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver, designer, and embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> All the gold from the wave offering used for the work on the sanctuary totaled 29 talents and 730 shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The total weight of the gold was approximately 1.1 tons or 1 metric ton.">h</a></sup>according to the sanctuary shekel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> The silver from those numbered among the congregation totaled 100 talents and 1,775 shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The total weight of the silver was approximately 3.79 tons or 3.44 metric tons.">i</a></sup>according to the sanctuary shekel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> a beka per person, that is, half a shekel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A beka is half a shekel, or approximately 0.2 ounces or 5.7 grams.">j</a></sup>according to the sanctuary shekel, from everyone twenty years of age or older who had crossed over to be numbered, a total of 603,550 men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> The hundred talents of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="100 talents is approximately 3.77 tons or 3.42 metric tons of silver.">k</a></sup>were used to cast the bases of the sanctuary and the bases of the veil—100 bases from the 100 talents, one talent per base.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> With the 1,775 shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1,775 shekels is approximately 44.6 pounds or 20.2 kilograms of silver.">l</a></sup>he made the hooks for the posts, overlaid their tops, and supplied bands for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The bronze from the wave offering totaled 70 talents and 2,400 shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The total weight of the bronze was approximately 2.67 tons or 2.42 metric tons.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> He used it to make the bases for the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the bronze altar and its bronze grating, all the utensils for the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> the bases for the surrounding courtyard and its gate, and all the tent pegs for the tabernacle and its surrounding courtyard.</span>
</div>
</div>
