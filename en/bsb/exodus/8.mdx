## The Second Plague: Frogs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 5:1"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Go to Pharaoh and tell him that this is what the LORD says: ‘Let My people go, so that they may worship Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:45; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 7:14"> But if you refuse to let them go, I will plague your whole country with frogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:30; Exodus 12:34"> The Nile will teem with frogs, and they will come into your palace and up to your bedroom and onto your bed, into the houses of your officials and your people, and into your ovens and kneading bowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 4:37; Acts 12:22–23"> The frogs will come up on you and your people and all your officials.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19"> And the LORD said to Moses, “Tell Aaron, ‘Stretch out your hand with your staff over the rivers and canals and ponds, and cause the frogs to come up onto the land of Egypt.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 105:30; Psalms 78:45; Revelation 16:13"> So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; Exodus 7:22; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> But the magicians did the same thing by their magic arts, and they also brought frogs up onto the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 10:17; Exodus 9:28; Exodus 8:25–28"> Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, “Pray to the LORD to take the frogs away from me and my people. Then I will let your people go, that they may sacrifice to the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 10:15; Judges 7:2"> Moses said to Pharaoh, “You may have the honor over me. When shall I pray for you and your officials and your people that the frogs (except for those in the Nile) may be taken away from you and your houses?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 46:9; Psalms 86:8; 1 Chronicles 17:20"> “Tomorrow,” Pharaoh answered.<br />“May it be as you say,” Moses replied, “so that you may know that there is no one like the LORD our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 8:3; Exodus 8:9"> The frogs will depart from you and your houses and your officials and your people; they will remain only in the Nile.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Exodus 8:30; Exodus 10:18"> After Moses and Aaron had left Pharaoh, Moses cried out to the LORD for help with the frogs that He had brought against Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10–12"> And the LORD did as Moses requested, and the frogs in the houses, the courtyards, and the fields died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 8:24; Exodus 7:21; Joel 2:20"> They were piled into countless heaps, and there was a terrible stench in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Hebrews 3:8"> When Pharaoh saw that there was relief, however, he hardened<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or made heavy; also in verse 32">a</a></sup>his heart and would not listen to Moses and Aaron, just as the LORD had said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Third Plague: Gnats

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 8:5; Exodus 8:17"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Tell Aaron, ‘Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the earth, that it may turn into swarms of gnats throughout the land of Egypt.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 105:31; Isaiah 23:9; Acts 12:23"> This they did, and when Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the earth, gnats came upon man and beast. All the dust of the earth turned into gnats throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; Exodus 9:11; Daniel 5:8"> The magicians tried to produce gnats using their magic arts, but they could not. And the gnats remained on man and beast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:20; Psalms 8:3; Exodus 7:5"> “This is the finger of God,” the magicians said to Pharaoh. But Pharaoh’s heart was hardened,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or stiffened or strengthened">b</a></sup>and he would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Fourth Plague: Flies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:13; Exodus 8:1"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Get up early in the morning, and when Pharaoh goes out to the water, stand before him and tell him that this is what the LORD says: ‘Let My people go, so that they may worship Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:45; Isaiah 7:18; Psalms 105:31"> But if you will not let My people go, I will send swarms of flies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally a noxious mixture; also in verses 22, 24, 29, and 31">c</a></sup>upon you and your officials and your people and your houses. The houses of the Egyptians and even the ground where they stand will be full of flies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 9:4; Malachi 3:18; Exodus 10:23"> But on that day I will give special treatment to the land of Goshen, where My people live; no swarms of flies will be found there. In this way you will know that I, the LORD, am in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> I will make a distinction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX and Vulgate; Hebrew I will set redemption">d</a></sup>between My people and your people. This sign will take place tomorrow.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 105:31; Psalms 78:45; Exodus 8:21"> And the LORD did so. Thick swarms of flies poured into Pharaoh’s palace and into the houses of his officials. Throughout Egypt the land was ruined by swarms of flies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:27; Exodus 10:16"> Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, “Go, sacrifice to your God within this land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:34; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> But Moses replied, “It would not be right to do that, because the sacrifices we offer to the LORD our God would be detestable to the Egyptians. If we offer sacrifices that are detestable before the Egyptians, will they not stone us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 34:11"> We must make a three-day journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to the LORD our God as He commands us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> Pharaoh answered, “I will let you go and sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness, but you must not go very far. Now pray for me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Jeremiah 42:20–21; Exodus 8:8"> “As soon as I leave you,” Moses said, “I will pray to the LORD, so that tomorrow the swarms of flies will depart from Pharaoh and his officials and his people. But Pharaoh must not act deceitfully again by refusing to let the people go and sacrifice to the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 8:12; Exodus 9:33; James 5:16"> Then Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the LORD did as Moses requested. He removed the swarms of flies from Pharaoh and his officials and his people; not one fly remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Exodus 4:21; Romans 2:5"> But Pharaoh hardened his heart this time as well, and he would not let the people go.</span>
</div>
</div>
