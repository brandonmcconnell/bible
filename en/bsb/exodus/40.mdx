## Setting Up the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> “On the first day of the first month you are to set up the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> Put the ark of the Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the ark of the covenant; also in verses 5 and 21">a</a></sup>in it and screen off the ark with the veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> Then bring in the table and set out its arrangement; bring in the lampstand as well, and set up its lamps.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> Place the gold altar of incense in front of the ark of the Testimony, and hang the curtain at the entrance to the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> Place the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance to the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7; Psalms 26:6"> And place the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and put water in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 40:33; Ephesians 4:11–12; Matthew 16:18"> Set up the surrounding courtyard and hang the curtain for the entrance to the courtyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> Take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and everything in it; consecrate it along with all its furnishings, and it shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> Anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils; consecrate the altar, and it shall be most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Anoint the basin and its stand and consecrate them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> Then bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> And you are to clothe Aaron with the holy garments, anoint him, and consecrate him, so that he may serve Me as a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> Bring his sons forward and clothe them with tunics.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> Anoint them just as you anointed their father, so that they may also serve Me as priests. Their anointing will qualify them for a permanent priesthood throughout their generations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> Moses did everything just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> So the tabernacle was set up on the first day of the first month of the second year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> When Moses set up the tabernacle, he laid its bases, positioned its frames, inserted its crossbars, and set up its posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> Then he spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the covering over the tent, just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> Moses took the Testimony and placed it in the ark, attaching the poles to the ark; and he set the mercy seat atop the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> Then he brought the ark into the tabernacle, put up the veil for the screen, and shielded off the ark of the Testimony, just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> Moses placed the table in the Tent of Meeting on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> He arranged the bread on it before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> He also placed the lampstand in the Tent of Meeting opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> and set up the lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> Moses placed the gold altar in the Tent of Meeting, in front of the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> and he burned fragrant incense on it, just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 2:18"> Then he put up the curtain at the entrance to the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> He placed the altar of burnt offering near the entrance to the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, and offered on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, just as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; Ezekiel 36:25"> He placed the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar and put water in it for washing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> and from it Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Exodus 30:19–20; Exodus 40:19"> They washed whenever they entered the Tent of Meeting or approached the altar, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 40:8; Exodus 27:9–16; John 17:4"> And Moses set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar, and he hung the curtain for the entrance to the courtyard. So Moses finished the work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cloud and the Glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Then the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 7:2"> Moses was unable to enter the Tent of Meeting because the cloud had settled on it, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> Whenever the cloud was lifted from above the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out through all the stages of their journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> If the cloud was not lifted, they would not set out until the day it was taken up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For the cloud of the LORD was over the tabernacle by day, and fire was in the cloud by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel through all their journeys.</span>
</div>
