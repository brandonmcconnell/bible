## God Promises Deliverance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 12:31; Exodus 3:19–20"> But the LORD said to Moses, “Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh, for because of My mighty hand he will let the people go; because of My strong hand he will drive them out of his land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Malachi 3:6; Acts 17:24–25"> God also told Moses, “I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 68:4; Genesis 17:1"> I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob as God Almighty,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew El-Shaddai">a</a></sup>but by My name the LORD I did not make Myself known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:4; Genesis 15:18; Genesis 17:7–8"> I also established My covenant with them to give them the land of Canaan, the land where they lived as foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Psalms 106:44–45; Isaiah 63:9"> Furthermore, I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, whom the Egyptians are enslaving, and I have remembered My covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; 1 Chronicles 17:21; Exodus 7:4"> Therefore tell the Israelites: ‘I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from under the yoke of the Egyptians and deliver you from their bondage. I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with mighty acts of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 29:13; Deuteronomy 4:20"> I will take you as My own people, and I will be your God. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from under the yoke of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 15:18; Ezekiel 20:42"> And I will bring you into the land that I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I will give it to you as a possession. I am the LORD!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; Job 21:4; Exodus 14:12"> Moses relayed this message to the Israelites, but on account of their broken spirit and cruel bondage, they did not listen to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 6:29; Exodus 5:23; Exodus 3:10"> “Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites go out of his land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:10; Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:30"> But in the LORD’s presence Moses replied, “If the Israelites will not listen to me, then why would Pharaoh listen to me, since I am unskilled in speech?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew I have uncircumcised lips; also in verse 30">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; 1 Timothy 6:13; Psalms 91:11"> Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them a charge concerning both the Israelites and Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Genealogies of Moses and Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; 1 Chronicles 5:3; Numbers 26:5–7"> These were the heads of their fathers’ houses:<br />The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi. These were the clans of Reuben.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:24; Genesis 46:10; Numbers 26:12–13"> The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zohar is a variant of Zerah; see Numbers 26:13 and 1 Chronicles 4:24.">c</a></sup>and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman. These were the clans of Simeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:17; Genesis 46:11; 1 Chronicles 6:1"> These were the names of the sons of Levi according to their records: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi lived 137 years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; Numbers 3:18; 1 Chronicles 23:7"> The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei, by their clans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:2; Numbers 3:19"> The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath lived 133 years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi.<br />These were the clans of the Levites according to their records.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 2:1–2; Exodus 6:18"> And Amram married his father’s sister Jochebed, and she bore him Aaron and Moses. Amram lived 137 years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:1; 1 Chronicles 6:37–38; Numbers 16:32"> The sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 10:4; Numbers 3:30; Nehemiah 3:20"> The sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Elzaphan is a variant of Elizaphan; see Numbers 3:30.">d</a></sup>and Sithri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 4:19–20; Numbers 2:3; Numbers 1:7"> And Aaron married Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–23; Numbers 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:1"> The sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Abiasaph is a variant of Ebiasaph; see 1 Chronicles 6:23 and 1 Chronicles 9:19.">e</a></sup>These were the clans of the Korahites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Numbers 25:7–13; Joshua 22:31–32"> Aaron’s son Eleazar married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas.<br />These were the heads of the Levite families by their clans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Exodus 12:17; Exodus 7:4"> It was this Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, “Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt by their divisions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:10; Psalms 77:20"> Moses and Aaron were the ones who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt in order to bring the Israelites out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now on the day that the LORD spoke to Moses in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 6:11; Exodus 7:2; Exodus 6:2"> He said to him, “I am the LORD; tell Pharaoh king of Egypt everything I say to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> But in the LORD’s presence Moses replied, “Since I am unskilled in speech, why would Pharaoh listen to me?”</span>
</div>
</div>
