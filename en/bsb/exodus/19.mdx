## Israel at Mount Sinai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 12:6; Leviticus 23:16–18"> In the third month, on the same day of the month<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew In the third month, on the same day; that is, two months after leaving Egypt; see Numbers 33:3.">a</a></sup>that the Israelites had left the land of Egypt, they came to the Wilderness of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 17:1; Exodus 3:12"> After they had set out from Rephidim, they entered the Wilderness of Sinai, and Israel camped there in front of the mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:4; Acts 7:38; Exodus 20:21"> Then Moses went up to God, and the LORD called to him from the mountain, “This is what you are to tell the house of Jacob and explain to the sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> ‘You have seen for yourselves what I did to Egypt, and how I carried you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18"> Now if you will indeed obey My voice and keep My covenant, you will be My treasured possession out of all the nations—for the whole earth is Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 2:5"> And unto Me you shall be a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.’ These are the words that you are to speak to the Israelites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:29–30; Exodus 3:16; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> So Moses went back and summoned the elders of the people and set before them all these words that the LORD had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Joshua 24:24"> And all the people answered together, “We will do everything that the LORD has spoken.”<br />So Moses brought their words back to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:36; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 24:15–16"> The LORD said to Moses, “Behold, I will come to you in a dense cloud, so that the people will hear when I speak with you, and they will always put their trust in you.”<br />And Moses relayed to the LORD what the people had said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow. They must wash their clothes</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Exodus 34:5; Deuteronomy 33:2"> and be prepared by the third day, for on the third day the LORD will come down on Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:20–21; Joshua 3:4; Exodus 19:21"> And you are to set up a boundary for the people around the mountain and tell them, ‘Be careful not to go up on the mountain or touch its base. Whoever touches the mountain shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Hebrews 12:20; Exodus 19:16"> No hand shall touch him, but he shall surely be stoned or shot with arrows—whether man or beast, he must not live.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 12:20">b</a></sup><br />Only when the ram’s horn sounds a long blast may they approach the mountain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or may they go up on the mountain">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:10"> When Moses came down from the mountain to the people, he consecrated them, and they washed their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:5; 1 Samuel 21:4–5; Exodus 19:11"> “Be prepared for the third day,” he said to the people. “Do not draw near to a woman.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Visits Sinai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:18–19; Psalms 18:11–14; Hebrews 12:21"> On the third day, when morning came, there was thunder and lightning. A thick cloud was upon the mountain, and a very loud blast of the ram’s horn went out, so that all the people in the camp trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 5:5"> Then Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet with God, and they stood at the foot of the mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:5; Exodus 3:2; Psalms 144:5"> Mount Sinai was completely enveloped in smoke, because the LORD had descended on it in fire. And the smoke rose like the smoke of a furnace, and the whole mountain quaked violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Exodus 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> And as the sound of the ram’s horn grew louder and louder, Moses spoke and God answered him in the thunder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Exodus 34:2; Exodus 19:11"> The LORD descended to the top of Mount Sinai and called Moses to the summit. So Moses went up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 3:5; Hebrews 12:28–29"> and the LORD said to him, “Go down and warn the people not to break through to see the LORD, lest many of them perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:5; Acts 5:10; 1 Chronicles 13:9–11"> Even the priests who approach the LORD must consecrate themselves, or the LORD will break out against them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 19:12; Joshua 3:4–5"> But Moses said to the LORD, “The people cannot come up Mount Sinai, for You solemnly warned us, ‘Put a boundary around the mountain and set it apart as holy.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 12:18–25; Hebrews 12:29"> And the LORD replied, “Go down and bring Aaron with you. But the priests and the people must not break through to come up to the LORD, or He will break out against them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.</span>
</div>
</div>
