## Constructing the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> Bezalel went on to construct the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The ark was approximately 3.75 feet long, 2.25 feet wide, and 2.25 feet high (114.3 centimeters long, 68.6 centimeters wide, and 68.6 centimeters high).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> He overlaid it with pure gold, both inside and out, and made a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he cast four gold rings for its four feet, two rings on one side and two on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> Then he made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> He inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark in order to carry it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Mercy Seat

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> He constructed a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The mercy seat was approximately 3.75 feet long and 2.25 feet wide (114.3 centimeters long and 68.6 centimeters wide).">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> He made two cherubim of hammered gold at the ends of the mercy seat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> one cherub on one end and one on the other, all made from one piece of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> And the cherubim had wings that spread upward, overshadowing the mercy seat. The cherubim faced each other, looking toward the mercy seat.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Table of Showbread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> He also made the table of acacia wood two cubits long, a cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The table was approximately 3 feet long, 1.5 feet wide, and 2.25 feet high (91.4 centimeters long, 45.7 centimeters wide, and 68.6 centimeters high).">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He overlaid it with pure gold and made a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made a rim around it a handbreadth wide<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A handbreadth is approximately 2.9 inches or 7.4 centimeters.">d</a></sup>and put a gold molding on the rim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He cast four gold rings for the table and fastened them to the four corners at its four legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The rings were placed close to the rim, to serve as holders for the poles used to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He made the poles of acacia wood for carrying the table and overlaid them with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> He also made the utensils for the table out of pure gold: its plates and dishes, as well as its bowls and pitchers for pouring drink offerings.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lampstand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> Then he made the lampstand out of pure hammered gold, all of one piece: its base and shaft, its cups, and its buds and petals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Six branches extended from the sides, three on one side and three on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> There were three cups shaped like almond blossoms on the first branch, each with buds and petals, three on the next branch, and the same for all six branches that extended from the lampstand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And on the lampstand were four cups shaped like almond blossoms with buds and petals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> A bud was under the first pair of branches that extended from the lampstand, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> The buds and branches were all of one piece with the lampstand, hammered out of pure gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> He also made its seven lamps, its wick trimmers, and trays of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He made the lampstand and all its utensils from a talent of pure gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A talent is approximately 75.4 pounds or 34.2 kilograms of gold.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The Altar of Incense

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> He made the altar of incense out of acacia wood. It was square, a cubit long, a cubit wide, and two cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The altar was approximately 1.5 feet in length and width, and 3 feet high (45.7 centimeters in length and width, and 91.4 centimeters high).">f</a></sup>Its horns were of one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he overlaid with pure gold the top and all the sides and horns. Then he made a molding of gold around it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He made two gold rings below the molding on opposite sides to hold the poles used to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> He also made the sacred anointing oil and the pure, fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer.</span>
</div>
