## The Visit of Jethro

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; Psalms 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> Now Moses’ father-in-law Jethro,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moses’ father-in-law Jethro was also called Reuel; see Exodus 2:18.">a</a></sup>the priest of Midian, heard about all that God had done for Moses and His people Israel, and how the LORD had brought Israel out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> After Moses had sent back his wife Zipporah, his father-in-law Jethro had received her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; Psalms 39:12"> along with her two sons. One son was named Gershom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gershom sounds like the Hebrew for sojourner.">b</a></sup>for Moses had said, “I have been a foreigner in a foreign land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> The other son was named Eliezer,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Eliezer means God is my helper.">c</a></sup>for Moses had said, “The God of my father was my helper and delivered me from the sword of Pharaoh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> Moses’ father-in-law Jethro, along with Moses’ wife and sons, came to him in the desert, where he was encamped at the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He sent word to Moses, “I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to you with your wife and her two sons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> So Moses went out to meet his father-in-law and bowed down and kissed him. They greeted each other and went into the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalms 107:2"> Then Moses recounted to his father-in-law all that the LORD had done to Pharaoh and the Egyptians for Israel’s sake, all the hardships they had encountered along the way, and how the LORD had delivered them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> And Jethro rejoiced over all the good things the LORD had done for Israel, whom He had rescued from the hand of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> Jethro declared, “Blessed be the LORD, who has delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians and of Pharaoh, and who has delivered the people from the hand of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> Now I know that the LORD is greater than all other gods, for He did this when they treated Israel with arrogance.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> Then Moses’ father-in-law Jethro brought a burnt offering and sacrifices to God, and Aaron came with all the elders of Israel to eat bread with Moses’ father-in-law in the presence of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jethro Advises Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> The next day Moses took his seat to judge the people, and they stood around him from morning until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When his father-in-law saw all that Moses was doing for the people, he asked, “What is this that you are doing for the people? Why do you sit alone as judge, with all the people standing around you from morning till evening?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> “Because the people come to me to inquire of God,” Moses replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> “Whenever they have a dispute, it is brought to me to judge between one man and another, and I make known to them the statutes and laws of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But Moses’ father-in-law said to him, “What you are doing is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> Surely you and these people with you will wear yourselves out, because the task is too heavy for you. You cannot handle it alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> Now listen to me; I will give you some advice, and may God be with you. You must be the people’s representative before God and bring their causes to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalms 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> Teach them the statutes and laws, and show them the way to live and the work they must do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> Furthermore, select capable men from among the people—God-fearing, trustworthy men who are averse to dishonest gain. Appoint them over the people as leaders of thousands, of hundreds, of fifties, and of tens.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> Have these men judge the people at all times. Then they can bring you any major issue, but all minor cases they can judge on their own, so that your load may be lightened as they share it with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If you follow this advice and God so directs you, then you will be able to endure, and all these people can go home in peace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> Moses listened to his father-in-law and did everything he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> So Moses chose capable men from all Israel and made them heads over the people as leaders of thousands, of hundreds, of fifties, and of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> And they judged the people at all times; they would bring the difficult cases to Moses, but any minor issue they would judge themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> Then Moses sent his father-in-law on his way, and Jethro returned to his own land.</span>
</div>
</div>
