## The Kings Defeated East of the Jordan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Judges 11:18; Joshua 1:15"> Now these are the kings of the land whom the Israelites struck down and whose lands they took beyond the Jordan to the east, from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon, including all the Arabah eastward:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 32:22; Judges 11:22; Psalms 136:19–20"> Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon. He ruled from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, along the middle of the valley, up to the Jabbok River (the border of the Ammonites), that is, half of Gilead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 13:20; Joshua 11:2"> as well as the Arabah east of the Sea of Chinnereth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">a</a></sup>to the Sea of the Arabah (the Salt Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">b</a></sup>), eastward through Beth-jeshimoth, and southward below the slopes of Pisgah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 3:10–11; Joshua 13:12"> And Og king of Bashan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew And the territory of Og king of Bashan">c</a></sup>one of the remnant of the Rephaim, who lived in Ashtaroth and Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; 1 Samuel 27:8; Joshua 13:11"> He ruled over Mount Hermon, Salecah, all of Bashan up to the border of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and half of Gilead to the border of Sihon king of Heshbon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:29; Luke 22:29–42"> Moses, the servant of the LORD, and the Israelites had struck them down and given their land as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.</span>
</div>
</div>
