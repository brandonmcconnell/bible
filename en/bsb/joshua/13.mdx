## Lands Yet Unconquered

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:10; Deuteronomy 31:3; Joshua 24:29"> Now Joshua was old and well along in years, and the LORD said to him, “You are old and well along in years, but very much of the land remains to be possessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; 1 Samuel 27:8; Joel 3:4"> This is the land that remains:<br />All the territory of the Philistines and the Geshurites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:23; Judges 3:3; Jeremiah 2:18"> from the Shihor east of Egypt to the territory of Ekron on the north (considered to be Canaanite territory)—that of the five Philistine rulers of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, as well as that of the Avvites;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 19:30; 1 Samuel 4:1; Judges 1:34–36"> to the south, all the land of the Canaanites, from Mearah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Arah">a</a></sup>of the Sidonians to Aphek, as far as the border of the Amorites;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 12:7; Psalms 83:7"> the land of the Gebalites;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the area of Byblos">b</a></sup><br />and all Lebanon to the east, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 11:8; Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:21–23"> All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim—all the Sidonians—I Myself will drive out before the Israelites. Be sure to divide it by lot as an inheritance to Israel, as I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Numbers 33:54; Ezekiel 48:23–29"> Now therefore divide this land as an inheritance to the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Inheritance East of the Jordan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Joshua 22:4"> The other half of Manasseh, along with the Reubenites and Gadites, had received the inheritance Moses had given them beyond the Jordan to the east, just as Moses the servant of the LORD had assigned to them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Numbers 21:30; Joshua 12:2"> The area from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, along with the city in the middle of the valley, the whole plateau of Medeba as far as Dibon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–26"> and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23; Joshua 12:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:47–48"> also Gilead and the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, all of Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Joshua 14:3–4; Numbers 21:23–35"> the whole kingdom of Og in Bashan, who had reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei and had remained as a remnant of the Rephaim.<br />Moses had struck them down and dispossessed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:12–13; Joshua 13:11; Numbers 33:55"> but the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites. So Geshur and Maacath dwell among the Israelites to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Joshua 14:3–4; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> To the tribe of Levi, however, Moses had given no inheritance. The food offerings to the LORD, the God of Israel, are their inheritance, just as He had promised them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Reuben’s Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> This is what Moses had given to the clans of the tribe of Reuben:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Joshua 12:2; Isaiah 16:7–9"> The territory from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, along with the city in the middle of the valley, to the whole plateau beyond Medeba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 22:41; Numbers 32:38; Numbers 21:19"> to Heshbon and all its cities on the plateau, including Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Joshua 21:36–37; 1 Chronicles 6:78–79"> Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 32:37–38"> Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Deuteronomy 4:46; Ezekiel 25:9"> Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Deuteronomy 3:10; Deuteronomy 2:30–36"> all the cities of the plateau and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon until Moses killed him and the chiefs of Midian (Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba), the princes of Sihon who lived in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:15"> The Israelites also killed the diviner Balaam son of Beor along with the others they put to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the border of the Reubenites was the bank of the Jordan.<br />This was the inheritance of the clans of the Reubenites, including the cities and villages.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gad’s Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:34–36"> This is what Moses had given to the clans of the tribe of Gad:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26; 2 Samuel 11:1; Numbers 32:35"> The territory of Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, near Rabbah;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27; 2 Samuel 2:8; Judges 10:17"> the territory from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="LXX, Syriac, and Vulgate; Hebrew Li-debir, a variant of Lo-debar; see 2 Samuel 9:4, 2 Samuel 17:27, and Amos 6:13.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 32:36; Numbers 34:11; Genesis 33:17"> and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, with the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon (the territory on the east side of the Jordan up to the edge of the Sea of Chinnereth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">d</a></sup>).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> This was the inheritance of the clans of the Gadites, including the cities and villages.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Manasseh’s Eastern Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> This is what Moses had given to the clans of the half-tribe of Manasseh, that is, to half the tribe of the descendants of Manasseh:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; Deuteronomy 3:13–15; Numbers 32:39–41"> The territory from Mahanaim through all Bashan—all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, including all the towns of Jair that are in Bashan, sixty cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Numbers 32:39–40"> half of Gilead; and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the royal cities of Og in Bashan.<br />All this was for the clans of the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, that is, half of the descendants of Machir.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> These were the portions Moses had given them on the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 18:7; Joshua 13:14; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> To the tribe of Levi, however, Moses had given no inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, just as He had promised them.</span>
</div>
