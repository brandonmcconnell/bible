## To Everything There Is a Season

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">To everything there is a season,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a time for every purpose under heaven:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to be born and a time to die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to plant and a time to uproot,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to kill and a time to heal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to break down and a time to build,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to weep and a time to laugh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to mourn and a time to dance,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to cast away stones and a time to gather stones together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to search and a time to count as lost,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to keep and a time to discard,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to tear and a time to mend,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to be silent and a time to speak,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to love and a time to hate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time for war and a time for peace.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Works Remain Forever

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What does the worker gain from his toil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen the burden that God has laid upon the sons of men to occupy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also set eternity in the hearts of men, yet they cannot fathom the work that God has done from beginning to end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I know that there is nothing better for them than to rejoice and do good while they live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> and also that every man should eat and drink and find satisfaction in all his labor—this is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I know that everything God does endures forever; nothing can be added to it or taken from it. God does it so that they should fear Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> What exists has already been, and what will be has already been, for God will call to account what has passed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## From Dust to Dust

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> Furthermore, I saw under the sun that in the place of judgment there is wickedness, and in the place of righteousness there is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> I said in my heart, “God will judge the righteous and the wicked, since there is a time for every activity and every deed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said to myself, “As for the sons of men, God tests them so that they may see for themselves that they are but beasts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> For the fates of both men and beasts are the same: As one dies, so dies the other—they all have the same breath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or spirit">a</a></sup>Man has no advantage over the animals, since everything is futile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> All go to one place; all come from dust, and all return to dust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Who knows if the spirit of man rises upward and the spirit of the animal descends into the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> I have seen that there is nothing better for a man than to enjoy his work, because that is his lot. For who can bring him to see what will come after him?</span>
</div>
