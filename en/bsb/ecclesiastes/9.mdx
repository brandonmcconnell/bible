## Death Comes to Good and Bad

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:3; John 10:27–30; 1 Peter 1:5"> So I took all this to heart and concluded that the righteous and the wise, as well as their deeds, are in God’s hands. Man does not know what lies ahead, whether love or hate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Malachi 3:5"> It is the same for all: There is a common fate for the righteous and the wicked, for the good and the bad,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX, Syriac, and Vulgate; Hebrew does not include and the bad.">a</a></sup>for the clean and the unclean, for the one who sacrifices and the one who does not. As it is for the good, so it is for the sinner; as it is for the one who makes a vow, so it is for the one who refuses to take a vow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Ecclesiastes 1:17"> This is an evil in everything that is done under the sun: There is one fate for everyone. Furthermore, the hearts of men are full of evil and madness while they are alive, and afterward they join the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:7–12; Isaiah 38:18; Luke 16:26–29"> There is hope, however, for anyone who is among the living; for even a live dog is better than a dead lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 26:14; Ecclesiastes 1:11; Psalms 6:5"> For the living know that they will die, but the dead know nothing. They have no further reward, because the memory of them is forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 146:3–4; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Job 3:17–18"> Their love, their hate, and their envy have already vanished, and they will never again have a share in all that is done under the sun.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Enjoy Your Portion in This Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Deuteronomy 12:7; Ecclesiastes 10:19"> Go, eat your bread with joy, and drink your wine with a cheerful heart, for God has already approved your works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:14; Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let your garments always be white,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and never spare the oil for your head.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; Ecclesiastes 2:10; Malachi 2:15"> Enjoy life with your beloved wife all the days of the fleeting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or futile; twice in this verse">b</a></sup>life that God has given you under the sun—all your fleeting days. For this is your portion in life and in your labor under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 5:16; Romans 12:11"> Whatever you find to do with your hands, do it with all your might, for in Sheol, where you are going, there is no work or planning or knowledge or wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Proverbs 21:30–31; Lamentations 3:37–38"> I saw something else under the sun: The race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong; neither is the bread to the wise, nor the wealth to the intelligent, nor the favor to the skillful. For time and chance happen to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Luke 21:34–36; Ecclesiastes 8:5–7"> For surely no man knows his time: Like fish caught in a cruel net or birds trapped in a snare, so men are ensnared in an evil time that suddenly falls upon them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Wisdom Is Better than Strength

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:16; Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 6:1"> I have also seen this wisdom under the sun, and it was great to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:15–22; 2 Kings 6:24–20"> There was a small city with few men. A mighty king came against it, surrounded it, and built large siege ramps against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:13; Genesis 40:23; Esther 6:2–3"> Now a poor wise man was found in the city, and he saved the city by his wisdom. Yet no one remembered that poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Proverbs 21:22"> And I said, “Wisdom is better than strength, but the wisdom of the poor man is despised, and his words are not heeded.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 3:17–18; Proverbs 28:23; James 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The calm words of the wise are heeded</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over the shouts of a ruler among fools.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Ecclesiastes 9:16; Joshua 7:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom is better than weapons of war,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but one sinner destroys much good.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
