## The Futility of Pleasure

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Ecclesiastes 8:15; James 5:5"> I said to myself, “Come now, I will test you with pleasure; enjoy what is good!”<br />But it proved to be futile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:13; Ecclesiastes 7:2–6; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> I said of laughter, “It is folly,” and of pleasure, “What does it accomplish?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 1:17; Proverbs 31:4–5"> I sought to cheer my body with wine and to embrace folly—my mind still guiding me with wisdom—until I could see what was worthwhile for men to do under heaven during the few days of their lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 7:1–12; 2 Chronicles 8:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> I expanded my pursuits. I built houses and planted vineyards for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> I made gardens and parks for myself, where I planted all kinds of fruit trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> I built reservoirs to water my groves of flourishing trees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4; Genesis 15:3; 1 Kings 4:23"> I acquired menservants and maidservants, and servants were born in my house. I also owned more herds and flocks than anyone in Jerusalem before me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; 1 Kings 9:28; 2 Samuel 19:35"> and I accumulated for myself silver and gold and the treasure of kings and provinces. I gathered to myself male and female singers, and the delights of the sons of men—many concubines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; Ecclesiastes 1:16; 1 Kings 10:23"> So I became great and surpassed all in Jerusalem who had preceded me; and my wisdom remained with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> Anything my eyes desired, I did not deny myself. I refused my heart no pleasure. For my heart took delight in all my work, and this was the reward for all my labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 John 2:16–17; Ecclesiastes 1:3"> Yet when I considered all the works that my hands had accomplished and what I had toiled to achieve, I found everything to be futile, a pursuit of the wind; there was nothing to be gained under the sun.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Wise and the Foolish

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> Then I turned to consider wisdom and madness and folly; for what more can the king’s successor do than what has already been accomplished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> And I saw that wisdom exceeds folly, just as light exceeds darkness:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wise man has eyes in his head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the fool walks in darkness.</span><br />Yet I also came to realize that one fate overcomes them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> So I said to myself, “The fate of the fool will also befall me. What then have I gained by being wise?”<br />And I said to myself that this too is futile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> For there is no lasting remembrance of the wise, just as with the fool, seeing that both will be forgotten in the days to come. Alas, the wise man will die just like the fool!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> So I hated life, because the work that is done under the sun was grievous to me. For everything is futile and a pursuit of the wind.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Futility of Work

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:6; Psalms 49:10; Luke 16:27–28"> I hated all for which I had toiled under the sun, because I must leave it to the man who comes after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 9:13"> And who knows whether that man will be wise or foolish? Yet he will take over all the labor at which I have worked skillfully under the sun. This too is futile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Job 17:11–15; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> So my heart began to despair over all the labor that I had done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21; 2 Chronicles 34:2"> When there is a man who has labored with wisdom, knowledge, and skill, and he must give his portion to a man who has not worked for it, this too is futile and a great evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 1:3; Philippians 4:6"> For what does a man get for all the toil and striving with which he labors under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 5:7; Psalms 6:6–7"> Indeed, all his days are filled with grief, and his task is sorrowful; even at night, his mind does not rest. This too is futile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 8:15; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Nothing is better for a man than to eat and drink and enjoy his work. I have also seen that this is from the hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:1–12; 1 Kings 4:21–24"> For apart from Him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts, LXX, Syriac; most Hebrew manuscripts apart from me or more than I">a</a></sup>who can eat and who can find enjoyment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> To the man who is pleasing in His sight, He gives wisdom and knowledge and joy, but to the sinner He assigns the task of gathering and accumulating that which he will hand over to one who pleases God. This too is futile and a pursuit of the wind.</span>
</div>
</div>
