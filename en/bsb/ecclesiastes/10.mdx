## Wisdom and Folly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:13–16; Galatians 2:12–14; Nehemiah 13:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">As dead flies bring a stench to the perfumer’s oil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so a little folly outweighs wisdom and honor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:18–20; Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 10:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A wise man’s heart inclines to the right,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the heart of a fool to the left.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 18:2; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even as the fool walks along the road, his sense is lacking,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he shows everyone that he is a fool.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 25:15; Ecclesiastes 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:24–44"> <span className="pl pl-1">If the ruler’s temper flares against you, do not abandon your post,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for calmness lays great offenses to rest.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is an evil I have seen under the sun—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an error that proceeds from the ruler:</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Esther 6:8; Proverbs 30:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have seen slaves on horseback,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while princes go on foot like slaves.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who digs a pit may fall into it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he who breaches a wall may be bitten by a snake.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who quarries stones may be injured by them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he who splits logs endangers himself.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:5; Colossians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 10:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">If the axe is dull and the blade unsharpened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">more strength must be exerted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but skill produces success.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">If the snake bites before it is charmed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is no profit for the charmer.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the lips of a fool consume him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The beginning of his talk is folly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the end of his speech is evil madness.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Isaiah 5:11–12; Isaiah 3:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to you, O land whose king is a youth,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or a servant">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whose princes feast in the morning.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 24:30–34; Proverbs 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through laziness the roof caves in,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in the hands of the idle, the house leaks.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
