## The Evil of Oppression

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:16; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Isaiah 5:7"> Again I looked, and I considered all the oppression taking place under the sun. I saw the tears of the oppressed, and they had no comforter; the power lay in the hands of their oppressors, and there was no comforter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> So I admired the dead, who had already died, above the living, who are still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> But better than both is he who has not yet existed, who has not seen the evil that is done under the sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I saw that all labor and success spring from a man’s envy of his neighbor. This too is futile and a pursuit of the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fool folds his hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and consumes his own flesh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; Psalms 37:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Better one handful with tranquility</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than two handfuls with toil and pursuit of the wind.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalms 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> Again, I saw futility under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> There is a man all alone, without even a son or brother. And though there is no end to his labor, his eyes are still not content with his wealth: “For whom do I toil and bereave my soul of enjoyment?” This too is futile—a miserable task.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> For if one falls down, his companion can lift him up; but pity the one who falls without another to help him up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> Again, if two lie down together, they will keep warm; but how can one keep warm alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> And though one may be overpowered, two can resist. Moreover, a cord of three strands is not quickly broken.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Futility of Power

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> Better is a poor but wise youth than an old but foolish king who no longer knows how to take a warning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For the youth has come from the prison to the kingship, though he was born poor in his own kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I saw that all who lived and walked under the sun followed this second one, the youth who succeeded the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> There is no limit to all the people who were before them. Yet the successor will not be celebrated by those who come even later. This too is futile and a pursuit of the wind.</span>
</div>
