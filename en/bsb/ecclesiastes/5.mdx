## Approaching God with Awe

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:19; Hebrews 12:28–29; Proverbs 15:8"> Guard your steps when you go to the house of God. Draw near to listen rather than to offer the sacrifice of fools, who do not know that they do wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:7; Proverbs 10:19; Isaiah 55:9"> Do not be quick to speak, and do not be hasty in your heart to utter a word before God. For God is in heaven and you are on earth. So let your words be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 10:19; Ecclesiastes 10:12–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a dream comes through many cares,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so the speech of a fool comes with many words.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; Deuteronomy 23:21–23; Psalms 76:11"> When you make a vow to God, do not delay in fulfilling it, because He takes no pleasure in fools. Fulfill your vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:4; Deuteronomy 23:22; Proverbs 20:25"> It is better not to vow than to make a vow and not fulfill it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:26; 2 John 1:8; James 3:2"> Do not let your mouth cause your flesh to sin, and do not tell the messenger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or angel">a</a></sup>that your vow was a mistake. Why should God be angry with your words and destroy the work of your hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> For as many dreams bring futility, so do many words. Therefore, fear God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Futility of Wealth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> If you see the oppression of the poor and the denial of justice and righteousness in the province, do not be astonished at the matter; for one official is watched by a superior, and others higher still are over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:7–23; Psalms 115:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> The produce of the earth is taken by all; the king himself profits from the fields.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15"> He who loves money is never satisfied by money, and he who loves wealth is never satisfied by income. This too is futile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Habakkuk 2:13; Psalms 119:36–37"> When good things increase, so do those who consume them; what then is the profit to the owner, except to behold them with his eyes?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24; Psalms 4:8; Jeremiah 31:26"> The sleep of the worker is sweet, whether he eats little or much, but the abundance of the rich man permits him no sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:1–2; Luke 18:22–23; Genesis 19:14"> There is a grievous evil I have seen under the sun: wealth hoarded to the harm of its owner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> or wealth lost in a failed venture, so when that man has a son there is nothing to pass on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> As a man came from his mother’s womb, so he will depart again, naked as he arrived. He takes nothing for his labor to carry in his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; John 6:27"> This too is a grievous affliction: Exactly as a man is born, so he will depart. What does he gain as he toils for the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ezekiel 4:16–17; Psalms 90:7–11"> Moreover, all his days he eats in darkness, with much sorrow, sickness, and anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> Here is what I have seen to be good and fitting: to eat and drink, and to find satisfaction in all the labor one does under the sun during the few days of life that God has given him—for this is his lot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> Furthermore, God has given riches and wealth to every man, and He has enabled him to enjoy them, to accept his lot, and to rejoice in his labor. This is a gift from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–24; Isaiah 65:13–14; Psalms 37:16"> For a man seldom considers the days of his life, because God keeps him occupied with the joy of his heart.</span>
</div>
</div>
