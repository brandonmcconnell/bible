<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely extortion turns a wise man into a fool,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a bribe corrupts the heart.</span></span>

## The Limits of Human Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> In my futile life I have seen both of these:<br />A righteous man perishing in his righteousness,<br />and a wicked man living long in his wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Do not be overly righteous, and do not make yourself too wise. Why should you destroy yourself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Do not be excessively wicked, and do not be a fool. Why should you die before your time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is good to grasp the one and not let the other slip from your hand. </div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom makes the wise man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">stronger than ten rulers in a city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely there is no righteous man on earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who does good and never sins.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Do not pay attention to every word that is spoken, or you may hear your servant cursing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> For you know in your heart that many times you yourself have cursed others.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All this I tested by wisdom, saying, “I resolve to be wise.” But it was beyond me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> What exists is out of reach and very deep. Who can fathom it?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I directed my mind to understand, to explore, to search out wisdom and explanations, and to understand the stupidity of wickedness and the folly of madness.</span>
