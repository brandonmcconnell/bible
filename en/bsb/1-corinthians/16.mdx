## The Collection for the Saints

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 2 Corinthians 8:1–9; 2 Corinthians 9:12–15"> Now about the collection for the saints, you are to do as I directed the churches of Galatia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–10; Deuteronomy 15:11–14; Deuteronomy 8:18"> On the first day of every week, each of you should set aside a portion of his income, saving it up, so that when I come no collections will be needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> Then, on my arrival, I will send letters with those you recommend to carry your gift to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> And if it is advisable for me to go also, they can travel with me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Travel Plans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> After I go through Macedonia, however, I will come to you; for I will be going through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> Perhaps I will stay with you awhile, or even spend the winter, so that you can help me on my journey, wherever I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> For I do not want to see you now only in passing; I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Weeks (see Exodus 34:22).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> because a great door for effective work has opened to me, even though many oppose me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Timothy and Apollos

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> If Timothy comes, see to it that he has nothing to fear while he is with you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, just as I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> No one, then, should treat him with contempt. Send him on his way in peace so that he can return to me, for I am expecting him along with the brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> Now about our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to go to you with the brothers. He was not at all inclined to go now, but he will go when he has the opportunity.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concluding Exhortations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Be on the alert. Stand firm in the faith. Be men of courage. Be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Do everything in love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> You know that Stephanas and his household were the first converts in Achaia, and they have devoted themselves to the service of the saints. Now I urge you, brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> to submit to such as these, and to every fellow worker and laborer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> I am glad that Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus have arrived, because they have supplied what was lacking from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> For they refreshed my spirit and yours as well. Show your appreciation, therefore, to such men.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brothers here send you greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> This greeting is in my own hand—Paul.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.<br />Amen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="SBL, WH, and NA do not include Amen.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
