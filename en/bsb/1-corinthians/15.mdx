## The Resurrection of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> Now, brothers, I want to remind you of the gospel I preached to you, which you received, and in which you stand firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> By this gospel you are saved, if you hold firmly to the word I preached to you. Otherwise, you have believed in vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalms 16:10–11"> that He was buried, that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that He appeared to Cephas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Peter">a</a></sup>and then to the Twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> After that, He appeared to more than five hundred brothers at once, most of whom are still living, though some have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> Then He appeared to James, then to all the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> And last of all He appeared to me also, as to one of untimely birth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> For I am the least of the apostles and am unworthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> But by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace to me was not in vain. No, I worked harder than all of them—yet not I, but the grace of God that was with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> Whether, then, it was I or they, this is what we preach, and this is what you believed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Resurrection of the Dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> But if it is preached that Christ has been raised from the dead, how can some of you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> If there is no resurrection of the dead, then not even Christ has been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is worthless, and so is your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> In that case, we are also exposed as false witnesses about God. For we have testified about God that He raised Christ from the dead, but He did not raise Him if in fact the dead are not raised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if the dead are not raised, then not even Christ has been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is futile; you are still in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> Then those also who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> If our hope in Christ is for this life alone, we are to be pitied more than all men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Order of Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> But Christ has indeed been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> For since death came through a man, the resurrection of the dead comes also through a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> For as in Adam all die, so in Christ all will be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> But each in his own turn: Christ the firstfruits; then at His coming, those who belong to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> Then the end will come, when He hands over the kingdom to God the Father after He has destroyed all dominion, authority, and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> For He must reign until He has put all His enemies under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> The last enemy to be destroyed is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> For “God has put everything under His feet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 8:6">b</a></sup>Now when it says that everything has been put under Him, this clearly does not include the One who put everything under Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> And when all things have been subjected to Him, then the Son Himself will be made subject to Him who put all things under Him, so that God may be all in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> If these things are not so, what will those do who are baptized for the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why are people baptized for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> And why do we endanger ourselves every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> I face death every day, brothers, as surely as I boast about you in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> If I fought wild beasts in Ephesus for human motives, what did I gain? If the dead are not raised,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let us eat and drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for tomorrow we die.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaiah 22:13">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Do not be deceived: “Bad company corrupts good character.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Probably a quote from the Greek comedy Thais by Menander">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Sober up as you ought, and stop sinning; for some of you are ignorant of God. I say this to your shame.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Resurrection Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But someone will ask, “How are the dead raised? With what kind of body will they come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> You fool! What you sow does not come to life unless it dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And what you sow is not the body that will be, but just a seed, perhaps of wheat or something else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> But God gives it a body as He has designed, and to each kind of seed He gives its own body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> Not all flesh is the same: Men have one kind of flesh, animals have another, birds another, and fish another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> There are also heavenly bodies and earthly bodies. But the splendor of the heavenly bodies is of one degree, and the splendor of the earthly bodies is of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalms 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> The sun has one degree of splendor, the moon another, and the stars another; and star differs from star in splendor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> So will it be with the resurrection of the dead: What is sown is perishable; it is raised imperishable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> It is sown in dishonor; it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness; it is raised in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> It is sown a natural body; it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> So it is written: “The first man Adam became a living being”;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Genesis 2:7">e</a></sup>the last Adam a life-giving spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> The spiritual, however, was not first, but the natural, and then the spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man was of the dust of the earth, the second man from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> As was the earthly man, so also are those who are of the earth; and as is the heavenly man, so also are those who are of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> And just as we have borne the likeness of the earthly man, so also shall we bear the likeness of the heavenly man.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Where, O Death, Is Your Victory?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> Now I declare to you, brothers, that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God, nor does the perishable inherit the imperishable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Listen, I tell you a mystery: We will not all sleep, but we will all be changed—</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in an instant, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, the dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> For the perishable must be clothed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or clothe itself">f</a></sup>with the imperishable, and the mortal with immortality.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> When the perishable has been clothed with the imperishable and the mortal with immortality,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="WH does not include and the mortal with immortality.">g</a></sup>then the saying that is written will come to pass: “Death has been swallowed up in victory.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isaiah 25:8">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalms 89:48; Acts 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Where, O Death, is your victory?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Where, O Death, is your sting?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hosea 13:14 (see also LXX); BYZ and TR “Where, O Death, is your sting? Where, O Hades, is your victory?”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> Therefore, my beloved brothers, be steadfast and immovable. Always excel in the work of the Lord, because you know that your labor in the Lord is not in vain.</span>
</div>
</div>
