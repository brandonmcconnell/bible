## The Prayer of the Afflicted

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A prayer of one who is afflicted, when he grows faint and pours out his lament before the LORD.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear my prayer, O LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let my cry for help come before You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not hide Your face from me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in my day of distress.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Incline Your ear to me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">answer me quickly when I call.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my days vanish like smoke,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my bones burn like glowing embers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart is afflicted, and withered like grass;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I even forget to eat my bread.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through my loud groaning</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my skin hangs on my bones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am like a desert owl,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like an owl among the ruins.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> <span className="pl pl-1">I lie awake;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am like a lone bird on a housetop.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">All day long my enemies taunt me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they ridicule me and curse me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Micah 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have eaten ashes like bread</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and mixed my drink with tears</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">because of Your indignation and wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You have picked me up and cast me aside.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My days are like lengthening shadows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I wither away like grass.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But You, O LORD, sit enthroned forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your renown endures to all generations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will rise up and have compassion on Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for it is time to show her favor—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the appointed time has come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Your servants delight in her stones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take pity on her dust.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the nations will fear the name of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the kings of the earth will fear Your glory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the LORD will rebuild Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has appeared in His glory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will turn toward the prayer of the destitute;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will not despise their prayer.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let this be written for the generation to come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that a people not yet created may praise the LORD.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">For He looked down from the heights of His sanctuary;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the LORD gazed out from heaven to earth</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">to hear a prisoner’s groaning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to release those condemned to death,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">that they may proclaim the name of the LORD in Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and praise Him in Jerusalem,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">when peoples and kingdoms assemble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to serve the LORD.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has broken my strength on the way;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has cut short my days.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalms 102:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say: “O my God, do not take me in the midst of my days!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your years go on through all generations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the beginning You laid the foundations of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the heavens are the work of Your hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will perish, but You remain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will all wear out like a garment.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Like clothing You will change them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be passed on.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But You remain the same,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your years will never end.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 1:10–12">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:35–36; Psalms 45:16–17; Psalms 22:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The children of Your servants will dwell securely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their descendants will be established before You.”</span></span>
</div>
