## The Transgression of the Wicked

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">An oracle is in my heart</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">regarding the transgression of the wicked man:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is no fear of God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before his eyes.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Romans 3:18">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For his eyes are too full of conceit</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to detect or hate his own sin.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremiah 4:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The words of his mouth are wicked and deceitful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he has ceased to be wise and well-doing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even on his bed he plots wickedness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he sets himself on a path that is not good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he fails to reject evil.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your loving devotion, O LORD, reaches to the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your faithfulness to the clouds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your righteousness is like the highest mountains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your judgments are like the deepest sea.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O LORD, You preserve man and beast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">How precious is Your loving devotion, O God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the children of men take refuge</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the shadow of Your wings!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They feast on the abundance of Your house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and You give them drink from Your river of delights.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For with You is the fountain of life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in Your light we see light.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Extend Your loving devotion to those who know You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your righteousness to the upright in heart.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let not the foot of the proud come against me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor the hand of the wicked drive me away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the evildoers lie fallen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thrown down and unable to rise.</span></span>
