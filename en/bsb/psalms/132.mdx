<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A song of ascents.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Psalms 65:1; Psalms 119:106"> <span className="pl pl-1">how he swore an oath to the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Matthew 6:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will not enter my house</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or get into my bed,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:4; Ruth 3:18; Genesis 24:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not give sleep to my eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or slumber to my eyelids,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Acts 7:46–49; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I find a place for the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX for the God of Jacob; see Acts 7:46.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:41–42; Numbers 10:35–36; Psalms 68:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Arise, O LORD, to Your resting place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You and the ark of Your strength.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 29:14; Revelation 19:8; Psalms 104:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">May Your priests be clothed with righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your saints shout for joy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 84:9; 2 Chronicles 6:42; 1 Kings 11:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the sake of Your servant David,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not reject Your anointed one.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12; Acts 2:30; Psalms 89:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD swore an oath to David,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a promise He will not revoke:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“One of your descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will place on your throne.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “From the fruit of your body I will set (one) on your throne. <div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:68–69; Hebrews 12:22; Psalms 87:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the LORD has chosen Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has desired it for His home:</span></span>
