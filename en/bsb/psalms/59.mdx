## Deliver Me from My Enemies

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Miktam is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 16 and 56–60.">a</a></sup>of David, when Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalms 18:48; Psalms 57:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from my enemies, O my God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">protect me from those who rise against me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:19; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 55:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from workers of iniquity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and save me from men of bloodshed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">See how they lie in wait for me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Fierce men conspire against me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for no transgression or sin of my own, O LORD.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For no fault of my own,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they move swiftly to attack me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Arise to help me, and take notice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 84:8; Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rouse Yourself to punish all the nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">show no mercy to the wicked traitors.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They return in the evening, snarling like dogs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and prowling around the city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">See what they spew from their mouths—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sharp words from their lips:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“For who can hear us?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But You, O LORD, laugh at them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You scoff at all the nations.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will keep watch for You, O my strength,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because You, O God, are my fortress.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">My God of loving devotion will come to meet me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will let me stare down my foes.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not kill them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or my people will forget.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Scatter them by Your power,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bring them down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Lord, our shield.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the sins of their mouths</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the words of their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let them be trapped in their pride,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the curses and lies they utter.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Consume them in wrath;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">consume them till they are no more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so it may be known to the ends of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that God rules over Jacob.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 59:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They return in the evening,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">snarling like dogs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and prowling around the city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalms 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">They scavenge for food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and growl if they are not satisfied.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will sing of Your strength</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For You are my fortress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my refuge in times of trouble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 59:9–10; Psalms 46:1; Psalms 18:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">To You, O my strength, I sing praises,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You, O God, are my fortress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my God of loving devotion.</span></span>
</div>
