</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 34:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, let me not be ashamed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I have called on You.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the wicked be put to shame;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let them lie silent in Sheol.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">How great is Your goodness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which You have laid up for those who fear You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which You have bestowed before the sons of men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on those who take refuge in You!</span></span>
