## God’s Enemies Are Scattered

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A song.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">God arises. His enemies are scattered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who hate Him flee His presence.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to God!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Sing praises to His name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Exalt Him who rides on the clouds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or rides through the deserts">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His name is the LORD—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rejoice before Him.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your flock settled therein;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O God, from Your bounty You provided for the poor.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord gives the command;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a great company of women proclaim it:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Kings and their armies flee in haste;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she who waits at home divides the plunder.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A mountain of God is Mount Bashan;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a mountain of many peaks is Mount Bashan.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The chariots of God are tens of thousands—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thousands of thousands are they;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Lord is in His sanctuary</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as He was at Sinai.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or the Lord has come from Sinai in His holiness">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have ascended on high;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have led captives away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have received gifts from men,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Ephesians 4:8">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even from the rebellious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the LORD God may dwell there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who daily bears our burden,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the God of our salvation.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God is a God of deliverance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Lord GOD is our rescuer from death.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely God will crush the heads of His enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the hairy crowns of those who persist in guilty ways.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord said, “I will retrieve them from Bashan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will bring them up from the depths of the sea,</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have seen Your procession, O God—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the march of my God and King into the sanctuary.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The singers lead the way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the musicians follow after,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">among the maidens playing tambourines.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sing praises to the Lord—</span>Selah</span>
