## Save Me by Your Name

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Maskil is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.">a</a></sup>of David. When the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?”</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; 1 Samuel 26:1; Psalms 20:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save me, O God, by Your name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and vindicate me by Your might!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 130:2; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 13:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear my prayer, O God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">listen to the words of my mouth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:14; Psalms 36:1; Psalms 53:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For strangers rise up against me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ruthless men seek my life—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">men with no regard for God.</span>Selah</span>
