## Rescue Me from Evil Men

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:48; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 71:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue me, O LORD, from evil men.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Protect me from men of violence,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 36:4; Psalms 64:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">who devise evil in their hearts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and stir up war all day long.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Romans 3:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sharpen their tongues like snakes;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the venom of vipers is on their lips.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Romans 3:13">a</a></sup>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:11; Psalms 71:4; Psalms 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Guard me, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the hands of the wicked.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Keep me safe from men of violence</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who scheme to make me stumble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 18:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The proud hide a snare for me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the cords of their net are spread along the path,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lures are set out for me.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say to the LORD, “You are my God.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Hear, O LORD, my cry for help.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 62:7; Psalms 89:26; Psalms 18:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You shield my head in the day of battle.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:2–3; Deuteronomy 32:27; Psalms 27:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not promote their evil plans,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest they be exalted.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the heads of those who surround me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be covered in the trouble their lips have caused.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">May burning coals fall on them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may they be thrown into the fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">into the miry pits, never to rise again.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">May no slanderer be established in the land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may calamity hunt down the man of violence.</span></span>
