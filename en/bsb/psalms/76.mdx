## God’s Name Is Great in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A song.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Daniel 4:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is known in Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His name is great in Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:18; Isaiah 12:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">His tent is in Salem,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Jerusalem">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His dwelling place in Zion.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">There He shattered the flaming arrows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the shield and sword and weapons of war.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1–4; Daniel 7:17–28; Ezekiel 19:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are resplendent with light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">more majestic than mountains filled with game.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The valiant lie plundered; they sleep their last sleep.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">No men of might could lift a hand.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Psalms 2:12; Psalms 89:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You alone are to be feared.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When You are angry, who can stand before You?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:29–30; Habakkuk 2:20; Judges 5:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">From heaven You pronounced judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the earth feared and was still</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Zephaniah 2:3; Isaiah 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">when God rose up to judge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to save all the lowly of the earth.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Matthew 24:22; Daniel 3:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even the wrath of man shall praise You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the survivors of wrath You will clothe Yourself.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Surely Your wrath against men brings You praise, and the survivors of Your wrath will be restrained.">b</a></sup></span>
