## The Hurtful Tongue

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O God, my voice of complaint;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">preserve my life from dread of the enemy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hide me from the scheming of the wicked,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the mob of workers of iniquity,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">who sharpen their tongues like swords</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and aim their bitter words like arrows,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">ambushing the innocent in seclusion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shooting suddenly, without fear.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They hold fast to their evil purpose;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they speak of hiding their snares.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Who will see them?” they say.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They devise injustice and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“We have perfected a secret plan.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For the inner man and the heart are mysterious.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God will shoot them with arrows;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">suddenly they will be wounded.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will be made to stumble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their own tongues turned against them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All who see will shake their heads.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then all mankind will fear</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and proclaim the work of God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so they will ponder what He has done.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take refuge in Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let all the upright in heart exult.</span></span>
</div>
