<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A song. A Psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Psalms 35:22; Psalms 50:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, be not silent; be not speechless;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or deaf">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be not still, O God.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">who perished at Endor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and became like dung on the ground.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">who said, “Let us possess for ourselves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the pastures of God.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make them like tumbleweed, O my God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like chaff before the wind.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">As fire consumes a forest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a flame sets the mountains ablaze,</span></span>
