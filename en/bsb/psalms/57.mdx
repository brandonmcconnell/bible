## In You My Soul Takes Refuge

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Miktam is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 16 and 56–60.">a</a></sup>of David, when he fled from Saul into the cave.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Psalms 36:7; Psalms 17:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for in You my soul takes refuge.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the shadow of Your wings I will take shelter</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until the danger has passed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Philippians 1:6; Hebrews 13:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cry out to God Most High,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Elohim-Elyon">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to God who fulfills His purpose for me.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3; Psalms 72:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be exalted, O God, above the heavens;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may Your glory cover all the earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 145:14; Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 28:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They spread a net for my feet;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my soul was despondent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They dug a pit before me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they themselves have fallen into it!</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:7; Ephesians 5:20; Psalms 34:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will sing and make music.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 108:1–3; Psalms 30:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, my glory!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or Awake, my soul!">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Awake, O harp and lyre!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will awaken the dawn.</span></span>
</div>
