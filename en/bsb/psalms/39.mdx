## I Will Watch My Ways

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. For Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “I will watch my ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that I will not sin with my tongue;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will guard my mouth with a muzzle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as long as the wicked are present.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was speechless and still;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I remained silent, even from speaking good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my sorrow was stirred.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart grew hot within me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as I mused, the fire burned.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then I spoke with my tongue:</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Show me, O LORD, my end</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the measure of my days.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let me know how fleeting my life is.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">You, indeed, have made my days as handbreadths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my lifetime as nothing before You.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Truly each man at his best</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">exists as but a breath.</span>Selah</span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> <span className="pl pl-1">And now, O Lord, for what do I wait?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My hope is in You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from all my transgressions;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not make me the reproach of fools.</span></span>
