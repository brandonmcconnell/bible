## The Song of the Slandered

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">May it be like a robe wrapped about him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a belt tied forever around him.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But You, O GOD, the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">deal kindly with me for the sake of Your name;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">deliver me by the goodness of Your loving devotion.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am fading away like a lengthening shadow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am shaken off like a locust.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am an object of scorn to my accusers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they see me, they shake their heads.</span></span>
