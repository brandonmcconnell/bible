## In the Day of Trouble I Sought the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. According to Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cried out to God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I cried aloud to God to hear me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the day of trouble I sought the Lord;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">through the night my outstretched hands did not grow weary;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my soul refused to be comforted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I remembered You, O God, and I groaned;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I mused and my spirit grew faint.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have kept my eyes from closing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am too troubled to speak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I considered the days of old,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the years long in the past.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">At night I remembered my song;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in my heart I mused, and my spirit pondered:</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Will the Lord spurn us forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and never show His favor again?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is His loving devotion gone forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Has His promise failed for all time?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has God forgotten to be gracious?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Has His anger shut off His compassion?”</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I said, “I am grieved</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the right hand of the Most High has changed.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “To this I will appeal: to the years of the right hand of the Most High.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will remember the works of the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yes, I will remember Your wonders of old.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will reflect on all You have done</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ponder Your mighty deeds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your way, O God, is holy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What god is so great as our God?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the God who works wonders;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You display Your strength among the peoples.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">With power You redeemed Your people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the sons of Jacob and Joseph.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters saw You, O God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the waters saw You and swirled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even the depths were shaken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The clouds poured down water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the skies resounded with thunder;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your arrows flashed back and forth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your thunder resounded in the whirlwind;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the lightning lit up the world;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the earth trembled and quaked.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your path led through the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your way through the mighty waters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but Your footprints were not to be found.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or were unknown">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You led Your people like a flock</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by the hand of Moses and Aaron.</span></span>
</div>
