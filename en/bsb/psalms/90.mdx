<div className="bt">

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You return man to dust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">saying, “Return, O sons of mortals.”</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For we are consumed by Your anger</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and terrified by Your wrath.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have set our iniquities before You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">our secret sins in the light of Your presence.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
