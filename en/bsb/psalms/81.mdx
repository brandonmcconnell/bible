## Sing for Joy to God Our Strength

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. According to Gittith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gittith is probably a musical or liturgical term; here and in Psalms 8 and 84.">a</a></sup>Of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:7; Psalms 66:1; Psalms 100:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing for joy to God our strength;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">make a joyful noise to the God of Jacob.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 149:1–3; Psalms 92:3; Colossians 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lift up a song, strike the tambourine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">play the sweet-sounding harp and lyre.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 10:1–10; Numbers 15:3; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sound the ram’s horn at the New Moon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and at the full moon on the day of our Feast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this is a statute for Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an ordinance of the God of Jacob.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 114:1; Deuteronomy 28:49; 1 Corinthians 14:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">He ordained it as a testimony for Joseph</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or in Joseph">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when he went out over the land of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where I heard an unfamiliar language:</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 10:27; Exodus 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I relieved his shoulder of the burden;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his hands were freed from the basket.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 19:19; Numbers 20:13; Exodus 2:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You called out in distress, and I rescued you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I answered you from the cloud of thunder;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I tested you at the waters of Meribah.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Meribah means quarreling; see Exodus 17:7.">c</a></sup>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:7; John 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O My people, and I will warn you:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Israel, if only you would listen to Me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; Deuteronomy 32:12; Deuteronomy 6:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">There must be no strange god among you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor shall you bow to a foreign god.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am the LORD your God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who brought you up out of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Open wide your mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will fill it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 2:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But My people would not listen to Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Israel would not obey Me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I gave them up to their stubborn hearts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to follow their own devices.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 5:29; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">If only My people would listen to Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if Israel would follow My ways,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 2:20–23; Numbers 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">how soon I would subdue their enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and turn My hand against their foes!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 1:30; Exodus 20:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who hate the LORD would feign obedience,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their doom would last forever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 147:14; 1 Samuel 14:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I would feed you the finest wheat;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.”</span></span>
