## The Evanescence of Wealth

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should I fear in times of trouble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when wicked usurpers surround me?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They trust in their wealth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and boast in their great riches.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">No man can possibly redeem his brother</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or redeem another">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or pay his ransom to God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the redemption of his soul is costly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and never can payment suffice,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">that he should live on forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and not see decay.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For it is clear that wise men die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the foolish and the senseless both perish</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and leave their wealth to others.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God will redeem my life from Sheol,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for He will surely take me to Himself.</span>Selah</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though in his lifetime he blesses his soul—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and men praise you when you prosper—</span></span>
