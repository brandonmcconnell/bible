## I Waited Patiently for the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I waited patiently for the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He inclined to me and heard my cry.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 119:133; Psalms 27:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He lifted me up from the pit of despair,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">out of the miry clay;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He set my feet upon a rock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and made my footsteps firm.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who has made the LORD his trust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who has not turned to the proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor to those who lapse into falsehood.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or who turn aside to false gods or who run after lies">a</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sacrifice and offering You did not desire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but my ears You have opened.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; some LXX manuscripts but a body You prepared for me">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Burnt offerings and sin offerings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You did not require.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, “Here I am, I have come—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is written about me in the scroll:</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I proclaim righteousness in the great assembly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">behold, I do not seal my lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as You, O LORD, do know.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">May those who seek my life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be ashamed and confounded;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may those who wish me harm</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be repelled and humiliated.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">May those who say to me, “Aha, aha!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be appalled at their own shame.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
