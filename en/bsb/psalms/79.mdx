## A Prayer for Deliverance

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations, O God, have invaded Your inheritance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have defiled Your holy temple</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and reduced Jerusalem to rubble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have given the corpses of Your servants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as food to the birds of the air,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the flesh of Your saints to the beasts of the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have poured out their blood like water</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all around Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there is no one to bury the dead.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have become a reproach to our neighbors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a scorn and derision to those around us.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O LORD?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will You be angry forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will Your jealousy burn like fire?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out Your wrath on the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that do not acknowledge You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the kingdoms</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that refuse to call on Your name,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">for they have devoured Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and devastated his homeland.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not hold past sins against us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let Your compassion come quickly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for we are brought low.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, O God of our salvation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the glory of Your name;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">deliver us and atone for our sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the sake of Your name.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should the nations ask,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Where is their God?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Before our eyes, make known among the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your vengeance for the bloodshed of Your servants.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the groans of the captives reach You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by the strength of Your arm preserve those condemned to death.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pay back into the laps of our neighbors</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sevenfold the reproach they hurled at You, O Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then we Your people, the sheep of Your pasture,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will thank You forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from generation to generation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will declare Your praise.</span></span>
</div>
