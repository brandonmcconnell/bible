## You Turned My Mourning into Dancing

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm. A song for the dedication of the temple. Of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will exalt You, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You have lifted me up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have not allowed my foes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to rejoice over me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD my God, I cried to You for help,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and You healed me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, You pulled me up from Sheol;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You spared me from descending into the Pit.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to the LORD, O you His saints,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and praise His holy name.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or and praise the memorial of His holiness; see Exodus 3:15.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For His anger is fleeting,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but His favor lasts a lifetime.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Weeping may stay the night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but joy comes in the morning.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> <span className="pl pl-1">In prosperity I said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I will never be shaken.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, You favored me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You made my mountain stand strong.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When You hid Your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was dismayed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">To You, O LORD, I called,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I begged my Lord for mercy:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">“What gain is there in my bloodshed,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or in my destruction">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in my descent to the Pit?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or to corruption">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will the dust praise You?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will it proclaim Your faithfulness?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear me, O LORD, and have mercy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O LORD, be my helper.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You turned my mourning into dancing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You peeled off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">that my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O LORD my God, I will give thanks forever.</span></span>
