<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">All their evil appears at Gilgal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for there I hated them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will drive them from My house</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the wickedness of their deeds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will no longer love them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all their leaders are rebellious.</span></span>
