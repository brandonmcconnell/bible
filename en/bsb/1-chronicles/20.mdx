## The Capture of Rabbah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 2 Samuel 12:26–31; Amos 1:14"> In the spring,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally At the turn of the year">a</a></sup>at the time when kings march out to war, Joab led out the army and ravaged the land of the Ammonites. He came to Rabbah and besieged it, but David remained in Jerusalem. And Joab attacked Rabbah and demolished it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> Then David took the crown from the head of their king.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or from the head of Milcom. Milcom, also called Molech, was god of the Ammonites; see Leviticus 18:21 and 1 Kings 11:7.">b</a></sup>It was found to weigh a talent of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A talent is approximately 75.4 pounds or 34.2 kilograms of gold.">c</a></sup>and was set with precious stones, and it was placed on David’s head. And David took a great amount of plunder from the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:2–5; 1 Kings 9:21; Judges 8:6–7"> David brought out the people who were there and put them to work<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or cut them">d</a></sup>with saws, iron picks, and axes. And he did the same to all the Ammonite cities. Then David and all his troops returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Battles against the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> Some time later, war broke out with the Philistines at Gezer. At that time Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Sippai is a variant of Saph; see 2 Samuel 21:18.">e</a></sup>a descendant of the Rephaim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or the giants; see also descendants of Rapha in verses 6 and 8.">f</a></sup>and the Philistines were subdued.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> Once again there was a battle with the Philistines, and Elhanan son of Jair killed Lahmi the brother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or Elhanan son of Jair the Bethlehemite killed the brother; see 2 Samuel 21:19.">g</a></sup>of Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> And there was also a battle at Gath, where there was a man of great stature with six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He too was descended from Rapha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 16:9; Isaiah 37:23"> and when he taunted Israel, Jonathan the son of David’s brother Shimei killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 14:12; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> So these descendants of Rapha in Gath fell at the hands of David and his servants.</span>
</div>
</div>
