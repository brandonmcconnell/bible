## David Fetches the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:20; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 2 Chronicles 29:20"> Then David conferred with all his leaders, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 4:4–20; 1 Samuel 31:1; 1 Chronicles 10:7"> And he said to the whole assembly of Israel, “If it seems good to you, and if this is of the LORD our God, let us send word far and wide to the rest of our brothers in all the land of Israel, and also to the priests and Levites in their cities and pasturelands, so that they may join us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> Then let us bring back the ark of our God, for we did not inquire of Him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or of it">a</a></sup>in the days of Saul.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 8:5; 2 Samuel 3:36; 1 Samuel 18:20"> And because this proposal seemed right to all the people, the whole assembly agreed to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> So David assembled all Israel, from the River Shihor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew from the Shihor">b</a></sup>in Egypt to Lebo-hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> David and all Israel went up to Baalah of Judah (that is, Kiriath-jearim) to bring up from there the ark of God the LORD, who is enthroned between the cherubim—the ark that is called by the Name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or the ark of God, which is called by the Name of the LORD who is enthroned between the cherubim; or the ark of God, the LORD, who is enthroned between the cherubim, where His Name is called">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> So they carried the ark of God from the house of Abinadab on a new cart, with Uzzah and Ahio guiding the cart.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Uzzah Touches the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–5; Psalms 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> David and all the Israelites were celebrating before God with all their might, with songs and on harps and lyres, with tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> When they came to the threshing floor of Chidon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Chidon is a variant of Nacon; see 2 Samuel 6:6.">d</a></sup>Uzzah reached out and took hold of the ark, because the oxen had stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> And the anger of the LORD burned against Uzzah, and He struck him down because he had put his hand on the ark. So he died there before God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> Then David became angry because the LORD had burst forth against Uzzah. So he named that place Perez-uzzah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Perez-uzzah means outbreak against Uzzah.">e</a></sup>as it is called to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> That day David feared God and asked, “How can I ever bring the ark of God to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> So he did not move the ark with him to the City of David; instead, he took it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Thus the ark of God remained with the family of Obed-edom in his house for three months, and the LORD blessed his household and everything he owned.</span>
</div>
</div>
