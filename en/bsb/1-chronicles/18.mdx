## David’s Triumphs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–18; 1 Samuel 5:8; 2 Samuel 1:20"> Some time later, David defeated the Philistines, subdued them, and took Gath and its villages from the hand of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4–5; 1 Kings 10:25; Isaiah 16:1"> David also defeated the Moabites, and they became subject to David and brought him tribute.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:3; Genesis 15:18"> As far as Hamath, David also defeated King Hadadezer of Zobah, who had marched out to establish his dominion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew his hand">a</a></sup>along the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:4; 1 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 4:2"> David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand charioteers, and twenty thousand foot soldiers, and he hamstrung all the horses except a hundred he kept for the chariots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:6; 2 Samuel 8:5–6; 1 Samuel 14:47"> When the Arameans of Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand of their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:8; Psalms 121:8; Proverbs 21:31"> Then he placed garrisons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX and Vulgate (see also 2 Samuel 8:6 and Syriac); Hebrew does not include garrisons.">b</a></sup>in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became subject to David and brought him tribute. So the LORD made David victorious<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or the LORD saved David; also in verse 13">c</a></sup>wherever he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And David took the gold shields that belonged to the officers of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:8; 2 Chronicles 4:12–18; 1 Kings 7:15–47"> And from Tibhath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Tibhath is a variant of Tebah; see 2 Samuel 8:8 LXX.">d</a></sup>and Cun, cities of Hadadezer, David took a large amount of bronze, with which Solomon made the bronze Sea, the pillars, and various bronze articles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:9"> When King Tou<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Tou is a variant of Toi; also in verse 10; see 2 Samuel 8:9.">e</a></sup>of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> he sent his son Hadoram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hadoram is a variant of Joram; see 2 Samuel 8:10.">f</a></sup>to greet King David and bless him for fighting and defeating Hadadezer, who had been at war with Tou. Hadoram brought all kinds of articles of gold and silver and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Chronicles 26:26–27; 1 Kings 7:51"> and King David dedicated these to the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had carried off from all these nations—from Edom and Moab, and from the Ammonites, Philistines, and Amalekites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:11; 2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 8:13–14"> Moreover, Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalms 144:10"> He placed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites were subject to David. So the LORD made David victorious wherever he went.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Officers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> Thus David reigned over all Israel and administered justice and righteousness for all his people:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 4:3; 2 Samuel 8:16"> Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army;<br />Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 4:3"> Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts, Vulgate, and Syriac (see also 2 Samuel 8:17); most Hebrew manuscripts Abimelech">g</a></sup>son of Abiathar were priests;<br />Shavsha<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Shavsha is also called Seraiah, Sheva, and Shisha; see 2 Samuel 8:17, 2 Samuel 20:25, and 1 Kings 4:3.">h</a></sup>was the scribe;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 15:18; 1 Kings 2:34–35"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites;<br />and David’s sons were chief officials at the king’s side.</span>
</div>
</div>
