## The Divisions of the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> When David was old and full of years, he installed his son Solomon as king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> Then he gathered all the leaders of Israel, as well as the priests and Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> The Levites thirty years of age or older were counted, and the total number of men was 38,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> “Of these,” said David,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew does not include said David,">a</a></sup>“24,000 are to oversee the work of the house of the LORD, 6,000 are to be officers and judges,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> 4,000 are to be gatekeepers, and 4,000 are to praise the LORD with the instruments I have made for giving praise.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> Then David divided the Levites into divisions according to the sons of Levi:<br />Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Gershonites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> The Gershonites: Ladan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ladan is a variant of Libni; also in verses 8 and 9; see 1 Chronicles 6:17.">b</a></sup>and Shimei.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sons of Ladan: Jehiel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jehiel is a variant of Jehieli; see 1 Chronicles 26:21.">c</a></sup>the first, Zetham, and Joel—three in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran—three in all. These were the heads of the families of Ladan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the sons of Shimei:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Shimei was possibly a son or grandson of the Shimei listed in verse 9.">d</a></sup>Jahath, Zina,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Most Hebrew manuscripts; one Hebrew manuscript, LXX, and Vulgate (see also verse 11) Zizah">e</a></sup>Jeush, and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei—four in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Jahath was the first and Zizah was the second; but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as one family and received a single assignment.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Kohathites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron and his descendants were set apart forever to consecrate the most holy things, to burn incense before the LORD, to minister before Him, and to pronounce blessings in His name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> As for Moses the man of God, his sons were named among the tribe of Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> The descendants of Gershom: Shebuel was the first.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> The descendants of Eliezer: Rehabiah was the first. Eliezer did not have any other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very numerous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> The sons of Izhar: Shelomith was the first.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriah was the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Uzziel: Micah was the first and Isshiah the second.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Merarites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi.<br />The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> Eleazar died without having any sons; he had only daughters. Their cousins, the sons of Kish, married them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jeremoth is a variant of Jerimoth; see 1 Chronicles 24:30.">f</a></sup>—three in all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Levite Duties Revised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the descendants of Levi by their families—the heads of families, registered individually by name—those twenty years of age or older who worked in the service of the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David had said, “The LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to His people and has come to dwell in Jerusalem forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> So now the Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle or any of the articles for its service.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> For according to the final instructions of David, the Levites twenty years of age or older were counted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> but their duty was to assist the descendants of Aaron with the service of the house of the LORD, being responsible for the courts and chambers, the purification of all the holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> as well as for the rows of the showbread, the fine flour for the grain offering, the wafers of unleavened bread, the baking, the mixing, and all measurements of quantity and size.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> They were also to stand every morning to give thanks and praise to the LORD, and likewise in the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> Whenever burnt offerings were presented to the LORD on the Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts, they were to serve regularly before the LORD in the numbers prescribed for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> So the Levites were to carry out the responsibilities for the Tent of Meeting and the Holy Place, and, under their brothers the descendants of Aaron, the service of the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
