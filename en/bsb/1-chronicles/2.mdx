<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:3; Genesis 38:13–30; Nehemiah 11:4"> Tamar, Judah’s daughter-in-law, bore to him Perez and Zerah. Judah had five sons in all.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Deuteronomy 7:26; Joshua 7:25"> The son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew sons">c</a></sup>of Carmi:<br />Achar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Achar means troubler; also called Achan in Joshua 7 and Joshua 22.">d</a></sup>who brought trouble upon Israel by violating the ban on devoted things.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The son of Ethan:<br />Azariah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:42; Matthew 1:3; Ruth 4:19"> The sons who were born to Hezron:<br />Jerahmeel, Ram, and Caleb.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew Chelubai, a variant of Caleb; see verse 18.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 1:4; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 2:3"> Ram was the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab was the father of Nahshon, a leader of the descendants of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Matthew 1:4–5; Luke 3:32"> Nahshon was the father of Salmon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX (see also Ruth 4:20–21); Hebrew Salma; twice in this verse">f</a></sup>and Salmon was the father of Boaz.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:12; 1 Chronicles 10:14; Isaiah 11:10"> Boaz was the father of Obed, and Obed was the father of Jesse.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Samuel 17:28; 1 Chronicles 20:7"> Jesse was the father of Eliab his firstborn; Abinadab was born second, Shimea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Shimea is a variant of Shammah, Shimeah, and Shimei; see 1 Samuel 16:9, 2 Samuel 13:3, and 2 Samuel 21:21.">g</a></sup>third,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nethanel fourth, Raddai fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:10–11; 1 Samuel 17:12–14"> Ozem sixth, and David seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:6; 2 Samuel 3:39; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. And the three sons of Zeruiah were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Kings 2:32; 2 Samuel 20:4–12"> Abigail was the mother of Amasa, whose father was Jether<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jether is a variant of Ithra; see 2 Samuel 17:25.">h</a></sup>the Ishmaelite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth. These were the sons of Azubah: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:50; 1 Chronicles 2:24; 1 Chronicles 4:4"> When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ephrath is a variant of Ephrathah; see verse 50.">i</a></sup>who bore to him Hur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 36:1–2; Exodus 37:1"> Hur was the father of Uri, and Uri was the father of Bezalel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Deuteronomy 3:15; Numbers 26:29"> Later, Hezron slept with the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead. He had married her when he was sixty years old, and she bore to him Segub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 13:30; Deuteronomy 3:14; Numbers 32:41"> Segub was the father of Jair, who had twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead.</span>

All these were descendants of Machir the father of Gilead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:5; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Samuel 30:14"> After Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah, his wife Abijah bore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or After Hezron died, Caleb had relations with Ephrathah, the wife of Hezron his father, and she bore; see also LXX.">k</a></sup>to him Ashhur the father<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or the founder; also in verses 42, 45, 49, and possibly elsewhere">l</a></sup>of Tekoa.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron:<br />Ram his firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Jerahmeel had another wife named Atarah, who was the mother of Onam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerahmeel:<br />Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The sons of Onam:<br />Shammai and Jada.<br />The sons of Shammai:<br />Nadab and Abishur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Abishur’s wife was named Abihail, and she bore to him Ahban and Molid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The sons of Nadab:<br />Seled and Appaim. Seled died without children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:34–35"> The son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew sons; three times in this verse">m</a></sup>of Appaim:<br />Ishi.<br />The son of Ishi:<br />Sheshan.<br />The son of Sheshan:<br />Ahlai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The sons of Jada the brother of Shammai:<br />Jether and Jonathan. Jether died without children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> The sons of Jonathan:<br />Peleth and Zaza.<br />These were the descendants of Jerahmeel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Sheshan had no sons, but only daughters. He also had an Egyptian servant named Jarha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Sheshan gave his daughter in marriage to his servant Jarha, and she bore to him Attai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:41"> Attai was the father of Nathan, Nathan was the father of Zabad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Zabad was the father of Ephlal, Ephlal was the father of Obed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Obed was the father of Jehu, Jehu was the father of Azariah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Azariah was the father of Helez, Helez was the father of Elasah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Elasah was the father of Sismai, Sismai was the father of Shallum,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Shallum was the father of Jekamiah, and Jekamiah was the father of Elishama.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23–24; Hebrews 12:23; Romans 8:29"> The sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel:<br />Mesha<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX Mareshah">n</a></sup>his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph, and Mareshah his second son, who was the father of Hebron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> The sons of Hebron:<br />Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Shema was the father of Raham the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem was the father of Shammai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Joshua 15:58"> The son of Shammai was Maon, and Maon was the father of Beth-zur.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:18–19; 1 Chronicles 2:48"> Caleb’s concubine Ephah was the mother of Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran was the father of Gazez.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> The sons of Jahdai:<br />Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 25:5–6; 1 Chronicles 2:46"> Caleb’s concubine Maacah was the mother of Sheber and Tirhanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Joshua 15:31; 1 Chronicles 2:42; Isaiah 10:31"> She was also the mother of Shaaph father of Madmannah, and of Sheva father of Machbenah and Gibea. Caleb’s daughter was Acsah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1; Joshua 15:60; 1 Chronicles 2:53"> These were the descendants of Caleb.<br />The sons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="LXX and Vulgate; Hebrew son">o</a></sup>of Hur the firstborn of Ephrathah:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Ephrathah is a variant of Ephrath; see verse 19.">p</a></sup><br />Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:4; John 7:42; Matthew 2:6"> Salma the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph the father of Beth-gader.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:2"> These were the descendants of Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim:<br />Haroeh, half the Manahathites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:38; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> and the clans of Kiriath-jearim—the Ithrites, Puthites, Shumathites, and Mishraites. From these descended the Zorathites and Eshtaolites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:26; Ezra 2:22; Nehemiah 12:28"> The descendants of Salma:<br />Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half the Manahathites, the Zorites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Kings 10:15; Judges 1:16; Jeremiah 35:19"> and the clans of the scribes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or of the Sopherites">q</a></sup>who lived at Jabez—the Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Sucathites.
