## David and Mephibosheth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:42; Mark 9:41; Matthew 25:40"> Then David asked, “Is there anyone left from the house of Saul to whom I can show kindness for the sake of Jonathan?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 19:17; 2 Samuel 19:27–29"> And there was a servant of the house of Saul named Ziba. They summoned him to David, and the king inquired, “Are you Ziba?”<br />“I am your servant,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:4; Titus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> So the king asked, “Is there anyone left of the house of Saul to whom I can show the kindness of God?”<br />Ziba answered, “There is still Jonathan’s son, who is lame in both feet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27–29"> “Where is he?” replied the king.<br />And Ziba said, “Indeed, he is in Lo-debar at the house of Machir son of Ammiel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So King David had him brought from the house of Machir son of Ammiel in Lo-debar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:4; 1 Chronicles 9:40; 1 Chronicles 8:34"> And when Mephibosheth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mephibosheth is also called Merib-baal; see 1 Chronicles 8:34.">a</a></sup>son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, came to David, he fell facedown in reverence.<br />Then David said, “Mephibosheth!”<br />“I am your servant,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:1; 1 Kings 2:7; 2 Samuel 19:28"> “Do not be afraid,” said David, “for surely I will show you kindness for the sake of your father Jonathan. I will restore to you all the land of your grandfather Saul, and you will always eat at my table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:9; Matthew 15:26–27; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> Mephibosheth bowed down and said, “What is your servant, that you should show regard for a dead dog like me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:29; 2 Samuel 16:4; Isaiah 32:8"> Then the king summoned Saul’s servant Ziba and said to him, “I have given to your master’s grandson all that belonged to Saul and to all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 19:28; 2 Kings 25:29"> You and your sons and servants are to work the ground for him and bring in the harvest, so that your master’s grandson may have food to eat. But Mephibosheth, your master’s grandson, is always to eat at my table.”<br />Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 19:26; 2 Samuel 19:17"> And Ziba said to the king, “Your servant will do all that my lord the king has commanded.”<br />So Mephibosheth ate at David’s table<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew my table">b</a></sup>like one of the king’s own sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:40–44; 1 Chronicles 8:8; 1 Chronicles 8:34–40"> And Mephibosheth had a young son named Mica,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Mica is a variant of Micah; see 1 Chronicles 8:34.">c</a></sup>and all who dwelt in the house of Ziba were servants of Mephibosheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:10–11"> So Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, because he always ate at the king’s table, and he was lame in both feet.</span>
</div>
</div>
