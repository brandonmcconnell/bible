## God’s Covenant with David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; 1 Kings 5:4"> After the king had settled into his palace and the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies around him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> he said to Nathan the prophet, “Here I am, living in a house of cedar, while the ark of God remains in a tent.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> And Nathan replied to the king, “Go and do all that is in your heart, for the LORD is with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> But that night the word of the LORD came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> “Go and tell My servant David that this is what the LORD says: Are you the one to build for Me a house to dwell in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought the Israelites up out of Egypt until this day, but I have moved about with a tent as My dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> In all My journeys with all the Israelites, have I ever asked any of the leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or tribes">a</a></sup>I appointed to shepherd My people Israel, ‘Why haven’t you built Me a house of cedar?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> Now then, you are to tell My servant David that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: I took you from the pasture, from following the flock, to be the ruler over My people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies from before you. Now I will make for you a name like that of the greatest in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> And I will provide a place for My people Israel and will plant them so that they may dwell in a place of their own and be disturbed no more. No longer will the sons of wickedness oppress them as they did at the beginning</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> and have done since the day I appointed judges over My people Israel. I will give you rest from all your enemies.<br />The LORD declares to you that He Himself will establish a house for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> And when your days are fulfilled and you rest with your fathers, I will raise up your descendant after you, who will come from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He will build a house for My Name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be his Father, and he will be My son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 1:5">b</a></sup>When he does wrong, I will discipline him with the rod of men and with the blows of the sons of men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> But My loving devotion will never be removed from him as I removed it from Saul, whom I removed from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Your house and kingdom will endure forever before Me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts and LXX; most Hebrew manuscripts you">c</a></sup>and your throne will be established forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> So Nathan relayed to David all the words of this entire revelation.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Prayer of Thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Then King David went in, sat before the LORD, and said, “Who am I, O Lord GOD, and what is my house, that You have brought me this far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> And as if this was a small thing in Your eyes, O Lord GOD, You have also spoken about the future of the house of Your servant. Is this Your custom with man, O Lord GOD?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or And this is Your instruction for mankind, O Lord GOD.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> What more can David say to You? For You know Your servant, O Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> For the sake of Your word and according to Your own heart, You have accomplished this great thing and revealed it to Your servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> How great You are, O Lord GOD! For there is none like You, and there is no God but You, according to everything we have heard with our own ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> And who is like Your people Israel—the one nation on earth whom God went out to redeem as a people for Himself and to make a name for Himself? You performed great and awesome wonders by driving out nations and their gods from before Your people, whom You redeemed for Yourself from Egypt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See LXX and 1 Chronicles 17:21; Hebrew You performed for Yourself great and awesome wonders for Your land, before Your people, whom You have redeemed for Yourself from Egypt, from nations and their gods.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> For You have established Your people Israel as Your very own forever, and You, O LORD, have become their God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> And now, O LORD God, confirm forever the word You have spoken concerning Your servant and his house. Do as You have promised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> so that Your name will be magnified forever when it is said, ‘The LORD of Hosts is God over Israel.’ And the house of Your servant David will be established before You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> For You, O LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, have revealed this to Your servant when You said, ‘I will build a house for you.’ Therefore Your servant has found the courage to offer this prayer to You.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> And now, O Lord GOD, You are God! Your words are true, and You have promised this goodness to Your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> Now therefore, may it please You to bless the house of Your servant, that it may continue forever before You. For You, O Lord GOD, have spoken, and with Your blessing the house of Your servant will be blessed forever.”</span>
</div>
</div>
