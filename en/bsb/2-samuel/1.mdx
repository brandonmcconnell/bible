## Saul’s Death Reported to David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> After the death of Saul, David returned from the slaughter of the Amalekites and stayed in Ziklag two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> On the third day a man with torn clothes and dust on his head arrived from Saul’s camp. When he came to David, he fell to the ground to pay him homage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> “Where have you come from?” David asked.<br />“I have escaped from the Israelite camp,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> “What was the outcome?” David asked. “Please tell me.”<br />“The troops fled from the battle,” he replied. “Many of them fell and died. And Saul and his son Jonathan are also dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> Then David asked the young man who had brought him the report, “How do you know that Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> “I happened to be on Mount Gilboa,” he replied, “and there was Saul, leaning on his spear, with the chariots and the cavalry closing in on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> When he turned around and saw me, he called out to me, and I answered, ‘Here I am!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> ‘Who are you?’ he asked.<br />So I told him, ‘I am an Amalekite.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then he begged me, ‘Stand over me and kill me, for agony has seized me, but my life still lingers.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I stood over him and killed him, because I knew that after he had fallen he could not survive. And I took the crown that was on his head and the band that was on his arm, and I have brought them here to my lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> Then David took hold of his own clothes and tore them, and all the men who were with him did the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> They mourned and wept and fasted until evening for Saul and his son Jonathan, and for the people of the LORD and the house of Israel, because they had fallen by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> And David inquired of the young man who had brought him the report, “Where are you from?”<br />“I am the son of a foreigner,” he answered. “I am an Amalekite.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> So David asked him, “Why were you not afraid to lift your hand to destroy the LORD’s anointed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> Then David summoned one of the young men and said, “Go, execute him!” So the young man struck him down, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> For David had said to the Amalekite, “Your blood be on your own head because your own mouth has testified against you, saying, ‘I killed the LORD’s anointed.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Song for Saul and Jonathan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the blood of the slain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the fat of the mighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the bow of Jonathan did not retreat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the sword of Saul did not return empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Saul and Jonathan, beloved and delightful in life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">were not divided in death.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were swifter than eagles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they were stronger than lions.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">O daughters of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">weep for Saul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who clothed you in scarlet and luxury,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who decked your garments with ornaments of gold.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen in the thick of battle!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Jonathan lies slain on your heights.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I grieve for you, Jonathan, my brother.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You were delightful to me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your love to me was extraordinary,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">surpassing the love of women.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the weapons of war have perished!”</span></span>
