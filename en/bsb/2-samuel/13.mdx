## Amnon and Tamar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; 2 Samuel 3:2–3; 2 Samuel 13:15"> After some time, David’s son Amnon fell in love with Tamar, the beautiful sister of David’s son Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; 1 Kings 21:4; Song 5:8"> Amnon was sick with frustration over his sister Tamar, for she was a virgin, and it seemed implausible for him to do anything to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19; James 3:15"> Now Amnon had a friend named Jonadab, the son of David’s brother Shimeah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shimeah is a variant of Shammah, Shimea, and Shimei; see 1 Samuel 16:9, 2 Samuel 21:21, and 1 Chronicles 2:13.">a</a></sup>Jonadab was a very shrewd man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> and he asked Amnon, “Why are you, the son of the king, so depressed morning after morning? Won’t you tell me?”<br />Amnon replied, “I am in love with Tamar, my brother Absalom’s sister.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 6:24–25; Proverbs 19:27; Acts 23:15"> Jonadab told him, “Lie down on your bed and pretend you are ill. When your father comes to see you, say to him, ‘Please let my sister Tamar come and give me something to eat. Let her prepare it in my sight so I may watch her and eat it from her hand.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> So Amnon lay down and feigned illness. When the king came to see him, Amnon said, “Please let my sister Tamar come and make a couple of cakes in my sight, so that I may eat from her hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then David sent word to Tamar at the palace: “Please go to the house of Amnon your brother and prepare a meal for him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Tamar went to the house of her brother Amnon, who was lying down. She took some dough, kneaded it, made cakes in his sight, and baked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> Then she brought the pan and set it down before him, but he refused to eat. “Send everyone away!” said Amnon. And everyone went out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Amnon said to Tamar, “Bring the food into the bedroom, so that I may eat it from your hand.”<br />Tamar took the cakes she had made and went to her brother Amnon’s bedroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> And when she had brought them to him to eat, he took hold of her and said, “Come lie with me, my sister!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> “No, my brother!” she cried. “Do not violate me, for such a thing should never be done in Israel. Do not do this disgraceful thing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> Where could I ever take my shame? And you would be like one of the fools in Israel! Please speak to the king, for he will not withhold me from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> But Amnon refused to listen to her, and being stronger, he violated her and lay with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:17"> Then Amnon hated Tamar with such intensity that his hatred was greater than the love he previously had. “Get up!” he said to her. “Be gone!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> “No,” she replied, “sending me away is worse than this great wrong you have already done to me!”<br />But he refused to listen to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Instead, he called to his attendant and said, “Throw this woman out and bolt the door behind her!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalms 45:13–14"> So Amnon’s attendant threw her out and bolted the door behind her. Now Tamar was wearing a robe of many colors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or a robe with long sleeves; see also Genesis 37:3.">b</a></sup>because this is what the king’s virgin daughters wore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremiah 2:37; 2 Samuel 1:11"> And Tamar put ashes on her head and tore her robe. And putting her hand on her head, she went away crying aloud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> Her brother Absalom said to her, “Has your brother Amnon been with you? Be quiet for now, my sister. He is your brother. Do not take this thing to heart.”<br />So Tamar lived as a desolate woman in the house of her brother Absalom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Samuel 2:29"> When King David heard all this, he was furious.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="DSS and LXX include But he would not punish his son Amnon, because he loved him, since he was his firstborn.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> And Absalom never said a word to Amnon, either good or bad, because he hated Amnon for violating his sister Tamar.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Absalom’s Revenge on Amnon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; 1 Samuel 25:4; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> Two years later, when Absalom’s sheepshearers were at Baal-hazor near Ephraim, he invited all the sons of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:8–15; Jeremiah 41:6–7; Psalms 55:21"> And he went to the king and said, “Your servant has just hired shearers. Will the king and his servants please come with me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; 2 Samuel 14:22"> “No, my son,” the king replied, “we should not all go, or we would be a burden to you.” Although Absalom urged him, he was not willing to go, but gave him his blessing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 11:13–15"> “If not,” said Absalom, “please let my brother Amnon go with us.”<br />“Why should he go with you?” the king asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> But Absalom urged him, so the king sent Amnon and the rest of his sons.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX and Vulgate include And Absalom prepared a feast fit for a king.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> Now Absalom had ordered his young men, “Watch Amnon until his heart is merry with wine, and when I order you to strike Amnon down, you are to kill him. Do not be afraid. Have I not commanded you? Be courageous and valiant!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 1:33; 2 Kings 1:9–12"> So Absalom’s young men did to Amnon just as Absalom had ordered. Then all the other sons of the king got up, and each one fled on his mule.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> While they were on the way, a report reached David: “Absalom has struck down all the sons of the king; not one of them is left!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 12:16; Job 1:20"> Then the king stood up, tore his clothes, and lay down on the ground. And all his servants stood by with their clothes torn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 2 Samuel 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> But Jonadab, the son of David’s brother Shimeah, spoke up: “My lord must not think they have killed all the sons of the king, for only Amnon is dead. In fact, Absalom has planned this since the day Amnon violated his sister Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19"> So now, my lord the king, do not take to heart the report that all the sons of the king are dead. Only Amnon is dead.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Absalom Flees to Geshur

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> Meanwhile, Absalom had fled. When the young man standing watch looked up, he saw many people coming down the road west of him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or behind him">e</a></sup>along the side of the hill. And the watchman went and reported to the king, “I see men coming from the direction of Horonaim, along the side of the hill.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew does not include And the watchman....">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> So Jonadab said to the king, “Look, the sons of the king have arrived! It is just as your servant said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 12:21; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And as he finished speaking, the sons of the king came in, wailing loudly. Then the king and all his servants also wept very bitterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 14:32; 2 Samuel 13:34"> Now Absalom fled and went to Talmai son of Ammihud, the king of Geshur. But David mourned for his son every day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:8; 2 Samuel 14:23; 2 Samuel 14:32"> After Absalom had fled and gone to Geshur, he stayed there three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> And King David<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="MT; DSS and LXX And the spirit of the king">g</a></sup>longed to go to Absalom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or ceased to go out after Absalom">h</a></sup>for he had been consoled over Amnon’s death.</span>
</div>
</div>
