## David Fetches the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:1–4; 1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:6–14"> David again assembled the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:4; 1 Samuel 7:1; Psalms 80:1"> And he and all his troops set out for Baale of Judah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Kiriath-jearim; see 1 Chronicles 13:6.">a</a></sup>to bring up from there the ark of God, which is called by the Name—<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX and Vulgate do not include the Name—">b</a></sup>the name of the LORD of Hosts, who is enthroned between the cherubim that are on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:7; Numbers 7:4–9; Numbers 4:5–12"> They set the ark of God on a new cart and brought it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill. Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, were guiding the new cart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:7; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> bringing with it the ark of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="DSS and some LXX manuscripts; MT includes from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill.">c</a></sup>And Ahio was walking in front of the ark.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Uzzah Touches the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Psalms 150:3–5; Amos 5:23"> David and all the house of Israel were celebrating before the LORD with all kinds of wood instruments,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally before the LORD with all woods of cypress; LXX playing before the Lord on well-tuned instruments mightily, and with songs; some of the instruments in this verse are uncertain; see 1 Chronicles 13:8.">d</a></sup>harps, stringed instruments, tambourines, sistrums, and cymbals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 4:15; Numbers 4:19–20"> When they came to the threshing floor of Nacon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Nacon is a variant of Chidon; see 1 Chronicles 13:9.">e</a></sup>Uzzah reached out and took hold of the ark of God, because the oxen had stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; 1 Chronicles 15:13; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> And the anger of the LORD burned against Uzzah, and God struck him down on the spot for his irreverence,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="MT; DSS for this">f</a></sup>and he died there beside the ark of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:1; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Jonah 4:9"> Then David became angry because the LORD had burst forth against Uzzah. So he named that place Perez-uzzah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Perez-uzzah means outbreak against Uzzah.">g</a></sup>as it is called to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Numbers 17:12–13"> That day David feared the LORD and asked, “How can the ark of the LORD ever come to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–8; 2 Samuel 15:19; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> So he was unwilling to move the ark of the LORD to the City of David; instead, he took it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:5; Genesis 30:27; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Thus the ark of the LORD remained in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite for three months, and the LORD blessed him and all his household.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ark Brought to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:25–3; 1 Kings 8:1; Matthew 10:42"> Now it was reported to King David, “The LORD has blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that belongs to him, because of the ark of God.”<br />So David went and had the ark of God brought up from the house of Obed-edom into the City of David with rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:15"> When those carrying the ark of the LORD had advanced six paces, he sacrificed an ox and a fattened calf.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; Exodus 15:20; Psalms 150:4"> And David, wearing a linen ephod, danced with all his might before the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 68:24–27; 1 Chronicles 15:28; 1 Chronicles 15:25"> while he and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the LORD with shouting and the sounding of the ram’s horn.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Michal’s Contempt for David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Chronicles 15:29; Psalms 69:7"> As the ark of the LORD was entering the City of David, Saul’s daughter Michal looked down from a window and saw King David leaping and dancing before the LORD, and she despised him in her heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 1:4; 1 Kings 8:5"> So they brought the ark of the LORD and set it in its place inside the tent that David had pitched for it. Then David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 8:55; 1 Chronicles 16:2; Genesis 14:19"> When David had finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; 1 Chronicles 16:3"> Then he distributed to every man and woman among the multitude of Israel a loaf of bread, a date cake,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or a portion of meat">h</a></sup>and a raisin cake. And all the people departed, each to his own home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:14; Judges 9:4"> When David returned home to bless his own household, Saul’s daughter Michal came out to meet him. “How the king of Israel has distinguished himself today!” she said. “He has uncovered himself today in the sight of the maidservants of his subjects, like a vulgar person would do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:22; Psalms 89:19–20; 1 Samuel 13:14"> But David said to Michal, “I was dancing before the LORD, who chose me over your father and all his house when He appointed me ruler over the LORD’s people Israel. I will celebrate before the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 51:7"> and I will humiliate and humble myself even more than this. Yet I will be honored by the maidservants of whom you have spoken.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:25; Isaiah 22:14; Isaiah 4:1"> And Michal the daughter of Saul had no children to the day of her death.</span>
</div>
</div>
