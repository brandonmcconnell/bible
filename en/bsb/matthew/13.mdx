## The Parable of the Sower

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> That same day Jesus went out of the house and sat by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> Such large crowds gathered around Him that He got into a boat and sat down, while all the people stood on the shore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> And He told them many things in parables, saying, “A farmer went out to sow his seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> And as he was sowing, some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and devoured it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> Some fell on rocky ground, where it did not have much soil. It sprang up quickly because the soil was shallow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> But when the sun rose, the seedlings were scorched, and they withered because they had no root.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> Other seed fell among thorns, which grew up and choked the seedlings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> Still other seed fell on good soil and produced a crop—a hundredfold, sixtyfold, or thirtyfold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> He who has ears,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR ears to hear; also in verse 43">a</a></sup>let him hear.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Purpose of Jesus’ Parables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> Then the disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Why do You speak to the people in parables?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> He replied, “The knowledge of the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven has been given to you, but not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> Whoever has will be given more, and he will have an abundance. Whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> This is why I speak to them in parables:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Though seeing, they do not see;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">though hearing, they do not hear or understand.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 29:4, Isaiah 42:20, Jeremiah 5:21, and Ezekiel 12:2.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> In them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You will be ever hearing but never understanding;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be ever seeing but never perceiving.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this people’s heart has grown callous;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they hardly hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have closed their eyes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Otherwise they might see with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">understand with their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turn, and I would heal them.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaiah 6:9–10 (see also LXX)">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> But blessed are your eyes because they see, and your ears because they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For truly I tell you, many prophets and righteous men longed to see what you see but did not see it, and to hear what you hear but did not hear it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Sower Explained

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> Consider, then, the parable of the sower:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When anyone hears the message of the kingdom but does not understand it, the evil one comes and snatches away what was sown in his heart. This is the seed sown<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally the one sown; also in verses 20, 22, and 23">d</a></sup>along the path.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> The seed sown on rocky ground is the one who hears the word and at once receives it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> But since he has no root, he remains for only a season. When trouble or persecution comes because of the word, he quickly falls away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> The seed sown among the thorns is the one who hears the word, but the worries of this life and the deceitfulness of wealth choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> But the seed sown on good soil is the one who hears the word and understands it. He indeed bears fruit and produces a crop—a hundredfold, sixtyfold, or thirtyfold.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Weeds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Jesus put before them another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like a man who sowed good seed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But while everyone was asleep, his enemy came and sowed weeds among the wheat, and slipped away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> When the wheat sprouted and bore grain, then the weeds also appeared.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> The owner’s servants came to him and said, ‘Sir, didn’t you sow good seed in your field? Where then did the weeds come from?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> ‘An enemy did this,’ he replied.<br />So the servants asked him, ‘Do you want us to go and pull them up?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> ‘No,’ he said, ‘if you pull the weeds now, you might uproot the wheat with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Let both grow together until the harvest. At that time I will tell the harvesters: First collect the weeds and tie them in bundles to be burned; then gather the wheat into my barn.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Mustard Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> He put before them another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed that a man took and planted in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> Although it is the smallest of all seeds, yet it grows into the largest of garden plants and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and nest in its branches.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Leaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> He told them still another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like leaven that a woman took and mixed into three measures of flour, until all of it was leavened.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> Jesus spoke all these things to the crowds in parables. He did not tell them anything without using a parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> So was fulfilled what was spoken through the prophet:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will open My mouth in parables;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will utter things hidden since the foundation of the world.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Psalms 78:2 (see also LXX); SBL, NE, and WH do not include of the world.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Weeds Explained

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then Jesus dismissed the crowds and went into the house. His disciples came to Him and said, “Explain to us the parable of the weeds in the field.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> He replied, “The One who sows the good seed is the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> The field is the world, and the good seed represents the sons of the kingdom. The weeds are the sons of the evil one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> and the enemy who sows them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the harvesters are angels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> As the weeds are collected and burned in the fire, so will it be at the end of the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of Man will send out His angels, and they will weed out of His kingdom every cause of sin and all who practice lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> And they will throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Daniel 12:3.">f</a></sup><br />He who has ears, let him hear.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parables of the Treasure and the Pearl

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field. When a man found it, he hid it again, and in his joy he went and sold all he had and bought that field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant in search of fine pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> When he found one very precious pearl, he went away and sold all he had and bought it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Net

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> Once again, the kingdom of heaven is like a net that was cast into the sea and caught all kinds of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> When it was full, the men pulled it ashore. Then they sat down and sorted the good fish into containers, but threw the bad away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> So will it be at the end of the age: The angels will come and separate the wicked from the righteous</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> and throw them into the fiery furnace, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Have you understood all these things?”<br />“Yes,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> Then He told them, “For this reason, every scribe who has been discipled in the kingdom of heaven is like a homeowner who brings out of his storeroom new treasures as well as old.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Rejection at Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> When Jesus had finished these parables, He withdrew from that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> Coming to His hometown, He taught the people in their synagogue, and they were astonished. “Where did this man get such wisdom and miraculous powers?” they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> “Isn’t this the carpenter’s son? Isn’t His mother’s name Mary, and aren’t His brothers James, Joseph,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="BYZ and TR Joses; see Mark 6:3.">g</a></sup>Simon, and Judas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Aren’t all His sisters with us as well? Where then did this man get all these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> And they took offense at Him.<br />But Jesus said to them, “Only in his hometown and in his own household is a prophet without honor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> And He did not do many miracles there, because of their unbelief.</span>
</div>
</div>
