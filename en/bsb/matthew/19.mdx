## Teachings about Divorce

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Matthew 19:1–9; Matthew 7:28"> When Jesus had finished saying these things, He left Galilee and went into the region of Judea beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:15; Mark 6:55–56; Matthew 15:30–31"> Large crowds followed Him, and He healed them there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:2; Hebrews 3:9; Matthew 16:1"> Then some Pharisees came and tested Him by asking, “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2"> Jesus answered, “Have you not read that from the beginning the Creator ‘made them male and female,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 2:21–24; 1 Corinthians 7:4; 1 Corinthians 7:2"> and said, ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Genesis 2:24 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup>?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Mark 10:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14"> So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let man not separate.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31; Mark 10:4"> “Why then,” they asked, “did Moses order a man to give his wife a certificate of divorce and send her away?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 24:1.">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 2:13–14; Genesis 2:24; Mark 10:5"> Jesus replied, “Moses permitted you to divorce your wives because of your hardness of heart. But it was not this way from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> Now I tell you that whoever divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another woman commits adultery.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="SBL and BYZ include And he who marries a divorced woman commits adultery; see Matthew 5:32.">d</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–35; Proverbs 19:13–14; Proverbs 21:19"> His disciples said to Him, “If this is the case between a man and his wife, it is better not to marry.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 7:35; 1 Corinthians 7:7–9"> “Not everyone can accept this word,” He replied, “but only those to whom it has been given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–38; 1 Corinthians 9:5; Isaiah 56:3–4"> For there are eunuchs who were born that way; others were made that way by men; and still others live like eunuchs for the sake of the kingdom of heaven. The one who can accept this should accept it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Blesses the Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Luke 18:15–17; Matthew 18:2–5"> Then little children were brought to Jesus for Him to place His hands on them and pray for them. And the disciples rebuked those who brought them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:16–17; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> But Jesus said, “Let the little children come to Me, and do not hinder them! For the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:16; 2 Timothy 3:15; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> And after He had placed His hands on them, He went on from there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Rich Young Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 10:17–30; John 3:15; Jude 1:21"> Just then a man came up to Jesus and inquired, “Teacher, what good thing must I do to obtain eternal life?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Luke 10:26–28; Galatians 3:11–13"> “Why do you ask Me about what is good?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="BYZ and TR 16...“Good Teacher, what good thing must I do to obtain eternal life?” 17 “Why do you call Me good?” See Mark 10:17–18 and Luke 18:18–19.">e</a></sup>Jesus replied. “There is only One who is good. If you want to enter life, keep the commandments.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Exodus 20:12–17; Mark 10:19"> “Which ones?” the man asked.<br />Jesus answered, “‘Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not bear false witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 20:12; Matthew 22:39"> honor your father and mother, and love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Exodus 20:12–16; Leviticus 19:18; Deuteronomy 5:16–20">f</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Mark 10:20–21; Philippians 3:6"> “All these I have kept,” said the young man. “What do I still lack?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 10:21; Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33"> Jesus told him, “If you want to be perfect, go, sell your possessions and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 13:22"> When the young man heard this, he went away in sorrow, because he had great wealth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Matthew 13:22; Mark 10:23"> Then Jesus said to His disciples, “Truly I tell you, it is hard for a rich man to enter the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:44; Luke 18:25; Mark 10:24–25"> Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 10:13; Matthew 24:22; Romans 11:5–7"> When the disciples heard this, they were greatly astonished and asked, “Who then can be saved?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Job 42:2"> Jesus looked at them and said, “With man this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Luke 18:28; Luke 5:11"> “Look,” Peter replied, “we have left everything to follow You. What then will there be for us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:28–30; 1 Corinthians 6:2–3; Matthew 25:31"> Jesus said to them, “Truly I tell you, in the renewal of all things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or in the regeneration">g</a></sup>when the Son of Man sits on His glorious throne, you who have followed Me will also sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:29–30; Mark 10:29–30; Philippians 3:8"> And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="NE, WH, and NA do not include or wife.">h</a></sup>or children or fields for the sake of My name will receive a hundredfold and will inherit eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 20:16; Luke 13:30; Mark 10:31"> But many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.</span>
</div>
</div>
