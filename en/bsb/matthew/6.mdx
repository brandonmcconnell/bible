## Giving to the Needy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; John 12:43; Hebrews 6:10"> “Be careful not to perform your righteous acts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR charitable acts or alms">a</a></sup>before men to be seen by them. If you do, you will have no reward from your Father in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15"> So when you give to the needy, do not sound a trumpet before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and on the streets, to be honored by men. Truly I tell you, they already have their full reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:44"> But when you give to the needy, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:18; Matthew 6:6; Jeremiah 17:10"> so that your giving may be in secret. And your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:10–11; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:13"> And when you pray, do not be like the hypocrites. For they love to pray standing in the synagogues and on the street corners to be seen by men. Truly I tell you, they already have their full reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> But when you pray, go into your inner room, shut your door, and pray to your Father, who is unseen. And your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–3; Ecclesiastes 5:7; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> And when you pray, do not babble on like pagans, for they think that by their many words they will be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Luke 12:30; Philippians 4:6"> Do not be like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:1–4; Revelation 4:11; Romans 8:15"> So then, this is how you should pray:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Our Father in heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hallowed be Your name.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 12:50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your kingdom come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your will be done,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on earth as it is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 30:8; Luke 11:3; Matthew 4:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give us this day our daily bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And forgive us our debts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as we also have forgiven our debtors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 7:14–17; Matthew 26:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">And lead us not into temptation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but deliver us from the evil one.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or from evil; BYZ and TR include For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory, forever. </div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:35"> But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive yours.</span>

## Proper Fasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3–5; Matthew 9:14–15; Daniel 9:3"> When you fast, do not be somber like the hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces to show men they are fasting. Truly I tell you, they already have their full reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 3:3; Ecclesiastes 9:8; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Matthew 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:18"> so that your fasting will not be obvious to men, but only to your Father, who is unseen. And your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Treasures in Heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 12:21"> Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or worm; also in verse 20">c</a></sup>destroy, and where thieves break in and steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Matthew 19:21; James 2:5"> But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moth and rust do not destroy, and where thieves do not break in and steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Luke 12:34; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lamp of the Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:34–36; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Colossians 3:22"> The eye is the lamp of the body. If your eyes are good,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally If your eye is sound; see Proverbs 22:9">d</a></sup>your whole body will be full of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:11; Matthew 6:22; Isaiah 5:20–21"> But if your eyes are bad,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally if your eye is evil; see Proverbs 23:6 and Proverbs 28:22">e</a></sup>your whole body will be full of darkness. If then the light within you is darkness, how great is that darkness!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Luke 16:13; James 4:4"> No one can serve two masters: Either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve both God and money.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Worry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7; Romans 8:32"> Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or drink; or about your body, what you will wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:29–31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:24–32"> Look at the birds of the air: They do not sow or reap or gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; Psalms 39:5–6"> Who of you by worrying can add a single hour to his life?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or a single cubit to his height; a cubit was approximately 18 inches or 45 centimeters.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; Philippians 4:6; Luke 12:27"> And why do you worry about clothes? Consider how the lilies of the field grow: They do not labor or spin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> Yet I tell you that not even Solomon in all his glory was adorned like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Luke 12:28; 1 Peter 1:24"> If that is how God clothes the grass of the field, which is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the furnace, will He not much more clothe you, O you of little faith?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Luke 12:29; Matthew 4:4"> Therefore do not worry, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:8; Luke 12:30; Ephesians 4:17"> For the Gentiles strive after all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 12:31; Matthew 5:6; John 6:27"> But seek first the kingdom of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="SBL, WH, and NE seek first His kingdom; see also Luke 12:31.">g</a></sup>and His righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; John 14:27; Hebrews 13:5–6"> Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Today has enough trouble of its own.</span>
</div>
</div>
