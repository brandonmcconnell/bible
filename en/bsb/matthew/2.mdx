## The Pilgrimage of the Magi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; Luke 2:4–7"> After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea, during the time of King Herod, Magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16"> asking, “Where is the One who has been born King of the Jews? We saw His star in the east<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or as it rose">a</a></sup>and have come to worship Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 11:47–48; Matthew 23:37"> When King Herod heard this, he was disturbed, and all Jerusalem with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Matthew 7:29; Acts 6:12"> And when he had assembled all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he asked them where the Christ was to be born.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 7:42; Ruth 1:1; 1 Samuel 16:1"> “In Bethlehem in Judea,” they replied, “for this is what the prophet has written:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–25; Micah 5:2; Isaiah 9:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘But you, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are by no means least among the rulers of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for out of you will come a ruler</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will be the shepherd of My people Israel.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Micah 5:2; see also 2 Samuel 5:2.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:21; Psalms 55:21"> Then Herod called the Magi secretly and learned from them the exact time the star had appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19–20; Jeremiah 41:5–7; Luke 20:20–21"> And sending them to Bethlehem, he said: “Go and search carefully for the Child, and when you find Him, report to me, so that I too may go and worship Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 25:12"> After they had heard the king, they went on their way, and the star they had seen in the east went ahead of them until it stood over the place where the Child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:3; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:10"> When they saw the star, they rejoiced with great delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10; Luke 2:16"> On coming to the house, they saw the Child with His mother Mary, and they fell down and worshiped Him. Then they opened their treasures and presented Him with gifts of gold and frankincense and myrrh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 2:22; Matthew 2:19; Matthew 2:13"> And having been warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they withdrew to their country by another route.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Flight to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:7; Acts 5:19; Matthew 1:20"> When the Magi had gone, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. “Get up!” he said. “Take the Child and His mother and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is going to search for the Child to kill Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 2:20–21; Acts 26:21; Matthew 1:24"> So he got up, took the Child and His mother by night, and withdrew to Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22; Numbers 24:8"> where he stayed until the death of Herod. This fulfilled what the Lord had spoken through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called My Son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hosea 11:1">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Weeping and Great Mourning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 28:15; Judges 16:10"> When Herod saw that he had been outwitted by the Magi, he was filled with rage. Sending orders, he put to death all the boys in Bethlehem and its vicinity who were two years old and under, according to the time he had learned from the Magi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then what was spoken through the prophet Jeremiah was fulfilled:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Jeremiah 9:17–21; Jeremiah 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">“A voice is heard in Ramah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">weeping and great mourning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rachel weeping for her children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and refusing to be comforted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they are no more.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jeremiah 31:15">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Return to Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Daniel 8:25; Matthew 2:12–13"> After Herod died, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:19; 1 Kings 12:1–3; Matthew 2:13"> “Get up!” he said. “Take the Child and His mother and go to the land of Israel, for those seeking the Child’s life are now dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 6:22"> So Joseph got up, took the Child and His mother, and went to the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 48:17–18; Luke 2:39; John 7:52"> But when he learned that Archelaus was reigning in Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there. And having been warned in a dream, he withdrew to the district of Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 13:5; John 1:45–46; Mark 1:24"> and he went and lived in a town called Nazareth. So was fulfilled what was spoken through the prophets: “He will be called a Nazarene.”</span>
</div>
</div>
