## The Parable of the Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song 8:11–12"> “For the kingdom of heaven is like a landowner who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> He agreed to pay them a denarius<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; similarly in verses 9, 10, and 13.">a</a></sup>for the day and sent them into his vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> About the third hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, about nine in the morning">b</a></sup>he went out and saw others standing in the marketplace doing nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> ‘You also go into my vineyard,’ he said, ‘and I will pay you whatever is right.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> So they went.<br />He went out again about the sixth hour and the ninth hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, about noon and again about three in the afternoon">c</a></sup>and did the same thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> About the eleventh hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, about five in the afternoon; also in verse 9">d</a></sup>he went out and found still others standing around. ‘Why have you been standing here all day long doing nothing?’ he asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> ‘Because no one has hired us,’ they answered.<br />So he told them, ‘You also go into my vineyard.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="BYZ and TR include and whatever is right, you shall receive.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> When evening came, the owner of the vineyard said to his foreman, ‘Call the workers and pay them their wages, starting with the last ones hired and moving on to the first.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> The workers who were hired about the eleventh hour came and each received a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So when the original workers came, they assumed they would receive more. But each of them also received a denarius.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> On receiving their pay, they began to grumble against the landowner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> ‘These men who were hired last worked only one hour,’ they said, ‘and you have made them equal to us who have borne the burden and the scorching heat of the day.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> But he answered one of them, ‘Friend, I am not being unfair to you. Did you not agree with me on one denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take your pay and go. I want to give this last man the same as I gave you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> Do I not have the right to do as I please with what is mine? Or are you envious because I am generous?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> So the last will be first, and the first will be last.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR include For many are called, but few are chosen.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Third Prediction of the Passion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> As Jesus was going up to Jerusalem, He took the twelve disciples aside and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> “Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be delivered over to the chief priests and scribes. They will condemn Him to death</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and will deliver Him over to the Gentiles to be mocked and flogged and crucified. And on the third day He will be raised to life.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Mother’s Request

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then the mother of Zebedee’s sons came to Jesus with her sons and knelt down to make a request of Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> “What do you want?” He inquired.<br />She answered, “Declare that in Your kingdom one of these two sons of mine may sit at Your right hand, and the other at Your left.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> “You do not know what you are asking,” Jesus replied. “Can you drink the cup I am going to drink?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="BYZ includes or be baptized with the baptism with which I am baptized. TR is similar.">g</a></sup><br />“We can,” the brothers answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> “You will indeed drink My cup,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="BYZ and TR include and be baptized with the baptism with which I am baptized.">h</a></sup>Jesus said. “But to sit at My right or left is not Mine to grant. These seats belong to those for whom My Father has prepared them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> When the ten heard about this, they were indignant with the two brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> But Jesus called them aside and said, “You know that the rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and their superiors exercise authority over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> It shall not be this way among you. Instead, whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> and whoever wants to be first among you must be your slave—</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> just as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Blind Men by the Road

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> As they were leaving Jericho, a large crowd followed Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> And there were two blind men sitting beside the road. When they heard that Jesus was passing by, they cried out, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> The crowd admonished them to be silent, but they cried out all the louder, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> Jesus stopped and called them. “What do you want Me to do for you?” He asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> “Lord,” they answered, “let our eyes be opened.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> Moved with compassion, Jesus touched their eyes, and at once they received their sight and followed Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
