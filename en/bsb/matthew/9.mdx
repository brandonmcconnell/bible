## Jesus Heals a Paralytic

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Luke 8:37; Matthew 8:18"> Jesus got into a boat, crossed over, and came to His own town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:18–26; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> Just then some men brought<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally And behold, they brought">a</a></sup>to Him a paralytic lying on a mat. When Jesus saw their faith, He said to the paralytic, “Take courage, son; your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Luke 5:21; Leviticus 24:16"> On seeing this, some of the scribes said to themselves, “This man is blaspheming!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> But Jesus knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NA, BYZ, and TR saw">b</a></sup>what they were thinking and said, “Why do you harbor evil in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 5:8–14; Mark 2:9–12; Acts 3:16"> Which is easier: to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Get up and walk’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins...” Then He said to the paralytic, “Get up, pick up your mat, and go home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the man got up and went home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Matthew 15:31; Acts 4:21"> When the crowds saw this, they were filled with awe and glorified God, who had given such authority to men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calls Matthew

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:27–38; Mark 2:14–22; Luke 19:2–10"> As Jesus went on from there, He saw a man named Matthew sitting at the tax booth. “Follow Me,” He told him, and Matthew got up and followed Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 5:29–32; Mark 2:15–17"> Later, as Jesus was dining at Matthew’s house, many tax collectors and sinners came and ate with Him and His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:19; Luke 5:30; Mark 2:16"> When the Pharisees saw this, they asked His disciples, “Why does your Teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 5:31; Revelation 3:17–18; Mark 2:17"> On hearing this, Jesus said, “It is not the healthy who need a doctor, but the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Hosea 6:6"> But go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hosea 6:6">c</a></sup>For I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="BYZ and TR but sinners, to repentance; see Luke 5:32.">d</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then John’s disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Why is it that we and the Pharisees fast so often,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="NE and WH do not include so often.">e</a></sup>but Your disciples do not fast?”</span>

</div>
</div>

## The Patches and the Wineskins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; Psalms 125:3"> No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment. For the patch will pull away from the garment, and a worse tear will result.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; Psalms 119:83"> Neither do men pour new wine into old wineskins. If they do, the skins will burst, the wine will spill, and the wineskins will be ruined. Instead, they pour new wine into new wineskins, and both are preserved.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Healing Touch of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While Jesus was saying these things, a synagogue leader came and knelt before Him. “My daughter has just died,” he said. “But come and place Your hand on her, and she will live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> So Jesus got up and went with him, along with His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> Suddenly a woman who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years came up behind Him and touched the fringe of His cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> She said to herself, “If only I touch His cloak, I will be healed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> Jesus turned and saw her. “Take courage, daughter,” He said, “your faith has healed you.” And the woman was healed from that very hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> When Jesus entered the house of the synagogue leader, He saw the flute players and the noisy crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> “Go away,” He told them. “The girl is not dead, but asleep.” And they laughed at Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> After the crowd had been put outside, Jesus went in and took the girl by the hand, and she got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> And the news about this spread throughout that region.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals the Blind and Mute

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> As Jesus went on from there, two blind men followed Him, crying out, “Have mercy on us, Son of David!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> After Jesus had entered the house, the blind men came to Him. “Do you believe that I am able to do this?” He asked.<br />“Yes, Lord,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then He touched their eyes and said, “According to your faith will it be done to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14; Matthew 17:9"> And their eyes were opened. Jesus warned them sternly, “See that no one finds out about this!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:36; Matthew 9:26; Mark 1:44–45"> But they went out and spread the news about Him throughout the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 12:22–24; Luke 11:14"> As they were leaving, a demon-possessed man who was mute was brought to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; Mark 7:32–37; Exodus 4:11–12"> And when the demon had been driven out, the man began to speak. The crowds were amazed and said, “Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:23–24"> But the Pharisees said, “It is by the prince of demons that He drives out demons.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord of the Harvest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–24; Acts 10:38; Mark 6:56"> Jesus went through all the towns and villages, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 6:34; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 50:6"> When He saw the crowds, He was moved with compassion for them, because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 10:2; Mark 16:15; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> Then He said to His disciples, “The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 10:1–2; Jeremiah 3:15; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into His harvest.”</span>
</div>
