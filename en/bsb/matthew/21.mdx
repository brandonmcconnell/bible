## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Mark 11:1–10; Luke 21:37"> As they approached Jerusalem and came to Bethphage on the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent out two disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:30–32; Mark 11:2–3; John 2:5–8"> saying to them, “Go into the village ahead of you, and at once you will find a donkey tied there, with her colt beside her. Untie them and bring them to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–2; Acts 17:25; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> If anyone questions you, tell him that the Lord needs them, and he will send them right away.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Matthew 1:22"> This took place to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9–10; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Say to the Daughter of Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘See, your King comes to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">gentle and riding on a donkey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on a colt, the foal of a donkey.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Zechariah 9:9">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:43; John 15:14; Exodus 40:16"> So the disciples went and did as Jesus had directed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Mark 11:4–8; Luke 19:32–35"> They brought the donkey and the colt and laid their cloaks on them, and Jesus sat on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; John 12:13; Leviticus 23:40"> A massive crowd spread their cloaks on the road, while others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:24–26; Luke 2:14; John 12:13–15"> The crowds that went ahead of Him and those that followed were shouting:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna to the Son of David!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hosanna is a transliteration of the Hebrew Hosia-na, meaning Save, we pray or Save now, which became a shout of praise; see Psalms 118:25; also in verse 15.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 118:26">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna in the highest!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “Hosanna in the highest heaven!” See Psalms 118:25 and Psalms 148:1.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 9:5; Ruth 1:19; John 12:16–19"> When Jesus had entered Jerusalem, the whole city was stirred and asked, “Who is this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:14; Luke 7:16; John 7:40"> The crowds replied, “This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Luke 19:45–47; Mark 11:15–18"> Then Jesus entered the temple courts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally the temple; also in verses 15 and 23; BYZ and TR the temple of God">e</a></sup>and drove out all who were buying and selling there. He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:11; Isaiah 56:7; Luke 19:46"> And He declared to them, “It is written: ‘My house will be called a house of prayer.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isaiah 56:7">f</a></sup>But you are making it ‘a den of robbers.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jeremiah 7:11">g</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 3:1–9; Acts 10:38; Matthew 11:4–5"> The blind and the lame came to Him at the temple, and He healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Luke 19:39–40; Isaiah 26:11"> But the chief priests and scribes were indignant when they saw the wonders He performed and the children shouting in the temple courts, “Hosanna to the Son of David!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Matthew 11:25; Luke 19:39–40"> “Do You hear what these children are saying?” they asked.<br />“Yes,” Jesus answered. “Have you never read:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘From the mouths of children and infants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have ordained praise’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Psalms 8:2 (see also LXX)">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:18; Mark 11:11; Luke 24:50"> Then He left them and went out of the city to Bethany, where He spent the night.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Barren Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 12:1"> In the morning, as Jesus was returning to the city, He was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 15:6; Mark 11:14; Titus 1:16"> Seeing a fig tree by the road, He went up to it but found nothing on it except leaves. “May you never bear fruit again!” He said. And immediately the tree withered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Mark 11:20–21; James 1:10–11"> When the disciples saw this, they marveled and asked, “How did the fig tree wither so quickly?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:6; Matthew 17:20; Mark 11:22–23"> “Truly I tell you,” Jesus replied, “if you have faith and do not doubt, not only will you do what was done to the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,’ it will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–15; Mark 11:24"> If you believe, you will receive whatever you ask for in prayer.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Authority Challenged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:7; Mark 11:27–33; 1 Chronicles 24:1–19"> When Jesus returned to the temple courts and began to teach, the chief priests and elders of the people came up to Him. “By what authority are You doing these things?” they asked. “And who gave You this authority?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Colossians 4:6; Matthew 10:16"> “I will also ask you one question,” Jesus replied, “and if you answer Me, I will tell you by what authority I am doing these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 3:20; Mark 1:1–11; John 3:18"> What was the source of John’s baptism? Was it from heaven or from men?”<br />They deliberated among themselves and said, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will ask, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Matthew 14:5; John 5:35"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ we are afraid of the people, for they all regard John as a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 1:18–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10; Luke 20:7–8"> So they answered, “We do not know.”<br />And Jesus replied, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I am doing these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Two Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 20:1; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But what do you think? There was a man who had two sons. He went to the first one and said, ‘Son, go and work today in the vineyard.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28–32; Matthew 3:2–8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> ‘I will not,’ he replied. But later he changed his mind and went.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="NE and WH But he went.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 23:3"> Then the man went to the second son and told him the same thing.<br />‘I will, sir,’ he said. But he did not go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 7:37–50; Matthew 7:21"> Which of the two did the will of his father?”<br />“The first,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="NE and WH The latter">j</a></sup>” they answered.<br />Jesus said to them, “Truly I tell you, the tax collectors and prostitutes are entering the kingdom of God before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 7:37–50; Zechariah 7:11–12; Luke 7:29–30"> For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him, but the tax collectors and prostitutes did. And even after you saw this, you did not repent and believe him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 12:1–12; Luke 20:9–19"> Listen to another parable: There was a landowner who planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it, dug a winepress in it, and built a tower. Then he rented it out to some tenants and went away on a journey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 20:10–19; Isaiah 5:4; Zechariah 1:3–6"> When the harvest time drew near, he sent his servants to the tenants to collect his share of the fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Matthew 5:12; Hebrews 11:36–37"> But the tenants seized his servants. They beat one, killed another, and stoned a third.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 22:4"> Again, he sent other servants, more than the first group. But the tenants did the same to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 3:16; John 3:35–36; Zephaniah 3:7"> Finally, he sent his son to them. ‘They will respect my son,’ he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 20:14; Genesis 37:18–20; Mark 12:7–8"> But when the tenants saw the son, they said to one another, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him and take his inheritance.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:23; James 5:6; Luke 22:52–54"> So they seized him and threw him out of the vineyard and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Mark 12:9; Luke 20:15–16"> Therefore, when the owner of the vineyard returns, what will he do to those tenants?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 21:43; Acts 28:28"> “He will bring those wretches to a wretched end,” they replied, “and will rent out the vineyard to other tenants who will give him his share of the fruit at harvest time.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Jesus said to them, “Have you never read in the Scriptures:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has become the cornerstone.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This is from the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it is marvelous in our eyes’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Psalms 118:22–23">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 21:41; Matthew 8:11–12; 1 Peter 2:9"> Therefore I tell you that the kingdom of God will be taken away from you and given to a people who will produce its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Peter 2:8; Daniel 2:34–35"> He who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, but he on whom it falls will be crushed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Tischendorf and some early manuscripts do not include verse 44; see also Luke 20:18.">l</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 20:19; Luke 11:45; Matthew 12:12"> When the chief priests and Pharisees heard His parables, they knew that Jesus was speaking about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Matthew 21:26; Luke 7:16"> Although they wanted to arrest Him, they were afraid of the crowds, because the people regarded Him as a prophet.</span>
</div>
</div>
