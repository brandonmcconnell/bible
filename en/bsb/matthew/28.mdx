## The Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> After the Sabbath, at dawn on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Now after the Sabbaths, it being dawn toward the first of the Sabbaths,">a</a></sup>Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> Suddenly there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, rolled away the stone, and sat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> His appearance was like lightning, and his clothes were white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> The guards trembled in fear of him and became like dead men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> But the angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here; He has risen, just as He said! Come, see the place where He lay.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR where the Lord lay">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> Then go quickly and tell His disciples, ‘He has risen from the dead and is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see Him.’ See, I have told you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> So they hurried away from the tomb in fear and great joy, and ran to tell His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> Suddenly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR They were going to tell His disciples, and suddenly">c</a></sup>Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!” They came to Him, grasped His feet, and worshiped Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> “Do not be afraid,” said Jesus. “Go and tell My brothers to go to Galilee. There they will see Me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Report of the Guards

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> While the women were on their way, some of the guards went into the city and reported to the chief priests all that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And after the chief priests had met with the elders and formed a plan, they gave the soldiers a large sum of money</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> and instructed them: “You are to say, ‘His disciples came by night and stole Him away while we were asleep.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> If this report reaches the governor, we will satisfy him and keep you out of trouble.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> So the guards took the money and did as they were instructed. And this account has been circulated among the Jews to this very day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Great Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> Meanwhile, the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain Jesus had designated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> When they saw Him, they worshiped Him, but some doubted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> Then Jesus came to them and said, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Therefore go and make disciples<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally Having gone, therefore, make disciples">d</a></sup>of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> and teaching them to obey all that I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”</span>
</div>
</div>
