## Jesus Delivered to Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> When morning came, all the chief priests and elders of the people conspired against Jesus to put Him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> They bound Him, led Him away, and handed Him over to Pilate the governor.</span>
</div>

## Judas Hangs Himself

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> When Judas, who had betrayed Him, saw that Jesus was condemned, he was filled with remorse and returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> “I have sinned by betraying innocent blood,” he said.<br />“What is that to us?” they replied. “You bear the responsibility.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> So Judas threw the silver into the temple and left. Then he went away and hanged himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> The chief priests picked up the pieces of silver and said, “It is unlawful to put this into the treasury, since it is blood money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> After conferring together, they used the money to buy the potter’s field as a burial place for foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> That is why it has been called the Field of Blood to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They took the thirty pieces of silver,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the price set on Him by the people of Israel,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">and they gave them for the potter’s field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as the Lord had commanded me.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Jeremiah 19:1–15, Jeremiah 32:6–9, and Zechariah 11:12–13.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus before Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> Meanwhile Jesus stood before the governor, who questioned Him: “Are You the King of the Jews?”<br />“You have said so,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And when He was accused by the chief priests and elders, He gave no answer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then Pilate asked Him, “Do You not hear how many charges they are bringing against You?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> But Jesus gave no answer, not even to a single charge, much to the governor’s amazement.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crowd Chooses Barabbas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now it was the governor’s custom at the feast to release to the crowd a prisoner of their choosing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> At that time they were holding a notorious prisoner named Barabbas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SBL and NA Jesus Barabbas; also in verse 17, but universally called Barabbas in verses 20, 21, and 26">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> So when the crowd had assembled, Pilate asked them, “Which one do you want me to release to you: Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he knew it was out of envy that they had handed Jesus over to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> While Pilate was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent him this message: “Have nothing to do with that innocent man, for I have suffered terribly in a dream today because of Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and elders persuaded the crowds to ask for Barabbas and to have Jesus put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “Which of the two do you want me to release to you?” asked the governor.<br />“Barabbas,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> “What then should I do with Jesus who is called Christ?” Pilate asked.<br />They all answered, “Crucify Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> “Why?” asked Pilate. “What evil has He done?”<br />But they shouted all the louder, “Crucify Him!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pilate Washes His Hands

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> When Pilate saw that he was accomplishing nothing, but that instead a riot was breaking out, he took water and washed his hands before the crowd. “I am innocent of this man’s blood,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally this blood; BYZ and TR this righteous blood">c</a></sup>” he said. “You bear the responsibility.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> All the people answered, “His blood be on us and on our children!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> So Pilate released Barabbas to them. But he had Jesus flogged, and handed Him over to be crucified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Soldiers Mock Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the governor’s soldiers took Jesus into the Praetorium and gathered the whole company around Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They stripped Him and put a scarlet robe on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> And they twisted together a crown of thorns and set it on His head. They put a staff in His right hand, knelt down before Him, and mocked Him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> Then they spit on Him and took the staff and struck Him on the head repeatedly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> After they had mocked Him, they removed the robe and put His own clothes back on Him. Then they led Him away to crucify Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> Along the way they found a man from Cyrene, named Simon, and they forced him to carry the cross of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> And when they came to a place called Golgotha, which means The Place of the Skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they offered Him wine to drink, mixed with gall; but after tasting it, He refused to drink it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> When they had crucified Him, they divided up His garments by casting lots.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 22:18; TR includes to fulfill what was said through the prophet: “They divided My garments among them, and cast lots for My clothing.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> And sitting down, they kept watch over Him there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> Above His head they posted the written charge against Him:<br />THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Two robbers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or insurrectionists; also in verse 44">e</a></sup>were crucified with Him, one on His right and the other on His left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> And those who passed by heaped abuse on Him, shaking their heads</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, “You who are going to destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days, save Yourself! If You are the Son of God, come down from the cross!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> In the same way, the chief priests, scribes, and elders mocked Him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> “He saved others, but He cannot save Himself. He is the King of Israel! Let Him come down now from the cross, and we will believe in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusts in God. Let God deliver Him now if He wants Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Psalms 22:8">f</a></sup>For He said, ‘I am the Son of God.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> In the same way, even the robbers who were crucified with Him berated Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> From the sixth hour until the ninth hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, from noon until three in the afternoon">g</a></sup>darkness came over all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> About the ninth hour Jesus cried out in a loud voice, “Eli, Eli,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="NE and WH Eloi, Eloi">h</a></sup>lema sabachthani?” which means, “My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Psalms 22:1">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> When some of those standing there heard this, they said, “He is calling Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> One of them quickly ran and brought a sponge. He filled it with sour wine,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or filled it with wine vinegar">j</a></sup>put it on a reed, and held it up for Jesus to drink.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Psalms 69:21.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the others said, “Leave Him alone. Let us see if Elijah comes to save Him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="WH includes And another took a spear and pierced His side, and water and blood flowed out; see John 19:34.">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> When Jesus had cried out again in a loud voice, He yielded up His spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> At that moment the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom. The earth quaked, and the rocks were split.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> The tombs broke open, and the bodies of many saints who had fallen asleep were raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> After Jesus’ resurrection, when they had come out of the tombs, they entered the holy city and appeared to many people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> When the centurion and those with him who were guarding Jesus saw the earthquake and all that had happened, they were terrified and said, “Truly this was the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And many women were there, watching from a distance. They had followed Jesus from Galilee to minister to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee’s sons.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Burial of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> When it was evening, there came a rich man from Arimathea named Joseph, who himself was a disciple of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> He went to Pilate to ask for the body of Jesus, and Pilate ordered that it be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> So Joseph took the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and placed it in his own new tomb that he had cut into the rock. Then he rolled a great stone across the entrance to the tomb and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were sitting there opposite the tomb.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Guards at the Tomb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> The next day, the one after Preparation Day, the chief priests and Pharisees assembled before Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> “Sir,” they said, “we remember that while He was alive that deceiver said, ‘After three days I will rise again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> So give the order that the tomb be secured until the third day. Otherwise, His disciples may come and steal Him away and tell the people He has risen from the dead. And this last deception would be worse than the first.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> “You have a guard,” Pilate said. “Go, make the tomb as secure as you know how.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went and secured the tomb by sealing the stone and posting the guard.</span>
</div>
