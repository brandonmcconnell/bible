<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 10:38; Luke 8:1"> After Jesus had finished instructing His twelve disciples, He went on from there to teach and preach in their cities.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, in the towns of Galilee">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> Meanwhile John heard in prison about the works of Christ, and he sent his disciples<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR he sent two of his disciples">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> to ask Him, “Are You the One who was to come, or should we look for someone else?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Jesus replied, “Go back and report to John what you hear and see:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> The blind receive sight, the lame walk, the lepers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A leper was one afflicted with a skin disease. See Leviticus 13.">c</a></sup>are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, and the good news is preached to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> Blessed is the one who does not fall away on account of Me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or who is not offended by Me">d</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Testifies about John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> As John’s disciples were leaving, Jesus began to speak to the crowds about John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed swaying in the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> Otherwise, what did you go out to see? A man dressed in fine clothes? Look, those who wear fine clothing are found in kings’ palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> What then did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> This is the one about whom it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, I will send My messenger ahead of You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare Your way before You.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Malachi 3:1">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> Truly I tell you, among those born of women there has risen no one greater than John the Baptist. Yet even the least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> From the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven has been subject to violence,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or has been forcefully advancing">f</a></sup>and the violent lay claim to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> For all the Prophets and the Law prophesied until John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> And if you are willing to accept it, he is the Elijah who was to come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Malachi 4:5.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> He who has ears,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="BYZ and TR ears to hear">h</a></sup>let him hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> To what can I compare this generation? They are like children sitting in the marketplaces and calling out to others:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘We played the flute for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you did not dance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we sang a dirge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you did not mourn.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, ‘He has a demon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, ‘Look at this glutton and drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ But wisdom is vindicated by her actions.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Woe to the Unrepentant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then Jesus began to denounce the cities in which most of His miracles had been performed, because they did not repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> “Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the miracles that were performed in you had been performed in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> But I tell you, it will be more bearable for Tyre and Sidon on the day of judgment than for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> And you, Capernaum, will you be lifted up to heaven? No, you will be brought down to Hades! For if the miracles that were performed in you had been performed in Sodom, it would have remained to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> But I tell you that it will be more bearable for Sodom on the day of judgment than for you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rest for the Weary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; Psalms 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> At that time Jesus declared, “I praise You, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because You have hidden these things from the wise and learned, and revealed them to little children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> Yes, Father, for this was well-pleasing in Your sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> All things have been entrusted to Me by My Father. No one knows the Son except the Father, and no one knows the Father except the Son and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> For My yoke is easy and My burden is light.”</span>
</div>
</div>
