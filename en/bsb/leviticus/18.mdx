## Unlawful Sexual Relations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> You must not follow the practices of the land of Egypt, where you used to live, and you must not follow the practices of the land of Canaan, into which I am bringing you. You must not walk in their customs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> You are to practice My judgments and keep My statutes by walking in them. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> Keep My statutes and My judgments, for the man who does these things will live by them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Romans 10:5 and Galatians 3:12; see also Ezekiel 20:11, 13, and 21.">a</a></sup>I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> None of you are to approach any close relative to have sexual relations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally to uncover (their) nakedness; here and throughout this chapter">b</a></sup>I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> You must not expose the nakedness of your father by having sexual relations with your mother. She is your mother; you must not have sexual relations with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> You must not have sexual relations with your father’s wife; it would dishonor your father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> You must not have sexual relations with your sister, either your father’s daughter or your mother’s daughter, whether she was born in the same home or elsewhere.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> You must not have sexual relations with your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter, for that would shame your family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You must not have sexual relations with the daughter of your father’s wife, born to your father; she is your sister.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> You must not have sexual relations with your father’s sister; she is your father’s close relative.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You must not have sexual relations with your mother’s sister, for she is your mother’s close relative.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> You must not dishonor your father’s brother by approaching his wife to have sexual relations with her; she is your aunt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> You must not have sexual relations with your daughter-in-law. She is your son’s wife; you are not to have sexual relations with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> You must not have sexual relations with your brother’s wife; that would shame your brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> You must not have sexual relations with both a woman and her daughter. You are not to marry her son’s daughter or her daughter’s daughter and have sexual relations with her. They are close relatives; it is depraved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> You must not take your wife’s sister as a rival wife and have sexual relations with her while your wife is still alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> You must not approach a woman to have sexual relations with her during her menstrual period.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> You must not lie carnally with your neighbor’s wife and thus defile yourself with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> You must not give any of your children to be sacrificed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew to make them pass through (the fire)">c</a></sup>to Molech, for you must not profane the name of your God. I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> You must not lie with a man as with a woman; that is an abomination.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> You must not lie carnally with any animal, thus defiling yourself with it; a woman must not stand before an animal to mate with it; that is a perversion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Do not defile yourselves by any of these practices, for by all these things the nations I am driving out before you have defiled themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> Even the land has become defiled, so I am punishing it for its sin, and the land will vomit out its inhabitants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> But you are to keep My statutes and ordinances, and you must not commit any of these abominations—neither your native-born nor the foreigner who lives among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> For the men who were in the land before you committed all these abominations, and the land has become defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> So if you defile the land, it will vomit you out as it spewed out the nations before you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> Therefore anyone who commits any of these abominations must be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> You must keep My charge not to practice any of the abominable customs that were practiced before you, so that you do not defile yourselves by them. I am the LORD your God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
