## Laws for Grain Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:14–18; Leviticus 9:17; Isaiah 66:20"> “When anyone brings a grain offering to the LORD, his offering must consist of fine flour. He is to pour olive oil on it, put frankincense on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:12; Leviticus 6:15; Acts 10:4"> and bring it to Aaron’s sons the priests. The priest shall take a handful of the flour and oil, together with all the frankincense, and burn this as a memorial portion on the altar, a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 10:12–13; Numbers 18:9; Leviticus 21:22"> The remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy part of the food offerings to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Psalms 22:14"> Now if you bring an offering of grain baked in an oven, it must consist of fine flour, either unleavened cakes mixed with oil or unleavened wafers coated with oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:21; Leviticus 7:9"> If your offering is a grain offering prepared on a griddle,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, a shallow pan for baking or frying">a</a></sup>it must be unleavened bread made of fine flour mixed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:1–19; Psalms 22:1–21; Mark 14:1–15"> Crumble it and pour oil on it; it is a grain offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; Leviticus 7:9"> If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, a deep pan or stew pan">b</a></sup>it must consist of fine flour with oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> When you bring to the LORD the grain offering made in any of these ways, it is to be presented to the priest, and he shall take it to the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Philippians 4:18; Leviticus 6:15"> The priest is to remove the memorial portion from the grain offering and burn it on the altar as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3"> But the remainder of the grain offering shall belong to Aaron and his sons; it is a most holy part of the food offerings to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; Luke 12:1; Exodus 23:18"> No grain offering that you present to the LORD may be made with leaven, for you are not to burn any leaven or honey as a food offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Genesis 23:10–11; Exodus 22:29"> You may bring them to the LORD as an offering of firstfruits, but they must not go up on the altar as a pleasing aroma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:24; Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6"> And you shall season each of your grain offerings with salt. You must not leave the salt of the covenant of your God out of your grain offering; you are to add salt to each of your offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Malachi 1:11; Numbers 28:2"> If you bring a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you shall offer crushed heads of new grain roasted on the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And you are to put oil and frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Romans 8:26–27; Isaiah 61:1"> The priest shall then burn the memorial portion of the crushed grain and the oil, together with all its frankincense, as a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
