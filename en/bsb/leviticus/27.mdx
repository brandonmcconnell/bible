## Rules about Valuations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> “Speak to the Israelites and say to them, ‘When someone makes a special vow to the LORD involving the value of persons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> if the valuation concerns a male from twenty to sixty years of age, then your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="50 shekels is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of silver; also in verse 16.">a</a></sup>according to the sanctuary shekel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A shekel weighed approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams; also in verse 25.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> Or if it is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30 shekels is approximately 12 ounces or 342 grams of silver.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And if the person is from five to twenty years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be twenty shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="20 shekels is approximately 8 ounces or 228 grams of silver.">d</a></sup>and for the female ten shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10 shekels is approximately 4 ounces or 114 grams of silver; also in verse 7.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> Now if the person is from one month to five years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be five shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5 shekels is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams of silver.">f</a></sup>and for the female three shekels of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3 shekels is approximately 1.2 ounces or 34.2 grams of silver.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> And if the person is sixty years of age or older, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15 shekels is approximately 6 ounces or 171 grams of silver.">h</a></sup>for the male and ten shekels for the female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> But if the one making the vow is too poor to pay the valuation, he is to present the person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or present himself">i</a></sup>before the priest, who shall set the value according to what the one making the vow can afford.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If he vows an animal that may be brought as an offering to the LORD, any such animal given to the LORD shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> He must not replace it or exchange it, either good for bad or bad for good. But if he does substitute one animal for another, both that animal and its substitute will be holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> But if the vow involves any of the unclean animals that may not be brought as an offering to the LORD, the animal must be presented before the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The priest shall set its value, whether high or low; as the priest values it, the price will be set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> If, however, the owner decides to redeem the animal, he must add a fifth to its value.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> Now if a man consecrates his house as holy to the LORD, then the priest shall value it either as good or bad. The price will stand just as the priest values it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> But if he who consecrated his house redeems it, he must add a fifth to the assessed value, and it will belong to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> If a man consecrates to the LORD a parcel of his land, then your valuation shall be proportional to the seed required for it—fifty shekels of silver for every homer of barley seed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A homer is a dry measure of approximately 6.24 bushels or 220 liters (probably about 291 pounds or 132 kilograms of barley seed).">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he consecrates his field during the Year of Jubilee, the price will stand according to your valuation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> But if he consecrates his field after the Jubilee, the priest is to calculate the price in proportion to the years left until the next Year of Jubilee, so that your valuation will be reduced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> And if the one who consecrated the field decides to redeem it, he must add a fifth to the assessed value, and it shall belong to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If, however, he does not redeem the field, or if he has sold it to another man, it may no longer be redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> When the field is released in the Jubilee, it will become holy, like a field devoted to the LORD; it becomes the property of the priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> Now if a man consecrates to the LORD a field he has purchased, which is not a part of his own property,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> then the priest shall calculate for him the value up to the Year of Jubilee, and the man shall pay the assessed value on that day as a sacred offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> In the Year of Jubilee the field shall return to the one from whom it was bought—the original owner of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> Every valuation will be according to the sanctuary shekel, twenty gerahs to the shekel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="20 gerahs is equivalent to one shekel (approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams).">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> But no one may consecrate a firstborn of the livestock, because a firstborn belongs to the LORD. Whether it is an ox or a sheep, it is the LORD’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> But if it is among the unclean animals, then he may redeem it according to your valuation and add a fifth of its value. If it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> Nothing that a man sets apart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD; similarly in verse 29.">l</a></sup>to the LORD from all he owns—whether a man, an animal, or his inherited land—can be sold or redeemed; everything so devoted is most holy to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> No person set apart for destruction may be ransomed; he must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instruction on Tithes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> Thus any tithe from the land, whether from the seed of the land or the fruit of the trees, belongs to the LORD; it is holy to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If a man wishes to redeem part of his tithe, he must add a fifth to its value.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> Every tenth animal from the herd or flock that passes under the shepherd’s rod will be holy to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> He must not inspect whether it is good or bad, and he shall not make any substitution. But if he does make a substitution, both the animal and its substitute shall become holy; they cannot be redeemed.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These are the commandments that the LORD gave to Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.</span>
</div>
</div>
