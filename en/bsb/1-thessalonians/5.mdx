## The Day of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> Now about the times and seasons, brothers, we do not need to write to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> For you are fully aware that the Day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> While people are saying, “Peace and security,” destruction will come upon them suddenly, like labor pains on a pregnant woman, and they will not escape.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, brothers, are not in the darkness so that this day should overtake you like a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> For you are all sons of the light and sons of the day; we do not belong to the night or to the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> So then, let us not sleep as the others do, but let us remain awake and sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> But since we belong to the day, let us be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and the helmet of our hope of salvation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God has not appointed us to suffer wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> He died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> Therefore encourage and build one another up, just as you are already doing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christian Living

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> But we ask you, brothers, to acknowledge those who work diligently among you, who care for you in the Lord and who admonish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> In love, hold them in highest regard because of their work. Live in peace with one another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> And we urge you, brothers, to admonish the unruly, encourage the fainthearted, help the weak, and be patient with everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> Make sure that no one repays evil for evil. Always pursue what is good for one another and for all people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Rejoice at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> Pray without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> Give thanks in every circumstance, for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Do not extinguish the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Do not treat prophecies with contempt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> but test all things. Hold fast to what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> Abstain from every form of evil.</span>
</div>
</div>
