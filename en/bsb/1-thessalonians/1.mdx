## Greetings to the Thessalonians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1; Acts 18:5"> Paul, Silvanus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Silas">a</a></sup>and Timothy,<br />To the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:<br />Grace and peace to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR include from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Ephesians 1:15–16; Philemon 1:4"> We always thank God for all of you, remembering you in our prayers</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; Revelation 2:19; Galatians 5:6"> and continually recalling before our God and Father your work of faith, your labor of love, and your enduring hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Titus 3:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> Brothers who are beloved by God, we know that He has chosen you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Mark 16:20; 1 Corinthians 2:2–5"> because our gospel came to you not only in word, but also in power, in the Holy Spirit, and with great conviction—just as you know we lived among you for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 4:16; Acts 13:52"> And you became imitators of us and of the Lord when you welcomed the message with the joy of the Holy Spirit, in spite of your great suffering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12"> As a result, you have become an example to all the believers in Macedonia and Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Romans 10:14–18; Isaiah 52:7"> For not only did the message of the Lord ring out from you to Macedonia and Achaia, but your faith in God has gone out to every place, so that we have no need to say anything more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Galatians 4:8–9"> For they themselves report what kind of welcome you gave us, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:9; Isaiah 25:8–9; Romans 5:9–10"> and to await His Son from heaven, whom He raised from the dead—Jesus our deliverer from the coming wrath.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
