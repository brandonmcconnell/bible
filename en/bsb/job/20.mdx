## Zophar: Destruction Awaits the Wicked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> Then Zophar the Naamathite replied:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“So my anxious thoughts compel me to answer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the turmoil within me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have heard a rebuke that insults me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my understanding prompts a reply.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though his arrogance reaches the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his head touches the clouds,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">he will perish forever, like his own dung;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who had seen him will ask, ‘Where is he?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will fly away like a dream, never to be found;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will be chased away like a vision in the night.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The eye that saw him will see him no more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his place will no longer behold him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">His sons will seek the favor of the poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for his own hands must return his wealth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The youthful vigor that fills his bones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will lie down with him in the dust.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though evil is sweet in his mouth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he conceals it under his tongue,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">though he cannot bear to let it go</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and keeps it in his mouth,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">yet in his stomach his food sours</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">into the venom of cobras within him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He swallows wealth but vomits it out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will force it from his stomach.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will suck the poison of cobras;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fangs of a viper will kill him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will not enjoy the streams,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the rivers flowing with honey and cream.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">He must return the fruit of his labor without consuming it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he cannot enjoy the profits of his trading.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he has oppressed and forsaken the poor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he has seized houses he did not build.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because his appetite is never satisfied,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he cannot escape with his treasure.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nothing is left for him to consume;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thus his prosperity will not endure.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the midst of his plenty, he will be distressed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the full force of misery will come upon him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he has filled his stomach,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will vent His fury upon him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">raining it down on him as he eats.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though he flees from an iron weapon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a bronze-tipped arrow will pierce him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is drawn out of his back,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the gleaming point from his liver.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally from his gall">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Terrors come over him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Total darkness is reserved for his treasures.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">A fire unfanned will consume him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and devour what is left in his tent.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heavens will expose his iniquity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the earth will rise up against him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The possessions of his house will be removed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">flowing away on the day of God’s wrath.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the wicked man’s portion from God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the inheritance God has appointed him.”</span></span>
