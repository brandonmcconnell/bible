## Job Submits Himself to the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job replied to the LORD:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I know that You can do all things</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that no plan of Yours can be thwarted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 131:1; Psalms 139:6; Job 38:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You asked, ‘Who is this</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who conceals My counsel without knowledge?’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Job 38:2">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Surely I spoke of things I did not understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">things too wonderful for me to know.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">You said, ‘Listen now, and I will speak.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will question you, and you shall inform Me.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Job 38:3 and Job 40:7">b</a></sup></span>

## The LORD Rebukes Job’s Friends

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> After the LORD had spoken these words to Job, He said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “My wrath is kindled against you and your two friends. For you have not spoken about Me accurately, as My servant Job has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalms 103:10"> So now, take seven bulls and seven rams, go to My servant Job, and sacrifice a burnt offering for yourselves. Then My servant Job will pray for you, for I will accept his prayer and not deal with you according to your folly. For you have not spoken accurately about Me, as My servant Job has.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as the LORD had told them; and the LORD accepted Job’s prayer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Blesses Job

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> After Job had prayed for his friends, the LORD restored his prosperity and doubled his former possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> All his brothers and sisters and prior acquaintances came and dined with him in his house. They consoled him and comforted him over all the adversity that the LORD had brought upon him. And each one gave him a piece of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew a kesitah; the value or weight of the kesitah is no longer known.">c</a></sup>and a gold ring.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> So the LORD blessed Job’s latter days more than his first. He owned 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 yoke of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3"> And he also had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He named his first daughter Jemimah, his second Keziah, and his third Keren-happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalms 144:12; Acts 7:20"> No women as beautiful as Job’s daughters could be found in all the land, and their father granted them an inheritance among their brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalms 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> After this, Job lived 140 years and saw his children and their children to the fourth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> And so Job died, old and full of years.</span>
</div>
</div>
