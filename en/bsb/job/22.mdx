## Eliphaz: Can a Man Be of Use to God?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Can a man be of use to God?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Can even a wise man benefit Him?</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is it for your reverence that He rebukes you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and enters into judgment against you?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sent widows away empty-handed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the strength of the fatherless was crushed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore snares surround you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sudden peril terrifies you;</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet you say: ‘What does God know?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Does He judge through thick darkness?</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Surely our foes are destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fire has consumed their excess.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Reconcile now and be at peace with Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thereby good will come to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Receive instruction from His mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lay up His words in your heart.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you return to the Almighty, you will be restored.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If you remove injustice from your tents</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">and consign your gold to the dust</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the gold of Ophir to the stones of the ravines,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">then the Almighty will be your gold</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the finest silver for you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely then you will delight in the Almighty</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lift up your face to God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will pray to Him, and He will hear you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will fulfill your vows.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your decisions will be carried out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and light will shine on your ways.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> <span className="pl pl-1">When men are brought low and you say, ‘Lift them up!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then He will save the lowly.</span></span>
