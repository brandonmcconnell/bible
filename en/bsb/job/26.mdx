<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Job 22:14; Habakkuk 3:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He covers the face of the full moon,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or of His throne">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">spreading over it His cloud.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:15; Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 89:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">By His power He stirred</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or stilled">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the sea;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by His understanding He shattered Rahab.</span></span>
