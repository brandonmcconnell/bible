<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will see Him for myself;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my eyes will behold Him, and not as a stranger.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">How my heart yearns</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew my kidneys yearn">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">within me!</span></span>
