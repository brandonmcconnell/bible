## Job Laments His Birth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed the day of his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> And this is what he said:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">“May the day of my birth perish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the night it was said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘A boy is conceived.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If only that day had turned to darkness!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">May God above disregard it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may no light shine upon it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">May darkness and gloom reclaim it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a cloud settle over it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may the blackness of the day overwhelm it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">If only darkness had taken that night away!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">May it not appear among the days of the year;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may it never be entered in any of the months.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, may that night be barren;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may no joyful voice come into it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">May it be cursed by those who curse the day</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or curse the sea">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those prepared to rouse Leviathan.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">May its morning stars grow dark;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may it wait in vain for daylight;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may it not see the breaking of dawn.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For that night did not shut the doors of the womb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to hide the sorrow from my eyes.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why did I not perish at birth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">why did I not die as I came from the womb?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why were there knees to receive me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and breasts that I should be nursed?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For now I would be lying down in peace;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I would be asleep and at rest</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">with kings and counselors of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who built for themselves cities now in ruins,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">or with princes who had gold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who filled their houses with silver.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Or why was I not hidden like a stillborn child,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like an infant who never sees daylight?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the wicked cease from raging,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there the weary find rest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The captives enjoy their ease;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not hear the voice of the oppressor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Both small and great are there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the slave is freed from his master.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why is light given to the miserable,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and life to the bitter of soul,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">who long for death that does not come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and search for it like hidden treasure,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">who rejoice and greatly exult</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they reach the grave?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why is life given to a man whose way is hidden,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whom God has hedged in?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I sigh when food is put before me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my groans pour out like water.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the thing I feared has overtaken me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and what I dreaded has befallen me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am not at ease or quiet;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have no rest, for trouble has come.”</span></span>
</div>
