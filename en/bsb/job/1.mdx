## Job’s Character and Wealth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20"> There was a man in the land of Uz whose name was Job. And this man was blameless and upright, fearing God and shunning evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Job 42:13; Psalms 128:3"> He had seven sons and three daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> and he owned 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 500 yoke of oxen, 500 female donkeys, and a very large number of servants. Job was the greatest man of all the people of the East.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Psalms 133:1"> Job’s sons would take turns holding feasts in their homes, and they would invite their three sisters to eat and drink with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; 1 Samuel 16:5"> And when the days of feasting were over, Job would send for his children to purify them, rising early in the morning to offer burnt offerings for all of them. For Job thought, “Perhaps my children have sinned and cursed God in their hearts.” This was Job’s regular practice.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Satan’s First Attack

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zechariah 3:1"> One day the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Accuser or the Adversary; here and throughout Job 1">a</a></sup>also came with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> “Where have you come from?” said the LORD to Satan.<br />“From roaming through the earth,” he replied, “and walking back and forth in it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> Then the LORD said to Satan, “Have you considered My servant Job? For there is no one on earth like him, a man who is blameless and upright, who fears God and shuns evil.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> Satan answered the LORD, “Does Job fear God for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> Have You not placed a hedge on every side around him and his household and all that he owns? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his possessions have increased in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But stretch out Your hand and strike all that he has, and he will surely curse You to Your face.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> “Very well,” said the LORD to Satan. “Everything he has is in your hands, but you must not lay a hand on the man himself.”<br />Then Satan went out from the presence of the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Job Loses His Children and Possessions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> One day, while Job’s sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:13; Jeremiah 51:31; 1 Samuel 4:17"> a messenger came and reported to Job: “While the oxen were plowing and the donkeys were grazing nearby,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> the Sabeans swooped down and took them away. They put the servants to the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:12; Genesis 19:24; 1 Kings 18:38"> While he was still speaking, another messenger came and reported: “The fire of God fell from heaven. It burned and consumed the sheep and the servants, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Habakkuk 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While he was still speaking, another messenger came and reported: “The Chaldeans formed three bands, raided the camels, and took them away. They put the servants to the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> While he was still speaking, another messenger came and reported: “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> when suddenly a mighty wind swept in from the desert and struck the four corners of the house. It collapsed on the young people and they are dead, and I alone have escaped to tell you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 2 Samuel 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> Then Job stood up, tore his robe, and shaved his head. He fell to the ground and worshiped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Naked I came from my mother’s womb,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and naked I will return.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Blessed be the name of the LORD.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all this, Job did not sin or charge God with wrongdoing.</span>
</div>
</div>
