## Elihu Rebukes Job’s Friends

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 33:9; Job 10:2"> So these three men stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> This kindled the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram. He burned with anger against Job for justifying himself rather than God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> and he burned with anger against Job’s three friends because they had failed to refute Job, and yet had condemned him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job because the others were older than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> But when he saw that the three men had no further reply, his anger was kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am young in years,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while you are old;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that is why I was timid and afraid</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to tell you what I know.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thought that age should speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and many years should teach wisdom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But there is a spirit</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the Spirit; similarly in verse 18">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in a man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the breath of the Almighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that gives him understanding.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not only the old</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or many or great">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who are wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or the elderly who understand justice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I too will declare what I know.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, I waited while you spoke;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I listened to your reasoning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as you searched for words,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-2">I paid you full attention.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But no one proved Job wrong;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not one of you rebutted his arguments.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">So do not claim, ‘We have found wisdom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let God, not man, refute him.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Job has not directed his words against me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will not answer him with your arguments.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Job’s friends are dismayed, with no more to say;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">words have escaped them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Must I wait, now that they are silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">now that they stand and no longer reply?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I too will answer;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yes, I will declare what I know.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am full of words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my spirit within me compels me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, my belly is like unvented wine;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is about to burst like a new wineskin.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I must speak and find relief;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I must open my lips and respond.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will be partial to no one,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor will I flatter any man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I do not know how to flatter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or my Maker would remove me in an instant.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
