## Job: How Can I Contend with God?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Yes, I know that it is so,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but how can a mortal be righteous before God?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">If one wished to contend with God,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or If God wished to contend with someone">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he could not answer Him one time out of a thousand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is wise in heart and mighty in strength.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who has resisted Him and prospered?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He moves mountains without their knowledge</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and overturns them in His anger.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shakes the earth from its place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that its foundations tremble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He commands the sun not to shine;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or rise">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He seals off the stars.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He alone stretches out the heavens</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and treads on the waves of the sea.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">He is the Maker of the Bear and Orion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the Pleiades and the constellations of the south.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does great things beyond searching out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wonders without number.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Were He to pass by me, I would not see Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">were He to move, I would not recognize Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">If He takes away,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or snatches someone in death">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who can stop Him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who dares to ask Him, ‘What are You doing?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God does not restrain His anger;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the helpers of Rahab cower beneath Him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">How then can I answer Him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or choose my arguments against Him?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For even if I were right, I could not answer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I could only beg my Judge for mercy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I summoned Him and He answered me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I do not believe He would listen to my voice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For He would crush me with a tempest</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and multiply my wounds without cause.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not let me catch my breath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but overwhelms me with bitterness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">If it is a matter of strength,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is indeed mighty!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If it is a matter of justice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who can summon Him</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See LXX; Hebrew me">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if I were righteous, my mouth would condemn me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if I were blameless, it would declare me guilty.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or He would declare me guilty">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though I am blameless, I have no concern for myself;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I despise my own life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is all the same, and so I say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the scourge brings sudden death,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He mocks the despair of the innocent.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The earth is given into the hand of the wicked;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He blindfolds its judges.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If it is not He, then who is it?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">My days are swifter than a runner;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they flee without seeing good.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sweep by like boats of papyrus,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like an eagle swooping down on its prey.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I were to say, ‘I will forget my complaint</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and change my expression and smile,’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I would still dread all my sufferings;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I know that You will not acquit me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Since I am already found guilty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">why should I labor in vain?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I should wash myself with snow</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or soap">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cleanse my hands with lye,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">then You would plunge me into the pit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and even my own clothes would despise me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For He is not a man like me, that I can answer Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that we can take each other to court.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nor is there a mediator between us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to lay his hand upon us both.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let Him remove His rod from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that His terror will no longer frighten me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I would speak without fear of Him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But as it is, I am on my own.</span></span>
