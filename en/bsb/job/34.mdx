<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you have understanding, hear this;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">listen to my words.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">who is not partial to princes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and does not favor rich over poor?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For they are all the work of His hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They die in an instant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the middle of the night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The people convulse and pass away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the mighty are removed without human hand.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no darkness or deep shadow</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where the workers of iniquity can hide.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For God need not examine a man further</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or have him approach for judgment.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shatters the mighty without inquiry</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sets up others in their place.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, He recognizes their deeds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He overthrows them in the night and they are crushed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He strikes them for their wickedness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in full view,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They caused the cry of the poor to come before Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He heard the outcry of the afflicted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when He remains silent, who can condemn Him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When He hides His face, who can see Him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet He watches over both man and nation,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> <span className="pl pl-2">that godless men should not rule</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or lay snares for the people.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Suppose someone says to God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘I have endured my punishment; I will offend no more.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Teach me what I cannot see;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should God repay you on your own terms</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when you have disavowed Him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You must choose, not I;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so tell me what you know.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men of understanding will declare to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wise men who hear me will say:</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">If only Job were tried to the utmost</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for answering like a wicked man.</span></span>
