## The Day of the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8; 2 Peter 3:7; Malachi 3:2"> “For behold, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, when all the arrogant and every evildoer will be stubble; the day is coming when I will set them ablaze,” says the LORD of Hosts. “Not a root or branch will be left to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 84:11; Isaiah 30:26"> “But for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the Sun of Righteousness will rise with healing in His wings">a</a></sup>and you will go out and leap like calves from the stall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:12; Zechariah 10:5; Romans 16:20"> Then you will trample the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day I am preparing,” says the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; James 2:9–13; Luke 16:29–31"> “Remember the law of My servant Moses, the statutes and ordinances I commanded him for all Israel at Horeb.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:17; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and awesome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or dreadful; LXX glorious">c</a></sup>Day of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 1:16–17; Isaiah 11:4; Hebrews 10:26–31"> And he will turn the hearts of the fathers to their children, and the hearts of the children to their fathers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Luke 1:17">d</a></sup>Otherwise, I will come and strike the land with a curse.”</span>
</div>
</div>
