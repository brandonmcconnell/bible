## The LORD’s Love for Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Haggai 1:1"> This is the burden of the word of the LORD to Israel through Malachi:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Malachi means My messenger.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Jeremiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 10:15"> “I have loved you,” says the LORD.<br />But you ask, “How have You loved us?”<br />“Was not Esau Jacob’s brother?” declares the LORD. “Yet Jacob I have loved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10; Ezekiel 35:3–4; Jeremiah 49:16–18"> but Esau I have hated,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Romans 9:13">b</a></sup>and I have made his mountains a wasteland and left his inheritance to the desert jackals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or to the serpents of the wilderness or to the dragons of the wilderness">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 35:9; Ezekiel 11:10"> Though Edom may say, “We have been devastated, but we will rebuild the ruins,” this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “They may build, but I will demolish. They will be called the Land of Wickedness, and a people with whom the LORD is indignant forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:7; Psalms 58:10–11; Ezekiel 38:16"> You will see this with your own eyes, and you yourselves will say, ‘The LORD is great—even beyond the borders of Israel.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Polluted Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:46; Isaiah 1:2; John 13:13–17"> “A son honors his father, and a servant his master. But if I am a father, where is My honor? And if I am a master, where is your fear of Me?” says the LORD of Hosts to you priests who despise My name.<br />“But you ask, ‘How have we despised Your name?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:27–32; Malachi 1:8"> By presenting defiled food on My altar.<br />But you ask, ‘How have we defiled You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX defiled it">d</a></sup>?’<br />By saying that the table of the LORD is contemptible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Leviticus 22:19–25; Hosea 8:13"> When you offer blind animals for sacrifice, is it not wrong? And when you present the lame and sick ones, is it not wrong? Try offering them to your governor! Would he be pleased with you or show you favor?” asks the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Jeremiah 27:18; 1 Peter 1:17"> “But ask now for God’s favor. Will He be gracious? Since this has come from your hands, will He show you favor?” asks the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:21–24; Jeremiah 6:20"> “Oh, that one of you would shut the temple doors, so that you would no longer kindle useless fires on My altar! I take no pleasure in you,” says the LORD of Hosts, “and I will accept no offering from your hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 54:5; Isaiah 66:19–20"> For My name will be great among the nations, from where the sun rises to where it sets. In every place, incense and pure offerings will be presented in My name, because My name will be great among the nations,” says the LORD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 1:6–8; Malachi 2:8; Amos 2:7"> “But you profane it when you say, ‘The table of the Lord is defiled, and as for its fruit, its food is contemptible.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Leviticus 22:19–23; Mark 14:37–38"> You also say: ‘Oh, what a nuisance!’ And you turn up your nose at it,” says the LORD of Hosts.<br />“You bring offerings that are stolen, lame, or sick! Should I accept these from your hands?” asks the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 5:1–10; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> “But cursed is the deceiver who has an acceptable male in his flock and vows to give it, but sacrifices a defective animal to the Lord. For I am a great King,” says the LORD of Hosts, “and My name is to be feared among the nations.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
