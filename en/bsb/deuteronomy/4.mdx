## An Exhortation to Obedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5"> Hear now, O Israel, the statutes and ordinances I am teaching you to follow, so that you may live and may enter and take possession of the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Proverbs 30:6; Revelation 22:18–19"> You must not add to or subtract from what I command you, so that you may keep the commandments of the LORD your God that I am giving you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; Psalms 106:28–29; Joshua 22:17"> Your eyes have seen what the LORD did at Baal-peor, for the LORD your God destroyed from among you all who followed Baal of Peor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 23:8; John 6:67–69"> But you who held fast to the LORD your God are alive to this day, every one of you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2; Hebrews 3:5"> See, I have taught you statutes and ordinances just as the LORD my God has commanded me, so that you may follow them in the land that you are about to enter and possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Observe them carefully, for this will show your wisdom and understanding in the sight of the peoples, who will hear of all these statutes and say, “Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; 2 Samuel 7:23; Psalms 34:18"> For what nation is great enough to have a god as near to them as the LORD our God is to us whenever we call on Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 7:12–14; Psalms 119:127–128"> And what nation is great enough to have righteous statutes and ordinances like this entire law I set before you today?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 78:3–8"> Only be on your guard and diligently watch yourselves, so that you do not forget the things your eyes have seen, and so that they do not slip from your heart as long as you live. Teach them to your children and grandchildren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Hebrews 12:18–19; Exodus 19:16"> The day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai; also in verse 15">a</a></sup>the LORD said to me, “Gather the people before Me to hear My words, so that they may learn to fear Me all the days they live on the earth, and that they may teach them to their children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:23; Exodus 19:16–18; Hebrews 12:18–19"> You came near and stood at the base of the mountain, a mountain blazing with fire to the heavens, with black clouds and deep darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:15; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And the LORD spoke to you out of the fire. You heard the sound of the words but saw no form; there was only a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 9:9–11"> He declared to you His covenant, which He commanded you to follow—the Ten Commandments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew the Ten Words">b</a></sup>that He wrote on two tablets of stone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:44–45; Ezekiel 21:1–23"> At that time the LORD commanded me to teach you the statutes and ordinances you are to follow in the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning against Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:12; Isaiah 40:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> So since you saw no form of any kind on the day the LORD spoke to you out of the fire at Horeb, be careful</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Deuteronomy 5:8; Exodus 20:4–5"> that you do not act corruptly and make an idol for yourselves of any form or shape, whether in the likeness of a male or female,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:23"> of any beast that is on the earth or bird that flies in the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> or of any creature that crawls on the ground or fish that is in the waters below.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:25; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Kings 21:3"> When you look to the heavens and see the sun and moon and stars—all the host of heaven—do not be enticed to bow down and worship what the LORD your God has apportioned to all the nations under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 8:51; Jeremiah 11:4; Exodus 19:5–6"> Yet the LORD has taken you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be the people of His inheritance, as you are today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:12; Psalms 106:32–33"> The LORD, however, was angry with me on account of you, and He swore that I would not cross the Jordan to enter the good land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:25; Deuteronomy 3:27; 2 Peter 1:13–15"> For I will not be crossing the Jordan, because I must die in this land. But you shall cross over and take possession of that good land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:9; Hebrews 3:12; Joshua 23:16"> Be careful that you do not forget the covenant of the LORD your God that He made with you; do not make an idol for yourselves in the form of anything He has forbidden you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Nahum 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:3"> For the LORD your God is a consuming fire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 12:29">c</a></sup>a jealous God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:16; Hosea 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> After you have children and grandchildren and you have been in the land a long time, if you then act corruptly and make an idol of any form—doing evil in the sight of the LORD your God and provoking Him to anger—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 6:2; Jeremiah 22:29"> I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you this day that you will quickly perish from the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess. You will not live long upon it, but will be utterly destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:62–64; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 12:15"> Then the LORD will scatter you among the peoples, and only a few of you will survive among the nations to which the LORD will drive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And there you will serve man-made gods of wood and stone, which cannot see or hear or eat or smell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Jeremiah 29:12–14; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> But if from there you will seek the LORD your God, you will find Him if you seek Him with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5"> When you are in distress and all these things have happened to you, then in later days you will return to the LORD your God and listen to His voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Psalms 116:5; Nehemiah 9:31"> For the LORD your God is a merciful God; He will not abandon you or destroy you or forget the covenant with your fathers, which He swore to them by oath.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Alone Is God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 8:8; Matthew 24:31; Psalms 44:1"> Indeed, ask now from one end of the heavens to the other about the days that long preceded you, from the day that God created man on earth: Has anything as great as this ever happened or been reported?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Deuteronomy 5:24; Exodus 33:20"> Has a people ever heard the voice of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or of a god">d</a></sup>speaking out of the fire, as you have, and lived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 6:6"> Or has any god tried to take as his own a nation out of another nation—by trials, signs, wonders, and war, by a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and by great terrors—as the LORD your God did for you in Egypt, before your eyes?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 45:18; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 45:5"> You were shown these things so that you would know that the LORD is God; there is no other besides Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Exodus 19:19; Deuteronomy 4:33"> He let you hear His voice from heaven to discipline you, and on earth He showed you His great fire, and you heard His words out of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 13:9"> Because He loved your fathers, He chose their descendants after them and brought you out of Egypt by His presence and great power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Deuteronomy 9:1–5; Exodus 23:27–28"> to drive out before you nations greater and mightier than you, and to bring you into their land and give it to you for your inheritance, as it is this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:35; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> Know therefore this day and take to heart that the LORD is God in heaven above and on the earth below; there is no other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:3; Ephesians 6:3; Deuteronomy 12:28"> Keep His statutes and commandments, which I am giving you today, so that you and your children after you may prosper, and that you may live long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you for all time.</span>
</div>
</div>
