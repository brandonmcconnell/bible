## The Altar on Mount Ebal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> Then Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people: “Keep all the commandments I am giving you today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> And on the day you cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD your God is giving you, set up large stones and coat them with plaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> Write on them all the words of this law when you have crossed over to enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> And when you have crossed the Jordan, you are to set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I am commanding you today, and you are to coat them with plaster.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> Moreover, you are to build there an altar to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. You must not use any iron tool on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> You shall build the altar of the LORD your God with uncut stones and offer upon it burnt offerings to the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> There you are to sacrifice your peace offerings, eating them and rejoicing in the presence of the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> And you shall write distinctly upon these stones all the words of this law.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10–11; Deuteronomy 26:16–18; Ephesians 5:8–9"> Then Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel: “Be silent, O Israel, and listen! This day you have become the people of the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> You shall therefore obey the voice of the LORD your God and follow His commandments and statutes I am giving you today.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Curses Pronounced from Ebal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> On that day Moses commanded the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> “When you have crossed the Jordan, these tribes shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And these tribes shall stand on Mount Ebal to deliver the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> Then the Levites shall proclaim in a loud voice to every Israelite:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> ‘Cursed is the man who makes a carved idol or molten image—an abomination to the LORD, the work of the hands of a craftsman—and sets it up in secret.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> ‘Cursed is he who dishonors his father or mother.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> ‘Cursed is he who moves his neighbor’s boundary stone.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> ‘Cursed is he who lets a blind man wander in the road.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> ‘Cursed is he who withholds justice from the foreigner, the fatherless, or the widow.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> ‘Cursed is he who sleeps with his father’s wife, for he has violated his father’s marriage bed.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or uncovered his father’s skirt">a</a></sup>And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> ‘Cursed is he who lies with any animal.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> ‘Cursed is he who sleeps with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> ‘Cursed is he who sleeps with his mother-in-law.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> ‘Cursed is he who strikes down his neighbor in secret.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> ‘Cursed is he who accepts a bribe to kill an innocent person.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> ‘Cursed is he who does not put the words of this law into practice.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX Cursed is every man who does not continue in all the words of this law; cited in Galatians 3:10">b</a></sup>And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
</div>
