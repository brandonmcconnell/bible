<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:6; Isaiah 57:3; Hebrews 12:8"> No one of illegitimate birth may enter the assembly of the LORD, nor may any of his descendants, even to the tenth generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> No Ammonite or Moabite or any of their descendants may enter the assembly of the LORD, even to the tenth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> For they did not meet you with food and water on your way out of Egypt, and they hired Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram-naharaim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Mesopotamia; Aram-naharaim means Aram of the two rivers, likely the region between the Euphrates and Balih Rivers in northwestern Mesopotamia.">a</a></sup>to curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> Yet the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam, and the LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loves you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> You are not to seek peace or prosperity from them as long as you live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> Do not despise an Edomite, for he is your brother. Do not despise an Egyptian, because you lived as a foreigner in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> The third generation of children born to them may enter the assembly of the LORD.</span>
</div>

## Uncleanness in the Camp

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When you are encamped against your enemies, then you shall keep yourself from every wicked thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If any man among you becomes unclean because of a nocturnal emission, he must leave the camp and stay outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> When evening approaches, he must wash with water, and when the sun sets he may return to the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You must have a place outside the camp to go and relieve yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> And you must have a digging tool in your equipment so that when you relieve yourself you can dig a hole and cover up your excrement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> For the LORD your God walks throughout your camp to protect you and deliver your enemies to you. Your camp must be holy, lest He see anything unclean among you and turn away from you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Miscellaneous Laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> Do not return a slave to his master if he has taken refuge with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> Let him live among you wherever he chooses, in the town of his pleasing. Do not oppress him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; 1 Kings 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> No daughter or son of Israel is to be a shrine prostitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> You must not bring the wages of a prostitute, whether female or male,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew or a dog">b</a></sup>into the house of the LORD your God to fulfill any vow, because both are detestable to the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> Do not charge your brother interest on money, food, or any other type of loan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You may charge a foreigner interest, but not your brother, so that the LORD your God may bless you in everything to which you put your hand in the land that you are entering to possess.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> If you make a vow to the LORD your God, do not be slow to keep it, because He will surely require it of you, and you will be guilty of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if you refrain from making a vow, you will not be guilty of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Be careful to follow through on what comes from your lips, because you have freely vowed to the LORD your God with your own mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> When you enter your neighbor’s vineyard, you may eat your fill of grapes, but you must not put any in your basket.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–2; Mark 2:23; Luke 6:1–2"> When you enter your neighbor’s grainfield, you may pluck the heads of grain with your hand, but you must not put a sickle to your neighbor’s grain.</span>
</div>
</div>
