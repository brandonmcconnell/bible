## Moses Blesses the Twelve Tribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the LORD became King in Jeshurun</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jeshurun means the upright one, a term of endearment for Israel; also in verse 26.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when the leaders of the people gathered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when the tribes of Israel came together.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let Reuben live and not die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or but let">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his men be few.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalms 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> And concerning Judah he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“O LORD, hear the cry of Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bring him to his people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With his own hands he defends his cause,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but may You be a help against his foes.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> Concerning Levi he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Give Your Thummim to Levi</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your Urim to Your godly one,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="DSS and LXX; MT does not include to Levi; literally Let Your Perfections and Your Lights be to Your godly one.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whom You tested at Massah</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Massah means testing; see Exodus 17:7.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and contested at the waters of Meribah.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Meribah means quarreling; see Exodus 17:7.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> <span className="pl pl-1">He said of his father and mother,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘I do not consider them.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He disregarded his brothers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and did not know his own sons,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he kept</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew they kept, most likely referring to Levi in the plural; similarly twice in verse 10">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your word</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and maintained Your covenant.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will teach Your ordinances to Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your law to Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will set incense before You</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whole burnt offerings on Your altar.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bless his substance, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and accept the work of his hands.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Smash the loins of those who rise against him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and of his foes so they can rise no more.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> Concerning Benjamin he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“May the beloved of the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rest secure in Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God shields</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew He shields">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">him all day long,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and upon His shoulders he rests.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> Concerning Joseph he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“May his land be blessed by the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the precious dew from heaven above</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the deep waters that lie beneath,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">with the bountiful harvest from the sun</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the abundant yield of the seasons,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">with the best of the ancient mountains</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the bounty of the everlasting hills,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">with the choice gifts of the land and everything in it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and with the favor of Him who dwelt in the burning bush.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May these rest on the head of Joseph</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and crown the brow of the prince of his brothers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">His majesty is like a firstborn bull,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his horns are like those of a wild ox.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With them he will gore the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even to the ends of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Such are the myriads of Ephraim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and such are the thousands of Manasseh.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> Concerning Zebulun he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, Zebulun, in your journeys,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Issachar, in your tents.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will call the peoples to a mountain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there they will offer sacrifices of righteousness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they will feast on the abundance of the seas</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hidden treasures of the sand.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> Concerning Gad he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed is he who enlarges</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the domain of Gad!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He lies down like a lion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and tears off an arm or a head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He chose the best land for himself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because a ruler’s portion was reserved for him there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He came with the leaders of the people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he administered the LORD’s justice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and His ordinances for Israel.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> Concerning Dan he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Dan is a lion’s cub,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">leaping out of Bashan.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> Concerning Naphtali he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Naphtali is abounding with favor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">full of the blessing of the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he shall take possession</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the sea</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or the west">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the south.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And concerning Asher he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“May Asher be the most blessed of sons;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may he be the most favored among his brothers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and dip his foot in oil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the bolts of your gate be iron and bronze,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and may your strength match your days.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There is none like the God of Jeshurun,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who rides the heavens to your aid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the clouds in His majesty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The eternal God is your dwelling place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and underneath are the everlasting arms.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He drives out the enemy before you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">giving the command, ‘Destroy him!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">So Israel dwells securely;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fountain of Jacob lives untroubled</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in a land of grain and new wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where even the heavens drip with dew.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed are you, O Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is the shield that protects you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the sword in which you boast.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your enemies will cower before you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you shall trample their high places.”</span></span>
