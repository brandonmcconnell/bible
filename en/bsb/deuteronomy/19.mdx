## Cities of Refuge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29; Deuteronomy 6:10; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> When the LORD your God has cut off the nations whose land He is giving you, and when you have driven them out and settled in their cities and houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2–7; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> then you are to set apart for yourselves three cities within the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Hebrews 12:13; Isaiah 35:8"> You are to build roads for yourselves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or You are to survey the way">a</a></sup>and divide into three regions the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, so that any manslayer can flee to these cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 35:15–24; Deuteronomy 4:42; Isaiah 30:33"> Now this is the situation regarding the manslayer who flees to one of these cities to save his life, having killed his neighbor accidentally, without intending to harm him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Proverbs 27:12; 2 Kings 6:5–7"> If he goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut timber and swings his axe to chop down a tree, but the blade flies off the handle and strikes and kills his neighbor, he may flee to one of these cities to save his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; 2 Samuel 14:7; Deuteronomy 21:22"> Otherwise, the avenger of blood might pursue the manslayer in a rage, overtake him if the distance is great, and strike him dead though he did not deserve to die, since he did not intend any harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> This is why I am commanding you to set apart for yourselves three cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:20; Exodus 34:24; Exodus 23:31"> And if the LORD your God enlarges your territory, as He swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land He promised them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 20:7–8; Deuteronomy 11:22–25; Deuteronomy 12:32"> and if you carefully keep all these commandments I am giving you today, loving the LORD your God and walking in His ways at all times, then you are to add three more cities to these three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 27:4–5"> Thus innocent blood will not be shed in the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, so that you will not be guilty of bloodshed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:16–21; Deuteronomy 27:24; Genesis 9:6"> If, however, a man hates his neighbor and lies in wait, attacks him and kills him, and then flees to one of these cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; 1 Kings 2:5–6"> the elders of his city must send for him, bring him back, and hand him over to the avenger of blood to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 2:31; Deuteronomy 21:9; Deuteronomy 7:16"> You must show him no pity. You are to purge from Israel the guilt of shedding innocent blood, that it may go well with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10"> You must not move your neighbor’s boundary marker, which was set up by your ancestors to mark the inheritance you shall receive in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Testimony of Two or Three Witnesses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Deuteronomy 17:6; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> A lone witness is not sufficient to establish any wrongdoing or sin against a man, regardless of what offense he may have committed. A matter must be established by the testimony of two or three witnesses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 18:16 and 2 Corinthians 13:1">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11; Exodus 23:1–7"> If a false witness testifies against someone, accusing him of a crime,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:9; Deuteronomy 21:5; Malachi 2:7"> both parties to the dispute must stand in the presence of the LORD, before the priests and judges who are in office at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 17:4; 2 Chronicles 19:6–7"> The judges shall investigate thoroughly, and if the witness is proven to be a liar who has falsely accused his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9; Daniel 6:24"> you must do to him as he intended to do to his brother. So you must purge the evil from among you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Cited in 1 Corinthians 5:13">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Deuteronomy 17:13; Deuteronomy 13:11"> Then the rest of the people will hear and be afraid, and they will never again do anything so evil among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:38–39; Exodus 21:23–25; Deuteronomy 19:13"> You must show no pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 5:38">d</a></sup>hand for hand, and foot for foot.</span>
</div>
</div>
