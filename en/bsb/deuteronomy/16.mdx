## Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 12:2–20; Exodus 13:4"> Observe the month of Abib<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Abib was the first month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April; twice in this verse.">a</a></sup>and celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, because in the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt by night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 12:26; Luke 22:15"> You are to offer to the LORD your God the Passover sacrifice from the herd or flock in the place the LORD will choose as a dwelling for His Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:39; Exodus 34:18; Exodus 12:19–20"> You must not eat leavened bread with it; for seven days you are to eat with it unleavened bread, the bread of affliction, because you left the land of Egypt in haste—so that you may remember for the rest of your life the day you left the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Exodus 12:10; Exodus 13:7"> No leaven is to be found in all your land for seven days, and none of the meat you sacrifice in the evening of the first day shall remain until morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; Deuteronomy 12:5–6"> You are not to sacrifice the Passover animal in any of the towns that the LORD your God is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Exodus 12:6–9; Numbers 9:11"> You must only offer the Passover sacrifice at the place the LORD your God will choose as a dwelling for His Name. Do this in the evening as the sun sets, at the same time you departed from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Exodus 12:8–9; John 2:23"> And you shall roast it and eat it in the place the LORD your God will choose, and in the morning you shall return to your tents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:6–8; Leviticus 23:6–8; Leviticus 23:36"> For six days you must eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day you shall hold a solemn assembly to the LORD your God, and you must not do any work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feast of Weeks

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 23:16; Acts 2:1"> You are to count off seven weeks from the time you first put the sickle to the standing grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Leviticus 12:8; Malachi 3:10–11"> And you shall celebrate the Feast of Weeks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Shavuot; see footnotes for verse 16.">b</a></sup>to the LORD your God with a freewill offering that you give in proportion to how the LORD your God has blessed you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 12:18"> and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God in the place He will choose as a dwelling for His Name—you, your sons and daughters, your menservants and maidservants, and the Levite within your gates, as well as the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widows among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:15; Lamentations 3:19–20; Romans 6:17–18"> Remember that you were slaves in Egypt, and carefully follow these statutes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feast of Tabernacles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; John 7:2; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> You are to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or Booths or Shelters; see footnotes for verse 16.">c</a></sup>for seven days after you have gathered the produce of your threshing floor and your winepress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9–12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Isaiah 30:29"> And you shall rejoice in your feast—you, your sons and daughters, your menservants and maidservants, and the Levite, as well as the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widows among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36–42; Deuteronomy 30:16; Deuteronomy 16:10"> For seven days you shall celebrate a feast to the LORD your God in the place He will choose, because the LORD your God will bless you in all your produce and in all the work of your hands, so that your joy will be complete.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Psalms 96:8; Haggai 1:9"> Three times a year all your men are to appear before the LORD your God in the place He will choose: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.">d</a></sup>the Feast of Weeks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).">e</a></sup>and the Feast of Tabernacles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as the Feast of Booths or the Feast of Shelters and originally called the Feast of Ingathering (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).">f</a></sup>No one should appear before the LORD empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–7; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Deuteronomy 16:10"> Everyone must appear with a gift as he is able, according to the blessing the LORD your God has given you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judges and Justice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Exodus 18:25–26; Romans 13:1–6"> You are to appoint judges and officials for your tribes in every town that the LORD your God is giving you. They are to judge the people with righteous judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:23"> Do not deny justice or show partiality. Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and twists the words of the righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Ezekiel 18:9; Ezekiel 18:5"> Pursue justice, and justice alone, so that you may live, and you may possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Forbidden Forms of Worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:3; 2 Kings 17:16; Exodus 34:13"> Do not set up any wooden Asherah pole next to the altar you will build for the LORD your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:4; Zechariah 8:17"> and do not set up for yourselves a sacred pillar, which the LORD your God hates.</span>
</div>
</div>
