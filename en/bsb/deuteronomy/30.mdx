## The Promise of Restoration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; 1 Kings 8:47–48; Deuteronomy 30:19"> “When all these things come upon you—the blessings and curses I have set before you—and you call them to mind in all the nations to which the LORD your God has banished you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Nehemiah 1:9; Deuteronomy 4:28–31"> and when you and your children return to the LORD your God and obey His voice with all your heart and all your soul according to everything I am giving you today,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Ezekiel 36:24; Psalms 147:2"> then He will restore you from captivity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or restore your fortunes">a</a></sup>and have compassion on you and gather you from all the nations to which the LORD your God has scattered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:9; Ezekiel 39:25–29; Deuteronomy 28:64"> Even if you have been banished to the farthest horizon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or to the extremity of the heavens">b</a></sup>He will gather you and return you from there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Cited in Nehemiah 1:8–9">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14"> And the LORD your God will bring you into the land your fathers possessed, and you will take possession of it. He will cause you to prosper and multiply more than your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 32:39"> The LORD your God will circumcise your hearts and the hearts of your descendants, and you will love Him with all your heart and with all your soul, so that you may live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Lamentations 4:21–22; Jeremiah 51:24–26"> Then the LORD your God will put all these curses upon your enemies who hate you and persecute you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:16; Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> And you will again obey the voice of the LORD and follow all His commandments I am giving you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:11–14; Jeremiah 32:41; Jeremiah 33:9"> So the LORD your God will make you abound in all the work of your hands and in the fruit of your womb, the offspring of your livestock, and the produce of your land. Indeed, the LORD will again delight in your prosperity, as He delighted in that of your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Deuteronomy 4:29; Nehemiah 1:9"> if you obey the LORD your God by keeping His commandments and statutes that are written in this Book of the Law, and if you turn to Him with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Choice of Life or Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:19; Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 16:25–26"> For this commandment I give you today is not too difficult for you or beyond your reach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 10:6–8; Proverbs 30:4; John 3:13"> It is not in heaven, that you should need to ask, ‘Who will ascend into heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Romans 10:6">d</a></sup>to get it for us and proclaim it, that we may obey it?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:33; Matthew 12:42; Proverbs 2:1–5"> And it is not beyond the sea, that you should need to ask, ‘Who will cross the sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Romans 10:7.">e</a></sup>to get it for us and proclaim it, that we may obey it?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 10:8–10; Jeremiah 12:2; Matthew 7:21"> But the word is very near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Cited in Romans 10:8">f</a></sup>so that you may obey it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:26; Deuteronomy 30:19; 1 John 5:11–12"> See, I have set before you today life and prosperity, as well as death and disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Matthew 22:37–38; 1 John 5:2–3"> For I am commanding you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in His ways, and to keep His commandments, statutes, and ordinances, so that you may live and increase, and the LORD your God may bless you in the land that you are entering to possess.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Hebrews 3:12; Proverbs 1:32"> But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, but are drawn away to bow down to other gods and worship them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Isaiah 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I declare to you today that you will surely perish; you shall not prolong your days in the land that you are crossing the Jordan to possess.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26; Psalms 119:30"> I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you today that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing. Therefore choose life, so that you and your descendants may live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 17:3"> and that you may love the LORD your God, obey Him, and hold fast to Him. For He is your life, and He will prolong your life in the land that the LORD swore to give to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”</span>
</div>
</div>
