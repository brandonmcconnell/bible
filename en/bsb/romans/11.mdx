## A Remnant Chosen by Grace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> I ask then, did God reject His people? Certainly not! I am an Israelite myself, a descendant of Abraham, from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God did not reject His people, whom He foreknew. Do you not know what the Scripture says about Elijah, how he appealed to God against Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed Your prophets and torn down Your altars. I am the only one left, and they are seeking my life as well”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1 Kings 19:10, 14">a</a></sup>?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> And what was the divine reply to him? “I have reserved for Myself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 Kings 19:18">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> In the same way, at the present time there is a remnant chosen by grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> And if it is by grace, then it is no longer by works. Otherwise, grace would no longer be grace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include But if it is by works, then it is no longer grace; otherwise work is no longer work.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? What Israel was seeking, it failed to obtain, but the elect did. The others were hardened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God gave them a spirit of stupor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">eyes that could not see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ears that could not hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to this very day.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 29:4 and Isaiah 29:10.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> And David says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“May their table become a snare and a trap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a stumbling block and a retribution to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">May their eyes be darkened so they cannot see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their backs be bent forever.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Psalms 69:22–23 (see also LXX)">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ingrafting of the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I ask then, did they stumble so as to fall beyond recovery?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or did they stumble so as to lose their share? Literally did they stumble that they might fall?">f</a></sup>Certainly not! However, because of their trespass, salvation has come to the Gentiles to make Israel jealous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> But if their trespass means riches for the world, and their failure means riches for the Gentiles, how much greater riches will their fullness bring!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> I am speaking to you Gentiles. Inasmuch as I am the apostle to the Gentiles, I magnify my ministry</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> in the hope that I may provoke my own people to jealousy and save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if their rejection is the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance be but life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> If the first part of the dough is holy, so is the whole batch; if the root is holy, so are the branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> Now if some branches have been broken off, and you, a wild olive shoot, have been grafted in among the others to share in the nourishment of the olive root,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not boast over those branches. If you do, remember this: You do not support the root, but the root supports you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> You will say then, “Branches were broken off so that I could be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> That is correct: They were broken off because of unbelief, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> For if God did not spare the natural branches, He will certainly not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or He will perhaps not; SBL, NE, and WH He will not">g</a></sup>spare you either.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Take notice, therefore, of the kindness and severity of God: severity to those who fell, but kindness to you, if you continue in His kindness. Otherwise you also will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And if they do not persist in unbelief, they will be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if you were cut from a wild olive tree, and contrary to nature were grafted into one that is cultivated, how much more readily will these, the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree!</span>
</div>
</div>

## All Israel Will Be Saved

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> I do not want you to be ignorant of this mystery, brothers, so that you will not be conceited: A hardening in part has come to Israel, until the full number of the Gentiles has come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> And so all Israel will be saved, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Deliverer will come from Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will remove godlessness from Jacob.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">And this is My covenant with them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I take away their sins.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isaiah 27:9 and Isaiah 59:20–21; see also LXX">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> Regarding the gospel, they are enemies on your account; but regarding election, they are loved on account of the patriarchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For God’s gifts and His call are irrevocable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> Just as you who formerly disobeyed God have now received mercy through their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so they too have now disobeyed, in order that they too may now receive mercy through the mercy shown to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally may now receive mercy through your mercy; BYZ and TR may receive mercy through your mercy">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For God has consigned everyone to disobedience so that He may have mercy on everyone.</span>
</div>
</div>
