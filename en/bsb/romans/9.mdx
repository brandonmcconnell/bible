## Paul’s Concern for the Jews

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I speak the truth in Christ; I am not lying, as confirmed by my conscience in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> I have deep sorrow and unceasing anguish in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I could wish that I myself were cursed and cut off from Christ for the sake of my brothers, my own flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> the people of Israel. Theirs is the adoption as sons; theirs the divine glory and the covenants; theirs the giving of the law, the temple worship, and the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> Theirs are the patriarchs, and from them proceeds the human descent of Christ, who is God over all, forever worthy of praise!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or forever blessed">a</a></sup>Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Sovereign Choice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> It is not as though God’s word has failed. For not all who are descended from Israel are Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> Nor because they are Abraham’s descendants are they all his children. On the contrary, “Through Isaac your offspring will be reckoned.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Genesis 21:12">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> So it is not the children of the flesh who are God’s children, but it is the children of the promise who are regarded as offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is what the promise stated: “At the appointed time I will return, and Sarah will have a son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Genesis 18:14">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> Not only that, but Rebecca’s children were conceived by one man, our father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> Yet before the twins were born or had done anything good or bad, in order that God’s plan of election might stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> not by works but by Him who calls, she was told, “The older will serve the younger.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Genesis 25:23">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> So it is written: “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Malachi 1:2–3">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What then shall we say? Is God unjust? Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For He says to Moses:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will have mercy on whom I have mercy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Exodus 33:19">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then, it does not depend on man’s desire or effort, but on God’s mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the Scripture says to Pharaoh: “I raised you up for this very purpose, that I might display My power in you, and that My name might be proclaimed in all the earth.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Exodus 9:16 (see also LXX)">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> Therefore God has mercy on whom He wants to have mercy, and He hardens whom He wants to harden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> One of you will say to me, “Then why does God still find fault? For who can resist His will?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> But who are you, O man, to talk back to God? Shall what is formed say to Him who formed it, “Why did You make me like this?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Does not the potter have the right to make from the same lump of clay one vessel for special occasions and another for common use?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> What if God, intending to show His wrath and make His power known, bore with great patience the vessels of His wrath, prepared for destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> What if He did this to make the riches of His glory known to the vessels of His mercy, whom He prepared in advance for glory—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> including us, whom He has called not only from the Jews, but also from the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As He says in Hosea:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will call them ‘My People’ who are not My people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will call her ‘My Beloved’ who is not My beloved,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hosea 2:23">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“It will happen that in the very place where it was said to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘You are not My people,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will be called</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘sons of the living God.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hosea 1:10">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> Isaiah cries out concerning Israel:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Though the number of the Israelites is like the sand of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">only the remnant will be saved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord will carry out His sentence on the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thoroughly and decisively.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Isaiah 10:22–23 (see also LXX)">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> It is just as Isaiah foretold:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Unless the Lord of Hosts had left us descendants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we would have become like Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we would have resembled Gomorrah.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Isaiah 1:9 (see also LXX)">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel’s Unbelief

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What then will we say? That the Gentiles, who did not pursue righteousness, have obtained it, a righteousness that is by faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> but Israel, who pursued a law of righteousness, has not attained it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why not? Because their pursuit was not by faith, but as if it were by works. They stumbled over the stumbling stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“See, I lay in Zion a stone of stumbling</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a rock of offense;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Isaiah 8:14">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one who believes in Him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never be put to shame.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Isaiah 28:16 (see also LXX)">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
