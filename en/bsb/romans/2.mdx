## God’s Righteous Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:3; John 8:7–9"> You, therefore, have no excuse, you who pass judgment on another. For on whatever grounds you judge the other, you are condemning yourself, because you who pass judgment do the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> And we know that God’s judgment against those who do such things is based on truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; Luke 12:14; Romans 1:32–1"> So when you, O man, pass judgment on others, yet do the same things, do you think you will escape God’s judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> Or do you disregard the riches of His kindness, tolerance, and patience, not realizing that God’s kindness leads you to repentance?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 1:18; Romans 2:2–3"> But because of your hard and unrepentant heart, you are storing up wrath against yourself for the day of wrath, when God’s righteous judgment will be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; Matthew 16:27"> God “will repay each one according to his deeds.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 62:12">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7–8; Luke 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:10"> To those who by perseverance in doing good seek glory, honor, and immortality, He will give eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:18–21; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; 1 Timothy 6:3–4"> But for those who are self-seeking and who reject the truth and follow wickedness, there will be wrath and anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:16"> There will be trouble and distress for every human being who does evil, first for the Jew, then for the Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but glory, honor, and peace for everyone who does good, first for the Jew, then for the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> For God does not show favoritism.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 3:10; Galatians 2:16–19"> All who sin apart from the law will also perish apart from the law, and all who sin under the law will be judged by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:22–25; 1 John 3:7; James 2:21–25"> For it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but it is the doers of the law who will be declared righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 1:19–20; Romans 2:27"> Indeed, when Gentiles, who do not have the law, do by nature what the law requires, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Romans 1:18–19"> So they show that the work of the law is written on their hearts, their consciences also bearing witness, and their thoughts either accusing or defending them</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Acts 17:31"> on the day when God will judge men’s secrets through Christ Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR through Jesus Christ">b</a></sup>as proclaimed by my gospel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Jews and the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> Now you, if you call yourself a Jew; if you rely on the law and boast in God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; James 4:17"> if you know His will and approve of what is superior because you are instructed by the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 3:17–18; Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> if you are convinced that you are a guide for the blind, a light for those in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 1:13"> an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of infants, because you have in the law the embodiment of knowledge and truth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:27; Matthew 23:3–28; Luke 12:47"> you, then, who teach others, do you not teach yourself? You who preach against stealing, do you steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; Matthew 12:39; Mark 11:17"> You who forbid adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 4:16–17; Romans 2:17; Romans 3:2"> You who boast in the law, do you dishonor God by breaking the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; 2 Peter 2:2"> As it is written: “God’s name is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaiah 52:5 (see also LXX)">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Galatians 5:3–6; Acts 7:51"> Circumcision has value if you observe the law, but if you break the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 7:18–19; Romans 8:4"> If a man who is not circumcised keeps the requirements of the law, will not his uncircumcision be regarded as circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; Romans 2:29; 2 Corinthians 3:6"> The one who is physically uncircumcised yet keeps the law will condemn you who, even though you have the written code and circumcision, are a lawbreaker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Matthew 3:9; Romans 9:6–8"> A man is not a Jew because he is one outwardly, nor is circumcision only outward and physical.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Philippians 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> No, a man is a Jew because he is one inwardly, and circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the written code. Such a man’s praise does not come from men, but from God.</span>
</div>
</div>
