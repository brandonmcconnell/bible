## Abraham Justified by Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:2; Romans 4:16; 2 Corinthians 11:22"> What then shall we say that Abraham, our forefather according to the flesh, has discovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:31; 2 Corinthians 11:30; Philippians 3:9"> If Abraham was indeed justified by works, he had something to boast about, but not before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–8"> For what does the Scripture say? “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Genesis 15:6">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Romans 9:32; Romans 11:35"> Now the wages of the worker are not credited as a gift, but as an obligation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Romans 3:22; Titus 3:3–7"> However, to the one who does not work, but believes in Him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is credited as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> And David speaks likewise of the blessedness of the man to whom God credits righteousness apart from works:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 32:1–2; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 7:47–50"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed are they whose lawless acts are forgiven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose sins are covered.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 32:2; 1 Peter 2:24; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose sin the Lord will never count against him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 32:1–2 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:26–28"> Is this blessing only on the circumcised, or also on the uncircumcised? We have been saying that Abraham’s faith was credited to him as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; Genesis 17:10"> In what context was it credited? Was it after his circumcision, or before? It was not after, but before.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally How then was it reckoned—being in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:22; Luke 19:9; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> And he received the sign of circumcision as a seal of the righteousness that he had by faith while he was still uncircumcised. So then, he is the father of all who believe but are not circumcised, in order that righteousness might be credited to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song 1:8; John 8:39–40; Proverbs 2:20"> And he is also the father of the circumcised who not only are circumcised, but who also walk in the footsteps of the faith that our father Abraham had before he was circumcised.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham Receives the Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Genesis 22:17–18; Genesis 28:14"> For the promise to Abraham and his offspring that he would be heir of the world was not given through the law, but through the righteousness that comes by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:18–24; Philippians 3:9; Romans 4:16"> For if those who live by the law are heirs, faith is useless and the promise is worthless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:13; Galatians 3:10; Romans 3:19–20"> because the law brings wrath. And where there is no law, there is no transgression.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Ephesians 2:8; Galatians 3:22"> Therefore, the promise comes by faith, so that it may rest on grace and may be guaranteed to all Abraham’s offspring—not only to those who are of the law, but also to those who are of the faith of Abraham. He is the father of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:28; Genesis 17:4–5; John 5:21"> As it is written: “I have made you a father of many nations.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Genesis 17:5">d</a></sup>He is our father in the presence of God, in whom he believed, the God who gives life to the dead and calls into being what does not yet exist.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:24; Genesis 15:5–6; Acts 27:25"> Against all hope, Abraham in hope believed and so became the father of many nations, just as he had been told, “So shall your offspring be.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Genesis 15:5">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11–19; Romans 4:20–21; Genesis 17:17"> Without weakening in his faith, he acknowledged the decrepitness of his body (since he was about a hundred years old) and the lifelessness of Sarah’s womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 1:45; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Daniel 10:19"> Yet he did not waver through disbelief in the promise of God, but was strengthened in his faith and gave glory to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> being fully persuaded that God was able to do what He had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 4:6; Romans 4:3"> This is why “it was credited to him as righteousness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Genesis 15:6">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Romans 15:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Now the words “it was credited to him” were written not only for Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:24; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30"> but also for us, to whom righteousness will be credited—for us who believe in Him who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 4:9–10; Isaiah 53:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> He was delivered over to death for our trespasses and was raised to life for our justification.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
