## God Remains Faithful

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> What, then, is the advantage of being a Jew? Or what is the value of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much in every way. First of all, they have been entrusted with the very words<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the oracles">a</a></sup>of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> What if some did not have faith? Will their lack of faith nullify God’s faithfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> Certainly not! Let God be true and every man a liar. As it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“So that You may be proved right when You speak</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and victorious when You judge.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or when You are judged; Psalms 51:4 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness highlights the righteousness of God, what shall we say? That God is unjust to inflict His wrath on us? I am speaking in human terms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> Certainly not! In that case, how could God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> However, if my falsehood accentuates God’s truthfulness, to the increase of His glory, why am I still condemned as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> Why not say, as some slanderously claim that we say, “Let us do evil that good may result”? Their condemnation is deserved!</span>
</div>
</div>

## There Is No One Righteous

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Are we any better? Not at all. For we have already made the charge that Jews and Greeks alike are all under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> As it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There is no one righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not even one.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one who understands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no one who seeks God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All have turned away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have together become worthless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is no one who does good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not even one.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 14:1–3 and Psalms 53:1–3 (see also LXX)">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their throats are open graves;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their tongues practice deceit.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Psalms 5:9">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“The venom of vipers is on their lips.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Psalms 140:3">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their mouths are full</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of cursing and bitterness.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Psalms 10:7 (see also LXX)">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their feet are swift to shed blood;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-2">ruin and misery lie in their wake,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> <span className="pl pl-2">and the way of peace they have not known.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isaiah 59:7–8 (see also LXX)">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There is no fear of God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before their eyes.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Psalms 36:1">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the law says, it says to those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Therefore no one will be justified in His sight by works of the law. For the law merely brings awareness of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Righteousness through Faith in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed, as attested by the Law and the Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> And this righteousness from God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe. There is no distinction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> and are justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> God presented Him as an atoning sacrifice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or as a propitiation">i</a></sup>in His blood through faith, in order to demonstrate His righteousness, because in His forbearance He had passed over the sins committed beforehand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> He did this to demonstrate His righteousness at the present time, so as to be just and to justify the one who has faith in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where, then, is boasting? It is excluded. On what principle? On that of works? No, but on that of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> For we maintain that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Is God the God of Jews only? Is He not the God of Gentiles too? Yes, of Gentiles too,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> since there is only one God, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through that same faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we, then, nullify the law by this faith? Certainly not! Instead, we uphold the law.</span>
</div>
</div>
