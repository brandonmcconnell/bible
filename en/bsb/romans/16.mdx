## Personal Greetings and Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> I commend to you our sister Phoebe, a servant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or deaconess">a</a></sup>of the church in Cenchrea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> Welcome her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and assist her with anything she may need from you. For she has been a great help to many people, including me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Greet Prisca<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Prisca is a variant of Priscilla; see Acts 18:2.">b</a></sup>and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> who have risked their lives for me. Not only I but all the churches of the Gentiles are grateful to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> Greet also the church that meets at their house.<br />Greet my beloved Epenetus, who was the first convert to Christ in the province of Asia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally in Asia; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Greet Mary, who has worked very hard for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Greet Andronicus and Junia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some translators Junias">d</a></sup>my fellow countrymen and fellow prisoners. They are outstanding among the apostles, and they were in Christ before I was.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ.<br />Greet those who belong to the household of Aristobulus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Greet Herodion, my fellow countryman.<br />Greet those from the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, women who have worked hard in the Lord.<br />Greet my beloved Persis, who has worked very hard in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who has been a mother to me as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas and all the saints with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Greet one another with a holy kiss.<br />All the churches of Christ send you greetings.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Avoid Divisions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> Now I urge you, brothers, to watch out for those who create divisions and obstacles that are contrary to the teaching you have learned. Turn away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For such people are not serving our Lord Christ, but their own appetites. By smooth talk and flattery they deceive the hearts of the naive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> Everyone has heard about your obedience, so I rejoice over you. But I want you to be wise about what is good and innocent about what is evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="NA, NE, and WH Lord Jesus">e</a></sup>be with you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Greetings from Paul’s Fellow Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Timothy, my fellow worker, sends you greetings, as do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my fellow countrymen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I, Tertius, who wrote down this letter, greet you in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Gaius, who has hosted me and all the church, sends you greetings.<br />Erastus, the city treasurer, sends you greetings, as does our brother Quartus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="SBL, BYZ, and TR include 24 May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Doxology

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:7; Romans 2:16; Colossians 1:26–27"> Now to Him who is able to strengthen you by my gospel and by the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery concealed for ages past</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 1:5; Galatians 3:8; Romans 15:18"> but now revealed and made known through the writings of the prophets by the command of the eternal God, in order to lead all nations to the obedience that comes from faith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally the obedience of faith">g</a></sup>—</span>
