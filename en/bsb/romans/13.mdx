## Submission to Authorities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Everyone must submit himself to the governing authorities, for there is no authority except that which is from God. The authorities that exist have been appointed by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> Consequently, whoever resists authority is opposing what God has set in place, and those who do so will bring judgment on themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers are not a terror to good conduct, but to bad. Do you want to be unafraid of the one in authority? Then do what is right, and you will have his approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> For he is God’s servant for your good. But if you do wrong, be afraid, for he does not carry the sword in vain. He is God’s servant, an agent of retribution to the wrongdoer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Therefore it is necessary to submit to authority, not only to avoid punishment, but also as a matter of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> This is also why you pay taxes. For the authorities are God’s servants, who devote themselves to their work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Pay everyone what you owe him: taxes to whom taxes are due, revenue to whom revenue is due, respect to whom respect is due, honor to whom honor is due.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Love Fulfills the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Be indebted to no one, except to one another in love. For he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> The commandments “Do not commit adultery,” “Do not murder,” “Do not steal,” “Do not covet,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Exodus 20:13–17; Deuteronomy 5:17–21">a</a></sup>and any other commandments, are summed up in this one decree: “Love your neighbor as yourself.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Leviticus 19:18">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love does no wrong to its neighbor. Therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Day Is Near

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> And do this, understanding the occasion. The hour has come for you to wake up from your slumber, for our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is nearly over; the day has drawn near. So let us lay aside the deeds of darkness and put on the armor of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let us behave decently, as in the daytime, not in carousing and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and debauchery, not in dissension and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> Instead, clothe yourselves with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or put on; see verse 12.">c</a></sup>the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the desires of the flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>
