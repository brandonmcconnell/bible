## Release from the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:6; Romans 6:3"> Do you not know, brothers (for I am speaking to those who know the law), that the law has authority over a man only as long as he lives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:39; 1 Corinthians 7:4"> For instance, a married woman is bound by law to her husband as long as he lives. But if her husband dies, she is released from the law of marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Matthew 5:32; Mark 10:6–12"> So then, if she is joined to another man while her husband is still alive, she is called an adulteress; but if her husband dies, she is free from that law and is not an adulteress if she marries another man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> Therefore, my brothers, you also died to the law through the body of Christ, that you might belong to another, to Him who was raised from the dead, in order that we might bear fruit to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 5:24"> For when we lived according to the flesh, the sinful passions aroused by the law were at work in our bodies, bearing fruit for death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:23–25; 2 Corinthians 3:6; Romans 7:4"> But now, having died to what bound us, we have been released from the law, so that we serve in the new way of the Spirit, and not in the old way of the written code.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Law Is Holy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56"> What then shall we say? Is the law sin? Certainly not! Indeed, I would not have been mindful of sin if not for the law. For I would not have been aware of coveting if the law had not said, “Do not covet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Exodus 20:17; Deuteronomy 5:21">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:11; 1 Corinthians 15:56; Romans 4:15"> But sin, seizing its opportunity through the commandment, produced in me every kind of covetous desire. For apart from the law, sin is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 8:7; James 2:10–11"> Once I was alive apart from the law; but when the commandment came, sin sprang to life and I died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:7; Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> So I discovered that the very commandment that was meant to bring life actually brought death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; Romans 7:8; Hebrews 3:13"> For sin, seizing its opportunity through the commandment, deceived me and through the commandment put me to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:8; Psalms 119:137; Psalms 19:7–12"> So then, the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, righteous, and good.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Struggling with Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:13–15; Galatians 3:21; Romans 8:3"> Did that which is good, then, become death to me? Certainly not! But in order that sin might be exposed as sin, it produced death in me through what was good, so that through the commandment sin might become utterly sinful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:18"> We know that the law is spiritual; but I am unspiritual, sold as a slave to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> I do not understand what I do. For what I want to do, I do not do. But what I hate, I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 7:12; Romans 7:14; 1 Timothy 1:8"> And if I do what I do not want to do, I admit that the law is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 7:20; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 7:18"> In that case, it is no longer I who do it, but it is sin living in me that does it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:6; Psalms 51:5; Galatians 5:17"> I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my flesh; for I have the desire to do what is good, but I cannot carry it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:15"> For I do not do the good I want to do. Instead, I keep on doing the evil I do not want to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 7:17"> And if I do what I do not want, it is no longer I who do it, but it is sin living in me that does it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:23; Psalms 119:133"> So this is the principle I have discovered: When I want to do good, evil is right there with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 1:2; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 119:35"> For in my inner being I delight in God’s law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; James 4:1; Romans 8:2"> But I see another law at work in my body, warring against the law of my mind and holding me captive to the law of sin that dwells within me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally captive to the law of sin being in my members.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; 2 Timothy 4:18; Titus 2:14"> What a wretched man I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:17–24; Colossians 3:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Thanks be to God, through Jesus Christ our Lord!<br />So then, with my mind I serve the law of God, but with my flesh I serve the law of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
