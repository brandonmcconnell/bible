## Habakkuk’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I saw the tents of Cushan in distress;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the curtains of Midian were trembling.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 105:41; Psalms 78:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You brandished Your bow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You called for many arrows.</span>Selah<br /><span className="pl pl-2">You split the earth with rivers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalms 93:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains saw You and quaked;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">torrents of water swept by.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The deep roared with its voice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lifted its hands on high.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:6; Psalms 105:15; Psalms 68:19–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You went forth for the salvation of Your people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to save Your anointed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You crushed the head of the house of the wicked</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and stripped him from head to toe.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">With his own spear You pierced his head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when his warriors stormed out to scatter us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">gloating as though ready</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to secretly devour the weak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">You trampled the sea with Your horses,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">churning the great waters.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I heard and trembled within;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my lips quivered at the sound.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Decay entered my bones;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I trembled where I stood.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet I must wait patiently for the day of distress</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to come upon the people who invade us.</span></span>

## Habakkuk Rejoices

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though the fig tree does not bud</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no fruit is on the vines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">though the olive crop fails</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the fields produce no food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">though the sheep are cut off from the fold</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no cattle are in the stalls,</span></span>
