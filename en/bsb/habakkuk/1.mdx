## Habakkuk’s First Complaint

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> This is the burden that Habakkuk the prophet received in a vision:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O LORD, must I call for help</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but You do not hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or cry out to You, “Violence!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but You do not save?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do You make me see iniquity?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why do You tolerate wrongdoing?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Destruction and violence are before me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Strife is ongoing, and conflict abounds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the law is paralyzed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and justice never goes forth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the wicked hem in the righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that justice is perverted.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD’s Answer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Look at the nations and observe—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be utterly astounded!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX Look, you scoffers, wonder and perish!">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I am doing a work in your days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you would never believe</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even if someone told you.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Acts 13:41">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that ruthless and impetuous nation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which marches through the breadth of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to seize dwellings not their own.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are dreaded and feared;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from themselves they derive justice and sovereignty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their horses are swifter than leopards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">fiercer than wolves of the night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their horsemen charge ahead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their cavalry comes from afar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They fly like a vulture,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">swooping down to devour.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">All of them come bent on violence;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their hordes advance like the east wind;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they gather prisoners like sand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">They scoff at kings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and make rulers an object of scorn.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They laugh at every fortress</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and build up siege ramps to seize it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they sweep by like the wind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pass through.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are guilty;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their own strength is their god.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Habakkuk’s Second Complaint

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Are You not from everlasting,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O LORD, my God, my Holy One?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We will not die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, You have appointed them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to execute judgment;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O Rock, You have established them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for correction.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your eyes are too pure to look upon evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and You cannot tolerate wrongdoing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So why do You tolerate the faithless?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why are You silent</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">while the wicked swallow up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those more righteous than themselves?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made men like the fish of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like creeping things that have no ruler.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The foe pulls all of them up</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally He pulls all of them up">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with a hook;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he catches them in his dragnet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and gathers them in his fishing net;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so he rejoices gladly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore he sacrifices to his dragnet</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and burns incense to his fishing net,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for by these things his portion is sumptuous</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his food is rich.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will he, therefore, empty his net</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and continue to slay nations without mercy?</span></span>
</div>
</div>
