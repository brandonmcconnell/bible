## Woe to the Chaldeans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will not all of these take up a taunt against him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">speaking with mockery and derision:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Woe to him who amasses what is not his</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and makes himself rich with many loans!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">How long will this go on?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will not your creditors suddenly arise</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who disturb you awaken?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then you will become their prey.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you have plundered many nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the remnant of the people will plunder you—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of your bloodshed against man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your violence against the land, the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all their dwellers.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to him who builds his house</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by unjust gain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to place his nest on high</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and escape the hand of disaster!</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and establishes a town by iniquity!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is it not indeed from the LORD of Hosts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the labor of the people only feeds the fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the nations weary themselves in vain?</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to him who gives drink to his neighbors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">pouring it from the wineskin until they are drunk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to gaze at their nakedness!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will be filled with shame instead of glory.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You too must drink</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and expose your uncircumcision!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="DSS, LXX, and Syriac and stagger">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The cup in the LORD’s right hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will come around to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and utter disgrace will cover your glory.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">What use is an idol,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that a craftsman should carve it—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or an image,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a teacher of lies?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For its maker trusts in his own creation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he makes idols that cannot speak.</span></span>
