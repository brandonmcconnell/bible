## Esther Pleads for Her People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 3:15"> So the king and Haman went to dine with Esther the queen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 5:3; John 16:24; Esther 5:6"> and as they drank their wine on that second day, the king asked once more, “Queen Esther, what is your petition? It will be given to you. What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom, it will be fulfilled.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:26; Psalms 122:6–9; Esther 7:7"> Queen Esther replied, “If I have found favor in your sight, O king, and if it pleases the king, grant me my life as my petition, and the lives of my people as my request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 3:9; Deuteronomy 28:68; Esther 3:13"> For my people and I have been sold out to destruction, death, and annihilation. If we had merely been sold as menservants and maidservants, I would have remained silent, because no such distress would justify burdening the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Genesis 27:33; Job 9:24"> Then King Xerxes spoke up and asked Queen Esther, “Who is this, and where is the one who would devise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew whose heart has filled him to do">a</a></sup>such a scheme?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:5–12; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Psalms 139:19–22"> Esther replied, “The adversary and enemy is this wicked man—Haman!”<br />And Haman stood in terror before the king and queen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Hanging of Haman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 1:12; Proverbs 19:12; Revelation 3:9"> In his fury, the king arose from drinking his wine and went to the palace garden, while Haman stayed behind to beg Queen Esther for his life, for he realized that the king was planning a terrible fate for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Esther 6:12; Job 9:24"> Just as the king returned from the palace garden to the banquet hall, Haman was falling on the couch where Esther was reclining. The king exclaimed, “Would he actually assault the queen while I am in the palace?”<br />As soon as the words had left the king’s mouth, they covered Haman’s face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 11:5–6; Esther 1:10"> Then Harbonah, one of the eunuchs attending the king, said: “There is a gallows fifty cubits high<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="50 cubits is approximately 75 feet or 22.9 meters high.">b</a></sup>at Haman’s house. He had it built for Mordecai, who gave the report that saved the king.”<br />“Hang him on it!” declared the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Daniel 6:24; Ezekiel 5:13"> So they hanged Haman on the gallows he had prepared for Mordecai. Then the fury of the king subsided.</span>
</div>
</div>
