## Mordecai Appeals to Esther

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Ezekiel 27:30–31; 2 Samuel 13:19"> When Mordecai learned of all that had happened, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth and ashes, and went out into the middle of the city, wailing loudly and bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But he went only as far as the king’s gate, because the law prohibited anyone wearing sackcloth from entering that gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Daniel 9:3; Isaiah 58:5"> In every province to which the king’s command and edict came, there was great mourning among the Jews. They fasted, wept, and lamented, and many lay in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 77:2; Jeremiah 31:15; Esther 1:12"> When Esther’s maidens and eunuchs came and told her about Mordecai, the queen was overcome with distress. She sent clothes for Mordecai to wear instead of his sackcloth, but he would not accept them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Esther 1:10"> Then Esther summoned Hathach, one of the king’s eunuchs appointed to her, and she dispatched him to Mordecai to learn what was troubling him and why.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 4:3; Esther 7:2; Esther 9:12"> So Hathach went out to Mordecai in the city square in front of the king’s gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 3:2–15"> and Mordecai told him all that had happened to him, including the exact amount of money that Haman had promised to pay into the royal treasury in order to destroy the Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Job 9:15; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Mordecai also gave Hathach a copy of the written decree issued in Susa for the destruction of the Jews, to show and explain to Esther, urging her to approach the king, implore his favor, and plead before him for her people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Hathach went back and relayed Mordecai’s response to Esther.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Esther spoke to Hathach and instructed him to tell Mordecai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 5:1–2; Esther 8:4; Daniel 2:9"> “All the royal officials and the people of the king’s provinces know that one law applies to every man or woman who approaches the king in the inner court without being summoned—that he be put to death. Only if the king extends the gold scepter may that person live. But I have not been summoned to appear before the king for the past thirty days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> When Esther’s words were relayed to Mordecai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 24:10–12; Philippians 2:30; John 12:25"> he sent back to her this reply: “Do not imagine that because you are in the king’s palace you alone will escape the fate of all the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 45:4–8; 1 Samuel 12:22; Deuteronomy 32:36"> For if you remain silent at this time, relief and deliverance for the Jews will arise from another place, but you and your father’s house will perish. And who knows if perhaps you have come to the kingdom for such a time as this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Esther sent this reply to Mordecai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:24; 2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 2:12–17"> “Go and assemble all the Jews who can be found in Susa, and fast for me. Do not eat or drink for three days, night or day, and I and my maidens will fast as you do. After that, I will go to the king, even though it is against the law. And if I perish, I perish!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or if I am destroyed, then I will be destroyed!">a</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Mordecai went and did all that Esther had instructed him.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
