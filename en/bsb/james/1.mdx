<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:15; 1 Peter 1:1; John 7:35"> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ,<br />To the twelve tribes of the Dispersion:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally To the twelve tribes in the Diaspora. ## Rejoicing in Trials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Consider it pure joy, my brothers, when you encounter trials of many kinds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> because you know that the testing of your faith develops perseverance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> Allow perseverance to finish its work, so that you may be mature and complete, not lacking anything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> Now if any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But he must ask in faith, without doubting, because he who doubts is like a wave of the sea, blown and tossed by the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> That man should not expect to receive anything from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> He is a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> The brother in humble circumstances should exult in his high position.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> But the one who is rich should exult in his low position, because he will pass away like a flower of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For the sun rises with scorching heat and withers the plant; its flower falls and its beauty is lost. So too, the rich man will fade away in the midst of his pursuits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Blessed is the man who perseveres under trial, because when he has stood the test, he will receive the crown of life that God has promised to those who love Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Good and Perfect Gifts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> When tempted, no one should say, “God is tempting me.” For God cannot be tempted by evil, nor does He tempt anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> But each one is tempted when by his own evil desires he is lured away and enticed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Then after desire has conceived, it gives birth to sin; and sin, when it is full-grown, gives birth to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights, with whom there is no change or shifting shadow.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or no change or shadow of turning.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> He chose to give us birth through the word of truth, that we would be a kind of firstfruits of His creation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or of His creatures.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Hearing and Doing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> My beloved brothers, understand this: Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to anger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> for man’s anger does not bring about the righteousness that God desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> Therefore, get rid of all moral filth and every expression of evil, and humbly accept the word planted in you, which can save your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> Be doers of the word, and not hearers only. Otherwise, you are deceiving yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> For anyone who hears the word but does not carry it out is like a man who looks at his face in a mirror,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> and after observing himself goes away and immediately forgets what he looks like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But the one who looks intently into the perfect law of freedom, and continues to do so—not being a forgetful hearer, but an effective doer—he will be blessed in what he does.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalms 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If anyone considers himself religious and yet does not bridle his tongue, he deceives his heart and his religion is worthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure and undefiled religion before our God and Father is this: to care for orphans and widows in their distress, and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
