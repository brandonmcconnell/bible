## A Warning against Pride

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> What causes conflicts and quarrels among you? Don’t they come from the passions at war within you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally passions warring among your members?">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You crave what you do not have; you kill and covet, but are unable to obtain it. You quarrel and fight. You do not have, because you do not ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> And when you do ask, you do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, that you may squander it on your pleasures.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> You adulteresses!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Hosea 3:1.">b</a></sup>Do you not know that friendship with the world is hostility toward God? Therefore, whoever chooses to be a friend of the world renders himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or is appointed">c</a></sup>an enemy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or do you think the Scripture says without reason that the Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or the spirit">d</a></sup>He caused to dwell in us yearns with envy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But He gives us more grace. This is why it says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God opposes the proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but gives grace to the humble.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Proverbs 3:34 (see also LXX)">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Drawing Near to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Grieve, mourn, and weep. Turn your laughter to mourning, and your joy to gloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Humble yourselves before the Lord, and He will exalt you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Brothers, do not slander one another. Anyone who speaks against his brother or judges him speaks against the law and judges it. And if you judge the law, you are not a practitioner of the law, but a judge of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> There is only one Lawgiver and Judge, the One who is able to save and destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Boast about Tomorrow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go to this or that city, spend a year there, carry on business, and make a profit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> You do not even know what will happen tomorrow! What is your life? You are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> Instead, you ought to say, “If the Lord is willing, we will live and do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> As it is, you boast in your proud intentions. All such boasting is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> Anyone, then, who knows the right thing to do, yet fails to do it, is guilty of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>
