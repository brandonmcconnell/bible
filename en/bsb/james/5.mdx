## A Warning to the Rich

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 11:4"> Come now, you who are rich, weep and wail over the misery to come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Job 13:28; Luke 12:33"> Your riches have rotted and moths have eaten your clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; James 5:7–8; Revelation 21:8"> Your gold and silver are corroded. Their corrosion will testify against you and consume your flesh like fire.<br />You have hoarded treasure in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Look, the wages you withheld from the workmen who mowed your fields are crying out against you. The cries of the harvesters have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:6; Luke 16:25; Luke 16:19"> You have lived on earth in luxury and self-indulgence. You have fattened your hearts in the day of slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–23; John 19:9–11; Isaiah 53:7"> You have condemned and murdered the righteous, who did not resist you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Patience in Suffering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1–3; James 5:8–9; Joel 2:23"> Be patient, then, brothers, until the Lord’s coming. See how the farmer awaits the precious fruit of the soil—how patient he is for the fall and spring rains.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally for it until it receives the early and the late">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 5:9; Galatians 5:22; Psalms 37:7"> You, too, be patient and strengthen your hearts, because the Lord’s coming is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; James 4:11"> Do not complain about one another, brothers, so that you will not be judged. Look, the Judge is standing at the door!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32–38; Matthew 5:11–12; Hebrews 13:7"> Brothers, as an example of patience in affliction, take the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Exodus 34:6; Job 1:21–22"> See how blessed we consider those who have persevered. You have heard of Job’s perseverance and have seen the outcome from the Lord. The Lord is full of compassion and mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:33–37; Matthew 23:16–22; 2 Corinthians 1:17–20"> Above all, my brothers, do not swear, not by heaven or earth or by any other oath. Simply let your “Yes” be yes, and your “No,” no, so that you will not fall under judgment.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Prayer of Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jonah 2:2; Acts 16:24–25"> Is any one of you suffering? He should pray. Is anyone cheerful? He should sing praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:13; Acts 28:8; Mark 16:18"> Is any one of you sick? He should call the elders of the church to pray over him and anoint him with oil in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 5:14–16; James 5:13; James 5:16"> And the prayer offered in faith will restore the one who is sick. The Lord will raise him up. If he has sinned, he will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Proverbs 15:29; Matthew 21:22"> Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Revelation 11:6"> Elijah was a man just like us. He prayed earnestly that it would not rain, and it did not rain on the land for three and a half years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 18:42–45; Jeremiah 14:22; 1 Kings 18:18"> Again he prayed, and the heavens gave rain, and the earth yielded its crops.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Restoring a Sinner

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15; James 5:20"> My brothers, if one of you should wander from the truth and someone should bring him back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 10:12; 1 Timothy 4:16"> consider this: Whoever turns a sinner from the error of his way will save his soul from death and cover over a multitude of sins.</span>
</div>
