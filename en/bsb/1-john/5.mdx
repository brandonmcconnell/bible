## Overcoming the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12–13; John 8:42; 1 John 2:29"> Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God, and everyone who loves the Father also loves those born of Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally and everyone loving the One having begotten also loves the one having been begotten from Him.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 3:22–24"> By this we know that we love the children of God: when we love God and keep His commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; John 14:21–24"> For this is the love of God, that we keep His commandments. And His commandments are not burdensome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; John 16:33"> because everyone born of God overcomes the world. And this is the victory that has overcome the world: our faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:15; 1 John 5:1"> Who then overcomes the world? Only he who believes that Jesus is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:26; Hebrews 9:14; John 16:13"> This is the One who came by water and blood, Jesus Christ—not by water alone, but by water and blood. And it is the Spirit who testifies to this, because the Spirit is the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Acts 5:32"> For there are three that testify:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR and GOC three that testify in heaven: The Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one. 8 And there are three that testify on earth:">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 5:6–7; John 15:26; 1 Peter 3:21"> the Spirit, the water, and the blood—and these three are in agreement.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Testimony about His Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:16–17; Matthew 17:5; Acts 17:31"> Even if we accept human testimony, the testimony of God is greater. For this is the testimony that God has given about His Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:16; Galatians 4:6; John 3:33"> Whoever believes in the Son of God has this testimony within him; whoever does not believe God has made Him out to be a liar, because he has not believed in the testimony that God has given about His Son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 John 2:25; John 3:36"> And this is that testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 3:36; John 5:24; John 1:12"> Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have the Son of God does not have life.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Effective Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> I have written these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, so that you may know that you have eternal life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include and that you may believe in the name of the Son of God.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> And this is the confidence that we have before Him: If we ask anything according to His will, He hears us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> And if we know that He hears us in whatever we ask, we know that we already possess what we have asked of Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If anyone sees his brother committing a sin not leading to death, he should ask God, who will give life to those who commit this kind of sin. There is a sin that leads to death; I am not saying he should ask regarding that sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> All unrighteousness is sin, yet there is sin that does not lead to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The True God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We know that anyone born of God does not keep on sinning; the One who was born of God protects him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or the one who was born of God protects himself or God protects the one born of Him">d</a></sup>and the evil one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or evil; similarly in verse 19">e</a></sup>cannot touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we are of God, and that the whole world is under the power of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, so that we may know Him who is true; and we are in Him who is true—in His Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life.</span>
</div>
