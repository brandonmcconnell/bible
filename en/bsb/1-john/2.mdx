## Jesus Our Advocate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; John 14:6"> My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an advocate before the Father—Jesus Christ, the Righteous One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 4:10"> He Himself is the atoning sacrifice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the propitiation">a</a></sup>for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the sins of the whole world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; Luke 6:46"> By this we can be sure that we have come to know Him: if we keep His commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Titus 1:16; James 2:14–16"> If anyone says, “I know Him,” but does not keep His commandments, he is a liar, and the truth is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; 1 John 4:12–13"> But if anyone keeps His word, the love of God has been truly perfected in him. By this we know that we are in Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; John 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> Whoever claims to abide in Him must walk as Jesus walked.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A New Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Beloved, I am not writing to you a new commandment, but an old one, which you have had from the beginning. This commandment is the message you have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; John 13:34"> Then again, I am also writing to you a new commandment, which is true in Him and also in you. For the darkness is fading and the true light is already shining.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:11; 1 John 3:13–17"> If anyone claims to be in the light but hates his brother, he is still in the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> Whoever loves his brother remains in the light, and there is no cause of stumbling in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 John 1:6"> But whoever hates his brother is in the darkness and walks in the darkness. He does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> I am writing to you, little children, because your sins have been forgiven through His name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:7; 1 John 2:14; Luke 10:22"> I am writing to you, fathers, because you know Him who is from the beginning.<br />I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or you have overcome evil; also in verse 14">b</a></sup><br />I have written to you, children, because you know the Father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 1 John 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> I have written to you, fathers, because you know Him who is from the beginning.<br />I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God abides in you, and you have overcome the evil one.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Love the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Do not love the world or anything in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalms 119:36–37"> For all that is in the world—the desires of the flesh, the desires of the eyes, and the pride of life—is not from the Father but from the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> The world is passing away, along with its desires; but whoever does the will of God remains forever.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware of Antichrists

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Children, it is the last hour; and just as you have heard that the antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have appeared. This is how we know it is the last hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went out from us, but they did not belong to us. For if they had belonged to us, they would have remained with us. But their departure made it clear that none of them belonged to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> You, however, have an anointing from the Holy One, and all of you know the truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR and you know all things">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Jude 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I have not written to you because you lack knowledge of the truth, but because you have it, and because no lie comes from the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:3; 1 John 2:18"> Who is the liar, if it is not the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This is the antichrist, who denies the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 4:15; John 8:19; John 5:23"> Whoever denies the Son does not have the Father, but whoever confesses the Son has the Father as well.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Remain in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 14:23; John 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13"> As for you, let what you have heard from the beginning remain in you. If it does, you will also remain in the Son and in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21"> And this is the promise that He Himself made to us: eternal life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 John 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> I have written these things to you about those who are trying to deceive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> And as for you, the anointing you received from Him remains in you, and you do not need anyone to teach you. But just as His true and genuine anointing teaches you about all things, so remain in Him as you have been taught.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 4:17; Mark 8:38; 1 John 3:2"> And now, little children, remain in Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally remain in Him">d</a></sup>so that when He appears, we may be confident and unashamed before Him at His coming.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 3:3–5; 1 John 3:7; 1 John 5:1"> If you know that He is righteous, you also know that everyone who practices righteousness has been born of Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
