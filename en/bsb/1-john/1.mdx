## The Word of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:20; John 20:27; 1 John 4:14"> That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our own eyes, which we have gazed upon and touched with our own hands—this is the Word of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:4; 1 John 5:20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> And this is the life that was revealed; we have seen it and testified to it, and we proclaim to you the eternal life that was with the Father and was revealed to us.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:11; John 16:24; 2 John 1:12"> We write these things so that our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR your">a</a></sup>joy may be complete.</span>

## Walking in the Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; James 1:17; Isaiah 60:19"> And this is the message we have heard from Him and announce to you: God is light, and in Him there is no darkness at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:4; John 3:19–21"> If we say we have fellowship with Him yet walk in the darkness, we lie and do not practice the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Revelation 1:5"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NA does not include But.">b</a></sup>if we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus His Son cleanses us from all sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2"> If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 33:8"> If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:8; 1 John 5:10; 1 John 2:4"> If we say we have not sinned, we make Him out to be a liar, and His word is not in us.</span>
</div>
