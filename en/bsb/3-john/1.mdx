## A Greeting from the Elder

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The elder,<br />To the beloved Gaius, whom I love in the truth:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Beloved, I pray that in every way you may prosper and enjoy good health, as your soul also prospers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> For I was overjoyed when the brothers came and testified about your devotion to the truth, in which you continue to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than to hear that my children are walking in the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gaius Commended for Hospitality

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, you are faithful in what you are doing for the brothers, and especially since they are strangers to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> They have testified to the church about your love. You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> For they went out on behalf of the Name, accepting nothing from the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> Therefore we ought to support such men, so that we may be fellow workers for the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Diotrephes and Demetrius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I have written to the church about this,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally I have written something to the church">a</a></sup>but Diotrephes, who loves to be first, will not accept our instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> So if I come, I will call attention to his malicious slander against us. And unsatisfied with that, he refuses to welcome the brothers and forbids those who want to do so, even putting them out of the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. The one who does good is of God; the one who does evil has not seen God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius has received a good testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself. We also testify for him, and you know that our testimony is true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I have many things to write to you, but I would prefer not to do so with pen and ink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> Instead, I hope to see you soon and speak with you face to face.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally and we will speak mouth to mouth; some translators begin a new verse (15) after face to face.">b</a></sup><br />Peace to you.<br />The friends here send you greetings.<br />Greet each of our friends there by name.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
