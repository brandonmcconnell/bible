## Solomon’s Proverbs: The Wise Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> The proverbs of Solomon:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">A wise son brings joy to his father,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a foolish son grief to his mother.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ill-gotten treasures profit nothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but righteousness brings deliverance from death.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but He denies the craving of the wicked.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Idle hands make one poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but diligent hands bring wealth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who gathers in summer is a wise son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who sleeps during harvest is a disgraceful son.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessings are on the head of the righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or but violence covers the mouth of the wicked; also in verse 11">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The memory of the righteous is a blessing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the name of the wicked will rot.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">A wise heart will receive commandments,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but foolish lips will come to ruin.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who walks in integrity walks securely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who perverts his ways will be found out.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who winks the eye causes grief,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and foolish lips will come to ruin.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or babbling fools will fall">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hatred stirs up dissension,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but love covers all transgressions.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1 Peter 4:8">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Isaiah 50:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom is found on the lips of the discerning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a rod is for the back of him who lacks judgment.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wise store up knowledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the mouth of the fool invites destruction.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wealth of the rich man is his fortified city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but poverty is the ruin of the poor.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The labor of the righteous leads to life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the gain of the wicked brings punishment.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoever heeds instruction is on the path to life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who ignores reproof goes astray.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who conceals hatred has lying lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whoever spreads slander is a fool.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">When words are many, sin is unavoidable,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who restrains his lips is wise.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tongue of the righteous is choice silver,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the heart of the wicked has little worth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lips of the righteous feed many,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but fools die for lack of judgment.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The blessing of the LORD enriches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He adds no sorrow to it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fool delights in shameful conduct,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a man of understanding has wisdom.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">What the wicked man dreads will overtake him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the desire of the righteous will be granted.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the whirlwind passes, the wicked are no more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the righteous are secure forever.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so is the slacker to those who send him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fear of the LORD prolongs life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the years of the wicked will be cut short.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The hope of the righteous is joy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the expectations of the wicked will perish.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The way of the LORD is a refuge to the upright,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but destruction awaits those who do evil.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The righteous will never be shaken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the wicked will not inhabit the land.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a perverse tongue will be cut out.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lips of the righteous know what is fitting,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the mouth of the wicked is perverse.</span></span>
