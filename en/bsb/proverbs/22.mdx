<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Drive out the mocker, and conflict will depart;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even quarreling and insults will cease.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who loves a pure heart and gracious lips</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will have the king for a friend.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mouth of an adulteress is a deep pit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he who is under the wrath of the LORD will fall into it.</span></span>

</div>

## Saying 1

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Incline your ear and hear the words of the wise—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">apply your mind to my knowledge—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> <span className="pl pl-1">for it is pleasing when you keep them within you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they are constantly on your lips.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">So that your trust may be in the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I instruct you today—yes, you.</span></span>
