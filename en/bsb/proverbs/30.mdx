## The Words of Agur

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> These are the words of Agur son of Jakeh—the burden that this man declared to Ithiel:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am weary, O God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and worn out.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or declared to Ithiel: “I am weary, O God, but I can prevail. <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely I am the most ignorant of men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I lack the understanding of a man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have not learned wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I have no knowledge of the Holy One.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who has ascended to heaven and come down?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who has gathered the wind in His hands?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who has bound up the waters in His cloak?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who has established all the ends of the earth?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What is His name, and what is the name of His Son—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">surely you know!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not add to His words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest He rebuke you and prove you a liar.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Two things I ask of You—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not refuse me before I die:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Keep falsehood and deceitful words far from me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Give me neither poverty nor riches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">feed me with the bread that is my portion.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Otherwise, I may have too much</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and deny You, saying, ‘Who is the LORD?’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or I may become poor and steal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">profaning the name of my God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not slander a servant to his master,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or he will curse you, and you will bear the guilt.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a generation of those who curse their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not bless their mothers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a generation of those who are pure in their own eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and yet unwashed of their filth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a generation—how haughty are their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pretentious are their glances—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">there is a generation whose teeth are swords</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whose jaws are knives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">devouring the oppressed from the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the needy from among men.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The leech has two daughters:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Give and Give.</span><br />There are three things that are never satisfied, four that never say, ‘Enough!’:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> Sheol,<br />the barren womb,<br />land never satisfied with water,<br />and fire that never says, ‘Enough!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for the eye that mocks a father</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and scorns obedience to a mother,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may the ravens of the valley pluck it out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and young vultures devour it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> There are three things too wonderful for me, four that I cannot understand:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> the way of an eagle in the sky,<br />the way of a snake on a rock,<br />the way of a ship at sea,<br />and the way of a man with a maiden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the way of an adulteress:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">She eats and wipes her mouth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and says, ‘I have done nothing wrong.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Under three things the earth trembles, under four it cannot bear up:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> a servant who becomes king,<br />a fool who is filled with food,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> an unloved woman who marries,<br />and a maidservant who supplants her mistress.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> Four things on earth are small, yet they are exceedingly wise:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants are creatures of little strength, yet they store up their food in the summer;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> the rock badgers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the coneys or the hyraxes">b</a></sup>are creatures of little power, yet they make their homes in the rocks;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> the locusts have no king, yet they all advance in formation;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the lizard can be caught in one’s hands, yet it is found in the palaces of kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> There are three things that are stately in their stride, and four that are impressive in their walk:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> a lion, mighty among beasts, refusing to retreat before anything;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> a strutting rooster;<br />a he-goat;<br />and a king with his army around him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or a king against whom there is no rising up">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you have foolishly exalted yourself</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or if you have plotted evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">put your hand over your mouth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">For as the churning of milk yields butter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the twisting of the nose draws blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so the stirring of anger brings forth strife.”</span></span>
</div>
