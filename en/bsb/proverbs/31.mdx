## The Sayings for King Lemuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> These are the words of King Lemuel—the burden that his mother taught him:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">What shall I say,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or What or What are you doing">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O my son?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What, O son of my womb?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What, O son of my vows?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not spend your strength on women</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or your vigor on those who ruin kings.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not for kings, O Lemuel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is not for kings to drink wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or for rulers to crave strong drink,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">lest they drink and forget what is decreed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">depriving all the oppressed of justice.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; Psalms 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give strong drink to one who is perishing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wine to the bitter in soul.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let him drink and forget his poverty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and remember his misery no more.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Open your mouth for those with no voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the cause of all the dispossessed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Open your mouth, judge righteously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and defend the cause of the poor and needy.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Virtues of a Noble Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">A wife</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Verses 10–31 are an acrostic poem, each verse beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of noble character, who can find?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">She is far more precious than rubies.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heart of her husband trusts in her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he lacks nothing of value.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">She brings him good and not harm</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the days of her life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">She selects wool and flax</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and works with eager hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">She is like the merchant ships,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">bringing her food from afar.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> <span className="pl pl-1">She rises while it is still night</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to provide food for her household</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and portions for her maidservants.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">She appraises a field and buys it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from her earnings she plants a vineyard.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">She girds herself</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew She girds her loins">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with strength</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shows that her arms are strong.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; Psalms 127:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">She sees that her gain is good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her lamp is not extinguished at night.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">She stretches out her hands to the distaff</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and grasps the spindle with her fingers.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">She opens her arms to the poor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and reaches out her hands to the needy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">When it snows, she has no fear for her household,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for they are all clothed in scarlet.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or doubly clothed">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> <span className="pl pl-1">She makes coverings for her bed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her clothing is fine linen and purple.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her husband is known at the city gates,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where he sits among the elders of the land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">She makes linen garments and sells them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she delivers sashes to the merchants.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Strength and honor are her clothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and she can laugh at the days to come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">She opens her mouth with wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and faithful instruction is on her tongue.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">She watches over the affairs of her household</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and does not eat the bread of idleness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her children rise up and call her blessed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her husband praises her as well:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Many daughters have done noble things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you surpass them all!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Charm is deceptive and beauty is fleeting,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give her the fruit of her hands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let her works praise her at the gates.</span></span>
</div>
