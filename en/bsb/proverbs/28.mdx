<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who walks with integrity will be kept safe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but whoever is perverse in his ways will suddenly fall.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who works his land will have plenty of food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but whoever chases fantasies will have his fill of poverty.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A faithful man will abound with blessings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but one eager to be rich will not go unpunished.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">To show partiality is not good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet a man will do wrong for a piece of bread.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">A stingy man</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew A man whose eye is evil">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hastens after wealth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and does not know that poverty awaits him.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A greedy man stirs up strife,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who trusts in the LORD will prosper.</span></span>
