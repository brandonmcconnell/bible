## Saul’s Disobedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 12:14; Psalms 2:10–11"> Then Samuel said to Saul, “The LORD sent me to anoint you king over His people Israel. Now therefore, listen to the words of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 24:20"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘I witnessed what the Amalekites did to the Israelites when they opposed them on their way up from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Numbers 24:20; Numbers 31:17"> Now go and attack the Amalekites and devote to destruction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering; also in verses 8, 9, 15, 18, 20, and 21.">a</a></sup>all that belongs to them. Do not spare them, but put to death men and women, children and infants, oxen and sheep, camels and donkeys.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 13:15"> So Saul summoned the troops and numbered them at Telaim—200,000 foot soldiers and 10,000 men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Saul came to the city of Amalek and lay in wait in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 10:29–32; Exodus 18:19"> And he warned the Kenites, “Since you showed kindness to all the Israelites when they came up out of Egypt, go on and get away from the Amalekites. Otherwise I will sweep you away with them.”<br />So the Kenites moved away from the Amalekites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; Genesis 25:18; 1 Samuel 14:48"> Then Saul struck down the Amalekites all the way from Havilah to Shur, which is east of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; Esther 3:1; Numbers 24:7"> He captured Agag king of Amalek alive, but devoted all the others to destruction with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 15:19; 1 Samuel 15:3"> Saul and his troops spared Agag, along with the best of the sheep and cattle, the fat calves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the grown bulls">b</a></sup>and lambs, and the best of everything else. They were unwilling to devote them to destruction, but they devoted to destruction all that was despised and worthless.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samuel Denounces Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the word of the LORD came to Samuel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 6:6; 2 Samuel 24:16"> “I regret that I have made Saul king, for he has turned away from following Me and has not carried out My instructions.”<br />And Samuel was distressed and cried out to the LORD all that night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:55; 2 Samuel 18:18; 1 Samuel 25:2"> Early in the morning Samuel got up to confront Saul, but he was told, “Saul has gone to Carmel, and behold, he has set up a monument for himself and has turned and gone down to Gilgal.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:2; Ruth 3:10; Proverbs 31:31"> When Samuel reached him, Saul said to him, “May the LORD bless you. I have carried out the LORD’s instructions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Jeremiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 2:34–37"> But Samuel replied, “Then what is this bleating of sheep and lowing of cattle that I hear?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:21; 1 Samuel 15:9; Exodus 32:22–23"> Saul answered, “The troops brought them from the Amalekites; they spared the best sheep and cattle to sacrifice to the LORD your God, but the rest we devoted to destruction.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:27; 1 Kings 22:16; 1 Samuel 12:7"> “Stop!” exclaimed Samuel. “Let me tell you what the LORD said to me last night.”<br />“Tell me,” Saul replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:22; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 15:1–3"> And Samuel said, “Although you were once small in your own eyes, have you not become the head of the tribes of Israel? The LORD anointed you king over Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Genesis 13:13; Genesis 15:16"> and sent you on a mission, saying, ‘Go and devote to destruction the sinful Amalekites. Fight against them until you have wiped them out.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:32; 2 Chronicles 33:6; 2 Chronicles 36:12"> So why did you not obey the LORD? Why did you rush upon the plunder and do evil in the sight of the LORD?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Matthew 19:20; Romans 10:3"> “But I did obey the LORD,” Saul replied. “I went on the mission that the LORD gave me. I brought back Agag king of Amalek and devoted the Amalekites to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; Exodus 32:22–23; Genesis 3:13"> The troops took sheep and cattle from the plunder, the best of the things devoted to destruction, in order to sacrifice them to the LORD your God at Gilgal.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Psalms 51:16–17; Proverbs 21:3"> But Samuel declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Does the LORD delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as much as in obedience to His voice?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, obedience is better than sacrifice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and attentiveness is better than the fat of rams.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 1 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For rebellion is like the sin of divination,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and arrogance is like the wickedness of idolatry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because you have rejected the word of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has rejected you as king.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul’s Confession

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; Numbers 22:34"> Then Saul said to Samuel, “I have sinned; I have transgressed the LORD’s commandment and your instructions, because I feared the people and obeyed their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:17"> Now therefore, please forgive my sin and return with me so I can worship the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 16:1"> “I will not return with you,” Samuel replied. “For you have rejected the word of the LORD, and He has rejected you as king over Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 11:30–31"> As Samuel turned to go, Saul grabbed the hem of his robe, and it tore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:17–18; Acts 13:22; Daniel 4:32"> So Samuel said to him, “The LORD has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today and has given it to your neighbor who is better than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:14; Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2"> Moreover, the Glory of Israel does not lie or change His mind, for He is not a man, that He should change His mind.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 5:44; John 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> “I have sinned,” Saul replied. “Please honor me now before the elders of my people and before Israel. Come back with me, so that I may worship the LORD your God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Samuel went back with Saul, and Saul worshiped the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Jeremiah 48:44"> Then Samuel said, “Bring me Agag king of the Amalekites.”<br />Agag came to him cheerfully,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or cautiously or in chains; see DSS and LXX.">c</a></sup>for he thought, “Surely the bitterness of death is past.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “Surely this is the bitterness of death.” See DSS and LXX.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 1:7; Genesis 9:6; Matthew 7:2"> But Samuel declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As your sword has made women childless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so your mother will be childless among women.”</span><br />And Samuel hacked Agag to pieces before the LORD at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; 1 Samuel 7:17"> Then Samuel went to Ramah, but Saul went up to his home in Gibeah of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; 1 Samuel 19:24; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And to the day of his death, Samuel never again visited Saul. Samuel mourned for Saul, and the LORD regretted that He had made Saul king over Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
