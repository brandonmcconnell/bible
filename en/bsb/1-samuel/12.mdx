## Samuel’s Farewell Address

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Then Samuel said to all Israel, “I have listened to your voice in all that you have said to me, and I have set over you a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 8:1; 1 Samuel 8:20"> Now here is the king walking before you, and I am old and gray, and my sons are here with you. I have walked before you from my youth until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Numbers 16:15; 1 Peter 5:2"> Here I am. Bear witness against me before the LORD and before His anointed: Whose ox or donkey have I taken? Whom have I cheated or oppressed? From whose hand have I accepted a bribe and closed my eyes? Tell me, and I will restore it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew And I will restore it; LXX Testify against me, and I will restore it">a</a></sup>to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Psalms 37:5–6; 3 John 1:12"> “You have not cheated us or oppressed us,” they replied, “nor have you taken anything from the hand of man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Acts 24:20; Acts 23:9"> Samuel said to them, “The LORD is a witness against you, and His anointed is a witness today, that you have not found anything in my hand.”<br />“He is a witness,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:4; Exodus 6:26; Psalms 99:6"> Then Samuel said to the people, “The LORD is the One who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX The LORD is the witness who">b</a></sup>appointed Moses and Aaron, and who brought your fathers up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Micah 6:1–5; Acts 17:3"> Now present yourselves, so that I may confront you before the LORD with all the righteous acts He has done for you and your fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:14–16; Exodus 2:23–24; Psalms 78:54–55"> When Jacob went to Egypt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX includes and the Egyptians oppressed them">c</a></sup>your fathers cried out to the LORD, and He sent them Moses and Aaron, who brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 10:7; Judges 3:12"> But they forgot the LORD their God, and He sold them into the hand of Sisera the commander of the army of Hazor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX the army of Jabin king of Hazor">d</a></sup>and into the hands of the Philistines and the king of Moab, who fought against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 10:10; Judges 2:13; Judges 3:7"> Then they cried out to the LORD and said, ‘We have sinned, for we have forsaken the LORD and served the Baals and Ashtoreths. Now deliver us from the hands of our enemies, that we may serve You.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 4:6; Judges 8:35"> So the LORD sent Jerubbaal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jerubbaal is another name for Gideon and probably means let Baal contend; see Judges 6:32.">e</a></sup>Barak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX and Syriac; Hebrew Bedan">f</a></sup>Jephthah, and Samuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="LXX and Syriac Samson">g</a></sup>and He delivered you from the hands of your enemies on every side, and you dwelt securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:23; 1 Samuel 8:5–7; Genesis 17:7"> But when you saw that Nahash king of the Ammonites was moving against you, you said to me, ‘No, we must have a king to rule over us’—even though the LORD your God was your king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 8:5"> Now here is the king you have chosen, the one you requested. Behold, the LORD has placed a king over you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 24:14; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Psalms 81:12–15"> If you fear the LORD and serve Him and obey His voice, and if you do not rebel against the command of the LORD, and if both you and the king who rules over you follow the LORD your God, then all will be well.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="then all will be well is implied; Literally If you fear the LORD and serve Him and obey His voice, and if you do not rebel against the command of the LORD, (then) both you and the king who rules over you will follow the LORD your God.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Isaiah 1:20; Isaiah 3:11"> But if you disobey the LORD and rebel against His command, then the hand of the LORD will be against you as it was against your fathers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX against your king">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:13; Exodus 14:31; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Now, therefore, present yourselves and see this great thing that the LORD will do before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Proverbs 26:1; 1 Samuel 7:9–10"> Is it not the wheat harvest today? I will call on the LORD to send thunder and rain, so that you will know and see what a great evil you have committed in the sight of the LORD by asking for a king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Ezra 10:9; Psalms 106:12–13"> So Samuel called to the LORD, and on that day the LORD sent thunder and rain.<br />As a result, all the people greatly feared the LORD and Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; 1 John 5:16; 1 Samuel 12:23"> They pleaded with Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for your servants so that we will not die! For we have added to all our sins the evil of asking for a king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:16; Joshua 23:6; 1 Peter 3:16"> “Do not be afraid,” Samuel replied. “Even though you have committed all this evil, do not turn aside from following the LORD, but serve the LORD with all your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 10:15; Jeremiah 16:19"> Do not turn aside after worthless things that cannot profit you or deliver you, for they are empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 14:2; Psalms 106:8"> Indeed, for the sake of His great name, the LORD will not abandon His people, because He was pleased to make you His own.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:9; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> As for me, far be it from me that I should sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you. And I will continue to teach you the good and right way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 5:12"> Above all, fear the LORD and serve Him faithfully with all your heart; consider what great things He has done for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Hosea 10:3; 1 Samuel 31:1–5"> But if you persist in doing evil, both you and your king will be swept away.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
