## Elkanah and His Wives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:34; 1 Chronicles 6:25–27; 1 Samuel 1:19"> Now there was a man named Elkanah who was from Ramathaim-zophim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or from Ramathaim, a Zuphite; see LXX and 1 Chronicles 6:26 and 35.">a</a></sup>in the hill country of Ephraim. He was the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Elihu is also called Eliab and Eliel; see 1 Chronicles 6:27 and 34.">b</a></sup>the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephraimite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:1–2; Judges 13:2; Genesis 29:23–29"> He had two wives, one named Hannah and the other Peninnah. And Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:41; Joshua 18:1; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Year after year Elkanah would go up from his city to worship and sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts at Shiloh, where Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17–18; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> And whenever the day came for Elkanah to present his sacrifice, he would give portions to his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:2; Genesis 29:30–31; Genesis 45:22"> But to Hannah he would give a double portion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or a choice portion">c</a></sup>for he loved her even though the LORD had closed her womb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:14; Job 24:21; Leviticus 18:18"> Because the LORD had closed Hannah’s womb, her rival would provoke her viciously to taunt her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:19"> And this went on year after year. Whenever Hannah went up to the house of the LORD, her rival taunted her until she wept and would not eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:15; Job 6:14; Isaiah 54:6"> “Hannah, why are you crying?” her husband Elkanah asked. “Why won’t you eat? Why is your heart so grieved? Am I not better to you than ten sons?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hannah Prays for a Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:3; Psalms 27:4; Psalms 5:7"> So after they had finished eating and drinking in Shiloh, Hannah stood up. Now Eli the priest was sitting on a chair by the doorpost of the temple of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 7:11; Luke 22:44; Job 10:1"> In her bitter distress, Hannah prayed to the LORD and wept with many tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Numbers 6:5; Genesis 29:32"> And she made a vow, saying, “O LORD of Hosts, if only You will look upon the affliction of Your maidservant and remember me, not forgetting Your maidservant but giving her a son, then I will dedicate him to the LORD all the days of his life, and no razor shall ever touch his head.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16; Luke 18:1"> As Hannah kept on praying before the LORD, Eli watched her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:26; Acts 2:13; Genesis 24:42–45"> Hannah was praying in her heart, and though her lips were moving, her voice could not be heard.<br />So Eli thought she was drunk</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Job 8:2; Proverbs 6:9"> and said to her, “How long will you be drunk? Put away your wine!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Lamentations 2:19; Psalms 42:4"> “No, my lord,” Hannah replied. “I am a woman troubled in spirit. I have not had any wine or strong drink, but I have poured out my soul before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Job 6:2–3; Matthew 12:34–35"> Do not take your servant for a wicked woman, for all this time I have been praying out of the depth of my anguish and grief.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Judges 18:6; Psalms 20:3–5"> “Go in peace,” Eli replied, “and may the God of Israel grant the petition you have asked of Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Romans 15:13; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> “May your maidservant find favor with you,” said Hannah. Then she went on her way, and she began to eat, and her face was no longer downcast.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Birth of Samuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:1; Genesis 30:22; 1 Samuel 1:11"> The next morning they got up early to bow in worship before the LORD, and then they returned home to Ramah.<br />And Elkanah had relations with his wife Hannah, and the LORD remembered her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:51–52; Matthew 1:21; Exodus 2:10"> So in the course of time, Hannah conceived and gave birth to a son. She named him Samuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Samuel sounds like the Hebrew for heard of God.">d</a></sup>saying, “Because I have asked for him from the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; Deuteronomy 12:11; Joshua 24:15"> Then Elkanah and all his house went up to make the annual sacrifice to the LORD and to fulfill his vow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:28; 1 Samuel 1:11; Luke 2:22"> but Hannah did not go. “After the boy is weaned,” she said to her husband, “I will take him to appear before the LORD and to stay there permanently.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="MT; DSS include I will offer him as a Nazirite for all time.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:25; Luke 11:27; Genesis 21:7–8"> “Do what you think is best,” her husband Elkanah replied, “and stay here until you have weaned him. Only may the LORD confirm His word.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="MT; DSS, LXX, and Syriac your word">f</a></sup><br />So Hannah stayed and nursed her son until she had weaned him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 15:9–10; Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> Once she had weaned him, Hannah took the boy with her, along with a three-year-old bull,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="DSS, LXX, and Syriac; MT three bulls">g</a></sup>an ephah of flour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="An ephah is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters (probably about 25.5 pounds or 11.6 kilograms of flour).">h</a></sup>and a skin of wine. Though the boy was still young, she brought him to the house of the LORD at Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 18:15–16; Luke 2:22"> And when they had slaughtered the bull, they brought the boy to Eli.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 4:30; 2 Kings 2:4; 2 Kings 2:6"> “Please, my lord,” said Hannah, “as surely as you live, my lord, I am the woman who stood here beside you praying to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 5:15; Psalms 118:5; Matthew 7:7"> I prayed for this boy, and since the LORD has granted me what I asked of Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:52; 2 Timothy 3:15"> I now dedicate the boy to the LORD. For as long as he lives, he is dedicated to the LORD.”<br />So they worshiped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="One DSS manuscript; MT he worshiped">i</a></sup>the LORD there.</span>
</div>
</div>
