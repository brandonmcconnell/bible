## David Spares Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:28–29; Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Samuel 23:19"> After Saul had returned from pursuing the Philistines, he was told, “David is in the wilderness of En-gedi.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:2; Psalms 104:18; Psalms 37:32"> So Saul took three thousand chosen men from all Israel and went to look for David and his men in the region of the Rocks of the Wild Goats.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:24; Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7"> Soon Saul came to the sheepfolds along the road, where there was a cave, and he went in to relieve himself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally cover his feet, a euphemism for relieving oneself">a</a></sup>And David and his men were hiding in the recesses of the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8–11; 1 Samuel 24:10; 1 Samuel 26:23"> So David’s men said to him, “This is the day about which the LORD said to you, ‘Behold, I will deliver your enemy into your hand, that you may do with him as you wish.’”<br />Then David crept up and stealthily cut off a corner of Saul’s robe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:10; 2 Kings 22:19; 1 John 3:20–21"> Afterward, David’s conscience was stricken because he had cut off the corner of Saul’s robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Job 31:29–30; 1 Samuel 26:9–11"> So he said to his men, “The LORD forbid that I should do such a thing to my master, the LORD’s anointed. May I never lift my hand against him, since he is the LORD’s anointed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Psalms 7:4; Romans 12:17–21"> With these words David restrained his men, and he did not let them rise up against Saul. Then Saul left the cave and went on his way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23–24; Romans 13:7; Exodus 20:12"> After that, David got up, went out of the cave, and called out to Saul, “My lord the king!”<br />When Saul looked behind him, David bowed facedown in reverence</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:21–22; Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 16:28"> and said to Saul, “Why do you listen to the words of men who say, ‘Look, David intends to harm you’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; 1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> Behold, this day you have seen with your own eyes that the LORD delivered you into my hand in the cave. I was told to kill you, but I spared you and said, ‘I will not lift my hand against my lord, since he is the LORD’s anointed.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 23:14; 1 Samuel 23:23"> See, my father, look at the corner of your robe in my hand. For I cut it off, but I did not kill you. Know and see that there is no evil or rebellion in my hands. I have not sinned against you, even though you are hunting me down to take my life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Judges 11:27; Psalms 35:1"> May the LORD judge between you and me, and may the LORD take vengeance on you, but my hand will never be against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:20; Matthew 7:16–18; Matthew 12:33–34"> As the old proverb says, ‘Wickedness proceeds from the wicked.’ But my hand will never be against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 17:43; 2 Samuel 9:8"> Against whom has the king of Israel come out? Whom are you pursuing? A dead dog? A flea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 35:1; Psalms 119:154"> May the LORD be our judge and decide between you and me. May He take notice and plead my case and deliver me from your hand.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Oath to Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 33:4; Luke 21:15"> When David had finished saying these things, Saul called back, “Is that your voice, David my son?”<br />Then Saul wept aloud</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; 1 Samuel 26:21; Genesis 38:26"> and said to David, “You are more righteous than I, for you have rewarded me with good, though I have rewarded you with evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 31:8; 1 Samuel 23:12"> And you have declared this day how you have treated me well, for when the LORD delivered me into your hand, you did not kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:20; 1 Samuel 23:21; Proverbs 25:21–22"> When a man finds his enemy, does he let him go away unharmed? May the LORD reward you with good for what you have done for me this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:17; Matthew 2:3–6; 2 Samuel 3:17–18"> Now I know for sure that you will be king and that the kingdom of Israel will be established in your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:23; 2 Samuel 21:6–8; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> So now, swear to me by the LORD that you will not cut off my descendants or wipe out my name from my father’s house.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:29; John 2:24; Matthew 10:16–17"> So David gave his oath to Saul. Then Saul returned home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.</span>
</div>
</div>
