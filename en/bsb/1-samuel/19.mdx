## Saul Tries to Kill David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:9"> Then Saul ordered his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David.<br />But Jonathan delighted greatly in David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; Acts 9:24; 1 Samuel 18:1–3"> so he warned David, saying, “My father Saul intends to kill you. Be on your guard in the morning; find a secret place and hide there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:9; 1 Samuel 20:13"> I will go out and stand beside my father in the field where you are, so I can ask about you. And if I find out anything, I will tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:20; Proverbs 31:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> Then Jonathan spoke well of David to his father Saul and said to him, “The king should not sin against his servant David; he has not sinned against you. In fact, his actions have been highly beneficial to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:13; 1 Chronicles 11:14; Judges 9:17"> He took his life in his hands when he struck down the Philistine, and the LORD worked a great salvation for all Israel. You saw it and rejoiced, so why would you sin against innocent blood by killing David for no reason?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–25; Jeremiah 5:2; Psalms 15:4"> Saul listened to the voice of Jonathan and swore an oath: “As surely as the LORD lives, David will not be put to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:2; 1 Samuel 16:21; 1 Samuel 18:13"> So Jonathan summoned David and told him all these things. Then Jonathan brought David to Saul, and David was with Saul as before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:3; Psalms 18:32–50"> When war broke out again, David went out and fought the Philistines and struck them with such a mighty blow that they fled before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 16:16"> But as Saul was sitting in his house with his spear in his hand, a spirit of distress<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or a harmful spirit">a</a></sup>from the LORD came upon him. While David was playing the harp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; 1 Samuel 18:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Saul tried to pin him to the wall with his spear. But David eluded him and the spear struck the wall. And David fled and escaped that night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–17; Judges 16:2"> Then Saul sent messengers to David’s house to watch him and kill him in the morning. But David’s wife Michal warned him, “If you do not run for your life tonight, tomorrow you will be dead!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; Acts 9:24–25; Psalms 34:19"> So Michal lowered David from the window, and he ran away and escaped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:17; Judges 18:14"> Then Michal took a household idol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or a household god; Hebrew teraphim; also in verse 16">b</a></sup>and laid it in the bed, placed some goat hair on its head, and covered it with a garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:5; 2 Samuel 16:17–19; 2 Samuel 17:20"> When Saul sent the messengers to seize David, Michal said, “He is ill.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3–4; Psalms 37:12; Job 31:31"> But Saul sent the messengers back to see David and told them, “Bring him up to me in his bed so I can kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when the messengers entered, there was the idol in the bed with the goat hair on its head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:17; Exodus 1:17–19"> And Saul said to Michal, “Why did you deceive me like this? You sent my enemy away, and he has escaped!”<br />Michal replied, “He said to me, ‘Help me get away, or I will kill you!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; Psalms 116:11; 1 Samuel 15:34"> So David ran away and escaped. And he went to Samuel at Ramah and told him all that Saul had done to him. Then he and Samuel went to Naioth and stayed there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 22:9–10; 1 Samuel 26:1"> When Saul was told that David was at Naioth in Ramah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 2:28; John 7:32; 1 Samuel 10:5–6"> he sent messengers to seize him. But when they saw the group of prophets prophesying, with Samuel leading them, the Spirit of God came upon them, and Saul’s messengers also began to prophesy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 27:22; Jeremiah 13:23; Joel 2:28"> When this was reported to Saul, he sent more messengers, but they began to prophesy as well.<br />So Saul tried again and sent messengers a third time, and even they began to prophesy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Finally, Saul himself left for Ramah and came to the large cistern at Secu, where he asked, “Where are Samuel and David?”<br />“At Naioth in Ramah,” he was told.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; 1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:20"> So Saul went to Naioth in Ramah. But the Spirit of God came upon even Saul, and he walked along prophesying until he came to Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20; Isaiah 20:2; Micah 1:8"> Then Saul stripped off his robes and also prophesied before Samuel. And he collapsed and lay naked all that day and night. That is why it is said, “Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
</div>
</div>
