## The Philistines Capture the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 5:1"> Thus the word of Samuel came to all Israel.<br />Now the Israelites went out to meet the Philistines in battle and camped at Ebenezer, while the Philistines camped at Aphek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Samuel 17:8; Joshua 7:5–8"> The Philistines arrayed themselves against Israel, and as the battle spread, Israel was defeated by the Philistines, who struck down about four thousand men on the battlefield.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Numbers 10:35; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5"> When the troops returned to the camp, the elders of Israel asked, “Why has the LORD brought defeat on us before the Philistines today? Let us bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD from Shiloh, so that it may go<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or He may go">a</a></sup>with us to save us from the hand of our enemies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:2; Numbers 7:89; Psalms 80:1"> So the people sent men to Shiloh, and they brought back the ark of the covenant of the LORD of Hosts, who sits enthroned between the cherubim. And the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of the covenant of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:5; Job 20:5; Amos 6:3"> When the ark of the covenant of the LORD entered the camp, all the Israelites raised such a great shout that the ground shook.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 32:17–18"> On hearing the noise of the shout, the Philistines asked, “What is this loud shouting in the camp of the Hebrews?”<br />And when they realized that the ark of the LORD had entered the camp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Exodus 14:25; Exodus 15:14–16"> the Philistines were afraid. “The gods have entered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or A god has entered">b</a></sup>their camp!” they said. “Woe to us, for nothing like this has happened before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:14; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 78:43–51"> Woe to us! Who will deliver us from the hand of these mighty gods? These are the gods who struck the Egyptians with all kinds of plagues in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Judges 13:1; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Take courage and be men, O Philistines! Otherwise, you will serve the Hebrews just as they served you. Now be men and fight!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 14:12; Deuteronomy 28:25"> So the Philistines fought, and Israel was defeated, and each man fled to his tent. The slaughter was very great—thirty thousand foot soldiers of Israel fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:34; Psalms 78:64; 1 Samuel 2:32"> The ark of God was captured, and Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, died.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Eli

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; 2 Samuel 15:32"> That same day a Benjamite ran from the battle line all the way to Shiloh, with his clothes torn and dirt on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:9; 1 Samuel 4:18; Joshua 7:9"> When he arrived, there was Eli, sitting on his chair beside the road and watching, because his heart trembled for the ark of God.<br />When the man entered the city to give a report, the whole city cried out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Eli heard the outcry and asked, “Why this commotion?”<br />So the man hurried over and reported to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Psalms 90:10; 1 Kings 14:4"> Now Eli was ninety-eight years old, and his gaze was fixed because he could not see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:4; 1 Samuel 3:6; Joshua 7:19"> “I have just come from the battle,” the man said to Eli. “I fled from there today.”<br />“What happened, my son?” Eli asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> The messenger answered, “Israel has fled before the Philistines, and there has been a great slaughter among the people. Your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are both dead, and the ark of God has been captured.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21–22; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Psalms 69:9"> As soon as the ark of God was mentioned, Eli fell backward from his chair by the city gate, and being old and heavy, he broke his neck and died. And Eli had judged<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or governed or led">c</a></sup>Israel forty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now Eli’s daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant and about to give birth. When she heard the news of the capture of God’s ark and the deaths of her father-in-law and her husband, she collapsed and gave birth, for her labor pains overtook her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:17–18; Psalms 77:2; John 16:21"> As she was dying, the women attending to her said, “Do not be afraid, for you have given birth to a son!”<br />But she did not respond or pay any heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Jeremiah 2:11; Psalms 78:61"> And she named the boy Ichabod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ichabod means no glory.">d</a></sup>saying, “The glory has departed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or gone into exile; also in verse 22">e</a></sup>from Israel,” because the ark of God had been captured and her father-in-law and her husband had been killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:5–6; John 2:17"> “The glory has departed from Israel,” she said, “for the ark of God has been captured.”</span>
</div>
</div>
