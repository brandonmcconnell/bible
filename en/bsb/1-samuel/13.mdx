## War with the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:5; Micah 6:6"> Saul was thirty years old<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A few late LXX manuscripts; MT Saul was a son of a year">a</a></sup>when he became king, and he reigned over Israel forty-two years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or over Israel forty years (see Acts 13:21); MT over Israel two years">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 14:31"> He chose for himself three thousand men of Israel: Two thousand were with Saul at Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel, and a thousand were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin. And the rest of the troops he sent away, each to his own home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; Judges 3:27; Judges 6:34"> Then Jonathan attacked the Philistine outpost at Geba, and the Philistines heard about it. So Saul blew the ram’s horn throughout the land, saying, “Let the Hebrews hear!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Exodus 5:21; Zechariah 11:8"> And all Israel heard the news: “Saul has attacked an outpost of the Philistines, and now Israel has become a stench to the Philistines!” Then the people were summoned to join Saul at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:23; Joshua 11:4; Joshua 18:12"> Now the Philistines assembled to fight against Israel with three thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some LXX manuscripts and Syriac; Hebrew thirty thousand">c</a></sup>chariots, six thousand horsemen, and troops as numerous as the sand on the seashore. They went up and camped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Hebrews 11:38; Judges 10:9"> Seeing that they were in danger because their troops were hard-pressed, the men of Israel hid in caves and thickets, among the rocks, and in cellars and cisterns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Leviticus 26:17; Numbers 32:1–5"> Some Hebrews even crossed the Jordan into the land of Gad and Gilead. Saul, however, remained at Gilgal, and all his troops were quaking in fear.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul’s Unlawful Sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8"> And Saul waited seven days for the time appointed by Samuel, but Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the troops began to desert Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> So he said, “Bring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings.” And he offered up the burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Ruth 2:4; Psalms 129:8"> Just as he finished offering the burnt offering, Samuel arrived, and Saul went out to greet him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 13:16"> “What have you done?” Samuel asked.<br />And Saul replied, “When I saw that the troops were deserting me, and that you did not come at the appointed time and the Philistines were gathering at Michmash,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Amos 8:5; 2 Corinthians 9:7"> I thought, ‘Now the Philistines will descend upon me at Gilgal, and I have not sought the favor of the LORD.’ So I felt compelled to offer the burnt offering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 2 Chronicles 16:9; 1 Samuel 15:28"> “You have acted foolishly,” Samuel declared. “You have not kept the command that the LORD your God gave you; if you had, the LORD would have established your kingdom over Israel for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 13:22; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:19–37"> But now your kingdom will not endure; the LORD has sought a man after His own heart and appointed him ruler over His people, because you have not kept the command of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> Then Samuel set out from Gilgal and went up to Gibeah in Benjamin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX Then Samuel set out, and the rest of the people went up after Saul to meet the army; they went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin.">d</a></sup>And Saul numbered the troops who were with him, about six hundred men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel without Weapons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:3"> Now Saul and Jonathan his son and the troops with them were staying in Geba of Benjamin, while the Philistines camped at Michmash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:23; 1 Samuel 14:15; Joshua 19:3"> And raiders went out of the Philistine camp in three divisions. One headed toward Ophrah in the land of Shual,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:34; Joshua 18:13–14; Joshua 16:3"> another toward Beth-horon, and the third down the border road overlooking the Valley of Zeboim facing the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 24:14; Judges 5:8; Jeremiah 24:1"> And no blacksmith could be found in all the land of Israel, because the Philistines had said, “The Hebrews must not be allowed to make swords or spears.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Instead, all the Israelites would go down to the Philistines to sharpen their plowshares, mattocks, axes, and sickles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew and plowshares; (so plowshare appears twice in the Hebrew).">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The charge was a pim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A pim possibly refers to a polished stone weighing approximately 0.25 ounces or 7 grams found in excavations. This is equivalent to about two-thirds of a shekel and likely refers to the price charged by the Philistines for the services listed.">f</a></sup>for sharpening a plowshare or mattock, a third of a shekel for sharpening a pitchfork or an axe, and a third of a shekel for repointing an oxgoad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew does not include the currency unit of a shekel charged for sharpening a pitchfork, an axe, or an oxgoad; alternatively, possibly a third of a pim for each.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Zechariah 4:6; 1 Samuel 17:50"> So on the day of battle not a sword or spear could be found in the hands of the troops with Saul and Jonathan; only Saul and his son Jonathan had weapons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:28; 1 Samuel 14:1; 1 Samuel 14:4–5"> And a garrison of the Philistines had gone out to the pass at Michmash.</span>
</div>
</div>
