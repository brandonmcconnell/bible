## Eli’s Wicked Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:28–30; Jeremiah 2:8; Judges 2:10"> Now the sons of Eli were wicked men; they had no regard for the LORD</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 7:29–34"> or for the custom of the priests with the people.<br />When any man offered a sacrifice, the servant of the priest would come with a three-pronged meat fork while the meat was boiling</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:27–28; Isaiah 56:11; 1 Samuel 2:29"> and plunge it into the pan or kettle or cauldron or cooking pot. And the priest would claim for himself whatever the meat fork brought up. This is how they treated all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 3:3–5; Romans 16:18"> Even before the fat was burned, the servant of the priest would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, “Give the priest some meat to roast, because he will not accept boiled meat from you, but only raw.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Leviticus 3:16; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> And if any man said to him, “The fat must be burned first; then you may take whatever you want,” the servant would reply, “No, you must give it to me right now. If you refuse, I will take it by force!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:7–9; Genesis 13:13; Malachi 2:13"> Thus the sin of these young men was severe in the sight of the LORD, for they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="DSS and LXX; MT men">b</a></sup>were treating the LORD’s offering with contempt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Exodus 28:4; 1 Samuel 2:11"> Now Samuel was ministering before the LORD—a boy wearing a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; 1 Samuel 1:21; Exodus 23:14"> Each year his mother would make him a little robe and bring it to him when she went with her husband to offer the annual sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:27–28; Luke 2:34; Ruth 4:11"> And Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife, saying, “May the LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she dedicated to the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="DSS; MT in place of the one requested from the LORD">c</a></sup>” Then they would go home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 2:40; 1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 21:1"> So the LORD attended to Hannah, and she conceived and gave birth to three sons and two daughters.<br />Meanwhile, the boy Samuel grew up in the presence of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; 1 Samuel 2:13–17; Jeremiah 7:9–10"> Now Eli was very old, and he heard about everything his sons were doing to all Israel and how they were sleeping with the women who served at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Philippians 3:19; Acts 9:4"> “Why are you doing these things?” Eli said to his sons. “I hear about your wicked deeds from all these people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 2:8; 1 Timothy 3:7; Acts 6:3"> No, my sons; it is not a good report I hear circulating among the LORD’s people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:14; Joshua 11:20; Hebrews 10:26"> If a man sins against another man, God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or the judges">d</a></sup>can intercede for him; but if a man sins against the LORD, who can intercede for him?”<br />But they would not listen to their father, since the LORD intended to put them to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 2:40; Acts 2:47"> And the boy Samuel continued to grow in stature and in favor with the LORD and with man.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Prophecy against the House of Eli

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Exodus 4:27; Exodus 4:14–16"> Then a man of God came to Eli and told him, “This is what the LORD says: ‘Did I not clearly reveal Myself to your father’s house when they were in Egypt under Pharaoh’s house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 8:7–8; Numbers 18:19"> And out of all the tribes of Israel I selected your father to be My priest, to offer sacrifices on My altar, to burn incense, and to wear an ephod in My presence. I also gave to the house of your father all the food offerings of the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 32:15; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> Why then do you kick at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or scorn">e</a></sup>My sacrifice and offering that I have prescribed for My dwelling place? You have honored your sons more than Me by fattening yourselves with the best of all the offerings of My people Israel.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:26; Jeremiah 18:9–10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Therefore, the LORD, the God of Israel, declares:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I did indeed say that your house</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the house of your father</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">would walk before Me forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But now the LORD declares:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Far be it from Me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will honor those who honor Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but those who despise Me will be disdained.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:17–20; 1 Kings 2:26–27; Psalms 37:17"> Behold, the days are coming when I will cut off your strength and the strength of your father’s house, so that no one in it will reach old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4; 1 Samuel 4:11; Psalms 78:59–64"> You will see distress in My dwelling place. Despite all that is good in Israel, no one in your house will ever again reach old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26–27; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Samuel 22:21–23"> And every one of you that I do not cut off from My altar, I will cause your eyes to fail and your heart to grieve.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX his eyes will fail and his heart will grieve">f</a></sup>All your descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or increase">g</a></sup>will die by the sword of men.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="DSS and LXX; MT will die as mortals or will die in the prime of life">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:11; 1 Kings 13:3; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And this sign shall come to you concerning your two sons Hophni and Phinehas: They will both die on the same day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Kings 11:38"> Then I will raise up for Myself a faithful priest. He will do whatever is in My heart and mind. And I will build for him an enduring house, and he will walk before My anointed one for all time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 2:27; Malachi 1:13; Ezekiel 44:10–12"> And everyone left in your house will come and bow down to him for a piece of silver or a morsel of bread, pleading, “Please appoint me to some priestly office so that I can eat a piece of bread.”’”</span>
</div>
