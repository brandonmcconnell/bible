## The Amalekites Raid Ziklag

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7; 1 Samuel 29:11; 1 Samuel 27:8–10"> On the third day David and his men arrived in Ziklag, and the Amalekites had raided the Negev, attacked Ziklag, and burned it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Job 38:11; Psalms 76:10"> They had taken captive the women and all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew does not include and all.">a</a></sup>who were there, both young and old. They had not killed anyone, but had carried them off as they went on their way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Peter 1:6–7; Revelation 3:9"> When David and his men came to the city, they found it burned down and their wives and sons and daughters taken captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Numbers 14:1; Judges 21:2"> So David and the troops with him lifted up their voices and wept until they had no strength left to weep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 2 Samuel 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:2"> David’s two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail the widow of Nabal of Carmel, had been taken captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 56:11; Psalms 62:5"> And David was greatly distressed because the people spoke of stoning him, because the soul of every man grieved for his sons and daughters. But David found strength in the LORD his God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Destroys the Amalekites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; 1 Samuel 22:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:2–9"> Then David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, “Bring me the ephod.”<br />So Abiathar brought it to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18; Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Samuel 23:2"> and David inquired of the LORD: “Should I pursue these raiders? Will I overtake them?”<br />“Pursue them,” the LORD replied, “for you will surely overtake them and rescue the captives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2"> So David and his six hundred men went to the Brook of Besor, where some stayed behind</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:9; 1 Samuel 30:21; 1 Samuel 14:31"> because two hundred men were too exhausted to cross the brook. But David and four hundred men continued in pursuit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Matthew 25:35"> Now his men found an Egyptian in the field and brought him to David. They gave the man water to drink and food to eat—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 15:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 30:13"> a piece of a fig cake and two clusters of raisins. So he ate and was revived, for he had not had any food or water for three days and three nights.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 2:13; Proverbs 12:10; Job 31:13–15"> Then David asked him, “To whom do you belong, and where are you from?”<br />“I am an Egyptian,” he replied, “the slave of an Amalekite. My master abandoned me three days ago when I fell ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:44"> We raided the Negev of the Cherethites, the territory of Judah, and the Negev of Caleb, and we burned down Ziklag.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Joshua 9:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16"> “Will you lead me to these raiders?” David asked.<br />And the man replied, “Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or deliver me into the hand of my master, and I will lead you to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; 2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 16:23–30"> So he led David down, and there were the Amalekites spread out over all the land, eating, drinking, and celebrating the great amount of plunder they had taken from the land of the Philistines and the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; Psalms 18:42; 1 Samuel 15:3"> And David struck them down from twilight until the evening of the next day. Not a man escaped, except four hundred young men who fled, riding off on camels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> So David recovered everything the Amalekites had taken, including his two wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:9–10; 1 Samuel 30:8; Job 1:10"> Nothing was missing, young or old, son or daughter, or any of the plunder the Amalekites had taken. David brought everything back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:26; Numbers 31:9–12; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And he took all the flocks and herds, which his men drove ahead of the other livestock, calling out, “This is David’s plunder!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Spoils Are Divided

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; 1 Samuel 30:10; Judges 8:15"> When David came to the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow him and who were left behind at the Brook of Besor, they came out to meet him and the troops with him. As David approached the men, he greeted them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> but all the wicked and worthless men among those who had gone with David said, “Because they did not go with us, we will not share with them the plunder we recovered, except for each man’s wife and children. They may take them and go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Psalms 44:2–7; Acts 7:2"> But David said, “My brothers, you must not do this with what the LORD has given us. He has protected us and delivered into our hands the raiders who came against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:27; Psalms 68:12"> Who will listen to your proposal? The share of the one who went to battle will match the share of the one who stayed with the supplies. They will share alike.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13"> And so it has been from that day forward. David established this statute and ordinance for Israel to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Genesis 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> When David arrived in Ziklag, he sent some of the plunder to his friends, the elders of Judah, saying, “Here is a gift for you from the plunder of the LORD’s enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 19:8; Judges 1:22–23"> He sent gifts to those in Bethel, Ramoth Negev, and Jattir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> to those in Aroer, Siphmoth, and Eshtemoa;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Samuel 27:10; 1 Samuel 15:6"> to those in Racal and in the cities of the Jerahmeelites and Kenites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4; Joshua 15:42"> to those in Hormah, Bor-ashan, and Athach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 2 Samuel 15:10; 2 Samuel 4:1"> and to those in Hebron and in all the places where David and his men had roamed.</span>
</div>
</div>
