## The Burden against Nineveh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> This is the burden against Nineveh, the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; Psalms 94:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the LORD is avenging and full of wrath.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The LORD takes vengeance on His foes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and reserves wrath for His enemies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is slow to anger</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and great in power;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the LORD will by no means</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">leave the guilty unpunished.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His path is in the whirlwind and storm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and clouds are the dust beneath His feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; Psalms 106:9; Psalms 114:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rebukes the sea and dries it up;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He makes all the rivers run dry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bashan and Carmel wither,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the flower of Lebanon wilts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains quake before Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hills melt away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the earth trembles at His presence—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the world and all its dwellers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can withstand His indignation?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who can endure His burning anger?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His wrath is poured out like fire;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even rocks are shattered before Him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a stronghold in the day of distress;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He cares for those who trust in Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But with an overwhelming flood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will make an end of Nineveh</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally of her place">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pursue His enemies into darkness.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; Psalms 33:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whatever you plot against the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will bring to an end.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Affliction will not rise up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a second time.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they will be entangled as with thorns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and consumed like the drink of a drunkard—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like stubble that is fully dry.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">From you, O Nineveh, comes forth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a plotter of evil against the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a counselor of wickedness.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Though they are allied and numerous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet they will be cut down and pass away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Though I have afflicted you, O Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will afflict you no longer.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will now break their yoke from your neck</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and tear away your shackles.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> The LORD has issued a command concerning you, O Nineveh:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There will be no descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to carry on your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off the carved image and cast idol</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the house of your gods;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will prepare your grave,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for you are contemptible.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look to the mountains—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the feet of one who brings good news,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who proclaims peace!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Celebrate your feasts, O Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">fulfill your vows.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the wicked will never again march through you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be utterly cut off.</span></span>
</div>
