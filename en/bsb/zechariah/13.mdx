## An End to Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; John 1:29; Psalms 51:2"> “On that day a fountain will be opened to the house of David and the people of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and impurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; Jeremiah 23:14–15; Hosea 2:17"> And on that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, I will erase the names of the idols from the land, and they will no longer be remembered. I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of impurity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the unclean spirit">a</a></sup>from the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6–11; Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 14:9"> And if anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will say to him, ‘You shall not remain alive, because you have spoken falsely in the name of the LORD.’ When he prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will pierce him through.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:6–7; Matthew 3:4; Revelation 11:3"> And on that day every prophet who prophesies will be ashamed of his vision, and he will not put on a hairy cloak in order to deceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 7:14; Acts 19:17–20"> He will say, ‘I am not a prophet; I work the land, for I was purchased as a servant in my youth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or I am a farmer, for the land has been my livelihood since my youth.">b</a></sup>’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:35; Psalms 22:16; Proverbs 27:5–6"> If someone asks him, ‘What are these wounds on your chest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally between your hands">c</a></sup>?’ he will answer, ‘These are the wounds I received in the house of my friends.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Shepherd Struck, the Sheep Scattered

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:27; Matthew 26:31; John 16:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, O sword, against My Shepherd,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against the man who is My Companion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 26:31 and Mark 14:27">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will turn My hand against the little ones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in all the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">two-thirds will be cut off and perish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a third will be left in it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 29:11–12; Acts 2:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">This third I will bring through the fire;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will refine them like silver</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and test them like gold.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will call on My name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will answer them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will say, ‘They are My people,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will say, ‘The LORD is our God.’”</span></span>
</div>
