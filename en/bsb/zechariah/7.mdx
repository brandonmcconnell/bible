## A Call to Justice and Mercy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:1; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 1:1"> In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, the month of Chislev.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Chislev is the ninth month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of November and December.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Jeremiah 26:19; 1 Samuel 13:12"> Now the people of Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech, along with their men,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Bethel-sharezer had sent Regem-melech, along with his men,">b</a></sup>to plead before the LORD</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 8:19; James 4:8–10; Malachi 2:7"> by asking the priests of the house of the LORD of Hosts, as well as the prophets, “Should I weep and fast in the fifth month, as I have done these many years?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> Then the word of the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:12; Isaiah 58:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> “Ask all the people of the land and the priests, ‘When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months for these seventy years, was it really for Me that you fasted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:20–21; 1 Corinthians 11:26–29; Hosea 8:13"> And when you were eating and drinking, were you not doing so simply for yourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–20; Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 7:5"> Are these not the words that the LORD proclaimed through the earlier prophets, when Jerusalem and its surrounding towns were populous and prosperous, and the Negev and the foothills<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">c</a></sup>were inhabited?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of the LORD came to Zechariah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Psalms 82:2–4; Proverbs 21:3"> “This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Administer true justice. Show loving devotion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">d</a></sup>and compassion to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Isaiah 1:23; James 5:4"> Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the foreigner or the poor. And do not plot evil in your hearts against one another.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Jeremiah 8:5; Hosea 4:16"> But they refused to pay attention and turned a stubborn shoulder; they stopped up their ears from hearing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew they made their ears too heavy to hear">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Nehemiah 9:29–30; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> They made their hearts like flint and would not listen to the law or to the words that the LORD of Hosts had sent by His Spirit through the earlier prophets. Therefore great anger came from the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–28; Jeremiah 14:12; Isaiah 1:15"> And just as I had called and they would not listen, so when they called I would not listen, says the LORD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 23:19"> But I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations that they had not known, and the land was left desolate behind them so that no one could come or go. Thus they turned the pleasant land into a desolation.”</span>
</div>
</div>
