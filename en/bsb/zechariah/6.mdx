## The Vision of the Four Chariots

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–19; Zechariah 6:5; Daniel 8:22"> And again I lifted up my eyes and saw four chariots coming out from between two mountains—mountains of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 1:8; Revelation 6:2–6; Zechariah 6:6"> The first chariot had red horses, the second black horses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:2; Revelation 6:8; Zechariah 1:8"> the third white horses, and the fourth dappled horses—all of them strong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 5:10; Zechariah 1:19–21"> So I inquired of the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:3–4"> And the angel told me, “These are the four spirits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or winds">a</a></sup>of heaven, going forth from their station before the Lord of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 6:1"> The one with the black horses is going toward the land of the north, the one with the white horses toward the west,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the one with the white horses after them">b</a></sup>and the one with the dappled horses toward the south.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; Zechariah 1:10; Job 1:6–7"> As the strong horses went out, they were eager to go and patrol the earth; and the LORD said, “Go and patrol the earth.” So they patrolled the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 18:3–4"> Then the LORD summoned me and said, “Behold, those going to the land of the north have given rest to My Spirit in the land of the north.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crown and the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:1; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:1"> The word of the LORD also came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Isaiah 66:20"> “Take an offering from the exiles—from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have arrived from Babylon—and go that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Psalms 21:3; Song 3:11"> Take silver and gold, make an ornate crown, and set it on the head of the high priest, Joshua son of Jehozadak.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jehozadak is a variant of Jozadak; see Ezra 3:2.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 3:8; Isaiah 4:2; Jeremiah 33:15"> And you are to tell him that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Here is a man whose name is the Branch, and He will branch out from His place and build the temple of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Isaiah 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> Yes, He will build the temple of the LORD; He will be clothed in splendor and will sit on His throne and rule. And He will be a priest on His throne,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or And there will be a priest on His throne">d</a></sup>and there will be peaceful counsel between the two.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> The crown will reside in the temple of the LORD as a memorial to Helem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew; Syriac Heldai; see verse 10.">e</a></sup>Tobijah, Jedaiah, and the gracious<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or and Hen; Syriac and Josiah; see verse 10.">f</a></sup>son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10; Zechariah 3:7; Isaiah 58:10–14"> Even those far away will come and build the temple of the LORD, and you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent Me to you. This will happen if you diligently obey the voice of the LORD your God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
