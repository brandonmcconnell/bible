## Daniel’s Vision by the Tigris

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 8:26"> In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia, a message was revealed to Daniel, who was called Belteshazzar. The message was true, and it concerned a great conflict.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or true and greatly burdensome">a</a></sup>And the understanding of the message was given to him in a vision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:4; Daniel 9:24–27; Ezra 9:4–5"> In those days I, Daniel, was mourning for three full weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:17; Daniel 11:8; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> I ate no rich food, no meat or wine entered my mouth, and I did not anoint myself with oil until the three weeks were completed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:14; Daniel 8:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, as I was standing on the bank of the great river, the Tigris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13–15; Daniel 12:6–7; Ezekiel 9:2"> I lifted up my eyes, and behold, there was a certain man dressed in linen, with a belt of fine gold from Uphaz around his waist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 19:12; Revelation 1:13–17; Matthew 17:2"> His body was like beryl, his face like the brilliance of lightning, his eyes like flaming torches, his arms and legs like the gleam of polished bronze, and his voice like the sound of a multitude.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Acts 22:9; Genesis 3:10"> Only I, Daniel, saw the vision; the men with me did not see it, but a great terror fell upon them, and they ran and hid themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:27"> So I was left alone, gazing at this great vision. No strength remained in me; my face grew deathly pale, and I was powerless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; Job 4:13; Genesis 15:12"> I heard the sound of his words, and as I listened, I fell into a deep sleep, with my face to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Jeremiah 1:9; Daniel 8:18"> Suddenly, a hand touched me and set me trembling on my hands and knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 26:16; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 8:16–17"> He said to me, “Daniel, you are a man who is highly precious. Consider carefully the words that I am about to say to you. Stand up, for I have now been sent to you.”<br />And when he had said this to me, I stood up trembling.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:30–31; Daniel 9:20–23; Daniel 10:19"> “Do not be afraid, Daniel,” he said, “for from the first day that you purposed to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard, and I have come in response to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Daniel 10:20–21"> However, the prince of the kingdom of Persia opposed me for twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, for I had been left there with the kings of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Daniel 2:28; Daniel 12:4"> Now I have come to explain to you what will happen to your people in the latter days, for the vision concerns those days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Ezekiel 24:27; Daniel 10:9"> While he was speaking these words to me, I set my face toward the ground and became speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 6:7; Daniel 8:15; Jeremiah 1:9"> And suddenly one with the likeness of a man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Most MT manuscripts; literally with the likeness of sons of man; DSS, LXX, and one MT manuscript with the likeness of the hand of a man">b</a></sup>touched my lips, and I opened my mouth and said to the one standing before me, “My lord, because of the vision, I am overcome with anguish, and I have no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Exodus 24:10–11; Mark 12:36"> How can I, your servant, speak with you, my lord? Now I have no strength, nor is any breath left in me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Luke 22:43; Daniel 8:18"> Again the one with the likeness of a man touched me and strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Joshua 1:9; Judges 6:23"> “Do not be afraid, you who are highly precious,” he said. “Peace be with you! Be strong now; be very strong!”<br />As he spoke with me, I was strengthened and said, “Speak, my lord, for you have strengthened me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:2–4; Daniel 8:5–8"> “Do you know why I have come to you?” he said. “I must return at once to fight against the prince of Persia, and when I have gone forth, behold, the prince of Greece<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew of Javan">c</a></sup>will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:13; Daniel 12:1; Revelation 12:7"> But first I will tell you what is inscribed in the Book of Truth. Yet no one has the courage to support me against these, except Michael your prince.</span>
</div>
</div>
