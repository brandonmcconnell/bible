## Jephthah Defeats Ephraim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:1; James 4:1–2; James 3:16"> Then the men of Ephraim assembled and crossed the Jordan to Zaphon. They said to Jephthah, “Why have you crossed over to fight the Ammonites without calling us to go with you? We will burn your house down with you inside!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> But Jephthah replied, “My people and I had a serious conflict with the Ammonites, and when I called, you did not save me out of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 28:21; Job 13:14"> When I saw that you would not save me, I risked my life and crossed over to the Ammonites, and the LORD delivered them into my hand. Why then have you come today to fight against me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> Jephthah then gathered all the men of Gilead and fought against Ephraim. And the men of Gilead struck them down because the Ephraimites had said, “You Gileadites are fugitives in Ephraim, living in the territories of Ephraim and Manasseh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> The Gileadites captured the fords of the Jordan leading to Ephraim, and whenever a fugitive from Ephraim would say, “Let me cross over,” the Gileadites would ask him, “Are you an Ephraimite?”<br />If he answered, “No,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:2; Psalms 69:15; Mark 14:70"> they told him, “Please say Shibboleth.”<br />If he said, “Sibboleth,” because he could not pronounce it correctly, they seized him and killed him at the fords of the Jordan. So at that time 42,000 Ephraimites were killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Jephthah judged Israel six years, and when he died, he was buried in one of the cities of Gilead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX in his city in Gilead">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> After Jephthah, Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> He had thirty sons, as well as thirty daughters whom he gave in marriage to men outside his clan; and for his sons he brought back thirty wives from elsewhere. Ibzan judged Israel seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Ibzan died, and he was buried in Bethlehem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> After Ibzan, Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:13; 1 Chronicles 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> Then Elon the Zebulunite died, and he was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After Elon, Abdon son of Hillel, from Pirathon, judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, who rode on seventy donkeys. And he judged Israel eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> Then Abdon son of Hillel, from Pirathon, died, and he was buried at Pirathon in Ephraim, in the hill country of the Amalekites.</span>
</div>
</div>
