## Jephthah Delivers Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32; Judges 6:12; 2 Kings 5:1"> Now Jephthah the Gileadite was a mighty man of valor; he was the son of a prostitute, and Gilead was his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Proverbs 2:16; Deuteronomy 23:2"> And Gilead’s wife bore him sons who grew up, drove Jephthah out, and said to him, “You shall have no inheritance in our father’s house, because you are the son of another woman.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 1 Samuel 22:2; 2 Samuel 10:8"> So Jephthah fled from his brothers and settled in the land of Tob, where worthless men gathered around him and traveled with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 10:9"> Some time later, when the Ammonites fought against Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 7:35–39; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> and made war with them, the elders of Gilead went to get Jephthah from the land of Tob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “Come,” they said, “be our commander, so that we can fight against the Ammonites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 45:4–5; Isaiah 60:14"> Jephthah replied to the elders of Gilead, “Did you not hate me and expel me from my father’s house? Why then have you come to me now, when you are in distress?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 10:18; 1 Kings 13:6; Exodus 10:17"> They answered Jephthah, “This is why we now turn to you, that you may go with us, fight the Ammonites, and become leader over all of us who live in Gilead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–29"> But Jephthah asked them, “If you take me back to fight the Ammonites and the LORD gives them to me, will I really be your leader?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Genesis 31:50; Jeremiah 29:23"> And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, “The LORD is our witness if we do not do as you say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 10:17; Judges 20:1"> So Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people made him their leader and commander. And Jephthah repeated all his terms in the presence of the LORD at Mizpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26; Matthew 18:15–16; Proverbs 25:8–9"> Then Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites, saying, “What do you have against me that you have come to fight against my land?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 32:22; Numbers 21:24–26; Deuteronomy 2:37"> The king of the Ammonites answered Jephthah’s messengers, “When Israel came up out of Egypt, they seized my land, from the Arnon to the Jabbok and all the way to the Jordan. Now, therefore, restore it peaceably.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> Jephthah again sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Numbers 21:27–30"> to tell him, “This is what Jephthah says: Israel did not take away the land of Moab or of the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:25; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 20:1"> But when Israel came up out of Egypt, they traveled through the wilderness to the Red Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the Sea of Reeds">a</a></sup>and came to Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Deuteronomy 2:4–9; Numbers 20:1"> Then Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, ‘Please let us pass through your land,’ but the king of Edom would not listen. They also sent messengers to the king of Moab, but he would not consent. So Israel stayed in Kadesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:1–8; Numbers 22:36; Numbers 21:4–13"> Then Israel traveled through the wilderness and bypassed the lands of Edom and Moab. They came to the east side of the land of Moab and camped on the other side of the Arnon. But they did not enter the territory of Moab, since the Arnon was its border.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 2:26–36; Deuteronomy 3:1–17"> And Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, and said to him, ‘Please let us pass through your land into our own place.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–32; Numbers 21:23–25; Psalms 136:17–21"> But Sihon would not trust Israel to pass through his territory. So he gathered all his people, encamped in Jahaz, and fought with Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then the LORD, the God of Israel, delivered Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, who defeated them. So Israel took possession of all the land of the Amorites who inhabited that country,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36"> seizing all the land from the Arnon to the Jabbok and from the wilderness to the Jordan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Now since the LORD, the God of Israel, has driven out the Amorites from before His people Israel, should you now possess it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Psalms 78:55; Micah 4:5"> Do you not possess whatever your god Chemosh grants you? So also, we possess whatever the LORD our God has granted us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> Are you now so much better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever contend with Israel or fight against them?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 12:5; Judges 10:8"> For three hundred years Israel has lived in Heshbon, Aroer, and their villages, as well as all the cities along the banks of the Arnon. Why did you not take them back during that time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:15; Genesis 16:5; 1 Samuel 24:12"> I have not sinned against you, but you have done me wrong by waging war against me. May the LORD, the Judge, decide today between the Israelites and the Ammonites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:11; Proverbs 16:18"> But the king of the Ammonites paid no heed to the message Jephthah sent him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jephthah’s Tragic Vow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> Then the Spirit of the LORD came upon Jephthah, and he passed through Gilead and Manasseh, then through Mizpah of Gilead. And from there he advanced against the Ammonites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11; Ecclesiastes 5:1–2"> Jephthah made this vow to the LORD: “If indeed You will deliver the Ammonites into my hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 66:13–14; Leviticus 27:28–29; 1 Samuel 1:28"> then whatever comes out the door of my house to greet me on my triumphant return from the Ammonites will belong to the LORD, and I will offer it up as a burnt offering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:4; Judges 2:18; Judges 3:10"> So Jephthah crossed over to the Ammonites to fight against them, and the LORD delivered them into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:17; Deuteronomy 2:36"> With a great blow he devastated twenty cities from Aroer to the vicinity of Minnith, as far as Abel-keramim. So the Ammonites were subdued before the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Exodus 15:20; Judges 10:17"> And when Jephthah returned home to Mizpah, there was his daughter coming out to meet him with tambourines and dancing! She was his only child; he had no son or daughter besides her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 14:7–9; Genesis 37:29–30; Genesis 42:36–38"> As soon as Jephthah saw her, he tore his clothes and said, “No! Not my daughter! You have brought me to my knees! You have brought great misery upon me, for I have given my word to the LORD and cannot take it back.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; Philippians 2:30; Romans 16:4"> “My father,” she replied, “you have given your word to the LORD. Do to me as you have said, for the LORD has avenged you of your enemies, the Ammonites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 1:25; 1 Samuel 1:6"> She also said to her father, “Let me do this one thing: Let me wander for two months through the mountains with my friends and mourn my virginity.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> “Go,” he said. And he sent her away for two months.<br />So she left with her friends and mourned her virginity upon the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Judges 11:31; Leviticus 27:28–29"> After two months, she returned to her father, and he did to her as he had vowed. And she had never had relations with a man.<br />So it has become a custom in Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 9:25; Judges 5:11"> that each year the young women of Israel go out for four days to lament the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite.</span>
</div>
</div>
