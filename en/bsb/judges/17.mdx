## Micah’s Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> Now a man named Micah from the hill country of Ephraim</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; 1 Samuel 23:21"> said to his mother, “The eleven hundred shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1,100 shekels is approximately 27.6 pounds or 12.5 kilograms of silver; also in verse 3.">a</a></sup>that were taken from you and about which I heard you utter a curse—I have the silver here with me; I took it.”<br />Then his mother said, “Blessed be my son by the LORD!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> And when he had returned the eleven hundred shekels of silver to his mother, she said, “I wholly dedicate the silver to the LORD for my son’s benefit, to make a graven image and a molten idol. Therefore I will now return it to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> So he returned the silver to his mother, and she took two hundred shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="200 shekels is approximately 5 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of silver.">b</a></sup>and gave them to a silversmith, who made them into a graven image and a molten idol. And they were placed in the house of Micah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> Now this man Micah had a shrine, and he made an ephod and some household idols, and ordained<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew filled the hand of; also in verse 12">c</a></sup>one of his sons as his priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> And there was a young Levite from Bethlehem in Judah who had been residing within the clan of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> This man left the city of Bethlehem in Judah to settle where he could find a place. And as he traveled, he came to Micah’s house in the hill country of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “Where are you from?” Micah asked him.<br />“I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah,” he replied, “and I am on my way to settle wherever I can find a place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> “Stay with me,” Micah said to him, “and be my father and priest, and I will give you ten shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10 shekels is approximately 4 ounces or 114 grams of silver.">d</a></sup>per year, a suit of clothes, and your provisions.”<br />So the Levite went in</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and agreed to stay with him, and the young man became like a son to Micah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> Micah ordained the Levite, and the young man became his priest and lived in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> Then Micah said, “Now I know that the LORD will be good to me, because a Levite has become my priest.”</span>
</div>
</div>
