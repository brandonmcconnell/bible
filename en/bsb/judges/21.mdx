## Wives for the Benjamites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Mark 6:23; Acts 23:12"> Now the men of Israel had sworn an oath at Mizpah, saying, “Not one of us will give his daughter in marriage to a Benjamite.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 20:26; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 30:4"> So the people came to Bethel and sat there before God until evening, lifting up their voices and weeping bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Joshua 7:7–9; Isaiah 63:17"> “Why, O LORD God of Israel,” they cried out, “has this happened in Israel? Today in Israel one tribe is missing!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; 1 Kings 8:64; Psalms 78:34–35"> The next day the people got up early, built an altar there, and presented burnt offerings and peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:1"> The Israelites asked, “Who among all the tribes of Israel did not come to the assembly before the LORD?” For they had taken a solemn oath that anyone who failed to come up before the LORD at Mizpah would surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> And the Israelites grieved for their brothers, the Benjamites, and said, “Today a tribe is cut off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> What should we do about wives for the survivors, since we have sworn by the LORD not to give them our daughters in marriage?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 2:5–6"> So they asked, “Which one of the tribes of Israel failed to come up before the LORD at Mizpah?” And, in fact, no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp for the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For when the people were counted, none of the residents of Jabesh-gilead were there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; 1 Samuel 11:7"> So the congregation sent 12,000 of their most valiant men and commanded them: “Go and put to the sword those living in Jabesh-gilead, including women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> This is what you are to do: Devote to destruction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.">a</a></sup>every male, as well as every female who has had relations with a man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12; Psalms 78:60"> So they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young women who had not had relations with a man, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh in the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> Then the whole congregation sent a message of peace to the Benjamites who were at the rock of Rimmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> And at that time the Benjamites returned and were given the women who were spared from Jabesh-gilead. But there were not enough women for all of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Isaiah 58:12"> The people grieved for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a void in the tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the elders of the congregation said, “What should we do about wives for those who remain, since the women of Benjamin have been destroyed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> They added, “There must be heirs for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe of Israel will not be wiped out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> But we cannot give them our daughters as wives.”<br />For the Israelites had sworn, “Cursed is he who gives a wife to a Benjamite.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> “But look,” they said, “there is a yearly feast to the LORD in Shiloh, which is north of Bethel east of the road that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So they commanded the Benjamites: “Go, hide in the vineyards</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> and watch. When you see the daughters of Shiloh come out to perform their dances, each of you is to come out of the vineyards, catch for himself a wife from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> When their fathers or brothers come to us to complain, we will tell them, ‘Do us a favor by helping them, since we did not get wives for each of them in the war. Since you did not actually give them your daughters, you have no guilt.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> The Benjamites did as instructed and carried away the number of women they needed from the dancers they caught. They went back to their own inheritance, rebuilt their cities, and settled in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And at that time, each of the Israelites returned from there to his own tribe and clan, each to his own inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.</span>
</div>
</div>
