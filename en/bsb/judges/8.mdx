## Gideon Defeats Zebah and Zalmunna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> Then the men of Ephraim said to Gideon, “Why have you done this to us? Why did you fail to call us when you went to fight against Midian?” And they contended with him violently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> But Gideon answered them, “Now what have I accomplished compared to you? Are not the gleanings of Ephraim better than the grape harvest of Abiezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> God has delivered Oreb and Zeeb, the two princes of Midian, into your hand. What was I able to do compared to you?” When he had said this, their anger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or their spirit">a</a></sup>against him subsided.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> Then Gideon and his three hundred men came to the Jordan and crossed it, exhausted yet still in pursuit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalms 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> So Gideon said to the men of Succoth, “Please give my troops some bread, for they are exhausted, and I am still pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> But the leaders of Succoth asked, “Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your possession, that we should give bread to your army?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> “Very well,” Gideon replied, “when the LORD has delivered Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, I will tear your flesh with the thorns and briers of the wilderness!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> From there he went up to Penuel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Penuel is a variant of Peniel; also in verses 9 and 17; see Genesis 32:30.">b</a></sup>and asked the same from them, but the men of Penuel gave the same response as the men of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> So Gideon told the men of Penuel, “When I return in triumph, I will tear down this tower!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their army of about fifteen thousand men—all that were left of the armies of the people of the east. A hundred and twenty thousand swordsmen had already fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> And Gideon went up by way of the caravan route east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and he attacked their army, taking them by surprise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> When Zebah and Zalmunna fled, Gideon pursued and captured these two kings of Midian, routing their entire army.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After this, Gideon son of Joash returned from the battle along the Ascent of Heres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> There he captured a young man of Succoth and interrogated him. The young man wrote down for him the names of the seventy-seven leaders and elders of Succoth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> And Gideon went to the men of Succoth and said, “Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me, saying, ‘Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your possession, that we should give bread to your weary men?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> Then he took the elders of the city, and using the thorns and briers of the wilderness, he disciplined the men of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> He also pulled down the tower of Penuel and killed the men of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalms 89:12"> Next, Gideon asked Zebah and Zalmunna, “What kind of men did you kill at Tabor?”<br />“Men like you,” they answered, “each one resembling the son of a king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> “They were my brothers,” Gideon replied, “the sons of my mother! As surely as the LORD lives, if you had let them live, I would not kill you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> So he said to Jether, his firstborn, “Get up and kill them.” But the young man did not draw his sword; he was fearful because he was still a youth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> Then Zebah and Zalmunna said, “Get up and kill us yourself, for as the man is, so is his strength.” So Gideon got up and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and he took the crescent ornaments from the necks of their camels.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gideon’s Ephod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> Then the Israelites said to Gideon, “Rule over us—you and your son and grandson—for you have saved us from the hand of Midian.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> But Gideon replied, “I will not rule over you, nor will my son. The LORD shall rule over you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> Then he added, “Let me make a request of you, that each of you give me an earring from his plunder.” (For the enemies had gold earrings because they were Ishmaelites.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> “We will give them gladly,” they replied.<br />So they spread out a garment, and each man threw an earring from his plunder onto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> The weight of the gold earrings he had requested was 1,700 shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1,700 shekels is approximately 42.7 pounds or 19.4 kilograms.">c</a></sup>in addition to the crescent ornaments, the pendants, the purple garments of the kings of Midian, and the chains from the necks of their camels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalms 106:39"> From all this Gideon made an ephod, which he placed in Ophrah, his hometown. But soon all Israel prostituted themselves by worshiping it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and his household.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Forty Years of Peace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> In this way Midian was subdued before the Israelites and did not raise its head again. So the land had rest for forty years in the days of Gideon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> and he—Jerubbaal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jerubbaal is another name for Gideon and probably means let Baal contend; also in verse 35; see Judges 6:32.">d</a></sup>son of Joash—returned home and settled down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> Gideon had seventy sons of his own,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew who came from his own loins">e</a></sup>since he had many wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> His concubine, who dwelt in Shechem, also bore him a son, and he named him Abimelech.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gideon’s Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> Later, Gideon son of Joash died at a ripe old age and was buried in the tomb of his father Joash in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> And as soon as Gideon was dead, the Israelites turned and prostituted themselves with the Baals, and they set up Baal-berith as their god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 78:42; Psalms 106:21; Psalms 78:11"> The Israelites failed to remember the LORD their God who had delivered them from the hands of all their enemies on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> They did not show kindness to the house of Jerubbaal (that is, Gideon) for all the good things he had done for Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
