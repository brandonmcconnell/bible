## Samson’s Revenge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 38:17; Genesis 29:21; Luke 15:29"> Later on, at the time of the wheat harvest, Samson took a young goat and went to visit his wife. “I want to go to my wife in her room,” he said. But her father would not let him enter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 14:20; Judges 14:16; Genesis 38:14"> “I was sure that you thoroughly hated her,” said her father, “so I gave her to one of the men who accompanied you. Is not her younger sister more beautiful than she? Please take her instead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 14:15"> Samson said to them, “This time I will be blameless in doing harm to the Philistines.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song 2:15; Psalms 63:10; Lamentations 5:18"> Then Samson went out and caught three hundred foxes. And he took torches, turned the foxes tail-to-tail, and fastened a torch between each pair of tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30; Exodus 22:6"> Then he lit the torches and released the foxes into the standing grain of the Philistines, burning up the piles of grain and the standing grain, as well as the vineyards and olive groves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Proverbs 22:8"> “Who did this?” the Philistines demanded.<br />“It was Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite,” they were told. “For his wife was given to his companion.”<br />So the Philistines went up and burned her and her father to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 14:4; Romans 12:19; Judges 14:19"> And Samson told them, “Because you have done this, I will not rest until I have taken vengeance upon you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> And he struck them ruthlessly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally he struck them hip and thigh">a</a></sup>with a great slaughter, and then went down and stayed in the cave at the rock of Etam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 15:17; Judges 15:19; Judges 15:14"> Then the Philistines went up, camped in Judah, and deployed themselves near the town of Lehi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally and spread out in Lehi">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “Why have you attacked us?” said the men of Judah.<br />The Philistines replied, “We have come to arrest Samson and pay him back for what he has done to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> In response, three thousand men of Judah went to the cave at the rock of Etam, and they asked Samson, “Do you not realize that the Philistines rule over us? What have you done to us?”<br />“I have done to them what they did to me,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Acts 7:25; 1 Kings 2:25"> But they said to him, “We have come down to arrest you and hand you over to the Philistines.”<br />Samson replied, “Swear to me that you will not kill me yourselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “No,” they answered, “we will not kill you, but we will tie you up securely and hand you over to them.” So they bound him with two new ropes and led him up from the rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 14:19; Judges 3:10; Psalms 118:11"> When Samson arrived in Lehi, the Philistines came out shouting against him. And the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon him. The ropes on his arms became like burnt flax, and the bonds broke loose from his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:8; Judges 3:31"> He found the fresh jawbone of a donkey, reached out his hand and took it, and struck down a thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Samson said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“With the jawbone of a donkey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have piled them into heaps.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or I have made them into donkeys">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the jawbone of a donkey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have slain a thousand men.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And when Samson had finished speaking, he cast the jawbone from his hand; and he named that place Ramath-lehi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ramath-lehi means the hill of the jawbone.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–8; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 8:4"> And being very thirsty, Samson cried out to the LORD, “You have accomplished this great deliverance through Your servant. Must I now die of thirst and fall into the hands of the uncircumcised?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 45:27; Psalms 34:6"> So God opened up the hollow place in Lehi, and water came out of it. When Samson drank, his strength returned, and he was revived. That is why he named it En-hakkore,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="En-hakkore means the spring of him who calls.">e</a></sup>and it remains in Lehi to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 16:31; Judges 13:5"> And Samson judged Israel for twenty years in the days of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
</div>
