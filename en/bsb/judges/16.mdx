## Samson and Delilah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Proverbs 22:14; Nehemiah 13:26"> Some time later, Samson fell in love with a woman in the Valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Judges 14:15; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> The lords of the Philistines went to her and said, “Entice him and find out the source of his great strength and how we can overpower him to tie him up and subdue him. Then each one of us will give you eleven hundred shekels of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1,100 shekels is approximately 27.6 pounds or 12.5 kilograms of silver.">a</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21; Proverbs 6:26; Proverbs 22:14"> So Delilah said to Samson, “Please tell me the source of your great strength and how you can be tied up and subdued.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:2–3; Romans 3:8; 1 Samuel 27:10"> Samson told her, “If they tie me up with seven fresh bowstrings that have not been dried, I will become as weak as any other man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26"> So the lords of the Philistines brought her seven fresh bowstrings that had not been dried, and she tied him up with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 58:9"> While the men were hidden in her room, she called out, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”<br />But he snapped the bowstrings like a strand of yarn seared by a flame. So the source of his strength remained unknown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:13; Luke 22:48; Judges 16:7"> Then Delilah said to Samson, “You have mocked me and lied to me! Now please tell me how you can be tied up.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:5; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 13:3"> He replied, “If they tie me up with new ropes that have never been used, I will become as weak as any other man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Delilah took new ropes, tied him up with them, and called out, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”<br />But while the men were hidden in her room, he snapped the ropes off his arms like they were threads.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Delilah said to Samson, “You have mocked me and lied to me all along! Tell me how you can be tied up.”<br />He told her, “If you weave the seven braids of my head into the web of a loom and tighten it with a pin, I will become as weak as any other man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See LXX and Vulgate; Hebrew does not include and tighten it with a pin, I will become as weak as any other man.">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Ezra 9:13–14"> So while he slept, Delilah took the seven braids of his hair and wove them into the web.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See LXX and Vulgate; Hebrew does not include So while he slept, Delilah took the seven braids of his hair and wove them into the web.">c</a></sup>Then she tightened it with a pin and called to him, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”<br />But he awoke from his sleep and pulled out the pin with the loom and the web.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Delilah Learns the Secret

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 14:16; 1 John 2:15–16; John 14:21–24"> “How can you say, ‘I love you,’” she asked, “when your heart is not with me? This is the third time you have mocked me and failed to reveal to me the source of your great strength!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21–23; Luke 18:5; Mark 14:24"> Finally, after she had pressed him daily with her words and pleaded until he was sick to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Judges 13:5; Proverbs 12:23"> Samson told her all that was in his heart: “My hair has never been cut, because I have been a Nazirite to God from my mother’s womb. If I am shaved, my strength will leave me, and I will become as weak as any other man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Judges 16:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> When Delilah realized that he had revealed to her all that was in his heart, she sent this message to the lords of the Philistines: “Come up once more, for he has revealed to me all that is in his heart.”<br />Then the lords of the Philistines came to her, bringing the money in their hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 7:26–27; Proverbs 7:21–23; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And having lulled him to sleep on her lap, she called a man to shave off the seven braids of his head. In this way she began to subdue him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew; some LXX manuscripts he began to grow weak">d</a></sup>and his strength left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:42–43; 1 Samuel 16:14; Joshua 7:12"> Then she called out, “Samson, the Philistines are here!”<br />When Samson awoke from his sleep, he thought, “I will escape as I did before and shake myself free.” But he did not know that the LORD had departed from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 2:19; Proverbs 14:14; Isaiah 47:2"> Then the Philistines seized him, gouged out his eyes, and brought him down to Gaza, where he was bound with bronze shackles and forced to grind grain in the prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:13–14; Psalms 106:44–45; Leviticus 26:44"> However, the hair of his head began to grow back after it had been shaved.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samson’s Vengeance and Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–5; Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:15–16"> Now the lords of the Philistines gathered together to offer a great sacrifice to their god Dagon. They rejoiced and said, “Our god has delivered Samson our enemy into our hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23; Judges 15:16"> And when the people saw him, they praised their god, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Our god has delivered into our hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">our enemy who destroyed our land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and multiplied our dead.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 19:6; Judges 9:27"> And while their hearts were merry, they said, “Call for Samson to entertain us.” So they called Samson out of the prison to entertain them. And they stationed him between the pillars.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Samson said to the servant who held his hand, “Lead me where I can feel the pillars supporting the temple, so I can lean against them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2; Joshua 2:8"> Now the temple was full of men and women; all the lords of the Philistines were there, and about three thousand men and women were on the roof watching Samson entertain them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:15; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 143:12"> Then Samson called out to the LORD: “O Lord GOD, please remember me. Strengthen me, O God, just once more, so that with one vengeful blow I may pay back the Philistines for my two eyes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Samson reached out for the two central pillars supporting the temple. Bracing himself against them with his right hand on one pillar and his left hand on the other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Matthew 16:25; Matthew 24:38–39"> Samson said, “Let me die with the Philistines.”<br />Then he pushed with all his might, and the temple fell on the lords and all the people in it. So in his death he killed more than he had killed in his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 15:20; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> Then Samson’s brothers and his father’s family came down, carried him back, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of his father Manoah. And he had judged Israel twenty years.</span>
</div>
