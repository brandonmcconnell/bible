## The Conquest of Canaan Proceeds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> After the death of Joshua, the Israelites inquired of the LORD, “Who will be the first to go up and fight for us against the Canaanites?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> “Judah shall go up,” answered the LORD. “Indeed, I have delivered the land into their hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> Then the men of Judah said to their brothers the Simeonites, “Come up with us to our allotted territory, and let us fight against the Canaanites. And we likewise will go with you to your territory.” So the Simeonites went with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> When Judah attacked, the LORD delivered the Canaanites and Perizzites into their hands, and they struck down ten thousand men at Bezek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there they found Adoni-bezek and fought against him, striking down the Canaanites and Perizzites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As Adoni-bezek fled, they pursued him, seized him, and cut off his thumbs and big toes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> Then Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off have gathered the scraps under my table. As I have done to them, so God has repaid me.” And they brought him to Jerusalem, where he died.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Capture of Jerusalem and Hebron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> Then the men of Judah fought against Jerusalem and captured it. They put the city to the sword and set it on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> Afterward, the men of Judah marched down to fight against the Canaanites living in the hill country, in the Negev, and in the foothills.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> Judah also marched against the Canaanites who were living in Hebron (formerly known as Kiriath-arba), and they struck down Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> From there they marched against the inhabitants of Debir (formerly known as Kiriath-sepher).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 18:23"> And Caleb said, “To the man who strikes down Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give my daughter Acsah in marriage.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> So Othniel son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz captured the city, and Caleb gave his daughter Acsah to him in marriage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> One day Acsah came to Othniel and urged him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX and Vulgate and he urged her; see Joshua 15:18.">b</a></sup>to ask her father for a field. When she got off her donkey, Caleb asked her, “What do you desire?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> “Give me a blessing,” she answered. “Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me springs of water as well.”<br />So Caleb gave her both the upper and lower springs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6"> Now the descendants of Moses’ father-in-law, the Kenite, went up with the men of Judah from the City of Palms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Jericho">c</a></sup>to the Wilderness of Judah in the Negev near Arad. They went to live among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> Then the men of Judah went with their brothers the Simeonites, attacked the Canaanites living in Zephath, and devoted the city to destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.">d</a></sup>So it was called Hormah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hormah means destruction.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> And Judah also captured<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX But Judah did not capture">f</a></sup>Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron—each with its territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; Psalms 60:12"> The LORD was with Judah, and they took possession of the hill country; but they could not drive out the inhabitants of the plains because they had chariots of iron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> Just as Moses had promised, Judah gave Hebron to Caleb, who drove out the descendants of the three sons of Anak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> The Benjamites, however, failed to drive out the Jebusites living in Jerusalem. So to this day the Jebusites live there among the Benjamites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; 2 Kings 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> The house of Joseph also attacked Bethel, and the LORD was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> They sent spies to Bethel (formerly known as Luz),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; 1 Samuel 30:15"> and when the spies saw a man coming out of the city, they said to him, “Please show us how to get into the city, and we will treat you kindly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> So the man showed them the entrance to the city, and they put the city to the sword but released that man and all his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; 2 Chronicles 1:17"> And the man went to the land of the Hittites, built a city, and called it Luz, which is its name to this day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Failure to Complete the Conquest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; Psalms 106:34–35"> At that time Manasseh failed to drive out the inhabitants of Beth-shean, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo, and their villages; for the Canaanites were determined to dwell in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When Israel became stronger, they pressed the Canaanites into forced labor, but they never drove them out completely.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16"> Ephraim also failed to drive out the Canaanites living in Gezer; so the Canaanites continued to dwell among them in Gezer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> Zebulun failed to drive out the inhabitants of Kitron and Nahalol; so the Canaanites lived among them and served as forced laborers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> Asher failed to drive out the inhabitants of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–35"> So the Asherites lived among the Canaanite inhabitants of the land, because they did not drive them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; Psalms 18:24"> Naphtali failed to drive out the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath. So the Naphtalites also lived among the Canaanite inhabitants of the land, but the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath served them as forced laborers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> The Amorites forced the Danites into the hill country and did not allow them to come down into the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> And the Amorites were determined to dwell in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. But when the house of Joseph grew in strength, they pressed the Amorites into forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> And the border of the Amorites extended from the Ascent of Akrabbim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or the Ascent of Scorpions or Scorpion Pass">g</a></sup>to Sela and beyond.</span>
</div>
</div>
