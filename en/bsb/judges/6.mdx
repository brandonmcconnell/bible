## Midian Oppresses Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26–29; Judges 2:19–20; Judges 2:13–14"> Again the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD; so He delivered them into the hand of Midian for seven years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:38; 1 Samuel 13:6; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> and the hand of Midian prevailed against Israel. Because of the Midianites, the Israelites prepared shelters for themselves in the mountains, caves, and strongholds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Judges 3:13; Judges 7:12"> Whenever the Israelites planted their crops, the Midianites, Amalekites, and other people of the east would come up and invade them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:51; Micah 6:15"> encamping against them as far as Gaza and destroying the produce of the land. They left Israel with no sustenance, neither sheep nor oxen nor donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 8:10; Jeremiah 46:23"> For the Midianites came with their livestock and their tents like a great swarm of locusts. They and their camels were innumerable, and they entered the land to ravage it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Jeremiah 5:17; Psalms 50:15"> Israel was greatly impoverished by Midian, and the Israelites cried out to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now when the Israelites cried out to the LORD because of Midian,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–32; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalms 136:10–16"> He sent them a prophet, who told them, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I brought you up out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 44:2–3"> I delivered you out of the hands of Egypt and all your oppressors. I drove them out before you and gave you their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:2; 2 Kings 17:35–39; Jeremiah 9:13"> And I said to you: ‘I am the LORD your God. You must not fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or worship">a</a></sup>the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you dwell.’ But you did not obey Me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Call of Gideon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 17:2; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 13:3"> Then the angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Angel; also in verses 12, 20, 21, and 22; corresponding pronouns may also be capitalized.">b</a></sup>of the LORD came and sat down under the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or terebinth or great tree; also in verse 19">c</a></sup>in Ophrah that belonged to Joash the Abiezrite, where his son Gideon was threshing wheat in a winepress to hide it from the Midianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Luke 1:28; Judges 2:18"> And the angel of the LORD appeared to Gideon and said, “The LORD is with you, O mighty man of valor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:7–9; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 59:1–2"> “Please, my Lord,” Gideon replied, “if the LORD is with us, why has all this happened to us? And where are all His wonders of which our fathers told us, saying, ‘Has not the LORD brought us up out of Egypt?’ But now the LORD has forsaken us and delivered us into the hand of Midian.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:5–9; Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 12:11"> The LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX The angel of the LORD or The Angel of the LORD; also in verse 16">d</a></sup>turned to him and said, “Go in the strength you have and save Israel from the hand of Midian. Am I not sending you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Exodus 3:11; Exodus 4:10"> “Please, my Lord,” Gideon replied, “how can I save Israel? Indeed, my clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest in my father’s house.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Exodus 3:12; Acts 11:21"> “Surely I will be with you,” the LORD replied, “and you will strike down all the Midianites as one man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:17; Exodus 33:13; Isaiah 7:11"> Gideon answered, “If I have found favor in Your sight, give me a sign that it is You speaking with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 13:15; Genesis 18:3; Genesis 18:5"> Please do not depart from this place until I return to You. Let me bring my offering and set it before You.”<br />And the LORD said, “I will stay until you return.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 13:15–19; Leviticus 2:4"> So Gideon went in and prepared a young goat and unleavened bread and an ephah of flour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="An ephah is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters (probably about 25.5 pounds or 11.6 kilograms of flour).">e</a></sup>He placed the meat in a basket and the broth in a pot and brought them out to present to Him under the oak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:19; 1 Kings 18:33–34"> And the angel of God said to him, “Take the meat and the unleavened bread, lay them on this rock, and pour out the broth.” And Gideon did so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Kings 18:38; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then the angel of the LORD extended the tip of the staff that was in his hand and touched the meat and the unleavened bread. And fire flared from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened bread. Then the angel of the LORD vanished from his sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Exodus 33:20; Deuteronomy 5:26"> When Gideon realized that it was the angel of the LORD, he said, “Oh no, Lord GOD! I have seen the angel of the LORD face to face!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; John 20:26; Genesis 43:23"> But the LORD said to him, “Peace be with you. Do not be afraid, for you will not die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Exodus 17:15; Judges 8:32"> So Gideon built an altar to the LORD there and called it The LORD Is Peace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew YHWH Shalom">f</a></sup>To this day it stands in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gideon Destroys Baal’s Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; Judges 3:7; Acts 5:29"> On that very night the LORD said to Gideon, “Take your father’s young bull and a second bull seven years old, tear down your father’s altar to Baal, and cut down the Asherah pole beside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Samuel 24:18; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Then build a proper altar to the LORD your God on the top of this stronghold. And with the wood of the Asherah pole you cut down, take the second bull and offer it as a burnt offering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; John 2:5; Deuteronomy 4:1"> So Gideon took ten of his servants and did as the LORD had told him. But because he was too afraid of his father’s household and the men of the city, he did it by night rather than in the daytime.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When the men of the city got up in the morning, there was Baal’s altar torn down, with the Asherah pole cut down beside it and the second bull offered up on the newly built altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> “Who did this?” they said to one another.<br />And after they had investigated thoroughly, they were told, “Gideon son of Joash did it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:9; Philippians 3:6; Jeremiah 50:38"> Then the men of the city said to Joash, “Bring out your son. He must die, because he has torn down Baal’s altar and cut down the Asherah pole beside it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 5:11"> But Joash said to all who stood against him, “Are you contending for Baal? Are you trying to save him? Whoever pleads his case will be put to death by morning! If Baal is a god, let him contend for himself with the one who has torn down his altar.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:11; Judges 7:1; 2 Samuel 11:21"> So on that day Gideon was called Jerubbaal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jerubbaal probably means let Baal contend.">g</a></sup>that is to say, “Let Baal contend with him,” because he had torn down Baal’s altar.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sign of the Fleece

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 17:16; Judges 6:3; Romans 8:35–39"> Then all the Midianites, Amalekites, and other people of the east gathered together, crossed over the Jordan, and camped in the Valley of Jezreel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 3:27; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Judges 3:10"> So the Spirit of the LORD came upon Gideon, who blew the ram’s horn and rallied the Abiezrites behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6–12; Judges 4:6"> Calling them to arms, Gideon sent messengers throughout Manasseh, as well as Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, so that they came up to meet him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 6:14; Matthew 16:1; Judges 6:17–20"> Then Gideon said to God, “If You are going to save Israel by my hand, as You have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 14:5; Matthew 10:5–6; Matthew 15:24"> then behold, I will place a fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If there is dew only on the fleece and all the ground is dry, then I will know that You are going to save Israel by my hand, as You have said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7"> And that is what happened. When Gideon arose the next morning, he squeezed the fleece and wrung out the dew—a bowlful of water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 18:32; Isaiah 50:2; Psalms 107:33–35"> Then Gideon said to God, “Do not be angry with me; let me speak one more time. Please allow me one more test with the fleece. This time let it be dry, and the ground covered with dew.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And that night God did so. Only the fleece was dry, and dew covered the ground.</span>
</div>
</div>
