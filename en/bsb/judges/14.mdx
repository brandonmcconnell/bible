## Samson’s Marriage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> One day Samson went down to Timnah, where he saw a young Philistine woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> So he returned and told his father and mother, “I have seen a daughter of the Philistines in Timnah. Now get her for me as a wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> But his father and mother replied, “Can’t you find a young woman among your relatives or among any of our people? Must you go to the uncircumcised Philistines to get a wife?”<br />But Samson told his father, “Get her for me, for she is pleasing to my eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> (Now his father and mother did not know this was from the LORD, who was seeking an occasion to move against the Philistines; for at that time the Philistines were ruling over Israel.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Samson went down to Timnah with his father and mother and came to the vineyards of Timnah. Suddenly a young lion came roaring at him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> and the Spirit of the LORD came powerfully upon him, and he tore the lion apart with his bare hands as one would tear a young goat. But he did not tell his father or mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Samson continued on his way down and spoke to the woman, because she was pleasing to his eyes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samson’s Riddle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> When Samson returned later to take her, he left the road to see the lion’s carcass, and in it was a swarm of bees, along with their honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> So he scooped some honey into his hands and ate it as he went along. And when he returned to his father and mother, he gave some to them and they ate it. But he did not tell them that he had taken the honey from the lion’s carcass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> Then his father went to visit the woman, and Samson prepared a feast there, as was customary for the bridegroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> And when the Philistines saw him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or when the bride’s parents saw him; literally when they saw him">a</a></sup>they selected thirty men to accompany him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> “Let me tell you a riddle,” Samson said to them. “If you can solve it for me within the seven days of the feast, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty sets of clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But if you cannot solve it, you must give me thirty linen garments and thirty sets of clothes.”<br />“Tell us your riddle,” they replied. “Let us hear it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> So he said to them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Out of the eater came something to eat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and out of the strong came something sweet.”</span><br />For three days they were unable to explain the riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> So on the fourth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX and Syriac; Hebrew seventh">b</a></sup>day they said to Samson’s wife, “Entice your husband to explain the riddle to us, or we will burn you and your father’s household to death. Did you invite us here to rob us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> Then Samson’s wife came to him, weeping, and said, “You hate me! You do not really love me! You have posed to my people a riddle, but have not explained it to me.”<br />“Look,” he said, “I have not even explained it to my father or mother, so why should I explain it to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> She wept the whole seven days of the feast, and finally on the seventh day, because she had pressed him so much, he told her the answer. And in turn she explained the riddle to her people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Before sunset on the seventh day, the men of the city said to Samson:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is sweeter than honey?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And what is stronger than a lion?”</span><br />So he said to them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If you had not plowed with my heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you would not have solved my riddle!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> Then the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon, killed thirty of their men, took their apparel, and gave their clothes to those who had solved the riddle. And burning with anger, Samson returned to his father’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> and his wife was given to one of the men who had accompanied him.</span>
</div>
</div>
