## Water from the Rock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> In the first month, the whole congregation of Israel entered the Wilderness of Zin and stayed in Kadesh. There Miriam died and was buried.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> Now there was no water for the congregation, so they gathered against Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> The people quarreled with Moses and said, “If only we had perished with our brothers before the LORD!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> Why have you brought the LORD’s assembly into this wilderness for us and our livestock to die here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> Why have you led us up out of Egypt to bring us to this wretched place? It is not a place of grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates—and there is no water to drink!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> Then Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. They fell facedown, and the glory of the LORD appeared to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> “Take the staff and assemble the congregation. You and your brother Aaron are to speak to the rock while they watch, and it will pour out its water. You will bring out water from the rock and provide drink for the congregation and their livestock.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> So Moses took the staff from the LORD’s presence, just as he had been commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> Then Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly in front of the rock, and Moses said to them, “Listen now, you rebels, must we bring you water out of this rock?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Then Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff, so that a great amount of water gushed out, and the congregation and their livestock were able to drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> But the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, “Because you did not trust Me to show My holiness in the sight of the Israelites, you will not bring this assembly into the land that I have given them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> These were the waters of Meribah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Meribah means quarreling; also in verse 24; see Exodus 17:7.">a</a></sup>where the Israelites quarreled with the LORD, and He showed His holiness among them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Edom Refuses Passage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> From Kadesh, Moses sent messengers to tell the king of Edom, “This is what your brother Israel says: You know all the hardship that has befallen us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> how our fathers went down to Egypt, where we lived many years. The Egyptians mistreated us and our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> and when we cried out to the LORD, He heard our voice, sent an angel, and brought us out of Egypt.<br />Now look, we are in Kadesh, a city on the edge of your territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> Please let us pass through your land. We will not go through any field or vineyard, or drink water from any well. We will stay on the King’s Highway; we will not turn to the right or to the left until we have passed through your territory.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But Edom answered, “You may not travel through our land, or we will come out and confront you with the sword.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> “We will stay on the main road,” the Israelites replied, “and if we or our herds drink your water, we will pay for it. There will be no problem; only let us pass through on foot.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> But Edom insisted, “You may not pass through.” And they came out to confront the Israelites with a large army and a strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> So Edom refused to allow Israel to pass through their territory, and Israel turned away from them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> After they had set out from Kadesh, the whole congregation of Israel came to Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> And at Mount Hor, near the border of the land of Edom, the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> “Aaron will be gathered to his people; he will not enter the land that I have given the Israelites, because both of you rebelled against My command at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take Aaron and his son Eleazar and bring them up Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> Remove Aaron’s priestly garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will be gathered to his people and will die there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So Moses did as the LORD had commanded, and they climbed Mount Hor in the sight of the whole congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> After Moses had removed Aaron’s garments and put them on his son Eleazar, Aaron died there on top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> When the whole congregation saw that Aaron had died, the entire house of Israel mourned for him thirty days.</span>
</div>
</div>
