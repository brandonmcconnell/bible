<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> Then Balaam said to Balak, “Build for me seven altars here, and prepare for me seven bulls and seven rams.”</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> “Stay here by your burnt offering while I am gone,” Balaam said to Balak. “Perhaps the LORD will meet with me. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> and God met with him. “I have set up seven altars,” Balaam said, “and on each altar I have offered a bull and a ram.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> Then the LORD put a message in Balaam’s mouth, saying, “Return to Balak and give him this message.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he returned to Balak, who was standing there beside his burnt offering, with all the princes of Moab.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And Balaam lifted up an oracle, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Balak brought me from Aram,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the king of Moab from the mountains of the east.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Come,’ he said, ‘put a curse on Jacob for me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">come and denounce Israel!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">How can I curse those whom God has not cursed?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">How can I denounce those whom the LORD has not denounced?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I see them from atop the rocky cliffs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I watch them from the hills.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, a people dwelling apart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not reckoning themselves among the nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can count the dust of Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or number even a fourth of Israel?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let me die the death of the righteous;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let my end be like theirs!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> Then Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I brought you here to curse my enemies, and behold, you have only blessed them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> But Balaam replied, “Should I not speak exactly what the LORD puts in my mouth?”</span>
</div>

## Balaam’s Second Oracle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Then Balak said to him, “Please come with me to another place where you can see them. You will only see the outskirts of their camp—not all of them. And from there, curse them for me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> So Balak took him to the field of Zophim, to the top of Pisgah, where he built seven altars and offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> Balaam said to Balak, “Stay here beside your burnt offering while I meet the LORD over there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> And the LORD met with Balaam and put a message in his mouth, saying, “Return to Balak and speak what I tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> So he returned to Balak, who was standing there by his burnt offering with the princes of Moab.<br />“What did the LORD say?” Balak asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> Then Balaam lifted up an oracle, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Arise, O Balak, and listen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">give ear to me, O son of Zippor.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have indeed received a command to bless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has blessed, and I cannot change it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He considers no disaster for Jacob;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He sees no trouble for Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The LORD their God is with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the shout of the King is among them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God brought them out of Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with strength like a wild ox.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there is no spell against Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no divination against Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will now be said of Jacob and Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘What great things God has done!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the people rise like a lioness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they rouse themselves like a lion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">not resting until they devour their prey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and drink the blood of the slain.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> Now Balak said to Balaam, “Then neither curse them at all nor bless them at all!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> But Balaam replied, “Did I not tell you that whatever the LORD says, I must do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> “Please come,” said Balak, “I will take you to another place. Perhaps it will please God that you curse them for me from there.”</span>
</div>
