## Forty-Eight Cities for the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> Again the LORD spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> “Command the Israelites to give, from the inheritance they will possess, cities for the Levites to live in and pasturelands around the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> The cities will be for them to live in, and the pasturelands will be for their herds, their flocks, and all their other livestock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The pasturelands around the cities you are to give the Levites will extend a thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1,000 cubits is approximately 1,500 feet or 457.2 meters.">a</a></sup>from the wall on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> You are also to measure two thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2,000 cubits is approximately 3,000 feet or 914.4 meters.">b</a></sup>outside the city on the east, two thousand on the south, two thousand on the west, and two thousand on the north, with the city in the center. These areas will serve as larger pasturelands for the cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> Six of the cities you give the Levites are to be appointed as cities of refuge, to which a manslayer may flee. In addition to these, give the Levites forty-two other cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> The total number of cities you give the Levites will be forty-eight, with their corresponding pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> The cities that you apportion from the territory of the Israelites should be given to the Levites in proportion to the inheritance of each tribe: more from a larger tribe and less from a smaller one.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Six Cities of Refuge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> designate cities to serve as your cities of refuge, so that a person who kills someone unintentionally may flee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> You are to have these cities as a refuge from the avenger, so that the manslayer will not die until he stands trial before the assembly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The cities you select will be your six cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> Select three cities across the Jordan and three in the land of Canaan as cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> These six cities will serve as a refuge for the Israelites and for the foreigner or stranger among them, so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally may flee there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> If, however, anyone strikes a person with an iron object and kills him, he is a murderer; the murderer must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> Or if anyone has in his hand a stone of deadly size, and he strikes and kills another, he is a murderer; the murderer must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If anyone has in his hand a deadly object of wood, and he strikes and kills another, he is a murderer; the murderer must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The avenger of blood is to put the murderer to death; when he finds him, he is to kill him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Likewise, if anyone maliciously pushes another or intentionally throws an object at him and kills him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or if in hostility he strikes him with his hand and he dies, the one who struck him must surely be put to death; he is a murderer. When the avenger of blood finds the murderer, he is to kill him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> But if anyone pushes a person suddenly, without hostility, or throws an object at him unintentionally,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or without looking drops a heavy stone that kills him, but he was not an enemy and did not intend to harm him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> then the congregation must judge between the slayer and the avenger of blood according to these ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> The assembly is to protect the manslayer from the hand of the avenger of blood. Then the assembly will return him to the city of refuge to which he fled, and he must live there until the death of the high priest, who was anointed with the holy oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if the manslayer ever goes outside the limits of the city of refuge to which he fled</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and the avenger of blood finds him outside of his city of refuge and kills him, then the avenger will not be guilty of bloodshed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> because the manslayer must remain in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest. Only after the death of the high priest may he return to the land he owns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> This will be a statutory ordinance for you for the generations to come, wherever you live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> If anyone kills a person, the murderer is to be put to death on the testimony of the witnesses. But no one is to be put to death based on the testimony of a lone witness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> You are not to accept a ransom for the life of a murderer who deserves to die; he must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> Nor should you accept a ransom for the person who flees to a city of refuge and allow him to return and live on his own land before the death of the high priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> Do not pollute the land where you live, for bloodshed pollutes the land, and no atonement can be made for the land on which the blood is shed, except by the blood of the one who shed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> Do not defile the land where you live and where I dwell. For I, the LORD, dwell among the Israelites.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
