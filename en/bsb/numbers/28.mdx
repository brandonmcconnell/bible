## The Daily Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> “Command the Israelites and say to them: See that you present to Me at its appointed time the food for My food offerings, as a pleasing aroma to Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> And tell them that this is the food offering you are to present to the LORD as a regular burnt offering each day: two unblemished year-old male lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew between the two evenings; also in verse 8">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> along with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A tenth of an ephah is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour); also in verses 13, 21, and 29.">b</a></sup>as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or a quarter hin of pure olive oil; Hebrew a quarter hin of pressed oil; that is, approximately 0.97 quarts or 0.92 liters; similarly in verses 7 and 14">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> This is a regular burnt offering established at Mount Sinai as a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> The drink offering accompanying each lamb shall be a quarter hin. Pour out the offering of fermented drink to the LORD in the sanctuary area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And offer the second lamb at twilight, with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning. It is a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sabbath Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> On the Sabbath day, present two unblemished year-old male lambs, accompanied by a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Two-tenths of an ephah is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour); also in verses 12, 20, and 28.">d</a></sup>mixed with oil, as well as a drink offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Monthly Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> At the beginning of every month, you are to present to the LORD a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> along with three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Three-tenths of an ephah is approximately 6 dry quarts or 6.6 liters (probably about 7.6 pounds or 3.5 kilograms of flour); also in verses 20 and 28.">e</a></sup>mixed with oil as a grain offering with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering with each lamb. This is a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Half a hin is approximately 1.9 quarts or 1.8 liters of wine.">f</a></sup>with each bull, a third of a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A third of a hin is approximately 1.3 quarts or 1.2 liters of wine.">g</a></sup>with the ram, and a quarter hin with each lamb. This is the monthly burnt offering to be made at each new moon throughout the year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> In addition to the regular burnt offering with its drink offering, one male goat is to be presented to the LORD as a sin offering.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> The fourteenth day of the first month is the LORD’s Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> On the fifteenth day of this month, there shall be a feast; for seven days unleavened bread is to be eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> On the first day there is to be a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> Present to the LORD a food offering, a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The grain offering shall consist of fine flour mixed with oil; offer three-tenths of an ephah with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> You are to present these in addition to the regular morning burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Offer the same food each day for seven days as a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. It is to be offered with its drink offering and the regular burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> On the seventh day you shall hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feast of Weeks

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> On the day of firstfruits, when you present an offering of new grain to the LORD during the Feast of Weeks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).">h</a></sup>you are to hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> Present a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old as a pleasing aroma to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> together with their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil—three-tenths of an ephah with each bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> Include one male goat to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> Offer them with their drink offerings in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering. The animals must be unblemished.</span>
</div>
