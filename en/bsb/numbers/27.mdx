## The Daughters of Zelophehad

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:33; Galatians 3:28; 1 Chronicles 7:15"> Now the daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, belonged to the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph. These were the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. They approached</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 15:33–34; Deuteronomy 17:8–10; Exodus 18:19–26"> the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders, and the whole congregation, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Ezekiel 18:4; Numbers 14:35"> “Our father died in the wilderness, but he was not among the followers of Korah who gathered together against the LORD. Instead, he died because of his own sin, and he had no sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:4; Psalms 109:13; Exodus 32:11"> Why should the name of our father disappear from his clan because he had no sons? Give us property among our father’s brothers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12–13; Job 23:4; Proverbs 3:5–6"> So Moses brought their case before the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Psalms 68:5–6"> and the LORD answered him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Jeremiah 49:11; Joshua 17:4"> “The daughters of Zelophehad speak correctly. You certainly must give them property as an inheritance among their father’s brothers and transfer their father’s inheritance to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Furthermore, you shall say to the Israelites, ‘If a man dies and leaves no son, you are to transfer his inheritance to his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If he has no daughter, give his inheritance to his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If he has no brothers, give his inheritance to his father’s brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:29; Ruth 4:3–6; Jeremiah 32:8"> And if his father has no brothers, give his inheritance to the next of kin from his clan, that he may take possession of it. This is to be a statutory ordinance for the Israelites, as the LORD has commanded Moses.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses Requests a Successor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:47–48; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Go up this mountain of the Abarim range<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX Go up Mount Nebo beyond the Jordan">a</a></sup>and see the land that I have given the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Numbers 31:2; Numbers 20:24–28"> After you have seen it, you too will be gathered to your people, as your brother Aaron was;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:8–13; Exodus 17:7"> for when the congregation contended in the Wilderness of Zin, both of you rebelled against My command to show My holiness in their sight regarding the waters.” Those were the waters of Meribah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meribah means quarreling; see Exodus 17:7.">b</a></sup>in Kadesh, in the Wilderness of Zin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So Moses appealed to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Jeremiah 3:15; Hebrews 12:9"> “May the LORD, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:5; Mark 6:34; 1 Kings 22:17"> who will go out and come in before them, and who will lead them out and bring them in, so that the congregation of the LORD will not be like sheep without a shepherd.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joshua to Succeed Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Numbers 27:23; Genesis 41:38"> And the LORD replied to Moses, “Take Joshua son of Nun, a man with the Spirit in him, and lay your hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:23"> Have him stand before Eleazar the priest and the whole congregation, and commission him in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 1:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:9; 2 Kings 2:9–10"> Confer on him some of your authority, so that the whole congregation of Israel will obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 9:14; 1 Samuel 28:6; Exodus 28:30"> He shall stand before Eleazar the priest, who will seek counsel for him before the LORD by the judgment of the Urim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally the Lights">c</a></sup>At his command, he and all the Israelites with him—the entire congregation—will go out and come in.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moses did as the LORD had commanded him. He took Joshua, had him stand before Eleazar the priest and the whole congregation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 27:19; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> and laid his hands on him and commissioned him, as the LORD had instructed through Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>
