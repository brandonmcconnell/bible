## Balaam’s Third Oracle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 23:23; Numbers 23:28"> And when Balaam saw that it pleased the LORD to bless Israel, he did not seek omens as on previous occasions, but he turned his face toward the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:20; 2 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Samuel 19:23"> When Balaam looked up and saw Israel encamped tribe by tribe, the Spirit of God came upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> and he lifted up an oracle, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“This is the prophecy of Balaam son of Beor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the prophecy of a man whose eyes are open,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">They spread out like palm groves,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or like valleys">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like gardens beside a stream,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like aloes the LORD has planted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like cedars beside the waters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:12; 1 Chronicles 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Water will flow from his buckets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his seed will have abundant water.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His king will be greater than Agag,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his kingdom will be exalted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">God brought him out of Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with strength like a wild ox,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to devour hostile nations and crush their bones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to pierce them with arrows.</span></span>

</div>

## Balak Dismisses Balaam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> Then Balak’s anger burned against Balaam, and he struck his hands together and said to Balaam, “I summoned you to curse my enemies, but behold, you have persisted in blessing them these three times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> Therefore, flee at once to your home! I said I would reward you richly, but instead the LORD has denied your reward.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> Balaam answered Balak, “Did I not already tell the messengers you sent me</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> that even if Balak were to give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not do anything of my own accord, good or bad, to go beyond the command of the LORD? I must speak whatever the LORD says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> Now I am going back to my people, but come, let me warn you what this people will do to your people in the days to come.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">the prophecy of one who hears the words of God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who has knowledge from the Most High,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who sees a vision from the Almighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who bows down with eyes wide open:</span></span>
