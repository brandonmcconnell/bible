## Israel’s Rebellion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:45; Numbers 11:1–4"> Then the whole congregation lifted up their voices and cried out, and that night the people wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 15:24; Exodus 17:3; Numbers 16:41"> All the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and the whole congregation said to them, “If only we had died in the land of Egypt, or if only we had died in this wilderness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:31–32; Jeremiah 9:3; Psalms 78:40"> Why is the LORD bringing us into this land to fall by the sword? Our wives and children will become plunder. Would it not be better for us to go back to Egypt?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:39; Nehemiah 9:16–17; Deuteronomy 17:16"> So they said to one another, “Let us appoint a leader and return to Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:22; Numbers 16:45"> Then Moses and Aaron fell facedown before the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:38"> Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had spied out the land, tore their clothes</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 1:25; Deuteronomy 8:7–9"> and said to the whole congregation of Israel, “The land we passed through and explored is an exceedingly good land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:20; Isaiah 62:4; Numbers 13:27"> If the LORD delights in us, He will bring us into this land, a land flowing with milk and honey, and He will give it to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:18; Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not be afraid of the people of the land, for they will be like bread for us. Their protection has been removed, and the LORD is with us. Do not be afraid of them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 17:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; Leviticus 9:23"> But the whole congregation threatened to stone Joshua and Caleb.<br />Then the glory of the LORD appeared to all the Israelites at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:32; Psalms 106:24; Psalms 78:22"> And the LORD said to Moses, “How long will this people treat Me with contempt? How long will they refuse to believe in Me, despite all the signs I have performed among them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:10; Numbers 25:9; 2 Samuel 24:12–15"> I will strike them with a plague and destroy them—and I will make you into a nation greater and mightier than they are.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses Intercedes for Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Ezekiel 20:9; Deuteronomy 32:27"> But Moses said to the LORD, “The Egyptians will hear of it, for by Your strength You brought this people from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:14; Exodus 33:11; Deuteronomy 5:4"> And they will tell it to the inhabitants of this land. They have already heard that You, O LORD, are in the midst of this people, that You, O LORD, have been seen face to face, that Your cloud stands over them, and that You go before them in a pillar of cloud by day and a pillar of fire by night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:16"> If You kill this people as one man, the nations who have heard of Your fame will say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9; Joshua 7:7"> ‘Because the LORD was unable to bring this people into the land He swore to give them, He has slaughtered them in the wilderness.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Matthew 9:8; Micah 3:8"> So now I pray, may the power of my Lord be magnified, just as You have declared:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Psalms 145:8; Exodus 20:5"> ‘The LORD is slow to anger and abounding in loving devotion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">a</a></sup>forgiving iniquity and transgression. Yet He will by no means leave the guilty unpunished; He will visit the iniquity of the fathers upon their children to the third and fourth generation.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 78:38; Psalms 106:45"> Pardon, I pray, the iniquity of this people, in keeping with the greatness of Your loving devotion, just as You have forgiven them ever since they left Egypt.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Forgiveness and Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; James 5:16; Psalms 106:23"> “I have pardoned them as you requested,” the LORD replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 72:19; Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3"> “Yet as surely as I live and as surely as the whole earth is filled with the glory of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:3; Genesis 31:7; Exodus 32:1"> not one of the men who have seen My glory and the signs I performed in Egypt and in the wilderness—yet have tested Me and disobeyed Me these ten times—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:11; Numbers 26:64; Ezekiel 20:15"> not one will ever see the land that I swore to give their fathers. None of those who have treated Me with contempt will see it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 14:6–9; Joshua 14:6–14; Ephesians 6:6"> But because My servant Caleb has a different spirit and has followed Me wholeheartedly, I will bring him into the land he has entered, and his descendants will inherit it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 13:29; Psalms 81:11–13"> Now since the Amalekites and Canaanites are living in the valleys, turn back tomorrow and head for the wilderness along the route to the Red Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the Sea of Reeds">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 16:28"> “How long will this wicked congregation grumble against Me? I have heard the complaints that the Israelites are making against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Hebrews 3:17; Numbers 14:2"> So tell them: As surely as I live, declares the LORD, I will do to you exactly as I heard you say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:45; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:17"> Your bodies will fall in this wilderness—all who were numbered in the census, everyone twenty years of age or older—because you have grumbled against Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 14:38; Deuteronomy 1:36–38"> Surely none of you will enter the land in which I swore to settle you, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:39; Psalms 106:24; Numbers 26:64"> But I will bring your children, whom you said would become plunder, into the land you have rejected—and they will enjoy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:29; Hebrews 3:17"> As for you, however, your bodies will fall in this wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Numbers 32:13; Deuteronomy 2:14"> Your children will be shepherds in the wilderness for forty years, and they will suffer for your unfaithfulness until the last of your bodies lies in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 13:25; Jeremiah 18:9–10"> In keeping with the forty days you spied out the land, you shall bear your guilt forty years—a year for each day—and you will experience My alienation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:19"> I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will surely do these things to this entire wicked congregation, which has conspired against Me. They will meet their end in the wilderness, and there they will die.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41; Matthew 7:21–23; Luke 13:25"> Early the next morning they got up and went up toward the ridge of the hill country. “We have indeed sinned,” they said, “but we will go to the place the LORD has promised.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; Job 4:9; Jeremiah 2:37"> But Moses said, “Why are you transgressing the commandment of the LORD? This will not succeed!</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 13:29; Numbers 14:25; 2 Chronicles 15:2"> For there the Amalekites and Canaanites will face you, and you will fall by the sword. Because you have turned away from the LORD, He will not be with you.”</span>
