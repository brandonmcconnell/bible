## The Order of the Camps

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; Numbers 10:22; Numbers 1:50"> “The Israelites are to camp around the Tent of Meeting at a distance from it, each man under his standard, with the banners of his family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10; Ruth 4:20; Luke 3:32–33"> On the east side, toward the sunrise, the divisions of Judah are to camp under their standard:<br />The leader of the Judahites is Nahshon son of Amminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:22; Numbers 1:27"> and his division numbers 74,600.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:8; Numbers 7:18; Numbers 7:23"> The tribe of Issachar will camp next to it. The leader of the Issacharites is Nethanel son of Zuar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 1:29"> and his division numbers 54,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:9; Numbers 7:24; Numbers 10:16"> Next will be the tribe of Zebulun. The leader of the Zebulunites is Eliab son of Helon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:31"> and his division numbers 57,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 10:14"> The total number of men in the divisions of the camp of Judah is 186,400; they shall set out first.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:5; Genesis 49:3–4; Numbers 7:35"> On the south side, the divisions of Reuben are to camp under their standard:<br />The leader of the Reubenites is Elizur son of Shedeur,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 1:21"> and his division numbers 46,500.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 10:19; Numbers 7:41"> The tribe of Simeon will camp next to it. The leader of the Simeonites is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 1:23"> and his division numbers 59,300.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 7:47"> Next will be the tribe of Gad. The leader of the Gadites is Eliasaph son of Deuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Many MT manuscripts, SP, and Vulgate (see also Numbers 1:14); most MT manuscripts Reuel">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 1:25"> and his division numbers 45,650.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:24"> The total number of men in the divisions of the camp of Reuben is 151,450; they shall set out second.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 10:21; Numbers 10:17; Numbers 3:38"> In the middle of the camps, the Tent of Meeting is to travel with the camp of the Levites. They are to set out in the order they encamped, each in his own place under his standard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 10:22; Genesis 48:14–20"> On the west side, the divisions of Ephraim are to camp under their standard:<br />The leader of the Ephraimites is Elishama son of Ammihud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:33; Numbers 26:37"> and his division numbers 40,500.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54; Numbers 10:23"> The tribe of Manasseh will be next to it. The leader of the Manassites is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Numbers 1:35"> and his division numbers 32,200.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24"> Next will be the tribe of Benjamin. The leader of the Benjamites is Abidan son of Gideoni,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:37; Numbers 26:41"> and his division numbers 35,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:22; Numbers 2:31; Psalms 80:2"> The total number of men in the divisions of the camp of Ephraim is 108,100; they shall set out third.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 1:12; Numbers 7:66; Numbers 10:25"> On the north side, the divisions of Dan are to camp under their standard:<br />The leader of the Danites is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 1:39"> and his division numbers 62,700.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:77; Numbers 7:72"> The tribe of Asher will camp next to it. The leader of the Asherites is Pagiel son of Ocran,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 1:41; Numbers 26:47"> and his division numbers 41,500.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 26:48–50"> Next will be the tribe of Naphtali. The leader of the Naphtalites is Ahira son of Enan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 1:42–43; Numbers 26:50"> and his division numbers 53,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 10:25; Numbers 2:16; Numbers 2:9"> The total number of men in the camp of Dan is 157,600; they shall set out last, under their standards.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 2:9"> These are the Israelites, numbered according to their families. The total of those counted in the camps, by their divisions, was 603,550.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:57–62; Numbers 1:47–49"> But the Levites were not counted among the other Israelites, as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 119:6; Numbers 24:2; Numbers 24:5–6"> So the Israelites did everything the LORD commanded Moses; they camped under their standards in this way and set out in the same way, each man with his clan and his family.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
