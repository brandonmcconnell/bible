## The Glory of the LORD Returns to the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:1; Ezekiel 42:15; Ezekiel 46:1"> Then the man brought me back to the gate that faces east,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 18:1; Ezekiel 11:23; Isaiah 6:3"> and I saw the glory of the God of Israel coming from the east. His voice was like the roar of many waters, and the earth shone with His glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:5; Ezekiel 9:1; Jeremiah 1:10"> The vision I saw was like the vision I had seen when He came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts and Vulgate; most Hebrew manuscripts when I came">a</a></sup>to destroy the city and like the visions I had seen by the River Kebar. I fell facedown,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:18–19; Ezekiel 44:2; Ezekiel 43:2"> and the glory of the LORD entered the temple through the gate facing east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:2–4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Ezekiel 8:3"> Then the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court, and the glory of the LORD filled the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Isaiah 66:6; Revelation 16:1"> While the man was standing beside me, I heard someone speaking to me from inside the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:9; Psalms 47:8; Leviticus 26:30"> and He said to me, “Son of man, this is the place of My throne and the place for the soles of My feet, where I will dwell among the Israelites forever. The house of Israel will never again defile My holy name—neither they nor their kings—by their prostitution and by the funeral offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the monuments; also in verse 9">b</a></sup>for their kings at their deaths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or for their kings on their high places">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:3–16; 2 Chronicles 33:7"> When they placed their threshold next to My threshold and their doorposts beside My doorposts, with only a wall between Me and them, they defiled My holy name by the abominations they committed. Therefore I have consumed them in My anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Ezekiel 37:26–28; Ezekiel 18:30–31"> Now let them remove far from Me their prostitution and the funeral offerings for their kings, and I will dwell among them forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 16:61; Ezekiel 16:63"> As for you, son of man, describe the temple to the people of Israel, so that they may be ashamed of their iniquities. Let them measure the plan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:20; Matthew 28:20"> and if they are ashamed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX and Vulgate and they will be ashamed">d</a></sup>of all they have done, then make known to them the design of the temple—its arrangement and its exits and entrances—its whole design along with all its statutes, forms, and laws. Write it down in their sight, so that they may keep its complete design and all its statutes and may carry them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Revelation 21:27; Psalms 93:5"> This is the law of the temple: All its surrounding territory on top of the mountain will be most holy. Yes, this is the law of the temple.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Altar of Sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 41:8"> These are the measurements of the altar in long cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The long cubit, about 21 inches or 53.3 centimeters, is the basic unit for linear measurement throughout Ezekiel 40–48.">e</a></sup>(a cubit and a handbreadth): Its gutter shall be a cubit deep and a cubit wide, with a rim of one span<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="One span is approximately 9 inches or 22.9 centimeters.">f</a></sup>around its edge.<br />And this is the height of the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The space from the gutter on the ground to the lower ledge shall be two cubits, and the ledge one cubit wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The space was to be approximately 3.5 feet (1.1 meters), and the ledge was to be approximately 1.75 feet wide (53.3 centimeters wide).">g</a></sup>The space from the smaller ledge to the larger ledge shall be four cubits, and the ledge one cubit wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The space was to be approximately 7 feet (2.1 meters), and the ledge was to be approximately 1.75 feet wide (53.3 centimeters wide).">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Psalms 118:27; Leviticus 9:9"> The altar hearth shall be four cubits high, and four horns shall project upward from the hearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1; 2 Chronicles 4:1; Exodus 38:1–2"> The altar hearth shall be square at its four corners, twelve cubits long and twelve cubits wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="12 (long) cubits is approximately 21 feet or 6.4 meters.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 20:26; Ezekiel 40:6; Exodus 25:25"> The ledge shall also be square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="14 (long) cubits is approximately 24.5 feet or 7.5 meters.">j</a></sup>with a rim of half a cubit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A half cubit is approximately 10.5 inches or 26.7 centimeters.">k</a></sup>and a gutter of a cubit all around it. The steps of the altar shall face east.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; Hebrews 10:4–12; Ezekiel 2:1"> Then He said to me: “Son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘These are the statutes for the altar on the day it is constructed, so that burnt offerings may be sacrificed on it and blood may be splattered on it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:15; Numbers 16:40"> You are to give a young bull from the herd as a sin offering to the Levitical priests who are of the family of Zadok, who approach Me to minister before Me, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 16:19; Leviticus 8:15; Exodus 29:12"> You are to take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge, and all around the rim; thus you will cleanse the altar and make atonement for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 8:17"> Then you are to take away the bull for the sin offering and burn it in the appointed part of the temple area outside the sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:25–26; Ezekiel 43:20; 1 Peter 1:19"> On the second day you are to present an unblemished male goat as a sin offering, and the altar is to be cleansed as it was with the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:1"> When you have finished the purification, you are to present a young, unblemished bull and an unblemished ram from the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50; Numbers 18:19"> You must present them before the LORD; the priests are to sprinkle salt on them and sacrifice them as a burnt offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:33; Exodus 29:35–37; Leviticus 8:35"> For seven days you are to provide a male goat daily for a sin offering; you are also to provide a young bull and a ram from the flock, both unblemished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 8:34; Exodus 32:29"> For seven days the priests are to make atonement for the altar and cleanse it; so they shall consecrate it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew fill its hand">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 9:1; Ezekiel 20:40–41; 1 Peter 2:5"> At the end of these days, from the eighth day on, the priests are to present your burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar. Then I will accept you, declares the Lord GOD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
