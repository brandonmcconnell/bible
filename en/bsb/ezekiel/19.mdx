## A Lament for the Princes of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:2; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Kings 24:6"> “As for you, take up a lament for the princes of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> and say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘What was your mother?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">A lioness among the lions!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She lay down among the young lions;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she reared her cubs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">She brought up one of her cubs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he became a young lion.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">After learning to tear his prey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he devoured men.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the nations heard of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he was trapped in their pit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With hooks they led him away</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the land of Egypt.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">When she saw that she had waited in vain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that her hope was lost,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">she took another of her cubs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and made him a young lion.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He prowled among the lions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and became a young lion.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">After learning to tear his prey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he devoured men.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He broke down their strongholds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or He knew their widows or He seized their widows">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and devastated their cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The land and everything in it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shuddered at the sound of his roaring.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the nations set out against him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the provinces on every side.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They spread their net over him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he was trapped in their pit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">With hooks they caged him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and brought him to the king of Babylon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They brought him into captivity</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that his roar was heard no longer</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the mountains of Israel.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your mother was like a vine in your vineyard,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts; most Hebrew manuscripts in your bloodline">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">planted by the water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was fruitful and full of branches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the abundant waters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">It had strong branches, fit for a ruler’s scepter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It towered high above the thick branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">conspicuous for its height</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and for its dense foliage.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it was uprooted in fury,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cast down to the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the east wind dried up its fruit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its strong branches were stripped off</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they withered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fire consumed them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now it is planted in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in a dry and thirsty land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fire has gone out from its main branch</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and devoured its fruit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on it no strong branch remains</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">fit for a ruler’s scepter.’</span><br />This is a lament and shall be used as a lament.”</span>
</div>
