## The Two Adulterous Sisters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:44–46; Jeremiah 3:7–10"> “Son of man, there were two women, daughters of the same mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 17:7; Ezekiel 16:22; Joshua 24:14"> and they played in Egypt, prostituting themselves from their youth. Their breasts were fondled there, and their virgin bosoms caressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Psalms 76:2; Jeremiah 2:2–3"> The older was named Oholah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Oholah means her own tent or she worships at a tent shrine, a metaphor for Samaria as an adulteress with Assyria.">a</a></sup>and her sister was named Oholibah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Oholibah means the tent is in her or she is a tent shrine, a metaphor for Jerusalem as an adulterous wife.">b</a></sup>They became Mine and gave birth to sons and daughters. As for their identities, Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; 2 Kings 17:3; 2 Kings 16:7"> Oholah prostituted herself while she was still Mine. She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians—warriors</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23"> clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all desirable young men, horsemen mounted on steeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Hosea 6:10; Ezekiel 22:3–4"> She offered sexual favors to all the elite of Assyria. She defiled herself with all the idols of those for whom she lusted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19; Ezekiel 23:3; Exodus 32:4"> She did not give up the prostitution she began in Egypt, when men slept with her in her youth, caressed her virgin bosom, and poured out their lust upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 11:5; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:23"> Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, the Assyrians for whom she lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> They exposed her nakedness, seized her sons and daughters, and put her to the sword. Thus she became a byword among women, and they executed judgment against her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Ezekiel 16:47–51; Ezekiel 23:4"> Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet in her lust and prostitution she was more depraved than her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:7–15; Ezekiel 23:23; 2 Chronicles 28:16–23"> She too lusted after the Assyrians—governors and commanders, warriors dressed in splendor, horsemen riding on steeds, all desirable young men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 12:1–2; 2 Kings 17:18–19; Ezekiel 23:31"> And I saw that she too had defiled herself; both of them had taken the same path.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Jeremiah 22:14; Jeremiah 50:2"> But Oholibah carried her prostitution even further. She saw the men portrayed on the wall, images of the Chaldeans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">c</a></sup>engraved in vermilion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:18; 1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 22:21"> wearing belts on their waists and flowing turbans on their heads; all of them looked like officers of the Babylonians in Chaldea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or Babylonia; also in verse 16">d</a></sup>the land of their birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:29; Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1"> At the sight of them, she lusted for them and sent messengers to them in Chaldea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:28; Ezekiel 23:22; Genesis 10:10"> Then the Babylonians came to her, to the bed of love, and in their lust they defiled her. But after she had been defiled by them, she turned away in disgust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 78:59; Psalms 106:40; Jeremiah 12:8"> When Oholibah openly prostituted herself and exposed her nakedness, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 20:7; Ezekiel 16:51"> Yet she multiplied her promiscuity, remembering the days of her youth, when she had prostituted herself in the land of Egypt</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Ezekiel 16:26; Jeremiah 5:8"> and lusted after their lovers, whose genitals were like those of donkeys and whose emission was like that of stallions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So you revisited the indecency of your youth, when the Egyptians caressed your bosom and pressed your young breasts.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Oholibah to Be Plagued

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Revelation 17:16; Jeremiah 6:22–23"> Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘I will incite your lovers against you, those from whom you turned away in disgust. And I will bring them against you from every side—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:21; 2 Kings 24:2; 2 Kings 20:14–17"> the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, the men of Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them—all desirable young men, governors and commanders, officers and men of renown, mounted on horses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:14; Ezekiel 26:10; Ezekiel 23:45"> They will come against you with a host of peoples,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="LXX They will all come against you from the north">e</a></sup>with weapons, chariots, and wagons. They will array themselves against you on every side with buckler and shield and helmet. I will delegate judgment to them, and they will punish you according to their own standards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 8:1–18; Ezekiel 15:6–7"> And I will set My jealous rage against you, and they will deal with you in fury. They will cut off your noses and ears, and your survivors will fall by the sword. They will seize your sons and daughters, and your remnant will be consumed by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Jeremiah 13:22; Ezekiel 23:29"> They will strip off your clothes and take your fine jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:41; Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 23:19"> So I will put an end to your indecency and prostitution, which began in the land of Egypt, and you will not lift your eyes to them or remember Egypt anymore.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:22; Ezekiel 23:17; Jeremiah 34:20"> For this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Surely I will deliver you into the hands of those you hate, from whom you turned away in disgust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 23:25–26; Ezekiel 23:45–47"> They will treat you with hatred, take all for which you have worked, and leave you naked and bare, so that the shame of your prostitution will be exposed. Your indecency and promiscuity</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Psalms 106:35–38; Jeremiah 2:18–20"> have brought these things upon you, because you have prostituted yourself with the nations and defiled yourself with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Jeremiah 7:14–15; Ezekiel 16:47–51"> Because you have followed the path of your sister, I will put her cup into your hand.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 60:3; Ezekiel 22:4–5"> This is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You will drink your sister’s cup,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a cup deep and wide.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will bring scorn and derision,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for it holds so much.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will be filled with drunkenness and grief,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with a cup of devastation and desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the cup of your sister Samaria.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will drink it and drain it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will dash it to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and tear your breasts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For I have spoken,’</span>declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 32:33"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because you have forgotten Me and have cast Me behind your back, you must bear the consequences of your indecency and prostitution.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment on Both Sisters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 16:2"> Then the LORD said to me: “Son of man, will you pass judgment against Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:36; Jeremiah 32:35; Jeremiah 7:31"> For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They have committed adultery with their idols. They have even sacrificed their children, whom they bore to Me, in the fire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally passed their children, whom they bore to Me, through the fire">f</a></sup>as food for their idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; 2 Kings 21:7; Ezekiel 7:20"> They have also done this to Me: On that very same day, they defiled My sanctuary and profaned My Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Kings 21:4; Ezekiel 44:7; Jeremiah 23:11"> On the very day they slaughtered their children for their idols, they entered My sanctuary to profane it. Yes, they did this inside My house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 16:13–16"> Furthermore, you sisters sent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew they sent">g</a></sup>messengers for men who came from afar; and behold, when they arrived, you bathed for them, painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Jeremiah 44:17; Isaiah 65:11"> You sat on a couch of luxury with a table spread before it, on which you had set My incense and My oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Revelation 12:3; Exodus 32:18–19"> accompanied by the sound of a carefree crowd. Drunkards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or Sabeans">h</a></sup>were brought in from the desert along with men from the rabble, who put bracelets on your wrists and beautiful crowns on your head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Jeremiah 13:23; Ezra 9:7"> Then I said of her who had grown old in adulteries: ‘Now let them use her as a prostitute, for that is all she is!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:9–13; Ezekiel 23:3"> And they slept with her as with a prostitute; they slept with Oholah and Oholibah, those lewd women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hosea 6:5; Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:36–39"> But righteous men will sentence them to the punishment of those who commit adultery and bloodshed, because they are adulteresses with blood on their hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 24:9"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Bring a mob against them and consign them to terror and plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40–41; Jeremiah 39:8; Ezekiel 24:21"> The mob will stone them and cut them down with their swords. They will kill their sons and daughters and burn down their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:6; Ezekiel 23:27; Ezekiel 16:41"> So I will put an end to indecency in the land, and all the women will be admonished not to imitate your behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 6:7"> They will repay you for your indecency, and you will bear the consequences of your sins of idolatry. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
