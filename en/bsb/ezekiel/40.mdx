## The Man with a Measuring Rod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 3:14"> In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the beginning of the year, on the tenth day of the month—in the fourteenth year after Jerusalem had been struck down—on that very day the hand of the LORD was upon me, and He took me there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> In visions of God He took me to the land of Israel and set me on a very high mountain, on whose southern slope was a structure that resembled a city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> So He took me there, and I saw a man whose appearance was like bronze. He was standing in the gateway with a linen cord and a measuring rod in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> “Son of man,” he said to me, “look with your eyes, hear with your ears, and pay attention to everything I am going to show you, for that is why you have been brought here. Report to the house of Israel everything you see.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The East Gate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And I saw a wall surrounding the temple area. Now the length of the measuring rod in the man’s hand was six long cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6 long cubits, the length of the reed used for measuring, is approximately 10.5 feet or 3.2 meters; similarly in verses 6, 7, and 12. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then he came to the gate facing east and climbed its steps. The inner threshold of the gate by the portico facing inward was one rod deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he measured the portico of the gateway inside;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally the portico of the gateway inside, one rod">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:19"> it was eight cubits deep,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8 (long) cubits is approximately 14 feet or 4.3 meters.">e</a></sup>and its jambs were two cubits thick.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2 (long) cubits is approximately 3.5 feet or 1.1 meters.">f</a></sup>And the portico of the gateway faced the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There were three gate chambers on each side of the east gate, each with the same measurements, and the gateposts on either side also had the same measurements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measured the width of the gateway entrance to be ten cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10 (long) cubits is approximately 17.5 feet or 5.3 meters.">g</a></sup>and its length was thirteen cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13 (long) cubits is approximately 22.75 feet or 6.9 meters.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In front of each gate chamber was a wall one cubit high, and the gate chambers were six cubits square.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then he measured the gateway from the roof of one gate chamber to the roof of the opposite one; the distance was twenty-five cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="25 (long) cubits is approximately 43.75 feet or 13.3 meters; also in verses 21, 25, 29, 30, 33, and 36.">i</a></sup>from doorway to doorway.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; Ezekiel 42:1"> Next he measured the gateposts to be sixty cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="60 (long) cubits is approximately 105 feet or 32 meters.">j</a></sup>The gateway extended around to the gatepost of the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the distance from the entrance of the gateway to the far end of its inner portico was fifty cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="50 (long) cubits is approximately 87.5 feet or 26.7 meters; also in verses 21, 25, 29, 33, and 36.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:5; 1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 41:26"> The gate chambers and their side pillars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or gateposts or jambs or projecting walls; here and throughout Ezekiel 40 and 41.">l</a></sup>had beveled windows all around the inside of the gateway. The porticos also had windows all around on the inside. Each side pillar was decorated with palm trees.</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Ezekiel 42:1; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> Then he brought me into the outer court, and there were chambers and a pavement laid out all around the court. Thirty chambers faced the pavement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> which flanked the gateways and corresponded to the length of the gates; this was the lower pavement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27; Ezekiel 46:1–2"> Then he measured the distance from the front of the lower gateway to the outside of the inner court; it was a hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="100 (long) cubits is approximately 175 feet or 53.3 meters; also in verses 23, 27, and 47.">m</a></sup>on the east side as well as on the north.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37; Ezekiel 40:6"> Its windows, portico, and palm trees had the same measurements as those of the gate facing east. Seven steps led up to it, with its portico opposite them.</span>

## Eight Tables for Sacrifices

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:6; Ezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> There was a chamber with a doorway by the portico<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew at the jambs">p</a></sup>in each of the inner gateways. There the burnt offering was to be washed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> Inside the portico of the gateway were two tables on each side, on which the burnt offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings were to be slaughtered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Outside, as one goes up to the entrance of the north gateway, there were two tables on one side and two more tables on the other side of the gate’s portico.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39–40"> So there were four tables inside the gateway and four outside—eight tables in all—on which the sacrifices were to be slaughtered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> There were also four tables of dressed stone for the burnt offering, each a cubit and a half long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The tables were approximately 2.6 feet in length and width, and 1.75 feet high (79.2 centimeters in length and width, and 53.3 centimeters high).">q</a></sup>On these were placed the utensils used to slaughter the burnt offerings and the other sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> The double-pronged hooks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or shelves">r</a></sup>each a handbreadth long,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="A handbreadth is approximately 2.9 inches or 7.4 centimeters long.">s</a></sup>were fastened all around the inside of the room, and the flesh of the offering was to be placed on the tables.</span>
</div>
