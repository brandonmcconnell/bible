## Waters from under the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Zechariah 14:8; Joel 3:18"> Then the man brought me back to the entrance of the temple, and I saw water flowing from under the threshold of the temple toward the east (for the temple faced east). The water was coming down from under the south side of the temple, south of the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:4; Ezekiel 44:2"> Next he brought me out through the north gate and led me around the outside to the outer gate facing east, and there I saw the water trickling out from the south side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Acts 10:45–46; Acts 11:16–18"> As the man went eastward with a measuring line in his hand, he measured off a thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1,000 (long) cubits is approximately 1,750 feet or 533.4 meters.">a</a></sup>and led me through ankle-deep water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:6; Romans 15:19; Acts 19:10–20"> Then he measured off a thousand cubits and led me through knee-deep water.<br />Again he measured a thousand cubits and led me through waist-deep water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Habakkuk 2:14; Matthew 13:31–32"> Once again he measured off a thousand cubits, but now it was a river that I could not cross, because the water had risen and was deep enough for swimming—a river that could not be crossed on foot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Ezekiel 40:4; Zechariah 4:2"> “Son of man, do you see this?” he asked. Then he led me back to the bank of the river.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 22:2; 2 Kings 2:13"> When I arrived, I saw a great number of trees along both banks of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; 2 Kings 2:19–22"> And he said to me, “This water flows out to the eastern region and goes down into the Arabah. When it empties into the Dead Sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">b</a></sup>the water there becomes fresh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew is healed; similarly in verses 9 and 11">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 6:63; John 4:14"> Wherever the river flows, there will be swarms of living creatures and a great number of fish, because it flows there and makes the waters fresh; so wherever the river flows, everything will flourish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Psalms 104:25; Numbers 34:6"> Fishermen will stand by the shore; from En-gedi to En-eglaim they will spread their nets to catch fish of many kinds, like the fish of the Great Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea; also in verses 15, 19, and 20">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Revelation 22:11; Jeremiah 17:6"> But the swamps and marshes will not become fresh; they will be left for salt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> Along both banks of the river, fruit trees of all kinds will grow. Their leaves will not wither, and their fruit will not fail. Each month they will bear fruit, because the water from the sanctuary flows to them. Their fruit will be used for food and their leaves for healing.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Borders of the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:4–6; Jeremiah 3:18; Numbers 34:2–12"> This is what the Lord GOD says: “These are the boundaries by which you are to divide the land as an inheritance among the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph shall receive two portions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Since Levi had no portion, Joseph’s sons Ephraim and Manasseh received land as two tribes.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–6; Genesis 12:7; Ezekiel 48:29"> You are to divide it equally among them. Because I swore with an uplifted hand to give it to your forefathers, this land will fall to you as an inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 34:8; Ezekiel 48:1"> This shall be the boundary of the land:<br />On the north side it will extend from the Great Sea by way of Hethlon through Lebo-hamath to Zedad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 13:21; 2 Samuel 8:8"> Berothah, and Sibraim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX; MT 15...through Lebo to Zedad, 16 Hamath, Berothah, and Sibraim">f</a></sup>(which is on the border between Damascus and Hamath), as far as Hazer-hatticon, which is on the border of Hauran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 34:9"> So the border will run from the Sea to Hazar-enan, along the northern border of Damascus, with the territory of Hamath to the north. This will be the northern boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:47"> On the east side the border will run between Hauran and Damascus, along the Jordan between Gilead and the land of Israel, to the Eastern Sea and as far as Tamar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Syriac; that is, of Israel, to the Dead Sea and as far as Tamar; Hebrew of Israel. And along the Eastern Sea you are to measure.">g</a></sup>This will be the eastern boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:28; Deuteronomy 32:51; Numbers 34:5"> On the south side it will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, and along the Brook of Egypt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew does not include of Egypt.">h</a></sup>to the Great Sea. This will be the southern boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Ezekiel 48:1"> And on the west side, the Great Sea will be the boundary up to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. This will be the western boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You are to divide this land among yourselves according to the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Acts 11:18"> You shall allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the foreigners who dwell among you and who have children. You are to treat them as native-born Israelites; along with you, they shall be allotted an inheritance among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In whatever tribe a foreigner dwells, you are to assign his inheritance there,” declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
