## A Prophecy against Ammon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:1–6; Amos 1:13–15; Ezekiel 6:2"> “Son of man, set your face against the Ammonites and prophesy against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 70:2–3; Proverbs 17:5"> Tell the Ammonites to hear the word of the Lord GOD, for this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because you exclaimed, “Aha!” when My sanctuary was profaned, when the land of Israel was laid waste, and when the house of Judah went into exile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:33; 1 Kings 4:30"> therefore I will indeed give you as a possession to the people of the East. They will set up their camps and pitch their tents among you. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 12:26; Isaiah 17:2"> I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels, and Ammon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew and the Ammonites">a</a></sup>a resting place for sheep. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8; Obadiah 1:12; Zephaniah 2:10"> For this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because you clapped your hands and stomped your feet and rejoiced over the land of Israel with a heart full of contempt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:4; Ezekiel 25:16; Ezekiel 6:14"> therefore I will indeed stretch out My hand against you and give you as plunder to the nations. I will cut you off from the peoples and exterminate you from the countries. I will destroy you, and you will know that I am the LORD.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Prophecy against Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Jeremiah 25:21; Jeremiah 48:1–47"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because Moab and Seir<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX does not include and Seir.">b</a></sup>said, “Look, the house of Judah is like all the other nations,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:23; Joshua 13:17; Joshua 13:19–20"> therefore I will indeed expose the flank of Moab beginning with its frontier cities—Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim—the glory of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:32; Psalms 83:3–6; Ezekiel 25:4"> I will give it along with the Ammonites as a possession to the people of the East, so that the Ammonites will no longer be remembered among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Ezekiel 30:19; Psalms 149:7"> So I will execute judgments on Moab, and they will know that I am the LORD.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Prophecy against Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Ezekiel 25:8; Psalms 137:7"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because Edom acted vengefully against the house of Judah, and in so doing incurred grievous guilt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:8; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 25:23"> therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will stretch out My hand against Edom and cut off from it both man and beast. I will make it a wasteland, and from Teman to Dedan they will fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:14; Genesis 27:29; Hebrews 10:30–31"> I will take My vengeance on Edom by the hand of My people Israel, and they will deal with Edom according to My anger and wrath. Then they will know My vengeance, declares the Lord GOD.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Prophecy against the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:6; Isaiah 14:29–31; Ezekiel 25:12"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because the Philistines acted in vengeance, taking vengeance with malice of soul to destroy Judah with ancient hostility,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; Zephaniah 2:4–15; Ezekiel 25:13"> therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I will stretch out My hand against the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethites and destroy the remnant along the coast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:11; Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 5:15"> I will execute great vengeance against them with furious reproof. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I lay My vengeance upon them.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
