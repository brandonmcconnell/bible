<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> Javan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Greece">d</a></sup>Tubal, and Meshech were your merchants. They exchanged slaves and bronze utensils for your merchandise.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Most Hebrew manuscripts; some Hebrew manuscripts and Syriac Edom">f</a></sup>was your customer because of your many products; they exchanged turquoise, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral, and rubies for your wares.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> Judah and the land of Israel traded with you; they exchanged wheat from Minnith, cakes and honey, oil and balm for your merchandise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> Because of your many products and your great wealth of goods, Damascus traded with you wine from Helbon, wool from Zahar,</span>

</div>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ships of Tarshish</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or A fleet of trading ships">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">carried your merchandise.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you were filled with heavy cargo</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the heart of the sea.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">As they wail and mourn over you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will take up a lament for you:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Who was ever like Tyre,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">silenced in the middle of the sea?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now you are shattered by the seas</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the depths of the waters;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your merchandise and the people among you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">have gone down with you.</span></span>
