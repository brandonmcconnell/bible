## The Parable of Two Eagles and a Vine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man, pose a riddle; speak a parable to the house of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> and tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘A great eagle with great wings and long pinions,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Pinions are the outer parts of a bird’s wings, including the flight feathers.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">full of feathers of many colors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">came to Lebanon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and took away the top of the cedar.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He plucked off its topmost shoot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">carried it to the land of merchants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and planted it in a city of traders.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He took some of the seed of the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and planted it in fertile soil;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew in a field of seed">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he placed it by abundant waters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and set it out like a willow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">It sprouted and became a spreading vine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">low in height, with branches turned toward him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet its roots remained where it stood.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So it became a vine and yielded branches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sent out shoots.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But there was another great eagle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with great wings and many feathers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And behold, this vine bent its roots toward him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It stretched out its branches to him from its planting bed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that he might water it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">It had been planted in good soil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by abundant waters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in order to yield branches and bear fruit</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and become a splendid vine.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> So you are to tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Will it flourish?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will it not be uprooted and stripped of its fruit</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that it shrivels?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All its foliage will wither!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will not take a strong arm or many people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to pull it up by its roots.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if it is transplanted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will it flourish?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will it not completely wither when the east wind strikes?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will wither on the bed where it sprouted.’”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable Explained

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Now say to this rebellious house: ‘Do you not know what these things mean?’<br />Tell them, ‘Behold, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem, carried off its king and officials, and brought them back with him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> He took a member of the royal family<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew the royal seed">c</a></sup>and made a covenant with him, putting him under oath. Then he carried away the leading men of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> so that the kingdom would be brought low, unable to lift itself up, surviving only by keeping his covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But this king rebelled against Babylon by sending his envoys to Egypt to ask for horses and a large army. Will he flourish? Will the one who does such things escape? Can he break a covenant and yet escape?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> ‘As surely as I live,’ declares the Lord GOD, ‘he will die in Babylon, in the land of the king who enthroned him, whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> Pharaoh with his mighty army and vast horde will not help him in battle, when ramps are built and siege walls constructed to destroy many lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> He despised the oath by breaking the covenant. Seeing that he gave his hand in pledge yet did all these things, he will not escape!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘As surely as I live, I will bring down upon his head My oath that he despised and My covenant that he broke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> I will spread My net over him and catch him in My snare. I will bring him to Babylon and execute judgment upon him there for the treason he committed against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> All his choice troops<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Many Hebrew manuscripts; MT All his fleeing troops">d</a></sup>will fall by the sword, and those who survive will be scattered to every wind. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> This is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will take a shoot from the lofty top of the cedar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will set it out.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pluck a tender sprig from its topmost shoots,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will plant it on a high and lofty mountain.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will plant it on the mountain heights of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that it will bear branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will yield fruit</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and become a majestic cedar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Birds of every kind will nest under it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">taking shelter in the shade of its branches.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then all the trees of the field will know</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I am the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I bring the tall tree down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and make the low tree tall.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I dry up the green tree</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and make the withered tree flourish.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I, the LORD, have spoken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I have done it.’”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
