## A Sign of Jerusalem’s Siege

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Ezekiel 12:3–16"> “Now you, son of man, take a brick, place it before you, and draw on it the city of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> Then lay siege against it: Construct a siege wall, build a ramp to it, set up camps against it, and place battering rams around it on all sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:6; Isaiah 8:18; Isaiah 20:3"> Then take an iron plate and set it up as an iron wall between yourself and the city. Turn your face toward it so that it is under siege, and besiege it. This will be a sign to the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> Then lie down on your left side and place the iniquity of the house of Israel upon yourself. You are to bear their iniquity for the number of days you lie on your side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Isaiah 53:6"> For I have assigned to you 390 days, according to the number of years of their iniquity. So you shall bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> When you have completed these days, lie down again, but on your right side, and bear the iniquity of the house of Judah. I have assigned to you 40 days, a day for each year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 6:2"> You must turn your face toward the siege of Jerusalem with your arm bared, and prophesy against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:25"> Now behold, I will tie you up with ropes so you cannot turn from side to side until you have finished the days of your siege.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Defiled Bread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Isaiah 28:25; Ezekiel 4:16"> But take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt; put them in a single container and make them into bread for yourself. This is what you are to eat during the 390 days you lie on your side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> You are to weigh out twenty shekels of food<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20 shekels is approximately 8 ounces or 228 grams of food.">a</a></sup>to eat each day, and you are to eat it at set times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; John 3:34; Ezekiel 4:16"> You are also to measure out a sixth of a hin of water<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A sixth of a hin is approximately 0.65 quarts or 0.61 liters of water.">b</a></sup>to drink, and you are to drink it at set times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> And you shall eat the food as you would a barley cake, after you bake it over dried human excrement in the sight of the people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Hosea 9:3–4"> Then the LORD said, “This is how the Israelites will eat their defiled bread among the nations to which I will banish them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Ezekiel 20:49; Ezekiel 9:8"> “Ah, Lord GOD,” I said, “I have never defiled myself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Ah, Lord GOD,” I said, “Behold, my soul has never been made unclean.">c</a></sup>From my youth until now I have not eaten anything found dead or mauled by wild beasts. No unclean meat has ever entered my mouth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “Look,” He replied, “I will let you use cow dung instead of human excrement, and you may bake your bread over that.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Isaiah 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> Then He told me, “Son of man, I am going to cut off the supply<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew staff">d</a></sup>of food in Jerusalem. They will anxiously eat bread rationed by weight, and in despair they will drink water by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Ezekiel 33:10"> So they will lack food and water; they will be appalled at the sight of one another wasting away in their iniquity.</span>
</div>
</div>
