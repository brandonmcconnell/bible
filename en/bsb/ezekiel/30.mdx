## A Lament for Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6; Ezekiel 21:12; Joel 1:11"> “Son of man, prophesy and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wail, ‘Alas</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for that day!’</span></span>

Anguish will come upon them on the day of Egypt’s doom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew the day of Egypt">e</a></sup>For it is indeed coming.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 46:25; Psalms 78:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will lay waste Pathros,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">set fire to Zoan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and execute judgment on Thebes.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew No; also in verses 15 and 16">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 16:1; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out My wrath on Pelusium,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew Sin; also in verse 16">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the stronghold of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cut off the crowds of Thebes.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 41:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">The young men of On and Pi-beseth</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, Heliopolis and Bubastis, as in LXX; Hebrew Aven and Pi-beseth">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will fall by the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those cities will go into captivity.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:3; Ezekiel 29:15; Isaiah 10:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The day will be darkened in Tahpanhes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I break the yoke of Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her proud strength comes to an end.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A cloud will cover her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her daughters will go into captivity.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Revelation 17:1; Ezekiel 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will execute judgment on Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will know that I am the LORD.”</span></span>
</div>

## Pharaoh’s Power Broken

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 29:1"> In the eleventh year, on the seventh day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:13; Jeremiah 46:11; Psalms 37:17"> “Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt. See, it has not been bound up for healing, or splinted for strength to hold the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 37:17; Jeremiah 37:7; 2 Kings 24:7"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt. I will break his arms, both the strong one and the one already broken, and will make the sword fall from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:26; Ezekiel 30:17–18; Ezekiel 29:12–13"> I will disperse the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:25; Zephaniah 2:12; Isaiah 45:5"> I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king and place My sword in his hand, but I will break the arms of Pharaoh, who will groan before him like a mortally wounded man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:21; Ezekiel 32:15; Ezekiel 29:16"> I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king, but Pharaoh’s arms will fall limp.<br />Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I place My sword in the hand of Babylon’s king, and he wields it against the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:42; Ezekiel 29:12; Ezekiel 30:23"> I will disperse the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”</span>
</div>
