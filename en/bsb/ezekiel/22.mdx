## The Sins of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 24:6; Ezekiel 24:9"> “As for you, son of man, will you judge her? Will you pass judgment on the city of bloodshed? Then confront her with all her abominations</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Romans 2:5; 2 Peter 2:3"> and tell her that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘O city who brings her own doom by shedding blood within her walls and making idols to defile herself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 44:13–14; Ezekiel 22:2"> you are guilty of the blood you have shed, and you are defiled by the idols you have made. You have brought your days to a close and have come to the end of your years. Therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations and a mockery to all the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> Those near and far will mock you, O infamous city, full of turmoil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Isaiah 1:23; Zechariah 3:3"> See how every prince of Israel within you has used his power to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> Father and mother are treated with contempt. Within your walls the foreign resident is exploited, the fatherless and the widow are oppressed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:38–39; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 20:13"> You have despised My holy things and profaned My Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Hosea 4:10"> Among you are slanderous men bent on bloodshed; within you are those who eat on the mountain shrines and commit acts of indecency.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Ezekiel 18:6"> In you they have uncovered the nakedness of their fathers; in you they violate women during their menstrual impurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; 2 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> One man commits an abomination with his neighbor’s wife; another wickedly defiles his daughter-in-law; and yet another violates his sister, his own father’s daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Ezekiel 23:35; Psalms 106:21"> In you they take bribes to shed blood. You engage in usury, take excess interest, and extort your neighbors. But Me you have forgotten, declares the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> Now look, I strike My hands together against your unjust gain and against the blood you have shed in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Ezekiel 17:24; Ezekiel 24:14"> Will your courage endure or your hands be strong in the day I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Ezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> I will disperse you among the nations and scatter you throughout the lands; I will purge your uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Psalms 83:18; Ezekiel 7:24"> And when you have defiled yourself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or And when I have allotted you your inheritance">a</a></sup>in the eyes of the nations, then you will know that I am the LORD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Refining Furnace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22; Jeremiah 6:28–30"> “Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to Me. All of them are copper, tin, iron, and lead inside the furnace; they are but the dross of silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Ezekiel 24:3–6; Micah 4:12"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because all of you have become dross, behold, I will gather you into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; Ezekiel 24:13"> Just as one gathers silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin into the furnace to melt with a fiery blast, so I will gather you in My anger and wrath, leave you there, and melt you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 50:3"> Yes, I will gather you together and blow on you with the fire of My wrath, and you will be melted within the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 20:33; Hosea 5:10"> As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted within the city. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out My wrath upon you.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel’s Wicked Leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–16; Jeremiah 2:30; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> “Son of man, say to her, ‘In the day of indignation, you are a land that has not been cleansed, upon which no rain has fallen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 2:34; Ezekiel 13:19"> The conspiracy of the princes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew prophets">b</a></sup>in her midst is like a roaring lion tearing its prey. They devour the people, seize the treasures and precious things, and multiply the widows within her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Ezekiel 22:8"> Her priests do violence to My law and profane My holy things. They make no distinction between the holy and the common, and they fail to distinguish between the clean and the unclean. They disregard My Sabbaths, so that I am profaned among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; Ezekiel 22:6"> Her officials within her are like wolves tearing their prey, shedding blood, and destroying lives for dishonest gain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremiah 23:25–32"> Her prophets whitewash these deeds by false visions and lying divinations, saying, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says,’ when the LORD has not spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> The people of the land have practiced extortion and committed robbery. They have oppressed the poor and needy and have exploited the foreign resident without justice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; Jeremiah 5:1; Psalms 106:23"> I searched for a man among them to repair the wall and stand in the gap before Me on behalf of the land, so that I should not destroy it. But I found no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:43; Ezekiel 7:8–9; Ezekiel 9:10"> So I have poured out My indignation upon them and consumed them with the fire of My fury. I have brought their ways down upon their own heads, declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
</div>
