<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> “Son of man, take up a lament for the king of Tyre and tell him that this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You were the seal of perfection,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> “Son of man, set your face against Sidon and prophesy against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> And you are to declare that this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, I am against you, O Sidon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will be glorified within you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will know that I am the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I execute judgments against her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and demonstrate My holiness through her.</span></span>

</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> And there they will dwell securely, build houses, and plant vineyards.
