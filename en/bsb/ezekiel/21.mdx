## God’s Sword of Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> “Son of man, set your face against Jerusalem and preach against the sanctuaries. Prophesy against the land of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> and tell her that this is what the LORD says: ‘I am against you, and I will draw My sword from its sheath and cut off from you both the righteous and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Because I will cut off both the righteous and the wicked, My sword will be unsheathed against everyone from south to north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> Then all flesh will know that I, the LORD, have taken My sword from its sheath, not to return it again.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> But you, son of man, groan! Groan before their eyes with a broken heart and bitter grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> And when they ask, ‘Why are you groaning?’ you are to say, ‘Because of the news that is coming. Every heart will melt, and every hand will go limp. Every spirit will faint, and every knee will turn to water.’ Yes, it is coming and it will surely happen, declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man, prophesy and tell them that this is what the Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘A sword, a sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sharpened and polished—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">it is sharpened for the slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">polished to flash like lightning!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Should we rejoice in the scepter of My son?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The sword despises every such stick.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sword is appointed to be polished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to be grasped in the hand.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is sharpened and polished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to be placed in the hand of the slayer.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cry out and wail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O son of man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the sword is wielded against My people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is against all the princes of Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are tossed to the sword with My people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">therefore strike your thigh.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or beat your breast">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely testing will come!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And what if even the scepter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which the sword despises,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">does not continue?’</span>declares the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘So then, son of man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">prophesy and strike your hands together.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the sword strike two times,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even three.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is a sword that slays,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a sword of great slaughter</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">closing in on every side!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">So that their hearts may melt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and many may stumble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have appointed at all their gates</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a sword for slaughter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yes, it is ready to flash like lightning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is drawn for slaughter.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Slash to the right;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">set your blade to the left—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">wherever your blade is directed.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I too will strike My hands together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will satisfy My wrath.’</span>I, the LORD, have spoken.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> “Now you, son of man, mark out two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to take, both starting from the same land. And make a signpost where the road branches off to each city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Mark out one road for the sword to come against Rabbah of the Ammonites, and another against Judah into fortified Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon stands at the fork in the road, at the junction of the two roads, to seek an omen: He shakes the arrows, he consults the idols, he examines the liver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> In his right hand appears the portent for Jerusalem, where he is to set up battering rams, to call for the slaughter, to lift a battle cry, to direct the battering rams against the gates, to build a ramp, and to erect a siege wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> It will seem like a false omen to the eyes of those who have sworn allegiance to him, but it will draw attention to their guilt and take them captive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Because you have drawn attention to your guilt, exposing your transgressions, so that your sins are revealed in all your deeds—because you have come to remembrance—you shall be taken in hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">And you, O profane and wicked prince of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the day has come for your final punishment.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> This is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Remove the turban,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take off the crown.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Things will not remain as they are:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Exalt the lowly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bring low the exalted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ruin, a ruin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will make it a ruin!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And it will not be restored</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until the arrival of Him to whom it belongs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to whom I have assigned the right of judgment.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or of Him to whom it rightfully belongs, to whom I have given it.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> Now prophesy, son of man, and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says concerning the Ammonites and their contempt:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘A sword! A sword</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is drawn for slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">polished to consume,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to flash like lightning—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">while they offer false visions for you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lying divinations about you—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to be placed on the necks</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the wicked who are slain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose day has come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the time of their final punishment.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Return the sword to its sheath!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the place where you were created,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the land of your origin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will judge you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out My anger upon you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will breathe the fire of My fury against you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will hand you over to brutal men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">skilled in destruction.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will be fuel for the fire.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your blood will stain your own land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will not be remembered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I, the LORD, have spoken.’”</span></span>
</div>
