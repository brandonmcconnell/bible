## Paul’s Greeting to the Ephesians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God,<br />To the saints in Ephesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts do not include in Ephesus.">a</a></sup>the faithful in Christ Jesus:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Galatians 1:3; Titus 1:4"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Blessings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly realms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> For He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless in His presence. In love</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> He predestined us for adoption as His sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of His will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> to the praise of His glorious grace, which He has freely given us in the Beloved One.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of His grace</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> that He lavished on us with all wisdom and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> And He has made known to us the mystery of His will according to His good pleasure, which He purposed in Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> as a plan for the fullness of time, to bring all things in heaven and on earth together in Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> In Him we were also chosen as God’s own, having been predestined according to the plan of Him who works out everything by the counsel of His will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> in order that we, who were the first to hope in Christ, would be for the praise of His glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> And in Him, having heard and believed the word of truth—the gospel of your salvation—you were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> who is the pledge of our inheritance until the redemption of those who are God’s possession, to the praise of His glory.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> For this reason, ever since I heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> I have not stopped giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in your knowledge of Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> I ask that the eyes of your heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR the eyes of your understanding">b</a></sup>may be enlightened, so that you may know the hope of His calling, the riches of His glorious inheritance in the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> and the surpassing greatness of His power to us who believe. These are in accordance with the working of His mighty strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; Psalms 110:1"> which He exerted in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly realms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> far above all rule and authority, power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in the present age but also in the one to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> And God put everything under His feet and made Him head over everything for the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all.</span>
</div>
</div>
