## Imitators of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Be imitators of God, therefore, as beloved children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> and walk in love, just as Christ loved us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NE and WH Christ loved you">a</a></sup>and gave Himself up for us as a fragrant sacrificial offering to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> But among you, as is proper among the saints, there must not be even a hint of sexual immorality, or of any kind of impurity, or of greed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> Nor should there be obscenity, foolish talk, or crude joking, which are out of character, but rather thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> For of this you can be sure: No immoral, impure, or greedy person (that is, an idolater) has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of such things the wrath of God is coming on the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Therefore do not be partakers with them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Children of Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> for the fruit of the light consists in all goodness, righteousness, and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> Test and prove what pleases the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> Have no fellowship with the fruitless deeds of darkness, but rather expose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For it is shameful even to mention what the disobedient do in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> But everything exposed by the light becomes visible,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SBL begins verse 14 after visible,">b</a></sup>for everything that is illuminated becomes a light itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> So it is said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Wake up, O sleeper,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rise up from the dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Christ will shine on you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> Pay careful attention, then, to how you walk, not as unwise but as wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> redeeming the time, because the days are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what the Lord’s will is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to reckless indiscretion. Instead, be filled with the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> Speak to one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs. Sing and make music in your hearts to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> always giving thanks to God the Father for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Wives and Husbands

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or in the fear of Christ.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wives, submit to your husbands as to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> For the husband is the head of the wife as Christ is the head of the church, His body, of which He is the Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> Now as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave Himself up for her</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> to sanctify her, cleansing her by the washing with water through the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> and to present her to Himself as a glorious church, without stain or wrinkle or any such blemish, but holy and blameless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> In the same way, husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> Indeed, no one ever hated his own body, but he nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ does the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> For we are members of His body.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="BYZ and TR include—of His flesh and of His bones.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> “For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Genesis 2:24 (see also LXX)">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This mystery is profound, but I am speaking about Christ and the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> Nevertheless, each one of you also must love his wife as he loves himself, and the wife must respect her husband.</span>
</div>
</div>
