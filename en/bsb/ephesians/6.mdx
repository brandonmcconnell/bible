## Children and Parents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; Matthew 15:4–6"> “Honor your father and mother” (which is the first commandment with a promise),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> “that it may go well with you and that you may have a long life on the earth.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> Fathers, do not provoke your children to wrath; instead, bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Serving with Honor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–21; Titus 2:9–10; Colossians 3:17–24"> Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect and fear and sincerity of heart, just as you would obey Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:22–23; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:10"> And do this not only to please them while they are watching, but as servants of Christ, doing the will of God from your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:5–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Serve with good will, as to the Lord and not to men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Luke 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> because you know that the Lord will reward each one for whatever good he does, whether he is slave or free.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:25–1; Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11"> And masters, do the same for your slaves. Give up your use of threats, because you know that He who is both their Master and yours is in heaven, and there is no favoritism with Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Full Armor of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> Finally, be strong in the Lord and in His mighty power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> Put on the full armor of God, so that you can make your stand against the devil’s schemes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this world’s darkness, and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> Therefore take up the full armor of God, so that when the day of evil comes, you will be able to stand your ground, and having done everything, to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand firm then, with the belt of truth buckled around your waist, with the breastplate of righteousness arrayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and with your feet fitted with the readiness of the gospel of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> In addition to all this, take up the shield of faith, with which you can extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> And take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> Pray in the Spirit at all times, with every kind of prayer and petition. To this end, stay alert with all perseverance in your prayers for all the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Pray also for me, that whenever I open my mouth, words may be given me so that I will boldly make known the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> for which I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may proclaim it fearlessly, as I should.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12"> Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will tell you everything, so that you also may know about me and what I am doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 2:17"> I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know about us, and that he may encourage your hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:16; Galatians 5:6; 1 Peter 5:14"> Peace to the brothers and love with faith from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Matthew 6:13"> Grace to all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.</span>
</div>
</div>
