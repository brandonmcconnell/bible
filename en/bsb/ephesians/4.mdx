## Unity in the Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> As a prisoner in the Lord, then, I urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling you have received:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> and with diligence to preserve the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to one hope when you were called;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> Now to each one of us grace has been given according to the measure of the gift of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> This is why it says:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or He says">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When He ascended on high,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He led captives away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gave gifts to men.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 68:18">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> What does “He ascended” mean, except that He also descended<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR except that He also descended first">c</a></sup>to the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> He who descended is the very One who ascended above all the heavens, in order to fill all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> And it was He who gave some to be apostles, some to be prophets, some to be evangelists, and some to be pastors and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> to equip the saints for works of ministry and to build up the body of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> until we all reach unity in the faith and in the knowledge of the Son of God, as we mature to the full measure of the stature of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Then we will no longer be infants, tossed about by the waves and carried around by every wind of teaching and by the clever cunning of men in their deceitful scheming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> Instead, speaking the truth in love, we will in all things grow up into Christ Himself, who is the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> From Him the whole body, fitted and held together by every supporting ligament, grows and builds itself up in love through the work of each individual part.</span>
</div>
</div>

## New Life in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> So I tell you this, and insist on it in the Lord, that you must no longer walk as the Gentiles do, in the futility of their thinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> They are darkened in their understanding and alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them due to the hardness of their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> Having lost all sense of shame, they have given themselves over to sensuality for the practice of every kind of impurity, with a craving for more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But this is not the way you came to know Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Surely you heard of Him and were taught in Him—in keeping with the truth that is in Jesus—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> to put off your former way of life, your old self, which is being corrupted by its deceitful desires;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> to be renewed in the spirit of your minds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and to put on the new self, created to be like God in true righteousness and holiness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Therefore each of you must put off falsehood and speak truthfully to his neighbor, for we are all members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> “Be angry, yet do not sin.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “In your anger do not sin.” Psalms 4:4">d</a></sup>Do not let the sun set upon your anger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> and do not give the devil a foothold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> He who has been stealing must steal no longer, but must work, doing good with his own hands, that he may have something to share with the one in need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building up the one in need and bringing grace to those who listen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, outcry and slander, along with every form of malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> Be kind and tenderhearted to one another, forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you.</span>
</div>
</div>
