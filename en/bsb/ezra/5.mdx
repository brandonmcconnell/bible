## Temple Rebuilding Resumes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 1:1; Zechariah 1:1–21"> Later, the prophets Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem in the name of the God of Israel, who was over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 3:2; Ezra 6:14; Zechariah 3:1–4"> Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jozadak is a variant of Jehozadak; see 1 Chronicles 6:14.">a</a></sup>rose up and began to rebuild the house of God in Jerusalem. And the prophets of God were with them, helping them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:6; Ezra 6:13; Ezra 5:9"> At that time Tattenai the governor of the region west of the Euphrates,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Aramaic the governor beyond the River; also in verse 6">b</a></sup>Shethar-bozenai, and their associates went to the Jews and asked, “Who authorized you to rebuild this temple and restore this structure?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They also asked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="LXX and Syriac; Aramaic Then we told them,">c</a></sup>“What are the names of the men who are constructing this building?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 33:18; Ezra 7:6; Ezra 7:28"> But the eye of their God was on the elders of the Jews, so that they were not stopped until a report was sent to Darius and written instructions about this matter were returned.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Tattenai’s Letter to Darius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 4:9; Ezra 4:11; Ezra 6:6"> This is the text of the letter that Tattenai the governor of the region west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates, the officials in the region, sent to King Darius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 4:17; Daniel 6:21; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> The report they sent him read as follows:<br />To King Darius:<br />All peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:6; Mark 13:1–2; Daniel 3:26"> Let it be known to the king that we went into the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. The people are rebuilding it with large stones and placing timbers in the walls. This work is being carried out diligently and is prospering in their hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 5:3–4"> So we questioned the elders and asked, “Who authorized you to rebuild this temple and restore this structure?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> We also asked for their names, so that we could write down the names of their leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Aramaic the names of the men at their heads">d</a></sup>for your information.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:16; Jonah 1:9; Acts 27:23"> And this is the answer they returned:<br />“We are servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the temple that was built many years ago, which a great king of Israel built and completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16–17; Daniel 9:5; 2 Kings 24:2"> But since our fathers angered the God of heaven, He delivered them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean who destroyed this temple and carried away the people to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 6:3–5; Isaiah 44:28–1; Ezra 1:1–8"> In the first year of his reign, however, Cyrus king of Babylon issued a decree to rebuild this house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 6:5; Daniel 5:2–3; Ezra 5:16"> He also removed from the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or palace">e</a></sup>of Babylon the gold and silver articles belonging to the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken and carried there from the temple in Jerusalem. King Cyrus gave these articles to a man named Sheshbazzar, whom he appointed governor</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 6:3; Ezra 1:2; Ezra 3:3"> and instructed, ‘Take these articles, put them in the temple in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be rebuilt on its original site.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 3:10; Ezra 6:15; Ezra 3:8"> So this Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundation of the house of God in Jerusalem, and from that time until now it has been under construction, but it has not yet been completed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 4:15; Proverbs 25:2; Ezra 6:1–5"> Now, therefore, if it pleases the king, let a search be made of the royal archives in Babylon to see if King Cyrus did indeed issue a decree to rebuild the house of God in Jerusalem. Then let the king send us his decision in this matter.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
