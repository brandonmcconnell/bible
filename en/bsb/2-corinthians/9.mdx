## God Loves a Cheerful Giver

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 8:4–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–10"> Now about the service to the saints, there is no need for me to write to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:10; 2 Corinthians 8:8; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> For I know your eagerness to help, and I have been boasting to the Macedonians that since last year you in Achaia were prepared to give. And your zeal has stirred most of them to do likewise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> But I am sending the brothers in order that our boasting about you in this matter should not prove empty, but that you will be prepared, just as I said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> Otherwise, if any Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we—to say nothing of you—would be ashamed of having been so confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> So I thought it necessary to urge the brothers to visit you beforehand and make arrangements for the generous gift you had promised. This way, your gift will be prepared generously and not begrudgingly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Luke 6:38; Proverbs 22:9"> Remember this: Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> Each one should give what he has decided in his heart to give, not out of regret or compulsion. For God loves a cheerful giver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Proverbs 22:8, LXX addition.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> And God is able to make all grace abound to you, so that in all things, at all times, having all that you need, you will abound in every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 112:9; Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> As it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He has scattered abroad His gifts to the poor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His righteousness endures forever.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 112:9">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> Now He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your store of seed and will increase the harvest of your righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> You will be enriched in every way to be generous on every occasion, and through us your generosity will produce thanksgiving to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> For this ministry of service is not only supplying the needs of the saints but is also overflowing in many expressions of thanksgiving to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> Because of the proof this ministry provides, the saints will glorify God for your obedient confession of the gospel of Christ, and for the generosity of your contribution to them and to all the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> And their prayers for you will express their affection for you, because of the surpassing grace God has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Ephesians 5:20; John 3:16"> Thanks be to God for His indescribable gift!</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
