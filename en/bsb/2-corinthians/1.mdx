<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:1; Philippians 1:1; Ephesians 1:1"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy our brother,<br />To the church of God in Corinth, together with all the saints throughout Achaia:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 6:23; Philippians 1:2"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The God of All Comfort

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 15:5–6"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Isaiah 51:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17"> who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those in any trouble with the comfort we ourselves have received from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; Philippians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11"> For just as the sufferings of Christ overflow to us, so also through Christ our comfort overflows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; 2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 4:15–18"> If we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; if we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which accomplishes in you patient endurance of the same sufferings we experience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 1:4–7; James 1:12"> And our hope for you is sure, because we know that just as you share in our sufferings, so also you will share in our comfort.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7–12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; 1 Corinthians 4:8"> We do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of the hardships we encountered in the province of Asia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally in Asia; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.">a</a></sup>We were under a burden far beyond our ability to endure, so that we despaired even of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Hebrews 11:19; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> Indeed, we felt we were under the sentence of death, in order that we would not trust in ourselves, but in God, who raises the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; Psalms 34:19; 2 Peter 2:9"> He has delivered us from such a deadly peril, and He will deliver us. In Him we have placed our hope that He will yet again deliver us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 1:19; 2 Corinthians 4:15; Acts 12:5"> as you help us by your prayers. Then many will give thanks on our behalf for the favor shown us in answer to their prayers.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Change of Plans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; James 3:13–18"> For this is our boast: Our conscience testifies that we have conducted ourselves in the world, and especially in relation to you, in the holiness and sincerity that are from God—not in worldly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally fleshly">b</a></sup>wisdom, but in the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; Philemon 1:6; 2 Corinthians 13:6"> For we do not write you anything that is beyond your ability to read and understand. And I hope that you will understand us completely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:19–20"> as you have already understood us in part, that you may boast of us just as we will boast of you in the day of our Lord Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR the Lord Jesus">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:19; Romans 1:11; Romans 15:29"> Confident of this, I planned to visit you first, so that you might receive a double blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:5–7; Acts 21:5; Acts 19:21–22"> I wanted to visit you on my way to Macedonia, and to return to you from Macedonia, and then to have you help me on my way to Judea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:37; James 5:12; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3"> When I planned this, did I do it carelessly? Or do I make my plans by human standards, so as to say “Yes, yes” and also “No, no”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; Revelation 3:7; John 7:28"> But as surely as God is faithful, our message to you is not “Yes” and “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:17; 2 John 1:9"> For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was proclaimed among you by me and Silvanus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Silas">d</a></sup>and Timothy, was not “Yes” and “No,” but in Him it has always been “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 2:24–25; Galatians 3:22; Revelation 7:12"> For all the promises of God are “Yes” in Christ. And so through Him, our “Amen” is spoken to the glory of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:27; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 John 2:20"> Now it is God who establishes both us and you in Christ. He anointed us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 1:13–14"> placed His seal on us, and put His Spirit in our hearts as a pledge of what is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 4:21; Romans 1:9"> I call God as my witness that it was in order to spare you that I did not return to Corinth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Corinthians 15:1; Philippians 1:25–26"> Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers with you for your joy, because it is by faith that you stand firm.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
