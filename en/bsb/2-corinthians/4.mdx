## The Light of the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:9; Ephesians 3:13"> Therefore, since God in His mercy has given us this ministry,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Therefore, having this ministry, as we have received mercy,">a</a></sup>we do not lose heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> Instead, we have renounced secret and shameful ways. We do not practice deceit, nor do we distort the word of God. On the contrary, by open proclamation of the truth, we commend ourselves to every man’s conscience in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> And even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled to those who are perishing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:40; 2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 26:18"> The god of this age has blinded the minds of unbelievers, so they cannot see the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6"> For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your servants for Jesus’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 60:2; Genesis 1:3; 1 Peter 2:9"> For God, who said, “Let light shine out of darkness,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Genesis 1:3">b</a></sup>made His light shine in our hearts to give us the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="SBL, NE, and WH in the face of Christ">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Treasure in Jars of Clay

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Ephesians 2:8–9"> Now we have this treasure in jars of clay to show that this surpassingly great power is from God and not from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Romans 8:35–37; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> We are hard pressed on all sides, but not crushed; perplexed, but not in despair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Micah 7:8; Psalms 37:24–25"> persecuted, but not forsaken; struck down, but not destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 6:5; 2 Timothy 2:11; 1 Peter 4:13"> We always carry around in our body the death of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 8:36; Psalms 44:22"> For we who are alive are always consigned to death for Jesus’ sake, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Corinthians 4:10; Acts 20:24"> So then, death is at work in us, but life is at work in you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 116:10; Hebrews 11:1–40; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> And in keeping with what is written, “I believed, therefore I have spoken,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Psalms 116:10 (see also LXX)">d</a></sup>we who have the same spirit of faith also believe and therefore speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; Romans 8:11; Jude 1:24"> knowing that the One who raised the Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="SBL who raised Jesus">e</a></sup>will also raise us with Jesus and present us with you in His presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Ephesians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> All this is for your benefit, so that the grace that is extending to more and more people may cause thanksgiving to overflow, to the glory of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Colossians 3:10; Isaiah 40:29"> Therefore we do not lose heart. Though our outer self is wasting away, yet our inner self is being renewed day by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:18; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 5:3–5"> For our light and momentary affliction is producing for us an eternal weight of glory that is far beyond comparison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 8:24–25; Hebrews 11:1"> So we fix our eyes not on what is seen, but on what is unseen. For what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.</span>
</div>
</div>
