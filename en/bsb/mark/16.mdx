## The Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 23:56–12; John 20:1–10; Matthew 28:1–10"> When the Sabbath was over, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices so they could go and anoint the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 20:1; Luke 24:1; Matthew 28:1"> Very early on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally And very early on the first of the Sabbaths,">a</a></sup>just after sunrise, they went to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> They were asking one another, “Who will roll away the stone from the entrance of the tomb?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Luke 24:2; John 20:1"> But when they looked up, they saw that the stone had been rolled away, even though it was extremely large.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> When they entered the tomb, they saw a young man dressed in a white robe sitting on the right side, and they were alarmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> But he said to them, “Do not be alarmed. You are looking for Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He has risen! He is not here! See the place where they put Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> But go, tell His disciples and Peter, ‘He is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see Him, just as He told you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> So the women left the tomb and ran away, trembling and bewildered. And in their fear they did not say a word to anyone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some early manuscripts end the Gospel of Mark after verse 8. ## The Great Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Later, as they were eating, Jesus appeared to the Eleven and rebuked them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they did not believe those who had seen Him after He had risen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> And He said to them, “Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> And these signs will accompany those who believe: In My name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new tongues;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="WH in tongues">d</a></sup></span>
