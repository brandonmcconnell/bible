## The Parable of the Sower

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> Once again Jesus began to teach beside the sea, and such a large crowd gathered around Him that He got into a boat and sat in it, while all the people crowded along the shore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> And He taught them many things in parables, and in His teaching He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> “Listen! A farmer went out to sow his seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> And as he was sowing, some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and devoured it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> Some fell on rocky ground, where it did not have much soil. It sprang up quickly because the soil was shallow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> But when the sun rose, the seedlings were scorched, and they withered because they had no root.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> Other seed fell among thorns, which grew up and choked the seedlings, and they yielded no crop.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> Still other seed fell on good soil, where it sprouted, grew up, and produced a crop—one bearing thirtyfold, another sixtyfold, and another a hundredfold.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> Then Jesus said, “He who has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Purpose of Jesus’ Parables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> As soon as Jesus was alone with the Twelve and those around Him, they asked Him about the parable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> He replied, “The mystery of the kingdom of God has been given to you, but to those on the outside everything is expressed in parables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> so that,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘they may be ever seeing but never perceiving,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ever hearing but never understanding;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">otherwise they might turn</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be forgiven.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isaiah 6:9–10 (see also LXX)">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Sower Explained

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> Then Jesus said to them, “Do you not understand this parable? Then how will you understand any of the parables?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> The farmer sows the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> Some are like the seeds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the word; similarly in verses 16, 18, and 20; literally Now these are the ones">b</a></sup>along the path, where the word is sown. As soon as they hear it, Satan comes and takes away the word that was sown in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> Some are like the seeds sown on rocky ground. They hear the word and at once receive it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> But they themselves have no root, and they remain for only a season. When trouble or persecution comes because of the word, they quickly fall away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> Others are like the seeds sown among the thorns. They hear the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> but the worries of this life, the deceitfulness of wealth, and the desire for other things come in and choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> Still others are like the seeds sown on good soil. They hear the word, receive it, and produce a crop—thirtyfold, sixtyfold, or a hundredfold.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lesson of the Lamp

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> Jesus also said to them, “Does anyone bring in a lamp to put it under a basket or under a bed? Doesn’t he set it on a stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> For there is nothing hidden that will not be disclosed, and nothing concealed that will not be brought to light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> He went on to say, “Pay attention to what you hear. With the measure you use, it will be measured to you, and even more will be added to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> For whoever has will be given more. But whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Seed Growing Secretly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> Jesus also said, “The kingdom of God is like a man who scatters seed on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> Night and day he sleeps and wakes, and the seed sprouts and grows, though he knows not how.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> All by itself the earth produces a crop—first the stalk, then the head, then grain that ripens within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> And as soon as the grain is ripe, he swings the sickle, because the harvest has come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Joel 3:13, including LXX.">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Mustard Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> Then He asked, “To what can we compare the kingdom of God? With what parable shall we present it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like a mustard seed, which is the smallest of all seeds sown upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> But after it is planted, it grows to be the largest of all garden plants and puts forth great branches, so that the birds of the air nest in its shade.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> With many such parables Jesus spoke the word to them, to the extent that they could understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> He did not tell them anything without using a parable. But privately He explained everything to His own disciples.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calms the Storm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> When that evening came, He said to His disciples, “Let us cross to the other side.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> After they had dismissed the crowd, they took Jesus with them, since He was already in the boat. And there were other boats with Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> Soon a violent windstorm came up, and the waves were breaking over the boat, so that it was being swamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> But Jesus was in the stern, sleeping on the cushion. So they woke Him and said, “Teacher, don’t You care that we are perishing?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> Then Jesus got up and rebuked the wind and the sea. “Silence!” He commanded. “Be still!” And the wind died down, and it was perfectly calm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> “Why are you so afraid?” He asked. “Do you still have no faith?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> Overwhelmed with fear, they asked one another, “Who is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?”</span>
</div>
</div>
