## Temple Destruction and Other Signs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:1–51; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 10:19"> As Jesus was leaving the temple, one of His disciples said to Him, “Teacher, look at the magnificent stones and buildings!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:7–8"> “Do you see all these great buildings?” Jesus replied. “Not one stone here will be left on another; every one will be thrown down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> While Jesus was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked Him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> “Tell us, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign that they are about to be fulfilled?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> Jesus began by telling them, “See to it that no one deceives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Many will come in My name, claiming, ‘I am He,’ and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> When you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be alarmed. These things must happen, but the end is still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places, as well as famines. These are the beginning of birth pains.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Witnessing to All Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> So be on your guard. You will be delivered over to the councils and beaten in the synagogues. On My account you will stand before governors and kings as witnesses to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> And the gospel must first be proclaimed to all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> But when they arrest you and hand you over, do not worry beforehand what to say. Instead, speak whatever you are given at that time, for it will not be you speaking, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> Brother will betray brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rise against their parents and have them put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> You will be hated by everyone because of My name, but the one who perseveres to the end will be saved.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Abomination of Desolation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> So when you see the abomination of desolation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Daniel 9:27, Daniel 11:31, and Daniel 12:11; BYZ and TR include spoken of by Daniel the prophet.">a</a></sup>standing where it should not be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or where he should not be">b</a></sup>(let the reader understand), then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> Let no one on the housetop go back inside to retrieve anything from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And let no one in the field return for his cloak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> How miserable those days will be for pregnant and nursing mothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pray that this will not occur in the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For those will be days of tribulation unseen from the beginning of God’s creation until now, and never to be seen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> If the Lord had not cut short those days, nobody would be saved. But for the sake of the elect, whom He has chosen, He has cut them short.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> At that time if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or ‘There He is!’ do not believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false Christs and false prophets will appear and perform signs and wonders to deceive even the elect, if that were possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> So be on your guard; I have told you everything in advance.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lesson of the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> Now learn this lesson<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or this parable">e</a></sup>from the fig tree: As soon as its branches become tender and sprout leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> So also, when you see these things happening, know that He is near,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or it is near">f</a></sup>right at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Truly I tell you, this generation will not pass away until all these things have happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will never pass away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Readiness at Any Hour

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> No one knows about that day or hour, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Be on your guard and stay alert!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="BYZ and TR Be on your guard, stay alert, and pray!">g</a></sup>For you do not know when the appointed time will come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> It is like a man going on a journey who left his house, put each servant in charge of his own task, and instructed the doorkeeper to keep watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Therefore keep watch, because you do not know when the master of the house will return—whether in the evening, at midnight, when the rooster crows, or in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> Otherwise, he may arrive without notice and find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> And what I say to you, I say to everyone: Keep watch!”</span>
</div>
