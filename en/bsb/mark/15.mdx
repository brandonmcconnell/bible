## Jesus Delivered to Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> Early in the morning, the chief priests, elders, scribes, and the whole Sanhedrin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the whole Council">a</a></sup>devised a plan. They bound Jesus, led Him away, and handed Him over to Pilate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> So Pilate questioned Him, “Are You the King of the Jews?”<br />“You have said so,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> And the chief priests began to accuse Him of many things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> Then Pilate questioned Him again, “Have You no answer? Look how many charges they are bringing against You!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> But to Pilate’s amazement, Jesus made no further reply.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crowd Chooses Barabbas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now it was Pilate’s custom at the feast to release to the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally Now at the feast he would release to them">b</a></sup>a prisoner of their choosing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> And a man named Barabbas was imprisoned with the rebels who had committed murder during the insurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the crowd went up and began asking Pilate to keep his custom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> “Do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?” Pilate asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> For he knew it was out of envy that the chief priests had handed Jesus over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to have him release Barabbas to them instead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pilate Delivers Up Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> So Pilate asked them again, “What then do you want me to do with the One you call the King of the Jews?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they shouted back, “Crucify Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> “Why?” asked Pilate. “What evil has He done?”<br />But they shouted all the louder, “Crucify Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> And wishing to satisfy the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas to them. But he had Jesus flogged, and handed Him over to be crucified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Soldiers Mock Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> Then the soldiers led Jesus away into the palace (that is, the Praetorium) and called the whole company together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They dressed Him in a purple robe, twisted together a crown of thorns, and set it on His head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> And they began to salute Him: “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> They kept striking His head with a staff and spitting on Him. And they knelt down and bowed before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> After they had mocked Him, they removed the purple robe and put His own clothes back on Him. Then they led Him out to crucify Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> Now Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus, was passing by on his way in from the country, and the soldiers forced him to carry the cross of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> They brought Jesus to a place called Golgotha, which means The Place of the Skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> There they offered Him wine mixed with myrrh, but He did not take it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> And they crucified Him.<br />They also divided His garments by casting lots to decide what each of them would take.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Psalms 22:18.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> It was the third hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, nine in the morning">d</a></sup>when they crucified Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> And the charge inscribed against Him read:<br />THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> Along with Jesus, they crucified two robbers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or insurrectionists">e</a></sup>one on His right and one on His left.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR include 28 So the Scripture was fulfilled that says, “And He was numbered with the transgressors.” See Isaiah 53:12 and Luke 22:37.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> And those who passed by heaped abuse on Him, shaking their heads and saying, “Aha! You who are going to destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> come down from the cross and save Yourself!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> In the same way, the chief priests and scribes mocked Him among themselves, saying, “He saved others, but He cannot save Himself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, so that we may see and believe!” And even those who were crucified with Him berated Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> From the sixth hour until the ninth hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, from noon until three in the afternoon">g</a></sup>darkness came over all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> At the ninth hour, Jesus cried out in a loud voice, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” which means, “My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Psalms 22:1">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> When some of those standing nearby heard this, they said, “Behold, He is calling Elijah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> And someone ran and filled a sponge with sour wine.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or a sponge with wine vinegar">i</a></sup>He put it on a reed and held it up for Jesus to drink,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Psalms 69:21.">j</a></sup>saying, “Leave Him alone. Let us see if Elijah comes to take Him down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> But Jesus let out a loud cry and breathed His last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> And the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> When the centurion standing there in front of Jesus saw how He had breathed His last,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="BYZ and TR saw how, having cried out, He had breathed His last">k</a></sup>he said, “Truly this man was the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> And there were also women watching from a distance. Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Joses is a variant of Joseph; see Matthew 27:56.">l</a></sup>and Salome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> These women had followed Jesus and ministered to Him while He was in Galilee, and there were many other women who had come up to Jerusalem with Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Burial of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> Now it was already evening. Since it was Preparation Day (that is, the day before the Sabbath),</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea, a prominent Council member who himself was waiting for the kingdom of God, boldly went to Pilate to ask for the body of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> Pilate was surprised to hear that Jesus was already dead, so he summoned the centurion to ask if this was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> When Pilate had confirmed it with the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> So Joseph bought a linen cloth, took down the body of Jesus, wrapped it in the cloth, and placed it in a tomb that had been cut out of the rock. Then he rolled a stone against the entrance to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joseph<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or Joses">m</a></sup>saw where His body was placed.</span>
</div>
</div>
