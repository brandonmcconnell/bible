## The Mission of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> This is the beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="ECM, NE, BYZ, and TR; SBL and WH the beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> As it is written in Isaiah the prophet:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR in the prophets:">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I will send My messenger ahead of You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare Your way.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Malachi 3:1">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“A voice of one calling in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Prepare the way for the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">make straight paths for Him.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isaiah 40:3 (see also LXX)">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> John the Baptist appeared in the wilderness, preaching a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> People went out to him from all of Jerusalem and the countryside of Judea. Confessing their sins, they were baptized by him in the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> John was clothed in camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> And he proclaimed: “After me will come One more powerful than I, the straps of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and untie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I baptize you with water,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or in water">e</a></sup>but He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or in the Holy Spirit">f</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> In those days Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> As soon as Jesus came up out of the water, He saw<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or he saw; see John 1:32–33">g</a></sup>the heavens breaking open and the Spirit descending on Him like a dove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> And a voice came from heaven: “You are My beloved Son; in You I am well pleased.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Temptation and Preaching of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> At once the Spirit drove Jesus into the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> and He was there for forty days, being tempted by Satan. He was with the wild animals, and the angels ministered to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> After the arrest of John, Jesus went into Galilee and proclaimed the gospel of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="BYZ and TR the gospel of the kingdom of God">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> “The time is fulfilled,” He said, “and the kingdom of God is near. Repent and believe in the gospel!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> As Jesus was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> “Come, follow Me,” Jesus said, “and I will make you fishers of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> And at once they left their nets and followed Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> Going on a little farther, He saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat, mending their nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> Immediately Jesus called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and followed Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Expels an Unclean Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> Then Jesus and His companions went to Capernaum, and right away Jesus entered the synagogue on the Sabbath and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> The people were astonished at His teaching, because He taught as one who had authority, and not as the scribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> Suddenly a man with an unclean spirit cried out in the synagogue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> “What do You want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are—the Holy One of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> But Jesus rebuked the spirit. “Be silent!” He said. “Come out of him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> At this, the unclean spirit threw the man into convulsions and came out with a loud shriek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> All the people were amazed and began to ask one another, “What is this? A new teaching with authority! He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> And the news about Jesus spread quickly through the whole region of Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals at Peter’s House

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> As soon as Jesus and His companions had left the synagogue, they went with James and John to the home of Simon and Andrew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> Simon’s mother-in-law was sick in bed with a fever, and they promptly told Jesus about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> So He went to her, took her by the hand, and helped her up. The fever left her, and she began to serve them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> That evening, after sunset, people brought to Jesus all the sick and demon-possessed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> and the whole town gathered at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> And He healed many who were ill with various diseases and drove out many demons. But He would not allow the demons to speak, because they knew who He was.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Prays and Preaches

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> Early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up and went out to a solitary place to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Simon and his companions went to look for Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> and when they found Him, they said, “Everyone is looking for You!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> But Jesus answered, “Let us go on to the neighboring towns so I can preach there as well, for that is why I have come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> So He went throughout Galilee, preaching in their synagogues and driving out demons.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Leper’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> Then a leper<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A leper was one afflicted with a skin disease. See Leviticus 13.">i</a></sup>came to Jesus, begging on his knees: “If You are willing, You can make me clean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> Moved with compassion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="SBL Moved with indignation">j</a></sup>Jesus reached out His hand and touched the man. “I am willing,” He said. “Be clean!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> And immediately the leprosy left him, and the man was cleansed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> Jesus promptly sent him away with a stern warning:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> “See that you don’t tell anyone. But go, show yourself to the priest and present the offering Moses prescribed for your cleansing, as a testimony to them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Leviticus 14:1–32.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But the man went out and openly began to proclaim and spread the news.<br />Consequently, Jesus could no longer enter a town in plain view, but He stayed out in solitary places. Yet people came to Him from every quarter.</span>
</div>
</div>
