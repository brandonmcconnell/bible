## The Demons and the Pigs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> On the other side of the sea, they arrived in the region of the Gerasenes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR Gadarenes; GOC Gergesenes">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> As soon as Jesus got out of the boat, He was met by a man with an unclean spirit, who was coming from the tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> This man had been living in the tombs and could no longer be restrained, even with chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> Though he was often bound with chains and shackles, he had broken the chains and shattered the shackles. Now there was no one with the strength to subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> Night and day in the tombs and in the mountains he kept crying out and cutting himself with stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> When the man saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and fell on his knees before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> And he shouted in a loud voice, “What do You want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg You before God not to torture me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> For Jesus had already declared, “Come out of this man, you unclean spirit!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> “What is your name?” Jesus asked.<br />“My name is Legion,” he replied, “for we are many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> And he begged Jesus repeatedly not to send them out of that region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> There on the nearby hillside a large herd of pigs was feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> So the demons begged Jesus, “Send us to the pigs, so that we may enter them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> He gave them permission, and the unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs, and the herd of about two thousand rushed down the steep bank into the sea and drowned in the water.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally and were drowned in the sea">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> Those tending the pigs ran off and reported this in the town and countryside, and the people went out to see what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> When they came to Jesus, they saw the man who had been possessed by the legion of demons sitting there, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Those who had seen it described what had happened to the demon-possessed man and also to the pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> And the people began to beg Jesus to leave their region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> As He was getting into the boat, the man who had been possessed by the demons begged to go with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> But Jesus would not allow him. “Go home to your own people,” He said, “and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and what mercy He has shown you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> So the man went away and began to proclaim throughout the Decapolis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Ten Cities">c</a></sup>how much Jesus had done for him. And everyone was amazed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Healing Touch of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> When Jesus had again crossed by boat to the other side, a large crowd gathered around Him beside the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> A synagogue leader named Jairus arrived, and seeing Jesus, he fell at His feet</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> and pleaded with Him urgently, “My little daughter is near death. Please come and place Your hands on her, so that she will be healed and live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> So Jesus went with him, and a large crowd followed and pressed around Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> And a woman was there who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> She had borne much agony under the care of many physicians and had spent all she had, but to no avail. Instead, her condition had only grown worse.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> When the woman heard about Jesus, she came up through the crowd behind Him and touched His cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For she kept saying, “If only I touch His garments, I will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> Immediately her bleeding stopped, and she sensed in her body that she was healed of her affliction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> At once Jesus was aware that power had gone out from Him. Turning to the crowd, He asked, “Who touched My garments?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> His disciples answered, “You can see the crowd pressing in on You, and yet You ask, ‘Who touched Me?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But He kept looking around to see who had done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> Then the woman, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before Him trembling in fear, and she told Him the whole truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> “Daughter,” said Jesus, “your faith has healed you. Go in peace and be free of your affliction.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While He was still speaking, messengers from the house of Jairus arrived and said, “Your daughter is dead; why bother the Teacher anymore?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But Jesus overheard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or ignored">d</a></sup>their conversation and said to Jairus, “Do not be afraid; just believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And He did not allow anyone to accompany Him except Peter, James, and John the brother of James.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> When they arrived at the house of the synagogue leader, Jesus saw the commotion and the people weeping and wailing loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> He went inside and asked, “Why all this commotion and weeping? The child is not dead, but asleep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> And they laughed at Him.<br />After He had put them all outside, He took the child’s father and mother and His own companions, and went in to see the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> Taking her by the hand, Jesus said, “Talitha koum!” which means, “Little girl, I say to you, get up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> Immediately the girl got up and began to walk around (she was twelve years old). And at once they were utterly astounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> Then Jesus gave strict orders that no one should know about this, and He told them to give her something to eat.</span>
</div>
</div>
