## A Greeting from Peter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ,<br />To the elect who are exiles of the Dispersion throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia, chosen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally To the elect sojourners of the Diaspora of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia. These provinces were located in what is now Turkey.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> according to the foreknowledge of God the Father and sanctified by the Spirit for obedience to Jesus Christ and sprinkling by His blood:<br />Grace and peace be yours in abundance.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Living Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! By His great mercy He has given us new birth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or has caused us to be born again or has begotten us again">b</a></sup>into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> and into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, reserved in heaven for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who through faith are shielded by God’s power for the salvation that is ready to be revealed in the last time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while you may have had to suffer grief in various trials</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> so that the proven character of your faith—more precious than gold, which perishes even though refined by fire—may result in praise, glory, and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> Though you have not seen Him, you love Him; and though you do not see Him now, you believe in Him and rejoice with an inexpressible and glorious joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> now that you are receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> Concerning this salvation, the prophets who foretold the grace to come to you searched and investigated carefully,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> trying to determine the time and setting to which the Spirit of Christ in them was pointing when He predicted the sufferings of Christ and the glories to follow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> It was revealed to them that they were not serving themselves, but you, when they foretold the things now announced by those who preached the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. Even angels long to look into these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Call to Holiness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Therefore prepare your minds for action.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally Wherefore gird up the loins of your mind">c</a></sup>Be sober-minded. Set your hope fully on the grace to be given you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> As obedient children, do not conform to the passions of your former ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> But just as He who called you is holy, so be holy in all you do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> for it is written: “Be holy, because I am holy.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 19:2">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> Since you call on a Father who judges each one’s work impartially, conduct yourselves in reverent fear during your stay as foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> For you know that it was not with perishable things such as silver or gold that you were redeemed from the empty way of life you inherited from your forefathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but with the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without blemish or spot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> He was known before the foundation of the world, but was revealed in the last times for your sake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> Through Him you believe in God, who raised Him from the dead and glorified Him; and so your faith and hope are in God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Enduring Word

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Since you have purified your souls by obedience to the truth so that you have a genuine love for your brothers, love one another deeply, from a pure heart.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="SBL, NE, and WH from the heart">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> For you have been born again, not of perishable seed, but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> For,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“All flesh is like grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all its glory like the flowers of the field;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the grass withers and the flowers fall,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> <span className="pl pl-2">but the word of the Lord stands forever.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isaiah 40:6–8">f</a></sup><br />And this is the word that was proclaimed to you.</span>
</div>
</div>
