## Instructions to Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> As a fellow elder, a witness of Christ’s sufferings, and a partaker of the glory to be revealed, I appeal to the elders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="SBL, NE, and WH I appeal therefore to the elders">a</a></sup>among you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> Be shepherds of God’s flock that is among you, watching over them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NE and WH do not include watching over them.">b</a></sup>not out of compulsion, but willingly, as God would have you;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="WH, BYZ, and TR but willingly">c</a></sup>not out of greed, but out of eagerness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> not lording it over those entrusted to you, but being examples to the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> And when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the crown of glory that will never fade away.</span>

<div className="bp poetry">

## Benediction and Farewell

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> And after you have suffered for a little while, the God of all grace, who has called you to His eternal glory in Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="BYZ and TR in Christ Jesus">e</a></sup>will Himself restore you, secure you, strengthen you, and establish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To Him be the power forever and ever. Amen.</span>
