## Thanksgiving and Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Colossians 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> We always thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, when we pray for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:15; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> because we have heard about your faith in Christ Jesus and your love for all the saints—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3–4; Ephesians 1:13; 2 Timothy 4:8"> the faith and love proceeding from the hope stored up for you in heaven, of which you have already heard in the word of truth, the gospel</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Philippians 1:11; Colossians 1:23"> that has come to you.<br />All over the world this gospel is bearing fruit and growing, just as it has been doing among you since the day you heard it and truly understood the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; Philemon 1:23; Colossians 4:7"> You learned it from Epaphras, our beloved fellow servant, who is a faithful minister of Christ on our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NE, NA, BYZ, and TR your">b</a></sup>behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 15:30; 2 Timothy 1:7; Galatians 5:22"> and who also informed us of your love in the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 3:17; Philippians 1:9–11; James 1:5"> For this reason, since the day we heard about you, we have not stopped praying for you and asking God to fill you with the knowledge of His will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; Hebrews 13:21; 2 Peter 1:2–3"> so that you may walk in a manner worthy of the Lord and may please Him in every way: bearing fruit in every good work, growing in the knowledge of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 4:2"> being strengthened with all power according to His glorious might so that you may have full endurance and patience, and joyfully</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:32; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:18"> giving thanks to the Father, who has qualified you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR us">c</a></sup>to share in the inheritance of the saints in the light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; 1 Peter 2:9"> He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and brought us into the kingdom of His beloved Son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Romans 3:24–25; 1 Peter 1:19–20"> in whom we have redemption,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR redemption through His blood,">d</a></sup>the forgiveness of sins.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Supremacy of the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; John 14:9; John 1:1"> The Son is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> For in Him all things were created, things in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities. All things were created through Him and for Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Acts 17:28"> He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:22–23; Revelation 1:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> And He is the head of the body, the church; He is the beginning and firstborn from among the dead, so that in all things He may have preeminence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 2:9; John 1:16; Ephesians 1:23"> For God was pleased to have all His fullness dwell in Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–21; Ephesians 1:10; 1 John 4:9–10"> and through Him to reconcile to Himself all things, whether things on earth or things in heaven, by making peace through the blood of His cross.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Titus 3:3–7; Ephesians 2:1–3"> Once you were alienated from God and were hostile in your minds, engaging in evil deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jude 1:24; Ephesians 1:4"> But now He has reconciled you by Christ’s physical body through death to present you holy, unblemished, and blameless in His presence—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; Matthew 24:13–14"> if indeed you continue in your faith, established and firm, not moved from the hope of the gospel you heard, which has been proclaimed to every creature<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or in all creation">e</a></sup>under heaven, and of which I, Paul, have become a servant.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Suffering for the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 5:3; 2 Timothy 1:8; 2 Corinthians 1:5–8"> Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and I fill up in my flesh what is lacking in regard to Christ’s afflictions for the sake of His body, which is the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 3:2; Colossians 1:23; Galatians 2:7–8"> I became its servant by the commission God gave me to fully proclaim to you the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:10; Romans 16:25–26; Ephesians 3:3–10"> the mystery that was hidden for ages and generations but is now revealed to His saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:20; Romans 8:10; Ephesians 3:16–17"> To them God has chosen to make known among the Gentiles the glorious riches of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jeremiah 3:15; Ephesians 4:11–13"> We proclaim Him, admonishing and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone perfect<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or fully mature">f</a></sup>in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Ephesians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> To this end I also labor, striving with all His energy working powerfully within me.</span>
</div>
</div>
