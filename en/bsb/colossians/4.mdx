<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8–20; James 2:13; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Masters, supply your slaves with what is right and fair, since you know that you also have a Master in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17–18; Philippians 4:6; Ephesians 6:18"> Devote yourselves to prayer, being watchful and thankful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 14:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:1–2"> as you pray also for us, that God may open to us a door for the word, so that we may proclaim the mystery of Christ, for which I am in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 4:6"> Pray that I may declare it clearly, as I should.</span>

## Greetings from Paul’s Fellow Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> Tychicus will tell you all the news about me. He is a beloved brother, a faithful minister, and a fellow servant in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know about us, and that he may encourage your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> With him I am sending Onesimus, our faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will tell you about everything here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> My fellow prisoner Aristarchus sends you greetings, as does Mark the cousin of Barnabas. You have already received instructions about him: If he comes to you, welcome him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> Jesus, who is called Justus, also sends greetings. These are the only Jews among my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Epaphras, who is one of you and a servant of Christ Jesus, sends you greetings. He is always wrestling in prayer for you, so that you may stand mature and fully assured in the full will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> For I testify about him that he goes to great pains for you and for those at Laodicea and Hierapolis.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Luke, the beloved physician, and Demas send you greetings.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Signature and Final Instructions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Greet the brothers in Laodicea, as well as Nympha and the church that meets at her house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> After this letter has been read among you, make sure that it is also read in the church of the Laodiceans, and that you in turn read the letter from Laodicea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> Tell Archippus: “See to it that you complete the ministry you have received in the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; 2 Timothy 4:22; 1 Timothy 6:21"> This greeting is in my own hand—Paul.<br />Remember my chains.<br />Grace be with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR include Amen.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
