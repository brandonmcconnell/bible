## Temple Construction Begins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> In the four hundred and eightieth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX four hundred and fortieth">a</a></sup>year after the Israelites had come out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ziv was the second month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of April and May; also in verse 37.">b</a></sup>the second month, he began to build the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> The house that King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The house was approximately 90 feet long, 30 feet wide, and 45 feet high (27.4 meters long, 9.1 meters wide, and 13.7 meters high).">c</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

## The Chambers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> Against the walls of the temple and the inner sanctuary, Solomon built a chambered structure around the temple, in which he constructed the side rooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The bottom floor was five cubits wide,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5 cubits is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters; also in verses 10 and 24.">f</a></sup>the middle floor six cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6 cubits is approximately 9 feet or 2.7 meters.">g</a></sup>and the third floor seven cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="7 cubits is approximately 10.5 feet or 3.2 meters.">h</a></sup>He also placed offset ledges around the outside of the temple, so that nothing would be inserted into its walls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> The temple was constructed using finished stones cut at the quarry, so that no hammer or chisel or any other iron tool was heard in the temple while it was being built.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The entrance to the bottom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="LXX and Targum; Hebrew middle">i</a></sup>floor was on the south side of the temple. A stairway led up to the middle level, and from there to the third floor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So Solomon built the temple and finished it, roofing it with beams and planks of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He built chambers all along the temple, each five cubits high and attached to the temple with beams of cedar.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Promise to Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the word of the LORD came to Solomon, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “As for this temple you are building, if you walk in My statutes, carry out My ordinances, and keep all My commandments by walking in them, I will fulfill through you the promise I made to your father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> And I will dwell among the Israelites and will not abandon My people Israel.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Temple’s Interior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So Solomon built the temple and finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> He lined the interior walls with cedar paneling from the floor of the temple to the ceiling, and he covered the floor with cypress<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or pine or juniper or fir; also in verse 34">j</a></sup>boards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> He partitioned off the twenty cubits at the rear of the temple with cedar boards from floor to ceiling to form within the temple an inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or the Holy of Holies">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the main hall in front of this room was forty cubits long.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="40 cubits is approximately 60 feet or 18.3 meters.">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> The cedar paneling inside the temple was carved with gourds and open flowers. Everything was cedar; not a stone could be seen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon also prepared the inner sanctuary within the temple to set the ark of the covenant of the LORD there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty cubits high. He overlaid the inside with pure gold, and he also overlaid the altar of cedar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or with cedar">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> So Solomon overlaid the inside of the temple with pure gold, and he extended gold chains<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or made gold chains to draw (the curtains)">n</a></sup>across the front of the inner sanctuary, which was overlaid with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> So he overlaid with gold the whole interior of the temple, until everything was completely finished. He also overlaid with gold the entire altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cherubim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim, each ten cubits high, out of olive wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long, and the other wing was five cubits long as well. So the full wingspan was ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The second cherub also measured ten cubits; both cherubim had the same size and shape,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the height of each cherub was ten cubits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> And he placed the cherubim inside the innermost room of the temple. Since their wings were spread out, the wing of the first cherub touched one wall, while the wing of the second cherub touched the other wall, and in the middle of the room their wingtips touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He also overlaid the cherubim with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> Then he carved the walls all around the temple, in both the inner and outer sanctuaries, with carved engravings of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> And he overlaid the temple floor with gold in both the inner and outer sanctuaries.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Doors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> For the entrance to the inner sanctuary, Solomon constructed doors of olive wood with five-sided doorposts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> The double doors were made of olive wood, and he carved into them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers and overlaid the cherubim and palm trees with hammered gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> In the same way he made four-sided doorposts of olive wood for the sanctuary entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> The two doors were made of cypress wood, and each had two folding panels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He carved into them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and he overlaid them with gold hammered evenly over the carvings.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> Solomon built the inner courtyard with three rows of dressed stone and one row of trimmed cedar beams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> The foundation of the house of the LORD was laid in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign, in the month of Ziv.</span>
