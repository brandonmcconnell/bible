## The Woman and the Dragon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 21:25; Ephesians 5:32; Luke 21:11"> And a great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed in the sun, with the moon under her feet and a crown of twelve stars on her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:21; Galatians 4:19; Revelation 12:4"> She was pregnant and crying out in the pain and agony of giving birth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Revelation 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> Then another sign appeared in heaven: a huge red dragon with seven heads, ten horns, and seven royal crowns on his heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:9–12; Revelation 12:9; John 8:44"> His tail swept a third of the stars from the sky, hurling them to the earth. And the dragon stood before the woman who was about to give birth, ready to devour her child as soon as she gave birth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:2"> And she gave birth to a son, a male child, who will rule all the nations with an iron scepter.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Psalms 2:9 (see also LXX).">a</a></sup>And her child was caught up to God and to His throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:5; Revelation 11:2–3; Revelation 12:14"> And the woman fled into the wilderness, where God had prepared a place for her to be nourished for 1,260 days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The War in Heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> Then a war broke out in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon, and the dragon and his angels fought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> But the dragon was not strong enough, and no longer was any place found in heaven for him and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> And the great dragon was hurled down—that ancient serpent called the devil and Satan, the deceiver of the whole world. He was hurled to the earth, and his angels with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> And I heard a loud voice in heaven saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Now have come the salvation and the power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the kingdom of our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the authority of His Christ.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the accuser of our brothers has been thrown down—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he who accuses them day and night before our God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and by the word of their testimony.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they did not love their lives</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so as to shy away from death.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalms 96:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore rejoice, O heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you who dwell in them!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But woe to the earth and the sea;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with great fury the devil has come down to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">knowing he has only a short time.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Woman Persecuted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> And when the dragon saw that he had been thrown to the earth, he pursued the woman who had given birth to the male child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> But the woman was given two wings of a great eagle to fly from the presence of the serpent to her place in the wilderness, where she was nourished for a time, and times, and half a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; Psalms 18:4"> Then from his mouth the serpent spewed water like a river to overtake the woman and sweep her away in the torrent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> But the earth helped the woman and opened its mouth to swallow up the river that the dragon had poured from his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> And the dragon was enraged at the woman and went to make war with the rest of her children, who keep the commandments of God and hold to the testimony of Jesus.<br />And the dragon stood on the shore of the sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally And he stood on the sand of the sea. BYZ and TR And I stood on the sand of the sea. Some texts number this sentence as verse 18; others include it with Revelation 13:1.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
