## The Marriage of the Lamb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:1; Revelation 14:2; Psalms 93:1"> And I heard a sound like the roar of a great multitude, like the rushing of many waters, and like a mighty rumbling of thunder, crying out:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hallelujah!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord our God</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SBL, BYZ, and TR the Lord God">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Almighty reigns.</span></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:6; Matthew 22:2–4; Luke 14:15–16"> Then the angel told me to write, “Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.” And he said to me, “These are the true words of God.”</span>
</div>

## The Rider on the White Horse

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Isaiah 11:3–5; Psalms 98:9"> Then I saw heaven standing open, and there before me was a white horse. And its rider is called Faithful and True. With righteousness He judges and wages war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> He has eyes like blazing fire, and many royal crowns on His head. He has a name written on Him that only He Himself knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> He is dressed in a robe dipped in blood,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="WH sprinkled with blood">c</a></sup>and His name is The Word of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> The armies of heaven, dressed in fine linen, white and pure, follow Him on white horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> And from His mouth proceeds a sharp sword with which to strike down the nations, and He will rule them with an iron scepter.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 2:9 (see also LXX).">d</a></sup>He treads the winepress of the fury of the wrath of God the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> And He has a name written on His robe and on His thigh:<br />KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Defeat of the Beast and False Prophet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:21; Revelation 8:13; Ezekiel 39:17–20"> Then I saw an angel standing in the sun, and he cried out in a loud voice to all the birds flying overhead, “Come, gather together for the great supper of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:18–20; Revelation 6:15; 1 Samuel 17:44"> so that you may eat the flesh of kings and commanders and mighty men, of horses and riders, of everyone slave and free, small and great.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:16; Revelation 16:14; Revelation 17:12–14"> Then I saw the beast and the kings of the earth with their armies assembled to wage war against the One seated on the horse, and against His army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:10; Revelation 21:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–11"> But the beast was captured along with the false prophet, who on its behalf had performed signs deceiving those who had the mark of the beast and worshiped its image. Both the beast and the false prophet were thrown alive into the fiery lake of burning sulfur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–15; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 19:17–19"> And the rest were killed with the sword that proceeded from the mouth of the One seated on the horse.<br />And all the birds gorged themselves on their flesh.</span>
</div>
