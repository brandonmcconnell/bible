## To the Church in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:20; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 1:11–13"> “To the angel of the church in Ephesus write:<br />These are the words of Him who holds the seven stars in His right hand and walks among the seven golden lampstands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21"> I know your deeds, your labor, and your perseverance. I know that you cannot tolerate those who are evil, and you have tested and exposed as liars those who falsely claim to be apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 6:9; James 5:7–11"> Without growing weary, you have persevered and endured many things for the sake of My name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 24:12–13; Jeremiah 2:2–5; Hebrews 6:10–11"> But I have this against you: You have abandoned your first love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:2–3; Revelation 3:19; Revelation 2:16"> Therefore, keep in mind how far you have fallen. Repent and perform the deeds you did at first. But if you do not repent, I will come to you and remove your lampstand from its place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 101:3; 2 John 1:9–10; Psalms 26:5"> But you have this to your credit: You hate the works of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, I will grant the right to eat from the tree of life in the Paradise of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the Church in Smyrna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Revelation 1:8; Revelation 1:11"> To the angel of the church in Smyrna write:<br />These are the words of the First and the Last, who died and returned to life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; 2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:5–6"> I know your affliction and your poverty—though you are rich! And I am aware of the slander of those who falsely claim to be Jews, but are in fact a synagogue of Satan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Matthew 10:22; Revelation 3:10–11"> Do not fear what you are about to suffer. Behold, the devil is about to throw some of you into prison to test you, and you will suffer tribulation for ten days. Be faithful even unto death, and I will give you the crown of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; Revelation 20:14; Revelation 21:8"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who overcomes will not be harmed by the second death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the Church in Pergamum

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:16; Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16"> To the angel of the church in Pergamum write:<br />These are the words of the One who holds the sharp, double-edged sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:9–10; 1 Timothy 5:8; Revelation 3:8–9"> I know where you live, where the throne of Satan sits, yet you hold fast to My name. You did not deny your faith in Me, even in the days of My faithful witness Antipas, who was killed among you where Satan dwells.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 31:16; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:20"> But I have a few things against you, because some of you hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to place a stumbling block before the Israelites so they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:6"> In the same way, some of you also hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:5"> Therefore repent! Otherwise I will come to you shortly and wage war against them with the sword of My mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Isaiah 65:15; Revelation 19:12–13"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, I will give the hidden manna. I will also give him a white stone inscribed with a new name, known only to the one who receives it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the Church in Thyatira

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 1:14–15; Revelation 1:11; John 3:35–36"> To the angel of the church in Thyatira write:<br />These are the words of the Son of God, whose eyes are like a blazing fire and whose feet are like polished bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Peter 1:7; John 15:2"> I know your deeds—your love, your faith, your service, your perseverance—and your latest deeds are greater than your first.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Corinthians 10:18–21"> But I have this against you: You tolerate that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess. By her teaching she misleads My servants to be sexually immoral and to eat food sacrificed to idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Romans 2:4–5; Revelation 9:20–21"> Even though I have given her time to repent of her immorality, she is unwilling.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 17:2; Luke 13:5; Ezekiel 16:37–41"> Behold, I will cast her onto a bed of sickness, and those who commit adultery with her will suffer great tribulation unless they repent of her deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Romans 8:27; Psalms 7:9"> Then I will strike her children dead, and all the churches will know that I am the One who searches minds and hearts, and I will repay each of you according to your deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Revelation 12:9; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> But I say to the rest of you in Thyatira, who do not hold to her teaching and have not learned the so-called deep things of Satan: I will place no further burden upon you</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 3:11; Revelation 3:3; Hebrews 10:23"> than to hold fast to what you have until I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 3:21; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 2:7"> And to the one who overcomes and continues in My work until the end, I will give authority over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 2:8–9; Revelation 12:5; Revelation 19:15"> He will rule them with an iron scepter and shatter them like pottery<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 2:9 (see also LXX)">a</a></sup>—just as I have received authority from My Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; 2 Peter 1:19; Luke 1:78–79"> And I will give him the morning star.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.</span>
</div>
</div>
