## The Lamb Takes the Scroll

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Isaiah 29:11; Revelation 5:7"> Then I saw a scroll in the right hand of the One seated on the throne. It had writing on both sides and was sealed with seven seals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> And I saw a mighty angel proclaiming in a loud voice, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> But no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or look inside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> And I began to weep bitterly, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look inside it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> Then one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep! Behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has triumphed to open the scroll and its seven seals.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> Then I saw a Lamb who appeared to have been slain, standing in the center of the throne, encircled by the four living creatures and the elders. The Lamb had seven horns and seven eyes, which represent the seven spirits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the sevenfold Spirit">a</a></sup>of God sent out into all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> And He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the One seated on the throne.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> When He had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each one had a harp, and they were holding golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> And they sang a new song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Worthy are You to take the scroll and open its seals,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because You were slain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and by Your blood You purchased for God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those from every tribe and tongue and people and nation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made them to be a kingdom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and priests to serve our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will reign upon</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or they will reign over; WH they reign upon">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lamb Exalted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> Then I looked, and I heard the voices of many angels encircling the throne, and the living creatures and the elders. And their number was myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> In a loud voice they were saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Worthy is the Lamb who was slain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to receive power and riches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and wisdom and strength</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and honor and glory and blessing!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalms 148:2–13"> And I heard every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea, and all that is in them, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“To Him who sits on the throne</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to the Lamb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">be praise and honor and glory and power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">forever and ever!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> And the four living creatures said, “Amen,” and the elders fell down and worshiped.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR includes Him who lives for ever and ever.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
