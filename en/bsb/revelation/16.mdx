## The First Six Bowls of Wrath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Jeremiah 10:25; Revelation 15:1"> Then I heard a loud voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, “Go, pour out on the earth the seven bowls of God’s wrath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:7; Revelation 16:11; Revelation 13:15–18"> So the first angel went and poured out his bowl on the earth, and loathsome, malignant sores broke out on those who had the mark of the beast and worshiped its image.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–21; Revelation 8:8–9; Revelation 11:6"> And the second angel poured out his bowl into the sea, and it turned to blood like that of the dead, and every living thing in the sea died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–20; Revelation 8:10–11; Hosea 13:15"> And the third angel poured out his bowl into the rivers and springs of water, and they turned to blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 11:17; Revelation 1:4"> And I heard the angel of the waters say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Righteous are You, O Holy One,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who is and was,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because You have brought these judgments.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Revelation 11:18; Revelation 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have spilled the blood of saints and prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and You have given them blood to drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as they deserve.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 6:9; Revelation 15:3–4"> And I heard the altar reply:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yes, Lord God Almighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">true and just are Your judgments.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:20; Revelation 14:18; Jonah 4:8"> Then the fourth angel poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was given power to scorch the people with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Revelation 16:21; Revelation 11:13"> And the people were scorched by intense heat, and they cursed the name of God, who had authority over these plagues. Yet they did not repent and give Him glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:2; Matthew 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> And the fifth angel poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast, and its kingdom was plunged into darkness, and men began to gnaw their tongues in anguish</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:2; Revelation 16:21"> and curse the God of heaven for their pains and sores. Yet they did not repent of their deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:14; Jeremiah 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> And the sixth angel poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates, and its water was dried up to prepare the way for the kings of the East.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 20:10"> And I saw three unclean spirits that looked like frogs coming out of the mouths of the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 4:1; Revelation 20:8"> These are demonic spirits that perform signs and go out to all the kings of the earth, to assemble them for battle on the great day of God the Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Matthew 24:42–43; 2 Peter 3:10"> “Behold, I am coming like a thief. Blessed is the one who remains awake and clothed, so that he will not go naked and let his shame be exposed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; Judges 5:19; Joel 3:9–14"> And they assembled the kings in the place that in Hebrew is called Armageddon.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Seventh Bowl of Wrath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:30; Revelation 21:6; Ephesians 6:12"> Then the seventh angel poured out his bowl into the air, and a loud voice came from the throne in the temple, saying, “It is done!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And there were flashes of lightning, rumblings, peals of thunder, and a great earthquake the likes of which had not occurred since men were upon the earth—so mighty was the great quake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:5; Revelation 17:18; Revelation 18:21"> The great city was split into three parts, and the cities of the nations collapsed. And God remembered Babylon the great and gave her the cup of the wine of the fury of His wrath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Revelation 20:11; Jeremiah 4:23–25"> Then every island fled, and no mountain could be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:9"> And great hailstones weighing almost a hundred pounds each<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek great hail as of a talent; that is, hailstones weighing approximately 75.4 pounds or 34.2 kilograms each">a</a></sup>rained down on them from above. And men cursed God for the plague of hail, because it was so horrendous.</span>
</div>
</div>
