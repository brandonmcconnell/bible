## Lament over Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Revelation 18:3; Revelation 17:2"> Then the kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her will weep and wail at the sight of the smoke rising from the fire that consumes her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally when they see the smoke of her burning; also in verse 18">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Revelation 18:8; Revelation 18:15–17"> In fear of her torment, they will stand at a distance and cry out:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Woe, woe to the great city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the mighty city of Babylon!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For in a single hour</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your judgment has come.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Revelation 18:23; Ezekiel 27:27–36"> And the merchants of the earth will weep and mourn over her, because there is no one left to buy their cargo—</span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Acts 19:24–27; Ezekiel 27:31"> The merchants who sold these things and gained their wealth from her will stand at a distance, in fear of her torment. They will weep and mourn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Luke 16:19–31; Revelation 18:10–11"> saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Woe, woe to the great city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">clothed in fine linen and purple and scarlet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:27–36; Revelation 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For in a single hour</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">such fabulous wealth has been destroyed!”</span><br />Every shipmaster, passenger, and sailor, and all who make their living from the sea, will stand at a distance</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Ezekiel 27:30–32; Revelation 18:9–10"> and cry out at the sight of the smoke rising from the fire that consumes her. “What city was ever like this great city?” they will exclaim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:10; Joshua 7:6; Ezekiel 27:30"> Then they will throw dust on their heads as they weep and mourn and cry out:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Woe, woe to the great city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where all who had ships on the sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">were enriched by her wealth!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For in a single hour</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she has been destroyed.”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Revelation 19:1–3; Luke 18:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rejoice over her, O heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you saints and apostles and prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because God has pronounced for you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His judgment against her.</span></span>
</div>
