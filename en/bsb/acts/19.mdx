## The Holy Spirit Received at Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:23–28; 1 Corinthians 16:12; 1 Corinthians 1:12"> While Apollos was at Corinth, Paul passed through the interior<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the highland">a</a></sup>and came to Ephesus. There he found some disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:39; Acts 11:15–17; Galatians 3:5"> and asked them, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you became believers?”<br />“No,” they answered, “we have not even heard that there is a Holy Spirit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 8:16"> “Into what, then, were you baptized?” Paul asked.<br />“The baptism of John,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:29–34; Acts 11:16; Acts 1:5"> Paul explained: “John’s baptism was a baptism of repentance. He told the people to believe in the One coming after him, that is, in Jesus.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:12; Acts 8:16; Romans 6:3–4"> On hearing this, they were baptized into the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> And when Paul laid his hands on them, the Holy Spirit came upon them, and they spoke in tongues and prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> There were about twelve men in all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Ministers in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> Then Paul went into the synagogue and spoke boldly there for three months, arguing persuasively about the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> But when some of them stubbornly refused to believe and publicly maligned the Way, Paul took his disciples and left the synagogue to conduct daily discussions in the lecture hall of Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> This continued for two years, so that everyone who lived in the province of Asia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally in Asia; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey; similarly in verses 22, 26, 27, and 31.">b</a></sup>Jews and Greeks alike, heard the word of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> God did extraordinary miracles through the hands of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> so that even handkerchiefs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek soudaria">c</a></sup>and aprons that had touched him were taken to the sick, and the diseases and evil spirits left them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Seven Sons of Sceva

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> Now there were some itinerant Jewish exorcists who tried to invoke the name of the Lord Jesus over those with evil spirits. They would say, “I command you by Jesus, whom Paul proclaims.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, were doing this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> But one day the evil spirit responded, “Jesus I know, and I know about Paul, but who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> Then the man with the evil spirit jumped on them and overpowered them all. The attack was so violent that they ran out of the house naked and wounded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> This became known to all the Jews and Greeks living in Ephesus, and fear came over all of them. So the name of the Lord Jesus was held in high honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalms 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> Many who had believed now came forward, confessing and disclosing their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> And a number of those who had practiced magic arts brought their books and burned them in front of everyone. When the value of the books was calculated, the total came to fifty thousand drachmas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or fifty thousand pieces of silver. A drachma was a silver coin worth about one day’s wages.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> So the word of the Lord powerfully continued to spread and prevail.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Riot in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> After these things had happened, Paul resolved in the Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or resolved in spirit">e</a></sup>to go to Jerusalem, passing through Macedonia and Achaia. “After I have been there,” he said, “I must see Rome as well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> He sent two of his helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia, while he stayed for a time in the province of Asia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> About that time there arose a great disturbance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally there arose no little disturbance">f</a></sup>about the Way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> It began with a silversmith named Demetrius who made silver shrines of Artemis, bringing much business<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally bringing no little business">g</a></sup>to the craftsmen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> Demetrius assembled the craftsmen, along with the workmen in related trades. “Men,” he said, “you know that this business is our source of prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> And you can see and hear that not only in Ephesus, but in nearly the whole province of Asia, this Paul has persuaded a great number of people to turn away. He says that man-made gods are no gods at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> There is danger not only that our business will fall into disrepute, but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis will be discredited and her majesty deposed—she who is worshiped by all the province of Asia and the whole world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> When the men heard this, they were enraged and began shouting, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> Soon the whole city was in disarray. They rushed together into the theatre, dragging with them Gaius and Aristarchus, Paul’s traveling companions from Macedonia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> Paul wanted to go before the assembly, but the disciples would not allow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> Even some of Paul’s friends who were officials of the province of Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or friends who were Asiarchs">h</a></sup>sent word to him, begging him not to venture into the theatre.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> Meanwhile the assembly was in turmoil. Some were shouting one thing and some another, and most of them did not even know why they were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> The Jews in the crowd pushed Alexander forward to explain himself, and he motioned for silence so he could make his defense to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> But when they realized that he was a Jew, they all shouted in unison for about two hours: “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> Finally the city clerk quieted the crowd and declared, “Men of Ephesus, doesn’t everyone know that the city of Ephesus is guardian of the temple of the great Artemis and of her image, which fell from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> Since these things are undeniable, you ought to be calm and not do anything rash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> For you have brought these men here, though they have neither robbed our temple nor blasphemed our goddess.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> So if Demetrius and his fellow craftsmen have a complaint against anyone, the courts are open and proconsuls are available. Let them bring charges against one another there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if you are seeking anything beyond this, it must be settled in a legal assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> For we are in jeopardy of being charged with rioting for today’s events, and we have no justification to account for this commotion.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> After he had said this, he dismissed the assembly.</span>
</div>
</div>
