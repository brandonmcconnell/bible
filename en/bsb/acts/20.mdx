## Paul in Macedonia and Greece

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5; Acts 19:21; 1 Timothy 1:3"> When the uproar had ended, Paul sent for the disciples. And after encouraging them, he said goodbye to them and left for Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> After traveling through that area and speaking many words of encouragement, he arrived in Greece,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> where he stayed three months. And when the Jews formed a plot against him as he was about to sail for Syria, he decided to go back through Macedonia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Paul was accompanied by Sopater son of Pyrrhus from Berea, Aristarchus and Secundus from Thessalonica, Gaius from Derbe, Timothy, and Tychicus and Trophimus from the province of Asia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally from Asia; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey; also in verses 16 and 18.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 4:13"> These men went on ahead and waited for us in Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:3; Acts 16:12; Exodus 23:15"> And after the Feast of Unleavened Bread,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally after the days of the Unleavened; see Exodus 12:14–20.">b</a></sup>we sailed from Philippi, and five days later we rejoined them in Troas, where we stayed seven days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Eutychus Revived at Troas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> On the first day of the week we came together to break bread. Since Paul was ready to leave the next day, he talked to them and kept on speaking until midnight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> Now there were many lamps in the upper room where we were gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> And a certain young man named Eutychus, seated in the window, was sinking into a deep sleep as Paul talked on and on. When he was sound asleep, he fell from the third story and was picked up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> But Paul went down, threw himself on the young man, and embraced him. “Do not be alarmed!” he said. “He is still alive!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> Then Paul went back upstairs, broke bread, and ate. And after speaking until daybreak, he departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> And the people were greatly relieved to take the boy home alive.</span>
</div>
</div>

## From Troas to Miletus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> We went on ahead to the ship and sailed to Assos, where we were to take Paul aboard. He had arranged this because he was going there on foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when he met us at Assos, we took him aboard and went on to Mitylene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 20:17"> Sailing on from there, we arrived the next day opposite Chios. The day after that we arrived at Samos, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR and, after remaining at Trogyllium,">c</a></sup>on the following day we came to Miletus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:8; Acts 2:1; Acts 19:21"> Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus to avoid spending time in the province of Asia, because he was in a hurry to reach Jerusalem, if possible, by the day of Pentecost.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Weeks (see Exodus 34:22).">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Farewell to the Ephesians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> From Miletus, Paul sent to Ephesus for the elders of the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> When they came to him, he said, “You know how I lived the whole time I was with you, from the first day I arrived in the province of Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> I served the Lord with great humility and with tears, especially in the trials that came upon me through the plots of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> I did not shrink back from declaring anything that was helpful to you as I taught you publicly and from house to house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> testifying to Jews and Greeks alike about repentance to God and faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="ECM, TR, and Tischendorf; SBL, NE, BYZ, and WH our Lord Jesus">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> And now, compelled by the Spirit, I am going to Jerusalem, not knowing what will happen to me there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> I only know that in town after town the Holy Spirit warns me that chains and afflictions await me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> But I consider my life of no value to me, if only I may finish my course and complete the ministry I have received from the Lord Jesus—the ministry of testifying to the good news of God’s grace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> Now I know that none of you among whom I have preached the kingdom will see my face again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> Therefore I testify to you this day that I am innocent of the blood of all men.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Ezekiel 33:8–9.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> For I did not shrink back from declaring to you the whole will of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> Keep watch over yourselves and the entire flock of which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers. Be shepherds of the church of God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Tischendorf of the Lord; BYZ and GOC of the Lord and God">g</a></sup>which He purchased with His own blood.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or with the blood of His own Son.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> I know that after my departure, savage wolves will come in among you and will not spare the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> Even from your own number, men will rise up and distort the truth to draw away disciples after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> Therefore be alert and remember that for three years I never stopped warning each of you night and day with tears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> And now I commit you to God and to the word of His grace, which can build you up and give you an inheritance among all who are sanctified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I have not coveted anyone’s silver or gold or clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> You yourselves know that these hands of mine have ministered to my own needs and those of my companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> In everything, I showed you that by this kind of hard work we must help the weak, remembering the words of the Lord Jesus Himself: ‘It is more blessed to give than to receive.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> When Paul had said this, he knelt down with all of them and prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> They all wept openly as they embraced Paul and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> They were especially grieved by his statement that they would never see his face again. Then they accompanied him to the ship.</span>
</div>
</div>
