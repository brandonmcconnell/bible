## Paul’s Testimony to Agrippa

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Proverbs 1:24; Acts 26:2"> Agrippa said to Paul, “You have permission to speak for yourself.”<br />Then Paul stretched out his hand and began his defense:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “King Agrippa, I consider myself fortunate to stand before you today to defend myself against all the accusations of the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:14; Acts 26:26; Acts 26:7"> especially since you are acquainted with all the Jewish customs and controversies. I beg you, therefore, to listen to me patiently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:13; 2 Timothy 3:10; Philippians 3:5"> Surely all the Jews know how I have lived from my earliest childhood among my own people, and also in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 22:3; Acts 24:5"> They have known me for a long time and can testify, if they are willing, that I lived as a Pharisee, adhering to the strictest sect of our religion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Acts 24:15; Acts 28:20"> And now I stand on trial because of my hope in the promise that God made to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:1; Philippians 3:11; 1 Timothy 5:5"> the promise our twelve tribes are hoping to see fulfilled as they earnestly serve God day and night. It is because of this hope, O king, that I am accused by the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12–20; Acts 4:2; Acts 17:31–32"> Why would any of you consider it incredible that God raises the dead?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Acts 22:8; Philippians 3:6"> So then, I too was convinced that I ought to do all I could to oppose the name of Jesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 8:3; Acts 9:13–14; Acts 9:21"> And that is what I did in Jerusalem. With authority from the chief priests I put many of the saints in prison, and when they were condemned to death, I cast my vote against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 22:19; Matthew 10:17; Acts 13:45"> I frequently had them punished in the synagogues, and I tried to make them blaspheme. In my raging fury against them, I even went to foreign cities to persecute them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:10; Acts 22:5–11; Acts 9:1–8"> In this pursuit I was on my way to Damascus with the authority and commission of the chief priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Revelation 21:23; Revelation 1:16"> About noon, O king, as I was on the road, I saw a light from heaven, brighter than the sun, shining around me and my companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 22:2; Acts 9:7"> We all fell to the ground, and I heard a voice say to me in Hebrew,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in Aramaic; literally in the Hebrew language">a</a></sup>‘Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me? It is hard for you to kick against the goads.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:20–21; Matthew 25:45; Exodus 16:8"> ‘Who are You, Lord?’ I asked.<br />‘I am Jesus, whom you are persecuting,’ the Lord replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 22:14–15; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 10:11"> ‘But get up and stand on your feet. For I have appeared to you to appoint you as a servant and as a witness of what you have seen from Me and what I will show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Jeremiah 1:19"> I will rescue you from your own people and from the Gentiles. I am sending you to them</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Ephesians 1:18; Ephesians 5:8"> to open their eyes, so that they may turn from darkness to light and from the power of Satan to God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance among those sanctified by faith in Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Galatians 1:16; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> So then, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Acts 3:19; Acts 2:38"> First to those in Damascus and Jerusalem, then to everyone in the region of Judea, and then to the Gentiles, I declared that they should repent and turn to God, performing deeds worthy of their repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 21:30–31; Acts 21:27; Acts 25:3"> For this reason the Jews seized me in the temple courts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally the temple">b</a></sup>and tried to kill me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:14; John 5:46; Romans 3:21"> But I have had God’s help to this day, and I stand here to testify to small and great alike. I am saying nothing beyond what the prophets and Moses said would happen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:26; Colossians 1:18"> that the Christ would suffer, and as the first to rise from the dead, would proclaim light to our people and to the Gentiles.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Festus Interrupts Paul’s Defense

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 4:10"> At this stage of Paul’s defense, Festus exclaimed in a loud voice, “You are insane, Paul! Your great learning is driving you to madness!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7–8"> But Paul answered, “I am not insane, most excellent Festus; I am speaking words of truth and sobriety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3; Acts 2:1–12; Isaiah 30:20"> For the king knows about these matters, and I can speak freely to him. I am confident that none of this has escaped his notice, because it was not done in a corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23"> King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know you do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Matthew 10:18; Acts 26:29"> Then Agrippa said to Paul, “Can you persuade me in such a short time to become a Christian?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 1:28; Romans 10:1"> “Short time or long,” Paul replied, “I wish to God that not only you but all who hear me this day may become what I am, except for these chains.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Acts 28:22; Acts 25:23"> Then the king and the governor rose, along with Bernice and those seated with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 23:9; Acts 28:18"> On their way out, they said to one another, “This man has done nothing worthy of death or imprisonment.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 28:18; Acts 25:11–12; Acts 25:25"> And Agrippa said to Festus, “This man could have been released if he had not appealed to Caesar.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
