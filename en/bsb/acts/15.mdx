## The Dispute over Circumcision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:5; Leviticus 12:3"> Then some men came down from Judea and were teaching the brothers, “Unless you are circumcised according to the custom of Moses, you cannot be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 15:22–23; Acts 15:6–7; Galatians 2:1–2"> And after engaging these men in sharp debate, Paul and Barnabas were appointed, along with some other believers, to go up to Jerusalem to see the apostles and elders about this question.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 21:5; 1 Corinthians 16:11"> Sent on their way by the church, they passed through Phoenicia and Samaria, recounting the conversion of the Gentiles and bringing great joy to all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:12; Acts 15:3"> On their arrival in Jerusalem, they were welcomed by the church and apostles and elders, to whom they reported all that God had done through them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Council at Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:1–3; Philippians 3:5–8; Acts 15:1"> But some believers from the party of the Pharisees stood up and declared, “The Gentiles must be circumcised and required to obey the law of Moses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 15:22; Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17"> So the apostles and elders met to look into this matter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Galatians 2:7–9; 1 Chronicles 28:4–5"> After much discussion, Peter got up and said to them, “Brothers, you know that in the early days God made a choice among you that the Gentiles would hear from my lips the message of the gospel and believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Acts 10:47; Acts 14:3"> And God, who knows the heart, showed His approval by giving the Holy Spirit to them, just as He did to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 5:6; Acts 10:28"> He made no distinction between us and them, for He cleansed their hearts by faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; Matthew 23:4; Galatians 4:9"> Now then, why do you test God by placing on the necks of the disciples a yoke that neither we nor our fathers have been able to bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Titus 3:4–7; Titus 2:11"> On the contrary, we believe it is through the grace of the Lord Jesus that we are saved, just as they are.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:4; Acts 21:19"> The whole assembly fell silent as they listened to Barnabas and Paul describing the signs and wonders God had done among the Gentiles through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Galatians 2:12; James 1:1"> When they had finished speaking, James declared, “Brothers, listen to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:1; Acts 15:7–9; Luke 2:31–32"> Simon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek Simeon, a variant of Simon">a</a></sup>has told us how God first visited the Gentiles to take from them a people to be His own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:8–12; Acts 13:47"> The words of the prophets agree with this, as it is written:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 9:11–12; Psalms 89:35–49; Luke 1:31–33"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘After this I will return and rebuild</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fallen tent of David.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its ruins I will rebuild,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will restore it,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 2:11; Zechariah 8:20–23; Isaiah 65:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">so that the remnant of men may seek the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the Gentiles who are called by My name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">says the Lord who does these things</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Ephesians 3:9; Isaiah 46:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-2">that have been known for ages.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Amos 9:11–12 (see also LXX); BYZ and TR says the Lord, who does all these things. 18 Known unto God are all His works from the ages.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 15:28; Galatians 1:7–10; Acts 15:24"> It is my judgment, therefore, that we should not cause trouble for the Gentiles who are turning to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Revelation 2:20; Leviticus 3:17"> Instead, we should write and tell them to abstain from food polluted by idols, from sexual immorality, from the meat of strangled animals, and from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:15; Acts 13:27; 2 Corinthians 3:14–15"> For Moses has been proclaimed in every city from ancient times and is read in the synagogues on every Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Letter to the Gentile Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:27; Acts 15:40; 1 Thessalonians 1:1"> Then the apostles and elders, with the whole church, decided to select men from among them to send to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas. They chose Judas called Barsabbas and Silas, two leaders among the brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:26; James 1:1; Galatians 1:21"> and sent them with this letter:<br />The apostles and the elders, your brothers,<br />To the brothers among the Gentiles in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia:<br />Greetings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; Galatians 5:10; Acts 15:1"> It has come to our attention that some went out from us without our authorization and unsettled you, troubling your minds by what they said.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR by saying that you must be circumcised and keep the law.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Peter 3:15; Acts 1:14; Acts 15:2"> So we all agreed to choose men to send to you along with our beloved Barnabas and Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 9:23–25; Philippians 2:29–30"> men who have risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 15:32; 3 John 1:13"> Therefore we are sending Judas and Silas to tell you in person the same things we are writing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:13; Revelation 2:24; Matthew 11:30"> It seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us not to burden you with anything beyond these essential requirements:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:20; Acts 21:25; Revelation 2:14"> You must abstain from food sacrificed to idols, from blood, from the meat of strangled animals, and from sexual immorality. You will do well to avoid these things.<br />Farewell.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Believers at Antioch Rejoice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 16:4; Acts 6:2; Acts 23:33"> So the men were sent off and went down to Antioch, where they assembled the congregation and delivered the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:4–5; Philippians 3:3; Galatians 5:1"> When the people read it, they rejoiced at its encouraging message.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:1"> Judas and Silas, who themselves were prophets, said much to encourage and strengthen the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 16:36; Hebrews 11:31"> After spending some time there, they were sent off by the brothers in peace to return to those who had sent them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR includes 34 Silas, however, decided to remain there.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:1"> But Paul and Barnabas remained at Antioch, along with many others, teaching and preaching the word of the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Second Missionary Journey Begins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 14:6; Acts 13:4; Acts 13:51"> Some time later Paul said to Barnabas, “Let us go back and visit the brothers in every town where we proclaimed the word of the Lord, to see how they are doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 13:5"> Barnabas wanted to take John, also called Mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:13; Proverbs 25:19; Luke 14:27–34"> But Paul thought it best not to take him, because he had deserted them in Pamphylia and had not accompanied them in the work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Colossians 4:10; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Acts 4:36"> Their disagreement was so sharp that they parted company. Barnabas took Mark and sailed for Cyprus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 14:26; Acts 11:23"> but Paul chose Silas and left, commended by the brothers to the grace of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Galatians 1:21; Acts 15:32"> And he traveled through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches.</span>
</div>
</div>
