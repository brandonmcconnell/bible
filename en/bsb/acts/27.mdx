## Paul Sails for Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 25:25; Acts 25:12; Acts 10:1"> When it was decided that we would sail for Italy, Paul and some other prisoners were handed over to a centurion named Julius, who belonged to the Imperial<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Augustan">a</a></sup>Regiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Philemon 1:24; Colossians 4:10"> We boarded an Adramyttian ship about to sail for ports along the coast of Asia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally sail to the places along Asia; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.">b</a></sup>and we put out to sea. Aristarchus, a Macedonian from Thessalonica, was with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Acts 28:16; Acts 27:43"> The next day we landed at Sidon, and Julius treated Paul with consideration, allowing him to visit his friends and receive their care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:39; Acts 13:4"> After putting out from there, we sailed to the lee of Cyprus because the winds were against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:9; Acts 13:13; Acts 15:41"> And when we had sailed across the open sea off the coast of Cilicia and Pamphylia, we came to Myra in Lycia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 28:11; Acts 27:1; Acts 6:9"> There the centurion found an Alexandrian ship sailing for Italy and put us on board.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 27:21; Titus 1:5; Titus 1:12"> After sailing slowly for many days, we arrived off Cnidus. When the wind impeded us, we sailed to the lee of Crete, opposite Salmone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> After we had moved along the coast with difficulty, we came to a place called Fair Havens, near the town of Lasea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 29:7; Leviticus 23:27–29; Leviticus 16:29–31"> By now much time had passed, and the voyage had already become dangerous because it was after the Fast.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement">c</a></sup>So Paul advised them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 2:30; Acts 27:20–26; Acts 27:41–44"> “Men, I can see that our voyage will be filled with disaster and great loss, not only to ship and cargo, but to our own lives as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:4; Hebrews 11:7; Ezekiel 3:17–18"> But contrary to Paul’s advice, the centurion was persuaded by the pilot and by the owner of the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:30; Acts 27:8"> Since the harbor was unsuitable to winter in, the majority decided to sail on, hoping that somehow they could reach Phoenix to winter there. Phoenix was a harbor in Crete facing both southwest and northwest.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Storm at Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:55; Song 4:16; Job 37:17"> When a gentle south wind began to blow, they thought they had their opportunity. So they weighed anchor and sailed along, hugging the coast of Crete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 4:37; Psalms 107:25–27; Exodus 14:21–27"> But it was not long before a cyclone called the Northeaster swept down across the island.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:4; Acts 27:27"> Unable to head into the wind, the ship was caught up. So we gave way and let ourselves be driven along.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Passing to the lee of a small island called Cauda,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="NE, BYZ, and TR Clauda">d</a></sup>we barely managed to secure the lifeboat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 27:29; Acts 27:26; Acts 27:41"> After hoisting it up, the crew used ropes to undergird the ship. And fearing that they would run aground on the sandbars of Syrtis, they lowered the sea anchor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or the sails">e</a></sup>and were driven along.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jonah 1:5; Acts 27:38; Matthew 16:26"> We were tossed so violently that the next day the men began to jettison the cargo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:4; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> On the third day, they threw the ship’s tackle overboard with their own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Matthew 8:24–25; Jeremiah 2:25"> When neither sun nor stars appeared for many days and the great storm continued to batter us, we abandoned all hope of being saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Psalms 107:5–6; Acts 27:7"> After the men had gone a long time without food, Paul stood up among them and said, “Men, you should have followed my advice not to sail from Crete. Then you would have averted this disaster and loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 27:25; Acts 27:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> But now I urge you to keep up your courage, because you will not experience any loss of life, but only of the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 4:17"> For just last night an angel of the God to whom I belong and whom I serve stood beside me</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 23:11; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Matthew 10:28"> and said, ‘Do not be afraid, Paul; you must stand before Caesar. And look, God has granted you the lives of all who sail with you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Luke 1:45; Numbers 23:19"> So take courage, men, for I believe God that it will happen just as He told me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 28:1; Acts 27:17; Acts 27:29"> However, we must run aground on some island.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Shipwreck

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Acts 27:30; Revelation 18:17"> On the fourteenth night we were still being driven across the Adriatic Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Adriatic Sea referred to an area extending well south of Italy to include the central portion of the Mediterranean Sea.">f</a></sup>About midnight the sailors sensed they were approaching land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> They took soundings and found that the water was twenty fathoms deep.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="20 fathoms is approximately 120 feet or 36.6 meters.">g</a></sup>Going a little farther, they took another set of soundings that read fifteen fathoms.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15 fathoms is approximately 90 feet or 27.4 meters.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 27:17; Hebrews 6:19; Deuteronomy 28:67"> Fearing that we would run aground on the rocks, they dropped four anchors from the stern and prayed for daybreak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 27:16; Acts 27:32"> Meanwhile, the sailors attempted to escape from the ship. Pretending to lower anchors from the bow, they let the lifeboat down into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13–14; Psalms 91:11–12; Jeremiah 29:11–13"> But Paul said to the centurion and the soldiers, “Unless these men remain with the ship, you cannot be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Luke 16:8"> So the soldiers cut the ropes to the lifeboat and set it adrift.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Right up to daybreak, Paul kept urging them all to eat: “Today is your fourteenth day in constant suspense, without taking any food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Kings 1:52; Luke 21:18"> So for your own preservation, I urge you to eat something, because not a single hair of your head will be lost.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 15:36; 1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> After he had said this, Paul took bread and gave thanks to God in front of them all. Then he broke it and began to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4–6; Acts 27:22; Psalms 27:14"> They were all encouraged and took some food themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Romans 13:1; 1 Peter 3:20"> In all, there were 276<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="WH 76">i</a></sup>of us on board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jonah 1:5; Matthew 6:25; Acts 27:18–19"> After the men had eaten their fill, they lightened the ship by throwing the grain into the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 28:1"> When daylight came, they did not recognize the land, but they sighted a bay with a sandy beach, where they decided to run the ship aground if they could.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Acts 27:29–30"> Cutting away the anchors, they left them in the sea as they loosened the ropes that held the rudders. Then they hoisted the foresail to the wind and made for the beach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:25–26; Ezekiel 27:26; Acts 27:26–29"> But the ship struck a sandbar and ran aground. The bow stuck fast and would not move, and the stern was being broken up by the pounding of the waves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 15:15–20; Ecclesiastes 9:3; Psalms 74:20"> The soldiers planned to kill the prisoners so none of them could swim to freedom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7; Acts 27:11"> But the centurion, wanting to spare Paul’s life, thwarted their plan. He commanded those who could swim to jump overboard first and get to land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 27:22; Psalms 107:28–30; John 6:39–40"> The rest were to follow on planks and various parts of the ship. In this way everyone was brought safely to land.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
