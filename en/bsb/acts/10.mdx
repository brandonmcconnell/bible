## Cornelius Sends for Peter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:1; Matthew 8:5–13; Matthew 27:27"> At Caesarea there was a man named Cornelius, a centurion in what was called the Italian Regiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Acts 10:4; Acts 10:22"> He and all his household were devout and God-fearing. He gave generously to the people and prayed to God regularly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:20–21; Acts 3:1; Acts 5:19"> One day at about the ninth hour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, about three in the afternoon; also in verse 30">a</a></sup>he had a clear vision of an angel of God who came to him and said, “Cornelius!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 10:31"> Cornelius stared at him in fear and asked, “What is it, Lord?”<br />The angel answered, “Your prayers and gifts to the poor have ascended as a memorial offering before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:9; Acts 9:38; Acts 15:7"> Now send men to Joppa to call for a man named Simon who is called Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:43; Acts 11:13–14; Romans 10:14–17"> He is staying with Simon the tanner, whose house is by the sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR includes He will tell you what you need to do.">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philemon 1:16; 1 Samuel 14:6–7; Matthew 8:9–10"> When the angel who spoke to him had gone, Cornelius called two of his servants and a devout soldier from among his attendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Psalms 119:59–60; Acts 10:33"> He explained what had happened and sent them to Joppa.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:5; Psalms 55:17; 1 Samuel 9:25"> The next day at about the sixth hour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, about noon">c</a></sup>as the men were approaching the city on their journey, Peter went up on the roof to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Revelation 4:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4"> He became hungry and wanted something to eat, but while the meal was being prepared, he fell into a trance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; John 1:51; Romans 16:25–26"> He saw heaven open and something like a large sheet being let down to earth by its four corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; John 7:37; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> It contained all kinds of four-footed animals and reptiles of the earth, as well as birds of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:31–34; Jeremiah 35:2–5; Acts 10:10"> Then a voice said to him: “Get up, Peter, kill and eat!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Ezekiel 4:14; Daniel 1:8"> “No, Lord!” Peter answered. “I have never eaten anything impure<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally common; similarly in verses 15 and 28">d</a></sup>or unclean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Matthew 15:11; Titus 1:15"> The voice spoke to him a second time: “Do not call anything impure that God has made clean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:32; John 21:17; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> This happened three times, and all at once the sheet was taken back up into heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter Called to Caesarea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:12; Acts 10:7–19; Acts 5:24"> While Peter was puzzling over the meaning of the vision, the men sent by Cornelius found Simon’s house and approached the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 10:5–6; Acts 11:11"> They called out to ask if Simon called Peter was staying there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Acts 11:12; Acts 21:4"> As Peter continued to reflect on the vision, the Spirit said to him, “Behold, three men are looking for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="ECM and TR; NE and WH two men are looking for you; SBL, BYZ, and Tischendorf men are looking for you">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:7–9; Zechariah 2:9–11; Isaiah 48:16"> So get up! Go downstairs and accompany them without hesitation, because I have sent them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Mark 10:51; Acts 10:29"> So Peter went down to the men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="TR the men sent to him by Cornelius">f</a></sup>and said, “Here am I, the one you are looking for. Why have you come?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Acts 10:2; Acts 22:12"> “Cornelius the centurion has sent us,” they said. “He is a righteous and God-fearing man with a good reputation among the whole Jewish nation. A holy angel instructed him to request your presence in his home so he could hear a message from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 11:12; 1 Peter 4:9"> So Peter invited them in as his guests. And the next day he got ready and went with them, accompanied by some of the brothers from Joppa.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter Visits Cornelius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Zechariah 8:20–23; Zechariah 3:10"> The following day he arrived in Caesarea, where Cornelius was expecting them and had called together his relatives and close friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Daniel 2:46"> As Peter was about to enter, Cornelius met him and fell at his feet to worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 14:14–15"> But Peter helped him up. “Stand up,” he said, “I am only a man myself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; John 4:35; Acts 10:24"> As Peter talked with him, he went inside and found many people gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:9; Ephesians 3:6–7; Galatians 2:12–14"> He said to them, “You know how unlawful it is for a Jew to associate with a foreigner or visit him. But God has shown me that I should not call any man impure or unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:19–20"> So when I was invited, I came without objection. I ask, then, why have you sent for me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 10:3; Daniel 9:20–21; Nehemiah 9:1–3"> Cornelius answered: “Four days ago I was in my house praying at this, the ninth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="BYZ and TR Four days ago I was fasting until this hour, and at the ninth hour I was praying in my house.">g</a></sup>Suddenly a man in radiant clothing stood before me</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:4; Daniel 9:23; Luke 1:13"> and said, ‘Cornelius, your prayer has been heard, and your gifts to the poor have been remembered before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Therefore send to Joppa for Simon, who is called Peter. He is a guest in the home of Simon the tanner, by the sea.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 1:19; Galatians 4:14; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> So I sent for you immediately, and you were kind enough to come. Now then, we are all here in the presence of God to listen to everything the Lord has instructed you to tell us.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Good News for the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 2:11; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Deuteronomy 10:17"> Then Peter began to speak: “I now truly understand that God does not show favoritism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:29; Acts 10:2; Romans 10:12–13"> but welcomes those from every nation who fear Him and do what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Acts 2:36; Revelation 17:14"> He has sent this message to the people of Israel, proclaiming the gospel of peace through Jesus Christ, who is Lord of all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 4:14; Acts 2:22; Acts 28:22"> You yourselves know what has happened throughout Judea, beginning in Galilee with the baptism that John proclaimed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:18; Acts 2:22; Isaiah 61:1–3"> how God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, and how Jesus went around doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, because God was with Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:8; Acts 10:41"> We are witnesses of all that He did, both in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem. And although they put Him to death by hanging Him on a tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30–31; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> God raised Him up on the third day and caused Him to be seen—</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 14:22; Acts 10:39; John 21:13"> not by all the people, but by the witnesses God had chosen beforehand, by us who ate and drank with Him after He rose from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Peter 4:5"> And He commanded us to preach to the people and to testify that He is the One appointed by God to judge the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Acts 2:38; Isaiah 53:11"> All the prophets testify about Him that everyone who believes in Him receives forgiveness of sins through His name.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Gentiles Receive the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 11:15; Acts 19:6; Acts 15:8"> While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell upon all who heard his message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 10:23; Acts 2:38; Galatians 3:13–14"> All the circumcised believers who had accompanied Peter were astounded that the gift of the Holy Spirit had been poured out even on the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> For they heard them speaking in tongues and exalting God.<br />Then Peter said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 8:36; Romans 10:12; Acts 11:15–17"> “Can anyone withhold the water to baptize these people? They have received the Holy Spirit just as we have!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 8:16; Galatians 3:27"> So he ordered that they be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to stay for a few days.</span>
</div>
</div>
