## Peter and John before the Sanhedrin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> While Peter and John were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple guard and the Sadducees came up to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> greatly disturbed that they were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> They seized Peter and John, and because it was evening, they put them in custody until the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> But many who heard the message believed, and the number of men grew to about five thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> The next day the rulers, elders, and scribes assembled in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> along with Annas the high priest, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and many others from the high priest’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> They had Peter and John brought in and began to question them: “By what power or what name did you do this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, “Rulers and elders of the people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> If we are being examined today about a kind service to a man who was lame, to determine how he was healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> then let this be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel: It is by the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom you crucified but whom God raised from the dead, that this man stands before you healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> This Jesus is<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘the stone you builders rejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which has become the cornerstone.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 118:22">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> Salvation exists in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Name Forbidden

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> When they saw the boldness of Peter and John and realized that they were unschooled, ordinary men, they marveled and took note that these men had been with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> And seeing the man who had been healed standing there with them, they had nothing to say in response.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> So they ordered them to leave the Sanhedrin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the Council">b</a></sup>and then conferred together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> “What shall we do with these men?” they asked. “It is clear to everyone living in Jerusalem that a remarkable miracle has occurred through them, and we cannot deny it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But to keep this message from spreading any further among the people, we must warn them not to speak to anyone in this name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> Then they called them in again and commanded them not to speak or teach at all in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But Peter and John replied, “Judge for yourselves whether it is right in God’s sight to listen to you rather than God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> For we cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> After further threats they let them go. They could not find a way to punish them, because all the people were glorifying God for what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> For the man who was miraculously healed was over forty years old.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Believers’ Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalms 119:63"> On their release, Peter and John returned to their own people and reported everything that the chief priests and elders had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> When the believers heard this, they lifted up their voices to God with one accord. “Sovereign Lord,” they said, “You made the heaven and the earth and the sea and everything in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> You spoke by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of Your servant,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or child; also in verses 27 and 30">c</a></sup>our father David:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Why do the nations rage</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the peoples plot in vain?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of the earth take their stand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the rulers gather together</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against His Anointed One.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or His Christ or His Messiah; Psalms 2:1–2">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> In fact, this is the very city where Herod and Pontius Pilate conspired with the Gentiles and the people of Israel against Your holy servant Jesus, whom You anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> They carried out what Your hand and will had decided beforehand would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> And now, Lord, consider their threats, and enable Your servants to speak Your word with complete boldness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> as You stretch out Your hand to heal and perform signs and wonders through the name of Your holy servant Jesus.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> After they had prayed, their meeting place was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke the word of God boldly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sharing among Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> The multitude of believers was one in heart and soul. No one claimed that any of his possessions was his own, but they shared everything they owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> With great power the apostles continued to give their testimony about the resurrection of the Lord Jesus. And abundant grace was upon them all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> There were no needy ones among them, because those who owned lands or houses would sell their property, bring the proceeds from the sales,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and lay them at the apostles’ feet for distribution to anyone as he had need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> Joseph, a Levite from Cyprus, whom the apostles called Barnabas (meaning Son of Encouragement),</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> sold a field he owned, brought the money, and laid it at the apostles’ feet.</span>
</div>
</div>
