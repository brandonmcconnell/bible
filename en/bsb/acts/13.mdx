## Paul’s First Missionary Journey Begins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:35; Acts 11:22–27"> Now in the church at Antioch there were prophets and teachers: Barnabas, Simeon called Niger, Lucius of Cyrene, Manaen (who had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch), and Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> While they were worshiping the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, “Set apart for Me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 2:2; Acts 13:2"> And after they had fasted and prayed, they laid their hands on them and sent them off.</span>
</div>

## On Cyprus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 13:2; Acts 11:19"> So Barnabas and Saul, sent forth by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia and sailed from there to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Acts 13:46; Acts 14:1"> When they arrived at Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the Jewish synagogues. And John was with them as their helper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 1 John 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> They traveled through the whole island as far as Paphos, where they found a Jewish sorcerer and false prophet named Bar-Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:38; Acts 13:12; Acts 18:12"> an attendant of the proconsul, Sergius Paulus. The proconsul, a man of intelligence, summoned Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Acts 13:6–7; 2 Timothy 4:14–15"> But Elymas the sorcerer (for that is what his name means) opposed them and tried to turn the proconsul from the faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 3:8; Acts 4:8; Acts 2:4"> Then Saul, who was also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked directly at Elymas</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 13:38"> and said, “O child of the devil and enemy of all righteousness, you are full of all kinds of deceit and trickery! Will you never stop perverting the straight ways of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 9:3; Psalms 32:4; Hebrews 10:31"> Now look, the hand of the Lord is against you, and for a time you will be blind and unable to see the light of the sun.” Immediately mist and darkness came over him, and he groped about, seeking someone to lead him by the hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Acts 13:7; Luke 4:22"> When the proconsul saw what had happened, he believed, for he was astonished at the teaching about the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## In Pisidian Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 15:38; Acts 2:10; Acts 14:24–25"> After setting sail from Paphos, Paul and his companions came to Perga in Pamphylia, where John left them to return to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:13; Acts 14:19; Acts 13:5"> And from Perga, they traveled inland to Pisidian Antioch, where they entered the synagogue on the Sabbath and sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 15:21; Mark 5:22"> After the reading from the Law and the Prophets, the synagogue leaders sent word to them: “Brothers, if you have a word of encouragement for the people, please speak.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 13:26; Acts 10:2"> Paul stood up, motioned with his hand, and began to speak: “Men of Israel and you Gentiles who fear God, listen to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalms 105:23–24"> The God of the people of Israel chose our fathers. He made them into a great people during their stay in Egypt, and with an uplifted arm He led them out of that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Psalms 95:8–11; Psalms 106:13–29"> He endured their conduct for about forty years in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Psalms 78:55; Acts 7:45"> And having vanquished seven nations in Canaan, He gave their land to His people as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:16; 1 Samuel 3:20; 2 Kings 23:22"> All this took about 450 years.<br />After this, God gave them judges until the time of Samuel the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 1 Samuel 15:1; 1 Samuel 12:12–19"> Then the people asked for a king, and God gave them Saul son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin, who ruled forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalms 78:70–72"> After removing Saul, He raised up David as their king and testified about him: ‘I have found David son of Jesse a man after My own heart; he will carry out My will in its entirety.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1 Samuel 13:14.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Psalms 132:11; Revelation 22:16"> From the descendants of this man, God has brought to Israel the Savior Jesus, as He promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 1:22; John 1:6–8; Luke 3:2–20"> Before the arrival of Jesus, John preached a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Mark 1:7; John 1:26–27"> As John was completing his course, he said, ‘Who do you suppose I am? I am not that One. But there is One coming after me whose sandals I am not worthy to untie.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Luke 3:16; see also Matthew 3:11, Mark 1:7, and John 1:27.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Romans 1:16; Luke 1:69"> Brothers, children of Abraham, and you Gentiles who fear God, it is to us that this message of salvation has been sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 3:17; Luke 24:24–27; Luke 24:20"> The people of Jerusalem and their rulers did not recognize Jesus, yet in condemning Him they fulfilled the words of the prophets that are read every Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 23:21–25; Luke 23:14–16; John 19:4"> And though they found no ground for a death sentence, they asked Pilate to have Him executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 23:53; John 19:28; John 19:30"> When they had carried out all that was written about Him, they took Him down from the tree and laid Him in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Acts 17:31; Matthew 28:6"> But God raised Him from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:3; Acts 1:11"> and for many days He was seen by those who had accompanied Him from Galilee to Jerusalem. They are now His witnesses to our people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Acts 26:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> And now we proclaim to you the good news: What God promised our fathers</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Hebrews 5:5; Hebrews 1:5–6"> He has fulfilled for us, their children, by raising up Jesus. As it is written in the second Psalm:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You are My Son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">today I have become Your Father.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 2:7; literally today I have begotten You">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 89:2–4; Romans 6:9"> In fact, God raised Him from the dead, never to see decay. As He has said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will give you the holy and sure blessings promised to David.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally I will give you the holy, the trustworthy of David; Isaiah 55:3">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:27–31; Psalms 49:9"> So also, He says in another Psalm:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You will not let Your Holy One see decay.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Psalms 16:10">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:29; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 2:10"> For when David had served God’s purpose in his own generation, he fell asleep. His body was buried with his fathers and saw decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 13:30; Acts 2:24"> But the One whom God raised from the dead did not see decay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 24:47; 1 John 2:12; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> Therefore let it be known to you, brothers, that through Jesus the forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Galatians 2:16; Romans 8:3"> Through Him everyone who believes is justified from everything from which you could not be justified by the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Hebrews 3:12; Malachi 3:2"> Watch out, then, that what was spoken by the prophets does not happen to you:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Peter 4:17; Romans 11:7–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Look, you scoffers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">wonder and perish!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I am doing a work in your days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you would never believe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even if someone told you.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Habakkuk 1:5 (see also LXX)">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## A Light for the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Acts 10:33; Matthew 19:30"> As Paul and Barnabas were leaving the synagogue, the people urged them to continue this message on the next Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 2:8"> After the synagogue was dismissed, many of the Jews and devout converts to Judaism followed Paul and Barnabas, who spoke to them and urged them to continue in the grace of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:3; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 11:10"> On the following Sabbath, nearly the whole city gathered to hear the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Acts 18:6; Acts 5:17"> But when the Jews saw the crowds, they were filled with jealousy, and they blasphemously contradicted what Paul was saying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Matthew 21:43; Acts 18:5–6"> Then Paul and Barnabas answered them boldly: “It was necessary to speak the word of God to you first. But since you reject it and do not consider yourselves worthy of eternal life, we now turn to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> For this is what the Lord has commanded us:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I have made you a light for the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to bring salvation to the ends of the earth.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isaiah 49:6">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 11:7"> When the Gentiles heard this, they rejoiced and glorified the word of the Lord, and all who were appointed for eternal life believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 19:10; Acts 19:26"> And the word of the Lord spread throughout that region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 13:45; Acts 14:2"> The Jews, however, incited the religious women of prominence and the leading men of the city. They stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas and drove them out of their district.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:14; Acts 18:6; Luke 9:5"> So they shook the dust off their feet in protest against them and went to Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Romans 15:13; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; Acts 4:31"> And the disciples were filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
