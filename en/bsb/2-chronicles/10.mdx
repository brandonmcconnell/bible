## Rebellion against Rehoboam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Joshua 20:7; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> Then Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all Israel had gone there to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard about this, he returned from Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 12:3"> So they sent for Jeroboam, and he and all Israel came to Rehoboam and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:4; Exodus 2:23; Matthew 11:29–30"> “Your father put a heavy yoke on us. But now you must lighten the burden of your father’s service and the heavy yoke he put on us, and we will serve you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; Proverbs 3:28"> Rehoboam answered, “Come back to me in three days.” So the people departed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 32:7; Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 27:10"> Then King Rehoboam consulted with the elders who had served his father Solomon during his lifetime. “How do you advise me to respond to these people?” he asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Genesis 49:21; 1 Kings 12:7"> They replied, “If you will be kind to these people and please them by speaking kind words to them, they will be your servants forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Proverbs 1:25; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> But Rehoboam rejected the advice of the elders; instead, he consulted the young men who had grown up with him and served him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:6; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 1 Kings 22:6–8"> He asked them, “What message do you advise that we send back to these people who have spoken to me, saying, ‘Lighten the yoke your father put on us’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 13:16; 2 Samuel 17:7–13"> The young men who had grown up with him replied, “This is how you should answer these people who said to you, ‘Your father made our yoke heavy, but you must make it lighter.’ This is what you should tell them: ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; Isaiah 47:6; Revelation 9:10"> Whereas my father burdened you with a heavy yoke, I will add to your yoke. Whereas my father scourged you with whips, I will scourge you with scorpions.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12–15; 2 Chronicles 10:5"> After three days, Jeroboam and all the people returned to Rehoboam, since the king had said, “Come back to me on the third day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:8; Proverbs 19:27; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> And the king answered them harshly. King Rehoboam rejected the advice of the elders</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 12:5; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> and spoke to them as the young men had advised, saying, “Whereas my father made your yoke heavy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX and many Hebrew manuscripts (see also 1 Kings 12:14); MT Whereas I made your yoke heavy">a</a></sup>I will add to your yoke. Whereas my father scourged you with whips, I will scourge you with scorpions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29–39; 1 Samuel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 25:16–20"> So the king did not listen to the people, and indeed this turn of events was from God, in order that the LORD might fulfill the word that He had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Kingdom Divided

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Psalms 76:10"> When all Israel saw that the king had refused to listen to them, they answered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Syriac, Vulgate, and many Hebrew manuscripts; MT And all Israel, since the king had refused to listen to them, answered.">b</a></sup>the king:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What portion do we have in David,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and what inheritance in the son of Jesse?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To your tents, O Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Look now to your own house, O David!”</span><br />So all the Israelites went home,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; 1 Kings 12:17; 1 Kings 11:36"> but Rehoboam still reigned over the Israelites living in the cities of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 5:14; 1 Kings 4:6; Acts 7:57–58"> Then King Rehoboam sent out Hadoram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hadoram is a variant of Adoram and Adoniram; see 2 Samuel 20:24 and 1 Kings 4:6.">c</a></sup>who was in charge of the forced labor, but the Israelites stoned him to death. And King Rehoboam mounted his chariot in haste and escaped to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 12:19–20; Ezra 9:7; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> So to this day Israel has been in rebellion against the house of David.</span>
</div>
</div>
