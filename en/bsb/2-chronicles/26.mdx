## Uzziah Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> All the people of Judah took Uzziah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Uzziah is also called Azariah; throughout this chapter; see 2 Kings 14:21.">a</a></sup>who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 2 Chronicles 25:28; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> Uzziah was the one who rebuilt Eloth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eloth is a variant of Elath; see LXX, 2 Kings 14:22, and 2 Kings 16:6.">b</a></sup>and restored it to Judah after King Amaziah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally after the king">c</a></sup>rested with his fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Uzziah was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem fifty-two years. His mother’s name was Jecoliah; she was from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2"> And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 24:2; 1 Chronicles 22:13"> He sought God throughout the days of Zechariah, who instructed him in the fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Many Hebrew manuscripts, LXX, and Syriac; other Hebrew manuscripts through the vision">d</a></sup>of God. And as long as he sought the LORD, God gave him success.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 5:1"> Uzziah went out to wage war against the Philistines, and he tore down the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod. Then he built cities near Ashdod and among the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16; 1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> God helped him against the Philistines, against the Arabs living in Gur-baal, and against the Meunites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; 2 Chronicles 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> The Ammonites brought tribute to Uzziah, and his fame spread as far as the border of Egypt, for he had become exceedingly powerful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Kings 14:13"> Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate, the Valley Gate, and the angle in the wall, and he fortified them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; 2 Kings 3:4"> Since he had much livestock in the foothills<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">e</a></sup>and in the plain, he built towers in the desert and dug many cisterns. And since he was a lover of the soil, he had farmers and vinedressers in the hill country and in the fertile fields.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2"> Uzziah had an army ready for battle that went out to war by assigned divisions, as recorded by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer under the direction of Hananiah, one of the royal officers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The total number of family leaders of the mighty men of valor was 2,600.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> Under their authority was an army of 307,500 trained for war, a powerful force to support the king against his enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> Uzziah supplied the entire army with shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and slingstones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And in Jerusalem he made skillfully designed devices to shoot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or to protect those who shoot">f</a></sup>arrows and catapult large stones from the towers and corners. So his fame spread far and wide, for he was helped tremendously until he became powerful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> But when Uzziah became powerful, his arrogance led to his own destruction. He was unfaithful to the LORD his God, for he entered the temple of the LORD to burn incense on the altar of incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:10; 1 Chronicles 26:6; 1 Chronicles 12:28"> Then Azariah the priest, along with eighty brave priests of the LORD, went in after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> They took their stand against King Uzziah and said, “Uzziah, you have no right to offer incense to the LORD. Only the priests, the descendants of Aaron, are consecrated to burn incense. Leave the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully; you will not receive honor from the LORD God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> Uzziah, with a censer in his hand to offer incense, was enraged. But while he raged against the priests in their presence in the house of the LORD before the altar of incense, leprosy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Leprosy was a term used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.">g</a></sup>broke out on his forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> When Azariah the chief priest and all the priests turned to him and saw his leprous forehead, they rushed him out. Indeed, he himself hurried to get out, because the LORD had afflicted him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; 2 Kings 7:3"> So King Uzziah was a leper until the day of his death. He lived in isolation, leprous and cut off from the house of the LORD, while his son Jotham had charge of the royal palace and governed the people of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> As for the rest of the acts of Uzziah, from beginning to end, they are recorded by the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 26:18"> And Uzziah rested with his fathers and was buried near them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally with his fathers">h</a></sup>in a field of burial that belonged to the kings, for the people said, “He was a leper.” And his son Jotham reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
