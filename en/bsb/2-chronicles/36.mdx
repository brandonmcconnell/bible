## Jehoahaz Succeeds Josiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:11; 2 Chronicles 33:25; 2 Chronicles 26:1"> Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah and made him king in Jerusalem in place of his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Jehoahaz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Joahaz, a variant of Jehoahaz; also in verse 4">a</a></sup>was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33"> And the king of Egypt dethroned him in Jerusalem and imposed on Judah a levy of a hundred talents of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="100 talents is approximately 3.77 tons or 3.42 metric tons of silver.">b</a></sup>and a talent of gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A talent is approximately 75.4 pounds or 34.2 kilograms of gold.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; 2 Kings 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> Then Neco king of Egypt made Eliakim brother of Jehoahaz king over Judah and Jerusalem, and he changed Eliakim’s name to Jehoiakim. But Neco took Eliakim’s brother Jehoahaz and carried him off to Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoiakim Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eleven years. And he did evil in the sight of the LORD his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; 2 Kings 24:5–6; Ezekiel 19:5–9"> Then Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against Jehoiakim and bound him with bronze shackles to take him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremiah 27:16–18"> Nebuchadnezzar also took to Babylon some of the articles from the house of the LORD, and he put them in his temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or palace">d</a></sup>in Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; 1 Chronicles 3:16–17"> As for the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, the abominations he committed, and all that was found against him, they are indeed written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. And his son Jehoiachin reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoiachin Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:8–17"> Jehoiachin was eighteen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="One Hebrew manuscript, some LXX manuscripts, and Syriac (see also 2 Kings 24:8); most Hebrew manuscripts eight">e</a></sup>years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem three months and ten days. And he did evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Kings 24:10–17"> In the spring,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally At the turn of the year">f</a></sup>King Nebuchadnezzar summoned Jehoiachin and brought him to Babylon, along with the articles of value from the house of the LORD. And he made Jehoiachin’s relative<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or brother or uncle">g</a></sup>Zedekiah king over Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zedekiah Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:18–20; Jeremiah 52:1–3; Jeremiah 27:1"> Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eleven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Jeremiah 37:2–21; Jeremiah 38:14–28"> And he did evil in the sight of the LORD his God and did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke for the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:2–3; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Ezekiel 17:11–20"> He also rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by God. But Zedekiah stiffened his neck and hardened<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or made strong or made courageous">h</a></sup>his heart against turning to the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Ezekiel 22:6; Micah 3:9–11"> Furthermore, all the leaders of the priests and the people multiplied their unfaithful deeds, following all the abominations of the nations, and they defiled the house of the LORD, which He had consecrated in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Fall of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 25:3–4"> Again and again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally Rising up early and sending (it),">i</a></sup>the LORD, the God of their fathers, sent word to His people through His messengers because He had compassion on them and on His dwelling place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; 2 Chronicles 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> But they mocked the messengers of God, despising His words and scoffing at His prophets, until the wrath of the LORD against His people was stirred up beyond remedy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra 9:7; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> So He brought up against them the king of the Chaldeans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians; also clarified in verse 19">j</a></sup>who put their young men to the sword in the sanctuary, sparing neither young men nor young women, neither elderly nor infirm. God gave them all into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Isaiah 39:6"> who carried off everything to Babylon—all the articles of the house of God, both large and small, and the treasures of the house of the LORD and of the king and his officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; 1 Kings 9:8; Jeremiah 52:13–15"> Then the Chaldeans set fire to the house of God and broke down the wall of Jerusalem. They burned down all the palaces and destroyed every article of value.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7; Ezra 1:1–11; 2 Chronicles 36:22"> Those who escaped the sword were carried by Nebuchadnezzar into exile in Babylon, and they became servants to him and his sons until the kingdom of Persia came to power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zechariah 1:12"> So the land enjoyed its Sabbath rest all the days of the desolation, until seventy years were completed, in fulfillment of the word of the LORD spoken through Jeremiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Jeremiah 25:12 and Jeremiah 29:10.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Proclamation of Cyrus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Ezra 1:1–3; Jeremiah 29:10"> In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, to fulfill the word of the LORD spoken through Jeremiah, the LORD stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia to send a proclamation throughout his kingdom and to put it in writing as follows:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26–28; Ezra 7:13; Romans 8:31"> “This is what Cyrus king of Persia says:<br />‘The LORD, the God of heaven, who has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, has appointed me to build a house for Him at Jerusalem in Judah.<br />Whoever among you belongs to His people, may the LORD his God be with him, and may he go up.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
