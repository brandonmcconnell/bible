## Ahaziah Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 8:24–29; 2 Chronicles 36:1"> Then the people of Jerusalem made Ahaziah, the youngest son of Jehoram, king in his place, since the raiders who had come into the camp with the Arabs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or since the marauding bands of Arabs">a</a></sup>had killed all the older sons. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 16:28; 2 Kings 8:26; 2 Chronicles 21:6"> Ahaziah was twenty-two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some LXX manuscripts and Syriac (see also 2 Kings 8:26); Hebrew forty-two">b</a></sup>years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem one year. His mother’s name was Athaliah, the granddaughter of Omri.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 27:12–13; Genesis 6:4–5; Judges 17:4–5"> Ahaziah also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother was his counselor in wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 24:17–18; Proverbs 19:27"> And he did evil in the sight of the LORD, as the house of Ahab had done, for to his destruction they were his counselors after the death of his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28–29; 2 Chronicles 19:2; Psalms 1:1"> Ahaziah also followed their counsel and went with Joram son of Ahab king of Israel to fight against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead. But the Arameans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some LXX manuscripts the archers">c</a></sup>wounded Joram.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew Jehoram, a variant of Joram; also in verses 6 and 7">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15; 2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 10:13–14"> So he returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds they had inflicted on him at Ramah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ramah is a variant of Ramoth; see verse 5.">e</a></sup>when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. Then Ahaziah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts, LXX, Vulgate, and Syriac (see also 2 Kings 8:29); most Hebrew manuscripts Azariah">f</a></sup>son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to Jezreel to visit Joram son of Ahab, because Joram had been wounded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:15; 2 Kings 9:21; Malachi 4:3"> Ahaziah’s downfall came from God when he went to visit Joram. When Ahaziah arrived, he went out with Joram to meet Jehu son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or grandson; see 2 Kings 9:14.">g</a></sup>of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to destroy the house of Ahab.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehu Kills the Princes of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 10:10–14"> So while Jehu was executing judgment on the house of Ahab, he found the rulers of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah’s brothers who were serving Ahaziah, and he killed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 21:20–1"> Then Jehu looked for Ahaziah, and Jehu’s soldiers captured him while he was hiding in Samaria. So Ahaziah was brought to Jehu and put to death. They buried him, for they said, “He is the grandson of Jehoshaphat, who sought the LORD with all his heart.”<br />So no one was left from the house of Ahaziah with the strength to rule the kingdom.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Athaliah and Joash

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–3; 2 Chronicles 22:2–4"> When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she proceeded to annihilate all the royal heirs of the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 15:4; 2 Kings 11:2; Isaiah 65:8"> But Jehoshabeath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jehoshabeath is a variant of Jehosheba; twice in this verse; see 2 Kings 11:2.">h</a></sup>daughter of King Jehoram took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the sons of the king who were being murdered, and she put him and his nurse in a bedroom. Because Jehoshabeath, the daughter of King Jehoram and the wife of Jehoiada the priest, was Ahaziah’s sister, she hid Joash from Athaliah so that she could not kill him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 12:8; Psalms 73:18–19; Jeremiah 12:1"> And Joash remained hidden with them in the house of God for six years while Athaliah ruled the land.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
