## Shishak Raids Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:17; 2 Chronicles 12:13; 2 Chronicles 26:13–16"> After Rehoboam had established his sovereignty and royal power, he and all Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Judah; in 2 Chronicles, Judah is occasionally called Israel, as representative of the true Israel.">a</a></sup>with him forsook the Law of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 7:19–20; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> In the fifth year of Rehoboam’s reign, because they had been unfaithful to the LORD, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:9; 2 Chronicles 16:8; Daniel 11:43"> with 1,200 chariots, 60,000 horsemen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or charioteers">b</a></sup>and countless troops who came with him out of Egypt—Libyans, Sukkites, and Cushites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, people from the upper Nile region">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–12; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 8:8"> He captured the fortified cities of Judah and came as far as Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 12:22"> Then Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and the leaders of Judah who had gathered at Jerusalem because of Shishak, and he said to them, “This is what the LORD says: ‘You have forsaken Me; therefore, I have forsaken you into the hand of Shishak.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:27; Daniel 9:14; Jeremiah 13:18"> So the leaders of Israel and the king humbled themselves and said, “The LORD is righteous.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:25; Isaiah 42:25; 1 Kings 21:28–29"> When the LORD saw that they had humbled themselves, the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah, saying, “They have humbled themselves; I will not destroy them, but will soon grant them deliverance. My wrath will not be poured out on Jerusalem through Shishak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Isaiah 26:13; Jeremiah 10:24"> Nevertheless, they will become his servants, so that they may learn the difference between serving Me and serving the kings of other lands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Kings 16:8"> So King Shishak of Egypt attacked Jerusalem and seized the treasures of the house of the LORD and of the royal palace. He took everything, including the gold shields that Solomon had made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 23:23; Lamentations 4:1"> Then King Rehoboam made bronze shields in their place and committed them to the care of the captains of the guard on duty at the entrance to the royal palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And whenever the king entered the house of the LORD, the guards would go with him, bearing the shields, and later they would return them to the guardroom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> Because Rehoboam humbled himself, the anger of the LORD turned away from him, and He did not destroy him completely. Indeed, conditions were good in Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam’s Reign and Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 14:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6"> Thus King Rehoboam established himself in Jerusalem and reigned. He was forty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the LORD had chosen from all the tribes of Israel in which to put His Name. His mother’s name was Naamah the Ammonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Corinthians 16:13; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> And Rehoboam did evil because he did not set his heart to seek the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:5; 1 Kings 12:22"> Now the acts of Rehoboam, from first to last, are they not written in the records of Shemaiah the Prophet and of Iddo the Seer concerning the genealogies? There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam throughout their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:10; 2 Chronicles 13:1; Matthew 1:7"> And Rehoboam rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. And his son Abijah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Abijah is a variant of Abijam; see 1 Kings 14:31.">d</a></sup>reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
