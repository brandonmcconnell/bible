## Solomon Blesses the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then Solomon declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The LORD has said that He would dwell</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the thick cloud.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4–6; Psalms 135:21; 1 Chronicles 28:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I have built You an exalted house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a place for You to dwell forever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:14; 2 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 8:55–61"> And as the whole assembly of Israel stood there, the king turned around and blessed them all</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; Psalms 68:32–35"> and said:<br />“Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who has fulfilled with His own hand what He spoke with His mouth to my father David, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; 1 Kings 8:16; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> ‘Since the day I brought My people out of the land of Egypt, I have not chosen a city from any tribe of Israel in which to build a house so that My Name would be there, nor have I chosen anyone to be ruler over My people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:4; Psalms 132:13; 2 Chronicles 12:13"> But now I have chosen Jerusalem for My Name to be there, and I have chosen David to be over My people Israel.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:1; 1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 22:7"> Now it was in the heart of my father David to build a house for the Name of the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; 1 Kings 8:18–21"> But the LORD said to my father David, ‘Since it was in your heart to build a house for My Name, you have done well to have this in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–13; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 1 Chronicles 17:11–12"> Nevertheless, you are not the one to build it; but your son, your own offspring, will build the house for My Name.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> Now the LORD has fulfilled the word that He spoke. I have succeeded my father David, and I sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised. I have built the house for the Name of the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 5:7; 1 Kings 8:21"> And there I have placed the ark, which contains the covenant of the LORD that He made with the children of Israel.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Prayer of Dedication

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Isaiah 50:10; Job 11:13"> Then Solomon stood before the altar of the LORD in front of the whole assembly of Israel and spread out his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; Psalms 95:6"> Now Solomon had made a bronze platform five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The platform was approximately 7.5 feet in length and width, and 4.5 feet high (2.3 meters in length and width, and 1.4 meters high).">a</a></sup>and had placed it in the middle of the courtyard. He stood on it, knelt down before the whole assembly of Israel, spread out his hands toward heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> and said:<br />“O LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like You in heaven or on earth, keeping Your covenant of loving devotion with Your servants who walk before You with all their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 8:24"> You have kept Your promise to Your servant, my father David. What You spoke with Your mouth You have fulfilled with Your hand this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; John 15:14–15; 2 Chronicles 7:18"> Therefore now, O LORD, God of Israel, keep for Your servant, my father David, what You promised when You said: ‘You will never fail to have a man to sit before Me on the throne of Israel, if only your descendants guard their way to walk in My law as you have walked before Me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:5; Isaiah 41:17; 2 Chronicles 6:4"> And now, O LORD, God of Israel, please confirm what You promised to Your servant David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 139:7–10"> But will God indeed dwell with man upon the earth? The heavens, even the highest heavens, cannot contain You, much less this temple I have built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> Yet regard the prayer and plea of Your servant, O LORD my God, so that You may hear the cry and the prayer that Your servant is praying before You.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Daniel 6:10; Psalms 121:5"> May Your eyes be open toward this temple day and night, toward the place where You said You would put Your Name, so that You may hear the prayer that Your servant prays toward this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Isaiah 43:25"> Hear the plea of Your servant and of Your people Israel when they pray toward this place. May You hear from heaven, Your dwelling place. May You hear and forgive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> When a man sins against his neighbor and is required to take an oath, and he comes to take an oath before Your altar in this temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremiah 51:56"> then may You hear from heaven and act. May You judge Your servants, condemning the wicked man by bringing down on his own head what he has done, and justifying the righteous man by rewarding him according to his righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> When Your people Israel are defeated before an enemy because they have sinned against You, and they return to You and confess Your name, praying and pleading before You in this temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> then may You hear from heaven and forgive the sin of Your people Israel. May You restore them to the land You gave to them and their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> When the skies are shut and there is no rain because Your people have sinned against You, and they pray toward this place and confess Your name, and they turn from their sins because You have afflicted them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35–36; Jeremiah 5:24; Zechariah 10:1"> then may You hear from heaven and forgive the sin of Your servants, Your people Israel, so that You may teach them the good way in which they should walk. May You send rain on the land that You gave Your people as an inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> When famine or plague comes upon the land, or blight or mildew or locusts or grasshoppers, or when their enemies besiege them in their cities, whatever plague or sickness may come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 32:2–6; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13"> then may whatever prayer or plea Your people Israel make—each knowing his own afflictions and spreading out his hands toward this temple—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; 1 Samuel 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> be heard by You from heaven, Your dwelling place. And may You forgive and repay each man according to all his ways, since You know his heart—for You alone know the hearts of men—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; Psalms 128:1"> so that they may fear You and walk in Your ways all the days they live in the land that You gave to our fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–7; 1 Kings 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> And as for the foreigner who is not of Your people Israel but has come from a distant land because of Your great name and Your mighty hand and outstretched arm—when he comes and prays toward this temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; 1 Kings 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> then may You hear from heaven, Your dwelling place, and do according to all for which the foreigner calls to You. Then all the peoples of the earth will know Your name and fear You, as do Your people Israel, and they will know that this house I have built is called by Your Name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:6–13; 2 Chronicles 18:31; 2 Chronicles 20:4"> When Your people go to war against their enemies, wherever You send them, and when they pray to You in the direction of this city You have chosen and the house I have built for Your Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Psalms 9:3–4; Jeremiah 5:28"> then may You hear from heaven their prayer and their plea, and may You uphold their cause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> When they sin against You—for there is no one who does not sin—and You become angry with them and deliver them to an enemy who takes them as captives to a land far or near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Isaiah 64:6–12"> and when they come to their senses in the land to which they were taken, and they repent and plead with You in the land of their captors, saying, ‘We have sinned and done wrong; we have acted wickedly,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> and when they return to You with all their heart and soul in the land of the enemies who took them captive, and when they pray in the direction of the land that You gave to their fathers, the city You have chosen, and the house I have built for Your Name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; 2 Chronicles 6:35; Psalms 25:18"> then may You hear from heaven, Your dwelling place, their prayer and pleas, and may You uphold their cause. May You forgive Your people who sinned against You.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; Psalms 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now, my God, may Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to the prayer offered in this place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalms 132:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now therefore, arise, O LORD God, and enter Your resting place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You and the ark of Your might.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May Your priests, O LORD God, be clothed with salvation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and may Your godly ones rejoice in goodness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 132:1; Isaiah 61:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD God, do not reject Your anointed one.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Remember Your loving devotion to Your servant David.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
