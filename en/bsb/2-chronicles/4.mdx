## The Bronze Altar and Molten Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Ezekiel 43:13–17; 1 Kings 9:25"> He made a bronze altar twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The altar was approximately 30 feet in length and width and 15 feet high (9.1 meters in length and width and 4.6 meters high).">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> He also made the Sea of cast metal. It was circular in shape, measuring ten cubits from rim to rim, five cubits in height, and thirty cubits in circumference.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Sea was approximately 15 feet from rim to rim, 7.5 feet in height, and 45 feet in circumference (4.6 meters from rim to rim, 2.3 meters in height, and 13.7 meters in circumference).">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> Below the rim, figures of oxen encircled it, ten per cubit all the way around the Sea, cast in two rows as a part of the Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> The Sea stood on twelve oxen, three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east. The Sea rested on them, with all their hindquarters toward the center.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> It was a handbreadth thick,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A handbreadth is approximately 2.9 inches or 7.4 centimeters.">c</a></sup>and its rim was fashioned like the brim of a cup, like a lily blossom. It could hold three thousand baths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3,000 baths is approximately 17,400 gallons or 66,000 liters.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ten Basins, Lampstands, and Tables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> He also made ten basins for washing and placed five on the south side and five on the north. The parts of the burnt offering were rinsed in them, but the priests used the Sea for washing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; John 8:12"> He made ten gold lampstands according to their specifications and placed them in the temple, five on the south side and five on the north.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> Additionally, he made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the south side and five on the north. He also made a hundred gold bowls.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Completion of the Bronze Works

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:40–51; 1 Kings 7:14"> Additionally, Huram made the pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls.<br />So Huram finished the work that he had undertaken for King Solomon in the house of God:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> the two pillars;<br />the two bowl-shaped capitals atop the pillars;<br />the two sets of network covering both bowls of the capitals atop the pillars;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> the four hundred pomegranates for the two sets of network (two rows of pomegranates for each network covering both the bowl-shaped capitals atop the pillars);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> the stands;<br />the basins on the stands;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the Sea;<br />the twelve oxen underneath the Sea;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> and the pots, shovels, meat forks, and all the other articles.<br />All these objects that Huram-abi made for King Solomon for the house of the LORD were of polished bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> The king had them cast in clay molds in the plain of the Jordan between Succoth and Zeredah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Zeredah is a variant of Zarethan; see 1 Kings 7:46.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Solomon made all these articles in such great abundance that the weight of the bronze could not be determined.</span>
</div>
</div>
