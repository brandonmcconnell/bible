## Josiah Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:2; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> Josiah was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem thirty-one years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:2; Joshua 1:7; 2 Kings 22:2"> And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD and walked in the ways of his father David; he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Josiah Destroys Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:22; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Proverbs 8:17"> In the eighth year of his reign, while he was still young, Josiah began to seek the God of his father David, and in the twelfth year he began to cleanse Judah and Jerusalem of the high places, the Asherah poles, the carved idols, and the cast images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Leviticus 26:30; 2 Kings 23:4–6"> Then in his presence the altars of the Baals were torn down, and he cut to pieces the incense altars that were above them. He shattered the Asherah poles, the carved idols, and the cast images, crushed them to dust, and scattered them over the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 13:2; Jeremiah 3:10; Numbers 35:33"> Then he burned the bones of the priests on their altars. So he cleansed Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:20–21; Isaiah 7:25; 2 Chronicles 30:1"> Josiah did the same in the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim, and Simeon, as far as Naphtali, and in the ruins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in the regions">a</a></sup>around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Chronicles 34:1"> He tore down the altars and Asherah poles, crushed the idols to powder, and cut to pieces all the incense altars throughout the land of Israel. Then he returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Josiah Repairs the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 1 Chronicles 18:15; Jeremiah 29:25"> Now in the eighteenth year of his reign, in order to cleanse the land and the temple, Josiah sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the governor of the city, and Joah son of Joahaz, the recorder, to repair the house of the LORD his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:18; 2 Chronicles 30:10; 2 Chronicles 35:8"> So they went to Hilkiah the high priest and gave him the money that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites who guarded the doors had collected from the people of Manasseh and Ephraim, from all the remnant of Israel, from all Judah and Benjamin, and from the people of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 12:11–12; Ezra 3:7; 2 Kings 22:5–6"> They put it into the hands of those supervising the work in the house of the LORD, who in turn gave it to the workmen restoring and repairing the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:4–7; 2 Chronicles 33:22"> They also gave money to the carpenters and builders to buy dressed stone, as well as timbers for couplings and beams for the buildings that the kings of Judah had allowed to deteriorate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 12:15; 1 Chronicles 23:5; 2 Kings 22:7"> And the men did the work faithfully. The Levites overseeing them were Jahath and Obadiah, descendants of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam, descendants of Kohath. Other Levites, all skilled with musical instruments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 2:18; Jeremiah 8:8"> were over the laborers and supervised all who did the work, task by task. Some of the Levites were secretaries, officers, and gatekeepers.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hilkiah Finds the Book of the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 8:36; Psalms 1:2; Leviticus 10:11"> While they were bringing out the money that had been taken into the house of the LORD, Hilkiah the priest found the Book of the Law of the LORD given by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew the Law of the LORD by the hand of">b</a></sup>Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Hilkiah said to Shaphan the scribe, “I have found the Book of the Law in the house of the LORD!” And he gave it to Shaphan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 22:9–10; Jeremiah 36:20–21"> Then Shaphan brought the book to the king and reported, “Your servants are doing all that has been placed in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They have paid out the money that was found in the house of the LORD and have put it into the hands of the supervisors and workers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:46; Jeremiah 36:20–21; Deuteronomy 17:19"> Moreover, Shaphan the scribe told the king, “Hilkiah the priest has given me a book.” And Shaphan read it in the presence of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Deuteronomy 28:3–68; 2 Kings 22:19"> When the king heard the words of the Law, he tore his clothes</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 26:24; Jeremiah 40:9"> and commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Abdon is a variant of Achbor; see 2 Kings 22:12.">c</a></sup>son of Micah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Micah is a variant of Micaiah; see 2 Kings 22:12.">d</a></sup>Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the servant of the king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; Ezekiel 14:1–11; 2 Chronicles 28:6"> “Go and inquire of the LORD for me and for those remaining in Israel and Judah concerning the words in the book that has been found. For great is the wrath of the LORD that has been poured out on us because our fathers have not kept the word of the LORD by doing all that is written in this book.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Huldah’s Prophecy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 2:36; Exodus 15:20; 2 Kings 22:14"> So Hilkiah and those the king had designated went and spoke to Huldah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="One Hebrew manuscript, Vulgate, and Syriac; most Hebrew manuscripts and those the king had told went to Huldah">e</a></sup>the prophetess, the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Tokhath is a variant of Tikvah; see 2 Kings 22:14.">f</a></sup>the son of Hasrah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hasrah is a variant of Harhas; see 2 Kings 22:14.">g</a></sup>the keeper of the wardrobe. She lived in Jerusalem, in the Second District.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or the Second Quarter, a newer section of Jerusalem; Hebrew the Mishneh">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7–10; Jeremiah 21:3–7; 2 Kings 22:15–20"> And Huldah said to them, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Tell the man who sent you</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–20; Joshua 23:16; Deuteronomy 28:15–68"> that this is what the LORD says: I am about to bring calamity on this place and on its people, according to all the curses written in the book that has been read in the presence of the king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Mark 9:43–48; Nahum 1:6"> because they have forsaken Me and burned incense to other gods, that they might provoke Me to anger with all the works of their hands. My wrath will be poured out upon this place and will not be quenched.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:23; 2 Chronicles 34:21"> But as for the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the LORD, tell him that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘As for the words that you heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:26; Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 34:18"> because your heart was tender and you humbled yourself before God when you heard His words against this place and against its people, and because you have humbled yourself before Me and have torn your clothes and wept before Me, I have heard you,’ declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 39:8; Psalms 37:37; 1 Kings 21:29"> ‘Now I will indeed gather you to your fathers, and you will be gathered to your grave in peace. Your eyes will not see all the calamity that I will bring on this place and on its people.’”<br />So they brought her answer back to the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Josiah Renews the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 23:1–3; 2 Chronicles 30:2; Mark 14:8"> Then the king summoned all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; 2 Kings 23:2; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> And he went up to the house of the LORD with all the people of Judah and Jerusalem, as well as the priests and the Levites—all the people great and small—and in their hearing he read all the words of the Book of the Covenant that had been found in the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:16; 2 Kings 23:3; 2 Kings 11:14"> So the king stood by the pillar and made a covenant before the LORD to follow the LORD and to keep His commandments, decrees, and statutes with all his heart and all his soul, and to carry out the words of the covenant that were written in this book.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; Jeremiah 3:10; 2 Chronicles 14:4"> Then he had everyone in Jerusalem and Benjamin take a stand in agreement to it. So all the people of Jerusalem carried out the covenant of God, the God of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:3–7; Joshua 24:31; 2 Kings 23:4–20"> And Josiah removed all the abominations from all the lands belonging to the Israelites, and he required everyone in Israel to serve the LORD their God. Throughout his reign they did not turn aside from following the LORD, the God of their fathers.</span>
</div>
</div>
