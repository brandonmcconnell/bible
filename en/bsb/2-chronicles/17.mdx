## Jehoshaphat Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; Ephesians 6:10; Matthew 1:8"> Asa’s son Jehoshaphat reigned in his place, and he strengthened himself against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; 2 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Chronicles 11:11–12"> He stationed troops in every fortified city of Judah and put garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> Now the LORD was with Jehoshaphat because he walked in the earlier ways<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in his early years he walked in the ways">a</a></sup>of his father David. He did not seek the Baals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> but he sought the God of his father and walked by His commandments rather than the practices of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> So the LORD established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah brought him tribute, so that he had an abundance of riches and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 2 Chronicles 20:33"> And his heart took delight<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or was exalted or was courageous">b</a></sup>in the ways of the LORD; furthermore, he removed the high places and Asherah poles from Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> In the third year of his reign, Jehoshaphat sent his officials Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah to teach in the cities of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> accompanied by certain Levites—Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah—along with the priests Elishama and Jehoram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> They taught throughout Judah, taking with them the Book of the Law of the LORD. They went throughout the towns of Judah and taught the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And the dread of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that surrounded Judah, so that they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:8; 2 Chronicles 9:14; 2 Samuel 8:2"> Some Philistines also brought gifts and silver as tribute to Jehoshaphat, and the Arabs brought him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats from their flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> Jehoshaphat grew stronger and stronger, and he built fortresses and store cities in Judah</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 26:10–15"> and kept vast supplies in the cities of Judah. He also had warriors in Jerusalem who were mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> These are their numbers according to the houses of their fathers:<br />From Judah, the commanders of thousands:<br />Adnah the commander, and with him 300,000 mighty men of valor;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> next to him, Jehohanan the commander, and with him 280,000;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> and next to him, Amasiah son of Zichri, the volunteer for the LORD, and with him 200,000 mighty men of valor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> From Benjamin:<br />Eliada, a mighty man of valor, and with him 200,000 armed with bows and shields;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and next to him, Jehozabad, and with him 180,000 armed for battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:2; 2 Chronicles 11:12; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> These were the men who served the king, besides those he stationed in the fortified cities throughout Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>
