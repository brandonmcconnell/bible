## Abijah Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Kings 15:1–8"> In the eighteenth year of Jeroboam’s reign, Abijah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Abijah is a variant of Abijam; see 1 Kings 14:31.">a</a></sup>became king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Kings 15:6–7; Judges 19:16"> and he reigned in Jerusalem three years. His mother’s name was Micaiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; most LXX manuscripts and Syriac Maacah; see 2 Chronicles 11:20 and 1 Kings 15:2.">b</a></sup>daughter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or granddaughter">c</a></sup>of Uriel; she was from Gibeah.<br />And there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:12–13; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 1 Samuel 17:1–3"> Abijah went into battle with an army of 400,000 chosen men, while Jeroboam drew up in formation against him with 800,000 chosen and mighty men of valor.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Civil War against Jeroboam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:22; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Genesis 10:18"> Then Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in the hill country of Ephraim and said, “Hear me, O Jeroboam and all Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50"> Do you not know that the LORD, the God of Israel, has given the kingship of Israel to David and his descendants forever by a covenant of salt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, a perpetual covenant">d</a></sup>?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:26; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Chronicles 10:19"> Yet Jeroboam son of Nebat, a servant of Solomon son of David, rose up and rebelled against his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 2 Chronicles 12:13; Acts 17:5"> Then worthless and wicked men gathered around him to resist Rehoboam son of Solomon when he was young, inexperienced, and unable to resist them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 2 Chronicles 11:15; Luke 19:14"> And now you think you can resist the kingdom of the LORD, which is in the hands of David’s descendants. You are indeed a vast army, and you have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam made for you as gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14–15; Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 29:35"> But did you not drive out the priests of the LORD, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites? And did you not make priests for yourselves as do the peoples of other lands? Now whoever comes to consecrate himself with a young bull and seven rams can become a priest of things that are not gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Zechariah 13:9"> But as for us, the LORD is our God. We have not forsaken Him; the priests who minister to the LORD are sons of Aaron, and the Levites attend to their duties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Leviticus 24:2–9; Exodus 29:38"> Every morning and every evening they present burnt offerings and fragrant incense to the LORD. They set out the rows of showbread on the ceremonially clean table, and every evening they light the lamps of the gold lampstand. We are carrying out the requirements of the LORD our God, while you have forsaken Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:39; Romans 8:31; Numbers 10:8–9"> Now behold, God Himself is with us as our head, and His priests with their trumpets sound the battle call against you. O children of Israel, do not fight against the LORD, the God of your fathers, for you will not succeed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; Joshua 8:9; Proverbs 21:30"> Now Jeroboam had sent troops around to ambush from the rear, so that while he was in front of Judah, the ambush was behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; Exodus 14:10; Joshua 8:20"> When Judah turned and discovered that the battle was both before and behind them, they cried out to the LORD. Then the priests blew the trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:12; Joshua 11:8; Psalms 118:4–7"> and the men of Judah raised the battle cry. And when they raised the cry, God routed Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; Judges 11:21; Joshua 10:12"> So the Israelites fled before Judah, and God delivered them into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 13:12"> Then Abijah and his people struck them with a mighty blow, and 500,000 chosen men of Israel fell slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Nahum 1:7"> Thus the Israelites were subdued at that time, and the men of Judah prevailed because they relied on the LORD, the God of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; Joshua 15:9; Joshua 10:39"> Abijah pursued Jeroboam and captured some cities from him: Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron, along with their villages.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:38; 1 Kings 14:20; Acts 12:23"> Jeroboam did not again recover his power during the days of Abijah, and the LORD struck him down and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Samuel 5:12–13; Judges 10:4"> But Abijah grew strong, married fourteen wives, and became the father of twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 24:27"> Now the rest of the acts of Abijah, along with his ways and his words, are written in the Treatise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or Exposition or Study">e</a></sup>of the Prophet Iddo.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
