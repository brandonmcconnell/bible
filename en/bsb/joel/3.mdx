## The LORD Judges the Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 30:3; Ezekiel 39:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Yes, in those days and at that time,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I restore Judah and Jerusalem from captivity,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–4; Isaiah 66:16; Zephaniah 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will gather all the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jehoshaphat means the LORD judges; also in verse 12.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There I will enter into judgment against them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">concerning My people, My inheritance, Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whom they have scattered among the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as they divided up My land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Obadiah 1:11; Amos 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They cast lots for My people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they bartered a boy for a prostitute</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sold a girl for wine to drink.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremiah 47:4; Isaiah 59:18"> Now what do you have against Me, O Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you rendering against Me a recompense? If you retaliate against Me, I will swiftly and speedily return your recompense upon your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:18"> For you took My silver and gold and carried off My finest treasures to your temples.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or palaces">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or to the peoples of Javan">d</a></sup>to send them far from their homeland.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Jeremiah 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> Behold, I will rouse them from the places to which you sold them; I will return your recompense upon your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Job 1:15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> I will sell your sons and daughters into the hands of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans—to a distant nation.”<br />Indeed, the LORD has spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:3–4; Isaiah 8:9–10; Ezekiel 38:7"> Proclaim this among the nations:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Prepare for war;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rouse the mighty men;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let all the men of war</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">advance and attack!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:8; Micah 4:3; 2 Chronicles 25:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Beat your plowshares into swords</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your pruning hooks into spears.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let the weak say, ‘I am strong!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 38:9–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come quickly, all you surrounding nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gather yourselves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bring down Your mighty ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O LORD.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Isaiah 2:4; Psalms 96:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the nations be roused</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and advance to the Valley of Jehoshaphat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for there I will sit down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to judge all the nations on every side.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Hosea 6:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Swing the sickle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the harvest is ripe.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="LXX the harvest has come; see also Mark 4:29.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Come, trample the grapes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the winepress is full;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the wine vats overflow</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because their wickedness is great.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 34:2–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Multitudes, multitudes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the valley of decision!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the Day of the LORD is near</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the valley of decision.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun and moon will grow dark,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the stars will no longer shine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Haggai 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD will roar from Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and raise His voice from Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">heaven and earth will tremble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the LORD will be a refuge for His people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a stronghold for the people of Israel.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:1–12; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in that day the mountains will drip with sweet wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hills will flow with milk.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All the streams of Judah will run with water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a spring will flow from the house of the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to water the Valley of Acacias.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or Valley of Shittim">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:35; Zechariah 14:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt will become desolate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Edom a desert wasteland,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the violence done to the people of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in whose land they shed innocent blood.</span></span>
