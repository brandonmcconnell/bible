## Jerusalem’s New Settlers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 48:2; Isaiah 52:1; Nehemiah 11:18"> Now the leaders of the people settled in Jerusalem, and the rest of the people cast lots to bring one out of ten to live in the holy city of Jerusalem, while the remaining nine<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew nine hands">a</a></sup>were to dwell in their own towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 5:9; 2 Corinthians 8:16–17; Job 29:13"> And the people blessed all the men who volunteered to live in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 2:1; Ezra 2:43; 1 Chronicles 9:1–34"> These are the heads of the provinces who settled in Jerusalem. (In the villages of Judah, however, each lived on his own property in their towns—the Israelites, priests, Levites, temple servants,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Nethinim; also in verse 21">b</a></sup>and descendants of Solomon’s servants—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 38:29; Matthew 1:3; Luke 3:33"> while some of the descendants of Judah and Benjamin settled in Jerusalem.)<br />From the descendants of Judah:<br />Athaiah son of Uzziah, the son of Zechariah, the son of Amariah, the son of Shephatiah, the son of Mahalalel, a descendant of Perez;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:5; Nehemiah 3:15; Numbers 26:20"> and Maaseiah son of Baruch, the son of Col-hozeh, the son of Hazaiah, the son of Adaiah, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zechariah, a descendant of Shelah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or of the Shilonite">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The descendants of Perez who settled in Jerusalem totaled 468 men of valor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:7–9"> From the descendants of Benjamin:<br />Sallu son of Meshullam, the son of Joed, the son of Pedaiah, the son of Kolaiah, the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ithiel, the son of Jeshaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and his followers Gabbai and Sallai—928 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:7"> Joel son of Zichri was the officer over them, and Judah son of Hassenuah was over the Second District of the city.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or was over the Second Quarter of the city, a newer section of Jerusalem; or was second in command of the city">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:10; Nehemiah 12:6; Nehemiah 12:19"> From the priests:<br />Jedaiah son of Joiarib;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or Jedaiah; the son of Joiarib;">e</a></sup>Jachin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:11; 1 Chronicles 6:7–14; Numbers 3:32"> Seraiah son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, the chief official of the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:12–13"> and their associates who did the work at the temple—822 men;<br />Adaiah son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi, the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and his associates, the leaders of families—242 men;<br />Amashai son of Azarel, the son of Ahzai, the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and his associates<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Most LXX manuscripts; Hebrew their associates">f</a></sup>—128 mighty men of valor. Zabdiel son of Haggedolim was their overseer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:14; 1 Chronicles 9:19"> From the Levites:<br />Shemaiah son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 6:2–3; Nehemiah 8:7; Ezra 8:33"> Shabbethai and Jozabad, two leaders of the Levites, who supervised the work outside the house of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:15; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; Nehemiah 12:8–9"> Mattaniah son of Mica, the son of Zabdi, the son of Asaph, who led in thanksgiving and prayer; Bakbukiah, second among his associates; and Abda son of Shammua, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1; Revelation 21:2; Daniel 9:24"> The Levites in the holy city totaled 284.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:45; 1 Chronicles 9:17–22; Psalms 84:10"> And the gatekeepers:<br />Akkub, Talmon, and their associates, who kept watch at the gates—172 men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Residents Outside Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:3"> The rest of the Israelites, with the priests and Levites, were in all the villages of Judah, each on his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:26; 2 Chronicles 27:3; Nehemiah 3:31"> The temple servants lived on the hill of Ophel, with Ziha and Gishpa over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:42; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; Ezra 8:19"> Now the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Mica.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Mica is a variant of Micaiah; see Nehemiah 12:35.">g</a></sup>He was one of Asaph’s descendants, who were the singers in charge of the service of the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 7:20–24; Nehemiah 12:47; Ezra 6:8–9"> For there was a command from the king concerning the singers, an ordinance regulating their daily activities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 38:30; 1 Chronicles 18:17; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, a descendant of Zerah son of Judah, was the king’s agent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew hand">h</a></sup>in every matter concerning the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 14:15; Joshua 15:21–22"> As for the villages with their fields, some of the people of Judah lived in Kiriath-arba, Dibon, Jekabzeel, and their villages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 15:26–27; Joshua 19:2"> in Jeshua, Moladah, and Beth-pelet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Joshua 15:28; Judges 20:1"> in Hazar-shual; in Beersheba and its villages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; Joshua 15:31"> in Ziklag; in Meconah and its villages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:41; Joshua 15:32–33; Joshua 15:35"> in En-rimmon, Zorah, Jarmuth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 15:8; Joshua 18:16"> Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages; in Lachish and its fields; and in Azekah and its villages. So they settled from Beersheba all the way to the Valley of Hinnom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Joshua 8:9; Joshua 18:13"> The descendants of Benjamin from Geba lived in Michmash, Aija, and Bethel with its villages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; Isaiah 10:30; 1 Samuel 21:1"> in Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 18:25"> Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:18"> Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:12; Nehemiah 7:37; 1 Chronicles 4:14"> Lod, and Ono; and in the Valley of the Craftsmen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew in Ge-harashim">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Genesis 49:7; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> And some divisions of the Levites of Judah settled in Benjamin.</span>
</div>
</div>
