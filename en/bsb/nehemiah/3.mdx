## The Builders of the Walls

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Jeremiah 31:38; John 5:2"> At the Sheep Gate, Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests began rebuilding. They dedicated it and installed its doors. After building as far as the Tower of the Hundred and the Tower of Hananel, they dedicated the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:36; Ezra 2:34; Nehemiah 10:12"> The men of Jericho built next to Eliashib, and Zaccur son of Imri built next to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 12:39; Zephaniah 1:10"> The Fish Gate was rebuilt by the sons of Hassenaah. They laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 8:33; Nehemiah 3:21; Nehemiah 10:7"> Next to them, Meremoth son of Uriah, the son of Hakkoz, made repairs. Next to him, Meshullam son of Berechiah, the son of Meshezabel, made repairs; and next to him, Zadok son of Baana made repairs as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:27; 2 Samuel 14:2; Jeremiah 27:2"> Next to him, the Tekoites made repairs, but their nobles did not put their shoulders to the work under their supervisors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or their Lord or the governor">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39"> The Jeshanah Gate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or The Old City Gate">b</a></sup>was repaired by Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah. They laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Joshua 9:3–27; Nehemiah 3:19"> Next to them, repairs were made by Melatiah the Gibeonite, Jadon the Meronothite, and the men of Gibeon and Mizpah, who were under the authority of the governor of the region west of the Euphrates.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew the governor beyond the River">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:38; Nehemiah 3:31–32; Genesis 50:2"> Next to them, Uzziel son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs. And next to him, Hananiah, one of the perfumers, made repairs. They fortified Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:12; Nehemiah 3:17"> Next to them, Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of a half-district of Jerusalem, made repairs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:28–30; Nehemiah 10:4; Nehemiah 3:23"> next to him, Jedaiah son of Harumaph made repairs across from his house; and next to him, Hattush son of Hashabneiah made repairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:38; Ezra 2:6; Nehemiah 10:14"> Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section, as well as the Tower of the Ovens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:9; Acts 21:8–9; Exodus 35:25"> And next to them, Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of the other half-district of Jerusalem, made repairs, with the help of his daughters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:13; Joshua 15:34; 2 Chronicles 26:9"> The Valley Gate was repaired by Hanun and the residents of Zanoah. They rebuilt it, installed its doors, bolts, and bars, and repaired a thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1,000 cubits is approximately 1,500 feet or 457.2 meters.">d</a></sup>of the wall as far as the Dung Gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Nehemiah 2:13; Nehemiah 3:12–13"> The Dung Gate was repaired by Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccherem. He rebuilt it and installed its doors, bolts, and bars.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:7; Isaiah 8:6; Nehemiah 12:37"> The Fountain Gate was repaired by Shallun<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew; Syriac Shallum">e</a></sup>son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah. He rebuilt it, roofed it, and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Pool of Shelah is another name for the Pool of Siloam.">f</a></sup>near the king’s garden, as far as the stairs that descend from the City of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:29; Nehemiah 3:9; 2 Kings 20:20"> Beyond him, Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of a half-district of Beth-zur, made repairs up to a point opposite the tombs of David, as far as the artificial pool and the House of the Mighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; Nehemiah 3:16; 1 Samuel 23:1–13"> Next to him, the Levites made repairs under Rehum son of Bani, and next to him, Hashabiah, ruler of a half-district of Keilah, made repairs for his district.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Next to him, their countrymen made repairs under Binnui<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Two Hebrew manuscripts and Syriac (see also LXX and Nehemiah 3:24); most Hebrew manuscripts Bavvai">g</a></sup>son of Henadad, ruler of the other half-district of Keilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:9; Nehemiah 10:9; Nehemiah 12:8"> And next to him, Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section opposite the ascent to the armory, near the angle in the wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:1; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Romans 12:11"> Next to him, Baruch son of Zabbai diligently repaired another section, from the angle to the doorway of the house of Eliashib the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:63; Ezra 2:61"> Next to him, Meremoth son of Uriah, the son of Hakkoz, repaired another section, from the doorway of the house of Eliashib to the end of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:28; Nehemiah 6:2"> And next to him, the priests from the surrounding area made repairs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:2; Nehemiah 8:4; Nehemiah 3:29–30"> Beyond them, Benjamin and Hasshub made repairs in front of their house, and next to them, Azariah son of Maaseiah, the son of Ananiah, made repairs beside his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:19; Nehemiah 3:27; Nehemiah 10:9"> After him, Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section, from the house of Azariah to the angle and the corner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 37:21; Nehemiah 12:39"> and Palal son of Uzai made repairs opposite the angle and the tower that juts out from the upper palace of the king near the courtyard of the guard. Next to him, Pedaiah son of Parosh</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:1; Nehemiah 11:21; Nehemiah 12:37"> and the temple servants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew the Nethinim; also in verse 31">h</a></sup>living on the hill of Ophel made repairs opposite the Water Gate toward the east and the tower that juts out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5"> And next to them, the Tekoites repaired another section, from a point opposite the great tower that juts out to the wall of Ophel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:15; Jeremiah 31:40; 2 Kings 11:16"> Above the Horse Gate, each of the priests made repairs in front of his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:2; Ezra 2:37; Nehemiah 7:40"> Next to them, Zadok son of Immer made repairs opposite his house, and next to him, Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the guard of the East Gate, made repairs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Next to him, Hananiah son of Shelemiah, as well as Hanun the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section. Next to them, Meshullam son of Berechiah made repairs opposite his own quarters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:8; Nehemiah 3:32"> Next to him, Malchijah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs as far as the house of the temple servants and the merchants, opposite the Inspection Gate, and as far as the upper room above the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:1; John 5:2; Nehemiah 12:39"> And between the upper room above the corner and the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and merchants made repairs.</span>
</div>
</div>
