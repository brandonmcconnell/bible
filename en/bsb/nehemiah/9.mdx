## The People Confess Their Sins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 13:2–3; Ezra 8:23; Nehemiah 8:2"> On the twenty-fourth day of the same month, the Israelites gathered together, fasting and wearing sackcloth, with dust on their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–10; Nehemiah 13:3; Ezra 10:11"> Those of Israelite descent separated themselves from all the foreigners, and they stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7–8; Nehemiah 8:3–4"> While they stood in their places, they read from the Book of the Law of the LORD their God for a quarter of the day, and they spent another quarter of the day in confession and worship of the LORD their God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7; Nehemiah 12:8; Nehemiah 9:5"> And the Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani—stood on the raised platform and cried out in a loud voice to the LORD their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 103:1–2; 1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> Then the Levites—Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah—said, “Stand up and bless the LORD your God from everlasting to everlasting:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be Your glorious name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and may it be exalted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">above all blessing and praise.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:16; Genesis 1:1; Revelation 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You alone are the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You created the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the highest heavens with all their host,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the earth and all that is on it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the seas and all that is in them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You give life to all things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the host of heaven worships You.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 51:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the God who chose Abram,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who brought him out of Ur of the Chaldeans</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gave him the name Abraham.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Genesis 15:18–21; Hebrews 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">You found his heart faithful before You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and made a covenant with him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to give the land of the Canaanites and Hittites,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the Amorites and Perizzites,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the Jebusites and Girgashites—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to give it to his descendants.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have kept Your promise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because You are righteous.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–9; Acts 7:34; Exodus 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You saw the affliction of our fathers in Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You heard their cry at the Red Sea.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the Sea of Reeds">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:20; Daniel 9:15; Exodus 18:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You performed signs and wonders against Pharaoh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all his officials, and all the people of his land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for You knew they had acted with arrogance</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against our fathers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You made a name for Yourself</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that endures to this day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22; Exodus 14:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">You divided the sea before them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they crossed through it on dry ground.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You hurled their pursuers into the depths</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a stone into raging waters.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 13:21–22; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 107:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You led them with a pillar of cloud by day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a pillar of fire by night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to light for them the way</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in which they should travel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:160; Exodus 20:1; Psalms 119:127–128"> <span className="pl pl-1">You came down on Mount Sinai</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and spoke with them from heaven.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You gave them just ordinances, true laws,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and good statutes and commandments.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–4; Deuteronomy 1:8; Exodus 17:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">In their hunger You gave them bread from heaven;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in their thirst You brought them water from the rock.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You told them to go in and possess the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that You had sworn to give them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Psalms 81:11–14; Hebrews 3:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they and our fathers became arrogant and stiff-necked</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and did not obey Your commandments.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Joel 2:13; Psalms 145:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They refused to listen and failed to remember</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the wonders You performed among them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They stiffened their necks and appointed a leader</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to return them to their bondage in Egypt.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX and a few Hebrew manuscripts; MT to return them to their bondage in their rebellion">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But You are a forgiving God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">gracious and compassionate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">slow to anger and rich in loving devotion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and You did not forsake them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–23; Exodus 32:4–8; Ezekiel 20:7–44"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even when they cast for themselves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an image of a calf and said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘This is your God who brought you up out of Egypt,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and when they committed terrible blasphemies,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Nehemiah 9:27; Malachi 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You in Your great compassion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">did not forsake them in the wilderness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">By day the pillar of cloud never turned away</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from guiding them on their path;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and by the night the pillar of fire</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">illuminated the way they should go.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 8:4; Psalms 34:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For forty years You sustained them in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that they lacked nothing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their clothes did not wear out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their feet did not swell.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 3:1–17; Deuteronomy 2:26–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">You gave them kingdoms and peoples</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and allotted to them every corner of the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So they took the land of Sihon</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="One Hebrew manuscript and LXX; most Hebrew manuscripts Sihon, the country of the">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">king of Heshbon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and of Og king of Bashan.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 27:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You multiplied their descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like the stars of heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and brought them to the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You had told their fathers to enter and possess.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 21:43; Joshua 11:23; Psalms 44:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">So their descendants went in and possessed the land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You subdued before them the Canaanites dwelling in the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You delivered into their hands the kings and peoples of the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to do with them as they wished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Nehemiah 9:35; Deuteronomy 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They captured fortified cities and fertile land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and took houses full of all goods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wells already dug,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">vineyards, olive groves, and fruit trees in abundance.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So they ate and were filled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they grew fat and delighted in Your great goodness.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Judges 2:11–12; Nehemiah 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they were disobedient and rebelled against You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they flung Your law behind their backs.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They killed Your prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who had admonished them to return to You.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They committed terrible blasphemies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 106:41–45; 2 Kings 14:27; 2 Kings 13:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">So You delivered them into the hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of enemies who oppressed them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in their time of distress they cried out to You.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">From heaven You heard them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in Your great compassion You gave them deliverers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who saved them from the hands of their enemies.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Leviticus 18:5; Nehemiah 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">You admonished them to turn back to Your law,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they were arrogant and disobeyed Your commandments.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They sinned against Your ordinances,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by which a man will live if he practices them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They turned a stubborn shoulder;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they stiffened their necks and would not obey.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Isaiah 42:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">You were patient with them for many years,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your Spirit admonished them through Your prophets.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet they would not listen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so You gave them into the hands of the neighboring peoples.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally into the hands of the peoples of the lands">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22; Jeremiah 4:27; Nehemiah 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But in Your great compassion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You did not put an end to them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor did You forsake them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You are a gracious and compassionate God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
