## The Work Ridiculed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:10; Hebrews 11:36; Psalms 35:15–16"> Now when Sanballat heard that we were rebuilding the wall, he was furious and filled with indignation. He ridiculed the Jews</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:9–10; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Ezekiel 37:3–13"> before his associates and the army of Samaria, saying, “What are these feeble Jews doing? Can they restore the wall by themselves?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Will they commit themselves to God?">a</a></sup>Will they offer sacrifices? Will they complete it in a day? Can they bring these burnt stones back to life from the mounds of rubble?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 2:10"> Then Tobiah the Ammonite, who was beside him, said, “If even a fox were to climb up on what they are building, it would break down their wall of stones!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 79:12; Psalms 123:3–4; Proverbs 3:34"> Hear us, O God, for we are despised. Turn their scorn back upon their own heads, and let them be taken as plunder to a land of captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 109:14; Jeremiah 18:23; Psalms 69:27–28"> Do not cover up their iniquity or let their sin be blotted out from Your sight, for they have provoked the builders.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or have provoked You to anger before the builders">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Nehemiah 6:15; Hebrews 13:21"> So we rebuilt the wall until all of it was joined together up to half its height, for the people had a mind to work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:10; Nehemiah 4:1; Acts 5:33"> When Sanballat and Tobiah, together with the Arabs, Ammonites, and Ashdodites, heard that the repair to the walls of Jerusalem was progressing and that the gaps were being closed, they were furious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–11; Isaiah 8:9–10; Jeremiah 20:10"> and all of them conspired to come and fight against Jerusalem and create a hindrance.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Discouragement Overcome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Acts 4:24–30; Psalms 50:15"> So we prayed to our God and posted a guard against them day and night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:1–2; Haggai 1:2; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> Meanwhile, the people of Judah said:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or Meanwhile, Judah said">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The strength of the laborer fails,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there is so much rubble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that we will never be able</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to rebuild the wall.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2; Acts 23:12; Psalms 56:6"> And our enemies said, “Before they know or see a thing, we will come into their midst, kill them, and put an end to the work.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Genesis 31:41; Numbers 14:22"> At that time the Jews who lived nearby came and told us ten times over, “Wherever you turn, they will attack us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:17–18; Ephesians 6:11–20; Song 3:7–8"> So I stationed men behind the lowest sections of the wall, at the vulnerable areas. I stationed them by families with their swords, spears, and bows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:12; Numbers 14:9; Isaiah 41:10–14"> After I had made an inspection, I stood up and said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, “Do not be afraid of them. Remember the Lord, who is great and awesome, and fight for your brothers, your sons and your daughters, your wives and your homes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Romans 12:11; Job 5:12–13"> When our enemies heard that we were aware of their scheme and that God had frustrated it, each of us returned to his own work on the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:23; Psalms 101:6; Nehemiah 5:15–16"> And from that day on, half of my servants did the work while the other half held spears, shields, bows, and armor.<br />The officers stationed themselves behind all the people of Judah</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; 2 Timothy 4:7; 2 Timothy 2:3"> who were rebuilding the wall. The laborers who carried materials worked with one hand and held a weapon with the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–17"> And each of the builders worked with his sword strapped at his side. But the trumpeter stayed beside me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then I said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people: “The work is great and extensive, and we are spread out far from one another along the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 1:30; Deuteronomy 20:4"> Wherever you hear the sound of the horn, rally to us there. Our God will fight for us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Colossians 1:29; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So we continued the work, while half of the men held spears from the break of dawn until the stars came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1–2"> At that time I also said to the people, “Let every man and his servant spend the night inside Jerusalem, so that they can stand guard by night and work by day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> So neither I nor my brothers nor my servants nor the guards with me changed out of our clothes; each carried his weapon, even to go for water.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or each carried his weapon in his right hand">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
