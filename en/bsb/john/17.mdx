## Prayer for the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:4–5; Philippians 2:9–11"> When Jesus had spoken these things, He lifted up His eyes to heaven and said, “Father, the hour has come. Glorify Your Son, that Your Son may glorify You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:28–29; John 6:39; Daniel 7:14"> For You granted Him authority over all people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally all flesh">a</a></sup>so that He may give eternal life to all those You have given Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:20; 1 John 5:11–12; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> Now this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom You have sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> I have glorified You on earth by accomplishing the work You gave Me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:15–17; John 10:30"> And now, Father, glorify Me in Your presence with the glory I had with You before the world existed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prayer for the Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> I have revealed Your name to those You have given Me out of the world. They were Yours; You gave them to Me, and they have kept Your word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> Now they know that everything You have given Me comes from You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> For I have given them the words You gave Me, and they have received them. They knew with certainty that I came from You, and they believed that You sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> I ask on their behalf. I do not ask on behalf of the world, but on behalf of those You have given Me; for they are Yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> All I have is Yours, and all You have is Mine; and in them I have been glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> I will no longer be in the world, but they are in the world, and I am coming to You.<br />Holy Father, protect them by Your name, the name You gave Me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally Your name, which You gave Me; TR Your name. These You have given Me; similarly in verse 12">b</a></sup>so that they may be one as We are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> While I was with them, I protected and preserved them by Your name, the name You gave Me. Not one of them has been lost, except the son of destruction, so that the Scripture would be fulfilled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> But now I am coming to You; and I am saying these things while I am in the world, so that they may have My joy fulfilled within them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> I have given them Your word and the world has hated them. For they are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> I am not asking that You take them out of the world, but that You keep them from the evil one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or from evil">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> Sanctify them by the truth; Your word is truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> As You sent Me into the world, I have also sent them into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> For them I sanctify Myself, so that they too may be sanctified by the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prayer for All Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> I am not asking on behalf of them alone, but also on behalf of those who will believe in Me through their message,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> that all of them may be one, as You, Father, are in Me, and I am in You. May they also be in Us, so that the world may believe that You sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> I have given them the glory You gave Me, so that they may be one as We are one—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> I in them and You in Me—that they may be perfectly united, so that the world may know that You sent Me and have loved them just as You have loved Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> Father, I want those You have given Me to be with Me where I am, that they may see the glory You gave Me because You loved Me before the foundation of the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> Righteous Father, although the world has not known You, I know You, and they know that You sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 17:23; John 17:6; John 15:9"> And I have made Your name known to them and will continue to make it known, so that the love You have for Me may be in them, and I in them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
