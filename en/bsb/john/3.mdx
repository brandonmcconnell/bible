## Jesus and Nicodemus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a leader of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> He came to Jesus at night and said, “Rabbi, we know that You are a teacher who has come from God. For no one could perform the signs You are doing if God were not with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Jesus replied, “Truly, truly, I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God unless he is born again.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or born from above; also in verse 7.">a</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> “How can a man be born when he is old?” Nicodemus asked. “Can he enter his mother’s womb a second time to be born?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, I tell you, no one can enter the kingdom of God unless he is born of water and the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> Flesh is born of flesh, but spirit is born of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Do not be amazed that I said, ‘You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Greek word for you is plural; also in verse 12.">b</a></sup>must be born again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The wind blows where it wishes. You hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> “How can this be?” Nicodemus asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> “You are Israel’s teacher,” said Jesus, “and you do not understand these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> Truly, truly, I tell you, we speak of what we know, and we testify to what we have seen, and yet you people do not accept our testimony.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If I have told you about earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you about heavenly things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> No one has ascended into heaven except the One who descended from heaven—the Son of Man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include who is in heaven.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> that everyone who believes in Him may have eternal life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or everyone who believes may have eternal life in Him.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or only begotten or unique; also in verse 18">e</a></sup>Son, that everyone who believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> Whoever believes in Him is not condemned, but whoever does not believe has already been condemned, because he has not believed in the name of God’s one and only Son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> And this is the verdict: The Light has come into the world, but men loved the darkness rather than the Light because their deeds were evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> Everyone who does evil hates the Light, and does not come into the Light for fear that his deeds will be exposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalms 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> But whoever practices the truth comes into the Light, so that it may be seen clearly that what he has done has been accomplished in God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some translators close this quotation after verse 15.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## John’s Testimony about Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After this, Jesus and His disciples went into the Judean countryside, where He spent some time with them and baptized.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> Now John was also baptizing at Aenon near Salim, because the water was plentiful there, and people kept coming to be baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> (For John had not yet been thrown into prison.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> Then a dispute arose between John’s disciples and a certain Jew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="TR and the Jews">g</a></sup>over the issue of ceremonial washing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> So John’s disciples came to him and said, “Look, Rabbi, the One who was with you beyond the Jordan, the One you testified about—He is baptizing, and everyone is going to Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> John replied, “A man can receive only what is given him from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> You yourselves can testify that I said, ‘I am not the Christ, but am sent ahead of Him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> The bride belongs to the bridegroom. The friend of the bridegroom stands and listens for him, and is overjoyed to hear the bridegroom’s voice. That joy is mine, and it is now complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalms 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> He must increase; I must decrease.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> The One who comes from above is above all. The one who is from the earth belongs to the earth and speaks as one from the earth. The One who comes from heaven is above all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Tischendorf The One comes from heaven.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> He testifies to what He has seen and heard, yet no one accepts His testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> Whoever accepts His testimony has certified that God is truthful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> For the One whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for God gives the Spirit without limit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in His hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life. Whoever rejects the Son will not see life. Instead, the wrath of God remains on him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some translators close this quotation after verse 30.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
