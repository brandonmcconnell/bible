## The Pool of Bethesda

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> Some time later there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Now there is in Jerusalem near the Sheep Gate a pool with five covered colonnades, which in Hebrew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in Aramaic">a</a></sup>is called Bethesda.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NA, NE, and WH Bethzatha">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> On these walkways<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally In these">c</a></sup>lay a great number of the sick, the blind, the lame, and the paralyzed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="NE, BYZ, and TR include awaiting the moving of the waters. 4 For from time to time an angel descended into the pool and stirred the water. As soon as it was stirred, the first to enter the pool would be healed of his disease.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> One man there had been an invalid for thirty-eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> When Jesus saw him lying there and realized that he had spent a long time in this condition, He asked him, “Do you want to get well?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> “Sir,” the invalid replied, “I have no one to help me into the pool when the water is stirred. While I am on my way, someone else goes in before me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Then Jesus told him, “Get up, pick up your mat, and walk.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> Immediately the man was made well, and he picked up his mat and began to walk.<br />Now this happened on the Sabbath day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> so the Jews said to the man who had been healed, “This is the Sabbath! It is unlawful for you to carry your mat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> But he answered, “The man who made me well told me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> “Who is this man who told you to pick it up and walk?” they asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> But the man who was healed did not know who it was, for Jesus had slipped away while the crowd was there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> Afterward, Jesus found the man at the temple and said to him, “See, you have been made well. Stop sinning, or something worse may happen to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> And the man went away and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him well.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Father and the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> Now because Jesus was doing these things on the Sabbath, the Jews began to persecute Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> But Jesus answered them, “To this very day My Father is at His work, and I too am working.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> Because of this, the Jews tried all the harder to kill Him. Not only was He breaking the Sabbath, but He was even calling God His own Father, making Himself equal with God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> So Jesus replied, “Truly, truly, I tell you, the Son can do nothing by Himself, unless He sees the Father doing it. For whatever the Father does, the Son also does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> The Father loves the Son and shows Him all He does. And to your amazement, He will show Him even greater works than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For just as the Father raises the dead and gives them life, so also the Son gives life to whom He wishes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Furthermore, the Father judges no one, but has assigned all judgment to the Son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> so that all may honor the Son just as they honor the Father. Whoever does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever hears My word and believes Him who sent Me has eternal life and will not come under judgment. Indeed, he has crossed over from death to life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> Truly, truly, I tell you, the hour is coming and has now come when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who hear will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For as the Father has life in Himself, so also He has granted the Son to have life in Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> And He has given Him authority to execute judgment, because He is the Son of Man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Do not be amazed at this, for the hour is coming when all who are in their graves will hear His voice</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and come out—those who have done good to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil to the resurrection of judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Daniel 12:2.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I can do nothing by Myself; I judge only as I hear. And My judgment is just, because I do not seek My own will, but the will of Him who sent Me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Testimonies about Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> If I testify about Myself, My testimony is not valid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> There is another who testifies about Me, and I know that His testimony about Me is valid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> You have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> Even though I do not accept human testimony, I say these things so that you may be saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> John was a lamp that burned and gave light, and you were willing for a season to bask in his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> But I have testimony more substantial than that of John. For the works that the Father has given Me to accomplish—the very works I am doing—testify about Me that the Father has sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> And the Father who sent Me has Himself testified about Me. You have never heard His voice nor seen His form,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> nor does His word abide in you, because you do not believe the One He sent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Witness of Scripture

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> You pore over the Scriptures because you presume that by them you possess eternal life. These are the very words that testify about Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> yet you refuse to come to Me to have life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> I do not accept glory from men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> but I know you, that you do not have the love of God within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> I have come in My Father’s name, and you have not received Me; but if someone else comes in his own name, you will receive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How can you believe if you accept glory from one another, yet do not seek the glory that comes from the only God?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Do not think that I will accuse you before the Father. Your accuser is Moses, in whom you have put your hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> If you had believed Moses, you would believe Me, because he wrote about Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But since you do not believe what he wrote, how will you believe what I say?”</span>
</div>
</div>
