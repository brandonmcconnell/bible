## Jesus Teaches at the Feast

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> After this, Jesus traveled throughout Galilee. He did not want to travel in Judea, because the Jews there were trying to kill Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> However, the Jewish Feast of Tabernacles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as the Feast of Booths or the Feast of Shelters and originally called the Feast of Ingathering (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).">a</a></sup>was near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> So Jesus’ brothers said to Him, “Leave here and go to Judea, so that Your disciples there may see the works You are doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> For no one who wants to be known publicly acts in secret. Since You are doing these things, show Yourself to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For even His own brothers did not believe in Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Therefore Jesus told them, “Although your time is always at hand, My time has not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world cannot hate you, but it hates Me, because I testify that its works are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Go up to the feast on your own. I am not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NE, WH, BYZ, and TR I am not yet">b</a></sup>going up to this feast, because My time has not yet come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Having said this, Jesus remained in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But after His brothers had gone up to the feast, He also went—not publicly, but in secret.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> So the Jews were looking for Him at the feast and asking, “Where is He?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> Many in the crowds were whispering about Him. Some said, “He is a good man.”<br />But others replied, “No, He deceives the people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> Yet no one would speak publicly about Him for fear of the Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> About halfway through the feast, Jesus went up to the temple courts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally the temple; also in verse 28">c</a></sup>and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> The Jews were amazed and asked, “How did this man attain such learning without having studied?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> “My teaching is not My own,” Jesus replied. “It comes from Him who sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If anyone desires to do His will, he will know whether My teaching is from God or whether I speak on My own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> He who speaks on his own authority seeks his own glory, but He who seeks the glory of the One who sent Him is a man of truth; in Him there is no falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Has not Moses given you the law? Yet not one of you keeps it. Why are you trying to kill Me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> “You have a demon,” the crowd replied. “Who is trying to kill You?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered them, “I did one miracle, and you are all amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> But because Moses gave you circumcision, you circumcise a boy on the Sabbath (not that it is from Moses, but from the patriarchs.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a boy can be circumcised on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses will not be broken, why are you angry with Me for making the whole man well on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Stop judging by outward appearances, and start judging justly.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Is Jesus the Christ?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Then some of the people of Jerusalem began to say, “Isn’t this the man they are trying to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> Yet here He is, speaking publicly, and they are not saying anything to Him. Have the rulers truly recognized that this is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But we know where this man is from. When the Christ comes, no one will know where He is from.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then Jesus, still teaching in the temple courts, cried out, “You know Me, and you know where I am from. I have not come of My own accord, but He who sent Me is true. You do not know Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> but I know Him, because I am from Him and He sent Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> So they tried to seize Him, but no one laid a hand on Him, because His hour had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> Many in the crowd, however, believed in Him and said, “When the Christ comes, will He perform more signs than this man?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> When the Pharisees heard the crowd whispering these things about Jesus, they and the chief priests sent officers to arrest Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> So Jesus said, “I am with you only a little while longer, and then I am going to the One who sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> You will look for Me, but you will not find Me; and where I am, you cannot come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> At this, the Jews said to one another, “Where does He intend to go that we will not find Him? Will He go where the Jews are dispersed among the Greeks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally Will He go to the Diaspora among the Greeks; the Jewish people living outside the land of Israel since the Babylonian exile were referred to as the Diaspora.">d</a></sup>and teach the Greeks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What does He mean by saying, ‘You will look for Me, but you will not find Me,’ and, ‘Where I am, you cannot come’?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Living Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> On the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood up and called out in a loud voice, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to Me and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> Whoever believes in Me, as the Scripture has said: ‘Streams of living water will flow from within him.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> He was speaking about the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were later to receive. For the Spirit had not yet been given,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally the Spirit was not yet; BYZ and TR the Holy Spirit was not yet">e</a></sup>because Jesus had not yet been glorified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Division over Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> On hearing these words, some of the people said, “This is truly the Prophet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others declared, “This is the Christ.”<br />But still others asked, “How can the Christ come from Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Doesn’t the Scripture say that the Christ will come from the line of David and from Bethlehem, the village where David lived?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Micah 5:2.">f</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So there was division in the crowd because of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> Some of them wanted to seize Him, but no one laid a hand on Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Unbelief of the Jewish Leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> Then the officers returned to the chief priests and Pharisees, who asked them, “Why didn’t you bring Him in?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> “Never has anyone spoken like this man!” the officers answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> “Have you also been deceived?” replied the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> “Have any of the rulers or Pharisees believed in Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this crowd that does not know the law—they are under a curse.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus, who had gone to Jesus earlier and who himself was one of them, asked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> “Does our law convict a man without first hearing from him to determine what he has done?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> “Aren’t you also from Galilee?” they replied. “Look into it, and you will see that no prophet comes out of Galilee.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some early manuscripts do not include John 7:53 through John 8:11.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> Then each went to his own home.</span>
</div>
</div>
