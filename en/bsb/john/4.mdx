## Jesus and the Samaritan Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> When Jesus realized that the Pharisees were aware He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally When therefore Jesus knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus; NE, WH, BYZ, and TR When therefore the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus">a</a></sup>was gaining and baptizing more disciples than John</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (although it was not Jesus who baptized, but His disciples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> He left Judea and returned to Galilee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> Now He had to pass through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> So He came to a town of Samaria called Sychar, near the plot of ground that Jacob had given to his son Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> Since Jacob’s well was there, Jesus, weary from His journey, sat down by the well. It was about the sixth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, about noon">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> When a Samaritan woman came to draw water, Jesus said to her, “Give Me a drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> (His disciples had gone into the town to buy food.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> “You are a Jew,” said the woman. “How can You ask for a drink from me, a Samaritan woman?” (For Jews do not associate with Samaritans.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Tischendorf does not include this sentence.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered, “If you knew the gift of God and who is asking you for a drink, you would have asked Him, and He would have given you living water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> “Sir,” the woman replied, “You have nothing to draw with and the well is deep. Where then will You get this living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Are You greater than our father Jacob, who gave us this well and drank from it himself, as did his sons and his livestock?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus said to her, “Everyone who drinks this water will be thirsty again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> But whoever drinks the water I give him will never thirst. Indeed, the water I give him will become in him a fount of water springing up to eternal life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman said to Him, “Sir, give me this water so that I will not get thirsty and have to keep coming here to draw water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus told her, “Go, call your husband and come back.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> “I have no husband,” the woman replied.<br />Jesus said to her, “You are correct to say that you have no husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> In fact, you have had five husbands, and the man you now have is not your husband. You have spoken truthfully.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> “Sir,” the woman said, “I see that You are a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, but you Jews say that the place where one must worship is in Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> “Believe Me, woman,” Jesus replied, “a time is coming when you will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> You worship what you do not know; we worship what we do know, for salvation is from the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But a time is coming and has now come when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth, for the Father is seeking such as these to worship Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is Spirit, and His worshipers must worship Him in spirit and in truth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman said, “I know that Messiah” (called Christ) “is coming. When He comes, He will explain everything to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus answered, “I who speak to you am He.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Disciples Return and Marvel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> Just then His disciples returned and were surprised that He was speaking with a woman. But no one asked Him, “What do You want from her?” or “Why are You talking with her?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> Then the woman left her water jar, went back into the town, and said to the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> “Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did. Could this be the Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> So they left the town and made their way toward Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> Meanwhile the disciples urged Him, “Rabbi, eat something.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But He told them, “I have food to eat that you know nothing about.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> So the disciples asked one another, “Could someone have brought Him food?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus explained, “My food is to do the will of Him who sent Me and to finish His work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Do you not say, ‘There are still four months until the harvest’? I tell you, lift up your eyes and look at the fields, for they are ripe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally white">d</a></sup>for harvest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> Already the reaper draws his wages and gathers a crop for eternal life, so that the sower and the reaper may rejoice together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For in this case the saying ‘One sows and another reaps’ is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap what you have not worked for; others have done the hard work, and now you have taken up their labor.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Many Samaritans Believe

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> Many of the Samaritans from that town believed in Jesus because of the woman’s testimony, “He told me everything I ever did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the Samaritans came to Him, they asked Him to stay with them, and He stayed two days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And many more believed because of His message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> They said to the woman, “We now believe not only because of your words; we have heard for ourselves, and we know that this man truly is the Savior of the world.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals the Official’s Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> After two days, Jesus left for Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> Now He Himself had testified that a prophet has no honor in his own hometown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> Yet when He arrived, the Galileans welcomed Him. They had seen all the great things He had done in Jerusalem at the feast, for they had gone there as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> So once again He came to Cana in Galilee, where He had turned the water into wine. And there was a royal official whose son lay sick at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> When he heard that Jesus had come from Judea to Galilee, he went and begged Him to come down and heal his son, who was about to die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Jesus said to him, “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will never believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> “Sir,” the official said, “come down before my child dies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> “Go,” said Jesus. “Your son will live.”<br />The man took Jesus at His word and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> And while he was still on the way, his servants met him with the news that his boy was alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So he inquired as to the hour when his son had recovered, and they told him, “The fever left him yesterday at the seventh hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, one in the afternoon">e</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> Then the father realized that this was the very hour in which Jesus had told him, “Your son will live.” And he and all his household believed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This was now the second sign that Jesus performed after coming from Judea into Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>
