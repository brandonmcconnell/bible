## The Feeding of the Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:31–44; John 6:23; Luke 5:1"> After this, Jesus crossed to the other side of the Sea of Galilee (that is, the Sea of Tiberias).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Matthew 12:15; Matthew 15:30–31"> A large crowd followed Him because they saw the signs He was performing on the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> Then Jesus went up on the mountain and sat down with His disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> Now the Jewish Feast of the Passover was near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> When Jesus looked up and saw a large crowd coming toward Him, He said to Philip, “Where can we buy bread for these people to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> But He was asking this to test him, for He knew what He was about to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> Philip answered, “Two hundred denarii<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.">a</a></sup>would not buy enough bread for each of them to have a small piece.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> One of His disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> “Here is a boy with five barley loaves and two small fish. But what difference will these make among so many?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> “Have the people sit down,” Jesus said. Now there was plenty of grass in that place, so the men sat down, about five thousand of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> Then Jesus took the loaves and the fish, gave thanks, and distributed to those who were seated as much as they wanted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> And when everyone was full, He said to His disciples, “Gather the pieces that are left over, so that nothing will be wasted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> So they collected them and filled twelve baskets with the pieces of the five barley loaves left over by those who had eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> When the people saw the sign that Jesus had performed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SBL, NA, NE, and WH He had performed">b</a></sup>they began to say, “Truly this is the Prophet who is to come into the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> Then Jesus, realizing that they were about to come and make Him king by force, withdrew again to a mountain by Himself.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Walks on Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When evening came, His disciples went down to the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> got into a boat, and started across the sea to Capernaum. It was already dark, and Jesus had not yet gone out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:25; Psalms 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> A strong wind was blowing, and the sea grew agitated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> When they had rowed about three or four miles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek about twenty-five or thirty stadia; that is, approximately 2.87 to 3.45 miles (4.62 to 5.55 kilometers)">c</a></sup>they saw Jesus approaching the boat, walking on the sea—and they were terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> But Jesus spoke up: “It is I; do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> Then they were willing to take Him into the boat, and at once the boat reached the shore where they were heading.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus the Bread of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> The next day, the crowd that had remained on the other side of the sea realized that only one boat had been there, and that Jesus had not boarded it with His disciples, but they had gone away alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> However, some boats from Tiberias landed near the place where the people had eaten the bread after the Lord had given thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> So when the crowd saw that neither Jesus nor His disciples were there, they got into the boats and went to Capernaum to look for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> When they found Him on the other side of the sea, they asked Him, “Rabbi, when did You get here?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus replied, “Truly, truly, I tell you, it is not because you saw these signs that you are looking for Me, but because you ate the loaves and had your fill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> Do not work for food that perishes, but for food that endures to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you. For on Him God the Father has placed His seal of approval.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> Then they inquired, “What must we do to perform the works of God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus replied, “The work of God is this: to believe in the One He has sent.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> So they asked Him, “What sign then will You perform, so that we may see it and believe You? What will You do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, as it is written: ‘He gave them bread from heaven to eat.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Psalms 78:24; see also Exodus 16:4.">d</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, it was not Moses who gave you the bread from heaven, but it is My Father who gives you the true bread from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> For the bread of God is He who comes down from heaven and gives life to the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalms 4:6"> “Sir,” they said, “give us this bread at all times.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> Jesus answered, “I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to Me will never hunger, and whoever believes in Me will never thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> But as I stated, you have seen Me and still you do not believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> Everyone the Father gives Me will come to Me, and the one who comes to Me I will never drive away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> For I have come down from heaven, not to do My own will, but to do the will of Him who sent Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> And this is the will of Him who sent Me, that I shall lose none of those He has given Me, but raise them up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> For it is My Father’s will that everyone who looks to the Son and believes in Him shall have eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> At this, the Jews began to grumble about Jesus because He had said, “I am the bread that came down from heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> They were asking, “Is this not Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How then can He say, ‘I have come down from heaven?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> “Stop grumbling among yourselves,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> “No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him, and I will raise him up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> It is written in the Prophets: ‘And they will all be taught by God.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isaiah 54:13">e</a></sup>Everyone who has heard the Father and learned from Him comes to Me—</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> not that anyone has seen the Father except the One who is from God; only He has seen the Father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> Truly, truly, I tell you, he who believes has eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I am the bread of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, yet they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> This is the bread that comes down from heaven, so that anyone may eat of it and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. And this bread, which I will give for the life of the world, is My flesh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> At this, the Jews began to argue among themselves, “How can this man give us His flesh to eat?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> So Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, unless you eat the flesh and drink the blood of the Son of Man, you have no life in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> For My flesh is real food, and My blood is real drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood remains in Me, and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> Just as the living Father sent Me and I live because of the Father, so also the one who feeds on Me will live because of Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> This is the bread that came down from heaven. Unlike your fathers, who ate the manna and died, the one who eats this bread will live forever.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Many Disciples Turn Back

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> Jesus said this while teaching in the synagogue in Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> On hearing it, many of His disciples said, “This is a difficult teaching. Who can accept it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> Aware that His disciples were grumbling about this teaching, Jesus asked them, “Does this offend you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> Then what will happen if you see the Son of Man ascend to where He was before?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> The Spirit gives life; the flesh profits nothing. The words I have spoken to you are spirit and they are life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> However, there are some of you who do not believe.” (For Jesus had known from the beginning which of them did not believe and who would betray Him.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> Then Jesus said, “This is why I told you that no one can come to Me unless the Father has granted it to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Luke 9:62; 1 John 2:19; Hebrews 10:38"> From that time on many of His disciples turned back and no longer walked with Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Confession of Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> So Jesus asked the Twelve, “Do you want to leave too?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Simon Peter replied, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="John 11:27; Luke 9:20; Mark 8:29"> We believe and know that You are the Holy One of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR You are the Christ, the Son of the living God">f</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Jesus answered them, “Have I not chosen you, the Twelve? Yet one of you is a devil!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:14–16; Jude 1:4; Acts 1:16–20"> He was speaking about Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot. For although Judas was one of the Twelve, he was later to betray Jesus.</span>
</div>
