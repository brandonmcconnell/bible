## The Beginning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was with God in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> Through Him all things were made, and without Him nothing was made that has been made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> In Him was life, and that life was the light of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> The Light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or comprehended">a</a></sup>it.</span>
</div>

## The Witness of John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> There came a man who was sent from God. His name was John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> He came as a witness to testify about the Light, so that through him everyone might believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> He himself was not the Light, but he came to testify about the Light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> The true Light, who gives light to everyone, was coming into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, and though the world was made through Him, the world did not recognize Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came to His own, and His own did not receive Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> But to all who did receive Him, to those who believed in His name, He gave the right to become children of God—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> children born not of blood, nor of the desire or will of man, but born of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Word Became Flesh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or and tabernacled among us">b</a></sup>We have seen His glory, the glory of the one and only Son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or the Only Begotten or the Unique One">c</a></sup>from the Father, full of grace and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John testified concerning Him. He cried out, saying, “This is He of whom I said, ‘He who comes after me has surpassed me because He was before me.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> From His fullness we have all received grace upon grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No one has ever seen God, but the one and only Son, who is Himself God and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or but the only begotten God, who; BYZ and TR but the only begotten Son, who">d</a></sup>is at the Father’s side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Greek in the Father’s bosom">e</a></sup>has made Him known.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Mission of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> And this was John’s testimony when the Jews of Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to ask him, “Who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> He did not refuse to confess, but openly declared, “I am not the Christ.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> “Then who are you?” they inquired. “Are you Elijah?”<br />He said, “I am not.”<br />“Are you the Prophet?”<br />He answered, “No.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> So they said to him, “Who are you? We need an answer for those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> John replied in the words of Isaiah the prophet:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am a voice of one calling in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Make straight the way for the Lord.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isaiah 40:3 (see also LXX)">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> Then the Pharisees who had been sent</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> asked him, “Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> “I baptize with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or in; also in verse 31 and twice in 33">g</a></sup>water,” John replied, “but among you stands One you do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> He is the One who comes after me, the straps of whose sandals I am not worthy to untie.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> All this happened at Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus the Lamb of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said, “Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This is He of whom I said, ‘A man who comes after me has surpassed me because He was before me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> I myself did not know Him, but the reason I came baptizing with water was that He might be revealed to Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> Then John testified, “I saw the Spirit descending from heaven like a dove and resting on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> I myself did not know Him, but the One who sent me to baptize with water told me, ‘The man on whom you see the Spirit descend and rest is He who will baptize with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 1:49; Psalms 89:26–27"> I have seen and testified that this is the Son of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="SBL the Chosen One of God">h</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> The next day John was there again with two of his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> When he saw Jesus walking by, he said, “Look, the Lamb of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> And when the two disciples heard him say this, they followed Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> Jesus turned and saw them following. “What do you want?” He asked.<br />They said to Him, “Rabbi” (which means Teacher), “where are You staying?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> “Come and see,” He replied. So they went and saw where He was staying, and spent that day with Him. It was about the tenth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, about four in the afternoon">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of the two who heard John’s testimony and followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalms 89:20"> He first found his brother Simon and told him, “We have found the Messiah” (which is translated as Christ).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> Andrew brought him to Jesus, who looked at him and said, “You are Simon son of John. You will be called Cephas” (which is translated as Peter).</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calls Philip and Nathanael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> The next day Jesus decided to set out for Galilee. Finding Philip, He told him, “Follow Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> Now Philip was from Bethsaida, the same town as Andrew and Peter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael and told him, “We have found the One Moses wrote about in the Law, the One the prophets foretold—Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> “Can anything good come from Nazareth?” Nathanael asked.<br />“Come and see,” said Philip.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> When Jesus saw Nathanael approaching, He said of him, “Here is a true Israelite, in whom there is no deceit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> “How do You know me?” Nathanael asked.<br />Jesus replied, “Before Philip called you, I saw you under the fig tree.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> “Rabbi,” Nathanael answered, “You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus said to him, “Do you believe just because I told you I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> Then He declared, “Truly, truly, I tell you, you will all see heaven open and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Genesis 28:12.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
