## The Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> Early on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Now on the first of the Sabbaths, early,">a</a></sup>while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene went to the tomb and saw that the stone had been removed from the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> So she came running to Simon Peter and the other disciple, the one whom Jesus loved. “They have taken the Lord out of the tomb,” she said, “and we do not know where they have put Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Then Peter and the other disciple set out for the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The two were running together, but the other disciple outran Peter and reached the tomb first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> He bent down and looked in at the linen cloths lying there, but he did not go in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Simon Peter arrived just after him. He entered the tomb and saw the linen cloths lying there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> The cloth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek soudarion">b</a></sup>that had been around Jesus’ head was rolled up, lying separate from the linen cloths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then the other disciple, who had reached the tomb first, also went in. And he saw and believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For they still did not understand from the Scripture that Jesus had to rise from the dead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Appears to Mary Magdalene

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> Then the disciples returned to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary stood outside the tomb weeping. And as she wept, she bent down to look into the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> and she saw two angels in white sitting where the body of Jesus had lain, one at the head and the other at the feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> “Woman, why are you weeping?” they asked.<br />“Because they have taken my Lord away,” she said, “and I do not know where they have put Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> When she had said this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing there. But she did not recognize that it was Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> “Woman, why are you weeping?” Jesus asked. “Whom are you seeking?”<br />Thinking He was the gardener, she said, “Sir, if you have carried Him off, tell me where you have put Him, and I will get Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus said to her, “Mary.”<br />She turned and said to Him in Hebrew,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or in Aramaic">c</a></sup>“Rabboni!” (which means “Teacher”).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> “Do not cling to Me,” Jesus said, “for I have not yet ascended to the Father. But go and tell My brothers, ‘I am ascending to My Father and your Father, to My God and your God.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary Magdalene went and announced to the disciples, “I have seen the Lord!” And she told them what He had said to her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Appears to the Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> It was the first day of the week, and that very evening, while the disciples were together with the doors locked for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood among them. “Peace be with you!” He said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> After He had said this, He showed them His hands and His side.<br />The disciples rejoiced when they saw the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Again Jesus said to them, “Peace be with you. As the Father has sent Me, so also I am sending you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> When He had said this, He breathed on them and said, “Receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> If you forgive anyone his sins, they are forgiven; if you withhold forgiveness from anyone, it is withheld.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Appears to Thomas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> Now Thomas called Didymus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Didymus means the twin.">d</a></sup>one of the Twelve, was not with the disciples when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> So the other disciples told him, “We have seen the Lord!”<br />But he replied, “Unless I see the nail marks in His hands, and put my finger where the nails have been, and put my hand into His side, I will never believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> Eight days later, His disciples were once again inside with the doors locked, and Thomas was with them. Jesus came and stood among them and said, “Peace be with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then Jesus said to Thomas, “Put your finger here and look at My hands. Reach out your hand and put it into My side. Stop doubting and believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas replied, “My Lord and my God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus said to him, “Because you have seen Me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Purpose of John’s Book

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Jesus performed many other signs in the presence of His disciples, which are not written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> But these are written so that you may believe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or may continue to believe">e</a></sup>that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in His name.</span>
</div>
