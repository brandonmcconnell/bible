## The Death of Lazarus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> At this time a man named Lazarus was sick. He lived in Bethany, the village of Mary and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> (Mary, whose brother Lazarus was sick, was to anoint the Lord with perfume and wipe His feet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally was the one having anointed the Lord with fragrant oil and having wiped His feet; see John 12:3.">a</a></sup>with her hair.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> So the sisters sent word to Jesus, “Lord, the one You love is sick.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Jesus heard this, He said, “This sickness will not end in death. No, it is for the glory of God, so that the Son of God may be glorified through it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> So on hearing that Lazarus was sick, He stayed where He was for two days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> and then He said to the disciples, “Let us go back to Judea.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> “Rabbi,” they replied, “the Jews just tried to stone You, and You are going back there?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered, “Are there not twelve hours of daylight? If anyone walks in the daytime, he will not stumble, because he sees by the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if anyone walks at night, he will stumble, because he has no light.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> After He had said this, He told them, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I am going there to wake him up.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> His disciples replied, “Lord, if he is sleeping, he will get better.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> They thought that Jesus was talking about actual sleep, but He was speaking about the death of Lazarus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> So Jesus told them plainly, “Lazarus is dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> and for your sake I am glad I was not there, so that you may believe. But let us go to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Then Thomas called Didymus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Didymus means the twin.">b</a></sup>said to his fellow disciples, “Let us also go, so that we may die with Him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Comforts Martha and Mary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> When Jesus arrived, He found that Lazarus had already spent four days in the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, a little less than two miles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek about fifteen stadia; that is, approximately 1.72 miles or 2.78 kilometers">c</a></sup>away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> and many of the Jews had come to Martha and Mary to console them in the loss of their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> So when Martha heard that Jesus was coming, she went out to meet Him, but Mary stayed at home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> But even now I know that God will give You whatever You ask of Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> “Your brother will rise again,” Jesus told her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha replied, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in Me will live, even though he dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> And everyone who lives and believes in Me will never die. Do you believe this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> “Yes, Lord,” she answered, “I believe that You are the Christ, the Son of God, who was to come into the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> After Martha had said this, she went back and called her sister Mary aside to tell her, “The Teacher is here and is asking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song 3:1–4"> And when Mary heard this, she got up quickly and went to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Now Jesus had not yet entered the village, but was still at the place where Martha had met Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> When the Jews who were in the house consoling Mary saw how quickly she got up and went out, they followed her, supposing she was going to the tomb to mourn there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> When Mary came to Jesus and saw Him, she fell at His feet and said, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus saw her weeping, and the Jews who had come with her also weeping, He was deeply moved in spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or He was indignant in spirit; similarly in verse 38">d</a></sup>and troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> “Where have you put him?” He asked.<br />“Come and see, Lord,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> Then the Jews said, “See how He loved him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them asked, “Could not this man who opened the eyes of the blind also have kept Lazarus from dying?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Raises Lazarus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus, once again deeply moved, came to the tomb. It was a cave with a stone laid across the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> “Take away the stone,” Jesus said.<br />“Lord, by now he stinks,” said Martha, the sister of the dead man. “It has already been four days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus replied, “Did I not tell you that if you believed, you would see the glory of God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> So they took away the stone. Then Jesus lifted His eyes upward and said, “Father, I thank You that You have heard Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> I knew that You always hear Me, but I say this for the benefit of the people standing here, so they may believe that You sent Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> After Jesus had said this, He called out in a loud voice, “Lazarus, come out!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> The man who had been dead came out with his hands and feet bound in strips of linen, and his face wrapped in a cloth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Greek soudariō">e</a></sup><br />“Unwrap him and let him go,” Jesus told them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Plot to Kill Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Therefore many of the Jews who had come to Mary, and had seen what Jesus did, believed in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> Then the chief priests and Pharisees convened the Sanhedrin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or the Council">f</a></sup>and said, “What are we to do? This man is performing many signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let Him go on like this, everyone will believe in Him, and then the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> But one of them, named Caiaphas, who was high priest that year, said to them, “You know nothing at all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> You do not realize that it is better for you that one man die for the people than that the whole nation perish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> Caiaphas did not say this on his own. Instead, as high priest that year, he was prophesying that Jesus would die for the nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not only for the nation, but also for the scattered children of God, to gather them together into one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day on they plotted to kill Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> As a result, Jesus no longer went about publicly among the Jews, but He withdrew to a town called Ephraim in an area near the wilderness. And He stayed there with the disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the Jewish Passover was near, and many people went up from the country to Jerusalem to purify themselves before the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> They kept looking for Jesus and asking one another as they stood in the temple courts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally the temple">g</a></sup>“What do you think? Will He come to the feast at all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> But the chief priests and Pharisees had given orders that anyone who knew where He was must report it, so that they could arrest Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
