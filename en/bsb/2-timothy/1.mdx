## Paul’s Greeting to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; John 17:3; John 10:28"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, according to the promise of life in Christ Jesus,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:1; 1 Timothy 1:2; Acts 16:1"> To Timothy, my beloved child:<br />Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Faithfulness under Persecution

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Hebrews 13:8; Acts 23:1"> I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as did my forefathers, as I constantly remember you night and day in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–1"> Recalling your tears, I long to see you so that I may be filled with joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Acts 16:1; 2 Timothy 3:15"> I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced is in you as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8"> For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God, which is in you through the laying on of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:18; Galatians 5:22"> For God has not given us a spirit of fear,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or timidity or cowardice">a</a></sup>but of power, love, and self-control.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Mark 8:38; 1 Peter 4:13–15"> So do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, or of me, His prisoner. Instead, join me in suffering for the gospel by the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; Titus 3:4–5; Ephesians 1:3–4"> He has saved us and called us to a holy calling, not because of our works, but by His own purpose and by the grace He granted us in Christ Jesus before time began.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally before times eternal">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:2; Romans 16:26; 2 Peter 3:18"> And now He has revealed this grace through the appearing of our Savior, Christ Jesus, who has abolished death and illuminated the way to life and immortality through the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:7–8; Acts 9:15"> to which I was appointed as a preacher, an apostle, and a teacher.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; Jude 1:24; 1 Peter 4:19"> For this reason, even though I suffer as I do, I am not ashamed; for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that He is able to guard what I have entrusted to Him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or what He has entrusted to me">c</a></sup>for that day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Holding to Sound Teaching

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 1:9; 2 Timothy 3:14; Philippians 4:9"> Hold on to the pattern of sound teaching<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally sound words">d</a></sup>you have heard from me, with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:17; Romans 8:13; 1 Timothy 6:20"> Guard the treasure entrusted to you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or the good deposit entrusted to you">e</a></sup>with the help of the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:16; Philippians 2:21; Acts 19:10"> You know that everyone in the Province of Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally in Asia; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.">f</a></sup>has deserted me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:19; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he has often refreshed me and was unashamed of my chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 28:30–31"> Indeed, when he arrived in Rome, he searched diligently until he found me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Timothy 1:3"> May the Lord grant Onesiphorus His mercy on that day. You know very well how much he ministered to me in Ephesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
