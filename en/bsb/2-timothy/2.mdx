## Grace and Perseverance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> You therefore, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> And the things that you have heard me say among many witnesses, entrust these to faithful men who will be qualified to teach others as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Join me in suffering, like a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> A soldier refrains from entangling himself in civilian affairs, in order to please the one who enlisted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Likewise, a competitor does not receive the crown unless he competes according to the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The hardworking farmer should be the first to partake of the crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Consider what I am saying, for the Lord will give you insight into all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, descended from David, as proclaimed by my gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> for which I suffer to the extent of being chained like a criminal. But the word of God cannot be chained!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> For this reason I endure all things for the sake of the elect, so that they too may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> This is a trustworthy saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">If we died with Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will also live with Him;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">if we endure,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will also reign with Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">if we deny Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will also deny us;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">if we are faithless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He remains faithful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for He cannot deny Himself.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord’s Approved Workman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Remind the believers of these things, charging them before God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="SBL, BYZ, and TR the Lord">a</a></sup>to avoid quarreling over words, which succeeds only in leading the listeners to ruin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman who accurately handles the word of truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But avoid irreverent, empty chatter, which will only lead to more ungodliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and the talk of such men will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who have deviated from the truth. They say that the resurrection has already occurred, and they undermine the faith of some.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> Nevertheless, God’s firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: “The Lord knows those who are His,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Numbers 16:5 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup>and, “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord must turn away from iniquity.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> A large house contains not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay. Some indeed are for honorable use, but others are for common use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> So if anyone cleanses himself of what is unfit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally cleanses himself of these">c</a></sup>he will be a vessel for honor: sanctified, useful to the Master, and prepared for every good work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> Flee from youthful passions and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, together with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But reject foolish and ignorant speculation, for you know that it breeds quarreling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> And a servant of the Lord must not be quarrelsome, but must be kind to everyone, able to teach, and forbearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> He must gently reprove those who oppose him, in the hope that God may grant them repentance leading to a knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> Then they will come to their senses and escape the snare of the devil, who has taken them captive to his will.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
