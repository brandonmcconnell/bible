## The Syrians Flee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Numbers 5:2–4; 2 Kings 7:4"> Now there were four men with leprosy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Leprosy was a term used for various skin diseases; here and in verse 8; see Leviticus 13.">d</a></sup>at the entrance of the city gate, and they said to one another, “Why just sit here until we die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Luke 15:17–19; 2 Samuel 14:14"> If we say, ‘Let us go into the city,’ we will die there from the famine in the city; but if we sit here, we will also die. So come now, let us go over to the camp of the Arameans. If they let us live, we will live; if they kill us, we will die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:25; 1 Samuel 30:17"> So they arose at twilight and went to the camp of the Arameans. But when they came to the outskirts of the camp, there was not a man to be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 31:1; 1 Kings 10:29"> For the Lord had caused the Arameans to hear the sound of chariots, horses, and a great army, so that they said to one another, “Look, the king of Israel must have hired the kings of the Hittites and Egyptians to attack us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 48:4–6; Hebrews 6:18"> Thus the Arameans had arisen and fled at twilight, abandoning their tents and horses and donkeys. The camp was intact, and they had run for their lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Joshua 7:21; Matthew 25:18"> When the lepers reached the edge of the camp, they went into a tent to eat and drink. Then they carried off the silver, gold, and clothing, and went and hid them. On returning, they entered another tent, carried off some items from there, and hid them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Philippians 2:4; Nahum 1:15"> Finally, they said to one another, “We are not doing what is right. Today is a day of good news. If we are silent and wait until morning light, our sin will overtake us. Now, therefore, let us go and tell the king’s household.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 7:11; Mark 13:34–35; Psalms 127:1"> So they went and called out to the gatekeepers of the city, saying, “We went to the Aramean camp and no one was there—not a trace—only tethered horses and donkeys, and the tents were intact.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The gatekeepers shouted the news, and it was reported to the king’s household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25–29; Joshua 8:4"> So the king got up in the night and said to his servants, “Let me tell you what the Arameans have done to us. They know we are starving, so they have left the camp to hide in the field, thinking, ‘When they come out of the city, we will take them alive and enter the city.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 7:4; Lamentations 4:9; 2 Kings 5:13"> But one of his servants replied, “Please, have scouts take five of the horses that remain in the city. Their plight will be no worse than all the Israelites who are left here. You can see that all the Israelites here are doomed. So let us send them and find out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then the scouts took two chariots with horses, and the king sent them after the Aramean army, saying, “Go and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 22:24; Matthew 24:16–18; Hebrews 12:1"> And they tracked them as far as the Jordan, and indeed, the whole way was littered with the clothing and equipment the Arameans had thrown off in haste. So the scouts returned and told the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha’s Prophecy Fulfilled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 7:1; Isaiah 33:4; Isaiah 33:23"> Then the people went out and plundered the camp of the Arameans. It was then that a seah of fine flour sold for a shekel, and two seahs of barley sold for a shekel, according to the word of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2; 2 Kings 6:32; Judges 20:43"> Now the king had appointed the officer on whose arm he leaned to be in charge of the gate, but the people trampled him in the gateway, and he died, just as the man of God had foretold when the king had come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:14; 2 Kings 7:1–2; 2 Kings 6:32"> It happened just as the man of God had told the king: “About this time tomorrow at the gate of Samaria, two seahs of barley will sell for a shekel, and a seah of fine flour will sell for a shekel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2"> And the officer had answered the man of God, “Look, even if the LORD were to make windows in heaven, could this really happen?”<br />So Elisha had replied, “You will see it with your own eyes, but you will not eat any of it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 20:20; Job 20:23"> And that is just what happened to him. The people trampled him in the gateway, and he died.</span>
</div>
</div>
