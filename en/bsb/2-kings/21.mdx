## Manasseh Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; 2 Chronicles 32:33–9; Matthew 1:10"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem fifty-five years. His mother’s name was Hephzibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; 2 Chronicles 36:14; 2 Chronicles 33:2–4"> And he did evil in the sight of the LORD by following the abominations of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Kings 17:16; Deuteronomy 17:3"> For he rebuilt the high places that his father Hezekiah had destroyed, and he raised up altars for Baal. He made an Asherah pole, as King Ahab of Israel had done, and he worshiped and served all the host of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:29; 2 Samuel 7:13; Jeremiah 32:34"> Manasseh also built altars in the house of the LORD, of which the LORD had said, “In Jerusalem I will put My Name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Kings 23:4; 2 Kings 23:12"> In both courtyards of the house of the LORD, he built altars to all the host of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 18:21"> He sacrificed his own son in the fire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally made his son pass through the fire">a</a></sup>practiced sorcery and divination, and consulted mediums and spiritists. He did great evil in the sight of the LORD, provoking Him to anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 9:3; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Manasseh even took the carved Asherah pole he had made and set it up in the temple, of which the LORD had said to David and his son Solomon, “In this temple and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will establish My Name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:28–29; Psalms 81:11–16"> I will never again cause the feet of the Israelites to wander from the land that I gave to their fathers, if only they are careful to do all I have commanded them—the whole Law that My servant Moses commanded them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Kings 14:16"> But the people did not listen and Manasseh led them astray, so that they did greater evil than the nations that the LORD had destroyed before the Israelites.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Manasseh’s Idolatries Rebuked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 23:34–37; 2 Chronicles 33:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15"> And the LORD spoke through His servants the prophets, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; 1 Kings 21:26; 2 Kings 21:9"> “Since Manasseh king of Judah has committed all these abominations, acting more wickedly than the Amorites who preceded him, and with his idols has caused Judah to sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:3; 1 Samuel 3:11; Daniel 9:12"> this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Behold, I am bringing such calamity upon Jerusalem and Judah that the news will reverberate in the ears of all who hear it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 7:7–8; Lamentations 2:8; Isaiah 34:11"> I will stretch out over Jerusalem the measuring line used against Samaria and the plumb line used against the house of Ahab, and I will wipe out Jerusalem as one wipes out a bowl—wiping it and turning it upside down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; 2 Kings 19:4; Psalms 89:38–45"> So I will forsake the remnant of My inheritance and deliver them into the hands of their enemies. And they will become plunder and spoil to all their enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:30; Deuteronomy 31:27; Ezekiel 20:21"> because they have done evil in My sight and have provoked Me to anger from the day their fathers came out of Egypt until this day.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:11"> Moreover, Manasseh shed so much innocent blood that he filled Jerusalem from end to end, in addition to the sin that he had caused Judah to commit, doing evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20–21; 2 Chronicles 33:1–20"> As for the rest of the acts of Manasseh, along with all his accomplishments and the sin that he committed, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:20; 2 Kings 21:26; 2 Chronicles 32:33"> And Manasseh rested with his fathers and was buried in his palace garden, the garden of Uzza. And his son Amon reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Amon Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:21–23; 1 Kings 22:51; 1 Chronicles 3:14"> Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem two years. His mother’s name was Meshullemeth daughter of Haruz; she was from Jotbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 23:32; 2 Kings 21:11; Acts 7:51"> And he did evil in the sight of the LORD, as his father Manasseh had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He walked in all the ways of his father, and he served and worshiped the idols his father had served.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 1 Kings 11:33; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> He abandoned the LORD, the God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 14:19; 2 Chronicles 33:24–25"> Then the servants of Amon conspired against him and killed the king in his palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 14:5; 2 Kings 11:17; 2 Kings 14:21"> But the people of the land killed all those who had conspired against King Amon, and they made his son Josiah king in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; 2 Kings 21:17"> As for the rest of the acts of Amon, along with his accomplishments, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; Matthew 1:10; 1 Kings 13:5"> And he was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and his son Josiah reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
