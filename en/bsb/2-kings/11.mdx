## Athaliah and Joash

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:1; 2 Kings 25:25; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12"> When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she proceeded to annihilate all the royal heirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 35:2; Isaiah 7:6–7"> But Jehosheba<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jehosheba is a variant of Jehoshabeath; see 2 Chronicles 22:11.">a</a></sup>daughter of King Joram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Joram is a variant spelling of Jehoram.">b</a></sup>the sister of Ahaziah, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the sons of the king who were being murdered. She put him and his nurse in a bedroom to hide him from Athaliah, and he was not killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 12:8; 2 Chronicles 22:12; Malachi 3:15"> And Joash remained hidden with his nurse in the house of the LORD for six years while Athaliah ruled the land.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joash Anointed King of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 11:19; 2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 11:17"> Then in the seventh year, Jehoiada sent for the commanders of hundreds, the Carites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or the mercenaries or the executioners; also in verse 19">c</a></sup>and the guards, and had them brought into the house of the LORD. There he made a covenant with them and put them under oath.<br />He showed them the king’s son</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; Ezekiel 44:2–3; 1 Chronicles 23:32"> and commanded them, “This is what you are to do: A third of you who come on duty on the Sabbath shall guard the royal palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:4–5; 1 Chronicles 26:13–19"> a third shall be at the gate of Sur, and a third at the gate behind the guards. You are to take turns guarding the temple—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:6; 2 Kings 11:5"> the two divisions that would go off duty on the Sabbath are to guard the house of the LORD for the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; 2 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Kings 2:28–31"> You must surround the king with weapons in hand, and anyone who approaches the ranks must be put to death. You must stay close to the king wherever he goes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:8; 2 Kings 11:4; 1 Chronicles 26:26"> So the commanders of hundreds did everything that Jehoiada the priest had ordered. Each of them took his men—those coming on duty on the Sabbath and those going off duty—and came to Jehoiada the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:7; 1 Chronicles 18:7; 2 Chronicles 5:1"> Then the priest gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears and shields of King David from the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 11:8; Ezekiel 8:16; 2 Kings 11:10"> And the guards stood with weapons in hand surrounding the king by the altar and the temple, from the south side to the north side of the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:16; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:39"> Then Jehoiada brought out the king’s son, put the crown on him, presented him with the Testimony, and proclaimed him king. They anointed him, and the people clapped their hands and declared, “Long live the king!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Athaliah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:12–15"> When Athaliah heard the noise from the guards and the people, she went out to the people in the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:31; 2 Kings 23:3; 2 Kings 9:23"> And she looked out and saw the king standing by the pillar, according to the custom. The officers and trumpeters were beside the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets.<br />Then Athaliah tore her clothes and screamed, “Treason! Treason!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:7; 2 Chronicles 23:14; 2 Chronicles 23:9"> And Jehoiada the priest ordered the commanders of hundreds in charge of the army, “Bring her out between the ranks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or out from the precincts">d</a></sup>and put to the sword anyone who follows her.” For the priest had said, “She must not be put to death in the house of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 2 Chronicles 23:15; Matthew 7:2"> So they seized Athaliah as she reached the horses’ entrance to the palace grounds, and there she was put to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoiada Restores the Worship of the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:3; 2 Chronicles 15:12–14; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> Then Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD and the king and the people that they would be the LORD’s people. He also made a covenant between the king and the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:3; 2 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 18:40"> So all the people of the land went to the temple of Baal and tore it down. They smashed the altars and idols to pieces, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal in front of the altars.<br />And Jehoiada the priest posted guards for the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25; Jeremiah 22:4; 2 Chronicles 23:19"> He took with him the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, the guards, and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the LORD and entered the royal palace by way of the Gate of the Guards.<br />Then Joash took his seat on the royal throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 29:2; 2 Kings 11:14"> and all the people of the land rejoiced. And the city was quiet, because Athaliah had been put to the sword at the royal palace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:1–14; 2 Kings 22:1; 2 Kings 11:4"> Joash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew Jehoash, a variant of Joash (son of Ahaziah) as in verse 2">e</a></sup>was seven years old when he became king.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
