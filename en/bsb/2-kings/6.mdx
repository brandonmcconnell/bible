## The Axe Head Floats

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 4:1; Joshua 17:14"> Now the sons of the prophets said to Elisha, “Please take note that the place where we meet with you is too small for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 21:3; Acts 18:3; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> Please let us go to the Jordan, where each of us can get a log so we can build ourselves a place to live there.”<br />“Go,” said Elisha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:28; Judges 19:6; 2 Kings 5:23"> Then one of them said, “Please come with your servants.”<br />“I will come,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:5; Deuteronomy 29:11"> So Elisha went with them, and when they came to the Jordan, they began to cut down some trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:21; Ecclesiastes 10:10; 2 Kings 6:15"> As one of them was cutting down a tree, the iron axe head fell into the water. “Oh, my master,” he cried out, “it was borrowed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Mark 8:23–25; 2 Kings 4:41"> “Where did it fall?” asked the man of God.<br />And when he showed him the place, the man of God cut a stick, threw it there, and made the iron float.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:36; Exodus 4:4; 2 Kings 4:7"> “Lift it out,” he said, and the man reached out his hand and took it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha Captures the Blinded Arameans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 6:24; Proverbs 21:30; Proverbs 20:18"> Now the king of Aram was at war against Israel. After consulting with his servants, he said, “My camp will be in such and such a place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Revelation 1:1; 2 Kings 6:12"> Then the man of God sent word to the king of Israel: “Be careful not to pass by this place, for the Arameans are going down there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; 2 Kings 5:14; Acts 27:24"> So the king of Israel sent word to the place the man of God had pointed out. Time and again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Not once and not twice">a</a></sup>Elisha warned the king, so that he was on his guard in such places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:7–11; Isaiah 57:20–21; Matthew 2:3–12"> For this reason the king of Aram became enraged and called his servants to demand of them, “Tell me, which one of us is on the side of the king of Israel?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:28–30; Amos 3:7; 2 Kings 6:9–10"> But one of his servants replied, “No one, my lord the king. For Elisha, the prophet in Israel, tells the king of Israel the very words you speak in your bedroom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:17; John 11:47–53; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> So the king said, “Go and see where he is, that I may send men to capture him.”<br />On receiving the report, “Elisha is in Dothan,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 23:26"> the king of Aram sent horses, chariots, and a great army. They went there by night and surrounded the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; 2 Chronicles 20:12; Psalms 53:5"> When the servant of the man of God got up and went out early in the morning, behold, an army with horses and chariots had surrounded the city. So he asked Elisha, “Oh, my master, what are we to do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Romans 8:31; 1 John 4:4"> “Do not be afraid,” Elisha answered, “for those who are with us are more than those who are with them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 6:1–7"> Then Elisha prayed, “O LORD, please open his eyes that he may see.”<br />And the LORD opened the eyes of the young man, and he saw that the hills were full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 13:11; John 12:40; Genesis 19:11"> As the Arameans came down against him, Elisha prayed to the LORD, “Please strike these people with blindness.” So He struck them with blindness, according to the word of Elisha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 24:16; 2 Samuel 16:18–19; Luke 9:23"> And Elisha told them, “This is not the way, and this is not the city. Follow me, and I will take you to the man you are seeking.” And he led them to Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Luke 24:31; Luke 16:23"> When they had entered Samaria, Elisha said, “O LORD, open the eyes of these men that they may see.”<br />Then the LORD opened their eyes, and they looked around and discovered that they were in Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 8:9; 2 Kings 2:12"> And when the king of Israel saw them, he asked Elisha, “My father, shall I kill them? Shall I kill them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Genesis 48:22; Hosea 2:18"> “Do not kill them,” he replied. “Would you kill those you have captured with your own sword or bow? Set food and water before them, that they may eat and drink and then return to their master.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2; 2 Kings 24:2; Luke 6:35"> So the king prepared a great feast for them, and after they had finished eating and drinking, he sent them away, and they returned to their master. And the Aramean raiders did not come into the land of Israel again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Siege and Famine of Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 22:31; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> Some time later, Ben-hadad king of Aram assembled his entire army and marched up to besiege Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 6:28–29; Jeremiah 14:18; Jeremiah 14:13–15"> So there was a great famine in Samaria. Indeed, they besieged the city so long that a donkey’s head sold for eighty shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="80 shekels is approximately 2 pounds or 907.2 grams of silver.">b</a></sup>and a quarter cab of dove’s dung<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or a quarter cab of seed pods; that is, approximately 0.28 dry quarts or 0.31 liters">c</a></sup>sold for five shekels of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5 shekels is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams of silver.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Isaiah 10:3; Luke 18:3"> As the king of Israel was passing by on the wall, a woman cried out to him, “Help me, my lord the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Psalms 124:1–3; Psalms 60:11"> He answered, “If the LORD does not help you, where can I find help for you? From the threshing floor or the winepress?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:5; Judges 18:23; Lamentations 4:10"> Then the king asked her, “What is the matter?”<br />And she answered, “This woman said to me, ‘Give up your son, that we may eat him, and tomorrow we will eat my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 26:29; Deuteronomy 28:53; Deuteronomy 28:57"> So we boiled my son and ate him, and the next day I said to her, ‘Give up your son, that we may eat him.’ But she had hidden her son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Kings 19:1"> When the king heard the words of the woman, he tore his clothes. And as he passed by on the wall, the people saw the sackcloth under his clothes next to his skin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Kings 19:2; John 11:50"> He announced, “May God punish me, and ever so severely, if the head of Elisha son of Shaphat remains on his shoulders through this day!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; 1 Kings 18:4; Ezekiel 8:1"> Now Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him. The king sent a messenger ahead, but before he arrived, Elisha said to the elders, “Do you see how this murderer has sent someone to cut off my head? Look, when the messenger comes, shut the door to keep him out. Is not the sound of his master’s footsteps behind him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 2:9; Isaiah 8:21; Genesis 4:13"> While Elisha was still speaking with them, the messenger came down to him. And the king said, “This calamity is from the LORD. Why should I wait for the LORD any longer?”</span>
</div>
</div>
