## Jesus Our Apostle and High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brothers, who share in the heavenly calling, set your focus on Jesus, the apostle and high priest whom we confess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> He was faithful to the One who appointed Him, just as Moses was faithful in all God’s house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally just as Moses in all His house; SBL just as Moses in His house">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For Jesus has been counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, just as the builder of a house has greater honor than the house itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> And every house is built by someone, but God is the builder of everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> Now Moses was faithful as a servant in all God’s house,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Numbers 12:7">b</a></sup>testifying to what would be spoken later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> But Christ is faithful as the Son over God’s house. And we are His house, if we hold firmly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NE, WH, BYZ, and TR include to the end.">c</a></sup>to our confidence and the hope of which we boast.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Harden Your Hearts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear His voice,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-2">do not harden your hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as you did in the rebellion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of testing in the wilderness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">where your fathers tested and tried Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and for forty years saw My works.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I was angry with that generation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Their hearts are always going astray,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have not known My ways.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I swore on oath in My anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘They shall never enter My rest.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Psalms 95:7–11 (see also LXX)">d</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The Peril of Unbelief

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> See to it, brothers, that none of you has a wicked heart of unbelief that turns away from the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> But exhort one another daily, as long as it is called today, so that none of you may be hardened by sin’s deceitfulness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> We have come to share in Christ if we hold firmly to the end the assurance we had at first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> As it has been said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear His voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not harden your hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as you did in the rebellion.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Psalms 95:7–8">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For who were the ones who heard and rebelled? Were they not all those Moses led out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> And with whom was God angry for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom did He swear that they would never enter His rest? Was it not to those who disobeyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> So we see that it was because of their unbelief that they were unable to enter.</span>
</div>
</div>
