## A Call to Endurance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> Therefore, since we are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, let us throw off every encumbrance and the sin that so easily entangles, and let us run with endurance the race set out for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> Let us fix our eyes on Jesus, the author<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or pioneer or founder">a</a></sup>and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy set before Him endured the cross, scorning its shame, and sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> Consider Him who endured such hostility from sinners, so that you will not grow weary and lose heart.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Disciplines His Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> In your struggle against sin, you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding your blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> And you have forgotten the exhortation that addresses you as sons:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, do not take lightly the discipline of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not lose heart when He rebukes you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalms 119:75"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord disciplines the one He loves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He chastises every son He receives.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally and He flogs every son He receives; Proverbs 3:11–12 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> Endure suffering as discipline; God is treating you as sons. For what son is not disciplined by his father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> If you do not experience discipline like everyone else, then you are illegitimate children and not true sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Furthermore, we have all had earthly fathers who disciplined us, and we respected them. Should we not much more submit to the Father of our spirits and live?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> Our fathers disciplined us for a short time as they thought best, but God disciplines us for our good, so that we may share in His holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> No discipline seems enjoyable at the time, but painful. Later on, however, it yields a harvest of righteousness and peace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness">c</a></sup>to those who have been trained by it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore strengthen your limp hands and weak knees.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isaiah 35:3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> Make straight paths for your feet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Proverbs 4:26 (see also LXX)">e</a></sup>so that the lame may not be disabled, but rather healed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Call to Holiness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Pursue peace with everyone, as well as holiness, without which no one will see the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> See to it that no one falls short of the grace of God, and that no root of bitterness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 29:18">f</a></sup>springs up to cause trouble and defile many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> See to it that no one is sexually immoral, or is godless like Esau, who for a single meal sold his birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> For you know that afterward, when he wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected. He could find no ground for repentance, though he sought the blessing with tears.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Unshakable Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For you have not come to a mountain that can be touched and that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally to what can be touched and">g</a></sup>is burning with fire; to darkness, gloom, and storm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> to a trumpet blast or to a voice that made its hearers beg that no further word be spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> For they could not bear what was commanded: “If even an animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Exodus 19:12–13">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> The sight was so terrifying that even Moses said, “I am trembling with fear.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Deuteronomy 9:19">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> Instead, you have come to Mount Zion, to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem. You have come to myriads of angels</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> in joyful assembly, to the congregation of the firstborn, enrolled in heaven. You have come to God the Judge of all, to the spirits of the righteous made perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> to Jesus the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> See to it that you do not refuse Him who speaks. For if the people did not escape when they refused Him who warned them on earth, how much less will we escape if we reject Him who warns us from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> At that time His voice shook the earth, but now He has promised, “Once more I will shake not only the earth, but heaven as well.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Haggai 2:6">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> The words “Once more” signify the removal of what can be shaken—that is, created things—so that the unshakable may remain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Therefore, since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us be filled with gratitude, and so worship God acceptably with reverence and awe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> “For our God is a consuming fire.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Deuteronomy 4:24">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
