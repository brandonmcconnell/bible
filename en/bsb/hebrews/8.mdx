## Christ’s Eternal Priesthood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> The point of what we are saying is this: We do have such a high priest, who sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> and who ministers in the sanctuary and true tabernacle set up by the Lord, not by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> And since every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices, it was necessary for this One also to have something to offer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> Now if He were on earth, He would not be a priest, since there are already priests who offer gifts according to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> The place where they serve is a copy and shadow of what is in heaven. This is why Moses was warned when he was about to build the tabernacle: “See to it that you make everything according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Exodus 25:40; see also Exodus 26:30.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The New Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> Now, however, Jesus has received a much more excellent ministry, just as the covenant He mediates is better and is founded on better promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that first covenant had been without fault, no place would have been sought for a second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> But God found fault with the people and said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the days are coming, declares the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I will make a new covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and with the house of Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will not be like the covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I made with their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I took them by the hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to lead them out of the land of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they did not abide by My covenant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I disregarded them,</span>declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this is the covenant I will make</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">after those days,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put My laws in their minds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and inscribe them on their hearts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be My people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">No longer will each one teach his neighbor or his brother,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">saying, ‘Know the Lord,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they will all know Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the least of them to the greatest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will forgive their iniquities</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will remember their sins no more.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jeremiah 31:31–34 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> By speaking of a new covenant,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally In saying new; here and in Hebrews 9:1 and 18, covenant is included for clarity but is not contained in the Greek. A broader interpretation could also include priesthood or tabernacle.">c</a></sup>He has made the first one obsolete; and what is obsolete and aging will soon disappear.</span>
</div>
