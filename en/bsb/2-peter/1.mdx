## A Greeting from Peter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Romans 3:21–26; Titus 1:4"> Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ,<br />To those who through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ have received a faith as precious as ours:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; John 17:3; Philippians 3:8"> Grace and peace be multiplied to you through the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Partakers of the Divine Nature

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30"> His divine power has given us everything we need for life and godliness through the knowledge of Him who called us by His own<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or to His own">a</a></sup>glory and excellence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> Through these He has given us His precious and magnificent promises, so that through them you may become partakers of the divine nature, now that you have escaped the corruption in the world caused by evil desires.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; 2 Peter 3:18"> For this very reason, make every effort to add to your faith virtue; and to virtue, knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Romans 5:3–4; Luke 21:19"> and to knowledge, self-control; and to self-control, perseverance; and to perseverance, godliness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:12"> and to godliness, brotherly kindness; and to brotherly kindness, love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; John 15:2; Hebrews 6:12"> For if you possess these qualities and continue to grow in them, they will keep you from being ineffective and unproductive in your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18–20; Ephesians 5:26; Titus 2:14"> But whoever lacks these traits is nearsighted to the point of blindness, having forgotten that he has been cleansed from his past sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Luke 6:47–49; Hebrews 6:11"> Therefore, brothers, strive to make your calling and election sure. For if you practice these things you will never stumble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Matthew 25:34; Revelation 3:21"> and you will receive a lavish reception into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> Therefore I will always remind you of these things, even though you know them and are established in the truth you now have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> I think it is right to refresh your memory as long as I live in the tent of my body,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally as long as I am in this tent">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> because I know that this tent will soon be laid aside, as our Lord Jesus Christ has made clear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> And I will make every effort to ensure that after my departure, you will be able to recall these things at all times.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Eyewitnesses of His Majesty

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:14; 1 Timothy 1:4"> For we did not follow cleverly devised fables when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we were eyewitnesses of His majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:7; Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17"> For He received honor and glory from God the Father when the voice came to Him from the Majestic Glory, saying, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Matthew 17:5; see also Mark 9:7 and Luke 9:35.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> And we ourselves heard this voice from heaven when we were with Him on the holy mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 119:105"> We also have the word of the prophets as confirmed beyond doubt. And you will do well to pay attention to it, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; Romans 12:6; Romans 6:6"> Above all, you must understand that no prophecy of Scripture comes from one’s own interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 1 Peter 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> For no such prophecy was ever brought forth by the will of man, but men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
