## The Coming Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 2 Peter 1:12–15; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, this is now my second letter to you. Both of them are reminders to stir you to wholesome thinking</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:17; 2 Peter 2:21; Luke 1:70"> by recalling what was foretold by the holy prophets and commanded by our Lord and Savior through your apostles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; 2 Peter 2:10"> Most importantly, you must understand that in the last days scoffers will come, scoffing and following their own evil desires.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Jude 1:18.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:15; Malachi 2:17; Jeremiah 5:12–13"> “Where is the promise of His coming?” they will ask. “Ever since our fathers fell asleep, everything continues as it has from the beginning of creation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 1:9; Psalms 24:2"> But they deliberately overlook the fact that long ago by God’s word the heavens existed and the earth was formed out of water and by water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:38–39; 2 Peter 2:5; Luke 17:27"> through which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NA through whom">b</a></sup>the world of that time perished in the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 51:6; 2 Peter 3:12"> And by that same word, the present heavens and earth are reserved for fire, being kept for the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Day of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:4; Romans 11:25; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Beloved, do not let this one thing escape your notice: With the Lord a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Psalms 90:4.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Romans 2:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> The Lord is not slow in keeping His promise as some understand slowness, but is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish but everyone to come to repentance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:7"> But the Day of the Lord will come like a thief. The heavens will disappear with a roar, the elements will be destroyed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or dissolved; also in verses 11 and 12.">d</a></sup>by fire, and the earth and its works will be laid bare.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or will not be found. BYZ and TR will be burned up; SBL, NE, and WH will be found, i.e., will be unable to hide.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27; 1 Peter 2:12"> Since everything will be destroyed in this way, what kind of people ought you to be? You ought to conduct yourselves in holiness and godliness</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 34:4; Micah 1:4"> as you anticipate and hasten the coming of the day of God, when the heavens will be destroyed by fire and the elements will melt in the heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Revelation 21:1; Isaiah 66:22"> But in keeping with God’s promise, we are looking forward to a new heaven and a new earth, where righteousness dwells.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Exhortations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 1:10"> Therefore, beloved, as you anticipate these things, make every effort to be found at peace—spotless and blameless in His sight.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or to be found by Him in peace, without spot and without blemish.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 1:16"> Consider also that our Lord’s patience brings salvation, just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote you with the wisdom God gave him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:6; Hebrews 5:11; James 1:8"> He writes this way in all his letters,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or in all the letters">g</a></sup>speaking in them about such matters. Some parts of his letters are hard to understand, which ignorant and unstable people distort,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="NA will distort">h</a></sup>as they do the rest of the Scriptures, to their own destruction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Colossians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Therefore, beloved, since you already know these things, be on your guard so that you will not be carried away by the error of the lawless and fall from your secure standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Ephesians 4:15; 1 Peter 2:2"> But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him be the glory both now and to the day of eternity.<br />Amen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="NE, WH, and NA do not include Amen.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
