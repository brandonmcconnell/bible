## Deliverance from False Prophets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Jude 1:4; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> Now there were also false prophets among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you. They will secretly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the Master who bought them—bringing swift destruction on themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Jude 1:10; 1 Peter 2:12"> Many will follow in their depravity, and because of them the way of truth will be defamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; Deuteronomy 32:35; Romans 16:18"> In their greed, these false teachers will exploit you with deceptive words. The longstanding verdict against them remains in force, and their destruction does not sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; Romans 8:32"> For if God did not spare the angels when they sinned, but cast them deep into hell,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek cast them into Tartarus; see the First Book of Enoch (1 Enoch 13:1–11 and 1 Enoch 20:1–4).">a</a></sup>placing them in chains of darkness to be held for judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 3:6; Jude 1:14–15"> if He did not spare the ancient world when He brought the flood on its ungodly people, but preserved Noah, a preacher of righteousness, among the eight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; Luke 17:28–30; Deuteronomy 29:23"> if He condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="WH does not include to destruction.">b</a></sup>reducing them to ashes as an example of what is coming on the ungodly;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or on future generations of the ungodly">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 19:16; Genesis 19:29; Genesis 19:7–8"> and if He rescued Lot, a righteous man distressed by the depraved conduct of the lawless</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 119:136"> (for that righteous man, living among them day after day, was tormented in his righteous soul by the lawless deeds he saw and heard)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 3:10"> if all this is so, then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trials and to hold the unrighteous for punishment on the day of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Jude 1:10; Jude 1:18"> Such punishment is specially reserved for those who indulge the corrupt desires of the flesh and despise authority. Bold and self-willed, they are unafraid to slander glorious beings.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or to blaspheme angelic majesties">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Psalms 104:4"> Yet not even angels, though greater in strength and power, dare to bring such slanderous charges against them before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Jeremiah 12:3; Galatians 6:8"> These men are like irrational animals, creatures of instinct, born to be captured and destroyed. They blaspheme in matters they do not understand, and like such creatures, they too will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:13; Romans 2:8–9; Hebrews 2:2–3"> The harm they will suffer is the wages of their wickedness.<br />They consider it a pleasure to carouse in broad daylight. They are blots and blemishes, reveling in their deception as they feast with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 2:3; 2 Peter 2:18; Ephesians 2:3"> Their eyes are full of adultery; their desire for sin is never satisfied; they seduce the unstable. They are accursed children with hearts trained in greed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 23:4–5"> They have left the straight way and wandered off to follow the way of Balaam son of Beor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="NA, SBL, BYZ, and TR Bosor">e</a></sup>who loved the wages of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 22:21–33; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Luke 16:11"> But he was rebuked for his transgression by a donkey, otherwise without speech, that spoke with a man’s voice and restrained the prophet’s madness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Ephesians 4:14; Jeremiah 14:3"> These men are springs without water and mists driven by a storm. Blackest darkness is reserved for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20; Jude 1:15–16; Romans 13:13"> With lofty but empty words, they appeal to the sensual passions of the flesh and entice those who are just escaping from others who live in error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:34; Romans 6:16–22; Galatians 5:13"> They promise them freedom, while they themselves are slaves to depravity. For a man is a slave to whatever has mastered him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Hebrews 6:4–8; Philippians 3:19"> If indeed they have escaped the corruption of the world through the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="SBL and WH the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ">f</a></sup>only to be entangled and overcome by it again, their final condition is worse than it was at first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 6:4–6; James 4:17"> It would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness than to have known it and then to turn away from the holy commandment passed on to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 26:11"> Of them the proverbs are true: “A dog returns to its vomit,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Proverbs 26:11">g</a></sup>and, “A sow that is washed goes back to her wallowing in the mud.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
