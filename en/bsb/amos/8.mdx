## The Basket of Summer Fruit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> This is what the Lord GOD showed me: I saw a basket of summer fruit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or ripe fruit; also in verse 2">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> “Amos, what do you see?” He asked.<br />“A basket of summer fruit,” I replied.<br />So the LORD said to me, “The end has come for My people Israel; I will no longer spare them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> “In that day,” declares the Lord GOD, “the songs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the singing women">b</a></sup>of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or palace">c</a></sup>will turn to wailing. Many will be the corpses, strewn in silence everywhere!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 14:4; Amos 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear this, you who trample the needy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who do away with the poor of the land,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">asking, “When will the New Moon be over,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that we may sell grain?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When will the Sabbath end,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that we may market wheat?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let us reduce the ephah and increase the shekel;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or Let us reduce the measure and increase the price">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let us cheat with dishonest scales.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let us buy the poor with silver</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the needy for a pair of sandals,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">selling even the chaff with the wheat!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will never forget any of their deeds.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will not the land quake for this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all its dwellers mourn?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All of it will swell like the Nile;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will surge and then subside</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like the Nile in Egypt.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in that day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the Lord GOD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will make the sun go down at noon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will darken the earth in the daytime.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will turn your feasts into mourning</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all your songs into lamentation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause everyone to wear sackcloth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every head to be shaved.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will make it like a time of mourning for an only son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its outcome like a bitter day.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the days are coming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the Lord GOD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I will send a famine on the land—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">not a famine of bread or a thirst for water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a famine of hearing the words of the LORD.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">People will stagger from sea to sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and roam from north to east,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seeking the word of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they will not find it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">In that day the lovely young women—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the young men as well—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will faint from thirst.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and say, ‘As surely as your god lives, O Dan,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or, ‘As surely as the way</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or the god">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Beersheba lives’—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will fall, never to rise again.”</span></span>
</div>
