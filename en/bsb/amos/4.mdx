## Punishment Brings No Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:12; Amos 6:1; Amos 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear this word,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you cows of Bashan on Mount Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you women who oppress the poor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and crush the needy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who say to your husbands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Bring us more to drink.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 89:35; Habakkuk 1:15–16"> The Lord GOD has sworn by His holiness:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the days are coming</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when you will be taken away with hooks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your posterity with fishhooks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:5; Ezekiel 12:12; Matthew 16:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will go out through broken walls,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each one straight ahead of her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will be cast out toward Harmon,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, possibly Mount Hermon">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Hosea 4:15; Numbers 28:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Go to Bethel and transgress;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rebel even more at Gilgal!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bring your sacrifices every morning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your tithes every three days.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or years">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 9:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Offer leavened bread as a thank offering,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and loudly proclaim your freewill offerings.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For that is what you children of Israel love to do,”</span>declares the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 9:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I afflicted all your cities with cleanness of teeth</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, with empty stomachs">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all your towns with lack of bread,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet you did not return to Me,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Jeremiah 3:3; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I also withheld the rain from you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when the harvest was three months away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I sent rain on one city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but withheld it from another.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">One field received rain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">another without rain withered.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 2:17; Joel 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I struck you with blight and mildew</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in your growing gardens and vineyards;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the locust devoured your fig and olive trees,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet you did not return to Me,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:60; Leviticus 26:25; Joel 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I sent plagues among you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like those of Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I killed your young men with the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">along with your captured horses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I filled your nostrils with the stench of your camp,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet you did not return to Me,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; Zechariah 3:2; Genesis 19:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Some of you I overthrew</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as I overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you were like a firebrand</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, a burning stick">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">snatched from a blaze,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet you did not return to Me,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–4; James 4:1–10; Amos 9:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Therefore, that is what I will do</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to you, O Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and since I will do this to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">prepare to meet your God, O Israel!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Micah 1:3; Amos 9:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For behold, He who forms the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who creates the wind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and reveals His thoughts to man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who turns the dawn to darkness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and strides on the heights of the earth—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the LORD, the God of Hosts, is His name.”</span></span>
