## The Locusts, Fire, and Plumb Line

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Amos 8:1; Joel 2:25"> This is what the Lord GOD showed me: He was preparing swarms of locusts just after the king’s harvest, as the late spring crop was coming up.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX A swarm of locusts coming from the east; and, behold, one grasshopper, king Gog.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> And when the locusts had eaten every green plant in the land, I said, “Lord GOD, please forgive! How will Jacob survive, since he is so small?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> So the LORD relented from this plan. “It will not happen,” He said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> This is what the Lord GOD showed me: The Lord GOD was calling for judgment by fire. It consumed the great deep and devoured the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> Then I said, “Lord GOD, please stop! How will Jacob survive, since he is so small?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; Psalms 90:13"> So the LORD relented from this plan. “It will not happen either,” said the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> This is what He showed me: Behold, the Lord was standing by a wall true to plumb, with a plumb line in His hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> “Amos, what do you see?” asked the LORD.<br />“A plumb line,” I replied.<br />“Behold,” said the Lord, “I am setting a plumb line among My people Israel; I will no longer spare them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The high places of Isaac will be deserted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the sanctuaries of Israel will be laid waste;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with My sword.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Amaziah Accuses Amos

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent word to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, “Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel. The land cannot bear all his words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> for this is what Amos has said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Jeroboam will die by the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Israel will surely go into exile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">away from their homeland.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> And Amaziah said to Amos, “Go away, you seer! Flee to the land of Judah; earn your bread there and do your prophesying there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> But never prophesy at Bethel again, because it is the sanctuary of the king and the temple of the kingdom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> “I was not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or I am not">b</a></sup>a prophet,” Amos replied, “nor was I the son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or nor am I the son">c</a></sup>of a prophet; rather, I was a herdsman and a tender of sycamore-fig trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> But the LORD took me from following the flock and said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to My people Israel.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> Now, therefore, hear the word of the LORD. You say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Do not prophesy against Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not preach against the house of Isaac.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> Therefore this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Your wife will become a prostitute in the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your sons and daughters will fall by the sword.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your land will be divided by a measuring line,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you yourself will die on pagan</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew unclean">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">soil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And Israel will surely go into exile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">away from their homeland.’”</span></span>
</div>
