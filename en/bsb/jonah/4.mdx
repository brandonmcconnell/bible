## Jonah’s Anger at the LORD’s Compassion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 15:28; Jonah 4:9; Jonah 4:4"> Jonah, however, was greatly displeased, and he became angry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or It was exceedingly evil to Jonah, and he became angry">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Exodus 34:6–7; Numbers 14:18–19"> So he prayed to the LORD, saying, “O LORD, is this not what I said while I was still in my own country? This is why I was so quick to flee toward Tarshish. I knew that You are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion—One who relents from sending disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> And now, O LORD, please take my life from me, for it is better for me to die than to live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonah 4:9; Micah 6:3"> But the LORD replied, “Have you any right to be angry?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; 1 Kings 19:13; Jeremiah 17:15–16"> Then Jonah left the city and sat down east of it, where he made himself a shelter and sat in its shade to see what would happen to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> So the LORD God appointed a vine,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew qiqayon; that is, possibly the castor oil plant; also in verses 7, 9, and 10">b</a></sup>and it grew up to provide shade over Jonah’s head to ease his discomfort, and Jonah was greatly pleased with the plant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalms 102:10"> When dawn came the next day, God appointed a worm that attacked the plant so that it withered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Psalms 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> As the sun was rising, God appointed a scorching east wind, and the sun beat down on Jonah’s head so that he grew faint and wished to die, saying, “It is better for me to die than to live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> Then God asked Jonah, “Have you any right to be angry about the plant?”<br />“I do,” he replied. “I am angry enough to die!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> But the LORD said, “You cared about the plant, which you neither tended nor made grow. It sprang up in a night and perished in a night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> So should I not care about the great city of Nineveh, which has more than 120,000 people who cannot tell their right hand from their left, and many cattle as well?”</span>
</div>
</div>
