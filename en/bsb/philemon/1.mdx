## Greetings from Paul and Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother,<br />To Philemon our beloved fellow worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> to Apphia our sister, to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets at your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="You and Your are singular throughout this letter, except in verses 3, 22, and 25.">a</a></sup>house:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Philemon’s Faith and Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I always thank my God, remembering you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> because I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> I pray that your partnership in the faith may become effective as you fully acknowledge every good thing that is ours in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> I take great joy and encouragement in your love, because you, brother, have refreshed the hearts of the saints.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Appeal for Onesimus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> So although in Christ I am bold enough to order you to do what is proper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> I prefer to appeal on the basis of love. For I, Paul, am now aged, and a prisoner of Christ Jesus as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I appeal to you for my child Onesimus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Onesimus means useful (see verse 11) or beneficial (see verse 20).">b</a></sup>whose father I became while I was in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> Formerly he was useless to you, but now he has become useful both to you and to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> I am sending back to you him who is my very heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> I would have liked to keep him with me, so that on your behalf he could minister to me in my chains for the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But I did not want to do anything without your consent, so that your goodness will not be out of compulsion, but by your own free will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> For perhaps this is why he was separated from you for a while, so that you might have him back for good—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no longer as a slave, but better than a slave, as a beloved brother. He is especially beloved to me, but even more so to you, both in person and in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> So if you consider me a partner, receive him as you would receive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> But if he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I will repay it—not to mention that you owe me your very self.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, let me have some benefit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek onaimēn, a play on the name Onesimus">c</a></sup>from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I ask.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> In the meantime, prepare a guest room for me, because I hope that through your prayers I will be restored to you.</span>
</div>
</div>
