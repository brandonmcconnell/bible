## Judgment to Come

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> This is the word of the LORD that came to Micah the Moreshite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah—what he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Psalms 11:4; Revelation 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O peoples, all of you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">listen, O earth, and everyone in it!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May the Lord GOD bear witness against you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Lord from His holy temple.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 2:19; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">All this is for the transgression of Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the sins of the house of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What is the transgression of Jacob?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Is it not Samaria?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And what is the high place of Judah?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Is it not Jerusalem?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:14; Micah 3:12; Lamentations 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I will make Samaria</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a heap of rubble in the open field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a planting area for a vineyard.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pour her stones into the valley</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and expose her foundations.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
