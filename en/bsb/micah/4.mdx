## The Mountain of the House of the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 2:1–4; Jeremiah 3:17; Acts 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be established as the chief of the mountains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will be raised above the hills,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the peoples will stream to it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:3; Jeremiah 31:6; Acts 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And many nations will come and say:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the house of the God of Jacob.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will teach us His ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that we may walk in His paths.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the law will go forth from Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Joel 3:9–16; Matthew 25:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then He will judge between many peoples</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and arbitrate for strong nations far and wide.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they will beat their swords into plowshares</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their spears into pruning hooks.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Nation will no longer take up the sword against nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor will they train anymore for war.</span></span>

## The Restoration of Zion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19; Ezekiel 34:12–17; Isaiah 35:3–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“On that day,” declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I will gather the lame;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will assemble the outcast,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even those whom I have afflicted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Isaiah 24:23; Micah 2:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will make the lame into a remnant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the outcast into a strong nation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then the LORD will rule over them in Mount Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from that day and forever.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Isaiah 5:25–30; Micah 7:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now many nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">have assembled against you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saying, “Let her be defiled,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let us feast our eyes on Zion.”</span></span>
