<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Proverbs 28:4; Psalms 53:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You hate good and love evil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You tear the skin from my people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and strip the flesh from their bones.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; 1 Corinthians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, however, I am filled with power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by the Spirit of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with justice and courage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to declare to Jacob his transgression</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to Israel his sin.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Psalms 58:1–2; Proverbs 17:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now hear this, O leaders of the house of Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rulers of the house of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who despise justice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pervert all that is right,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Isaiah 1:23; Micah 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her leaders judge for a bribe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her priests teach for a price,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her prophets practice divination for money.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet they lean upon the LORD, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Is not the LORD among us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">No disaster can come upon us.”</span></span>
