## The Coming Glory of God’s House

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:10"> On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Nehemiah 8:9; Ezra 2:63"> “Speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jehozadak is a variant of Jozadak; also in verse 4; see Ezra 3:2.">a</a></sup>the high priest, and also to the remnant of the people. Ask them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 3:12; Haggai 2:9; Ezekiel 7:20"> ‘Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory? How does it look to you now? Does it not appear to you like nothing in comparison?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:20; Joshua 1:9; Zechariah 8:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now be strong, O Zerubbabel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be strong, O Joshua son of Jehozadak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the high priest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And be strong, all you people of the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Work! For I am with you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Exodus 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the promise I made to you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when you came out of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And My Spirit remains among you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not be afraid.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–28; Isaiah 10:25; Mark 13:24–26"> For this is what the LORD of Hosts says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Once more, in a little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will shake the heavens and the earth,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 12:26">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the sea and the dry land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; 1 Kings 8:11; John 7:37–39"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will shake all the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will come with all their treasures,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will fill this house with glory,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the LORD of Hosts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:10–12; Psalms 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The silver is Mine, and the gold is Mine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 85:8–9; 2 Corinthians 3:9–10; Colossians 1:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The latter glory of this house</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be greater than the former,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the LORD of Hosts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And in this place I will provide peace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Blessings for a Defiled People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10–11; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> “This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Ask the priests for a ruling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:27; Matthew 23:19; Leviticus 7:6"> If a man carries consecrated meat in the fold of his garment, and it touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any other food, does that item become holy?’”<br />“No,” replied the priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2–3; Numbers 19:11–22; Leviticus 22:4–6"> So Haggai asked, “If one who is defiled by contact with a corpse touches any of these, does it become defiled?”<br />“Yes, it becomes defiled,” the priests answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Titus 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Then Haggai replied, “So it is with this people and this nation before Me, declares the LORD, and so it is with every work of their hands; whatever they offer there is defiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> Now consider carefully from this day forward:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or backward; also in verse 18">c</a></sup>Before one stone was placed on another in the temple of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Malachi 2:2; Proverbs 3:9–10"> from that time, when one came expecting a heap of twenty ephahs of grain,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally a heap of twenty">d</a></sup>there were but ten. When one came to the winepress to draw out fifty baths,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally to draw out fifty from the winepress">e</a></sup>there were but twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I struck you—all the work of your hands—with blight, mildew, and hail, but you did not turn to Me, declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> Consider carefully from this day forward—from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the day the foundation of the LORD’s temple was laid—consider carefully:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Habakkuk 3:17–18; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Is there still seed in the barn? The vine, the fig, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not yet yielded fruit. But from this day on, I will bless you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zerubbabel the LORD’s Signet Ring

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the second time that day, the twenty-fourth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to Haggai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> “Tell Zerubbabel governor of Judah that I am about to shake the heavens and the earth:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 5:10; Judges 7:22; Zephaniah 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will overturn royal thrones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and destroy the power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the kingdoms of the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will overturn chariots and their riders;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">horses and their riders will fall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each by the sword of his brother.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; Song 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will take you, My servant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will make you like My signet ring,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I have chosen you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts.”</span></span>
</div>
