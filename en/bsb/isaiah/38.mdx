## Hezekiah’s Illness and Recovery

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Chronicles 32:24; 2 Kings 20:1–11"> In those days Hezekiah became mortally ill. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him and said, “This is what the LORD says: ‘Put your house in order, for you are about to die; you will not recover.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Psalms 91:15; 1 Kings 8:30"> Then Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 18:20–27; Hebrews 6:10"> saying, “Please, O LORD, remember how I have walked before You faithfully and with wholehearted devotion; I have done what is good in Your sight.” And Hezekiah wept bitterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the word of the LORD came to Isaiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:5–6; 1 John 5:14–15; Revelation 7:17"> “Go and tell Hezekiah that this is what the LORD, the God of your father David, says: ‘I have heard your prayer; I have seen your tears. Behold, I will add fifteen years to your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; Isaiah 31:4–5; Isaiah 12:6"> And I will deliver you and this city from the hand of the king of Assyria. I will defend this city.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="MT and LXX; DSS includes for My sake and for the sake of My servant David; see 2 Kings 20:6.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> This will be a sign to you from the LORD that He will do what He has promised:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:9–11; Joshua 10:12–14; Matthew 16:1"> I will make the sun’s shadow that falls on the stairway of Ahaz go back ten steps.’”<br />So the sunlight went back the ten steps it had descended.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hezekiah’s Song of Thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:1; Exodus 15:1–21; Job 5:18"> This is a writing by Hezekiah king of Judah after his illness and recovery:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:24; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 107:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “In the prime</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or In the quiet or In the middle">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of my life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I must go through the gates of Sheol</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be deprived of the remainder of my years.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Psalms 116:8–9; Psalms 31:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “I will never again see the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even the LORD, in the land of the living;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will no longer look on mankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with those who dwell in this world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 73:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">My dwelling has been picked up and removed from me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a shepherd’s tent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have rolled up my life like a weaver;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He cuts me off from the loom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from day until night You make an end of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:8; Daniel 6:24; Psalms 50:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I composed myself</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or I cried out; see Targum Yonaton.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until the morning.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Like a lion He breaks all my bones;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from day until night You make an end of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 123:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I chirp like a swallow or crane;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I moan like a dove.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My eyes grow weak as I look upward.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Lord, I am oppressed; be my security.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; Job 7:11; Job 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">What can I say?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has spoken to me, and He Himself has done this.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will walk slowly all my years</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the anguish of my soul.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Psalms 119:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, by such things men live,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in all of them my spirit finds life.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have restored me to health</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have let me live.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:18–19; Jonah 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely for my own welfare</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I had such great anguish;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but Your love has delivered me from the pit of oblivion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You have cast all my sins behind Your back.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 30:9; Psalms 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Sheol cannot thank You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Death cannot praise You.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who descend to the Pit</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cannot hope for Your faithfulness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Psalms 119:175; Psalms 78:3–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The living, only the living, can thank You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as I do today;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fathers will tell their children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">about Your faithfulness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 150:4; Psalms 145:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD will save me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will play songs on stringed instruments</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the days of our lives</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the house of the LORD.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 20:7–8; Mark 7:33; John 9:6"> Now Isaiah had said, “Prepare a lump of pressed figs and apply it to the boil, and he will recover.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 118:18–19; 2 Kings 20:8; John 5:14"> And Hezekiah had asked, “What will be the sign that I will go up to the house of the LORD?”</span>
</div>
</div>
