## Heaven Is My Throne

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:48–50; Acts 17:24; Matthew 5:34–35"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Heaven is My throne,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and earth is My footstool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What kind of house will you build for Me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or where will My place of repose be?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Psalms 34:18; Psalms 51:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has not My hand made all these things?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Acts 7:49–50">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And so they came into being,”</span>declares the LORD.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“This is the one I will esteem:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he who is humble and contrite in spirit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who trembles at My word.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 1:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoever slaughters an ox is like one who slays a man;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whoever sacrifices a lamb is like one who breaks a dog’s neck;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whoever presents a grain offering is like one who offers pig’s blood;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whoever offers frankincense is like one who blesses an idol.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, they have chosen their own ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and delighted in their abominations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Jeremiah 7:13; Proverbs 10:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will choose their punishment</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will bring terror upon them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because I called and no one answered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I spoke and no one listened.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But they did evil in My sight</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and chose that in which I did not delight.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:18–20; Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who tremble at His word,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hear the word of the LORD:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your brothers who hate you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and exclude you because of My name</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have said, ‘Let the LORD be glorified</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that we may see your joy!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But they will be put to shame.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:7–16; Amos 1:2; Isaiah 34:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear the uproar from the city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">listen to the voice from the temple!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is the voice of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">repaying His enemies what they deserve!</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Rejoice with Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–5; Isaiah 54:1; Galatians 4:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Before she was in labor, she gave birth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before she was in pain, she delivered a boy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Isaiah 49:20–22; Romans 15:18–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who has heard of such as this?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who has seen such things?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can a country be born in a day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or a nation be delivered in an instant?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet as soon as Zion was in labor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she gave birth to her children.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Genesis 18:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shall I bring a baby to the point of birth and not deliver it?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Or will I who deliver close the womb?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says your God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Deuteronomy 32:43; Psalms 137:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be glad for Jerusalem and rejoice over her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who love her.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rejoice greatly with her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who mourn over her,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Joel 3:18; Isaiah 60:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">so that you may nurse and be satisfied</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at her comforting breasts;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you may drink deeply and delight yourselves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in her glorious abundance.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:14"> For this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will extend peace to her like a river,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wealth of nations like a flowing stream;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will nurse and be carried on her arm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bounced upon her knees.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 40:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a mother comforts her son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so will I comfort you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will be consoled over Jerusalem.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 10:7; Ezra 8:31; Ezra 7:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you see, you will rejoice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will flourish like grass;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then the hand of the LORD will be revealed to His servants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but His wrath will be shown to His enemies.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Judgments against the Wicked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:33; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For behold, the LORD will come with fire—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His chariots are like a whirlwind—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to execute His anger with fury</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and His rebuke with flames of fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21–22; Isaiah 30:30; Ezekiel 39:2–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For by fire and by His sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the LORD will execute judgment on all flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and many will be slain by the LORD.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3–4; Deuteronomy 14:3–8; Isaiah 1:28–29"> “Those who consecrate and purify themselves to enter the groves—to follow one in the center of those who eat the flesh of swine and vermin and rats—will perish together,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Revelation 11:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> “And I, knowing their deeds and thoughts, am coming<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally And I, their deeds and their thoughts, am coming">b</a></sup>to gather all nations and tongues, and they will come and see My glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Romans 15:21; Isaiah 55:5"> I will establish a sign among them, and I will send survivors from among them to the nations—to Tarshish, Put, and the archers of Lud; to Tubal, Javan, and the islands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or coastlands">c</a></sup>far away who have not heard of My fame or seen My glory.<br />So they will proclaim My glory among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:3–14; Isaiah 65:11; Isaiah 11:9"> And they will bring all your brothers from all the nations as a gift to the LORD on horses and chariots and wagons, on mules and camels, to My holy mountain Jerusalem,” says the LORD, “just as the Israelites bring an offering in a clean vessel to the house of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:6; Revelation 1:6"> “And I will select some of them as priests and Levites,” says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For just as the new heavens and the new earth,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX the new heaven and the new earth; see also Isaiah 65:17 and Revelation 21:1.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which I will make, will endure before Me,”</span>declares the LORD,<br /><span className="pl pl-2">“so your descendants and your name will endure.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1; Psalms 86:9; Colossians 2:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">From one New Moon to another</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from one Sabbath to another,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all mankind will come to worship before Me,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the LORD.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 14:10–11; Isaiah 14:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“As they go forth, they will see the corpses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the men who have rebelled against Me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for their worm will never die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their fire will never be quenched,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Cited in Mark 9:48">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will be a horror</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to all mankind.”</span></span>
