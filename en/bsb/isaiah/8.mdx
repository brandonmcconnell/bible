## Darkness and Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:13; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> When men tell you to consult mediums and spiritists who whisper and mutter, shouldn’t a people consult their God instead? Why consult the dead on behalf of the living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, they have no light of dawn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 19:3; Lamentations 4:4–5"> They will roam the land, dejected and hungry. When they are famished, they will become enraged; and looking upward, they will curse their king and their God.</span>

</div>
