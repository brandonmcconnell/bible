## Isaiah’s Message of Deliverance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 19:1–37; Ezra 9:5; Isaiah 36:22–38"> On hearing this report, King Hezekiah tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and entered the house of the LORD.</span>
