<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, a king will reign in righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and princes will rule with justice.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">In a little more than a year you will tremble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O secure ones.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the grape harvest will fail</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the fruit harvest will not arrive.</span></span>
</div>
