## Salvation for Foreigners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:11–14; Psalms 85:9; Matthew 3:2"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Maintain justice and do what is right,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for My salvation is coming soon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and My righteousness will be revealed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 31:13–16; Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 112:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the man who does this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the son of man who holds it fast,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and keeps his hand from doing any evil.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:17; Acts 10:34; Matthew 19:12–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let no foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“The LORD will utterly exclude me from His people.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And let the eunuch not say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I am but a dry tree.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Isaiah 27:5"> For this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“To the eunuchs who keep My Sabbaths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who choose what pleases Me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hold fast to My covenant—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give them, in My house and within My walls,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a memorial and a name</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">better than that of sons and daughters.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will give them an everlasting name</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that will not be cut off.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 56:2–4; Isaiah 58:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to minister to Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to love the name of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to be His servants—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all who keep the Sabbath without profaning it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and who hold fast to My covenant—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Isaiah 2:2–3; Malachi 1:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will bring them to My holy mountain</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and make them joyful in My house of prayer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their burnt offerings and sacrifices</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be accepted on My altar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for My house will be called a house of prayer</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 21:13, Mark 11:17, and Luke 19:46">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all the nations.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 54:7; Isaiah 60:3–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thus declares the Lord GOD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who gathers the dispersed of Israel:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will gather to them still others</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">besides those already gathered.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel’s Sinful Leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come, all you beasts of the field;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">eat greedily, all you beasts of the forest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 58:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Israel’s watchmen are blind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are all oblivious;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are all mute dogs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they cannot bark;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are dreamers lying around,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">loving to slumber.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–3; Ezekiel 13:19; Micah 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like ravenous dogs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are never satisfied.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are shepherds with no discernment;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they all turn to their own way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each one seeking his own gain:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 10:6; Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Come, let me get the wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let us imbibe the strong drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and tomorrow will be like today,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">only far better!”</span></span>
