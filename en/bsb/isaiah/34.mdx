<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Isaiah 41:1; Isaiah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come near, O nations, to listen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">pay attention, O peoples.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the earth hear, and all that fills it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the world and all that springs from it.</span></span>

## Judgment on Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Malachi 1:4; Isaiah 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">When My sword has drunk its fill in the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then it will come down upon Edom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">upon the people I have devoted to destruction.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1; Jeremiah 49:13; Isaiah 34:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sword of the LORD is bathed in blood.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It drips with fat—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the blood of lambs and goats,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the fat of the kidneys of rams.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the LORD has a sacrifice in Bozrah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a great slaughter in the land of Edom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremiah 50:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the wild oxen will fall with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the young bulls with the strong ones.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their land will be drenched with blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their soil will be soaked with fat.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:4; Isaiah 61:2; Jeremiah 46:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the LORD has a day of vengeance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a year of recompense for the cause of Zion.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or of recompense for (Edom’s) hostility against Zion.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Edom’s streams will be turned to tar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her soil to sulfur;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her land will become a blazing pitch.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 13:20; Malachi 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will not be quenched—day or night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Its smoke will ascend forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">From generation to generation it will lie desolate;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no one will ever again pass through it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:8; 2 Kings 21:13; Zephaniah 2:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The desert owl and screech owl will possess it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the great owl and raven will dwell in it.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The precise identification of these birds is uncertain.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The LORD will stretch out over Edom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a measuring line of chaos</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a plumb line of destruction.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6–8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; Jeremiah 39:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">No nobles will be left to proclaim a king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all her princes will come to nothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 10:22; Malachi 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her towers will be overgrown with thorns,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her fortresses with thistles and briers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She will become a haunt for jackals,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or serpents or dragons">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an abode for ostriches.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally for daughters of an ostrich or for daughters of an owl">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The desert creatures will meet with hyenas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and one wild goat will call to another.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There the night creature</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew Lilith">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will settle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and find her place of repose.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the owl will make her nest;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she will lay and hatch her eggs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gather her brood under her shadow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even there the birds of prey will gather,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each with its mate.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Joshua 1:8; Matthew 5:18"> Search and read the scroll of the LORD:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Not one of these will go missing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not one will lack her mate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because He has ordered it by His mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He will gather them by His Spirit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Acts 17:26; Isaiah 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has allotted their portion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His hand has distributed it by measure.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will possess it forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will dwell in it from generation to generation.</span></span>
</div>
