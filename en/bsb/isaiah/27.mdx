## The LORD’s Vineyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Ezekiel 29:3; Isaiah 66:16"> In that day the LORD will take His sharp, great, and mighty sword, and bring judgment on Leviathan the fleeing serpent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew nachash; translated in most cases as snake; twice in this verse">a</a></sup>—Leviathan the coiling serpent—and He will slay the dragon of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Isaiah 5:1–7"> In that day:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sing about a fruitful vineyard.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; John 15:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-2">I, the LORD, am its keeper;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I water it continually.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I guard it night and day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so no one can disturb it;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> <span className="pl pl-2">I am not angry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If only thorns and briers confronted Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I would march and trample them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I would burn them to the ground.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Or let them lay claim to My protection;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let them make peace with Me—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yes, let them make peace with Me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31; Hosea 14:5–6; Romans 11:16–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the days to come, Jacob will take root.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Israel will bud and blossom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fill the whole world with fruit.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 10:20–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has the LORD struck Israel as He struck her oppressors?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Was she killed like those who slayed her?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 4:11; Ezekiel 19:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">By warfare and exile</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or By driving her away into exile or Measure by measure, by exile">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You contended with her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and removed her with a fierce wind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as on the day the east wind blows.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore Jacob’s guilt will be atoned for,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the full fruit of the removal of his sin will be this:</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="LXX Therefore the guilt of Jacob will be taken away, and this is his blessing when I take away his sin; cited in Romans 11:27">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When he makes all the altar stones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like crushed bits of chalk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no Asherah poles or incense altars</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will remain standing.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the fortified city lies deserted—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a homestead abandoned, a wilderness forsaken.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There the calves graze, and there they lie down;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they strip its branches bare.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">When its limbs are dry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are broken off.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Women come and use them for kindling;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for this is a people without understanding.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore their Maker has no compassion on them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their Creator shows them no favor.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> In that day the LORD will thresh from the flowing Euphrates<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew River">d</a></sup>to the Wadi of Egypt, and you, O Israelites, will be gathered one by one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> And in that day a great ram’s horn will sound, and those who were perishing in Assyria will come forth with those who were exiles in Egypt. And they will worship the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
