## The Burden against Tyre

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:4–8; Amos 1:9–10; Genesis 10:4"> This is the burden against Tyre:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wail, O ships of Tarshish,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Wail, O fleet of trading ships; also in verse 14">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for Tyre is laid waste,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">without house or harbor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Word has reached them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the land of Cyprus.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Kittim; also in verse 12">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 41:1; Ezekiel 28:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be silent, O dwellers of the coastland,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you merchants of Sidon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose traders have crossed the sea.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="DSS and LXX; MT whom the seafarers have enriched">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:18; 1 Chronicles 13:5; Ezekiel 27:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">On the great waters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">came the grain of Shihor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the harvest of the Nile was the revenue of Tyre;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she was the merchant of the nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 47:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be ashamed, O Sidon, the stronghold of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the sea has spoken:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I have not been in labor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or given birth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have not raised young men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or brought up young women.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Exodus 15:14–16; Revelation 18:17–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the report reaches Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will writhe in agony over the news of Tyre.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 23:12; Isaiah 21:15; Isaiah 23:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cross over to Tarshish;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">wail, O inhabitants of the coastland!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 32:13; Joshua 19:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is this your jubilant city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose origin is from antiquity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose feet have taken her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to settle far away?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Jeremiah 50:44–45; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who planned this against Tyre,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the bestower of crowns,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose traders are princes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose merchants are renowned on the earth?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:6; Isaiah 13:11; Job 40:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD of Hosts planned it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to defile all its glorious beauty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to disgrace all the renowned of the earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 12:21; Isaiah 23:14; Psalms 18:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cultivate</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="DSS and some LXX manuscripts; MT Overflow">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your land like the Nile, O Daughter of Tarshish;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is no longer a harbor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3–4; Exodus 14:21; Jeremiah 47:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD has stretched out His hand over the sea;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has made kingdoms tremble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has given a command</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the strongholds of Canaan be destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13–14; Isaiah 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He said, “You shall rejoice no more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O oppressed Virgin Daughter of Sidon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Get up and cross over to Cyprus—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even there you will find no rest.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:6; Isaiah 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look at the land of the Chaldeans</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a people now of no account.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Assyrians destined it for the desert creatures;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they set up their siege towers and stripped its palaces.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They brought it to ruin.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 27:25–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, O ships of Tarshish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for your harbor has been destroyed!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:22; Jeremiah 25:9–11; Daniel 7:14"> At that time Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years—the span of a king’s life. But at the end of seventy years, it will happen to Tyre as in the song of the harlot:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Proverbs 7:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Take up your harp,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">stroll through the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O forgotten harlot.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Make sweet melody,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sing many a song,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so you will be remembered.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nahum 3:4; Ezekiel 27:6–36; Revelation 19:2"> And at the end of seventy years, the LORD will restore Tyre. Then she will return to hire as a prostitute and sell herself to all the kingdoms on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; Deuteronomy 26:12–14; Philippians 4:17–18"> Yet her profits and wages will be set apart to the LORD; they will not be stored or saved, for her profit will go to those who live before the LORD, for abundant food and fine clothing.</span>
</div>
