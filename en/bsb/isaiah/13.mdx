## The Burden against Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 50:1–64; Isaiah 1:1"> This is the burden against Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz received:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 5:26; Jeremiah 50:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Raise a banner on a barren hilltop;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">call aloud to them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wave your hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that they may enter the gates of the nobles.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have commanded My sanctified ones;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have even summoned My warriors</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to execute My wrath</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and exult in My triumph.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen, a tumult on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like that of a great multitude!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen, an uproar among the kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like nations gathered together!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The LORD of Hosts is mobilizing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an army for war.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–46; Matthew 24:31; Jeremiah 50:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are coming from faraway lands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the ends of the heavens—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the LORD and the weapons of His wrath—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to destroy the whole country.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; Amos 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, for the Day of the LORD is near;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will come as destruction from the Almighty.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Shaddai">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terror, pain, and anguish will seize them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will writhe like a woman in labor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will look at one another,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their faces flushed with fear.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the Day of the LORD is coming—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cruel, with fury and burning anger—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to make the earth a desolation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to destroy the sinners within it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the stars of heaven and their constellations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will not give their light.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The rising sun will be darkened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the moon will not give its light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the world for its evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wicked for their iniquity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will end the haughtiness of the arrogant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lay low the pride of the ruthless.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; Isaiah 24:6; Job 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make man scarcer than pure gold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and mankind rarer than the gold of Ophir.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I will make the heavens tremble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the earth will be shaken from its place</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at the wrath of the LORD of Hosts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the day of His burning anger.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoever is caught will be stabbed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whoever is captured will die by the sword.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their infants will be dashed to pieces</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their houses will be looted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their wives will be ravished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, I will stir up against them the Medes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who have no regard for silver</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no desire for gold.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their bows will dash young men to pieces;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will have no mercy on the fruit of the womb;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will not look with pity on the children.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 51:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Babylon, the jewel of the kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the glory of the pride of the Chaldeans,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be overthrown by God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like Sodom and Gomorrah.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">She will never be inhabited</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or settled from generation to generation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no nomad will pitch his tent there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no shepherd will rest his flock there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Isaiah 34:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But desert creatures will lie down there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and howling creatures will fill her houses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ostriches</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally Daughters of an ostrich or Daughters of an owl">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will dwell there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wild goats will leap about.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 35:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hyenas will howl in her fortresses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and jackals</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or serpents or dragons">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in her luxurious palaces.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Babylon’s time is at hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her days will not be prolonged.</span></span>
</div>
