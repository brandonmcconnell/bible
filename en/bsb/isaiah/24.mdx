<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terror and pit and snare await you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O dweller of the earth.</span></span>
