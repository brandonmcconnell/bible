## The Burden against Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremiah 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> This is the burden against Moab:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ar in Moab is ruined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">destroyed in a night!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Kir in Moab is devastated,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">destroyed in a night!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Dibon goes up to its temple</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to weep at its high places.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Moab wails over Nebo,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as well as over Medeba.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every head is shaved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every beard is cut off.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Jeremiah 19:13; Deuteronomy 22:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">In its streets they wear sackcloth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the rooftops and in the public squares</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they all wail, falling down weeping.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Heshbon and Elealeh cry out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their voices are heard as far as Jahaz.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the soldiers of Moab cry out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their souls tremble within.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:5; Jeremiah 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart cries out over Moab;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her fugitives flee as far as Zoar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as far as Eglath-shelishiyah.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Zoar, like a heifer three years of age.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With weeping they ascend the slope of Luhith;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they lament their destruction on the road to Horonaim.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters of Nimrim are dried up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the grass is withered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the vegetation is gone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the greenery is no more.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:36; Nahum 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">So they carry their wealth and belongings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over the Brook of the Willows.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Poplars">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; Jeremiah 48:31–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">For their outcry echoes to the border of Moab.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their wailing reaches Eglaim;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is heard in Beer-elim.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters of Dimon</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="MT, twice in this verse; DSS and Vulgate Dibon; Dimon, a wordplay on Dibon (see verse 2), sounds like the Hebrew for blood.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">are full of blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I will bring more upon Dimon—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a lion upon the fugitives of Moab</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and upon the remnant of the land.</span></span>
</div>
