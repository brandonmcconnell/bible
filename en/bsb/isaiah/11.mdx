## The Root of Jesse

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16; Zechariah 6:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then a shoot will spring up from the stump of Jesse,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a Branch from his roots will bear fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; John 16:13; Ephesians 1:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Spirit of the LORD will rest on Him—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Spirit of wisdom and understanding,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Spirit of counsel and strength,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Spirit of knowledge and fear of the LORD.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cow will graze with the bear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their young will lie down together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the lion will eat straw like the ox.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5; Psalms 140:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The infant will play by the cobra’s den,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the toddler will reach into the viper’s nest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 52:10; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will neither harm nor destroy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on all My holy mountain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the earth will be full of the knowledge of the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as the sea is full of water.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

The nations will seek Him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX On that day the Root of Jesse will appear, One who will arise to rule over the Gentiles; in Him the Gentiles will put their hope; cited in Romans 15:12.">b</a></sup>and His place of rest will be glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:12; Isaiah 10:20; Zechariah 10:8–12"> On that day the Lord will extend His hand a second time to recover the remnant of His people from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the upper Nile region">c</a></sup>from Elam, from Shinar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Babylonia">d</a></sup>from Hamath, and from the islands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or coastlands">e</a></sup>of the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; Isaiah 43:6; Zephaniah 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will raise a banner for the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gather the exiles of Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will collect the scattered of Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the four corners of the earth.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:19; Isaiah 25:10; Jeremiah 49:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will swoop down on the slopes of the Philistines to the west;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">together they will plunder the sons of the east.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will lay their hands on Edom and Moab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the Ammonites will be subject to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 50:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD will devote to destruction</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or will completely dry up">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the gulf</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew the tongue">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the Sea of Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with a scorching wind He will sweep His hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over the Euphrates.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew the River">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will split it into seven streams</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for men to cross with dry sandals.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23; Isaiah 35:8–10; Isaiah 11:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">There will be a highway for the remnant of His people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who remain from Assyria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as there was for Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they came up from the land of Egypt.</span></span>
</div>
