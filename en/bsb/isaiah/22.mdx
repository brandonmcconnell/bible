## The Valley of Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> This is the burden against the Valley of Vision:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">What ails you now,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you have all gone up to the rooftops,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">O city of commotion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O town of revelry?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your slain did not die by the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor were they killed in battle.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">All your rulers have fled together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">captured without a bow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All your fugitives were captured together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">having fled to a distant place.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Turn away from me, let me weep bitterly!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not try to console me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over the destruction of the daughter of my people.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord GOD of Hosts has set a day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of tumult and trampling and confusion in the Valley of Vision—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of breaking down the walls</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and crying to the mountains.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Elam takes up a quiver, with chariots and horsemen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Kir uncovers the shield.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your choicest valleys are full of chariots,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and horsemen are posted at the gates.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has uncovered</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the defenses of Judah.</span><br />On that day you looked to the weapons in the House of the Forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> You saw that there were many breaches in the walls of the City of David. You collected water from the lower pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> You counted the houses of Jerusalem and tore them down to strengthen the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> You built a reservoir between the walls for the waters of the ancient pool, but you did not look to the One who made it, or consider Him who planned it long ago.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day the Lord GOD of Hosts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">called for weeping and wailing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for shaven heads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wearing of sackcloth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">But look, there is joy and gladness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">butchering of cattle and slaughtering of sheep,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eating of meat and drinking of wine:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in 1 Corinthians 15:32">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> The LORD of Hosts has revealed in my hearing:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Until your dying day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this sin of yours will never be atoned for,”</span>says the Lord GOD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Message for Shebna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> This is what the Lord GOD of Hosts says: “Go, say to Shebna, the steward in charge of the palace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> What are you doing here, and who authorized you to carve out a tomb for yourself here—to chisel your tomb in the height and cut your resting place in the rock?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Look, O mighty man! The LORD is about to shake you violently. He will take hold of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> roll you into a ball, and sling you into a wide land. There you will die, and there your glorious chariots will remain—a disgrace to the house of your master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> I will remove you from office, and you will be ousted from your position.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> On that day I will summon My servant, Eliakim son of Hilkiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> I will clothe him with your robe and tie your sash around him. I will put your authority in his hand, and he will be a father to the dwellers of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> I will place on his shoulder the key to the house of David. What he opens no one can shut, and what he shuts no one can open.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Revelation 3:7.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> I will drive him like a peg into a firm place, and he will be a throne of glory for the house of his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> So they will hang on him all the glory of his father’s house: the descendants and the offshoots—all the lesser vessels, from bowls to every kind of jar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, the peg driven into a firm place will give way; it will be sheared off and fall, and the load upon it will be cut down.”<br />Indeed, the LORD has spoken.</span>
</div>
</div>
