## Woe to Those Who Rely on Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Jeremiah 17:5; Deuteronomy 17:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who rely on horses,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who trust in their abundance of chariots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in their multitude of horsemen.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They do not look to the Holy One of Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not seek the LORD.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet He too is wise and brings disaster;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He does not call back His words.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will rise up against the house of the wicked</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against the allies of evildoers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Ezekiel 20:33–34; Ezekiel 28:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Egyptians are men, not God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their horses are flesh, not spirit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When the LORD stretches out His hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the helper will stumble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one he helps will fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">both will perish together.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; Amos 3:8; Isaiah 42:13"> For this is what the LORD has said to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Like a lion roaring</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or a young lion over its prey—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and though a band of shepherds is called out against it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is not terrified by their shouting</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or subdued by their clamor—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so the LORD of Hosts will come down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to do battle on Mount Zion and its heights.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Psalms 37:40; Isaiah 37:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like birds hovering overhead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so the LORD of Hosts will protect Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will shield it and deliver it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will pass over it and preserve it.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 3:14; Isaiah 55:7"> Return to the One against whom you have so blatantly rebelled, O children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 2:20; Ezekiel 36:25"> For on that day, every one of you will reject the idols of silver and gold that your own hands have sinfully made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 14:25; Genesis 49:15; Isaiah 37:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Then Assyria will fall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but not by the sword of man;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a sword will devour them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but not one made by mortals.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will flee before the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their young men will be put to forced labor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 29:6; Leviticus 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their rock will pass away for fear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their princes will panic at the sight of the battle standard,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">declares the LORD, whose fire is in Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose furnace is in Jerusalem.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
