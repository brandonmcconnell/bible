<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he trusts in You.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
