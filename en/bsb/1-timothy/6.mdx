## Serving with Honor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Peter 2:17–20; Titus 2:8–10"> All who are under the yoke of slavery should regard their masters as fully worthy of honor, so that God’s name and our teaching will not be discredited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; 1 Timothy 4:11; Genesis 16:4–5"> Those who have believing masters should not show disrespect because they are brothers, but should serve them all the more, since those receiving their good service are beloved believers. Teach and encourage these principles.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Reject False Doctrines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:3; Romans 16:17; 1 Timothy 1:10"> If anyone teaches another doctrine and disagrees with the sound words of our Lord Jesus Christ and with godly teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 3:4; 1 Timothy 3:6"> he is conceited and understands nothing. Instead, he has an unhealthy interest in controversies and disputes about words, out of which come envy, strife, abusive talk, evil suspicions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3"> and constant friction between men of depraved mind who are devoid of the truth. These men regard godliness as a means of gain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR include Withdraw yourself from such.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Godliness with Contentment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:8; Proverbs 16:8"> Of course, godliness with contentment is great gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 1:21; Psalms 49:17; Luke 12:20–21"> For we brought nothing into the world, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR so certainly">b</a></sup>we cannot carry anything out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:25–33"> But if we have food and clothing, we will be content with these.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 3:7"> Those who want to be rich, however, fall into temptation and become ensnared by many foolish and harmful desires that plunge them into ruin and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 1:19; 1 Timothy 6:9; Psalms 32:10"> For the love of money is the root of all kinds of evil. By craving it, some have wandered away from the faith and pierced themselves with many sorrows.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Fight the Good Fight

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 3:17; 1 Timothy 4:12"> But you, O man of God, flee from these things and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, perseverance, and gentleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25–26"> Fight the good fight of the faith. Take hold of the eternal life to which you were called when you made the good confession before many witnesses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:36–37; Matthew 27:11; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I charge you in the presence of God, who gives life to all things, and of Christ Jesus, who made the good confession in His testimony before Pontius Pilate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Keep this commandment without stain or reproach until the appearance of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 1:17"> which the blessed and only Sovereign One—the King of kings and Lord of lords—will bring about in His own time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; John 1:18; 1 John 1:5"> He alone is immortal and dwells in unapproachable light. No one has ever seen Him, nor can anyone see Him. To Him be honor and eternal dominion! Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Charge to the Rich

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; Acts 14:17; Proverbs 27:24"> Instruct those who are rich in the present age not to be conceited and not to put their hope in the uncertainty of wealth, but in God, who richly provides all things for us to enjoy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 John 3:17; Psalms 37:3"> Instruct them to do good, to be rich in good works, and to be generous and ready to share,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; 1 Timothy 6:12; 2 Timothy 2:19"> treasuring up for themselves a firm foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of that which is truly life.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Guard the Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 2 Timothy 2:16; 2 Timothy 1:12–14"> O Timothy, guard what has been entrusted to you. Avoid irreverent, empty chatter and the opposing arguments of so-called “knowledge,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:18; 1 Timothy 1:6; 2 Timothy 4:22"> which some have professed and thus swerved away from the faith.<br />Grace be with you all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include Amen.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
