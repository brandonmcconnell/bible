## A Warning against Apostasy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Now the Spirit expressly states that in later times some will abandon the faith to follow deceitful spirits and the teachings of demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> influenced by the hypocrisy of liars, whose consciences are seared with a hot iron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> They will prohibit marriage and require abstinence from certain foods that God has created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> For every creation of God is good, and nothing that is received with thanksgiving should be rejected,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> because it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Good Servant of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> By pointing out these things to the brothers, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, nourished by the words of faith and sound instruction that you have followed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But reject irreverent, silly myths. Instead, train yourself for godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> For physical exercise is of limited value, but godliness is valuable in every way, holding promise for the present life and for the one to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> This is a trustworthy saying, worthy of full acceptance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> To this end we labor and strive,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="SBL, BYZ, and TR and suffer reproach">a</a></sup>because we have set our hope on the living God, who is the Savior of everyone, and especially of those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> Command and teach these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no one despise your youth, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading of Scripture, to exhortation, and to teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given you through the prophecy spoken over you at the laying on of the hands of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Be diligent in these matters and absorbed in them, so that your progress will be evident to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Pay close attention to your life and to your teaching. Persevere in these things, for by so doing you will save both yourself and those who hear you.</span>
</div>
</div>
