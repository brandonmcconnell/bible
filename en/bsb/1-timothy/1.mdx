## Paul’s Greeting to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:14–17"> To Timothy, my true child in the faith:<br />Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Correcting False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> As I urged you on my departure to Macedonia, you should stay on at Ephesus to instruct certain men not to teach false doctrines</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> or devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies, which promote speculation rather than the stewardship of God’s work, which is by faith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally rather than the stewardship of God in faith">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> The goal of our instruction is the love that comes from a pure heart, a clear conscience, and a sincere faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> Some have strayed from these ways and turned aside to empty talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> They want to be teachers of the law, but they do not understand what they are saying or that which they so confidently assert.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> Now we know that the law is good, if one uses it legitimately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> We realize that law is not enacted for the righteous, but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinful, for the unholy and profane, for killers of father or mother, for murderers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> for the sexually immoral, for homosexuals, for slave traders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or for kidnappers">b</a></sup>and liars and perjurers, and for anyone else who is averse to sound teaching</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> that agrees with the glorious gospel of the blessed God, with which I have been entrusted.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Grace to Paul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who has strengthened me, that He considered me faithful and appointed me to service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> I was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and a violent man; yet because I had acted in ignorance and unbelief, I was shown mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> And the grace of our Lord overflowed to me, along with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> This is a trustworthy saying, worthy of full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the worst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> But for this very reason I was shown mercy, so that in me, the worst of sinners, Christ Jesus might display His perfect patience as an example to those who would believe in Him for eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Now to the King eternal, immortal, and invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> Timothy, my child, I entrust you with this command in keeping with the previous prophecies about you, so that by them you may fight the good fight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> holding on to faith and a good conscience, which some have rejected and thereby shipwrecked their faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan to be taught not to blaspheme.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
