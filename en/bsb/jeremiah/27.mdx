## The Yoke of Nebuchadnezzar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:3; Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 27:12"> At the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A few Hebrew manuscripts and Syriac (see also verses 3 and 12, and Jeremiah 28:1); most Hebrew manuscripts Jehoiakim">a</a></sup>son of Josiah king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Most LXX manuscripts do not include this verse.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:4; Jeremiah 27:12"> This is what the LORD said to me:<br />“Make for yourself a yoke out of leather straps and put it on your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 1:9–3; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Ezekiel 17:15–21"> Send word to the kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon through the envoys who have come to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:19; Jeremiah 10:10; Jeremiah 25:27"> Give them a message from the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, to relay to their masters:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 115:15–16"> By My great power and outstretched arm, I made the earth and the men and beasts on the face of it, and I give it to whom I please.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:14; Jeremiah 25:9; Daniel 2:37–38"> So now I have placed all these lands under the authority of My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. I have even made the beasts of the field subject to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:31; Revelation 13:5–10; Daniel 5:25–31"> All nations will serve him and his son and grandson, until the time of his own land comes; then many nations and great kings will enslave him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:21; Ezekiel 17:19–21; Jeremiah 38:17–19"> As for the nation or kingdom that does not serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and does not place its neck under his yoke, I will punish that nation by sword and famine and plague, declares the LORD, until I have destroyed it by his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 29:8"> But as for you, do not listen to your prophets, your diviners, your interpreters of dreams, your mediums, or your sorcerers who declare, ‘You will not serve the king of Babylon.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:31; Jeremiah 28:16; Jeremiah 23:25"> For they prophesy to you a lie that will serve to remove you from your land; I will banish you and you will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:8; Jeremiah 27:2; Jeremiah 38:2"> But the nation that will put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serve him, I will leave in its own land, to cultivate it and reside in it, declares the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 38:17; 2 Chronicles 36:11–13"> And to Zedekiah king of Judah I spoke the same message: “Put your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon; serve him and his people, and live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31; Jeremiah 27:8; Proverbs 8:36"> Why should you and your people die by sword and famine and plague, as the LORD has decreed against any nation that does not serve the king of Babylon?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Do not listen to the words of the prophets who say, ‘You must not serve the king of Babylon,’ for they are prophesying to you a lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; Jeremiah 6:13–15"> For I have not sent them, declares the LORD, and yet they are prophesying falsely in My name; therefore I will banish you, and you will perish—you and the prophets who prophesy to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 28:3; Daniel 1:2"> Then I said to the priests and to all this people, “This is what the LORD says: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you, saying, ‘Look, very soon now the articles from the house of the LORD will be brought back from Babylon.’ They are prophesying to you a lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 7:34"> Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon and live! Why should this city become a ruin?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:8; Jeremiah 18:20; 1 Samuel 12:19"> If they are indeed prophets and the word of the LORD is with them, let them now plead with the LORD of Hosts that the articles remaining in the house of the LORD, in the palace of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem, not be taken to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 25:13; Jeremiah 52:17–23; 2 Kings 25:17"> For this is what the LORD of Hosts says about the pillars, the sea, the bases, and the rest of the articles that remain in this city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:14–16"> which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he carried Jeconiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jeconiah is a variant of Jehoiachin; see 2 Kings 24:12.">c</a></sup>son of Jehoiakim king of Judah into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon, along with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yes, this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says about the articles that remain in the house of the LORD, in the palace of the king of Judah, and in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Ezra 7:19; Jeremiah 32:5"> ‘They will be carried to Babylon and will remain there until the day I attend to them again,’ declares the LORD. ‘Then I will bring them back and restore them to this place.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
