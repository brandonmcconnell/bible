## Mourning Turned to Joy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; Jeremiah 23:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, I will bring them from the land of the north</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gather them from the farthest parts of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">including the blind and the lame,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">expectant mothers and women in labor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will return as a great assembly!</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O nations, the word of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and proclaim it in distant coastlands:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The One who scattered Israel will gather them and keep them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a shepherd keeps his flock.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremiah 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“A voice is heard in Ramah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">mourning and great weeping,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rachel weeping for her children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and refusing to be comforted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they are no more.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 2:18">b</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremiah 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Keep your voice from weeping</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your eyes from tears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the reward for your work will come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then your children will return</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the land of the enemy.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have surely heard Ephraim’s</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the northern kingdom of Israel; also in verse 20">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">moaning:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You disciplined me severely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like an untrained calf.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Restore me, that I may return,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You are the LORD my God.</span></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “When I restore them from captivity,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or restore their fortunes">f</a></sup>they will once again speak this word in the land of Judah and in its cities: ‘May the LORD bless you, O righteous dwelling place, O holy mountain.’</span>

## The New Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> At this I awoke and looked around. My sleep had been most pleasant to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> “The days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of man and of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> Just as I watched over them to uproot and tear down, to demolish, destroy, and bring disaster, so I will watch over them to build and to plant,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> “In those days, it will no longer be said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The fathers have eaten sour grapes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the teeth of the children are set on edge.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“But this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days, declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put My law in their minds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and inscribe it on their hearts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be My people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">No longer will each man teach his neighbor or his brother,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">saying, ‘Know the LORD,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they will all know Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the least of them to the greatest, declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will forgive their iniquities</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will remember their sins no more.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 8:8–12 and Hebrews 10:16–17">h</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> Thus says the LORD, who gives the sun for light by day, who sets in order the moon and stars for light by night, who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar—the LORD of Hosts is His name:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Only if this fixed order departed from My presence,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">would Israel’s descendants ever cease</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to be a nation before Me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Only if the heavens above could be measured</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the foundations of the earth below searched out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">would I reject all of Israel’s descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of all they have done,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> “The days are coming,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally Behold, the days; alternate MT reading Behold, the days are coming">i</a></sup>declares the LORD, “when this city will be rebuilt for Me, from the tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> The measuring line will once again stretch out straight to the hill of Gareb and then turn toward Goah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> The whole valley of the dead bodies and ashes, and all the fields as far as the Kidron Valley, to the corner of the Horse Gate to the east, will be holy to the LORD. It will never again be uprooted or demolished.”</span>
