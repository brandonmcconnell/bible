## The Good and Bad Figs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:2; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:12–16"> After Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried away Jeconiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jeconiah is a variant of Jehoiachin; see 2 Kings 24:12.">a</a></sup>son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, as well as the officials of Judah and the craftsmen and metalsmiths from Jerusalem, and had brought them to Babylon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or metalsmiths, and had brought them from Jerusalem to Babylon">b</a></sup>the LORD showed me two baskets of figs placed in front of the temple of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Isaiah 5:4; Micah 7:1"> One basket had very good figs, like those that ripen early, but the other basket contained very poor figs, so bad they could not be eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:5–11; Matthew 25:32–33"> “Jeremiah,” the LORD asked, “what do you see?”<br />“Figs!” I replied. “The good figs are very good, but the bad figs are very bad, so bad they cannot be eaten.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Nahum 1:7; Galatians 4:9"> “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Like these good figs, so I regard as good the exiles from Judah, whom I have sent away from this place to the land of the Chaldeans.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:10; Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 33:7"> I will keep My eyes on them for good and will return them to this land. I will build them up and not tear them down; I will plant them and not uproot them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Jeremiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:24–28"> I will give them a heart to know Me, that I am the LORD. They will be My people, and I will be their God, for they will return to Me with all their heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:1; Jeremiah 39:2–9; Jeremiah 24:2"> But like the bad figs, so bad they cannot be eaten,’ says the LORD, ‘so will I deal with Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, and the remnant of Jerusalem—those remaining in this land and those living in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 29:18; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I will make them a horror and an offense to all the kingdoms of the earth, a disgrace and an object of scorn, ridicule, and cursing wherever I have banished them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Ezekiel 5:12–17; Jeremiah 16:4"> And I will send against them sword and famine and plague, until they have perished from the land that I gave to them and their fathers.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
