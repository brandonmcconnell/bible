## The Obedience of the Rechabites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 23:35; Jeremiah 1:3; Jeremiah 25:1"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:55; 1 Chronicles 9:26; 1 Kings 6:5–6"> “Go to the house of the Rechabites, speak to them, and bring them to one of the chambers of the house of the LORD to offer them a drink of wine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, the son of Habazziniah, and his brothers and all his sons—the entire house of the Rechabites—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; 2 Kings 12:9; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> and I brought them into the house of the LORD, to a chamber occupied by the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, a man of God. This room was near the chamber of the officials, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah son of Shallum the doorkeeper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Amos 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9"> Then I set pitchers full of wine and some cups before the men of the house of the Rechabites, and I said to them, “Drink some wine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Chronicles 2:55; 2 Kings 10:15"> “We do not drink wine,” they replied, “for our forefather Jonadab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jonadab is a variant of Jehonadab; here and throughout this chapter; see 2 Kings 10:15.">a</a></sup>son of Rechab commanded us, ‘Neither you nor your descendants are ever to drink wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:10; Genesis 25:27; Nehemiah 8:14–16"> Nor are you ever to build a house or sow seed or plant a vineyard. Those things are not for you. Instead, you must live in tents all your lives, so that you may live a long time in the land where you wander.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:20; Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 1:8–9"> And we have obeyed the voice of our forefather Jonadab son of Rechab in all he commanded us. So we have not drunk wine all our lives—neither we nor our wives nor our sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6; Jeremiah 35:7; Psalms 37:16"> Nor have we built houses in which to live, and we have not owned any vineyards or fields or crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But we have lived in tents and have obeyed and done exactly as our forefather Jonadab commanded us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 4:5–7; Daniel 1:1–2"> So when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon marched into the land, we said: ‘Come, let us go into Jerusalem to escape the armies of the Chaldeans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">b</a></sup>and the Arameans.’ So we have remained in Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judah Rebuked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:33; Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 6:8–10"> “This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Go and tell the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem: ‘Will you not accept discipline and obey My words?’ declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3–4; Jeremiah 7:24–26; Proverbs 1:20–33"> The words of Jonadab son of Rechab have been carried out. He commanded his sons not to drink wine, and they have not drunk it to this very day because they have obeyed the command of their forefather. But I have spoken to you again and again,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally But I have spoken to you, rising up early and speaking,">c</a></sup>and you have not obeyed Me!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Ezekiel 18:30–32; Jeremiah 25:5–6"> Again and again I have sent you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally Rising up early and sending (you), I have sent you">d</a></sup>all My servants the prophets, proclaiming: ‘Turn now, each of you, from your wicked ways, and correct your actions. Do not go after other gods to serve them. Live in the land that I have given to you and your fathers.’ But you have not inclined your ear or listened to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Malachi 1:6; Jeremiah 35:14; Luke 15:11–13"> Yes, the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab carried out the command their forefather gave them, but these people have not listened to Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21"> Therefore this is what the LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Behold, I will bring to Judah and to all the residents of Jerusalem all the disaster I have pronounced against them, because I have spoken to them but they have not obeyed, and I have called to them but they have not answered.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3"> Then Jeremiah said to the house of the Rechabites: “This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Because you have obeyed the command of your forefather Jonadab and have kept all his commandments and have done all that he charged you to do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:19; Luke 21:36; Jeremiah 33:17–18"> this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Jonadab son of Rechab will never fail to have a man to stand before Me.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
