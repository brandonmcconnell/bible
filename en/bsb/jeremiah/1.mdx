## The Call of Jeremiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> These are the words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests in Anathoth in the territory of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in the thirteenth year of the reign of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> and through the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, until the fifth month of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah, when the people of Jerusalem went into exile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> The word of the LORD came to me, saying:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Before I formed you in the womb I knew you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and before you were born I set you apart</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and appointed you as a prophet to the nations.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> “Ah, Lord GOD,” I said, “I surely do not know how to speak, for I am only a child!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> But the LORD told me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do not say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘I am only a child.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For to everyone I send you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you must go,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all that I command you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you must speak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not be afraid of them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I am with you to deliver you,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> Then the LORD reached out His hand, touched my mouth, and said to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I have put My words</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in your mouth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">See, I have appointed you today</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over nations and kingdoms</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to uproot and tear down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to destroy and overthrow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to build and plant.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> And the word of the LORD came to me, asking, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”<br />“I see a branch of an almond tree,” I replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> “You have observed correctly,” said the LORD, “for I am watching<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew for watching sounds like the Hebrew for almond tree.">a</a></sup>over My word to accomplish it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, asking, “What do you see?”<br />“I see a boiling pot,” I replied, “and it is tilting toward us from the north.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> Then the LORD said to me, “Disaster from the north will be poured out on all who live in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For I am about to summon all the clans and kingdoms of the north,” declares the LORD.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their kings will come and set up their thrones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will attack all her surrounding walls</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the other cities of Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will pronounce My judgments against them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all their wickedness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they have forsaken Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have burned incense to other gods</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and worshiped the works of their own hands.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> Get yourself ready.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Gird up your loins.">b</a></sup>Stand up and tell them everything that I command you. Do not be intimidated by them, or I will terrify you before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> Now behold, this day I have made you like a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the whole land—against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests, and the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> They will fight against you but will never overcome you, since I am with you to deliver you,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
