<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremiah 32:24"> For this is what the LORD of Hosts says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Cut down the trees</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and raise a siege ramp against Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This city must be punished;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is nothing but oppression in her midst.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Jeremiah 16:16; Obadiah 1:5–6"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Glean the remnant of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as thoroughly as a vine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Pass your hand once more like a grape gatherer</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over the branches.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 23:17; Jeremiah 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They dress the wound of My people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with very little care,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saying, ‘Peace, peace,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when there is no peace at all.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 31:10; Micah 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore hear, O nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and learn, O congregations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">what will happen to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 8:9; Jeremiah 17:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O earth! I am bringing disaster on this people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fruit of their own schemes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they have paid no attention to My word</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have rejected My instruction.</span></span>

</div>
