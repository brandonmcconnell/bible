## The Fall of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:1–2; Jeremiah 52:4–7; 2 Kings 25:1–12"> In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon marched against Jerusalem with his entire army and laid siege to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6–7; Micah 2:12–13; 2 Kings 25:3–4"> And on the ninth day of the fourth month of Zedekiah’s eleventh year, the city was breached.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:4; Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 39:13"> Then all the officials of the king of Babylon entered and sat in the Middle Gate: Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsekim">a</a></sup>the Rabsaris,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Rabsaris is the title of the chief eunuch in the Assyrian military; also in verse 13.">b</a></sup>Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew Rabmag is the title of the chief soothsayer or chief of princes in the Assyrian military; also in verse 13.">c</a></sup>and all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 2:14; 2 Chronicles 32:5; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> When Zedekiah king of Judah and all the soldiers saw them, they fled. They left the city at night by way of the king’s garden, through the gate between the two walls, and they went out along the route to the Arabah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or the Jordan Valley">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Joshua 5:10; Jeremiah 52:26–27"> But the army of the Chaldeans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians; also in verse 8">e</a></sup>pursued them and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho. They seized him and brought him up to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he pronounced judgment on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 34:19–21; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> There at Riblah the king of Babylon slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and he also killed all the nobles of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; 2 Kings 25:7; Jeremiah 52:11"> Then he put out Zedekiah’s eyes and bound him with bronze chains to take him to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Jeremiah 21:10"> The Chaldeans set fire to the palace of the king and to the houses of the people, and they broke down the walls of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:1; Genesis 37:36; Jeremiah 10:18"> Then Nebuzaradan captain of the guard carried away to Babylon the remnant of the people who had remained in the city, along with the deserters who had defected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 52:16"> But Nebuzaradan left behind in the land of Judah some of the poor people who had no property, and at that time he gave them vineyards and fields.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah Delivered

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Jeremiah 15:21; Job 5:19"> Now Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had given orders about Jeremiah through Nebuzaradan captain of the guard, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 40:4; Proverbs 21:1"> “Take him, look after him, and do not let any harm come to him; do for him whatever he says.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:3; Jeremiah 39:9"> So Nebuzaradan captain of the guard, Nebushazban<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Nebushazban is possibly a variant of Nebo-sarsekim; see verse 3.">f</a></sup>the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the captains of the king of Babylon</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 26:24"> had Jeremiah brought from the courtyard of the guard, and they turned him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, to take him home. So Jeremiah remained among his own people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Jeremiah 32:1–2; Jeremiah 36:1–5"> And while Jeremiah had been confined in the courtyard of the guard, the word of the LORD had come to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:14–15"> “Go and tell Ebed-melech the Cushite that this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘I am about to fulfill My words against this city for harm and not for good, and on that day they will be fulfilled before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 41:1–2; Psalms 91:14–15; Psalms 50:15"> But I will deliver you on that day, declares the LORD, and you will not be delivered into the hands of the men whom you fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Psalms 34:22; Jeremiah 21:9"> For I will surely rescue you so that you do not fall by the sword. Because you have trusted in Me, you will escape with your life like a spoil of war, declares the LORD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
