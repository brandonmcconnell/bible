## The Excellence of the Restored Nation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> While Jeremiah was still confined in the courtyard of the guard, the word of the LORD came to him a second time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 37:26"> “Thus says the LORD who made the earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew who made it">a</a></sup>the LORD who formed it and established it, the LORD is His name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Isaiah 65:24; Ephesians 3:20"> Call to Me, and I will answer and show you great and unsearchable things you do not know.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Habakkuk 1:10; Ezekiel 4:2"> For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says about the houses of this city and the palaces of the kings of Judah that have been torn down for defense against the siege ramps and the sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 21:4–7"> The Chaldeans are coming to fight<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or They are coming to fight the Chaldeans; that is, the Babylonians">b</a></sup>and to fill those places with the corpses of the men I will strike down in My anger and in My wrath. I have hidden My face from this city because of all its wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:10; Isaiah 30:26; Titus 3:5–6"> Nevertheless, I will bring to it health and healing, and I will heal its people and reveal to them the abundance of peace and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 30:3; Amos 9:14–15"> I will restore Judah and Israel from captivity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or restore the fortunes of Judah and Israel">c</a></sup>and will rebuild them as in former times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:2"> And I will cleanse them from all the iniquity they have committed against Me, and will forgive all their sins of rebellion against Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Isaiah 60:5; Zephaniah 3:17–20"> So this city will bring Me renown, joy, praise, and glory before all the nations of the earth, who will hear of all the good I do for it. They will tremble in awe because of all the goodness and prosperity that I will provide for it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:43; Jeremiah 32:36; Ezekiel 37:11"> This is what the LORD says: In this place you say is a wasteland without man or beast, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem that are deserted—inhabited by neither man nor beast—there will be heard again</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:22"> the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of the bride and bridegroom, and the voices of those bringing thank offerings into the house of the LORD, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Give thanks to the LORD of Hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the LORD is good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His loving devotion endures forever.’</span><br />For I will restore the land from captivity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or restore the fortunes of the land">d</a></sup>as in former times, says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Ezekiel 34:12–15; Jeremiah 36:29"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says: In this desolate place, without man or beast, and in all its cities, there will once more be pastures for shepherds to rest their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:32; Luke 15:4; Jeremiah 17:26"> In the cities of the hill country, the foothills,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">e</a></sup>and the Negev, in the land of Benjamin and the cities surrounding Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, the flocks will again pass under the hands of the one who counts them, says the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Covenant with David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–9; Jeremiah 23:5; 1 Chronicles 17:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the days are coming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I will fulfill the gracious promise</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I have spoken</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the house of Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1–5; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">In those days and at that time</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will cause to sprout for David a righteous Branch,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He will administer justice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and righteousness in the land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Isaiah 45:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">In those days Judah will be saved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Jerusalem will dwell securely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and this is the name by which it will be called:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The LORD Our Righteousness.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew YHWH Tsidqenu">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; Psalms 89:29–37; Luke 1:32–33"> For this is what the LORD says: David will never lack a man to sit on the throne of the house of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Revelation 1:6; Deuteronomy 18:1"> nor will the priests who are Levites ever fail to have a man before Me to offer burnt offerings, to burn grain offerings, and to present sacrifices.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 8:22; Psalms 89:37; Isaiah 54:9–10"> “This is what the LORD says: If you can break My covenant with the day and My covenant with the night, so that day and night cease to occupy their appointed time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 89:34; 2 Chronicles 7:18; 2 Samuel 23:5"> then My covenant may also be broken with David My servant and with My ministers the Levites who are priests, so that David will not have a son to reign on his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Hebrews 11:12"> As the hosts of heaven cannot be counted and as the sand on the seashore cannot be measured, so too will I multiply the descendants of My servant David and the Levites who minister before Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Moreover, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 44:13–14"> “Have you not noticed what these people are saying: ‘The LORD has rejected the two families He had chosen’? So they despise My people and no longer regard them as a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 31:35–36; Psalms 104:19"> This is what the LORD says: If I have not established My covenant with the day and the night and the fixed order of heaven and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:37; Isaiah 14:1; Hosea 2:23"> then I would also reject the descendants of Jacob and of My servant David, so as not to take from his descendants rulers over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. For I will restore them from captivity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or restore their fortunes">g</a></sup>and will have compassion on them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
