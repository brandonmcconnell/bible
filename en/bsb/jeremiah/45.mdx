## Jeremiah’s Message to Baruch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 32:12"> This is the word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah when he wrote these words on a scroll at the dictation of Jeremiah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Isaiah 63:9; 2 Corinthians 7:6"> “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:1; Galatians 6:9"> You have said, ‘Woe is me because the LORD has added sorrow to my pain! I am worn out with groaning and have found no rest.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:7–10; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 31:28"> Thus Jeremiah was to say to Baruch:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Thus you shall say to him:">a</a></sup>“This is what the LORD says: Throughout the land I will demolish what I have built and uproot what I have planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:18; Matthew 6:25–32; Jeremiah 21:9"> But as for you, do you seek great things for yourself? Stop seeking! For I will bring disaster on every living creature, declares the LORD, but wherever you go, I will grant your life as a spoil of war.”</span>
</div>
</div>
