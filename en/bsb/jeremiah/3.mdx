## The Wages of the Harlot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Ezekiel 16:26; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">“If a man divorces his wife</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and she leaves him to marry another,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">can he ever return to her?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Would not such a land be completely defiled?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you have played the harlot with many lovers—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you would return to Me?”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Genesis 38:14; Ezekiel 8:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Lift up your eyes to the barren heights and see.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Is there any place where you have not been violated?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You sat beside the highways waiting for your lovers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a nomad in the desert.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have defiled the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with your prostitution and wickedness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the showers have been withheld,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no spring rains have fallen.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet you have the brazen look of a prostitute;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you refuse to be ashamed.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 3:19; Psalms 71:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you not just called to Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘My Father, You are my friend from youth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Isaiah 57:16; Ezekiel 22:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will He be angry forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will He be indignant to the end?’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This you have spoken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you keep doing all the evil you can.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Judah Follows Israel’s Example

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:7–17; Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 7:24"> Now in the days of King Josiah, the LORD said to me, “Have you seen what faithless Israel has done? She has gone up on every high hill and under every green tree to prostitute herself there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Hosea 6:1–4; Hosea 14:1"> I thought that after she had done all these things, she would return to Me. But she did not return, and her unfaithful sister Judah saw it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1; Hosea 4:15–17; Isaiah 50:1"> She saw<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="DSS, one LXX manuscript, and Syriac; MT I saw">a</a></sup>that because faithless Israel had committed adultery, I gave her a certificate of divorce and sent her away. Yet that unfaithful sister Judah had no fear and prostituted herself as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Isaiah 57:6; Jeremiah 3:2"> Indifferent to her own infidelity, Israel had defiled the land and committed adultery with stones and trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Jeremiah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 34:33–18"> Yet in spite of all this, her unfaithful sister Judah did not return to Me with all her heart, but only in pretense,” declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Call to Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:11; Ezekiel 16:51–52; Hosea 11:7"> And the LORD said to me, “Faithless Israel has shown herself more righteous than unfaithful Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Ezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Go, proclaim this message toward the north:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Return, O faithless Israel,’ declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘I will no longer look on you with anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I am merciful,’ declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘I will not be angry forever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only acknowledge your guilt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you have rebelled against the LORD your God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have scattered your favors to foreign gods</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">under every green tree</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have not obeyed My voice,’”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 31:32"> “Return, O faithless children,” declares the LORD, “for I am your master, and I will take you—one from a city and two from a family—and bring you to Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:4; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 4:11–12"> Then I will give you shepherds after My own heart, who will feed you with knowledge and understanding.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 37:26"> “In those days, when you multiply and increase in the land,” declares the LORD, “they will no longer discuss the ark of the covenant of the LORD. It will never come to mind, and no one will remember it or miss it, nor will another one be made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Micah 4:1–5; Jeremiah 17:12"> At that time they will call Jerusalem The Throne of the LORD, and all the nations will be gathered in Jerusalem to honor the name of the LORD. They will no longer follow the stubbornness of their evil hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Hosea 1:11; Amos 9:15"> In those days the house of Judah will walk with the house of Israel, and they will come together from the land of the north to the land that I gave to your fathers as an inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:4; Jeremiah 31:9; Isaiah 63:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, ‘How I long to make you My sons</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and give you a desirable land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the most beautiful inheritance</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of all the nations!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I thought you would call Me ‘Father’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and never turn away from following Me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 5:11; Hosea 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as a woman may betray her husband,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so you have betrayed Me, O house of Israel,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremiah 31:9; Jeremiah 2:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">A voice is heard on the barren heights,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the children of Israel weeping and begging for mercy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they have perverted their ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and forgotten the LORD their God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 14:1; Hosea 14:4; Zechariah 13:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Return, O faithless children,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will heal your faithlessness.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Here we are. We come to You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for You are the LORD our God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely deception comes from the hills,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and commotion from the mountains.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Surely the salvation of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is in the LORD our God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Ezekiel 16:63; Ezekiel 16:61"> <span className="pl pl-1">From our youth, that shameful god</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has consumed what our fathers have worked for—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their flocks and herds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their sons and daughters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:21; Proverbs 5:13; Isaiah 50:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let us lie down in our shame;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let our disgrace cover us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We have sinned against the LORD our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">both we and our fathers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from our youth even to this day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we have not obeyed the voice of the LORD our God.”</span></span>
</div>
