## The Restoration of Israel and Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:15; Jeremiah 1:1–2"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Write in a book all the words that I have spoken to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 16:15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will restore from captivity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or restore the fortunes of">a</a></sup>My people Israel and Judah, declares the LORD. I will restore them to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they will possess it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> These are the words that the LORD spoke concerning Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> Yes, this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“A cry of panic is heard—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a cry of terror, not of peace.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Joel 2:6; Jeremiah 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ask now, and see:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Can a male give birth?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why then do I see every man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with his hands on his stomach like a woman in labor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every face turned pale?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">How awful that day will be!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">None will be like it!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is the time of Jacob’s distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he will be saved out of it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nahum 1:13; Ezekiel 34:27; Isaiah 9:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD of Hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will break the yoke off their necks</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and tear off their bonds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no longer will strangers enslave them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead, they will serve the LORD their God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and David their king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whom I will raise up for them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for you, O Jacob My servant, do not be afraid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not be dismayed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will surely save you out of a distant place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your descendants from the land of their captivity!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jacob will return to quiet and ease,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with no one to make him afraid.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am with you to save you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Though I will completely destroy all the nations to which I have scattered you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not completely destroy you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet I will discipline you justly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will by no means leave you unpunished.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> For this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your injury is incurable;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your wound is grievous.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Jeremiah 8:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one to plead your cause,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no remedy for your sores,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no recovery for you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Job 30:21; Lamentations 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">All your lovers have forgotten you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they no longer seek you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I have struck you as an enemy would,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the discipline of someone cruel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of your great iniquity</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your numerous sins.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 30:12; Jeremiah 6:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you cry out over your wound?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your pain has no cure!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because of your great iniquity</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your numerous sins</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have done these things to you.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; Isaiah 33:1; Exodus 23:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless, all who devour you will be devoured,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all your adversaries—every one of them—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will go off into exile.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who plundered you will be plundered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all who raided you will be raided.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Jeremiah 33:6; 1 Peter 2:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will restore your health and heal your wounds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they call you an outcast,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Zion, for whom no one cares.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will restore the fortunes of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or restore from captivity">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jacob’s tents</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have compassion on his dwellings.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the city will be rebuilt on her own ruins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the palace will stand in its rightful place.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thanksgiving will proceed from them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a sound of celebration.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will multiply them, and they will not be decreased;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will honor them, and they will not be belittled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their children will be as in days of old,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their congregation will be established before Me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will punish all their oppressors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their leader will be one of their own,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their ruler will arise from their midst.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will bring him near, and he will approach Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for who would dare on his own to approach Me?”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 31:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And you will be My people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will be your God.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the storm of the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has gone out with fury,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a whirlwind swirling down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">upon the heads of the wicked.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fierce anger of the LORD will not turn back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until He has fully accomplished the purposes of His heart.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the days to come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will understand this.</span></span>
</div>
