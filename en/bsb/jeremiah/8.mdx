## Judah’s Sin and Punishment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:5; 2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 7:32–34"> “At that time,” declares the LORD, “the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of the officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the people of Jerusalem will be removed from their graves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:5; Jeremiah 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> They will be exposed to the sun and moon, and to all the host of heaven which they have loved, served, followed, consulted, and worshiped. Their bones will not be gathered up or buried, but will become like dung lying on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:3"> And wherever I have banished them, the remnant of this evil family will choose death over life,” declares the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Micah 7:8; Hosea 6:1"> So you are to tell them this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do men fall and not get up again?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Does one turn away and not return?</span></span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12–13; Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I will give their wives to other men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their fields to new owners.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For from the least of them to the greatest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all are greedy for gain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from prophet to priest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all practice deceit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Lamentations 2:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They dress the wound of the daughter of My people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with very little care,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">saying, ‘Peace, peace,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when there is no peace at all.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremiah 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will take away their harvest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There will be no grapes on the vine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor figs on the tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and even the leaf will wither.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Whatever I have given them will be lost to them.”</span></span>
