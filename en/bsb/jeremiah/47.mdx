## Judgment on the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Genesis 10:19; 1 Kings 4:24"> This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about the Philistines before Pharaoh struck down Gaza.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:20; Jeremiah 8:16; Isaiah 8:7–8"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“See how the waters are rising from the north</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and becoming an overflowing torrent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will overflow the land and its fullness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the cities and their inhabitants.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The people will cry out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all who dwell in the land will wail</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Judges 5:22; Nahum 3:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">at the sound of the galloping hooves of stallions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the rumbling of chariots,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the clatter of their wheels.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The fathers will not turn back for their sons;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their hands will hang limp.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:7; Deuteronomy 2:23; Isaiah 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the day has come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to destroy all the Philistines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to cut off from Tyre and Sidon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every remaining ally.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, the LORD is about to destroy the Philistines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the remnant from the coasts of Caphtor.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Crete">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:37; Jeremiah 25:20; Jeremiah 41:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Gaza will shave their heads in mourning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Ashkelon will be silenced.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">O remnant of their valley,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean coast or plain">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how long will you gash yourself?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:3–5; Jeremiah 12:12; John 18:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Alas, O sword of the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how long until you rest?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Return to your sheath;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cease and be still!’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">How can it rest</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when the LORD has commanded it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has appointed it against Ashkelon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the shore of its coastland.”</span></span>
</div>
