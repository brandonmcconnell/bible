## Naomi Becomes a Widow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 26:1; Ezekiel 14:13"> In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. And a certain man from Bethlehem in Judah, with his wife and two sons, went to reside in the land of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:19; Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 17:21"> The man’s name was Elimelech, his wife’s name was Naomi, and the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah, and they entered the land of Moab and settled there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; Psalms 34:19; Hebrews 12:6"> Then Naomi’s husband Elimelech died, and she was left with her two sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:5; 1 Kings 11:1–2; Deuteronomy 23:3"> who took Moabite women as their wives, one named Orpah and the other named Ruth.<br />And after they had lived in Moab about ten years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Psalms 89:30–32; Isaiah 49:21"> both Mahlon and Chilion also died, and Naomi was left without her two sons and without her husband.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ruth’s Loyalty to Naomi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 132:15; Exodus 3:16; Psalms 111:5"> When Naomi heard in Moab that the LORD had attended to His people by providing them with food, she and her daughters-in-law prepared to leave the land of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 8:3; Ruth 1:14; Exodus 18:27"> Accompanied by her two daughters-in-law, she left the place where she had been living and set out on the road leading back to the land of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Ruth 1:5; Colossians 3:18"> Then Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, “Go back, each of you to your mother’s home. May the LORD show you loving devotion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">a</a></sup>as you have shown to your dead and to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 3:1; Acts 20:37; Genesis 29:11"> May the LORD enable each of you to find rest in the home of your new husband.”<br />And she kissed them as they wept aloud</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 16:3; Psalms 119:63"> and said, “Surely we will return with you to your people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Genesis 38:11"> But Naomi replied, “Return home, my daughters. Why would you go with me? Are there still sons in my womb to become your husbands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; 1 Timothy 5:9"> Return home, my daughters. Go on, for I am too old to have another husband. Even if I thought there was hope for me to have a husband tonight and to bear sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 19:21; Judges 2:15; Psalms 32:4"> would you wait for them to grow up? Would you refrain from having husbands? No, my daughters, it is much more bitter for me than for you, because the hand of the LORD has gone out against me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Matthew 16:24; Matthew 10:37"> Again they wept aloud, and Orpah kissed her mother-in-law goodbye, but Ruth clung to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 11:24; Joshua 24:15; Zephaniah 1:6"> “Look,” said Naomi, “your sister-in-law has gone back to her people and her gods; follow her back home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:21; Ruth 2:11–12; 2 Kings 2:2–6"> But Ruth replied:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do not urge me to leave you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or to turn from following you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For wherever you go, I will go,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wherever you live, I will live;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your people will be my people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your God will be my God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:17; 1 Kings 2:23; 2 Kings 6:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where you die, I will die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there I will be buried.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May the LORD punish me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ever so severely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">if anything but death</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">separates you and me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:14; Ephesians 6:10; Acts 2:42"> When Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her, she stopped trying to persuade her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Return to Bethlehem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:10; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:15"> So Naomi and Ruth traveled until they came to Bethlehem. When they entered Bethlehem, the whole town was stirred because of them, and the women of the town exclaimed, “Can this be Naomi?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 6:4; Hebrews 12:11; Psalms 73:14"> “Do not call me Naomi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Naomi means pleasant.">b</a></sup>” she replied. “Call me Mara,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Mara means bitter.">c</a></sup>because the Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew Shaddai; also in verse 21">d</a></sup>has dealt quite bitterly with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Job 1:21; Job 16:8"> I went away full, but the LORD has brought me back empty. Why call me Naomi? After all, the LORD has testified against me, and the Almighty has afflicted me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:23; 2 Samuel 21:9; Exodus 9:31–32"> So Naomi returned from the land of Moab with her daughter-in-law Ruth the Moabitess. And they arrived in Bethlehem at the beginning of the barley harvest.</span>
</div>
</div>
