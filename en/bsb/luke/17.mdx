<div className="bp">

</div>

## The Power of Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> The apostles said to the Lord, “Increase our faith!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> And the Lord answered, “If you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you can say to this mulberry tree, ‘Be uprooted and planted in the sea,’ and it will obey you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> Which of you whose servant comes in from plowing or shepherding in the field will say to him, ‘Come at once and sit down to eat’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> Instead, won’t he tell him, ‘Prepare my meal and dress yourself to serve me while I eat and drink, and afterward you may eat and drink’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Does he thank the servant because he did what he was told?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> So you also, when you have done everything commanded of you, should say, ‘We are unworthy servants; we have only done our duty.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ten Lepers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> While Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem, He was passing between Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> As He entered one of the villages, He was met by ten lepers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A leper was one afflicted with a skin disease. See Leviticus 13.">a</a></sup>They stood at a distance</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> and raised their voices, shouting, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> When Jesus saw them, He said, “Go, show yourselves to the priests.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Leviticus 14:1–32.">b</a></sup>And as they were on their way, they were cleansed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> When one of them saw that he was healed, he came back, praising God in a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> He fell facedown at Jesus’ feet in thanksgiving to Him—and he was a Samaritan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> “Were not all ten cleansed?” Jesus asked. “Where then are the other nine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalms 106:13"> Was no one found except this foreigner to return and give glory to God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> Then Jesus said to him, “Rise and go; your faith has made you well!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or has saved you">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming of the Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> When asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would come, Jesus replied, “The kingdom of God will not come with observable signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> Nor will people say, ‘Look, here it is,’ or ‘There it is.’ For you see, the kingdom of God is in your midst.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or within you or within your grasp">d</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> Then He said to the disciples, “The time is coming when you will long to see one of the days of the Son of Man, but you will not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> People will tell you, ‘Look, there He is!’ or ‘Look, here He is!’ Do not go out or chase after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> For just as the lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one end to the other, so will be the Son of Man in His day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first He must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> Just as it was in the days of Noah, so also will it be in the days of the Son of Man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> People were eating and drinking, marrying and being given in marriage, up to the day Noah entered the ark. Then the flood came and destroyed them all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> It was the same in the days of Lot: People were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> But on the day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained down from heaven and destroyed them all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> It will be just like that on the day the Son of Man is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> On that day, let no one on the housetop come down to retrieve his possessions. Likewise, let no one in the field return for anything he has left behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember Lot’s wife!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whoever tries to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life will preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I tell you, on that night two people will be in one bed: one will be taken and the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> Two women will be grinding grain together: one will be taken and the other left.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="TR includes 36 Two men will be in the field. One will be taken and the other left; see Matthew 24:40.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> “Where, Lord?” they asked.<br />Jesus answered, “Wherever there is a carcass, there the vultures will gather.”</span>
</div>
