## The Faith of the Centurion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> When Jesus had concluded His discourse in the hearing of the people, He went to Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> There a highly valued servant of a centurion was sick and about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> When the centurion heard about Jesus, he sent some Jewish elders to ask Him to come and heal his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> They came to Jesus and pleaded with Him earnestly, “This man is worthy to have You grant this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> for he loves our nation and has built our synagogue.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> So Jesus went with them. But when He was not far from the house, the centurion sent friends with the message: “Lord, do not trouble Yourself, for I am not worthy to have You come under my roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Psalms 33:9; Luke 4:36"> That is why I did not consider myself worthy to come to You. But just say the word, and my servant will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For I myself am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I tell one to go, and he goes, and another to come, and he comes. I tell my servant to do something, and he does it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> When Jesus heard this, He marveled at the centurion. Turning to the crowd following Him, He said, “I tell you, not even in Israel have I found such great faith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> And when the messengers returned to the house, they found the servant in good health.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Raises a Widow’s Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> Soon afterward, Jesus went to a town called Nain. His disciples went with Him, accompanied by a large crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> As He approached the town gate, He saw a dead man being carried out, the only son of his mother, and she was a widow. And a large crowd from the town was with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> When the Lord saw her, He had compassion on her and said, “Do not weep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> Then He went up and touched the coffin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally bier, probably a wooden plank or open coffin">a</a></sup>and those carrying it stood still. “Young man,” He said, “I tell you, get up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> And the dead man sat up and began to speak! Then Jesus gave him back to his mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> A sense of awe swept over all of them, and they glorified God. “A great prophet has appeared among us!” they said. “God has visited His people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> And the news about Jesus spread throughout Judea and all the surrounding region.</span>
</div>
</div>

## John’s Inquiry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> Then John’s disciples informed him about all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> So John called two of his disciples and sent them to ask the Lord, “Are You the One who was to come, or should we look for someone else?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> When the men came to Jesus, they said, “John the Baptist sent us to ask, ‘Are You the One who was to come, or should we look for someone else?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalms 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> At that very hour Jesus healed many people of their diseases, afflictions, and evil spirits, and He gave sight to many who were blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> So He replied, “Go back and report to John what you have seen and heard: The blind receive sight, the lame walk, the lepers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A leper was one afflicted with a skin disease. See Leviticus 13.">b</a></sup>are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, and the good news is preached to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> Blessed is the one who does not fall away on account of Me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or who is not offended by Me">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Testifies about John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> After John’s messengers had left, Jesus began to speak to the crowds about John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed swaying in the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> Otherwise, what did you go out to see? A man dressed in fine clothes? Look, those who wear elegant clothing and live in luxury are found in palaces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> What then did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> This is the one about whom it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, I will send My messenger ahead of You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare Your way before You.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Malachi 3:1">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> I tell you, among those born of women there is no one greater than John, yet even the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> All the people who heard this, even the tax collectors, acknowledged God’s justice. For they had received the baptism of John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> But the Pharisees and experts in the law rejected God’s purpose for themselves, because they had not been baptized by John.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> “To what, then, can I compare the men of this generation? What are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children sitting in the marketplace and calling out to one another:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We played the flute for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you did not dance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we sang a dirge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you did not weep.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> For John the Baptist came neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and you say, ‘He has a demon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and you say, ‘Look at this glutton and drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> But wisdom is vindicated by all her children.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Sinful Woman Anoints Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> Then one of the Pharisees invited Jesus to eat with him, and He entered the Pharisee’s house and reclined at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> When a sinful woman from that town learned that Jesus was dining there, she brought an alabaster jar of perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> As she stood behind Him at His feet weeping, she began to wet His feet with her tears and wipe them with her hair. Then she kissed His feet and anointed them with the perfume.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> When the Pharisee who had invited Jesus saw this, he said to himself, “If this man were a prophet, He would know who this is and what kind of woman is touching Him—for she is a sinner!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> But Jesus answered him, “Simon, I have something to tell you.”<br />“Tell me, Teacher,” he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> “Two men were debtors to a certain moneylender. One owed him five hundred denarii,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.">e</a></sup>and the other fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> When they were unable to repay him, he forgave both of them. Which one, then, will love him more?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> “I suppose the one who was forgiven more,” Simon replied.<br />“You have judged correctly,” Jesus said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> And turning toward the woman, He said to Simon, “Do you see this woman? When I entered your house, you did not give Me water for My feet, but she wet My feet with her tears and wiped them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> You did not greet Me with a kiss, but she has not stopped kissing My feet since I arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> You did not anoint My head with oil, but she has anointed My feet with perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> Therefore I tell you, her many sins have been forgiven, for she has loved much. But he who has been forgiven little loves little.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> Then Jesus said to her, “Your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> But those at the table began to say to themselves, “Who is this who even forgives sins?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> And Jesus told the woman, “Your faith has saved you; go in peace.”</span>
</div>
</div>
