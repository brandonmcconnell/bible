## Jesus and Zacchaeus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> Then Jesus entered Jericho and was passing through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> And there was a man named Zacchaeus, a chief tax collector, who was very wealthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> He was trying to see who Jesus was, but could not see over the crowd because he was small in stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> So he ran on ahead and climbed a sycamore tree to see Him, since Jesus was about to pass that way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> When Jesus came to that place, He looked up and said, “Zacchaeus, hurry down, for I must stay at your house today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> So Zacchaeus hurried down and welcomed Him joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> And all who saw this began to grumble, saying, “He has gone to be the guest of a sinful man!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> But Zacchaeus stood up and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord, half of my possessions I give to the poor, and if I have cheated anyone, I will repay it fourfold.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because this man too is a son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Ten Minas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> While the people were listening to this, Jesus proceeded to tell them a parable, because He was near Jerusalem and they thought the kingdom of God would appear imminently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> So He said, “A man of noble birth went to a distant country to lay claim to his kingship and then return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> Beforehand, he called ten of his servants and gave them ten minas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, he gave each servant one mina. A mina was most likely a silver coin worth a hundred drachmas, that is, about a hundred days’ wages.">a</a></sup>‘Conduct business with this until I return,’ he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his subjects hated him and sent a delegation after him to say, ‘We do not want this man to rule over us.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> When he returned from procuring his kingship, he summoned the servants to whom he had given the money, to find out what each one had earned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> The first servant came forward and said, ‘Master, your mina has produced ten more minas.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> His master replied, ‘Well done, good servant! Because you have been faithful in a very small matter, you shall have authority over ten cities.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> The second servant came and said, ‘Master, your mina has made five minas.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> And to this one he said, ‘You shall have authority over five cities.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> Then another servant came and said, ‘Master, here is your mina, which I have laid away in a piece of cloth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek soudariō">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> For I was afraid of you, because you are a harsh man. You withdraw what you did not deposit and reap what you did not sow.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> His master replied, ‘You wicked servant, I will judge you by your own words. So you knew that I am a harsh man, withdrawing what I did not deposit and reaping what I did not sow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> Why then did you not deposit my money in the bank, and upon my return I could have collected it with interest?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> Then he told those standing by, ‘Take the mina from him and give it to the one who has ten minas.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> ‘Master,’ they said, ‘he already has ten!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> He replied, ‘I tell you that everyone who has will be given more; but the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> And these enemies of mine who were unwilling for me to rule over them, bring them here and slay them in front of me.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> After Jesus had said this, He went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> As He approached Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives, He sent out two of His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying, “Go into the village ahead of you, and as you enter it, you will find a colt tied there, on which no one has ever sat. Untie it and bring it here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> If anyone asks, ‘Why are you untying it?’ tell him, ‘The Lord needs it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> So those who were sent went out and found it just as Jesus had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> As they were untying the colt, its owners asked, “Why are you untying the colt?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> “The Lord needs it,” they answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> Then they led the colt to Jesus, threw their cloaks over it, and put Jesus on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> As He rode along, the people spread their cloaks on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> And as He approached the descent from the Mount of Olives, the whole multitude of disciples began to praise God joyfully in a loud voice for all the miracles they had seen:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed is the King who comes in the name of the Lord!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 118:26">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 148:1.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> But some of the Pharisees in the crowd said to Him, “Teacher, rebuke Your disciples!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> “I tell you,” He answered, “if they remain silent, the very stones will cry out.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Weeps over Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> As Jesus approached Jerusalem and saw the city, He wept over it</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> and said, “If only you had known on this day what would bring you peace! But now it is hidden from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> For the days will come upon you when your enemies will barricade you and surround you and hem you in on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> They will level you to the ground—you and the children within your walls. They will not leave one stone on another, because you did not recognize the time of your visitation from God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally your visitation">e</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> Then Jesus entered the temple courts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally the temple">f</a></sup>and began to drive out those who were selling there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> He declared to them, “It is written: ‘My house will be a house of prayer.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isaiah 56:7">g</a></sup>But you have made it ‘a den of robbers.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jeremiah 7:11">h</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> Jesus was teaching at the temple every day, but the chief priests, scribes, and leaders of the people were intent on killing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> Yet they could not find a way to do so, because all the people hung on His words.</span>
</div>
