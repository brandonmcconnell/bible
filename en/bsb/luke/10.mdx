## Jesus Sends the Seventy-Two

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:7–13; Luke 9:52; Numbers 11:24–26"> After this, the Lord appointed seventy-two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NE, BYZ, and TR seventy; also in verse 17">a</a></sup>others and sent them two by two ahead of Him to every town and place He was about to visit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36–38; John 4:35–38; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> And He told them, “The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into His harvest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Acts 20:29; Ezekiel 2:3–6"> Go! I am sending you out like lambs among wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:35; 2 Kings 4:29; Luke 10:4–12"> Carry no purse or bag or sandals. Do not greet anyone along the road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Matthew 10:12–13; Isaiah 57:19"> Whatever house you enter, begin by saying, ‘Peace to this house.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Ephesians 5:6"> If a man of peace is there, your peace will rest on him; if not, it will return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Matthew 10:10–11; Galatians 6:6"> Stay at the same house, eating and drinking whatever you are offered. For the worker is worthy of his wages.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Leviticus 19:13 and Deuteronomy 24:14–15; cited in 1 Timothy 5:18.">b</a></sup>Do not move around from house to house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Matthew 10:40; Luke 10:10"> If you enter a town and they welcome you, eat whatever is set before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; John 3:5; Luke 10:11"> Heal the sick who are there and tell them, ‘The kingdom of God is near you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 10:14; Acts 13:51"> But if you enter a town and they do not welcome you, go into the streets and declare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:9; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:26"> ‘Even the dust of your town that clings to our feet, we wipe off as a testimony against you. Yet be sure of this: The kingdom of God is near.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:24; Lamentations 4:6"> I tell you, it will be more bearable on that day for Sodom than for that town.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Woe to the Unrepentant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8; Ezekiel 26:1–26"> Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the miracles that were performed in you had been performed in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:22–25; Romans 2:1; Luke 12:47–48"> But it will be more bearable for Tyre and Sidon at the judgment than for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Matthew 4:13; Luke 13:28"> And you, Capernaum, will you be lifted up to heaven? No, you will be brought down to Hades!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:20; Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Whoever listens to you listens to Me; whoever rejects you rejects Me; and whoever rejects Me rejects the One who sent Me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Joyful Return

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Mark 16:17; Luke 9:1"> The seventy-two returned with joy and said, “Lord, even the demons submit to us in Your name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 12:7–9; Isaiah 14:12; Revelation 9:1"> So He told them, “I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:13; Mark 16:18; Romans 16:20"> Behold, I have given you authority to tread on snakes and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy. Nothing will harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:15; Revelation 20:12; Hebrews 12:23"> Nevertheless, do not rejoice that the spirits submit to you, but rejoice that your names are written in heaven.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Prayer of Thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Matthew 13:11–16; Matthew 11:25–27"> At that time Jesus rejoiced in the Holy Spirit and declared, “I praise You, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because You have hidden these things from the wise and learned, and revealed them to little children. Yes, Father, for this was well-pleasing in Your sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:27; John 1:18; John 6:44–46"> All things have been entrusted to Me by My Father. No one knows who the Son is except the Father, and no one knows who the Father is except the Son and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17"> Then Jesus turned to the disciples and said privately, “Blessed are the eyes that see what you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For I tell you that many prophets and kings desired to see what you see but did not see it, and to hear what you hear but did not hear it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Good Samaritan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> One day an expert in the law stood up to test Him. “Teacher,” he asked, “what must I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 3:21–22"> “What is written in the Law?” Jesus replied. “How do you read it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 12:30–31; Matthew 22:37–40; Deuteronomy 6:5"> He answered, “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength and with all your mind’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Deuteronomy 6:5">c</a></sup>and ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Leviticus 19:18">d</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5; Matthew 19:17"> “You have answered correctly,” Jesus said. “Do this and you will live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Romans 4:2; Leviticus 19:34"> But wanting to justify himself, he asked Jesus, “And who is my neighbor?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 88:4; Luke 18:31; Lamentations 2:12"> Jesus took up this question and said, “A man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho when he fell into the hands of robbers. They stripped him, beat him, and went away, leaving him half dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Psalms 69:20; Jeremiah 5:31"> Now by chance a priest was going down the same road, but when he saw him, he passed by on the other side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 27:10; Acts 18:17; Psalms 109:25"> So too, when a Levite came to that spot and saw him, he passed by on the other side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; Jeremiah 38:7–13; Luke 7:13"> But a Samaritan on a journey came upon him, and when he saw him, he had compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 12:20; Psalms 147:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> He went to him and bandaged his wounds, pouring on oil and wine. Then he put him on his own animal, brought him to an inn, and took care of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 14:13; Romans 16:23"> The next day he took out two denarii<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.">e</a></sup>and gave them to the innkeeper. ‘Take care of him,’ he said, ‘and on my return I will repay you for any additional expense.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 7:42; Matthew 21:28–31; Matthew 17:25"> Which of these three do you think was a neighbor to the man who fell into the hands of robbers?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> “The one who showed him mercy,” replied the expert in the law.<br />Then Jesus told him, “Go and do likewise.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Martha and Mary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> As they traveled along, Jesus entered a village where a woman named Martha welcomed Him into her home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Luke 8:35; John 12:3"> She had a sister named Mary, who sat at the Lord’s feet listening to His message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> But Martha was distracted by all the preparations to be made. She came to Jesus and said, “Lord, do You not care that my sister has left me to serve alone? Tell her to help me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> “Martha, Martha,” the Lord replied, “you are worried and upset about many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 16:5–6; John 10:27–28"> But only one thing is necessary. Mary has chosen the good portion, and it will not be taken away from her.”</span>
</div>
</div>
