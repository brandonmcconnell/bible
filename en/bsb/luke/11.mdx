## Ask, Seek, Knock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:1–8"> Then Jesus said to them, “Suppose one of you goes to his friend at midnight and says, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves of bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> because a friend of mine has come to me on a journey, and I have nothing to set before him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Galatians 6:17; Luke 7:6"> And suppose the one inside answers, ‘Do not bother me. My door is already shut, and my children and I are in bed. I cannot get up to give you anything.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Colossians 4:12; Romans 15:30"> I tell you, even though he will not get up to provide for him because of his friendship, yet because of the man’s persistence, he will get up and give him as much as he needs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 11:24; John 16:23–24; Matthew 21:22"> So I tell you: Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:3; Lamentations 3:54–58; Psalms 31:22"> For everyone who asks receives; he who seeks finds; and to him who knocks, the door will be opened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> What father among you, if his son asks for a fish,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="BYZ and TR if his son asks for bread, will give him a stone, or for a fish; see Matthew 7:9–10.">d</a></sup>will give him a snake instead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Luke 10:19; Ezekiel 2:6"> Or if he asks for an egg, will give him a scorpion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; Romans 8:32"> So if you who are evil know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A House Divided

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:22–24; Matthew 9:32–34; Mark 7:32–37"> One day Jesus was driving out a demon that was mute. And when the demon was gone, the man who had been mute spoke. The crowds were amazed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:34; Matthew 12:24–30; John 8:48"> but some of them said, “It is by Beelzebul,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="WH Beezeboul; Vulgate Beelzebub; also in verses 18 and 19">e</a></sup>the prince of the demons, that He drives out demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 6:30; 1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12"> And others tested Him by demanding a sign from heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 3:23–27; Matthew 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself will be laid waste, and a house divided against a house will fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 12:26; Matthew 12:31–34; Luke 11:15"> If Satan is divided against himself, how can his kingdom stand? After all, you say that I drive out demons by Beelzebul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:49; Job 15:6; Romans 3:19"> And if I drive out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons drive them out? So then, they will be your judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Matthew 12:28; Luke 10:9"> But if I drive out demons by the finger of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:27; Matthew 12:29"> When a strong man, fully armed, guards his house, his possessions are secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 John 3:8"> But when someone stronger attacks and overpowers him, he takes away the armor in which the man trusted, and then he divides up his plunder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 9:50; Mark 9:40"> He who is not with Me is against Me, and he who does not gather with Me scatters.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Unclean Spirit Returns

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 12:43–45; 1 Peter 5:8; Job 2:2"> When an unclean spirit comes out of a man, it passes through arid places seeking rest and does not find it. Then it says, ‘I will return to the house I left.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; 2 Peter 2:10–19; Jude 1:8–13"> On its return, it finds the house swept clean and put in order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 5:14; Matthew 12:45; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> Then it goes and brings seven other spirits more wicked than itself, and they go in and dwell there. And the final plight of that man is worse than the first.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 6:47–48; Luke 8:21; Psalms 112:1"> But He replied, “Blessed rather are those who hear the word of God and obey it.”</span>

</div>

## The Sign of Jonah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Mark 8:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> As the crowds were increasing, Jesus said, “This is a wicked generation. It demands a sign, but none will be given it except the sign of Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 12:40–42; Jonah 1:17; Jonah 3:2–10"> For as Jonah was a sign to the Ninevites, so the Son of Man will be a sign to this generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; 1 Kings 10:1–13; Romans 2:27"> The Queen of the South will rise at the judgment with the men of this generation and condemn them; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and now One greater than Solomon is here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Luke 11:31; Jonah 3:5–4"> The men of Nineveh will stand at the judgment with this generation and condemn it; for they repented at the preaching of Jonah, and now One greater than Jonah is here.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lamp of the Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:16–17; Mark 4:21–22; Matthew 5:15–16"> No one lights a lamp and puts it in a cellar or under a basket. Instead, he sets it on a stand, so those who enter can see the light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:22–23; Acts 26:18; Jeremiah 5:21"> Your eye is the lamp of your body. When your eyes are good,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally When your eye is sound; see Proverbs 22:9.">f</a></sup>your whole body also is full of light. But when they are bad,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally when it is evil; see Proverbs 23:6 and Proverbs 28:22.">g</a></sup>your body is full of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; 2 Peter 1:9; James 3:13–17"> Be careful, then, that the light within you is not darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; Proverbs 6:23; Proverbs 4:18–19"> So if your whole body is full of light, with no part of it in darkness, you will be radiant, as though a lamp were shining on you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Woes to Pharisees and Experts in the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 7:36; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23; Luke 14:1"> As Jesus was speaking, a Pharisee invited Him to dine with him; so He went in and reclined at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Mark 7:2–5; John 3:25"> But the Pharisee was surprised to see that Jesus did not first wash before the meal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 30:12; Genesis 6:5"> Then the Lord said, “Now you Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and dish, but inside you are full of greed and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 15:36; Matthew 23:26"> You fools! Did not the One who made the outside make the inside as well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 1:27; Titus 1:15"> But give as alms the things that are within you, and behold, everything will be clean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Luke 18:12; Proverbs 21:3"> Woe to you Pharisees! For you pay tithes of mint, rue, and every herb, but you disregard justice and the love of God. You should have practiced the latter without neglecting the former.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7–11"> Woe to you Pharisees! For you love the chief seats in the synagogues and the greetings in the marketplaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 23:27–28; Psalms 5:9; Acts 23:3"> Woe to you! For you are like unmarked graves, which men walk over without even noticing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 9:40; Matthew 22:35; Jeremiah 6:10"> One of the experts in the law told Him, “Teacher, when You say these things, You insult us as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; Matthew 23:2–4"> “Woe to you as well, experts in the law!” He replied. “For you weigh men down with heavy burdens, but you yourselves will not lift a finger to lighten their load.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:51; Matthew 23:29–33"> Woe to you! For you build tombs for the prophets, but it was your fathers who killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 5:10; Matthew 23:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> So you are witnesses consenting to the deeds of your fathers: They killed the prophets, and you build their tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; Ephesians 4:11"> Because of this, the wisdom of God said, ‘I will send them prophets and apostles; some of them they will kill and others they will persecute.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Revelation 18:20–24; 2 Kings 24:4"> As a result, this generation will be charged with the blood of all the prophets that has been shed since the foundation of the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Matthew 23:35; Hebrews 11:4"> from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who was killed between the altar and the sanctuary.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally the house">h</a></sup>Yes, I tell you, all of it will be charged to this generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Luke 11:45–46"> Woe to you experts in the law! For you have taken away the key to knowledge. You yourselves have not entered, and you have hindered those who were entering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Jeremiah 20:10; Luke 20:20"> As Jesus went on from there, the scribes and Pharisees began to oppose Him bitterly and to ply Him with questions about many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 12:13; Matthew 22:15; Matthew 22:35"> waiting to catch Him in something He might say.</span>
</div>
</div>
