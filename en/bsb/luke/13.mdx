## The Parable of the Barren Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> Then Jesus told this parable: “A man had a fig tree that was planted in his vineyard. He went to look for fruit on it but did not find any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> So he said to the keeper of the vineyard, ‘Look, for the past three years I have come to search for fruit on this fig tree and haven’t found any. Therefore cut it down!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="SBL, NE, WH Cut it down!">a</a></sup>Why should it use up the soil?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Joel 2:17; Romans 10:1"> ‘Sir,’ the man replied, ‘leave it alone again this year, until I dig around it and fertilize it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> If it bears fruit next year, fine. But if not, you can cut it down.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals a Disabled Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> One Sabbath Jesus was teaching in one of the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> and a woman there had been disabled by a spirit for eighteen years. She was hunched over and could not stand up straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> When Jesus saw her, He called her over and said, “Woman, you are set free from your disability.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> Then He placed His hands on her, and immediately she straightened up and began to glorify God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> But the synagogue leader was indignant that Jesus had healed on the Sabbath. “There are six days for work,” he told the crowd. “So come and be healed on those days and not on the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> “You hypocrites!” the Lord replied. “Does not each of you on the Sabbath untie his ox or donkey from the stall and lead it to water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> Then should not this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan has kept bound for eighteen long years, be released from her bondage on the Sabbath day?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Psalms 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> When Jesus said this, all His adversaries were humiliated. And the whole crowd rejoiced at all the glorious things He was doing.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:30–34; Luke 17:21; Matthew 13:31–32"> Then Jesus asked, “What is the kingdom of God like? To what can I compare it?</span>

<div className="bp">

## The Narrow Door

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> Then Jesus traveled throughout the towns and villages, teaching as He made His way toward Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> “Lord,” someone asked Him, “will only a few people be saved?”<br />Jesus answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> “Make every effort to enter through the narrow door. For many, I tell you, will try to enter and will not be able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> After the master of the house gets up and shuts the door, you will stand outside knocking and saying, ‘Lord, open the door for us.’<br />But he will reply, ‘I do not know where you are from.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Then you will say, ‘We ate and drank with you, and you taught in our streets.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> And he will answer, ‘I tell you, I do not know where you are from. Depart from me, all you evildoers.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth when you see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the prophets in the kingdom of God, but you yourselves are thrown out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> People will come from east and west and north and south, and will recline at the table in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> And indeed, some who are last will be first, and some who are first will be last.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lament over Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9–11; Psalms 11:1–2; Matthew 14:1"> At that very hour, some Pharisees came to Jesus and told Him, “Leave this place and get away, because Herod wants to kill You.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 5:9; John 17:4–5"> But Jesus replied, “Go tell that fox, ‘Look, I will keep driving out demons and healing people today and tomorrow, and on the third day I will reach My goal.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Matthew 21:11; Acts 13:27"> Nevertheless, I must keep going today and tomorrow and the next day, for it is not admissible for a prophet to perish outside of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 23:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Acts 7:52"> O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those sent to her, how often I have longed to gather your children together as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but you were unwilling!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Mark 11:9–10; Matthew 21:9"> Look, your house is left to you desolate. And I tell you that you will not see Me again until you say, ‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 118:26">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
