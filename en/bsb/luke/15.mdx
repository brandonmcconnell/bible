## The Parable of the Lost Sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> Now all the tax collectors and sinners were gathering around to listen to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> So the Pharisees and scribes began to grumble: “This man welcomes sinners and eats with them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Jesus told them this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> “What man among you, if he has a hundred sheep and loses one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the pasture and go after the one that is lost, until he finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> And when he finds it, he joyfully puts it on his shoulders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> comes home, and calls together his friends and neighbors to tell them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my lost sheep!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I tell you that in the same way there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous ones who do not need to repent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Lost Coin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> Or what woman who has ten silver coins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek ten drachmas, each worth about a day’s wages">a</a></sup>and loses one of them does not light a lamp, sweep her house, and search carefully until she finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> And when she finds it, she calls together her friends and neighbors to say, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my lost coin.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> In the same way, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of God’s angels over one sinner who repents.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Prodigal Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> Then Jesus said, “There was a man who had two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> The younger son said to him, ‘Father, give me my share of the estate.’ So he divided his property between them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> After a few days, the younger son got everything together and journeyed to a distant country, where he squandered his wealth in wild living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> After he had spent all he had, a severe famine swept through that country, and he began to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> So he went and hired himself out to a citizen of that country, who sent him into his fields to feed the pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> He longed to fill his belly with the pods the pigs were eating, but no one would give him a thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> Finally he came to his senses and said, ‘How many of my father’s hired servants have plenty of food, but here I am, starving to death!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I will get up and go back to my father and say to him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> I am no longer worthy to be called your son. Make me like one of your hired servants.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> So he got up and went to his father. But while he was still in the distance, his father saw him and was filled with compassion. He ran to his son, embraced him, and kissed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> The son declared, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you. I am no longer worthy to be called your son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="WH includes Make me like one of your hired servants; see verse 19.">b</a></sup>’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> But the father said to his servants, ‘Quick! Bring the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> Bring the fattened calf and kill it. Let us feast and celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> For this son of mine was dead and is alive again! He was lost and is found!’ So they began to celebrate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> Meanwhile the older son was in the field, and as he approached the house, he heard music and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So he called one of the servants and asked what was going on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> ‘Your brother has returned,’ he said, ‘and your father has killed the fattened calf, because he has him back safe and sound.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> The older son became angry and refused to go in. So his father came out and pleaded with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> But he answered his father, ‘Look, all these years I have served you and never disobeyed a commandment of yours. Yet you never gave me even a young goat so I could celebrate with my friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> But when this son of yours returns from squandering your wealth with prostitutes, you kill the fattened calf for him!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> ‘Son, you are always with me,’ the father said, ‘and all that is mine is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> But it was fitting to celebrate and be glad, because this brother of yours was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
