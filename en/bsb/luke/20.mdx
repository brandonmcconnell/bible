## Jesus’ Authority Challenged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> One day as Jesus was teaching the people in the temple courts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally the temple">a</a></sup>and proclaiming the gospel, the chief priests and scribes, together with the elders, came up to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> “Tell us,” they said, “by what authority are You doing these things, and who gave You this authority?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> “I will also ask you a question,” Jesus replied. “Tell Me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> John’s baptism—was it from heaven, or from men?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> They deliberated among themselves and said, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will ask, ‘Why did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ all the people will stone us, for they are convinced that John was a prophet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> So they answered that they did not know where it was from.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> And Jesus replied, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I am doing these things.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Then He proceeded to tell the people this parable: “A man planted a vineyard, rented it out to some tenants, and went away for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> At harvest time, he sent a servant to the tenants to collect his share of the fruit of the vineyard. But the tenants beat the servant and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> So he sent another servant, but they beat him and treated him shamefully, sending him away empty-handed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then he sent a third, but they wounded him and threw him out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> ‘What shall I do?’ asked the owner of the vineyard. ‘I will send my beloved son. Perhaps they will respect him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the tenants saw the son, they discussed it among themselves and said, ‘This is the heir. Let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> So they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him.<br />What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and kill those tenants and give the vineyard to others.”<br />And when the people heard this, they said, “May such a thing never happen!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> But Jesus looked directly at them and said, “Then what is the meaning of that which is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has become the cornerstone’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 118:22">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Everyone who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, but he on whom it falls will be crushed.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paying Taxes to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> When the scribes and chief priests realized that Jesus had spoken this parable against them, they sought to arrest Him that very hour. But they were afraid of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> So they watched Him closely and sent spies who pretended to be sincere. They were hoping to catch Him in His words in order to hand Him over to the rule and authority of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> “Teacher,” they inquired, “we know that You speak and teach correctly. You show no partiality but teach the way of God in accordance with the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawful for us to pay taxes to Caesar or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But Jesus saw through their duplicity and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> “Show Me a denarius.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.">c</a></sup>Whose image and inscription are on it?”<br />“Caesar’s,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> So Jesus told them, “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> And they were unable to trap Him in His words before the people. And amazed at His answer, they fell silent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sadducees and the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> Then some of the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to question Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> “Teacher,” they said, “Moses wrote for us that if a man’s brother dies and leaves a wife but no children, the man is to marry his brother’s widow and raise up offspring for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Deuteronomy 25:5">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> Now there were seven brothers. The first one married a wife but died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then the second<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="BYZ and TR include married the widow, and he also died,">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the third married the widow, and in the same way all seven died, leaving no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> And last of all, the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> So then, in the resurrection, whose wife will she be? For all seven were married to her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> Jesus answered, “The sons of this age marry and are given in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> But those who are considered worthy to share in the age to come and in the resurrection from the dead will neither marry nor be given in marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> In fact, they can no longer die, because they are like the angels. And since they are sons of the resurrection, they are sons of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> Even Moses demonstrates that the dead are raised, in the passage about the burning bush. For he calls the Lord ‘the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Exodus 3:6">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> He is not the God of the dead, but of the living, for to Him all are alive.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> Some of the scribes answered, “Teacher, You have spoken well!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> And they did not dare to question Him any further.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Whose Son Is the Christ?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> Then Jesus declared, “How can it be said that the Christ is the Son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> For David himself says in the book of Psalms:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Sit at My right hand</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I make Your enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a footstool for Your feet.”’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Psalms 110:1">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> Thus David calls Him ‘Lord.’ So how can He be David’s son?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware of the Scribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> In the hearing of all the people, Jesus said to His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> “Beware of the scribes. They like to walk around in long robes, and they love the greetings in the marketplaces, the chief seats in the synagogues, and the places of honor at banquets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> They defraud widows of their houses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally They devour widows’ houses">h</a></sup>and for a show make lengthy prayers. These men will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
</div>
