## The First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> On one occasion, while Jesus was standing by the Lake of Gennesaret<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">a</a></sup>with the crowd pressing in on Him to hear the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> He saw two boats at the edge of the lake. The fishermen had left them and were washing their nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> Jesus got into the boat belonging to Simon and asked him to put out a little from shore. And sitting down, He taught the people from the boat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> When Jesus had finished speaking, He said to Simon, “Put out into deep water and let down your nets for a catch.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> “Master,” Simon replied, “we have worked hard all night without catching anything. But because You say so, I will let down the nets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> When they had done so, they caught such a large number of fish that their nets began to tear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> So they signaled to their partners in the other boat to come and help them, and they came and filled both boats so full that they began to sink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> When Simon Peter saw this, he fell at Jesus’ knees. “Go away from me, Lord,” he said, “for I am a sinful man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Psalms 8:8; Mark 9:6"> For he and his companions were astonished at the catch of fish they had taken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> and so were his partners James and John, the sons of Zebedee.<br />“Do not be afraid,” Jesus said to Simon. “From now on you will catch men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> And when they had brought their boats ashore, they left everything and followed Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Leper’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> While Jesus was in one of the towns, a man came along who was covered with leprosy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Leprosy was a term used for various skin diseases. See Leviticus 13.">b</a></sup>When he saw Jesus, he fell facedown and begged Him, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> Jesus reached out His hand and touched the man. “I am willing,” He said. “Be clean!” And immediately the leprosy left him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> “Do not tell anyone,” Jesus instructed him. “But go, show yourself to the priest and present the offering Moses prescribed for your cleansing, as a testimony to them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Leviticus 14:1–32.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> But the news about Jesus spread all the more, and great crowds came to hear Him and to be healed of their sicknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> Yet He frequently withdrew to the wilderness to pray.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals a Paralytic

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> One day Jesus was teaching, and the Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting there. People had come from Jerusalem and from every village of Galilee and Judea, and the power of the Lord was present for Him to heal the sick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> Just then some men came carrying a paralyzed man on a mat. They tried to bring him inside to set him before Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> but they could not find a way through the crowd. So they went up on the roof and lowered him on his mat through the tiles into the middle of the crowd, right in front of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> When Jesus saw their faith, He said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> But the scribes and Pharisees began thinking to themselves, “Who is this man who speaks blasphemy? Who can forgive sins but God alone?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> Knowing what they were thinking, Jesus replied, “Why are you thinking these things in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier: to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Get up and walk’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on the earth to forgive sins...” He said to the paralytic, “I tell you, get up, pick up your mat, and go home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalms 107:20–22"> And immediately the man stood up before them, took what he had been lying on, and went home glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> Everyone was taken with amazement and glorified God. They were filled with awe and said, “We have seen remarkable things today.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calls Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> After this, Jesus went out and saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax booth. “Follow Me,” He told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> and Levi got up, left everything, and followed Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> Then Levi hosted a great banquet for Jesus at his house. A large crowd of tax collectors was there, along with others who were eating with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> But the Pharisees and their scribes complained to Jesus’ disciples, “Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> Jesus answered, “It is not the healthy who need a doctor, but the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners, to repentance.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Questions about Fasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> Then they said to Him, “John’s disciples and those of the Pharisees frequently fast and pray, but Yours keep on eating and drinking.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> Jesus replied, “Can you make the guests of the bridegroom fast while He is with them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> But the time will come when the bridegroom will be taken from them; then they will fast.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Patches and the Wineskins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> He also told them a parable: “No one tears a piece of cloth from a new garment and sews it on an old one. If he does, he will tear the new garment as well, and the patch from the new will not match the old.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalms 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> And no one pours new wine into old wineskins. If he does, the new wine will burst the skins, the wine will spill, and the wineskins will be ruined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> Instead, new wine is poured into new wineskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> And no one after drinking old wine wants new, for he says, ‘The old is better.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
