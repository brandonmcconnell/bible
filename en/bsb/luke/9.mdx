## The Ministry of the Twelve

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 14:12; Luke 10:19; Matthew 16:19"> Then Jesus called the Twelve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR His twelve disciples">a</a></sup>together and gave them power and authority over all demons, and power to cure diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 10:7–8; Luke 10:9; Matthew 3:2"> And He sent them out to proclaim the kingdom of God and to heal the sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Psalms 37:3; Luke 10:4–12"> “Take nothing for the journey,” He told them, “no staff, no bag, no bread, no money,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or silver coins">b</a></sup>no second tunic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:11; Mark 6:10; Luke 10:5–8"> Whatever house you enter, stay there until you leave that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:51; Acts 18:6; Mark 6:11"> If anyone does not welcome you, shake the dust off your feet when you leave that town, as a testimony against them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:30; Mark 16:20; Acts 5:15"> So they set out and went from village to village, preaching the gospel and healing people everywhere.</span>

## Herod Tries to See Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 6:14–28; Matthew 14:1–12; Luke 3:1"> When Herod the tetrarch heard about all that was happening, he was perplexed. For some were saying that John had risen from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:21; Luke 9:19; Mark 6:15"> others that Elijah had appeared, and still others that a prophet of old had arisen.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 13:31–32"> “I beheaded John,” Herod said, “but who is this man I hear such things about?” And he kept trying to see Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feeding of the Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Hebrews 13:17; Matthew 14:13–21"> Then the apostles returned and reported to Jesus all that they had done. Taking them away privately, He withdrew to a town called Bethsaida.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> But the crowds found out and followed Him. He welcomed them and spoke to them about the kingdom of God, and He healed those who needed healing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:5–15; Hosea 13:5"> As the day neared its end, the Twelve came to Jesus and said, “Dismiss the crowd so they can go to the surrounding villages and countryside for lodging and provisions. For we are in a desolate place here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; John 6:5–9; 2 Kings 4:42–43"> But Jesus told them, “You give them something to eat.”<br />“We have only five loaves of bread and two fish,” they answered, “unless we go and buy food for all these people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 8:6; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Mark 6:39–40"> (There were about five thousand men.)<br />He told His disciples, “Have them sit down in groups of about fifty each.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> They did so, and everyone was seated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; 1 Timothy 4:4–5; Matthew 15:36"> Taking the five loaves and the two fish and looking up to heaven, Jesus spoke a blessing and broke them. Then He gave them to the disciples to set before the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 4:44; Matthew 15:37–38; John 6:11–13"> They all ate and were satisfied, and the disciples picked up twelve basketfuls of broken pieces that were left over.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 9:7–8; Matthew 14:2"> They replied, “Some say John the Baptist; others say Elijah; and still others, that a prophet of old has arisen.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:15–17; John 4:29; John 7:41"> “But what about you?” Jesus asked. “Who do you say I am?”<br />Peter answered, “The Christ of God.”</span>

</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

## Take Up Your Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 10:38–39; Luke 14:26–27; Romans 8:13"> Then Jesus said to all of them, “If anyone wants to come after Me, he must deny himself and take up his cross daily and follow Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:33; John 12:25; Acts 20:23–24"> For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake will save it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 8:36; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 49:6–8"> What does it profit a man to gain the whole world, yet lose or forfeit his very self?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 12:8–9"> If anyone is ashamed of Me and My words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of him when He comes in His glory and in the glory of the Father and of the holy angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; John 21:22–23"> But I tell you truly, some who are standing here will not taste death before they see the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Transfiguration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:1–13; Matthew 26:37–39; Mark 14:33–36"> About eight days after Jesus had said these things, He took with Him Peter, John, and James, and went up on a mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> And as He was praying, the appearance of His face changed, and His clothes became radiantly white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11; Luke 1:17"> Suddenly two men, Moses and Elijah, began talking with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; 1 Peter 1:11–12; 1 Peter 5:10"> They appeared in glory and spoke about His departure, which He was about to accomplish at Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> Meanwhile Peter and his companions were overcome by sleep, but when they awoke, they saw Jesus’ glory and the two men standing with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Mark 10:38; Psalms 4:6–7"> As Moses and Elijah were leaving, Peter said to Jesus, “Master, it is good for us to be here. Let us put up three shelters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or three tabernacles">c</a></sup>—one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.” (He did not know what he was saying.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–38; Matthew 17:5–7; Judges 13:22"> While Peter was speaking, a cloud appeared and enveloped them, and they were afraid as they entered the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 3:22; Acts 3:22–23; Matthew 3:17"> And a voice came from the cloud, saying, “This is My Son, whom I have chosen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="BYZ and TR This is My beloved Son; see Matthew 17:5.">d</a></sup>Listen to Him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 17:9; Mark 9:9–10; Mark 9:6"> After the voice had spoken, only Jesus was present with them. The disciples kept this to themselves, and in those days they did not tell anyone what they had seen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Boy with an Evil Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 17:14–20; Mark 9:14–29"> The next day, when they came down from the mountain, Jesus was met by a large crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; John 4:47; Luke 7:12"> Suddenly a man in the crowd cried out, “Teacher, I beg You to look at my son, for he is my only child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Luke 8:29; Luke 4:35"> A spirit keeps seizing him, and he screams abruptly. It throws him into convulsions so that he foams at the mouth. It keeps mauling him and rarely departs from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Acts 19:13–16; Luke 10:17–19"> I begged Your disciples to drive it out, but they were unable.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:5; Romans 2:4; John 20:27"> “O unbelieving and perverse generation!” Jesus replied. “How long must I remain with you and put up with you? Bring your son here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 9:26–27; Luke 7:15; Revelation 12:12"> Even while the boy was approaching, the demon slammed him to the ground in a convulsion. But Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, healed the boy, and gave him back to his father.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:43–45; Luke 5:26; Matthew 17:22"> And they were all astonished at the greatness of God.<br />While everyone was marveling at all that Jesus was doing, He said to His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Acts 2:23; Luke 2:19"> “Let these words sink into your ears: The Son of Man is about to be delivered into the hands of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 2:50; John 12:16"> But they did not understand this statement. It was veiled from them so that they could not comprehend it, and they were afraid to ask Him about it.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Mark 9:33–37; Matthew 20:20–22"> Then an argument started among the disciples as to which of them would be the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; John 16:30; Luke 5:22"> But Jesus, knowing the thoughts of their hearts, had a little child stand beside Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:37"> And He said to them, “Whoever welcomes this little child in My name welcomes Me, and whoever welcomes Me welcomes the One who sent Me. For whoever is the least among all of you, he is the greatest.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Numbers 11:27–29; Mark 10:13–14"> “Master,” said John, “we saw someone driving out demons in Your name, and we tried to stop him, because he does not accompany us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; 1 Corinthians 12:3"> “Do not stop him,” Jesus replied, “for whoever is not against you is for you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Samaritans Reject Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5–9; Mark 16:19; Luke 13:22"> As the day of His ascension approached, Jesus resolutely set out for Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 10:5; Luke 17:16; Luke 10:1"> He sent messengers on ahead, who went into a village of the Samaritans to make arrangements for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:9; John 4:40–42; Luke 9:48"> But the people there refused to welcome Him, because He was heading for Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Kings 1:10–14; James 3:14–18; James 1:19–20"> When the disciples James and John saw this, they asked, “Lord, do You want us to call down fire from heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="BYZ and TR from heaven, just as Elijah did; see 2 Kings 1:10–12.">e</a></sup>to consume them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Peter 3:9; Jeremiah 17:9"> But Jesus turned and rebuked them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR include and He said, “You do not know what kind of spirit you are of. 56 For the Son of Man did not come to destroy the lives of men, but to save them.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 12:21; John 3:17"> And He and His disciples went on to another village.</span>

## The Cost of Discipleship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 8:19–22; John 13:37; Luke 9:57–60"> As they were walking along the road, someone said to Jesus, “I will follow You wherever You go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Luke 14:26–33; James 2:5"> Jesus replied, “Foxes have dens and birds of the air have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay His head.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Matthew 6:33; 1 Kings 19:20"> Then He said to another man, “Follow Me.”<br />The man replied, “Lord, first let me go and bury my father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:6; John 21:15–17"> But Jesus told him, “Let the dead bury their own dead. You, however, go and proclaim the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Luke 14:18–20; 1 Kings 19:20"> Still another said, “I will follow You, Lord; but first let me bid farewell to my family.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Philippians 3:13; Hebrews 10:38; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> Then Jesus declared, “No one who puts his hand to the plow and then looks back is fit for the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
