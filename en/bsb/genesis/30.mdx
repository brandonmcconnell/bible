## Dan and Naphtali

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> When Rachel saw that she was not bearing any children for Jacob, she envied her sister. “Give me children, or I will die!” she said to Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> Jacob became angry with Rachel and said, “Am I in the place of God, who has withheld children from you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> Then she said, “Here is my maidservant Bilhah. Sleep with her, that she may bear children for me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally bear children on my knees">a</a></sup>so that through her I too can build a family.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> So Rachel gave Jacob her servant Bilhah as a wife, and he slept with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and Bilhah conceived and bore him a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> Then Rachel said, “God has vindicated me; He has heard my plea and given me a son.” So she named him Dan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan means He has judged or He has vindicated.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Rachel’s servant Bilhah conceived again and bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> Then Rachel said, “In my great struggles, I have wrestled with my sister and won.” So she named him Naphtali.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Naphtali sounds like the Hebrew for wrestling.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Gad and Asher

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> When Leah saw that she had stopped having children, she gave her servant Zilpah to Jacob as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Leah’s servant Zilpah bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> Then Leah said, “How fortunate!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alternate MT reading (see also LXX); the other alternate reads “A troop is coming!”">d</a></sup>So she named him Gad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gad sounds like the Hebrew for good fortune, or alternately for band of raiders.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> When Leah’s servant Zilpah bore Jacob a second son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song 6:9"> Leah said, “How happy I am! For the women call me happy.” So she named him Asher.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Asher means happy.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> Now during the wheat harvest, Reuben went out and found some mandrakes in the field. When he brought them to his mother, Rachel begged Leah, “Please give me some of your son’s mandrakes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> But Leah replied, “Is it not enough that you have taken away my husband? Now you want to take my son’s mandrakes as well?”<br />“Very well,” said Rachel, “he may sleep with you tonight in exchange for your son’s mandrakes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When Jacob came in from the field that evening, Leah went out to meet him and said, “You must come with me, for I have hired you with my son’s mandrakes.” So he slept with her that night.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And God listened to Leah, and she conceived and bore a fifth son to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> Then Leah said, “God has rewarded me for giving my maidservant to my husband.” So she named him Issachar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Issachar sounds like the Hebrew for wages or reward.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Again Leah conceived and bore a sixth son to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> “God has given me a good gift,” she said. “This time my husband will honor me, because I have borne him six sons.” And she named him Zebulun.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zebulun sounds like the Hebrew for honor.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> After that, Leah gave birth to a daughter and named her Dinah.</span>

## Jacob Prospers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> Now after Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, “Send me on my way so I can return to my homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Give me my wives and children for whom I have served you, that I may go on my way. You know how hard I have worked for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> But Laban replied, “If I have found favor in your eyes, please stay. I have learned by divination that the LORD has blessed me because of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> And he added, “Name your wages, and I will pay them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> Then Jacob answered, “You know how I have served you and how your livestock have thrived under my care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> Indeed, you had very little before my arrival, but now your wealth has increased many times over. The LORD has blessed you wherever I set foot. But now, when may I also provide for my own household?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> “What can I give you?” Laban asked.<br />“You do not need to give me anything,” Jacob replied. “If you do this one thing for me, I will keep on shepherding and keeping your flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> Let me go through all your flocks today and remove from them every speckled or spotted sheep, every dark-colored lamb, and every spotted or speckled goat. These will be my wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> So my honesty will testify for me when you come to check on my wages in the future. If I have any goats that are not speckled or spotted, or any lambs that are not dark-colored, they will be considered stolen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> “Agreed,” said Laban. “Let it be as you have said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> That very day Laban removed all the streaked or spotted male goats and every speckled or spotted female goat—every one that had any white on it—and every dark-colored lamb, and he placed them under the care of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then he put a three-day journey between himself and Jacob, while Jacob was shepherding the rest of Laban’s flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> Jacob, however, took fresh branches of poplar, almond, and plane trees, and peeled the bark, exposing the white inner wood of the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then he set the peeled branches in the watering troughs in front of the flocks coming in to drink. So when the flocks were in heat and came to drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> they mated in front of the branches. And they bore young that were streaked or speckled or spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jacob set apart the young, but made the rest face the streaked dark-colored sheep in Laban’s flocks. Then he set his own stock apart and did not put them with Laban’s animals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Whenever the stronger females of the flock were in heat, Jacob would place the branches in the troughs, in full view of the animals, so that they would breed in front of the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But if the animals were weak, he did not set out the branches. So the weaker animals went to Laban and the stronger ones to Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> Thus Jacob became exceedingly prosperous. He owned large flocks, maidservants and menservants, and camels and donkeys.</span>
</div>
</div>
