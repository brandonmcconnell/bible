## Jacob Meets Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 32:6–7; Genesis 27:41–42; Genesis 32:16"> Now Jacob looked up and saw Esau coming toward him with four hundred men. So he divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two maidservants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 37:3"> He put the maidservants and their children in front, Leah and her children next, and Rachel and Joseph at the rear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> But Jacob himself went on ahead and bowed to the ground seven times as he approached his brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalms 34:4; Luke 15:20"> Esau, however, ran to him and embraced him, threw his arms around his neck, and kissed him. And they both wept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> When Esau looked up and saw the women and children, he asked, “Who are these with you?”<br />Jacob answered, “These are the children God has graciously given your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the maidservants and their children approached and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Leah and her children also approached and bowed down, and then Joseph and Rachel approached and bowed down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> “What do you mean by sending this whole company to meet me?” asked Esau.<br />“To find favor in your sight, my lord,” Jacob answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> “I already have plenty, my brother,” Esau replied. “Keep what belongs to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> But Jacob insisted, “No, please! If I have found favor in your sight, then receive this gift from my hand. For indeed, I have seen your face, and it is like seeing the face of God, since you have received me favorably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> Please accept my gift<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or blessing or treaty of peace">a</a></sup>that was brought to you, because God has been gracious to me and I have all I need.” So Jacob pressed him until he accepted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then Esau said, “Let us be on our way, and I will go ahead of you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> But Jacob replied, “My lord knows that the children are frail, and I must care for sheep and cattle that are nursing their young. If they are driven hard for even a day, all the animals will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Please let my lord go ahead of his servant. I will continue on slowly, at a comfortable pace for the livestock and children, until I come to my lord at Seir.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> “Let me leave some of my people with you,” Esau said.<br />But Jacob replied, “Why do that? Let me find favor in the sight of my lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So that day Esau started on his way back to Seir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> but Jacob went on to Succoth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Succoth means booths or shelters or tabernacles; twice in this verse.">b</a></sup>where he built a house for himself and shelters for his livestock; that is why the place was called Succoth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob Settles in Shechem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:1; Acts 7:16; Joshua 24:1"> After Jacob had come from Paddan-aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, northwest Mesopotamia">c</a></sup>he arrived safely at the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan, and he camped just outside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> And the plot of ground where he pitched his tent, he purchased from the sons of Hamor, Shechem’s father, for a hundred pieces of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew a hundred kesitahs; the value or weight of the kesitah is no longer known">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 21:33; Genesis 32:28"> There he set up an altar and called it El-Elohe-Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="El-Elohe-Israel means God is the God of Israel or mighty is the God of Israel.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
