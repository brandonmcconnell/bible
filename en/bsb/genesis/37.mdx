## Joseph’s Dreams

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 23:4; Genesis 36:7"> Now Jacob lived in the land where his father had resided, the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:25–26; Genesis 35:22; 1 Samuel 2:22–24"> This is the account of Jacob. When Joseph was seventeen years old, he was tending the flock with his brothers, the sons of his father’s wives Bilhah and Zilpah, and he brought their father a bad report about them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Genesis 37:32; 2 Samuel 13:18"> Now Israel loved Joseph more than his other sons, because Joseph had been born to him in his old age; so he made him a robe of many colors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Possibly a robe with long sleeves; also in verses 23 and 32">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 27:41; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 4:20"> When Joseph’s brothers saw that their father loved him more than any of them, they hated him and could not speak a kind word to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 42:9; John 17:14"> Then Joseph had a dream, and when he told it to his brothers, they hated him even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 44:18; Judges 9:7"> He said to them, “Listen to this dream I had:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 44:14; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 43:26"> We were binding sheaves of grain in the field, and suddenly my sheaf rose and stood upright, while your sheaves gathered around and bowed down to mine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Acts 7:35; Hebrews 10:29"> “Do you intend to reign over us?” his brothers asked. “Will you actually rule us?” So they hated him even more because of his dream and his statements.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:9–14; Genesis 37:7; Daniel 8:10"> Then Joseph had another dream and told it to his brothers. “Look,” he said, “I had another dream, and this time the sun and moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Philippians 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> He told his father and brothers, but his father rebuked him and said, “What is this dream that you have had? Will your mother and brothers and I actually come and bow down to the ground before you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And his brothers were jealous of him, but his father kept in mind what he had said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph Sold into Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 37:1; Genesis 33:18; Genesis 34:25–31"> Some time later, Joseph’s brothers had gone to pasture their father’s flocks near Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; 1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:16"> Israel said to him, “Are not your brothers pasturing the flocks at Shechem? Get ready; I am sending you to them.”<br />“I am ready,” Joseph replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:27; Luke 19:42; Psalms 125:5"> Then Israel told him, “Go now and see how your brothers and the flocks are faring, and bring word back to me.”<br />So he sent him off from the Valley of Hebron. And when Joseph arrived in Shechem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:27; Judges 4:22; Genesis 21:14"> a man found him wandering in the field and asked, “What are you looking for?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Song 1:7"> “I am looking for my brothers,” Joseph replied. “Can you please tell me where they are pasturing their flocks?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13"> “They have moved on from here,” the man answered. “I heard them say, ‘Let us go to Dothan.’” So Joseph set out after his brothers and found them at Dothan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Psalms 37:12; Psalms 37:32"> Now Joseph’s brothers saw him in the distance, and before he arrived, they plotted to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 37:11; Genesis 37:5"> “Here comes that dreamer!” they said to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Proverbs 27:4; Matthew 2:2–16"> “Come now, let us kill him and throw him into one of the pits. We can say that a vicious animal has devoured him. Then we shall see what becomes of his dreams!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Genesis 35:22; Joshua 10:28"> When Reuben heard this, he tried to rescue Joseph from their hands. “Let us not take his life,” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 22:12; Deuteronomy 13:9; Genesis 42:22"> “Do not shed his blood. Throw him into this pit in the wilderness, but do not lay a hand on him.” Reuben said this so that he could rescue Joseph from their hands and return him to his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Matthew 27:28; Genesis 42:21"> So when Joseph came to his brothers, they stripped him of his robe—the robe of many colors he was wearing—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Zechariah 9:11"> and they took him and threw him into the pit. Now the pit was empty, with no water in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Genesis 43:11; Jeremiah 8:22"> And as they sat down to eat a meal, they looked up and saw a caravan of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead. Their camels were carrying spices, balm, and myrrh on their way down to Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 4:10; Genesis 25:32"> Then Judah said to his brothers, “What profit will we gain if we kill our brother and cover up his blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Genesis 42:21; 1 Samuel 18:17"> Come, let us sell him to the Ishmaelites and not lay a hand on him; for he is our brother, our own flesh.” And they agreed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 45:4–5"> So when the Midianite traders passed by, his brothers pulled Joseph out of the pit and sold him for twenty shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20 shekels is approximately 8 ounces or 228 grams of silver.">b</a></sup>to the Ishmaelites, who took him to Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Genesis 37:34; Genesis 44:13"> When Reuben returned to the pit and saw that Joseph was not there, he tore his clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Jeremiah 31:15; Genesis 42:32"> returned to his brothers, and said, “The boy is gone! What am I going to do?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob Mourns Joseph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:23; Proverbs 28:13; Genesis 37:3"> Then they took Joseph’s robe, slaughtered a young goat, and dipped the robe in its blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Genesis 37:3; Genesis 44:20–23"> They sent the robe of many colors to their father and said, “We found this. Examine it to see whether it is your son’s robe or not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 44:28; John 13:7"> His father recognized it and said, “It is my son’s robe! A vicious animal has devoured him. Joseph has surely been torn to pieces!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31; Psalms 69:11"> Then Jacob tore his clothes, put sackcloth around his waist, and mourned for his son many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Job 2:11; 2 Samuel 12:17"> All his sons and daughters tried to comfort him, but he refused to be comforted. “No,” he said. “I will go down to Sheol mourning for my son.” So his father wept for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Genesis 37:28; 2 Kings 25:8"> Meanwhile, the Midianites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew the Medanites">c</a></sup>sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard.</span>
</div>
