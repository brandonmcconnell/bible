## Jacob Settles in Goshen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 46:31; Genesis 46:28"> So Joseph went and told Pharaoh: “My father and my brothers, with their flocks and herds and all they own, have come from the land of Canaan and are now in Goshen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Colossians 1:28; Jude 1:24"> And he chose five of his brothers and presented them before Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:33–34; 2 Thessalonians 3:10; Genesis 4:2"> “What is your occupation?” Pharaoh asked Joseph’s brothers.<br />“Your servants are shepherds,” they replied, “both we and our fathers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Genesis 15:13; Deuteronomy 26:5"> Then they said to Pharaoh, “We have come to live in the land for a time, because there is no pasture for the flocks of your servants, since the famine in the land of Canaan has been severe. So now, please allow your servants to settle in the land of Goshen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Now that your father and brothers have come to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 47:11"> the land of Egypt is before you; settle your father and brothers in the best part of the land. They may dwell in the land of Goshen. And if you know of any talented men among them, put them in charge of my own livestock.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; 2 Samuel 8:10; 1 Samuel 2:20"> Then Joseph brought in his father Jacob and presented him before Pharaoh, and Jacob blessed Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> “How many years have you lived?” Pharaoh asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 39:12; Psalms 39:5; Job 14:1"> “My travels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew sojourns; twice in this verse">a</a></sup>have lasted 130 years,” Jacob replied. “My years have been few and hard, and they have not matched the years of the travels of my fathers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:7; Psalms 129:8; Hebrews 7:7"> Then Jacob blessed Pharaoh and departed from his presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:37"> So Joseph settled his father and brothers in the land of Egypt and gave them property in the best part of the land, the district of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; 1 Timothy 5:8; Genesis 47:1"> Joseph also provided his father and brothers and all his father’s household with food for their families.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Famine Continues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:11; 1 Kings 18:5; Lamentations 4:9"> There was no food, however, in all that region, because the famine was so severe; the lands of Egypt and Canaan had been exhausted by the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:56; Luke 16:10–12; Luke 16:1–2"> Joseph collected all the money to be found in the land of Egypt and the land of Canaan in exchange for the grain they were buying, and he brought it into Pharaoh’s palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:8; Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:11"> When the money from the lands of Egypt and Canaan was gone, all the Egyptians came to Joseph and said, “Give us food. Why should we die before your eyes? For our funds have run out!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Daniel 6:5–7; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> “Then bring me your livestock,” said Joseph. “Since the money is gone, I will sell you food in exchange for your livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:4; Exodus 9:3; Isaiah 31:1"> So they brought their livestock to Joseph, and he gave them food in exchange for their horses, their flocks and herds, and their donkeys. Throughout that year he provided them with food in exchange for all their livestock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:26; Jeremiah 38:9"> When that year was over, they came to him the second year and said, “We cannot hide from our lord that our money is gone and all our livestock belongs to you. There is nothing left for our lord except our bodies and our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:2–3; Job 2:4; Lamentations 1:11"> Why should we perish before your eyes—we and our land as well? Purchase us and our land in exchange for food. Then we, along with our land, will be slaves to Pharaoh. Give us seed that we may live and not die, and that the land may not become desolate.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Joseph acquired for Pharaoh all the land in Egypt; the Egyptians, one and all, sold their fields because the famine was so severe upon them. The land became Pharaoh’s,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:48"> and Joseph reduced the people to servitude<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SP, LXX, Vulgate; Hebrew removed the people to the cities">b</a></sup>from one end of Egypt to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Nehemiah 13:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> However, he did not acquire the priests’ portion of the land, for it had been given to them by Pharaoh. They ate the rations that Pharaoh supplied; so they did not sell their land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Psalms 41:1; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> Then Joseph said to the people, “Now that I have acquired you and your land for Pharaoh this day, here is seed for you to sow in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 41:34; Psalms 41:1; Psalms 112:5"> At harvest time, you are to give a fifth of it to Pharaoh, and four-fifths will be yours as seed for the field and food for yourselves and your households and children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Proverbs 11:26–27; Genesis 18:3"> “You have saved our lives,” they said. “We have found favor in our lord’s eyes, and we will be Pharaoh’s servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 47:22; Ezekiel 7:24"> So Joseph established a law that a fifth of the produce belongs to Pharaoh, and it is in effect in the land of Egypt to this day. Only the priests’ land does not belong to Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Israelites Prosper in Goshen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7; Acts 7:17"> Now the Israelites settled in the land of Egypt, in the region of Goshen. They acquired property there and became fruitful and increased greatly in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 90:10; Genesis 47:8–9; Genesis 37:2"> And Jacob lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years, and the length of his life was 147 years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:14; Genesis 24:49; Genesis 24:2"> When the time drew near for Israel to die, he called his son Joseph and said to him, “If I have found favor in your eyes, put your hand under my thigh and promise to show me kindness and faithfulness. Do not bury me in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–32; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 25:9"> but when I lie down with my fathers, carry me out of Egypt and bury me with them.”<br />Joseph answered, “I will do as you have requested.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 1:47; Hebrews 11:21; Genesis 24:3"> “Swear to me,” Jacob said.<br />So Joseph swore to him, and Israel bowed in worship at the head of his bed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX Israel worshiped as he leaned on the top of his staff; see Hebrews 11:21.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
