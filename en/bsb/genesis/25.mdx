## Abraham and Keturah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Genesis 23:1–2; Genesis 28:1"> Now Abraham had taken another wife, named Keturah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> and she bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:20"> Jokshan was the father of Sheba and Dedan. And the sons of Dedan were the Asshurites, the Letushites, and the Leummites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah.<br />All these were descendants of Keturah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> Abraham left everything he owned to Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> But while he was still alive, Abraham gave gifts to the sons of his concubines and sent them away from his son Isaac to the land of the east.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> Abraham lived a total of 175 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; 1 Chronicles 29:28"> And at a ripe old age he breathed his last and died, old and contented, and was gathered to his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah near Mamre, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> This was the field that Abraham had bought from the Hittites. Abraham was buried there with his wife Sarah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> After Abraham’s death, God blessed his son Isaac, who lived near Beer-lahai-roi.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Descendants of Ishmael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> This is the account of Abraham’s son Ishmael, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s maidservant, bore to Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song 1:5"> These are the names of the sons of Ishmael in the order of their birth: Nebaioth the firstborn of Ishmael, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> Mishma, Dumah, Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:19; Job 2:11"> Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> These were the sons of Ishmael, and these were their names by their villages and encampments—twelve princes of their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> Ishmael lived a total of 137 years. Then he breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; 2 Kings 23:29"> Ishmael’s descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, which is near the border of Egypt as you go toward Asshur.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Assyria">a</a></sup>And they lived in hostility toward all their brothers.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob and Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> This is the account of Abraham’s son Isaac. Abraham became the father of Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> and Isaac was forty years old when he married Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Paddan-aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, northwest Mesopotamia">b</a></sup>and the sister of Laban the Aramean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> Later, Isaac prayed to the LORD on behalf of his wife, because she was barren. And the LORD heard his prayer, and his wife Rebekah conceived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> But the children inside her struggled with each other, and she said, “Why is this happening to me?” So Rebekah went to inquire of the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> and He declared to her:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Two nations are in your womb,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and two peoples from within you will be separated;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">one people will be stronger than the other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the older will serve the younger.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Cited in Romans 9:12">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When her time came to give birth, there were indeed twins in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> The first one came out red, covered with hair like a fur coat; so they named him Esau.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Esau sounds like a Hebrew term that means hairy.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> After this, his brother came out grasping Esau’s heel; so he was named Jacob.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jacob means he grasps the heel or he deceives.">e</a></sup>And Isaac was sixty years old when the twins were born.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> When the boys grew up, Esau became a skillful hunter, a man of the field, while Jacob was a quiet man who stayed at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> Because Isaac had a taste for wild game, he loved Esau; but Rebekah loved Jacob.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Esau Sells His Birthright

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> One day, while Jacob was cooking some stew, Esau came in from the field and was famished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> He said to Jacob, “Let me eat some of that red stew, for I am famished.” (That is why he was also called Edom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Edom means red.">f</a></sup>)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> “First sell me your birthright,” Jacob replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> “Look,” said Esau, “I am about to die, so what good is a birthright to me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> “Swear to me first,” Jacob said.<br />So Esau swore to Jacob and sold him the birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Then Jacob gave some bread and lentil stew to Esau, who ate and drank and then got up and went away. Thus Esau despised his birthright.</span>
</div>
</div>
