## Joseph Reveals His Identity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15; Acts 10:41; Genesis 43:30–31"> Then Joseph could no longer control himself before all his attendants, and he cried out, “Send everyone away from me!”<br />So none of them were with Joseph when he made himself known to his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> But he wept so loudly that the Egyptians heard him, and Pharaoh’s household soon heard of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> Joseph said to his brothers, “I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?”<br />But they were unable to answer him, because they were terrified in his presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> Then Joseph said to his brothers, “Please come near me.” And they did so.<br />“I am Joseph, your brother,” he said, “the one you sold into Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> And now, do not be distressed or angry with yourselves that you sold me into this place, because it was to save lives that God sent me before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> For the famine has covered the land these two years, and there will be five more years without plowing or harvesting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Acts 7:35; Psalms 18:50"> God sent me before you to preserve you as a remnant on the earth and to save your lives by a great deliverance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or and to keep you alive as a great band of survivors">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> Therefore it was not you who sent me here, but God, who has made me a father to Pharaoh—lord of all his household and ruler over all the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph Sends for His Father

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> Now return quickly to my father and tell him, ‘This is what your son Joseph says: God has made me lord of all Egypt. Come down to me without delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> You shall settle in the land of Goshen and be near me—you and your children and grandchildren, your flocks and herds, and everything you own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> And there I will provide for you, because there will be five more years of famine. Otherwise, you and your household and everything you own will come to destitution.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> Behold! You and my brother Benjamin can see that I, Joseph, am the one speaking with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> Tell my father about all my splendor in Egypt and everything you have seen. And bring my father down here quickly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> Then Joseph threw his arms around his brother Benjamin and wept, and Benjamin wept as they embraced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; Psalms 77:4"> Joseph kissed each of his brothers as he wept over them. And afterward his brothers talked with him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pharaoh Invites Jacob to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> When the news reached Pharaoh’s house that Joseph’s brothers had come, Pharaoh and his servants were pleased.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Tell your brothers, ‘Do as follows: Load your animals and return to the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> Then bring your father and your families and return to me. I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and you shall eat from the fat of the land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> You are also directed to tell them: ‘Take wagons from the land of Egypt for your young children and your wives, and bring your father and come back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> But pay no regard to your belongings, for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> So the sons of Israel did as they were told. Joseph gave them wagons as Pharaoh had instructed, and he also gave them provisions for their journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> He gave new garments to each of them, but to Benjamin he gave three hundred shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="300 shekels is approximately 7.5 pounds or 3.4 kilograms of silver.">b</a></sup>and five sets of clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> And he sent to his father the following: ten donkeys loaded with the best of Egypt, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain and bread and provisions for his father’s journey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; Psalms 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> Then Joseph sent his brothers on their way, and as they were leaving, he said to them, “Do not quarrel on the way!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Revival of Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So the brothers went up out of Egypt and came to their father Jacob in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> “Joseph is still alive,” they said, “and he is ruler over all the land of Egypt!”<br />But Jacob was stunned, for he did not believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> However, when they relayed all that Joseph had told them, and when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him back, the spirit of their father Jacob was revived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> “Enough!” declared Israel. “My son Joseph is still alive! I will go to see him before I die.”</span>
</div>
</div>
