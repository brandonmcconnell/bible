## The War of the Kings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 37:12"> In those days Amraphel king of Shinar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Babylonia; also in verse 9">a</a></sup>Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Deuteronomy 29:23; Genesis 10:19"> went to war against Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela (that is, Zoar).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; Numbers 34:12"> The latter five came as allies to the Valley of Siddim (that is, the Salt Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">b</a></sup>).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:25–26; Ezekiel 17:15"> For twelve years they had been subject to Chedorlaomer, but in the thirteenth year they rebelled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 15:20; Deuteronomy 1:4"> In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings allied with him went out and defeated the Rephaites in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzites in Ham, the Emites in Shaveh-kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Deuteronomy 2:12; Genesis 21:21"> and the Horites in the area of Mount Seir, as far as El-paran, which is near the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 20:1"> Then they turned back to invade En-mishpat (that is, Kadesh), and they conquered the whole territory of the Amalekites, as well as the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:22; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:20"> Then the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela (that is, Zoar) marched out and arrayed themselves for battle in the Valley of Siddim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Valley of the Dead Sea">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar—four kings against five.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abram Rescues Lot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:17; Genesis 19:30; Isaiah 24:18"> Now the Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits, and as the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, some men fell into the pits, but the survivors fled to the hill country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:21; Genesis 14:16; Deuteronomy 28:31"> The four kings seized all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food, and they went on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:5; Genesis 11:27; Revelation 3:19"> They also carried off Abram’s nephew Lot and his possessions, since Lot was living in Sodom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 14:24; Genesis 40:15"> Then an escapee came and reported this to Abram the Hebrew. Now Abram was living near the Oaks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or Terebinths or Great Trees">d</a></sup>of Mamre the Amorite, a brother of Eshcol and Aner, all of whom were bound by treaty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew berit are translated in most passages as covenant.">e</a></sup>to Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Genesis 15:3; Genesis 17:27"> And when Abram heard that his relative had been captured, he mobilized the 318 trained men born in his household, and they set out in pursuit as far as Dan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Psalms 112:5; 1 Kings 15:18"> During the night, Abram divided his forces and routed Chedorlaomer’s army, pursuing them as far as Hobah, north of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18–19; 1 Samuel 30:8; Genesis 12:2"> He retrieved all the goods, as well as his relative Lot and his possessions, together with the women and the rest of the people.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Melchizedek Blesses Abram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> After Abram returned from defeating Chedorlaomer and the kings allied with him, the king of Sodom went out to meet him in the Valley of Shaveh (that is, the King’s Valley).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> Then Melchizedek king of Salem brought out bread and wine—since he was priest of God Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew El-Elyon; also in verses 19, 20, and 22; cited in Hebrews 7:1">f</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalms 24:1"> and he blessed Abram and said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed be Abram by God Most High,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Creator of heaven and earth,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:22; Hebrews 7:4–9; Malachi 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">and blessed be God Most High,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who has delivered your enemies into your hand.”</span><br />Then Abram gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The king of Sodom said to Abram, “Give me the people, but take the goods for yourself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> But Abram replied to the king of Sodom, “I have raised my hand to the LORD God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 2 Corinthians 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> that I will not accept even a thread, or a strap of a sandal, or anything that belongs to you, lest you should say, ‘I have made Abram rich.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Proverbs 3:27; Matthew 7:12"> I will accept nothing but what my men have eaten and the share for the men who went with me—Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre. They may take their portion.”</span>
</div>
</div>
