## The Return to Egypt with Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Acts 7:11–13; Lamentations 5:10"> Now the famine was still severe in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 15:16"> So when Jacob’s sons had eaten all the grain they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, “Go back and buy us a little more food.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; 2 Samuel 3:13"> But Judah replied, “The man solemnly warned us, ‘You will not see my face again unless your brother is with you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If you will send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Genesis 44:26; Genesis 42:38"> But if you will not send him, we will not go; for the man told us, ‘You will not see my face again unless your brother is with you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “Why did you bring this trouble upon me?” Israel asked. “Why did you tell the man you had another brother?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:13; Genesis 43:27"> They replied, “The man questioned us in detail about ourselves and our family: ‘Is your father still alive? Do you have another brother?’ And we answered him accordingly. How could we possibly know that he would say, ‘Bring your brother here’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 42:2; Deuteronomy 33:6; Genesis 45:19"> And Judah said to his father Israel, “Send the boy with me, and we will go at once, so that we may live and not die—neither we, nor you, nor our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 42:37; Philemon 1:18–19; Hebrews 7:22"> I will guarantee his safety. You may hold me personally responsible. If I do not bring him back and set him before you, then may I bear the guilt before you all my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:16"> If we had not delayed, we could have come and gone twice by now.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 8:22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Then their father Israel said to them, “If it must be so, then do this: Put some of the best products of the land in your packs and carry them down as a gift for the man—a little balm and a little honey, spices and myrrh, pistachios and almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 42:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> Take double the silver with you so that you may return the silver that was put back into the mouths of your sacks. Perhaps it was a mistake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 42:38"> Take your brother as well, and return to the man at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; Genesis 17:1; Acts 21:14"> May God Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew El-Shaddai">a</a></sup>grant you mercy before the man, that he may release your other brother along with Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So the men took these gifts, along with double the amount of silver, and Benjamin as well. Then they hurried down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph’s Hospitality to His Brothers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:1; Genesis 43:19; Genesis 24:2–10"> When Joseph saw Benjamin with his brothers, he said to the steward of his house, “Take these men to my house. Slaughter an animal and prepare it, for they shall dine with me at noon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The man did as Joseph had commanded and took the brothers to Joseph’s house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 42:35; Romans 7:8; Genesis 42:21"> But the brothers were frightened that they had been taken to Joseph’s house. “We have been brought here because of the silver that was returned in our bags the first time,” they said. “They intend to overpower us and take us as slaves, along with our donkeys.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So they approached Joseph’s steward and spoke to him at the entrance to the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 42:3; Genesis 42:10; Genesis 42:27"> “Please, sir,” they said, “we really did come down here the first time to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:27–35; Romans 13:8"> But when we came to the place we lodged for the night, we opened our sacks and, behold, each of us found his silver in the mouth of his sack! It was the full amount of our silver, and we have brought it back with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> We have brought additional silver with us to buy food. We do not know who put our silver in our sacks.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:26; Genesis 42:24; John 14:27"> “It is fine,” said the steward. “Do not be afraid. Your God, the God of your father, gave you the treasure that was in your sacks. I received your silver.” Then he brought Simeon out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Genesis 24:32; Luke 7:44"> And the steward took the men into Joseph’s house, gave them water to wash their feet, and provided food for their donkeys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:11; Genesis 43:16"> Since the brothers had been told that they were going to eat a meal there, they prepared their gift for Joseph’s arrival at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Philippians 2:10–11; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 42:6"> When Joseph came home, they presented him with the gifts they had brought, and they bowed to the ground before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; 1 Samuel 25:5; 1 Samuel 17:22"> He asked if they were well, and then he asked, “How is your elderly father you told me about? Is he still alive?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:7; Genesis 43:26; 2 Samuel 14:4"> “Your servant our father is well,” they answered. “He is still alive.” And they bowed down to honor him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Numbers 6:25; Genesis 30:22–24"> When Joseph looked up and saw his brother Benjamin, his own mother’s son, he asked, “Is this your youngest brother, the one you told me about?” Then he declared, “May God be gracious to you, my son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:24; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 Kings 3:26"> Joseph hurried out because he was moved to tears for his brother, and he went to a private room to weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; 1 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 42:14"> Then he washed his face and came back out. Regaining his composure, he said, “Serve the meal.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:16"> They separately served Joseph, his brothers, and the Egyptians. They ate separately because the Egyptians would not eat with the Hebrews, since that was detestable to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> They were seated before Joseph in order by age, from the firstborn to the youngest, and the men looked at one another in astonishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 1:5"> When the portions were served to them from Joseph’s table, Benjamin’s portion was five times larger than any of the others. So they feasted and drank freely with Joseph.</span>
</div>
</div>
