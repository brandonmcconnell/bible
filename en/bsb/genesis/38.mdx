## Judah and Tamar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> About that time, Judah left his brothers and settled near a man named Hirah, an Adullamite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> There Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite man named Shua, and he took her as a wife and slept with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> So she conceived and gave birth to a son, and Judah named him Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> Again she conceived and gave birth to a son, and she named him Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> Then she gave birth to another son and named him Shelah; it was at Chezib that she gave birth to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> Now Judah acquired a wife for Er, his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> But Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the LORD; so the LORD put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Then Judah said to Onan, “Sleep with your brother’s wife. Perform your duty as her brother-in-law and raise up offspring for your brother.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> But Onan knew that the offspring would not belong to him; so whenever he would sleep with his brother’s wife, he would spill his seed on the ground so that he would not produce offspring for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> What he did was wicked in the sight of the LORD, so He put Onan to death as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> Then Judah said to his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Live as a widow in your father’s house until my son Shelah grows up.” For he thought, “He may die too, like his brothers.” So Tamar went to live in her father’s house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> After a long time Judah’s wife, the daughter of Shua, died. When Judah had finished mourning, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite went up to his sheepshearers at Timnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When Tamar was told, “Your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> she removed her widow’s garments, covered her face with a veil to disguise herself, and sat at the entrance to Enaim, which is on the way to Timnah. For she saw that although Shelah had grown up, she had not been given to him as a wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When Judah saw her, he thought she was a prostitute because she had covered her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> Not realizing that she was his daughter-in-law, he went over to her and said, “Come now, let me sleep with you.”<br />“What will you give me for sleeping with you?” she inquired.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> “I will send you a young goat from my flock,” Judah answered.<br />But she replied, “Only if you leave me something as a pledge until you send it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> “What pledge should I give you?” he asked.<br />She answered, “Your seal and your cord, and the staff in your hand.” So he gave them to her and slept with her, and she became pregnant by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> Then Tamar got up and departed. And she removed her veil and put on her widow’s garments again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Now when Judah sent his friend Hirah the Adullamite with the young goat to collect the items he had left with the woman, he could not find her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He asked the men of that place, “Where is the shrine prostitute who was beside the road at Enaim?”<br />“No shrine prostitute has been here,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Hirah returned to Judah and said, “I could not find her, and furthermore, the men of that place said, ‘No shrine prostitute has been here.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> “Let her keep the items,” Judah replied. “Otherwise we will become a laughingstock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or we will become despised">a</a></sup>After all, I did send her this young goat, but you could not find her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> About three months later, Judah was told, “Your daughter-in-law Tamar has prostituted herself, and now she is pregnant.”<br />“Bring her out!” Judah replied. “Let her be burned to death!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> As she was being brought out, Tamar sent a message to her father-in-law: “I am pregnant by the man to whom these items belong.” And she added, “Please examine them. Whose seal and cord and staff are these?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> Judah recognized the items and said, “She is more righteous than I, since I did not give her to my son Shelah.” And he did not have relations with her again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Birth of Perez and Zerah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> When the time came for Tamar to give birth, there were twins in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And as she was giving birth, one of them put out his hand; so the midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it around his wrist. “This one came out first,” she announced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> But when he pulled his hand back and his brother came out, she said, “You have broken out first!” So he was named Perez.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Perez means breaking out.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> Then his brother came out with the scarlet thread around his wrist, and he was named Zerah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zerah can mean scarlet or brightness.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
