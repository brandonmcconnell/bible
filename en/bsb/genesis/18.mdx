## The Three Visitors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 13:18"> Then the LORD appeared to Abraham by the Oaks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Terebinths or Great Trees">a</a></sup>of Mamre in the heat of the day, while he was sitting at the entrance of his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> And Abraham looked up and saw three men standing nearby. When he saw them, he ran from the entrance of his tent to meet them and bowed low to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 32:5"> “My lord,” said Abraham, “if I have found favor in your sight, please do not pass your servant by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 24:32; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 43:24"> Let a little water be brought, that you may wash your feet and rest yourselves under the tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Judges 13:15; Judges 6:18"> And I will bring a bit of bread so that you may refresh yourselves. This is why you have passed your servant’s way. After that, you may continue on your way.”<br />“Yes,” they replied, “you may do as you have said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> So Abraham hurried into the tent and said to Sarah, “Quick! Prepare three seahs of fine flour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3 seahs is approximately 19.8 dry quarts or 21.9 liters (probably about 24.5 pounds or 11.1 kilograms of flour).">b</a></sup>knead it, and bake some bread.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> Meanwhile, Abraham ran to the herd, selected a tender and choice calf, and gave it to a servant, who hurried to prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> Then Abraham brought curds and milk and the calf that had been prepared, and he set them before the men and stood by them under the tree as they ate.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sarah Laughs at the Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> “Where is your wife Sarah?” they asked.<br />“There, in the tent,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> Then the LORD said, “I will surely return to you at this time next year, and your wife Sarah will have a son!”<br />Now Sarah was behind him, listening at the entrance to the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> And Abraham and Sarah were already old and well along in years; Sarah had passed the age of childbearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> So she laughed to herself, saying, “After I am worn out and my master is old, will I now have this pleasure?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> And the LORD asked Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh and say, ‘Can I really bear a child when I am old?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> Is anything too difficult for the LORD? At the appointed time I will return to you—in about a year—and Sarah will have a son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Cited in Romans 9:9">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> But Sarah was afraid, so she denied it and said, “I did not laugh.”<br />“No,” replied the LORD, “but you did laugh.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham Intercedes for Sodom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> When the men got up to leave, they looked out over Sodom, and Abraham walked along with them to see them off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalms 25:14; John 15:15"> And the LORD said, “Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> Abraham will surely become a great and powerful nation, and through him all the nations of the earth will be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> For I have chosen him, so that he will command his children and his household after him to keep the way of the LORD by doing what is right and just, in order that the LORD may bring upon Abraham what He has promised.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> Then the LORD said, “The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great. Because their sin is so grievous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I will go down to see if their actions fully justify the outcry that has reached Me. If not, I will find out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> And the two men turned away and went toward Sodom, but Abraham remained standing before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> Abraham stepped forward and said, “Will You really sweep away the righteous with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> What if there are fifty righteous ones in the city? Will You really sweep it away and not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous ones who are there?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> Far be it from You to do such a thing—to kill the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous and the wicked are treated alike. Far be it from You! Will not the Judge of all the earth do what is right?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> So the LORD replied, “If I find fifty righteous ones within the city of Sodom, on their account I will spare the whole place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> Then Abraham answered, “Now that I have ventured to speak to the Lord—though I am but dust and ashes—</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> suppose the fifty righteous ones lack five. Will You destroy the whole city for the lack of five?”<br />He replied, “If I find forty-five there, I will not destroy it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> Once again Abraham spoke to the LORD, “Suppose forty are found there?”<br />He answered, “On account of the forty, I will not do it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> Then Abraham said, “May the Lord not be angry, but let me speak further. Suppose thirty are found there?”<br />He replied, “If I find thirty there, I will not do it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> And Abraham said, “Now that I have ventured to speak to the Lord, suppose twenty are found there?”<br />He answered, “On account of the twenty, I will not destroy it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> Finally, Abraham said, “May the Lord not be angry, but let me speak once more. Suppose ten are found there?”<br />And He answered, “On account of the ten, I will not destroy it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> When the LORD had finished speaking with Abraham, He departed, and Abraham returned home.</span>
</div>
</div>
