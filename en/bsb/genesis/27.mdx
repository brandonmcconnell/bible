## Isaac Blesses Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Genesis 48:10; Genesis 25:23–25"> When Isaac was old and his eyes were so weak that he could no longer see, he called his older son Esau and said to him, “My son.”<br />“Here I am,” Esau replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Proverbs 27:1"> “Look,” said Isaac, “I am now old, and I do not know the day of my death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–28; Genesis 10:9; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> Take your weapons—your quiver and bow—and go out into the field to hunt some game for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:20; Genesis 48:9; Genesis 49:28"> Then prepare a tasty dish that I love and bring it to me to eat, so that I may bless you before I die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now Rebekah was listening to what Isaac told his son Esau. So when Esau went into the field to hunt game and bring it back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 25:28"> Rebekah said to her son Jacob, “Behold, I overheard your father saying to your brother Esau,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; 1 Samuel 24:19; Deuteronomy 33:1"> ‘Bring me some game and prepare me a tasty dish to eat, so that I may bless you in the presence of the LORD before I die.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Genesis 27:13; Acts 5:29"> Now, my son, listen to my voice and do exactly as I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:15; 1 Samuel 16:20"> Go out to the flock and bring me two choice young goats, so that I can make them into a tasty dish for your father—the kind he loves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then take it to your father to eat, so that he may bless you before he dies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:25"> Jacob answered his mother Rebekah, “Look, my brother Esau is a hairy man, but I am smooth-skinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachi 1:14"> What if my father touches me? Then I would be revealed to him as a deceiver, and I would bring upon myself a curse rather than a blessing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Samuel 25:24; 2 Samuel 14:9"> His mother replied, “Your curse be on me, my son. Just obey my voice and go get them for me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> So Jacob went and got two goats and brought them to his mother, who made the tasty food his father loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> And Rebekah took the finest clothes in the house that belonged to her older son Esau, and she put them on her younger son Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> She also put the skins of the young goats on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then she handed her son Jacob the tasty food and bread she had made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So Jacob went to his father and said, “My father.”<br />“Here I am!” he answered. “Which one are you, my son?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> Jacob said to his father, “I am Esau, your firstborn. I have done as you told me. Please sit up and eat some of my game, so that you may bless me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> But Isaac asked his son, “How did you ever find it so quickly, my son?”<br />“Because the LORD your God brought it to me,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> Then Isaac said to Jacob, “Please come closer so I can touch you, my son. Are you really my son Esau, or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Jacob came close to his father Isaac, who touched him and said, “The voice is the voice of Jacob, but the hands are the hands of Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> Isaac did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like those of his brother Esau; so he blessed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> Again he asked, “Are you really my son Esau?”<br />And he replied, “I am.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> “Serve me,” said Isaac, “and let me eat some of my son’s game, so that I may bless you.”<br />Jacob brought it to him, and he ate; then he brought him wine, and he drank.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then his father Isaac said to him, “Please come near and kiss me, my son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> So he came near and kissed him. When Isaac smelled his clothing, he blessed him and said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Ah, the smell of my son</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is like the smell of a field</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the LORD has blessed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; Psalms 133:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">May God give to you the dew of heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the richness of the earth—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an abundance of grain and new wine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">May peoples serve you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and nations bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May you be the master of your brothers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and may the sons of your mother bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May those who curse you be cursed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who bless you be blessed.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Esau’s Lost Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> As soon as Isaac had finished blessing him and Jacob had left his father’s presence, his brother Esau returned from the hunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> He too made some tasty food, brought it to his father, and said to him, “My father, sit up and eat of your son’s game, so that you may bless me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> But his father Isaac replied, “Who are you?”<br />“I am Esau, your firstborn son,” he answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> Isaac began to tremble violently and said, “Who was it, then, who hunted the game and brought it to me? Before you came in, I ate it all and blessed him—and indeed, he will be blessed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Samuel 30:4"> When Esau heard his father’s words, he let out a loud and bitter cry and said to his father, “Bless me too, O my father!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachi 2:10"> But Isaac replied, “Your brother came deceitfully and took your blessing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> So Esau declared, “Is he not rightly named Jacob?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jacob means he grasps the heel or he deceives.">a</a></sup>For he has cheated me twice. He took my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing.” Then he asked, “Haven’t you saved a blessing for me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> But Isaac answered Esau: “Look, I have made him your master and given him all his relatives as servants; I have sustained him with grain and new wine. What is left that I can do for you, my son?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> Esau said to his father, “Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too, O my father!” Then Esau wept aloud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> His father Isaac answered him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, your dwelling place shall be</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">away from the richness of the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">away from the dew of heaven above.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You shall live by the sword</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and serve your brother.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But when you rebel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will tear his yoke from your neck.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> Esau held a grudge against Jacob because of the blessing his father had given him. And Esau said in his heart, “The days of mourning for my father are at hand; then I will kill my brother Jacob.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; 1 Samuel 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> When the words of her older son Esau were relayed to Rebekah, she sent for her younger son Jacob and told him, “Look, your brother Esau is consoling himself by plotting to kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> So now, my son, obey my voice and flee at once to my brother Laban in Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> Stay with him for a while, until your brother’s fury subsides—</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> until your brother’s rage against you wanes and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send for you and bring you back from there. Why should I lose both of you in one day?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> Then Rebekah said to Isaac, “I am weary of my life because of these Hittite women.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or daughters of Heth">b</a></sup>If Jacob takes a Hittite wife from among them, what good is my life?”</span>
</div>
