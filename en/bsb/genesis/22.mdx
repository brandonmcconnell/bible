## The Offering of Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; James 2:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Some time later God tested Abraham and said to him, “Abraham!”<br />“Here I am,” he answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:32; Hebrews 11:17"> “Take your son,” God said, “your only son Isaac, whom you love, and go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains, which I will show you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26"> So Abraham got up early the next morning, saddled his donkey, and took along two of his servants and his son Isaac. He split the wood for a burnt offering and set out for the place God had designated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Hosea 6:2"> On the third day Abraham looked up and saw the place in the distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> “Stay here with the donkey,” Abraham told his servants. “The boy and I will go over there to worship, and then we will return to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and placed it on his son Isaac. He himself carried the fire and the sacrificial knife, and the two of them walked on together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> Then Isaac said to his father Abraham, “My father!”<br />“Here I am, my son,” he replied.<br />“The fire and the wood are here,” said Isaac, “but where is the lamb for the burnt offering?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> Abraham answered, “God Himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering, my son.” And the two walked on together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> When they arrived at the place God had designated, Abraham built the altar there and arranged the wood. He bound his son Isaac and placed him on the altar, atop the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> Then Abraham reached out his hand and took the knife to slaughter his son.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Provides the Sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> Just then the angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Angel; also in verses 12 and 15">a</a></sup>of the LORD called out to him from heaven, “Abraham, Abraham!”<br />“Here I am,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; 1 Samuel 15:22"> “Do not lay a hand on the boy or do anything to him,” said the angel, “for now I know that you fear God, since you have not withheld your only son from me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or from Me">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Psalms 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> Then Abraham looked up and saw behind him a ram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Most MT manuscripts; other Hebrew manuscripts, SP, LXX, and Syriac saw one ram">c</a></sup>in a thicket, caught by its horns. So he went and took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> And Abraham called that place The LORD Will Provide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew YHWH Yireh">d</a></sup>So to this day it is said, “On the mountain of the LORD it will be provided.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the angel of the LORD called to Abraham from heaven a second time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; Psalms 105:9"> saying, “By Myself I have sworn, declares the LORD, that because you have done this and have not withheld your only son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremiah 33:22"> I will surely bless you, and I will multiply your descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 6:14">e</a></sup>like the stars in the sky and the sand on the seashore. Your descendants will possess the gates of their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> And through your offspring all nations of the earth will be blessed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Cited in Acts 3:25">f</a></sup>because you have obeyed My voice.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> Abraham went back to his servants, and they got up and set out together for Beersheba. And Abraham settled in Beersheba.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sons of Nahor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> Some time later, Abraham was told, “Milcah has also borne sons to your brother Nahor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; Psalms 60:1–12"> Uz the firstborn, his brother Buz, Kemuel (the father of Aram),</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> And Bethuel became the father of Rebekah. Milcah bore these eight sons to Abraham’s brother Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:3; Genesis 25:6; Proverbs 15:25"> Moreover, Nahor’s concubine, whose name was Reumah, bore Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.</span>
</div>
</div>
