## Abram and Sarai in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> Now there was a famine in the land. So Abram went down to Egypt to live there for a while because the famine was severe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> As he was about to enter Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, “Look, I know that you are a beautiful woman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> and when the Egyptians see you, they will say, ‘This is his wife.’ Then they will kill me but will let you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Please say you are my sister, so that I will be treated well for your sake, and on account of you my life will be spared.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> So when Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> When Pharaoh’s officials saw Sarai, they commended her to him, and she was taken into the palace of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> He treated Abram well on her account, and Abram acquired sheep and cattle, male and female donkeys, menservants and maidservants, and camels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; Psalms 105:14–15"> The LORD, however, afflicted Pharaoh and his household with severe plagues because of Abram’s wife Sarai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> So Pharaoh summoned Abram and asked, “What have you done to me? Why didn’t you tell me she was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why did you say, ‘She is my sister,’ so that I took her as my wife? Now then, here is your wife. Take her and go!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> Then Pharaoh gave his men orders concerning Abram, and they sent him away with his wife and all his possessions.</span>
