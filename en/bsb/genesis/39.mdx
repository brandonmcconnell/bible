## Joseph and Potiphar’s Wife

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> Meanwhile, Joseph had been taken down to Egypt, where an Egyptian named Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, bought him from the Ishmaelites who had taken him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 15:20"> And the LORD was with Joseph, and he became a successful man, serving in the household of his Egyptian master.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> When his master saw that the LORD was with him and made him prosper in all he did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> Joseph found favor in his sight and became his personal attendant.<br />Potiphar put him in charge of his household and entrusted him with everything he owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> From the time that he put Joseph in charge of his household and all he owned, the LORD blessed the Egyptian’s household on account of him. The LORD’s blessing was on everything he owned, both in his house and in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> So Potiphar left all that he owned in Joseph’s care; he did not concern himself with anything except the food he ate.<br />Now Joseph was well-built and handsome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:11; Ezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> and after some time his master’s wife cast her eyes upon Joseph and said, “Sleep with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> But he refused. “Look,” he said to his master’s wife, “with me here, my master does not concern himself with anything in his house, and he has entrusted everything he owns to my care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> No one in this house is greater than I am. He has withheld nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. So how could I do such a great evil and sin against God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> Although Potiphar’s wife spoke to Joseph day after day, he refused to go to bed with her or even be near her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachi 3:5"> One day, however, Joseph went into the house to attend to his work, and not a single household servant was inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> She grabbed Joseph by his cloak and said, “Sleep with me!” But leaving his cloak in her hand, he escaped and ran outside.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph Falsely Imprisoned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When she saw that he had left his cloak in her hand and had run out of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> she called her household servants. “Look,” she said, “this Hebrew has been brought to us to make sport of us. He came to me so he could sleep with me, but I screamed as loud as I could.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When he heard me scream for help, he left his cloak beside me and ran out of the house.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 9:3–5"> So Potiphar’s wife kept Joseph’s cloak beside her until his master came home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; 1 Kings 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> Then she told him the same story: “The Hebrew slave you brought us came to me to make sport of me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> but when I screamed for help, he left his cloak beside me and ran out of the house.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> When his master heard the story his wife told him, saying, “This is what your slave did to me,” he burned with anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> So Joseph’s master took him and had him thrown into the prison where the king’s prisoners were confined.<br />While Joseph was there in the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> the LORD was with him and extended kindness to him, granting him favor in the eyes of the prison warden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; 1 Samuel 2:30"> And the warden put all the prisoners under Joseph’s care, so that he was responsible for all that was done in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> The warden did not concern himself with anything under Joseph’s care, because the LORD was with Joseph and gave him success in whatever he did.</span>
</div>
</div>
