## Jacob Returns to Bethel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 31:13; Hosea 12:4; Psalms 91:15"> Then God said to Jacob, “Arise, go up to Bethel, and settle there. Build an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:14; Exodus 19:10; Ezekiel 18:31"> So Jacob told his household and all who were with him, “Get rid of the foreign gods that are among you. Purify yourselves and change your garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 32:7; Genesis 31:3"> Then let us arise and go to Bethel. I will build an altar there to God, who answered me in my day of distress. He has been with me wherever I have gone.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Deuteronomy 7:25; Exodus 32:20"> So they gave Jacob all their foreign gods and all their earrings, and Jacob buried them under the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or terebinth or great tree">a</a></sup>near Shechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; 2 Chronicles 17:10; Joshua 5:1"> As they set out, a terror from God fell over the surrounding cities, so that they did not pursue Jacob’s sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 28:22; Genesis 12:8"> So Jacob and everyone with him arrived in Luz (that is, Bethel) in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:13"> There Jacob built an altar, and he called that place El-bethel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="El-bethel means God of Bethel.">b</a></sup>because it was there that God had revealed Himself to Jacob as he fled from his brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:59; 1 Samuel 31:13; Judges 2:5"> Now Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died and was buried under the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or great tree">c</a></sup>below Bethel. So Jacob named it Allon-bacuth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Allon-bacuth means oak of weeping.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 35:1; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 26:2"> After Jacob had returned from Paddan-aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, northwest Mesopotamia; also in verse 26">e</a></sup>God appeared to him again and blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:5; Genesis 32:27–28; Genesis 17:15"> And God said to him, “Though your name is Jacob,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jacob means he grasps the heel or he deceives.">f</a></sup>you will no longer be called Jacob. Instead, your name will be Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Israel means he struggles with God.">g</a></sup>” So God named him Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 22:17"> And God told him, “I am God Almighty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew El-Shaddai">h</a></sup>Be fruitful and multiply. A nation—even a company of nations—shall come from you, and kings shall descend from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:3–4"> The land that I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give this land to your descendants after you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Genesis 18:33; Judges 6:21"> Then God went up from the place where He had spoken with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–19; 1 Samuel 7:12; Exodus 17:15"> So Jacob set up a pillar in the place where God had spoken with him—a stone marker—and he poured out a drink offering on it and anointed it with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 28:19"> Jacob called the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Bethel means house of God.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Benjamin Born, Rachel Dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; 2 Kings 5:19"> Later, they set out from Bethel, and while they were still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel began to give birth, and her labor was difficult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:24; 1 Samuel 4:19–21"> During her severe labor, the midwife said to her, “Do not be afraid, for you are having another son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Acts 7:59; Luke 23:46"> And with her last breath—for she was dying—she named him Ben-oni.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Ben-oni could mean son of my sorrow or son of my strength.">j</a></sup>But his father called him Benjamin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Benjamin means son of my right hand.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Ruth 4:11"> So Rachel died and was buried on the way to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; Genesis 35:14; 2 Samuel 18:17–18"> Jacob set up a pillar on her grave; it marks Rachel’s tomb to this day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sons of Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 4:8; Luke 2:8"> Israel again set out and pitched his tent beyond the Tower of Eder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:1; Leviticus 18:8; 2 Samuel 20:3"> While Israel was living in that region, Reuben went in and slept with his father’s concubine Bilhah, and Israel heard about it.<br />Jacob had twelve sons:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 30:18–20; Genesis 46:8–15; Genesis 29:32–35"> The sons of Leah were Reuben the firstborn of Jacob, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 35:16–18"> The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 46:23–25; Genesis 37:2; Genesis 30:4–8"> The sons of Rachel’s maidservant Bilhah were Dan and Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 30:9–13; Genesis 46:16–18; Genesis 25:20"> And the sons of Leah’s maidservant Zilpah were Gad and Asher.<br />These are the sons of Jacob, who were born to him in Paddan-aram.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 18:1"> Jacob returned to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had stayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 25:7; Genesis 50:26; Genesis 47:28"> And Isaac lived 180 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 49:33; Genesis 49:31"> Then he breathed his last and died and was gathered to his people, old and full of years. And his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.</span>
</div>
