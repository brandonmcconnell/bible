## Hagar and Ishmael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:24; Genesis 12:16; Genesis 15:2–3"> Now Abram’s wife Sarai had borne him no children, but she had an Egyptian maidservant named Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:4; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 3:17"> So Sarai said to Abram, “Look now, the LORD has prevented me from bearing children. Please go to my maidservant; perhaps I can build a family by her.”<br />And Abram listened to the voice of Sarai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> So after he had lived in Canaan for ten years, his wife Sarai took her Egyptian maidservant Hagar and gave her to Abram to be his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> And he slept with Hagar, and she conceived. But when Hagar realized that she was pregnant, she began to despise her mistress.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or her mistress became despised in her sight">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> Then Sarai said to Abram, “May the wrong done to me be upon you! I delivered my servant into your arms, and ever since she saw that she was pregnant, she has treated me with contempt. May the LORD judge between you and me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> “Here,” said Abram, “your servant is in your hands. Do whatever you want with her.” Then Sarai treated Hagar so harshly that she fled from her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> Now the angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Angel; also in verses 9, 10, and 11; corresponding pronouns may also be capitalized.">b</a></sup>of the LORD found Hagar by a spring of water in the desert—the spring along the road to Shur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> “Hagar, servant of Sarai,” he said, “where have you come from, and where are you going?”<br />“I am running away from my mistress Sarai,” she replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> So the angel of the LORD told her, “Return to your mistress and submit to her authority.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> Then the angel added, “I will greatly multiply your offspring so that they will be too numerous to count.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> The angel of the LORD proceeded:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, you have conceived and will bear a son.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And you shall name him Ishmael,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Ishmael means God hears.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the LORD has heard your cry of affliction.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will be a wild donkey of a man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his hand will be against everyone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and everyone’s hand against him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will live in hostility</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">toward all his brothers.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> So Hagar gave this name to the LORD who had spoken to her: “You are the God who sees me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew El-Roi">d</a></sup>” for she said, “Here I have seen the One who sees me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Therefore the well was called Beer-lahai-roi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Beer-lahai-roi means well of the Living One who sees me.">e</a></sup>It is located between Kadesh and Bered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> And Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram gave the name Ishmael to the son she had borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to him.</span>
</div>
</div>
