## Carry One Another’s Burdens

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:15"> Brothers, if someone is caught in a trespass, you who are spiritual should restore him with a spirit of gentleness. But watch yourself, or you also may be tempted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; John 13:34"> Carry one another’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> If anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 26:2; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> Each one should test his own work. Then he will have reason to boast in himself alone, and not in someone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Romans 14:10–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10–11"> For each one should carry his own load.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Romans 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> Nevertheless, the one who receives instruction in the word must share in all good things with his instructor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Do not be deceived: God is not to be mocked. Whatever a man sows, he will reap in return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:13; James 3:18; 2 Peter 2:19"> The one who sows to please his flesh, from the flesh will reap destruction; but the one who sows to please the Spirit, from the Spirit will reap eternal life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> Let us not grow weary in well-doing, for in due time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Therefore, as we have opportunity, let us do good to everyone, and especially to the family of faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Warnings and Blessings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See what large letters I am using to write to you with my own hand!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> Those who want to make a good impression outwardly are trying to compel you to be circumcised. They only do this to avoid persecution for the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For the circumcised do not even keep the law themselves, yet they want you to be circumcised that they may boast in your flesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But as for me, may I never boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or through whom">a</a></sup>the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything. What counts is a new creation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> Peace and mercy to all who walk by this rule, even to the Israel of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> From now on let no one cause me trouble, for I bear on my body the marks of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Timothy 4:22; Philemon 1:25"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers.<br />Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
