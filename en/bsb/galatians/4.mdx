## Sons and Heirs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Galatians 4:29; Genesis 24:2–3"> What I am saying is that as long as the heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, although he is the owner of everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He is subject to guardians and trustees until the date set by his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> So also, when we were children, we were enslaved under the basic principles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or elemental forces; similarly in verse 9">a</a></sup>of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> But when the time had fully come, God sent His Son, born of a woman, born under the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> to redeem those under the law, that we might receive our adoption as sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> And because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying out, “Abba, Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> So you are no longer a slave, but a son; and since you are a son, you are also an heir through God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Concern for the Galatians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> Formerly, when you did not know God, you were slaves to those who by nature are not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> But now that you know God, or rather are known by God, how is it that you are turning back to those weak and worthless principles? Do you wish to be enslaved by them all over again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You are observing special days and months and seasons and years!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I fear for you, that my efforts for you may have been in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I beg you, brothers, become like me, for I became like you. You have done me no wrong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> You know that it was because of an illness that I first preached the gospel to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> And although my illness was a trial to you, you did not despise or reject me. Instead, you welcomed me as if I were an angel of God, as if I were Christ Jesus Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> What then has become of your blessing? For I can testify that, if it were possible, you would have torn out your eyes and given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> Have I now become your enemy by telling you the truth?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> Those people are zealous for you, but not in a good way. Instead, they want to isolate you from us, so that you may be zealous for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> Nevertheless, it is good to be zealous if it serves a noble purpose—at any time, and not only when I am with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My children, for whom I am again in the pains of childbirth until Christ is formed in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> how I wish I could be with you now and change my tone, because I am perplexed about you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hagar and Sarah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, you who want to be under the law, do you not understand what the law says?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the slave woman and the other by the free woman.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Genesis 16:15 and Genesis 21:2–3.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> His son by the slave woman was born according to the flesh, but his son by the free woman was born through the promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> These things serve as illustrations, for the women represent two covenants. One covenant is from Mount Sinai and bears children into slavery: This is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> Now Hagar stands for Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the present-day Jerusalem, because she is in slavery with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, O barren woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who bears no children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">break forth and cry aloud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you who have never travailed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because more are the children of the desolate woman</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than of her who has a husband.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaiah 54:1">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> Now you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="WH, BYZ, and TR we">d</a></sup>brothers, like Isaac, are children of promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> At that time, however, the son born by the flesh persecuted the son born by the Spirit. It is the same now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> But what does the Scripture say? “Expel the slave woman and her son, for the slave woman’s son will never share in the inheritance with the free woman’s son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Genesis 21:10">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> Therefore, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman, but of the free woman.</span>
</div>
</div>
