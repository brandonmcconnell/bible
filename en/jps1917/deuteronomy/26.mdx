<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14; Deuteronomy 13:9; Deuteronomy 6:1–10"> And it shall be, when thou art come in unto the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee for an inheritance, and dost possess it, and dwell therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 23:16; Ezekiel 44:30"> that thou shalt take of the first of all the fruit of the ground, which thou shalt bring in from thy land that the LORD thy God giveth thee; and thou shalt put it in a basket and shalt go unto the place which the LORD thy God shall choose to cause His name to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:9–10; Deuteronomy 19:17; Genesis 26:3"> And thou shalt come unto the priest that shall be in those days, and say unto him: 'I profess this day unto the LORD thy God, that I am come unto the land which the LORD swore unto our fathers to give us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Matthew 5:23–24; Hebrews 13:10–12"> And the priest shall take the basket out of thy hand, and set it down before the altar of the LORD thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 31:20"> And thou shalt speak and say before the LORD thy God: 'A wandering Aramean was my father, and he went down into Egypt, and sojourned there, few in number; and he became there a nation, great, mighty, and populous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 5:23"> And the Egyptians dealt ill with us, and afflicted us, and laid upon us hard bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–4; Ephesians 3:20–21; Exodus 3:9"> And we cried unto the LORD, the God of our fathers, and the LORD heard our voice, and saw our affliction, and our toil, and our oppression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Deuteronomy 5:15; Psalms 78:12–13"> And the LORD brought us forth out of Egypt with a mighty hand, and with an outstretched arm, and with great terribleness, and with signs, and with wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Psalms 105:44; Psalms 107:7–8"> And He hath brought us into this place, and hath given us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Deuteronomy 18:4; Revelation 22:9"> And now, behold, I have brought the first of the fruit of the land, which Thou, O LORD, hast given me.' And thou shalt set it down before the LORD thy God, and worship before the LORD thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7; Philippians 4:4"> And thou shalt rejoice in all the good which the LORD thy God hath given unto thee, and unto thy house, thou, and the Levite, and the stranger that is in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; Numbers 18:24; Philippians 4:18–19"> When thou hast made an end of tithing all the tithe of thine increase in the third year, which is the year of tithing, and hast given it unto the Levite, to the stranger, to the fatherless, and to the widow, that they may eat within thy gates, and be satisfied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:176; Psalms 119:153; Psalms 119:141"> then thou shalt say before the LORD thy God: 'I have put away the hallowed things out of my house, and also have given them unto the Levite, and unto the stranger, to the fatherless, and to the widow, according to all Thy commandment which Thou hast commanded me; I have not transgressed any of Thy commandments, neither have I forgotten them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20; Leviticus 21:11; Hosea 9:4"> I have not eaten thereof in my mourning, neither have I put away thereof, being unclean, nor given thereof for the dead; I have hearkened to the voice of the LORD my God, I have done according to all that Thou hast commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Zechariah 2:13; Jeremiah 31:23"> Look forth from Thy holy habitation, from heaven, and bless Thy people Israel, and the land which Thou hast given us, as Thou didst swear unto our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:1; John 14:15; 1 John 5:2–3"> This day the LORD thy God commandeth thee to do these statutes and ordinances; thou shalt therefore observe and do them with all thy heart, and with all thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Isaiah 12:2; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> Thou hast avouched the LORD this day to be thy God, and that thou wouldest walk in His ways, and keep His statutes, and His commandments, and His ordinances, and hearken unto His voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:9"> And the LORD hath avouched thee this day to be His own treasure, as He hath promised thee, and that thou shouldest keep all His commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 28:1"> and to make thee high above all nations that He hath made, in praise, and in name, and in glory; and that thou mayest be a holy people unto the LORD thy God, as He hath spoken.</span>
