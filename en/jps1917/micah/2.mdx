<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Isaiah 32:7; Psalms 7:14–16"> Woe to them that devise iniquity and work evil upon their beds! When the morning is light, they execute it, because it is in the power of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:8; Jeremiah 22:17; Amos 8:4"> And they covet fields, and seize them; and houses, and take them away; thus they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:3; Amos 5:13; Isaiah 2:11–12"> Therefore thus saith the LORD: Behold, against this family do I devise an evil, from which ye shall not remove your necks, neither shall ye walk upright; for it shall be an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:6; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 9:17–21"> In that day shall they take up a parable against you, and lament with a doleful lamentation, and say: 'We are utterly ruined; he changeth the portion of my people; how doth he remove it from me! Instead of restoring our fields, he divideth them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Joshua 18:4; Joshua 18:10"> Therefore thou shalt have none that shall cast the line by lot in the congregation of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:12; Isaiah 30:10; Acts 5:40"> 'Preach ye not', they preach; 'They shall not preach of these things, that they shall not take shame.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 15:2; Jeremiah 15:16"> Do I change, O house of Jacob? Is the spirit of the LORD straitened? Are these His doings? Do not My words do good to him that walketh uprightly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 120:6–7; 2 Samuel 20:19; Isaiah 9:21"> But of late My people is risen up as an enemy; with the garment ye strip also the mantle from them that pass by securely, so that they are as men returning from war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Ezekiel 39:21; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> The women of My people ye cast out from their pleasant houses; from their young children ye take away My glory for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Psalms 106:38; 2 Kings 17:6"> Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your resting-place; because of the uncleanness thereof, it shall destroy you, even with a sore destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 30:10–11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> If a man walking in wind and falsehood do lie: 'I will preach unto thee of wine and of strong drink'; he shall even be the preacher of this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 4:6–7; Micah 7:18; Hosea 1:11"> I will surely assemble, O Jacob, all of thee; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel; I will render them all as sheep in a fold; as a flock in the midst of their pasture; they shall make great noise by reason of the multitude of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Zechariah 9:14–15; Isaiah 45:1–2"> The breaker is gone up before them; they have broken forth and passed on, by the gate, and are gone out thereat; and their king is passed on before them, and the LORD at the head of them.</span>
