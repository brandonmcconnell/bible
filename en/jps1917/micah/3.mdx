<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:10; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Psalms 82:1–5"> And I said: Hear, I pray you, ye heads of Jacob, and rulers of the house of Israel: is it not for you to know justice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Proverbs 28:4; Psalms 53:4"> Who hate the good, and love the evil; who rob their skin from off them, and their flesh from off their bones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 14:4; Ezekiel 11:6–7; Ezekiel 11:3"> Who also eat the flesh of my people, and flay their skin from off them, and break their bones; yea, they chop them in pieces, as that which is in the pot, and as flesh within the caldron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 8:18"> Then shall they cry unto the LORD, but He will not answer them; yea, He will hide His face from them at that time, according as they have wrought evil in their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 14:14–15; Ezekiel 22:25–29"> Thus saith the LORD concerning the prophets that make my people to err; that cry: 'Peace', when their teeth have any thing to bite; and whoso putteth not into their mouths, they even prepare war against him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20–22; Amos 8:9–10; Isaiah 29:10"> Therefore it shall be night unto you, that ye shall have no vision; and it shall be dark unto you, that ye shall not divine; and the sun shall go down upon the prophets, and the day shall be black over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:4; Isaiah 44:25; 1 Samuel 28:6"> And the seers shall be put to shame, and the diviners confounded; yea, they shall all cover their upper lips; for there shall be no answer of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; 1 Corinthians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> But I truly am full of power by the spirit of the LORD, and of justice, and of might, to declare unto Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Psalms 58:1–2; Proverbs 17:15"> Hear this, I pray you, ye heads of the house of Jacob, and rulers of the house of Israel, that abhor justice, and pervert all equity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17; Matthew 27:25"> That build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Isaiah 1:23; Micah 3:5"> The heads thereof judge for reward, and the priests thereof teach for hire, and the prophets thereof divine for money; yet will they lean upon the LORD, and say: 'Is not the LORD in the midst of us? No evil shall come upon us'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Psalms 79:1; Micah 4:1–2"> Therefore shall Zion for your sake be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of a forest.</span>
