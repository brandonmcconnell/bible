<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Hear diligently my speech; and let this be your consolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Suffer me, that I may speak; and after that I have spoken, mock on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> As for me, is my complaint to man? Or why should I not be impatient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Turn unto me, and be astonished, and lay your hand upon your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> Even when I remember I am affrighted, and horror hath taketh hold on my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Wherefore do the wicked live, become old, yea, wax mighty in power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their seed is established in their sight with them, and their offspring before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are safe, without fear, neither is the rod of God upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> They send forth their little ones like a flock, and their children dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They sing to the timbrel and harp, and rejoice at the sound of the pipe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> They spend their days in prosperity, and peacefully they go down to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> Yet they said unto God: 'Depart from us; for we desire not the knowledge of Thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> What is the Almighty, that we should serve Him? And what profit should we have, if we pray unto Him?' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Lo, their prosperity is not in their hand; the counsel of the wicked is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How oft is it that the lamp of the wicked is put out? that their calamity cometh upon them? that He distributeth pains in His anger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> That they are as stubble before the wind, and as chaff that the storm stealeth away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> 'God layeth up his iniquity for his children!' — let Him recompense it unto himself, that he may know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> Let his own eyes see his destruction, and let him drink of the wrath of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what pleasure hath he in his house after him? seeing the number of his months is determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Shall any teach God knowledge? seeing it is He that judgeth those that are high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One dieth in his full strength, being wholly at ease and quiet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His pails are full of milk, and the marrow of his bones is moistened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> And another dieth in bitterness of soul, and hath never tasted of good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> They lie down alike in the dust, and the worm covereth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> Behold, I know your thoughts, and the devices which ye wrongfully imagine against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For ye say: 'Where is the house of the prince? And where is the tent wherein the wicked dwelt?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Have ye not asked them that go by the way; and will ye misdeem their tokens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> That the evil man is reserved to the day of calamity, that they are led forth to the day of wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> But who shall declare his way to his face? And who shall repay him what he hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> For he is borne to the grave, and watch is kept over his tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> The clods of the valley are sweet unto him, and all men draw after him, as there were innumerable before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> How then comfort ye me in vain? And as for your answers, there remaineth only faithlessness?</span>
