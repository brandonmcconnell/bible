<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> And dost Thou open Thine eyes upon such a one, and bringest me into judgment with Thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> Seeing his days are determined, the number of his months is with Thee, and Thou hast appointed his bounds that he cannot pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> Look away from him, that he may rest, till he shall accomplish, as a hireling, his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope of a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that the tender branch thereof will not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> Though the root thereof wax old in the earth, and the stock thereof die in the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> Yet through the scent of water it will bud, and put forth boughs like a plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man dieth, and lieth low; yea, man perisheth, and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> As the waters fail from the sea, and the river is drained dry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So man lieth down and riseth not; till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be roused out of their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> Oh that Thou wouldest hide me in the nether-world, that Thou wouldest keep me secret, until Thy wrath be past, that Thou wouldest appoint me a set time, and remember me! —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If a man die, may he live again? All the days of my service would I wait, till my relief should come —</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Thou wouldest call, and I would answer Thee; Thou wouldest have a desire to the work of Thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But now Thou numberest my steps, Thou dost not even wait for my sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My transgression is sealed up in a bag, and Thou heapest up mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> And surely the mountain falling crumbleth away, and the rock is removed out of its place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> The waters wear the stones; the overflowings thereof wash away the dust of the earth; so Thou destroyest the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou prevailest for ever against him, and he passeth; Thou changest his countenance, and sendest him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not; and they are brought low, but he regardeth them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> But his flesh grieveth for him, and his soul mourneth over him.</span>
