<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> How long will ye vex my soul, and crush me with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> These ten times have ye reproached me; ye are not ashamed that ye deal harshly with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> And be it indeed that I have erred, mine error remaineth with myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> If indeed ye will magnify yourselves against me, and plead against me my reproach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Know now that God hath subverted my cause, and hath compassed me with His net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold, I cry out: 'Violence!' but I am not heard; I cry aloud, but there is no justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> He hath fenced up my way that I cannot pass, and hath set darkness in my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> He hath stripped me of my glory, and taken the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He hath broken me down on every side, and I am gone; and my hope hath He plucked up like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> He hath also kindled His wrath against me, and He counteth me unto Him as one of His adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His troops come on together, and cast up their way against me, and encamp round about my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> He hath put my brethren far from me, and mine acquaintance are wholly estranged from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My kinsfolk have failed, and my familiar friends have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> They that dwell in my house, and my maids, count me for a stranger; I am become an alien in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I call unto my servant, and he giveth me no answer, though I entreat him with my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My breath is abhorred of my wife, and I am loathsome to the children of my tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Even urchins despised me; if I arise, they speak against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> All my intimate friends abhor me; and they whom I loved are turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> My bone cleaveth to my skin and to my flesh, and I am escaped with the skin of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Have pity upon me, have pity upon me, O ye my friends; for the hand of God hath touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do ye persecute me as God, and are not satisfied with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> Oh that my words were now written! Oh that they were inscribed in a book!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> That with an iron pen and lead they were graven in the rock for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> But as for me, I know that my Redeemer liveth, and that He will witness at the last upon the dust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And when after my skin this is destroyed, then without my flesh shall I see God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Whom I, even I, shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another's. My reins are consumed within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> If ye say: 'How we will persecute him!' seeing that the root of the matter is found in me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Be ye afraid of the sword; for wrath bringeth the punishments of the sword, that ye may know there is a judgment.</span>
