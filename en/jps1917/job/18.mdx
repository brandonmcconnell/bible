<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> How long will ye lay snares for words? Consider, and afterwards we will speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Wherefore are we counted as beasts, and reputed dull in your sight?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> Thou that tearest thyself in thine anger, shall the earth be forsaken for thee? Or shall the rock be removed out of its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Yea, the light of the wicked shall be put out, and the spark of his fire shall not shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> The light shall be dark in his tent, and his lamp over him shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> The steps of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> For he is cast into a net by his own feet, and he walketh upon the toils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> A gin shall take him by the heel, and a snare shall lay hold on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> A noose is hid for him in the ground, and a trap for him in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrors shall overwhelm him on every side, and shall entrap him at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> His trouble shall be ravenous, and calamity shall be ready for his fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> It shall devour the members of his body, yea, the first-born of death shall devour his members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> That wherein he trusteth shall be plucked out of his tent; and he shall be brought to the king of terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> There shall dwell in his tent that which is none of his; brimstone shall be scattered upon his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> His roots shall dry up beneath, and above shall his branch wither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His remembrance shall perish from the earth, and he shall have no name abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> He shall be driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He shall have neither son nor son's son among his people, nor any remaining in his dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> They that come after shall be astonished at his day, as they that went before are affrighted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Surely such are the dwellings of the wicked, and this is the place of him that knoweth not God.</span>
