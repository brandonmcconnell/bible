<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; 1 Samuel 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> Knowest thou the time when the wild goats of the rock bring forth? Or canst thou mark when the hinds do calve?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24"> Canst thou number the months that they fulfil? Or knowest thou the time when they bring forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bow themselves, they bring forth their young, they cast out their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young ones wax strong, they grow up in the open field; they go forth, and return not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who hath sent out the wild ass free? Or who hath loosed the bands of the wild ass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Whose house I have made the wilderness, and the salt land his dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He scorneth the tumult of the city, neither heareth he the shoutings of the driver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> The range of the mountains is his pasture, and he searcheth after every green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Will the wild-ox be willing to serve thee? Or will he abide by thy crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Canst thou bind the wild-ox with his band in the furrow? Or will he harrow the valleys after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Wilt thou trust him, because his strength is great? Or wilt thou leave thy labour to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Wilt thou rely on him, that he will bring home thy seed, and gather the corn of thy threshing-floor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The wing of the ostrich beateth joyously; but are her pinions and feathers the kindly stork's?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For she leaveth her eggs on the earth, and warmeth them in dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And forgetteth that the foot may crush them, or that the wild beast may trample them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> She is hardened against her young ones, as if they were not hers; though her labour be in vain, she is without fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> Because God hath deprived her of wisdom, neither hath He imparted to her understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> When the time cometh, she raiseth her wings on high, and scorneth the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Hast thou given the horse his strength? Hast thou clothed his neck with fierceness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Hast thou made him to leap as a locust? The glory of his snorting is terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> He paweth in the valley, and rejoiceth in his strength; he goeth out to meet the clash of arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He mocketh at fear, and is not affrighted; neither turneth he back from the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> The quiver rattleth upon him, the glittering spear and the javelin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> He swalloweth the ground with storm and rage; neither believeth he that it is the voice of the horn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> As oft as he heareth the horn he saith: 'Ha, ha!' and he smelleth the battle afar off, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Doth the hawk soar by thy wisdom, and stretch her wings toward the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> Doth the vulture mount up at thy command, and make her nest on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> She dwelleth and abideth on the rock, upon the crag of the rock, and the stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From thence she spieth out the prey; her eyes behold it afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> Her young ones also suck up blood; and where the slain are, there is she.</span>
