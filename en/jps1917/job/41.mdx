<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> (40-27) Will he make many supplications unto thee? or will he speak soft words unto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> (40-28) Will he make a covenant with thee, that thou shouldest take him for a servant for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> (40-29) Wilt thou play with him as with a bird? Or wilt thou bind him for thy maidens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> (40-30) Will the bands of fishermen make a banquet of him? Will they part him among the merchants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> (40-31) Canst thou fill his skin with barbed irons? or his head with fish-spears?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> (40-32) Lay thy hand upon him; think upon the battle, thou wilt do so no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> (41-1) Behold, the hope of him is in vain; shall not one be cast down even at the sight of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> (41-2) None is so fierce that dare stir him up; who then is able to stand before Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> (41-3) Who hath given Me anything beforehand, that I should repay him? Whatsoever is under the whole heaven is Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> (41-4) Would I keep silence concerning his boastings, or his proud talk, or his fair array of words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> (41-5) Who can uncover the face of his garment? Who shall come within his double bridle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> (41-6) Who can open the doors of his face? Round about his teeth is terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> (41-7) His scales are his pride, shut up together as with a close seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> (41-8) One is so near to another, that no air can come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> (41-9) They are joined one to another; they stick together, that they cannot be sundered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> (41-10) His sneezings flash forth light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> (41-11) Out of his mouth go burning torches, and sparks of fire leap forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> (41-12) Out of his nostrils goeth smoke, as out of a seething pot and burning rushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> (41-13) His breath kindleth coals, and a flame goeth out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> (41-14) In his neck abideth strength, and dismay danceth before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> (41-15) The flakes of his flesh are joined together; they are firm upon him; they cannot be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> (41-16) His heart is as firm as a stone; yea, firm as the nether millstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> (41-17) When he raiseth himself up, the mighty are afraid; by reason of despair they are beside themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> (41-18) If one lay at him with the sword, it will not hold; nor the spear, the dart, nor the pointed shaft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> (41-19) He esteemeth iron as straw, and brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> (41-20) The arrow cannot make him flee; slingstones are turned with him into stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> (41-21) Clubs are accounted as stubble; he laugheth at the rattling of the javelin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> (41-22) Sharpest potsherds are under him; he spreadeth a threshing-sledge upon the mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> (41-23) He maketh the deep to boil like a pot; he maketh the sea like a seething mixture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> (41-24) He maketh a path to shine after him; one would think the deep to be hoary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> (41-25) Upon earth there is not his like, who is made to be fearless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Psalms 73:6; Job 28:8"> (41-26) He looketh at all high things; he is king over all the proud beasts.</span>
