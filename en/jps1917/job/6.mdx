<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> Oh that my vexation were but weighed, and my calamity laid in the balances altogether!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; therefore are my words broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, the poison whereof my spirit drinketh up; the terrors of God do set themselves in array against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Doth the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or loweth the ox over his fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Can that which hath no savour be eaten without salt? or is there any taste in the juice of mallows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> My soul refuseth to touch them; they are as the sickness of my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Oh that I might have my request, and that God would grant me the thing that I long for!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> Even that it would please God to crush me; that He would let loose His hand, and cut me off!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> Then should I yet have comfort; yea, I would exult in pain, though He spare not; for I have not denied the words of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What is my strength, that I should wait? and what is mine end, that I should be patient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? or is my flesh of brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Is it that I have no help in me, and that sound wisdom is driven quite from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> To him that is ready to faint kindness is due from his friend, even to him that forsaketh the fear of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brethren have dealt deceitfully as a brook, as the channel of brooks that overflow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are black by reason of the ice, and wherein the snow hideth itself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> What time they wax warm, they vanish, when it is hot, they are consumed out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The paths of their way do wind, they go up into the waste, and are lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> The caravans of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba waited for them —</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They were ashamed because they had hoped; they came thither, and were confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> For now ye are become His; ye see a terror, and are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Did I say: 'Give unto me'? or: 'Offer a present for me of your substance'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> or: 'Deliver me from the adversary's hand'? or: 'Redeem me from the hand of the oppressors'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach me, and I will hold my peace; and cause me to understand wherein I have erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How forcible are words of uprightness! But what doth your arguing argue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Do ye hold words to be an argument, but the speeches of one that is desperate to be wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Yea, ye would cast lots upon the fatherless, and dig a pit for your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Now therefore be pleased to look upon me; for surely I shall not lie to your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Return, I pray you, let there be no injustice; yea, return again, my cause is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there injustice on my tongue? Cannot my taste discern crafty devices?</span>
