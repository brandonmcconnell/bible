<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Can a man be profitable unto God? Or can he that is wise be profitable unto Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it any advantage to the Almighty, that thou art righteous? Or is it gain to Him, that thou makest thy ways blameless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Is it for thy fear of Him that He reproveth thee, that He entereth with thee into judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not thy wickedness great? And are not thine iniquities without end?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou hast taken pledges of thy brother for nought, and stripped the naked of their clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou hast not given water to the weary to drink, and thou hast withholden bread from the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> And as a mighty man, who hath the earth, and as a man of rank, who dwelleth in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares are round about thee, and sudden dread affrighted thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or darkness, that thou canst not see, and abundance of waters cover thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not God in the height of heaven? And behold the topmost of the stars, how high they are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> And thou sayest: 'What doth God know? Can He judge through the dark cloud?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Thick clouds are a covering to Him, that He seeth not; and He walketh in the circuit of heaven.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Wilt thou keep the old way which wicked men have trodden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who were snatched away before their time, whose foundation was poured out as a stream;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who said unto God: 'Depart from us'; and what could the Almighty do unto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> Yet He filled their houses with good things — but the counsel of the wicked is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous saw it, and were glad, and the innocent laugh them to scorn:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> 'Surely their substance is cut off, and their abundance the fire hath consumed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Acquaint now thyself with Him, and be at peace; thereby shall thine increase be good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receive, I pray thee, instruction from His mouth, and lay up His words in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up — if thou put away unrighteousness far from thy tents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> And lay thy treasure in the dust, and the gold of Ophir among the stones of the brooks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> And the Almighty be thy treasure, and precious silver unto thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> Then surely shalt thou have thy delight in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou shalt make thy prayer unto Him, and He will hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee, and light shall shine upon thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> When they cast thee down, thou shalt say: 'There is lifting up'; for the humble person He saveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He delivereth him that is innocent, yea, thou shalt be delivered through the cleanness of thy hands.</span>
