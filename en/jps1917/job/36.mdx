<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu also proceeded, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Suffer me a little, and I will tell thee; for there are yet words on God's behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> For truly my words are not false; one that is upright in mind is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Behold, God is mighty, yet He despiseth not any; He is mighty in strength of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He preserveth not the life of the wicked; but giveth to the poor their right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not His eyes from the righteous; but with kings upon the throne He setteth them for ever, and they are exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if they be bound in fetters, and be holden in cords of affliction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then He declareth unto them their work, and their transgressions, that they have behaved themselves proudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> He openeth also their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they hearken and serve Him, they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they hearken not, they shall perish by the sword, and they shall die without knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> But they that are godless in heart lay up anger; they cry not for help when He bindeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> Their soul perisheth in youth, and their life as that of the depraved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> He delivereth the afflicted by His affliction, and openeth their ear by tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> Yea, He hath allured thee out of distress into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and that which is set on thy table is full of fatness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> And thou art full of the judgment of the wicked; judgment and justice take hold on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> For beware of wrath, lest thou be led away by thy sufficiency; neither let the greatness of the ransom turn thee aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Will thy riches avail, that are without stint, or all the forces of thy strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Desire not the night, when peoples are cut off in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take heed, regard not iniquity; for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Behold, God doeth loftily in His power; who is a teacher like Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath enjoined Him His way? Or who hath said: 'Thou hast wrought unrighteousness'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Remember that thou magnify His work, whereof men have sung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All men have looked thereon; man beholdeth it afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Behold, God is great, beyond our knowledge; the number of His years is unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> For He draweth away the drops of water, which distil rain from His vapour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> Which the skies pour down and drop upon the multitudes of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> Yea, can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, the crashings of His pavilion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Behold, He spreadeth His light upon it; and He covereth the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For by these He judgeth the peoples; He giveth food in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> He covereth His hands with the lightning, and giveth it a charge that it strike the mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> The noise thereof telleth concerning it, the cattle also concerning the storm that cometh up.</span>
