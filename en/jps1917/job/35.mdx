<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover Elihu answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Thinkest thou this to be thy right, or sayest thou: 'I am righteousness before God',</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> That thou inquirest: 'What advantage will it be unto Thee?' And: 'What profit shall I have, more than if I had sinned?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I will give thee answer, and thy companions with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Look unto the heavens, and see; and behold the skies, which are higher than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou hast sinned, what doest thou against Him? And if thy transgressions be multiplied, what doest thou unto Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If thou be righteous, what givest thou Him? Or what receiveth He of thy hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Thy wickedness concerneth a man as thou art; and thy righteousness a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> By reason of the multitude of oppressions they cry out; they cry for help by reason of the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But none saith: 'Where is God my Maker, who giveth songs in the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and maketh us wiser than the fowls of heaven?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they cry, but none giveth answer, because of the pride of evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Surely God will not hear vanity, neither will the Almighty regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Yea, when thou sayest thou canst not see Him — the cause is before Him; therefore wait thou for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> And now, is it for nought that He punished in His anger? And hath He not full knowledge of arrogance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> But Job doth open his mouth in vanity; he multiplieth words without knowledge.</span>
