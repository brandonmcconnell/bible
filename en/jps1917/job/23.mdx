<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Even to-day is my complaint bitter; my hand is become heavy because of my groaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> Oh that I knew where I might find Him, that I might come even to His seat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would order my cause before Him, and fill my mouth with arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I would know the words which He would answer me, and understand what He would say unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would He contend with me in His great power? Nay; but He would give heed unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There the upright might reason with Him; so should I be delivered for ever from my Judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> Behold, I go forward, but He is not there, and backward, but I cannot perceive Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> On the left hand, when He doth work, but I cannot behold Him, He turneth Himself to the right hand, but I cannot see Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> For He knoweth the way that I take; when He hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My foot hath held fast to His steps, His way have I kept, and turned not aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> I have not gone back from the commandment of His lips; I have treasured up the words of His mouth more than my necessary food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But He is at one with Himself, and who can turn Him? And what His soul desireth, even that He doeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For He will perform that which is appointed for me; and many such things are with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore am I affrighted at His presence; when I consider, I am afraid of Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> Yea, God hath made my heart faint, and the Almighty hath affrighted me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither did He cover the thick darkness from my face.</span>
