<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He sendeth it forth under the whole heaven, and His lightning unto the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After it a voice roareth; He thundereth with the voice of His majesty; and He stayeth them not when His voice is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God thundereth marvellously with His voice; great things doeth He, which we cannot comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For He saith to the snow: 'Fall thou on the earth'; likewise to the shower of rain, and to the showers of His mighty rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> He sealeth up the hand of every man, that all men whom He hath made may know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> Then the beasts go into coverts, and remain in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> Out of the Chamber cometh the storm; and cold out of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> By the breath of God ice is given, and the breadth of the waters is straitened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> Yea, He ladeth the thick cloud with moister, He spreadeth abroad the cloud of His lightning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And they are turned round about by His guidance, that they may do whatsoever He commandeth them upon the face of the habitable world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> Whether it be for correction, or for His earth, or for mercy, that He cause it to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Hearken unto this, O Job; stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Dost thou know how God enjoineth them, and causeth the lightning of His cloud to shine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Dost thou know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of Him who is perfect in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> Thou whose garments are warm, when the earth is still by reason of the south wind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Canst thou with Him spread out the sky, which is strong as a molten mirror?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Teach us what we shall say unto Him; for we cannot order our speech by reason of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Shall it be told Him that I would speak? Or should a man wish that he were swallowed up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> And now men see not the light which is bright in the skies; but the wind passeth, and cleanseth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> Out of the north cometh golden splendour, about God is terrible majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> The Almighty, whom we cannot find out, is excellent in power, yet to judgment and plenteous justice He doeth no violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Men do therefore fear Him; He regardeth not any that are wise of heart.</span>
