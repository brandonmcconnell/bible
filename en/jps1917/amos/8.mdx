<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> Thus the Lord GOD showed me; and behold a basket of summer fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> And He said: 'Amos, what seest thou?' And I said: 'A basket of summer fruit.' Then said the LORD unto me: The end is come upon My people Israel; I will not again pardon them any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> And the songs of the palace shall be wailings in that day, saith the Lord GOD; the dead bodies shall be many; in every place silence shall be cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Hear this, O ye that would swallow the needy, and destroy the poor of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> Saying: 'When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell grain? and the sabbath, that we may set forth corn? making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances of deceit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> That we may buy the poor for silver, and the needy for a pair of shoes, and sell the refuse of the corn?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> The LORD hath sworn by the pride of Jacob: Surely I will never forget any of their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> Shall not the land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein? Yea, it shall rise up wholly like the River; and it shall be troubled and sink again, like the River of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord GOD, that I will cause the sun to go down at noon, and I will darken the earth in the clear day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> And I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; and I will bring up sackcloth upon all loins, and baldness upon every head; and I will make it as the mourning for an only son, and the end thereof as a bitter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord GOD, that I will send a famine in the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> And they shall wander from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east; they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> In that day shall the fair virgins and the young men faint for thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> They that swear by the sin of Samaria, and say: 'As thy God, O Dan, liveth'; and: 'As the way of Beer-sheba liveth'; even they shall fall, and never rise up again.</span>
