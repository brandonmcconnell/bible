<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> 'Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep' —</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> That winketh with his eyes, that scrapeth with his feet, that pointeth with his fingers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> Frowardness is in his heart, he deviseth evil continually; he soweth discord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly; on a sudden shall he be broken, and that without remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> There are six things which the LORD hateth, yea, seven which are an abomination unto Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> A heart that deviseth wicked thoughts, feet that are swift in running to evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> A false witness that breatheth out lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, keep the commandment of thy father, and forsake not the teaching of thy mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Bind them continually upon thy heart, tie them about thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> When thou walkest, it shall lead thee, when thou liest down, it shall watch over thee; and when thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the commandment is a lamp, and the teaching is light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> To keep thee from the evil woman, from the smoothness of the alien tongue.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> For on account of a harlot a man is brought to a loaf of bread, but the adulteress hunteth for the precious life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his clothes not be burned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Or can one walk upon hot coals, and his feet not be scorched?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife; whosoever toucheth her shall not go unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> Men do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> But if he be found, he must restore sevenfold, he must give all the substance of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> He that committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding; he doeth it that would destroy his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> Wounds and dishonour shall he get, and his reproach shall not be wiped away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> For jealousy is the rage of a man, and he will not spare in the day of vengeance.</span>
