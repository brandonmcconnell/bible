<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:10; Proverbs 9:7–8; Psalms 119:97–100"> Whoso loveth knowledge loveth correction; but he that is brutish hateth reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Acts 11:24; Psalms 112:5"> A good man shall obtain favour of the LORD; but a man of wicked devices will He condemn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:25; Ephesians 3:17; Proverbs 12:12"> A man shall not be established by wickedness; but the root of the righteous shall never be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 14:30; Proverbs 31:10–25; Proverbs 14:1"> A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband; but she that doeth shamefully is as rottenness in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; Isaiah 55:7; Psalms 140:1–3"> The thoughts of the righteous are right; but the counsels of the wicked are deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:3; Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:11–19"> The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood; but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–27; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 10:25"> The wicked are overthrown, and are not; but the house of the righteous shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15"> A man shall be commended according to his intelligence; but he that is of a distorted understanding shall be despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Proverbs 13:7"> Better is he that is lightly esteemed, and hath a servant, than he that playeth the man of rank, and lacketh bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; Genesis 33:13–14; Numbers 22:28–32"> A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast; but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:19; Proverbs 14:23; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12"> He that tilleth his ground shall have plenty of bread; but he that followeth after vain things is void of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 15:5; Luke 8:13–15"> The wicked desireth the prey of evil men; but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 11:8; Psalms 64:8"> In the transgression of the lips is a snare to the evil man; but the righteous cometh out of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:20–21; Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 13:2"> A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth, and the doings of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 3:7; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:16"> The way of a fool is straight in his own eyes; but he that is wise hearkeneth unto counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:12"> A fool's vexation is presently known; but a prudent man concealeth shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 14:5; Proverbs 14:25; Proverbs 24:28"> He that breatheth forth truth uttereth righteousness; but a false witness deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:4; Psalms 57:4"> There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword; but the tongue of the wise is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:9; Psalms 52:4–5; Job 20:5"> The lip of truth shall be established for ever; but a lying tongue is but for a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; 1 Peter 3:8–13; Proverbs 12:12"> Deceit is in the heart of them that devise evil; but to the counsellors of peace is joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:13; Psalms 91:10; Romans 8:28"> There shall no mischief befall the righteous; but the wicked are filled with evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 11:20; Revelation 22:15"> Lying lips are an abomination to the LORD; but they that deal truly are His delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 10:19"> A prudent man concealeth knowledge; but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 21:25–26"> The hand of the diligent shall bear rule; but the slothful shall be under tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22"> Care in the heart of a man boweth it down; but a good word maketh it glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 John 2:26; Matthew 5:46–48; Proverbs 17:27"> The righteous is guided by his friend; but the way of the wicked leadeth them astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 26:15; Proverbs 15:16"> The slothful man shall not hunt his prey; but the precious substance of men is to be diligent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="3 John 1:11; Romans 5:21; Ezekiel 18:20–24"> In the way of righteousness is life, and in the pathway thereof there is no death.</span>
