<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> And the LORD said unto Joshua: 'Fear not, neither be thou dismayed; take all the people of war with thee, and arise, go up to Ai; see, I have given into thy hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; 2 Chronicles 20:22"> And thou shalt do to Ai and her king as thou didst unto Jericho and her king; only the spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof, shall ye take for a prey unto yourselves; set thee an ambush for the city behind it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> So Joshua arose, and all the people of war, to go up to Ai; and Joshua chose out thirty thousand men, the mighty men of valour, and sent them forth by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> And he commanded them, saying: 'Behold, ye shall lie in ambush against the city, behind the city; go not very far from the city, but be ye all ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> And I, and all the people that are with me, will approach unto the city; and it shall come to pass, when they come out against us, as at the first, that we will flee before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> And they will come out after us, till we have drawn them away from the city; for they will say: They flee before us, as at the first; so we will flee before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; 2 Kings 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> And ye shall rise up from the ambush, and take possession of the city; for the LORD your God will deliver it into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> And it shall be, when ye have seized upon the city, that ye shall set the city on fire; according to the word of the LORD shall ye do; see, I have commanded you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> And Joshua sent them forth; and they went to the ambushment, and abode between Beth-el and Ai, on the west side of Ai; but Joshua lodged that night among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> And Joshua rose up early in the morning, and numbered the people, and went up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the people, even the men of war that were with him, went up, and drew nigh, and came before the city, and pitched on the north side of Ai — now there was a valley between him and Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And he took about five thousand men, and set them in ambush between Beth-el and Ai, on the west side of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:12; Joshua 8:8"> So the people set themselves in array, even all the host that was on the north of the city, their rear lying in wait on the west of the city; and Joshua went that night into the midst of the vale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw it, that the men of the city hastened and rose up early and went out against Israel to battle, he and all his people, at the time appointed, in front of the Arabah; but he knew not that there was an ambush against him behind the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> And Joshua and all Israel made as if they were beaten before them, and fled by the way of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> And all the people that were in Ai were called together to pursue after them; and they pursued after Joshua, and were drawn away from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> And there was not a man left in Ai or Beth-el, that went not out after Israel; and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; 1 Samuel 17:41"> And the LORD said unto Joshua: 'Stretch out the javelin that is in thy hand toward Ai; for I will give it into thy hand.' And Joshua stretched out the javelin that was in his hand toward the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the ambush arose quickly out of their place, and they ran as soon as he had stretched out his hand, and entered into the city, and took it; and they hastened and set the city on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> And when the men of Ai looked behind them, they saw, and, behold, the smoke of the city ascended up to heaven, and they had no power to flee this way or that way; and the people that fled to the wilderness turned back upon the pursuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city ascended, then they turned back, and slew the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> And the other came forth out of the city against them; so they were in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that side; and they smote them, so that they let none of them remain or escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> And the king of Ai they took alive, and brought him to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> And it came to pass, when Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, even in the wilderness wherein they pursued them, and they were all fallen by the edge of the sword, until they were consumed, that all Israel returned unto Ai, and smote it with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> And all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:11–12; Joshua 8:18"> For Joshua drew not back his hand, wherewith he stretched out the javelin, until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalms 50:10"> Only the cattle and the spoil of that city Israel took for a prey unto themselves, according unto the word of the LORD which He commanded Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Kings 19:25"> So Joshua burnt Ai, and made it a heap for ever, even a desolation, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until the eventide; and at the going down of the sun Joshua commanded, and they took his carcass down from the tree, and cast it at the entrance of the gate of the city, and raised thereon a great heap of stones, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then Joshua built an altar unto the LORD, the God of Israel, in mount Ebal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> as Moses the servant of the LORD commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, an altar of unhewn stones, upon which no man had lifted up any iron; and they offered thereon burnt-offerings unto the LORD, and sacrificed peace-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> And he wrote there upon the stones a copy of the law of Moses, which he wrote before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And all Israel, and their elders and officers, and their judges, stood on this side the ark and on that side before the priests the Levites, that bore the ark of the covenant of the LORD, as well the stranger as the home-born; half of them in front of mount Gerizim and half of them in front of mount Ebal; as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded at the first, that they should bless the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> And afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessing and the curse, according to all that is written in the book of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremiah 26:2"> There was not a word of all that Moses commanded, which Joshua read not before all the assembly of Israel, and the women, and the little ones, and the strangers that walked among them.</span>
