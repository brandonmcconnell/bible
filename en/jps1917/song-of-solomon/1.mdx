<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:1–4; Isaiah 13:20; Psalms 23:1–2"> Tell me, O thou whom my soul loveth, where thou feedest, where thou makest thy flock to rest at noon; for why should I be as one that veileth herself beside the flocks of thy companions?</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 2:10; John 15:14–15"> I have compared thee, O my love, to a steed in Pharaoh's chariots.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:3; Revelation 3:20; Mark 14:3"> While the king sat at his table, my spikenard sent forth its fragrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 19:39; Psalms 45:8; Ephesians 3:17"> My beloved is unto me as a bag of myrrh, that lieth betwixt my breasts.</span>
