But David tarried at Jerusalem. And Joab smote Rabbah, and overthrew it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> And David took the crown of Malcam from off his head, and found it to weigh a talent of gold, and there were precious stones in it; and it was set upon David's head; and he brought forth the spoil of the city, exceeding much.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> And it came to pass after this, that there arose war at Gezer with the Philistines; then Sibbecai the Hushathite slew Sippai, of the sons of the giants; and they were subdued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> And there was again war with the Philistines; and Elhanan the son of Jair slew Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite, the staff of whose spear was like a weaver's beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> And there was again war at Gath, where was a man of great stature, whose fingers and toes were four and twenty, six on each hand, and six on each foot; and he also was born unto the giant.</span>
