<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 1 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Chronicles 17:5"> And it came to pass, when David dwelt in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet: 'Lo, I dwell in a house of cedar, but the ark of the covenant of the LORD dwelleth under curtains.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zechariah 8:23"> And Nathan said unto David: 'Do all that is in thy heart; for God is with thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> And it came to pass the same night, that the word of God came to Nathan, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> 'Go and tell David My servant: Thus saith the LORD: Thou shalt not build Me a house to dwell in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; 1 Kings 8:16"> for I have not dwelt in a house since the day that I brought up Israel, unto this day; but have gone from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> In all places wherein I have walked among all Israel, spoke I a word with any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed My people, saying: Why have ye not built Me a house of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; 1 Samuel 17:15"> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto My servant David: Thus saith the LORD of hosts: I took thee from the sheepcote, from following the sheep, that thou shouldest be prince over My people Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; 1 Chronicles 17:2; Psalms 113:7–8"> and I have been with thee whithersoever thou wentest, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee; and I will make thee a name, like unto the name of the great ones that are in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Ezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> And I will appoint a place for My people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in their own place, and be disquieted no more; neither shall the children of wickedness waste them any more, as at the first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> even from the day that I commanded judges to be over My people Israel; and I will subdue all thine enemies. Moreover I tell thee that the LORD will build thee a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:21; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> And it shall come to pass, when thy days are fulfilled that thou must go to be with thy fathers, that I will set up thy seed after thee, who shall be of thy sons; and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> He shall build Me a house, and I will establish his throne for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 Chronicles 17:12"> I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to Me for a son; and I will not take My mercy away from him, as I took it from him that was before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 72:17"> but I will settle him in My house and in My kingdom for ever; and his throne shall be established for ever.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 7:17"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> Then David the king went in, and sat before the LORD; and he said: 'Who am I, O LORD God, and what is my house, that Thou hast brought me thus far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> And this was a small thing in Thine eyes, O God; but Thou hast spoken of Thy servant's house for a great while to come, and hast regarded me after the manner of a man of high degree, O LORD God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 2:30; 2 Samuel 7:20–24"> What can David say yet more unto Thee concerning the honour which is done to Thy servant? for Thou knowest Thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 Samuel 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> O LORD, for Thy servant's sake, and according to Thine own heart, hast Thou wrought all this greatness, to make known all these great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Jeremiah 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> O LORD, there is none like Thee, neither is there any God beside Thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> And who is like Thy people Israel, a nation one in the earth, whom God went to redeem unto Himself for a people, to make Thee a name by great and tremendous things, in driving out nations from before Thy people, whom Thou didst redeem out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> For Thy people Israel didst Thou make Thine own people for ever; and Thou, LORD, becamest their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Jeremiah 11:5; Luke 1:38"> And now, O LORD, let the word that Thou hast spoken concerning Thy servant, and concerning his house, be established for ever, and do as Thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremiah 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> Yea, let it be established, and let Thy name be magnified for ever, that it may be said: The LORD of hosts is the God of Israel, even a God to Israel; and the house of David Thy servant shall be established before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; Ezekiel 36:37; Psalms 10:17"> For Thou, O my God, hast revealed to Thy servant that Thou wilt build him a house; therefore hath Thy servant taken heart to pray before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> And now, O LORD, Thou alone art God, and hast promised this good thing unto Thy servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; Psalms 72:17"> and now it hath pleased Thee to bless the house of Thy servant, that it may continue for ever before Thee; for Thou, O LORD, hast blessed, and so let Thy servant be blessed for ever.'</span>
