<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 3:14"> In the five and twentieth year of our captivity, in the beginning of the year, in the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after that the city was smitten, in the selfsame day, the hand of the LORD was upon me, and He brought me thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> In the visions of God brought He me into the land of Israel, and set me down upon a very high mountain, whereon was as it were the frame of a city on the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> And He brought me thither, and, behold, there was a man, whose appearance was like the appearance of brass, with a line of flax in his hand, and a measuring reed; and he stood in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> And the man said unto me: 'Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and set thy heart upon all that I shall show thee, for to the intent that I might show them unto thee art thou brought thither; declare all that thou seest to the house of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And behold a wall on the outside of the house round about, and in the man's hand a measuring reed of six cubits long, of a cubit and a hand-breadth each; so he measured the breadth of the building, one reed, and the height, one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then came he unto the gate which looketh toward the east, and went up the steps thereof; and he measured the jamb of the gate, one reed broad, and the other jamb, one reed broad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:33; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And every cell was one reed long, and one reed broad; and the space between the cells was five cubits; and the jambs of the gate by the porch of the gate within were one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He measured also the porch of the gate toward the house, one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:19"> Then measured he the porch of the gate, eight cubits; and the posts thereof, two cubits; and the porch of the gate was inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the cells of the gate eastward were three on this side, and three on that side; they three were of one measure; and the posts had one measure on this side and on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measured the breadth of the entry of the gate, ten cubits; and the length of the gate, thirteen cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and a border before the cells, one cubit on this side, and a border, one cubit on that side; and the cells, six cubits on this side, and six cubits on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he measured the gate from the roof of the one cell to the roof of the other, a breadth of five and twenty cubits; door against door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; Ezekiel 42:1"> He made also posts of threescore cubits; even unto the posts of the court in the gates round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And from the forefront of the gate of the entrance unto the forefront of the inner porch of the gate were fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:5; 1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 41:26"> And there were narrow windows to the cells and to their posts within the gate round about, and likewise to the arches; and windows were round about inward; and upon each post were palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Ezekiel 42:1; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> Then brought he me into the outer court, and, lo, there were chambers and a pavement, made for the court round about; thirty chambers were upon the pavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the pavement was by the side of the gates, corresponding unto the length of the gates, even the lower pavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27; Ezekiel 46:1–2"> Then he measured the breadth from the forefront of the lower gate unto the forefront of the inner court without, a hundred cubits, eastward as also northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:6"> And the gate of the outer court that looked toward the north, he measured the length thereof and the breadth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29–30; Ezekiel 40:25–26; Ezekiel 40:10–16"> And the cells thereof were three on this side and three on that side; and the posts thereof and the arches thereof were after the measure of the first gate; the length thereof was fifty cubits, and the breadth five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37; Ezekiel 40:6"> And the windows thereof, and the arches thereof, and the palm-trees thereof, were after the measure of the gate that looketh toward the east; and it was ascended by seven steps; and the arches thereof were before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:27–28; Exodus 38:9–12"> And there was a gate to the inner court over against the other gate, northward as also eastward; and he measured from gate to gate a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 40:6; Ezekiel 40:20–21"> And he led me toward the south, and behold a gate toward the south; and he measured the posts thereof, and the arches thereof according to these measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:33; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:21–22"> And there were windows in it and in the arches thereof round about, like those windows; the length was fifty cubits, and the breadth five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:6"> And there were seven steps to go up to it, and the arches thereof were before them; and it had palm-trees, one on this side, and another on that side, upon the posts thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 40:19"> And there was a gate to the inner court toward the south; and he measured from gate to gate toward the south a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:32"> Then he brought me to the inner court by the south gate; and he measured the south gate according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:7"> and the cells thereof, and the posts thereof, and the arches thereof, according to these measures; and there were windows in it and in the arches thereof round about; it was fifty cubits long, and five and twenty cubits broad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> And there were arches round about, five and twenty cubits long, and five cubits broad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:26; Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:22"> And the arches thereof were toward the outer court; and palm-trees were upon the posts thereof; and the going up to it had eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:28–31"> And he brought me into the inner court toward the east; and he measured the gate according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> and the cells thereof, and the posts thereof, and the arches thereof, according to these measures; and there were windows therein and in the arches thereof round about; it was fifty cubits long, and five and twenty cubits broad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37"> And the arches thereof were toward the outer court; and palm-trees were upon the posts thereof, on this side, and on that side; and the going up to it had eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:2; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 44:4"> And he brought me to the north gate; and he measured it according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29; Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:16"> the cells thereof, the posts thereof, and the arches thereof; and there were windows therein round about; the length was fifty cubits, and the breadth five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:31"> And the posts thereof were toward the outer court; and palm-trees were upon the posts thereof, on this side, and on that side; and the going up to it had eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:6; Ezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> And a chamber with the entry thereof was by the posts at the gates; there was the burnt-offering to be washed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> And in the porch of the gate were two tables on this side, and two tables on that side, to slay thereon the burnt-offering and the sin-offering and the guilt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And on the one side without, as one goeth up to the entry of the gate toward the north, were two tables; and on the other side of the porch of the gate were two tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39–40"> Four tables were on this side, and four tables on that side, by the side of the gate; eight tables, whereupon to slay the sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> Moreover there were four tables for the burnt-offering, of hewn stone, a cubit and a half long, and a cubit and a half broad, and one cubit high, whereupon to lay the instruments wherewith the burnt-offering and the sacrifice are slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> And the slabs, a handbreadth long, were fastened within round about; and upon the tables was to be the flesh of the offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–32; 1 Chronicles 16:41–43; Ezekiel 40:27"> And without the inner gate were chambers for the guard in the inner court, which was at the side of the north gate, and their prospect was toward the south; one at the side of the east gate having the prospect toward the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:23; Psalms 134:1; Leviticus 8:35"> And he said unto me: 'This chamber, whose prospect is toward the south, is for the priests, the keepers of the charge of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:15; Ezekiel 43:19; 1 Kings 2:35"> And the chamber whose prospect is toward the north is for the priests, the keepers of the charge of the altar; these are the sons of Zadok, who from among the sons of Levi come near to the LORD to minister unto Him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27"> And he measured the court, a hundred cubits long, and a hundred cubits broad, foursquare; and the altar was before the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> Then he brought me to the porch of the house, and measured each post of the porch, five cubits on this side, and five cubits on that side; and the breadth of the gate was three cubits on this side, and three cubits on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–21; Ezekiel 40:34; 2 Chronicles 3:17"> The length of the porch was twenty cubits, and the breadth eleven cubits; and it was by steps that it was ascended; and there were pillars by the posts, one on this side, and another on that side.</span>
