<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 24:6; Ezekiel 24:9"> 'Now, thou, son of man, wilt thou judge, wilt thou judge the bloody city? then cause her to know all her abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Romans 2:5; 2 Peter 2:3"> And thou shalt say: Thus saith the Lord GOD: O city that sheddest blood in the midst of thee, that thy time may come, and that makest idols unto thyself to defile thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 44:13–14; Ezekiel 22:2"> thou art become guilty in thy blood that thou hast shed, and art defiled in thine idols which thou hast made; and thou hast caused thy days to draw near, and art come even unto thy years; therefore have I made thee a reproach unto the nations, and a mocking to all the countries!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> Those that are near, and those that are far from thee, shall mock thee, thou defiled of name and full of tumult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Isaiah 1:23; Zechariah 3:3"> Behold, the princes of Israel, every one according to his might, have been in thee to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> In thee have they made light of father and mother; in the midst of thee have they dealt by oppression with the stranger; in thee have they wronged the fatherless and the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:38–39; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 20:13"> Thou hast despised My holy things, and hast profaned My sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Hosea 4:10"> In thee have been talebearers to shed blood; and in thee they have eaten upon the mountains; in the midst of thee they have committed lewdness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Ezekiel 18:6"> In thee have they uncovered their fathers' nakedness; in thee have they humbled her that was unclean in her impurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; 2 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> And each hath committed abomination with his neighbour's wife; and each hath lewdly defiled his daughter-in-law; and each in thee hath humbled his sister, his father's daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Ezekiel 23:35; Psalms 106:21"> In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken interest and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by oppression, and hast forgotten Me, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> Behold, therefore, I have smitten My hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Ezekiel 17:24; Ezekiel 24:14"> Can thy heart endure, or can thy hands be strong, in the days that I shall deal with thee? I the LORD have spoken it, and will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Ezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And I will scatter thee among the nations, and disperse thee through the countries; and I will consume thy filthiness out of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Psalms 83:18; Ezekiel 7:24"> And thou shalt be profaned in thyself, in the sight of the nations; and thou shalt know that I am the LORD.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22; Jeremiah 6:28–30"> 'Son of man, the house of Israel is become dross unto Me; all of them are brass and tin and iron and lead, in the midst of the furnace; they are the dross of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Ezekiel 24:3–6; Micah 4:12"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD: Because ye are all become dross, therefore, behold, I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; Ezekiel 24:13"> As they gather silver and brass and iron and lead and tin into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, to melt it; so will I gather you in Mine anger and in My fury, and I will cast you in, and melt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 50:3"> Yea, I will gather you, and blow upon you with the fire of My wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 20:33; Hosea 5:10"> As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I the LORD have poured out My fury upon you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–16; Jeremiah 2:30; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> 'Son of man, say unto her: Thou art a land that is not cleansed, nor rained upon in the day of indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 2:34; Ezekiel 13:19"> There is a conspiracy of her prophets in the midst thereof, like a roaring lion ravening the prey; they have devoured souls, they take treasure and precious things, they have made her widows many in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Ezekiel 22:8"> Her priests have done violence to My law, and have profaned My holy things; they have put no difference between the holy and the common, neither have they taught difference between the unclean and the clean, and have hid their eyes from My sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; Ezekiel 22:6"> Her princes in the midst thereof are like wolves ravening the prey: to shed blood, and to destroy souls, so as to get dishonest gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremiah 23:25–32"> And her prophets have daubed for them with whited plaster, seeing falsehood, and divining lies unto them, saying: Thus saith the Lord GOD, when the LORD hath not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> The people of the land have used oppression, and exercised robbery, and have wronged the poor and needy, and have oppressed the stranger unlawfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; Jeremiah 5:1; Psalms 106:23"> And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the breach before Me for the land, that I should not destroy it; but I found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:43; Ezekiel 7:8–9; Ezekiel 9:10"> Therefore have I poured out Mine indignation upon them; I have consumed them with the fire of My wrath; their own way have I brought upon their heads, saith the Lord GOD.'</span>
