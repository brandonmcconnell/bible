<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it came to pass in the seventh year, in the fifth month, the tenth day of the month, that certain of the elders of Israel came to inquire of the LORD, and sat before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Matthew 15:8–9; Psalms 50:15–21"> 'Son of man, speak unto the elders of Israel, and say unto them: Thus saith the Lord GOD: Are ye come to inquire of Me? As I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will not be inquired of by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 23:36; Ezekiel 14:20"> Wilt thou judge them, son of man, wilt thou judge them? cause them to know the abominations of their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Ezekiel 20:15; Exodus 3:8"> and say unto them: Thus saith the Lord GOD: In the day when I chose Israel, and lifted up My hand unto the seed of the house of Jacob, and made Myself known unto them in the land of Egypt, when I lifted up My hand unto them, saying: I am the LORD your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Jeremiah 32:22; Ezekiel 20:15"> in that day I lifted up My hand unto them, to bring them forth out of the land of Egypt into a land that I had sought out for them, flowing with milk and honey, which is the beauty of all lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; Ezekiel 20:8; Leviticus 11:44"> and I said unto them: Cast ye away every man the detestable things of his eyes, and defile not yourselves with the idols of Egypt; I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:7; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 20:21"> But they rebelled against Me, and would not hearken unto Me; they did not every man cast away the detestable things of their eyes, neither did they forsake the idols of Egypt; then I said I would pour out My fury upon them, to spend My anger upon them in the midst of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Ezekiel 20:22; Exodus 32:12"> But I wrought for My name's sake, that it should not be profaned in the sight of the nations, among whom they were, in whose sight I made Myself known unto them, so as to bring them forth out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 15:22; Exodus 13:17–18; Exodus 14:17–22"> So I caused them to go forth out of the land of Egypt, and brought them into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5; Deuteronomy 4:8"> And I gave them My statutes, and taught them Mine ordinances, which if a man do, he shall live by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:20; Ezekiel 37:28; Leviticus 21:23"> Moreover also I gave them My sabbaths, to be a sign between Me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD that sanctify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:21; Ezekiel 20:8; Isaiah 56:6"> But the house of Israel rebelled against Me in the wilderness; they walked not in My statutes, and they rejected Mine ordinances, which if a man do, he shall live by them, and My sabbaths they greatly profaned; then I said I would pour out My fury upon them in the wilderness, to consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 36:22–23; Ezekiel 20:22"> But I wrought for My name's sake, that it should not be profaned in the sight of the nations, in whose sight I brought them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Psalms 106:26; Hebrews 3:18"> Yet also I lifted up My hand unto them in the wilderness, that I would not bring them into the land which I had given them, flowing with milk and honey, which is the beauty of all lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> because they rejected Mine ordinances, and walked not in My statutes, and profaned My sabbaths — for their heart went after their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Jeremiah 4:27; Jeremiah 5:18"> Nevertheless Mine eye spared them from destroying them, neither did I make a full end of them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:2–4; Psalms 78:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18"> And I said unto their children in the wilderness: Walk ye not in the statutes of your fathers, neither observe their ordinances, nor defile yourselves with their idols;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Deuteronomy 5:32–8; Ezekiel 37:24"> I am the LORD your God; walk in My statutes, and keep Mine ordinances, and do them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Jeremiah 17:22; Exodus 31:13–17"> and hallow My sabbaths, and they shall be a sign between Me and you, that ye may know that I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; Numbers 21:5; Ezekiel 20:8"> But the children rebelled against Me; they walked not in My statutes, neither kept Mine ordinances to do them, which if a man do, he shall live by them; they profaned My sabbaths; then I said I would pour out My fury upon them, to spend My anger upon them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:14; Ezekiel 20:9; Jeremiah 14:21"> Nevertheless I withdrew My hand, and wrought for My name's sake, that it should not be profaned in the sight of the nations, in whose sight I brought them forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; Deuteronomy 32:26–27"> I lifted up My hand unto them also in the wilderness, that I would scatter them among the nations, and disperse them through the countries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 6:9; Amos 2:4"> because they had not executed Mine ordinances, but had rejected My statutes, and had profaned My sabbaths, and their eyes were after their fathers' idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 20:39"> Wherefore I gave them also statutes that were not good, and ordinances whereby they should not live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 20:31"> and I polluted them in their own gifts, in that they set apart all that openeth the womb, that I might destroy them, to the end that they might know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:11"> Therefore, son of man, speak unto the house of Israel, and say unto them: Thus saith the Lord GOD: In this moreover have your fathers blasphemed Me, in that they dealt treacherously with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; Jeremiah 3:6"> For when I had brought them into the land, which I lifted up My hand to give unto them, then they saw every high hill, and every thick tree, and they offered there their sacrifices, and there they presented the provocation of their offering, there also they made their sweet savour, and there they poured out their drink-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:24–25; Ezekiel 16:31"> Then I said unto them: What meaneth the high place whereunto ye go? So the name thereof is called Bamah unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 7:26; Judges 2:19"> Wherefore say unto the house of Israel: Thus saith the Lord GOD: When ye pollute yourselves after the manner of your fathers, and go after their abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:26; Psalms 106:37–39; Jeremiah 7:31"> and when, in offering your gifts, in making your sons to pass through the fire, ye pollute yourselves with all your idols, unto this day; shall I then be inquired of by you, O house of Israel? As I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will not be inquired of by you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:5; Jeremiah 44:17; Psalms 139:2"> and that which cometh into your mind shall not be at all; in that ye say: We will be as the nations, as the families of the countries, to serve wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:5; Ezekiel 8:18; Jeremiah 44:6"> As I live, saith the Lord GOD, surely with a mighty hand, and with an outstretched arm, and with fury poured out, will I be king over you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:38; Amos 9:9–10; Ezekiel 34:16"> and I will bring you out from the peoples, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered, with a mighty hand, and with an outstretched arm, and with fury poured out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Jeremiah 2:35; Ezekiel 17:20"> and I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there will I plead with you face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–10; Numbers 11:1–35; Ezekiel 20:13"> Like as I pleaded with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so will I plead with you, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Leviticus 27:32; Matthew 25:32–33"> And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9; Amos 9:9–10; Psalms 95:11"> and I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against Me; I will bring them forth out of the land where they sojourn, but they shall not enter into the land of Israel; and ye shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hosea 4:17; Isaiah 1:13–15; Ezekiel 43:7"> As for you, O house of Israel, thus saith the Lord GOD: Go ye, serve every one his idols, even because ye will not hearken unto Me; but My holy name shall ye no more profane with your gifts, and with your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Malachi 3:4; Isaiah 60:7"> For in My holy mountain, in the mountain of the height of Israel, saith the Lord GOD, there shall all the house of Israel, all of them, serve Me in the land; there will I accept them, and there will I require your heave-offerings, and the first of your gifts, with all your holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Ezekiel 11:17; Ezekiel 38:23"> With your sweet savour will I accept you, when I bring you out from the peoples, and gather you out of the countries wherein ye have been scattered; and I will be sanctified in you in the sight of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> And ye shall know that I am the LORD, when I shall bring you into the land of Israel, into the country which I lifted up My hand to give unto your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 5:15; Jeremiah 31:18"> And there shall ye remember your ways, and all your doings, wherein ye have polluted yourselves; and ye shall loathe yourselves in your own sight for all your evils that ye have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:24; Psalms 115:1; Ezekiel 20:9"> And ye shall know that I am the LORD, when I have wrought with you for My name's sake, not according to your evil ways, nor according to your corrupt doings, O ye house of Israel, saith the Lord GOD.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> (21-1) And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Amos 7:16; Ezekiel 21:2; Jeremiah 13:19"> (21-2) 'Son of man, set thy face toward the South, and preach toward the South, and prophesy against the forest of the field in the South;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Isaiah 24:1–6; Ezekiel 17:24"> (21-3) and say to the forest of the South: Hear the word of the LORD: Thus saith the Lord GOD: Behold, I will kindle a fire in thee, and it shall devour every green tree in thee, and every dry tree, it shall not be quenched, even a flaming flame; and all faces from the south to the north shall be seared thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:2–3; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> (21-4) And all flesh shall see that I the LORD have kindled it; it shall not be quenched.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 16:25; Acts 17:18; Ezekiel 17:2"> (21-5) Then said I: 'Ah Lord GOD! they say of me: Is he not a maker of parables?'</span>
