<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:12; 2 Chronicles 9:30; 1 Kings 12:19–20"> And king Solomon was king over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 2 Samuel 8:15–18"> And these were the princes whom he had: Azariah the son of Zadok, the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 Chronicles 18:15"> Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the host; and Zadok and Abiathar were priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:33; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 16:16"> and Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers; and Zabud the son of Nathan was chief minister and the king's friend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 12:18"> and Ahishar was over the household; and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the levy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, who provided victuals for the king and his household: each man had to make provision for a month in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> And these are their names: The son of Hur, in the hill-country of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> the son of Deker, in Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> the son of Hesed, in Arubboth; to him pertained Socoh, and all the land of Hepher;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> the son of Abinadab, in all the region of Dor; he had Taphath the daughter of Solomon to wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Baana the son of Ahilud, in Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean which is beside Zarethan, beneath Jezreel, from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah, as far as beyond Jokmeam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> the son of Geber, in Ramoth-gilead; to him pertained the villages of Jair the son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead; even to him pertained the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, threescore great cities with walls and brazen bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; 2 Samuel 17:24; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Ahinadab the son of Iddo, in Mahanaim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Ahimaaz, in Naphtali; he also took Basemath the daughter of Solomon to wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Baana the son of Hushai, in Asher and Bealoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23"> Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zechariah 12:13"> Shimei the son of Ela, in Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Geber the son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, the country of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan; and one officer that was over all the officers in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking and making merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalms 68:29"> (5-1) And Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the River unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt; they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> (5-2) And Solomon's provision for one day was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> (5-3) ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and a hundred sheep, beside harts, and gazelles, and roebucks, and fatted fowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; Psalms 72:11; Psalms 72:3"> (5-4) For he had dominion over all the region on this side the River, from Tiphsah even to Gaza, over all the kings on this side the River; and he had peace on all sides round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> (5-5) And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig-tree, from Dan even to Beer-sheba, all the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> (5-6) And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> (5-7) And those officers provided victual for king Solomon, and for all that came unto king Solomon's table, every man in his month; they let nothing be lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> (5-8) Barley also and straw for the horses and swift steeds brought they unto the place where it should be, every man according to his charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> (5-9) And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea-shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> (5-10) And Solomon's wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east, and all the wisdom of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> (5-11) For he was wiser than all men: than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol; and his fame was in all the nations round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> (5-12) And he spoke three thousand proverbs; and his songs were a thousand and five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Psalms 51:7; Psalms 92:12"> (5-13) And he spoke of trees, from the cedar that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall; he spoke also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:23; 1 Kings 10:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> (5-14) And there came of all peoples to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, who had heard of his wisdom.</span>
