<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then spoke Solomon: The LORD hath said that He would dwell in the thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4–6; Psalms 135:21; 1 Chronicles 28:6"> But I have built Thee a house of habitation, and a place for Thee to dwell in for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:14; 2 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 8:55–61"> And the king turned his face, and blessed all the congregation of Israel; and all the congregation of Israel stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; Psalms 68:32–35"> And he said: 'Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who spoke with His mouth unto David my father, and hath with His hands fulfilled it, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; 1 Kings 8:16; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> Since the day that I brought forth My people out of the land of Egypt, I chose no city out of all the tribes of Israel to build a house in, that My name might be there; neither chose I any man to be prince over My people Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:4; Psalms 132:13; 2 Chronicles 12:13"> but I have chosen Jerusalem, that My name might be there; and have chosen David to be over My people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:1; 1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 22:7"> Now it was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; 1 Kings 8:18–21"> But the LORD said unto David my father: Whereas it was in thy heart to build a house for My name, thou didst well that it was in thy heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–13; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 1 Chronicles 17:11–12"> nevertheless thou shalt not build the house, but thy son that shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build the house for My name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> And the LORD hath established His word that He spoke; for I am risen up in the room of David my father, and sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised, and have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 5:7; 1 Kings 8:21"> And there have I set the ark, wherein is the covenant of the LORD, which He made with the children of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Isaiah 50:10; Job 11:13"> And he stood before the altar of the LORD in the presence of all the congregation of Israel, and spread forth his hands —</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; Psalms 95:6"> for Solomon had made a brazen scaffold, of five cubits long, and five cubits broad, and three cubits high, and had set it in the midst of the court; and upon it he stood, and kneeled down upon his knees before all the congregation of Israel, and spread forth his hands toward heaven —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> and he said: 'O LORD, the God of Israel, there is no God like Thee, in the heaven, or in the earth; who keepest covenant and mercy with Thy servants, that walk before Thee with all their heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 8:24"> who hast kept with Thy servant David my father that which Thou didst promise him; yea, Thou spokest with Thy mouth, and hast fulfilled it with Thy hand, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; John 15:14–15; 2 Chronicles 7:18"> Now therefore, O LORD, the God of Israel, keep with Thy servant David my father that which Thou hast promised him, saying: There shall not fail thee a man in My sight to sit on the throne of Israel; if only thy children take heed to their way, to walk in My law as thou hast walked before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:5; Isaiah 41:17; 2 Chronicles 6:4"> Now therefore, O LORD, the God of Israel, let Thy word be verified, which Thou spokest unto Thy servant David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 139:7–10"> But will God in very truth dwell with men on the earth? behold, heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain Thee; how much less this house which I have builded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> Yet have Thou respect unto the prayer of Thy servant, and to his supplication, O LORD my God, to hearken unto the cry and to the prayer which Thy servant prayeth before Thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Daniel 6:10; Psalms 121:5"> that Thine eyes may be open toward this house day and night, even toward the place whereof Thou hast said that thou wouldest put Thy name there; to hearken unto the prayer which Thy servant shall pray toward this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Isaiah 43:25"> And hearken Thou to the supplications of Thy servant, and of Thy people Israel, when they shall pray toward this place; yea, hear Thou from Thy dwelling-place, even from heaven; and when Thou hearest, forgive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> If a man sin against his neighbour, and an oath be exacted of him to cause him to swear, and he come and swear before Thine altar in this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremiah 51:56"> then hear Thou from heaven, and do, and judge Thy servants, requiting the wicked, to bring his way upon his own head; and justifying the righteous, to give him according to his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> And if Thy people Israel be smitten down before the enemy, when they sin against Thee, and shall turn again and confess Thy name, and pray and make supplication before Thee in this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> then hear Thou from heaven, and forgive the sin of Thy people Israel, and bring them back unto the land which Thou gavest to them and to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> When the heaven is shut up, and there is no rain, when they sin against Thee; if they pray toward this place, and confess Thy name, turning from their sin, when Thou dost afflict them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35–36; Jeremiah 5:24; Zechariah 10:1"> then hear Thou in heaven, and forgive the sin of Thy servants, and of Thy people Israel, when Thou dost direct them on the good way wherein they should walk; and send rain upon Thy land, which Thou hast given to Thy people for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> If there be in the land famine, if there be pestilence, if there be blasting or mildew, locust or caterpillar; if their enemies besiege them in the land of their cities; whatsoever plague or whatsoever sickness there be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 32:2–6; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13"> what prayer and supplication soever be made by any man, or by all Thy people Israel, who shall know every man his own plague and his own pain, and shall spread forth his hands toward this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; 1 Samuel 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> then hear Thou from heaven Thy dwelling-place, and forgive, and render unto every man according to all his ways, whose heart Thou knowest — for Thou, even Thou only, knowest the hearts of the children of men —</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; Psalms 128:1"> that they may fear Thee, to walk in Thy ways, all the days that they live in the land which Thou gavest unto our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–7; 1 Kings 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> Moreover concerning the stranger, that is not of Thy people Israel, when be shall come out of a far country for Thy great name's sake, and Thy mighty hand, and Thine outstretched arm; when they shall come and pray toward this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; 1 Kings 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> then hear Thou from heaven, even from Thy dwelling-place, and do according to all that the stranger calleth to Thee for; that all the peoples of the earth may know Thy name, and fear Thee, as doth Thy people Israel, and that they may know that Thy name is called upon this house which I have built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:6–13; 2 Chronicles 18:31; 2 Chronicles 20:4"> If Thy people go out to battle against their enemies, by whatsoever way Thou shalt send them, and they pray unto Thee toward this city which Thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for Thy name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Psalms 9:3–4; Jeremiah 5:28"> then hear Thou from heaven their prayer and their supplication, and maintain their cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> If they sin against Thee — for there is no man that sinneth not — and Thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captive unto a land far off or near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Isaiah 64:6–12"> yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they are carried captive, and turn, and make supplication unto Thee in the land of their captivity, saying: We have sinned, we have done iniquitously, and have dealt wickedly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> if they return unto Thee with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity, whither they have carried them captive, and pray toward their land, which Thou gavest unto their fathers, and the city which Thou hast chosen, and toward the house which I have built for Thy name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; 2 Chronicles 6:35; Psalms 25:18"> then hear Thou from heaven, even from Thy dwelling-place, their prayer and their supplications, and maintain their cause; and forgive Thy people who have sinned against Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; Psalms 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now, O my God, let, I beseech Thee, Thine eyes be open, and let Thine ears be attent, unto the prayer that is made in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalms 132:8–10"> Now therefore arise, O LORD God, into Thy resting-place, Thou, and the ark of Thy strength; let Thy priests, O LORD God, be clothed with salvation, and let Thy saints rejoice in good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 132:1; Isaiah 61:1"> O LORD God, turn not away the face of Thine anointed; remember the good deeds of David Thy servant.'</span>
