<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> The burden concerning the Valley of Vision. What aileth thee now, that thou art wholly gone up to the housetops,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> Thou that art full of uproar, a tumultuous city, a joyous town? Thy slain are not slain with the sword, nor dead in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All thy rulers are fled together, without the bow they are bound; all that are found of thee are bound together, they are fled afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> Therefore said I: 'Look away from me, I will weep bitterly; strain not to comfort me, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For it is a day of trouble, and of trampling, and of perplexity, from the Lord, the GOD of hosts, in the Valley of Vision; Kir shouting, and Shoa at the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And Elam bore the quiver, with troops of men, even horsemen; and Kir uncovered the shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> And it came to pass, when thy choicest valleys were full of chariots, and the horsemen set themselves in array at the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> And the covering of Judah was laid bare, that thou didst look in that day to the armour in the house of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> And ye saw the breaches of the city of David, that they were many; and ye gathered together the waters of the lower pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And ye numbered the houses of Jerusalem, and ye broke down the houses to fortify the wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> ye made also a basin between the two walls for the water of the old pool — but ye looked not unto Him that had done this, neither had ye respect unto Him that fashioned it long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And in that day did the Lord, the GOD of hosts, call to weeping, and to lamentation, and to baldness, and to girding with sackcloth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> And behold joy and gladness, slaying oxen and killing sheep, eating flesh and drinking wine — 'Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we shall die!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And the LORD of hosts revealed Himself in mine ears: Surely this iniquity shall not be expiated by you till ye die, saith the Lord, the GOD of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> Thus saith the Lord, the GOD of hosts: Go, get thee unto this steward, even unto Shebna, who is over the house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> What hast thou here, and whom hast thou here, that thou hast hewed thee out here a sepulchre, thou that hewest thee out a sepulchre on high, and gravest a habitation for thyself in the rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Behold, the LORD will hurl thee up and down with a man's throw; yea, He will wind thee round and round;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> He will violently roll and toss thee like a ball into a large country; there shalt thou die, and there shall be the chariots of thy glory, thou shame of the lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And I will thrust thee from thy post, and from thy station shalt thou be pulled down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Eliakim the son of Hilkiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> And I will clothe him with thy robe, and bind him with thy girdle, and I will commit thy government into his hand; and he shall be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; and he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I will fasten him as a peg in a sure place; and he shall be for a throne of honour to his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And they shall hang upon him all the glory of his father's house, the offspring and the issue, all vessels of small quantity, from the vessels of cups even to all the vessels of flagons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day, saith the LORD of hosts, shall the peg that was fastened in a sure place give way; and it shall be hewn down, and fall, and the burden that was upon it shall be cut off; for the LORD hath spoken it.</span>
