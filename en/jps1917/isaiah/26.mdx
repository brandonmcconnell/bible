<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah: We have a strong city; walls and bulwarks doth He appoint for salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open ye the gates, that the righteous nation that keepeth faithfulness may enter in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> The mind stayed on Thee Thou keepest in perfect peace; because it trusteth in Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 62:8; Psalms 55:22"> Trust ye in the LORD for ever, for the LORD is GOD, an everlasting Rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> For He hath brought down them that dwell on high, the lofty city, laying it low, laying it low even to the ground, bringing it even to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> The foot shall tread it down, even the feet of the poor, and the steps of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The way of the just is straight; Thou, Most Upright, makest plain the path of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> Yea, in the way of Thy judgments, O LORD, have we waited for Thee; to Thy name and to Thy memorial is the desire of our soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalms 63:1; Psalms 119:62"> With my soul have I desired Thee in the night; yea, with my spirit within me have I sought Thee earnestly; for when Thy judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> Let favour be shown to the wicked, yet will he not learn righteousness; in the land of uprightness will he deal wrongfully, and will not behold the majesty of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> LORD, Thy hand was lifted up, yet they see not; they shall see with shame Thy zeal for the people; yea, fire shall devour Thine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 57:2; Psalms 29:11"> LORD, Thou wilt establish peace for us; for Thou hast indeed wrought all our works for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> O LORD our God, other lords beside Thee have had dominion over us; but by Thee only do we make mention of Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> The dead live not, the shades rise not; to that end hast Thou punished and destroyed them, and made all their memory to perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> Thou hast gotten Thee honour with the nations, O LORD, yea, exceeding great honour with the nations; Thou art honoured unto the farthest ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Psalms 50:15; Psalms 77:1–2"> LORD, in trouble have they sought Thee, silently they poured out a prayer when Thy chastening was upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> Like as a woman with child, that draweth near the time of her delivery, is in pain and crieth out in her pangs; so have we been at Thy presence, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Psalms 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> We have been with child, we have been in pain, we have as it were brought forth wind; we have not wrought any deliverance in the land; neither are the inhabitants of the world come to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> Thy dead shall live, my dead bodies shall arise — awake and sing, ye that dwell in the dust — for Thy dew is as the dew of light, and the earth shall bring to life the shades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Psalms 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers, and shut thy doors about thee; hide thyself for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For, behold, the LORD cometh forth out of His place to visit upon the inhabitants of the earth their iniquity; the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall no more cover her slain.</span>
