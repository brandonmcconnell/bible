<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Isaiah 40:25; Psalms 89:6"> To whom will ye liken Me, and make Me equal, and compare Me, that we may be like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 40:19–20; Habakkuk 2:18–20"> Ye that lavish gold out of the bag, and weigh silver in the balance; ye that hire a goldsmith, that he make it a god, to fall down thereto, yea, to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 46:1"> He is borne upon the shoulder, he is carried, and set in his place, and he standeth, from his place he doth not remove; yea, though one cry unto him, he cannot answer, nor save him out of his trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:18–21; Psalms 115:8; Ezekiel 18:28"> Remember this, and stand fast; bring it to mind, O ye transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 45:21–22"> Remember the former things of old: that I am God, and there is none else; I am God, and there is none like Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times things that are not yet done; saying: 'My counsel shall stand, and all My pleasure will I do';</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 14:24–27; Job 23:13"> Calling a bird of prey from the east, the man of My counsel from a far country; yea, I have spoken, I will also bring it to pass, I have purposed, I will also do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 76:5"> Hearken unto Me, ye stout-hearted, that are far from righteousness:</span>
