<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Behold, the LORD maketh the earth empty and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad the inhabitants thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the creditor, so with the debtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The earth shall be utterly emptied, and clean despoiled; for the LORD hath spoken this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The earth fainteth and fadeth away, the world faileth and fadeth away, the lofty people of the earth do fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> The earth also is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; because they have transgressed the laws, violated the statute, broken the everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore hath a curse devoured the earth, and they that dwell therein are found guilty; therefore the inhabitants of the earth waste away, and men are left few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine faileth, the vine fadeth; all the merry-hearted do sigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The mirth of tabrets ceaseth, the noise of them that rejoice endeth, the joy of the harp ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They drink not wine with a song; strong drink is bitter to them that drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> Broken down is the city of wasteness; every house is shut up, that none may come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> There is a crying in the streets amidst the wine; all joy is darkened, the mirth of the land is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> In the city is left desolation, and the gate is smitten unto ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> For thus shall it be in the midst of the earth, among the peoples, as at the beating of an olive-tree, as at the gleanings when the vintage is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> Those yonder lift up their voice, they sing for joy; for the majesty of the LORD they shout from the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> 'Therefore glorify ye the LORD in the regions of light, even the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the isles of the sea.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the uttermost part of the earth have we heard songs: 'Glory to the righteous.' But I say: I waste away, I waste away, woe is me! The treacherous deal treacherously; yea, the treacherous deal very treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Terror, and the pit, and the trap, are upon thee, O inhabitant of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> And it shall come to pass, that he who fleeth from the noise of the terror shall fall into the pit; and he that cometh up out of the midst of the pit shall be taken in the trap; for the windows on high are opened, and the foundations of the earth do shake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is broken, broken down, the earth is crumbled in pieces, the earth trembleth and tottereth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth reeleth to and fro like a drunken man, and swayeth to and fro as a lodge; and the transgression thereof is heavy upon it, and it shall fall, and not rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD will punish the host of the high heaven on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> And they shall be gathered together, as prisoners are gathered in the dungeon, and shall be shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> Then the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed; for the LORD of hosts will reign in mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, and before His elders shall be Glory.</span>
