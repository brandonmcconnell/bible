<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Isaiah 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> 'Who is this that cometh from Edom, with crimsoned garments from Bozrah? This that is glorious in his apparel, stately in the greatness of his strength?' — 'I that speak in victory, mighty to save.' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> 'Wherefore is Thine apparel red, and Thy garments like his that treadeth in the winevat?' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> 'I have trodden the winepress alone, and of the peoples there was no man with Me; yea, I trod them in Mine anger, and trampled them in My fury; and their lifeblood is dashed against My garments, and I have stained all My raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of vengeance that was in My heart, and My year of redemption are come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I looked, and there was none to help, and I beheld in astonishment, and there was none to uphold; therefore Mine own arm brought salvation unto Me, and My fury, it upheld Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> And I trod down the peoples in Mine anger, and made them drunk with My fury, and I poured out their lifeblood on the earth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> I will make mention of the mercies of the LORD, and the praises of the LORD, according to all that the LORD hath bestowed on us; and the great goodness toward the house of Israel, which He hath bestowed on them according to His compassions, and according to the multitude of His mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> For He said: 'Surely, they are My people, children that will not deal falsely'; so He was their Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> In all their affliction He was afflicted, and the angel of His presence saved them; in His love and in His pity He redeemed them; and He bore them, and carried them all the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> But they rebelled, and grieved His holy spirit; therefore He was turned to be their enemy, Himself fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> Then His people remembered the days of old, the days of Moses: 'Where is He that brought them up out of the sea with the shepherds of His flock? Where is He that put His holy spirit in the midst of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> That caused His glorious arm to go at the right hand of Moses? That divided the water before them, to make Himself an everlasting name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> That led them through the deep, as a horse in the wilderness, without stumbling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> As the cattle that go down into the valley, the spirit of the LORD caused them to rest; so didst Thou lead Thy people, to make Thyself a glorious name.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Look down from heaven, and see, even from Thy holy and glorious habitation; Where is Thy zeal and Thy mighty acts, the yearning of Thy heart and Thy compassions, now restrained toward me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> For Thou art our Father; for Abraham knoweth us not, and Israel doth not acknowledge us; Thou, O LORD, art our Father, our Redeemer from everlasting is Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> O LORD, why dost Thou make us to err from Thy ways, and hardenest our heart from Thy fear? Return for Thy servants' sake, the tribes of Thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> Thy holy people they have well nigh driven out, our adversaries have trodden down Thy sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We are become as they over whom Thou never borest rule, as they that were not called by Thy name.</span>
