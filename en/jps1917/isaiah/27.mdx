<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Ezekiel 29:3; Isaiah 66:16"> In that day the LORD with His sore and great and strong sword will punish leviathan the slant serpent, and leviathan the tortuous serpent; and He will slay the dragon that is in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Isaiah 5:1–7"> In that day sing ye of her: 'A vineyard of foaming wine!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; John 15:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:9"> I the LORD do guard it, I water it every moment; lest Mine anger visit it, I guard it night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> Fury is not in Me; would that I were as the briers and thorns in flame! I would with one step burn it altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> Or else let him take hold of My strength, that he may make peace with Me; yea, let him make peace with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31; Hosea 14:5–6; Romans 11:16–26"> In days to come shall Jacob take root, Israel shall blossom and bud; and the face of the world shall be filled with fruitage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 10:20–25"> Hath He smitten him as He smote those that smote him? Or is he slain according to the slaughter of them that were slain by Him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 4:11; Ezekiel 19:12"> In full measure, when Thou sendest her away, Thou dost contend with her; He hath removed her with His rough blast in the day of the east wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> Therefore by this shall the iniquity of Jacob be expiated, and this is all the fruit of taking away his sin: when he maketh all the stones of the altar as chalkstones that are beaten in pieces, so that the Asherim and the sun-images shall rise no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> For the fortified city is solitary, a habitation abandoned and forsaken, like the wilderness; there shall the calf feed, and there shall he lie down, and consume the branches thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> When the boughs thereof are withered, they shall be broken off; the women shall come, and set them on fire; for it is a people of no understanding; therefore He that made them will not have compassion upon them, and He that formed them will not be gracious unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD will beat off His fruit from the flood of the River unto the Brook of Egypt, and ye shall be gathered one by one, O ye children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that a great horn shall be blown; and they shall come that were lost in the land of Assyria, and they that were dispersed in the land of Egypt; and they shall worship the LORD in the holy mountain at Jerusalem.</span>
