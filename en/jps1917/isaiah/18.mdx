<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; 2 Kings 19:9; Zephaniah 2:12"> Ah, land of the buzzing of wings, which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 18:7; Genesis 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 16:8"> That sendeth ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of papyrus upon the waters! Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation tall and of glossy skin, to a people terrible from their beginning onward; a nation that is sturdy and treadeth down, whose land the rivers divide!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 26:11; Matthew 13:9"> All ye inhabitants of the world, and ye dwellers on the earth, when an ensign is lifted up on the mountains, see ye; and when the horn is blown, hear ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Isaiah 26:21; Isaiah 18:7"> For thus hath the LORD said unto me: I will hold Me still, and I will look on in My dwelling-place, like clear heat in sunshine, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Song of Solomon 2:13; Ezekiel 17:6–10"> For before the harvest, when the blossom is over, and the bud becometh a ripening grape, He will cut off the sprigs with pruning-hooks, and the shoots will He take away and lop off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 32:4–6"> They shall be left together unto the ravenous birds of the mountains, and to the beasts of the earth; and the ravenous birds shall summer upon them, and all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Acts 8:27–28; Zechariah 14:16–17"> In that time shall a present be brought unto the LORD of hosts of a people tall and of glossy skin, and from a people terrible from their beginning onward; a nation that is sturdy and treadeth down, whose land the rivers divide, to the place of the name of the LORD of hosts, the mount Zion.</span>
