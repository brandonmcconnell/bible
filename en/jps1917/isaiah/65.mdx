<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> I gave access to them that asked not for Me, I was at hand to them that sought Me not; I said: 'Behold Me, behold Me', unto a nation that was not called by My name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremiah 4:14"> I have spread out My hands all the day unto a rebellious people, that walk in a way that is not good, after their own thoughts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Isaiah 66:17"> A people that provoke Me to My face continually, that sacrifice in gardens, and burn incense upon bricks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Isaiah 66:3"> That sit among the graves, and lodge in the vaults; that eat swine's flesh, and broth of abominable things is in their vessels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> That say: 'Stand by thyself, come not near to me, for I am holier than thou'; these are a smoke in My nose, a fire that burneth all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:3; Jeremiah 16:18; Psalms 79:12"> Behold, it is written before Me; I will not keep silence, except I have requited, yea, I will requite into their bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:27–28; Isaiah 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> Your own iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together, saith the LORD, that have offered upon the mountains, and blasphemed Me upon the hills; therefore will I first measure their wage into their bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> Thus saith the LORD: As, when wine is found in the cluster, one saith: 'Destroy it not, for a blessing is in it'; so will I do for My servants' sakes, that I may not destroy all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I will bring forth a seed out of Jacob, and out of Judah an inheritor of My mountains; and Mine elect shall inherit it, and My servants shall dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 33:9; Hosea 2:15"> And Sharon shall be a fold of flocks, and the valley of Achor a place for herds to lie down in, for My people that have sought Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2; Isaiah 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> But ye that forsake the LORD, that forget My holy mountain, that prepare a table for Fortune, and that offer mingled wine in full measure unto Destiny,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> I will destine you to the sword, and ye shall all bow down to the slaughter; because when I called, ye did not answer, when I spoke, ye did not hear; but ye did that which was evil in Mine eyes, and chose that wherein I delighted not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD: Behold, My servants shall eat, but ye shall be hungry; Behold, My servants shall drink, but ye shall be thirsty; Behold, My servants shall rejoice, but ye shall be ashamed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalms 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> Behold, My servants shall sing for joy of heart, but ye shall cry for sorrow of heart, and shall wail for vexation of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> And ye shall leave your name for a curse unto Mine elect: 'So may the Lord GOD slay thee'; but He shall call His servants by another name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18; Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 31:5"> So that he who blesseth himself in the earth shall bless himself by the God of truth; and he that sweareth in the earth shall swear by the God of truth; because the former troubles are forgotten, and because they are hid from Mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Isaiah 66:22"> For, behold, I create new heavens and a new earth; and the former things shall not be remembered, nor come into mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Isaiah 66:10–14"> But be ye glad and rejoice for ever in that which I create; for, behold, I create Jerusalem a rejoicing, and her people a joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Isaiah 25:8"> And I will rejoice in Jerusalem, and joy in My people; and the voice of weeping shall be no more heard in her, nor the voice of crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> There shall be no more thence an infant of days, nor an old man, that hath not filled his days; for the youngest shall die a hundred years old, and the sinner being a hundred years old shall be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Isaiah 62:8–9; Isaiah 32:18"> And they shall build houses, and inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 91:16; Psalms 21:4"> They shall not build, and another inhabit, they shall not plant, and another eat; for as the days of a tree shall be the days of My people, and Mine elect shall long enjoy the work of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Jeremiah 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> They shall not labour in vain, nor bring forth for terror; for they are the seed blessed of the LORD, and their offspring with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Isaiah 58:9"> And it shall come to pass that, before they call, I will answer, and while they are yet speaking, I will hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Micah 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> The wolf and the lamb shall feed together, and the lion shall eat straw like the ox; and dust shall be the serpent's food. They shall not hurt nor destroy in all My holy mountain, saith the LORD.</span>
