<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Psalms 137:8; Zechariah 2:7"> Come down, and sit in the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon, sit on the ground without a throne, O daughter of the Chaldeans; for thou shalt no more be called tender and delicate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5"> Take the millstones, and grind meal; remove thy veil, strip off the train, uncover the leg, pass through the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Jeremiah 50:27–28"> Thy nakedness shall be uncovered, yea, thy shame shall be seen; I will take vengeance, and will let no man intercede.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 49:26"> Our Redeemer, the LORD of hosts is His name, The Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 18:16–19; Habakkuk 2:20; Isaiah 47:7"> Sit thou silent, and get thee into darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans; for thou shalt no more be called the mistress of kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:9; Zechariah 1:15; Deuteronomy 28:50"> I was wroth with My people, I profaned Mine inheritance, and gave them into thy hand; thou didst show them no mercy; upon the aged hast thou very heavily laid thy yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Isaiah 47:5; Jeremiah 5:31"> And thou saidst: 'For ever shall I be mistress'; so that thou didst not lay these things to thy heart, neither didst remember the end thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:15; Isaiah 32:9; Isaiah 47:10"> Now therefore hear this, thou that art given to pleasures, that sittest securely, that sayest in thy heart: 'I am, and there is none else beside me; I shall not sit as a widow, neither shall I know the loss of children';</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 47:12–13"> But these two things shall come to thee in a moment in one day, the loss of children, and widowhood; in their full measure shall they come upon thee, for the multitude of thy sorceries, and the great abundance of thine enchantments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Isaiah 29:15; Romans 1:22"> And thou hast been secure in thy wickedness, thou hast said: 'None seeth me'; thy wisdom and thy knowledge, it hath perverted thee; and thou hast said in thy heart. 'I am, and there is none else beside me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 18:9–10; Daniel 5:25–30"> Yet shall evil came upon thee; thou shalt not know how to charm it away; and calamity shall fall upon thee; thou shalt not be able to put it away; and ruin shall come upon thee suddenly, before thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:8–12; Nahum 3:4; Jeremiah 2:28"> Stand now with thine enchantments, and with the multitude of thy sorceries, wherein thou hast laboured from thy youth; if so be thou shalt be able to profit, if so be thou mayest prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; Daniel 2:2–10; Ezekiel 24:12"> Thou art wearied in the multitude of thy counsels; let now the astrologers, the stargazers, the monthly prognosticators, stand up, and save thee from the things that shall come upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Nahum 1:10; Ezekiel 15:7"> Behold, they shall be as stubble; the fire shall burn them; they shall not deliver themselves from the power of the flame; it shall not be a coal to warm at, nor a fire to sit before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 27:12–25; Jeremiah 51:6–9"> Thus shall they be unto thee with whom thou hast laboured; they that have trafficked with thee from thy youth shall wander every one to his quarter; there shall be none to save thee.</span>
