<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> And when Rachel saw that she bore Jacob no children, Rachel envied her sister; and she said unto Jacob: 'Give me children, or else I die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> And Jacob's anger was kindled against Rachel; and he said: 'Am I in God's stead, who hath withheld from thee the fruit of the womb?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> And she said: 'Behold my maid Bilhah, go in unto her; that she may bear upon my knees, and I also may be builded up through her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> And she gave him Bilhah her handmaid to wife; and Jacob went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Bilhah conceived, and bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> And Rachel said: 'God hath judged me, and hath also heard my voice, and hath given me a son.' Therefore called she his name Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Bilhah Rachel's handmaid conceived again, and bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> And Rachel said: 'With mighty wrestlings have I wrestled with my sister, and have prevailed.' And she called his name Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> When Leah saw that she had left off bearing, she took Zilpah her handmaid, and gave her to Jacob to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Zilpah Leah's handmaid bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> And Leah said: 'Fortune is come!' And she called his name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Zilpah Leah's handmaid bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> And Leah said: 'Happy am I! for the daughters will call me happy.' And she called his name Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> And Reuben went in the days of wheat harvest, and found mandrakes in the field, and brought them unto his mother Leah. Then Rachel said to Leah: 'Give me, I pray thee, of thy son's mandrakes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> And she said unto her: 'Is it a small matter that thou hast taken away my husband? and wouldest thou take away my son's mandrakes also?' And Rachel said: 'Therefore he shall lie with thee to-night for thy son's mandrakes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jacob came from the field in the evening, and Leah went out to meet him, and said: 'Thou must come in unto me; for I have surely hired thee with my son's mandrakes.' And he lay with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And God hearkened unto Leah, and she conceived, and bore Jacob a fifth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> And Leah said: 'God hath given me my hire, because I gave my handmaid to my husband. And she called his name Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Leah conceived again, and bore a sixth son to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> And Leah said: 'God hath endowed me with a good dowry; now will my husband dwell with me, because I have borne him six sons.' And she called his name Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> And afterwards she bore a daughter, and called her name Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> And God remembered Rachel, and God hearkened to her, and opened her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> And she conceived, and bore a son, and said: 'God hath taken away my reproach.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> And she called his name Joseph, saying: 'The LORD add to me another son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> And it came to pass, when Rachel had borne Joseph, that Jacob said unto Laban: 'Send me away, that I may go unto mine own place, and to my country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Give me my wives and my children for whom I have served thee, and let me go; for thou knowest my service wherewith I have served thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> And Laban said unto him: 'If now I have found favour in thine eyes — I have observed the signs, and the LORD hath blessed me for thy sake.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> And he said: 'Appoint me thy wages, and I will give it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> And he said unto him: 'Thou knowest how I have served thee, and how thy cattle have fared with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> For it was little which thou hadst before I came, and it hath increased abundantly; and the LORD hath blessed thee whithersoever I turned. And now when shall I provide for mine own house also?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And he said: 'What shall I give thee?' And Jacob said: 'Thou shalt not give me aught; if thou wilt do this thing for me, I will again feed thy flock and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> I will pass through all thy flock to-day, removing from thence every speckled and spotted one, and every dark one among the sheep, and the spotted and speckled among the goats; and of such shall be my hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> So shall my righteousness witness against me hereafter, when thou shalt come to look over my hire that is before thee: every one that is not speckled and spotted among the goats, and dark among the sheep, that if found with me shall be counted stolen.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban said: 'Behold, would it might be according to thy word.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> And he removed that day the he-goats that were streaked and spotted, and all the she-goats that were speckled and spotted, every one that had white in it, and all the dark ones among the sheep, and gave them into the hand of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And he set three days' journey betwixt himself and Jacob. And Jacob fed the rest of Laban's flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> And Jacob took him rods of fresh poplar, and of the almond and of the plane-tree; and peeled white streaks in them, making the white appear which was in the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he set the rods which he had peeled over against the flocks in the gutters in the watering-troughs where the flocks came to drink; and they conceived when they came to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> And the flocks conceived at the sight of the rods, and the flocks brought forth streaked, speckled, and spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jacob separated the lambs — he also set the faces of the flocks toward the streaked and all the dark in the flock of Laban — and put his own droves apart, and put them not unto Laban's flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it came to pass, whensoever the stronger of the flock did conceive, that Jacob laid the rods before the eyes of the flock in the gutters, that they might conceive among the rods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> but when the flock were feeble, he put them not in; so the feebler were Laban's, and the stronger Jacob's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> And the man increased exceedingly, and had large flocks, and maid-servants and men-servants, and camels and asses.</span>
