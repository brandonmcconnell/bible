<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Ruth 3:12; Ruth 3:2"> And Naomi had a kinsman of her husband's, a mighty man of valour, of the family of Elimelech, and his name was Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And Ruth the Moabitess said unto Naomi: 'Let me now go to the field, and glean among the ears of corn after him in whose sight I shall find favour.' And she said unto her: 'Go, my daughter.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:12; Matthew 10:29; Luke 10:31"> And she went, and came and gleaned in the field after the reapers; and her hap was to light on the portion of the field belonging unto Boaz, who was of the family of Elimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 129:7–8; Judges 6:12; Luke 1:28"> And, behold, Boaz came from Beth-lehem, and said unto the reapers: 'The LORD be with you.' And they answered him: 'The LORD bless thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> Then said Boaz unto his servant that was set over the reapers: 'Whose damsel is this?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Matthew 24:45; Genesis 24:2"> And the servant that was set over the reapers answered and said: 'It is a Moabitish damsel that came back with Naomi out of the field of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 18:23; Proverbs 15:33"> and she said: Let me glean, I pray you, and gather after the reapers among the sheaves; so she came, and hath continued even from the morning until now, save that she tarried a little in the house.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; 1 Samuel 3:6"> Then said Boaz unto Ruth: 'Hearest thou not, my daughter? Go not to glean in another field, neither pass from hence, but abide here fast by my maidens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Let thine eyes be on the field that they do reap, and go thou after them; have I not charged the young men that they shall not touch thee? and when thou art athirst, go unto the vessels, and drink of that which the young men have drawn.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; Ruth 2:13; Luke 1:43"> Then she fell on her face, and bowed down to the ground, and said unto him: 'Why have I found favour in thy sight, that thou shouldest take cognizance of me, seeing I am a foreigner?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Luke 18:29–30; Luke 5:11"> And Boaz answered and said unto her: 'It hath fully been told me, all that thou hast done unto thy mother-in-law since the death of thy husband; and how thou hast left thy father and thy mother, and the land of thy nativity, and art come unto a people that thou knewest not heretofore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 36:7; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 63:7"> The LORD recompense thy work, and be thy reward complete from the LORD, the God of Israel, under whose wings thou art come to take refuge.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:18; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 33:8"> Then she said: 'Let me find favour in thy sight, my LORD; for that thou hast comforted me, and for that thou hast spoken to the heart of thy handmaid, though I be not as one of thy handmaidens.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 2:18; 2 Kings 4:43–44; Matthew 14:20"> And Boaz said unto her at meal-time: 'Come hither, and eat of the bread, and dip thy morsel in the vinegar.' And she sat beside the reapers; and they reached her parched corn, and she did eat and was satisfied, and left thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 1:5"> And when she was risen up to glean, Boaz commanded his young men, saying: 'Let her glean even among the sheaves, and put her not to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 1 John 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And also pull out some for her of purpose from the bundles, and leave it, and let her glean, and rebuke her not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–12; Proverbs 31:27; Exodus 16:36"> So she gleaned in the field until even; and she beat out that which she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:14; John 6:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:4"> And she took it up, and went into the city; and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned; and she brought forth and gave to her that which she had left after she was satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Psalms 41:1; 1 Kings 7:21"> And her mother-in-law said unto her: 'Where hast thou gleaned to-day? and where wroughtest thou? blessed be he that did take knowledge of thee.' And she told her mother-in-law with whom she had wrought, and said: 'The man's name with whom I wrought to-day is Boaz.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:5; Ruth 3:9–10; Leviticus 25:25"> And Naomi said unto her daughter-in-law: 'Blessed be he of the LORD, who hath not left off His kindness to the living and to the dead.' And Naomi said unto her: 'The man is nigh of kin unto us, one of our near kinsmen.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Ruth 2:7–8; Ruth 2:22"> And Ruth the Moabitess said: 'Yea, he said unto me: Thou shalt keep fast by my young men, until they have ended all my harvest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Proverbs 27:10"> And Naomi said unto Ruth her daughter-in-law: 'It is good, my daughter, that thou go out with his maidens, and that thou be not met in any other field.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8; Deuteronomy 16:9; Ephesians 6:1–3"> So she kept fast by the maidens of Boaz to glean unto the end of barley harvest and of wheat harvest; and she dwelt with her mother-in-law.</span>
