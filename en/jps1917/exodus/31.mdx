<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD spoke unto Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> 'See, I have called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 Kings 7:14"> and I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14"> to devise skilful works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> and in cutting of stones for setting, and in carving of wood, to work in all manner of workmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> And I, behold, I have appointed with him Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and in the hearts of all that are wise-hearted I have put wisdom, that they may make all that I have commanded thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the tent of meeting, and the ark of the testimony, and the ark-cover that is thereupon, and all the furniture of the Tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> and the table and its vessels, and the pure candlestick with all its vessels, and the altar of incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> and the altar of burnt-offering with all its vessels, and the laver and its base;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> and the plaited garments, and the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to minister in the priest's office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> and the anointing oil, and the incense of sweet spices for the holy place; according to all that I have commanded thee shall they do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the LORD spoke unto Moses, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> 'Speak thou also unto the children of Israel, saying: Verily ye shall keep My sabbaths, for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that ye may know that I am the LORD who sanctify you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> Ye shall keep the sabbath therefore, for it is holy unto you; every one that profaneth it shall surely be put to death; for whosoever doeth any work therein, that soul shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> Six days shall work be done; but on the seventh day is a sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD; whosoever doeth any work in the sabbath day, he shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> Wherefore the children of Israel shall keep the sabbath, to observe the sabbath throughout their generations, for a perpetual covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> It is a sign between Me and the children of Israel for ever; for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day He ceased from work and rested.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And He gave unto Moses, when He had made an end of speaking with him upon mount Sinai, the two tables of the testimony, tables of stone, written with the finger of God.</span>
