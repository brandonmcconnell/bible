<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 4:36"> And God spoke all these words, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Hosea 13:4; Leviticus 26:13"> I am the LORD thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalms 81:9"> Thou shalt have no other gods before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make unto thee a graven image, nor any manner of likeness, of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> thou shalt not bow down unto them, nor serve them; for I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; John 14:21; 1 John 5:3"> and showing mercy unto the thousandth generation of them that love Me and keep My commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> Thou shalt not take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh His name in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> but the seventh day is a sabbath unto the LORD thy God, in it thou shalt not do any manner of work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy man-servant, nor thy maid-servant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested on the seventh day; wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> Honour thy father and thy mother, that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> Thou shalt not murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> (20-13) Thou shalt not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> (20-13) Thou shalt not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalms 15:3"> (20-13) Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> (20-14) Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house; thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's wife, nor his man-servant, nor his maid-servant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:16–18; Jeremiah 23:23; Psalms 139:7–8"> (20-15) And all the people perceived the thunderings, and the lightnings, and the voice of the horn, and the mountain smoking; and when the people saw it, they trembled, and stood afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> (20-16) And they said unto Moses: 'Speak thou with us, and we will hear; but let not God speak with us, lest we die.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Samuel 12:20"> (20-17) And Moses said unto the people: 'Fear not; for God is come to prove you, and that His fear may be before you, that ye sin not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 18:9"> (20-18) And the people stood afar off; but Moses drew near unto the thick darkness where God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> (20-19) And the LORD said unto Moses: Thus thou shalt say unto the children of Israel: Ye yourselves have seen that I have talked with you from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Zephaniah 1:5; 2 Kings 17:33"> (20-20) Ye shall not make with Me — gods of silver, or gods of gold, ye shall not make unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> (20-21) An altar of earth thou shalt make unto Me, and shalt sacrifice thereon thy burnt-offerings, and thy peace-offerings, thy sheep, and thine oxen; in every place where I cause My name to be mentioned I will come unto thee and bless thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> (20-22) And if thou make Me an altar of stone, thou shalt not build it of hewn stones; for if thou lift up thy tool upon it, thou hast profaned it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> (20-23) Neither shalt thou go up by steps unto Mine altar, that thy nakedness be not uncovered thereon.</span>
