<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Isaiah 12:2; Proverbs 18:10"> The God who is my rock, in Him I take refuge; my shield, and my horn of salvation, my high tower, and my refuge; my saviour, Thou savest me from violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> Praised, I cry, is the LORD, and I am saved from mine enemies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> Then the earth did shake and quake, the foundations of heaven did tremble; they were shaken, because He was wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> Smoke arose up in His nostrils, and fire out of His mouth did devour; coals flamed forth from Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> He bowed the heavens also, and came down; and thick darkness was under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And He rode upon a cherub, and did fly; yea, He was seen upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Samuel 22:10; Job 36:29"> And He made darkness pavilions round about Him, gathering of waters, thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:9"> At the brightness before Him coals of fire flamed forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> The LORD thundered from heaven, and the Most High gave forth His voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> And He sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were laid bare by the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of His nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sent from on high, He took me; He drew me out of many waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivered me from mine enemy most strong, from them that hated me, for they were too mighty for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> They confronted me in the day of my calamity; but the LORD was a stay unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> He brought me forth also into a large place; He delivered me, because He delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath He recompensed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all His ordinances were before me; and as for His statutes, I did not depart from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> And I was single-hearted toward Him, and I kept myself from mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> Therefore hath the LORD recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in His eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the merciful Thou dost show Thyself merciful, with the upright man Thou dost show Thyself upright,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> With the pure Thou dost show myself pure; and with the crooked Thou dost show Thyself subtle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And the afflicted people Thou dost save; but Thine eyes are upon the haughty, that Thou mayest humble them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For Thou art my lamp, O LORD; and the LORD doth lighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For by Thee I run upon a troop; by my God do I scale a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is tried; He is a shield unto all them that take refuge in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> For who is God, save the LORD? and who is a Rock, save our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> The God who is my strong fortress, and who letteth my way go forth straight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> Who maketh my feet like hinds', and setteth me upon my high places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> Who traineth my hands for war, so that mine arms do bend a bow of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> Thou hast also given me Thy shield of salvation; and Thy condescension hath made me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, and my feet have not slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; neither did I turn back till they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And I have consumed them, and smitten them through, that they cannot arise; yea, they are fallen under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> For Thou hast girded me with strength unto the battle; Thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> Thou hast also made mine enemies turn their backs unto me; yea, them that hate me, that I might cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They looked, but there was none to save; even unto the LORD, but He answered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> Then did I beat them small as the dust of the earth, I did stamp them as the mire of the streets, and did tread them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> Thou also hast delivered me from the contentions of my people; Thou hast kept me to be the head of the nations; a people whom I have not known serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> The sons of the stranger dwindle away before me; as soon as they hear of me, they obey me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> The sons of the stranger fade away, and come halting out of their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> The LORD liveth, and blessed be my Rock; and exalted be the God, my Rock of salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> Even the God that executeth vengeance for me, and bringeth down peoples under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> And that bringeth me forth from mine enemies; yea, Thou liftest me up above them that rise up against me; Thou deliverest me from the violent man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore I will give thanks unto Thee, O LORD, among the nations, and will sing praises unto Thy name.</span>
