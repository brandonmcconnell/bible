<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8"> The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the droughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Jeremiah 8:21; 1 Samuel 5:12"> Judah mourneth, and the gates thereof languish, they bow down in black unto the ground; and the cry of Jerusalem is gone up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Psalms 40:14; 2 Kings 18:31"> And their nobles send their lads for water: they come to the pits, and find no water; their vessels return empty; they are ashamed and confounded, and cover their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 1:19–20; Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 3:3"> Because of the ground which is cracked, for there hath been no rain in the land, the plowmen are ashamed, they cover their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> Yea, the hind also in the field calveth, and forsaketh her young, because there is no grass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> And the wild asses stand on the high hills, they gasp for air like jackals; their eyes fail, because there is no herbage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:20–21"> Though our iniquities testify against us, O LORD, work Thou for Thy name's sake; for our backslidings are many, we have sinned against Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 50:7; Isaiah 43:3"> O Thou hope of Israel, the Saviour thereof in time of trouble, why shouldest Thou be as a stranger in the land, and as a wayfaring man that turneth aside to tarry for a night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; Jeremiah 15:16"> Why shouldest thou be as a man overcome, as a mighty man that cannot save? Yet Thou, O LORD, art in the midst of us, and Thy name is called upon us; leave us not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13; Psalms 119:101"> Thus saith the LORD unto this people: Even so have they loved to wander, they have not refrained their feet; therefore the LORD doth not accept them, now will He remember their iniquity, and punish their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> And the LORD said unto me: 'Pray not for this people for their good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> When they fast, I will not hear their cry; and when they offer burnt-offering and meal-offering, I will not accept them; but I will consume them by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> Then said I: 'Ah, Lord GOD! behold, the prophets say unto them: Ye shall not see the sword, neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Then the LORD said unto me: 'The prophets prophesy lies in My name; I sent them not, neither have I commanded them, neither spoke I unto them; they prophesy unto you a lying vision, and divination, and a thing of nought, and the deceit of their own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> Therefore thus saith the LORD: As for the prophets that prophesy in My name, and I sent them not, yet they say: Sword and famine shall not be in this land, by sword and famine shall those prophets be consumed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> and the people to whom they prophesy shall be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem because of the famine and the sword; and they shall have none to bury them, them, their wives, nor their sons, nor their daughters; for I will pour their evil upon them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:21; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 13:17"> And thou shalt say this word unto them: Let mine eyes run down with tears night and day, and let them not cease; for the virgin daughter of my people is broken with a great breach, with a very grievous blow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremiah 8:10"> If I go forth into the field, then behold the slain with the sword! And if I enter into the city, then behold them that are sick with famine! For both the prophet and the priest are gone about to a land, and knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 5:22; Jeremiah 8:15; Jeremiah 6:30"> Hast Thou utterly rejected Judah? Hath Thy soul loathed Zion? Why hast Thou smitten us, and there is no healing for us? We looked for peace, but no good came; and for a time of healing, and behold terror!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 32:5; 1 John 1:7–9"> We acknowledge, O LORD, our wickedness, even the iniquity of our fathers; for we have sinned against Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 3:17"> Do not contemn us, for Thy name's sake, do not dishonour the throne of Thy glory; remember, break not Thy covenant with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:23; Jeremiah 5:24; Psalms 135:7"> Are there any among the vanities of the nations that can cause rain? or can the heavens give showers? Art not Thou He, O LORD our God, and do we not wait for Thee? For Thou hast made all these things.</span>
