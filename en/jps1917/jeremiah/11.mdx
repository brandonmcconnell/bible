<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> 'Hear ye the words of this covenant, and speak unto the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> and say thou unto them: Thus saith the LORD, the God of Israel: Cursed be the man that heareth not the words of this covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> which I commanded your fathers in the day that I brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, saying: Hearken to My voice, and do them, according to all which I command you; so shall ye be My people, and I will be your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalms 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> that I may establish the oath which I swore unto your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as at this day.' Then answered I, and said: 'Amen, O LORD.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> And the LORD said unto me: 'Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, saying: Hear ye the words of this covenant, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> For I earnestly forewarned your fathers in the day that I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, even unto this day, forewarning betimes and often, saying: Hearken to My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> Yet they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear, but walked every one in the stubbornness of their evil heart; therefore I brought upon them all the words of this covenant, which I commanded them to do, but they did them not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> And the LORD said unto me: 'A conspiracy is found among the men of Judah, and among the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalms 78:8–10"> They are turned back to the iniquities of their forefathers, who refused to hear My words; and they are gone after other gods to serve them; the house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken My covenant which I made with their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> Therefore thus saith the LORD: Behold, I will bring evil upon them, which they shall not be able to escape; and though they shall cry unto Me, I will not hearken unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> Then shall the cities of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem go and cry unto the gods unto whom they offer; but they shall not save them at all in the time of their trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> For according to the number of thy cities are thy gods, O Judah; and according to the number of the streets of Jerusalem have ye set up altars to the shameful thing, even altars to offer unto Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 John 5:16"> Therefore pray not thou for this people, neither lift up cry nor prayer for them; for I will not hear them in the time that they cry unto Me for their trouble.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> What hath My beloved to do in My house, seeing she hath wrought lewdness with many, and the hallowed flesh is passed from thee? When thou doest evil, then thou rejoicest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 52:8"> The LORD called thy name a leafy olive-tree, fair with goodly fruit; with the noise of a great tumult He hath kindled fire upon it, and the branches of it are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> For the LORD of hosts, that planted thee, hath pronounced evil against thee, because of the evil of the house of Israel and of the house of Judah, which they have wrought for themselves in provoking Me by offering unto Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> And the LORD gave me knowledge of it, and I knew it; then Thou showedst me their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 83:4"> But I was like a docile lamb that is led to the slaughter; and I knew not that they had devised devices against me: 'Let us destroy the tree with the fruit thereof, and let us cut him off from the land of the living, that his name may be no more remembered.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> But, O LORD of hosts, that judgest righteously, that triest the reins and the heart, let me see Thy vengeance on them; for unto Thee have I revealed my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> Therefore thus saith the LORD concerning the men of Anathoth, that seek thy life, saying: 'Thou shalt not prophesy in the name of the LORD, that thou die not by our hand';</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> therefore thus saith the LORD of hosts: Behold, I will punish them; the young men shall die by the sword, their sons and their daughters shall die by famine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> And there shall be no remnant unto them; for I will bring evil upon the men of Anathoth, even the year of their visitation.</span>
