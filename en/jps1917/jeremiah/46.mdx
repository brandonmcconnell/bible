<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> The word of the LORD which came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> Of Egypt: concerning the army of Pharaoh-neco king of Egypt, which was by the river Euphrates in Carchemish, which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon smote in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> Make ready buckler and shield, and draw near to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> Harness the horses, and mount, ye horsemen, and stand forth with your helmets; furbish the spears, put on the coats of mail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> Wherefore do I see them dismayed and turned backward? and their mighty ones are beaten down, and they are fled apace, and look not back; terror is on every side, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> The swift cannot flee away, nor the mighty man escape; in the north by the river Euphrates have they stumbled and fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> Who is this like the Nile that riseth up, like the rivers whose waters toss themselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> Egypt is like the Nile that riseth up, and like the rivers whose waters toss themselves; and he saith: 'I will rise up, I will cover the earth, I will destroy the city and the inhabitants thereof.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> Prance, ye horses, and rush madly, ye chariots; and let the mighty men go forth: Cush and Put, that handle the shield, and the Ludim, that handle and bend the bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> For the Lord GOD of hosts shall have on that day a day of vengeance, that He may avenge Him of His adversaries; and the sword shall devour and be satiate, and shall be made drunk with their blood; for the Lord GOD of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> Go up into Gilead, and take balm, O virgin daughter of Egypt; in vain dost thou use many medicines; there is no cure for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> The nations have heard of thy shame, and the earth is full of thy cry; for the mighty man hath stumbled against the mighty, they are fallen both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> The word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet, how that Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon should come and smite the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> Declare ye in Egypt, and announce in Migdol, and announce in Noph and in Tahpanhes; say ye: 'Stand forth, and prepare thee, for the sword hath devoured round about thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> Why is thy strong one overthrown? He stood not, because the LORD did thrust him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> He made many to stumble; yea, they fell one upon another, and said: 'Arise, and let us return to our own people, and to the land of our birth, from the oppressing sword.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> They cried there: 'Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise; he hath let the appointed time pass by.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> As I live, saith the King, whose name is the LORD of hosts, surely like Tabor among the mountains, and like Carmel by the sea, so shall he come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> O thou daughter that dwellest in Egypt, furnish thyself to go into captivity; for Noph shall become a desolation, and shall be laid waste, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> Egypt is a very fair heifer; but the gadfly out of the north is come, it is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> Also her mercenaries in the midst of her are like calves of the stall, for they also are turned back, they are fled away together, they did not stand; for the day of their calamity is come upon them, the time of their visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> The sound thereof shall go like the serpent's; for they march with an army, and come against her with axes, as hewers of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> They cut down her forest, saith the LORD, though it cannot be searched; because they are more than the locusts, and are innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> The daughter of Egypt is put to shame; she is delivered into the hand of the people of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> The LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, saith: Behold, I will punish Amon of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, with her gods, and her kings; even Pharaoh, and them that trust in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> and I will deliver them into the hand of those that seek their lives, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of his servants; and afterwards it shall be inhabited, as in the days of old, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> But fear not thou, O Jacob My servant, neither be dismayed, O Israel; for, lo, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall again be quiet and at ease, and none shall make him afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> Fear not thou, O Jacob My servant, saith the LORD, for I am with thee; for I will make a full end of all the nations whither I have driven thee, but I will not make a full end of thee; and I will correct thee in measure, but will not utterly destroy thee.</span>
