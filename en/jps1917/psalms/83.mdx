<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Psalms 35:22; Psalms 50:3"> (83-1) A Song, a Psalm of Asaph. (83-2) O God, keep not Thou silence; hold not Thy peace, and be not still, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> (83-3) For, lo, Thine enemies are in an uproar; and they that hate Thee have lifted up the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:2; Psalms 27:5; Psalms 31:20"> (83-4) They hold crafty converse against Thy people, and take counsel against Thy treasured ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Psalms 74:8"> (83-5) They have said: 'Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> (83-6) For they have consulted together with one consent; against Thee do they make a covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; Psalms 137:7"> (83-7) The tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites; Moab, and the Hagrites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> (83-8) Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> (83-9) Assyria also is joined with them; they have been an arm to the children of Lot. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> (83-10) Do Thou unto them as unto Midian; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook Kishon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> (83-11) Who were destroyed at En-dor; they became as dung for the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> (83-12) Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, and like Zebah and Zalmunna all their princes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> (83-13) Who said: 'Let us take to ourselves in possession the habitations of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> (83-14) O my God, make them like the whirling dust; as stubble before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> (83-15) As the fire that burneth the forest, and as the flame that setteth the mountains ablaze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 58:9"> (83-16) So pursue them with Thy tempest, and affright them with Thy storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 9:19–20; Psalms 6:10; Psalms 109:29"> (83-17) Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek Thy name, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14–15; Psalms 35:26"> (83-18) Let them be ashamed and affrighted for ever; yea, let them be abashed and perish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Psalms 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> (83-19) That they may know that it is Thou alone whose name is the LORD, the Most High over all the earth.</span>
