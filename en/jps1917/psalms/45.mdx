<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> (45-1) For the Leader; upon Shoshannim; a Psalm of the sons of Korah. Maschil. A Song of loves. (45-2) My heart overfloweth with a goodly matter; I say: 'My work is concerning a king'; my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> (45-3) Thou art fairer than the children of men; grace is poured upon thy lips; therefore God hath blessed thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> (45-4) Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O mighty one, thy glory and thy majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> (45-5) And in thy majesty prosper, ride on, in behalf of truth and meekness and righteousness; and let thy right hand teach thee tremendous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Numbers 24:8; Psalms 38:2"> (45-6) Thine arrows are sharp — the peoples fall under thee — they sink into the heart of the king's enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalms 93:2; John 1:1"> (45-7) Thy throne given of God is for ever and ever; a sceptre of equity is the sceptre of thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalms 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> (45-8) Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated wickedness; therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> (45-9) Myrrh, and aloes, and cassia are all thy garments; out of ivory palaces stringed instruments have made thee glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> (45-10) Kings' daughters are among thy favourites; at thy right hand doth stand the queen in gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> (45-11) 'Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear; forget also thine own people, and thy father's house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Solomon 4:1–5"> (45-12) So shall the king desire thy beauty; for he is thy lord; and do homage unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> (45-13) And, O daughter of Tyre, the richest of the people shall entreat thy favour with a gift.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> (45-14) All glorious is the king's daughter within the palace; her raiment is of chequer work inwrought with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Solomon 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> (45-15) She shall be led unto the king on richly woven stuff; the virgins her companions in her train being brought unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> (45-16) They shall be led with gladness and rejoicing; they shall enter into the king's palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> (45-17) Instead of thy fathers shall be thy sons, whom thou shalt make princes in all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; Psalms 72:17–19"> (45-18) I will make thy name to be remembered in all generations; therefore shall the peoples praise thee for ever and ever.</span>
