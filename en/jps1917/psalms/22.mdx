<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> (22-1) For the Leader; upon Aijeleth ha-Shahar. A Psalm of David. (22-2) My God, my God, why hast Thou forsaken me, and art far from my help at the words of my cry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> (22-3) O my God, I call by day, but Thou answerest not; and at night, and there is no surcease for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> (22-4) Yet Thou art holy, O Thou that art enthroned upon the praises of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> (22-5) In Thee did our fathers trust; they trusted, and Thou didst deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> (22-6) Unto Thee they cried, and escaped; in Thee did they trust, and were not ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> (22-7) But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> (22-8) All they that see me laugh me to scorn; they shoot out the lip, they shake the head:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> (22-9) 'Let him commit himself unto the LORD! let Him rescue him; let Him deliver him, seeing He delighteth in him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> (22-10) For Thou art He that took me out of the womb; Thou madest me trust when I was upon my mother's breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> (22-11) Upon Thee I have been cast from my birth; Thou art my God from my mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> (22-12) Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> (22-13) Many bulls have encompassed me; strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> (22-14) They open wide their mouth against me, as a ravening and a roaring lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> (22-15) I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint; my heart is become like wax; it is melted in mine inmost parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> (22-16) My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my throat; and Thou layest me in the dust of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> (22-17) For dogs have encompassed me; a company of evil-doers have inclosed me; like a lion, they are at my hands and my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> (22-18) I may count all my bones; they look and gloat over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> (22-19) They part my garments among them, and for my vesture do they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> (22-20) But Thou, O LORD, be not far off; O Thou my strength, hasten to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> (22-21) Deliver my soul from the sword; mine only one from the power of the dog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> (22-22) Save me from the lion's mouth; yea, from the horns of the wild-oxen do Thou answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> (22-23) I will declare Thy name unto my brethren; in the midst of the congregation will I praise Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> (22-24) 'Ye that fear the LORD, praise Him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify Him; and stand in awe of Him, all ye the seed of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> (22-25) For He hath not despised nor abhorred the lowliness of the poor; neither hath He hid His face from him; but when he cried unto Him, He heard.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> (22-26) From Thee cometh my praise in the great congregation; I will pay my vows before them that fear Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> (22-27) Let the humble eat and be satisfied; let them praise the LORD that seek after Him; may your heart be quickened for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> (22-28) All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn unto the LORD; and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> (22-29) For the kingdom is the LORD'S; and He is the ruler over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> (22-30) All the fat ones of the earth shall eat and worship; all they that go down to the dust shall kneel before Him, even he that cannot keep his soul alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> (22-31) A seed shall serve him; it shall be told of the Lord unto the next generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> (22-32) They shall come and shall declare His righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that He hath done it.</span>
