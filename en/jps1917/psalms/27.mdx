<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> A Psalm of David. The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 9:3"> When evil-doers came upon me to eat up my flesh, even mine adversaries and my foes, they stumbled and fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> Though a host should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war should rise up against me, even then will I be confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4"> One thing have I asked of the LORD, that will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the graciousness of the LORD, and to visit early in His temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> For He concealeth me in His pavilion in the day of evil; He hideth me in the covert of His tent; He lifteth me up upon a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 107:22"> And now shall my head be lifted up above mine enemies round about me; and I will offer in His tabernacle sacrifices with trumpet-sound; I will sing, yea, I will sing praises unto the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 130:2–4; Psalms 13:3; Psalms 5:2"> Hear, O LORD, when I call with my voice, and be gracious unto me, and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> In Thy behalf my heart hath said: 'Seek ye My face'; Thy face, LORD, will I seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> Hide not Thy face from me; put not Thy servant away in anger; Thou hast been my help; cast me not off, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> For though my father and my mother have forsaken me, the LORD will take me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8; Psalms 86:11"> Teach me Thy way, O LORD; and lead me in an even path, because of them that lie in wait for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> Deliver me not over unto the will of mine adversaries; for false witnesses are risen up against me, and such as breathe out violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> If I had not believed to look upon the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living! —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> Wait on the LORD; be strong, and let thy heart take courage; yea, wait thou for the LORD.</span>
