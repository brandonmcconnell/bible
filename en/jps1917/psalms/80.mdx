<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Psalms 50:2; Psalms 77:20"> (80-1) For the Leader; upon Shoshannim. A testimony. A Psalm of Asaph. (80-2) Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, Thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; Thou that art enthroned upon the cherubim, shine forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Numbers 2:18–24; Psalms 44:23–26"> (80-3) Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh, stir up Thy might, and come to save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 5:21; Numbers 6:25–26; Psalms 85:4"> (80-4) O God, restore us; and cause Thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 85:5; Isaiah 58:2–3; Psalms 59:5"> (80-5) O LORD God of hosts, how long wilt Thou be angry against the prayer of Thy people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Isaiah 30:20; Psalms 102:9"> (80-6) Thou hast fed them with the bread of tears, and given them tears to drink in large measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Isaiah 36:8; Isaiah 37:23"> (80-7) Thou makest us a strife unto our neighbours; and our enemies mock as they please.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Isaiah 64:5; Jeremiah 4:14"> (80-8) O God of hosts, restore us; and cause Thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Psalms 44:2; Ezekiel 17:6"> (80-9) Thou didst pluck up a vine out of Egypt; Thou didst drive out the nations, and didst plant it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Isaiah 27:6; 1 Kings 4:20"> (80-10) Thou didst clear a place before it, and it took deep root, and filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:16"> (80-11) The mountains were covered with the shadow of it, and the mighty cedars with the boughs thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 72:8; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 1 Kings 4:24"> (80-12) She sent out her branches unto the sea, and her shoots unto the River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:5; Psalms 89:40–41; Isaiah 18:5–6"> (80-13) Why hast Thou broken down her fences, so that all they that pass by the way do pluck her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> (80-14) The boar out of the wood doth ravage it, that which moveth in the field feedeth on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Psalms 90:13; Daniel 9:16–19"> (80-15) O God of hosts, return, we beseech Thee; look from heaven, and behold, and be mindful of this vine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalms 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> (80-16) And of the stock which Thy right hand hath planted, and the branch that Thou madest strong for Thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> (80-17) It is burned with fire, it is cut down; they perish at the rebuke of Thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 89:21; Psalms 110:1"> (80-18) Let Thy hand be upon the man of Thy right hand, upon the son of man whom Thou madest strong for Thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:107; Psalms 119:25; Psalms 119:40"> (80-19) So shall we not turn back from Thee; quicken Thou us, and we will call upon Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22–23; Psalms 80:3; Psalms 27:4"> (80-20) O LORD God of hosts, restore us; cause Thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.</span>
