<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 145:11–12; Romans 10:13"> O give thanks unto the LORD, call upon His name; make known His doings among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:27; Psalms 98:5; Isaiah 12:5–6"> Sing unto Him, sing praises unto Him; speak ye of all His marvellous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:29"> Glory ye in His holy name; let the heart of them rejoice that seek the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Zephaniah 2:2–3; 2 Chronicles 6:41"> Seek ye the LORD and His strength; seek His face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:11; Psalms 119:13; Deuteronomy 32:7"> Remember His marvellous works that He hath done, His wonders, and the judgments of His mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; John 15:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> O ye seed of Abraham His servant, ye children of Jacob, His chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 26:17–18; Exodus 20:2"> He is the LORD our God; His judgments are in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 106:45; Psalms 111:5"> He hath remembered His covenant for ever, the word which He commanded to a thousand generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:2; Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 26:3"> The covenant which He made with Abraham, and His oath unto Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; 2 Samuel 23:5; Genesis 28:13–15"> And He established it unto Jacob for a statute, to Israel for an everlasting covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 15:18; Psalms 78:55"> Saying: 'Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Hebrews 11:9"> When they were but a few men in number. Yea, very few, and sojourners in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when they went about from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:5; Exodus 7:16–17; Genesis 26:14–33"> He suffered no man to do them wrong, yea, for their sake He reproved kings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 26:11; Genesis 20:7"> 'Touch not Mine anointed ones, and do My prophets no harm.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Genesis 41:54; Isaiah 3:1"> And He called a famine upon the land; He broke the whole staff of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 45:4–5"> He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold for a servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 16:24; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:15"> His feet they hurt with fetters, his person was laid in iron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> Until the time that his word came to pass, the word of the LORD tested him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> The king sent and loosed him; even the ruler of the peoples, and set him free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:40–44; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 41:55"> He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> To bind his princes at his pleasure, and teach his elders wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 7:11–15; Acts 13:17; Psalms 78:51"> Israel also came into Egypt; and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7–9; Acts 7:17"> And He increased His people greatly, and made them too mighty for their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 9:16"> He turned their heart to hate His people, to deal craftily with His servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 29:5–46; Exodus 28:12"> He sent Moses His servant, and Aaron whom He had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:43–51; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Psalms 105:23"> They wrought among them His manifold signs, and wonders in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:2"> He sent darkness, and it was dark; and they rebelled not against His word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 7:20–21; Psalms 78:44; Isaiah 50:2"> He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:13–14; Exodus 8:3–14; Psalms 78:45"> Their land swarmed with frogs, in the chambers of their kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 8:16–18; Exodus 8:21–24; Isaiah 7:18"> He spoke, and there came swarms of flies, and gnats in all their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> He gave them hail for rain, and flaming fire in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> He smote their vines also and their fig-trees; and broke the trees of their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> He spoke, and the locust came, and the canker-worm without number,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And did eat up every herb in their land, and did eat up the fruit of their ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalms 78:51"> He smote also all the first-born in their land, the first-fruits of all their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> And He brought them forth with silver and gold; and there was none that stumbled among His tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> Egypt was glad when they departed; for the fear of them had fallen upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 78:14; Isaiah 4:5"> He spread a cloud for a screen; and fire to give light in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> They asked, and He brought quails, and gave them in plenty the bread of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> He opened the rock, and waters gushed out; they ran, a river in the dry places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> For He remembered His holy word unto Abraham His servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremiah 31:11–12"> And He brought forth His people with joy, His chosen ones with singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalms 78:55"> And He gave them the lands of the nations, and they took the labour of the peoples in possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Titus 2:14; Ephesians 2:8–10"> That they might keep His statutes, and observe His laws. Hallelujah.</span>
