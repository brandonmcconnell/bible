<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalms 18:48; Psalms 57:1"> (59-1) For the Leader; Al-tashheth. A Psalm of David; Michtam; when Saul sent, and they watched the house to kill him. (59-2) Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God; set me on high from them that rise up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:19; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 55:23"> (59-3) Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from the men of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> (59-4) For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul; the impudent gather themselves together against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> (59-5) Without my fault, they run and prepare themselves; awake Thou to help me, and behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 84:8; Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:15"> (59-6) Thou therefore, O LORD God of hosts, the God of Israel, arouse Thyself to punish all the nations; show no mercy to any iniquitous traitors. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> (59-7) They return at evening, they howl like a dog, and go round about the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> (59-8) Behold, they belch out with their mouth; swords are in their lips: 'For who doth hear?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> (59-9) But Thou, O LORD, shalt laugh at them; Thou shalt have all the nations in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> (59-10) Because of his strength, I will wait for Thee; for God is my high tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> (59-11) The God of my mercy will come to meet me; God will let me gaze upon mine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> (59-12) Slay them not, lest my people forget, make them wander to and fro by Thy power, and bring them down, O Lord our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> (59-13) For the sin of their mouth, and the words of their lips, let them even be taken in their pride, and for cursing and lying which they speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> (59-14) Consume them in wrath, consume them, that they be no more; and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob, unto the ends of the earth. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 59:6"> (59-15) And they return at evening, they howl like a dog, and go round about the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalms 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> (59-16) They wander up and down to devour, and tarry all night if they have not their fill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> (59-17) But as for me, I will sing of Thy strength; yea, I will sing aloud of Thy mercy in the morning; for Thou hast been my high tower, and a refuge in the day of my distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 59:9–10; Psalms 46:1; Psalms 18:1"> (59-18) O my strength, unto Thee will I sing praises; for God is my high tower, the God of my mercy.</span>
