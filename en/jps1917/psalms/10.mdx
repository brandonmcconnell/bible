<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Psalms 27:9; Psalms 13:1–3"> Why standest Thou afar off, O LORD? Why hidest Thou Thyself in times of trouble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 59:12; Psalms 36:11; Psalms 7:16"> Through the pride of the wicked the poor is hotly pursued, they are taken in the devices that they have imagined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> For the wicked boasteth of his heart's desire, and the covetous vaunteth himself, though he contemn the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> The wicked, in the pride of his countenance, saith: 'He will not require'; all his thoughts are: 'There is no God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 12:5"> His ways prosper at all times; Thy judgments are far above out of his sight; as for all his adversaries, he puffeth at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> He saith in his heart: 'I shall not be moved, I who to all generations shall not be in adversity.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 140:3"> His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and oppression; under his tongue is mischief and iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> He sitteth in the lurking-places of the villages; in secret places doth he slay the innocent; his eyes are on the watch for the helpless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 17:12; Psalms 59:3; Micah 7:2"> He lieth in wait in a secret place as a lion in his lair, he lieth in wait to catch the poor; he doth catch the poor, when he draweth him up in his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> He croucheth, he boweth down, and the helpless fall into his mighty claws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 94:7; Psalms 73:11"> He hath said in his heart: 'God hath forgotten; He hideth His face; He will never see.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalms 9:12; Psalms 94:2"> Arise, O LORD; O God, lift up Thy hand; forget not the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalms 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God, and say in his heart: 'Thou wilt not require'?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 146:9; Psalms 55:22"> Thou hast seen; for Thou beholdest trouble and vexation, to requite them with Thy hand; unto Thee the helpless committeth himself; Thou hast been the helper of the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 37:17; Job 38:15"> Break Thou the arm of the wicked; and as for the evil man, search out his wickedness, till none be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> The LORD is King for ever and ever; the nations are perished out of His land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalms 34:15"> LORD, Thou hast heard the desire of the humble: Thou wilt direct their heart, Thou wilt cause Thine ear to attend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> To right the fatherless and the oppressed, that man who is of the earth may be terrible no more.</span>
