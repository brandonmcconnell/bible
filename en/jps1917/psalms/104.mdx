<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> Bless the LORD, O my soul. O LORD my God, Thou art very great; Thou art clothed with glory and majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> Who coverest Thyself with light as with a garment, who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> Who layest the beams of Thine upper chambers in the waters, who makest the clouds Thy chariot, who walkest upon the wings of the wind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> Who makest winds Thy messengers, the flaming fire Thy ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> Who didst establish the earth upon its foundations, that it should not be moved for ever and ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> Thou didst cover it with the deep as with a vesture; the waters stood above the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> At Thy rebuke they fled, at the voice of Thy thunder they hasted away —</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> The mountains rose, the valleys sank down — unto the place which Thou hadst founded for them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> Thou didst set a bound which they should not pass over, that they might not return to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> Who sendest forth springs into the valleys; they run between the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> They give drink to every beast of the field, the wild asses quench their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> Beside them dwell the fowl of the heaven, from among the branches they sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> Who waterest the mountains from Thine upper chambers; the earth is full of the fruit of Thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> Who causeth the grass to spring up for the cattle, and herb for the service of man; to bring forth bread out of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, making the face brighter than oil, and bread that stayeth man's heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> The trees of the LORD have their fill, the cedars of Lebanon, which He hath planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> Wherein the birds make their nests; as for the stork, the fir-trees are her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high mountains are for the wild goats; the rocks are a refuge for the conies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> Who appointedst the moon for seasons; the sun knoweth his going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> Thou makest darkness, and it is night, wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their food from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> The sun ariseth, they slink away, and couch in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> How manifold are Thy works, O LORD! In wisdom hast Thou made them all; the earth is full of Thy creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> Yonder sea, great and wide, therein are creeping things innumerable, living creatures, both small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> There go the ships; there is leviathan, whom Thou hast formed to sport therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> All of them wait for Thee, that Thou mayest give them their food in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> Thou givest it unto them, they gather it; Thou openest Thy hand, they are satisfied with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou hidest Thy face, they vanish; Thou withdrawest their breath, they perish, and return to their dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Thou sendest forth Thy spirit, they are created; and Thou renewest the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> May the glory of the LORD endure for ever; let the LORD rejoice in His works!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> Who looketh on the earth, and it trembleth; He toucheth the mountains, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> I will sing unto the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praise to my God while I have any being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> Let my musing be sweet unto Him; as for me, I will rejoice in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> Let sinners cease out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless the LORD, O my soul. Hallelujah.</span>
