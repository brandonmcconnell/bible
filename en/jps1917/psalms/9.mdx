<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 86:12; 1 Chronicles 16:24; Psalms 145:1–3"> (9-1) For the Leader; upon Muthlabben. A Psalm of David. (9-2) I will give thanks unto the LORD with my whole heart; I will tell of all Thy marvellous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 5:11; Psalms 7:17"> (9-3) I will be glad and exult in Thee; I will sing praise to Thy name, O Most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:9; Revelation 6:12–17; Psalms 80:16"> (9-4) When mine enemies are turned back, they stumble and perish at Thy presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; 1 Peter 2:23; Psalms 98:9"> (9-5) For Thou hast maintained my right and my cause; Thou sattest upon the throne as the righteous Judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Deuteronomy 9:14; Proverbs 13:9"> (9-6) Thou hast rebuked the nations, Thou hast destroyed the wicked, Thou hast blotted out their name for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:22–23; Exodus 14:13; Micah 7:8"> (9-7) O thou enemy, the waste places are come to an end for ever; and the cities which thou didst uproot, their very memorial is perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:19; Hebrews 13:8; Psalms 102:12"> (9-8) But the LORD is enthroned for ever; He hath established His throne for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Psalms 98:9; Revelation 20:12–13"> (9-9) And He will judge the world in righteousness, He will minister judgment to the peoples with equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 91:1–2; Nahum 1:7"> (9-10) The LORD also will be a high tower for the oppressed, a high tower in times of trouble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:14; Proverbs 18:10; Isaiah 26:3–4"> (9-11) And they that know Thy name will put their trust in Thee; for thou, LORD, hast not forsaken them that seek Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:22; Psalms 96:1–2; Psalms 148:1–5"> (9-12) Sing praises to the LORD, who dwelleth in Zion; declare among the peoples His doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Exodus 3:7; Revelation 16:6"> (9-13) For He that avengeth blood hath remembered them; He hath not forgotten the cry of the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 30:3; Psalms 107:18"> (9-14) Be gracious unto me, O LORD, behold mine affliction at the hands of them that hate me; Thou that liftest me up from the gates of death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 51:12; Psalms 106:2"> (9-15) That I may tell of all Thy praise in the gates of the daughter of Zion, that I may rejoice in Thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 35:8"> (9-16) The nations are sunk down in the pit that they made; in the net which they hid is their own foot taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Judges 1:7; Proverbs 12:13"> (9-17) The LORD hath made Himself known, He hath executed judgment, the wicked is snared in the work of his own hands. Higgaion. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Job 8:13; Romans 2:8–9"> (9-18) The wicked shall return to the nether-world, even all the nations that forget God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Proverbs 23:18; Proverbs 24:14"> (9-19) For the needy shall not alway be forgotten, nor the expectation of the poor perish for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> (9-20) Arise, O LORD, let not man prevail; let the nations be judged in Thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Psalms 82:6–7; Isaiah 31:3"> (9-21) Set terror over them, O LORD; let the nations know they are but men. Selah</span>
