<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1; Deuteronomy 16:18–19; Psalms 72:1–4"> (58-1) For the Leader; Al-tashheth. A Psalm of David; Michtam. (58-2) Do ye indeed speak as a righteous company? Do ye judge with equity the sons of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; Ezekiel 22:27"> (58-3) Yea, in heart ye work wickedness; ye weigh out in the earth the violence of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 51:5; Isaiah 48:8; Proverbs 22:15"> (58-4) The wicked are estranged from the womb; the speakers of lies go astray as soon as they are born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> (58-5) Their venom is like the venom of a serpent; they are like the deaf asp that stoppeth her ear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> (58-6) Which hearkeneth not to the voice of charmers, or of the most cunning binder of spells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Hosea 5:14"> (58-7) Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth; break out the cheek-teeth of the young lions, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> (58-8) Let them melt away as water that runneth apace; when he aimeth his arrows, let them be as though they were cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> (58-9) Let them be as a snail which melteth and passeth away; like the untimely births of a woman, that have not seen the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> (58-10) Before your pots can feel the thorns, He will sweep it away with a whirlwind, the raw and the burning alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> (58-11) The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance; he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> (58-12) And men shall say: 'Verily there is a reward for the righteous; verily there is a God that judgeth in the earth.'</span>
