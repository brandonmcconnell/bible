<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> (64-1) For the Leader. A Psalm of David. (64-2) Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint; preserve my life from the terror of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> (64-3) Hide me from the council of evil-doers; from the tumult of the workers of iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> (64-4) Who have whet their tongue like a sword, and have aimed their arrow, a poisoned word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> (64-5) That they may shoot in secret places at the blameless; suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> (64-6) They encourage one another in an evil matter; they converse of laying snares secretly; they ask, who would see them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> (64-7) They search out iniquities, they have accomplished a diligent search; even in the inward thought of every one, and the deep heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> (64-8) But God doth shoot at them with an arrow suddenly; thence are their wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> (64-9) So they make their own tongue a stumbling unto themselves; all that see them shake the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> (64-10) And all men fear; and they declare the work of God, and understand His doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> (64-11) The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall take refuge in Him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.</span>
