<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Psalms 138:2; Jeremiah 10:6"> (75-1) For the Leader; Al-tashheth. A Psalm of Asaph, a Song. (75-2) We give thanks unto Thee, O God, we give thanks, and Thy name is near; men tell of Thy wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Acts 17:31; Acts 1:7"> (75-3) 'When I take the appointed time, I Myself will judge with equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Isaiah 24:19; Hebrews 1:3"> (75-4) When the earth and all the inhabitants thereof are dissolved, I Myself establish the pillars of it.' Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 8:5"> (75-5) I say unto the arrogant: 'Deal not arrogantly'; and to the wicked: 'Lift not up the horn.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ezekiel 2:4; Isaiah 48:4"> (75-6) Lift not up your horn on high; speak not insolence with a haughty neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3"> (75-7) For neither from the east, nor from the west, nor yet from the wilderness, cometh lifting up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Daniel 2:21–22; Psalms 147:6"> (75-8) For God is judge; He putteth down one, and lifteth up another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 21:20; Jeremiah 25:15; Psalms 11:6"> (75-9) For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup, with foaming wine, full of mixture, and He poureth out of the same; surely the dregs thereof, all the wicked of the earth shall drain them, and drink them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:14; Psalms 145:1–2; Psalms 40:10"> (75-10) But as for me, I will declare for ever, I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 92:10; Psalms 89:17; Jeremiah 48:25"> (75-11) All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off; but the horns of the righteous shall be lifted up.</span>
