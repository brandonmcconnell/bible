<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 79:13; Psalms 100:3"> Maschil of Asaph. Why, O God, hast Thou cast us off for ever? Why doth Thine anger smoke against the flock of Thy pasture?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> Remember Thy congregation, which Thou hast gotten of old, which Thou hast redeemed to be the tribe of Thine inheritance; and mount Zion, wherein Thou hast dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> Lift up Thy steps because of the perpetual ruins, even all the evil that the enemy hath done in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> Thine adversaries have roared in the midst of Thy meeting-place; they have set up their own signs for signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> It seemed as when men wield upwards axes in a thicket of trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> And now all the carved work thereof together they strike down with hatchet and hammers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> They have set Thy sanctuary on fire; they have profaned the dwelling-place of Thy name even to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> They said in their heart: 'Let us make havoc of them altogether'; they have burned up all the meeting-places of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> We see not our signs; there is no more any prophet; neither is there among us any that knoweth how long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:50–51; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 89:46"> How long, O God, shall the adversary reproach? Shall the enemy blaspheme Thy name for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalms 78:65–66; Psalms 44:23"> Why withdrawest Thou Thy hand, even Thy right hand? Draw it out of Thy bosom and consume them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> Yet God is my King of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalms 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> Thou didst break the sea in pieces by Thy strength; Thou didst shatter the heads of the sea-monsters in the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 104:25–26"> Thou didst crush the heads of leviathan, Thou gavest him to be food to the folk inhabiting the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalms 105:41"> Thou didst cleave fountain and brook; Thou driedst up ever-flowing rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 104:19"> Thine is the day, Thine also the night; Thou hast established luminary and sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> Thou hast set all the borders of the earth; Thou hast made summer and winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> Remember this, how the enemy hath reproached the LORD, and how a base people have blasphemed Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalms 9:18"> O deliver not the soul of Thy turtle-dove unto the wild beast; forget not the life of Thy poor for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> Look upon the covenant; for the dark places of the land are full of the habitations of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 9:18"> O let not the oppressed turn back in confusion; let the poor and needy praise Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 74:18; Psalms 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> Arise, O God, plead Thine own cause; remember Thy reproach all the day at the hand of the base man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalms 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> Forget not the voice of Thine adversaries, the tumult of those that rise up against Thee which ascendeth continually.</span>
