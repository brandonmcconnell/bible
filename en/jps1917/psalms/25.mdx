<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:8; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 24:4"> A Psalm of David. Unto Thee, O LORD, do I lift up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 41:11; Psalms 71:1"> O my God, in Thee have I trusted, let me not be ashamed; let not mine enemies triumph over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Isaiah 40:31; Isaiah 49:23"> Yea, none that wait for Thee shall be ashamed; they shall be ashamed that deal treacherously without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Psalms 27:11; Psalms 143:8"> Show me Thy ways, O LORD; teach me Thy paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Isaiah 30:18; Psalms 25:10"> Guide me in Thy truth, and teach me; for Thou art the God of my salvation; for Thee do I wait all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> Remember, O LORD, Thy compassions and Thy mercies; for they have been from of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions; according to Thy mercy remember Thou me, for Thy goodness' sake, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:5; Psalms 32:8; Ephesians 1:17–18"> Good and upright is the LORD; therefore doth He instruct sinners in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 23:3; Matthew 5:5"> He guideth The humble in justice; and He teacheth the humble His way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 5:11; Romans 8:28; Hebrews 8:8–12"> All the paths of the LORD are mercy and truth unto such as keep His covenant and His testimonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; 1 John 2:12; Isaiah 43:25"> For Thy name's sake, O LORD, pardon mine iniquity, for it is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 32:8; John 7:17"> What man is he that feareth the LORD? him will He instruct in the way that He should choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Proverbs 1:33; Proverbs 19:23"> His soul shall abide in prosperity; and his seed shall inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:15; John 14:21–23; John 7:17"> The counsel of the LORD is with them that fear Him; and His covenant, to make them know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 141:8; Psalms 124:7–8; Psalms 31:4"> Mine eyes are ever toward the LORD; for He will bring forth my feet out of the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 86:16; Daniel 9:17; Micah 7:19"> Turn Thee unto me, and be gracious unto me; for I am solitary and afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:6; Psalms 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> The troubles of my heart are enlarged; O bring Thou me out of my distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Psalms 119:132; Psalms 51:8–9"> See mine affliction and my travail; and forgive all my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 56:2; Psalms 52:2; Psalms 38:19"> Consider how many are mine enemies, and the cruel hatred wherewith they hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 71:1–2; Psalms 121:7; Joel 2:26–27"> O keep my soul, and deliver me; let me not be ashamed, for I have taken refuge in Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Psalms 41:12; Proverbs 20:7"> Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, because I wait for Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 130:8; Psalms 51:18–19; Psalms 14:7"> Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles.</span>
