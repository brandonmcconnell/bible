<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–7; Psalms 10:4; Luke 12:20"> (53-1) For the Leader; upon Mahalath. Maschil of David. (53-2) The fool hath said in his heart: 'There is no God'; they have dealt corruptly, and have done abominable iniquity; there is none that doeth good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 27:8"> (53-3) God looked forth from heaven upon the children of men, to see if there were any man of understanding, that did seek after God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Psalms 14:3"> (53-4) Every one of them is unclean, they are together become impure; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Jeremiah 10:25; Psalms 27:2"> (53-5) 'Shall not the workers of iniquity know it, who eat up My people as they eat bread, and call not upon God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:17"> (53-6) There are they in great fear, where no fear was; for God hath scattered the bones of him that encampeth against thee; Thou hast put them to shame, because God hath rejected them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Jeremiah 30:18; Psalms 126:1–4"> (53-7) Oh that the salvation of Israel were come out of Zion! When God turneth the captivity of His people, let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad.</span>
