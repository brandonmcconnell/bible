<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> A Psalm of Asaph. God, God, the LORD, hath spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun unto the going down thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God cometh, and doth not keep silence; a fire devoureth before Him, and round about Him it stormeth mightily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> He calleth to the heavens above, and to the earth, that He may judge His people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> 'Gather My saints together unto Me; those that have made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> And the heavens declare His righteousness; for God, He is judge. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> 'Hear, O My people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: God, thy God, am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> I will not reprove thee for thy sacrifices; and thy burnt-offerings are continually before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> I will take no bullock out of thy house, nor he-goats out of thy folds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> For every beast of the forest is Mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I know all the fowls of the mountains; and the wild beasts of the field are Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> If I were hungry, I would not tell thee; for the world is Mine, and the fulness thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Offer unto God the sacrifice of thanksgiving; and pay thy vows unto the Most High;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> And call upon Me in the day of trouble; I will deliver thee, and thou shalt honour Me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> But unto the wicked God saith: 'What hast thou to do to declare My statutes, and that thou hast taken My covenant in thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest My words behind thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> When thou sawest a thief, thou hadst company with him, and with adulterers was thy portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> Thou hast let loose thy mouth for evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> These things hast thou done, and should I have kept silence? Thou hadst thought that I was altogether such a one as thyself; but I will reprove thee, and set the cause before thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Now consider this, ye that forget God, lest I tear in pieces, and there be none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> Whoso offereth the sacrifice of thanksgiving honoureth Me; and to him that ordereth his way aright will I show the salvation of God.'</span>
