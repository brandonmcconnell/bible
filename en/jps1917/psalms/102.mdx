<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> (102-1) A Prayer of the afflicted, when he fainteth, and poureth out his complaint before the LORD. (102-2) O LORD, hear my prayer, and let my cry come unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> (102-3) Hide not Thy face from me in the day of my distress; incline Thine ear unto me; in the day when I call answer me speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> (102-4) For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burned as a hearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> (102-5) My heart is smitten like grass, and withered; for I forget to eat my bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> (102-6) By reason of the voice of my sighing my bones cleave to my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> (102-7) I am like a pelican of the wilderness; I am become as an owl of the waste places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> (102-8) I watch, and am become like a sparrow that is alone upon the housetop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> (102-9) Mine enemies taunt me all the day; they that are mad against me do curse by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Micah 1:10"> (102-10) For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> (102-11) Because of Thine indignation and Thy wrath; for Thou hast taken me up, and cast me away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> (102-12) My days are like a lengthening shadow; and I am withered like grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> (102-13) But Thou, O LORD, sittest enthroned for ever; and Thy name is unto all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> (102-14) Thou wilt arise, and have compassion upon Zion; for it is time to be gracious unto her, for the appointed time is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> (102-15) For Thy servants take pleasure in her stones, and love her dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> (102-16) So the nations will fear the name of the LORD, and all the kings of the earth Thy glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> (102-17) When the LORD hath built up Zion, when He hath appeared in His glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> (102-18) When He hath regarded the prayer of the destitute, and hath not despised their prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> (102-19) This shall be written for the generation to come; and a people which shall be created shall praise the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> (102-20) For He hath looked down from the height of His sanctuary; from heaven did the LORD behold the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> (102-21) To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> (102-22) That men may tell of the name of the LORD in Zion, and His praise in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> (102-23) When the peoples are gathered together, and the kingdoms, to serve the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> (102-24) He weakened my strength in the way; He shortened my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalms 102:12"> (102-25) I say: 'O my God, take me not away in the midst of my days, Thou whose years endure throughout all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> (102-26) Of old Thou didst lay the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the work of Thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> (102-27) They shall perish, but Thou shalt endure; yea, all of them shall wax old like a garment; as a vesture shalt Thou change them, and they shall pass away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> (102-28) But Thou art the selfsame, and Thy years shall have no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:35–36; Psalms 45:16–17; Psalms 22:30–31"> (102-29) The children of Thy servants shall dwell securely, and their seed shall be established before Thee.'</span>
