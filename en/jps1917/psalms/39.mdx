<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> (39-1) For the Leader, for Jeduthun. A Psalm of David. (39-2) I said: 'I will take heed to my ways, that I sin not with my tongue; I will keep a curb upon my mouth, while the wicked is before me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> (39-3) I was dumb with silence; I held my peace, had no comfort; and my pain was held in check.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> (39-4) My heart waxed hot within me; while I was musing, the fire kindled; then spoke I with my tongue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> (39-5) 'LORD, make me to know mine end, and the measure of my days, what it is; let me know how short-lived I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> (39-6) Behold, Thou hast made my days as hand-breadths; and mine age is as nothing before Thee; surely every man at his best estate is altogether vanity. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> (39-7) Surely man walketh as a mere semblance; surely for vanity they are in turmoil; he heapeth up riches, and knoweth not who shall gather them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> (39-8) And now, Lord, what wait I for? My hope, it is in Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> (39-9) Deliver me from all my transgressions; make me not the reproach of the base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> (39-10) I am dumb, I open not my mouth; because Thou hast done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> (39-11) Remove Thy stroke from off me; I am consumed by the blow of Thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> (39-12) With rebukes dost Thou chasten man for iniquity, and like a moth Thou makest his beauty to consume away; surely every man is vanity. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> (39-13) Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear unto my cry; keep not silence at my tears; for I am a stranger with Thee, a sojourner, as all my fathers were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> (39-14) Look away from me, that I may take comfort, before I go hence, and be no more.'</span>
