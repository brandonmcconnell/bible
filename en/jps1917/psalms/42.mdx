<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Psalms 84:10; John 7:37"> (42-3) My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God: 'When shall I come and appear before God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 79:10; Psalms 115:2; Joel 2:17"> (42-4) My tears have been my food day and night, while they say unto me all the day: 'Where is Thy God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 30:29; Psalms 122:1; 1 Samuel 1:15–16"> (42-5) These things I remember, and pour out my soul within me, how I passed on with the throng, and led them to the house of God, with the voice of joy and praise, a multitude keeping holyday.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Hebrews 10:36–37; Psalms 61:2"> (42-6) Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why moanest thou within me? Hope thou in God; for I shall yet praise Him for the salvation of His countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Jonah 2:7; Deuteronomy 3:8–9"> (42-7) O my God, my soul is cast down within me; therefore do I remember Thee from the land of Jordan, and the Hermons, from the hill Mizar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Psalms 88:7"> (42-8) Deep calleth unto deep at the voice of Thy cataracts; all Thy waves and Thy billows are gone over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 63:6; Psalms 16:7; Psalms 149:5"> (42-9) By day the LORD will command His lovingkindness, and in the night His song shall be with me, even a prayer unto the God of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2; Psalms 18:2"> (42-10) I will say unto God my Rock: 'Why hast Thou forgotten me? Why go I mourning under the oppression of the enemy?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Joel 2:17; Luke 2:35"> (42-11) As with a crushing in my bones, mine adversaries taunt me; while they say unto me all the day: 'Where is Thy God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 42:5; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 43:5"> (42-12) Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why moanest thou within me? Hope thou in God; for I shall yet praise Him, the salvation of my countenance, and my God.</span>
