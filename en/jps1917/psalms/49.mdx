<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> (49-1) For the Leader; a Psalm of the sons of Korah. (49-2) Hear this, all ye peoples; give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> (49-3) Both low and high, rich and poor together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> (49-4) My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart shall be understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> (49-5) I will incline mine ear to a parable; I will open my dark saying upon the harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> (49-6) Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil, when the iniquity of my supplanters compasseth me about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> (49-7) Of them that trust in their wealth, and boast themselves in the multitude of their riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> (49-8) No man can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him —</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> (49-9) For too costly is the redemption of their soul, and must be let alone for ever —</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> (49-10) That he should still live alway, that he should not see the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> (49-11) For he seeth that wise men die, the fool and the brutish together perish, and leave their wealth to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> (49-12) Their inward thought is, that their houses shall continue for ever, and their dwelling-places to all generations; they call their lands after their own names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> (49-13) But man abideth not in honour; he is like the beasts that perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> (49-14) This is the way of them that are foolish, and of those who after them approve their sayings. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> (49-15) Like sheep they are appointed for the nether-world; death shall be their shepherd; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning; and their form shall be for the nether-world to wear away, that there be no habitation for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> (49-16) But God will redeem my soul from the power of the nether-world; for He shall receive me. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> (49-17) Be not thou afraid when one waxeth rich, when the wealth of his house is increased;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> (49-18) For when he dieth he shall carry nothing away; his wealth shall not descend after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> (49-19) Though while he lived he blessed his soul: 'Men will praise thee, when thou shalt do well to thyself';</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> (49-20) It shall go to the generation of his fathers; they shall never see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> (49-21) Man that is in honour understandeth not; he is like the beasts that perish.</span>
