<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> (30-1) A Psalm; a Song at the Dedication of the House; of David. (30-2) I will extol thee, O LORD, for Thou hast raised me up, and hast not suffered mine enemies to rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> (30-3) O LORD my God, I cried unto Thee, and Thou didst heal me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> (30-4) O LORD, Thou broughtest up my soul from the nether-world; Thou didst keep me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> (30-5) Sing praise unto the LORD, O ye His godly ones, and give thanks to His holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> (30-6) For His anger is but for a moment, His favour is for a life-time; weeping may tarry for the night, but joy cometh in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> (30-7) Now I had said in my security: 'I shall never be moved.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> (30-8) Thou hadst established, O LORD, in Thy favour my mountain as a stronghold — Thou didst hide Thy face; I was affrighted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> (30-9) Unto Thee, O LORD, did I call, and unto the LORD I made supplication:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> (30-10) 'What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to the pit? Shall the dust praise Thee? shall it declare Thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> (30-11) Hear, O LORD, and be gracious unto me; LORD, be Thou my helper.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> (30-12) Thou didst turn for me my mourning into dancing; Thou didst loose my sackcloth, and gird me with gladness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> (30-13) So that my glory may sing praise to Thee, and not be silent; O LORD my God, I will give thanks unto Thee for ever.</span>
