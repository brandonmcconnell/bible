<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> (88-1) A Song, a Psalm of the sons of Korah; for the Leader; upon Mahalath Leannoth. Maschil of Heman the Ezrahite. (88-2) O LORD, God of my salvation, what time I cry in the night before Thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> (88-3) Let my prayer come before Thee, incline Thine ear unto my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> (88-4) For my soul is sated with troubles, and my life draweth nigh unto the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> (88-5) I am counted with them that go down into the pit; I am become as a man that hath no help;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> (88-6) Set apart among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, whom Thou rememberest no more; and they are cut off from Thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> (88-7) Thou hast laid me in the nethermost pit, in dark places, in the deeps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> (88-8) Thy wrath lieth hard upon me, and all Thy waves Thou pressest down. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> (88-9) Thou hast put mine acquaintance far from me; Thou hast made me an abomination unto them; I am shut up, and I cannot come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> (88-10) Mine eye languisheth by reason of affliction; I have called upon Thee, O LORD, every day, I have spread forth my hands unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> (88-11) Wilt Thou work wonders for the dead? Or shall the shades arise and give Thee thanks? Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> (88-12) Shall Thy mercy be declared in the grave? or Thy faithfulness in destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> (88-13) Shall Thy wonders be known in the dark? and Thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> (88-14) But as for me, unto Thee, O LORD, do I cry, and in the morning doth my prayer come to meet Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> (88-15) LORD, why castest Thou off my soul? Why hidest Thou Thy face from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> (88-16) I am afflicted and at the point of death from my youth up; I have borne Thy terrors, I am distracted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> (88-17) Thy fierce wrath is gone over me; Thy terrors have cut me off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> (88-18) They came round about me like water all the day; they compassed me about together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> (88-19) Friend and companion hast Thou put far from me, and mine acquaintance into darkness.</span>
