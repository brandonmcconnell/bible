<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:4; Psalms 69:13–16; Psalms 59:1"> (56-1) For the Leader; upon Jonath-elem-rehokim. A Psalm of David; Michtam; when the Philistines took him in Gath. (56-2) Be gracious unto me, O God, for man would swallow me up; all the day he fighting oppresseth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Psalms 57:3; Psalms 92:8"> (56-3) They that lie in wait for me would swallow me up all the day; for they are many that fight against me, O Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; 2 Corinthians 7:5–6"> (56-4) In the day that I am afraid, I will put my trust in Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Romans 8:31–39"> (56-5) In God — I will praise His word — in God do I trust, I will not be afraid; what can flesh do unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15; Isaiah 29:20–21"> (56-6) All the day they trouble mine affairs; all their thoughts are against me for evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 71:10; Psalms 59:3; Psalms 140:2"> (56-7) They gather themselves together, they hide themselves, they mark my steps; according as they have waited for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Psalms 94:20–21; Isaiah 28:15"> (56-8) Because of iniquity cast them out; in anger bring down the peoples, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 39:12; Psalms 126:5–6"> (56-9) Thou has counted my wanderings; put Thou my tears into Thy bottle; are they not in Thy book?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Psalms 46:11; Psalms 118:11–13"> (56-10) Then shall mine enemies turn back in the day that I call; this I know, that God is for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 1:4; Genesis 32:11"> (56-11) In God — I will praise His word — in the LORD — I will praise His word —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Isaiah 51:12–13; Isaiah 51:7–8"> (56-12) In God do I trust, I will not be afraid; what can man do unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:1–3; 1 Samuel 1:24–28; Psalms 50:14"> (56-13) Thy vows are upon me, O God; I will render thank-offerings unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 116:8–9; Job 33:30; Revelation 21:23–24"> (56-14) For thou hast delivered my soul from death; hast Thou not delivered my feet from stumbling? that I may walk before God in the light of the living.</span>
