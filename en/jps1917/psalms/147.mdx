<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Job 5:18; Jeremiah 33:6"> Who healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 148:3"> He counteth the number of the stars; He giveth them all their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 139:17–18; Psalms 96:4"> Great is our Lord, and mighty in power; His understanding is infinite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 146:8–9; Psalms 25:9; 1 Peter 5:6"> The LORD upholdeth the humble; He bringeth the wicked down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:1–2; Psalms 47:6–7; Psalms 68:32"> Sing unto the LORD with thanksgiving, sing praises upon the harp unto our God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:10; Psalms 65:9–13; Genesis 9:14"> Who covereth the heaven with clouds, who prepareth rain for the earth, who maketh the mountains to spring with grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Job 38:41; Matthew 6:26"> He giveth to the beast his food, and to the young ravens which cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Isaiah 31:1; Proverbs 21:31"> He delighteth not in the strength of the horse; He taketh no pleasure in the legs of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 33:18; Malachi 3:16–17; Zephaniah 3:17"> The LORD taketh pleasure in them that fear Him, in those that wait for His mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Isaiah 52:7; Joel 2:23"> Glorify the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:3–6; Psalms 115:14–15; Isaiah 44:3–5"> For He hath made strong the bars of thy gates; He hath blessed thy children within thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:17–18; Psalms 29:11; Psalms 81:16"> He maketh thy borders peace; He giveth thee in plenty the fat of wheat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 37:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Psalms 107:20"> He sendeth out His commandment upon earth; His word runneth very swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:29; Job 37:6; Psalms 148:8"> He giveth snow like wool; He scattereth the hoar-frost like ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Job 38:22–23; Job 37:9–10"> He casteth forth His ice like crumbs; who can stand before His cold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 147:15; Psalms 33:9; Job 37:10"> He sendeth forth His word, and melteth them; He causeth His wind to blow, and the waters flow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:4; Psalms 78:5; Deuteronomy 33:2–4"> He declareth His word unto Jacob, His statutes and His ordinances unto Israel.</span>
