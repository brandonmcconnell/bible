<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> A Psalm of David. Strive, O LORD, with them that strive with me; fight against them that fight against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise up to my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 27:2; Psalms 51:12"> Draw out also the spear, and the battle-axe, against them that pursue me; say unto my soul: 'I am Thy salvation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be ashamed and brought to confusion that seek after my soul; let them be turned back and be abashed that devise my hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> Let them be as chaff before the wind, the angel of the LORD thrusting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Psalms 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> Let their way be dark and slippery, the angel of the LORD pursuing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 9:15"> For without cause have they hid for me the pit, even their net, without cause have they digged for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> Let destruction come upon him unawares; and let his net that he hath hid catch himself; with destruction let him fall therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Psalms 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> And my soul shall be joyful in the LORD; it shall rejoice in His salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 51:8"> All my bones shall say: 'LORD, who is like unto Thee, who deliverest the poor from him that is too strong for him, yea, the poor and the needy from him that spoileth him?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> Unrighteous witnesses rise up; they ask me of things that I know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; Psalms 38:20"> They repay me evil for good; bereavement is come to my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth, I afflicted my soul with fasting; and my prayer, may it return into mine own bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> I went about as though it had been my friend or my brother; I bowed down mournful, as one that mourneth for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Psalms 7:2; Psalms 35:25–26"> But when I halt they rejoice, and gather themselves together; the abjects gather themselves together against me, and those whom I know not; they tear me, and cease not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; Psalms 37:12"> With the profanest mockeries of backbiting they gnash at me with their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; Psalms 22:20–21; Psalms 57:4"> Lord, how long wilt Thou look on? Rescue my soul from their destructions, mine only one from the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; Psalms 22:22–25; Psalms 116:18"> I will give Thee thanks in the great congregation; I will praise Thee among a numerous people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> Let not them that are wrongfully mine enemies rejoice over me; neither let them wink with the eye that hate me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> For they speak not peace; but they devise deceitful matters against them that are quiet in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:13; Psalms 40:15; Psalms 70:3"> Yea, they open their mouth wide against me; they say: 'Aha, aha, our eye hath seen it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Psalms 10:1; Psalms 28:1"> Thou hast seen, O LORD; keep not silence; O Lord, be not far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Rouse Thee, and awake to my judgment, even unto my cause, my God and my Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> Judge me, O LORD my God, according to Thy righteousness; and let them not rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Let them not say in their heart: 'Aha, we have our desire'; let them not say: 'We have swallowed him up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 38:16; Job 19:5; Psalms 132:18"> Let them be ashamed and abashed together that rejoice at my hurt; let them be clothed with shame and confusion that magnify themselves against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; Psalms 149:4; Psalms 40:16"> Let them shout for joy, and be glad, that delight in my righteousness; yea, let them say continually: 'Magnified be the LORD, who delighteth in the peace of His servant.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:21; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:5"> And my tongue shall speak of Thy righteousness, and of Thy praise all the day.</span>
