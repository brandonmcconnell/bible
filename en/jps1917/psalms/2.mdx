<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:5; Luke 19:14; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> 'Let us break their bands asunder, and cast away their cords from us.'</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Revelation 14:1; Ephesians 1:22"> 'Truly it is I that have established My king upon Zion, My holy mountain.'</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 60:10–11; Isaiah 60:3"> Now therefore, O ye kings, be wise; be admonished, ye judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 12:28–29"> Serve the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 40:4; John 5:23; Psalms 84:12"> Do homage in purity, lest He be angry, and ye perish in the way, when suddenly His wrath is kindled. Happy are all they that take refuge in Him.</span>
