<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:30; Psalms 81:2; Psalms 33:2"> (108-3) Awake, psaltery and harp; I will awake the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 117:1"> (108-4) I will give thanks unto Thee, O LORD, among the peoples; and I will sing praises unto Thee among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Ephesians 2:4–7; Psalms 103:11"> (108-5) For Thy mercy is great above the heavens, and Thy truth reacheth unto the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 57:5; Matthew 6:9–10; Psalms 8:1"> (108-6) Be Thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; and Thy glory be above all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 60:5–12; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22; Colossians 3:12"> (108-7) That Thy beloved may be delivered, save with Thy right hand, and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Joshua 20:7; 2 Peter 1:3–4"> (108-8) God spoke in His holiness, that I would exult; that I would divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Psalms 122:5; 2 Samuel 2:8"> (108-9) Gilead is mine, Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the defence of my head; Judah is my sceptre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29–32; Ruth 4:7–8; 2 Samuel 21:15–22"> (108-10) Moab is my washpot; upon Edom do I cast my shoe; over Philistia do I cry aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3–4; Isaiah 63:1–6; Psalms 20:6–8"> (108-11) Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who will lead me unto Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:9; 2 Chronicles 13:12; 1 Samuel 17:36"> (108-12) Hast not Thou cast us off, O God? and Thou goest not forth, O God, with our hosts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–8; Psalms 146:3–5; Isaiah 31:3"> (108-13) Give us help against the adversary; for vain is the help of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–13; Psalms 60:12; Isaiah 63:3"> (108-14) Through God we shall do valiantly; for He it is that will tread down our adversaries.</span>
