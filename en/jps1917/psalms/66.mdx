<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 98:4"> For the Leader. A Song, a Psalm. Shout unto God, all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> Sing praises unto the glory of His name; make His praise glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> Say unto God: 'How tremendous is Thy work! Through the greatness of Thy power shall Thine enemies dwindle away before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> All the earth shall worship Thee, and shall sing praises unto Thee; they shall sing praises to Thy name.' Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> Come, and see the works of God; He is terrible in His doing toward the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> He turned the sea into dry land; they went through the river on foot; there let us rejoice in Him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> Who ruleth by His might for ever; His eyes keep watch upon the nations; let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> Bless our God, ye peoples, and make the voice of His praise to be heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> Who hath set our soul in life, and suffered not our foot to be moved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> For Thou, O God, hast tried us; Thou hast refined us, as silver is refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> Thou didst bring us into the hold; Thou didst lay constraint upon our loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> Thou hast caused men to ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water; but Thou didst bring us out unto abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> I will come into Thy house with burnt-offerings, I will perform unto Thee my vows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> Which my lips have uttered, and my mouth hath spoken, when I was in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> I will offer unto Thee burnt-offerings of fatlings, with the sweet smoke of rams; I will offer bullocks with goats. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> Come, and hearken, all ye that fear God, and I will declare what He hath done for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> I cried unto Him with my mouth, and He was extolled with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> If I had regarded iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not hear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> But verily God hath heard; He hath attended to the voice of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> Blessed be God, who hath not turned away my prayer, nor His mercy from me.</span>
