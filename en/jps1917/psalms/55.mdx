<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 86:6; Psalms 61:1"> (55-1) For the Leader; with string-music. Maschil of David. (55-2) Give ear, O God, to my prayer; and hide not Thyself from my supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Psalms 66:19; Psalms 77:3"> (55-3) Attend unto me, and hear me; I am distraught in my complaint, and will moan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:7–8; Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11"> (55-4) Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked; for they cast mischief upon me, and in anger they persecute me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Mark 14:33–34; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> (55-5) My heart doth writhe within me; and the terrors of death are fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Job 21:6; 2 Samuel 15:14"> (55-6) Fear and trembling come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalms 139:9"> (55-7) And I said: 'Oh that I had wings like a dove! then would I fly away, and be at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; 2 Samuel 17:21–22; Jeremiah 37:12"> (55-8) Lo, then would I wander far off, I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> (55-9) I would haste me to a shelter from the stormy wind and tempest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> (55-10) Destroy, O Lord, and divide their tongue; for I have seen violence and strife in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> (55-11) Day and night they go about it upon the walls thereof; iniquity also and mischief are in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalms 10:7; Psalms 5:9"> (55-12) Wickedness is in the midst thereof; oppression and guile depart not from her broad place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Matthew 26:21–23"> (55-13) For it was not an enemy that taunted me, then I could have borne it; neither was it mine adversary that did magnify himself against me, then I would have hid myself from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> (55-14) But it was thou, a man mine equal, my companion, and my familiar friend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> (55-15) We took sweet counsel together, in the house of God we walked with the throng.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 59:13; Psalms 64:7; Psalms 9:17"> (55-16) May He incite death against them, let them go down alive into the nether-world; for evil is in their dwelling, and within them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 109:4; Psalms 73:28"> (55-17) As for me, I will call upon God; and the LORD shall save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalms 141:2"> (55-18) Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I complain, and moan; and He hath heard my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:3; Psalms 3:6–7; 2 Samuel 22:1"> (55-19) He hath redeemed my soul in peace so that none came nigh me; for they were many that strove with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Revelation 6:10–11; Jeremiah 48:11"> (55-20) God shall hear, and humble them, even He that is enthroned of old, Selah, such as have no changes, and fear not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalms 89:34; Psalms 7:4"> (55-21) He hath put forth his hands against them that were at peace with him; he hath profaned his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 5:3–4"> (55-22) Smoother than cream were the speeches of his mouth, but his heart was war; his words were softer than oil, yet were they keen-edged swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 37:5; Philippians 4:6–7"> (55-23) Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and He will sustain thee; He will never suffer the righteous to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 5:6; Job 15:32"> (55-24) But Thou, O God, wilt bring them down into the nethermost pit; men of blood and deceit shall not live out half their days; but as for me, I will trust in Thee.</span>
