<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Isaiah 38:13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> (7-3) Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it in pieces, while there is none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; 1 Samuel 24:11; 1 Samuel 26:18–19"> (7-4) O LORD my God, if I have done this; if there be iniquity in my hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:20; 1 Samuel 22:14; 1 Samuel 24:17–19"> (7-5) If I have requited him that did evil unto me, or spoiled mine adversary unto emptiness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 17:13"> (7-6) Let the enemy pursue my soul, and overtake it, and tread my life down to the earth; yea, let him lay my glory in the dust. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 35:23; Psalms 3:7"> (7-7) Arise, O LORD, in Thine anger, lift up Thyself in indignation against mine adversaries; yea, awake for me at the judgment which Thou hast commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> (7-8) And let the congregation of the peoples compass Thee about, and over them return Thou on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 43:1"> (7-9) O LORD, who ministerest judgment to the peoples, judge me, O LORD, according to my righteousness, and according to mine integrity that is in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:20; Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 20:12"> (7-10) Oh that a full measure of evil might come upon the wicked, and that Thou wouldest establish the righteous; for the righteous God trieth the heart and reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 125:4; Psalms 84:11; Proverbs 28:18"> (7-11) My shield is with God, who saveth the upright in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:2; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 94:15"> (7-12) God is a righteous judge, yea, a God that hath indignation every day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:10; Deuteronomy 32:41"> (7-13) If a man turn not, He will whet His sword, He hath bent His bow, and made it ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 45:5; Psalms 64:7"> (7-14) He hath also prepared for him the weapons of death, yea, His arrows which He made sharp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Isaiah 33:11"> (7-15) Behold, he travaileth with iniquity; yea, he conceiveth mischief, and bringeth forth falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 4:8; Psalms 141:10; Proverbs 26:27"> (7-16) He hath digged a pit, and hollowed it, and is fallen into the ditch which he made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esther 9:25; Psalms 37:12–13; 1 Kings 2:32"> (7-17) His mischief shall return upon his own head, and his violence shall come down upon his own pate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:2; Psalms 98:2; Psalms 71:15–16"> (7-18) I will give thanks unto the LORD according to His righteousness; and will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.</span>
