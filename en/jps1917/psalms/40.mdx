<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> (40-1) For the Leader. A Psalm of David. (40-2) I waited patiently for the LORD; and He inclined unto me, and heard my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 119:133; Psalms 27:5"> (40-3) He brought me up also out of the tumultuous pit, out of the miry clay; and He set my feet upon a rock, He established my goings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> (40-4) And He hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise unto our God; many shall see, and fear, and shall trust in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> (40-5) Happy is the man that hath made the LORD his trust, and hath not turned unto the arrogant, nor unto such as fall away treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> (40-6) Many things hast Thou done, O LORD my God, even Thy wonderful works, and Thy thoughts toward us; there is none to be compared unto Thee! If I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> (40-7) Sacrifice and meal-offering Thou hast no delight in; mine ears hast Thou opened; burnt-offering and sin-offering hast Thou not required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> (40-8) Then said I: 'Lo, I am come with the roll of a book which is prescribed for me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> (40-9) I delight to do Thy will, O my God; yea, Thy law is in my inmost parts.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> (40-10) I have preached righteousness in the great congregation, lo, I did not refrain my lips; O LORD, Thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> (40-11) I have not hid Thy righteousness within my heart; I have declared Thy faithfulness and Thy salvation; I have not concealed Thy mercy and Thy truth from the great congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> (40-12) Thou, O LORD, wilt not withhold Thy compassions from me; let Thy mercy and Thy truth continually preserve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> (40-13) For innumerable evils have compassed me about, mine iniquities have overtaken me, so that I am not able to look up; they are more than the hairs of my head, and my heart hath failed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> (40-14) Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; O LORD, make haste to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> (40-15) Let them be ashamed and abashed together that seek after my soul to sweep it away; let them be turned backward and brought to confusion that delight in my hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> (40-16) Let them be appalled by reason of their shame that say unto me: 'Aha, aha.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> (40-17) Let all those that seek Thee rejoice and be glad in Thee; let such as love Thy salvation say continually: 'The LORD be magnified.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> (40-18) But, as for me, that am poor and needy, the Lord will account it unto me; Thou art my help and my deliverer; O my God, tarry not.</span>
