<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 4:1; Zechariah 8:3; Psalms 96:4"> (48-1) A Song; a Psalm of the sons of Korah. (48-2) Great is the LORD, and highly to be praised, in the city of our God, His holy mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:2; Lamentations 2:15; Matthew 5:35"> (48-3) Fair in situation, the joy of the whole earth; even mount Zion, the uttermost parts of the north, the city of the great King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 46:7"> (48-4) God in her palaces hath made Himself known for a stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6–19; Revelation 17:12–14; Revelation 20:8–9"> (48-5) For, lo, the kings assembled themselves, they came onward together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 2 Kings 7:6–7; 2 Kings 19:35–37"> (48-6) They saw, straightway they were amazed; they were affrighted, they hasted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 13:6–8; Isaiah 21:3"> (48-7) Trembling took hold of them there, pangs, as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Ezekiel 27:25–26; 1 Kings 22:48"> (48-8) With the east wind Thou breakest the ships of Tarshish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 87:5; Psalms 78:3–6; Psalms 46:5"> (48-9) As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God — God establish it for ever. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> (48-10) We have thought on Thy lovingkindness, O God, in the midst of Thy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; Psalms 113:3"> (48-11) As is Thy name, O God, so is Thy praise unto the ends of the earth; Thy right hand is full of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> (48-12) Let mount Zion be glad, let the daughters of Judah rejoice, because of Thy judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; Matthew 24:1–2"> (48-13) Walk about Zion, and go round about her; count the towers thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 122:7; Deuteronomy 11:19"> (48-14) Mark ye well her ramparts, traverse her palaces; that ye may tell it to the generation following.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 23:3–4; Psalms 73:24"> (48-15) For such is God, our God, for ever and ever; He will guide us eternally.</span>
