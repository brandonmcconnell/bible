<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 32:7; Psalms 27:5; Psalms 36:7"> O thou that dwellest in the covert of the Most High, and abidest in the shadow of the Almighty;</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 124:7; Psalms 141:9; Job 5:10–22"> That He will deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 61:4; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 17:8"> He will cover thee with His pinions, and under His wings shalt thou take refuge; His truth is a shield and a buckler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Job 5:19–27; Proverbs 3:23–25"> Thou shalt not be afraid of the terror by night, nor of the arrow that flieth by day;</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:21; Psalms 121:7; Deuteronomy 7:15"> There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:10–11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:14"> For He will give His angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:24; Proverbs 3:23; Luke 4:11"> They shall bear thee upon their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Daniel 6:22; Mark 16:18"> Thou shalt tread upon the lion and asp; the young lion and the serpent shalt thou trample under feet.</span>
