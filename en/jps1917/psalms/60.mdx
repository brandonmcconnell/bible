<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:3; Psalms 44:9"> (60-1) For the Leader; upon Shushan Eduth; Michtam of David, to teach; (60-2) when he strove with Aram-naharaim and with Aram-zobah, and Joab returned, and smote of Edom in the Valley of Salt twelve thousand. (60-3) O God, Thou hast cast us off, Thou hast broken us down; Thou hast been angry; O restore us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> (60-4) Thou hast made the land to shake, Thou hast cleft it; heal the breaches thereof; for it tottereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:20; Isaiah 51:22; Isaiah 51:17"> (60-5) Thou hast made Thy people to see hard things; Thou hast made us to drink the wine of staggering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 20:5; Isaiah 11:12; Exodus 17:15"> (60-6) Thou hast given a banner to them that fear Thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:6–13; Psalms 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> (60-7) That Thy beloved may be delivered, save with Thy right hand, and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; Psalms 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> (60-8) God spoke in His holiness, that I would exult; that I would divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> (60-9) Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the defence of my head; Judah is my sceptre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; Psalms 108:9–10"> (60-10) Moab is my washpot; upon Edom do I cast my shoe; Philistia, cry aloud because of me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> (60-11) Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who will lead me unto Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalms 60:1"> (60-12) Hast not Thou, O God, cast us off? And Thou goest not forth, O God, with our hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 108:12; Psalms 130:8"> (60-13) Give us help against the adversary; for vain is the help of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 63:3"> (60-14) Through God we shall do valiantly; for He it is that will tread down our adversaries.</span>
