<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> They compassed me about also with words of hatred, and fought against me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> In return for my love they are my adversaries; but I am all prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> And they have laid upon me evil for good, and hatred for my love:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> 'Set Thou a wicked man over him; and let an adversary stand at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> When he is judged, let him go forth condemned; and let his prayer be turned into sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> Let his days be few; let another take his charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> Let his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> Let his children be vagabonds, and beg; and let them seek their bread out of their desolate places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> Let the creditor distrain all that he hath; and let strangers make spoil of his labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> Let there be none to extend kindness unto him; neither let there be any to be gracious unto his fatherless children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> Let his posterity be cut off; in the generation following let their name be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> Let the iniquity of his fathers be brought to remembrance unto the LORD; and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> Let them be before the LORD continually, that He may cut off the memory of them from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> Because that he remembered not to do kindness, but persecuted the poor and needy man, and the broken in heart he was ready to slay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> Yea, he loved cursing, and it came unto him; and he delighted not in blessing, and it is far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> He clothed himself also with cursing as with his raiment, and it is come into his inward parts like water, and like oil into his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> Let it be unto him as the garment which he putteth on, and for the girdle wherewith he is girded continually.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> This would mine adversaries effect from the LORD, and they that speak evil against my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> But Thou, O GOD the Lord, deal with me for Thy name's sake; because Thy mercy is good, deliver Thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> For I am poor and needy, and my heart is wounded within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> I am gone like the shadow when it lengtheneth; I am shaken off as the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> My knees totter through fasting; and my flesh is lean, and hath no fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> I am become also a taunt unto them; when they see me, they shake their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> Help me, O LORD my God; O save me according to Thy mercy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> That they may know that this is Thy hand; that Thou, LORD, hast done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> Let them curse, but bless Thou; when they arise, they shall be put to shame, but Thy servant shall rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> Mine adversaries shall be clothed with confusion, and shall put on their own shame as a robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> I will give great thanks unto the LORD with my mouth; yea, I will praise Him among the multitude;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> Because He standeth at the right hand of the needy, to save him from them that judge his soul.</span>
