<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 71:2; Psalms 31:1; Psalms 140:6"> A Psalm of David. O LORD, hear my prayer, give ear to my supplications; in Thy faithfulness answer me, and in Thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> And enter not into judgment with Thy servant; for in Thy sight shall no man living be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; he hath crushed my life down to the ground; he hath made me to dwell in darkness, as those that have been long dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> And my spirit fainteth within me; my heart within me is appalled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> I remember the days of old; I meditate on all Thy doing; I muse on the work of Thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I spread forth my hands unto Thee; my soul thirsteth after Thee, as a weary land. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> Answer me speedily, O LORD, my spirit faileth; hide not Thy face from me; lest I become like them that go down into the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> Cause me to hear Thy lovingkindness in the morning, for in Thee do I trust; cause me to know the way wherein I should walk, for unto Thee have I lifted up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliver me from mine enemies, O LORD; with Thee have I hidden myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do Thy will, for Thou art my God; let Thy good spirit lead me in an even land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> For Thy name's sake, O LORD, quicken me; in Thy righteousness bring my soul out of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> And in Thy mercy cut off mine enemies, and destroy all them that harass my soul; for I am Thy servant.</span>
