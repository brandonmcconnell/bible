<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> (77-1) For the Leader; for Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph. (77-2) I will lift up my voice unto God, and cry; I will lift up my voice unto God, that He may give ear unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> (77-3) In the day of my trouble I seek the Lord; with my hand uplifted, mine eye streameth in the night without ceasing; my soul refuseth to be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> (77-4) When I think thereon, O God, I must moan; when I muse thereon, my spirit fainteth. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> (77-5) Thou holdest fast the lids of mine eyes; I am troubled, and cannot speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> (77-6) I have pondered the days of old, the years of ancient times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> (77-7) In the night I will call to remembrance my song; I will commune with mine own heart; and my spirit maketh diligent search:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> (77-8) 'Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will He be favourable no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> (77-9) Is His mercy clean gone for ever? Is His promise come to an end for evermore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> (77-10) Hath God forgotten to be gracious? Hath He in anger shut up his compassions?' Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> (77-11) And I say: 'This is my weakness, that the right hand of the Most High could change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> (77-12) I will make mention of the deeds of the LORD; yea, I will remember Thy wonders of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> (77-13) I will meditate also upon all Thy work, and muse on Thy doings.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> (77-14) O God, Thy way is in holiness; who is a great god like unto God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> (77-15) Thou art the God that doest wonders; Thou hast made known Thy strength among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> (77-16) Thou hast with Thine arm redeemed Thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> (77-17) The waters saw Thee, O God; the waters saw Thee, they were in pain; the depths also trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> (77-18) The clouds flooded forth waters; the skies sent out a sound; Thine arrows also went abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> (77-19) The voice of Thy thunder was in the whirlwind; the lightnings lighted up the world; the earth trembled and shook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> (77-20) Thy way was in the sea, and Thy path in the great waters, and Thy footsteps were not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> (77-21) Thou didst lead Thy people like a flock, by the hand of Moses and Aaron.</span>
