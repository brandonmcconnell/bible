<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 84:2; Psalms 42:1–2"> (63-1) A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah. (63-2) O God, Thou art my God, earnestly will I seek Thee; my soul thirsteth for Thee, my flesh longeth for Thee, in a dry and weary land, where no water is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 84:2–11"> (63-3) So have I looked for Thee in the sanctuary, to see Thy power and Thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:16; Psalms 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> (63-4) For Thy lovingkindness is better than life; my lips shall praise Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 134:2; Psalms 28:2"> (63-5) So will I bless Thee as long as I live; in Thy name will I lift up my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 36:7–9; Isaiah 25:6; Ezra 3:11–13"> (63-6) My soul is satisfied as with marrow and fatness; and my mouth doth praise Thee with joyful lips;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Psalms 149:5"> (63-7) When I remember Thee upon my couch, and meditate on Thee in the night-watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalms 61:4; Psalms 27:9"> (63-8) For Thou hast been my help, and in the shadow of Thy wings do I rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 18:35; Psalms 73:23"> (63-9) My soul cleaveth unto Thee; Thy right hand holdeth me fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:17; Psalms 40:14"> (63-10) But those that seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the nethermost parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 39:4"> (63-11) They shall be hurled to the power of the sword; they shall be a portion for foxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalms 21:1; Isaiah 65:16"> (63-12) But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by Him shall glory; for the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.</span>
