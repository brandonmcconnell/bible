<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 28:7"> (21-1) For the Leader. A Psalm of David. (21-2) O LORD, in Thy strength the king rejoiceth; and in Thy salvation how greatly doth he exult!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Psalms 20:4–5; Psalms 2:8–9"> (21-3) Thou hast given him his heart's desire, and the request of his lips Thou hast not withholden. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> (21-4) For Thou meetest him with choicest blessings; Thou settest a crown of fine gold on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalms 119:77"> (21-5) He asked life of Thee, Thou gavest it him; even length of days for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> (21-6) His glory is great through Thy salvation; honour and majesty dost Thou lay upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> (21-7) For Thou makest him most blessed for ever; Thou makest him glad with joy in Thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 91:2; Psalms 20:7–8"> (21-8) For the king trusteth in the LORD, yea, in the mercy of the Most High; he shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 110:1–2"> (21-9) Thy hand shall be equal to all thine enemies; thy right hand shall overtake those that hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> (21-10) Thou shalt make them as a fiery furnace in the time of thine anger; the LORD shall swallow them up in His wrath, and the fire shall devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> (21-11) Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:2; Psalms 2:1; Psalms 31:13"> (21-12) For they intended evil against thee, they imagined a device, wherewith they shall not prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> (21-13) For thou shalt make them turn their back, thou shalt make ready with thy bowstrings against the face of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 19:1–6; Revelation 15:3–4"> (21-14) Be Thou exalted, O LORD, in Thy strength; so will we sing and praise Thy power.</span>
