<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> (69-1) For the Leader; upon Shoshannim. A Psalm of David. (69-2) Save me, O God; for the waters are come in even unto the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> (69-3) I am sunk in deep mire, where there is no standing; I am come into deep waters, and the flood overwhelmeth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> (69-4) I am weary of my crying; my throat is dried; mine eyes fail while I wait for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> (69-5) They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head; they that would cut me off, being mine enemies wrongfully, are many; should I restore that which I took not away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> (69-6) O God, Thou knowest my folly; and my trespasses are not hid from Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> (69-7) Let not them that wait for Thee be ashamed through me, O Lord GOD of hosts; let not those that seek Thee be brought to confusion through me, O God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> (69-8) Because for Thy sake I have borne reproach; confusion hath covered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> (69-9) I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother's children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> (69-10) Because zeal for Thy house hath eaten me up, and the reproaches of them that reproach Thee are fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> (69-11) And I wept with my soul fasting, and that became unto me a reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> (69-12) I made sackcloth also my garment, and I became a byword unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> (69-13) They that sit in the gate talk of me; and I am the song of the drunkards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> (69-14) But as for me, let my prayer be unto Thee, O LORD, in an acceptable time; O God, in the abundance of Thy mercy, answer me with the truth of Thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> (69-15) Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink; let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> (69-16) Let not the waterflood overwhelm me, neither let the deep swallow me up; and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> (69-17) Answer me, O LORD, for Thy mercy is good; according to the multitude of Thy compassions turn Thou unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> (69-18) And hide not Thy face from Thy servant; for I am in distress; answer me speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> (69-19) Draw nigh unto my soul, and redeem it; ransom me because of mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> (69-20) Thou knowest my reproach, and my shame, and my confusion; mine adversaries are all before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> (69-21) Reproach hath broken my heart; and I am sore sick; and I looked for some to show compassion, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> (69-22) Yea, they put poison into my food; and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> (69-23) Let their table before them become a snare; and when they are in peace, let it become a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> (69-24) Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to totter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> (69-25) Pour out Thine indignation upon them, and let the fierceness of Thine anger overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> (69-26) Let their encampment be desolate; let none dwell in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> (69-27) For they persecute him whom Thou hast smitten; and they tell of the pain of those whom Thou hast wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> (69-28) Add iniquity unto their iniquity; and let them not come into Thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> (69-29) Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> (69-30) But I am afflicted and in pain; let Thy salvation, O God, set me up on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> (69-31) I will praise the name of God with a song, and will magnify Him with thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> (69-32) And it shall please the LORD better than a bullock that hath horns and hoofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> (69-33) The humble shall see it, and be glad; ye that seek after God, let your heart revive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> (69-34) For the LORD hearkeneth unto the needy, and despiseth not His prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> (69-35) Let heaven and earth praise Him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> (69-36) For God will save Zion, and build the cities of Judah; and they shall abide there, and have it in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> (69-37) The seed also of His servants shall inherit it; and they that love His name shall dwell therein.</span>
