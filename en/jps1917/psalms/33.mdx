<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Titus 1:2; Deuteronomy 32:4"> For the word of the LORD is upright; and all His work is done in faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Psalms 119:64; Psalms 104:24"> He loveth righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the lovingkindness of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; 2 Peter 3:5; Job 33:4"> By the word of the LORD were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:8; Joshua 3:16; Joshua 3:13"> He gathereth the waters of the sea together as a heap; He layeth up the deeps in storehouses.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10; Job 23:13"> The counsel of the LORD standeth for ever, the thoughts of His heart to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; 1 Peter 2:9; John 15:16"> Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD; the people whom He hath chosen for His own inheritance.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; 1 Kings 8:27; Isaiah 57:15"> From the place of His habitation He looketh intently upon all the inhabitants of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Psalms 44:21; Hosea 7:2"> He that fashioneth the hearts of them all, that considereth all their doings.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10"> A horse is a vain thing for safety; neither doth it afford escape by its great strength.</span>
