<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Be Thou to me a sheltering rock, whereunto I may continually resort, which Thou hast appointed to save me; for Thou art my rock and my fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:1–4; Psalms 59:1–2; 2 Samuel 17:1–2"> O my God, rescue me out of the hand of the wicked, out of the grasp of the unrighteous and ruthless man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7; Psalms 39:7; Psalms 119:81"> For Thou art my hope; O Lord GOD, my trust from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 22:9–10; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 139:15–16"> Upon Thee have I stayed myself from birth; Thou art He that took me out of my mother's womb; my praise is continually of Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> I am as a wonder unto many; but Thou art my strong refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 104:1"> My mouth shall be filled with Thy praise, and with Thy glory all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalms 71:18"> Cast me not off in the time of old age; when my strength faileth, forsake me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For mine enemies speak concerning me, and they that watch for my soul take counsel together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Psalms 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> Saying: 'God hath forsaken him; pursue and take him; for there is none to deliver.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:22; Psalms 38:21–22; Psalms 70:5"> O God, be not far from me; O my God, make haste to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be ashamed and consumed that are adversaries to my soul; let them be covered with reproach and confusion that seek my hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> But as for me, I will hope continually, and will praise Thee yet more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Psalms 35:28; Psalms 71:8"> My mouth shall tell of Thy righteousness, and of Thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalms 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> I will come with Thy mighty acts, O Lord GOD; I will make mention of Thy righteousness, even of Thine only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 71:5; Psalms 119:9; Psalms 119:102"> O God, Thou hast taught me from my youth; and until now do I declare Thy wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Psalms 71:9; Psalms 78:4"> And even unto old age and hoary hairs, O God, forsake me not; until I have declared Thy strength unto the next generation, Thy might to every one that is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> Thy righteousness also, O God, which reacheth unto high heaven; Thou who hast done great things, O God, who is like unto Thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 138:7; Psalms 60:3; Psalms 86:13"> Thou, who hast made me to see many and sore troubles, wilt quicken me again, and bring me up again from the depths of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; 2 Samuel 3:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Thou wilt increase my greatness, and turn and comfort me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; 2 Kings 19:22; Psalms 33:2"> I also will give thanks unto Thee with the psaltery, even unto Thy truth, O my God; I will sing praises unto Thee with the harp, O Thou Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 103:4; Luke 1:46–47"> My lips shall greatly rejoice when I sing praises unto Thee; and my soul, which Thou hast redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 71:13; Psalms 18:37–43; 1 Corinthians 15:25"> My tongue also shall tell of Thy righteousness all the day; for they are ashamed, for they are abashed, that seek my hurt.</span>
