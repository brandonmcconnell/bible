<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:21; 2 Corinthians 9:8–14"> (41-1) For the Leader. A Psalm of David. (41-2) Happy is he that considereth the poor; the LORD will deliver him in the day of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:4–5; Psalms 27:12; 1 Timothy 4:8"> (41-3) The LORD preserve him, and keep him alive, let him be called happy in the land; and deliver not Thou him unto the greed of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5–6; Psalms 73:26; Philippians 2:26–27"> (41-4) The LORD support him upon the bed of illness; mayest Thou turn all his lying down in his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; James 5:15–16; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> (41-5) As for me, I said: 'O LORD, be gracious unto me; heal my soul; for I have sinned against Thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 102:8; Psalms 38:12"> (41-6) Mine enemies speak evil of me: 'When shall he die, and his name perish?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Luke 11:53–54; Proverbs 26:24–26"> (41-7) And if one come to see me, he speaketh falsehood; his heart gathereth iniquity to itself; when he goeth abroad, he speaketh of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 56:5–6; Psalms 31:13"> (41-8) All that hate me whisper together against me, against me do they devise my hurt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:11; Job 2:7–8; Matthew 27:41–46"> (41-9) 'An evil thing cleaveth fast unto him; and now that he lieth, he shall rise up no more.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–14; Matthew 26:23"> (41-10) Yea, mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted, who did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–10; Luke 19:27; Psalms 18:37–42"> (41-11) But Thou, O LORD, be gracious unto me, and raise me up, that I may requite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:15; Psalms 124:6; Psalms 147:11"> (41-12) By this I know that Thou delightest in me, that mine enemy doth not triumph over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 36:7; Psalms 73:23–24; Psalms 37:17"> (41-13) And as for me, Thou upholdest me because of mine integrity, and settest me before Thy face for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Ephesians 1:3; Revelation 4:8"> (41-14) Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting and to everlasting. Amen, and Amen.</span>
