<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> (38-1) A Psalm of David, to make memorial. (38-2) O LORD, rebuke me not in Thine anger; neither chasten me in Thy wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> (38-3) For Thine arrows are gone deep into me, and Thy hand is come down upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> (38-4) There is no soundness in my flesh because of Thine indignation; neither is there any health in my bones because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> (38-5) For mine iniquities are gone over my head; as a heavy burden they are too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> (38-6) My wounds are noisome, they fester, because of my foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> (38-7) I am bent and bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> (38-8) For my loins are filled with burning; and there is no soundness in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> (38-9) I am benumbed and sore crushed; I groan by reason of the moaning of my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> (38-10) Lord, all my desire is before Thee; and my sighing is not hid from Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> (38-11) My heart fluttereth, my strength faileth me; as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> (38-12) My friends and my companions stand aloof from my plague; and my kinsmen stand afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> (38-13) They also that seek after my life lay snares for me; and they that seek my hurt speak crafty devices, and utter deceits all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> (38-14) But I am as a deaf man, I hear not; and I am as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> (38-15) Yea, I am become as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> (38-16) For in Thee, O LORD, do I hope; Thou wilt answer, O Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> (38-17) For I said: 'Lest they rejoice over me; when my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> (38-18) For I am ready to halt, and my pain is continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> (38-19) For I do declare mine iniquity; I am full of care because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> (38-20) But mine enemies are strong in health; and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> (38-21) They also that repay evil for good are adversaries unto me, because I follow the thing that is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> (38-22) Forsake me not, O LORD; O my God, be not far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> (38-23) Make haste to help me, O LORD, my salvation.</span>
