<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> The LORD executeth righteousness, and acts of justice for all that are oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> He made known His ways unto Moses, His doings unto the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> The LORD is full of compassion and gracious, slow to anger, and plenteous in mercy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> Like as a father hath compassion upon his children, so hath the LORD compassion upon them that fear Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> For He knoweth our frame; He remembereth that we are dust.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> To such as keep His covenant, and to those that remember His precepts to do them.</span>
