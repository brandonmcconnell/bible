<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:34; 2 Samuel 22:35; Psalms 18:2"> A Psalm of David. Blessed be the LORD my Rock, who traineth my hands for war, and my fingers for battle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:2–3; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:2"> My lovingkindness, and my fortress, my high tower, and my deliverer; my shield, and He in whom I take refuge; who subdueth my people under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> LORD, what is man, that Thou takest knowledge of him? or the son of man, that Thou makest account of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:11; Psalms 39:5–6; Psalms 109:23"> Man is like unto a breath; his days are as a shadow that passeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:32; Psalms 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> O LORD, bow Thy heavens, and come down; touch the mountains, that they may smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Psalms 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> Cast forth lightning, and scatter them; send out Thine arrows, and discomfit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Psalms 18:16; Psalms 54:3"> Stretch forth Thy hands from on high; rescue me, and deliver me out of many waters, out of the hand of strangers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 41:6; Psalms 62:4"> Whose mouth speaketh falsehood, and their right hand is a right hand of lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:2–3; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 149:1"> O God, I will sing a new song unto Thee, upon a psaltery of ten strings will I sing praises unto Thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:50; Psalms 140:7; Psalms 33:16–18"> Who givest salvation unto kings, who rescuest David Thy servant from the hurtful sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; Psalms 12:2"> Rescue me, and deliver me out of the hand of strangers, whose mouth speaketh falsehood, and their right hand is a right hand of lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> We whose sons are as plants grown up in their youth; whose daughters are as corner-pillars carved after the fashion of a palace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> Whose garners are full, affording all manner of store; whose sheep increase by thousands and ten thousands in our fields;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> Whose oxen are well laden; with no breach, and no going forth, and no outcry in our broad places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 146:5"> Happy is the people that is in such a case. Yea, happy is the people whose God is the LORD.</span>
