<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> BOOK III A Psalm of Asaph. Surely God is good to Israel, even to such as are pure in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> But as for me, my feet were almost gone; my steps had well nigh slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> For I was envious at the arrogant, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> For there are no pangs at their death, and their body is sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> In the trouble of man they are not; neither are they plagued like men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore pride is as a chain about their neck; violence covereth them as a garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> Their eyes stand forth from fatness; they are gone beyond the imaginations of their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> They scoff, and in wickedness utter oppression; they speak as if there were none on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> They have set their mouth against the heavens, and their tongue walketh through the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> Therefore His people return hither; and waters of fullness are drained out by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> And they say: 'How doth God know? And is there knowledge in the Most High?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> Behold, such are the wicked; and they that are always at ease increase riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> Surely in vain have I cleansed my heart, and washed my hands in innocency;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> For all the day have I been plagued, and my chastisement came every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> If I had said: 'I will speak thus', behold, I had been faithless to the generation of Thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> And when I pondered how I might know this, it was wearisome in mine eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> Until I entered into the sanctuary of God, and considered their end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> Surely Thou settest them in slippery places; Thou hurlest them down to utter ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> How are they become a desolation in a moment! They are wholly consumed by terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> As a dream when one awaketh, so, O Lord, when Thou arousest Thyself, Thou wilt despise their semblance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> For my heart was in a ferment, and I was pricked in my reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> But I was brutish, and ignorant; I was as a beast before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> Nevertheless I am continually with Thee; Thou holdest my right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Thou wilt guide me with Thy counsel, and afterward receive me with glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> Whom have I in heaven but Thee? And beside Thee I desire none upon earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> My flesh and my heart faileth; but God is the rock of my heart and my portion for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> For, lo, they that go far from Thee shall perish; Thou dost destroy all them that go astray from Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> But as for me, the nearness of God is my good; I have made the Lord GOD my refuge, that I may tell of all Thy works.</span>
