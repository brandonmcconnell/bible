<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 24:19"> A Psalm of David. Fret not thyself because of evil-doers, neither be thou envious against them that work unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 92:7; Psalms 90:5–6; James 1:10–11"> For they shall soon wither like the grass, and fade as the green herb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Matthew 6:31–33; Psalms 62:8"> Trust in the LORD, and do good; dwell in the land, and cherish faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 145:19; John 15:16"> So shalt thou delight thyself in the LORD; and He shall give thee the petitions of thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:6–7"> Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in Him, and He will bring it to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Job 11:17; Isaiah 58:8"> And He will make thy righteousness to go forth as the light, and thy right as the noonday.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:7–11; Psalms 27:14; Psalms 62:5"> Resign thyself unto the LORD, and wait patiently for Him; fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Proverbs 16:32; James 1:19–20"> Cease from anger, and forsake wrath; fret not thyself, it tendeth only to evil-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:21; Psalms 25:13; Psalms 37:29"> For evil-doers shall be cut off; but those that wait for the LORD, they shall inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 103:16; Psalms 37:35–36"> And yet a little while, and the wicked is no more; yea, thou shalt look well at his place, and he is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 26:3"> But the humble shall inherit the land, and delight themselves in the abundance of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Psalms 35:16; Psalms 31:13"> The wicked plotteth against the righteous, and gnasheth at him with his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 1:26; 1 Samuel 26:10"> The Lord doth laugh at him; for He seeth that his day is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Psalms 11:2; 1 John 3:12"> The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow; to cast down the poor and needy, to slay such as are upright in the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14–15; Psalms 35:8; Jeremiah 51:56"> Their sword shall enter into their own heart, and their bows shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; 1 Timothy 6:6"> Better is a little that the righteous hath than the abundance of many wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 63:8; Psalms 145:14"> For the arms of the wicked shall be broken; but the LORD upholdeth the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:6; Romans 6:23; Psalms 103:17"> The LORD knoweth the days of them that are wholehearted; and their inheritance shall be for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:20–22"> They shall not be ashamed in the time of evil; and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 68:2; Psalms 102:3; Luke 13:3"> For the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the LORD shall be as the fat of lambs — they shall pass away in smoke, they shall pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Psalms 112:5; Proverbs 22:7"> The wicked borroweth, and payeth not; but the righteous dealeth graciously, and giveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Galatians 3:13"> For such as are blessed of Him shall inherit the land; and they that are cursed of Him shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Psalms 119:133; Proverbs 4:26"> It is of the LORD that a man's goings are established; and He delighted in his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 145:14; Psalms 94:18"> Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down; for the LORD upholdeth his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 37:28; Psalms 112:2"> I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Matthew 5:7; Psalms 37:21"> All the day long he dealeth graciously, and lendeth; and his seed is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; Proverbs 16:6; 1 John 2:16–17"> Depart from evil, and do good; and dwell for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 21:10"> For the LORD loveth justice, and forsaketh not His saints; they are preserved for ever; but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:21; Psalms 37:9; 2 Peter 3:13"> The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 25:11–13; Ephesians 4:29"> The mouth of the righteous uttereth wisdom, and his tongue speaketh justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 40:8; Deuteronomy 6:6; Isaiah 51:7"> The law of his God is in his heart; none of his steps slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Psalms 10:8–10; Psalms 37:12"> The wicked watcheth the righteous, and seeketh to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 109:31; 2 Peter 2:9; Romans 8:1"> The LORD will not leave him in his hand, nor suffer him to be condemned when he is judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 37:9; Proverbs 20:22"> Wait for the LORD, and keep His way, and He will exalt thee to inherit the land; when the wicked are cut off, thou shalt see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 5:3; Ezekiel 31:18; Isaiah 14:14–19"> I have seen the wicked in great power, and spreading himself like a leafy tree in its native soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Acts 12:22–23; Isaiah 10:16–19"> But one passed by, and, lo, he was not; yea, I sought him, but he could not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 57:1–2; 2 Timothy 4:6–8"> Mark the man of integrity, and behold the upright; for there is a future for the man of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Proverbs 14:32; Psalms 52:5; Psalms 1:4–6"> But transgressors shall be destroyed together; the future of the wicked shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 91:15; Isaiah 12:2"> But the salvation of the righteous is of the LORD; He is their stronghold in the time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 31:5; 1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 6:23"> And the LORD helpeth them, and delivereth them; He delivereth them from the wicked, and saveth them, because they have taken refuge in Him.</span>
