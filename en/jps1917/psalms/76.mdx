<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Daniel 4:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> (76-1) For the Leader; with string-music. A Psalm of Asaph, a Song. (76-2) In Judah is God known; His name is great in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:18; Isaiah 12:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> (76-3) In Salem also is set His tabernacle, and His dwelling-place in Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> (76-4) There He broke the fiery shafts of the bow; the shield, and the sword, and the battle. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1–4; Daniel 7:17–28; Ezekiel 19:6"> (76-5) Glorious art Thou and excellent, coming down from the mountains of prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> (76-6) The stout-hearted are bereft of sense, they sleep their sleep; and none of the men of might have found their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Psalms 80:16; Exodus 15:21"> (76-7) At Thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, they are cast into a dead sleep, the riders also and the horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Psalms 2:12; Psalms 89:7"> (76-8) Thou, even Thou, art terrible; and who may stand in Thy sight when once Thou art angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:29–30; Habakkuk 2:20; Judges 5:20"> (76-9) Thou didst cause sentence to be heard from heaven; the earth feared, and was still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Zephaniah 2:3; Isaiah 11:4"> (76-10) When God arose to judgment, to save all the humble of the earth. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Matthew 24:22; Daniel 3:19–20"> (76-11) Surely the wrath of man shall praise Thee; the residue of wrath shalt Thou gird upon Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalms 50:14; Psalms 68:29"> (76-12) Vow, and pay unto the LORD your God; let all that are round about Him bring presents unto Him that is to be feared;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:6; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Psalms 68:12"> (76-13) He minisheth the spirit of princes; He is terrible to the kings of the earth.</span>
