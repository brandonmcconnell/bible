<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Psalms 36:7; Psalms 17:7–8"> (57-1) For the Leader; Al-tashheth. A Psalm of David; Michtam; when he fled from Saul, in the cave. (57-2) Be gracious unto me, O God, be gracious unto me, for in Thee hath my soul taken refuge; yea, in the shadow of Thy wings will I take refuge, until calamities be overpast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Philippians 1:6; Hebrews 13:21"> (57-3) I will cry unto God Most high; unto God that accomplisheth it for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Psalms 43:3; Acts 12:11"> (57-4) He will send from heaven, and save me, when he that would swallow me up taunteth; Selah; God shall send forth His mercy and His truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 35:17"> (57-5) My soul is among lions, I do lie down among them that are aflame; even the sons of men, whose teeth are spears and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3; Psalms 72:19"> (57-6) Be Thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; Thy glory be above all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 145:14; Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 28:10"> (57-7) They have prepared a net for my steps, my soul is bowed down; they have digged a pit before me, they are fallen into the midst thereof themselves. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:7; Ephesians 5:20; Psalms 34:4"> (57-8) My heart is stedfast, O God, my heart is stedfast; I will sing, yea, I will sing praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 108:1–3; Psalms 30:12"> (57-9) Awake, my glory; awake, psaltery and harp; I will awake the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 96:3; Psalms 22:22–23; Psalms 18:49"> (57-10) I will give thanks unto Thee, O Lord, among the peoples; I will sing praises unto Thee among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11; Psalms 36:5"> (57-11) For Thy mercy is great unto the heavens, and Thy truth unto the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:5; Psalms 8:1; Psalms 8:9"> (57-12) Be Thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; Thy glory be above all the earth.</span>
