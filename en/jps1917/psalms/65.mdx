<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> (65-1) For the Leader. A Psalm. A Song of David. (65-2) Praise waiteth for Thee, O God, in Zion; and unto Thee the vow is performed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> (65-3) O Thou that hearest prayer, unto Thee doth all flesh come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> (65-4) The tale of iniquities is too heavy for me; as for our transgressions, Thou wilt pardon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> (65-5) Happy is the man whom Thou choosest, and bringest near, that he may dwell in Thy courts; may we be satisfied with the goodness of Thy house, the holy place of Thy temple!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> (65-6) With wondrous works dost Thou answer us in righteousness, O God of our salvation; Thou the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of the far distant seas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Micah 6:2; Habakkuk 3:6"> (65-7) Who by Thy strength settest fast the mountains, who art girded about with might;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Isaiah 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> (65-8) Who stillest the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, and the tumult of the peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> (65-9) So that they that dwell in the uttermost parts stand in awe of Thy signs; Thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> (65-10) Thou hast remembered the earth, and watered her, greatly enriching her, with the river of God that is full of water; Thou preparest them corn, for so preparest Thou her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> (65-11) Watering her ridges abundantly, settling down the furrows thereof, Thou makest her soft with showers; Thou blessest the growth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> (65-12) Thou crownest the year with Thy goodness; and Thy paths drop fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> (65-13) The pastures of the wilderness do drop; and the hills are girded with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Acts 14:17; Isaiah 35:1–2"> (65-14) The meadows are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, yea, they sing.</span>
