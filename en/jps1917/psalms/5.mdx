<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Psalms 54:2; 1 Peter 3:12"> (5-1) For the Leader; upon the Nehiloth. A Psalm of David. (5-2) Give ear to my words, O LORD, consider my meditation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 65:2; Psalms 3:4; Isaiah 33:22"> (5-3) Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King, and my God; for unto Thee do I pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:147; Psalms 88:13; Psalms 130:6"> (5-4) O LORD, in the morning shalt Thou hear my voice; in the morning will I order my prayer unto Thee, and will look forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Hebrews 12:14; Malachi 2:17"> (5-5) For Thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness; evil shall not sojourn with Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Psalms 1:5; Hosea 9:15"> (5-6) The boasters shall not stand in Thy sight; Thou hatest all workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Revelation 21:8; Revelation 22:15"> (5-7) Thou destroyest them that speak falsehood; the LORD abhorreth the man of blood and of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:7; Psalms 138:2; Hebrews 4:16"> (5-8) But as for me, in the abundance of Thy lovingkindness will I come into Thy house; I will bow down toward Thy holy temple in the fear of Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 27:11; Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6"> (5-9) O LORD, lead me in Thy righteousness because of them that lie in wait for me; make Thy way straight before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Jeremiah 9:3–6; Psalms 62:4"> (5-10) For there is no sincerity in their mouth; their inward part is a yawning gulf, their throat is an open sepulchre; they make smooth their tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Psalms 140:9–10; 2 Samuel 17:14"> (5-11) Hold them guilty, O God, let them fall by their own counsels; cast them down in the multitude of their transgressions; for they have rebelled against Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Revelation 19:1–7; Psalms 68:3"> (5-12) So shall all those that take refuge in Thee rejoice, they shall ever shout for joy, and Thou shalt shelter them; let them also that love Thy name exult in Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 32:10; Psalms 115:13"> (5-13) For Thou dost bless the righteous; O LORD, Thou dost encompass him with favour as with a shield.</span>
