<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Jeremiah 30:18; Ezekiel 39:25"> (85-1) For the Leader. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. (85-2) LORD, Thou hast been favourable unto Thy land, Thou hast turned the captivity of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Psalms 32:1; Colossians 2:13"> (85-3) Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of Thy people, Thou hast pardoned all their sin. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Deuteronomy 13:17; Jonah 3:9"> (85-4) Thou hast withdrawn all Thy wrath; Thou hast turned from the fierceness of Thine anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Lamentations 5:21; Psalms 80:7"> (85-5) Restore us, O God of our salvation, and cause Thine indignation toward us to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 80:4; Psalms 74:1"> (85-6) Wilt Thou be angry with us for ever? Wilt Thou draw out Thine anger to all generations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 80:18; Isaiah 57:15"> (85-7) Wilt Thou not quicken us again, that Thy people may rejoice in Thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Psalms 50:23; Jeremiah 42:12"> (85-8) Show us Thy mercy, O LORD, and grant us Thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Revelation 3:19; Zechariah 9:10"> (85-9) I will hear what God the LORD will speak; for He will speak peace unto His people, and to His saints; but let them not turn back to folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:13; Zechariah 2:5; John 7:17"> (85-10) Surely His salvation is nigh them that fear Him; that glory may dwell in our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 100:5"> (85-11) Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:8; John 14:6; 1 John 5:20–21"> (85-12) Truth springeth out of the earth; and righteousness hath looked down from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 84:11; Psalms 67:6"> (85-13) Yea, the LORD will give that which is good; and our land shall yield her produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:14; John 13:34; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> (85-14) Righteousness shall go before Him, and shall make His footsteps a way.</span>
