<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> BOOK IV A Prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, Thou hast been our dwelling-place in all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 102:24–27"> Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever Thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, Thou art God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou turnest man to contrition; and sayest: 'Return, ye children of men.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> For a thousand years in Thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; Psalms 73:20; Job 22:16"> Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep; in the morning they are like grass which groweth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 92:7; James 1:11"> In the morning it flourisheth, and groweth up; in the evening it is cut down, and withereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> For we are consumed in Thine anger, and by Thy wrath are we hurried away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Thou hast set our iniquities before Thee, our secret sins in the light of Thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 78:33; Psalms 39:5; Psalms 90:4"> For all our days are passed away in Thy wrath; we bring our years to an end as a tale that is told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> The days of our years are threescore years and ten, or even by reason of strength fourscore years; yet is their pride but travail and vanity; for it is speedily gone, and we fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> Who knoweth the power of Thine anger, and Thy wrath according to the fear that is due unto Thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalms 39:4; John 9:4"> So teach us to number our days, that we may get us a heart of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Psalms 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Return, O LORD; how long? And let it repent Thee concerning Thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 31:7"> O satisfy us in the morning with Thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 30:5; John 16:20"> Make us glad according to the days wherein Thou hast afflicted us, according to the years wherein we have seen evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Let Thy work appear unto Thy servants, and Thy glory upon their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> And let the graciousness of the Lord our God be upon us; establish Thou also upon us the work of our hands; yea, the work of our hands establish Thou it.</span>
