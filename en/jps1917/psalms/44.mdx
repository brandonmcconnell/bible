<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> (44-1) For the Leader; a Psalm of the sons of Korah. Maschil. (44-2) O God, we have heard with our ears, our fathers have told us; a work Thou didst in their days, in the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> (44-3) Thou with Thy hand didst drive out the nations, and didst plant them in; Thou didst break the peoples, and didst spread them abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> (44-4) For not by their own sword did they get the land in possession, neither did their own arm save them; but Thy right hand, and Thine arm, and the light of Thy countenance, because Thou wast favourable unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 74:12; Psalms 42:8; Psalms 149:2"> (44-5) Thou art my King, O God; command the salvation of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> (44-6) Through Thee do we push down our adversaries; through Thy name do we tread them under that rise up against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Hosea 1:7; 1 Samuel 17:47"> (44-7) For I trust not in my bow, neither can my sword save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> (44-8) But Thou hast saved us from our adversaries, and hast put them to shame that hate us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremiah 9:24"> (44-9) In God have we gloried all the day, and we will give thanks unto Thy name for ever. Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> (44-10) Yet Thou hast cast off, and brought us to confusion; and goest not forth with our hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> (44-11) Thou makest us to turn back from the adversary; and they that hate us spoil at their will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> (44-12) Thou hast given us like sheep to be eaten; and hast scattered us among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> (44-13) Thou sellest Thy people for small gain, and hast not set their prices high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> (44-14) Thou makest us a taunt to our neighbours, a scorn and a derision to them that are round about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 19:21; Job 16:4"> (44-15) Thou makest us a byword among the nations, a shaking of the head among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> (44-16) All the day is my confusion before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> (44-17) For the voice of him that taunteth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and the revengeful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> (44-18) All this is come upon us; yet have we not forgotten Thee, neither have we been false to Thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:51; Psalms 119:157; Psalms 78:57"> (44-19) Our heart is not turned back, neither have our steps declined from Thy path;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 3:5; Psalms 23:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> (44-20) Though Thou hast crushed us into a place of jackals, and covered us with the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 81:9; Job 11:13; Deuteronomy 6:14"> (44-21) If we had forgotten the name of our God, or spread forth our hands to a strange god;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 23:24; Hebrews 4:12–13"> (44-22) Would not God search this out? For He knoweth the secrets of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 44:11"> (44-23) Nay, but for Thy sake are we killed all the day; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:65; Psalms 7:6; Psalms 35:23"> (44-24) Awake, why sleepest Thou, O Lord? Arouse Thyself, cast not off for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:24; Deuteronomy 32:20; Psalms 42:9"> (44-25) Wherefore hidest Thou Thy face, and forgettest our affliction and our oppression?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:25; Lamentations 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> (44-26) For our soul is bowed down to the dust; our belly cleaveth unto the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 130:7–8; Psalms 25:22; Psalms 26:11"> (44-27) Arise for our help, and redeem us for Thy mercy's sake.</span>
