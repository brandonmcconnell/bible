<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> A Psalm of Solomon. Give the king Thy judgments, O God, and Thy righteousness unto the king's son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> That he may judge Thy people with righteousness, and Thy poor with justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> Let the mountains bear peace to the people, and the hills, through righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> May he judge the poor of the people, and save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They shall fear Thee while the sun endureth, and so long as the moon, throughout all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> May he come down like rain upon the mown grass, as showers that water the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> In his days let the righteous flourish, and abundance of peace, till the moon be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> May he have dominion also from sea to sea, and from the River unto the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> Let them that dwell in the wilderness bow before him; and his enemies lick the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall render tribute; the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> Yea, all kings shall prostrate themselves before him; all nations shall serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> For he will deliver the needy when he crieth; the poor also, and him that hath no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He will have pity on the poor and needy, and the souls of the needy he will save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> He will redeem their soul from oppression and violence, and precious will their blood be in his sight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> That they may live, and that he may give them of the gold of Sheba, that they may pray for him continually, yea, bless him all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> May he be as a rich cornfield in the land upon the top of the mountains; may his fruit rustle like Lebanon; and may they blossom out of the city like grass of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> May his name endure for ever; may his name be continued as long as the sun; may men also bless themselves by him; may all nations call him happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who only doeth wondrous things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> And blessed be His glorious name for ever; and let the whole earth be filled with His glory. Amen, and Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.</span>
