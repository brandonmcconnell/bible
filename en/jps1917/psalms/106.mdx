<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalms 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> Hallelujah. O give thanks unto the LORD; for He is good; for His mercy endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalms 145:3–12"> Who can express the mighty acts of the LORD, or make all His praise to be heard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> Happy are they that keep justice, that do righteousness at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O LORD, when Thou favourest Thy people; O think of me at Thy salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalms 105:43; Psalms 1:3"> That I may behold the prosperity of Thy chosen, that I may rejoice in the gladness of Thy nation, that I may glory with Thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalms 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> We have sinned with our fathers, we have done iniquitously, we have dealt wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalms 78:42; Psalms 78:11"> Our fathers in Egypt gave no heed unto Thy wonders; they remembered not the multitude of Thy mercies; but were rebellious at the sea, even at the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> Nevertheless He saved them for His name's sake, that He might make His mighty power to be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> And He rebuked the Red Sea, and it was dried up; and He led them through the depths, as through a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalms 107:2; Micah 6:4"> And He saved them from the hand of him that hated them, and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalms 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> And the waters covered their adversaries; there was not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> Then believed they His words; they sang His praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> They soon forgot His works; they waited not for His counsel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> But lusted exceedingly in the wilderness, and tried God in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; Psalms 78:29–31"> And He gave them their request; but sent leanness into their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> They were jealous also of Moses in the camp, and of Aaron the holy one of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> And a fire was kindled in their company; the flame burned up the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> They made a calf in Horeb, and worshipped a molten image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> Thus they exchanged their glory for the likeness of an ox that eateth grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 78:42–51; Psalms 106:13"> They forgot God their saviour, who had done great things in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> Wondrous works in the land of Ham, terrible things by the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> Therefore He said that He would destroy them, had not Moses His chosen stood before Him in the breach, to turn back His wrath, lest He should destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> Moreover, they scorned the desirable land, they believed not His word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> And they murmured in their tents, they hearkened not unto the voice of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> Therefore He swore concerning them, that He would overthrow them in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> And that He would cast out their seed among the nations, and scatter them in the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> They joined themselves also unto Baal of Peor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> Thus they provoked Him with their doings, and the plague broke in upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> Then stood up Phinehas, and wrought judgment, and so the plague was stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> And that was counted unto him for righteousness, unto all generations for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> They angered Him also at the waters of Meribah, and it went ill with Moses because of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalms 107:11; James 3:2"> For they embittered his spirit, and he spoke rashly with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> They did not destroy the peoples, as the LORD commanded them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> But mingled themselves with the nations, and learned their works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> And they served their idols, which became a snare unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> Yea, they sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> And shed innocent blood, even the blood of their sons and of their daughters, whom they sacrificed unto the idols of Canaan; and the land was polluted with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> Thus were they defiled with their works, and went astray in their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> Therefore was the wrath of the LORD kindled against His people, and He abhorred His inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> And He gave them into the hand of the nations; and they that hated them ruled over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> Their enemies also oppressed them, and they were subdued under their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalms 1:1"> Many times did He deliver them; but they were rebellious in their counsel, and sank low through their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> Nevertheless He looked upon their distress, when He heard their cry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> And He remembered for them His covenant, and repented according to the multitude of His mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> He made them also to be pitied of all those that carried them captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from among the nations, that we may give thanks unto Thy holy name, that we may triumph in Thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 89:52; Psalms 105:45–1"> Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting even to everlasting, and let all the people say: 'Amen.' Hallelujah.</span>
