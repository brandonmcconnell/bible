<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:2; Psalms 71:1–3"> (31-1) For the Leader. A Psalm of David. (31-2) In thee, O LORD, have I taken refuge; let me never be ashamed; deliver me in Thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:2–3; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 86:1"> (31-3) Incline Thine ear unto me, deliver me speedily; be Thou to me a rock of refuge, even a fortress of defence, to save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalms 79:9"> (31-4) For Thou art my rock and my fortress; therefore for Thy name's sake lead me and guide me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:15; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 19:14"> (31-5) Bring me forth out of the net that they have hidden for me; for Thou art my stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> (31-6) Into Thy hand I commit my spirit; Thou hast redeemed me, O LORD, Thou God of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; Psalms 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> (31-7) I hate them that regard lying vanities; but I trust in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 119:153"> (31-8) I will be glad and rejoice in Thy lovingkindness; for Thou hast seen mine affliction, Thou hast taken cognizance of the troubles of my soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalms 18:19"> (31-9) And Thou hast not given me over into the hand of the enemy; Thou hast set my feet in a broad place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 73:26"> (31-10) Be gracious unto me, O LORD, for I am in distress; mine eye wasteth away with vexation, yea, my soul and my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 88:15; Psalms 32:3–4; Psalms 38:3"> (31-11) For my life is spent in sorrow, and my years in sighing; my strength faileth because of mine iniquity, and my bones are wasted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18"> (31-12) Because of all mine adversaries I am become a reproach, yea, unto my neighbours exceedingly, and a dread to mine acquaintance; they that see me without flee from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; Psalms 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> (31-13) I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind; I am like a useless vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> (31-14) For I have heard the whispering of many, terror on every side; while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 43:5"> (31-15) But as for me, I have trusted in Thee, O LORD; I have said: 'Thou art my God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> (31-16) My times are in Thy hand; deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> (31-17) Make Thy face to shine upon Thy servant; save me in Thy lovingkindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 34:5"> (31-18) O LORD, let me not be ashamed, for I have called upon Thee; let the wicked be ashamed, let them be put to silence in the nether-world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Psalms 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> (31-19) Let the lying lips be dumb, which speak arrogantly against the righteous, with pride and contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> (31-20) Oh how abundant is Thy goodness, which Thou hast laid up for them that fear Thee; which Thou hast wrought for them that take their refuge in Thee, in the sight of the sons of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:5; Psalms 32:7; Psalms 91:1–4"> (31-21) Thou hidest them in the covert of Thy presence from the plottings of man; Thou concealest them in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; Psalms 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> (31-22) Blessed be the LORD; for He hath shown me His wondrous lovingkindness in an entrenched city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 88:16"> (31-23) As for me, I said in my haste: 'I am cut off from before Thine eyes'; nevertheless Thou heardest the voice of my supplications when I cried unto Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Psalms 94:2; Psalms 145:20"> (31-24) O love the LORD, all ye His godly ones; the LORD preserveth the faithful, and plentifully repayeth him that acteth haughtily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> (31-25) Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all ye that wait for the LORD.</span>
