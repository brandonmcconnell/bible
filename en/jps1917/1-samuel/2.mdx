<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Isaiah 44:6; Psalms 86:8"> There is none holy as the LORD, for there is none beside Thee; neither is there any rock like our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 8:13; 1 Kings 8:39; Proverbs 24:12"> Multiply not exceeding proud talk; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth; for the LORD is a God of knowledge, and by Him actions are weighed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Psalms 37:15; Psalms 46:9"> The bows of the mighty men are broken, and they that stumbled are girded with strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:9; Luke 1:53; Psalms 113:9"> They that were full have hired out themselves for bread; and they that were hungry have ceased; while the barren hath borne seven, she that had many children hath languished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 26:19; Revelation 1:18"> The LORD killeth, and maketh alive; He bringeth down to the grave, and bringeth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 75:7; Job 5:11; Deuteronomy 8:17–18"> The LORD maketh poor, and maketh rich; He bringeth low, He also lifteth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Daniel 2:48; James 2:5"> He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, He lifteth up the needy from the dung-hill, to make them sit with princes, and inherit the throne of glory; for the pillars of the earth are the LORD'S, and He hath set the world upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 91:11–12; Psalms 33:16–17"> He will keep the feet of His holy ones, but the wicked shall be put to silence in darkness; for not by strength shall man prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Psalms 89:24; Psalms 18:13–14"> They that strive with the LORD shall be broken to pieces; against them will He thunder in heaven; the LORD will judge the ends of the earth; and He will give strength unto His king, and exalt the horn of His anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; 1 Samuel 2:18; 1 Samuel 1:28"> And Elkanah went to Ramah to his house. And the child did minister unto the LORD before Eli the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:28–30; Jeremiah 2:8; Judges 2:10"> Now the sons of Eli were base men; they knew not the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 7:29–34"> And the custom of the priests with the people was, that, when any man offered sacrifice, the priest's servant came, while the flesh was in seething, with a flesh-hook of three teeth in his band;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:27–28; Isaiah 56:11; 1 Samuel 2:29"> and he struck it into the pan, or kettle, or caldron, or pot; all that the flesh-hook brought up the priest took therewith. So they did unto all the Israelites that came thither in Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 3:3–5; Romans 16:18"> Yea, before the fat was made to smoke, the priest's servant came, and said to the man that sacrificed: 'Give flesh to roast for the priest; for he will not have sodden flesh of thee, but raw.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Leviticus 3:16; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> And if the man said unto him: 'Let the fat be made to smoke first of all, and then take as much as thy soul desireth'; then he would say: 'Nay, but thou shalt give it me now; and if not, I will take it by force.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:7–9; Genesis 13:13; Malachi 2:13"> And the sin of the young men was very great before the LORD; for the men dealt contemptuously with the offering of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Exodus 28:4; 1 Samuel 2:11"> But Samuel ministered before the LORD, being a child, girded with a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; 1 Samuel 1:21; Exodus 23:14"> Moreover his mother made him a little robe, and brought it to him from year to year, when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:27–28; Luke 2:34; Ruth 4:11"> And Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife, and say: 'The LORD give thee seed of this woman for the loan which was lent to the LORD.' And they would go unto their own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 2:40; 1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 21:1"> So the LORD remembered Hannah, and she conceived, and bore three sons and two daughters. And the child Samuel grew before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; 1 Samuel 2:13–17; Jeremiah 7:9–10"> Now Eli was very old; and he heard all that his sons did unto all Israel, and how that they lay with the women that did service at the door of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Philippians 3:19; Acts 9:4"> And he said unto them: 'Why do ye such things? for I hear evil reports concerning you from all this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 2:8; 1 Timothy 3:7; Acts 6:3"> Nay, my sons; for it is no good report which I hear the LORD'S people do spread abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:14; Joshua 11:20; Hebrews 10:26"> If one man sin against another, God shall judge him; but if a man sin against the LORD, who shall entreat for him?' But they hearkened not unto the voice of their father, because the LORD would slay them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 2:40; Acts 2:47"> And the child Samuel grew on, and increased in favour both with the LORD, and also with men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Exodus 4:27; Exodus 4:14–16"> And there came a man of God unto Eli, and said unto him: 'Thus saith the LORD: Did I reveal Myself unto the house of thy father, when they were in Egypt in bondage to Pharaoh's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 8:7–8; Numbers 18:19"> And did I choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be My priest, to go up unto Mine altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before Me? and did I give unto the house of thy father all the offerings of the children of Israel made by fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 32:15; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> Wherefore kick ye at My sacrifice and at Mine offering, which I have commanded in My habitation; and honourest thy sons above Me, to make yourselves fat with the chiefest of all the offerings of Israel My people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:26; Jeremiah 18:9–10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Therefore the LORD, the God of Israel, saith: I said indeed that thy house, and the house of thy father, should walk before Me for ever; but now the LORD saith: Be it far from Me: for them that honour Me I will honour, and they that despise Me shall be lightly esteemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:17–20; 1 Kings 2:26–27; Psalms 37:17"> Behold, the days come, that I will cut off thine arm, and the arm of thy father's house, that there shall not be an old man in thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4; 1 Samuel 4:11; Psalms 78:59–64"> And thou shalt behold a rival in My habitation, in all the good which shall be done to Israel; and there shall not be an old man in thy house for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26–27; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Samuel 22:21–23"> Yet will I not cut off every man of thine from Mine altar, to make thine eyes to fail, and thy heart to languish; and all the increase of thy house shall die young men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:11; 1 Kings 13:3; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And this shall be the sign unto thee, that which shall come upon thy two sons, on Hophni and Phinehas: in one day they shall die both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Kings 11:38"> And I will raise Me up a faithful priest, that shall do according to that which is in My heart and in My mind; and I will build him a sure house; and he shall walk before Mine anointed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 2:27; Malachi 1:13; Ezekiel 44:10–12"> And it shall come to pass, that every one that is left in thy house shall come and bow down to him for a piece of silver and a loaf of bread, and shall say: Put me, I pray thee, into one of the priests' offices, that I may eat a morsel of bread.'</span>
