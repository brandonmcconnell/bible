<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:9–11; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> (2-2) A maul is come up before thy face; guard the defences, watch the way, make thy loins strong, fortify thy power mightily! —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Ezekiel 37:23; Zephaniah 3:11"> (2-3) For the LORD restoreth the pride of Jacob, as the pride of Israel; for the emptiers have emptied them out, and marred their vine-branches. —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–15; Isaiah 63:1–3; Zechariah 6:2"> (2-4) The shield of his mighty men is made red, the valiant men are in scarlet; the chariots are fire of steel in the day of his preparation, and the cypress spears are made to quiver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 26:10; Nahum 3:2–3"> (2-5) The chariots rush madly in the streets, they jostle one against another in the broad places; the appearance of them is like torches, they run to and fro like the lightnings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:12; Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 50:29"> (2-6) He bethinketh himself of his worthies; they stumble in their march; they make haste to the wall thereof, and the mantelet is prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> (2-7) The gates of the rivers are opened, and the palace is dissolved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 59:11; Luke 23:48"> (2-8) And the queen is uncovered, she is carried away, and her handmaids moan as with the voice of doves, tabering upon their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 46:5"> (2-9) But Nineveh hath been from of old like a pool of water; yet they flee away; 'Stand, stand'; but none looketh back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Nahum 2:12–13"> (2-10) Take ye the spoil of silver, take the spoil of gold; for there is no end of the store, rich with all precious vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> (2-11) She is empty, and void, and waste; and the heart melteth, and the knees smite together, and convulsion is in all loins, and the faces of them all have gathered blackness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:29; Jeremiah 2:15; Isaiah 31:4"> (2-12) Where is the den of the lions, which was the feeding-place of the young lions, where the lion and the lioness walked, and the lion's whelp, and none made them afraid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Isaiah 10:6–14"> (2-13) The lion did tear in pieces enough for his whelps, and strangled for his lionesses, and filled his caves with prey, and his dens with ravin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Nahum 3:5"> (2-14) Behold, I am against thee, saith the LORD of hosts, and I will burn her chariots in the smoke, and the sword shall devour thy young lions; and I will cut off thy prey from the earth, and the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard.</span>
