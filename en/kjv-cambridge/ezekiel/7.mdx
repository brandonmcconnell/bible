<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:3"> Also, thou son of man, thus saith the Lord God unto the land of Israel; An end, the end is come upon the four corners of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 33:20"> Now is the end come upon thee, and I will send mine anger upon thee, and will judge thee according to thy ways, and will recompense upon thee all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> And mine eye shall not spare thee, neither will I have pity: but I will recompense thy ways upon thee, and thine abominations shall be in the midst of thee: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Kings 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> Thus saith the Lord God; An evil, an only evil, behold, is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Ezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> An end is come, the end is come: it watcheth for thee; behold, it is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> The morning is come unto thee, O thou that dwellest in the land: the time is come, the day of trouble is near, and not the sounding again of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> Now will I shortly pour out my fury upon thee, and accomplish mine anger upon thee: and I will judge thee according to thy ways, and will recompense thee for all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> And mine eye shall not spare, neither will I have pity: I will recompense thee according to thy ways and thine abominations that are in the midst of thee; and ye shall know that I am the Lord that smiteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Behold the day, behold, it is come: the morning is gone forth; the rod hath blossomed, pride hath budded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> Violence is risen up into a rod of wickedness: none of them shall remain, nor of their multitude, nor of any of theirs: neither shall there be wailing for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> The time is come, the day draweth near: let not the buyer rejoice, nor the seller mourn: for wrath is upon all the multitude thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the seller shall not return to that which is sold, although they were yet alive: for the vision is touching the whole multitude thereof, which shall not return; neither shall any strengthen himself in the iniquity of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 51:27"> They have blown the trumpet, even to make all ready; but none goeth to the battle: for my wrath is upon all the multitude thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> The sword is without, and the pestilence and the famine within: he that is in the field shall die with the sword; and he that is in the city, famine and pestilence shall devour him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> But they that escape of them shall escape, and shall be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them mourning, every one for his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> All hands shall be feeble, and all knees shall be weak as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> They shall also gird themselves with sackcloth, and horror shall cover them; and shame shall be upon all faces, and baldness upon all their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> They shall cast their silver in the streets, and their gold shall be removed: their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them in the day of the wrath of the Lord: they shall not satisfy their souls, neither fill their bowels: because it is the stumblingblock of their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> As for the beauty of his ornament, he set it in majesty: but they made the images of their abominations and of their detestable things therein: therefore have I set it far from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> And I will give it into the hands of the strangers for a prey, and to the wicked of the earth for a spoil; and they shall pollute it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> My face will I turn also from them, and they shall pollute my secret place: for the robbers shall enter into it, and defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> Make a chain: for the land is full of bloody crimes, and the city is full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> Wherefore I will bring the worst of the heathen, and they shall possess their houses: I will also make the pomp of the strong to cease; and their holy places shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> Destruction cometh; and they shall seek peace, and there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> Mischief shall come upon mischief, and rumour shall be upon rumour; then shall they seek a vision of the prophet; but the law shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the ancients.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The king shall mourn, and the prince shall be clothed with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land shall be troubled: I will do unto them after their way, and according to their deserts will I judge them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
