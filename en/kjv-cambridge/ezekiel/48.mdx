<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; Genesis 30:7–8"> And by the border of Asher, from the east side even unto the west side, a portion for Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–11; Joshua 13:29–31; Genesis 48:14–20"> And by the border of Naphtali, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:8–10; Joshua 17:14–18; Joshua 16:1–10"> And by the border of Manasseh, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–21; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 49:3–4"> And by the border of Ephraim, from the east side even unto the west side, a portion for Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:1–63; Joshua 19:9; Genesis 29:35"> And by the border of Reuben, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:1–6; Isaiah 12:6; Isaiah 33:20–22"> And by the border of Judah, from the east side unto the west side, shall be the offering which ye shall offer of five and twenty thousand reeds in breadth, and in length as one of the other parts, from the east side unto the west side: and the sanctuary shall be in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The oblation that ye shall offer unto the Lord shall be of five and twenty thousand in length, and of ten thousand in breadth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:4; Ezekiel 44:28; Matthew 10:10"> And for them, even for the priests, shall be this holy oblation; toward the north five and twenty thousand in length, and toward the west ten thousand in breadth, and toward the east ten thousand in breadth, and toward the south five and twenty thousand in length: and the sanctuary of the Lord shall be in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:10; Matthew 24:45–46"> It shall be for the priests that are sanctified of the sons of Zadok; which have kept my charge, which went not astray when the children of Israel went astray, as the Levites went astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:21; Ezekiel 45:4"> And this oblation of the land that is offered shall be unto them a thing most holy by the border of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:3; Deuteronomy 12:19; Ezekiel 45:5"> And over against the border of the priests, the Levites shall have five and twenty thousand in length, and ten thousand in breadth: all the length shall be five and twenty thousand, and the breadth ten thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Leviticus 27:9–10; Exodus 22:29"> And they shall not sell of it, neither exchange, nor alienate the firstfruits of the land: for it is holy unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Ezekiel 45:6; Ezekiel 44:23"> And the five thousand, that are left in the breadth over against the five and twenty thousand, shall be a profane place for the city, for dwelling, and for suburbs: and the city shall be in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:16"> And these shall be the measures thereof; the north side four thousand and five hundred, and the south side four thousand and five hundred, and on the east side four thousand and five hundred, and the west side four thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the suburbs of the city shall be toward the north two hundred and fifty, and toward the south two hundred and fifty, and toward the east two hundred and fifty, and toward the west two hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 9:27; Ezra 2:43–58; Ezekiel 45:6"> And the residue in length over against the oblation of the holy portion shall be ten thousand eastward, and ten thousand westward: and it shall be over against the oblation of the holy portion; and the increase thereof shall be for food unto them that serve the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:6; 1 Kings 4:7–23; Nehemiah 11:1–36"> And they that serve the city shall serve it out of all the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:16; Hebrews 12:17"> All the oblation shall be five and twenty thousand by five and twenty thousand: ye shall offer the holy oblation foursquare, with the possession of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:22; Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 48:8"> And the residue shall be for the prince, on the one side and on the other of the holy oblation, and of the possession of the city, over against the five and twenty thousand of the oblation toward the east border, and westward over against the five and twenty thousand toward the west border, over against the portions for the prince: and it shall be the holy oblation; and the sanctuary of the house shall be in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moreover from the possession of the Levites, and from the possession of the city, being in the midst of that which is the prince’s, between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, shall be for the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 18:21–28; Ezekiel 48:1–7; Genesis 35:16–19"> As for the rest of the tribes, from the east side unto the west side, Benjamin shall have a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; Genesis 29:33; Genesis 49:5–7"> And by the border of Benjamin, from the east side unto the west side, Simeon shall have a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23; Genesis 30:14–18"> And by the border of Simeon, from the east side unto the west side, Issachar a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 19:10–16; Genesis 30:19–20"> And by the border of Issachar, from the east side unto the west side, Zebulun a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 13:24–28; Genesis 30:10–11"> And by the border of Zebulun, from the east side unto the west side, Gad a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:15; 2 Chronicles 20:2; Ezekiel 47:19–20"> And by the border of Gad, at the south side southward, the border shall be even from Tamar unto the waters of strife in Kadesh, and to the river toward the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 13:1–21; Numbers 34:2; Numbers 34:13"> This is the land which ye shall divide by lot unto the tribes of Israel for inheritance, and these are their portions, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:16; Revelation 21:16; Ezekiel 48:32–35"> And these are the goings out of the city: on the north side, four thousand and five hundred measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 21:12–13; Isaiah 26:1–2; Revelation 21:21"> And the gates of the city shall be after the names of the tribes of Israel: three gates northward; one gate of Reuben, one gate of Judah, one gate of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And at the east side four thousand and five hundred: and three gates; and one gate of Joseph, one gate of Benjamin, one gate of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And at the south side four thousand and five hundred measures: and three gates; one gate of Simeon, one gate of Issachar, one gate of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> At the west side four thousand and five hundred, with their three gates; one gate of Gad, one gate of Asher, one gate of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Zechariah 2:10; Revelation 22:3"> It was round about eighteen thousand measures: and the name of the city from that day shall be, The Lord is there.</span>
