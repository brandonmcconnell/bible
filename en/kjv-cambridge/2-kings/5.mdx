<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:27; Romans 15:18; Proverbs 21:31"> Now Naaman, captain of the host of the king of Syria, was a great man with his master, and honourable, because by him the Lord had given deliverance unto Syria: he was also a mighty man in valour, but he was a leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:23; 2 Kings 13:20; 1 Samuel 13:17–18"> And the Syrians had gone out by companies, and had brought away captive out of the land of Israel a little maid; and she waited on Naaman’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:29; Luke 17:12–14; Numbers 11:29"> And she said unto her mistress, Would God my lord were with the prophet that is in Samaria; for he would recover him of his leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26–27; John 4:28–29; John 1:42–46"> And one went in, and told his lord, saying, Thus and thus said the maid that is of the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 14:12; 2 Kings 8:8–9; Numbers 22:17–18"> And the king of Syria said, Go to, go, and I will send a letter unto the king of Israel. And he departed, and took with him ten talents of silver, and six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he brought the letter to the king of Israel, saying, Now when this letter is come unto thee, behold, I have therewith sent Naaman my servant to thee, that thou mayest recover him of his leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; 1 Kings 20:7; Genesis 30:2"> And it came to pass, when the king of Israel had read the letter, that he rent his clothes, and said, Am I God, to kill and to make alive, that this man doth send unto me to recover a man of his leprosy? wherefore consider, I pray you, and see how he seeketh a quarrel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–37; Hosea 12:13; Ezekiel 2:5"> And it was so, when Elisha the man of God had heard that the king of Israel had rent his clothes, that he sent to the king, saying, Wherefore hast thou rent thy clothes? let him come now to me, and he shall know that there is a prophet in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 16:37–39; 2 Kings 6:32; Isaiah 60:14"> So Naaman came with his horses and with his chariot, and stood at the door of the house of Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:7; 1 Corinthians 6:11; Leviticus 14:7"> And Elisha sent a messenger unto him, saying, Go and wash in Jordan seven times, and thy flesh shall come again to thee, and thou shalt be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Luke 14:11; Matthew 8:8"> But Naaman was wroth, and went away, and said, Behold, I thought, He will surely come out to me, and stand, and call on the name of the Lord his God, and strike his hand over the place, and recover the leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:9; 2 Kings 5:17; 2 Kings 2:14"> Are not Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? may I not wash in them, and be clean? So he turned and went away in a rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 6:21; 2 Kings 13:14; 2 Kings 2:12"> And his servants came near, and spake unto him, and said, My father, if the prophet had bid thee do some great thing, wouldest thou not have done it? how much rather then, when he saith to thee, Wash, and be clean?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 33:25; Luke 4:27; Luke 5:13"> Then went he down, and dipped himself seven times in Jordan, according to the saying of the man of God: and his flesh came again like unto the flesh of a little child, and he was clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Daniel 6:26–27; Joshua 2:9–11"> And he returned to the man of God, he and all his company, and came, and stood before him: and he said, Behold, now I know that there is no God in all the earth, but in Israel: now therefore, I pray thee, take a blessing of thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 5:26; 2 Kings 5:20; 2 Kings 3:14"> But he said, As the Lord liveth, before whom I stand, I will receive none. And he urged him to take it; but he refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Kings 5:12; Romans 14:1"> And Naaman said, Shall there not then, I pray thee, be given to thy servant two mules’ burden of earth? for thy servant will henceforth offer neither burnt offering nor sacrifice unto other gods, but unto the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2; 2 Kings 7:17; 2 Kings 17:35"> In this thing the Lord pardon thy servant, that when my master goeth into the house of Rimmon to worship there, and he leaneth on my hand, and I bow myself in the house of Rimmon: when I bow down myself in the house of Rimmon, the Lord pardon thy servant in this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:17; Mark 5:34; Exodus 4:18"> And he said unto him, Go in peace. So he departed from him a little way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 4:12; 2 Kings 4:31; 2 Kings 6:31"> But Gehazi, the servant of Elisha the man of God, said, Behold, my master hath spared Naaman this Syrian, in not receiving at his hands that which he brought: but, as the Lord liveth, I will run after him, and take somewhat of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 7:6–7; 2 Kings 9:17–22; 2 Kings 4:26"> So Gehazi followed after Naaman. And when Naaman saw him running after him, he lighted down from the chariot to meet him, and said, Is all well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 5:5; Isaiah 59:3; Acts 5:3–4"> And he said, All is well. My master hath sent me, saying, Behold, even now there be come to me from mount Ephraim two young men of the sons of the prophets: give them, I pray thee, a talent of silver, and two changes of garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:10; 1 Kings 20:7; 2 Kings 2:17"> And Naaman said, Be content, take two talents. And he urged him, and bound two talents of silver in two bags, with two changes of garments, and laid them upon two of his servants; and they bare them before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 21:16; Joshua 7:11–12; Joshua 7:1"> And when he came to the tower, he took them from their hand, and bestowed them in the house: and he let the men go, and they departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 5:22; Genesis 16:8; Proverbs 30:20"> But he went in, and stood before his master. And Elisha said unto him, Whence comest thou, Gehazi? And he said, Thy servant went no whither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; 2 Kings 6:12"> And he said unto him, Went not mine heart with thee, when the man turned again from his chariot to meet thee? Is it a time to receive money, and to receive garments, and oliveyards, and vineyards, and sheep, and oxen, and menservants, and maidservants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:6; Numbers 12:10; 2 Kings 15:5"> The leprosy therefore of Naaman shall cleave unto thee, and unto thy seed for ever. And he went out from his presence a leper as white as snow.</span>
</div>
