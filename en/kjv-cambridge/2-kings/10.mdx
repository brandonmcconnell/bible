<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:30; 1 Kings 13:32; 1 Kings 21:8–14"> And Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. And Jehu wrote letters, and sent to Samaria, unto the rulers of Jezreel, to the elders, and to them that brought up Ahab’s children, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:6"> Now as soon as this letter cometh to you, seeing your master’s sons are with you, and there are with you chariots and horses, a fenced city also, and armour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 2 Samuel 2:12–17"> look even out the best and meetest of your master’s sons, and set him on his father’s throne, and fight for your master’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 9:27; Isaiah 27:4"> But they were exceedingly afraid, and said, Behold, two kings stood not before him: how then shall we stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 20:32; 1 Kings 20:4; Joshua 9:11"> And he that was over the house, and he that was over the city, the elders also, and the bringers up of the children, sent to Jehu, saying, We are thy servants, and will do all that thou shalt bid us; we will not make any king: do thou that which is good in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–23; Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 25:4"> Then he wrote a letter the second time to them, saying, If ye be mine, and if ye will hearken unto my voice, take ye the heads of the men your master’s sons, and come to me to Jezreel by to morrow this time. Now the king’s sons, being seventy persons, were with the great men of the city, which brought them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 11:1; 2 Kings 10:9"> And it came to pass, when the letter came to them, that they took the king’s sons, and slew seventy persons, and put their heads in baskets, and sent him them to Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 2 Samuel 11:18–21; Mark 6:28"> And there came a messenger, and told him, saying, They have brought the heads of the king’s sons. And he said, Lay ye them in two heaps at the entering in of the gate until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:14–24; Isaiah 5:3; Hosea 1:4"> And it came to pass in the morning, that he went out, and stood, and said to all the people, Ye be righteous: behold, I conspired against my master, and slew him: but who slew all these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:29; 1 Kings 21:19; 2 Kings 9:7–10"> Know now that there shall fall unto the earth nothing of the word of the Lord, which the Lord spake concerning the house of Ahab: for the Lord hath done that which he spake by his servant Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:19; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:6"> So Jehu slew all that remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, and all his great men, and his kinsfolks, and his priests, until he left him none remaining.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he arose and departed, and came to Samaria. And as he was at the shearing house in the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 8:24; 2 Chronicles 21:17"> Jehu met with the brethren of Ahaziah king of Judah, and said, Who are ye? And they answered, We are the brethren of Ahaziah; and we go down to salute the children of the king and the children of the queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Kings 10:6; 2 Kings 10:10–11"> And he said, Take them alive. And they took them alive, and slew them at the pit of the shearing house, even two and forty men; neither left he any of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:14–19; Ezra 10:19; Ezekiel 17:18"> And when he was departed thence, he lighted on Jehonadab the son of Rechab coming to meet him: and he saluted him, and said to him, Is thine heart right, as my heart is with thy heart? And Jehonadab answered, It is. If it be, give me thine hand. And he gave him his hand; and he took him up to him into the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; Matthew 6:5; 2 Kings 10:31"> And he said, Come with me, and see my zeal for the Lord. So they made him ride in his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 9:8; 2 Chronicles 22:8; Malachi 4:1"> And when he came to Samaria, he slew all that remained unto Ahab in Samaria, till he had destroyed him, according to the saying of the Lord, which he spake to Elijah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31–32; 2 Kings 3:2; Romans 3:8"> And Jehu gathered all the people together, and said unto them, Ahab served Baal a little; but Jehu shall serve him much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Now therefore call unto me all the prophets of Baal, all his servants, and all his priests; let none be wanting: for I have a great sacrifice to do to Baal; whosoever shall be wanting, he shall not live. But Jehu did it in subtilty, to the intent that he might destroy the worshippers of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Exodus 32:5; 1 Kings 18:19–20"> And Jehu said, Proclaim a solemn assembly for Baal. And they proclaimed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 16:32; 2 Kings 11:18; Joel 3:2"> And Jehu sent through all Israel: and all the worshippers of Baal came, so that there was not a man left that came not. And they came into the house of Baal; and the house of Baal was full from one end to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Matthew 22:11–12"> And he said unto him that was over the vestry, Bring forth vestments for all the worshippers of Baal. And he brought them forth vestments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 25:32–33; Matthew 13:41"> And Jehu went, and Jehonadab the son of Rechab, into the house of Baal, and said unto the worshippers of Baal, Search, and look that there be here with you none of the servants of the Lord, but the worshippers of Baal only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30–42"> And when they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings, Jehu appointed fourscore men without, and said, If any of the men whom I have brought into your hands escape, he that letteth him go, his life shall be for the life of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 16:6–7; Ezekiel 22:21–22; 1 Kings 18:40"> And it came to pass, as soon as he had made an end of offering the burnt offering, that Jehu said to the guard and to the captains, Go in, and slay them; let none come forth. And they smote them with the edge of the sword; and the guard and the captains cast them out, and went to the city of the house of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 19:18"> And they brought forth the images out of the house of Baal, and burnt them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 6:11; Daniel 2:5"> And they brake down the image of Baal, and brake down the house of Baal, and made it a draught house unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Thus Jehu destroyed Baal out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–30; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 13:33–34"> Howbeit from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin, Jehu departed not from after them, to wit, the golden calves that were in Beth-el, and that were in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:1; 2 Kings 13:10"> And the Lord said unto Jehu, Because thou hast done well in executing that which is right in mine eyes, and hast done unto the house of Ahab according to all that was in mine heart, thy children of the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Proverbs 4:23; Hebrews 2:1"> But Jehu took no heed to walk in the law of the Lord God of Israel with all his heart: for he departed not from the sins of Jeroboam, which made Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 1 Kings 19:17; 2 Kings 13:22"> In those days the Lord began to cut Israel short: and Hazael smote them in all the coasts of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 1:3–4; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:9–12"> from Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead, the Gadites, and the Reubenites, and the Manassites, from Aroer, which is by the river Arnon, even Gilead and Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:41; 1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:19"> Now the rest of the acts of Jehu, and all that he did, and all his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Jehu slept with his fathers: and they buried him in Samaria. And Jehoahaz his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the time that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty and eight years.</span>
</div>
