<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 19:7–9; Mark 10:4–12; Isaiah 50:1"> When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her: then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 10:11; 1 Corinthians 7:15; Ezekiel 44:22"> And when she is departed out of his house, she may go and be another man’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And if the latter husband hate her, and write her a bill of divorcement, and giveth it in her hand, and sendeth her out of his house; or if the latter husband die, which took her to be his wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Joshua 22:17–18; Leviticus 18:24–28"> her former husband, which sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife, after that she is defiled; for that is abomination before the Lord; and thou shalt not cause the land to sin, which the Lord thy God giveth thee for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18; Deuteronomy 20:7; Genesis 2:24"> When a man hath taken a new wife, he shall not go out to war, neither shall he be charged with any business: but he shall be free at home one year, and shall cheer up his wife which he hath taken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19; Luke 12:15; Revelation 18:22"> No man shall take the nether or the upper millstone to pledge: for he taketh a man’s life to pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 21:16; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:1–4"> If a man be found stealing any of his brethren of the children of Israel, and maketh merchandise of him, or selleth him; then that thief shall die; and thou shalt put evil away from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2; Mark 1:44; Leviticus 13:1–46"> Take heed in the plague of leprosy, that thou observe diligently, and do according to all that the priests the Levites shall teach you: as I commanded them, so ye shall observe to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 12:10–15; 1 Corinthians 10:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Remember what the Lord thy God did unto Miriam by the way, after that ye were come forth out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:8; Exodus 22:27"> When thou dost lend thy brother any thing, thou shalt not go into his house to fetch his pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou shalt stand abroad, and the man to whom thou dost lend shall bring out the pledge abroad unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Job 22:6; Job 24:3"> And if the man be poor, thou shalt not sleep with his pledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:25; Daniel 4:27; Exodus 22:26–27"> in any case thou shalt deliver him the pledge again when the sun goeth down, that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee: and it shall be righteousness unto thee before the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 14:31; Proverbs 22:16"> Thou shalt not oppress a hired servant that is poor and needy, whether he be of thy brethren, or of thy strangers that are in thy land within thy gates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Proverbs 3:27–28; James 5:4"> at his day thou shalt give him his hire, neither shall the sun go down upon it; for he is poor, and setteth his heart upon it: lest he cry against thee unto the Lord, and it be sin unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Chronicles 25:4; Jeremiah 31:29–30"> The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: every man shall be put to death for his own sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 23:6"> Thou shalt not pervert the judgment of the stranger, nor of the fatherless; nor take a widow’s raiment to pledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:15; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> but thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in Egypt, and the Lord thy God redeemed thee thence: therefore I command thee to do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 14:29"> When thou cuttest down thine harvest in thy field, and hast forgot a sheaf in the field, thou shalt not go again to fetch it: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow: that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 19:10"> When thou beatest thine olive tree, thou shalt not go over the boughs again: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 24:19"> When thou gatherest the grapes of thy vineyard, thou shalt not glean it afterward: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; 1 John 4:10–11; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in the land of Egypt: therefore I command thee to do this thing.</span>
