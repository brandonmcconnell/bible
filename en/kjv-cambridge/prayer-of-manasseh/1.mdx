<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O Lord, Almighty God of our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and of their righteous seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> who hast made heaven and earth, with all the ornament thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> who hast bound the sea by the word of thy commandment; who hast shut up the deep, and sealed it by thy terrible and glorious name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> whom all men fear, and tremble before thy power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> for the majesty of thy glory cannot be borne, and thine angry threatening towards sinners is importable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> but thy merciful promise is unmeasurable and unsearchable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> for thou art the most high Lord, of great compassion, longsuffering, very merciful, and repentest of the evils of men: thou, O Lord, according to thy great goodness hast promised repentance and forgiveness to them that have sinned against thee: and of thine infinite mercies hast appointed repentance unto sinners, that they may be saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thou therefore, O Lord, that art the God of the just, hast not appointed repentance to the just, as to Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, which have not sinned against thee; but thou hast appointed repentance unto me that am a sinner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for I have sinned above the number of the sands of the sea. My transgressions, O Lord, are multiplied: my transgressions are multiplied, and I am not worthy to behold and see the height of heaven for the multitude of mine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> I am bowed down with many iron bands, that I cannot lift up mine head, neither have any release: for I have provoked thy wrath, and done evil before thee: I did not thy will, neither kept I thy commandments: I have set up abominations, and have multiplied offences.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Now therefore I bow the knee of mine heart, beseeching thee of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I have sinned, O Lord, I have sinned, and I acknowledge mine iniquities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> wherefore, I humbly beseech thee, forgive me, O Lord, forgive me, and destroy me not with mine iniquities. Be not angry with me for ever, by reserving evil for me; neither condemn me into the lower parts of the earth. For thou art the God, even the God of them that repent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and in me thou wilt shew all thy goodness: for thou wilt save me, that am unworthy, according to thy great mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore I will praise thee for ever all the days of my life: for all the powers of the heavens do praise thee, and thine is the glory for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
