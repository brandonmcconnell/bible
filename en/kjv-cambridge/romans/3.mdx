<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> For what if some did not believe? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness commend the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (I speak as a man)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> God forbid: for then how shall God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie unto his glory; why yet am I also judged as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> And not rather, (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? are we better than they? No, in no wise: for we have before proved both Jews and Gentiles, that they are all under sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> as it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> there is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> their feet are swift to shed blood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> destruction and misery are in their ways:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> and the way of peace have they not known:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> there is no fear of God before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe: for there is no difference:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> to declare, I say, at this time his righteousness: that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where is boasting then? It is excluded. By what law? of works? Nay: but by the law of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Is he the God of the Jews only? is he not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> seeing it is one God, which shall justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law.</span>
