<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:7"> And Joel the son of Zichri was their overseer: and Judah the son of Senuah was second over the city.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:12–13"> And their brethren that did the work of the house were eight hundred twenty and two: and Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi, the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashur, the son of Malchiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and his brethren, chief of the fathers, two hundred forty and two: and Amashai the son of Azareel, the son of Ahasai, the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and their brethren, mighty men of valour, an hundred twenty and eight: and their overseer was Zabdiel, the son of one of the great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:14; 1 Chronicles 9:19"> Also of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hashub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni;</span>
