<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Of fowls also of the air by sevens, the male and the female; to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living substance that I have made will I destroy from off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> And Noah did according unto all that the Lord commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> And Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters was upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And Noah went in, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons’ wives with him, into the ark, because of the waters of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of clean beasts, and of beasts that are not clean, and of fowls, and of every thing that creepeth upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> there went in two and two unto Noah into the ark, the male and the female, as God had commanded Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And it came to pass after seven days, that the waters of the flood were upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> In the selfsame day entered Noah, and Shem, and Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah’s wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, into the ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> they, and every beast after his kind, and all the cattle after their kind, and every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth after his kind, and every fowl after his kind, every bird of every sort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> And they went in unto Noah into the ark, two and two of all flesh, wherein is the breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> And they that went in, went in male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded him: and the Lord shut him in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> And the flood was forty days upon the earth; and the waters increased, and bare up the ark, and it was lift up above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> And the waters prevailed, and were increased greatly upon the earth; and the ark went upon the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth; and all the high hills, that were under the whole heaven, were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> Fifteen cubits upward did the waters prevail; and the mountains were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> And all flesh died that moved upon the earth, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of beast, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, and every man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> all in whose nostrils was the breath of life, of all that was in the dry land, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And every living substance was destroyed which was upon the face of the ground, both man, and cattle, and the creeping things, and the fowl of the heaven; and they were destroyed from the earth: and Noah only remained alive, and they that were with him in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the waters prevailed upon the earth an hundred and fifty days.</span>
