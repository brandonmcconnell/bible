<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Judges 6:33; Hosea 12:12"> Then Jacob went on his journey, and came into the land of the people of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:6"> And he looked, and behold a well in the field, and lo, there were three flocks of sheep lying by it; for out of that well they watered the flocks: and a great stone was upon the well’s mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And thither were all the flocks gathered: and they rolled the stone from the well’s mouth, and watered the sheep, and put the stone again upon the well’s mouth in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:10; Genesis 27:43; Acts 7:2"> And Jacob said unto them, My brethren, whence be ye? And they said, Of Haran are we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:24; Genesis 24:29; Genesis 31:53"> And he said unto them, Know ye Laban the son of Nahor? And they said, We know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; Genesis 37:14; Exodus 18:7"> And he said unto them, Is he well? And they said, He is well: and behold, Rachel his daughter cometh with the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16; Galatians 6:9–10"> And he said, Lo, it is yet high day, neither is it time that the cattle should be gathered together: water ye the sheep, and go and feed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 16:3; Luke 24:2; Genesis 34:14"> And they said, We cannot, until all the flocks be gathered together, and till they roll the stone from the well’s mouth; then we water the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Exodus 2:15–16"> And while he yet spake with them, Rachel came with her father’s sheep: for she kept them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> And it came to pass, when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban his mother’s brother, and the sheep of Laban his mother’s brother, that Jacob went near, and rolled the stone from the well’s mouth, and watered the flock of Laban his mother’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 45:2"> And Jacob kissed Rachel, and lifted up his voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> And Jacob told Rachel that he was her father’s brother, and that he was Rebekah’s son: and she ran and told her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> And it came to pass, when Laban heard the tidings of Jacob his sister’s son, that he ran to meet him, and embraced him, and kissed him, and brought him to his house. And he told Laban all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> And Laban said to him, Surely thou art my bone and my flesh. And he abode with him the space of a month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> And Laban said unto Jacob, Because thou art my brother, shouldest thou therefore serve me for nought? tell me, what shall thy wages be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 49:31; Genesis 31:4"> And Laban had two daughters: the name of the elder was Leah, and the name of the younger was Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> Leah was tender eyed; but Rachel was beautiful and well favoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> And Jacob loved Rachel; and said, I will serve thee seven years for Rachel thy younger daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalms 12:2"> And Laban said, It is better that I give her to thee, than that I should give her to another man: abide with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> And Jacob served seven years for Rachel; and they seemed unto him but a few days, for the love he had to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> And Jacob said unto Laban, Give me my wife, for my days are fulfilled, that I may go in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Ruth 4:10–13; Judges 14:10–18"> And Laban gathered together all the men of the place, and made a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Genesis 38:14–15; Genesis 24:65"> And it came to pass in the evening, that he took Leah his daughter, and brought her to him; and he went in unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:1; Genesis 30:9–12; Genesis 46:18"> And Laban gave unto his daughter Leah Zilpah his maid for a handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And it came to pass, that in the morning, behold, it was Leah: and he said to Laban, What is this thou hast done unto me? did not I serve with thee for Rachel? wherefore then hast thou beguiled me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Laban said, It must not be so done in our country, to give the younger before the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> Fulfil her week, and we will give thee this also for the service which thou shalt serve with me yet seven other years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Jacob did so, and fulfilled her week: and he gave him Rachel his daughter to wife also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> And Laban gave to Rachel his daughter Bilhah his handmaid to be her maid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> And he went in also unto Rachel, and he loved also Rachel more than Leah, and served with him yet seven other years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> And when the Lord saw that Leah was hated, he opened her womb: but Rachel was barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> And Leah conceived, and bare a son, and she called his name Reuben: for she said, Surely the Lord hath looked upon my affliction; now therefore my husband will love me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> And she conceived again, and bare a son; and said, Because the Lord hath heard that I was hated, he hath therefore given me this son also: and she called his name Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> And she conceived again, and bare a son; and said, Now this time will my husband be joined unto me, because I have born him three sons: therefore was his name called Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> And she conceived again, and bare a son: and she said, Now will I praise the Lord: therefore she called his name Judah; and left bearing.</span>
</div>
