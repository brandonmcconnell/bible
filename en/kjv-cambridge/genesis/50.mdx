<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8; Genesis 50:10"> And forty days were fulfilled for him; for so are fulfilled the days of those which are embalmed: and the Egyptians mourned for him threescore and ten days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:3; Esther 4:2"> And when the days of his mourning were past, Joseph spake unto the house of Pharaoh, saying, If now I have found grace in your eyes, speak, I pray you, in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; Matthew 27:60"> My father made me swear, saying, Lo, I die: in my grave which I have digged for me in the land of Canaan, there shalt thou bury me. Now therefore let me go up, I pray thee, and bury my father, and I will come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 48:21"> And Pharaoh said, Go up, and bury thy father, according as he made thee swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> And Joseph went up to bury his father: and with him went up all the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:24–27; Exodus 10:26; Exodus 10:8–9"> and all the house of Joseph, and his brethren, and his father’s house: only their little ones, and their flocks, and their herds, they left in the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; 2 Kings 18:24; Song of Solomon 1:9"> And there went up with him both chariots and horsemen: and it was a very great company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 2:13; Acts 8:2; 2 Samuel 1:17"> And they came to the threshingfloor of Atad, which is beyond Jordan, and there they mourned with a great and very sore lamentation: and he made a mourning for his father seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Genesis 24:6; Deuteronomy 11:30"> And when the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, saw the mourning in the floor of Atad, they said, This is a grievous mourning to the Egyptians: wherefore the name of it was called Abel-mizraim, which is beyond Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:16; Genesis 49:29–32; Ephesians 6:1"> And his sons did unto him according as he commanded them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–31; Genesis 23:16–18; Acts 7:16"> for his sons carried him into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah, which Abraham bought with the field for a possession of a buryingplace of Ephron the Hittite, before Mamre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Joseph returned into Egypt, he, and his brethren, and all that went up with him to bury his father, after he had buried his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Genesis 42:21–22; Psalms 53:5"> And when Joseph’s brethren saw that their father was dead, they said, Joseph will peradventure hate us, and will certainly requite us all the evil which we did unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25"> And they sent a messenger unto Joseph, saying, Thy father did command before he died, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Matthew 6:14–15; Genesis 45:8"> So shall ye say unto Joseph, Forgive, I pray thee now, the trespass of thy brethren, and their sin; for they did unto thee evil: and now, we pray thee, forgive the trespass of the servants of the God of thy father. And Joseph wept when they spake unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:7–11; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 45:3"> And his brethren also went and fell down before his face; and they said, Behold, we be thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 45:5; Genesis 30:2"> And Joseph said unto them, Fear not: for am I in the place of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Psalms 119:71; Genesis 45:5–8"> But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; 1 Peter 3:9; Genesis 45:10–11"> Now therefore fear ye not: I will nourish you and your little ones. And he comforted them, and spake kindly unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Joseph dwelt in Egypt, he, and his father’s house: and Joseph lived an hundred and ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:39; Genesis 30:3; Job 42:16"> And Joseph saw Ephraim’s children of the third generation: the children also of Machir the son of Manasseh were brought up upon Joseph’s knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 3:16–17; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 26:3"> And Joseph said unto his brethren, I die: and God will surely visit you, and bring you out of this land unto the land which he sware to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Joshua 24:32; Genesis 47:29–31"> And Joseph took an oath of the children of Israel, saying, God will surely visit you, and ye shall carry up my bones from hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 50:2–3; Genesis 47:9; Joshua 24:29"> So Joseph died, being an hundred and ten years old: and they embalmed him, and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.</span>
