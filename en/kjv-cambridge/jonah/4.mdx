<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 15:28; Jonah 4:9; Jonah 4:4"> But it displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Exodus 34:6–7; Numbers 14:18–19"> And he prayed unto the Lord, and said, I pray thee, O Lord, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> Therefore now, O Lord, take, I beseech thee, my life from me; for it is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonah 4:9; Micah 6:3"> Then said the Lord, Doest thou well to be angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; 1 Kings 19:13; Jeremiah 17:15–16"> So Jonah went out of the city, and sat on the east side of the city, and there made him a booth, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> And the Lord God prepared a gourd, and made it to come up over Jonah, that it might be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his grief. So Jonah was exceeding glad of the gourd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalms 102:10"> But God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd, that it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Psalms 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> And it came to pass, when the sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and wished in himself to die, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> And God said to Jonah, Doest thou well to be angry for the gourd? And he said, I do well to be angry, even unto death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> Then said the Lord, Thou hast had pity on the gourd, for the which thou hast not laboured, neither madest it grow; which came up in a night, and perished in a night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> and should not I spare Nineveh, that great city, wherein are more than sixscore thousand persons that cannot discern between their right hand and their left hand; and also much cattle?</span>
