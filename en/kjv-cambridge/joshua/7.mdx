<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 22:20; Ecclesiastes 9:18; 1 Chronicles 2:6–7"> But the children of Israel committed a trespass in the accursed thing: for Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took of the accursed thing: and the anger of the Lord was kindled against the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Hosea 4:15; 1 Samuel 14:23"> And Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is beside Beth-aven, on the east side of Beth-el, and spake unto them, saying, Go up and view the country. And the men went up and viewed Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11–12; Luke 13:24; 2 Peter 1:5"> And they returned to Joshua, and said unto him, Let not all the people go up; but let about two or three thousand men go up and smite Ai; and make not all the people to labour thither; for they are but few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Leviticus 26:17; Deuteronomy 32:30"> So there went up thither of the people about three thousand men: and they fled before the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9; Leviticus 26:36"> And the men of Ai smote of them about thirty and six men: for they chased them from before the gate even unto Shebarim, and smote them in the going down: wherefore the hearts of the people melted, and became as water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; Genesis 37:34; Revelation 18:19"> And Joshua rent his clothes, and fell to the earth upon his face before the ark of the Lord until the eventide, he and the elders of Israel, and put dust upon their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; 2 Kings 3:10; Matthew 17:20"> And Joshua said, Alas, O Lord God, wherefore hast thou at all brought this people over Jordan, to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us? would to God we had been content, and dwelt on the other side Jordan!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:5–6; Ezra 9:10; Habakkuk 2:1"> O Lord, what shall I say, when Israel turneth their backs before their enemies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:12; Psalms 83:4; Ezekiel 36:22–23"> For the Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land shall hear of it, and shall environ us round, and cut off our name from the earth: and what wilt thou do unto thy great name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Exodus 14:15; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Get thee up; wherefore liest thou thus upon thy face?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Hebrews 4:13; Joshua 6:17–19"> Israel hath sinned, and they have also transgressed my covenant which I commanded them: for they have even taken of the accursed thing, and have also stolen, and dissembled also, and they have put it even amongst their own stuff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Isaiah 59:2; Joshua 6:18"> Therefore the children of Israel could not stand before their enemies, but turned their backs before their enemies, because they were accursed: neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the accursed from amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Joshua 3:5; Matthew 7:5"> Up, sanctify the people, and say, Sanctify yourselves against to morrow: for thus saith the Lord God of Israel, There is an accursed thing in the midst of thee, O Israel: thou canst not stand before thine enemies, until ye take away the accursed thing from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Jonah 1:7; Joshua 7:17–18"> In the morning therefore ye shall be brought according to your tribes: and it shall be, that the tribe which the Lord taketh shall come according to the families thereof; and the family which the Lord shall take shall come by households; and the household which the Lord shall take shall come man by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 1 Samuel 14:38–39; Judges 20:6"> And it shall be, that he that is taken with the accursed thing shall be burnt with fire, he and all that he hath: because he hath transgressed the covenant of the Lord, and because he hath wrought folly in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Joshua 3:1; Genesis 22:3"> So Joshua rose up early in the morning, and brought Israel by their tribes; and the tribe of Judah was taken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4–7; Genesis 38:30"> and he brought the family of Judah; and he took the family of the Zarhites: and he brought the family of the Zarhites man by man; and Zabdi was taken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:23; Acts 5:1–10; 1 Samuel 14:42"> and he brought his household man by man; and Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:5; Jeremiah 13:16; John 9:24"> And Joshua said unto Achan, My son, give, I pray thee, glory to the Lord God of Israel, and make confession unto him; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide it not from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 38:18; Job 33:27; Genesis 42:21"> And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the Lord God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:15; Psalms 119:37; 1 John 2:15–16"> when I saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold of fifty shekels weight, then I coveted them, and took them; and behold, they are hid in the earth in the midst of my tent, and the silver under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran unto the tent; and behold, it was hid in his tent, and the silver under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they took them out of the midst of the tent, and brought them unto Joshua, and unto all the children of Israel, and laid them out before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Joshua 7:26"> And Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, and the garment, and the wedge of gold, and his sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep, and his tent, and all that he had: and they brought them unto the valley of Achor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; 1 Chronicles 2:7; Leviticus 24:14"> And Joshua said, Why hast thou troubled us? the Lord shall trouble thee this day. And all Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire, after they had stoned them with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Hosea 2:15; Deuteronomy 13:17"> And they raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day. So the Lord turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, The valley of Achor, unto this day.</span>
</div>
