<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> And the third day there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee; and the mother of Jesus was there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> and both Jesus was called, and his disciples, to the marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> And when they wanted wine, the mother of Jesus saith unto him, They have no wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus saith unto her, Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother saith unto the servants, Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> And there were set there six waterpots of stone, after the manner of the purifying of the Jews, containing two or three firkins apiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus saith unto them, Fill the waterpots with water. And they filled them up to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> And he saith unto them, Draw out now, and bear unto the governor of the feast. And they bare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> When the ruler of the feast had tasted the water that was made wine, and knew not whence it was: (but the servants which drew the water knew;) the governor of the feast called the bridegroom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and saith unto him, Every man at the beginning doth set forth good wine; and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse: but thou hast kept the good wine until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This beginning of miracles did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested forth his glory; and his disciples believed on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, and his brethren, and his disciples: and they continued there not many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> And the Jews’ passover was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> and found in the temple those that sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> and when he had made a scourge of small cords, he drove them all out of the temple, and the sheep and the oxen; and poured out the changers’ money, and overthrew the tables;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> and said unto them that sold doves, Take these things hence; make not my Father’s house a house of merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> And his disciples remembered that it was written, The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> Then answered the Jews and said unto him, What sign shewest thou unto us, seeing that thou doest these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered and said unto them, Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then said the Jews, Forty and six years was this temple in building, and wilt thou rear it up in three days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But he spake of the temple of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> When therefore he was risen from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this unto them; and they believed the scripture, and the word which Jesus had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> Now when he was in Jerusalem at the passover, in the feast day, many believed in his name, when they saw the miracles which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus did not commit himself unto them, because he knew all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and needed not that any should testify of man: for he knew what was in man.</span>
