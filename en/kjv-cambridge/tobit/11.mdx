<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> After these things Tobias went his way, praising God that he had given him a prosperous journey, and blessed Raguel and Edna his wife, and went on his way till they drew near unto Nineve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then Raphael said to Tobias, Thou knowest, brother, how thou didst leave thy father:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> let us haste before thy wife, and prepare the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And take in thine hand the gall of the fish. So they went their way, and the dog went after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now Anna sat looking about towards the way for her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And when she espied him coming, she said to his father, Behold, thy son cometh, and the man that went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then said Raphael, I know, Tobias, that thy father will open his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore anoint thou his eyes with the gall, and being pricked therewith, he shall rub, and the whiteness shall fall away, and he shall see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Anna ran forth, and fell upon the neck of her son, and said unto him, Seeing I have seen thee, my son, from henceforth I am content to die. And they wept both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Tobit also went forth toward the door, and stumbled: but his son ran unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and took hold of his father: and he strake of the gall on his father’s eyes, saying, Be of good hope, my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And when his eyes began to smart, he rubbed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the whiteness pilled away from the corners of his eyes: and when he saw his son, he fell upon his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he wept, and said, Blessed art thou, O God, and blessed is thy name for ever; and blessed are all thy holy angels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> for thou hast scourged, and hast taken pity on me: for behold, I see my son Tobias. And his son went in rejoicing, and told his father the great things that had happened to him in Media.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Tobit went out to meet his daughter in law at the gate of Nineve, rejoicing, and praising God: and they which saw him go marvelled, because he had received his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But Tobit gave thanks before them, because God had mercy on him. And when he came near to Sara his daughter in law, he blessed her, saying, Thou art welcome, daughter: God be blessed, which hath brought thee unto us, and blessed be thy father and thy mother. And there was joy amongst all his brethren which were at Nineve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Achiacharus, and Nasbas his brother’s son, came:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and Tobias’ wedding was kept seven days with great joy.</span>
