<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Tobit called his son Tobias, and said unto him, My son, see that the man have his wages, which went with thee, and thou must give him more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Tobias said unto him, O father, it is no harm to me to give him half of those things which I have brought:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> for he hath brought me again to thee in safety, and made whole my wife, and brought me the money, and likewise healed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then the old man said, It is due unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So he called the angel, and he said unto him, Take half of all that ye have brought, and go away in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then he took them both apart, and said unto them, Bless God, praise him, and magnify him, and praise him for the things which he hath done unto you in the sight of all that live. It is good to praise God, and exalt his name, and honourably to shew forth the works of God; therefore be not slack to praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> It is good to keep close the secret of a king, but it is honourable to reveal the works of God. Do that which is good, and no evil shall touch you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Prayer is good with fasting and alms and righteousness. A little with righteousness is better than much with unrighteousness. It is better to give alms than to lay up gold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for alms doth deliver from death, and shall purge away all sin. Those that exercise alms and righteousness shall be filled with life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> but they that sin are enemies to their own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Surely I will keep close nothing from you. For I said, It was good to keep close the secret of a king, but that it was honourable to reveal the works of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Now therefore, when thou didst pray, and Sara thy daughter in law, I did bring the remembrance of your prayers before the Holy One: and when thou didst bury the dead, I was with thee likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when thou didst not delay to rise up, and leave thy dinner, to go and cover the dead, thy good deed was not hid from me: but I was with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And now God hath sent me to heal thee and Sara thy daughter in law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I am Raphael, one of the seven holy angels, which present the prayers of the saints, and which go in and out before the glory of the Holy One.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then they were both troubled, and fell upon their faces: for they feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But he said unto them, Fear not, for it shall go well with you; praise God therefore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For not of any favour of mine, but by the will of our God I came; wherefore praise him for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All these days I did appear unto you; but I did neither eat nor drink, but you did see a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now therefore give God thanks: for I go up to him that sent me; but write all things which are done in a book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when they rose, they saw him no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then they confessed the great and wonderful works of God, and how the angel of the Lord had appeared unto them.</span>
