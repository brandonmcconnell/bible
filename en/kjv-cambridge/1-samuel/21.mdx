<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:12; Colossians 3:9; 1 Samuel 22:22"> And David said unto Ahimelech the priest, The king hath commanded me a business, and hath said unto me, Let no man know any thing of the business whereabout I send thee, and what I have commanded thee: and I have appointed my servants to such and such a place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6; Judges 9:29; 1 Samuel 21:4"> Now therefore what is under thine hand? give me five loaves of bread in mine hand, or what there is present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Matthew 12:3–4; Exodus 25:30"> And the priest answered David, and said, There is no common bread under mine hand, but there is hallowed bread; if the young men have kept themselves at least from women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> And David answered the priest, and said unto him, Of a truth women have been kept from us about these three days, since I came out, and the vessels of the young men are holy, and the bread is in a manner common, yea, though it were sanctified this day in the vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:3–4; Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:3–4"> So the priest gave him hallowed bread: for there was no bread there but the shewbread, that was taken from before the Lord, to put hot bread in the day when it was taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:9; Psalms 52:1–9; 1 Chronicles 27:29"> Now a certain man of the servants of Saul was there that day, detained before the Lord; and his name was Doeg, an Edomite, the chiefest of the herdmen that belonged to Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And David said unto Ahimelech, And is there not here under thine hand spear or sword? for I have neither brought my sword nor my weapons with me, because the king’s business required haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:2; 1 Samuel 17:50–54; Exodus 28:6–14"> And the priest said, The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom thou slewest in the valley of Elah, behold, it is here wrapt in a cloth behind the ephod: if thou wilt take that, take it: for there is no other save that here. And David said, There is none like that; give it me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:21; 1 Kings 19:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–2"> And David arose, and fled that day for fear of Saul, and went to Achish the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:5; 1 Samuel 18:7–8; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And the servants of Achish said unto him, Is not this David the king of the land? did they not sing one to another of him in dances, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Saul hath slain his thousands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And David his ten thousands?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Psalms 56:3; Psalms 34:4"> And David laid up these words in his heart, and was sore afraid of Achish the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Ecclesiastes 7:7; Psalms 34:1–22"> And he changed his behaviour before them, and feigned himself mad in their hands, and scrabled on the doors of the gate, and let his spittle fall down upon his beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:7"> Then said Achish unto his servants, Lo, you see the man is mad: wherefore then have ye brought him to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Have I need of mad men, that ye have brought this fellow to play the mad man in my presence? shall this fellow come into my house?</span>
