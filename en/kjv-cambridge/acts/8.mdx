<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> And Saul was consenting unto his death.<br />And at that time there was a great persecution against the church which was at Jerusalem; and they were all scattered abroad throughout the regions of Judea and Samaria, except the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> And devout men carried Stephen to his burial, and made great lamentation over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> As for Saul, he made havock of the church, entering into every house, and haling men and women committed them to prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Therefore they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Then Philip went down to the city of Samaria, and preached Christ unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> And the people with one accord gave heed unto those things which Philip spake, hearing and seeing the miracles which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For unclean spirits, crying with loud voice, came out of many that were possessed with them: and many taken with palsies, and that were lame, were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> And there was great joy in that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> But there was a certain man, called Simon, which beforetime in the same city used sorcery, and bewitched the people of Samaria, giving out that himself was some great one:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> to whom they all gave heed, from the least to the greatest, saying, This man is the great power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And to him they had regard, because that of long time he had bewitched them with sorceries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed Philip preaching the things concerning the kingdom of God, and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Then Simon himself believed also: and when he was baptized, he continued with Philip, and wondered, beholding the miracles and signs which were done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> Now when the apostles which were at Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent unto them Peter and John:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> who, when they were come down, prayed for them, that they might receive the Holy Ghost:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> (for as yet he was fallen upon none of them: only they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then laid they their hands on them, and they received the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> And when Simon saw that through laying on of the apostles’ hands the Holy Ghost was given, he offered them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> saying, Give me also this power, that on whomsoever I lay hands, he may receive the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said unto him, Thy money perish with thee, because thou hast thought that the gift of God may be purchased with money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> Thou hast neither part nor lot in this matter: for thy heart is not right in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent therefore of this thy wickedness, and pray God, if perhaps the thought of thine heart may be forgiven thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I perceive that thou art in the gall of bitterness, and in the bond of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Then answered Simon, and said, Pray ye to the Lord for me, that none of these things which ye have spoken come upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> And they, when they had testified and preached the word of the Lord, returned to Jerusalem, and preached the gospel in many villages of the Samaritans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> And the angel of the Lord spake unto Philip, saying, Arise, and go toward the south unto the way that goeth down from Jerusalem unto Gaza, which is desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> And he arose and went: and behold, a man of Ethiopia, an eunuch of great authority under Candace queen of the Ethiopians, who had the charge of all her treasure, and had come to Jerusalem for to worship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> was returning, and sitting in his chariot read Esaias the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> Then the Spirit said unto Philip, Go near, and join thyself to this chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> And Philip ran thither to him, and heard him read the prophet Esaias, and said, Understandest thou what thou readest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he said, How can I, except some man should guide me? And he desired Philip that he would come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> The place of the scripture which he read was this, He was led as a sheep to the slaughter; and like a lamb dumb before his shearer, so opened he not his mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> in his humiliation his judgment was taken away: and who shall declare his generation? for his life is taken from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> And the eunuch answered Philip, and said, I pray thee, of whom speaketh the prophet this? of himself, or of some other man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> Then Philip opened his mouth, and began at the same scripture, and preached unto him Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> And as they went on their way, they came unto a certain water: and the eunuch said, See, here is water; what doth hinder me to be baptized?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he commanded the chariot to stand still: and they went down both into the water, both Philip and the eunuch; and he baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> And when they were come up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught away Philip, that the eunuch saw him no more: and he went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philip was found at Azotus: and passing through he preached in all the cities, till he came to Cesarea.</span>
</div>
