<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have heard of thee by the hearing of the ear:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But now mine eye seeth thee.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> And it was so, that after the Lord had spoken these words unto Job, the Lord said to Eliphaz the Temanite, My wrath is kindled against thee, and against thy two friends: for ye have not spoken of me the thing that is right, as my servant Job hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalms 103:10"> Therefore take unto you now seven bullocks and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt offering; and my servant Job shall pray for you: for him will I accept: lest I deal with you after your folly, in that ye have not spoken of me the thing which is right, like my servant Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went, and did according as the Lord commanded them: the Lord also accepted Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> And the Lord turned the captivity of Job, when he prayed for his friends: also the Lord gave Job twice as much as he had before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> Then came there unto him all his brethren, and all his sisters, and all they that had been of his acquaintance before, and did eat bread with him in his house: and they bemoaned him, and comforted him over all the evil that the Lord had brought upon him: every man also gave him a piece of money, and every one an earring of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> So the Lord blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning: for he had fourteen thousand sheep, and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3"> He had also seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he called the name of the first, Jemima; and the name of the second, Kezia; and the name of the third, Keren-happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalms 144:12; Acts 7:20"> And in all the land were no women found so fair as the daughters of Job: and their father gave them inheritance among their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalms 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> After this lived Job an hundred and forty years, and saw his sons, and his sons’ sons, even four generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> So Job died, being old and full of days.</span>
