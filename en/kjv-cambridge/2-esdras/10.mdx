<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it so came to pass, that when my son was entered into his wedding chamber, he fell down, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then we all overthrew the lights, and all my neighbours rose up to comfort me: so I took my rest unto the second day at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And it came to pass, when they had all left off to comfort me, to the end I might be quiet; then rose I up by night, and fled, and came hither into this field, as thou seest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And I do now purpose not to return into the city, but here to stay, and neither to eat nor drink, but continually to mourn and to fast until I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then left I the meditations wherein I was, and spake to her in anger, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Thou foolish woman above all other, seest thou not our mourning, and what happeneth unto us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> How that Sion our mother is full of all heaviness, and much humbled, mourning very sore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And now, seeing we all mourn and are sad, for we are all in heaviness, art thou grieved for one son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For ask the earth, and she shall tell thee, that it is she which ought to mourn for the fall of so many that grow upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For out of her came all at the first, and out of her shall all others come, and behold, they walk almost all into destruction, and a multitude of them is utterly rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Who then should make more mourning than she, that hath lost so great a multitude; and not thou, which art sorry but for one?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But if thou sayest unto me, My lamentation is not like the earth’s, because I have lost the fruit of my womb, which I brought forth with pains, and bare with sorrows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> but the earth not so: for the multitude present in it according to the course of the earth is gone, as it came:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> then say I unto thee, Like as thou hast brought forth with labour; even so the earth also hath given her fruit, namely, man, ever sithence the beginning unto him that made her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now therefore keep thy sorrow to thyself, and bear with a good courage that which hath befallen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if thou shalt acknowledge the determination of God to be just, thou shalt both receive thy son in time, and shalt be commended amongst women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Go thy way then into the city to thine husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And she said unto me, That will I not do: I will not go into the city, but here will I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So I proceeded to speak further unto her, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Do not so, but be counselled by me: for how many are the adversities of Sion? be comforted in regard of the sorrow of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For thou seest that our sanctuary is laid waste, our altar broken down, our temple destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> our psaltery is laid on the ground, our song is put to silence, our rejoicing is at an end, the light of our candlestick is put out, the ark of our covenant is spoiled, our holy things are defiled, and the name that is called upon us is almost profaned: our children are put to shame, our priests are burnt, our Levites are gone into captivity, our virgins are defiled, and our wives ravished; our righteous men carried away, our little ones destroyed, our young men are brought in bondage, and our strong men are become weak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and, which is the greatest of all, the seal of Sion hath now lost her honour; for she is delivered into the hands of them that hate us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And therefore shake off thy great heaviness, and put away the multitude of sorrows, that the Mighty may be merciful unto thee again, and the Highest shall give thee rest and ease from thy labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And it came to pass, while I was talking with her, behold, her face upon a sudden shined exceedingly, and her countenance glistered, so that I was afraid of her, and mused what it might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And behold, suddenly she made a great cry very fearful: so that the earth shook at the noise of the woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And I looked, and behold, the woman appeared unto me no more, but there was a city builded, and a large place shewed itself from the foundations: then was I afraid, and cried with a loud voice, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Where is Uriel the angel, who came unto me at the first? for he hath caused me to fall into many trances, and mine end is turned into corruption, and my prayer to rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And as I was speaking these words, behold, he came unto me, and looked upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And lo, I lay as one that had been dead, and mine understanding was taken from me: and he took me by the right hand, and comforted me, and set me upon my feet, and said unto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> What aileth thee? and why art thou so disquieted? and why is thine understanding troubled, and the thoughts of thine heart?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And I said, Because thou hast forsaken me, and yet I did according to thy words, and I went into the field, and lo, I have seen, and yet see, that I am not able to express.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he said unto me, Stand up manfully, and I will advise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then said I, Speak on, my lord, in me; only forsake me not, lest I die frustrate of my hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For I have seen that I knew not, and hear that I do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Or is my sense deceived, or my soul in a dream?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Now therefore I beseech thee that thou wilt shew thy servant of this vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> He answered me then, and said, Hear me, and I shall inform thee, and tell thee wherefore thou art afraid: for the Highest will reveal many secret things unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> He hath seen that thy way is right: for that thou sorrowest continually for thy people, and makest great lamentation for Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> This therefore is the meaning of the vision which thou lately sawest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> thou sawest a woman mourning, and thou beganst to comfort her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> but now seest thou the likeness of the woman no more, but there appeared unto thee a city builded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And whereas she told thee of the death of her son, this is the solution:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> this woman, whom thou sawest, is Sion: and whereas she said unto thee, (even she whom thou seest as a city builded,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> whereas, I say, she said unto thee, that she hath been thirty years barren: those are the thirty years wherein there was no offering made in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> But after thirty years Solomon builded the city, and offered offerings: and then bare the barren a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And whereas she told thee that she nourished him with labour: that was the dwelling in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> But whereas she said unto thee, That my son coming into his marriage chamber happened to have a fall, and died: this was the destruction that came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And behold, thou sawest her likeness, and because she mourned for her son, thou beganst to comfort her: and of these things which have chanced, these are to be opened unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> For now the most High seeth that thou art grieved unfeignedly, and sufferest from thy whole heart for her, so hath he shewed thee the brightness of her glory, and the comeliness of her beauty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and therefore I bade thee remain in the field where no house was builded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> for I knew that the Highest would shew this unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> Therefore I commanded thee to go into the field, where no foundation of any building was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> For in the place wherein the Highest beginneth to shew his city, there can no man’s building be able to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And therefore fear not, let not thy heart be affrighted, but go thy way in, and see the beauty and greatness of the building, as much as thine eyes be able to see:</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> and then shalt thou hear as much as thine ears may comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> For thou art blessed above many other, and art called with the Highest; and so are but few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> But to morrow at night thou shalt remain here;</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> and so shall the Highest shew thee visions of the high things, which the most High will do unto them that dwell upon earth in the last days. So I slept that night and another, like as he commanded me.</span>
