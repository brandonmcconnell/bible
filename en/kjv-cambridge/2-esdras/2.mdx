<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Thus saith the Lord, I brought this people out of bondage, and I gave them my commandments by my servants the prophets; whom they would not hear, but despised my counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The mother that bare them saith unto them, Go your way, ye children; for I am a widow and forsaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I brought you up with gladness; but with sorrow and heaviness have I lost you: for ye have sinned before the Lord your God, and done that thing that is evil before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But what shall I now do unto you? I am a widow and forsaken: go your way, O my children, and ask mercy of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> As for me, O father, I call upon thee for a witness over the mother of these children, which would not keep my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> that thou bring them to confusion, and their mother to a spoil, that there may be no offspring of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Let them be scattered abroad among the heathen, let their names be put out of the earth: for they have despised my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Woe be unto thee, Assur, thou that hidest the unrighteous in thee! O thou wicked people, remember what I did unto Sodom and Gomorrha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> whose land lieth in clods of pitch and heaps of ashes: even so also will I do unto them that hear me not, saith the Almighty Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Thus saith the Lord unto Esdras, Tell my people that I will give them the kingdom of Jerusalem, which I would have given unto Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Their glory also will I take unto me, and give these the everlasting tabernacles, which I had prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They shall have the tree of life for an ointment of sweet savour; they shall neither labour, nor be weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Go, and ye shall receive: pray for few days unto you, that they may be shortened: the kingdom is already prepared for you: watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Take heaven and earth to witness; for I have broken the evil in pieces, and created the good: for I live, saith the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Mother, embrace thy children, and bring them up with gladness, make their feet as fast as a pillar: for I have chosen thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And those that be dead will I raise up again from their places, and bring them out of the graves: for I have known my name in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Fear not, thou mother of the children: for I have chosen thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For thy help I will send my servants Esay and Jeremie, after whose counsel I have sanctified and prepared for thee twelve trees laden with divers fruits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and as many fountains flowing with milk and honey, and seven mighty mountains, whereupon there grow roses and lilies, whereby I will fill thy children with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Do right to the widow, judge for the fatherless, give to the poor, defend the orphan, clothe the naked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> heal the broken and the weak, laugh not a lame man to scorn, defend the maimed, and let the blind man come into the sight of my clearness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Keep the old and young within thy walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Wheresoever thou findest the dead, take them and bury them, and I will give thee the first place in my resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Abide still, O my people, and take thy rest, for thy quietness shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Nourish thy children, O thou good nurse; stablish their feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> As for the servants whom I have given thee, there shall not one of them perish; for I will require them from among thy number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Be not weary: for when the day of trouble and heaviness cometh, others shall weep and be sorrowful, but thou shalt be merry and have abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The heathen shall envy thee, but they shall be able to do nothing against thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> My hands shall cover thee, so that thy children shall not see hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Be joyful, O thou mother, with thy children; for I will deliver thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Remember thy children that sleep, for I shall bring them out of the sides of the earth, and shew mercy unto them: for I am merciful, saith the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Embrace thy children until I come and shew mercy unto them: for my wells run over, and my grace shall not fail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> I Esdras received a charge of the Lord upon the mount Oreb, that I should go unto Israel; but when I came unto them, they set me at nought, and despised the commandment of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And therefore I say unto you, O ye heathen, that hear and understand, look for your Shepherd, he shall give you everlasting rest; for he is nigh at hand, that shall come in the end of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Be ready to the reward of the kingdom, for the everlasting light shall shine upon you for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Flee the shadow of this world, receive the joyfulness of your glory: I testify my Saviour openly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> O receive the gift that is given you, and be glad, giving thanks unto him that hath called you to the heavenly kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Arise up and stand, behold the number of those that be sealed in the feast of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> which are departed from the shadow of the world, and have received glorious garments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Take thy number, O Sion, and shut up those of thine that are clothed in white, which have fulfilled the law of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> The number of thy children, whom thou longedst for, is fulfilled: beseech the power of the Lord, that thy people, which have been called from the beginning, may be hallowed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> I Esdras saw upon the mount Sion a great people, whom I could not number, and they all praised the Lord with songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And in the midst of them there was a young man of a high stature, taller than all the rest, and upon every one of their heads he set crowns, and was more exalted; which I marvelled at greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So I asked the angel, and said, Sir, what are these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> He answered and said unto me, These be they that have put off the mortal clothing, and put on the immortal, and have confessed the name of God: now are they crowned, and receive palms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Then said I unto the angel, What young person is it that crowneth them, and giveth them palms in their hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So he answered and said unto me, It is the Son of God, whom they have confessed in the world. Then began I greatly to commend them that stood so stiffly for the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the angel said unto me, Go thy way, and tell my people what manner of things, and how great wonders of the Lord thy God thou hast seen.</span>
</div>
