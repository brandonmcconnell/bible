<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He answered me then, and said, Measure thou the time diligently in itself: and when thou seest part of the signs past, which I have told thee before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> then shalt thou understand, that it is the very same time, wherein the Highest will begin to visit the world which he made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Therefore when there shall be seen earthquakes and uproars of the people in the world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> then shalt thou well understand, that the most High spake of those things from the days that were before thee, even from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For like as all that is made in the world hath a beginning and an end, and the end is manifest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> even so the times also of the Highest have plain beginnings in wonders and powerful works, and endings in effects and signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, and by faith, whereby ye have believed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> shall be preserved from the said perils, and shall see my salvation in my land, and within my borders: for I have sanctified them for me from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then shall they be in pitiful case, which now have abused my ways: and they that have cast them away despitefully shall dwell in torments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For such as in their life have received benefits, and have not known me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and they that have lothed my law, while they had yet liberty, and, when as yet place of repentance was open unto them, understood not, but despised it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> the same must know it after death by pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And therefore be thou not curious how the ungodly shall be punished, and when: but inquire how the righteous shall be saved, whose the world is, and for whom the world is created.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then answered I and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I have said before, and now do speak, and will speak it also hereafter, that there be many moe of them which perish, than of them which shall be saved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> like as a wave is greater than a drop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he answered me, saying, Like as the field is, so is also the seed; as the flowers be, such are the colours also; such as the workman is, such also is the work; and as the husbandman is himself, so is his husbandry also: for it was the time of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And now when I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live, no man spake against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For then every one obeyed: but now the manners of them which are created in this world that is made are corrupted by a perpetual seed, and by a law which is unsearchable rid themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So I considered the world, and behold, there was peril because of the devices that were come into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I saw, and spared it greatly, and have kept me a grape of the cluster, and a plant of a great people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let the multitude perish then, which was born in vain; and let my grape be kept, and my plant; for with great labour have I made it perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Nevertheless, if thou wilt cease yet seven days moe, (but thou shalt not fast in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> but go into a field of flowers, where no house is builded, and eat only the flowers of the field; taste no flesh, drink no wine, but eat flowers only;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and pray unto the Highest continually, then will I come and talk with thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So I went my way into the field which is called Ardath, like as he commanded me; and there I sat amongst the flowers, and did eat of the herbs of the field, and the meat of the same satisfied me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> After seven days I sat upon the grass, and my heart was vexed within me, like as before:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and I opened my mouth, and began to talk before the most High, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> O Lord, thou that shewest thyself unto us, thou wast shewed unto our fathers in the wilderness, in a place where no man treadeth, in a barren place, when they came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And thou spakest, saying, Hear me, O Israel; and mark my words, thou seed of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For behold, I sow my law in you, and it shall bring fruit in you, and ye shall be honoured in it for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But our fathers, which received the law, kept it not, and observed not thy ordinances: and though the fruit of thy law did not perish, neither could it, for it was thine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> yet they that received it perished, because they kept not the thing that was sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And lo, it is a custom, when the ground hath received seed, or the sea a ship, or any vessel meat or drink, that, that being perished wherein it was sown or cast into,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> that thing also which was sown, or cast therein, or received, doth perish, and remaineth not with us: but with us it hath not happened so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For we that have received the law perish by sin, and our heart also which received it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Notwithstanding the law perisheth not, but remaineth in his force.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And when I spake these things in my heart, I looked back with mine eyes, and upon the right side I saw a woman, and behold, she mourned and wept with a loud voice, and was much grieved in heart, and her clothes were rent, and she had ashes upon her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then let I my thoughts go that I was in, and turned me unto her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and said unto her, Wherefore weepest thou? why art thou so grieved in thy mind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And she said unto me, Sir, let me alone, that I may bewail myself, and add unto my sorrow, for I am sore vexed in my mind, and brought very low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And I said unto her, What aileth thee? tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> She said unto me, I thy servant have been barren, and had no child, though I had a husband thirty years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and those thirty years I did nothing else day and night, and every hour, but make my prayer to the Highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> After thirty years God heard me thine handmaid, looked upon my misery, considered my trouble, and gave me a son: and I was very glad of him, so was my husband also, and all my neighbours: and we gave great honour unto the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And I nourished him with great travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So when he grew up, and came to the time that he should have a wife, I made a feast.</span>
</div>
