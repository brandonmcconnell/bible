<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the thirtieth year after the ruin of the city I was in Babylon, and lay troubled upon my bed, and my thoughts came up over my heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> for I saw the desolation of Sion, and the wealth of them that dwelt at Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And my spirit was sore moved, so that I began to speak words full of fear to the most High, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Lord, who bearest rule, thou spakest at the beginning, when thou didst plant the earth, and that thyself alone, and commandedst the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and gavest a body unto Adam without soul, which was the workmanship of thine hands, and didst breathe into him the breath of life, and he was made living before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And thou leddest him into paradise, which thy right hand had planted, before ever the earth came forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And unto him thou gavest commandment to love thy way: which he transgressed, and immediately thou appointedst death in him and in his generations, of whom came nations, tribes, people, and kindreds, out of number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And every people walked after their own will, and did wonderful things before thee, and despised thy commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And again in process of time thou broughtest the flood upon those that dwelt in the world, and destroyedst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And it came to pass in every of them, that as death was to Adam, so was the flood to these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Nevertheless one of them thou leftest, namely, Noah with his household, of whom came all righteous men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And it happened, that when they that dwelt upon the earth began to multiply, and had gotten them many children, and were a great people, they began again to be more ungodly than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now when they lived so wickedly before thee, thou didst choose thee a man from among them, whose name was Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Him thou lovedst, and unto him only thou shewedst thy will:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and madest an everlasting covenant with him, promising him that thou wouldest never forsake his seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And unto him thou gavest Isaac, and unto Isaac also thou gavest Jacob and Esau. As for Jacob, thou didst choose him to thee, and put by Esau: and so Jacob became a great multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it came to pass, that when thou leddest his seed out of Egypt, thou broughtest them up to the mount Sina.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And bowing the heavens, thou didst set fast the earth, movedst the whole world, and madest the depths to tremble, and troubledst the men of that age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And thy glory went through four gates, of fire, and of earthquake, and of wind, and of cold; that thou mightest give the law unto the seed of Jacob, and diligence unto the generation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And yet tookest thou not away from them a wicked heart, that thy law might bring forth fruit in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For the first Adam bearing a wicked heart transgressed, and was overcome; and so be all they that are born of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Thus infirmity was made permanent; and the law also in the heart of the people with the malignity of the root; so that the good departed away, and the evil abode still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So the times passed away, and the years were brought to an end: then didst thou raise thee up a servant, called David:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> whom thou commandedst to build a city unto thy name, and to offer incense and oblations unto thee therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When this was done many years, then they that inhabited the city forsook thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and in all things did even as Adam and all his generations had done: for they also had a wicked heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and so thou gavest thy city over into the hands of thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Are their deeds then any better that inhabit Babylon, that they should therefore have the dominion over Sion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For when I came thither, and had seen impieties without number, then my soul saw many evildoers in this thirtieth year, so that my heart failed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For I have seen how thou sufferest them sinning, and hast spared wicked doers: and hast destroyed thy people, and hast preserved thine enemies, and hast not signified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> I do not remember how this way may be left: Are they then of Babylon better than they of Sion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Or is there any other people that knoweth thee besides Israel? or what generation hath so believed thy covenants as Jacob?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And yet their reward appeareth not, and their labour hath no fruit: for I have gone here and there through the heathen, and I see that they flow in wealth, and think not upon thy commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Weigh thou therefore our wickedness now in the balance, and theirs also that dwell in the world; and so shall thy name no where be found but in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Or when was it that they which dwell upon the earth have not sinned in thy sight? or what people hath so kept thy commandments?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Thou shalt find that Israel by name hath kept thy precepts; but not the heathen.</span>
