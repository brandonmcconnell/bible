<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it came to pass upon the third day, I sat under an oak, and behold, there came a voice out of a bush over against me, and said, Esdras, Esdras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And I said, Here am I, Lord. And I stood up upon my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then said he unto me, In the bush I did manifestly reveal myself unto Moses, and talked with him, when my people served in Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and I sent him, and led my people out of Egypt, and brought him up to the mount of Sinai, where I held him by me a long season,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and told him many wondrous things, and shewed him the secrets of the times, and the end; and commanded him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> These words shalt thou declare, and these shalt thou hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And now I say unto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> That thou lay up in thy heart the signs that I have shewed, and the dreams that thou hast seen, and the interpretations which thou hast heard:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for thou shalt be taken away from all, and from henceforth thou shalt remain with my Son, and with such as be like thee, until the times be ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the world hath lost his youth, and the times begin to wax old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the world is divided into twelve parts, and the ten parts of it are gone already, and half of a tenth part:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and there remaineth that which is after the half of the tenth part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now therefore set thine house in order, and reprove thy people, comfort such of them as be in trouble, and now renounce corruption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> let go from thee mortal thoughts, cast away the burdens of man, put off now the weak nature,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and set aside the thoughts that are most heavy unto thee, and haste thee to flee from these times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For yet greater evils than those which thou hast seen happen shall be done hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For look how much the world shall be weaker through age, so much the more shall evils increase upon them that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For the truth is fled far away, and leasing is hard at hand: for now hasteth the vision to come, which thou hast seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then answered I before thee, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Behold, Lord, I will go, as thou hast commanded me, and reprove the people which are present: but they that shall be born afterward, who shall admonish them? thus the world is set in darkness, and they that dwell therein are without light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For thy law is burnt, therefore no man knoweth the things that are done of thee, or the works that shall begin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if I have found grace before thee, send the Holy Ghost into me, and I shall write all that hath been done in the world since the beginning, which were written in thy law, that men may find thy path, and that they which will live in the latter days may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he answered me, saying, Go thy way, gather the people together, and say unto them, that they seek thee not for forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But look thou prepare thee many box trees, and take with thee Sarea, Dabria, Selemia, Ecanus, and Asiel, these five which are ready to write swiftly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and come hither, and I shall light a candle of understanding in thine heart, which shall not be put out, till the things be performed which thou shalt begin to write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And when thou hast done, some things shalt thou publish, and some things shalt thou shew secretly to the wise: to morrow this hour shalt thou begin to write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then went I forth, as he commanded, and gathered all the people together, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Hear these words, O Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Our fathers at the beginning were strangers in Egypt, from whence they were delivered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and received the law of life, which they kept not, which ye also have transgressed after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then was the land, even the land of Sion, parted among you by lot: but your fathers, and ye yourselves, have done unrighteousness, and have not kept the ways which the Highest commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And forasmuch as he is a righteous judge, he took from you in time the thing that he had given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And now are you here, and your brethren among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Therefore if so be that you will subdue your own understanding, and reform your hearts, ye shall be kept alive, and after death ye shall obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For after death shall the judgment come, when we shall live again: and then shall the names of the righteous be manifest, and the works of the ungodly shall be declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Let no man therefore come unto me now, nor seek after me these forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So I took the five men, as he commanded me, and we went into the field, and remained there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the next day, behold, a voice called me, saying, Esdras, open thy mouth, and drink that I give thee to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then opened I my mouth, and behold, he reached me a full cup, which was full as it were with water, but the colour of it was like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And I took it, and drank: and when I had drunk of it, my heart uttered understanding, and wisdom grew in my breast, for my spirit strengthened my memory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> and my mouth was opened, and shut no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The Highest gave understanding unto the five men, and they wrote the wonderful visions of the night that were told, which they knew not: and they sat forty days, and they wrote in the day, and at night they ate bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> As for me, I spake in the day, and held not my tongue by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> In forty days they wrote two hundred and four books.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And it came to pass, when the forty days were fulfilled, that the Highest spake, saying, The first that thou hast written publish openly, that the worthy and unworthy may read it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> but keep the seventy last, that thou mayest deliver them only to such as be wise among the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> for in them is the spring of understanding, the fountains of wisdom, and the stream of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And I did so.</span>
