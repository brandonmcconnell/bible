<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then saw I a dream, and behold, there came up from the sea an eagle, which had twelve feathered wings, and three heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And I saw, and behold, she spread her wings over all the earth, and all the winds of the air blew on her, and were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I beheld, and out of her feathers there grew other contrary feathers; and they became little feathers and small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But her heads were at rest: the head in the midst was greater than the other, yet rested it with the residue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Moreover I beheld, and lo, the eagle flew with her feathers, and reigned upon earth, and over them that dwelt therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And I saw that all things under heaven were subject unto her, and no man spake against her, no, not one creature upon earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I beheld, and lo, the eagle rose upon her talons, and spake to her feathers, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Watch not all at once: sleep every one in his own place, and watch by course:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> but let the heads be preserved for the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And I beheld, and lo, the voice went not out of her heads, but from the midst of her body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And I numbered her contrary feathers, and behold, there were eight of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I looked, and behold, on the right side there arose one feather, and reigned over all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and so it was, that when it reigned, the end of it came, and the place thereof appeared no more: so the next following stood up, and reigned, and had a great time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and it happened, that when it reigned, the end of it came also, like as the first, so that it appeared no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then came there a voice unto it, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Hear thou that hast borne rule over the earth so long: this I say unto thee, before thou beginnest to appear no more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> There shall none after thee attain unto thy time, neither unto the half thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then arose the third, and reigned as the other before, and appeared no more also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So went it with all the residue one after another, as that every one reigned, and then appeared no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then I beheld, and lo, in process of time the feathers that followed stood up upon the right side, that they might rule also; and some of them ruled, but within a while they appeared no more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> for some of them were set up, but ruled not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> After this I looked, and behold, the twelve feathers appeared no more, nor the two little feathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and there was no more upon the eagle’s body, but three heads that rested, and six little wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then saw I also that two little feathers divided themselves from the six, and remained under the head that was upon the right side: for the four continued in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And I beheld, and lo, the feathers that were under the wing thought to set up themselves, and to have the rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And I beheld, and lo, there was one set up, but shortly it appeared no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the second was sooner away than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And I beheld, and lo, the two that remained thought also in themselves to reign:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and when they so thought, behold, there awaked one of the heads that were at rest, namely, it that was in the midst; for that was greater than the two other heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And then I saw that the two other heads were joined with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And behold, the head was turned with them that were with it, and did eat up the two feathers under the wing that would have reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But this head put the whole earth in fear, and bare rule in it over all those that dwelt upon the earth, with much oppression; and it had the governance of the world more than all the wings that had been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And after this I beheld, and lo, the head that was in the midst suddenly appeared no more, like as the wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But there remained the two heads, which also in like sort ruled upon the earth, and over those that dwelt therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And I beheld, and lo, the head upon the right side devoured it that was upon the left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then I heard a voice, which said unto me, Look before thee, and consider the thing that thou seest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And I beheld, and lo, as it were a roaring lion chased out of the wood: and I saw that he sent out a man’s voice unto the eagle, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Hear thou, I will talk with thee, and the Highest shall say unto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Art not thou it that remainest of the four beasts, whom I made to reign in my world, that the end of their times might come through them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And the fourth came, and overcame all the beasts that were past, and had power over the world with great fearfulness, and over the whole compass of the earth with much wicked oppression; and so long time dwelt he upon the earth with deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> For the earth hast thou not judged with truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> For thou hast afflicted the meek, thou hast hurt the peaceable, thou hast loved liars, and destroyed the dwellings of them that brought forth fruit, and hast cast down the walls of such as did thee no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Therefore is thy wrongful dealing come up unto the Highest, and thy pride unto the Mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> The Highest also hath looked upon the proud times, and behold, they are ended, and his abominations are fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And therefore appear no more, thou eagle, nor thy horrible wings, nor thy wicked feathers, nor thy malicious heads, nor thy hurtful claws, nor all thy vain body:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> that all the earth may be refreshed, and may return, being delivered from thy violence, and that she may hope for the judgment and mercy of him that made her.</span>
