<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and upon the one she leaned, as carrying herself daintily;</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And she was ruddy through the perfection of her beauty, and her countenance was cheerful and very amiable: but her heart was in anguish for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then having passed through all the doors, she stood before the king, who sat upon his royal throne, and was clothed with all his robes of majesty, all glittering with gold and precious stones; and he was very dreadful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then lifting up his countenance that shone with majesty, he looked very fiercely upon her: and the queen fell down, and was pale, and fainted, and bowed herself upon the head of the maid that went before her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then God changed the spirit of the king into mildness, who in a fear leaped from his throne, and took her in his arms, till she came to herself again, and comforted her with loving words, and said unto her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Esther, what is the matter? I am thy brother, be of good cheer:</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And so he held up his golden sceptre, and laid it upon her neck,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and embraced her, and said, Speak unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then said she unto him, I saw thee, my lord, as an angel of God, and my heart was troubled for fear of thy majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For wonderful art thou, lord, and thy countenance is full of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And as she was speaking, she fell down for faintness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the king was troubled, and all his servants comforted her.</span>
