<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Mardocheus said, God hath done these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For I remember a dream which I saw concerning these matters, and nothing thereof hath failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A little fountain became a river, and there was light, and the sun, and much water: this river is Esther, whom the king married, and made queen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and the two dragons are I and Aman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the nations were those that were assembled to destroy the name of the Jews:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and my nation is this Israel, which cried to God, and were saved: for the Lord hath saved his people, and the Lord hath delivered us from all those evils, and God hath wrought signs and great wonders, which have not been done among the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Therefore hath he made two lots, one for the people of God, and another for all the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And these two lots came at the hour, and time, and day of judgment, before God amongst all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So God remembered his people, and justified his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Therefore those days shall be unto them in the month Adar, the fourteenth and fifteenth day of the same month, with an assembly, and joy, and with gladness before God, according to the generations for ever among his people.</span>
