<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> 8 Then Mardocheus thought upon all the works of the Lord, and made his prayer unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> 9 Saying, O Lord, Lord, the King Almighty: for the whole world is in thy power, and if thou hast appointed to save Israel, there is no man that can gainsay thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> 10 for thou hast made heaven and earth, and all the wondrous things under the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> 11 Thou art Lord of all things, and there is no man that can resist thee, which art the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> 12 Thou knowest all things, and thou knowest, Lord, that it was neither in contempt nor pride, nor for any desire of glory, that I did not bow down to proud Aman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> 13 For I could have been content with good will for the salvation of Israel to kiss the soles of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> 14 But I did this, that I might not prefer the glory of man above the glory of God: neither will I worship any but thee, O God, neither will I do it in pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> 15 And now, O Lord God and King, spare thy people: for their eyes are upon us to bring us to nought; yea, they desire to destroy the inheritance, that hath been thine from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> 16 Despise not the portion, which thou hast delivered out of Egypt for thine own self.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> 17 Hear my prayer, and be merciful unto thine inheritance: turn our sorrow into joy, that we may live, O Lord, and praise thy name: and destroy not the mouths of them that praise thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> 18 All Israel in like manner cried most earnestly unto the Lord, because their death was before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> 1 Queen Esther also, being in fear of death, resorted unto the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> 2 and laid away her glorious apparel, and put on the garments of anguish and mourning: and instead of precious ointments, she covered her head with ashes and dung, and she humbled her body greatly, and all the places of her joy she filled with her torn hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> 3 And she prayed unto the Lord God of Israel, saying, O my Lord, thou only art our King: help me, desolate woman, which have no helper but thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> 4 for my danger is in mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> 5 From my youth up I have heard in the tribe of my family, that thou, O Lord, tookest Israel from among all people, and our fathers from all their predecessors, for a perpetual inheritance, and thou hast performed whatsoever thou didst promise them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> 6 And now we have sinned before thee: therefore hast thou given us into the hands of our enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> 7 because we worshipped their gods: O Lord, thou art righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> 8 Nevertheless it satisfieth them not, that we are in bitter captivity: but they have stricken hands with their idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> 9 that they will abolish the thing that thou with thy mouth hast ordained, and destroy thine inheritance, and stop the mouth of them that praise thee, and quench the glory of thy house, and of thine altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> 10 and open the mouths of the heathen to set forth the praises of the idols, and to magnify a fleshly king for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> 11 O Lord, give not thy sceptre unto them that be nothing, and let them not laugh at our fall; but turn their device upon themselves, and make him an example, that hath begun this against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> 12 Remember, O Lord, make thyself known in time of our affliction, and give me boldness, O King of the nations, and Lord of all power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> 13 Give me eloquent speech in my mouth before the lion: turn his heart to hate him that fighteth against us, that there may be an end of him, and of all that are likeminded to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> 14 but deliver us with thine hand, and help me that am desolate, and which have no other helper but thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> 15 Thou knowest all things, O Lord; thou knowest that I hate the glory of the unrighteous, and abhor the bed of the uncircumcised, and of all the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> 16 Thou knowest my necessity: for I abhor the sign of my high estate, which is upon mine head in the days wherein I shew myself, and that I abhor it as a menstruous rag, and that I wear it not when I am private by myself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> 17 and that thine handmaid hath not eaten at Aman’s table, and that I have not greatly esteemed the king’s feast, nor drunk the wine of the drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> 18 Neither had thine handmaid any joy since the day that I was brought hither to this present, but in thee, O Lord God of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> 19 O thou mighty God above all, hear the voice of the forlorn, and deliver us out of the hands of the mischievous, and deliver me out of my fear.</span>
