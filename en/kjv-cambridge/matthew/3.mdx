<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–6; John 1:6–8; Luke 1:76"> In those days came John the Baptist, preaching in the wilderness of Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Mark 1:15; Daniel 2:44"> and saying, Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1; John 1:23"> For this is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> And the same John had his raiment of camel’s hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Luke 3:7; John 3:23"> Then went out to him Jerusalem, and all Judea, and all the region round about Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:13–16; Acts 2:38–41"> and were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 5:9"> But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism, he said unto them, O generation of vipers, who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Acts 26:20; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:27–29"> and think not to say within yourselves, We have Abraham to our father: for I say unto you, that God is able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:6; Luke 3:9; Matthew 7:19"> And now also the axe is laid unto the root of the trees: therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 13:30; Luke 3:17"> whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> Then cometh Jesus from Galilee to Jordan unto John, to be baptized of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 3:3–7; Acts 1:5–8; John 13:6–8"> But John forbad him, saying, I have need to be baptized of thee, and comest thou to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:34; John 15:10; Psalms 40:7–8"> And Jesus answering said unto him, Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness. Then he suffered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Mark 1:10; Luke 3:21–22"> And Jesus, when he was baptized, went up straightway out of the water: and lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 2:7; Matthew 17:5"> and lo a voice from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.</span>
</div>
