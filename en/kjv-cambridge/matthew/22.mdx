<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Jesus answered and spake unto them again by parables, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> The kingdom of heaven is like unto a certain king, which made a marriage for his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> and sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden to the wedding: and they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again, he sent forth other servants, saying, Tell them which are bidden, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come unto the marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his farm, another to his merchandise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> and the remnant took his servants, and entreated them spitefully, and slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> But when the king heard thereof, he was wroth: and he sent forth his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burnt up their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then saith he to his servants, The wedding is ready, but they which were bidden were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go ye therefore into the highways, and as many as ye shall find, bid to the marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> So those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good: and the wedding was furnished with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man which had not on a wedding garment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> and he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding garment? And he was speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then said the king to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called, but few are chosen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then went the Pharisees, and took counsel how they might entangle him in his talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they sent out unto him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Master, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God in truth, neither carest thou for any man: for thou regardest not the person of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Tell us therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Cesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, Why tempt ye me, ye hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Shew me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And he saith unto them, Whose is this image and superscription?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They say unto him, Cesar’s. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Cesar the things which are Cesar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> When they had heard these words, they marvelled, and left him, and went their way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> The same day came to him the Sadducees, which say that there is no resurrection, and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, Master, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first, when he had married a wife, deceased, and, having no issue, left his wife unto his brother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> likewise the second also, and the third, unto the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all the woman died also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Therefore in the resurrection whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Jesus answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But as touching the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the multitude heard this, they were astonished at his doctrine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But when the Pharisees had heard that he had put the Sadducees to silence, they were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> Then one of them, which was a lawyer, asked him a question, tempting him, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Master, which is the great commandment in the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jesus said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the first and great commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> While the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> saying, What think ye of Christ? whose son is he? They say unto him, The Son of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He saith unto them, How then doth David in spirit call him Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David then call him Lord, how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no man was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any moe questions.</span>
</div>
