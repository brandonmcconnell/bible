<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> Abraham begat Isaac; and Isaac begat Jacob; and Jacob begat Judas and his brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> and Judas begat Phares and Zara of Thamar; and Phares begat Esrom; and Esrom begat Aram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> and Aram begat Aminadab; and Aminadab begat Naasson; and Naasson begat Salmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> and Salmon begat Booz of Rachab; and Booz begat Obed of Ruth; and Obed begat Jesse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> and Jesse begat David the king; and David the king begat Solomon of her that had been the wife of Urias;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> and Solomon begat Roboam; and Roboam begat Abia; and Abia begat Asa;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> and Asa begat Josaphat; and Josaphat begat Joram; and Joram begat Ozias;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> and Ozias begat Joatham; and Joatham begat Achaz; and Achaz begat Ezekias;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> and Ezekias begat Manasses; and Manasses begat Amon; and Amon begat Josias;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> and Josias begat Jechonias and his brethren, about the time they were carried away to Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> and after they were brought to Babylon, Jechonias begat Salathiel; and Salathiel begat Zorobabel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and Zorobabel begat Abiud; and Abiud begat Eliakim; and Eliakim begat Azor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and Azor begat Sadoc; and Sadoc begat Achim; and Achim begat Eliud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and Eliud begat Eleazar; and Eleazar begat Matthan; and Matthan begat Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> and Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; and from David until the carrying away into Babylon are fourteen generations; and from the carrying away into Babylon unto Christ are fourteen generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But while he thought on these things, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying, Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> Now all this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken of the Lord by the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> Behold, a virgin shall be with child, and shall bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> and knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS.</span>
