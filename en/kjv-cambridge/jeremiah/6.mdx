<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:14; Judges 1:21; Joshua 18:21–28"> O ye children of Benjamin, gather yourselves to flee out of the midst of Jerusalem, and blow the trumpet in Tekoa, and set up a sign of fire in Beth-haccerem: for evil appeareth out of the north, and great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Isaiah 1:8; Isaiah 3:16–17"> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Jeremiah 39:1–3; 2 Kings 24:10–12"> The shepherds with their flocks shall come unto her; they shall pitch their tents against her round about; they shall feed every one in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Zephaniah 2:4; Joel 3:9"> Prepare ye war against her; arise, and let us go up at noon. Woe unto us! for the day goeth away, for the shadows of the evening are stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:13; Isaiah 32:14; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> Arise, and let us go by night, and let us destroy her palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremiah 32:24"> For thus hath the Lord of hosts said, Hew ye down trees, and cast a mount against Jerusalem: this is the city to be visited; she is wholly oppression in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:8; Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 7:11"> As a fountain casteth out her waters, so she casteth out her wickedness: violence and spoil is heard in her; before me continually is grief and wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:18; Hosea 9:12; Jeremiah 17:23"> Be thou instructed, O Jerusalem, lest my soul depart from thee; lest I make thee desolate, a land not inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Jeremiah 16:16; Obadiah 1:5–6"> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall throughly glean the remnant of Israel as a vine: turn back thine hand as a grapegatherer into the baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 7:26; John 7:7"> To whom shall I speak, and give warning, that they may hear? behold, their ear is uncircumcised, and they cannot hearken: behold, the word of the Lord is unto them a reproach; they have no delight in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; Jeremiah 20:9; Micah 3:8"> Therefore I am full of the fury of the Lord; I am weary with holding in: I will pour it out upon the children abroad, and upon the assembly of young men together: for even the husband with the wife shall be taken, the aged with him that is full of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; Isaiah 5:25; Deuteronomy 28:39–43"> And their houses shall be turned unto others, with their fields and wives together: for I will stretch out my hand upon the inhabitants of the land, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:11; Jeremiah 8:10; Micah 3:11"> For from the least of them even unto the greatest of them every one is given to covetousness; and from the prophet even unto the priest every one dealeth falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 23:17; Jeremiah 4:10"> They have healed also the hurt of the daughter of my people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when there is no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Matthew 15:14; Jeremiah 3:3"> Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were not at all ashamed, neither could they blush: therefore they shall fall among them that fall: at the time that I visit them they shall be cast down, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Deuteronomy 32:7; Jeremiah 18:15"> Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:4; Isaiah 58:1; Zechariah 7:11"> Also I set watchmen over you, saying, Hearken to the sound of the trumpet. But they said, We will not hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 31:10; Micah 6:5"> Therefore hear, ye nations, and know, O congregation, what is among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 8:9; Jeremiah 17:10"> Hear, O earth: behold, I will bring evil upon this people, even the fruit of their thoughts, because they have not hearkened unto my words, nor to my law, but rejected it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 60:6"> To what purpose cometh there to me incense from Sheba, and the sweet cane from a far country? your burnt offerings are not acceptable, nor your sacrifices sweet unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Ezekiel 3:20; Jeremiah 13:16"> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will lay stumblingblocks before this people, and the fathers and the sons together shall fall upon them; the neighbour and his friend shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:41–43; Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 6:1"> Thus saith the Lord, Behold, a people cometh from the north country, and a great nation shall be raised from the sides of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 13:18; Jeremiah 50:42; Luke 21:25–26"> They shall lay hold on bow and spear; they are cruel, and have no mercy; their voice roareth like the sea; and they ride upon horses, set in array as men for war against thee, O daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 50:43; Jeremiah 49:24"> We have heard the fame thereof: our hands wax feeble: anguish hath taken hold of us, and pain, as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 31:13"> Go not forth into the field, nor walk by the way; for the sword of the enemy and fear is on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Zechariah 12:10; Isaiah 32:11"> O daughter of my people, gird thee with sackcloth, and wallow thyself in ashes: make thee mourning, as for an only son, most bitter lamentation: for the spoiler shall suddenly come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Jeremiah 15:20; Ezekiel 3:8–10"> I have set thee for a tower and a fortress among my people, that thou mayest know and try their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Jeremiah 5:23; Jeremiah 18:18"> They are all grievous revolters, walking with slanders: they are brass and iron; they are all corrupters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 4:12"> The bellows are burnt, the lead is consumed of the fire; the founder melteth in vain: for the wicked are not plucked away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22"> Reprobate silver shall men call them, because the Lord hath rejected them.</span>
