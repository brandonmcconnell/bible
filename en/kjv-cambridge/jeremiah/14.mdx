<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8"> The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah concerning the dearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Jeremiah 8:21; 1 Samuel 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Judah mourneth, and the gates thereof languish;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are black unto the ground;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the cry of Jerusalem is gone up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Psalms 40:14; 2 Kings 18:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">And their nobles have sent their little ones to the waters:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They came to the pits, and found no water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They returned with their vessels empty;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were ashamed and confounded, and covered their heads.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 1:19–20; Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because the ground is chapt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For there was no rain in the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The plowmen were ashamed, they covered their heads.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea, the hind also calved in the field, and forsook it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because there was no grass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the wild asses did stand in the high places,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They snuffed up the wind like dragons;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their eyes did fail, because there was no grass.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:20–21"> O Lord, though our iniquities testify against us, do thou it for thy name’s sake: for our backslidings are many; we have sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 50:7; Isaiah 43:3"> O the hope of Israel, the saviour thereof in time of trouble, why shouldest thou be as a stranger in the land, and as a wayfaring man that turneth aside to tarry for a night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; Jeremiah 15:16"> Why shouldest thou be as a man astonied, as a mighty man that cannot save? yet thou, O Lord, art in the midst of us, and we are called by thy name; leave us not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13; Psalms 119:101"> Thus saith the Lord unto this people, Thus have they loved to wander, they have not refrained their feet, therefore the Lord doth not accept them; he will now remember their iniquity, and visit their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> Then said the Lord unto me, Pray not for this people for their good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> When they fast, I will not hear their cry; and when they offer burnt offering and an oblation, I will not accept them: but I will consume them by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> Then said I, Ah Lord God! behold, the prophets say unto them, Ye shall not see the sword, neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Then the Lord said unto me, The prophets prophesy lies in my name: I sent them not, neither have I commanded them, neither spake unto them: they prophesy unto you a false vision and divination, and a thing of nought, and the deceit of their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that prophesy in my name, and I sent them not, yet they say, Sword and famine shall not be in this land; By sword and famine shall those prophets be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> And the people to whom they prophesy shall be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem because of the famine and the sword; and they shall have none to bury them, them, their wives, nor their sons, nor their daughters: for I will pour their wickedness upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:21; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 13:17"> Therefore thou shalt say this word unto them;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let mine eyes run down with tears night and day, and let them not cease:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the virgin daughter of my people is broken with a great breach, with a very grievous blow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I go forth into the field, then behold the slain with the sword:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And if I enter into the city, then behold them that are sick with famine:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yea, both the prophet and the priest go about into a land that they know not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 5:22; Jeremiah 8:15; Jeremiah 6:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hast thou utterly rejected Judah? hath thy soul lothed Zion?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why hast thou smitten us, and there is no healing for us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We looked for peace, and there is no good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And for the time of healing, and behold trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 32:5; 1 John 1:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">We acknowledge, O Lord, our wickedness, and the iniquity of our fathers:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For we have sinned against thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 3:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not abhor us, for thy name’s sake,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not disgrace the throne of thy glory:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Remember, break not thy covenant with us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:23; Jeremiah 5:24; Psalms 135:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Are there any among the vanities of the Gentiles that can cause rain?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or can the heavens give showers?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Art not thou he, O Lord our God? therefore we will wait upon thee:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For thou hast made all these things.</span></span>
