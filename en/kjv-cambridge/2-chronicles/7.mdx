<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:23–24"> Now when Solomon had made an end of praying, the fire came down from heaven, and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices; and the glory of the Lord filled the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; 2 Chronicles 5:14; Exodus 24:17"> And the priests could not enter into the house of the Lord, because the glory of the Lord had filled the Lord’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 2 Chronicles 20:21; Luke 1:50"> And when all the children of Israel saw how the fire came down, and the glory of the Lord upon the house, they bowed themselves with their faces to the ground upon the pavement, and worshipped, and praised the Lord, saying, For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:62–63"> Then the king and all the people offered sacrifices before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:22; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 30:24"> And king Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty and two thousand oxen, and an hundred and twenty thousand sheep: so the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:12; 1 Chronicles 15:16–21; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> And the priests waited on their offices: the Levites also with instruments of musick of the Lord, which David the king had made to praise the Lord, because his mercy endureth for ever, when David praised by their ministry; and the priests sounded trumpets before them, and all Israel stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64–66; Hebrews 13:10–12; 2 Chronicles 36:14"> Moreover Solomon hallowed the middle of the court that was before the house of the Lord: for there he offered burnt offerings, and the fat of the peace offerings, because the brasen altar which Solomon had made was not able to receive the burnt offerings, and the meat offerings, and the fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65; Nehemiah 8:13–18"> Also at the same time Solomon kept the feast seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great congregation, from the entering in of Hamath unto the river of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36; 1 Kings 8:65; Joel 1:14"> And in the eighth day they made a solemn assembly: for they kept the dedication of the altar seven days, and the feast seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:66; Psalms 100:2; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And on the three and twentieth day of the seventh month he sent the people away into their tents, glad and merry in heart for the goodness that the Lord had shewed unto David, and to Solomon, and to Israel his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:1–9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 2:1"> Thus Solomon finished the house of the Lord, and the king’s house: and all that came into Solomon’s heart to make in the house of the Lord, and in his own house, he prosperously effected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; 1 Kings 9:2; 2 Chronicles 7:16"> And the Lord appeared to Solomon by night, and said unto him, I have heard thy prayer, and have chosen this place to myself for a house of sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:26–28; Deuteronomy 11:17; Revelation 11:6"> If I shut up heaven that there be no rain, or if I command the locusts to devour the land, or if I send pestilence among my people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; James 4:9–10; Isaiah 55:6–7"> if my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 6:40; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now mine eyes shall be open, and mine ears attent unto the prayer that is made in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; 2 Chronicles 7:12; 2 Chronicles 6:5–6"> For now have I chosen and sanctified this house, that my name may be there for ever: and mine eyes and mine heart shall be there perpetually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Ezekiel 36:27; 1 Kings 11:38"> And as for thee, if thou wilt walk before me, as David thy father walked, and do according to all that I have commanded thee, and shalt observe my statutes and my judgments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:16; Jeremiah 33:20–21; 2 Samuel 7:13–16"> then will I stablish the throne of thy kingdom, according as I have covenanted with David thy father, saying, There shall not fail thee a man to be ruler in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 26:14; Deuteronomy 28:15; Leviticus 26:33–46"> But if ye turn away, and forsake my statutes and my commandments, which I have set before you, and shall go and serve other gods, and worship them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Deuteronomy 28:37; Jeremiah 24:9"> then will I pluck them up by the roots out of my land which I have given them; and this house, which I have sanctified for my name, will I cast out of my sight, and will make it to be a proverb and a byword among all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Jeremiah 16:10–12; Jeremiah 19:8"> And this house, which is high, shall be an astonishment to every one that passeth by it; so that he shall say, Why hath the Lord done thus unto this land, and unto this house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Lamentations 2:16–17; Ezekiel 14:23"> And it shall be answered, Because they forsook the Lord God of their fathers, which brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, and laid hold on other gods, and worshipped them, and served them: therefore hath he brought all this evil upon them.</span>
