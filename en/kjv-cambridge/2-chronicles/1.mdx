<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Kings 2:12; Genesis 39:2"> And Solomon the son of David was strengthened in his kingdom, and the Lord his God was with him, and magnified him exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; 1 Chronicles 27:1; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> Then Solomon spake unto all Israel, to the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and to the judges, and to every governor in all Israel, the chief of the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:29; 1 Chronicles 16:39; Leviticus 1:1"> So Solomon, and all the congregation with him, went to the high place that was at Gibeon; for there was the tabernacle of the congregation of God, which Moses the servant of the Lord had made in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Chronicles 15:25–28; 2 Samuel 6:2"> But the ark of God had David brought up from Kirjath-jearim to the place which David had prepared for it: for he had pitched a tent for it at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 38:1–7; 1 Chronicles 2:19–20"> Moreover the brasen altar, that Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, had made, he put before the tabernacle of the Lord: and Solomon and the congregation sought unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Kings 8:63; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> And Solomon went up thither to the brasen altar before the Lord, which was at the tabernacle of the congregation, and offered a thousand burnt offerings upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; 1 John 5:14–15; John 16:23"> In that night did God appear unto Solomon, and said unto him, Ask what I shall give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5; Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 22:51–1"> And Solomon said unto God, Thou hast shewed great mercy unto David my father, and hast made me to reign in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 132:11–12"> Now, O Lord God, let thy promise unto David my father be established: for thou hast made me king over a people like the dust of the earth in multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 4:7; Proverbs 2:2–6"> Give me now wisdom and knowledge, that I may go out and come in before this people: for who can judge this thy people, that is so great?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 23:7; 1 Samuel 16:7"> And God said to Solomon, Because this was in thine heart, and thou hast not asked riches, wealth, or honour, nor the life of thine enemies, neither yet hast asked long life; but hast asked wisdom and knowledge for thyself, that thou mayest judge my people, over whom I have made thee king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; 2 Chronicles 9:22; Matthew 6:33"> wisdom and knowledge is granted unto thee; and I will give thee riches, and wealth, and honour, such as none of the kings have had that have been before thee, neither shall there any after thee have the like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 4:24–25"> Then Solomon came from his journey to the high place that was at Gibeon to Jerusalem, from before the tabernacle of the congregation, and reigned over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26–29; 2 Chronicles 9:25"> And Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen: and he had a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, which he placed in the chariot cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:27; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the king made silver and gold at Jerusalem as plenteous as stones, and cedar trees made he as the sycomore trees that are in the vale for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:28; 1 Kings 10:28–29"> And Solomon had horses brought out of Egypt, and linen yarn: the king’s merchants received the linen yarn at a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29"> And they fetcht up, and brought forth out of Egypt a chariot for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for an hundred and fifty: and so brought they out horses for all the kings of the Hittites, and for the kings of Syria, by their means.</span>
