<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 1 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Chronicles 17:5"> Now it came to pass, as David sat in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, Lo, I dwell in a house of cedars, but the ark of the covenant of the Lord remaineth under curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zechariah 8:23"> Then Nathan said unto David, Do all that is in thine heart; for God is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> And it came to pass the same night, that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> Go and tell David my servant, Thus saith the Lord, Thou shalt not build me a house to dwell in:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; 1 Kings 8:16"> for I have not dwelt in a house since the day that I brought up Israel unto this day; but have gone from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Wheresoever I have walked with all Israel, spake I a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why have ye not built me a house of cedars?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; 1 Samuel 17:15"> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, even from following the sheep, that thou shouldest be ruler over my people Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; 1 Chronicles 17:2; Psalms 113:7–8"> and I have been with thee whithersoever thou hast walked, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee, and have made thee a name like the name of the great men that are in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Ezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> Also I will ordain a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell in their place, and shall be moved no more; neither shall the children of wickedness waste them any more, as at the beginning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> and since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. Moreover I will subdue all thine enemies. Furthermore I tell thee that the Lord will build thee a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:21; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> And it shall come to pass, when thy days be expired that thou must go to be with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed after thee, which shall be of thy sons; and I will stablish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> He shall build me a house, and I will stablish his throne for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 Chronicles 17:12"> I will be his father, and he shall be my son: and I will not take my mercy away from him, as I took it from him that was before thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 72:17"> but I will settle him in mine house and in my kingdom for ever: and his throne shall be established for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 7:17"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> And David the king came and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord God, and what is mine house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> And yet this was a small thing in thine eyes, O God; for thou hast also spoken of thy servant’s house for a great while to come, and hast regarded me according to the estate of a man of high degree, O Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 2:30; 2 Samuel 7:20–24"> What can David speak more to thee for the honour of thy servant? for thou knowest thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 Samuel 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> O Lord, for thy servant’s sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all this greatness, in making known all these great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Jeremiah 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> O Lord, there is none like thee, neither is there any God besides thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> And what one nation in the earth is like thy people Israel, whom God went to redeem to be his own people, to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness, by driving out nations from before thy people, whom thou hast redeemed out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> For thy people Israel didst thou make thine own people for ever; and thou, Lord, becamest their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Jeremiah 11:5; Luke 1:38"> Therefore now, Lord, let the thing that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant and concerning his house be established for ever, and do as thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremiah 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> Let it even be established, that thy name may be magnified for ever, saying, The Lord of hosts is the God of Israel, even a God to Israel: and let the house of David thy servant be established before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; Ezekiel 36:37; Psalms 10:17"> For thou, O my God, hast told thy servant that thou wilt build him a house: therefore thy servant hath found in his heart to pray before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> And now, Lord, thou art God, and hast promised this goodness unto thy servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; Psalms 72:17"> now therefore let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may be before thee for ever: for thou blessest, O Lord, and it shall be blessed for ever.</span>
