<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:6; Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60"> Now these are the divisions of the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron; Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 10:2; Numbers 3:4; Numbers 26:61"> But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Samuel 21:1"> And David distributed them, both Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their offices in their service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 25:11–13; 1 Chronicles 15:6–12"> And there were moe chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar: and thus were they divided. Among the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen chief men of the house of their fathers, and eight among the sons of Ithamar according to the house of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:31; Joshua 18:10; Acts 4:1"> Thus were they divided by lot, one sort with another; for the governors of the sanctuary, and governors of the house of God, were of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:16; Matthew 8:19; Nehemiah 8:1"> And Shemaiah the son of Nethaneel the scribe, one of the Levites, wrote them before the king, and the princes, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and before the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites: one principal household being taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 11:10; Nehemiah 12:19"> Now the first lot came forth to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:39; Nehemiah 7:35; Nehemiah 12:15"> the third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:17"> the fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:5; Nehemiah 12:4; Nehemiah 12:17"> the seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 12:10; Nehemiah 7:39"> the ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10"> the eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> the thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Ezra 10:20"> the fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezekel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the one and twentieth to Jachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the three and twentieth to Delaiah, the four and twentieth to Maaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Hebrews 7:11"> These were the orderings of them in their service to come into the house of the Lord, according to their manner, under Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 23:16; 1 Chronicles 6:18"> And the rest of the sons of Levi were these: Of the sons of Amram; Shubael: of the sons of Shubael; Jehdeiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17; 1 Chronicles 26:25"> Concerning Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, the first was Isshiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Izharites; Shelomoth: of the sons of Shelomoth; Jahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:31"> And the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:20"> Of the sons of Uzziel; Michah: of the sons of Michah; Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:20; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 1 Chronicles 23:20"> The brother of Michah was Isshiah: of the sons of Isshiah; Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:21"> The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi: the sons of Jaaziah; Beno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sons of Merari by Jaaziah; Beno, and Shoham, and Zaccur, and Ibri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> Of Mahli came Eleazar, who had no sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Concerning Kish: the son of Kish was Jerahmeel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:23; 1 Chronicles 6:47"> The sons also of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites after the house of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5–6; 1 Chronicles 26:13; 1 Chronicles 25:8"> These likewise cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron in the presence of David the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites, even the principal fathers over against their younger brethren.</span>
