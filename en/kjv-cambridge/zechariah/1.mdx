<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; Zechariah 13:7"> I saw by night, and behold a man riding upon a red horse, and he stood among the myrtle trees that were in the bottom; and behind him were there red horses, speckled, and white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:4–5; Zechariah 5:5; Zechariah 2:3"> Then said I, O my lord, what are these? And the angel that talked with me said unto me, I will shew thee what these be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> And the man that stood among the myrtle trees answered and said, These are they whom the Lord hath sent to walk to and fro through the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 103:20–21"> And they answered the angel of the Lord that stood among the myrtle trees, and said, We have walked to and fro through the earth, and behold, all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 29:10; Psalms 102:13"> Then the angel of the Lord answered and said, O Lord of hosts, how long wilt thou not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which thou hast had indignation these threescore and ten years?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> And the Lord answered the angel that talked with me with good words and comfortable words.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I am very sore displeased with the heathen that are at ease:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I was but a little displeased, and they helped forward the affliction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 2:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore thus saith the Lord; I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My house shall be built in it, saith the Lord of hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a line shall be stretched forth upon Jerusalem.</span><br /></span>
