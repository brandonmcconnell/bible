<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their horses also are swifter than the leopards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And are more fierce than the evening wolves:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their horsemen shall spread themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their horsemen shall come from far;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They shall fly as the eagle that hasteth to eat.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they shall scoff at the kings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the princes shall be a scorn unto them:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They shall deride every strong hold;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they shall heap dust, and take it.</span><br /></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
