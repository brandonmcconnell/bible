<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> And he said unto them, Behold, when ye are entered into the city, there shall a man meet you, bearing a pitcher of water; follow him into the house where he entereth in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> And ye shall say unto the goodman of the house, The Master saith unto thee, Where is the guestchamber, where I shall eat the passover with my disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> And he shall shew you a large upper room furnished: there make ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> And they went, and found as he had said unto them: and they made ready the passover.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> And when the hour was come, he sat down, and the twelve apostles with him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Psalms 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and said, Take this, and divide it among yourselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> for I say unto you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, until the kingdom of God shall come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> And he took bread, and gave thanks, and brake it, and gave unto them, saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> Likewise also the cup after supper, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalms 109:6–15; Psalms 55:12–15"> And truly the Son of man goeth, as it was determined: but woe unto that man by whom he is betrayed.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> And there was also a strife among them, which of them should be accounted the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> And he said unto them, The kings of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them; and they that exercise authority upon them are called benefactors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But ye shall not be so: but he that is greatest among you, let him be as the younger; and he that is chief, as he that doth serve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> For whether is greater, he that sitteth at meat, or he that serveth? is not he that sitteth at meat? but I am among you as he that serveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> Ye are they which have continued with me in my temptations.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> that ye may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> but I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> And he said unto him, Lord, I am ready to go with thee, both into prison, and to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> And he said, I tell thee, Peter, the cock shall not crow this day, before that thou shalt thrice deny that thou knowest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> And he said unto them, When I sent you without purse, and scrip, and shoes, lacked ye any thing? And they said, Nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> Then said he unto them, But now, he that hath a purse, let him take it, and likewise his scrip: and he that hath no sword, let him sell his garment, and buy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For I say unto you, that this that is written must yet be accomplished in me, And he was reckoned among the transgressors: for the things concerning me have an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> And they said, Lord, behold, here are two swords. And he said unto them, It is enough.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> And he came out, and went, as he was wont, to the mount of Olives; and his disciples also followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> And when he was at the place, he said unto them, Pray that ye enter not into temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> And he was withdrawn from them about a stone’s cast, and kneeled down, and prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> saying, Father, if thou be willing, remove this cup from me: nevertheless not my will, but thine, be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalms 91:11–12"> And there appeared an angel unto him from heaven, strengthening him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 22:12–21"> And being in an agony he prayed more earnestly: and his sweat was as it were great drops of blood falling down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> And when he rose up from prayer, and was come to his disciples, he found them sleeping for sorrow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> and said unto them, Why sleep ye? rise and pray, lest ye enter into temptation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> And while he yet spake, behold a multitude, and he that was called Judas, one of the twelve, went before them, and drew near unto Jesus to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> But Jesus said unto him, Judas, betrayest thou the Son of man with a kiss?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> When they which were about him saw what would follow, they said unto him, Lord, shall we smite with the sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> And one of them smote the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> And Jesus answered and said, Suffer ye thus far. And he touched his ear, and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> Then Jesus said unto the chief priests, and captains of the temple, and the elders, which were come to him, Be ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and staves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> When I was daily with you in the temple, ye stretched forth no hands against me: but this is your hour, and the power of darkness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> Then took they him, and led him, and brought him into the high priest’s house. And Peter followed afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the hall, and were set down together, Peter sat down among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> But a certain maid beheld him as he sat by the fire, and earnestly looked upon him, and said, This man was also with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> And he denied him, saying, Woman, I know him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> And after a little while another saw him, and said, Thou art also of them. And Peter said, Man, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> And about the space of one hour after another confidently affirmed, saying, Of a truth this fellow also was with him: for he is a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> And Peter said, Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately, while he yet spake, the cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> And the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> And Peter went out, and wept bitterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> And the men that held Jesus mocked him, and smote him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> And when they had blindfolded him, they stroke him on the face, and asked him, saying, Prophesy, who is it that smote thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> And many other things blasphemously spake they against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> And as soon as it was day, the elders of the people and the chief priests and the scribes came together, and led him into their council, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> Art thou the Christ? tell us. And he said unto them, If I tell you, you will not believe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> and if I also ask you, you will not answer me, nor let me go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Mark 16:19"> Hereafter shall the Son of man sit on the right hand of the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> Then said they all, Art thou then the Son of God? And he said unto them, Ye say that I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> And they said, What need we any further witness? for we ourselves have heard of his own mouth.</span>
