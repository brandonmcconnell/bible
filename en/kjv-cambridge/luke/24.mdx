<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> Now upon the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they came unto the sepulchre, bringing the spices which they had prepared, and certain others with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> And they found the stone rolled away from the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> And they entered in, and found not the body of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it came to pass, as they were much perplexed thereabout, behold, two men stood by them in shining garments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> and as they were afraid, and bowed down their faces to the earth, they said unto them, Why seek ye the living among the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake unto you when he was yet in Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> saying, The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembered his words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> and returned from the sepulchre, and told all these things unto the eleven, and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> It was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and other women that were with them, which told these things unto the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> And their words seemed to them as idle tales, and they believed them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> Then arose Peter, and ran unto the sepulchre; and stooping down, he beheld the linen clothes laid by themselves, and departed, wondering in himself at that which was come to pass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And behold, two of them went that same day to a village called Emmaus, which was from Jerusalem about threescore furlongs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> And they talked together of all these things which had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it came to pass, that while they communed together and reasoned, Jesus himself drew near, and went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were holden that they should not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And he said unto them, What manner of communications are these that ye have one to another, as ye walk, and are sad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And the one of them, whose name was Cleopas, answering said unto him, Art thou only a stranger in Jerusalem, and hast not known the things which are come to pass there in these days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And he said unto them, What things? And they said unto him, Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, which was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him to be condemned to death, and have crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we trusted that it had been he which should have redeemed Israel: and beside all this, to day is the third day since these things were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Yea, and certain women also of our company made us astonished, which were early at the sepulchre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and when they found not his body, they came, saying, that they had also seen a vision of angels, which said that he was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And certain of them which were with us went to the sepulchre, and found it even so as the women had said: but him they saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Then he said unto them, O fools, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and to enter into his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drew nigh unto the village, whither they went: and he made as though he would have gone further.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> But they constrained him, saying, Abide with us: for it is towards evening, and the day is far spent. And he went in to tarry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to pass, as he sat at meat with them, he took bread, and blessed it, and brake, and gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> And their eyes were opened, and they knew him; and he vanished out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said one to another, Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And they rose up the same hour, and returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and them that were with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, The Lord is risen indeed, and hath appeared to Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they told what things were done in the way, and how he was known of them in breaking of bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> And as they thus spake, Jesus himself stood in the midst of them, and saith unto them, Peace be unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But they were terrified and affrighted, and supposed that they had seen a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And he said unto them, Why are ye troubled? and why do thoughts arise in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Behold my hands and my feet, that it is I myself: handle me, and see; for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had thus spoken, he shewed them his hands and his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> And while they yet believed not for joy, and wondered, he said unto them, Have ye here any meat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gave him a piece of a broiled fish, and of a honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> And he took it, and did eat before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then opened he their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> and said unto them, Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise from the dead the third day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> And ye are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And behold, I send the promise of my Father upon you: but tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And he led them out as far as to Bethany, and he lift up his hands, and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them, and carried up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And they worshipped him, and returned to Jerusalem with great joy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.</span>
</div>
