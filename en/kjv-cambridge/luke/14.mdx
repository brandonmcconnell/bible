<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus answering spake unto the lawyers and Pharisees, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath day?</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> and answered them, saying, Which of you shall have an ass or an ox fallen into a pit, and will not straightway pull him out on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they could not answer him again to these things.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> When thou art bidden of any man to a wedding, sit not down in the highest room; lest a more honourable man than thou be bidden of him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> and he that bade thee and him come and say to thee, Give this man place; and thou begin with shame to take the lowest room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when thou art bidden, go and sit down in the lowest room; that when he that bade thee cometh, he may say unto thee, Friend, go up higher: then shalt thou have worship in the presence of them that sit at meat with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For whosoever exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> Then said he also to him that bade him, When thou makest a dinner or a supper, call not thy friends, nor thy brethren, neither thy kinsmen, nor thy rich neighbours; lest they also bid thee again, and a recompence be made thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But when thou makest a feast, call the poor, the maimed, the lame, the blind:</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> And when one of them that sat at meat with him heard these things, he said unto him, Blessed is he that shall eat bread in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> Then said he unto him, A certain man made a great supper, and bade many:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> and sent his servant at supper time to say to them that were bidden, Come; for all things are now ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> And they all with one consent began to make excuse. The first said unto him, I have bought a piece of ground, and I must needs go and see it: I pray thee have me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I go to prove them: I pray thee have me excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said, I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> So that servant came, and shewed his lord these things. Then the master of the house being angry said to his servant, Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in hither the poor, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the servant said, Lord, it is done as thou hast commanded, and yet there is room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> And the lord said unto the servant, Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I say unto you, That none of those men which were bidden shall taste of my supper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> If any man come to me, and hate not his father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brethren, and sisters, yea, and his own life also, he cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> And whosoever doth not bear his cross, and come after me, cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Lest haply, after he hath laid the foundation, and is not able to finish it, all that behold it begin to mock him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king, going to make war against another king, sitteth not down first, and consulteth whether he be able with ten thousand to meet him that cometh against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> Or else, while the other is yet a great way off, he sendeth an ambassage, and desireth conditions of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> So likewise, whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple.</span>
