<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> And it came to pass in those days, that there went out a decree from Cesar Augustus, that all the world should be taxed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> (And this taxing was first made when Cyrenius was governor of Syria.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And all went to be taxed, every one into his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, unto the city of David, which is called Bethlehem; (because he was of the house and lineage of David:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> to be taxed with Mary his espoused wife, being great with child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> And so it was that, while they were there, the days were accomplished that she should be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And there were in the same country shepherds abiding in the field, keeping watch over their flock by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And lo, the angel of the Lord came upon them, and the glory of the Lord shone round about them: and they were sore afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> And the angel said unto them, Fear not: for behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this shall be a sign unto you; Ye shall find the babe wrapped in swaddling clothes, lying in a manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host praising God, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Glory to God in the highest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And on earth peace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Good will towards men.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> And it came to pass, as the angels were gone away from them into heaven, the shepherds said one to another, Let us now go even unto Bethlehem, and see this thing which is come to pass, which the Lord hath made known unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> And they came with haste, and found Mary, and Joseph, and the babe lying in a manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> And when they had seen it, they made known abroad the saying which was told them concerning this child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> And all they that heard it wondered at those things which were told them by the shepherds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, as it was told unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> And when eight days were accomplished for the circumcising of the child, his name was called JESUS, which was so named of the angel before he was conceived in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> And when the days of her purification according to the law of Moses were accomplished, they brought him to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> (as it is written in the law of the Lord, Every male that openeth the womb shall be called holy to the Lord;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> and to offer a sacrifice according to that which is said in the law of the Lord, A pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem, whose name was Simeon; and the same man was just and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel: and the Holy Ghost was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> And it was revealed unto him by the Holy Ghost, that he should not see death, before he had seen the Lord’s Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> And he came by the Spirit into the temple: and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him after the custom of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> then took he him up in his arms, and blessed God, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, now lettest thou thy servant depart</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In peace, according to thy word:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For mine eyes have seen thy salvation,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Which thou hast prepared before the face of all people;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">A light to lighten the Gentiles, and the glory of thy people Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> And Joseph and his mother marvelled at those things which were spoken of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> And Simeon blessed them, and said unto Mary his mother, Behold, this child is set for the fall and rising again of many in Israel; and for a sign which shall be spoken against;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> (yea, a sword shall pierce through thy own soul also,) that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> And there was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Aser: she was of a great age, and had lived with a husband seven years from her virginity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and she was a widow of about fourscore and four years, which departed not from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> And she coming in that instant gave thanks likewise unto the Lord, and spake of him to all them that looked for redemption in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And when they had performed all things according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their own city Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> And the child grew, and waxed strong in spirit, filled with wisdom: and the grace of God was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> Now his parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when he was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem after the custom of the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> And when they had fulfilled the days, as they returned, the child Jesus tarried behind in Jerusalem; and Joseph and his mother knew not of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> But they, supposing him to have been in the company, went a day’s journey; and they sought him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And when they found him not, they turned back again to Jerusalem, seeking him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> And it came to pass, that after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the doctors, both hearing them, and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> And all that heard him were astonished at his understanding and answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> And when they saw him, they were amazed: and his mother said unto him, Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> And he said unto them, How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father’s business?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> And they understood not the saying which he spake unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> And he went down with them, and came to Nazareth, and was subject unto them: but his mother kept all these sayings in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man.</span>
