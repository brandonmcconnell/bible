<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A copy of an epistle, which Jeremie sent unto them which were to be led captives into Babylon by the king of the Babylonians, to certify them, as it was commanded him of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Because of the sins which ye have committed before God, ye shall be led away captives into Babylon by Nabuchodonosor king of the Babylonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So when ye be come unto Babylon, ye shall remain there many years, and for a long season, namely, seven generations: and after that I will bring you away peaceably from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Now shall ye see in Babylon gods of silver, and of gold, and of wood, borne upon shoulders, which cause the nations to fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Beware therefore that ye in no wise be like to strangers, neither be ye afraid of them, when ye see the multitude before them and behind them, worshipping them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But say ye in your hearts, O Lord, we must worship thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For mine angel is with you, and I myself caring for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> As for their tongue, it is polished by the workman, and they themselves are gilded and laid over with silver; yet are they but false, and cannot speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And taking gold, as it were for a virgin that loves to go gay, they make crowns for the heads of their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Sometimes also the priests convey from their gods gold and silver, and bestow it upon themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yea, they will give thereof to the common harlots, and deck them as men with garments, being gods of silver, and gods of gold, and wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Yet cannot these gods save themselves from rust and moths, though they be covered with purple raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They wipe their faces because of the dust of the temple, when there is much upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he that cannot put to death one that offendeth him holdeth a sceptre, as though he were a judge of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He hath also in his right hand a dagger and an axe: but cannot deliver himself from war and thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whereby they are known not to be gods: therefore fear them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For like as a vessel that a man useth is nothing worth when it is broken; even so it is with their gods: when they be set up in the temple, their eyes be full of dust through the feet of them that come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And as the doors are made sure on every side upon him that offendeth the king, as being committed to suffer death: even so the priests make fast their temples with doors, with locks, and bars, lest their gods be spoiled with robbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They light them candles, yea, more than for themselves, whereof they cannot see one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They are as one of the beams of the temple, yet they say their hearts are gnawed upon by things creeping out of the earth; and when they eat them and their clothes, they feel it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Their faces are blacked through the smoke that comes out of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Upon their bodies and heads sit bats, swallows, and birds, and the cats also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> By this you may know that they are no gods: therefore fear them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Notwithstanding the gold that is about them to make them beautiful, except they wipe off the rust, they will not shine: for neither when they were molten did they feel it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The things wherein there is no breath are bought for a most high price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They are borne upon shoulders, having no feet, whereby they declare unto men that they be nothing worth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> They also that serve them are ashamed: for if they fall to the ground at any time, they cannot rise up again of themselves: neither, if one set them upright, can they move of themselves: neither, if they be bowed down, can they make themselves straight: but they set gifts before them, as unto dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> As for the things that are sacrificed unto them, their priests sell and abuse; in like manner their wives lay up part thereof in salt; but unto the poor and impotent they give nothing of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Menstruous women and women in childbed eat their sacrifices: by these things ye may know that they are no gods: fear them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For how can they be called gods? because women set meat before the gods of silver, gold, and wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the priests sit in their temples, having their clothes rent, and their heads and beards shaven, and nothing upon their heads.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> They roar and cry before their gods, as men do at the feast when one is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> The priests also take off their garments, and clothe their wives and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Whether it be evil that one doeth unto them, or good, they are not able to recompense it: they can neither set up a king, nor put him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> In like manner, they can neither give riches nor money: though a man make a vow unto them, and keep it not, they will not require it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> They can save no man from death, neither deliver the weak from the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They cannot restore a blind man to his sight, nor help any man in his distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> They can shew no mercy to the widow, nor do good to the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Their gods of wood, and which are overlaid with gold and silver, are like the stones that be hewn out of the mountain: they that worship them shall be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> How should a man then think and say that they are gods?<br />When even the Chaldeans themselves dishonour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Who if they shall see one dumb that cannot speak, they bring him, and intreat Bel that he may speak, as though he were able to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Yet they cannot understand this themselves, and leave them: for they have no knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> The women also with cords about them, sitting in the ways, burn bran for perfume: but if any of them, drawn by some that passeth by, lie with him, she reproacheth her fellow, that she was not thought as worthy as herself, nor her cord broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Whatsoever is done among them is false: how may it then be thought or said that they are gods?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> They are made of carpenters and goldsmiths: they can be nothing else than the workmen will have them to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And they themselves that made them can never continue long; how should then the things that are made of them be gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> For they left lies and reproaches to them that come after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> For when there cometh any war or plague upon them, the priests consult with themselves, where they may be hidden with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> How then cannot men perceive that they be no gods, which can neither save themselves from war, nor from plague?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> For seeing they be but of wood, and overlaid with silver and gold, it shall be known hereafter that they are false:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> and it shall manifestly appear to all nations and kings that they are no gods, but the works of men’s hands, and that there is no work of God in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Who then may not know that they are no gods?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> For neither can they set up a king in the land, nor give rain unto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Neither can they judge their own cause, nor redress a wrong, being unable: for they are as crows between heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> Whereupon when fire falleth upon the house of gods of wood, or laid over with gold or silver, their priests will flee away, and escape; but they themselves shall be burnt asunder like beams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Moreover they cannot withstand any king or enemies: how can it then be thought or said that they be gods?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Neither are those gods of wood, and laid over with silver or gold, able to escape either from thieves or robbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Whose gold, and silver, and garments wherewith they are clothed, they that are strong do take, and go away withal: neither are they able to help themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Therefore it is better to be a king that sheweth his power, or else a profitable vessel in a house, which the owner shall have use of, than such false gods; or to be a door in a house, to keep such things safe as be therein, than such false gods; or a pillar of wood in a palace, than such false gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> For sun, moon, and stars, being bright, and sent to do their offices, are obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> In like manner the lightning when it breaketh forth is easy to be seen; and after the same manner the wind bloweth in every country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And when God commandeth the clouds to go over the whole world, they do as they are bidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And the fire sent from above to consume hills and woods doeth as it is commanded: but these are like unto them neither in shew nor power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> Wherefore it is neither to be supposed nor said that they are gods, seeing they are able neither to judge causes, nor to do good unto men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> Knowing therefore that they are no gods, fear them not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> For they can neither curse nor bless kings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> neither can they shew signs in the heavens among the heathen, nor shine as the sun, nor give light as the moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> The beasts are better than they: for they can get under a covert, and help themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> It is then by no means manifest unto us that they are gods: therefore fear them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> For as a scarecrow in a garden of cucumbers keepeth nothing: so are their gods of wood, and laid over with silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71"> And likewise their gods of wood, and laid over with silver and gold, are like to a white thorn in an orchard, that every bird sitteth upon; as also to a dead body, that is cast into the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72"> And you shall know them to be no gods by the bright purple that rotteth upon them: and they themselves afterward shall be eaten, and shall be a reproach in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73"> Better therefore is the just man that hath none idols: for he shall be far from reproach.</span>
</div>
