<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Joshua 2:1; Joshua 13:20; Numbers 25:1–9"> And they pitched by Jordan, from Beth-jesimoth even unto Abel-shittim in the plains of Moab.</span>

<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Judges 2:3; Psalms 106:34–36; Deuteronomy 7:16"> But if ye will not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you; then it shall come to pass, that those which ye let remain of them shall be pricks in your eyes, and thorns in your sides, and shall vex you in the land wherein ye dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 21:23–24; Deuteronomy 28:63; Leviticus 20:23"> Moreover it shall come to pass, that I shall do unto you, as I thought to do unto them.</span>
