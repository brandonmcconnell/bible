<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:11"> I speak not this to condemn you: for I have said before, that you are in our hearts to die and live with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 3:12; James 1:2"> Great is my boldness of speech toward you, great is my glorying of you: I am filled with comfort, I am exceeding joyful in all our tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Acts 20:1"> For, when we were come into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but we were troubled on every side; without were fightings, within were fears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> Nevertheless God, that comforteth those that are cast down, comforted us by the coming of Titus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> and not by his coming only, but by the consolation wherewith he was comforted in you, when he told us your earnest desire, your mourning, your fervent mind toward me; so that I rejoiced the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; Matthew 26:21–22"> For though I made you sorry with a letter, I do not repent, though I did repent: for I perceive that the same epistle hath made you sorry, though it were but for a season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; 2 Corinthians 7:10–11"> Now I rejoice, not that ye were made sorry, but that ye sorrowed to repentance: for ye were made sorry after a godly manner, that ye might receive damage by us in nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Acts 11:18; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of: but the sorrow of the world worketh death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> For behold this selfsame thing, that ye sorrowed after a godly sort, what carefulness it wrought in you, yea, what clearing of yourselves, yea, what indignation, yea, what fear, yea, what vehement desire, yea, what zeal, yea, what revenge! In all things ye have approved yourselves to be clear in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Timothy 3:5; 2 Corinthians 2:17"> Wherefore, though I wrote unto you, I did it not for his cause that had done the wrong, nor for his cause that suffered wrong, but that our care for you in the sight of God might appear unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> Therefore we were comforted in your comfort: yea, and exceedingly the more joyed we for the joy of Titus, because his spirit was refreshed by you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Thessalonians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 1:18–20"> For if I have boasted any thing to him of you, I am not ashamed; but as we spake all things to you in truth, even so our boasting, which I made before Titus, is found a truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 John 3:17"> And his inward affection is more abundant toward you, whilst he remembereth the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling you received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:3; Philemon 1:8; Philemon 1:21"> I rejoice therefore that I have confidence in you in all things.</span>
