<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Not long after this the king sent an old man of Athens to compel the Jews to depart from the laws of their fathers, and not to live after the laws of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and to pollute also the temple in Jerusalem, and to call it the temple of Jupiter Olympius; and that in Garizim, of Jupiter the Defender of strangers, as they did desire that dwelt in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The coming in of this mischief was sore and grievous to the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> for the temple was filled with riot and revelling by the Gentiles, who dallied with harlots, and had to do with women within the circuit of the holy places, and besides that, brought in things that were not lawful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The altar also was filled with profane things, which the law forbiddeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Neither was it lawful for a man to keep sabbath days or ancient feasts, or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And in the day of the king’s birth every month they were brought by bitter constraint to eat of the sacrifices; and when the feast of Bacchus was kept, the Jews were compelled to go in procession to Bacchus, carrying ivy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Moreover there went out a decree to the neighbour cities of the heathen, by the suggestion of Ptolemee, against the Jews, that they should observe the same fashions, and be partakers of their sacrifices:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and whoso would not conform themselves to the manners of the Gentiles should be put to death. Then might a man have seen the present misery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For there were two women brought, who had circumcised their children; whom when they had openly led round about the city, the babes hanging at their breasts, they cast them down headlong from the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And others, that had run together into caves near by, to keep the sabbath day secretly, being discovered to Philip, were all burnt together, because they made a conscience to help themselves for the honour of the most sacred day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Now I beseech those that read this book, that they be not discouraged for these calamities, but that they judge those punishments not to be for destruction, but for a chastening of our nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For it is a token of his great goodness, when wicked doers are not suffered any long time, but forthwith punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For not as with other nations, whom the Lord patiently forbeareth to punish, till they be come to the fulness of their sins, so dealeth he with us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> lest that, being come to the height of sin, afterwards he should take vengeance of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And therefore he never withdraweth his mercy from us: and though he punish with adversity, yet doth he never forsake his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But let this that we have spoken be for a warning unto us. And now will we come to the declaring of the matter in few words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Eleazar, one of the principal scribes, an aged man, and of a well favoured countenance, was constrained to open his mouth, and to eat swine’s flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But he, choosing rather to die gloriously, than to live stained with such an abomination, spit it forth, and came of his own accord to the torment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> as it behoved them to come, that are resolute to stand out against such things, as are not lawful for love of life to be tasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But they that had the charge of that wicked feast, for the old acquaintance they had with the man, taking him aside, besought him to bring flesh of his own provision, such as was lawful for him to use, and make as if he did eat of the flesh taken from the sacrifice commanded by the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> that in so doing he might be delivered from death, and for the old friendship with them find favour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But he began to consider discreetly, and as became his age, and the excellency of his ancient years, and the honour of his gray head, whereunto he was come, and his most honest education from a child, or rather the holy law made and given by God: therefore he answered accordingly, and willed them straightways to send him to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For it becometh not our age, said he, in any wise to dissemble, whereby many young persons might think that Eleazar, being fourscore years old and ten, were now gone to a strange religion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and so they through mine hypocrisy, and desire to live a little time and a moment longer, should be deceived by me, and I get a stain to mine old age, and make it abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For though for the present time I should be delivered from the punishment of men: yet should I not escape the hand of the Almighty, neither alive, nor dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Wherefore now, manfully changing this life, I will shew myself such a one as mine age requireth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and leave a notable example to such as be young to die willingly and courageously for the honourable and holy laws. And when he had said these words, immediately he went to the torment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> they that led him changing the good will they bare him a little before into hatred, because the foresaid speeches proceeded, as they thought, from a desperate mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> But when he was ready to die with stripes, he groaned, and said, It is manifest unto the Lord, that hath the holy knowledge, that whereas I might have been delivered from death, I now endure sore pains in body by being beaten: but in soul am well content to suffer these things, because I fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And thus this man died, leaving his death for an example of a noble courage, and a memorial of virtue, not only unto young men, but unto all his nation.</span>
</div>
