<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:40; Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 24:18"> And when the people saw that Moses delayed to come down out of the mount, the people gathered themselves together unto Aaron, and said unto him, Up, make us gods, which shall go before us; for as for this Moses, the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we wot not what is become of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:24–27; Exodus 12:35–36; Ezekiel 16:17"> And Aaron said unto them, Break off the golden earrings, which are in the ears of your wives, of your sons, and of your daughters, and bring them unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Isaiah 40:19–20; Jeremiah 10:9"> And all the people brake off the golden earrings which were in their ears, and brought them unto Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16; 1 Kings 12:28"> And he received them at their hand, and fashioned it with a graving tool, after he had made it a molten calf: and they said, These be thy gods, O Israel, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> And when Aaron saw it, he built an altar before it; and Aaron made proclamation, and said, To morrow is a feast to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:7; Numbers 25:2; Acts 7:41–42"> And they rose up early on the morrow, and offered burnt offerings, and brought peace offerings; and the people sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up to play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Hosea 9:9; Judges 2:19"> And the Lord said unto Moses, Go, get thee down; for thy people, which thou broughtest out of the land of Egypt, have corrupted themselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> they have turned aside quickly out of the way which I commanded them: they have made them a molten calf, and have worshipped it, and have sacrificed thereunto, and said, These be thy gods, O Israel, which have brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:13; Isaiah 48:4"> And the Lord said unto Moses, I have seen this people, and behold, it is a stiffnecked people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> now therefore let me alone, that my wrath may wax hot against them, and that I may consume them: and I will make of thee a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; Psalms 106:23"> And Moses besought the Lord his God, and said, Lord, why doth thy wrath wax hot against thy people, which thou hast brought forth out of the land of Egypt with great power, and with a mighty hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> Wherefore should the Egyptians speak, and say, For mischief did he bring them out, to slay them in the mountains, and to consume them from the face of the earth? Turn from thy fierce wrath, and repent of this evil against thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, thy servants, to whom thou swarest by thine own self, and saidst unto them, I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and all this land that I have spoken of will I give unto your seed, and they shall inherit it for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:10; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 26:13"> And the Lord repented of the evil which he thought to do unto his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And Moses turned, and went down from the mount, and the two tables of the Testimony were in his hand: the tables were written on both their sides; on the one side and on the other were they written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> And the tables were the work of God, and the writing was the writing of God, graven upon the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> And when Joshua heard the noise of the people as they shouted, he said unto Moses, There is a noise of war in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> And he said, It is not the voice of them that shout for mastery, neither is it the voice of them that cry for being overcome: but the noise of them that sing do I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremiah 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> And it came to pass, as soon as he came nigh unto the camp, that he saw the calf, and the dancing: and Moses’ anger waxed hot, and he cast the tables out of his hands, and brake them beneath the mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Kings 23:6; 2 Kings 23:15"> And he took the calf which they had made, and burnt it in the fire, and ground it to powder, and strawed it upon the water, and made the children of Israel drink of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; 1 Kings 21:22"> And Moses said unto Aaron, What did this people unto thee, that thou hast brought so great a sin upon them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> And Aaron said, Let not the anger of my lord wax hot: thou knowest the people, that they are set on mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> For they said unto me, Make us gods, which shall go before us: for as for this Moses, the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we wot not what is become of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> And I said unto them, Whosoever hath any gold, let them break it off. So they gave it me: then I cast it into the fire, and there came out this calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> And when Moses saw that the people were naked; (for Aaron had made them naked unto their shame amongst their enemies:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Matthew 12:30; 2 Samuel 20:11"> then Moses stood in the gate of the camp, and said, Who is on the Lord’s side? let him come unto me. And all the sons of Levi gathered themselves together unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> And he said unto them, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Put every man his sword by his side, and go in and out from gate to gate throughout the camp, and slay every man his brother, and every man his companion, and every man his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachi 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> And the children of Levi did according to the word of Moses: and there fell of the people that day about three thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zechariah 13:3"> For Moses had said, Consecrate yourselves to day to the Lord, even every man upon his son, and upon his brother; that he may bestow upon you a blessing this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:20; Numbers 25:13; 2 Kings 17:21"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that Moses said unto the people, Ye have sinned a great sin: and now I will go up unto the Lord; peradventure I shall make an atonement for your sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> And Moses returned unto the Lord, and said, Oh, this people have sinned a great sin, and have made them gods of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> Yet now, if thou wilt forgive their sin; and if not, blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book which thou hast written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Ezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> And the Lord said unto Moses, Whosoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> Therefore now go, lead the people unto the place of which I have spoken unto thee: behold, mine Angel shall go before thee: nevertheless in the day when I visit, I will visit their sin upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> And the Lord plagued the people, because they made the calf, which Aaron made.</span>
