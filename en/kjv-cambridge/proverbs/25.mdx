<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is the glory of God to conceal a thing:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the honour of kings is to search out a matter.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heaven for height, and the earth for depth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the heart of kings is unsearchable.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take away the dross from the silver,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And there shall come forth a vessel for the finer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take away the wicked from before the king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And his throne shall be established in righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Put not forth thyself in the presence of the king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And stand not in the place of great men:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For better it is that it be said unto thee, Come up hither;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than that thou shouldest be put lower in the presence of the prince</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Whom thine eyes have seen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go not forth hastily to strive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest thou know not what to do in the end thereof,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When thy neighbour hath put thee to shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Debate thy cause with thy neighbour himself;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And discover not a secret to another:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lest he that heareth it put thee to shame,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And thine infamy turn not away.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A word fitly spoken</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is like apples of gold in pictures of silver.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">As an earring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is a wise reprover upon an obedient ear.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">As the cold of snow in the time of harvest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is a faithful messenger to them that send him:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For he refresheth the soul of his masters.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoso boasteth himself of a false gift</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is like clouds and wind without rain.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">By long forbearing is a prince persuaded,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And a soft tongue breaketh the bone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hast thou found honey? eat so much as is sufficient for thee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest thou be filled therewith, and vomit it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Withdraw thy foot from thy neighbour’s house;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest he be weary of thee, and so hate thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">A man that beareth false witness against his neighbour</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is a maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Confidence in an unfaithful man in time of trouble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is like a broken tooth, and a foot out of joint.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">As he that taketh away a garment in cold weather,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And as vinegar upon nitre,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is he that singeth songs to a heavy heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And if he be thirsty, give him water to drink:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the Lord shall reward thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The north wind driveth away rain:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So doth an angry countenance a backbiting tongue.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Than with a brawling woman and in a wide house.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">As cold waters to a thirsty soul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So is good news from a far country.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">A righteous man falling down before the wicked</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is as a troubled fountain, and a corrupt spring.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not good to eat much honey:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So for men to search their own glory is not glory.</span><br /></span>
