<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> So then, my son, grow strong in the grace of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> Take what you heard me say in front of many witnesses and share it with those who are trustworthy, who then can teach others as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Suffer together with me like a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> A soldier on active duty doesn't get caught up in the business of everyday life. He wants to please the one who enlisted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Similarly athletes who compete in the games don't win a prize if they don't keep to the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The farmer that does all the hard work should be the first to benefit from the crop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Think about what I'm telling you. The Lord will help you to understand all this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Focus your mind on Jesus Christ, a descendant of David, who was raised from the dead. This is my good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> and I'm suffering imprisonment because of it as if I were a criminal—but the word of God is not imprisoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Despite all this I'm willing to keep on going for the sake of God's people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “the elect.”">a</a></sup>so that they may gain the salvation of Christ Jesus that is eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> This is a trustworthy saying: “If we died with him, we will also live with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> if we keep going, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he will also deny us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> If we are not trustworthy, he is still trustworthy, for he cannot be untrue to himself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> This is what you should remind people about, telling them before God that they shouldn't have meaningless arguments over words. To do this only harms those who listen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Make sure you work hard so you can present yourself to God and gain his approval. Be a worker that doesn't need to be embarrassed, using the word of truth correctly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> Avoid pointless chatter because people who do this are heading away from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> Their teachings are as destructive as gangrene that destroys healthy flesh. Hymenaeus and Philetus are like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> They have deviated from the truth by saying that the resurrection has already happened, which has ruined some people's trust in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> But God's solid, trustworthy foundation stone stands firm, with this inscription, “The Lord knows those who belong to him,” and “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord should stay away from all that is evil.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Numbers 16:5.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> A stately home doesn't just have cups and bowls<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “vessels” or “utensils.” There seems to be no good English equivalent for “home containers.”">c</a></sup>made of gold and silver. It also has ones made from wood and clay. Some are made for special use; others for more commonplace functions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> So if you get rid of what is evil, you will become a cup or bowl that is holy and special, useful to the Lord, ready to do all that's good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> Run away from anything that fires up the desires of youth. Run after what is right and trustworthy, run after love and peace along with those who are true and pure Christians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> Avoid foolish and childish debates, for you know they only lead to fights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> For the Lord's minister must not fight, but be kind to everyone, able to teach, patient,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> gently correcting opponents. Perhaps God may help them to come to repent and to understand the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; PSALMS 124:7"> In this way they may come to their senses and escape the devil's trap. For he has captured them and made them do whatever he wants.</span>
</div>
</div>
