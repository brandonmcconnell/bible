<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> Be aware that there will be troubled times in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:21; Romans 1:29–31; Jude 1:16"> People will love themselves and love money. They will be boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, and without God in their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; Romans 1:31; Matthew 10:21"> Heartless and unforgiving, they'll commit slander and have no self-control. Brutal people who hate what's good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; 1 Timothy 3:6; Jude 1:4"> they will betray others and be totally thoughtless. They are absurdly full of themselves, living so much for pleasure that they don't care about loving God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 23:27–28"> They may give an outward impression of being religious, but they don't actually believe it works. Stay away from these people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 3:17; Titus 3:3"> They're the kind of people that slip into homes and take control of vulnerable women who are burdened down by the guilt of sin and distracted by all kinds of desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 2:4"> These women are always trying to learn but they're never able to understand the truth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:8–11; Revelation 2:20; 1 Timothy 6:5"> Just like Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, these teachers also oppose the truth. They are people with corrupted minds whose supposed trust in God is a lie.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “whose trust in God is counterfeit.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they won't get far. Their stupidity will be obvious to everyone, just like that of Jannes and Jambres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Titus 2:7; 2 Peter 1:5–7"> But you know all about my teaching and behavior, and my aim in life. You know my trust in God and my love. You know what I've had to endure—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28"> how I've been persecuted and what I've suffered. You know what happened to me at Antioch, at Iconium, and at Lystra—the troubles I had and how the Lord rescued me from all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:12–16; 1 Peter 2:20–21"> Of course it's true that everyone who wants to live a life devoted to God in Christ Jesus will experience persecution,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1; 2 Peter 2:20; Titus 3:3"> while wicked people and frauds will do well, going from bad to worse, deceiving others and deceiving themselves too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 2 Timothy 2:2; 2 Timothy 1:13"> But you must hold onto the things you've learned and are convinced are true. You know who taught you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 119:98–99; Luke 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> From your childhood you've known the holy Scriptures which can give you understanding for salvation by trusting in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Hebrews 4:12; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> All Scripture inspired by God is useful for teaching, for confronting what is wrong, for setting us straight, and for telling us what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:21; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> This is how God provides a thorough preparation for those who work for him to accomplish all that's good.</span>
