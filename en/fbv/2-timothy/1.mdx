<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; John 17:3; John 10:28"> This letter comes from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus chosen by God, sent to tell about the promise of real life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Greek simply uses the word for life, but Paul is surely referring here to the abundant life that relates to eternal life (see 1 Timothy 1:16).">a</a></sup>that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:1; 1 Timothy 1:2; Acts 16:1"> I'm sending it to you Timothy, my dear son. May you have grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Hebrews 13:8; Acts 23:1"> I'm always thinking of you and I'm so thankful to God, whom I serve as my ancestors did, with a clear conscience. I never forget to mention you in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–1"> I remember how you cried, and I so want to see you! That would make me really happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Acts 16:1; 2 Timothy 3:15"> I keep in mind your sincere trust in God, the same trust that your grandmother Lois and mother Eunice also had—and I know that same trust continues in you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8"> That's why I want to remind you to revitalize God's gracious gift to you which you received when I placed my hands on you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Paul clearly “laid his hands” on Timothy in a way to designate a special blessing.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:18; Galatians 5:22"> God didn't give us a spirit that makes us fearful, but a spirit of power and love and good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Mark 8:38; 1 Peter 4:13–15"> So don't be ashamed to tell others about our Lord, or be ashamed of me. Instead be ready to share in suffering for the good news as God gives you strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; Titus 3:4–5; Ephesians 1:3–4"> He is the one who has saved us and called us to live a holy life—not through what we do, but by God's own plan and through his grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:2; Romans 16:26; 2 Peter 3:18"> He gave this grace to us in Christ Jesus before the beginning of time, and is now revealed in the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus. He destroyed death, making life and immortality brilliantly clear through the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:7–8; Acts 9:15"> I was appointed speaker, apostle, and teacher of this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; Jude 1:24; 1 Peter 4:19"> That's also the reason I'm suffering all this, but I'm not ashamed, because I know whom I've trusted. I'm confident that he can look after what I've entrusted to him until the Day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Day,” referring to the end-time Day of Judgment.">c</a></sup>he returns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 1:9; 2 Timothy 3:14; Philippians 4:9"> You should follow the model of good advice that you learned from me, with an attitude of trust and love in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:17; Romans 8:13; 1 Timothy 6:20"> Guard the truth that was entrusted to you through the Holy Spirit who lives in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:16; Philippians 2:21; Acts 19:10"> You already know that everyone from Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Roman province of Asia Minor (now in modern-day Turkey).">d</a></sup>has abandoned me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:19; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> May the Lord be kind to the family of Onesiphorus, because he often took care of me and wasn't embarrassed about me being in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 28:30–31"> When he was in Rome, he took the trouble to search for me, and he found me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Timothy 1:3"> May the Lord grant him his blessing in the Day of Judgment. (Timothy, you're very much aware of how much Onesiphorus did for me when he was in Ephesus.)</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
