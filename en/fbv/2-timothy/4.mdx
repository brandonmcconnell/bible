<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> This is what I instruct you to do before God and Christ Jesus who will judge the living and the dead when he comes to establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> Speak God's word whether it is convenient or not, tell people where they're going wrong, warn them, encourage them. Do this with a great deal of patient teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> For the time is coming when people won't bother to listen to true teaching. Instead they will be curious to listen to something different,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “having itching ears.”">a</a></sup>and will surround themselves with teachers who'll tell them what they want to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> They will stop listening to the truth and wander off following myths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> You need to keep your wits about you at all times. Put up with troubles, do the work of sharing the good news, fulfill your ministry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> For I'm about to be sacrificed—the time of my death is approaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept my trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> Now there's a prize reserved for me—the crown for a life lived according to what is good and right. The Lord (who is the judge that always does what's right), will give that to me on that Day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See note on 1:12.">b</a></sup>And not just to me, but to everyone who longs for his coming.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Please try to come to me as soon as you can.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> Demas has abandoned me because he loves the things of this world more, and has gone to Thessalonica. Crescens has gone to Galatia, and Titus to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> Only Luke is here with me. Bring Mark with you, because he can help me with my work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> When you come please bring the cloak I left behind with Carpus in Troas—and the books, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; PSALMS 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith caused me a lot of trouble—may God judge him for what he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> You watch out for him too, because he was very much against what we were saying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; John 16:32; Mark 14:50"> When it came to my first defense,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referring to a judicial trial.">c</a></sup>no one stood up for me—everyone abandoned me. May they not be blamed for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord stood by me and gave me strength so that I could declare the whole of the message so that all the foreigners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">d</a></sup>could hear it. I was rescued out of the lion's mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Jude 1:24–25"> The Lord will rescue me from all the evil things done against me, and will bring me safely to his heavenly kingdom. His is the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> My greetings to Prisca<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Called Priscilla in Acts 18:2.">e</a></sup>and Aquila, and to Onesiphorus's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus stayed on in Corinth. I left Trophimus behind in Miletus because he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 John 1:14"> Please try to come before winter. Eubulus sends his best wishes—and Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers and sisters too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> The Lord be with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “be with your spirit.”">f</a></sup>May his grace be with you all.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
