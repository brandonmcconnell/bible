<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jonah 1:1; John 21:15–17"> Then the Lord spoke to Jonah for a second time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Jeremiah 1:17; Jonah 3:3"> “Go immediately to the great city of Nineveh and announce the message I'm giving you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–29; PSALMS 80:10; Genesis 22:3"> Jonah did what God told him. He set out and went to Nineveh, a city that was so big<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “great to God.”">a</a></sup>it took three days to walk through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremiah 18:7–10; 2 Kings 20:1"> Jonah went into the city, walking for one day, shouting out, “In forty days Nineveh will be destroyed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Matthew 12:41; Joel 2:12–17"> The people of Nineveh believed in God. They announced a fast, and all of them, from the greatest to the least, put on sackcloth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="To show their repentance.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:6–10; Esther 4:1–4; Daniel 9:3"> When the news of what was happening reached the king of Nineveh he came down from his throne, took off his robe, put on sackcloth, and sat in ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:15–16"> Then the king and the nobles issued a proclamation throughout Nineveh: “No person, no animal, no herd, and no flock, shall eat or drink anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Isaiah 55:6–7; Ezekiel 18:21–24"> Every person and every animal is to wear sackcloth. Everyone is to pray sincerely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “with force.”">c</a></sup>to God, give up the evil things they do, and stop using violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:22; Joel 2:13–14; PSALMS 106:45"> Who knows? God may change his mind and relent. He may decide not to destroy us in his fierce anger.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; 1 Kings 21:27–29; Luke 15:20"> God saw what they had done—that they had given up their evil ways—so he changed his mind and did not carry out the destruction he had threatened.</span>
</div>
</div>
