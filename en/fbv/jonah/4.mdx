<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> The Lord God had a plant grow up and provide shade over Jonah's head to ease his discomfort. Jonah was very happy with the plant.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; PSALMS 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> Then as the sun came up the Lord arranged for a scorching east wind to blow, and the sun beat down on Jonah's head so that he became faint and wanted to die. “I'd rather die than live!” he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> But the Lord asked Jonah, “Do you have a good reason to be so angry about the plant?”<br />“Yes I do!” Jonah replied. “I'm angry enough to die!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> Shouldn't I be concerned about the great city of Nineveh where one hundred and twenty thousand people live who don't know their right hand from their left,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In other words they are spiritually ignorant.">e</a></sup>not to mention all the animals?”</span>
