<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17–18; Philippians 4:6; Ephesians 6:18"> Remember to keep on praying, with an alert and thankful mind!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 14:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:1–2"> Pray for us too that God may open a door of opportunity to spread the message, to tell about the revealed mystery of Christ—which is the reason I'm here in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 4:6"> Pray that I may make it as clear as I should when I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15–17; 1 Thessalonians 4:12; James 3:13"> Behave wisely with outsiders, making the best use of every opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; 1 Peter 3:15; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Always be gracious when you speak. Make sure it's in good taste, and think about how best to answer everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> Tychicus will tell you all about what's happening to me. He's a dear brother, a trustworthy minister and co-worker in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> I'm sending him to you for this very reason—so that you'll know how things are with me and this will cheer you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> Onesimus is going with him too, a trusted and dear brother, who's one of you. They'll explain to you everything that's going on here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> Aristarchus who's with me here in prison sends his best wishes; likewise Mark, Barnabas' cousin (you've already had instructions to welcome him if he visits you),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> and Jesus—also called Justus—who are the only Jewish Christians<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “belonging to the circumcision.”">a</a></sup>among those working with me here for the kingdom of God, men who have been a great help to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Epaphras who is one of you and a servant of Christ Jesus, sends his greetings. He's always passionate in his prayers on your behalf, praying that you'll stand firm as grown-up Christians, totally convinced about everything as God would want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> For the record I can tell you that he's done a lot for you, and for those in Laodicea and Hierapolis as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Luke, our dear doctor, and Demas also send their best.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Greet the believers that are in Laodicea—Nympha too, and the church that meets in her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> And when this letter has been read to you, make sure it's read to the church in Laodicea too, and that you also read the letter sent to Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> Tell Archippus, “See that you follow through in doing the ministry God gave you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; 2 Timothy 4:22; 1 Timothy 6:21"> I, Paul, write my closing greetings with my own hand. Remember I'm in prison. Grace be with you.</span>
</div>
