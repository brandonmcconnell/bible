<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:29; Philippians 1:30; Revelation 3:14–22"> I want you to know how hard I'm working for you, and for those at Laodicea—in fact for all those who haven't met me personally—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; Colossians 4:8; Ephesians 6:22"> so that you may be encouraged. May you be bound together in love, experiencing the great benefit of being completely sure in your understanding, for this is what the true knowledge of God brings. May you know the revealed mystery of God, which is Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Romans 11:33"> In him you can discover all the rich wisdom and knowledge of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Ephesians 4:14; Romans 16:18–19"> I'm telling you this so that no one will fool you by spinning you a tale.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “false arguments.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> Even though I'm not physically there with you, I'm with you in spirit. I'm so happy to see the way you stick together and how firm you are in your trust in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 1 John 2:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:1"> Just as you accepted Christ Jesus as Lord, go on following him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Ephesians 2:20–22"> grounded in him and built up by him. May your trust in him continue to grow strong, following what you were taught, full of gratitude to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:20; Ephesians 5:6; Matthew 7:15"> Watch out that nobody enslaves you through their philosophy and worthless delusions, following human traditions and concepts of this world, and not following Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; John 10:30; John 1:14"> For the fullness of God's divine nature lives in Christ in bodily form,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:19; Colossians 1:16–18; Ephesians 1:20–23"> and you have been made full<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “complete,” “whole.”">b</a></sup>in him. He is supreme over every ruler and authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 2:29; Philippians 3:3; Romans 6:6"> You were “circumcised” in him but not by human hands. You have been set free from sinful human nature by the “circumcision” Christ performed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Clearly this is not talking about physical circumcision but what it was supposed to mean spiritually.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:3–5; 1 Peter 3:21; Galatians 3:27"> You were buried with him in baptism, and you were raised with him through your trust in what God did by raising him from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5–6; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 2:1"> And even though you were dead because of your sins and being physically “uncircumcised,” he brought you to life together with him. He has forgiven us all our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Ephesians 2:14–16; Isaiah 43:25"> He wiped out the record of our debts according to the Law that was written down against us; he took away this barrier<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Note that the barrier is on our side, not God's.">d</a></sup>by nailing it to the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; Ephesians 4:8; Matthew 12:29"> He stripped away the power of spiritual rulers and authorities,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Clearly speaking about evil spiritual agencies.">e</a></sup>and having publicly revealed what they were truly like, he led them captive behind him in victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 14:5–6; Romans 14:10; Galatians 4:10"> So don't let anyone criticize you for what you eat or what you drink, or regarding which religious festival, new moon ritual, or ceremonial sabbaths you choose to observe.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="In this context Paul is referring to the various yearly festival days of the Jewish ceremonial system.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 8:5; John 1:17"> These are just a shadow of what was to come, for the physical reality is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 1 John 4:1–2; Colossians 2:23"> Don't let anyone cheat you out of your prize by insisting you have to beat yourself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Self-mortification; the phrase could also be translated “false humility.”">g</a></sup>or worship angels. They think they are better than anyone else because of visions they say they've had, and become ridiculously conceited in their sinful minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:15–16; John 15:4–6; 2 Peter 3:18"> Such people are not connected to the head that directs the body, nourished and joined together through the body's sinews and muscles. As the body is united together it grows the way God wants it to grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Colossians 2:16; Galatians 4:3"> If you died with Christ to the religious demands that this world insists upon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Probably referring both to Jewish religious regulations and to pagan ceremonies and taboos.">h</a></sup>why would you make yourself subject to such demands as if you were still part of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; Genesis 3:3; Isaiah 52:11"> Things like: don't handle that, don't taste that, don't touch that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:14; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> These commands refer to things that don't last since they're used up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This tends to indicate that the rules are about food and drink which will of course be consumed and then “used up.”">i</a></sup>and they're based on man-made requirements and teachings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:8; Colossians 2:18; Colossians 2:8"> Such rules may make some kind of sense to those who practice self-centered piety, who are so proud of being humble, and who “mortify the body;” but in reality they don't help at all in dealing with sinful desires.</span>
</div>
