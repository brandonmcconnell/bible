<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 8:6; Matthew 6:33; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> So if you've been brought back to life with Christ, look for what comes from above, where Christ is, sitting at God's right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; Colossians 3:1"> Fix your mind on what's above, not what's here on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:2; Colossians 2:20"> You died, and your life is kept safe with Christ in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:25; 1 John 3:2; Galatians 2:20"> When Christ—your life—is revealed, then you will also share in his visible glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:19–21; Galatians 5:24"> So kill your worldly nature—sexual sin, immorality, lust, evil desires, greedily wanting to have more—this is the worship of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18; 1 Peter 1:14"> Because of such things, those who disobey experience God's judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:2; 1 Peter 4:3–4; Romans 6:19–20"> At one time you lived like that when you behaved in such a way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Ephesians 4:22; James 1:20–21"> but now you should get rid of such things as anger, rage, wickedness, abuse, and using obscenities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Don't lie to each other, since you've discarded your old self and what you used to do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 2:10"> and put on your new self that is always being made more like your Creator, understanding better who he really is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 10:12"> In this new situation there's no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, foreigner, barbarian,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Scythian,” a “barbarian” people from what is now southern Russia, the Ukraine, and Central Asia.">a</a></sup>slave or free, for Christ is everything, and he lives in all of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Ephesians 4:2; 1 John 4:19"> Since you are God's special people, holy and dearly loved, adopt a sympathetic nature that is kind, humble, gentle, and patient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; James 2:13"> Be patient with one another, forgive others among you if you have grievances against one another. Just as the Lord forgave you, you should do the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 13:1–13"> Above all, love one another, which is the perfect bond that will hold you together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> May the peace of Christ direct your thinking, because you were called to this by God who makes you one, and thank God for it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 119:11; Ephesians 5:19; John 15:7"> Let Christ's message fully live in you. In every wise way teach and instruct one another through psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing praises to God in gratitude and sincerity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> Whatever you do, whether in word or action, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, praising God the Father through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:22–9; Titus 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> You married women, respect your husbands appropriately in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; Colossians 3:21"> You married men, love your wives and don't treat them badly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> You children, always do what your parents tell you because this is what pleases the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:4; PSALMS 103:13"> You fathers, don't make your children mad, so they won't feel like giving up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:5–7; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Galatians 1:10"> Those of you who are servants, do everything your human masters tell you, not with just an eye to please them, but honestly and sincerely, respecting the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Ephesians 6:6–7; Romans 14:8"> Do whatever you do really well, as if you're doing it for God, and not for people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8; Galatians 1:10; Acts 20:32"> because you know that the Lord will give you your reward—an inheritance! You're serving Christ the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Whoever does what's wrong will be paid back for the wrong they've done, and God has no favorites.</span>
