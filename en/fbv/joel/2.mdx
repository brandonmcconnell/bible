<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:15; Joel 1:15; 1 Peter 4:7"> Blow the trumpet in Zion! Sound the alarm on my holy mountain! Let all who live in the land tremble for the day of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The day of the Lord is the time of God's judgment.">a</a></sup>is coming—it's definitely near!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:6; Zephaniah 1:14–15; Joel 2:10–11"> It's a gloomy day of darkness, a day of thick clouds and heavy shadows. Like the dawn spreading over the mountains, an army appears,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="While the initial focus in Joel is on a devastating plague of locusts, what follows in chapter 2 morphs into a picture of an invading human army.">b</a></sup>so large and powerful that there has never ever been anything like it before, nor will there ever be anything like it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 1:19–20; Genesis 2:8"> Fire blazes in front of them, behind them flames are burning. In front of them the land looks like the Garden of Eden, behind them is a desert of total desolation: not a single survivor remains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:7"> They have the appearance of horses; they charge like cavalry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:9; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 30:30"> Listen to the sound: they are like rumbling chariots leaping over the mountain tops, they are like crackling flames burning stubble, they are like a mighty army marching in formation into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 30:6"> Terror seizes all those in their path; all the people's faces grow pale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 30:27; 2 Samuel 1:23; 2 Samuel 2:18–19"> They attack like mighty warriors; they scale walls like soldiers. They all march as one, never breaking ranks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:10; Job 33:18; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> They do not jostle one another, they stay on track; and even if some are cut down, they are not stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; John 10:1; Exodus 10:6"> They rush to and fro in the city; they run along the walls;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The fact that they can do this shows that the city's defenders are dead.">c</a></sup>they climb into houses, entering through the windows like thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Isaiah 13:10; Joel 3:15–16"> The earth quakes before them, the heavens tremble, the sun and the moon grow dark, the stars stop shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 30:7"> The Lord shouts his commands at the head of his army. His camp is immense, and those who carry out his orders are powerful. The day of the Lord is totally terrifying—who can stand it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:8–9; Isaiah 55:6–7; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> “Yet even now,” says the Lord, “Come back to me while there is still time. Come to me with all your heart, with fasting and prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 86:15; PSALMS 34:18; Exodus 34:6–7"> Repent in your minds, and not by tearing your clothes.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Tearing one's clothes was a sign of intense emotion, grief, and repentance.">d</a></sup>Come back to the Lord your God for he is gracious and kind. He is slow to get angry and full of faithful love, and changes his mind about sending punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Haggai 2:19; 2 Kings 19:4"> Who knows? Maybe he will change his mind, leaving behind a blessing so that you will be able to make grain and wine offerings to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Joel 2:1; 1 Kings 21:12"> Blow the trumpet in Zion! Proclaim a fast; call a solemn assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 20:13; 1 Corinthians 7:5"> Gather the people together: the elders, the children, even the babies. Let the bridegroom leave his room; let the bride leave her room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 115:2; Ezekiel 8:16; PSALMS 79:10"> Let the priests, the ministers of the Lord, weep between the porch and the altar of the Temple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Of the Temple”: implied.">e</a></sup>Let them say, “Lord, please take pity on your people, and do not let your inheritance be disgraced, ruled over by foreign nations, so that the people of these nations could ask, ‘Where is their God?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; PSALMS 103:13; Zechariah 1:14"> Now the Lord is very protective<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “jealous.”">f</a></sup>of his land and takes pity on his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Ezekiel 36:15; Isaiah 62:8–9"> The Lord will reply, saying to his people, “Look! I am sending you grain and new wine and olive oil, and you will be satisfied. You will no longer be a disgrace among the foreign nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 14:8; Amos 4:10; Isaiah 34:3"> I will remove from you the army from the north; I will drive them into the desolate wilderness—the front into the eastern sea, and the rear into the western sea. The stench of the dead army<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Of the dead army”: implied.">g</a></sup>will rise up—a real stink—for it has done terrible things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:16–17; Isaiah 54:4; Isaiah 41:10"> Don't be afraid, people of the land! Be happy and celebrate, for the Lord has done incredible things!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; PSALMS 65:12; PSALMS 67:6"> Don't be afraid, wild animals! For the pastures of the wilderness are turning green. The trees are fruiting again—the fig trees and the grapevines are producing a good crop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 5:7–8; Philippians 4:4; Leviticus 26:4"> People of Jerusalem! Celebrate and be happy in the Lord your God because he has given you rain to show his goodness. As before he sends the autumn and the spring rains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Amos 9:13; Joel 3:18"> The threshing floors will be full of grain, the vats will overflow with new wine and olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Joel 1:4–7; Joel 2:2–11"> “I will give back to you what you lost over the years to the swarming, hopping, destroying, and cutting locusts, this great army that I sent against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:11; PSALMS 22:26; Romans 5:5"> You will have plenty to eat, and you will be satisfied, and you will praise the name of the Lord your God who has done miracles for you. My people will never again be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:26–28; Leviticus 26:11–12; Zephaniah 3:17"> You will know that I am in the midst of my people Israel, that I am the Lord your God, and that there is no other. My people will never again be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:16–21; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 32:15"> After this I will pour out my Spirit on everyone. Your sons and daughters will be my prophets, your old men will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11"> In those days I will also pour out my Spirit on male slaves and female slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Luke 21:11; Matthew 24:29"> I will place wonders in the heavens and on earth: blood, and fire, and columns of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 6:12–13; Matthew 24:29; Malachi 4:1"> The sun will turn dark, and the moon will turn red like blood at the coming of the great and fearful day of the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 10:11–14; Acts 2:20–21; Isaiah 46:13"> Then everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved, rescued from Mount Zion and Jerusalem, as the Lord has said—these are among the survivors the Lord has called.</span>
