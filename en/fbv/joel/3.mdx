<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 30:3; Ezekiel 39:25"> At that time when this happens, when I bring back the exiles to Judah and Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–4; Isaiah 66:16; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will gather together all the nations in the valley of Jehoshaphat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Meaning “the Lord judges.”">a</a></sup>and I will judge them there over my people Israel, my inheritance, whom they have scattered among the nations, and have divided up my land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Obadiah 1:11; Amos 2:6"> They cast lots for my people; they traded boys in payment for prostitutes and girls to buy wine to drink.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The point being made here is the casual way the victims were treated, and how little they were valued: being traded to buy a prostitute's services or some wine to drink.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremiah 47:4; Isaiah 59:18"> Also, what have you got to do with me, Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you trying to get back at me? If you are trying to pay me back, I will quickly pay you back what you deserve for what you've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:18"> You stole my silver and my gold and my best treasures, and you put them in your temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks so they could be sent far away from their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Jeremiah 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> But watch out! I will get them moving from the places you sold them to, and bring them back, and I will pay you back what you deserve for what you've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Job 1:15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> I will sell your sons and daughters to the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation. I the Lord have spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:3–4; Isaiah 8:9–10; Ezekiel 38:7"> Proclaim this among the nations: “Prepare for war!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “Sanctify a battle.” Joel is presumably being ironic here. The same word is used in 1:14for calling a sacred assembly.">c</a></sup>Call up the mighty warriors! Let all the soldiers get ready and advance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:8; Micah 4:3; 2 Chronicles 25:8"> Hammer your plow blades and turn them into swords. Turn your pruning hooks into spears. Even the weak should say ‘I am a strong fighter!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 38:9–18"> Hurry up and come, all nations from everywhere, and gather there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In the valley of Jehoshaphat, 3:2.">d</a></sup>Bring down your warriors, Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Isaiah 2:4; PSALMS 96:13"> Let the nations get ready, let them come to the valley of Jehoshaphat for there I will sit in judgment on all the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Hosea 6:11"> Start swinging the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Come and tread the grapes for the winepress is full and the vats are overflowing because their wickedness has become so great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 34:2–8"> Huge mobs are in the valley of the Lord's verdict.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The usual translation “in the valley of decision” suggests that these “multitudes” still are making their decision. However, this decision is that of God, and is the execution of that decision—in other words the verdict or sentence.">e</a></sup>For the day of the Lord is near in the valley of his verdict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> The sun and moon will become dark, and the stars will stop shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Haggai 2:6"> The Lord will roar from Zion, shouting in a loud voice from Jerusalem, shaking the heavens and the earth. But the Lord will shelter his people, protecting the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 52:1"> Then you will know that I, the Lord your God, dwell in Zion, my holy mountain, and Jerusalem will be a holy place forever, and no foreigners will ever march through her again.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referring to conquest by other nations.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:1–12; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> At that time new wine will pour down the mountains, and the hills will flow with milk, and all the riverbeds of Judah will run with water. A spring will flow out of the Temple and will water the valley of Shittim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Shittim means “acacia trees,” usually found in arid areas.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:35; Zechariah 14:18–19"> But Egypt will become desolate, and Edom a desert wasteland, because of the violence they did against Judah, for in their land they shed innocent blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 33:20"> Judah will be lived in forever, and Jerusalem for all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> Will I pardon and leave unpunished the shedding of innocent blood? The Lord lives in Zion!”</span>
</div>
