<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Hosea 5:1; Job 8:8"> Listen to this, elders; pay attention, everyone who lives in the land. Has anything ever happened like this before in your experience, or that of your forefathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Exodus 13:14; PSALMS 145:4"> Tell your children about it, and have your children tell it to their children, and their children tell the next generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Amos 4:9; Isaiah 33:4"> What the cutting locusts left, the swarming locusts have eaten; what the swarming locusts left, the hopping locusts have eaten; what the hopping locusts left, the destroying locusts have eaten.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Whether these terms for locusts refer to different species or different stages of the locust's life cycle is uncertain.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:1; Isaiah 32:10–12; Luke 21:34–36"> Wake up, you drunks, and weep! Howl you wine drinkers, because the new wine has been suddenly taken away from your mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Revelation 9:7–10; Proverbs 30:14"> A nation has invaded my land: powerful and too many to count. Their teeth are like lion's teeth, their fangs like those of a lioness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Amos 4:9; Joel 1:12"> It has ruined my grapevines and destroyed my fig trees, stripping them completely and reducing them to stumps, white and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Malachi 2:15; Jeremiah 3:4"> Mourn like a bride dressed in sackcloth, mourning the death of her husband-to-be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The image is of a woman betrothed to a man who dies before the marriage is consummated.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Joel 2:14; Hosea 9:4"> Grain and wine offerings have stopped in the Temple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “house of the Lord.”">c</a></sup>The priests who minister before the Lord are in mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Hosea 9:2; Jeremiah 12:11"> The fields are devastated, the earth mourns; for the grain is ruined, the new wine dries up, the olive oil fails.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Isaiah 17:11; Jeremiah 9:12"> Be ashamed, farmers, and wail in sorrow, keepers of vineyards, over the wheat and the barley, for the crops from the fields are ruined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Isaiah 24:11; Song of Songs 2:3"> The vines are shriveled, the fig trees are withered; the pomegranate, the palm, and the apricot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This fruit is more likely than apple as is usually translated.">d</a></sup>trees—all the fruit trees have dried up, and at the same time the people's happiness has also dried up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 1:8–9; Jeremiah 4:8; Joel 2:17"> Dress in sackcloth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The traditional sign of mourning.">e</a></sup>you priests, and mourn; weep, you who minister before the altar! Go and spend the night in sackcloth, you ministers of my God, for the grain and wine offerings have stopped in the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 29:10–11; 2 Chronicles 20:13"> Proclaim a fast; call a sacred assembly. Have the elders and all people of the land gather in the Temple, and cry out to your God, to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6–9; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Jeremiah 30:7"> What a terrible day! For the day of the Lord is near, it will come as destruction from the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:6–7; PSALMS 43:4; Isaiah 62:8–9"> Haven't we seen our food taken away from us, right before our eyes? There is no joy and happiness in God's Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Genesis 23:16"> Seeds planted in the ground shrivel up; the storehouses are empty, the barns are torn down because the grain has dried up.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Hebrew in this verse is obscure. The Greek Septuagint for the first part of the verse reads “the heifers leap at their mangers.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 14:5–6"> The farm animals moan with hunger. The herds of cattle wander everywhere because they can't find grass to eat; the flocks of sheep are suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; Jeremiah 9:10; Amos 7:4"> To you, Lord, I cry out!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Joel makes it clear he is calling on Yahweh, while many of his fellow-countrymen would be calling on Baal, the fertility god, for help.">g</a></sup>For fire has destroyed the pastures in the wilderness; flames have burned up all the orchards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 104:21; 1 Kings 17:7; Job 38:41"> Even the wild animals long for you because the streams have dried up, and fire has destroyed the pastures in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
