<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Ezekiel 12:3–16"> “Son of man, you are to take a brick, put in front of you, and draw a picture of the city of Jerusalem on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> Show that it's under siege: establish a siege perimeter all around it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This was to prevent the inhabitants escaping.">a</a></sup>build a siege ramp against it, set up the enemy camps beside it, and put battering rams on all sides around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:6; Isaiah 8:18; Isaiah 20:3"> Put an iron plate between yourself and the city so it's like an iron wall. Face the city and demonstrate that it's under siege, and that you are the one attacking it. This is a symbolic warning to the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> Then lie on your left side and take the Israelites' sins on yourself. You will carry their sins for the number of days you lie on your side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Isaiah 53:6"> I will make you stay there for 390 days, representing the number of years of their sins. You will bear the sins of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> Once you have finished doing this for these days, then you are to lie down again, but this time on your right side, and carry the sins of the people of Judah. I will make you stay there for 40 days, one day for every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 6:2"> Keep your face towards the siege of Jerusalem with your arm bared, and prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:25"> Be ready, because now I'm going to tie you up with ropes so you can't move from side to side until the days of your siege are over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Isaiah 28:25; Ezekiel 4:16"> Get some wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt, and mix them together in a storage jar. Use them to make bread for yourself. This is what you are to eat for the 390 days that you lie on your side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> You are allowed to eat twenty shekels weight<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Twenty shekels weight”: around eight ounces, a very small ration to symbolize the lack of food during the siege,">b</a></sup>of food each day, and you are to eat it at regular times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; John 3:34; Ezekiel 4:16"> Measure out a sixth of a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Sixth of a hin”: around a pint and a half.">c</a></sup>of water to drink, and you are to drink it at regular times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> You are to eat the bread as you would a barley loaf. You are to bake it over a fire burning dried human excrement as everyone watches.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Hosea 9:3–4"> Then the Lord said, “This is the way the Israelites will eat their unclean bread among the nations where I'll exile them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Ezekiel 20:49; Ezekiel 9:8"> “Please no, Lord God!” I answered. “I have never made myself unclean. I haven't eaten anything found dead or killed by wild animals from the time I was young until now. I've never put unclean meat in my mouth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “All right,” he said. “I'll let you use cow manure instead of human excrement You can bake your bread over a fire using that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Isaiah 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> Then he went on, “Son of man, watch! I am going to put a stop to Jerusalem's food supply. Worried sick, they will eat bread rationed by weight. Despairing at what's happening, they will drink water measured in small amounts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Ezekiel 33:10"> Running out of food and water, they will be horrified as they look at one another wasting away because of their sins.”</span>
