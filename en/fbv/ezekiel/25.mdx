<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:1–6; Amos 1:13–15; Ezekiel 6:2"> “Son of man, face towards the Ammonites and prophesy against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; PSALMS 70:2–3; Proverbs 17:5"> Tell the Ammonites to listen to the message from the Lord God, for this is what the Lord God says: Because you said, ‘Oh good!’ when my sanctuary was made unclean, when the country of Israel was turned into a wasteland, and when the people of Judah were taken into exile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:33; 1 Kings 4:30"> this is why I will make you subject to the people of the East. They will make camp and set up their tents right where you live. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 12:26; Isaiah 17:2"> I will make Rabbah a camel pasture, and Ammon a sheep-pen. Then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8; Obadiah 1:12; Zephaniah 2:10"> For this is what the Lord God says: Because you clapped your hands and stamped your feet in a celebration of hate over what happened to Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:4; Ezekiel 25:16; Ezekiel 6:14"> this is why I will reach out and attack you, and hand you over to be looted by other nations. I will wipe you out so completely that you will no longer exist as a nation. I will destroy you, and then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Jeremiah 25:21; Jeremiah 48:1–47"> This is what the Lord God says: Because the people of Moab and Seir said, ‘Look, the people of Judah are just like everybody else,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:23; Joshua 13:17; Joshua 13:19–20"> that's why I will open Moab's border to attack, including Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim, the country's famous cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:32; PSALMS 83:3–6; Ezekiel 25:4"> I will hand Moab over to the people of the East for them to rule so that they will no longer exist as a nation just like the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Ezekiel 30:19; PSALMS 149:7"> This is how I'm going to punish the Moabites, and they will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Ezekiel 25:8; PSALMS 137:7"> This is what the Lord God says: Because Edom committed serious sins by taking vicious revenge on the people of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:8; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 25:23"> this is why the Lord God says: I will reach out to attack Edom and destroy all its people and animals. I will turn it into a wasteland. All the way from Teman in the north to Dedan in the south they will die by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:14; Genesis 27:29; Hebrews 10:30–31"> I will take revenge on the Edomites through my people Israel. They will deal with the Edomites so that they experience my anger. Then they will know what my vengeance is like, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:6; Isaiah 14:29–31; Ezekiel 25:12"> This is what the Lord God says: Because the Philistines in their hatred repeatedly took revenge on Judah, trying to destroy the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; Zephaniah 2:4–15; Ezekiel 25:13"> this is why the Lord God says: Watch out! I'm going to reach out to attack the Philistines, and I will wipe out the Kerethites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Kerethites”: one of the major warrior tribes of the Philistines.">a</a></sup>and destroy those who are left along the coast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:11; Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 5:15"> My revenge against them will be severe as I punish them in my anger. Then they will know that I am the Lord, when they experience my anger.”</span>
