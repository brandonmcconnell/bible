<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2–3; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 41:23"> He took me to the Temple and measured the posts as six cubits wide on both sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:17; 2 Chronicles 3:3; 1 Kings 6:2"> The entrance was ten cubits wide, and the sides of the entrance were five cubits long on both sides. He measured the outer sanctuary as forty cubits long and twenty cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16"> He went into the inner sanctuary and measured the entrance posts as two cubits wide. The entrance was six cubits wide, and the walls on both sides were seven cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20; Hebrews 9:3–8; Exodus 26:33–34"> He measured the room beside the inner sanctuary as twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. He told me, “This is the Most Holy Place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 42:3–14; Ezekiel 41:6–9"> He measured Temple wall as six cubits thick, and each side room around the Temple was four cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:10; 1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 40:17"> There were three levels of side rooms above one another, each having thirty rooms. The wall of the Temple had external supports for the side rooms, so that they would not be fixed into the wall of the Temple itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:8; Hebrews 6:1; Matthew 13:32"> The side rooms around the Temple became wider at each higher level, because as the structure around the Temple went up the Temple wall grew narrower A stairway provided access from the bottom story to the top, going through the middle level.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Revelation 21:16"> I saw that the Temple was on a raised platform that surrounded it. This was the foundation for the side rooms. Its height was the complete length of a measuring rod, six long cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:11; Ezekiel 42:4; Ezekiel 42:1"> The thickness of the outer wall of the side rooms was five cubits, and there was open space between the side rooms of the Temple</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:17"> and the outer chambers that measured twenty cubits wide all round the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:9; Ezekiel 42:4"> The doors of the side rooms opened into this area, with one entrance to the north and another one to the south. The open space was five cubits wide on each side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> Another building faced the Temple courtyard on the west side. It measured seventy cubits wide and ninety cubits long, with walls all the way round that were five cubits thick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> He measured the Temple as one hundred cubits long. The Temple courtyard and the building including its walls were also one hundred cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The Temple courtyard on the east side (including the front of the Temple), was one hundred cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:3; Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 41:12"> He measured the length of the building that faced the Temple courtyard towards the rear of the Temple, including its open halls on each side. It was one hundred cubits long. The outer sanctuary, the inner sanctuary, and the porches facing the courtyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16"> as well as the thresholds and the narrow windows and the surrounding open halls with their three levels up to and including the threshold, were covered with wood on every side. This extended from the ground up to and including the windows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:15"> On the outside of all the walls by the entrance to the inner sanctuary, spaced at regular intervals around the inner and outer sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> were carved designs of cherubim and palm trees. Every cherub had two faces:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10"> a man's face looked in the direction of a palm tree on one side, and the face of a young lion looked in the direction of the palm tree on the other side. These carvings extended the whole way round the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> Designs of cherubim and palm trees were carved on the Temple wall from the floor up to the space above the doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> The Temple's doorframe was rectangular, as was the doorframe of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; Ezekiel 23:41; Malachi 1:7"> An altar made of wood stood there, three cubits high and two cubits long. All of it—its corners, base, and sides—were made of wood. The man told me, “This is the table that stands before the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 6:31–35; 2 Chronicles 4:22; Ezekiel 41:1"> The Temple and the sanctuary both had hinged double doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; Ezekiel 40:48"> Each door had two panels that opened. There were two panels for one door, and two panels for the other door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> There were carvings of cherubim and palm trees on the Temple doors like those on the walls, and there was a wooden roof that covered the outside part of the porch at the front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:16; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:5"> There were narrow windows and palm tree designs on the walls of the porch. The side rooms of the Temple also had roofs.</span>
