<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6; 1 Kings 6:8"> Since they didn't have pillars like the courtyards, the upper rooms were set farther back than the lower and middle levels.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13–14"> The rooms on the outer courtyard extended for fifty cubits, but those that faced the Temple were a hundred cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Ezekiel 44:5"> Below these rooms was an entrance on the east side coming from the outer courtyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:12–15"> Along the wall on the south<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“South”: Septuagint reading.">a</a></sup>side of the outer courtyard were rooms next to the courtyard and opposite the building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:4"> There was a walkway in front of them, just like the rooms on the north side. These rooms had the same length and width, the same doors, floor plan, and exits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Similarly there was an entrance under the rooms on the south side of the building, coming from the east side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17; Leviticus 7:6; Ezekiel 40:46"> The man told me, “The north and south rooms that face the Temple courtyard are holy rooms where the priests who go before the Lord will eat the most holy offerings. They will place the most holy offerings there because the place is holy, and include the grain offerings, the sin offerings, and the guilt offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> Once the priests have entered these rooms, they are not to go to the outer courtyard before leaving their priestly clothes, because these are holy. They must change into other clothes before they go where the ordinary people are.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Revelation 11:1–2; Zechariah 2:1"> Using the measuring rod he measured the east side. It was five hundred cubits long.</span>
