<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; PSALMS 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> “Son of man, is wood from a vine better than the wood from any other tree in the forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Can you make anything useful from vine wood? Can you use it to make even just a peg to hang up pots and pans?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; PSALMS 80:16"> No, you just throw it on the fire to keep the fire burning. Even then the fire burns both ends, but only chars the middle part. Can you use it for anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Even before you burned it you couldn't make it into anything useful. It's even less useful once the fire has burned and charred it!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> So this is what the Lord God says: In the same way that I have taken the wood of a vine from the forest and thrown it on the fire to be burned, so I'm going to throw away the people of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; PSALMS 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> I will turn against them. While they might have escaped this fire, another fire is going to burn them up. When I turn against them, then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> I'm going to turn the country into a wasteland, because they were unfaithful to me, declares the Lord God.”</span>
</div>
