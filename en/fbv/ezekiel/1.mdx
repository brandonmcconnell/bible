<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 3:16; Ezekiel 11:24; Acts 7:56"> On the fifth day of the fourth month of the thirtieth year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ezekiel is not specific regarding what the “thirtieth year” applies to. Some have considered that it refers to his age.">a</a></sup>I was with the other exiles by the River Kebar. The heavens opened and I saw visions of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–15; Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> (On the fifth day of the month of the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 3:22; 2 Kings 3:15"> a message from Lord was given to Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of Babylonia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Babylonia”: literally, “land of the Chaldeans.”">b</a></sup>by the River Kebar. There the Lord's power came over him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:27; Ezekiel 8:2; Jeremiah 23:19"> I looked up and saw a windstorm racing in from the north, a tremendous cloud with fire flashing like lightning and surrounded by brilliant light. The middle of the fire looked like glowing metal</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:6–8; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 6:6"> and within it was the shape of four beings.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="They are identified as “cherubim” in 10:2.">c</a></sup><br />This was what they looked like: They had a human shape,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7–8; Ezekiel 1:15"> but they each had four faces and four wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:15; Daniel 10:6; PSALMS 104:4"> They had straight legs, and the soles of their feet looked like the hooves of calves, and shone like polished bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:21; Ezekiel 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> They had human hands under their wings on each of their four sides. The four of them all had faces and wings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:11–12"> and each of their wings touched the wings of the one next to it. As they moved they didn't turn—they all moved straight in one direction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> This what their faces looked like. They had a human face to the front, a lion's face to the right, a bull's face to the left, and lastly an eagle's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; Ezekiel 1:23; Ezekiel 10:19"> These were their faces.<br />Their wings were spread out above them. They all had two wings that touched the wings of the one beside it, as well as two wings that covered its body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 10:22; Hebrews 1:14"> Each of them went together in whatever direction the Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Spirit”: while the word used here can also mean breath or wind, the fact that this is the agent directing these beings indicates that it is divine.">d</a></sup>wished them to go, moving without turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; PSALMS 104:4; Matthew 28:3"> These beings looked like bright burning coals or torches. Blazing fire moved to and fro between the beings, and lightning flashed out from the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Zechariah 4:10; Mark 13:27"> The beings raced to and fro as fast as the lightning flashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 10:9; Ezekiel 1:19–21"> As I was watching these beings, I noticed a wheel on the ground next to each of the beings with their four faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Ezekiel 10:9–11; Exodus 39:13"> The wheels looked like they were made of topaz,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Topaz”: a semi-precious stone. The identification of gems in the Bible is uncertain. Other possibilities include beryl, jasper, and chrysolite. (The Septuagint has chrysolite, indicating that the stone was golden-yellow in color.)">e</a></sup>and all four wheels were the same. In the middle of each wheel was what looked like another wheel set crossways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> They could move in any direction without turning as they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:12; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 4:8"> The wheel rims were impressively high, and all of the four rims were covered in eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 10:19; PSALMS 103:20"> As the beings moved so did the wheels next to them, and when the beings flew up, the wheels did too.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:12; Ezekiel 10:17; 1 Corinthians 14:32"> They would go wherever the Spirit went. The wheels would lift up beside them, because the Spirit of the beings was in the wheels to direct them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:17; Ezekiel 1:19–20; Romans 8:2"> When the beings moved, the wheels moved; when they stopped, the wheels stopped; and when they flew up from the ground, the wheels flew up beside them, because the Spirit of the beings was in the wheels to direct them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 4:6"> Extending above the heads of the being was something that looked like an amazing expanse<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Expanse”: The same word is used in Genesis 1:6to describe the expanse later called the sky.">f</a></sup>that glittered like ice crystal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 4:18; Ezekiel 1:11–12; PSALMS 89:7"> Under this expanse the wings of the beings spread out toward one another. Each of them also had two wings that covered its body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 1:15"> When the beings moved, I heard the sound their wings made. It was like the roar of a thundering waterfall, like the voice of the Almighty, like the noise of an army on the march.<br />Whenever they stopped, they folded their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:22"> A voice spoke from beyond the expanse over their heads while they were standing still with their wings folded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Exodus 24:10; Revelation 1:13"> Above the expanse over their heads I saw what looked like a throne made of lapis lazuli.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Lapis lazuli”: a semi-precious stone, bright blue in color">g</a></sup>Sitting on the throne high above was a being who looked like a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 8:2; Hebrews 12:29"> From what seemed to be his waist upwards, he looked like glowing metal with fire inside and everywhere around. From what seemed to be his waist downwards, I saw what looked like fire. He was surrounded by very bright light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 10:1; Revelation 4:3; Genesis 17:3"> The bright light surrounding him was like a rainbow shining through the clouds on a rainy day. This was what the glory of the Lord looked like. When I saw it, I fell facedown on the ground, and then I heard someone speaking to me.</span>
</div>
</div>
