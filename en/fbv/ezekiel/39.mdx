<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:2–3; Ezekiel 35:3; Nahum 3:5"> “Son of man, prophesy against Gog and announce that this is what the Lord God says: Watch out, because I'm condemning you Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 40:14; Ezekiel 38:15; Isaiah 37:29"> I will send you in a different direction, drag you along, bring you from the distant places of the north, and send you to attack the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 76:3; Hosea 1:5; Jeremiah 21:4–5"> Then I will knock the bow out of your left hand and make you drop the arrows from your right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 38:21"> You will be killed on the mountains of Israel, you and your whole army and armies of your allies. I will provide you as food to all kinds of flesh-eating birds and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:19; Ezekiel 32:4; Jeremiah 8:2"> You will fall and die out in the open, for I have spoken, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:16; Amos 1:4; Ezekiel 30:8"> I will set Magog on fire, as well as the coastlands where people think it's safe to live, and then they will acknowledge that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Exodus 20:7"> In this way I will make my reputation for holiness known among my people Israel and won't allow it to be ruined anymore. Then the nations will acknowledge that I am the Lord, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:17; Revelation 16:17; Ezekiel 7:2–10"> Yes, it's coming! It will definitely happen, declares the Lord God. This is the day that I have spoken about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 46:9; Isaiah 66:24; Malachi 1:5"> Then those living in the towns of Israel will go out to light fires and burn the weapons—the large and small shields, the bows and arrows, the clubs and spears. They will use the weapons to make fires for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 14:2; Micah 5:8"> They won't need to go and gather firewood from the countryside or cut it from the forests, because they'll use the weapons to make fires. They will loot and plunder those who looted and plundered them, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:18; Numbers 34:11; Ezekiel 38:2"> At that time I will give Gog a place to be buried in Israel, the Travelers' Valley, east of the Sea. People won't be able to travel through because all his army will be buried there. So it will be called the Valley of Gog's Army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; Ezekiel 39:14; Ezekiel 39:16"> It will take the people of Israel seven months to bury them in order to make the country clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:22; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 33:9"> Everyone in the country will be involved in burying them, and this will give them a good reputation when I reveal my glory, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:12; Numbers 19:11–19"> Men will be chosen to go repeatedly through the country to make it clean it by burying the bodies of the invaders that are still left lying the ground. They will start doing this at the end of the seven months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:44"> As they search the country, if they find a human bone they will place a marker next to it so that those in charge of burials can have it buried in the Valley of Gog's Army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Even the town there will be named Hamonah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Hamonah,” referring to the “army.”">a</a></sup>In this way they will make the country clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:7; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 34:6"> Son of man, this is what the Lord God says: Call out to every kind of flesh-eating bird and wild animal: Come from everywhere around and gather for the sacrifice I'm going to prepare for you, a great sacrificial feast on the mountains of Israel where you'll have flesh to eat and blood to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 22:12; Jeremiah 51:40; Revelation 19:17–18"> You will eat the flesh of the powerful and drink the blood of the world's leaders just as if they were rams, lambs, goats, and bulls—all the fattened animals that come from Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> You will eat fat until you are totally full and drink blood until you are drunk at the sacrifice I'm going to prepare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 19:18; Haggai 2:22; PSALMS 76:5–6"> You will eat at my table until you're full, consuming horses and riders, powerful men and all kinds of warriors, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Isaiah 37:20"> I will reveal my glory to the other nations, and all of them will see the punishment I hand out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 28:26; Ezekiel 39:28"> From that time on the people of Israel will know that I am the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Isaiah 1:15; Isaiah 59:2"> The nations will realize that the people of Israel were taken prisoner because of their sins, because they were unfaithful to me. So I gave up on them and handed them over to their enemies, so that they were all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“All”: an example of hyperbole, since clearly they were not all killed.">b</a></sup>killed by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:19; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 4:18"> I dealt with them because of their uncleanness and sins, and I gave up on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Isaiah 27:12–13; Hosea 1:11"> So this is what the Lord God says: Now I will bring back the descendants of Jacob from exile and I will show mercy to all the people of Israel, and I will demonstrate my reputation for holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 4:4; 1 Kings 4:25; Ezekiel 16:63"> They will forget their shameful actions and all the ways they were unfaithful to me once they live in safety in their country, with no one to threaten them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> When I bring them home from the nations, gathering them from the countries of their enemies, I will reveal my holiness as many nations watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:22; Amos 9:9; Isaiah 27:12"> Then they will know that I am the Lord their God, when I bring them home to their own country again, not leaving any of them behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 2:28; 1 John 3:24; Isaiah 32:15"> I won't give up on them anymore, for I will fill the people of Israel with my Spirit, declares the Lord God.”</span>
