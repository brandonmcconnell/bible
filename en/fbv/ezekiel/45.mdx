<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:29; Zechariah 14:20–21; Numbers 34:13"> When you allocate the land ownership by lot, you are to make a provision for the Lord, a holy allocation of the land that measures 25,000 cubits long and 20,000<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“20,000.” Septuagint reading. The Hebrew is “10,000,” but since this allocation is divided into two sections of 10,000 cubits each, the Septuagint is likely correct.">a</a></sup>cubits wide. This whole area is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:16–20; Ezekiel 27:28"> Inside this there is the section for the sanctuary that measures 500 cubits square, with an open area around it 50 cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:10"> From this holy allocation, measure out a section that is 25,000 cubits by 10,000 cubits. This will contain the sanctuary, the Most Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 40:45"> This area is a holy allocation of the land which will be used by the priests who serve in the sanctuary, who come before the Lord to minister. It will be where they can live, and a holy place for the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:13; Nehemiah 10:38–39; Ezekiel 48:20"> The other section measuring 25,000 cubits by 10,000 cubits will be assigned the Levites who work in the Temple where they can live.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Where they can live”: literally, “property for twenty rooms.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:15–18; Ezekiel 48:30–35"> Allocate to the city an area 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long beside the sanctuary section. It is for the all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:21; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 46:16–18"> The prince will be assigned the territory on both sides of the area that includes the holy section and that of the city. It will extend to the western border of the country from the west side and to the eastern border from the east side, in the same way as the allocation to the tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–5; Ezekiel 46:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> This territory will be his to own in the country of Israel. My princes won't exploit my people anymore, and will make sure the people of Israel occupy the country according to their tribal allocations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 22:3; Ezekiel 44:6"> This is what the Lord God says: You've done enough damage, princes of Israel! Stop using violence and mistreating people! Do what is good and right. Stop evicting people and stealing their property, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:4–6; Proverbs 16:11"> You must use correct methods of measurement—whether it's weights on scales, or ephah for measuring solids, and a bath for measuring liquids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10"> The ephah and the bath shall be the same quantity of volume. The bath will be equivalent to a tenth of a homer, and the ephah will also be equivalent to a tenth of a homer. The homer is to be the fundamental measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> Twenty gerahs make one shekel. Sixty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Sixty”: given as 20 plus 25 plus 15. No reason for this is clear.">c</a></sup>shekels make one mina.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> These are the taxes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The taxes are to be paid to the prince for the operating costs of the Temple.">d</a></sup>you are to contribute: one sixth of an ephah from every homer of wheat or barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> In the case of olive oil, one tenth of a bath from every cor. (The measurement is based on baths, and a cor consists of ten baths or one homer, since ten baths equal one homer.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 6:30; Colossians 1:21"> In addition one sheep is to be supplied from every flock of two hundred from the green pastures of Israel. These are to provide for the grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings, to set the people right, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:14–15; Isaiah 16:1"> Everyone in the country has to make these contributions to the prince in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–12; 2 Chronicles 31:3; Isaiah 66:23"> The prince's role is to provide the burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings for the religious festivals, New Moons, and Sabbaths—in fact for all the regular religious festivals of the people of Israel. He is to provide the sin offerings, grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to set the people of Israel right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Exodus 12:2; Ezekiel 43:22"> This is what the Lord God says: On the first day of the first month you are to sacrifice a young bull that has no defects to purify the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:20; Ezekiel 43:14; Leviticus 16:18–20"> The priest shall take some of the blood from the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the Temple, on the four corners of the upper ledge of the altar, and on the gateposts of the inner courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 19:12; Leviticus 4:27–35; Ezekiel 45:15"> You are also to do this on the seventh day of the month on behalf of anyone who does wrong unintentionally or in ignorance. By doing this you purify the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Numbers 9:2–14"> You are to keep the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month. This is a religious festival that lasts seven days, during which time bread made without yeast is to be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Matthew 20:28"> On that day the prince will provide a bull as a sin offering for himself and for everyone in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 42:8; Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 23:1–2"> Every day for the seven days of the festival, he will provide seven bulls and seven rams without defects as a burnt offering to the Lord, together with a male goat for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 28:12–15"> He will also provide an ephah of grain and a hin of olive oil as an offering to accompany each bull and each ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> For the seven days of the festival<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Festival of Shelters.">e</a></sup>that begins on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, he is to provide the same number of sin offerings, burnt offerings, grain offerings, and olive oil.</span>
