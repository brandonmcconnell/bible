<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Jeremiah 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> “Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds who lead Israel. Prophesy and tell them that this is what the Lord God says: Disaster is coming to the shepherds of Israel, who only look after themselves, and not the sheep! Shouldn't shepherds make sure their flock is fed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 11:16; Jeremiah 2:30; Ezekiel 33:25–26"> You eat the cream, you use the wool for clothes, and you slaughter the fattened sheep, but you don't feed the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 18:12–13; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:16"> You haven't taken care of the weak, healed the sick, bandaged the injured, brought home the strays, or looked for the lost. On the contrary, you ruled them using violence and cruelty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; 2 Chronicles 18:16; Jeremiah 23:2"> They were scattered because they didn't have a shepherd. When they scattered they were preyed on by all the wild animals for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 142:4; 1 Peter 2:25; John 10:16"> My sheep strayed, going to all the mountains and high hills. They were scattered everywhere, and there was no one to go and look for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:2–3; Micah 3:8–9; PSALMS 82:1–7"> So, you shepherds, listen to what the Lord has to say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Acts 20:33; Ezekiel 34:5–6"> As I live, declares the Lord God, because my sheep didn't have a shepherd and were preyed on by all the wild animals for food, and because my shepherds didn't look after my sheep but instead only fed themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> so, you shepherds, you listen to what the Lord has to say!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Zechariah 10:3; Ezekiel 13:8"> This is what the Lord God says: Watch out, because I'm condemning the shepherds, and I will take my sheep back and stop them from looking after the sheep so they won't be able to feed themselves anymore. I will take away my sheep from them, and I won't let them eat the sheep anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:16; Luke 19:10; Jeremiah 23:3"> For this is what the Lord God says: Watch as I myself will go looking for my sheep, searching to find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; John 10:11–12; Luke 19:10"> Just like a shepherd goes looking for his lost sheep when he is with the flock, so I will look for my sheep. I will rescue them from everywhere they were scattered on a dark and dismal day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25–26; Ezekiel 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> I will take them out from among the other nations. I will gather them from the different countries, and bring them back to their own country. Like a shepherd I will have them feed them on the mountains of Israel, in the valleys and everywhere that people live in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:9; Jeremiah 31:25; PSALMS 31:8–10"> I will provide them with good pasture and places to graze in the high mountains of Israel. They will also be able to rest there in the good pasture and in the places to graze in mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; PSALMS 23:1–2; John 21:15"> I myself will look after my sheep and give them a place to rest, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> I will go looking for the lost, bring home the strays, bandage the injured, and strengthen the weak. However, I will destroy those who are fat and strong. Because I do what's right, I will be sure to take care of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20–22; Matthew 25:32–33; Zechariah 10:3"> My flock, this is what the Lord God says to you: Watch, because I will judge between one sheep and the next, between the rams and the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 11:52; Numbers 16:13"> Aren't you satisfied with feeding in good pasture? Do you have to trample down the rest of the pasture with your feet? Aren't you satisfied to drink the clear water? Do you have to muddy the rest of it with your feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why does my flock have to feed on what your feet have trampled down, and drink the water that your feet have muddied?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; Ezekiel 34:10; PSALMS 22:12–16"> That's why the Lord God says to them: Watch, because I myself will judge between the fat sheep and the skinny sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 11:5; Deuteronomy 33:17; Luke 13:14–16"> Since you push others around with your sides and your shoulders, and use your horns to attack all the weak ones until you have chased them away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:2–3; PSALMS 72:12–14; Ezekiel 34:10"> I will rescue my flock, and they will no longer be your victims. I will judge between one sheep and the next.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> I will put one shepherd in charge of them, my servant David, and he will take care of them. He will take care of them and be their shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> I the Lord will be their God, and my servant David will be their leader. I the Lord have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Ezekiel 37:26; Leviticus 26:6"> I will make an agreement of peace with them, and get rid of the wild animals from the country, so that they can live safely in the wilderness and sleep securely in the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; Leviticus 26:4"> I will bless them everywhere around my hill. I will send rain at the right time. They will be showers of blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Leviticus 26:4; Leviticus 26:13"> The trees in the orchards will produce their fruit; the earth will grow its crops; and my flock will live in safety in their country. Then they will know that I am the Lord, when I break their yokes of slavery, and set them free from those who made them slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27; Jeremiah 30:10; Ezekiel 39:26"> They won't be the victims of other nations anymore, and wild animals won't eat them. They will live in safety, and no one will terrorize them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; Ezekiel 34:26–27"> I will make their fields produce remarkable harvests, and they won't suffer famine in their country again or be mocked by others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; Ezekiel 34:24; PSALMS 46:11"> Then they will know that I am with them as the Lord their God, and that the people of Israel belong to me, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; John 10:16; PSALMS 78:52"> You are my sheep, the sheep that I feed. You are my people, and I am your God, declares the Lord God.”</span>
