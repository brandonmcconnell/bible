<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> “Son of man, face towards Mount Seir and prophesy against it</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> Announce that this is what the Lord God says: Watch out, because I'm condemning you, Mount Seir. I'm going to attack you and turn you into a wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> I will ruin your towns; you will be completely devastated. Then you will acknowledge that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because of your lasting hatred for the Israelites you let them be attacked with swords as they went through terrible disasters when the time of their punishment was coming to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> So as I live, declares the Lord God, I will let you be violently attacked and chased down. Since you did not hate bloodshed, bloodshed will come after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> I will turn Mount Seir into a ruined wasteland. I will wipe out anyone who leaves or who tries to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> I will fill its mountains with the dead. Those killed by the sword will fall in your hills, valleys, and all your ravines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> I will ruin you forever. Your towns will not be inhabited again. Then you will acknowledge that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; PSALMS 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because you said, ‘These two nations and their lands belong to us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perhaps a reference back to how Jacob deceived Esau in obtaining his birthright. The Edomites were descended from Esau.">a</a></sup>and we will take them over, even though the Lord was there with them,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; Matthew 7:2; PSALMS 73:17–18"> then as I live, declares the Lord God, I will deal with you in the same way that you showed anger and jealousy to them in your hatred of them. They will realize that I'm punishing you because of the way you treated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; PSALMS 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> You will also realize that I, the Lord, heard all the nasty things you said about the mountainous land of Israel when you announced, ‘The mountains have been left empty; they're ours to take!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> You made a lot of boasts attacking me—you had much to say against me. I heard it for myself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> This is what the Lord God says: I will destroy you as everyone else in the world celebrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> Just as you celebrated when Israel was destroyed, I will do the same to you. Mount Seir, you will become a wasteland, and so will the rest of Edom. Then they will acknowledge that I am the Lord.”</span>
