<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Jeremiah 52:5–6; Ezekiel 1:2"> On the first day of the third month of the eleventh year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dating from the beginning of King Jehoiachin's exile.">a</a></sup>a message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 31:18"> “Son of man, tell Pharaoh king of Egypt and all his many people: Who is as great as you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; Daniel 4:10; Daniel 4:20–23"> Look at Assyria. It was like a cedar in Lebanon, with its beautiful branches providing shade in the forest. It was so high that its top reached the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 17:15; Proverbs 14:28; Ezekiel 17:5"> Deep springs of water made it grow tall and sent streams all around it to water all the other trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:11; Ezekiel 17:5; PSALMS 1:3"> It became really tall, higher than any other tree in the forest. Its branches grew thick and long because it was so well-watered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:23; Matthew 13:32; Daniel 4:12"> All kinds of birds nested in its branches, while underneath it different wild animals had their young, and all the powerful nations lived in its shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> It was beautiful and majestic with its long branches, because its roots reached down to the plentiful water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> The cedars in God's garden were no match for it. No pine tree had such great branches, nor did any plane tree. No tree in God's garden was as beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Daniel 2:37–38; Genesis 13:10"> I made it beautiful with its many branches. All the trees of Eden in God's garden envied it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:17; Daniel 5:20; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> So this is what the Lord God says: Since it became so tall, reaching up into the clouds, it grew proud because of its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11–12; Nahum 3:18; Daniel 5:18–19"> So I handed it over to the leader of a foreign nation who will punish it for its wickedness. I have thrown it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 35:8; Ezekiel 30:11"> Foreigners from the cruelest of all the nations chopped it down and left it lying there. Its branches lie fallen and broken in the mountains and valleys of the countryside. Everybody in earth came out of its shadow and abandoned it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:4; Ezekiel 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> Birds came to live on its fallen trunk, and wild animals hid among its fallen branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 Peter 2:6"> This was in order that no other trees growing by the water would grow up so high and have their tops reach the clouds. This was so that no other trees, however much water they had, would be as tall as them, For they all will die and go down into earth, just like human beings who go down into the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 3:4; Nahum 2:8–10; Revelation 18:9–11"> This is what the Lord God says: On the day it was brought down to Sheol,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Sheol,” the place of the dead.">b</a></sup>I had the deep waters mourn for it; I stopped its rivers flowing; I held back all its waters. I covered Lebanon in darkness, and all the trees in the forest withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; Ezekiel 26:15"> I made the nations tremble when they heard it fall, when I threw it down into Sheol with those who die. Then all the trees of Eden, the finest and best in Lebanon, all the trees that had plenty of water, were happy that this tree joined them in the earth below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 9:17; Ezekiel 31:6; Ezekiel 31:3"> They also went down with it into Sheol, to those who had been killed by the sword. They had been part of its army living in its shade among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:19; Ezekiel 32:21; Ezekiel 28:10"> Who is as great and glorious as you among the trees of Eden? But you too will be brought down to the earth below to be with the trees of Eden. You will lie there with the heathen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Heathen”: literally, “uncircumcised.”">c</a></sup>with those killed by the sword. This is what will happen to Pharaoh and all his many people, declares the Lord God.”</span>
