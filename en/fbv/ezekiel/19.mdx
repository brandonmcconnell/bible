<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:2; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Kings 24:6"> Sing this funeral song for the princes of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> with these words: “What was your mother? She was a lioness among the lions! She lay down in her place among the young lions and reared her cubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> She raised one of her cubs, and he grew up to be a young lion. Once he had learned how to tear up his prey, he started eating people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> But when the nations heard about him, he was caught in their trap. They used hooks to drag him away to Egypt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring to Jehoahaz, also called Shallum.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> When she realized that the hope she had been waiting for was gone, she made another of her cubs into a young lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> He went around with the other lions, and became strong. Once he had learned how to tear up his prey, he started eating people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> He tore down their fortresses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Following the reading in the Targums and Theodotion's Greek translation. The Hebrew text reads “widows.”">b</a></sup>and destroyed their towns. All the people living in the country were appalled when they heard him roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> Then the people of the countries all around attacked him. They threw their net over him; he was caught in their trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> They used hooks to put him in a cage and took him to the king of Babylon. They led him away and imprisoned him so his roar wasn't heard any more in Israel's mountains.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referring to Jehoiachin.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> Your mother was like a vine planted in your vineyard at the waterside. It produced a lot of fruit and had many branches because it had plenty of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; PSALMS 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> Its branches were strong like rulers' scepters. It grew high above the tree canopy. People could see how tall and full of leaves it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> But it was uprooted in anger and thrown down on the ground. The east wind blew and dried up its fruit. Its strong branches were stripped of leaves and they withered. Then they were burned up in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; PSALMS 63:1"> Now the vine has been replanted in the desert, in a dry and waterless land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> A fire blazed out from its main trunk and burned up its fruit. None of its branches that were once like a ruler's scepter are strong any more.” This is a funeral song and is to be used for mourning.</span>
</div>
