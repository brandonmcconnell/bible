<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="EZK" data-book-slug="ezekiel" data-book-name="Ezekiel" data-chapter="18" data-verses="32">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29–30; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 37:11"> “What's this proverb you people are quoting about the country of Israel:<br />‘The fathers ate the unripe grapes, but their children got the sour taste’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, “it's not our fault this is happening to us; it's due to our forefathers' sins, not ours.” See also Jeremiah 31:29.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 33:11–20; Ezekiel 18:30"> As I live, declares the Lord God, you won't quote this proverb in Israel any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Romans 6:23; Numbers 16:22"> Can't you see that everyone belongs to me? Whether parents or children, they are all mine. It's the person who sins who will die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:14; 1 John 3:7; Proverbs 21:3"> Take the example of a man who is a good person, doing what is fair and right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:15; Deuteronomy 4:19; Ezekiel 6:13"> He doesn't go to the pagan shrines in the mountains to eat a religious meal, or worship Israel's idols. He doesn't have sex with someone else's wife or with a woman during her period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:16; Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12"> He doesn't exploit anyone. He gives back what a debtor has given him as security. He doesn't rob others. He feeds the hungry and clothes the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Zechariah 8:16; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> He doesn't lend with interest or make a profit from loans. He refuses to do wrong and makes sure he's truly fair in his decisions between people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Amos 5:4"> He follows my rules and keeps my regulations faithfully. Such a man lives by what's right and he will certainly live, declares the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 John 3:12; John 18:40"> What if that man has a son who is a violent criminal, who kills and does do such evil things just listed</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:14; Matthew 7:21–27; 1 Kings 13:8"> even though the father doesn't act like that at all. The son goes to the pagan shrines in the mountains to eat a religious meal. He has sex with someone else's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Ezekiel 8:6; 2 Kings 21:11"> He exploits the poor and those in need. He robs others, and he doesn't give back what a debtor has given him as security. He worships idols. He commits disgusting sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 20:9; Ezekiel 3:18"> He lends with interest and makes a profit from loans. Is someone like this going to live? No, he won't! Because he has done all these offensive things, he will die for certain, and he will be responsible for his own death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Proverbs 23:24; Jeremiah 8:6"> Now what if this man has a son who sees all the sins his father has committed. He sees them but doesn't do the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:6–7; Ezekiel 18:11–13"> He doesn't go to the pagan shrines in the mountains to eat a religious meal, or worship Israel's idols. He doesn't have sex with someone else's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:7; Isaiah 58:7–10; Job 22:7"> He doesn't exploit anyone. He doesn't demand security for a loan. He doesn't rob others. He feeds the hungry and clothes the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:8–9; Matthew 18:27–35; Ezekiel 18:13"> He refuses to do wrong and does not charge interest or make a profit on loans. He keeps my regulations and follows my laws. A man like that won't die because of his father's sins, he will certainly live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 18:24"> But his father will die for his own sins, because he exploited others, robbed his relatives, and did wrong to his own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Zechariah 1:3–6; Deuteronomy 5:9"> You ask, ‘Why shouldn't the son pay for his father's sins?’ If the son has done what is fair and right, keeping all my laws, then he'll live, he won't be punished.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“He won't be punished”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; 2 Kings 14:6; Ezekiel 18:4"> It's the person who sins who will die. A son won't pay for his father's sins, and a father won't pay for his son's sins. The good consequences of living right will come to those who are good; the evil consequences of wickedness will come to those who are evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Titus 2:11–14; Ezekiel 33:19"> However, if someone who is wicked stops sinning and keeps my laws, doing what is fair and right, they will certainly live—they won't die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Ezekiel 33:16; Isaiah 43:25"> None of their sins will be held against them. Because they are now doing what's right, they will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; 1 Timothy 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9"> Do I like it when wicked people die? declares the Lord God. Of course not—I would be delighted if they stopped sinning and lived!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; Proverbs 21:16; 2 John 1:8"> But if someone who is living according to what's right stops and does wrong, doing the same offensive things as the wicked, will that person live? Of course not! In fact, all the good things they did previously will be forgotten. That person will die because of their betrayal of me and the sins they've committed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:17; Malachi 3:13–15; Malachi 2:17"> Even so you say, ‘What the Lord does isn't right.’<br />People of Israel, listen to me! Is it what I'm doing that isn't right? Isn't it what you're doing that isn't right?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If someone who lives according to what's right stops doing right and does wrong, they will die. They will die because of wrong things they've done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 55:7"> But if someone wicked stops doing wrong and does what is fair and right, they will save their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Luke 15:17–18; James 2:10–12"> Because they thought about it and gave up their wicked ways, they will certainly live—they won't die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Proverbs 19:3; Ezekiel 18:2"> But the people of Israel say, ‘The Lord's way isn't right.’<br />Are my ways unfair, people of Israel? Isn't it your ways that aren't fair?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 3:2"> Consequently I'm going to judge you, people of Israel! I will judge each of you depending on what you've done, declares the Lord God. Repent and stop rebelling so that your sins won't bring you down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 51:10; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> Get rid of all your rebellious sins! Change your way of thinking and have a new spirit. Why do you want to die, people of Israel?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23; Ezekiel 33:11"> I don't like it when anybody dies, declares the Lord God. So repent so you can live!”</span>
</div>
</div>
