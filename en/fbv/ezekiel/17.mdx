<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man, present this riddle—share it as a parable with the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> Tell them that this is what the Lord God says:<br />There was a great eagle that had large wings, long feathers, and multi-colored plumage. It came to Lebanon and took off the top of the cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> He broke off its highest branch and took it to a country of merchants, and planted it in a city of traders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> He took some of the seed of the land and planted it in good soil beside a large river where he could grow it like a willow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> The seed sprouted and grew into a low, spreading vine, with its branches facing toward him, and its roots went down into the ground beneath. So the vine developed, growing leaves and branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> But there was another great eagle that had large wings and many feathers. This vine sent out its roots toward him. It stretched out its branches to him from where it had been planted, wanting him to water it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> Yet it had been planted in good soil beside a large river so it could grow strong, produce fruit, and become a superb vine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> So you tell them this is what the Lord God says: ‘Will it grow well? Won't it be uprooted and its fruit ripped off so that it withers? All its leaves will dry up. You wouldn't need strong arms or lots of people to pull it up by its roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> Even if it's transplanted, is it going to survive? Won't it wither up completely when the east wind blows? In fact, it will wither right where it was planted.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Tell these rebellious people: ‘Don't you know what this riddle means?’<br />Explain to them, ‘Look, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem, made its king and officials his prisoners, and took them back with him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> He made an agreement with one of the royal family and made him take an oath that he would be a loyal subject as king.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“As king”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>Then he took into exile the country's leaders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> so that the kingdom would be kept in subjection and wouldn't be strong enough to rebel—it would only survive by keeping its agreement with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> However, this king did rebel against Babylon, and sent ambassadors to Egypt to ask for help by supplying horses and many soldiers. Will he do well? Would someone who acts like this succeed? Could he break an agreement and not be punished?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> As I live, declares the Lord God, ‘He is going to die in Babylon, in the country of the king who put him on the throne, whose oath he disregarded and whose agreement he broke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> Pharaoh with his powerful army of many soldiers won't help him in battle, when attack ramps are piled up and siege walls built and many people end up being killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> He disregarded his oath by breaking the agreement. Because he shook hands as a sign of the promise he made, yet rebelled in this way, he won't go unpunished!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> So this is what the Lord God says: As I live, I will punish him for disregarding my oath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“My oath”: clearly the oath was made in the name of the Lord.">b</a></sup>and for breaking my agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> I will throw my net over him and catch him; I will trap him in my snare. I will take him to Babylon and sentence him to punishment there for his treason towards me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> All his best soldiers will be killed in battle, and those who do survive will be scattered in all directions. Then you will know that I, the Lord, have spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> This is what the Lord God says: I'm going to break off a branch from the high top of the cedar, and I will plant it on the top of a high mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> I will plant it on Israel's high mountain so that it can grow branches, produce fruit and become a magnificent cedar. All kinds of birds will live in it, nesting in the shade of its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> All the trees in the countryside will know that I am the Lord. I can bring down the tall and have the low tree grow tall. I can make the green tree dry up, and make the withered tree become green again. I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it.”</span>
</div>
</div>
