</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Ezekiel 16:47–51; Ezekiel 23:4"> Oholibah saw what happened to her sister, but she became even worse than her in her wanting sex and practicing prostitution.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 12:1–2; 2 Kings 17:18–19; Ezekiel 23:31"> I saw that she had also made herself unclean—both sisters were doing the same thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Jeremiah 22:14; Jeremiah 50:2"> However, Oholibah's prostitution was even worse. She saw pictures of Babylonian soldiers wearing bright red uniforms painted on the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:18; 1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 22:21"> They were wearing belts on their waists and large turbans on their heads. They all looked like Babylonian officers in Chaldea, the country where they were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:29; Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1"> When she saw the paintings of them, she wanted to have sex with them and sent messengers to them in Chaldea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:28; Ezekiel 23:22; Genesis 10:10"> The Babylonians came to her and her love bed, and made her unclean with their sexual desire. However, after she had been dishonored by them, she rejected them in disgust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 78:59; PSALMS 106:40; Jeremiah 12:8"> When Oholibah so blatantly prostituted herself, exposing herself naked, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had done to her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 20:7; Ezekiel 16:51"> But she practiced even more prostitution, remembering how she'd been a prostitute in Egypt when she was young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Ezekiel 16:26; Jeremiah 5:8"> She wanted to have sex with her lovers who had genitals like donkeys and who ejaculated like stallions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You went back to the indecent acts from when you were young, when the Egyptians squeezed your nipples and fondled your young breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Revelation 17:16; Jeremiah 6:22–23"> So Oholibah, this is what the Lord God says: I'm going to encourage your lovers to attack you, those you rejected in disgust. I will bring them to attack you from every direction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:21; 2 Kings 24:2; 2 Kings 20:14–17"> the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, the men of Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians along with them—all handsome young men, leaders and commanders, chariot captains and important officers, all riding horses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:14; Ezekiel 26:10; Ezekiel 23:45"> They will attack you from the north,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“From the north”: Septuagint reading.">b</a></sup>invading with a great army, chariots, and wagons. They will come with their shields and helmets and surround you. I will hand you over to them for punishment, and they will judge and punish you following their own laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 8:1–18; Ezekiel 15:6–7"> I feel so strongly about what you've done that I will oppose you, and they will treat you very badly. They will cut off your noses and ears, and they will kill those of you who are left. They will take away your sons and daughters as prisoners, and those of you who are left will be destroyed by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Jeremiah 13:22; Ezekiel 23:29"> They will tear off your clothes and take your beautiful jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:41; Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 23:19"> I'm going to put a stop to your immorality and prostitution, which started in Egypt. You won't think longingly about those times, and you'll forget all about Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:22; Ezekiel 23:17; Jeremiah 34:20"> For this is what the Lord God says: Believe it when I say that I'm going to hand you over to the people you hate, the ones you rejected in disgust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 23:25–26; Ezekiel 23:45–47"> They will treat you with hatred. They will take away everything you've worked for, leaving you stark naked with nothing, so that the indecent acts of your prostitution will be exposed. Your immorality and prostitution</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; PSALMS 106:35–38; Jeremiah 2:18–20"> is why all this has happened to you, because you acted as a prostitute with the nations and made yourself unclean by worshiping their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Jeremiah 7:14–15; Ezekiel 16:47–51"> Because you did what your sister did, I will give you her cup to drink.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Being handed a cup to drink was symbolic of experiencing punishment.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; PSALMS 60:3; Ezekiel 22:4–5"> This is what the Lord God says: You will drink from your sister's cup—a large, deep cup. You will be laughed at and insulted. You'll have to endure much of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> It will make you drunk and full of grief because it's a cup of that brings horror and destruction, the cup your sister Samaria drank from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 18:7"> You will drink it all down, smash it to pieces on the ground, and tear at your breasts. This is what I have spoken, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 32:33"> In summary this is what the Lord God says: Because you have forgotten about me and have ignored me, you will have to experience the consequences of your immorality and prostitution.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 16:2"> Then the Lord said to me: “Son of man, are you going to condemn Oholah and Oholibah? Expose the disgusting things they've done!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:36; Jeremiah 32:35; Jeremiah 7:31"> For they have committed adultery, and they are guilty of murder. They have committed adultery with their idols, and have even offered their children, whom they had for me, as sacrifices to their idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; 2 Kings 21:7; Ezekiel 7:20"> They also did this to me: On the very same day they both made my sanctuary unclean and violated my Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Kings 21:4; Ezekiel 44:7; Jeremiah 23:11"> On the very day that they killed their children for their idols, they went into my sanctuary and made it unclean. Look at what they did right inside my Temple!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 16:13–16"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring back to the two sisters.">d</a></sup>even kept on sending messengers for men to come from far away. When the men arrived, you would get yourselves ready for them by bathing, doing your eye makeup, and putting on your jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Jeremiah 44:17; Isaiah 65:11"> You sat on an expensive couch, with my incense and my oil on a table placed in front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Revelation 12:3; Exodus 32:18–19"> The room was full of the noise of people partying. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Jeremiah 13:23; Ezra 9:7"> Then I said about the old worn-out woman, ‘Now they can have her as a prostitute because that's what she is!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:9–13; Ezekiel 23:3"> So they had sex with her like a prostitute. They had sex with Oholah and Oholibah, those immoral women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hosea 6:5; Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:36–39"> But men who believe in doing what's right will judge them and impose the punishment for committing adultery and murder, because they are adulterers and murderers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 24:9"> This is what the Lord God says: Have a mob attack them! Make them terrified and rob them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40–41; Jeremiah 39:8; Ezekiel 24:21"> The mob will stone them and kill them with their swords, slaughtering their sons and daughters and burning down their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:6; Ezekiel 23:27; Ezekiel 16:41"> This is how I will put an end to immorality in the country, and all the women will be warned not to do what you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 6:7"> They will punish you for your immorality, and you will have to experience the consequences of your idol worship. Then you will know that I am the Lord God.”</span>
</div>
