<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Ezekiel 2:8–9"> “Son of man,” he told me, “eat what you find placed before you. Eat this scroll, then go and speak to the people of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:17; Acts 26:19"> I opened my mouth, and he gave me the scroll to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Revelation 10:9–10; PSALMS 119:103"> “Son of man,” he said to me, “eat and fill yourself up with this scroll I'm giving you.” So I ate the scroll, and it tasted as sweet as honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Acts 1:8; Ezekiel 2:3"> Then he told me, “Son of man, now you are to go to the people of Israel and give my message to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Isaiah 33:19; Jonah 3:2–4"> I'm not sending you to those whose language is foreign to you, to people that you don't know—but to the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 11:20–24; Romans 9:30–33; Jonah 3:5–10"> As I say, I'm not sending you to those whose language is foreign to you, to people that you don't know, whose words you don't understand. On the other hand, if I had sent you to foreigners, they would have listened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Ezekiel 2:4; 1 Samuel 8:7"> But the people of Israel won't want to listen to you, because they don't want to listen to me. All the Israelites are strong-minded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Strong-minded”: literally, “have hard foreheads.”">a</a></sup>and hard-hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Acts 7:51–56; Micah 3:8"> Look! I'm going to make you as tough as them, and as strong-minded as them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ezekiel's name means “God strengthens.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 50:7"> I will make your mind like adamant,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Adamant”: the word used here is often translated “diamond,” but it is not thought that diamonds were known at this time.">c</a></sup>harder than flint. Don't be afraid of what they say or discouraged by the way they look at you, even though they are a rebellious people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 119:11; Job 22:22; Ezekiel 3:1–3"> “Son of man,” he added, “pay close attention to everything I tell you, and think deeply about it yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 33:30"> Go to your people who are in exile. Tell them this is what the Lord God says, whether they listen or not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 11:1"> The Spirit lifted me up, and I heard a really loud noise behind me that sounded like an earthquake. (Praise to the Lord's glory where he lives!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Ezekiel 10:16–17; Ezekiel 10:5"> It was the sound made by the wings of the beings as they brushed against each other and the noise made by the wheels next to them, a really loud noise that sounded like an earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1; Jeremiah 20:7–9; 2 Kings 3:15"> As I said, the Spirit lifted me up and carried me away. As I went I was feeling upset and angry; however the Lord's power had taken full control of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 137:1; Job 2:13; Genesis 50:10"> I arrived at the place where the exiles lived, Tel-abib by the River Kebar. I just sat with them, staying with them there for seven days. I was completely overcome with emotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:7"> After the seven days had passed, a message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Hebrews 13:17; 2 Chronicles 19:10"> “Son of man, I have chosen you as a watchman for the people of Israel. Whenever I give you a message, then you must warn them for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:6; James 5:19–20; Ezekiel 3:20"> For example, if I give a warning to someone who's wicked, saying, ‘You are going to die for sure,’ but you don't warn them, if you don't speak out to warn them to stop their wicked ways so they can go on living, then that wicked person will die in their sins, and I will hold you responsible for their death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:9; Ezekiel 14:20; 1 Timothy 4:16"> But if you warn them, and they don't stop their wicked ways, they will die in their sins, but you will have saved yourself—you won't die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 33:12–13"> Again, if someone who lives right stops doing so and sins and disregards my attempts to correct them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“My attempts to correct them”: literally, “I place stumbling blocks before him.”">d</a></sup>then they will die. If you didn't warn them, they will die in their sins, and the good things they did won't be remembered. In addition, I will hold you responsible for their death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; Proverbs 9:9; Titus 2:15"> However, if you warn those who are living right not to sin, and they don't sin, they will live for sure, because they paid attention to your warning, and you will have saved yourself—you won't die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Ezekiel 37:1; Ezekiel 8:4"> The Lord's power took control of me there, and he told me, “Get up! Go to the valley, and I will talk to you there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Acts 7:55; Revelation 1:17"> So I got up and went to the valley, and there I saw the Lord's glory. It was like the glory I had seen by the River Kebar. I fell facedown on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:19; Daniel 10:8–10"> Then the Spirit came into me and made me stand upright. He told me, “Go into your house and shut the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:8; Acts 9:16; Mark 3:21"> You will be tied up with ropes, son of man. You will be bound so that you won't be able to go out among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:27; Micah 3:6–7; Amos 8:11–12"> I'll make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth. You won't be able to speak and complain to them, even though they are a rebellious people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2–3; Ezekiel 24:27; Ezekiel 3:11"> However, when I talk with you, I will open your mouth so you can tell them that this is what the Lord God says. Those who want to listen will listen, and those who want to refuse will refuse, for they are a rebellious people.”</span>
