<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Revelation 4:2–3; Revelation 1:13"> I looked up and saw what looked like a throne made of lapis lazuli beyond the expanse, way above the heads of the cherubim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1:26.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:5; Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 1:13"> Speaking from there the Lord told the man in linen, “Go in between the wheels underneath the cherubim. Collect as many burning coals as you can. Carry them from among the cherubim and scatter them all over the city.” I watched as he went in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:4; Ezekiel 9:3; Ezekiel 8:16"> The cherubim were standing on the south side of the Temple when the man went in. A cloud filled the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> Then the Lord's glory rose up from above the cherubim and moved to the entrance of the Temple. The cloud filled the Temple, and the brightness of the Lord's glory filled the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Job 40:9; Exodus 19:16"> The noise made by the cherubim's wings could even be heard in the outer courtyard, and sounded like the voice of God Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; PSALMS 99:1; PSALMS 80:1"> When the Lord ordered the man in linen, “Go and get some fire from in between the wheels, from among the cherubim,” the man went in and stood beside one of the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 13:41–42; Ezekiel 1:13; Ezekiel 41:23–26"> Then one of the cherubim reached out and took some of the fire among them. He handed it to the man in linen, who took it and left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:8; Ezekiel 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> (All the cherubim had what looked like human hands under their wings.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:15–17; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 21:20"> I saw four wheels next to the cherubim, one wheel beside each cherub. The wheels shone like topaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; PSALMS 36:6; PSALMS 104:24"> All four wheels looked the same, and had a wheel within a wheel, set crossways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> The cherubim could go in any direction they faced, moving without turning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> The whole of their bodies, including their backs, hands, and wings, were covered in eyes, as were all four wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I heard the wheels being referred to as “the chariot wheels.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This second reference to a wheel is normally used to refer to a chariot wheel. In this sense it is applicable, for the structure referred to is essentially the Lord's “chariot.”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Ezekiel 10:21; 1 Kings 7:36"> Each of them had four faces: the first face was a cherub's face, the second a man's face, the third a lion's face, and the fourth an eagle's face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> Then the cherubim rose up in the air. They were the beings I'd seen beside the River Kebar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved alongside them. Even when they opened their wings to take off, the wheels stayed beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> When the cherubim stopped, the wheels stopped too. When they took off, the wheels did too, because the spirit of the living creatures was in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; PSALMS 78:60–61"> Then the glory of the Lord left the entrance to the Temple and stopped above the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> As I watched, the cherubim lifted their wings and took off, with the wheels alongside them. They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the Lord's Temple, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> These were the beings I had seen beneath the God of Israel by the River Kebar. I knew that they were cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> Each had four faces and four wings and had what looked like human hands under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> Their faces looked like the faces I had seen by the River Kebar. Each of them moved directly ahead.</span>
</div>
