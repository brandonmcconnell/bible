<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> “Son of man, face towards Jerusalem and condemn their places of worship. Prophesy against the people living in Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> and tell them that this is what the Lord says: Watch out, because I'm going to attack you! I'm going to take out my sword and destroy you, both the good and the bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Because I'm going to destroy both the good and the bad, I will attack everyone from the north to the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> Then the whole world will know that I, the Lord, have taken out my sword, and won't put it back again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> You, son of man, you are to groan. Groan as if you're broken up inside, showing terrible sadness while they watch you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> When they ask, ‘What are you groaning for?’ you are to tell them, ‘Because of the news that's coming. All of you will lose your courage and you'll be paralyzed with fear. All of you will be weak with worry—you won't be able to stand up!’ Watch out, because it's coming! It's going to happen! declares the Lord God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Another message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man, prophesy! Tell them this is what the Lord says: There's a sword, a sword that's being sharpened and polished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> It's sharpened for killing and polished so it will flash like lightning! (Are we to be happy, saying, ‘The scepter of my son despises every other stick’?)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew of this verse is unclear and capable of several interpretations. It appears to be a reference to Genesis 49:10that is being quoted to suggest that any threat of enemy invasion is not possible because of God's previous assurances. This appears to be confirmed by another allusion to Genesis 49:10in verse 27 of this chapter. The mention of a “stick” is a dismissive reference to the scepters or rods of power of other nations.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> The sword is being polished right now, ready to be used. It's sharp and polished, ready to be handed over to the killer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> Weep and wail, son of man, and slap your thigh in grief, for the sword is going to be used to attack my people, to attack all the leaders of Israel! They will be thrown away, killed by the sword along with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> They will be tested. What if the scepter that despises others doesn't continue? declares the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> So son of man, prophesy and clap your hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Clap your hands”: not applause, but a sign of horror or of anger. It may even be symbolic of the three attacks mentioned in the verse.">b</a></sup>The sword will attack twice, and then a third time.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="These three attacks may well refer to the three invasions of Nebuchadnezzar.">c</a></sup>It is a sword of death, killing many people by coming at them from every direction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> I have placed a sword at all the gates of their city so that they may lose their courage, and many may fall. Oh no! It's made to flash like lightning and used to kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Slash right and left, whichever way you're facing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> I will also clap my hands, and then my anger will be over. I, the Lord, have spoken.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Another message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> “Son of man, mark the two roads that the king of Babylon's army could take, beginning from the same country. Make a signpost where the road forks, leading to two different cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Have one sign point down the road for an attack on the Ammonite city of Rabbah, and another to attack Judah and the fortified city of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> The king of Babylon is standing at the fork in the road where the two roads meet looking for a prophetic sign: he casts lots using arrows, he asks advice from idols, and he examines the liver of sacrificial animals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> He holds the sign for Jerusalem in his right hand. This is where he is going to set up battering rams, to give the command to attack, to shout the war-cry. There he will order the battering rams to smash the gates, set up an attack ramp, and build a siege wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> To those who have sworn to be loyal to Nebuchadnezzar this will look like a false sign, but it will reveal their guilt and they will be taken prisoner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> So this is what the Lord God says: Because you have revealed your guilt and demonstrated your rebellion, showing your sins in everything you've done, now that you've made all this clear, you will be taken prisoner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> As for you, you unclean, wicked prince of Israel, the time has come to complete your punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; PSALMS 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> This is what the Lord God says: Take off your turban, and your crown. Things won't continue as they were. Give power to the common people and bring down the powerful!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> I'm going to destroy everything, make it all a ruin! It won't be restored until its owner arrives, the one I've given the authority to judge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> Prophesy, son of man, and announce that this is what the Lord God says about the Ammonites and their insults: A sword! A sword is ready for killing, polished to destroy, to flash like lightning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; PSALMS 37:13"> even as your prophets give you false visions and prophecies that are lies. This sword will cut the necks of the wicked, killing them—those whose day has come for their punishment to be completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> Sword, go back to where you came from! I'm going to judge you right where you were created, in your homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> I will deal with you in my anger; I will breathe my fire of anger on you; I will hand you over to cruel men who are experts in destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> You will be burned up like firewood. Your blood will be shed where you live. You will be forgotten, for I, the Lord, have spoken.”</span>
</div>
