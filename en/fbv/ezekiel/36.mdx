<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 37:22"> “And you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel and say: Mountains of Israel, listen to this message from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Ezekiel 25:3; Ezekiel 35:10"> This is what the Lord God says: The enemy said about you, ‘Aha! These old high places now belong to us,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremiah 18:16"> so you must prophesy and announce that this is what the Lord God says: They have turned you into a wasteland, attacking you from every direction, so that you became the property of other nations and people gossiped about you and slandered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Ezekiel 36:6"> So now, mountains of Israel, listen to the message from the Lord God. This is what the Lord God says to the mountains and hills, to the ravines and valleys, to the deserted ruins and abandoned towns, that the other nations around you have looted and mocked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:8; Ezekiel 36:3; PSALMS 137:7"> Yes, this is what the Lord God says: I have spoken passionately against these other nations, and against all the Edomites, who took over my country and made it theirs, happily celebrating as they looted the land and treated me with complete contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 123:3–4; Ezekiel 34:29; PSALMS 74:23"> So prophesy concerning the country of Israel and tell the mountains and hills, the ravines and valleys, that this is what the Lord God says: Just watch, for I have spoken passionately about this because you have had to put up with this mockery from other nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Ezekiel 20:15"> This is what the Lord God says: I hold up my hand and swear that the other nations around you will suffer their own shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 34:26–29"> But you, mountains of Israel, will grow trees that will produce fruit for my people Israel, for soon they'll be coming home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 46:11; Hosea 2:21–23; Ezekiel 36:34"> Look, I am for you and I will help you. Your land will be plowed, and crops will be sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:33; Jeremiah 33:12; Ezekiel 36:37"> You will support the people of Israel as they return and grow in number. People will live in the towns again and rebuild the ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:12; Jeremiah 23:5–8; Jeremiah 31:27"> The number of your people will grow— they will have many children. The animals too will have many young. You mountains, I will make sure that you are inhabited just as you used to be, and I will make you more prosperous than you were before. Then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Ezekiel 36:13; Jeremiah 32:44"> You mountains, I will have my people Israel walk on you again. You will be theirs; you will belong to them, and you will no longer rob your nation of their children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Rob your nation of their children”: this strange phrase is thought by some to refer to the times when the Israelites practiced child sacrifice.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> This is what the Lord God says: Because people say to you, ‘You eat people, and rob your nation of their children,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> from now on you won't eat people or rob your nation of their children, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; PSALMS 89:50; Ezekiel 36:6"> I'm going to stop these nations insulting you, and you won't have to put up with their taunts any more, or make the nation fall, declares the Lord God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Another message from Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Micah 2:10"> “Son of man, when the people of Israel were living in their country, they made it unclean by the way they behaved, by what they did. The way they were behaving in my presence was like the ceremonial uncleanness of a woman's period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Ezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> So I became angry with them because of murders they committed in the country, and because they made it unclean by worshiping idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:12"> I scattered them among the different nations and countries. I judged them according to the way they had behaved and what they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Jeremiah 33:24"> But wherever they went among the nations, they ruined my reputation, because people said about them, ‘These are the Lord's people, but they had to leave his country.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; PSALMS 74:18; Ezekiel 20:9"> So I had to take care of my reputation for holiness which the people of Israel had ruined among the nations where they'd gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 106:8; Ezekiel 36:32; PSALMS 115:1–2"> Tell the people of Israel that this is what the Lord God says: I'm not doing this for you, people of Israel, but for my reputation for holiness, which you ruined among the nations where you went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; Ezekiel 39:7"> I will reveal the holy nature and importance of my reputation, which has been ruined among the nations, the reputation you have ruined among them. Then the nations will know that I am the Lord, declares the Lord God, when I reveal my holiness through you as they watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:21; Ezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> For I will bring you back from among the nations, gathering you from all the different countries, and I will lead you back into your own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremiah 33:8"> I will also pour pure water over you, and you will be clean. I will wash you clean from all your uncleanness and from all your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; PSALMS 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> I will give you a new mind and I will put a new spirit inside you. I will take away your stubborn mind and I will give you a loving mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> I will put my Spirit inside you so you will follow my laws and remember to do what I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27"> Then you will live in the country I gave to your forefathers. You will be my people, and I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Hosea 2:21–23"> I will save you from everything that makes you unclean. I will make sure you have plenty of grain. I won't send you any famines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> I will also make sure you have good harvests from your orchards and fields, so that you won't be mocked by other nations anymore because of famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> Then you will remember your evil ways and terrible things you've done, and you'll hate yourselves for your sins and disgusting actions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Ezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> You need to realize that I'm not doing this for your sake, declares the Lord God. You should be ashamed and embarrassed for what you've done, people of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:10; Jeremiah 33:10"> This is what the Lord God says: On the day I make you clean from all your sins, I will have you live in your towns again and make sure the ruins are rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Jeremiah 25:9–11; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The land that was deserted will be cultivated again instead of looking abandoned to everyone passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremiah 33:9"> Then they will say, ‘This deserted land is now like the garden of Eden. The towns that were demolished, abandoned, and destroyed have been restored and strengthened, and people are living in them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Ezekiel 17:24"> Then those nations around you that are left will acknowledge that I, the Lord, have rebuilt what was demolished, and that I have replanted what was destroyed. I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will carry it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Ezekiel 14:3"> This is what the Lord God says: I will once again answer the prayers of the people of Israel. This is what I will do for them: I will have them increase in number like a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> Just like all the many flocks that are brought to Jerusalem to be sacrificed during the religious festivals, so the towns that were once demolished will be full of flocks of people. Then they will know that I am the Lord.”</span>
