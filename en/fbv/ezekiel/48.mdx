The crops it produces will provide food for those who work in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:6; 1 Kings 4:7–23; Nehemiah 11:1–36"> These workers who farm the land will be from all the Israelite tribes.</span>

</div>

In the middle of them will be the holy section and the Temple sanctuary.</span>
</div>
