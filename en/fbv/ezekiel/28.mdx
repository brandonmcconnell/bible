<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13–14"> “Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre this is what the Lord God says: You're so proud of yourself, saying, ‘I'm a god! I sit on my throne like a god in the middle of the sea.’ But you're only a man. You're not a god, even though you think you are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> You even think you're wiser than Daniel and there's no secret that's hidden from you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Proverbs 23:4–5; Proverbs 18:11"> Yes, you have used your wisdom and cleverness to make yourself rich, collecting plenty of gold and silver for your treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 62:10; PSALMS 52:7; Ezekiel 28:2"> You became wealthy through your superb trading skills, but your wealth only made you proud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> So this is what the Lord God says: Because you think that you're a god,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 30:11"> watch as I bring foreigners to attack you. They are more cruel than any other nation. They will use their swords to destroy you and your wonderful wisdom; they will humble your proud glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Ezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> They will drag you down into the grave. You will die horribly out there in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 31:3; PSALMS 82:7"> Are you still going to claim, ‘I'm a god,’ to those who are killing you? You'll just be another human victim, not a god, in the eyes of your attackers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> You will be killed like some vile person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Killed like some vile person”: literally “die the death of the uncircumcised.”">a</a></sup>by these foreigners. I myself have spoken, declares the Lord God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Another message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> “Son of man, sing a funeral song for the king of Tyre and tell him this is what the Lord God says: Once you were complete and perfect,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Complete and perfect”: the phrase is literally, “seal of measurement,” seal as in God's “seal of approval,” and measurement as something measured to an agreed standard. The word translated “perfect” here is translated “measured” as a verb in Isaiah 40:13, for example; while in Ezekiel 43:10it is translated as a “plan” that is measured.">b</a></sup>full of wisdom and flawless in beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Ezekiel 27:16"> You were there in Eden, God's garden. You wore all kinds of precious stones: carnelian, topaz, and amethyst; beryl, onyx, and jasper; lapis lazuli, turquoise, and emerald.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The identification of precious stones mentioned in Scripture is uncertain.">c</a></sup>They were placed in gold mountings and settings using skilled craftsmanship, and were made on the day when you were created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Ezekiel 28:13"> I gave you the position of guardian cherub, and I anointed you. You lived on God's holy mountain and you walked among the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Ezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> You were innocent in everything you did from the day you were created until you were found to be doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Habakkuk 2:8; Habakkuk 2:17"> You were so busy with all your trading schemes that they destroyed you inside, leading you to sin and filling you with violence. So I sent you away in disgrace from God's mountain, and I removed you from your position as guardian cherub from your place among the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:5; Ezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> You became proud because of your beauty, you ruined your wisdom because you thought you were so wonderful. So I threw you down to the ground and I made sure kings saw what happened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> By all your sins and your dishonest trading you have made your sanctuaries unclean. So I had fire come from inside you, and it burned you up. I turned you into ashes on the ground as everybody there watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> All who know you among the nations are horrified at what happened to you. Disaster has brought you down and you're finished forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> “Son of man, face towards Sidon and prophesy against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> Tell her this is what the Lord God says: Watch out, Sidon, for I'm condemning you, and I will be vindicated by what happens to you. People will know that I am the Lord when I punish her and show my holy character through her experience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> I'm going to send a disease to plague her, and have people killed in her streets. Those who are killed will fall inside the city as the enemy attacks with swords from every side. Then they will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> The people of Israel will no longer have to put up with these people who are thorns in their side, painful brambles and sharp thorns who treat Israel with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:37; Ezekiel 20:41; Ezekiel 36:28"> This is what the Lord God says: When I bring back the people of Israel from the nations where they've been scattered. I will show my holy character through them as everyone watches.<br />Then they will live in their own country, which I gave to my servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:24; Ezekiel 28:22; Jeremiah 32:15"> They will live there in safety, building houses and planting vineyards. They will live there in safety when I punish all those around them who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord their God.”</span>
</div>
