<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Ezekiel 28:12"> “Son of man, sing a funeral song for Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:4; Isaiah 23:11; Isaiah 23:2–3"> Tell Tyre, the city that stands at the gateway to the sea, supplying goods to many overseas nations, that this is what the Lord God says: Tyre, you say to yourself, ‘I'm so beautiful!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:5"> Your borders extend far across the sea. Your builders put you together perfectly like a beautiful ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; Song of Songs 4:8"> They built you with pine timber from Mount Hermon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Hermon,” literally, “Senir,” another name for this mountain.">a</a></sup>They used a cedar from Lebanon to make your mast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; Zechariah 11:2"> They made your oars with oaks from Bashan. They had wood from the island of Cyprus brought to make your deck and inlaid it with ivory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> They made your sail with the best linen from Egypt—this was your flag that everyone recognized. They used cloth dyed blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah to make your awnings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; 1 Kings 9:27; Ezekiel 27:11"> Men from Sidon and Arvad did the rowing in your ship. You had your own skilled sailors from Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 13:5; PSALMS 83:7"> Expert craftsmen from Gebal were onboard to repair any leaks. All the other ships at sea came to trade with you and their sailors bartered for your goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:5; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> Men from Persia, Lydia, and Put were soldiers in your army. They lived among you, hanging up their shields and helmets with you, making you famous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:3–4"> Men of Arvad and Helech defended all your city walls, while those from Gammad protected your towers. They hung their shields all around your walls. They made you so successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; Ezekiel 38:13"> Tarshish traded with you because you had so much of everything. They paid for your goods with silver, iron, tin, and lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> Greece,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Greece”: literally, “Javan.”">b</a></sup>Tubal, and Meshech traded with you. They bought your goods, paying with slaves and objects made of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 38:6; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> People from Beth-togarmah traded horses, war horses, and mules for your goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; 1 Kings 10:22"> People from Dedan traded with you. You had marketplaces in many coastal areas where the people bartered with you using ivory tusks and ebony wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> The Arameans traded with you because you had so much merchandise. They provided turquoise, purple cloth, embroidery, fine linen, coral, and red jasper<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Red jasper”: one possible candidate for a red precious stone. Rubies were apparently not known at this time.">c</a></sup>in exchange for your goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> Judah and the country of Israel traded with you, paying you with wheat from Minnith, pastries<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Pastries”: the word is only ever used here and its meaning is uncertain.">d</a></sup>and honey, olive oil and balm in return for your goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> Because you had many different products, in fact so much of everything, Damascus traded with you using wine from Helbon, wool from Zahar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; PSALMS 45:8; Song of Songs 4:13–14"> Vedan and Javan from Izal bought your goods using wrought iron, cassia, and sweet calamus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Ezekiel 27:15"> Dedan traded with you, providing riding blankets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; Genesis 25:13; 1 Kings 10:15"> Arabia and all the leaders of Kedar traded with you, supplying lambs, rams, and goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; PSALMS 72:10"> The merchants of Sheba and Raamah traded with you, exchanging gold, the very best spices, and precious stones for your goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Haran, Canneh, and Eden traded with you, as did Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8"> In your marketplaces they traded with you, providing the finest blue clothing, embroidery, carpets made with different colored threads, and strong ropes of twisted strands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; PSALMS 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> Trade ships from Tarshish transported your goods. You were like a ship sailing the sea, filled with heavy cargo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 48:7; Ezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> Your rowers took you a long way out on the water, but the east wind broke you apart out there in the open ocean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Ezekiel 27:24; Ezekiel 27:12"> You've lost everything! Your wealth, your goods, your cargo, your crew of sailors, officers, and repairmen, your traders and all the soldiers you had, along with everyone else on board, fall into the depths of the sea on the day of your shipwreck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Ezekiel 31:16"> The land around shakes when your sailors scream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> All the rowers abandon their ships. The sailors and all the ships' officers stand on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> They shout out for you and burst out crying. They mourn for you by throwing dust on their heads and rolling in ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> They shave their heads for you and clothe themselves in sackcloth. They weep for you, crying out in agony and heartfelt mourning</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> As they weep and wail over you, they sing a funeral song for you: ‘Who could compare to Tyre, now destroyed out in the sea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 28:16"> You made many people happy when they received your goods from far across the sea. You made the kings of the earth rich with all your valuable goods and products.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> Now you have been shipwrecked by the sea, sunk in deep water, and your cargo and your people have gone down with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 26:15–18"> Everyone living on the coastlands is shocked at what happened to you. Their kings are horrified; their faces twisted with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; PSALMS 37:10; Zephaniah 2:15"> The traders of the nations hiss at you in derision; disaster has brought you down and you're finished forever.’”</span>
