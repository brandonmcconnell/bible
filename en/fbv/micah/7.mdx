<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> I'm totally miserable! I've become like someone trying to glean summer fruit after the grape harvest is over. I can't find any grapes to eat, and there are no early figs that I love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> All the good people in the country are gone, there's no one who does right left anywhere. Everyone is looking to murder others; they try to trap even their own brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> They are experts at evil;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “they do evil with both hands well.”">a</a></sup>both officials and judges ask for bribes; the powerful demand their evil desires; and they scheme together to get what they want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> Even the best of them is like a thorny bush, the most honest like a thorn hedge. Your day of judgment that was announced by the prophets,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “the day of your watchmen.”">b</a></sup>your time of punishment, has arrived. You're thrown into complete confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; PSALMS 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Don't trust a neighbor, don't rely on a friend. Watch what you say even to the one who lies in your arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For a son treats his father with contempt; a daughter opposes her mother, and a daughter-in-law her mother-in-law. Your enemies are members of your own family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; PSALMS 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> But as for me, I look to the Lord. I will wait for the God who saves me. My God will hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; PSALMS 27:1"> Don't gloat over me, my enemies! Even though I fall, I will rise again. Though I sit in darkness, the Lord is my light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> I will endure the Lord's anger because I have sinned against him. But after that he will argue my case and give me justice. He will bring me into the light and I will see his integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; PSALMS 42:10"> Then my enemies will see it and cover their faces in shame for taunting me, asking, “Where is the Lord your God?” With my own eyes I will see what happens to them; they will be trodden down like mud in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> On that day it will be time to build your walls. On that day your boundaries will be extended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> On that day people will come from Assyria and the cities of Egypt, from Egypt to the Euphrates River, from sea to sea, from mountain to mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> But the rest of the earth will become desolate because of what those who live there have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; PSALMS 100:3"> Protect your people with your shepherd's rod. Take care of your flock, your special people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “inheritance.” Also in verse 18.">c</a></sup>who live alone in the wilderness and in cultivated land. Let them feed in Basham and Gilead as they did long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; PSALMS 68:22"> Like the time when you left the land of Egypt, I will do miracles for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> The nations will see, and they will be humiliated despite their strength. They will cover their mouths with their hands, and their ears shall become deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; PSALMS 72:9; PSALMS 18:45"> They will lick the dust like snakes; they will be like the creepy-crawlies of the earth. They will come trembling from their fortresses to meet the Lord our God, fearful and afraid before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; PSALMS 103:8–9"> Who is a God like you who forgives sin and passes over the rebellion of those who are left of his special people? You do not remain angry forever because you delight in showing faithful love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> You will have compassion on us again. You will tread our sins under your feet, and you will throw our sins into the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> You will give your trustworthy truth to the people of Jacob, your faithful love to the people of Abraham, as you promised our forefathers long ago.</span>
