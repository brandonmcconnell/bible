<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:10; Jeremiah 5:4–5; PSALMS 82:1–5"> Then I said, “Please listen, leaders of the descendants of Jacob, people of Israel. You are meant to know the difference between right and wrong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Proverbs 28:4; PSALMS 53:4"> you who hate good and love evil! You tear off their skin, and rip the flesh from their bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 14:4; Ezekiel 11:6–7; Ezekiel 11:3"> You eat the flesh of my people, you strip off their skin, you break their bones. You chop them up like meat for the saucepan, like flesh for the cooking pot.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 8:18"> Then they'll cry out to the Lord, but he won't answer them. He will turn away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “hide his face.”">a</a></sup>from them at that time because of the evil things they have done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 14:14–15; Ezekiel 22:25–29"> This is what the Lord says about the prophets who deceive my people. To those who feed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “biting with their teeth.”">b</a></sup>them they prophesy peace to them, while to those who do not they prophesy war against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20–22; Amos 8:9–10; Isaiah 29:10"> Therefore it will be like night for you, with no visions; dark, with no predictions. The sun will set on these prophets; their day will end in darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 13:4; Isaiah 44:25; 1 Samuel 28:6"> The seers will be disgraced, the fortune-tellers put to shame, and they will cover their faces because none of them receive any answer from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; 1 Corinthians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> But as for me, I am filled with power, with the spirit of the Lord. I am filled with justice and strength to make clear to the descendants of Jacob their rebellion, and to the people of Israel their sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; PSALMS 58:1–2; Proverbs 17:15"> Listen to me, leaders of the descendants of Jacob and the people of Israel, you who hate justice and distort all that is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17; Matthew 27:25"> You are building Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Isaiah 1:23; Micah 3:5"> Your leaders judge for a bribe, and your priests teach for a price, and your prophets only prophesy for pay. Yet they all lean on the Lord saying, “Isn't the Lord here with us? Nothing bad can happen to us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; PSALMS 79:1; Micah 4:1–2"> Therefore because of you Zion will become a plowed field and Jerusalem a heap of rubble, and the Temple mount will be overgrown.</span>
</div>
</div>
