<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:30; Job 16:10; Matthew 26:67"> Gather your troops together, Jerusalem!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Jerusalem”: literally, “daughter of troops.”">a</a></sup>An enemy is besieging us. They will strike Israel's leader on the cheek with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; John 7:42; Luke 1:31–33"> But from you, Bethlehem Ephrathah (though you are only a small place in Judah), will come a ruler of Israel to do my will. His existence is from the ages of eternity past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–2; Romans 9:27–28; Micah 4:7"> So the Lord will give up on them until the woman in labor has given birth. Then the rest of his brothers will return to the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:10–11; Micah 7:14; Luke 1:32"> He will stand up and feed his flock in the strength of the Lord, in the majesty of the name of the Lord his God. They will live in safety because his greatness is recognized all over the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:14; Zechariah 10:3; Ephesians 2:14–17"> He will be our source of peace when the Assyrians invade our land and destroy our fortresses. Then we will appoint many strong leaders,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “seven shepherds and eight leaders.” The numbers are not considered of particular significance; they simply indicate there would be sufficient for the situation.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:8–11; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 14:2"> and they will rule Assyria with their swords, the land of Nimrod with drawn swords. He shall rescue us from the Assyrians when they invade our land, marching across our borders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:3; Isaiah 44:3; PSALMS 72:6"> Then those who are left of the people of Jacob shall be in the center of many nations, like dew from the Lord, like showers on the grass, which wait for no one, and which no one can delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Micah 4:13; Zechariah 9:15"> Those who are left of the people of Jacob shall be among many nations, in the center of many peoples. They will be like a lion among the wild animals of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep, clawing and tearing as it passes through, with no one to come to the rescue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; PSALMS 21:8; Isaiah 14:2–4"> Lift your hand in triumph against your enemies; all of them will be destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Hosea 14:3; PSALMS 20:7–8"> On that day, says the Lord, I will kill your horses and break your chariots.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Lord is intent on removing everything the people trusted in apart from himself—in this case their military power.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 38:11; Hosea 10:14"> I will tear down your city walls and demolish your fortresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–12; Isaiah 8:19–20; Isaiah 2:20"> I will stop the witchcraft you practice; there will be no more fortune-tellers for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Hosea 14:8"> I will smash down the idols and stone pillars that stand among you: you shall not bow down and worship idols that you make with your own hands any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:13"> I will pull up the Asherah poles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Idol poles set up as part of pagan worship.">d</a></sup>that you have and destroy your pagan places.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “cities.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 149:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Micah 5:8"> In anger and fury I will execute vengeance on those nations that do not obey me.</span>
</div>
