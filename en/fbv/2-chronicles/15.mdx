<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> The Spirit of God came upon Azariah the son of Oded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> He went out to meet Asa and said to him, “Listen to me, Asa and all of Judah and Benjamin. The Lord is with you while you are with him. If you look for him, you will find him; but if you abandon him, he will abandon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> For many years Israel was without the true God, without a priest to teach them, and without the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> But when they were in trouble they returned to the Lord, the God of Israel—they looked for him, and they found him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; PSALMS 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> During those times travel was dangerous, for all the inhabitants of the lands were in great turmoil. People everywhere had terrible problems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> Nation fought nation, and town fought town, for God threw them into a panic with all kinds of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> But you need to be strong, not weak, for you will be rewarded for the work you do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> When Asa heard these words of prophecy from Azariah the prophet, son of Oded, he was encouraged. He removed the vile idols from the whole territory of Judah and Benjamin and from the towns he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim. Then he repaired the altar of the Lord that stood in front of the porch of the Lord's Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> Then Asa summoned all of Judah and Benjamin, along with those Israelites from the tribes of Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon who were living among them, for many people had deserted Israel and come over to Asa when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> They gathered in Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of Asa's reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> That day they sacrificed to the Lord seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep from the plunder they had brought back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> Then they made an agreement to conscientiously and completely follow the Lord, the God of their forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; PSALMS 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> They also agreed that anyone who refused to follow the Lord, the God of Israel, would be put to death, whether young or old, man or woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; PSALMS 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> They declared their oath with a loud shout, accompanied by trumpets and blasts from rams' horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> The whole of Judah was happy at the oath they had conscientiously sworn. They looked for him sincerely, and they found him. The Lord gave them peace from all their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> King Asa also removed Maacah from her position as queen mother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Actually she was Asa's grandmother.">a</a></sup>for making an offensive Asherah pole. Asa cut down her vile idol, crushed it up, and burned it in the Kidron Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> While the high places were not removed from Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In 14:3and 14:5the removal of the high places is recorded. Of course this did not refer to Israel, the northern kingdom, but only the territory over which Asa had authority.">b</a></sup>Asa was completely devoted to the Lord all his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> He brought into God's Temple the silver and gold articles he and his father had dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> There was no more war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.</span>
