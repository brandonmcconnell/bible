<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Kings 2:12; Genesis 39:2"> Solomon, son of David, strengthened his hold over the kingdom, and the Lord God was with him and made him extremely powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; 1 Chronicles 27:1; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> Solomon sent for all the Israelite leaders, to the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, to the judges, and to every family leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:29; 1 Chronicles 16:39; Leviticus 1:1"> Solomon went with the whole assembly to the high place at Gibeon, for this was the site of God's Tent of Meeting that Moses, the Lord's servant, had made in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Chronicles 15:25–28; 2 Samuel 6:2"> David had brought up the Ark of God from Kiriath-jearim to the place in Jerusalem where he had set up a tent for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 38:1–7; 1 Chronicles 2:19–20"> However, the bronze altar made by Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, was there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In Gibeon.">a</a></sup>in front of the Tent of the Lord, so that is where Solomon and the assembly went to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Kings 8:63; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> Solomon went up to the bronze altar before the Lord, in front of the Tent of Meeting. There he presented one thousand burnt offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; 1 John 5:14–15; John 16:23"> That night God appeared to Solomon and told him, “Ask what you want me to give you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5; Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 22:51–1"> Solomon responded to God, “You showed trustworthy love without limit to my father David, and you have made me king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Genesis 13:16; PSALMS 132:11–12"> Lord God, please keep the promise you made to my father David. You have made me king over a nation that has as many people as the dust of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 4:7; Proverbs 2:2–6"> Please give me wisdom and knowledge to lead<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Lead this people”: literally, “go out and come in before this people.”">b</a></sup>this people—for who can rule with justice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Rule with justice”: the word here really means “judge.”">c</a></sup>this great people of yours?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 23:7; 1 Samuel 16:7"> God told Solomon: “Because this is what you really wanted, and you didn't ask for wealth, possessions, or honor, or the death of those who hate you, or for a long life, but instead you asked for wisdom and knowledge so you can rule with justice my people that I have made you king over;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; 2 Chronicles 9:22; Matthew 6:33"> wisdom and knowledge is given to you. I will give you wealth, possessions, and honor as well, much more than any king who came before you has had, or who comes after you will ever have.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 4:24–25"> Then Solomon returned to Jerusalem from the Tent of Meeting in Gibeon, and he ruled over Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26–29; 2 Chronicles 9:25"> Solomon built up an army of chariots and horses. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horses, which he placed in the chariot cities, and also with him in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:27; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> The king made silver and gold as plentiful in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar wood as plentiful as sycamore-fig trees in the foothills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:28; 1 Kings 10:28–29"> Solomon imported horses for himself from Egypt and Kue; the king's traders bought them in Kue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29"> A chariot could be imported from Egypt for six hundred silver shekels, and a horse for a hundred and fifty. In the same way they exported them to all the Hittite kings and the Aramean kings.</span>
</div>
