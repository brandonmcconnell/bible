<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then Solomon spoke, “The Lord said that he lives in deep darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4–6; PSALMS 135:21; 1 Chronicles 28:6"> However, I have built you a magnificent Temple, a place for you to live forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:14; 2 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 8:55–61"> Then the king turned and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, as they all stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; PSALMS 68:32–35"> He said, “Praise the Lord God of Israel, who has kept the promise he made to my father David when he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; 1 Kings 8:16; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> ‘Ever since the day I brought my people out of the land of Egypt, I have not chosen a town from any tribe of Israel where a Temple could be built to honor me, and I have not chosen anyone to be ruler over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:4; PSALMS 132:13; 2 Chronicles 12:13"> But now I have chosen Jerusalem so that I will be honored there, and I have chosen David to rule over my people Israel.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:1; 1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 22:7"> My father David really wanted to build this Temple to honor the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; 1 Kings 8:18–21"> But the Lord told my father David, ‘You really wanted to build me a Temple to honor me—and it was good for you to want to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–13; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 1 Chronicles 17:11–12"> But you are not going to build the Temple. Your son, one of your children, will build the Temple to honor me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> Now the Lord has kept the promise he made. For I have taken the place of my father, and I sit on the throne of Israel as the Lord said, and I have built the Temple to honor the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 5:7; 1 Kings 8:21"> I have placed the Ark there, which has inside it the agreement the Lord made with the sons of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 63:4; Isaiah 50:10; Job 11:13"> Then Solomon stood in front of the altar of the Lord before the whole assembly of Israel and spread out his hands in prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; PSALMS 95:6"> Solomon had made a bronze platform five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high. He had set it in the middle of the courtyard, and he was standing on it. Then he knelt down before the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> He said, “Lord God of Israel, there is no god like you in heaven or on earth, keeping your agreement of trustworthy love with your servants who follow you with complete devotion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 8:24"> You have kept the promise you made to your servant, my father David. With your own mouth you made that promise, and with your own hands you have fulfilled it today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; John 15:14–15; 2 Chronicles 7:18"> So now, Lord God of Israel, please keep the promise you made to your servant David, my father, when you told him, ‘If your descendants pay close attention to follow my way, and to keep my law as you have done, you will never fail to have one of them to sit on the throne of Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:5; Isaiah 41:17; 2 Chronicles 6:4"> Now, Lord God of Israel, please fulfill this promise you made to your servant David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; PSALMS 139:7–10"> But will God really live here on earth among people? The heavens, even highest heaven, cannot contain you, much less this Temple I have built!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> Please listen to the prayer of your servant and his request, Lord my God. Please hear the appeals and the prayers that your servant is presenting before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15; Daniel 6:10; PSALMS 121:5"> May you watch over this Temple day and night, caring for the place where you said you would be honored. May you hear the prayer your servant prays toward this place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Isaiah 43:25"> and hear the request of your servant and your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Please hear from heaven where you live. May you hear and forgive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> When someone sins against another and is required to take an oath declaring the truth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Declaring the truth”: implied.">a</a></sup>before your altar in this Temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremiah 51:56"> listen from heaven, act and judge your servants. Pay back the guilty; vindicate and reward those who do right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> When your people Israel are defeated by an enemy because they have sinned against you, and if they come back in repentance to you, praying for forgiveness in this Temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of your people Israel, and bring them back to the land you gave them and their forefathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> If the skies are closed shut and there is no rain because your people have sinned against you, if they pray toward this place and if they come back in repentance to you, turning away from their sin because you have punished them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35–36; Jeremiah 5:24; Zechariah 10:1"> then hear from heaven and forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. Teach them the right way so that they can follow it, and please send rain on the land that you have given to your people to own.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> If there is famine in the land, or disease, or blight or mildew on the crops, or if there are locusts or caterpillars, or if an enemy comes to lay siege to the towns in the land—it can be whatever kind of plague or whatever kind of disease—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 32:2–6; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13"> then whatever kind of prayer or whatever kind of appeal is made by anyone or all your people Israel, in fact anyone who, knowing their problems and pains, prays toward this Temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; 1 Samuel 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> then hear from heaven, the place where you live, and forgive. Give according to the way they live their lives, for you know what people are really like inside, and you alone know the true character of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; PSALMS 128:1"> Then they will respect you and follow your ways all the time they live in the land you gave to our forefathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–7; 1 Kings 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> As for the foreigners who do not belong to your people Israel but who come from a distant land, having heard of your great nature and power and ability to help, when they come and pray toward this Temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; 1 Kings 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> then hear from heaven, the place where you live, and give them what they're asking. That way, everyone on earth will come to know and respect you, just as your own people Israel do. They will also know that this Temple I have built honors you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:6–13; 2 Chronicles 18:31; 2 Chronicles 20:4"> When your people go to fight against their enemies, wherever you send them, and when they pray to you towards the city you have chosen and the house I have built to honor you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; PSALMS 9:3–4; Jeremiah 5:28"> then hear from heaven what they are praying and asking for, and support their cause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> If they sin against you—and there is nobody who does not sin—you may become angry with them and hand them over to an enemy who takes them away as prisoners to a foreign land, near or far.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Isaiah 64:6–12"> But if they think again in their land of captivity and repent and plead for mercy from you, saying, ‘We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted wickedly,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> and they come back to you with complete sincerity in their thoughts and attitudes there in their land of captivity; and they pray towards the land you gave their forefathers and the city you chose and the Temple I have built to honor you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; 2 Chronicles 6:35; PSALMS 25:18"> then hear from heaven, the place where you live, and respond and support their cause. Forgive your people who have sinned against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; PSALMS 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now my God, please open your eyes, and may your ears pay attention to the prayers offered in this place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 1 Chronicles 28:2; PSALMS 132:8–10"> ‘Come, Lord, and enter your home,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “resting place,” but with the sense that this is the usual place where someone resides, hence “home.”">b</a></sup>together with your Ark of power. May your priests wear salvation like clothing; may your faithful people shout for joy in your goodness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; PSALMS 132:1; Isaiah 61:1"> Lord God, don't reject the king you have chosen. Remember your trustworthy love for your servant David.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Verses 41 and 42 come from Psalms 132.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
