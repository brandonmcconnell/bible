<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Kings 15:1–8"> Abijah became king of Judah in the eighteenth year of the reign of Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Kings 15:6–7; Judges 19:16"> He reigned in Jerusalem for three years. His mother's name was Micaiah, daughter of Uriel—she was from Gibeah. Abijah and Jeroboam were at war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:12–13; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 1 Samuel 17:1–3"> Abijah went out to fight with an army of 400,000 brave warriors, while Jeroboam opposed him with his army of 800,000 chosen warriors of great strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:22; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Genesis 10:18"> Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim, in the hill country of Ephraim, and said, “Listen to me, Jeroboam and all of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50"> Don't you understand that the Lord, the God of Israel, gave the kingdom of Israel to David and his descendants forever by a binding agreement?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Binding agreement”: literally, “a covenant of salt.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:26; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Chronicles 10:19"> Yet Jeroboam, son of Nebat, just a servant of Solomon, son of David, had the audacity to rebel against his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 2 Chronicles 12:13; Acts 17:5"> Then some good-for-nothing evil men gathered round him and defied Rehoboam, son of Solomon, when he was young and inexperienced, and couldn't confront them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 2 Chronicles 11:15; Luke 19:14"> Now do you really think you can oppose the kingdom of the Lord, held by David's descendants? You may be a large horde, and have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam made for you as gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14–15; Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 29:35"> But didn't you drive out the priests of the Lord, Aaron's descendants, and the Levites, and make priests for yourselves just like people in other nations do? Now anyone who wants to can come and dedicate himself, sacrificing a young bull and seven rams, and he can become a priest of things that really are not gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Zechariah 13:9"> But for us, the Lord is our God! We have not abandoned him. We have priests serving the Lord who are descendants of Aaron, and we have Levites who help them in their ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Leviticus 24:2–9; Exodus 29:38"> Morning and evening they present burnt offerings and burn fragrant incense to the Lord. They place the rows of showbread on the purified table, and light the lamps of the gold lampstand every evening. We are doing what the Lord our God told us to do, while you have abandoned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:39; Romans 8:31; Numbers 10:8–9"> God is leading us! His priests blow their trumpets to go into battle against you. People of Israel, don't fight against the Lord, the God of your fathers, for you won't win!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; Joshua 8:9; Proverbs 21:30"> But Jeroboam had sent troops around to attack from the rear, so that while he and the main force was in front of Judah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here Judah refers to the southern kingdom, and Israel to the northern.">b</a></sup>the ambush was behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; Exodus 14:10; Joshua 8:20"> Judah turned around and realized that they were having to fight front and rear. They cried out to the Lord for help. Then the priests blew their trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:12; Joshua 11:8; PSALMS 118:4–7"> and the men of Judah gave a loud shout. When they shouted, God struck Jeroboam and all Israel in front of Abijah and Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; Judges 11:21; Joshua 10:12"> The Israelites ran away from Judah, and God handed them over to Judah, defeated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 13:12"> Abijah and his men hit them hard, and 500,000 of Israel's best warriors were killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Nahum 1:7"> So the Israelites were subdued at that time, and the people of Judah were victorious because they relied on the Lord, the God of their forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; Joshua 15:9; Joshua 10:39"> Abijah chased Jeroboam and captured some towns from him: Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron, along with their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:38; 1 Kings 14:20; Acts 12:23"> Jereboam never regained his power during Abijah's reign. Eventually the Lord struck him down and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Samuel 5:12–13; Judges 10:4"> But Abijah grew stronger and stronger. He married fourteen wives and had twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 24:27"> The rest of what Abijah did—what he said and what he accomplished—is recorded in the history written by Iddo the Prophet.</span>
</div>
