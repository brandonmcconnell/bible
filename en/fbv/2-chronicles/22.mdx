<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 8:24–29; 2 Chronicles 36:1"> The people of Jerusalem made Ahaziah, Jehoram's youngest son, king in succession to his father, since the invaders who had entered the camp with the Arabians had killed all the older sons. So Ahaziah, son of Jehoram, became king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 16:28; 2 Kings 8:26; 2 Chronicles 21:6"> Ahaziah was twenty-two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Twenty-two”: according to 2 Kings 8:26. Here his age is given in the Hebrew text as forty-two.">a</a></sup>when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for one year. His mother's name was Athaliah, the granddaughter of Omri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 27:12–13; Genesis 6:4–5; Judges 17:4–5"> Ahaziah also followed the evil ways of the family of Ahab, for his mother encouraged him to do wicked things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 24:17–18; Proverbs 19:27"> He did what was evil in the Lord's sight, just as the family of Ahab had done. For after his father's death they were his counselors, to his ruin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28–29; 2 Chronicles 19:2; PSALMS 1:1"> He also followed their advice in joining with Joram, son of Ahab, king of Israel, in attacking Hazael, king of Aram, in Ramoth-gilead. The Arameans wounded Joram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15; 2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 10:13–14"> and he returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds he'd received in Ramah fighting against Hazael king of Aram. Ahaziah, son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went to Jezreel to visit Joram, son of Ahab, because Joram was wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:15; 2 Kings 9:21; Malachi 4:3"> Ahaziah's downfall came from God when he went to see Joram. When Ahaziah got there, he went with Joram to meet Jehu, son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Actually “grandson.” His father was Jehoshaphat.">b</a></sup>of Nimri. The Lord had anointed Jehu to destroy Ahab and his family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 10:10–14"> While Jehu was carrying out judgment on the family of Ahab, he came across the leaders of Judah and Ahaziah's relatives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Azariah's relatives”: Septuagint reading. See also 2 Kings 10:13. Hebrew: “sons of Ahaziah's brothers.”">c</a></sup>who were assisting Ahaziah, and he killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 21:20–1"> Then Jehu went looking for Ahaziah. His men found him in Samaria and captured him, and took him to Jehu where they killed him. They buried him, for they said, “He is the grandson of Jehoshaphat, who was completely committed to following the Lord.” There was no one left from Ahaziah's family to rule the kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–3; 2 Chronicles 22:2–4"> When Athaliah Ahaziah's mother found out that her son was dead, she proceeded to kill all who remained of the royal family of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 15:4; 2 Kings 11:2; Isaiah 65:8"> But Jehoshabeath, daughter of King Jehoram, grabbed Joash, son of Ahaziah, and took him from the sons of the king who were about to be killed, and she placed him and his nurse in a bedroom. Because Jehoshabeath, the daughter of King Jehoram and the wife of Jehoiada the priest, was Ahaziah's sister, she hid Joash from Athaliah so she couldn't kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 12:8; PSALMS 73:18–19; Jeremiah 12:1"> And they kept Joash hidden with them in God's Temple for six years while Athaliah ruled the country.</span>
</div>
</div>
