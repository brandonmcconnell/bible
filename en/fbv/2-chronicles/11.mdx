<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; PSALMS 33:10; PSALMS 33:16"> When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he gathered the men from the households of Judah and Benjamin— 180,000 chosen warriors—to go and fight against Israel to bring the kingdom back to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> But a message from the Lord came to Shemaiah the man of God that said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> “Tell Rehoboam, son of Solomon, king of Judah, and all the Israelites living in Judah and Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> ‘This is what the Lord says: Don't fight against your relatives. Every one of you, go home! For what has happened is down to me.’” So they obeyed what the Lord told them and did not fight against Jeroboam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> Rehoboam stayed in Jerusalem, and he strengthened the defenses of the towns in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> He built up Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> Zorah, Aijalon and Hebron. These are the fortified towns in Judah and in Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> He strengthened their fortresses and put commanders in charge of them, together with supplies of food, olive oil, and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> He stored shields and spears in all the towns and made them very strong. So he held Judah and Benjamin under his rule.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The priests and Levites throughout Israel chose to side with Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> The Levites even left their pasturelands and properties behind, and came to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons refused to allow them to serve as priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> Jeroboam chose his own priests for the high places<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“High places”: associated with pagan shrines.">a</a></sup>and for the goat and calf idols he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; PSALMS 84:5–7"> Those from every tribe of Israel who were committed to worshiping their God followed the Levites to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the Lord, the God of their forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> So they supported the kingdom of Judah and for three years they were loyal to Rehoboam, son of Solomon, because they followed the way of David and Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> Rehoboam married Mahalath, who was the daughter of David's son Jerimoth and of Abihail, the daughter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Probably granddaughter.">b</a></sup>of Eliab, son of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> She was the mother of his sons Jeush, and Shamariah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> After her he married Maacah Absalom's daughter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Probably granddaughter.">c</a></sup>and she was the mother of his sons Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> Rehoboam loved Maacah Absalom's daughter more than all his other wives and concubines. He had a total of eighteen wives and sixty concubines, twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Rehoboam made Abijah son of Maacah crown prince among his brothers, planning to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> Rehoboam was also wise to place some of his sons throughout the land of Judah and Benjamin, and to all the fortified towns. He gave them plenty of supplies and sought many wives for them. He worked to arrange many wives for them.</span>
</div>
</div>
