<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 83:5–8; Isaiah 16:6; 2 Chronicles 32:1"> Then, after this, the Moabites and Ammonites, as well as some of the Meunites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Meunites”: according to some manuscripts of the Septuagint. The Hebrew repeats the word “Ammonites.”">a</a></sup>came to attack Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:7; 1 Samuel 23:29; Joshua 15:62"> Some people came and told Jehoshaphat, “A large army is coming to fight you from Edom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“From Edom,” more likely than “from Aram,” as most Hebrew manuscripts read.">b</a></sup>from the other side of the Dead Sea. They have already reached Hazazon-tamar,” (otherwise called En-gedi).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Samuel 7:6"> Jehoshaphat was afraid, and went to ask the Lord what to do. He also ordered everyone throughout Judah to fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 60:10–12; PSALMS 69:35"> So the people of Judah gathered in Jerusalem to pray to the Lord—in fact, they came from all the towns of Judah to commit themselves to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:15–19; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> Jehoshaphat stood before the assembled people of Judah and Jerusalem at the Temple, in front of the new courtyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Daniel 4:17"> and said, “Lord, God of our forefathers, aren't you the God of heaven? Don't you rule over all earthly kingdoms? You possess strength and power, and no one can stand against you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; James 2:23; PSALMS 44:2"> Our God, didn't you drive out before your people Israel those who were living in this land? Didn't you give this land to the descendants of your friend Abraham forever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:10; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> They are living in the land and have built a Temple for you here to honor you, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:28–30; 2 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Kings 8:33"> ‘If disaster strikes us, whether it's invasion or judgment, disease or famine, we will stand in front of this Temple and before you, for this Temple is yours. We will cry out to you to help us in our suffering, and you will hear us and save us.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 6:24-30.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 2:9"> Look, here come the armies of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir, those very countries you did not let Israel invade when they came out of Egypt. Israel left them alone and did not destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 83:3–12; Judges 11:23–24; Jeremiah 18:20"> See how they're rewarding us, coming to steal the land you gave us to possess forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 121:1–2; PSALMS 25:15; PSALMS 141:8"> Our God, won't you punish them, because we don't have the power to confront such a great army that is marching against us? We don't know what to do. We're looking to you for help.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:1; Acts 21:5; Deuteronomy 29:10"> All the men of Judah stood before the Lord, together with their wives and children and babies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Acts 10:31"> Then the Spirit of the Lord came over Jahaziel while he was standing there in the assembly. He was the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, a Levite from the descendants of Asaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 14:13–14"> He said, “Listen, everyone from Judah, people of Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat. This is what the Lord has to say to you: Don't be afraid or discouraged because of this large army. This isn't your battle—it's God's!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Tomorrow march down to face them. You will see them coming up the pass at Ziz—you'll find them at the end of the valley in front of the desert of Jeruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–14; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 41:10"> But you do not need to fight this battle. Just stand still and watch the Lord's victory. He is with you, Judah and Jerusalem! Don't be afraid or discouraged! March down to face them, for the Lord is with you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Genesis 24:26"> Jehoshaphat bowed down with his face to the ground, and all the people of Judah and Jerusalem fell to the ground before the Lord, worshiping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:16–22; 1 Chronicles 16:42; 1 Chronicles 16:5"> Then the Levites from the Kohathites and Korahites stood to praise the Lord, the God of Israel, shouting loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:31; John 14:1; John 11:40"> They got up early the next morning and went to the desert of Tekoa. As they left, Jehoshaphat stood up and said, “Listen to me, people of Judah and Jerusalem. Trust in the Lord your God, and you will be vindicated; trust in his prophets, and you will be successful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 107:1; PSALMS 106:1; 2 Chronicles 5:13"> After discussion with the people, he appointed singers to praise the Lord for his glorious, holy goodness. They led the way at the front of the army, singing, “Praise the Lord, for his trustworthy love lasts forever!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:20; Isaiah 19:2; Ezekiel 38:21"> As soon as they started singing and praising, the Lord ambushed the men of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir who were coming to attack Judah, and they were defeated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Ezekiel 35:2–3"> The men of Ammon and Moab turned on the men from Mount Seir, killing all of them. Once they'd finished wiping out the army from Seir, they turned on each other, destroying themselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“They turned on each other, destroying themselves”: literally, “each helped his neighbor to destruction.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36; PSALMS 110:6; 1 Chronicles 5:22"> So when the men of Judah came to the watchtower in the desert, they looked out to see the enemy army and all they saw were corpses lying on the ground! No one had escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:19–20; 2 Kings 7:9–16; Numbers 31:15"> When Jehoshaphat and his people came to collect the plunder, they found a great deal of cattle, equipment, clothing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Clothing”: Some manuscripts and the Vulgate. Most manuscripts have “corpses.”">e</a></sup>and other valuable articles, more than they could carry. It took three days to collect the plunder because there was so much of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:15; Genesis 32:20; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> On the fourth day they gathered in the Valley of Blessing. They gave it this name because this was where they blessed the Lord. It is still called the Valley of Blessing to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:43; Hebrews 6:20; PSALMS 30:1"> Then all the men of Judah and Jerusalem celebrated as they returned to Jerusalem, with Jehoshaphat leading them, full of joy for the Lord's victory over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 14:2–3; PSALMS 57:8; PSALMS 92:3"> They entered Jerusalem and went straight to the Temple of the Lord, accompanied by music from harps, lyres, and trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; 2 Kings 7:6; Exodus 23:27"> All the kingdoms around were in awe of God when they heard that the Lord had fought against the enemies of Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 17:10.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Samuel 7:1"> Jehoshaphat and his kingdom were at peace, for God gave him rest—there were no attacks from any direction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 22:41–44"> So Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah, having become king when he was thirty-five years old, and he reigned in Jerusalem for twenty-five years. His mother's name was Azubah, daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11–13"> Jehoshaphat followed the way of his father Asa and did not turn away from it. He did what was right in the Lord's sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:14"> However, the high places were not removed, and the people were not committed to the God of their forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:7"> The rest of what Jehoshaphat did, from beginning to end, is written down in the chronicles of Jehu, son of Hanani, recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48–49; 2 Kings 1:2–16"> Later in his life, Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, allied himself with Ahaziah, king of Israel, who did wicked things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; 2 Chronicles 9:21; 1 Kings 9:26"> They agreed to work together and send ships to Tarshish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A joint trading venture.">g</a></sup>The ships were built in Ezion-geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> But Eliezer, son of Dodavahu of Mareshah, prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, “Because you have made an alliance with Ahaziah, the Lord will destroy what you're doing.” The ships were wrecked and couldn't sail to Tarshish.</span>
