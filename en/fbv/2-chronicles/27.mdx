<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:12; Isaiah 1:1; Matthew 1:9"> Jotham was twenty-five when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for sixteen years. His mother's name was Jerushah, daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 15:34–35; Acts 5:13; 2 Chronicles 26:4"> He did what was right in the Lord's sight as his father Uzziah had done, and he did not enter the Lord's Temple as his father had.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“As his father had”: implied.">a</a></sup>But the people still acted corruptly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; Jeremiah 20:2"> Jotham rebuilt the Upper Gate of the Lord's Temple, and did extensive building work on the wall at Ophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–10; 2 Chronicles 26:9–10; Joshua 14:12–13"> He built towns in the hill country of Judah, and fortresses and towers in the mountains and forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 11:4–33; 2 Samuel 10:1–14; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> Jotham fought with the king of the Ammonites and defeated them. The Ammonites gave him every year for three years one hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand cors of wheat and ten thousand of barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:5; 2 Chronicles 19:3"> Jotham grew powerful because he made sure what he did followed the ways of the Lord his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22–23; 2 Kings 15:36; 2 Chronicles 32:32–33"> The rest of what Jotham did, his wars and other events, are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> He was twenty-five when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for sixteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:38"> Jotham died and was buried in the City of David. His son Ahaz took over as king.</span>
