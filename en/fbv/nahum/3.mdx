<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:1–3; Habakkuk 2:12; Hosea 4:2"> What a disaster is coming upon this murderous city with all its treachery! It is full of wealth it has robbed, and always has plenty of victims.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “its prey never departs.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:3; Nahum 2:3–4; Job 39:22–25"> Listen to the noise: whips crack, wheels clatter, horses gallop, chariots rattle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:3"> Horsemen charge, swords flash, spears glitter! Many dead, piles of corpses, countless bodies—so many that people stumble over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:9; Isaiah 47:12–13; Revelation 17:1–5"> All this happens because the repeated prostitution of Nineveh the prostitute, the beautiful mistress with her deadly charms who seduces nations into slavery by her prostitution and her witchcraft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:26; Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:22"> Watch out! For I am against you, declares the Lord Almighty. I will lift your skirts over your face and let the nations see your nakedness, and kingdoms see your shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 2:9; Jeremiah 51:37; Job 9:31"> I will throw filth at you. I will treat you with contempt, and make a spectacle of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:5; Isaiah 51:19; Revelation 18:10"> Then everyone who sees you will shun you saying, “Nineveh is ruined! But who is going to mourn your loss?” Where should I look to find anyone to comfort you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:2; Ezekiel 30:14–16; Jeremiah 46:25–26"> Are you any better than the city of Thebes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “No Amon,” the city of the Egyptian god Amen. It had earlier been destroyed by the Assyrians.">b</a></sup>on the River Nile, surrounded by water? Water was its defense, water was its wall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Qumran pesher (commentary) on Nahum makes it clear that the pronoun refers to the city.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:5; Ezekiel 27:10; Isaiah 20:5"> The city ruled Egypt and Ethiopia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Cush.”">d</a></sup>Put and Libya were its allies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Joel 3:3; Hosea 13:16"> Yet its people were also taken away into exile, into captivity. Their babies were dashed to pieces in every street. Their nobles were bound in chains and taken away as servants, chosen by lot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Isaiah 2:10; PSALMS 75:8"> You too will behave like a drunk. You will hide in fear, trying to find a place safe from the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 6:13; Isaiah 28:4; Habakkuk 1:10"> All your fortresses are like fig trees ripe with fruit—they fall when they are shaken into the mouth of the one eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Isaiah 19:16; Jeremiah 50:37"> Look! Your soldiers are women among you. The gates of your land are wide open to your enemies; the bars of the gates will be set on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:3–4; Nahum 2:1; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Store water to prepare for a siege! Strengthen your fortresses! Go to the clay pits to tread it well, mix the cement, get your brick molds ready!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Nahum 2:13; Nahum 3:13"> But even so, there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to the siege.">e</a></sup>the fire will consume you, you will be cut down by the sword. You will be destroyed as if you were devoured by a swarm of locusts. So multiply yourselves like locusts, like a swarm of locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; Jeremiah 33:22"> You increased your merchants so that there are more of them than the stars of heaven. But like locusts they strip everything and then fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27; Revelation 9:7"> Your leaders are like locusts, your officials like a swarm of locusts. They rest on walls on a cold day, but when the sun rises they fly away, and no one knows where they've gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; PSALMS 76:5–6; Jeremiah 50:18"> King of Assyria, your shepherds are asleep, your princes are slumbering.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="In Scripture death is often referred to as sleep, so this verse means all the leaders who cared for the people are dead.">f</a></sup>Your people are scattered across the mountains and no one can gather them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 1:9; Lamentations 2:15; Job 27:23"> There is no way to heal your injuries, you are too badly wounded. Everyone who hears this news will applaud what has happened to you, for was there anyone who escaped your continuous cruelty?</span>
</div>
</div>
