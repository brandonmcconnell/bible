<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:13; Jonah 1:2; Zechariah 9:1"> A prophecy about Nineveh: the scroll of the vision that came to Nahum the Elkoshite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Exodus 20:5; PSALMS 94:1"> The Lord is a jealous and avenging God—the Lord God is avenging and angry. The Lord takes vengeance on his enemies, he is angry with those who are hostile to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 147:5; Exodus 34:6–7; Nehemiah 9:17"> The Lord is slow to become angry, has great power, and will not leave the guilty unpunished. He walks among the whirlwind and the storm; the clouds are like dust beneath his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:9; PSALMS 106:9; PSALMS 114:5"> He commands the sea and makes it dry; he dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Two places famous for good pasture.">a</a></sup>wither; the blossom of Lebanon fades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 1:4; 2 Samuel 22:8; Jeremiah 4:24"> Mountains quake in his presence; hills melt away. The earth trembles before him—the whole world and everyone who lives there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; Malachi 3:2; Deuteronomy 32:22–23"> Who can withstand his fury? Who can endure the heat of his anger? His rage pours out like molten fire; he shatters rocks to pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:27; 2 Timothy 2:19; Lamentations 3:25"> The Lord is good, a place of safety in a time of trouble. He takes care of those who place their trust in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 8:22; Ezekiel 13:13"> but those who oppose him will be swept away by a tremendous flood to their destruction. He pursues his enemies into the darkness of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 21:11; Proverbs 21:30; PSALMS 33:10"> Why are you plotting against the Lord? He will end it completely; misery won't arise twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 9:18"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="God's enemies.">b</a></sup>entangle themselves like people caught in thorn bushes; they are like drunks full of drink. They will be completely burned up like dry straw.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This verse is acknowledged as one of the hardest verses to translate in the Bible as the specific interpretation is uncertain. However, the main point of destruction of those opposed to God is clear.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Chronicles 13:7; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> One of you is plotting evil against the Lord, someone who is planning wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Joel 2:19; Isaiah 17:14"> This is what the Lord says: Even though they are strong and numerous, they will be cut down and they will pass away. Though I have caused you trouble, I will not do so anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 10:27; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 2:20"> Now I will break their yoke from your necks and tear away the chains which bind you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 109:13; Isaiah 46:1–2; Micah 5:13"> This is what the Lord has ordered concerning you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring to the people of Nineveh.">d</a></sup>You will have no descendants to carry on your name. I will destroy the gods in your temples, all the wooden and metal idols. I am digging your grave, for you are depraved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; Isaiah 40:9–10"> Look! A messenger is coming over the mountains, bringing good news and proclaiming peace. Judah, celebrate your religious festivals and keep your vows, for never again will wicked enemies invade your land—they will be completely destroyed.</span>
