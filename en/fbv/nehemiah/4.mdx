<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:10; Hebrews 11:36; PSALMS 35:15–16"> When Sanballat found out that we were rebuilding the wall, he was furious—really mad! He made fun of the Jews</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:9–10; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Ezekiel 37:3–13"> in front of his colleagues and the army of Samaria, saying, “What are these useless Jews trying to achieve? Do they think they can rebuild the wall? Are they going to offer sacrifices? Are they going to finish it in a day? Do they think they can re-use stones from piles of rubble and dirt, especially since they've all been burned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 2:10"> Tobiah the Ammonite, standing beside him, commented, “Even a fox walking on what they're building would knock down their wall of stones!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 79:12; PSALMS 123:3–4; Proverbs 3:34"> I prayed, “Lord, please listen to us, because we are being treated with contempt. Rain down their insults on their own heads! Let them be taken away like plunder, prisoners in a foreign land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 109:14; Jeremiah 18:23; PSALMS 69:27–28"> Don't forgive their guilt or blot out their sins, for they have made you angry in front of the builders.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“For they have made you angry in front of the builders”: or, “for they have provoked the builders.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Nehemiah 6:15; Hebrews 13:21"> So we rebuilt the wall until it was all connected, reaching half its height, because the people were keen to work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:10; Nehemiah 4:1; Acts 5:33"> When Sanballat and Tobiah, and the Arabs, Ammonites, and Ashdodites, heard that the repair to the walls of Jerusalem was progressing and that the gaps were being filled in, they were furious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 83:3–11; Isaiah 8:9–10; Jeremiah 20:10"> They all plotted together to come and attack Jerusalem and to throw everything into confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Acts 4:24–30; PSALMS 50:15"> So we prayed to our God, and we had guards ready to defend against them day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 11:1–2; Haggai 1:2; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> Then people of Judah started grumbling, saying, “The workmen are exhausted. There's just too much rubble to clear. We'll never be able to finish the wall.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2; Acts 23:12; PSALMS 56:6"> Our enemies were saying to themselves, “Before they know it, before they're aware of anything, we'll appear right in among them, kill them, and put a stop to what they're doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Genesis 31:41; Numbers 14:22"> The Jews who lived close by came and told us over and over again, “They're going to attack us from all directions!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Presumed meaning, the Hebrew is obscure.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:17–18; Ephesians 6:11–20; Song of Songs 3:7–8"> So I positioned defenders behind the lowest, most vulnerable sections, of the wall. I had them take their positions by families, armed with their swords, spears, and bows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:12; Numbers 14:9; Isaiah 41:10–14"> After inspecting our defenses, I stood up and addressed the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, saying, “Don't be afraid of them! Remember the Lord, who is powerful and formidable! Fight for your brothers, your sons and your daughters, your wives and your homes!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; Romans 12:11; Job 5:12–13"> When our enemies discovered that we knew about their plan, and that God had foiled it, we all went back to our work on the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:23; PSALMS 101:6; Nehemiah 5:15–16"> From then on, half of my men were doing the work while the other half were ready to fight, holding their spears, shields, bows, and armor. The leaders stood behind all the people of Judah</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; 2 Timothy 4:7; 2 Timothy 2:3"> who were building the wall. Those carrying materials worked with one hand, holding a weapon in the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–17"> All the builders carried a sword strapped to their side, and the trumpeter stood beside me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="To provide warning on an attack.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then I told the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people: “We have a lot to do everywhere, so we're very spread out along the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 1:30; Deuteronomy 20:4"> Wherever you are and you hear the sound of the trumpet, run to join us there. Our God will fight for us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Colossians 1:29; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> We went on working, with half of the men holding spears from dawn until the stars came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1–2"> I also told the people, “Everyone including servants must spend the night inside Jerusalem, so they can be on guard duty at night and work during the day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> During that time none of us changed our clothes—not me or my brothers or my men or the guards with me. Everyone carried their weapons at all times, even to go for water.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Everyone carried their weapons at all times, even to go for water.” The Hebrew is unclear. Literally it reads “each his weapon the water.”">d</a></sup></span>
