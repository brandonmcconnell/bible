<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 17:24–27; Exodus 30:11–16; Proverbs 3:9"> We accept the obligation to pay one third of a shekel for the operation of the Temple of God,</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Isaiah 40:16; Leviticus 6:12–13"> We have allocated by lot among the priests, Levites, and the people, to determine who will bring wood to the Temple of our God to burn on the altar of the Lord our God at specific times during the year, as required by the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> We also promise to bring the first part of the produce from our fields and from every fruit tree to the Lord's Temple every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Leviticus 27:26–27; Exodus 13:12–15"> We will bring the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock and of our herds and flocks to the Temple of our God, to the priests who minister there, as required by the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:17; Numbers 18:21; Nehemiah 13:9"> We will bring to the storerooms of the Temple of our God, to the priests, the first part of our coarse-ground flour, of our grain offerings, of the fruit of all our trees, and of our new wine and olive oil. We will also bring a tithe of our produce to the Levites, for the Levites are the ones who collect the tithes in all the farming towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12–13; Numbers 18:26–28; 2 Chronicles 31:11–12"> A priest descended from Aaron will accompany the Levites when they collect the tithe, and the Levites are to bring a tithe of these tithes to the rooms of the storehouse in the Temple of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12; Hebrews 10:25; Deuteronomy 14:23–27"> The people of Israel and the Levites are to bring the offerings of grain, new wine, and olive oil to the storerooms where the sanctuary objects are kept, where the ministering priests, the gatekeepers, and the singers are. We will not forget the Temple of our God.”</span>
