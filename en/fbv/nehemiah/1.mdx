<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Zechariah 7:1; Esther 1:2"> This is the account of Nehemiah, son of Hacaliah. In the month of Kislev, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes' reign, I was in the fortress at Susa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Ezekiel 6:9; Jeremiah 44:14"> Hanani, one of my brothers, came from Judah with some other men. I asked them about the remnant of the Jewish exiles who had returned from captivity, and also about Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 Kings 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> They told me, “The remnant who are left from the exile are there in the province, but they are having a great deal of trouble and feel humiliated. Jerusalem's walls have been knocked over, and its gates burned down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; PSALMS 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> When I heard the news, I sat down, weeping and mourning for days, fasting and praying to the God of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; Daniel 9:4–19; Deuteronomy 7:21"> Then I prayed, “Please, Lord God of heaven—the great and awesome God who keeps his agreement of trustworthy love with those who love him and keep his commandments—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Daniel 9:4; PSALMS 34:15"> please listen and focus your attention on the prayer of your servant that I am praying to you now, day and night, on behalf of your servants, the Israelites. I confess the sins that we Israelites have committed against you, including my own sins and those of my family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 9:5–6; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:13"> We have done terrible things to offend you and we have not kept the commandments, laws, and regulations that you gave to your servant Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Deuteronomy 4:25–27; Leviticus 26:33–46"> Please remember what you told Moses when you said, ‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11–14; Deuteronomy 30:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> but if you come back to me and follow my commandments and obey them, then even if you are exiled to the ends of the earth, I will gather them together and bring them to the place I have chosen where I will be honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:29; Daniel 9:15–27; Exodus 32:11"> They are your servants and our people. You saved them by your great power and incredible ability.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Proverbs 21:1"> Lord, please respond to my prayer and to the prayers of those who love to worship you. Please let me be successful today and make the king sympathetic to me.’” I was the king's cupbearer.</span>
</div>
</div>
