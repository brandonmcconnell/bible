<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> The Israelites moved on and camped on the plains of Moab east of the Jordan, opposite Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> Balak, son of Zippor, had seen all that the Israelites had done to the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> The Moabites were terrified of the Israelites because there were so many of them. The Moabites dreaded the arrival of the Israelites</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> and told the leaders of Midian, “This horde will eat up everything we have, just like an ox eats up grass in the field!” (Balak son of Zippor, was king of Moab at that time.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> He sent messengers to call Balaam, son of Beor, who lived in Pethor near the Euphrates River in his own country.<br />“Listen, a group of people has arrived here who came from Egypt,” Balak said in his message to Balaam. “There are hordes of them and they present a real threat to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> Please come immediately and curse these people for me, because they are stronger than me. Maybe then I'll be able to attack them and drive them out of my country because I know that whoever you bless is blessed, and whoever you curse is cursed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> The Moabite and Midianite leaders departed, taking payment for the fortune-telling with them. When they arrived they gave Balaam the message from Balak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> “Stay the night and I'll let you know the answer the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Even though Balaam is not an Israelite he uses their name for God.">a</a></sup>gives me,” Balaam told them. So the Moabite leaders stayed there with Balaam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> God came to Balaam and asked him, “Who are these men staying with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Balaam told God, “Balak, son of Zippor, the king of Moab, sent me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> ‘Listen, a group of people has arrived here who came from Egypt. There are hordes of them. Please come immediately and curse these people for me. Maybe then I'll be able to fight them and drive them out of my country.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> But God told Balaam, “You are not to go back with them. You must not curse this people, for they are blessed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> In the morning Balaam got up and said to Balak's messengers, “Go back to where you came from because the Lord has refused to allow me to go with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> The Moabite leaders left. They returned to Balak, and told him, “Balaam refused to come back with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> Then Balak sent even more leaders who were more prestigious than before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When they arrived they told Balaam, “This is what Balak son of Zippor says: ‘Please don't let anything stop you from coming to see me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> because I will pay you a great deal and follow all the advice you give me. Please come and curse these people for me!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> But Balaam told Balak's officials, “Even if Balak gave me his whole palace full of silver and gold, I couldn't disobey the command of the Lord my God in any way.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“In any way”: literally, “whether little or much.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> Now you should also stay the night so I can see if the Lord has anything else to tell me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> God came to Balaam during the night and told him, “Since these men have come for you, get up and go with them. But only do what I tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> In the morning Balaam got up, put a saddle on his donkey, and left with the Moabite leaders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> God got angry because Balaam had decided to go. The angel of the Lord came and stood in the road to confront him. Balaam was riding on his donkey, and he was accompanied by his two servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> The donkey saw the angel of the Lord standing in the road holding a drawn sword, so it turned from the road and went into a field. So Balaam beat it to make it go back to the road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then the angel of the Lord stood in a narrow part of the road that passed between two vineyards, with walls on both sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> The donkey saw the angel of the Lord and tried to get past.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“And tried to get past”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup>It pushed up against the wall and crushed Balaam's foot against it. So he beat it again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> Then the angel of the Lord went ahead and stood in a narrow place where there was no room to get past, either on the right or the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> The donkey saw the angel of the Lord and lay down under Balaam. He got angry and beat it with his walking stick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> The Lord gave the donkey the ability to speak and it said to Balaam, “What have I done to you for you to beat me three times?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> “You made me look stupid!” Balaam told the donkey. “If I was holding a sword, I'd kill you now!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> But the donkey asked Balaam, “Aren't I the donkey you've ridden all your life right up to today? Have I ever treated you this way before?”<br />“No,” he admitted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> Then the Lord gave Balaam the ability to see the angel of the Lord standing in the road holding a drawn sword. Balaam bowed low and fell facedown on the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> The angel of the Lord asked him, “Why did you beat your donkey three times? Listen, I have come to confront you because you're being obstinate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> The donkey saw me and avoided me three times. If it had not avoided me, by now I definitely would have killed you and allowed the donkey to live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> “I have sinned because I did not realize that you were standing in the road to confront me,” Balaam said to the angel of the Lord, “So if this isn't what you want, I'll go back home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> The angel of the Lord told Balaam, “No, you can go with the men, but only say what I tell you.” So Balaam continued on with Balak's officials.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> When Balak found out that Balaam was on his way, he went out to meet him at the Moabite town on the border at the Arnon River, the farthest point of his territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> He said to Balaam, “Didn't you think my call for you to come was urgent? Why didn't you come to me straight away? Did you think I couldn't pay you enough?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> “Look, I'm here with you now, aren't I?” Balaam replied. “But do you think I can just say anything? I can only speak the words that God gives me to say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Balaam went with Balak and they arrived at Kiriath-huzoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep, and he shared the meat with Balaam and the leaders who were with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> The next morning Balak took Balaam up to Bamoth-baal.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Bamoth-baal”: meaning “the High Places of Baal.” Some have concluded that a pagan temple to Baal occupied this high point.">d</a></sup>From there he could see the extent of the Israelite camp.</span>
</div>
</div>
