<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:33; Galatians 3:28; 1 Chronicles 7:15"> The daughters of Zelophehad came to present their case.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See also Joshua 17:3-6.">a</a></sup>Their father Zelophehad was the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, and was from the tribe of Manasseh, son of Joseph. The names of his daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. They came</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 15:33–34; Deuteronomy 17:8–10; Exodus 18:19–26"> and stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders, and all the Israelites at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. They said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Ezekiel 18:4; Numbers 14:35"> “Our father died in the desert, but he wasn't one of Korah's followers who joined together to rebel against the Lord. No, he died from his own sins, and he didn't have any sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:4; PSALMS 109:13; Exodus 32:11"> Why should our family name be lost simply because he didn't have a son? Give us land to own alongside our uncles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12–13; Job 23:4; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Moses took their case before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; PSALMS 68:5–6"> The Lord gave him this answer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Jeremiah 49:11; Joshua 17:4"> “What the daughters of Zelophehad are saying is right. You really must give them land to own alongside their uncles—give to them what would have been allocated to their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> In addition, tell the Israelites, ‘If a man dies and doesn't have a son, give his property to his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If he doesn't have a daughter, give his property to his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If he doesn't have brothers, give his property to his father's brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:29; Ruth 4:3–6; Jeremiah 32:8"> If his father doesn't have any brothers, give his property to his family's next of kin so that they can own it. This is a legal regulation for the Israelites, given as an order by the Lord to Moses.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:47–48; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> The Lord told Moses, “Go up into the Abarim mountains so you can see the land that I have given the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Numbers 31:2; Numbers 20:24–28"> After you have seen it, you will also join your forefathers in death, just as your brother Aaron did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:8–13; Exodus 17:7"> because when the Israelites complained in the Desert of Zin, you both rebelled against my instructions to show my holiness before them in regard to providing water.” (These were the waters of Meribah in Kadesh, in the Desert of Zin.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Moses pleaded with the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Jeremiah 3:15; Hebrews 12:9"> “May the Lord, the God who gives life to all living beings, choose a man to lead the Israelites</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:5; Mark 6:34; 1 Kings 22:17"> who will tell them what to do and show them where to go, so that the people of the Lord won't be like sheep without a shepherd.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Numbers 27:23; Genesis 41:38"> The Lord told Moses, “Call for Joshua, son of Nun, a man who has the Spirit in him, and place your hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:23"> Make him stand in front of Eleazar the priest and all the Israelites, and dedicate him while they watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 1:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:9; 2 Kings 2:9–10"> Hand over some of your authority to him so that all the Israelites will obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 9:14; 1 Samuel 28:6; Exodus 28:30"> When he needs instructions, he is to go before Eleazar the priest who will ask the Lord on his behalf and find out the decision using the Urim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Something used to determine the Lord's will. See Exodus 28:30, Leviticus 8:8.">b</a></sup>Joshua will give orders to all the Israelites concerning everything they are to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moses followed the Lord's instructions. He had Joshua come and stand in front of Eleazar the priest and all the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 27:19; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> Moses placed his hands on Joshua and dedicated him, just as the Lord had told him to do.</span>
