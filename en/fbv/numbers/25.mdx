<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Numbers 33:49; Joshua 2:1"> When the Israelites were staying at Shittim the men started to have sex with Moabite women</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> who invited them to the sacrifices made to their gods. The Israelites ate the pagan meals and bowed down before these gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; PSALMS 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> In this way the Israelites devoted themselves in worship to Baal of Peor, and the Lord was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> The Lord told Moses, “Arrest all the Israelite leaders and kill them before the Lord where everyone can see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Where everyone can see”: literally, “before the sun.”">a</a></sup>in order to turn the Lord's furious anger away from the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> So Moses instructed Israel's judges,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="These were “judge leaders” who fulfilled more than simply a legal role in Israelite society.">b</a></sup>“Each of you has to kill all of your men who have devoted themselves to worshiping Baal of Peor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> Right then an Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his family tent in full view of Moses and all the Israelites as they were crying at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; PSALMS 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> When he saw this, Phinehas, son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, left the assembly, grabbed a spear</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; PSALMS 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> and followed the man into his tent. There Phinehas drove the spear through both of them, through the Israelite and on into the woman's stomach. This action stopped the plague that had started to kill the Israelites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> but 24,000 had already died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; PSALMS 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> “Phinehas son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, has turned my anger away from the Israelites, because out of all of them he was passionately dedicated to me, so I did not destroy the Israelites in my passionate anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> So make an announcement that I am granting him my peace agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> It will be an agreement that ensures a permanent priesthood for him and his descendants, because he was passionately dedicated to his God and set the Israelites right.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> The name of the Israelite who was killed with the Midianite woman was Zimri, son of Salu, a family leader of the tribe of Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> The name of the Midianite woman who was killed was Cozbi, daughter of Zur, a family leader of a Midianite tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> “Attack the Midianites and kill them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> because they attacked you deceptively, leading you astray by using Peor and their woman Cozbi, the daughter of the Midianite leader—the woman who was killed on the day the plague came because of their devotion to Peor.”</span>
