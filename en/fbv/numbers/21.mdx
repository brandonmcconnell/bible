<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> The Canaanite king of Arad who lived in the Negev learned that the Israelites were approaching on the road to Atharim. He went and attacked Israel and took some of them prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> So Israel made a solemn promise to the Lord: “If you hand these people over to us, we pledge to completely destroy their towns.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; PSALMS 10:17"> The Lord responded to their appeal and handed over the Canaanites to them. The Israelites completely destroyed them and their towns, and named the place Hormah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Hormah” means “destruction.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> The Israelites left Mount Hor by the road leading to the Red Sea so they could avoid traveling through the country of Edom. But the people became bad-tempered on the way</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> and made complaints against God and against Moses, saying, “Why did you lead us out of Egypt to die in the desert? We don't have bread or water, and we hate this awful food!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Awful food”: referring to the manna.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> So the Lord sent poisonous snakes to attack them, and many Israelites were bitten and died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; PSALMS 78:34"> The people went to see Moses and told him, “We were wrong to make complaints against the Lord and against you. Please pray to the Lord to get rid of the snakes from us.” Moses prayed to the Lord on their behalf.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; PSALMS 106:43–45; PSALMS 145:8"> The Lord told Moses, “Make a model of a snake and put it on a pole. When anyone who has been bitten looks at it, they will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> Moses made a snake out of bronze and put it on a pole. Those who looked at it did live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> The Israelites left and camped at Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then they moved on from Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim in the desert on the east side of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> They left there and camped in the Valley of Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> Then they moved on from there and camped on the far side of the Arnon River, in the desert near Amorite territory. The Arnon River is the border between the Moab and the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> That's why the Book of the Wars of the Lord refers to “the town of Waheb in Suphah and the canyon of the Arnon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> the canyon slopes that reach the village of Ar that lies on the border with Moab.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> From there they moved on to Beer, the well where the Lord told Moses, “Have the people gather together so I can give them water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; PSALMS 106:12"> Then the Israelites sang this song: “Pour out water, well! Everyone of you sing to it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> The tribal chiefs dug the well; yes the leaders of the people dug the well with their rods of authority and their walking sticks.”<br />The Israelites left the desert and carried on to Mattanah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> From Mattanah they traveled to Nahaliel, from Nahaliel to Bamoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> and from Bamoth to the valley in the territory of Moab where the top of Mount Pisgah looks down on the wastelands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> Then Israel sent messengers to Sihon, king of the Amorites, with the following request:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> “Please allow us to travel through your country. We won't cross any of your fields or vineyards, or drink water from any of your wells. We will stay on the King's Highway until we have passed through your country.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> But Sihon refused to allow the Israelites to travel through his territory. Instead, he called out his whole army and went out to meet the Israelites head-on in the desert. When he arrived at Jahaz, he attacked the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> The Israelites defeated them, killing them with their swords. They took over his land from the Arnon River to the Jabbok River —but only as far as the border of the Ammonites, because it was well defended.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> The Israelites conquered all the Amorite towns and took them over, including Heshbon and its surrounding villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Heshbon was the capital of Sihon, king of the Amorites, who had fought against the previous king of Moab and had taken from him all his land as far as the Arnon River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> That's why the old songwriters wrote: “Come to Heshbon and have it be rebuilt; restore the town of Sihon!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> For a fire blazed out from Heshbon, a flame from the town of Sihon. It burned up Ar in Moab where the rulers live on the high places of Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> What a disaster you face, Moab! You are all going to die, people of Chemosh!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Chemosh was a god to whom human sacrifices were made.">c</a></sup>You handed over your sons as exiles and your daughters as prisoners to Sihon, king of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> But now we have defeated the Amorites! Heshbon's rule has been destroyed all the way to Dibon. We wiped them out all the way to Nophah and on to Medeba.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> The Israelites occupied the country of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> Moses sent men to explore Jazer. The Israelites conquered its surrounding villages and expelled the Amorites living there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> Then they continued on the road towards Bashan. Og, king of Bashan, led his whole army out to meet them head on, and fought them at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> The Lord told Moses, “You don't need to be afraid of him, because I have handed him over to you, along with all his people and his land. Do to him what you did to Sihon, king of the Amorites, who ruled from Heshbon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> So they killed Og, his sons, and all his army. Nobody survived, and the Israelites took over his country.</span>
</div>
</div>
