<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="NUM" data-book-slug="numbers" data-book-name="Numbers" data-chapter="16" data-verses="50">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 11:6; Exodus 6:21"> Korah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Korah was a cousin of Moses and Aaron, and jealousy of their position may have been behind his rebellion.">a</a></sup>son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi tried to take over leadership, along with Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On, son of Peleth, who were from the tribe of Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:9; Ezekiel 23:10; Genesis 6:4"> They rebelled against Moses, and were joined by 250 well-respected Israelite leaders and members of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 106:16; Numbers 14:14; Exodus 19:6"> They joined together in opposition to Moses and Aaron, telling them, “You've taken too much power for yourselves! Every one of the Israelites is holy, and the Lord is among them. So why do you set yourselves above the Lord's assembly?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 20:6; Joshua 7:6"> When Moses heard what they were saying, he fell facedown on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 65:4; Numbers 17:5; 2 Timothy 2:19"> Then he told Korah and all those with him, “In the morning the Lord is going to make it clear who is his and who is holy, and allow that person to approach him. He will only allow whoever he chooses to approach him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Numbers 16:46–48; 1 Kings 18:21–23"> This is what you, Korah, and everyone with you are going to do. Take some incense burners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 16:3; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Kings 18:17–18"> and tomorrow put incense in them and set it on fire in the presence of the Lord. Then the man the Lord chooses is the one who is holy. It is you Levites who are taking too much power for yourselves!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Moses also told Korah, “Listen, you Levites!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> Do you think it's something insignificant that the God of Israel chose you from all the other Israelites and allowed you to approach him and carry out the work in the Lord's Tabernacle, to stand before the Israelites and serve them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; 3 John 1:9; Numbers 18:7"> He has allowed you the privilege of approaching him, Korah, you and all the other Levites, but now you want to have the priesthood as well!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:7–8; Exodus 17:2; Numbers 16:3"> So in reality you and those who have joined you are fighting against the Lord—because who is Aaron that you should complain about him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13–14; Jude 1:8; Proverbs 29:9"> Moses summoned Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, but they replied, “We're not going to appear before you!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words they refused to acknowledge Moses' authority to require them to appear before him to be judged.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Exodus 2:14; Numbers 11:5"> Haven't you done enough by taking us away from a land flowing with milk and honey in order to kill us out here in the desert? Do you have to make yourself a dictator as well, someone to rule us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:24; Numbers 36:8–10; Numbers 20:5"> On top of that you haven't taken us to a land flowing with milk and honey or given us fields and vineyards to own. Do you really think you can fool everyone?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The expression used here “Will you gouge out the eyes of these men?” is taken to mean something like “Are you going to pull the wool over their eyes?”">c</a></sup>No, we will not attend!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:4–5; 2 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:10"> Moses got really angry and said to the Lord, “Don't accept their offerings. I have never taken even a donkey from them or treated any of them badly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3; 1 Samuel 12:7; 2 Timothy 2:14"> Moses told Korah, “You and all those who have joined you must appear before the Lord tomorrow—all of you and Aaron too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:7"> Each one will take his incense burner, put incense in it, and offer it before the Lord. All 250 of you will use your incense burners and Aaron will as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So each one took his incense burner, put incense in it, set it on fire, and stood together with Moses and Aaron at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:10; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 16:7"> When Korah had gathered all his rebellious group at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the Lord's glory appeared before the whole congregation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Lord told Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:45; Exodus 32:10; PSALMS 73:19"> “Step away from these Israelites and I will destroy them right away.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:16; Zechariah 12:1; Job 12:10"> But Moses and Aaron fell facedown on the ground said, “God—God of everything that lives—when it's only one man who sins, must you be angry with everybody?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 16:21"> “Tell the people to move away from the homes of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:16–17; Numbers 11:25; Numbers 11:30"> So Moses went over to Dathan and Abiram, and the Israelite elders of Israel followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Revelation 18:4"> He ordered the people, “Move away from the tents of these wicked men and don't touch anything that belongs to them, otherwise you will be destroyed along with them in all their sins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 9:4; Isaiah 28:14; Job 40:10–11"> The people moved away from the homes of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrances to their tents along with their wives, children and little ones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:38; Exodus 3:12; John 14:11"> Moses said, “This is how you will know that the Lord did send me to carry out everything I've done, for it wasn't anything I thought up.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“It wasn't anything I thought up”: literally, “not from my heart,” because it was believed that the heart was the place where thinking occurred.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 4:22; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 32:34"> If these men die a natural death, experiencing the destiny of every human being, then the Lord didn't send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 55:15; Numbers 16:33; Job 31:3"> But if the Lord does something totally different, and the earth opens up and swallows them down along with everything that belongs to them so that they go down alive into Sheol, then you will know that these men have acted with contempt to the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 106:17–18; Numbers 27:3; Deuteronomy 11:6"> Immediately after Moses had finished saying all this, the ground under the rebels split apart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 106:17; Numbers 26:11; Revelation 12:16"> and the earth opened up and swallowed them and their households down, as well as everyone who was there with Korah and everything that belonged to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Isaiah 14:9; PSALMS 9:15"> They went down alive into Sheol with all that they had. The earth closed over them, and they were no more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; Isaiah 33:3; Revelation 6:15–17"> When they heard their cries, all the Israelites nearby ran away, shouting, “Watch out! The earth could swallow us too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Leviticus 10:2; PSALMS 106:18"> Fire burst out from the Lord and burned up the 250 men who were offering incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Numbers 16:18; Numbers 16:7"> “Tell Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, to collect the holy incense burners from among those who have been burned up, and scatter the coals used for the incense well away from the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Ezekiel 14:8; 2 Peter 2:6"> Have the incense burners of those who sinned at the expense of their own lives hammered into metal sheets as a covering for the altar, because they were offered before the Lord, and so have become holy. They will be a reminder to Israelites of what happened.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Eleazar the priest collected the bronze incense burners used by those who had been burned up, and had them hammered out as a covering for the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 18:4–7; 2 Chronicles 26:16–21"> following the instructions given to him from the Lord through Moses. This was to remind the Israelites that no one who is not a descendant of Aaron should come and offer incense before the Lord, otherwise they could end up like Korah and those with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–7; PSALMS 106:23; Numbers 14:2"> The next day all the Israelites complained to Moses and Aaron, saying, “You have killed the Lord's people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Leviticus 9:23; Numbers 20:6"> But as the people gathered to confront them, Moses and Aaron went over to the Tent of Meeting, and suddenly the cloud covered it and the Lord's glory appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Moses and Aaron went and stood at the front of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 16:21–22; Numbers 16:24; Numbers 20:6"> “Get away from these people and I will finish them off immediately.” Moses and Aaron fell facedown on the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Leviticus 10:6"> Moses told Aaron, “Put some coals from the altar and some incense in your incense burner. Then run to the people and make them right before the Lord, for the Lord is angry with them and a plague has started.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Deuteronomy 33:10–11; PSALMS 106:29"> Aaron took the incense burner just as Moses had told him and ran into the middle of the assembly. He saw that the plague had started to affect the people so he offered the incense and made the people right with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; Hebrews 7:24–25; Numbers 16:18"> He stood between those who had died and those who were still alive, and the plague was stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 25:9; Numbers 16:32–35; Hebrews 2:1–3"> However, 14,700 died from the plague in addition to those who'd died because of Korah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26–30; Numbers 16:43"> Then Aaron went back to Moses at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting because the plague had been stopped.</span>
</div>
</div>
