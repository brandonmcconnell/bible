<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Exodus 40:2"> The Lord spoke to Moses in the Sinai desert in the first month of the second year after Israel had left Egypt. He told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 35:1"> “The Israelites are to keep the Passover at its designated time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–11; Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> Observe it at the time required—in the evening after sunset on the fourteenth day of this month, and do so in accordance with its rules and regulations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Moses told the Israelites to observe the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 12:1–13"> So they kept the Passover in the Sinai desert, beginning in the evening after sunset on the fourteenth day of the first month. The Israelites followed all the instructions the Lord had given to Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:2; Exodus 18:15; Numbers 5:2"> However, there were some men who were unclean because they had been in contact with a dead body, so they could not keep the Passover on that day. They went to see Moses and Aaron the same day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Exodus 12:27"> and explained to Moses, “We are unclean because of a dead body, but why should that mean we're prevented from giving our offering to the Lord with the other Israelites at the appropriate time?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 85:8; Numbers 27:5; 1 Corinthians 4:4"> “Stay here while I find out what the Lord's instructions are concerning you,” Moses responded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> “Tell the Israelites: ‘If you or your descendants are unclean because of a dead body, or are away traveling, you can still keep the Lord's Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2–15; Exodus 12:43–49; John 19:36"> You are to observe it in the evening after sunset on the fourteenth day of the second month. You are to eat the lamb with the bread made without yeast, and the bitter herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 19:36; Exodus 12:46; Exodus 12:10"> You must not leave any of it until the following morning and you must not break any of its bones. You must observe the Passover according to all the regulations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15; Numbers 9:7"> However, anyone ceremonially clean and not away traveling who neglects to observe the Passover must be expelled from their people, because they didn't present the Lord's offering at the appropriate time. They will bear the responsibility for the consequences of their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:48–49; Leviticus 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> Any foreigners living among you who want to observe the Lord's Passover can do so following the Passover rules and regulations. The same rules apply to the foreigners as they do to you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Exodus 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> The cloud covered the Tent of the Testimony (the Tabernacle) on the day that it was set up, and looked like fire above it from evening until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Exodus 40:38; 1 Corinthians 10:1"> It was always like this. The cloud covered the Tabernacle during the day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“During the day”: Septuagint reading.">a</a></sup>and at night it looked like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 40:36–38; PSALMS 32:8; John 10:9"> When the cloud rose from over the Tent, the Israelites would move on, and where the cloud stopped, the Israelites would set up camp there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1; 2 John 1:6; Exodus 17:1"> The Israelites moved on when the Lord told them to, and set up camp when the Lord told them to. While the cloud stayed over the Tabernacle, they remained camped there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Numbers 1:52–53; Numbers 3:8"> Even if the cloud didn't move for a long time, the Israelites did what the Lord told them and didn't move on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Sometimes the cloud only stayed over the Tabernacle for a few days. As always they followed the Lord's command as to whether to camp or to move on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> Sometimes the cloud only stayed overnight, so when it rose in the morning they would move on. Whenever the cloud rose, day or night, they would leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; Exodus 40:36–37; Exodus 40:16"> If the cloud stayed in one place for two days, or a month, or longer, the Israelites stayed where they were and didn't leave as long as the cloud remained over the Tabernacle. However, once it rose, they would leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 22:3; Numbers 9:19; Genesis 26:5"> They camped when the Lord told them to, and left when he told them to. They followed the Lord's instructions that he gave to Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>
