<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> “Tell the Israelites, ‘These are instructions about what you are to do once you arrive in the country I'm giving you to live in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> When you bring an offering to the Lord from your herd or flock (whether it's a burnt offering, a sacrifice to fulfill a promise you made, or a freewill or festival offering) that will be acceptable to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> then you shall also present a grain offering of one tenth of an ephah of the best flour mixed with a quarter hin of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> Add a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering to the burnt offering or the sacrifice of a lamb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> When it comes to a ram, present a grain offering of two tenths of an ephah of the best flour mixed with a third of a hin of olive oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> along with a third of a hin of wine as a drink offering, all of them to be acceptable to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> When you bring a young bull as a burnt offering or a sacrifice to fulfill a promise you made or as a peace offering to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> then you shall also bring with the bull a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of the best flour mixed with half a hin of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> Add a half a hin of wine as a drink offering. All this is an offering to be acceptable to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> This is to be done for every bull, ram, lamb, or goat brought as an offering.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Brought as an offering”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> This is what you need to do for each one, however many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Every Israelite is to follow these instructions when they present an offering that is accepted by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> This also applies for all future generations that if a foreigner living among you or anyone else among you wishes to present an offering acceptable to the Lord: they are to do exactly what you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> The whole congregation must have the same rules for you and for the foreigner living among you. This is a permanent law for all future generations You and the foreigner are to be treated the same way before the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The same rules and regulations apply to you and the foreigner living among you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then the Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> “Tell the Israelites, ‘When you get to the country where I'm leading you</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> and you eat the food produced there, you shall give some of it as an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> You are to give some of the flour you make into loaves as a gift—present it just like an offering from the threshing floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> For all future generations, you are to give the Lord an offering from the first of your flour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> Now if you collectively sin without meaning to and don't follow all these instructions that the Lord has given Moses—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> everything that the Lord has ordered you to do through Moses from the time that the Lord gave them and for all future generations—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> and if it was done unintentionally without everyone knowing about it, then the whole congregation is to present a young bull as a burnt offering to be accepted by the Lord, along with its grain offering and drink offering presented according to the rules, as well as a male goat as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> In this way the priest is to make the whole congregation of Israel right with the Lord so that they can be forgiven, because the sin was unintentional and they have presented the Lord with a burnt offering and a sin offering, offered before the Lord for their unintentional sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> Then the whole congregation of Israel and the foreigners living among them will be forgiven, because the people sinned unintentionally.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> In the case of an individual who sins without meaning to—they are to present a one-year-old female goat as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> The priest will make the person who sinned unintentionally right before the Lord on their behalf. Once they have been made right, they will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> You shall apply the same law for the one who mistakenly sins to an Israelite or a foreigner living among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; PSALMS 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> But the person who sins in defiance, whether an Israelite or foreigner, is blaspheming<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Blaspheming”: in the sense of deliberately abusing the Lord.">b</a></sup>the Lord. They shall be expelled from their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> They have to be expelled, because they have treated the word of the Lord with contempt and broken his commandment. They are responsible for the consequences of their own guilt.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> During the time the Israelites were wandering in the desert, a man was caught collecting firewood on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> The people who found him collecting wood brought him before Moses, Aaron, and the rest of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> They placed him under guard because it wasn't clear what should happen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> The Lord told Moses, “This man has to be executed. All the Israelites are to stone him outside the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> So they all took the man outside the camp and stoned him to death as the Lord had ordered Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Sometime later the Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> “Tell the Israelites that for all future generations you must make tassels for the hems of your clothes and attach them with blue cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; PSALMS 73:27"> When you look at these tassels you will be reminded to keep all the commandments of the Lord and not be unfaithful, following your own thoughts and desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> In this way you'll remember to keep all my commandments and you will be holy to your God. I am the Lord your God who led you out of Egypt to be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am the Lord your God!”</span>
</div>
</div>
