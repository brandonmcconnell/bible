<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13; Joel 1:14; PSALMS 81:3"> “Make two trumpets out of hammered silver. They are to be used for summoning the Israelites and to have the camp move out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Joel 2:15–16"> When both trumpets are blown, all the Israelites are to gather before you at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 1:4–16"> But if only one is blown, then only the tribal leaders are to gather before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 10:14; Isaiah 58:1"> When you blow the trumpet loudly, which is the alarm signal to move out, the camps on the east side are to march out first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:10–16"> When you blow the trumpet loudly the second time, the camps on the south side are to march out. That's their signal to start moving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Numbers 10:3–4"> To summon the people, blow the trumpets normally, not the loud alarm signal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:4–16; Numbers 31:6; 1 Chronicles 15:24"> Aaron's descendants are to blow the trumpets. This regulation is for all time and for all future generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:18; Judges 2:18; Genesis 8:1"> When you're in your own land and have to go into battle against an enemy who has attacked you, blow the alarm signal and the Lord your God won't forget you—he will save you from your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 81:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26"> Blow the trumpets when you celebrate too, at your regular feasts and at the beginning of each month. This is when you give your burnt offerings and fellowship offerings which act as a reminder for you before your God. I am the Lord your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:17; Numbers 1:1; Numbers 9:11"> The cloud rose up from the Tabernacle of the Testimony on the twentieth day of the second month of the second year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 21:21; Numbers 12:16; Numbers 9:5"> The Israelites left the Desert of Sinai and moved from place to place until the cloud stopped in the Desert of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Numbers 9:23"> This was the first time they moved out following the Lord's command through Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 7:12"> The tribal divisions of Judah went first, marching out under their flag, with Nahshon, son of Amminadab, in charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 1:8"> Nethanel, son of Zuar, was in charge of the tribal division of Issachar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 1:9"> and Eliab, son of Helon, was in charge of the tribal division of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:6–8; Numbers 1:51; Hebrews 12:28"> Then the Tabernacle was dismantled, and the Gershonites and the Merarites set off, carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 2:10–16; Numbers 1:5; Numbers 26:5–18"> Then came the divisions of Reuben who marched out under their flag, with Elizur, son of Shedeur, in charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 7:36"> Shelumiel, son of Zurishaddai, was in charge of the tribal division of Simeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42"> and Eliasaph, son of Deuel, was in charge of the tribal division of Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:4–16"> Then the Kohathites set off, carrying the holy objects. The Tabernacle would be put up before they arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 2:18–24; Numbers 1:10; Numbers 26:23–41"> Then came the divisions of Ephraim who marched out under their flag, with Elishama, son of Ammihud in charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54"> Gamaliel, son of Pedahzur, was in charge of the tribal division of Manasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60"> and Abidan, son of Gideoni, was in charge of the tribal division of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:9; Numbers 1:12; Genesis 49:16–17"> Lastly came the divisions of Dan who marched out under their flag, defending the rear of all the tribal groups, with Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai, in charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:72"> Pagiel, son of Ocran, was in charge of the tribal division of Asher,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 7:78"> and Ahira, son of Enan, was in charge of the tribal division of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 2:34; Numbers 24:4–5; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> This was the order in which the Israelite tribal divisions moved out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Judges 4:11; Exodus 3:1"> Moses explained to Hobab, the son of Moses' father-in-law Reuel the Midianite,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This would make Hobab Moses' brother-in-law.">a</a></sup>“We're leaving for the place of which the Lord promised, ‘I'm going to give it to you.’ Come with us, and we'll be good to you, because the Lord has promised good things to Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:15–17; PSALMS 45:10; Matthew 21:29"> “No, I won't go—I'll return to my own country and my own people,” Hobab replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 29:15; Galatians 6:2; 1 Corinthians 12:14–21"> “Please don't abandon us now,” Moses said, “because you're the one who knows where we should camp in the desert and you can guide us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 10:18; 1 John 1:3"> If you come with us, whatever good things the Lord blesses us with we'll share with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:33; Exodus 3:1; PSALMS 95:11"> They left the mountain of the Lord to go on a three-day journey. The Ark of the Lord's Agreement led the way for them during these three days to find a place for them to camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; PSALMS 105:39"> The cloud of the Lord was above them during day as they moved on from the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 68:1–2; Isaiah 17:12–14; PSALMS 132:8"> Whenever the Ark was carried out, Moses would call out, “Stand up, Lord, and may your enemies be scattered, and may those who hate you run away from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; PSALMS 90:13–17; Isaiah 63:17"> Whenever it was set down, he would call out, “Return, Lord, to the thousands and thousands of the people of Israel.”</span>
