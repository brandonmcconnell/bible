<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 19:1; Numbers 10:11–12"> The Lord spoke to Moses in the Tent of Meeting while they were in the Sinai desert. This was on the first day of the second month of the second year after the Israelites had left Egypt. He told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 1:18"> “Census all the Israelites according to their tribe and family. Count every man and keep a record of each individual's name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:14; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 33:1"> Those aged twenty or older who can do military service are to be registered by you and Aaron in their Israelite army divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 1:16; Exodus 18:25; Numbers 13:2–15"> A representative from each tribe, the head of a family, must be there with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:30; Genesis 30:5–20; Genesis 49:1–33"> These are the names of the men who will work with you: From the tribe of Reuben, Elizur, son of Shedeur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 7:36; Numbers 2:12"> from the tribe of Simeon, Shelumiel, son of Zurishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Numbers 10:14; Matthew 1:2–5"> from the tribe of Judah, Nahshon, son of Amminadab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 10:15; Numbers 2:5"> from the tribe of Issachar, Nethanel, son of Zuar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 10:16; Numbers 2:7"> from the tribe of Zebulun, Eliab, son of Helon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 7:54; Numbers 7:48; 1 Chronicles 7:26–27"> from the sons of Joseph: from the tribe of Ephraim, Elishama, son of Ammihud, and from the tribe of Manasseh, Gamaliel, son of Pedahzur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24; Numbers 2:22"> from the tribe of Benjamin, Abidan, son of Gideoni;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 7:66; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 10:25"> from the tribe of Dan, Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 7:72; Numbers 10:26; Numbers 2:27"> from the tribe of Asher, Pagiel, son of Ocran;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 10:20"> from the tribe of Gad, Eliasaph, son of Deuel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:78; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 2:29"> and from the tribe of Naphtali, Ahira, son of Enan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Exodus 18:25"> These were the men chosen from the Israelite community. They were the leaders of their fathers' tribes; the heads of the families of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:3; Revelation 7:4–17; Numbers 1:5–15"> Moses and Aaron summoned these men who had been selected by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:59; Nehemiah 7:61; Hebrews 7:3"> They had all the Israelites gather together on the first day of the second month, and recorded the people's genealogy according to their tribe and family, and counted up the names of all those aged twenty or older,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:2; Numbers 26:1–2; 2 Samuel 24:1–10"> as the Lord had told Moses to do. Moses conducted this census in the Sinai desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:5–7; Numbers 2:10–11; Genesis 46:9"> The descendants of Reuben (he was Israel's firstborn son), men aged twenty or over, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 2:10–11"> from the tribe of Reuben totaled 46,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 26:12–14; Genesis 34:25–30; Numbers 2:12–13"> The descendants of Simeon, men aged twenty or over, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 2:13; Numbers 25:8–9"> from the tribe of Simeon totaled 59,300.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–18; Genesis 46:16; Numbers 2:14–15"> The descendants of Gad, men aged twenty or over, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 2:15"> from the tribe of Gad totaled 45,650.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:35; Numbers 26:19–22; 2 Samuel 24:9"> The descendants of Judah, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:9; Numbers 26:22; 2 Chronicles 17:14–16"> from the tribe of Judah, totaled 74,600.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 46:13; Genesis 49:14–15; Genesis 30:18"> The descendants of Issachar, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 2:6"> from the tribe of Issachar, totaled 54,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Genesis 49:13; Genesis 30:20"> The descendants of Zebulun, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 26:27; Numbers 2:8"> from the tribe of Zebulun, totaled 57,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 26:35–37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Genesis 46:20"> The descendants of Joseph: the descendants of Ephraim, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 2:19"> from the tribe of Ephraim, totaled 40,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 26:28–34"> And the descendants of Manasseh, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Genesis 48:19–20; Numbers 2:21"> from the tribe of Manasseh, totaled 32,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 44:20; Revelation 7:8"> The descendants of Benjamin, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:44–46; 2 Chronicles 17:17"> from the tribe of Benjamin, totaled 35,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 46:23; Genesis 30:5–6; Genesis 49:16–17"> The descendants of Dan, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 2:26"> from the tribe of Dan, totaled 62,700.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 30:12–13; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 49:20"> The descendants of Asher, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 26:47; Numbers 2:28"> from the tribe of Asher, totaled 41,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Numbers 26:48–50; Genesis 46:24"> The descendants of Naphtali, men aged twenty or older, were recorded by name according to the genealogical records of their tribe and families. All those registered who could serve in the army</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 26:50; Numbers 2:30"> from the tribe of Naphtali, totaled 53,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 26:64; Numbers 1:2–16"> These were the totals of the men counted and registered by Moses and Aaron, with the help of the twelve leaders of Israel, who each represented their family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> In this way all the Israelite men aged twenty or older who were able to serve in Israel's army were registered according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 26:51; Exodus 38:26"> The sum total of those registered was 603,550.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:33; Numbers 3:1–49; Numbers 26:57–62"> However, the Levites were not registered with the others according to their tribe and families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> This was because the Lord had told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Numbers 2:33"> “Don't register the tribe of Levi; don't count them in the census with the other Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; Exodus 31:18; Numbers 4:15"> Put the Levites in charge of the Tabernacle and of the Testimony,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Testimony refers to the stone tablets of the Ten Commandments contained inside the Ark.">a</a></sup>as well as all its furniture and everything it contains. They are the ones responsible for carrying the Tabernacle and all its items. They are to care for it, and to make their camp around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:10; 1 Samuel 6:19"> When it's time to move the Tabernacle, the Levites shall take it down, and when it's time to make camp, the Levites shall put it up. Any outsider who goes near it must be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Numbers 2:2; Numbers 2:34; Numbers 10:1–36"> The Israelites are to make camp according to their tribal divisions, each person in their own camp under their own flag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Numbers 16:46; 1 Chronicles 23:32; Leviticus 10:6"> But the Levites are to set up their camp around the Tabernacle of the Testimony to prevent anyone from making me angry with the Israelites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Presumably by preventing anyone who was not a priest approaching too close to the Tabernacle.">b</a></sup>The Levites are responsible for looking after the Tabernacle of the Testimony.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:32"> The Israelites did everything that the Lord ordered Moses that they should do.</span>
