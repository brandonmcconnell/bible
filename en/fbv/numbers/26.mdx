<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 25:9"> After the plague was over, the Lord told Moses and Eleazar son of Aaron the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 38:25–26; Numbers 1:2–3; Exodus 30:11–16"> “Census all the Israelites by family—all those twenty years of age or older who are eligible for military service in the army of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 33:48; Numbers 35:1"> There on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan opposite Jericho, Moses and Eleazar the priest gave the order,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; 1 Chronicles 21:1"> “Census the men twenty years of age or older, following the instructions the Lord gave to Moses.” The following is the genealogical record of those who left the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:3; Genesis 46:8–9; Exodus 6:14"> These were the descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn: Hanoch, ancestor of the Hanochite family; Pallu, ancestor of the Palluite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Hezron, ancestor of the Hezronite family; and Carmi, ancestor of the Carmite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Numbers 26:21; Numbers 2:11"> These were the families descended from Reuben and they numbered 43,730.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Pallu's son was Eliab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 1:16; Numbers 16:1–35; PSALMS 106:17"> and Eliab's sons were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. (It was Dathan and Abiram, leaders chosen by the Israelites, who joined the rebellion against Moses and Aaron with the followers of Korah when they rebelled against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 16:38; 2 Peter 2:6; Numbers 16:31–35"> The earth opened up and swallowed them down, along with Korah. His followers died when the fire burned up 250 men. What happened to them was a warning to the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 6:24; PSALMS 42:1; Numbers 16:33"> But Korah's sons didn't die.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:24; Genesis 46:10; Exodus 6:15"> These were the descendants of Simeon by family: Nemuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Jemuel,” see the parallel lists in Genesis 46:10and Exodus 6:15.">a</a></sup>ancestor of the Nemuelite family; Jamin, ancestor of the Jaminite family; Jachin, ancestor of the Jachinite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 46:10"> Zerah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Spelled “Zohar” in the parallel lists in Genesis 46:10and Exodus 6:15.">b</a></sup>ancestor of the Zerahite family; and Shaul, ancestor of the Shaulite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:22–23; Numbers 2:12–13"> These were the families descended from Simeon and they numbered 22,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 46:16; Numbers 2:14"> These were the descendants of Gad by family: Zephon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Spellied Ziphion in Genesis 46:15.">c</a></sup>ancestor of the Zephonite family; Haggi, ancestor of the Haggite family; Shuni, ancestor of the Shunite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 46:16"> Ozni, ancestor of the Oznite family; Eri, ancestor of the Erite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:16"> Arod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Spelled “Arodi” in Genesis 46:16.">d</a></sup>ancestor of the Arodite family; Areli, ancestor of the Arelite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:24–25; Numbers 2:14–15"> These were the families descended from Gad and they numbered 40,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:1–10; 1 Chronicles 2:3–8"> The sons of Judah who died in Canaan were Er and Onan. These were the descendants of Judah by family:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:24; Genesis 46:12; Luke 3:33"> Shelah, ancestor of the Shelanite family; Perez, ancestor of the Perezite family; Zerah, ancestor of the Zerahite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> These were the descendants of Perez: Hezron, ancestor of the Hezronite family; and Hamul, ancestor of the Hamulite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 1:26–27; Numbers 2:3–4; Genesis 49:8"> These were the families descended from Judah and they numbered 76,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 46:13; 1 Chronicles 7:1; Genesis 30:17–18"> These were the descendants of Issachar by family: Tola, ancestor of the Tolaite family; Puvah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Spelled “Puah” in some ancient translations.">e</a></sup>ancestor of the Punite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:13"> Jashub, ancestor of the Jashubite family; and Shimron, ancestor of the Shimronite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 2:5–6; Numbers 1:28–29"> These were the families descended from Isaachar and they numbered 64,300.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 46:14; Genesis 30:19–20"> These were the descendants of Zebulun by family: Sered, ancestor of the Seredite family; Elon, ancestor of the Elonite family; and Jahleel, ancestor of the Jahleelite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:30–31; Numbers 2:7–8"> These were the families descended from Zebulun, and they numbered 60,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; Genesis 41:51–52"> These were descendants of Joseph by family through Manasseh and Ephraim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Numbers 36:1; 1 Chronicles 7:14–19"> The descendants of Manasseh: Machir (he was the father of Gilead), ancestor of the Machirite family; and Gilead, ancestor of the Gileadite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 6:11; Judges 6:34; Judges 6:24"> The descendants of Gilead: Iezer, ancestor of the Iezerite family; Helek, ancestor of the Helekite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Asriel, ancestor of the Asrielite family; Shechem, ancestor of the Shechemite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Shemida, ancestor of the Shemidaite family; and Hepher, ancestor of the Hepherite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 36:10–12"> (Zelophehad, son of Hepher, didn't have any sons, only daughters. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 1:34–35; Numbers 2:20–21"> These were the families descended from Manasseh, and they numbered 52,700.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:20–21"> These were the descendants of Ephraim by family: Shuthelah, ancestor of the Shuthelahite family; Becher, ancestor of the Becherite family; and Tahan, ancestor of the Tahanite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The descendant of Shuthelah was Eran, ancestor of the Eranite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 1:32–33; Numbers 2:18–19"> These were the families descended from Ephraim, and they numbered 32,500. These families were the descendants of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:1; 1 Chronicles 7:6–12; Genesis 46:2"> These were the descendants of Benjamin by family: Bela, ancestor of the Belaite family; Ashbel, ancestor of the Ashbelite family; Ahiram, ancestor of the Ahiramite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 46:21"> Shupham,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “Shephupham.”">f</a></sup>ancestor of the Shuphamite family; and Hupham, ancestor of the Huphamite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:3"> The descendants of Bela were Ard, ancestor of the Ardite family; and Naaman, ancestor of the Naamite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 2:22–23; Genesis 46:21; Numbers 1:36–37"> These were the families descended from Benjamin, and they numbered 45,600.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 46:23"> These were the descendants of Dan by family: Shuham, ancestor of the Shuhamite families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 1:38–39; Numbers 2:25–26"> They were all Shuhamite families, and they numbered 64,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 46:17; 1 Chronicles 7:30"> These were the descendants of Asher by family: Imnah, ancestor of the Imnite family; Ishvi, ancestor of the Ishvite family; and Beriah, ancestor of the Beriite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> The descendants of Beriah were Heber, ancestor of the Heberite family; and Malchiel, ancestor of the Malchielite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 46:17"> The name of Asher's daughter was Serah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:27–28; Numbers 1:40–41"> These were the families descended from Asher, and they numbered 53,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 46:24; 1 Chronicles 7:13"> These were the descendants of Naphtali by family: Jahzeel, ancestor of the Jahzeelite family; Guni, ancestor of the Gunite family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:13"> Jezer, ancestor of the Jezerite family; and Shillem, ancestor of the Sheillemite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 1:42–43; Numbers 2:29–30"> These were the families descended from Naphtali, and they numbered 45,400.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Numbers 2:32; Exodus 12:37"> The total of all those counted was 601,730.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Joshua 14:1; PSALMS 105:44"> “Divide the land that is to be owned based on the number of those censused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 32:3; Numbers 32:5"> Give a larger area of land to a larger tribe, and a smaller area to a smaller tribe. Each tribe shall receive their allotment of land depending on their number counted in the census.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Numbers 34:13; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 14:2"> The land has to be divided up by casting lots. Everyone shall receive their allotted land based on the name of their ancestor's tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Romans 11:7"> Every allocation of land is to be divided by casting lots among the tribes, whether large or small.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; 1 Chronicles 6:1; Exodus 6:16–19"> These were the Levites censused by family: Gershon, ancestor of the Gershonite family; Kohath, ancestor of the Kohathite family; and Merari, ancestor of the Merarite family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Numbers 16:1; Numbers 3:17–21; Exodus 6:20"> The following were the families of the Levites: the Libnite family, the Hebronite family, the Mahlite family, the Mushite family, and the Korahite family. Kohath was the father of Amram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Exodus 2:1–2; Leviticus 18:12"> and the name of Amram's wife was Jochebed. She was a descendant of Levi, born while the Levites were in Egypt. She had children with Amram: Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Numbers 3:2; Numbers 3:8"> Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Numbers 3:4; Leviticus 10:1–2; 1 Chronicles 24:1–2"> but Nadab and Abihu died when they offered forbidden fire in the Lord's presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:9; Numbers 35:2–8; Numbers 3:39"> The number of the Levites censused totaled 23,000. This included every male one month old or older. However, they were not counted with the other Israelites, because no land allotment was given to them with the other Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Numbers 26:3"> This is the record of those who were censused by Moses and Eleazar the priest when they counted the Israelites on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan opposite Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:14–15; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; 1 Corinthians 10:5"> However, they did not include a single one who had previously been censused by Moses and Aaron the priest when they counted the Israelites in the Sinai Desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–6; Numbers 14:38; Romans 11:22"> because the Lord had told them that they would all die in the desert. No one was left except Caleb, son of Jephunneh, and Joshua, son of Nun.</span>
