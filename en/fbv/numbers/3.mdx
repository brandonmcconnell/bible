<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> This is the account concerning Aaron and Moses when the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> The names of the sons of Aaron were: Nadab (firstborn), Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These were the names of Aaron's sons who were anointed and ordained to serve as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> Nadab and Abihu died in the Lord's presence when they offered forbidden fire before the Lord in the Sinai desert. Since they didn't have any sons, Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests while their father Aaron was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> “Gather the tribe of Levi together and present them to Aaron the priest to help him with the ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> They are to carry out duties on his behalf and for all the Israelites at the Tent of Meeting, looking after the service of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> They are responsible for caring for all the furniture of the Tent of Meeting, serving the Israelites through what they do in the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> The Levites are to work exclusively for Aaron and his sons because this is their assignment among the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> You are to appoint Aaron and his sons to have the responsibility of the priesthood. Anyone else who tries to act as a priest must be executed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> “I've taken the Levites from the Israelites in place of every one of their firstborn. The Levites belong to me</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> because all the firstborn are mine. When I killed every firstborn in Egypt I set apart as holy to me all the firstborn of Israel, human and animal. They are mine. I am the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The Lord spoke to Moses in the Sinai desert. He told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> “Register the Levites by their father's line and family. Count every male aged one month or older.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> So Moses registered them following the Lord's instructions, just as he had been told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> These were the names of Levi's sons: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> These were the names of Gershon's sons by family: Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> Kohath's sons by family were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> Merari's sons by family were Mahli and Mushi. These were the families of the Levites, according to their father's line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The family of Libni and the family of Shimei came from Gershon. These were the families of Gershon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> The total of all males aged one month or older was 7,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The camp of families of Gershon was to the west, behind the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The leader of the Gershon families was Eliasaph, son of Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> Their assigned responsibility for the Tent of Meeting was to take care of the Tabernacle and tent, its covering, the curtain at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> the courtyard curtains, the curtain at the entrance to the courtyard surrounding the Tabernacle and altar, the ropes, and everything connected with their use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The families of Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel came from Kohath. These were the families of Kohath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> The total of all males aged one month or older was 8,600. Their assigned responsibility was to take care of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The camp of families of Kohath was on the south side of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The leader of the Kohath families was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> Their assigned responsibility was to care for the Ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the articles of the sanctuary used with them, the veil, and everything connected with these items.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> The chief of the leaders of the Levites was Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest. He was in charge of those responsible for serving in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> The family of Mahli and the family of Mushi came from Merari. These were the families of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> The total of all males aged one month or older was 6,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> The leader of the Merari families was Zuriel, son of Abihail. Their camp was on the north side of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> Their assigned responsibility was to take care of the Tabernacle's frames, crossbars, posts, stands, all its equipment, and everything connected with their use,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> as well as the posts of the surrounding courtyard with their stands, tent pegs, and ropes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> The camp of Moses, Aaron, and Aaron's sons was to the east of the Tabernacle, in the direction of the sunrise, in front of the Tent of Meeting. They were responsible for the sanctuary on behalf of the Israelites. Anyone else who tried to act as a priest was to be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> The sum total of Levites registered by Moses and Aaron as the Lord ordered was 22,000. This included all males aged one month or older.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; PSALMS 87:6"> The Lord told Moses, “Conduct a census of all the firstborn Israelite males aged one month or older, and register their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> Assign the Levites to me. I am the Lord. They are in place of all of the Israelites' firstborn children. The livestock of the Levites are in place of all the Israelites' firstborn livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Moses conducted a census of all the firstborn of the Israelites, as the Lord had instructed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> The sum total of the firstborn males a month old or more, registered by name, was 22,273.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> The Lord spoke to Moses and told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> “You are to take the Levites in place of all the firstborn children of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock, because the Levites belong to me. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> In order to buy back the 273 firstborn Israelites who are more than the number of Levites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> collect five shekels for each of them (using the sanctuary shekel standard of twenty gerahs).</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> He handed the money over to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for the extra Israelites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Moses collected the redemption money for those extra Israelites that were more than the number redeemed by the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> He collected the money given on behalf of the Israelites' firstborn children. It came to 1,365 shekels (using the sanctuary shekel standard).</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> Moses gave this redemption money to Aaron and his sons as the Lord had told him to, following the Lord's instructions.</span>
</div>
