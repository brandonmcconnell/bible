<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> “Send some men to explore the land of Canaan, the country I'm giving to the Israelites. Choose one of the leaders from each of the tribes to go and do this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> Moses did as the Lord had ordered and sent the men out from the Desert of Paran. They were all leaders of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their names were: Shammua son of Zaccur, from the tribe of Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Shaphat, son of Hori, from the tribe of Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> Caleb, son of Jephunneh, from the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Igal, son of Joseph, from the tribe of Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> Hoshea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Also called Joshua, see verse 16.">a</a></sup>son of Nun, from the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Palti son of Raphu, from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Gaddiel, son of Sodi, from the tribe of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Gaddi, son of Susi, from the tribe of Manasseh (a tribe of Joseph).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Ammiel, son of Gemalli, from the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Sethur, son of Michael, from the tribe of Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nahbi, son of Vophsi, from the tribe of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Geuel, son of Machi, from the tribe of Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> These were the names of the men that Moses sent to explore the country. Moses called Hoshea Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> Moses sent them out to explore the land of Canaan, telling them, “Go through the Negev and on into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> See what the place looks like, and find out about the people living there—are they strong or weak? Are there many of them or only a few?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Is the land where they're living good or bad? Are their towns like open camps, or do they have defensive walls?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> Is the soil productive or not? Is it forested? Be brave, and bring back some of the country's fruit.” (It was the beginning of the grape harvest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> So the men went and explored the land all the way from the Desert of Zin to Rehob, bear Lebo-hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 78:12; PSALMS 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> They went through the Negev and arrived in Hebron where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, lived. This town was built seven years before the Egyptian town of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> When they arrived at the Valley of Eshcol they chopped down a branch that had just one bunch of grapes. They had to carry it on a pole held between two men. They also collected some pomegranates and figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> (The place was named the Valley of Eshcol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Eshcol” means “bunch.”">b</a></sup>because of the bunch of grapes they took from there.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> Forty days later the men returned from exploring the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> They went to see Moses and Aaron, and all the Israelites gathered there at their camp in Kadesh in the Desert of Paran. They gave a report before everyone and showed them the fruit they had brought back from the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> This is the report they gave to Moses: “We went and explored the country you sent us to, and it is definitely very productive, as if it was flowing with milk and honey. Just look at some of its fruit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> But the people living there are strong, and their towns are big and have defensive walls. We also saw some descendants of Anak there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> The Amalekites live in the Negev. The Hittites, Jebusites, and the Amorites live in the hill country. The Canaanites live on the sea coast and also beside the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> Then Caleb asked for quiet as the people stood before Moses and told them, “Let's go and take over the land. We can conquer the country, no doubt about it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> But the men who had gone with him disagreed. “We can't go and fight these people! They're much stronger than us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> They spread a negative report among the Israelites about the country they had explored. They told people, “The country we explored destroys the people that live there. Plus everyone we saw was really big!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> We even saw giants there—people descended from Anak the giant! Compared to them we looked like grasshoppers, and we must have looked like that to them too!”</span>
