<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> The Lord spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan opposite Jericho. He told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> “Order the Israelites to provide from their land allocation towns for the Levites to live in and pastures around the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> The towns are for them to live in, and the pastures will be for their herds and their flocks—for all their livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The pastures around the towns you give to the Levites are to extend out from the wall a thousand cubits on all sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Measure two thousand cubits outside the town on the east, two thousand on the south, two thousand on the west, and two thousand on the north, with the town in the middle. These larger areas will be their pastures around the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; PSALMS 9:9"> Six of the towns you give the Levites are to be sanctuary towns,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See also Joshua 20.">a</a></sup>where a person who kills someone can run for protection. As well as these towns, give the Levites forty-two more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> The total number of towns you are to give the Levites is forty-eight, along with their pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> The towns that you allocate to be given to the Levites from the territory of the Israelites will be more from a larger tribe and less from a smaller one. The number will be in proportion to the size of the land allocation of each tribe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> “Tell the Israelites: ‘When you cross over the Jordan into Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> choose towns as your sanctuary towns, so a person who kills someone by mistake may run there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> These towns will be for you places of sanctuary from those seeking revenge, so that the killer will not die until they are tried in court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The towns you choose will be your six sanctuary towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> Choose three cities on the other side of the Jordan and three in Canaan as cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> These six cities will be places of sanctuary for the Israelites and for foreigners or settlers among them, so that anyone who kills a person by mistake may run there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> But if anyone deliberately hits someone with something made of iron and kills them, that person is a murderer and must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> If anyone picks up something made of stone that could be used as a weapon and hits someone with it, and kills them, that person is a murderer and must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> If anyone picks up something made of wood that could be used as a weapon and hits someone with it, and kills them, that person is a murderer and must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The avenger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Avenger:” this was the closest relative to the victim: literally, “the avenger of blood.”">b</a></sup>is to execute the murderer. When he finds the murderer, he is to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Similarly, if anyone hates someone and knocks them down or deliberately throws something at them, and they're killed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or if someone hits another with his hand and they die, the one who hit him must be executed because he is a murderer. When the avenger finds the murderer, he is to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> But if anyone knocks someone else down without meaning to and without hating them, or throws something at them not meaning to hurt them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or carelessly drops a heavy stone that kills them, but not as an enemy or intending to harm them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> then the community must judge between the killer and the avenger following these regulations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> The court is to protect the killer from being attacked by the avenger and must return him to the sanctuary town that he ran to, and he must stay there until the death of the high priest, who was anointed with holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if the killer ever leaves the limits of the sanctuary town where he ran to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and the avenger finds him outside his sanctuary town and kills him, then the avenger will not be guilty of murder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> because the killer has to stay in his sanctuary town until the death of the high priest. Only after the death of the high priest is he allowed to return to the land he owns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> These regulations apply to all future generations wherever you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> If anyone kills a person, the murderer is to be executed based on the evidence given by witnesses, plural. No one is to be executed based on the evidence given by a single witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; PSALMS 51:14"> You are not to accept payment instead of executing a murderer who has been found guilty—they must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> Also you are not allowed to accept payment for a person who runs to a sanctuary town and permit him to return and live on his own land before the death of the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; PSALMS 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> Don't pollute the land where you live because bloodshed pollutes the land, and the land where blood is shed can't be purified except by the blood of the one who shed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; PSALMS 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> Don't make the land impure where you live because I live there too. I am the Lord, and I live with the Israelites.’”</span>
</div>
