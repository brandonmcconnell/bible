<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> “Register the descendants of Kohath from the tribe of Levi, according to their family and father's line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3–5; 2 Kings 11:4–12"> Count men aged thirty to fifty—all who are entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Mark 13:34; Numbers 4:30"> This work that they are to do in the Tent of Meetings involves the things that are most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3; Exodus 40:3"> Whenever you move camp, Aaron and his sons will go in, take down the veil, and place it over the Ark of the Testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:13–15; Exodus 35:19; Numbers 4:7–8"> On top of this they are to put a fine leather covering, spread a cloth of solid blue over it, and then insert its carrying poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 37:10–16; Exodus 25:23–30"> Have them spread a blue cloth over the table of the Presence as well, and put the plates and cups on it, as well as the bowls and jugs for the drink offering. The current bread offering is to stay on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:6–7; Numbers 4:11–13; Numbers 4:9"> Over everything they are to spread a crimson cloth, next a fine leather covering, and then insert its carrying poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Exodus 37:17–24; Revelation 1:20–1"> Using a blue cloth, they are to cover the lampstand of light, together with its lamps, wick tongs, and trays, as well as the jars of olive oil used to fill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:12; Numbers 4:6"> Then have them wrap it together with all its utensils inside a fine leather covering and place it on the carrying frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Exodus 30:1–19; Exodus 40:26–27"> They are to spread a blue cloth over the gold altar, cover it with fine leather, and then insert its carrying poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:29; Exodus 25:9; 2 Chronicles 4:22"> They are to place all the utensils used for the service in the sanctuary in a blue cloth, cover them with fine leather, and place them on the carrying frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Exodus 39:1; Exodus 39:41"> Have them clean the ashes from the bronze altar and spread a purple cloth over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:19; Exodus 38:1–7"> and put on it all the equipment used in the services on the altar: the firepans, meat forks, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. Spread over it a fine leather covering and then insert its carrying poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 7:9; Deuteronomy 31:9"> Once Aaron and his sons have finished covering these holy things and all the equipment connected with them, when the camp is ready to move, the priests of the Kohath family are to come and carry them. But they are forbidden to touch anything holy, otherwise they'll die. These are their responsibilities for moving the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:6; Leviticus 24:2; Exodus 30:23–38"> Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, is to supervise obtaining olive oil for the lamps, the aromatic incense, the daily grain offering, and the anointing oil. He is in charge of the whole Tabernacle and everything in it—all the holy things and equipment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The Lord told Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 17:10; Numbers 18:5"> “Make sure the families of Kohath are not wiped out from the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 4:15"> This is what you have to do so that they'll live and won't die by getting too close to something holy. Aaron and his sons must go in and tell each of them what they have to do and what they have to carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 19:21; Numbers 4:15"> But they must not go in and look at the things that are most holy, even for just a moment, otherwise they'll die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 3:18; Numbers 3:21; Numbers 3:24"> “Register the descendants of Gershon, according to their family and father's line</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:7"> Count men aged thirty to fifty—all who are entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Numbers 4:31–32; Numbers 4:47"> This is how the families of Gershon will serve in terms of working and carrying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 3:25–26; Exodus 26:14; Numbers 7:5–7"> Have them carry the Tabernacle curtains, the Tent of Meeting with its fine leather covering, the curtains for the entrance to the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 27:9"> the courtyard curtains, the curtain at the entrance to the courtyard surrounding the Tabernacle and altar, the ropes, and everything connected with their use. The families of Gershon are responsible for everything required involving these items.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:70; 1 Corinthians 11:2"> All that they do is to be under the supervision of Aaron and his sons—all their work and carrying assignments. You are to tell them all they are required to carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; 1 Corinthians 12:5–6"> These are their responsibilities for moving the Tent of Meeting, carried out under the direction of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 3:33–35"> Register the descendants of Merari, according to their family and father's line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 2:4"> Count men aged thirty to fifty—all who are entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 3:36–37; Exodus 26:15; Numbers 7:8–9"> This is how they will serve in handling the Tent of Meeting: they are to carry the frames of the Tabernacle with its crossbars, posts, and stands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; 1 Chronicles 9:29; Numbers 3:8"> the posts of the surrounding courtyard with their stands, tent pegs, and ropes, all their equipment and everything connected with their use. You are to tell them by name what they are responsible for carrying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 4:28; Joshua 3:6; Isaiah 3:6"> These are their responsibilities for all their work in moving the Tent of Meeting, carried out under the direction of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Moses, Aaron, and the Israelite leaders registered the families of Kohath according to their family and father's line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> They counted men aged thirty to fifty—all who were entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The total by families was 2,750.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> This was the total of the families of Kohath—all who were entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron registered them in accordance with the Lord's instructions to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> The families of Gershon were counted, according to their family and father's line,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> men aged thirty to fifty—all who were entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:32"> The total by families and father's line was 2,630.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> This was the total of the families of Gershon —all who were entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting. They were registered by Moses and Aaron in accordance with the Lord's instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The families of Merari were counted, according to their family and father's line,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> men aged thirty to fifty—all who were entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Corinthians 12:8–12"> The total by families was 3,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 4:29"> This was the total of the families of Merari registered by Moses and Aaron in accordance with the Lord's instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> This is how Moses, Aaron, and the Israelite leaders registered all the Levites according to their family and father's line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–31; Romans 12:6–8; Numbers 4:3"> They counted men aged thirty to fifty—all who were entitled to do the work of serving in the Tent of Meeting and carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Matthew 20:16; Matthew 22:15"> The sum total was 8,580.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 1:54; Numbers 4:31; Numbers 4:15"> It was in response to the Lord's instructions that they were registered by Moses. Each of those registered were told what to do and what to carry, as the Lord had ordered Moses.</span>
