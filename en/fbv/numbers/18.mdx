<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:38; Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:24"> The Lord told Aaron, “You and your sons and the other Levites bear the responsibility for sins relating to the sanctuary. You and your sons alone bear the responsibility for sins relating to your priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; Numbers 18:4; Ezekiel 44:15"> Have your brothers from the tribe of Levi, your father's tribe, join you to help you and your sons with your service in the Tent of the Testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 4:19–20"> They will take care of your responsibilities and those relating to the Tent, but they must not come too close to the sacred objects of the sanctuary or the altar, otherwise they will die, and you will too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:10"> They are to help you and take care of the responsibilities of the Tent of Meeting, doing all the work at the Tent, but they are not allowed to be with you during your priestly ministry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“During your priestly ministry”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Leviticus 24:3; Numbers 16:46"> You are to carry out the responsibilities relating to the sanctuary and the altar, so that my anger will not fall on the Israelites again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:9; Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45"> Look, I myself have chosen your brothers the Levites from the Israelites as my gift to you, dedicated to the Lord to do the work that relates to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:15–16; John 3:27; Numbers 3:10"> But only you and your sons are responsible for your priesthood, doing all that concerns the altar and is behind the veil. Only you are to perform that service. I am giving you the gift of your priesthood, but anyone else who approaches the sanctuary must be executed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:16; Leviticus 7:6; Exodus 29:29"> The Lord told Aaron, “Listen, I have put you in charge of officiating at my offerings. All the holy contributions of the Israelites that they bring are reserved for you, and this is a permanent rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 14:13; Leviticus 6:25–26"> Part of the most holy offerings taken from the burnt offerings are yours. Part of all the offerings they give me as most holy offerings, whether it's grain offerings or sin offerings or guilt offerings, that part belongs to you and your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6; Leviticus 6:29; Leviticus 6:16"> You shall eat it in a most holy place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="As required by Levitical law—see for example Leviticus 6:16, Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 7:6.">b</a></sup>Every male is allowed to eat it. You are to regard it as something holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3; Exodus 29:27–28; Leviticus 10:14"> The following also belongs to you: the voluntary gifts as well as all the wave offerings of the Israelites. I have given this to you and your sons and daughters as a permanent rule. Everyone in your household who is ceremonially clean is allowed to eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> I am giving you all the best olive oil and all the best new wine and grain that the Israelites give as firstfruits to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:29; Exodus 23:19; Micah 7:1"> The firstfruits of all the crops they produce in their land that they bring to the Lord are yours. Everyone in your family who is ceremonially clean is allowed to eat them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Ezekiel 44:29"> Everything in Israel that is dedicated to the Lord is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13"> Every firstborn, whether human or animal, that is offered to the Lord is yours. But you must buy back every firstborn son and every firstborn male of unclean animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:2–7"> When they are one month old you shall pay the redemption price of five shekels of silver (using the sanctuary shekel standard), equivalent to twenty gerahs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 29:16; Deuteronomy 15:19–22; Leviticus 3:2–5"> But you are not allowed to redeem the firstborn of an ox, a sheep, or a goat because they are holy. You shall sprinkle their blood on the altar, and burn their fat as a burnt offering accepted by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 7:31–34; Exodus 29:26–28"> Their meat is yours, in the same way that the breast and right thigh of the wave offering are yours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:5; Leviticus 2:13; Numbers 18:11"> I am giving all the voluntary gifts that the Israelites present to the Lord to you and to your sons and daughters as a permanent rule. It is a permanent agreement of salt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Permanent agreement of salt”: meaning an agreement that could not be broken. Salt was used as a preservative, and offerings to the Lord included salt (see Leviticus 2:13).">c</a></sup>before the Lord for you and your descendants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Deuteronomy 10:9; Joshua 13:33"> You won't possess property in their country, and you won't have a share in their land. I am your share and your possession among the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Deuteronomy 14:22–29; Nehemiah 10:37"> Instead I have given the Levites all the tithes in Israel as compensation for the service they provide in doing the work in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; Numbers 1:51–52; Numbers 3:38"> The Israelites are no longer allowed to approach the Tent of Meeting, or they will commit an offense and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Numbers 18:20; Numbers 18:1"> The Levites are to carry out the work in the Tent of Meeting, and they must take responsibility for any sins involved. This is a permanent rule for all future generations. The Levites won't receive a share of land among the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Malachi 3:8–10"> Instead I have given to the Levites as their compensation the tithe that the Israelites give to the Lord as a contribution. That's why I told them that they wouldn't receive a share of land among the Israelites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38; Numbers 18:21"> “Talk to the Levites and explain to them, ‘When you receive from the Israelites the tithe that I have given you as your compensation, you must return part of it as an offering to the Lord: a tithe of the tithe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:30; Deuteronomy 15:14; 2 Kings 6:27"> Your offering will be considered as if it were the firstfruits of grain from your threshing floor or grape juice from the winepress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–10; Genesis 14:18"> In this way you are to contribute an offering to the Lord from every tithe you receive from the Israelites, giving the Lord's offering to Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> From all the gifts you receive you are to contribute as the Lord's offering the very best, the holiest part of every gift.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Genesis 43:11; Matthew 6:33"> So tell the Levites, ‘When you have presented the best part, it will be considered as your contribution produced by your threshing floor or winepress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:10–14; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; Luke 10:7"> You and your families may eat it anywhere because it's compensation for your service in the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 19:8; Leviticus 22:2; Malachi 1:7"> You will not be considered to have sinned if you have presented the best part of it. But if you treat the sacred offerings of the Israelites with disrespect you will die.’”</span>
</div>
