<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; Exodus 18:25"> Moses told the tribal leaders of Israel, “This is what the Lord commands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 50:14; PSALMS 116:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> If a man makes a solemn promise to the Lord, or pledges to do something by swearing an oath, he must not break his promise. He must do everything he said he would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> If a woman who's young and still living in her father's house makes a solemn promise to the Lord or pledges to do something by swearing an oath</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and her father finds out about her promise or pledge but doesn't say anything to her, all the promises or pledges she has sworn to do will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Matthew 15:4–6; Ephesians 6:1"> But if her father disallows them as soon as he finds out, then none of her promises or pledges remain valid. The Lord will release her from keeping them because her father has disallowed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 56:12"> If a woman marries after having made a solemn promise or sworn an oath without thinking</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and her husband finds out about it but doesn't say anything to her right away, all the promises or pledges she has sworn to do will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 14:34"> But if her husband disallows them when he finds out about it, then none of her promises or oaths remain valid and the Lord will release her from keeping them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:37; Romans 7:2; Leviticus 21:7"> Every solemn promise made by a widow or a woman who is divorced must be kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If a woman living with her husband makes a solemn promise to the Lord or pledges to do something by swearing an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and her husband finds out about her promise or pledge but doesn't say anything to her and doesn't disallow them, then all of her promises or pledges remain valid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 30:8; 1 Corinthians 11:3; Numbers 15:25"> But if her husband disallows them as soon as he finds out about it, then none of her promises or oaths remain valid. The Lord will release her from keeping them because her husband has disallowed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Isaiah 58:5"> Her husband may also confirm or disallow any solemn promise or pledge the woman may make for self-denial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But if her husband doesn't ever say a word to her about it, then he is assumed to have confirmed all the solemn promises and pledges she has made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> However, if he disallows them some time later after finding out about them, then he will bear the responsibility for her breaking them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54–57; Leviticus 11:46–47; Numbers 5:29–30"> These are the regulations that the Lord gave to Moses regarding the relationship between a man and his wife, and between a father and a daughter who is young and still living at home.</span>
