<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; PSALMS 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Give this command to the Israelites: When you enter the country of Canaan, it will be allocated to you to own with the following boundaries:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Other passages which include boundary demarcations include: Joshua 13:8-33, Joshua 14:1to Joshua 19:51, Ezekiel 47:13-20.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> The southern extent of your country will be from the Desert of Zin along the border of Edom. Your southern border will run east from the end of the Dead Sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> cross south of Scorpion Pass, on to Zin, and reach its southern limit south of Kadesh-barnea. Then it will go to Hazar-addar and on to Azmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> There the boundary will turn from Azmon to the Wadi of Egypt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Usually identified with Wadi El-Arish. It is not thought to refer to the Nile.">b</a></sup>ending at the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> Your western border will be the coast of the Mediterranean Sea. This is to be your boundary to the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> Your northern border will run from the Mediterranean Sea straight to Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> From Mount Hor the boundary will go to Lebo-hamath, then on to Zedad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> to Ziphron, ending at Hazar-enan. This will be your boundary to the north.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Your eastern border will run straight from Hazar-enan to Shepham</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> Then the boundary will go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain. It will pass down along the slopes east of the Sea of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> Then the boundary will go down along the Jordan, ending at the Dead Sea. This is to be your land with its surrounding borders.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> Moses gave the order to the Israelites, “Allocate ownership of this land by lot. The Lord has commanded it to be given to the nine and a half tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> The tribes of Reuben and Gad, together with the half-tribe of Manasseh, have already received their allocation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> These two and a half tribes have received their allocation on the east side of the Jordan opposite Jericho.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These are the names of the men who will be in charge of allocating ownership of the land for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua, son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> Have one leader from each tribe help in the distribution of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> These are their names:<br />From the tribe of Judah: Caleb, son of Jephunneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> From the tribe of Simeon: Shemuel, son of Ammihud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; PSALMS 68:27"> From the tribe of Benjamin: Elidad, son of Chislon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A leader from the tribe of Dan: Bukki, son of Jogli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A leader from the tribe of Manasseh, son of Joseph: Hanniel son of Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> A leader from the tribe of Ephraim: Kemuel, son of Shiphtan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> A leader from the tribe of Zebulun: Eli-zaphan, son of Parnach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A leader from the tribe of Issachar: Paltiel, son of Azzan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A leader from the tribe of Asher: Ahihud, son of Shelomi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A leader from the tribe of Naphtali: Pedahel, son of Ammihud.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are the names of those the Lord put in charge of allocating land ownership in the country of Canaan.</span>
</div>
</div>
