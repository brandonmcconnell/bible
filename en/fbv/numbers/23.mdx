<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> Then Balaam told Balak, “Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me to sacrifice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> Balak did as Balaam had said, and together they offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> Balaam said to Balak, “Wait here beside your burnt offering while I go and see if perhaps the Lord will come and meet with me. Whatever he reveals to me, I'll share with you.” Then Balaam left to climb up a rocky crag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> God met with him there, and Balaam said. “I have built seven altars and on each altar I have offered a bull and a ram.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> The Lord gave Balaam a message to share. He told him, “Go back to Balak and this is what you are to say to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he went back to Balak, who was waiting there beside his burnt offering, along with all the Moabite leaders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> This is the declaration that Balaam gave: “Balak brought me from Aram; the king of Moab brought me from the mountains of the east. He said, ‘Come and curse Jacob for me! Come and condemn Israel!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> But how can I curse what God has not cursed? How can I condemn what the Lord has not condemned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> For I'm looking down on them from the top of rocky crags; I'm watching them from the hills. I see a people who live on their own, different from the other nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 37:37; Genesis 13:16; PSALMS 116:15"> Who can count Jacob's descendants? They are so many they're like dust! Who can count even a quarter of the Israelites? I would like to die as a good person dies! Let the end of my life be like their end!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> Then Balak complained to Balaam, “What on earth have you done to me? I brought you here to curse my enemies, and now look! All you have done is bless them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> But Balaam replied, “Don't you think I should say precisely what the Lord tells me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Then Balak said to him, “Please come with me to another place where you can see them. But you'll only see a part of their camp—you won't see all of them. You can curse them for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> He took him to the field of Zophim at the top of Mount Pisgah. There he built seven altars and offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> Balaam told Balak, “Wait here beside your burnt offering while I meet the Lord over there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> The Lord met Balaam and gave him a message to share. He told him, “Go back to Balak and this is what you are to say to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> So he went back to Balak, who was waiting there beside his burnt offering, along with all the Moabite leaders. “What did the Lord say?” Balak asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> This is the prophecy that Balaam delivered: “Stand up, Balak, and pay attention! Listen to me, son of Zippor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> God isn't a human being who would lie. He's not a mere mortal who changes his mind. Does he say he's going to do something but doesn't? Does he make promises he doesn't carry out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> Look, I have been ordered to give a blessing. God has blessed, and I can't change that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> He's not expecting anything bad will happen to Jacob; he doesn't foresee any trouble for Israel. The Lord their God is with them; they celebrate him as their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> God led them out of Egypt with great power, as strong as an ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> No spell can be cast against Jacob; no magic can be used against Israel. People will talk about Jacob and Israel, saying, ‘What amazing things God has done for them!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Look! The Israelites go out hunting like a lioness; they chase like a lion. They don't rest until they eat their prey, and drink the blood of their dead victim.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 2:1–3"> Then Balak said to Balaam, “If you can't give them any curses then at least don't give them any blessings!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> But Balaam replied, “Didn't I explain to you that I have to do whatever the Lord tells me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> “Please come with me and I will take you somewhere else,” Balak said. “Maybe God would let you curse them for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; PSALMS 106:28"> Balak took Balaam to the top of Mount Peor, which looks down on the wastelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> Balaam told Balak, “Build seven altars for me here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me to sacrifice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Balak did what Balaam told him, and he offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
