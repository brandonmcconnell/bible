<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> “Punish the Midianites because of what they did to the Israelites. After that you will join your forefathers in death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> Moses instructed the people, “Have some of your men get ready for battle, so they can go and attack the Midianites and carry out the Lord's punishment on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> You are to contribute one thousand men from each Israelite tribe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So one thousand men were chosen from each Israelite tribe, making twelve thousand troops ready for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> Moses sent them into battle, one thousand from each tribe, together with Phinehas, son of Eleazar the priest. He carried with him the holy objects from the sanctuary and the trumpets used for giving signals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> They attacked the Midianites, as the Lord had told Moses to do, and they killed all the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> Among those killed were the five kings of Midian, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. They also killed Balaam, son of Beor, with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> The Israelites captured the Midianite women and children, and they took as plunder all their herds, flocks, and possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> They set fire to all the Midianite towns and camps where they had lived,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> and carried away all the plunder and loot, including people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They brought the prisoners, loot, and plunder to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the rest of the Israelites where they were camped on the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan opposite Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the Israelite leaders went out of the camp to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> Moses was angry with the army officers, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, who returned from the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> “Why did you let all the women live?” he asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> “You realize that these were the same women who seduced the Israelite men, leading them to be unfaithful to the Lord at Peor, following Balaam's advice! That's why the Lord's people suffered from the plague!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> So go and kill all the boys, and every woman who has slept with a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> Let all the girls who are virgins live. They are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> All those of you who killed someone or touched a dead body must stay outside the camp for seven days. Purify yourselves and your prisoners on the third day and the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> Also purify all your clothing and anything made of leather, goat's hair, or wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Eleazar the priest told the soldiers who had gone into battle, “These are the legal regulations that the Lord has ordered Moses to be carried out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> All that's made of gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> anything that doesn't burn—must be put through fire to make it clean. But it still has to be purified using water of purification. Anything that burns must be put through the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> Wash your clothes on the seventh day and you will be clean. Then you can enter the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “You, Eleazar the priest, and the Israelite family leaders are to take a record of the people and animals that were captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; PSALMS 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> Then divide them between the troops who went into battle and the rest of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> Take as a contribution to the Lord from what is allocated to the troops who went into battle one out of every five hundred people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Take this from their half share and give it to Eleazar the priest as an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> From the Israelites half share, take one out of every fifty people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep, or other animals, and give them to the Levites who take care of the Lord's Tabernacle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the Lord had ordered Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> This was the list of plunder remaining that had been looted by the troops: 675,000 sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> 72,000 cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> 61,000 donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and 32,000 virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> This was the half share for those who had gone to fight: 337,500 sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> with a contribution for the Lord of 675,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> 36,000 cattle, with a Lord's contribution of 72,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> 30,500 donkeys, with a Lord's contribution of 61,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and 16,000 people, with a Lord's contribution of 32.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> Moses gave the contribution to Eleazar the priest as an offering to the Lord as the Lord had ordered Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The Israelites' half share left after Moses had given the half share to the troops who had gone to fight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> consisted of: 337,500 sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> 36,000 cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> 30,500 donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and 16,000 people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> Moses took from the Israelites' half one out of every fifty people and animals and gave them to the Levites who take care of the Lord's Tabernacle, as the Lord had ordered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> The army officers, the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, came to Moses</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; PSALMS 72:14"> and told him, “We, your servants, have checked the troops we command and not a single man is missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; PSALMS 116:12"> So we have brought the Lord an offering of the gold objects each man received—armlets, bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces—in order that we may be right before the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> Moses and Eleazar the priest accepted from them all the objects made out of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> The gold that the commanders of thousands and of hundreds gave as an offering to the Lord weighed in total 16,750 shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> (The men who had fought in the battle had each taken plunder for himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> Moses and Eleazar the priest accepted the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds and took it into the Tent of Meeting as a memorial offering for the Israelites in the presence of the Lord.</span>
