<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:32; Numbers 32:3; Genesis 13:10–11"> The tribes of Reuben and Gad had large numbers of livestock and saw that the land of Jazer and Gilead was a good place to rear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> So they came to Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the Israelite leaders and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> “The towns of Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Listed as Sibmah in verse 38.">a</a></sup>Nebo, and Beon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> which the Lord conquered in full view of the Israelites, are well-suited for the livestock that we your servants own.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> They continued, “Please respond to our request favorably: give us this land. Don't make us cross the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> In reply Moses asked the tribes of Gad and Reuben, “Are you expecting your brothers to go and fight while you just sit here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why discourage the Israelites from crossing into the country that the Lord has given them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> This is just what your fathers did when I sent them out from Kadesh-barnea to explore the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> After your fathers traveled up the valley of Eshcol and explored the land, they discouraged the Israelites, persuading them not to enter the country that the Lord had given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; PSALMS 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> As a result, they made the Lord very angry that day, and he swore this oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> ‘Not a single one of those I saved from Egypt who is twenty years old or older will ever see the land I promised with an oath to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, because they were not completely committed to me—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> no one except Caleb, son of Jephunneh, the Kenizzite, and Joshua, son of Nun, because they were completely committed to me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> The Lord was angry with Israel, and made them wander around in the desert for forty years, until the whole generation who had done evil in his sight had died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; PSALMS 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> Now look at you, you brood of sinners who have come to take your fathers' place to make the Lord even angrier with Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> If you give up following him, he will abandon these people in the desert once again, and their deaths will be your fault!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> Then the tribes of Gad and Reuben came to Moses and told him, “We would plan to build stone walls to keep our livestock safe and towns for our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> But we will still get ourselves ready for battle, and we will be prepared to lead the Israelites until they can safely occupy their land. In the meantime, our children will stay behind, living in the fortified towns to protect them from the local people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We won't return to our homes until every Israelite is in possession of their allotted land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> However, we won't own any land on the other side of the Jordan because we've received this land to own on this eastern side of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> Moses responded, “If this is what you will actually do, if you will get yourselves ready for battle under the Lord's direction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and if all your troops cross the Jordan with the Lord until he has driven out his enemies ahead of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> then once the country is conquered with the Lord's help then you may return, and you will have fulfilled your obligations to the Lord and to Israel. You will own this land, granted to you by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> But if you fail to do this, you will clearly be sinning against the Lord, and the consequences of your sin will catch up with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> Go ahead and build towns for your children and stone walls for your flocks, but make sure you do what you've promised!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> The tribes of Gad and Reuben promised Moses, “Sir, we your servants will do just as you have ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our wives and children, our livestock and all our animals, will all remain here in the towns of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> But we your servants are ready for battle, and all our troops will cross over to fight with the Lord's help, just as you have said, sir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> Moses gave the following instructions about them to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua, son of Nun, and to the family leaders of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses told them, “If the tribes of Gad and Reuben cross the Jordan with you, with all their troops ready for battle with the Lord's help, and the land is conquered as you advance, then give them the land of Gilead to own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they don't get themselves ready for battle and cross over with you, then they must accept their allotted land among you in the country of Canaan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The tribes of Gad and Reuben responded, “We will do just as the Lord has told us, your servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will cross over and enter the country of Canaan ready for battle with the Lord's help, so that we may have our allotted share of land on this side of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> So Moses gave to the tribes of Gad and Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh, son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon, king of the Amorites, and the kingdom of Og, king of Bashan. This land included its towns and their surrounding area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> The people of Gad rebuilt Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> Atroth-shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> Beth-nimrah, and Beth-haran as fortified towns, and they built stone walls for their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The people of Reuben rebuilt Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> as well as Nebo and Baal-meon (changing their names), and Sibmah. In fact they renamed those towns they rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> The descendants of Machir, son of Manasseh, attacked Gilead and captured it. They drove out the Amorites who were living there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> So Moses gave Gilead to the family of Machir, son of Manasseh, and they settled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> Jair, a descendant of Manasseh, attacked their villages and captured them. He named them the Villages of Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 49:11; 2 Samuel 18:18"> Nobah attacked Kenath and captured it, along with its villages. He named it Nobah after himself.</span>
