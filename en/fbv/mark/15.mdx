<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> Early the next morning, the chief priest, elders, and religious teachers—the whole governing council—came to a decision. They had Jesus bound and sent him to be handed over to Pilate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?”<br />“You said it,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> The chief priests kept on making many accusations against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> Pilate questioned him again, “Aren't you going to answer? See how many charges they're bringing against you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; PSALMS 71:7"> But Jesus didn't give any more answers, much to Pilate's surprise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now it was Pilate's custom at the Passover feast to release a prisoner to the people, whoever they requested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> One of the prisoners was a man called Barabbas who belonged to a group of rebels who had committed murder during an uprising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The crowd went to Pilate and asked him to release a prisoner following his usual custom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> “Do you want me to release to you the King of Jews?” he asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> for he realized that it was because of their jealousy of Jesus that the chief priests had handed Jesus over to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to have him release Barabbas to them instead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> “Then what should I do with the one you call the King of the Jews?” he asked them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “Crucify him!” they shouted back.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> “Why? What crime has he committed?” Pilate asked them.<br />“Crucify him!” they shouted back even louder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> Wanting to please the mob, Pilate released Barabbas to them. First he had Jesus flogged and then handed him over to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> The soldiers took him away into the Praetorium<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Roman governor's official residence.">a</a></sup>courtyard, where they called out the whole battalion.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A battalion was a group of some 600 soldiers.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They put royal purple robes on him and made a crown of thorns that they placed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> Then they saluted him, saying, “Hail King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> They repeatedly beat him around the head with a rod, spat at him, and fell on their knees before him as if in worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> After they finished mocking him, they took off the purple robes, and put his own clothes back on him. Then they led him away to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> They forced a passer-by, Simon of Cyrene, who was coming from the countryside, to carry his cross. Simon was the father of Alexander and Rufus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> They brought Jesus to the place called Golgotha, which means “the Place of the Skull.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> They offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he refused it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> Then they crucified him. They divided his clothes, and threw dice to decide who would have what.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Psalms 22:18.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> It was nine in the morning when they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> A sign with the written charge against him read: “The King of the Jews.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> They crucified two rebels with him, one on his left and one on his right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Verse 28 is not found in many of the early manuscripts.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; PSALMS 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> People passing by shouted insults at him, shaking their heads, and saying. “Aha! You who claimed you were going to destroy the Temple and rebuild it in three days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save yourself and come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> Likewise the chief priests and the religious teachers made fun of him, saying to each other, “He saved others, but he can't save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> If he really is the Messiah, the King of Israel, then why doesn't he come down from the cross so we can see and believe!” Even those who were crucified with him insulted him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> At noon darkness fell over the whole land until three in the afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> At three o'clock Jesus cried out, “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani,” which means, “My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 22:1.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> Some of those standing there heard this, and said, “He's calling for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> One man ran and filled a sponge with vinegar, put it on a stick, and tried to give it to Jesus to drink.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Psalms 69:21.">f</a></sup>“Leave him alone,” he said. “Let's see if Elijah will come to take him down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> Then Jesus groaned loudly, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> The Temple veil was ripped in two from top to bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> When the centurion standing there in front of Jesus saw how he died, he said, “This man was truly the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> Some women were watching from a distance including Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and Joses, and Salome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> They had followed Jesus and had taken care of him while he was in Galilee. Many other women who had come with him to Jerusalem were also there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> It was Friday,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “Preparation day.”">g</a></sup>the day before the Sabbath. When evening came,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea, a member of the governing council who was himself waiting for the kingdom of God, had the courage to go to Pilate and ask for Jesus' body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> Pilate was surprised that Jesus had died so soon, so he summoned the centurion and asked him if Jesus had already died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> Once he had confirmation from the centurion, Pilate gave permission for Joseph to take the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> Joseph bought a linen sheet. Then he took Jesus' body down from the cross and wrapped it in the sheet, and placed it in a tomb that had been cut out of rock. Then he rolled a heavy stone up against the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses were watching where he was laid.</span>
</div>
</div>
