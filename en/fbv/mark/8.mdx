<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:32–39; Mark 6:34–44"> Around this time there was another large crowd that had nothing to eat. Jesus called the disciples together and told them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Micah 7:19; PSALMS 103:13"> “I feel for the crowd because they have already stayed with me for three days and they have nothing to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Judges 8:4–6; 1 Samuel 30:10–12"> If I send them home without food, they'll faint on the way. Some have come from a long way away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Kings 7:2; Mark 6:52"> “Where could anybody find enough bread to feed them here in this wilderness?” answered his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 14:15–17; Matthew 15:34; Luke 9:13"> “How many loaves do you have?” he asked. “Seven,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Matthew 15:35–36; John 6:10–11"> He told the crowd to sit down on the ground. Then he took the seven loaves of bread, and gave thanks. He broke the bread and handed the pieces to his disciples to give to the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; John 21:5; Luke 24:41–42"> They had a few fish as well, so having blessed them, he said, “Take these and share them too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; PSALMS 145:16; Matthew 16:10"> They ate until they were full, and then collected up seven baskets of leftovers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There were four thousand people there. After sending them on their way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:39"> Jesus climbed into a boat with his disciples and went to the region of Dalmanutha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Matthew 12:38; Deuteronomy 6:16"> The Pharisees arrived and began to argue with him, wanting him to give them a miraculous sign from heaven, trying to get him to prove himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:39–40; Mark 7:34; Matthew 16:4"> Jesus sighed deeply, and asked, “Why do you people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “this generation.”">a</a></sup>want a sign? I tell you the truth: I will not give you a sign.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 12:36; Acts 13:45–46; PSALMS 81:12"> So he left them behind, climbed into the boat, and went back across the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:5"> But the disciples had forgotten to bring bread with them. All they had in the boat was one loaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:6–8; Matthew 16:6; Matthew 16:11–12"> “Watch out—beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and of Herod!” he warned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Luke 20:5; Matthew 16:7–8"> “He's saying that because we don't have any bread,” they concluded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Isaiah 63:17; Mark 2:8"> Jesus knew what they were saying, and said, “Why are you talking about not having any bread? Are you still not thinking and not understanding? Have you closed your minds?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Have you hardened your hearts?”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 28:26–27; Matthew 13:14–15; Jeremiah 5:21"> You've got eyes to see, haven't you? And ears to hear?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="An Old Testament expression: see Deuteronomy 29:4, Isaiah 42:20, Jeremiah 5:21, and Ezekiel 12:2.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:5–13"> Don't you remember when I shared five loaves among five thousand? How many basketfuls of leftovers did you pick up?” “Twelve,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:1–9"> “And the seven loaves divided among four thousand. How many basketfuls of leftovers did you pick up?” “Seven,” they answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Matthew 16:11–12; Mark 8:12"> “Do you still not understand?” he asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Mark 6:45; Luke 10:13"> They went to Bethsaida where some people brought a blind man to Jesus. They begged Jesus to touch him and heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:33; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:23"> Jesus took the blind man by the hand and took him outside the village. Jesus spat on the man's eyes and touched him with his hands. Then Jesus asked him, “Can you see anything?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 32:3; Isaiah 29:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–12"> The man looked around, and said, “I can see people, but they look like trees walking.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Peter 3:18; Proverbs 4:18"> So Jesus touched the man's eyes again. He could see properly—he was cured and his sight was clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:23; Mark 5:43"> Jesus sent the man home, and told him, “Don't go back through the village.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In other words, do not spread the news of what has happened.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–20; Luke 9:18–20"> Jesus and his disciples left to go to the villages of Caesarea Philippi. On the way he asked his disciples, “Who do people say I am?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:21; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> “Some say John the Baptist, some say Elijah, others say one of the prophets,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Matthew 16:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> “But who do you say I am?” he asked them. “You are the Messiah!” Peter answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:21; Matthew 16:20; Mark 9:9"> Jesus warned them not to tell anybody about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 10:33–34; John 2:19; Luke 24:26"> Then Jesus began to explain to them that the Son of man would suffer many things and be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and religious teachers. He would be killed, but three days later would rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 16:22; John 16:25; John 16:29"> Jesus explained this to them very clearly. But Peter took Jesus aside and started to reprimand him for what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 4:10; Philippians 3:19; 1 John 2:15"> Jesus turned around and looking at his disciples, reprimanded Peter. “Get away from me, Satan,” he said. “You're not thinking as God thinks, but as human beings do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:26–27; John 10:27"> Jesus called the crowd and his disciples over to him, and told them, “If you want to follow me, you must give up on yourselves, pick up your cross and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:11–13; 1 Corinthians 9:23; Luke 17:33"> If you want to save your life you will lose it, but if you lose your life because of me and the good news you will save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Luke 9:25; PSALMS 49:17"> What use is it for you to gain everything in the whole world, and lose your life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> What would you give in exchange for your life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:8–9; Romans 1:16; 1 John 2:23"> If you're ashamed to acknowledge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Ashamed to acknowledge me,” or, “do not take your stand for me.”">e</a></sup>me and what I say among this unfaithful and sinful people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “generation.”">f</a></sup>then the Son of man will be ashamed to acknowledge you when he comes with his Father's glory with the holy angels.”</span>
