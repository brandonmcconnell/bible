<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 23:56–12; John 20:1–10; Matthew 28:1–10"> Once the Sabbath had ended, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought aromatic ointments so that they could go and anoint Jesus' body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 20:1; Luke 24:1; Matthew 28:1"> Very early on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Sunday.">a</a></sup>just at sunrise, they went to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 15:46–47; Matthew 27:60–66"> They were asking each other, “Who will roll away the stone for us from the entrance to the tomb?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Luke 24:2; John 20:1"> But when they arrived and looked, they saw that the very large, heavy stone had already been rolled away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 20:11–12; Luke 24:3–5; John 20:8"> When they went into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right, wearing a long white robe. They became very frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–7; Luke 24:46"> “Don't be frightened,” he told them. “You are looking for Jesus the Nazarene, the one who was crucified. He is risen from the dead. He is not here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:28; Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10"> Look, this is the place where they laid him to rest. Now go, and tell his disciples and Peter that he's going ahead of you to Galilee. You will see him there just as he told you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Luke 24:9–11; Mark 16:5–6"> They left and ran from the tomb, shaking and confused. They said nothing to anyone because they were too scared.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Many of the early manuscripts of Mark end here. Others continue as shown.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 20:14–19; Luke 8:2; John 20:12"> When Jesus rose from the dead on the first day of the week, he appeared first of all to Mary Magdalene, from whom he'd driven out seven demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:18; Luke 24:17; John 16:6"> She went and told those who had been with him as they mourned and cried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 24:11; Mark 16:13–14; Mark 9:19"> When they heard that Jesus was alive and that she had seen him, they didn't believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:13–32; John 21:14; John 21:1"> Later Jesus appeared in a different form to two other disciples who'd left to go to the countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:25; Luke 24:33–35; John 20:8"> They returned and told the others, but they didn't believe them either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Luke 24:36–43; Hebrews 3:7–8"> After this he appeared to the eleven disciples as they were eating. He rebuked them for their lack of trust and their stubbornness, because they had not believed those who'd seen him after he had risen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 13:10; Acts 1:8"> Then he told them, “Go to the whole world, and announce the good news to everyone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “all creation.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 3:36; Romans 10:9–14"> Anyone who trusts in me and is baptized will be saved, but anyone who chooses not to trust will be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:16; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Acts 8:7"> The following signs will accompany those who trust in me: in my name they will drive out demons; they will speak new languages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 10:19; James 5:14–15; Acts 28:3–6"> they will be able to handle snakes; if they drink something poisonous they won't be harmed; they will place their hands on the sick and they will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Hebrews 1:3; Hebrews 12:2"> Then, the Lord Jesus, when he had finished speaking to them, was taken up into heaven where he sat down at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 14:3; Romans 15:19"> The disciples went out and spread the good news everywhere, and the Lord worked with them, confirming the message through miraculous signs that accompanied it.</span>
