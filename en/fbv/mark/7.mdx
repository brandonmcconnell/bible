<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> The Pharisees and religious leaders who had come down from Jerusalem to meet Jesus</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> noticed that some of his disciples ate with “unclean” (meaning unwashed) hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> (The Pharisees and all Jews don't eat until they wash their hands, following the tradition of their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> In the same way, they don't eat when they return from the market until they have had a wash. They observe many other rituals, like the washing of cups, pots, and pans.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="While hygienic, the focus was on making sure everything was ceremonially clean.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> So the Pharisees and religious leaders asked Jesus, “Why don't your disciples follow the tradition of our ancestors? They eat food<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “bread.”">b</a></sup>with unclean hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> Jesus replied, “Isaiah was right about you hypocrites when he said, ‘These people claim they honor me, but in their thinking they are far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> There's no point in their worship of me, for what they teach as doctrines are merely human rules.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 29:13.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> You disregard God's law, and instead you carefully observe human traditions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> “How cleverly you set aside God's law so you can support your traditions!” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> “Moses said, ‘Honor your father and mother,’ and ‘Whoever curses their father or mother should die.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 20:12and Exodus 21:17.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But you say that if someone tells their father or mother, ‘Anything you might have received from me is now Corban,’ (that means dedicated to God),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> then you don't permit them to do anything further for their mother or father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> By means of this tradition of yours that you pass down, you make God's word null and void. You do many other things like this.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> Jesus called the crowd to him again and told them, “Please, everyone listen to me and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> It's not what's on the outside and goes into you that makes you unclean. It's what comes out that makes you unclean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The earliest manuscripts do not have verse 16.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> Then Jesus went inside to escape the crowd, and his disciples asked him about his illustration.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> “Don't you understand it either?” he asked them. “Don't you see that what you eat doesn't make you unclean?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> It doesn't go into your mind, but into your stomach, and then passes out of the body. So all foods are ceremonially ‘clean.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some scholars believe this sentence is a later addition.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> It's what comes out of you that makes you unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> It's from the inside, from people's minds, that evil thoughts come: sexual immorality, theft, murder, adultery,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> greed, malice, deceit, indecency, envy, slander, pride, thoughtlessness—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> all these evils come from inside and defile people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> Then Jesus left and went to the region of Tyre. He didn't want anyone to know he was staying in a house there, but he couldn't keep it a secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> As soon as a woman whose little daughter had an evil spirit heard about him, she came and fell at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> The woman was Greek, born in Syrophoenicia. She begged Jesus to drive out the demon from her daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> “First let the children eat until they're full,” Jesus replied. “It's not right to take the children's food and throw it to the dogs.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or puppies, small dogs.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> “True, sir,” she said, “but even the dogs under the table eat the scraps the children leave.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> Jesus told her, “For such an answer you may go—the demon has left your daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> She went home and found the child lying on the bed, the demon gone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> Leaving the region of Tyre, Jesus passed through Sidon and then on to the Sea of Galilee and the territory of the Ten Cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> There they brought him a deaf man who also could not speak properly. They asked Jesus to touch the man with his hand and heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> After Jesus took him aside from the crowd, Jesus put his fingers in the deaf man's ears. Then he touched the man's tongue with spit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> Jesus looked up to heaven and with a sigh he said, “Ephphatha,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This is the Aramaic word meaning “cause to be open.”">h</a></sup>which means, “Open!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; PSALMS 33:9; Mark 2:12"> The man's ears were opened, his speech impediment was gone, and he began speaking properly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> Jesus gave them strict orders not to tell anyone, but the more he said this, the more they spread the news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> They were totally amazed and said, “Everything he does is marvelous. He even makes the deaf hear, and the dumb speak.”</span>
</div>
</div>
