<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> They arrived on the other side of the lake in the region of the Gerasenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> When Jesus got out of the boat a man with an evil spirit came from the graveyard to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> This man lived among the tombs, and it had been impossible to tie him up any longer, even with a chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> He had often been tied up with chains and shackles, but he simply tore the chains apart and broke the shackles into pieces. No one had the strength to subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> Day and night he was always shouting out among the tombs and in the hills nearby, cutting himself with sharp stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; PSALMS 66:3; Luke 4:41"> Seeing Jesus from a distance he ran and kneeled in front of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> In a loud voice he cried out, “What have you to do with me, Jesus, Son of Almighty God? Swear by God that you won't torture me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> For Jesus had already told the evil spirit to leave the man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> Then Jesus asked him, “What is your name?”<br />“My name is Legion, because we are many!” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> He also repeatedly pleaded with Jesus not to send them a long way away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “out of the region.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> A large herd of pigs was feeding on the hillside nearby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> The evil spirits pleaded with him, “Send us to the pigs so we can enter them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> Jesus allowed them to do this. The evil spirits left the man and went into the pigs. The whole herd, about two thousand, rushed down the steep cliff into the sea and drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> The pig-keepers ran away, and spread the news all over town and in the countryside. People came out to see what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> When they found Jesus they saw the man who had been demon-possessed sitting there, dressed, and in his right mind—the one who had the legion of evil spirits—and they became frightened!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then those who had seen what had happened to the man who had been demon-possessed and the pigs told the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> They began pleading with Jesus to leave their district.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> As Jesus climbed into the boat, the man who had been demon-possessed begged to go with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> But Jesus refused, telling him, “Go home to your own people, and tell them how much the Lord has done for you and how he has been merciful to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> So the man went on his way and began to tell the people of the Ten Cities everything Jesus had done for him, and everyone was amazed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> Jesus went back again by boat to the other side of the lake where a large crowd gathered around him at the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> A synagogue leader, a man named Jairus, came to him. When he saw Jesus, he fell at his feet</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> and pleaded with him, saying, “My little daughter is about to die. Please come and place your hands on her so she may be healed and live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> So Jesus went with him. Everybody followed, crowding and jostling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> A woman was there who had been ill from bleeding for twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; PSALMS 108:12"> She'd suffered considerably under the care of many doctors, and had spent all she had. But nothing helped her—in fact she'd grown worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> She had heard about Jesus, so she came up behind him through the crowd, and touched his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> She was telling herself, “If I can just touch his cloak, I'll be healed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; PSALMS 30:2; Mark 3:10"> The bleeding stopped immediately, and she felt her body healed from her disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> Jesus, sensing at once that power had gone out from him, turned around in the crowd and asked, “Who touched my cloak?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> “Look at the crowd jostling you. What do you mean, ‘Who touched me?’” the disciples replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But Jesus went on looking around to see who had done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> The woman, realizing what had happened to her, came and fell down before him, and told him the whole truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> “My daughter, your trust in me has healed you. Go in peace. You have been completely cured of your disease,” Jesus told her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While he was still speaking some people came from the home of the synagogue leader. “Your daughter is dead,” they said. “You don't need to bother the Teacher any longer.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But Jesus paid no attention to what they said. He told the synagogue leader, “Don't be afraid, just trust in me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“In me,” implied.">b</a></sup>”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> He wouldn't let anyone go with him except Peter, James, and James' brother John.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> When they arrived at the synagogue leader's house, Jesus saw all the commotion, with people crying and wailing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> He went in and asked them, “Why are you making such a commotion with all this crying? The little girl isn't dead, she's just sleeping.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> They laughed scornfully at him.<br />Jesus made everyone leave. Then he went into the room where the little girl was lying, taking with him the child's father and mother, and the three disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> Taking the little girl's hand he said, “Talitha koum,” which means, “Little girl, get up!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> The little girl, who was twelve years old, got up immediately and began walking around. They were completely astonished at what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> He gave them strict instructions not to let anyone know, and he told them to give the little girl something to eat.</span>
</div>
