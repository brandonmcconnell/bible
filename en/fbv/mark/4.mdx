<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> Jesus began teaching beside the Sea again. So many people came to listen to him that he climbed into a boat and sat in it on the water while the crowd listened from the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> He illustrated his teachings using many stories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> “Listen,” he said. “A sower went out to sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> Now as he was scattering the seeds, some fell on the path, and birds came and ate them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> Other seeds fell on stony ground where there wasn't much earth. In the shallow soil the plants started growing quickly, but because the soil wasn't deep</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> they were scorched when the sun came up. Since they didn't have any real roots, they soon withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> Other seeds fell among thorns. The thorns grew up and choked the sprouting seeds, so they produced nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> Other seeds fell on good soil where they sprouted and grew. They produced a harvest of some thirty, some sixty, and some a hundred times what was planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> If you have ears to hear, listen to what I'm saying.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> When he was by himself, his twelve disciples and the others who were with him asked him what the illustrations meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> He told them, “The mystery of God's kingdom has been given to you to understand. But outsiders only have the stories,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> so that even though they see, they don't really ‘see,’ and even though they hear, they don't understand, otherwise they might turn to me and be forgiven.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 6:9-10.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> “Don't you understand this story?” Jesus asked them. “If you can't, how are you going to understand all the other stories?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> The sower sows the word.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Word—or “message,” the Word of God that Jesus came to bring. (See also John 1:1.)">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> The seeds on the path where the word is sown illustrate those who hear the message, but then Satan immediately comes along and takes away the word that's been sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> Likewise the seeds on the rocky ground illustrate those who hear the word, and happily accept it right away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> But since they have no real roots, they only last for a while until trouble or persecution comes, and then they quickly fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> Those sown among the thorns illustrate those who hear the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> but worries of this world, the temptation of wealth, and other distractions choke the growth of the word, and it becomes unproductive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> But the seeds sown on good soil illustrate those who hear the word, accept it, and are productive—producing thirty, sixty, and a hundred times what was originally sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> Who puts a lamp under a bucket, or beneath a bed?” Jesus asked them. “No, you put a lamp up on a lamp-stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> Everything that is hidden will be revealed, and everything that is secret will be brought out into the open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If you have ears to hear, listen to what I'm saying!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> Pay attention to what you're hearing,” he told them, “for you will be given according to how much you want to receive, measure for measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> More will be given to those who already have understanding, but those who don't want to know will have what little understanding they have taken from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> God's kingdom is like a man sowing seed in the ground,” Jesus said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> “He goes to bed and gets up, day after day, but the man has no knowledge of how the seeds sprout and grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> The earth produces a harvest by itself. First a shoot appears, then the heads of grain, and then the heads of grain ripen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> When the grain is ripe, the farmer reaps it with a sickle, because the harvest is ready.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Possibly a reference to Joel 3:13.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> What can we compare God's kingdom to? What illustration shall we use?” he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> “It's like a mustard seed, the tiniest seed of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> But when it's sown it grows into a plant that's larger than other plants. It has branches big enough that birds can roost in its shade.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> Jesus used many of these illustrated stories when he spoke to the people so they would understand as much as they could.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> In fact, when he spoke publicly, he only used stories; however in private he explained everything to his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> Later that day, in the evening, he said to his disciples, “Let's go across to the other side of the Sea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> Leaving the crowd behind, the disciples went with Jesus, just as he was, and got into a boat. Other boats went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> Soon a terrible storm started blowing, and waves crashed against the boat, filling it with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> Jesus was asleep in the stern, resting his head on a cushion. The disciples woke him up, shouting at him, “Teacher, don't you care that we're about to drown?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 107:29; PSALMS 89:9; PSALMS 65:7"> Jesus woke up. He told the wind to die down and told the waves, “Be quiet! Be still.” The wind stopped, and the water became completely calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> “Why are you so frightened?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word used is for cowards.">d</a></sup>Haven't you learned to trust me?” he asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; PSALMS 89:7"> They were stunned and terrified.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Although the aspect of fear is usually brought out in translations, the text indicates that they had been frightened previously. Now they were amazed, in awe at what had happened, though no doubt still terrified.">e</a></sup>They asked each other, “Who is this? Even the wind and the waves obey him!”</span>
