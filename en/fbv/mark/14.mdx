<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Exodus 12:6–20; John 13:1"> It was now two days before Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the religious leaders were trying to find a surreptitious way to arrest Jesus and have him killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:40; Proverbs 21:30; John 12:19"> “But not during Passover,” they said to themselves, “otherwise the people may riot.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8; John 11:2"> Meanwhile Jesus was in Bethany, eating a meal at Simon the leper's home. A woman came in with an alabaster jar of very expensive pure nard perfume.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Nard: an essential oil derived from the roots of the spikenard plant native to China and India.">a</a></sup>She broke the jar open and poured the perfume on Jesus' head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> Some of those who were there became annoyed and said, “Why waste this perfume?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:29; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 10:10"> It could've been sold for a year's wages<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “300 denarii.”">b</a></sup>and the money given to the poor.” They were angry with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 10:24; Hebrews 13:21"> But Jesus replied, “Leave her alone! Why are you criticizing her for doing something beautiful to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Matthew 26:11; James 2:14–16"> You'll always have the poor with you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 15:11.">c</a></sup>and you can help them whenever you want. But you won't always have me with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; John 12:7; Mark 15:42–1"> She did what she could: she anointed my body in anticipation of my burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 26:12–13; Mark 16:15; Matthew 24:14"> I tell you the truth: wherever the good news is spread in the world, people will remember what she did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 26:14–16; John 13:2"> Then Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve disciples, went to the chief priests and arranged to betray Jesus to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Hosea 7:3; Jude 1:11"> When they heard this, they were delighted, and promised to pay him. So Judas began to look for an opportunity to betray Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18; Matthew 26:17–19"> On the first day of the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the time when the Passover lamb is sacrificed, Jesus' disciples asked him, “Where do you want us to go and prepare the Passover meal for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:10–13; Matthew 26:18–19; Luke 19:30–33"> He sent two of his disciples, telling them, “Go into the city and there you'll meet a man carrying a water pot. Follow him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Mark 11:3; John 13:13"> and when he goes into a house, ask the owner where I and my disciples can celebrate the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:13; PSALMS 110:3; Proverbs 21:1–2"> He will take you to a large upstairs room that is furnished and ready. You can make preparations for us there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 16:4; Luke 22:13; Luke 22:35"> The disciples went into the city, and found things just as he'd described them. They prepared the Passover meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:14; John 13:21–26; Matthew 26:20–24"> In the evening Jesus went there with the twelve disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; John 6:47; John 6:70"> While they were sitting eating, Jesus said, “I tell you the truth: one of you is going to betray me; one who is eating with me now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:21–23; Matthew 26:22; John 13:22"> They were shocked, and they each asked, “It's not me, is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 13:26; Matthew 26:23; John 6:71"> “It's one of the Twelve, one of you sharing this food with me,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:24–25; Isaiah 53:1–12; John 19:28"> “The Son of man will die, just as the Scriptures predicted. But how terrible it will be for the man who betrays the Son of man! It would be better for that man if he'd never been born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–29; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> As they were eating, Jesus picked up some bread. He blessed it, and gave it to them. “Take it. This is my body,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:22; Matthew 26:27; 1 Corinthians 10:16"> Then he picked up the cup. He blessed it, and gave it to them. They all drank from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Exodus 24:8; Revelation 7:9–17"> “This is my blood,” he told them, “the agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “covenant,” or “pledge.”">d</a></sup>that's being poured out for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; PSALMS 104:15; Luke 22:16–18"> I tell you the truth, I won't drink of the fruit of the vine until the day I drink it fresh in God's kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:30; Luke 22:39; Acts 16:25"> After they had sung a psalm, they left for the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Matthew 26:31"> “All of you will abandon me,” Jesus told them. “For as the Scriptures say, ‘I will attack the shepherd, and the sheep will be completely scattered.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Zechariah 13:7.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 28:16; Mark 16:7; Matthew 28:7"> But after I have risen from the dead, I will go before you to Galilee.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 26:33–35; Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38"> “I won't abandon you even if everyone else does,” Peter replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:66–72; Genesis 1:5"> Jesus answered him, “I tell you the truth that today, this very night, before the rooster crows twice, you will deny that you even know me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; John 13:37; Proverbs 16:18"> But Peter was totally adamant, saying, “Even if I have to die with you, I will never deny you.” All of them said the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 14:36; Mark 14:39; PSALMS 18:5–6"> They arrived at a place called Gethsemane,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Meaning “olive press.”">f</a></sup>where Jesus told his disciples, “Sit here while I go and pray.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:44; Hebrews 5:7; Mark 5:37"> He took Peter, James, and John with him. He began to be very disturbed and troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 12:27; Mark 13:35–37; 1 Peter 5:8"> Jesus told them, “My agony is so painful it feels like I'm dying. Please, remain here and stay awake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 14:41; Deuteronomy 9:18; Hebrews 5:7"> He went a little farther on and then fell to the ground. He prayed, asking to be spared the time<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “hour.”">g</a></sup>that was coming, if it were possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Romans 8:15–16; John 5:30"> “Abba, Father! You can do everything,” he said. “Please, take this cup of suffering away from me. Yet it's not what I want, but what you want.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 26:40; Mark 14:29–31; Hebrews 12:3"> Then Jesus returned and found the disciples asleep. “Simon, are you sleeping?” he asked Peter. “Couldn't you stay awake for just an hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 26:41; Matthew 24:42; Luke 21:36"> Stay awake, and pray so you won't fall into temptation. The spirit is willing, but the body is weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 26:42–44; 2 Corinthians 12:8; Matthew 6:7"> He left them once more, and prayed, saying the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 9:33–34; Romans 3:19; Genesis 44:16"> Then he returned, and again he found them sleeping because they couldn't keep their eyes open.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “their eyes were burdened down.”">h</a></sup>They didn't know what to say!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:35; Mark 9:31; John 17:1"> He returned a third time, and asked them, “Are you still asleep? Are you still resting? Well that's enough,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The meaning of the Greek word here is unclear. It usually means “paid in full.”">i</a></sup>because the time has come! Look, the Son of man is about to be betrayed into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:46; John 18:1–2"> Get up! Let's go! See, here comes my betrayer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 18:3–11; Luke 22:47–53; Matthew 26:47–56"> Just as he was saying this, Judas—one of the twelve disciples—arrived with a mob carrying swords and clubs, sent by the chief priests, religious leaders, and elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 26:48–50; Proverbs 27:6; PSALMS 55:20–21"> Now the betrayer had arranged a sign with them: “He's the one that I kiss. Arrest him, and take him away under guard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 23:7–10; Malachi 1:6; Mark 12:14"> Judas went right up to Jesus. “Rabbi,” he said, and kissed him affectionately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 18:12; Lamentations 4:20; Acts 2:23"> So they grabbed hold of Jesus and arrested him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> But one of those standing there pulled out his sword and struck the high priest's servant, cutting off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 22:52–53; 1 Samuel 26:18; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> “Am I some kind of rebel that you have come to arrest me with swords and clubs?” Jesus asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:20; John 8:2; Luke 22:37"> “I was there with you, teaching in the Temple every day. Why didn't you arrest me then? But this is happening to fulfill the Scriptures.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 14:27; John 16:32; PSALMS 88:18"> Then all Jesus' disciples deserted him and ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> (One of his followers was a young man who was wearing only a linen garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 13:14–16; Genesis 39:12; Job 2:4"> They seized hold of him, but he ran off naked, leaving the garment behind.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> They took Jesus to the high priest's house where all the chief priests, elders, and religious teachers had gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> Peter followed him at a distance, and went into the courtyard of the high priest's house. He sat down with the guards and warmed himself by the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> Inside the chief priests and the whole governing council<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, “Sanhedrin.”">j</a></sup>were trying to find some evidence to have Jesus put to death, but they couldn't find anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Many were giving false testimony against him, but their statements didn't agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> Some of them got up to speak falsely against Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> “We heard him say, ‘I will destroy this Temple that human hands built, and in three days I will build another without hands.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> But even so their testimony didn't agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> Then the high priest stood up in front of the council, and asked Jesus, “Have you nothing to say in response to these charges made against you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> But Jesus remained silent and didn't answer. So the high priest asked again, “Are you the Messiah, the Son of the Blessed One?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; PSALMS 110:1; Hebrews 1:3"> “I am,” Jesus replied, “and you will see the Son of man sitting on the right of the Mighty One, and coming with the clouds of heaven.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Psalms 110:1and Daniel 7:13.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Isaiah 36:22–1; Numbers 14:6"> The high priest tore his clothes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="In those times an expression of great anguish.">l</a></sup>and asked, “Why do we need any more witnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> You have heard the blasphemy! What's your reaction?” They all found him guilty and condemned him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 26:67–68; Acts 23:2; Isaiah 50:6"> Then some of them began to spit on him. They blindfolded him, hit him with their fists, and said, “Why don't you prophesy then, you ‘Prophet’!” The guards took him away and beat him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:69–75; John 18:25–27"> Meanwhile Peter was down below in the courtyard. One of the high priest's servant-girls passed by,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Mark 10:47; Matthew 2:23"> and seeing Peter warming himself, looked straight at him and said, “You were with Jesus of Nazareth too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> But he denied it. “I don't know what you're talking about or what you mean,” he replied. Then he went out to the forecourt, and a rooster crowed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="“And a rooster crowed.” This phrase is not found in some of the early manuscripts.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Luke 22:58; Mark 14:38; Galatians 6:1"> Seeing him there, the servant girl repeated to those standing around, “This man is one of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 22:59–60; Matthew 26:73–74; Acts 2:7"> Once more Peter denied it. A little while later they said to Peter again, “You're definitely one of them because you're a Galilean too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; 1 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Kings 10:32"> Peter began calling down curses on himself and he swore, “I don't know this man you're talking about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Mark 14:30; Mark 14:68; Ezekiel 7:16"> Immediately the rooster crowed the second time. Then Peter remembered what Jesus had said to him: “Before the rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times.” When he realized what he'd done, he burst into tears.</span>
