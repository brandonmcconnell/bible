<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:47; Luke 5:18; Luke 18:35–38"> A few days later Jesus returned home to Capernaum, and news spread that he was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Mark 1:45; PSALMS 40:9"> So many people crowded inside the house that it was packed, even outside the door, as Jesus explained the message<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “the word,” here used for the first time to mean the message of God's good news.">a</a></sup>to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:18–26; Matthew 9:1–8; Matthew 4:24"> Four men had brought a man who was paralyzed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 5:19; Deuteronomy 22:8"> but they could not get near Jesus because of the crowds. So they went up on the roof and took it apart. After they had made an opening above Jesus, they lowered down the mat with the paralyzed man lying on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; James 5:15; Mark 2:9–10"> When Jesus saw the trust these men had, Jesus said to the paralyzed man, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:21–22; Mark 8:17; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> Some of the religious teachers sitting there thought to themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Luke 5:21; Micah 7:18"> “Why is he talking like this? He is blaspheming! Who can forgive sins? Only God can do that!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 139:2; Acts 8:22; Luke 5:22"> Jesus knew right away what they were thinking. He said to them, “Why are you thinking like this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:22–25; Matthew 9:5"> What's easier: to say to the paralyzed man, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or ‘Get up, pick up your mat, and walk’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Matthew 9:6–8; John 5:20–27"> But to convince you that the Son of man has the right to forgive sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 1:41; John 5:8–10; John 6:63"> I say to you (the paralyzed man), ‘Get up, pick up your mat, and go home.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 9:33; Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:16"> He stood up, picked up his mat, and walked out in front of everyone there. They were all amazed, and praised God, saying, “We've never ever seen anything like this!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 4:1; Proverbs 1:20–22; Luke 19:48"> Jesus went out beside the sea once more and taught the crowds that came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:27–32; Luke 6:15; Matthew 9:9–13"> As he walked along, he saw Levi son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax collector's booth.<br />“Follow me,” Jesus told him. Levi got up and followed Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:1; Luke 5:29–30; Matthew 9:10–11"> That evening Jesus ate dinner at Levi's house. Many tax collectors and “sinners”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Sinners” here refers to those who were not seen as so strict in their keeping of the religious law as the religious teachers and the Pharisees.">b</a></sup>joined Jesus and his disciples for the meal, for there were many of these people that followed Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 19:7; Luke 18:11"> When the religious leaders of the Pharisees saw Jesus eating with such people, they asked Jesus' disciples, “Why does he eat with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:12–13; Luke 15:7; Luke 5:31–32"> When Jesus heard this, he told them, “It's not healthy people who need a doctor, but those who are sick. I haven't come to invite those who live right, but those who don't—the sinners.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:12; Matthew 6:18; Matthew 9:14–17"> Now John's disciples and the Pharisees were fasting.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Fasting: choosing not to eat on certain days for religious reasons.">c</a></sup>Some of them came to Jesus, and asked him, “Why is it that John's disciples and the Pharisees fast, but your disciples don't?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 25:1–10; Genesis 29:22; Judges 14:10–11"> “Do wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them?” Jesus asked them. “No. While the bridegroom's with them, they can't fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Revelation 21:9; Isaiah 54:5"> But the day is coming when the bridegroom will be taken from them, and then they'll fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:16; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Isaiah 57:16"> No one puts a patch that's not shrunk on old clothes. Otherwise the new piece will shrink away from the old, and make the tear worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:17; PSALMS 119:83; Luke 5:37–38"> No one puts new wine in old wineskins. Otherwise the wine will burst the wineskins, and both the wine and wineskins will be wasted. No. You put new wine in new wineskins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–8; Deuteronomy 23:24–25; Luke 6:1–5"> One Sabbath day as Jesus was walking through the grain fields, his disciples started picking heads of grain as they walked along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:10; Hebrews 12:3; Matthew 12:2"> The Pharisees asked Jesus, “Look, why are they doing what is not permitted on the Sabbath?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 10:26; Matthew 22:31"> “Haven't you ever read what David did when he and his men were hungry and in need?” Jesus asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; Leviticus 24:5–9; 1 Samuel 21:1"> “He went into God's house when Abiathar was high priest, and ate the consecrated bread which no one except the priests are permitted to eat, and gave it to his men too.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 2:16; Luke 6:9; Deuteronomy 5:14"> “The Sabbath was made for your benefit, not for you to benefit the Sabbath,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 12:8; Revelation 1:10; John 5:17"> “So the Son of man is Lord even of the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
</div>
