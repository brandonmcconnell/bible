<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Mark 4:2; John 11:7"> Jesus left Capernaum and went to the region of Judea and Transjordan. Once again people flocked to see him, and he was teaching them like he always did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 11:53–54; John 8:6"> Some Pharisees came to see him. They tried to test him by asking the question, “Is divorce legal?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 4:21; Luke 10:25"> “What did Moses tell you to do?” he asked in reply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31–32; Isaiah 50:1"> “Moses allowed a man to write a certificate of divorce and send the woman away,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 24:1.">a</a></sup>they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 19:8; Nehemiah 9:26; Acts 7:51"> Then Jesus told them, “Moses only wrote down this rule for you because of your hard-hearted attitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2; Genesis 1:1"> However, in the beginning, from creation, God made male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5–6; Ephesians 5:31"> That's why a man leaves his father and mother and is joined in marriage to his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Genesis 2:24; 1 Corinthians 6:16"> and the two become one body. They are no longer two but one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Genesis 2:24.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 7:1–3; 1 Chronicles 7:10–13"> Let no one separate what God has joined together.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 9:33; Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> When they were back indoors, the disciples began asking him about this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:31–32; Matthew 19:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11"> “Any man who divorces his wife and marries again commits adultery against her,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:11; 1 Corinthians 7:13"> “And if the wife divorces her husband and marries again she commits adultery.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–15; Luke 18:15–17; Exodus 10:9–11"> Some people brought their children to Jesus so that he could bless them, but the disciples told them off and tried to keep the children away from Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 19:14; Matthew 18:10; Matthew 18:4"> But when Jesus saw what they were doing, he became very upset and told them, “Let the children come to me! Don't stop them, for the kingdom of God belongs to those who are like these children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:17; John 3:3–6"> I tell you the truth, anyone who doesn't welcome the kingdom of God like a child won't enter it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 9:36; Isaiah 40:11; John 21:15–17"> He hugged the children, placed his hands on them, and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 19:16–30; Luke 18:18–30; Romans 2:7"> As Jesus set out on his journey,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="To Jerusalem, see 11:1.">c</a></sup>a man came running over and kneeled down before him. “Good teacher, what should I do to make sure I have eternal life?” he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:19; Matthew 19:17; Romans 3:12"> “Why do you call me good?” Jesus asked him. “No one is good, only God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Romans 13:9; James 2:11"> You know the commandments: you shall not murder, you shall not commit adultery, you shall not steal, you shall not give false testimony, you shall not cheat, honor your father and mother…”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 20:12-16or Deuteronomy 5:16-20.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; 2 Timothy 3:5; Philippians 3:6"> “Teacher,” the man replied, “I've obeyed all these commandments since I was small.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; Revelation 2:4; Luke 18:22"> Jesus looked at him with love and said, “You're only missing one thing. Go and sell everything you own, give the money to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come and follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15"> At this, the man's face fell, and he left feeling very sad, for he was very wealthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26; Luke 18:24; Mark 3:5"> Jesus looked around, and said to his disciples, “It's only with difficulty that wealthy people enter the kingdom of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:1–3; Proverbs 11:28; PSALMS 52:7"> The disciples were shocked at this. But Jesus went on, “My friends, it is difficult to enter God's kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 19:24–25; Luke 18:25; Matthew 23:24"> It's easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for the rich to enter God's kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 16:31; 2 Corinthians 11:23; Romans 10:9–13"> The disciples were even more confused. “Then who on earth can be saved?” they asked one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Job 42:2"> Looking right at them, Jesus replied, “From a human point of view, it's impossible—but not with God. Everything is possible with God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 18:28–30; Luke 14:33"> Peter spoke up, “We've left everything to follow you…”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:23; Matthew 5:10–11; Mark 8:35"> “I tell you the truth,” Jesus replied, “anyone who has left behind their home or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or lands because of me, and for the sake of the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 18:30; Philippians 3:8; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> will receive in return in due course a hundred times as many homes and brothers and sisters and children and lands—as well as persecution. In the world to come they will receive eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Luke 13:30; Matthew 20:16"> However, many of the first will be last, and the last first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Luke 18:31–34; Luke 9:51"> They continued on their way to Jerusalem, with Jesus walking on ahead. The disciples were apprehensive and the other followers were afraid. So Jesus took the disciples aside and began to explain to them what was about to happen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Matthew 20:17–19; Mark 8:31"> “We're going to Jerusalem,” he told them, “and the Son of man will be betrayed to the chief priests and religious teachers. They will condemn him to death and hand him over to the foreigners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In this context meaning the Romans.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Matthew 16:21; Mark 14:65"> They will mock him, spit on him, flog him, and kill him. But three days later he will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:20–28; 2 Samuel 14:4–11; 1 Kings 2:20"> James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came to see him. “Teacher,” they said, “We want you to do for us whatever we ask you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 15:7; 1 Kings 3:5–15; Mark 10:51"> “So what do you want me to do for you?” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:26"> “When you're victorious and sit on your throne,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Implied.">f</a></sup>make sure we sit beside you, one on the right, the other on the left,” they told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:50; Matthew 20:21–22; PSALMS 75:8"> “You don't know what you're asking,” replied Jesus. “Can you drink the cup I drink? Can you be baptized with the baptism of pain I will suffer?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 12:2; Revelation 1:9; John 17:14"> “Yes, we can,” they replied. “You will drink the cup I drink, and you will be baptized with the same baptism as me,” Jesus told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:23; Matthew 25:34; John 17:24"> “But it's not for me to grant who should sit on my right or on my left. These places are reserved for those for whom they have been prepared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 20:24; Proverbs 13:10; Romans 12:10"> When the other ten disciples heard about this, they started getting upset with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; Luke 22:25; Matthew 20:25"> Jesus called the disciples together and told them, “You realize that those who claim to rule over nations oppress their people. The rulers act like tyrants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 23:8–12; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:35"> But for you it's not like this. Anyone of you who wants to be a ruler must be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and anyone who wants to be first among you must be the slave of all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Philippians 2:5–8; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> For even the Son of man did not come to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 18:35–1; Matthew 20:29–34; John 9:8"> Jesus and his disciples passed through Jericho, and as they were leaving town, accompanied by a large crowd, Bartimaeus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="For his non-Jewish readers Mark also adds that Bartimaeus means the “son of Timaeus.”">g</a></sup>a blind beggar, was sitting at the roadside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Matthew 9:27; Revelation 22:16"> When he heard it was Jesus of Nazareth, he started shouting out, “Jesus, son of David, please have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; Mark 7:26–29; Hebrews 5:7"> Lots of people told him to be quiet, but that only made him shout even more, “Jesus, son of David, please have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 18:40; Matthew 20:32–34; John 11:28"> Jesus stopped, and said, “Tell him to come here.” So they called him over, telling him, “Good news!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “take courage.”">h</a></sup>Get up. He's calling for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Hebrews 12:1"> Bartimaeus jumped up, threw off his coat, and rushed over to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 18:41–43; Mark 10:36; Matthew 7:7"> “What do you want me to do for you?” Jesus asked him. “Teacher,” he said to Jesus, “I want to see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Acts 26:18; Mark 8:25"> “You can go. Your trust in me has healed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “saved.” The word can mean both “save” and “heal.”">i</a></sup>you.” Immediately Bartimaeus could see and he followed Jesus as he went on his way.</span>
