<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> As they approached Jerusalem, near to Bethphage and Bethany, beside the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two of his disciples on ahead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> He told them, “Go into the village ahead of you, and as soon as you enter, you'll find a colt tied up that no one has ever ridden before. Untie it and bring it here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; PSALMS 110:3"> If anyone asks you what you are doing, tell them, ‘The Lord needs it and will send it back soon.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> So they set off, and found a colt tied to a door, out on the street, and they untied it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Some of the people standing around asked, “What are you doing, untying that colt”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The disciples replied just as Jesus told them to, and the people let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> They brought the colt to Jesus, put their cloaks on it, and Jesus sat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> Many people spread their cloaks on the road, while others laid down leafy branches that they'd cut in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> Those leading in the front and those that were following were all shouting, “Hosanna!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Hosanna!” This is simply a transliteration of the Aramaic word meaning “Save!”">a</a></sup>Bless the one coming in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> Bless the kingdom of our father David that is coming! Hosanna in the highest!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:26.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> Jesus entered Jerusalem and went in to the Temple. He looked around him, observing everything, and then, since it was getting late, he returned to Bethany with the twelve disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> The next day, as they left Bethany, Jesus was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> From some distance away he saw a fig tree with leaves, so he went over to it to see if it had any fruit. But when he got there, he found it had only leaves, because it was not the season for figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> He said to the tree, “May no one eat fruit from you ever again.” His disciples heard his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> They arrived back in Jerusalem, and Jesus went into the Temple. He started driving out the people who were buying and selling in the Temple. He overturned the tables of the money-changers and the chairs of the people selling doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He stopped anyone carrying things through the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> He explained to them, “Doesn't Scripture say, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 56:7.">c</a></sup>But you have turned it into a den of thieves!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Jeremiah 7:11.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> The chief priests and religious teachers heard what had happened, and tried to find a way to kill Jesus. But they were afraid of him, for everyone was so impressed by his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> When evening came Jesus and his disciples left the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> The following morning as they returned, they saw the fig tree, withered from the roots up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> Peter remembered what Jesus had done, and said to him, “Rabbi, look, the fig tree that you cursed has withered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> “Trust in God,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> “Believe me when I say to you that if you told this mountain, ‘Get out of here and be thrown into the sea,’ and you don't doubt in your heart, but are convinced about what you're asking, then it will happen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> I'm telling you that whatever you pray for, whatever you ask, trust that you've received it, and it's yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> But when you're praying, if you have something against someone, forgive them, so that your Father in heaven may also forgive you your sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The earliest manuscripts do not contain verse 26, which is inserted from Matthew 6:15.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; PSALMS 2:1–5"> They returned to Jerusalem, and as he was walking in the Temple, the chief priests, religious teachers, and the leaders approached him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> “By what authority are you doing all this?” they demanded. “Who gave you the right?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> “Let me ask you a question,” Jesus told them. “You answer me, and I'll tell you by whose authority I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> John's baptism—did that come from heaven, or from people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> They discussed it among themselves. They said, “If we say it's from heaven, he'll reply, ‘So why didn't you believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> But if we say, it's of human origin, well…” They were afraid of the crowd, because everyone believed that John was a true prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> So they answered Jesus, “We don't know.” “Then I'm not telling you by whose authority I do these things,” replied Jesus.</span>
