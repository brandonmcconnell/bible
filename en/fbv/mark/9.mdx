<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; John 14:8–9; Revelation 22:3–4"> Peter spoke up and said, “Rabbi, it's great for us to be here! We should make three shelters—one each for you, Moses, and Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:15–19; Mark 16:5–8"> (He really didn't know what to say because they were all so frightened!)</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Matthew 3:17; Mark 1:11"> Then a cloud covered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “overshadowed.”">a</a></sup>them, and a voice came from the cloud, saying, “This is my Son, the one I love. Listen to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 10:16; Acts 8:39–40; Luke 24:31"> Then, all of a sudden, as they were watching, there was nobody. Only Jesus was with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Matthew 17:9–13; Mark 5:43"> As they came down the mountain Jesus instructed them not to tell anyone what they'd seen until the Son of man had risen from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Luke 24:7–8; Luke 18:33–34"> They kept this to themselves, but they did argue with each other over what it meant to rise from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:14"> “Why do the religious teachers state that Elijah has to come first?” they asked him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> “It's true that Elijah comes first to prepare everything,” Jesus replied. “But why then does it say in Scripture that the Son of man has to suffer so much and be treated with contempt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 14:3–11; Matthew 17:12–13; Matthew 11:14"> However, I tell you that Elijah has come, and they abused him in whatever way they wanted, just as Scripture said they would.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> When they returned to the other disciples, they saw them surrounded by a large crowd and some religious teachers arguing with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> As soon as the crowd saw Jesus they were in total awe, and ran to greet him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> “What are you arguing with them about?” Jesus asked them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> One of the people in the crowd answered, “Rabbi, I brought my son to you. He has an evil spirit that prevents him speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> Whenever he has a seizure it throws him down, and he foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and he becomes rigid. I asked your disciples to drive it out of him, but they couldn't do it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> “You unbelieving people!” Jesus responded. “How long must I remain here with you? How long do I have to put up with you? Bring him over here to me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> So they brought him to Jesus. When the evil spirit saw Jesus it immediately sent the boy into convulsions and threw him on the ground, where he rolled around, foaming at the mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> “How long has he had this?” Jesus asked the boy's father.<br />“Since he was small,” the father replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> “Often it throws him into the fire to burn him to death, or throws him into water to drown him. Please have mercy on us and help, if you can.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> “Why do you say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied. Jesus was asking why the man said to him, “if you can,” which suggested doubt as to what Jesus could do.">b</a></sup>‘if you can?’” replied Jesus. “Whoever trusts has every power!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> “I do trust in you,” the man shouted out right away. “Help me not to distrust you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> Jesus, seeing that the crowd was closing in,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “running together.” This word is only used once in the New Testament.">c</a></sup>commanded the evil spirit, “Spirit that causes deafness and dumbness, I order you to come out of him and never return.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> The spirit screamed and threw the boy into severe convulsions. Then the spirit came out of the boy and left him for dead—to the extent that many of the people said, “He's dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> But Jesus took the boy by the hand and helped him up, and he got to his feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> Later, when Jesus had gone indoors, his disciples asked him in private, “Why couldn't we drive the evil spirit out?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> “This kind can't be driven out except by prayer,” Jesus told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:43–45; Mark 6:31–32"> They left and passed through Galilee. Jesus didn't want anyone to know where he was</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 9:12; Luke 24:44–46"> because he was teaching his disciples.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In other words, he wanted to spend time teaching his disciples.">d</a></sup>“The Son of man will be betrayed to human authorities,” he told them. “They will kill him, but three days later he will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 2:50; Luke 9:45; Luke 18:34"> They didn't understand what he meant and were too afraid to ask him about it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Hebrews 4:13; Luke 9:46–48"> They arrived at Capernaum, and once they were inside the house where they were staying, Jesus asked them, “What were you talking about on the way?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Matthew 20:21–24; Matthew 18:1–5"> But they didn't say anything because they had been arguing over who was the most important.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:42–45; Luke 22:26"> Jesus sat down and called the twelve disciples together. “If anyone wants to be first, he has to be the very last, the servant of everyone else,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 10:16; Matthew 19:14–15; Matthew 18:2"> He took a small child and had the child stand right in the middle of them. Then he picked up and hugged the child, and told them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–5; Luke 10:16; Matthew 10:40–42"> “Whoever welcomes a child like this in my name, welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me doesn't welcome me but the one who sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 9:49–50; Numbers 11:26–29; Luke 11:19"> John said to Jesus, “Rabbi, we saw someone driving out demons in your name. We tried to stop him because he wasn't one of us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Matthew 13:28–29"> “Don't stop him,” Jesus replied. “For no one who is doing miracles in my name can curse me at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23"> Anyone who is not against us is for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Matthew 25:40; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> Anyone who gives a cup of water to you in my name, because you belong to Christ, won't lose their reward, believe me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> But if anyone leads one of these little ones who trust in me into sin, it would be better for them if they were thrown into the sea with a large millstone tied around their neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Matthew 18:8–9; Matthew 25:41"> If your hand leads you to sin, cut it off! It's better to enter eternal life as a cripple than to go with both hands into Gehenna,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The word used here is literally, “Gehenna,” sometimes translated “hell” or “hellfire.” Gehenna was the place outside of Jerusalem where fires were lit to dispose of rubbish by burning. “Hell” is a concept that is derived from Nordic and Anglo-Saxon mythology and does not adequately convey the meaning here. Also verses 45 and 47. See note on Matthew 5:22.">e</a></sup>into the fire that can't be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Verse 44 is not found in the early manuscripts.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:22; Mark 9:43"> If your foot causes you to sin, cut it off! It's better to enter eternal life lame than to be thrown into Gehenna still having two feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Verse 46 is not found in the early manuscripts.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 18:9; Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:22"> If your eye causes you to sin, gouge it out! It's better to enter the kingdom of God with just one eye than to be thrown into Gehenna still having both eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Mark 9:43–45; Matthew 25:41"> where the worm doesn't die and the fire never goes out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 43:24"> Everybody will be ‘salted’ by fire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Both salt and fire are symbols of purification.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Romans 12:18; Matthew 5:13"> Salt is good, but if it loses its taste, how could you make it salty again? You need to be like salt, and live in peace with one another.”</span>
</div>
