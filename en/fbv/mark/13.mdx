<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:1–51; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 10:19"> As Jesus was leaving the Temple, one of his disciples said to him, “Teacher, look at these massive stones and magnificent buildings!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “what sort of stones and what sort of buildings.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:7–8"> “You see all these great buildings?” Jesus replied. “Not one stone will be left on top of another. Everything will be torn down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> As Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives overlooking the Temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him in private,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> “Tell us: When this will happen? What's the sign that all this is about to be fulfilled?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Because the disciples' question merges the two events, Jesus' answer combines aspects of the destruction of the Temple and the final end-time.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> Jesus began telling them, “Make sure no one deceives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Many will come in my name claiming, ‘I am the Christ.’ They will deceive many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> Don't be troubled when you hear of wars nearby and wars far away. These things must happen but this is not the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; PSALMS 48:6"> Nation will fight against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places, and famines too. These are the beginnings of the world's birth pains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> Watch out for yourselves! They will hand you over to the courts to be tried. You will be beaten in synagogues. Because of me you will have to stand before governors and kings, and you will be witnesses to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> The good news must first be announced in every nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> When they come to arrest you and put you on trial, don't worry about what to say. Just say what you're told at that time, because it's not you speaking, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> Brother will betray brother to death, and a father will betray his child. Children will turn against their parents and have them condemned to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> You will be hated by everyone because of me, but whoever endures until the end will be saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> But when you see the ‘idolatry that causes devastation’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “the idolatry that causes desolation.” See Daniel 9:27, Daniel 11:31, and Daniel 12:11.">c</a></sup>standing where it shouldn't be (let the reader understand), then those who are in Judea should run to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> Those who are on the roof—don't go back inside the house to get anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Those who are out in the fields—don't go home to get a coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> How hard it will be for those who are pregnant or nursing at that time!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pray that this won't happen during the winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For these will be days of trouble like never before since the beginning of God's creation until now, and they won't ever come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> If God doesn't cut short these days, no one will survive. However, for the sake of those God has chosen, he has cut them short.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> So if anyone tells you, ‘Look, here is the Messiah,’ or ‘Look, there he is,’ don't believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false Messiahs and false prophets will appear, and they will perform miraculous signs and wonders to deceive the chosen of God, if that were possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> Watch out! I've told you everything before it happens.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> This is what will happen after those troubles: ‘The sun will become dark, the moon won't shine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> the stars will fall from the sky, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Isaiah 13:10.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> Then they will see the Son of man as he comes on the clouds, possessing great power and glory.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Daniel 7:13-14.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> He will send out the angels, and gather together all his chosen ones from wherever they are,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “from the four winds.”">f</a></sup>from the most distant part of the earth to the farthest point of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> Learn a lesson from the fig tree. When its branches grow soft and send out leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> In the same way, when you see these things happening, you know that it's near—right outside the door!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> I tell you the truth, this generation won't come to an end until all these things have happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will come to an end, but my teachings will not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> No one knows the day or hour when this will happen—not even the angels in heaven, not even the Son; only the Father knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Keep watch! Stay awake! For you don't know when this will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> It's like a man who went away on a journey. He left his house, and gave each of his servants the authority to do what he told them. He told the doorkeeper to stay awake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> So keep watch, because you don't know when the owner of the house is coming back. It may be in the evening, in the middle of the night, before dawn, or in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> You don't want to be caught sleeping if he returns unexpectedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> What I'm telling you, I'm telling everyone: Watch!”</span>
</div>
</div>
