<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> Jesus went to the synagogue again. A man was there who had a crippled hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> Some of those there were watching Jesus to see if he would heal the man on the Sabbath, because they were looking for a reason to accuse him of breaking the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> Jesus told the man with the crippled hand, “Come and stand here in front of everyone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> “Is it lawful to do good on the Sabbath, or to do evil? Should you save life, or should you kill?” he asked them. But they didn't say a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> He looked around at them in exasperation, very upset by their hard-hearted attitude. Then he told the man, “Hold out your hand.” The man held out his hand, and it was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> The Pharisees left, and immediately began plotting with Herod's party as to how they might kill Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> Meanwhile Jesus returned to the Sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Of Galilee.">a</a></sup>and a large crowd followed him. They were from Galilee, Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> Jerusalem, Idumea, Transjordan, and from the regions of Tyre and Sidon. So many people came to see him because they'd heard about all he was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> Jesus told his disciples to have a small boat ready in case the crowd crushed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> because he had healed so many that all the sick people kept on pressing towards him so they could touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> Whenever the evil spirits saw him they would fall down in front of him and shout out, “You are the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> But he commanded them not to reveal who he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> Then Jesus went away to the hill country. He called those he wanted to be with him, and they came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> He chose twelve to be with him, and called them apostles. They were to be with him, and he would send them out to announce the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and with the authority to drive out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> These are the twelve he chose: Simon (whom he called Peter),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> James the son of Zebedee and his brother John (who he called Boanerges, meaning “sons of thunder”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Revolutionary,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Luke 6:15.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot (who betrayed him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> Jesus went home, but such a large crowd gathered again that he and his disciples didn't even have time to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> When Jesus' family<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “those who were close to him.”">c</a></sup>heard about it, they came to take him away, saying, “He's lost his senses!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> But the religious leaders from Jerusalem said, “He's possessed by Beelzebub! It's by the ruler of demons that he's driving out demons!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> But Jesus called them over to him. Using illustrations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “parables,” meaning analogies, comparisons, illustrations.">d</a></sup>he asked them, “How can Satan throw out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> A kingdom fighting itself can't continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; PSALMS 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> A household that's divided is doomed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If Satan is divided and fights against himself, he won't last long—he'll soon come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> Clearly, if someone breaks into the house of a strong man and tries to take his things, he won't get far in his theft unless he ties up the strong man first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> “I tell you the truth: all sins and blasphemies can be forgiven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but if people blaspheme by rejecting the Holy Spirit they can never be forgiven, because they are guilty of an eternal sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> (Jesus said this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Implied.">e</a></sup>because they said, “He has an evil spirit.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> Then Jesus' mother and brothers arrived. They waited outside and sent him a message, asking him to come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The crowd that was sitting around him told him, “Your mother and brothers are outside asking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> “Who is my mother? Who are my brothers?” he responded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> Looking around at all those sitting there in a circle, he said, “Here is my mother! Here are my brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> Whoever does what God wants, they are my brother, and sister, and mother.”</span>
</div>
