<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Songs 8:11–12"> Then Jesus began to speak to them using illustrated stories.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See on 3:23.">a</a></sup>“Once there was a man who planted a vineyard. He put a fence around it, dug a pit for a winepress, and built a watchtower. Then he leased it to some farmers, and left on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> When harvest-time came, he sent one of his servants to the tenant farmers to collect some of the grapes from the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they grabbed hold of him, beat him up, and sent him away with nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the man sent another servant. They hit him over the head and abused him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> He sent another servant, and this one they killed. He sent many other servants, and they beat some of them and killed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> In the end the only one left was his son whom he loved, and eventually he sent him, thinking ‘They will respect my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But the farmers said to themselves, ‘Here's the owner's heir—if we kill him, we can get what he would have inherited!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> So they took him and killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> Now what is the owner of the vineyard going to do? He will come and kill those farmers, and then he will lease the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Haven't you even read this Scripture: ‘The stone rejected by the builders has become the chief cornerstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> This is from the Lord, and it's marvelous to see!’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoted from Psalms 118:22-23.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> The Jewish leaders tried to have him arrested because they realized that the illustration was directed at them, but they were afraid of the crowd. So they left him alone and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Later they sent some Pharisees with some of Herod's supporters to Jesus in an attempt to catch him out by what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> They arrived and said, “Teacher, we know you are a truthful person and you don't look for approval, because you don't care about status or position.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “You have no concern for anyone because you do not look at the face of men.” However, this literal translation could make it seem that Jesus was uncaring and indifferent.">c</a></sup>Instead you teach God's way in accordance with the truth. So is it right to pay tribute to Caesar or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Should we pay up, or should we refuse?” Jesus, realizing how hypocritical they were, asked them, “Why are you trying to catch me out? Bring me a coin to look at.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> They gave him a coin. “Whose is this image, and whose inscription?” Jesus asked them. “Caesar's,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> “Then give back to Caesar what belongs to him, and give back to God what belongs to him,” Jesus told them. They were amazed at his reply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then the Sadducees, who deny the resurrection, came and asked a question:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses instructed us that if a man dies, leaving his widow childless, then his brother should marry his wife, and have children by her on his behalf.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 25:5.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> Once there were seven brothers. The first one got married, and then died without having children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The second married his widow, and then died, childless. The third did the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In fact all seven died without having children. In the end the woman died too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, whose wife will she be, because she was the wife of all seven brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus told them, “This proves you're mistaken, and that you don't know the Scriptures or the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> When the dead rise, they don't marry, and aren't given in marriage. They're like the angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> But concerning the resurrection, haven't you read in Moses' writings the story of the burning bush, where God spoke to Moses and told him, ‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Exodus 3:2-6.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He's not the God of the dead, but of the living. You are completely mistaken!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> One of the religious teachers came and heard them arguing. He recognized that Jesus had given them a good answer. So he asked him, “Which is the most important commandment of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus replied, “The first commandment is, ‘Hear, Israel, the Lord our God is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your spirit, with all your mind, and with all your strength.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:4.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> The second is ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 19:18.">g</a></sup>There's no more important commandment than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> “That's right, Teacher,” the man replied. “It's true as you said that God is one, and there is no other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> We are to love him with all our heart, all our understanding, and all our strength, and we are to love our neighbor as ourselves. This is far more important than offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> Jesus saw that he gave a thoughtful answer, and said, “You're not far from the kingdom of God.” After this no one was brave enough to ask him any more questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> While Jesus was teaching in the Temple, he asked, “Why do the religious teachers state that Christ is the son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> David himself declared, inspired by the Holy Spirit, that the Lord said to my Lord, ‘Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies under your feet.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 110:1.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> Since David himself calls him Lord, how can he be David's son?” The large crowd listened to what Jesus said with great delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> Jesus continued to teach them, saying, “Beware of religious leaders! They love to walk around in long robes, to be greeted respectfully in the marketplaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> They love to have the most important seats in the synagogues, and the best places at banquets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> They cheat widows out of what they own,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “devour widow's houses.”">i</a></sup>and cover up the kind of people they really are with long-winded prayers. They will receive severe condemnation in the judgment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> Jesus sat down opposite the treasury collection box, watching people tossing in coins. Many of the rich were extravagantly throwing in a lot of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then a poor widow came along and put in just two small coins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, two lepta,, of little value.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> He called his disciples together and told them, “I tell you the truth: this poor widow has put in more than all the rest together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> All of them gave from their wealth what they had, but she gave from her poverty what she didn't have. She put in all she had to live on.”</span>
