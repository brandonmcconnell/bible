<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> I hope you can put up with a little more foolishness from me—well you already do put up with me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> I agonize over you with a divine kind of jealousy, for I promised you to a single husband—Christ—so that I could present you as a pure virgin to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> I worry that in some way, just as the serpent deceived Eve with his devious cunning, that you might be led astray in your thinking from your sincere and pure commitment to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> If anyone comes and tells you about a different Jesus to the one we shared with you, you easily go along with them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, they are too tolerant of others who come with a very different understanding of the good news.">a</a></sup>accepting a different spirit to the one you received, and a different kind of good news to the one you believed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> I don't believe I'm inferior to these “super-apostles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> Even though I may not be skilled in giving speeches, I do know what I'm talking about. We have made this absolutely clear to you in every way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Was it wrong of me to humble myself so you could be elevated, since I shared the good news with you at no charge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I robbed as it were other churches, taking pay from them so I could work for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> When I was there with you and needed something, I wasn't a burden to anyone because the believers who came from Macedonia took care of my needs. I was determined never to be a burden to you and I never shall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> This is as certain as the truth of Christ that is in me: nobody in all Achaia will stop me boasting about this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> And why? Because I don't love you? God knows that I do!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> I'll continue to do what I've always done, so as to remove any opportunity for those who want to boast that their work is the same as ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> These people are false apostles, dishonest workers, who pretend<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “transform themselves into.” Also in verse 14.">b</a></sup>to be apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Don't be surprised at this for even Satan himself pretends to be an angel of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> So it's no wonder then if those who serve him pretend to be agents of good. But their final end will be in accordance with what they've done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> Let me say it again: Please don't think I'm being foolish. However, even if you do, accept me as someone who is foolish, and let me also boast a little.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Paul suggests that he also should be allowed to boast as the false apostles were boasting.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> What I'm saying is not as the Lord would say it—all this foolish boasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> But since many others are boasting in the way the world does, let me boast too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> (You're happy to put up with fools, since you are so wise!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Clearly a sarcastic or ironic comment; also what follows…">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> You put up with people who make you slaves, who take what you have, who exploit you, who arrogantly put you down, who hit you in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> I'm so sorry that we were too weak to do anything like that! But whatever people dare to boast about, I dare to do too. (Here I'm talking like a fool again.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> Are they Hebrews? Me too. Are they Israelites? Me too. Are they descendants of Abraham? Me too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Are they servants of Christ? (I know I'll sound like I'm crazy, talking like this.) But I have done so much more. I've worked harder, been imprisoned more often, whipped more times than I can count, faced death time and again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> Five times I received from the Jews the forty lashes less one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> Three times I was beaten with rods, once I was stoned, three times I was shipwrecked. Once I spent twenty-four hours adrift on the ocean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> During my many journeys I have faced the dangers of crossing rivers, robber gangs, attacks from my own countrymen, as well as from foreigners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">e</a></sup>I have faced danger in cities, in the deserts, and on the sea. I have faced the danger of people who pretend to be Christians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> I have faced hard labor and struggles, many sleepless nights, hunger and thirst, often going without food, cold, without enough clothing to keep warm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> Besides all this, I face the daily concerns of dealing with all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is weak, and I don't feel weak too? Who is led into sin, and I don't burn up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> If I have to boast, I will boast about how weak I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The God and Father of the Lord Jesus—may he be praised forever—knows I am not lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> While I was in Damascus, the governor under King Aretas had the city guarded in order to capture me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> But I was lowered in a basket from a window in the city wall, and so I escaped from him.</span>
</div>
</div>
