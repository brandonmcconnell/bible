<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 8:4–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–10"> I really don't need to write to you about this offering for God's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:10; 2 Corinthians 8:8; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> I know how keen you are to help—I was boasting about this to those in Macedonia that you in Achaia have been ready for over a year, and your enthusiasm has encouraged many of them to give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> But I'm sending these brothers so that my boasting about you regarding this won't be proved wrong, and that you're prepared, just as I said you would be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> This is just in case some Macedonians should come with me and find you unprepared. We, not to mention you, would be really embarrassed if this project failed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> That's why I decided I should ask these brothers to visit you in advance, and complete the arrangements to collect this offering, so that it would be ready as a gift, and not as something demanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Luke 6:38; Proverbs 22:9"> I want to remind you of this: If you only sow a little, you'll only reap a little; if you sow plenty, you'll reap plenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> Everybody should give as they've already decided—not reluctantly, or because they have to, for God loves those who give with a cheerful spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Proverbs 22:8.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> God is able to graciously provide you with everything, so that you will always have all you need—with plenty to help others too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 112:9; PSALMS 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> As Scripture says, “He gives generously to the poor; his generosity is everlasting.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 112:9. In the context of the Psalm this refers to a generous man.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> God, who provides seed to the sower and gives bread for food, will provide and multiply your “seed” and increase your harvest of generosity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> You will be made rich in every way so that you can always be very generous, and your generosity will lead others to be grateful to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> When you serve in this way, not only are the needs of God's people met, but also many will give grateful thanks to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> By giving this offering you show your true nature, and those who receive it will thank God for your obedience, since it shows your commitment to the good news of Christ and your generosity in giving to them and everyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> They will pray for you with much fondness because of God's abundant grace working through you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Ephesians 5:20; John 3:16"> Thank God for his gift that is far greater than words can express!</span>
</div>
