<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; Hebrews 12:15; 1 Peter 4:10"> As workers together with God we also plead with you not to make your acceptance of God's grace worthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:8; Hebrews 4:7; Hebrews 3:13"> Just as God said, “At the right time I heard you, and on the day of salvation I rescued you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 49:8.">a</a></sup>Believe me: now is the right time! Now is the day of salvation!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> We don't put anything in anyone's way that would trip them up, making sure the work we do won't be criticized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> Instead we try to demonstrate we are good servants of God in every way we can. With a great deal of patience we put up with all kinds of trouble, hardship, and distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> We have been beaten up, thrown into prison, and attacked by mobs. We have been worked to the bone, suffering sleepless nights and hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> We demonstrate who we are by living blameless lives in the knowledge of God, with a great deal of patience, being kind and filled with the Holy Spirit, showing sincere love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> We speak truthfully,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “word of truth,” meaning the gospel.">b</a></sup>living in the power of God. Our weapons consist of what is true and right; we attack with our right hand and we defend with our left.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “weapons of the right and left.” This may mean the use of a sword with the right hand and a shield with the left.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> We continue whether we are honored or dishonored, whether we are cursed or praised. People call us frauds but we tell the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> We are disregarded, even though we are well-known; given up for dead, but we're still alive; lashed, but not killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> Seen as miserable, we are always rejoicing; as poor, but we make many rich; as destitute, but we own everything!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; PSALMS 119:32"> I've been speaking bluntly, my Corinthian friends, loving you with a big heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> We haven't kept our love from you, but you have kept your love from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> Please respond in the same way, as if you were my children, and be big-hearted!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Don't join together with people who don't believe—for what connection does goodness have with wickedness? What do light and darkness have in common?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> How could Christ and the devil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Belial.”">d</a></sup>ever agree? How could a believer and an unbeliever share together?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> What compromise could the Temple of God make with idols? For we are a temple of the living God, just as God said: “I will live in them and walk among them. I will be their God, and they will be my people.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 26:12and Ezekiel 37:27.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> “So leave them, and separate yourselves from them, says the Lord. Don't touch anything that is unclean, and I will accept you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referencing Isaiah 52:11and Ezekiel 20:34, Ezekiel 20:41.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> “I will be like a Father to you, and you will be sons and daughters to me, says the Lord Almighty.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Referencing 2 Samuel 7:14or 1 Chronicles 17:13.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
