<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11; 2 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Are we starting to speak well of ourselves again? Or do we need to have some letter of recommendation for you, or from you, as some people do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 1:8"> You are our letter, written in our experience with you, that everybody reads and knows about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Jeremiah 31:33; Hebrews 8:10"> You demonstrate that you are a letter from Christ, delivered by us; not written with ink but with the Spirit of the living God; not written on stone slabs but in our living experience as human beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 3:12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> We have total confidence before God through Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Philippians 4:13; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Not that we would consider ourselves able to do this on our own—it's God who gives us this capacity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 6:63; Romans 7:6; Jeremiah 31:31"> He also gave us the ability to be ministers of a new agreement,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">a</a></sup>based not on the letter of the law, but on the spirit. The letter of the law kills, but the spirit gives life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:9–11; Romans 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> However, the old way of relating to God, written in letters carved in stone, ended in death, even though it came with God's glory—so much so that the Israelites couldn't even bear to look at Moses' face because it shone so brightly, even though the glory was fading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 3:14"> If that was so, shouldn't the new way of relating to God in the Spirit come with even greater glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:21–22; Hebrews 12:18–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> If the old way that condemns us has glory, the new way that makes us right with God has so much more glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; Acts 26:13"> For the old that was once glorious has no glory at all in comparison with the incredible glory of the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:20–21; Hebrews 8:13; Hebrews 7:21–25"> If the old that is fading away had glory, the new that continues has so much more glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; Acts 4:13; Philippians 1:20"> Since we have such a confident hope, we are truly bold!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; Hebrews 10:1–9; Ephesians 2:14–15"> We don't have to be like Moses, who had to put on a veil to cover his face so the Israelites wouldn't be dazzled by the glory, even though it was fading away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 26:18; Romans 11:25"> In spite of that, they had a hard, stubborn attitude. For right up until today when the old agreement is read, the same “veil”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, they are not able to see clearly.">b</a></sup>remains. Only through Christ can it be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:27–29"> Even today, whenever the books of Moses are read, a veil covers their minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> But when they turn and accept the Lord, the veil is removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:13; John 8:32"> Now the Lord is the Spirit, and wherever the Spirit of the Lord is, there's freedom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:2"> So all of us, with our faces unveiled, see and reflect the glory of the Lord as in a mirror. We are being transformed into the same mirror image, whose glory grows brighter and brighter. This is what the Lord the Spirit does.</span>
