<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 19:25–26; 1 John 3:2; 2 Peter 1:13–14"> We know that when this earthly “tent”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The symbolism here is of the earthly body as a tent, and the heavenly body as a house—both of which “clothe” the person.">a</a></sup>we live in is taken down, we have a house prepared by God, not made by human hands. It is eternal and is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 8:23; 1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:3–4"> We sigh with longing for this, wanting so badly to be clothed with this new heavenly home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:7–11; Revelation 16:15; Revelation 3:18"> When we put on this clothing we won't be seen naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:2; Isaiah 25:8"> While we are in this “tent” we sigh, being weighed down by this life. It's not so much that we want to take off the clothing of this life but that we look forward to what we shall be clothed with, so that what is mortal may be overwhelmed by life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 1:13–14; Romans 8:23"> It's God himself who prepared us for all this, and who provided the Spirit as a guarantee to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; Philippians 3:20–21; Hebrews 11:13"> So we remain confident, knowing that while we are at home here in our physical bodies, we are away from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:1–27; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> (For we live by trusting in God, not by seeing him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 2 Corinthians 5:6; John 14:3"> As I say, we are confident, wanting to be away from the body so we can be at home with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Peter 3:14; Romans 14:18"> That's why our goal, whether home here in our bodies or not, is to make sure we please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 22:12; Ephesians 6:8"> For all of us must appear before Christ's seat of judgment. Each of us will receive what we deserve for what we have done in this life, whether it is good or bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:23; Hebrews 10:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–26"> Knowing how we are in awe of the Lord, we try to convince others. It's clear to God what we are, and I hope that it's clear to your minds too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:1; 2 Corinthians 1:14; Galatians 6:12–14"> We are not trying to speak well of ourselves again, just trying to give you the opportunity to be proud of us, so you can answer those who are proud of outward show and not what they are inside.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “in the heart.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16–17; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6"> If we are “crazy people,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This may have been a criticism of Paul and his companions by those in Corinth.">c</a></sup>it's for God. If we make good sense, it's for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 14:7–9; Matthew 10:37–38"> Christ's love urges us on, because we're absolutely sure that he died for everyone, and so everyone died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:7–9; 1 Peter 4:2–4; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> Christ died for everyone so that they shouldn't live any longer for themselves, but for him who died and rose again for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Philippians 3:7–8; John 6:63"> From now on we don't look at anyone from a human point of view. Even though we once viewed Christ this way, we don't do so any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Ephesians 4:22–24; Isaiah 43:18–19"> That's why anyone who is in Christ is a new being—what was old is gone, the new has come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20; Romans 5:10–11"> God did all this by changing us from enemies into friends through Christ. God gave us this same work of changing his enemies into his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; 1 John 2:1–2"> For God was in Christ, bringing the world back from hostility to friendship with him, not counting anyone's sins against them, and giving us this message to change his enemies into his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Luke 14:23; Malachi 2:7"> So we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God is pleading through us: “Please, won't you come back and be God's friend?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–24; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 5:19"> God made Jesus, who never personally sinned, experience the consequences of sin so that we could have a character that is good and right just as God is good and right.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “we could become right just as God is right,” meaning morally right, not in the sense of being correct.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
