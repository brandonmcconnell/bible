<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> Brothers and sisters, we want to let you know about the grace of God shown to the Macedonian churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> Even though they have suffered terrible trouble, they are overflowing with happiness; and even though they are very poor, they are also overflowing in their generosity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> I can confirm that they gave what they could, in fact even more than that! By their own choice</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> they kept on pleading with us to have a part in this privilege of sharing in this ministry to God's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> They didn't just do what we hoped they would do, but they gave themselves to the Lord, and then to us, as God wanted them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> So we have encouraged Titus—since it was he who started this work with you—to return and complete this gracious ministry of yours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> Since you have an abundance of everything—your trust in God, your eloquence, your spiritual knowledge, your complete dedication, and in your love for us—make sure that your abundance also extends to this gracious ministry of giving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I'm not ordering you to do this, but to prove how sincere your love is in comparison with the dedication of others.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Presumably meaning the other churches such as those in Macedonia.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ. Even though he was rich, he became poor for you, so that through his poverty you could become rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> This is my advice: it would be good if you finished what you started. Last year you were not only the first to give but the first to want to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> Now finish what you planned to do. Be as keen to finish as you were to plan, and give as you are able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> If the willingness is there, it's fine to give what you have, not what you don't have!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> This is not to make things easier for others and harder for you, but simply to be fair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> At the moment you have more than enough and can meet their needs, and in turn when they have more than enough they can meet your needs. In that way everyone is treated fairly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> As Scripture says, “The one who had much didn't have any excess, and the one who didn't have much didn't have too little.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This refers to the gathering of manna in Exodus 16:8.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> Thanks be to God that he gave to Titus the same devotion for you that I have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> Though he agreed to do what we told him, he's coming to see you because he really wants to, and had already decided to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> We're also sending with him a brother who is praised by all the churches for his work in spreading the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> He was also appointed by the churches to go with us as we deliver this gracious offering we're carrying. We do this to honor the Lord and to show our eagerness to help others.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1 Corinthians 16:3-4.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> We want to guard against anyone criticizing us about how we use this gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> We're concerned to do things the right way, not only in the eyes of the Lord, but also in the eyes of everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> We're also sending with them another brother who has proved to be reliable on many occasions, and who is eager to help. He is now even more eager to help because of the great confidence he has in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> If anyone asks about Titus, he is my companion. He works with me on your behalf. The other brothers are representatives from the churches and an honor to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> So please welcome them before all the churches and show them your love, proving how rightly proud we are of you.</span>
</div>
