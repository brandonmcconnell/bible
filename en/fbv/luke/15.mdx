<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> Tax collectors and other “sinners” often used to come and listen to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> As a result the Pharisees and the religious teachers complained, “This man welcomes sinners, and eats with them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So Jesus told them this story as an illustration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> “Imagine a man who had a hundred sheep lost one of them. Wouldn't he leave the ninety-nine in the open pasture, and search for the one that's lost until he finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> When he finds it, he joyfully puts it on his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> Once he gets home, he calls his friends and neighbors together, saying, ‘Come and celebrate with me! I've found my lost sheep!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I tell you that there's more joy in heaven over a sinner that repents than over ninety-nine good people who don't need to repent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> Imagine a woman who has ten silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, drachma.">a</a></sup>coins, and loses one of them. Wouldn't she light a lamp and sweep the house, carefully searching until she finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> When she finds it, she calls her friends and neighbors together, saying, ‘Come and celebrate with me! I've found the silver coin that I lost.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> I tell you there is joy in the presence of God's angels over one sinner that repents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> Once there was a man who had two sons,” Jesus explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> “The younger one told his father, ‘Father, give me my inheritance now.’ So the man divided his property between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> A few days later the younger son packed up what he had and left for a distant country. Here he wasted all his money living a reckless life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> After he'd spent everything, the country was hit by a severe famine and he was starving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> So he went and took a job with one of the farmers there who sent him to his fields to feed pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> He was so hungry that he would have eaten even the pig food,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “seed pods the pigs were eating.”">b</a></sup>but no one gave him anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> When he came to his senses, he said to himself, ‘All of my father's workers have more than enough to eat—why am I dying from hunger here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I'm going home to my father! I'll tell him: Father, I've sinned against heaven and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; PSALMS 84:10"> I'm no longer worthy to be called your son. Please treat me as one of your hired workers.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 103:10–13; PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 86:15"> So he left and went home to his father.<br />Even though he was still far away in the distance, his father saw him coming, and his heart went out to his son. The father ran to his son, hugging and kissing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> The son said to him, ‘Father, I've sinned against heaven and against you. I'm no longer worthy to be called your son.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> But the father told his servants, ‘Quick—bring the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; PSALMS 63:5"> Bring the calf we've been fattening and kill it. Let's have a feast to celebrate</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> because this is my son who was dead, but who has returned alive; he was lost but now he's found.’ So they started celebrating.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> Now the older son was working out in the fields. As he walked towards the house, he heard music and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So he called one of the servants and asked what was going on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> ‘Your brother is back,’ he replied, ‘and your father has killed the fattened calf, because he's come home safe and sound.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> The brother became angry. He refused to go in. So his father came out to plead with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> He told his father, ‘Look, all these years I've served you, and never disobeyed you, but you never once gave me even a young goat so I could have a party with my friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> Now this son of yours comes back, having spent your money on prostitutes, and you kill the fattened calf for him!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> ‘Son,’ the father replied, ‘you are always here with me. Everything I have is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> But we should be happy and celebrate! This is your brother who was dead, but who has returned alive; he was lost but now he's found!’”</span>
</div>
</div>
