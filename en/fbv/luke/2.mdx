<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> It was the time when Caesar Augustus issued a decree that there should be a census of everyone in the Roman Empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This was the first census under governor Quirinius of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So everybody went to their own city to be registered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Joseph was descended from King David, so he left Nazareth in Galilee to go to Bethlehem, the city of David, in Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> He went to register there, together with Mary, who was pledged in marriage to him and expecting a baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> While they were there, the time came for her to have her baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> She gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him up in strips of cloth and laid him in a manger because the inn had no rooms left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; PSALMS 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> Nearby some shepherds were spending the night out in the fields, keeping watch over their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> An angel of the Lord appeared to them, and the glory of the Lord shone all around them. They were scared out of their wits!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> “Don't be afraid!” the angel told them. “I'm here to bring you good news that will make the all the people really glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> The Savior has been born to you today, here in David's city. He is the Messiah, the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> You will recognize him by this sign: you will find the child wrapped in strips of cloth and lying in a manger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; PSALMS 148:2"> Suddenly many heavenly beings appeared, praising God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> “Glory to the God of heaven, and on earth peace to those who please him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> After the angels had left them and returned to heaven, the shepherds said to one another, “Let's go to Bethlehem and see what's happened concerning these things the Lord has told us about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> They hurried there and found Mary, Joseph, and the baby, who was lying in the manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 66:16; Luke 8:39; PSALMS 71:17–18"> After they'd seen for themselves, they spread the news of what had happened and what they had been told about this baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> All those who heard about it were amazed at what the shepherds told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary was careful to remember all the things that had happened and often thought about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> The shepherds went back to watching their flocks, glorifying and thanking God for all that they'd heard and seen, for it was just as they'd been told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> After eight days, the time came for the baby to be circumcised, and he was named Jesus. This was the name given to him by the angel before he had even been conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> When the time of their purification according to the law of Moses was finished, Joseph and Mary took him to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> as the law of the Lord states, “Every firstborn son must be dedicated to the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 13:2.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> There they made a sacrifice, as God's law also states, of “a pair of turtle doves or two young pigeons.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 12:8.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> Living in Jerusalem at that time was a man called Simeon. Simeon did what was good and was very devout. He was waiting expectantly for the hope of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> The Holy Spirit had shown him that he would not die before he saw the Lord's Messiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Christ.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> Following the Spirit's leading, he went to the Temple. When Jesus' parents brought in the little boy to be dedicated as required by the Law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> Simeon took Jesus in his arms, thanked God, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> “Lord and Master, now you can let your servant die in peace as you promised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> because I have seen for myself your salvation</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; PSALMS 98:2–3"> which you have prepared for everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> He is a light that will show you to the nations, the glory of your people Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> Jesus' father and mother were shocked at what Simeon said about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> Then Simeon blessed them, and said to Jesus' mother Mary, “This child is destined to cause many in Israel to fall and many to rise. He is a sign from God that many will reject,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> revealing what they really think. For you it will feel like a sword piercing right through you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> Anna the prophetess also lived in Jerusalem. She was the daughter of Phanuel of the tribe of Asher, and she was very old. She had been married for seven years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; PSALMS 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and then she had been widowed. She was eighty-four years old. She spent her time at the Temple in worship, fasting and praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> She came up to them right at that moment, and began praising God. She spoke about Jesus to all those who were looking forward to the time when God would set Jerusalem free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> Once they had completed everything God's law required, they returned to their home town of Nazareth in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> The child grew strong, and was very wise. God's blessing was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> Jesus' parents traveled to Jerusalem every year for the Passover festival.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> When Jesus was twelve years old, they went to the festival as they always did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> When the festival had finished and it was time to return home, the boy Jesus remained in Jerusalem, but his parents didn't realize this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; PSALMS 42:4"> They assumed he was with all the others traveling home. A day passed before they started looking for him among their friends and relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When they couldn't find him they returned to Jerusalem to look for him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> It was three days before they found him in the Temple. He was sitting among the religious teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; PSALMS 119:99"> All who heard him speak were surprised by his understanding and his answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> His parents were totally confused when they saw what he was doing. His mother asked him, “Son, why have you treated us like this? Your father and I—we've been worried sick! We've been looking everywhere for you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> “Why is it that you were looking for me?” Jesus replied. “Didn't you know I should be here in my Father's house?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> But they didn't understand what he meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> Then he went back with them to Nazareth, and did what he was told. His mother kept a careful note of everything that happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> Jesus grew steadily wiser and stronger, favored both by God and by the people.</span>
</div>
