<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Lamentations 2:20; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> It was around this time that some people told Jesus about Pilate's killing of some Galileans while they were offering sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:2; Acts 28:4; Job 22:5–16"> “Do you think that these Galileans were worse sinners than any other Galileans because they suffered like this?” Jesus asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38–40"> “No, I tell you. But unless you repent, you will all perish as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:15; Job 1:19; John 9:11"> What about those eighteen people that were killed when the tower in Siloam fell on them? Do you think they were the worst people in the whole of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Luke 13:3; Isaiah 28:10–13"> No, I tell you. But unless you repent, you will all perish as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> Then he told them this story as an illustration. “Once there was man who had a fig tree planted in his vineyard. He came to look for fruit on the tree, but he didn't find any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> So he told the gardener, ‘Look, for three years I've been coming to look for fruit on this fig tree and I haven't found any. Chop it down! Why should it be taking up space?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Joel 2:17; Romans 10:1"> ‘Master,’ the man replied, ‘please leave it alone for just one more year. I'll dig the soil around it and put down some fertilizer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> If it produces fruit, then that's fine. If not, then chop it down.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> One Sabbath Jesus was teaching in a synagogue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> and a woman was there who had been crippled by an evil spirit for eighteen years. She was bent over and could not stand straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> When Jesus saw her, he called her over and told her, “You're freed of your sickness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> Then he placed his hands on her, and immediately she straightened up, and she praised God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> However, the synagogue leader was upset that Jesus had healed on the Sabbath. He said to the crowd, “There are six days for work. Come and be healed on those days, not on the Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> But the Lord answered him, “You hypocrites! Doesn't every one of you untie your ox or donkey from the stall and take it to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> Why shouldn't this woman, this daughter of Abraham whom Satan has kept tied up for eighteen years, be untied and set free this Sabbath day?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; PSALMS 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> What he said shamed all his opponents, but everyone in the crowd was delighted by all the wonderful things he was doing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:30–34; Luke 17:21; Matthew 13:31–32"> Then Jesus asked, “So what is the kingdom of God like? What should I compare it to?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:12; Daniel 4:21; Daniel 2:44–45"> It's like a mustard seed that a man planted in his garden. It grew into a tree, and the birds came and nested in its branches.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He asked again, “What shall I compare the kingdom of God to?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:23–24"> It's like yeast that a woman took and mixed into three measures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="About 3 gallons or 13 liters.">a</a></sup>of flour which made the whole batch rise.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> Jesus went around the towns and villages, teaching on his way to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> Someone asked him, “Lord, will only a few be saved?”<br />Jesus replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> “Try very hard to enter the narrow doorway, because I tell you that many will try to go in, and won't succeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> Once the house owner gets up and closes the door, you'll be standing outside knocking on the door, saying, ‘Master, please open the door for us.’ But he'll reply, ‘I don't know you or where you're from.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Then you'll say, ‘But we ate and drank with you, and you taught in our streets!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> He'll reply, ‘I tell you, I don't know you or where you're from. Get away from me, all of you who don't do what's good!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> There will be crying and gnashing of teeth when you see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and all the prophets in the kingdom of God but you are thrown out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> People will come from the east and the west, the north and the south, and they will sit down to eat in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> For the last shall be first, and the first shall be last.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9–11; PSALMS 11:1–2; Matthew 14:1"> At that moment some Pharisees came to Jesus and told him, “You should leave here. Herod wants to kill you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 5:9; John 17:4–5"> Jesus replied, “Go tell that fox that I will go on driving out demons and healing people for today and tomorrow, and on the third day I'll achieve what I came to do.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “achieve my goal.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Matthew 21:11; Acts 13:27"> Well anyway I must continue on my way for today and tomorrow, and the day after. For it wouldn't be right for a prophet to die outside of Jerusalem!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 23:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Acts 7:52"> Oh Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you kill prophets and stone those who are sent to you! How often I have wanted to gather all your children together just like a hen does with her chicks under her wings, but you refused!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 118:26; Mark 11:9–10; Matthew 21:9"> Look, your house is left desolate, and I tell you that you won't see me again until you say, ‘Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:26.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
