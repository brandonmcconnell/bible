<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="LUK" data-book-slug="luke" data-book-name="Luke" data-chapter="11" data-verses="54">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 19:14; Luke 22:39–45; Romans 8:26–27"> Once Jesus was praying in a certain place. When he finished, one of his disciples asked him, “Lord, please teach us to pray, just as John taught his disciples.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6–15; Ecclesiastes 5:2; PSALMS 72:18–19"> Jesus told them, “When you pray, say, ‘Father, let your name be honored as holy. May your kingdom come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:34; Proverbs 30:8"> Give us every day the food we need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:12–15; Matthew 26:41; Isaiah 43:25–26"> Forgive us our sins, just as we forgive everyone who sins against us. Keep us from temptation.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:1–8"> Jesus went on to tell them, “Say you have a friend and you go to him in the middle of the night and ask, ‘My friend, lend me three loaves of bread</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> because a friend of mine has come to visit me, and I don't have any food to give him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Galatians 6:17; Luke 7:6"> Your friend in the house might reply, ‘Don't bother me—I've already locked the door, and I and my children have gone to bed. I can't get up to give you anything now.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Colossians 4:12; Romans 15:30"> I tell you, even though he refuses to get up and give you anything, despite you being his friend, if you are persistent, your friend will get up and give you everything you need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 11:24; John 16:23–24; Matthew 21:22"> I tell you: ask, and you shall receive; seek, and you shall find; knock, and the door shall be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:3; Lamentations 3:54–58; PSALMS 31:22"> For everyone who asks, receives; and everyone who seeks, finds; and to everyone who knocks the door will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> Which of you fathers, if your son asks for a fish, would give him a snake instead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Luke 10:19; Ezekiel 2:6"> Or if he asks for an egg, would you give him a scorpion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; Romans 8:32"> So if you, even though you are evil, still know to give good gifts to your children, how much more will the heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:22–24; Matthew 9:32–34; Mark 7:32–37"> Jesus was driving out a demon that had made a man dumb. When the demon had left, the man who was dumb spoke, and the crowds were amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:34; Matthew 12:24–30; John 8:48"> But some of them said, “He is driving out demons using the power of Beelzebub, the ruler of demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 6:30; 1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12"> Others were trying to test Jesus by demanding a miraculous sign from heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 3:23–27; Matthew 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> Jesus knew what they were thinking and said, “Any kingdom divided against itself will collapse. A family<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “house.”">a</a></sup>divided against itself will fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 12:26; Matthew 12:31–34; Luke 11:15"> If Satan is divided against himself, how can his kingdom stand? You say that I cast out demons using the power of Beelzebub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:49; Job 15:6; Romans 3:19"> But if I drive out demons by the power of Beelzebub, by whose power do your own people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “sons.”">b</a></sup>drive them out? They themselves will condemn you as wrong!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Matthew 12:28; Luke 10:9"> However, if I am driving out demons by the power of God, then this proves God's kingdom has arrived. It's right here among you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:27; Matthew 12:29"> When a strong man who is fully armed guards his house, all he owns is safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 John 3:8"> But if a stronger man comes and defeats him, taking from him all his weapons that he depended on, then he can carry off all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 9:50; Mark 9:40"> Anyone who is not with me is against me, and anyone who doesn't build together with me is breaking it all apart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 12:43–45; 1 Peter 5:8; Job 2:2"> When an evil spirit leaves someone, it goes through the desert looking for a place to stay. When it doesn't find anywhere, it says, ‘I'll return to the house I left.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; 2 Peter 2:10–19; Jude 1:8–13"> When it returns, it finds its old home is swept and tidy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 5:14; Matthew 12:45; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> So it goes and finds seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they go in and live there. In the end that man is worse off than before.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:48; Luke 23:29"> As he was speaking, a woman in the crowd called out, “Blessed is the womb from where you came and the breasts that nursed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 6:47–48; Luke 8:21; PSALMS 112:1"> But Jesus said, “Even more blessed are those who hear God's word, and follow what it says.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Mark 8:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> As people crowded around him, Jesus began telling them, “This is an evil generation for it's looking for some miraculous sign, but no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 12:40–42; Jonah 1:17; Jonah 3:2–10"> In the same way that Jonah was a sign to the people of Nineveh, so will the Son of man be a sign to this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; 1 Kings 10:1–13; Romans 2:27"> The queen of the south will rise in the judgment together with the people of this generation and will condemn them, for she came from the ends of the earth to hear Solomon's wisdom, and now there is someone greater than Solomon here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Luke 11:31; Jonah 3:5–4"> The people of Nineveh rise in the judgment together with this generation, and will condemn it, for they repented when they heard Jonah's message, and now there's someone greater than Jonah here!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:16–17; Mark 4:21–22; Matthew 5:15–16"> No one lights a lamp and then hides it or places it under a bowl. No, you put it on a lamp stand so that those who come into the house can see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:22–23; Acts 26:18; Jeremiah 5:21"> Your eye is the light for your body. When your eye is good, your whole body is full of light. But when your eye is bad, your body is in the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; 2 Peter 1:9; James 3:13–17"> So make sure the light you have in you is not actually darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; Proverbs 6:23; Proverbs 4:18–19"> If your whole body is full of light, with no areas of darkness, then it will be completely illuminated, just as a bright lamp gives you light.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 7:36; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23; Luke 14:1"> After Jesus had finished speaking, a Pharisee invited him to come and eat with him. So Jesus went and sat down to eat a meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Mark 7:2–5; John 3:25"> The Pharisee was surprised that Jesus didn't wash his hands before eating as ceremonially required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 30:12; Genesis 6:5"> So the Lord told him, “You Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and the dish, but inside you're full of greed and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 15:36; Matthew 23:26"> You're so foolish! Don't you think the one who made the outside made the inside as well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 1:27; Titus 1:15"> If, acting from within, you do acts of kindness to others, then everything will be clean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Luke 18:12; Proverbs 21:3"> Shame on you Pharisees! You pay tithe on herbs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “mint and rue.”">c</a></sup>and plants, but you neglect justice and the love of God. You need to pay attention to the latter, while not leaving the former things undone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7–11"> Shame on you Pharisees! You love to have the best seats in the synagogues, and to be greeted with respect when you go to the markets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 23:27–28; PSALMS 5:9; Acts 23:3"> Shame on you! You are like unmarked graves that people walk over without knowing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 9:40; Matthew 22:35; Jeremiah 6:10"> One of the experts in religious law reacted, saying, “Teacher, when you talk like this, you're insulting us too!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; Matthew 23:2–4"> Jesus replied, “Shame on you lawyers too! You place burdens on people that are too hard to carry, but you don't lift a finger to help them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:51; Matthew 23:29–33"> Shame on you! You build memorial tombs to honor the prophets, but it was your own fathers who killed them in the first place!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 5:10; Matthew 23:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> By doing this you are witnesses showing that you agree with what your fathers did. They killed the prophets, and you build their tombs!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; Ephesians 4:11"> This is why God in his wisdom said, ‘I will send them prophets and apostles; some they will kill, and others they will persecute.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Revelation 18:20–24; 2 Kings 24:4"> Consequently, this generation will be held accountable for the blood of all the prophets shed from the beginning of the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Matthew 23:35; Hebrews 11:4"> from the blood of Abel right up to the blood of Zachariah who was killed between the altar and the sanctuary. Yes, I tell you, this generation will be held accountable for all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Luke 11:45–46"> Shame on you lawyers! You have removed the key of knowledge. You didn't go in yourselves, and you prevented others from going in too.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Jeremiah 20:10; Luke 20:20"> As Jesus was leaving, the religious teachers and the Pharisees began to attack him aggressively, asking questions to provoke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 12:13; Matthew 22:15; Matthew 22:35"> They were hoping to catch him out, trying to get him to say something they could use against him.</span>
</div>
</div>
