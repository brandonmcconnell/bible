<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> Looking around, Jesus watched rich people putting their gifts into the collection box.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In the Temple. (See 20:1.)">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> He also saw a very poor widow put in two small coins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Small low value coins called lepta.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> “I tell you the truth,” he said, “this poor widow has put in more than all the rest together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> All of them gave from their wealth what they had, but she gave from her poverty all she had to live on.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> Some of those<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Probably some of the disciples, see Mark 13:1.">c</a></sup>there were talking about the Temple, its fine stonework and the beautiful gifts that had been donated. But Jesus said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> “Regarding these things you're looking at… The time is coming when not one stone will be left on another; everything will be destroyed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> “Teacher, when will this happen?” they asked him. “What will be the sign that these things are about to happen?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> “Make sure you're not deceived,” Jesus warned them. “Many people will come claiming to be me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “come in my name.”">d</a></sup>saying, ‘Here I am!’ and, ‘The time has come!’ but don't follow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> When you hear of wars and revolutions, don't be frightened, because these things have to happen first, but the end won't come immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Nation will fight nation, and kingdom will fight kingdom,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> “There will be severe earthquakes, famines, and epidemic diseases in many lands, and extraordinary signs in the sky that are terrifying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all this, they will seize you and persecute you. They will drag you before synagogues and throw you into prison, and put you on trial before kings and governors on account of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> But this will provide you an opportunity to speak on my behalf before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> So make up your mind beforehand not to worry about how to defend yourselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> for I will give you words of wisdom that your enemies won't be able to dispute or contradict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> You will be betrayed even by your parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, and they will kill some of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> Everyone will hate you because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> But not a single hair of your head will be lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> By standing firm you will gain your lives.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Meaning eternal life, or even that you gain who you really were intended to be.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> However, when you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then you know that its destruction is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Those who are in Judea should run away to the mountains, and those who are in Jerusalem should leave, and those who are the countryside shouldn't enter the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> For these are days of punishment, fulfilling all that's written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> How hard it will be for those who are pregnant or nursing babies at that time! For terrible trouble is coming on the land and punishment against this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> They will be killed by the sword and taken away as prisoners to all the nations. Jerusalem will be trodden down by the foreign nations until their time is fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> There will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars, and on the earth the nations will be in distress, confused by the sea's rolling and crashing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> People will be faint from fear, terrified about what is happening to the world, for the powers of heaven will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> Then they'll see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Daniel 7:13-14.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> But when these things happen, stand up and look up, because you will soon be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> Then he told them this story as an illustration. “Look at the fig tree, or any other kind of tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> When you see new leaves appear, you don't need to be told that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> In the same way, when you see these things happening, you don't need to be told that God's kingdom is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> I tell you the truth, this generation won't come to an end before all this happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will come to an end, but my word will not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> Watch out that you don't become distracted by partying or getting drunk or by the worries of this life, so that this day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“This day”—the coming of the Son of man.">g</a></sup>catches you by surprise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; PSALMS 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> For this day will come upon everyone who lives on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> Always keep watch and pray, so that you may be able to escape all that will happen and stand before the Son of man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> Every day Jesus taught in the Temple, and every evening he went and stayed on the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> All the people came early in the morning to listen to him in the Temple.</span>
