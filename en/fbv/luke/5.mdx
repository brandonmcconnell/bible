<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> One day, as Jesus was standing beside the Sea of Galilee, people crowded around him to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> Jesus noticed two boats lying on the shore, left there by fishermen who were washing their nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> Jesus got into a boat, the one that belonged to Simon, and asked him to push it out into the water, just offshore. Then Jesus sat down in the boat and taught the people from there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> After he'd finished speaking, he told Simon, “Go out into deeper water, and let down your nets for a catch.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> “Teacher,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Teacher.” The word used here is not the same in the Greek as that in verse 8. It is often translated “Master,” but this form of address is not usual in modern English.">a</a></sup>we worked hard all night, and didn't catch anything. But if you say so, I'll let down the nets,” Simon replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> Having done this, a large shoal of fish filled the nets full to breaking point.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> They waved to their partners in the other boat, asking them to come over and help. The others came over and together they filled both of the boats with fish. The boats were so full that they began to sink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> When Simon Peter saw what had happened, he dropped to his knees before Jesus. “Lord, please stay away from me, for I am a sinful man!” he exclaimed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; PSALMS 8:8; Mark 9:6"> For he and everybody with him were completely amazed by the catch of fish that they had landed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> James and John, the sons of Zebedee, Simon's partners, felt the same way.<br />“Don't be afraid,” Jesus told Simon. “From now on you'll be fishing for people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> So they dragged the boats onto the shore, left everything, and followed Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> Once when Jesus was visiting one of the towns, he met a man there who had a very bad case of leprosy. The man fell with his face to the ground and begged Jesus, “Please Lord, if you're willing, you can make me clean.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Clean.” Of course, what the man was looking for was healing from his leprosy, however his leprosy made him ceremonially unclean. So “clean” not only healed his disease but also made him socially acceptable.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> Jesus reached out and touched the man. “I am willing,” he said. “Be clean!” Immediately the leprosy disappeared.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> “Say nothing to anyone,” Jesus instructed him. “Go and show yourself to the priest and make the ceremonial offerings as required by the law of Moses as proof that you've been healed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Leviticus 14:1-32.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> Yet the news about Jesus spread more and more. Large crowds came to hear Jesus and to be healed from their diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> But Jesus often used to retreat to quiet places and pray.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> One day when Jesus was teaching, the Pharisees and religious teachers who had come from all over Galilee, Judea, and Jerusalem were sitting there. The power of the Lord to heal was with him so he could heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> Some men arrived carrying a paralyzed man on a mat. They tried to take him in and lay him in front of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> But they couldn't find any way through the crowd, so they went up on the roof and made a hole in the roof tiles. Then they lowered the man down on the mat, right into the crowd in front of Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> When Jesus saw the trust they had in him, he said to the man, “Your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> The religious teachers and the Pharisees began to argue with that. “Who is this who's speaking blasphemies?” they asked. “Who can forgive sins? Only God can do that!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; PSALMS 139:2"> Jesus knew what they were arguing about, so he asked them, “Why are you thinking to question this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> What is easier? To say your sins are forgiven, or to say get up and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> However, I will prove to you that the Son of man has the authority here on earth to forgive sins.” Then he said to the paralyzed man, “I tell you: Get up, pick up your mat, and go home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; PSALMS 107:20–22"> Immediately the man stood up in front of them. He picked up the mat he'd been lying on, and went home, praising God as he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> Everyone was completely astonished at what had happened, and in great awe they praised God, saying, “What we saw today was amazing!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> Later, as Jesus was leaving the town, he saw a tax collector called Levi sitting at his tax booth.<br />“Follow me,” Jesus told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> Levi stood up, left everything, and followed Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> Levi organized a large banquet at his home in Jesus' honor. Many tax collectors and others were in the crowd that sat down to eat with them. But the Pharisees and the religious teachers complained to Jesus' disciples, asking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> “Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> “Healthy people don't need a doctor—but sick people do,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> “I didn't come to call those who are living right to repentance—I came to call sinners.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> “Well, John's disciples often fast and pray, and the Pharisees' disciples do so as well. But your disciples don't—they go on eating and drinking,” they told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> “Should the groomsmen fast while the bridegroom is with them?” Jesus asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> “No—but the time is coming when the bridegroom will be taken away from them. Then they can fast.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Then he gave them an illustration: “You don't tear out a patch from new clothes to mend old clothes. Otherwise you'd ruin new clothes, and the patch from the new wouldn't match the old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; PSALMS 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> You don't put new wine into old wineskins, because if you did the new wine would burst the wineskins. Then both wine and wineskins would be wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> You put new wine in new wineskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> And nobody after drinking old wine wants new wine, for they say, ‘the old tastes good.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
