<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> Jesus said to his disciples, “Temptations are unavoidable, but it will be a disaster for those through whom they come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> For such people it would be better to have a millstone hung around the neck and be thrown into the sea than to cause these little ones to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> So take care what you do. If your brother sins, warn him; and if he repents, forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> Even if he sins against you seven times a day, and seven times comes back and tells you, ‘I'm really sorry,’ forgive him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> The apostles said to the Lord, “Help us to have more trust!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> The Lord replied, “Even if your trust was as small as a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, ‘Uproot yourself, and plant yourself in the sea,’ and it would obey you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> Say you have a servant who does plowing or shepherding. When he comes in from work, do you say to him, ‘Come in and sit down now for a meal’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> No. You say to him, ‘Prepare a meal for me, get yourself dressed, and serve me until I've finished my meal. After that you can have your meal.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And do you thank the servant for doing what you told him? No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Likewise once you've done everything you were told, you should simply say, ‘We are undeserving servants. We just did our duty.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> As Jesus continued on his way to Jerusalem, he passed along the border between Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> As he entered a particular village, ten lepers met him, standing at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> They called out, “Jesus, Master, please have mercy on us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> When Jesus saw them, he said to them, “Go and show yourselves to the priests.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Leviticus 14.">a</a></sup>On their way there, they were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 30:11–12; PSALMS 103:1–4; PSALMS 118:18–19"> One of them when he saw that he was healed, returned to Jesus, shouting praises to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> He fell down at Jesus' feet, thanking him. He was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; PSALMS 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> “Weren't ten lepers healed?” Jesus asked. “Where are the other nine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; PSALMS 106:13"> Didn't anyone else come back to praise God—only this foreigner?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> Jesus told the man, “Get up and go on your way. Your trust has healed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> Once, when the Pharisees came and asked him when God's kingdom would come, Jesus replied, “God's kingdom doesn't come with visible signs that you can observe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> People won't be saying, ‘Look, it's here’ or ‘Look, it's there,’ for God's kingdom is among you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “within you.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> Then Jesus told the disciples, “The time is coming when you'll long to see the day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “one of the days.”">c</a></sup>when the Son of man comes, but you won't see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> They'll be telling you, ‘Look, there he is,’ or ‘Look, here he is,’ but don't go running off after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> The day when the Son of man comes will be just like lightning that flashes, lighting up the sky from one side to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first he will have to suffer many things, and be rejected by this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> The time when the Son of man comes will be like it was in Noah's day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> People went on eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage until the day Noah went into the ark. Then the flood came and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> It will be like it was in Lot's day. People went on eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> But on the day Lot left Sodom, fire and brimstone rained down from heaven and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> The day when the Son of man appears will be just like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> If you're up on the roof that day don't go down and get your things; and if you're out in the fields don't go back home either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember Lot's wife!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> If you try to hold on to your life you'll lose it; but if you lose your life you'll save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I tell you, at that time two will be in bed at night; one will be taken and the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> Two women will be grinding grain, one will be taken; and the other left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Verse 36 is not found in the early manuscripts.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> “Where, Lord?” they asked. “Where the carcass is, that's where the vultures gather,” Jesus replied.</span>
