<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–23; Matthew 26:2–5; John 11:55–57"> Now the Festival of Unleavened Bread, also called the Passover, was approaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 11:47–53; Matthew 12:14; Luke 20:19"> The chief priests and religious teachers were looking for a way to kill Jesus, but were afraid of what the people would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 13:2; Mark 14:10–11; John 13:26–27"> Satan entered into Judas, surnamed Iscariot, one of the twelve disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:24; Luke 22:52"> He went and discussed with the chief priests and guard officers how he could betray Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:15–16; Acts 1:18; Matthew 27:3–5"> They were delighted, and offered him money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:2; Matthew 26:5"> He agreed, and began to look for an opportunity to hand over Jesus when a crowd would not be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> The Day of Unleavened Bread arrived when the Passover animal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Animal”: the Greek text does not specify which animal was sacrificed, though usually it was a lamb or a kid.">a</a></sup>had to be sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> Jesus sent Peter and John, telling them, “Go and prepare the Passover meal, so we can eat it together.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They asked him, “Where do you want us to prepare for it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> He replied, “When you enter the city you'll meet a man carrying a jar of water. Follow him and go into the house he enters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> Tell the owner of the house that the Teacher asks you, ‘Where is the dining room where I can eat the Passover with my disciples?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> He'll show you a large upstairs room that already has the necessary furniture. Prepare the meal there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> They went and found that everything was just as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover meal there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> When the time came, he sat down at the table with his apostles. He told them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:50; John 4:34; John 17:1"> “I've been really looking forward to eating this Passover meal with you before my sufferings begin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 14:15; Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30"> I tell you I shall not eat it again until the time is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; PSALMS 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> Jesus took the cup, and after he had given thanks, he said, “Take this and share it among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> I tell you that I won't drink again of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> He picked up some bread, and after he had given thanks, he broke it into pieces and gave it to them. “This is my body which is given for you; do this in order to remember me,” Jesus told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> In the same way after they had finished supper, he picked up the cup and said, “This cup is the new agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">b</a></sup>in my blood which is poured out for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> “In spite of this, my betrayer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “the hand of the one betraying me.”">c</a></sup>is sitting right here with me at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; PSALMS 109:6–15; PSALMS 55:12–15"> For it has been determined that the Son of man will die, yet how disastrous it will be for his betrayer!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> They began to argue among themselves as to who this might be and who could do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> At the same time they also got into a quarrel about which of them was the most important.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> Jesus told them, “Foreign kings lord it over their subjects, and those having power even want people to call them ‘benefactors.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But it should not be so with you! Whoever is highest among you should be like the lowest, and the leader should be like a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> Who is greater—the one who sits at the table, or the one who serves? Isn't it the one who sits at the table? But I'm among you as the one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> You have stayed with me throughout my trials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> And I grant to you authority to rule, just as my Father granted it to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> so that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> “Simon, Simon—Satan has asked to have all of you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The first “you” in this statement is plural, the rest are singular, applying specifically to Peter.">d</a></sup>to sift like wheat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> but I have prayed for you that your trust in me may not fail. And when you have returned,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Meaning “return to the truth.”">e</a></sup>encourage your brothers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> Peter said, “Lord, I'm ready to go with you to prison, and to die with you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus replied, “I'm telling you, Peter, before the cock crows today, you will deny three times that you know me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; PSALMS 23:1"> Jesus asked them, “When I sent you out without money, without a bag, and without an extra pair of sandals, did you lack anything?” “No, nothing,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> “But now, if you have money you should take it, as well as a bag, and if you don't have a sword, sell your cloak and buy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> I tell you that this statement in Scripture about me must be fulfilled: ‘He was counted with the wicked.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 53:12.">f</a></sup>What was said about me is now being fulfilled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> “Look, Lord, here are two swords,” they said. “That's enough,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> Jesus left and as usual went to the Mount of Olives together with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> When he arrived he said to them, “Pray that you don't give in to temptation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> Then he left them and walked about a stone's throw away, where he kneeled down and prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> “Father,” he prayed, “if you're willing, please take away this cup of suffering from me. But I want to do what you want, not what I want.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; PSALMS 91:11–12"> Then an angel appeared from heaven to strengthen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; PSALMS 22:12–21"> In great distress Jesus prayed even harder, and his sweat fell like drops of blood onto the ground.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The authenticity of verses 43 and 44 are disputed. Manuscript evidence is divided.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> He finished praying, stood up, and went over to the disciples. He found them asleep, worn out by grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> “Why are you sleeping?” he asked them. “Get up and pray so that you won't fall into temptation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> While he was still speaking, a crowd appeared led by Judas, one of the twelve disciples. Judas went up to Jesus to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> But Jesus asked him, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of man with a kiss?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> Jesus' followers asked him, “Lord, should we attack them with our swords?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> And one of them struck the high priest's servant, cutting off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> “Stop! No more of this!” said Jesus. He touched the man's ear and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> Then Jesus spoke to the chief priests, and the officers of the Temple guard, and the elders. “Am I some kind of criminal that you had to come with swords and clubs?” he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> “You didn't arrest me before, even though I was with you in the Temple every day. But this is your moment now, the time when darkness is in power.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> They arrested him and led him away, taking him to the chief priest's house. Peter followed at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="PSALMS 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> They started a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down by it. Peter was there among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> As he sat there, a servant girl noticed him in the firelight, and stared directly at him and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> “This man was with him.” But Peter denied it. “Woman, I don't know him!” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> A little later someone else looked at him and said, “You're also one of them.” “No, I'm not!” Peter replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> About an hour later, another person insisted, “I'm sure he was with him too—he's a Galilean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> “I've no idea what you're talking about!” Peter replied. Right then, while he was still speaking, the cock crowed. The Lord turned and looked at Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> And Peter remembered what the Lord had said, how he'd told him: “Before the cock crows today, you'll deny me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> Peter went out and wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> The men guarding Jesus began to mock him and beat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> They put a blindfold on him, and then asked him, “If you can prophesy, tell us who hit you that time!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> and shouted many other insults at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> Early in the morning the council of elders gathered together with the chief priests and religious teachers. Jesus was led before the council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> “If you really are the Messiah, then tell us,” they said. “Even if I were to tell you, you wouldn't believe me,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> “And if I were to ask you a question, you wouldn't answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; PSALMS 110:1; Mark 16:19"> But from now on the Son of man will sit at the right hand of the mighty God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Psalms 110:1.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> They all asked, “So are you the son of God?” “You say that I am,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> “Why do we need any more witnesses?” they said. “We've heard it ourselves from his own mouth!”</span>
