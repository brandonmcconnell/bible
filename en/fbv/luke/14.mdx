<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; PSALMS 62:4"> One Sabbath Jesus went to have a meal at the home of one of the leaders of the Pharisees where they watched him closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A man was there whose arms and legs were swollen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> So Jesus asked the experts in religious law and the Pharisees, “Does the law allow healing on the Sabbath, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they kept quiet. Jesus touched the man, healed him, and sent him on his way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> Then Jesus said to them, “If your son or your ox happened to fall into a well on the Sabbath, wouldn't you go and pull him out immediately?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> They weren't able to give an answer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> So he told a story to the guests, noticing how they'd chosen to sit in places of honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> “When you're invited to a wedding reception, don't take the place of honor, because someone more important than you may have been invited,” he began.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> “Your host who invited you both will come and tell you, ‘Give this man your place.’ Then in embarrassment you'll have to move to whatever place is left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> Instead, when you're invited, take the lowest place, so that when your host comes in, he'll tell you, ‘My friend, please move to a better seat.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Proverbs 25:7.">a</a></sup>Then you'll be honored before all the guests sitting with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For those who exalt themselves will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be exalted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> Then he said to the man who had invited him, “When you give a lunch or a dinner, don't invite your friends, or your brothers, or your relatives, or your rich neighbors, for they may invite you back, and then you'd be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> Instead, when you give a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, and the blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and you will be blessed, for they have nothing to repay you with, and you'll be rewarded at the resurrection of the good.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> When one of them eating at the table with Jesus heard this, he said to Jesus, “How wonderful it will be for those who feast in the kingdom of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> “Once there was a man who prepared a great banquet, and invited many guests,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> “When it was time to eat he sent his servant out to tell everyone who had been invited, ‘Come, because the banquet's ready.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> But they all started to make excuses. The first said, ‘I've just bought a field and I have to go and see it. Please excuse me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Another said, ‘I've just bought five pairs of oxen and have to go and try them out. Please excuse me.’ Still another said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> ‘I've just got married, so I can't come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> The servant returned and told his master what they'd said. The home-owner became angry and told his servant, ‘Quickly, go out into the streets and alleys of the town, and bring in the poor and crippled and blind and lame.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> Then the servant said, ‘Master, I did what you told me, but there are still empty places.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> So the master told the servant, ‘Go out on the country roads and lanes, and make people come—I want my house to be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> I tell you, not a single one of those people I invited will get a taste of my banquet.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> A large crowd was accompanying Jesus. He turned to them and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> “If you want to follow me but you don't hate your father and mother, and wife and children, and brothers and sisters—even your own life—you can't be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> If you don't carry your cross and follow me, you can't be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> If you planned to build a tower, wouldn't you first work out how much it would cost, and see if you had enough money to complete it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Otherwise, if after laying the foundation you weren't able to finish it, everyone who saw it would laugh at you, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> ‘Look at him: he started building but he couldn't finish.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> What king goes to war with another king without first sitting down with his advisors to work out whether he and his ten thousand can defeat the one marching against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> If he can't, he'll send representatives to ask for peace while the other king is still a long way off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> In the same way every one of you who doesn't give up everything can't be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> Salt is good, but if it loses its taste, how can you make it salty again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It's no good for the soil or for fertilizer—you just toss it out. Whoever has ears, then listen!”</span>
</div>
</div>
