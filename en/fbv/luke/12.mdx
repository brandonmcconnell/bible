<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:22; Luke 8:17; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For there's nothing hidden that won't be revealed, nothing secret that won't be made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:27; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> Whatever you have said in the dark will be heard in the light, and whatever you whispered in private will be announced from the rooftops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Jeremiah 1:8; Revelation 2:10"> I tell you, my friends, don't be afraid of those who kill the body, for once they've done that there's no more they can do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 10:28; Revelation 14:7"> Let me make it clear whom you should be afraid of. You should be afraid of the one who after he has killed has the power to dispose of them in Gehenna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word here is literally, “Gehenna,” sometimes translated “hell” or “hellfire.” Gehenna was the place outside of Jerusalem where fires were lit to dispose of rubbish by burning. “Hell” is a concept that is derived from Nordic and Anglo-Saxon mythology and does not parallel the idea here. See note on Matthew 5:22.">a</a></sup>That's the one you should be afraid of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:24; PSALMS 145:15–16; PSALMS 147:9"> Aren't five sparrows sold for two pennies? But God doesn't forget a single one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:30–31; Matthew 6:26; Isaiah 43:3–4"> Even the hairs on your head have been counted. Don't be afraid—you're worth more than many sparrows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 1 John 2:23; Romans 10:9–10"> I tell you the truth, those who declare they belong to me, the Son of man will also declare they belong to him before God's angels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Mark 8:38"> but those who deny me will be denied before God's angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Everyone who speaks against the Son of man will be forgiven, but anyone who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–20; Luke 21:12–14; Acts 4:5–7"> When you're brought to trial before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, don't worry about how to defend yourself, or what you should say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:20; Acts 4:8"> The Holy Spirit will teach you at that time what's important to say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Timothy 6:5; Ezekiel 33:31"> Someone in the crowd asked Jesus, “Teacher, please tell my brother to share the inheritance with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:3; Romans 2:1; Romans 9:20"> “My friend,” Jesus replied, “Who appointed me as your judge to decide how your inheritance should be divided?” He told the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:6–10; PSALMS 119:36–37"> “Watch out, and beware of all greedy thoughts and actions, for a person's life isn't summed up by all the things they own.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:12–14; Hosea 2:8; Genesis 41:47–49"> Then he told them a story as an illustration. “Once there was a rich man who owned land that was very productive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Isaiah 58:7; Romans 12:13"> After thinking things through, the man said to himself, ‘What shall I do? I've nowhere to store my crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:15; James 4:15; Luke 12:21"> I know what I'll do,’ he decided. ‘I'll pull down my barns and build bigger ones, and then I'll be able to store all my crops and everything I own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Then I'll tell myself: You have enough to live on for many years, so take life easy: eat, drink, and enjoy yourself!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> But God said to him, ‘You foolish man! Your life will be demanded back this very night, and then who will get everything you've stored up?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Luke 12:33; James 2:5"> This is what happens to people who hoard up wealth for themselves but are not rich as far as God is concerned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> Jesus told his disciples, “That's why I tell you don't worry about life, about what to eat, or about what clothes you should wear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> Life is about more than food, and the body is about more than wearing clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; PSALMS 147:9; Luke 12:7"> Look at the ravens. They don't sow or reap, they don't have any storerooms or barns, but God feeds them. And you're far more valuable than birds!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:27; Matthew 5:36; Luke 19:3"> Can you add an hour to your life by worrying about it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; PSALMS 39:6; Ecclesiastes 7:13"> If you can't do anything about such small things, why worry about the rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:28–30; Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Think of the lilies and how they grow. They don't work, and they don't spin thread for clothes, but I tell you, not even Solomon in all his glory was as beautifully dressed as one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Isaiah 40:6; Matthew 6:30"> So if God clothes the fields with such beautiful flowers, which are here today but gone tomorrow when they are burned in a fire to heat an oven, how much more will God clothe you, you who have so little trust!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 6:31; Luke 12:22; Luke 22:35"> Don't be concerned about what you're going to eat or drink—don't worry about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Matthew 6:8; Ephesians 4:17"> These are all things that people in the world worry about, but your Father knows you need them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 13:5; PSALMS 34:9"> Search for God's kingdom, and you'll be given these things as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:28; 1 Peter 1:3–5; Revelation 1:6"> Don't be afraid, little flock, for your Father is happy to give you the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; Acts 4:34–35; Acts 2:45"> Sell what you have, and give the money to the poor. Get yourselves purses that don't wear out: treasure in heaven that will never run out, where no thief can steal it and no moth can destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Matthew 6:21; Philippians 3:20"> For what you value the most shows who you really are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:14; Matthew 25:4–10"> Be dressed and ready, and keep your lamps lit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jude 1:20–21; James 5:7–8; Revelation 3:20"> like servants waiting for their master when he returns from his wedding feast, prepared to open the door quickly for him when he comes and knocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; Matthew 24:42; John 13:4–5"> How good it will be for those servants that the master finds watching when he returns. I tell you the truth, he will get dressed, have them sit down for a meal, and will come and serve them himself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:4–5; Matthew 25:6"> Even if he comes at midnight, or just before dawn—how good for them if he finds them watching and ready!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> But remember this: if the master knew when a thief was coming, he would keep watch, and not allow his house to be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13; Romans 13:11"> You must also be ready, for the Son of man is coming when you don't expect him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 13:37; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8"> “Is this story you're telling just for us, or for everyone?” Peter asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–46; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Hebrews 13:17"> The Lord replied, “Who then is the trustworthy and wise manager, the one person in the household that the master puts in charge to share out their food at the right time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> It will be good for that servant when his master returns and finds him doing what he should.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> I tell you the truth, the master will put that servant in charge of everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 24:48–50; Revelation 18:7–8; Philippians 3:18–19"> But what if the servant were to say to himself, ‘My master is taking a long time in coming,’ and then starts beating the other servants, both men and women, feasting and getting drunk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 12:40; Matthew 24:51; PSALMS 11:5"> That servant's master will return unexpectedly one day at a time he didn't anticipate, and will punish him severely, treating him as totally untrustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 4:17; John 9:41; John 12:48"> That servant who knew what his master wanted and yet didn't get ready or follow his instructions, will be beaten severely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 3:1; Leviticus 5:17; Luke 16:10–12"> but the servant who didn't know and did things deserving punishment will be beaten only lightly. From those who are given much, much will be required, and from those who are entrusted with more, more will be demanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Luke 12:51–52; Matthew 3:10–12"> I have come to set the earth on fire, and I really wish it was already burning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 12:27–28; John 19:30; John 7:6–8"> But I have a baptism to go through, and I'm in agony, wishing it was over!</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> Do you think that I came to bring peace on earth? No, I tell you, I bring division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Acts 14:1–4; John 15:18–21; Micah 7:5–6"> From now on, if there are five in a family, they will be divided against each other: three against two, and two against three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Matthew 10:21–22; Matthew 24:10"> They will be divided against each other—father against son, son against father, mother against daughter, daughter against mother, mother-in-law against daughter-in-law, and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Micah 7:6.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds. “When you see a cloud rising in the west, you immediately say, ‘a rain-shower's coming,’ and it happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Job 37:17; Matthew 20:12"> When a south wind blows, you say ‘it's going to be hot,’ and it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 16:3; Matthew 24:32–33; Matthew 11:25"> You hypocrites, how is it that you know how to rightly interpret the weather but you don't know how to interpret the present time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; John 7:24; Acts 13:26–38"> Why don't you think for yourselves and judge what's the right thing to do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8–9; Matthew 5:23–26; Luke 13:24–28"> As you go with your accuser to the magistrate, on the way you should be working on a settlement. Otherwise you may be dragged before the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Mark 12:42; Matthew 18:34; Matthew 25:46"> I tell you, you won't get out until you've paid the last penny.”</span>
