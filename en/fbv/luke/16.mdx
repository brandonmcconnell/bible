<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Matthew 25:14–30; James 4:3"> Jesus told his disciples this story. “There was once a rich man whose manager was accused of wasting what belonged to his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:9–10; 1 Peter 4:5; Revelation 20:12"> So the rich man called in his manager, and asked him, ‘What's this I hear about you? Bring in your accounts, because you won't be continuing as manager.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 10:3; Proverbs 18:9"> The manager said to himself, ‘Now what will I do since my master is going to fire me from my job? I'm not strong enough to dig, and I'm ashamed to beg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:15"> Oh, I know what I'm going to do so that when I'm sacked as manager people will make me welcome in their homes.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:41–42; Matthew 18:24"> So he invited all those who were in debt to his master to come and see him. He asked the first one, ‘How much do you owe my master?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:10; Luke 16:12; Luke 16:9"> The man replied, ‘A hundred units<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="One batos “bath” was equivalent to around 6 gallons or 22 liters.">a</a></sup>of oil.’ He said to him, ‘Sit down quickly. Take your bill, and change it to fifty.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:11–12; Luke 20:9; Luke 20:12"> Then he said to another, ‘How much do you owe?’ The man replied, ‘A hundred units<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="One koros “kor” was equivalent to 11 bushels or 390 liters.">b</a></sup>of wheat.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill and change it to eighty.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5; Ephesians 5:8; Proverbs 6:6–8"> The rich man commended his dishonest manager for his cunning idea. The children of this world are more cunning towards one another than are the children of light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Daniel 4:27"> I tell you, use the wealth of this world to make friends for yourselves so that when it's gone, you'll be welcomed into an eternal home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:11–12; Matthew 25:21; Luke 19:17"> If you can be trusted with very little you can also be trusted with much; if you are dishonest with very little you will also be dishonest with much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 2:5; Proverbs 8:18–19"> So if you can't be trusted when it comes to worldly wealth, who will trust you with true riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 19:13–26; Matthew 25:14–29; Job 1:21"> And if you can't be trusted with what belongs to someone else, who will trust you with what is yours?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Matthew 6:24; James 4:4"> No servant can obey two masters. Either he will hate one and love the other, or he will be devoted to one and despise the other. You can't serve both God and Money.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Luke 20:47; Jeremiah 6:13"> The Pharisees, who loved money, heard what Jesus said and laughed at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> But Jesus told them, “You appear pious to people, but God knows what you're thinking. What people value highly is detested by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 11:9–14; Matthew 21:32; Acts 3:24–25"> What was written in the law and the prophets lasted until John. From then on the good news of the kingdom of God is being spread, and everyone is forcing their way in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Luke 21:33; 1 Peter 1:25"> However, it's easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for the smallest point of the Law to disappear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12; Matthew 5:31–32"> Any man who divorces his wife and marries someone else commits adultery, and a man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 6:4–6; Luke 18:24–25"> Once there was a man who was rich. He wore purple clothes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Purple clothes were very expensive.">c</a></sup>and fine linen, and enjoyed a luxurious life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:2; 1 Samuel 2:8; James 2:5"> A beggar named Lazarus used to sit at his gate, covered in sores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Mark 7:28; John 6:12"> longing to eat the leftovers from the rich man's table. Even the dogs came and licked his sores.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; PSALMS 49:16–19; James 1:11"> Then the beggar died, and angels carried him away to be with Abraham. The rich man also died and was buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Luke 16:28; Matthew 18:9"> In Hades, where he was in torment, he looked up and saw Abraham in the far distance, with Lazarus beside him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 4:10; Revelation 22:1; Revelation 7:16–17"> ‘Father Abraham,’ he called out, ‘Have mercy on me and send Lazarus to dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue, because I'm burning in agony.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:24; 1 John 2:15; PSALMS 17:14"> But Abraham replied, ‘My son, remember that you enjoyed the good things of life, while Lazarus had a very poor life. Now he is here being comforted, while you suffer in torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 3:36; PSALMS 49:14; PSALMS 50:22"> Apart from that, there's a great gulf that stretches between us and you. Nobody who wants to cross from here to you can do so, and nobody can cross from there over to us.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The rich man said, ‘Then Father, I beg you, please send him to my father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:40; PSALMS 49:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> For I have five brothers and he can warn them so that they don't end up here in this place of torment.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:21; Luke 24:27; Luke 16:16"> But Abraham replied, ‘They have Moses and the prophets. They should listen to them.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Luke 16:24; Luke 13:5"> ‘No, Father Abraham,’ said the man. ‘But they would repent if someone went to them from the dead!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Acts 19:8; John 11:43–53"> Abraham said to him, ‘If they won't listen to Moses and the prophets, they won't be convinced even if someone returns from the dead.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
