<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan River and was led by the Spirit into the desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> where he was tempted by the devil for forty days. He didn't eat anything during that whole time, so at the end he was starving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> The devil said to him, “If you are the Son of God, command this stone to become bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> “It is written in Scripture, ‘You shall not live on bread alone,’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 8:3.">a</a></sup>Jesus answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> The devil led him up to a high place, and in a rapid view showed him all the kingdoms of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> Then the devil said to Jesus, “I will give you authority over all of them, and their glory. This authority has been given to me, and I can give it to anyone I want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; PSALMS 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> Bow down and worship me and you can have it all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> “It is written in Scripture, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and you shall serve only him,’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:13.">b</a></sup>Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> The devil took Jesus to Jerusalem, set him on the top of the Temple, and told him, “If you are the Son of God, then jump!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> For it is written in Scripture, ‘He will order his angels to care for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 91:12"> holding you up to protect you from stumbling over a stone.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 91:11-12.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> “It is written in Scripture, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God,’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:16.">d</a></sup>Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> When the devil had completed all his temptations, he waited for another opportunity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “for an opportune time.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> Jesus returned to Galilee, full of the Spirit's power. News about him spread everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> Jesus taught in their synagogues, and everyone praised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> When he arrived in Nazareth, where he had grown up, he went to the synagogue on the Sabbath day as usual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> The scroll of the prophet Isaiah was handed to him. Jesus unrolled the scroll and found the place where it's written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; PSALMS 146:7–8"> “The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, for he has anointed me to announce good news to the destitute. He has sent me to proclaim that prisoners will be released, the blind will see, the oppressed will be freed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> and to proclaim the time of the Lord's favor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> He rolled up the scroll and gave it back to the attendant. Then he sat down. Everybody in the synagogue was staring at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> “This Scripture you've just heard has been fulfilled today!” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; PSALMS 45:2; Acts 6:10"> Everybody expressed their approval of him, amazed at the gracious words that came from his lips. “Isn't this Joseph's son?” they wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> Jesus replied, “I'm sure you'll repeat this proverb to me, ‘Physician, heal yourself!’ and ask, ‘Why don't you do here in your own hometown what we heard you did in Capernaum?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> But I tell you the truth, no prophet is accepted in his hometown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> I guarantee that there were many widows in Israel during the time of Elijah when there was a drought for three and a half years, causing a great famine throughout the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> Yet Elijah wasn't sent to any of them. He was sent to a widow in Zarephath in the region of Sidon!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> Even though there were many lepers in Israel in the time of Elisha, the only one who was healed was Naaman the Syrian!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> When they heard this everyone in the synagogue became furious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> They jumped to their feet and threw him out of the town. Then they dragged him to the top of the hill on which the town was built in order to throw him off the cliff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> But he walked right through them and went on his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> Jesus went down to Capernaum, a town in Galilee. On Sabbath he started teaching them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> They were amazed at what he taught them for he spoke with authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> In the synagogue there was a man who was possessed by a demon. He cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> “So, what do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: God's Holy One!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> Jesus interrupted him, saying. “Be quiet!” Then he ordered the demon, “Come out of him!” Throwing him to the ground right before them, the demon left the man without injuring him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> They were all amazed and asked each other, “What is this teaching? With power and authority he orders evil spirits to leave—and they do!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> News about Jesus spread throughout the nearby region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> Leaving the synagogue, Jesus went to Simon's house. Simon's mother-in-law was sick with a high fever and those who were there asked Jesus to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> Jesus went and stood over her. He told the fever to leave her—which it did. She got up right away and prepared a meal for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> When the sun set, they brought to him all who were sick, suffering from various diseases. Jesus placed his hands on them, one after the other, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> Demons came out of many people, shouting, “You are the Son of God.” But Jesus stopped them and refused to let them speak because they knew he was the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> Early the following morning Jesus left to find some peace and quiet. But the crowds went out looking for him, and finally found him. They tried to stop him leaving because they did not want him to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> But he told them, “I have to go to other towns to tell them the good news of the kingdom of God too, because that is what I was sent to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> So Jesus went on traveling around, teaching the good news in the synagogues of Judea.</span>
