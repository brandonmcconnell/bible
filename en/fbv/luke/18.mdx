<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> Jesus told them this story to encourage them to pray at all times, and not to become discouraged.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; PSALMS 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> “Once there was a judge in this particular town who didn't respect God or care about anyone,” Jesus explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> “In the same town lived a widow who time and again went to the judge, saying, ‘Give me justice in the case against my enemy!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> For a while he didn't want to do anything about it, but eventually he said to himself, ‘Even though I don't respect God or care about anyone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> this widow is so annoying I'll make sure she receives justice. Then she won't wear me out by her coming to see me so often.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> Listen to what even an unjust judge decided,” said the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> “Don't you think that God will make sure his chosen people receive justice, those who cry out to him day and night? Do you think he will make them wait?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> No, I tell you, he will give them justice quickly. However, when the Son of man comes, will he find people on earth who trust in him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> He also told this story about those who are so sure that they are living right, and who put everybody else down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went to the Temple to pray. One was a Pharisee, and the other a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood up and prayed to himself, ‘God, I thank you that I am not like other people—cheats, criminals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “unright.”">a</a></sup>adulterers—or even like this tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice a week, and I pay tithe on my income.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> But the tax collector stood at a distance. He wouldn't even look up to heaven. Instead he beat his chest and prayed, ‘God, please be merciful to me. I am a sinner.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I tell you, it was this man who went home right in God's sight and not the other. For those who exalt themselves will be humbled, while those who humble themselves will be exalted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> Parents were bringing their infants to Jesus to have him bless them by his touch. When the disciples saw what was happening, they tried to stop them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus called the children to him. “Let the little children come to me,” he said. “Don't prevent them, for the kingdom of God belongs to those who are like them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; PSALMS 131:1–2"> I tell you the truth, anyone who doesn't welcome the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> One of the rulers came to Jesus and asked him, “Good Teacher, what do I have to do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> “Why do you call me good?” Jesus replied. “No one is good, only God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> You know the commandments: do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not give false testimony, honor your father and mother.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 20:12-16or Deuteronomy 5:16-20.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> “I've kept all these commandments since I was young,” the man replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> When Jesus heard this he told the man, “You still lack one thing. Go and sell all you have, give the money to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come and follow me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when the man heard this he became very sad, for he was very wealthy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> When he saw his reaction, Jesus said, “How difficult it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> It's easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> Those who heard this wondered, “Who can be saved then?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> Jesus replied, “What is impossible in human terms is possible for God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> Peter said, “We left everything to follow you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> “I tell you the truth,” Jesus told them, “anybody who leaves behind their home, wife, brothers, parents, or children for the sake of God's kingdom</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> will receive so much more in this life, and eternal life in the world to come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; PSALMS 22:1–31"> Jesus took the twelve disciples aside, and told them, “We're going to Jerusalem, and all that the prophets wrote about the Son of man will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> He will be handed over to the foreigners,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">c</a></sup>he will be mocked, insulted, and spat upon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> They will flog him and kill him, but on the third day he will rise again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> But they didn't understand anything Jesus told them. The meaning was hidden from them and they didn't grasp what he was talking about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> As Jesus approached Jericho a blind man was sitting beside the road begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> He heard the crowd going past, so he asked what was happening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> They told him, “Jesus of Nazareth is passing by.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> He called out, “Jesus, son of David, please have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; PSALMS 141:1; Luke 18:15"> Those at the front of the crowd told him to stop shouting and be quiet, but he only shouted louder, “Son of David, please have mercy on me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> Jesus stopped and told them to bring the blind man to him. As he came over, Jesus asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> “What do you want me to do for you?”<br />“Lord, please, I want to see,” he pleaded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> “Then see!” Jesus told him. “Your trust in me has healed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> Immediately the man could see. He followed Jesus, praising God. Everyone there who saw what happened also praised God.</span>
</div>
</div>
