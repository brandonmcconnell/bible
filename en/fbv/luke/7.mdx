<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> When he'd finished speaking to the people, Jesus left for Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> A centurion lived there who had a servant he greatly valued who was sick and was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> When he heard about Jesus, the centurion sent some Jewish elders to him, asking him to come and heal his servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> When the elders came to Jesus, they pleaded with him strongly, saying, “Please come and do what he asks. He deserves your help,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> because he loves our people and he built a synagogue for us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> Jesus went with them and as they approached the house, the centurion sent some friends to Jesus to tell him, “Lord, please don't trouble yourself by coming into my house, because I'm not worthy of that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; PSALMS 33:9; Luke 4:36"> I didn't even think that I was worthy to come and see you. Just give the command, and my servant will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For I'm under the authority of my superior officers, and I have soldiers under my authority too. I command one to go and he goes, another to come and he comes. I command my servant to do something and he does it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> When Jesus heard this he was astounded. He turned to the crowd that was following him, and said, “I tell you, I haven't found trust like this even in Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> Then the centurion's friends returned to the house and found the servant in good health.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> Soon after Jesus went to a town called Nain, accompanied by his disciples and a large crowd.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> As he approached the town gate a funeral procession was coming the other way. The man who had died was the only son of a widow, and a sizeable crowd from the town was with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; PSALMS 86:15"> When the Lord saw her he was filled with compassion for her. “Don't cry,” he told her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> Jesus went over to the coffin and touched it, and the pall-bearers stopped.<br />Jesus said, “Young man, I tell you, get up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> The dead man sat up and began to talk, and Jesus gave him back to his mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> A sense of awe filled everyone there and they praised God, saying, “A great prophet has arisen among us,” and “God has visited his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> News about Jesus spread throughout Judea, and all around.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> The disciples of John told John about all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> John called two of his disciples and told them to go and see Jesus, and ask, “Are you the one we've been expecting, or should we wait for someone else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> When they came to Jesus, they said, “John the Baptist sent us to you, to ask you, ‘Are you the one we've been expecting or should we wait for someone else?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; PSALMS 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> At that very moment Jesus was healing many people of their diseases, illnesses, evil spirits, and making the blind to see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> Jesus answered John's disciples, “Go and tell John what you've seen and heard. The blind see, the lame walk, the lepers cured, the deaf hear, the dead raised back to life, the poor are told the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> How good it is for those who are not offended because of me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> After the messengers from John had left, Jesus began telling the crowd, “About John: what did you expect to see when you went out to meet him in the desert? Some reed blown about by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> Did you come looking for a man dressed in fine clothes? No, those who have stylish clothes and live in luxury are found in palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> Were you looking for a prophet? Yes he is, and I'm telling you, he's much more than a prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> It was written about him in Scripture: ‘Look, I'm sending my messenger to go before you to prepare your way.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Malachi 3:1.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> I tell you, no one born of women is greater than John, but even the most unimportant person in God's kingdom is greater than he is!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> When they heard him, all of them—even the tax collectors—followed what God said was good and right, for they had been baptized by John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> But the Pharisees and the religious teachers rejected what God wanted them to do, for they had refused to be baptized by John.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> “What shall I compare these people to?” asked Jesus. “What are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They're like children sitting in the market who tell one another, ‘We played the flute for you but you didn't dance; we sang sad songs but you didn't cry.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> When John the Baptist came he didn't eat bread or drink wine, but you say he's demon-possessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> Now the Son of man is here, and eats and drinks with people, but you say, ‘Look, he spends his time eating too much food and drinking too much wine.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Eating too much food and drinking too much wine.” The words used here indicate excess, in comparison to the basic words used earlier in the verse.">b</a></sup>Plus he's a friend of tax collectors and sinners.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> However, God's wise ways are proved right by all who follow him!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Probably a proverb. Literally, “Wisdom is proved right by all her children,” meaning that the proof is in the consequences…">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> One of the Pharisees invited Jesus to come and eat with him. Jesus went to the Pharisee's house and sat down to eat the meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> A woman who was a sinner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Usually understood to mean that she was living an immoral life.">d</a></sup>in that town found out that Jesus was eating in the Pharisee's house. She went there, carrying an alabaster jar of perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="PSALMS 126:5–6; PSALMS 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> She kneeled beside Jesus and with her tears wet his feet, and dried them with her hair. She kissed his feet, and then she poured the perfume over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> When the Pharisee who had invited Jesus saw this he said to himself, “If this man was really a prophet he would know who this woman was who's touching him, and what kind of person she was—that she's a sinner!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> Jesus spoke up and said, “Simon, I have something to tell you.”<br />“Tell me, Teacher,” he responded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> “Once two people were in debt to a money-lender. One owed five hundred denarii,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Denarius: equivalent to a day's wage.">e</a></sup>the other only fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> Neither of them could repay him, so he forgave the debts. Which one will love him the most?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> “The one he forgave the most, I would think,” Simon answered.<br />“You're absolutely right,” said Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> Turning to the woman, he said to Simon, “You see this woman? When I came into your house, you didn't give me water to wash my feet. But she has washed my feet with her tears, and wiped them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> You didn't give me a kiss, but since I came in she hasn't stopped kissing my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> You didn't anoint my head with oil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A sign of hospitality and respect.">f</a></sup>but she poured perfume over my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> So I tell you, her many sins have been forgiven—that's why she loves so much.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This verse is sometimes understood as if it's the woman's love that brings the great forgiveness. However, the context (especially verse 43) makes it clear that it's the extent of forgiveness that engenders the great love.">g</a></sup>But whoever is forgiven little, only loves a little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> Then Jesus said to the woman, “Your sins have been forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> Those who were sitting eating with him began talking among themselves, saying, “Who is this who even forgives sins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> But Jesus told the woman, “Your trust has saved you, go in peace.”</span>
</div>
