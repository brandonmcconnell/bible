<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> Jesus entered Jericho and walked through the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> A man was there named Zacchaeus, a chief tax collector. He was very rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> He wanted to see who Jesus was, but because he was short he couldn't see over the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> So he ran ahead, and climbed up a sycamore tree to see Jesus as he passed by.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> When Jesus got there, he looked up and said, “Zacchaeus, come down quickly! I need to stay at your house today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> Zacchaeus climbed down quickly and was so happy to welcome Jesus to his home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> When the people saw this they all complained, “He's gone to stay with such a sinner!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> But Zacchaeus stood up and said before the Lord, “Look, I'm giving half of everything I own to the poor, and if I've cheated anybody, I'll pay them back four times as much!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> In response Jesus said, “Today salvation has come to this house, because this man has shown he is a son of Abraham too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> For the Son of man came to search for and save those who are lost.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> While they were still paying attention, Jesus told them a story for they were close to Jerusalem and the people thought that God's kingdom was going to become a reality right away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> “Once there was a nobleman who left home to go to a distant country to be crowned king there, and then to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> He called ten of his servants, divided money<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Money: actually one mina, worth 100 drachmas, equivalent to 100 days pay.">a</a></sup>equally among them, and told them, ‘Invest this money until I return.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his people hated him, and sent a delegation after him to say, ‘We won't have this man as king over us.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; PSALMS 2:4–6"> After he had been crowned king he returned. He ordered his servants brought to him. He wanted to know what profit they had made by investing the money he had given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> The first servant came in and said, ‘Lord, your money has earned ten times as much.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> ‘Well done! You're a good servant,’ said the king. ‘Since you proved yourself trustworthy in a very small matter, I'm placing you in charge of ten cities.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> The second servant came in and said, ‘Lord, your money has earned five times as much.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> ‘I'm placing you in charge of five cities,’ said the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> Another servant came in and said, ‘Lord, look, here's your money back. I kept it safe, wrapped up in a cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> I was afraid of you because you're a hard man. You take what doesn't belong to you, and you harvest what you didn't plant.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> ‘I'll judge you by your own words,’ the king replied. ‘You know I'm a hard man, who as you say, takes what doesn't belong to me, and I harvest what I didn't plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> Why then didn't you deposit my money in the bank, so that when I returned I could have had my money with interest?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> The king told those who were standing beside him, ‘Take the money away from him, and give it to the one who made ten times as much.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> ‘But master, he already has ten times as much,’ they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> The king said, ‘I tell you, to those who have, more will be given; but those who do not have, even what they have will be taken away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; PSALMS 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> And as for my enemies who didn't want me to be king over them, bring them here and kill them in front of me.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> After he'd finished telling them this story, Jesus left for Jerusalem, walking on ahead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> As he approached Bethphage and Bethany on the Mount of Olives, he sent two disciples, telling them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> “Go to the village farther on. As you enter it you'll find a colt tied up that no one has ever ridden. Untie it and bring it here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 24:1; PSALMS 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ just say, ‘The Lord needs it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> So the two disciples went and found that everything was just as Jesus had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> As they were untying the colt, its owners asked them, “Why are you untying the colt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> The disciples replied, “The Lord needs it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> They brought the colt to Jesus. Then they threw their cloaks over it, and sat Jesus on its back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> As he rode along, people spread their cloaks on the road.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> As he approached Jerusalem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Jerusalem,” implied.">b</a></sup>right where the road goes down from the Mount of Olives, the crowd of disciples all began shouting joyful praises to God at the top of their voices for all the miracles they had seen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; PSALMS 118:22–26"> “Blessed is the king who comes in the name of the Lord,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Psalms 118:26.">c</a></sup>they shouted. “Peace in heaven and glory in highest heaven.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 148:1.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> Some of the Pharisees who were in the crowd said to Jesus, “Teacher, stop your disciples from saying that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> But Jesus replied, “I tell you that if they keep quiet then the stones will shout!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; PSALMS 119:136"> But as he got closer, he saw the city and wept over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> “I really wish today that you, even you, had known the path that leads to peace!” he said. “But now it's hidden from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> The time is coming upon you when your enemies will besiege you, building ramps to attack you, encircling you and closing you in from every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> They will smash you to the ground, you and your children within you. They won't leave one stone on another within you, for you refused to accept salvation when it came to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> Jesus entered the Temple and began driving out all the people trading<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Particularly in reference to those selling animals for sacrifice.">e</a></sup>there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; PSALMS 93:5"> He told them, “Scriptures state that my house will be a house of prayer,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 56:7.">f</a></sup>but you've turned it into a den of thieves.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Jeremiah 7:11.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> He was teaching in the Temple every day. The chief priests, the religious teachers, and the leaders of the people were trying to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> But they couldn't find a way to do it because everyone appreciated him, hanging on his every word.</span>
</div>
</div>
