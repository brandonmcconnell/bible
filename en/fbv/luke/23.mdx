<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> The whole council rose and took him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> There they started to accuse him. “We found this man deceiving our nation, telling people not to pay taxes to Caesar, and claiming he is Messiah, a king,” they said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> “Are you the King of the Jews?” Pilate asked him.<br />“So you say,” replied Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, “I don't find this man guilty of any crime.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they insisted, saying, “He is inciting rebellion all over Judea with his teachings, from Galilee to right here in Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> When he heard this, Pilate asked, “Is this man a Galilean?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> When he discovered that Jesus came under Herod's jurisdiction, he sent him to Herod who was also in Jerusalem at the time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> Herod was very pleased to see Jesus since he had wanted to meet him for a long time. He had heard about Jesus and was hoping to see him perform a miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> He asked Jesus many questions, but Jesus did not answer him at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> The chief priests and religious teachers stood there, angrily accusing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; PSALMS 69:19–20"> Herod and his soldiers treated Jesus with contempt and mocked him. Then they placed a royal robe on him and sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; PSALMS 83:4–6"> From that day on Herod and Pilate were friends—before that they had been enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> Pilate called together the chief priests, rulers, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> and told them, “You brought this man before me, accusing him of inciting the people to rebellion. I've carefully examined him in your presence, and do not find him guilty of the charges you have brought against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Neither did Herod, for he sent him back to us. He has done nothing that demands he should be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> So I will have him flogged and then release him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Verse 17 is not in most early manuscripts.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they all shouted together, “Kill this man, and release Barabbas to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> (Barabbas had been put in prison for taking part in a rebellion in the city, and for murder.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate wanted to release Jesus, so spoke to them again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But they kept on shouting, “Crucify him! Crucify him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> Pilate asked them for the third time, “But why? What crime has he committed? I don't find any reason for executing him. So I will have him flogged and then release him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 22:12–13"> But they continued insisting with loud shouts, demanding that he be crucified. Their shouting succeeded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> and Pilate gave the sentence they demanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> He released the man imprisoned for rebellion and murder, but he sent Jesus to be put to death in accordance with their demands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “handed Jesus over to their will.” It does not say that Pilate handed Jesus over to the Jews, since Jesus was executed by the Romans, but that Pilate acceded to their demands for Jesus' death.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> As the soldiers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Implied.">c</a></sup>led him away, they seized a man called Simon of Cyrene, who had come in from the countryside. They put the cross on him and made him carry it behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> A large crowd followed him, along with women who were mourning and lamenting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:4; Song of Songs 5:8; Song of Songs 1:5"> Jesus turned to them and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, don't weep for me. Weep for yourselves and your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For the time is coming when they'll say, ‘Happy are those who are childless, and those who never had babies, and those who never nursed them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> They'll say to the mountains, ‘Fall down on us,’ and to the hills, ‘Cover us.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Hosea 10:8.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if they do this to wood that is green, what will happen when it's dried out?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Meaning that things become worse later.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> They also took two others who were criminals to be executed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> When they reached the place called the Skull they crucified him together with the criminals, one on his right, and the other on his left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> Jesus said, “Father, please forgive them, for they don't know what they're doing.” They divided up his clothes by throwing dice for them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Psalms 22:18.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> The people stood and watched and the leaders sneered at Jesus. “He saved others, let him save himself, if he is really God's Messiah, the Chosen One,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="SeePsalms 22:7.">g</a></sup>they said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> The soldiers also mocked him, coming up to him and offering him wine vinegar, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> “If you're the King of the Jews, then save yourself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> Above Jesus was a sign on which it was written, “This is the King of the Jews.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> One of the criminals hanging there joined in the insults against Jesus. “Aren't you the Messiah?” he asked. “Then save yourself—and us too!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other criminal disagreed and argued with him, “Don't you fear God even when you're suffering the same punishment?” he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> “For us the sentence is right since we're being punished for what we did, but this man didn't do anything wrong.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then he said, “Jesus, please remember me when you come into your kingdom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; PSALMS 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> Jesus replied, “I promise you today you will be with me in paradise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> By this time it was around noon and darkness fell over the whole land until three in the afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> The sun's light was shut out, and the Temple veil was torn in two.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> Jesus called out in a loud voice, “Father, I place myself in your hands.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 31:5.">h</a></sup>Having said this he breathed his last.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Myself”: Literally, “my spirit.” Breath and spirit are the same word.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> When the centurion saw what had happened he praised God and said, “Surely this man was innocent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> When all the crowds that had come to watch saw what happened they went home beating their chests in grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; PSALMS 38:11; Luke 23:55"> But all those who knew Jesus, including the women who'd followed him from Galilee, watched from a distance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> There was a man called Joseph who was good and honest. He was a member of the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> but he hadn't agreed with its decisions and actions. He came from the Jewish town of Arimathea, and was waiting expectantly for the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> Joseph went to Pilate and asked for Jesus' body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> Once he'd taken it down, he wrapped it in a linen cloth. He laid Jesus in an unused tomb cut into the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> It was preparation day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Meaning Friday.">j</a></sup>and the Sabbath would soon begin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> The women who had come with Jesus from Galilee had followed Joseph and had seen the tomb where Jesus' body had been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> They returned home and prepared spices and ointments.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="To anoint Jesus' body.">k</a></sup>But on the Sabbath they rested, observing the commandment.</span>
</div>
