<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:7–13; Luke 9:52; Numbers 11:24–26"> After this, the Lord appointed seventy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some early texts read “seventy-two.”">a</a></sup>other disciples, and sent them in pairs to every town and place that he planned to visit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36–38; John 4:35–38; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> “The harvest is large, but the number of workers is small,” he told them. “Pray to the Lord of the harvest to send workers to his harvest fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Acts 20:29; Ezekiel 2:3–6"> So get on your way: I'm sending you like sheep among wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:35; 2 Kings 4:29; Luke 10:4–12"> Don't take any money or a bag or extra sandals, and don't spend time chatting with people you meet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Matthew 10:12–13; Isaiah 57:19"> Whatever house you enter, first of all say, ‘May this house have peace.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Ephesians 5:6"> If there's a peaceful person living there, then your peace will rest on them; if not, it will return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Matthew 10:10–11; Galatians 6:6"> Stay in that house, eating and drinking whatever they give you, for a worker deserves to be paid. Don't go from house to house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Matthew 10:40; Luke 10:10"> If you enter a town and the people there welcome you, then eat what's set before you</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; John 3:5; Luke 10:11"> and heal those who are sick. Tell them, ‘God's kingdom has come to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 10:14; Acts 13:51"> But if you enter a town and the people there don't welcome you, go through their streets telling them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:9; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:26"> ‘We are wiping off even the dust from your town that clings to our feet to show you our disapproval.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Disapproval”—implied.">b</a></sup>But you should recognize this: God's kingdom has come.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:24; Lamentations 4:6"> I tell you, in the Day of Judgment it will be better for Sodom than for such a town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8; Ezekiel 26:1–26"> Shame on you Korazin! Shame on you Bethsaida! For if the miracles you saw happen had happened in Tyre and Sidon they would have repented a long time ago, and they would be sitting in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:22–25; Romans 2:1; Luke 12:47–48"> That's why in the judgment it will be better for Tyre and Sidon than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Matthew 4:13; Luke 13:28"> And you, Capernaum, you won't be exalted to heaven; you will go down to Hades.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:20; Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Anyone who hears you hears me, and anyone who rejects you rejects me. But anyone who rejects me rejects the one who sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Mark 16:17; Luke 9:1"> The seventy disciples returned in great excitement, saying, “Lord, even the demons do what we tell them in your name!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 12:7–9; Isaiah 14:12; Revelation 9:1"> Jesus replied, “I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 91:13; Mark 16:18; Romans 16:20"> Yes, I have given you power to tread on snakes and scorpions, and to overcome all the enemy's strength, and nothing will harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:15; Revelation 20:12; Hebrews 12:23"> But don't take delight that the spirits do what you tell them—just be glad that your names are written in heaven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Matthew 13:11–16; Matthew 11:25–27"> At that moment Jesus was filled with the joy of the Holy Spirit, and said, “I thank you Father, Lord of heaven and earth, for you hid these things from the wise and clever people and revealed them to children! Yes, Father, you were pleased to do it in this way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:27; John 1:18; John 6:44–46"> My Father has handed over everything to me. No one understands the Son except the Father, and no one understands the Father except the Son, and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17"> When they were by themselves Jesus turned to the disciples and told them, “Those who see what you're seeing should be really happy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> I tell you, many prophets and kings have wanted to see what you're seeing, but they didn't see, and wanted to hear the things you're hearing, but didn't hear.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> Once an expert in religious law stood up and tried to trap Jesus. “Teacher,” he asked, “What do I have to do to gain eternal life?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 3:21–22"> “What is written in the law? How do you read it?” asked Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 12:30–31; Matthew 22:37–40; Deuteronomy 6:5"> “You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and your whole spirit, and your whole strength, and your whole mind; and love your neighbor as yourself,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:5and Leviticus 19:18.">c</a></sup>the man replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5; Matthew 19:17"> “You're right,” Jesus told him. “Do this, and you will live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Romans 4:2; Leviticus 19:34"> But the man wanted to vindicate himself, so he asked Jesus, “And who is my neighbor?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 88:4; Luke 18:31; Lamentations 2:12"> Jesus replied, saying, “A man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho. He was attacked by robbers who stripped him and beat him, and left him for dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; PSALMS 69:20; Jeremiah 5:31"> It so happened that a priest was going the same way. He saw the man, but he passed by on the other side of the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 27:10; Acts 18:17; PSALMS 109:25"> Then a Levite came along. But when he got to the place and saw the man, he also passed by on the other side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; Jeremiah 38:7–13; Luke 7:13"> Finally a Samaritan man came along. As he passed by, he saw the man and felt sorry for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 12:20; PSALMS 147:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> He went over and treated the man's wounds with oil and wine, and bandaged them. Then he placed the man on his own donkey and took him to an inn where he took care of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 14:13; Romans 16:23"> The next day he gave two denarii to the innkeeper and told him, ‘Take care of him, and if you spend more than this, I'll pay you back when I return.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 7:42; Matthew 21:28–31; Matthew 17:25"> Which one of these three do you think was a neighbor to the man who was attacked by robbers?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> “The one who showed him kindness,” the man replied.<br />“You go and do the same,” Jesus told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> While they were on their way,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="To Jerusalem.">d</a></sup>Jesus arrived at a village, and a woman called Martha invited him to her home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Luke 8:35; John 12:3"> She had a sister called Mary, who sat at the Lord's feet and listened to his teaching.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="By doing this Mary identified herself as one of Jesus' disciples, and the fact that Jesus allowed this and defended her action shows that he accepted her as such. It may be that Martha's concern was more about this breaking of normal social protocol than for kitchen help, and that her complaint to Jesus was more of an excuse. Jesus' reply indicates that Mary's choice to be his disciple was more important, calling it the right thing that wouldn't be taken away from her.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> Martha was concerned about all that needed to be done to prepare the meal, so she came to Jesus and said, “Master, don't you care that my sister has left me to do all the work by myself? Tell her to come and help me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> “Martha, Martha,” the Lord replied, “you're worried and upset about all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 16:5–6; John 10:27–28"> But only one thing is really necessary. Mary has chosen the right thing, and it shall not be taken away from her.”</span>
</div>
</div>
