<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> As you know, many others have attempted to put down in writing the things that have been fulfilled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “achieved,” “accomplished.”">a</a></sup>that involve us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> They based their accounts on evidence from the earliest eyewitnesses and ministers of the Word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> and so I also decided that since I have followed these things very carefully from the beginning, it would be a good idea to write out an accurate account of all that happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> I have done this Theophilus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Theophilus” means “one who loves God.” Also in Acts 1:1.">b</a></sup>Your Excellency, so you can be certain that what you were taught is completely reliable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> During the time when Herod was king of Judea, there was a priest called Zechariah, who came from the Abijah priestly division. He was married to Elizabeth, who was also descended from Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> They both did what was right before God, being careful to follow all the Lord's commandments and regulations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> They had no children because Elizabeth wasn't able to have any, and they were both growing old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> While Zechariah was serving as a priest before God, on behalf of his priestly division,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> he was chosen by lot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A system of random choice like “choosing straws” was used because more wanted to serve than there were places available.">c</a></sup>according to priestly custom to enter the Temple of the Lord and burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> During the time of offering incense a large crowd of people were praying outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> An angel of the Lord appeared to Zechariah, standing to the right of the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> When Zechariah saw the angel, he was startled and became terrified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel told him, “Don't be afraid, Zechariah. Your prayer has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall call him John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> He will bring you joy and gladness, and many will celebrate his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> He will be great in the sight of the Lord. He will refuse to drink wine or other alcoholic drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he's born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> He will turn many Israelites back to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> He will go ahead of the Lord in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the fathers back to thinking about their children, and to turn those who are rebellious back to a right understanding—to prepare a people ready for the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Malachi 4:5-6.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> “How can I be sure about this?” Zechariah asked the angel. “I'm an old man, and my wife is getting old too.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> “I am Gabriel,” the angel replied. “I stand in God's presence, and I was sent to speak to you and give you this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> But since you didn't believe what I told you, you'll become dumb, unable to speak, until the appointed time when my words come true.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> Outside the people were waiting for Zechariah, wondering why he was taking so long in the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> When eventually he came out, he couldn't speak to them. They realized he'd seen a vision in the Temple, for though he could make gestures, he was completely unable to speak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> After he'd finished his time of service, he went back home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Some time later his wife Elizabeth became pregnant. She stayed at home for five months.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> “The Lord has done this for me,” she said, “now that he's taken away my disgrace in the eyes of others.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> In the sixth month of her pregnancy, God sent the angel Gabriel to a young girl called Mary who lived in the town of Nazareth in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> She was engaged to a man named Joseph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> The angel greeted her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In fact the angel uses the common form of greeting of the time that literally meant “Rejoice” but was actually equivalent to saying “Hello.” Even modern translations have trouble here, with the angel saying “Hail, favored woman” or “Greetings, O favored one,” neither of which seem particularly suitable. On the other hand an angel arriving and saying “Hi” hardly fits either…">e</a></sup>“You are very privileged,” he told her. “The Lord is with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> Mary was very puzzled at what he said, and wondered what this greeting meant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> “Don't worry, Mary,” the angel went on, “for God has shown his graciousness to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. You shall call him Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He will be very great, and he will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of David his father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. His kingdom will never come to an end.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> “How is this possible?” Mary asked. “I'm still a virgin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> He replied, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will cover you. The baby who is to be born is holy, and will be called the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Elizabeth, your relative, even she is pregnant in her old age. The woman that people said couldn't have children is already six months pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> Nothing is impossible for God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; PSALMS 119:38; PSALMS 116:16"> “Here I am, ready to be the Lord's servant,” said Mary. “May it happen to me just as you said.” Then the angel left her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> A little while later, Mary got herself ready and hurried up into the hills of Judea, to the town where</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Zechariah's house was. She called out to Elizabeth as she went in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary's voice, the baby jumped for joy inside her. Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> and cried out in a loud voice:<br />“How blessed you are among women, and how blessed will be the child born to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> Why am I so honored that the mother of my Lord should visit me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> As soon as I heard you call out in greeting, my baby jumped for joy inside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> How fortunate you are, because you are convinced that the Lord will do what he has promised you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; PSALMS 34:2–3; PSALMS 35:9"> Mary replied, “How I praise the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> I am so happy with God my Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> because he decided that I, his servant, was worthy of his consideration, despite my humble background. From now on every generation will say I was blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2–3; PSALMS 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> God Almighty has done great things for me; his name is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> His mercy lasts for generation after generation to those who respect him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “fear,” but in modern use this can have the idea of abject terror.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> With his power<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “strong arm.”">g</a></sup>he has broken to pieces those who arrogantly think they're so clever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; PSALMS 107:40–41"> He tears the powerful down from their thrones, and elevates those who are humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="PSALMS 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; PSALMS 34:10"> He fills the hungry with good things to eat, and he sends the rich away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="PSALMS 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> He has helped his servant Israel, remembering him in mercy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="PSALMS 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; PSALMS 132:11–17"> just as he promised our fathers, to Abraham and his descendants for ever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Mary stayed with her for three months and then returned home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> The time came for Elizabeth to have her baby and she gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> Her neighbors and relatives heard how the Lord had showed her great kindness, and they celebrated together with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> Eight days later they came to circumcise the boy. They planned to call him Zechariah after his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> “No,” Elizabeth said. “He shall be called John.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> “But there's nobody among your relatives who has this name,” they told her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> Through gestures they asked Zechariah, the boy's father, what he wanted to call his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> Zechariah motioned for something to write on. To everyone's surprise he wrote, “His name is John.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> Immediately he could speak again, and he started praising God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> All those living nearby were in awe at what had happened, and the news spread throughout the hill country of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> Everyone who heard the news wondered what it meant. “What will the little boy grow up to be?” they asked, for it was clear he was very special to God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “for the hand of the Lord was with him.”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> Zechariah, his father, filled with the Holy Spirit, spoke this prophecy:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="PSALMS 106:48; Luke 7:16; PSALMS 41:13"> “The Lord, the God of Israel, he is wonderful, for he has come to his people and set them free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="PSALMS 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> He has given us a great Savior from the line of his servant David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> as he promised through his holy prophets long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="PSALMS 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> He promised to save us from our enemies, from those who hate us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; PSALMS 106:45"> He was merciful to our fathers, remembering his holy agreement—</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; PSALMS 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> the promise that he made to our father Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> He gives us freedom from fear and rescues us from our enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> so we can serve him by doing what is good and right for our whole lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> Even though you are only a small child, you will be called the prophet of the Most High, for you will go ahead of the Lord to prepare his way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> providing knowledge of salvation to his people through the forgiveness of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> Through God's caring kindness to us, heaven's dawn will break upon us</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> to shine on those who live in darkness and under the shadow of death, and to guide us along the path of peace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Mark 1:3–4; Luke 2:52"> The boy John grew and became spiritually strong. He lived in the desert until the time came for his public ministry to Israel.</span>
</div>
