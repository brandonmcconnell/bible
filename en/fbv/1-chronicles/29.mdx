<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 3:7; 2 Chronicles 13:7"> Then King David said to everyone gathered there, “My son Solomon, chosen by God alone, is young and inexperienced, and the work to be done is great because this Temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Temple”: the word can also be translated as “palace” or “fortress.”">a</a></sup>will not be for man, but for the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:3–5; 2 Corinthians 8:3; Isaiah 54:11–12"> Using all my means I have provided for the house of my God—gold for the articles made of gold, silver for the silver, bronze for the bronze, iron for the iron, and wood for the wood; onyx stones and stones for settings: turquoise, different colored stones, every kind of precious stone; and plenty of marble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:24; PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 26:8"> More than this, because of my devotion to the house of my God, I'm now giving my personal fortune of gold and silver, in addition to everything that I have provided for this holy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:28; 1 Chronicles 22:14; Job 28:16"> 3,000 talents of gold—the gold of Ophir—and 7,000 talents of refined silver will go to cover the walls of the buildings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–3; Exodus 35:5–9; Ezra 7:15–16"> gold for the gold work, and silver for the silver work, and for all the work done by the craftsmen. Now who willingly wants to commit themselves to giving to the Lord today?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 27:25–1; Isaiah 60:3–10"> They gave willingly—the family leaders, those in charge of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials in charge of the king's work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–71; Ezra 2:69"> They gave to the service of the house of God 5,000 talents and 10,000 darics<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A daric was a Persian coin.">b</a></sup>of gold, 10,000 talents of silver, 18,000 talents of bronze, and 100,000 talents of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; 1 Chronicles 26:21–22"> Those who had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the Lord, under the supervision of Jehiel the Gershonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:3; 1 Kings 8:61"> The people celebrated because their leaders had been so willing to give to the Lord, freely and wholeheartedly. King David was also really delighted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 1:7"> Then David praised the Lord before the whole assembly:<br />“Praise to you, Lord, the God of Israel our father, for ever and ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Daniel 4:34–35; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Lord, yours is the greatness, the power, the glory, the splendor, and the majesty, for everything in heaven and on earth is yours. Lord, yours is the kingdom, and you are admired as ruler of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; 2 Chronicles 20:6; Deuteronomy 8:18"> Riches and honor come from you and you reign supreme. You possess power and might, and have the ability to make people great and to give strength to all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 106:1; 2 Corinthians 9:15; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> Now our God we thank you, and we praise you and your glorious character.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Romans 11:36"> But who am I, and who are my people, that we are able to give as willingly as this? For everything we have comes from you—we are only giving back what you have given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 144:4; Leviticus 25:23; PSALMS 102:11"> In your eyes we are strangers and foreigners, just like our forefathers. Our time here on earth passes like a shadow, we have no hope of staying here long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:14; PSALMS 24:1; 2 Chronicles 31:10"> Lord our God, even all this wealth that we have provided to build you a house for your holy name comes from what you give, and it all belongs to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Proverbs 11:20; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> I know, my God, that you look on the inside and are happy when we live right. Everything I have given willingly and with an honest heart, and now I have seen your people here giving happily and willingly to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 10:23; Hebrews 13:21"> Lord, the God of Abraham, Isaac, Israel, and our forefathers, please keep these thoughts and commitments in the minds of your people forever, and make sure they stay loyal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Make sure they stay loyal”: literally, “from their heart.”">c</a></sup>to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 72:1; 1 Chronicles 28:9; James 1:17"> Please also give my son Solomon a desire to whole-heartedly keep your commandments, decrees, and statutes, and to do all he can to build your Temple that I have provided for.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 95:6; Exodus 4:31; PSALMS 135:19–21"> Then David said to everyone there, “Praise the Lord your God!” So everyone praised the Lord, the God of their fathers. They bowed down in reverence to the Lord and to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:4–9; Numbers 15:7; Ezra 6:17"> The following day they presented sacrifices and burnt offerings to the Lord: one thousand bulls, one thousand rams, and one thousand lambs, with their drink offerings and plenty of sacrifices for all of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Chronicles 23:1; Exodus 24:11"> Then they ate and drank in the presence of the Lord with great happiness that day. They made Solomon, the son of David, king for a second time, and they anointed him as ruler for the Lord, and anointed Zadok as priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:12; 1 Chronicles 28:5; Romans 13:1"> So Solomon took the throne of the Lord as king in place of David, his father. He was successful, and all the Israelites obeyed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3–9; Ezekiel 17:18; Genesis 24:2"> All the officials and warriors, as well as all of King David's sons, gave a solemn promise of loyalty to King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; 2 Chronicles 1:1; Ecclesiastes 2:9"> The Lord made Solomon highly respected throughout Israel, and gave him greater royal majesty than had been given to any king of Israel before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:14; PSALMS 78:71–72"> So David, son of Jesse, ruled over all of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> He ruled over Israel forty years—seven in Hebron, and thirty-three in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Acts 13:36; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> David died at a good old age, having lived a long life, blessed with wealth and honor. Then his son Solomon took over and ruled in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 12:1–7; 1 Chronicles 21:9–11"> Everything that King David did, from beginning to end, is written down in the Records of Samuel the Seer, the Records of Nathan the Prophet, and the Records of Gad the Seer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:23; Daniel 2:21"> These include all the details of his reign, his power, and what happened to him, and to Israel, and all to the kingdoms of the neighboring countries.</span>
</div>
</div>
