<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; Ezra 6:16–18; 2 Chronicles 5:6–7"> They brought the Ark of God and placed it in the tent that David had prepared for it. They presented burnt offerings and friendship offerings to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> Once David had finished presenting the burnt offerings and friendship offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8"> Then he shared out to every Israelite, to every man and woman, a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:27–32; PSALMS 72:18; PSALMS 103:2"> David assigned some of the Levites to serve as ministers before the Ark of the Lord, to remember, to thank, and to praise the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> Asaph was the one in charge, Zechariah was second, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. They played harps and lyres, and Asaph struck the cymbals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:12; 2 Chronicles 29:26–28; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> and the priests Benaiah and Jahaziel blew the trumpets continually in front of the Ark of God's Agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1; PSALMS 18:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> This was the day that David first instructed Asaph and his relatives to express thanks to the Lord in this way:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“In this way”: implied. What follows is a selection from Psalms 105, Psalms 96, Psalms 107, and Psalms 106.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 105:1–15; Isaiah 12:4; 2 Kings 19:19"> Give the Lord thanks, worship his wonderful nature, let people know what he's done!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; PSALMS 96:1–3; PSALMS 98:1–4"> Sing to him, sing his praises; tell everyone the great things he's done!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 29:13; PSALMS 34:2"> Be proud of his holy character; be happy, all who come to the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:8–9; Zephaniah 2:2–3; PSALMS 68:35"> Look for the Lord, and his strength; always look to be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 111:4; PSALMS 119:20; PSALMS 103:2"> Remember all the wonderful things he's done, the miracles he's performed, and the judgments he's carried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; PSALMS 135:4"> descendants of Israel, children of Jacob, his chosen people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 48:10–11; Isaiah 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He is the Lord, our God, his judgments cover the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 105:8; Malachi 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> He remembers his agreement forever, the promise he made lasts for a thousand generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> the agreement he made with Abraham, the vow he gave to Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> The Lord legally confirmed it with Jacob, he made this everlasting agreement with Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Micah 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> saying, “I will give the land of Canaan to you for you to possess.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> He said this when there were only a few of you, just a small group of foreigners in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> They wandered from country to country, from one kingdom to the next.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He didn't let anyone treat them badly; warning kings to leave them alone:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; PSALMS 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> “Don't touch my chosen people; don't harm my prophets!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 96:1–13; PSALMS 71:15; Isaiah 51:6–8"> Sing to the Lord, all the earth, sing to the Lord! Each day let everyone know of his salvation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–6; Daniel 4:1–3; PSALMS 22:27"> Declare his glorious acts among the nations, the wonderful things he does among all peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 89:7; PSALMS 76:7; PSALMS 66:3–5"> For the Lord is great, and deserves the best praise! He is to be respected with awe above all gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Isaiah 42:5"> For all the gods of other nations are idols; but the Lord made the heavens!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 16:11; PSALMS 96:6; PSALMS 28:7–8"> Splendor and majesty are his; power and glory are in his sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; PSALMS 100:1–2"> Give the Lord credit, nations of the world, credit him with glory and strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 100:4; PSALMS 96:9; Isaiah 6:3"> Give the Lord the glory he deserves; bring an offering and come before him. Worship the Lord in his magnificent holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 33:9; PSALMS 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Let everyone on earth tremble in his presence. The world is held together firmly—it cannot be broken apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 96:10; Revelation 19:6; PSALMS 97:1"> Let the heavens sing for joy, let the earth be happy. Tell the nations, “The Lord is in charge!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 148:9–10; Isaiah 44:23; PSALMS 98:7–8"> Let the sea and everything in it shout with praise! Let the fields and everything there celebrate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 98:9; PSALMS 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Let all the trees in the forest sing for joy, for he is coming to judge the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 106:1; 2 Chronicles 7:3; PSALMS 107:1"> Say thank you to the Lord, for he is good! His trustworthy love continues forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; PSALMS 14:7"> Shout out, “Save us, Lord, our God! Bring us back together from among the nations, rescue us, so we can thank you and praise how magnificent and holy you are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 8:15; PSALMS 72:18–19; 1 Kings 8:56"> How wonderful is the Lord, the God of Israel, who lives forever and ever! Then all the people said, “Amen!” and “Praise the Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; Ezra 3:4; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> Then David made sure Asaph and his brothers would minister continually before the Ark of the Lord's Agreement, performing whatever services were needed every day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> as well as Obed-edom and his sixty-eight relatives. Obed-edom, son of Jeduthun, and Hosah, were gatekeepers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 15:11; 2 Chronicles 1:3–4"> David put Zadok the priest and his fellow priests in charge of the Ark of the Lord at the high place in Gibeon</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; 2 Chronicles 31:3"> to present burnt offerings to the Lord on the altar of burnt offerings, morning and evening, according to all that was written in the law of the Lord which he had ordered Israel to follow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> They were accompanied by Heman, Jeduthun, and the rest of those chosen and identified by name to give thanks to the Lord, for “His trustworthy love lasts forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 150:3–6; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; 2 Chronicles 29:25–28"> Heman and Jeduthun used their trumpets and cymbals to make music to accompany the songs of God. The sons of Jeduthun guarded the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; PSALMS 101:2"> Then all the people went home, and David went to bless his family.</span>
