<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> Let us bring the Ark of our God back<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Bring… back”: interestingly the verb root has the basic meaning of “surround.”">b</a></sup>to us, because we forgot about it during the time of Saul.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 8:5; 2 Samuel 3:36; 1 Samuel 18:20"> The whole assembly was pleased with the proposal, agreeing that this would be a good thing to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> So David summoned all of Israel, from the Shihor River of Egypt up to Lebo-hamath, to help bring the Ark from Kiriath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> So David and all of Israel went to Baalah (otherwise called Kiriath-jearim) in Judah to bring back the Ark of God the Lord, whose throne is between the cherubim and who is called by the Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> They loaded the Ark of God onto a brand new cart and brought it from Adinadab's house, with Uzzah and Ahio directing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 150:3–5; PSALMS 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> David and all of Israel were celebrating before the Lord as loudly as possible, singing songs and playing music on lyres, harps, tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> But when they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, the oxen stumbled and Uzzah stretched out his hand to stop the Ark falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> The Lord was angry with Uzzah for daring to touch the Ark like this so he struck him down, and Uzzah died there before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> David became angry with the Lord for his violent outburst against Uzzah. He named the place Perez-uzzah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Perez-uzzah means “outburst against Uzzah.”">c</a></sup>and it is still called that today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> David became afraid of God that day. “How can I ever bring back the Ark of God home to me?” he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> So David did not move the Ark of God to be with him in the City of David. Instead, he had it taken to the home of Obed-edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The Ark of God remained in Obed-edom's home for three months, and the Lord blessed Obed-edom's household and all that he had.</span>
</div>
