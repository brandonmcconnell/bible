<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; PSALMS 44:1"> This is a list of the divisions of the gatekeepers. From the Korahites: Meshelemiah son of Kore, one of the sons of Asaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> The sons of Meshelemiah: Zechariah (first), Jediael (second), Zebadiah (third), Jathniel (fourth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam (fifth), Jehohanan (sixth), and Eliehoenai (seventh).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> The sons of Obed-edom: Shemaiah (first), Jehozabad (second), Joah (third), Sacar (fourth), Nethanel (fifth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 128:1"> Ammiel (sixth), Issachar (seventh), and Peullethai (eighth), for God had blessed Obed-edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> Shemaiah, Obed-edom's son, had sons who were capable leaders and had great authority in their father's family</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad. His relatives, Elihu and Semakiah, were also capable men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these descendants of Obed-edom, plus their sons and grandsons, a total of sixty-two, were capable men, well qualified for their service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> Meshelemiah's eighteen sons and brothers were also capable men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Hosah, one of the sons of Merari, made Shimri as leader among his sons, though he was not the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> His other sons included Hilkiah (second), Tebaliah (third), and Zechariah (fourth). The total of Hosah's sons and relatives was thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> These divisions of the gatekeepers, through their family leaders, served in the house of the Lord, just like their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> Every gate was assigned by lot to different families, the least important equal to the most important.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The lot for the east gate fell to Meshelemiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 26:2. The Hebrew here reads “Shelemiah.”">a</a></sup>Then they cast lots for his son Zechariah, a wise counselor with insight, and the lot for the north gate fell to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> The lot for the south gate fell to Obed-edom, and the lot for the storehouse fell to his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> Shuppim and Hosah received the west gate and the gate of Shallecheth on the road that leads up. They were always guarded.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “guard of like guard.” The meaning is uncertain.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> There were six Levites on duty every day at the east gate, four at the north gate, four at the south gate, and two at a time at the storehouse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> Six were on duty every day at the west gate, four at the main road, and two at the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These were the divisions of the gatekeepers from the sons of Korah and the sons of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> Other Levites under Ahijah were in charge of the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of what had been dedicated to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> From the sons of Ladan, who were the descendants of the Gershonites through Ladan, and were the family leaders of Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> The sons of Jehieli, Zetham and his brother Joel, were in charge of the treasuries of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> From the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> Shebuel, a descendant of Gershom, son of Moses, who was the chief officer in charge of the treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> His relatives by Eliezer were Rehabiah, Jeshaiah, Joram, Zicri, and Shelomoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Shelomoth and his relatives were in charge of all the treasuries for all that had been dedicated by King David, by the family leaders who were the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and by the army commanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> They dedicated a part of the plunder they had won in battle to help maintain the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> Shelomoth and his relatives also looked after the gifts dedicated to the Lord by Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah. All the dedicated gifts were the responsibility of Shelomoth and his relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> From the Izharites: Kenaniah and his sons were given outside duties as officials and judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> From the Hebronites: Hashabiah and his relatives, 1,700 capable men, were put in charge of Israel west of the Jordan, responsible for everything involving the Lord's work and the king's service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> Also from the Hebronites came Jerijah, the leader of the Hebronites according to the family genealogies. In the fortieth year of David's reign the records were examined, and men of great ability were discovered at Jazer in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> Among the relatives of Jerijah were 2,700 capable men who were family leaders. King David put them in charge of the tribes of Reuben and Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh. They were responsible for everything involving the Lord's work and the king's service.</span>
