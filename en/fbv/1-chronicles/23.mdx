<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> When David was old, having lived a long life, he made his son Solomon king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> He also summoned all the leaders of Israel, the priests, and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> The Levites over thirty years old were counted, and there was 38,000 in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> “Of these 24,000 will be in charge of the work of the house of the Lord, while 6,000 will be officers and judges,” David instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; PSALMS 87:7"> “And 4,000 will be gatekeepers, while 4,000 will praise the Lord using the musical instruments I have provided for praise worship.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> David divided them into sections corresponding to the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> The sons of Gershon: Ladan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sons of Ladan: Jehiel (chief), and Zetham, and Joel, three in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran, three in total. These were the leaders of the families of Ladan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zizah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Zizah”: as per the Septuagint and Vulgate, Hebrew reads “Zina” (but note the following verse).">a</a></sup>Jeush, and Beriah—four in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Jahath (chief), and Zizah (second); but because Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons they were counted as a single family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—a total of four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was dedicated to service with the most holy things, that he and his sons should always present offerings to the Lord, and minister before him, and give blessings in his name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; PSALMS 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> As for Moses, the man of God, his sons were included with the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> The sons of Gershom: Shebuel (chief).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> The sons of Eliezer: Rehabiah (chief). Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah had many sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> The sons of Izhar: Shelomith (chief).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriah (chief), Amariah (second), Jahaziel (third), and Jekameam (fourth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Uzziel: Micah (chief) and Isshiah (second).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> Eleazar died without having sons, only daughters. Their cousins, the sons of Kish, married them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—three in total.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the descendants of Levi by family, the heads of families chiefs listed individually by name—those twenty years of age or more who served in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David said, “The Lord, the God of Israel, has given peace to his people, and he will live in Jerusalem forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> So the Levites don't need to carry the Tent any longer, or anything needed for its service.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; PSALMS 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> In accordance with David's final instructions, Levites twenty years of age or more were counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> Their assignment was to help the descendants of Aaron with the service of the house of the Lord They were responsible for the courtyards and rooms, for cleansing all the holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> They were also responsible for the showbread that was placed on the table, the special flour for the grain offerings, the unleavened bread, the baking, the mixing, and dealing with all amounts and measurements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> They also had to stand every morning to give thanks and praise to the Lord, and do the same in the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> and whenever burnt offerings were presented to the Lord whether on Sabbaths, new moons, and feast days. They were to serve regularly before the Lord according to the number required for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> So the Levites were to carry out the responsibility of caring for the Tent of Meeting and the sanctuary, and with their brothers the descendants of Aaron, they served the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
