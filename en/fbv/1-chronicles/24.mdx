<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:6; Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60"> The sons of Aaron were placed in divisions as follows. The sons of Aaron were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 10:2; Numbers 3:4; Numbers 26:61"> But Nadab and Abihu died before their father did, and they had no sons. Only Eleazar and Ithamar carried on as priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Samuel 21:1"> With the help of Zadok, a descendant of Eleazar, and Ithamar, a descendant Ahimelech, David placed them in divisions according to their appointed duties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 25:11–13; 1 Chronicles 15:6–12"> Because Eleazar's descendants had more leaders than those of Ithamar, they were divided like this: sixteen family leaders from the descendants of Eleazar, and eight from the descendants of Ithamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:31; Joshua 18:10; Acts 4:1"> They were divided by casting lots, without preference, for there were officers of the sanctuary and officers of God from both the sons of Eleazar and the sons of Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:16; Matthew 8:19; Nehemiah 8:1"> Shemaiah son of Nethanel, a Levite, was the secretary. He wrote down the names and assignments in the presence of the king, the officials, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the family leaders of the priests and Levites. One family from Eleazar and one from Ithamar were chosen in turn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 11:10; Nehemiah 12:19"> The first lot fell to Jehoiarib. The second to Jedaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:39; Nehemiah 7:35; Nehemiah 12:15"> The third to Harim. The fourth to Seorim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:17"> The fifth to Malkijah. The sixth to Mijamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:5; Nehemiah 12:4; Nehemiah 12:17"> The seventh to Hakkoz. The eighth to Abijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 12:10; Nehemiah 7:39"> The ninth to Jeshua. The tenth to Shecaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10"> The eleventh to Eliashib. The twelfth to Jakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The thirteenth to Huppah. The fourteenth to Jeshebeab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Ezra 10:20"> The fifteenth to Bilgah. The sixteenth to Immer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The seventeenth to Hezir. The eighteenth to Happizzez.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The nineteenth to Pethahiah. The twentieth to Jehezkel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The twenty-first to Jakin. The twenty-second to Gamul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The twenty-third to Delaiah. The twenty-fourth to Maaziah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Hebrews 7:11"> This was the order in which each group were to serve when they came into the house of the Lord, following the procedure defined for them by their forefather Aaron, as instructed by the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 23:16; 1 Chronicles 6:18"> These were the rest of the sons of Levi: from the sons of Amram: Shubael; from the sons of Shubael: Jehdeiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17; 1 Chronicles 26:25"> For Rehabiah, from his sons: Isshiah (first).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> From the Izharites: Shelomoth;<br />from the sons of Shelomoth: Jahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:31"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriah (first), Amariah (second), Jahaziel (third), and Jekameam (fourth).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:20"> The son of Uzziel: Micah; from the sons of Micah: Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:20; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 1 Chronicles 23:20"> The brother of Micah: Isshiah; from the sons of Isshiah: Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:21"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The son of Jaaziah: Beno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sons of Merari: from Jaaziah: Beno, Shoham, Zaccur and Ibri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> From Mahli: Eleazar, who did not have any sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> From Kish: the son of Kish, Jerahmeel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:23; 1 Chronicles 6:47"> The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth.<br />These were the sons of the Levites, according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5–6; 1 Chronicles 26:13; 1 Chronicles 25:8"> They also cast lots in the same way their relatives the descendants of Aaron did. They did this in the presence of King David, and of Zadok, Ahimelech, and the family leaders of the priests and of the Levites, the family leaders and their youngest brothers alike.</span>
</div>
