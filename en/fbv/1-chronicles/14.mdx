<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3; 2 Samuel 5:11–16; Ezra 3:7"> Then Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David along with cedar timber, stonemasons, and carpenters to build him a palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; PSALMS 89:20–37; 2 Samuel 7:16"> In this way David realized that the Lord had placed him on the throne as king of Israel and had blessed him by supporting his kingdom for the sake of the Lord's people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> David married more wives in Jerusalem, and had more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; 2 Samuel 5:14; Luke 3:31"> This is a list of the names of the children he had in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:15; 1 Chronicles 3:6"> Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 2 Samuel 5:16"> Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; PSALMS 2:1–6"> When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over all of Israel, they gathered their whole army to go after him. But David heard they were coming and went out to confront them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> The Philistines arrived and raided the valley of Rephaim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> David consulted God, asking “Shall I go and attack the Philistines? Will you make me victorious over them?”<br />“Go ahead,” the Lord told him, “I will make you victorious over them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> So David attacked and defeated them there at Baal-perazim. “God used me to defeat my enemies like a torrent of water that bursts out,” he declared. That's why the place was named Baal-perazim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Baal-perazim means “the Lord bursts through.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> The Philistines had left their gods behind, so David gave orders that they should be burned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> However, the Philistines returned and made another raid on the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; PSALMS 27:4"> David consulted God again. “Don't make a frontal attack,” God told him. “Instead go around behind them and attack them in front of the balsam trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> As soon as you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, go and attack for the Lord has gone before you to strike down the Philistine army.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> So David did what God told him, striking down the Philistine army all the way from Gibeon to Gezer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> As a result David's reputation was spread everywhere, and the Lord made all the nations afraid of David.</span>
</div>
