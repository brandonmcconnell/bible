<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:1–25; Zechariah 3:1; Job 2:4–6"> Satan interfered to cause trouble for Israel. He provoked David to do a census of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:23–24; 2 Samuel 3:10; 2 Samuel 24:15"> So David told Joab and the army commanders, “Go and count the Israelites from Beersheba to Dan. Then report back to me so I can have a total number.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Isaiah 26:15; 1 Chronicles 19:13"> But Joab replied, “May the Lord multiply his people a hundred times over. Your Majesty, aren't they all your subjects? Why do you want to do this? Why should you make Israel guilty?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; Daniel 3:18"> But the king was adamant so Joab left and went all over Israel. Eventually he returned to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:9; 1 Chronicles 27:23"> and he gave David the number of people censused. In Israel there were 1,100,000 fighting men who could handle a sword, and 470,000 in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:24; Numbers 1:47–49; 2 Samuel 20:9–10"> However, Joab did not include Levi and Benjamin in the census total, because he disagreed with what the king had ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; Joshua 7:5; 2 Samuel 21:14"> The Lord considered the census a bad thing to do and he punished Israel for it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; 1 John 1:9; Genesis 34:7"> Then David said to God, “I have committed a terrible sin by doing this. Please take away the guilt of your servant, for I have been very stupid.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 24:11; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> The Lord told Gad, David's seer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 13:21–22; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:29–31"> “Go and tell David that this is what the Lord says: ‘I'm giving you three options. Choose one of them, and that's what I'll do to you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20"> So Gad went and told David, “This is what the Lord says: ‘Make your choice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27; Acts 12:23; Ezekiel 14:19–21"> either three years of famine; or three months of devastation, running from the swords of your enemies; or three days of the Lord's sword—in other words three days of plague in the land, with an angel of the Lord causing destruction throughout the whole of Israel.’ Now you have to decide how I should reply to the one who sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 130:7; Isaiah 55:7; Philippians 1:23"> David replied to Gad, “This is an awful situation for me! Please, let the Lord decide my punishment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Let the Lord decide my punishment”: literally, “let me fall into the hands of the Lord.” Also at the end of the verse, “Do not let me fall into human hands.”">a</a></sup>for he is so merciful. Don't let me be punished by people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 2 Kings 19:35"> So the Lord a plague on Israel, and 70,000 Israelites died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:6; Exodus 32:14; Jeremiah 26:18"> God also sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem. But just as the angel was about to destroy it, the Lord saw it, and he relented from causing such a disaster. He told the destroying angel, “That's enough. You can stop now.” Right then the angel of the Lord was standing beside the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; 1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 6:17"> When David looked up and saw the angel of the Lord standing between earth and heaven, holding his drawn sword extended over Jerusalem, David and the elders, wearing sackcloth, fell on their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:63; 2 Samuel 24:17; John 10:11–12"> David said to God, “Wasn't it me who ordered the census of the people? I'm the one who has sinned and acted wickedly. But these sheep, what have they done? Lord my God, please punish me and my family, but don't punish your people with this plague.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1; 2 Samuel 24:18; 1 Chronicles 21:11"> Then the angel of the Lord told Gad to tell David to go and build an altar to the Lord on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:6; John 2:5; 2 Kings 5:10–14"> So David went and did what Gad had told him in the name of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 6:11"> Ornan was busy threshing wheat. He turned around and saw the angel; and his four sons who were with him went and hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; 2 Samuel 24:18–20"> When David arrived, Ornan looked out and saw David. He left the threshing floor and bowed down before David with his face to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:21; 1 Kings 21:2; Numbers 25:8"> David said to Ornan, “Please let me have the threshing floor. I'll buy it at its full price. Then I can build an altar to the Lord here so that the plague on the people may be stopped.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:22–23; 1 Samuel 6:14; Genesis 23:4–6"> “Take it, and Your Majesty can do whatever you want with it,” Ornan told David. “You can have the oxen for burnt offerings, the threshing boards for firewood, and the wheat for a grain offering. I'll give it all to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16–17; Genesis 14:23; Genesis 23:13"> “No, I insist, I will pay the full price,” replied King David “I won't take for the Lord what is yours or present burnt offerings that didn't cost me anything.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:24–25"> So David paid Ornan six hundred shekels of gold for the place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Judges 6:21; Proverbs 15:8"> David built an altar to the Lord there and presented burnt offerings and friendship offerings. He called on the Lord in prayer, and the Lord answered him with fire from heaven on the altar of burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:11; PSALMS 103:20; 1 Chronicles 21:15–16"> Then the Lord told the angel to put his sword back into its sheath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When David saw that the Lord had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he offered sacrifices there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:39; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 2 Chronicles 1:13"> At that time the tent of the Lord that Moses had made in the wilderness, and the altar of burnt offering, were at the high place in Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Hebrews 12:28–29; Revelation 15:4"> But David did not want to go there to ask God's will,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Ask God's will”: literally, “inquire of God.”">b</a></sup>because he was afraid of the sword of the angel of the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
