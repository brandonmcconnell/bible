<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1–13; 1 Chronicles 10:8; 2 Samuel 1:6"> The Philistines attacked Israel and all the Israelites soldiers ran away from them. Many Israelites were cut down on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; Exodus 20:5; 1 Samuel 14:6"> The Philistines chased down Saul and his sons. They killed Saul's sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:23–24; 1 Samuel 31:3–6; 2 Samuel 1:4–10"> The battle raged intensely around Saul. The enemy archers saw where he was, and they wounded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4; Judges 9:54; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Saul told his armor bearer, “Take out your sword and kill me before these heathen come and torment me.” But his armor bearer refused—he was too afraid to do it. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When his armor bearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his sword and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:18"> So Saul and three of his sons died there, along with his royal line.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “all of his house died together,” however this must be taken in the context that none of his sons succeeded him, for his son Ishbosheth did survive.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:2"> When all the Israelites in the valley saw that their army had run away, and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their towns and they also ran away. The Philistines came and occupied them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:8; 2 Kings 3:23; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> The following day, when the Philistines came to strip the dead, they discovered the bodies of Saul and his sons on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Judges 16:23–24; Matthew 14:11"> They stripped him, cut off his head, and took his armor. Then they sent the news throughout the land of Philistia, to their idols and their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–7; 1 Samuel 31:10"> They put Saul's armor in the temple of their idols and fixed his head to the temple of Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> However, when everyone in Jabesh-gilead heard everything the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; 2 Samuel 3:35; Genesis 50:10"> all their fighting men went and recovered the bodies of Saul and his sons. They brought them back and buried them under the large tree in Jabesh. Then they fasted for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 15:23"> Saul died because he was unfaithful to the Lord. He did not keep the Lord's commands, and he also went to consult a medium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 28:17"> He did not consult the Lord, so the Lord put him to death and he handed over the kingship to David, son of Jesse.</span>
