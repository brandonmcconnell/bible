<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 2 Samuel 12:26–31; Amos 1:14"> In spring, at the time of year when kings go out to make war, Joab led the Israelite army in attacks against the country of the Ammonites, also besieging Rabbah. However, David remained behind in Jerusalem. Joab attacked Rabbah and destroyed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> David took the crown from the head of their idol Milcom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Milcom”: or “their king.”">a</a></sup>It was made of gold, and was set with gems. It weighed a talent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent was equal to around 75 pounds.">b</a></sup>and was placed on David's head. David also took a great deal of plunder from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:2–5; 1 Kings 9:21; Judges 8:6–7"> David made the people there work with saws, iron picks, and axes. He also did the same to all the Ammonite towns. Then David and all his army returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> Some time after this war broke out with the Philistines at Gezer. But then Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, a descendant of the Rephaim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Rephaim”: a race of giants. Similar word is used in 20:8.">c</a></sup>and the Philistines were forced to submit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> In another battle with the Philistines, Elhanan, son of Jair, killed Lahmi, the brother of Goliath the Gittite. The shaft of his spear was as thick as a weaver's rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> In yet another battle at Gath, there was a gigantic man, who had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot, making twenty-four all. He too was descended from the giants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 16:9; Isaiah 37:23"> But when he insulted Israel, Jonathan, son of Shimea, David's brother, killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 14:12; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> These were the descendants of the giants in Gath, but they were all killed by David and his men.</span>
