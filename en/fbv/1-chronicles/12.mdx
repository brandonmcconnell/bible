<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2–6; 1 Chronicles 11:24–25; 1 Chronicles 8:33"> The following is a list of the men who joined David when he was at Ziklag, still hiding from Saul, son of Kish. They were some of the leading warriors who fought on David's side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> They were all skilled archers, and could shoot arrows or slingshots with their right or their left hands. They were relatives of Saul from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 21:6; 1 Chronicles 11:33"> Ahiezer was their leader, then Joash; the sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet the sons of Azmaveth; Beracah; Jehu the Anathothite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 15:36; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> Ishmaiah the Gibeonite (a strong warrior among the Thirty, and leader over the Thirty); Jeremiah; Jahaziel; Johanan; Jozabad the Gederathite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Eluzai; Jerimoth; Bealiah; Shemariah; Shephatiah the Haruphite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam (they were Korahites);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:58; 1 Chronicles 4:18; 1 Chronicles 4:39"> and Joelah and Zebadiah, the sons of Jeroham from Gedor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 17:10; 2 Samuel 23:20"> Some warriors from the tribe of Gad went over to David's side when he was at the stronghold in the desert. They were strong and experienced warriors, battle-hardened, experts in the use of shields and spears. Their faces looked as fierce as lions, and they ran as fast as gazelles in the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Ezer was the leader, Obadiah (second), Eliab (third),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Mishmannah (fourth), Jeremiah (fifth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Attai (sixth), Eliel (seventh),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Johanan (eighth), Elzabad (ninth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Jeremiah (tenth), Machbannai (eleventh).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8"> These warriors from Gad were army officers. The least able of them was in charge of 100 men; the best was in charge of 1,000.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Joshua 4:18; Jeremiah 49:19"> These were ones who crossed the Jordan River in the first month of the year when it overflows its banks. They chased out all the people living in the valley, both to the east and to the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Some others from the tribes of Benjamin and Judah also came to join David at the stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Jude 1:9; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> David went out to meet them and told them, “If you've come in peace to help me, we can be friends.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“We can be friends”: literally, “my heart will be together with you.”">a</a></sup>But if you've come to betray me to my enemies, even though I've done no wrong, then may the God of our fathers see what you're doing and condemn you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Judges 3:10; 2 Samuel 17:25"> Then the Spirit came upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Came upon”: literally, “clothed.”">b</a></sup>Amasai, the leader of the Thirty. “We are yours, David, and we are with you, son of Jesse! May peace, prosperity, and success<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Peace, prosperity, and success”: literally, “Shalom, shalom to you, and shalom whoever helps you.”">c</a></sup>be yours and those who help you, for God is the one who is helping you.” So David allowed them to join him, and put them in charge of his bands of raiders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:2–9"> Others came over to David's side from the tribe of Manasseh and joined him when he went with Philistines to attack Saul. However, the Philistine rulers eventually decided to send them away, saying to themselves, “It will cost us our heads if he deserts us and goes over to his master Saul.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; 1 Samuel 29:11"> The following is a list of the men from Manasseh who went over to David's side as he returned to Ziklag: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, leaders of thousands in Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1–17; 1 Chronicles 12:20; 1 Chronicles 5:24"> They helped David against raiders for they were all strong and experienced warriors and commanders in the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> Men arrived daily to help David until he had a large army, like the army of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> This is a list of the numbers of armed warriors who came and joined David in Hebron to turn over Saul's kingdom to him, as the Lord had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> From the tribe of Judah, 6,800 warriors carrying shields and spears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> From the tribe of Simeon, 7,100 strong warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From the tribe of Levi, 4,600,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> including Jehoiada, leader of the family of Aaron, and with him 3,700,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> and Zadok, a strong young warrior, with 22 members of his family, all officers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> From the tribe of Benjamin, from among Saul's relatives, 3,000, most of whom had remained loyal to Saul up until this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> From the tribe of Ephraim, 20,800 strong warriors, each highly regarded in his own clan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> From the half-tribe of Manasseh, 18,000 men were designated by name to come and make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> From the tribe of Issachar came leaders who knew and could understand the signs of the times and what Israel should do—a total of 200 leaders of the tribe together with their relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2; John 1:47"> From the tribe of Zebulun, 50,000 warriors. They were fully armed and battle-ready, and totally dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> From the tribe of Naphtali, 1,000 officers and 37,000 warriors carrying shields and spears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> From the tribe of Dan, 28,600 warriors, all battle-ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> From the tribe of Asher, 40,000 experienced warriors, all battle-ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> From the east side of the Jordan River, from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, 120,000 warriors carrying all kinds of weapons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; PSALMS 101:2"> All these men came to Hebron dressed for battle, completely committed to making David king. All of Israel agreed that David should become king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> They stayed three days there, eating and drinking together, for their relatives had provided them with supplies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Their neighbors, even as far away as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, arrived bringing food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen. They had plenty of flour, fig cakes, bunches of raisins, wine, olive oil, cattle, and sheep, for Israel was so happy.</span>
</div>
