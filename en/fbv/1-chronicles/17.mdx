<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 1 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Chronicles 17:5"> Once David had settled into his palace, he spoke to the prophet Nathan. “Look,” he said, “I'm living in a cedar palace while the Ark of the Lord's Agreement is kept in a tent!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zechariah 8:23"> “Do what you think you should, for God is with you,” Nathan replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> But that night God told Nathan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> “Go and talk to my servant David. Tell him, this is what the Lord says: You are not to build a house for me to live in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; 1 Kings 8:16"> I have not lived in a house from the time I led Israel out of Egypt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Out of Egypt”: implied—these words are not in the Hebrew text.">a</a></sup>until now. I have lived in tents, moving from place to place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> But in all those travels with all of Israel did I ever ask any Israelite leader I'd ordered to take care of my people, ‘Why haven't you built a cedar house for me?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; 1 Samuel 17:15"> So then, go and tell my servant David this is what the Lord Almighty says. It was me who took you from the fields, from looking after sheep, to become a leader of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; 1 Chronicles 17:2; PSALMS 113:7–8"> I have been with you wherever you've gone. I have struck down all your enemies right in front of you, and I will make your reputation as great as the most famous people on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Ezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> I will choose a place for my people Israel. I will settle them there and they won't be disturbed anymore. Evil people won't persecute them as they used to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> from the time I placed judges in charge of my people. I will defeat all of your enemies. Also I want to make it clear that I the Lord will build a house for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, the Lord would build a “house” for David in the sense of establishing a royal dynasty.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; 1 Kings 1:21; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> For when you come to the end of your life and join your ancestors in death, I will bring to power one of your descendants, one of your sons, and make sure his kingdom is successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> He will be the one to build me a house, and I will make sure his kingdom lasts forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 Chronicles 17:12"> I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to me. I will never take away my kindness and love from him, as I did in the case of the one who ruled before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; PSALMS 72:17"> I will put him in charge of my house and my kingdom forever, and his dynasty will last forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 7:17"> This is what Nathan explained to David—everything he was told in this divine revelation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> Then King David went and sat down in the presence of the Lord. He prayed, “Who am I, Lord God, and what is significant about my family, that you have brought me to this place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> God, you talk as if this was a small thing in your eyes, and you also have spoken about the future of my house, my family dynasty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“My family dynasty”: explaining the meaning of “house” in this context.">c</a></sup>You also see me as someone very important, Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 2:30; 2 Samuel 7:20–24"> What more can I, David, say to you for honoring me in this way? You know your servant all too well!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 Samuel 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> Lord, you're doing all this for me, your servant, and because it's what you want—doing all these amazing things and letting people know about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Jeremiah 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> Lord, there really is no one like you; there is no other God, only you. We have never heard about anyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> Who else is as fortunate as your people Israel? Who else on earth did God go and redeem to make his own people? You gained a wonderful reputation for yourself by all the tremendous, amazing things you did in driving out other nations before your people as you redeemed them from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> You made your people Israel your own forever, and you, Lord, have become their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:49; Jeremiah 11:5; Luke 1:38"> So now, Lord, please ensure that what you have said about me and my house happens, and lasts forever. Please do as you have promised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremiah 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> and may your true nature be recognized and honored forever, with people declaring, ‘The Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, is Israel's God!’ May the house of your servant David continue to be there in your presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; Ezekiel 36:37; PSALMS 10:17"> You, my God, have explained to me, your servant, that you will build me a house. That's why your servant has had the courage to pray to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> For you, Lord, are God! You are the one who has promised all these good things to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; PSALMS 72:17"> So now, please bless your servant's house that it may continue in your presence forever. For when you bless, Lord, it is blessed forever.”</span>
