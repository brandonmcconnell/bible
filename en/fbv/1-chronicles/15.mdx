<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; PSALMS 132:5"> Once David had finished building houses for himself in the City of David, he made a place for the Ark of God and set up a tent there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then he gave orders: “Nobody is to carry the Ark of God except the Levites, for the Lord himself chose them to carry the Ark of the Lord and to serve him forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> David summoned all of Israel to Jerusalem to bring the Ark of the Lord to the place he'd prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> This is the list of the Levites, the sons of Aaron, that David called to attend:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> From the sons of Kohath, Uriel (chief), and 120 of his relatives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> from the sons of Merari, Asaiah (chief), with 220 of his relatives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> from the sons of Gershom, Joel (chief), with 130 of his relatives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> from the sons of Elizaphan, Shemaiah (chief), with 200 of his relatives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> from the sons of Hebron, Eliel (chief), with 80 of his relatives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> from the sons of Uzziel, Amminadab (chief), with 112 of his relatives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> Then David summoned the priests Zadok and Abiathar, and the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> He told them, “You are the heads of the Levite families. You must make yourselves and your relatives ceremonially clean and pure<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Ceremonially clean and pure”: following the religious rules and requirements.">a</a></sup>before you bring back the Ark of God, the Lord of Israel to the place I have made for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> Because you weren't there the first time to carry the Ark the Lord our God burst out in violence against us. We didn't treat it in accordance with his instructions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> So the priests and the Levites purified themselves so that they could bring back the Ark of the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> Then the Levites carried the Ark of God in the way that Moses had ordered, according to what God had said—on their shoulders using the special carrying poles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> David also instructed the leaders of the Levites to assign from their relatives singers who would sing loudly for joy, accompanied by musicians playing lyres, and harps, and cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> So the Levites assigned Heman the son of Joel; and from his relatives Asaph the son of Berechiah; and from the sons of Merari, their relatives, Ethan the son of Kushaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> The second group of Levites consisted of Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, and Mikneiah; and the gatekeepers Obed-edom and Jeiel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> The musicians Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to strike the bronze cymbals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 46:1–11"> Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play harps “according to alamoth,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; PSALMS 81:1–2"> while Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to lead the music with lyres “according to sheminith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> Kenaniah, the leader of the Levites in singing, was chosen to lead the music because of his ability.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> Berechiah and Elkanah were appointed to guard the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer the priests were to blow the trumpets in front of the Ark of God. Obed-edom and Jehiah were also appointed to guard the Ark.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> Then David, the elders of Israel, and the senior army commanders,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Senior army commanders”: literally, “commanders of thousands.”">b</a></sup>went with great celebration to bring back the Ark of the Lord's Agreement from the home of Obed-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; PSALMS 66:13–15"> Because God helped the Levites who carried the Ark of the Lord's Agreement, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> David wore a robe of fine linen like all the Levites who were carrying the Ark, and the singers and Kenaniah the leader of the music and singers. David also wore a linen ephod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Ephod”: special clothing worn by priests.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> So all of Israel brought back the Ark of the Lord's Agreement with a lot of shouting, accompanied by horns, trumpets, and cymbals, and music played on harps and lyres.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> But as Ark of the Lord's Agreement entered the City of David, Saul's daughter Michal looked down from a window. Seeing David the king jumping and dancing for joy, she was full of contempt for him.</span>
</div>
</div>
