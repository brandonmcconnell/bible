<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:21; Zechariah 11:15–17; Ezekiel 34:2–10"> What trouble is coming to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture! declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 7:4"> This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says about the shepherds who were meant to look after my people: You have scattered my flock. You have chased them away. You didn't take care of them, so now I will take care of you for all the evil you've done, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 32:37; Isaiah 43:5–6"> I myself will gather what's left of my flock from all the countries where I exiled them, and I will bring them back to their pasture, where they will increase in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:14–15; 1 Peter 1:5; Ezekiel 34:23–31"> I will put shepherds in charge of them who will take care of them, and they won't be afraid or discouraged anymore, and none of them will be missing, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Isaiah 4:2; Isaiah 9:7"> Look, the time is coming, declares the Lord, when I will choose a descendant of David who does what is right. As king he will rule with wisdom and do what is just and right throughout the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> When he is king, Judah will be saved, and Israel will live in safety. This is the name he will be called: The Lord Who Makes Us Right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:14–15; Jeremiah 23:3; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Look, the time is coming, declares the Lord, when people won't say anymore, “On the Lord's life, who led the Israelites out of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 34:13"> Instead they'll say, “On the Lord's life, who led the Israelites back from the northern country and all the other countries where he had exiled them.” Then they'll live in their own country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Lamentations 3:15; PSALMS 60:3"> When it comes to the prophets: I'm really disturbed—I'm shaking inside! I stagger like a drunk, like someone who's had too much wine, because of what the Lord is like, because of his holy words.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jeremiah is alarmed at the contrast between what he knows of the Lord's nature and the degraded state of the nation—and what this means in terms of the coming disaster.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:2–3; PSALMS 107:34; Jeremiah 9:2"> For the country is full of people committing adultery so it's under a curse. The land mourns and the desert pastures have dried up. The people live evil lives, using their energy to do wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:4; Jeremiah 6:13; Matthew 21:12–13"> Both prophets and priests show no respect for me. I see wickedness even in my Temple, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:16; Jeremiah 11:23; PSALMS 35:6"> That's why their path will become slippery; they will be chased away in the dark and fall down. I'm going to bring disaster on them at the time when they're punished, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:8; Isaiah 9:16; 1 Kings 18:18–21"> I saw the prophets of Samaria doing something really offensive: They prophesied in the name of Baal and led my people Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 29:23; Ezekiel 22:25"> But now I see the prophets of Jerusalem doing something even more disgusting: They commit adultery and their lives are a lie. They support the wicked, so no one stops sinning. To me they're all like Sodom; the people of Jerusalem are like Gomorrah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:5"> So this is what the Lord Almighty says about the prophets: I will give them wormwood to eat and poisoned water to drink, because evil has spread across the country from the prophets of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Ezekiel 13:6; Galatians 1:8–9"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: Don't pay attention to what these prophets say when they prophesy to you. They're fooling you with visions they make up in their own minds. They're not from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 13:10; Amos 9:10"> They keep on telling people who don't respect me, “The Lord says that you'll live in peace,” and to everyone following their own stubborn attitude, “Nothing bad is going to happen to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:22; Isaiah 40:13–14; Amos 3:7"> But who of them has attended the Lord's council to hear and understand what he's saying? Who has paid attention to his instructions and followed them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:23; Jeremiah 25:32; Amos 1:14"> Watch out! The Lord has sent out a furious storm, a tornado swirling around the heads of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:24; Isaiah 55:11; Zechariah 1:6"> The Lord's anger won't fade until he's finished doing everything he wants. Only then will you really understand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:32"> I didn't send these prophets, but they run to deliver their messages. I didn't tell them to say anything, but they still go on prophesying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:18; Zechariah 1:4; Jeremiah 25:5"> Now if they had attended my council, they would have delivered my instructions to my people and brought them back from their evil way of life, from their evil actions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 139:1–10; 1 Kings 20:23; 1 Kings 20:28"> Am I only a local God and not a God who operates widely? asks the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; PSALMS 139:7; PSALMS 90:8"> Can people hide in secret places where I can't see them? asks the Lord. Don't I operate everywhere in heaven and on earth? asks the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:32; Jeremiah 23:28; Jeremiah 29:8"> I've listened to the prophets who prophesy lies in my name. They say, “I've had a dream! I've had a dream!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1–2; Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> How long will this continue? How long will these prophets go on prophesying these lies which are just the product of their own deluded minds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 8:33–34; Judges 3:7; Acts 13:8"> They think the dreams that they repeat to one another will lead my people to forget me, just like their forefathers forgot me by worshiping Baal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Timothy 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13"> A prophet who has a dream should say it's just a dream, but anyone I've spoken to should deliver my message faithfully. What is straw in comparison to grain? asks the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Jeremiah 5:14; Luke 24:32"> Doesn't my word burn like fire? asks the Lord. Isn't it like a hammer smashing a rock?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> Pay attention to this, declares the Lord. I'm opposed to those prophets who steal words from one another and then say it's a message from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; 2 Chronicles 18:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:5"> Pay attention to this, declares the Lord. I'm opposed to those prophets who make up their own stories<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Make up their own stories”: literally, “take their own tongues.”">b</a></sup>and then announce, “This is what the Lord says.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–18; Zephaniah 3:4; Lamentations 2:14"> Pay attention to this, declares the Lord, I'm opposed to those who prophesy fictional dreams. They tell them in order to lead my people into sin with their wild lies. I didn't send them or give them any instructions, and they don't do anybody any good, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Malachi 1:1; Jeremiah 12:7; Habakkuk 1:1"> So when a prophet or priest or anyone else comes and asks you, “What is ‘the burden of the Lord?’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“The burden of the Lord” in the sense of some requirement that the Lord is imposing on the people. Clearly the people saw the various laws and regulations as “burdens” and related to the Lord as this legalistic “burden-giver.” Though they lived evil lives it seems they thought that if they observed such “burdens,” including new ones, then the Lord would be satisfied.">c</a></sup>tell them, I'm not giving you a burden. I'm giving up on you, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Lamentations 2:14"> If a prophet or priest or anyone else claims, “This is the burden of the Lord,” I will punish that person and their family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Jeremiah 42:4; Jeremiah 33:3"> This is what everybody should say to their friends and relatives: “What answer has the Lord given?” or, “What has the Lord said?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; 2 Peter 3:16; Isaiah 3:8"> Don't talk about “the burden of the Lord” anymore, because everybody has different ideas about this “burden,” perverting the words of the living God, the Lord Almighty, our God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> This is what you are to ask any prophet: “What message has the Lord given you?” and “What has the Lord told you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:13–14"> If they say, “This is the burden of the Lord,” then this is the Lord's response: Because you said, “This is the burden of the Lord,” and I warned you not to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 13:13"> now I'm going to pick you up like a burden and throw you away, you and the city that I gave to you and your forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Ezekiel 5:14–15; Jeremiah 42:18"> I will disgrace you forever, your shame will never be forgotten.</span>
</div>
</div>
