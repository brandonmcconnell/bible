<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="JER" data-book-slug="jeremiah" data-book-name="Jeremiah" data-chapter="36" data-verses="32">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:1; 2 Chronicles 36:5; Jeremiah 45:1"> This message from the Lord came to Jeremiah in the fourth year of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:2; Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 1:10"> Get a scroll and write down everything I've told you condemning Israel, Judah, and all the other nations, from the time I first spoke to you during the reign of Josiah right up till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; Acts 3:19; Jeremiah 26:3"> Maybe when the people of Judah hear about all the disasters I plan to bring down on them, everyone will stop their evil ways. Then I will forgive their guilt and sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 36:32; Ezekiel 2:9"> So Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah to come, and while Jeremiah dictated, Baruch wrote down on a scroll everything that the Lord had told Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 33:1; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> Then Jeremiah gave Baruch these instructions: “I'm a prisoner here so I can't go into the Lord's Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:8–9; Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 22:2"> So you have to go to the Lord's Temple on a day when people are fasting, and read to them the Lord's messages from the scroll I dictated to you. Read them to all the people of Judah who come in from their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; Jeremiah 21:5; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> Maybe they will come and ask the Lord for forgiveness, and all of them will stop their evil ways, for the Lord is threatening terrible anger against them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:17; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Luke 4:16–30"> Baruch son of Neriah did exactly what Jeremiah the prophet had told him to do. He went and read the Lord's message from the scroll in the Temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Jonah 3:5; Esther 4:16"> This is how it happened.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>A fast to honor the Lord was declared involving all the people of Jerusalem and everyone who had come there from the towns of Judah. This was in the ninth month of the fifth year of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:11; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 52:25"> Baruch read from the scroll what Jeremiah had dictated so that everyone could hear. He read it from the room of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe. This was in the upper Temple courtyard at the New Gate entrance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:10; 2 Chronicles 34:20; 2 Kings 25:22"> When Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, heard all the Lord's messages read from the scroll,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:22; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 36:25"> he went down to the royal secretary's room in the king's palace, where all the officials had gathered. Elishama the secretary, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the other officials were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 22:10; 2 Chronicles 34:24; 2 Chronicles 34:16–18"> Micaiah gave them a report of everything he'd heard Baruch read from the scroll to the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:2; Jeremiah 41:18; Jeremiah 41:1–2"> The officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to summon Baruch, telling him, “Bring the scroll that you read to the people, and come here.” So Baruch went to see them taking the scroll with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “Please sit down and read it to us,” they said. So Baruch read it to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:18; Jeremiah 36:24; Amos 7:10–11"> After they'd heard everything, they were frightened and looked at each other. They said to Baruch, “We've got to tell the king about all this.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:15; John 9:10–11; John 9:26–27"> Then they asked Baruch, “Now tell us, how did you come to write all this? Did Jeremiah dictate it to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:2; Proverbs 26:4–5"> “Yes, he dictated it,” Baruch replied. “I wrote down in ink on the scroll everything he told me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 17:3; 1 Kings 18:10; Jeremiah 26:20–24"> The officials told Baruch, “You and Jeremiah are going to have to hide. Don't tell anyone where you are.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:12; Jeremiah 36:21"> Then the officials went to see the king in the courtyard. They had kept the scroll safe in Elishama the secretary's room while they gave a full report to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 22:10; 2 Chronicles 34:18; Ezekiel 2:4–5"> The king sent Jehudi to fetch the scroll. He went and got it from Elishama the secretary's room. Then Jehudi read it to the king and all the officials who were there standing next to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Jeremiah 22:14–16; Jeremiah 36:9"> It was the ninth month and the king was sitting in front of a fire in his winter quarters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 22:8; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Kings 22:27"> Whenever Jehudi finished reading three or four columns, Jehoiakim would chop them off with a scribe's knife and toss them into the fire. Eventually the whole scroll was completely burned up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:16; PSALMS 36:1; Jonah 3:6"> Despite hearing all these messages, the king and his attendants weren't frightened and didn't tear their clothes in remorse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:22; Jeremiah 36:12; Acts 5:34–39"> Even when Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah pleaded with the king not to burn the scroll, he refused to listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:3; 1 Kings 19:1–3; 1 Kings 19:14"> In fact the king ordered Jerahmeel, one of the princes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“One of the princes”: literally, “a son of the king,” but Jehoiakim would have been too young to have had a grown son.">b</a></sup>as well as Seraiah son of Azriel and Shelemiah son of Abdeel, to go and arrest Baruch and Jeremiah. But the Lord hid them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:4"> After the king burned the scroll Jeremiah had dictated to Baruch, a message from the Lord came to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 1:5–6; Job 23:13; Jeremiah 44:28"> Get another scroll and write everything out that was on the first scroll that Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 29:19"> Regarding Jehoiakim king of Judah announce that this is what the Lord says: You have burned the scroll and asked, “Why did you write that the king of Babylon is going to come and destroy this country and kill all its people and animals?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; 2 Kings 24:12–15; Genesis 31:40"> So this is what the Lord says about Jehoiakim king of Judah: He won't have anyone to succeed him as king, sitting on David's throne. His body will be thrown out to lie in the heat of the day and the cold of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Jeremiah 19:15; Jeremiah 35:17"> I'm going to punish him and his descendants and officials for their sins. I will bring down on them and on the people living in Jerusalem and Judah, all the disasters I warned them about but they refused to listen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:18; Exodus 4:15–16"> Jeremiah got another scroll and gave it to Baruch. Jeremiah dictated everything that was in the scroll that Jehoiakim had burned in the fire and Baruch wrote it down. Even more messages of a similar kind were added.</span>
</div>
</div>
