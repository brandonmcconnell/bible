<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremiah 14:11"> The Lord told me: Even if Moses and Samuel were standing before me pleading with me on behalf of these people, I wouldn't feel sorry for them. Send them away from me. Have them leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Zechariah 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> If they ask you, “Where shall we go?” tell them that this is what the Lord says: Those to be killed by the plague, to the plague; those to be killed by the sword, to the sword; those to be killed by famine, to famine; and those to be killed in captivity, to captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> I will put four kinds of destroyers in charge of them, declares the Lord: swords to kill, dogs to drag their bodies away, and the birds of prey and wild animals to eat them and destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 23:26–27; 2 Kings 24:3–4"> I will make all the kingdoms of the world horrified by them, because of the evil things Manasseh, son of Hezekiah, king of Judah did in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Nahum 3:7; Jeremiah 21:7"> Who will be sorry for you, Jerusalem? Who will mourn for you? Who will stop to ask you how you are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Zephaniah 1:4; Isaiah 1:4"> You have deserted me, declares the Lord. You have turned your back on me. So I will take action against you and destroy you; I am tired of showing you mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 41:16"> I will scatter you with a winnowing fork<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Winnowing fork”: a tool used to separate the chaff from the grain.">a</a></sup>from every town in the country. I will destroy my people and take away their children because they refuse to give up their evil ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 3:25–1; Jeremiah 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> There will be more widows than the sand of the sea. I will bring a destroyer at noon and mothers will lose their young sons. They will suddenly experience agony and shock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:5; Isaiah 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> A mother of seven children will collapse; she will gasp for breath. Her sun will set while it's still daytime; she will be ashamed and humiliated. I will let the enemies kill the rest of them, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> How sad I am, my mother, that you gave birth to me! I'm a victim of argument and conflict wherever I go in the country. I have never lent anyone anything, or borrowed anything, but still everyone curses me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 29:11–14; PSALMS 106:46"> But the Lord told me: Don't worry, I'm going to take away your problems so you can do good. I will make your enemies plead with you whenever they're in trouble or suffering.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “I will talk to your enemies for you whenever you're in trouble, whenever you're suffering.” The Hebrew is ambiguous.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremiah 21:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Can anyone break iron, iron from the north, or bronze?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:3; PSALMS 44:12; Isaiah 52:3"> I will give away your wealth and valuable possessions for free. They will become plunder for your enemies because of all the sins you committed throughout your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremiah 17:4"> Then I will have your enemies make you their slaves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Make you their slaves”: or “take you to.”">c</a></sup>in an unfamiliar country, because my anger will be like lighting a fire that will burn you up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:3; PSALMS 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> You know what's happening to me, Lord. Please remember me and look after me. Punish my persecutors! Please be patient—don't let me die! You know I put up with criticism because I want to honor you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:1–3; PSALMS 119:101–103"> When I received your messages, I ate them up! What you said made me really happy—I was delighted. I belong to you, Lord God Almighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; PSALMS 26:4–5"> I didn't join in with a bunch of jokers as they had fun. I stayed by myself because you have called me, and you have filled me with outrage.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Filled me with outrage”: at the actions of mocking partygoers and the nation generally.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:15; Job 34:6; Micah 1:9"> Why doesn't my pain ever stop? Why is my wound incurable? Why can't it be healed? You've really become like a seasonal stream to me, an unreliable source of water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> So this is what the Lord says: If you come back to me, I will take you back and you will serve me again. If what you talk about are worthwhile subjects and not nonsense, you will be my spokesman, Jeremiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Jeremiah”: supplied for clarity.">e</a></sup>They must be the ones who follow you; you must not follow them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Ezekiel 3:9"> Then I will make you a wall to these people, a strong wall of bronze. They will fight against you but they won't defeat you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See also 1:18-19.">f</a></sup>I am with you to save you and rescue you, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Isaiah 49:24–25; Jeremiah 50:34"> I will liberate you from the power of the wicked, and free you from the clutches of the cruel.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
