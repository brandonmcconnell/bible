<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15; Amos 3:6"> This is what the Lord says: Look! I'm going to stir up a destructive wind against Babylon and against the people of Babylonia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Leb-kamai,” a code word for Babylonia.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Matthew 3:12; Isaiah 41:16"> I will send foreign soldiers to attack Babylon to winnow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Winnowing” is the process by which grain is thrown into the air to that the wind can blow away the chaff.">b</a></sup>her and turn her country into a wasteland—they will attack her from all directions when her time of trouble comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:14; Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:21"> The archer doesn't need to use his bow; the infantryman doesn't need to put on his armor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew of this line has been interpreted in different ways. It seems most likely that it is saying that the attacking army was able to conquer Babylon without much trouble.">c</a></sup>Don't spare her young soldiers; set apart her whole army for destruction!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See footnote 50:21for the meaning of “set apart for destruction.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 50:30; Isaiah 13:15"> They will fall down wounded in her streets, killed in the country of Babylonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:1–2; Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 12:8"> Israel and Judah have not been deserted by their God, the Lord Almighty, even though they sinned against the Holy One of Israel everywhere in their country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:8; Jeremiah 25:14; Jeremiah 50:28"> Escape from Babylon! Run for your lives! Don't get caught up in her punishment and die, for this is the time when the Lord pays her back for her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 17:2"> Once Babylon was a golden cup the Lord held in his hand. She made the whole earth drunk. The nations drank her wine which is why they went mad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Isaiah 21:9; Revelation 14:8"> Now, all of a sudden, Babylon has fallen. She has been smashed to pieces. Weep for her; get her some treatment for her pain. Maybe she can be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:16; Revelation 18:5; Isaiah 13:14"> “We tried to heal her, but she couldn't be helped. So give up on her! All of us should go home to where we came from. The news of her punishment has reached everywhere, all the way to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:28; PSALMS 37:6; Isaiah 51:11"> The Lord has encouraged and supported us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Encouraged and supported us.” This is often translated as “vindication,” however this frequently has the meaning of being “proved right,” which is not the case here for the Israelites who went into exile because they were not right with God.">e</a></sup>Come on, let's tell people here in Jerusalem what the Lord has done for us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:45; Jeremiah 46:9"> Sharpen the arrows! Pick up the shields!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “Fill the quivers!”">f</a></sup>The Lord has encouraged the kings of the Medes, because his plan is directed at the destruction of Babylon. The Lord is paying them back for what happened to his Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:2; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:29"> Raise the signal flag to attack the walls of Babylon; strengthen the guard; have the watchmen take their places; get the ambush ready. The Lord planned and carried out his threats against the people of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:1; Isaiah 45:3; Revelation 17:15"> You people who live beside by many waters, and have so much wealth, this is the time of your end—your life will be cut short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Jeremiah 50:15; Jeremiah 49:13"> The Lord Almighty vowed by his own life, saying, I'm going to make sure to fill you with so many enemy soldiers they'll be like locusts. They will shout as they celebrate their victory over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Jeremiah 10:12–16; Jeremiah 32:17"> It was God who made the earth by his power. He created the world by his wisdom and by his understanding he put the heavens in place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 135:7; PSALMS 18:13; Jonah 1:4"> The waters of the heavens rain down with a roar at his command. He makes the clouds to rise all over the earth. He makes lightning to accompany rain, and sends the wind from his storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:14; Isaiah 44:18–20"> Everyone is stupid; they don't know anything. Every metalworker is embarrassed by the idols they make. For their images made of molten metal are fraudulent—they're not alive!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; Isaiah 19:1; Zephaniah 2:11"> They are useless, an object to be laughed at. They will be destroyed at the time of their punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 73:26; Jeremiah 10:16; PSALMS 33:12"> The God of Jacob is not like these idols, for he is the creator of everything, including his own people that are special to him. The Lord Almighty is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15–16; Micah 4:13; Isaiah 37:26"> You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“You”: referring to Babylon.">g</a></sup>are my war club, the weapon I use in battle. I use you to destroy nations; I use you to destroy kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Zechariah 10:5; Haggai 2:22"> I use you to destroy horses and their riders; I use you to destroy chariots and their drivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:18; 1 Samuel 15:3"> I use you to destroy men and women; I use you to destroy old men and youths; I use you to destroy young men and girls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57"> I use you to destroy shepherds and their flocks; I use you to destroy farmers and their cattle; I use you to destroy rulers and state officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:15; Revelation 18:24; Isaiah 63:1–4"> Right in front of you I'm going to pay back Babylon and everyone who live in Babylonia for all the evil they did to Jerusalem, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 8:8; Zechariah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:31"> Watch out, because I am against you, you destructive monster who devastates the whole world, declares the Lord. I will reach out to attack you; I will roll you down the cliffs; I will turn you into a mountain of ash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Jeremiah 51:43; Revelation 18:20–24"> Nobody will be even able to find themselves a cornerstone or a foundation stone among your ruins, because you will be so utterly destroyed, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Genesis 8:4; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> Raise a signal flag in the country! Blow the trumpet call to war among the nations! Get the nations ready to attack her; Summon the kingdoms to march against her: Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz. Choose a commander to lead the armies to attack her; send into battle cavalry like a swarm of locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 25:25; Esther 1:3"> Have the armies of the nations prepare for battle against her. This applies to the kings of the Medes, their leaders and all their officers, and every country they rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11–12; Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 8:16"> The earth quakes and trembles because the Lord is determined to carry out what he threatened against Babylon—to turn Babylonia into a wasteland where no one lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 50:36–37; Lamentations 2:9"> Babylon's defenders have given up fighting—they're just sitting in their fortresses. They're worn out; they've become like frightened women. Babylon's houses are burning; the bars securing her gates have been smashed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6; 2 Samuel 18:19–31; Esther 8:14"> A runner hands his message over to another to carry; one messenger follows another messenger, all of them alerting the king of Babylon to the news that his city has been completely conquered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:37–38; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 51:30"> the river crossings have been captured, the marshlands set on fire and his soldiers are panicking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 21:10; Hosea 6:11; Joel 3:13"> This is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says: The people of Babylon are like a threshing floor when the grain is trampled out. Her time of harvest will come very soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 51:44; Matthew 23:13"> Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon chewed me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Me”: referring to Jerusalem.">h</a></sup>up and sucked me dry, making me as empty as a jar with nothing in it. He swallowed me down as if he were some monster, filling himself with the tastiest parts of me and throwing the rest away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Jeremiah 50:29; Isaiah 26:20–21"> “Babylon must bear the responsibility for the violent attacks on us,” say the inhabitants of Zion. “The people of Babylonia must bear the responsibility for the blood shed in my city,” says Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:38; Romans 12:19; PSALMS 140:12"> This is what the Lord says: Watch as I present your case for you and make your enemies pay for what they did to you. I'm going to dry up her river and her springs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 18:21–23; Jeremiah 18:16"> Babylon will be turned into a pile of rubble, a home for jackals, a place that horrifies people, a place they jeer at, a place where no one lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; PSALMS 58:6; Judges 16:20"> The Babylonians will roar together like powerful lions and growl like lion cubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 25:27; Nahum 3:11"> But while their passions are aroused, I will serve them a banquet and get them drunk. They'll celebrate so much that they'll pass out and won't ever wake up, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 34:6; PSALMS 44:22"> I will take them down like lambs to be slaughtered, like rams and goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 49:25"> How could it be? Babylon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “Sheshach”: a code name for Babylon.">i</a></sup>has fallen! The most famous city in the world has been conquered! What a horrible sight Babylon has become to everyone watching!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 9:26; PSALMS 18:4"> It's as if the sea has flooded over Babylon, covering her in crashing waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 51:29"> The towns of Babylonia are in ruins, turned into a dry desert wasteland where no one lives—no one even passes by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 2:2"> I will punish Bel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Bel”: the major Babylonian god.">j</a></sup>in Babylon. I will force him to spit out what he swallowed. People of other nations won't run to worship him anymore. Even Babylon's wall has fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4; Isaiah 48:20"> My people, come out of her! Each one of you, save yourselves from the Lord's furious anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3–5; Isaiah 19:2; Isaiah 21:2–3"> Don't lose courage, and don't be afraid when you hear different rumors going around the country. There'll be one rumor one year, and another one the next, talking about violent revolution, of one ruler fighting against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 51:52"> Look, the time is coming when I will punish Babylon's idols. The whole country will be humiliated; it will be full of the dead bodies of those who have been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Isaiah 44:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> Then everyone in heaven and on earth will celebrate with shouts of joy at what's happened to Babylon, because the destroyers from the north will come and attack her, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Jeremiah 51:24; PSALMS 137:8–9"> Babylon has to fall because of the Israelites and people of other nations she killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:28; PSALMS 122:6; Revelation 18:4"> Those of you who have managed to escape being killed, leave now! Don't delay! Remember the Lord in this far away place; think about Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; PSALMS 44:13–16; PSALMS 74:3–7"> “We are embarrassed because we've been mocked, and we held our heads in shame because foreigners went into the holy places of the Lord's Temple,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="These words are spoken by the Israelites.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:47; Jeremiah 50:38; Daniel 5:30–31"> So keep watching, declares the Lord, because the time is coming when I will punish her for worshiping idols, and the sound of wounded people moaning will be heard throughout the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 11:4; Jeremiah 49:16; PSALMS 139:8–10"> Even if Babylon could climb up into the sky to strengthen her high fortresses, those I send to attack her will destroy her, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:22; Jeremiah 48:3–5; Jeremiah 50:46"> A cry comes from Babylon; the noise of terrible destruction comes from the country of Babylonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 15:1"> For the Lord is going to destroy Babylon; he will put an end to her boastful talk. The waves of attacking army will roar like the crashing sea; the noise of their shouts will echo all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="PSALMS 46:9; Jeremiah 51:48; PSALMS 94:1–2"> A destroyer is coming to attack Babylon. Her soldiers will be taken prisoner, and their bows will be smashed, for the Lord is a God who punishes fairly; he will definitely pay them back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 48:15"> I will make her leaders and wise men drunk, along with her commanders, officers, and soldiers. Then they will pass out, and won't ever wake up, declares the King, whose name is the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:13; Jeremiah 51:64; Jeremiah 51:44"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: Babylon's massive walls will be knocked down to the ground and her high gates burned. All that the people worked for will be for nothing; other nations who came to help will wear themselves out, only to see what they've done go up in flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 45:1"> This is the message that Jeremiah the prophet gave to Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, when he accompanied King Zedekiah of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of Zedekiah's reign. Seraiah was the king's personal assistant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:2–3; Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8"> Jeremiah had written on a scroll a description of all the disasters that would come to Babylon—all these words written here about Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Mark 13:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; 1 Thessalonians 4:18"> Jeremiah told Seraiah, “When you arrive in Babylon, make sure you read out loud everything written here,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 50:3; Ezekiel 35:9"> and announce, ‘Lord, you have promised to destroy this place so that none will be left—no people or animals. In fact it will be deserted forever.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Revelation 18:21; Jeremiah 19:10–11"> After you finish reading this scroll out loud, tie a stone to it and throw it into the Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Job 31:40; Nahum 1:8–9"> Then say, ‘This is how Babylon will sink and won't ever rise again, because of the disaster I'm bringing down on her. Her people will grow tired.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="This final sentence appears to be out of place.">l</a></sup>This is the end of Jeremiah's messages.</span>
</div>
