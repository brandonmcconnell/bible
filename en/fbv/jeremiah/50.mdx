<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26–27; Jeremiah 51:1–14; Isaiah 13:1–3"> This is the Lord's message that he told Jeremiah the prophet to give about Babylon and the country of Babylonia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The country of Babylonia”: literally, “the land of the Chaldeans.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:44"> Tell everyone the news! Hold up a sign and shout it out, don't hold back! Tell them, Babylon has fallen! Her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Since here and in Revelation the city of Babylon is identified with the female pronoun, the allusion is maintained here.">b</a></sup>god Bel is humiliated; the power of her god Marduk is broken; all Babylon's idols are humiliated and their power is broken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:62"> A northern nation will come and attack her and turn the country into a wasteland. No one will live there—both people and animals will desert it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31; Hosea 1:11; Hosea 3:5"> That's the time when the people of Israel and Judah will join together, weeping as they go to worship the Lord their God, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Hebrews 8:6–10; Isaiah 55:3"> They will ask the way to Zion, and start going in that direction. They will arrive and commit themselves to the Lord in an everlasting agreement that won't ever be forgotten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:14"> My people are sheep that are lost, led astray by their shepherds, making them wander aimlessly in the mountains. They go from place to place in the mountains and hills, forgetting where they used to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:8; Jeremiah 2:3; Zechariah 11:5"> All those who came across them attacked them. Their enemies declared, “We're not to blame! They're the ones who sinned against the Lord, their true resting place; the Lord who was the hope of their forefathers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4"> Run away from the city of Babylon; get away from the country of Babylonia! Lead the way like the male goats that lead the flock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 15:14; 2 Samuel 1:22"> Look! I'm assembling a coalition of strong northern nations who will come and attack Babylon. They will line up in battle against her; she will be conquered from the north. Their arrows will be like the best warriors—they don't miss!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Isaiah 33:23; Isaiah 33:4"> Babylonia will be plundered—everyone who plunders her will have plenty of loot, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Ezekiel 25:15–17; Ezekiel 26:2–3"> Though for now you Babylonians celebrate and sing triumphantly as you plunder my special people, though for now you jump around like a frisky young cow treading out the grain, and neigh like stallions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 49:2; Galatians 4:26"> you are going to bring shame on your mother, you are going to disgrace the one who gave birth to you. Watch as she becomes the least important of all the nations, a wilderness, a dry desert land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:17; Lamentations 2:15–16"> Because of Lord's angry punishment, she will be deserted, completely desolate. Everyone who passes by will be horrified at what has happened to Babylon, and sneer at all her wounds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Habakkuk 2:17; 1 Samuel 17:20"> All you archers, line up ready for battle around Babylon. Shoot at her! Don't save your arrows, because she has sinned against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Revelation 18:6; Jeremiah 46:10"> Shout war cries against her from every side! She holds up her hands in surrender. Her towers have collapsed; her walls are demolished. This is the Lord paying her back, so you too can pay her back—do the same to her as she did to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:16; Isaiah 13:14"> Stop the sower from sowing in the country of Babylon, and stop the harvester swinging the sickle to reap the grain. Under the threat of the enemies' swords, everyone runs home to their own people, they go back to where they came from.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; John 10:10–12; 2 Kings 18:9–13"> The Israelites are a flock that's been chased and scattered by lions. The first on the attack was the king of Assyria; then lastly Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon crushed their bones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; Zephaniah 2:13–15; Nahum 1:1–3"> So this is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says: I'm going to punish the king of Babylon and his country just like I punished the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Micah 7:14; Jeremiah 30:18"> I will lead the Israelites back to their pasturelands, to feed on Carmel and Bashan, to satisfy their appetites on the hills of Ephraim and Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 1:9"> This will be when the guilt and the sins of Israel and Judah will be looked for, but none will be found, because I will forgive those people who remain that I'm looking after, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23; 1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 50:3"> Go and attack the land of Merathaim, and the people living in Pekod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="These are not actual place names. They mean “double rebellion” and “punishment.” However, they are similar in sound to two locations in Babylonia.">c</a></sup>Kill them with swords, set them apart for destruction,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Set apart for destruction”: a special term that described a dedication to the Lord, indicating that what was promised was to be given over to the Lord and not retained.">d</a></sup>along with everything they leave behind. Make sure you do everything I have ordered you to do, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19–21; Jeremiah 51:54–56; Isaiah 21:2–4"> The noise of battle is heard in the country, the noise of terrible destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:4–6; Revelation 18:16"> See how the hammer of the whole earth is lying broken in pieces in the ground! The nations look with horror on what Babylon has become!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 9:4; Daniel 5:30–31; Job 40:2"> Babylon, I set a trap for you, and you were caught before you realized it. You were hunted down and captured because you fought against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:55; Jeremiah 51:25; Amos 3:6"> In his anger the Lord opened his armory to bring out his weapons, for this is what the Lord God Almighty is doing in the country of Babylonia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:10; Isaiah 45:3"> Come and attack her from every side! Open her granaries; collect the loot you take from her like piles of grain. Set her apart for destruction; don't leave any survivors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:21; Isaiah 34:7; Jeremiah 48:44"> Kill all her young bulls<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Bulls”: referring to the young men serving as soldiers for Babylon.">e</a></sup>with the sword; let them be slaughtered. What a disaster for them, because their time has come when they're punished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 50:15; Lamentations 1:10"> (Listen to the refugees and survivors who have returned from Babylonia, announcing in Zion, “The Lord our God is paying them back for what happened to his Temple.”)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:56; Isaiah 47:10; Daniel 4:37"> Call up the archers to attack Babylon, yes, all of them! Completely surround her—don't let anyone escape. Pay her back for what she's done, because in her pride she defied the Lord, Israel's Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 18:21; Jeremiah 9:21"> As a result her young men will be killed in the streets; all her soldiers will die that day, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Jeremiah 50:29; Ezekiel 39:1"> Watch out, because I'm against you, you arrogant people! declares the Lord God Almighty. Your time has come when I will punish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Jeremiah 49:27; Isaiah 10:12–15"> You arrogant people will trip and fall. Nobody will be there to pick you up. I'm going to set fire to your towns and burn up everything around you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 14:17; Exodus 9:2–3; Isaiah 58:6"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: The people of Israel and Judah are being mistreated. All who captured them are holding onto them, refusing to let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Jeremiah 15:21; Isaiah 41:14"> But the one who rescues them is powerful; the Lord Almighty is his name. He will defend them and their cause, so he may bring peace on earth, but trouble to the people of Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Daniel 5:30; Daniel 5:7–8"> A sword is raised to attack the Babylonians, declares the Lord, ready to attack those who live in Babylon, and her officials and wise men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:22; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 44:25"> A sword is raised to attack her false prophets, and they will become fools. A sword is raised to attack her soldiers, and they will be terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 51:21"> A sword is raised to attack her horses and chariots, along with all the foreign soldiers with her, and they will become like frightened women. A sword is raised to attack her stores of treasure, and they will be plundered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 50:2"> A drought has hit her rivers, and they will dry up. For it's a country full of pagan images. These horrible idols drive their worshipers mad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20–22; Revelation 18:2; Jeremiah 25:12"> Consequently desert animals and hyenas will live there, and it will be a home for owls. It will be uninhabited forever—it won't be lived in from one generation to the next.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 19:24–25; Jude 1:7; Isaiah 13:19–20"> In the same way that God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighboring towns, declares the Lord, no one will live there, no one will stay there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Isaiah 13:2–5"> Look! An army is advancing from the north. A great nation and many kings are coming against you from the distant lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Isaiah 13:17–18; Isaiah 47:6"> They're carrying bows and javelins. They are cruel and merciless. When they shout it's like the sea roaring. They ride on horses and attack in formation against you, people of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Jeremiah 49:24; Isaiah 21:3–4"> The king of Babylon has heard the news and he's terrified. He's overcome with fear, in pain like a woman in labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:19–21; Isaiah 46:11; PSALMS 89:6"> Watch out!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The following passage parallels that given against Edom in 49:19-21.">f</a></sup>I'm going to come like a lion from the tangled undergrowth beside the Jordan to attack the animals grazing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Attack the animals grazing”: supplied for clarity.">g</a></sup>the green pasture. In fact I'm going to chase the Babylonians from their land very quickly.<br />Who will I choose to conquer them? Who is like me? Who can challenge me? Which leader<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Leader”: literally, “shepherd.”">h</a></sup>could oppose me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:20; Jeremiah 51:10–11; Revelation 17:16–17"> So listen to what the Lord has planned to do to Babylon and the country of Babylonia: Their children will be dragged away like lambs from the flock, and because of them their pasture will become a wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:21; Revelation 18:9–19; Ezekiel 31:16"> The sound of Babylon's capture will make the earth shake; their cries will be heard throughout the nations.</span>
</div>
</div>
