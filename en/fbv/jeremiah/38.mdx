<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:9; Ezra 2:3; Acts 4:6–10"> Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jucal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Jehucal.” Jeremiah 37:3.">a</a></sup>son of Shelemiah, and Pashhur son of Malchijah heard what Jeremiah was telling everyone:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:18; Jeremiah 34:17; Jeremiah 45:5"> This is what the Lord says: Anyone who remains in this city will die from war and famine and disease, but anyone who goes over to the Babylonians will live. Their reward will be to save their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 32:3–5"> This is what the Lord says: Be sure of this—Jerusalem will be handed over to the army of the king of Babylon. He is going to capture it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:11; Acts 16:20; Nehemiah 6:9"> The officers told the king, “This man deserves to die because he's demoralizing the defenders left in the city, and all the people too, by telling them this. This man isn't trying to help these people, he's only going to destroy them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 2 Samuel 3:39; 2 Samuel 19:22"> “Well, you can do whatever you want to him,” King Zedekiah replied. “I can't stop you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 38:22; Jeremiah 37:16"> So they took Jeremiah and put him in the cistern belonging to Malchiah, the king's son, which was in the guard's courtyard. They lowered Jeremiah down with ropes into the cistern. It didn't have any water, only mud, and Jeremiah sank into the mud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Amos 5:10; Jeremiah 29:2"> Ebed-melech the Cushite,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Cush” is usually thought to refer to upper Egypt. “Ebed-melech” means “servant of the king.”">b</a></sup>a royal official in the king's palace, found out that they had put Jeremiah in the cistern. The king was sitting at the Benjamin Gate,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This probably means that the king was deciding legal cases.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> so Ebed-melech left the palace and went to talk to the king</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21–6; Esther 7:4–6; Proverbs 31:8–9"> “My lord the king, all these terrible things these men have done to Jeremiah the prophet are evil. They've put him in the cistern, and he'll starve to death there because there's no more bread left in the city.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 21:1; Esther 8:7; Esther 5:2"> So the king gave the order to Ebed-melech the Cushite, “Take thirty men with you and go and pull Jeremiah the prophet out of the cistern before he dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ebed-melech took the men and went to the storehouse under the palace. He took some rags and old clothes from there and then went to the cistern where he lowered them with ropes to Jeremiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:32; Romans 12:15"> Ebed-melech the Cushite called down to Jeremiah, “Put these rags and old clothes under your arms to protect you from the ropes.” Jeremiah did so,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Acts 28:16; Acts 23:35"> and using the ropes they pulled him up and took him out of the cistern. Jeremiah stayed there in the guard's courtyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; 1 Kings 22:16; Jeremiah 21:1–2"> Then King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah the prophet and met him at the third Temple entrance. “I need to ask you something,” the king told Jeremiah. “You mustn't hide anything from me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:67–68"> “If I tell you then you'll definitely have me killed,” Jeremiah replied. “Even if I were to give you advice, you wouldn't listen to me anyway.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:17; Jeremiah 38:1–6; Isaiah 57:16"> King Zedekiah solemnly promised Jeremiah in private, “As the Lord lives, who gave us this life, I won't have you killed, and I won't hand you over to those who want to kill you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:12; Jeremiah 27:17; Jeremiah 38:2"> Then Jeremiah told Zedekiah, “This is what the Lord God Almighty, the God of Israel, says: ‘If you hand yourself over to the officers of the king of Babylon, then you'll live. Jerusalem won't be burned down, and you and your family will survive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:3; Jeremiah 24:8–10; 2 Kings 25:4–10"> But if you don't hand yourself over to the officers of the king of Babylon, then this city will be given to the Babylonians. They will burn it down, and you yourself won't escape being captured.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:42; Jeremiah 38:22; John 19:12–13"> But King Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, “I am afraid of those people of Judah who have gone over to the Babylonians, because the Babylonians might hand me over to them so they could abuse me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 4:27; Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 26:13"> “They won't hand you over,” Jeremiah replied. “If you obey what the Lord says by doing what I tell you, then things will go well for you and you'll live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 2:4–5; Jeremiah 26:15"> But if you refuse to hand yourself over, this is what the Lord has told me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:6; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 5:11"> All the women who are left in the palace of the king of Judah will be led out and handed over to the officials of the king of Babylon Those women will say: ‘Those good friends of yours! You were taken in by them and conquered by them. Your feet got stuck in the mud, so they abandoned you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:10; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 38:18"> All your wives and children will be handed over to the Chaldeans. And you yourself will not escape, for you will be captured by the king of Babylon, and Jerusalem will be burned down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Zedekiah warned Jeremiah, “Nobody can find out about this conversation, otherwise you'll die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:27; Jeremiah 38:4–6"> If the officers find out that I've talked with you, and they come and ask you, ‘Tell us what you and the king were talking about! Don't hide anything from us, or we'll kill you;’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:20; Jeremiah 37:15; Esther 4:8"> then you are to tell them, ‘I was asking the king to grant my request not to return me to the house of Jonathan to die there.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:15–16; 2 Kings 6:19; Acts 23:6"> When all the officers came to Jeremiah wanting to know, he repeated to them exactly what the king had told him to say. So they didn't ask him anything more because no one had heard what had been said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 39:14; PSALMS 23:4"> Jeremiah remained there in the guard's courtyard until the day Jerusalem was captured.</span>
</div>
</div>
