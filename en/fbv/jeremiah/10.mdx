<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Jeremiah 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Listen to the message the Lord is sending to you, people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> This is what the Lord says: Don't adopt the practices of other nations. Don't be terrified as they are by signs in the heavens that they interpret as predicting disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> The religious beliefs of the peoples are pointless. They chop down a tree in the forest and a craftsman carves the wood with a tool to make an idol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; PSALMS 115:4"> They decorate it with silver and gold, and nail it down with a hammer so it won't fall over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> Just like a scarecrow in a field of cucumbers, their idols can't speak. They have to be carried around because they can't walk. You don't have to be afraid of them because they can't hurt you—and they can't do you any good either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 96:4; Exodus 15:11; PSALMS 48:1"> There's no one like you, Lord! You are so great! You are incredibly powerful!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; PSALMS 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Everyone should respect you, King of the nations. This is how you should be treated. There's no one like you among all the wise men of every nation and kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; PSALMS 115:8"> Yet these “wise men” are completely foolish and stupid, because they think they can be taught by useless idols made of wood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> Sheets of hammered silver are shipped in from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz, to be used by craftsmen and metal-workers. These idols are dressed in clothes of blue and purple made by experts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> But the Lord is the only true God. He is the living God and eternal King. The earth shakes when he is angry; the nations can't withstand his fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> This is what you are to tell the nations: “These gods, who didn't make either the heavens or the earth, will be wiped out from this earth and from under these heavens.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This verse which is addressed to foreign nations is written in Aramaic, the common language of the time.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> It was God who made the earth by his power. He created the world by his wisdom and by his understanding he put the heavens in place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> The waters of the heavens rain down with a roar at his command. He makes the clouds to rise all over the earth. He makes lightning to accompany rain, and sends the wind from his storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 135:16–18; PSALMS 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> Everyone is stupid; they don't know anything. Every metalworker is embarrassed by the idols they make. For their images made of molten metal are fraudulent—they're not alive!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> They are useless, an object to be laughed at. They will be destroyed at the time of their punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> The God of Jacob is not like these idols, for he is the Creator of everything, and Israel is the tribe that belongs to him. The Lord Almighty is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> You people living in Jerusalem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“People living in Jerusalem”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup>under siege, get all your things together ready to leave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> because this is what the Lord says: Look! Right now I'm about to throw out the people living in this country, bringing them trouble they will really feel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The meaning of the Hebrew of this last clause is uncertain.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> The people of Jerusalem responded,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“The people of Jerusalem responded”: supplied for clarity.">d</a></sup>“We're suffering terribly because we've been badly hurt—our injuries are really serious. We thought it wouldn't be that bad and that we could bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> Our tents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Tents” are symbolic of the houses in Jerusalem.">e</a></sup>have been destroyed; all our ropes have been broken. Our children have been taken from us and are no more. We don't have anyone left to put up our tents or hang our curtains.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> The “shepherds”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Shepherds”: referring to the nation's leaders.">f</a></sup>have become stupid—they don't ask the Lord for advice. That's why they have failed, and all their flock has been scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> Listen to the news that a noisy army is invading from a country to the north. The towns of Judah will be knocked down, they will be places where only jackals live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; PSALMS 37:23"> I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jeremiah is speaking.">g</a></sup>realize, Lord, that people don't control their own lives—no one really knows how to choose their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 38:1; PSALMS 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> Please discipline me fairly, Lord—not while you're angry, otherwise you'll kill me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out your fury on the nations that don't recognize you as God, and on their families that don't worship you. For they have completely destroyed the Israelites, wiping us out. They have devastated our country.</span>
