<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Micah 6:1; Isaiah 51:1–4"> Listen to the Lord's message, descendants of Jacob, all you Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Romans 1:21; Jeremiah 10:14–15"> This is what the Lord says: What did your forefathers think was wrong with me that they went so far away from me? <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Deuteronomy 8:14–16; Hosea 13:4"> They didn't ask themselves, “Where is the Lord who led us from Egypt, who led us through the wasteland, through a land of deserts and ravines, a land of drought and darkness, a land that no one travels through and where no one lives?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 16:18; Numbers 13:27"> I led you into a productive land to eat all the good things that grow there. But you came and made my land unclean, making it offensive to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 10:21"> Your priests did not ask, “Where is the Lord?” Your teachers of the law no longer believed in me, and your leaders rebelled against me. Your prophets prophesied by calling on Baal and followed worthless idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:35–36; Jeremiah 2:35; Exodus 20:5"> So I'm going to confront you again, declares the Lord, and I will bring charges against your children's children.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 120:5; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> Travel over to the islands of Cyprus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew: “Kittim.” The meaning is to go to the farthest west.">b</a></sup>and take a look; go to the land of Kedar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Kedar was far to the east.">c</a></sup>and examine carefully to see if anything like this has ever happened before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Isaiah 37:19; Jeremiah 16:20"> Has a nation ever changed its gods? —even though they're not gods at all! Yet my people have traded their glorious God for worthless idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:22; Isaiah 50:1; Genesis 15:3"> Are Israelites slaves? Were they born into slavery? So why have they become victims?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 9:11"> The young lions roared at you; they growled loudly. They have devastated your country; your towns lie in ruins. No one lives there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Jeremiah 44:1"> The men of Memphis<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Memphis”: Septuagint reading. </div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Leviticus 26:13; Jeremiah 17:2"> You broke off your yoke and ripped off your chains long ago. “I won't worship you!” you declared. On the contrary, you lay down like a prostitute on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:4; Exodus 15:17; PSALMS 44:2"> I was the one who planted you like the finest vine, grown from the very best seed. How could you degenerate into a useless wild vine?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Job 14:17; Hosea 13:12"> Even bleach and plenty of soap can't get rid of your guilty stains. I still see them, declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Proverbs 30:12; Proverbs 30:20"> How dare you say, “I'm not unclean! <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:6; Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:15"> You're a female donkey living in the desert, sniffing the wind for a mate because she's in heat. No one can control her at mating time. All those who're looking for her won't have trouble finding her when she's in heat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 3:13"> You don't have to run around barefoot or have your throat go dry. But you reply, “No, it's impossible! I'm in love with foreign gods—I must go to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Deuteronomy 32:37; Judges 10:14"> So where are these “gods” of yours that you made for yourselves? </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Daniel 9:11; Jeremiah 5:1"> Why are you complaining to me? It's all of you who have rebelled against me! declares the Lord.</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:7–9; Deuteronomy 32:15; Micah 6:9"> You people of today, think about what the Lord is saying: Israel, have I treated you like an empty desert, or a land of thick darkness? </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 106:21; Jeremiah 13:25; Jeremiah 3:21"> Does a girl forget her jewelry or a bride her wedding dress?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Dress”: probably a “sash.”">f</a></sup>Yet my people have forgotten me for too many years to count.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:7–10; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezekiel 16:27"> How cleverly you look for your lovers! Even prostitutes could learn something from you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Jeremiah 19:4; 2 Kings 24:4"> On top of that, your clothes are stained with the blood of the poor and the innocent. It's not like you killed them breaking into your homes.<br />Despite all this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 1 John 1:8–10; Job 33:9"> you go on saying, “I'm innocent! Surely he can't still be upset with me!”<br />Watch out! I'm going to punish you because you go on saying, “I haven't sinned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:22; Hosea 5:13; 2 Chronicles 28:16"> You're just so fickle—you keep on changing your mind! You will end up just as disappointed by your alliance with Egypt as you were with Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:19; Jeremiah 37:7–10; Jeremiah 17:5"> In fact you will go into exile with your hands on your head as prisoners, because the Lord will have nothing to do with those you trust; they will be no help to you.</span>
</div>
