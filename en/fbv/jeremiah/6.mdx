<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:14; Judges 1:21; Joshua 18:21–28"> Run and hide, descendants of Benjamin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jerusalem was part of the original territory of Benjamin.">a</a></sup>get out of Jerusalem! Blow the trumpet in Tekoa; light a signal fire in Beth-haccherem, for disaster and terrible destruction is arriving from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Isaiah 1:8; Isaiah 3:16–17"> Even though she is pretty and delightful, I will destroy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word used here usually means to “resemble,” but is used in the sense of “destroy” in Hosea 4:5.">b</a></sup>the Daughter of Zion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Jeremiah 39:1–3; 2 Kings 24:10–12"> “Shepherds” and their “flocks”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Clearly an allusion to the invading armies with their generals.">c</a></sup>will come to attack her; they will set up their tents all around her, each one looking after his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Zephaniah 2:4; Joel 3:9"> They get ready for battle against her, saying, “Come on, we'll attack at noon! Oh no, the day is almost over, the evening shadows are growing long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:13; Isaiah 32:14; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> Come on, we'll attack at night instead and destroy her fortresses!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremiah 32:24"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: Cut down the trees and make a siege ramp to use against Jerusalem. This city needs to be punished because it's full of people mistreating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:8; Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 7:11"> Like a spring gushing out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Gushing out”: or “keeping cool.”">d</a></sup>water, so she pours out her evil. The sounds of violence and abuse echo inside her. I see people sick and wounded everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:18; Hosea 9:12; Jeremiah 17:23"> I'm warning you, people of Jerusalem, I'm going to give up on you in disgust. I will destroy you and leave your country uninhabited.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Jeremiah 16:16; Obadiah 1:5–6"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: Even those left in Israel will be taken, just as grapes left on a vine are taken by the one harvesting the grapes who checks the branches again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 7:26; John 7:7"> Who can I give this warning to? Who's going to listen to me? Can't you see that they refuse to listen?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“They refuse to listen”: literally, “They have uncircumcised ears.”">e</a></sup>They can't hear what I'm saying. See how offensive the Lord's message is to them. They don't like it at all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; Jeremiah 20:9; Micah 3:8"> But as for me, I'm full of the Lord's anger; I have a hard time keeping it in.<br />The Lord replies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“The Lord replies”: supplied for clarity.">f</a></sup>Pour it out on children in the street, and on groups of young people, because both husband and wife are going to be captured—it's everyone, and it doesn't matter how old they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; Isaiah 5:25; Deuteronomy 28:39–43"> Their houses will be given to others, their fields and wives too, because I'm going to punish everyone living in this country, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:11; Jeremiah 8:10; Micah 3:11"> Everyone cheats because they're greedy, poor and rich alike. Even prophets and priests—they are all dishonest liars!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 23:17; Jeremiah 4:10"> They give my wounded people first aid, but they don't really care about them. They tell them, “Don't worry! We have peace!” even as war approaches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Matthew 15:14; Jeremiah 3:3"> Were they ashamed of the disgusting things they did? No, they weren't ashamed at all, they couldn't even blush. So they will fall just like the others, when I punish them; they will drop down dead, says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Deuteronomy 32:7; Jeremiah 18:15"> This is what the Lord says: Go and stand where the roads divide, and look. Find out which are the old paths. Ask, “What's the right way?” Then follow it and you'll be content.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“You'll be content:” literally, “you'll find rest for your souls.”">g</a></sup>But you refused, saying, “We won't go that way!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:4; Isaiah 58:1; Zechariah 7:11"> I put watchmen in charge of you and told you, Make sure you listen for the trumpet call warning you of danger. But you answered, “We won't listen!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 31:10; Micah 6:5"> So now you other nations can listen and find out what's going to happen to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 8:9; Jeremiah 17:10"> Earth, you listen too! I am bringing disaster down on this people, the end result of what they themselves planned. It's because they paid no attention to what I said, and rejected my instructions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11; Amos 5:21–22; Isaiah 60:6"> What's the point of offering me frankincense from Sheba or sweet calamus from a faraway land? I don't accept your burnt offerings; I'm not pleased with your sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Ezekiel 3:20; Jeremiah 13:16"> So this is what the Lord says: I'm going to put blocks in front of these people to trip them up. Fathers and sons will fall down dead, friends and neighbors too.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:41–43; Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 6:1"> This is what the Lord says: Look! An army is invading from the north; a powerful nation is getting ready to attack all the way from the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 13:18; Jeremiah 50:42; Luke 21:25–26"> They pick up their bows and spears. They are cruel and show no mercy. Their war cries are like the sea roaring, and they ride horses lined up ready to attack you, Daughter of Zion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 50:43; Jeremiah 49:24"> The people reply,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“The people reply”: supplied for clarity.">h</a></sup>“We've heard the news, and our hands are limp with shock! We're overcome with agony, suffering pain like a woman in labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 20:10; PSALMS 31:13"> Don't go to the field! Don't walk down the road! The enemy is armed with swords! Terror is everywhere!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Zechariah 12:10; Isaiah 32:11"> Oh my people, put on sackcloth and roll around in ashes. Mourn and cry bitterly like you would for an only son, because the destroyer will come down on you suddenly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Jeremiah 15:20; Ezekiel 3:8–10"> Jeremiah, I have made you a tester of metals so you can test my people as if they were metal, so you can know what they're made of and how they act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Jeremiah 5:23; Jeremiah 18:18"> They're all stubborn rebels going around talking slander. They are hard as bronze and iron; they are all corrupt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 4:12"> The bellows in the refiner's furnace blow powerfully, burning away the lead. But this refining is pointless, because the wicked are not purified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; PSALMS 119:119; Isaiah 1:22"> They are identified as impure silver to be rejected, because the Lord has rejected them.</span>
</div>
</div>
