<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:13–15; Zephaniah 2:8–11; Ezekiel 25:2–10"> This is what the Lord says about the Ammonites:<br />Don't the Israelites have any children? Don't they have heirs to inherit their property? So why has Milcom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Milcom”: the name of the principal Ammonite god, also identified as “Molech.”">a</a></sup>taken over the territory belonging to the tribe of Gad? Why are his people living in their towns?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19; Ezekiel 21:20; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Watch out! The time is coming, declares the Lord, when I will signal the attack on the Ammonite town of Rabbah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Rabbah”: the Ammonite capital, known today as Amman, Jordan.">b</a></sup>It will be turned into a pile of ruins, and its villages will be burned down. Then the Israelites will drive out the people who took over their land, says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:37; Isaiah 15:2"> Weep, people of Heshbon, for the town of Ai has been destroyed. Cry for help, people of Rabbah! Put on clothes made of sackcloth and start mourning; run to and fro inside your city walls, because your god Milcom will go into exile along with his priests and leaders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 9:23; Jeremiah 21:13"> Why do you boast that your valleys are so productive, you unfaithful people? You trust in your wealth, saying, “Who would dare attack us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 15:8"> Watch out! I'm going to bring the surrounding nations to terrorize you, declares the Lord God Almighty. You will all be driven out and scattered, and no one will be able to gather you refugees together again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 48:47; Ezekiel 16:53"> However, later on I will bring you Ammonites back from exile, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Jeremiah 25:21; Genesis 36:15"> This is what the Lord Almighty says about Edom:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="For a parallel prophecy against Edom see Obadiah, who uses similar language.">c</a></sup><br />Aren't there any wise people left in Teman?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Teman was a district in the country of Edom.">d</a></sup>Isn't there any good advice from those with insight? Has their wisdom rotted away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 46:21; Jeremiah 49:30"> Turn and run away! Find somewhere to hide, people of Dedan, because I'm bringing disaster down on you descendants of Esau when I punish you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:5–6; Isaiah 17:6"> If people harvesting grapes came to you, they'd leave some behind, wouldn't they? If thieves came during the night, they'd only steal what they wanted, wouldn't they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:14; Malachi 1:3–4; Isaiah 45:3"> But I'm going to strip the whole country bare, leaving its people with nowhere to hide. All of Esau's descendants will be destroyed, along with their relatives and friends—they will all be gone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 68:5; Hosea 14:3; James 1:27"> However, you can leave your orphans to me because I will protect them. Have your widows put their trust in me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:28–29; Obadiah 1:16; Jeremiah 25:15"> This is what the Lord says: If those who were not required to drink from the cup of judgment had to do so, how could you not be punished yourselves? You won't go unpunished, because you have to drink it too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Isaiah 34:6; Genesis 36:33"> I made myself a solemn promise, declares the Lord, that the town of Bozrah will become a place that horrifies people, a complete humiliation, a pile of ruins, and a name that's used as a curse word. All its surrounding towns will also be left in ruins forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:4; Isaiah 37:7; Ezekiel 7:25–26"> I received this message from the Lord. He has sent a messenger to the nations:<br />Get yourselves ready to attack Edom! Prepare for battle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Obadiah 1:2; Micah 7:10"> Watch as I will make you insignificant compared to other nations—everyone will look down on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25; Amos 9:2; Job 39:27"> The fear you once created in others, and your pride in yourselves, has fooled you into overconfidence, you people living on the rocky mountain tops. Though you make your homes high out of reach like an eagle's nest, I will tear you down, even from there, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:7; Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 49:13"> People will be horrified at what's happened to Edom. Everyone passing by will be shocked, and will sneer at all its damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:33; Deuteronomy 29:23; Jeremiah 50:40"> Just as Sodom and Gomorrah were destroyed, along with their neighboring towns, says the Lord, nobody will live there—they will become uninhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Jeremiah 30:21; Jeremiah 50:44–46"> Watch out! I'm going to come like a lion from the tangled undergrowth beside the Jordan to attack the animals grazing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Attack the animals grazing”: supplied for clarity.">e</a></sup>the green pasture. In fact I'm going to chase the Edomites from their land very quickly.<br />Who will I choose to conquer them? Who is like me? Who can challenge me? Which leader<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Leader”: literally, “shepherd.”">f</a></sup>could oppose me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 49:7"> So listen to what the Lord has planned to do to Edom and the people of Teman: Their children will be dragged away like lambs from the flock, and because of them their pasture will become a wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:46; Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 14:4–15"> When they fall, the sound they make will make the earth shake; their cries will be heard all the way to the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:40–41; Hosea 8:1; Isaiah 13:8"> Watch as an enemy like an eagle flies high, then swoops down, spreading its wings as it attacks Bozrah. At that time Edom's warriors will be as scared as a woman in labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 18:34; Genesis 15:2"> A prophecy about Damascus:<br />The towns of Hamath and Arpad<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Two towns some considerable distance north of Damascus.">g</a></sup>are disturbed, because they've received bad news. They are fearful, restless like the sea. Nothing can calm their worries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The people of Damascus are demoralized—they turn and run away in panic, overcome by pain and anguish like a woman in labor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Jeremiah 51:41; Isaiah 1:26"> Why isn't the city that is praised deserted, the city that made me happy?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some believe this verse is not spoken by the Lord but by one of the city's residents.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:30; Amos 4:10; Jeremiah 11:22"> For on that day its young men will die in its streets, all its defenders will be killed, declares the Lord of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; 1 Kings 15:18–20; Jeremiah 43:12"> I'm going to set fire to the walls of Damascus; that will burn down the fortresses of Ben-hadad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Genesis 25:13; Isaiah 11:14"> A prophecy about the land of Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor that were attacked by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. This is what the Lord says:<br />Go and attack Kedar; destroy the people of the east!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Jeremiah 6:25; Habakkuk 3:7"> Take their tents and their flocks! Carry off their tent curtains and all their possessions! Take their camels for yourselves. Shout out to them: “Terror is everywhere!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 27:6; Isaiah 10:7"> Run!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This verse is addressed to those being attacked.">i</a></sup>Get away as far as you can! Find somewhere to hide, people of Hazor, declares the Lord. For Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has made plans to attack and destroy you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:11; Numbers 23:9; Deuteronomy 33:28"> Go and attack<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The Lord is speaking to Nebuchadnezzar again.">j</a></sup>that self-satisfied nation that thinks it's safe, declares the Lord. They don't have any barred gates and have no allies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 49:36"> Their camels and large herds will be plunder for you. I will scatter them everywhere, these desert people who trim their hair on the sides of their heads. I will bring disaster down on them from all directions, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:22; Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 51:37"> Hazor will become a place where jackals live, a place abandoned forever. No one will live there; it will become uninhabited.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 10:22; Daniel 8:2; Jeremiah 25:25"> This is the Lord's message that came to Jeremiah the prophet about Elam. This was at the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6; Jeremiah 51:56; PSALMS 46:9"> This is what the Lord Almighty says:<br />Look! I'm going to smash the bows of the Elamites, the weapon they rely on for their power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:32; Revelation 7:1; Ezekiel 5:10"> I will bring winds from all directions to attack Elam, and I will scatter them in all directions. There won't be a nation that doesn't have some of Elam's exiles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:16; Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 49:22"> I will smash the Elamites in front of their enemies, before those who want to kill them. In my furious anger I will bring disaster down on them, declares the Lord. I will chase them down with the sword until I have destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Daniel 7:9–14"> I will set up my throne in Elam, and destroy its king and officials, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> However, later on I will bring the Elamites back from exile, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
