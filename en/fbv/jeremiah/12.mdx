<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 3:15; PSALMS 92:7; PSALMS 37:1"> Lord, when I complain to you, you always show yourself to be in the right. Even so I still want to present my case to you. Why is it that wicked people do so well? Why do those who are unfaithful to you live so comfortably?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:8"> You planted them, and they've taken root, grown, and produced fruit. They're always talking about you but they don't think of you, even for a moment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:18; 2 Kings 20:3; PSALMS 17:3"> But you know me, Lord, you see me, and examine what I think about you. Drag off these people like sheep to be slaughtered; keep them apart for the time when they're killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:10; PSALMS 107:34; Jeremiah 4:25"> How long will the land have to mourn and the grass in every field be dried up because of the evil of the people who live there? The animals and birds have died out because the people have said, “He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“He” could apply to the Lord or to Jeremiah, either refuting God's foreknowledge or Jeremiah's prophetic claims.">a</a></sup>doesn't know what's going to happen to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 24:10; Hebrews 12:3–4"> The Lord says,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“The Lord says”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup>Once you've become worn out in a foot race against men, how would you win racing against horses? If you trip up in open ground, how would you do in the tangled undergrowth beside the Jordan?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:25; Jeremiah 9:4; Micah 7:5–6"> Even your own brothers and your father's family have betrayed you; they have publicly criticized you. Don't trust them when they talk nicely to you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:6; Jeremiah 11:15; PSALMS 78:59–60"> I have given up on my people; I have abandoned the nation I chose. I have handed over the ones I truly love to their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:15; Jeremiah 2:15"> They've turned into a wild lion, roaring at me—that's why I hate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:9; Jeremiah 7:33; 2 Kings 24:2"> My people are like a spotted bird of prey<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Spotted bird of prey”: or “hyena.”">c</a></sup>to me with other birds of prey circling to attack it. Go and bring all the wild animals to eat up the carcass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–7; Jeremiah 23:1; Isaiah 63:18"> Many shepherds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Shepherds”: referring to the leaders of invading armies.">d</a></sup>have come and destroyed my vineyard; they have trampled down the crops in my field. They have turned my pleasant land into an empty wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Isaiah 42:25; Jeremiah 14:2"> They have made it into a desert; it mourns before me, lying desolate. The whole country is a wasteland, but no one cares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Amos 9:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> The destructive armies have crossed all the bare desert hills for the Lord's sword destroys from one end of the country to the other. No one has any peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:38; Haggai 1:6"> My people sowed wheat but harvested thorns. They wore themselves out but gained no benefit. You should be ashamed of such a poor harvest, caused by the Lord's furious anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Zechariah 2:8; Zechariah 10:6–12"> This is what the Lord says: When it comes to these evil nearby nations who attack the country that I gave to my people Israel, I'm going to uproot them from their land. I'm also going to uproot the people of Judah from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:39"> However, once I have uprooted them, I will have mercy on them again, and bring each one back to their property and their land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Joshua 23:7; Zephaniah 1:5"> If they will honestly learn the ways of my people and respect me, making their vows to me, just as they once taught my people to swear by Baal, then they will do well among my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 60:12; PSALMS 2:8–12; Daniel 11:4"> But if they refuse to obey, then I will not only uproot that nation but I will completely destroy it, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
