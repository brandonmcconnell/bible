<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; 2 Samuel 24:11–12"> This is what the Lord says: Go to the palace of the king of Judah and give this message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; Jeremiah 22:4"> Tell them: Listen to what the Lord has to say to you, king of Judah, sitting on the throne of David, you and your officials and the people here with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:17"> This is what the Lord says: Do what is fair and right. Protect those who are being unjustly treated by corrupt people. Don't do anything wrong to foreigners, orphans, or the widows. Don't use violence against them. Don't kill innocent people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25"> If you will honestly do what I tell you, then kings who sit on David's throne will ride on chariots and horses with their officials through the gates of this palace. They'll be accompanied by the people of Judah and those living in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> But if you refuse to obey what I say, then I swear by myself, declares the Lord, that this palace will be turned into rubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 27:10"> This is what the Lord says about the royal family of the king of Judah: You are as dear to me as the forests on Gilead and on the mountains of Lebanon. But I will turn you into a desert, into towns where no one lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Isaiah 10:33–34; Jeremiah 4:6–7"> I will choose men to come and destroy you, each with his own ax. They will chop down your fine cedars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The palace was made of many large cedars. See 21:14.">a</a></sup>and throw them in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; 2 Chronicles 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> Foreigners from many nations will pass by this city and ask each another, “Why has the Lord done such terrible things to this great city?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 2 Chronicles 34:25; Jeremiah 50:7"> People will answer, “Because they broke the agreement of the Lord their God. They went and worshiped other gods.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremiah 22:11; Isaiah 57:1"> Don't weep over the king that died. Don't mourn for him. Instead weep for the king who is exiled, who will never return, who will never see his homeland again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Chronicles 36:1–4; 2 Kings 23:34"> This is what the Lord says about Jehoahaz<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here called “Shallum.”.">b</a></sup>of Josiah, king of Judah. He succeeded his father Josiah but was taken away. He will never return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> He will die in exile; he will never see this country again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Micah 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> Trouble is coming to Jehoiakim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jehoiakim is not actually mentioned by name until verse 18.">c</a></sup>because he mistreats others in building his palace, by dealing unfairly with those constructing the upper floors. He makes his own people work for nothing—he doesn't pay them any wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> He says to himself, “I'm going to build myself a great palace, with large upper rooms.” He has windows inserted, puts in cedar panels, and paints it bright red with vermilion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> Does it make you a king just because you have more cedar than anyone else? Your father had food and drink, didn't he? He ruled fairly and honestly, and he had a good life because of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Isaiah 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> He defended the poor and those in need, and so things went well. Isn't this what knowing me really means? declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Jeremiah 22:3"> But all you're looking for, all you think about, is getting whatever you want, however dishonestly. You kill the innocent, you violently mistreat and exploit your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:30; Jeremiah 16:6; Jeremiah 16:4"> So this is what the Lord says about Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, king of Judah: They won't mourn for him, saying: “How sad, my brother! How sad my sister!” They won't mourn for him, saying: “How sad, my lord! How sad, his majesty!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; 1 Kings 21:23–24; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> His burial will be that of a donkey. He'll be dragged off and thrown away outside the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Ezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> Go to Lebanon and cry for help! Shout in Bashan! Scream from Abarim! For all your lovers have been destroyed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="All the places mentioned are mountains, so the image is of a call being shouted out from there. The lovers would most likely refer to those nations with which an alliance had been made.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> I warned you when you thought you were safe and sound. But you replied, “I'm not going to listen!” That's been your attitude since you were young—you never did what I told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30–31; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> The wind will blow away all your “shepherds,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Shepherds”: a reference to the leaders of the nation.">e</a></sup>and your lovers will go into exile. Then you will be shamed and disgraced because of all the evil things you've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:24; Jeremiah 4:30–31; Jeremiah 22:6"> You who live in “Lebanon” in your cedar nest,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Another reference to Solomon's cedar palace.">f</a></sup>how much you're going to groan when agonizing pains hit you like a woman in labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Songs 8:6; 1 Chronicles 3:16"> The Lord said to Jehoiachin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Here called “Coniah.” Also verse 28.">g</a></sup>son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah: As I live, declares the Lord, even if you were a signet ring on a finger of my right hand, I would pull you off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> I'm going to hand you over to those who terrify you and who want to kill you, to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and the Babylonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> I'm going to throw you out—you and the mother who gave birth to you—sending you to another country. Neither of you were born there, but both of you will die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; 2 Kings 25:27–30; PSALMS 86:4"> You won't ever return to the country you love so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> Who is this man Jehoiachin? A broken pot that's been thrown away, something nobody wants? Why have he and his children been thrown out, exiled in an unfamiliar country?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> My country, my country, my country! Listen to what the Lord has to say!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; PSALMS 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> This is what the Lord says: Put this man down as having no children. He's a man who won't ever be successful in his whole life. None of his children will ever be successful either. None of them will sit on David's throne or be king in Judah.</span>
