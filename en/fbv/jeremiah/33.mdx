<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> A second message came from the Lord to Jeremiah while he was still being held in the courtyard of the guard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 37:26"> This is what the Lord says, the Lord who made the earth, the Lord who shaped it and set it in place, the Lord is his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Isaiah 65:24; Ephesians 3:20"> Call out to me, and I will answer you, explaining to you amazing, hidden things about which you don't have any idea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Habakkuk 1:10; Ezekiel 4:2"> For this is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says about Jerusalem's houses and the palaces of the kings of Judah that were demolished for materials to be used to defend against the siege ramps and the enemy attacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 21:4–7"> They come to fight the Babylonians but will only fill those houses with the dead bodies of those I'm going to kill in my furious anger. I have given up on this city because of all its wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:10; Isaiah 30:26; Titus 3:5–6"> But even so, in the future I will restore and repair it, and I will heal its people and give them lasting peace and safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 30:3; Amos 9:14–15"> I will bring Judah and Israel back from exile and will make them as strong as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Micah 7:18–19; PSALMS 51:2"> I will wash away all their sins that they committed against me, and I will forgive all their guilt from when they sinned in rebelling against me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2–3; Isaiah 60:5; Zephaniah 3:17–20"> Then this city will bring me a glorious reputation, celebrated and praised by all the nations of the earth who get to hear of all the good things I do for it. They will tremble, amazed at how much good I have done for it, how I have made it so prosperous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:43; Jeremiah 32:36; Ezekiel 37:11"> This is what the Lord says: You call this place “a wasteland where there are no people or animals.” Well, here in the towns of Judah and in the empty streets of Jerusalem where no people or animals live, one day</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; PSALMS 106:1; PSALMS 107:22"> the sounds of joy and celebration will be heard there again, the happy voices of bride and bridegroom, and the shouts of praise of those bringing thank offerings to the Lord's Temple, saying: “Thank the Lord Almighty! For the Lord is good; his trustworthy love lasts forever.” For I will bring the land back from its “captivity” too, says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Ezekiel 34:12–15; Jeremiah 36:29"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: In this wasteland where there are no people or animals, and in all its towns, once again there'll be pastures where shepherds can take their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:32; Luke 15:4; Jeremiah 17:26"> In all the towns, whether in the hill country, or the foothills, or the Negev, or the land of Benjamin or the towns around Jerusalem, or throughout the cities of Judah, flocks will once again be counted by their shepherds, says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–9; Jeremiah 23:5; 1 Chronicles 17:13–14"> Look! The time is coming, declares the Lord, when I will keep my promise to do good to the people of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1–5; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 4:2"> At that time, right there and then, I will give you a good king from the line of David.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “I will cause to sprout for David a righteous Branch.”">a</a></sup>He will do what is just and right throughout the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Isaiah 45:17"> This is when Judah will be saved, and the people of Jerusalem will live in safety. This is the name he will be called: The Lord Who Makes Us Right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; PSALMS 89:29–37; Luke 1:32–33"> This is what the Lord says: David will always have a descendant who will be king of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Revelation 1:6; Deuteronomy 18:1"> and the Levitical priests will always have a descendant to present burnt offerings, grain offerings, and sacrifices to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> A message from the Lord came to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 8:22; PSALMS 89:37; Isaiah 54:9–10"> This is what the Lord says: If you were able to break my agreement with the day and with the night, so that they wouldn't come at the right time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 89:34; 2 Chronicles 7:18; 2 Samuel 23:5"> only then would my agreement be broken with David my servant and with the Levites who serve as my priests, so that David would not have a descendant to rule on his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Hebrews 11:12"> In the same way that the stars of heaven can't be counted, and the sand on the seashore can't be measured, that's how many times I will multiply the number of my servant David's descendants and the Levites who serve me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Another message from the Lord came to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; PSALMS 83:4; PSALMS 44:13–14"> Have you heard what people are saying: “The Lord chose two families, but now he's rejected them”? So they look down on my people and don't consider them worthy of being called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Worthy of being called” supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup>a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 74:16–17; Jeremiah 31:35–36; PSALMS 104:19"> This is what the Lord says: Just as I can't break my agreement with the day and the night and the laws that regulate heaven and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:37; Isaiah 14:1; Hosea 2:23"> so I can't reject the descendants of Jacob and of my servant David, and I can't fail to make his descendants rulers over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I will bring them back from exile and I will be kind to them.</span>
</div>
</div>
