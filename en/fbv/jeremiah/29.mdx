<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 9:20; Acts 15:23; Hebrews 13:22"> Jeremiah the prophet wrote this letter and sent it from Jerusalem to the elders who were left among the exiles, to the priests, the prophets, and everyone else who had been exiled from Jerusalem to Babylon by Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24–28; Jeremiah 27:20; 2 Chronicles 36:9–10"> This was after King Jehoiachin, the queen mother, the court officials, the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem, the craftsmen, and the metals-workers had been exiled from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11; 2 Chronicles 34:20; Jeremiah 39:14"> Elasah, son of Shaphan, and Gemariah, son of Hilkiah, took the letter with them when Zedekiah king of Judah sent them to King Nebuchadnezzar in Babylon. In the letter Jeremiah wrote:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 45:7"> This is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says to all the exiles who were taken from Jerusalem to Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:28; Jeremiah 29:10; Ezekiel 28:26"> Build yourselves houses there to live in. Plant gardens and grow food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3–4; Genesis 24:51; Genesis 28:1–4"> Get married and have children. Arrange for your children to get married so they can have children too. Increase in number, don't decrease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:1–2; Ezra 6:10; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> Help make the city where I've exiled you more prosperous. Pray to the Lord for it, since as it prospers, so will you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 5:31; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> This is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says: Don't be fooled by your prophets and fortune-tellers, and don't listen to any dreams they interpret for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 29:31; Jeremiah 29:23"> They are prophesying lies to you in my name; I have not sent them, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 24:6–7"> This is what the Lord says: When the seventy years exile in Babylon is over, I will see to you and keep my promise to bring you back to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–12; PSALMS 33:11; Micah 4:12"> I know what I intend to do for you, declares the Lord. I plan good things for you and not bad. I'm going to give you a future and a hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 33:3; PSALMS 50:15"> Then you will call for my help, you will come and pray to me, and I will answer you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:29–31; PSALMS 119:2; Matthew 7:7"> You will look for me and you will find me when you're completely committed to looking for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1–38; Jeremiah 30:3; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> I will let you find me, declares the Lord. I will end your captivity, gathering you from all the nations and places where I scattered you, declares the Lord. I will bring you back home to the place from where I sent you into exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8–9; Jeremiah 28:1–17; Ezekiel 1:3"> But if you argue, “The Lord has provided prophets for us in Babylon,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2–3; Jeremiah 38:17–23; Ezekiel 17:12–21"> this is what the Lord says about the king who sits on David's throne and everyone who's left in Jerusalem, your fellow citizens who weren't taken with you into exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8–10; Jeremiah 29:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: I'm going to send war and famine and disease against them. I'll make them like rotten figs, so bad that they can't be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 42:18"> I will chase them down with war and famine and disease. I will make all the kingdoms of the earth horrified by them. They will become a curse word, totally ruined, people to be mocked and criticized among all the nations where I scatter them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:19; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 7:24–26"> I'm going to do this because they haven't obeyed my words, declares the Lord, which I sent to them time and again through my servants the prophets. You exiles haven't obeyed me either, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:5; Micah 4:10; Ezekiel 3:15"> So listen to the word of the Lord, all you exiles I sent from Jerusalem to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> This is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who are prophesying lies to you in my name. I'm going to hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will kill them right before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 65:15; Daniel 3:6; Daniel 3:21"> Because of what happens to them, all the exiles of Judah in Babylon will curse others like this: “May the Lord treat you like Zedekiah and Ahab, burned alive by the king of Babylon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Hebrews 4:13; Malachi 3:5"> They did outrageous things in Israel—they committed adultery with their neighbors' wives and told lies in my name. I didn't tell them to say anything. I am the one who knows what they did, and I can witness to it, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:31–32"> Tell Shemaiah the Nehelamite</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 29:29; Jeremiah 52:24"> that this is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says: On your own authority you sent out letters to all the people of Jerusalem, to the priest Zephaniah, son of Maaseiah, and to all the priests, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:1–2; 2 Kings 9:11; John 10:20"> “Zephaniah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Name supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>the Lord has chosen you as priest to replace Jehoiada, to be in charge of the Lord's Temple. In that capacity you are required to put in the stocks and neck irons any crazy person who claims to be a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; Jeremiah 43:2–3; Jeremiah 29:26"> So why haven't you punished Jeremiah of Anathoth, who claims to be a prophet among you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:5; Jeremiah 29:1; Jeremiah 29:10"> You should have done this because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“You should have done this because”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup>he has sent a letter to us here in Babylon, stating, ‘The exile will last a long time. So build yourselves houses there to live in. Plant gardens and grow food to eat.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:25"> However, Zephaniah the priest read this letter to Jeremiah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then the Lord told Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:9; Jeremiah 29:23–24; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> Send this message to all the exiles: This is what the Lord says about Shemaiah the Nehelamite. Since Shemaiah has prophesied to you, even though I didn't send him, and has convinced you to believe in a lie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 2 Kings 7:19–20; Jeremiah 17:6"> this is what the Lord says: I'm going to punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his descendants. He won't have any family left among this people, and he won't experience the good things that I'm going to do for my people, declares the Lord, for he has promoted rebellion against the Lord.</span>
