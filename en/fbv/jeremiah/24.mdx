<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:2; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:12–16"> The Lord showed me in vision two baskets of figs placed in front of the Lord's Temple. This happened after Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had taken to Babylon Jehoiachin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Here called “Jeconiah.”">a</a></sup>son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, as well as the leaders of Judah, and the craftsmen and metal-workers from Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Isaiah 5:4; Micah 7:1"> One basket was full of very good figs, like those that ripen early, but the other basket only had very bad figs, so bad they couldn't be eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:5–11; Matthew 25:32–33"> “Jeremiah,” the Lord asked, “what can you see?”<br />“I see figs!” I replied. “The good figs look very good, but the bad figs look very bad, so bad they can't be eaten.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then a message from the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Nahum 1:7; Galatians 4:9"> This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: The good figs represent to me the exiles from Judah, those I have sent away from here to the country of Babylonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:10; Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 33:7"> I will watch over them and I will bring them back to this country. I will build them up and not tear them down; I will plant them and not uproot them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Jeremiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:24–28"> I will give them the desire to know me, to know that I am the Lord. They will be my people, and I will be their God, for they will come back completely committed to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:1; Jeremiah 39:2–9; Jeremiah 24:2"> But the bad figs, so bad they cannot be eaten, says the Lord, represent the way I will deal with Zedekiah, king of Judah, his officials, and those who are left of Jerusalem, as well as those remaining in this country and those living in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 29:18; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I'm going to make an example of them that will horrify and offend everyone on earth. They will be disgraced, mocked, ridiculed, and cursed everywhere I've exiled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Ezekiel 5:12–17; Jeremiah 16:4"> I'm going to attack them with war and famine and plague, until they're completely wiped out from the country that I gave to them and their forefathers.</span>
</div>
</div>
