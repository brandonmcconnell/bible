<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:5; 2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 7:32–34"> When that happens, declares the Lord, the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of the officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the people of Jerusalem will be taken out of their graves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:5; Jeremiah 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> They will lie exposed to the sun and moon, and to all the stars which they loved, which they served, which they followed, which they consulted, and which they worshiped.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The repetition emphasizes the inability of these “gods” to do anything to protect even the bones of their worshipers.">a</a></sup>Their bones will not be collected or reburied, but will be left like manure lying on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:3"> Those who are left of this evil family will prefer to die rather than live in every place I've scattered them, declares the Lord Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Micah 7:8; Hosea 6:1"> Tell them this is what the Lord says: When people fall down, don't they get up again? When people go the wrong way, don't they turn around?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:6; Jeremiah 5:3; Zechariah 7:11"> So why have these people of Jerusalem gone the wrong way? Why do they refuse to turn around from their repeated betrayals, holding on to all their lies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 14:2; Ezekiel 18:28; Luke 15:17–19"> I've heard exactly what they said, but they don't tell the truth. No one is sorry for doing wrong, asking, “What have I done?” Everyone chooses their own way, like a horse charging into battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Even storks high in the sky know when it's time to migrate. Turtledoves, swifts, and songbirds know when to fly away at the right time of the year. But my people don't know the laws of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 9:41; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 10:1–2"> How on earth can you say, “We're wise, and we have the Law of the Lord?” Can't you see that the writings of your teachers of the Law have turned it into lies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:15; PSALMS 119:98–100; Job 5:12"> The wise will be shown to be foolish; they will be shocked at being caught out. Can't you see that they've rejected what the Lord says—so do they have any wisdom at all?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12–13; Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> I'm going to give their wives to others, and their fields to different owners, since everyone cheats because they're greedy, poor and rich alike. Even prophets and priests—they are all dishonest liars!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Lamentations 2:14"> They give my wounded people first aid, but they don't really care about them. They tell them, “Don't worry! We have peace!” even as war approaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 6:15"> Are they ashamed of the disgusting things they did? No, they're not ashamed at all, they can't even blush. So they will fall just like the others, when I punish them; they will drop down dead, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremiah 17:8"> I'm going to destroy them, declares the Lord. There will be no grapes left on the vines, no figs on the trees—even the leaves will wither. They will lose whatever I gave them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:19"> The people say, “Why are we sitting around here? Let's get together and run to the fortified towns. We can die there, for the Lord our God is killing us by giving us poisoned water to drink, because we sinned against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 8:11"> We hoped for peace, but instead nothing good has come; we hoped for a time of healing, but instead there was only sudden terror.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Judges 5:22; Jeremiah 4:15–16"> The snorting of enemy horses can be heard from Dan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Dan was in the north of the country and would be the first to experience invasion.">b</a></sup>The whole country shakes in fear at the sound of the neighing of these strong stallions, They have come to destroy the country and everything in it; Jerusalem and everyone who lives there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:11; PSALMS 58:4–5; Deuteronomy 32:24"> Watch out! I'm sending snakes among you, vipers that can't be charmed. They will come and bite you, declares the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 1:16–17; Jeremiah 6:24; Isaiah 22:4"> Nothing comforts me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jeremiah is the speaker.">c</a></sup>in my suffering;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew of this line is obscure.">d</a></sup>I feel terrible inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 39:3; Isaiah 13:5; PSALMS 135:21"> Listen to my people crying out for help from a distant land, asking “Isn't the Lord present in Zion anymore? Has her King left?”<br />Why have they made me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Lord is the speaker.">e</a></sup>angry, worshiping their carved images and their useless foreign idols?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Matthew 25:1–12; Luke 13:25"> “The harvest is over, the summer is finished, but we're not saved,” say the people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Say the people”: supplied for clarity">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Joel 2:6; Nahum 2:10"> I'm crushed by the injuries suffered by my people; I mourn for them. I'm horrified at what's happened!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 51:8"> Isn't there any ointment from Gilead to help heal them? Aren't there any doctors there? So why haven't my people been healed from their wounds?</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
