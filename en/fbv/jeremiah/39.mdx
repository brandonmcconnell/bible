<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:1–2; Jeremiah 52:4–7; 2 Kings 25:1–12"> In the tenth month of the ninth year of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his whole army arrived at Jerusalem and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:6–7; Micah 2:12–13; 2 Kings 25:3–4"> On the ninth day of the fourth month of the eleventh year of Zedekiah's reign, the city wall was broken through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:4; Jeremiah 38:17; Jeremiah 39:13"> All the officials of the king of Babylon came in and took over the city, making their headquarters at the Middle Gate. They were Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Rabsaris” and “Rabmag” are functions that are not clear. However, the clearly refer to high royal officials.">a</a></sup>and all the other officials of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 2:14; 2 Chronicles 32:5; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> When Zedekiah king of Judah and all the defenders saw them there, they ran away. They escaped from the city during the night through the king's garden, passing through the gate between the two walls, and took the road to the Arabah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“The Arabah”: the Jordan Valley.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Joshua 5:10; Jeremiah 52:26–27"> But the Babylonian army chased after them and caught up with Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho. They captured him and brought him before Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he put him on trial and punished him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 34:19–21; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> The king of Babylon had Zedekiah's sons killed as Zedekiah watched, and he also executed all of Judah's leaders there in Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; 2 Kings 25:7; Jeremiah 52:11"> Then he had Zedekiah's eyes gouged out, and had him bound with bronze chains and taken away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Jeremiah 21:10"> The Babylonians burned down the king's palace and the people's houses, and they demolished Jerusalem's city walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:1; Genesis 37:36; Jeremiah 10:18"> Then Nebuzaradan the commander of the guard carried off to Babylon the rest of the people who had stayed in the city, together with those who had deserted and gone over to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 52:16"> But he left behind in the land of Judah some of the poorest people who didn't have any property. He gave them vineyards and fields at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Jeremiah 15:21; Job 5:19"> Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had given orders to Nebuzaradan the commander of the guard regarding Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 40:4; Proverbs 21:1"> “Go and get Jeremiah and watch out that nothing bad happens to him. Do whatever he wants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:3; Jeremiah 39:9"> So Nebuzaradan the commander of the guard, Nebushazban the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the army captains of the king of Babylon</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; 2 Kings 22:12; Jeremiah 26:24"> took Jeremiah from the guard's courtyard, and they handed him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, to take him home. Jeremiah stayed there with his own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Jeremiah 32:1–2; Jeremiah 36:1–5"> During the time that Jeremiah had been kept prisoner in the guard's courtyard, a message from the Lord had come to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:14–15"> “Go and tell Ebed-melech the Cushite that this is what the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, says: I am about to keep my promise I made against this city—to harm it and not to help it—you'll see it for yourself when it happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 41:1–2; PSALMS 91:14–15; PSALMS 50:15"> But when that day comes I'm going to save you, declares the Lord. You won't be handed over to the people you're afraid of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; PSALMS 34:22; Jeremiah 21:9"> I promise to rescue you so that you're won't be killed. Your reward will be your life, because you trusted in me, declares the Lord.”</span>
