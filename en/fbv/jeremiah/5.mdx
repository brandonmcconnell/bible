<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–32; 2 Chronicles 16:9; Ezekiel 22:30"> Go everywhere through the streets of Jerusalem. Look and pay attention! Search all through her city squares to see if you can find even just one person who does what's right, anyone who is faithful, and I'll forgive the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 4:2; Hosea 4:1–2"> They may make promises in my name, but they're not sincere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Jeremiah 7:28; Isaiah 9:13"> Lord, aren't you always looking for faithfulness? You beat them, but they didn't care. You just about destroyed them, but they refused to accept your discipline. They were stubborn, hard as rock, and they wouldn't repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 28:9–13"> Then I said to myself, “These people are only the poor—they're just fools who don't know any better. They certainly don't know what the Lord wants, God's right way of living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:20; James 2:5–7; Matthew 19:23–26"> Let me go to and talk to the ones in charge. They would surely know what the Lord wants, God's right way of living.” But they had all broken off the yoke as well, and ripped off the chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Habakkuk 1:8; Zephaniah 3:3"> As a result a lion from the forest will attack them; a wolf from the desert will rip them apart. A leopard will lie in wait for them near their towns, ready to tear to pieces anyone who goes outside. For they keep on rebelling, and turn away from me so often.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Joshua 23:7; Jeremiah 2:11"> Why should I forgive you? Your children have abandoned me and believe in gods that are not gods. I gave them everything they need, yet they went and committed adultery, gathering together at prostitutes' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:27; Deuteronomy 5:18; 2 Samuel 11:2–4"> They're like virile stallions wanting sex, each of them neighing with lust after his neighbor's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> Shouldn't I punish them for all this? declares the Lord. Shouldn't I retaliate for what this nation has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:27; Matthew 22:7; Amos 9:8"> Go through her vineyards and damage them, but don't destroy them completely. Rip off her branches, because they don't belong to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; Isaiah 48:8; Hosea 6:7"> The people of Israel and Judah have completely betrayed me, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Ezekiel 12:22–28; Jeremiah 23:14–17"> They have lied about the Lord, saying, “He won't do anything. Nothing bad will happen to us. We won't have war or famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:15; Job 8:2; Jeremiah 14:13"> The prophets are just like the wind. The Lord doesn't speak through them. What they predict can happen to them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Jeremiah 1:9; Hosea 6:5"> So this is the reply from the Lord God Almighty: Because of what you've said, I will make my words like a fire in your mouth and you people like the wood it burns up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 4:16; Isaiah 33:19"> Look! I am bringing a nation from far away to attack you, people of Israel, declares the Lord. It's a powerful nation that has existed for a long time; it's a nation whose language you don't know, and when they speak you can't understand them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; PSALMS 5:9; Romans 3:13"> Their arrows bring death;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Their arrows bring death”: literally, “their quivers are like an open grave.”">a</a></sup>they are all strong warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:33; Hosea 8:14"> They will consume your harvest and your food; they will destroy your sons and your daughters; they will eat your flocks and your herds; they will feed on your vines and your fig trees. They will attack and will destroy the fortified towns that you have so much confidence in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:10; Romans 11:1–5; Jeremiah 4:27"> But even at that time I won't completely destroy you, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> When people ask you Jeremiah, “Why did the Lord our God do all these things to us?” you are to tell them, “In the same way you have abandoned me and served foreign gods here in your country, so you will serve foreigners in a country that isn't your own.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Announce this to the people of Jacob and Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 28:26; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Listen to this, you foolish, stupid people, who have eyes but don't see, who have ears but don't hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; PSALMS 104:9; Job 26:10"> Aren't you afraid of what I can do? declares the Lord. Don't you think you should tremble in my presence? I am the one who set up the shore as the sea's boundary, an everlasting limit that it can't cross. The waves crash against it, but they can't defeat it. They roar, but they can't cross the barrier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28; Deuteronomy 21:18; PSALMS 95:10"> But you people have a stubborn and rebellious attitude. You have left me and gone your own way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 147:8; Joel 2:23; Genesis 8:22"> You didn't think to say, “We should appreciate the Lord our God, who sends the autumn and spring rains at the right time, who makes sure we can have a harvest every year.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:2; PSALMS 107:17; PSALMS 107:34"> Your wrong actions have taken these benefits from you; your sins have deprived you of my blessings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 1:11; PSALMS 64:5; Ezekiel 22:2–12"> For there are wicked men among my people. They're like bird-trappers, secretly watching and waiting to catch people in their snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 1:12; Jeremiah 9:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> Their homes are full of their ill-gotten gains, like cages full of birds. That's why they've become powerful and rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Deuteronomy 32:15; Jeremiah 7:6"> They've grown fat and smooth, and have become experts in evil. They deny justice to orphans, and they don't defend the rights of those in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 9:9"> Shouldn't I punish them for all this? declares the Lord. Shouldn't I retaliate for what this nation has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14; Isaiah 1:2"> Something horrible, something terrible has happened here in this country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Jeremiah 14:14; Micah 2:11"> The prophets give false prophecies; the priests rule as they please. My people love it like this, but what will you do when it all falls apart?</span>
