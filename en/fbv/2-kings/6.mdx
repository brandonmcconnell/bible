<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 4:1; Joshua 17:14"> The sons of the prophets told Elisha, “Look, the place we meet with you is too small for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 21:3; Acts 18:3; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> Let's go to the Jordan and each of us can carry one log back. We can build a new place there for us to meet.” “Go ahead,” said Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:28; Judges 19:6; 2 Kings 5:23"> One of them asked, “Please come with your servants.” “I'll come,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:5; Deuteronomy 29:11"> So he went with them. When they got to the Jordan, they started cutting down trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:21; Ecclesiastes 10:10; 2 Kings 6:15"> But as one of them was cutting down a tree, the iron ax head fell into the water. “Oh no! My master, it was one that was borrowed!” he shouted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Mark 8:23–25; 2 Kings 4:41"> “Where did it fall?” the man of God asked. When he showed him the place, the man of God cut a stick, threw it in there, and made the iron ax head float.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:36; Exodus 4:4; 2 Kings 4:7"> “Pick it up,” Elisha told the man. So he reached out his hand and picked it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 6:24; Proverbs 21:30; Proverbs 20:18"> The Aramean king was at war with Israel. After consulting with his officers, he said, “I will set up my camp in this particular place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Revelation 1:1; 2 Kings 6:12"> Then the man of God sent a warning to the king of Israel: “Watch out if you go near this place, because the Arameans are going to be there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; 2 Kings 5:14; Acts 27:24"> So the king of Israel sent a warning to the place the man of God had indicated. Elisha repeatedly warned the king, so that he was on the alert in those places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:7–11; Isaiah 57:20–21; Matthew 2:3–12"> This made the Aramean king really mad. He summoned his officers, demanding an answer: “Tell me, which of us is on the side of the king of Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:28–30; Amos 3:7; 2 Kings 6:9–10"> “It's none of us, my lord the king,” one of his officers replied. “It's Elisha, the prophet who lives in Israel—he tells the king of Israel even what you say in your bedroom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:17; John 11:47–53; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> So the king gave the order, “Go and find out where he is so I can send soldiers to capture him.” He was told, “Elisha is in Dothan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 23:26"> So he sent horses, chariots, and a large army. They came at night and surrounded the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; 2 Chronicles 20:12; PSALMS 53:5"> Early in the morning when the servant of the man of God got up, he went out and saw that an army with horses and chariots had surrounded the city. “Oh, my master, what are we going to do?” he asked Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Romans 8:31; 1 John 4:4"> Elisha replied, “Don't be afraid, for there are many more who are with us than there are with them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 68:17; PSALMS 34:7; Zechariah 6:1–7"> Elisha prayed, saying, “Lord, please open his eyes so he can see.” The Lord opened the servant's eyes, and when he looked he saw the hills full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 13:11; John 12:40; Genesis 19:11"> As the army<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring to the Arameans.">a</a></sup>descended on him, Elisha prayed to the Lord, “Please strike these people with blindness.” So he struck them with blindness, as Elisha had asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 24:16; 2 Samuel 16:18–19; Luke 9:23"> Then Elisha went and told them, “This isn't the right road, and this isn't the right town. Follow me, and I'll take you to the man you're looking for.” He led them to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Luke 24:31; Luke 16:23"> After they had entered Samaria, Elisha prayed, “Lord, open the eyes of these men so they can see.” The Lord opened their eyes, and they looked around and saw that they were in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 8:9; 2 Kings 2:12"> When the king of Israel saw them, he asked Elisha, “My father, shall I kill them? Shall I kill them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Genesis 48:22; Hosea 2:18"> “No, don't you kill them!” he replied. “Would you kill prisoners you captured with your own sword or bow? Give them some food and water so that they may eat and drink, and then let them go back to their master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2; 2 Kings 24:2; Luke 6:35"> So the king had a great feast prepared for them, and once they had finished eating and drinking, he sent them back to their master. The Aramean raiders did not enter the land of Israel again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 22:31; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> Sometime after this Ben-hadad king of Aram called up all his army and went to lay siege to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 6:28–29; Jeremiah 14:18; Jeremiah 14:13–15"> So there was a major famine in Samaria. In fact the siege lasted so long that a donkey's head cost eighty shekels of silver, and a quarter cab of dove's dung<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Dove's dung”: Some believe this referred to a kind of wild vegetable. One “cab” was equivalent to about 1.2 liters.">b</a></sup>cost five shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Isaiah 10:3; Luke 18:3"> As the king of Israel was walking by on the city wall, a woman called out to him, “Help me, my lord the king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 118:8–9; PSALMS 124:1–3; PSALMS 60:11"> “If the Lord doesn't help you, why would you think I can help you?” the king replied. “I don't have grain from the threshing floor, or wine from the winepress.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:5; Judges 18:23; Lamentations 4:10"> But then he asked her, “What's the problem?” “This woman told me, ‘Give up your son and we'll eat him today, and tomorrow we'll eat my son,’” she answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 26:29; Deuteronomy 28:53; Deuteronomy 28:57"> “So we cooked my son and we ate him. The next day I said to her, ‘Give up your son so we can eat him,’ but she's hidden her son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Kings 19:1"> When the king heard what the woman said he ripped his clothes. As he walked by on the wall, the people saw that he was wearing sackcloth under his clothes next to his skin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Kings 19:2; John 11:50"> “May God punish me very severely if the head of Elisha, son of Shaphat, remains on his shoulders today!” he declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; 1 Kings 18:4; Ezekiel 8:1"> Elisha was sitting in his house with the elders. The king had sent a messenger on ahead, but before he got there, Elisha told the elders, “Can you see how this murderer is sending someone to cut off my head? So, as soon as the messenger arrives, close the door and hold it shut against him. Isn't that the sound of his master's footsteps following him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 2:9; Isaiah 8:21; Genesis 4:13"> While Elisha was still speaking with them, the messenger arrived. The king said, “This disaster is from the Lord. Why should I wait for the Lord any longer?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The king believed the Lord had caused the problems and since there seemed to be no action from the Lord to solve them, the king was taking matters into his own hands. He was rejecting God, and intended to take vengeance on God's prophet Elisha.">c</a></sup></span>
