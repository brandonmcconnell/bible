<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; 2 Chronicles 32:33–9; Matthew 1:10"> Manasseh was twelve when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for fifty-five years. His mother's name was Hephzibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; 2 Chronicles 36:14; 2 Chronicles 33:2–4"> He did what was evil in the Lord's sight by following the disgusting pagan practices of the nations that the Lord had driven out before the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Kings 17:16; Deuteronomy 17:3"> He rebuilt the high places his father Hezekiah had destroyed, and he erected altars for Baal. He made an Asherah idol pole, just as Ahab, king of Israel, had done, and he worshiped and served the sun, moon, and stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:29; 2 Samuel 7:13; Jeremiah 32:34"> He set up pagan altars in the Lord's Temple, right where the Lord had said, “I will place my name in Jerusalem forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Kings 23:4; 2 Kings 23:12"> He set up altars to worship the sun, moon, and stars in the two courtyards of the Lord's Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 18:21"> He even he sacrificed his own son as a burnt offering, and used fortune-telling and witchcraft, and he dealt with mediums and with spiritists. He did a great deal of evil in the Lord's sight, making the Lord angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 9:3; 2 Samuel 7:13"> He took the Asherah idol pole he had made and placed in the Temple. This was the place referred to by the Lord when he told David and Solomon, his son, “In this Temple and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, I will place my name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:28–29; PSALMS 81:11–16"> I will never again make the Israelites wander from the land I gave their forefathers if they are careful to follow everything I have ordered them to do—the whole law that my servant Moses gave them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Kings 14:16"> The people refused to listen and Manasseh led them to sin so that the evil they did was even worse than the nations the Lord had destroyed before the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 23:34–37; 2 Chronicles 33:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15"> The Lord said through his servants the prophets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; 1 Kings 21:26; 2 Kings 21:9"> “Since Manasseh, king of Judah, has committed all these disgusting sins, doing even more evil things than the Amorites who lived before him, and by his encouragement of idol worship has made Judah sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:3; 1 Samuel 3:11; Daniel 9:12"> this is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: Watch out! I am going to bring down upon Jerusalem and Judah such a disaster that it will cause ringing in the ears of everyone who hears it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 7:7–8; Lamentations 2:8; Isaiah 34:11"> I will extend over Jerusalem the measuring line used against Samaria and the plumb line used against the house of Ahab,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, God says he will apply the same standard for judging Judah as he did for Israel.">a</a></sup>and I will wipe away Jerusalem as people wipe clean a bowl, wiping it and turning it upside down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; 2 Kings 19:4; PSALMS 89:38–45"> I will give up on the remnant of my special people and hand them over to their enemies. They will be plunder and loot to all their enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:30; Deuteronomy 31:27; Ezekiel 20:21"> because they have done what is evil in my sight, and have made me angry from the day their fathers left Egypt until today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:11"> On top of that, Manasseh murdered so many innocent people that Jerusalem was filled from one side to the other with their blood. This was in addition to the sin that he had made Judah commit, doing evil in the Lord's sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20–21; 2 Chronicles 33:1–20"> The rest of what happened in Manasseh's reign, all he did, as well as the sins he committed, are recorded in the Book of Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:20; 2 Kings 21:26; 2 Chronicles 32:33"> Manasseh died, and was buried in his palace garden, the garden of Uzza. His son Amon succeeded him as king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:21–23; 1 Kings 22:51; 1 Chronicles 3:14"> Amon was twenty-two when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for two years. His mother's name was Meshullemeth, daughter of Haruz. She came from Jotbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 23:32; 2 Kings 21:11; Acts 7:51"> He did what was evil in the Lord's sight, just as his father Manasseh had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He followed all the ways of his father, and he served the idols his father had served, bowing down in worship to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 1 Kings 11:33; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> He rejected the Lord, the God of his forefathers, and did not follow the way of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 14:19; 2 Chronicles 33:24–25"> Amon's officials plotted against him and murdered him in his royal palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 14:5; 2 Kings 11:17; 2 Kings 14:21"> But then the people of the land killed everyone who had plotted against King Amon, and they chose his son Josiah king to succeed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; 2 Kings 21:17"> The rest of what happened in Amon's reign, and all he did, are recorded in the Book of Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; Matthew 1:10; 1 Kings 13:5"> He was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and his son Josiah succeeded him as king.</span>
