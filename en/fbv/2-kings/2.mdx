<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; Joshua 1:1–2; Acts 1:11"> The sons of the prophets who lived in Jericho came to Elisha and told him, “You do know that the Lord is going to take your master away from you today, don't you?”<br />“Yes, I know,” he replied. “Don't talk about it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 3:15"> Then Elijah said to him, “Please stay here, Elisha, for the Lord has sent me to the Jordan.”<br />He replied, “As the Lord lives, and as you live, I will not leave you.” So they traveled on together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 2:17; 1 Kings 18:4"> Then a group of fifty of the sons of the prophets went and stood facing Elijah and Elisha at a distance as the two of them stood by the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17"> Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and hit the water. It divided to the one side and to the other and both of them crossed over on dry ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Deuteronomy 21:17; John 16:7"> When they arrived on the other side, Elijah asked Elisha, “What can I do for you before I'm taken from you?”<br />“Please let me have a double amount of your spirit,” Elisha replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 11:22–24; 2 Kings 2:12; John 16:24"> “What you have asked for is hard,” Elijah responded. “But if you see me when I am taken from you, you will have it, otherwise not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; PSALMS 104:3–4; Hebrews 1:14"> As they were walking along, talking, a chariot of fire and horses of fire came between them, and Elijah was taken up in the whirlwind to heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; Isaiah 37:21; Mark 16:19"> Elisha saw what happened and cried out, “My father! My father! Look! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!” Then Elisha couldn't see him anymore. He took his clothes and ripped them to pieces.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A symbolic act of great distress.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26"> Then Elisha picked up Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him, and went back and stood on the bank of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:12; PSALMS 42:10; PSALMS 115:2"> He took Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him, hit the water, and cried out, “Where is the Lord, the God of Elijah?” When he hit the water, it divided to the one side and to the other and Elisha crossed over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Acts 1:8; Joshua 3:7"> The sons of the prophets who lived in Jericho saw him from the opposite side and shouted out, “Elijah's spirit now rests upon Elisha!” They went over to meet him, and bowed to the ground before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; Acts 8:39; 2 Corinthians 12:2–3"> “Look,” they said to Elisha, “we your servants have fifty good men here. Please allow them to go and search for your master. Maybe the Spirit of the Lord has taken him and put him down on a mountain or in a valley somewhere.”<br />“Don't bother sending them,” Elisha replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 8:11; Hebrews 11:5; Romans 10:2"> But they went on trying to persuade him until he was too embarrassed to say no. “Go ahead and send them,” he told them. So they sent fifty men, who searched for Elijah for three days but couldn't find him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When they returned to Elisha, who was staying in Jericho, he said to them, “Didn't I tell you not to bother going?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Deuteronomy 28:2–4; Deuteronomy 28:11"> The townspeople said to Elisha, “Look, sir, while our town has a good location as you can see, the water is bad and the soil is poor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:45; Zephaniah 2:9; Ezekiel 47:11"> “Bring me a brand new bowl and put some salt in it,” he replied. So they brought it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:25–26; 2 Kings 4:41; Leviticus 2:13"> Then Elisha went to the spring, threw the salt into it, and said, “This is what the Lord says: ‘I have purified this water. It won't cause death or miscarriages anymore.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The water there is still pure to this very day, just as Elisha said it would be.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Job 19:18; Job 30:1"> Elisha went on from there to Bethel. As he was walking along the road, a group of youths came from the town. They made fun of him, calling out, “Go on up, baldy! Go on up, baldy!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It seems they were taunting Elisha by saying he should leave in the same way as Elijah.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:9–11; Acts 5:5; Hosea 13:8"> Turning around, he looked at them and called down a curse on them in the Lord's name. Suddenly two female bears came out of the forest and mauled forty-two of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 18:19–20; 1 Kings 18:42"> Elisha carried on to Mount Carmel, and from there he went back to Samaria.</span>
</div>
