<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; 1 Kings 5:4"> By now the king was comfortable in his palace and the Lord had given him peace from all the enemy nations around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> So he said to Nathan the prophet, “Look at me—I live in a palace made of cedar, but the Ark of God is still in a tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> “Go ahead, do whatever you want, for the Lord is with you,” Nathan told the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> But that night the Lord spoke to Nathan and told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> “Go and tell my servant David, This is what the Lord says: Should you be the one to build a house for me to live in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have never lived in a house, from the time I led the Israelites out of Egypt up till now. I have always moved from place to place, living in a tent and a Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> But in all those travels with all of Israel did I ever ask any Israelite leader I'd ordered to take care of my people, ‘Why haven't you built a cedar house for me?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> So then, tell my servant David this is what the Lord Almighty says. I was the one who took you from the fields, from looking after sheep, to become a leader of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> I have been with you wherever you've gone. I have destroyed all your enemies right in front of you, and I will make your reputation as great as the most famous people on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> I will choose a place for my people Israel. I will settle them there and they won't be disturbed anymore. Evil people won't persecute them as they used to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> from the time I placed judges in charge of my people. I will defeat all of your enemies. Also I want to make it clear that I the Lord will build a house for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, the Lord would build a “house” for David in the sense of establishing a royal dynasty.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> For when you come to the end of your life and join your ancestors in death, I will bring to power one of your descendants, one of your sons, and make sure his kingdom is successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He will be the one to build me a house, and I will make sure his kingdom lasts forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to me. If he does wrong, I will discipline him with the rod like people do, like a parent punishing a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; PSALMS 89:28"> But I will never take away my kindness and love from him, as I did in the case of Saul whom I removed before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; PSALMS 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Your house and your kingdom will last forever; your dynasty will be secure forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> This is what Nathan explained to David—everything he was told in this divine revelation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Then King David went and sat down in the presence of the Lord. He prayed, “Who am I, Lord God, and what is significant about my family, that you have brought me to this place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; PSALMS 36:7"> God, you talk as if this was a small thing in your eyes, and you also have spoken about the future of my house, my family dynasty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“My family dynasty”: explaining the meaning of “house” in this context.">b</a></sup>Is this your usual way of dealing with human beings, Lord God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; PSALMS 139:1"> What more can I, David, tell you? You know exactly what your servant is like, Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 115:1; PSALMS 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> You're doing all this for me and you have explained it to me, your servant, because of your promise and because it's what you want to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:10; Exodus 15:11; PSALMS 86:8"> How great you are, Lord God! There really is no one like you; there is no other God, only you. We have never heard about anyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> Who else is as fortunate as your people Israel? Who else on earth did God go and redeem to make his own people? You gained a wonderful reputation for yourself by all the tremendous, amazing things you did in driving out other nations and their gods before your people as you redeemed them from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> You made your people Israel your own forever, and you, Lord, have become their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> So now, Lord God, please ensure that what you have said about me and my house happens, and is confirmed forever. Please do as you have promised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; PSALMS 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> and may your true nature be honored forever, with people declaring, ‘The Lord Almighty is Israel's God!’ May the house of your servant David continue to be there in your presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; PSALMS 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> Lord Almighty, God of Israel, you have revealed this to me, your servant, telling me, ‘I will build a house for you.’ That's why your servant has had the courage to pray this prayer to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> Lord Almighty, you are God! Your words are truth, and you are the one who has promised these good things to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; PSALMS 115:12–15"> So now, please bless your servant's house that it may continue in your presence forever. For you have spoken, Lord God, and with your blessing the house of your servant will be blessed forever.”</span>
