<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:1–4; 1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:6–14"> Once again David called up all the specially chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:4; 1 Samuel 7:1; PSALMS 80:1"> He went with all his men to Baalah in Judah to bring back the Ark of God, which belongs to the Lord Almighty who sits between the cherubim that are on the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:7; Numbers 7:4–9; Numbers 4:5–12"> They placed the Ark of God on a new cart and brought it from Abinadab's house, which was on a hill. Uzzah and Ahio, Abinadab's sons, were directing the cart</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:7; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> with the Ark of God on it, with Ahio walking in front of it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Septuagint reading, supported by one of the Dead Sea Scrolls.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; PSALMS 150:3–5; Amos 5:23"> David and all the Israelites were celebrating in the Lord's presence, singing songs accompanied by zithers, harps, tambourines, rattles, and cymbals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The reference on the Hebrew to fir trees is improbable here, and the parallel passage in 1 Chronicles 13:8helps provide clarity.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 4:15; Numbers 4:19–20"> But when they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, the oxen stumbled, so Uzzah reached out to stop the Ark of God from falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; 1 Chronicles 15:13; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> The Lord was angry with Uzzah, and God struck him down right there for his disobedience,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The meaning of the word used here is uncertain. It may indicate an action that is rash or irreverent. Here it seems to be reflective of a presumptuous attitude that treated the Ark as simply an ordinary object.">c</a></sup>and he died beside the Ark of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:1; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Jonah 4:9"> David was angry because of the Lord's violent outburst against Uzzah. He called the place Perez-uzzah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Meaning “outburst against Uzzah.”">d</a></sup>which is still its name today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Numbers 17:12–13"> David became afraid of the Lord that day. “How can I ever bring back the Ark of God home to me?” he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–8; 2 Samuel 15:19; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> Not wanting to bring the Ark of the Lord to be with him in the City of David, he had it taken to the home of Obed-edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:5; Genesis 30:27; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The Ark of the Lord remained in Obed-edom's home for three months, and the Lord blessed Obed-edom's whole household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:25–3; 1 Kings 8:1; Matthew 10:42"> King David was told, “The Lord has blessed Obed-edom's household and all that he has because of the Ark of God.” So David went and had the Ark of God brought from Obed-edom's house to the City of David. There was a lot of celebration!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:15"> After those carrying the Ark of the Lord had taken six steps, he sacrificed a bull and a fattened calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; Exodus 15:20; PSALMS 150:4"> Wearing a priest's ephod, David danced as hard as he could before the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 68:24–27; 1 Chronicles 15:28; 1 Chronicles 15:25"> as he and all the Israelites brought along the Ark of the Lord, with plenty of shouting and the sound of horns being blown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Chronicles 15:29; PSALMS 69:7"> As the Ark of the Lord was carried into the City of David, Saul's daughter Michal looked down from a window. She watched King David leaping and dancing before the Lord, and she really loathed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 1:4; 1 Kings 8:5"> They brought the Ark of the Lord and put it in its place inside the tent that David had set up for it. Then David offered burnt offerings and friendship offerings before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 8:55; 1 Chronicles 16:2; Genesis 14:19"> Once he had finished offering the sacrifices, David blessed the people in the name of the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; 1 Chronicles 16:3"> He gave all the Israelites, both men and women, a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake. Then he sent everyone home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:14; Judges 9:4"> When David got home to bless his family, Michal, Saul's daughter came out to meet him and said, “How distinguished the king of Israel made himself look today, taking off his robes so everyone's servant girls could see, just as somebody crude would expose himself!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:22; PSALMS 89:19–20; 1 Samuel 13:14"> David told Michal, “I was dancing before the Lord, who chose me instead of your father and all his family when he named me ruler over the Lord's people Israel. I will continue to celebrate before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 51:7"> in fact I'm going to make myself even less distinguished, become even more humble. However, I will be respected by those servant girls you spoke about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:25; Isaiah 22:14; Isaiah 4:1"> And Michal, Saul's daughter, never had any children.</span>
</div>
