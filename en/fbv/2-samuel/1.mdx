<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> Then on the third day a man arrived from Saul's camp. His clothes were torn and he had dust on his head. When he approached David, he bowed before him, and fell to the ground in respect.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> “Where have you come from?” David asked him.<br />“I got away from the Israelite camp,” he replied.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> “Tell me what happened,” David asked.<br />“The army ran away from the battle,” the man replied. “Many of them died, and Saul and his son Jonathan also died.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> “How do you know Saul and Jonathan died?” David asked the man giving the report.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> “I just happened to be there on Mount Gilboa,” he replied. “I saw Saul, leaning on his spear, with the enemy chariots and the charioteers advancing on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> He turned around and saw me. He called out and I replied, ‘I'm here to help!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> He asked me, ‘Who are you?’<br />I told him, ‘I'm an Amalekite.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then he told me, ‘Please come over here and kill me! I'm in terrible agony but life is still hanging on.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I went over him and killed him, because I knew that wounded as he was he couldn't last long. I took the crown from his head and his bracelet from his arm, and I've brought them here to you, my lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> David grabbed hold of his clothes and ripped them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A sign of extreme emotion, usually grief.">a</a></sup>as did his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; PSALMS 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> They mourned and cried and fasted until the evening for Saul and his son Jonathan, and for the army of the Lord, the Israelites, that had been killed by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> David asked the man who brought him the report, “Where are you from?”<br />“I'm the son of a foreigner,” he replied. “I'm an Amalekite.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> “Why weren't you worried about killing the Lord's anointed one?” David asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> David called over one of his men and said, “Go ahead, kill him!” So the man cut the Amalekite down and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> David had told the Amalekite, “Your death is your own fault because you testified against yourself when you said, ‘I killed the Lord's anointed one.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> Then David sang this lament for Saul and his son Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> He ordered it to be taught to the people of Judah. It is called “The Bow” and is recorded in the Book of the Just:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> “Israel, the glorious one lies dead on your mountains. How the mighty have fallen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Don't announce it in the town of Gath, don't proclaim it in the streets of Ashkelon, so that the Philistine women won't rejoice, so that the heathen women won't celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Mountains of Gilboa, may no dew or rain fall on you! May you have no fields that produce offerings of grain. For it was there that the shield of the mighty was defiled; Saul's shield, no longer cared for with olive oil.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Saul's shield would be ritually defiled by blood, and would no longer be looked after by regular applications of olive oil.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> Jonathan with his bow did not retreat from attacking the enemy; Saul with his sword did not return empty-handed from shedding blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> During their lives, Saul and Jonathan were much loved and very pleasant, and death did not divide them. They were faster than eagles, stronger than lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> Women of Israel, mourn for Saul, who gave you fine scarlet clothes decorated with gold ornaments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How the mighty have fallen in battle! Jonathan lies dead on your mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> I weep so much for you, my brother Jonathan! You were so very dear to me! Your love for me was so wonderful, greater than the love women have!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> How the mighty have fallen! The weapons of war are gone!”</span>
</div>
