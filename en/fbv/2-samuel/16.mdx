<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 15:32; 2 Samuel 9:2–13"> After David had gone a little way past the top of the mountain, there was Ziba, Mephibosheth's servant, waiting to meet him. He had two donkeys already saddled with him carrying two hundred loaves of bread, a hundred raisin cakes, a hundred summer fruits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Summer fruits”: probably figs.">a</a></sup>and a skin of wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:29; Judges 10:4; Judges 5:10"> “What did you bring these for?” David asked Ziba.<br />Ziba replied, “The donkeys are for the king's family to ride on, the bread and summer fruit are for the men to eat, and the wine is for those to drink who get worn out in the wilderness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:9–10; PSALMS 15:3; 2 Samuel 19:24–30"> “Where is your master's grandson?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring to Mephibosheth.">b</a></sup>the king asked.<br />Ziba answered, “He decided to stay in Jerusalem. He's saying, ‘Today the people of Israel will give me back my grandfather's kingdom.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 19:2"> The king told Ziba, “I give you everything that belongs to Mephibosheth!”<br />“I bow before you,” Ziba replied. “May you approve of me, Your Majesty.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Samuel 17:43; Exodus 22:28"> As King David arrived at the town of Bahurim, a man from Saul's family was just leaving. His name was Shimei, son of Gera, and he was shouting out curses as he came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He threw stones at David and all the king's officers, even though the king's men and all his bodyguards surrounded David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:17; 1 Kings 21:10"> “Get out of here, just get out, you murderer, you wicked man!” Shimei said as he cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Kings 2:32–33"> “The Lord has paid you back for all of Saul's family that you killed, and for stealing Saul's throne. The Lord has given the kingdom to your son Absalom. Look how you've ended up in disaster because you're a murderer!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; 2 Samuel 9:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> Abishai, son of Zeruiah, asked the king, “Why should this dead dog curse Your Majesty? Let me go and cut off his head!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; Romans 9:20; John 18:11"> “What's that got to do with you, you sons of Zeruiah?” the king replied. “If he's cursing me because the Lord told him to, then who can question what he's doing?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:1–4; Genesis 45:5; 2 Samuel 12:11–12"> David said to Abishai and to all his officers, “Look, if my very own son is trying to kill me, why shouldn't this Benjamite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The people of the tribe of Benjamin were generally Saul's supporters, and Saul is described as being a Benjamite in 1 Samuel 9:21.">c</a></sup>want to even more! Leave him alone; let him curse me, for the Lord told him to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Hebrews 12:10; PSALMS 25:18"> Perhaps the Lord will see how I'm suffering and will pay me back with good for his curses today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 23:23; 2 Samuel 16:5–6"> David and his men continued down the road, with Shimei keeping up with them on the hillside opposite. He went on cursing as he went along, throwing stones and dirt at David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The king and everyone with him were tired out when they arrived at the Jordan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Jordan.” Not in the Hebrew, but given by some of the Septuagint manuscripts. Since the destination was given in 15:28as “the fords of the wilderness” this appears reasonable.">d</a></sup>David rested there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:12"> In the meantime Absalom and all the Israelites with him arrived in Jerusalem, along with Ahithophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 11:12; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:25"> Hushai the Archite, David's friend, went to see Absalom and declared, “Long live the king! Long live the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:25; 2 Samuel 15:32–37; Deuteronomy 32:6"> “Is this how you show loyalty to your friend?” Absalom asked. “Why didn't you leave with your friend?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; 2 Samuel 5:1–3"> “Certainly not!” Hushai replied. “I'm on the side of the one chosen by the Lord, by the army, and by all the people of Israel. I will remain loyal to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:34; PSALMS 55:21; 1 Samuel 28:2"> In any case, why shouldn't I serve his son? In the same way I served your father I will serve you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; PSALMS 37:12–13; Isaiah 29:15"> Then Absalom asked Ahithophel, “Give me your advice. What shall we do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:3; 2 Samuel 15:16; Zechariah 8:13"> Ahithophel told him, “Go and sleep with your father's concubines—the ones he left here to look after the palace. Then everyone in Israel will realize that you have so offended your father there's no turning back, which will encourage all your supporters.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11–12; 2 Samuel 15:16; 2 Samuel 20:3"> So they put up a tent on the palace roof and Absalom went in and had sex with his father's concubines in the full view of everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:23"> At that time Ahithophel's advice was like receiving messages from God himself. This was how both David and Absalom viewed Ahithophel's advice.</span>
</div>
