<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 20:35–41; PSALMS 51:1–19; 2 Samuel 11:10–17"> The Lord sent Nathan to see David. When he got there, he said, “Once there were two men living in the same town. One was rich, and one was poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:16; Job 1:3; 2 Samuel 5:13–16"> The rich man had many thousands of sheep and cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:5; 2 Samuel 11:3; Proverbs 5:18–19"> but the poor man didn't have anything but one small ewe lamb that he had bought. He cared for it, and it grew up with him and his children. It would eat from his plate and drink from his cup. It slept on his lap and was like a daughter to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:14; 2 Samuel 11:3–4; Genesis 18:2–7"> One day the rich man had a visitor. He didn't want to take one of his own sheep or cattle to feed his visitor. He took the poor man's lamb instead to prepare a meal for his visitor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Romans 2:1; 1 Samuel 26:16"> David became absolutely furious with what that man did, and angrily told Nathan. “As the Lord lives, the man who did this should be put to death!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:1; Luke 19:8; Proverbs 6:31"> He must repay that lamb with four<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Exodus 22:1.">a</a></sup>of his own for doing this, for being so heartless.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:14; 1 Samuel 16:13; 2 Samuel 22:1"> “You are that man!” Nathan told David. “This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: I anointed you king of Israel, and I saved you from Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 84:11; Romans 8:32; 2 Samuel 9:7"> I gave your master's house to you and placed your master's wives in your lap. I gave you the kingdom of Israel and Judah, and if that hadn't been enough, I would have given you so much more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:19; PSALMS 139:1–2; 1 Samuel 15:23"> So why have you treated what the Lord said with contempt by doing evil in his sight? You killed Uriah the Hittite with the sword and stole his wife—you killed him using the sword of the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:14–15; Matthew 26:52; Romans 2:4"> So your descendants will always face the sword<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Sword” used in these verses refers to any kind of violent death.">b</a></sup>that kills because you treated me with contempt and stole Uriah's wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; Deuteronomy 28:30; 2 Samuel 15:10"> This is what the Lord says: ‘I'm going to bring disaster on you from your own family. I will take your wives before your very eyes and give them to someone else, and he will sleep openly with your wives where everyone can see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:22; 2 Samuel 11:4–15; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> You did it all in secret, but I will do it openly where everyone in all of Israel can see.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 28:13"> David said to Nathan, “I have sinned against the Lord.”<br />“The Lord has forgiven your sins. You're not going to die,” Nathan replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; PSALMS 89:31–33"> “But because by doing this you have treated the Lord with complete contempt, the son you have will die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:38; PSALMS 104:29; 2 Chronicles 13:20"> Then Nathan went home.<br />The Lord made the child that Uriah's wife had borne to David become very sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Acts 9:9; PSALMS 69:10"> David pleaded with God on behalf of the boy. He fasted, went to his bedroom, and spent the night lying in sackcloth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“In sackcloth”: Septuagint and Dead Sea Scrolls reading.">c</a></sup>on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; 1 Samuel 28:23"> His senior officials approached him and tried to help him up from the ground, but he didn't want to, and he refused their appeals to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 20:15"> On the seventh day the child died. But David's officials were scared to tell him that the child was dead, for they said to each other, “Look, while the child was still alive, we talked with him, and he refused to listen to us. How on earth can we tell him the child is dead? He may do something really bad!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But when David saw his officials were whispering among themselves, he realized that the child was dead. So he asked his officials, “Did the child die?”<br />“Yes, he died,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:20; Ruth 3:3; Lamentations 3:39–41"> David got up from the ground, washed and put on scented oils, and changed his clothes. Then he went to the house of the Lord and worshiped. Afterwards he went back home, and asked for some food. So they served him a meal which he ate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:15"> “Why are you acting like this?” his officials asked him. “While the child was still alive, you fasted and cried aloud, but now that he's dead, you get up and eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–3; Isaiah 38:5; Jonah 3:9–10"> David replied, “While the child was still alive, I fasted and cried aloud, for I thought to myself, ‘Who knows? Maybe the Lord will be gracious to me and let him live.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Job 30:23; Luke 23:43"> But now that he's dead, what's the point for me to go on fasting? Can I bring him back again? One day I will die and go to him, but he will never come back to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:6; 1 Chronicles 28:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:9–10"> David consoled his wife Bathsheba, and he made love to her. She gave birth to a son, and named him Solomon. The Lord loved the child,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:4; Nehemiah 13:26; 1 Kings 1:11"> so he sent a message through Nathan the prophet to name him Jedidiah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Meaning “loved by the Lord.”">d</a></sup>because the Lord loved him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:1–3; Deuteronomy 3:11; 2 Samuel 11:25"> At this time Joab had been fighting against the Ammonite town of Rabbah, and had captured the royal fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 11:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Joab sent messengers to David to tell him, “I have attacked Rabbah and I have also captured its water supply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 7:18"> So please call up the rest of the army, besiege the town, and capture it. Otherwise I will capture the city, and I will get the credit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So David called up the rest of the army and marched on Rabbah. He attacked it and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:2"> He took the crown from the head of their king, and it was placed on David's head. It weighed a talent of gold and was decorated with precious stones. David took a large amount of plunder from the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:3; PSALMS 21:8–9; Amos 1:3"> David took the inhabitants and forced them to work with saws, iron picks, and axes, and he also made them work making bricks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Hebrew here is unclear.">e</a></sup>He did the same in all the Ammonite towns. Then David and the whole Israelite army returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
