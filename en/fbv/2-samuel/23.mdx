<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; PSALMS 89:20"> These are David's last words. The divine message of David son of Jesse, the divine message of the man made great by God, the one anointed by the God of Jacob, the wonderful psalm-writer of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:21; Matthew 22:43; Mark 12:36"> “The Spirit of the Lord spoke through me; my tongue gave his message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Deuteronomy 32:4; Exodus 18:21"> Israel's God spoke; Israel's Rock told me, ‘He who rules the people justly, he who rules respecting God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 5:31; PSALMS 72:6; Hosea 6:5"> is like the light of the morning sunrise on a cloudless dawn; like the shining of raindrops on the new grass growing from the earth.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 7:14–16; Isaiah 9:6–7"> Isn't this how my family is with God? For he has made an everlasting agreement with me, set out in detail and with every part guaranteed. He will make sure to save me and to give me all I want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 33:12; Deuteronomy 13:13"> But evil people are like thorns to be thrown aside; they can't be held in the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 6:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Isaiah 27:4"> The only way to deal with them is to use an iron tool or the handle of a spear. They are completely burned up right where they are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of the leading warriors who supported David: Josheb-Basshebeth, a Tahkemonite, leader of the Three. Using his spear, he once killed eight hundred men in a single battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> After him came Eleazar, son of Dodai, the Ahohite, one of the Three leading warriors. He was with David when they defied the Philistines gathered for battle at Pas-dammin. The Israelites retreated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> but Eleazar took his stand and went on killing Philistines until his hand stuck to his sword. The Lord saved them by granting them a great victory. The Israelite army did return, but only to strip the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> After him came Shammah, son of Agee, the Hararite. When the Philistines gathered at Lehi, in a field full of lentils, the Israelite army ran away from them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; PSALMS 3:8"> but Shammah took his stand in the middle of the field, defending it and killing the Philistines. The Lord gave them a great victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> At harvest-time, the Three, who were part of the Thirty leading warriors, went down to meet David when he was at the cave of Adullam. The Philistine army was camped in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> At the time David was in the stronghold, and the Philistine garrison was in Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; PSALMS 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> David was feeling really thirsty, and he said, “If only someone could bring me a drink of water from the well beside the entrance gate to Bethlehem!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> The Three leading warriors broke through the Philistine defenses, took some water from the well at Bethelehem's gate, and brought it back to David. But David refused to drink it, and poured it out as an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> “Lord, never let me do this!” he said. “Isn't it like drinking the blood of these men who risked their lives?” So he did not drink it. These are just some of the things the Three leading warriors did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> Abishai, Joab's brother, was leader of the second Three.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="However, Jashobeam has already been mentioned as leader of the Three (11:11), and the killing of 300 by his spear has also been mentioned. Some suggest a confusion of names or alternate spelling, or that this refers to another person altogether as leader not of the Three but the Thirty, or that there was another “Three.”">a</a></sup>Using his spear, he once killed 300 men, and became famous among the Three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> He was the most highly regarded of the Three and was their commander, though he was not one of the first Three.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Identifying a first and second Three seems to be the simplest solution to what are confusing verses.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, a strong warrior from Kabzeel, did many amazing things. He killed two sons of Ariel of Moab.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Septuagint understanding; it may refer to two fighting champions of Moab.">c</a></sup>He also went after a lion into a pit in the snow and killed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> Another time he killed a huge Egyptian. The Egyptian had a spear in his hand but Benaiah attacked him with just a club. He grabbed the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These were the kind of things Benaiah did that made him as famous as the Three leading warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He was the most highly regarded of the Thirty, though he was not one of the Three. David put him in charge of his personal bodyguard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 1 Chronicles 27:7; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Included in the Thirty were: Asahel, Joab's brother; Elhanan, son of Dodo, from Bethlehem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 7:1; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28"> Shammoth the Harorite; Elika the Harodite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28; 1 Chronicles 27:9–10"> Helez the Paltite; Ira, son of Ikkesh, from Tekoa;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:19; 1 Chronicles 11:28"> Abiezer from Anathoth; Mebunnai the Hushathite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:13; 2 Kings 25:23; 1 Chronicles 11:30"> Zalmon the Ahohite; Maharai the Netophahite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30–31; Joshua 18:28; 1 Chronicles 27:15"> Heleb, son of Baanah the Netophahite; Ittai, son of Ribai from Gibeah of the Benjamites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 12:15; 1 Chronicles 27:14; Judges 12:13"> Benaiah the Pirathonite; Hiddai from the streams of Gaash;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Chronicles 11:32–33"> Abi-albon the Arbathite; Azmaveth the Baharumite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:34"> Eliahba the Shaalbonite; the sons of Jashem; Jonathan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:11; 1 Chronicles 11:35"> son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew does not have “son of.”">d</a></sup>Shagee the Hararite; Ahiam, son of Sachar the Hararite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; 2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Eliphelet, son of Ahasbai, son of the Maacathite, Eliam, son of Ahithophel, the Gilonite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; Joshua 15:55"> Hezro the Carmelite, Paarai the Arbite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:3; 1 Chronicles 11:38"> Igal, son of Nathan of Zobah, Bani the Gadite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; 1 Chronicles 11:39; 2 Samuel 4:2"> Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Beerothite, the armor-bearer of Joab, son of Zeruiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:53; 2 Samuel 20:26; 1 Chronicles 11:40"> Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; Matthew 1:6; 1 Kings 15:5"> and Uriah the Hittite; a total of thirty-seven.</span>
