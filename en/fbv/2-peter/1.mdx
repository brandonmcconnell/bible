<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Romans 3:21–26; Titus 1:4"> This letter comes from Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who share with us the same priceless trust in our God and Savior Jesus Christ, the one who is truly good and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; John 17:3; Philippians 3:8"> May you have ever more grace and peace as you grow in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30"> Through his divine power we have been given everything that's important to live a God-centered life. This comes through knowing him who called us to himself by his own glory and goodness. In this way he's given us wonderful, priceless promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> Through these promises you can share in the divine nature, being rid of the corruption caused by this world's evil desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; 2 Peter 3:18"> For this same reason, do everything you can! Add to your trust in God, goodness; add to goodness, knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Romans 5:3–4; Luke 21:19"> add to knowledge, self-control; add to self-control, patience; add to patience, reverence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:12"> add to reverence, affection for fellow-believers, add to affection for fellow-believers, love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; John 15:2; Hebrews 6:12"> The more you have these qualities, the more they will keep you from being ineffective and unproductive in your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18–20; Ephesians 5:26; Titus 2:14"> For anyone who doesn't have such qualities is short-sighted, or blind. They forget that they've been cleansed from their past sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Luke 6:47–49; Hebrews 6:11"> So, brothers and sisters, be all the more determined to make sure you are truly “called and chosen.” If you do this, you won't ever fall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="There has been much debate over this verse. The idea is that you are meant to do all you can to hold onto salvation. It does not necessarily lead to the conclusion that you cannot lose salvation.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Matthew 25:34; Revelation 3:21"> You will receive a tremendous welcome into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> That's why I always remind you about these things, even though you know them already, and you stand firm in the truth that you possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> But I still think it's a good idea to encourage you by giving you reminders while I live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> I know the time when I shall leave this life will be coming soon— our Lord Jesus Christ has made this clear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; PSALMS 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> I will do my best so that when I'm gone you will always be able to remember these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:14; 1 Timothy 1:4"> We didn't follow made-up myths when we told you about the coming in power of our Lord Jesus Christ—we saw his majesty for ourselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This passage is a reference to the Transfiguration.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:7; Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17"> He received honor and glory from God the Father when the voice of majestic glory spoke to him and announced, “This is my Son, the one I love, and who truly pleases me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Matthew 17:5or parallel passage.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> We ourselves heard this voice speak from heaven when we were with him on the holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; PSALMS 119:105"> We also have the confirming word of prophecy that's absolutely trustworthy, and you will benefit from paying close attention to it. It's like a lamp that shines in a dark place until the day dawns, and the morning star shines to illuminate your minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; Romans 12:6; Romans 6:6"> Most of all you should realize that no prophecy of scripture is a matter for interpretation based on the whims of an individual,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 1 Peter 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> for no prophecy originated in human ideas, but prophets spoke for God as they were moved by the Holy Spirit.</span>
