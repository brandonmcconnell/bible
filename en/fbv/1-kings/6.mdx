<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> Four hundred and eighty years after the Israelites left Egypt, in the fourth year of King Solomon's reign, in the month of Ziv, Solomon began building the Temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> The Temple King Solomon built for the Lord measured sixty cubits long by twenty cubits wide by thirty cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> The entrance room at the front of the Temple was twenty cubits wide. It ran the whole width of the Temple and projected out ten cubits in front of the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> He had latticed windows made to be placed high up in the Temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> He also had a structure built against the outer wall of the Temple, both the outer and the inner sanctuary, to provide a series of side rooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The ground floor measured five cubits wide, the first floor six cubits, and the second floor seven cubits. In addition he had offset ledges placed all the way around the outside, so that beams would not have to be inserted into the Temple walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> When the Temple was built the stones were finished at the quarry so that no noise from any hammer, ax, or any iron tool was heard in the Temple during construction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The entrance to the ground floor was on the south side of the Temple. Stairs led to the first floor, and then on to the second floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So Solomon finished building the Temple, covering it with a roof made of cedar rafters and planks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He built the external structure along the whole of the Temple. It was five cubits high, joined to the Temple with cedar joists.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord sent this message to Solomon, telling him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “About this Temple you are building: if you follow what I have said, fulfilling my requirements and keeping my commandments in what you do, I will keep the promise I made to your father David through you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> I will live among the Israelites and will not abandon Israel, my people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> Solomon finished building the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> He lined the walls with cedar panels from floor to ceiling and he covered the floor of the Temple with cypress boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> He sectioned off twenty cubits at the rear of the Temple with boards of cedar from floor to ceiling, making an inner sanctuary as the Most Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The main Temple in front of this room measured forty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> The cedar panels inside the Temple were decorated with carvings of gourds and open flowers. Everything was lined with cedar—none of the stone was visible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; PSALMS 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> He also had the inner sanctuary made within the Temple where the Ark of the Lord's Agreement would be placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> The inner sanctuary measured twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty cubits high. He had the interior covered with a coating with pure gold, as well as the altar of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon had the whole of the inside of the Temple covered with pure gold, He had gold chains stretched across the front of the inner sanctuary, which was also covered with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> He covered the whole inside of the Temple with a coating of gold until it was all finished. He also covered with gold the entire altar for the inner sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> He had two cherubim made out of olive wood for the inner sanctuary, each one ten cubits tall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Both wings of the cherub measured five cubits, making a total wingspan of ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The other cherub was also ten cubits tall, since they were identical both in size and shape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Both cherubs were ten cubits tall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> He had the cherubim placed inside the inner sanctuary of the Temple. Since their wings were fully spread, a wing of the first cherub touched one wall, a wing of the second cherub touched the other wall, and in the middle of the Temple their wings touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The cherubim were also covered with a gold coating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; PSALMS 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> He had all the Temple walls, both the inner and outer sanctuaries, carved with designs of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> He also had the floor of the Temple covered with gold, both the inner and outer sanctuaries.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> He had doors of olive wood made for the entrance to the inner sanctuary, together with a lintel and doorposts that had five sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> These olive wood double doors had carved into them designs of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers. The cherubim and palm trees carvings were covered with beaten gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Similarly he had four-sided doorposts of olive wood made for the entrance to the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> Its doors were made of cypress wood, each with two hinged panels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He had them carved with designs of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and covered them with gold beaten evenly over the carvings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> Solomon had the inner courtyard built with three courses of dressed stone and one of cedar beams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> The foundation of the Lord's Temple was laid in the fourth year of Solomon's reign, in the month of Ziv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> The Temple was finished exactly as specified and planned in Solomon's eleventh year, in the eighth month, the month of Bul. So it took him seven years to build the Temple.</span>
</div>
</div>
