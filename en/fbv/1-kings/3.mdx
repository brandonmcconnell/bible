<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 11:1; 1 Kings 6:1–15"> Solomon made a marriage alliance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Marriage alliance”: the word here literally means “make oneself a daughter's husband.”">a</a></sup>with Pharaoh of Egypt. He married Pharaoh's daughter and brought her to live in the City of David until he finished building his palace, the Lord's Temple, and the walls surrounding Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:43; 2 Chronicles 33:17; Deuteronomy 12:2–5"> In those days, however, the people still sacrificed on the high places because a Temple to honor the Lord hadn't yet been built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; PSALMS 31:23; Deuteronomy 30:16"> Solomon showed he loved the Lord by following the instructions of his father David, except that he sacrificed and burned offerings on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:39; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 2 Chronicles 7:5"> The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for it was the leading high place. Solomon presented one thousand burnt offerings on the altar there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 9:2; James 1:5–6; Numbers 12:6"> The Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream at Gibeon. God said to him, “Ask what you want me to give you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 1:48; 2 Corinthians 9:11"> Solomon replied, “You showed your servant David, my father, great trustworthy love because he lived his life before you with faithfulness, doing what was right and committed to principle. You have continued to show this great trustworthy love by giving him a son to sit on his throne to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:1; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 1:6"> Now, Lord God, you have made me king in place of my father David. But I am like an inexperienced young boy who doesn't know what to do.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“What to do”: literally, “going out or coming in.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 13:16; 1 Chronicles 21:2"> I, your servant, am here among your chosen people, a great people that are so many they cannot be counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 16:16; Hebrews 5:14"> So please give me a mind that's receptive so I can rule<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Rule not so much in terms of imposing control but in governing wisely.">c</a></sup>your people well, understanding the difference between right and wrong, for who can rule this difficult people of yours?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8"> The Lord considered that what Solomon asked for was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:26; James 4:2–3; Proverbs 16:31"> So God told him, “Because you asked for this, and you didn't ask for a long life, or wealth, or the death of your enemies, but instead you asked for understanding to know what is right,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Ecclesiastes 1:16; Matthew 12:42"> I am giving you what you asked for. I am giving you a mind that is wise, with an understanding of what is right, more than anyone else before you or after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:16"> I am also giving you what you did not ask for, wealth and status—so much so that no king will compare to you for the whole of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; PSALMS 91:16; 1 Kings 15:5"> And if you follow my ways by keeping my laws and my commands, as your father David did, I will give you a long life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 41:7; 1 Kings 8:65; Esther 1:3"> Then Solomon woke up and realized he'd had a dream. He went back to Jerusalem, and stood in front of the Ark of the Lord's Agreement and he presented burnt offerings and friendship offerings, and he held a feast for all his officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:16; Judges 11:1; Numbers 27:2"> Later two prostitutes came to the king and stood before him for judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“For judgment”: implied by the fact they came before him.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Genesis 43:20"> One of the women spoke up, saying, “If it please Your Majesty: I share a house with this woman. I had a baby while she was living in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Three days after the birth of my baby, this woman also had a baby. We were together; there was no one else in the house, just the two of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> During the night this woman's son died because she rolled over on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 13:25; PSALMS 139:11"> She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while I was sleeping. She lay him close beside her to cuddle him, and she lay her dead son close beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; 1 Samuel 1:23; Lamentations 4:3–4"> When I got up in the morning to nurse my son I saw that he was dead. When I looked closely at him in the light I realized it wasn't my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 3:23–24"> The other woman argued, “No! My son is the one that's alive. Your son is the one that's dead.” The first woman objected, “No! Your son is the one that's dead. My son is the one that's alive.” They went on quarrelling in front of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The king intervened, saying, “So this woman says, ‘My son is the one that's alive. Your son is the one that's dead,’ while the other woman says, ‘No! Your son is the one that's dead. My son is the one that's alive.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> “Bring me a sword,” the king ordered. So they brought him a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8"> “Cut the child that's alive in two, and give half to one woman and half to another,” he commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 John 3:17"> But the woman whose son was alive had so much love for him as a mother that she cried out to the king, “Please, Your Majesty, give her the boy! Don't kill him!” But the other woman said, “He won't be mine or yours—cut him in two!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The king gave his verdict. “Give the child that's alive to the first woman,” he ordered. “On no account kill him, for she is his real mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; 1 Kings 3:9–12; Colossians 2:3"> When everyone in Israel heard about the verdict the king had delivered, they had great respect for the king, because they recognized the wisdom God had given him to judge rightly.</span>
