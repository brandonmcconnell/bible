<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; 1 Kings 13:33–34; 1 Samuel 31:2"> It was at that time that Abijah, Jeroboam's son, fell ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Samuel 14:2"> So Jeroboam told his wife, “Please go and disguise yourself so nobody will know you're Jeroboam's wife. Then go to Shiloh and look for Ahijah the prophet. He was the one who told me I would become king over this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; 1 Kings 13:7; 2 Kings 4:42"> Take with you ten loaves of bread, some cakes, and a jar of honey for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“For him”: implied.">a</a></sup>He will explain to you what will happen to the boy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29; 1 Samuel 3:2; 1 Samuel 4:15"> Jeroboam's wife did as she was told. She got up and went to Ahijah's house in Shiloh. Ahijah could not see—he had become blind because of his age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 6:8–12; Proverbs 21:30; 2 Kings 4:27"> But the Lord had told Ahijah, “Look, Jeroboam's wife is coming to ask you about her son, because he's ill. This is what you are to tell her, because she'll come in disguise.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:17–28; Acts 5:9–10; Luke 20:20–23"> So as soon as Ahijah heard her footsteps at the door, he called out, “Come in, wife of Jeroboam! Why do you bother coming in disguise? I have been given some bad news for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 16:2; 2 Samuel 12:7–8; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Go and tell Jeroboam this is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: I picked you from the masses and I made you ruler over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 11:33–38; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> I took the kingdom from the house of David and gave it to you. But you were not like my servant David, who kept my commandments and was totally committed to following me, only doing what was right in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; PSALMS 50:17"> You have done more evil than all those<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Probably referring to previous kings.">b</a></sup>who lived before you. You have gone and made other gods for yourself, idols made of molten metal that made me angry. You have tossed me aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 14:26"> Now pay attention, because as a result of this I am going to bring disaster on the house of Jeroboam. I will totally exterminate every one of your descendants in Israel, whether slave or free. I will burn the house of Jeroboam like a man burning refuse until it's all gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; Jeremiah 15:3; 1 Kings 21:23–24"> Those of Jeroboam's family who die in the town will be eaten by dogs, and those who die in the countryside will be eaten by birds. For the Lord has spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 1 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 1:6"> As for you, get up and go home. As soon as you arrive in the city, the child will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:12; Job 19:28"> All of Israel will mourn for him, and they will bury him. He alone of Jeroboam's family will be buried in a tomb<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Receiving a proper burial was considered very important in Israelite society.">c</a></sup>because only in him has the Lord, the God of Israel found anything good—of the whole family of Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27–29; Ezekiel 7:2–7; Ezekiel 12:22–28"> The Lord will choose for himself a king to rule over Israel who will destroy the house of Jeroboam. This is starting to happen even now!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:15–16; PSALMS 52:5; 2 Kings 15:29"> The Lord will strike Israel like a reed jerked to and fro by the water. He will pull up Israel by the roots from this good land that he gave their forefathers and scatter them beyond the Euphrates, because they have made their pagan Asherah poles, making the Lord angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 16:2; 1 Kings 13:34"> He will abandon Israel because of the sins of Jeroboam, those that he committed himself, and those he made Israel commit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 16:15; 1 Kings 16:23"> Jeroboam's wife got up and left for Tirzah. As soon as she stepped through the doorway of her home, the boy died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:13"> All of Israel buried him and mourned for him, just as the Lord had said through his servant Ahijah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–20; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 1 Kings 16:20"> The rest of what Jeroboam did, how he engaged in warfare and how he reigned, they are recorded in the Book of Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:12; PSALMS 3:5; 1 Kings 11:43"> Jeroboam reigned for twenty-two years, and then he died. His son Nadab succeeded him as king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:13; 1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 14:31"> Rehoboam, son of Solomon, reigned in Judah. He was forty-one when he became king, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the Lord had chosen from all the tribes of Israel where he would be honored. The name of his mother was Naamah the Ammonite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 1 Corinthians 10:22; PSALMS 78:58"> Judah did what was evil in the Lord's sight, and because of the sins they committed they made his jealous anger even greater than all their fathers had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 Kings 17:9–10"> They also set up for themselves high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Corinthians 6:9; 1 Kings 15:12"> There were even cult prostitutes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring to both males and females.">d</a></sup>in the land. They followed all the disgusting practices of the nations that the Lord had driven out before the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 12:2–4; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> In the fifth year of King Rehoboam's reign, Shishak, king of Egypt, attacked Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 7:51"> He took the treasures of the Lord's Temple and the royal palace. He took everything, including all the gold shields Solomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 22:17; 1 Kings 18:46"> So King Rehoboam made bronze shields to replace them and handed them over to the captains of the guard to look after. They stood on duty at the entrance to the royal palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:11"> Whenever the king went to the Lord's Temple, the guards would carry the shields. Afterwards they returned them to the guardroom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 22:45; 1 Kings 15:23; 1 Kings 14:19"> The rest of what happened in Rehoboam's reign and everything that he did are recorded in the Book of Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 1 Kings 12:21; 1 Kings 15:6–7"> Rehoboam and Jeroboam were always at war with each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; Matthew 1:7; 1 Kings 15:3"> Rehoboam died and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. The name of his mother was Naamah the Ammonite. His son Abijam succeeded him as king.</span>
</div>
