<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12"> The queen of Sheba heard how famous Solomon was, so she came to Jerusalem to test him with tough questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:23; PSALMS 4:4; Job 4:2"> She brought with her a very large entourage, with camels loaded with spices, large amounts of gold, and precious gemstones. She came to Solomon and asked him about everything she had on her mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5–6; Matthew 13:11; 1 Kings 10:1"> Solomon answered all her questions. There was nothing he couldn't explain to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; 1 Kings 3:28; 2 Chronicles 9:3–4"> When the queen of Sheba saw Solomon's wisdom, and the palace he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:16; 1 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 16:18"> the food on the table, how his officials lived, how his servants operated and how they were dressed, the clothes of the waiters, and the burnt offerings he presented at the Lord's Temple, she was so astonished<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“She was so astonished”: implied by the phrase (literally) “there was no longer breath in her.”">a</a></sup>she could hardly breathe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:5–6"> She told the king, “It's true what I heard in my own country about your proverbs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Proverbs”: literally, “words.”">b</a></sup>and your wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; John 20:25–29; 1 John 3:2"> But I didn't believe what they told me until I came and saw with my own eyes. In fact, I wasn't told the half of it—the extent of your wisdom far exceeds what I heard!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Proverbs 10:21; Luke 11:31"> How happy your people must be! How happy those who work for you, who stand here every day listening to your wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 72:2; 1 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 8:15"> Praise the Lord your God who is so pleased with you, who placed you on his throne as king to rule on his behalf. Because of the love of your God for Israel he has made them secure forever, and he has made you king over them to do what is fair and right.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 10:2; Matthew 2:11; 1 Kings 9:14"> She presented the king with one hundred and twenty talents of gold, huge amounts of spices and precious stones. Never before had there been spices like those the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 8:18; PSALMS 45:9"> (Hiram's fleet of ships brought gold from Ophir, and also carried algum wood and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–31; PSALMS 150:3–5; Revelation 14:2–3"> The king used the algum wood to make steps<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Steps”: or “railings.”">c</a></sup>for the Temple and for the royal palace, and into lyres and harps for the musicians. Nothing like them had ever been seen before in the land of Judah.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; Matthew 15:28; PSALMS 20:4"> King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba all she wanted, whatever she asked for. This was in addition to the usual gifts he had generously given her. Then she and her attendants returned home to her own country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:13–28; 1 Kings 9:28"> The weight of gold that Solomon received each year was 666 talents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 72:10; 1 Chronicles 9:24; Isaiah 21:13"> not including that received from traders and merchants, and all the kings of Arabia and governors of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26–28; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16"> King Solomon made two hundred shields of hammered gold. Each shield required six hundred shekels of hammered gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 14:26"> He also made three hundred small shields of hammered gold. Each of these shields required three gold minas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A mina was worth around 50 shekels.">d</a></sup>The king placed them in the Palace of the Forest of Lebanon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; 1 Kings 10:22; Revelation 20:11"> The king also made a great throne of ivory, and covered it with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The throne had six steps, with a rounded top<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Rounded top”: the Septuagint has “calves,” in other words a carving depicting calves.">e</a></sup>at the back. There were armrests on both sides of the seat, with lions standing beside the armrests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 23:24"> Twelve lions stood on the six steps, one on opposite ends of each step. Nothing like this had ever been made for any kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 10:17; 1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 9:20–22"> All of King Solomon's drinking cups were gold, and all the utensils of the Palace of the Forest of Lebanon were pure gold. No silver was used, because it was not valued in the days of Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Genesis 10:4; 2 Chronicles 20:36–37"> The king had a fleet of ships from Tarshish crewed by Hiram's sailors. Once every three years the ships of Tarshish would arrive with a cargo of gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12–13; Colossians 2:2–3; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> King Solomon was greater than any other king on earth in wealth and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; 1 Kings 3:28"> The whole world wanted to meet Solomon to hear the wisdom that God had placed in his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 42:11; PSALMS 72:10; PSALMS 72:15"> Year after year, every visitor would bring gifts—articles of silver and gold, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Kings 4:26; Deuteronomy 17:16"> Solomon accumulated 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen. He kept them in the chariot towns, and also with him in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> The king made silver as plentiful in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar wood as plentiful as sycamore-figs in the foothills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; 2 Chronicles 9:28; Isaiah 31:1–3"> Solomon's horses were imported from Egypt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Egypt”: or Musri (Cappadocia).">f</a></sup>and Kue—the royal merchants purchased them in Kue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; 2 Kings 7:6–7; Malachi 1:1"> A chariot imported from Egypt cost six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse a hundred and fifty. They also exported them to all the Hittite kings, and to the Aramean kings.</span>
</div>
</div>
