<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:12; 2 Chronicles 9:30; 1 Kings 12:19–20"> King Solomon ruled over all of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 2 Samuel 8:15–18"> These were his officials: Azariah, son of Zadok, was the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 Chronicles 18:15"> Elihoreph and Ahijah, sons of Shisha, were the king's secretaries. Jehoshaphat, son of Ahilud, kept the royal records.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada was the army commander. Zadok and Abiathar were priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:33; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 16:16"> Azariah, son of Nathan, was in charge of the governors. Zabud, son of Nathan, was a priest and the king's counselor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 12:18"> Ahishar was the palace manager. Adoniram, son of Abda, was in charge of those forced to work for the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> Solomon had twelve area governors whose responsibilities covered the whole of Israel, providing food for the king and his household. Each one in turn arranged supplies for one month of the year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> Their names were: Ben-hur, in the hill country of Ephraim;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> Ben-deker in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh and Elon-beth-hanan;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> Ben-hesed in Arubboth (Socoh and all the land of Hepher was his);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> Ben-abinadab, in the whole of Naphath-dor (Taphath, the daughter of Solomon, was his wife);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Baana, son of Ahilud, in Taanach and Megiddo, the whole of Beth-shan near Zarethan below Jezreel, and from Beth-shan to Abel-meholah and across to Jokmeam;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> Ben-geber, in Ramoth-gilead (the towns of Jair, son of Manasseh, in Gilead belonged to him, as well as the region of Argob in Bashan, with sixty great cities having walls and bronze bars);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; 2 Samuel 17:24; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Ahinadab, son of Iddo, in Mahanaim;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Ahimaaz, in Naphtali (he had married Basemath the daughter of Solomon);</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Baana, son of Hushai, in Asher and in Aloth;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23"> Jehoshaphat, son of Paruah, in Issachar;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zechariah 12:13"> Shimei, son of Ela, in Benjamin;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Geber son of Uri in the land of Gilead (the former country of Sihon, king of the Amorites, and of Og, king of Bashan. There was also a governor who was over the land of Judah).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew text is unclear. The Septuagint reading is followed here, indicating that there was also a governor over Judah. Some however take this to refer to the previous governor mentioned who was the sole governor during this reign.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> Judah and Israel had become as numerous as the sand on the seashore. They ate, they drank, they were happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; PSALMS 68:29"> Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the Euphrates River to the land of the Philistines, right up to the border of Egypt. They presented tribute to Solomon and served him during his lifetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The food required every day for Solomon's court was thirty cors of the best flour and sixty cors of meal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> ten fattened cattle, twenty range cattle, one hundred sheep, as well as deer, gazelles, roe deer, and fattened poultry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; PSALMS 72:11; PSALMS 72:3"> For Solomon ruled over the whole region west of the Euphrates, from Tiphsah to Gaza—over all the kingdoms west of the Euphrates. And he had peace on all sides around him. He had peace on every border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> During Solomon's lifetime, everyone in Judah and Israel lived in safety, from Dan to Beersheba. Each one had their own vine and fig tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> Solomon had 40,000 stalls for his chariot horses, and 12,000 charioteers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Every month the area governors provided in turn food for King Solomon and all who ate at his table. They made sure that nothing was missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> They also delivered barley and straw where they were needed for the chariot horses and cart horses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> God gave to Solomon wisdom, very great discernment, and understanding as extensive as the sand on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> Solomon's wisdom was greater than that of all the Eastern wise men, greater than all of Egypt's wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> He was wiser than anyone, wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, wiser than Heman, Calcol, and Darda, sons of Mahol. His reputation spread through the nations around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> Solomon composed three thousand proverbs and one thousand and five songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; PSALMS 51:7; PSALMS 92:12"> He was able to discuss knowledge of trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop that grows in the wall. He taught about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:23; 1 Kings 10:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> People from all nations came to hear Solomon's wisdom. They were sent by all the kings of the earth, who had heard about his wisdom.</span>
</div>
