<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 9:6; 2 Chronicles 10:1–19; Judges 9:1"> Rehoboam went to Shechem because that is where the whole of Israel had gone to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 10:2–3; 1 Kings 11:26–31"> Jeroboam, son of Nebat, was still in Egypt when he heard about this. (He had run away to Egypt to escape from King Solomon and was living there.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The Israelite leaders sent for him. Jeroboam and the whole assembly of Israelites went to talk with Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; 1 Kings 4:7; Matthew 11:29–30"> “Your father placed a heavy burden on us,” they told him. “But now if you lighten the load from when we served your father and the heavy demands he imposed on us, we will serve you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12"> Rehoboam answered, “Go away and come back in three days' time.” So the people left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 12:12; Job 32:7; 2 Samuel 16:20"> King Rehoboam asked for advice from the elders who had served his father Solomon when he was alive. “How do you advise me to reply to these people about this?” he asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; 2 Chronicles 10:6–7; 1 Kings 12:13"> They replied, “If you are a servant to these people today, if you serve them and answer them, by speaking kindly to them, they will always serve you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Ecclesiastes 10:2–3; 2 Chronicles 10:8"> But Rehoboam dismissed the advice of the elders. Instead he consulted the young men he had grown up with, and who were close to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6–8; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:9"> He asked them, “What response do you advise that we send back to these people who have told me, ‘Lighten the burden your father put on us’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:10–11; Proverbs 10:14; Isaiah 47:6"> The young men he had grown up with told him, “This is what you have to tell these people who said to you, ‘Your father made our burden heavy, but you should make it lighter.’ This is what you should answer them: ‘My little finger is thicker than my father's waist!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 28:13–14"> My father placed a heavy burden on you, and I will make it even heavier. My father punished you with whips; I will punish you with scorpions.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 10:12–14"> Three days' later, Jeroboam and all the people returned to Rehoboam, because the king had told them, “Come back in three days time.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 13:20; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> The king answered the people sharply. Dismissing the advice of the elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 5:16–18; 1 Kings 12:10–11; Exodus 5:5–9"> he replied using the advice of the young men. He said, “My father placed a heavy burden on you, and I will make it even heavier. My father punished you with whips; I will punish you with scorpions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; Deuteronomy 2:30; Judges 14:4"> The king did not listen to what the people said, for this change in circumstances was from the Lord, to fulfill what the Lord had told Jeroboam son of Nebat through Ahijah the Shilonite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:16"> When all the Israelites saw that the king wasn't listening to them, they told the king: “What share do we have in David, and what part do we have in the son of Jesse? Go home, Israel! You're on your own, house of David!”<br />So all the Israelites went home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 11:13; 2 Chronicles 11:13–17"> However, Rehoboam still ruled over the Israelites who lived in Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 5:14"> Then King Rehoboam sent out Hadoram, who was in charge of forced labor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="He was sent out to put down the rebellion.">a</a></sup>but the Israelites stoned him to death. King Rehoboam quickly jumped into his chariot and raced back to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Joshua 4:9"> As a result, Israel has been in rebellion against the house of David to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 11:32; Hosea 8:4"> When all the Israelites heard that Jeroboam had returned, they sent for him, summoning him to the assembly and made him king over all Israel. Only the tribe of Judah was left to the house of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1–3; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8"> When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he gathered the men from the households of Judah and Benjamin— 180,000 chosen warriors—to go and fight against Israel to bring the kingdom back to Rehoboam, son of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 1 Kings 13:1"> But a message from the Lord came to Shemaiah the man of God that said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 12:17"> “Say to Rehoboam, son of Solomon, king of Judah, to Judah and Benjamin, and to the rest of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:7–8; Hosea 8:4"> ‘This is what the Lord says. Don't fight against your Israelite relatives. Every one of you, go home! For it was me that made this happen.’” So they obeyed what the Lord told them and went home, as the Lord had said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 8:17; Judges 8:8; Judges 9:45–49"> Jeroboam strengthened<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Strengthened”: literally, “built,” but Shechem existed long before this (see for example Genesis 12:6).">b</a></sup>the town of Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim and lived there. From there he went and built Penuel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:18–21; Isaiah 7:9; Mark 2:6–8"> Jeroboam said to himself, “The kingdom could easily return to the house of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Proverbs 29:25; Deuteronomy 12:14"> When people from here go to offer sacrifices at the Lord's Temple in Jerusalem, they will transfer their loyalty back to Rehoboam, king of Judah. Then they will kill me and go back to King Rehoboam.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Exodus 32:8; 2 Kings 17:16"> So after taking advice, the king had two golden calves made, and he told the people, “Don't bother going to Jerusalem any more. Look, Israel, here are your gods who led you out of the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Judges 18:27–31; Hosea 4:15"> He placed one in Bethel, and the other in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 1 Kings 13:34; 2 Kings 10:31"> This action brought about sin, because the people went as far north as Dan to worship the idol there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; 2 Kings 17:32; 1 Kings 13:32–33"> In addition Jeroboam had shrines built on high places and appointed as priests all kinds of people who were not Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Kings 8:2; Amos 7:10–13"> Jeroboam initiated a festival on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, just like the festival held in Judah, and he offered sacrifices on the altar. He made this offering in Bethel, sacrificing to the calves he had made, and appointed priests in Bethel for the high places he had built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Numbers 15:39; Matthew 15:6"> So on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, a month he had chosen himself, Jeroboam offered sacrifices on the altar he had set up in Bethel. In this way he instituted a festival for the Israelites, offering sacrifices on the altar and burning incense.</span>
</div>
