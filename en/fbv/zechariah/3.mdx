</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Isaiah 11:1; Zechariah 6:12"> Pay attention, high priest Joshua and all your fellow-priests that you teach!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “friends who sit before you.”">d</a></sup>You are a sign of good things to come. Look! I am going to bring my servant, the Branch.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Both “my servant” and “the branch” are titles applied to the Messiah.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> See that I have placed a precious stone in front of Joshua, a single stone with seven faces. Watch, for I myself am going to engrave seven eyes on it, declares the Lord Almighty, and I will wipe away the sins of this land in a single day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4; Zechariah 2:11"> On that day everyone will invite their friends to sit in peace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“In peace” implied.">f</a></sup>under their vines and fig trees, says the Lord Almighty.”</span>
