<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Jeremiah 26:19; 1 Samuel 13:12"> Bethel-sharezer sent Regem-melech and his men to ask for the Lord's blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 8:19; James 4:8–10; Malachi 2:7"> They were to ask the priests of the Temple of the Lord Almighty and the prophets, “Should I go on mourning and fasting in the fifth month, as I have done for so many years?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> The Lord Almighty sent a message to me saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:12; Isaiah 58:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Tell everyone in the land and the priests, when you fasted and mourned in the fifth month and the seventh month during these seventy years, was it really me you were fasting for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:20–21; 1 Corinthians 11:26–29; Hosea 8:13"> And when you eat and drink, don't you eat and drink for yourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–20; Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 7:5"> Isn't this what the Lord told you to do through the former prophets, when Jerusalem was prosperous and inhabited, and when people were living in the Negev and the Shephelah?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The Negev and the Shephelah”: the area to the south and the west.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The Lord Almighty sent another message to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:8; PSALMS 82:2–4; Proverbs 21:3"> This is what the Lord says. Judge fairly and truthfully. Show mercy and kindness to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Isaiah 1:23; James 5:4"> Don't exploit those who are widowed or fatherless, foreigners or the poor. Don't think up ways of mistreating one other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Jeremiah 8:5; Hosea 4:16"> But they refused to listen. They were obstinate, turning their backs and closing their ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Nehemiah 9:29–30; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> They made their minds unreceptive, as hard as stone. They refused to listen to the law or to what the Lord Almighty told them by his Spirit through the former prophets. That's why the Lord Almighty became very angry with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–28; Jeremiah 14:12; Isaiah 1:15"> So since they didn't listen when I called out to them, I wouldn't listen when they called out to me, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 23:19"> With the winds of a storm I scattered them among the nations where they lived as foreigners. The land they left became so desolate that no one even traveled through it. They turned the Promised Land into a desert.</span>
</div>
