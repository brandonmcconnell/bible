<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 6:1"> The chariot with the black horses went north, the one with the white horses went west, and the one with the dappled grey horses went south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; Zechariah 1:10; Job 1:6–7"> When the strong horses came out they were eager to set off to patrol the earth. And he said, “Go and patrol the earth!” So they left and patrolled the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 18:3–4"> Then the angel called to me, saying, “Look! Those who went north have achieved what the Lord wanted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Achieved what the Lord wanted”: literally, “set at rest my spirit.”">b</a></sup>in the land of the north.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:1; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:1"> Then the Lord gave me another message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Isaiah 66:20"> Take the gifts brought by Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, the exiles returning from Babylon, and go immediately to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; PSALMS 21:3; Song of Songs 3:11"> Use the silver and the gold they brought to make a crown, and place it on the head of Josiah, son of Jehozadak, the high priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 3:8; Isaiah 4:2; Jeremiah 33:15"> Tell him this is what the Lord Almighty says: Look! The man who is called the Branch will grow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sprout.”">c</a></sup>from where he is and will build the Lord's Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 110:4; Isaiah 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> He is the one who will build the Lord's Temple, and he will be the one given the honor to rule from both the royal throne and the priestly throne and there will be peace and understanding between the two roles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> The crown will be kept in the Temple of the Lord as a memorial to Heldai, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Joshua<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Hen.”">d</a></sup>the son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10; Zechariah 3:7; Isaiah 58:10–14"> People who live in distant lands will come and build the Temple of the Lord, and you will know that the Lord Almighty sent me to you. This will happen if you listen attentively to what the Lord tells you.</span>
</div>
