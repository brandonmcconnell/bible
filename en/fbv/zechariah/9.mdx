<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremiah 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> A prophecy:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “burden.”">a</a></sup>A message from the Lord to the land of Hadrach, and Damascus its main objective.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Main objective” Literally, “resting place.” In other words this was the city to which the prophecy was particularly directed.">b</a></sup>For the eyes of human beings together with all the tribes of Israel are on the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This could also be translated as “for the Lord has his eye on humanity as well as all the tribes of Israel.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> and Hamath too since it is close by. Also Tyre and Sidon for they are very wise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> The people of Tyre built a fortress, and piled up silver like dust and gold like the dirt of the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> But look at what will happen. The Lord will take away everything they have, and knock down their strong defenses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Strong defenses”: literally, “power.” Alternatively this could refer to the Lord destroying the sea power of Tyre.">d</a></sup>into the sea. The city will be burned down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> The people of Ashkelon will see this and be terrified; those in Gaza will thrash around in agony like a woman giving birth; and the people of Ekron too, because their hopes are shattered. The king of Gaza will be killed, and Ashkelon will be deserted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> Mixed-race people will live in Ashdod, and I will wipe out the Philistine's pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> I will remove the blood-filled meat from their mouths; the unclean food from their jaws.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Philistines did not follow the Jewish regulations regarding slaughtering animals, particularly in draining the blood from the flesh. Consequently their eating of meat still containing blood made them unclean.">e</a></sup>Those who remain will belong to our God—they shall become like a family of Judah—and those from Ekron will become part of my people, just as the Jebusites did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> I will make my camp in my Temple to guard it from invaders, and no oppressors will conquer them, for now I myself am keeping watch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Be happy and celebrate, people of Zion! Shout loudly, people of Jerusalem! Look, your king is coming to you. He does what is right and has salvation;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Has salvation,” or “is victorious.”">f</a></sup>he is humble, riding on a donkey—on a colt, the foal of a donkey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> (I will remove the chariots from Ephraim and the warhorses from Jerusalem. I will destroy the bows used in battle.) He will proclaim peace to the nations, and he will rule from sea to sea, from the Euphrates River to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; PSALMS 102:19–21"> And as for you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Referring back to the people of Jerusalem in 9:9.">g</a></sup>because my agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “covenant.”">h</a></sup>with you, sealed with blood, I will set you free from the waterless pit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Waterless pit”: referring to the exile.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Return to the stronghold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Understood to refer to Jerusalem.">j</a></sup>you prisoners who have hope! Today I promise I will repay you double what you lost.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“What you lost” implied.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> I will use Judah as my bow, and I will load it with Ephraim my arrow. I will call up you men of Zion to fight against you men of Greece, wielding you like a warrior's sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; PSALMS 144:5–6"> Then the Lord will appear over his people and his arrow will flash like lightning! The Lord God will blow the trumpet and march out like a windstorm from the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> The Lord Almighty will protect them. They will destroy their enemies, conquering them with slingshots. They will drink and shout loudly like people who are drunk. They will be filled like a bowl, soaked liked the corners of an altar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="This refers to the sacrificial system where a bowl was used to catch the blood of the sacrifice and sprinkle the blood on the corners of the altar.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> On that day the Lord their God will save them—his people that are his flock—for they will glitter as crown jewels in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> How lovely and beautiful they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Some believe these words are applied to the Lord, but the context suggests they refer back to the jewels, the people of God.">m</a></sup>will be! Young men will grow strong on grain, and young women flourish on the new wine.</span>
</div>
</div>
