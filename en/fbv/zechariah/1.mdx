<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:1"> The Lord sent a message to Zechariah the prophet, son of Berekiah, son of Iddo, on the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month (the month of Shebat) of the second year of king Darius' reign:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; Zechariah 13:7"> During the night I saw a man sitting on a red horse that stood among some myrtle trees in a narrow valley. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> The man who was there among the myrtle trees said, “These are the ones the Lord has sent out to patrol the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; PSALMS 103:20–21"> The riders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Implied.">f</a></sup>reported to the angel of the Lord who was among the myrtle trees, “We have been patrolling the earth and saw that the whole earth has been pacified.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Pacified.” In the context this “peace” has more to do with being forced into submission and defeat than a time of harmony and tranquility. <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> So the Lord replied to the angel I was talking to with kind and comforting words.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> and I am extremely angry with the arrogant nations who think they are secure. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Zechariah 2:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> Announce this as well, says the Lord Almighty: Prosperity will flood out of my cities. I the Lord will comfort Zion, and Jerusalem will be my chosen city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; 2 Kings 24:1–20; Zechariah 5:9"> Then I looked and saw four animal horns.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Horns in prophetic writings are symbolic of powers.">l</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 10:3–5; Zechariah 12:2–6; Zechariah 9:12–16"> Then the Lord showed me four craftsmen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Probably blacksmiths or metal-workers.">m</a></sup></span>

These craftsmen have to come to terrify these nations, and to destroy them—those who used their power against the land of Judah, scattering the people.”</span>
