<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 5:2; Isaiah 8:1; Ezekiel 2:9–10"> I looked again and saw a flying scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:14; Jeremiah 1:11–14; Amos 7:8"> “What do you see?” asked the angel. “I see a flying scroll,” I replied. “It's thirty feet long and fifteen feet wide.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Twenty cubits in length and ten cubits in width.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:15; Isaiah 43:28; Isaiah 24:6"> He told me, “This is the curse that is going out to all the world. Anyone who steals will be purged<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “cut off.”">b</a></sup>from society, according to one side of the scroll. Anyone who swears lies under oath will be purged from society, according to the other side of the scroll.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="One side of the scroll/other side. This is the commonest understanding of the meaning here, but there are other interpretations.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:9–11; Proverbs 3:33; Malachi 3:5"> “I have sent the curse out and it will go into the house of the thief and into the house of the one who swears lies in my name, declares the Lord Almighty. The curse will remain in that house, and will destroy it, both the timbers and the stones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:14; Zechariah 2:3"> Then the angel I had been talking to came over to me and said, “Look. What do you see moving away?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “going forth.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 8:5; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> “What is it?” I asked. “What you see moving away is a barrel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “ephah,” a container for measuring grain; sometimes translated “basket.” However, in this case it obviously needs to be large enough to hold a woman inside (5:7), so the word barrel has been used for translation.">e</a></sup>full of the sins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Septuagint reading. The Hebrew text currently reads “eye,” but this is hard to understand in this context, and the revised reading is only one letter different in the Hebrew.">f</a></sup>of everyone in the country,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The country of Judah.">g</a></sup>he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> Then the lead lid was lifted from the barrel and there was a woman sitting inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Amos 9:1–4; Proverbs 5:22"> “She represents wickedness,” he said, and pushed her back inside, forcing the lead lid shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> I looked once more and saw two women flying towards me. Their wings looked like those of a stork. They picked up the barrel and flew away, high into the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “Where are they taking it?” I asked the angel I was talking to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 11:2; Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2"> “They're taking it to the land of Babylon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “Shinar.”">h</a></sup>to build a house for it. When the house is ready, the barrel will be placed at its base.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“At its base.” Some interpret this to mean the woman who represents wickedness will be worshiped, and that the “house” is actually a temple.">i</a></sup></span>
