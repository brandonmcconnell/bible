<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Acts 4:35; Acts 2:47"> At this time, when the number of believers was increasing rapidly, the Greek-speaking believers started arguing with the Aramaic-speaking believers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Hellenists” and “Hebrews.”">a</a></sup>They complained that their widows were being discriminated against in the daily distribution of food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:9–14; Exodus 18:17–26; 2 Timothy 2:4"> The twelve apostles called all the believers together, and told them, “It's not appropriate for us to give up spreading the word of God so we can wait at tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Brothers, choose from among you seven trustworthy men full of the Spirit and wisdom. We will hand this responsibility over to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:14; 1 Timothy 4:13–16; Philippians 1:9–11"> We ourselves will give our full attention to prayer and the ministry of spreading the word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> Everybody was happy with the arrangement, and they chose Stephen (a man full of trust in God and of the Holy Spirit), Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolaus (originally a Jewish convert from Antioch).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> These men were presented to the apostles who prayed for them and placed their hands on them in blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> The word of God continued to spread, and the number of disciples in Jerusalem greatly increased, with a large number of priests committing themselves to trusting in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> Stephen, full of grace and God's power, performed wonderful miracles among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> But some started arguing with him. They were from the synagogue called “the Free,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Presumably a synagogue made up of former slaves.">b</a></sup>as well as Cyrenians, Alexandrians, and people from Cilicia and Asia Minor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> But they weren't able to stand against Stephen's wisdom or the Spirit with which he was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> So they bribed some men to say, “We heard this man blaspheme Moses, and God too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> They stirred up the people, and together with the elders and the teachers of the law, they came and arrested him. They brought him before the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> and called false witnesses who testified against him. “This man is always slandering the holy Temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “this holy place,” also in 6:14.">c</a></sup>and the law,” they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> “We've heard him say that this Jesus of Nazareth will destroy the Temple, and will change the laws<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Also translated “customs”; however in the context this has far more to do with legal and ceremonial requirements.">d</a></sup>we received from Moses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> Everyone sitting on the council looked closely at Stephen, and his face shone like the face of an angel.</span>
