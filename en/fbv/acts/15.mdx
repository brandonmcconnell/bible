<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:5; Leviticus 12:3"> Then some men arrived from Judea who started teaching the believers, “Unless you're circumcised according to the rules set down by Moses, you can't be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 15:22–23; Acts 15:6–7; Galatians 2:1–2"> Paul and Barnabas had many arguments and debates with them. So Paul and Barnabas and some others were appointed to go to Jerusalem and talk to the apostles and leaders there about this issue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 21:5; 1 Corinthians 16:11"> The church sent them on their way, and as they traveled through Phoenicia and Samaria, they explained how foreigners were being converted, which made all the believers very happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:12; Acts 15:3"> When they arrived in Jerusalem they were welcomed by the church members, the apostles, and the elders. They explained everything God had done through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:1–3; Philippians 3:5–8; Acts 15:1"> But they were opposed by some of the believers who belonged to the Pharisee faction. They said, “These converts have to be circumcised, and instructed to observe the law of Moses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 15:22; Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17"> The apostles and elders met together to discuss the issue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Galatians 2:7–9; 1 Chronicles 28:4–5"> After much debate, Peter stood up and said to them, “Brothers, you know that some time ago God chose me from among you so that the foreigners could hear the message of good news and trust in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Acts 10:47; Acts 14:3"> God, who knows what we're thinking,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “knows the way we think.”">a</a></sup>has shown that he accepts them, giving them the Holy Spirit just as he did to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 5:6; Acts 10:28"> He doesn't make any distinction between us and them—he cleansed their thoughts as they trusted in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; Matthew 23:4; Galatians 4:9"> So why do you want to oppose God and put a burden on the believers that our fathers weren't able to bear, and we can't either?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Titus 3:4–7; Titus 2:11"> We're convinced that we're saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus, in the same way they are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:4; Acts 21:19"> Everyone listened attentively to Barnabas and Paul as they explained the miraculous signs that God had performed through them among the foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Galatians 2:12; James 1:1"> After they had finished speaking, James spoke up, saying, “Brothers, listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:1; Acts 15:7–9; Luke 2:31–32"> Simon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Simon Peter.">b</a></sup>has described how God first revealed his concern for the foreigners by taking from them a people committed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:8–12; Acts 13:47"> This is in accordance with the words of the prophets, as it's written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 9:11–12; PSALMS 89:35–49; Luke 1:31–33"> ‘In the future I will return, and I will rebuild the fallen house of David; I will rebuild its ruins and set it straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 2:11; Zechariah 8:20–23; Isaiah 65:1"> I will do this so that those who are left may come to the Lord, including the foreigners who call on my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Ephesians 3:9; Isaiah 46:9–10"> This is what the Lord says, who revealed these things long ago.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Amos 9:11-12.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 15:28; Galatians 1:7–10; Acts 15:24"> So my decision is that we shouldn't make it difficult for foreigners who turn to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Revelation 2:20; Leviticus 3:17"> We should write to them and tell them to avoid food sacrificed to idols,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “pollutions of idols.”">d</a></sup>sexual immorality, meat of animals that have been strangled, and from consuming blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:15; Acts 13:27; 2 Corinthians 3:14–15"> For the law of Moses has been taught in every town for a long, long time—it's read in the synagogues every Sabbath.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:27; Acts 15:40; 1 Thessalonians 1:1"> Then the apostles and elders, together with the whole church, decided it would be good to choose some representatives and send them to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas. They chose Judas Barsabbas and Silas, leaders among the brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:26; James 1:1; Galatians 1:21"> and sent them with this letter: “Greetings from us, the apostles and elders and brothers, to the non-Jewish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “Gentile.”">e</a></sup>brothers in Antioch, Syria, and Cilicia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; Galatians 5:10; Acts 15:1"> We have heard that some from our group have confused you with their teachings, causing you trouble. We certainly didn't tell them to do this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Peter 3:15; Acts 1:14; Acts 15:2"> So we have agreed to choose some representatives and send them to you together with our much-loved brothers Barnabas and Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 9:23–25; Philippians 2:29–30"> who have risked their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 15:32; 3 John 1:13"> So we are sending to you Judas and Silas who can verbally confirm what we're saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:13; Revelation 2:24; Matthew 11:30"> It seemed best to the Holy Spirit and to us not to place on you any heavier burden than these important requirements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:20; Acts 21:25; Revelation 2:14"> You should avoid: anything sacrificed to idols; blood; meat from strangled animals; and sexual immorality. You will do well to observe these requirements. God bless you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 16:4; Acts 6:2; Acts 23:33"> The men were sent on their way to Antioch. When they arrived they called everybody together and delivered the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:4–5; Philippians 3:3; Galatians 5:1"> After they had read it, the people were so happy for the encouraging message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:1"> Judas and Silas, who were also prophets, encouraged the brothers, explaining many things, and strengthening them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 16:36; Hebrews 11:31"> After spending some time there they were sent back by the brothers with their blessing to the believers in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Verse 34 is not thought to be part of the original.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:1"> But Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch, teaching and proclaiming the word of God along with many others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 14:6; Acts 13:4; Acts 13:51"> Some time later Paul said to Barnabas, “Let's go back and visit the believers in every town where we shared the word of the Lord, and see how they're doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 13:5"> Barnabas planned to take along John Mark too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:13; Proverbs 25:19; Luke 14:27–34"> But Paul didn't think it was a good idea to take him with them, since he'd left them in Pamphylia and hadn't continued working with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Colossians 4:10; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Acts 4:36"> They had such a strong disagreement that they separated. Barnabas took Mark with him and sailed to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 14:26; Acts 11:23"> Paul chose Silas, and as they left, the believers committed them to the grace of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Galatians 1:21; Acts 15:32"> Paul traveled through Syria and Cilicia, encouraging the churches there.</span>
</div>
