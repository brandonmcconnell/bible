<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> Dear Theophilus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Meaning “One who loves God,” either a specific person, or more generically. The “previous book” mentioned is the Gospel of Luke.">a</a></sup>in my previous book I wrote about all that Jesus did and taught from the beginning</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> until the day he was taken up to heaven. This was after he'd given instructions through the Holy Spirit to his chosen apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> Over the course of forty days after the death he suffered, he showed himself to them, proving that he was alive with convincing evidence. He appeared to them, and told them about the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> While he was still with them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “while he was sharing a meal with them.”">b</a></sup>he instructed them, “Do not leave Jerusalem. Wait to receive what the Father promised, just as you heard it from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> It's true that John baptized in water, but in just a few days' time you will be baptized in the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> So when the disciples met with Jesus, they asked him, “Lord, is this the time when you will re-establish Israel's kingdom?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> “You don't need to know about the dates and times that are set by the Father's authority,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> “But you will be given power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, throughout Judea and Samaria, and to the farthest places on earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> After Jesus had told them this, he was taken up as they were watching and a cloud hid him from their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> While they were staring intently at the sky as he ascended, two men dressed in white were suddenly standing beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> “Men of Galilee, why are you standing here staring at the sky?” they asked. “This same Jesus who has been taken up from you to heaven shall come in the same way you saw him go into heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then the disciples went back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives which is about a Sabbath day's walk from Jerusalem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In other words, a relatively short distance.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> When they arrived, they went upstairs to the upper room where they were staying: Peter, John, James and Andrew; Philip and Thomas; Bartholomew and Matthew; James the son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas the son of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> They all joined together in prayer, together with the women and Mary, Jesus' mother, and his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> At this time Peter stood up and addressed a crowd of around one hundred and twenty believers who had gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; John 13:18; PSALMS 55:12–15"> “My brothers and sisters,” he said, “The Scriptures, spoken by the Holy Spirit through David, had to be fulfilled regarding Judas, who guided those who arrested Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> He was counted as one of us, and shared in this ministry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; PSALMS 55:23"> (Judas had bought a field with his ill-gotten gains. There he fell down headfirst, and his body burst apart, spilling out all his intestines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> Everybody who lived in Jerusalem heard about this so that the field was called in their language “Akeldama,” which means, “Field of blood.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 69:25; PSALMS 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> As it's written in the book of Psalms, “Let his home be abandoned, and no one live there,” and “Let someone else take over his position.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Citing Psalms 69:25and Psalms 109:8.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> “So now we have to choose someone who has been with us the whole time that Jesus was with us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> from the time John was baptizing up until the day Jesus was taken up to heaven from us. One of these must be chosen to join together with us as we witness, giving evidence of Jesus' resurrection.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> Two names were put forward: Joseph Justus, also known as Barsabbas, and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> They prayed together and said, “Lord, you know everyone's thoughts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“You know everyone's thoughts”—literally, “heart-knower.”">e</a></sup>Please show us which of these two you have chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to replace Judas as an apostle in this ministry that he gave up to go to where he belongs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> They cast lots, and Matthias was chosen. He was counted as an apostle with the other eleven.</span>
