<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 18:19–21; Matthew 26:61–62; Acts 6:13–14"> “Are these allegations true?” the high priest asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:1; Genesis 11:31; PSALMS 29:3"> “Brothers and fathers, listen to me!” Stephen replied. “God in his glory appeared to our father Abraham when he was living in Mesopotamia, before he moved to Haran.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Hebrews 11:8; Joshua 24:3"> God told him, ‘Leave your country and your relatives, and go to the country that I'm going to show you.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 12:1.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 12:4–5; Genesis 11:31–32; Isaiah 41:9"> So he left the country of the Chaldeans and lived in Haran. After his father's death, God sent him here to this country where you now live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 13:15"> God didn't give Abraham an inheritance here, not even one square foot. But God did promise Abraham that he would give him and his descendants possession of the land, even though he had no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> God also told him that his descendants would live in a foreign country, and that they would be enslaved there, and would be mistreated for four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 3:12; PSALMS 105:27–36; Nehemiah 9:9–11"> God said, ‘I will punish the nation that enslaves them. Eventually they will leave and come here to worship me.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 15:13-14; Exodus 3:12.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:23–26; Genesis 17:9–14; Genesis 21:1–4"> God also gave Abraham the agreement regarding circumcision,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “circumcision covenant.”">c</a></sup>and so when Isaac was born, Abraham circumcised him on the eighth day. Isaac was the father of Jacob, and Jacob the father of the twelve patriarchs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; PSALMS 105:17; Isaiah 43:2"> The patriarchs, who were jealous of Joseph, sold him into slavery in Egypt. But God was with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; Proverbs 2:6"> and rescued him from all his troubles. He gave him wisdom and helped him gain the favor of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, who made him governor over Egypt and the royal household.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> Now a famine occurred throughout Egypt and Canaan. It caused terrible misery, and our forefathers had no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:1–24; Genesis 43:2"> When Jacob heard there was grain in Egypt he sent our forefathers down on their first visit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 45:1–18; Genesis 46:31–10"> During their second visit, Joseph revealed to his brothers who he was, and Pharaoh discovered Joseph's family background.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:26–27; Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 1:5"> Joseph sent for his father and all his relatives—seventy-five in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 49:33; Exodus 1:6; Genesis 46:3–7"> Jacob traveled to Egypt, and died there—as did our forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 23:16; Exodus 13:19"> Their bodies were brought back to Shechem and placed in the tomb that Abraham had bought with silver from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–12; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> As the time approached regarding the promise that God had made to Abraham, the number of our people in Egypt increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 1:8"> A new king came to the throne in Egypt who knew nothing about Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 105:25; Exodus 1:9–22; Revelation 12:4–5"> He took advantage of our people and treated our ancestors badly, forcing them to abandon their babies so they would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> It was at this time that Moses was born. He was a handsome child, and for three months he was looked after in his father's home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> When he had to be abandoned, Pharaoh's daughter rescued him and took care of him as her own son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> Moses received instruction in all areas of Egyptian knowledge, and he became a powerful speaker and leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> However, when he was forty years old, he decided to visit his relatives, the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; John 18:25–27"> He saw one of them being mistreated, so he intervened to defend him. On behalf of the man he took revenge and killed the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> Moses thought his fellow Israelites would see that God was rescuing them through him, but they didn't.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 John 3:11–15"> The next day when he arrived, two Israelites were fighting one another. He tried to reconcile them and stop the fight. ‘Men! You are brothers!’ he told them. ‘Why are you attacking each other?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But the man who had started the fight pushed Moses away. ‘Who put you in charge over us? Are you our judge now?’ he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> ‘Are you going to kill me like you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 2:13-14.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> When he heard this, Moses ran away. He went and lived in exile in the land of Midian, where two sons were born to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 3:1–2; Acts 7:35; Isaiah 43:2"> Forty years later, in the desert of Mount Sinai, an angel appeared to him in the flames of a burning bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:3–4"> When Moses saw this, he was amazed at the sight, and went over to take a closer look. The voice of the Lord spoke to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> ‘I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 3:6.">e</a></sup>Moses shook with fear and didn't dare look up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> The Lord told him, ‘Take off your sandals, because where you are standing is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; PSALMS 106:44"> I have closely observed the suffering of my people in Egypt, and I have heard their groans. I have come down to rescue them. Now come over here, for I'm sending you to Egypt.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 3:5-10.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; PSALMS 113:7–8"> This was the same Moses that the people had rejected when they said, ‘Who made you a ruler and judge over us?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 2:14.">g</a></sup>God sent him to be both a ruler and a liberator, by means of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> Moses led them out after performing miraculous signs in Egypt, and in the Red Sea, and continued to do so in the desert for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; John 18:37"> This is the same Moses who promised the Israelites, ‘God will send you a prophet like me from among your people.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 18:15.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> Moses was with God's assembled people in the desert when the angel spoke to him at Mount Sinai, and there with our forefathers he received God's living word to give to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> He was the one our fathers wouldn't listen to. They rejected him and decided to return to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> They told Aaron, ‘Make gods for us to lead us, because we don't know what's happened to this Moses who led us out of the land of Egypt.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 32:1.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PSALMS 106:19–21; Nehemiah 9:18; Revelation 9:20"> Then they made an idol in the shape of a calf, sacrificed to it, and celebrated what they themselves had made!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> So God gave up on them. He left them to their worship of the stars in the sky. This is what the prophets wrote, ‘Were you giving offerings or making sacrifices to me during the forty years in the desert, you Israelites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> No, you carried the Tabernacle of the god Moloch and the image of the god Rephan's star, images that you made so you could worship them. So I will banish you in exile beyond Babylon.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Quoting Amos 5:25-27.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 38:21; Hebrews 8:5"> Our ancestors had the Tabernacle of Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Meaning that it gave God's message, and provided evidence of his presence.">k</a></sup>in the desert. God had told Moses how he should make it following the blueprint he had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 44:2; Joshua 18:1; Joshua 23:9"> Later on, our forefathers carried it with them when they went in with Joshua to occupy the land taken from the nations the Lord drove out before them. It stayed there until the time of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 132:1–5; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:1–5"> David found favor with God and asked to make a more permanent home for the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; 1 Kings 6:37–38; 2 Samuel 7:13"> But it was Solomon who built a Temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally, “house.”">l</a></sup>for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 17:24–25; Isaiah 66:1–2; 1 Kings 8:27"> Of course the Almighty doesn't live in temples we make. As the prophet said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; PSALMS 11:4; John 4:21"> ‘Heaven is my throne, and the earth the place I put my feet. What kind of dwelling could you build for me?’ the Lord asks. ‘What bed could you make for me to rest in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; PSALMS 33:6–9; Acts 14:15"> Didn't I make everything?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 66:1-2.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> You arrogant, hard-hearted people! You never listen!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally, “uncircumcised in heart and ears.”">n</a></sup>You always fight against the Holy Spirit! You act just like your fathers did!</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 1 Kings 19:10"> Was there ever a prophet your fathers didn't persecute? They killed those who prophesied about the coming of the one who is truly good and right. He is the one you betrayed and murdered—</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Galatians 3:19; Acts 7:38"> you who received the law by means of the angels, but refused to keep it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Acts 5:33; PSALMS 35:16; Matthew 25:30"> When they heard this, the council members became mad with rage, and snarled at him, grinding their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 16:19; Hebrews 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> But Stephen, full of the Holy Spirit, gazed up into heaven and saw God's glory, with Jesus standing at God's right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 26:64–65; Acts 10:11; Revelation 11:19"> “Look,” he said, “I see heaven open, and the Son of Man standing at God's right hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; PSALMS 58:4; Acts 23:27"> But they held their hands over their ears and shouted as loudly as they could. They rushed together at him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Acts 8:1; Acts 6:13"> dragged him out of the city, and began to stone him. His accusers laid their coats down beside a young man called Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Luke 23:46; PSALMS 31:5; Acts 22:16"> As they went on stoning him, Stephen prayed, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Luke 23:34; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> He kneeled down, calling out, “Lord, please don't hold this sin against them!” And after he said this, he died.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally, “fell asleep.” Death is often spoken of as a sleep in the New Testament.">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
