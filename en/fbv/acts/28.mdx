<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> When we were safely ashore, we discovered that we were on the island of Malta.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> The people there were very kind—they started a fire and called all of us over so we could warm up from the rain and the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> Paul collected a bundle of firewood and threw it on the fire. But a poisonous snake was driven out of the bundle because of the heat and bit him, fastening itself on his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> When the people there saw the snake hanging from his hand, they said to each other, “This man must be a murderer. Even though he escaped death from the sea, Justice won't let him live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; PSALMS 91:13"> However, Paul shook the snake off into the fire, and suffered no ill effects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> They were expecting him to swell up, or suddenly fall down dead. But after waiting a long time, they saw that nothing bad had happened to him so they changed their minds and decided he must be a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Nearby were lands that belonged to Publius, the chief official of the island. He welcomed us and looked after us for three days very hospitably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> Now Publius' father was sick, lying in bed suffering from fever and dysentery. Paul went in to see him, and prayed for him, placed his hands on him, and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> After this happened, everyone else who was sick on the island came and was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> They presented us with many gifts, and when we had to sail they provided everything we needed for the voyage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> After a three month stay we set sail aboard a ship from Alexandria that had spent the winter at the island. It had a figurehead of the Heavenly Twins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Named after the twin gods Castor and Pollux.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> We stopped at Syracuse, and spent three days there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> From there we sailed on to Rhegium. The following day a south wind blew, and on the second day we arrived at the port of Puteoli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> where we discovered some believers. They asked us to stay with them for a week. So we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> When some of the believers from Rome heard we had arrived they came to meet us at the Forum of Appius and the Three Taverns. When Paul saw them, he was thankful to God and much encouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> When we entered Rome, Paul was permitted to stay under house arrest with a soldier to guard him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> Three days later Paul invited the Jewish leaders there to come to see him. When they were all together he told them, “Brothers, even though I had not done anything wrong against the people or the customs of our forefathers, I was arrested in Jerusalem and handed over to the Roman authorities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> After they had interrogated me they wanted to release me because I had done nothing that warranted execution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But the Jewish leaders opposed this, so I was forced to appeal to Caesar—not that I had any accusations against my own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> That's why I asked to see you, and talk to you, because it is on account of the hope of Israel that I am chained up like this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> “We have not received any letters from Judea about you, and none of our people have come here with reports against you or to speak badly of you,” they told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> “But we do want to hear from you what you believe—especially regarding this sect, which we know is condemned everywhere.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> They made an appointment to meet with him. On that day many people came to where he was staying. From morning till night Paul was explaining to them, telling them about the kingdom of God. He tried to convince them about Jesus using the writings of the law of Moses and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> Some accepted what Paul said, but some refused to believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> They couldn't agree among themselves, and they left after Paul told them this: “The Holy Spirit said it well through Isaiah the prophet to your forefathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> ‘Go to these people and tell them: Even though you hear, you won't ever understand, and even though you see, you won't ever comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> For the minds of these people have become calloused and hard, their ears cannot hear, their eyes are shut, in case they might see with their eyes and hear with their ears, and understand with their minds, and might come back to me and I would heal them.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 6:9-10.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> Consequently you should know that this salvation that comes from God has been sent to the foreigners and they will listen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This verse is not in all manuscripts, and some commentators think it may have been added.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For two full years Paul stayed there in the house he rented, welcoming everyone who came to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> He spoke of the kingdom of God, and taught about the Lord Jesus Christ very boldly. No one prevented him.</span>
