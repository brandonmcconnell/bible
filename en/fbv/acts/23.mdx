<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:16; 2 Timothy 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Paul, looking sraight at the council, said, “Brothers, right up to now I have always conducted myself before God with a clear conscience.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 18:22; Acts 24:1; Micah 5:1"> Ananias the high priest ordered the officers standing beside Paul to hit him on the mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1–2; John 7:51; Matthew 23:27–28"> Paul said to him, “God will hit you, you whitewashed wall! You're sitting there to judge me according to the law, and yet you order me to be hit in contravention of the law!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The officers standing beside Paul said, “How dare you insult the high priest!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Jude 1:8–9"> “Brothers, I didn't know that he was the high priest,” Paul replied. “As Scriptures say, ‘You must not speak evil of any ruler of your people.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 22:28.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:15; Acts 24:21; Acts 26:5–8"> When Paul realized that some of the council were Sadducees and the others Pharisees, he shouted out, “Brothers, I am a Pharisee, the son of a Pharisee! I'm on trial because of my hope in the resurrection of the dead!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 55:9; John 7:40–43; Matthew 10:34"> When he said this, a tremendous argument broke out between the Pharisees and Sadducees that split the council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 20:27; Matthew 22:23; Mark 12:18"> (The Sadducees say there is no resurrection from the dead, no angels, and no spirits, but Pharisees believe in all of these.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 22:7; Acts 22:17–18; John 12:29"> A great commotion erupted and some of the Pharisee teachers of the law stood up and argued fiercely, saying, “We find this man not guilty! Maybe a spirit spoke to him, or an angel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:14–2; Acts 22:24"> The argument was getting out of hand, so the commander, concerned that they would tear Paul to pieces, ordered the soldiers to go and rescue him from them by force, and take him back into the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 18:9; Acts 19:21; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Afterwards, during the night, the Lord stood by Paul, and told him, “Keep up your courage! Just as you have given your testimony about me in Jerusalem, so you must be my witness in Rome as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Isaiah 8:9–10; Acts 23:21"> Next day some Jews plotted together, and they took an oath not to eat or drink until they had killed Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 16:2; 2 Samuel 15:31; 2 Samuel 15:12"> Over forty were part of this conspiracy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Jeremiah 8:12; Isaiah 3:9"> They went to the chief priests and leaders and said, “We have taken a solemn oath not to eat anything until we have killed Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; PSALMS 37:32–33; Proverbs 1:16"> So you and the council should send word to the commander that he should bring Paul down to meet with you, as if you wanted to examine his case in more detail. We're ready to kill him along the way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:10; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Acts 23:32"> But Paul's nephew (his sister's son) heard about their planned ambush, and he went into the fortress and told Paul about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Matthew 8:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Paul called over one of the centurions, and told him, “Take this young man to the commander, because he has some information to share with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 3:1; Ephesians 4:1; Acts 28:17"> So the centurion took Paul's nephew and brought him to the commander and told him, “The prisoner Paul called me over and asked me to bring this young man to you. He has something to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Esther 9:12; Esther 7:2"> The commander took the young man by the hand and drew him aside. “What do you have to tell me?” he asked quietly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 23:14–15; Acts 23:1; PSALMS 12:2"> “The Jews have arranged to ask you to bring Paul to the council tomorrow, as if they wanted to ask more detailed questions about his case,” he explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:12–14; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> “Please don't listen to them, because they have planned to ambush him with more than forty men who have taken an oath not to eat or drink until they have killed him. They're ready right now, waiting for you to agree to the request.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 1:44; Joshua 2:14"> The commander sent the young man on his way, warning him, “Don't tell anyone that you've told me about this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:25; Acts 23:33; Luke 12:38"> He summoned two centurions and told them, “Get two hundred soldiers ready to go to Caesarea, together with seventy cavalry-men and two hundred spearmen. Be ready to leave at nine p.m. tonight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 25:14; Acts 23:26; Acts 24:10"> Provide horses for Paul to ride to make sure he gets to Governor Felix safely.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He also wrote a letter that went like this:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> “From Claudius Lysias to His Excellency Governor Felix, greetings!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Acts 21:31–33; Acts 24:6"> This man was seized by the Jews and they were about to kill him, when I arrived on the scene with soldiers and rescued him, because I had found out he was a Roman citizen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 22:30"> I wanted to know the reason for their accusations, so I took him before their council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 26:31; Acts 25:25; Acts 18:15"> I found out the charges against him had to do with issues regarding their law, but he was not guilty of anything that merited death or imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:16; Acts 25:5–6"> When I discovered a plot against this man I sent him to you right away, ordering his accusers to make their complaints before you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; Luke 7:8; Acts 23:23–24"> So the soldiers followed their orders and took Paul overnight to Antipatris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:23; Acts 23:10"> The next morning they sent him on with the cavalry, and went back to the fortress.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 23:23–24; Acts 28:16"> When the cavalry arrived at Caesarea they delivered the letter to the governor and brought Paul before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 25:1; Daniel 2:49"> After reading the letter, the governor asked Paul what province he came from. When he learned he was from Cilicia he told Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16; Matthew 27:27"> “I will investigate your case when your accusers arrive.” He ordered Paul detained in Herod's palace.</span>
</div>
</div>
