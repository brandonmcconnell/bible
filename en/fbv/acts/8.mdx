<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> Saul approved of his killing. On that very day terrible persecution broke out against the church in Jerusalem, and all except for the apostles were scattered throughout Judea and Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> (Some faithful followers of God buried Stephen, with great mourning.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> But Saul set about destroying the church, going from house to house, dragging both men and women off to prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Those who had been scattered spread the word wherever they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Philip went to the town of Samaria, and told them about the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> When the crowds heard what Philip was saying and saw the miracles he did they all paid attention to what he was telling them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> Many were freed from possession by evil spirits that screamed as they came out, and many who were lame or disabled were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> The people who lived in the city were overjoyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> Now there was a man named Simon who lived in the city. He practiced sorcery. He claimed that he was someone very important, and had astounded the people of Samaria</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> so they all paid attention to him. From the lowest to the highest in society they said, “This man is ‘God the Great Power.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> They were impressed by him because he had amazed them with his magic for so long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed in what Philip told them about the good news about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Simon too believed and was baptized. He accompanied Philip, amazed at the miraculous signs and wonders he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> When the apostles back in Jerusalem heard that the people of Samaria had accepted the word of God, they sent Peter and John to visit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> When they arrived they prayed for the converts in Samaria to receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> The Holy Spirit hadn't come to any of these converts yet—they had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> The apostles placed their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> When Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given when the apostles placed their hands on people, he offered them money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> “Give me this power, too,” he asked them, “so that anyone I place my hands on will receive the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> “May your money be destroyed together with you for thinking God's gift is something that can be bought!” Peter replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; PSALMS 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> “You're not part of any of this. None of this work belongs to you, because in God's eyes your attitude is totally wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent of your evil ways! Pray to the Lord and ask forgiveness for thinking like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> I can see that you are full of bitter envy, and chained down by your sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> “Please pray for me, that nothing you've said may happen to me!” Simon replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> After they had given their testimony and shared the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, sharing the good news in many Samaritan villages along the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> An angel of the Lord told Philip, “Get ready and go south to the desert road that leads from Jerusalem to Gaza.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; PSALMS 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> So Philip set out, and met an Ethiopian man, a eunuch who had a high position in the service of the Kandake,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Kandake is not the personal name of the Queen, but her title, like “Pharaoh.”">a</a></sup>Queen of Ethiopia. He was her chief treasurer. He had gone to Jerusalem to worship there, and</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> was returning from his trip, sitting in his chariot. He was reading out loud from the book of the prophet Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> The Spirit told Philip, “Go over close to the chariot.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> So Philip ran over, and heard the man reading from the prophet Isaiah. “Do you understand what you're reading?” Philip asked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; PSALMS 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> “How can I, unless someone explains it?” the man replied. He invited Philip to get in and sit beside him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> The Scripture passage he was reading was this: “He was led like a sheep to be slaughtered. Like a lamb is silent in front of his shearer, he didn't say a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> He was humiliated and denied justice. No one can speak of his descendants, for his life came to an end.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “his life was taken from the earth.” The quotation is from Isaiah 53:7-8.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> The eunuch asked Philip, “Tell me, who is the prophet talking about? Is it himself, or someone else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> Philip started explaining, beginning from this Scripture, telling him about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> As they continued on, they came to a stretch of water. The eunuch said, “Look, there's water here—why shouldn't I be baptized?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This verse is not found in the earliest manuscripts.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> He ordered that the chariot be stopped. Philip and the eunuch both went down into the water and Philip baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> When they came out of the water the Spirit of the Lord took Philip away. The eunuch never saw him again, but he continued on his way full of joy. Philip found himself at Azotus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> He spread the good news in all the towns along the way until he arrived at Caesarea.</span>
