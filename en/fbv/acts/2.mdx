<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> When the day of Pentecost came, they were all meeting together in one place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; PSALMS 18:10"> Suddenly a noise came from heaven like a roaring wind that filled the whole house where they were staying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> They saw what looked like separate tongue-shaped flames that settled on each of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> All of them were filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in different languages as the Spirit gave them the ability to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> At that time there were devout Jews from every nation on earth living in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> When they heard this noise, a large crowd of them gathered. They were puzzled because everyone heard their own language being spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> They were totally amazed, saying, “Look, these people who are speaking—aren't they all Galileans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So how is it that we can all hear them speaking in our own mother tongue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians, Medes and Elamites; people from Mesopotamia, Judaea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia and Pamphylia; from Egypt and the area of Libya around Cyrene; visitors from Rome, both Jews and converts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; PSALMS 78:4"> Cretans and Arabians—we hear them speaking in our own languages about all the great things God has done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> They were all amazed and confused. “What does this mean?” they asked each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> But others jeered and said, “They've been drinking too much wine!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> Then Peter stood up with the eleven disciples and spoke in a loud voice: “Fellow Jews and everyone living here in Jerusalem: pay attention to me and I'll explain all this to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> These men aren't drunk as you presume. It's only nine in the morning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> What's happening is what was predicted by the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> God says, ‘In the last days I will pour out my Spirit on everyone. Your sons and daughters will prophesy. Your young men will see visions. Your old men will dream dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> I will pour out my Spirit on my servants, both male and female, and they will prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> I will also give you miraculous signs in the heavens above and on the earth below—blood, fire, and swirling smoke!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> The sun will become dark, and the moon will become red like blood before the great and glorious day of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; PSALMS 86:5"> But whoever calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Joel 2:28-32.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> People of Israel, listen to this: Jesus of Nazareth was a man confirmed by God to you by the powerful miracles and signs that God performed through him, right here among you—as you well know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> God, knowing beforehand what would happen, followed his plan and resolved to hand him over to you. By means of the hands of wicked men, you killed him by nailing him to a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> But God raised him back to life, freeing him from the burden of death, because death did not have the power to keep him a prisoner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; PSALMS 62:6"> David says of him, ‘I saw the Lord always in front of me. I will not be shaken, for he is right beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 16:9; PSALMS 63:5; PSALMS 71:23"> That's why I'm so happy! That's why my words are full of joy! That's why my body lives in hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> For you will not abandon me in the grave,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Hades,” the place of the dead. Also verse 31.">b</a></sup>nor will you allow your Holy One to experience decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 16:11; PSALMS 25:4; PSALMS 21:6"> You have revealed to me the ways of life. You will fill me with joy with your presence.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 16:8-11.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> My brothers and sisters, let me tell you plainly that our ancestor David died and was buried, and his tomb is here with us to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11–18; PSALMS 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> But he was a prophet, and knew that God had promised on oath to place one of his descendants on his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; PSALMS 16:10; Acts 13:35"> David saw what would happen and spoke about the resurrection of Christ—for Christ was not abandoned to the grave nor did he experience decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> God has raised this Jesus from the dead, and we're all witnesses of that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Now he's been exalted to God's right hand, and has received from the Father the Holy Spirit whom he promised, and has poured out what you're seeing and hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David didn't ascend into heaven, but he did say: The Lord told my Lord, ‘Sit down here at my right hand</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> until I have made your enemies a stool to put your feet on.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 110:1. A sign of victory.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Now let everyone in Israel be totally convinced of this: God has made this Jesus, whom you killed on a cross, both Lord and Messiah!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Messiah (Hebrew) is equivalent to Christ (Greek).">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> When the people heard this they were conscience-stricken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “cut to the heart.”">f</a></sup>They asked Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what should we do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> “Repent!” Peter told them. “All of you must be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> This promise is given to you, to your children, and to all who are far away—everyone the Lord our God calls.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> Peter went on speaking, giving them much more evidence. He warned them, “Save yourselves from this perverted generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> Those who accepted what he'd said were baptized, adding about three thousand people to the group of believers that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> They committed themselves to what the apostles had taught them, and to the fellowship of the believers, “breaking bread”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This is probably a reference to the Lord's Supper, and not just ordinary meals, though they would also be included.">g</a></sup>and praying together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> Everyone was in awe, and many miracles and signs were done through the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> All the believers were together and shared everything they had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> They sold their property and belongings, sharing the proceeds with everyone as they needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> Day after day they continued to meet together in the Temple, and ate together in their homes. They enjoyed their meals humbly and happily. They praised God, and everyone thought favorably of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> Every day the Lord added to their number those who were being saved.</span>
</div>
