<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:17"> Paul went first to Derbe, and then on to Lystra, where he met a believer by the name of Timothy. He was the son of a Jewish Christian mother, and his father was Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Timothy 3:15; Acts 14:21"> The brothers at Lystra and Iconium spoke well of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20; Galatians 5:6"> Paul wanted Timothy to travel with him, so he circumcised him because the Jews in the area all knew that Timothy's father was Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 15:28–29; Acts 15:2; Acts 11:30"> As they went through the different towns they gave them the requirements the apostles and elders in Jerusalem had said should be observed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Acts 2:47; Acts 6:7"> The churches were strengthened in their trust in the Lord and every day their numbers increased.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 18:23; Acts 16:7; Acts 13:2–4"> They traveled through the districts of Phrygia and Galatia, since the Holy Spirit prevented them from going to the province of Asia to speak the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:9; Philippians 1:19; Galatians 4:6"> When they arrived at the border of Mysia they tried to enter Bithynia, but the Spirit of Jesus would not allow them to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; 2 Timothy 4:13; Acts 16:11"> So they passed by Mysia and went down to Troas.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:10–12; Acts 19:21; Acts 18:5"> There Paul saw in vision during the night a man from Macedonia standing up, pleading with him, “Please come over to Macedonia and help us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12–13; Acts 14:7; Acts 16:10–17"> After Paul saw this vision, we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The change to “we” indicates that the writer, Luke, had joined them.">a</a></sup>immediately made arrangements to go to Macedonia, since we concluded that God had called us to share the good news with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:1; Acts 16:8"> We set sail from Troas and made straight for Samothrace. The next day we went on to Neapolis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Philippians 1:1; Acts 20:6"> and from there to Philippi, the most important city in Macedonia, and also a Roman colony. We stayed in this city for several days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Colossians 1:23; Acts 17:2"> On the Sabbath day we went out of the citytown gates down to the riverside where we thought people would come to pray. We sat down and talked with the women who had gathered there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Luke 24:45; Isaiah 50:5"> One of them was called Lydia, who sold purple cloth from the town of Thyatira. She worshiped God, and she listened to us. The Lord opened her mind to what Paul was saying, and she accepted what he told her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:33"> After she and all her household were baptized, she pleaded with us, “If you really think that I'm truly committed to the Lord, then come and stay at my house.” She kept on insisting until we agreed!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> One day when we were going down to the place of prayer we met a slave girl possessed by an evil spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “python spirit,” a spirit of divination.">b</a></sup>She earned her masters a great deal of money by fortune-telling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Peter 2:16; Mark 5:7"> This girl followed Paul and the rest of us around, shouting, “These men are the servants of Almighty God. They are telling you how to be saved!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19; Acts 3:6"> She went on doing this for several days. This bothered Paul so he turned around and told the spirit, “I command you in the name of Jesus Christ to leave her!” The spirit immediately left her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Acts 21:30; Acts 8:3"> But when her masters saw they had lost their means of making money, they grabbed hold of Paul and Silas and dragged them before the authorities at the marketplace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 4:4; John 15:18–20; Acts 28:22"> They brought them in front of the magistrates, and accused them: “These Jewish men are causing a great disturbance in our town,” they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; Acts 26:3; Jeremiah 10:3"> “They're advocating things that are illegal for us as Romans to accept or to practice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> The crowd joined together in an attack on them. The magistrates tore off Paul and Silas' clothes, and ordered them beaten with rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> After giving them a severe beating, they threw them into prison, ordering the jailer to keep them locked up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> The jailer followed his orders. He threw Paul and Silas into the inner cell and shackled their feet in the stocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; PSALMS 119:62; James 1:2"> Around midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing praises to God, and the other prisoners were listening to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Acts 5:19; Acts 12:10"> Suddenly a tremendous earthquake shook the foundations of the prison. Immediately all the doors flew open and everyone's chains fell off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 12:19; Matthew 27:5; Judges 9:54"> The jailer woke up and saw the doors of the prison wide open. He drew his sword and was about to kill himself, thinking that the prisoners had escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 23:34; Luke 6:27–28"> But Paul shouted out, “Don't hurt yourself—we're all still here!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Acts 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> The jailer asked for lights to be brought and rushed in. Shaking with fear he fell down before Paul and Silas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:37; John 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> He escorted them out and asked them, “Sirs, what do I have to do so I can be saved?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:12; Acts 2:38–39; John 3:36"> “Trust in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved—you and your whole household,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Colossians 1:27–28; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> Then they shared the word of the Lord with him and everyone who lived in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Acts 16:15; Acts 16:25"> Even though it was late at night he bathed their wounds, and he was baptized right away, along with all his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Acts 2:46; Romans 5:11"> He took them home and had a meal prepared for them. The jailer and his whole family were full of joy because they trusted in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; PSALMS 76:10; Acts 5:40"> Early the next day the magistrate sent officials to the jailer, telling him, “Release those men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 15:33; John 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> The jailer told Paul, “The magistrates have sent word to release you. So you can leave, and go in peace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; PSALMS 58:1–2; Matthew 10:16"> But Paul told them, “They publicly beat us without a trial—and yet we're Roman citizens! Then they threw us in prison. Now they want to quietly let us go? No, they should come themselves and release us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:29; Matthew 21:46; Matthew 14:5"> The officials went back and reported this to the magistrates. When they heard that Paul and Silas were Roman citizens they were really worried,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 5:17; Daniel 6:23"> and went to apologize to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="It was illegal to punish a Roman citizen without a trial.">c</a></sup>They escorted them out and begged them to leave town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Acts 16:14; Acts 14:22"> So Paul and Silas left the prison and went to Lydia's house. There they met with the believers, encouraged them, and then went on their way.</span>
</div>
</div>
