<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Proverbs 1:24; Acts 26:2"> Agrippa then said to Paul, “You are free to speak on your own behalf.” With a sweep of his arm, Paul began his defense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “I am delighted, King Agrippa, to make my defense before you today regarding everything I am accused of by the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:14; Acts 26:26; Acts 26:7"> particularly because you are an expert in all Jewish issues and customs. I beg your patient indulgence as you listen to what I have to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:13; 2 Timothy 3:10; Philippians 3:5"> All the Jews know my life story—from my earliest days beginning in my own country and then in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 22:3; Acts 24:5"> They have known me for a long time and can verify, if they choose to, that I have followed the religious school that observes our faith in the strictest way—I lived as a Pharisee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Acts 24:15; Acts 28:20"> Now I am standing here to be judged regarding the promised hope God gave to our fathers</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:1; Philippians 3:11; 1 Timothy 5:5"> that our twelve tribes hoped to receive as they continually dedicated themselves in God's service. Yes, it's because of this hope that I'm accused by the Jews, Your Majesty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12–20; Acts 4:2; Acts 17:31–32"> Why should any of you think it's unbelievable that God raises the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Acts 22:8; Philippians 3:6"> Previously I was sincerely convinced I should do as much as I could to oppose the name of Jesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 8:3; Acts 9:13–14; Acts 9:21"> This is what I did in Jerusalem. I threw many of the believers in prison, having been given authority to do this by the chief priests. When they were sentenced to death I cast my vote against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 22:19; Matthew 10:17; Acts 13:45"> I had them punished in all the synagogues, trying to make them recant. I was so furiously opposed to them that I went to cities outside our country to persecute them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:10; Acts 22:5–11; Acts 9:1–8"> That's why one day I was on my way to Damascus with the authority and orders from the chief priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Revelation 21:23; Revelation 1:16"> At about noon as I was on my way, Your Majesty, I saw a light from heaven that blazed brighter than the sun. It shone around me and those who were traveling with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 22:2; Acts 9:7"> All of us fell to the ground. Then I heard a voice speaking to me in Aramaic, ‘Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me? It's hard for you to fight against me!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “kick against the goads”—the image of the prods used to direct livestock.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:20–21; Matthew 25:45; Exodus 16:8"> ‘Who are you, Lord?’ I asked. ‘I am Jesus, the one you're persecuting,’ the Lord replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 22:14–15; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 10:11"> ‘But pick yourself up and get to your feet. The reason why I've appeared to you is to appoint you as my servant, to be a witness for me, telling others how you have seen me and everything I will reveal to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Jeremiah 1:19"> I will save you from your own people and from the foreigners. I am sending you to them</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Ephesians 1:18; Ephesians 5:8"> to open their eyes so they can turn from darkness to light, from the power of Satan to God, and so they can receive forgiveness for their sins and a place with those who are set right as they trust in me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Galatians 1:16; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> Clearly, King Agrippa, I could not disobey this vision from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Acts 3:19; Acts 2:38"> First in Damascus, then in Jerusalem, and then all over Judea and also to the foreigners I shared the message of repentance: how they should turn to God, demonstrating their repentance through their actions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 21:30–31; Acts 21:27; Acts 25:3"> That's why the Jews seized me in the Temple and tried to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:14; John 5:46; Romans 3:21"> God has looked after me so I can stand here today as a witness to everyone, both to ordinary people and to those who are important. I am only repeating what Moses and the prophets said would happen—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:26; Colossians 1:18"> how the Messiah had to suffer, and that by being the first to rise from the dead he would announce the light of God's salvation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied. The original simply says “light.”">b</a></sup>to both Jews and foreigners.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 4:10"> Festus interrupted Paul as he made his defense, shouting out, “Paul, you've gone mad! All your knowledge is driving you insane!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7–8"> “I'm not mad, Festus your Excellency,” Paul replied. “What I am saying is true and makes sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3; Acts 2:1–12; Isaiah 30:20"> The king recognizes this, and I'm explaining it very clearly. I am sure that he is aware of what's been happening, because none of this took place as if it were hidden in a corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23"> King Agrippa, do you believe what the prophets said? I'm sure you do!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Matthew 10:18; Acts 26:29"> “Do you think you can convince me to become a Christian so quickly?” Agrippa asked Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 1:28; Romans 10:1"> “Whether it takes a short time or a long time doesn't matter,” Paul answered. “But my prayer to God is that not just you, but everybody listening to me today would become like me—except for these chains!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Acts 28:22; Acts 25:23"> The king stood up, along with the governor and Bernice, and everyone who had been sitting with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 23:9; Acts 28:18"> They conferred together after they had left. “This man hasn't done anything that deserves death or imprisonment,” they concluded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 28:18; Acts 25:11–12; Acts 25:25"> Agrippa told Festus, “He could have been freed if he hadn't appealed to Caesar.”</span>
