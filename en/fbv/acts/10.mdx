<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:1; Matthew 8:5–13; Matthew 27:27"> In Caesarea lived a man named Cornelius who was a Roman centurion of the Italian battalion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Acts 10:4; Acts 10:22"> He was a religious man who, together with everyone in his household, had great reverence for God. He gave generously to the poor, and prayed regularly to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:20–21; Acts 3:1; Acts 5:19"> At about 3 p.m. one day he had a vision in which he saw very clearly an angel of God who came to him and called to him, “Cornelius!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 10:31"> Frightened, Cornelius stared at him and asked, “What do you want, Lord?”<br />“God has paid attention to your prayers, and recognized your generosity to the poor,” he told Cornelius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:9; Acts 9:38; Acts 15:7"> “Now send some men to Joppa, and fetch Simon, also called Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:43; Acts 11:13–14; Romans 10:14–17"> who is staying at Simon the tanner's house down by the sea shore.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philemon 1:16; 1 Samuel 14:6–7; Matthew 8:9–10"> When the angel who had spoken to him had left, Cornelius called in two of his house-servants and a soldier of his personal guard, a religious man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:36; PSALMS 119:59–60; Acts 10:33"> After he'd explained to them all that had happened he sent them to Joppa.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:5; PSALMS 55:17; 1 Samuel 9:25"> The next day, as they were on their way and approaching the city, Peter went up onto the top of the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Houses of the time had flat roofs that served as open-air rooms.">a</a></sup>to pray. It was about noon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Revelation 4:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4"> and he was getting hungry, wanting to eat. But while the meal was being prepared, he fell into a trance, and</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; John 1:51; Romans 16:25–26"> he saw heaven opened. He saw something coming down that looked like a large sheet held by its four corners, being lowered onto the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; John 7:37; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Inside were all kinds of animals and reptiles and birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:31–34; Jeremiah 35:2–5; Acts 10:10"> He heard a voice say, “Get up Peter, kill and eat!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Ezekiel 4:14; Daniel 1:8"> But Peter replied, “Certainly not, Lord! I have never eaten anything that is impure and unclean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Matthew 15:11; Titus 1:15"> He heard the voice speak again, “Don't you call unclean what God has made clean!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:32; John 21:17; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> This happened three times, and then the sheet was quickly taken back into heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:12; Acts 10:7–19; Acts 5:24"> While Peter was puzzling over what the vision he'd seen really meant, the men sent by Cornelius had found out where Simon's house was and were standing at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 10:5–6; Acts 11:11"> They called out, asking whether Simon, also called Peter, was staying there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Acts 11:12; Acts 21:4"> While Peter was still wondering about the vision, the Spirit said to him, “Look, there are three men looking for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:7–9; Zechariah 2:9–11; Isaiah 48:16"> Get up, go downstairs, and go with them. Don't worry at all because I'm the one who sent them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Mark 10:51; Acts 10:29"> So Peter went downstairs to meet the men. “I'm the one you're looking for,” he said. “Why are you here?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Acts 10:2; Acts 22:12"> “We come from Cornelius, a good, religious man who has reverence for God and is widely respected among the Jewish people,” they replied. “A holy angel instructed him to send for you to come to his house so he can hear what you have to tell him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 11:12; 1 Peter 4:9"> So he invited them in and they stayed there.<br />The next day he got up and left with them. Some of the brothers from Joppa went with them too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Zechariah 8:20–23; Zechariah 3:10"> The following day they arrived in Caesarea where Cornelius was waiting for them with his relatives and close friends whom he'd called together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Daniel 2:46"> When Peter entered the house, Cornelius met him and fell down at his feet and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 14:14–15"> But Peter pulled him back up, telling him, “Stand up! I'm only a man!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; John 4:35; Acts 10:24"> Peter spoke with Cornelius, and then went on in where he found many other people waiting for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:9; Ephesians 3:6–7; Galatians 2:12–14"> He said to them, “You certainly know that it's not permitted for a Jew to be associated with or to visit foreigners. But God has shown me that it's not for me to call anyone impure or unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:19–20"> That's why I came without any argument when I was sent for. So now I want to know the reason why you sent for me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 10:3; Daniel 9:20–21; Nehemiah 9:1–3"> “Four days ago, at about this time—three in the afternoon—I was praying in my house,” Cornelius explained. “Suddenly I saw a man standing in front of me, dressed in clothes that shone brightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:4; Daniel 9:23; Luke 1:13"> He told me, ‘Cornelius, your prayers have been heard, and God has recognized your generosity to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Send someone to Joppa for Simon Peter. He's staying at Simon the tanner's house, down by the sea shore.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 1:19; Galatians 4:14; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> So immediately I sent for you, and it was good of you to come. That's why we're all here, meeting together before God, ready to hear everything the Lord has told you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 2:11; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Deuteronomy 10:17"> Peter replied, “I'm totally convinced that God has no favorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:29; Acts 10:2; Romans 10:12–13"> In every nation God accepts those who respect him, and do what is good and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Acts 2:36; Revelation 17:14"> You know the message he sent to Israel, sharing the good news of peace that comes from Jesus Christ, who is Lord of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 4:14; Acts 2:22; Acts 28:22"> You know that this good news spread throughout Judea, beginning in Galilee, following John's call to baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:18; Acts 2:22; Isaiah 61:1–3"> It's about Jesus of Nazareth—how God anointed him with the Holy Spirit and with power, and how he went around doing good, healing all those who were under the devil's control, for God was with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:8; Acts 10:41"> We can testify to all that he did in Judea and Jerusalem. They killed him by hanging him on a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30–31; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> But God raised him back to life on the third day, and had him appear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 14:22; Acts 10:39; John 21:13"> not to everyone, but to those witnesses chosen by God—including us, who ate and drank with him after he rose from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Peter 4:5"> He gave us the responsibility of publicly telling this to the people, to testify that he is the one God chose as the Judge of the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Acts 2:38; Isaiah 53:11"> He is the one all the prophets spoke about, that everyone who trusts in him will receive forgiveness through his name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 11:15; Acts 19:6; Acts 15:8"> While Peter was still speaking, the Holy Spirit fell on all of them who were listening to the message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 10:23; Acts 2:38; Galatians 3:13–14"> The Jewish believers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “those of the circumcision that believed.”">b</a></sup>who had come with Peter were astonished, because the gift of the Holy Spirit was also poured out on the foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> They heard them speaking in tongues, glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 8:36; Romans 10:12; Acts 11:15–17"> Then Peter asked, “Is anybody going to prevent them being baptized in water, since they have received the Holy Spirit just as we have?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 8:16; Galatians 3:27"> He gave orders for them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they pleaded with him to spend some time with them.</span>
</div>
</div>
