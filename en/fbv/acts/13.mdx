<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:35; Acts 11:22–27"> The church at Antioch had prophets and teachers: Barnabas, Simeon Niger, Lucius of Cyrene, Manaen (the childhood friend of Herod the tetrarch), and Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> While they were worshiping the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, “Set apart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “dedicate.”">a</a></sup>Barnabas and Saul to do the work I've called them to.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 2:2; Acts 13:2"> After they had fasted, prayed, and placed their hands on them in blessing, they sent them on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 13:2; Acts 11:19"> So Barnabas and Saul, directed by the Holy Spirit, went to Seleucia. From there they sailed to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Acts 13:46; Acts 14:1"> Arriving at Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the Jewish synagogues. John<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This John is John Mark (12:25).">b</a></sup>was with them as their assistant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 1 John 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> They traveled throughout the island and eventually came to Paphos. There they found a Jewish magician, a false prophet by the name of Bar-jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:38; Acts 13:12; Acts 18:12"> He was close to the governor, Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man. Sergius Paulus invited Barnabas and Saul to come and visit him since he wanted to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Acts 13:6–7; 2 Timothy 4:14–15"> But the magician Elymas (his Greek name) opposed them, trying to prevent the governor from trusting in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 3:8; Acts 4:8; Acts 2:4"> Saul, also called Paul, was filled with the Holy Spirit, and looked straight at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 13:38"> “You are full of deception and all kinds of evil, you son of the devil, you enemy of all that is right! Will you never give up perverting the Lord's true ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 9:3; PSALMS 32:4; Hebrews 10:31"> Look, the Lord's hand is on you and you will become blind. You will not see the sun for some time.” Immediately mist and darkness fell on him, and he had to find someone who could lead him by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Acts 13:7; Luke 4:22"> When the governor saw what happened he trusted in God, amazed at the teaching about the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 15:38; Acts 2:10; Acts 14:24–25"> Then Paul and those with him sailed from Paphos and went to Perga in Pamphylia, while John left them and went back to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:13; Acts 14:19; Acts 13:5"> They went through Perga and on to Antioch of Pisidia. On the Sabbath day they went into the synagogue and were seated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 15:21; Mark 5:22"> After the readings from the Law and the Prophets, the synagogue leaders sent them a message saying, “Brothers, please share with the congregation any words of encouragement you may have.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 13:26; Acts 10:2"> Paul stood up, motioned with his hand to get their attention, and began speaking. “Men of Israel, and all of you who have reverence for God, listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; PSALMS 105:23–24"> The God of the people of Israel chose our forefathers, and gave our people prosperity during their stay in the land of Egypt. Then with his mighty power he led them out of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 7:36; PSALMS 95:8–11; PSALMS 106:13–29"> and he patiently dealt with them in the desert for about forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; PSALMS 78:55; Acts 7:45"> After he had overthrown seven nations living in the land of Canaan, God divided their land among the Israelites and gave it to them to inherit. This took about four hundred and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:16; 1 Samuel 3:20; 2 Kings 23:22"> Then he provided them with judges as leaders until the time of the prophet Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 1 Samuel 15:1; 1 Samuel 12:12–19"> Then the people asked for a king, and God gave them Saul, son of Kish from the tribe of Benjamin, who ruled for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Kings 15:5; PSALMS 78:70–72"> Then God removed Saul, and made David their king. God approved of David, saying ‘I found David the son of Jesse to be a man according to my own heart; he will do everything I intend.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1 Samuel 13:14.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; PSALMS 132:11; Revelation 22:16"> Jesus is David's descendant; he is the Savior that God promised to bring to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 1:22; John 1:6–8; Luke 3:2–20"> Before Jesus came, John announced the baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Mark 1:7; John 1:26–27"> As John was completing his mission, he said, ‘Who do you think I am? I am not the one you're looking for. But after me one is coming whose sandals I'm not worthy to untie.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Luke 3:16.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Romans 1:16; Luke 1:69"> My brothers, children of Abraham and those of you who have reverence for God: the message of this salvation has been sent to us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 3:17; Luke 24:24–27; Luke 24:20"> The people living in Jerusalem and their leaders didn't recognize Jesus or understand the words spoken by the prophets that are read every Sabbath. In fact they fulfilled the prophetic words by condemning him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 23:21–25; Luke 23:14–16; John 19:4"> Even though they couldn't find any evidence to sentence him to death, they still asked Pilate to have him killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 23:53; John 19:28; John 19:30"> After they had fulfilled everything predicted that they would do to him, they took him down from the cross and buried him in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Acts 17:31; Matthew 28:6"> But God raised him from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:3; Acts 1:11"> and he appeared over the course of many days to those who had followed him from Galilee to Jerusalem. They are now his witnesses to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Acts 26:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> We are here to bring to you the good news of the promise that God made to our forefathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; Hebrews 5:5; Hebrews 1:5–6"> that he has now fulfilled to us their children by raising Jesus from the dead. As it is written in Psalm 2: ‘You are my Son; today I have become your Father.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 2:7.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; PSALMS 89:2–4; Romans 6:9"> God raised him from the dead, never to die again, as he indicated by saying, ‘I will give you what is holy and trustworthy, as I promised to David.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referring to Isaiah 55:3.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 16:10; Acts 2:27–31; PSALMS 49:9"> As another psalm says, ‘You will not allow your Holy One to see decay.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 16:10.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:29; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 2:10"> But David died, after he had done what God wanted in his own time, and he was buried with his ancestors, and his body decayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 13:30; Acts 2:24"> The one God raised from the dead saw no decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 24:47; 1 John 2:12; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> My brothers, I want you to understand that we're telling you that through this man sins are forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Galatians 2:16; Romans 8:3"> Through him everyone who trusts in him is made morally right<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Morally right”: this is far more than simply being right in the sense of “correct,” so the word “morally” is added here">h</a></sup>from all that is wrong—in a way that you could never be set right by the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Hebrews 3:12; Malachi 3:2"> Make sure that what the prophets said doesn't happen to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Peter 4:17; Romans 11:7–14"> ‘You who are scornful, look in amazement, and die! For what I'm doing in your lifetime is something that you could never believe, even if someone told you!’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Quoting Habakkuk 1:5.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Acts 10:33; Matthew 19:30"> As they were leaving, the people pleaded with them to tell them more the next Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 2:8"> After the meeting in the synagogue, many of the Jews and the converts to Judaism followed Paul and Barnabas who spoke with them, encouraging them to continue to hold on to the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 110:3; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 11:10"> The following Sabbath almost the whole town turned out to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Acts 18:6; Acts 5:17"> However, when the Jews saw the crowds, they became extremely jealous, contradicting what Paul was saying and cursing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Matthew 21:43; Acts 18:5–6"> So Paul and Barnabas spoke out strongly, saying “We had to speak the word of God to you first. But now that you're rejecting it—you're deciding that you're not worthy of eternal life—well now we're turning to the foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> That's what the Lord has told us to do: ‘I've made you a light to the foreigners, and through you salvation will go to the ends of the earth.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 49:6.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 11:7"> When the foreigners heard this they were overjoyed, praising the Lord's word, and all those chosen for eternal life trusted in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 19:10; Acts 19:26"> So God's word was spread throughout the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 13:45; Acts 14:2"> But the Jews incited the prominent religious women and leaders of the city to persecute Paul and Barnabas, and had them expelled from their territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:14; Acts 18:6; Luke 9:5"> So they shook the dust off their feet against them as a sign of protest, and went on to Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Romans 15:13; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; Acts 4:31"> And the believers continued to be filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.</span>
