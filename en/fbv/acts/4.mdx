<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> While they were talking to the people, the priests, the captain of the Temple guard, and the Sadducees came up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> They were angry that they were teaching the people, telling them that through Jesus there is resurrection from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> They arrested them and placed them under guard until the following day since it was already evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> But many who had heard the message believed it, and the total number of believers grew to about five thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> The next day, the rulers, elders, and religious leaders met together in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> They included the high priest Annas, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and others of the high priest's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> They brought Peter and John before them and asked, “By what power or by whose authority have you done this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, answered them. “Rulers of the people, and elders:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> Are we being interrogated regarding a good deed done to a man who couldn't help himself, and how he came to be healed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> If so, all of you should know, and all the people of Israel, that it was in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, the one you killed on a cross and whom God raised from the dead—it's because of him that this man stands before you completely healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> ‘He is the stone you builders rejected, but he has been made the chief cornerstone.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:22.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> There is no salvation in anyone else; there is no other name under heaven given to humanity that can possibly save us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> When they saw Peter and John's confidence, and realized they were uneducated, ordinary men, they were very surprised. They also recognized them as Jesus' companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> Since they could see the man who had been healed standing right there with them, they had nothing to say in response to what had happened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> So they instructed them to wait outside the council while they discussed the matter among themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> “What should we do with these men?” they asked. “We can't deny a significant miracle has happened through them. Everybody living here in Jerusalem knows about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But to prevent it spreading among the people any further, we should threaten them never to speak to anybody in this name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Clearly the name of Jesus, but they did not even want to mention the actual name.">b</a></sup>again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> So they called them back in and ordered them never to speak or teach in the name of Jesus again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But Peter and John responded, “Whether it's right in God's eyes to obey you rather than God—you decide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> We can't help talking about what we've seen and heard!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> After making more threats they let them go. They couldn't work out how to punish them because everyone was glorifying God for what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> For the man who received this miracle of healing was more than forty years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; PSALMS 119:63"> After the disciples had been released, they went to the other believers and told them everything the chief priests and elders had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> When they heard what had happened, they prayed to God together:<br />“Lord, you made heaven and earth and sea, and everything that is in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> You spoke by the Holy Spirit through David, our forefather and your servant, saying ‘Why did the people of the other nations become so angry? Why did they plot so foolishly against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> The kings of the earth prepared for war;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“For war,” implied.">c</a></sup>the rulers united together against the Lord and against his Chosen One.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Anointed.” The quote is from Psalms 2:1-2.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> Now this has really happened right here in this city! Both Herod and Pontius Pilate, together with the foreigners and the people of Israel, united together against your holy servant Jesus, whom you anointed as Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> They did whatever you had already decided because you had the power and the will to do it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> Now Lord: look at all their threats against us! Help us your servants to speak your word really boldly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> As you exercise your power to heal, may signs and miracles be done through the name of your holy servant Jesus!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> When they had finished praying, the building they were meeting in was shaken. All of them were filled with the Holy Spirit, and spoke the word of God boldly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> All the believers thought and felt the same way. None of them claimed anything they had as their own, but shared everything with each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> The apostles gave their testimony regarding the resurrection of the Lord Jesus with tremendous power, and God greatly blessed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> None of them needed anything because those who had lands or properties sold them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> They took the proceeds and presented them to the apostles to be shared with those in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> Joseph, the one the apostles called Barnabas (meaning “son of encouragement”), was a Levite, a Cypriot national.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> He sold a field that belonged to him. Then he brought the money and presented it to the apostles.</span>
</div>
