<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 22:27; Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 32:15"> The apostles and brothers in Judea heard that foreigners had also accepted the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 10:9; Acts 15:1"> When Peter arrived back in Jerusalem, those who believed circumcision<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “those of the circumcision,” which could mean simply “Jews.” However, from the context it would appear that these were Jewish Christians concerned over relationships with “foreigners.”">a</a></sup>was still essential argued with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Galatians 2:12; Luke 15:2"> “You went into the homes of uncircumcised men, and ate with them,” they said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Acts 14:27; Joshua 22:21–31"> Peter began to explain to them everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 10:9–32; Acts 9:10; Acts 22:17"> “While I was in the town of Joppa I was praying, and in a trance I saw a vision. Something that looked like a large sheet was being let down by its four corners from heaven, and it came down to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 4:20; Acts 3:4"> When I looked inside I saw animals, wild beasts, reptiles, and birds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then I heard a voice that told me, ‘Get up, Peter, kill and eat.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Hosea 9:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But I replied, ‘Absolutely not, Lord! Nothing impure or unclean has ever entered my mouth!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:9; Hebrews 9:13–14; Acts 10:34–35"> The voice from heaven spoke again, and said, ‘Don't you call unclean what God has made clean!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 21:17; Numbers 24:10; 2 Corinthians 12:8"> This happened three times, and then it was all taken back into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 4:27; Acts 9:10–12; Exodus 4:14"> At that very moment three men were standing in front of the house where we were staying. They had been sent from Caesarea to see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Romans 3:22; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> The Spirit told me to go with them, and not to worry about who they were. These six brothers here also went with me, and we went into the man's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:30–32; Acts 10:3–6; Acts 9:43"> He explained to us how an angel had appeared to him in his house, who told him, ‘Send someone to Joppa, and fetch Simon, also called Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:15; Acts 10:22; Acts 16:31–34"> who will tell you what you need to hear so you can be saved—you and your whole household.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 10:34–46; Acts 2:2–12"> When I started speaking, the Holy Spirit fell on them, just as happened to us in the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Mark 1:8; Proverbs 1:23"> Then I remembered what the Lord said, ‘John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:47; Romans 9:20–26; Daniel 4:35"> Since God gave them the same gift as he gave us when we trusted in the Lord Jesus Christ, what power did I have to oppose God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 36:26"> After they had heard this explanation, they didn't argue with him anymore, and praised God, saying, “Now God has granted the opportunity to repent and have eternal life to foreigners as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 21:2; Acts 15:3"> Now those who had been scattered by the persecution that happened when Stephen was killed, traveled all the way to Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch. They only spread the good news among the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 1:23–24"> But when some of them who were from Cyprus and Cyrene arrived in Antioch, they shared the good news with the Greeks too, telling them about the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:66; Acts 2:47; Acts 5:14"> The power of the Lord was with them and a large number trusted in the Lord and turned to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:27; Acts 8:14; Acts 13:1–3"> News about what had happened reached the church in Jerusalem, and they sent Barnabas to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:43"> When he arrived and saw for himself how God's grace was working, he was delighted. He encouraged all of them to completely dedicate themselves to God and to stay true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 5:14; Acts 9:31"> Barnabas was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit, who put his whole trust in God. Many people were brought to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 9:11; Acts 9:27; Acts 9:30"> Then Barnabas went on to Tarsus to look for Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; Acts 26:28; 1 Peter 4:14"> and when he found him, he took Saul back with him to Antioch. Over the course of the next year they worked together with the church, teaching the message to crowds of people. It was in Antioch that the believers were first called “Christians.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11"> It was during this time that some prophets went from Jerusalem to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 21:10; Acts 18:2; Luke 3:1"> One of them called Agabus stood up and gave a prophetic warning by the Spirit that there would be a terrible famine that would affect the known world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “the inhabited world,” basically referring to the Roman Empire.">b</a></sup>(This came true in the reign of Emperor Claudius.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12–14; Galatians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–4"> The believers decided to send funds to help the brothers that lived in Judea, with everyone giving according to what they had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:23; Acts 12:25; Acts 15:4"> So they did this and sent the money with Barnabas and Saul to the church leaders there.</span>
</div>
