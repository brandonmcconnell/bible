<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Lastly, brothers and sisters, please pray for us, so that the Lord's message may spread and be truly appreciated, just as it is by you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Romans 15:31; Deuteronomy 32:20"> and that we may be kept safe from immoral and evil men, because not everyone trusts in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 10:13"> But the Lord is trustworthy and will strengthen you, and protect you from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Philemon 1:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:10"> We have great confidence in the Lord that you are doing and will continue to do what we told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> May the Lord lead you into a deeper understanding of God's love for you and the endurance of Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The endurance of Christ” could mean the endurance demonstrated by Christ, or the endurance given by Christ—or both.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 3:7; Romans 16:17"> Now we want to tell you, brothers and sisters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, not to associate with any believer who can't be bothered to work for their living—those who don't follow the teachings they learned from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> You certainly know that you should follow our example, because while we were with you we weren't lazy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; Ephesians 4:28"> —we didn't eat anyone's food without paying for it. Instead we worked hard day and night so we wouldn't be a burden to any of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 2:6; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> Not that we don't have the right to do so—but we wanted to be an example to you, so that you could copy what we did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> Even while we were with you we gave strict instructions that anybody who didn't want to work shouldn't eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> But now we hear that there are some lazy busybodies among you who don't work at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> We're ordering such people, urging them in the Lord Jesus, to settle down and work to pay for their own food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> Brothers and sisters, don't give up doing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> Take note of anyone who doesn't do what we're telling you in this letter, and make sure you don't associate with them, so that they may become embarrassed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, that they would be encouraged to do what they should.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> Don't consider them enemies, but warn them as a brother or sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 29:11; John 16:33; Matthew 1:23"> May the Lord of peace himself grant you peace in every situation in every way. May the Lord be with all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21; 2 Thessalonians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> Notice the greeting to you from me, Paul, in my own handwriting. This is my signature on every letter I write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.</span>
