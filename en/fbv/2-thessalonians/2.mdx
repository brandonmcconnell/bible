<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> Talking about the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and how we're brought together to him, brothers and sisters—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> please don't become upset or concerned by any spiritual revelation, or message, or letter supposedly from us that makes you think that the day of the Lord has already come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 John 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Don't let anyone deceive you in any way, because the Rebellion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “apostasy,” the end-time, final rejection of God.">a</a></sup>must come first, and the lawless man be revealed, the one whose end is destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> He is the enemy of God, and proudly sets himself up over everything that is called God and is worshiped. He even installs himself in God's Temple, claiming to be God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Don't you remember that I told you all this while I was still with you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> Now you know what's keeping him in check, because he will be revealed for what he is at the appropriate time.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This whole section (2:6-12) is much debated as to its meaning.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the secret ways of lawlessness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “mystery of lawlessness,” indicating the “revealed secret” of the anti-God power that is not only law-breaking but is truly law-less—without any respect for law.">c</a></sup>are already at work; however he who now restrains it will continue to do so until he is out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> Then the lawless one will be revealed, the one whom the Lord Jesus will wipe out, blowing him away,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “by the breath of his mouth.”">d</a></sup>destroying him by the brilliance of his coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> He (the lawless one) comes to do Satan's work, having all kinds of powers, using miracles, and performing amazing but deceptive displays.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> Using every type of evil trick he deludes those who are on their way to destruction, because they refused to love the truth and so be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; John 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Because of this God sends them a convincing delusion so that they put their trust in the lie.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The lie: in this context surely meaning the fundamental Lie about the nature and character of God told by the Enemy (Isaiah 14, Ezekiel 28), who even claims to be God (2:4). In terms of God's “sending” of this delusion, note that in Scripture God is often speaking of causing that which he does not prevent.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> As a result everyone who did not trust in the truth will be condemned, for they preferred what is evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> But we just have to keep on thanking God for you, brothers and sisters loved by the Lord, because God from the beginning chose you to be saved through the Spirit who makes you right as you trust in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> This is what he called you to through the good news we shared with you, so that you could participate in the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> So, brothers and sisters, stand firm, and hold on to what you've been taught, whether by what you were told, or through a letter from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; John 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> Now may our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God the Father (who through his grace gave us eternal confidence and a trustworthy hope),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> encourage you and strengthen you so you can say and do everything that is good.</span>
</div>
