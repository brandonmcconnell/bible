<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> Wives, accept your husbands' authority in the same way, so that if some husbands refuse to accept the Word, they may still be won over without words by the way you behave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> recognizing your pure and reverent behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> Don't focus on outward attractiveness—hairstyles, gold jewelry, or fashionable clothes—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> instead let it come from within, the lasting attractiveness of a gentle and peaceful spirit on the inside. That's what God values.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> This is how holy women of the past who put their confidence in God made themselves beautiful, yielding to their husbands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> like Sarah who obeyed Abraham, and called him “lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “master.” Today such formality in a marriage relationship would be unusual.">a</a></sup>You are her daughters if you do what's good and right and are not intimidated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> Husbands, in just the same way, be considerate to your wives as you live together with them. Though she may not be as strong as you are, you should honor her since she will inherit equally with you God's gift of life. Make sure to do this so nothing will get in the way of your prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, you should all have the same attitude. Be sympathetic and love each other. Be compassionate, and humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> Don't pay back evil for evil, or retaliate when you're abused, instead you should give a blessing—because that's what you were called to do, so you could gain a blessing yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> Remember: “Those who want to love life, and to see good days, must refrain from speaking evil, and not tell lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; PSALMS 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> Turn away from evil and do good; search for peace—run after it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> For God sees those who do right, and he hears their prayers, but he opposes those who do evil.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 34:12-16.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Who will harm you if you're keen to do good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> For even if you suffer for doing what's right, you're still better off. Don't be scared over what people threaten, don't worry about it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> just keep your mind focused on Christ as Lord. Always be ready to explain to anyone who asks you the reason for the hope that you have. Do this in a gentle and respectful way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> Make sure your conscience is clear, so that if anyone makes accusations against you, they will be embarrassed for speaking badly about the good way you live your life in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> It's certainly better to suffer for doing good (if that's what God wants), than to suffer for doing wrong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Jesus died because of sins, once and for all, the one who is completely true and good and right died for those who are bad,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “the right for the unright.” In this verse the inevitable results of sin are made clear through the death of Jesus. He experienced the consequences of sin in the most dramatic and conclusive way possible, and also proved it was not God who kills, but that sin itself brings its inevitable fatal result (see Romans 6:23).">c</a></sup>so that he could bring you to God. He was put to death in the body, but he came to life in the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> He went to speak to those “imprisoned”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “imprisoned souls.” There has been much debate over this phrase. The same word used for “souls” should note the same word is used for the eight people in verse 20. Some understand those “imprisoned” as those people at the time of the Flood who were “held captive” by their sinfulness (see Genesis 6:5).">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> that refused to believe, when God patiently waited in the time of Noah while the ark was being built. Only a few—in fact eight people—were saved “through water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> This water symbolizes baptism that now saves you, not by washing off dirt from your body, but as a positive response to God that comes from a clear conscience. It is the resurrection of Jesus Christ that makes your salvation possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> Having ascended to heaven, he stands at God's right hand, with angels, authorities, and powers placed under his control.</span>
