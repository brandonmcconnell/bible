<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> I want to encourage the elders among you. I'm also an elder, a witness to Christ's sufferings, and I will share in the glory that is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> Look after God's flock you have been given to care for, watching over it not because you're forced to, but gladly, as God wants you to. Do this willingly, not looking to make a profit from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> Don't be arrogant, lording it over those who have been put in your care, but be an example to the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> When the chief Shepherd appears, you will receive a glorious crown that will never fade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> Young people, do what the elders tell you. Indeed all of you should serve one another in humility, because “God is against the proud, but favors the humble.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Proverbs 3:34.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Humble yourself before God's powerful hand so that he will lift you up at the appropriate time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 55:22; Philippians 4:6; PSALMS 37:5"> Surrender all your worries to him, because he cares for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be responsible, and stay alert. The devil, your enemy, is prowling around like a roaring lion looking for someone to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> Stand firm against him, trusting in God. Remember that your fellow-believers around the world are going through similar troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> After you have suffered for a little while, the God of all grace who called you into his eternal glory in Christ, will himself restore you, support you, strengthen you, and give you a solid foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To him be power forever and ever! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> With the help of Silvanus, whom I consider a trustworthy brother, I send this letter to you. In these few words I've written I want to encourage you and testify that this is the true grace of God. Stand firm in God's grace!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; PSALMS 87:4"> The believers here in “Babylon,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Those in Babylon.” Babylon in the New Testament is frequently a symbol for Rome.">b</a></sup>chosen together with you, send their greetings to you, as does Mark, my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 8:1"> Greet each other with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.</span>
