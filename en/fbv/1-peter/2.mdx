<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; James 1:21; James 4:11"> So give up all the evil things that you do: all your dishonesty, hypocrisy, and jealousy, all the ways you speak badly about others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–13; Ephesians 4:15; 2 Peter 3:18"> You should become like newborn babies who only want pure spiritual milk, so you can grow in salvation</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 34:8; Hebrews 6:5–6; PSALMS 63:5"> now you've tasted how good the Lord really is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> As you come to him—the living stone that people rejected as useless, but is chosen by God and precious to him—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> you also become like living stones, being built into a spiritual house. You are a holy priesthood that offers spiritual sacrifices that God welcomes through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> As Scripture<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 28:16.">a</a></sup>says, “See! I'm setting in Zion its main cornerstone, specially-chosen and valuable. Whoever trusts in him will not be disappointed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “ashamed.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> He is very valuable to you who do trust. But for those who don't, “The stone the builders rejected that became the main cornerstone”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:22.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> is “The stone that trips you up and the rock that makes you fall.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 8:14.">d</a></sup>People stumble over this message because they refuse to accept it—which for them is entirely predictable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> In complete contrast, you are a specially-chosen family, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people belonging to God. Consequently you can reveal the wonderful things he's done, calling you out of darkness into his marvelous light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> In the past you were nobodies, but now you are God's people. In the past you hadn't received mercy, but now you have received mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> My friends, I'm pleading with you as foreigners and strangers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Foreigners and strangers”—not seeing this world as home.">e</a></sup>in this world not to give in to physical desires that are in conflict with what is spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> Be sure to act appropriately when you're with non-Christians, so even if they accuse you of doing wrong, they will see the good things you do and glorify God when he comes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “day of visitation.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Obey human authority for the Lord's sake, whether it is the king as the highest authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or governors that God appoints to punish those who do evil and to commend those who do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> By doing what is good and right God wants you to silence the ignorant accusations of foolish people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> Yes, you are free people! So don't use your freedom as a cover-up for evil, but live as God's servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Respect everyone. Show your love to the community of believers. Have reverence for God. Respect the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> If you are a servant then submit to your master—not just those who are good and kind, but those who are harsh masters as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this is what grace is: to endure life's pain and unfair suffering, keeping your focus on God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> However, there's no credit when you're punished for doing something wrong. But if you suffer for doing what is good and right, and you put up with it, then God's grace is with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> In fact this is what you were called to do, because Christ suffered for you and gave you an example, so you should follow in his footsteps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> He never sinned, he never lied;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 53:9.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> and when he was badly treated, he didn't retaliate. When he suffered, he didn't threaten to take revenge. He simply placed himself in the hands of the one who always judges rightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> He took the consequences of our sins on himself in his body on the cross, so that we could die to sin and live rightly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“The consequences of our sins”: it is the results of sin that are demonstrated in Jesus' death. Sins are innately non-transferable—they are committed by the sinner and cannot be passed to anyone or anything else, since sins are the specific actions of the individual sinner.">h</a></sup>“By his wounds you are healed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 53:5, explaining that salvation is to do with the healing of our fatal sin-sickness, not some legal readjustment with God, or payment to him.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> At one time you were like sheep who had lost their way,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Referencing Isaiah 53:6.">j</a></sup>but now you've returned to the Shepherd—the one who watches over you.</span>
</div>
