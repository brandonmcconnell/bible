<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> This letter comes from Peter, apostle of Jesus Christ, and is sent to God's chosen people: exiles scattered throughout the provinces of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> You were chosen by God the Father in his wisdom, made holy by the Spirit, so that you might obey Jesus Christ and be sprinkled with his blood. May you have grace and peace more and more!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! Through his great mercy we have been born again and given a living hope<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “a hope that brings us life.”">a</a></sup>through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> This inheritance is eternal, it never spoils or fades, and it is kept safe in heaven for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> Through your trust in him, God will protect you by his power until salvation arrives—the salvation that is ready to be revealed at the last day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> So be glad about this, even though you may be sad for a short while as you endure various trials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> These prove that your trust in God is genuine, just as fire is used to prove if gold is genuine—even though that too can be destroyed—and your faith is more valuable than gold! In this way your trust in God will be recognized and you will receive praise, glory, and honor when Jesus Christ appears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> You love him even though you have never seen him. Though you cannot see him now, you trust in him and you're filled with wonderful, indescribable happiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> Your reward for trusting in him is that you will be saved!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> It was this salvation that the prophets searched for and investigated when they spoke of the grace that was prepared for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> They tried to find out when and how this would take place, for the Spirit of Christ inside them spoke clearly about Christ's sufferings and glory to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> It was explained to them that they were not doing this for themselves, but for you—since what they spoke about you've now learned from those who shared the good news with you by the Holy Spirit that heaven sent. Even the angels are eager to find out about all this!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Make sure your minds are in gear. Be clear-headed. Fix your hope exclusively on the grace you'll be given when Jesus is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> Live like children who do what they're told. Don't allow yourselves to be shaped by your former sinful desires when you didn't know any better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> Now you need to be holy in everything you do, just as the one who called you is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> As Scripture says, “You are to be holy, for I am holy.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 11:44-45or Leviticus 19:2">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> Since you call him Father, and recognize he judges everyone impartially based on what they do, take your time here on earth seriously, respecting him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; PSALMS 49:7–8"> You already know that you were not set free from your pointless way of living that you inherited from your forefathers by things that don't have lasting value, like silver or gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> You were set free by the costly blood of Christ, who was like a lamb with no flaw or defect.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> He was chosen before the world was created, but he was revealed in these last days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “at the end of time.”">c</a></sup>for your benefit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> Through him you trust in God who raised him from the dead, and glorified him, so that you could have confidence and hope in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Now that you've dedicated yourselves by following the truth, love each other sincerely like a true family.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “with brotherly love.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> You've been born again, not the product of a mortal “seed,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The emphasis here is that different “seeds” produce different kinds of “life.”">e</a></sup>but immortal, through the living, eternal word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; PSALMS 103:15; James 4:14"> For: “All people are like grass, and all their glory like wildflowers. The grass withers and the flowers fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; PSALMS 119:89"> But the word of the Lord remains forever.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 40:6-8.">f</a></sup>This word is the good news that was explained to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
