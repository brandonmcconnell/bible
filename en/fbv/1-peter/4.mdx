<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 3:18; Colossians 3:3–5"> Since Christ suffered physically, you must prepare yourselves with the same attitude he had—for those who suffer physically have turned away from sin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is a difficult verse, since clearly suffering does not mean sinlessness. The implication is that just as Christ suffered unjustly, when Christians suffer unjustly they take part in the experience of Christ.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22–24; 1 Peter 1:14; Titus 3:3–8"> You will not live the rest of your lives following human desires, but doing what God wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:5"> In the past you've spent enough time following the ways of the world: immorality, sexual indulgence, drunken orgies, partying, binge drinking, and revolting idol-worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; 1 Peter 2:12; Jude 1:10"> People think it's strange that you don't join them anymore in this wild and excessive lifestyle, so they curse at you. But they will have to explain what they've done to the one who is ready to judge the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:9; Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:1"> That's why the good news was shared with those who've already died—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:2; 1 Peter 3:19; Romans 8:9–11"> so that although they were rightly judged as far as being sinful human beings is concerned, they could live in the spirit as far as God is concerned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:8–9; 1 Peter 1:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> Everything is coming to an end! So think clearly and stay alert when you pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Colossians 3:14; Proverbs 17:9"> Most of all, you should love one another very deeply, for love covers many of the wrong things people do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Romans 12:13; Philippians 2:14"> Show hospitality to one another, and don't complain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> Whatever gift you've been given, share it with others among you, as people who wisely demonstrate God's grace in all its different forms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Ephesians 6:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> Anyone who speaks should do so as though God is speaking through them. Anyone who wants to help others should do so through the strength that God gives, so that in everything God may be glorified through Jesus Christ. May glory and power be his forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; 1 Peter 1:6–7; 1 Peter 5:9"> My friends, don't be alarmed at the “ordeals of fire”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “a burning to test you.”">b</a></sup>you're having, as if these were unexpected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:17; James 1:2–3; Romans 5:3"> Be happy to the extent that you share in Christ's suffering, because when he appears in his glory, you will be incredibly happy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; Luke 6:22; Matthew 5:11"> If you are cursed in Christ's name, you're blessed, for God's glorious Spirit rests on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:13"> If you suffer, it should not be as a murderer, or a thief, or a criminal, or as a gossip—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Philippians 1:29; 1 Peter 4:19"> but if it's as a Christian, then you don't need to be ashamed. Instead praise God that you're called by that name!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:29; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 2:9"> For the time of judgment has come—and it begins with the house of God. If it begins with us, what will be the end of those who reject God's good news?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:31; Ezekiel 18:24; Luke 23:31"> “If it's hard for those who live right to be saved, what will happen to sinners, those who oppose God?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Proverbs 11:31.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 37:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 3:17"> So then those who suffer according to the will of God, the trustworthy Creator, should make sure they are doing good.</span>
</div>
