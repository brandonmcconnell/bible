<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> Don't do anything from a spirit of selfishness or pride, but humbly think of others better than you do of yourself.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> Though in his nature he was always God, he wasn't concerned to cling onto his equality with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> Instead he emptied<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Indicating that Jesus chose to “empty” himself of his divine powers.">a</a></sup>himself, taking the nature of a servant, becoming like a human being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> Coming in human form, humbling himself, he submitted himself to death—even death on a cross.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> That's why God placed him in the position of greatest honor and power, and gave him the most prestigious name—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> so that in the name of Jesus everyone should bow in respect, whether in heaven or on earth or under the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> and all will declare that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So, my good friends, continue to work towards the goal of salvation with complete reverence and respect for God, following what you were told—not just when I was with you, but even more so now I'm away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> For it's God who is working within you, creating the will and the ability to do what he wants you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> Do everything without complaining or arguing</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> so that you'll be sincere, innocent of any wrong. Be God's blameless children living in the middle of a dishonest and corrupt people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 32:5.">b</a></sup>Shine among them as lights to the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> holding out to them the word of life. That way I'll have something to be proud of when Christ returns, proving I didn't run around and work for nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> So even if I pour out my life as a sacrifice and offering so you may trust in God, I'm happy for it, and I'm glad together with all of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> just as you have such joy and are glad with me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> I'm hoping, if that's what the Lord Jesus wants, to send Timothy to you soon. It will cheer me up once I know how you're doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> I don't know anyone who genuinely cares about you as he does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> Other people only worry about their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> But you already know what he's like—just as a child working to help his father, so he's worked with me to spread the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> So I hope to send him as soon as I see how I'm doing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> and I trust in the Lord that shortly I'll be able to come too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> But I thought it was important to send Epaphroditus to you. He's a brother to me, a co-worker and fellow-soldier. He's the one you sent to look after me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> and he's been longing to see all of you, worried about you because you'd heard he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; Philippians 2:30; PSALMS 103:3–4"> He certainly was sick—he nearly died—but God had mercy on him. Not just on him, but on me too, so that I wouldn't have tragedy upon tragedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> That's why I'm so keen to send him, so that when you see him you'll be happy, and I won't have to be so anxious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> So welcome him with much happiness in the Lord. Honor people like him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> because in working for Christ he nearly died, putting his life on the line to make up for the help you weren't in a position to give me.</span>
</div>
