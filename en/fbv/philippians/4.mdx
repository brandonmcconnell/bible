<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> I'm so happy in the Lord that you've at last thought about me again—realizing that you were concerned about me before but you couldn't do anything about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–9; Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:31–34"> I'm not talking about my own needs, because I've already learned to be satisfied in whatever situation I find myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> I'm used to having nothing, and I've experienced having plenty too. In every possible situation I've learned the secret of dealing with having plenty and going hungry, of being rich and of living in poverty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I can do anything through him who makes me strong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> Even so it was good of you to share with me during my troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 11:8–12; Acts 16:40–5"> You Philippians remember that right at the beginning of sharing the good news, when I left Macedonia, that yours was the only church that helped me financially.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> Even when I was in Thessalonica you helped me out not once but twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:9–13"> Not that I'm looking for a donation—I'm looking for your “account” to increase in “profit.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Paul is using business terms, but probably referring figuratively to their spiritual “account.”">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; PSALMS 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> My God will fill you with everything you need in accordance with his glorious wealth in Christ Jesus!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:36; PSALMS 115:1; Revelation 14:7"> Glory be to God the Father, forever and ever. Amen.</span>
