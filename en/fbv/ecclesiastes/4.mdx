Look at the tears of the oppressed—there's no one to comfort them! Powerful people oppress them, and there's no one to comfort them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> I congratulated those who were already dead, for the dead are better off than those who are still alive and being oppressed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“And being oppressed”: supplied, but this is the context for the Teacher's statement.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> But best of all are those who have never existed—they haven't seen the evil things people do to each other here on earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I observed that every skill in work comes from competition with others. Once again this is hard to understand, like trying to hold on to the elusive wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> Fools fold their arms and do nothing—so in the end they're all used up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; PSALMS 37:16"> It's better to earn a little<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “one handful.”">b</a></sup>without stress than a lot with too much stress and chasing after the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; PSALMS 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> Then I turned to consider something else here on earth that is also frustratingly hard to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> What about someone who has no family to help him, no brother or son, who works all the time, but isn't satisfied with the money he makes. “Who am I working for?” he asks himself. “Why am I preventing myself from enjoying life?” Such a situation is hard to explain—it's an evil business!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better than one, for they can help each other in their work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> If one of them falls down, the other can help them up, but how sad it is for someone who falls down and doesn't have anyone to help them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> In the same way, if two people lie down together, they keep each other warm, but you can't keep yourself warm if you're alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> Someone fighting against one other person may win, but not if they are fighting against two. A cord made of three strands can't be torn apart quickly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> A poor young person who's wise is better than an old and foolish king who no longer accepts advice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> He may even come out of prison<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Perhaps to be understood as “the prison of bad circumstances.”">c</a></sup>to reign over his kingdom, even though he was born poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I observed everyone here on earth following the youth who takes his place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Presumably the young man mentioned in 4:13who replaces the old king.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> He is surrounded by crowds of supporters, but the next generation isn't happy with him. This also illustrates the passing nature of life whose meaning is elusive, like pursuing the wind for understanding.</span>
</div>
