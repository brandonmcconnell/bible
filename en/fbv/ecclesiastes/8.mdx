<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:50; 2 Peter 1:20"> Who can compare to the truly wise?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In the sense that to be wise is the highest ambition. Literally, “Who is like the wise?”">a</a></sup>Who knows how to interpret things? If you have wisdom your face lights up, and your stern look is softened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 2:43; Titus 3:1; Proverbs 24:21"> My advice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Taking the initial “I” of the sentence to mean “I say.”">b</a></sup>is to do what the king says, since that's what you promised God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Acts 5:8–9; Daniel 4:35"> Don't be quick to walk out on the king without thinking what you're doing, and don't get involved with those who plot against him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “the matter is unpleasant.” This term is believed to refer to a plot or a rebellion against a king.">c</a></sup>for the king can do what he pleases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 9:12; Romans 13:1–4; Daniel 4:35"> The king's orders have supreme authority—who is going to question him, saying, “What are you doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; 1 Peter 3:13–14; Philippians 1:9–10"> Those who follow his commands will not be involved in doing evil. Wise people think, recognizing there's a right time, and a right way.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alternatively, “Wise people know there's a time of judgment.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:11"> For there's a right time and a right way for everything, even when things are going badly for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Even when things are going badly for you”: literally, “even though trouble is heavy on the mortal.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 24:22"> No one knows what's going to happen, so who can say what the future holds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 89:48; Job 14:5; Hebrews 9:27"> No one can hold onto the breath of life; no one can prevent the day when they die. There's no way of escaping that battle, and the wicked won't be saved by their wickedness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:7–8; Ecclesiastes 5:13"> I examined all these things, and thought about all that happens here on earth, and the damage that's caused when people dominate others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Proverbs 10:7"> Yes, I have seen wicked people buried with great honor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Hebrew simply says “the wicked were buried,” but since this is unremarkable it is understood that they were buried with much show and ceremony.">f</a></sup>They used to go to the holy place,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Hebrew text is unclear. “Holy place” would refer to the Temple or the synagogue.">g</a></sup>and were praised in the very city where they did their evil. This is hard to understand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; PSALMS 50:21–22; Romans 2:4–5"> When people are not punished quickly for their crimes they are even more determined to do wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; 2 Peter 2:9; Luke 1:50"> Even though a sinner may do wrong a hundred times, and live a long life, I'm convinced that those who do what God says will be better off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:12; Isaiah 3:11; Job 14:2"> In fact, the wicked will not live long, passing like a shadow, because they refuse to follow God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 2:14; Malachi 3:15"> Another thing that is hard to understand is this: good people are treated as the wicked should be, and the wicked are treated as good people should be. As I say, this is difficult to comprehend!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13"> So I recommend enjoying life. There's nothing better for us here on earth than to eat and drink and be happy. Such an attitude will go with us as we work, and as we live our lives that God gives us here on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; PSALMS 127:2; Ecclesiastes 2:23"> When I applied my mind to discovering wisdom and observing everything people do here on earth, I couldn't get any sleep, day or night.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“I couldn't get any sleep, day or night”: alternatively, “no one has any rest, day or night.”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:11; PSALMS 73:16; Romans 11:33"> Then I studied everything God does, and I realized that no one can fully understand what happens here. However hard they try, however wise they claim to be, they can't really comprehend it.</span>
