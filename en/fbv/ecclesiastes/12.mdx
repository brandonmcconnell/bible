<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:27; Ecclesiastes 11:8; PSALMS 71:17–18"> Remember your Creator while you are young, before days of trouble come and you grow old, saying, “I'm not enjoying life anymore.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; 1 Samuel 4:18; 1 Samuel 4:15"> Before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Before”: the call is to remember the Creator before all the rest that follows in the succeeding verses.">a</a></sup>the light fades—sun, moon, and stars—and rain clouds return to darken the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 90:9–10; PSALMS 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> Before the guards of the house tremble and the strong men are bent over, the grinders stop working because there are only a few left, and the ones looking through the windows only see dimly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The allusions to the problems of increasing age are obvious.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; 2 Samuel 19:35; Revelation 18:22"> and the doors to the street are shut. Before the sound of the grinding mill grows low, and you wake up early when the birds are singing, but you can hardly hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:13; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Job 30:23"> Before you develop a fear of heights and worry about going out on the streets; when the almond tree blossoms, the grasshopper crawls along, and desire fails,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Desire fails”: this word only occurs once in the Old Testament. Some think it refers to the caperberry, a fruit reputed to be an aphrodisiac. The whole verse again references the experience of growing old and approaching death.">c</a></sup>for everyone has to go to their eternal home<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Eternal home”: referring to the grave.">d</a></sup>as the mourners go up and down the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2–3"> Before the silver cord is snapped and the golden bowl is broken; before the water jug is smashed at the spring, or the pulley is broken at the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ecclesiastes 3:20–21; PSALMS 146:4"> Then the dust returns to the earth from which it came, and the breath of life returns to God who gave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:2; Ecclesiastes 8:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14"> “Everything passes! It's all so hard to understand!” says the Teacher.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Here the theme introduced in 1:2is summarized again.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:32; Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 10:8"> Not only was the Teacher a wise man, he also taught what he knew to others. He thought about many proverbs, studying them and arranging them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:5; 1 Timothy 1:15; Proverbs 16:21–24"> The Teacher looked for the best way to explain things, writing truthfully and honestly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Hebrews 4:12; Ezekiel 34:23"> The words of the wise are like cattle prods. Their collected sayings are like nails driven home, given by one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:18; John 21:25; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> In addition, my student, take care, for there's no end to book writing, and too much study wears you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:2"> To sum up now that everything has been discussed: Respect God by keeping his commandments, for that's what everyone should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:16; Matthew 12:36"> God is going to judge us for everything we do, including what we do secretly, whether good or bad.</span>
