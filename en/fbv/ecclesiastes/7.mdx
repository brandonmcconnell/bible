<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Proverbs 22:1; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> A good reputation is better than expensive perfume, and the day you die is better than the day you were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It's better to go to a funeral than to a party.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “It is better to go to the house of mourning than the house of feasting.”">a</a></sup>In the end, everyone dies, and those who are still alive should think about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Sorrow is better than laughter, for tragedy helps us by making us think.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “by sadness of countenance the heart is good.” In Hebrew thought, the heart was where thinking occurred.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> Wise people think about the impact of death, while those who are fools only think about having a good time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; PSALMS 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It's better to listen to criticism from a wise person than to hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 58:9; PSALMS 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> The laughter of fools is like the crackling of thorn twigs burning under a pot—without sense and quickly over.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Thorn twigs used for fuel are of limited value, for they while they burn hot, the flames die quickly.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> Extorting money from others makes wise people into fools, and accepting bribes corrupts the mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; PSALMS 126:5–6"> Completing something is better than starting it. Being patient is better than being proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Don't be quick to get angry, for anger controls the minds of fools.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Anger controls the minds of fools”: literally, “anger lodges in the bosom of fools.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; PSALMS 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Don't ask, “Why were the good old days better than now?” Asking such questions shows you are not wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is good—it's like receiving an inheritance. It benefits everyone in life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“It benefits everyone in life”: literally, “It is an advantage to those seeing the sun.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For wisdom provides security, as does money, but the advantage for those who have wisdom is that they are kept safe and sound!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; PSALMS 107:43"> Think about what God does. If he makes something bent, you can't straighten it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> On a good day, be happy. When a bad day comes, stop and think. God made each day, so you don't know what will happen to you next.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; PSALMS 73:3–13"> Throughout my life I've seen so much that is hard to understand. Good people who die young despite doing what is right,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Here the emphasis seems to be on doing right as defined by the Law.">f</a></sup>and wicked people who live long evil lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Don't think you can make yourself right by a lot of religious observance, and don't pretend to be so wise.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “You must not be righteous excessively, and you must not act wisely excessively.” The word “excessively” here refers to self-reliance rather than quantity.">g</a></sup>Do you want to destroy yourself?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In the sense of trying to make yourself right, and wise, by your own efforts.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; PSALMS 55:23; Job 22:16"> On the other hand,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Implied.">i</a></sup>don't decide to live an evil life—don't be a fool! Why die before your time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> You ought to keep in mind these warnings. Those who follow God will be sure to avoid both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom gives a wise person greater power than ten town councilors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> There's not one good person in all the world who always does what is right and never sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Don't take to heart everything that people say, otherwise you may hear your servant talking badly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Talking badly”—this in the sense of speaking disparagingly rather than cursing, as some translations suggest.">j</a></sup>about you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> for you know how many times you yourself have talked badly about others!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> I have examined all this using the principles of wisdom. I told myself, “I will think wisely.” But wisdom eluded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Everything that exists is beyond our grasp—too deep for our understanding. Who can comprehend it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I turned my thoughts to discover, investigate, and to find out more about wisdom and what makes sense. I wanted to know more about how stupid evil really is, and how ridiculous it is to be a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> I discovered something more horrible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally, “bitter.”">k</a></sup>than death: foolishness like a woman<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="“Woman”: symbolic of Folly, see Proverbs 5and Proverbs 7.">l</a></sup>who tries to entrap you, who wants to use her mind and hands to catch you and tie you up. Those who follow God will not be caught, but sinners will fall into her trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> This is what I discovered after putting two and two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew: “one and one.”">m</a></sup>together to try and find out what it all meant, says the Teacher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; PSALMS 12:1"> Although I really searched, I didn't find what I was looking for. People say, “I found one man among a thousand, but not one woman.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="This appears to have been some kind of proverb. It's exact meaning is uncertain.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> But I did find this one thing: God made people to do what's right, but they have followed their own ideas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally, “but they have sought out many devices.”">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
