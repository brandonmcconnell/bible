<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> There are certainly many dreams, questions of meaning, and lots of different ideas, but you are to follow God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> If you see poor people somewhere<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Somewhere”: literally, “in the province”—a general term.">f</a></sup>being oppressed, or truth and justice violated, don't be shocked at it, for every official is responsible to others higher up, and there are officials over them as well.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Hebrew meaning is unclear. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:7–23; PSALMS 115:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> However, what the land produces is for everyone—even the king benefits from what is grown.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Again the meaning is unclear in this verse.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Habakkuk 2:13; PSALMS 119:36–37"> The more you earn, the more you have eating into your income. It only looks like you have more!</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> They put their money into bad investments and lose everything.<br />Now when we're born, we don't bring anything into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> When we die, we leave just as naked as when we were born, taking nothing with us from all we've worked for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; John 6:27"> This also makes me sick! What do people gain, working for the wind?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="As noted in footnote for 1:14, the word here can mean “wind,” “breath,” or “spirit.” So it could just as easily be translated as “What do you gain, working for a breath of air?” etc.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 127:2; Ezekiel 4:16–17; PSALMS 90:7–11"> They live their lives in darkness, very frustrated, sick, and resentful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> In addition, everyone to whom God gives wealth and possessions he also gives the ability to enjoy these gifts, to be grateful for what they are given, and to enjoy the work they do. This is also God's gift to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–24; Isaiah 65:13–14; PSALMS 37:16"> In fact such people have little time to think about life because God keeps them busy with all that makes them happy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Whether this is a good thing or not is not indicated.">k</a></sup></span>
