<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:31; PSALMS 83:2–4; 2 Samuel 15:6"> Abimelech, son of Jerub-baal, went to his mother's brothers at Shechem and told them and all his mother's relatives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:14; Judges 8:30; Hebrews 2:14"> “Please ask all the leaders of Shechem, ‘What's best for you? That seventy men, all of them Jerub-baal's sons, rule over you—or just one man?’ Remember I'm your own flesh and blood!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; PSALMS 10:3; Proverbs 1:11–14"> His mother's brothers shared his proposal with all the leaders of Shechem, and they decided to follow Abimelech, because they said, “He is our relative.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Acts 17:5; 2 Chronicles 13:7"> They gave him seventy shekels of silver from the temple of Baal-berith. Abimelech used the money to hire some arrogant troublemakers as his gang.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–2; 2 Chronicles 21:4; Judges 6:24"> He went to his father's house in Ophrah, and on one stone killed his seventy half-brothers, the sons of Jerub-baal. But Jotham, Jerub-baal's youngest son, escaped by going into hiding.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:9; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 12:20"> Then the leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo all assembled by the oak at the pillar in Shechem and made Abimelech their king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; John 4:20; Deuteronomy 27:12"> When Jotham got to hear this, he went up to the top of Mount Gerizim, and shouted in a loud voice: “Listen to me, leaders of Shechem, and God may listen to you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 8:22–23; Daniel 4:10–18; 2 Kings 14:9"> Once upon a time the trees were determined to anoint a king to rule over them. They said to the olive tree, ‘You shall be our king.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:7; Acts 4:27"> But the olive tree replied, ‘Should I stop giving my rich oil that benefits both gods and men just to go and sway to and fro over the trees?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the trees asked the fig tree, ‘You come and be our king.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:6–7"> But the fig tree replied, ‘Should I stop giving my good sweet fruit just to go and sway to and fro over the trees?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then the trees asked the grape vine, ‘You come and be our king.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Numbers 15:5"> But the grape vine replied, ‘Should I stop giving my wine that makes both gods and men happy just to go and sway to and fro over the trees?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 14:9"> Then all the trees asked the thorn bush, ‘You come and be our king.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:2; Judges 9:20; Hosea 14:7"> The thorn bush replied to the trees, ‘If you're really sincere about anointing me as your king, come and find shelter in my shade. But if not, may fire flame out of the thorn bush and burn up the cedars of Lebanon!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:35"> Have you acted sincerely and honestly by making Abimelech your king? Have you acted honorably towards Jerub-baal and his family? Have you respected him for all that he accomplished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:4; Romans 5:8; Revelation 12:11"> Don't forget how my father fought for you and risked his own life to save you from the Midianites!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:5–6; Judges 8:30–31; Judges 9:14"> But you have rebelled against my father's family today. You have killed his seventy sons on one stone and have made Abimelech, the son of his slave woman, king over the leaders of Shechem simply because he's related to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:6; James 4:16; Philippians 3:3"> Have you acted sincerely and honestly toward Jerub-baal and his family today? If so, may you be happy with Abimelech, and may he be happy with you too!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:15; Judges 9:56–57; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> But if you haven't, then may fire flame out from Abimelech, and may it burn up the leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo, and may fire flame out from the leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo and burn up Abimelech!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 21:16; 2 Samuel 20:14; Joshua 19:8"> Then Jotham escaped and ran away. He went to Beer and stayed there because of the threat of Abimelech his brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Abimelech ruled over Israel for three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 19:14; Isaiah 19:2"> Then God sent an evil spirit to cause trouble between Abimelech and the leaders of Shechem. The leaders of Shechem betrayed Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 2:32; PSALMS 7:16; Esther 9:25"> This happened because of the murder of the seventy sons of Jerub-baal and that responsibility for their blood be placed on Abimelech their brother, who killed them, and on the leaders of Shechem, who provided the means to kill his brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:12–13; Proverbs 1:11–12; Joshua 8:4"> The leaders of Shechem sent men to the hill passes to lie in wait and attack Abimelech, and, in the meantime, they robbed everyone who passed by on the road. Abimelech found out what was happening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 19:7"> Gaal, son of Ebed, had moved to Shechem with his relatives, and he gained the loyalty of the leaders of Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 9:4; Judges 9:46; PSALMS 109:17"> At harvest-time they went out into the countryside and gathered the grapes from their vineyards and trod them. They celebrated by having a festival in the temple of their god, where they ate and drank, and cursed Abimelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 34:2; Genesis 34:6; 1 Samuel 25:10"> “Who is this Abimelech?” asked Gaal, son of Ebed. “And who is Shechem, that we should have to serve him? Isn't he the son of Jerub-baal, while Zebul is actually the one in charge? You should serve the family of Hamor, the father of Shechem. Why should we have to serve Abimelech?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:4; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; Isaiah 36:8–9"> If I was the one in charge of you people, I would dispose of Abimelech! I would tell him, ‘Get your army together, and come and fight!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When Zebul, the governor of the city, got to hear what Gaal was saying, he became very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He secretly sent messengers to Abimelech to tell him, “Look, Gaal, son of Ebed, and his relatives have arrived in Shechem, and they are stirring up the town to rebel against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 3:15; PSALMS 36:4; Proverbs 1:11–16"> So come at night with your army and hide in the countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:7; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Leviticus 25:26"> In the morning as soon as the sun comes up, go and attack the town. When Gaal and his men come out to fight you, you can do whatever you want to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Abimelech left at night along with his army, and they separated into four companies that lay in wait near Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> When Gaal, son of Ebed, went out and stood at the town's entrance gate, Abimelech and his army came out from where they had been hiding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 8:24; Ezekiel 7:7"> Gaal saw the army approaching and said to Zebul, “Look! Some people are coming down from the hilltops!”<br />“That's just shadows made by the hills that look like men,” Zebul replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:14"> “No really, people are coming down from the high ground,” Gaal repeated. “Plus, there's another company coming down the road that passes the diviners' oak tree.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 9:28–29; 2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Samuel 2:26–27"> “Where's your big mouth now? You're the one who said, ‘Who is this Abimelech, that we should have to serve him?’” Zebul told him. “Aren't these the people you detested? Go on then—go and fight with them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Gaal led the leaders of Shechem out of the town and fought with Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 20:18–21; 1 Kings 20:30"> Abimelech attacked, and chased him and his men as they ran away, killing many of them as they tried to get back to the town gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Judges 9:30"> Abimelech went back to Arumah while Zebul expelled Gaal and his relatives from Shechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The following day the people of Shechem went out to the fields, and Abimelech was informed about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> He divided his army into three companies and had them lay in ambush in the fields. When he saw the people coming out of the city, he attacked and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Judges 9:15; Judges 9:20"> Abimelech and his company raced to occupy the town's entrance gate, while the two companies raced to attack everyone in the fields and kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Deuteronomy 29:23; James 2:13"> The battle for the town lasted all day but eventually Abimelech captured it. He killed the people, demolished the town, and scattered salt over the ground.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="To prevent anything from growing.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 9:4; 1 Kings 8:26"> When all the leaders of the tower of Shechem realized what had happened, they took refuge in the strongroom of the temple of El-berith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> When Abimelech found out that all the leaders in the tower of Shechem had gathered there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 68:14; Proverbs 1:11–12; Judges 7:17–18"> he and all the men with him went up Mount Zalmon. Abimelech grabbed hold of an ax and cut a branch from the trees. He lifted it onto his shoulder, and told his men, “Quick! You saw what I did. Do the same!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; James 3:16; Judges 9:20"> Each of them cut down a branch and followed Abimelech. They placed the branches against the strongroom and set it on fire. So all the people who lived in the tower of Shechem died, around one thousand men and women.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then Abimelech went to attack Thebez and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> But there was a strong tower inside the city. All the men and women and the town leaders ran there and barricaded themselves in, and then went up to the roof of the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Judges 9:48–49; 2 Kings 14:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> Abimelech went up to the tower to attack it. But as he approached the tower's entrance to set it on fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:21; Job 31:3; Jeremiah 49:20"> a woman dropped a millstone down on Abimelech's head and cracked his skull open.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4–5"> He quickly called the young man who carried his weapons, and ordered him, “Draw your sword and kill me, so they won't say about me that a woman killed him.” So the young man drove his sword through him, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:16; 2 Samuel 20:21–22; 1 Kings 22:35–36"> When the Israelites saw that Abimelech was dead, they all left and went home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="PSALMS 94:23; Judges 9:24; Proverbs 5:22"> This is how God paid back Abimelech's crime against his father of murdering his seventy brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Judges 9:20; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> He also repaid the people of Shechem for their evil, and the curse of Jotham, son of Jerub-baal, came down upon them.</span>
</div>
</div>
