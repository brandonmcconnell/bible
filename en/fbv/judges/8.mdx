<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> Then the men of Ephraim asked Gideon, “Why have you treated us like this? Why didn't you call us when you went to attack the Midianites?” They argued furiously with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> “Now what have I achieved in comparison to you?” Gideon replied. “Even Ephraim's left-over grapes are better than Abiezer's whole grape harvest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> God handed over to you Oreb and Zeeb, the two Midianite commanders. What have I managed to achieve in comparison to you?” When he told them this their animosity towards him died down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> Then Gideon crossed the Jordan with his three hundred men. Even though they were exhausted they continued the chase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; PSALMS 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> When they got to Succoth, Gideon asked the people there, “Please provide some bread to the men with me because they're worn out—I'm pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the Midianite kings.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> But the Succoth town leaders replied, “Why should we give your army bread when you haven't even captured Zebah and Zalmunna yet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> “In that case, once the Lord has handed Zebah and Zalmunna over to me, I'll return and thrash you with thorns and briers from the desert!” Gideon replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> He left and went to Penuel and asked them the same thing, but the people of Penuel answered the same way as the people of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> So he told them, “When I return victorious, I'll demolish this tower!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their armies of around fifteen thousand men. These were all who remained of the armies of the people of the East—one hundred and twenty thousand swordsmen had already been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> Gideon took the caravan route to the east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and he attacked their army, catching them offguard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> Zebah and Zalmunna ran away, but he chased after the two Midianite kings and captured them, defeating the whole of their terrified army.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Gideon, son of Joash, returned from the battle through the Heres Pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> There he captured a young man from Succoth and questioned him. The man wrote down for him the names of the seventy-seven leaders and elders of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> Gideon went and said to the Sukkoth town leaders, “Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, the ones you taunted me about when you said, ‘Why should we give your exhausted army bread when you haven't even captured Zebah and Zalmunna yet?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> So he took the town elders of Succoth and taught them a lesson using thorns and briers from the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> He also demolished the tower of Peniel and killed the men of the town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; PSALMS 89:12"> Then Gideon asked Zebah and Zalmunna, “What were they like, the men you killed at Tabor?”<br />“They looked like you,” they answered. “Each of them had the stature of a prince.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> “Those were my brothers, my mother's sons,” Gideon burst out. “As the Lord lives, if you had let them live, I wouldn't kill you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> He told Jether, his oldest son, “Go on, kill them!” But the boy refused to draw his sword, because he was young and afraid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> Zebah and Zalmunna said to Gideon, “Come on, you do it! Show yourself a man and kill us!” So Gideon went over and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and he took the crescent-shaped ornaments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Probably made of gold and indicated that the camels belonged to the kings.">a</a></sup>from the necks of their camels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> Then the Israelites said to Gideon, “You must become our ruler, you, your son, and your grandson;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Suggesting a hereditary kingship.">b</a></sup>because you've saved us from the Midianites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> “I won't be your ruler, and my son won't either,” Gideon replied. “The Lord will be your ruler.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> Then Gideon said, “I have a request to ask of you: that each of you give me an earring from your plunder.” (Their enemies were Ishmaelites and wore gold earrings.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> “We'll happily give them to you,” they replied. They spread out a cloak, and each of them threw on it earrings from their plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> The weight of the earrings he'd asked for was 1,700 shekels, not including the ornaments, the pendants, and the purple garments worn by the Midianite kings or the chains that were round their camels' necks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; PSALMS 106:39"> From the gold Gideon made an ephod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The breastplate worn by the high priest. This action by Gideon suggests that he thought a center of worship should be established in Ophrah.">c</a></sup>which he placed in his hometown of Ophrah. All Israel prostituted themselves there by worshiping it as an idol,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“By worshiping it as an idol”: supplied for clarity.">d</a></sup>and it became a trap to Gideon and his family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> This is how the Midianites were subjugated before the Israelites and did not gain power again. So the land was at peace for forty years during the lifetime of Gideon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> Jerub-baal, son of Joash, went home to live in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> Gideon had seventy sons, all his own, because he had many wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> His concubine, who lived in Shechem, also had a son. He named him Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> Gideon, son of Joash, died at a good old age and was buried in the tomb of his father Joash, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> But as soon as Gideon died, the Israelites went back to prostituting themselves, worshiping before the Baals. They made Baal-berith their god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 78:42; PSALMS 106:21; PSALMS 78:11"> They forgot about the Lord their God, who had saved them from all their enemies that surrounded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> They did not show any respect to the family of Jerub-baal (Gideon) for all the good he had done for Israel.</span>
</div>
