<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 38:17; Genesis 29:21; Luke 15:29"> Some time later when the wheat was being harvested, Samson went to pay his wife a visit, taking with him a young goat as a present. “I want to go to my wife in her bedroom,” he said when he arrived,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“When he arrived”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>but her father would not let him go in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 14:20; Judges 14:16; Genesis 38:14"> “I thought you must totally hate her, so I gave her to your best man,” he told Samson. “But her younger sister is even more attractive—why don't you marry her instead?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 14:15"> “This time I can't be blamed for the trouble I'm going to cause the Philistines,” Samson declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:15; PSALMS 63:10; Lamentations 5:18"> He went and caught three hundred foxes and tied their tails together, two by two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30; Exodus 22:6"> He attached a torch to each of the tied tails and set them on fire. Then he let them loose in the grain fields of the Philistines, setting fire to all the grain, harvested and unharvested, as well as the vineyards and olive groves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Proverbs 22:8"> “Who did this?” the Philistines asked. “It was Samson, the son-in-law of the man from Timnah,” they were told. “That man gave Samson's wife to Samson's best man.” So the Philistines went and burned her and her father to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 14:4; Romans 12:19; Judges 14:19"> Samson told them, “If this is the way you're going to act, then I won't stop until I take my revenge on you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> He attacked them violently,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“He attacked them violently”: literally, “he struck them hip and thigh,” meaning “completely.”">b</a></sup>killing them, and then left to go and live in a cave at the rock of Etam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 15:17; Judges 15:19; Judges 15:14"> So the Philistine army came and camped in Judah, drawn up for battle near Lehi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The people of Judah asked, “Why have you invaded us?”<br />“We've come to capture Samson, to do to him what he's done to us!” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> Three thousand men of Judah went to the cave at the rock of Etam and asked Samson, “Don't you understand that the Philistines rule over us? What do you think you're doing to us?”<br />“I only did what they did to me,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Acts 7:25; 1 Kings 2:25"> “Well, we've come to take you prisoner and hand you over to the Philistines,” they told him.<br />“Just swear to me that you're not going to kill me yourselves,” Samson answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “No, we won't,” they assured him. “We'll only tie you up and hand you over to the Philistines. We certainly aren't going to kill you!” They tied him using two new ropes and led him up from the rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 14:19; Judges 3:10; PSALMS 118:11"> When Samson got close to Lehi, the Philistines ran towards him, shouting at him. But the Spirit of the Lord swept over him, and the ropes tying his arms together became as weak as burnt flax, and his hands broke free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:8; Judges 3:31"> He grabbed the fresh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In other words, the bone was not dry and brittle.">c</a></sup>jawbone of a donkey, using it to kill a thousand Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Samson declared, “With a donkey's jawbone I have piled the dead into heaps. With a donkey's jawbone I have killed a thousand men.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> After Samson had finished his speech, he threw away the jawbone, and he named the place Hill of the Jawbone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–8; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 8:4"> Samson was now extremely thirsty, and he called out to the Lord, saying, “You have achieved this amazing victory<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “salvation.”">d</a></sup>through your servant, but now do I have to die of thirst and be captured by the heathen?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 45:27; PSALMS 34:6"> So God split open a rock seam in Lehi, and water came out of it. Samson drank and his strength returned—he felt much better. That's why he named it the Spring of the Caller, and it's still there in Lehi to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 16:31; Judges 13:5"> Samson led Israel as judge for twenty years during the time of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
