<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Genesis 12:6"> Jerub-baal (Gideon) and those who were with him got up early and went and camped by the Harod spring. The Midianite camp was to the north in the valley near the Moreh hill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> The Lord told Gideon, “There are too many soldiers with you for me to hand over the Midianites to them, otherwise Israel will brag to me, saying, ‘I saved myself by my own strength.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Revelation 17:14; Revelation 21:8"> So tell the soldiers, ‘Anyone who is worried or afraid can leave Mount Gilead and go back home.’” Twenty-two thousand of them went back home, but ten thousand stayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 33:16; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 14:6"> Then the Lord told Gideon, “There are still too many soldiers. Take them down to the water and I will reduce<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “test,” “sift,” “purge.”">a</a></sup>them for you. Whoever I tell you, ‘He shall go with you,’ he shall go. But anyone that I say, ‘He shall not go with you,’ he shall not go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Gideon took the soldiers down to the water. The Lord told Gideon, “Set to one side those who lap the water with their tongues, like a dog does, and on the other side those who kneel down to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Three hundred lapped water from their hands to their mouths. All the rest knelt down to drink the water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:6; Isaiah 41:14–16; Judges 7:18–22"> The Lord told Gideon, “With these three hundred men that lapped I will save you and hand over the Midianites to you. Let all the rest of the soldiers go home.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:20; Numbers 10:9; Joshua 6:4"> The three hundred took over the supplies and trumpets of the others. Gideon sent all the rest home, but held onto the three hundred men.<br />The Midianite camp was below him in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 46:2–3; Judges 3:28; Acts 18:9–10"> That night the Lord spoke to Gideon, “Get up, go down and attack the camp, for I have handed it over to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Judges 4:8–9"> But if you are afraid to go down, go with your servant Purah to the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Judges 7:13–15; Isaiah 35:3–4"> You'll hear what they're talking about and then you'll have the courage to attack the camp.” So he took his servant Purah with him and went to the edge of the camp where armed men were on guard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 6:5; Joshua 11:4"> The Midianites, Amalekites, and all the peoples of the East filled the valley like a swarm of locusts, and as for their camels, they were as uncountable as the sand on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; Isaiah 41:14–15; Judges 4:9"> Just as Gideon arrived, a man was telling his friend about a dream he'd had. He was saying, “I had this dream. I dreamed I saw a round loaf of barley bread come rolling into the Midianite camp. It hit a tent, knocking it upside-down, flat on the ground!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6–7; Joshua 2:9; Joshua 2:24"> “This can only represent the victory by the sword of Gideon, son of Joash, a man of Israel,” his friend answered. “God has handed over to him the Midianites and everyone else camped here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–6; Genesis 40:8; 2 Chronicles 20:18–19"> When Gideon heard the dream and what it meant, he bowed in thanks to God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“In thanks to God”: implied. The Hebrew simply says, “he bowed.”">b</a></sup>He went back to the Israelite camp and announced, “On your feet! For the Lord has handed over the Midianite camp to you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7"> He divided the three hundred men into three companies. He handed them all trumpets, and empty jars with torches inside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; Matthew 16:24; Hebrews 13:7"> “Watch me and follow my example,” he told them. “So when I get to the edge of the camp, do exactly what I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:15–17; 1 Samuel 17:47"> When I and those with me blow the trumpets, then you are to blow your trumpets from all around the camp, and shout, ‘For the Lord and for Gideon!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 2:9; Exodus 14:24; Matthew 25:6"> Gideon and the hundred men who with him arrived at the edge of the camp around midnight,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “the beginning of the middle watch.”">c</a></sup>after the guards were changed. They blew their trumpets and smashed the jars they were holding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 27:13; 2 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> All three companies blew their trumpets and smashed their jars. They held the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right hands, and they shouted, “A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Proverbs 28:1; 2 Kings 7:6–7"> Each man stood in his place encircling the camp, and all the enemy soldiers ran around shouting—then they fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Joshua 6:20; Joshua 6:4"> When they blew the three hundred trumpets, the Lord made all the men in the camp attack one other with their swords. The enemy army fled to Beth-shittah near Zererah, all the way to the border of Abel Meholah near Tabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 6:35; 1 Samuel 14:21–22"> The Israelite soldiers were summoned from Naphtali, Asher, and all of Manasseh, and they chased after the Midianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 3:27–28; Philippians 1:27; Romans 15:30"> Gideon sent messengers through all the hill country of Ephraim saying, “Come and attack the Midianites, and take control of the Jordan fords ahead of them as far as Beth-barah.” So all the men of Ephraim were summoned, and they took control of the Jordan fords as far as Beth-barah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:26; Judges 8:3–4; PSALMS 83:11–12"> They also captured Oreb and Zeeb, two of the Midianite commanders. They killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb, and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb. They continued chasing down the Midianites and brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb back to Gideon who was on the other side of the Jordan.</span>
</div>
