<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> After Joshua died, the Israelites asked the Lord, “Which tribe from among us should go first and attack the Canaanites?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> “Judah is to go first,” the Lord replied. “I have handed the land over to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> The men of Judah said to their relatives of the tribe of Simeon, “Come with us to the land that has been allotted to us, and fight together with us against the Canaanites. Then we'll do the same for you and your allotted land.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The allocations of land were next to each other.">a</a></sup>So the tribe of Simeon joined with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> The men of Judah attacked the Canaanites and Perizzites, and the Lord handed them over in defeat. They killed ten thousand of the enemy at the town of Bezek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There they confronted Adoni-bezek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meaning “the lord of Bezek.”">b</a></sup>and fought with him, defeating the Canaanites and Perizzites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Adoni-bezek ran away, but they chased after him and captured him, and then cut off his thumbs and big toes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> Adoni-bezek said, “I had seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off picking up left-overs from under my table. Now God has paid me back in the same way what I did to them.” They took him to Jerusalem where he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> The men of Judah attacked Jerusalem and conquered it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Clearly this was not a permanent conquest since David had to take Jerusalem several centuries later.">c</a></sup>They killed the inhabitants with the sword and burned the town down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> After this the men of Judah went to fight against the Canaanites living in the hill country, in the Negev, and in the foothills of the lowlands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> They attacked the Canaanites who lived in Hebron (previously known as Kiriath Arba) and defeated Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> From there they went on to attack the people living in Debir (previously known as Kiriath Sepher).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 18:23"> Caleb announced, “I will give my daughter Acsah in marriage to whoever attacks and captures Kiriath Sepher.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> Othniel, son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, was the one who captured it, so he gave him his daughter Acsah in marriage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> When Acsah came to Othniel, she encouraged him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew text. Some versions of the Septuagint read. “He encouraged her.”">d</a></sup>to ask her father for a field. As she got off her donkey, Caleb asked her, “What do you want?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> “Please give me a blessing,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Blessing”: this refers to the custom of the father of the bride giving his daughter a special blessing on the occasion of her marriage.">e</a></sup>she replied. “You gave me land that's like the desert, so please give me springs of water as well.” So Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6"> The descendants of Moses' father-in-law, the Kenite, went with the people of Judah from the city of palms to the wilderness of Judah in the Negev near Arad where they settled among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> Then Judah joined Simeon and defeated the Canaanites living in Zephath. They completely destroyed the town, so they named it Hormah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Hormah”: meaning “devoted to destruction.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> Judah also captured the towns of Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron, each with its surrounding territory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; PSALMS 60:12"> The Lord was with Judah, and they took over the hill country, but they could not drive out the people living on the plain because they had iron chariots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> As Moses had stipulated, Hebron was given to Caleb, who drove out from it the descendants of three sons of Anak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> However, Benjamin could not drive out the Jebusites, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so the Jebusites live among the people of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this very day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See also Joshua 15:63where Judah was similarly unable to take Jerusalem.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; 2 Kings 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> The descendants of Joseph<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Meaning the tribe of Ephraim and the half tribe of Manasseh.">h</a></sup>went and attacked the town of Bethel, and the Lord was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> They sent spies to investigate Bethel, which was previously known as Luz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; 1 Samuel 30:15"> The spies saw a man leaving the town and told him, “Please show us how to get into town, and we will treat you well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> The man showed them the way into the town, and they killed all the inhabitants except the man and his family, whom they let go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; 2 Chronicles 1:17"> The man moved to the country of the Hittites, and built a town there and called it Luz, which is its name to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; PSALMS 106:34–35"> However, Manasseh didn't drive out the people living in the towns of Beth Shan, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo and their surrounding villages because the Canaanites insisted on living in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When the Israelites grew stronger, they made the Canaanites do forced labor, but they never completely drove them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16"> Ephraim didn't drive out the Canaanites living in the town of Gezer, so the Canaanites went on living there among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> Zebulun didn't drive out the people living in the towns of Kitron and Nahalol, so the Canaanites went on living there among them. However, the Canaanites were made to do forced labor for the people of Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> Asher didn't drive out the people living in the towns of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–35"> so the people of Asher went on living there among the Canaanite inhabitants of the land because they hadn't driven them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; PSALMS 18:24"> Naphtali didn't drive out the people living in the towns of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath. So the people of Asher went on living there among the Canaanite inhabitants of the land because they hadn't driven them out. However, the people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath were made to do forced labor for the people of Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> The Amorites pushed the people of Dan back into the hill country—they did not let them come down into the lowlands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> The Amorites insisted on remaining in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim, but when the tribes of Joseph grew stronger, the Amorites were made to do forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> The border with the Amorites ran from Scorpion Pass through Sela and on up from there.</span>
</div>
</div>
