<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:30; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Corinthians 5:10–12"> So all of you Israelites have to decide here and now what you're going to do about it!”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Judges 20:1; Proverbs 21:3"> Everyone stood up and unitedly declared, “None of us are going home to our tents! None of us are going home to our houses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 1:7; Nehemiah 11:1; Joshua 14:2"> This is what we're going to do to Gibeah: we will attack it with our forces chosen by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> We'll take ten men from a hundred from all the Israelite tribes, then a hundred from a thousand, then a thousand from ten thousand, to arrange food for the army, so when the troops reach Gibeah in Benjamin, they can pay them back for all these disgusting things they've done in Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All the men of Israel were in agreement and gathered to attack the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 20:10; Romans 12:18"> The Israelite tribes also sent men throughout the territory of Benjamin, asking the people, “What are you doing about this terrible evil that has taken place among you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; Judges 19:22"> Hand over these wicked men so we can execute them and get rid of this evil from Israel!” But the Benjamites refused to listen to what their fellow Israelites had to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 20:20; Job 15:25–26; Numbers 21:23"> They left their towns and assembled at Gibeah to go and fight the other Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:25; Numbers 1:37"> That day a total of twenty-six thousand men armed with swords were called up from the towns of Benjamin, in addition to the seven hundred seasoned warriors from Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:2; 1 Samuel 17:49–50"> Making up part of this army were seven hundred experienced soldiers who used their left hands. All of them could fire a slingshot and not miss by even a hair's breadth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; 2 Chronicles 17:14–18; Numbers 26:51"> The Israelite army (excluding Benjamin) numbered four hundred thousand seasoned warriors, all armed with swords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Judges 20:26–27; Numbers 27:21"> The Israelites went to Bethel and asked God, “Which ones of us should be the first to go and fight the Benjamites?”<br />“Judah is to go first,” the Lord replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:16; Joshua 3:1; Joshua 6:12"> The next morning the Israelites left and set up their camp near Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then they marched out for battle with the army of Benjamin, taking up their positions to attack Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 20:25; PSALMS 73:18–19; 2 Chronicles 28:10"> But the Benjamites came out of Gibeah and slaughtered twenty-two thousand Israelites on the battlefield that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 64:5; Judges 20:17; 1 Samuel 30:6"> But the Israelites encouraged one another to be confident, and they took up the same positions they had on the first day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:26–27; PSALMS 78:34–36; Joshua 7:6"> The Israelites went and cried before the Lord until the evening and asked, “Should we go and attack the Benjamites again, our relatives?”<br />“Go and attack them,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So the second day they advanced to attack the army of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 20:21; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:5"> However, Benjamites came out of Gibeah once more and slaughtered eighteen thousand Israelites, all armed with swords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Joel 2:12–18; Judges 20:18"> Then all the Israelites and all their army went to Bethel, and sat crying there before the Lord. That day they fasted until evening and gave burnt offerings and friendship offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> The Israelites asked the Lord what to do. At that time the Ark of God's Agreement was kept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 7:9; Joshua 24:33; Deuteronomy 18:5"> Phinehas, son of Eleazar and grandson of Aaron, was the priest. The Israelites asked the Lord, “Should we go and fight against our relatives from Benjamin again, or not?”<br />“Yes, go! Tomorrow I will hand them over to you,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; 2 Samuel 5:23; Judges 20:34"> Then the Israelites set up an ambush around Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> On the third day they took up the same positions they had as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 19:13–14; Joshua 7:5; Isaiah 10:29"> The Benjamites came out to attack them and were lured away from the town as they began to kill Israelites as they had before. Some thirty Israelites died on the battlefield and along the roads, the one that goes towards Bethel and the other that goes back towards Gibeah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–16"> “We're defeating them, just like before,” the Benjamites shouted.<br />But the Israelites said, “Let's run away from them and lure them away from the town towards the roads.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 8:18–22"> The main army of Israelites left where they were and took up positions at Baal-tamar, while those in the ambush west of Gibeah charged out to attack from where they had been hiding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:14; Isaiah 47:11; Job 21:13"> Ten thousand seasoned Israelite warriors attacked Gibeah, and the fighting was so intense the Benjamites didn't realize they were on the brink of disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 20:5; Judges 20:15; Judges 20:44–46"> So the Lord defeated Benjamin before Israel. That day the Israelites killed twenty-five thousand one hundred Benjamites, all armed with swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–29"> The Benjamites saw that they were defeated.<br />The Israelites had fallen back before the Benjamites because they were confident the ambush they had put in place near Gibeah would be successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 8:19; Joshua 6:5; Exodus 19:13"> The men from the ambush raced to attack the town, and they killed everyone in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Genesis 17:21; 2 Kings 4:16"> The agreement was that they would send up a great cloud of smoke to show the town had fallen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Show the town had fallen”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Judges 20:32"> The Israelite army turned to attack the Benjamites, who had already killed about thirty Israelites. The Benjamites were saying, “We're completely defeating them, just like the first battle!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Revelation 19:3; Genesis 19:28"> However, when the Israelites saw the columns of smoke rising heavenwards to form a great cloud over the whole of the town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Luke 21:26; Revelation 6:15–17"> they turned on their enemies. The Benjamites were horrified when they saw it and realized they were doomed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 8:15; Hosea 9:9; Lamentations 1:3"> They turned and ran from the Israelites towards the desert, but the battle caught up with them, and the Israelites also killed those who left the towns on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 8:20–22"> Chasing after the Benjamites, the Israelites surrounded them, easily overtaking them east of Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Eighteen thousand Benjamites were killed, all of them courageous warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Joshua 15:32; Judges 21:13; Zechariah 14:10"> Some of the Benjamites that were left ran towards Pomegranate Rock in the desert, and the Israelites killed another five thousand men on the way. They chased another group of Benjamites as far as Gidom and killed another thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 20:35; Judges 20:15"> So that day twenty-five thousand Benjamites were killed, all armed with swords and all courageous warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; PSALMS 103:9–10; Habakkuk 3:2"> There were six hundred who ran away to Pomegranate Rock in the desert and they stayed there four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:15–17; 2 Chronicles 25:13; 2 Chronicles 28:6–9"> The Israelites went back into the territory of the Benjamites, and going from town to town, they killed everything: people, animals, everything they found. Then they burned down every town on their way.</span>
