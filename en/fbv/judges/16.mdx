</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 2:24; PSALMS 107:16; Isaiah 63:1–5"> But Samson only stayed until halfway through the night. Putting them on his shoulders, he carried them to the hill opposite Hebron.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gaza to Hebron is around 40 miles.">a</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Proverbs 22:14; Nehemiah 13:26"> Later he fell in love with a woman named Delilah living in the Sorek Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Judges 14:15; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> The Philistine leaders approached her, saying, “See if you can seduce him and get him to show you the secret of his incredible strength, and find out how we can overpower him and tie him up so he can't do anything. We'll all give you eleven hundred shekels of silver each.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21; Proverbs 6:26; Proverbs 22:14"> Delilah went and pleaded with Samson, “Please tell me where your incredible strength comes from, and what can be used to tie you up so you can't do anything.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:2–3; Romans 3:8; 1 Samuel 27:10"> “If I'm tied up with seven supple bowstrings that haven't dried out, I'll become just as weak,” Samson told her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26"> The Philistine leaders brought her seven supple bowstrings that hadn't dried out, and she tied him up with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 58:9"> Having arranged for men to hide in her bedroom ready to attack him, she shouted out, “Samson, the Philistines are here to get you!” But he snapped the bowstrings like a thread snaps when a flame touches it. So nobody found out where his strength came from.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:13; Luke 22:48; Judges 16:7"> Later Delilah said to Samson, “You've made me look stupid, telling me these lies! So now please tell me what can be used to tie you up.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:5; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 13:3"> “If I'm tied up tight with new ropes that haven't been used before, I'll become just as weak as anyone else,” he told her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Delilah got some new ropes and tied him up with them. She shouted out, “Samson, the Philistines are here to get you!” As before, men were hiding in her bedroom. But again Samson snapped the ropes from his arms as if they were thin threads.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Delilah said to Samson, “You keep on making me look stupid, telling me these lies! Just tell me what can be used to tie you up!”<br />“If you were to weave the seven braids of my hair into the web on the loom and tighten it with the pin, I'll become as weak as anyone else,” he told her. So while he was asleep, Delilah took the seven braids of his head, weaving them into the web,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 106:43; Ezra 9:13–14"> and tightening the pin. She shouted out, “Samson, the Philistines are here to get you!” But Samson woke up and ripped out both the pin and the web from the loom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew of this and the following verse appears damaged. Septuagint reading used.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 14:16; 1 John 2:15–16; John 14:21–24"> Then Delilah complained to Samson, “How can you tell me, ‘I love you,’ when you don't let me into your confidence?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Tell me your private thoughts”: literally, “your heart is not with me.”">c</a></sup>Three times you've made me look stupid, not telling me where your incredible strength comes from!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21–23; Luke 18:5; Mark 14:24"> She nagged and complained all the time, pestering him until he wanted to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Judges 13:5; Proverbs 12:23"> Eventually Samson confided in her, sharing everything. “My hair has never been cut, because I've been dedicated as a Nazirite to God from my birth. If I'm shaved, my strength will leave me, and I'll become as weak as anyone else.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Judges 16:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> Delilah realized that he had truly confided in her and shared everything, she sent a message to the Philistine leaders telling them, “Come back once more, because this time he's confided in me and told me everything.” So the Philistine leaders returned, bringing with them the money to give to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 7:26–27; Proverbs 7:21–23; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> Delilah soothed him to sleep on her lap, and then called in someone to shave off the seven braids of hair. She started to torment him but he couldn't do anything for his strength left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:42–43; 1 Samuel 16:14; Joshua 7:12"> She shouted out, “Samson, the Philistines are here to get you!”<br />Samson woke up and thought to himself, “I'll do like before and shake myself free.” But he didn't know that the Lord had left him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 2:19; Proverbs 14:14; Isaiah 47:2"> The Philistines grabbed him and gouged out his eyes. Then they took him to Gaza where they imprisoned him in bronze chains. He was made to work grinding grain at the mill in the prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 107:13–14; PSALMS 106:44–45; Leviticus 26:44"> But his hair began to grow back after it had been shaved off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–5; Proverbs 24:17; PSALMS 35:15–16"> The Philistine leaders gathered for a great religious festival to sacrifice to their god Dagon and to celebrate, saying, “Our god has handed Samson our enemy over to us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23; Judges 15:16"> When the people saw him, they praised their god, and said, “Our god has handed our enemy over to us, the one who devastated our land and killed so many of us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 19:6; Judges 9:27"> As they began to get drunk, they shouted, “Summon Samson so he can entertain us!” So they summoned Samson from the prison to entertain them, and made him stand between the two main pillars of the building.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Samson said to the servant boy who was leading him by the hand, “Leave me by the pillars on which the temple rests so I can feel them, and lean against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2; Joshua 2:8"> The temple was full of people. All the Philistine rulers were there, and on the roof were the ordinary people watching what Samson was doing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:15; Lamentations 3:31–32; PSALMS 143:12"> Samson called out to the Lord, “Lord God, please remember me and give me strength. Please God, do this just once more, so that with one act I may pay the Philistines back in revenge for the loss of my two eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Samson reached for the two middle pillars that supported the temple. With his right hand leaning on one pillar and his left hand on the other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Matthew 16:25; Matthew 24:38–39"> Samson shouted, “Let me die with the Philistines!” and he pushed with all his strength. The temple collapsed on the rulers and all the people in it. So those killed at his death were more than he killed in his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 15:20; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> Then his brothers and his whole family came and took him back and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of Manoah his father. He led Israel as judge for twenty years.</span>
</div>
