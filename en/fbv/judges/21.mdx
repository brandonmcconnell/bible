</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> The Israelites felt sorry for their brother Benjamin, saying, “Today one tribe has been hacked off from Israel!</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 2:5–6"> Then they asked, “Which one of all the tribes of Israel didn't attend the assembly we held before the Lord at Mizpah?” They found out that no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp for the assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for once they had done a head count, there was nobody there from Jabesh-gilead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; 1 Samuel 11:7"> So the assembly sent twelve thousand of their best warriors there. They gave them orders, saying, “Go and kill the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with your swords, even the women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> This is what you have to do: Destroy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “devote to destruction”: this was the way God had ordered the Israelites to deal with the Canaanite towns (for example Jericho). Now it is being illegitimately applied to another Israelite town.">a</a></sup>every male and every woman who has had sex with a man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12; PSALMS 78:60"> They managed to find among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred virgins who had not had sex with a man. They took them to the camp in Shiloh, in the land of Canaan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It seems that this reference to Israel as “the land of Canaan” is deliberate and is intended to show how far Israel had fallen into idolatry.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> Then the whole Israelite assembly sent a message to the Benjamites at Pomegranate Rock to tell them, “Peace!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> So the men of Benjamin went back home, and the Israelites gave them the four hundred women from Jabesh-gilead who had been spared as wives. However, there weren't enough for all of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Isaiah 58:12"> The people felt sorry for the Benjamites because the Lord had made this empty hole among the Israelite tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The elders of the assembly asked, “What shall we do to supply the remaining wives because all the women of Benjamin have been destroyed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> They added, “There have to be heirs for the Benjamite survivors—an Israelite tribe of Israel can't be wiped out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> But we can't let them have our daughters as wives, since we as the people of Israel swore a sacred oath, saying, ‘Anyone who gives a wife to a Benjamite is cursed!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> Then they said, “Look! Every year there's the Lord's festival in Shiloh. It's held north of Bethel, and east of the road that goes from Bethel to Shechem, south of Lebonah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So they ordered the Benjamites, “Go and hide in the vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> Keep a lookout, and when you see the young women from Shiloh come out to perform their dances, run out from the vineyards, and each of you abduct<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The word used here is an unusual one and means to seize someone by force.">c</a></sup>a wife for yourself and go back home to the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> If their fathers or brothers come complaining to us, we'll tell them, ‘Please do us a favor, because we couldn't find enough wives for them in the war.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring to the attack on Jabesh-gilead">d</a></sup>And it's not as if you're guilty of breaking the oath since you didn't give them in marriage.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> The Benjaminites did as they were ordered. Each man grabbed one of the women dancers up to the total needed and carried her off to be his wife. Then they went back to their own land, where they rebuilt their towns and lived in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then the Israelites left and went home to their tribes and families, each one going to the land they owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> At that time Israel didn't have a king—everyone did what they themselves thought was the right thing to do.</span>
</div>
