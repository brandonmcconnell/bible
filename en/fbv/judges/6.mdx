<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26–29; Judges 2:19–20; Judges 2:13–14"> The Israelites did what was evil in the Lord's sight. So the Lord handed them over to the Midianites for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:38; 1 Samuel 13:6; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> The Midianite oppression was so great that because of them the Israelites made themselves hiding places in mountains, caves, and fortifications.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Judges 3:13; Judges 7:12"> Whenever the Israelites planted their crops, the Midianites, Amalekites, and other peoples from the east would come and attack them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:51; Micah 6:15"> They would set up their camps and destroy the country's crops as far away as Gaza. They didn't leave anything to eat in the whole of Israel, and they took for themselves all the sheep, cattle, and donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 8:10; Jeremiah 46:23"> They arrived in huge numbers with their livestock and tents like swarms of locusts, with so many camels they couldn't be counted. They invaded the land to completely devastate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Jeremiah 5:17; PSALMS 50:15"> The Israelites were made desperately poor by the Midianites and they called out to the Lord for help.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When the Israelites cried out to the Lord for help because of the Midianites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–32; Isaiah 63:9–14; PSALMS 136:10–16"> the Lord sent the Israelites a prophet. He told them, “This is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says: ‘I brought you out of Egypt; I led you out from the place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “house.”">a</a></sup>where you were slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 44:2–3"> I saved you from the power of the Egyptians and from everyone who oppressed you. I expelled them before you and gave their land to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:2; 2 Kings 17:35–39; Jeremiah 9:13"> I warned you: I am the Lord your God. You must not worship the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you're now living.’ But you didn't listen to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 17:2; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 13:3"> The angel of the Lord came and sat under the oak tree in Ophrah that belonged to Joash the Abiezrite. His son Gideon was threshing wheat in a winepress there to hide it from the Midianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Luke 1:28; Judges 2:18"> The angel of the Lord appeared to him and said, “The Lord is with you, great man of courage!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 77:7–9; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 59:1–2"> “Excuse me, my lord, but if the Lord is with us, why has all this happened to us?” Gideon replied. “Where are all his wonderful miracles that our forefathers reminded us about when they said, ‘Wasn't it the Lord who led us out of Egypt?’ But now the Lord has given up on us and has handed us over to the Midianites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:5–9; Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 12:11"> The Lord turned to him and said, “Go in the strength that you have and save Israel from the Midianites. Aren't I the one sending you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Exodus 3:11; Exodus 4:10"> “Excuse me, my lord, but how can I save Israel?” Gideon replied. “My family is the least important of the tribe of Manasseh, and I am the least important person of that family!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Exodus 3:12; Acts 11:21"> “I will be with you,” the Lord told him. “You will defeat the Midianites as if they were just one man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 86:17; Exodus 33:13; Isaiah 7:11"> “Please, Lord, if you think well of me, give me a sign that it's really you telling me this,” Gideon asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 13:15; Genesis 18:3; Genesis 18:5"> “Don't leave until I come back and present my offering to you.”<br />“I will remain here until you return,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 13:15–19; Leviticus 2:4"> Gideon went and cooked a young goat, and baked some unleavened bread from an ephah of flour. He put the meat in a basket and the broth in a pot. He carried them out and presented them to the angel under the oak tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:19; 1 Kings 18:33–34"> The angel of God told him, “Place the meat and the unleavened bread on this rock and pour the broth over them.” So Gideon did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Kings 18:38; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> The angel of the Lord held out the staff he was holding and touched the meat and unleavened bread with the tip. Fire flamed from the rock and burned up the meat and unleavened bread. Then the angel vanished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Exodus 33:20; Deuteronomy 5:26"> When Gideon realized that it was the angel of the Lord, he cried out, “Oh no, Lord God! I've seen the angel of the Lord face to face!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; John 20:26; Genesis 43:23"> But the Lord told him, “Peace! Don't worry, you're not going to die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Exodus 17:15; Judges 8:32"> So Gideon built an altar to the Lord there and called it “The Lord is Peace.” It's still there today, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; Judges 3:7; Acts 5:29"> That night the Lord told Gideon, “Take your father's bull and a second bull seven years old, and tear down your father's altar of Baal, and cut down the Asherah pole beside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Samuel 24:18; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Then build an altar to the Lord your God in the proper way on this hilltop. Using the wood of the Asherah pole you cut down as firewood, take the second bull and present it as a burnt offering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; John 2:5; Deuteronomy 4:1"> Gideon accompanied by ten of his servants did what the Lord had told him. However, because he was afraid of his family and the people of the town, he did it during the night rather than in the day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Early in the morning when the people of the town got up, they saw that the altar of Baal had been torn down and the Asherah pole beside it had been cut down, with the second bull sacrificed on the altar that had just been built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They asked one another, “Who did this?” They made inquiries and they were told, “Gideon, son of Joash, did it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:9; Philippians 3:6; Jeremiah 50:38"> “Hand over your son,” the people of the town ordered Joash. “He must die, because he has torn down the altar of Baal and cut down the Asherah pole beside it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 5:11"> Joash replied to all those confronting him, “Are you arguing on Baal's behalf? Do you have to save him? Anyone who argues for him will be put to death by morning! If he is a god let him fight for himself against those who tore down his altar.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:11; Judges 7:1; 2 Samuel 11:21"> That day Gideon was called Jerub-baal, which means “Let Baal fight with him,” because he had torn down his altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 17:16; Judges 6:3; Romans 8:35–39"> All the Midianites, Amalekites, and other peoples of the East gathered together and crossed over the Jordan. They camped in the Valley of Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 3:27; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Judges 3:10"> The Spirit of the Lord came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “clothed.”">b</a></sup>on Gideon, and he blew the trumpet, calling the Abiezrites to join him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6–12; Judges 4:6"> He sent messengers through the whole territory of Manasseh, calling them to join him, and also to Asher, Zebulun and Naphtali, so they also came and joined the others.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 6:14; Matthew 16:1; Judges 6:17–20"> Gideon said to God, “If you will save Israel through me as you promised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 14:5; Matthew 10:5–6; Matthew 15:24"> then look—I will put a fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If the fleece is wet with dew but the ground is dry, then I will know that you are going to save Israel through me as you promised.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7"> That's what happened. When Gideon got up early the next morning, he pressed on the fleece and squeezed out the dew, enough water to fill a bowl.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 18:32; Isaiah 50:2; PSALMS 107:33–35"> Then Gideon said to God, “Please don't get cross with me. Just let me make one more request. Let me do one more test with the fleece. This time let the fleece be dry and the whole ground covered with dew.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> That night God did exactly that. The fleece alone was dry and the whole ground was covered with dew.</span>
</div>
</div>
