<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> So the Danites chose from among their tribes five leading men who came from the towns of Zorah and Eshtaol to scout out the land and explore it.<br />“Go and explore the land,” they told them. When the men came to the hill country of Ephraim, they arrived at Micah's house where they spent the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> While they were there, they recognized the young Levite's accent, so they went to him and asked him, “So who brought you here, and what are you doing in this place? Why are you here?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> “Micah arranged things for me, and he hired me as his priest,” he told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> “Please ask the Lord for us so we can find out if our journey will be successful,” they asked him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; PSALMS 33:18"> “Go in peace,” the priest replied. “The journey you are taking is being observed by the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Note that the priest is not declaring success or otherwise. The quick response by the priest also leads to the suspicion that he did not really spend much time asking the Lord for an answer.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> The five men left and went to the town of Laish. They observed that the people there lived in safety, and followed the customs of the Sidonians. The people were unsuspecting and confident of their security, at home in a productive land. They didn't have a strong ruler, they lived a long way from the Sidonians, and had no other allies to help them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“To help them.” Supplied for clarity. In addition, the lack of a “strong ruler” (literally, “a possessor of restraint”) meant that the town's defense would not be under a powerful military commander.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> When the men returned to Zorah and Eshtaol, their relatives asked them, “What did you…?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> “Come on, let's go and attack them!” the men interrupted. “We've surveyed the land, and it's excellent! Aren't you going to do something? Don't put off going there and occupying the land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When you get there you'll find the people are unsuspecting and the land is extensive. God has given you a place where there's no shortage of anything!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> So six hundred Danite armed men left Zorah and Eshtaol, ready to attack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> En route they camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. That's why the place west of Kiriath-jearim is called the Camp of Dan to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> Then they left from there and went into the hill country of Ephraim and came to Micah's house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Then the five men who had gone to scout out the land of Laish told the other tribesmen, “Do you realize that here in these houses there's an ephod, household gods, and a carved idol, an image made with molten silver? So you know what you should do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> The five men left the road and went to where the young Levite was living in Micah's home to ask how he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> The six hundred Danite armed men stood at the entrance by the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> The five men went inside and took the carved idol, the ephod, the household idols, and the image made with molten silver. The priest was standing by the gate with the six hundred armed men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When the priest saw them taking all the religious objects<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The text repeats the items listed in verse 17.">c</a></sup>from Micah's home, he asked them, “What are you doing?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> “Be quiet! Don't say anything! Come with us, and you can be our ‘father’ and priest. Wouldn't it be better for you if instead of being a priest for just one man's household that you were the priest of an Israelite tribe and family?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> This seemed like a good idea to the priest and he left with them. Carrying the ephod, the household idols, and the image made with molten silver, he marched with the people all around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> They continued their journey, putting their children, livestock, and possessions ahead of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The Danites were already quite a way from Micah's home when men from Micah's village caught up with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> shouting at them. The Danites turned around to face them and asked Micah, “What's the matter with you? Why call out these men to come after us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; PSALMS 115:8"> “You stole the gods I made, and my priest too, and then left. What have you left me with? How can you ask me, ‘What's the matter with you?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> “Don't complain to us!” Danites replied. “Otherwise some hot-tempered people here might attack you, and you and your family will lose your lives!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The Danites carried on their way. Micah saw that they were too strong for him to fight so he turned around and went back home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> No one could save them because they were a long way from Sidon and had no other allies to help them. The town was in the valley belonging to Beth-rehob. The Danites rebuilt the city and lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> They renamed the city Dan after their forefather, the son of Israel. Laish was its former name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> The Danites erected the carved idol to worship, and Jonathan, son of Gershom, son of Moses, and his sons became priests for the tribe of Dan until the time when the people went into captivity from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> They worshiped the carved idol that Micah had made the whole time God's Temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Though no specific reference is made to a Temple being built at Shiloh, it is believed a more permanent structure was in place there, otherwise the location would have been referred to as the “Tent of Meeting.” The account in the beginning of 1 Samuelsupports this view.">d</a></sup>was at Shiloh.</span>
