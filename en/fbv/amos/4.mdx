<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 22:12; Amos 6:1; Amos 5:11"> Listen to this message, you well-fed cows of Bashan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It is thought that this refers to the high society women of Samaria who lived well. The reference to the cows of Bashan is that they were fattened cattle (see Ezekiel 39:18).">a</a></sup>who live on Mount Samaria, who oppress the poor and crush the needy, and who order your husbands,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word used for husbands is not the usual term, but one meaning “lord” or “master.” It is used here to show that the wives are reversing roles in that “masters” are acting as servants.">b</a></sup>“Bring us drinks!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; PSALMS 89:35; Habakkuk 1:15–16"> The Lord God has sworn by his holiness: Watch out! For the time is coming when they will drag you away with hooks, each one of you will be like a fish on a hook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:5; Ezekiel 12:12; Matthew 16:26"> You will leave through breaches in the city walls, thrown out in the direction of Mount Hermon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Hosea 4:15; Numbers 28:3–4"> Why don't you go to Bethel and sin? Go to Gilgal and add to your sins even more?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Clearly the tone is sarcastic.">c</a></sup>Offer your sacrifices in the morning, bring your tithes after three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; PSALMS 81:12; Hosea 9:10"> Burn a thank offering of bread made with yeast,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This was expressly forbidden: Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 7:12.">d</a></sup>and announce what you're giving as freewill offerings so everyone will know. This is what you love to do! declares the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 9:13"> It was I who made sure you had nothing to eat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “cleanness of teeth.”">e</a></sup>in all your cities, and a lack of food wherever you lived, but still you wouldn't return to me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Jeremiah 3:3; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> It was I who kept the rain from falling when there were still three months before harvest.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This was the critical time in order to ensure a good harvest. No rain at this time would mean the crops would fail.">f</a></sup>One city would have rain, while another would not. One field would have rain, another would dry up from lack of rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:6; 1 Kings 18:5; Haggai 1:6"> People wandered from town to town looking for water, yet were still thirsty. But still you did not return to me, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 2:17; Joel 1:4"> I struck your many farms and vineyards with blight and mildew; locusts devoured your fig trees and olive trees. But still you did not return to me, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:60; Leviticus 26:25; Joel 2:20"> I sent a plague on you like I did in Egypt. I killed your young men in battle; I took away your horses; I made you smell the stench of dead bodies in your camps. But still you did not return to me, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; Zechariah 3:2; Genesis 19:24–25"> Some of you I destroyed as I destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. You were like a burning stick pulled out of a fire. But still you did not return to me, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–4; James 4:1–10; Amos 9:1–4"> Therefore, this is what I am going to do to you, Israel. Because I am going to do this, Israel, prepare to meet your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Micah 1:3; Amos 9:6"> Watch! The One who made the mountains, who created the winds, who reveals his thoughts to humankind, who turns the sunlight into darkness, who walks on the high places of the earth—the Lord God of power is his name!</span>
