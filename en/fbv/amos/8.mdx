<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> This is what the Lord God showed me. I saw a basket of fruit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Probably figs.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremiah 24:1–3"> He asked me, “What do you see, Amos?” I said, “A basket of fruit.” Then the Lord told me, “This is the end of my people Israel! I won't ignore their sins anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremiah 9:21–22"> On that day the Temple songs will become sorrowful wailing. There will be dead bodies scattered everywhere. Silence!” declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; PSALMS 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Listen to this, you who trample down the needy, and wipe out the poor of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Micah 6:10–11; Hosea 12:7"> You who ask, “When will the holy day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “new moon”—one of Israel's religious festivals.">b</a></sup>be over so we can get back to selling grain? When will the Sabbath be over so we can open our storehouses, and cheat people with short measures and unfair scales?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> You buy the poor for silver, the needy for a pair of sandals, and you sell grain mixed with chaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13"> The Lord God, of whom the descendants of Jacob are so proud, has sworn an oath: I will never forget the evil you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 46:8"> Shall not the land tremble because of this, and everyone who lives there mourn? The earth will rise up like the Nile River in flood, be tossed around, and then fall again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremiah 15:9"> On that day, declares the Lord God, I will make the sun go down at noon, and darken the land during the daytime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Zechariah 12:10; Job 20:23"> I will turn your festivals into times of mourning, your happy songs into laments. I will make you wear sackcloth and shave your heads.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Signs of mourning for the dead.">c</a></sup>I will make your mourning like that for an only son. At the end of it all it will be a bitter day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:1; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> The time is coming, declares the Lord, when I will send a famine on the land—not a famine of bread, or a lack of water, but a famine of hearing the words of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> People will wander from sea to sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="From the Mediterranean to the Dead Sea.">d</a></sup>from north to east, running to and fro searching for the word of the Lord, but they will not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> On that day, even the beautiful girls and healthy young men will collapse from thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; 1 Kings 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> Those who swear by the disgraceful idols<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“disgraceful idols”: literally, “guilt.”">e</a></sup>of Samaria, who take oaths like, “By the life of your god, Dan,” or “A pilgrimage to the god of Beersheba”—they will fall, and never rise again.</span>
