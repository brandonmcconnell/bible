<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; James 5:5; Luke 12:17–20"> How disastrous it will be for you who have an easy life in Zion, and you who feel secure living on Mount Samaria—you who are the most famous men in all of Israel to whom people come for help!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nahum 3:8; Genesis 10:10; 2 Kings 18:34"> But go to Calneh, and see what took place there. Then go on to the great city of Hamath, and down to the Philistine city of Gath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Foreign cities destroyed by invaders.">a</a></sup>Were they better kingdoms than yours? Did they have more territory than you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:10; Isaiah 56:12; Ezekiel 12:27"> You put out of your minds any thought of coming disaster, yet you are bringing closer the time when violence reigns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Luke 16:19; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> How disastrous it will be for you people who lie on beds decorated with ivory and lounge on couches, eating lamb from your flocks and fattened calves from the stall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Amos 5:23; Amos 8:3"> You make up songs accompanied by the harp, thinking you're great composers like David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Matthew 26:7–9"> You drink wine by the bowlful, and anoint yourselves with the finest oils, but you do not grieve over the ruin of Joseph's descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 7:11; Daniel 5:4–6; Amos 5:5"> So you will be at the head of those led away into exile; the feasting and lazing around will be over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> The Lord God has sworn an oath on his own life. This is what the Lord God of power declares: I detest Jacob's arrogance and I hate his fortresses; I will hand over this city and all in it to the enemy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“To the enemy”: implied.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; PSALMS 109:13"> If there are ten people in a house, they will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And when a relative comes to take away the bodies from the house, he will ask someone inside, “Is there anyone else with you?” The person will reply, “No...” Then the other will say, “Quiet! Don't even mention the Lord's name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> Watch out! When the Lord gives the command, large houses will be reduced to rubble, and small houses smashed to pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> Can horses gallop over rocks? Can oxen plow the sea? But you have turned justice into poison, and the fruit of doing good into bitterness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> You joyfully celebrate your conquest of Lodebar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The name of this town means “nothing.”">c</a></sup>and say “Didn't we in our own strength capture Karnaim?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Karnaim means “strength.”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> Watch out, people of Israel! I am going to send an enemy nation to attack you, says the Lord God of power, and they will oppress you from the Pass of Hamath to the Arabah Valley.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Meaning from the north to the south. The Arabah Valley is the Jordan Valley.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
