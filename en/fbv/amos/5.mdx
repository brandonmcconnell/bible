<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 7:29"> Listen, people of Israel, to this funeral lament that I sing about you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremiah 2:27"> Virgin Israel has fallen, and will never rise again! She lies abandoned on the ground, and there is no one to help her up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Ezekiel 12:16; Isaiah 6:13"> This is what the Lord says: From a city that sends out a thousand soldiers, only a hundred will return; from a city that sends out a hundred soldiers, only ten will return.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Zephaniah 2:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> This is what the Lord says to the people of Israel: Look to me so you may live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> Do not look to the false gods of Bethel, do not go to the pagan shrines of Gilgal or travel to those of Beersheba. For Gilgal will go into exile, and Bethel will come to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Isaiah 55:6; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Look to the Lord so you may live! Otherwise he will explode like fire against the descendants of Joseph and no one from Bethel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Possibly meaning the false gods of Bethel will be able to do nothing.">a</a></sup>will be able to quench it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Zephaniah 1:6"> You twist justice and make it bitter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “wormwood,” a plant with a very bitter taste.">b</a></sup>you throw integrity to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> The One who made the Pleiades and Orion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Two constellations of stars.">c</a></sup>who turns the darkness into morning, and daytime into night, who calls for the water of the seas, and pours it down as rain upon the earth—the Lord is his name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Jeremiah 37:10; Micah 5:11"> In a flash he cuts down the strong and destroys the fortresses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:21; 1 Kings 22:8; John 8:45–47"> You hate anyone who confronts injustice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is the meaning of the Hebrew “in the gate”—the place where legal cases were heard.">d</a></sup>and loathe anyone who speaks honestly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Micah 6:15; Zephaniah 1:13"> Because you trample down the poor and impose a tax on their grain, building for yourselves impressive houses, you will not live in them, and you will not drink wine from the fine vineyards you have planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Isaiah 1:23"> For I know the extent of your wrongdoing and your numerous sins. You oppress good people by taking bribes, and you prevent the poor from getting justice in the courts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> So smart people keep quiet in such evil times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Micah 6:8"> Do what is right, and not evil, so you may live. Then the Lord God of power will be with you, as you claim he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; PSALMS 97:10"> Hate evil and love good—and make sure justice wins out in your courts. Maybe the Lord God of power will have mercy on those who are left of Jacob's people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Jeremiah 9:10"> For this is what the Lord, the Lord God of power says: There will be weeping in the city squares and wailing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “saying ‘Alas! Alas!’”">e</a></sup>in the streets. They will call even the farmers to grieve, as well as the professional mourners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremiah 48:33; Isaiah 16:10"> There will be wailing in every vineyard, for I will pass through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="In punishment. Vineyards, normally places of laughter and celebration, are filled with weeping and wailing.">f</a></sup>the midst of you, says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> How disastrous it will be for those of you who long for the day of the Lord. Why would you want the day of the Lord to come? It will bring darkness, not light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> It will be like a man running away from a lion only to meet a bear; or a man who goes home and leans his hand on a wall, only to be bitten by a snake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Zephaniah 1:15"> Isn't the day of the Lord darkness without light? Yes, pitch dark without a glimmer of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> I hate, I despise your festivals and take no delight in your religious assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Isaiah 66:3"> Even though you present me with burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. As for your peace offerings of fattened cattle—I will not even look at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Stop your noisy worship songs. I will not listen to the melody of your harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Hosea 6:6"> Rather let justice flow like a river, and doing right like an ever-flowing stream.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Ezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Did you bring me sacrifices during those forty years in the desert, people of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 23:12–13; 1 Kings 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> But now you carry idols of Sakkuth your king and Kaiwan your star god that you made for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; 2 Kings 17:6; Amos 4:13"> So I will send you into exile in a land beyond Damascus, says the Lord, whose name is the God of power.</span>
</div>
</div>
