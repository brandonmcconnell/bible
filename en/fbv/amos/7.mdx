<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Amos 8:1; Joel 2:25"> This is what the Lord God showed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In vision.">a</a></sup>me. I saw that he was preparing a swarm of locusts just when the spring crops began to grow. (Spring crops begin to grow right after the time when the king's hay is cut.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Assumed meaning; Hebrew is unclear.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> And so when the locusts finished eating every green plant in the fields, I pleaded with the Lord God, “Please forgive your people! How can the descendants of Jacob survive? They are so weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> So the Lord changed his mind. “It won't happen,” said the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> This is what the Lord God showed me. I saw that the Lord God was calling for a judgment of fire. The fire burned up the depths of the sea and destroyed the farmland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> I pleaded with the Lord God, “Please stop! How can the descendants of Jacob survive? They are so weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; PSALMS 90:13"> So the Lord changed his mind. “This too won't happen,” said the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> This is what he showed me. I saw the Lord was standing beside a wall that had been built with a plumb line.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This term only occurs here, so the meaning is not certain.">c</a></sup>He was holding a plumb line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> And the Lord asked me, “Amos, what do you see?” I replied, “A plumb line.” And the Lord God said, “I am placing a plumb line in the midst of my people Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="As a standard to measure his people's faithfulness.">d</a></sup>I won't ignore their sins anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> The high places<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Where pagan worship occurred.">e</a></sup>of the descendants of Isaac will be torn down, and the holy places of Israel will be destroyed. Sword in hand, I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Then Amaziah, priest of Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam, king of Israel, saying, “Amos is plotting against you among the people of Israel. What he's saying is unbearable!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; PSALMS 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> For he's saying Jeroboam will be killed by the sword, and the people will be taken away from their land into exile.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> Amaziah said to Amos, “Go away prophet! Run away to the land of Judah. Go and prophesy there to earn your bread.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Implying a selfish motive for Amos' prophesying—to earn a living.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> But don't ever prophesy again at Bethel, for this is where the king worships, the national Temple.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> But Amos replied, “I'm not a trained prophet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Meaning that Amos had not attended the schools of the prophets.">g</a></sup>or the son of a prophet. I was just a shepherd, and also took care of fig trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> The Lord took me from following my flock, and the Lord told me, ‘Go, and give my message to my people of Israel.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> So now hear what the Lord is telling you: You say, “Don't prophesy against Israel, and don't preach against the descendants of Isaac.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> But this is what the Lord says: Your wife will become a prostitute in the city; your sons and daughters will be killed by the sword. Your land will be measured and divided up, and you yourself will die in a foreign land. The people of Israel will definitely be taken from the land and go into exile.</span>
</div>
