<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 48:25"> I will cut down their king and all their princes with him, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:24; Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 20:13"> This is what the Lord says: The people of Judah have repeatedly sinned and so I will not hesitate to punish them, because they rejected the law of the Lord and have not kept his commands. Their lies have led them astray, the same lies that their forefathers followed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 8:14; Jeremiah 17:27; Jeremiah 21:10"> So I will send down fire on Judah, and I will burn up the fortresses of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Micah 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> This is what the Lord says: The people of Israel have repeatedly sinned and so I will not hesitate to punish them, because they sell good people for silver, and poor people for a pair of sandals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; Ezekiel 22:11"> They trample the heads of the poor into the dust, and push the needy out of the way. A man and his father have sex with the same servant girl so that my holy name is profaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> At every altar they stretch themselves out in clothes taken from debtors as collateral, in the temple of their god they drink wine taken from the people they fined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachi 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Yet it was I who was the one who destroyed the Amorites before you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Before you”: or “for your sake.”">b</a></sup>even though they were as tall as cedars and as strong as oaks. I destroyed them root and branch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> It was I who brought you out of the land of Egypt and led you through the desert for forty years so that you could take the Amorites' land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; 1 Kings 20:41"> I appointed some of your sons to be prophets, and some of your young men to be Nazirites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Nazirites: men who took special vows of service for God, and who were not allowed to drink wine or cut their hair.">c</a></sup>Isn't this true, people of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; Jeremiah 11:21"> But you made the Nazirites sin by drinking wine, and you told the prophets, “Don't tell us what God says.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Don't tell us what God says”: literally, “You shall not prophesy.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; Ezekiel 16:43"> Now see what I am going to do. I will crush you where you are, as if crushed by a heavy cart loaded down with sheaves of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; PSALMS 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> Not even your fastest runners will escape; strong men will lose their strength. Even the greatest warrior will not be able to save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 33:16–17; Ezekiel 39:3"> The archer will not stand his ground. The quickest on his feet will not be able to get away, and even those on horseback will not save themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremiah 48:41; 2 Kings 7:8–20"> On that day even the bravest of the mighty warriors will run away naked, says the Lord.</span>
