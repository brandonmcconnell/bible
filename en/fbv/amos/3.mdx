<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:10; Jeremiah 8:3; Micah 3:1"> People of Israel, listen to this message that the Lord has spoken against you—all of you, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 10:15"> I chose a special relationship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word often translated here as “know” is related to choice and intimacy, not mere knowledge.">a</a></sup>with you alone out of all the families of the earth, and so I will punish you for your wrongdoing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 5:22"> Can two people walk together unless they have arranged to meet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 11:10; PSALMS 104:21; Amos 1:2"> Does a lion roar in the forest before finding its prey? Does a young lion growl from its den if it has caught nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Jeremiah 31:28; Daniel 9:14"> Does a bird fall into a trap and get caught unless the spring is set? Does a trap spring shut if there's nothing there to catch?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Zephaniah 1:16; Isaiah 14:24–27"> When the trumpet sounds in the city, shouldn't the people be alarmed? When a disaster comes to a city, isn't it the Lord who does it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:15; Genesis 18:17; PSALMS 25:14"> For the Lord God does nothing without revealing his intentions to his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 20:9; Amos 3:4"> The lion has roared—so who isn't scared? The Lord God has spoken—so who can refuse to speak for him?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Speak for him”: or “prophesy.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 4:1; Amos 6:1; Amos 1:8"> Announce this to the fortresses of Ashdod and to the fortresses in the land of Egypt: Assemble yourselves on Samaria's mountains and see all the turmoil and oppression happening in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Zephaniah 1:9; Zechariah 5:3–4"> They don't know how to do what is right, declares the Lord. They have stored up in their fortresses what they've taken by violence and robbery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 18:9–11; 2 Kings 17:3–6; 2 Kings 15:19"> As a result, says the Lord, an enemy will surround you, break down your strongholds, and plunder your fortresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:34–37; Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> This is what the Lord says: Just as a shepherd tries to rescue a sheep from a lion's mouth, but only saves a couple of legs or a piece of an ear, so it will be for the people of Israel living in Samaria—all that will be “saved” will be the corner of a couch and a piece of a leg from a bed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The point being made here is not how little is saved, but nothing is saved except proof of total destruction. A shepherd took the remains of a sheep to the owner to prove how the animal died; otherwise he would have to pay for it himself. Similarly with Israel, it's destruction would be so complete that the only evidence left would only confirm this.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:18–19; 2 Chronicles 24:19; Acts 20:21"> Listen! Warn the house of Jacob, declares the Lord God of power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 23:15; Hosea 10:5–8; Hosea 10:14–15"> For on that day I will punish Israel for their sins. I will destroy the altars of Bethel: the corners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “horns.” If anyone held on to these horns on the corner of an altar they were given sanctuary. By cutting them down the Lord shows that nowhere can be seen as a place of safety.">d</a></sup>of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; Jeremiah 36:22; Amos 6:11"> I will tear down their winter houses and their summer houses as well, their houses filled with ivory will be ruined—their many houses will be destroyed.</span>
