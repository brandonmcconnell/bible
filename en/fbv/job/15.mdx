<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> “Would a wise man answer with such empty ‘knowledge’ that is just a lot of hot air?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Hot air”: literally, “fill his belly with the east,” the direction of a hot wind blowing in the desert.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> He wouldn't argue with unprofitable speeches using words that do no good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> But you are doing away with the fear of God, and destroying communion with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> It's your sins that are doing the talking, and you are choosing deceptive words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Your own mouth is condemning you, not me; your own lips are testifying against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Were you the very first person to be born? Were you born before the hills were created?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Note the parallel to Proverbs 8:25.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Were you there listening in God's council?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referring to the council in heaven, 1:6; 2:1.">c</a></sup>Does wisdom only belong to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What do you know that we don't? What do you understand that we don't?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> We have among us old, gray-haired people much older than your father!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Are the comforts God provides too little for you? Are God's gentle words not enough for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why do you let yourself be carried away by your emotions?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Why does your heart take you?”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> Why do your eyes flash in anger that you turn against God and let yourself speak this way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> Who can say they are clean? Which human being can say that they do what is right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Look, God doesn't even trust his angels—even the heavenly beings are not pure in his sight!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> How much less pure are those who are unclean and corrupt, drinking in sin like water!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> If you are ready to listen to me, I will show you. I will explain my insights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; PSALMS 71:18"> This is what wise men have said, confirmed by their ancestors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> those to whom alone the land was given before foreigners ever were there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Implying that the influence of foreigners might have impacted traditional wisdom.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> The wicked writhe in pain all their lives, through all the years these oppressors survive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrifying sounds fill their ears; even when they think they're safe, the destroyer will attack them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> They don't believe they will escape the darkness—they know a sword is waiting for them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Meaning that they expect to be murdered.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 109:10; PSALMS 59:15; Job 18:12"> They wander around looking for food, asking ‘Where is it?’ They know that their day of darkness is close at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Misery and torment overwhelm them like a king preparing for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; PSALMS 52:7; PSALMS 73:9"> They shake their fists in God's face, defiantly challenging the Almighty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> insolently attacking him with their shields.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; PSALMS 73:7"> They have become fat in their rebellion, their bellies bloated with fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> But their cities will become desolate; they will live in abandoned houses that are crumbling into ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; PSALMS 49:16–17; James 1:11"> They will lose their riches, their wealth will not endure, their possessions will not spread over the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> They will not escape from the darkness. Like a tree whose shoots are burned up in a forest fire, the breath of God will blow him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; PSALMS 62:10"> They should not trust in things that are worthless, for their reward will be worthless.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Worthless,” or “vanity.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; PSALMS 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> This will be paid in full before their time has come. They are like tree branches that wither,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> like vines that lose their unripe grapes, or olive trees that lose their flowers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In other words fruit trees that fail to produce a good harvest.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For those who reject God are barren,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “unproductive.”">i</a></sup>and fire will burn up the homes of those who love bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> They plan trouble and produce evil, giving birth to deception.”</span>
</div>
