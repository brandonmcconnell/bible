<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Hosea 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> Surely anger slays the fool and jealousy kills the simple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 73:18–20; PSALMS 37:35–36; PSALMS 92:7"> I have seen a fool growing strong,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “taking root.”">a</a></sup>but I immediately cursed his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 127:5; Job 18:16–19; PSALMS 119:155"> His sons are never safe;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eliphaz may even have Job in mind here since Job has lost all his children.">b</a></sup>they are crushed in court<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “in the gate”—the town gate where justice was dispensed.">c</a></sup>with no one to defend them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> The hungry eat everything he has harvested, taking even that protected by a thorn hedge,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The meaning of the Hebrew is unclear.">d</a></sup>while others look to steal his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For evil doesn't come from the dust; neither does trouble grow from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> But human beings are born for trouble just as certainly as sparks from a fire fly upwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; Job 22:21; PSALMS 37:5"> If it were me, I would go to God and put my case before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 40:5; Job 9:10; PSALMS 86:10"> He is the one who does amazing, incredible things; miracles that can't be counted!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; PSALMS 147:8"> He gives rain to the earth and sends water to the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> He exalts the humble, and rescues those who mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 33:10–11; PSALMS 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> He frustrates the plans of the cunning so that they are unsuccessful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; PSALMS 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> He traps the wise in their own clever thinking, and the schemes of twisted people are cut short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> In the daytime they're in the dark, and they stumble around at noon like it's night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 35:10; PSALMS 140:12; PSALMS 109:31"> But God is the one who saves from their cutting remarks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “the sword of their mouth.”">e</a></sup>and the poor from the actions of the powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; PSALMS 63:11"> As a result those who are helpless have hope, and the wicked have to shut their mouths!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; PSALMS 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> See how happy is the person God corrects—so don't despise the Almighty's discipline.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For he causes pain but he provides relief; he wounds but his hands heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> He will save you from many disasters; a multitude of evils will not affect you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The numbers 6 and 7 are used, but are an example of number parallelism which simply indicate an increasing amount.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 33:19; Hosea 13:14; PSALMS 37:19"> In times of famine he will rescue you from death, and in times of war he will save you from the power of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 31:20; PSALMS 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> You will be protected from sharp-tongued slander; and when violence comes you will not be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; PSALMS 91:13"> You will laugh at violence and famine; you won't be afraid of wild animals—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> for you will be at peace with the stones of the field<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The meaning of the Hebrew is unclear. Some suggest that stones in a field cause difficulties for farming and that this will not be a problem.">g</a></sup>and the wild animals will be at peace with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 121:7–8; PSALMS 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> You will be certain that your home is safe, for you will go to where you live and find nothing missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 112:2; PSALMS 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> You will also be sure that you will have many children; your descendants will be like the grass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In other words as numerous as countless blades of grass.">h</a></sup>of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; PSALMS 91:16"> You will live to a ripe old age like a sheaf of grain when it is harvested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Look, we've examined it, and it's true! Listen to what I'm saying and apply it to yourself!”</span>
