<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> Then Job responded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> “If my grief could be weighed and my troubles placed on the scales</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; PSALMS 40:5"> they would be heavier than the sand of the sea. That's why I spoke so rashly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 38:2; Job 30:15; PSALMS 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Almighty are in me; their poison saps my spirit. God's terrors are lined<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="As in battle lines.">a</a></sup>up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; PSALMS 42:1"> Don't wild donkeys bray when their grass is gone? Don't cattle groan when they don't have food!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, “I have a right to complain!”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Can something that's tasteless be eaten without salt? Is there any taste in the white of an egg?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> I just can't touch any food—even the thought makes me feel sick!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Oh, if only I could have what I really want, that God would give me what I most desire—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> that God would be willing to crush me to death, that he would just let me die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> But it still comforts me to know, making me happy through the never-ending pain, that I have never rejected the words of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “Holy One.”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 103:14–16; PSALMS 102:23; PSALMS 39:5"> Why should I go on waiting when I don't have the strength? Why should I keep going when I don't know what is going to happen to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Am I as strong as rock? Am I made out of bronze?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> How can I help myself now that any chance of success is ripped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “driven.”">d</a></sup>away from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> Anyone who isn't kind to a friend has given up respecting the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; PSALMS 41:9"> My brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to Job's friends.">e</a></sup>have acted as deceptively as a desert stream, rushing waters in the desert that vanish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The stream floods when it is full of dark ice and melting snow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> but in the heat it dries up and disappears, vanishing from where it once was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Camel caravans turn aside to look for water, but don't find any and they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> Caravans from Tema looked, travelers from Sheba were confident,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> but their hopes were dashed—they came and found nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> Now you are no help, just like that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Job is saying that Eliphaz is a helpful as a dried up stream that provides no water.">f</a></sup>—you see my trouble and you're afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Have I asked you for anything? Have I told you to bribe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “pay a ransom.”">g</a></sup>anyone for me from your wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 49:15; PSALMS 49:7–8; PSALMS 107:2"> Have I asked you to rescue me from an enemy? Have I told you to save me from my oppressors?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; PSALMS 39:1–2"> Explain this to me, and I'll be quiet. Show me where I'm wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> Honest words are painful,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “forceful.”">h</a></sup>but what do your arguments prove?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Are you going to argue over what I said, when the words of someone in despair should be left to blow away in the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> You would play dice to win an orphan; you would bargain away your friend!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Look me in the eye and see if I'm lying to your face!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Don't talk like this!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “turn.” Job is asking Eliphaz to turn away from this line of argument.">i</a></sup>Don't be unjust! What I'm saying is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> I'm not telling lies—don't you think I wouldn't know if I was wrong?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, “Can't my palate discern calamity?”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
