<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then Zophar the Naamathite began speaking and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> “So many words should be answered, shouldn't they? Or is a person proved right by doing a lot of talking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Should all your babbling force people to keep quiet? When you mock, shouldn't someone make you feel ashamed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> You said, ‘What I teach is the pure truth,’ and ‘in God's eyes I am clean.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> If only God would speak up and tell you directly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> because then he would explain to you the secrets of wisdom. True wisdom has many sides to it. God's treatment of you is far better than your guilt deserves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; PSALMS 145:3"> Can you find out the mysteries of God? Can you discover all the wisdom of the Almighty?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Can you find out the limit of the Almighty?”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; PSALMS 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> It is greater than the heavens—what can you do? It is deeper than Sheol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Sheol: the underworld, the place of the dead.">b</a></sup>—what can you know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; PSALMS 65:5–8"> It extends farther than the earth, and wider than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If God comes and imprisons someone, or assembles the court for judgment, who can stop him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For God knows who are deceivers—when he sees someone sin, he pays attention to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> Stupid people will become wise when wild donkeys are born tame!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Wild donkeys, like horses, are not naturally born tame, so the statement is saying that stupid people becoming wise is very unlikely.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 143:6; PSALMS 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> But if you repent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “set right your heart.”">d</a></sup>and spread out your hands to pray to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> if you get rid of your sins and don't let wickedness continue in your life,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “do not let unrighteousness reside in your tents.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> then you would be able to hold your head high in innocence, you will be firm and unafraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> You will forget your misery, remembering it only as water under the bridge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; PSALMS 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> Your life will shine brighter than the noonday sun; even darkness will be like the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; PSALMS 43:5; PSALMS 3:5"> You will have confidence because there is hope; you will be protected and rest in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> You will lie down to sleep with no one to frighten you, and many will come asking for your favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the wicked will fail, they will not be able to escape, and their only hope is in death.”</span>
</div>
