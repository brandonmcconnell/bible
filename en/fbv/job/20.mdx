<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; PSALMS 31:22; Job 20:3"> “I'm forced to reply because I'm really upset!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; PSALMS 78:2–5"> What I hear you say insults me, but I know how to reply to you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> Don't you know that since ancient times, since human beings were put on this earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> that the triumph of the wicked doesn't last long, that those who reject God are only happy for a short time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> Even though they are so tall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “their pride.”">a</a></sup>they reach the heavens, even though their heads reach the clouds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> they will vanish forever like their own excrement. People who knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “have seen him.”">b</a></sup>them will ask, ‘Where have they gone?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 90:5; PSALMS 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> They will vanish like a dream, never to be found, fleeing like a vision of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> Those who once saw them will not see them anymore; their families will never set eyes on them again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> Their children will have to pay back the poor; they will have to return their wealth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Implying that the wicked parents had improperly taken money from the poor.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; PSALMS 25:7; Job 21:26"> Though the wicked have bodies that are young and strong, they will die and be buried.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “it will lie down with him in the dust.”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; PSALMS 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> Though evil tastes sweet in their mouths and they hide it under their tongues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> not letting it go but keeping it in their mouths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; PSALMS 51:8–9; PSALMS 32:3–4"> in their stomachs it turns bitter, becoming like snake venom inside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> They swallow wealth and vomit it up again; God forces it from their stomachs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> They suck in snake venom; the bite of the viper will kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> They will not live to enjoy the streams, the rivers of milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> They will have to give back what they have gained and will not have any benefit;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “will not swallow it down.”">e</a></sup>they will not enjoy any of their profits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> For they have oppressed and abandoned the poor; they have seized houses they did not build.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> Because their greed was never satisfied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “knew no quietness in the belly.”">f</a></sup>nothing they liked is left that they didn't consume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> Nothing escapes their ravenous appetites, so their happiness doesn't last long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> Even when the wicked have all that they want they run into trouble; all kinds of misery will fall upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; PSALMS 11:6"> While they are busy filling their bellies, God's hostility will burn against them, raining down on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> As they flee to escape an iron weapon, a bronze arrow will strike them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> The arrow is pulled out of their gall bladder, glistening with blood. They are absolutely terrified!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 21:9; PSALMS 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> All they value will disappear into the darkness; divine fire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “a fire that is not fanned”—in other words a fire not started by human hand.">g</a></sup>will destroy them; all they have left will go up in smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> The heavens reveal what they have done wrong; the earth will rise up against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> All their possessions will be carried from their homes; they will be dragged off on the day of God's judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; PSALMS 11:5–6"> This is the share the wicked receive from God, the inheritance God says they should have.”</span>
</div>
</div>
