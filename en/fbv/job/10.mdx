<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> You granted me life and showed me your kindness; you have taken great care of me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; PSALMS 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> to watch me, and if I sinned, then you would not forgive my wrongs.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> If I do hold my head high you hunt me down like a lion, showing how powerful you are in hurting me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> You repeat your arguments against me, you pour out more and more of your anger against me, you send fresh armies against me.</span>
