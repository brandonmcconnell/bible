<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; PSALMS 35:15–16; PSALMS 69:12"> But now people much younger than me laugh at me; people whose fathers I would not put to work with my sheepdogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> They are too weak to be any use to me; they're all worn-out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> Thin through hunger and want, they try to eat the dry ground in the dark, desolate wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> There they pick desert herbs and the leaves of bushes, and eat the roots of broom trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; PSALMS 109:10"> They were driven out of the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> People shouted after them as if they were thieves. They have to live in dangerous ravines, in caves and among the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They shout out like animals among the bushes; they huddle together in the weeds for shelter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> They are foolish, nameless people that have been driven from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> Yet now they mock me in their songs; I have become a joke to them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They despise and shun me; they don't hesitate to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> God has made my bowstring loose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, God has rendered Job powerless.">a</a></sup>and humbled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> The rabble rise up against me, they send me running;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew is unclear.">b</a></sup>like a city under siege they devise ways to destroy me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The phrase alludes to the building of ramps to attack a city under siege.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; PSALMS 69:26"> They cut off my way of escape; they bring about my downfall and do this without anyone's help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; PSALMS 18:4; PSALMS 69:14–15"> They come in through a wide breach; they rush in as the wall comes tumbling down.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The illustration of a besieged city continues with the image of invaders entering through a city wall that has been brought down.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors overcome me; my honor is blown away by the wind; my salvation vanishes like a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; PSALMS 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my life is ebbing away; every day despair<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “days of affliction.”">e</a></sup>grips me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> At night my bones are in agony; the pain gnaws at me and never stops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> God grabs me roughly by my clothes; he pulls me by the collar of my shirt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He has thrown me in the mud; he has humbled me like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; PSALMS 80:4–5; PSALMS 22:2"> God, I cry to you but you don't answer; I stand before you, but you don't even notice me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> You have turned cruel to me; you use your power to make me suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; PSALMS 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> You pick me up and blow me along in the wind; tossing me about in the whirlwind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> I know you're taking me to my death, to the place where all the living go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Who would want to kick a man when he is down,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="More literally, “Surely no one who raise their hand against the needy.” Or “Yet doesn't someone in a heap of ruins reach out their hand?” The Hebrew is unclear.">f</a></sup>when they cry for help in their time of trouble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; PSALMS 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Didn't I weep for those having hard times? Didn't I grieve at what the poor suffered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> But when I looked for good, only evil came, and when I waited for the light, all that came was darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> Inside I am in turmoil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “My intestines are boiling.”">g</a></sup>it never stops; I face days of despair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 42:9; PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 43:2"> I am so depressed; seeing the sun doesn't help.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “I am blackened, but not by the sun.” This may alternatively refer to some skin disease.">h</a></sup>I stand up in the assembly and cry for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; PSALMS 102:6; PSALMS 44:19"> I am like a brother to the jackals, a companion to owls.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or ostriches. Some believe this expands on the cries mentioned in the preceding verse—like the mournful calls of animals and birds.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; PSALMS 102:3; PSALMS 119:83"> My skin turns black on me; and my bones burn within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> My lyre only plays sad songs, and my pipe is the voice of those who weep.</span>
