<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Job replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> “Please listen carefully to what I say—that would be one comfort you could give me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In 15:11Eliphaz asks if God's comforts are too little for Job. Job here turns it around and asks for some comfort from his friends.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Bear with me; let me speak. After I've spoken you can resume mocking me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 102:1; PSALMS 42:11; PSALMS 142:2–3"> Am I complaining against people? Of course not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup>Why shouldn't I be impatient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Just take a look at me. Aren't you appalled? Cover your mouth with your hand in shock!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; PSALMS 119:120; PSALMS 88:15"> Every time I think of what's happened to me I am horrified and I shake all over with fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Why do the wicked continue to live, to grow old and increasingly powerful?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zophar has just said that the triumph of wicked doesn't last long (20:5).">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their children are with them; they watch their grandchildren grow up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; PSALMS 73:5; PSALMS 73:19"> They live in their homes in safety—they are not afraid. God does not use his rod to beat them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In 9:34Job complains about being beaten by God's rod.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their bulls always breed successfully; their cows give birth to calves and do not miscarry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 107:41; PSALMS 127:3–5"> They send out their little ones like lambs to play; their children dance around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They sing accompanied by the tambourine and lyre; they celebrate with the music of the flute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; PSALMS 73:4"> They live out their lives contentedly, and go down to Sheol in peace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Sheol: the place of the dead.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> Yet they tell God, ‘Get lost! We don't want anything to do with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> Who does the Almighty think he is for us to serve him as slaves? What benefit is there for us if we pray to him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Such people believe they make their own fortune, but I don't accept their way of thinking.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “the plans of the wicked are repugnant to me.”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How often is the lamp of the wicked snuffed out?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="In 18:5Bildad stated that the lamp of the wicked is snuffed out.">g</a></sup>How often does disaster come upon them?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In 18:12Bildad mentioned that the wicked suffer disaster.">h</a></sup>How often does God punish the wicked in his anger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 1:4; PSALMS 83:13; Job 13:25"> Are they blown along like straw in the wind? Does a tornado come in and carry them away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> Some say, ‘God saves up people's punishment for their children.’ But I say, ‘God should punish those people themselves so that they can learn from it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; PSALMS 75:8"> Let them see their destruction themselves, and drink deeply from God's anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; PSALMS 55:23"> For they don't care what happens to their families once they're dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Can anyone teach God anything he doesn't already know, since he is the one who judges even heavenly beings?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “the exalted ones.”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; PSALMS 73:4–5"> One person dies in good health, totally comfortable and secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; PSALMS 17:10; Job 15:27"> Their body is fat from eating well; their bones still strong.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, “the marrow of their bones is moist.”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> Another dies after a miserable life without ever experiencing happiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> Yet they are both buried in the same dust; they are treated alike in death, eaten by maggots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; PSALMS 119:86; PSALMS 59:4"> Look, I know what you're thinking, and your schemes to do me wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> You may ask me, ‘Where is the home of the great man? Where is the place where the wicked live?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 129:8"> Haven't you asked people who travel? Don't you pay attention to what they tell you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> Wicked people are spared in times of disaster; they are rescued from the day of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who confronts them with their actions? Who pays them back for what they have done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; PSALMS 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> When they eventually die and are carried to the graveyard, their tomb is guarded. The earth of the grave softly covers them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> Everyone attends their funeral service; a huge procession of people comes to pay their last respects.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally, “those who go before him are innumerable.”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> Why do you try to comfort me with worthless nonsense?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally, “vapor.”">l</a></sup>Your answers are just a pack of lies!”</span>
</div>
</div>
