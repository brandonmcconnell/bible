<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> Then Bildad the Shuhite spoke up and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> “How long will you go on talking, hunting for the right words to say?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In the Hebrew the sense is that Job is hunting for words, trying to trap them in a snare.">a</a></sup>Talk sense if you want us to reply!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Do you think we're dumb animals?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In 12:7Job had told his friends to ask the animals.">b</a></sup>Do we look stupid to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> You tear yourself apart with your anger.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In 16:9Job said that God had torn him apart in his anger. Bildad says it's all self-inflicted.">c</a></sup>Do you think the earth has to be abandoned, or the mountains moved, just because of you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In other words, “Do you expect the world to be changed just to suit you?”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> It's certain that the life of the wicked will end like a lamp that is snuffed out—their flame will shine no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; PSALMS 18:28"> The light in their home goes out, the lamp hanging above is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; PSALMS 33:10; PSALMS 18:36"> Instead of taking strong strides they stumble, and their own plans cause them to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 9:15; Job 22:10; PSALMS 35:8"> Their own feet trip them up and they are caught in a net; as they walk along they fall into a pit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The pit is a trap set by laying branches over a deep hole.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> A trap grabs them by the heel; a snare tightens around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; PSALMS 11:6"> A noose is hidden on the ground for them; a rope is stretched across the path to trip them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrors scare the wicked, coming at them from every side, chasing them, biting at their heels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; PSALMS 34:10"> Hunger robs them of strength; disaster waits for them when they fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> Disease devours their skin; deadly disease<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “the first-born of death.”">f</a></sup>consumes their limbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; PSALMS 55:4; Job 11:20"> They are torn from the homes they trusted in and taken to the king of terrors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This term occurs nowhere else in the Bible. In the context it is probably a synonym for death.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> People they don't know will live in their homes; sulfur will be scattered where they used to live.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Sulfur was used as a disinfectant, especially after a death.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> They wither away, roots below and branches above;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; PSALMS 109:13"> the memory of them fades from the earth; nobody remembers their names any more.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “he has no name in the street.”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> They are thrown out of light into darkness, driven from the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> They have no children or descendants among their people, and no survivors where they used to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> People of the west are appalled at what happens to them. People of the east are shocked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> This is what happens to the homes of the wicked, to the places of those who reject God.”</span>
