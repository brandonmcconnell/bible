<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Job replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> “How long will you go on tormenting me? How long will you go on crushing me with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> Ten times already you have humiliated me. Aren't you ashamed for treating me so badly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> Even if I did sin, that's my problem, and has nothing to do with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 35:26; PSALMS 38:16; PSALMS 55:12"> You think you're so much better than me, and you use my degradation against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> But you should realize that it's God who has wronged me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In 8:3Bildad asked “Does God pervert justice?” Job uses the same verb for “perverts” here to say God has done him wrong.">a</a></sup>he has trapped me in his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Even though I cry for help, I get no answer; even though I shout my objections, I get no justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> God has walled me in so I can't escape; he has plunged my path into darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 89:44; Job 29:7–14; PSALMS 89:39"> He has stripped my honor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “wealth.”">b</a></sup>from me; he has taken away my reputation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “removed the crown from my head.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; PSALMS 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He tears me down from all sides until I am finished; he has destroyed my hope like a tree that is uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> His anger burns against me; he treats me as one of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> God's troops assemble to attack me. They build ramparts against me. They encircle and besiege my home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 88:8; PSALMS 88:18; PSALMS 69:8"> He has driven my brothers far away from me; all my former friends are estranged from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My relatives have abandoned me; my close friends have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; PSALMS 123:3"> My house guests and my maidservants treat me as a stranger—to them I have become a foreigner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I call my servant, but he doesn't reply. I have to beg him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “My breath.”">d</a></sup>am repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to my own brothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“My own brothers”: literally, “the sons of my own mother.” This also could be interpreted to mean “my own children.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Even young children despise me; when I stand up they ridicule me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; PSALMS 109:4–5; PSALMS 55:12–14"> All my closest friends despise me, and those I loved have turned on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> I've been reduced to skin and bones and I survive by the skin of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; PSALMS 38:2; Job 2:5"> Have pity on me, my friends, have pity on me, because God has struck me down!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why are you persecuting me like God does? Aren't you satisfied with getting your pound of flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> I wish my words could be written down, recorded in a book,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> or engraved with an iron pen and molten lead in the rock forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; PSALMS 78:35"> I know my Redeemer is alive, and that he shall finally take the stand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The concept here is to take the stand as a witness.">f</a></sup>for me on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; PSALMS 17:15"> Even though my skin is destroyed, in my body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “flesh.”">g</a></sup>I shall see God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> I myself will see him—with my own eyes, and not those of someone else! The thought overcomes me!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “my kidneys are exhausted in my chest”—the kidneys being seen as the source of emotions in the body.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; PSALMS 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> You say to yourselves, ‘How can we make him suffer so he can see he is the source of his problems?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; PSALMS 58:10–11"> You yourselves should fear being punished by God, for you know anger brings God's punishment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Punished/punishment” literally, “sword.”">i</a></sup>that accompanies judgment.”</span>
</div>
