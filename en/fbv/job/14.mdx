<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> “Life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “man that is born of woman,” so this is referring to human life generally.">a</a></sup>is short and full of trouble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> like a flower that blooms and withers, like a passing shadow that soon disappears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 144:3; PSALMS 143:2; PSALMS 8:4"> Do you even notice me, God, and why do you have to drag me to court?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can bring something clean of what is unclean? No one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> You have determined how long we shall live—the number of months, a time limit on our lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; PSALMS 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> So leave us alone and give us some peace—so like a laborer we could enjoy a few hours of rest at the end of the day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> Even a tree that's cut down has the hope of sprouting again, of sending up shoots and continuing to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> Even though its roots grow old in the earth, and its stump dies in the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> just a trickle of water<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “scent of water.”">b</a></sup>will make it bud and grow branches like a young plant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But human beings die, their strength dwindles away; they perish, and where are they then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> Like water evaporating from a lake and a river that dries up and disappears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> so human beings lie down and don't get up again. Until the heavens cease to exist they will not awake from their sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> I wish you would hide me in Sheol;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Sheol: the place of the dead.">c</a></sup>conceal me there until your anger is gone. Set a definite time for me there, and remember me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> Will the dead live again? Then I would have hope through all my time of trouble until my release comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; PSALMS 138:8; Job 10:8"> You would call and I would answer you; you would long for me, the being that you made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; PSALMS 139:1–4"> Then you would look after me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “count my steps.”">d</a></sup>and wouldn't be watching me to see if I sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My sins would be sealed up in a bag and you would cover my guilt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> But just as the mountains crumble and fall, and the rocks tumble down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> as water wears away the stones, as floods wash away the soil, so you destroy the hope people have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> You continually overpower them and they pass away; you distort their faces in death and send them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> Their children may become important or fall from their positions, but they don't know or see any of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> As people die they only know their own pain and are sad for themselves.”</span>
</div>
