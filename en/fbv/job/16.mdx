<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Job replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; PSALMS 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> “I've heard all this before. You are comforters who just cause trouble!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Job uses the same word “trouble” to describe his counselors as Eliphaz has just done in concluding his speech in 15:35. It's as if Job is saying to his friends, “You are these evil people who cause trouble!”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Will your windy speeches never cease?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Job is referring back to the beginning of Eliphaz's speech where he accuses Job of the same thing.">b</a></sup>What's bothering you that you have to answer me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 109:25; PSALMS 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I could talk like you do if you were in my place, stringing words together to criticize you, ridiculing you with a toss of my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> I could build you up by my mouth speaking words; the movement of my lips would ease your pain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Whether Job is being sincere or sarcastic is debated.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; PSALMS 88:15–18; PSALMS 77:1–9"> For me, if I speak it doesn't ease my pain, and if I don't speak the pain is still there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; PSALMS 6:6–7"> God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“God” or “he” or “it”—the Hebrew is unclear.">d</a></sup>you have worn me out. You have destroyed my whole family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> You have made me shrivel up, which is a witness against me; my thin body testifies against me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some understand this as Job saying his “shriveling up” and “thinness” are proof by God that he is a sinner.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> He has torn me apart in his anger; in his hostility he has gnashed his teeth at me; my enemy pierces me with his fierce look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 35:15; PSALMS 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> People stare at me with their mouths wide open, they slap me on my cheeks to mock me, they crowd around me to attack me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:12; PSALMS 7:14; PSALMS 31:8"> God has handed me over to evil people; he has thrown me into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I was living in peace and he smashed me to pieces. He grabbed me by the neck and dashed me to pieces. He set me up as his target.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His archers surround me. His arrows pierce my kidneys without mercy. He pours out my gall on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> Like a wall he breaks me down, breach upon breach, rushing at me like a warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; PSALMS 7:5; PSALMS 75:5"> I have sown sackcloth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Putting on sackcloth was a sign of mourning.">f</a></sup>to cover my skin; my strength lies broken in the dust.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “I have thrust my horn in the dust.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; PSALMS 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is red from crying and dark shadows surround my eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; PSALMS 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> although I have done nothing wrong<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “there is no violence in my hands.”">h</a></sup>and my prayer is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> Earth, do not cover up my blood. May my cry find no place to hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Look, right now my witness is in heaven; the one who speaks for me is on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; PSALMS 142:2; Job 16:4"> My friends scorn me, but my eyes pour out tears to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> I want my witness to speak for me with God as someone does for their friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> For in a few short years I shall go down that road from which I shall not return.”</span>
