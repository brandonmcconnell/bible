<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Jeremiah 5:22; Exodus 19:16"> “At this my heart trembles, beating rapidly within me!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Beating rapidly”: literally, “leaping from its place.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 37:5; Job 36:33; PSALMS 104:7"> Listen carefully to God's thunderous voice that rumbles as he speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He sends it across the heaven; his lightning flashes to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 29:3–9; PSALMS 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> Then comes the roar of thunder, his majestic voice holding nothing back when he speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God's thunderous voice is wonderful! We can't comprehend the great things he does!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> He tells the snow to fall and the rain to pour down on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; PSALMS 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> By this he stops people working so that everyone can understand what he does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 104:22; Job 38:40"> Even the animals take shelter and remain in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; PSALMS 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> The south wind blows in storms, while the north wind blows in cold weather.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “From its chamber comes the whirlwind; and cold from the scattering wind.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; PSALMS 147:16–18; PSALMS 78:47"> God's breath produces ice, freezing the surface of water solid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> He fills the clouds with moisture; he scatters his lightning from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> They swirl around under his control; they move over all the earth as he commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> He does this to accomplish his will, either to discipline or to show his goodness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew is unclear.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; PSALMS 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Listen to this, Job! Stop for a moment and consider the wonderful things God does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Do you know how God controls the clouds, or makes his lightning flash from them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Do you know how clouds float<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “balance.”">d</a></sup>in the sky—the wonderful work of him who knows everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> You know how your clothes drip with sweat when the south wind brings air that is hot and heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; PSALMS 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Can you hammer out the sky so it becomes like a molten mirror, as he does?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; PSALMS 73:22; Job 42:3"> So why don't you teach us what to tell God? We cannot make our case because we are in the dark!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Once again Elihu is being ironic.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; PSALMS 139:4; Job 6:3"> Should God be told that I want to speak? Anyone who wanted to would be destroyed!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Destroyed”: or “swallowed up.” Elihu is saying he fears that if he spoke directly to God he would be killed.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> After all, we can't look at the sun when it blazes brightly in the sky, after the wind has cleared away the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> Out of the north God comes shining like gold, surrounded in awesome majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; PSALMS 99:4"> We cannot approach the Almighty for he is far beyond us in power and justice, and doing right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; PSALMS 130:4"> He does not act like a tyrant—no wonder people are in awe of him, though he does not value those who think they're wise.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="It's interesting that Elihu who thinks he is so wise should finish his speech with such a comment!">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
