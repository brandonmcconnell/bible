<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> Job went on speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> “I wish I was back in the old days when God looked after me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> His light shone above me and lit my way through the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; PSALMS 27:5"> When I was young and strong, God was my friend and spoke to me in my home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 128:3; PSALMS 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> The Almighty was still with me and I was surrounded with my children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> My herds produced much milk,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “My steps were washed with butter.”">a</a></sup>and oil flowed freely from my olive presses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> I went out to the city gate and took my seat in the public square.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This was where the city elders met to discuss matters and to judge disputes.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me and moved out of the way; the elders would stand up for me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Thus showing respect.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> The leaders remained silent and covered their mouths with their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The voices of the officials were hushed; they held their tongues in my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> Everyone who listened to me praised me; whoever saw me commended me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> because I gave to the poor who called out to me and the orphans who had no one to help them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> Those who were about to die blessed me; I made the widow sing for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; PSALMS 132:9"> Being true and acting right<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “righteousness and justice,” but these terms are dated and sometimes misunderstood. Righteousness for example is the nature of a person rather than some outward show, while justice is not so much concerned with legal aspects but doing what is true and right.">d</a></sup>were what I wore for clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was like eyes for the blind and feet for the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; PSALMS 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was like a father to the poor, and I defended the rights of strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> I broke the jaw of the wicked and made them drop their prey from their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> I thought I would die at home, after many years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “I shall multiply my days like sand.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> Like a tree my roots spread out to the water; the dew rests on my branches overnight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; PSALMS 18:34"> Fresh honors were always being given to me; my strength was renewed like an unfailing bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> People listened carefully to what I had to say; they kept quiet as they listened to my advice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Songs 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> Once I had spoken they had nothing else to say; what I said was enough.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “my words dropped on them.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; PSALMS 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> They waited for me like people waiting for rain; their mouths wide open for the spring rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 89:15; PSALMS 4:6; PSALMS 126:1"> When I smiled at them they could hardly believe it; my approval meant all the world to them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “the light of my countenance they did not cast down.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I decided the way forward as their leader, living like a king among his soldiers, and when they were sad I comforted them.”</span>
