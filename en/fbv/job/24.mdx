<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:17; 2 Peter 3:7–8"> “Why doesn't the Almighty set a definite time to punish the wicked?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Wicked” implied.">a</a></sup>Why do those who follow him never see him act in judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 23:10"> The wicked move boundary stones;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Thus stealing land.">b</a></sup>they seize other people's flocks and move them to their own pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; 1 Samuel 12:3"> They steal the orphan's donkey; they take the widow's ox as security for a debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Ezekiel 22:29"> They push the poor out of their way; the destitute are forced to hide from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Job 39:5–7; PSALMS 104:23"> Like wild donkeys in the desert, the poor have to scavenge for their food, looking for anything to feed their children in the wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> They are forced to find what they can in other people's fields, to glean among the vineyards of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> They spend the night naked because they have no clothes; they have nothing to cover themselves against the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Songs 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> They are soaked by the cold mountain storms, and huddle beside the rocks for shelter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; 2 Kings 4:1"> Fatherless children are snatched from their mother's breasts, taking the babies of the poor as security for a debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> Because they have no clothes to wear they have to go naked, harvesting sheaves of grain while they themselves are hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> In the olive groves they work to produce oil, but do not taste it; they tread the winepress, but are thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachi 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> In the city the dying groan, and the wounded cry for help, but God ignores their prayers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “But God does not charge anyone with wrongdoing.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> These are people who rebel against the light. They do not want to know its ways, or to stay on its paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 2:1–2; PSALMS 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> The murderer gets up at dawn to kill the poor and needy, and when night falls he becomes a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; PSALMS 94:7"> The adulterer waits for dusk, saying to himself, ‘No one will see me now,’ and he covers his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> Thieves break into houses during the night and they sleep during the day. They don't even know what the light is like!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremiah 2:26; PSALMS 73:18–19"> Total darkness is like light to them, for they are familiar with the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 69:22; PSALMS 58:7; Job 9:26"> Like bubbles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Implied.">d</a></sup>on the surface of a river they are quickly carried away. The land they own is cursed by God. They don't enter their own vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> Just as heat and drought dry up snowmelt, so Sheol takes away those who have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> Even their mothers forget them, maggots feast on them, they are no longer remembered, and their wickedness becomes like a tree that is broken into pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> They mistreat childless women and are mean to widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> God prolongs the life of the wicked by his power; but when they arise, they have no assurance of life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alternatively: “God drags off the mighty through his power. God rises up and they despair of life.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> He supports them and gives them security, but he is always watching what they're doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 37:10; PSALMS 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> Though they may be illustrious for a while, soon they are gone. They are brought down like all others, cut off like the heads of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> If this isn't so, who can prove I'm a liar and there's nothing to what I say?”</span>
