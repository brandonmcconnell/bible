<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite responded and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> “How can anyone be of help to God? Even wise people are only helpful to themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; PSALMS 147:10–11"> Is it any benefit to the Almighty if you're a good person?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Good person”: or “righteous.”">a</a></sup>What does he gain if you do what's right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; PSALMS 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Does he correct you and bring charges against you because of your reverence?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eliphaz is saying that if Job was truly innocent he wouldn't be suffering God's punishment.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> No: it's because you're so wicked! Your sins are never-ending!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For no reason at all you took your brother's clothing as a security for a debt, and left them stripped naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> You refused water to the thirsty; you denied food to the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> Is it because the land belongs to the powerful, and only the privileged have a right to live there?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Suggesting that this is Job's attitude.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> You have sent widows away empty-handed; you have crushed the outstretched arms of orphans, begging for help.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Begging for help” implied.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> That's why you're surrounded by traps to catch you, and why you suddenly panic in terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> That's why it's so dark you cannot see, and why you feel like you're drowning.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “the abundance of water covers you.”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; PSALMS 8:3–4"> Doesn't God live high in heaven and looks down on even the highest stars?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; PSALMS 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> But you ask, ‘What does God know? How can he see and judge what happens down here in darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; PSALMS 139:11–12; PSALMS 33:14"> Thick clouds cover him so he can't see anything as he walks around in heaven.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Eliphaz accuses Job of believing in a distant, uninterested God who is immune to what happens on earth.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Why do you insist on following the traditional thinking of the wicked?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “the old paths on which evil people walked.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; PSALMS 55:23"> They were taken before their time; all they had built was washed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; PSALMS 4:6"> They had told God, ‘Get lost! What can the Almighty do to us?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> And yet he was the one who had filled their homes with good things—but I don't accept their way of thinking.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Eliphaz uses exactly the same words as Job does in 21:16.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 58:10; PSALMS 107:42; PSALMS 52:6"> Those who do right rejoice when they see the destruction of the wicked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “it,” referring back to verse 16.">i</a></sup>and the innocent mock them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> saying, ‘Our enemies are destroyed, and fire has burned up all that's left of them.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Come back to God and be reconciled to him, and you'll be prosperous again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Listen to what he tells you and keep his words in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If you return to God you will be restored. If you renounce your sinful life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, “remove wickedness from your tent.”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> and give up your love of money and desire for possessions,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally, “throw your gold in the dust, the gold of Ophir in the riverbed.” Since Job has lost everything already, this needs to be seen figuratively.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; PSALMS 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> then the Almighty will be your gold and your precious silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> Then you will find delight in the Almighty, and be able to face him without feeling ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; PSALMS 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> You will pray to him, and he will hear you, and you will keep your promises to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; PSALMS 90:17; PSALMS 20:4"> Whatever you decide to do will be successful, and wherever you go, light will shine on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> When others are humbled, and you say, ‘please help them,’ God will save them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> God saves those who are innocent, and you will be saved if you do what is right.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally, “if your hands are clean.”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
