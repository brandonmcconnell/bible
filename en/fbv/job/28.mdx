<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; Malachi 3:2–3; Isaiah 48:10"> “There are silver mines and places where gold is refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Numbers 31:22; Genesis 4:22"> Iron is extracted from the earth and copper is smelted from its ore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Habakkuk 2:13; Matthew 6:33"> Miners take lamps into the darkness underground and search for ore as far as they can go in the shadows and the gloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They dig a mineshaft far from where people live or anyone ever goes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “forgotten by the foot.”">a</a></sup>They swing from ropes that hang in the pits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:25–29; PSALMS 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> Bread comes from the earth, but underneath it looks as if it has been turned upside-down by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11"> Here the rocks contain lapis lazuli<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “sapphires,” but it seems more likely that the blue stone lapis lazuli is meant here.">b</a></sup>and the dust contains gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> No birds of prey can see these paths, no falcon's eye can perceive.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hawks and falcons have amazing eyesight, but even their eyes cannot see below ground.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> No wild beasts have passed that way; the lion has not walked there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:4–6"> Miners attack the hard rock; they overturn the roots of mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> They tunnel through the rock, looking carefully for every precious stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> They dam the sources of the rivers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Perhaps part of some mining process.">d</a></sup>and bring to light what is hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> But where can wisdom be found?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The purpose of the long illustration above is to set the scene: while human beings can do amazing things, they can't “mine” wisdom!">e</a></sup>Where is the place to gain understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> Human beings do not know the way to wisdom; it is not found among the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The deep waters say, ‘It's not here,’ and the sea says, ‘It's not here either.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> It cannot be bought with gold; nor can it be purchased with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; Ezekiel 28:13; PSALMS 45:9"> Its value cannot be measured, even with the gold of Ophir;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The most valuable form of gold then known.">f</a></sup>it is more precious than onyx or lapis lazuli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Gold or fine glass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Glass was rare and very valuable at the time.">g</a></sup>cannot compare with wisdom; it cannot be exchanged for gold jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> Coral and crystal are not worth mentioning; the price of wisdom is far above rubies.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The actual gemstone identified here is not known, but is thought to be red in color.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Ezekiel 28:13"> Topaz from Ethiopia can't compare with it; it cannot be bought with the purest gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> So where does wisdom come from? Where is the place to gain understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> Wisdom is hidden from the sight of all living things, even the birds of the air cannot see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Abaddon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “destruction.”">i</a></sup>and Death say, ‘We've only heard a rumor of it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> Only God understands the path to wisdom; he knows where it is found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For he looks to the very end of the earth; he sees everything under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> He decided how strong the wind should blow, and regulated the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zechariah 10:1"> He set a limit for the rain and made a path for the lightning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then he considered wisdom. He examined it, gave it his approval, and declared it good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 111:10; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 9:10"> He said to humankind, ‘To reverence the Lord is wisdom, and to turn away from evil is understanding.’”</span>
