<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> And you ask, ‘What benefit do I get? What good has it done me by not sinning?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I'll tell you, and your friends too!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Just look up at the sky and see. Observe the clouds high above you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If you sin, how does that harm God?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “what does that accomplish against him?”">a</a></sup>How do your many sins affect God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; PSALMS 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If you do what's right, what good are you doing for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> No—your sins only affect people like yourself, and whatever good you do only affects them too.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> People cry out because of terrible persecution; they call for someone to save them from their oppressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 42:8; Acts 16:25; PSALMS 149:5"> But no one asks, ‘Where is the God my maker, the one who gives songs in the night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> who teaches us more than the animals, and makes us wiser than the birds?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; PSALMS 123:3–4"> When they call out for help, God doesn't answer them because they are proud and evil people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> God doesn't listen to their empty cries;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Empty in the sense that they are vain and insincere.">b</a></sup>the Almighty doesn't pay them any attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; PSALMS 37:5–6"> How much less will God hear you when you say he can't see you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Elihu is addressing Job directly and saying that since Job says God doesn't see him, why should God listen to him?">c</a></sup>Your case is before him, so you have to wait for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> You're saying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Implied.">d</a></sup>that God doesn't punish people in his anger and pays little attention to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> You, Job, are talking nonsense, making long speeches when you know nothing!”</span>
