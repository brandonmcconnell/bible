<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> I have given it the wilderness as its home, the salt plains as a place to live.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 104:27–28; PSALMS 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> It hunts in the mountains for pastureland, searching for all kinds of green plants to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; PSALMS 92:10"> Is the wild ox willing to serve you? Will it spend the night at your manger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; PSALMS 129:3; Job 39:7"> Can you tie a wild ox to a plow? Can you make it till your fields for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; PSALMS 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Because it's so powerful can you trust it? Can you depend on it to do your heavy work for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Are you sure it will gather your grain and bring it to your threshing floor?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The ostrich proudly flaps her wings, but they are nothing like the flight feathers of the stork.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The ostrich is flightless, while the stork is a master of the air. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The ostrich abandons her eggs on the ground, leaving them to be warmed in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> She doesn't think that they can be crushed underfoot, trampled by a wild animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> She is tough towards her young, acting as if they didn't belong to her. She doesn't care that all her work was for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> For I, God, made her forget wisdom—she didn't get her share of intelligence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> But when she needs to, she can jump up and run, mocking a horse and its rider with her speed.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 147:10; PSALMS 93:1; PSALMS 104:1"> Did you give the horse its strength? Did you place a mane upon its neck?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Did you make it able to jump like a locust? Its loud snorting is terrifying!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; PSALMS 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> It paws at the ground, rearing up with power as it charges into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> It laughs at fear; it is not frightened at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> The quiver full of arrows rattles against it; the spear and the javelin flash in the sunlight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> Shaking with rage it gallops across the ground; it cannot remain still when the trumpet sounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> Whenever the trumpet calls, it is ready;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “he says, ‘Aha!”">d</a></sup>he senses the sound of battle from far away, he hears the commanders shouting.</span>
</div>
