<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 13:6; Mark 4:9; PSALMS 49:1–3"> “Now listen to me, Job. Pay attention to everything I have to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:1; Matthew 5:2; PSALMS 78:2"> Look, I'm about to speak; my mouth is ready to talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 27:4; Proverbs 20:15; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–4"> My words come from my upright heart; my lips speak sincerely of what I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:8; Genesis 2:7; Job 27:3"> The spirit of God made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:32–33; Job 32:1; PSALMS 50:21"> Answer me, if you can. Stand in front of me and prepare to defend yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 4:19; Job 10:9; Job 13:12"> Look, before God we are both the same. I was also made from a piece of clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:34; PSALMS 32:4"> See here, you don't need to be frightened of me. I won't be too hard on you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “My hand will not be heavy upon you.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Jeremiah 29:23"> You spoke in my hearing and I have listened to everything you had to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 16:17; Job 9:21"> You say, ‘I'm clean, I've done nothing wrong; I'm pure, I have not sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:11; Job 13:24–25; Job 9:30–31"> See how God finds fault with me and treats me as his enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 31:4; Daniel 4:35"> He puts my feet in the stocks and watches everything I do.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Job 36:5; Job 1:22"> But look, you are wrong—let me explain. God is greater than any human being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 40:2; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 11:34"> Why are you fighting against him, complaining that God isn't answering your questions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24; 2 Chronicles 33:10; Job 33:29"> God speaks over and over again,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “once, and twice,” but this is number parallelism which indicates an increasing number.">b</a></sup>but people don't notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Job 4:13; Genesis 20:3"> Through dreams and visions in the night, when people fall into deep sleep, resting on their beds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:10; Job 36:15; PSALMS 40:6"> God speaks to them with solemn warnings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “terrifies them with warnings.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; James 4:10; Genesis 20:6"> to turn them away from doing wrong and to stop them from becoming proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:30; Job 33:28; 2 Peter 3:9"> He saves them from the grave and spares them from violent death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 30:17; Job 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> People are also disciplined on a bed of pain with constant aching in their bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 107:17–18; Job 3:24; Genesis 3:6"> They have no desire to eat; they do not even want their favorite dishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 19:20; Job 13:28; Proverbs 5:11"> Their flesh wastes away to nothing; all that's left is skin and bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:28; 2 Samuel 24:16; Job 17:13–16"> They are close to death;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “the pit.”">d</a></sup>their lives approaching the executioner.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “the destroyers,” which could refer to the angels of death or terminal diseases.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> But if an angel appears, a mediator, one of God's thousands of angels, to tell someone the right way for them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> he will be gracious to them. He will say, ‘Save them from going down into the grave, for I have found a way to free them.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “for I have found a ransom.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 103:5; 2 Kings 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> Then their bodies will be renewed as if they were young again; they will be as strong as when they were in their prime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 30:5; Acts 9:11"> They will pray to God, and he will accept them; they come into God's presence with joy, and he will set things right for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:13; PSALMS 14:2"> They sing, and tell others, ‘I sinned, I distorted what is right, but it did not do me any good.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “but I was not paid back (punished) as I deserved.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> He saved me from going down into the grave and I will live in the light.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:11; 1 Corinthians 12:6; Philippians 2:13"> Look, God does this time and again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “twice, three times.”">h</a></sup>for people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 56:13; Isaiah 38:17; Job 33:28"> he saves them from the grave so they might see the light of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 18:2; Job 13:6; Job 21:2"> Pay attention Job, and listen to me! Be quiet—let me speak!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:27; Job 15:4–5; Job 22:5–9"> But if you have anything to say, then speak up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 34:11; Proverbs 5:1–2; PSALMS 49:3"> If not, listen to me. Keep quiet and I will teach you wisdom.”</span>
