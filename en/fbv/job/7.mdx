<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 39:4; Job 14:5–6; Job 5:7"> “Isn't life for human beings like serving a sentence of hard labor? Don't their days pass like those of a hired laborer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> Like some slave longing for a bit of shade, like a hired hand anxiously waiting for pay day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; PSALMS 39:5"> I've been given months of emptiness and nights of misery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> When I go to bed I ask, ‘When shall I get up?’ But the night goes on and on, and I toss and turn until dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Isaiah 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> My body is covered with maggots and caked in dirt; my skin is cracked, with oozing sores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> My days pass quicker than a weaver's shuttle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The image is of a weaver working on a loom, whipping the shuttle from side to side.">a</a></sup>and they come to an end without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; PSALMS 78:39; Jeremiah 15:15"> Remember that my life is just a breath; I will not see happiness again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; PSALMS 37:36"> Those watching me won't see me anymore; your eyes will be looking for me, but I will be gone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Will be gone”: in other words, he will be dead; also in verse 21.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; 2 Samuel 14:14"> When a cloud disappears, it's gone, just as anyone who goes down to Sheol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Sheol”: the place of the dead.">c</a></sup>does not come back up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; PSALMS 103:16"> They will never return home, and the people they knew will forget them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “His place will not know him anymore.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 40:9; 1 Samuel 1:10; Job 10:1"> So, no, I won't hold my tongue—I will speak in the agony of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> Am I the sea or a sea monster that you have to guard me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 6:6; PSALMS 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> If I tell myself, ‘I'll feel better if I lie down on my bed,’ or ‘it will help me to lie down on my couch,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> then you scare me so much with dreams and terrify me with visions</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Samuel 17:23"> that I would rather be strangled—I would rather die than become just a bag of bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> I hate my life! I know I won't live long. Leave me alone because my life is just a breath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Just a breath”: in other words, it has no purpose or meaning.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; PSALMS 8:4; PSALMS 144:3"> Why are human beings so important to you; why are you so concerned about them</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:12–13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> that you inspect them every morning and test them every moment? Won't you ever stop staring at me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; PSALMS 13:1–3"> Won't you ever leave me alone long enough to catch my breath?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Catch my breath”: literally, “swallow my spittle.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> What have I done wrong? What have I done to you, Watcher of Humanity? Why have you made me your target, so that I'm a burden even to myself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> If so why don't you pardon my sins, and take away my guilt? Right now I'm going to lie down in the dust, and though you will look for me, I will be gone.”</span>
