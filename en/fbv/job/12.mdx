<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Job replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> “You really think you're special people, don't you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “No doubt you are the people.”">a</a></sup>Obviously when you die, wisdom will die with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> But I too have insights, and you're no better than me. Doesn't everyone know the things you've said?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; PSALMS 91:15"> But my friends laugh at me because I called on God and he answered me: the innocent man who does right has become an object of derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; PSALMS 17:5"> People who are comfortable have contempt for those who are in trouble, ready to push over those who are already slipping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; PSALMS 73:11–12; PSALMS 17:14"> Robbers live in peace, and those who make God angry live in safety, trusting their own strength as their ‘god.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> But if you ask the animals they will teach you, the birds in the sky will tell you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> ask the earth and it will teach you, and the fishes in the sea will tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Which of all these doesn't know that the Lord has done this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> He gives life to every living thing, life to all humankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> The ear distinguishes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “tests.”">b</a></sup>words just like the palate distinguishes foods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> Wisdom to distinguish belongs to the old, and the ability to rightly discriminate belongs to those with long experience.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Could also be a question: “Is wisdom with the old, and understanding in length of days?”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> God has wisdom and power, counsel and understanding belong to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> If he tears something down, nobody can rebuild it. If he imprisons someone, nobody can free them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> If God holds back the waters, everything dries up; if he releases the waters, the earth floods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> He is mighty and victorious;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “He has power and sound wisdom.”">d</a></sup>both deceivers and those deceived are subject to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He leads counselors away stripped of their wisdom, he makes judges into fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; PSALMS 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He removes the chains of office from kings and makes them wear loincloths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In other words he turns kings into manual workers.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leads priests away stripped of their religious garments, he overthrows the powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He takes away the advice of trusted advisors, he removes the discernment of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He pours scorn upon princes and takes away power<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “loosens the belt.”">f</a></sup>from the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He reveals what is hidden in darkness, and brings into the light the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He makes nations great and he destroys them; he expands nations and ruins them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He removes the understanding of rulers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “He strips the hearts of the heads of the people of the earth.”">g</a></sup>and makes them wander in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; PSALMS 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They grope around in the dark without a light. He makes them stagger like drunk people.”</span>
</div>
