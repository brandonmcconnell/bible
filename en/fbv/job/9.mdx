<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Job replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> “Yes, I know all that! But how can anyone be right before God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If you wanted to argue with God, God could ask a thousand questions that no one could answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> God is so wise and so powerful that no one could challenge him and win.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> God moves the mountains suddenly; he overturns them in his anger.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring to earthquakes.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; PSALMS 75:3"> He shakes the earth, making its foundations quake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> He is the one who can command the sun not to rise and the stars not to shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; PSALMS 104:2–3"> He alone is the one who stretches out the heavens and walks on the waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> He made the constellations of the Bear, Orion, the Pleiades, and the stars of the southern sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; PSALMS 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> He is the one who does incredible things that are beyond our understanding, marvelous things that are uncountable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; PSALMS 77:19"> But when he passes by me, I don't see him; when he moves on, he is invisible to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> If he takes away,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Takes away.” This may refer to “taking away” through death. The verb here means “snatches” and occurs nowhere else in the Bible.">b</a></sup>who can prevent him? Who is going to ask him, ‘What are you doing?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God does not restrain his anger; he crushes Rahab's helpers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Rahab is thought to refer to a mythical sea monster, and its helpers those who assist in destruction and causing chaos.">c</a></sup>underfoot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> So how much less could I answer God, or choose my words to argue with him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Even though I'm right,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “innocent.”">d</a></sup>I can't answer him. I must plead for mercy from my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; PSALMS 66:18–20"> Even if I called him to come and he responded, I don't believe he would listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> He pounds me with the winds of a storm; he wounds me time and again, without giving a reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; PSALMS 88:15–18"> He doesn't give me a chance even to catch my breath; instead he fills my life with bitter suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If it's a question of strength, then God is the strongest. If it's a question of justice, then who will set a time for my case?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “who will take him to court?”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; PSALMS 143:2"> Even though I am right, my own mouth would condemn me; even though I am innocent, he would prove me wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; PSALMS 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> I am innocent! I don't care what happens to me. I hate my life!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> That's why I say, ‘It makes no difference to God. He destroys both the innocent and the wicked.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> When disaster<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “scourge.”">f</a></sup>strikes suddenly he mocks the despair of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; PSALMS 73:3–7"> The earth has been handed over to the wicked; he blinds the eyes of the judges<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Meaning that there is no justice.">g</a></sup>—if it's not him who does this, then who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; PSALMS 90:9–10"> The days of my life race by like a runner, rushing past without me seeing any happiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They pass by like fast sailing ships, like an eagle swooping down on its prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I said to myself, ‘I will forget my complaints; I will stop crying and be happy,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; PSALMS 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I would still be terrified at all my suffering because you, God, will not say I'm innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> Since I'm condemned, what's the point in arguing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; PSALMS 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> Even if I wash myself with pure mountain water and clean my hands with soap,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> you would toss me into a slime pit so that even my own clothes would hate me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; PSALMS 143:2"> For God is not a mortal being like me, I can't defend myself or take him to court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> If only there was an arbitrator<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “judge.”">h</a></sup>who could bring us both together!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> I wish God would stop beating me with his rod and terrifying me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then I could speak up without being afraid—but since I am, I can't!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “for I am not so in myself.”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
