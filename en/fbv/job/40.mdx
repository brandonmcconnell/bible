<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 38:1; Job 40:6"> God continued speaking to Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:14; Job 33:13"> “Are you still going to fight with the Almighty and try to set him straight?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Shall a faultfinder contend with God?”">a</a></sup>Anyone who argues with God must give some answers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Job answered the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> “Me—I am nothing at all. I have no answers. I put my hand in front of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> I have already said far too much and I won't say anything more.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; PSALMS 50:3–4"> Then the Lord answered Job out of the whirlwind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> “Prepare yourself, be strong, for I am going to question you, and you must answer me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Are you really going to say my decisions are wrong?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “invalidate my justice.”">b</a></sup>Are you going to condemn me so you can be right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Are you as powerful as I am? Does your voice thunder like mine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 93:1; PSALMS 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Why don't you dress yourself with majesty and dignity, and clothe yourself with glory and splendor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> Let loose your fierce anger. Humble the proud with a glance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; PSALMS 60:12"> Bring down the proud with your gaze; tread the wicked underfoot right where they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> Bury them in the dust; lock them away in the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; PSALMS 44:6"> Then I will also agree that your own strength can save you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; PSALMS 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Consider Behemoth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some identify Behemoth as the hippopotamus; others with a legendary creature. Behemoth is the plural of the usual word for animal.">c</a></sup>a creature I made just like I made you. It eats grass like cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Look at its powerful loins, the muscles of its belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> It bends its tail like a cedar; its thigh sinews are strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> Its bones are like bronze tubes; its limbs like iron rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> It is the most important example of what God can do; only the one who made it can approach it with a sword.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew is unclear. The verse may mean that only its Creator can defeat it.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 104:26; PSALMS 104:14; Job 40:15"> The hills produce food for it, and all the wild animals play there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Presumably the other animals are safe from the Behemoth since it is vegetarian.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> It lies under the lotus;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some identify this with the Lotus plant, others with the Lotus tree.">f</a></sup>it hides in the reeds of the marsh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> The lotus covers it with shade; the willow trees of the valley surround it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Even if the river is in flood, it is not concerned; it remains calm when the Jordan river surges against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> No one can catch it while it is watching, or pierce its nose with a noose.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “It can take it with his eyes: it can pierce a snare with its nose.”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
