<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> “I know you can do anything. No one can prevent you doing what you want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 131:1; PSALMS 139:6; Job 38:2"> You asked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Implied. Job in this verse and the next is referring back to what God previously said.">a</a></sup>‘Who is this who questions my wisdom by talking so ignorantly?’ I was speaking about things I didn't understand—things too wonderful for me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> You told me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup>‘Listen to me, I am going to speak. I am going to question you, and you must answer me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> Before, I had only heard about you, but now I've seen you for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> That is why I'm sorry for what I said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “reject,” “retract.”">c</a></sup>and I repent in dust and ashes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> After the Lord had finished speaking to Job he said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “I am angry with you and your two friends because you have not told the truth about me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “said of me what is right.”">d</a></sup>as my servant Job did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; PSALMS 103:10"> So take seven bulls and seven rams and go to my servant Job, and you shall offer a burnt offering for yourselves. My servant Job will pray on your behalf and I will accept his prayer and not deal with you according to your foolishness, because you have not told the truth about me, as my servant Job did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite did what the Lord commanded them, and the Lord accepted Job's prayer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> The Lord restored Job's prosperity when he prayed for his friends, and gave him twice as much as he had before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> Then all his brothers and sisters and friends he'd previously known came and ate with him at his home. They showed him sympathy and comforted him because of all the trouble<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “evil.”">e</a></sup>the Lord had caused him. Each one of them gave him money and a gold ring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> The Lord blessed the last part of Job's life more than the first part. Now he owned 14,000 sheep, 6,000 camels, 1,000 teams of oxen, and 1,000 female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; PSALMS 107:41; PSALMS 127:3"> Job also had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Job called the first daughter Jemimah, the second Keziah, and the third Keren-Happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; PSALMS 144:12; Acts 7:20"> Nowhere in all the land were there women as beautiful as Job's daughters, and Job gave them the same inheritance as their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; PSALMS 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> Job lived 140 years after this, seeing his children, and grandchildren, in fact four generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> Then Job died at an old age, having lived a very full life.</span>
</div>
