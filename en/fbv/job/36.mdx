<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu continued speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> “Be patient with me just a little while longer and let me explain. I still have something to say on God's behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will share my extensive knowledge,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “I will bring my knowledge from far away.”">a</a></sup>and I will prove my Creator is in the right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; PSALMS 49:3"> I assure you that what I'm saying are not lies, for I am a man whose knowledge is of the highest order.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “one whose knowledge is perfect is with you.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 22:24; PSALMS 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> God is mighty, but doesn't despise anyone; he is powerful in strength and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 72:12–14; PSALMS 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He does not keep the wicked alive, but gives justice to the oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15; PSALMS 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He always pays attention<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “he does not withdraw his eyes.”">c</a></sup>to those who do right, and places them on thrones with kings, honoring them eternally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; PSALMS 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> If they are bound in chains, tied down by ropes of suffering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> then he explains to them what they've done—their arrogant sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> He makes them pay attention<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “he opens their ears to instruction.”">d</a></sup>and orders them to stop sinning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they listen and do what God says they will live out their lives in happiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they do not listen they will die a violent death,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “by the sword.”">e</a></sup>ignorant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> Those who reject God hold on to their bitterness. Even when he disciplines them they do not cry out to him for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> They die in their youth; their lives end among the male temple prostitutes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Male temple prostitutes”: indicative of dying in shameful ways.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> Through suffering God saves those who suffer; he gets their attention through their troubles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 23:5; PSALMS 118:5; PSALMS 18:19"> God is trying to rescue you from the jaws of trouble to a place of freedom and safety,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “to an open space free of restriction.”">g</a></sup>filling your table full of the very best food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> But you are preoccupied with the fate of the wicked; judgment and justice fill your mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> But be careful that your anger doesn't seduce you into mockery; and don't let the size of the ‘bribe’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Taken together with the first line of this verse, “bribe” could be any kind of “reward” that sin might bring.">h</a></sup>lead you into sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Will your cry for help sustain you when troubles come?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This could also be translated, “Will God value your wealth or gold or your strength?” However, Job has already lost all these.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Do not long for the night when people are suddenly taken away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Presumably a reference to longing for death to bring an end to suffering.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; PSALMS 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Watch out that you don't turn to evil! For it's because of this that you are being tested through suffering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Look how much power God has! What teacher is like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who has instructed him what to do? Who can say to him ‘You have done wrong’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; PSALMS 111:2–4; PSALMS 72:18"> Instead you should praise him for what he has done, as people have done in song.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="In the Psalms, for example.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Everyone has seen God's creation, though only from a distance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Later God asks Job if he was there during creation.">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 90:2; PSALMS 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> See how great God is—more than we can understand! No one can count his years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 147:8; PSALMS 148:8; PSALMS 65:9–13"> He draws up the water and distils it into the dew and the rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> The clouds pour down rain, falling plentifully on humankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; PSALMS 18:13"> Can anyone understand how the clouds spread out, or how thunder roars from where he lives?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally, “the thunderings of his pavilion.”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> See how he scatters lightning around him, and covers the depths of the sea in darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> By these actions he rules the people, he provides abundant food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 135:7; PSALMS 147:8–9; PSALMS 148:8"> He holds lightning in his hands and commands where it should strike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> Thunder declares his presence—even cattle know when a storm is coming.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Hebrew is unclear.">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
