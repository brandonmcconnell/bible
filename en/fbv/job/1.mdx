<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20"> Once there was a man called Job who lived in the land of Uz. He was a man of integrity who did what was right. He respected<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word is “fear” but is used in the sense of reverence and awe rather than being frightened or terrified.">a</a></sup>God and avoided evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 127:3–5; Job 42:13; PSALMS 128:3"> He had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> He owned seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred teams of oxen, and five hundred female donkeys, as well as many servants. He was more wealthy than anyone else in the East.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; PSALMS 133:1"> Job's sons used to take turns holding feasts in their homes on their specific day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The “specific day” could be a set date or a birthday, for example.">b</a></sup>also inviting their sisters to come and eat and drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; 1 Samuel 16:5"> When the days of feasting were over, Job would send for them and purify them by getting up early in the morning to offer a burnt offering for each of them. He was concerned, thinking to himself, “Maybe my children have sinned in some way and have unintentionally offended<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The word used here is “blessed” but is usually understood as a euphemism for “cursed.”">c</a></sup>God.” This is what Job always did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zechariah 3:1"> One day the angels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “the sons of God.”">d</a></sup>came to present themselves to the Lord, and Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The name means “Adversary.”">e</a></sup>came with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> The Lord asked Satan, “What have you been doing?” “Traveling around on earth, going here and there,” Satan replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> “Have you paid attention to Job, my servant?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The term “servant” is used here as a positive term to describe someone who admires and respects rather than someone who simply obeys commands.">f</a></sup>asked the Lord. “There's no one like him on earth, a man of integrity who does what is right, who respects God and avoids evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> “Is it for nothing that Job respects God?” Satan answered. “You've placed a protective fence around him and his family—in fact around everything he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; Zechariah 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> You have blessed everything he does. His flocks and herds have increased so much they fill the whole land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But if you reach out and take everything he has, he will definitely curse you to your face.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> The Lord told Satan, “Very well, you have the power to do whatever you want to everything he has, but don't hurt him physically.” Then Satan left the Lord's presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> The day came when while Job's sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother's house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:13; Jeremiah 51:31; 1 Samuel 4:17"> a messenger came to Job and told him, “As the oxen were plowing and female donkeys were grazing nearby,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> the Sabeans attacked and took them, killing the herdsmen. Only I escaped to bring you this news.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:12; Genesis 19:24; 1 Kings 18:38"> While he was still speaking another messenger arrived and said, “The fire of God fell from heaven and set the sheep and shepherds on fire and burned them up. Only I escaped to bring you this news.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Habakkuk 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While he was still speaking another messenger arrived and said, “Three armed companies of Chaldeans attacked the camels and took them, killing the herdsmen. Only I escaped to bring you this news.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> While he was still speaking another messenger arrived and said, “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their eldest brother</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> when all of a sudden a tremendous wind came in from the desert. It hit the house on all four sides so that it collapsed on them and they died. Only I escaped to bring you this news.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 2 Samuel 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> Then Job stood up and tore his robe.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="An expression of intense feeling.">g</a></sup>Then cut off his hair and fell to the ground in worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> “I was naked when I came out of my mother's womb, and I shall be naked when I leave,” he said. “The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away. May the Lord's name be honored.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all of this Job did not sin, nor blame God.</span>
