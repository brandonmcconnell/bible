<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 41:21; Job 31:35; Job 13:22"> But I would still like to speak to the Almighty: I want to prove myself to God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 119:69; Job 16:2; Jeremiah 23:32"> As for you, you cover things up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “you are plasterers of lies.”">a</a></sup>by telling lies! You are all like doctors who can't heal anyone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:28; Job 13:13; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> I wish you would all be quiet! That would be the wisest thing for you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:2; Job 21:2–3; Proverbs 8:6–7"> Listen to my argument and pay attention to what I have to say.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 36:4; Job 27:4; Job 32:21–22"> Do you think you can tell lies to defend God? Are you talking deceitfully on his behalf?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Exodus 23:2–3; Malachi 2:9"> Or are you wanting to show God favoritism? Are you going to argue God's case for him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Jeremiah 17:10; PSALMS 44:21"> Will you be found to be doing good when God examines you? Can you fool him as if he's a human being?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:9; PSALMS 82:2; PSALMS 50:21–22"> No, he will definitely rebuke you if you secretly show him favoritism!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 31:23; PSALMS 119:120; Exodus 15:16"> Isn't his majesty terrifying to you? Aren't you so afraid of him you're paralyzed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 18:17; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Isaiah 26:14"> Your sayings are as helpful as ashes; your arguments as weak as clay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 7:11; Job 21:3; Job 13:5"> Be quiet. Don't talk to me. Let me speak, come what may.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:3; PSALMS 119:109; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> I take responsibility for myself; I am ready to risk my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 23:4; Job 23:10; Job 19:25–28"> Even though he kills me, I will hope in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “I have no hope.”">b</a></sup>I am still going to defend my ways before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1–2; PSALMS 118:21; Isaiah 33:14"> By doing this I will be saved since no godless person could come before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:2; Job 33:1; Job 13:6"> Listen carefully to what I say, pay attention to my explanation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 23:4; Job 9:2–3; Job 16:21"> Look, I've prepared my case—I know I will be proved right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7–8; Job 7:11; Job 33:32"> Who wants to argue with me? If I'm proved wrong, I'm prepared to be quiet and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 139:12; Job 9:34–35; Revelation 6:15–16"> God, I have two requests, then I can face you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 33:7; PSALMS 39:10"> Stop beating me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “withdraw your hand far from me.”">c</a></sup>and stop terrifying me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:15; Job 9:32; Job 42:3–6"> Then call, and I will answer. Or let me speak, and then answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 44:20–21; PSALMS 139:23; Job 36:8–9"> What are my sins and iniquities? Show me what have I done wrong; how have I rebelled against you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 19:11; PSALMS 13:1; Lamentations 2:5"> Why are you unfriendly towards me?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “hide your face from me.”">d</a></sup>Why do you treat me as your enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Job 21:18; 1 Samuel 24:14"> Would you frighten a leaf blown by the wind or hunt down a piece of straw?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 25:7; John 5:14; Proverbs 5:11–13"> For you write down bitter things against me and pay me back for the sins of my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 33:11; Job 10:6; Acts 16:24"> You put my feet in the stocks. You keep an eye on every step I take. You even inspect my footprints!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 39:11; Job 4:19; Job 30:17–19"> I'm falling apart like something rotten, like moth-eaten clothes.</span>
