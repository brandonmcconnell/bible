<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; PSALMS 32:4; Job 7:11"> “Just the same, my complaints today remain bitter. In spite of my groaning he is still punishing me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “his hand is heavy on me.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> If only I knew where I could find him so I could go to where he sits in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> There I would lay out my case before him and present all my arguments in full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I would discover how he would answer me and learn what he has to say to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would he fight against me using his mighty power? No, he would pay attention to what I have to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There a good<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “upright.”">b</a></sup>person could reason with him, and I would be acquitted forever by my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> If I go to the east, he's not there; if I go the west, I don't find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> If he's working in the north, I don't perceive him; if he's moving south, I don't see him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> Yet he always knows where I'm going. When he has proved me, I will come out shining like gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The image here is of testing gold in a crucible: once the impurities are burned away, the liquid gold has a brilliant surface.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> I have kept in step with him; I have followed his way without turning aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; PSALMS 119:103; PSALMS 119:11"> I have not neglected his commands, for I value what he has told me to do more than the food I eat every day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But God is unchangeable—who can turn him from his purposes? He does whatever he wants to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> So he will finish whatever he has planned for me—and he has many plans for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; PSALMS 77:3; Job 31:23"> That's why I'm terrified at meeting him;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “his presence.”">d</a></sup>when I think of him I tremble with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 22:14; Job 27:2; PSALMS 88:16"> God has made me faint-hearted; the Almighty has scared me to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> Yet I'm still here despite the dark—even though I can't see through the utter darkness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Hebrew here is difficult: “Because I am not cut off by reason of darkness and from my face which thick darkness covers.”">e</a></sup></span>
