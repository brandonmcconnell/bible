<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Elihu continued,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> “Listen to my words, you men who say you are wise; pay attention to what I'm saying you who think you know.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Since Elihu has already criticized them, his words here are surely ironic.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> The ear distinguishes words just as the palate distinguishes foods.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This is what Job stated in 12:11.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us discern for ourselves what is right: let us decide among ourselves what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> Job said, ‘I am innocent, and God has denied me justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Even though I'm right, I'm treated like a liar; I am dying from my wounds, even though I've done nothing wrong.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job stated this in 9:21and 27:2.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> Has there ever been a man like Job with such a thirst for ridiculing others?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “who drinks scorn like water.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> He keeps company with wicked people; he associates with those who do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> He's even said, ‘What benefit is there in being God's friend?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> So listen to me, you men of understanding! It's impossible for God to do evil and the Almighty to do wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> He pays people back for what they've done, and treats them as they deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; PSALMS 11:7; PSALMS 145:17"> It's absolutely sure that God doesn't act wickedly; the Almighty would never pervert justice.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Elihu is repeating what Bildad said in 8:3.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who put him in charge of the earth? Who gave him the responsibility for all the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If he were to withdraw<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Following the Septuagint reading.">f</a></sup>his spirit, if he were to take back his breath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> all living things would immediately die and human beings would return to dust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> If you have understanding then hear this; pay attention to what I'm saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Do you really think someone who hated justice could govern? Are you going to condemn Almighty God who always does what is right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> He's the one who tells kings, ‘You're useless!’ or says to nobles, ‘You're wicked!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> He doesn't think more of the rich than the poor, for they are all people he himself made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> They die in a moment; at midnight they shudder and pass away; the mighty are gone without effort.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For he watches what they're doing and sees everywhere they go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness so deep where people who do evil can hide themselves from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; PSALMS 119:137; Romans 9:20"> God doesn't need to examine anyone in any greater detail that they should come before him for judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Meaning that God knows the destiny of all based on what they have done.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 113:7–8; PSALMS 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He brings down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “shatters.”">h</a></sup>the mighty without needing an investigation; he sets up others in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Knowing what they've done he overthrows them in a night and destroys them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> He strikes them down for their wickedness in public where they can be seen</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> because they turned away from following him, disregarding all his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> They made the poor call out to him, and he heard the cries of the oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; PSALMS 27:9"> Yet if God wants to remain silent, who can condemn him? If he chooses to hide his face, who can see him? Whether it concerns a nation or an individual,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> a person who rejects God should not rule so they don't mislead<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “ensnare.”">i</a></sup>people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> If you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “anyone.”">j</a></sup>were to say to God, ‘I have sinned, but I won't do bad things any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; PSALMS 25:4–5; PSALMS 139:23–24"> Show me what I cannot see. If I have done wrong, I won't do it again,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; PSALMS 135:6"> then should God reward you for following your own opinions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="In the context, probably referring to Job's own opinions as to what is right and wrong.">k</a></sup>since you have rejected his? You're the one who has to choose, not me! Tell us what you think.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> For people who understand—those who are wise who have heard what I said—will tell me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> ‘Job doesn't know what he's talking about. What he says doesn't make any sense.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> If only Job were thoroughly condemned because he speaks like evil people do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> Now he has added rebellion to his sins; he claps his hands at us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="An action of ridicule and disrespect (see for example 27:23).">l</a></sup>making long speeches full of accusations against God.”</span>
</div>
