<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 74:14; PSALMS 104:26; Job 3:8"> “Can you pull out Leviathan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Leviathan: some identify this creature with the crocodile, or a mythical beast. It is also mentioned in 3:8.">a</a></sup>with a hook? Can you tie its mouth shut?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Ezekiel 29:4–5; 2 Kings 19:28"> Can you thread a rope through its nose? Can you pass a hook through its jaw?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; PSALMS 55:21"> Will it beg you to let it go? Or will it talk softly to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; PSALMS 8:5–6"> Will it make a contract with you? Will it agree to be your slave forever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Will you play with it like a pet bird? Will you put it on a leash for your girls?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> Will your trading partners decide on a price for him, and divide him up among the merchants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Can you pierce his skin with many harpoons, its head with fishing spears?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> If you were to grab hold of it, imagine the battle you would have! You wouldn't do that again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> Any hope to capture it is foolish. Anyone who tries is thrown to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; PSALMS 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> Since no one has the courage to provoke Leviathan, who would dare to stand up against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; PSALMS 24:1; Job 35:7"> Who has confronted me with any claim that I should repay? Everything under heaven belongs to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> Let me tell you about Leviathan: its powerful legs and graceful proportions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; PSALMS 32:9; James 3:3"> Who can remove its hide? Who can penetrate its double coat of armor?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “who can approach it with a double bridle?”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 58:6"> Who can open its jaws? Its teeth are terrifying!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> Its pride<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “back.”">c</a></sup>is its rows of scales, closed tightly together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Its scales are so close together that no air can pass between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Each scale attaches to the next; they lock together and nothing can penetrate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> When it sneezes light shines out. Its eyes are like the rising sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 18:8"> Flames pour from its mouth, sparks of fire shoot out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> Smoke comes from its nostrils, like steam from a kettle on a fire made of reeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> Its breath sets fire to charcoal as flames shoot from its mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> Its neck is powerful, and all who face him shake with terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Its body is dense and solid, as if it is made from cast metal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> Its heart is rock-hard, like a millstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; PSALMS 107:28"> When it rises, even the powerful are terrified; they retreat as it thrashes about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> Swords just bounce off it, as do spears, darts, and javelins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> It brushes aside iron like straw, and bronze like rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> Arrows cannot make it run away; stones from slingshots are like pieces of stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> Clubs are also treated like stubble; it laughs at the sound made by flying spears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> Its underparts are covered with points as sharp as broken pots; when it drags itself through the mud it leaves marks like a threshing sledge.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Threshing sledge: a board with sharp spikes for threshing grain.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> It churns up the sea like water in a boiling pot, like a steaming bowl when ointment is mixed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> It leaves a glistening wake behind it as if the sea had white hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> There is nothing on earth like it: a creature that has no fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; PSALMS 73:6; Job 28:8"> It looks down on all other creatures. It is the proudest of all.”</span>
