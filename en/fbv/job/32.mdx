<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 33:9; Job 10:2"> Job's three friends stopped responding to him because he kept on protesting his innocence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> Then Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, grew angry. He was angry with Job for claiming he was right rather than God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> Elihu was also angry at Job's three friends because they made it seem that God was wrong, because they had not been able to answer Job.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “because they had not been able to answer Job, yet still condemned him.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> Elihu had waited for the other three to speak with Job since they were older than he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> But when he saw that they could not answer Job, he grew very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, said, “I am young, and you are old, which is why I was reluctant to tell you what I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> I told myself, ‘Those who are older should speak—those who are elderly should teach wisdom.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> However, there is a spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Spirit and breath are closely linked. See for example Genesis 2:7.">b</a></sup>in human beings, the breath of the Almighty, that gives them understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> It is not the old who are wise, or the elderly who know what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> That's why I'm telling you to listen to me now—let me tell you what I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Well, I waited to hear what you had to say, listening for your insights as you looked for the right words to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> I paid close attention to all of you, and none of you have proved Job wrong or answered his arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Don't say to yourselves, ‘We are so wise,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “We have found wisdom.”">c</a></sup>for God will prove him wrong, not a human being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Job didn't line up his arguments against me, and I won't answer him the way you did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> You sit there speechless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “dismayed.”">d</a></sup>with nothing left to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> Should I continue to wait since you're no longer talking, just standing there saying nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> No—now I'll give my answer too. I'll tell you what I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> I have so much to say I can't hold the words back!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “I am full of words, my spirit presses in my belly.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Inside I'm like fermenting wine all bottled up; like new wineskins ready to explode!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I have to speak before I burst;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “I must speak to find relief.”">f</a></sup>I will open my lips to answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> I will not take sides, and I'm not going to flatter anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> I don't know how to flatter, and if I did my Creator would soon destroy me.”</span>
