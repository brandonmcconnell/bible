<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11"> Then Bildad the Shuhite spoke up and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; 1 Kings 19:11"> “How much longer will you go on talking like this? The words coming out of your mouth are a lot of hot air!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“A lot of hot air”: literally, “a strong wind.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Does God pervert justice? Does the Almighty pervert what is right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> Your children must have sinned against him, and so they deserved the punishment he inflicted on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> But if you pray to God and ask for his help,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> if you live a clean life and do what is right, then he would act to make things right for you in your home.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “restore your rightful habitation.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> Though you began with next to nothing, you will end up with so much!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> Why don't you ask what previous generations discovered, examine what our ancestors found out? We were born yesterday and don't know anything!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; PSALMS 144:4; Job 14:2"> Our days on earth fade as quickly as a passing shadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> Won't they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“They—referring to the ancestors mentioned in verse 8.”">c</a></sup>teach you and explain what they know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> Can papyrus grow where there is no marsh? Can reeds grow without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; PSALMS 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> Even without being cut, while they are still flowering, they wither faster than grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> This is what happens to everyone who forgets God. The hopes of those who live without God come to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> Their confidence is like holding on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In the sense of “for support.”">d</a></sup>to a flimsy spider's web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> They look to their home to provide security, but it provides no support. They try to hang onto it, but it doesn't last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> Those who live without God are like a luxuriant plant growing in the sun, spreading its shoots all over the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> It twists its roots down through the stones, and holds on to rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; PSALMS 37:36; Job 7:8"> But when it's cut down, the place where it was disowns it, saying, ‘I never even saw you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; PSALMS 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Its life is over, and others spring up from the earth to take its place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; PSALMS 37:37; PSALMS 37:24"> Look, God doesn't reject someone who is innocent, and he doesn't support those who are guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2; Genesis 21:6; PSALMS 126:6"> He can make you laugh with happiness again and shout for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 35:26"> Those who hate you will be put to shame, and the place where the wicked live<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Place where the wicked live”: literally, “tent of the wicked.”">e</a></sup>will be destroyed.”</span>
</div>
