I don't want to upset you but who could keep quiet and not respond?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, the situation demanded a response.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> You've certainly encouraged many people and supported those who are weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; PSALMS 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> Your advice has helped those who are stumbling not to fall, and you have strengthened those whose knees are weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now you're the one suffering and you're upset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Wasn't it your reverence for God that gave you confidence and your integrity that gave you hope?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Think about it: since when did the innocent die? Since when were good people destroyed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> From what I've seen it's those who plant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “plow.”">b</a></sup>evil and sow trouble who reap the same!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> A breath from God destroys them; a blast of his anger wipes them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; PSALMS 3:7"> Lions may roar and growl, but their teeth still break.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> Even a lion dies from lack of food, and the lioness' cubs are scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; PSALMS 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> A word quietly crept up on me; a whisper reached my ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> Troubling thoughts came to me in nightmares when you fall into a deep sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> I became terrified and trembled; all my bones were shaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; PSALMS 104:4"> Then a breath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “spirit”—the word is the same for both.">c</a></sup>brushed my face and gave me goose-pimples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> Something stopped, but I couldn't see its face. My eyes could only make out a shape. It was totally quiet, and then I heard a voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> ‘Can anyone be right before God? Can anyone be pure before their Maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> If he doesn't even trust his servants, and he says his angels make mistakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> how much more does this apply to those who live in these houses made of clay,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring to human beings and their creation by God from the dust of the ground.">d</a></sup>whose foundations are based on dust, who fall apart like clothing to a moth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> Alive in the morning, they are dead by evening. They die, unnoticed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; PSALMS 39:11; Job 8:22"> They are like tent ropes that are pulled up, and they collapse in death. They die without wisdom.’”</span>
