<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 40:6; Job 37:1–2; 2 Kings 2:11"> Then the Lord answered Job out of the whirlwind,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Whirlwind,” or “storm.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 42:3; 1 Timothy 1:7; Job 35:16"> “Who is this who questions my wisdom by talking so ignorantly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 40:7; Exodus 12:11; Jeremiah 1:17"> Prepare yourself, be strong,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “like a man.”">b</a></sup>for I am going to question you, and you must answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 104:5; Hebrews 1:10; Proverbs 30:4"> Where were you when I laid the foundation of the earth? Tell me, if you have such knowledge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:12; Zechariah 2:1–2; Job 11:9"> Who decided its dimensions? Don't you know? Who stretched out a measuring line?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 26:7; PSALMS 104:5; PSALMS 93:1"> What do its foundations rest upon? Who laid its cornerstone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:11; Job 1:6; Ezra 3:11–12"> when the stars of the morning sang together and all the angels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “sons of God” as in 1:6. The parallel there of “the stars of the morning” would similarly apply to heavenly beings.">c</a></sup>shouted for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:9; Proverbs 8:29; Jeremiah 5:22"> Who laid down the boundaries of the sea when it was born?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “who shut in the sea with doors when it burst forth from the womb.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:2"> Who clothed it with clouds, and wrapped it in a blanket of deep darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; PSALMS 104:9; PSALMS 33:7"> I set its limits, marking its borders.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “I set bars and doors.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 89:9; Proverbs 8:29; PSALMS 65:6–7"> I said, ‘You may come here, but no farther. Here is where your proud waves stop.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 74:16; PSALMS 148:3–5; Genesis 1:5"> During your lifetime, have you ever ordered the morning to begin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 104:35; Job 37:3; Exodus 14:27"> Have you ever told the dawn where to appear that it might take hold of the corners of the earth and shake the wicked out?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This poetic image is meant to convey the idea that when daylight comes the wicked cease their evil.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:6; PSALMS 104:2"> The earth is changed like clay under a seal;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Like a seal impressing an image on a smooth piece of clay.">g</a></sup>its features stand out like a crumpled garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 10:15; PSALMS 37:17; Job 18:5"> The ‘light’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Of course the wicked's “light” is the reverse—darkness.">h</a></sup>of the wicked is taken away from them; their acts of violence are stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:24; Job 26:5–6; PSALMS 77:19"> Have you entered the sources of the sea? Have you explored their hidden depths?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 9:13; PSALMS 107:18; Job 3:5"> Have you been shown where the gates of death are? Have you seen the gates of utter darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 89:11–12; Revelation 20:9; Jeremiah 31:37"> Do you know how far the earth extends? Tell me if you know all this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:8; Genesis 1:14–18; Genesis 1:3–4"> In which direction does light live? Where does darkness dwell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Job 26:10; Genesis 23:17"> Can you take them home? Do you know the way to where they live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 15:7; Job 38:12; Job 38:4"> Of course you know, because you were already born then! You've lived so long!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:6; PSALMS 135:7; PSALMS 33:7"> Have you been to where the snow is kept? Have you seen where the hail is held?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30; Exodus 9:18"> I have stored them up for the time of trouble, for the day of war and battle.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hail is often associated with God's involvement in battle: Joshua 10:11; Exodus 9:22; Isaiah 28:17; Ezekiel 13:13.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 38:12–13; Jonah 4:8; Matthew 24:27"> Do you know the way to where light comes from, or to where the east wind blows over the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 28:26; PSALMS 29:3–10; Job 37:3–6"> Who cuts a channel for the rain to flow? Who creates a path for the thunderbolt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Hebrews 6:7–8; PSALMS 107:35"> Who brings rain to an uninhabited land, to a desert where nobody lives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 107:35; PSALMS 104:14"> to water a parched wasteland to make the green grass grow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; PSALMS 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13"> Does the rain have a father? Who was the father of the dewdrops?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 147:16–17; Job 37:10; Job 38:8"> Who was the mother of ice? Does the frost of the air have a mother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 37:10"> Water turns into rock-hard ice; its surface freezes solid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Job 9:9"> Can you tie together the stars of the Pleiades? Can you loosen the belt of the Orion constellation?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="God is speaking figuratively regarding the stars in these constellations, previously mentioned in 9:9.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:31; Job 9:9; 2 Kings 23:5"> Can you guide the stars of Mazzaroth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A star grouping whose name is not known. It may refer to the 12 constellations of the Zodiac.">k</a></sup>at the right time? Can you direct the Great Bear constellation and its other stars?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 8:22; Job 38:12–13"> Do you know the laws of the heavens?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="In the context, God is referring particularly to the laws governing heavenly bodies.">l</a></sup>Can you apply them to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 22:11; Job 36:27–28; Amos 5:8"> Can you shout to the clouds and command them to pour rain down on you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 37:3; Isaiah 65:1"> Can you send out bolts of lightning and direct them, so that they can answer you saying, ‘Here we are’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 32:8; PSALMS 51:6; Proverbs 2:6"> Who has placed wisdom inside people? Who has given understanding to the mind?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The interpretation of this verse is unclear and many possible translations have been suggested.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PSALMS 147:4; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 8:1"> Who is clever enough to count the clouds? Who can turn heaven's water jars on their sides</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> when the dust has baked into a solid mass?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="In other words, who can cause the rain to fall when the earth is parched?">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 104:21; Job 4:10–11; PSALMS 34:10"> Can you hunt prey for the lion? Can you feed the lion cubs</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 37:8; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:9"> as they crouch down in their dens and lie in wait in the bushes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Matthew 6:26; PSALMS 147:9"> Who provides food for the raven when its young cry out to God, weak from starvation?”</span>
</div>
