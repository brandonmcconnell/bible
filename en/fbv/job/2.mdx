<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 1:6; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 1:19"> One day the heavenly beings came to present themselves to the Lord, and Satan came with them to present himself to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Job 1:7; Genesis 16:8"> The Lord asked Satan, “What have you been doing?”<br />“Traveling around on earth, going here and there,” Satan replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 1:8; Job 27:5–6"> “Have you noticed my servant Job?” asked the Lord. “There's no one like him on earth, a man of integrity who does what is right, who respects God and avoids evil. And he has kept his integrity, even though you wanted me to harm him for no reason.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:8–10; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 6:25"> “Skin for skin!” Satan replied. “A man will give up everything to save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 1:11; Leviticus 24:15; Job 19:20–21"> But reach out and hurt his bones and his flesh, and he will definitely curse you to your face.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Luke 22:31–32; Job 1:12"> So the Lord said to Satan, “Very well, you have the power to do whatever you want to him, but don't kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 30:30; Isaiah 1:6"> Then Satan left the Lord's presence and inflicted Job with horrible skin sores from the bottom of his feet to the top of his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 42:6; Ezekiel 27:30; Jonah 3:6"> Job picked up a piece of broken pottery to scratch himself as he sat in ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 2:3; Malachi 3:14"> His wife said to him, “Are you still holding on to your integrity? Curse God and die!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Lamentations 3:38–41; Job 1:21–22"> But he told her, “You're talking just like any foolish woman would! Should we receive only good from God, and not evil?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Of course Job is not aware that his sufferings are the result of Satan's actions, not God's.">a</a></sup>Despite all this, Job said nothing sinful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Job 42:11; Jeremiah 49:7"> When three of Job's friends heard of all the trouble that had happened to him, they each left their homes: Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. They met together and went to comfort and console him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:30; Nehemiah 9:1"> When they saw Job from a distance they hardly recognized him. They broke out into loud wailing, tore their robes, and threw dust into the air over their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 50:10; Nehemiah 1:4; Ezekiel 3:15"> They sat down on the ground with him for seven days and seven nights. None of them said anything to him because they saw how badly he was suffering.</span>
</div>
