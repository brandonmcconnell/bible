<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Esther 4:16; Revelation 3:21"> Three days later Esther put on her royal robes and went and stood in the inner court of the king's palace, opposite the king's hall. The king was sitting on his royal throne in the king's hall, facing the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 8:4; Esther 4:11; Proverbs 21:1"> When the king saw Queen Esther standing in the inner court she won his approval so he acted favorably by holding out his scepter to her. So Esther went over and touched the end of the scepter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:23; Esther 7:2; Esther 5:6"> The king asked her, “What is it, Queen Esther? What do you want? I'll give it to you, as much as half my empire!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Proverbs 29:11; Esther 3:15"> Esther replied, “If it please Your Majesty, may the king and Haman come today to a dinner I have prepared for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 6:14"> “Bring Haman here at once so we can do what Esther has requested,” the king ordered. The king and Haman went to the dinner that Esther had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 9:12"> As they drank their wine, the king asked Esther, “What are you really asking for? It will be given to you. What do you want? You shall have it, as much as half my empire!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Esther answered, “This is what I'm asking for, and this is what I want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 7:3; Proverbs 16:9; Esther 8:5"> If the king looks on me favorably, and if it please Your Majesty to grant my request and do as I ask, may the king and Haman come to a dinner I will prepare for them. Tomorrow I will answer Your Majesty's question.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 3:5; Matthew 2:16; PSALMS 27:3"> When Haman left that day he was very happy and pleased with himself. But when he saw Mordecai at the palace gate and that he didn't stand up or tremble with fear before him, Haman was furious with Mordecai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 6:13; Ecclesiastes 7:9; 2 Samuel 13:22–23"> However, Haman controlled himself and he went home. There he invited his friends over. Once they and his wife Zeresh had gathered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 9:7–10; Esther 3:1; Luke 12:19–20"> Haman went into great detail about how much money and possessions he had, and how many children, and how the king had made him so important by promoting him above all the other nobles and officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; PSALMS 37:35–36; Job 20:5–8"> “Added to all that,” Haman went on, “I was the only other person Queen Esther invited to come to a dinner she had prepared for the king. I'm also invited by her to eat together with the king tomorrow.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:20; Job 18:4; Philippians 4:11–12"> Then he said, “But all of this is worthless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Worthless”: in other words it brought him no satisfaction.">a</a></sup>to me while I keep on seeing Mordecai the Jew sitting at the palace gate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 6:4; PSALMS 37:14; 1 Kings 21:7"> His wife Zeresh and his friends told him, “Arrange for a pole to be set up, fifty cubits high. Then, in the morning, go and ask the king to have Mordecai impaled on it. Afterwards, you'll be happy as you go with the king to the dinner.” Haman thought this was good advice, so he had the pole set up.</span>
