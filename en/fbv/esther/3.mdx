<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Esther 5:11; Daniel 6:2"> Some time after this, King Xerxes honored Haman, son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, giving him a position higher than all his fellow officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 3:5; PSALMS 15:4; Esther 5:9"> All the officials in royal employment bowed down and showed respect to Haman, for this is what the king had commanded. But Mordecai would not bow down and show respect to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esther 3:2; Exodus 1:17; Esther 2:19"> The king's officials asked Mordecai, “Why are you disobeying the king's command?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:10; Jonah 1:9; Daniel 6:20–28"> They talked to him about it day after day, but he refused to listen. So they told Haman about it to see if he would put up with what Mordecai was doing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“If he would put up with what Mordecai was doing”: alternatively, “if Mordecai would continue what he was doing.”">a</a></sup>for Mordecai had told them he was a Jew.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; Esther 5:9; Proverbs 21:24"> Haman was furious when he saw that Mordecai did not bow down and show respect to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 83:4; Revelation 12:12"> Having found out who Mordecai's people were, he dismissed the idea of just killing Mordecai. He decided to kill every Jew in the whole Persian Empire—all of Mordecai's people!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 6:15; Ezekiel 21:21–22; Esther 9:24–26"> In the twelfth year of King Xerxes, in the first month, the month of Nisan, “pur” (meaning a “lot”) was cast in Haman's presence to choose a day and month,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Casting lots was an ancient way of determining the most “favorable” time for a particular action, in this case Haman's plan to destroy the Jews.">b</a></sup>taking each day and each month one at a time. The lot fell on the twelfth month, the month of Adar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:12–15; Acts 16:20–21; Deuteronomy 4:27"> Haman went to King Xerxes and said, “There's a particular people living among others in many different places throughout the provinces of your empire who cut themselves off from everybody else. They have their own laws which are different from those of any other people, and what's more, they don't obey the king's laws. So it's not a good idea for Your Majesty to ignore them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Matthew 18:24"> If it please Your Majesty, issue a decree to destroy them, and I will personally contribute 10,000 silver talents to those who carry out the king's business to be placed in the royal treasury.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Esther 7:6"> The king took off his signet ring and handed it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A sign that the king accepted the proposal.">c</a></sup>to Haman, son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, the enemy of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Jeremiah 40:4; Jeremiah 26:14"> The king told Haman, “You can have the money, and do to the people whatever you want.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 21:8; Esther 1:22; Daniel 6:12"> On the thirteenth day of the first month the king's secretaries were summoned. A decree was issued in accordance with everything Haman demanded and sent to the king's chief officers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Chief officers”: literally, “satraps.”">d</a></sup>the governors of the different provinces and the nobles of the various peoples in the provinces. It was sent in the script of each province and in the language of every people, with the authorization of King Xerxes and sealed with his signet ring.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6; Esther 9:10; Esther 8:10–14"> Letters were sent by messenger to all the provinces in the king's empire with orders to destroy, kill, and annihilate all Jews, young and old, women and children, and confiscate their possessions, all in one day—the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, the month of Adar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 8:13–14"> A copy of the decree was to be issued as law in every province and publicized to the people so that they would be ready for the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esther 8:15; John 16:20; Proverbs 29:2"> By order of the king the messengers hurried on their way. The decree was also issued in the fortress of Susa. The king and Haman sat down for a drink while the people in city of Susa were very disturbed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Not only the Jews living there, but other ethnic/religious minorities must have been concerned about such a precedent.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
