<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> Two days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “on the third day” (by inclusive reckoning).">a</a></sup>later a wedding was held at Cana in Galilee, and Jesus' mother was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> Jesus and his disciples had also been invited to the wedding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> The wine ran out, so Jesus' mother told him, “They don't have any more wine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> “Mother, why should you involve me?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “What to me and to you?” (that is, “What has this got to do with me or you?”)">b</a></sup>My time hasn't come yet,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother told the servants, “Do whatever he tells you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Standing nearby were six stone jars used by the Jews for ceremonial washing, each one holding twenty to thirty gallons.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “two or three measures.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> “Fill the jars with water,” Jesus told them. So they filled them right up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> Then he told them, “Pour some out, and take it to the master of ceremonies.” So they took him some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; PSALMS 119:100"> The master of ceremonies didn't know where it had come from, only the servants knew. But when he tasted the water that had been turned to wine, he called the bridegroom over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> “Everyone serves out the best wine first,” he told him, “and once people have had plenty to drink, then they put out the cheaper wine. But you have kept the best wine till last!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This was the very first of Jesus' miraculous signs, and was performed in Cana of Galilee. Here he revealed his glory, and his disciples put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this Jesus left for Capernaum with his mother, brothers, and disciples where they stayed for a few days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> Since it was almost time for the Jewish Passover, Jesus went on to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> In the Temple he found people selling cattle, sheep, and doves; and money-changers sitting at their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> He made a whip out of cords and drove everyone out of the Temple, along with the sheep and cattle, scattering coins of the money-changers and turning over their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> He ordered the dove-sellers, “Take these things out of here! Don't turn my Father's house into a market!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 69:9; PSALMS 119:139"> His disciples remembered the Scripture that says, “My devotion for your house is like a fire burning inside me!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 69:9.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> The Jewish leaders reacted, asking him, “What right do you have to do this? Show us some miraculous sign to prove it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus replied, “Destroy this Temple, and in three days I'll raise it up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> “It took forty-six years to build this Temple, and you're going to raise it up in three days?” the Jewish leaders replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But the Temple Jesus was speaking of was his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> After he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered what he said, and so they believed in Scripture and Jesus' own words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> As a result of the miracles Jesus did while he was in Jerusalem during the Passover, many believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus did not trust himself to them, because he knew all about people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> He didn't need anyone to tell him about human nature for he knew the way people think.</span>
