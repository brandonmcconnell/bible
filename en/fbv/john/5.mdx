<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> After this there was a Jewish festival, so Jesus went to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Now near the Sheep Gate in Jerusalem is a pool called Bethzatha in Hebrew, with five porches beside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> Crowds of sick people were lying in these porches—those who were blind, lame, or paralyzed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This text is not in the earliest manuscripts and appears to have been added to explain verse 7. It is added here for information: “There they waited for the water to move, for an angel of the Lord would come down to the pool every so often and stir the water. Whoever got into the pool first after the water was stirred was healed of whatever disease they had.” It seems that this idea was what was believed by some at the time.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> One man who was there had been sick for thirty-eight years. Jesus looked at him, knowing he had been lying there for a long time, and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> “Do you want to be healed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> “Sir,” the sick man answered, “I don't have anyone to help me get into the pool when the water is stirred. While I'm trying to get there, someone always gets in before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> “Stand up, pick up your mat, and start walking!” Jesus told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> Immediately the man was healed. He picked up his mat and started walking. Now the day that this happened was the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> So the Jews said to the man who'd been healed, “This is the Sabbath! It's against the law to carry a mat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> “The man who healed me told me to pick up my mat and start walking,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> “Who's this person who told you to carry your mat and walk?” they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> However, the man who'd been healed didn't know who it was, for Jesus had slipped away into the surrounding crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; PSALMS 118:18"> Later on Jesus found the man in the Temple, and told him, “Look, now you've been healed. So stop sinning or something worse may happen to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man went and told the Jews it was Jesus who had healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> So the Jews started to harass Jesus because he was doing things on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> But Jesus told them, “My Father is still working, and so am I.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “My Father is always working, and I am working too.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> This was why the Jews tried even harder to kill him, for not only did he break the Sabbath but also called God his Father, making himself equal with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> So Jesus explained to them, “I tell you the truth, the Son can do nothing by himself; he can only do what he sees the Father doing. Whatever the Father does the Son does as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the Father loves the Son, and reveals to him everything he does; and the Father will show to him even more incredible things that will completely amaze you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For just as the Father gives life to those he resurrects from the dead, in the same way the Son also gives life to those that he wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> The Father judges no one. He has given to the Son all the authority to judge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> so that everyone may honor the Son just as they honor the Father. Anyone who doesn't honor the Son doesn't honor the Father who sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> I tell you the truth: those who follow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “hear.”">c</a></sup>what I say and trust the one who sent me have eternal life. They won't be condemned, but have gone from death to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> I tell you the truth: The time is coming—in fact it's here already—when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who hear will live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> Just as the Father has life-giving power in himself, so has he given the Son the same life-giving power in himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> The Father also granted the authority for judgment to him, for he is the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Don't be surprised at this, for the time is coming when all those in the grave will hear his voice</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and will rise again: those who have done good to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil to the resurrection of condemnation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Daniel 12:2.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I can do nothing by myself. I judge based on what I'm told,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Implying “told by God the Father.”,">e</a></sup>and my decision is right, for I'm not doing my own will but the will of the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> If I were to make claims about myself, such claims wouldn't be valid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> but someone else gives evidence about me, and I know what he says about me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> You asked John about me, and he told the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> but I don't need any human endorsement. I'm explaining this to you so you can be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> John was like a brightly-burning light, and you were willing to enjoy his light for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> But the evidence I'm giving is greater than John's. For I am doing the work that the Father gave me to do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> and this is the proof that the Father sent me. The Father who sent me, he himself speaks on my behalf. You've never heard his voice, and you've never seen what he looks like,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> and you don't accept what he says, because you don't trust in the one he sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> You search the Scriptures because you think that through them you'll gain eternal life. But the evidence they give is in support of me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; PSALMS 81:11"> And yet you don't want to come to me so that you might live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> I'm not looking for human approval</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> —I know you, and that you don't have God's love in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> For I've come to represent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “in the name of.”">f</a></sup>my Father, and you won't accept me; but if someone comes representing themselves, then you accept them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How can you trust in me when you look for praise from one another and yet you don't look for praise from the one true God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> But don't think I will be making accusations about you to the Father. It's Moses who is accusing you, the one in whom you place such confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> For if you really trusted Moses you would trust in me, because he wrote about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But since you don't trust what he said, why would you trust what I say?”</span>
</div>
