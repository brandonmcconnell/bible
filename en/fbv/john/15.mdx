<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 27:2–3; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> “I am the true vine, and my Father is the gardener.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 12:10–11; Matthew 15:13"> He cuts off every one of my branches that doesn't bear fruit. He prunes every branch that bears fruit so it can bear even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 17:17; Ephesians 5:26; 1 Peter 1:22"> You are already pruned and made clean<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word for pruning in this context is literally, “to cleanse.”">a</a></sup>through what I've told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Galatians 2:20; John 8:31"> Remain in me, and I will remain in you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Obviously the word “in” should be taken as “in connection with” as the rest of the verse makes clear.">b</a></sup>Just as a branch cannot produce fruit unless it remains part of the vine, so it is for you: you cannot bear fruit unless you remain in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> I'm the vine, you're the branches. Those who remain in me, and I in them, will produce plenty of fruit—for apart from me you can't do anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> Anyone who doesn't remain in me is like a branch that is thrown out and dries up. Such branches are gathered together, thrown into the fire and burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, then you can ask for whatever you want, and it will be given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:31; Matthew 5:16; Philippians 1:11"> My Father is glorified as you produce plenty of fruit, proving you are my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:23–24; John 17:26; 1 John 2:28"> As the Father loved me, so I have loved you. Remain in my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 3:21–24"> If you do what I say, you will remain in my love, just as I do what my Father says and remain in his love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> I've explained this to you so that my joy may be in you and that your joy may be complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> This is my command: love one another as I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 10:11; Ephesians 5:2; Romans 5:6–8"> There is no greater love than to give your life for your friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> You're my friends if you do what I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 25:14; 1 Corinthians 2:9–12; Matthew 13:11"> I don't call you servants any longer, for servants are not taken into their master's confidence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “a servant doesn't know what his master is doing.”">c</a></sup>I call you friends, for everything my Father told me I've explained to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> You didn't choose me, I chose you. I have given you the responsibility to go and produce lasting fruit. So the Father will give you whatever you ask in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> This is my command to you: love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> If the world hates you, remember that it hated me before it hated you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> If you were part of this world, it would love you as its own. But you're not part of the world, and I chose you out of the world—that's why the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Remember what I told you: servants aren't more important than their master. If they persecuted me, they will persecute you too. If they did what I told them, they would do what you tell them too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> But everything they do to you will be because of me, for they don't know the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> If I hadn't come and spoken to them, they wouldn't be guilty of sin—but now they have no excuse for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> Anyone who hates me hates my Father as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:36; John 14:9; John 7:31"> If I had not given them such a demonstration through things that no one had ever done before, they wouldn't be guilty of sin, but despite seeing all this they hated both me and my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 109:3; PSALMS 69:4; PSALMS 35:19"> But this just fulfilled what Scripture says, ‘They hated me for no reason at all.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 35:19or Psalms 69:5.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 16:13–15"> But I will send you the Comforter from the Father. When he comes, he will give evidence about me. He is the Spirit of truth who comes from the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> You will also give evidence about me because you were with me from the beginning.”</span>
