</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 13:1; John 18:7; PSALMS 27:3"> Jesus knew everything that was going to happen to him. He went to meet them, and asked, “Who are you looking for?”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 19:19; Matthew 2:23"> “Are you Jesus of Nazareth?” they asked.<br />“I am,” Jesus told them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jesus' words are not only an affirmation of his identity but also echo the name of God from Exodus.">a</a></sup>Judas the betrayer was standing with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 27:2; PSALMS 70:2–3; 2 Kings 1:9–15"> When Jesus said “I am,” they fell back and dropped to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 18:4"> So he asked them again, “Who are you looking for?”<br />“Are you Jesus of Nazareth?” they asked again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:28; Matthew 26:56; 2 Corinthians 12:9"> “I already told you I am,” Jesus replied. “So if I'm the one you're looking for, let these others go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:12"> These words fulfilled what he had previously said: “I have not lost any of those you gave me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 14:47; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> Then Simon Peter drew a sword and struck Malchus, the high priest's servant, cutting off his right ear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 20:22; Matthew 26:39; John 20:17"> Jesus told Peter, “Put the sword away! Do you think<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Do you think”—implied.">b</a></sup>I shouldn't drink the cup the Father has given me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:57; John 18:3; Acts 21:37"> Then the soldiers, their commander, and the Jewish guards arrested Jesus and tied his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:24; John 11:51; Matthew 26:3"> First they took him to Annas, the father-in-law of Caiaphas, the current high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:49–52"> Caiaphas was the one who had told the Jews, “It's better that one man die for the people.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 11:50.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:54; Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:58–68"> Simon Peter followed Jesus, and another disciple did so too. This disciple was well-known to the high priest, and so he entered the high priest's courtyard with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:69; Mark 14:66–68; Luke 22:55–57"> Peter had to remain outside by the door. So the other disciple who was known to the high priest went and spoke to the servant girl watching the door and brought Peter inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:69–70; John 21:15; Luke 22:56–57"> The girl asked Peter, “Aren't you one of that man's disciples?”<br />“Me? No, I'm not,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:54; John 18:25; Mark 14:67"> It was cold, and the servants and guards were standing by a fire they had made, warming themselves. Peter went and stood with them, warming himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:55–65; Luke 20:20; Luke 22:63–71"> Then the chief priest questioned Jesus about his disciples and what he had been teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:26; Isaiah 48:16; Matthew 4:23"> “I've spoken openly to everyone,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “to the world.”">d</a></sup>Jesus replied. “I always taught in the synagogues and in the Temple where all the Jewish people meet. I haven't said anything in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:12–13; Luke 22:67"> So why are you questioning me? Ask the people who heard me what I told them. They know what I said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:3; Mark 14:65; Luke 22:63–64"> When he said this, one of the guards standing nearby slapped Jesus, saying, “Is that any way to speak to the high priest?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:20–23; 2 Corinthians 10:1; Matthew 5:39"> Jesus replied, “If I said something wrong, tell everyone what was wrong with it. But if what I said was right, why did you hit me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:13; Matthew 26:57"> Annas sent him, his hands still tied, to Caiaphas the high priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 14:67–72; John 18:17–18; Genesis 18:15"> As Simon Peter stood warming himself by the fire, the people there asked him, “Aren't you one of his disciples?”<br />Peter denied it and said, “No, I'm not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 18:1; John 18:10; Matthew 26:73"> One of the high priest's servants, a relative of the man whose ear Peter had cut off, asked Peter, “Didn't I see you in the olive grove with him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:71–72; Luke 22:60–62"> Peter denied it again, and immediately a cock crowed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 18:33; John 19:9; Matthew 27:27"> Early in the morning they took Jesus from Caiaphas to the palace of the Roman governor. The Jewish leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Implied.">e</a></sup>didn't enter the palace because if they did they would become ceremonially unclean and they wanted to be able to eat the Passover meal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 27:11–14"> So Pilate came out to meet them. “What charge are you bringing against this man?” he asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 3:13; Luke 20:19–26; Mark 10:33"> “If he wasn't a criminal, we would not have handed him over to you,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:6–7; John 19:15; Acts 25:18–20"> “Then you take him and judge him according to your law,” Pilate told them.<br />“We're not permitted to execute anyone,” the Jews answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; Matthew 20:19; Matthew 26:2"> This fulfilled what Jesus had said about how he would die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 23:3–4; John 18:37; John 19:3"> Pilate went back into the governor's palace. He summoned Jesus, and asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> “Did you think of this question yourself, or did others talk to you about me?” Jesus responded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 18:14–16; Nehemiah 4:2; Romans 3:1–2"> “Am I a Jew?” Pilate countered. “It was your own people and high priests who handed you over to me. What is it that you've done?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 17:20–21; Colossians 1:12–14; Romans 14:17"> Jesus answered, “My kingdom is not of this world. If it was of this world, my subjects would fight to keep me from being handed over to the Jews. But my kingdom is not from here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; John 8:47; John 8:14"> Then Pilate asked, “So you are a king, then?”<br />“You say that I'm a king,” Jesus replied. “The reason why I was born and I came to the world was to give evidence for the truth. All those who accept the truth pay attention to what I say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:4; Luke 23:4; John 19:6"> “What is truth?” Pilate asked.<br />Having said this Pilate went back out to the Jews and told them, “I find him not guilty of any crime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:15–18; Luke 23:16–23; Mark 15:6–14"> However, it is customary for me to release a prisoner to you at Passover. Do you want me to release the King of the Jews?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Mark 15:15; Matthew 27:16"> “No, not him! We want Barabbas instead!” they shouted back. Barabbas was a rebel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Usually translated “robber.” It may be that Barabbas had taken part in some insurrection.">f</a></sup></span>
