<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> Early on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Sunday.">a</a></sup>while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene went to the tomb and saw that the stone had been moved from the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> So she ran to tell Simon Peter and the other disciple, the one Jesus loved, “They've taken the Lord out of the tomb, and we don't know where they've put him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Then Peter and the other disciple went to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The two of them were running together, but the other disciple ran faster and reached the tomb first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> He bent down, and looking in he saw the grave-clothes lying there, but he didn't go in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Then Simon Peter arrived after him and went right into the tomb. He saw the linen grave-clothes lying there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and that the cloth that had been on Jesus' head wasn't with the other grave-clothes but had been folded and placed on its own.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then the other disciple who had reached the tomb first went inside as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> He looked around and believed it was true<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That Jesus had risen from the dead.">b</a></sup>—for up till then they hadn't understood the Scripture that Jesus had to rise from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> Then the disciples went back to where they were staying.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Clearly not back to their homes in Galilee, but to whatever lodging they had found in Jerusalem.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary stayed outside the tomb crying. As she was crying, she bent down and looked into the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> She saw two angels in white, one sitting at the head and the other at the foot of where Jesus' body had been lying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> “Why are you crying?” they asked her.<br />She answered, “Because they've taken my Lord away, and I don't know where they've put him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> After she'd said this, she turned round and saw Jesus standing there, but she didn't realize it was Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> “Why are you crying?” he asked her. “Who are you looking for?”<br />Assuming he was the gardener, she said to him, “Sir, if you've taken him away, tell me where you've put him so I can go and get him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus said to her, “Mary!”<br />She turned to him and said, “Rabboni,” which means “Teacher” in Hebrew.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> “Don't hold onto me,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Meaning “don't detain me by holding me back.”">d</a></sup>Jesus said to her, “for I haven't yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brothers and tell them I am ascending to my Father and your Father, my God and your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> So Mary Magdalene went and told the disciples, “I've seen the Lord,” and she explained to them what he had said to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> That evening, on the first day of the week, as the disciples were meeting together behind locked doors because they were afraid of the Jews, Jesus came and stood among them and said, “May you have peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> After this greeting he showed them his hands and his side. The disciples were full of joy to see the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> “May you have peace!” Jesus told them again. “In the same way the Father sent me, so I'm sending you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> Saying this, he breathed on them, and told them, “Receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> If you forgive anyone's sins, they are forgiven; if you hold them unforgiven, unforgiven they remain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> One of the twelve disciples, Thomas, who was called the Twin, wasn't with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> So the other disciples told him, “We've seen the Lord.”<br />But he replied, “I won't believe it unless I see the nail marks in his hands and put my finger in them, and put my hand in his side.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> One week later the disciples were together inside the house; and Thomas was with them. The doors were closed, and Jesus came and stood among them.<br />“May you have peace!” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he said to Thomas, “Put your finger here, and look at my hands. Put your hand in the wound on my side. Stop doubting and trust in me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> “My Lord and my God!” Thomas responded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> “You trust in me because you've seen me,” Jesus told him. “Happy are those that haven't seen me yet still trust in me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Jesus did many other miraculous signs while he was with his disciples that are not recorded in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> But these are written down here so that you may trust that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and that by trusting in him as he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “in his name.”">e</a></sup>you will have life.</span>
</div>
