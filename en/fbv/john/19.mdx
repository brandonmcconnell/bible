<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then Pilate took Jesus and had him flogged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> Soldiers made a crown of thorns and placed it on his head, and put a purple robe on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> Time and again they went up to him and said, “Hail, King of the Jews!” and slapped him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Pilate went outside once more and said to them, “I'm bringing him out here to you so you'll know I find him not guilty of any crime.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> Then Jesus came out wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe. “Look, here's the man,” said Pilate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When the chief priests and the guards saw Jesus, they shouted out, “Crucify him! Crucify him!”<br />“You take him and crucify him,” Pilate answered. “I find him not guilty.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jewish leaders replied, “We have a law, and according to that law he must die because he claimed to be the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When Pilate heard this he was more afraid than ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; PSALMS 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and he went back into the governor's palace. He asked Jesus, “Where do you come from?” But Jesus didn't respond.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> “Are you refusing to talk to me?” Pilate said to him. “Don't you realize that I have the power to have you released or to crucify you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> “You would have no power over me unless it had been given to you from above,” Jesus answered. “Therefore the one who handed me over to you is guilty of the greater sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> Because of this statement Pilate tried to set Jesus free, but the Jewish leaders shouted, “If you set this man free you're not Caesar's friend. Anyone who sets himself up as a king is rebelling against Caesar.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> When Pilate heard this, he brought Jesus outside and sat down on the judgment seat at a place called Stone Pavement (Gabbatha in Hebrew).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> It was around noon on the preparation day before the Passover.<br />“Look, here is your king,” he said to the Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> “Kill him! Kill him! Crucify him!” they screamed out.<br />“Do you want me to crucify your king?” Pilate asked.<br />“The only king we have is Caesar,” the chief priests replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> So he handed Jesus over to them to be crucified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> They led Jesus away, who carried his own cross, and went out to the “Place of the Skull,” (Golgotha in Hebrew).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> They crucified him there, and two others with him: one on either side, with Jesus between them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> Pilate had a notice made and placed on the cross which said, “Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> Many people read the notice because the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city, and it was written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> Then the chief priests came to Pilate and asked him, “Don't write ‘the King of the Jews,’ but ‘This man said I am the King of the Jews.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 76:10; PSALMS 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate replied, “What I have written I have written.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> When the soldiers had crucified Jesus they took his clothes and divided them in four so that each soldier had his share. There was also his robe, made without seams, woven in one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> So they said to each other, “Let's not tear it, but let's decide who will have it by rolling dice.” This fulfilled the Scripture that says, “They divided my garments among them and rolled dice for my clothing.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 22:18.">a</a></sup>So that is what the soldiers did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> Standing near the cross were Jesus' mother and his mother's sister, Mary the wife of Clopas and Mary Magdalene.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It is not clear whether there were three women present or four. Some believe Mary's sister is the same person as Mary, wife of Clopas.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> When Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple he loved standing beside her, he said to his mother, “Mother,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “woman,” but this does not work in English.">c</a></sup>this is your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then he said to the disciple, “This is your mother.” From then on the disciple took her into his home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; PSALMS 22:15; John 19:30"> Jesus now realized that he had finished all that he had come to do. In fulfillment of Scripture, he said, “I'm thirsty.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 69:21.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> A jar of wine vinegar was standing there, so they soaked a sponge in the vinegar, put it on a hyssop stick, and held it to his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> After he'd had the vinegar, Jesus said, “It's finished!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Finished”: this can also mean “completed” or “fulfilled.”">e</a></sup>Then he bowed his head and breathed his last.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> It was preparation day, and the Jewish leaders didn't want to leave the bodies on the crosses during the Sabbath day (in fact this was a special Sabbath), so they asked Pilate to break the legs, so that the bodies could be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> So the soldiers came and broke the legs of the first one and then the other of those crucified with Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> but when they came to Jesus they saw he was already dead, so they didn't break his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> However, one of the soldiers stuck a spear into his side, and blood mixed with water came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> The one who saw this has given this evidence, and his evidence is true. He's certain that what he says is true so you can believe it too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> It happened like this so Scripture would be fulfilled: “Not one of his bones will be broken,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 34:20.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; PSALMS 22:16–17"> and as another Scripture says, “They will look at the one they pierced.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Referring to Exodus 12:46, Numbers 9:12, or Psalms 34:20.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> After this Joseph of Arimathea asked Pilate if he could take down the body of Jesus, and Pilate gave his permission. Joseph was a disciple of Jesus, but in secret because he feared the Jews. So Joseph came and took the body away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Songs 4:14; John 3:1–21"> He was joined by Nicodemus, the man who had first visited Jesus at night. He brought with him a mixture of myrrh and aloes weighing about seventy-five pounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> They took Jesus' body and wrapped it in linen cloth together with the mixture of spices, in accordance with Jewish burial customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> There was a garden near where Jesus was crucified; and in the garden was a new, unused tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> Since it was the Jewish day of preparation and the tomb was nearby, they laid Jesus to rest there.</span>
</div>
</div>
