<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> After this, Jesus spent his time going from place to place in Galilee. He did not want to do so in Judea because the Jews were out to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> But as it was almost time for the Jewish festival of the Tabernacles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> his brothers told him, “You ought to leave and go to Judea so your followers will be able to see what miracles you can do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> No one who wants to be famous keeps what they do hidden. If you can do such miracles, then show yourself to the world!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> For even his own brothers really didn't believe in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Jesus told them, “This is not my time to go, not yet; but you can go whenever you want, for any time's the right time for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world has no reason to hate you, but it does hate me, because I make it clear that its ways are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> You go on to the festival. I'm not going to this festival because this is not the right time for me, not yet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> After saying this he stayed behind in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> After his brothers left to go to the festival, Jesus also went, but not openly—he stayed out of sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> Now at the festival the Jewish leaders were searching for him and kept on asking, “Where is he?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> Many people in the crowds were complaining about him. Some said, “He's a good man,” while others argued, “No! He deceives people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> But no one dared to speak openly about him because they were afraid of what the Jewish leaders would do to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> When the festival was halfway through, Jesus went to the Temple and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> The Jewish leaders were very surprised, and asked, “How does this man have so much learning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In the sense of religious education.">a</a></sup>when he hasn't been educated?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus answered, “My teaching is not from me but from the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> Anyone who chooses to follow what God wants will know if my teaching comes from God or if I'm only speaking for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> Those who speak for themselves want to glorify themselves, but someone who glorifies the one who sent him is truthful and not deceitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Moses gave you the law, didn't he? Yet none of you keeps the law! Why are you trying to kill me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> “You're demon-possessed!” the crowd replied. “No one's trying to kill you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> “I did one miracle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="On the Sabbath, referring back to what happened in 5:1-9.">b</a></sup>and you're all shocked by it,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> “However, because Moses told you to circumcise—not that it really came from Moses, but from your forefathers before him—you perform circumcision on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If you circumcise on the Sabbath to make sure that the law of Moses isn't broken, why are you angry with me for healing someone on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Don't judge by appearances; decide what is right!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Then some of those from Jerusalem began wondering, “Isn't this the one they're trying to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> But see how openly he's speaking, and they're saying nothing to him. Do you think the authorities believe he's the Messiah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But that's not possible because we know where he comes from. When the Messiah comes, nobody will know where he's from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> While he was teaching in the Temple, Jesus called out in a loud voice, “So you think you know me and where I'm from? However, I did not come for my own sake. The one who sent me is true. You don't know him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> but I know him, for I come from him, and he sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> So they tried to arrest him, but no one laid a hand on him because his time had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> However, many of the crowd did put their trust in him. “When the Messiah appears, will he do more miraculous signs than this man has done?” they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> When the Pharisees heard the crowd whispering this about him, they and the chief priests sent guards to arrest Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Then Jesus told the people, “I'll be with you just a little longer, but then I'll return to the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> You'll search for me but you won't find me; and you can't come where I'm going.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> The Jews said to each other, “Where could he be going that we couldn't find him? Is he planning to go to those scattered among the foreigners,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “Greeks.”">c</a></sup>and teach the foreigners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What does he mean by saying, ‘You'll search for me but you won't find me; and you can't come where I'm going’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> On the last and most important day of the festival, Jesus stood up and shouted out in a loud voice, “If you're thirsty, come to me and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> If you trust in me, you will have streams of life-giving water flowing out from within you, as Scripture says.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The closest reference appears to be Song of Songs 4:15.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> He was referring to the Spirit that those who trusted in him would later receive. The Spirit hadn't been given yet because Jesus hadn't yet been glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> When they heard these words, some of the people said, “This man is definitely the Prophet!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 6:14.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others said, “He is the Messiah!” Still others said, “How can the Messiah come from Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Doesn't Scripture say that the Messiah comes from David's lineage, and from David's home town of Bethlehem?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referring to Micah 5:2.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So the crowd had a strong difference of opinion about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> Some wanted to arrest him, but nobody laid a hand on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> Then the guards returned to the chief priests and the Pharisees who asked them, “Why didn't you bring him in?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> “Nobody ever spoke like this man does,” the guards replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> “Have you been fooled too?” the Pharisees asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> “Has a single one of the rulers or Pharisees believed in him? No!</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this crowd knows nothing about teachings of the law—they're damned anyway!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus, who had previously gone to meet Jesus, was one of them and asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> “Does our law condemn a man without a hearing and without finding out what he actually did?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> “So you're a Galilean as well, are you?” they replied. “Check the Scriptures and you'll discover that no prophet comes from Galilee!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="PSALMS 33:10; Job 5:12–13; PSALMS 76:10"> Then they all went home,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The following section (7:53-8:11) does not appear in this location in all manuscripts. However, it surely represents an authentic account.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
