<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> A man named Lazarus was sick. <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> Mary was the one who had anointed the Lord with perfume and wiped his feet with her hair. It was her brother Lazarus who was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> So the sisters sent a message to Jesus: “Lord, your close friend is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Jesus heard the news he said, “The end result of this sickness will not be death. Through this God's glory will be revealed so that the Son of God may be glorified.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Even though Jesus loved Martha, Mary, and Lazarus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> and had heard that Lazarus was sick, he remained where he was for two more days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then he told the disciples, “Let's return to Judea.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples replied, “Rabbi, just a few days ago the Jews were trying to stone you. Do you really want to go back there now?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> “Aren't there twelve hours in a day?” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> “If you walk during the day you don't stumble because you can see where you're going by the light of this world. But if you walk during the night you stumble because you have no light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> After telling them this, he said, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I'm going to go there and wake him up!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The disciples said, “Lord, if he's sleeping then he'll get better.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Jesus had been referring to the death of Lazarus, but the disciples thought he meant actual sleep.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In the New Testament death is represented as a sleep.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> So Jesus told them plainly, “Lazarus is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> For your sake I'm glad I wasn't there, because now you will be able to trust in me. Let's go and see him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Thomas, the Twin, said to his fellow-disciples, “Let's go too so we can die with him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Meaning Jesus.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> When he arrived, Jesus learned that Lazarus had been in the tomb for four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Bethany was just two miles from Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> and many Jews had come to console Mary and Martha at the loss of their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> When Martha found out that Jesus was coming, she went to meet him, but Mary stayed at home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if you'd been here, my brother wouldn't have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> But I'm certain that even now God will give you whatever you ask.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus told her, “Your brother will rise again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> “I know he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day,” Martha answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said, “I am the resurrection and the life. Those who trust in me will live, even though they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> All who live in me and trust in me will never die. Do you believe this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> “Yes, Lord,” she replied, “I believe you are the Messiah, the Son of God, the one expected to come to this world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> When she had said this, she went and told her sister Mary in private, “The Teacher's here, and asking to see you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; PSALMS 27:8; Song of Songs 3:1–4"> As soon as she heard, Mary quickly got up and went to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Jesus hadn't arrived in the village yet. He was still at the place where Martha had met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Jews who had been comforting Mary in the home saw how she'd got up quickly and left. So they followed her, thinking she was going to the tomb to weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> When Mary arrived at the place where Jesus was and saw him, she fell at his feet and said, “Lord, if you'd been here, my brother wouldn't have died.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus saw her crying, and the Jews who had come with her crying as well, he was very troubled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word used here expresses intense emotion, even anger. Also used in verse 38.">d</a></sup>and upset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> “Where have you laid him?” he asked.<br />They replied, “Lord, come and see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Then Jesus cried too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> “See how much he loved him,” the Jews said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said, “If he could open the eyes of a blind man, couldn't he have kept Lazarus from dying?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Very troubled, Jesus went to the tomb. It was a cave with a large stone placed at the entrance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; PSALMS 49:7"> “Remove the stone,” Jesus told them.<br />But Martha, the dead man's sister, said, “Lord, by now there will be a terrible smell, for he's been dead for four days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> “Didn't I tell you that if you trusted me you would see God's glory?” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; PSALMS 123:1"> So they removed the stone. Jesus looked heavenwards, and said, “Father, thank you for listening to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> I know you always listen to me. I said this because of the crowd standing here so that they will believe that you sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> After saying this, Jesus shouted, “Lazarus, come out!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> The dead man came out, his hands and feet bound with strips of linen, and with a cloth around his face.<br />“Unbind him and set him free,” Jesus told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Consequently many of the Jews who had come to comfort Mary and who saw what Jesus did put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But others went to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> Then the chief priests and the Pharisees called a meeting of the ruling council. “What shall we do?” they asked. “This man is doing many miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we allow him to continue, everybody will believe in him, and then the Romans will destroy both the Temple and our status as a nation.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “the place and the nation.”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> “You don't understand anything!” said Caiaphas, who was high priest that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> “Can't you see that it's better for you that one man die for the people so that the whole nation won't be destroyed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> He didn't say this on his own behalf, but as chief priest that year he was prophesying that Jesus would die for the nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> And this was not just for the Jewish nation, but for all the scattered children of God so that they might be gathered together and be made into one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> From that time on they made plans as to how they might kill Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> So Jesus did not travel openly among the Jews but went to a town called Ephraim in the region near the desert and stayed there with his disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> It was nearly time for the Jewish Passover, and many people went from the countryside to Jerusalem to purify<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="By a series of religious rituals.">f</a></sup>themselves for the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> People were looking for Jesus and talking about him as they stood in the Temple. “What do you think?” they asked each other. “Isn't he coming to the festival?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> The chief priests and the Pharisees had given orders that anyone who knew where Jesus was should report it so they could arrest him.</span>
</div>
