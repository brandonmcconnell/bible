<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> In the beginning the Word already was.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, the Word existed from eternity past. The concept of the Word means more than letters making up a word: it is the divine mind, the expression of God, the active aspect of divinity that speaks into existence as in Genesis 1:1.">a</a></sup>The Word was with God, and the Word was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> From the very beginning he was with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> Everything came into being through him; nothing came into being without him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> In him was life, the life that was the light of everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not extinguished it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word in the original can also mean “overpowered” or “understood.”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> God sent a man named John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> He came as a witness to explain about the light so that everyone might believe through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> He himself was not the light, but he came to witness to the light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> The true light was coming into the world to give light to everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, and though the world was made through him, the world didn't know who he was.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “didn't recognize him.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came to his own people, but they didn't accept him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “He came to his own home but his own people did not welcome him.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> But to all those who accepted him and trusted in him, he gave the right to become God's children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> These are the children born not in the usual way, not as the result of human desire or a father's decision, but born of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> The Word became human and lived among us, and we saw his glory, the glory of the Father's one and only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “only begotten.” This refers to position and uniqueness rather than birth.">e</a></sup>Son, full of grace and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John gave his testimony about him, shouting out to the people, “This is the one I was telling you about when I said, ‘The one who is coming after me is more important than me, for before I ever existed he already was.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> We have all been recipients of his generous nature, one gracious gift after another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> While no one has ever seen God, God the one and only, who is close beside the Father, has shown us what God is like.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “has made him known.”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> This is what John publicly stated when the Jewish leaders sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, “Who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> John declared plainly and clearly without hesitation, “I am not the Messiah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> “So then, who are you?” they asked. “Elijah?”<br />“No, I'm not,” he answered.<br />“Are you the Prophet?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="In Jewish thought a special prophet was expected before the End.">g</a></sup><br />“No,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> “Well, who are you, then?” they asked. “We have to give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> “I am a voice calling in the desert, ‘Make the Lord's way straight!’” he said, using the words of the prophet Isaiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 40:3.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> The priests and Levites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Priests and Levites”: implied from verse 19.">i</a></sup>sent by the Pharisees</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> asked him, “Why then are you baptizing, if you're not the Messiah, or Elijah, or the Prophet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John replied, “I baptize with water, but standing among you is someone you don't know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> He is coming after me, but I am not even worthy to untie his sandals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> This all happened in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> The next day John saw Jesus approaching him, and said, “Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This is the one I was talking about when I said, ‘A man who is coming after me is more important than me, for before I ever existed he already was.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> I didn't know myself who he was, but I came baptizing with water so that he could be revealed to Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> John gave his evidence about him, saying, “I saw the Spirit descend from heaven like a dove and rest upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> I wouldn't have known him except he who sent me to baptize with water had told me, ‘The one you see the Spirit descend to and rest upon, he is the one who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; John 1:49; PSALMS 89:26–27"> I saw it happen and I declare that this is the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> The next day John was standing there with two of his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> He saw Jesus passing by, and said, “Look! This is the Lamb of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> When the two disciples heard what he said they went and followed Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> Jesus turned round and saw them following him. “What are you looking for?” he asked them.<br />“Rabbi (which means ‘Teacher’), where are you staying?” they asked in reply.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> “Come and see,” he told them. So they went with him and saw where he was staying. It was about four p.m., and they spent the rest of the day with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of these two disciples who had heard what John said and followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; PSALMS 89:20"> He went at once to find his brother Simon and told him, “We've found the Messiah!” (which means “Christ”).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Both Messiah and Christ mean “the Anointed One.”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> He took him to Jesus. Looking directly at Simon, Jesus said, “You are Simon, son of John. But now you will be called Cephas,” (which means “Peter”).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Cephas and Peter both mean “rock” or “stone.”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> The next day Jesus decided to go to Galilee. Jesus found Philip there, and told him, “Follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> Philip was from Bethsaida, the same town that Andrew and Peter came from.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael and told him, “We've found the one that Moses wrote about in the law and that the prophets did too—Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> “From Nazareth? Can anything good come from there?” Nathanael wondered.<br />“Just come and see,” Philip replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; PSALMS 32:2; PSALMS 73:1"> As Jesus saw Nathanael approaching, he said about him, “Look, here's a true Israelite! There's nothing false about him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; PSALMS 139:1–2"> “How do you know who I am?” Nathanael asked.<br />“I saw you there under the fig tree, before Philip called you,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> “Rabbi, you are the Son of God, the king of Israel!” Nathaniel exclaimed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> “You believe this just because I told you I saw you under the fig tree?” Jesus replied. “You'll get to see much more than that!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> Then Jesus said, “I tell you the truth, you will all see heaven open, and the angels of God going up and down on the Son of man.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Referring to Jacob's experience in Genesis 28:12, with the term “Son of man” replacing the word “ladder.”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
