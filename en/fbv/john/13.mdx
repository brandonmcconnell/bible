<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> It was the day before the Passover festival, and Jesus knew that the time had come to leave this world and go to his Father. He had loved those in the world who were his own, and he now demonstrated his complete love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Complete love.” The word used here means both “complete” and “end,” so an alternative translation would be “he loved them to the end,” which would also be appropriate in context. However, Jesus demonstration of love which includes the cross is really to show the full extent of God's love for us.">a</a></sup>for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> It was during supper, and the devil had already put the thought of betraying Jesus into the mind of Judas, son of Simon Iscariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> Jesus knew that the Father had placed everything under his authority,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “into his hands.”">b</a></sup>and that he had come from God and was going back to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> So Jesus stood up from eating supper, took off his robe and wrapped a towel around his waist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; PSALMS 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> He poured water into a basin and began to wash his disciples' feet, drying them with the towel he had wrapped around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> He came to Simon Peter, who asked him, “Lord, are you going to wash my feet?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus replied, “You won't realize what I'm doing for you now. But one day you'll understand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> “No!” Peter protested. “You'll never wash my feet!” Jesus replied, “If I don't wash you, you have no part with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 51:7; PSALMS 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> “Then, Lord, wash not just my feet but my hands and my head as well!” Simon Peter exclaimed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus responded, “Those who have had a bath only need to wash their feet and then they're clean all over. You are clean—but not all of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For he knew who was betraying him. That's why he said, “Not all of you are clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> After Jesus had washed their feet, he put his robe back on, and sat down. “Do you understand what I've done to you?” he asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> “You call me ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord,’ and rightly so, for that's who I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> So if I, your Teacher and Lord, washed your feet, you ought to wash one another's feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> I have set you an example, so you should do just as I did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> I tell you the truth, servants are not more important than their master, and the one sent is not greater than the sender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> Now that you understand these things, you will be blessed if you do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> I'm not talking about all of you—I know those I have chosen. But this is to fulfill Scripture: ‘The one who shares my food has turned against me.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 41:9.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> I'm telling you this now, before it happens, so when it does happen you will be convinced that I am who I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> I tell you the truth, whoever welcomes anyone I send welcomes me; and whoever welcomes me, welcomes the one who sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> After he had said this, Jesus was deeply troubled, and declared: “I tell you the truth, one of you is going to betray me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples looked at each other, wondering which of them Jesus was talking about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> The disciple whom Jesus loved<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Usually understood as John referring to himself. (See also 20:2, 21:7, 21:20.)">d</a></sup>was sitting next to Jesus at the table, leaning close to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> Simon Peter motioned to him to ask Jesus which one he was talking about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> So he leaned over to Jesus and asked, “Lord, who is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus replied, “It's the one to whom I will give a piece of bread after I have dipped it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> After dipping the piece of bread, he gave it to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot. Once Judas had taken the bread, Satan entered him. “What you're going to do, do it quickly,” Jesus told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> No one at the table understood what Jesus meant by this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> Since Judas was in charge of the money, some of them thought that Jesus was telling him to go and buy what was needed for the Passover festival, or to donate something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> Judas left immediately after he'd taken the piece of bread, and went out into the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> After he'd left, Jesus said, “Now the Son of man is glorified, and through him God is glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> If God is glorified through him, then God will glorify the Son himself, and will glorify him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> My children, I will be with you only a little while longer. You will look for me, but I'm telling you now just as I told the Jews: you cannot come where I'm going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> I am giving you a new command: Love one another. Love one another in the same way I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> If you love one another you will prove to everyone that you are my disciples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter asked him, “Where are you going, Lord?” Jesus answered, “You can't follow me now where I'm going. You will follow me later.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> “Lord, why can't I follow you now?” Peter asked. “I'll lay down my life for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> “Are you really ready to die for me? I tell you the truth: before the cock crows you will deny me three times,” Jesus replied.</span>
