<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; John 13:36; Hebrews 13:14"> In my Father's house there are many rooms. If this wasn't so I would have told you. I'm going to prepare a place for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; John 12:26; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Once I've gone and prepared a place for you, I will come again and take you back with me, so that you can be there with me too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:9; John 16:28; John 13:3"> You know the way to where I'm going.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 11:16; Mark 9:19; John 20:25–28"> Thomas said to him, “Lord, we don't know where you're going. How can we know the way?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:12; John 10:9; Ephesians 2:18"> Jesus replied, “I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; John 8:19; Luke 10:22"> If you had known me, you would know my Father as well. From now on, you do know him and you have seen him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:18–23; Revelation 22:3–5; John 1:43–46"> Philip said, “Lord, show us the Father, and we'll be convinced.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 12:45; John 14:7"> Jesus replied, “Have I been with you such a long time, Philip, and yet you still don't know me? Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father. How can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 5:19; John 10:38; John 10:30"> Don't you believe that I live in the Father and the Father lives in me? The words I speak are not mine; it's the Father living in me who is doing his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:38; John 10:25; John 5:36"> Believe me when I tell you that I live in the Father, and the Father lives in me, or at least believe because of the evidence provided by all that I've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:21; Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19"> I tell you the truth, anyone who trusts in me will do the same things I am doing. In fact they will do even greater things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greater in extent.">b</a></sup>because I am going to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:7; Mark 11:24; Matthew 7:7"> I'll do whatever you ask in my name, so that the Father may be glorified through the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whatever you ask for in my name, I will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> If you love me, you will keep my commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Comforter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Comforter. The word in the original (transliterated into English as “Paraclete”) refers to one who is called to “come alongside” and help. See also 14:26, 15:26, 16:7, and 1 John 2:1.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> the Spirit of truth, who will always be with you. The world cannot accept him because it isn't looking for him and doesn't know him. But you know him because he lives with you and will be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; PSALMS 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> I will not abandon you like orphans: I will come back to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> Soon the world will not see me anymore, but you will see me. Because I live, you will live too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> On that day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring to verse 18, primarily referencing his coming to them after his resurrection.">d</a></sup>you will know that I live in the Father, you live in me, and I live in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> Those who keep my commands are the ones who love me, those who love me will be loved by my Father. I will love them too, and will reveal myself to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> Judas (not Iscariot) responded, “Lord, why would you reveal yourself to us and not to the world?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jesus replied, “Those who love me will do as I say. My Father will love them, and we will come and make our home with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> Those who don't love me don't do what I say. These words don't come from me, they come from the Father who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> I'm explaining this to you while I'm still here with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> But when the Father sends the Comforter, the Holy Spirit, in my place,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “in my name.” This phrase can mean “with my authority,” “through me,” “for me,” “belonging to me” etc. It really is a way of referring to the person and their character.">e</a></sup>he will teach you everything and remind you of all that I told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> Peace I leave with you; my peace I'm giving you. The peace I give you is nothing like what the world gives. Don't be anxious, and don't be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> You've heard me tell you, ‘I am going away, but I will come back to you.’ If you really loved me, you would be happy because I'm going to the Father, for the Father is greater than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> I've explained this to you now before it happens so that when it does happen you will be convinced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> I can't talk to you much longer, for the prince of this world is coming. He has no power to control me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> but I'm doing what my Father told me to do so that the world will know that I love the Father. Now get up. Let's go.”</span>
