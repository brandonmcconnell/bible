<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> but Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> Early in the morning he returned to the Temple where many people gathered around him and he sat down and taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The religious teachers and the Pharisees brought in a woman caught committing adultery and made her stand before everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They said to Jesus, “Teacher, this woman was caught in the act of adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> Now in the Law Moses commanded us to stone such women. What do you say?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> They said this to try and trap Jesus so they could condemn him. But Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> They kept on demanding an answer, so he stood up and told them, “Whichever one of you has never sinned may throw the first stone at her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he bent down again and went on writing on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> When they heard this they began to leave, one by one, starting with the oldest, leaving Jesus alone in the middle of the crowd with the woman still standing there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> Jesus straightened up and asked her, “Where are they? Didn't anybody stay to condemn you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> “No one did, sir,” she replied. “I don't condemn you either,” Jesus told her. “Go, and don't sin anymore.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Jesus spoke again to the people, telling them, “I am the light of the world. If you follow me you won't walk in darkness for you will have the life-giving light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> The Pharisees replied, “You can't be your own witness!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “you're just making claims about yourself!”">a</a></sup>What you say doesn't prove anything!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> “Even if I am my own witness, my testimony is true,” Jesus told them, “for I know where I came from and where I'm going. But you don't know where I came from or where I'm going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge in a typically human way, but I don't judge anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> Even if I did judge, my judgment would be right because I am not doing this alone. The Father who sent me is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> Your own law states<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 17:6and Deuteronomy 19:15.">b</a></sup>that the testimony of two witnesses is valid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am my own witness, and my other witness is my Father who sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> “Where is your father?” they asked him. “You don't know me or my Father,” Jesus replied. “If you knew me then you would know my Father as well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> Jesus explained this while he was teaching near the Temple treasury. Yet no one arrested him because his time had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> Jesus told them again, “I'm leaving, and you'll search for me, but you'll die in your sin. You can't come where I'm going.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; PSALMS 22:6; John 8:48"> The Jews wondered out loud, “Is he going to kill himself? Is that what he means when he says ‘You can't come where I'm going’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> Jesus told them, “You are from below; I am from above. You are of this world; I am not of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> That is why I told you that you'll die in your sins. For if you don't trust in me, the ‘I am,’ you'll die in your sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> Then they asked him, “Who are you?” “Exactly who I told you I was from the beginning,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> “There's much I could say about you, and much I could condemn. But the one who sent me tells the truth, and what I'm saying to you here in this world is what I heard from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They didn't understand that he was talking to them about the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> So Jesus explained to them: “When you have lifted up the Son of man then you'll know that I am the ‘I am,’ and that I do nothing of myself, but only say what the Father taught me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> The one who sent me is with me; he has not abandoned me, for I always do what pleases him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> Many who heard Jesus say these things put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Then Jesus said to those Jews who trusted in him, “If you follow my teaching then you really are my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> “We're descendants of Abraham! We've never been anyone's slaves,” they answered. “How can you say that we'll be set free?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus replied, “I tell you the truth, everyone who sins is a slave of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> A slave doesn't have a permanent place in the family, but the son is part of the family forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> If the Son sets you free, then you're truly free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I know you're Abraham's descendants, yet you're trying to kill me because you refuse to accept my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I'm telling you what the Father has revealed to me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “what I have seen with the Father.”">c</a></sup>while you do what your father told you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> “Abraham is our father,” they answered. “If you really were children of Abraham, you'd do what Abraham did,” Jesus told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> “But you are trying to kill me now, because I told you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham would never have done that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> You're doing what your father does.” “Well we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word in the original is emphasized, implying that while they were not illegitimate, Jesus was.">d</a></sup>are not illegitimate,” they responded. “God alone is our father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus replied, “If God really was your father, you would love me. I came from God and now I am here. The decision to come wasn't mine, but the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why can't you understand what I'm saying? It's because you refuse to hear my message!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> Your father is the devil, and you love to follow your father's evil desires. He was a murderer from the beginning. He never stood for the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he lies he reveals his true character, for he's a liar and the father of lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> So because I tell you the truth, you don't believe me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Can any one of you prove that I'm guilty of sin? If I'm telling you the truth, why don't you believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> Anyone who belongs to God listens to what God says. The reason you don't listen is because you don't belong to God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> “Aren't we right to call you a Samaritan who is demon-possessed?” said the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> “No, I don't have a demon,” Jesus replied. “I honor my Father, but you dishonor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> I'm not here looking to glorify myself. But there is one who does this for me and who judges in my favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> I tell you the truth, anyone who follows my teaching will never die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> “Now we know you're demon-possessed,” said the Jews. “Abraham died, and the prophets did too, and you're telling us ‘anyone who follows my teaching will never die’!</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Are you greater than our father Abraham? He died, and the prophets died. Who do you think you are?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory means nothing. But it is God himself who glorifies me, the one you claim, ‘He is our God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> You don't know him, but I know him. If I were to say, ‘I don't know him,’ I'd be a liar, just like you. But I do know him, and I do what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Your father Abraham was delighted as he looked forward to see my coming, and was so happy when he saw it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Jews replied, “You're not even fifty years old, and you've seen Abraham?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> “I tell you the truth, before Abraham was born, I am,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “Before Abraham was, I am.” Once again Jesus uses the name of God himself given in Exodus 3:14. That the significance is not lost on his hearers is shown in their reaction of wanting to stone him for blasphemy.">e</a></sup>said Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> At this they picked up stones to stone him, but Jesus was hidden from them and he left the Temple.</span>
