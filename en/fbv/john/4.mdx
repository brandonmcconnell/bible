<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> When Jesus realized that the Pharisees had discovered that he was gaining and baptizing more disciples than John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (although it wasn't Jesus who was baptizing, but his disciples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> he left Judea and returned to Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> On the way he had to pass through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> So he came to the Samaritan city of Sychar, near to the field that Jacob had given his son Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> Jacob's well was there, and Jesus, who was tired from the journey, sat straight down beside the well. It was around noon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> A Samaritan woman came to fetch water. Jesus said to her, “Please could you give me a drink?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> for his disciples had gone to the town to buy food.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> “You're a Jew, and I'm a Samaritan woman. How can you ask me for a drink?” the woman replied, for Jews don't associate with Samaritans.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Jews do not share dishes with Samaritans.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered her, “If you only recognized God's gift, and who is asking you, ‘Please could you give me a drink?’ you would have asked him and he would have given you the water of life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> “Sir, you don't have a bucket, and the well is deep. Where are you going to get the water of life from?” she replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> “Our father Jacob gave us the well. He drank from it himself, as well as his sons and his livestock. Are you greater than he?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered, “Everyone who drinks water from this well will become thirsty again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> But those who drink the water I give won't ever be thirsty again. The water I give becomes a bubbling spring of water inside them, bringing them eternal life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; PSALMS 4:6"> “Sir,” replied the woman, “Please give me this water so I won't be thirsty, and I won't have to come here to fetch water!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> “Go and call your husband, and come back here,” Jesus told her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> “I don't have a husband,” the woman answered.<br />“You're right in saying you don't have a husband,” Jesus told her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> “You've had five husbands, and the one you're living with now is not your husband. So what you say is true!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> “I can see you're a prophet, sir,” the woman replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> “Tell me this: our ancestors worshiped here on this mountain, but you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="As a Jew.">b</a></sup>say that Jerusalem is where we must worship.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus replied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jesus addresses her as “woman” which is the normal term used, but sounds impolite in English.">c</a></sup>“Believe me the time is coming when you won't worship the Father either on this mountain or in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> You really don't know the God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “what.”">d</a></sup>you're worshiping, while we worship the God we know, for salvation comes from the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But the time is coming—and in fact it's here already—when true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth, for these are the kind of worshipers the Father wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is Spirit, so worshipers must worship in spirit and in truth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman said, “Well, I know that the Messiah is coming,” (the one who is called Christ). “When he comes he will explain it all to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus replied, “I AM—the one who is speaking to you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“I AM” is used in the Old Testament as a name for God. Jesus is telling her he is the Messiah and also identifying his divinity.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> Just then the disciples returned. They were shocked that he was talking to a woman, but none of them asked “What are you doing?” or “Why are you talking with her?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> The woman left her water jar behind and ran back to the town, telling the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> “Come and meet a man who told me everything I ever did! Could this be the Messiah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> So they went out of the town to go and see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> Meanwhile Jesus' disciples were urging him, “Rabbi, please eat something!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; PSALMS 119:103; John 4:34"> But Jesus replied, “I have food to eat that you know nothing about.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> “Did someone bring him food?” the disciples asked one another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus told them, “My food is to do the will of the one who sent me, and to complete his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Don't you have a saying, ‘four more months until harvest?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="It was usually four months between sowing and reaping.">f</a></sup>Open your eyes and look around! The crops in the fields are ripe, ready for harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> The reaper is being paid and harvesting a crop for eternal life so that both the sower and the reaper can celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> So the proverb ‘one sows, another reaps,’ is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap what you didn't work for. Others did the hard work and you have reaped the benefits of what they did.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> Many Samaritans from that town trusted in him because of what the woman said: “He told me everything I ever did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when they came to see him they pleaded with him to stay with them. He stayed for two days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> and because of what he told them many more trusted in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> They said to the woman, “Now our trust in him isn't just because of what you told us but because we have heard him for ourselves. We're convinced that he really is the Savior of the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> After the two days he continued on to Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> Jesus himself had made the comment that a prophet is not respected in his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> But when he arrived in Galilee, the people welcomed him, because they had also been at the Passover feast and had seen everything he'd done in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> He visited Cana in Galilee again, where he had turned water into wine. Nearby in the town of Capernaum lived a royal official whose son was very sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> When he heard that Jesus had returned from Judea to Galilee, he went to Jesus and begged him to come and heal his son who was close to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> “Unless you see signs and wonders you people really won't trust me,” said Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; PSALMS 88:10–12; PSALMS 40:17"> “Lord, just come before my child dies,” the official pleaded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> “Go on home,” Jesus told him. “Your son will live!”<br />The man trusted what Jesus told him and left for home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> While he was on his way, his servants met him with the news that his son was alive and recovering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> He asked them what time it was when his son began to get better. “Yesterday at one p.m. the fever left him,” they told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> Then the father realized this was the precise time when Jesus had told him, “Your son will live!” So he and everyone in his household trusted in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This was the second miraculous sign Jesus did after coming from Judea to Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>
