<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:31–44; John 6:23; Luke 5:1"> Some time later Jesus left to go to the other side of the Sea of Galilee (also known as the Sea of Tiberias).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Matthew 12:15; Matthew 15:30–31"> A large crowd was following him, for they'd seen his miracles of healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> Jesus went up a hill and sat down there with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> The time for the Jewish festival of the Passover was approaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> When Jesus looked up, and saw a large crowd coming towards him, he asked Philip, “Where can we buy bread to feed these people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> He only asked this to see how Philip would respond, because Jesus already knew what he was going to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> “Two hundred silver coins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, denarius. One denarius was worth a day's wage.">a</a></sup>wouldn't buy enough bread to give everyone only a little,” Philip replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, spoke up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> “There's a boy here who has five barley loaves and a couple of fish, but what good is that when there are so many people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> “Have everybody sit down,” Jesus said. There was plenty of grass there, so they all sat down, the men numbering around five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> Jesus took the bread, gave thanks, and had it handed out to the people as they sat there. Then he did the same with the fishes, making sure the people had as much as they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> Once they were all full, he said to his disciples, “Collect what's left over so nothing is wasted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> So they collected and filled twelve baskets with the pieces of the five barley loaves the people had eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> When the people saw this miracle, they said, “Surely this is the Prophet who was to come into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> Jesus realized that they were about to force him to become their king, so he left them and went up into the hills to be by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When evening came, his disciples went down to the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> climbed into a boat, and headed across the water towards Capernaum. By now it was night and Jesus had not joined them yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 107:25; PSALMS 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> A strong wind began blowing and the sea grew rough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> When they had rowed three or four miles, they saw Jesus walking on the sea, coming towards the boat. They were very frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> “Don't be afraid!” he told them. “It's me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> Then they gladly took him into the boat, and immediately they reached the shore where they were going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> The next day the crowd that had stayed on the other side of the sea noticed that there had been only one boat there, and that Jesus had not got into the boat with his disciples, but they had left without him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> Then other boats arrived from Tiberias, landing near to the place where they'd eaten the bread after the Lord had blessed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> Once the crowd realized that neither Jesus nor his disciples were there, they got into the boats and went over to Capernaum, looking for Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> When they found him on the other side of the sea, they asked him, “Rabbi, when did you get here?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="An oblique question, for they were really wondering how he got there…">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> “I tell you the truth,” Jesus replied, “you're looking for me because you ate as much bread as you wanted, not because you understood the miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> Don't be preoccupied about food that doesn't last, but concentrate on the lasting food of eternal life which the Son of man will give you, for God the Father has placed his seal of approval on him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> So they asked him, “What should we be doing in order to do what God wants?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus replied, “What God wants you to do is to trust in the one he sent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> “What miracle are you going to perform for us to see so we can trust you? What are you able to do?” they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; PSALMS 105:40; PSALMS 78:24–25"> “Our forefathers ate manna in the desert in fulfillment of the Scripture that says, ‘He gave them bread from heaven to eat.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 78:24referring to Exodus 16:4.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> “I tell you the truth, it wasn't Moses who gave you bread from heaven,” Jesus replied. “It's my Father who gives you the true bread of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> For the bread of God is the one who comes from heaven and gives life to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; PSALMS 4:6"> “Lord, please give us this kind of bread all the time!” they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> “I am the bread of life,” Jesus replied. “Anyone who comes to me will never be hungry again, and anyone who trusts in me will never be thirsty again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> But as I explained to you before, you have seen me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Referring to all that Jesus had done, not just seeing his person. In fact the word “me” is not in all ancient manuscripts.">d</a></sup>but you still don't trust me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> All those the Father gives me will come to me, and I won't reject any of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> For I came down from heaven not to do what I want, but to do what the one who sent me wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> What he wants is for me not to lose anyone he has given to me, but for me to raise them up at the last day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Last day,” referring to the day of judgment. Also verses 40, 44, and 54.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> What my Father wants is for everyone who sees the Son and trusts in him to have eternal life, and for me to raise them up at the last day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> Then the Jews began to grumble about him because he had said, “I am the bread that came down from heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> They said, “Isn't this Jesus, the son of Joseph? We know his father and his mother. So how can he now tell us, ‘I came down from heaven’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> “Stop grumbling to each other,” Jesus said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> “No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me attracts them, and I will raise them up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> As is written in Scripture by the prophets, ‘Everyone will be taught by God.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 54:13.">f</a></sup>Everyone who listens to and learns from the Father comes to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> Not that anyone has seen God, except he who is from God; he has seen the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> I tell you the truth: anyone who trusts in him has eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I am the bread of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> Your forefathers ate manna in the desert but they still died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> But this is the bread that comes down from heaven, and anyone who eats it won't ever die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I am the life-giving bread from heaven, and anyone who eats this bread will live forever. The bread is my flesh that I give so that the world may live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> Then the Jews argued heatedly among themselves. “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?” they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Jesus told them, “I tell you the truth, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of man and drink his blood, you cannot truly live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> Those who eat my flesh and drink my blood have eternal life, and I will raise them up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> Those who eat my flesh and drink my blood remain in me, and I remain in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> Just as the life-giving Father sent me and I live because of the Father, so anyone who feeds on me will live because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> Now this is the bread that came down from heaven, not the kind your forefathers ate and still died. Anyone who eats this bread will live forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> Jesus explained this while he was teaching in a synagogue at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> Many of his disciples when they heard it said, “This is hard to accept! Who can follow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Follow” not only in the sense of “understand,” but also “observe” or “agree with.”">g</a></sup>it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> Jesus saw that his disciples were complaining about this, so he asked them, “Are you offended by this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> Then what if you were to see the Son ascend to where he was before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; PSALMS 119:93"> The Spirit gives life; the physical body doesn't do anything.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “counts for nothing.”">h</a></sup>The words I've told you are spirit and life!</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> Yet there are some of you who don't trust me.” (Jesus had known from the very beginning who didn't trust him, and who would betray him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> Jesus added, “This is why I told you that no one can come to me unless it is made possible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “granted.”">i</a></sup>by the Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Luke 9:62; 1 John 2:19; Hebrews 10:38"> From this time on many of Jesus' disciples gave up and no longer followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> Then Jesus asked the twelve disciples, “What about you? You don't want to leave as well, do you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="PSALMS 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Simon Peter answered, “Lord, who would we follow? You're the one who has the words of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="John 11:27; Luke 9:20; Mark 8:29"> We trust in you, and we're convinced that you are God's Holy One.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Jesus replied, “Didn't I choose you, the twelve disciples? Yet one of you is a devil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:14–16; Jude 1:4; Acts 1:16–20"> (Jesus was referring to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot. He was the one of the twelve disciples who would betray Jesus.)</span>
