<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Six days before the Passover, Jesus went to Bethany, to the home of Lazarus who had been raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> There a dinner was arranged in his honor. Martha helped serve the food while Lazarus sat at the table with Jesus and the other guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Mary brought a pint<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A litra, about 12 ounces, or half a liter.">a</a></sup>of pure nard perfume and anointed Jesus' feet, wiping them dry with her hair. The scent of the perfume filled the whole house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> But one of the disciples, Judas Iscariot, who would later betray Jesus, asked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> “Why wasn't this perfume sold and the money given to the poor? It was worth three hundred denarii.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="About a year's wages at one denarius per day.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; PSALMS 50:16–20; James 2:6"> He wasn't saying this because he cared about the poor but because he was a thief. He was the one who looked after the disciples' money and he often took some for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> “Don't criticize her,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “leave her alone.”">c</a></sup>Jesus replied. “She did this in preparation for the day of my burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> You'll always have the poor with you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 15:11.">d</a></sup>but you won't always have me here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> A large crowd of Jews had found out that he was there. They came there not just to see Jesus but because they wanted to see Lazarus, the man Jesus had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> So the chief priests planned to kill Lazarus as well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> since it was because of him that many people were no longer following the Jewish leaders but putting their trust in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> The following day the crowds of people who had come for the Passover festival heard that Jesus was on his way to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> They cut off palm branches and went to welcome him, shouting, “Hosanna! Blessed is the one coming in the name of the Lord. Blessed is the king of Israel.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:26.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> Jesus found a young donkey and rode on it, as Scripture says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> “Don't be afraid, daughter of Zion. Look, your king is coming, riding a donkey's colt.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Zechariah 9:9.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> At the time, Jesus' disciples did not understand what these things meant. It was only later when he was glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Glorified: in his resurrection and ascension.">g</a></sup>that they realized what had happened had been prophesied and applied to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> Many in the crowd had seen Jesus call Lazarus from the tomb and raise him from the dead, and were telling the story.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> That was the reason so many people went to meet Jesus—because they had heard about this miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; PSALMS 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> The Pharisees said to one other, “Look, we're getting nowhere. Everyone's running after him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now some Greeks had come to the festival to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> They came to Philip of Bethsaida in Galilee, and said, “Sir, we'd like to see Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip went and told Andrew. Then they both went to Jesus and told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> Jesus responded, “The time has come for the Son of man to be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> I tell you the truth: unless a grain of wheat is planted in the soil and dies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Meaning that it looks like it dies.">h</a></sup>it remains just one grain. But if it dies, it produces many more grains of wheat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> If you love your own life you will lose it, but if you don't love your own life in this world you will keep your life forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If you want to serve me you need to follow me. My servants will be where I am, and my Father will honor anyone who serves me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> Now I am troubled. What should I say? ‘Father, save me from this coming time of suffering?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “this hour.”">i</a></sup>’ No, for this is why I came—to go through this time of suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, show the glory of your character.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “name.” Name is synonymous with character.">j</a></sup>A voice came from heaven, saying, “I have shown its glory, and I will show it again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> The crowd that was standing there heard it. Some said it thundered; others said an angel had spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus told them, “This voice spoke not for my sake, but for yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now is the judgment of this world; now the prince of this world will be thrown out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> But when I am lifted up from the earth I will attract everyone to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> (He said this to point out the kind of death he was going to die.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The crowd responded, “The Law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Referring to what we call the Old Testament.">k</a></sup>tells us that the Messiah will live forever, so how can you say the Son of man must be ‘lifted up’? Who is this ‘Son of man’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus replied, “The light is here with you for a little longer. Walk while you have the light so that the darkness doesn't overtake you. Those who walk in the dark don't know where they're going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> Put your trust in the light while you still have it so that you can become children of light.” When Jesus had told them this, he left and hid himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> But despite all the miracles he had done in their presence, they still did not trust in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> This fulfilled the message of Isaiah the prophet, who said, “Lord, who has believed what we told them? To whom has the Lord's power been revealed?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 53:1.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> They were not able to trust him, and as a result they fulfilled what Isaiah also said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> “He blinded their eyes, and made their minds dull, so that their eyes would not see, and their minds would not think, and they would not turn to me—for if they did I would heal them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 6:10.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> Isaiah saw Jesus' glory and said this in reference to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Even so many of the leaders did trust in him. However, they did not openly admit it because they did not want the Pharisees to expel them from the synagogue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> loving human admiration more than God's approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> Jesus called out, “If you trust in me you're not just trusting in me but also in the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> When you see me, you're seeing the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; PSALMS 36:9; John 9:5"> I have come as a light shining into the world, so if you trust in me you won't remain in the dark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> I don't judge anyone who hears my words but doesn't do what I say. I came to save the world, not to judge it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> Anyone who rejects me and does not accept my words will be judged at the end-time judgment in accordance with what I have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> For I'm not speaking for myself but for my Father who sent me. He is the one who instructed me what to say and how to say it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> I know that what he told me to say brings eternal life—so whatever I say is what the Father told me.”</span>
