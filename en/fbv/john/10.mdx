<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> “I tell you the truth, anyone who doesn't come in through the gate of the sheepfold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Sheepfold,” or “courtyard.” This referred to the enclosure beside a house and often attached to it, surrounded by a wall. The setting is a village rather than the open countryside.">a</a></sup>but climbs in some other way is a thief and a robber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> The one who comes in through the gate is the shepherd of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> The gatekeeper opens it for him, and the sheep respond to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name, and leads them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> After bringing them out, he walks ahead of them, and the sheep follow him because they recognize his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> They won't follow strangers. In fact they run away from a stranger because they don't recognize the voice of strangers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; PSALMS 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> When Jesus gave this illustration those who were listening to him didn't understand what he meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> So Jesus explained again, “I tell you the truth: I am the gate of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> All those who came before me were thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn't listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I am the gate. Anyone who comes in through me will be healed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “saved.”">b</a></sup>They will be able to come and go, and find the food they need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief comes only to steal, kill, and destroy. I've come to bring you life, life full to overflowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; PSALMS 23:1; John 15:13"> I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> The man paid to look after the sheep is not the shepherd and he runs away when he sees the wolf coming. He abandons the sheep because they're not his, and the wolf attacks and scatters the flock</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> for the man is only working for pay and doesn't care about the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> I am the good shepherd. I know who are mine, and they know me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> just as the Father knows me and I know him. I lay down my life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> I have other sheep that are not in this sheepfold. I must bring them too. They will listen to my voice and there will be one flock with one shepherd.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> This is why the Father loves me, because I lay down my life so I may take it up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> No one takes it from me; I choose to lay it down. I have the right to give it up, and I have the right to take it back. This is the command my Father gave me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> The Jews were again divided in their opinion about Jesus over these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> Many of them said, “He's demon-possessed! He's mad! Why are you listening to him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; PSALMS 94:9"> Others said, “These aren't the words of someone who's demon-possessed. Besides, a demon can't open eyes that are blind.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> It was winter, and the time of the Festival of Dedication in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> Jesus was walking in the Temple through Solomon's Porch. The Jews surrounded him, asking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> “How long are you going to keep us hanging in suspense?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A colloquial expression that is literally “lift up our souls,” and means creating a position of uncertainty.">c</a></sup>If you are the Messiah then tell us plainly!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus replied, “I already told you but you refuse to believe it. The miracles I do in my Father's name prove who I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> You don't believe me because you are not my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep recognize my voice; I know them, and they follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> I give them eternal life; they shall never be lost, and no one can snatch them from me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “out of my hand.” Similarly verse 29.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father who gave them to me is greater than anyone else; no one can snatch them from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and the Father are one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Once again the Jews picked up stones to stone him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; PSALMS 35:12"> Jesus said to them, “You've seen many good deeds that I've done from the Father. Which one are you stoning me for?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews replied, “We're not stoning you for a good deed, but for blasphemy because you are just a man but you're claiming to be God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; PSALMS 82:1"> Jesus answered them, “Isn't it written in your law, ‘I said, you are gods’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 82:6.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> He called those people ‘gods,’ the people to whom the word of God came—and Scripture can't be altered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> So why are you saying the one whom the Father set apart and sent into the world is blaspheming because I said ‘I am the Son of God’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I'm not doing what my Father does, then don't believe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> But if that is what I'm doing, even though you don't believe me, you should believe because of the evidence of what I've done. That way you can know and understand that the Father is in me, and I am in the Father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> Once again they tried to arrest him, but he escaped from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> He went back across the Jordan River to the place where John had begun baptizing, and he stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> Many people came to him, and they said, “John didn't perform miracles, but everything he said about this man has come true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> Many who were there put their trust in Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
