<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:22; Acts 26:9–11; Romans 10:2–3"> They will expel you from the synagogues—in fact the time is coming when those who kill you will think they are doing God a service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:21; John 17:25; John 8:55"> They'll do this because they have never known the Father or me. I've told you this so that when these things happen, you'll remember what I told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 14:29; Mark 13:23; John 17:12–13"> I didn't need to tell you this right at the beginning because I was going to be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> But now I'm going to the one who sent me, and yet not one of you is asking me, ‘Where are you going?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> Of course, now that I've told you, you're full of grief.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> But I'm telling you the truth: it's better for you that I go away, for if I don't the Comforter won't come to you. If I go away, I will send him to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> When he comes, he will convince those in the world that they have wrong ideas regarding sin, about what is right, and about judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> Sin, for they don't trust in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> What is right, for I'm going to the Father and you won't see me any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> Judgment, for the ruler of this world has been condemned.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “judged.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> There's much more I want to explain to you, but you couldn't stand it now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> However, when the Spirit of truth comes, he will teach you the whole truth. He doesn't speak for himself, but he only says what he hears, and he will tell you what's going to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He brings me glory for he teaches you whatever he receives from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> All that belongs to the Father is mine. This is why I said that the Spirit teaches you whatever he receives from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> In a little while you won't see me anymore, but then a little while after that you will see me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> Some of his disciples said to one another, “What does he mean, ‘In a little while you won't see me, but a little while after that you will see me’? and ‘For I'm going to the Father’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> They were asking, “What does he mean by ‘in a little while’? We don't know what he's talking about.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> Jesus realized that they wanted to ask him about this. So he asked them, “Are you wondering about my comment, ‘In a little while you won't see me, but a little while after that you will see me’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; PSALMS 126:5–6"> I tell you the truth, you will weep and mourn, but the world will rejoice. You will grieve, but your grief will turn into joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> A woman in labor suffers pain because her time has come, but once the baby is born, she forgets the agony because of the joy that a child has been brought into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> Yes, you're grieving now, but I will see you again; and you will rejoice, and no one can take away your joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> When that time comes you won't need to ask me about anything. I tell you the truth, the Father will give you whatever you ask in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> Until now you haven't asked for anything in my name, so ask and you shall receive, and your happiness will be complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:12; Mark 4:13; Matthew 13:34–35"> I've been talking to you using picture language. But shortly I won't use such picture language any more when I speak to you. Instead I'll explain the Father to you very plainly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> At that time you will ask in my name. I'm not saying to you that I will plead with the Father on your behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:23; John 17:23"> for the Father himself loves you—because you love me and believe that I came from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:1; John 13:3; John 8:14"> I left the Father and came into the world; now I leave the world and return to my Father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 16:25"> Then the disciples said, “Now you're talking very plainly and not using picture language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:17; John 17:8; John 16:17"> Now we're certain that you know everything, and that in order to know what questions people are thinking you don't need to ask them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring back to what happened in 16:19.">b</a></sup>This convinces us that you came from God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> “Are you really convinced now?” Jesus asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> “The time is coming—in fact it's just about to happen—when you will be scattered, each of you to your own homes, leaving me all alone. But I'm not really alone, for the Father is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> I've told you all this so that you may have peace because you are one with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “peace in me.”">c</a></sup>You will suffer in this world, but be brave—I have defeated the world!”</span>
</div>
