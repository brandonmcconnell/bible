<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> There was a man named Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a member of the Jewish ruling council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> He came at night to where Jesus was and said, “Rabbi, we know you are a teacher who has come from God, for nobody could do the miraculous signs you're doing unless God was with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> “I tell you the truth,” Jesus replied, “Unless you are reborn,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “born from above.”">a</a></sup>you can't experience God's kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> “How can you be reborn when you're old?” Nicodemus asked. “You can't go back into your mother's womb and be born a second time!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> “I tell you the truth, you can't enter God's kingdom unless you are born of water and the Spirit,” Jesus told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> “What's born of the body is body, and what's born of the Spirit is Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Don't be surprised at my telling you, ‘You must be reborn.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The first “you” refers to Nicodemus in the singular. The second “you” is plural, and refers to a wider audience.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The wind blows wherever it wants, and just as you hear the sound it makes, but don't know where it's coming from or where it's going, that's how it is for everyone who is born of the Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> “How is this possible?” Nicodemus asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> “You're a famous teacher in Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “you are the teacher of Israel.”">c</a></sup>and yet you don't understand such things?” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> “I tell you the truth, we speak of what we know and give evidence regarding what we have seen, but you refuse to accept our testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If you don't trust what I say when I tell you about earthly things, how would you ever trust what I say if I were to tell you about heavenly things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> No one has gone up to heaven, but the Son of man came down from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> Just as Moses lifted up the serpent in the desert,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Numbers 21:9.">d</a></sup>so the Son of man must be lifted up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> so that everyone who trusts in him will have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For God loved the world, and this is how:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The word often translated “so” (as in “so loved”) is primarily describing the way or manner in which God loves rather than the extent or intensity of his love.">e</a></sup>he gave his one and only Son, so that everyone who trusts in him shouldn't die, but have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> God didn't send the Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> Those who trust in him are not condemned, while those who don't trust in him are condemned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “have condemned themselves.”">f</a></sup>already because they didn't trust in the one and only Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> This is how the decision<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “judgment.”">g</a></sup>is made: the light came to the world, but people loved the darkness rather than the light, for their actions were evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> All those who do evil hate the light and don't come into the light, because they don't want their actions to be exposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; PSALMS 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> But those who do good<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “doing the truth.”">h</a></sup>come into the light, so that what God accomplishes in them can be revealed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After this Jesus and his disciples went into Judea and spent some time with the people, baptizing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> John was also baptizing at Aenon near Salim, because there was plenty of water there and people kept coming to be baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> (This was before John was imprisoned.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> An argument developed between John's disciples and a Jew over ceremonial purification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> They went to John and told him, “Rabbi, the man you were with on the other side of the Jordan River, the one you testified in support of—see, now he's baptizing, and everyone is going to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> “No one receives anything unless they're given it from heaven,” John replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> “You yourselves can testify that I declared, ‘I'm not the Messiah. I've been sent to prepare his way.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> The bridegroom is the one who has the bride! The best man waits, listening for the bridegroom, and is so happy when he hears the bridegroom's voice—in the same way my happiness is now complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; PSALMS 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> He must become more important, and I must become less important.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> He who comes from above is greater<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “above” in the sense of authority.">i</a></sup>than all; he who comes from the earth belongs to the earth and talks about earthly things. He who comes from heaven is greater than all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> He gives evidence about what he has seen and heard, but no one accepts what he has to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> Yet anyone who accepts what he says confirms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, “stamp of approval.”">j</a></sup>that God is truthful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> For the one God sent speaks God's words, because God doesn't restrict the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father loves the Son and has placed everything in his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> Anyone who trusts in the Son has eternal life, but anyone who refuses to trust the Son will not experience eternal life but remains under God's condemnation.</span>
</div>
