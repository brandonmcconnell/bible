<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> As Jesus was passing by, he saw a man born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, why was this man born blind? Was it him who sinned, or was it his parents?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus replied, “It wasn't because of the sins of the man or his parents. But so that what God can do may be shown in his life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> we have to keep on doing the work of the one who sent me as long as it is still daytime. The night is coming when no one can work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> While I'm here in the world I am the light of the world.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> After he'd said this, Jesus spat on the ground and made some mud with the saliva which he put on the man's eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> Then Jesus told him, “Go and wash in the Pool of Siloam” (which means “sent”). So the man went and washed, and when he went home he could see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> His neighbors and those who had known him as a beggar, asked, “Isn't this the man who used to sit and beg?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said he was, while others said “no, it's just someone who looks like him.” But the man kept saying, “It is me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> “So how is it you can see?” they asked him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He replied, “A man called Jesus made some mud and put it on my eyes and told me, ‘Go and wash yourself in the Pool of Siloam.’ So I went and washed, and now I can see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> “Where is he?” they asked.<br />“I don't know,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They took the man who had been blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now it was the Sabbath when Jesus had made the mud and opened the blind man's eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> So the Pharisees also asked him how he could see. He told them, “He put mud on my eyes, and I washed, and now I can see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Some of the Pharisees said, “The man who did this can't be from God because he doesn't keep the Sabbath.” But others wondered, “How could a sinner do such miracles?” So they were divided in their opinion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> So they went on questioning the man. “What's your opinion about him, then, since it's your eyes he opened,” they asked.<br />“He's surely a prophet,” the man replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jewish leaders still refused to believe that the man who had been blind could now see until they had called in the man's parents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> They asked them, “Is this your son whom you say was born blind? So how is it that now he can see?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered, “We know this is our son who was born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But we've no idea how he can see now, or who healed him. Why don't you ask him, he's old enough. He can speak for himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> The reason his parents said this was because they were afraid of what the Jewish leaders would do. The Jewish leaders had already announced that anyone who declared that Jesus was the Messiah would be thrown out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> That was why his parents said, “Ask him, he's old enough.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> Once more they called in the man who had been blind, and told him, “Give God the glory! We know this man is a sinner.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> The man replied, “Whether he's a sinner or not, I don't know. All I know is that I was blind and now I can see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then they asked him, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> The man replied, “I already told you. Weren't you listening? Why do you want to hear it again? You don't want to become his disciples too, do you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They shouted abuse at him, and said, “You're that man's disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We're disciples of Moses. We know that God spoke to Moses, but as for this person, we don't even know where he comes from.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; PSALMS 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered, “That's incredible! You don't know where he comes from but he opened my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God doesn't listen to sinners, but he does listen to anyone who worships him and does what he wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Never before in the whole of history has anyone heard of a man born blind being healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man weren't from God, he could do nothing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> “You were born totally sinful, and yet you're trying to lecture us,” they replied. And they threw him out of the synagogue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> When Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, he went and found the man, and asked him, “Do you trust in the Son of man?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Songs 5:9; John 1:38"> The man replied, “Tell me who he is, sir, so I can put my trust in him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; PSALMS 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> “You've already seen him. He's the one speaking with you now!” Jesus told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> “I trust you, Lord!” he said, and he kneeled in worship before Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> Then Jesus told him, “I've come into the world to bring judgment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Judgment” in terms of making a decision rather than condemnation.">a</a></sup>so that those who are blind may see, and those who see will become blind.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Some Pharisees who were there with Jesus asked him, “We're not blind too, are we?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus answered, “If you were blind, you wouldn't be guilty. But now that you say you see, your guilt remains.”</span>
</div>
</div>
