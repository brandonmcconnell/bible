<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> Later Jesus appeared again to the disciples by the Sea of Galilee.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Sea of Tiberias.”">a</a></sup>This is how it happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas the Twin, Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, and Zebedee's sons, and two other disciples were together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> “I'm going fishing,” Simon Peter said to them. “We'll come with you,” they replied. So they left and went out in the boat, but all night they caught nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> When dawn came Jesus was standing on the shore, but the disciples didn't know it was him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> Jesus called to them, “My friends, haven't you caught anything?”<br />“No,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> “Throw the net out on the right side of the boat, and you'll find some,” he told them. So they threw out the net, and they weren't able to haul it in because it had so many fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> The disciple Jesus loved said to Peter, “It's the Lord.” When Peter heard it was the Lord, he put some clothes on because he was undressed, and jumped into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> The other disciples followed in the boat, pulling the net full of fish, because they were not far from the shore, only about a hundred yards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> Once they'd landed they saw a fire with fish cooking on it, and some bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus told them, “Bring some of the fish you've just caught.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simon Peter went aboard and pulled the net full of fish ashore. There were 153 large fish, yet even so the net hadn't torn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> “Come and eat some breakfast,” Jesus said to them. None of the disciples was brave enough to ask him, “Who are you?” They knew it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Jesus took the bread and gave it to them and the fish as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This was the third time Jesus had appeared to the disciples after being raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> After breakfast, Jesus asked Simon Peter, “Simon, son of John, do you love me more than these?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“These.” This could refer to the objects around them, meaning the fisherman's trade, but is more likely that it refers to the other disciples. It is Peter's love for Jesus which is in question.">b</a></sup><br />“Yes, Lord,” he replied, “you know I love you.”<br />“Take care of my lambs,” Jesus told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> “Simon, son of John, do you love me?” he asked for the second time.<br />“Yes, Lord,” he answered, “you know I love you.”<br />“Look after my sheep,” Jesus said to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> “Simon, son of John, do you love me?” he asked a third time.<br />Peter was hurt that Jesus had asked him for the third time if he loved him. “Lord, you know everything. You know I love you,” Peter told him.<br />“Take care of my sheep,” said Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> “I tell you the truth,” said Jesus, “when you were young, you dressed yourself and went wherever you wanted. But when you're old, you'll hold out your hands and someone will dress you and take you where you don't want to go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> Jesus said this to explain the kind of death by which he would glorify God. Then he said to Peter, “Follow me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> As Peter turned round, he saw the disciple Jesus loved following them, the one who had leaned over to Jesus during the supper and asked, “Lord, who is going to betray you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> Peter asked Jesus, “What about him, Lord?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus told him, “If I want him to remain alive here until I return, why is that your concern? You follow me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> This is why the saying spread among the believers that this disciple would not die. But Jesus didn't say to him that he wouldn't die, just that “If I want him to remain alive here until I return, why is that your concern?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This disciple confirms what happened and wrote all this down. We know that what he says is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 40:5; John 20:30–31; PSALMS 71:15"> Jesus did many other things as well, and if it all was written down, I doubt the whole world could hold all the books that would be written.</span>
</div>
