</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; PSALMS 119:11"> I was delighted when some fellow-believers arrived and told me about your commitment to the truth, and how you continue living in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> Nothing makes me happier than to hear how my dear friends are following the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> My friend, you show you are trustworthy in what you're doing by looking after the brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Christian believers traveling around spreading the good news.">b</a></sup>even those you don't know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> They have spoken well about your love in front of the church. Please be so kind as to send them on their journey in a way that God would appreciate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> for they are traveling in his name, not accepting anything from non-believers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> We ought to support them so we can share together in the truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I did write about this to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves being in charge,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “loves to be the first.”">d</a></sup>refuses to accept our authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> So if I do visit, I will make it clear what he's been doing. He's been making malicious accusations against us. Not satisfied with doing that, he refuses to welcome any other brothers. He won't let anyone else welcome them either, and throws those who do so out of the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; PSALMS 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> My friend, don't imitate what is evil, but what is good. Those who do what's good belong to God; those who do evil don't know God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “have not seen God.”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Everyone speaks well of Demetrius—and the truth does so too! We also speak well of him, and you know we tell the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I have so much to say to you, but I don't want to do it in writing with pen and ink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> I hope to see you soon so we can talk face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:3"> May peace be with you! The friends here send their greetings to you. Please personally greet our friends there by name.</span>
