<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> An heir is subject to guardians and managers until the time set by the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> It's just the same for us. When we were children, we were slaves subject to the basic rules<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word translated “rules” here is subject to wide interpretation. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> But at the appropriate time God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the rule of law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> so that he could rescue those who were kept under the rule of law, so that we could receive the inheritance of adopted children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> To show you are his children, God sent the Spirit of his Son to convince us, causing us to call out, “Abba,” meaning “Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> Since you are now no longer a slave, but a child, and if you are his child, then God has made you his heir.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> At the time when you didn't know God, you were enslaved by the supposed “gods” of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> But now you've come to know God—or better, to be known by God. So how can you go back to those useless and worthless rules? Do you want to be slaves to those rules all over again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You're observing special days and months, seasons and years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This refers to the observing of special feast days and times in the Old Testament system.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I'm concerned that all I did for you has been wasted!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I'm pleading with you, my friends: be like me, because I became like you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In other words, a “liberated Gentile.”">c</a></sup>You've never treated me badly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> You remember that it was because I was sick that I shared with you the good news on my first visit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="It seems that because Paul was delayed due to sickness that he had the chance to share the good news with the Galatians.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> Even though my illness was difficult for you, you didn't despise or reject me—in fact you treated me like an angel of God, like Christ Jesus himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> So what's happened to all your gratefulness? I tell you, back then if you could have pulled out your eyes and given them to me, you would have!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> So what has happened—have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> These people are keen to gain your support, but not for any good reasons. On the contrary, they want to keep you away from us so that you will enthusiastically support them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> Of course it's good to want to do good. But this should be at all times, not just when I'm there with you!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Suggesting these people were only wanting to be helpful to the Galatians to fulfill their own agenda.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My dear friends, I want to work with you until Christ's character is duplicated in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> I really wish I could be with you right now so I could change my tone of voice… I'm so worried about you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Answer me this, you people who want to live under the law: Don't you hear what the law is saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> As Scripture says, Abraham had two sons, one from the servant girl, and one from the free woman.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Genesis 16:15and Genesis 21:2-3.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> However, the son from the servant girl was born following a human plan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Referring to Sarah's plan to have a child through her servant-girl.">g</a></sup>while the son from the free woman was born as the result of the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> This provides an analogy: these two women represent two agreements. One agreement is from Mount Sinai—Hagar—and she gives birth to slave children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> Hagar symbolizes Mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to the current Jerusalem, because she is in slavery with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the heavenly Jerusalem is free. She is our mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; PSALMS 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> As Scripture says, “Rejoice you who are childless and who have never given birth! Shout aloud for joy, you who have never been in labor—for the abandoned woman has more children than the woman who has a husband!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 54:1.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> Now my friends, we are children of promise just like Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> However, just as the one born according to a human plan persecuted the one born through the Spirit, so it is today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> But what does Scripture say? “Send away the servant girl and her son, for the son of the servant girl will not be an heir together with the son of the free woman.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 21:10.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> Therefore, my friends, we're not children of a servant girl, but of the free woman.</span>
</div>
