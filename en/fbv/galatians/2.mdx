<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; Acts 14:12; 2 Corinthians 8:23"> Fourteen years later I returned to Jerusalem with Barnabas. I took Titus along with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:6; Philippians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:5"> I went because of what God had shown me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “according to revelation.”">a</a></sup>I met with the recognized church leaders there in private and explained to them the good news I was sharing with the foreigners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">b</a></sup>I didn't want the course I had followed, and what I was working so hard for, to come to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:2–6; Acts 16:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20–21"> But as it turned out, nobody even insisted that Titus who was with me should be circumcised, though he was Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:4; Galatians 5:1; Galatians 4:3"> (That issue only arose because some false Christians slipped in to spy on the freedom we have in Christ Jesus, trying to make us slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 2:14; Galatians 4:16; Colossians 1:5"> We never gave in to them, not even for a moment. We wanted to make sure to keep the truth of the good news unchanged for you.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:2; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> But those considered to be important didn't add anything<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “made no changes.”">c</a></sup>to what I said. (It doesn't concern me what kind of leaders they were, because God doesn't judge people the way we do.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Galatians 1:16"> On the contrary, once they realized that I'd been given responsibility to share the good news with the foreigners just as Peter had been given the responsibility to share the good news with the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Acts 22:21; Acts 2:14–41"> (for the same God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “the One.”">d</a></sup>who worked through Peter as apostle to the Jews also worked through me as apostle to the foreigners),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Revelation 3:12; Galatians 2:2"> and once they recognized the grace that had been given to me, then James, Peter, and John, who bore the responsibility<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “considered pillars.”">e</a></sup>of church leadership, shook Barnabas and me by the hand as their fellow-workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 24:17; 1 John 3:17; Hebrews 13:16"> We were to work for the foreigners, while they would work for the Jews. Their only instruction was to remember to look after the poor, something I was already very committed to.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 5:16; Acts 15:1"> However, when Peter came to Antioch I did have to confront him directly, because he was clearly wrong in what he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Luke 15:2; Acts 11:2–3"> Before some of James' friends arrived, Peter used to eat with the foreigners. But when these people came he stopped doing this and stayed away from the foreigners. He was afraid of being criticized by those who insisted that men had to be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 4:14; Hebrews 13:9"> As well as Peter, other Jewish Christians became hypocritical too, to the extent that even Barnabas was persuaded to follow their hypocrisy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:5; Acts 10:28; Acts 15:10–11"> When I realized that they weren't taking a firm stand for the truth of the good news, I said to Peter in front of everyone, “If you're a Jew yet live like the foreigners and not like Jews, why are you forcing the foreigners to live like Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 2:3; Ephesians 2:11–12"> We may be Jews by birth, and not ‘sinners’ like the foreigners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; PSALMS 143:2; Romans 9:30"> but we know that nobody is made right by doing what the law demands—it is only through trusting in Jesus Christ. We have trusted in Christ Jesus so that we could be made right by placing our confidence in Christ, and not through doing what the law says—because nobody is made right by observing the requirements of the law.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:1–2; 1 John 3:8–10; 1 John 3:5"> For if, as we look to be made right in Christ, we ourselves prove to be sinners, does that then mean that Christ is in the service of sin?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The idea here is that by giving up observance of the Jewish law we then become sinners, and Christ has led us into sin—a concept that Paul strongly rejects.">f</a></sup>Of course not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:9–12; Galatians 2:4–5; Galatians 2:12–16"> For if I were to rebuild what I've destroyed, then I only demonstrate I'm a law-breaker.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="In other words, if I return to the old system of law as the means of being set right with God, all I do is prove that I am in violation of the law as a sinner.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:4; Romans 6:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> For through the law I died to the law in order that I could live for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 2 Corinthians 5:15; Romans 6:4–6"> I've been crucified with Christ— so it's no longer I who lives, but it is Christ living in me. The life I now live in this body, I live by trusting in the Son of God, who loved me, and who gave himself for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:11"> How could I dismiss God's grace? For if we could be made right through keeping the law then Christ died a pointless death!</span>
</div>
</div>
