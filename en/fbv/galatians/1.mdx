<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> This letter comes from Paul, an apostle not appointed by any human organization or human authority.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “not by men nor through man.”">a</a></sup>Quite the contrary: I was appointed by Jesus Christ and God the Father who raised Jesus from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> All the brothers and sisters here with me join in sending this letter to the churches in Galatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> May the grace and peace of God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ be with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> Jesus gave himself for our sins to set us free from this current world of evil, following the will of our God and Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; PSALMS 72:19"> To him be glory forever and ever! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I'm shocked at how quickly you're abandoning the God who by the grace of Christ called you. You are turning to a different kind of good news</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> that isn't good news at all! Some people there are confusing you, wanting to pervert the good news of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But if anyone, even we ourselves, or even an angel from heaven, should promote any other kind of good news<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Clearly Paul doesn't think this is good news at all, so maybe should be in ironic quotes: “good news.”">b</a></sup>than what we have already told you, let them be condemned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> I repeat what we've told you before: if anyone promotes any other kind of good news<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="As 1:8.">c</a></sup>than what you've already accepted, let them be condemned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> Whose approval do you think I want—that of people, or of God? Do you think I'm trying to please people? If I wanted to please people I wouldn't be a servant of Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> Let me make it clear, my friends, regarding the good news I'm declaring—it did not come from any human being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> I didn't receive it from anyone, and nobody taught it to me—it was Jesus Christ who revealed it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> You heard how I behaved as a follower of the Jewish religion—how I fanatically persecuted God's church, savagely trying to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> I surpassed my contemporaries in the practice of the Jewish religion because I was so fervently devoted to the traditions of my ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when God (who had set me apart from birth) called me through his grace, and was pleased</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> to reveal his Son to me so that I could announce the good news to the nations,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “Gentiles.”">d</a></sup>I didn't discuss this with anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> I didn't go to Jerusalem to talk to those who preceded me as apostles; instead I left for Arabia, and then later returned to Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> After three years I went to Jerusalem to visit Peter. I stayed with him for two weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> I didn't see any other apostles except James, the Lord's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> (Let me assure you before God that I'm not lying about what I'm writing to you!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I went to Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> Even so, my face wasn't known to those in the churches of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> They had only heard people say, “The man who used to persecute us is now spreading the faith he once tried to destroy!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> —and they praised God because of me.</span>
</div>
