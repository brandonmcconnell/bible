<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="GAL" data-book-slug="galatians" data-book-name="Galatians" data-chapter="3" data-verses="29">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> You Galatians, you've lost your heads!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word used here is usually translated as “foolish”; however today that has become more of an epithet. Paul's point is that they are not thinking—the word really means “mindless.” The same word is used again in verse 3.">a</a></sup>Who has put you under a spell? The death of Jesus Christ on a cross was clearly presented to you so you could see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> So tell me—did you receive the Spirit by keeping the law, or by placing your trust in what you heard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> You really have lost your heads! You began living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meaning, “You began your Christian lives.”">b</a></sup>in the Spirit. Do you really think you can now make yourselves perfect by your own human efforts?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “by human means.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Did you go through so much suffering for nothing? (It really wasn't for nothing was it?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> Let me ask you: does God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “the one.”">d</a></sup>give you the Spirit and do so many miracles among you because you keep the law, or is it because you trust in what you heard?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> It's just like Abraham who “trusted God, and was considered to be right.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 15:6.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> So you should acknowledge that those who trust in God are the children of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> In Scripture it was foreseen that God would make right the foreigners who trusted in him. The good news is revealed to Abraham beforehand with the words, “Through you all the nations will be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> Consequently those who trust in God are blessed along with Abraham who trusted God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> All those who rely on keeping the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="As a means of salvation.">f</a></sup>are under a curse, for as Scripture says, “Cursed is everyone who doesn't carefully obey everything that's written in the book of the law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> Clearly nobody is made right with God by attempting to keep the law, for “Those who are made right will live by trusting God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Habakkuk 2:4.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> Obedience to the law has nothing to do with trusting God. Scripture only says, “You will live if you observe everything the law requires.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 18:5.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ has rescued us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“By becoming a curse for us”: meaning by experiencing the consequences of sin. The idea that this was an imposed curse by God is not the meaning here. It is sin itself that brings the curse (see Romans 6:23).">i</a></sup>As Scripture says, “Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 21:23.">j</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> so that through Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham could come to the foreigners as well, and we could receive the promise of the Spirit by trusting God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brothers and sisters, here's an example from daily life. If a contract is drawn up and agreed, signed and sealed, nobody can ignore it or add to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> Now the promises were given to Abraham, and to his son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally, “seed.” See Genesis 12:7and Genesis 13:15.">k</a></sup>It doesn't say, “sons” as if plural, but singular: “and to your son,” meaning Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> Let me explain. The law, coming four hundred and thirty years later, doesn't cancel the previous agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">l</a></sup>that God made, breaking the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> If the inheritance is derived from obedience to the law, it no longer comes from the promise. But God graciously gave it to Abraham by means of the promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> What was the point of the law, then? It was added to show what wrongdoing really is, until the son came to whom the promise had been made. The law was put in place by angels through the hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> But a mediator isn't needed when there's only one person involved. And God is one!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The idea here is that the Old Testament law needed a mediator (Moses). But in the case of the promise this was made directly to Abraham, and according the argument that Paul is developing here, directly fulfilled that promise through Jesus Christ. In this way, says Paul, the promise and its fulfillment are superior to the law.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> So does the law work against God's promises? Of course not! For if there was a law that could give life, then we could be made right by keeping it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> But Scripture tells us that we all are prisoners of sin. The only way we can receive God's promises is by trusting in Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> Before we trusted in Jesus we remained in the custody of the law until this way of trusting was revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> The law was our guardian until Christ came, so that we could be made right by trusting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> But now this way of trusting Jesus has come, we no longer need such a guardian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> For you are all God's children through your trust in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> All of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There's no longer Jew or Greek, slave or free, male or female—you are all one in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> If you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's children, and you are heirs of the promise!</span>
</div>
</div>
