<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> However, the Spirit very clearly explains that in the last times some will abandon their trust in God, and will listen to deceptive spirits and beliefs that come from demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> These hypocritical liars, whose consciences have been seared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> tell people to not marry and not to eat certain foods made by God that should be accepted with thanks by those who trust and know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> Everything created by God is good, and nothing should be rejected but should be received with thanks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> since it is made sacred by the word of God and by prayer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> If you point out these things to the brothers and sisters you will be a good minister<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word is actually the one used for “deacon.”">a</a></sup>of Christ Jesus. You gain strength by trusting in the truth, and in the good teaching you've followed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> Reject what is irreligious, and old wives' tales. Make sure your exercise is spiritual—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; PSALMS 37:3–4"> for while physical exercise is useful to some extent, spiritual exercise is much more useful. For “it has promise for the present life, and for the life to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> You can trust this saying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Assuming the saying is the previous verse.">b</a></sup>that should be accepted by everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> The reason we work and do the best we can is because our hope is in the living God. He is the Savior of all people, especially of those who trust in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> This is what you should teach. Tell everyone to follow your instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young. Be an example to those who trust in God—in the way you speak, the kind of life you live, in love, in trust, and in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Until I get there, make sure you read the Scriptures to the church, and encourage them by your speaking and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Don't neglect the gracious gift you have that was given to you by prophetic inspiration when the church elders laid their hands on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; PSALMS 143:5"> Think carefully about these things, and dedicate yourself completely to them so that everyone can see the progress you're making.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Mind what you're doing and what you're teaching. Keep going with all of this, because as you do so you'll save both yourself and those who listen to you.</span>
</div>
