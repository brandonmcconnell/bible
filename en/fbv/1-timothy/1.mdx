<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> This letter comes from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus appointed by the authority of God our Savior and Christ Jesus, who is our hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:14–17"> I'm sending it to you Timothy. You are my true son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Actually “little child.”">a</a></sup>because of your trust in God. May you have grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> When I was on my way to Macedonia I asked you to stay behind in Ephesus, so that you could speak with those who teach false ideas and insist that they stop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> They shouldn't concern themselves with legends and never-ending obsessions about ancestry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “endless genealogies”—a situation that is not clear from the context.">b</a></sup>Such ideas only lead to pointless debates, instead of understanding God as we trust in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> The reason why I insist on this is so we may have love that comes from a pure heart, from a clear conscience and sincere trust in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> Some have deviated from these things, and have ended up talking nonsense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> They have ambitions to be teachers of the law, but they have no idea what they're talking about or what they're so boldly announcing!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; PSALMS 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> Now we recognize that the law is good if people use it properly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> We also know that law isn't laid down for those who do what is good and right, but for those who are rebellious and ignore the law. It applies to people who have no use for God, who are sinful, who treat nothing as holy and are completely irreligious. It's for those who kill fathers and mothers, for murderers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> for those who are sexually immoral, homosexuals, kidnappers, liars, false witnesses—and anything else that's opposed to good teachings</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> as determined by the wonderful good news of our blessed God which he entrusted to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I'm just so grateful to Christ Jesus our Lord for the strength he's given me, and that he considered me trustworthy, appointing me to work for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> Even though I used to insult God, and persecuted and abused God's people, God showed me mercy because of my ignorance and unbelief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Our Lord in his grace filled me to overflowing with trust and love that comes from Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> You can trust this saying that everyone should accept: “Christ Jesus came to this world to save sinners,” and I'm the worst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> For this reason I was shown mercy—since I'm the worst sinner, Jesus Christ could demonstrate his infinite patience as an example to those who choose to trust in him and gain eternal life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Honor and glory forever and ever to the eternal King, the immortal, invisible, and only God! Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> These are the instructions I want to give to you, Timothy, my son, following the prophecies that brought you this far,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Greek is ambiguous—it could refer to prophecies about Timothy, or that Timothy followed the prophecies…">c</a></sup>so you can fight the good fight!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> Keep on trusting God and make sure you have a clear conscience. Some have refused to do this and have shipwrecked their trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Hymenaeus and Alexander are such people, and I have “handed them over to Satan”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The phrase “handed over to Satan” is not explained and could mean being “excommunicated” from the church community, or allowing them to experience the results of their sins. The fact they are supposed to learn something indicates that Paul believed their redemption was still possible.">d</a></sup>to teach them not to misrepresent God.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
