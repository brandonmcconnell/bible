<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> Pray like this for kings and all types of leaders so that we may live quiet and peaceful lives, always thinking about God and taking life seriously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> This is what is good, and what pleases God our Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> He wants everyone to be saved, and come to understand what truth really is.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God, and one mediator of God and mankind—the man Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> He gave himself so we could all be won back, demonstrating the evidence at the right time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> I was appointed to share this message and be its messenger,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “an apostle.”">a</a></sup>a teacher to the foreigners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">b</a></sup>about trusting God and the truth (I'm not lying, I'm telling the truth!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 134:2; PSALMS 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> What I really want is for men everywhere to pray sincerely to God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “holding up holy hands.”">c</a></sup>No anger or arguments!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; PSALMS 149:4"> In the same way, women should dress sensibly, modestly, and appropriately. They should be attractive not in their hairstyle or by wearing gold or pearls or expensive clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but by the good things they do—as is appropriate for women who claim to follow God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Women should learn quietly, respecting their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> I don't allow women to be instructors, or to dominate men; let them remain quiet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or tranquil, calm.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was made first, and then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> Adam wasn't deceived, but Eve was completely deceived, and she fell into sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> However, women will be saved through becoming mothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Through child-bearing.” The meaning and implication of this verse are much debated…">e</a></sup>as long as they continue to trust and love, and to live holy, sensible lives.</span>
