<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Don't rebuke a man who is older than you. Instead, encourage him as if he were your father. Treat younger men as brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Matthew 12:50; Philippians 4:8"> older women as mothers, younger women as sisters, with the highest standards of decency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Help widows who don't have a family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> For the Christian responsibility of a widow's children or grandchildren is to do their duty for their own family, and repay their parents by helping them out. This is what pleases God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> Now a real widow, having no family, alone and without support, puts her hope in God and prays for help night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> But a widow who concentrates on pleasing herself is already dead, even though she's still physically alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Give people these instructions so that they will be above criticism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But those who don't look after their relatives, especially their own family, have denied their beliefs, and are worse than those who don't believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> Only widows over sixty who have been faithful to their husbands should be put on the list.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The list of those widows who should be helped by the church.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> The widow should have a reputation for doing good. Did she bring up children properly? Has she been hospitable? Has she washed the feet of church members? Has she helped those who were in trouble? Has she really tried to do good in every way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> Don't take on widows who are younger, because when their physical desires make them want to remarry they abandon their dedication to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> In this they are guilty of breaking their previous commitment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The idea here is that the widows have made a commitment to serve Christ in the church, and they are helped by the church because of this. To remarry would annul this commitment.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> They also get used to a lazy life, just visiting each other in their homes. Not only lazy, but they gossip and meddle, talking about things they shouldn't.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> So my best advice is that younger widows marry and have children and take care of the home. That way there's no opportunity for criticism from the Enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> However, some have already gone the way of Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> Any Christian woman who has widows in the family should help them, so that the church is not burdened with the responsibility, and can help those widows who are truly in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Elders who direct the church well should be seen as doubly valuable,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “should receive adequate pay.”">c</a></sup>particularly those who work in speaking the Word and in teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> As Scripture says, “Don't muzzle the ox when it's threshing out the grain.” Also, “The worker deserves to be paid.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 25:4; see also Luke 10:7.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Don't entertain any accusation against an elder unless two or three witnesses support it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Censure those who sin in front of everyone as a warning to others as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Before God and Christ Jesus, and the holy angels, I instruct you to follow these instructions without bias. Don't do anything from an attitude of favoritism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Don't be in a hurry to lay hands on anyone; and don't get involved in the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> Don't just drink water, but use a little bit of wine because of your bad stomach—you're sick so often!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> The sins of some people are really obvious, and they are clearly guilty, but the sins of others are not seen until later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; PSALMS 37:5–6"> Similarly some good works are obvious, and even those that are hidden won't be for long.</span>
</div>
