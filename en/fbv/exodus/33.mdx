<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Exodus 32:13; Exodus 32:7"> Then the Lord told Moses, “Leave this place, you and the people you led out of Egypt, and go to the land I promised with an oath to give to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, telling them, ‘I will give this land to your descendants.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 23:27–31"> I will send an angel out in front of you, and I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:9–10; Exodus 3:8; Acts 7:51"> Enter a land flowing with milk and honey, but I will not accompany you because you are a rebellious people. Otherwise I would destroy you on the way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:39; Numbers 14:1; Ezekiel 26:16"> When the people heard these words of criticism, they mourned and didn't put on their jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Numbers 16:45–46; Exodus 33:3–4"> For the Lord had previously told Moses, “Tell the people of Israel, ‘You are a rebellious people. If I was with you for just a moment, I would wipe you out. Now remove your jewelry, and I'll decide what to do with you.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Exodus 32:3; Jeremiah 2:19"> So the Israelites took off their jewelry from the time they left Mount Sinai.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Mount Sinai”: literally, “Mount Horeb,” another name for the same mountain.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; Exodus 29:42–43; Proverbs 15:29"> Moses used to set up the Tent of Meeting some way outside the camp. Anyone who wanted to ask the Lord anything could go out there to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:27"> Whenever Moses went out to the Tent, all the people would go and stand at the entrance of their tents. They would watch him until he had gone inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 99:7; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 13:21–22"> As soon as Moses went into the Tent, the cloud column would descend and stand in the doorway as the Lord spoke with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:22; 1 Kings 8:14; Exodus 4:31"> When the people saw the cloud column standing in the doorway to the Tent, everyone would stand up and bow in worship at the entrance of their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 12:8; Deuteronomy 34:10; Exodus 24:13"> Moses would speak to the Lord face to face as you would talk to a friend, and then return to the camp. However, his young assistant Joshua, son of Nun, stayed in the Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 33:17; Jeremiah 1:5; John 10:14–15"> Moses said to the Lord, “Look, you've been telling me, ‘Go and lead these people,’ but you haven't let me know who you're going to send with me. And yet you have stated, ‘I know you personally,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Personally”: literally “by name.” Also in verse 17.">b</a></sup>and I'm happy with you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 119:33; PSALMS 25:4; PSALMS 27:11"> Now if it's true that you're happy with me, please teach me your ways so I can get to know you, and go on pleasing you. Remember that the people of this nation are yours.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 21:44; Matthew 11:28; Joshua 1:5"> The Lord replied, “I myself will go with you, and I will support you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Support you”: literally, “give you rest.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; PSALMS 4:6; Exodus 33:3"> “If you don't go with us yourself, then please don't take us away from here,” Moses responded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:7; Numbers 14:14; 1 Kings 8:53"> “How will others know that you are happy with me, and with your people, if you don't accompany us? How would anyone tell us apart—me and your people—from every other people who live on the earth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; 1 John 5:14–15; Exodus 33:12"> The Lord told Moses, “I promise to do what you've asked, because I'm happy with you and I know you personally.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Exodus 33:20; Titus 2:13"> “Now please reveal to me your glory,” Moses asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:15–18; Ephesians 1:6–8; Jeremiah 31:14"> “I will make all the goodness of my character pass in front of you, I will call out the name ‘Yahweh,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Yahweh”: this is the word usually translated as “the Lord,” so in the following verses note that “Yahweh” and “the Lord” are the same.">d</a></sup>I will show grace to those I want to show grace, and I will show mercy to those I want to show mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> But you won't be able to see my face, because no one can see my face and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:31; Zechariah 3:7; Joshua 20:4"> “Come here and stand by me on this rock,” the Lord went on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 91:1; Isaiah 2:21; PSALMS 91:4"> “and as my glory goes by I'll put you in a crevice of the rock, and I'll cover you with my hand until I have passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 33:20; John 1:18; 1 Timothy 6:16"> Then I'll take my hand away and you'll see my back; but you won't see my face.”</span>
