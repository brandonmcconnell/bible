<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> When Aaron saw this, he built an altar in front of the golden calf and shouted out, “Tomorrow will be a festival to honor the Lord!”</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> They have been so quick to abandon the way I ordered them to follow. They're saying, ‘These are the gods that brought you out of the land of Egypt.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:13; Isaiah 48:4"> “I know what these people are like,” the Lord continued saying to Moses. “They are so rebellious!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Rebellious” or “perverse”: the image is of a horse being tugged by the reins in one direction but deliberately going in the opposite direction. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> Now leave me! I am angry with them—let me finish them off! I will make you into a great nation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; PSALMS 106:23"> But Moses pleaded with the Lord his God, saying, “Why are you angry with the people you brought out of the land of Egypt with tremendous power and great strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> Why should the Egyptians be able to say, ‘He brought them out with the evil purpose of killing them in the mountains, wiping them off the face of the earth’? Turn from your fierce anger. Please change your mind over the threat against your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> Remember you swore a promise your servants Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Jacob”: literally, “Israel.”">c</a></sup>telling them, ‘I will make your descendants as numerous as the stars of heaven, and give you all the land I promised to them, and they shall own it forever.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:10; PSALMS 106:45; Jeremiah 26:13"> The Lord changed his mind over the disaster he threatened to cause his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> Moses turned and went down the mountain, carrying the two stone tablets of the Law written on both sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> God had made the tablets, and God had engraved the writing himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> When Joshua heard all the shouting from the camp, he said to Moses, “It sounds like fighting in the camp!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> But Moses replied, “These are not the shouts of victory or of defeat. What I'm hearing is people partying!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremiah 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> As he approached the camp he saw the bull calf idol and the dancing. He got so angry that he threw down the stone tablets and smashed them there at the foot of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Kings 23:6; 2 Kings 23:15"> He took the bull calf and burned it, and ground it into powder. Then he mixed this with water and made the Israelites drink it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; 1 Kings 21:22"> Then Moses asked Aaron, “What did these people do to you that you made them sin so badly?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> “Please don't get angry with me, my lord,” Aaron replied, “You yourself know how liable these people are to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> They told me, ‘Make some gods for us who can lead us because this man Moses who brought us out of the land of Egypt—we don't know what's happened to him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> So I said to them, ‘Anybody who has gold jewelry, take it off and give it to me.’ I threw the gold into the furnace and out came this bull calf.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> Moses saw the people going completely wild because Aaron had let them, and that this had brought ridicule on them from their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Matthew 12:30; 2 Samuel 20:11"> So he went and stood at the entrance to the camp, and shouted out, “Whoever is on the Lord's side, come and join me!” All the Levites gathered around him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> Moses told them, “This is what the Lord, the God of Israel says: Each of you strap on a sword. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachi 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> The Levites did what Moses had told them, and that day around 3,000 men were killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zechariah 13:3"> Moses told the Levites, “Today you have dedicated yourselves to the Lord because you took action against your sons and brothers. Today you have gained a blessing for yourselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:20; Numbers 25:13; 2 Kings 17:21"> The following day Moses spoke to the people, saying, “You have sinned very badly. But now I will go up to the Lord. Maybe I can get him to forgive your sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> So Moses went back to the Lord. He said, “Please—the people have sinned very badly by making gods of gold for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> But now, if you will, forgive their sin. Otherwise just blot me out of the scroll in which you keep your records.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Ezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> But the Lord replied to Moses, “It's those who sinned against me who will be blotted out of my scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> Now go and lead the people to the place I told you about. Look, my angel will go before you, but at the time I decide to punish, I will punish them for their sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> The Lord brought a plague on the people because they made Aaron make the bull calf.</span>
