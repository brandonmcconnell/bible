<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> After this Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and told him, “This is what the Lord, the God of Israel says, ‘Let my people go so they can hold a religious festival for me in the desert.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> “Who is this ‘Lord’ that I should listen to his request to let Israel go?” Pharaoh replied. “I don't know the Lord and I certainly won't let Israel leave!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> “The God of the Hebrews came to us,” they added. “Please allow us to go a three-day journey into the desert and offer sacrifices to the Lord our God. Otherwise he'll kill us by disease or by the sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> “Moses and Aaron, why are you trying to take people away from their assigned labor?” Pharaoh asked. “Get back to work!” he ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> “Look here,” he went on. “There are many of your people here in our country and you are preventing them from doing their assigned labor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> That very day he ordered the slave drivers and those in charge of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> “Don't give them any more straw to make bricks like before. Have them go and collect the straw themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:41"> But still make them produce the same quantity of bricks as before. They're lazy people—that's why they're calling out, asking, ‘Please let us go and offer sacrifices to our god.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Make their work harder so they can get on with it and not pay any attention to all these lies!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> So the slave drivers and those in charge went out and told the people, “This is what Pharaoh has ordered: ‘I won't give you any more straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> Go and get straw for yourselves wherever you can find it, because your work quota won't be reduced.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> So the people went all over Egypt collecting stubble for straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The slave drivers kept on forcing them, saying, “You still have to do the same work as you did when you had straw provided!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> They beat the Israelite supervisors they had put in charge, shouting at them, “Why haven't you met your quota of bricks as you did before?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The Israelite supervisors went to Pharaoh to complain, saying, “Why are you treating us your servants like this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> You don't give us any straw but your slave drivers demand that we make bricks, and they beat us! Your people are treating us badly!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> “No, you're just lazy, lazy people!” Pharaoh replied. “That's why you keep on asking, ‘Please let us go and offer sacrifices to the Lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> Now get out of here and go back to work! You won't be given any straw but you'll still have to produce the full quota of bricks!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> The Israelite supervisors realized they were in trouble when they were told, “You must not reduce the daily production of bricks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They went over to Moses and Aaron who were waiting for them after their meeting with Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> and said, “May the Lord see what you have done and judge you accordingly! You have made us like a bad smell to Pharaoh and his officials. You have put a sword in their hands to kill us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> Moses went back to the Lord and complained, “Why have you caused all this trouble for your own people, Lord? Was this why you sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> Ever since I went to Pharaoh to speak for you, he has been even harder on your people, and you haven't done anything to save them!”</span>
