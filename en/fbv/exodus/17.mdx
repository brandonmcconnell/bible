<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 19:2; Exodus 17:8"> Then all the Israelites left the Desert of Sin, going from place to place as they followed the Lord's commands. They camped at Rephidim, but there wasn't any water for the people to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 78:18; Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> Some of them came and complained to Moses, saying, “Give us water to drink!” “Why are you complaining to me?” Moses asked. “Why are you trying to challenge the Lord?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:2–3; Exodus 15:24"> But the people were so thirsty for water there that they went on complaining to Moses, saying, “Why did you have to bring us out of Egypt? Are you trying to kill us and our children and livestock by thirst?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:6; Numbers 14:10; John 8:59"> Moses cried out to the Lord, “What am I going to do with these people? A bit more of this and they're going to stone me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–20; Numbers 20:8–11; Acts 20:23–24"> The Lord told Moses, “Go ahead of the people, and take some of the elders of Israel with you. Take with you the walking stick you used to hit the Nile, and just go on ahead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Numbers 20:9–11; Nehemiah 9:15"> Look, I will stand there beside you by the rock at Horeb. When you hit the rock water will pour out for the people to drink.” So Moses did this as the elders of Israel watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 81:7; PSALMS 95:8; Numbers 20:13"> He called the place Massah and Meribah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Massah means “testing” and Meribah means “complaining.”">a</a></sup>because the Israelites argued there, and because they challenged the Lord, saying, “Is the Lord with us, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 36:12; Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Numbers 24:20"> Then some Amalekites came and attacked the Israelites at Rephidim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Deuteronomy 32:44; Exodus 4:20"> Moses told Joshua, “Choose some men and go out and fight the Amalekites. Tomorrow I'll stand on the top of this hill holding the walking stick of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; Exodus 31:2; Exodus 17:12"> Joshua did what Moses told him and fought the Amalekites, while Moses, Aaron, and Hur climbed to the top of the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; James 5:16; PSALMS 56:9"> As long as Moses held up the walking stick<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“The walking stick”: implied.">b</a></sup>in his hands, the Israelites were the ones winning, but when he lowered them, it was the Amalekites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Philippians 1:19; Hebrews 12:12"> So when Moses' hands became heavy, the others took a stone and put it under him for him to sit on. Aaron and Hur stood on each side of Moses and held his hands up. In this way his hands were kept firmly up until the sun went down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:37; Joshua 10:32"> As a result Joshua defeated the Amalekite army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:27; Numbers 24:20; 1 Samuel 30:1"> The Lord told Moses, “Write all this down on a scroll as a reminder and read it out loud to Joshua, because I'm going to completely wipe out the Amalekites so nobody on earth will remember them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Judges 6:24; PSALMS 60:4"> Moses built an altar and called it “The Lord is my Banner of Victory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 21:8–11; Isaiah 66:1; Acts 7:49"> “Hold up the victory banner of the Lord!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “a hand was lifted up towards the Lord's throne.” The Hebrew is unclear.">c</a></sup>Moses declared. “The Lord will go on fighting the Amalekites for all generations!”</span>
