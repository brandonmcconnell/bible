<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:1–5; Numbers 17:2–9"> Have your brother Aaron come to you, along with his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. They of all the Israelites are to serve me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 31:10; Revelation 19:8"> You are to have holy clothing made for your brother Aaron to make him look splendid and dignified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11; Isaiah 11:2"> You are to give instructions to all the skilful workers, to those who have received from me their abilities, as to how to make clothing for Aaron's dedication, so that he can serve me as priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Revelation 9:17; Isaiah 59:17"> These are the clothes for them to make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a pleated tunic, a turban, and a sash. These are the holy clothes they shall make for your brother Aaron and his sons so that they can serve me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:3–4; Exodus 39:2–3"> The workers are to use gold thread, together with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:2–7; Exodus 26:1"> They are to make the ephod of finely-woven linen embroidered with gold, and with blue, purple, and crimson thread, skillfully worked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 39:4"> Two shoulder pieces are to be attached to the front and back pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:27–28; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:5"> The waistband of the ephod is to be one piece made in the same way, using gold thread, with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and with finely-woven linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Chronicles 2:7; Isaiah 49:16"> Engrave on two onyx stones the names of the Israelite tribes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–4; Genesis 43:33"> six names on one stone and six on the other, in birth order.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“In birth order”: literally, “as their generations.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; Revelation 7:2; Jeremiah 22:24"> Engrave the names on the two stones the same way a jeweler engraves a personal seal. Then place the stones in ornamental gold settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:29; Joshua 4:7; Exodus 39:6–7"> Attach both stones to the shoulder pieces of the ephod as reminders for the Israelite tribes. Aaron is to wear their names on his two shoulders to remind the Israelites that he represents them when he goes in to the Lord's presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Make ornamental gold settings</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:17–18; 1 Kings 7:17"> and two braided chains of pure gold, and fasten these chains to the settings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:30; Exodus 26:1; Exodus 28:4"> You are also to make a breastpiece for decisions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“For decisions”: the breastpiece was to hold the Urim and the Thummin used for determining the Lord's will and decisions on different questions (see verse 30).">b</a></sup>in the same skilful way as the ephod, to be used in determining the Lord's will. Make it using gold thread, with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and with finely-woven linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It has to be square when folded, measuring around nine inches<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Nine inches”: literally, “a span,” the distance between thumb and little finger when the hand is stretched.">c</a></sup>in length and width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Exodus 39:10–21; Proverbs 8:11"> Attach an arrangement of precious stones in four rows as follows.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="None of the following stones are identified with certainty.">d</a></sup>In the first row carnelian, peridot, and emerald.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Job 28:6; Song of Songs 5:14"> In the second row turquoise, lapis lazuli, and sardonyx.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:12; Exodus 39:12"> In the third row jacinth, agate, and amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Revelation 4:3; Daniel 10:6"> In the fourth row topaz, beryl, and jasper. Place these stones in ornamental gold settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:12; Revelation 7:4–8; 1 Kings 18:31"> Each of the twelve stones will be engraved like a personal seal with the name of one of the twelve Israelite tribes and will represent them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Make cords of braided chains from pure gold to attach the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–15"> Make two gold rings and fasten them to the two top corners of the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Attach the two gold chains to the two gold rings on the corners of the breastpiece,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:4; Exodus 28:14"> and then fasten the opposite ends of the two chains to the ornamental gold settings on the shoulder pieces of the front side of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Make two more gold rings and attach them to the two lower corners of the breastpiece, on the inside edge next to the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Make two more gold rings and attach them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the front side of the ephod, near where it joins its woven waistband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:38; Exodus 28:31; Exodus 39:30–31"> Tie the rings of the breastpiece to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue thread, so that the breastpiece won't come loose from the ephod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:30; Jeremiah 30:21"> Like this, whenever Aaron enters the Holy Place, he will wear the names of the Israelite tribes over his heart on the breastpiece, as a constant reminder before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Ezra 2:63; Numbers 27:21"> Place the Urim and Thummim in the breastpiece of decision, so that they too will be over Aaron's heart whenever he comes into the Lord's presence. Aaron will continually carry the means of gaining decisions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Means of gaining decisions”: referring to the Urim and the Thummim.">e</a></sup>over his heart before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 39:22–26"> Make the robe that goes with the ephod exclusively from blue cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:16; John 19:23–24; Exodus 39:28"> with an opening in the middle at the top. Stitch a woven collar around the opening to strengthen it so it won't tear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 39:24–26; Zechariah 14:20; 1 Kings 7:18"> Make pomegranates using blue, purple, and crimson thread and attach them all the way around its hem, with gold bells between them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Song of Songs 8:2; John 15:4–8; Colossians 1:10"> having the gold bells and the pomegranates alternate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Leviticus 16:2"> Aaron is to wear the robe whenever he serves, and the sound it makes will be heard when he enters or leaves the sanctuary as he goes into the Lord's presence, so that he won't die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 14:20; Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 39:30"> Make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it like a seal, “Holy to the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:6; Exodus 28:4; Zechariah 3:5"> Attach it to the front of the turban using a blue cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 10:17"> Aaron will wear it on his forehead, so that he may take responsibility for the guilt of the offerings the Israelites make, and this applies to all their holy gifts. It must always remain on his forehead in order that the people are accepted in the Lord's presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 45:14"> Weave the tunic with finely-spun linen and make the turban of the same material, and also make a sash and have it embroidered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 39:41; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:2"> Make tunics, sashes, and headdresses for Aaron's sons, so that they will look splendid and dignified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:1–36; Exodus 40:15"> Put these clothes on your brother Aaron and his sons and then anoint them and ordain them. Dedicate them so that they can serve me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; Leviticus 6:10; Leviticus 16:4"> Make linen undershorts to cover their bare bodies, reaching from waist to thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 20:26; Leviticus 5:17"> Aaron and his sons are to wear them when they enter the Tent of Meeting or when they approach the altar to serve in the Holy Place, so that they will not bring guilt on themselves and die. This is a law for Aaron and his descendants forever.</span>
</div>
</div>
