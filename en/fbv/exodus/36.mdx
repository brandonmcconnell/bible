<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> “So Bezalel, Oholiab, and all the other craftsmen with the necessary expertise, given skill and ability by the Lord, are to work out how to accomplish all the work of constructing the sanctuary as commanded by the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> Moses summoned Bezalel, Oholiab, and all the skilled people who had been given special abilities by the Lord, everyone willing to come and do the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> Moses gave them everything the Israelites had contributed to carry out the work of constructing the sanctuary. In the meantime the people went on bringing freewill offerings every morning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> so much so that all the craftsmen who were working on the sanctuary stopped what they were doing</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> and went and told Moses, “The people have already brought enough to complete the work the Lord has ordered us to do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Moses gave the order, and an announcement was made throughout the camp: “Men and women, don't bring anything more as an offering for the sanctuary.” So the people were stopped from bringing anything more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> since there was already more than enough to do all the work necessary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> The skilled craftsmen among the workers made the ten curtains for the Tabernacle. They were made of finely-spun linen together with blue, purple, and crimson thread, embroidered with cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Each curtain was twenty-eight cubits long by four cubits wide, and they were all the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; PSALMS 122:3"> They joined together five of the curtains as one set, and the other five as a second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> They used blue material to make loops on the edge of the last curtain of both sets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> They made fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the last curtain of the second set, lining up the loops with each another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> They also made fifty gold clips and joined the curtains together with the clips, so that the Tabernacle was a single structure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> They made eleven curtains of goat hair as a tent to cover the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Each of the eleven curtains was the same size—thirty cubits long by four cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They joined five of the curtains together as one set and the other six as another set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They made fifty loops on the edge of the last curtain in the first set, and fifty loops on the edge of the last curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They made fifty bronze clips to join the tent together as a single cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> They made a covering for the goat hair tent from tanned ram skins, and placed an extra covering of fine leather over that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> They made an upright framework of acacia wood for the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Each frame was ten cubits long by one and a half cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Each frame had two pegs so the frames could be connected to each other. They made all the frames of the Tabernacle like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> They made twenty frames for the south side of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They made forty silver stands as supports for the twenty frames using two stands per frame, one under every frame peg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Similarly for the north side of the Tabernacle, they made twenty frames</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and forty silver stands, two stands per frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> They made six frames for the back (west side) of the Tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> along with two frames for its two back corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> They joined these corner frames at the bottom and at the top near to the first ring. This is how they made the two corner frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> In total there were eight frames and sixteen silver stands, two under each frame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> They made five crossbars of acacia wood to hold together the frames on the south side of the Tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> five for those on the north, and five for those at the back of the Tabernacle, to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> They made the central crossbar which was placed halfway up the frames and ran from one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> They covered the frames with gold, and made gold rings to hold the crossbars in place. They covered the crossbars with gold too.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> They made a veil out of blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen, embroidered with cherubim by someone who was skilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> They made four posts of acacia wood for it and covered them with gold. They made gold hooks for the posts and cast their four silver stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> They made a screen for the entrance to the tent using blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen, and had it embroidered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> They also made five posts of acacia wood with hooks to hang the screen. They covered the tops of the posts and their bands with gold, and their five stands were made of bronze.</span>
</div>
</div>
