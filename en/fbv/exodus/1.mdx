<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:1–2; Genesis 46:8–26; Genesis 35:23–26"> These were the names of the sons of Israel (Jacob) who came with him to Egypt along with their families:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:22"> Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Genesis 35:23"> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> Jacob had 70 descendants there, including Joseph who was already in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> Eventually Joseph and all his brothers and all that generation died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> However, the Israelites had many children and their numbers increased rapidly. In fact there were so many of them that they became very powerful—the country was full of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> Then a new king came to power who didn't know anything about Joseph.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It is thought to refer to a different Egyptian dynasty.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> He conferred with his fellow Egyptians and said, “Look at these Israelites—there are more of them than us, and they're more powerful than us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; PSALMS 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Let's deal wisely with them before they become so many that if there's a war they'll side with our enemies and fight us, and flee the country.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> So the Egyptians made them do forced labor and put taskmasters in charge of them. They used them to build the storage towns of Pithom and Rameses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> But the more the Israelites were mistreated, the more they grew in numbers and spread out—and the more the Egyptians detested<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Detested” or “feared.”">b</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> The Egyptians worked the Israelites brutally,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> making their lives a misery. They made them do hard labor, building with mortar and brick, and all kinds of heavy work in the fields. In all of this hard labor they treated them brutally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the king gave orders to the Hebrew midwives, Shiphrah and Puah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> He told them, “When you assist the Hebrew women during childbirth, if you see it's a boy, kill him; but if it's a girl, let her live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> But because the midwives revered God, they didn't do what the king of Egypt had ordered. They let the boys live as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> The king of Egypt called the midwives in and demanded to know, “Why have you done this— letting the male children live?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> “Hebrew women aren't like Egyptian women,” the midwives told Pharaoh. “They give birth more easily—they have them before we midwives arrive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> God treated the midwives well, and the people increased in number so there were even more of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; PSALMS 37:3"> Because the midwives revered God, he gave them families of their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> Then Pharaoh issued this order to all his people: “Throw every Hebrew boy that's born into the Nile, but let every girl live.”</span>
