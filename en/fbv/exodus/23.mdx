<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 101:5; Proverbs 19:5; Deuteronomy 19:16–21"> “You must not spread stories that are lies. You must not help bad people by giving evidence that's malicious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–11; Proverbs 1:15; Luke 23:23–24"> Don't follow the crowd in doing wrong. When you give evidence in a lawsuit, don't corrupt justice by siding with the majority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; Leviticus 19:15; PSALMS 82:2–3"> Don't show favoritism to poor people in their legal cases either.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Either”: supplied for clarity. Justice has to be even-handed, so showing favoritism to any party is wrong. However, the more usual problem is the denial of justice to the poor (see for example verse 6).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:1–4; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:44"> If you come across your enemy's ox or donkey that has wandered off, take it back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> If you see the donkey of someone who hates you that has fallen under its load, don't just leave it there. You must stop and help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> You must not prevent the poor from getting justice in their lawsuits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Exodus 34:7; Exodus 23:1"> Don't have anything to do with making false accusations. Don't kill the innocent or those who do right, for I won't let the guilty go unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> Don't accept bribes, for a bribe blinds those who can see, and undermines the evidence of the honest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> Don't abuse foreigners living among you, since you know very well what it's like to be foreigners, for you were once foreigners in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> Six years you are to sow your land and harvest crops,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> but in the seventh year you are to let it rest and leave it uncultivated, so that poor people may eat what grows naturally<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“What grows naturally”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup>from the field and the wild animals can finish what's left. Follow the same procedure for your vineyards and olive groves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> You have six days to do your work, but on the seventh day you must stop working, so that your ox and your donkey can rest, and your slaves' families can catch their breath, as well as the foreigners living among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> Make sure to pay attention to everything I've told you. Don't think to call on the names of other gods—you must not even mention them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Three times every year you are to celebrate a feast dedicated to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> You are to observe the Festival of Unleavened Bread as I instructed you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See chapter 13.">c</a></sup>You are to eat bread without yeast for seven days at the appropriate time in the month of Abib, because that was the month you left Egypt. No one can come before me without bringing an offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> You are also to observe the Festival of Harvest when you present the firstfruits of the produce from what you've sown in the fields. Lastly you are to observe the Festival of Gathering-In the Harvest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The more familiar name, given later, is the Festival of Shelters.">d</a></sup>at the end of the year, when you gather in the harvest of the rest of your crops from the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Every Israelite male is to come before the Lord God at these three times every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> You must not offer the blood of my sacrifices together with anything that contains yeast, and the fat from the offerings presented at my festival must not be left until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> Bring the best firstfruits of your crops to the house of the Lord your God. Don't cook a young goat in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; PSALMS 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> Look, I'm sending an angel ahead of you to protect you on the way and to take you to the place I've prepared for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 78:56; PSALMS 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Make sure you pay attention to him and do what he says. Don't oppose him, because he won't forgive rebellion, for he carries my authority.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Carries my authority”: literally, “my name is in his midst.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> However, if you listen to him carefully, and do everything that I tell you, then I will be an enemy to your enemies and I will fight those who fight against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> For my angel will go ahead of you and take you into the land of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I will wipe them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> You must not bow down to their gods or worship them or follow their pagan practices. No, you must demolish their idols and smash their sacred pillars into pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> You are to worship the Lord your God, and he will bless your food and water. I will make sure none of you fall sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> No woman will have a miscarriage or be without children. I will make sure you live long lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> I will send terror about me ahead of you which will throw every nation you meet into panic. I will make all your enemies turn and run away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> I will send hornets<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Hornets”: the meaning of the word used here is debated. Some see it in a similar way to the “terror” of the previous verse that causes panic.">f</a></sup>ahead of you to drive out before you the Hivites and Canaanites and Hittites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not drive them out in just one year, because the land would become desolate and you would have to deal with increased numbers of wild animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Bit by bit I will drive them out ahead of you, until there are enough of you to take possession of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> I will fix your borders from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Sea of the Philistines”: the Mediterranean.">g</a></sup>and from the desert to the Euphrates River. I will hand the inhabitants of the land over to you, and you will drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> You must not make any agreement with them or with their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> They must not be allowed to stay in your land, otherwise they will lead you to sin against me. For if you worship their gods, they will definitely become a trap for you.”</span>
