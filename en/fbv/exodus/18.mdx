<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; PSALMS 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> Moses' father-in-law Jethro,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Also called Reuel in chapter 2.">a</a></sup>the priest of Midian, heard about everything God had done for Moses and his people the Israelites, and how the Lord had led them out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> When Moses had sent home his wife Zipporah, his father-in-law Jethro had welcomed her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; PSALMS 39:12"> together with her two sons. One son was named Gershom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 2:22.">b</a></sup>for Moses had said, “I have been a foreigner in a foreign land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> The other son was named Eliezer,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Meaning, “my God is my help.”">c</a></sup>because Moses had said, “The God of my father was my helper, and saved me from death at Pharaoh's hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> Moses' father-in-law Jethro, together with Moses' wife and sons, came to see him in the desert at the camp near the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Moses was told beforehand, “I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to see you together with your wife and her two sons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> Moses went out to meet his father-in-law and bowed down and kissed him. They asked each other how they were and then went into the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; PSALMS 107:2"> Moses told his father-in-law about everything the Lord had done to Pharaoh and the Egyptians on behalf of the Israelites, about all the troubles they had experienced on the way, and about how the Lord had saved them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> Jethro was delighted to hear about all the good things the Lord had done for Israel when he'd saved them from the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> Jethro announced, “May the Lord be blessed, who saved you from the Egyptians and from Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> This is so convincing to me that the Lord is greater than all other gods, for he saved the people from the Egyptians when they acted so arrogantly towards the Israelites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> Then Jethro presented a burnt offering and sacrifices to God, and Aaron came with all the elders of Israel to eat a meal with him in God's presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> The following day Moses sat as a judge for the people, and they presented their cases to him from morning to evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When his father-in-law saw everything that Moses was doing for the people, he asked, “What's all this you're doing for the people? Why are you sitting alone as judge, with everyone presenting their cases to you from morning to evening?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> “Because the people come to me to ask God for his decision,” Moses replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> “When they argue over something, the case is brought before me to decide between them, and I explain to them God's laws and regulations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Jethro told him, “What you're doing is not the best.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> You, and these people who come to you, are all going to wear yourselves out, because the workload is far too heavy. You can't handle it alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> So please listen to me. I'm going to give you some advice, and God will be with you. Yes, you must continue to be the people's representative before God, and take their cases to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; PSALMS 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> Go on teaching them the laws and regulations. Show them how to live and the work they are to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> But now you should choose men who are competent from among the people, men who respect God and who are trustworthy and not corrupt. Put them in charge of the people as leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> These men are to judge the people on a continuing basis. Major issues they can bring to you, but they can decide all the small matters themselves. In this way your burden will be made lighter as they share it with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If you follow my advice, and if it's what God tells you to do, then you will be able to survive, and all these people can go home satisfied that their cases have been heard.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Satisfied that their cases have been heard”: literally, “in peace.” The word shalom however means more than peace, it also has the meaning of well-being and harmony within the community.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> Moses listened to what his father-in-law said and followed all his advice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> So Moses chose competent men from all of Israel and put them in charge of the people as leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> They acted as judges for the people on a continuing basis. They brought the difficult cases to Moses, but they would judge the small matters themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> Then Moses sent Jethro on his way, and he went back to his own country.</span>
</div>
</div>
