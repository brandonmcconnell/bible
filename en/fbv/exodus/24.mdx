<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> Then he sent out some young Israelite men who went and offered burnt offerings and sacrificed young bulls as peace offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> Moses put half of the blood into bowls, and sprinkled the other half on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:11–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; Exodus 24:3–4"> Then he picked up the Book of the Agreement and read it to the people. They replied, “We will do everything the Lord says. We will obey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> So Moses took the blood, sprinkled it on the people, and said, “Look, this is the blood of the agreement that the Lord has made with you following these terms.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of Israel's elders climbed up the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> and they saw the God of Israel. Under his feet was something like a tiled pavement made of lapis lazuli, as clear blue as the sky itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> But God did not harm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Harm”: this was because of the expectation that anyone who saw God would die (Genesis 32:30; Judges 6:22), endorsed by God himself (33:20)">b</a></sup>the leaders of Israel—they saw him, and they ate and drank a sacred meal.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“A sacred meal”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> Then the Lord told Moses, “Come up the mountain to me, and stay here, so I can give you the stone tablets, with the instructions and commands I have written for them to learn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> So Moses left with Joshua his attendant and climbed up the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> He told the elders, “Stay here and wait for us to return. Aaron and Hur are with you. If anyone has a problem, they can talk with them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> As Moses climbed up on the mountain, the cloud covered it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> The Lord's glory came down on Mount Sinai, covering it for six days. On the seventh day the Lord called to Moses from inside the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> To the Israelites the Lord's glory looked like a burning fire on the top of the mountain.</span>
