<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> It was around this time that a man from the tribe of Levi married a woman, also a Levite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; PSALMS 112:5"> She became pregnant and had a son. She saw he was a lovely baby, and she hid him for three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> But when she couldn't hide him anymore, she got a papyrus basket and covered it with tar and pitch. Then she put her baby in the basket and placed it among the reeds along the bank of the Nile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> His sister waited some distance away, keeping an eye on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 9:9"> Then Pharaoh's daughter arrived to bathe in the Nile. Her ladies-in-waiting were walking along the bank of the river. When she saw the basket among the reeds, she sent her maid to get it and bring it to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> When she opened it she saw the baby boy. He was crying and she felt sorry for him. “This must be one of the Hebrew boys,” she said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> His sister asked Pharaoh's daughter, “Would you like me to go and find one of the Hebrew women to nurse him for you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; PSALMS 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> “Yes, go and do that,” she replied. So the girl went and called the baby's mother to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “Take this baby boy and nurse him for me,” Pharaoh's daughter told his mother. “I will pay you myself.” So his mother took him home and nursed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> When the boy was older she took him to Pharaoh's daughter, who adopted him as her son. She called him Moses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Moses” sounds like the Hebrew word “pull out.” In Egyptian it is an abbreviation meaning “son of…”">a</a></sup>because she said, “I pulled him out of the water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> Later, when Moses had grown up, he went to visit his people, the Hebrews. He saw them doing hard labor. He also saw an Egyptian beating a Hebrew, one of his own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> He looked all around to make sure no one was watching, and then he killed the Egyptian and buried his body in the sand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> The following day he went back and he saw two Hebrews fighting with each other. He said to the one at fault, “Why are you beating one of your own people?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> “Who put you in charge to judge us?” the man replied. “Are you going to kill me like you did the Egyptian?” Moses became frightened at this, and said to himself, “People know what I've done!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> When Pharaoh found out, he tried to have Moses killed, but Moses ran away from Pharaoh and went to live in Midian.<br />One day as he was sitting by a well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> the Midianite priest's seven daughters came to fetch water to fill up the troughs so their father's flock could drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> Some shepherds arrived and chased them off, but Moses intervened and rescued them, and watered their flock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> When they got home, their father Reuel asked them, “How did you get back so quickly today?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> “An Egyptian rescued us from some shepherds who attacked us,” they replied. “Then he even fetched water for us so the flock could drink.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> “So where is he?” Reuel asked his daughters. “You didn't just leave him there, did you? Go and invite him to eat with us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> Moses agreed to stay with the man, who arranged for his daughter Zipporah to marry Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> She had a son, and Moses named him Gershom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Gershom” sounds like “a foreigner there.”">b</a></sup>for he said, “I'm an exile living in a foreign country.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> Years later, the king of Egypt died. But the Israelites were still groaning under their hard labor. Their cries for help because of their hardship reached God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; PSALMS 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> God heard their groans, and recalled his agreement with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; PSALMS 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> God also looked sympathetically on the Israelites, and was concerned for them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Was concerned for them”: literally, “knew.”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
