<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–19; Exodus 26:36; Exodus 26:31"> Make ten curtains for the Tabernacle of finely-spun linen, using blue, purple, and crimson threads. Have them embroidered with cherubim by someone who is skilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Numbers 4:25; 1 Chronicles 17:1"> Each curtain is to measure twenty-eight cubits long by four cubits wide, and all the curtains are to be the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:10; Colossians 2:2; Ephesians 2:21–22"> Join five of the curtains together, and then do the same to the other five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 36:11–12; Exodus 36:17; Exodus 26:10–11"> Use blue material to make loops on the edge of the last curtain of both sets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Make fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the last curtain of the second set, lining up the loops with each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 36:13; Exodus 26:11; Exodus 26:33"> Then make fifty gold clips and join the curtains together with the clips, so that the Tabernacle will be a single structure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 26:14; PSALMS 45:13; Exodus 26:9"> Make eleven curtains of goat hair as a tent to cover the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:13; Exodus 26:2"> Each of the eleven curtains is to be the same size—thirty cubits long by four cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Join five of the curtains together as one set and the other six as another set. Then fold the sixth curtain in two at the front of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Make fifty loops on the edge of the last curtain in the first set, and fifty loops along the edge of the last curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:3; Exodus 26:6"> Make fifty bronze clips and put them in the loops to join the tent together as a single cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The extra half-curtain of this tent covering will be left to hang over the back of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 26:2; Exodus 26:8"> The tent curtains will be a cubit longer on every side, and the extra length will hang over the sides of the Tabernacle so it is all covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 36:19; Exodus 25:5; Exodus 35:23"> Make a covering for the goat hair tent from tanned ram skins, and place an extra covering of fine leather over that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 36:20–33; Numbers 4:31–32; Exodus 40:17–18"> Make an upright framework of acacia wood for the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Each frame is to be ten cubits long by one and a half cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 36:22; Exodus 26:19; Exodus 36:24"> Each frame will have two pegs so the frames can be connected to each other. Make all the frames of the Tabernacle like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Make twenty frames for the south side of the Tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 38:27; Exodus 38:30–31; Exodus 26:37"> Make forty silver stands as supports for the twenty frames using two stands per frame, one under every frame peg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Similarly for the north side of the Tabernacle, make twenty frames</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and forty silver stands, two stands per frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Make six frames for the back (west side) of the Tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> along with two frames for its two back corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 36:29–30; PSALMS 133:1–3; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Join these corner frames at the bottom and at the top near to the first ring. This is how you are to make the two corner frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In total there will be eight frames and sixteen silver stands, two under each frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 15:1; Numbers 4:31; Galatians 6:1–2"> Make five crossbars of acacia wood to hold together the frames on the south side of the Tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> five for those on the north, and five for those at the back of the Tabernacle, to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The central crossbar which is placed halfway up the frames will run from one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–12"> Cover the frames with gold, and make gold rings to hold the crossbars in place. Cover the crossbars with gold too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 25:9"> Assemble the Tabernacle following the design you were shown on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; 2 Chronicles 3:14; Exodus 36:35"> Make a veil out of blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen, embroidered with cherubim by someone who is skilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 36:38; Esther 1:6"> Using gold hooks hang it from four posts of acacia wood covered with gold, held up by four silver stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2–3; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 40:21"> Place the veil under the clips<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Clips”: see verse 6.">a</a></sup>and take the Ark of the Testimony inside behind the veil. The veil will separate the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:20; Exodus 25:21; Hebrews 9:5"> Put the atonement cover on the Ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 40:24; Exodus 40:22; Hebrews 9:2"> Place the table outside the veil on the north side of the Tabernacle and put the lampstand opposite it on the south side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 36:37; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 26:31"> Make a screen for the entrance to the tent using blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen. Have it embroidered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 36:38"> Make five posts of acacia wood with gold hooks to hang the screen, and cast five bronze stands to hold them.</span>
