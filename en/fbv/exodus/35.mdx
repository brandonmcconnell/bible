<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> Moses summoned all the Israelites and told them, “This is what the Lord has ordered you to do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> Six days you can work, but the seventh day is to be a holy Sabbath of rest to the Lord. Anyone who does any work on the Sabbath day must be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> Don't even light a fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> Moses also told all the Israelites, “This is what the Lord has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; PSALMS 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Collect an offering to the Lord from what you have. Everyone who wants to should bring an offering to the Lord: gold, silver, and bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> blue, purple, and crimson thread; finely-woven linen and goat hair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> tanned ram skins and fine leather; acacia wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> olive oil for the lamps; spices for the anointing oil and for the aromatic incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> and onyx stones and gemstones for making the ephod and breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> All your skilled craftsmen are to come and make everything the Lord has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> the Tabernacle with its tent and covering, its clips and frames, its crossbars, posts, and stands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> the Ark with its poles and atonement cover, and the veil to hang over it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> the table with its poles, all its equipment, and the Bread of the Presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> the lampstand of light with its equipment and lamps and olive oil to provide lighting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> the altar of incense with its poles; the anointing oil and aromatic incense; the screen for the Tabernacle entrance and all its accessories;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> the altar of burnt offering with its bronze grate, its poles and all its utensils; the basin plus its stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> the curtains of the courtyard with its posts and bases, and the curtain for the courtyard entrance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> the tent pegs for the Tabernacle and for the courtyard, as well as their ropes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> and the woven clothes for serving in the holy place: the sacred clothing for Aaron the priest and for his sons to serve as priests.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Israelites left Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> and all those who felt moved to do so and who had a willing spirit came and brought an offering to the Lord for the work making the Tent of Meeting, for everything required for its services, and for the sacred clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> So everyone who was willing, both men and women, came and presented their gold as a thanks offering to the Lord, including brooches, earrings, rings, and necklaces—all kinds of gold jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> Everyone who had blue, purple, and crimson thread, finely-woven linen, goat hair, tanned ram skins, and fine leather, contributed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> Those who could present an offering of silver or bronze brought it as a gift to the Lord. Everyone who had acacia wood for any part of the work donated it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> Every woman skilled in spinning with her hands brought what she had spun: blue, purple, or crimson thread, or finely-woven linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> All the women who were willing to use their skills spun the goat hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> The leaders brought onyx stones and gemstones to make the ephod and breastpiece,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> as well as spices and olive oil for lighting, for the anointing oil, and for the aromatic incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> All the Israelite men and women who were willing brought a freewill offering to the Lord for all the work in making what the Lord through Moses had ordered them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> Then Moses told the Israelites, “The Lord has chosen by name Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> He has filled him with the Spirit of God giving him ability, creativity, and expertise in all kinds of craftsmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> He can produce designs in gold, silver, and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> he can cut gemstones to place in settings, and he can carve wood. He is a master of every craft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> The Lord has also given him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, the ability to teach others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> He has equipped them with skill to do all kinds of work as engravers, designers, embroiderers in blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-woven linen, and as weavers—in fact as skilled designers in all kinds of different crafts.”</span>
