<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Exodus 31:18; PSALMS 119:89"> The Lord told Moses, “Cut out two stone tablets like the first ones, and I will write on them again the same words that were on the first tablets—the ones you broke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:20; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 19:11"> Get ready in the morning, and then come up Mount Sinai. Stand before me there on the mountain top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:21; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Nobody else can come up with you—I don't want to see anyone anywhere on the mountain, and no flocks or herds should graze at the foot of the mountain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Moses cut out two stone tablets like the previous ones and went up Mount Sinai early in the morning as the Lord had ordered him, taking with him the two stone tablets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Numbers 11:25; Luke 9:34–35"> The Lord descended in a cloud, stood there with him, and called out the name “Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 86:15; Joel 2:13; PSALMS 103:8–13"> The Lord passed in front of him, calling out, “Yahweh! Yahweh! I am the God of grace and mercy! I am slow to become angry, full of trustworthy love and always faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; PSALMS 103:3; Deuteronomy 5:9–10"> I go on showing my trustworthy love to thousands, forgiving guilt, rebellion, and sin. But I will definitely not clear the wicked—the impact of sin will affect not only the parents, but also their children and grandchildren, up until the third and fourth generation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 20:18; Genesis 17:3"> Moses quickly bowed down to the ground, and worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 33:12; Deuteronomy 32:9; PSALMS 94:14"> He said, “Lord, if it's true that you're happy with me, please accompany us. Admittedly this is a rebellious people, but please forgive our guilt and sin. Accept us as specially belonging to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; PSALMS 77:14; Isaiah 64:3"> The Lord said, “Look, I'm making an agreement with you. Right in front of all of you I will do miracles that have never been done before—not among anyone anywhere on earth. Everyone here and those around will see the Lord at work, for what I'm going to do for you will be incredible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8"> But you must carefully follow what I tell you to do today. Pay attention! I'm going to drive out ahead of you the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:32–33; Joshua 23:12–13; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Make sure you don't agree to a peace treaty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Agree to a peace treaty”: the word is the same as the “agreement” with God in verse 10. Also in verse 15.">a</a></sup>with the people living in the land where you are going. Otherwise they will become a trap for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> For you must tear down their altars, knock down their idolatrous pillars, and cut down their Asherah poles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 32:21; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> because you must not worship any other gods than the Lord. His name stands for being exclusive,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Being exclusive”: literally “jealous,” however this in human terms has become associated with envy and resentment. God is “jealous” in wanting to be the only God who is worshiped.">b</a></sup>because he is a God who demands an exclusive relationship.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 2:17; PSALMS 106:28; Numbers 25:2"> Make sure you don't agree to a peace treaty with the people living in the land, for when they prostitute themselves by worshiping and sacrificing to their gods, they will invite you to join them, and you will eat from their pagan sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; 1 Kings 11:2–4; Ezra 9:2"> When you have their daughters marry your sons and those daughters prostitute themselves to their gods, they will make your sons worship their gods in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Exodus 32:8; Isaiah 46:6–7"> Don't make any idols for yourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 13:6–7"> Observe the Festival of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast, as I ordered you to do. You are to do this at the specified time in the month of Abib, because that was the month when you left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Exodus 13:12"> Every firstborn is mine. That includes all the firstborn males of your livestock from your cattle herds, and flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:16"> You can redeem the firstborn of a donkey with a lamb, but if you don't, you must break its neck. All your firstborn sons must be redeemed. No one is to come before me without an offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 21:4; Luke 13:14"> You shall work for six days, but you shall rest on the seventh day. Even during the times of sowing and harvest you shall rest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:10–15; John 7:2"> Observe the Festival of Weeks when you offer the firstfruits of wheat harvest, and the Harvest Festival at the end of the agricultural year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:14; Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:17"> Three times every year all your males must appear before the Lord Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 78:55; Exodus 33:2; Proverbs 16:7"> I will drive out the nations ahead of you and expand your borders, and no one will come and take your land when you go three times a year to appear before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 12:10; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8"> Don't offer bread made with yeast when you present a sacrifice to me, don't keep any sacrifice from the Passover Feast until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:2"> When you harvest your crops, bring the firstfruits to the house of the Lord your God.<br />Don't cook a young goat in milk from its mother.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 24:4; Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 4:13"> Then the Lord told Moses, “Write down these words, because these are the basis for the agreement I have made with you and with Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 34:1; Exodus 24:18; Deuteronomy 9:18"> Moses spent forty days and forty nights there with the Lord without eating bread or drinking water. He wrote down on the tablets the words of the agreement, the Ten Commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Luke 9:29; Exodus 32:15"> When Moses came down from Mount Sinai carrying the two tablets of the Law, he didn't realize that his face was shining brightly because he had been speaking with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Luke 5:8; Numbers 12:8"> When Aaron and the Israelites saw Moses with his face shining so brightly they were frightened to approach him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Exodus 24:1–3; Genesis 45:3"> But Moses called out to them, so Aaron and all the community leaders went over to him, and he talked with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:14; Exodus 24:3; Matthew 28:20"> Afterwards all the Israelites came over and he gave them all the Lord's instructions he'd received on Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4–6; 2 Corinthians 3:13–18; Romans 10:4"> After Moses finished talking with them, he put a veil over his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 4:16"> However, whenever Moses went in to talk with the Lord, he would take off the veil until he came out again. Then he would tell the Israelites the Lord's instructions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 34:29–30; John 5:35; Daniel 12:3"> and the Israelites would see his face shining brightly. So he would put the veil over his face until the next time he went to talk with the Lord.</span>
</div>
