<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> Bezalel made the altar of burnt offering from acacia wood. It was square, and measured five cubits long by five cubits wide by three cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> He made horns for each of its corners, all one piece with the altar, and covered the whole altar with bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> He made all its utensils: buckets for removing ashes, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans. He made all its utensils of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He made a bronze mesh grate for the altar and placed it under the ledge of the altar, so that the mesh came halfway down the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> He cast four bronze rings for the four corners of the grate as holders for the poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> He made poles of acacia wood for the altar and covered them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> He put the poles through the rings on either side of the altar so it could be carried. He made the altar hollow, using boards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> He made the bronze basin with its stand with bronze from the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; PSALMS 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> Then he made a courtyard. For the south side of the courtyard he made curtains of finely-spun linen, a hundred cubits long on one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> with twenty posts and twenty bronze stands, with silver hooks and bands on the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Similarly he made curtains placed on the north side in an identical arrangement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He made curtains for the west side of the courtyard fifty cubits wide, with ten posts and ten stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The east side of the courtyard that faces the sunrise was fifty cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> He made the curtains on one side fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three stands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the curtains on the other side just the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the curtains around the courtyard were of finely-woven linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The stands for the posts were bronze, the hooks and bands were silver, and the tops of the posts were covered with silver. All the posts around the courtyard had silver bands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> The curtain for the entrance to the courtyard was embroidered with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen. It was twenty cubits long by five cubits high, the same height as the courtyard curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> It was held up by four posts and four stands. The posts had silver hooks, tops, and bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> All the tent pegs for the Tabernacle and for the surrounding courtyard were made of bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> The following is what was used for the Tabernacle, the Tabernacle of the Testimony, recorded at Moses' direction by the Levites under the supervision of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; PSALMS 119:6"> Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah, made everything that the Lord had ordered Moses to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> He was assisted by Oholiab, son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, an engraver, designer, and embroiderer using blue, purple, and crimson thread and finely-woven linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> The total amount of gold from the offering that was used for the work on the sanctuary was 29 talents and 730 shekels (using the sanctuary shekel standard).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> The total amount of silver from those who had been counted in the census was 100 talents and 1,775 shekels (using the sanctuary shekel standard).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> This represents a beka per person, or half a shekel (using the sanctuary shekel standard) from everyone twenty years of age or older who had been censused, a total of 603,550 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> The hundred talents of silver were used to cast the sanctuary stands and the curtain stands, 100 bases from the 100 talents, or one talent per base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> Bezalel used the 1,775 shekels of silver to make the hooks for the posts, cover their tops, and make bands for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The total amount of bronze from the offering was 70 talents and 2,400 shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> Bezalel used it to make the stands for the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the bronze altar and its bronze grate, all the utensils for the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> the stands for the courtyard and its entrance, and all the tent pegs for the Tabernacle and the courtyard.</span>
</div>
</div>
