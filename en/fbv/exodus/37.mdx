<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He made a border around it the width of a hand and put a gold trim on the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He cast four gold rings for the table and attached them to the four corners of the table by the legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The rings were close to the border to hold the poles used to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He made the poles of acacia wood for carrying the table and covered them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> He made utensils for the table from pure gold: plates and dishes, bowls and pitchers for pouring out drink offerings.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> It had six branches coming out of the sides of the lampstand, three on each side. It had three cups shaped like almond flowers on the first branch, each with buds and petals, and three on the next branch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Each of the six branches that came out had three cups shaped like almond flowers, all complete with buds and petals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> On the six branches that came out of it, he placed a bud under the first pair of branches, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair.</span>
