<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring to the craftsmen.">a</a></sup>made woven clothing from blue, purple, and crimson thread for serving in the sanctuary. They also made holy garments for Aaron, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> They made the ephod of finely-woven linen embroidered with gold, and with blue, purple, and crimson thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> They hammered out thin sheets of gold and cut threads from them to weave in with the blue, purple, and scarlet thread, together with fine linen, all skillfully worked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Two shoulder pieces were attached to the front and back pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> The waistband of the ephod was one piece made in the same way, using gold thread, with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and with finely-woven linen, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> They placed the onyx stones in ornamental gold settings, engraved with the names of the Israelite tribes in the same way a jeweler engraves a personal seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> They attached both stones to the shoulder pieces of the ephod as reminders for the Israelite tribes, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> They also made a breastpiece for decisions in the same skilful way as the ephod, to be used in determining the Lord's will. They made it using gold thread, with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and with finely-woven linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was square when folded, measuring around nine inches<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Nine inches”: literally, “a span,” the distance between thumb and little finger when the hand is stretched.">b</a></sup>in length and width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> They attached an arrangement of precious stones in four rows as follows.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="None of the following stones are identified with certainty.">c</a></sup>In the first row carnelian, peridot, and emerald.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> In the second row turquoise, lapis lazuli, and sardonyx.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In the third row jacinth, agate, and amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In the fourth row topaz, beryl, and jasper. They were all placed in ornamental gold settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> Each of the twelve stones were engraved like a personal seal with the name of one of the twelve Israelites tribes and represented them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> They made cords of braided chains from pure gold to attach the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> They made two gold settings and two gold rings and fastened the rings to the two top corners of the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They attached the two gold chains to the two gold rings on the corners of the breastpiece,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Songs 1:10"> and then fastened the opposite ends of the two chains to the ornamental gold settings on the shoulder pieces of the front side of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They made two more gold rings and attached them to the two lower corners of the breastpiece, on the inside edge next to the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> They made two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the front side of the ephod, near where it joins its woven waistband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> They tied the rings of the breastpiece to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue thread, so that the breastpiece wouldn't come loose from the ephod, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> They made the robe that goes with the ephod exclusively from woven blue cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> with an opening in the middle at the top. They stitched a woven collar around the opening to strengthen it so it wouldn't tear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> They made pomegranates using blue, purple, and crimson thread and finely-woven linen and attached them all the way around its hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Songs 4:13; PSALMS 89:15"> They made pure gold bells and attached them between the pomegranates all the way around its hem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Songs 4:13; Song of Songs 6:7"> having the bells and the pomegranates alternate. The robe was to be used for priestly service, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> They made tunics with finely-spun linen made by a weaver for Aaron and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> They also made turbans, headdresses, and headbands of fine linen, and finely-woven linen undershorts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> as well as sashes of finely-woven linen embroidered with blue, purple, and crimson thread, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> They made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold and wrote on it, engraved like a seal, “Holy to the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> They tied a blue cord to it to attach it to the front of the turban, as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> So all the work for the Tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting, was finished. The Israelites did everything as the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> Then they presented the Tabernacle to Moses: the tent with all its furniture, its clips, its frames, its crossbars, and its posts and stands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> the covering of tanned ram skins, the covering of fine leather, and the veil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the Ark of the Testimony with its poles and the atonement cover;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> the table with all its equipment and the Bread of the Presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the pure gold lampstand with its lamps placed in a row, and all its equipment, as well as the olive oil for the lamps;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> the gold altar, the anointing oil, the aromatic incense, and the screen for the tent's entrance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> the bronze altar with its bronze grate, its poles, and all its utensils; the basin plus its stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> the curtains of the courtyard and its posts and stands; the curtain for the courtyard entrance, its ropes and tent pegs, and all the equipment for the services of the Tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> and the woven garments for serving in the sanctuary, the holy clothes for Aaron the priest and for his sons to serve as priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> The Israelites did all the work that the Lord had ordered Moses to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> Moses inspected all the work and made sure they had done it as the Lord had ordered. Then Moses blessed them.</span>
</div>
