<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> “Make from acacia wood an altar for burning incense.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is in addition to the altar mentioned in chapter 27.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> It shall be square, measuring a cubit by a cubit, by two cubits high, with horns on its corners that are all one piece with the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> Cover its top, its side, and its horns with pure gold, and make a gold trim to go all around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> On two of the altar's sides make two gold rings and attach them below the trim to hold the poles that carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> Make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> Put the altar in front of the veil that hangs before the Ark of the Testimony and the atonement cover that is over the Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Testimony”: referring to the tablets of stone inscribed with the Ten Commandments.">b</a></sup>where I will meet with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> Aaron is to burn fragrant incense on the altar every morning when he takes care of the lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> When he lights the lamps in the evening he must burn incense again so that incense will always be burned in the Lord's presence for generations to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> Don't offer on this altar any unapproved incense<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Unapproved incense”: in other words, incense not prepared according to the instructions given in verses 34-38.">c</a></sup>or any burnt offering or grain offering, and do not pour out a drink offering upon it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> Once a year Aaron must perform the atonement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Atonement”: the Hebrew word is associated with the concept of “covering” sin, in other words, taking care of it. In this way people are reconciled to God and become “at one” with him. This yearly atonement ritual must be carried out for generations to come. This is the Lord's holy altar.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> “When you take a census of the Israelites, each man must pay the Lord to buy back his life when he's counted. Then they won't suffer from the plague when they're counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> Each one who crosses over to those counted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Clearly there was some process by which as people moved from the “uncounted” group to the “counted” group the payment was made.">e</a></sup>must give a half shekel (using the sanctuary shekel standard, which weighs twenty gerahs). This half shekel is an offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> This offering to the Lord is required from everyone twenty years old and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> When you give this offering to buy back your lives the rich are not to give more than a half shekel and the poor are not to give less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> Take this money paid by the Israelites and use it for the expenses involved in the services of the Tent of Meeting. It will serve as a reminder for the Israelites to buy back your lives in the presence of the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> “Make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing. Place it between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and put water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; PSALMS 26:6"> Aaron and his sons will use it to wash their hands and feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> Whenever they enter the Tent of Meeting, they are to wash with water so that they will not die. When they approach the altar to present burnt offerings to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> they must also wash so that they will not die. This requirement is to be observed by them and their descendants for all generations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Songs 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> “Take the best quality spices: 500 shekels of liquid myrrh, 250 shekels of sweet-smelling cinnamon, 250 shekels of aromatic reed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> 500 shekels of cassia (weights using the sanctuary shekel standard), and a hin of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> Mix these together into holy anointing oil, an aromatic blend like the product of an expert perfumer. Use it as holy anointing oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> Use it to anoint the Tent of Meeting, the Ark of the Testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> the table and all its equipment, the lampstand and its equipment, the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin plus its stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> Dedicate them so that they will be especially holy. Whatever touches them will become holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> Anoint Aaron and his sons too and dedicate them to serve as priests for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; PSALMS 89:20"> Tell the Israelites, ‘This is to be my holy anointing oil for all generations to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> Don't use it on ordinary people and don't make anything like it using the same formula. It is holy, and you must treat it as being holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Anyone who mixes anointing oil like it, or puts it on someone other than a priest,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Someone other than a priest”: literally, “a stranger.”">f</a></sup>will be expelled from their people.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> The Lord told Moses, “Take equal amounts of these aromatic spices: balsam resin, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> Add some salt and make pure and holy incense blended like the product of an expert perfumer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> Grind some of it into powder and place it in front of the Ark of the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting, where I will meet with you. It shall be especially holy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> Don't make for yourselves any incense like it using the same formula. You are to consider this incense as holy to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Anyone who makes incense like this for themselves to enjoy will be expelled from their people.”</span>
</div>
