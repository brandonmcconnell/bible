<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> “These are the regulations you are to present to them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> If you buy a Hebrew slave, he is to work for you for six years. But in the seventh year, he is to be freed without having to pay anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he was single when he came, he is to leave single. If he had a wife when he came, she is to leave with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master provides him a wife and she has children with him, the woman and her children shall belong to her master, and only the man shall be freed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> However, if the slave formally states, ‘I love my master and my wife and children; I do not want to be freed,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then his master is to take him before the judges.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word used here also can refer to God, but in this context it seems a civil court is what is being referred to. Also in 22:8,9.">a</a></sup>Then he shall have him stand against the door or doorpost and use a metal tool to make a hole in his ear. Then he shall work for his master for life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> If a man sells his daughter as a slave, she is not to be freed in the same way as male slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If the man who chose her for himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Chose her for himself”: probably meaning she was a concubine.">b</a></sup>is not pleased with her, he must let her be bought back. He is not permitted to sell her to foreigners, since he has been unfair to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If he chooses to give her to his son, he must treat her as a daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he takes another woman, he must not reduce the food and clothing allowances and marital rights of the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If he doesn't give her these three things, she is free to leave without paying anything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> Anyone who hits and kills someone else must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> However, if it wasn't intentional and God let it happen, then I will arrange a place for you where they can run to and be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> But if someone deliberately plans and purposely kills another, you must take them away from my altar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“From my altar”: where people went to claim sanctuary.">c</a></sup>and execute them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> Anyone who hits their father or mother must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> Anyone who kidnaps someone else must be executed, whether the victim is sold or is still in their possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> Anyone who despises their father or mother must be executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> If men are fighting and one hits the other with a stone or with his fist, and the injured man doesn't die but has to stay in bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> and then gets up and walks around outside with his walking stick, then the one who hit him won't be punished. Even so, he must still compensate the man for the time lost from his work and make sure that he's completely healed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> Anyone who hits their male or female slave with a rod, and the slave dies as a result, must be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> However, if after a day or two the slave gets better, the owner won't be punished because the slave is their property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> If men who are fighting happen to hit a pregnant woman so she gives birth prematurely,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Gives birth prematurely”: or, “has a miscarriage.”">d</a></sup>but no serious injury occurs, he must be fined whatever amount the woman's husband demands and as permitted by the judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> But if a serious injury does occur, then you must pay a life for a life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, a foot for a foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> a burn for a burn, a wound for a wound, and a bruise for a bruise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; PSALMS 72:12–14"> Anyone who hits their male or female slave in the eye and blinds them must free the slave as compensation for the eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Anyone who knocks out the tooth of their male or female slave must free the slave as compensation for the tooth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> If an ox uses its horns to kill a man or woman, the ox must be stoned to death, and its meat must not be eaten. But the owner of the ox won't be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the ox has repeatedly hurt people with its horns, and its owner has been warned but still doesn't keep it under control, and it kills a man or woman, then the ox must be stoned to death and its owner must also be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> But if instead the payment of compensation is required, the owner may buy back his or her life by paying the full amount of compensation demanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If the ox uses its horns and kills a son or a daughter the same rule is applicable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox uses its horns and kills a male or female slave, the owner of the ox must pay thirty shekels of silver to the slave's master, and the ox must be stoned to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> If someone removes the cover of a cistern or digs one and doesn't cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit must pay compensation to the animal's owner and keep the dead animal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> If someone's ox injures another's ox and it dies, they must sell the live one and share money received; they must also share the dead animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> But if it was known that the ox had repeatedly hurt people with its horns, and its owner had been warned but still didn't keep it under control, full compensation must be paid, ox for ox, but the owner can keep the dead animal.”</span>
</div>
</div>
