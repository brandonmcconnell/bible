</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 24:43; Matthew 6:19–20; Numbers 35:27"> If a thief is discovered breaking into someone's house and is beaten to death, no one shall be guilty of murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; Isaiah 50:1; Matthew 18:25"> But if it happens during daylight, then someone is guilty of murder. A thief must pay everything back that's stolen. If he doesn't have anything, then he must be sold to pay for what was stolen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 22:7; Exodus 22:1; Exodus 22:9"> If what was stolen is a live animal he still has, whether it's an ox, donkey, or sheep, he must pay back double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Exodus 22:12; Job 20:18"> If livestock are grazing in a field or vineyard and their owner lets them stray so that they graze in someone else's field, their owner must pay compensation from the best of their own fields or vineyards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:9; Exodus 21:33–34; 2 Samuel 14:30–31"> If a fire is started and it spreads to thorn bushes and then burns stacked or standing grain, or even the whole field, the person who started the fire must pay full compensation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Proverbs 6:30–31; Jeremiah 2:26"> If someone gives his neighbor money or possessions to be kept safe and they are stolen from the neighbor's house, if the thief is caught they must pay back double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 21:6"> If the thief isn't caught the owner of the house must appear before the judges to find out whether he took his neighbor's property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1; Exodus 22:4; Numbers 5:6–7"> If there's an argument over the ownership of an ox, a donkey, a sheep, a garment, or anything that was lost that someone says, ‘This is mine,’ both parties are to bring their case before the judges. The one whom the judges find in the wrong must pay the other back double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:48; 2 Timothy 1:12; Luke 16:11"> If someone asks a neighbor to look after a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any other animal, but it dies or is injured or is stolen without anybody noticing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Leviticus 5:1; 1 Kings 2:42–43"> then an oath must be taken before the Lord to decide if the neighbor has taken the owner's property. The owner must accept the oath and not demand compensation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:39; Exodus 22:7"> However, if the animal really was stolen from the neighbor, he must compensate the owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 2:12; Micah 5:8; Ezekiel 4:14"> If it was killed and torn to pieces by a wild animal, the neighbor shall present the carcass as evidence and does not need to pay compensation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Exodus 21:34; Deuteronomy 15:2"> If someone borrows a neighbor's animal and it's injured or dies while its owner is not present, they must pay compensation in full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:10"> If the owner was present, no compensation is to be paid. If the animal was hired, only the hire charge needs to be paid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:28–29; Genesis 34:2–4"> If a man seduces a virgin who is not engaged to be married and sleeps with her, he must pay the full bride-price for her to become his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:25; Deuteronomy 22:29"> If her father adamantly refuses to give her to him, the man still must pay the same amount as the bride-price for a virgin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 28:3"> You must not allow a woman who practices witchcraft to live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Leviticus 20:15–16"> Anyone who has sex with an animal must be executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Numbers 25:7–8; Deuteronomy 18:20"> Anyone who sacrifices to any other god than the Lord must be set apart and executed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Set apart and executed”: the term used here means “devoted to destruction” in the sense they now are to suffer God's punishment.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:33"> You must not exploit or mistreat a foreigner. Remember that you yourselves were once foreigners in Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Isaiah 1:17; Isaiah 1:23"> You must not take advantage of any widow or orphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 18:6; PSALMS 10:17–18; Luke 18:7"> If you mistreat them, and they call out to me for help, I will definitely respond to their cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 109:9; Jeremiah 15:8; PSALMS 76:7"> I will become angry, and I will kill you with the sword. Your wives will become widows and your children will be fatherless.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 15:5; Leviticus 25:35–37; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> If you lend money to any of my people because they're poor, you must not behave as a moneylender to them. You must not charge them any interest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Amos 2:8; Deuteronomy 24:6"> If you require your neighbor's cloak as security for a loan, you must return it to him by sunset,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 34:6; 2 Chronicles 30:9; PSALMS 136:10–11"> because it's the only clothing he has for his body. What would he sleep in otherwise? If he calls out to me for help, I will listen, for I am considerate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Exodus 22:8–9"> You must not despise God or curse your people's leader.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 13:2; James 1:18"> You must not hold on to the required offerings of your produce, olive oil, and wine.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Olive oil, and wine”: literally, “best drippings.”">b</a></sup>You must give me the firstborn of your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Deuteronomy 15:19; Exodus 13:12"> You must also give me the firstborn of your cattle, sheep, and goats. They can stay with their mothers for their first seven days, but give them to me on the eighth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Leviticus 19:2; Deuteronomy 14:21"> You are to be holy people to me. You must not eat any animal carcass that you find in the countryside that has been killed by wild animals. Throw it to the dogs to eat.”</span>
</div>
