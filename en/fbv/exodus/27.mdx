<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> Make an altar of acacia wood. It is to be square and measure five cubits long by five cubits wide by three cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> Make horns for each of its corners, all one piece with the altar, and cover the whole altar with bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> Make all its utensils of bronze: buckets for removing ashes, shovels, sprinkling bowls, meat forks, and firepans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> Make a bronze mesh grate for it with a bronze ring on each of its corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> Put the grate under the ledge of the altar, so that the mesh comes halfway down the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> Make poles of acacia wood for the altar and cover them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> The poles are to be placed in the rings so that the poles are on either side of the altar when it is carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Make the altar hollow, using boards, just as you were shown on the mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> Make a courtyard for the Tabernacle. For the south side of the courtyard make curtains of finely-spun linen, a hundred cubits long on one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> with twenty posts and twenty bronze stands, with silver hooks and bands on the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Similarly there are to be curtains placed on the north side in an identical arrangement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The curtains for the west side of the courtyard are to be fifty cubits wide, with ten posts and ten stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The east side of the courtyard that faces the sunrise is to be fifty cubits wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> Make the curtains on one side fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three stands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the curtains on the other side just the same.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> The entrance to the courtyard is to be twenty cubits wide, with a curtain embroidered with blue, purple, and crimson thread, and finely-spun linen, held up by four posts and four stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the posts around the courtyard are to have silver bands, silver hooks, and bronze stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The whole courtyard is to be a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide, with curtains made of finely-spun linen five cubits high, and with bronze stands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the rest of the equipment used in the Tabernacle, including its tent pegs and those for the courtyard, are to be made of bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; PSALMS 23:5"> You are to order the Israelites to bring you pure, hand-pressed olive oil for the lamps so they can go on burning, giving light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil in front of the Testimony, Aaron and his sons are to keep the lamps burning in the Lord's presence from evening until morning. This requirement is to be observed by the Israelites for all generations.</span>
</div>
