<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> “Instruct the Israelites to bring me an offering. You are to receive my offering from everyone who willingly wants to give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> These are the items you are to accept from them as contributions: gold, silver, and bronze;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> blue, purple, and crimson thread; finely-spun linen and goat hair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> ram skins that have been tanned, and fine leather; acacia wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> olive oil for the lamps; spices for the olive oil used in anointing and for the fragrant incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> and onyx stones and other gemstones to be used in making the ephod and breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> They are to make me a sanctuary so I can live among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> You must make the Tabernacle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The English word “Tabernacle” comes from the Latin for “tent,” and translates the Hebrew which refers to a residence or dwelling place.">a</a></sup>and all its furnishings according to the design I'm going to show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> They are to make an Ark of acacia wood that measures two and a half cubits long by a cubit and a half wide by one and a half cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> Cover it with pure gold on the inside and the outside, and make a gold trim to go around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> Cast four gold rings and attach them to its four feet, two on one side and two on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> Make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Place the poles into the rings on the sides of the Ark, so it can be carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The poles are to stay in the rings of the Ark; don't take them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> Place inside the Ark the Testimony which I'm going to give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> You are to make an atonement cover<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Atonement cover”: the word used here means to “cover over,” in the sense of dealing with sins. The traditional translation of “mercy seat” originated with Martin Luther. From a physical viewpoint it was the “lid” of the Ark.">b</a></sup>of pure gold, two and a half cubits long by a cubit and a half wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> Make two cherubim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A kind of angel.">c</a></sup>of hammered gold for the ends of the atonement cover,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and put one cherub on each end. All of this is to be made from one piece of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> The cherubim are to be designed with spread wings pointing upward, covering the atonement cover. The cherubim are to be placed facing each another, looking down towards the atonement cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> Place the atonement cover on top of the Ark, and put the Testimony that I'm going to give you inside the Ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> I will meet with you there as arranged above the atonement cover, between the two cherubim that stand over the Ark of the Testimony, and I will talk with you about all the commands I will give the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> Then you are to make a table of acacia wood two cubits long by a cubit wide by a cubit and a half high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> Cover it with pure gold and make a gold trim to go around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> Make a border around it the width of a hand and put a gold trim on the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Make four gold rings for the table and attach them to the four corners of the table by the legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> The rings are to be close to the border to hold the poles used to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> Make the poles of acacia wood for carrying the table and cover them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> Make plates and dishes for the table, as well as pitchers and bowls for pouring out drink offerings. Make all of them out of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> Place the Bread of the Presence on the table so it is always in my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> Make a lampstand of pure, hammered gold. The whole of it is to be made of one piece—its base, shaft, cups, buds, and flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> It is to have six branches coming out of the sides of the lampstand, three on each side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Have three cups shaped like almond flowers on the first branch, each with buds and petals, three on the next branch. Each of six branches that come out will have three cups shaped like almond flowers, all complete with buds and petals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> On the main shaft of the lampstand make four cups shaped like almond flowers, complete with buds and petals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> On the six branches that come out from the lampstand, place a bud under the first pair of branches, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> The buds and branches are to be made with the lampstand as one piece, hammered out of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> Make seven lamps and place them on the lampstand so they can light up the area in front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> The wick tongs and their trays are to be made of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The lampstand and all these utensils will require a talent of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> Be sure to make everything according to the design you were shown on the mountain.”</span>
