<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 12:6; Leviticus 23:16–18"> Two months to the day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Two months to the day”: literally, “on the day of the third new moon.”">a</a></sup>after they left Egypt, the Israelites arrived at the Sinai desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 17:1; Exodus 3:12"> They had set out from Rephidim, and after they entered the Sinai desert they camped there in front of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:4; Acts 7:38; Exodus 20:21"> Moses went up the mountain to God. The Lord spoke to Moses from the mountain, and told him, “This is what you are to say to the descendants of Jacob, the Israelites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> ‘You saw for yourselves what I did to the Egyptians, and how I carried you on eagles' wings, how I brought you to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18"> Now if you really obey what I say and keep the agreement with me, then out of all the nations you will be a special people that belong to me. While the whole world is mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 2:5"> for me you will be a kingdom of priests, a holy nation.’ This is what you are to tell the Israelites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:29–30; Exodus 3:16; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> So Moses went back down and summoned the elders of the people, and presented to them everything the Lord had ordered him to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Joshua 24:24"> Everyone answered, “We promise to do everything the Lord says.” Then Moses took the people's answer back to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:36; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 24:15–16"> The Lord told Moses, “Look! I'm going to come to you in a thick cloud so the people will hear me speaking with you and as a result they will always trust you.” Then Moses reported to the Lord what the people had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> The Lord told Moses, “Go back down and prepare them spiritually<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Prepare them spiritually”: literally, “consecrate, set apart,” probably through some religious rituals. Also in verses 14 and 22.">b</a></sup>today and tomorrow. They must wash their clothes</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Exodus 34:5; Deuteronomy 33:2"> and be ready on the third day because that is when the Lord will descend upon Mount Sinai in the full view of everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:20–21; Joshua 3:4; Exodus 19:21"> Set up a boundary for the people all around and warn them, ‘Watch out! Don't try to go up the mountain—don't even touch it! For anyone who touches the mountain will most certainly be killed. Don't touch any person or any animal that has touched the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Hebrews 12:20; Exodus 19:16"> Make sure they are stoned or shot with arrows—they must not be allowed to live.’ Only when there is a long blast on the ram's horn can the people come up the mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:10"> Moses went down the mountain and prepared the people spiritually and they washed their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:5; 1 Samuel 21:4–5; Exodus 19:11"> He instructed the people, “Get ready for the third day, and don't be intimate with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Don't be intimate with”: literally, “don't go near a woman.”">c</a></sup>a woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:18–19; PSALMS 18:11–14; Hebrews 12:21"> When morning came on the third day there was thunder and lightning, and a thick cloud covered the mountain. There was the very loud sound of a ram's horn, and everyone in the camp shook with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 5:5"> Moses led the people out from the camp to meet God. They stood at the foot of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:5; Exodus 3:2; PSALMS 144:5"> Smoke poured out over the whole of Mount Sinai because the Lord's presence had come down like fire. The smoke rose up like smoke from a furnace, and the whole mountain shook furiously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 81:7; Exodus 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> As the sound of the ram's horn grew louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him in a loud, thunderous voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Exodus 34:2; Exodus 19:11"> The Lord descended on the top of Mount Sinai, and he called Moses to come up there. So Moses went up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 3:5; Hebrews 12:28–29"> and the Lord told him, “Go back down, and warn the people not to force their way across the boundary to try to come to the Lord or they will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:5; Acts 5:10; 1 Chronicles 13:9–11"> Even the priests, who come before the Lord, must prepare themselves spiritually, so that the Lord does not punish them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 19:12; Joshua 3:4–5"> But Moses said to the Lord, “The people cannot come up Mount Sinai. You yourself warned us, ‘Set up a boundary around the mountain, and treat it as holy.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Treat it as holy”: the same word is used as for the people, being spiritually prepared, “consecrated.” Clearly however an inanimate object such as a mountain cannot be “consecrated” in the same way as a person.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 12:18–25; Hebrews 12:29"> The Lord told him, “Go down and bring Aaron back up with you. But the priests and the people must not force their way to come up to the Lord, or he will punish them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So Moses went down and explained to the people what the Lord had said.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“What the Lord had said”: supplied for clarity.">e</a></sup></span>
