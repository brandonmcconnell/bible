<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Deuteronomy 1:38; Deuteronomy 31:23"> After the death of Moses, the servant of the Lord, the Lord spoke to Joshua, the son of Nun, who had been Moses' assistant. He told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7; Numbers 27:16–21"> “My servant Moses has died. So go and cross the Jordan, you and all the people, and enter the country I am giving to the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 14:9; Titus 1:2"> As I promised Moses, everywhere you set foot will be land I am giving to you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring not to Joshua, but to all the people.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 3:25"> from the desert to Lebanon, and on up to the River Euphrates; all the land of the Hittites, and to the shore of the Mediterranean Sea to the west. This will be your territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Matthew 28:20"> No one will be able to stand against you while you live. Just as I was with Moses, I will be with you. I will never leave you; I will never abandon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9"> Be strong! Be brave! You will be the people's leader as they occupy the land I promised their ancestors that I would give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:8"> Just be strong and very brave, and be sure to obey all the law that my servant Moses instructed you to follow. Don't turn aside from it, either to the right or to the left, so that you may be successful in everything you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 119:15; PSALMS 19:14; Colossians 3:16"> Keep on reminding the people about the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “The scroll of the law shall not depart from your mouth.”">b</a></sup>Meditate on it day and night, so you can be sure to do what it requires. Then you will be successful and prosper in what you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Jeremiah 1:7–8; Joshua 1:6–7"> Don't forget what I told you: Be strong! Be brave! Don't be afraid! Don't get discouraged! For the Lord your God is with you wherever you go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Joshua gave an order to those in charge of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1; Hosea 6:2; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Go through the whole camp and tell the people, ‘Get everything ready, because in three days time we will cross the Jordan, to go and take the land God is giving to you to possess.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–22"> But to the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and half of the tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–28; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 22:1–4"> “Remember what Moses, the servant of the Lord, ordered you to do: ‘The Lord your God is giving you rest, and will give you this land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Exodus 13:18; Revelation 17:4"> Your wives and children, and your livestock will remain here in the land that Moses allotted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="It appears that generally the land divisions were decided by casting lots, so this seems the best word to use.">c</a></sup>you on the east side of the Jordan. But all your armed men, ready for battle, will cross over ahead of your brothers to help them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:4; Numbers 32:17–22; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> until the Lord lets them rest, as he has let you rest, and they too have taken possession of the land that the Lord is giving to you. Then you can return and occupy your own land which Moses allotted to you on the east side of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Deuteronomy 5:27; 1 Peter 2:13–15"> They said to Joshua, “We will do everything you have ordered us to do, and we will go wherever you send us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; 1 Kings 1:37; Joshua 1:9"> We will obey you just as we obeyed Moses in everything. May the Lord God be with you as he was with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9; Ezra 10:4"> Anyone who rebels against what you say and doesn't follow your orders, who doesn't obey everything you command, will be put to death. So be strong! Be brave!”</span>
