<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:2; 1 Samuel 10:19; Genesis 33:18–19"> Joshua summoned all the tribes of Israel to Shechem. Then he called the elders, leaders, judges, and officials and they came and stood before the Tabernacle of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:15; Ezekiel 16:3; Genesis 31:30"> Joshua said to all the people, “The Lord, the God of Israel, says this: ‘Long, long ago your forefathers, including Terah, the father of Abraham and Nahor, lived beyond the Euphrates River, and they worshiped other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Genesis 21:2–3; Acts 7:2–3"> I brought your father Abraham from beyond the Euphrates and led him through all the land of Canaan and gave him many descendants. I gave him Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Deuteronomy 2:5; Genesis 25:24–26"> I gave to Isaac Jacob and Esau. I gave to Esau the hill country of Seir to own, but Jacob and his children went down to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 7:1–12; PSALMS 105:26–36"> I sent Moses and Aaron, and I brought plagues on the people of Egypt, and I brought you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The story uses “your forefathers” and “you” interchangeably, perhaps to enhance the involvement of the audience.">a</a></sup>out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Hebrews 11:29; Isaiah 63:12–13"> Yes, I brought your forefathers out, but when you arrived at the Red Sea the Egyptians were chasing after your forefathers with chariots and horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Exodus 14:10; Exodus 14:20"> Your forefathers called out to the Lord for help, and he placed darkness between you and the Egyptians. Then he brought the sea back over them and they drowned. You saw what I did with your own eyes in Egypt. Then you lived for many years in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; PSALMS 135:10–11; Nehemiah 9:22"> After that I brought you to the land of the Amorites who lived on the other side of the Jordan. They fought against you but I handed them over to you to defeat and you took over their land. I destroyed them before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Micah 6:5; Numbers 22:2"> When Balak, son of Zippor, the king of Moab, wanted to fight against Israel, he sent for Balaam, son of Beor, to come and curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 24:5–10; Isaiah 54:17"> But I wasn't willing to listen to Balaam, so instead he blessed you repeatedly, and saved you from Balak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 4:10–12; Joshua 3:14–17; PSALMS 114:5"> You crossed the Jordan and arrived at Jericho where the men of Jericho fought against you. So did the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Exodus 23:28; PSALMS 44:3–6"> But I handed them over to you to defeat. And I sent the hornet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “panic.” See similar statements in Exodus 23:28and Deuteronomy 7:20.">b</a></sup>before you to drive out the two kings of the Amorites. You did not win using your swords or your bows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Deuteronomy 8:7; Joshua 21:45"> I gave you a land you didn't work for and towns you didn't build. Now you live in them and eat from vineyards and olive groves you didn't plant.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Deuteronomy 10:12; Genesis 35:2"> So respect the Lord and worship him, sincerely and faithfully. Get rid of the gods your forefathers worshiped beyond the Euphrates and in Egypt, and worship the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; John 6:67–68; Ruth 1:15–16"> But if you don't want to worship the Lord, choose today who you want to worship! Is it going to be the gods your forefathers worshiped beyond the Euphrates? Or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you're now living? But as for me and my family, we will worship the Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38–39; 1 Samuel 12:23; Romans 6:2"> The people responded, “We are never going to give up on the Lord and worship other gods!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 63:7–14; Isaiah 46:4"> For the Lord our God brought us and our forefathers out of slavery in Egypt. He was the one who did great miracles before our very eyes. He looked after us on the way as we traveled through the lands of many nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 4:2; Exodus 10:2; PSALMS 116:16"> The Lord drove out before us the Amorites and all the other nations who were living in the land. So we will worship the Lord, for he is our God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 5:16; Exodus 23:21; PSALMS 99:9"> Joshua told the people, “Remember that the Lord is a holy and jealous God. You won't be able to worship him, and he won't forgive your rebellion and sins</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Ezekiel 18:24; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> if you give up on him and worship foreign gods. He will turn against you and destroy you despite all the good he has done for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 44:5; Deuteronomy 5:27–28"> “Don't say that!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Don't say that!”: literally, “No!”">c</a></sup>the people replied. “We will worship the Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:173; PSALMS 119:11; Job 15:6"> Then Joshua cautioned the people, “You have made yourselves witnesses against yourselves by saying that you have chosen to worship the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In other words they can never say they were not aware of this choice since they had publicly acknowledged it.">d</a></sup>“Yes, we are witnesses,” the people replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16–18; Joshua 24:14; Hebrews 12:28–29"> “Then get rid of those foreign gods you have and promise to be loyal only to the Lord, the God of Israel,” Joshua told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Deuteronomy 5:27–29"> The people said to Joshua, “We will worship the Lord our God and we will obey him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:38; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Exodus 15:25"> So Joshua made a solemn agreement between the people and the Lord that day in Shechem, obligating them to follow all the Lord's laws and instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Genesis 35:4; Joshua 4:20–24"> Joshua recorded this in the Book of the Law of God, and he set up a large stone under the oak tree near the Lord's sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 22:34; Joshua 22:27–28"> Joshua said to the people, “Look at this stone. It stands here as a witness against us for it has heard everything the Lord has told us, and it will be a witness against you if you ever deny what you have promised your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 2:6"> Then Joshua dismissed the people, sending them to their allotted lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:8; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 34:5"> Later, after all this, Joshua, son of Nun, servant of the Lord, died at the age of one hundred and ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 23:30"> They buried him in Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash, the land he had been allocated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 2:7; Deuteronomy 31:13; Deuteronomy 11:2"> The Israelites continued to worship the Lord throughout Joshua's life, and throughout the lifetimes of the elders who outlived him, those who had seen all that the Lord had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 50:25; Genesis 33:19; Exodus 13:19"> The bones of Joseph, which the Israelites had brought with them from Egypt, they buried in Shechem in the piece of ground Jacob had bought from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred pieces of silver. This land was inherited by Joseph's sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 22:13; Exodus 6:25; Isaiah 57:1–2"> And Eleazar, son of Aaron, died and they buried him at Gibeah, in the hill country of Ephraim, land which had been given to his son Phinehas.</span>
