<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 22:20; Ecclesiastes 9:18; 1 Chronicles 2:6–7"> However, the Israelites had not been faithful regarding those things set apart for the Lord. Achan had taken some of them which made the Lord very angry with the Israelites. Achan was the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Hosea 4:15; 1 Samuel 14:23"> Joshua sent men from the camp near<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The camp near”: implied. These were obviously not men from Jericho itself.">a</a></sup>Jericho to the town of Ai, which is near Bethaven, east of Bethel. “Go and explore the land,” he told them. So they went and explored around Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11–12; Luke 13:24; 2 Peter 1:5"> When they returned they told Joshua, “The whole army isn't needed. Two or three thousand men is enough to go and attack Ai. Don't bother sending everyone—there's only a few of them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Leviticus 26:17; Deuteronomy 32:30"> So around three thousand men went to fight, but they were beaten by the men of Ai and they ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9; Leviticus 26:36"> The men of Ai killed about thirty-six of them, chasing the Israelites from the town gate until they were defeated,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Until they were defeated”: or, “to the quarries.”">b</a></sup>killing them on their way down. At this the Israelites became afraid, losing all their courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; Genesis 37:34; Revelation 18:19"> Joshua ripped his clothes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A symbol of grief.">c</a></sup>and fell down with his face to the ground in front of the Ark of the Lord until the evening. The elders did likewise, and he and the elders threw dust on their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; 2 Kings 3:10; Matthew 17:20"> Joshua cried, “Why, oh why, Lord God, did you bring us across the Jordan River only to hand us over to the Amorites for them to destroy us? We should have been satisfied to stay on the other side of the Jordan!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:5–6; Ezra 9:10; Habakkuk 2:1"> Excuse me, Lord, but what can I say now that Israel has turned tail and run away from its enemies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:12; PSALMS 83:4; Ezekiel 36:22–23"> The Canaanites and everyone living in the land will come and surround us and wipe us out so completely that even our name will be forgotten. Then what will happen to your great reputation?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Exodus 14:15; 1 Samuel 16:1"> But the Lord replied to Joshua, “Stand up! What do you think you're doing lying on your face like that?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Hebrews 4:13; Joshua 6:17–19"> Israel has sinned and has broken<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “transgressed,” in the sense of going outside of what was promised. The actual word means to pass over, or to cross, and is exactly the same word that Joshua uses when he asks why the Lord brought them across the Jordan. So in a modern language parallel Joshua asks why the Lord bothered to help the Israelites to “cross” the river, and the Lord replies that they have “crossed” (or even “double-crossed”) him.">d</a></sup>my agreement that I ordered them to keep. They have taken some of the things set apart for me; they have acted dishonestly; they have hidden the stolen items with their own belongings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 5:4–5; Isaiah 59:2; Joshua 6:18"> That's why the Israelites can't stand up to their enemies. That's why they turn and run from their enemies, and have themselves become set apart for destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="They became liable to be destroyed because they had taken things which were to be destroyed. See 6:18.">e</a></sup>You will not be able to stand against your enemies until you have removed from among you those things set apart for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Joshua 3:5; Matthew 7:5"> Get up, and make sure the people are pure. Tell them, ‘Make yourselves pure in readiness for tomorrow, because this is what the Lord, the God of Israel, says, There are things set apart for me that are hidden among you, Israel. You will not be able to stand against your enemies until you remove all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Jonah 1:7; Joshua 7:17–18"> In the morning, you will come forward, tribe by tribe. Whichever tribe the Lord chooses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="It appears that the decision was made by casting lots.">f</a></sup>will come forward clan by clan. The clan that the Lord chooses will come forward family by family. The family that the Lord chooses will come forward man by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 1 Samuel 14:38–39; Judges 20:6"> The one who is caught with what was set apart for destruction will be burned by fire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This does not mean the culprit would be burned alive, as is clear from subsequent verses.">g</a></sup>along with all that is his, for he broke the Lord's agreement and committed a terrible act in Israel.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; Joshua 3:1; Genesis 22:3"> Joshua got up early the next morning and called Israel forward, tribe by tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4–7; Genesis 38:30"> The tribe of Judah was chosen. The clans of Judah came forward and the Zerahites were chosen. The clan of Zerahites came forward, and the family of Zabdi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“The family of Zabdi” (some Hebrew manuscripts). Others jump right to the individual chosen, and identify Zimri, though the following verse makes this problematic.">h</a></sup>was chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:23; Acts 5:1–10; 1 Samuel 14:42"> The family of Zabdi came forward, and Achan, son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:5; Jeremiah 13:16; John 9:24"> Joshua said to Achan, “My son, honor the Lord, the God of Israel, and confess. Tell me what you have done. Don't hide it from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 38:18; Job 33:27; Genesis 42:21"> “It's true!” Achan replied. “I have sinned against the Lord, the God of Israel. This is what I did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:15; PSALMS 119:37; 1 John 2:15–16"> Among the plunder I saw a beautiful cloak from Babylonia, two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Around five pounds of silver, and over a pound of gold.">i</a></sup>I really wanted them, so I took them. They're hidden in the ground inside my tent, with the silver buried deeper.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Joshua sent men who ran over to search the tent. They found what had been hidden, with the silver buried deeper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The men took the things from the tent and brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites. There they spread them out before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Joshua 7:26"> Then Joshua, with all the Israelites, took Achan, the son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak, and the bar of gold, together with his sons and daughters, his cattle, his donkeys, his sheep, and his tent—everything he had—and brought them to the Valley of Achor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Meaning “the Valley of Trouble.”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; 1 Chronicles 2:7; Leviticus 24:14"> Then Joshua said to Achan, “Why did you bring so much trouble down on us? Today the Lord will bring trouble down on you.” All the Israelites stoned Achan. Then when they had stoned the rest they burned their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Hosea 2:15; Deuteronomy 13:17"> They set up a great pile of stones over him which remains to this day. The Lord was no longer angry. This is why the place was called the Valley of Achor ever since.</span>
