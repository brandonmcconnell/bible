<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; PSALMS 2:1–2"> So they gathered to fight together as a united army against Joshua and the Israelites.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> But when the people of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> they decided on a cunning plan. They sent messengers to Joshua, their donkeys wearing worn-out saddles and carrying old wineskins that were torn and patched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> They put on worn sandals that had been mended and wore old clothes. All their bread was dry and moldy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “crumbled.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> They went to Joshua at the camp in Gilgal and told him and the men of Israel, “We have come from a land far away, so please make a treaty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Make a treaty”: literally, “cut a covenant.” The Israelites were allowed to make treaties with peoples from distant lands, but not those nearby. See Deuteronomy 7:1-2 Deuteronomy 20:10-15.">b</a></sup>with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> But the Israelites said to the Hivites, “Maybe you live close by. If you do, we cannot make a treaty with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> “We are your servants,” they replied.<br />“But who are you? Where do you come from?” Joshua asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> “Your servants have come from a land far away,” they replied. “For we have heard of the reputation of the Lord your God, and reports of all that he did in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> and what he did to the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan—to Sihon, king of Heshbon, and Og, king of Bashan, who ruled in Ashtaroth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="It's interesting that they deliberately leave out any mention of Jericho and Ai because they would not have known about these recent victories if they had come from a distant country.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> So our leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Apparently the Gibeonites did not have a king.">d</a></sup>and everyone who lives in our land told us: Take what you need with you for the journey. Go and meet with them, and tell them, ‘We are your servants. Please make a treaty with us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> Look at this bread. It was warm when we took it from our houses on the day we set out to come here. But now it's dry and moldy, as you can see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> These wineskins were new when we filled them, but look at them now—they're split and damaged. These clothes of ours and our sandals are all worn out because the journey took so long.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> The Israelites tried some of the food. However, they did not consult the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> Then Joshua made a treaty with them, promising to spare their lives, and the leaders of the assembly swore an oath to guarantee it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> Three days after they had made the treaty, the Israelites learned that the Gibeonites lived nearby, right among them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> The Israelites left to go to the Gibeonite towns, and arrived there on the third day. The towns were Gibeon, Kephirah, Beeroth and Kiriath Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> But the Israelites did not attack them because of the treaty sworn by the leaders of the assembly in the name of the Lord, the God of Israel. At this all the Israelites protested against the leaders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> But the leaders replied to the people, “We swore to them by the Lord, the God of Israel, so we cannot lay a hand on them now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> So this is what we're going to do to them. We'll let them live, so that we won't be punished for breaking the oath that we swore to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> The leaders concluded, “Let them live.” So the Gibeonites became woodcutters and water-carriers in service to the entire assembly, as the Israelite leaders had ordered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> Then Joshua summoned the Gibeonites and asked them, “Why did you trick us? You told us, ‘We live a long way from you,’ but you live right next door to us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> Consequently you are under a curse. From now on you shall forever be servants, woodcutters and water-carriers for the house of my God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> They answered Joshua, “We your servants were told very clearly that the Lord your God had ordered Moses to give you all this land, and that all its inhabitants were to be wiped out before you. So we really feared for our lives because of you. That's why we did what we did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> Now we're in your hands. Do to us what you think is right and just.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Joshua did as he had said. He saved them from the Israelites, so that they did not kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> That day Joshua made them woodcutters and water-carriers in service to the entire assembly and for the altar of the Lord wherever the Lord should choose. That is what they do right up to this very day.</span>
</div>
