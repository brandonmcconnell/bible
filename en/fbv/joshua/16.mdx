<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 18:13; Joshua 16:2"> This was the territory allocated to the tribe of Ephraim, by families. The boundary of their allocation ran from Ataroth-addar in the east to Upper Beth-horon</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:7; Joshua 18:1"> and then on to the sea. From Michmethath in the north the boundary turned east passing Taanath-shiloh to the east of Janoah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:28; Joshua 6:26; Joshua 3:16"> From Janoah it went down to Ataroth and Naarah, then touched Jericho and ended at the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 17:8–9; Joshua 16:3–6; Joshua 12:17"> From Tappuah the boundary ran west to the Brook of Kanah and then out to the sea. This was the land allotted to the tribe of Ephraim, by families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 17:9"> Also some towns with their associated villages that lay in the land allotted to the tribe of Manasseh were assigned to the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:29; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; 1 Kings 9:16"> However, they did not drive out the Canaanites living in Gezer, so the Canaanites live among the tribe of Ephraim to this very day, but as forced laborers.</span>
