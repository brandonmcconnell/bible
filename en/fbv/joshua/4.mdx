<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 4:19–20; Joshua 3:13; Joshua 4:8"> and tell them, ‘Pick up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, from right where the priests are standing. Then carry them and set them down at the place where you will camp tonight.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Mark 3:14–19"> So Joshua sent for the twelve men he had chosen, one from each tribe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and told them, “Go back into the middle of the Jordan, right in front of the Ark of the Agreement of the Lord your God, and each of you pick up a stone and carry it on your shoulder, one for each of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Joshua 4:21; PSALMS 44:1"> This will be a memorial among you so when your children one day ask, ‘What do these stones mean?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 28:12"> you can tell them, ‘It's about the time the Jordan River stopped flowing when the Ark of the Lord's Agreement went across. When it crossed over the water stopped. These stones are a memorial to the people of Israel forever.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 4:2–5; Joshua 1:16–18; Joshua 4:20"> The people of Israel did as Joshua told them. The men picked up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan as the Lord had instructed Joshua. They carried them to the place where they camped overnight and placed the stones there, one for each of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; Judges 1:26; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan right where the priests carrying the Ark of the Agreement had stood, and they are still there to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; PSALMS 119:60; Proverbs 27:1"> The priests carrying the Ark remained standing in the middle of the Jordan until everything was done just as the Lord had told the people to do, all that Moses had told Joshua to do.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This repetition and the additional mention of Moses leads some commentators to believe that the author was using multiple sources for his account.">a</a></sup>The people crossed over quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 3:8; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:17"> Once all the people had crossed over, they watched as the Ark of the Lord was carried across by the priests.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Once all the people had crossed over, the Ark of the Lord was carried across by the priests and went ahead of the people.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–32; Joshua 1:14"> The armed men from the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh crossed at the head of the people of Israel, as Moses had stipulated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jeremiah 39:5; Jeremiah 52:8"> They numbered about 40,000 men, armed and ready for battle, who crossed in the presence of the Lord to the plains of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; 2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Kings 3:28"> On that day the Lord confirmed Joshua as a great leader in the sight of all the Israelites, and they were in awe of him just as they had been in awe of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The Lord had told Joshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Exodus 25:16–22; Joshua 3:3–6"> “Order the priests carrying the Ark of the Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Ark was also given this designation as it contained the ten commandments, God's testimony or witness to his people.">c</a></sup>to come up out of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 8:16–18; Acts 16:35–39; Acts 16:23"> So Joshua told the priests, “Come up out of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:15; Joshua 3:13"> The priests came up out of the Jordan carrying the Ark of the Agreement, and as soon as their feet touched dry ground the waters of the Jordan returned to where they had been, overflowing its banks as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; Micah 6:5; Exodus 12:2–3"> The people went up from the Jordan and camped at Gilgal, to the east of Jericho, on the tenth day of the first month.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Late March or early April.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:3; Joshua 4:8"> Joshua set up at Gilgal the twelve stones that had been taken from the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; PSALMS 145:4–7; PSALMS 105:2–5"> He told the Israelites, “When some day your children ask you their parents, ‘What do these stones mean?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 44:27"> you can explain to them, ‘This is where the Israelites crossed the Jordan on dry ground.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:12–14"> For the Lord your God made the Jordan River dry up right in front of you so you all could cross, just as the Lord your God did at the Red Sea which he dried up so we could all cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 106:8; PSALMS 89:13; 1 Kings 8:42–43"> He did this so everyone on earth would know how powerful the Lord is, and so that you might be in awe of the Lord your God forever.”</span>
