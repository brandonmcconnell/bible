<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; 1 Samuel 1:3; Joshua 21:2"> The land had been subdued<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Though subdued, it had not been fully conquered, as events of the time and later demonstrate.">a</a></sup>and lay before them. The Israelites gathered together at Shiloh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Shiloh” means “place of rest.”">b</a></sup>and set up the Tent of Meeting.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Tabernacle.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> However, seven of the Israelite tribes had not received their land allocations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="From what follows it's clear that the problem was not one of land allocation but a lack of desire to go and take possession of the land.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 18:9; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4"> So Joshua asked the Israelites, “How long will you go on being reluctant to go and take possession of the land that the Lord gave your forefathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:6; Joshua 4:2; Joshua 18:3"> Choose three men from each tribe and I will send them out to explore the land. Then they can write a description regarding the land allocations and bring it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 19:1–9; Joshua 15:1–12"> They are to divide the land into seven parts, up to the boundary of Judah's land in the south and Joseph's<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to Ephraim and Manasseh.">e</a></sup>land in the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:2; Joshua 18:10; Acts 13:19"> Once you have written the description about the land, divided into seven parts, you will bring it to me here and I will cast lots for you in the presence of the Lord our God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Joshua 13:8–31; Numbers 18:20"> But the Levites do not receive a share, for their role as priests of the Lord is their allocation. Also, Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their allocation that Moses, the servant of the Lord, gave them on the east side of the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 18:10; Romans 14:19; 1 Samuel 14:41"> As the men started out on their way to explore the land. Joshua told them, “Go throughout the land and write a description of what you find. Then return to me and I will cast lots for you in the presence of the Lord here in Shiloh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:19"> So the men went and explored the land and wrote down on a scroll a description of the seven parts, recording the towns in each part. Then they returned to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Acts 13:19; Joshua 18:6"> where Joshua cast lots for them in the presence of the Lord. There Joshua divided the land up and allotted the different parts to the remaining<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Remaining”: implied.">f</a></sup>Israelite tribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 15:1–8; Deuteronomy 10:1–22"> The first lot cast went to the tribe of Benjamin, by families. The land allotted lay between that of the tribe of Judah and the tribe of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 16:1; Joshua 7:2; Joshua 2:1"> Their boundary began at the Jordan, and went north of the slope of Jericho, west through the hill country, coming out at the wilderness of Beth-aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Joshua 16:2–3; Joshua 10:11"> Then the boundary went south to Luz (or Bethel) and on down to Ataroth-addar on the mountain south of Lower Beth-horon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:9; 1 Chronicles 13:5–6; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> Here the boundary turned south along the western side of the mountain facing Beth-horon, finishing at Kiriath-baal (or Kiriath-jearim), a town of the tribe of Judah. This was the western boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:9"> The southern boundary began at the edge of Kiriath-jearim. It ran<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The text says “west” but this is the wrong direction.">g</a></sup>to the spring at Nephtoah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Joshua 15:7–8; Isaiah 30:33"> and then down to the foot of the mountain that faces the valley of Ben-hinnom, at the north end of the valley of Rephaim. Then it went down the valley of Hinnom, along the slope near the Jebusite town,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jerusalem.">h</a></sup>south towards En-rogel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 15:6"> From there it headed north to En-shemesh and on to Geliloth, opposite the heights of Adummim, and then on down to the Stone of Bohan (Reuben's son).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; Joshua 15:61"> Then it went along the ridge opposite the Jordan Valley to the north, and then down into the Jordan Valley itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Joshua 15:2; Numbers 34:3"> From there it ran along the north slope of Beth-hoglah, ending at the north bay of the Salt Sea, the southern end of the Jordan. This was the southern boundary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The eastern boundary was the Jordan.<br />These were the boundaries around the land of the tribe of Benjamin, by families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 6:1; Joshua 18:19"> These were the towns of the tribe of Benjamin, according to families: Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; 1 Kings 12:29–32; Joshua 18:18"> Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17"> Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 2:26; Joshua 21:17; Isaiah 10:29"> Kephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba—twelve towns with their associated villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:17; 1 Kings 3:4–5; Joshua 10:2"> In addition: Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 10:17; Ezra 2:25; Joshua 9:17"> Mizpah, Kephirah, Mozah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Joshua 15:8; Judges 20:4–5"> Zela, Haeleph, Jebus (or Jerusalem), Gibeah, and Kiriath-jearim—fourteen towns with their associated villages. This was the land allotted to the tribe of Benjamin, by families.</span>
</div>
