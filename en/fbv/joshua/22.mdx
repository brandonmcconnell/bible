<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:18–33; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> Then Joshua summoned the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:12–18; Deuteronomy 3:16–20; Numbers 32:20–29"> He told them, “You have done everything that Moses, the servant of the Lord, told you to do, and you have followed all the commands I gave you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:23–27"> You have never abandoned your brothers all this time, right up to the present day. You have carefully followed what the Lord your God ordered you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Numbers 32:18; Deuteronomy 3:20"> Now that the Lord your God has given peace to your brothers, as he promised, go back home to your land that Moses, the servant of the Lord, gave you on the other side of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; Exodus 20:6; Romans 8:28"> But make sure you keep the commandments and the law as Moses instructed you. Love the Lord your God, follow all his ways, keep his commandments, stay close to him, and serve him with your whole being.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:13; Luke 24:50; Genesis 47:7"> Joshua blessed them, sent them on their way, and they went home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–12; Numbers 32:33; Joshua 13:29–31"> Moses had given to the half-tribe of Manasseh the land of Bashan, and to the other half of the tribe Joshua had given land west of the Jordan. Joshua blessed them and sent them home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 31:27; Deuteronomy 8:9–14; Proverbs 3:16"> He told them, “Take all the wealth you have gained back home: the great herds of livestock, the things made of gold, silver, copper, and iron, the huge quantity of clothes. Share all this plunder with your brothers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Numbers 32:26; Numbers 32:1"> So the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh left the rest of the Israelites at Shiloh in the land of Canaan and went home to their land in Gilead that they had received at the Lord's command through Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:26–27; Genesis 31:46–52; Joshua 4:5–9"> When they approached the Jordan region, still in the land of Canaan, the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built a large and impressive<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “for appearance.” This may also indicate that the altar was not to function as a place for sacrifice, simply that it looked like one.">a</a></sup>altar beside the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Leviticus 17:8–9; Joshua 2:7"> The Israelites were told, “Look, the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built an altar in the Jordan region of the land of Canaan, on the side belonging to the Israelites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–11; Deuteronomy 13:15; Galatians 4:17–18"> The Israelites gathered at Shiloh to go to war against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 25:7; PSALMS 106:30–31; Exodus 6:25"> Before they did so, they sent Phinehas, the son of Eleazar the priest to the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:4; Exodus 18:25"> Ten leaders went with him, one from each of the ten tribes of Israel, and each the head of his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 15:23; Deuteronomy 30:17"> After they arrived they told the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:13–14"> “This is what all of the Lord's people say, ‘What is this disloyal act you have committed against the God of Israel by building an altar for yourselves? How could you turn away from him now in such rebellion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–18; PSALMS 106:28–29; Deuteronomy 4:3–4"> Wasn't our sin at Peor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Numbers 25:1-9. It is likely that Phinehas as leader of the delegation spoke, and he was the one who had taken decisive action as recorded in Numbers 25:7-8.">b</a></sup>enough? Even now we're still not clean from the plague that attacked the Lord's people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This may not mean that the disease was still present, but the effects of lost relatives still had an effect. In addition it may mean that the cause of the plague—the worship of false gods—was still a problem, as is indicated by God's warning against them in 24:14-23.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Joshua 22:16; Joshua 7:11"> So why are you turning away from the Lord now? If you rebel against the Lord today, he will be angry with all of us tomorrow!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Acts 10:14–15"> But if you think your land is tainted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A possible reason for building an altar might be that the land was viewed as “unclean” and needed to be “purified.”">d</a></sup>then come over to the land of the Lord where the Tabernacle of the Lord is located and share some of our land with us. Just don't rebel against the Lord, or against us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Or against us”: or, “or make us rebels too.”">e</a></sup>, by building for yourselves an altar other than the altar of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:1; Joshua 7:5; Joshua 7:18"> When Achan, son of Zerah, acted disloyally in taking consecrated things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 7:1-26.">f</a></sup>didn't the whole of Israel suffer punishment? He wasn't the only one who died because of his sin!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 16:1; 1 Peter 3:15"> Then the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the Israelite leaders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; PSALMS 44:21; Deuteronomy 10:17"> “The Lord is God of gods,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “The Lord, the Mighty One, is God!”">g</a></sup>the Lord is God of gods, and he knows! May Israel also know!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In other words, God knows their motives for building the altar, and the Israelite leaders should also know their motives.">h</a></sup>If we're in rebellion against God or being disloyal to him then kill us right now!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16; Deuteronomy 18:19; Ezekiel 3:18"> If our action of building an altar was to turn away from the Lord, or to use the altar to make burnt offerings or grain offerings or friendship offerings, then may the Lord punish us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Deuteronomy 6:20; Genesis 30:33"> We did this because we were worried that in the future your descendants might say to ours, ‘What have you got to do with the Lord, the God of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; Ezra 4:2–3; 1 Samuel 26:19"> The Lord put a border—the Jordan River—between us and you, descendants of Reuben and Gad. You don't belong to the Lord.’ So your descendants might stop our descendants from worshiping the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So we said to ourselves, ‘Let's build an altar, not for burnt offerings or for sacrifices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Genesis 31:48; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> but as a witness between us and you, and for the generations that come after us, that we will come to worship the Lord in his presence with our burnt offerings, sacrifices, and friendship offerings.’ Then your descendants will not be able to say to ours in the future, ‘You don't belong to the Lord.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Hebrews 8:5"> If they were to do so in the future, our descendants could reply, ‘Look at this copy of the Lord's altar which our forefathers made, not for burnt offerings or for sacrifices, but as a witness between us and you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 24:16; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Joshua 22:23"> We would never think to rebel against the Lord or to turn away from him now by building an altar to make burnt offerings or grain offerings or sacrifices. The only altar of the Lord our God is the one that stands in front of his Tabernacle.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:33; Esther 1:21; Proverbs 15:1"> When Phinehas and the Israelite leaders heard this from the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh they were delighted.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“They were delighted”: literally, “it was good in their eyes.”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Leviticus 26:11–12; Isaiah 12:6"> Phinehas replied to the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, “Today we know that the Lord is with us because you have not acted disloyally in doing this. Now you have saved the Israelites from being punished by the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="If the Israelites had wrongly gone to war against the other tribes this would have surely brought on them divine judgment.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 22:12–14; Proverbs 25:13"> Then Phinehas and the Israelite leaders left the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead and returned to the land of Canaan to explain the situation to the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:20; Luke 2:28; Daniel 2:19"> The Israelites were pleased with the report and God blessed them. They didn't talk about going to war any more to destroy the land where the tribes of Reuben and Gad were living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 22:27; Matthew 4:10; Isaiah 43:10"> The tribes of Reuben and Gad called the altar, “Witness,” because they said, “It is a witness between us that the Lord is also our God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
