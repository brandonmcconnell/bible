<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 11:10; PSALMS 2:1–4; Judges 4:2"> When Jabin, king of Hazor, heard about what happened, he sent a message<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A call to arms.">a</a></sup>to Jobab, king of Madon, to the kings of Shimron and Achshaph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11"> and to the kings of the northern hill country, the Jordan Valley south of Kinnereth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Kinnereth”: the Sea of Galilee.">b</a></sup>, the western foothills, and the foothills of Dor to the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:3; Joshua 18:26; Genesis 31:49"> to the kings of the Canaanites, both east and west, the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites in the hill country, and the Hivites living near Mount Hermon in the land of Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:12; 1 Samuel 13:5; Genesis 32:12"> All their armies assembled together, a vast horde as numberless as the sand of the seashore, together with many, many horses and chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 118:10–12; Revelation 16:14; Isaiah 8:9"> All these kings joined forces and set up camp beside the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; 2 Samuel 8:4; Judges 20:28"> The Lord said to Joshua, “Don't be afraid because of them, for by this time tomorrow I myself will hand them all over to Israel, dead. Cripple their horses and burn their chariots.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> Joshua and the Israelite army went and attacked them without warning at the waters of Merom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:6; Joshua 19:28; Zechariah 9:2"> The Lord handed them over to the Israelites who cut them down and chased them as far as Greater Sidon and Misrephoth Maim, and east to the valley of Mizpah, killing them until none were left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> Afterwards Joshua did what the Lord had ordered: he crippled the horses and burned the chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:2"> Then Joshua turned on Hazor. He captured it and killed its king, for Hazor was at that time the chief of all these kingdoms.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The king of Hazor had been the leader of the coalition against Israel—see 10:1.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 10:40; Deuteronomy 20:16"> Joshua had all the inhabitants killed, leaving no survivors. He set it apart and completely destroyed it—no one was left alive. Then he burned down Hazor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Deuteronomy 7:2; Numbers 33:50–53"> Joshua captured all these towns and killed their kings. He set them apart and completely destroyed them, as Moses the servant of the Lord had ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18"> However, Israel did not burn any of the towns built on mounds, except Hazor, which Joshua did burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9; Joshua 8:27"> The Israelites did take for themselves all the plunder and livestock from these towns. But they killed all the inhabitants, destroying them all so none were left alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Exodus 34:11–13; Acts 20:20"> As the Lord had instructed Moses, so Moses had instructed Joshua. Joshua did what he had been told—he did everything that the Lord had instructed Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 10:41; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 12:8"> So Joshua conquered the whole land—the hill country, the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the western foothills, the Jordan Valley, the mountains of Israel, and the eastern foothills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 11:3; Joshua 13:5"> This covered land from Mount Halak leading to Seir in the south, right up to Baal Gad in the north, below Mount Hermon in the valley of Lebanon. Joshua captured and killed all their kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 14:7–10; Joshua 11:23"> Joshua fought a long war with all these kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–27"> Not a single town made peace with the Israelites except the Hivites, the inhabitants of Gibeon. All the rest were conquered in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 4:21"> For the Lord made them stubborn, wanting to fight the Israelites so that they might be set apart and completely destroyed, wiped out without mercy, as the Lord had instructed Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:2; Numbers 13:22–23; Joshua 15:13–14"> During this time Joshua also annihilated the descendants of Anak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A race of giants which had terrified the spies originally sent out to the Promised Land. See Numbers 13:28-33.">d</a></sup>living in the hill country of Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and all the hill country of Judah and Israel. Joshua set apart and completely destroyed their towns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:4; 1 Chronicles 18:1; Joshua 15:46"> and there were no descendants of Anak left in the land of Israel, only some in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Philistine towns on the coastal plain.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 21:44–45; Numbers 26:52–55; Joshua 14:1–15"> So Joshua took the entire land in accordance with what the Lord had instructed Moses, giving it to Israel to own as it was divided up among the tribes. Then the land was at peace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“At peace”: literally, “rested from war.”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
