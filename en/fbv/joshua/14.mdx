<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:17–29"> This was the land that was allotted to the Israelites to own in the land of Canaan by Eleazar the priest, Joshua, son of Nun, and the leaders of the tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 34:13; Numbers 26:55–56"> The decision regarding the land division among the nine and a half tribes was made by casting lots, as the Lord had instructed Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:14; Joshua 13:8; Joshua 13:32–33"> Moses had allotted land to the two and a half tribes east of the Jordan but he had made no allocation to the Levites among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 48:5; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2; Numbers 35:2–8"> The descendants of Joseph had become two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. The Levites were not given any land, only towns to live in and pasture for their flocks and herds.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Herds”: literally, “possessions,” meaning movable personal property.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:2; Numbers 35:1–2"> So the Israelites followed the instructions the Lord had given Moses in allocating the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:24"> Leaders from the tribe of Judah approached Joshua at Gilgal, and Caleb, son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, said to him, “You remember what the Lord told Moses, the man of God, at Kadesh-barnea about me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 13:26–33; Numbers 14:6–10"> I was forty when Moses, the servant of God, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land. When I returned I told the truth in my report.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 13:31"> But those who went with me made our people afraid. However, I have faithfully followed the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:3; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:22–24"> At that time Moses made a solemn promise, telling me, ‘The land where you have walked will belong to you and your children forever, because you faithfully followed the Lord my God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:33–34; Numbers 14:30; Joshua 11:18"> Look—the Lord has kept me alive these past forty-five years as he promised, from the time the Lord told Moses this while Israel was wandering in the wilderness. Now I'm eighty-five,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; PSALMS 103:5; Deuteronomy 31:2"> but I'm still as strong today as when Moses sent me out. I'm as strong and ready for battle or for whatever may come as I was then.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:33; Philippians 4:13; Numbers 13:33"> So give me the hill country that the Lord spoke about. You heard at that time about the descendants of Anak who lived there in large, fortified towns. If the Lord is with me I will drive them out as the Lord promised.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:20; Joshua 22:6; 1 Chronicles 6:55–56"> So Joshua blessed Caleb and granted him ownership of Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 14:8–9; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So Hebron belonged to Caleb, son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, from that day to this because he had faithfully followed the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Genesis 23:2; Joshua 15:13"> (Hebron was formerly called Kiriath-arba, named after a great leader of the descendants of Anak.) And the land was at peace.</span>
