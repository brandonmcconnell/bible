<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> The Lord said to Joshua, “Don't be afraid or downhearted! Take all the fighting men with you and attack Ai, for I'm handing over to you the king of Ai, his people, his town, and his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; 2 Chronicles 20:22"> You will do to Ai and its king what you did to Jericho and its king. However, this time you may keep for yourselves the plunder and the livestock. Set up an ambush behind the town.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> So Joshua and all the people got ready to attack Ai. He chose thirty thousand of his best fighting men and sent them out at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> He ordered them, “You lie in ambush behind the town, not far away. All of you need to be ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> When I and the rest of the men with me approach the town, the defenders will run out to attack us just like before, and we'll run away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> They'll chase after us as we draw them away from the town, because they'll say to each other, ‘They're running away from us just like before.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; 2 Kings 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> While we're running away from them, you will get up from your ambush positions and take the town, for the Lord God will hand it over to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> Once you've captured the town, set it on fire, as the Lord has instructed. Now follow your orders.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> Joshua sent them out, and they went to lie in ambush between Bethel and the west side of Ai. But that night Joshua stayed with the people in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; PSALMS 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> Early the next morning Joshua got up early and gathered the people and went up to attack Ai, accompanied by the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All the fighting men who were with him approached the front of the town, and camped there on the north side, with a valley between them and Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> He took about five thousand men and had them lie in ambush between Bethel and Ai, to the west of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:12; Joshua 8:8"> So the army took up their positions—the main army to the north of the town, and the ambush to the west. Joshua went that night to the valley.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> As soon as the king of Ai saw the situation, he and all his men rushed out early in the morning to attack the Israelites where they had before, at a place overlooking the Jordan valley.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="To the east of the town.">a</a></sup>He didn't know about the ambush waiting on the other side of the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> Joshua and the Israelites allowed themselves to be driven back, and ran away in the direction of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> All the men of the town were called out to chase after them, and as they pursued Joshua they were drawn father from the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> There wasn't a man left in Ai and Bethel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Presumably the men from the nearby town of Bethel joined in what they thought was the rout of the Israelites. The Septuagint omits the mention of Bethel.">b</a></sup>who didn't go out to chase the Israelites. They left the town wide open as they pursued the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; 1 Samuel 17:41"> Then the Lord told Joshua, “Hold up the spear in your hand and point it at Ai, because I'm giving it to you.” So Joshua held up the spear in his hand and pointed it at the town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> As soon as they saw this signal the men lying in ambush got up and rushed into the town. They captured it, and quickly set it on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> When the men of Ai looked back they saw the smoke rising up into the sky from the town. They had nowhere to run to, because the Israelites who had been running away towards the wilderness now turned on their pursuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For when Joshua and the Israelites saw that the ambush group had captured the town, and that smoke was rising from it, they turned around and attacked the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> The men from the ambush also came from the town and attacked them, so they were caught in the middle, with the Israelites on both sides. The Israelites cut them down—not a single man survived or got away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> Only the king of Ai was captured alive, and he was brought before Joshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> When the Israelites had finished killing the men of Ai who had chased them towards the wilderness—once they had all been cut down by the sword—the whole Israelite army returned to the town and killed everyone living there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> Those who were killed that day numbered twelve thousand, men and women—all the inhabitants of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:11–12; Joshua 8:18"> For Joshua had continued to hold up his spear until all the people of Ai had been destroyed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Destroyed”: The word for destruction here is the same as that used for the destruction of Jericho—“devoted to the Lord.” See the footnote for 6:17.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; PSALMS 50:10"> Only the livestock and plunder were taken from the town by the Israelites, as the Lord had instructed Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Kings 19:25"> So Joshua burned the town of Ai, making it permanently into a heap of ruins where no one lives to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> He killed the king of Ai and hung his body on a tree until evening. When the sun went down Joshua ordered the body taken down. They threw it down in front of the entrance to the town gate and piled a heap of rocks over it which is still there today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then Joshua built an altar on Mount Ebal for the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> He did what Moses, the servant of the Lord, had told the Israelites to do, as recorded in the book of the Law of Moses: an altar of uncut stones which no one has worked with iron tools. On the altar they made burnt offerings and friendship offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> There in the presence of the Israelites, Joshua inscribed on the stones a copy of the Law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> All the Israelites, the elders, the officers, and the judges stood in two groups facing each other with the priests, the Levites, and the Ark of the Lord's Agreement between them. (Included were the foreigners as well as the native born.) Half of them stood in front of Mount Gerizim, and half in front of Mount Ebal, just as Moses had ordered, for the blessing of the people this first time.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The first blessing having entered the Promised Land.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> Then Joshua read out the whole Law—all the blessings and curses recorded in the book of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremiah 26:2"> Joshua read out every word of Moses' instruction to the whole Israelite assembly, including the women, the children, and the foreigners who lived among them.</span>
</div>
