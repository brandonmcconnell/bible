<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 13:1; Joshua 21:44; Joshua 11:23"> A long time later, after the Lord had given peace to the Israelites from conflict with their enemies around them, Joshua, by now really old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; Deuteronomy 31:28; 1 Chronicles 28:1"> summoned all the Israelites—the elders, leaders, judges, and officials—and told them, “I'm old, getting on in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:14"> You've seen everything the Lord your God has done to all the nations on your behalf—the Lord your God has been fighting for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:6–7; Joshua 18:10; Joshua 13:2"> I have allotted the land of the remaining nations for you to own, as well as the nations already conquered, from the Jordan to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 13:6; Exodus 33:2"> The Lord your God will make them retreat from you. He will drive them out before you and you will take possession of their land, as the Lord your God promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 28:14; Proverbs 4:26–27"> Be sure to observe everything that is written in the book of the Law of Moses. Don't stray from it, either to the left or to the right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; PSALMS 16:4; Deuteronomy 7:2–3"> Don't associate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Particularly regarding intermarriage. See verse 12.">a</a></sup>with the nations that are left. Don't mention the names of their gods, and don't swear by them, worship them, or bow down to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 13:4"> Stay close to the Lord your God, as you have done up to now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 23:5"> The Lord has driven out strong, powerful nations before you. No one has been able to stand up to you to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8; Deuteronomy 3:22"> Just one of you can chase away a thousand enemies, for the Lord your God is fighting for you, as he promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 6:5–12; Proverbs 4:23"> Take great care to love the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Ezekiel 18:24; Zephaniah 1:6"> For if you do turn from him, and adopt the ways of the nations that are left, if you intermarry with them, associating with one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Numbers 33:55; Exodus 23:33"> you can be absolutely sure that the Lord your God will definitely not drive out these nations before you. On the contrary,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup>they will be a trap and a snare to you, a whip on your back and thorns in your eyes until you die out completely from this good land the Lord your God has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 21:33; 1 Kings 2:2; Leviticus 26:3–13"> Now I am about to die, which is the fate of every living thing on the earth. Deep down you know that not a single one of the Lord's good promises has failed. Everything has come true. Not a single one has failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:14–46; Judges 3:8"> But in the same way that you received all the good things that the Lord your God promised, the Lord will bring on you all the bad things he has threatened until you're completely wiped out from this good land the Lord your God has given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26; 2 Kings 24:20"> If you break the agreement the Lord your God made with you, and you go and worship other gods, bowing down before them, then the Lord will become angry with you, and you will be quickly wiped out from the good land he has given you.”</span>
