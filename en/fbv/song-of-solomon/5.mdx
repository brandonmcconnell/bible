Let us be drunk with love!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some take this last line as being spoken by the women of Jerusalem, in which case it could be translated, “Friends, eat and drink, and become drunk with love.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Song of Songs 2:14; Song of Songs 6:9"> Though I was asleep, my mind<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Mind”: literally, “heart,” but in Hebrew the heart is primarily the source of thought. I heard my love knocking, and calling out, “Please open the door, my sister, my darling, my dove, my perfect love. My head is soaked with dew, my hair is wet from the night mist.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Romans 7:22–23; Proverbs 13:4"> I replied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Implied.">c</a></sup>“I've already got undressed. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:4; Genesis 43:30; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> My love thrust his hand into the opening. Deep inside I longed for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:13; Ephesians 3:17; Song of Songs 5:2"> I got up to let my love in. My hands dripped with myrrh, my fingers with liquid myrrh, as I grabbed the handles of the bolt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:2; Revelation 3:19; Hosea 5:15"> I opened up to my love, but he had left—he was gone! I called him but he didn't answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 3:3; Matthew 21:33–41; Acts 5:40–41"> The watchmen found me as they went through the city. They beat me, they hurt me, and stole my cloak, those watchmen of the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:7; Song of Songs 2:5; Song of Songs 3:5"> Women of Jerusalem, promise me if you find my love and wonder what you should tell him, tell him I am weak with love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:8; Song of Songs 6:1; Song of Songs 6:9–10"> Why is the one you love better than any other, most beautiful of women? In what way is the one you love better than any other that we should promise you that?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 45:2; Song of Songs 2:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> My love has dazzling good looks and is very fit—better than ten thousand others!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:5; Song of Songs 5:2; Daniel 7:9"> His head is like the finest gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="It is unsure as to what comparison is being made—some believe it is to a bronzed complexion, others to some valued beauty.">e</a></sup>, his hair is wavy and black as the raven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:1; Song of Songs 1:15; Hebrews 4:13"> His eyes are like doves beside springs of water, washed with milk and mounted like sparkling jewels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Mounted like sparkling jewels”: or, “sitting beside pools.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:10; Song of Songs 2:1; Song of Songs 6:2"> His cheeks are like a flowerbed of spices that produces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Septuagint reading. Hebrew “towers.”">g</a></sup>fragrance. His lips are like lilies, dripping with liquid myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> His arms are round bars of gold inlaid with jewels. His abdomen is like carved ivory inlaid with lapis lazuli.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Lapis lazuli”: sometimes translated “sapphires” but it seems these were unknown at the time.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 1:15–16; 1 Kings 4:33; Matthew 17:2"> His legs are columns of alabaster set on bases of gold. He looks strong, like the mighty cedars of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 45:2; Song of Songs 7:9; Isaiah 9:6–7"> His mouth is the sweetest ever; he is totally desirable! This is my love, my friend, women of Jerusalem.</span>
