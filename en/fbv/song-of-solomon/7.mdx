<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Songs 4:1; Micah 7:14"> Your head is as magnificent as Mount Carmel; your black hair has a purple sheen, as if a king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Purple was the color of royalty.">c</a></sup>was held captive in your locks!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:15–16; PSALMS 45:11; Song of Songs 4:10"> How beautiful you are, my love—how attractive are your charms!</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Songs 2:5"> I tell myself, “I will climb the palm tree and take hold of the fruit.” May your breasts be like bunches of grapes on the vine, and your breath have the scent of apples!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> May your kisses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “mouth.”">d</a></sup>be like the best wine, going down smoothly, gliding over lips and teeth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Over lips and teeth”: as per some versions. Hebrew: “over lips of sleepers.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 45:11; Song of Songs 2:16; Song of Songs 6:3"> My love is mine, and I am the one he desires!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:8; Song of Songs 2:10–13; Song of Songs 1:4"> Come, my love, let's go out into the countryside, and spend the night among the henna flowers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Henna flowers”: or “villages.” The same word “henna” is used in 1:14and 4:13. <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 30:14; Matthew 13:52; 1 Peter 4:11"> The mandrakes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A plant thought to be an aphrodisiac and associated with fertility.
