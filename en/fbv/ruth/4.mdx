<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7; Ruth 3:12; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Boaz went to the town gate,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Civil affairs including legal matters were conducted in the area around the town gate.">a</a></sup>and sat down there. The family redeemer that Boaz had mentioned happened to pass by, so Boaz said to him, “Come over here, friend, and sit down.” The man came over and sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 31:23; 1 Kings 21:8; Acts 6:12"> Then Boaz selected ten of the town elders and asked them to sit there with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; PSALMS 112:5"> Boaz said to the family redeemer, “Naomi who has returned from the country of Moab is selling the piece of land that belonged to Elimelech, our relative.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25–29; Jeremiah 32:7–12; Romans 12:17"> I decided I should tell you in case you want to buy it here in the presence of these elders of the people. If you want to redeem it, then go ahead. But if you don't, then tell me so I'll know, because you are first in line to redeem it, and I'm next.”<br />“I want to redeem it,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The answer is not a strongly positive one.">b</a></sup>said the family redeemer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:8; Deuteronomy 25:5–6; Matthew 22:24"> “When you buy the land from Naomi you also acquire Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's widow, so you can marry her and have children with her to ensure the man's line continues,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The marriage provision is found in Deuteronomy 25:5-10and following, while the land transfer laws are in Leviticus 25:23-28.">c</a></sup>Boaz explained.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25; Ruth 3:13"> “Well, I can't do it then,” the family redeemer replied. “If I were to redeem it, that could jeopardize what I already own.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The man was concerned that any property he already had would also be included in the legacy to any son Ruth would have, and it would be credited to her dead husband's line.">d</a></sup>You redeem it for yourself, because I can't.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7–10"> (Now during those times it was the custom in Israel to confirm the action of family redeemer, property transfer, or any similar legal matter by removing a sandal and handing it over. This was the way of validating a transaction in Israel.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the family redeemer took off his sandal and told Boaz, “You buy it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–12; Genesis 23:16–18"> Then Boaz said to the elders and all the people present, “You are witnesses that today I have bought from Naomi all that belonged to Elimelech, Mahlon, and Chilion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:6; Ephesians 5:25; Genesis 29:27"> I have also acquired Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's widow, as my wife. By having children who may inherit his property his name will be kept alive in his family and in his home town. You are witnesses of this today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–24; Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:6"> The elders and all the people present at the town gate said, “Yes, we are witnesses. May the Lord make the woman who is coming to your house like Rachel and Leah who between them gave birth to the people of Israel. May you become prosperous in Ephrathah, and famous in Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 38:29; 1 Samuel 2:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4"> May your descendants the Lord gives you through this young woman become like the descendants of Perez, the son Tamar bore to Judah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 33:5; Genesis 29:31; Ruth 3:11"> Boaz took Ruth home, and she became his wife. He slept with her, and the Lord arranged for her to become pregnant, and she gave birth to a son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:5–20; Luke 1:58; Genesis 12:2"> The women of the town<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 1:19.">e</a></sup>came to Naomi and said, “Praise the Lord, for today he didn't leave you without a family redeemer by giving you this grandson<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“By giving you this grandson”: implied.">f</a></sup>—may he become famous throughout Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 55:22; Isaiah 46:4; Genesis 45:11"> He will give you a new lease on life and provide for you in your old age, for your daughter-in-law, who loves you and who is better than seven sons to you, has given birth to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Naomi picked up the child and hugged him. She looked after him like her own son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “she became his nurse.”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:58–63"> The neighbor women named him Obed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Obed,” meaning “servant” as in “servant of God.”">h</a></sup>saying “Naomi now has a son!” He was the father of Jesse who was the father of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 1:3–6; 1 Chronicles 2:4–8; Luke 3:33"> This is the family line of Perez: Perez was the father of Hezron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:33; 1 Chronicles 2:9–10; Matthew 1:4"> Hezron was the father of Ram. Ram was the father of Amminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:7; Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:4"> Amminadab was the father of Nahshon. Nahshon was the father of Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:5; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> Salmon was the father of Boaz. Boaz was the father of Obed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:15; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 1:6"> Obed was the father of Jesse. Jesse was the father of David.</span>
</div>
