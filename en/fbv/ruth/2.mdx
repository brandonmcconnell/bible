<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Ruth 3:12; Ruth 3:2"> Now Naomi had a relative on her husband's side whose name was Boaz. He was a rich and influential man from the family of Elimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> Soon after, Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, “Please let me go to the fields and pick up grain that's been left behind—if I can find someone who'll give me permission.” “Yes, go ahead, my daughter,” Naomi replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:12; Matthew 10:29; Luke 10:31"> So she went and picked up grain the reapers had left behind. She happened to be working in a field that belonged to Boaz, a relative of Elimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 129:7–8; Judges 6:12; Luke 1:28"> Later on Boaz arrived from Bethlehem, and said to the reapers, “May the Lord be with you!” They replied, “The Lord bless you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> Then Boaz asked his servant who was in charge of the harvesters, “Who is this young woman related to?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Whose is that young woman?”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Matthew 24:45; Genesis 24:2"> “The young woman is a Moabite who came back with Naomi from Moab,” the servant replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 18:23; Proverbs 15:33"> “She asked me, ‘Please may I have permission to pick up grain behind the reapers.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew adds “among the sheaves,” but this is probably transposed from verse 15. She was given this unusual permission by Boaz only later.">b</a></sup>So she came, and she's been working here from morning until now, except for a brief rest in the shelter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; Song of Songs 1:7–8; 1 Samuel 3:6"> Boaz went and spoke to Ruth. “Listen to me, my daughter,” he told her. “Don't leave to go and pick up grain in someone else's field. Stay close to my women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Pay attention to what part of the field the men are reaping and follow the women.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="It is thought that the men did the work of cutting the stalks of grain, while the women followed behind tying them into sheaves.">c</a></sup>I've told the men not to bother you. When you get thirsty, go and have a drink from the water jars the servants have filled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; Ruth 2:13; Luke 1:43"> She bowed down with her face to the ground. “Why are you being so kind to me or even notice me, seeing I'm a foreigner?” she asked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Luke 18:29–30; Luke 5:11"> “I've heard about all you've done for your mother-in-law since your husband died,” Boaz replied. “And also how you left your father and mother, and the land of your birth, to come and live among people you didn't know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 36:7; PSALMS 91:4; PSALMS 63:7"> May the Lord fully reward you for all you've done—the Lord, the God of Israel, to whom you've come for protection.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “under whose wings you have sought refuge.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:18; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 33:8"> “Thank you for being so good to me, sir,” she replied. “You have reassured me by speaking to me kindly. I'm not even one of your servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 2:18; 2 Kings 4:43–44; Matthew 14:20"> When it was time to eat, Boaz called her over. “Come here,” he said. “Take some bread and dip it in wine vinegar.” So she sat down with the workers and Boaz passed her some roasted grain to eat. She ate until she'd had enough with some left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 1:5"> After Ruth went back to work Boaz told his men, “Let her pick up grain even among the sheaves. Don't say anything to embarrass her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 1 John 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> In fact, pull out some stalks from the bundles you're cutting and leave them for her to pick up. Don't tell her off.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–12; Proverbs 31:27; Exodus 16:36"> Ruth worked in the field until the evening. When she beat out the grain that she had picked up it was a large amount.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Large amount,”: literally, “an ephah,” a unit of measurement of uncertain quantity, estimated at between 22 and 45 liters.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:14; John 6:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:4"> She picked it up and took it back to town to show her mother-in-law how much she had collected. Ruth also gave her what she had left over from her meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; PSALMS 41:1; 1 Kings 7:21"> Naomi asked her, “Where did you pick up grain today? Exactly where did you work? Bless whoever cared enough about you to pay you some attention!” So she told her mother-in-law about who she had worked with. “The man I worked with today is called Boaz.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:5; Ruth 3:9–10; Leviticus 25:25"> “The Lord bless him!” Naomi exclaimed to her daughter-in-law. “He goes on showing his kindness to the living and the dead. That man is a close relative to us—a ‘family redeemer.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Family redeemer”: a term for someone who had the responsibility to protect the interests of the family, particularly in the case of someone who died.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:7–8; Ruth 2:7–8; Ruth 2:22"> Ruth added, “He also told me, ‘Stay close to my workers until they have finished harvesting my entire crop.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:8; Proverbs 27:10"> “That's good, my daughter,” Naomi told Ruth. “Stay with his women workers. Don't go to other fields where you might be molested.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8; Deuteronomy 16:9; Ephesians 6:1–3"> So Ruth stayed with Boaz's women workers, picking up grain until the end of the barley harvest, and then on to the end of the wheat harvest. She lived with her mother-in-law the whole time.</span>
