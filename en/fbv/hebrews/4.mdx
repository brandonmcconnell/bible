<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For we've heard the good news just like they did, but it didn't help them because they didn't accept and trust in what they heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> However, those of us who trust in God have entered into that rest God mentioned when he said, “In my frustration I vowed, ‘They shall not enter my rest.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 95:11.">a</a></sup>(This is so even though God's plans were already complete when he created the world.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> Regarding the seventh day there's a place in Scripture that says, “God rested on the seventh day from all his work.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 2:2.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> And as the previous passage stated, “They shall not enter my rest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> God's rest is still there for some to enter, even though those who previously heard the good news failed to enter because of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> So God again sets a day—today—saying a long time later through David as he did before, “If you hear what God is saying to you today, don't have a hard-hearted attitude.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 95:7.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Joshua had been able to give them rest, God wouldn't have spoken later about another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> So a Sabbath rest remains for God's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For whoever enters God's rest also rests from what he's doing, just as God did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> As a result we should try hard to enter that rest so nobody falls through following the same bad example of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For God's word is alive and effective, sharper than any twin-edged sword, so penetrating it separates life and breath,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Greek “psuche” and “pneuma,” sometimes translated “soul” and “spirit,” though it is hard to understand the meaning since in common thought there is no difference between “soul” and “spirit.” The translation of “life” and “breath” is employed since it is considered that this better expresses the original thought.">d</a></sup>bone joints and their marrow, judging the thoughts and intentions of the mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; PSALMS 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> No living being is hidden from his sight; everything is exposed and visible to the one we're accountable to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Since we have such a great high priest who has ascended to heaven, Jesus the Son of God, let us make sure we hold on to what we say we believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For the high priest we have isn't one who doesn't sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who was tempted in all the ways we are, but did not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> So we should go confidently to God on his throne of grace so we can receive mercy, and discover grace to help us when we really need it.</span>
