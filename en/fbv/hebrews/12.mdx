<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> For that reason, since we are surrounded by such a great crowd of people who gave evidence of their trust in God, let's get rid of everything that holds us back, the seductive sins that trip us up, and let's keep on running the race placed before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> We should keep on looking to Jesus, the one who begins and completes our trust in God. Because of the joy ahead of him Jesus endured the cross, disregarding its shame, and sat down at the right hand of God's throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> Think about Jesus who endured such hostility from sinful people so you don't grow tired and become discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> Your resistance so far hasn't cost you blood as you fight against sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> Have you forgotten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “You have forgotten.”">a</a></sup>God's appeal to you when he reasons with you as his children? He says, “My child, don't treat the Lord's discipline lightly, and don't give up when he corrects you either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; PSALMS 119:75"> The Lord disciplines everyone he loves, and he punishes everyone he welcomes as his child.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> Be patient as you experience God's discipline because he is treating you as his children. What child doesn't experience a father's discipline?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; PSALMS 73:1"> If you are not disciplined (which everyone has experienced), then you are illegitimate and not true children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> For if we respected our earthly fathers who disciplined us, shouldn't we even more be subject to the discipline of our spiritual Father which leads to life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> They disciplined us for a short while as they thought appropriate, but God does so for our benefit in order that we can share his holy character.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> When it happens, all discipline seems painful and not something to be happy about. But later on it produces peace in those who have been trained in this way so that they do what is good and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> So strengthen your feeble hands, and your weak knees!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 35:3.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> Make straight paths to walk on,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Proverbs 4:26.">c</a></sup>so that those who are crippled won't lose their way, but will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Do your best to live in peace with everybody, and look for holiness—if you don't have this you won't see the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> Make sure that none of you lack God's grace, in case some cause of bitterness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 29:18.">d</a></sup>arises to give trouble and end up corrupting many of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> Make sure there's nobody who is sexually immoral, or irreligious, like Esau was. He sold his birthright for a single meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> You remember that even when he wanted to receive the blessing later on he was refused. Even though he really tried, and cried hard, Esau couldn't change what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> You haven't arrived at a physical mountain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Clearly the reference in context is to Mount Sinai.">e</a></sup>that can be touched, that burned with fire, to a stormy place of black darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> where the sound of a trumpet and a voice speaking was heard—and those who heard the voice begged never to hear it speak to them again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> For they couldn't take what they were told to do, such as, “Even if a farm animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned to death.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 19:12-13">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> The sight was so terrifying that Moses himself said, “I'm so scared I'm shaking!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 9:19.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> But you have arrived at Mount Zion, the city of the living God, heavenly Jerusalem, with its thousands and thousands of angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> You have come to the church of the firstborn whose names are written down in heaven, to God, the judge of everyone, and to those good people whose lives are complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> You have come to Jesus, who shares with us the new agreed relationship, to the sprinkled blood that means more than that of Abel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Probably meaning that Jesus shed his blood in a spirit of forgiveness, while in the context of the first murder God references Abel's blood as calling out for vengeance.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> Make sure you don't reject the one who is speaking! If they didn't escape when they rejected God on earth, it is even more certain that we won't escape if we turn away from God who warns us from heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> Back then God's voice shook the earth, but his promise is now: “One more time I'm going to shake not only the earth, but heaven too.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Quoting Haggai 2:6.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> This expression, “one more time,” indicates that all creation that is shaken is removed so that everything that is not shaken may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Since we're receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us have a gracious attitude, so we can serve God in a way that pleases him, with reverence and respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> For “our God is a fire that consumes.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 4:24.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
