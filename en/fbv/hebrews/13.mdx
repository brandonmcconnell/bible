<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 Peter 3:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> May love for each other as brothers and sisters always continue!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:35; Genesis 18:1–3; 1 Peter 4:9"> Don't forget to show love for strangers too, because by doing so some have welcomed angels without knowing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:36; Hebrews 10:34; Romans 12:15"> Remember those who are locked up in prison as if you were imprisoned with them. Remember those who are mistreated as if you were physically suffering with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Proverbs 5:15–23; Galatians 5:19"> Everyone should honor marriage. Husbands and wives should be faithful to each other.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “the bed undefiled.”">a</a></sup>God will judge adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Don't love money; be content with what you have. God himself has said, “I'll never let you down; I'll never give up on you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 31:6-8; Joshua 1:5.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 118:6–9; Isaiah 41:10; Romans 8:31"> That's why we can confidently say, “The Lord is the one who helps me, so I won't be afraid. What can anyone do to me?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:6.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Remember your leaders who explained God's word to you. Look again at the results of their lives, and imitate their trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Don't get distracted by different kinds of strange teachings. It's good for the mind to be convinced by grace, not by laws concerning what we eat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word here is simply “food,” but the following context indicates that it refers to the ceremonial law and what kinds of food were permissible.">d</a></sup>Those who followed such laws didn't gain anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> We have an altar that those priests of the Tabernacle have no right to eat from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> The carcasses of the animals, whose blood is carried by the high priest into the most holy place as an offering for sin, are burned outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> In the same way Jesus also died outside the city gate so that he could make God's people holy through his own blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> So let us go out to him, outside the camp, experiencing and sharing in his shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> For we don't have a permanent city to live in here; we're looking for the home that is still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 107:21–22; PSALMS 50:14; Romans 12:1"> So let us through Jesus always offer a sacrifice of praise to God—this means speaking well of God, declaring his character.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “name,” which frequently refers to the nature and character of the person described. This is found in such English expressions as “having a good name,” i.e. character.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> And don't forget to do what is good, and to share with others what you have, for God is pleased when you make such sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Follow your leaders, and do what they tell you, for they are watching out for you and are accountable. Act in such a way that they can do this happily—and not with sadness, for that wouldn't help you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Please pray for us. We're convinced we have acted in good conscience, always wanting to do what's good and right in every situation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> I really want you to pray hard so that I can come back to see you soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; PSALMS 23:1"> Now may the God of peace who raised our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, from the dead, with the blood of an eternal agreement—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> may he provide you with all that is good so you can do his will. May he work in us, doing everything that pleases him, through Jesus Christ—glory to him forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> I want to encourage you, brothers and sisters, to pay attention to what I've written to you in this short letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> You should know that Timothy has been set free. If he gets here soon, I will come with him to see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Give my greetings to all your leaders, and to all the believers there. The believers here in Italy send their greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; 2 Timothy 4:22; Titus 3:15"> May God's grace be with all of you. Amen.</span>
