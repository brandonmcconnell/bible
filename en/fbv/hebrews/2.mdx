<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> So we should pay even closer attention to what we've learned so we don't drift away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> If the message the angels brought was trustworthy, and every sin and disobedient act brings its own consequence,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “receives its reward.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> how will we escape if we don't pay attention to this great salvation that the Lord first announced, and then we had confirmed to us by those who heard him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> God also testified through signs and miracles, by acts that show his power, and by the gifts of the Holy Spirit, given as he chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> It's not angels who will be in charge of the world to come that we're talking about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 8:4–8; PSALMS 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But as it's been said, “What are human beings that you should worry about them? What is a son of man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Son of man”: in normal use means just a human being; however Jesus applied this generic term to himself.">b</a></sup>that you should care about him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> You set him a little lower than the angels; you crowned him with glory and honor, and placed him over all your creation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Instead of just referring to humanity, this can also refer to Jesus: “You made him less than the angels for a while, and then you crowned him with glory and honor.” The whole passage can be seen in this dual way, referring to Jesus as the son of man, both representative and Savior of humanity.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; PSALMS 8:6"> You gave him authority over everything.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Again this can apply to humanity, God giving authority over the creatures as mentioned in Genesis 1, or it can apply to the authority of Jesus as Lord.">d</a></sup>Nothing was left out when God gave him authority over everything. However, we see that not everything is subject to his authority yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see Jesus, for a little while placed a little lower than the angels, through the suffering of death crowned with glory and honor. Through God's grace Jesus experienced death for everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> It was appropriate that God, who creates and maintains everything, should bring many of his children to glory, and to completely prepare through suffering the one who leads them to salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both the one who makes people holy and those who are made holy belong to the same family.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “all from one.”">e</a></sup>That's why he doesn't hesitate to call them “brothers,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 22:22; PSALMS 40:10; John 18:20"> saying “I will announce your name to my brothers; I will praise you among your people when they meet together.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Meet together”: the word is “ecclesia” which eventually came to mean “church.” The quotation is from Psalms 22:22.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; PSALMS 91:2"> Also: “I will place my trust in him,” and “Here I am, together with the children God has given to me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 8:17-18.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Because the children share flesh and blood in common, he shared in this in the same way so that through death he could destroy the one that had the power of death—the devil—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and free everyone who through fear of death were enslaved all their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> Of course it's not angels he's concerned about; he's concerned to help the children of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> That's why it was necessary for him to become like his brothers in everything, so that he could become a merciful and trustworthy high priest in the things of God, to forgive his people's sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> Because he himself suffered when he was tempted, he is able to help those who are tempted.</span>
