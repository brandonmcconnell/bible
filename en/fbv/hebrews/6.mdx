<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> So let's not get stuck on the basic teachings about Christ, but let's progress to a more mature understanding. We don't need to go over again the ideas of repenting from what we used to do, about trusting in God—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> or teachings about baptism, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> So let's get on with it, as God gives us the opportunity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> It's impossible for those who once understood and experienced God's heavenly gift—who shared in receiving the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> who had known God's good word and the power of the coming age—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and then completely abandon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Completely abandon.” The word in the Greek text is only used once in the New Testament and means to totally renounce and repudiate a belief. It is not the usual word for apostasy.">a</a></sup>God, for them to be brought back to repentance once more. They themselves have crucified the Son of God all over again and publicly humiliated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; PSALMS 65:9–13"> Land that has been watered by rain, and produces crops for those who farm it, has God's blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> But land that only produces weeds and thorns is worthless, and is about to be condemned. In the end all that can be done is to burn it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But dear friends, we believe better of you and your salvation, even if we talk like this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> God wouldn't be so unjust as to forget what you've done and the love you've shown for him by the care you've shown for fellow-believers—something you're still doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> We want each of you to show the same kind of commitment, confident in God's hope until it's fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> Don't be spiritually lazy, but follow the example of those who through trusting in God and patience inherit what God has promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> When God gave his promise to Abraham he could swear by no one greater so he took an oath on himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “I will definitely bless you, and multiply your descendants.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 22:17.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so, after patiently waiting, Abraham received the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> People swear on things that are greater than they are, and when they have some dispute the oath is taken as the final word on the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> That's why God wanted to demonstrate more clearly to those who would inherit the promise that he would never ever change his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> So by these two actions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the promise and the oath.">c</a></sup>that can't be changed, and since it's impossible for God to lie, we can have total confidence, having run for safety to take hold of the hope God presented to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> This hope is our spiritual anchor—it's both certain and reliable, and it takes us past the curtain to the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> That's where Jesus went in on our behalf, because he had become a high priest according to the order of Melchizedek.</span>
