<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> God, who in the past spoke to our forefathers through the prophets at various times and in many ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> has in these last days spoken to us through his Son. God appointed the Son heir of everything, and made the universe through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> The Son is the radiant glory of God, and the visible expression of his true character. He sustains everything by his powerful command. When he had provided cleansing for sin he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> He was placed much higher than the angels since he received a greater name than them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> God never said to any angel, “You are my Son; today I have become your Father,” or “I will be a Father to him, and he will be a Son to me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrews is full of Old Testament quotations and allusions, some of which are not quoted exactly or are given in summary form. It is therefore difficult at times to identify the exact source or sources. Old Testament quotes that are relatively clear will be identified here. Note that they are taken from the Septuagint, the Greek translation of the Hebrew Scriptures. The quotations referenced in this verse appear to be: Psalms 2:7, 2 Samuel 7:14, and 1 Chronicles 17:13.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> Also, when he brought his firstborn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Firstborn”: this term is not to be taken that there was a time when Jesus did not exist; it is used to identify rank rather than chronology.">b</a></sup>Son into the world, he said, “Let all God's angels worship him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 32:43from the Septuagint.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> Regarding the angels, he says, “He makes his angels winds, and his servants flames of fire,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 104:4.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> but about the Son he says, “Your throne, God, lasts forever and ever, and justice is the ruling scepter of your kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 45:7; PSALMS 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> You love what is right, and hate what is lawless. That is why God, your God, has placed you above everyone else by anointing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The ancient practice of pouring oil over the head of a person was to indicate they were being chosen for a specific position, a high honor.">e</a></sup>you with the oil of joy.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 45:6-7.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> “You, Lord, laid the foundations of the earth in the beginning. The heavens are the product of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> They will come to an end, but you will continue. They will wear out like clothes do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> and you will roll them up like a cloak. Like clothes, they will be changed, but you never change, and your life never ends.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “your years never fail.” The quotation is from Psalms 102:25-27.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> But he never said to any angel, “Sit at my right hand until I place your enemies in subjection under your feet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 110:1.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; PSALMS 103:20–21; PSALMS 91:11–12"> What are the angels? They are beings that serve, sent out to help those who will receive salvation.</span>
