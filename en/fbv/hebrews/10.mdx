<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> The law is just a shadow of the good that was to come, and not the actual reality. So it can never through the repeated sacrifices offered year after year make right those who come to worship God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; PSALMS 103:12"> Otherwise wouldn't the sacrifices have stopped being offered? If the worshipers had been made clean once and for all, they wouldn't have had guilty consciences any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in fact those sacrifices remind people of sins year after year,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> because it's impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to remove sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> That's why when Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The original simply says “he.” Christ is inferred from 9:24, 9:28.">a</a></sup>came into the world he said, “You didn't want sacrifices or offerings, but you did prepare a body for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; PSALMS 147:11"> Burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin gave you no pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> Then I said, ‘God, see I've come to do what you want me to do, just as it says about me in the book.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Actually “the heading of a scroll,” meaning the Scriptures. The quoatation is from Psalms 40:6-8.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> As mentioned above, “You didn't want sacrifices or offerings, and burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin gave you no pleasure,” (even though they are offered in accordance with the law's requirements).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> Then he said, “See, I've come to do what you want.” He gets rid of the first agreement so he can set up the second,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> through which we all are made holy through Jesus Christ offering his body once and for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> Every priest officiates in the services day after day, again and again offering the same sacrifices that can never remove sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But this Priest, after he had offered for sins a single sacrifice that lasts forever, sat down at God's right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; PSALMS 110:1; Luke 20:43"> Now he waits until all his enemies are conquered, becoming like a footstool for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by a single sacrifice he has set right forever those who are being made holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> As the Holy Spirit also tells us, for having said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> “This is the agreement that I will make with them later on, says the Lord. I will put my laws inside them, and I will write them in their minds.” Then he adds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> “I won't remember their sins and lawlessness anymore.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Jeremiah 31:33-34.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> Once free from such things, sin offerings are no longer needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Now we have the confidence, brothers and sisters, to enter the Most Holy Place by the blood of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> Through his life and death,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“His life and death”: Literally, “his body.”">d</a></sup>he opened up a brand-new, living way through the veil to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> Since we have such a great priest placed in charge over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us come close to God with sincere minds, totally trusting in him. Our minds have been sprinkled to purify them from our evil way of thinking, and our bodies have been washed clean by pure water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> So let us hold onto the hope we're telling others about, never hesitating, for God who promised is trustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> Let's think about how we can motivate one another to love and to do what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> We should not give up meeting together, as some have done. In fact we should be encouraging one another, especially as you see the End<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “the Day.”">e</a></sup>approaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For if we deliberately go on sinning after we've understood the truth, there's no longer any sacrifice for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> All that's left is fear, expecting the impending judgment and the terrible fire that destroys those who are hostile to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Someone who rejects the law of Moses is put to death without mercy on the evidence of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> How much worse do you think someone will deserve their punishment if they have trampled underfoot the Son of God, if they have disregarded the blood that sealed the agreement and makes us holy, treating it as ordinary and trivial, and have abused the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> We know God, and he said, “I will make sure that justice is done; I will give people what they deserve.” He also said, “The Lord will judge his people.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 32:35-36; Psalms 135:14.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It's terrifying to come under the power of the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> Just remember the past when, after you understood the truth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “were enlightened.”">g</a></sup>you experienced a great deal of suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> Sometimes you were made a spectacle of, being insulted and attacked; at other times you stood in solidarity with those who were suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> You showed your sympathy for those in prison, and took it cheerfully when your possessions were confiscated, knowing that you have something better coming that will truly last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> So don't lose your confidence in God—it will be richly rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; PSALMS 37:7; James 5:7–11"> You need to be patient so that having done what God wants, you'll receive what he has promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> “In just a little while he will come, as he said—he won't delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> Those who do what is right will live by trusting in God, and if they draw back from their commitment, I won't be pleased with them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This is a rather free reference to Isaiah 26:20and Habakkuk 2:3-4. Clearly the one promising to return is viewed in this context as Jesus.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we're not the kind of people who draw back and end up being lost. We are those who trust in God to save us.</span>
</div>
