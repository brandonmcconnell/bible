<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> Now our trust in God is the guarantee of what we hope for, the evidence of what we can't see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Hebrews 11:4"> People who lived long ago trusted God and this is what gained them God's approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 33:6; 2 Peter 3:5; John 1:3"> Through our trust in God we understand that the whole universe was created by God's command, that what can be seen was made out of what cannot be seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12"> By trusting him Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain, and as a result God identified him as someone who lived right. God showed this by accepting his offering. Even though Abel has been dead for a long time, he still speaks to us through what he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; Jude 1:14; Hebrews 11:6"> By trusting God Enoch was taken to heaven so he didn't experience death. He couldn't be found on earth because God took him to heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Genesis 5:24.">a</a></sup>Before this happened he was known as someone God was pleased with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; Matthew 6:33"> You can't expect God to be pleased with you if you don't trust him! Anyone who comes to God must believe that God exists, and that he rewards those who are searching for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; 1 Peter 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> Noah trusted God, and was warned by him about things that had never happened before. Because Noah paid serious attention to what God said, he constructed an ark to save his family. By trusting God, Noah showed that the world was wrong, and received the reward of being set right by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4; Matthew 7:24–25"> Through trusting God Abraham obeyed when God called him to go to the land God was going to give him. He left, not knowing where he was going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 13:18; Genesis 13:3"> Trusting in God he lived in the promised land—but as a foreigner, living in tents, together with Isaac and Jacob who shared with him in inheriting the same promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 12:22"> For Abraham was looking forward to a city built on foundations that last, with God as its builder and maker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Hebrews 10:23; Genesis 18:11–14"> By her trust in God even Sarah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some versions say Abraham.">b</a></sup>herself was given the ability to conceive a child though she was too old, because she trusted God who had made the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> That is why the descendants of Abraham (who was as good as dead!) became as numerous as the stars of heaven, as countless as the sand of the sea shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; PSALMS 39:12; Hebrews 11:27"> They all died still trusting in God. Though they didn't receive the things God promised, they were still looking for them as it were from a distance and welcomed them, acknowledging that on this earth they were foreigners, people just passing through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18–7; Hebrews 11:16; Hebrews 13:14"> People who say such things make it clear that they're looking for a country of their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> For if they cared about the country they'd left behind, they could have returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Hebrews 11:10; Philippians 3:20"> But they're looking for a better country instead, a heavenly country. That is why God isn't disappointed with them, and is happy to be called their God, for he has built a city for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; James 2:21–24"> Abraham trusted God when he was tested and offered Isaac to God. Abraham, who had accepted God's promises, was still ready to offer to God his only son,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Of course Isaac was not literally Abraham's only son; the term in Greek indicates primacy.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Romans 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> even though he'd been told, “It's through Isaac that your descendants will be counted.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Genesis 21:12.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 4:17–21; Matthew 9:28; Ephesians 3:20"> Abraham had thought it through and decided that God could bring Isaac back to life from the dead. In a sense that's what did happen—Abraham received Isaac back from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> Trusting in God, Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau in regard to what the future would bring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> Trusting in God, Jacob as he was dying blessed each of Joseph's sons, and worshiped God, leaning on his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> Trusting in God, Joseph too, when his own death approached, spoke about the exodus of the Israelites, and gave instructions about what to do with his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; Hebrews 13:6"> Trusting in God, Moses' parents hid him for three months after he was born. They recognized he was a special child. They weren't afraid to go against what the king had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; Acts 7:21–24"> Trusting in God, Moses, when he grew up, refused to be known as the adopted son of Pharaoh's daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 84:10; 2 Timothy 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> Instead he chose to share in the sufferings of God's people rather than to enjoy the temporary pleasures of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Hebrews 11:6; 1 Peter 4:14"> He counted the rejection he experienced from following Christ to be of far greater value than the wealth of Egypt—because he was focused on the reward to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:1"> Trusting in God, he left Egypt and wasn't scared of Pharaoh's anger—he kept going with his eyes fixed on the invisible God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; Hebrews 9:19; Hebrews 12:24"> Trusting in God he observed the Passover and the sprinkling of blood, so that the destroying angel would not touch the Israelites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Angel” and “Israelites” supplied by context.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; PSALMS 106:9–11; PSALMS 78:13"> Trusting in God the Israelites crossed the Red Sea as if it were dry land. When the Egyptians attempted to do the same they were drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> Trusting in God, the Israelites marched around the walls of Jericho for seven days, and the walls fell down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; Matthew 1:5"> Trusting in God, Rahab the prostitute didn't die with those who rejected God, because she had welcomed the Israelite spies in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> What other examples should I give? I don't have time to talk about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah; about David, Samuel, and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 144:1–2; Hebrews 6:12–15; Hebrews 10:36"> Because of their trust in God they conquered kingdoms, did what was good and right, received God's promises, shut the mouths of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> put out blazing fires, escaped being killed by the sword, were weak but made strong, did mighty deeds in war, and routed enemy armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 4:27–37; 1 Kings 17:22–24; Philippians 3:11"> Women were given their dead family members back through resurrection. Others were tortured, refusing to compromise and be pardoned, because they wanted to be part of a better resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:2; Genesis 39:20; Jeremiah 37:15–21"> Yet others suffered insults and whippings; they were put in chains and imprisoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21; Jeremiah 26:23; 1 Kings 19:10"> Some were stoned, cut in pieces, tempted, killed by the sword. Some dressed in sheepskins and goatskins: destitute, oppressed, and mistreated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Samuel 22:1; 1 Kings 19:9"> The world was not worthy to have such people who wandered in the deserts and mountains, living in caves and holes in the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:2"> All of these people, even though they had God's approval, didn't receive what God had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 6:11; Hebrews 8:6; Hebrews 10:11–14"> God has given us something even better, so that they can't be complete without us.</span>
