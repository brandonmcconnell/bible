<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> This letter is sent from Paul, prisoner of Jesus Christ, and from our brother Timothy, to Philemon, our good friend and co-worker;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> to our sister Apphia, to Archippus who fights alongside us, and to your house church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> May you have grace and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I always thank my God for you, remembering you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> because I hear of your trust in the Lord Jesus and your love for all believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> I pray that you will put the generous nature of your trust in God into action as you recognize all the good things we share in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> Your love, my dear brother, has brought me much happiness and encouragement. You have really inspired those of us who believe!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> That's why even though I'm brave enough in Christ to order you to do your duty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> I would rather ask you this favor out of love. Old Paul, now also a prisoner of Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> is appealing to you on behalf of Onesimus who became my adopted son during my imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> In the past he was of no use to you, but now he's useful to both you and me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> I send him to you with my fondest wishes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “heartfelt affections.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> I'd have preferred to keep him here with me so that he could have helped me as you would have done while I am in chains for telling the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But I decided not to do anything without your permission. I didn't want you to be forced to do good, but to do so willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> Maybe you lost him for a while so that you could have him back forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> He's not a servant anymore, because he's more than a servant. He's a specially-loved brother, particularly to me, and even more to you, both as a man and as a fellow-believer in the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “in the flesh and in the Lord.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> So if you consider me as a colleague working together with you for the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“A colleague working together with you for the Lord.” The word in Greek is “partner,” but this needs explaining because of modern usage.">c</a></sup>welcome him as if you were welcoming me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> If he has done you any wrong, or owes you anything, charge it to my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I Paul am signing this with my own hand: I will repay you. Of course I won't mention what you owe me, including your very self!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, I'm expecting this favor from you in the Lord; please make me happy in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> I'm writing about this to you because I'm convinced you'll do as I ask—in fact I know you'll do even more than I've asked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> In the meantime please have a room ready for me, for I hope to be able to return to see you soon in answer to your prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras who is here in prison with me sends his greetings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> as do my co-workers Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.</span>
</div>
