<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 4:5; John 14:21–24; Deuteronomy 6:6–9"> My son, don't forget my instructions. Always keep my commands in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 4:10; PSALMS 91:16"> Then you will live a long time, and have a full<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word used here is “shalom,” meaning peace and prosperity.">a</a></sup>life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 7:3; 2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16"> Hold on to kindness and truth. Tie them around your neck; write them in your mind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Write them in your mind”: literally, “write them on the tablet of your heart.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:52; 1 Samuel 2:26; Romans 14:18"> That way you'll gain a good reputation and be appreciated by both God and people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:5; Jeremiah 17:7–8; Proverbs 28:26"> Put your trust totally in the Lord—don't rely on what you think you know!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; PSALMS 32:8; Philippians 4:6"> Remember him in everything you do, and he'll show you the right way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Romans 12:16; Proverbs 26:12"> Don't think you're wise—respect God and avoid evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; PSALMS 147:3; Proverbs 16:24"> Then you will be healed and made strong.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally this verse is, “Healing shall be to your navel, and drink for your bones.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2–15; 1 Corinthians 16:2; Exodus 23:19"> Honor the Lord with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all the crops you grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 22:9"> Then your barns will be filled with produce, and your vats will overflow with new wine.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“New wine”: in other words, grape juice.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:17; Revelation 3:19; Hebrews 12:5–12"> My son, don't reject the Lord's discipline or resent it when he corrects you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:5; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 29:17"> for the Lord corrects those he loves, as a father corrects a son who pleases him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Septuagint reads, “he punishes a son who pleases him.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–35; Proverbs 4:5–9; Ecclesiastes 9:15–18"> Happy are those who find wisdom and gain understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:19; Job 28:13–19"> for wisdom is worth more than silver, and pays better than gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Job 28:18; PSALMS 73:25–26"> She is more valuable than rubies—everything you could ever want just doesn't compare!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; 2 Corinthians 6:10; Proverbs 22:4"> She offers long life in one hand, and riches and honor in the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Isaiah 26:3; Philippians 4:8–9"> She brings true happiness, and leads to peaceful prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:30; Revelation 2:7; Proverbs 15:4"> Wisdom is a tree of life to everyone who embraces her, blessing those who accept her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 104:24; Jeremiah 10:12; John 1:3"> It was through wisdom that the Lord created the earth, and through understanding he set the heavens in place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; PSALMS 65:9–12; Job 36:27–28"> It was through his knowledge the waters of the deep were broken open, and the clouds sent down the dew.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This probably refers to the creation account in Genesis.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Deuteronomy 32:46–47"> My son, hold on to good judgment and wise decisions—don't let them out of your sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Proverbs 1:9; Isaiah 38:16"> for they will be life to you, and an ornament for your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Proverbs 10:9; PSALMS 121:3"> You will be able to walk confidently on your way, and you won't trip up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 3:5; PSALMS 4:8; Proverbs 6:22"> When you rest, you won't be afraid; when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; PSALMS 112:7; 1 Peter 3:14"> You won't be afraid of a sudden panic, or of disasters that hit the wicked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 91:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; Proverbs 14:26"> for the Lord will be the one you can trust in, and he will prevent you being caught in a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; James 2:15–16; Romans 13:7"> Don't hold back good from those who deserve it when it's something you have the power to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:12–15; 2 Corinthians 8:11"> Don't tell your neighbor, “Go away. Come back tomorrow and then I'll give it to you,” when you've already got it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 6:14; Proverbs 16:29–30; PSALMS 55:20"> Don't plan to harm your neighbor who lives nearby and trusts you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24; Proverbs 29:22; Romans 12:18–21"> Don't quarrel with anybody for no reason, when they haven't done anything to hurt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 24:1; PSALMS 37:1; Galatians 5:21"> Don't be jealous of violent people—don't choose to follow their example!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 25:14; John 15:15; Proverbs 11:20"> For the Lord hates deceitful people, but he is a friend to those who do what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 37:22; Leviticus 26:14–46; Job 8:6–7"> The houses of the wicked are cursed by the Lord, but he blesses the homes of those who live right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 4:6; 1 Peter 5:5; PSALMS 138:6"> He mocks those who mock, but he is kind to the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 132:18; Isaiah 65:13–15; PSALMS 73:24"> The wise will receive honor, but fools are held up in disgrace.</span>
