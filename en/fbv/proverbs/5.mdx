<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:17; Proverbs 4:20; Proverbs 2:1"> My son, pay attention to my wisdom, listen carefully as I explain what makes sense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23; Proverbs 15:7; Malachi 2:6–7"> so you can make good decisions and protect knowledge with your lips.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Protect knowledge in what you say”: literally, “your lips will guard knowledge.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 7:5"> For the lips of an immoral woman may taste as sweet as honey, the kisses of her mouth may be as smooth as oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 55:21"> but in the end what you get from her is the bitterness of wormwood and the sharp pain of being cut with a two-edged sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19"> She leads you down to death; she takes you down to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26; Proverbs 5:21; PSALMS 16:11"> She doesn't follow the path that leads to life—she wanders away and doesn't even know she's lost.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> Now, my son,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew has the plural “sons” here, but for the rest of the chapter uses singular pronouns, so “son” is used here, following the Septuagint and the Vulgate.">b</a></sup>listen to me; don't reject what I have taught you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 6:13"> Stay far away from her! Don't go near the door of her house!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Hosea 4:13–14; Genesis 38:23–26"> Otherwise you'll surrender your honor to others, and your character to cruel people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Proverbs 6:35; Proverbs 31:3"> Strangers will spend your wealth; everything you've worked for will go to someone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Revelation 22:15; Hebrews 13:4"> When you come to the end of your life you'll moan in pain as disease destroys your body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> You'll say, “How I used to hate discipline, and my mind rebelled against being corrected!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> I didn't listen to what my teacher said. I didn't pay attention to my instructors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Peter 2:10–18; 1 Corinthians 10:6–8"> Now I'm on the verge of being completely disgraced before everyone in the community.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2–5; Hebrews 13:4; Proverbs 5:18–19"> Drink water from your own cistern, flowing water from your own well.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This whole section uses imagery to make the point that husbands and wives should be faithful to each other.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 128:3; PSALMS 127:3; Isaiah 48:21"> Why should your springs be spilled outside, your streams of water poured out in the streets?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Keep them for yourselves alone. They're not for you to share with strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Malachi 2:14–15; Song of Songs 4:15"> May your spring of water be blessed, and may you enjoy the wife you married when you were young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:3; Song of Songs 8:14; Song of Songs 4:5"> May she be to you a loving deer, a graceful doe. May her breasts always be intoxicating to you; may you be drunk on her love forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 7:5; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 23:33"> Why, my son, become intoxicated with an immoral woman? Why embrace the breasts of a woman who acts like a prostitute?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Proverbs 15:3; Job 34:21"> For the Lord sees everything people do, the Lord investigates everywhere they go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:5; PSALMS 9:15; Proverbs 1:18"> The wicked are trapped by their evil actions; the cords of their sins tie them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 36:12; Job 4:21; Proverbs 10:21"> They will die because they lack self-control, lost because of their great stupidity.</span>
