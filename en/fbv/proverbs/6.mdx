<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:26; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 20:16"> My son, suppose you have pledged to guarantee your neighbor's debt, or you have shaken hands in agreement with a stranger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> then you've trapped yourself by what you promised, you've been caught by what you said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:10; PSALMS 31:8; 2 Samuel 24:14"> So this is what you have to do. You need to get out of it, because you've put yourself in that person's power. Go to your neighbor in all humility and ask him to release you from the commitment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“To release you from the commitment”: implied.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Don't delay, saying that you'll sleep on it. Don't rest until you've done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 91:3; PSALMS 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> Get out of it like a gazelle escaping from a trap, like a bird from a bird-catcher's net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go and observe the ants, you slacker! Learn from what they do and become wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> They don't have a leader, an officer, or a ruler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> yet they work hard during the summer getting their food, gathering what they need during the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremiah 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> So how long are you going to lie there, you slacker? When are you going to get up from your sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> You may say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“You may say”: implied.">b</a></sup>“Please, just a little more sleep, a little longer snooze, a little more folding of the arms to rest”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> and poverty will attack you like a robber, destitution like an armed warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> Rebellious and wicked people go around telling lies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> winking slyly, giving sneaky foot gestures, rudely pointing with their fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> Their warped minds plot evil schemes; they're always causing trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> As a result, disaster falls suddenly on such people; in just a moment they're destroyed without hope of healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> There are six things that the Lord hates, seven things that he absolutely detests:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; PSALMS 101:5"> arrogant eyes, a lying tongue, hands that murder the innocent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> a mind that plots evil schemes, feet that hurriedly run to do wrong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> a false witness that speaks lies, those who caused arguments in families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, pay attention to your father's instruction, and don't reject your mother's teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Keep them always in mind. Tie them around your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; PSALMS 119:24"> They will lead you as you walk along; they will watch over you as you sleep; when you wake up they will talk with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; PSALMS 19:8"> For the instruction is a lamp, and the teaching is a light. The correction that comes from discipline is the way to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> It will protect you from an evil woman and the seductive words of a prostitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Don't let your mind lust after her beauty, or let her hypnotize you with her fluttering eyelashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> You can buy a prostitute for the price of a loaf of bread, but adultery with another man's wife can cost you your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Can you put fire in your lap and not burn your clothes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Can you walk on hot coals and not scorch your feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> It's the same for anyone who sleeps with another man's wife. No one who touches her will remain unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> People don't condemn a thief if he steals to satisfy himself when he's hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> But if he's caught, he has to pay back seven times what he stole, even if it means handing over everything he has in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> Any man who commits adultery with a woman has no sense; he who does so destroys himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; PSALMS 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> He will be wounded and dishonored. His disgrace will never be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Songs 8:6"> For jealousy makes a husband furious, and he won't hold back when he takes revenge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:25; Malachi 2:9; Proverbs 7:13"> He refuses any compensation, and won't be paid off, however big the amount.</span>
