<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Don't be jealous of evil people; don't long to spend time with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 7:14; PSALMS 28:3"> for they think up cruel plans and discuss how to cause trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> A house is built by wisdom; its foundation is made secure through understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> Its rooms are filled by knowledge with all kinds of valuable and beautiful objects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> If you have wisdom, you are strong; if you have knowledge, your power increases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> for with wise guidance you can go to war; you will be victorious by having many good advisors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; PSALMS 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom goes over the heads of stupid people; they have nothing to contribute when important matters are discussed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “he does not open his mouth in the gate.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> Anyone who plans to do evil will be seen as a troublemaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> Plans thought up by stupid people are sinful. Everyone hates those who are scornful of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> If you give up in times of trouble, it shows how weak you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Rescue those who are being led away to be executed; save those who are stumbling on their way to be slaughtered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If you say, “Look, we didn't know anything about this,” don't you think the God who judges your motives will see what's happening? He who watches over you knows, and he will pay people back for what they've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Songs 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> My son, eating honey is good for you; the honeycomb tastes sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> In the same way, you should know that wisdom is good for you; if you find it, there will be a future for you, and your hope will not be crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Don't be like a criminal waiting to ambush the home of good people; don't attack the place where they live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; PSALMS 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> Those who do right may fall down seven times, and still get up again; but the wicked are brought down by disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Don't celebrate when your enemies fall; don't be glad when they trip up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> otherwise when the Lord sees it, he'll be unhappy with you and won't punish your enemies as he planned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 119:115; PSALMS 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Don't get all upset over the wicked, or be jealous of those who do wrong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; PSALMS 11:6"> for evil people have no future—the lamp of the wicked will be snuffed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, honor the Lord and the king, and don't join those who are rebellious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> for disaster will suddenly fall on them. Who knows how the Lord and king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“The Lord and king”: literally “they both,” referring back to the previous verse.">b</a></sup>will punish them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These are more sayings of the wise: Showing favoritism when passing judgment is wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> Those who tell the guilty, “You're innocent,” will be cursed by the people and hated by the nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> while those who convict the guilty will be appreciated, and will receive a rich blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> An honest answer is a kiss on the lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Do the work you need to do outside first, then prepare and sow your fields, and only after that start building your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Don't testify against your neighbors without having a good reason, and don't tell lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Don't say to yourself, “I'm going to do to him what he did to me! I'll pay him back for what he's done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I walked past the field of a lazy man, past a vineyard of someone with no sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> It was all overgrown with thorns, the ground was covered with weeds, and the stone wall had fallen down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> As I looked I thought about it, and what I saw taught me a lesson:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> You may say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“You may say”: implied.">c</a></sup>“Please, just a little more sleep, a little longer snooze, a little more folding of the arms to rest”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> and poverty will attack you like a robber, destitution like an armed warrior.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Verses 33 and 34 are the same as 6:10-11.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
