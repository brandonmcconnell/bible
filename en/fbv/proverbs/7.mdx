<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 21:27; Leviticus 7:11; Proverbs 17:1"> “I sacrificed my peace offering today, and paid my vows.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="These offerings were shared with the donor. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:9; Ezekiel 27:7; 1 Kings 10:28"> My bed is made, covered with colorful linens from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; Isaiah 57:7–9; Song of Songs 4:13–14"> I've sprinkled perfume on my bed—myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.</span>
