<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When you sit down for a meal with a ruler, look carefully at what is placed before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> and cut down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “put a knife to your throat.”">a</a></sup>if you have a big appetite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; PSALMS 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Don't be greedy for his fancy food, for it's offered with deceptive motives.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “for it is the bread of lies.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Don't wear yourself out trying to get rich—be wise enough not to bother!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> It disappears in the blink of an eye, suddenly growing wings and flying off into the sky like an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Don't accept meal invitations from miserly people; don't be greedy for their fancy food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2; PSALMS 55:21; Judges 16:15"> for what they're thinking inside is what they really are. They say, “Come on, eat and drink!”—but in their minds they don't really care about you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “his heart is not with you.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> You'll vomit up the little pieces you've eaten, and your kind words of appreciation will be wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Don't talk to stupid people because they'll ridicule your wise words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Don't move ancient boundary markers, and don't encroach on fields belonging to orphans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> for their Protector<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word used here is the kinsman-redeemer who had the responsibility to marry a widowed relative and protect the family. See Leviticus 25and Numbers 35. In this case it is applied to God.">d</a></sup>is powerful and he will plead their case against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Focus your mind on instruction; listen intently to words of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Don't keep from disciplining your children—a beating won't kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> If you use physical correction you can save them from death.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Death” literally, “Sheol,” the place of the dead, the grave.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, if you think wisely then I'll be happy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> I'll be delighted when you say what's right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Don't think enviously of sinners, but always remember to honor the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; PSALMS 9:18"> for there's definitely a future for you, and your hope will not be crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Pay attention, my son, and be wise; make sure your mind concentrates on following the right way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Don't join in with people who drink too much wine or who stuff themselves with meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For people who get drunk and overeat lose all they've got, and they spend so much time dozing that all they have left to wear is rags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Pay attention to your father, and don't disregard your mother when she's old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; PSALMS 119:72"> Invest in truth—and don't sell it! Invest in wisdom, instruction, and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> Children who do right make their fathers very happy; a wise son brings joy to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Make your father and mother happy; bring joy to her who gave birth to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 119:2; PSALMS 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> My son, give me your undivided attention, and cheerfully follow my example.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> A prostitute is like being trapped in a pit; an immoral woman is like getting stuck a narrow well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> Like a robber, she lies in wait ready to ambush men and make more of them unfaithful to their wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who's in trouble? Who's in pain? Who's arguing? Who's complaining? Who's injured for no reason? Who's got bloodshot eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> —those who spend a long time drinking wine, those who are always trying some new cocktail.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Cocktail”: literally “mixed drink.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; PSALMS 119:37"> Don't let the look of wine tempt you—how red it is, how it sparkles in the cup, how smooth it feels as it goes down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> In the end it bites like a serpent, it stings like a snake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; PSALMS 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> You'll hallucinate, seeing strange things, and your confused mind will make you say all kinds of craziness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> You'll stumble around like you're on the rolling ocean, you'll be tossed about like someone lying down at the top of a ship's mast, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> “People punched me, but it didn't hurt me; they beat me, but I didn't feel a thing. I've got to get up because I need another drink.”</span>
