<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 16:18; Revelation 3:12; 1 Kings 7:2–3"> Wisdom has built her house; she has prepared its seven pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 22:3–14; Isaiah 25:6; Luke 14:16–17"> She has slaughtered her animals for meat; she has mixed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Through the addition of spices.">a</a></sup>her wine; and she has set her table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 8:1–3; Matthew 22:3–4"> She has sent out her servant girls with invitations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“With invitations”: implied.">b</a></sup>She calls out from the highest places of the town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 9:16; Proverbs 8:5"> “Everybody who needs to learn, come and see me!” To people who don't have any sense she says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 6:27; Song of Songs 5:1; Proverbs 9:17"> “Come, eat my food, and drink the wine I have mixed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Proverbs 4:11; Luke 13:24"> Leave your foolish ways and you will live; follow the path that makes sense.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:12; Proverbs 23:9; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> If you correct a mocker all you get are insults; if you argue with the wicked all you get is abuse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:5; Proverbs 13:18; Proverbs 23:9"> So don't argue with mockers or they'll only hate you; argue with the wise and they'll love you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; Matthew 13:11–12; 1 John 5:13"> Educate the wise and they'll become even wiser; teach those who live right and they will increase their learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 111:10; Job 28:28; Proverbs 1:7"> Honoring the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; knowledge of the Holy One brings insight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Proverbs 3:16; Proverbs 3:2"> Through wisdom you'll have many more days, increasing the years of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ezekiel 18:20"> If you are wise, you are the one to profit from it; if you scoff, you alone will have to suffer the consequences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 7:11; Proverbs 5:6; 1 Timothy 6:4"> Stupidity is like a loud, ignorant woman who doesn't know anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Proverbs 7:10–12"> She sits at the door of her house, on a seat in the high places of the town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25–27; Proverbs 23:27–28; Proverbs 7:13–15"> calling out to those passing by, going about their business,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 9:4"> “Everybody who needs to learn, come and see me!” To people who don't have any sense she says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Proverbs 23:31–32; James 1:14–15"> “Stolen water is sweet, and food eaten in secret tastes good!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> But they don't know that the dead are there, that those she's invited are in the depths of the grave.</span>
