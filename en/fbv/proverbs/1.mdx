<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> The proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> They are for achieving wisdom and instruction, and to recognize sayings that bring insight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> They provide education in what makes sense, living right, judging correctly, and acting fairly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; PSALMS 119:9"> They give discernment to the immature, knowledge and discretion to the young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Wise people will listen and gain in learning, and those who have good judgment will gain skills in guidance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 78:2; Mark 4:11; PSALMS 49:4"> understanding the proverbs and puzzles, the sayings and questions of the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; PSALMS 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> Honoring the Lord is where true knowledge begins, but fools treat wisdom and good advice with contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> My son, pay attention to your father's instruction, and don't reject your mother's teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> They are a wreath of grace to decorate your head; they are pendants for your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; PSALMS 1:1"> My son, if evil people try to tempt you, don't give into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> They may tell you, “Come with us. Let's go and hide, ready to kill someone. Let's ambush someone for fun!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 124:3; PSALMS 28:1; Romans 3:13"> Let's bury them alive; let's put them in the grave while they're still healthy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We'll take from them all kinds of valuable things and fill our homes with the stuff we steal!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Come and join us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “throw in your lot with us.”">a</a></sup>and we'll all share what we get!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 119:101; PSALMS 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son, don't follow their ways. Don't go in that direction with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For they rush to do evil; they hurry to commit violence and murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> There's no point in trying to lure birds into a net when they can see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> However, these evil people hide ready to kill others, but they themselves are the victims. They're only ambushing themselves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> This is what happens to you if you try to become rich through crime—it kills you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom calls out in the streets; she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“She”: in Proverbs, wisdom is personified as a woman.">b</a></sup>shouts aloud in the squares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> She cries out at busy corners; she explains her message at the town gates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; PSALMS 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> “How long will you stupid people love stupidity? How long will scornful people enjoy their mocking? How long will fools hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Pay attention to my warnings, and I'll pour out my deepest thoughts to you—I'll explain what I know to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> For I have called you, but you refused to listen; I reached out my hand to you, but you didn't care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> You ignored everything I said; you wouldn't accept any of my warnings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; PSALMS 37:13"> So I'll laugh at you when you're in trouble; I'll mock you when you're in a panic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; PSALMS 58:9"> When panic rains down on you like a storm, when trouble hits you like a whirlwind, when sorrow and pain come on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> then you'll call out to me for help, but I won't answer; you'll search hard for me, but won't find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Why?—because they hated knowledge, and they didn't choose to respect the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> They're not willing to accept my advice; they despise all my warnings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> So they'll have to eat the fruit of their own choices, bloated by their own devious schemes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> Stupid people are killed by their rebellion; foolish people are destroyed by their lack of concern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 25:12–13; PSALMS 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But everyone who listens to me will be kept safe, and will live without worrying about problems.”</span>
