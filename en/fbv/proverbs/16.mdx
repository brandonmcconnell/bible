<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Proverbs 16:9; Luke 21:14–15"> Human beings may make plans in their minds, but the final decision is the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 21:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; Luke 16:15"> People think that whatever they do is fine, but the Lord looks at their intentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4–5; Proverbs 3:6; PSALMS 55:22"> Trust whatever you do to the Lord, and your plans will be successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; Romans 9:22; Isaiah 43:21"> The Lord has a purpose in everything he does, even the wicked for the day of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–17; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 8:13"> The Lord hates the arrogant. You can be certain of this: the wicked won't go unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; Job 28:28; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Sin is forgiven through trustworthy love and faithfulness; by honoring the Lord people are saved from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 3:22; Colossians 1:10"> When the way that people live pleases the Lord, he makes even their enemies be at peace with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:16; PSALMS 37:16; 1 Timothy 6:6–9"> Better to have just a little and be honest than to have a lot and be dishonest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; PSALMS 37:23; Proverbs 16:1"> You can plan in your mind what to do, but the Lord will guide you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1–2; Genesis 44:5; Genesis 44:15"> The king is inspired in what he says; he is not unreliable in his decisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 20:10; Proverbs 20:23"> Accurate scales and balances are important to the Lord. He has determined all the weights in the bag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 25:5; Proverbs 29:14; Luke 12:48"> It's a terrible thing for a king to do evil, for his throne is based on doing right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 14:35; PSALMS 101:5–7; Proverbs 22:11"> People who tell the truth please kings; they love those who say what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 19:12; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> An angry king can put you to death. If you're wise you'll try and calm him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 72:6; Job 29:23–24; Hosea 6:3"> If the king is smiling, you'll live; his blessing is like the clouds that bring spring rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:10–11; Matthew 16:26; Proverbs 8:19"> Far better to get wisdom than gold; far better to choose knowledge than silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 19:16; Proverbs 10:9"> The highway of the good leads away from evil. If you watch where you're going you'll save your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:2; Isaiah 2:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Pride leads to destruction; and an arrogant spirit leads to a fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Isaiah 57:15; PSALMS 138:6"> Better to have a humble spirit and live with the poor than to share plunder with the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; PSALMS 2:12; Isaiah 30:18"> If you pay attention to wise instruction you will do well; you'll be happy if you trust in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 15:7"> If you think wisely you'll be called perceptive; if you speak pleasantly you'll be persuasive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; John 6:63; John 5:24"> If you have good sense it will be a fountain of life to you, but stupid people are punished by their stupidity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34–35; Colossians 3:16"> A wise mind makes sure to say sensible things; the words spoken are persuasive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 24:13–14; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Kind words are like a honeycomb—they taste sweet and are healthy for the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:12; Proverbs 12:15; 2 Corinthians 13:5"> There's a way that seems to be right, but in the end it's the way of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:7; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 9:12"> A good appetite helps workers—hunger encourages them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:6; Proverbs 6:14; Isaiah 5:18"> Worthless people plot evil and their words burn like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 17:9; Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 15:18"> Quarrelsome people cause conflict, and a gossip comes between the closest friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:12–15; Proverbs 12:26; Proverbs 1:10–14"> Violent people lure their friends, and lead them in a direction that's not good for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Micah 7:3; Matthew 14:7–8"> People who give you a sly wink are plotting bad things; they purse their lips and make evil happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:29; Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 3:1–2"> Gray hair is a glorious crown; it's achieved by living right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 25:28"> Better to be slow to anger than to be powerful, better to have self-control than to conquer a town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 18:18; Jonah 1:7; Acts 1:26"> The lot is thrown into the lap,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="As a way of making a choice.">a</a></sup>but the Lord makes every decision.</span>
