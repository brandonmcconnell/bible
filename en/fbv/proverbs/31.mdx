<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 1:8; 2 Timothy 1:5"> These are the words of King Lemuel, an oracle, taught to him by his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Samuel 1:28"> What shall I tell you, my son?—the son I gave birth to, the son given in response to my vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; Proverbs 7:26–27"> Don't waste your strength sleeping<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Sleeping” implied.">a</a></sup>with women, those that bring down kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Ecclesiastes 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> Lemuel, kings shouldn't be drinking wine, rulers shouldn't be drinking alcohol.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Alcohol”: literally, “strong drink.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5"> For if they drink, they'll forget what the law says, and pervert the rights of those who are suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:23; PSALMS 104:15; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Give alcohol to those who are dying, and wine to those who are in terrible distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18"> Let them drink so they can forget their poverty, and not remember their troubles any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 82:3–4; Proverbs 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> Speak up for those who have no voice, for the rights of those marginalized by society.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Amos 5:11–12"> Speak up and judge fairly; defend the poor and destitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 12:4; Proverbs 18:22"> Who can find a strong, capable wife? She's worth more than jewels!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Jewels”: though often translated as “rubies,” it seems that they were not known at this time. The red jewels were likely to be carnelian or red coral.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–7; 2 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 4:9–10"> Her husband has complete confidence in her, and with her he'll never be poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18–22; 1 Samuel 25:26–27"> She brings him good, not evil, all her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10–12"> She gets wool and flax, and with her eager hands turns them into clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 31:24; 1 Kings 9:26–28; Ezekiel 27:3–36"> Like a merchant's ship, she brings food from far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Mark 1:35; Luke 12:42"> She gets up while it's still dark to make breakfast for her family, and to plan what her servant girls need to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Song of Songs 8:12; Joshua 15:18"> She looks at a field, and decides to buy it; from the money she's earned she buys a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 38:3; 1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:10"> She's keen to get ready, and works hard with her strong arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Matthew 25:3–10; PSALMS 127:2"> She knows that the things she makes are valuable. She keeps busy—her lamp burns late into the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:25–26"> She spins the thread and weaves the cloth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew refers to the distaff and spindle used in spinning and weaving, but not much known in modern life.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 22:9"> She's generous to the poor, and gives help to the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 1:24; Proverbs 25:20"> She doesn't worry if it snows, because her whole family has warm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Warm” following the implications of the Septuagint reading. Hebrew has “scarlet,” simply meaning expensive clothing.">e</a></sup>clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Proverbs 7:16; Genesis 41:42"> She makes herself bedspreads; she dresses in fine linen and purple clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:4; Ruth 4:1; Deuteronomy 16:18"> Her husband is well-respected in the council at the town gates, where he sits with the town elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Luke 16:19; Proverbs 31:19"> She makes linen clothes for sale, and supplies merchants with belts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 40:10; Romans 13:14; Job 29:14"> She's clothed with strength and dignity, and she is happy about the future.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 10:31"> She speaks wise words, and she's kind when she gives instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Titus 2:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> She looks after the needs of her whole household, and she's never idle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Timothy 1:5; 1 Kings 2:19"> Her children are quick to bless her. Her husband praises her too, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:8–9; Ephesians 5:27"> “Many women do great things, but you are better than all of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; 1 Peter 3:4–5; Proverbs 11:22"> Charm can deceive, and beauty fades, but a woman who honors the Lord should be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 128:2; Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 7:16"> Give her the recognition she deserves; praise her publicly for what she's done.</span>
