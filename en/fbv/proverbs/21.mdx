<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Proverbs 20:24; Ezra 6:22"> The Lord directs the king's decisions like a stream of water that he sends whichever way he wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:2; Proverbs 16:25; Luke 16:15"> People think that whatever they do is fine, but the Lord looks at their motives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8"> Doing what's right and fair pleases the Lord more than sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; 1 Peter 5:5; Isaiah 2:17"> Pride and arrogance are the sins the wicked live by.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Proud eyes and arrogant heart the lamp of the wicked sin.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Ephesians 4:28"> Those who plan ahead and work hard will have plenty, while those who act rashly end up poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:11; Proverbs 10:2; Proverbs 20:21"> Money made by lies is like smoke in the wind—a chase that ends in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:21; Ephesians 5:6; Proverbs 1:18–19"> The destruction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “violence.”">b</a></sup>caused by the wicked will destroy them, for they refuse to do what's right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:15; 1 John 2:29; PSALMS 14:2–3"> Guilty people live crooked lives, but the innocent follow straight paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It's better to live in a corner of a housetop than to share a whole house with an argumentative wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Proverbs 3:29; PSALMS 36:4"> Evil people love to do wrong, they don't care what pain they cause anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:25; Proverbs 18:15; Proverbs 9:9"> When a mocker is punished, an immature person can learn wisdom. When the wise are educated, they gain knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 14:11; PSALMS 37:35–36; 2 Peter 3:6–7"> The God of justice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “the righteous one.” This is taken to refer to God particularly because of the second line.">c</a></sup>sees what happens in the homes of the wicked, and brings the wicked down in disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Matthew 6:14; Proverbs 1:28"> If you refuse to hear the cries of the poor, your cries won't be heard either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 17:8"> A gift given in secret calms down anger, and a hidden bribe soothes furious rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Matthew 7:23; Matthew 13:41–42"> When justice is done, the good are happy, but it brings terror to those who do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38; PSALMS 125:5; PSALMS 49:14"> Anyone who wanders away from the path of understanding ends up with the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; 1 Timothy 5:6; 2 Timothy 3:4"> If you love pleasure you'll become poor; if you love wine and olive oil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The extensive use of wine and olive oil was associated with luxury.">d</a></sup>you won't ever be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:8; Isaiah 43:3–4; Isaiah 53:4–5"> The wicked pay the price and not the good; those who are deceitful and not those who live right.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The exact intention of this verse is unclear, but seems to be similar to the thought in 11:8.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; PSALMS 120:5–6; PSALMS 55:6–7"> It's better to live in a desert than with an argumentative and bad-tempered wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:3–4; PSALMS 112:3; Ecclesiastes 7:11"> The wise hold on to their wealth and olive oil, but stupid people use up everything they have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; 1 Corinthians 15:58; Proverbs 22:4"> If you pursue goodness and trustworthy love, you'll find life, prosperity, and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Ecclesiastes 9:13–18"> The wise can overcome a city belonging to strong warriors, and tear down the fortress they trusted to protect them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; Proverbs 18:21; James 1:26"> If you watch what you say, you can save yourself a lot of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:22"> A proud, conceited, mocker—that's the name of those who act with insolent arrogance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 12:24; Matthew 25:26"> Slackers die hungry because they refuse to work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 37:26; Acts 20:33–35; Ephesians 4:28"> Some people only want more and more all the time, but the good give generously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Proverbs 15:8; Jeremiah 6:20"> The sacrifices given by the wicked are obnoxious, worse still when they're offered with evil motives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:13; Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9"> The lies of a false witness vanish away, but the words of a reliable witness will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:11; Proverbs 11:5; Haggai 2:18–19"> The wicked act as bold as brass, but those who live right consider carefully what they're doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Acts 5:39; Jeremiah 9:23"> Whatever wisdom, understanding, or guidance you may have is nothing before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 20:7; Isaiah 31:1; PSALMS 33:17–18"> You can get your horse ready for battle, but the victory is the Lord's.</span>
