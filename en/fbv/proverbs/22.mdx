<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:1; Luke 10:20; 1 Kings 1:47"> A good reputation is a much better choice than plenty of money; respect is better than silver and gold.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> If you're sensible you see danger coming and get out of the way; but stupid people just keep going and pay for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; PSALMS 34:9–10"> The reward you receive for being humble and respecting the Lord is wealth, honor, and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Thorns and traps lie in the path of crooked people; those who value their lives will stay away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Teach children the right way to live, and when they grow up they'll go on doing so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The rich rule the poor, and borrowers are slaves to their lenders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Isaiah 30:31"> Those who sow injustice will reap disaster, and the angry beatings they inflict on others will be stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> If you're generous, you'll be blessed, for you share your food with those in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Get rid of the scornful, and you'll get rid of conflict too—no more arguments or insults!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; PSALMS 101:6"> Anyone who loves sincerity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “purity of heart.”">a</a></sup>and a gracious way of speaking will have the king as their friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The Lord watches over true knowledge, but counteracts the words of liars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> Lazy people make claims like, “There's a lion outside! I might be killed if I go out there!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The seductive words of an immoral woman are a dangerous trap—if the Lord's angry<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, if you have a bad relationship with the Lord, you are prone to such sinful actions.">b</a></sup>with you, then you'll fall right in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Children are naturally foolish; physical correction helps them to see sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; PSALMS 12:5; James 2:13"> If you oppress the poor to make yourself rich, or if you're generous to the rich, you'll end up poor yourself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The translation and meaning of this verse is uncertain.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; PSALMS 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Pay attention and listen to the words of the wise, and think carefully about my teachings—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; PSALMS 119:103"> for it's good to keep them in mind so you can be ready to share them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> I'm explaining this to you today so you can trust in the Lord—yes, you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Haven't I written down for you thirty sayings of advice and wisdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> They are to make it clear to you what's right and true, so you can give a truthful explanation to those who sent you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> For you shouldn't steal from the poor just because they're poor; and you shouldn't crush those with limited means in court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> for the Lord will plead their case, and he will take back whatever was stolen from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Don't make friends with someone who gets upset easily; don't associate with angry people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; PSALMS 106:35"> in case you learn to be like them and make a mess of your life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “get yourself caught in a trap.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Don't shake hands and guarantee someone's debt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> for if you can't pay, why should your bed be taken away from beneath you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Don't move ancient boundary markers that your forefathers put in place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 11:28; Matthew 25:21; Proverbs 12:24"> If you see someone skilled in what they do, they'll work for kings and not for ordinary people.</span>
