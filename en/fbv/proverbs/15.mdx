<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Proverbs 25:15; Proverbs 15:18"> A kind reply wards off anger, but hurtful words make people mad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:23; Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 13:16"> What wise people say makes knowledge attractive, but stupid people talk a lot of nonsense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 16:17; Jeremiah 23:24"> The Lord sees everywhere, watching the evil and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> Gentle words are a source<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Source,” literally “tree.”">a</a></sup>of life, but telling lies does a lot of damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> Only a foolish son despises his father's instruction, but one who accepts correction is sensible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> There's plenty of treasure where good people live, but the income of the wicked brings them trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> Wise people share their knowledge, but stupid people don't think like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The Lord hates the sacrifices of the wicked, but he loves the prayers of the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The Lord hates the ways of the wicked, but he loves those who do what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> If you leave the right path you will be severely disciplined; anyone who hates correction will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; PSALMS 139:8"> Those already dead<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew simply references “Sheol” and “Abaddon,” the grave and the world of the dead.">b</a></sup>have no secrets from the Lord—how much more does he know our thoughts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> Scoffers don't like to be corrected, so they don't go to the wise for advice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> If you're happy inside, you'll have a cheerful face, but if you're sad, you look crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> An insightful mind looks for knowledge, but stupid people feed on foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> The life of poor people is hard, but if you stay cheerful, it's a continual feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; PSALMS 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> It's better to respect the Lord and only have a little than to have plenty of money and the trouble that comes with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better a dinner of vegetables where there's love than eating meat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “fattened ox.”">c</a></sup>with hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> Short-tempered people stir up trouble, but those slow to anger calm things down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> The way of lazy people is overgrown with thorns, but the path of the good is an open highway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son makes his father happy, but a stupid man despises his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Stupidity makes people with no sense happy, but sensible people do what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> Plans fall apart without good advice, but with plenty of advisors they're successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A good answer makes people happy—it's great to say the right thing at the right moment!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; PSALMS 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> Life's path for the wise leads upwards, so that they can avoid the grave below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 146:9; PSALMS 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The Lord pulls down the house of the proud, but he protects the boundaries<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In the sense that he does not allow boundary markers to be moved.">d</a></sup>of the widow's property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> The Lord hates the thoughts of the wicked, but he honors the words of the pure.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Septuagint reading.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> People greedy for ill-gotten gains make trouble for their families, but those who hate bribes will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> Good people think how best to answer questions, but stupid people say all kinds of evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; PSALMS 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> The Lord keeps his distance from the wicked, but he hears the prayers of the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> Bright eyes make you happy, and good news makes you feel good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> If you pay attention to positive advice you'll be one of the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> If you ignore instruction you have no self-esteem, but if you listen to correction you gain understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> Respect for the Lord teaches wisdom; humility goes before honor.</span>
