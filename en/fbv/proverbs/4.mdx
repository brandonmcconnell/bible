<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Chronicles 29:1; Romans 12:16"> When I was a son with my father—a tender young boy, my mother's only child—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Ephesians 6:4; John 12:50"> he was the one who taught me. He told me, “Pay attention to the words I say and keep them in mind; do what I tell you and you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 3:13–18; Proverbs 1:22–23"> Get wisdom, find out what makes sense. Don't forget my words or reject them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10; Proverbs 4:21–22; Ephesians 3:17"> Don't give up on wisdom for she will keep you safe. Love wisdom and she will protect you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12; Ecclesiastes 9:16–18; Proverbs 23:23"> The first thing in becoming wise is to get wisdom! Whatever else you gain, gain insight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 1 Samuel 2:30; Proverbs 3:35"> Treasure wisdom, and she will praise you; embrace her, and she will honor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 1:9; 1 Peter 5:4; Hebrews 2:7–9"> She will place on your head a wreath of grace; she will present to you a crown of glory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Proverbs 3:2; Jeremiah 9:20"> Listen, my son, if you accept what I tell you, then you will live a long life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 25:4–5; Ecclesiastes 12:9; Proverbs 8:9"> I have explained to you the way of wisdom; I have guided you along the right paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:23; PSALMS 18:36; 1 Peter 2:8"> Nothing will block your way when you walk, you won't stumble when you run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Proverbs 3:18; Proverbs 23:23"> Hold on to these instructions—don't let them go. Protect them, for they are life's foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 1:10"> Don't go the way of the wicked or follow the example of those who do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Job 22:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> Avoid that way completely. Don't go there! Turn away, and walk on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 36:4; Micah 2:1; Isaiah 57:20"> Evil people won't rest until they've done something wrong; they can't sleep unless they've tripped someone up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:4–5; PSALMS 14:4; Micah 6:12"> For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:12; Job 11:17; Daniel 12:3"> The way of life of the good is like the light of the sunrise, glowing brighter and brighter until the full light of day shines out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:35; Isaiah 59:9–10; Job 18:5–6"> But the way of life of the wicked is like complete darkness; they don't even know what they're tripping over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1; Isaiah 55:3; Proverbs 7:1"> My son, pay attention to what I tell you; listen to the words I'm saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:21; Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Don't lose sight of them; think about them deeply,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 12:18; Jeremiah 33:6"> for they are life to anyone who finds them, and make the whole body healthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 12:34–35; Mark 7:21–23"> Most of all, protect your mind, for everything in life flows from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 8:8; Ephesians 4:25–31"> Don't ever lie, and don't say anything dishonest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 119:37; Matthew 6:22; Job 31:1"> Focus on what's ahead of you; look at what's in front of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:13; PSALMS 37:23; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Pay attention to the path you decide to follow, and you'll be safe wherever you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; Deuteronomy 28:14"> Don't deviate to the right or the left, and turn away from evil.</span>
