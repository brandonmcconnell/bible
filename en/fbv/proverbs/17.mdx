<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:17; PSALMS 37:16; Proverbs 21:19"> Better to eat a dry scrap in peace than to feast in a house full of people arguing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Ecclesiastes 4:13; Proverbs 14:35"> A servant who acts wisely will be put in charge of a disgraced son, and will share the family inheritance with the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Jeremiah 17:10; PSALMS 26:2"> A crucible tests silver, and a furnace tests gold, but the Lord tests the attitude of mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; 1 John 4:5; Proverbs 28:4"> Evil people listen to spiteful talk, and liars pay attention to malicious words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> Anyone who oppresses the poor insults their Maker, and anyone who enjoys the suffering of others will be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Old people are proud of their grandchildren, and children are proud of their parents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; PSALMS 50:16–17"> Fine words don't suit stupid people, how much less are lies suited to a ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Micah 7:3; 1 Samuel 25:35"> Whoever gives a bribe thinks it's a magical stone—that wherever they turn they'll have success!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> If you forgive a wrong you encourage friendship, but if you keep talking about it you'll lose your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> A reprimand hits a thinking person more than one hundred blows hit someone stupid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 2 Samuel 16:5–9; 2 Samuel 18:15"> Evil people are only looking to rebel, so a cruel messenger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Cruel messenger”: perhaps referring to death.">a</a></sup>will be sent to attack them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 2 Samuel 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> Better to meet a she-bear robbed of her cubs than a stupid person and their foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> If you repay evil for good, evil will never leave your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> The beginning of a quarrel is like the first leak in a water dam, so drop it before a major argument bursts out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Isaiah 5:23"> The Lord hates it when the wicked are acquitted and the innocent condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> Is there any point in stupid people trying to buy wisdom when they don't want to learn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; 1 Samuel 20:17"> A true friend is always there to love you, and family<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Family”: literally, “a brother.”">b</a></sup>provides help when troubles come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> It's not a wise idea to make a pledge and guarantee a neighbor's debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> People who love sin like to argue; those who build high gates invite destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The meaning of the second line is unclear.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> People with warped minds don't succeed; those who tell lies get into trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> A stupid son brings grief to his father; the father of a child who does stupid things has no joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A cheerful attitude is like good medicine, but discouragement makes you sick.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Makes you sick”: literally, “dries out the bones.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> The wicked take hidden bribes to pervert the course of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> Sensible people focus on wisdom, but the eyes of stupid people are always wandering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 2 Samuel 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A stupid son brings grief to his father and sadness to his mother who gave birth to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> It's not right to impose a fine on someone who's innocent or to flog good leaders for their honesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> If you're wise, you'll be careful what you say; and if you're sensible, you'll keep your temper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Even stupid people who keep quiet are considered wise; if they don't say anything they appear intelligent.</span>
