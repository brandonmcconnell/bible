<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Isn't wisdom calling? Isn't understanding raising her voice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> On the top of the hill by the road, she stands up at the crossroads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> Beside the gates of the town, right there at the entrance, she cries out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; PSALMS 50:1"> “I'm calling out to you, everyone! My call is to everyone in the whole world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> If you're immature, learn how to grow up. If you're stupid, learn what makes good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Listen to me because I have valuable things to explain to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> I say what's right, because I tell the truth and I hate wickedness in all its forms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; PSALMS 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the words I say are true; none are false or misleading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> My words are straightforward to anyone with understanding; they are correct to those who have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; PSALMS 119:127; PSALMS 119:72"> Choose my instruction over silver; choose knowledge over pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is more valuable than rubies; everything you could ever want just doesn't compare!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I, wisdom, am at home with good decisions. I know how to find knowledge and discernment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> Honoring the Lord means hating evil. So I hate pride and arrogance, wicked behavior and telling lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> I have advice and good judgment; I know what makes sense, and I have power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> It's because of me that kings reign, and rulers issue fair decrees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It's because of me that leaders and nobles govern—all who rule justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; PSALMS 91:14"> I love those who love me, and those who really look for me will find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> With me go riches and honor, lasting wealth and prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> The fruit I produce is better than gold, even pure gold, and my harvest is better than even the best silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; PSALMS 32:8"> I live in a way that's right; I follow the paths of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> I grant wealth to those who love me; I fill their treasure storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> The Lord created me first of all; I was made before anything else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; PSALMS 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> I was formed a long time ago, at the first, before the world began.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> I was born when there were no ocean depths, when there were no springs pouring out water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 90:2; PSALMS 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> I was born before the mountains or the hills had been formed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> when he hadn't made the earth and its fields or any of the earth's dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> I was there when he placed the heavens in position, when he drew the horizon over the ocean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> when he made the clouds in the sky above, when he created the springs of the oceans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> when he set limits on the sea so that the waters would not go farther than he ordered, and when he laid out the foundations of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then I was beside him as a master craftsman. I made him happy every day, and I was always full of joy in his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 16:3; John 13:1; PSALMS 40:6–8"> I was so happy in the world he created, celebrating together with human beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; PSALMS 119:1–2; PSALMS 1:1–4"> And now, my sons, listen to me, for happy are those who follow my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; PSALMS 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Listen to my instruction and be wise—do not reject it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> Happy are those who listen to me, watching for me every day at my doors, waiting at my entrance way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For those who find me, find life, and are accepted by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But those who don't find me hurt themselves; everyone who hates me loves death.”</span>
