<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Proverbs 1:3; Proverbs 4:1"> My son, if you accept what I say and value my instructions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 18:1; PSALMS 119:111–112"> if you pay attention to wisdom and really try to understand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> if you cry out for insight and call loudly for help in understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Proverbs 16:16; Matthew 19:29"> if you look for it as if it were silver and search for it as if it were hidden treasure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Jeremiah 24:7; Jeremiah 32:40–41"> then you will understand how to relate to the Lord and discover the truth about God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> The Lord is the source of wisdom; what he says provides knowledge that makes sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; PSALMS 84:11"> He gives good judgment to those who live right; he defends those who have good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 23:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; John 10:28–29"> He supports those who act fairly and protects those who trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; PSALMS 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> Then you will be able to recognize what is right and just and fair, in fact all that is good in the way you should live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Colossians 3:16; Job 23:12"> For wisdom will fill your mind, and knowledge will make you happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6; Ephesians 5:15; PSALMS 25:21"> Good decisions will keep you on track; thinking logically will keep you safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 13:20"> Doing this will save you from the ways of evil, from men who tell twisted lies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> who turn away from following what is right to walk down paths of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremiah 11:15"> They happily do wrong; they love how twisted evil is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> They live crooked lives doing deceitful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> Doing this will also save you from a woman who acts immorally,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“A woman who acts immorally”: literally “a strange woman.”">a</a></sup>from a woman who like a prostitute<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“A woman who like a prostitute”: literally “a foreign woman.” It is thought that this comes from the idea that prostitutes were often women from other nations,">b</a></sup>tries to seduce you with flattering words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:59–60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 3:4"> Such a woman has left her husband she married when she was young, forgetting the promises she made before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> What happens in her house leads to death; following her way leads to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; PSALMS 81:12; Hosea 4:14"> No one who goes to her comes back; they don't ever find the way back to life again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; 3 John 1:11; PSALMS 119:63"> So you should follow the way of the good, and make sure you stay on the paths of those who do right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 84:11; PSALMS 37:29; PSALMS 37:9"> For only people who live right will live in the land; only honest people will remain there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 52:5; Deuteronomy 7:22; Isaiah 3:10–11"> But the wicked will be thrown out of the land; those who are untrustworthy will be pulled out by the roots.</span>
