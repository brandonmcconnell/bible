<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 26:37; Mark 5:37"> Six days later Jesus took Peter, James and his brother John, with him and they went up a high mountain to be by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 104:2; Matthew 28:3; Luke 9:29"> He was transformed in front of them. His face was shining like the sun. His clothes turned white as light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> Then suddenly Moses and Elijah appeared before them, talking together with Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> Peter spoke up, saying to Jesus, “Lord, it's really good to be here. If you want I'll make three shelters—one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While he was still speaking, a bright cloud covered them. A voice came from the cloud, saying, “This is my son whom I love, who pleases me. Listen to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> When they heard this, the disciples fell down on their faces, absolutely terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> Jesus went over to them and touched them. “Get up,” he told them. “Don't be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> When they looked up they didn't see anybody there except Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> As they descended the mountain Jesus gave them strict instructions. “Don't tell anybody what you saw until the Son of man has risen from the dead,” he told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> “So why do the religious teachers say that Elijah has to come first?” his disciples asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> “It's true that Elijah comes to put everything in place,” Jesus replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> “but I tell you that Elijah has already come and yet people didn't recognize who he was. They did whatever they felt like to him. In the same way the Son of man will also suffer at their hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples realized that Jesus was referring to John the Baptist.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> When they approached the crowd, a man came to Jesus, and kneeled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> “Lord, please have mercy on my son,” he said. “He loses his mind<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “moonstruck.” This parallels the English term “lunatic” from the Latin “lunaticus.”">a</a></sup>and suffers such terrible fits that he often falls in the fire or into water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> I brought him to your disciples but they couldn't heal him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> “You people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “generation.”">b</a></sup>refuse to trust me, and are so corrupt!” Jesus responded. “How long do I have to remain here with you? How long do I have to put up with you? Bring him here to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> Jesus confronted the demon and it left the boy, and he was healed straight away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> Later the disciples came to Jesus in private and asked him, “Why couldn't we drive it out?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:6; Mark 11:23; Luke 1:37"> “Because you don't trust enough,” Jesus told them. “I tell you, even if your trust was as small as a mustard seed, you could say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it would move. Nothing would be impossible for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Verse 21 is not in the earliest manuscripts.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> As they were walking together through Galilee, Jesus told them, “The Son of man is going to be betrayed and people will have power<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “betrayed into the hands of men.”">d</a></sup>over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> They will kill him, but on the third day he will rise again.” The disciples were filled with sadness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> When they arrived at Capernaum, those who were in charge of collecting the half-shekel Temple tax came to Peter and asked him, “Your teacher does pay the half-shekel tax, doesn't he?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> “Yes, of course,” Peter replied.<br />When he returned to where they were staying, Jesus anticipated the issue. “What do you think, Simon?” Jesus asked him. “Do the kings of this world collect their taxes and duties from their own sons or from others?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “From others,” Peter replied. So Jesus told him, “In that case the sons are exempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> But to avoid giving offense to anyone, go to the lake, and throw out a fishing line with a hook. Pull in the first fish you catch, and when you open its mouth there you'll find a stater coin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Worth one shekel. The Temple tax of the time was half a shekel per person.">e</a></sup>Take the coin and give it to them for both me and you.”</span>
</div>
</div>
