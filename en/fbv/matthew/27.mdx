<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> Early in the morning all the chief priests and elders of the people consulted together and decided to have Jesus put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> They bound him, led him away, and handed him over to Pilate, the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> When Judas, the one who'd betrayed Jesus, saw that Jesus had been condemned to death, he regretted what he'd done and returned the thirty silver coins to the chief priests and the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> “I've sinned! I've betrayed innocent blood!” he told them. “What's that got to do with us?” they replied. “That's your problem!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> Judas threw the silver coins into the sanctuary and left. He went away and hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> The chief priests took the silver coins and said, “This is blood money, so it's not lawful to put this in the Temple treasury.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So they agreed to buy the potter's field as a place to bury foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> That's why the field is still called today the “Field of Blood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> This fulfilled the prophecy spoken through the prophet Jeremiah: “They took thirty silver coins—the ‘value’ of the one who was bought at the price set by some of the children of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and used them to pay for the potter's field, just as the Lord instructed me to do.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Zechariah 11:12-13, referencing Jeremiah 32:6-15.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> Jesus was brought before Pilate the governor who asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” “You said it,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> But when the chief priests and elders brought charges against him, Jesus did not answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> “Don't you hear how many charges they're bringing against you?” Pilate asked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; PSALMS 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> But Jesus didn't say anything, not a single word. This greatly surprised the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now it was the custom of the governor to release to the crowd during the festival whichever prisoner they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> At that time a notorious prisoner was being held, a man named Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> So Pilate asked the crowds that had gathered, “Who do you want me to release to you? Barabbas, or Jesus, called the Messiah?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> (He had realized it was because of jealousy that they had handed Jesus over to him to be tried.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> While he was sitting on the judge's seat, his wife sent a message to him that said, “Don't do anything to this innocent man, for I've suffered terribly today as a result of a dream about him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and the elders convinced the crowds to ask for Barabbas, and to have Jesus put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> When the governor asked them, “So which of the two do you want me to release to you?” they answered, “Barabbas.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> “Then what shall I do with Jesus, called the Messiah?” he asked them. They all shouted out, “Have him crucified!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> “Why? What crime has he committed?” Pilate asked. But they shouted even louder, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; PSALMS 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> When Pilate saw it was a lost cause, and that a riot was developing, he took some water and washed his hands in front of the crowd. “I'm innocent of this man's blood. It's on your heads!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “see to it yourselves.”">b</a></sup>he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> All the people answered, “His blood is on us, and on our children!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released Barabbas to them, but he had Jesus flogged and sent to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> The governor's soldiers took Jesus into the Praetorium<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The military headquarters.">c</a></sup>and the whole battalion surrounded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They stripped him and put a scarlet cloak on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; PSALMS 69:19–20"> They made a crown of thorns and placed it on his head, and put a stick in his right hand. They kneeled down in front of him and mocked him, saying, “We salute you, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> They spat on him, and took the stick and beat him over the head with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> When they'd finished mocking him, they took off the robe and put his own clothes back on him. Then they led him away to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> On the way they found a man called Simon, from Cyrene, and they forced him to carry Jesus' cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> When they arrived at Golgotha, meaning “Place of the Skull,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they gave him wine mixed with gall. But having tasted it, he refused to drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> After they had crucified him, they rolled dice to divide his clothes between them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 22:18.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> Then they sat down and kept watch over him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> They placed a sign over his head with the charge against him. It read, “This is Jesus, the King of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> They crucified two rebels with him, one on the right, and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; PSALMS 69:7–12"> Those who passed by shouted insults at him, shaking their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> saying, “You who promised to destroy the Temple and rebuild it in three days, why don't you save yourself! If you really are the Son of God, then come down from the cross.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PSALMS 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; PSALMS 22:12–13"> The chief priests mocked him in the same way, along with the religious teachers and elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> “He saved other people, but he can't save himself!” they said. “If he really is the king of Israel, let him come down from the cross, and then we'll believe him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusts God so confidently—well let God rescue him now if he wants him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="What makes this even worse is that they were quoting Scripture back at him: Psalms 22:8.">e</a></sup>because he claimed ‘I am the Son of God.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> The rebels who were crucified with him also insulted him in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> From noon until three o'clock darkness covered the whole country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> At about three o'clock Jesus shouted out loud, “Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?” which means, “My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 22:1.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> When some of those standing there heard it, they said, “He's calling for Elijah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> Immediately one of them ran and grabbed a sponge, soaked it in vinegar, put it on a stick, and gave it to Jesus to drink.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Psalms 69:21.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the others said, “Leave him alone. Let's see if Elijah will come and save him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; PSALMS 22:14–15"> Jesus cried out again with a loud voice, and breathed his last.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The expression is a Hebrew one meaning that he died.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> Right then the veil of the Temple was torn apart from top to bottom. The ground shook, the rocks split apart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> and the graves were opened. Many of those who had lived good lives and had fallen asleep in death were raised to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> After the resurrection of Jesus they went out from the graveyards into the holy city<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Referring to Jerusalem.">i</a></sup>where many people saw them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> When the centurion and those with him who were guarding Jesus saw the earthquake and what happened, they were terrified, and said, “This really was the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> Many women were also watching from a distance, those who had followed Jesus from Galilee and had supported him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> These included Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> When evening came, a rich man named Joseph, from Arimathea (who was also a disciple of Jesus),</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. Pilate ordered it to be handed over to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Joseph took the body and wrapped it up in a fresh linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and placed it in his own new tomb, cut out of solid rock. He rolled a large stone across the entrance to the tomb, and left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> Mary Magdalene and the other Mary were there, sitting opposite the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> The next day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Referring to the Sabbath.">j</a></sup>after the Preparation day, the chief priests and the Pharisees went together to see Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> They said to him, “Sir, we recall that the imposter said while he was still alive, ‘After three days, I'll rise again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Give orders to secure the tomb until the third day. That way his disciples can't come and steal his body and tell people that he was raised from the dead, and the deception in the end will become worse than it was at first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> “I'll give you a guard of soldiers,” Pilate told them. “Now go, and make it as secure as you possibly can.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> So they went and made the tomb secure, sealing the entrance stone and posting soldiers as guards.</span>
