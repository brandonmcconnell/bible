<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Then Jesus was led by the Spirit into the desert to be tempted by the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> After fasting forty days and nights, he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> The tempter came and said to him, “If you really are the Son of God, order these stones to turn into bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> Jesus answered, “As Scripture says, ‘Human beings do not live by only eating bread, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 8:3.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the devil took him to the holy city<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring to Jerusalem.">b</a></sup>and placed him on the top of the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; PSALMS 34:7"> “If you really are the son of God, then throw yourself off,” he told Jesus. “As Scripture says, ‘He will order his angels to save you from harm. They will catch you so that you won't fall by tripping over a stone.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 91:11-12.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; PSALMS 78:18"> Jesus answered, “As Scripture also says, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:16.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; PSALMS 49:16–17"> Then the devil took Jesus to a very high mountain and showed him all the kingdoms of the world in all their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> He said to Jesus, “I will give you all these if you fall down on your knees and worship me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> “Go away, Satan!” said Jesus. “As Scripture says, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God and serve him, and him alone.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:13.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the devil left him, and angels came to care for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> When Jesus heard that John had been arrested, he returned to Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> Leaving Nazareth, he came to stay in Capernaum, on the shores of the sea in the regions of Zebulun and Naphthali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> This fulfilled what Isaiah the prophet said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> “In the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphthali, on the road to the sea, across the Jordan River, in Galilee where the foreigners live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> The people living in darkness saw a great light; the light of dawn has shined on those living in the land of the shadow of death.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 9:1-2.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time on Jesus began declaring his message, saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has arrived!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> As he was walking by the Sea of Galilee Jesus saw two brothers. Simon, also called Peter, and his brother Andrew, were casting a net in the sea. They made their living by catching fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> “Come and follow me, and I will teach you how to catch people,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> They left their nets immediately and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> Continuing on he saw two other brothers, James and John. They were in a boat with their father Zebedee, mending their nets. He called them to follow him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“To follow him,” implied.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> They left the boat and their father immediately, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> Jesus traveled throughout Galilee, teaching in the synagogues, telling the good news of the kingdom, and healing all the diseases and illnesses the people had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> News about him spread throughout the province of Syria.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The area to the north of Galilee.">h</a></sup>They brought everyone who was sick to him: people troubled by seizures, the demon-possessed, those who were mentally ill, people who were paralyzed, and he healed all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> Large crowds followed him from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and the region beyond Jordan.</span>
