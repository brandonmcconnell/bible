<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and his disciples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> “The religious teachers and the Pharisees are responsible as interpreters of the law of Moses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “sit in Moses' seat.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> so obey them and do everything they tell you. But don't follow what they do, because they don't practice what they preach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> They tie up heavy burdens, and place them on people's shoulders, but they themselves don't lift a finger to help them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> Everything they do is to make sure they get noticed. They make themselves large prayer boxes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “phylacteries.” These were leather boxes tied to the forehead and arms containing written texts: Exodus 13:1-6and Deuteronomy 6:4-9; Deuteronomy 11:13-21.">b</a></sup>to wear and long tassels on their clothes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="These tassels were to show their devotion to God. See Numbers 15:37-41.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> They love to have the places of honor at banquets and the best seats in the synagogues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> They love to be greeted with respect in the marketplaces, and for people to call them, ‘Rabbi.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is a Hebrew word meaning “my great one,” and was used as a term of respect for religious teachers.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> Don't let people call you ‘Rabbi.’ Only one is your Master Teacher, and you are all brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> Don't call anyone by the title ‘Father’ here on earth. Only one is your Father, who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Don't let people call you ‘Teacher.’ Only one is your Teacher, the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> The greatest among you will be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> Those who make themselves great will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be made great.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> But what a disaster is coming on you religious teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You slam shut the door of the kingdom of heaven in people's faces. You yourselves don't go in, yet you don't let anyone in who is trying to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Verse 14 is not in the most reliable early manuscripts.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> What a disaster is coming on you, religious teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! For you travel over land and sea to make a single convert, and when you do, you make him twice a son of Gehenna<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Gehenna” (see under 5:22). The emphasis here is on the destiny of the wicked.">f</a></sup>as you are yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> What a disaster is coming on those of you who say, ‘If you swear by the Temple that doesn't count, but if you swear by the gold of the Temple, then you have to keep your oath.’ What blind guides you are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; PSALMS 94:8"> What is greater—the gold, or the Temple that makes the gold holy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> You say, ‘If you swear on the altar that doesn't count, but if you swear on the sacrifice that's on the altar, then you have to keep your oath.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> How foolish and blind you are! What is greater—the sacrifice, or the altar that makes the sacrifice holy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If you swear by the altar, you swear by it and by everything that's on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> If you swear by the Temple you swear by it and by the one who lives there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; PSALMS 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> If you swear by heaven you swear by the throne of God and the one who sits there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> What a disaster is coming on you religious teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You pay a tithe of mint, aniseed, and cumin, but you neglect the vital aspects of the law—doing good, showing mercy, exercising trust. Yes, you should pay your tithe, but don't forget these other things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> You blind guides—you strain what you drink to keep out a fly but then you swallow a camel!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> What a disaster is coming on you religious teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You clean the outside of the cup and the plate, but inside you're full of greed and self-indulgence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> You blind Pharisees! First clean the inside of the cup and the plate, so that the outside will also be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> What a disaster is coming on you religious teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You are like whitewashed tombs, looking good on the outside, but on the inside full of skeletons and all kinds of rottenness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “uncleanness.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; PSALMS 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> You're just the same. On the outside you look like good people to others, but on the inside you're full of hypocrisy and wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> What a disaster is coming on you religious teachers and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You build tombs as memorials to the prophets, and decorate the tombs of the good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> and you say, ‘If we had lived in the times of our ancestors we would not have joined them in shedding the blood of the prophets.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> But by saying this you testify against yourselves, proving that you belong to those who murdered the prophets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> So get on with it—finish it all off using your forefathers' methods!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> You snakes, you brood of vipers, how will you escape the judgment of Gehenna?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Gehenna” (see under 5:22). Referring to the end-time judgment.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> That's why I'm sending you prophets, wise men, and teachers. Some of them you will kill, some of them you will crucify, and some of them you will flog in your synagogues, hunting them from town to town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> As a result, you will be held accountable for the blood of all the good people that has been poured out on the land—from the blood of Abel, who did what was right, to the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the Temple and the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> I'm telling you, the consequences of all this will fall on this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; PSALMS 91:4"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you who kill the prophets and stone those who are sent to you! So often I wanted to gather your children as a mother hen gathers her chicks under her wings—but you wouldn't let me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Now look—your house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The word house may refer to the Temple.">i</a></sup>is left abandoned, totally empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> I tell you this: you won't see me from now on until you say, ‘Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the Lord.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 118:26.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
