<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> “Don't judge others, so that you won't be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For whatever standard you use to judge others will be used to judge you, and whatever measurement you use to measure others will be used to measure you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> Why do you see the speck that's in your brother's eye? Don't you notice the plank that's in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> How can you say to your brother, ‘Let me take out that speck from your eye’ when you have a plank in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; PSALMS 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> You're being hypocritical! First get rid of the plank that's in your own eye. Then you'll be able to see clearly to take out the speck from your brother's eye.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Don't give dogs what's holy. Don't throw your pearls to pigs. That way the pigs won't trample them underfoot, and the dogs won't turn and attack you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In the original these are present imperatives, and could be translated “Keep on asking” etc.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 81:10; PSALMS 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> Everyone who asks, receives; everyone who seeks, finds; and everyone who knocks has the door opened for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Would any of you give your son a stone if he asked for bread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he asked for fish, would you give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> So if even you who are evil know to give good things to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give good things to those who ask him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> Treat others the way you want them to treat you. This sums up the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Enter by the narrow entrance. For the entrance is wide, and the way is broad that leads to destruction, and many travel that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> But the entrance is narrow, and the way is difficult that leads to life, and only a few find it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Watch out for false prophets who come wearing sheep's clothing, but who on the inside are vicious wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> You can recognize them by their fruits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, you can recognize them by the results of what they do.">b</a></sup>Do people harvest grapes from thorn bushes, or figs from thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> So every good tree produces good fruit, while a bad tree produces bad fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree can't produce bad fruit, and a bad tree can't produce good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that doesn't produce good fruit is chopped down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> So you'll recognize them by their fruits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not everyone who calls me ‘Lord, Lord’ will enter the kingdom of heaven—only those who do the will of my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many will say to me at the Day of Judgment, ‘Lord, Lord, didn't we prophesy in your name and drive out demons in your name, and perform many miracles in your name?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; PSALMS 6:8; Luke 13:25"> Then I will tell them, ‘I never knew you. Leave me, you people who practice wickedness!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Psalms 6:8.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Everyone that hears the words I say, and follows them, is like a wise man who built his house on solid rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; PSALMS 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> The rain poured down, and the floods rose, and the winds blew hard against the house, but it didn't fall down, because its foundations were on solid rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> Everyone that hears the words I say, and doesn't follow them, is like a foolish man who built his house on the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> The rain poured down, and the floods rose, and the winds blew hard against the house, and it fell down—it totally collapsed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> When Jesus finished explaining these things, the crowds were amazed at his teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> for he taught like someone with authority, and not like their religious teachers.</span>
</div>
