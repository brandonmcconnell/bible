<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; John 12:43; Hebrews 6:10"> “Make sure not to do your good deeds in front of people, just so they can be seen. Otherwise you won't have any reward from your Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15"> When you give to the poor, don't be like the hypocrites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A word taken from the Greek which literally means “play-acting.”">a</a></sup>who blow their own trumpets to announce what they're doing in the synagogues and in the streets so that people will praise them. I tell you the truth: they already have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:44"> When you give to the poor, don't let your left hand know what your right hand's doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:18; Matthew 6:6; Jeremiah 17:10"> That way what you give will be in secret, and your Father who sees what happens in secret will reward you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:10–11; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:13"> When you pray, don't be like the hypocrites, for they love to stand up and pray in the synagogues and on the street corners so that people can see them. I promise you, they already have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> But you, when you pray, go indoors and close the door, and pray to your Father in private, and your Father who sees what happens in private will reward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–3; Ecclesiastes 5:7; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> When you pray, don't babble on meaninglessly like the foreigners do, who think they will be heard because of all the words they repeat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Luke 12:30; Philippians 4:6"> Don't be like them, for your Father knows what you need even before you ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:1–4; Revelation 4:11; Romans 8:15"> So pray like this:<br />Our heavenly Father, may your name be honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 12:50"> May your kingdom come! May your will be carried out in earth as it is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 30:8; Luke 11:3; Matthew 4:4"> Please give to us today the food we need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 1:7"> Forgive our sins, just as we have forgiven those who have sinned against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 7:14–17; Matthew 26:41"> Please don't let us give in to temptation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Please don't allow us to enter temptation.” The sense here is permissive rather than causative. In other words the prayer is for God not to allow temptation to overcome us. He is not the one bringing the temptation.">b</a></sup>and save us from the evil one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Colossians 3:13"> For if you forgive those who sin against you, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:35"> But if you don't forgive those who sin against you, then your heavenly Father won't forgive your sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3–5; Matthew 9:14–15; Daniel 9:3"> When you fast, don't be like the hypocrites who put on sad faces and make themselves look terrible so that everyone can see they're fasting. I tell you the truth, they already have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 3:3; Ecclesiastes 9:8; 2 Samuel 12:20"> Instead, when you fast, wash your face and look smart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Matthew 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:18"> so that people won't see you're fasting, and your unseen Father who sees what happens in private will reward you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 12:21"> Don't pile up wealth here on earth where moths and rust ruin it, and where thieves break in and steal it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Matthew 19:21; James 2:5"> Instead, you should store up your wealth in heaven, where moths and rust don't ruin it, and where thieves don't break in and steal it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Luke 12:34; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For what you value the most shows who you really are.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:34–36; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Colossians 3:22"> The eye is like a lamp that lights the body. So if your eye is healthy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “good, innocent.”">c</a></sup>then your whole body will have light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:11; Matthew 6:22; Isaiah 5:20–21"> But if your eye is evil, then your whole body will be in the dark. If the ‘light’ you have is actually darkness, how dark is that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Luke 16:13; James 4:4"> No one can serve two masters. Either you'll hate one and love the other, or you'll be devoted to one and despise the other. You can't serve both God and Money.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Mammon,” a transliteration from the Aramaic word for the Syrian god of money and wealth.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7; Romans 8:32"> That's why I'm telling you not to worry about your life. Don't worry about what to eat, or what to drink, or what clothes to put on. Isn't life more than food, and the body more than clothes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:29–31; PSALMS 147:9; Luke 12:24–32"> Look at the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “birds of the air,” referring to wild birds rather than domesticated birds.">e</a></sup>—they don't sow or reap or store food in barns, for your heavenly Father feeds them. Aren't you worth more than they are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; PSALMS 39:5–6"> Who of you by worrying can add a minute to your life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; Philippians 4:6; Luke 12:27"> And why are you worried about clothes? Look at the beautiful flowers in the field. See how they grow: they don't work hard, they don't spin thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> But I tell you, not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of these flowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Luke 12:28; 1 Peter 1:24"> So if God decorates the fields like this, grass which is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the fire, won't he do much more for you, you people who trust so little?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Luke 12:29; Matthew 4:4"> So don't worry, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:8; Luke 12:30; Ephesians 4:17"> These are all the things that the heathen chase after, but your heavenly Father knows everything you need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 12:31; Matthew 5:6; John 6:27"> Seek his kingdom first, and his way of living right, and everything will be given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; John 14:27; Hebrews 13:5–6"> So don't worry about tomorrow, because tomorrow can worry about itself. There's already enough evil in every day.”</span>
</div>
