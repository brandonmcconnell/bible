<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–6; John 1:6–8; Luke 1:76"> Some time later John the Baptist appeared on the scene, proclaiming in the Judean desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Mark 1:15; Daniel 2:44"> “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has arrived!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Has arrived.” The verb used here is in the perfect (i.e. past) tense, indicating the event has already happened. The word itself means “to approach,” or in older language, “draw near.” However, if something “has approached” then it “has arrived.” Also in 4:17, 10:7.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1; John 1:23"> He was the one Isaiah spoke about when he said, “A voice is heard crying out in the desert, ‘Prepare the way of the Lord. Make the paths straight for him.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Isaiah 40:3.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> John had clothes made of camel hair, with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Probably locust beans.">c</a></sup>and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Luke 3:7; John 3:23"> People came to him from Jerusalem, all of Judea, and the entire Jordan region,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:13–16; Acts 2:38–41"> and were baptized in the Jordan River, publicly admitting their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 5:9"> But when John saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to be baptized, he said to them, “You vipers' brood! Who warned you to run away from the coming judgment?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “wrath.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Acts 26:20; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Show by what you do that you have truly repented,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “Produce fruit worthy of repentance.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:27–29"> and don't presume to say proudly to yourselves, ‘Abraham is our father.’ I tell you that God could make children of Abraham from these stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:6; Luke 3:9; Matthew 7:19"> In fact the ax is ready to chop down the trees. Every tree that doesn't produce good fruit will be chopped down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> Yes, I baptize you in water to show repentance, but after me is coming one who is greater than I am. I'm not worthy even to remove his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 13:30; Luke 3:17"> He has his winnowing tool<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Used after harvest to separate the grain from the chaff.">f</a></sup>ready in his hand. He will clean up the threshing floor and gather the wheat into the storehouse, but he will burn the chaff with fire that can't be put out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan River to be baptized by John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 3:3–7; Acts 1:5–8; John 13:6–8"> But John tried to change his mind. He told Jesus, “I need to be baptized by you, and you come to me to baptize you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:34; John 15:10; PSALMS 40:7–8"> “Please do so, because it's good for us to do what God says is right,” Jesus told him. So John agreed to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Mark 1:10; Luke 3:21–22"> Immediately after he was baptized, Jesus came out of the water. The heavens were opened, and he saw God's Spirit like a dove descending, landing on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; PSALMS 2:7; Matthew 17:5"> A voice from heaven said, “This is my son whom I love,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “This is my son, the beloved one.”">g</a></sup>who pleases me.”</span>
