<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Luke 8:37; Matthew 8:18"> So Jesus took a boat back across the lake to the town where he lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:18–26; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> There they brought to him a paralyzed man lying on a mat. When Jesus saw how they trusted him, he told the paralyzed man, “My friend,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “child.”">a</a></sup>cheer up! Your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Luke 5:21; Leviticus 24:16"> In response some of the religious teachers said to themselves, “He's speaking blasphemy!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> But Jesus knew what they were thinking. He asked them, “Why are you thinking evil thoughts in your minds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 5:8–14; Mark 2:9–12; Acts 3:16"> What is easier to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or ‘Get up and walk’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> But to convince you that the Son of man does have the authority to forgive sins…” he said to the paralyzed man, “Get up, pick up your mat, and go home!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The man got up and went home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Matthew 15:31; Acts 4:21"> When the crowds saw what had happened, they were scared. Then they praised God that he had given human beings such power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:27–38; Mark 2:14–22; Luke 19:2–10"> As Jesus moved on from there he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax booth. Jesus called to him, “Follow me!” He got up, and followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 5:29–32; Mark 2:15–17"> While Jesus was eating at Matthew's home, many tax collectors and sinners came and sat down at the table with Jesus and his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:19; Luke 5:30; Mark 2:16"> When the Pharisees saw this they asked Jesus' disciples, “Why does your Teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 5:31; Revelation 3:17–18; Mark 2:17"> When Jesus heard the question, he replied, “Those who are well don't need a doctor, but sick people do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Hosea 6:6"> Go and discover what this means: ‘I want mercy, not sacrifice.’ For I didn't come to call those who do what's right—I came to call sinners.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Hosea 6:6.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then the disciples of John came and asked, “Why is that we and the Pharisees fast frequently, but your disciples don't?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; John 3:29; Revelation 21:2"> “Do wedding guests mourn while the bridegroom is with them?” Jesus responded. “But the time is coming when the bridegroom will be taken from them, and then they will fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; PSALMS 125:3"> No one puts a new patch on old clothes, otherwise it will shrink and make the tear worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; PSALMS 119:83"> No one puts new wine in old wineskins either, otherwise the wineskins will burst, spilling the wine and ruining the wineskins. No, new wine is put into new wineskins, and both last.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While he was telling them this, one of the leading officials came and bowed before him. “My daughter has just died,” the man told Jesus. “But if you come and place your hand on her, I know she will come back to life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> Jesus and his disciples got up and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> At that moment a woman who had been sick with bleeding for twelve years came up behind him and touched the hem of his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> She had told herself, “If I can just touch his cloak, I'll be healed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> Jesus turned and saw her. “Be happy, for your trust in me has healed you,” he told her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jesus calls her “daughter,” but such a form of address is not usual in modern English.">c</a></sup>The woman was immediately healed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> Jesus arrived at the official's house. He saw the flute-players and the crowd that was weeping loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> “Please leave,” he told them, “For the girl isn't dead, she's just asleep.” But they laughed and made fun of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> But once the crowd had been sent out, he went in and took the girl by the hand, and she got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> News of what happened spread throughout that region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> As Jesus continued on from there, two blind men followed him, shouting, “Son of David, please have mercy on us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> When Jesus arrived at the house where he was staying, the blind men came in too.<br />“Are you convinced that I'm able to do this?” he asked them.<br />“Yes, Lord,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then Jesus touched their eyes, and said, “Because of your trust in me it will happen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14; Matthew 17:9"> And they could see. Then Jesus warned them, “Make sure nobody knows about this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:36; Matthew 9:26; Mark 1:44–45"> But they went and spread the word about Jesus everywhere.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 12:22–24; Luke 11:14"> As Jesus and his disciples were leaving, a man was brought to him who was dumb and demon-possessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; Mark 7:32–37; Exodus 4:11–12"> Once the demon had been thrown out of him, the dumb man spoke, and the crowds were amazed. “Nothing ever happened like this before in Israel,” they said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:23–24"> But the Pharisees remarked, “He throws out demons using the power of the chief of demons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–24; Acts 10:38; Mark 6:56"> Jesus went all over, visiting the towns and villages. He taught in their synagogues, telling them about the good news of the kingdom, and he healed all kinds of sicknesses and diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 6:34; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 50:6"> When he saw the crowds, Jesus was deeply moved with compassion for them, because they were troubled and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 10:2; Mark 16:15; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> He told his disciples, “The harvest is large, but there are only a few workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 10:1–2; Jeremiah 3:15; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Pray to the Lord of the harvest, and ask him to send more workers for his harvest.”</span>
</div>
