<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> After Sabbath, at dawn on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This would correspond to the day we identify as Sunday. The text clearly identifies this as “day one,” the day after the Sabbath “the seventh day.”">a</a></sup>Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> All of a sudden there was a tremendous earthquake, for an angel of the Lord came down from heaven and rolled away the stone, and sat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> His face blazed like lightning, and his clothes were as white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> The guards shook with fear, falling down as if they were dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> The angel told the women, “Don't be afraid! I know you're looking for Jesus, who was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He's not here. He's risen from the dead, just as he said he would. Come and see where the Lord was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> Now go quickly and tell his disciples that he's risen from the dead and that he's going on ahead of you to Galilee. You will see him there, I promise you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> They quickly left the tomb, both afraid and very happy, running to tell his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> Suddenly Jesus came to meet them, and greeted them. They went over to him, held on to his feet and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then Jesus said to them, “Don't be afraid! Go and tell my brothers to leave for Galilee, and they will see me there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> As they left, some of the guards went into the city and explained to the chief priests everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> After the chief priests had met with the elders, and worked out a plan, they bribed the soldiers with a great deal of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> “Say that his disciples came during the night and stole him while we were sleeping,” they told the soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> “And if the governor hears about this, we'll talk to him and you won't have to worry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> So the soldiers took the money and did what they were told. This story has been spread among the Jewish people to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> But the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had told them to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 45:11; PSALMS 2:12; John 5:23"> When they saw him they worshiped him, though some doubted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> Jesus came to them and told them, “All power in heaven and on earth has been given to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> So go and make disciples of people of all nations, and baptize them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> Teach them to follow all the commands I have given you. Remember, I am always with you, to the very end of the world.”</span>
