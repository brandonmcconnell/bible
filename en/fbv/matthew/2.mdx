<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; Luke 2:4–7"> After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea during the reign of King Herod, wise men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Magi.” These are believed to be religious priest-rulers, probably from Persia, who studied the stars.">a</a></sup>came from the east to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16"> “Where is the King of the Jews who has been born?” they asked. “We saw his star in the east and we've come to worship him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 11:47–48; Matthew 23:37"> When King Herod heard about it, he was very disturbed, and the whole of Jerusalem with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Matthew 7:29; Acts 6:12"> Herod summoned all the chief priests and religious teachers of the people, and asked them where the Messiah was supposed to be born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 7:42; Ruth 1:1; 1 Samuel 16:1"> “Bethlehem in Judea,” they told him, “for that's what the prophet wrote:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–25; Micah 5:2; Isaiah 9:6–7"> ‘Bethlehem in the land of Judea, you are certainly not the least important of Judah's leading cities,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Cities,” implied.">b</a></sup>for a leader will come from you who will be the shepherd for my people Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referencing Micah 5:2and 2 Samuel 5:2.">c</a></sup>’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:21; PSALMS 55:21"> Then Herod called the wise men and met with them in private and found out from them exactly when the star appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19–20; Jeremiah 41:5–7; Luke 20:20–21"> He sent them to Bethlehem, telling them, “When you get there, search for the child. Once you find him let me know so that I can come and worship him too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Proverbs 8:17; PSALMS 25:12"> After they had heard what the king had to say they went on their way, and the star which they had seen in the east led them until it stopped right above the place where the child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 105:3; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:10"> When they saw the star<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Clearly meaning when they saw the star stop, since they had already seen the star and followed it all the way from their home in the east.">d</a></sup>they couldn't contain their happiness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; PSALMS 72:10; Luke 2:16"> They went into the house and saw the child with Mary his mother. They bowed and worshiped him. Then they opened their bags of treasure and presented him with gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 2:22; Matthew 2:19; Matthew 2:13"> Warned by a dream not to return to Herod, they left for their own country by a different route.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:7; Acts 5:19; Matthew 1:20"> After they'd left, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, and told him, “Get up, and take the child and his mother, and flee to Egypt. Remain there until I tell you, because Herod is going to search for the child to kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 2:20–21; Acts 26:21; Matthew 1:24"> So Joseph got up and took the boy and his mother, and left during the night for Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22; Numbers 24:8"> They stayed there until Herod's death. This fulfilled what the Lord said through the prophet, “I called my son out of Egypt.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Hosea 11:1.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 28:15; Judges 16:10"> When Herod realized he'd been fooled by the wise men, he got very angry. He sent men to kill all the young boys in Bethlehem and the regions nearby who were two years old and under. This was based on the time frame he'd discovered from the wise men.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="In other words, it had been up to two years previously that the star had first appeared to the wise men.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> In this way Jeremiah's prophecy was fulfilled:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Jeremiah 9:17–21; Jeremiah 4:31"> “The sound of terrible weeping and mourning was heard in Rama, Rachel crying for her children. They are dead, and she can't be comforted.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Jeremiah 31:15.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Daniel 8:25; Matthew 2:12–13"> After Herod died the angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt, and told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:19; 1 Kings 12:1–3; Matthew 2:13"> “Get up! Take the child and his mother, and return to the land of Israel, because those who were trying to kill the child are dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 6:22"> So Joseph got up and took the boy and his mother, and returned to the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 48:17–18; Luke 2:39; John 7:52"> But Joseph was afraid to go there after he learned that Archelaus had succeeded his father Herod as king of Judea. Warned in a dream, Joseph went to the Galilee area</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 13:5; John 1:45–46; Mark 1:24"> and settled down in Nazareth. This fulfilled what the prophets had said, “He will be called a Nazarene.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Referring to Jesus. Matthew is not citing one specific prophecy here.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
