<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:25; Luke 17:12–19; Matthew 18:26"> A leper approached him and bowed down, worshiping him, and said, “Lord, if you're willing, please heal me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:13; PSALMS 33:9; 2 Kings 5:14"> Jesus reached out and touched him with his hand. “I am willing,” he said. “Be healed!” Immediately he was healed from his leprosy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 5:14; Luke 17:14"> “Make sure you don't tell anyone,” Jesus told him. “Go and show yourself to the priest and give the offering which Moses required as public proof.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Proof that he had been healed and was ceremonially clean. See Leviticus 14.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:1–10; Acts 27:43; Acts 10:1–33"> When Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion came up to him, begging for help,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 9:33"> “Lord, my servant is at home, lying down, unable to move. He's in terrible agony.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 9:18–19; Luke 7:6; Mark 5:23–24"> “I will come and heal him,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; Luke 7:6–7; PSALMS 33:9"> The centurion answered, “Lord, I don't merit a visit to my home. Just say the word, and my servant will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:35–36; Titus 2:9; Colossians 3:22"> For I am myself under the authority of superiors, while I also have soldiers under my command. I command one, ‘Go!’ and he goes. I command another, ‘Come!’ and he comes. I tell my servant, ‘Do this!’ and he does it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Luke 7:9; Mark 6:6"> When Jesus heard what he said, he was astonished. He told those who were following him, “I tell you the truth, I haven't found this kind of trust anywhere in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:28–29; Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 3:28–29"> I tell you that many will come from the east and the west, and sit down with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:30; Luke 13:28; Matthew 21:43"> But the heirs of the kingdom will be thrown out into utter darkness where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; Mark 9:23; John 4:50"> Then Jesus told the centurion, “Go home. Because you trusted that it would happen, what you asked for has been done.” The servant was healed right away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 4:38–41; Mark 1:29–34; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> When Jesus arrived at Peter's house, he saw that Peter's mother-in-law was sick in bed with a high fever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; Luke 4:38–39; Luke 8:54"> He touched her hand and the fever left her. She got up and began making him a meal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Matthew 14:14; Mark 1:32–34"> When evening came they brought many who were demon-possessed to Jesus. He made the spirits leave at his command, and he healed all those who were sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; 1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 1:22"> This fulfilled what the prophet Isaiah said: “He healed our diseases, and freed us from our illnesses.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 53:4.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 4:42–43; Luke 8:22; Mark 4:35"> When Jesus saw the crowds around him, he gave instructions that they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“They” meaning Jesus and the disciples.">c</a></sup>should go to the other side of the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38; Luke 14:25–27"> One of the religious teachers approached him and said, “Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 84:3; Luke 2:7; Isaiah 53:2–3"> “Foxes have their dens, and wild birds have their nests, but the Son of man has nowhere he can lie down and rest,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “lay down his head.”">d</a></sup>Jesus told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 9:59–62; Leviticus 21:11–12; Numbers 6:6–7"> Another disciple said to Jesus, “Lord, first let me go and bury my father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Luke 15:32; Ephesians 5:14"> “Follow me! Leave the dead to bury their own dead,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 8:22–25; Mark 4:36–41; Matthew 9:1"> Then Jesus got into a boat and his disciples went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 8:23; PSALMS 107:23–27; Mark 4:37–38"> A violent storm suddenly blew up, and waves crashed over the boat, but Jesus went on sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Mark 4:38–39"> The disciples went over to him and woke him up. “Save us, Lord! We're going to drown!” they shouted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; PSALMS 65:7; PSALMS 107:28–30"> “Why are you so afraid? Why do you trust so little?” he asked them. Then he got up, and commanded the winds and the waves to stop. Everything became absolutely calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:33"> The disciples were astonished, and said, “Who is this? Even the winds and the waves obey him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> When he arrived on the other side, in the region of the Gadarenes, two demon-possessed men came out of the graveyard to meet him. They were so dangerous that nobody dared to travel that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; James 2:19"> They shouted out, “What have you got to do with us, you Son of God? Have you come to torture us before our time?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> In the distance a large herd of pigs was feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> The demons pleaded with him, “If you're going to drive us out, send us into the herd of pigs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> “Go!” said Jesus. The demons left the men and went into the pigs. The whole herd ran down the steep hillside into the sea and drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:34–36; Mark 5:14–16; Acts 19:15–17"> The pig herders ran away. They went to the town and told the people there everything that had taken place, and what had happened to the demon-possessed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:37–39; Luke 5:8"> The whole town came out to meet Jesus. When they found him, they begged him to leave their district.</span>
</div>
