<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 10:38; Luke 8:1"> When Jesus had finished giving instructions to his twelve disciples, he left to go and teach and speak publicly in the nearby towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> While John was in prison he heard what the Messiah was doing, so he sent his disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> to ask on his behalf, “Are you the one we were expecting to come, or should we continue to look for someone else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Jesus answered them, “Go back and tell John what you hear and what you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> The blind can see, the crippled can walk, the lepers are healed, the deaf can hear, the dead are resurrected, and the poor hear the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> Blessed are those who don't reject me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> As they left, Jesus began to talk to the crowds about John. “What were you expecting to see when you went out into the desert? A reed tossed about by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> So what did you go to see? A man dressed in fancy clothes? People dressed like that live in kings' palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> So what did you go to see then? A prophet? Yes, and I tell you he is much more than a prophet!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> He's the one this Scripture was written about: ‘I'm sending my messenger on ahead of you. He will prepare your way for you.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Malachi 3:1.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> I tell you the truth, that among humanity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “among those who are born of women.”">b</a></sup>there's no one greater than John the Baptist, but even the least important in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> From the time of John the Baptist up till now the kingdom of heaven continues to be under attack, and violent people are trying to seize it by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> For all the prophets and the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referring to the message of the Old Testament.">c</a></sup>spoke for God until John came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> If you're prepared to believe it, he is Elijah, the one who was expected to come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Malachi 4:5.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> Anybody with ears should be listening!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> What shall I compare this generation to? It's like children sitting in the marketplaces shouting to each other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> ‘We played the flute for you and you didn't dance; we sang sad songs and you didn't cry.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> John didn't come feasting and drinking, so people say, ‘He's devil-possessed!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> On the other hand the Son of man came and did feast and drink, and people say, ‘Look, he's greedy and drinks too much; he's a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ But wisdom is shown to be right by what it does…”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then he began to reprimand the towns where he'd done most of his miracles because they had not repented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> “Shame on you, Korazin! Shame on you, Bethsaida! If the miracles had been performed in Tyre and Sidon which happened among you, they would have repented in sackcloth and ashes long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> But I tell you that on the Day of Judgment it will be better for Tyre and Sidon than it will for you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> And what about you, Capernaum? Will you be exalted to heaven? No, you'll go down to Hades! If the miracles had been performed in Sodom which happened among you, Sodom would still be here today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> But I tell you that on the Day of Judgment it will be better for Sodom than it will for you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; PSALMS 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> Then Jesus prayed, “I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, for you've hidden these things from the clever and sophisticated. Instead you've revealed them to ordinary people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “infants.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> Yes, Father, you were pleased to do this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> Everything has been entrusted to me by my Father, and no one really understands the Son, except the Father, and no one really understands the Father, except the Son, and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> Come to me, all of you who struggle and who are burdened down. I will give you rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> Accept my yoke, and learn from me. For I am kind and have a humble heart, and in me you will find the rest you need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> For my yoke is gentle, and my burden is light.”</span>
</div>
