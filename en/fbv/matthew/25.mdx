<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> “The kingdom of heaven is like ten girls, who took their lamps with them to go and meet the bridegroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> Five were foolish, and five were wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> The foolish girls took their lamps but didn't take any oil with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> while the wise took jars of oil with them as well as their lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> The bridegroom took a long time and all the girls became drowsy and fell asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> At midnight the shout came, ‘Look, the bridegroom's here! Come out and meet him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> All the girls got up and trimmed the wicks of their lamps. The foolish girls said to the wise ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> ‘Give us some of your oil, because our lamps are going out.’ But the wise girls replied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> ‘No, because otherwise there won't be enough for both you and for us. Go to the shopkeepers and buy some oil for yourselves.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> While they went to buy oil, the bridegroom arrived, and those who were ready went in with him to the wedding, and the door was locked shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> The other girls came later. ‘Lord, Lord,’ they called, ‘Open the door for us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he answered, ‘I tell you the truth: I don't know you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> So stay alert, for you don't know the day, or the hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> It's like a man who went away on a trip. He called in his servants and entrusted them with what he owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> To one of them he gave five talents,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Probably referring to talents of silver, a large amount of money.">a</a></sup>to another he gave two, and to another one, according to their different abilities. Then he left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> Immediately the one with five talents went and invested them in a business, and made another five talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> In the same way the one with two talents made another two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But the man who'd received the one talent went off and dug a hole and hid his master's money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> A long time later the master of those servants returned, and settled accounts with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> The one with five talents came and presented the other five talents. ‘Master,’ he said, ‘You gave me five talents. Look, I've made a profit of five talents.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His master said to him, ‘You've done well—you are a good, faithful servant. You have proved yourself trustworthy over small things, so now I'm placing you in charge over many things. Be happy because I'm really pleased with you!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> The one with two talents also came. ‘Master,’ he said, ‘You gave me two talents. Look, I've made a profit of two talents.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His master said to him, ‘You've done well—you are a good, faithful servant. You have proved trustworthy over small things, so now I'm placing you in charge over many things. Be happy because I'm really pleased with you!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> Then the man with one talent came. ‘Master,’ he said, ‘I know that you're a hard man. You reap where you didn't sow, and you harvest crops that you didn't plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> So since I was afraid of you I went and buried your talent in the ground. Look, you can have back what belongs to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> But his master answered him, ‘You wicked and lazy servant! If you think I reap where I don't sow, and harvest crops I didn't plant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> then you should have deposited my silver in the bank so that when I returned I could have had my money with interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Take the talent away from him, and give it to the one with ten talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> For everyone who has will be given even more; and everyone who doesn't have anything, even what they have will be taken away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> Now throw this useless servant out into the darkness where there'll be crying and gnashing of teeth.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> But when the Son of man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then he will sit on his majestic throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Everyone will be brought before him. He will separate them from one another, as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> He'll place the sheep on his right hand, and the goats on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then the king will say to the ones on his right, ‘Come, you who are blessed by my Father, and inherit the kingdom that's been prepared for you from the beginning of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For I was hungry, and you gave me food to eat. I was thirsty, and you gave me a drink. I was a stranger, and you invited me in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> I was naked and you clothed me. I was sick and you looked after me. I was in prison and you visited me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then those who are right will answer, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry and fed you, or thirsty and gave you a drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When did we see you as a stranger and invite you in, or naked and clothe you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> When did we see you sick, or in prison, and visit you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> The king will tell them, ‘I tell you the truth: whatever you did for one of these least important brothers of mine you did for me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> He'll also say to those on his left, ‘Go away from me, you who are doomed, into the eternal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eternal in consequence, rather than in duration.">b</a></sup>fire prepared for the devil and his angels!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> For I was hungry and you didn't give me anything to eat. I was thirsty and you didn't give me a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger and you didn't invite me in. I was naked and you didn't clothe me. I was sick and in prison and you didn't visit me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> Then they will also answer, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and didn't look after you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then he will tell them, ‘I tell you the truth: whatever you didn't do for one of these least important brothers of mine you didn't do for me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> They will go away into eternal condemnation, but those who are good will enter eternal life.”</span>
</div>
