<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> Later that day Jesus left the house, and sat down to teach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Implied. The religious teachers sat down when they wanted to instruct their disciples.">a</a></sup>beside the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> So many people gathered around him that he got into a boat and sat down there to teach, while all the crowds stood on the beach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; PSALMS 78:2"> He explained many things to them, using stories as illustrations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Stories as illustrations,” literally, “parables.”">b</a></sup><br />“The sower went out to sow,” he began.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> “As he was sowing, some of the seeds fell on the path. The birds came and ate them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> Other seeds fell on stony ground where there wasn't much earth where they sprouted quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> The sun rose and scorched them and they withered because they had no roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> Other seeds fell among thorns that grew up and choked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> Still other seeds fell on good soil. They produced a harvest—some one hundred, some sixty, and some thirty times what had been planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> Anybody who has ears should be listening!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> The disciples came to Jesus and asked him, “Why do you use illustrations when you speak to the people?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> “You're privileged to have revealed to you the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but they're not given such insights,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> “Those who already have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Probably meaning “have understanding.”">c</a></sup>will have more given to them, more than enough. But those who don't have, whatever they have will be taken away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> That's why I speak to them in illustrations. For even though they see, they don't see; and even though they hear, they don't hear; nor do they understand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus was referencing the Old Testament here. It could be any of these: Deuteronomy 29:4, Isaiah 42:20, Jeremiah 5:21, Ezekiel 12:2.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> The prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled in them: ‘Even though you hear, you won't understand, and even though you see, you won't perceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> They have a hard-hearted attitude, they don't want to listen, and they've closed their eyes. If they didn't they might be able to see with their eyes, hear with their ears, and understand in their minds. Then they could return to me and I would heal them.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 6:9-10.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> Your eyes are blessed, for they see. Your ears are blessed too, for they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> I'm telling you, many prophets and good people longed to see what you're seeing, but didn't see it. They longed to hear what you're hearing, but didn't hear it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> So listen to the story of the sower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When people hear the message about the kingdom and don't understand it, the evil one comes along and rips out what was sown in them. This is what happens to the seeds sown on the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; PSALMS 106:12–13; John 5:35"> The seeds sown on stony ground are people who hear the message and happily accept it straight away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> They last for a while, but because they don't have roots, when problems and troubles come, they quickly fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> The seeds sown among thorns are people who hear the message, but then life's worries and the temptation of money choke the message so that they become fruitless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> The seeds sown on good soil are people who hear the message, and understand it, and who produce a good harvest—some one hundred, some sixty, and some thirty times what was sown.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Then he told them another illustrated story: “The kingdom of heaven is like a farmer who sowed good seeds in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But while his workers were sleeping, an enemy came and sowed weed seeds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Actually “darnel,” or “false wheat,” a weed that looked similar to wheat.">f</a></sup>on top of the wheat. Then they left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> So when the wheat grew and produced ears of grain, the weeds also grew up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> The farmer's workers came and asked him, ‘Sir, didn't you sow good seeds in your field? Where did the weeds come from?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> ‘Some enemy has done this,’ he replied. ‘Do you want us to go and pull up the weeds?’ they asked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> ‘No,’ he answered, ‘as you pull up the weeds, you might uproot the wheat too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Let them both grow until harvest, and then at harvest-time I'll tell the reapers to first gather the weeds, tie them up into bundles and burn them, and then gather the wheat and store it in my barn.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> He gave them another illustration: “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed that a farmer sowed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> Even though it's the tiniest of seeds it grows much bigger than other plants. In fact it grows into a tree big enough for birds to roost in its branches.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> He told them another illustrated story: “The kingdom of heaven is like yeast that a woman mixed with a large quantity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Approximately 50 pounds, or 23 kilos.">g</a></sup>of flour, until all the dough was raised.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> Jesus explained all these things to the crowds using illustrated stories—in fact he didn't speak to them without using stories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> This fulfilled the prophet's words: “I will speak using stories, and I will explain things hidden from the creation of the world.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 78:2.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then Jesus left the crowds and went into a house. His disciples came over to him, and asked him, “Please explain to us the story about the weeds in the field.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> “The one who sows the good seed is the Son of man,” Jesus explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> “The field is the world. The good seeds are the children of the kingdom. The weed seeds are the children of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> The enemy that sowed the weed seeds is the devil. The harvest is the end of the world. The reapers are angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> Just as the weeds are harvested and burned so it will be at the end of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of man will send out his angels, and they will gather up every sinful thing and everyone who does evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> and will throw them into the blazing furnace, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then those who live right will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="See Daniel 12:3.">i</a></sup>Anybody with ears should be listening!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field. A man found it, reburied it, and then full of joy went and sold all he had and bought that field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> The kingdom of heaven is also like a trader looking for good pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> When he found the most expensive pearl ever he went and sold all he had and bought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> Or again, the kingdom of heaven is like a fishing net thrown into the sea that caught all kinds of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> When it was full it was dragged ashore. The good fish were put into baskets while the bad ones were thrown away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> That's the way it will be when the end of the world comes. The angels will go out and separate the evil people from the good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> and throw them into the blazing furnace where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Now do you understand everything?”<br />“Yes,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> “This is why every religious teacher who's learned about the kingdom of heaven is like a house-owner who brings out from his storeroom both new and old treasures,” Jesus said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> After Jesus finished telling these stories, he left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> He went back to his home town<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Nazareth.">j</a></sup>and taught in the synagogue there. People were amazed, and asked, “Where does he get his wisdom and miracles from?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Isn't this the carpenter's son? Isn't his mother's name Mary, and his brothers James, and Joseph, and Simon, and Judas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Don't his sisters live here among us? So where does he get all this from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> And so they refused to believe in him.<br />“A prophet is honored everywhere except in his homeland and in his family,” Jesus told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> Since they failed to trust in him, he did not do many miracles there.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
