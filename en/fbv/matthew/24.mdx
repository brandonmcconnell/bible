<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 13:1–37; Luke 21:5–36; Ezekiel 8:6"> As Jesus was leaving the Temple his disciples came to him, pointing proudly to the Temple buildings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Jeremiah 26:18; Micah 3:12"> But Jesus replied, “You see all these buildings? I tell you the truth: there won't be one stone left upon another. Every last one will come crashing down!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> As Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to him in private, and asked, “Please tell us when this will happen. What will be the sign of your coming and the end of the world?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> “Make sure no one misleads you,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> “Many will come claiming to be me, saying, ‘I'm the Messiah,’ and they will deceive many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> You'll hear about wars, and rumors about wars, but don't be anxious. These things have to happen, but this isn't the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> Nations will attack other nations, and kingdoms will fight against other kingdoms. There will be famines and earthquakes in various places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> but all these things are just the beginnings of birth pains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> Then they will arrest you, they will persecute you, and they will kill you. All people will hate you because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> At that time many believers will give up believing. They will betray one another and hate one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> Many false prophets will come and deceive many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> The increasing evil will lead the love of many to grow cold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> but those who hold out to the end will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> The good news of the kingdom will be proclaimed around the whole world so everyone will be able to hear it, and then the end will come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> So when you see the ‘idolatry that causes devastation’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “desolating sacrilege,” referring back to Daniel 9:27, Daniel 11:31, Daniel 12:11.">a</a></sup>standing in the holy place that the prophet Daniel spoke about (those who read this please consider carefully),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> then the people living in Judea should run away to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> Anyone who is on the housetop must not go down and fetch what's in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Anyone who is out in the fields must not go back to get a coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> How awful it will be for those who are pregnant, and those who are nursing babies at that time!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> Pray that you don't have to run away in winter, or on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> For at that time there will be terrible persecution—more terrible than anything that has happened from the beginning of the world up till now, and won't ever happen again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> Unless those days are cut short, nobody will be saved, but for the sake of the chosen those days will be cut short.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> So if anyone tells you, ‘Look, here's the Messiah,’ or, ‘There he is,’ don't believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> For false messiahs will appear, and false prophets too, and they will perform incredible signs and miracles in order to deceive the chosen, if that were possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> Notice that I've told you this before it even happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> So if they tell you, ‘Look, he's out in the desert,’ don't go looking there; or if they say, ‘Look, he's secretly here,’ don't believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> For the coming of the Son of man will be like the lightning that flashes brightly from east to west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> ‘Vultures gather where the carcass is.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> But right after those days of persecution the sun will be darkened, the moon will not shine, the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of heaven will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> Then the sign of the Son of man will appear in heaven, and all the peoples of the earth will mourn. They will see the Son of man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and in brilliant glory.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Daniel 7:13-14.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> With a blast from a trumpet he will send his angels to gather his chosen ones from every place, from one end of heaven and earth to the other.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “from the four winds, from one end of the heavens to the other.”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> Learn an illustration from the fig tree. When its shoots become tender and leaves begin to sprout, you know that summer is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> In the same way when you see all these things happening, you know that his coming is near, in fact it's right at the door!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> I tell you the truth: this generation won't pass away until all these things have happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> Heaven and earth may pass away, but my words won't pass away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> But no one knows the day or hour when this will take place, not the angels in heaven, nor the Son. Only the Father knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> When the Son of man comes it will be like it was during the time of Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> It will be as in the days before the flood, when they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, right up until the day that Noah went into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> They didn't realize what was going to happen until the flood came and swept them all away. That's how the coming of the Son of man will be.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Two men will be working in the fields. One will be taken, the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> Two women will be grinding grain with a mill. One will be taken, the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> So stay alert, because you don't know what day your Lord is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> But consider this: if the house-owner knew what time a thief was coming, he would keep watch. He would not allow his house to be broken into and robbed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> You also need to be ready, because the Son of man is coming at a time when you're not expecting him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> For who is the trustworthy and thoughtful servant? It's the one his master puts in charge of his household to provide them with food at the proper time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> How good it will be for that servant to be found doing that when his master returns!</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> I tell you the truth: the master will put that servant in charge over everything he has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if he was evil, the servant would say to himself, ‘My master is taking a long time,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> and would start beating the other servants, feasting and drinking with the drunks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> Then that servant's master will return when the man doesn't expect him, at a time he doesn't know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> He'll cut him to pieces, and treat him in the same way as the hypocrites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Those who claim to follow their Lord but in reality don't.">d</a></sup>sending him to a place where there's crying and gnashing of teeth.”</span>
</div>
</div>
