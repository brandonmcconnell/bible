<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Matthew 16:6; Acts 4:1"> The Pharisees and Sadducees came to test<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Since the test was in an attempt to discredit Jesus, this could also be translated “came to trap Jesus.”">a</a></sup>Jesus, demanding that he show them a sign from heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:54–56"> Jesus replied, “In the evening you say, ‘It'll be a fine day tomorrow, because the sky is red,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 12:56; 1 Chronicles 12:32; Matthew 11:5"> while in the morning you say, ‘It'll be bad weather today, because the sky is red and overcast.’ You know how to predict the weather by what the sky looks like, but you can't recognize the signs of the times!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:29–30; Matthew 12:39–40; Jonah 1:17"> An evil and adulterous generation are the ones who look for a miraculous sign, and no sign will be given to them except the sign of Jonah.” He left them and went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:13–14; Matthew 15:39"> Having crossed to the other side of the lake, the disciples realized they'd forgotten to bring bread with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Galatians 5:9; Matthew 16:12"> “Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees,” Jesus told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 8:16–18; Matthew 15:16–18; Acts 10:14"> The disciples started arguing among themselves. “He's saying that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup>because we didn't bring any bread,” they concluded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:30; Matthew 14:31; Matthew 8:26"> Jesus knew what they were saying and said, “You have so little trust in me! Why are you arguing among yourselves about not having any bread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:9–13; Luke 9:13–17"> Haven't you worked it out yet? Don't you remember the five loaves that fed five thousand? How many baskets of leftovers did you collect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:17–21; Mark 8:5–9"> And what about the seven loaves that fed the four thousand? How many baskets of leftovers did you collect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 16:6; Luke 12:56; Mark 8:21"> Haven't you worked it out yet that I wasn't talking to you about bread? Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Acts 23:8; Matthew 15:4–9"> Then they realized that he wasn't warning them to watch out for bread yeast, but about the teachings of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:18–20; Mark 8:27–29; Daniel 7:13"> When Jesus arrived in the region of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, “Who do people say that the Son of man is?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:15; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> “Some say John the Baptist, some say Elijah, and others say Jeremiah or one of the other prophets,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 9:20; Matthew 13:11; Mark 8:29"> “But what about you?” he asked them. “Who do you say I am?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; John 11:27; 1 John 4:15"> “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God,” Simon Peter replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 6:45; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 11:25–27"> “You are truly blessed, Simon son of John,” Jesus told him. “For this wasn't revealed to you by human flesh and blood, but by my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Corinthians 3:9–11; Isaiah 28:16"> I also tell you that you are Peter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Peter means “a stone,” in contrast to the word for solid rock also used in this verse.">c</a></sup>and on this rock I will build my church and the powers of death<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “gates of Hades.”">d</a></sup>won't overcome it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Isaiah 22:22; Revelation 1:18"> I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you ban on earth will have been banned in heaven, and whatever you allow on earth will have been allowed in heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Luke 9:21; John 1:41"> Then he warned his disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Messiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 24:6–7; Matthew 20:17–19; Matthew 17:22–23"> From that time on Jesus began to explain to his disciples that he would have to go to Jerusalem, and that he would suffer terribly at the hands of the elders, chief priests, and religious teachers; and that he would be killed, but he would rise again on the third day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 8:32; John 13:6–8; Matthew 16:16–17"> Peter took Jesus aside and started to tell him he shouldn't talk like that. “God forbid, Lord, that this would ever happen to you!” he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 8:5–8; Colossians 3:2; Philippians 3:19"> Jesus turned to Peter, and told him, “Get away from me, Satan! You are a trap to trip me up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, a “stumbling block” or a “snare.”">e</a></sup>because you're thinking in a human way, and not as God thinks!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:27; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> Then Jesus told his disciples, “If you want to be a follower of mine, you must deny yourself, take up your cross, and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Mark 8:35; Revelation 12:11"> For if you want to save your life you will lose it, and if you lose your life for my sake you will save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 8:36–37; Job 27:8; Luke 9:25"> What benefit is it for you to gain the whole world, yet lose your life? What would you give in exchange for your life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; PSALMS 62:12; Romans 2:6"> For the Son of man is going to come in his Father's glory, together with his angels. Then he will give everyone what they deserve according to what they've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Mark 9:1; John 8:52"> I tell you the truth, there are some standing here who won't die<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “taste death.”">f</a></sup>before they see the Son of man coming in his kingdom.”</span>
</div>
</div>
