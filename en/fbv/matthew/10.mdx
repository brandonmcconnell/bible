<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> Jesus called his twelve disciples together and gave them power to throw out evil spirits, and to heal all kinds of diseases and sicknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> These are the names of the twelve apostles: first, Simon (also called Peter), Andrew his brother, James the son of Zebedee, John his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew the tax collector, James the son of Alphaeus, Thaddeus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon the revolutionary, and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Jesus sent out these Twelve, telling them, “Don't go to the foreigners, or to any Samaritan city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> You are to go to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> Wherever you go, tell the people, ‘The kingdom of heaven has arrived.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heal those who are sick. Resurrect the dead. Cure the lepers. Drive out demons. You received freely, so give freely!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Don't carry any gold, silver, or copper coins in your pockets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> or a bag for your journey, or two cloaks, or sandals, or a walking stick, for a worker deserves to be supported.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fed.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> Wherever you go, whatever town or village, ask for someone who lives according to good principles, and remain there until you leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> When you enter the house, give it your blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; PSALMS 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> If the home deserves it, let your peace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Peace,” meaning blessing.">b</a></sup>rest on it, but if it doesn't deserve it, let your peace return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> If someone doesn't welcome you, and refuses to listen to what you have to say, then leave that house or that town, shaking its dust off your feet as you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> I tell you the truth, it will be better for Sodom and Gomorrah at the Day of Judgment than for that town!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Look, I'm sending you out like sheep among wolves. So be as wise as serpents and harmless as doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> Watch out for those who will hand you over to be tried by town councils<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “sanhedrins,” local religious courts.">c</a></sup>and will whip you in their synagogues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; PSALMS 2:1–6"> You will be dragged before governors and kings because of me, to witness to them and to the foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But when they put you on trial, don't worry about how you should speak or what you should say, because you'll be told what to say at the right time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For it isn't you who will speak but the Spirit of the Father will speak through you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> Brother will betray brother and have him killed, and a father will do the same to his child. Children will rebel against their parents, and have them put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> Everyone will hate you because you follow me, but those who endure until the end will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> When you're persecuted in one town, run away to the next. I'm telling the truth: you won't finish going to the towns of Israel before the Son of man comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> Disciples are not more important than their teacher; servants are not more important than their master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> Disciples should be satisfied to become like their teacher, and servants like their master. If the head of the house has been called the head demon Beelzebub,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Beelzebub, referring to Satan.">d</a></sup>then the members of his household will be demonized even more!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> So don't be frightened of them, for there's nothing covered that won't be exposed, and nothing hidden that won't be made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> What I tell you here in the dark, declare when it's light, and what you hear whispered in your ear, shout from the rooftops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> Don't be afraid of those who can kill you physically, but can't kill you spiritually. Instead, be afraid of the one who can destroy you physically and spiritually in Gehenna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Gehenna.” See note under 5:22.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; PSALMS 104:27–30"> Aren't two sparrows sold for just one penny? But not a single one of them falls to the ground without your Father knowing about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> Even the hairs on your head have all been counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; PSALMS 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> So don't worry—you're worth more than many sparrows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> Anyone who publicly declares their commitment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “confess.”">f</a></sup>to me, I will also declare my commitment to them before my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But anyone who publicly denies me, I will also deny before my Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> Don't think I've come to bring peace on earth. I haven't come to bring peace, but a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> I've come ‘to turn a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; PSALMS 41:9"> Your enemies will be those of your own family!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Referencing Micah 7:6.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> If you love your father or mother more than me you don't deserve to be mine, and if you love your son or daughter more than me you don't deserve to be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> If you don't pick up your cross and follow me you don't deserve to be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> If you try to save your life, you will lose it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In other words, if you try to hold on to your life by your own human effort, you will not succeed.">h</a></sup>but if you lose your life because of me you will save it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> Those who welcome you welcome me, and those who welcome me welcome the one that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> Those who welcome a prophet because that's what they are, will receive the same reward as a prophet. Those who welcome someone who does right will receive the same reward as someone who does right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> I tell you the truth, those who give a drink of cool water to the least important of my disciples will definitely not miss out on their reward.”</span>
</div>
