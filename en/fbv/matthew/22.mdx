<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> Jesus spoke to them using more illustrated stories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> “The kingdom of heaven is like a king who organized a wedding celebration for his son,” Jesus explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> “He sent out his servants to everyone who was invited to the wedding to tell them to come, but they refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> So he sent out more servants, with the instructions, ‘Tell those who are invited that I've prepared the wedding banquet. The bulls and fattened calves have been killed—everything's ready. So come to the wedding!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they ignored the invitation and left. One went to his fields; another to take care of his business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> The rest grabbed the king's servants, mistreated them, and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> The king became furious. He sent his soldiers to destroy those murderers and burn down their town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then the king said to his servants, ‘The wedding banquet is ready, but those who were invited didn't deserve to attend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go into the streets and invite everyone you find to come to the wedding.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> So the servants went out into the streets and brought back everyone they could find, both the good and the bad. The wedding hall was full.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to meet the guests, he noticed a man who didn't have a wedding robe on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> He asked him, ‘My friend, how did you get in here without a wedding robe?’ The man had nothing to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king told his servants, ‘Bind his hands and feet, and throw him out into the dark, where there'll be crying and grinding of teeth.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are invited, but few are chosen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; PSALMS 2:2"> Then the Pharisees left and met together to plot how they could trap him by what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> They sent their disciples to him, together with some of Herod's supporters.<br />“Teacher, we know you are a honest man, and that you teach God's way is the truth,” they began. “You don't allow yourself to be influenced by anyone, and you don't care about rank or status.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> So let us know your opinion. Is it right to pay Caesar's taxes, or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> Jesus knew their motives were evil. He asked them, “Why are you trying to trap me, you hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show me the coin that is used to pay the tax.” They brought a denarius coin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A silver Roman coin used for paying the tax imposed by the Romans.">a</a></sup>to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> “Whose image and whose title is inscribed here?” he asked them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> “Caesar's,” they replied.<br />“You should give back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> When they heard Jesus' answer they were stunned. They went away and left him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> Later the same day some Sadducees came to see him. (They are the ones who say there's no resurrection.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> They asked him, “Teacher, Moses said that if a married<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup>man dies without having children, his brother should marry his widow and have children on behalf of his brother.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 25:5-6.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Well, once there were seven brothers here with us. The first married, and died, and since he had no children he left his widow to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The same thing happened to the second and third husband, right up to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> In the end the woman died too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So when the resurrection takes place, whose wife of the seven brothers will she be, for she married all of them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Jesus replied, “Your mistake is you don't know Scripture or what God can do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection people don't marry, and they aren't given in marriage either—they're like the angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> As for the resurrection of the dead—haven't you read what God said to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> ‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’? He's not the God of the dead, but of the living.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 3:6.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> When the crowds heard what he said, they were amazed at his teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> When the Pharisees heard that he'd left the Sadducees speechless, they got together and went to ask some more questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> One of them, who was a legal expert, asked him a question to try to trap him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> “Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the law?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jesus told them, “‘Love the Lord your God in all you think, in all you feel, and in all you do.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 6:5.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the greatest commandment, the first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> The second is just like it, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 19:18.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> All biblical law and the writings of the prophets depend on these two commandments.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> While the Pharisees were gathered there, Jesus asked them a question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> “What do you think about the Messiah?” he asked. “Whose son is he?”<br />“The son of David,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> “But how can David under inspiration call him ‘Lord’?” Jesus asked them. “He says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> ‘The Lord said to my Lord, Sit down at my right hand until I defeat all your enemies.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “place all your enemies under your feet.” Quoting Psalms 110:1.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David called him Lord, how can he be his son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> Nobody could answer him, and from then on nobody dared to ask him any more questions.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
