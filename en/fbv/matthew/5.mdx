<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; 2 Corinthians 7:9–10; John 16:20–22"> Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; Isaiah 29:19; James 3:13"> Blessed are those who are kind<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Meaning meek, mild tempered.">a</a></sup>for they will own the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:14; PSALMS 107:9; John 6:27"> Blessed are those whose greatest desire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “those who are hungry and thirsty.”">b</a></sup>is to do what is right, for they will be satisfied.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Luke 21:17; Mark 13:13"> Blessed are you when people insult you and persecute you, and accuse you of all kinds of evil things because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:23; James 1:2; Colossians 3:24"> Be glad, be really glad, for you will receive a great reward in heaven—for they persecuted the prophets who came before you in just the same way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 14:34–35; Mark 9:49–50"> You are the salt of the earth, but if the salt becomes tasteless,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “useless.”">c</a></sup>how can you make it salty again? <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; John 8:12; Ephesians 5:8–14"> You are the light of the world. A city built on a hill can't be hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Mark 4:21; Luke 8:16"> No one lights a lamp and then puts it under a bucket. No, it's placed on a lamp-stand and it provides light to everyone in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Titus 2:7; John 15:8"> In the same way you should let your light shine before everyone so they can see the good things you do and praise your heavenly Father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Luke 16:17; Galatians 4:4–5"> Don't think I came to abolish the law or the writings of the prophets. I didn't come to abolish them, but to fulfill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:17; Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25"> I assure you, until heaven and earth come to an end, not a single letter, not a single dot of the law will come to an end before everything is fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; James 2:10–11; Matthew 28:20"> So whoever disregards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “invalidates.”">d</a></sup>the least important commandment, and teaches people to do so, will be called the least in the kingdom of heaven; but whoever practices and teaches the commandments will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Philippians 3:9; Romans 9:30–32"> I tell you, unless your moral rightness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Moral rightness”: Literally, “righteousness.” However, this term is little used in general non-church language and so in this translation it is replaced with phrases such as “those who live right” or “those who do good/right” etc. This is more than being correct, it means living a life that is morally right.">e</a></sup>is more than that of the religious teachers and the Pharisees, you can never enter the kingdom of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:17; Exodus 20:13; Numbers 35:30–34"> You've heard that the law said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “You've heard that it was said.” This phrase is used often in this passage by Jesus to refer to the laws found in the Old Testament.">f</a></sup>to the people of long ago: ‘You shall not murder, and anyone who commits murder will be condemned as guilty.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “liable to judgment.” Exodus 20:13or Deuteronomy 5:17.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:14–15; 1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 2:9"> But I tell you, anyone who is angry with his brother will be condemned as guilty. Whoever calls his brother an idiot has to answer to the council,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Probably the Sanhedrin council.">h</a></sup>but whoever verbally abuses others is liable to the fire of Gehenna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Gehenna” is sometimes translated “hell” or “hellfire.” However, “hell” is a concept that is derived from Nordic and Anglo-Saxon mythology and does not parallel the idea here. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 11:25; Leviticus 6:2–6; Matthew 5:24"> If you're at the altar making an offering, and remember that your brother has something against you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:16; Romans 12:17–18; Matthew 18:15–17"> leave your offering on the altar and go and make peace with him first, and afterwards come back and make your offering.</span>

Otherwise your opponent might hand you over to the judge, and the judge will hand you over to the court official, and you will be thrown into jail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:34; Luke 12:59; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> I tell you the truth: you won't get out of there until you've paid every last penny.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:18; Proverbs 6:32; Exodus 20:14"> You've heard that the law said, ‘Don't commit adultery.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 20:14or Deuteronomy 5:18.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; Proverbs 6:25"> But I tell you that everyone who looks lustfully at a woman has already committed adultery with her in his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Mark 9:43–48; Colossians 3:5"> If your right eye leads you to sin, then tear it out and throw it away, because it's better to lose one part of your body than to have your whole body thrown into the fire of Gehenna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:29; 1 Peter 2:8"> If your right hand leads you to sin, then cut it off and throw it away, for it's better for you to lose one of your limbs than for your whole body to go into the fire of Gehenna.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 19:7"> The law also said, ‘If a man divorces his wife, he should give her a certificate of divorce.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 24:1.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 7:3; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 16:18"> But I tell you that any man who divorces his wife except for sexual immorality causes her to commit adultery, and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:21; Leviticus 19:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4–6"> And again, you've heard that the law said to the people of long ago, ‘You shall not perjure yourself. <span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 12:25; Matthew 6:27; Matthew 23:16–21"> Don't even swear by your head, because you're not able to make a single hair white or black.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:12; Colossians 4:6; Colossians 3:9"> Simply say yes or no—more than this comes from the evil one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 24:19–20; Deuteronomy 19:21; Exodus 21:22–27"> You've heard that the law said, ‘An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 21:24; Leviticus 24:20; Deuteronomy 19:21.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–19"> But I tell you, don't resist someone who is evil. If someone slaps you on the right cheek, turn the other cheek to them as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 6:29; 1 Corinthians 6:7"> If someone wants to sue you in court and takes your shirt, give them your coat too.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The coat was a much more valuable article of clothing.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:26; Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21"> If someone demands that you go one mile, go with them two.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Probably referring to a Roman soldier demanding his belongings be carried for him.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 13:16; Luke 6:30–36"> Give to those who ask you, and don't turn away those who want to borrow from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; PSALMS 139:21–22; Galatians 5:13–14"> You've heard that the law said, ‘Love your neighbor and hate your enemy.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 19:18.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–28"> But I tell you, love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 145:9; Luke 6:35; Acts 14:17"> so you may become children of your heavenly Father. For his sun shines on both the good and the bad; and he makes the rain fall on both those who do right and those who do wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:32–35; 1 Peter 2:20–23; Matthew 9:10–11"> For if you only love those who love you, what reward do you have? Don't even the tax collectors do that?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Luke 6:32; 1 Peter 2:20"> If you only speak kindly to your family, what more are you doing than anyone else? Even the heathen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally, “nations,” or “Gentiles.” A common term used in the New Testament to identify non-Jews, those regarded as not following the true God.">q</a></sup>do that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; James 1:4; Leviticus 19:2"> Grow up and become completely trustworthy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally, “perfect, complete, undivided, whole, mature.” The idea here is of a way of life totally dedicated to God rather than focusing on some abstract concept of perfection.
