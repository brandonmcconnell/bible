<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then some Pharisees and religious teachers from Jerusalem came to Jesus and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> “Why do your disciples break the tradition of our forefathers by not washing their hands before they eat a meal?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> “Why do you break God's commandment because of your tradition?” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> “For God said, ‘Honor your father and mother,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 20:12or Deuteronomy 5:16.">a</a></sup>and ‘Those who curse their father or mother should be put to death.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 21:17or Leviticus 20:9.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But you say that if someone tells their father or mother, ‘Whatever you might have expected to get from me is now a gift to God,’ then</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> they don't have to honor their father. In this way you've annulled God's word for the sake of your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> You hypocrites! How well Isaiah described you when he prophesied:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> ‘These people say they honor me but in their minds they don't care about me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “These people honor me with their lips, but their hearts are a long way from me.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> Their worship of me is pointless. What they teach are just man-made requirements.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 29:13.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> He called the crowd over and told them, “Listen, and understand this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> it's not what goes into you through your mouth that defiles you. It's what comes out of your mouth that defiles you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then Jesus' disciples came to him and said, “You do realize that the Pharisees were offended by what you said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> “Every plant my heavenly Father didn't plant will be uprooted,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> “Forget about them—they are blind guides.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to the Pharisees.">e</a></sup>If a blind man leads another blind man, then they'll both fall into a ditch.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> Then Peter asked, “Please tell us what you mean by this illustration.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referring to what Jesus said in verse 11.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> “Haven't you understood this yet?” replied Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> “Don't you see that whatever goes in through the mouth then passes through the stomach, and then leaves the body as waste?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “discharged into the sewer.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But what comes out through the mouth comes from the mind, and that's what defiles you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For what comes from the mind are evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false witness, and blasphemy</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> and those are what defile you. Eating with unwashed hands doesn't defile you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> Jesus left and went to the region of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> A Canaanite woman from that area came, shouting out, “Lord, son of David! Please have mercy on me, because my daughter is suffering badly because she's possessed by a demon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But Jesus didn't reply at all. His disciples came to him and told him, “Tell her to stop following us. All her shouting is really annoying!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> “I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel,” Jesus said to the woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> But the woman came and kneeled before him, and said, “Lord, please help me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> “It's not right to take the children's food and throw it to the dogs,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The word used for dogs indicates pet dogs, or puppies.">h</a></sup>” Jesus told her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> “Yes, Lord, but even dogs are allowed to eat crumbs that fall from their master's table,” she replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; PSALMS 145:19"> “You have great trust in me,” Jesus answered. “Your wish is granted!” And her daughter was healed immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> Jesus returned, passing by the Sea of Galilee. He went into the mountains nearby where he sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> Huge crowds came to him, bringing those who were lame, blind, crippled, dumb, and many others who were sick. They laid them on the ground at his feet, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> The crowd was astounded at what they saw happen: the deaf could speak, the crippled were healed, the lame could walk, and the blind could see. They praised the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Jesus called his disciples over and told them, “I feel really sorry for all these people, because they've been with me now for three days, and they have nothing to eat. I don't want to send them away hungry, in case they faint on their way home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> “Where could we find enough bread here in this desert to feed such a huge crowd?” the disciples responded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> “How many loaves of bread do you have?” Jesus asked. “Seven, and a few small fish,” they replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> Jesus told the crowd to sit down on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> He took the seven loaves and the fish, and after blessing the food he broke it into pieces and gave it to the disciples, and the disciples gave it to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> Everybody ate until they were full, and then they collected the leftovers, filling seven baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Four thousand men ate the food, not counting women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> Then he sent the crowds away, got into a boat, and went to the Magadan region.</span>
