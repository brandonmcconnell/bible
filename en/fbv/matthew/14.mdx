<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the tetrarch<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Tetrarch,” meaning a ruler of a fourth part. In this case it was the region of Galilee.">a</a></sup>heard what Jesus was doing</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and he told his servants, “He must be John the Baptist resurrected from the dead, and that's why he has such powers!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> Herod had detained John, and had him put in chains and imprisoned on account of Herodias, the wife of Philip, his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For John had been telling him, “It's not legal for you to marry her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> Herod wanted to kill John but he was afraid of the people's reaction for they considered John a prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> However, on Herod's birthday the daughter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Usually identified as Salome.">b</a></sup>of Herodias danced for the party, and Herod was delighted with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> So he promised with an oath to give her whatever she wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> Prompted by her mother, she said, “Give me here on a plate the head of John the Baptist.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> Then the king regretted the promise he had made, but because of the oaths he'd made in front of his dinner guests, he gave the order to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> The order was passed on and John was beheaded in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> John's head was brought on a plate and given to the girl, who took it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> John's disciples came and took the body, and buried him. Then they went and told Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> When Jesus heard the news he went away by boat to a quiet place to be alone, but when the crowds learned where he was, they followed him on foot from the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> When Jesus got out of the boat and saw the huge crowds, he was full of sympathy for them, and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> As evening came the disciples went up to him and said, “This place is miles from anywhere and it's getting late. Send the crowds away so they can go to the villages and buy food for themselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus told them, “They don't need to leave. You give them something to eat!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> “All we have here are five loaves and a couple of fish,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> “Bring them to me,” said Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> He told the crowds to sit down on the grass. Then he took the five loaves and the two fish, looked up to heaven and blessed them. After that he broke the loaves into pieces and gave the bread to the disciples, and the disciples gave it to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> Everybody ate until they were full. Then they collected up the leftovers which filled twelve baskets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Some five thousand men ate the food, not counting women and children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> Right after this he made the disciples get into the boat and go back to the other side of the lake while he sent the crowds on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> After he'd sent the crowds away, he went up into the mountains to pray. Evening came and he was there all alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> By now the boat was a long way out from land, being pounded by the waves because the wind was blowing against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> About 3 a.m.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “the fourth watch of the night.”">c</a></sup>Jesus caught up with them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> When the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were terrified. They screamed out in fear, “It's a ghost!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But immediately Jesus said to them, “Don't worry, it's me! Don't be afraid!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> “Lord, if it's really you, tell me to come over to you, walking on the water,” Peter replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> “Come on then,” said Jesus. Peter got out of the boat and walked on the water towards Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when he saw how strong the wind was, he became scared, and started to sink. “Lord! Save me!” he cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; PSALMS 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> Immediately Jesus reached out and grabbed hold of him, and said, “You have such little trust in me. Why did you doubt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> When they got into the boat, the wind died down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; PSALMS 2:7; Romans 1:4"> and those in the boat worshiped him, saying, “You really are the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> After crossing the lake they arrived at Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> When the people there realized Jesus was there, they let everyone in the whole area know. They brought all who were sick to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> and pleaded with him to let the sick just touch the edge of his cloak. Everyone that touched him was healed.</span>
</div>
