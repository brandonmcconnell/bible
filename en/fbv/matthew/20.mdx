<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Songs 8:11–12"> For the kingdom of heaven is like a landowner who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> He agreed to pay the workers one denarius for the day, and sent them to work in his vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> Around 9 a.m. he went out and saw others without work standing in the marketplace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> ‘Go and work in the vineyard too, and I'll pay you what's right,’ he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> So they went to work. Around noon and 3 p.m. he went out and did the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> At 5 p.m. he went out and found others standing there. ‘Why are you standing around all day doing nothing?’ he asked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> ‘Because nobody has hired us,’ they replied. ‘Go and work in the vineyard too,’ he told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> When evening came, the owner of the vineyard told his manager, ‘Call the workers in, and pay them their wages. Begin with the workers hired last and then move on to those hired first.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> When those who were hired at 5 p.m. came in, they each received one denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So when those who were hired first came in, they thought they would get more, but they also received one denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> When they received their pay, they complained to the owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> ‘Those who were hired last only worked for an hour, and you've paid them the same as us who worked the whole day in the burning heat,’ they grumbled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> The owner answered one of them, ‘My friend, I haven't treated you unfairly. Didn't you agree with me to work for one denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take your pay and go. I want to pay those who were hired last the same as I paid you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> Can't I choose to do what I want with my own money? Why should you give me evil looks because I want to do good?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> In this way the last shall be first and the first shall be last.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> On the way to Jerusalem, Jesus took the twelve disciples aside as they walked along and told them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> “Look, we're going to Jerusalem, and the Son of man will be betrayed to the chief priests and religious teachers. They will condemn him to death</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and hand him over to the foreigners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Here referring to the Romans.">a</a></sup>to mock him, whip him, and crucify him. But on the third day he will be raised from the dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then the mother of the sons of Zebedee came with her two sons to Jesus. She kneeled down before him to make a request.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> “What is it you are asking me for?” Jesus said to her.<br />“Please appoint my sons to sit beside you in your kingdom, one on your right and the other on your left,” she asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> “You don't know what you're asking,” Jesus told them. “Are you able to drink the cup<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meaning the cup of suffering.">b</a></sup>I'm about to drink?”<br />“Yes, we are able to do that,” they told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> “You will certainly drink from my cup,” he said to them, “but the privilege to sit on my right or on my left isn't mine to give. My Father is the one who has decided who that will be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “it is for those for whom it has been prepared by my Father.”">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> When the other ten disciples heard what they had asked, they were annoyed with the two brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> Jesus called them together and told them, “You know that foreign rulers lord it over their subjects, and powerful leaders oppress them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> It shall not be like that for you. Whoever among you wants to be the most important will be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> Whoever among you wants to be first will be like a slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> In the same way, the Son of man didn't come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> As they left Jericho, a huge crowd followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> Two blind men were sitting at the side of the road. When they heard that Jesus was passing by, they called out, “Have mercy on us, Lord, son of David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> The crowd told them to be quiet, but they shouted even louder, “Have mercy on us, Lord, son of David!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> Jesus stopped. He called them over, asking, “What do you want me to do for you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> “Lord, please make us able to see,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> Jesus had pity on them and touched their eyes. Immediately they could see, and they followed him.</span>
</div>
</div>
