<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Matthew 19:1"> After Jesus had said all this, he told his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Mark 14:1–2; John 13:1–2"> “You know that it's Passover in two days time, and the Son of man will be handed over to be crucified.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then the chief priests and the elders of the people gathered in the courtyard of Caiaphas, the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:14; PSALMS 2:2; Acts 13:10"> There they plotted to arrest Jesus on some deceitful pretext<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “with guile.”">a</a></sup>and kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:24; Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5"> But they said, “Let's not do this during the festival so that the people don't riot.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> While Jesus was staying at Simon the leper's house in Bethany,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> a woman came over to him carrying an alabaster jar of very expensive perfume. She poured it on Jesus' head while he was sitting eating.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> But when the disciples saw what she did, they were upset.“What a terrible waste!” they objected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> “This perfume could've been sold for a lot of money and given to the poor!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Jesus was aware of what was going on and told them, “Why are you upset with this woman? She's done something wonderful for me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> You'll always have the poor with you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 15:11.">b</a></sup>but you won't always have me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> By pouring this perfume on my body she's prepared me for burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; PSALMS 112:6"> I tell you the truth: wherever in the world this good news is spread, the story of what this woman has done will also be told in memory of her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve disciples, went to the chief priests</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> and asked them, “How much will you pay me for betraying Jesus to you?” They paid him thirty silver coins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> From then on he looked for an opportunity to betray Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> On the first day of the Festival of Unleavened Bread, the disciples came to Jesus and asked him, “Where do you want us to prepare the Passover meal for you to eat?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> Jesus told them, “Go into the city and find this particular man, and tell him that the Teacher says, ‘My time is approaching. I'm coming to celebrate the Passover with my disciples at your house.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> The disciples did as Jesus told them, and prepared the Passover meal there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> When evening came he sat down to eat with the Twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> While they were eating he told them, “I tell you the truth: one of you is going to betray me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> They were extremely upset. One after the other they asked him, “Lord, it's not me, is it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; PSALMS 41:9; John 13:26–28"> “The one who's dipped his hand into the dish with me will betray me,” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> “The Son of man will die just as it was prophesied about him, but what a disaster it will be for the man who betrays the Son of man! It would be better for that man if he'd never been born!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> Judas, the one who would betray Jesus, asked, “It's not me, is it, Rabbi?”<br />“You said it,” Jesus replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> While they were eating, Jesus took some bread and blessed it. Then he broke it and gave pieces to the disciples. “Take and eat this for it is my body,” said Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; PSALMS 116:13"> Then he picked up the cup, blessed it, and gave it to them. “Drink from it, all of you,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> “For this is my blood of the agreement,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">c</a></sup>poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> However, I tell you, I won't drink this fruit of the vine until the day I drink it new with you in the kingdom of my Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> After they'd sung a song, they left for the Mount of Olives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> “All of you will abandon me tonight,” Jesus told them. “As Scripture says, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the flock of sheep will be completely scattered.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Zechariah 13:7.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But after I have risen, I'll go ahead of you to Galilee.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> But Peter objected, “Even if everyone else abandons you, I'll never abandon you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> “I'm telling you the truth,” Jesus told him, “This very night, before the cock crows, you'll deny me three times.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> “Even if I have to die with you, I'll never deny you!” Peter insisted. And all the disciples said the same thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then Jesus went with his disciples to a place called Gethsemane. He told them, “Sit down here while I go over there and pray.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> He took Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with him, and he began to suffer agonizing sorrow and distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then he said to them, “I am so overwhelmed with sadness that it's killing me. Wait here and keep watch with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> He went a little farther forward, fell facedown, and prayed.<br />“My Father, please, if it's possible, let this cup of suffering be taken from me,” Jesus asked. “Even so, may it not be what I want but what you want.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> He went back to the disciples and found them asleep. He said to Peter, “What, you couldn't stay awake with me for just one hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> Stay awake and pray, so that you don't fall into temptation. Yes, the spirit is willing, but the body is weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> He went away a second time and prayed.<br />“My Father, if this cup cannot be taken from me without me drinking from it, then your will be done,” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> He went back and found them sleeping, for they just couldn't stay awake.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “their eyes were weighed down.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> So he left them once more, and went off and prayed a third time, repeating the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then he returned to the disciples, and told them, “How can you still be sleeping and resting? Look, the time has come. The Son of man is about to be betrayed into the hands of sinners!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Get up, let's go! Look, the one who's betraying me has arrived.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> As he said this, Judas, one of the Twelve, arrived with a large mob armed with swords and clubs sent by the chief priests and elders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 55:20–21; PSALMS 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> The betrayer had arranged to give them a signal: “The one that I kiss, that's him—arrest him,” he'd told them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> Judas came up to Jesus immediately, and said, “Hello, Rabbi,” and kissed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; PSALMS 41:9"> “My friend, do what you came to do,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, “What are you doing here?”">f</a></sup>Jesus said to Judas. So they came and grabbed hold of Jesus, and arrested him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> One of those who was with Jesus reached for his sword and pulled it out. He struck the high priest's servant, cutting off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> But Jesus told him, “Put your sword away. Everyone who fights with the sword will die by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Don't you think I could ask my Father, and he'd immediately send more than twelve legions of angels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> But then how could the Scriptures be fulfilled that say it must be like this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> Then Jesus told the mob, “Have you come with swords and clubs to arrest me as if I was some kind of dangerous criminal? Every day I sat in the Temple teaching and you didn't arrest me then.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> But all this is happening to fulfill what the prophets wrote.” Then all the disciples abandoned him and ran away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; PSALMS 56:5–6"> Those who'd arrested Jesus took him to the home of Caiaphas, the high priest, where the religious teachers and elders had gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> Peter followed him at a distance, and went into the high priest's courtyard. He sat there with the guards to see how things would end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> The chief priests and the whole council were trying to find some false evidence against Jesus so they could put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; PSALMS 35:11; PSALMS 27:12"> But they couldn't find anything, even though many false witnesses came forward. Eventually two came forward</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> and reported, “This man said, ‘I can destroy God's Temple, and rebuild it in three days.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> The high priest stood up and asked Jesus, “Have you no answer? What do you have to say in your defense?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Jesus remained silent.<br />The high priest said to Jesus, “In the name of the living God I place you under oath. Tell us if you are the Messiah, the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; PSALMS 110:1"> “You said it,” Jesus replied. “And I also tell you that in the future you'll see the Son of man sitting at the right hand of the Almighty, and coming on the clouds of heaven.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Psalms 110:1and Daniel 7:13.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the high priest tore his clothes, and said, “He's speaking blasphemy! Why do we need any witnesses? Look, now you've heard for yourselves his blasphemy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What's your verdict?”<br />“Guilty! He deserves to die!” they answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then they spat in his face and beat him. Some of them slapped him with their hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> and said, “Prophesy to us, you ‘Messiah’! Who just hit you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> Meanwhile Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard. A servant girl came up to him, and said, “You, you were with Jesus the Galilean!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> But he denied it in front of everyone. “I don't know what you're talking about,” he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> When he went back to the courtyard entrance another servant girl saw him and said to the people there, “This man was with Jesus of Nazareth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> Once again he denied it, saying with an oath, “I don't know him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> A little while later the people standing there came up to Peter and said, “You definitely are one of them. Your accent gives you away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then he started to swear: “Curses on me if I'm a liar!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “he called down curses on himself.”">h</a></sup>I don't know the man!” Immediately the cock crowed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> Then Peter remembered what Jesus had told him: “Before the cock crows, three times you will deny knowing me.” He went outside and wept bitterly.</span>
</div>
