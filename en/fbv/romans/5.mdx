<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Romans 15:13; John 16:33"> Now that we have been made right by God by trusting in him, we have peace with him through our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 12:12"> It's through Jesus, trusting in him, that we've gained access to this position of grace where we now stand, looking forward with happiness and confidence to sharing in God's glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:2–3; James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> Not only this—we also remain confident when problems come, because we know that experiencing problems develops spiritual stamina.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “endurance.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> Spiritual stamina in turn develops a mature character, and this mature character results in confident hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Ephesians 3:16–19; Philippians 1:20"> Since we have this hope we're never disappointed, for God's love has been poured into us through the Holy Spirit he's given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–5; Romans 5:8; Colossians 2:13"> When we were completely helpless, at just the right time Christ died for us ungodly people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:13; 1 John 3:16; Romans 16:4"> Who would die for anybody else, even someone who does what's right? (Though perhaps somebody would be brave enough to die for someone really good.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:13; John 3:16; Romans 5:6"> But God demonstrates his love for us in that Christ died for us while we were still sinners.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 8:1; Ephesians 2:13"> Now that we are made right by his death,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “blood.”">b</a></sup>we can be absolutely sure we'll be saved by him from the coming judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:32"> While we were his enemies, God made us his friends through the death of his Son, and so we can be absolutely sure that he will save us through the life of his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; Romans 5:10; Isaiah 61:10"> In addition to this we now celebrate what God has done through our Lord Jesus Christ to reconcile us and make us his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; Romans 5:19; Romans 6:23"> For through one man sin entered the world, and sin led to death. In this way death came to everyone, for everyone sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56; 1 John 3:4"> Even before the law was given, sin was in the world, however it wasn't counted as sin because there was no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:45; Romans 5:17; Hebrews 9:27"> But death still ruled from Adam until Moses, even over those who didn't sin in the same way as Adam did.<br />Now Adam prefigured the one to come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In other words, Adam was a symbol or type of Jesus who was to come.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:12"> But the gift of Jesus is not like the sin of Adam.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Supplying for clarity “of Jesus” and “of Adam,” making explicit what is meant by gift and sin.">d</a></sup>Though many people died because of one man's sin, God's grace is so much greater and has been shared with so many through his gracious gift in the person of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:38–39; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 44:22"> The result of the gift is not like that of the sin. The result of Adam's sin was judgment and condemnation, but the gift sets us right with God, despite our many sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; 1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 5:12"> As a result of one man's sin, death ruled because of him. But God's grace is so much greater and his gift sets us right, for everyone who receives it will rule in life through the person of Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12; Romans 5:15"> Just as one sin brought condemnation on everyone, in the same way one right act gave everyone the opportunity to live right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 5:18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Philippians 2:8"> Just as through one man's disobedience many became sinners, in the same way through one man's obedience many are made right with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Titus 3:3–7; Romans 6:14"> When the law was introduced, sin became even more obvious. But while sin became much more obvious, grace became even more obvious!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Romans 5:14; Romans 8:10"> Just as sin ruled us and brought us death, now grace rules by making us right with God, bringing us eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
</div>
