<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> Those of us who are spiritually strong ought to support those who are spiritually weak. We shouldn't just please ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> We should all encourage others to do what's morally right, building them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> Christ didn't live to please himself, but as Scripture says of him, “The insults of those who abused you have fallen on me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 69:9.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> These Scriptures were written down in the past to help us understand, and to encourage us so that we could wait patiently in hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> May the God who gives patience and encouragement help you to be in full agreement among yourselves as you follow Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> so that you can with one mind and with one voice together glorify God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> So accept one another, just as Christ accepted you, and give God the glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> I maintain that Christ came as a servant to the Jews<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “of circumcision.”">b</a></sup>to show God tells the truth, keeping the promises he made to their forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; PSALMS 18:49; Romans 3:29"> He also came that foreigners could praise God for his mercy, as Scripture says, “Therefore I will praise you among the foreigners; I will sing praises to your name.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 18:49.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And also: “Foreigners, celebrate with his people!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 32:43.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 117:1"> And again: “All you foreigners, praise the Lord, let all peoples praise him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 117:1.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And again, Isaiah says, “Jesse's descendant will come to rule the nations, and foreigners will put their hope in him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 11:10. “Jesse's descendant.” This refers to Jesse, the father of King David, who was the beginning of the royal line.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> May the God of hope completely fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in him, so that you will overflow with hope through the power of the Holy Spirit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> I'm convinced that you, my brothers and sisters, are full of goodness, and that you are filled with every kind of knowledge, so you are well able to teach one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> I've been blunt in the way I have written to you about some of these things, but this is just to remind you. For God gave me grace</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the foreigners, like a priest sharing God's good news, so that they could become an acceptable offering, made holy by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> So even though I have something to boast about because of my service for God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> (I wouldn't dare talk about any of this except as Christ has done it through me), I have led foreigners to obedience through my teaching and demonstration,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> through the power of signs and miracles done with the Holy Spirit's power. From Jerusalem all the way over to Illyricum, everywhere I've shared fully the good news of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> In fact I was keen to spread the good news in places that hadn't heard the name of Christ, so that I wouldn't be building on what others have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> As Scripture says, “Those who haven't been told the good news will discover him, and those who haven't heard will understand.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 52:15">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> That's why I was prevented so many times from coming to see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now, as there's nowhere left here to work, and since I've looked forward to visiting you for many years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> when I go to Spain I hope to see you when I pass through. Maybe you can give me some help for my journey once we've enjoyed some time together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> At the moment I'm on my way to Jerusalem to help the believers there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> because the believers in Macedonia and Achaia thought it was a good idea to send a contribution to the poor among the believers in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> They were happy to do this because they're in their debt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Meaning that the foreigners are indebted to the Jews for sharing the good news of God. This particular instance applies specifically to the believers in Jerusalem, so the foreigners are happy to send a gift to help them.">h</a></sup>Now that foreigners are sharing their spiritual benefits, they owe it to the Jewish believers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Implied.">i</a></sup>to help them in material things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> So once I've done this, and have safely delivered this contribution to them, I'll visit you on my way to Spain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; PSALMS 16:11"> I know that when I come, Christ will give us his full blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> I want to encourage you, my brothers and sisters through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to join together in praying hard for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> Pray that I may be kept safe from the unbelievers in Judea. Pray that my work in Jerusalem will be welcomed by the believers there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> Pray that I will come to you with gladness, as God wills, so we can enjoy one another's company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> May the God of peace be with you all. Amen.</span>
