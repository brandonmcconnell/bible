<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Romans 6:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> So I encourage you, my brothers and sisters, because of God's compassion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “mercy.”">a</a></sup>for you, to dedicate your bodies as a living offering that is holy and pleasing to God. This is the logical way to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:14; Ephesians 4:22–24; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Don't follow the ways of this world; instead be transformed by the spiritual renewal of your mind so you can demonstrate what God's will really is—good, pleasing, and perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> Let me explain to all of you, through the grace given to me, that no one should think of themselves better than they ought to. You should think about yourselves realistically, according to the degree of trust God has shared with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> Just as there are many parts to the body, but they don't all do the same thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> so we are one body in Christ, even though we are many—and we all belong to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> We each have different gifts that vary according to the grace given to us. So if it's speaking for God, then you should do so depending on how much you trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> If it's the ministry of service then you should serve; if teaching then you should teach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> if encouragement then you should encourage; if giving then you should give generously; if leadership then you should lead with commitment; if being merciful then you should do so gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Love must be genuine. Hate what is evil; hold on tightly to what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> Be completely dedicated to each other in your love as family; value others more than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> Don't be unwilling to work hard; serve the Lord with an enthusiastic spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; PSALMS 62:8"> Remain cheerful in the hope you have, put up with the troubles that come, keep on praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> Share in providing for the needs of God's people, and welcome strangers with hospitality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless those who persecute you—bless them, and don't curse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; PSALMS 35:13–14"> Be happy with those who are happy; cry with those who are crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> Think about one another.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Live in harmony with one another.”.">b</a></sup>Don't consider yourself more important than others; live humbly. Don't be conceited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> Don't pay back anyone evil for evil. Make sure you show everybody that what you're doing is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> and as far as it's up to you, live at peace with everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; Hebrews 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> My dear friends, don't seek revenge, but leave it to God to execute judgment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “give room to wrath.”">c</a></sup>—as Scripture points out, “‘It's for me to dispense justice, I will repay,’ says the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 32:35.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:27"> If those who hate you are hungry, give them food; if they're thirsty, give them a drink; for by doing so you pile fiery coals on their heads.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Meaning they will be caused great embarrassment and shame. The quotation is from Proverbs 25:21-22">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Don't be defeated by evil—conquer evil with good.</span>
