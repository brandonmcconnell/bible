<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Accept those who are still struggling to trust in God, and don't get into arguments over personal opinions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> One person may believe they can eat anything, while another whose trust is weak only eats vegetables.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is nothing to do with the issue of vegetarianism or diet, but with food offered to idols. (Similarly in 1 Corinthians 8.)">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> Those who eat anything must not look down on those who won't, and those who won't eat must not criticize those who do—for God has accepted them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> What right do you have to judge someone else's servant? It's their own master who decides whether they are right or wrong. With the Lord's help they will be able to take their stand for right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> Some people consider some days more important than others, while others consider each day the same. Everyone should be completely convinced in their own minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> Those who respect a special day do so for the Lord; and those who eat without worrying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eating/not eating refers to whether it was right to eat food that had previously been offered to pagan idols.">b</a></sup>do so for the Lord since they give thanks to God; while those who avoid eating certain things do so for the Lord, and they also give thanks to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> None of us live for ourselves, or die for ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> If we live, we live for the Lord, or if we die, we die for the Lord—so whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> This was the reason Christ died and came back to life—so that he could be the Lord of both the dead and the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> So why do you criticize your fellow-believer? Why do you despise your fellow-believer? For all of us will stand before God's throne of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; PSALMS 72:11"> For the Scriptures say, “‘As surely as I am alive,’ the Lord says, ‘Every knee shall bow before me, and every tongue will declare that I am God.’”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Isaiah 45:23.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So every one of us will have to explain ourselves to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Therefore let's not judge each other anymore, but decide to do this instead—we won't put obstacles in the way of fellow-believers, or cause them to fall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I'm certain—persuaded by the Lord Jesus—that nothing in itself is ceremonially unclean. But if someone considers it to be unclean, to them it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> If your fellow-believer is hurt by you over matters of food, then you're no longer behaving in a loving way. Don't destroy someone for whom Christ died by the food you choose to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Don't let the good things you do be misrepresented—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> for God's kingdom is not about eating and drinking, but about living right, having peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> Anyone who serves Christ in this way pleases God, and is appreciated by others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; PSALMS 34:14"> So let's pursue the path of peace, and find ways to encourage each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Don't destroy the work of God with arguments over food. Everything is clean—but it would be wrong to eat and offend others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It's better not to eat meat or drink wine or anything else that would cause your fellow-believer to stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> What you personally believe is between you and God. Happy are those who don't condemn themselves for doing what they think is right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But if you have doubts whether it's right or wrong to eat something, then you shouldn't, because you're not convinced it's right. Whatever isn't based on conviction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “faith.”">d</a></sup>is sin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Sin is doing something you don't believe is right.”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
