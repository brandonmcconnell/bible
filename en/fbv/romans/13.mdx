<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Everyone should obey government authorities, because no one has the power to rule unless God gives permission. These authorities have been put there by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> Whoever resists the authorities opposes what God has put in place, and those who do so shall find themselves judged accordingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers don't frighten those who do right, but those who do wrong. If you don't want to live in fear of the authorities, then do what is right, and you'll have their approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> Those in power are God's servants, put there for your own good. If you do wrong, you should be frightened—it's not without reason that authorities have the power to punish! They are God's servants, punishing offenders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> So it's important to do as you're told, not just because of the threat of punishment, but because of what your conscience tells you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> That's why you have to pay taxes, for the authorities are God's servants taking care of such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Pay whatever you owe: taxes to the tax authorities, fees to the fee-collectors; give respect to those who should be respected, honor those who should be honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Don't owe anybody anything, except love for one another—for those who love their neighbor have kept the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> “You must not commit adultery, you must not kill, you must not steal, you must not jealously want things for yourself”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “covet.” The quotation is from Exodus 20:13-17or Deuteronomy 5:17-21.">a</a></sup>—these and the other commandments are summed up in the statement, “Love your neighbor as yourself.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 19:18.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love doesn't do wrong to anyone,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “doesn't harm anyone.”">c</a></sup>so love fulfils the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> You should do this because you realize how urgent the time is—that it's high time for you to wake up from your sleep. For salvation is closer to us now than when we first put our trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is nearly over, the day is almost here! So let's get rid of our dark deeds and put on the armor of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let's behave properly, showing that we're people who are living in the light. We shouldn't spend our time going to wild parties and getting drunk, or having affairs and acting immorally, or getting into fights and being jealous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> Instead put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and forget about following your sinful desires.</span>
