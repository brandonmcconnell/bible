<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 15:16; 1 Corinthians 1:1"> This letter comes from Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ. I was called to be an apostle by God. God appointed me to announce the good news</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:2; Luke 1:70; Romans 3:21"> that he had previously promised through his prophets in the Holy Scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Galatians 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> The good news is about his Son, whose human forefather was David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 5:5–6; Ephesians 1:19–23"> but who was revealed as God's Son by his resurrection from the dead through the power of the Holy Spirit. He is Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 16:26; Acts 6:7"> It was through him that I received the privilege of becoming an apostle to call all nations to obedient trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 17:14; 2 Peter 1:10"> You are also included among those who were called to belong to Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Jude 1:1–2; Ephesians 1:2"> I'm writing to all of you in Rome who are loved by God, and called to be his special people. Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 16:19; 1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> Let me begin by saying that I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because the way in which you trust in God is spoken about all over the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 2 Timothy 1:3; Ephesians 1:16–19"> I'm always praying for you, as God can confirm—the God I serve with the whole of my being as I share the good news about his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:30–32; Acts 18:21; Philemon 1:22"> In my prayers I'm always asking that I might eventually come and see you, if that's what God wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Romans 16:25; Romans 15:29"> I really want to visit you and share with you a spiritual blessing to strengthen you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:7–10; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> In this way we can be encouraged together by each others' trust in God, both your trust and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 4:17; 2 Timothy 4:17"> I want you to know, my brothers and sisters, I often planned to visit you, but I was kept from coming up till now. I want to see some good spiritual results among you just as I've seen among other people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Gentiles.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–23; Acts 9:15; Colossians 3:11"> For I have an obligation to work for both the civilized and the uncivilized, both the educated and the uneducated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> That's why I'm really keen to come to Rome and share the good news with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 8:38; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 4:16"> I'm certainly not ashamed about the good news, for it's God's power to save everyone who trusts in him—to the Jewish people first, and then to everyone else as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38"> For in the good news God is revealed as good and right,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “God's righteousness.”">b</a></sup>trustworthy from start to finish. As Scripture says, “Those who are right with God live by trusting him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The actual words in the original are, “the right (one) from trust will live.” The quotation is from Habakkuk 2:4.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; Romans 1:32"> God's hostility<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “anger.” There are issues in ascribing negative human emotions to God.">d</a></sup>is revealed from heaven against those who are godless and who are not morally right,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Right”: throughout Romans, Paul is speaking about moral rightness, not about being simply correct.">e</a></sup>those who suppress the truth through the evil that they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:16–17; PSALMS 19:1–6; Romans 1:20"> What can be known about God is obvious, because he has made it very clear to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; PSALMS 19:1–6"> Ever since the creation of the world, the invisible aspects of God—his eternal power and divinity—are clearly visible in what he has made. Such people have no excuse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> because even though they knew God, they did not praise him or thank him, but instead their thinking about God turned into complete foolishness, and darkness filled their empty minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:19–21; Romans 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> Even though they claimed to be wise, they became foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; Acts 17:29"> They exchanged the glory of the immortal God for idols, images of mortal human beings, birds, animals, and reptiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 81:11–12; Acts 7:42; Acts 14:16"> So God abandoned them to the evil desires of their depraved minds, and they did shameful, degrading things to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; 1 John 2:15–16"> They exchanged God's truth for a lie, worshiping and serving creatures instead of the Creator, who deserves praise forever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Romans 1:24"> That's why God abandoned them to their evil desires. Their women exchanged natural sex for that which is unnatural,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; Romans 1:23–24"> and in the same way the men gave up sex with women and burned with lust for each other. Men did indecent things to each other, and as a result they suffered the inevitable consequences of their perversions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Romans 1:21; Titus 1:16"> Since they didn't consider it worthwhile to get to know God, he abandoned them to their worthless, distrustful way of thinking, doing things that should never be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; PSALMS 41:7"> They filled themselves with all that's wrong: evil, greed, hate, envy, murder, quarreling, deception, malice, and gossip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2; Jude 1:16; 2 Peter 2:18"> They're back-stabbers and God-haters. They're arrogant, proud, and boastful. They devise new ways of sinning. They rebel against their parents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; Romans 3:11"> They don't want to understand, they don't keep their promises, they don't show any kindness or compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 2:1–5; Romans 1:21; Romans 6:21"> Even though they realize exactly what God requires, they do things that deserve death. Not only do they do such things themselves, they also support others in doing them.</span>
