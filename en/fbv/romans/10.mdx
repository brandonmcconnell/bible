<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> My brothers and sisters, my heart's desire—my prayer to God—is for the salvation of the people of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> I can testify to their passionate dedication to God, but it's not based on knowing him as he truly is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> They don't understand how God makes us right with him, and they try to make themselves right. They refuse to accept God's way of making people right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the fulfillment of the law. All those who trust in him are made right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> Moses wrote, “Whoever does what is right by obeying the law will live.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Leviticus 18:5.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the attitude of doing right that comes from trust says this: “Don't ask ‘who'll go to heaven?’ (asking to bring Christ down),”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or “‘who'll go to the place of the dead?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “the abyss,” bottomless pit.">b</a></sup>(asking to bring Christ back from the dead).”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 30:12.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> What Scripture actually says is: “The message is very close to you—it's what you talk about and what's in your mind.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="These allusions are to Deuteronomy 30:11-14. They were originally applied to the law, and were to indicate that the law was not distant and unapproachable, clearly denying it was difficult to observe. Paul now applies this to the person of Christ, making it clear that this “message of the law” was fulfilled in him.">d</a></sup>In fact it's this message based on trust that we're presenting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> For if you declare that you accept Jesus as Lord, and you are convinced in your mind that God raised him from the dead, then you will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> Your trust in God makes you right and good, and your declaration of accepting God saves you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> As Scripture says, “Those who trust in him will not be disappointed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 28:16. Disappointed: or “ashamed.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> There's no difference between Jew and Greek—for the same Lord is Lord of everyone, and he gives generously to everyone who asks him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For “everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Joel 2:32.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> But how can people call on someone they don't trust? How can they trust someone they haven't heard about? How can they hear unless they're told?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> How can they go out and tell others unless they're sent? Just as Scripture says, “Those who come bringing the good news are truly welcome!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 52:7.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But not everyone has accepted the good news. As Isaiah asks: “Lord, who trusted in the news they heard from us?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 53:1.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> Trusting in God comes from hearing—hearing the message of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> It's not that they haven't heard. Quite the opposite: “The voices of those speaking for God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Implied.">i</a></sup>have been heard all over the earth—their message went out to the whole world.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="QuotingPsalms 19:4.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> So my question is, “Didn't Israel know?” First of all Moses says, “I'll make you jealous by using people who aren't even a nation; I will make you angry by using ignorant foreigners!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 32:21.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> Then Isaiah said it even more strongly: “I was found by people who weren't even looking for me; I revealed myself to people who weren't even asking for me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="QuotingIsaiah 65:1.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> As God says to Israel, “All day long I reached out my hands to a disobedient and stubborn people.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="QuotingIsaiah 65:2.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
