<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:18–19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 3:13"> So there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 6:14; 2 Corinthians 3:17; Romans 6:18"> The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has set me free from the law of sin and death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 7:18–19"> What the law couldn't do because it was powerless due to our sinful nature,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Sinful nature,” Literally, “flesh,” meaning the sinful, fallen, physical nature of humanity. This word is used frequently in the following verses in contrast to the spiritual nature.">a</a></sup>God was able to do! By sending his own Son in human form, God dealt with the whole problem of sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “by making a sacrifice of himself for sin.”">b</a></sup>and destroyed sin's power in our sinful human nature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Galatians 5:22–25; Colossians 1:22"> In this way we could fulfill the good requirements of the law by following the Spirit and not our sinful nature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; John 3:6"> Those who follow their sinful nature are preoccupied with sinful things, but those who follow the Spirit concentrate on spiritual things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> The sinful human mind results in death, but having the mind led by the Spirit results in life and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 John 2:15–16; James 4:4"> The sinful human mind is hostile to God because it refuses to obey the law of God—in fact it never can,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> and those who follow their sinful nature can never please God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> But you're not following your sinful nature, but the Spirit—if it's true that the Spirit of God is living in you. For those that don't have the Spirit of Christ in them don't belong to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> However, if Christ is in you, even though your body is going to die because of sin, the Spirit gives you life because you're now right with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> The Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you. He who raised Jesus from the dead will also give life to your dead bodies through his Spirit that lives in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:2–15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> So brothers and sisters, we don't have to follow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “have no obligation to.”">c</a></sup>our sinful nature that operates according to our human desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> For if you live under the control of your sinful nature, you're going to die. But if you follow the way of the Spirit, putting to death the evil things you do, then you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; John 1:12; Galatians 3:26"> All those who are led by the Spirit of God are God's children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:5–7; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Corinthians 2:12"> You were not given a spirit to enslave and terrify you once more. No, what you received was the spirit that makes you children in God's family. Now we can shout out, “God is our Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5"> The Spirit himself agrees with us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “our spirit.”">d</a></sup>that we're God's children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 4:7; Galatians 3:29; Ephesians 3:6"> If we're his children, then we're his heirs. We are heirs of God, and heirs together with Christ. But if we want to share in his glory we must share in his sufferings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> Yet I'm convinced that what we suffer in the present is nothing compared to the future glory that will be revealed to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 John 3:2"> All of creation is patiently waiting, longing for God to reveal his children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> For God allowed the purpose of creation to be frustrated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> But creation itself waits in hope for the time when it will be set free from the slavery of decay and share the glorious freedom of God's children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; John 16:21"> We know that all creation groans with longing, suffering birth-pains even up till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> Not only creation, but we too, who have a foretaste of the Spirit, we groan inwardly as we wait for God to “adopt” us—the redemption of our bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For we were saved by hope. Yet hope that's already seen isn't hope at all. Who hopes for what they can already see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; PSALMS 27:14"> Since we're hoping for what we haven't yet seen, we wait for it patiently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> Similarly the Spirit helps us in our weakness. We don't know how to speak with God, but the Spirit himself intercedes with and through us by groans that can't be put into words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 John 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> The one who examines the minds of everyone knows the Spirit's motives,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “the mind of the Spirit.”">e</a></sup>because the Spirit pleads God's cause on behalf of the believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; Genesis 50:20"> We know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, those whom he has called to be part of his plan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4–5; Jeremiah 1:5; Ephesians 1:11"> For God, choosing them in advance, set them apart to be like his Son, so that the Son would be the first of many brothers and sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Romans 8:28; Ephesians 1:11"> Those that he chose, he also called; and those that he called, he also made right; and those that he made right, he also glorified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 118:6; Jeremiah 1:19; PSALMS 56:11"> So what's our response to all this? If God is for us, who can be against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:28; PSALMS 84:11"> God, who did not hold back his own Son, but gave him up for all of us, won't he also freely give us everything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 12:10–11; Isaiah 50:8–9; Isaiah 54:17"> Who can accuse God's special people of anything? It's God who sets us right,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Romans 8:27; Hebrews 9:24"> so who can condemn us? It's Christ Jesus who died—more importantly, who was raised from the dead—who stands at God's right-hand, presenting our case.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 10:28; PSALMS 103:17; John 16:33"> Who can separate us from Christ's love? Can oppression, distress, or persecution? Or hunger, poverty, danger, or violence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 44:22; Isaiah 53:7; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> Just as Scripture says, “For your sake we're in danger of being killed all the time. We're treated like sheep to be slaughtered.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 44:22.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> No—in all that happens to us we're more than conquerors through him who loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 10:28; Romans 14:8; 2 Timothy 1:12"> I'm absolutely convinced that neither death nor life, neither angels nor devils, neither the present nor the future, nor powers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:28–30; Romans 8:35; Romans 5:8"> neither height nor depth, in fact nothing in all creation can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
