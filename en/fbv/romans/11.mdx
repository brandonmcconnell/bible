<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> But then I ask, “Has God rejected his people?” Of course not! I'm an Israelite myself, from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God has not rejected his chosen people. Don't you recall what Scripture says about Elijah? How he complained about Israel to God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they've killed your prophets and destroyed your altars. I'm the only one left, and they're trying to kill me too!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> How did God answer him? “I still have seven thousand left who have not worshiped Baal.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting 1 Kings 19:10-18.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> Today it's just the same: there are still some faithful people left, chosen by God's grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> And since it's through grace, then clearly it's not based on what people do, otherwise grace wouldn't be grace!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> So what do we conclude? That the people of Israel didn't achieve what they were striving for—only the chosen, while the rest became hard-hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> As Scripture says, “God dulled their minds so their eyes could not see and their ears could not hear, to this very day.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referencing either Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 6:9-10; Isaiah 29:10.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; PSALMS 28:4"> David adds, “May their feasts become a trap for them, a net that catches them, a temptation that brings punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> May their eyes become blind so they cannot see, and may their backs always be bent low in dejection.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 69:22-23.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> So am I saying that they stumbled and consequently failed completely? Not at all! But as a result of their mistakes, salvation came to other nations, to “make them jealous.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> Now if even their failure benefits the world, and their loss profits the foreigners, how much more beneficial it would be if they were to completely fulfill what they were meant to be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Implied.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> Now let me speak to you foreigners. Insofar as I'm a missionary to foreigners, I promote what I'm doing</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> that somehow I might make my people jealous and save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> If the result of God's rejection of them is that the world becomes God's friends, the result of God's acceptance of them would be like the dead coming back to life!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> If the first part of the bread dough given as an offering is holy, then so is all the rest; if the roots of a tree are holy, then so are the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> Now if some of the branches have been broken off, and you—a wild olive shoot—have been grafted in, and have shared with them the benefit of nourishment from the olive tree's roots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> then you shouldn't look down on the other branches. If you're tempted to boast, remember it's not you who are supporting the roots, but the roots that are supporting you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> You could make the claim, “Branches were broken off so I could be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> All well and good—but they were broken off because of their failure to trust in God, and you stay there because you trust in God. So don't think highly of yourselves, but be respectful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> because if God didn't spare the original branches, he won't spare you either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> You should recognize both God's kindness and toughness—he was tough on the fallen, but God is kind to you so long as you trust in his kindness—otherwise you'll be removed too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> If they no longer refuse to trust in God, they can be grafted in as well, for God is able to graft them back in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> If you could be cut from a wild olive tree, and then be grafted artificially onto a cultivated olive tree, how much more easily they could be grafted back naturally to their own tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> I don't want you, my brothers and sisters to miss this previously-hidden truth, for otherwise you could become conceited. The people of Israel have become hard-hearted in part, until the process of the foreigners coming in is complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; PSALMS 14:7"> This is how all Israel will be saved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This is not teaching universal salvation, but that at this point the whole of Israel (that is made up of both foreigners and Jews who accept salvation through God's grace) will then be saved.">e</a></sup>As Scripture says, “The Savior will come from Zion, and he will turn Jacob away from his opposition to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> My promise to them is that I'll take away their sins.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Combining Isaiah 59:20-21; Isaiah 27:9.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> Though they are enemies of the good news—and this is to your benefit—they are still the chosen people, and loved because of their forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> God's gifts and his calling can't be withdrawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> At one time you disobeyed God, but now God has shown you mercy as a result of their disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> In just the same way that they're now disobedient as you were, they will also be shown mercy like you received.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For God treated everyone as prisoners because of their disobedience so that he could be merciful to everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; PSALMS 92:5"> Oh how deep are God's riches, wisdom, and knowledge! How incredible his decisions, how unimaginable his methods!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> Who can know God's thoughts? Who can give him advice?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 40:13.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> Who has ever given anything to God that God would be obliged to repay?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Job 41:11.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> Everything comes from him, everything exists through him, and everything is for him. Glory to him forever, Amen!</span>
</div>
</div>
