<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 1 Peter 4:8; Galatians 5:22"> If I were to have eloquence in human languages—even the language of angels—but have no love, then I would only be an echoing gong or a clashing cymbal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:3; 1 John 4:20–21"> If I were to speak prophecies, to know every secret mystery and be completely knowledgeable, and if I were able to have so much faith I could move mountains, but have no love, then I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:14–17; John 15:13; Matthew 23:5"> If I were to donate everything I own to the poor, or if I were to sacrifice myself to be burned as a martyr, and have no love, then I gain nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 5:21–22"> Love is patient and kind. Love is not jealous. Love is not boastful. Love is not proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:3–5; 1 John 3:16–17"> Love does not act improperly, or insist on having its own way. Love is not argumentative and doesn't keep a record of wrongs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 John 1:4"> Love takes no delight in evil but celebrates the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4; 2 Timothy 2:24"> Love never gives up, keeps on trusting, stays confident, and remains patient whatever happens.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Galatians 5:6"> Love never fails. Prophecies will come to an end. Tongues will become silent. Knowledge will become useless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 2:2–3; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 2:9"> For our knowledge and our prophetic understanding are incomplete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; 2 Corinthians 5:7–8"> But when completeness comes, then what is incomplete disappears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2; Galatians 4:1"> When I was a child, I spoke like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. When I grew up I left behind such childlike ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 3:2; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–10"> At the moment we peer into a mirror's dim reflection, but then we shall see face to face. For now I only have partial knowledge, but then I shall know completely, just as I am completely known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:14; Colossians 3:14; 1 John 4:7–18"> Trust, hope, and love last forever—but the most important is love.</span>
</div>
