<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="1CO" data-book-slug="1-corinthians" data-book-name="1 Corinthians" data-chapter="9" data-verses="27">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Haven't I seen Jesus our Lord? Aren't you the results of my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> Even if I'm not an apostle to others, at least I am one to you. The proof of my being an apostle of the Lord is you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> Here is my reply to those who question me about this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Don't we have the right to be provided with food and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Don't we have a right to be accompanied by a Christian wife, like the rest of the apostles, the Lord's brothers, and Peter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Is it only Barnabas and myself who have to work to support ourselves?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The original is given in terms of a double negative. The suggestion is that only Paul and Barnabas are not given the privilege of not having to work.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Which soldier ever had to pay his own salary? Who plants a vineyard and doesn't get to eat its fruits? Who feeds a flock and doesn't consume its milk?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> Am I just speaking from a human point of view? Doesn't the law say the same thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; PSALMS 104:27"> In the law of Moses it's written, “Don't muzzle the ox when it's threshing out the grain.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Deuteronomy 25:4.">b</a></sup>Was God just thinking about oxen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> Wasn't he directing this at us? Surely this was written for us—for anyone who plows should plow in hope and anyone who threshes should hope to share in the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we sowed what is spiritual with you, does it really matter if we reap some material benefit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others exercise this right over you, don't we deserve it even more? Even so we did not exercise this right. On the contrary we would rather put up with anything than hold back the gospel of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Don't you know that those who work in temples receive their meals from temple offerings, and those who serve at the altar receive their portion of the sacrifice on the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> In just the same way the Lord ordered that those who announce the good news should live from supporters of the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have not made use of any of these provisions, and I'm not writing about this to suggest it should be done in my case. I'd rather die than to have anyone take away my pride in not having received any benefit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> I have nothing to boast about in sharing the good news because it's something I feel compelled to do. In fact it's dreadful for me if I don't share the good news!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> If I'm doing this work because of my own choice, then I have a reward. But if it wasn't my choice, and an obligation was placed on me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> then what reward do I have? It's the opportunity to share the good news without charging for it, not demanding my rights as a worker for the good news.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> Even though I am free and serve no one, I have placed myself in service to everyone so that I might gain more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> To the Jews I behave like a Jew so that I might win Jews. To those who are under the law I behave as someone under the law (even though I am not obligated under the law), so that I might win those under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> To those who don't operate according to the law,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Meaning those who are not Jews, who do not observe the law of Moses.">c</a></sup>I behave like them (though not disregarding God's law, but operating under the law of Christ), so that I might gain those who don't observe the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> To those who are weak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Probably referring to the “weaker believer” issue mentioned in 8:7and on.">d</a></sup>I share in their weakness so that I may win the weak. I have ended up being “everyman” to everyone so that by using every possible means I might win some!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> I do all this for the sake of the good news so that I too may share in its blessings!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Wouldn't you agree that there are many runners in a race, but only one gets the prize? So run your best, so you may win!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Every competitor who participates in the games maintains a strict training discipline. Of course they do so to win a crown that doesn't last. But our crowns will last forever!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> That's why I run straight in the right direction. I fight accurately, not punching the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> I also treat my body severely to bring it under my control, for I don't want somehow to be disqualified after sharing the good news with everybody else.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
