<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> Now I want to remind you about the good news I announced to you. You accepted it, and you have stood firm for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> It is through this good news that you are saved if you hold on to the message that I gave you. Otherwise you trusted for nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> I passed on to you what I myself had also received, a message of vital importance: that Christ died for our sins, according to Scripture;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; PSALMS 16:10–11"> he was buried and was raised from the dead on the third day, again in accordance with Scripture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> He appeared to Peter, then to the Twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> After that he appeared to more than five hundred brothers and sisters at the same time, most of whom are still alive today, though some have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> He appeared to James, then all the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> Last of all, he also appeared to me, someone born as it were at the wrong time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> For I'm the least important apostle of all, not even fit to be called an apostle since I persecuted God's church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> But by God's grace I am what I am, and his grace given to me wasn't wasted. On the contrary I've worked harder than all of them—though not me, but God's grace working through me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> So whether it's I or they, this is the message we shared with you that brought you to trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> Now if the message declares that Christ has been raised from the dead, how is it that some of you say there's no resurrection of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> If there's no resurrection of the dead then Christ hasn't been raised either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> And if Christ isn't raised, then our message we shared with you is pointless, and your trust in God is pointless too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> In addition, we would be shown to be false witnesses of God when we testified that God raised Christ from the dead. But God didn't raise Christ from the dead if it's true that there's no resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If the dead are not raised, then Christ hasn't been raised either,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> and if Christ hasn't been raised, then your trust in God is useless, and you are still in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> This also means that those who died in Christ are lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> If our hope in Christ is only for this life, we're the most pitiful people of all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> But Christ has been raised from the dead, the firstfruits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word “firstfruits” refers to the first sample of a harvest that was given as an offering to God, also seen as a guarantee of a successful harvest. Christ as the firstfruits therefore means he is both the initial harvest from the dead and the guarantee of their resurrection.">a</a></sup>of the harvest from those who have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> Just as death came through a man, the resurrection of the dead came through a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> Just as in Adam all die, so in Christ all will be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> But each in their own turn: Christ the firstfruits, then those who belong to Christ when he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> After this comes the end, when Christ hands over the kingdom to God the Father, having destroyed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Destroyed in the sense of ending their power.">b</a></sup>all rulers, authorities, and powers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; PSALMS 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> Christ has to rule until he has put all his enemies under his feet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Meaning they have been conquered and humbled.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> The last enemy that will be destroyed is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> As Scripture says, “He put everything under his feet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 8:6.">d</a></sup>(Of course when it says “everything” is put under him it's obvious this doesn't refer to God who placed everything under Christ's authority.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> When everything has been placed under Christ's authority, then the Son will also place himself under God's authority, so that God who gave the Son authority over everything may be all in all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“All in all.” Various explanations have been given for this phrase. It is obviously referring to the completeness of God's rulership in the Universe, and is perhaps best translated literally from the original as “all in all.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Otherwise what will those people do who are baptized for the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why then would people be baptized for them?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The theological meaning of this verse is much debated. However, the actual words are translated simply enough.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> As for us, why do we place ourselves in danger hour after hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> I die every day—let me say it bluntly, my brothers and sisters. This is just as sure as the pride I have for what Christ Jesus has done in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> Humanly speaking, what would I gain by fighting with those people in Ephesus who were like wild animals, if the dead are not raised? If the dead are not raised, “let's eat and drink, for tomorrow we die”!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 22:13.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Don't be fooled: “Bad company ruins good character.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Come to your senses as you should, and stop sinning! Some of you don't know God. I tell you this to shame you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> Of course somebody will ask, “How exactly are the dead raised? What kind of body will they have?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> What a foolish question! What you sow doesn't sprout into life unless it dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> When you sow, you don't sow the plant it will grow into, just the bare seed, whether wheat or whatever you're planting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> God makes the plant grow into the form he has chosen, and different seeds produce different plants with different forms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> What living things are made from is not the same. Human beings have one kind of body tissue, while animals have another, birds another, and fish another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies. Heavenly bodies have one kind of beauty, earthly bodies another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; PSALMS 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> The sun shines in one way, and the moon another, while the stars are different again, with each one shining in a different way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> It's the same with the resurrection of the dead. The body is sown in decay; it is raised to last forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> It is sown in shame; it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness; it is raised in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> It is sown as a natural body; it is raised as a spiritual body. Just as there are natural bodies there are spiritual bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> As Scripture says, “The first man, Adam, became a living being”;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 2:7.">h</a></sup>but the last Adam a life-giving spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> The spiritual did not come first, but the natural—the spiritual came after that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man is from the dust of the earth; the second man is from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> Earthly people are like the man made from the earth; heavenly people are like the man from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> Just as we bore the likeness of the earthly man so we shall bear the likeness of the man from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> However, I tell you this, my brothers and sisters: our present bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “flesh and blood.”">i</a></sup>cannot inherit the kingdom of God. These decaying bodies cannot inherit what lasts forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Listen, I'm going to reveal a mystery! Not all of us will die—but we will all be changed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in a moment, in the blink of an eye, at the sound of the last trumpet. The trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised never to die again, and we will be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> For this perishable body must be clothed with a body that never perishes. This mortal life must be clothed with immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> When this perishable body has been clothed with a body that never perishes, and this mortal life has been clothed with immortality, then the Scripture will come true that says, “Death has been totally conquered and destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; PSALMS 89:48; Acts 2:27"> Death—where's your victory? Death—where's your sting?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Referencing Isaiah 25:8; Hosea 13:14.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> The sting that causes death is sin; and the power of sin is the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> but praise God who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> So my dear brothers and sisters: be strong, stand firm, doing everything you can for the Lord's work, since you know that nothing you do in the Lord is wasted.</span>
</div>
