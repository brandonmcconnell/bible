<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> Now about “food sacrificed to idols.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Paul continues answering the issues the Corinthians have raised.">a</a></sup>So “we all have knowledge” about this subject. Knowledge makes us proud, but love strengthens us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> If anyone thinks they know anything, they don't know as they really should know!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> But whoever loves God is known by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; 1 Corinthians 10:19–20"> So regarding eating food sacrificed to idols: we know that there are no such things as idols in the world, and that there is only one real God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> Even though there are some things called “gods,” whether in heaven or on earth—in fact there are many “gods” and “lords.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> But for us there is only one God, the Father, from whom everything was made, and he is the goal of our existence; and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom everything was made, and he is the means of our existence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This is a complex verse the meaning of which is much debated. It is seen as an early “creed” or declaration, identifying God as Creator and Re-creator, the focus of our lives. It literally says, “But to us one God the Father, from whom the all and we into him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, through whom the all and we through him.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> But not everyone has this “knowledge.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Paul takes issue with this knowledge being misapplied, as seen in verse 10 when it could be seen as being proud and arrogant.">c</a></sup>Some who up to now have been so used to idols as a reality that when they eat food sacrificed to an idol, their conscience (which is weak) tells them they have defiled themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> But food doesn't gain us God's approval! If we don't eat this food, we're not bad, and if we do eat this food, we're not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> Just take care not to use this freedom you have to eat food sacrificed to idols to become offensive to those with a weaker attitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> If another believer sees you who have such “better knowledge”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See under 8:7.">d</a></sup>eating food in an idol temple, won't his weak conscience be convinced to eat food sacrificed to idols?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In other words, deciding to follow another's example, while still believing it is a sin.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> By your “better knowledge” the weaker believer is destroyed, a believer for whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> In this way you sin against other believers, wounding their weaker consciences, and you sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 14:21; 2 Corinthians 6:3; 2 Corinthians 11:29"> So if eating food sacrificed to idols would cause my fellow believer to stumble, I will never eat such meat ever again, so that I don't offend any believer.</span>
