<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> My brothers and sisters, I couldn't talk<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Presumably while he was visiting them previously.">a</a></sup>with you as spiritual believers, but as worldly people—like baby Christians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> I gave you milk—I couldn't give you solid food to eat, because you weren't ready for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> Even now you're not ready for it, because you're still worldly. Since you're still jealous and argumentative, doesn't that show you're worldly, behaving just like ordinary people do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> When one of you says, “I follow Paul,” while another says, “I follow Apollos,” doesn't that show you're being so very human?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> Who is Apollos, anyway? And who is Paul? We're just servants through whom you believed. Each of us does the work God gave us to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I did the planting, Apollos did the watering—but it was God who made you grow!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; PSALMS 115:1"> So the one who does the planting doesn't count for anything, any more than the one who does the watering. The only one who matters is God who is growing you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> The one planting and the one watering have the same goal, and both will be rewarded according to what they've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> We are workers together with God—and you are God's field, his building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> Through the grace of God that he gave to me, I laid down the foundation like a skilled building supervisor. Now someone else is building on it. Whoever does the building needs to watch what they're doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> For no one can lay any other foundation than what's already laid—that is Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> Those who build on that foundation may use gold, silver, precious stone, wood, hay, or straw—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> but whatever is used to build with will be exposed. For on the Day of Judgment, fire will reveal it and test it. Everyone's work will be shown for what it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Those whose building survives will be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> Those whose building is burned up will lose out. They will still be saved, but it will be like passing through fire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Don't you know you are God's temple and that God's Spirit lives in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> Anyone who destroys God's temple will be destroyed by God, for God's temple is holy, and you are the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Don't deceive yourselves. If there's any one of you who thinks they're worldly wise, they should become fools so they can become truly wise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; PSALMS 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> This world's wisdom is plain foolishness to God. As Scripture says, “He uses the cleverness of the wise to catch them out,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Job 5:13.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> and “The Lord knows the arguments of the wise are pointless.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 94:11.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> So don't boast about people. For you have everything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> whether it's Paul or Apollos or Peter—or the world, or life, or death, or the present, or the future. You have everything—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> and you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.</span>
