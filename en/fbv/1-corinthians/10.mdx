<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; PSALMS 78:13–14"> Now I want to explain this to you, brothers and sisters. Our forefathers lived under the cloud, and they all passed through the sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The cloud of God's presence, and the passage through the Red Sea.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> Symbolically then they were baptized “into Moses” in the cloud and in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> They all ate the same spiritual food</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> and drank the same spiritual drink, for they “drank from a spiritual rock” that accompanied them. That rock was Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> However, God wasn't happy with most of them, and they perished in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> Now these experiences are examples to us to show us we should not desire what is evil, as they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; PSALMS 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> You must not worship idols, as some of them did, as it's recorded in Scripture: “The people feasted and drank, and indulged in pagan worship.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Exodus 32:6.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; PSALMS 106:29"> We must not commit sexual sins, as some of them did, and as a result 23,000 died in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; PSALMS 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> Nor should we push God to the limit, as some of them did, and were killed by snakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> Don't complain at God, as some did, and died at the hands of the destroying angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> All the things that happened to them are examples to us and were written down to warn us, we who are living close to the end of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> So if you think you're strong enough to stay standing—be careful you don't fall!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> You won't experience any greater temptation than anyone else, and God is trustworthy. He won't allow you to be tempted more than you can bear, and when you are tempted, he will provide for you a way out so you can stay strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> So my good friends, stay away from idol worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I'm talking to sensible people, so you decide whether I'm telling the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> When we give thanks for the cup we use in the Lord's Supper, don't we share in the blood of Christ? When we break the communion bread, don't we share in the body of Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> By eating from one loaf of bread, we show that even though we are many, we are one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Look at the people of Israel. Don't those who eat the sacrifices made on the altar share together?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> What am I saying then? That anything sacrificed to idols means anything, or that an idol has any real existence? Of course not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; PSALMS 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> The pagans are sacrificing to demons, and not to God. I don't want you to have anything to do with demons!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You can't drink the Lord's cup as well as the cup of demons; you can't eat from the Lord's table as well as the table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Are we trying to make the Lord jealous? Are we stronger than he is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> Some say, “I'm free to do anything”—but not everything is appropriate! “I'm free to do anything”—but not everything is constructive!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 6:12.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> You shouldn't look out for yourself, but for your neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Eat whatever is sold in the market without asking questions because of your conscience,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This refers back to the issue of food sacrificed to idols">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 24:1; Exodus 19:5; PSALMS 50:12"> for “the earth and everything in it belong to God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 24:1">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> If a non-Christian invites you to a meal, and you feel like going, eat whatever you are served, without asking questions because of your conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if someone tells you, “This food has been sacrificed to idols,” don't eat it for the sake of the one who mentioned it and for conscience' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> His conscience I mean, not yours. For why should my freedom be determined by someone else's conscience?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This question seems to be at odds with the previous verse. Paul is arguing for tolerance—both of the one who is offended at eating meat sacrificed to idols, and the other who sees no problem with it since the “gods” of idols do not exist.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I choose to eat with thankfulness, why should I be criticized for eating what I'm thankful to God for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, make sure to do everything to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Don't cause offense, whether it's to Jews or Greeks or the church of God—</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> just as I try to please everybody in all I do. I don't think of what benefits me, but what benefits others, so that they may be saved.</span>
</div>
