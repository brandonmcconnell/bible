<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:9"> So think of us as Christ's servants given the responsibility for “the mysteries of God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Again, in the NT mysteries are the revealed truths of God.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> More than this, those who have such responsibilities are required to be trustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> Personally it hardly matters to me if you or anyone else judges me—in fact I don't even judge myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; PSALMS 130:3"> I don't know of anything I've done wrong, but that doesn't make me morally right. It's the Lord who judges me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> So don't judge anything before the right time—when the Lord comes. He will bring to light all the darkest secrets that are hidden, and he will reveal people's motives. God will give everyone whatever praise they deserve.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> Now, brothers and sisters, I have applied this to Apollos and myself as an example for you. That way you will learn not to go beyond what has been written, and not in arrogance prefer one over the other.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The meaning of original is debated. This is shown in the many differences in translations.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> Who made you so special? What do you have that you weren't given? Since you were given it, why do you proudly claim you weren't?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> You think you have all you need. You think you're so wealthy. You think you're kings already, and don't need us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “without us.”">c</a></sup>I wish you were really ruling as kings, so we could rule with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> The way I see it, God has put us apostles on display as the last in the line, condemned to die. We have been made a public show before the entire universe, to angels and to human beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> We're Christ's fools, but you are so wise in Christ! We're the weak ones, but you are so strong! You have the glory, but we are despised!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="From the context Paul appears to be speaking rather ironically.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> Right up till now we're hungry and thirsty. We have no clothes. We're badly beaten up, and we have no place to call home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> We struggle on doing manual work. When people curse us, we bless them. When they persecute us, we put up with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> When they insult us, we respond with kindness. Even now we are treated like dirt, the worst trash in the whole world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> I'm not writing like this to make you feel ashamed, but to caution you as my children whom I love so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> Even though you may have thousands of Christian instructors, you don't have many fathers—it was in Christ Jesus that I became your father when I shared the good news with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> So I'm pleading with you to imitate me!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> That's why I sent Timothy to you, my trustworthy son in the Lord whom I love so much. He will remind you about the way I follow Christ, just as I always teach in every church I visit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> Some among you have become arrogant, thinking I wouldn't bother coming to see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> But I am coming to visit you soon, if that's what the Lord wants. Then I'll find out what these arrogant people are saying, and what kind of power they have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> For the kingdom of God is not about mere words, but about power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> So what do you want? Shall I come with a stick to beat you, or in love and a gentle spirit?</span>
</div>
</div>
