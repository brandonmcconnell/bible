<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15; Galatians 6:1–2"> Hand over this man to Satan so his sinful nature may be destroyed and he himself be saved on the day of the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is not to suggest that Satan “cooperates” in the salvation process. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 15:33; James 4:16"> You shouldn't be proud about this. Don't you know that it only takes a little yeast to make the whole batch of dough rise?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, it only takes a small amount of sin to infect the whole church.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 4:22; Colossians 3:5–9"> Get rid of this old yeast so that you can be a new batch of dough to make bread without yeast. Christ our Passover lamb has been sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 12:15"> So let us celebrate this festival<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="At the time of the Passover the Jews ate bread made without yeast, and threw out all the yeast from their homes. Paul is using this imagery to say that the yeast of sin needs to be removed just as the symbol of sin (yeast) was removed at the time of the sacrifice of the Passover.">c</a></sup>, not with old yeast or the yeast of evil and wickedness, but with the bread made without yeast, the bread of sincerity and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> In my previous letter I wrote that you should not associate with immoral people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; John 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> I wasn't referring to the immoral people of this world, those who are greedy and cheat others, or those who worship idols, otherwise you'd have to leave this world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> What I meant when I wrote was that you shouldn't associate with anyone who is called a Christian who is immoral or greedy or worships idols; or who is abusive or a drunkard or who cheats others. Don't even share a meal with anyone like that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> It's not my place to judge those outside the church. But shouldn't you judge those who are inside the church?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 21:21"> God judges those that are outside the church. “Expel the wicked from among you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is a quotation from Deuteronomy, repeated in several places: Deuteronomy 13:5, Deuteronomy 17:7, Deuteronomy 19:19, Deuteronomy 22:24, Deuteronomy 24:7.">d</a></sup></span>
