<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> Now about “spiritual gifts.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Paul takes up another issue that the Corinthians have asked him about.">a</a></sup>My brothers and sisters, I want to explain this to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; PSALMS 115:5"> You know that when you were pagans, you were deceived, being led astray by worshiping idols who couldn't even speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> Let me make it clear to you: no one who speaks in the Spirit of God says, “Curse Jesus!” and no one can say, “Jesus is Lord!” except by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> Now there are different kinds of spiritual gifts, but they come from the same Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> There are different kinds of ministries,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “service.”">b</a></sup>but they come from the same Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> There are different ways of working, but they come from the same God, who is at work in all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> The Spirit is given to each of us and is revealed for the good of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; PSALMS 143:10"> One person is given by the Spirit the ability to speak words of wisdom. Another is given a message of knowledge by the same Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> Another receives the gift of strongly trusting in God by the same Spirit. Another receives gifts of healing from that one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> Another is given the ability to perform miracles. Another receives the gift of prophecy. Another is given the gift of spiritual discernment. Another receives the ability to speak different languages, while another is given the gift of interpreting languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> But all of these gifts are the work of the one and the same Spirit, sharing with each person as he alone chooses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Just like the human body is one unit but has many parts—all the parts of the body even though there are many of them, make up one body—so is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For it was through one Spirit that we were all baptized into one body. It doesn't matter whether we are Jews or Greeks, slave or free—we all were given the one Spirit to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> The body is not made of one part, but many parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foot were to say, “Because I'm not a hand, I'm not part of the body,” would that make it not part of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> If the ear were to say, “Because I'm not an eye, I'm not part of the body,” would that make it not part of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; PSALMS 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body was an eye, how could you hear anything? If the whole body was an ear, how could you smell anything?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But God has arranged each part in the body, every last one of them, placing them just as he wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If they were all the same part, what would happen to the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> However, since there are many parts, they make up the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> The eye can't tell the hand, “I don't need you,” or the head tell the feet, “I don't need you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> Quite the opposite: some of those parts of the body that seem the most insignificant are the most essential.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> In fact those parts of the body we do not consider to be decent to reveal we “honor” more by covering them up—what is indecent we treat with greater modesty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> What's presentable doesn't need such covering up. God has so arranged the body that more honor is given to the parts that are less presentable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> This is so there wouldn't be any conflict within the body—the different parts should care equally for each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> So when one part is suffering, all the other parts of the body suffer with it, and when one part is treated well, then all the other parts of the body are happy too!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Here Paul seems to be thinking far more of the body of the church than a physical body.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Now you are the body of Christ, and each one makes up a part of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> In the church, God has arranged first for some to be apostles, secondly for some to be prophets, thirdly teachers. Then there are those who perform miracles, those with healing gifts, those who can help others, those good at administration, and those who can speak different languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> Not everyone is an apostle, or a prophet, or a teacher, or able to perform miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> Not all have healing gifts, or the ability to speak languages, or to interpret languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> But you should really want to have the most significant gifts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Having discussed the different spiritual gifts, Paul states that believers should desire the most important ones. Of course which these are would be a matter of much debate. What he really is doing is setting the scene for the next chapter, for without love none of these gifts, even those considered the most significant, are worth anything.">d</a></sup>So now I will show you a far better way.</span>
</div>
