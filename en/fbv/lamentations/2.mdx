<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; PSALMS 132:7; Matthew 11:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the Lord has spread a cloud of his anger over the Daughter of Zion! He has thrown down Israel's glory from heaven to earth. He has deserted his Temple</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Temple”: literally, “footstool,” see for example Psalms 99:5, Psalms 132:7.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the time he became angry.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; PSALMS 89:39–40; Lamentations 3:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has destroyed without mercy all the places where the descendants of Jacob lived. In his anger he has torn down the fortresses of the Daughter of Judah. He has demolished and degraded the kingdom and its leaders.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 74:11; PSALMS 75:10; PSALMS 75:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his fury he has destroyed all of Israel's power, refusing to help as the enemy attacked. He was like a fire blazing in the land of Jacob, burning everything up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:5; Lamentations 3:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has fired arrows from his bow as if he were their enemy; he has used his power against them like an attacker. He has killed all the beloved children; he has poured out his anger like fire on the tent of the Daughter of Zion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:2; Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 52:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has become like an enemy, completely destroying Israel and its palaces, demolishing its fortresses, making the Daughter of Judah cry and mourn more and more.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 1:4; Zephaniah 3:18; Lamentations 4:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has torn down his Temple as if was a garden shed; he has destroyed his meeting place. The Lord has made Zion forget about her religious festivals and Sabbaths. In his fury he has disowned both king and priest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Amos 2:5; PSALMS 74:3–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has discarded his altar; he has abandoned his Temple. He has handed it</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“It”: literally, “the walls of her palaces,” taken to refer to the structure of the Temple.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">over to the enemy. They were shouting triumphantly in the Lord's Temple just as worshipers did on festival days.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Isaiah 34:11; Isaiah 3:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord was determined to destroy the defensive walls of the Daughter of Zion. He measured out</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Measured out”: literally, “stretched out a measuring line.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">what he would destroy, and didn't hesitate to do it. He made the battlements and walls weep; they both disintegrated.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:26; Hosea 3:4; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her gates have collapsed to the ground; he has destroyed their bars, breaking them apart. Her king and her princes have been exiled to other countries. No one follows the Law anymore, and even her prophets no longer receive visions from the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 15:3; Job 2:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The elders of the Daughter of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have thrown dust over their heads and put on clothes made of sackcloth. The young women of Jerusalem have bowed down, their heads to the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:20; Job 16:13; Lamentations 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">My eyes are worn out from crying; inside I am in turmoil. I'm sick and exhausted over the destruction of Jerusalem, the daughter of my people, because children and infants are fainting in the city streets.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Ezekiel 30:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They cry out to their mothers, “We need food and drink!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“We need food and drink!” literally, “Where is the grain and wine?” an unlikely diet for infants.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fainting in the city streets as if they're wounded, their lives fading away in their mothers' arms.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Jeremiah 8:22; 2 Samuel 5:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">What can I tell you? What shall I compare you to, Daughter of Jerusalem? What can I say you're like in order to comfort you, virgin Daughter of Zion? For your wound gapes as wide as the sea—who could ever heal you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Isaiah 58:1; Jeremiah 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The visions of your prophets were false and worthless; they didn't point out your guilt to prevent you being taken into captivity. Instead they presented to you visions that were false and deceptive.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; PSALMS 48:2; PSALMS 50:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who passes by mock you, clapping their hands, hissing and shaking their heads in scorn at the Daughter of Jerusalem. “Is this really the city that people called the perfection of beauty, the joy of all the earth?” they ask.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 35:21; Lamentations 3:46; Job 16:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">All your enemies open their mouths at you, hissing and gnashing their teeth, “We've swallowed her up! This is the day we've been waiting for. Now it's here and we've seen it happen!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 89:42; Ezekiel 5:11; Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has achieved what he decided; he has done what he warned he would. Just as he determined to do long ago, he has destroyed you, showing no mercy. He has allowed the enemy to gloat over your defeat, and he has given power to those who attack you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:145; Jeremiah 9:1; Lamentations 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people cry out to the Lord from the bottom of their hearts. Daughter of Zion, let your walls stream down with tears like a river all day and night. Don't stop, go on crying!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; PSALMS 119:147–148; Isaiah 26:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Get up and cry out as the night begins. Pour out your sad thoughts like water before the Lord. Hold up your hands to him in prayer for the lives of your children who are fainting from hunger at every street corner.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10; PSALMS 78:64"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, please think about it! Who have you ever treated this way? Should women have to eat their children, the little ones they love? Should priests and prophets be killed in the Lord's Temple?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Lamentations 3:43; Zechariah 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The dead, young and old, lie together in the dust of the streets. My young men and young women, have been killed by the sword. You killed them when you were angry; you have slaughtered them without mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:25; PSALMS 31:13; Jeremiah 16:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You invited enemies to attack me from every direction like you were inviting them to a religious festival. At the time when the Lord was angry nobody escaped, nobody survived. My enemy has destroyed the children I looked after and loved.</span></span>
