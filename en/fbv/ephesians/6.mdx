<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, do what your parents tell you, for this is the right thing to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; Matthew 15:4–6"> “Honor your father and mother.” This is the first commandment that has a promise attached:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> “that things may go well for you and you may live a long life on the earth.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Exodus 20:12or Deuteronomy 5:16.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> Fathers, don't make your children mad, but look after them by disciplining them and instructing them about God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–21; Titus 2:9–10; Colossians 3:17–24"> Servants, obey those who are your human masters, with proper respect and awe, doing things from sincere motives as if you were serving Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:22–23; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:10"> Don't just work well when you're being watched to gain approval, but work like servants of Christ, honestly doing God's will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:5–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> happily serving as if you were doing it for the Lord, and not for people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Luke 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> You know that whoever does what's good will be rewarded by the Lord, whether that person is a servant or someone who is free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:25–1; Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11"> Masters, you should treat your servants the same way. Don't threaten them, remember that the Lord in heaven is both their master and yours, and he treats people the same, without favoritism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> Lastly, stay strong in the Lord, and in his mighty power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> Put on all the armor of God so you can withstand all the devil's attacks!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> We're not fighting against human forces, but against supernatural rulers and powers, the dark lords of this world, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> Take all the weapons God provides so that you may be able to stand your ground in the day of evil and after doing all you can, to find yourself still standing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> So stand up, wrap the belt of truth around your waist, tie on the breastplate of justice and the right,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and put on the shoes of readiness to share the good news of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> Above all, take up the shield of trusting God, by which you'll be able to put out all the devil's flaming arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> Wear the helmet of salvation, and carry the sword of the Spirit—the Spirit that's the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> Always pray in the Spirit as you do all this. Stay awake and keep on praying for all God's people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Pray for me too so that I'll be given the right words to say, and that I'll be able to explain confidently the hidden truths of the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> I'm an imprisoned ambassador for the sake of the good news, so please pray that I will speak fearlessly, as I should.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12"> Tychicus, our good friend and faithful minister, will give you all my news and explain everything so you'll know how I'm doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 2:17"> That's why I'm sending him to you—to tell you what's happened to us and to encourage you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:16; Galatians 5:6; 1 Peter 5:14"> Peace to all the Christians there, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, with love and trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Matthew 6:13"> Grace to all those who eternally love our Lord Jesus.</span>
