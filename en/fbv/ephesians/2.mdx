<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Ephesians 2:5–6; Colossians 3:1–4"> At one time you were dead in your sins and evil actions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> living your lives according to the ways of the world, under the control of the devil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “the ruler of the power of the air.”">a</a></sup>whose spirit is at work in those who disobey God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> All of us were once like that, our behavior determined by the desires of our sinful human nature and our evil way of thinking. Like everyone else, in our nature we were children of anger.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Greek literally says “Children in nature of anger.” Following the previous thought about human nature, this could mean we are “naturally angry” or rebellious towards God. Alternatively, this could be viewed that we are the object of divine anger, though God is not specifically mentioned here.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; PSALMS 86:15"> But God in his generous mercy, because of the amazing love he had for us</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> even while we were dead in our sins, has made us alive together with Christ. Trusting in him has saved you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> He raised us up with Christ, and in Christ Jesus seated us with him in heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> so that in all eternity to come he could demonstrate the amazing extent of his grace in showing us kindness through Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> For you've been saved by grace through trusting in him—it's not through yourselves, it's the gift of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> This has nothing to do with human effort, so don't be proud of yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> We're the product of what God does, created in Christ Jesus to do good, which God already planned that we should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> So you who are “foreigners” humanly-speaking, called “uncircumcised” by those who are “circumcised”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Jews (circumcised) and the Gentiles (uncircumcised).">c</a></sup>(which is only a procedure carried out by human beings), you need to remember</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> that once you had no relationship to Christ. You were barred as foreigners from being citizens of Israel, strangers to the agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">d</a></sup>God had promised. You had no hope and you lived in the world without God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> But now, in Christ Jesus, you who were once a long way off have been brought near by the blood of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> Christ is our peace. Through his body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="From the context it appears that Paul is referring here to Jesus' crucifixion.">e</a></sup>he made the two into one, and broke down the separating wall of hostility that divided us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> freeing us from the law with its requirements and regulations. He did this so he could create in himself one new person out of the two and so make peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and completely reconcile both of them to God through the cross as if they were just one body, having destroyed our hostility towards each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> He came and shared the good news of peace with those of you who were far away, and those who were nearby,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> because through him we both gain access to the Father through the same Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> That means you're no longer strangers and foreigners; you are fellow citizens of God's people and belong to God's family</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> that's being built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus as the cornerstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> In him the whole building is joined together, growing to form a holy temple for the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> You too are being built together in him as a place for God to live by the Spirit.</span>
