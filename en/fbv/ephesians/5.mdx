<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> So then, imitate God since you're his much-loved children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> Live in love, just as Christ loved you. He gave himself for us, a gift and sacrificial offering to God like a sweet-smelling perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> Sexual immorality or any kind of indecency or greed should never be mentioned concerning you, as God's people should not be doing such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> Obscene talk, stupid chatter, and coarse jokes are totally inappropriate—instead you should be thanking God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> You know it's absolutely sure that no one who is sexually immoral, or commits indecency, or who is greedy, or is an idol-worshiper will inherit anything in the kingdom of Christ and God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Don't let anyone fool you with lying words, for it's because of such things that God's judgment comes upon the children of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> So don't partner with them in this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> At one time you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. You are to live as children of light</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> (and the fruit of light is everything that's good and right and true),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; PSALMS 19:14"> demonstrating what the Lord really appreciates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> Don't have anything to do with the pointless things that darkness produces—instead expose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> It's shameful even to speak about the things such people do secretly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> but when anything is exposed by the light then it's revealed as it is. Light makes everything visible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> That's why it's said, “Wake up, those of you who are sleeping, rise up from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> So be careful how you live your life, not foolishly, but wisely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> making the best use of opportunities because the days are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> So don't be ignorant—find out what the Lord's will is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> Don't get drunk on wine which will wreck your life, but be filled with the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; PSALMS 95:2"> Share together with one another, using psalms and hymns and sacred songs, singing and making music to the Lord to express what you feel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> Always thank God the Father for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> Each of you should be willing to accept what others tell you to do out of reverence for Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wives, do what your own husbands tell you to do, as you would if the Lord told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> The husband is head of the wife in the same way as Christ is head of the church—his body and its savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> In the same way that the church does what Christ says, wives should do what their husbands tell them in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, you should love your wives in the same way as Christ loved the church and gave himself for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> He made it holy, he made it clean by washing in the water of the word,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Presumably an allusion to baptism.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> so that he could make the church his own, with no flaw or blemish or any kind of fault, but holy and blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> Husbands should love their wives just like this, as they love their own bodies. A man who loves his wife loves himself—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> for no one ever hated his own body, but feeds it and looks after it, just as Christ does for the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> for we are parts of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> “This is why a man leaves his father and mother, and is joined to his wife, and the two are united in one.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Genesis 2:24.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This is a deep hidden truth—but I'm talking about Christ and the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> However, each husband should love his own wife as he does himself, and the wife should respect her husband.</span>
