<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 1:8; Philippians 1:13–16"> This is why, I, Paul, a prisoner of Jesus Christ for the sake of you foreigners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:25–27; 1 Timothy 1:11; 2 Timothy 1:11"> (well, I'm assuming you've heard that God gave me the specific responsibility of sharing God's grace with you),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> how, by what God showed me, made clear the mystery that was previously hidden. I wrote to you briefly before about this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> and when you read this you'll be able to understand my insight into the mystery of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> In past generations this wasn't made clear to anyone, but now it's been revealed to God's holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 John 1:3; 1 John 2:25"> that foreigners are joint heirs, part of the same body, and in Christ Jesus share together in the promise through the good news.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> I became a minister of this good news through the gift of God's grace that I was given by his power that was at work in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> This grace was given to me, the least important of all Christians, in order to share with the foreigners the incredible value of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and to help everyone see the purpose of the mystery which from the very beginning was hidden in God who made everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> God's plan was that through the church the various aspects of his wisdom would be revealed to the rulers and authorities in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> This was in accordance with God's eternal purpose that he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> Because of him and our trust in him we can come to God in total freedom and confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> So I'm asking that you don't get discouraged that I'm suffering—it's for you and you should value that!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> This is why I kneel before the Father</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> from whom every family in heaven and on earth receives its nature and character,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> asking him that out of his wealth of glory he may strengthen you in your innermost being with power through his Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> May Christ live in you as you trust in him, so that as you are planted deep in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> you may have the power to comprehend with all God's people the breadth and length and height and depth of Christ's love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> May you know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you're made full and complete by the fullness of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> Now may he who—through his power working in us—can do infinitely more than we ever ask for or even think about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> may he be glorified in the church and in Christ Jesus through all generations for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
