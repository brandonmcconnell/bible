<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> This letter comes from Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus according to God's will, to the Christians<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “saints.”">a</a></sup>in Ephesus and those who trust in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Galatians 1:3; Titus 1:4"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Praise God the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with all that's spiritually good in the heavenly world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> just as he chose us to be in him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “in union with him.”">b</a></sup>before the beginning of this world, so that in love we could be holy and without fault before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> He decided in advance to adopt us as his children, working through Jesus Christ to bring us to himself. He was happy to do this because this is what he wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> So we praise him for his glorious grace that he so kindly gave us in his beloved Son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Son is assumed here. The Greek says “beloved one.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> Through him we gain salvation through his blood, the forgiveness of our sins as a result of his priceless grace</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> that he so generously provided for us, together with all wisdom and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> He revealed to us his previously-hidden will through which he was happy to pursue his plan</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> at the appropriate time to bring everyone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Paralleling Colossians 1:20.">d</a></sup>together in Christ—those in heaven and those on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> In him—we were chosen beforehand, according to the plan of the one who is working everything out according to his will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> for the purpose that we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“We” meaning Jewish Christians.">e</a></sup>who were the first to hope in Christ could praise his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> In him—you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“You” meaning Gentile Christians.">f</a></sup>too have heard the word of truth, the good news of your salvation. In him—because you trusted in him you were stamped with the seal of the Holy Spirit's promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> which is a down-payment on our inheritance when God redeems what he's kept safe for himself—us, who will praise and give him glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> That's the reason, because I've heard of your trust in the Lord Jesus and the love that you have for all Christians,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> why I never stop thanking God for you and remember you in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> I pray that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, may give you a spirit of wisdom to see and know him as he really is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> May your minds be enlightened so you can understand the hope he's called you to— the glorious riches he promises as an inheritance to his holy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> May you also understand God's amazing power</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; PSALMS 110:1"> that he demonstrated in raising Christ from the dead. God seated Christ at his right hand in heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> far above any other ruler, authority, power, or lord, or any leader with all their titles—not only in this world, but also in the coming world too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> God has made everything subject to the authority of Christ, and has given him the responsibility as head over everything for the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is his body, filled full and made complete by Christ, who fills and brings everything to completion.</span>
