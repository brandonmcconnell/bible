<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> So I—this prisoner in the Lord—am encouraging you to make sure you live according to the principles to which you were called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> Don't think proudly of yourselves; be gentle and patient, showing tolerance to each other in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> Make every effort to remain one in the Spirit through the peace that binds you together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> For there's one body, and one Spirit, just as you were called to one hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> The Lord is one, our trust in him is one, and there's one baptism;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> there's one God and Father of everyone. He is over all and through all and in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> To each of us grace was given in proportion to Christ's generous gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> As Scripture says, “When he ascended to the heights he took captives with him, and gave gifts to humanity.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Quoting Psalms 68:18.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> (Regarding this: it says he ascended, but that means he also had previously descended to our lowly world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> The one that descended is the same one who also ascended to the highest heaven in order that he could make the whole universe complete.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> The gifts he gave were so that some could be apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> with the purpose of preparing God's people for the work of helping others, to help the body of Christ to grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> We grow together until we all reach the state of being one in our trust in and knowledge of the Son of God, completely grown up, measuring up to full maturity in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> We shouldn't be little children any more, tossed about and blown along by every passing breeze of doctrine, confused by human trickery, led into error by crafty people who deceitfully scheme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> Instead by speaking the truth in love we ought to grow up in everything into Christ, who is our head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> It's because of him that the whole body operates, every joint holding it together as each individual part does what it's supposed to, and the whole body grows, building itself up in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> So let me say this to you—in fact I insist on it in the Lord—that you should no longer live like the foreigners do, in their empty-headed way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> In the darkness of their minds they don't understand, and they have been cut off from the life of God because they don't know anything and in their stubbornness they don't want to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> Because they are past caring they abandon themselves to sensuality, and greedily do all kinds of disgusting things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But that's not what you learned about Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> Didn't you hear about him? Weren't you taught concerning him? Didn't you learn the truth as it is in Jesus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> So get rid of your former lifestyle, that old nature that ruins you through deceptive desires!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; PSALMS 51:10"> Let yourselves be remade spiritually and mentally,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and put on your new nature that God created so you will be like him, right and holy in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> So reject lies, and tell the truth to each other—for we belong to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; PSALMS 4:4; PSALMS 37:8"> Don't sin by getting angry; don't let evening come and find you still mad—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> and don't give the devil any opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> Thieves, stop your stealing, and do honest, productive work with your hands, so you'll have something to give to those who need it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Don't use bad language. Speak words that will encourage people as necessary, so that those who listen will be helped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> Don't disappoint the Holy Spirit of God that marked you as belonging to him until the day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Get rid of every kind of bitterness, rage, anger, verbal abuse, and insults, along with all forms of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> Be kind and compassionate to each other, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ forgave you.</span>
