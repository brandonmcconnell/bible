<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Nehemiah 8:9; Ezra 2:63"> Say to Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and Joshua, son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remaining people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 3:12; Haggai 2:9; Ezekiel 7:20"> Is there anyone left among you who saw this house in its former glory?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some of the old people may have seen the previous Temple that was destroyed almost 70 years earlier.">a</a></sup>How does it look to you now? Does it seem like nothing to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:20; Joshua 1:9; Zechariah 8:9"> So then be strong, Zerubbabel! Be strong, Joshua, son of Jehozadak, high priest! Be strong, all you people who remain in the land! Work, for I am with you, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Exodus 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> Just as I promised you when you left Egypt, my Spirit continues to be among you. Don't be afraid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–28; Isaiah 10:25; Mark 13:24–26"> This is what the Lord Almighty says: Soon I will shake the heavens and the earth once more, and the sea and the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; 1 Kings 8:11; John 7:37–39"> I will shake all the nations, and the one desired<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “treasures.”">b</a></sup>by all of the nations will come and I will fill this house with glory, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 50:10–12; PSALMS 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> The silver is mine, and the gold is mine, declares the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 85:8–9; 2 Corinthians 3:9–10; Colossians 1:19–21"> The glory of this second house will be greater than the first, says the Lord Almighty, and I will bring peace in this place. So declares the Lord Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of the reign of king Darius, the Lord sent a message through the prophet Haggai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10–11; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> “This is what the Lord Almighty says: Ask the priests about the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:27; Matthew 23:19; Leviticus 7:6"> If someone carries some meat from a holy sacrifice in a fold of their clothes and that fold touches some bread or stew or wine or olive oil or any other kind of food, does that food become holy?” And the priests answered, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2–3; Numbers 19:11–22; Leviticus 22:4–6"> Then Haggai asked, “If someone becomes unclean by touching a dead body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Implied. See Numbers 19.">c</a></sup>and then touches any of these foods, do they become unclean?” The priests answered, “Yes, it does become unclean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Titus 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Then Haggai responded, “This is how it is with these people, and with the nation before me, says the Lord. All that they do, every offering they bring, is unclean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> Now think about what you're doing from this day on. Before a stone was laid on another stone in the house of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Malachi 2:2; Proverbs 3:9–10"> how was it for you? You expected a store of grain with twenty measures but only found ten. You thought you could empty out fifty measures from the winepress but there was only twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I struck with blight and mildew and hail everything you were working so hard for, but even so you refused to come back to me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> Think about what you're doing from this day on, today the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month when a foundation was laid for the house of the Lord. Think about it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Habakkuk 3:17–18; Proverbs 3:9–10"> the seed is still in the barn. The vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate tree, and the olive tree have not yet given fruit. But from this day on I will bless you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Lord sent another message through the prophet Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> Tell Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, I am going to shake the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 5:10; Judges 7:22; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will overthrow the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the power of the nations' kingdoms. I will overthrow the chariots and their riders. The horses and riders will fall, and the men will kill each other by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; Song of Songs 8:6"> On that day, says the Lord, I will take you, Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, my servant, and make you like my signet ring, for I have chosen you. So declares the Lord Almighty.</span>
</div>
