<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:1–12; 1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21"> Meanwhile the Philistines had attacked Israel, and the Israelite army ran away from them, and many were killed on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 14:49; 1 Samuel 23:17"> The Philistines chased down Saul and his sons, killing Saul's sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:6; Genesis 49:23; 1 Kings 22:34"> The fighting became very intense around Saul, and the arrows of the Philistine archers found their target, wounding Saul very badly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:54; 2 Samuel 1:6; 2 Samuel 1:9–10"> Saul said to his armor-bearer, “Take your sword and kill me, or these heathen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Heathen”: literally, “uncircumcised.”">a</a></sup>men will come and kill and torture me!”<br />But the armor-bearer didn't want to do it because he was too afraid. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:5"> When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his own sword and died with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Chronicles 10:6"> Saul, his three sons, his armor-bearer, and all the men with him, died the same day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Leviticus 26:32; Leviticus 26:36"> When the Israelites who lived along the valley and those on the other side of the Jordan realized that the Israelite army had run away, and that Saul and his sons had died, they abandoned their cities and they also ran away. So the Philistines came and took them over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:25; 1 Chronicles 10:8"> The next day, when the Philistines went to strip the dead, they found Saul and his three sons lying on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Judges 16:23–24; 1 Samuel 17:54"> They cut off Saul's head, stripped him of his armor, and sent messengers throughout the country of the Philistines to announce the news in the temples of their idols and to their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 21:9"> They placed his armor in the temple of the Ashtoreths, and nailed his body to the town wall of Beth-shan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4"> However, when the people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> all their strong warriors set out, traveled all night, and took down the bodies of Saul and his sons from the wall of Beth-shan. When they got back to Jabesh, they burned the bodies there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:12–14; Genesis 50:10; 1 Samuel 22:6"> Then they took their bones and buried them under the tamarisk tree in Jabesh, and fasted for seven days.</span>
</div>
