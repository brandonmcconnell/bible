<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 1 John 3:21"> David ran from Naioth in Ramah to Jonathan and asked him, “What have I done? What is my wrong have I done? What terrible thing have I done to your father that he wants to kill me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; John 17:8; John 15:15"> “Nothing!” Jonathan replied. “You're not going to die! Listen! My father tells me everything he's planning, whatever it is. Why would my father keep something like this from me? It's not true!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Deuteronomy 6:13; 2 Kings 2:6"> But David swore an oath again, saying, “Your father knows very well that I'm your friend, and so he's told himself, ‘Jonathan can't find out about this, otherwise he'll be really upset.’ I swear on the life of the Lord, and on your own life, my life is hanging by a thread.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“My life is hanging by a thread”: literally, “there's just a step between me and death.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “Tell me what you want me to do for you and I'll do it,” Jonathan told David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:2; Numbers 28:11"> “Well, the New Moon festival is tomorrow, and I'm meant to sit down and eat with the king. But if it's all right with you, I plan to go and hide in the field until the evening three days from now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:58; 1 Samuel 9:12; Deuteronomy 12:5"> If your father does indeed miss me, tell him, ‘David had to urgently ask my permission to hurry down to Bethlehem, his hometown, because of a yearly sacrifice there for his whole family group.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Esther 7:7; Deuteronomy 1:23"> If he says, ‘That's fine,’ then there's no problem for me, your servant, but if he gets mad, you'll know he intends to do me harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:32; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> So please treat me well, as you promised when you made an agreement with me before the Lord. If I've done wrong, then kill me yourself! Why take me to your father for him to do it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:38"> “Absolutely not!” Jonathan replied. “If I knew for certain that my father had plans to harm you, don't you think I'd tell you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:14; 1 Samuel 25:17; Genesis 42:30"> “So who's going to let me know if your father gives you a nasty answer?” David asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “Come on, let's go out into the countryside,” Jonathan said. So they both went out into the countryside.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 22:22; Job 31:4; Proverbs 25:2–3"> Jonathan said to David, “I promise by the Lord, the God of Israel, that I will question my father by this time tomorrow or the day after. If things look good for you, I'll send a message to you and let you know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Chronicles 22:11; Joshua 1:5"> But if my father plans to do you harm, then may the Lord punish me very severely, if I don't let you know by sending you a message so you can get away safely. May the Lord be with you, just as he was with my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; Ephesians 5:1–2"> While I live, please show me trustworthy love like that of the Lord so I don't die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 24:21; 2 Samuel 9:1–7"> and please don't ever remove your trustworthy love for my family, even when the Lord has removed every one of your enemies from the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 21:8"> Jonathan made a solemn agreement with the family of David, saying, “May the Lord impose retribution on David's enemies.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This and the previous verses have a number of problems in translation.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:3"> Jonathan made David swear this once more by making an oath based on David's love for him, for Jonathan already loved David as he loved himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:25; 1 Samuel 20:5"> Then Jonathan said to David, “The New Moon festival is tomorrow. You'll be missed, because your place will be empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:5"> In three days time, go quickly to where you hid when all this started, and stay there beside the pile of stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I'll shoot three arrows to the side of it as if I were shooting at a target.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:2; Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 12:16"> Then I'll send a boy and tell him, ‘Go and find the arrows!’ Now, if I say to him specifically, ‘Look, the arrows are this side of you; bring them over here,’ then I swear on the life of the Lord it's safe for you to come out—there's no danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:37"> But if I tell the boy, ‘Look, the arrows are way past you,’ then you'll have to leave, for the Lord wants you to go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:14–15; 1 Samuel 20:42; Genesis 31:50"> As for what you and I talked about, remember that the Lord is a witness between you and me forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 4:17; Isaiah 1:11–15; Proverbs 21:3"> So David hid himself in the field. When the New Moon festival arrived, the king sat down to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:18; Judges 16:20"> He sat in his usual place by the wall opposite Jonathan. Abner sat next to Saul, but David's place was empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 11:27"> Saul didn't say anything that day because he thought, “Something has probably happened to David to make him ceremonially unclean—yes, he must be unclean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4; 1 Samuel 19:15; 1 Samuel 18:11"> But the second day, the day after the New Moon, David's place was still empty. Saul asked his son Jonathan, “Why hasn't the son of Jesse come to dinner either yesterday or today?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:6"> Jonathan answered, “David had to urgently ask my permission to go to Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:28"> He told me, ‘Please let me go, because our family is having a sacrifice in the town and my brother told me I had to be there. If you think well of me, please let me go and see my brothers.’ That's why he's absent from the king's table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 21:24; James 1:19–20; Proverbs 25:28"> Saul got very angry with Jonathan and said, “You rebellious son of a whore! Don't you think I know that you prefer the son of Jesse? Shame on you! You're a disgrace to the mother who bore you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:6; 1 Samuel 20:8; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> While the son of Jesse remains alive, you and your kingship are not secure. Now go and bring him here to me, for he has to die!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; 1 Samuel 19:5; Luke 23:22"> “Why does he have to be put to death?” Jonathan asked. “What has he done?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; 1 Samuel 18:11; 1 Samuel 19:10"> Saul threw his spear at Jonathan, trying to kill him, so he knew that his father definitely wanted David dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Mark 3:5; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Jonathan left the table absolutely furious. He would not eat anything on the second day of the festival, for he was so upset by the shameful way his father had treated David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:5; 1 Samuel 20:19"> In the morning Jonathan went to the field to the place he had agreed with David, and a young boy was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:20–22"> He told the boy, “Run and find the arrows that I shoot.” The boy started running and Jonathan shot an arrow past him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:22"> When the boy got to the place where Jonathan's arrow had landed, Jonathan shouted to him, “Isn't the arrow farther past you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="PSALMS 55:6–9; Proverbs 6:4–5; Mark 13:14–16"> Hurry up! Do it quickly! Don't wait!” The boy picked up the arrows and took them back to his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The boy didn't suspect anything—only Jonathan and David knew what it meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jonathan gave his bow and arrows to the boy and said, “Take these back to town.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 9:6"> After the boy had gone, David got up from beside the pile of stones, fell facedown to the ground, and bowed three times. Then he and Jonathan kissed each other and cried together as friends, though David cried the hardest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Samuel 23:18; 1 Samuel 20:22–23"> Jonathan said to David, “Go in peace, for the two of us have sworn a solemn oath in the name of the Lord. We said, ‘The Lord will be a witness between me and you, and between my descendants and your descendants forever.’” Then David left, and Jonathan went back to town.</span>
</div>
