<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:28–29; Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Samuel 23:19"> When Saul returned from chasing the Philistines, it was reported to him, “David is in the Desert of En-gedi.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:2; PSALMS 104:18; PSALMS 37:32"> So Saul took three thousand specially-picked men from all Israel and went searching for David and his men in the area around Wild Goats' Rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:24; PSALMS 57:1–11; PSALMS 142:1–7"> As Saul passed the sheep pens on the way, there was a cave, and he went in to relieve himself. David and his men were hiding deep inside the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8–11; 1 Samuel 24:10; 1 Samuel 26:23"> David's men said to him, “Today's the day the Lord promised you when he told you, ‘Listen, I'm going to hand over your enemy to you, so you can do to him whatever you want.’” Then David crept up quietly and cut a piece from the edge of Saul's robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:10; 2 Kings 22:19; 1 John 3:20–21"> But afterwards David felt really bad because he'd cut off a piece of Saul's robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Job 31:29–30; 1 Samuel 26:9–11"> He told his men, “May the Lord prevent me from doing anything like this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Like this”: probably referring his men's desire to attack the king.">a</a></sup>to my master, the Lord's anointed one. I will never attack him, for he is the Lord's anointed one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; PSALMS 7:4; Romans 12:17–21"> He reprimanded his men and didn't allow them to attack Saul. Saul stood up and went on his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23–24; Romans 13:7; Exodus 20:12"> A little later David came out of the cave and shouted, “My master the king!” When Saul looked around, David bowed down with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:21–22; Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 16:28"> “Why do you pay attention to people who say, ‘Watch out, David wants to harm you’?” David asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 105:15; 1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> “Just look! You've seen with your own eyes today that the Lord handed you over to me in the cave. Some urged me to kill you, but I showed you compassion, and said, ‘I refuse to attack my master, for he is the Lord's anointed one.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 23:14; 1 Samuel 23:23"> Take a look, my father! You see this piece of your robe I'm holding. Yes, I did cut it off, but I didn't kill you. Now you can see for yourself and you can be sure that I have done nothing evil or rebellious. I have not sinned against you, but you are hunting me down, trying to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; Judges 11:27; PSALMS 35:1"> May the Lord decide between you and me as to who of us is right, and may the Lord punish you, but I myself will never try to harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:20; Matthew 7:16–18; Matthew 12:33–34"> As the old saying goes, ‘From the wicked comes wickedness,’ but I myself will never try to harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 17:43; 2 Samuel 9:8"> Who is the king of Israel coming after? Who are you chasing? A dead dog! Just a flea!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 35:1; PSALMS 119:154"> May the Lord decide and choose between you and me. May he pay attention to my case and support it; may he save me from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 33:4; Luke 21:15"> After David finished saying this, Saul asked, “Is that you speaking, David my son?” and he wept out loud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; 1 Samuel 26:21; Genesis 38:26"> He told David, “You are a better person than I am, because you have repaid me with good, but I have repaid you with evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:23; PSALMS 31:8; 1 Samuel 23:12"> Today you have demonstrated how well you have treated me—for when the Lord handed me over to you, you didn't kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 18:20; 1 Samuel 23:21; Proverbs 25:21–22"> For if a man caught hold of his enemy, would he let him escape unharmed? The Lord reward you well for how you've treated me today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:17; Matthew 2:3–6; 2 Samuel 3:17–18"> Listen, I know you definitely will be king, and your rule over the kingdom of Israel will be secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:23; 2 Samuel 21:6–8; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> Now swear to me by the Lord that you will not destroy my descendants who follow me, and that you will not wipe out my name from my family line.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:29; John 2:24; Matthew 10:16–17"> So David promised this to Saul with an oath. Then Saul went back home, but David and his men returned to the stronghold.</span>
