</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:23; Joshua 11:4; Joshua 18:12"> The Philistines gathered to fight against Israel. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Hebrews 11:38; Judges 10:9"> When the Israelite men realized the tough situation they were in, and that the army was taking a beating, they hid themselves in caves, holes, rocks, pits, and cisterns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Leviticus 26:17; Numbers 32:1–5"> Some of the Hebrews even crossed the Jordan into the territory of Gad and Gilead, but Saul stayed at Gilgal, and all the men with him were trembling with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8"> Saul waited there seven days for the time that Samuel had said, but Samuel did not arrive at Gilgal, and the army started to desert him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> So Saul ordered, “Bring the burnt offering and the friendship offerings to me,” and he presented the burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Ruth 2:4; PSALMS 129:8"> Just as he finished presenting the burnt offering, he saw Samuel arrive. Saul went over to meet him and say hello.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 13:16"> “What have you done?” Samuel asked.<br />Saul replied, “Well I saw my men were deserting me, and you hadn't arrived when you said you would, and the Philistines were gathering at Michmash to attack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 66:3; Amos 8:5; 2 Corinthians 9:7"> I said to myself, ‘The Philistines are about to attack me at Gilgal, and I haven't asked the Lord for his help.’ So I felt I had to present the burnt offering myself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 2 Chronicles 16:9; 1 Samuel 15:28"> “You've been really stupid,” Samuel told him. “You haven't kept the commands of the Lord your God. If you had, the Lord would have made your kingdom over Israel secure forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 13:22; 1 Samuel 15:28; PSALMS 89:19–37"> But now your kingdom won't last. The Lord has found for himself a man who thinks like him, and has chosen him as ruler over his people, because you haven't kept the commands of the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> Then Samuel left Gilgal. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:3"> Saul, his son Jonathan, and the soldiers with them were staying in Geba of Benjamin, while the Philistines were camped at Michmash.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:34; Joshua 18:13–14; Joshua 16:3"> one towards Beth-horon, and one towards the border that looks down on the Valley of Zeboim by the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 24:14; Judges 5:8; Jeremiah 24:1"> There wasn't a blacksmith to be found anywhere in Israel, because the Philistines had said, “So that the Hebrews don't make swords and spears.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> All the Israelites had to go to the Philistines to sharpen their iron plowshares, pickaxes, axes, and sickles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The fee was two-thirds of a shekel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Two-thirds of a shekel”: literally “one pim.”">d</a></sup>for plowshares and pickaxes, and a third of a shekel for sharpening the axes and cattle prods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Zechariah 4:6; 1 Samuel 17:50"> So when it came to the day of battle none of the soldiers with Saul and Jonathan had swords or spears—only Saul and his son Jonathan had such weapons.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:28; 1 Samuel 14:1; 1 Samuel 14:4–5"> A Philistine garrison had taken control of the pass at Michmash.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This verse is better taken with the following chapter.">e</a></sup></span>
