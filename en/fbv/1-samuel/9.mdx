<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:51; Acts 13:21; 1 Chronicles 9:36–39"> There was a wealthy and influential man from the tribe of Benjamin, whose name was Kish son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Bekorath, son of Aphiah, a descendant of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Numbers 13:33; 1 Samuel 16:7"> Kish had a son called Saul, the most handsome young man in all of Israel. He was head and shoulders taller than anyone else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 5:10; 1 Samuel 10:2; Judges 10:4"> One time the donkeys of Saul's father Kish went astray. Kish told his son Saul, “Please go and look for the donkeys. You can take one of the servants with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42; Joshua 24:33; John 3:23"> Saul searched the hill country of Ephraim and then the land of Shalishah, but didn't find the donkeys. He and the servant searched the region of Shaalim, but they weren't there. Then they searched the land of Benjamin, and they still couldn't find them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; 1 Samuel 1:1; Luke 12:11"> When they got to the land of Zuph, Saul told his servant, “Come on, let's go back, otherwise my father won't be worrying about the donkeys, but about us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Deuteronomy 33:1; 1 Timothy 6:11"> But the servant replied, “Hold on! There's a man of God here in this town. He's very well thought of, and everything he says comes true. Let's go and see him. Maybe he can tell us which way we should take.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 2 Kings 8:8; 2 Kings 4:42"> “But if we do go, what can we give him?” Saul replied. “All the bread in our bags is gone. We don't have anything to take to the man of God. What do we have with us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> “Look, I've got a quarter shekel of silver with me. I'll give it to the man of God so he can tell us the way we should take,” the servant told Saul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; 2 Kings 17:13; Amos 7:12"> (In the old days in Israel, someone who went to inquire of God would say, “Come on, let's go to the seer,” because prophets used to be known as seers.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13–14"> “That's good,” Saul told his servant. “So let's go.” They went to the town where the man of God was.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 2:16; Genesis 24:18–20"> As they were going up the hill to the town, they met some young women coming out to draw water and asked them, “Is the seer here?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 1 Samuel 16:2; 1 Samuel 10:5"> “He is,” they replied, “Up ahead of you. But you'll have to hurry. He's come to town today because the people are having a sacrifice at the high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:11; Matthew 26:26; Luke 24:30"> When you enter the town you can find him before he goes up to eat at the high place. The people won't eat before he comes, because he has to bless the sacrifice. After that, those who've been invited will eat. If you leave now you'll catch him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So they carried on their way up to the town. As they arrived there was Samuel going in the opposite direction. They met him on his way up to the high place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:21; 1 Samuel 15:1; Job 33:16"> The day before Saul arrived, the Lord had told Samuel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; PSALMS 106:44; Exodus 3:7–9"> “Around this time tomorrow I'm going to send you a man from the land of Benjamin. Anoint him as ruler over my people Israel, and he will rescue them from the Philistines. I have seen what's happening to my people and I have heard their cry for help.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 16:6–12; Nehemiah 13:25"> When Samuel saw Saul, the Lord said to him, “This is the man I told you about. He is the one who is going to rule my people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Saul went up to Samuel in the gateway and asked him, “Could you please tell me where the house of the seer is?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:25; John 4:29"> “I am the seer,” Samuel told Saul. “Go up ahead of me and we will eat together. Then in the morning I will answer all your questions and then send you on your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 9:3"> About the donkeys you lost three days ago—don't worry about them because they've been found. But now—doesn't the hope of all of Israel rest on you and your family line?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:17; Judges 20:46–48; PSALMS 68:27"> “But I'm from the tribe of Benjamin, the smallest tribe in Israel, and my family is the least significant of all the families of the tribe of Benjamin!” Saul replied. “Why are you telling me this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Luke 14:10"> Then Samuel brought Saul and his servant into the hall, and sat them at the head of the thirty people who'd been invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:5; Genesis 43:34"> Samuel told the cook, “Bring the special piece of meat I gave you and told you to keep to one side.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:4; Leviticus 7:32–33; Exodus 29:22"> So the cook took the upper thigh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Saul was given the meat that only priests were meant to eat. See Leviticus 10:14-15.">a</a></sup>of meat and what was with it and placed it before Saul. Then Samuel said, “See, this is what was kept to one side. It was reserved for you. Eat it, for it was kept for you for this particular time, right from when I said, ‘I have invited the people.’” So Saul ate with Samuel that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:9; Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2"> Once they had returned from the high place to the town, Samuel spoke with Saul on the roof of his house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In the absence of other rooms, the flat roof of the house was used as temporary accommodation.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:4; Joshua 7:13; Judges 19:28"> At dawn the next day Samuel called out to Saul on the roof, “Get up! I need to send you on your way!” So Saul got up, and he went outside with Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:14–15; 1 Samuel 20:38–39; 2 Kings 9:5–6"> As they approached the edge of town, Samuel said to Saul, “Tell your servant to go on ahead of us. Once he's gone stay here for a while, because I have a message from God to give you.” So the servant went on ahead.</span>
</div>
</div>
