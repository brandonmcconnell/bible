</div>

If you don't, I'll take it by force.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:7–9; Genesis 13:13; Malachi 2:13"> The sins of these young men were extremely serious from the Lord's perspective because they were treating with contempt the Lord's offerings.</span>
</div>

The boy Samuel grew up in the presence of the Lord.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Philippians 3:19; Acts 9:4"> He asked them, “Why are you doing all this? I keep on hearing from everyone about your evil actions.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 2:40; Acts 2:47"> The boy Samuel grew physically, and also grew in the approval of both the Lord and the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

I also gave to your forefather's family all the Israelites' offerings made with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 32:15; 1 Samuel 2:13–17"> So why have you treated with contempt my sacrifices and offerings that I have ordered for my place of worship? </div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26–27; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Samuel 22:21–23"> Anyone of your family not cut off from serving at my altar will make your eyes weep and cause you grief. All your descendants will die when full of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:11; 1 Kings 13:3; 1 Samuel 4:17"> Here is a sign for you that this will happen regarding your two sons Hophni and Phinehas: both will die on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Kings 11:38"> I will choose for myself a trustworthy priest who will do what I really want, what I have in mind. <span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 2:27; Malachi 1:13; Ezekiel 44:10–12"> Every one of your descendants who is left will come and bow down to him, asking for money and food, saying, ‘Please give me work as a priest so that I can have food to eat.’”</span>
