<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 19:30; 1 Kings 20:30"> The Philistines gathered all their armies at Aphek, and the Israelites set up camp by the spring in Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:1–2; Joshua 13:3; 1 Samuel 29:6–7"> The Philistine rulers were marching out in their divisions of hundreds and thousands with David and his men at the rear with King Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:5; 1 Samuel 27:7; John 19:6"> But the Philistine commanders asked, “What are these Hebrews doing here?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This could also be translated, “Who are these Hebrews?” since the text simply says “What these Hebrews?”">a</a></sup>Achish replied to the Philistine commanders, “That's David, an officer of King Saul of Israel. He's been with me for a long time, years even, and I haven't found any fault in him since the day he came over to our side up till now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:21; 1 Chronicles 12:19; Luke 16:8"> But the Philistine commanders got angry with Achish, and told him, “Send him back to the place he came from, to the town you assigned to him. He can't go with us into battle. What if he turns on us during the fighting? What a great way for him to please his master—by handing over the heads of our men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 18:6–7; Proverbs 27:14"> Isn't this the David they sing about in their dances: ‘Saul has killed his thousands, and David his tens of thousands’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:25; 2 Kings 19:27; 1 Samuel 20:3"> So Achish called David over and told him, “As the Lord lives, you are honest and you've done what's right as far as I can see. As far as I'm concerned you should march with me into battle because I haven't found any fault in you from the day you arrived until now. But the other leaders don't approve of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 22:34"> So go back home in peace, and that way you won't do anything to upset the Philistine leaders.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; PSALMS 34:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:2"> “But what have I done?” David asked. “What fault have you found in me, your servant, from the day I came to you until now, that would prevent me from going to fight the enemies of my lord the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:20"> “As far as I'm concerned, you're as good as an angel of God,” Achish replied. “But the Philistine commanders have stated, ‘He can't go into battle with us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:19; 1 Samuel 30:1–2; 1 Chronicles 12:22"> So get up early tomorrow morning and leave with your men as soon as it's light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 2 Samuel 4:4; Joshua 19:18"> David and his men got up early in the morning and went back to the country of the Philistines. But the Philistines advanced on Jezreel.</span>
