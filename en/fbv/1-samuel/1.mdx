<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:34; 1 Chronicles 6:25–27; 1 Samuel 1:19"> Once there was a man from Ramathaim-zophim in the hill country of Ephraim. His name was Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Elihu, son of Tohu, son of Zuph, from the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:1–2; Judges 13:2; Genesis 29:23–29"> He had two wives. The name of the first wife was Hannah, and the name of the second, Peninnah. Peninnah had children, but Hannah had none.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:41; Joshua 18:1; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Every year Elkanah would leave his town and go to worship and sacrifice to the Lord Almighty at Shiloh, where Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were the Lord's priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17–18; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Whenever Elkanah offered a sacrifice, he would give portions of it to Peninnah his wife and to all her sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:2; Genesis 29:30–31; Genesis 45:22"> He would give an extra portion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="By giving an extra portion, Elkanah was showing everyone that he treated Hannah as if she did have one child.">a</a></sup>to Hannah, to show his love for her even though the Lord hadn't given her any children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:14; Job 24:21; Leviticus 18:18"> Her rival—the other wife—would taunt her badly to upset her because the Lord hadn't given her any children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:19"> This went on for years. Whenever Hannah went to the Lord's Temple, Peninnah would taunt her until she cried and couldn't eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:15; Job 6:14; Isaiah 54:6"> “Hannah, what are you crying for?” her husband Elkanah would ask. “Why don't you eat? Why are you so upset? Aren't I better to you than ten sons?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:3; PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 5:7"> One time when they had finished eating and drinking in Shiloh, Hannah got up and went to the Temple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“And went to the Temple”: supplied for clarity.">b</a></sup>Eli the priest was sitting on his seat by the entrance to the Lord's Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 7:11; Luke 22:44; Job 10:1"> She was terribly upset, and prayed to the Lord as she cried and cried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Numbers 6:5; Genesis 29:32"> She made a vow, asking, “Lord Almighty, if only you would pay attention to the suffering of your servant and remember me, and not forget me but give me a son, then I will dedicate him to the Lord for his whole life, and no razor shall ever touch his head.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16; Luke 18:1"> As Hannah went on praying before the Lord, Eli watched her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:26; Acts 2:13; Genesis 24:42–45"> Hannah was praying in her head, and though her lips were moving, her voice made no sound. Eli thought she must be drunk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Job 8:2; Proverbs 6:9"> “Do you have to come here drunk?” he asked. “Get rid of your wine!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; Lamentations 2:19; PSALMS 42:4"> “It's not that, my lord,” Hannah replied. “I'm a very miserable woman. I haven't been drinking wine or beer; I'm just pouring my heart out to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Job 6:2–3; Matthew 12:34–35"> Please don't think I'm a bad woman! I have been praying because of all my troubles and grief.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Judges 18:6; PSALMS 20:3–5"> “Go in peace, and may the God of Israel give you what you have asked him for,” Eli replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Romans 15:13; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> “Thank you for your kindness to your servant,” she said. Then she went on her way, had something to eat, and she didn't look sad any more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:1; Genesis 30:22; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Elkanah and Hannah got up early the next morning to worship the Lord, and then they went home to Ramah. Elkanah made love to his wife Hannah, and the Lord honored her request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:51–52; Matthew 1:21; Exodus 2:10"> In due course she became pregnant and gave birth to a son. She named him Samuel, saying, “Because I asked the Lord for him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; Deuteronomy 12:11; Joshua 24:15"> Elkanah and all his family went to make the yearly sacrifice to the Lord and to fulfill his vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:28; 1 Samuel 1:11; Luke 2:22"> But Hannah did not go. She told her husband, “Once the boy is weaned I will take him to be presented to the Lord and to remain there forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:25; Luke 11:27; Genesis 21:7–8"> “Do as you see fit,” her husband Elkanah replied. “Stay here until you have weaned him. May the Lord fulfill what he has said.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“What he has said”: referring to the Lord. The Septuagint and a scroll from Qumran read “what you have said,” referring to Hannah.">c</a></sup>So Hannah stayed behind and nursed her son until she had weaned him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 15:9–10; Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> When she had weaned him, Hannah took the boy with her, along with a three-year-old bull,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“A three-year-old bull”: Septuagint reading.">d</a></sup>an ephah of flour, and a wineskin containing wine. Even though the boy was young, she brought him to the Lord's Temple at Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 18:15–16; Luke 2:22"> After they had slaughtered the bull, they presented the boy to Eli.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 4:30; 2 Kings 2:4; 2 Kings 2:6"> “Please, my lord,” said Hannah, “as surely as you live, my lord, I am the woman who stood here with you praying to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 5:15; PSALMS 118:5; Matthew 7:7"> I prayed for this boy, and since the Lord has given me what I asked him for,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:52; 2 Timothy 3:15"> now I'm giving him to the Lord. As long as he lives he will be dedicated to the Lord.” And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“He”: presumably referring to Elkhanah. Some versions change this to “they.”">e</a></sup>worshiped the Lord there.</span>
</div>
</div>
