<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> When Samuel grew old, he made his sons leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Again the word used is “judges,” but in this period of Israel's history before they had kings the judges did not only settle legal cases but acted as rulers.">a</a></sup>over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> His first son was named Joel, and his second son was named Abijah. They were rulers in Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; PSALMS 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> However, his sons did not follow his ways. They were corrupt, making money by accepting bribes and perverting justice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> So the elders of Israel all joined together and came to meet Samuel at Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> “Look here,” they told him, “you are old, and your sons do not follow your ways. Choose a king to rule over us like all the other nations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> Samuel thought this was a bad idea when they said, “Give us a king to rule over us,” so he prayed to the Lord about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> “Do what the people tell you,” the Lord said to Samuel, “because it's not you they're rejecting, but me as their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> They're doing just as they have always done since I led them out of Egypt right up to now. They have abandoned me and worshiped other gods, and they're doing just the same to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> So do what they want, but give them a solemn warning—explain to them what a king will do when he rules over them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Samuel repeated all that the Lord had said to the people asking him to give them a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> “This is what a king will do when he rules over you,” he told them. “He will take your sons and make them serve as charioteers and horsemen, and to run as a guard in front of his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> Some of them he will make commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties, and some will have to plow his fields and reap his harvest. Some will be assigned to making weapons of war and chariot equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He will take your daughters and have them work as perfume-makers, cooks, and bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> He will take your best fields, vineyards, and olive groves and give them to his officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> He will take a tenth of your grain harvest and the produce of your vineyards and allocate it to his chief officers and officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He will take your male servants and your female servants, and your best young men and your donkeys, and make them work for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will be his slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> On that day you will plead to be rescued from the king you have chosen, but the Lord won't answer you then.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> But the people refused to listen to what Samuel said. “No!” they insisted. “We want our own king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; PSALMS 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> That way we can be like all the other nations. Our king will rule us, and will lead us when we go out to fight our battles.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> Samuel listened to everything the people said and repeated it to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> The Lord told Samuel, “Do what they say and give them a king.” Then Samuel told the Israelites, “Go back to your homes.”</span>
</div>
</div>
