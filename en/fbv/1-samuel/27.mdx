<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Chronicles 2:25"> When Achish asked him, “Where have you been raiding today?” David would reply, “In the desert<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Desert,” literally “the Negev,” the southern arid region.">a</a></sup>of Judah,” or “the desert of Jerahmeel,” or “the desert of the Kenites.”</span>
