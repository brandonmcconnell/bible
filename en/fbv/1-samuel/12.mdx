<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Then Samuel said to all of Israel, “I have paid attention to everything you asked from me, and I have given you a king to rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 8:1; 1 Samuel 8:20"> Now your king is your leader. I am old and gray, and my sons are here with you. I have led you from the time I was a boy up to today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Numbers 16:15; 1 Peter 5:2"> I stand here before you. Bring any charges you have against me in the presence of the Lord and of his anointed one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Anointed one”: referring to the king.">a</a></sup>Have I taken anyone's ox or donkey? Have I wronged anyone? Have I oppressed anyone? Have I accepted a bribe from anyone to turn a blind eye? Tell me, and I will repay you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; PSALMS 37:5–6; 3 John 1:12"> “No, you have never cheated or oppressed us,” they replied, “and you have never taken anything from anyone.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Acts 24:20; Acts 23:9"> Samuel told them, “The Lord is a witness, and his anointed is a witness today, in this case involving you, that you have not found me guilty of anything.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Not found me guilty of anything”: literally, “you have not found anything in my hand.”">b</a></sup><br />“He is a witness,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:4; Exodus 6:26; PSALMS 99:6"> “The Lord is a witness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Septuagint reading.">c</a></sup>he who appointed Moses and Aaron,” Samuel went on. “He led your forefathers out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Micah 6:1–5; Acts 17:3"> So stand here as I present to you in the presence of the Lord the evidence of all the good things the Lord has done for you and your forefathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:14–16; Exodus 2:23–24; PSALMS 78:54–55"> After Jacob had gone to Egypt, your fathers cried out to the Lord for help, and he sent Moses and Aaron to lead your forefathers out of Egypt and settle them here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 10:7; Judges 3:12"> But they forgot about the Lord their God, so he abandoned them to Sisera, the commander of the army of Hazor, to the Philistines, and to the king of Moab, who attacked them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 10:10; Judges 2:13; Judges 3:7"> They cried out to the Lord for help and said, ‘We have sinned, for we have rejected the Lord and worshiped the Baals and Ashtoreths. Please save us from the hands of our enemies, and we will worship you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 4:6; Judges 8:35"> Then the Lord sent Gideon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Gideon”: Here called “Jerub-Baal.”">d</a></sup>Barak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Septuagint and Syriac reading. The Hebrew has “Bedan.”">e</a></sup>Jephthah and Samuel, and he saved you from your enemies who surrounded you so you could live in safety.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:23; 1 Samuel 8:5–7; Genesis 17:7"> But when you saw that Nahash, king of the Ammonites, was coming to attack you, you told me, ‘No, we want our own king,’ even though the Lord your God was your king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 8:5"> So here's the king you have chosen, the one you asked for. Look—the Lord now gives him to you as your king!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 24:14; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; PSALMS 81:12–15"> If you honor the Lord, worship him, do what he tells you, and don't rebel against the Lord's instructions, and if both you and your king follow the Lord your God, then good!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Isaiah 1:20; Isaiah 3:11"> However, if you refuse to do what he tells you, and rebel against the Lord's instructions, then the Lord will be against you as he was against your forefathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:13; Exodus 14:31; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Now stand still and watch what the Lord is going to do, right before your very eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Proverbs 26:1; 1 Samuel 7:9–10"> Isn't it the time of wheat harvest?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Rain did not usually occur at this time.">f</a></sup>Well, I will ask the Lord to send thunder and rain. Then you will realize how very evil you were in the Lord's sight when you demanded your own king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Ezra 10:9; PSALMS 106:12–13"> Then Samuel prayed to the Lord, and that very day the Lord sent thunder and rain. Everyone was in absolute awe of the Lord and of Samuel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; 1 John 5:16; 1 Samuel 12:23"> “Please pray to the Lord your God for us your servants, so we will not die!” they begged Samuel. “For we have added to all our sins the evil of asking for our own king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:16; Joshua 23:6; 1 Peter 3:16"> “Don't be afraid,” Samuel replied. “Even though you have indeed done all these evil things, don't give up following the Lord, but dedicate yourselves completely to worshiping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 10:15; Jeremiah 16:19"> Don't worship worthless idols that can't help you or save you, for they are nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 14:2; PSALMS 106:8"> The fact is because of the kind of person the Lord is, he won't give up on his people, because he was happy to claim you as his own.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:9; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> As for me, how could I sin against the Lord by no longer praying for you? I will also continue to teach you the way of goodness and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 5:12"> Make sure you honor God and worship him faithfully, with complete dedication. Think of all the tremendous things he has done for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Hosea 10:3; 1 Samuel 31:1–5"> But if you continue to do what is evil, you and your king will be wiped out.”</span>
</div>
</div>
