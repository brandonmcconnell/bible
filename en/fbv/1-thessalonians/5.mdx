<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> Brothers and sisters, we don't need to write anything to you about prophetic times and dates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> You yourselves know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> When people talk about peace and security suddenly they will be completely ruined. It will be just like the sudden onset of childbirth, and they certainly won't escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, brothers and sisters, are not in the dark about this so that you won't be caught by surprise when the Day of Judgment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Day.”">a</a></sup>arrives suddenly like a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> For you are all children of light and children of the day. We don't belong to the night or to darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> So then we shouldn't be sleeping like everybody else—we should stay awake and keep ourselves clear-headed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> For it's during the night that people sleep; and it's at night that they get drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> But since we belong to the day we should keep our heads clear. We strap on the breastplate of trust and love. We put on the hope of salvation as a helmet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God hasn't placed us in a position to be punished, but has reserved us for salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> He's the one who died for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“For” in the sense of “for the sake of” rather than “in place of.”">b</a></sup>us so that whether we're alive or dead we shall live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> So encourage and strengthen one another, just as you are doing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> Brothers and sisters, we're asking you to respect those who work with you, who lead you in the Lord and teach you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> You should value them highly in love for the work they do. Live in peace with each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> We urge you, brothers and sisters, to warn those who are lazy, encourage those who are anxious, help those who are weak, and be patient with everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> Make sure none of you pays back evil for evil, but always try to do good to one another, and to everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Always be full of joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> never stop praying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> be thankful in every situation—because this is what God in Christ Jesus wants you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Don't hold the Spirit back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> don't look down on prophecy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> make sure to check everything. Hold on to whatever is good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> keep away from every kind of evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> May the God of peace himself make you completely holy, and may your whole being—body, mind and spirit—be kept blameless for when our Lord Jesus Christ returns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> The one who calls you is trustworthy, and he will do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:18–19; Philippians 1:19; Philemon 1:22"> Brothers and sisters, pray for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Corinthians 16:20"> Greet all the believers there affectionately.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “with a holy kiss.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 4:16; Acts 19:13; 1 Timothy 1:3"> I'm requiring you by the Lord that this letter be read to all the believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:23"> May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
</div>
