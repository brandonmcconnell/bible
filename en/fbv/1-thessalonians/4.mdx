<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Peter 3:18"> A few more things: Brothers and sisters, we plead with you and urge you in the Lord Jesus to behave in ways that please God, just as we instructed you. Of course you're already doing this, just do it more and more!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> You remember the instructions we gave you on behalf of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> What God wants is for you to live holy lives. So stay away from sexual immorality</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> in order that each of you may control yourselves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This could also be translated as “know how to take a wife.”">a</a></sup>in a way that is holy and respectful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> not indulging passionate lust like the heathen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Heathen,” meaning “Gentiles,” or “other nations.”">b</a></sup>who have no knowledge of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> Don't cheat or take advantage of another Christian in these matters, for the Lord is the one who executes justice in all such things, as we've already clearly explained to you, and warned you about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> For God did not call us to live impure lives, but holy lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> So anyone who rejects this position isn't just rejecting what a human being is saying; they are rejecting God, who gives his Holy Spirit to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> We certainly don't need to write and tell you to love fellow-believers, because God teaches you to love one another—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> and in fact you are demonstrating this love to all the believers throughout Macedonia. Even so we want to encourage you, brothers and sisters, to love more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> Try to live a quiet life, minding your own business, doing your own work, as we've already explained to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> so that your way of life may be respected by non-Christians and you won't have to rely on anyone to provide what you need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> We don't want you to be confused about what happens when people die,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “fall asleep.”">c</a></sup>brothers and sisters, so that you won't grieve like others who don't have any hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> Since we're convinced that Jesus died and rose again, we're also sure that God will bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Bring… those,” obviously those who are resurrected from the earth, from the context of the two following verses.">d</a></sup>with Jesus those who have died trusting in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> What we're telling you comes from the Lord: Those of us who are alive and still here when the Lord comes certainly won't precede those who have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> For the Lord himself will come down from heaven with a commanding shout, with the cry of the archangel, and with the sound of God's trumpet, and those who have died in Christ will rise first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> Then those of us who are alive and still here will be carried up together with them into the clouds, and we shall meet the Lord in the air. Then we will be with the Lord forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Luke 21:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> So encourage one another with these words.</span>
</div>
