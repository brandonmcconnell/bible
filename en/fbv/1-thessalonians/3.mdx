<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:22–23; Ephesians 6:21–22; Philippians 2:19–25"> and send Timothy on to you. He is our brother and God's co-worker in spreading the good news of Christ. We sent him to strengthen and encourage you in your trust in God</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Romans 5:3; Acts 9:16"> so that none of you would be upset by your troubles—for you know we should expect such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; 2 Corinthians 8:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> Even while we were with you we kept on warning you that we would suffer persecution soon— and as you well know that is exactly what has happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:13–14; Matthew 4:3; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> This is why, when I couldn't stand it anymore, I sent to find out whether you were still trusting in God. I was concerned that the devil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The devil,” Literally, “the tempter.”">a</a></sup>had successfully tempted you and that all our work had been for nothing!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Successfully tempted”—the original is actually just “tempted” but in English this doesn't imply what Paul fears—that the temptation has succeeded. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> Now Timothy has just returned from visiting you and he has brought us good news of your trust in God and the love you have. He's told us that you still have fond memories of us, longing to see us just as we long to see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> This news really encouraged us while we were suffering troubles ourselves, brothers and sisters, knowing that you continue to hold on to your trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> For us life is worth living now because you're standing firm in the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 71:14–15; PSALMS 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> As we go into the presence of our God, how can we ever thank God enough for you because of all the joy you bring to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> Night and day we pray for you as earnestly as possible, hoping to see you again face to face, and to help you continue developing your trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> May God our Father and our Lord Jesus make it possible for us to come to see you soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> May the Lord increase your love so it overflows to one another, and to everyone, just as we love you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> In this way may the Lord strengthen you so you may stand with minds that are holy and blameless before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his holy ones.</span>
