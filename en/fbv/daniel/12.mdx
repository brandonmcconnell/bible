<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:7; Matthew 24:21; Mark 13:19"> “At that time Michael the great prince, the protector of your people, will stand up and there will be a time of trouble like never before, since nations first existed. But at that time your people will be saved, all whose names are written in the book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Isaiah 26:19; Matthew 25:46"> Millions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Millions”: the usual translation of “many” seems inappropriate here. It really signifies “a great number” which in modern-day idiom would be “millions.”">a</a></sup>sleeping in the ground in death will awake, some to eternal life, and some to eternal shame and disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Proverbs 4:18; James 5:19–20"> Those who are wise will shine as bright as the sky; those who have shown many the right way to live will shine as bright as the stars forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:9; Revelation 22:10; Daniel 8:26"> But as for you, Daniel, keep this message secret, and seal the book closed until the time of the end. Many will search this way and that way,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Theodotion in his Greek translation renders this as “peruse carefully,” which would then mean that the increase in knowledge would refer to an increased understanding of the prophecy.">b</a></sup>and knowledge will grow greater and greater.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 10:4–6; Daniel 10:16; Daniel 10:10"> Then I, Daniel, noticed two others, each standing on opposite sides of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 8:13; Ezekiel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12–13"> One of them asked the man dressed in linen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 10:4.">c</a></sup>who was above the river's waters, “How long before these outrageous things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 11:36.">d</a></sup>come to an end?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 8:24"> The man dressed in linen, who was above the river's waters, lifted both hands to heaven and made a solemn promise by the One who lives eternally. I heard him say, “It will last for a time, times, and half a time. When the scattering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Scattering”: or, “shattering.”">e</a></sup>of the power of the holy people has come to an end, then all these things will also come to an end.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Peter 1:11; Daniel 10:14"> I heard the answer, but I didn't understand it. So I asked, “My lord, what's the final result of all this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 10:4"> “Daniel, you can go on your way now,” he replied, “for this message is secret and kept sealed until the time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:11; Daniel 11:35; Hosea 14:9"> Many will be purified and cleansed and refined,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 11:35.">f</a></sup>but the wicked will go on being wicked. None of the wicked will understand, but the wise will understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 11:31; Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14"> From the time the continual ministry is stopped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See 8:11, 11:31.">g</a></sup>in order to set up the idolatry that causes devastation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See 9:27, 11:31.">h</a></sup>will be one thousand two hundred and ninety days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Daniel 8:14; Romans 11:15"> Blessed are those who patiently wait for and reach the one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Zechariah 3:7"> But as for you, go your way until your life ends, and then rest. You will rise to receive your reward at the end of time.”</span>
