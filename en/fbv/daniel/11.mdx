<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="DAN" data-book-slug="daniel" data-book-name="Daniel" data-chapter="11" data-verses="45">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Daniel 5:31; Daniel 10:18"> And I myself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“I myself” refers to the angel, not to Daniel. His speech continues until 12:4.">a</a></sup>in the first year of Darius the Mede, took my stand to support and defend him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The meaning of this verse is debated, however this statement clearly continues the speech of God's messenger. The next chapter really begins at verse 2.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:5–6; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 21:5"> So now let me reveal the truth to you. There are still three kings to come to power in Persia, and then a fourth who will be far richer than all the others. When he becomes strong through his wealth, he will rally the whole kingdom against Greece.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Rally the whole kingdom against Greece.” Alternatively, this could be read as “rally all the kingdoms of Greece,” though this is a minority opinion.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 8:21"> Then a mighty king will come to power. He will rule with great authority and do whatever he wants.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Do whatever he wants”: see 8:4and 11:16and 11:36.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:22; Daniel 8:8; Jeremiah 12:17"> But as he extends his power his kingdom will be broken up, divided towards the four winds of heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Towards the four winds of heaven”: in other words, “into four parts.”">e</a></sup>It will not go to his descendants, and it will not be ruled as he ruled. It will be pulled up and given to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 11:14; Daniel 11:11; Daniel 11:25"> The king of the south will grow strong, but one of his officers will grow even stronger and will rule his kingdom with great authority.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:13; Daniel 11:40; Daniel 11:7"> Some years later they will form an alliance, and the daughter of the king of the south will be married to the king of the north to guarantee the peace treaty. However, she will not be able to keep her hold on power, nor will his power<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “offspring.”">f</a></sup>continue. She and her attendants will be betrayed, along with her child and husband.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Husband”: literally, “the one supporting her,” generally understood to mean her husband.">g</a></sup>Later on, however,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 11:38–39; Daniel 11:19–20; Isaiah 9:14"> a new king of the south from her family will take over. He will come to attack the army of the king of the north and enter his fortress. He will fight against them and will win.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 43:12–13"> In addition he will take back with him to Egypt the idols of their gods, along with their expensive vessels of silver and gold. For some years he will leave the king of the north alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the king of the north will march into the kingdom of the king of the south but will have to retreat to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Daniel 11:7; Daniel 11:26"> However, his sons will prepare for war, assembling a huge number of troops. One of them will lead an advance that rushes forward like a river bursting its banks, crossing over and pressing forward to attack the enemy fortress.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 11:5; PSALMS 33:16–17"> This will infuriate the king of the south, who will go out into battle against the large army assembled by the king of the north and will defeat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 12:22–23; 2 Kings 14:10; Isaiah 10:7–12"> After capturing such a large army, he will become very proud, and will kill thousands. But this triumph will not last long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:16; Daniel 12:7; Daniel 11:6–7"> Years later the king of the north will once again raise an army, even larger than before, and will invade with this huge army, accompanied by plenty of supplies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Acts 4:25–28"> At the same time many will rebel against the king of the south. Violent men from your own people will rebel in order to fulfill this vision,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“This vision”: referring back to the vision beginning in 8:13.">h</a></sup>but they shall fail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Ezekiel 17:17; Ezekiel 4:2"> Then the king of the north will come and build siege ramps and he will capture a city with strong defenses. The forces of the south will not be able to prevent it—even his best troops won't be able to resist the attack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 8:9; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 11:3"> The invader will do whatever he wants;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Do whatever he wants”: see 8:4and 11:3and 11:36">i</a></sup>no one will be able to oppose him. He will stand in the Beautiful Land<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Israel.">j</a></sup>with the power to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Ezekiel 4:3; Ezekiel 4:7"> He will be determined to come with all the power of his kingdom and will make a peace treaty with the king of the south. To secure this he will give him a daughter of women to marry in order to destroy the kingdom. But she will not be successful and will not support him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:14; Jeremiah 31:10; Zephaniah 2:11"> Then he will turn to attack the coastlands and will conquer many, but a commander will put a stop to his arrogant behavior, paying him back for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; PSALMS 37:36; Job 20:8"> He will return to the fortresses of his own land, but he will stumble and fall, and will be gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:17; Daniel 11:7; Deuteronomy 15:2–3"> His successor will send out a tax collector to maintain royal wealth. However, in a short time he will die, but not violently or in battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 11:34; Daniel 8:25; Judges 9:1–20"> A contemptible person will follow him who will not be given royal majesty. He will take over peacably<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“Peacably” or “in a time of peace.” This word is repeated in verse 23, and contrasts with the violence mentioned in verse 22.">k</a></sup>and assume control of the kingdom through deception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–11; Daniel 11:10; Daniel 9:26"> Great armies will be swept away before him. They will be broken, as well as the prince of the agreement.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="See 9:27.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 8:25; 2 Thessalonians 2:9; PSALMS 52:2"> After making an alliance with him, he will act deceptively. He will come to power peacably, becoming strong though only having a few supporters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Proverbs 23:7; Matthew 9:4"> He will invade the richest parts of the land and do what his fathers and forefathers never did—he will distribute plunder, spoil, and possessions. He will make plans to attack fortresses, but only for a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Daniel 11:2; Proverbs 28:25"> Then he will draw on his strength and courage to gather a large army against the king of the south. The king of the south will prepare for war, fighting back with a large and powerful army, but he will not be successful because of plots made against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:10; Daniel 11:40; 2 Samuel 4:2–12"> Those closest to him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="“Those closest to him”: literally, “those who eat his royal food.”">m</a></sup>will destroy him. His army will be wiped out—many will fall in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2; Jeremiah 9:3–5; Habakkuk 2:3"> The two kings, with evil intentions, will tell each other lies even as they sit together at the same table. But their scheming is pointless—the end will come at the time predicted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 11:30–32; Daniel 8:24; Acts 3:25"> The king of the north will return to his own country with all the wealth he has looted.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“He has looted”: implied.">n</a></sup>He will be determined to attack<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="“Be determined to attack”: literally, “set his heart against.” In addition, the word “people” is added as an explanation, since the action taken is against them as believers.">o</a></sup>the people of the holy agreement, and do all he can to destroy it before returning to his own country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Isaiah 14:31; Daniel 11:25"> At the time predicted he will return to invade the south again, but this time it won't be like before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:24; Jeremiah 2:10"> Ships from Cyprus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew: “Kittim.”">p</a></sup>will come to attack him, frightening him off so that he retreats. But this will make him mad, and he will attack the people of the holy agreement, recognizing those who abandon their commitment to the holy agreement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 12:11; Daniel 9:27; Matthew 24:15"> His forces will occupy and defile the Temple fortress. They will put a stop to the continual service,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="“Continual service”: see on 8:11.">q</a></sup>and set up a form of idolatry that causes devastation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="“Idolatry that causes devastation”: see 9:27.">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; PSALMS 9:10; John 17:3"> The king will use flattery to corrupt those who break the solemn agreement,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="“Solemn agreement”: or “covenant,” but this word is not often used today outside of legal contexts.">s</a></sup>but the people who know their God will stand firm in their resistance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 16:2; Malachi 2:7"> Wise leaders of the people will teach many, though for a time they will be killed by sword and fire, or they will be imprisoned and robbed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Romans 16:18; Acts 20:29–30"> During this time of persecution they will receive a little help, and many who join them won't be sincere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Malachi 3:2–4; Revelation 2:10"> Some of the wise will be killed, so that they may be refined and purified and cleansed until the time of the end, for the predicted time is still to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Deuteronomy 10:17"> The king will do whatever he wants,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="“Do whatever he wants”: see 8:4and 11:3and 11:16.">t</a></sup>praising himself and considering himself greater than any god, even saying outrageous things against the God of gods. He will be successful until the time of anger has finished, for what has been decided will be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13; 1 Timothy 4:3"> He will have no time for the gods of his forefathers, nor for the one loved by women, nor for any other god, for he considers himself greater than any of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Revelation 17:1–5; Isaiah 44:9; Revelation 13:12–17"> Instead he will honor the god of fortresses—a god unknown to his forefathers—giving him offerings of gold and silver and precious stones and expensive gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Revelation 18:9–13"> He will deal with strong fortresses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Whether these are his fortresses or those he attacks is not clear.">u</a></sup>with the help of this foreign god. He will give riches to those who acknowledge him, making them rulers over the people, and distributing the land at a price.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Daniel 11:35; Daniel 11:10; Isaiah 5:28"> At the time of the end the king of the south will attack him. But the king of the north will retaliate with force like a storm, with chariots and horsemen and many ships. He will advance, sweeping through many lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:6; Isaiah 11:13–15"> He will invade the Beautiful Land<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Referring to Israel.">v</a></sup>and kill many people there. However, Moab, Edom, and most of the Ammonites will escape his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10–11; Ezekiel 29:14; Revelation 11:8"> He will extend his attacks against different countries—even the land of Egypt will not be able to escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; Nahum 3:9; Ezekiel 30:4–5"> He will acquire the gold and silver and riches of Egypt, ruling over them and also the Libyans and Ethiopians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–21; Revelation 16:12; Revelation 17:13"> But news from the east and the north will alarm him, and in a furious rage he will set out to destroy and exterminate many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Daniel 8:25; Revelation 19:19–21"> He will set up his royal camp between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain. But he will die with no one to help him.</span>
</div>
</div>
