<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:4; Daniel 9:2; Daniel 7:28"> In the third year of King Belshazzar's reign I, Daniel, saw another vision after the one I had seen previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 1:2; Nehemiah 1:1; Genesis 10:22"> In my vision I looked around and saw I was in the castle at Susa in the province of Elam. In the vision I was beside the River Ulai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 8:20; Daniel 10:5; Zechariah 6:1"> I looked around and saw a ram standing beside the river. It had two long horns, one longer than the other, even though the longer one had grown last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 8:7"> I watched the ram charging west, north, and south. No animal could stand up to it—nor was there any chance of rescue from its power. It did whatever it wanted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Compare 11:3, 11:16, 11:36.">a</a></sup>and grew powerful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:3; Daniel 8:8"> As I was thinking about what I'd seen, a male goat came in from the west, racing in across the surface of the earth so fast it didn't touch the ground. It had a large, prominent horn between its eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> It approached the ram with the two horns which I had seen standing beside the river, rushing in to attack in a furious rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 11:11; Joshua 8:20"> I watched as the goat charged furiously at the ram, hitting it and breaking off its two horns. The ram did not have the strength to resist the goat's attack. The goat threw the ram to the ground, trampling on it, and there was no possibility of rescuing it from the goat's power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:2; Revelation 7:1; Daniel 5:20"> The goat grew very powerful, but at the height of its power its large horn was broken off. In its place four large horns came up, pointing to the four winds of heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Four winds of heaven”: north, south, east, and west.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Ezekiel 20:6; Ezekiel 20:15"> A little horn came up from one of them, and grew extremely powerful to the south and to the east and to the Beautiful Land.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“The beautiful land”: a reference to the land of Israel.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13; Revelation 12:4; Daniel 8:7"> It grew in power until it reached the heavenly army, throwing some of them and some of the stars down to the earth and trampled on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:11; Revelation 13:5–7; Daniel 8:25"> It even tried to make itself as great as the Prince of the heavenly army—it removed the continual service,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Continual service”: the word here refers to the continuing sanctuary services that were carried out on a daily basis. (The word used here simply says “daily.”) Some translations restrict this to “daily sacrifice” but the daily ministry in the sanctuary involved much more than this. The same term is used in verses 12 and 13, and 11:31. The continual service was initiated in Exodus 29:38. It was expected to be continual (Leviticus 6:13, Numbers 28:1-15).">d</a></sup>and the place of his sanctuary was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:1; Isaiah 59:14; Daniel 11:31–36"> An army of people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The army referenced here is clearly different from the heavenly army mentioned in verse 10. The word for “army” or “host” can simply mean a large group.">e</a></sup>and the continual service were handed over to it because of rebellion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Hebrew of the first part of this verse is unclear.">f</a></sup>and it overthrew truth, and it was successful in everything it did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Revelation 6:10; Luke 21:24"> Then I heard a holy one speaking, and another holy one asked the one who was speaking, “How long is this vision for—the removal of the continual service, the rebellion that causes devastation, the handing over of the sanctuary and the army of people to be trampled down?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 11:2–3"> He replied, “For two thousand three hundred evenings and mornings, then the sanctuary will be cleansed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Cleansed”: some have suggested “justified” or “restored,” however the Septuagint and the Vulgate both read “cleansed.”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Revelation 1:13; 1 Peter 1:10–11"> As I, Daniel, tried to work out what this vision meant suddenly I saw someone who looked like a man standing in front of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Luke 1:26; Daniel 8:2"> I also heard a human voice calling from the River Ulai, “Gabriel, explain to this man the meaning of the vision.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19; Daniel 12:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> As he approached me, I was terrified and fell facedown before him. “Son of man,” he told me, “You need to understand that this vision refers to the time of the end.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:16"> As he spoke to me I lost consciousness as I laid facedown on the ground. But he took hold of me and helped me to my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Daniel 11:27; Revelation 17:17"> He told me, “Pay attention! I'm going to explain to you what's going to happen during the time of anger, which refers to the appointed time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:3; Daniel 11:1–2"> The ram with two horns that you saw symbolizes the kings of Media and Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:20; Daniel 11:3; Daniel 8:5–8"> The male goat is the kingdom of Greece, and the large horn between its eyes is its first king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 11:4; Daniel 8:3; Daniel 8:8"> The four horns that came up in place of the large horn that was broken represent the four kingdoms that arose from that nation, but not as powerful as the first.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 13:11–14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 19:20"> When those kingdoms come to an end, when their sins have reached their fullest extent, a ferocious and treacherous kingdom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Kingdom” literally, “king,” but here expressive of more than a single person.">h</a></sup>will rise to power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 8:12; Daniel 11:31–36; Daniel 7:25"> It will become very powerful but not by its own power. It will be terribly destructive, and will succeed in everything it does. It will destroy great leaders and God's dedicated people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 8:11; Revelation 17:14; Job 34:20"> Through its deviousness, its lies will be convincing and successful. It shows its arrogance both in thought and action, destroying those who thought they were perfectly safe. It even fights in opposition against the Prince of princes, but it will be defeated, though not by any human power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 10:1; Daniel 12:9; Daniel 12:4"> The vision about the evenings and mornings that has been explained to you is true, but for now seal up this vision because it refers to the distant future.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:7"> After this, I, Daniel, became exhausted, and I was sick for days. Then I got up and went back to working for the king, but I was devastated at what I'd seen in vision and I couldn't understand it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Footnotes

<ol className="fnl">
<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">8:4</span> Compare 11:3, 11:16, 11:36.</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">8:8</span> “Four winds of heaven”: north, south, east, and west.</li>
<li id="fn-c"><a href="#fnref-c" className="fnb">c</a> <span className="fnr">8:9</span> “The beautiful land”: a reference to the land of Israel.</li>
<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">8:11</span> “Continual service”: the word here refers to the continuing sanctuary services that were carried out on a daily basis. (The word used here simply says “daily.”) Some translations restrict this to “daily sacrifice” but the daily ministry in the sanctuary involved much more than this. The same term is used in verses 12 and 13, and 11:31. The continual service was initiated in Exodus 29:38. It was expected to be continual (Leviticus 6:13, Numbers 28:1-15).</li>
<li id="fn-e"><a href="#fnref-e" className="fnb">e</a> <span className="fnr">8:12</span> The army referenced here is clearly different from the heavenly army mentioned in verse 10. The word for “army” or “host” can simply mean a large group.</li>
<li id="fn-f"><a href="#fnref-f" className="fnb">f</a> <span className="fnr">8:12</span> The Hebrew of the first part of this verse is unclear.</li>
<li id="fn-g"><a href="#fnref-g" className="fnb">g</a> <span className="fnr">8:14</span> “Cleansed”: some have suggested “justified” or “restored,” however the Septuagint and the Vulgate both read “cleansed.”</li>
<li id="fn-h"><a href="#fnref-h" className="fnb">h</a> <span className="fnr">8:23</span> “Kingdom” literally, “king,” but here expressive of more than a single person.</li>
</ol>
