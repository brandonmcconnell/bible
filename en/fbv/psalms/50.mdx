<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord, the Almighty God, speaks! He summons everyone on earth, from the east to the west.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 48:2; PSALMS 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">God shines out of Mount Zion, perfect in beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God arrives, and does not stay quiet. Fire flames before him, burning everything up; a violent storm rages around him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He summons the heavens above and the earth to witness the judgment of his people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Bring me those who trust in me—those who confirmed the agreement</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “covenant,” also verse 16.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with me through sacrifice.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:6; PSALMS 75:7; PSALMS 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heavens declare his decisions are right, for God himself is the judge. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; PSALMS 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“My people, listen to what I have to say. I am bringing charges against you, Israel. I am God, your God!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm not complaining about your sacrifices or burnt offerings that you offer all the time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; PSALMS 69:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I do not require bulls from your barns or goats from your pens,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; PSALMS 8:6–8; PSALMS 104:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">for I own all the animals of the forest, and the cattle on a thousand hills belong to me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know every bird of the mountains; all living things in the fields are mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; PSALMS 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I were hungry, I wouldn't tell you, for the earth and everything in it are mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do I eat the flesh of bulls or drink the blood of goats?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; PSALMS 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give an offering of thanks to God; keep the promises you made to the Most High,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 107:28; PSALMS 91:15; PSALMS 34:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">and call on me when you're in trouble. I will rescue you, and you will thank me.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; PSALMS 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But to the wicked God says, “What's the point of mechanically repeating my laws or making empty promises about obeying the agreement?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You hate my discipline,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “teaching.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you toss my words away, leaving them behind you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you see people stealing, you admire them; you associate with adulterers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 52:2–4; PSALMS 55:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">With your mouth you say evil things; you use your tongue to spread lies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sit there and speak against your brother, slandering your own mother's son.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I kept quiet when you did these things. You thought I was someone just like you. But now I confront you, and bring my charges against you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; PSALMS 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Think again, you people who dismiss God, or I will tear you apart, and no one will be able to save you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 91:16; James 3:13; PSALMS 24:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But those who give an offering of thanks honor me, and to those who follow the right I will show them the salvation of God.”</span></span>
