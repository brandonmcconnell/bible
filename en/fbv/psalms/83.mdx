<div className="hs">A song. A psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 28:1; PSALMS 35:22; PSALMS 50:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, please do not stay silent! You can't remain unmoved! God, you must not keep quiet!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can't you hear the roars of your enemies? Can't you see how those who hate you are defiantly lifting up their heads?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 64:2; PSALMS 27:5; PSALMS 31:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">They invent cunning plans to conspire against your people; they plot against those you treasure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; PSALMS 74:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They say, “Come on! Let's destroy them as a nation so the name ‘Israel’ will be completely forgotten.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They all agree in their conspiracy; they've made a treaty together to attack you—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; PSALMS 137:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">the people of Edom, the Ishmaelites, Moab, and the Hagirites;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">the people of Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia, and the inhabitants of Tyre.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Assyria too has joined them, allied with the descendants of Lot. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do to them what you did to Midian, what you did to Sisera and Jabin</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sisera and Jabin: see Judges 4.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at the Kishon River.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">They were destroyed at Endor and became like manure to fertilize the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb; all their rulers like Zebah and Zalmunna,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Oreb and Zeeb; Zebah and Zalmunna: see Judges 6and Judges 7.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; PSALMS 83:4; PSALMS 74:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">for they said, “Let's take the pastures of God for ourselves!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; PSALMS 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">My God, make them like whirling tumbleweeds, chaff blown away by the wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Just like fire that burns the forest, a flame that sets fire to the mountains,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; PSALMS 50:3; PSALMS 58:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">in the same way chase them down with your storm, terrify them with your whirlwind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 9:19–20; PSALMS 6:10; PSALMS 109:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shame them in defeat</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “fill their faces with shame.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that they come to you, Lord!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 35:4; PSALMS 40:14–15; PSALMS 35:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make them ashamed, terrify them forever so that they die in disgrace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; PSALMS 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them understand that you alone, called the Lord, are the Most High who rules over all the earth.</span></span>
