<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the music director. According to “Do Not Destroy.” A psalm (miktam) of David, concerning the time Saul sent out soldiers to watch David's house in order to kill him.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; PSALMS 18:48; PSALMS 57:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue me from my enemies, God! Protect me from those who are attacking me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 139:19; PSALMS 26:9; PSALMS 55:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue me from these evil people! Save me from these murderers!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 56:6; PSALMS 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">See how they're lying in wait to kill me! Powerful men are gathered against me, Lord, even though I have not sinned or done anything wrong.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This could be either some offense against the enemies or against God.">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even though I'm not guilty they hurry to prepare an attack on me. Please stand up, come and help me, and see what's going on.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 84:8; PSALMS 55:15; PSALMS 9:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are God, the Lord Almighty, God of Israel. Stand up, and punish all the nations. Don't have any mercy on these evil traitors. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 59:14; PSALMS 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the evening they return, snarling like dogs as they roam around the city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; PSALMS 10:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">See what evil talk comes pouring out of their mouths! Words sharp as swords, for they say to themselves,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Who can hear us?”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; PSALMS 37:13; PSALMS 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Lord, you laugh at them; you pour scorn on all the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 62:2; PSALMS 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are my strength! I will look to you, God, for you are the one who protects me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 54:7; PSALMS 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his trustworthy love, God will come and save me. He will show me how my enemies are defeated.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't kill them right away, otherwise my people will quickly forget what you have done.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Implied—the text simply says “lest my people forget.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">By your power make them stagger around and then fall down, Lord our protection.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; PSALMS 10:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be caught out by the evil things they say, the words they proudly speak—brought down by their curses and the lies they tell!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 83:18; PSALMS 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Destroy them in your anger! Destroy them completely! Then everyone will know that God rules in Israel!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Jacob.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 22:16; PSALMS 59:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the evening they return, snarling like dogs as they roam around the city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; PSALMS 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">They wander around, looking for things to eat, and howl when they're not satisfied.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will sing of your strength; in the morning I will sing for joy about your trustworthy love. For you have been my protection; my place of safety in my time of trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 59:9–10; PSALMS 46:1; PSALMS 18:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are my strength, I sing praises to you, for you are the one who protects me—the God who shows me his trustworthy love!</span></span>
