<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I go up to heaven, you are there. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 19:6; PSALMS 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I were to fly away on wings of the dawn to the east; if I were to live on the far western shore of the sea,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; PSALMS 73:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">even there your hand would lead me, your right hand would support me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; PSALMS 94:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I asked the darkness to hide me, and light to become night around me,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 3:5; PSALMS 139:3; PSALMS 40:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I tried to count them, they would be more than every grain of sand. <span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 5:6; PSALMS 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, if you would only kill the wicked! Murderers,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “men of blood.”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">get away from me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; PSALMS 74:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">When they speak of you they're being deceptively evil. Since they are your enemies, they call on you in vain.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 119:158; PSALMS 31:6; PSALMS 26:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, don't I hate those who hate you? I despise those who rebel against you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hate them with absolute hatred—they have become my enemies!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Examine me carefully, God, so you can be sure of my true feelings. Check me out so you can know what I really think.</span><br /></span>
