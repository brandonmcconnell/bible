<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 37:23; PSALMS 119:133; PSALMS 27:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He pulled me out of the pit of destruction,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word used here is usually translated “tumult” or “roaring” but is here thought to mean “desolation” or “destruction.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out from the mud and the slime. He set me on a rock, and gave me a safe place to stand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 33:3; PSALMS 103:1–5; PSALMS 34:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He gave me a new song to sing, a song of praise to our God. Many people will see and be in awe, and they will put their trust in him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; PSALMS 84:11–12; PSALMS 34:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Happy are those who trust in the Lord, who do not look for help from the arrogant or from those who worship idols.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It is unclear from the text whether people or false gods are being described here.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; PSALMS 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord my God, you have done so many wonderful things for us, and you have so many plans for us. No one compares to you. I want to tell all that you've done—blessings too many to count.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You don't want sacrifices and offerings. Now you have helped me understand</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “you have dug ears for me.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—you're not asking for burnt offerings or sin offerings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, “Look! Here I am! I've come to do what is written about me in the scroll of the Book.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am pleased to do your will, my God—your Law guides my mind.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “your Law is in the midst of my intestines.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; PSALMS 22:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have announced the good news of salvation to the whole congregation.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Probably referring to the large meetings at the Temple.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I don't keep quiet, Lord, as you know.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have not kept the truth of your goodness hidden inside me—I have spoken of your trustworthiness and your salvation. I have not concealed your unfailing love and your faithfulness from the whole congregation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 23:6; PSALMS 43:3; PSALMS 57:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, please don't keep back your mercy from me. May your trustworthy love and faithfulness always be my protection.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 38:4; PSALMS 73:26; PSALMS 69:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I'm experiencing so many problems—I can't even count them! My sins have piled up so high I can't see over them. They're more than the number of hairs on my head! I feel like giving up!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “my heart fails me.”">f</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 25:17–18; PSALMS 70:1–5; PSALMS 38:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, please save me! Hurry, Lord, help me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 35:26; PSALMS 35:4; PSALMS 71:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please defeat and humiliate the people who are trying to kill me; turn back in disgrace those who want to hurt me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 35:21; PSALMS 69:24–25; PSALMS 35:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">May they be shocked at their defeat, those who taunted me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 35:27; PSALMS 68:3; Acts 19:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But may everyone who comes to you be happy and glad; may those who love your salvation always say, “The Lord is great!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I am poor and needy. May the Lord think about me. Help me! Save me! My God, please don't wait!</span></span>
