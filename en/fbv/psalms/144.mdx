<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 18:34; 2 Samuel 22:35; PSALMS 18:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord—he is my rock. He trains me for battle, he gives me skill for war.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:2–3; PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 91:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He is the one who faithfully loves me, protects me, and defends me. He is the one who rescues me, shields me from danger, and keeps me safe. He defeats nations and places them under my rule.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, what are human beings that you should care about them? What are people that you should concern yourself with them?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 102:11; PSALMS 39:5–6; PSALMS 109:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Humanity is like a breath; their lives are like a passing shadow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 104:32; PSALMS 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Part your heavens and come down.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Psalms 18:9.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Touch the mountains so that they give off smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; PSALMS 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> <span className="pl pl-1">Scatter your enemies</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“You enemies”: implied.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with flashes of lightning! Let your arrows fly and send them running in confusion!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 69:1–2; PSALMS 18:16; PSALMS 54:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Stretch down your hand from heaven and set me free. Rescue me from raging waters, from the oppression of foreign enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2; PSALMS 41:6; PSALMS 62:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are such liars, even telling lies under oath.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “their right hand is a right hand of falsehood”—referring to the practice of raising the right hand when swearing an oath.">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 33:2–3; PSALMS 40:3; PSALMS 149:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, I will sing a new song to you, accompanied by a ten-stringed harp,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 18:50; PSALMS 140:7; PSALMS 33:16–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">to you, the one who gives victory to kings. You saved your servant David from death by the sword.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; PSALMS 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Set me free. Rescue me from the oppression of foreign enemies. They are such liars, even telling lies under oath.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then our sons will grow up like plants in their youth and become mature, and our daughters will be like beautiful pillars carved to support a palace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our storehouses will be full of all kinds of crops; our flocks of sheep will grow by thousands, increasing by tens of thousands in the pastures.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our cattle will grow fat. No one will break down our city walls, there will be no exile, no cries of mourning in our town squares.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; PSALMS 146:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people who live like this will be happy. Happy are those whose God is the Lord.</span></span>
