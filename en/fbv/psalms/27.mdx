<div className="hs">A psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; PSALMS 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is my light and my salvation! How could anyone scare me? The Lord is my life's fortress! How could anyone terrify me?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 118:12; PSALMS 14:4; PSALMS 9:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">When evil people come at me to tear me apart, it's my enemies, those who are against me, they're the ones who trip and fall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if an army surrounds me, I won't be frightened. Even if I am attacked, I will still trust in God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 26:8; PSALMS 23:6; PSALMS 65:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I only ask the Lord for one thing: I just want to live in the house of the Lord throughout my life, contemplating the Lord's goodness and thinking about his Temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; PSALMS 31:20; PSALMS 138:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">When troubles come he will protect me and keep me safe, hiding me in his house,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“House”: literally, “tent.” Also verse 6.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as secure as if he'd placed me on a high rock.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; PSALMS 107:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will hold my head high over my enemies who are all around me. I shall offer sacrifices in his house with shouts of joy, and I will sing praises to the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 130:2–4; PSALMS 13:3; PSALMS 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to me, Lord, when I call for help. Be gracious and answer me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You told me, “Come to me.” I am coming to you, Lord.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “I told myself, ‘I will go to the Lord.’ Lord, I am coming to you.”">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 69:17; PSALMS 143:7; PSALMS 102:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't refuse to talk to me. Don't be angry with your servant and turn away. You are the one who helps me, so please don't reject me and abandon me, God of my salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if my father and my mother abandon me, the Lord will take care of me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 25:4–5; PSALMS 5:8; PSALMS 86:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, teach me your way and lead me along the right path, because my enemies are against me</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Are against me”: implied.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 31:8; PSALMS 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> <span className="pl pl-1">don't hand me over to them and their evil intentions, for they are making false accusations against me and threatening me with violence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">However, I'm absolutely sure that I will see the Lord's goodness even in this life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; PSALMS 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Trust in the Lord! Be confident—he will give you courage! Trust in the Lord!</span></span>
