<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 18:6; PSALMS 130:1–2; PSALMS 142:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, please hear my prayer, my cry for help!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 69:17; PSALMS 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't hide your face from me in my time of trouble! Turn and listen to me, and answer me quickly when I call.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; PSALMS 37:20; Job 30:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">My life is disappearing like smoke; my body feels like it's on fire!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; PSALMS 37:2; PSALMS 77:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm like grass that's dried up, withered away—I even forget to eat!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; PSALMS 6:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All my groaning has worn me out; my bones show through my skin.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm like a desert owl,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Owl”: some identify this bird as a pelican or cormorant.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a little owl among the ruins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 77:4; PSALMS 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> <span className="pl pl-1">I can't sleep. I'm like a lonely bird on a rooftop.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; PSALMS 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">My enemies taunt me all day long. They mock me and swear at me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 42:3; PSALMS 80:5; Micah 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ashes are the food I eat; my tears drip into my drink,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 38:3; PSALMS 90:7–9; PSALMS 147:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">because of your anger and hostility, for you have picked me up and tossed me away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; PSALMS 109:23; PSALMS 144:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My life is fading away like a shadow that lengthens—I'm withering away like grass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Lord, reign</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “sit.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">forever, your fame will last for all generations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will take action and have pity on Jerusalem, for it's time to be kind to the city, the time has come.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the people who follow you love its stones; they value even its dust!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; PSALMS 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the nations will be in awe of who you are, Lord; all the kings of the earth will be in awe of your glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; PSALMS 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord will rebuild Jerusalem; he will appear in glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; PSALMS 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will pay attention to the prayers of the homeless; he will not disregard their requests.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 79:11; PSALMS 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">to respond to the groans of prisoners, to set free the children of death.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Meaning “those condemned to die.”">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; PSALMS 79:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a result</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Implied.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the wonderful nature of the Lord will be celebrated with praise in Jerusalem,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; PSALMS 72:8–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">when the people of many kingdoms gather together to worship the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Added for clarity.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he broke my health while I was still young, cutting my life short.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; PSALMS 102:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cried out, “My God, don't take my life while I'm young! You are the one who lives forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Long ago you created the earth; you made the heavens.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will come to an end, but you will not. They will all wear out, like clothes—you will change them, and throw them away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you are the one who always is;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“You are the one who always is”: literally, “you are he.”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your years never come to an end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 69:35–36; PSALMS 45:16–17; PSALMS 22:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our children will live with you, and our children's children will grow in your presence.”</span></span>
