<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A psalm of Solomon.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, please give the king fairness, and give the king's son the ability to do what's right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; PSALMS 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he rightly judge your people, and may he be fair to the poor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; PSALMS 96:11–13; PSALMS 72:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the mountains bring peace</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “prosperity.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the people, and the hills bring goodness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; PSALMS 109:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he defend the poor and save their children, may he crush those who oppress them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 89:36–37; PSALMS 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">May they respect him</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “you.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as long as the sun and the moon shine above, for all generations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">May his reign be like rain falling on new grass,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “mown grass.” A period of rain was needed after the first crop was cut in order for the second crop to grow.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like showers that water the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; PSALMS 92:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">May those who live right prosper under his rule; may there be great prosperity until the moon is no more.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; PSALMS 80:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he rule from sea to sea, from the river to the ends of the earth.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="From sea to sea probably refers to the Mediterranean and the Dead Sea (or Red Sea); the river to the Euphrates.">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Desert tribes will kneel before him; and his enemies will bite the dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; PSALMS 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of Tarshish and the islands will bring him tribute; the kings of Sheba and Seba will present him with gifts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every king will bow down to him; every nation will serve him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; PSALMS 102:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will help the poor when they cry out to him, and help those who are suffering that have no one to support them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; PSALMS 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has pity on the poor and needy—he saves their lives!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 116:15; Revelation 19:2; PSALMS 69:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rescues them from violence and oppression, for their lives mean so much to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he live long! May the gold from Sheba be given to him. May people always pray for him and bless him all day long.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; PSALMS 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">May there be plenty of grain in the land, even growing on mountain-tops. May the fruit on the trees sway like the trees of Lebanon. May the people in the city flourish like grass in a field.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; PSALMS 89:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">May his fame live forever, may it last as long as the sun. May all nations be blessed through him, and may they praise him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 41:13; PSALMS 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord God, the God of Israel, who is the only one who can do such fantastic things!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; PSALMS 41:13"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Praise his wonderful nature forever! May the whole world be full of his glory! Amen and amen!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> <span className="pl pl-1">(This is the end of the psalms of David, son of Jesse.)</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="It is thought that this is an editorial comment that concludes the second book of Psalms (42-72).">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
