<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Tell me every morning about your trustworthy love, because I put my confidence in you. Show me the way I should go, because I dedicate myself to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; PSALMS 61:3–4; PSALMS 59:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save me from those who hate me, Lord—I run to you for protection.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Teach me your will for you are my God. May your spirit of goodness lead me and make my way smooth.</span><br /></span>
