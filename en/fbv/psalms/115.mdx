<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have ears, but can't hear. They have noses, but can't smell.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 118:3; Numbers 18:7; Exodus 28:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Descendants of Aaron, trust in the Lord! He is the one who helps you and protects you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:26; Proverbs 30:5; Acts 10:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who honor the Lord, trust in the Lord! He is the one who helps you and protects you.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 134:3; Revelation 14:7; Genesis 1:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">May you be blessed by the Lord who made heaven and earth.</span><br /></span>
