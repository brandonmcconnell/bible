<div className="hs">A psalm of David. A song of praise.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 47:6–8; PSALMS 45:6; PSALMS 34:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will glorify you, my God and King! I will praise who you are forever and ever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:15; PSALMS 71:6; PSALMS 119:164"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will praise you every day; I will praise your character forever and ever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 147:5; Romans 11:33; Job 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is great and deserves much praise! His greatness cannot be measured!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 78:3–7; PSALMS 71:18; Deuteronomy 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let every generation tell the next all that you do, sharing the stories of your amazing miracles!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; PSALMS 119:27; PSALMS 72:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will speak</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“They will speak”: Qumran text.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of your majesty, your glorious splendor, and I will meditate on the wonderful things you have done.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Daniel 3:28–29; PSALMS 107:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will speak of the power of your awesome actions, and I will say how wonderful you are.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; PSALMS 36:10; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will explain to everyone how famous you are for your tremendous goodness, and joyfully celebrate how you always do what is right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:18; PSALMS 103:8; PSALMS 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is gracious and merciful, not easily angered, and full of trustworthy love.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:7; PSALMS 100:5; Matthew 5:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is good to everyone, and he shows mercy to all his created beings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 19:1; PSALMS 103:22; Revelation 7:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the created beings will thank you, Lord, and all who faithfully follow you will praise you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; PSALMS 93:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will praise the glory of your kingdom, and talk about your power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:10; PSALMS 105:1; Matthew 28:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will explain to people your wonderful miracles, and the glorious splendor of your kingdom.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Daniel 7:14; PSALMS 10:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your kingdom never ends, and your rule continues for all generations. What the Lord promises can always be trusted; he is merciful in all he does.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The second half of this verse is found in the Septuagint and the Syriac version, and also in the Qumran text. Since this poem is an acrostic, each verse beginning with successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet, these lines provide the missing letter (nun).">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 146:8; PSALMS 37:24; PSALMS 94:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord helps up all those who fall down; he lifts up all those who are bowed down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; PSALMS 104:27; Acts 17:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">All eyes are on you, waiting in hope, and you give food at just the right time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 104:28; PSALMS 107:9; PSALMS 132:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You give generously and satisfy the needs of every living thing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 45:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord does right in everything—his actions are always trustworthy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; PSALMS 34:18; Deuteronomy 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is close to all who ask for his help, to all who ask in sincerity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; Ephesians 3:16–20; John 15:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He satisfies the needs of those who follow him. He hears their cries for help and he saves them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; John 10:27–29; Romans 8:28–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord takes care of all those who love him, but he will destroy all those who are wicked.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 71:8; PSALMS 51:15; PSALMS 150:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will speak in praise of the Lord, and may everyone honor his holy character, forever and ever.</span></span>
