<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; PSALMS 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord! Thank the Lord, for he is good! His trustworthy love lasts forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; PSALMS 145:3–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can give an account of all the wonderful things the Lord has done?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, no one knows the full extent of God's activities.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who can give him all the praise he is due?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Happy are those who treat people fairly, who always do what is right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please remember me when you are generous to your people; think of me when you come to save.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; PSALMS 105:43; PSALMS 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let me see your chosen people prosperous; let me be happy together with your nation; let me share in being proud of you with those who belong to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; PSALMS 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have sinned just like our forefathers. We have done wrong. We are guilty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; PSALMS 78:42; PSALMS 78:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They didn't pay attention to the wonderful things you did. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even so he saved them because of the kind of person he is, and to show his power</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He gave his order</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “rebuked.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the Red Sea, and it dried up. He led his people across the seabed as if it were a desert.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; PSALMS 107:2; Micah 6:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rescued them from those who hated them; he saved them from their enemies' power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; PSALMS 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The water drowned their enemies—none of them survived,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then his people trusted in what he promised, and sang his praises.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they quickly forgot what he had done for them, and they didn't listen to his advice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They were filled with desperate cravings</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="For food, particularly meat.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the wilderness; they provoked God in the desert.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; PSALMS 78:29–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He gave them what they wanted, but sent them a plague</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Numbers 11:33-34.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as well.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the camp the people became jealous of Moses, and of Aaron, the Lord's holy priest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The earth split open and swallowed up Dathan; it buried Abiram and his followers.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Numbers 16.">f</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fire broke out among them—a flame that burned them up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">At Mount Sinai</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, Horeb: another name for Mount Sinai.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they made a calf, they bowed down before a metal idol.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Exodus 32.">h</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They replaced their God of glory with a bull that eats grass!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; PSALMS 78:42–51; PSALMS 106:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">They forgot God, their Savior, who had done marvelous things in Egypt;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 105:23; PSALMS 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">performing miracles in the land of Ham,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Land of Ham”—another name for Egypt.">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">doing amazing things at the Red Sea.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">So he said he was going to destroy them. But Moses, his chosen leader, placed himself between the Lord and the people to persuade the Lord not to destroy them in his anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Later the people refused to enter the Promised Land; they didn't trust him to do as he'd promised.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">They complained in their tents about the Lord, and refused to obey what he told them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">So he raised his hand to give them a serious warning that he would bring them down in the wilderness,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> <span className="pl pl-1">that he would disperse</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Following the parallel passage in Ezekiel 20:23.">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their descendants among the heathen nations, scattering them among countries far away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They gave their allegiance to Baal Peor,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A heathen god, see Numbers 25.">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ate food sacrificed to the dead.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They provoked the Lord by what they did, making him angry, so a plague broke out among them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Phinehas took a stand for the Lord and intervened, and the plague was stopped in its tracks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has been considered as a man who lived right</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The same phrase is used about Abraham in Genesis 15:6.">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from that time on and for all generations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They also angered him at the waters of Meribah where things went badly for Moses because of them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; PSALMS 107:11; James 3:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They upset him so much that he spoke without thinking in the heat of the moment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They did not destroy the heathen peoples as the Lord had told them to do,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">but instead they joined in with them and adopted their way of life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They worshiped their pagan idols which became a trap for them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The even sacrificed their sons and daughters to these demons.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shed the blood of innocent children, their sons and daughters, sacrificing them to the idols of Canaan. By doing so they defiled the land with blood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">They also defiled themselves by what they did: their actions were spiritual adultery.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the Lord was angry with his people—he loathed those who belonged to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He handed them over to the heathen nations. These people who hated them now became their rulers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their enemies dominated and subdued them with their power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; PSALMS 1:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord repeatedly rescued them, but they continued with their rebellious ideas, until they were finally destroyed by their own sins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Despite all this, the Lord was moved by their suffering; he heard their sad cries.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He remembered the agreement he had made with them, and he held back because of his great kindness and love.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He made the people who captured them treat them with mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save us, Lord, our God! Bring us back together from among the nations, so we can thank you and declare how magnificent and holy you are.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 41:13; PSALMS 89:52; PSALMS 105:45–1"> <span className="pl pl-1">How wonderful is the Lord, the God of Israel, who lives forever and ever! Let all the people say “Amen”! Praise the Lord!</span></span>
