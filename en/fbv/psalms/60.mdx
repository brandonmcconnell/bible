<div className="hs">For the music director. According to “Lily of the Testimony.” A psalm (miktam) of David, useful for teaching, about the time he fought against Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and then Joab returned and killed 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:3; PSALMS 44:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You, God, have rejected us! You have broken us; you have been angry with us; but now you have to welcome us back!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “restore.”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have sent earthquakes on our land and split it apart. Now heal the cracks, for the land is still having tremors.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 71:20; Isaiah 51:22; Isaiah 51:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have been very hard on your people; you gave us wine that made us stagger around.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 20:5; Isaiah 11:12; Exodus 17:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you have given those who respect you the banner of truth to unfurl and rally around.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew is unclear and capable of many interpretations.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 108:6–13; PSALMS 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue those you love! Answer us, and save us by your power!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; PSALMS 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God has spoken from his Temple: “Triumphantly I divide up Shechem, and portion out the Valley of Succoth.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This presumably refers to the dividing up of the country when the Israelites entered the Promised Land.">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Both Gilead and Manasseh belong to me. Ephraim is my helmet, and Judah is my scepter.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ephraim was the tribe of many warriors, and synonymous with the northern kingdom, while Judah was the tribe from which the kings came, and symbolized the southern kingdom.">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; PSALMS 108:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will treat Moab as my washbasin; I will place my sandal on Edom; I will shout in triumph over Philistia.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="These are all symbols of victory and subjugation.">e</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who will lead me into Edom?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Since it is unlikely that the speaker of these lines is God, it is conjectured that it is now the king that is speaking. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 108:11; Joshua 7:12; PSALMS 60:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you rejected us, God? Won't you lead our armies?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 146:3; PSALMS 108:12; PSALMS 130:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please give us help against our enemies, for human help is worthless.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “the help of man is vain.”">g</a></sup><br /></span>
