<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Oppose my opponents, Lord; fight those who are fighting against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Put your armor on, and pick up your shield. Get ready, come and help me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; PSALMS 27:2; PSALMS 51:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Draw your spear and your javelin, confront those who are chasing me. Tell me, “I am your salvation!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; PSALMS 35:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shame them! Humiliate those who are trying to kill me! Turn them back! Disgrace those who are planning to hurt me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be like chaff blown by the wind; let the angel of the Lord drive them away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; PSALMS 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">May their path be dark and slippery, with the angel of the Lord chasing them down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; PSALMS 140:5; PSALMS 9:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For no reason they set a net to catch me; for no reason they dug a pit to trap me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let disaster come on them suddenly; let the net they set catch them instead; let the pit they dug trap them instead.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; PSALMS 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will be glad in the Lord; I will be happy in his salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 140:12; Exodus 15:11; PSALMS 51:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every part of me</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “all of my bones.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will say, Who can compare to you, Lord? You rescue the weak from the strong; the poor and needy from robbers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hostile witnesses stand up to testify against me, accusing me of crimes I don't know anything about.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; PSALMS 38:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">They pay me back with evil instead of good. I feel like giving up.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “my soul is forlorn.”">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when they were sick, I put on clothes of sackcloth</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Clothes of sackcloth”—a sign of mourning.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in sympathy. I denied myself through fasting. May my prayer for them return to bless me too.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “my prayer returned to my bosom.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was upset for them, as if they were my own friends or family; I bowed down in grief as if I was mourning my own mother.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; PSALMS 7:2; PSALMS 35:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when I was in trouble, they gathered around and laughed at me. Even strangers I don't know attacked me, constantly tearing me apart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; PSALMS 37:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like irreligious people mocking a cripple</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Hebrew here is unclear.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they mock me and call me names, gnashing their teeth at me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; PSALMS 22:20–21; PSALMS 57:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, Lord, will you sit by and just watch? Save me from their vicious attacks; defend the only life I have from these lions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; PSALMS 22:22–25; PSALMS 116:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will thank you in front of the whole congregation and praise you in front of all the people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't let my enemies take pleasure in my troubles, those people who hate me and tell lies about me, gloating over me for no reason.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are not looking for peace; they invent malicious and deceptive schemes against innocent peace-loving people.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “the quiet ones of the land.”">f</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:13; PSALMS 40:15; PSALMS 70:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">They open their mouths wide to make accusations against me, saying, “Look! Look! We saw it with our own eyes!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; PSALMS 10:1; PSALMS 28:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Lord, you've seen all this! Say something! Don't give up on me, Lord!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 44:23; PSALMS 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wake up! Get up and defend me, my Lord and my God! Take up my case and make sure justice is done!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 26:1; PSALMS 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Vindicate me, my Lord and my God, because you are just and right. Don't let them gloat over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; PSALMS 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't let them say to themselves, “Look! We got what we wanted!” Don't let them say, “We completely destroyed him!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 38:16; Job 19:5; PSALMS 132:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make them totally ashamed, all those who are happy for the trouble I'm in. Let those who are celebrating over me be covered with shame and dishonor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; PSALMS 149:4; PSALMS 40:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead let those who are pleased I've been vindicated celebrate and shout for joy. Let them always say, “How great is the Lord! He is happy when his servants live in peace and have what they need.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The word used here “shalom” is often translated as simply “peace.” However, it is not simply the absence of conflict but also refers to a happy and prosperous life.">g</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 145:21; PSALMS 71:24; PSALMS 145:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will tell others about your character of truth and right, praising you all day long!</span></span>
</div>
