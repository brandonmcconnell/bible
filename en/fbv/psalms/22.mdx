<div className="hs">For the music director. To the tune “Doe of the Dawn.” A psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">My God, my God, why have you abandoned me? Why are you so far away when I groan, asking for help?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> <span className="pl pl-1">My God, every day I cry out to you, but you don't answer; at night too, but I get no rest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet you are holy, and the praises of Israel are your throne.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The image is thought to be of the Ark of Agreement within the Temple as the earthly location of the presence of God.">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our forefathers trusted in you; they trusted and you rescued them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They cried out to you for help, and they were saved. They trusted in you and were not defeated.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Defeated”: literally, “put to shame.” The concept of being disgraced is closely linked to defeat and is used in this connection in several psalms.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I'm a worm, not a man, scorned and despised by everyone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; PSALMS 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">People who see me mock me. They laugh at me</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “they open wide their lips.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shake their heads, saying,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; PSALMS 91:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He trusts in the Lord—well then, let the Lord save him! If the Lord is such a friend, then let the Lord rescue him!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; PSALMS 71:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">However, you brought me safely through birth, and led me to trust in you at my mother's breasts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was entrusted to you from birth; from the time I was born you have been my God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 71:12; PSALMS 72:12; PSALMS 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not be distant from me, because trouble is close by and no one else can help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; PSALMS 68:30; Acts 4:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Enemies surround me like a herd of bulls; strong bulls from Bashan have encircled me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; PSALMS 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like roaring lions tearing at their prey they open their mouths wide against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I feel like I'm being poured out like water. I'm falling apart as if all my bones have become loose. My mind feels like it's wax melting inside me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; PSALMS 69:3; PSALMS 69:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">My strength has dried up like a piece of broken pottery. My tongue is stuck to the roof of my mouth. You're burying me as if I'm already dead.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “you lay me down in the dust of death.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Evil men surround me like a pack of dogs. They have pierced</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Dead Sea Scrolls. The Hebrew text reads “like a lion.”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my hands and feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm so thin</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Implied.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I can count all my bones. People stare at me and gloat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They divide my clothing among them; they roll dice</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “cast lots.”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for my clothes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 22:11; PSALMS 40:13; PSALMS 40:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Lord, don't be far away from me! You are my strength—hurry, come and help me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; PSALMS 17:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save me from death by the sword! Save my life—the only one I have—from the dogs!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue me from the mouth of the lion and from the wild bulls!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; PSALMS 40:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will tell my people all the wonderful things you have done;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “proclaim your name.”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will praise you in the congregation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; PSALMS 107:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord, all who worship him! Honor him, every descendant of Jacob! Be in awe of him, every descendant of Israel!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; PSALMS 116:3–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he has not ridiculed or scorned the suffering of the poor; he has not turned away from them, he has listened to their cries for help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 40:9–10; PSALMS 35:18; PSALMS 111:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the subject of my praise in the great assembly. I will fulfill my promises before those who worship you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 69:32; PSALMS 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The poor shall eat, and they shall be satisfied. All who come to the Lord will praise him—may you all live forever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; PSALMS 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone in the whole world will repent and return to the Lord; all the nations will worship before you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; PSALMS 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For kingly power belongs to the Lord; he is the one who rules over the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; PSALMS 45:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All who prosper come to feast and worship. Bow down before him, all those destined for the grave—for none can keep themselves alive.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Hebrew of this verse is unclear.">i</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; PSALMS 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our descendants will serve him; they will tell the next generation about the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 78:6; PSALMS 86:9; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will come and tell those yet to be born how good the Lord is, and all that he has done!</span></span>
