<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Isaiah 24:19; Hebrews 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the earth quakes, and all its inhabitants tremble, I am the one who holds it steady.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “hold firm its pillars.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Selah.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For no one, from the east to the west, or from the wilderness, should think so highly of themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Daniel 2:21–22; PSALMS 147:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is the one who decides—who he will bring down and who he will lift up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 21:20; Jeremiah 25:15; PSALMS 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord has a cup in his hand, full of bubbling wine mixed with spices.
