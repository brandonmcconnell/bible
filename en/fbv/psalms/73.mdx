<div className="hs">A psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; PSALMS 51:10; Luke 12:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly God is good to Israel, to those whose minds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “hearts.” In Hebrew understanding the heart was where thinking was done.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">are pure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; PSALMS 116:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I was stumbling, my feet were starting to slide,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The psalmist is indicating he was losing faith.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; PSALMS 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">because I was jealous of self-important people—I saw how well the wicked were doing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They don't ever seem to get sick; they are strong and healthy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; PSALMS 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">They don't have problems like other people; they don't get hit by disasters like everybody else.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">They wear their pride like a necklace; they clothe themselves with violence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; PSALMS 17:10; Job 15:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their eyes bulge out because they're so fat; their minds are full of selfish vanity.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “from their evil hearts pours out iniquity.”">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; PSALMS 17:10; PSALMS 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They mock people, and speak maliciously; they arrogantly threaten cruelty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They talk irreverently against heaven above, and defame people here on earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 75:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a result people turn to them and drink in everything they say.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew text is hard to understand: “Therefore he brings back his people here and abundant waters are drained by them.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“God won't find out,” they say. “The Most High doesn't even know what's going on!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 52:7; PSALMS 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look at these wicked people! They don't have a care in the world, and they're always making money!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; PSALMS 26:6; Job 34:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">It's been pointless to keep my mind pure and my hands clean.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; PSALMS 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm cursed with suffering all day long; every morning I'm punished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I'd talked like this to others,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring back to what the wicked said in verse 11.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I would have betrayed your people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; PSALMS 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I thought about it and tried to understand,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The psalmist is trying to understand why the wicked prosper.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but it looked like a lot of hard work to me—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; PSALMS 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I went into God's Temple. Then I understood what happens to the wicked in the end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you send them on a slippery path; you throw them down to destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">How quickly they're destroyed! They come to a terrifying end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; PSALMS 78:65; PSALMS 90:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like waking up from a dream, Lord—when you get up you will forget all about them!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PSALMS 73:3; Job 16:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time my thoughts were bitter. I felt like I had been stabbed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; PSALMS 92:6; PSALMS 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was stupid and ignorant. I was like a brute beast to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; PSALMS 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet I'm always with you; you hold me by the hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You tell me what to do; and ultimately you will welcome me in glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; PSALMS 16:2; PSALMS 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who is there in heaven for me except you? And I want nothing on earth except you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; PSALMS 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My body and my mind may fail, but God is the foundation of my life. He is mine forever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; PSALMS 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those people who are far from God will die. You destroy all those unfaithful to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; PSALMS 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I love to stay close to God! I have chosen the Lord God to protect me. I will share all that you have done.</span></span>
