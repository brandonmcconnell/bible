<div className="hs">A psalm (maskil) of Ethan the Ezraite</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 101:1; PSALMS 89:8; PSALMS 119:90"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will sing of the Lord's trustworthy love forever; I will tell all generations of your faithfulness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; PSALMS 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have said, “Your unfailing love lasts forever; your faithfulness endures as long as the heavens.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; PSALMS 89:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">You</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring to God.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">said, “I have made an agreement</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or covenant.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with my chosen one, I gave a binding promise to my servant David:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; PSALMS 132:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make sure your lineage lasts forever; I will keep your throne secure for all generations.” Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 97:6; PSALMS 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the heavenly beings will sing of the wonderful things you have done, Lord; angels will gather to sing of your faithfulness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 86:8; PSALMS 71:19; PSALMS 113:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who in heaven can compare to the Lord? Who is like the Lord even among the angels?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 47:2; PSALMS 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heavenly council are in awe of God; all who surround him are overwhelmed by him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord God Almighty, who is as powerful as you? In all this,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“In all this”: implied.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you are completely trustworthy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 65:7; PSALMS 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">You rule the restless seas; you calm their stormy waves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 87:4; PSALMS 68:30; PSALMS 144:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You crushed Rahab the sea-monster to death; by your power you scattered your enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; PSALMS 115:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heavens belong to you, and the earth too; you made the world and everything in it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You created north and south; Mount Tabor and Mount Hermon celebrate you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; PSALMS 89:10; PSALMS 62:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your arm is powerful. Your hand is strong. Your right hand is held up high in command.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“In command”: implied.">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; PSALMS 45:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your character of goodness and fairness is the basis for the way you rule; dependable love and trustworthiness are always with you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 44:3; Job 29:3; PSALMS 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">How happy are those who know how to shout your praises, Lord.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This may have involved the shouts that accompanied the start of religious festivals.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They live in the light of your presence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">They celebrate the person you are all day long, so glad that you do what's right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 75:10; PSALMS 92:10; PSALMS 148:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They rely on you, their glory and strength; by your grace you lift us up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 47:9; PSALMS 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, the Lord is the one who shields us, and our king belongs to the Holy One of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; PSALMS 89:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Once you spoke in a vision to your faithful servant and said,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 2 Samuel 7:4-17.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I have given strength to the warrior I have chosen from the people to become king.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have selected David my servant, and I have anointed him with the oil of my holiness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; PSALMS 18:32–39; PSALMS 89:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have placed my hand on him to steady him; and I have made him strong by my powerful arm.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">His enemies will not deceive him; the wicked will not bring him down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; PSALMS 18:40"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will wipe out his enemies before him; I will strike down those who hate him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; PSALMS 91:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">My trustworthiness and unfailing love will be with him, and through me he will be victorious.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “In my name his horn will be exalted.”">g</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 80:11; PSALMS 2:8; PSALMS 72:8–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will extend his rule from the Mediterranean Sea to the Euphrates River.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “I will set his hand on the sea and his right hand on the rivers.”">h</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; PSALMS 95:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will call out to me, ‘You are my Father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; PSALMS 2:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will also make him my first-born son, the highest of the kings of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will love him faithfully forever; my agreement</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with him shall never come to an end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; PSALMS 89:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make sure his royal line lasts forever; his dynasty</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally, “throne.”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will continue as long as the heavens endure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; PSALMS 119:53"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if his descendants abandon my laws, if they do not follow my rules,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 55:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">if they break my decrees, and do not keep my commandments,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish their rebellion by beating them with a rod, and their sin by lashing them with a whip.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">However, I will not take away my love from him; I will not break my promise to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not annul the agreement I have with him; I will not alter a single word I've said.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">By my holy character I have made a vow to David that I will not lie to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 72:5; PSALMS 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">His royal line will last forever, and his dynasty will continue before me as long as the sun endures.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; PSALMS 104:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will continue forever like the moon, an enduring witness in the heavens.” Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; PSALMS 106:40"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The psalmist is now addressing God.">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have rejected and abandoned him! You are angry with your chosen king!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally, “anointed one.”">l</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; PSALMS 74:7; PSALMS 89:34–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have broken the agreement you had with him; you have thrown his crown to the ground!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have torn down his defensive walls; you have ruined his fortresses.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The king is used as a symbol for Jerusalem which has been attacked and destroyed.">m</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="PSALMS 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who passes by has robbed him; he has become an object of mockery to the nations nearby.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made his enemies strong; you have made them celebrate their victory.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“Their victory”—implied.">n</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; PSALMS 44:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have repelled his sharp sword; you have not helped him in battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; PSALMS 89:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">You took away his glory; you threw his throne to the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 44:15; Micah 7:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made him grow old before his time; you have totally humiliated him. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 79:5; PSALMS 78:63; PSALMS 88:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, Lord? Will you hide yourself from us forever, your anger burning like fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember me—my life is so short! Why did you bother creating futile humanity?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">There's no one who doesn't die—no one can save themselves from the power of the grave. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where is the trustworthy love you used to have, Lord, that you faithfully promised to David?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 74:22; PSALMS 74:18; PSALMS 69:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't forget, Lord, how your servants are being humiliated! I'm burdened down</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="“I am burdened down”: literally, “I carry in my lap.”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the insults of so many nations!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; PSALMS 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your enemies taunt me, Lord, mocking your king</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally, “anointed one.”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wherever he goes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="PSALMS 72:18–19; PSALMS 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the Lord be blessed for ever. Amen and amen.</span></span>
