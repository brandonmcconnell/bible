<div className="hs">A song. A psalm of the descendants of Korah. For the music director. To the tune “Mahalath Leannoth.” A maskil by Heman the Ezrahite</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; PSALMS 22:2; Luke 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, God of my salvation, I cry out to you day and night.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 31:2; PSALMS 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please listen to my prayer; hear my words as I plead with you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">My life is full of troubles, and my death is approaching.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “my life reaches out to Sheol.”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; PSALMS 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm counted among the dying; a man with no strength.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; PSALMS 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am abandoned among the dead, lying like a corpse in the grave, forgotten and beyond your care.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 69:15; PSALMS 86:13; PSALMS 143:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have placed me in a deep pit, in the depths of darkness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your hostility crushes me; you are drowning me under your crashing waves. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made my friends avoid me by making me look repulsive to them. I'm trapped, I can't go out.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Perhaps because he is regarded as unclean.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; PSALMS 38:10; PSALMS 86:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I've cried my eyes out begging you every day for your help, Lord, holding out my hands to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; PSALMS 30:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you do miracles among the dead? Do the dead stand up to praise you? Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; PSALMS 73:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your faithful love—is it mentioned in the grave? Your trustworthiness—is it discussed in the place of destruction?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Place of destruction”: literally, “Abaddon.”">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Are the wonderful things you do known in the darkness? Is your goodness known in the land of forgetfulness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 5:3; PSALMS 30:2; Mark 1:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I cry out to you for help; every morning I pray to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; PSALMS 13:1; PSALMS 44:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, why do you reject me? Why do you turn away from me?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have been sick since I was young, often at death's door. I have had to bear the terrible things you've done to me. I'm in despair!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your anger has overcome me; the terrible things you do have destroyed me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 22:16; PSALMS 116:3; PSALMS 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They surround me all the time like floodwaters, swallowing me up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 88:8; PSALMS 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made my family and friends avoid me. Darkness is my only friend.</span></span>
