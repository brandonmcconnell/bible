<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 146:1–2; PSALMS 57:7–11; John 4:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let every part of me praise the Lord; let my whole being praise his holy character.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; PSALMS 116:12; PSALMS 105:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let every part of me praise the Lord; don't let me forget the wonderful things he's done for me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Isaiah 53:5; Jeremiah 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He forgives my sins, and he heals all my diseases.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 56:13; PSALMS 71:23; PSALMS 103:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">He saves me from death; he honors</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “crowns.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">me with trustworthy love and mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; PSALMS 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He fills my life with all that's good; he makes me young again, strong as an eagle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 72:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord does what is right, and vindicates those who are exploited.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; PSALMS 147:19; PSALMS 78:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He explained his ways to Moses: he told the people of Israel what he was going to do.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is kind and gracious, not quick-tempered, and full of trustworthy love.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 57:16; PSALMS 30:5; Micah 7:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He doesn't keep on accusing us; he doesn't stay angry with us forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not punish us for our sins as he ought to; he does not pay us back for our wrongdoing as we deserve.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 57:10; Luke 1:50; PSALMS 36:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">For as high as the heavens are above the earth is the extent of his trustworthy love to those who honor him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Micah 7:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">As far as the east is from the west is how far the Lord has taken our sins away from us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like a loving father, the Lord is kind and compassionate to those who follow him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; PSALMS 78:38–39"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he knows how we are made; he remembers we are only dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Isaiah 51:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Human lifetimes are like grass: we blossom like flowers in a field,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">but then the wind blows and we are gone, disappearing without a trace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Lord's trustworthy love lasts for all eternity to those who accept him; his goodness to all generations,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; PSALMS 132:12; Luke 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">to those who keep his agreement and who remember to follow his commandments.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; PSALMS 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has set up his throne in the heavens, and he rules over all.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; PSALMS 148:2; Luke 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord, you angels, you powerful ones who do what he says, listening to what he tells you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord, you heavenly armies who serve him and carry out his will!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; PSALMS 145:10; Isaiah 42:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise the Lord, everything in his creation, everyone under his rule, and let every part of me praise the Lord!</span></span>
