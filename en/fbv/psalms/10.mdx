<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1; PSALMS 27:9; PSALMS 13:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, why are you so distant? Why do you hide from me in times of trouble?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 59:12; PSALMS 36:11; PSALMS 7:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked chase down the poor with impunity. May they be trapped by the evil schemes they themselves invented.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the wicked boast about what they want to do. They praise the greedy, but treat the Lord with contempt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked are too proud to come to God. In all their thinking they never consider God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; PSALMS 12:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">What they do always seems to go well. They're unaware of God's judgments. They ridicule all their enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They tell themselves, “Nothing bad is going to happen to me. I'll never be in trouble!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; PSALMS 140:9; PSALMS 140:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their speech is full of cursing, lies, and threats; their tongues are always ready to spread trouble and cause evil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They hide out in ambush in the villages, ready to kill innocent passers-by. They are always on the lookout for their next victim.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 17:12; PSALMS 59:3; Micah 7:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They lie in ambush like a lion ready to attack, ready to leap out from their hiding place to seize their victim. They capture the helpless, throwing a net over them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their victims are knocked down, laid low.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It is uncertain as to whether this line refers to the victims or the wicked. If it is the latter, it could be translated, “They crouch down and lie low.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They fall under the strength of the wicked.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; PSALMS 94:7; PSALMS 73:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They tell themselves, “God won't notice. He's looking the other way. He won't ever see anything.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; PSALMS 9:12; PSALMS 94:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take a stand, Lord! Raise your hand to strike! Don't forget those who can't defend themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; PSALMS 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do the wicked think they can treat God with such contempt? Why do they think that God won't make them accountable?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 68:5; PSALMS 146:9; PSALMS 55:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you do see the trouble and grief they cause. Take it into your own hands. The helpless trust in you; you defend the orphans.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; PSALMS 37:17; Job 38:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Destroy the power of the wicked, those evil people! Call each of them to account until there are none left!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you are King forever and ever! The nations will vanish from their lands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; PSALMS 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you have heard the longing cries of those who are suffering. You will encourage them, for you will listen to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will defend the rights of orphans and the oppressed so that those who are mere human beings here on earth will never terrorize them again.</span></span>
