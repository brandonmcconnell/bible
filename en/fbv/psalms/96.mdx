<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:15; PSALMS 71:15; PSALMS 103:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to the Lord, and of his wonderful reputation!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Reputation”: literally, “name.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Each day let everyone know of his salvation!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 22:27; Daniel 4:1–3; Luke 24:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">Declare his glorious acts among the nations, the amazing things he does among all peoples.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 145:3; PSALMS 18:3; Revelation 15:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord is great, and deserves the best praise! He is to be respected with awe above all gods.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 115:15; Isaiah 42:5; Jeremiah 10:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For all the gods of other nations are idols; but the Lord made the heavens!</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:1; Matthew 6:13; 1 Chronicles 29:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give the Lord acclamation, nations of the world, acclaim him with glory and strength.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 100:4; Romans 12:1; Exodus 34:5–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give the Lord the glory he deserves; bring an offering and come into his courts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 29:2; PSALMS 114:7; PSALMS 110:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Worship the Lord in his magnificent holiness; let all the earth tremble in his presence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 93:1; PSALMS 67:4; PSALMS 9:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Tell the nations, “The Lord is in charge!” The world is held together firmly—it cannot be broken apart. He will judge everyone fairly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 69:34; Isaiah 49:13; PSALMS 98:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the heavens sing for joy, let the earth be happy, let the sea and everything in it shout with praise!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12–13; Isaiah 35:1; Isaiah 42:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the fields and everything there celebrate; let all the trees in the forest sing for joy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:11; 2 Timothy 4:8; Isaiah 25:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">In fact, let everything that stands before the Lord sing praises,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Expanded for clarity.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he is coming, coming to judge the earth.
