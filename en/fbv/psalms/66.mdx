<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; PSALMS 100:1; PSALMS 98:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone on earth, shout for joy to God!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing about his marvelous reputation; praise him for his goodness!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 18:44; PSALMS 65:5; PSALMS 81:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Tell God, “What you do is awesome! Your enemies are forced to bow before you because of your power.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 22:27; PSALMS 117:1; PSALMS 96:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone on earth worships you, singing your praises—singing praises because of who you are.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “your name.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; PSALMS 66:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and see what God has done! What he does for people is truly awesome!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He turned the Red Sea into dry land, and his people walked across through the waters. We celebrated there because of what he did.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 11:4; Matthew 28:18; PSALMS 140:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rules forever through his power. He keeps an eye on the nations, watching to make sure no rebels rise up in opposition. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let all people everywhere bless our God, and loudly sing praises to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 121:3; PSALMS 112:6; PSALMS 94:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has kept us alive, and he has not let us fall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; PSALMS 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, you have tested us, refining us like silver.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> <span className="pl pl-1">You caught us in your net; you placed heavy burdens on our backs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; PSALMS 40:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You let people ride roughshod over us;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The image is of an enemy army overwhelming them.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we suffered through fire and flooding, but you brought us to a safe place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 100:4; PSALMS 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will come to your Temple with burnt offerings; I will fulfill my promises to you,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; PSALMS 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">the promises I made when I was in trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will sacrifice burnt offerings to you of fattened livestock—the smoke of the sacrifice of rams, offerings of bulls and goats. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 71:15; PSALMS 34:11; PSALMS 71:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and listen, everyone who honors God, and I will tell you all he has done for me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 34:6; PSALMS 145:1; PSALMS 116:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I called out to him, and praised him with my voice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I had focused my mind on evil,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “If I had seen evil in my heart.” It was believed that it was in the heart that thinking happened.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Lord would not have listened to me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God did hear me! He listened to what I had to say in my prayer!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 86:12–13; PSALMS 68:35; PSALMS 51:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise God, who did not disregard my prayer or take his trustworthy love from me.</span></span>
