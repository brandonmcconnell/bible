<div className="hs">A psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; PSALMS 74:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, heathen nations have invaded your land. They have defiled your holy Temple. They have turned Jerusalem into heaps of rubble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have given the dead bodies of your servants as food to the birds of the air, the flesh of your faithful people to the beasts of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have poured out the blood of Jerusalem's people like water all through the city; no one remains to bury the dead.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; PSALMS 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have been made a mockery before our neighbors, ridiculed and laughed at by those around us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 89:46; PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, Lord? Will you be angry with us forever? Will your jealousy always burn like fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; PSALMS 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out your anger on the heathen nations that don't know you, and on those kingdoms that don't worship you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have destroyed the descendants of Jacob, and turned our country into a wasteland.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; PSALMS 142:6; PSALMS 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't hold the sins of our forefathers against us! Come to us quickly for we desperately need your compassion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; PSALMS 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, God of our salvation, because of your wonderful character! Save us and forgive us our sins because that's the kind of person you are!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “for your name's sake.”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 42:10; Exodus 7:5; PSALMS 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should the heathen nations be able to say, “Where is their God?” May they experience your punishment for shedding the blood of your servants, and may we see it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 102:20; PSALMS 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the groans of the prisoners; with your great power save those condemned to die.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pay each of these neighbors back seven times for the scorn and ridicule they directed against you, Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; PSALMS 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then we your people, the flock of your pasture, will praise you forever. We will thank you for all generations to come.</span></span>
