<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 71:2–3; PSALMS 102:2; PSALMS 86:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please listen to me, and be quick to rescue me. Be my rock of protection, my strong fortress of safety.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; PSALMS 79:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are my rock and my fortress. For the sake of your reputation, please lead me and guide me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 25:15; PSALMS 140:5; PSALMS 19:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help me escape from the hidden net that they set to trap me, for you are the one who protects me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I put myself in your hands. Save me, Lord, for you are a trustworthy God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; PSALMS 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “You.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hate those who devote themselves to pointless idols; I trust in the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; PSALMS 119:153"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will celebrate, happy in your love that never fails, for you have seen the problems I face and have paid attention to my troubles.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; PSALMS 18:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You haven't handed me over to my enemies; you have set me free.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“You have set me free”: literally, “you have set my feet in an open area.”">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 6:7; PSALMS 88:9; PSALMS 73:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be kind to me Lord, for I am upset. I can hardly see from so much crying. I am completely wasting away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 88:15; PSALMS 32:3–4; PSALMS 38:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Grief is killing me; my life is cut short by sadness; I am falling apart</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Falling apart”: literally, “stumbling.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of my troubles; I am worn to the bone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 38:11; PSALMS 88:8; PSALMS 88:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am ridiculed by my enemies, particularly by my neighbors. My friends dread meeting me; people who see me in the street run the other way.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 88:4–5; PSALMS 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have been forgotten as if I were dead; I'm ignored like a broken pot.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hear many people whispering about me; terror surrounds me. They plot together against me, planning to kill me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 56:3–4; PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 43:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I put my trust in you, Lord, saying, “You are my God!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My whole life</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“My whole life,” literally, “my times.”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is in your hands! Save me from those who hate me and persecute me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 4:6; PSALMS 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">May you look kindly on me,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “Let your face shine upon me.”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your servant. Save me because of your trustworthy love.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; PSALMS 34:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, don't let me be disgraced, for I'm calling out to you. Instead let the wicked be disgraced, let them be silent in the grave.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; PSALMS 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shut the mouths of those who tell lies against good people—those who speak contemptuously in their pride and arrogance!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">How wonderful is the goodness you have reserved for those who honor you! In front of everyone you give this goodness to those who come to you for help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 27:5; PSALMS 32:7; PSALMS 91:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You shelter and protect them with your presence, far from their enemies who scheme against them. You keep them safe from attacks and accusations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; PSALMS 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bless the Lord, for he has shown me his wonderful trustworthy love when my city was being attacked</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; PSALMS 88:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terrified I cried out, “I am being destroyed right in front of you!” But you heard my cry when I called out for you to help me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 97:10; PSALMS 94:2; PSALMS 145:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Love the Lord, all you who trust in him! The Lord takes care of those who trust him, but he pays back in full those who are arrogant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; PSALMS 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be strong and be confident,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “strong of heart.”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all you who put your hope in the Lord.</span></span>
