<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">To the music director. A psalm of David the servant of the Lord, who sang the words of this song to the Lord on the day when the Lord saved him from all his enemies, and from Saul. He sang:</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I love you, Lord. You are my strength.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 144:2; PSALMS 62:7; PSALMS 19:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is my rock, my fortress, and my Savior. He is my God, my rock who protects me. He shields me from harm, his power protects me,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “horn of my salvation.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he keeps me safe.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 91:15; PSALMS 145:3; PSALMS 62:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I call for help from the Lord who should be praised, and he saves me from those who hate me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ropes of death encircled me, surging waters of destruction flooded over me;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 86:13; PSALMS 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The grave wound its ropes around me; death set snares for me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; PSALMS 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">In my despair I called on the Lord—I cried out to my God for help. He heard my voice from his Temple—my cry for help reached his ears.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; PSALMS 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The earth shook to and fro; the foundations of the mountains trembled, shaking because of his anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 21:9; PSALMS 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Smoke came out of his nostrils, and fire came from his mouth; burning coals blazed before him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He parted the heavens and came down, with dark clouds beneath his feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; PSALMS 99:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Riding on an angel</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “cherub,” but in English this has become associated with an angelic baby.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he flew, swooping on the wings of the wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; PSALMS 91:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He hid himself in darkness, covering himself with black thunderclouds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hailstones and burning coals flew out from his brightness, passing through his thick clouds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; PSALMS 140:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord thundered from heaven; the voice of the Most High sounded among the hailstones and burning coals.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He fired his arrows, scattering his enemies;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Implied.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he routed them with his lightning bolts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You roared, Lord, and by the wind from the breath of your nostrils the valleys of the sea were exposed and the foundations of the earth were uncovered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; PSALMS 144:7; PSALMS 57:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He reached down his hand from above and grabbed hold of me. He pulled me out of the deep water.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; PSALMS 35:10; PSALMS 142:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rescued me from my powerful enemies, from those who hated me and who were much stronger than me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; PSALMS 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They came at me at my worst possible moment</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “my day of disaster.”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">, but the Lord supported me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 118:5; PSALMS 31:8; PSALMS 18:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">He set me free,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “brought me out to a spacious place.”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he rescued me because he's my friend.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “he delights in me.”">f</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 24:4; Matthew 6:4; PSALMS 58:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord rewarded me because I do what's right; he repaid me because I am innocent.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “because of the cleanness of my hands.”">g</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 119:102; PSALMS 17:4; PSALMS 26:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have followed the Lord's ways; I have not sinned by turning away from my God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:13; PSALMS 119:112; PSALMS 119:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have kept all his laws in mind; I have not ignored his commandments.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; PSALMS 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am blameless in his sight; I keep myself from sinning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord rewarded me for doing what's right. I am innocent in his sight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; PSALMS 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">You show trust to those who trust; you show integrity to those with integrity,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The word used here means “complete” or “sound.”">h</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You show yourself pure to those who are pure, but you show yourself smart to those who are crafty.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Both words used here have to do with being “twisted,” the first in the sense of being clever, the second in the sense of being devious and perverse.">i</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You save the humble, but you bring down the proud.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You light my lamp! Lord, my God, you light up my darkness!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">With you, I can charge down a troop of soldiers; with you, my God, I can climb a fortress wall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">God's way is absolutely right.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The word used here, often translated as “perfect,” is the same as in 18:25.">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What the Lord says is trustworthy. He is a shield to all who come to him for protection.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; PSALMS 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who is God except the Lord? Who is the rock, except our God?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; PSALMS 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">God makes me strong and keeps me safe.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">He makes me surefooted like the deer, able to walk the heights in safety.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 144:1; PSALMS 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">He teaches me how to fight in battle; he gives me the strength to draw a bronze bow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; PSALMS 28:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You protect me with the shield of your salvation; you support me with your powerful right hand; your help has made me great.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You gave me space in which to walk, and prevented my feet from slipping.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; PSALMS 44:5; PSALMS 35:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I chased my enemies, and caught up with them. I did not turn around until I had destroyed them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I struck them down—they couldn't get up. They fell at my feet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; PSALMS 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made me strong for battle; you made those who rose up against me kneel down before me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 21:12; PSALMS 94:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made my enemies run away; I destroyed all my enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">They cried out for help, but no one came to rescue them. They even called out to the Lord, but he did not answer them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I ground them into dust, like dust in the wind. I threw them out like mud in the street.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You rescued me from rebellious people; you made me ruler over nations—people I didn't know now serve me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 66:3; PSALMS 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">As soon as they hear of me, they obey; foreigners cringe before me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They lose heart, and come trembling in surrender from their strongholds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; PSALMS 57:5; PSALMS 18:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord lives! Blessed be my rock! May the God who saves me be praised!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">God avenges me, he subdues peoples under me,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 140:1; PSALMS 140:4; PSALMS 86:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">he rescues me from those who hate me. You keep me safe from those who rebel against me, you save me from violent men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; PSALMS 72:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">That's why I will praise you among the nations, Lord; I will sing praises about who you are.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“About who you are”: Literally, “to your name”: the concept of name in Hebrew is far more than a simple designation; it refers to the character of the person.">k</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 144:10; PSALMS 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have saved the king so often,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “You have given many victories to the king.”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">showing your trustworthy love to David, your anointed, and to his descendants forever.</span></span>
