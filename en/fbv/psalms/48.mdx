<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 87:5; PSALMS 78:3–6; PSALMS 46:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Just as we had heard, but now we have seen the city of the Lord Almighty. This is the city of our God; he makes it secure forever. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 26:3; PSALMS 40:10; PSALMS 77:10–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, we recall your trustworthy love as we worship in the Temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; PSALMS 113:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">As is fitting for your reputation,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “name.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, praises to you stretch all around the world. What you do is always right!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the people of Mount Zion be glad; let the people of Judah celebrate because your judgments are always fair!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; Matthew 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go and take a look at Zion. Count the towers as you walk around.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 71:18; PSALMS 122:7; Deuteronomy 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Inspect the fortifications. Examine the citadels, so you can describe everything to the next generation, telling them:</span><br /></span>
