<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A psalm (maskil) of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; PSALMS 79:13; PSALMS 100:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, why have you rejected us? Is it forever? Why does your anger burn so hot against the sheep of your own field?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember the people you made your own long ago, the tribe you redeemed and made your own. Remember too Mount Zion, the place where you live.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; PSALMS 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and walk through the complete devastation.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Of Jerusalem.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The enemy has totally destroyed your Temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The enemy shouted in triumph right where you met with us.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring to the Temple.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There they set up their war banners as signs of their victory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They acted like men chopping down a forest with axes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">With axes and hammers they smashed in the carved wooden panels.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; PSALMS 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they set fire to your Temple, burning it to the ground. They defiled the place where you live, the place that bears your name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They said to themselves, “Let's destroy it all!” So they burned down every place where God was worshiped throughout the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">We no longer see any signs.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Generally taken to mean miraculous signs from God.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There are no prophets left. And no one among us knows how long this will last.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 89:50–51; PSALMS 44:16; PSALMS 89:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long will the enemy ridicule you, God? Will they insult your character</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “name.”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">forever?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; PSALMS 78:65–66; PSALMS 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you hold back from doing something? Take action and destroy them!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Hebrew imagery here needs some interpretation. Literally, “Why do you hold back your hand? And your right hand from your bosom consume.”">e</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, God, are our</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew has “my.”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">king from long ago. You have saved us many times in the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; PSALMS 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You were the one</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The personal pronoun is emphasized here and in subsequent verses. It may be to identify that it was the true God of Creation who did these things, in contrast to similar stories that gave credit to pagan gods.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who split apart the sea by your strength; you broke the heads of the sea monsters.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; PSALMS 72:9; PSALMS 104:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">You were the one who crushed the heads of Leviathan,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A sea monster also mentioned in Job.">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you gave its body to the desert animals to eat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; PSALMS 105:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">You were the one who made springs and rivers flow with water. You made permanent rivers dry up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; PSALMS 104:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You created the day, and also the night; you made the moon</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, “luminary,” this may also include light from the stars.">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sun.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You set the boundaries of the earth;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Genesis 1:9-10.">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you made summer and winter.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">So keep in mind how the enemy ridiculed you, Lord, and how irreverent people insulted your reputation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:14; Matthew 10:16; PSALMS 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't let wild animals kill your turtledoves!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The people of Israel are compared to doves in their meekness and inability to defend themselves.">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Don't abandon your people forever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember your promises in the agreement,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally, “covenant.”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the land is full of dark places and violence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 35:10; PSALMS 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't let those who suffer be mistreated again. Let the poor and needy praise you for you who are.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 74:18; PSALMS 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Stand up, God, and plead your case. Don't forget how these foolish people insulted you all the time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; PSALMS 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't ignore what your enemies have said, for their loud accusations against you are getting worse and worse!</span></span>
</div>
