<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 25:2; Daniel 4:37; PSALMS 35:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I want to tell everyone how good you are, Lord, for you saved me, and you didn't let my enemies triumph over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; PSALMS 147:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, my God, I called out to you for help, and you have healed me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 86:13; PSALMS 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you rescued me from the grave, you brought me back to life, saving me from going down into the pit of death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who trust the Lord, sing praises to him, and be thankful for his holy character!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; PSALMS 126:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For his anger only lasts a moment, but his favor lasts a lifetime. You may spend the night in tears, but happiness comes with the morning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; PSALMS 119:117"> <span className="pl pl-1">When things were going well, I said, “Nothing will ever shake me!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 104:29; PSALMS 143:7; PSALMS 30:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, when you showed me your favor I stood as strong as a mountain; when you turned away from me I was terrified.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; PSALMS 34:6; PSALMS 130:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I called out to you for help, Lord. I asked the Lord for grace, saying,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 6:5; PSALMS 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">What would you gain if I die, if I go down into the pit of death? Would my dust praise you? Would it tell of your trustworthiness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 54:4; PSALMS 143:1; PSALMS 143:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please listen to me, Lord, and be gracious to me! Lord, you are the one who helps me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have turned my weeping into dancing. You have taken off my clothes of sackcloth</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Wearing sackcloth was a sign of mourning.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and dressed me in happiness,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 145:2; PSALMS 146:1–2; PSALMS 44:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">so I can sing praises to you and not keep quiet. Lord my God, I will thank you forever!</span></span>
