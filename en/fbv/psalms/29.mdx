<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:26; PSALMS 46:3; Isaiah 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord's voice causes an earthquake in the desert; the desert of Kadesh shakes.</span><br /></span>
