<li id="fn-c"><a href="#fnref-c" className="fnb">c</a> <span className="fnr">2:2</span> In this context “anointed one” means the king of Israel.</li>

<li id="fn-f"><a href="#fnref-f" className="fnb">f</a> <span className="fnr">2:12</span> “Son”: referring to verse 7.</li>
<li id="fn-g"><a href="#fnref-g" className="fnb">g</a> <span className="fnr">2:12</span> “Anger”: throughout the Psalms God is spoken of as becoming angry, usually in response to sin and wickedness.
