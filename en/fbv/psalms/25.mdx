<div className="hs">A psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 143:8; PSALMS 86:4; PSALMS 24:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, I come to worship you.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “I lift up my soul to you.”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:11; PSALMS 41:11; PSALMS 71:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I trust in you, my God. Don't let me be humiliated, and don't let my enemies triumph over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Isaiah 40:31; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">No one who trusts in you will be humiliated, but humiliation comes to those who are deliberately dishonest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 86:11; PSALMS 27:11; PSALMS 143:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Show me your ways, Lord; teach me to follow your paths.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Isaiah 30:18; PSALMS 25:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lead me in your truth and teach me, for you are the God of my salvation. All day long I put my trust in you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember, Lord, your compassion and trustworthy love—they are eternal!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please don't remember the sins of my youth when I rebelled against you. Instead, remember me according to your trustworthy love, remember me because of your goodness, Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:5; PSALMS 32:8; Ephesians 1:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is good and he is right; that is why he shows the way to those who are lost.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Those who are lost”: often translated “sinners,” the underlying meaning is “those who miss the right path,” “go astray.”">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; PSALMS 23:3; Matthew 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He leads those who are humble in doing what's right, teaching them his way.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 5:11; Romans 8:28; Hebrews 8:8–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord's paths are those of trustworthy love and truth for those who keep his agreement</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “covenant.” Also verse 14.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do as he says.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 79:9; 1 John 2:12; Isaiah 43:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, because of your nature, forgive my many sins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:23; PSALMS 32:8; John 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who are those who respect the Lord? He will show them the path to choose.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; Proverbs 1:33; Proverbs 19:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will continue to be prosperous, and their descendants will inherit the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:15; John 14:21–23; John 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is friends with those who respect him, explaining to them his agreement.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 141:8; PSALMS 124:7–8; PSALMS 31:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I always look to the Lord, for he rescues me when I'm trapped.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Rescues me when I'm trapped”: literally, “pulls my feet out of the net” (for catching birds).">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 86:16; Daniel 9:17; Micah 7:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Watch over me and be good to me, because I am all alone and suffering.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 107:6; PSALMS 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">My problems get bigger and bigger. Please save me from all I'm going through.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; PSALMS 119:132; PSALMS 51:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">See all my troubles and trials. Please forgive my sins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 56:2; PSALMS 52:2; PSALMS 38:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look at how many enemies I have, and how they really hate me!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “hate me with violent hatred.”">e</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 71:1–2; PSALMS 121:7; Joel 2:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please look after me and save me! Don't let me be humiliated, for you are the one who protects me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; PSALMS 41:12; Proverbs 20:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">May integrity and honesty defend me because I place my confidence in you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 130:8; PSALMS 51:18–19; PSALMS 14:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, please deliver the people of Israel from all their troubles!</span></span>
