<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; PSALMS 18:35; PSALMS 73:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hold on to you; your strong arms lift me up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 55:15; PSALMS 9:17; PSALMS 40:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who are trying to destroy me will go down into the grave.</span><br /></span>
