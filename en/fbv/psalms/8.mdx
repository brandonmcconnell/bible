<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; PSALMS 33:6; PSALMS 111:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I contemplate the heavens that your hands made, the moon and stars that you placed there,</span><br /></span>
