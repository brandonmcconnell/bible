<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 64:10; PSALMS 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But those who are right with God are happy, and they celebrate in God's presence, full of joy.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; PSALMS 82:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He is a father to the orphans, a protector of widows. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, when you led your people out,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referring to the Exodus.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when you marched through the desert, Selah,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">the earth quaked, and the heavens shook</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “poured down rain.”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before God, the one of Sinai; before God, the God of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; PSALMS 65:9–13; PSALMS 78:24–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sent plenty of rain to water the promised land; refreshing it when it was dry.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This line could also be directly applied to the people of Israel, since the “inheritance” was both the land and the people.">h</a></sup><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; PSALMS 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord gives the command, and a great army of women spread the good news.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of the foreign armies are quick to run away, and the women</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The women of Israel.">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who stayed at home divide the plunder!</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 87:1–2; PSALMS 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Mountain of God,” Mount Bashan, with your many high peaks, Mount Bashan,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; PSALMS 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">why do you look enviously, mountain with many peaks, at the mountain God chose as his home, where the Lord will live forever?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="While this verse is complex, it seems to be suggesting that there were those who questioned the superiority of Mount Zion, and that their own mountains (Mount Bashan) were equally acceptable as places for God to dwell and be worshiped. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God's chariots can't be counted; there are thousands and thousands of them. He comes among them from Sinai into his Temple.</span><br /></span>

Selah.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; PSALMS 7:12; PSALMS 110:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God will crush the heads of his enemies, the hairy heads</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="This may refer to the practice of warriors not cutting their hair during times of war.">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of those who continue to sin.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord says, “I will drag them down from Bashan; I will drag them up from the depths of the sea,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">People watch your processions, God—the processions of my God and King as they go into the Temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The singers are at the front, the musicians at the back, and in the middle girls playing tambourines.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; PSALMS 26:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise God, everyone who has come to worship; praise the Lord, everyone who belongs to Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is the little tribe of Benjamin, followed by the many leaders from Judah; then come the leaders of Zebulun and Naphtali.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 138:8; PSALMS 71:3; PSALMS 42:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Display your power, God! Reveal your strength, Lord, as you have done for us in the past.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; PSALMS 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of your Temple in Jerusalem, kings bring tribute to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 89:10; PSALMS 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Condemn the beasts of the reeds, the bulls and calves!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="The meaning of this line is not clear. Scatter the war-loving nations!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; PSALMS 44:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let Egypt come with bronze gifts;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The word only occurs here in the OT. <span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; PSALMS 117:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to God, kingdoms of the earth, sing praises to the Lord. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 104:3; PSALMS 18:10; PSALMS 68:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to the rider of the ancient heavens,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="In other words, the Creator.">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his strong voice sounding like thunder!</span><br /></span>
