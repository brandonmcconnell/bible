<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; PSALMS 33:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains rose up and the valleys sank down to where you had determined.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You set a boundary for the oceans so they would not come back and cover the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; PSALMS 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You make springs flow into the streams, running down between the hills.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 145:16; Job 39:5–8; PSALMS 104:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">They provide water for all the wild animals, and wild donkeys quench their thirst.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You send down rain on the mountains from your home high above; you fill the earth with good things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; Job 28:5; PSALMS 147:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You make the grass grow for the cattle, and plants for people to eat—crops produced by the earth for food,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">and wine to make them happy, olive oil to make their faces shine, and bread to make them strong.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; PSALMS 92:2; PSALMS 29:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord's trees are well-watered, the cedars of Lebanon that he planted.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wild goats live high in the mountains; hyraxes hide among the rocks.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 69:34; PSALMS 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Consider the sea, deep and wide, full of all kinds of living things, large and small.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; PSALMS 74:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ships sail there, and Leviathan,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A sea monster.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you made to play in it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; PSALMS 147:9; Job 38:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">All creatures look to you to give them food at the right time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 145:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you provide it, they gather it up. You hand out food to them, and they are well-fed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; PSALMS 146:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you turn away from them, they're terrified; when you take away their breath, they die and return to dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you send your breath, they are created, and life covers the earth again.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the Lord's glory last forever! The Lord is happy with all he has made.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 144:5; PSALMS 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He only has to look at the earth and it trembles; mountains pour out smoke at his touch.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 63:4; PSALMS 146:2; PSALMS 145:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will sing to the Lord for as long as I live; I will sing praises to my God my whole life.</span><br /></span>
