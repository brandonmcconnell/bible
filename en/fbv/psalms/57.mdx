<div className="hs">For the music director. According to “Do Not Destroy.” A psalm (miktam) of David concerning the time he fled from Saul and hid in the cave.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 91:4; PSALMS 36:7; PSALMS 17:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please be kind</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “gracious.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to me, God! Be kind to me because I come to you for protection. I will shelter under the shadow of your wings until the danger is past.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 138:8; Philippians 1:6; Hebrews 13:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cry out for help to God Most High, to God who vindicates me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 40:11; PSALMS 43:3; Acts 12:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">From heaven he sends his help, and he saves me. He defeats</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “disgraces.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who persecute me. Selah. God sends me his unfailing love, showing he is completely trustworthy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; PSALMS 35:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am surrounded by man-eating lions—I am forced to live with them! Their teeth are spears and arrows, and their tongues are swords.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3; PSALMS 72:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, your greatness is above the highest heavens; and your glory covers the whole earth!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 145:14; PSALMS 7:15–16; Proverbs 28:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They set a trap to catch me. I was so depressed. They dug a pit on my path but they were the ones who fell into it. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 112:7; Ephesians 5:20; PSALMS 34:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have confidence in you, God, I have confidence in you. I will sing songs of praise to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 16:9; PSALMS 108:1–3; PSALMS 30:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say to myself, “Wake up!” Wake up, harp and lyre! I will wake up the dawn!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 96:3; PSALMS 22:22–23; PSALMS 18:49"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will thank you among the peoples, Lord; I will sing your praises among the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 108:4; PSALMS 103:11; PSALMS 36:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your faithful love reaches to the heavens; your trustworthiness to the clouds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 57:5; PSALMS 8:1; PSALMS 8:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, your greatness is above the highest heavens; and your glory covers the whole earth!</span></span>
