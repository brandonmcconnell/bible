<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, we have heard with our own ears—our forefathers have told us—all you did in their times, long ago.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through your power you drove out the other nations so you could settle our ancestors there; you defeated</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “afflicted.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the nations and you sent our ancestors to occupy the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They did not conquer the land using their swords; it was not through their own strength that the victory was won—it was by your strength, your power, and your presence with them, because you loved them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 74:12; PSALMS 42:8; PSALMS 149:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, you are my King; command victories for Jacob!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 108:13; PSALMS 60:12; PSALMS 118:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only through you can we repel our enemies; only in your name can we defeat our opponents.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 20:7; Hosea 1:7; 1 Samuel 17:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">I do not trust my bow; I do not have confidence in my sword to save me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 136:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the one who saves us from our enemies; you defeat</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “humiliated.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who hate us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremiah 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, we proudly thank you all day long, and praise your name forever. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 60:10; PSALMS 60:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now you have rejected and disgraced us; you no longer accompany our armies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made us run from our enemies, and those who hate us have taken whatever they wanted.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “plundered us.”">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have handed us over like sheep to be slaughtered; you have scattered us among the other nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have sold your own people for next to nothing, making no profit on the sale.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 79:4; PSALMS 80:6; PSALMS 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made a mockery of us before our neighbors, we are ridiculed and laughed at by those around us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 19:21; Job 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made us a joke to the other nations; they scornfully shake their heads at us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 69:7; Jeremiah 3:25; PSALMS 89:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are humiliated all day long; we hold our heads in shame,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 8:2; PSALMS 74:10; PSALMS 74:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">because of all the insults from the people mocking us, because our vengeful enemies are right in front of us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All this has happened to us even though we didn't forget you; we haven't been unfaithful to the promises we made to you.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “covenant.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:51; PSALMS 119:157; PSALMS 78:57"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have not turned away from you, not in thought, nor in action.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “Our hearts have not turned back and our steps have not turned aside from your way.”">e</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 3:5; PSALMS 23:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even so, you crushed us, and made us into a jackal's den. You have covered us with the darkness of death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 81:9; Job 11:13; Deuteronomy 6:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we had forgotten the name of our God, or worshiped other gods,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “spread out our hands to another god.”">f</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 23:24; Hebrews 4:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">wouldn't God have been aware of this, because he knows everyone's thoughts?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; PSALMS 44:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But because of you we are killed all day long; we're considered just sheep to be slaughtered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 78:65; PSALMS 7:6; PSALMS 35:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, wake up! Why are you sleeping? Get up! Don't turn your back on us forever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:24; Deuteronomy 32:20; PSALMS 42:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you look away from us and take no notice of our suffering and misery?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 119:25; Lamentations 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">We lie ruined in the dust, our bodies facedown in the dirt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 130:7–8; PSALMS 25:22; PSALMS 26:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Stand up! Come and help us! Save us because of your trustworthy love!</span></span>
