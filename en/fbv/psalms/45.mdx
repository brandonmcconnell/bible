<div className="hs">For the music director. To the tune “Lilies.” A psalm (maskil) of the sons of Korah. A love song.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The theme is one of a royal wedding.">a</a></sup></div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am moved to write about this wonderful subject. Let me share what I have written for the king. What I say comes from the pen of a skilled author.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are more handsome than anyone else. You always speak graciously, for God has blessed you forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Strap on your sword, mighty warrior, stride out in glory and majesty!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In your majesty ride out to victory, in the defense of truth, humility, and right, because you are strong and act powerfully.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Let your right hand teach you fearful deeds.”">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 21:12; Numbers 24:8; PSALMS 38:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your sharp arrows pierce the hearts of your enemies; the nations fall under you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; PSALMS 93:2; John 1:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your throne comes from God, and lasts forever and ever. The scepter with which you rule is a scepter of fairness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; PSALMS 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You love what is right and hate what is wrong. That is why God, your God, has placed you above everyone else by anointing you with the oil of joy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your robes are perfumed with aloes, myrrh, and cassia; music played on stringed instruments in palaces decorated with ivory makes you happy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The daughters of kings are among the noblewomen; the queen stands beside you on your right, wearing jewelry made of gold from Ophir.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to what I have to say, daughter;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The psalmist is addressing the queen.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">please pay attention. Don't pine for your people and your family.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Songs 4:1–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the king desire you in your beauty; respect him, for he is your lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “daughter.”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Tyre will come with gifts; rich people will look for your favor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Inside her preparation room the princess bride looks wonderful in her golden gown.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Songs 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wearing her beautiful clothes she is brought to the king, followed by her bridesmaids.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">What a happy, joyful procession enters the king's palace!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your sons will take the place of your fathers; as princes you will make them rulers throughout the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; PSALMS 72:17–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through my words</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Implied. Clearly the psalmist himself would not live through all generations.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will be famous through all generations, and nations will praise you forever and ever.</span></span>
