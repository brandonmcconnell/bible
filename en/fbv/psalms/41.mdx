<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5–6; PSALMS 73:26; Philippians 2:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord looks after them when they're sick; he makes them well from their illness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 103:3; James 5:15–16; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, please be gracious to me,” I asked. “Please heal me, for I have sinned against you.”</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:28; PSALMS 56:5–6; PSALMS 31:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">All who hate me talk in whispers about my condition, hoping for the worst.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 71:11; Job 2:7–8; Matthew 27:41–46"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He's been cursed with some terrible disease,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “a thing of wickedness has been poured out on him,” suggesting they think is under some kind of punishment.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they say. “Now he's bed-ridden he'll never recover!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:18; PSALMS 55:12–14; Matthew 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even my best friend,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “the man of my peace.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the one I trusted, who shared meals with me—even he has turned against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 21:8–10; Luke 19:27; PSALMS 18:37–42"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you Lord, please be gracious to me, make me well so I can repay them!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:15; PSALMS 124:6; PSALMS 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know you are pleased with me because you have not let my enemies win and shout in triumph over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 36:7; PSALMS 73:23–24; PSALMS 37:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have supported me because of my integrity, you have brought me into your presence forever.</span><br /></span>
