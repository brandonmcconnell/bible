<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; PSALMS 146:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You turn people back into dust, saying, “Return to dust, human beings.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; PSALMS 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">In your eyes, a thousand years are like a day that's already gone; like a few passing hours of the night.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; PSALMS 73:20; Job 22:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You bring people's lives to a sudden end, like dreams that vanish. They are like grass that grows up in the morning—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; PSALMS 92:7; James 1:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">it sprouts in the morning, fresh and new, but by the evening it is withered and dead.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 39:11; PSALMS 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are burned up by your anger, terrified by your fury.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have set out our sins before you—our secret sins are visible in the light of your presence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 78:33; PSALMS 39:5; PSALMS 90:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our lives fade away under your anger, coming to an end as quickly as a sigh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; PSALMS 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">We live for seventy years—eighty if we have the strength. But even in the prime of life all that we have is hardship and suffering. Soon our lives are over and we're gone.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can know the power of your anger? Who understands your fury so you can be shown reverence?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; PSALMS 39:4; John 9:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Teach us to value every one of our days so we can live wisely!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 106:45; PSALMS 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, how long will it be before you come back to us and have pity on us your servants?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 23:6; PSALMS 86:4; PSALMS 31:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Show us every morning your trustworthy love so we may be happy, full of joy throughout our lives.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 126:5–6; PSALMS 30:5; John 16:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make us glad for as many days as you made us sad, for all the years we suffered!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; PSALMS 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">May we, your servants, see you at work for us</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“For us”—implied.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">again; may our children see your glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">May our Lord God be pleased with us, blessing what we do, blessing what we do.</span></span>
