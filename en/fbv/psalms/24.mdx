<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; PSALMS 51:10; James 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who have clean hands</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Clean” in the sense of innocent and not guilty of sin.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and pure minds, who don't worship idols,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Don't worship idols” or “don't take the name of the Lord in vain.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and who don't lie under oath.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will take with them the blessing of the Lord, vindicated by the God who saves them.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:2; PSALMS 118:19–20; PSALMS 97:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Open up, you gates! Swing wide, you ancient doors! Let the King of glory come in!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–21; Exodus 15:3; Revelation 6:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who is this King of glory? The Lord, strong and powerful, mighty in battle.</span><br /></span>
