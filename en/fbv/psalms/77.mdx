<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 3:4; PSALMS 142:1–3; PSALMS 50:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cry out to God for help—I even shout. If only he would listen to me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I was in trouble I prayed to the Lord. All night long I stretched out my hands to him in prayer but nothing brought me any comfort.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 61:2; PSALMS 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thought about God, groaning loudly; I meditated on him, but I was discouraged. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; PSALMS 6:6; Job 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You stop me going to sleep;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “you seize my eyelids.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I'm so upset I can't even talk.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; PSALMS 143:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I think of the old days, years ago.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 42:8; PSALMS 4:4; PSALMS 139:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I remember the songs I sang in the night. I meditate and ask myself:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; PSALMS 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has the Lord given up on me</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“On me.” No object is given in these verses—it could also be “us” or “the people.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">forever? Won't he ever be pleased with me again?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has his trustworthy love disappeared forever? Have his promises permanently ended?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has God forgotten to be kind? Has he angrily slammed the door shut on his compassion? Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, “This is what hurts me the most: the Most High doesn't treat me the way he used to.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “And I said, ‘This is my wound: the right hand of the Most High has changed.’”">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 143:5; PSALMS 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I remember what you have done, Lord; I remember all the wonderful things you did long ago.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 71:24; PSALMS 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will think about all you have accomplished; I will think deeply about how you have acted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 73:17; Exodus 15:11; PSALMS 89:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, your ways are holy; is any god as great as you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; PSALMS 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the God who does wonderful things! You have revealed your power to the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through your strength you saved your people, the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the waters saw you, God, when they looked they trembled! Yes, they shook down to the very depths!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word used here relates to the deep mentioned in Genesis 1:2. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The clouds poured rain down; the skies crashed with thunder; your lightning flew like arrows.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your thunder rumbled from the whirlwind; lightning flashes lit up the world; the earth quaked and shook.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; PSALMS 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your way led through the sea; your path passed through the deep sea; yet your footprints were invisible.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; PSALMS 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You led your people like a flock, shepherded by Moses and Aaron.</span></span>
