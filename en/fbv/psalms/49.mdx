<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PSALMS 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't be disturbed when people become rich, filling their houses with possessions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they won't take anything with them when they die; their wealth will not go down into the grave</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Into the grave” implied.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with them.</span><br /></span>
