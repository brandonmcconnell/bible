<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; PSALMS 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am the Lord your God who brought you out of the land of Egypt. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I sent them away to follow their stubborn thinking, living as they chose.</span><br /></span>
