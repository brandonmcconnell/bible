<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, please save me, because the water is up to my neck!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm sinking deeper into the mud—there's no solid ground for me to stand. I find myself in deep water; floods wash over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 6:6; PSALMS 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm so tired of screaming out for help my throat is totally raw. My eyes are worn out looking for my God to help me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; PSALMS 35:19; PSALMS 109:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who hate me for no reason are more than the number of hairs on my head. Many enemies try to destroy me by telling lies. How can I give back what I didn't steal?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 38:9; PSALMS 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, you know how foolish I am! My sins are not hidden from you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 25:3; PSALMS 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't let those who trust you be ashamed because of me, Lord God Almighty. Don't let those who follow you be disgraced because of me, God of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For your sake I put up with the insults; my face shows my embarrassment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have become a foreigner to my brother Israelites; a stranger to my very own brothers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; PSALMS 119:139"> <span className="pl pl-1">My devotion for your house is burning me up inside; the insults of those who insulted you have fallen on me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 35:13; PSALMS 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">I wept and I fasted, but they mocked me;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I mourned in sackcloth, but they laughed at me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">People sitting by the town gate gossip about me. I'm the subject of rude songs sung by drunks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; PSALMS 55:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my prayer is to you, Lord, believing this is a good time to respond to me. God, in your wonderful trustworthy love, answer me with your sure salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 144:7; PSALMS 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please rescue me from the mud—don't let me sink! Save me from those who hate me and from drowning in the deep waters!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't let the flood waters wash over me. Don't let the deep waters pull me down. Don't let the grave close over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 109:21; PSALMS 25:16; PSALMS 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Please answer my prayers, Lord, for you are good and love me with your trustworthy love; because of your kindness, please help me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 27:9; PSALMS 102:2; PSALMS 70:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't turn away from me, your servant. Please answer me quickly because I'm in trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 22:19; PSALMS 22:1; PSALMS 111:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come here and rescue me; set me free from my enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You—you know my shame, my disgrace, my humiliation. You see everything my enemies are doing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; PSALMS 142:4; Mark 14:50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Insults have broken my heart—I am so sick there is no cure. I looked for some sympathy, but there was no one! Nobody showed me any compassion.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead they gave me bitter</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “poisonous.”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">herbs to eat and vinegar to drink.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the table set before them become a trap for them, a net that catches them, bringing punishment.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Following the Septuagint text.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">May their eyes become blind so they cannot see, and may their backs always be bent low in dejection.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out your judgment</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “indignation.”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on them; chase them down in your fierce anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the places where they live be deserted. Let their homes be abandoned.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they persecute those you have punished, and make it even more painful for those you have disciplined.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Punish them for the evil they have done. Don't acquit them.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, this verse reads, “Give guilt to their guilt. Do not let them come to your righteousness.”">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blot out their names from the Book of Life. Don't let them be listed with those who do right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 91:14–16; PSALMS 40:17; PSALMS 20:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am suffering and in physical pain. God, please save me and keep me safe.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 28:7; PSALMS 34:3; PSALMS 118:28–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will praise God's character</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “name.”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in song; I will say how incredible he is and how thankful I am.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; PSALMS 50:13–14; PSALMS 50:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is happier with this than with offering animals as sacrifices: cattle, or bulls with horns and hooves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 22:26; PSALMS 34:2; John 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The humble will see this and be happy. May God encourage everyone who comes to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">God hears the poor, and he does not ignore his people who are in prison.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; PSALMS 150:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise him heaven and earth, the seas and everything that lives in them!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; PSALMS 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For God will save Zion, and rebuild the cities of Judah. They</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="God's people.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will live there and own the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 102:28; PSALMS 37:29; Acts 2:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of those who follow him will inherit the land, and those who love him will live there.</span></span>
