<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 34:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; 2 Corinthians 7:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when I'm afraid, I trust in you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; PSALMS 118:6; Romans 8:31–39"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thank God for his promises. I trust in God, so why should I be fearful? What can mere human beings</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “flesh.”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do to me?</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 71:10; PSALMS 59:3; PSALMS 140:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They gather together in their hiding places to keep watch on me, hoping to kill me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 55:23; PSALMS 94:20–21; Isaiah 28:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will they escape when they do so much evil? God, in anger bring these people down!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; PSALMS 39:12; PSALMS 126:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You've kept track of all my wanderings. You've collected all my tears in your bottle.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="To record the extent of the psalmist's sorrow.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You've kept a record of each one.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:31; PSALMS 46:11; PSALMS 118:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then those who hate me will run away when I call for your help. For I know this: God is for me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 1:4; Genesis 32:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thank God for his promises. I thank the Lord for his promises.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:1; Isaiah 51:12–13; Isaiah 51:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I trust in God, so why should I be fearful? What can mere human beings do to me?</span><br /></span>
