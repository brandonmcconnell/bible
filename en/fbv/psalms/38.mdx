<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, please don't condemn me because you're angry with me; don't punish me because you're furious with me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; PSALMS 32:4; PSALMS 64:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your arrows have pierced me deeply, your hand has come down hard on me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 6:2; PSALMS 51:8; PSALMS 31:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you're so upset with me, not a single part of my body is healthy; I am completely sick because of my sins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm drowning in guilt—the burden is too heavy to bear.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">My wounds are infected—they're smelling—all because of my stupidity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; PSALMS 35:14; PSALMS 42:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am bent over, doubled up in pain. The whole day I walk around crying my eyes out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; PSALMS 102:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Inside I'm burning up with fever; no part of my body is healthy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; PSALMS 32:3; Job 30:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm worn out, totally down. I groan because of the anguish I feel in my heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 10:17; PSALMS 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you know what I desperately want, you hear every sigh I make.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 6:7; PSALMS 88:9; PSALMS 69:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart is racing, leaving me with no strength; my eyesight is failing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> <span className="pl pl-1">My loved ones and my friends don't come near me because they're afraid of what I've got. Even my family keeps me at a distance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:20; PSALMS 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who are trying to kill me set traps for me; those who want to hurt me make threats against me, working on their deceitful schemes all day long.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; PSALMS 39:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I act as if I'm deaf to what they're saying,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Implied.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and pretend to be dumb so I don't have to speak.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like a man who can't hear, and who doesn't reply—that's me!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 39:7; PSALMS 17:6; PSALMS 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I'm waiting on you, Lord! You will answer for me, my Lord and my God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; PSALMS 35:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I'm asking you, Lord, please don't let my enemies gloat over me, don't let them be glad when I trip up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 6:6; PSALMS 35:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I'm about ready to collapse—the pain never stops.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">I do confess my sins; I am terribly sorry for what I've done.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; John 15:18–25; PSALMS 69:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have many powerful enemies—they are very active, hating me for no reason.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">They pay me back evil for good; they accuse me for the good I try to do.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:19; PSALMS 22:24; PSALMS 22:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't give up on me, my Lord and my God, don't stay away from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 27:1; PSALMS 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hurry, come and help me, Lord my salvation.</span></span>
