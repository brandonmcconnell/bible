<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 24:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don't be upset over wicked people, or be jealous of those who do wrong.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 92:7; PSALMS 90:5–6; James 1:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For like grass, they will quickly dry up; like plants, they will soon wither away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Matthew 6:31–33; PSALMS 62:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Trust in the Lord, and do good. Live in the land and feed on faithfulness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:7; PSALMS 145:19; John 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Find your happiness in the Lord, and he will give you what you want the most.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Entrust all that you do to the Lord. Place your confidence in him and he will help you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Job 11:17; Isaiah 58:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will make your vindication shine like a light, the rightness of your cause like the noonday sun.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:7–11; PSALMS 27:14; PSALMS 62:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be still in God's presence; wait patiently for him. Don't be upset over people who prosper when they carry out their evil schemes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Proverbs 16:32; James 1:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give up your anger! Let go of your rage! Don't get mad—it only results in evil!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:21; PSALMS 25:13; PSALMS 37:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the wicked will be destroyed, and those who trust in the Lord will take possession of the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:24; PSALMS 103:16; PSALMS 37:35–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">In a little while the wicked will be no more—though you look for them you won't find them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 26:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The humble will own the land; they will live there happily, in peace and prosperity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:1; PSALMS 35:16; PSALMS 31:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked plot evil against those who do good, gnashing their teeth at them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 2:4; Proverbs 1:26; 1 Samuel 26:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Lord laughs at them, for he sees their coming day of judgment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; PSALMS 11:2; 1 John 3:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked draw their swords, and bend their bows to destroy the poor and needy, to kill those who live right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14–15; PSALMS 35:8; Jeremiah 51:56"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the swords of the wicked</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Implied.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will slice through their own hearts, and their bows will be broken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; 1 Timothy 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">It's better to do right and only have a little, than to be wicked and rich.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; PSALMS 63:8; PSALMS 145:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the power of the wicked will be broken, but the Lord supports those who do right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 1:6; Romans 6:23; PSALMS 103:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord knows what is happening to the innocent and grants them an eternal inheritance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 33:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:20–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will not be humiliated in bad times; even in days of famine they will have plenty to eat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 68:2; PSALMS 102:3; Luke 13:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the wicked will die. The enemies of the Lord are like the flowers of the field—they will vanish like smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; PSALMS 112:5; Proverbs 22:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked borrow, but don't repay; while those who do right give generously.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Galatians 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who are blessed by God will own the land, but those he curses will die.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; PSALMS 119:133; Proverbs 4:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord shows the right path to those who follow him, and is happy with the way they live.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; PSALMS 145:14; PSALMS 94:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though they may trip up, they will not fall to the ground, for the Lord holds their hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; PSALMS 37:28; PSALMS 112:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Once I was young, and now I've grown old, yet I've never seen those who do right abandoned, nor their children having to beg for bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 112:5; Matthew 5:7; PSALMS 37:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are always kind, and generous with their loans; their children are a blessing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 34:14; Proverbs 16:6; 1 John 2:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Reject evil, do what is good, and you will live forever in the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 11:7; Proverbs 2:22; PSALMS 21:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord loves fairness and he will never abandon those who are trustworthy. He will protect them forever. But the children of the wicked will die.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:21; PSALMS 37:9; 2 Peter 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who do right will own the land and will live there forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 25:11–13; Ephesians 4:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">People who do right give good advice, explaining what is fair.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 40:8; Deuteronomy 6:6; Isaiah 51:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God's law lives in their minds, so they will not slip from his way.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; PSALMS 10:8–10; PSALMS 37:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked lie in wait for those who do good, intending to kill them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 109:31; 2 Peter 2:9; Romans 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Lord will not let them fall into the hands of the wicked, and he will not let those who do good be condemned when they are put on trial.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; PSALMS 37:9; Proverbs 20:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Trust in the Lord, and stay on his path. He will lift you up and give you the land to own. You will see when the wicked are destroyed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 5:3; Ezekiel 31:18; Isaiah 14:14–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have watched the wicked acting brutally, spreading like a large tree in its native land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 37:10; Acts 12:22–23; Isaiah 10:16–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when I passed that way the next time, they were gone. I searched for them but couldn't find them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 57:1–2; 2 Timothy 4:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Observe the innocent, look at those who do right! Those who love peace have a future!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Proverbs 14:32; PSALMS 52:5; PSALMS 1:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But those who are rebellious will be altogether destroyed; the wicked have no future.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 91:15; Isaiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord saves those who live right; he is their protection in times of trouble.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 31:5; 1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord helps them and rescues them from the wicked. He saves them because they go to him for protection.</span></span>
