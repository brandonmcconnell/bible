<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="DEU" data-book-slug="deuteronomy" data-book-name="Deuteronomy" data-chapter="14" data-verses="29">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> You are the children of the Lord your God. Don't mutilate yourselves or shave your forehead like pagans do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Like pagans do”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>when they mourn the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> because you are a holy people who belong to the Lord your God. The Lord chose you as his special people out of all the nations on earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> You must not eat anything repulsive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the animals that you are allowed to eat:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See also the list in Leviticus 11. Not all identifications of the different animals and birds are certain.">b</a></sup>cattle, sheep, goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> deer, gazelles, wild goats, antelopes, and mountain sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; PSALMS 1:1–2"> You can eat any animal that both has a divided hoof and also chews the cud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> But you are not allowed to eat those that either chew the cud or have a divided hoof. This includes: camels, rabbits, and rock hyraxes. Even though they chew the cud, they don't have a divided hoof. You must treat them as unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> The same applies to the pig. Even though it has a divided hoof, it does not chew the cud. So you must treat it as unclean. You must not eat its meat or touch its dead body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> When it comes to creatures that live in the water, you are allowed to eat anything that has fins and scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But you must not eat anything that does not have fins and scales. You must treat them as unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You are allowed to eat any clean bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> But you must not eat the following: eagle, griffon vulture, bearded vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> buzzard, kites, any kind of falcon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> any member of the crow family,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> tawny owl, long-eared owl, gulls, any kind of hawk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> little owl, eagle owl, barn owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> desert owl, Egyptian vulture, fish owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> storks, any kind of heron, hoopoe, or bats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> All insects that fly are unclean to you; you must not eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But you are allowed to eat any clean animal that has wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> You must not eat any animal that dies. You can give it to a foreigner in your town, and they can eat it, or you can sell it to a foreigner. You, however, are a holy people who belong to the Lord your God. Don't cook a young goat in its mother's milk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Make sure you pay a tithe of every crop grown each year in your fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> You are to eat a tenth of your grain, new wine, and olive oil, and the firstborn of your herds and flocks, in the presence of the Lord your God at the place he will choose to live with you, so that you will learn to always respect the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> However, if it's too far to carry all the tithe that the Lord your God has blessed you with, because the place where the Lord your God will choose to live with you is too distant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then you can exchange it for money, take the money with you and go to the place the Lord your God will choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> Then you can spend the money on anything you like—cattle, sheep, wine, alcoholic drink, or anything else you want. Then you can have a feast there in the presence of the Lord your God and celebrate with your family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Don't forget to take care of the Levites in your town, because they don't have any share in the allotment of land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of every third year, collect a tenth of all your produce for that year and store it in your town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> This way the Levites (who don't have any share in the allotment of land), the foreigners, the orphans, and the widows in your town will be provided with what they need. The Lord your God will bless you in everything you do.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
