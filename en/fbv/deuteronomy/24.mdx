<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 19:7–9; Mark 10:4–12; Isaiah 50:1"> Should a man marry a woman, but then isn't pleased with her because he finds out something shameful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Shameful”: the word means “nakedness” or “genitals.” The issue is likely to be something related to sex.">a</a></sup>about her, he is allowed to write out a certificate of divorce for her, give it to her, and send her out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 10:11; 1 Corinthians 7:15; Ezekiel 44:22"> Suppose that after leaving his house, she goes and marries another man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and suppose the second man also ends up hating her, writes out a certificate of divorce for her, gives it to her, and sends her out of his house—or he may happen to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Joshua 22:17–18; Leviticus 18:24–28"> The first husband who divorced her is not permitted to marry her again after she was shamed, for that offends the Lord. You are not to bring guilt upon the land that the Lord your God is giving you to own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18; Deuteronomy 20:7; Genesis 2:24"> If a man has just got married, he is not to be sent to war or made to do any other duties. He is free to stay at home for one year and make his wife happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19; Luke 12:15; Revelation 18:22"> Don't accept a pair of millstones, or even just an upper millstone, as security for a debt, because that would put the borrower's life in danger.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="For without the mill the family would not be able to grind grain to make bread.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 21:16; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:1–4"> Anyone caught kidnapping a fellow Israelite must be executed, whether the kidnapper makes him a slave or sells him. You must eliminate the evil from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2; Mark 1:44; Leviticus 13:1–46"> When it comes to infectious skin diseases, make sure you follow carefully all the instructions of the Levitical priests. Be careful to follow the orders I've given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 12:10–15; 1 Corinthians 10:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Remember what the Lord your God did to Miriam on the journey out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:8; Exodus 22:27"> If you lend anything to someone, don't go into their house to take some kind of security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Stand outside while they go inside and bring the security out to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Job 22:6; Job 24:3"> If he is a poor man he may give his cloak as security,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“He may give his cloak as security”: supplied for clarity. From the context this is what has happened, but the text does not make this explicit.">c</a></sup>but you must not keep it when you go to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:25; Daniel 4:27; Exodus 22:26–27"> Make sure you give it back by sunset, so that he can sleep in his own cloak and thank you, and you will be counted as doing good by the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 14:31; Proverbs 22:16"> Don't mistreat a paid servant who is poor and needy, whether he's an Israelite or a foreigner living in one of your towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Proverbs 3:27–28; James 5:4"> Pay his wages every day before sunset, because he is poor and is relying on them. If you don't he may complain to the Lord about you, and you will be found guilty of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Chronicles 25:4; Jeremiah 31:29–30"> Fathers are not to be executed because of their children, and children are not to be executed because of their fathers. Each person is to be executed because of their own sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 23:6"> Don't treat foreigners or orphans unjustly; don't take a widow's cloak as security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:15; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> Remember that you were once slaves in Egypt, and the Lord your God rescued you from that place. That's why I'm ordering you to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 14:29"> If when you're harvesting in your field you forget a sheaf there, don't go back for it. Leave it for the foreigners, the orphans, and the widows, so that the Lord your God may bless you in everything you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 19:10"> When you shake the olive trees to knock down the olives, don't go over the branches again. What's left is for the foreigners, the orphans, and the widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 24:19"> When you harvest the grapes in your vineyard, don't go over the vines again. What's left is for the foreigners, the orphans, and the widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; 1 John 4:10–11; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> Remember you were once slaves in Egypt. That's why I'm ordering you to do this.</span>
