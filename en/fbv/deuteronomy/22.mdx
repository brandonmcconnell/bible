<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 23:4–5; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> If you see someone's cow or sheep wandering around, don't just ignore it. Make sure you take it back to its owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> If its owner doesn't live near you, or if you don't know who it is, take the animal home with you and keep it until the owner comes looking, then you can return it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Do the same for whatever else you find that someone has lost—a donkey, a cloak, anything. Don't just ignore it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:1–2; Hebrews 12:12–13; Matthew 5:44"> If you see someone's donkey or cow that has fallen down on the road, don't just ignore it. Help lift it up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:4–15; Deuteronomy 18:12"> A woman must not wear men's clothing, and a man must not wear women's clothing. Anyone who does this offends the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 22:28; Proverbs 12:10; Genesis 8:17"> If you happen to find a bird's nest with chicks or eggs, whether it's in a tree or on the ground beside the road, and the mother is sitting on the chicks or eggs, don't take the mother with the young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> You may take the young, but make sure you let the mother go, so that you will do well and have a good long life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; Exodus 21:28–36"> When you build a new house, be sure to install a railing around your roof, so that you won't be held guilty if someone dies falling from it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> Don't plant your vineyard with another kind of crop. Otherwise everything you produce—the crop you planted and the fruit of your vineyard—must be dedicated to the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“To the Lord”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16"> Don't harness an ox and a donkey together when you plow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19"> Don't wear clothes made of wool and linen woven together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Numbers 15:37–41"> Put tassels on the hem of the cloak you use to cover yourself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> It may happen that a man marries a woman and sleeps with her, but ends up hating her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> and accuses her of being immoral, giving her a bad name, saying, “I married this woman and slept with her, but I found out she wasn't a virgin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The woman's parents are to bring the proof of her virginity to the elders at the town gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and the father will explain to them, “I gave my daughter in marriage to this man, but he has ended up hating her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now he has accused her of being immoral, saying, ‘I found out that your daughter wasn't a virgin.’ But here's the proof of her virginity.” The parents shall spread out the sheet in front of the town elders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> Then the elders of that city shall take the man and punish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Probably by a public whipping.">b</a></sup>him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> They shall also fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give the money to the young woman's father, because this man has given an Israelite virgin a bad name. She is to remain his wife; he is not allowed to divorce her as long as he lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if the accusation is true, and there's no proof of the woman's virginity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> she is to be taken to the door of her father's house, and there the men of her town are to stone her to death. She has done something disgraceful in Israel by acting immorally in her father's house. You must eliminate the evil from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> If a man is found sleeping with someone else's wife, both the man and the woman must die. You must eliminate the evil from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If a man meets a girl, a virgin engaged to another man, and sleeps with her there in the town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> then you must take both of them to the town gate and stone them to death. The young woman is guilty because she did not cry out for help in the town, and the man is guilty because he has brought shame on someone else's fiancée. You must eliminate the evil from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> However, if the man happens to meet a woman who is engaged out in the countryside, and he attacks her and rapes her, only the man has to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> Don't do anything to the woman, because she has not committed a sin punishable by death. (This is the same kind of situation as when a man attacks someone else and murders them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> When the man raped the woman who was engaged out in the countryside, she shouted out, but there wasn't anyone there to rescue her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> If a man happens to meet a virgin who is not engaged, and he grabs her and rapes her, and someone sees them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> the man must pay the woman's father fifty shekels of silver, and he must marry her because he has brought shame on her. He is not allowed to divorce her as long as he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> A man must not marry his father's wife, so that he won't bring shame on his father.</span>
</div>
