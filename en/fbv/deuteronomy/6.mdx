<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> These are the commandments and rules and regulations that the Lord your God has ordered me to teach you to follow in the country that you're going to enter and occupy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> This is so you and your children and grandchildren will show respect to the Lord your God throughout your lives by keeping all his rules and regulations that I give you, and so that you may have a long life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> Listen, people of Israel, and be careful to observe them, so that you may do well and have many descendants in a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the Lord, the God of your forefathers, promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Listen, people of Israel, The Lord our God, the Lord is the only one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Is the only one”: or “is one.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> You shall love the Lord your God with all your mind and with all your being and with all your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> The commands I'm giving you today must stay in your minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; PSALMS 78:4–6"> You are to explain them carefully to your children and talk about them when you're at home and when you're traveling, when you lie down and when you get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> Tie them on your hands as reminders and put them on your foreheads as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> Write them on the doorposts of your homes and on your gates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; PSALMS 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> The Lord your God is going to take you into the country he promised to give you and your forefathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. It's a country with large, prosperous towns that you didn't build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> with houses full of plenty of good things that you didn't provide, that have wells you didn't dig, and vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant. When you eat and are full</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> make sure you don't forget the Lord who led you out of Egypt, out of the prison-house of slavery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Respect the Lord your God. Worship only him, and make promises only in his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> Don't worship any other gods, the gods of the peoples around you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> because the Lord your God, who lives among you, is an exclusive God, and the Lord your God will become angry with you, and he will wipe you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> Don't test the Lord your God like you did at Massah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Exodus 17:7.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; PSALMS 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You must be careful to keep the commandments of the Lord your God and the laws and rules he's given you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> Do what's good and right in the Lord's sight so that it will go well for you and that you may enter and take over the good land that the Lord your God promised to give your forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> He will drive out all your enemies ahead of you, just as he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> In the future, when your children come and ask you, “What's the point of decrees and rules and regulations that the Lord our God ordered you to follow?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> then you are to tell them, “Once we were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt, but the Lord by his power led us out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; PSALMS 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> Before our very eyes the Lord brought down incredible and terrifying signs and miracles on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> But he led us out of there to take us here, giving us this country that he had promised to our forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; PSALMS 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> The Lord ordered us to keep all these laws and to respect the Lord our God, so that we would always be safe and well, as we are today</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> And if we carefully observe each of these commandments as we live in the presence of the Lord, as he instructed us, then we will be right with him.”</span>
</div>
