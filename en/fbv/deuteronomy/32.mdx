<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> His children have acted immorally towards him; so they are no longer his children because of their sinful stains. They are a perverse and corrupt people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Is this any way to repay the Lord, you foolish, stupid people? Isn't he your Father who created you? Isn't he the one who turned you into a nation and made you strong?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; PSALMS 44:1"> Remember the olden days; think about the generations of the past. Go and ask your father about them, and he will explain them to you. Talk to your elders, and they will tell you what you need to know.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; PSALMS 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He found you in a desert land, in a desolate wasteland of whirlwinds. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> Like an eagle watching over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Watching over”: Septuagint reading. <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; PSALMS 81:16"> The Lord gave you the high country to rule,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“He gave him the high country to rule”: literally, “He set him on the backs of the land.”">e</a></sup>and fed you with the crops of the field to eat. <div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; PSALMS 81:16; PSALMS 147:14"> with yogurt from the herd and milk from the flock, with the fat of lambs, with rams from Bashan, and goats, along with the best wheat. You drank the wine made from the best grapes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> You made the Lord jealous by worshiping foreign gods; you made him angry with such disgusting practices.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> You offered sacrifices to demons instead of God, to gods you didn't know anything about, to brand-new gods that your forefathers didn't worship.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have made me jealous by worshiping things that are not God; they have made me angry with their useless idols. <div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; PSALMS 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I will pile disasters on them; I will use up my arrows shooting at them.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> They will waste away from hunger, destroyed by disease and poisonous plague. I will send wild animals to bite them with their teeth, the fangs of snakes that slide along the ground.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> He'll ask, “What happened to your gods, the rock where you went for protection?</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; PSALMS 50:13; Judges 10:14"> Who ate the fat of your sacrifices and drank the wine of your drink offerings? Have them come and help you; have them come and protect you!</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> Listen! I am the only God! There is no other God except me! I bring death, and I give life; I wound, and I heal. No one can be rescued from my power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> I hold up my hand to heaven and solemnly declare on my eternal life,</span>

<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> Then Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and recited all the words of this song for the people to hear.</span>
