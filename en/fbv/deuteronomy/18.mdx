<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> The Levitical priests, in fact the whole tribe of Levi, will not have a share in the land allotment with the other Israelites. They are to eat from the food offerings presented to the Lord—that is what they are entitled to receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; PSALMS 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> While they don't own land among the other tribes, the Lord takes care of them, as he promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> The priests' share of a sacrifice of a bull or a sheep that is brought by the people is as follows. The priests are to be given the shoulder, the jaw, and the insides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; 2 Chronicles 31:4–10"> You are to provide them with the firstfruits of your grain, new wine, and olive oil, as well as the first wool sheared from your flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> For the Lord your God has chosen Levi and his descendants from all your tribes to stand before him and serve in his name forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> If a Levite is convinced he should move from any Israelite town and goes to the place the Lord will choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2–4"> then he shall be allowed to serve in the name of the Lord his God like all his fellow Levites who stand there in service before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 Chronicles 31:4"> They will all receive the same food allowances, despite him having received money from the sale of his father's property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> Once you enter the country the Lord your God is giving you, don't copy the offensive ways of the nations there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> No one is to sacrifice their son or daughter in the fire, practice divination or fortune-telling, use magic or sorcery,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> practice witchcraft, visit a medium or spiritist, or communicate with the dead. Anyone who does such things offends the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> It's because of these offensive things that the Lord your God is driving out the nations before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> You are to be innocent of such things in the presence of the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> Even though these nations, whose land you will occupy, listen to magicians and fortune-tellers, the Lord your God forbids you to do so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> The Lord your God will send you a prophet like me from your people. You must listen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> Remember what you told the Lord your God at Horeb when you were all assembled there. You said, “Please don't let us go on hearing the Lord our God speak to us, or see this terrifying fire anymore! Otherwise we're going to die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> Then the Lord told me, “They are right in what they're saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> I'm going to send them a prophet like you from their people. I will give him my messages, and he will tell them everything I order him to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> I will deal with anyone who does not listen to my message that the prophet speaks in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> However, if any prophet claims to be delivering a message in my name that I have not ordered him to give, or speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must be executed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> You may be wondering, “How can we be sure that a message hasn't come from the Lord?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> When a prophet speaks in the Lord's name and what he says doesn't happen or come true, then that is a not a message from the Lord. The prophet has spoken with contempt for the Lord. Don't be intimidated by him.</span>
</div>
</div>
