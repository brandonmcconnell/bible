<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5"> People of Israel, pay attention to the rules and regulations I'm teaching you to observe. That way you can stay alive, and go in and take over the country the Lord, the God of your forefathers, is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Proverbs 30:6; Revelation 22:18–19"> You are not to add to or take away from what I'm telling you, so that you can keep the commandments of the Lord your God that I'm ordering you to follow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; PSALMS 106:28–29; Joshua 22:17"> You've seen for yourselves what the Lord did at Baal-peor where the Lord your God killed everyone among you who worshiped Baal of Peor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 23:8; John 6:67–69"> Those of you who were faithful to the Lord your God are still alive today, every one of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2; Hebrews 3:5"> Listen, I have taught you the rules and regulations just as the Lord my God ordered me to do, so you can follow them in the country that you are about to enter and take over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:28; PSALMS 111:10; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Be careful to observe them, because this will demonstrate your wisdom and insight to the other people living there who are watching. They will find out about all these rules and say, “The people of this great nation are very wise and have good insight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; 2 Samuel 7:23; PSALMS 34:18"> What nation is as great as us, having a god so close to them as the Lord our God is to us whenever we call on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 147:19–20; Romans 7:12–14; PSALMS 119:127–128"> What nation is as great as us, having such good rules and regulations like all these laws that I'm placing before you today?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 11:19; PSALMS 78:3–8"> Just make sure to be very careful, and really watch what you do, so you don't forget the things you've seen. Keep them in mind as long as you live. Teach them to your children and grandchildren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Hebrews 12:18–19; Exodus 19:16"> That day when you stood in the presence of the Lord your God at Horeb,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Horeb”: another name for Mount Sinai.">a</a></sup>the Lord told me, “Have the people come to me and to hear what I have to say, so they may learn to have respect for me throughout their lives here on earth, and so they may teach this to their children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:23; Exodus 19:16–18; Hebrews 12:18–19"> You gathered and stood at the foot of the mountain. The mountain was on fire, shooting out flames into the sky and producing thick dark clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:15; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 5:22"> The Lord spoke to you from the fire. You heard the words, but you didn't see the form of anyone speaking—there was just a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 9:9–11"> He explained to you his agreement, the Ten Commandments, which he ordered you to follow. He wrote them down on two tablets of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 105:44–45; Ezekiel 21:1–23"> This was the time that the Lord ordered me to teach you the rules and regulations that you are to follow when you arrive in the country you're going to own once you've crossed the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:12; Isaiah 40:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> You didn't see any form when the Lord spoke to you from the fire at Horeb, so be very careful</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Deuteronomy 5:8; Exodus 20:4–5"> that you don't spoil your relationship with the Lord by making an idol for yourselves in any shape or form, whether it looks like a male or female,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:23"> or any land animal or bird that flies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> or of any animal that crawls on the ground or any fish in the deep sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:25; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Kings 21:3"> When you look up at the sky and see the sun, moon, and stars—all the heavenly bodies—don't be tempted to bow down to them. Don't worship them like all the other nations on earth in the way that the Lord has allowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 8:51; Jeremiah 11:4; Exodus 19:5–6"> Remember that the Lord took you and led you out of the iron furnace which was in Egypt to be his own people, just as you are today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:12; PSALMS 106:32–33"> But the Lord was angry with me because of you, and he vowed that I wouldn't cross the Jordan to enter the good land that the Lord your God is giving you to own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:25; Deuteronomy 3:27; 2 Peter 1:13–15"> So I will not be crossing the Jordan, because I have to die here in this land. But you will cross over and occupy that good land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:9; Hebrews 3:12; Joshua 23:16"> However, be careful not to forget the agreement of the Lord your God that he made with you. Don't make an idol for yourselves in the form of anything, because he has forbidden you to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Nahum 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:3"> For the Lord your God is a fire that burns everything up. He is an exclusive God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:16; Hosea 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> After you have been living in the country for a long time and have had children and grandchildren, you may then spoil the relationship you have with the Lord and make an idol in some form. The Lord your God sees this as evil and it makes him angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 6:2; Jeremiah 22:29"> If you do this, I call on heaven and earth to be witnesses against you today that you will be completely wiped out from the country that you are crossing the Jordan to occupy. You will not live long there—you will be totally destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:62–64; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 12:15"> The Lord will scatter you among the other nations, and not many of you will survive in the countries where the Lord has exiled you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 28:36"> There you will worship gods of wood and stone made by human beings. These idols can't see or hear or eat or smell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 119:2; Jeremiah 29:12–14; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> However, if while you're there you decide to come to the Lord your God, you will find him if you come to him whole-heartedly, with complete commitment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5"> When you are in trouble, after you've experienced all these things, then eventually you will return to the Lord your God and do what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; PSALMS 116:5; Nehemiah 9:31"> The Lord your God is a merciful God. He won't abandon you or destroy you or forget the agreement he made with your forefathers, confirmed by a solemn promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 8:8; Matthew 24:31; PSALMS 44:1"> Think about it! Examine the whole of history from the beginning of time, long before you existed, when God made human beings, right up until now. Ask this question anywhere, from one end of the earth to the other: Has anything as amazing as this ever happened before; has anyone ever heard anything like this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Deuteronomy 5:24; Exodus 33:20"> Has a people ever heard the voice of God speaking out of the fire, like you have, and survived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 6:6"> Has any god tried to take a nation out of another nation and make them his own, using tests and signs and miracles and war—with his great power and incredible strength and terrifying actions—like the Lord your God did for you in Egypt, before your very eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 45:18; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 45:5"> You were shown all this so you could be certain that the Lord is God and there is no one like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Exodus 19:19; Deuteronomy 4:33"> He made you hear his voice from heaven so you would obey him. On earth he revealed himself through blazing fire, and you heard him speak to you from the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 13:9"> Because he loved your forefathers, he chose you, their descendants. He himself led you out of Egypt by his great power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Deuteronomy 9:1–5; Exodus 23:27–28"> driving out ahead of you nations that were greater and stronger than you, in order to lead you to their land and give it to you as yours to own, just as you do today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:35; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> So today be sure about this and never forget: the Lord is the only God in heaven above and on the earth below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:3; Ephesians 6:3; Deuteronomy 12:28"> Observe his rules and regulations that I'm giving you right now, so that you and your children will do well, and that you may have long lives in the country that the Lord your God is giving you for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:6; Numbers 35:14–15; Joshua 20:2–9"> Then Moses assigned three sanctuary towns to the east of the Jordan</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Deuteronomy 19:1–10; Numbers 35:15–28"> where a person could run to after accidentally killing someone without deliberate hatred. To save their life they could run to one of these towns:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; 1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 21:27"> Bezer in the desert plain belonging to the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead belonging to the tribe of Gad, or Golan in Bashan belonging to the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:3; Leviticus 27:34; Malachi 4:4"> This is the law that Moses placed before the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:1; PSALMS 119:7"> These are the laws, rules, and regulations Moses gave them after they had come out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:29; Deuteronomy 4:47; Deuteronomy 1:4–5"> This was when they were in the valley to the east of the Jordan near Beth-peor in the country of Sihon king of the Amorites. He had ruled from Heshbon and had been defeated by Moses and the Israelites after they had left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:1–14; Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> They took over his country and the country of Og king of Bashan. (They were the two kings of the Amorites who previously ruled on the east side of the Jordan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Deuteronomy 3:12"> The territory went from the town of Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley all the way to Mount Sirion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Sirion,” here “Sion,” but see 3:9.">b</a></sup>otherwise known as Mount Hermon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 13:20; Deuteronomy 34:1"> and included all the Arabah to the east of the Jordan down as far as the Dead Sea beside the slopes of Mount Pisgah.</span>
