<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="DEU" data-book-slug="deuteronomy" data-book-name="Deuteronomy" data-chapter="7" data-verses="26">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:3; Acts 13:19; Deuteronomy 4:38"> The Lord your God is going to lead you into the land that you are taking over in order to own it, and will drive out ahead of you many other nations: the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, seven nations that are larger and stronger than you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 2:2; Exodus 23:32–33; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> When the Lord your God has handed them over to you to defeat them, then you must set them apart for destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See footnote under 2:34.">a</a></sup>Don't make any peace treaty with them and don't show them any mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Joshua 23:12–13; Exodus 34:15–16"> Don't intermarry with them. Don't let your daughters marry their sons or have their daughters marry your sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:15; Judges 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:26"> because they will lead your children away from following me to worship other gods. Then the Lord will be angry with you, and he will quickly come and kill you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 12:2–3; Exodus 34:13"> On the contrary, this is what you are to do to them: tear down their altars, knock down their idolatrous pillars, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn down their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9"> For you are the Lord your God's holy people. The Lord your God has chosen you to be his own special people, valued above all other peoples on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Romans 9:11–15; Romans 11:6"> It wasn't because there were so many of you that the Lord loved you more than any other nation, in fact he chose you though there were so few of you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Exodus 32:13; Ephesians 2:4–5"> However, because the Lord loved you and kept the promise he gave to your forefathers, He led you out by his power and rescued you from the prison-house of slavery, from the rule of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; 2 Timothy 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> That's why you can be sure that the Lord your God is the true God, the faithful God who keeps his agreement based on trustworthy love to a thousand generations of those who love him and keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Nahum 1:2; Romans 12:19"> But he doesn't hesitate to pay back those who hate him by destroying them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 14:15; Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32"> So keep the commandments and rules and regulations that I'm giving you to follow today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Deuteronomy 7:9; Leviticus 26:3–13"> If you listen to these regulations and are careful to observe them, then the Lord your God will keep his agreement and the trustworthy love that he promised to your forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:21; John 15:10; Deuteronomy 28:3–5"> He will love you and bless you and give you many descendants. He will bless your children and the crops your land produces—your grain, new wine, and olive oil, the calves of your cattle herds and the lambs of your flocks—in the land that he promised your forefathers to give you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 23:26–33; Deuteronomy 28:4; PSALMS 127:3"> You will be blessed more than any other nation; none of you will be childless, and none of your livestock will fail to have young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; PSALMS 105:36–37; Leviticus 26:3–4"> The Lord will keep you from having any kind of sickness. He will not let you have any of the terrible diseases you saw in Egypt, but he will let all who hate you suffer from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:33; Judges 8:27; Deuteronomy 13:8"> You are to destroy all the nations the Lord your God hands over to you. Don't look on them with sympathy. Don't worship their gods, for that will end up being a trap to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 13:32; Numbers 33:53; Deuteronomy 18:21"> You may say to yourselves, “These nations are stronger than us. How on earth can we drive them out?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 105:5; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:29"> But don't be afraid of them. Make sure you remember what the Lord your God did to Pharaoh and the whole of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Jeremiah 32:20–21"> the amazing tests you witnessed, the signs and miracles, the great power and incredible strength the Lord your God used to lead you out. The Lord your God is going to deal with all the nations you're currently afraid of in the same way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Exodus 23:28–30"> On top of this, the Lord your God will send the hornet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See note under Joshua 24:12,">b</a></sup>against them until anyone who has survived and anyone hiding from you have been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:5; Deuteronomy 10:17; Nehemiah 9:32"> Don't be frightened of these nations, for the Lord your God, who is with you, is a powerful and awesome God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:29–30; Joshua 15:63"> The Lord your God will drive out these nations ahead of you in stages. You won't be able to get rid of them all at the same time, or you'd be overwhelmed by wild animals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Exodus 23:29.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:20; Deuteronomy 2:15; Jeremiah 17:18"> But the Lord your God will hand them over to you and throw them into a terrible panic, until they are wiped out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; Joshua 23:9; Joshua 1:5"> He will give their defeated kings to you to be killed, and even their names will be forgotten. No one will be able to withstand you; you will completely destroy them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Chronicles 14:12; Deuteronomy 17:1"> You must burn the idols of their god. Don't desire the silver and gold that covers them. Don't take it or otherwise it will be a trap for you because the Lord your God detests anything to do with idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Hosea 14:8; Ezekiel 11:18"> Don't bring any detestable idol into your house, otherwise you will be treated just like it—by being set apart for destruction. You are to treat idols as totally despicable, and stay far away from them, because they are set apart for destruction.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
