<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29; Deuteronomy 6:10; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> After the Lord your God has destroyed the nations whose land he's giving you, and after you have driven them out and settled in their cities and houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2–7; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> then you are to choose three sanctuary towns in the country that the Lord your God is giving you to own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Hebrews 12:13; Isaiah 35:8"> You are to build roads to these towns. Divide the country the Lord is giving you into three sections. This way anyone who kills someone else can easily run to these towns for protection.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 35:15–24; Deuteronomy 4:42; Isaiah 30:33"> This is what is to happen when a person accidentally kills someone else without meaning to, and runs to one of these sanctuary towns to save their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Proverbs 27:12; 2 Kings 6:5–7"> For example, if a man goes to cut wood in the forest with his friend and swings his axe to chop down a tree, but the head flies off the handle and hits and kills his friend, he may run to one of these towns to save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; 2 Samuel 14:7; Deuteronomy 21:22"> Otherwise the avenger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Numbers 35:19.">a</a></sup>in his anger might chase after the man and catch up with him and kill him, if it's a long distance. The man would be killed even though he didn't deserve to die, because he hadn't meant to cause any harm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> This is the reason why I'm giving this order to choose three sanctuary towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:20; Exodus 34:24; Exodus 23:31"> Should the Lord your God expand your territory, as he promised your forefathers, and give you all the land he said he would,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 20:7–8; Deuteronomy 11:22–25; Deuteronomy 12:32"> and if you are careful to keep all these commandments I'm giving you today, loving the Lord your God and always following his ways, then you are to choose three additional sanctuary towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 27:4–5"> In this way the blood of innocent people won't be shed in the country the Lord your God is giving you to own and you won't be responsible for the death of innocent people who aren't guilty of losing their lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:16–21; Deuteronomy 27:24; Genesis 9:6"> On the other hand, if a man hates someone, hides in wait, and attacks and kills them, and then the killer runs to one of the sanctuary towns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; 1 Kings 2:5–6"> the elders of his home town must send for him, bring him back, and hand him over to the avenger to be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 2:31; Deuteronomy 21:9; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Show him no mercy. You are to eliminate from Israel the guilt of shedding the blood of the innocent, and then all will be well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10"> Don't move your neighbor's boundary marker. It was placed there by your ancestors to mark the land allotment you will receive in the country that the Lord your God is giving you to own.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Deuteronomy 17:6; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> The evidence given by a single witness is not enough to prove a sin or a crime against someone, never mind what offense they are alleged to have committed. The facts must be confirmed by the evidence given by two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 27:12; PSALMS 35:11; Exodus 23:1–7"> If someone falsely accuses another person of a crime,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:9; Deuteronomy 21:5; Malachi 2:7"> both those involved must come to be judged in the presence of the Lord by the priests and judges who are then in office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 17:4; 2 Chronicles 19:6–7"> The judges shall conduct a full investigation, and if the accuser proves to be a liar who has made false accusations</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9; Daniel 6:24"> then you must punish the accuser in the same way they wanted to punish their victim. You must eliminate this evil from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Deuteronomy 17:13; Deuteronomy 13:11"> Then the rest of the people will hear and be afraid, and they will never again do anything so evil among you. Then everyone else will hear about it and be afraid, and won't ever do anything so evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:38–39; Exodus 21:23–25; Deuteronomy 19:13"> Don't show any mercy. The rule is life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, and foot for foot.</span>
</div>
</div>
