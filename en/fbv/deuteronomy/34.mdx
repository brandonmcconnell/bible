<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Deuteronomy 32:49; Joshua 19:47"> Moses left the plains of Moab and went to Mount Nebo, climbing right to the top of Pisgah that faces in the direction of Jericho. The Lord showed him the whole country, from Gilead all the way to Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Numbers 34:6; Exodus 23:31"> This included all the territories of Naphtali, Ephraim and Manasseh, Judah as far as the Mediterranean Sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:15; Judges 1:16; Judges 3:13"> the Negev, and the Jordan Valley from Jericho (the city of palms) down to Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:52; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13"> The Lord told him, “This is the country I solemnly promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob when I said, ‘I'm giving it to your descendants.’ I have allowed you to see it for yourself, but you're not going to enter it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:50; Joshua 1:1–2; Hebrews 3:3–6"> Moses, the servant of the Lord, died there in the country of Moab, just as the Lord had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Deuteronomy 3:29"> He buried him in a valley there in Moab that is opposite Beth-peor, but no one knows to this day where Moses' grave is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:2; Joshua 14:10–11; Genesis 27:1"> Moses was 120 when he died, yet he was still seeing clearly and he was still strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:3; Numbers 20:29; Genesis 50:10"> The Israelites mourned for Moses on the plains of Moab for thirty days, until the time of mourning was over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Daniel 6:3; Acts 6:6"> Joshua, son of Nun, was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses had placed his hands on him to dedicate him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Numbers 27:18.">a</a></sup>The Israelites paid attention to him, and followed the instructions the Lord had given to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 12:6–8; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> Since then there has never been a prophet in Israel like Moses who was the Lord's close friend.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Who was the Lord's close friend”: literally, “whom the Lord knew face to face.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; PSALMS 105:26–38"> The Lord sent him to carry out all the signs and miracles in Egypt to convince Pharaoh and his officials, and all the people in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Moses did these amazing things with tremendous power as the Israelites watched.</span>
