<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:40"> If the person who is guilty is sentenced to be flogged, the judge shall order them to lie down and be flogged before him with the number of lashes the crime deserves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 18:3; James 2:2–3; Luke 15:30"> They are not to receive more than forty lashes. More than that would be to publicly humiliate them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Proverbs 12:10"> Don't muzzle an ox when it is treading out the grain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> When two brothers live near to each other and one of them dies without having a son, the widow is not to marry a stranger outside the family. Her husband's brother is to marry her and sleep with her, fulfilling the requirements of a brother-in-law to provide her with children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Genesis 38:8.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; PSALMS 9:5; PSALMS 109:13"> The first son she has will be named after the dead brother, so that his name won't be forgotten in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> However, if the man refuses to marry his brother's widow, she shall go to the elders at the town gate and tell them, “My husband's brother is refusing to keep his brother's name alive in Israel. He doesn't want to perform the requirements of a brother-in-law for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> The town elders are to summon him and talk with him. If he continues to refuse and says, “I don't want to marry her,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Isaiah 50:6"> his brother's widow is to confront him in the presence of the elders, pull off his sandal, spit in his face, and announce, “This is what happens to the man who refuses to keep his brother's family name alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> From then on his family name in Israel will be called “The Family of the Pulled-off Sandal.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> If two men are fighting, and one of their wives intervenes to save her husband from being beaten, and she grabs hold of the attacker's genitals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> you are to cut her hand off. Don't show her any mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> Don't have two different measuring weights in your bag, one that's heavy and one that's light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Don't have two different measuring containers in your house, one that's large and one that's small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> Make sure you always use accurate and true weights and measures. In that way you will have long lives in the country the Lord your God is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> Anyone who doesn't do so and cheats like this offends the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what the Amalekites did to you on your way out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; PSALMS 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> They came out to confront you when you were tired and weary from your journey, and they attacked all those of you who were lagging behind. They didn't have any respect for God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> Once the Lord your God gives you peace after fighting your enemies in the country that he's giving you to take over and own, you are to wipe out even the memory of the Amalekites from the earth. Don't forget!</span>
</div>
