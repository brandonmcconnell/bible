<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> No Ammonite or Moabite or any of their descendants are allowed to enter the Lord's sanctuary, up to the tenth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> For they did not come to meet you with food and water on your journey from Egypt, and they hired Balaam, son of Beor, from Pethor in Mesopotamia, to curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> But the Lord your God refused to listen to Balaam. The Lord your God turned what was meant to be a curse into a blessing for you because the Lord your God loves you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Don't arrange a peace treaty with them or help them out as long as you live.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> Don't look down on an Edomite, for they are your relatives. Don't look down on an Egyptian either, because you lived as foreigners in their country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> The third generation of their children are allowed to enter the Lord's sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When you are in an army camp during a war with your enemies, make sure you avoid anything wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> Any man there who becomes unclean because of a release of semen must leave the camp and remain outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> Towards the end of the day he must wash himself with water, and at sunset he may return to the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Choose a place outside the camp to be used as a toilet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> You need to have a spade as part of your equipment so that you can dig a hole, and then when you're finished you can cover up your excrement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> The Lord your God is present with you in your camp to keep you safe and to defeat your enemies. Your camp must be kept holy, because if he sees anything unclean among you, he will leave you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> Don't send a slave back to their master if they have come to you for protection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> Let the slave live in your country wherever they want, in whatever town they want. Don't mistreat them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> Don't bring into the house of the Lord your God any money from a prostitute, whether a woman or a man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Man”: the Hebrew uses the derogatory term, “dog.”">b</a></sup>using it to fulfill a promise to the Lord, for both are offensive to the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; PSALMS 15:5"> Don't charge a fellow Israelite interest on money, food, or any other kind of loan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You may charge a foreigner interest, but not an Israelite, so that the Lord your God may bless you in everything you do in the country that you are going in to occupy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; PSALMS 66:13–14; PSALMS 116:18"> If you make a promise to the Lord your God, don't be slow in keeping it, because he will definitely demand that you fulfill it and you will be guilty of sin if you don't.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> If you don't make such promises then you won't be guilty of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> But make sure to carry out what you've said to the Lord your God, because it was you who freely chose to make such a promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> When you walk through your neighbor's vineyard, you can eat as many grapes as you want, but you must not collect any to take with you.</span>
