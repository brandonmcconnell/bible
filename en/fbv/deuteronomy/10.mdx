<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:1–2; Exodus 25:10–15; Deuteronomy 10:3–4"> After that the Lord told me, “Cut out two stone tablets just like the first ones, make an Ark out of wood, and come up to me on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 8:9; Deuteronomy 10:5; Deuteronomy 4:13"> I will write the same words on the tablets that were on the first ones, which you broke. Then put them in the Ark.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:4; Exodus 37:1–9; Exodus 25:10"> I made an Ark out of acacia wood, cut out two stone tablets like the first ones, and went up the mountain carrying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:10; Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 12:18–19"> The Lord wrote what he had before on the tablets, the Ten Commandments that he'd told you when he spoke from the fire on the mountain when we were all assembled there. The Lord gave them to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:29; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 10:2"> and I went back down the mountain and put them in the Ark I'd made following the Lord's instructions. They have been there ever since.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> The Israelites went from the wells of the people of Jaakan to Moserah. Aaron died there and was buried, and Eleazar his son took over as priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> From there they moved on to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land that had many streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> At this time the Lord put the tribe of Levi in charge of carrying the Ark of the Lord's Agreement, as well as serving the Lord by standing in his presence, and of pronouncing blessings in his name, as they continue to do to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> That's why the tribe of Levi has no land allowance or share among the other tribes. The Lord provides what they need, just as the Lord your God promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> I remained on the mountain forty days and forty nights as before, and during that time the Lord listened to my prayers once more and agreed not to destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> Then the Lord told me, “Get ready and continue your journey leading the people so they may enter and take over the land that I promised their forefathers to give them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> People of Israel, what does the Lord your God want from you? He wants you to respect the Lord your God by following all his ways. He wants you to love him. He wants you to worship the Lord your God with all your mind and with all your being,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> He wants you to keep the commandments and regulations of the Lord that I am giving you today for your own good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; PSALMS 24:1; PSALMS 115:16"> Look! Everything belongs to the Lord your God: the heavens, the highest heavens, and the earth and all that is there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> Yet the Lord was attracted to your forefathers and he loved them. He has also chosen you, their descendants, above any other people, even until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> Dedicate yourselves to God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Consecrate yourselves to God” literally, “circumcise the foreskin of your heart.”">a</a></sup>Don't be stubborn and hard-hearted anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> For the Lord your God is God of gods and Lord of lords. He is the great, powerful, and awesome God. He shows no favoritism and accepts no bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 146:9; PSALMS 68:5; Jeremiah 49:11"> He makes sure that orphans and widows receive justice, and he loves the foreigners, providing them with food and clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> You too must love the foreigner because you yourselves were once foreigners in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> You must respect the Lord your God and worship him. Hold onto him and make your promises in his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:14; PSALMS 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is the one you should praise and he is your God, who has carried out for you these incredible and awesome miracles that you've seen with your own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> When your forefathers went to Egypt there were only seventy of them in total, but now God has increased your numbers so much that there are as many of you as there are stars in the sky.</span>
