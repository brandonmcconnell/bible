<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; PSALMS 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> The following is the blessing that Moses the man of God gave to the Israelites before he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Habakkuk 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> He said, The Lord came from Mount Sinai and shone on us from Mount Seir; he blazed out from Mount Paran coming with ten thousand holy ones, holding flaming fire at his right hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> How much you love the people; you hold all the holy ones in your hand. They sit down at your feet to listen to your words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; PSALMS 119:111; John 7:19"> the law that Moses delivered to us that belongs to all the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> The Lord became King in Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Israel”: literally, “Jeshurun.” See 32:15. Also verse 26.">a</a></sup>when the people's leaders gathered, when the tribes of Israel assembled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> To Reuben he said, “May he live and not die, but may he only have a few men.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; PSALMS 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> To Judah he said: “Lord, please hear the cry of Judah and reunite him with his people. Even though he fights for himself, may you help him against his enemies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> To Levi he said: “Your Thummim were given to Levi and your Urim to those dedicated to God, the ones you tested at Massah and argued with at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> Levi said that he didn't pay attention to his father and mother, that he didn't acknowledge his brothers, and that he didn't recognize his children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Meaning that he wasn't influenced by the opinion of anyone in his family.">b</a></sup>The Levites did what you said and kept your agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; PSALMS 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> They will teach your regulations to Jacob and your law to Israel. They will place incense before you, and sacrifice whole burnt offerings on your altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Lord, please bless what they have, and accept their service for you. Destroy those who attack them; make sure their enemies never rise again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; PSALMS 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> To Benjamin he said: “May the one the Lord loves be kept safe and secure in the Lord. The Lord always protects him, letting him rest on his shoulders.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> To Joseph he said: “May the Lord bless his land with the best gifts of heaven—with the dew and water from the depths below;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:19; Matthew 5:45; PSALMS 8:3"> with the best crops ripened by the sun and the best produce of the seasons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> with the finest contributions of the ancient mountains and the best materials of the everlasting hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; PSALMS 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> with the best gifts of the land and everything in it, along with the appreciation of the one who was in the burning bush. May these blessings rest on the head of Joseph like a crown for this prince among his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; PSALMS 44:5"> He is as majestic as a firstborn bull; his horns are like those of a wild ox. He will use them to gore the nations, driving them to the ends of the earth. The horns represent the ten thousands of Ephraim, and the thousands of Manasseh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> To Zebulun he said: “Celebrate, Zebulun, in your travels and Issachar, in your tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; PSALMS 4:5"> They will summon the peoples<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Peoples”: whether this refers to other Israelite tribes or foreign nations is unclear.">c</a></sup>to a mountain; will offer the appropriate sacrifices there. They will enjoy the rich produce of the seas and from trading on the seashores.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> To Gad he said: “Blessed is he who makes Gad's territory larger! Gad is like a lion lying in wait, ready to rip off an arm or a head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> He chose the best land for himself, for he was allocated a ruler's share. He met with the people's leaders; he did what the Lord said was right, following the Lord's regulations for Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> To Dan he said: “Dan is a young lion that leaps out of Bashan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; PSALMS 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> To Naphtali he said: “Naphtali is really favored, full of the Lord's blessing. He shall take over the land to the west and south.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> To Asher he said: “May Asher be blessed more than all the other sons; may he be favored above his brothers and bathe his feet in olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; PSALMS 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> May the bolts of your gate be strong as iron and bronze, and may you be strong all your life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> There is no one like the God of Israel, who rides across the heavens to come to help you; who rides the clouds in majesty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 90:1–2; PSALMS 46:1"> The eternal God is your home, and he holds you up with his everlasting arms. He drives out the enemy ahead of you, and gives the order, “Destroy him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> As a result Israel lives in peace; Jacob has no trouble in a country of grain and new wine, where the heavens drip with dew.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; PSALMS 115:9–11"> How blessed you are, Israel! Is there anyone like you, a people saved by the Lord? He is the shield that protects you, the sword that gives you confidence. Your enemies will tremble before you, and you shall tread them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “their high places,” the pagan shrines.">d</a></sup>underfoot.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
