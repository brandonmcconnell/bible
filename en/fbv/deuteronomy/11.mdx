<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Deuteronomy 10:12; Luke 1:74–75"> Love the Lord your God and do everything he says—his rules, regulations, and commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2–5; Deuteronomy 29:10; Acts 26:22"> You should realize that it's not your children who have experienced the Lord your God's discipline. It was you who saw his greatness, his power and strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; PSALMS 105:27–45"> and his miracles. You saw what he did to Pharaoh king of Egypt and his whole country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 106:11; Exodus 14:23–31; Exodus 15:19"> You saw what he did to the Egyptian army and its horses and chariots when he swept them away in the Red Sea, drowning them as they chased after you. The story hasn't changed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 77:20; PSALMS 106:12–48; PSALMS 78:14–72"> You saw what he did for you in the desert until you arrived here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:9–10; PSALMS 106:17; Numbers 16:1–35"> You saw what he did, right there among you, to Dothan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab of the tribe of Reuben, when the earth split open and swallowed them down—their families, their tents, and all their animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 145:4–6; PSALMS 145:12; PSALMS 150:2"> You saw with your own eyes all the amazing things that the Lord has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:6–7; Ephesians 3:16"> So you must keep every commandment I am giving you today That way you will have the strength to enter and take over the country that you are crossing the Jordan to have as your own,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 6:2"> You will also have long lives in the country that the Lord promised to give your forefathers and their descendants, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 14:18"> The country you're entering to occupy isn't like the country of Egypt that you've come from. There you had to sow your seed and work hard irrigating the ground, like taking care of a vegetable garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; PSALMS 65:12–13"> But here in the country that you are crossing the Jordan to take over is a land of mountains and valleys that receives plenty of rain</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; PSALMS 33:18; 1 Kings 9:3"> It is a land that the Lord your God takes care of. The Lord your God is always watching over it, all year long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Deuteronomy 4:29; Deuteronomy 6:17"> So if you pay careful attention to keep the commandments that I'm giving to you today, if you love the Lord your God and worship him with all your mind and with all your being,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 5:7; Leviticus 26:4; Ezekiel 34:26"> then I will send rain for your land at the right time, rain in autumn and spring, so that you can harvest your grain, new wine, and olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14; Deuteronomy 6:11; Joel 2:19"> I will also provide grass in the fields for your livestock. You will have more than enough to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:27; Hebrews 3:12; Hebrews 2:1"> But make sure that you're not fooled into worshiping and bowing down to other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Deuteronomy 6:15; 2 Chronicles 6:26"> or the Lord will become angry with you. He won't send any rain, and the land won't produce any crops, and you will soon die, even in the good land that the Lord is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Proverbs 3:1"> Focus your minds on the words that I'm saying. Tie them on your hands as reminders and put them on your foreheads as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Isaiah 38:19; Deuteronomy 4:9–10"> Teach them to your children and talk about them when you're at home and when you're traveling, when you lie down and when you get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:9"> Write them on the doorposts of your homes and on your gates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 3:2; Proverbs 9:11"> so that as long as the earth lasts, you and your children may have long lives in the country the Lord promised to give your forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:17; Deuteronomy 11:13; Deuteronomy 10:20"> If you are careful to keep all these commandments I am giving you to follow, if you love the Lord your God and follow all his ways and hold onto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> then the Lord will drive out all these nations ahead of you, and you will take over the country from nations that are greater and stronger than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 14:9; Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21"> Everywhere you walk will belong to you. Your territory will extend from the desert all the way to Lebanon, and from the Euphrates River to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Exodus 23:27; Joshua 1:5"> No one will be able to withstand you; the Lord your God will make everyone living there absolutely terrified of you wherever you go, just as he promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:1; Deuteronomy 30:15–20; Galatians 3:10"> Look! Today I'm placing before you both a blessing and a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Isaiah 3:10"> You will receive a blessing if you obey the commandments of the Lord your God that I'm giving you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 3:10"> But you will receive a curse if you disobey the commandments of the Lord your God and don't keep to the path I'm ordering you to follow today by worshiping some other unknown gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:12–26; Joshua 8:30–35"> When the Lord your God takes you into the country you are going in to occupy, announce the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Judges 7:1; Joshua 4:19"> (These mountains are to the west of the Jordan, in the country of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah near Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 1:11; Deuteronomy 9:1; Joshua 3:13–17"> Shortly you're going to cross the Jordan to enter and occupy the country the Lord your God is giving you. When you take it over and settle there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Deuteronomy 12:32; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> make sure you follow all the rules and regulations that I'm giving you today.</span>
