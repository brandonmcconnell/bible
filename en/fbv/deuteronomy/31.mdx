<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Once Moses had finished<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dead Sea Scrolls and Septuagint reading.">a</a></sup>giving these instructions to all the Israelites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> he told them, I'm now a hundred and twenty years old. I can't get around like I used to, and the Lord has told me, “You are not to cross the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 3:28"> The Lord your God himself is going to lead you across. He will destroy these nations as you advance, and you will take over their land. Joshua will cross ahead of you, as the Lord said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:28–31; Deuteronomy 2:33; Deuteronomy 3:3–11"> The Lord will do to them what he did to Sihon and Og, the kings of the Amorites, and their land when he destroyed them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 23:32–33"> The Lord will hand them over to you, and you will deal with them exactly as I've told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10; PSALMS 27:14"> Be strong! Be brave! Don't be frightened or terrified of them, because the Lord your God will go with you. He won't ever leave you or abandon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:38"> Moses called for Joshua and told him in front of all the Israelites, “Be strong!! Be brave! You will accompany this people into the country that the Lord promised to give their forefathers, and you are to allocate it for them to own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Deuteronomy 31:6; Joshua 1:9"> The Lord himself goes ahead of you. He will be with you. He won't ever leave you or abandon you. Don't be afraid and don't be discouraged.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Joshua 3:3; Deuteronomy 17:18"> Moses wrote this law down and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the Ark of the covenant of the Lord, and to all the Israelite elders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34–43; Deuteronomy 15:1–2"> Moses gave them these orders, “At the end of every seven years, the year when debts are canceled during the Festival of Shelters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:2; Joshua 8:34–35; Deuteronomy 16:16–17"> when all the Israelites come before the Lord your God at the place he will choose, you are to read this law for everyone to hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; PSALMS 34:11–14; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Have the people gather together—the men, women, children, and the foreigners living with you—so that they can listen and learn to respect the Lord your God and to carefully observe all the instructions of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:2; Deuteronomy 6:7; Proverbs 22:6"> Then their children who are ignorant of the law will listen and learn to respect the Lord your God for as long as you live in the country you are going to occupy once you cross the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Deuteronomy 31:23; Job 2:1"> Then the Lord told Moses, “Listen, you are soon going to die. Tell Joshua to meet you, and then both of you are to come and stand before me at the Tent of Meeting, so that I may appoint him as leader.”<br />Moses and Joshua went to stand before the Lord at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 40:38; PSALMS 99:7; Exodus 33:9–10"> The Lord appeared there at the tent in a pillar of cloud. The cloud stood at the entrance to the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Judges 10:13; Judges 2:12"> The Lord told Moses, “You will join your ancestors in death, and these people are going to prostitute themselves by worshiping the foreign gods of the country they are entering. They will abandon me and break the agreement I've made with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:42; Isaiah 8:17; PSALMS 104:29"> Then I will abandon them and turn away from them, so they will be destroyed, and they will experience many disasters and problems.<br />At that time they'll say, ‘We're suffering these disasters because our God has abandoned us!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> Then I will definitely ignore them because of all the evil they have done by worshiping other gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 31:21–22; Deuteronomy 31:26"> So write down this song and teach it to the Israelites. Help them learn to sing it, so it will provide evidence for me against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Nehemiah 9:25–26"> Once I've led them into the country that I promised to give their forefathers, a land flowing with milk and honey, they will have plenty to eat and will do well. Then they will go and worship other gods, and they will abandon me and break my agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; PSALMS 139:2; John 2:24–25"> Once they've experienced plenty of trouble and suffering, this song will testify against them, because their descendants won't forget the words. I know what they're like, even before I lead them into the country that I promised to give them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Deuteronomy 31:9"> Right then Moses wrote down this song and taught it to the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:14; Acts 7:45"> Then the Lord appointed Joshua son of Nun as leader, and told him, “Be strong! Be brave! For you are going to lead the Israelites into the country that I promised to give them, and I will be with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 17:18"> After Moses had finished writing down this Law in a book from start to finish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9"> he ordered the Levites who carried the Ark of the Lord's Agreement:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; 2 Kings 22:8–11; Galatians 2:19"> “Place this book of the Law beside the Ark of the Lord your God's Agreement, so that it will be kept there as a evidence against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:20; Deuteronomy 9:24; Acts 7:51"> I know how stubborn and rebellious you are. If you've already started rebelling against the Lord while I'm still alive, how much worse will you get after I die?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 4:26; Deuteronomy 30:19"> Summon all the elders of your tribes and all your officers to gather here before me so that I can tell them this directly and call on heaven and earth as witnesses against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:19; Deuteronomy 32:5; Luke 21:24"> I know that after my death you will become completely immoral, leaving the path I have ordered you to follow. In the future, evil things will happen to you because of the evil sins you commit in the sight of the Lord, making him angry by what you do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2; Deuteronomy 4:5; John 12:49"> Then Moses recited all the words of this song as the Israelites listened.</span>
</div>
</div>
