<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Matthew 7:21; Luke 13:25"> Israel calls out to me, “Our God, we know you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:66; Deuteronomy 28:25; 1 Timothy 5:12"> But Israel has rejected what is good. An enemy will chase after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 15:10–30; Hosea 13:9–10; Hosea 2:8"> They appointed kings without my consent and chose princes without my knowledge. With their silver and gold they made idols for their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Jeremiah 13:27; Proverbs 1:22"> Samaria, I reject this calf idol you have made! My anger burns against them! How long will you be incapable of being good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:2; Isaiah 44:9–20; PSALMS 135:15–18"> This idol is from Israel! A craftsman made it—it's not God! The calf of Samaria will be broken in pieces!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 22:8; Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7"> Those who sow the wind will reap the whirlwind. The stalk has no head of grain; it will produce no flour. Even if it did produce grain, foreigners would swallow it all up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 51:34; Romans 9:22"> Israel is swallowed up; among the nations they are like something nobody wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Hosea 7:11; Ezekiel 16:33–34"> They have gone up to Assyria like a donkey wandering alone. Ephraim has hired lovers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Ezekiel 23:22–26; Isaiah 10:8"> Even though they have hired allies among the nations, I will gather them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This could refer either to the Israelites or to the nations. Even though Israel tried to hire allies, eventually these allies worked together against Israel.">b</a></sup>together. Then they will writhe under the burden of the great king.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referring to the burden of taxation needed to pay off foreign invaders, particularly the Assyrians.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 10:1–2; Hosea 10:8"> Though Ephraim built many altars to make sin offerings, they instead became altars of sin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:6–8; Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 22:20"> I wrote down for them the many aspects of my law, but they looked on them as foreign.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In other words, they regarded the law as not applying to them.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 9:6; Amos 8:7"> They present me with their sacrifices, and they eat the meat, but I, the Lord, do not accept them. Now he will remember their wickedness, and punish them for their sins. They will return to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 17:27; Amos 2:5"> Israel has forgotten his maker, and built palaces; Judah has built many fortified cities. But I will rain fire down on their cities and burn up their fortresses.</span>
