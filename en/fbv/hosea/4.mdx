<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Micah 6:2; Isaiah 3:13–14"> Hear the word of the Lord, people of Israel, for the Lord has an accusation against the inhabitants of the land! “There is no faithfulness, no loyalty, and no knowledge of God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 6:7; Hosea 7:1"> Instead there is only cursing, lying, murdering, stealing, and adultery. They commit violence, and bloodshed follows bloodshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Isaiah 24:4–12; Nahum 1:4"> As a result the land dries up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “is mourning.”">a</a></sup>and everyone who lives there is wasting away, along with the wild animals and the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea—they are all dying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Hosea 4:17; Amos 5:13"> But no one should accuse or blame anyone else. My quarrel is with you the priests—you are the ones responsible.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="What seems to be meant here is that the priests have done such a poor job of representing God and leading the people in correct ways that there's no point in ordinary people blaming one another.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:8; Hosea 2:2; Galatians 4:26"> Consequently you will stumble in daylight, and the prophet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Presumably false prophets.">c</a></sup>will stumble together with you in the night, and I will destroy your mother.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Mother—meaning the nation of Israel.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Proverbs 19:2; Hosea 6:6"> My people are dying because they don't know me. Because you have refused to know me, I refuse to accept you as my priests. You have forgotten my teachings, so I will forget your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Philippians 3:19; 1 Samuel 2:30"> The more there were of them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to the priests.">e</a></sup>the more they sinned against me, so I will change their glory into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Isaiah 56:11; Titus 1:11"> They feed on the sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “sin offerings.” Because the priests were given a share of what was offered, it was in their interest to increase the need for sacrifices.">f</a></sup>of my people, greedy for their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 24:2; Jeremiah 5:31; Matthew 15:14"> So it will be for the people like the priests: I will punish them for what they have done, repaying them for their actions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 1:6"> They will eat, but they will not be satisfied; they will engage in prostitution,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Prostitution here can have various meanings—the basic act of adultery, visiting temple priestesses/prostitutes as part of pagan “worship,” and spiritual prostitution through breaking vows to the true God by following heathen gods.">g</a></sup>but they will not prosper because they have given up on the Lord to go and prostitute themselves to other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 28:7"> They destroy their minds with old wine and new wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:4; Habakkuk 2:19"> My people consult their wooden idols and their divining rods give them answers, for a spirit of prostitution makes them go astray. Prostituting themselves, they have deserted their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 3:6; Ezekiel 6:13"> They offer sacrifices on the tops of mountains; they burn incense in the hills under the oak, poplar, and terebinth trees where the shade is pleasant. That's the reason why your daughters practice prostitution and your daughters-in-law commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; John 8:43; Ephesians 4:18"> I will not punish your daughters when they practice prostitution, nor your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery, because you men visit whores and sacrifice with temple prostitutes. A people who lack understanding end up in disaster.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 9:15; Amos 4:4"> Though you, Israel, have become a prostitute, may Judah not offend in the same way and not enter Gilgal, nor go up to Beth-aven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="One place that was called Gilgal was where the Israelites crossed the Jordan and set up a stone monument (see Joshua 4). <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 8:5; Jeremiah 3:11"> For Israel is as stubborn as a stubborn cow. Should the Lord then take care of them like a lamb in a wide pasture?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 81:12; Revelation 22:11; Hosea 13:2"> Ephraim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Ephraim was the leading tribe of the northern kingdom of Israel and so was often used to describe the whole kingdom.">i</a></sup>is bewitched<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The word used can mean “joined to” or “enchanted, under a spell.”">j</a></sup>by idols, so leave him alone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:12; 2 Kings 17:7–17"> Once the leaders finish drinking, they go off and find prostitutes to sleep with. They love their shameful acts more than honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15"> A wind will carry them away; they will be ashamed of their pagan worship.”</span>
</div>
