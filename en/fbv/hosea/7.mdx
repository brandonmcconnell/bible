<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Micah 7:3–7; Hosea 6:4"> when I heal Israel, Ephraim's sins will be exposed, together with the evil actions of Samaria. They deal in lies; they are thieves who break in to homes and who rob people on the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremiah 14:10"> But they don't realize that I remember all of their wickedness. Their sins surround them and are always before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremiah 28:1–4"> They make their king happy with their wickedness, and the princes with their lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremiah 5:7–8"> All of them are adulterers, burning with lust like an oven whose fire stays hot, even though it is not tended by the baker, having left the dough to rise after kneading.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The image is of a very hot fire that is prepared for baking bread. The rising of the dough is compared to the time of Israel before their end, carried off into captivity. It also seems to be a reference to the king who does nothing to keep the fire of apostasy under control.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> On the king's birthday<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “the day of the king.”">b</a></sup>the princes drink so much they are sick, while he joins in with those who mock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 7:7; Hosea 7:4; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> Their minds are fired up like an oven; they go to him with their plots. All through the night their anger burns; in the morning it blazes out uncontrollably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 15:10"> All of them are as hot as a burning oven, and they exhaust their leaders. All of their kings have fallen, and not one of them calls on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim mixes with the foreign nations; he is as useless as half-baked bread!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “a flat bread that is not turned over.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 8:7; Isaiah 42:22–25; 2 Kings 13:3–7"> Foreigners drain his strength, and he doesn't even realize it. His hair is turning grey and he doesn't even notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:5; Isaiah 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> Israel's pride testifies against him, but for all of this he still does not return to the Lord their God or look for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 5:13; Hosea 11:11"> Ephraim is like a dove, foolish and without sense—calling to Egypt, then going to Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> When they go I will throw my net over them; I will bring them down like wild birds. When I hear them flocking together I will punish them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:12; Hosea 11:12; Isaiah 63:8"> What disaster is coming to them because they have strayed from me! Destruction is coming to them because they have rebelled against me! I wish I could redeem them, but they tell lies about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremiah 3:10"> They do not cry out to me with sincere minds; instead they lie on their beds howling. They assemble together and gash themselves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A practice used in pagan worship—see 1 Kings 18:28.">d</a></sup>to gain grain and new wine, but they turn away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; PSALMS 62:3; Jeremiah 17:9"> I myself trained them and made them strong, but now they plot evil against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 78:57; Hosea 9:3; Ezekiel 23:32"> They turn, but not to the Most High; they are like a useless bow. Their leaders will be killed by the sword because of their cursing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Usually understood to be cursing against God.">e</a></sup>Because of this they will be ridiculed in Egypt.</span>
