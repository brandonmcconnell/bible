<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:18; Deuteronomy 32:39; 1 Samuel 2:6"> “Come on! Let's go back to the Lord. He has torn us to pieces, but now he will heal us. He has struck us down, but now he will bind up our wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5; Isaiah 26:19; Ezekiel 37:11–13"> In two days he will heal us; after three days he will raise us up so we can live in his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hosea 14:5; PSALMS 72:6"> Let's get to know the Lord, really strive to know him, and he will appear to us as surely as the sun will rise; he will come to us as certainly as the spring rains fall on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Hosea 11:8; PSALMS 78:34–37"> Ephraim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ephraim was taken as representative of the northern kingdom of Israel, and Judah the south.">a</a></sup>what shall I do with you? Judah, what shall I do with you? Your love disappears like mist at dawn; it vanishes like dew in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Jeremiah 1:10; Hebrews 4:12"> So I cut you down through the prophets; I killed you with my words. My judgment shines out like a light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:13; Matthew 12:7; Proverbs 21:3"> I want your true love, not your sacrifices; I want you to know me, not your burnt offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:7; Hosea 8:1; Genesis 3:11"> But you, just like Adam, broke our agreement;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “covenant.”">b</a></sup>there you were unfaithful to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Micah 7:2; 1 Kings 2:5"> Gilead is a city of evil people where footprints are tracked in blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Hosea 7:1; Zephaniah 3:3"> Priests are like a gang of bandits, lying in wait to ambush travelers on the road. They commit murder in Shechem, carrying out wicked crimes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 5:30–31"> I have seen in the house of Israel something totally disgusting: Ephraim engages in prostitution and Israel is sexually corrupt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The image of prostitution and sexual immorality is used to describe Israel's spiritual adultery in following other gods.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Job 42:10; Jeremiah 51:33"> And as for you, Judah, your harvest-time has been set! When I restore the fortunes of my people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“When I restore the fortunes of my people.” This latter phrase is better taken with the beginning of the following chapter.">d</a></sup></span>
