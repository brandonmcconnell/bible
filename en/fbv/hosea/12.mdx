<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:12; Hosea 8:7; Ezekiel 17:10"> Ephraim tries to herd the wind, chasing after the east wind all day long. Their lies and violence keep on increasing. They make a treaty with Assyria, and send olive oil to Egypt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, while making a treaty with Assyria they are covering their options by trying to gain support from Egypt as well by sending supplies of olive oil.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Isaiah 10:6; Hosea 4:1"> The Lord also has an accusation against Judah, and will punish Jacob for the way the people act; he will repay them for what they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; Genesis 32:24–28; Romans 9:11"> Even in the womb he fought with his brother;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “he grasped his brother's heel.”">b</a></sup>when he became a man he fought with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:15; Malachi 3:1; Genesis 35:9–10"> He fought with the angel, and he won. He wept, and begged him for a blessing. Jacob found God at Bethel, and spoke with him there—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; PSALMS 135:13"> the Lord God Almighty, the Lord is his memorable name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Hosea 14:1; Micah 7:7"> You must return to your God. Act lovingly and do what is right, and always wait for your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:5–6; Proverbs 11:1"> A merchant who holds in his hands dishonest scales loves to swindle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; PSALMS 62:10; PSALMS 52:7"> Ephraim says, “I'm rich! I've made myself wealthy! I've made so much from my work, and nobody can prove me guilty of doing wrong.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> But I am the Lord your God who brought you out of the land of Egypt. I will make you live again in tents like you do at the time of that particular festival.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This refers to the Festival of Tabernacles where the people lived outside in tents or shelters for a week to remember their journey through the wilderness.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> I spoke through the prophets. I myself gave many visions and parables through the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> If Gilead is idolatrous,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The word used here is the same as in Beth-aven, the parody name for Bethel, signifying the worship of idols who are truly worthless, they are nothing.">d</a></sup>they will surely come to nothing. In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls. Even their altars are like heaps of rocks in the furrows of the field.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This verse suggests mixed worship that merged idols with the true God. Like rocks in a plowed field, this was barrier to knowing the true God.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> Jacob fled to the land of Aram; Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Israel was the new name God gave to Jacob.">f</a></sup>worked there to gain a wife, earning a wife by looking after sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; PSALMS 77:20"> Through a prophet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The prophet referenced here is Moses.">g</a></sup>the Lord brought Israel up from Egypt, and by a prophet they were cared for.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The same word is used for Jacob looking after sheep and the Lord looking after Israel through Moses.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Daniel 11:18; 2 Kings 17:7–18"> Ephraim has really upset the Lord, and the Lord will let them have the consequences of their bloodshed and pay them back for their contempt.</span>
</div>
