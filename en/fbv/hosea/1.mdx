<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 9:25; Micah 1:1; Isaiah 1:1"> The Lord sent a message to Hosea son of Beeri at the time when Uzziah, Jotham, and Ahaz were the kings of Judah, and Jeroboam son of Jehoash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Spelled here “Joash.”">a</a></sup>was the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:4–5; Hosea 3:1; Deuteronomy 31:16"> The Lord began speaking through Hosea by telling him, “Go and take a wife who is a prostitute and have children born to a prostitute,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “children of prostitution.” There is much debate over this phrase. Some believe this indicates that Gomer had children as a result of her prostitution before marrying Hosea. Others believe this could have happened later as she returned to her former ways (note that only the first child, Jezreel, is clearly identified as being Hosea's). Still others believe that this means any children Hosea had with Gomer would be tainted by her reputation as a prostitute.">b</a></sup>because those living in this land have committed terrible prostitution by turning away from the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:1–3"> So Hosea went and married Gomer, the daughter of Diblaim. She became pregnant and bore Hosea a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 10:7–8; 2 Kings 10:10–11; Ezekiel 23:10"> The Lord told Hosea, “Call him Jezreel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jezreel means “the Lord will sow” (or scatter, since that was the way in which seed was sown). Jezreel was historically a place of much violence and bloodshed.">c</a></sup>because I am going to punish the house of Jehu for the blood he shed at Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Joshua 17:16; Hosea 2:18"> On that day I will break Israel's military power in the valley of Jezreel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; Hosea 2:4"> Gomer became pregnant again and gave birth to a daughter. The Lord told Hosea, “Call her Lo-ruhamah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The name means “unloved.”">d</a></sup>because I will no longer love the house of Israel and certainly not forgive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; Isaiah 12:2; 2 Kings 19:35"> But I will have pity on the house of Judah, and I will save them—but I will not save them by bow or sword or war or horses and riders.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> After Gomer had weaned Lo-ruhamah she became pregnant again and gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1"> The Lord told Hosea, “Call him Lo-ammi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The name means “not my people.”">e</a></sup>because you are not my people and I am not your God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “I am not yours.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Galatians 4:6–7; John 1:12"> Even so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Even so”—transition supplied. The Hebrew simply says “and.”">g</a></sup>the number of the people of Israel will be like the sand on the seashore that cannot be measured or counted. Then right there at the place where they were told ‘you are not my people’ they will be called ‘the children of the living God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 11:12–13; Jeremiah 50:4–5"> The people of Israel and the people of Judah will be gathered together and they will choose one leader for themselves and they will take possession of the land, and the day of Jezreel will be great.”</span>
</div>

## Footnotes

<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">1:1</span> Spelled here “Joash.”</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">1:2</span> Literally “children of prostitution.” There is much debate over this phrase. Some believe this indicates that Gomer had children as a result of her prostitution before marrying Hosea. Others believe this could have happened later as she returned to her former ways (note that only the first child, Jezreel, is clearly identified as being Hosea's). Still others believe that this means any children Hosea had with Gomer would be tainted by her reputation as a prostitute.</li>

<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">1:6</span> The name means “unloved.”</li>
<li id="fn-e"><a href="#fnref-e" className="fnb">e</a> <span className="fnr">1:9</span> The name means “not my people.”</li>
