</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; PSALMS 4:4; Obadiah 1:9"> God came from Teman; the Holy One from Mount Paran.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Teman is in the land of Edom, while Mount Paran is in the Sinai Peninsula.">b</a></sup>Selah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Selah”: an unknown term often also used in Psalms.">c</a></sup>His glory covered the heavens; the earth was full of his praise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> His brightness is like lightning; rays flash from his hand from where his power is hidden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; PSALMS 78:50–51"> Plague goes before him, disease<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “bolts of fire.”">d</a></sup>follows at his feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Nahum 1:5"> Where he stands, the earth shakes. When he looks the nations tremble. The ancient mountains shatter, the age-old hills collapse, but his ways are eternal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> I saw the tents of Cushan suffering, the tent curtains of the land of Midian tremble.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="By this Habakkuk probably means the people that lived in these tents.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Did you burn with rage against the rivers, Lord? Were you angry with the rivers? Were you furious with the sea when you rode your horses and chariots of salvation?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 7:12–13; PSALMS 105:41; PSALMS 78:15–16"> You took out your bow; you filled your quiver with arrows. Selah. You split the earth open with rivers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; PSALMS 93:3"> Mountains saw you and shook. Water poured down and swept by. The deep called out, lifting high its waves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “hands.”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 144:5–6; PSALMS 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> The sun and moon stood still in the sky as your sparkling arrows flew and your spears flashed bright.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> Indignant, you marched across the earth, trampling the nations in your anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 110:6; PSALMS 105:15; PSALMS 68:19–23"> You came out to save your people, to save your chosen people. You crushed the head of the house of the wicked, stripping him from thick to neck.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This verse has been interpreted in many ways.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; PSALMS 118:10–12"> With his own arrows you pierced the heads of his warriors, those who came like a whirlwind to scatter me, gloating like those who secretly abuse the poor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> You trod upon the sea with your horses, churning up the mighty waters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> I shook inside when I heard this; my lips quivered at the sound; my bones turned to jelly; I trembled where I stood. I wait quietly for the day of trouble that will come upon those who attacked us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> Even though there are no blossoms on the fig trees and there are no grapes on the vines; even though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no harvest; even though there are no animals in the pen and no cattle in the stalls;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Micah 7:7"> still I will be happy in the Lord, joyful in the God of my salvation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:34; PSALMS 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> The Lord God is my strength. He makes me able to walk in the highest mountains, as sure-footed as a deer.<br />(To the music director: with my stringed instruments.)</span>
</div>
</div>
