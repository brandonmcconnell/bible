<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> This is the message that Habakkuk saw in vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; PSALMS 13:1–2"> Lord, how long do I have to cry out for help and you don't listen? I cry out, “Violence!” but you don't save us from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> Why do you force me to see this wickedness and suffering? Why do you just observe such destruction and violence? Arguments and fighting happen right in front of me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> As a result the law is paralyzed, and justice never wins. The wicked crowd out those who do right so that the course of justice is perverted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> Look around at the nations, watch and be surprised and amazed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The is the beginning of the Lord's response.">a</a></sup>Something is going to happen in your time that you wouldn't believe even if you were told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> Watch! I am raising up the Babylonians,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “Chaldeans.”">b</a></sup>a cruel and brutal people who will march across the world to seize other lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> They are fearsome and terrifying, and so proud of themselves that they set their own rules.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In other words, they do whatever they like.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> Their horses are faster than leopards and fiercer than hungry wolves. Their cavalry charges, racing in from far away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Masoretic Text has “their horsemen, yes their horsemen.” The Habakkuk pesher (commentary) from Qumran is the basis for the reading here.">d</a></sup>Like eagles, they swoop down to eat their prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> Here they come, all intent on violence. Their armies advance in frontal assault as rapidly as the desert wind, capturing so many prisoners they are like sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> They mock kings and scoff at rulers. They laugh in scorn at fortresses—they pile up earth ramps and capture them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> Then they sweep on by like the wind and are gone. They are guilty because their own strength is their god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> Haven't you existed from eternity past? You are Lord my God, my Holy One, you do not die. Lord, you appointed them to execute judgment; God our Rock, you sent them to punish us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 5:4–5; PSALMS 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Your eyes are too pure to look upon evil; you cannot stand the sight of wrong. So why do you put up with untrustworthy people? Why are you silent when the wicked destroy those who do less evil than they do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> You make people become like fish in the sea, or like crawling insects, that have no ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; PSALMS 10:9"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Babylonians.">e</a></sup>drag everyone up with hooks, they pull them out with nets, catching them in dragnets. Then they happily celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> They worship their nets as if they were gods, making sacrifices and burning incense to them, because by their nets they live in luxury, eating rich food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> Will they keep on unsheathing their swords<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Unsheathing their swords”: Habbakuk pesher (commentary) from Qumran reading.">f</a></sup>forever, killing nations without mercy?</span>
</div>
