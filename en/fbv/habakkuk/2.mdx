<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 21:8; Isaiah 62:6; 2 Samuel 18:24"> I will climb my watchtower; I will take my place on the city wall. I will keep watch and see what he will say to me, how he will answer my grievances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Revelation 14:13; Revelation 1:18–19"> Then the Lord told me, Write down the vision, inscribe it on tablets, so it can be easily read.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “so he who runs can read it.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; Hebrews 10:36–37; Ezekiel 12:25"> For the vision is for a future time, it is about the end and it does not lie. If it seems slow in being fulfilled, wait for it, for it will definitely come—it will not be delayed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> Look at those who are proud!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Again this applies to the main subject of the vision, the Babylonian people.">b</a></sup>They do not live right. But those who live right do so through their trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11–12; Proverbs 27:20; Proverbs 20:1"> In addition wealth provides no security.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Wealth provides no security”: following one understanding of the Habbakuk pesher (commentary) from Qumran. The Masoretic Text has “wine is deceptive.”">c</a></sup>Those who are arrogant never have any peace; their greedy mouths are as wide open as the grave,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Sheol,” the place of the dead.">d</a></sup>and like death they are never satisfied. They gather nations like possessions, swallowing up many peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> Won't all these peoples taunt them? They will ridicule them, saying, “What disaster is coming to you who pile up things that don't belong to you! You make yourselves rich by forcing debtors to pay! How long can you go on doing this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> Won't your debtors suddenly act? Won't they wake up to the situation and make you tremble? You will be plunder for them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> Because you have plundered many nations, those who are left will plunder you—for the human blood you have shed and the destruction you brought on lands and cities, and those who lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> What disaster is coming to you who build houses through dishonest gain! You think you can place your “nest” so high it will be safe from disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> Your evil schemes have brought shame upon your families, by destroying many nations you have forfeited your own lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> Even the stones in the wall cry out in condemnation, and the wooden rafters join in too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> What disaster is coming to you who build cities with bloodshed, who found cities built on wickedness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Hasn't the Lord Almighty decided that all the hard work of such nations is going to be burned up—that they wear themselves out for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; PSALMS 22:27; PSALMS 72:19"> For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the Lord's glory as the waters fill the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> What disaster is coming to you who make your neighbors drunk! You force your cup of anger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “poison.”">e</a></sup>on them and make them drink so you may see them naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> In turn you will be filled with shame instead of glory. Drink and expose your nakedness!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Expose your nakedness”: or “stagger”: Habbakuk pesher (commentary) from Qumran reading.">f</a></sup>The cup the Lord holds in his right hand will be passed round to you and your glory will turn to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; PSALMS 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> As you destroyed the forests of Lebanon you will also be destroyed; you hunted the animals there, and now they will hunt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “terrify.”">g</a></sup>you. For you shed human blood and you destroyed lands and cities, along with those who lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> What use is a wooden idol carved by human hands, or a metal image that teaches lies? What is the point of their makers trusting in their own handiwork, creating idols that can't speak?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> What disaster is coming to you who say to something made of wood, “Wake up!” or to lifeless stone, “Get up!” Can it teach you anything? Look at it! It's covered with gold and silver, but there is no life inside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; PSALMS 46:10"> But the Lord is in his holy Temple; let all the earth be silent in his presence.</span>
