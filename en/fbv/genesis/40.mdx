<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> Later on the cupbearer and the baker committed some offense against their master, the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; PSALMS 76:10"> Pharaoh was angry with these two royal officials—the chief cupbearer and chief baker—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> and imprisoned them in the house of the commander of the guard, the same prison where Joseph was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; PSALMS 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> The commander of the guard assigned Joseph to them as their personal attendant. They were kept in prison for some time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> One night while they were in prison the cupbearer and the baker for the king of Egypt each had a dream. They were different dreams, each with its own meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> When Joseph arrived the next morning he noticed they both looked depressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> So he asked Pharaoh's officials who were imprisoned with him in his master's house, “Why are you looking so depressed?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> “We've both had dreams but can't find anyone to explain what they mean,” they said.<br />So Joseph told them, “Isn't God the one who can interpret the meaning of dreams? Tell me your dreams.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> So the chief cupbearer told Joseph his dream. “In my dream there was a vine right in front of me,” he explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “The vine had three branches. As soon as it budded, it flowered, and produced clusters of ripe grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> I was holding Pharaoh's wine cup, so I picked the grapes and squeezed them into the cup and gave it to Pharaoh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> “This is the meaning,” Joseph told him. “The three branches represent three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> In three days' time Pharaoh will take you out of prison and give you back your job, and you will hand Pharaoh his cup as you used to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> But when things go well for you, please remember me with kindness and speak to Pharaoh on my behalf, and please get me out of this prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> I was cruelly kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews, and now I'm here in this pit even though I've done nothing wrong.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> When the chief baker saw that the interpretation was positive, he said to Joseph, “I also had a dream. I had three baskets of cakes on my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> In the top basket were all the cakes and pastries for Pharaoh to eat, and the birds were eating them from the basket on my head.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> “This is the meaning,” Joseph told him. “The three baskets represent three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> In three days' time Pharaoh will take you out of prison and hang you on a pole, and birds will eat your flesh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> Three days later it happened to be Pharaoh's birthday, and he arranged a banquet for all his officials. He had the chief cupbearer and the chief baker released from prison and brought there before his officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> He gave the chief cupbearer his job back, and he returned to his duties of handing Pharaoh his cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> But he hanged the chief baker just as Joseph had said when he interpreted their dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 31:12; Job 19:14; PSALMS 105:19"> But the chief cupbearer didn't remember to say anything about Joseph—in fact he forgot all about him.</span>
</div>
</div>
