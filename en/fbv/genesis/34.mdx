<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Dinah, Jacob and Leah's daughter, went to visit some of the local women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite, the ruler of that area, saw her. He grabbed hold of her and raped her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> However, then he fell deeply in love with Dinah and tried to get her to love him too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> He went and asked his father, “Get this young girl for me so I can marry her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> Jacob found out that Shechem had violated<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Violated”: the word here is the one used in connection with being unclean.">a</a></sup>his daughter Dinah, but as his sons were away looking after the flocks in the fields he didn't say anything until they came home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In the meantime Hamor, Shechem's father, arrived to talk with Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> When Jacob's sons returned from the fields they were very upset when they heard the news and became extremely angry because Shechem had done something outrageous in Israel by having sex with Jacob's daughter—something that should never be done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; PSALMS 119:20; PSALMS 84:2"> Hamor told them, “My son Shechem is very much in love with your daughter and your sister Dinah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“And sister Dinah”: supplied for clarity, since Hamor is addressing both Jacob and his sons.">b</a></sup>Please allow him to marry her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> In fact we can have more marriages—you can give us your daughters and you can have our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> You can live among us and settle down wherever you like. You can go where you want and buy land for yourselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Then Shechem himself spoke up, and said to Dinah's father and brothers, “Please accept me and my proposal, and I'll do whatever you ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> You can set the bride price as high as you like, and I'll pay it along with all the gifts I'll give. Just let me have the girl so I can marry her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> Jacob's sons weren't honest when they answered him and his father Hamor because Shechem had violated their sister Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> They told them, “We can't do this! We can't allow our sister to marry a man who's not circumcised. That would bring disgrace on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> We will only agree to it with this condition: all of you must be circumcised like us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then we will give you our daughters and take your daughters, and we will live among you and become one family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you don't agree with us that you should be circumcised, then we'll take our sister and leave.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Hamor and his son Shechem agreed to what was proposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> The young man Shechem didn't waste any time in arranging this because he was infatuated with Jacob's daughter, and he was viewed as the most important person in his father's family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> Hamor and Shechem went to the town gate and spoke to the other leaders there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are our friends,” they told them. “Let's have them live here in our country and allow them to go wherever they want—it's big enough for all of them too. We can take their daughters as wives, and we can give our daughters to them to marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But they will only agree to this on one condition: they will only join us and become one family if every male among us is circumcised like they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> If that happens, won't all their livestock and property—all their animals—end up belonging to us? We just have to agree to this and they will come and live among us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> Everyone there at the town gate agreed with Hamor and Shechem so every male in the town was circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> Three days later while they were still suffering pain, Simeon and Levi, two of Jacob's sons and Dinah's brothers, came with their swords into the town. Unopposed, they slaughtered every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and Shechem with their swords, took Dinah from Shechem's house, and left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> Jacob's other sons arrived, robbed the dead bodies, and looted the town where their sister had been violated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their sheep, goats, cattle, and donkeys. They took whatever was in the town, and in the fields—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> all their possessions. They captured all their women and children, and plundered everything in their homes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> But Jacob criticized Simeon and Levi, telling them, “You've just caused me a lot of trouble! You've made me like a bad smell among the people in this country, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I only have a few men, and if these people gather to attack me, I and my whole family will be wiped out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But they replied, “Should we have let him treat our sister like a prostitute?”</span>
</div>
</div>
