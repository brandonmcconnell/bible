<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> When it came back to him in the evening it had a freshly-picked olive leaf in its beak, so Noah knew the floodwaters were mainly gone from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> Again he waited another seven days and sent the dove out again, but this time it didn't return to him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Let all the animals go—the birds, the wild animals, the creatures that run along the ground—so that they can breed and increase their numbers on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 121:8"> So Noah and his wife, his sons and their wives, left the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All the animals, all the creatures that run along the ground, all the birds—everything that lives on land—also left, each kind leaving together.</span>
