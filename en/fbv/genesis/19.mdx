<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 18:1–5; Hebrews 13:2"> The two angels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Angels”: the account alternates between calling the two visitors “angels” and “men.”">a</a></sup>arrived at Sodom in the evening. Lot happened to be sitting at the entrance to Sodom, and when he saw them he stood up to meet them, and bowed low with his face to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Luke 24:28–29; Hebrews 13:2"> “Sirs, please come and stay with me for the night,” he said. “You can wash your feet and then be on your way early in the morning.”<br />They replied, “No, it's fine. We'll spend the night here in the square.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 6:19; 1 Samuel 28:24"> But Lot insisted, and they went with him to his house. He made them a meal and baked bread for them to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Proverbs 4:16; Exodus 23:2"> But they hadn't even gone to bed before the men of Sodom, young and old, from every part of town, came and surrounded the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Isaiah 3:9; Jude 1:7"> They shouted out to Lot, “Where are the men who came to stay with you tonight? Bring them out here to us so we can have sex with them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:23"> Lot went out to talk to them in the doorway, closing the door behind him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:13"> “My friends, please don't do such an evil thing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 19:31–38; Judges 9:15"> Listen, I've got two virgin daughters. Let me bring them out to you, and you can do to them whatever you want, but please don't do anything to these men. It's my responsibility to look after them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“It's my responsibility to look after them”: literally, “They have come under the protection of my roof.”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; 2 Peter 2:7–8; Genesis 13:12"> “Out of our way!” they shouted. “Who do you think you are, coming to live here and now trying to judge us? We'll do even worse things to you than we were going to do to these men!” They rushed forward at Lot and tried to break down the door.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But the men inside reached out and grabbed Lot, dragged him inside, and slammed the door shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:18; Acts 13:11; Deuteronomy 28:28–29"> Then they made all the men in the doorway, young and old, suddenly go blind so they couldn't find the door.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; 2 Peter 2:7; Revelation 18:4"> The two men asked Lot, “Is there anyone else here who's part of your family—sons-in-law, or sons or daughters, or anyone else in the town? If there are, make sure they leave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:20; Matthew 13:49–50; Jude 1:7"> because we are about to destroy this place. The complaints that have reached the Lord about its people have become so bad that he has sent us to destroy it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Jeremiah 51:6; Numbers 16:45"> Lot went immediately to speak to the men who were engaged to marry his daughters. “Get up quickly and leave,” he said, “because the Lord is about to destroy the town!” But they thought it was just a joke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:24–27; Revelation 18:4; Genesis 19:22"> At dawn, the angels begged Lot to be quick, telling him, “Hurry up! Leave right now with your wife and your two daughters here, otherwise you'll be wiped out when the city is punished.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:15–16; Titus 3:5; John 6:44"> But Lot hesitated. The men grabbed his hand, and those of his wife and two daughters, and dragged them along, leaving them outside the town. The Lord was kind to them to do this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:16–18; Luke 9:62; Philippians 3:13–14"> As soon as they were outside, one of the men said, “Run for your lives! Don't look back, and don't stop anywhere in the valley! Run to the mountains otherwise you'll be destroyed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:6–8; Acts 10:14; Genesis 32:26"> “Please sir, not that!” Lot replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:1; PSALMS 103:1–22; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> “If you don't mind, since you have already been so kind to me by saving my life, don't make me run to the mountains—I just can't make it. The destruction will overtake me and I'll die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 119:175; Proverbs 3:5–7; Genesis 19:30"> Look, there's a town nearby that's close enough to run to, and it's so small. Please let me run there—it's really very small. It would save my life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 145:19; PSALMS 34:15; PSALMS 102:17"> “Fine—I'll do as you ask,” he replied. “I won't destroy this town you've mentioned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 14:2; Genesis 32:25–28"> But hurry up and run there quickly, because I can't do anything until you get there.” (This is why the town was called Zoar.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Zoar means “little place.” Originally it was called Bela (see 14:2).">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The sun had already risen by the time Lot reached Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> Then the Lord rained down fire and burning sulfur from the sky on Sodom and Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10"> He completely destroyed the towns and all their inhabitants, the whole valley and everything growing there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 17:31–32; Genesis 19:17; Proverbs 14:14"> But Lot's wife, who was lagging behind, looked back, and she turned into a pillar of salt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1; PSALMS 5:3; Hebrews 2:1"> Abraham got up early the next morning and went back to where he had stood before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 18:9; Revelation 9:2; Revelation 21:8"> He looked down at Sodom and Gomorrah and the whole valley floor, and saw the land burning, sending up smoke like from a furnace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Deuteronomy 9:5; Genesis 18:23–33"> When God destroyed the towns of the valley he didn't forget the promise he made Abraham, and he saved Lot from the destruction of the towns where Lot was living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Isaiah 15:5; James 1:8"> Lot was afraid to stay in Zoar, so he left town and went to live in a cave in the mountains with his two daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Isaiah 4:1; Genesis 16:2"> Sometime later the older daughter said to the younger one, “Our father is growing old, and there's no men left to give us children like everyone does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6–7; Genesis 11:3; Genesis 9:21"> Come on, let's get our father drunk with wine and sleep with him so we can keep his family line going.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1"> So they got their father drunk with wine that night. The older daughter went and slept with him, and he didn't notice when she lay down or when she got up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Jeremiah 6:15; Isaiah 3:9"> The next day the older daughter said to the younger one, “Last night I slept with our father. Let's get him drunk with wine again tonight and you can go and sleep with him so we can keep his family line going.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; PSALMS 8:4; 1 Peter 4:7"> So once again that night they got their father drunk with wine and the younger daughter went and slept with him, and he didn't notice when she lay down or when she got up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:15; Judges 1:7; Matthew 7:2"> This is how both Lot's daughters became pregnant by their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; 2 Kings 3:1–27; Numbers 21:29"> The older daughter had a son, and she called him Moab.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Moab”: understood to mean “son of my father.”">d</a></sup>He is the ancestor of the Moabites of today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Judges 10:6–40; PSALMS 83:4–8"> The younger daughter had a son too, and she called him Ben-ammi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Ben-ammi”: “son of my people.”">e</a></sup>He is the ancestor of the Ammonites of today.</span>
</div>
