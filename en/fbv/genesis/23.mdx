<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:17"> Sarah lived to be 127,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 23:19; Genesis 13:18; 1 Chronicles 6:57"> and then she died at Kiriath-arba (or Hebron) in the land of Canaan. Abraham went in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Went in”: possibly into the tent where the body lay.">a</a></sup>to mourn her death and to weep over her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 10:15; Genesis 23:7; 1 Samuel 26:6"> Then Abraham got up from beside his wife's body and went to talk with the leaders of the Hittites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 17:8"> “I am a foreigner, a stranger living among you,” he said. “Please let me buy a burial site so I can bury my dead wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The Hittites answered Abraham, telling him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 14:14; Exodus 32:22"> “Listen, my lord, you are a highly-respected prince among us. Choose the very best of our burial sites to bury your dead. None of us will say no to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> Abraham got up and bowed low before the Hittites, the local people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> and said to them, “If you agree to help me bury my dead, listen to my proposal. Could you please ask Ephron, son of Zohar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> to sell me the cave of Machpelah that belongs to him, down at the end of his field. I'm willing to pay him the full price here in your presence so I can have my own burial site.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> Ephron the Hittite was sitting there among his people. He replied to Abraham in the presence of the Hittites who were there at the town gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; Deuteronomy 19:15"> “No, my lord,” he said. “Please listen to me. I give you the field and the cave that is there. I give it to you and my people are my witnesses. Please go and bury your dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> Abraham bowed low before the local people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> and said to Ephron so everyone could hear, “Please listen to me. I will pay the price for the field. Take the money and let me go and bury my dead there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Ephron replied to Abraham, telling him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:12; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 30:15"> “My lord, please listen to me. The land is worth four hundred pieces of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Four hundred pieces of silver”: it is generally agreed that this was an exorbitant amount.">b</a></sup>But what's that between us? Go and bury your dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> Abraham accepted Ephron's offer. Abraham weighed out and gave to Ephron the four hundred pieces of silver he'd mentioned, using the standard weights used by merchants, and with the Hittites acting as witnesses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 23:20"> So the property was legally transferred. It comprised Ephron's field in Machpelah near Mamre, both the field and the cave there, as well as all the trees in the field, and all the area up to the existing boundaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Jeremiah 32:12; Genesis 34:20"> This all became Abraham's property, and the transaction was witnessed by the Hittites who were there at the town gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> Then Abraham went and buried Sarah his wife in the cave in the field at Machpelah near Mamre (or Hebron) in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> Ownership of the field and the cave there was transferred from the Hittites to Abraham to serve as his burial place.</span>
</div>
</div>
