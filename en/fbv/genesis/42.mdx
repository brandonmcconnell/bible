<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:12; Joshua 7:10; 2 Kings 8:3–4"> When Jacob found out grain was available in Egypt, he asked his sons, “Why do you keep on looking at each other to do something?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 43:8; Genesis 45:9; Genesis 43:2"> I've heard there's grain in Egypt. Go there and buy some for us so we can stay alive—if not, we're going to die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:5; Genesis 42:13"> So ten of Joseph's brothers went to Egypt to buy grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 11:4"> But Jacob did not send Joseph's brother Benjamin with his other brothers, for he said, “I'm afraid something bad might happen to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:57; Acts 7:11; Genesis 12:10"> So Israel's sons went to buy grain along with everyone else, because there was famine in Canaan too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 37:7–10; Genesis 41:40–41"> Joseph was the governor of the country and he sold grain to all the people there. So Joseph's brothers went to him, and bowed low before him with their faces to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 42:30; Genesis 42:14–17; Matthew 15:23–26"> Joseph recognized them as soon as he saw them, but he acted like a stranger towards them and spoke to them in a severe way, saying, “Where are you from?” “From the country of Canaan,” they replied. “We've come to buy food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 37:2; John 20:14; Luke 24:16"> Even though Joseph recognized his brothers, they didn't recognize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 37:5–9; Exodus 32:35; Joshua 2:1"> Joseph thought back to the dreams he'd had about them, and told them, “No! You're spies! You've come to discover our country's weaknesses!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 37:8; 1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 27:37"> “That's not true, my lord!” they responded. “We, your servants, have just come to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 42:19; 2 Corinthians 6:4; John 7:18"> We're all the sons of one man and we're honest. We're not spies!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “No! You've come to find our country's weaknesses!” he insisted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:30; Genesis 42:32; Genesis 44:20"> “Your servants are twelve brothers, the sons of one man living in the country of Canaan,” they explained. “The youngest is right now with our father, and one has passed away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Job 13:24; Genesis 42:9–11"> “As I said before, you're spies!” Joseph declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; Jeremiah 5:7; Genesis 42:16"> “This is how your story will be checked. I swear on Pharaoh's life that you'll never leave this country unless your younger brother comes here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> One of you go back and bring your other brother here. The others of you will be kept here in prison until it's clear that you're telling the truth. If not, then I swear on Pharaoh's life it proves you're spies!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Hebrews 12:10; Genesis 40:7"> So Joseph put all of them in prison for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:43; Nehemiah 5:15; Luke 18:2"> On the third day he told them, “Since I'm someone who respects God, do as I tell you and you'll live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 45:23; Isaiah 42:7; Genesis 40:3"> If you're truly honest, choose one of your brothers to stay here in prison. The rest of you can go back home with grain for your hungry families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; Genesis 42:34"> But you must bring your youngest brother here to me to prove what you're saying is true. If not, you will all die.” They agreed to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Mark 9:43–45; Genesis 37:23–28"> “Clearly we're being punished for what we did to our brother,” they said to each other. “We watched him in agony pleading with us for mercy, but we refused to listen to him. That's why we're in all this trouble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:22; PSALMS 9:12; 1 Kings 2:32"> Reuben said to them, “Didn't I tell you, ‘Don't harm the boy!’ But you didn't listen to me. Now we're paying the price for what we did to him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “Now his blood is required.” The concept is that the blood of the victim cries out for vengeance.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 16:13–14"> They didn't realize that Joseph understood what they were saying because they were talking to him through an interpreter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 43:30; Jude 1:22–23; Hebrews 4:15"> Joseph stepped away from them because he started crying. He came back when he was able to speak to them again. He chose Simeon and had him tied up as they watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–21; Genesis 45:21"> Joseph gave the order to fill up their sacks with grain, and also to return the money they had paid by placing it in the sacks as well. He also ordered that they should be provided with food for their journey home. All this was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The brothers loaded the grain onto their donkeys and then set off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Luke 2:7; Luke 10:34"> On their way they stopped for the night, and one of them opened up his sack to give his donkey something to eat and saw his money there at the top of the sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:37; Genesis 42:36"> He told his brothers, “My money's been returned to me. It's right here at the top of my sack!” They were horrified! Trembling with fear they asked each other, “What is this that God's done to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> When they arrived home in Canaan, they told their father Jacob everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:7"> “The man who is the country's governor spoke to us in a severe way, and accused us of spying on the land,” they explained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> “We told him, ‘We are honest men. We're not spies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We are twelve brothers, the sons of one father. One has passed away and the youngest is right now with our father in the country of Canaan.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 42:19–20; Genesis 42:15"> Then the man who is the country's governor said to us, ‘This is how I'll find out if you're telling the truth: you are to leave one of your brothers here with me while the rest take grain home for your hungry families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 34:21; 1 Kings 10:15"> Then bring your youngest brother to me. That way I'll know you're not spies but you're telling the truth. I'll release your brother to you, and you can stay in the country and trade.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 43:21; Genesis 43:12; Genesis 43:15"> As they emptied their sacks, each one's money bag was there in his sack! When they and their father saw the money bags, they were horrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 27:9"> Jacob their father accused them, “You have taken Joseph from me—he's gone! Simeon is gone too! Now you want to take Benjamin away! I'm the one who's suffering from all of this!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The sentence is literally, “on me are all these things.” The Hebrew construction focuses “on me” making it clear that Jacob is holding them responsible for his suffering.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Micah 6:7; Genesis 43:9"> “You can kill my two sons if I don't bring him back to you,” Reuben assured him. “Trust me with him, and I will bring him home to you myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Genesis 42:4; Genesis 37:33"> “My son won't go there with you!” Jacob declared. “His brother is dead, and he's the only one I have left. If anything bad happens to him on the journey you're planning, you'll send this old man to his grave in grief.”</span>
