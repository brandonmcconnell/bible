<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then God told Noah and his sons who were there with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> “Listen, I'm making my agreement with you and your descendants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; PSALMS 36:5–6"> and with all the animals around you—the birds, the livestock, and all the wild animals of the earth—every animal that accompanied you on the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> In my agreement I'm promising you that I won't ever again destroy all life by means of a flood—there won't be a destructive flood like this again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> Then God said, “I'm going to give you a sign to confirm the agreement I'm making between me and you and all living creatures, an agreement that will last for all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> I've placed my rainbow in the clouds, and this will be the sign of my agreement with you and with all life on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whenever I make clouds form over the earth and the rainbow appears,</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> I will see the rainbow in the clouds and it will remind me of the eternal agreement between God and every kind of living creature that lives on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then God told Noah, “This is the sign of the agreement I'm making between me and every creature on earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> Noah's sons who left the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. (Ham was the father of the Canaanites.)</span>

He will be the lowest kind of slave and will serve his brothers!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> Then Noah continued, “May the Lord be blessed, the God of Shem, and may Canaan be his slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> May God give Japtheth plenty of space to accommodate his many descendants, and may they live at peace among Shem's people, and may Canaan also be his slave.”</span>

Now both the plants and the animals themselves are permitted as human food. After the flood there would have been little food immediately available to eat.</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">9:25</span> Why Canaan is the one cursed and not Ham has long been a matter of debate.
