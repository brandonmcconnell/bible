<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 8:20; Leviticus 10:10; Leviticus 11:1–47"> Take with you seven pairs, male and female, of every kind of clean animal, and one pair, male and female, of every kind of unclean animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In addition take seven pairs, male and female, of all the birds, so their different kinds will survive throughout the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> In seven days I'm going to make it rain for forty days and nights. I'm going to wipe out from the surface of the earth all the living creatures I made.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> Noah did exactly what the Lord ordered him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> Noah was 600 when the flood waters covered the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> Noah went into the ark, taking with him his wife and his sons and their wives, because of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Clean and unclean animals, birds, and creatures that run along the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> went into the ark with Noah. They came in pairs, male and female, just as God had told Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> After seven days the floodwaters swept over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> Noah was 600 when on the seventeenth day of the second month all the subterranean waters burst through the earth, and heavy rain poured down from the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> Rain continued to fall on the earth for forty days and nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> That was the actual day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Actual day”: referring back to the day mentioned in verse 11.">a</a></sup>when Noah, his wife, and their sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth together with their three wives went into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> They had with them every kind of wild animals, livestock, creatures that run along the ground, and birds—everything with wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> They all came into the ark with Noah in pairs—every living thing that breathes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; PSALMS 91:1–10"> A male and a female of every creature entered, as God had told Noah. Then the Lord shut the door behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> The flood increased for forty days, lifting the ark so that it floated up from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; PSALMS 69:15; Job 22:16"> The floodwaters surged and grew deeper and deeper over the earth, but the ark floated along on the surface.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 104:6–9; PSALMS 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> Finally the water grew so deep that even the highest mountains were covered—all that could be seen was sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; PSALMS 104:6"> The water rose so much that it was higher than the mountains by fifteen cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> Everything living on earth died—the birds, livestock, wild animals, all creatures that run along the ground, and all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> Everything on land that breathed, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> The Lord wiped out all life on earth—people, livestock, creatures that run along the ground, and birds. All were killed. The only ones left were Noah and those with him on the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> The earth remained flooded for 150 days.</span>
