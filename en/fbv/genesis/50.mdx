<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 46:4; Deuteronomy 6:7–8; Acts 8:2"> Joseph went and hugged his father, weeping over him and kissing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; 2 Chronicles 16:14; John 19:39–40"> Then Joseph instructed the physicians who worked for him to embalm his father's body. So the physicians embalmed Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8; Genesis 50:10"> This took a full 40 days, the normal time for the process, and the Egyptians mourned for him for 70 days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:3; Esther 4:2"> Once the time of mourning was over, Joseph said to Pharaoh's officials, “If you'd be so kind, please speak to Pharaoh on my behalf, and explain to him that</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; Matthew 27:60"> my father made me swear an oath, telling me, ‘You must bury me in the tomb I've prepared for myself in Canaan. Please allow me to go and bury my father and then I'll return.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 48:21"> Pharaoh replied, “Go and bury your father as he made you swear to do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> Joseph went to bury his father, and all Pharaoh's officials went with him—all Pharaoh's senior advisors and all the leaders of Egypt—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:24–27; Exodus 10:26; Exodus 10:8–9"> as well as Joseph's family, his brothers, and his father's family. They only left the small children and their flocks and herds back in Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; 2 Kings 18:24; Song of Songs 1:9"> They were accompanied by chariots and horsemen—a really large procession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 2:13; Acts 8:2; 2 Samuel 1:17"> When they got to the threshing floor of Atad, on the other side of the Jordan, they wept loudly in sorrow. Joseph held a seven-day ceremony of mourning for his father there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Genesis 24:6; Deuteronomy 11:30"> The Canaanites who lived there watched the ceremony of mourning at the threshing floor of Atad. They said, “This is a very sad time of mourning for the Egyptians,” so they renamed the place Abel-mizraim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Abel-mizraim”: meaning “mourning of the Egyptians.”">a</a></sup>which is on the other side of the Jordan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:16; Genesis 49:29–32; Ephesians 6:1"> Jacob's sons did what he had instructed them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–31; Genesis 23:16–18; Acts 7:16"> They carried his body to Canaan and buried him in the cave at Machpelah in the field near Mamre, which Abraham had bought from Ephron the Hittite as a burial site.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> After they had buried their father, Joseph and his brothers returned to Egypt along with all those who had gone with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Genesis 42:21–22; PSALMS 53:5"> However, now that their father was dead, Joseph's brothers became worried, saying, “Maybe Joseph is holding a grudge against us, and he'll pay us back for all the bad things we did to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25"> So they sent a message to Joseph to tell him, “Before your father died, he gave this order:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Matthew 6:14–15; Genesis 45:8"> ‘This is what you are to tell Joseph: Forgive your brothers their sins, the bad things they did to you, treating you in such a nasty way.’ Now please forgive us our sins, we who are servants of the God of your father.” When Joseph received their message, he cried.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:7–11; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 45:3"> Then his brothers themselves came and fell down before Joseph and said, “We are your slaves!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 45:5; Genesis 30:2"> “You don't need to be afraid!” he told them. “I don't stand in the place of God, do I?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:28; PSALMS 119:71; Genesis 45:5–8"> While you planned bad things for me, God planned it for good so that in the end many lives could be saved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 45:5, 45:7.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; 1 Peter 3:9; Genesis 45:10–11"> So don't worry. I'll go on taking care of you and your children.” Speaking kindly like this he calmed them down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Joseph remained in Egypt, together with his father's whole family. He lived to be 110,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:39; Genesis 30:3; Job 42:16"> and saw three generations of his son Ephraim, and the sons of Makir, Manasseh's son, were placed in his lap when they were born.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 3:16–17; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 26:3"> “I'm going to die soon,” Joseph told his brothers, “but God will be with you, and he will lead you out of this country to the land that he swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Joshua 24:32; Genesis 47:29–31"> Joseph made the sons of Israel swear an oath, saying, “When God comes to be with you, you must take my bones with you when you leave.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“When you leave”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 50:2–3; Genesis 47:9; Joshua 24:29"> Joseph died when he was 110. After his body was embalmed, he was placed in a coffin in Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>
