<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> But he cried so loudly that the Egyptians could hear him, and they told Pharaoh's household about it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> “I'm Joseph!” he announced to his brothers. “Is my father still alive?” They couldn't answer him as they were so surprised to see him face to face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> “Please come closer to me,” he told his brothers. They came over to him. “I'm your brother Joseph who you sold into slavery in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> But don't be worried or get angry with yourselves that you sold me to be a slave here, because it was God who sent me ahead of you to save lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> The country has suffered from famine for two years already, but there will be five more years with no plowing or reaping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 44:4; Acts 7:35; PSALMS 18:50"> God sent me ahead of you to make sure you would still have descendants, to save your lives in this miraculous way.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“In this miraculous way”: or “with many survivors.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> So it wasn't you who sent me here, but God. He was the one who made me Pharaoh's chief advisor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Pharaoh's chief advisor”: literally, “a father to Pharaoh.”">b</a></sup>in charge of all his affairs, and ruler of the whole country of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> Now hurry! Go back to my father and tell him, ‘This message comes from your son Joseph: God has made me the ruler of the whole of Egypt. Come to me now; don't wait.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> You'll live in the land of Goshen so you can be close to me with your children and grandchildren, and with your flocks and herds and everything that belongs to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> I'll make sure you have food, because there are still five more years of famine to come. Otherwise you and your family and your animals are going to starve.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> Then Joseph told his brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Then Joseph told his brothers”: supplied for clarity to show that Joseph is now addressing his brothers directly again.">c</a></sup>“You can all see for yourselves—including my brother Benjamin—that it's really me talking to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> Tell my father how much I'm respected in Egypt. Tell him everything that you've seen. Hurry! Bring my father here quickly!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> He hugged Benjamin, and Benjamin hugged him back. They both wept for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; PSALMS 77:4"> He kissed all his brothers and wept over them, and after that his brothers were able to start talking with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> Word soon reached Pharaoh's palace that Joseph's brothers had arrived. Pharaoh and his officials were glad to hear the news.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Tell your brothers, ‘This is what you are to do: Load your donkeys with grain and go back to the country of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> Then bring your father and your families and return here to me. I will give you the best land in Egypt, and you shall eat the best food the country has to offer.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> Tell them to do this as well: ‘Take some wagons from Egypt for your children and your wives. Bring them and your father here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Don't worry about bringing your possessions because the best of all Egypt is yours.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> So that's what the sons of Israel did. Joseph provided them with wagons, as Pharaoh had ordered. He also gave them supplies for their journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> He gave each of them new clothes. But to Benjamin he gave five sets of clothes and 300 pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> Joseph also sent to his father the following: ten donkeys carrying the best things from Egypt, and ten female donkeys carrying grain and bread and supplies needed for his father's journey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; PSALMS 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> Then he saw his brothers off, and as they left he told them, “Don't argue on the way!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So they left Egypt and went back home to their father Jacob in the country of Canaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> “Joseph's still alive!” they told him. “It's true, and he's the ruler of the whole country of Egypt!” Jacob was stunned at the news—he just couldn't believe it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> But when they told him everything that Joseph had said to them, and when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to take him to Egypt, Jacob came back to his senses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> Israel said, “All right, I believe you! My son Joseph is still alive! I'm going to go and see him before I die.”</span>
</div>
