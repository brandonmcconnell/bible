</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Hosea 6:2"> After traveling for three days Abraham could see the place in the distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> He told his servants, “Wait here with the donkey while I go with the boy and worship God. Then we'll return.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> Abraham had Isaac carry the wood for the burnt offering, while he carried the fire and the knife, and they walked up together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> Isaac said to Abraham, “Father?”<br />“Yes, my son?” Abraham replied.<br />“Well, we have the fire and the wood, but where's the lamb for the burnt offering?” Isaac asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> “God will provide the lamb for the burnt offering, my son,” Abraham replied, and they went on walking up together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> When they arrived at the place where God had told him to go, Abraham built an altar and placed the wood on it. Then he bound his son Isaac and placed him on the altar on top of the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> Abraham picked up the knife, ready to slaughter his son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> But the angel of the Lord shouted to him from heaven, “Abraham! Abraham!”<br />“Yes, I'm here,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; 1 Samuel 15:22"> The angel said, “Don't touch the boy! Don't do anything to him, because now I know that you truly do what God tells you. You didn't refuse to give me your son, your only son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; PSALMS 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> Abraham looked up and saw a ram caught by its horns in some bushes. He brought the ram over and sacrificed it as a burnt offering in place of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> Abraham called the place “The Lord will Provide.” That's still a saying people use today: “the Lord will provide on his mountain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The angel of the Lord shouted again to Abraham from heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; PSALMS 105:9"> “I swear by myself, says the Lord, that because you have done this and didn't refuse to give me your son, your only son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremiah 33:22"> you can be sure that I will bless you and give you many descendants. They will be as numerous as the stars of heaven and the sand of the seashore, and they will conquer their enemies.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Conquer their enemies”: literally, “take possession of the gates of their enemies.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> All the nations of the earth will be blessed by your descendants because you did what I told you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> Then Abraham returned to his servants, and they went back together to Beersheba where Abraham was living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> Sometime later, Abraham was told, “Milcah has had sons for your brother Nahor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; PSALMS 60:1–12"> Uz was the firstborn, then his brother Buz, Kemuel (who became the ancestor of Arameans),</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> (Bethuel was Rebekah's father.) Milcah had these eight sons for Abraham's brother Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:3; Genesis 25:6; Proverbs 15:25"> In addition, Reumah his concubine had Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.</span>
</div>
