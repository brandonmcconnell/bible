<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 13:18"> The Lord appeared to Abraham at the oaks of Mamre. Abraham was sitting at the entrance to his tent as the day became really hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> He looked up and all of a sudden he saw three men standing there. As soon as he saw them, he ran over to meet them and bowed low to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 32:5"> He said, “Sir,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It seems Abraham was addressing just one of them, perhaps seeing him as their leader.">a</a></sup>if you please, don't continue your journey without spending some time here with me, your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 24:32; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 43:24"> Let me have some water brought so you can wash your feet and rest under the tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; Judges 13:15; Judges 6:18"> Also let me bring some food so you can get your strength back before you go on your way, now that you've come to visit me here.”<br />“That would be fine,” they answered. “Please do as you've suggested.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> Abraham hurried back to the tent and told Sarah, “Quick! Make some bread using three large measures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Large measures”: literally “seahs,” variously estimated as 20 quarts or 44 pounds.">b</a></sup>of the best flour. Knead the dough and bake the bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> Then Abraham ran to the cattle herd and chose a good, young calf and gave it to his servant who quickly killed and cooked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> Then he took some yogurt, milk, and the cooked meat, and placed the food in front of them. He stood nearby under a tree while they ate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> “Where is your wife Sarah?” they asked him.<br />“Over there, inside the tent,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> Then one said, “I promise you that I will come back to visit you about this time next year, and your wife Sarah will have a son.” Sarah was listening, hiding just inside the entrance to the tent behind him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> Abraham and Sarah were old, getting on in years. Sarah was well past the age of having children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> Sarah was laughing inside, saying to herself, “Now that I'm old and worn out, how would I experience pleasure? My husband is old too!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> The Lord asked Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh and ask, ‘How could it possibly be true that I'll have a child now I'm so old?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> Is anything too difficult for the Lord to do? I will come back next year when I said I would, in the spring, and Sarah will have a son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> Sarah denied it because she was afraid, claiming, “I didn't laugh.”<br />“Yes, you did laugh,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> Then the men left. They looked down on Sodom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Clearly they could see Sodom in the valley below from their viewpoint higher up.">c</a></sup>and headed in that direction. Abraham accompanied them part of the way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; PSALMS 25:14; John 15:15"> Then the Lord said, “Should I keep from Abraham what I'm going to do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> Abraham will definitely become a great and powerful nation, and all the nations on earth will be blessed through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> I've chosen him so that he will teach his sons and their families to follow the way of the Lord by doing what is right and good, so that I, the Lord, can do for Abraham what I promised.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> Then the Lord said, “There are many complaints made against Sodom and Gomorrah because they sin so blatantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I'm going to see if these complaints that have reached me are completely true. I'll know if they're not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> The two men turned and went towards Sodom, but the Lord stayed there with Abraham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> Abraham came to him and asked: “Are you really going to wipe out the good people along with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> What if there are fifty good people in the town? Are you still going to wipe it out despite the fifty good people there?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> No, you can't do something like that! You can't kill the good with the wicked, otherwise you would be treating the good and the wicked in the same way. You can't do that! Isn't the Judge of all the earth going to do the right thing?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> “If I find fifty good people in Sodom, I'll spare the whole town because of them,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> “Since I've started, let me go on speaking to my Lord, even though I'm nothing but dust and ashes,” Abraham continued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> “What if there are forty-five good people, just five less? Are you still going to wipe out the whole town just because there are five fewer people?”<br />“I won't destroy it if I find forty-five,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> Abraham spoke up again and he asked the Lord, “What if only forty can be found?”<br />“I won't do it for the sake of the forty,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 89:7; PSALMS 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> “My Lord, please don't get angry with me,” Abraham went on. “Let me ask this—what if only thirty were found?”<br />“I won't do it if I find thirty,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> “I admit I've been very bold to speak to my Lord like this,” Abraham said. “What if only twenty are found there?”<br />“I won't do it for the sake of the twenty,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> “Please don't get angry with me, my Lord,” Abraham said. “Just let me ask one more thing. What if only ten are found there?”<br />“I won't destroy it for the sake of the ten,” the Lord replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> The Lord left once he had finished speaking with Abraham, and Abraham went home.</span>
</div>
</div>
