<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; PSALMS 35:24; PSALMS 43:1"> Rachel said, “God has judged in my favor! He listened to me and gave me a son.” So she named him Dan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Dan means “judge.”">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> Rachel said, “I've had a hard struggle with my sister, but I've won.” So she named him Naphtali.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Naphtali means “struggle.”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> Leah realized she wasn't having any more children, so she gave her personal maid Zilpah to Jacob as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Zilpah had a son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> Leah said, “I'm really fortunate!” So she named him Gad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gad means “fortunate.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Leah's personal maid Zilpah became pregnant again and had a second son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Songs 6:9"> Leah said, “I'm so happy, and the other women will say I'm happy too!” So she named him Asher.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Asher means “happy.”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> At the time of the wheat harvest Reuben found some mandrake plants when he was out in the fields. He took them back to his mother Leah. Rachel asked Leah, “Please give me some of the mandrakes your son found.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> “Aren't you satisfied with stealing my husband?” Leah replied. “Are you going to take my son's mandrakes too?”<br />“Fine, he can sleep with you tonight if you give me some mandrakes in return,” Rachel responded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When Jacob came in from the fields that evening, Leah went out to meet him. “You have to sleep with me because I've paid for you with my son's mandrakes,” she told him. So he slept with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> God heard Leah's request, and she became pregnant and had a fifth son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> Leah said, “The Lord has rewarded me for giving my personal maid to my husband.” So she named him Issachar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Issachar means “reward.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then Leah became pregnant again and had a sixth son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> Leah said, “God has given me a good gift. Now my husband will honor me because I've given him six sons.” So she named him Zebulun.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Zebulun is related to both the words for “gift” and “honor.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> Later she had a daughter she named Dinah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; PSALMS 113:9; PSALMS 127:3"> Then God paid attention to Rachel and listened to her appeals, and helped her to have children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> She became pregnant and had a son. “God has removed my disgrace,” she said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> She named him Joseph,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Joseph may mean both “may he add,” and “he takes away,” referring to Rachel's “disgrace.”">g</a></sup>saying, “May the Lord give me an additional son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> Once Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, “Let me leave so I can return to my home and my own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Give me my wives and children because I worked for you to have them. Let me go now because you know very well how much work I've done for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> “Please be so kind as to stay,” Laban replied, “because I have discovered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Discovered”: or “learned by divination.”">h</a></sup>that the Lord has blessed me because of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> Then Laban continued, “Tell me how much to pay you and I'll give it to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> “You certainly know how much work I've done for you, and how well your flocks have done under my care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> You hardly had anything before I arrived, but now you have so much! The Lord has blessed you through what I've done. When am I going to be able to provide for my own family?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; PSALMS 118:8"> “Well, what do you propose I give you?” Laban asked again.<br />“You don't have to give me anything,” Jacob replied. “If you want to do something for me, then how about this: I'll go on looking after your flocks, making sure they're fed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> Let me go through your flocks today and take all the sheep that are speckled or spotted, and all the dark ones, as well as all the speckled and spotted goats. They'll be my wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> In the future you'll be able to prove that I've been honest. When you check my flock, any goats that don't have speckles or spots, or any sheep that aren't dark will be considered stolen from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> “Very good,” Laban agreed. “We'll do as you say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> However, the same day Laban went and removed all the striped and spotted male goats, all the speckled and spotted female goats, and all the dark sheep. He had his sons look after them and sent them away—</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> a three day journey between them and Jacob, while Jacob was looking after the rest of Laban's flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> Then Jacob cut some sticks from poplar, almond, and plane trees that had white wood under the bark. He peeled off some of the bark, making the sticks look streaked with white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> He put the sticks he'd peeled in the water troughs where the flocks came to drink because that's where they mated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> The flocks mated in front of the sticks and gave birth to young that were streaked, speckled, and spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jacob separated all these ones out. Then he made his flock face towards those in Laban's flock that were streaked and dark. This is the way he kept his flocks apart from Laban's flock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> When the strong females were ready to breed, Jacob put the sticks in the troughs where the flocks could see them when they mated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> He didn't do this for the weaker females. The weaker ones went to Laban, and the strong ones went to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> In this way Jacob became an extremely rich man with large flocks, and many male and female slaves, camels, and donkeys.</span>
</div>
