<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> Around this time, Judah left his brothers and set up his tents at Adullam, near to a local man named Hirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> There Judah happened to see the daughter of a Canaanite man named Shua and married her. He slept with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> and she became pregnant and had a son he named Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> She became pregnant again and had a son she named Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> Then she had another son she named Shelah who was born in Kezib.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> Much later, Judah arranged for Er, his firstborn son, to marry a woman named Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> But Er did what was evil in the Lord's sight, so the Lord put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Judah told Onan, “Go and sleep with your brother's wife to fulfill the requirements of a brother-in-law to have children on behalf of your brother.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> Onan realized that any children he had wouldn't be his own, so whenever he slept with his brother's wife he made sure she wouldn't become pregnant by withdrawing and spilling his semen on the ground. In this way he prevented any children being born on behalf of his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> But what he did was evil in the Lord's sight, so he also put Onan to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> Then Judah told his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Go to your father's house and live there as a widow until my son Shelah grows up.” For he thought, “Maybe he'll die too, just like his brothers.” So Tamar went and stayed in her father's house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> A long time later Judah's wife, the daughter of Shua, died. After Judah had finished the time of mourning, he went to visit his sheepshearers at Timnah with his friend Hirah from Adullam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Tamar was told, “Your father-in-law is going to Timnah to shear his sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> So she took off her widow's clothes and covered herself with a veil, disguising herself. She sat down beside the entrance to Enaim, which is on the way to Timnah. She had realized that even though Shelah had now grown up, nothing had been done about her marrying him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Judah saw her and thought she must be a prostitute because she had veiled her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> He went over to her at the side of the road and said, “I want to sleep with you.” He didn't know she was his daughter-in-law.<br />“What will you give me if I let you sleep with me?” she asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> “I'll send you a young goat from my flock,” he replied.<br />“What guarantee will you give me to make sure you'll send it?” she asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> “What guarantee do I have to give you?” he asked.<br />“Your signet seal and its cord, and your walking stick that you're holding,” she replied. He handed them over to her. He slept with her and she became pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> She left, went home, took off her veil, and put on her widow's clothes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Judah sent his friend Hirah from Adullam with a young goat to get back his belongings he'd left as a guarantee from the woman, but he couldn't find her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Hirah asked the men there, “Where's the cult prostitute that sits by the entrance road to Enaim?”<br />“There's no cult prostitute here,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Hirah went back to Judah and told him, “I couldn't find her, and the men there said, ‘There's no cult prostitute here.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> “Let her keep what I gave her,” Judah replied. “We'll look ridiculous to people if we go on searching. In any case I did try to send her the young goat as promised but you couldn't find her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> Then about three months later Judah was told, “Tamar your daughter-in-law has had sex like a prostitute and now as a result she's pregnant!”<br />“Bring her out and burn her to death!” Judah ordered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> As she was brought out, she sent a message to her father-in-law, saying, “I'm pregnant by the man who owns these things.” Then she added, “Please look carefully at this signet seal and its cord and walking stick. Who do they belong to?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> Judah recognized them right away and said, “She has honored the law more than I have, because I didn't give her in marriage to my son Shelah.” He didn't sleep with Tamar again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> When the time came for Tamar to give birth, she was found to be carrying twins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> One baby put out his hand, and the midwife tied a scarlet thread around his wrist and said, “This one came out first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> But then he pulled back his hand and his brother was born, she said, “How did you burst out?” So he was named Perez.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Perez” means “burst out.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> Then his brother with the scarlet thread on his wrist was born. He was named Zerah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Zerah” means “rising” (as in “sun”) with perhaps the implication of red color.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
