<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; PSALMS 105:8–11"> I will make my agreement between me and you, and I will give you many, many descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> Abram bowed down with his face to the ground, and God told him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> “Listen! This is the agreement I'm making with you. You will be the father of many nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> so your name won't be Abram any longer. Instead, your name will be Abraham<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The name change is usually interpreted to be from Abram (“exalted father”) to Abraham (“father of many”).">b</a></sup>because I'm going to make you the father of many nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> I will make sure you have a large number of descendants. They will become many nations, and some of their kings will also come from your line.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> I promise to continue my agreement with you, and with your descendants who come after you, for generations to come. This is an eternal agreement. I will always be your God, and the God of your descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; PSALMS 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> I will give you and your descendants the whole country of Canaan—where you've been living as a foreigner—as land to own forever, and I will be their God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; PSALMS 25:10; PSALMS 103:18"> Then God told Abraham, “Your part is to keep my agreement—you and your descendants for generations to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> This is my agreement with you and your descendants who come after you, the agreement you are to keep: every male among you shall be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> You are to circumcise your foreskin, and this will be the sign of the agreement between me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> From now and for all generations to come every male among you who is eight days old must be circumcised. This includes not only your sons but also those born in your household or bought from foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> You must circumcise those born in your household or bought from foreigners as a sign in your bodies of my eternal agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> Any uncircumcised male who refuses circumcision will be excluded from his people because he has broken my agreement.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> Then God told Abraham, “Now about Sarai your wife. Don't call her Sarai any longer. Instead, her name will be Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> I will bless her and I promise to give you a son by her. I will bless her so that she will become the mother of nations, and kings will be among her descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> Abraham bowed down with his face to the ground. But inside he was laughing, asking himself, “How on earth could I have a son at the age of one hundred? How could Sarah have a child when she is ninety?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; PSALMS 41:12; PSALMS 4:6"> Abraham said to God, “May Ishmael always live under your blessing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> “No, it's your wife Sarah who is going to have a son for you!” God replied. “You are to call him Isaac.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaac means “he laughs.”">c</a></sup>I will keep my agreement with him and his descendants as an eternal agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> Now about Ishmael. I heard what you said, and I will also bless him. I will make sure he has a great many descendants. He will be the father of twelve princes, and I will make him into a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> But it's with Isaac that I will keep my agreement, the son Sarah will give birth to about this time next year.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> When God had finished speaking with Abraham he left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> That day Abraham circumcised his son Ishmael as well as all those born in his household or purchased, in fact every male among the members of Abraham's household, just as God had told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> Abraham was 99 when he was circumcised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and his son Ishmael was thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> Both Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> All the males in Abraham's household, including those born in his household or purchased from foreigners, were circumcised with him.</span>
