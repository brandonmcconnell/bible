<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; PSALMS 49:16–17; Job 31:31"> Jacob found out that Laban's sons were saying, “Jacob has taken everything that belonged to our father. All the wealth he has actually came from our father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 Samuel 18:9–11; Genesis 4:5"> Jacob also noticed that Laban was treating him differently to the way he had before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 32:9; Hebrews 13:5"> The Lord told Jacob, “Go back to the country of your forefathers, to your family home. I will be with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Jacob sent for Rachel and Leah, telling them to come and meet him out in the fields where he was with his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:2–3; Genesis 31:53; Genesis 48:15"> “I've noticed that your father is treating me differently to the way he did before,” he told them. “But the God of my father will be with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 30:29; Ephesians 6:5–8; Titus 2:9–10"> You both know very well how hard I worked for your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:28; Genesis 31:29; Numbers 14:22"> But he's been cheating me—he's reduced my wages ten times! However, God hasn't let him hurt me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 30:32"> If he said, ‘You can have the speckled ones as your wages,’ then the whole flock had speckled young. If he said, ‘You can have the streaked ones as your wages,’ then the whole flock had streaked young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:1; Genesis 31:16; Proverbs 13:22"> This is how God took your father's livestock and gave them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 30:39; Genesis 20:6"> During the time the flock was breeding I had a dream where I saw that the male goats mating with the flock were all streaked, speckled, or spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Genesis 31:13; 1 Samuel 3:6"> Then in the dream the angel of the Lord spoke to me and said, ‘Jacob!’ I replied, ‘I'm here.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Deuteronomy 24:15; Genesis 31:42"> He told me, ‘Take a look and you'll see that all the male goats mating with the flock are streaked, speckled or spotted, for I've been watching everything that Laban has been doing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:3; Genesis 32:9; Genesis 28:12–22"> I am the God of Bethel, where you poured olive oil on the stone pillar and made a solemn promise to me. Now get ready to leave this land and go back to your homeland.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 2:24; Genesis 29:24"> “There's nothing for us to inherit from our father's estate anyway,” Rachel and Leah replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:15–20; Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:27–30"> “He treats us like foreigners because he sold us to you, and now he's spent all that money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:35–1; Genesis 31:9; PSALMS 45:10"> All the wealth that God has taken from him belongs to us and our children, so do whatever God has told you to do!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; Genesis 24:61; Genesis 24:10"> So Jacob got ready. He helped his children and his wives onto the camels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:1–2; Genesis 27:41; Genesis 35:27–29"> and drove all his livestock in front of him. He took with him all his possessions and livestock he'd gained during his time in Paddan-aram, and left to go back to his father in the country of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 31:30; Hosea 3:4"> While Laban was away from home shearing his sheep, Rachel stole the household idols<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Household idols”: small figurines considered important and “lucky,” representative of pagan gods and consulted for making decisions. Often they were female figures, and associated with fertility. They also seem to be significant in determining issues of ownership of property and land, which is perhaps another reason why Rachel took them and why Laban was so keen to have them returned.">a</a></sup>that belonged to her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Jacob also deceived Laban the Aramean by not informing him that he was going to run away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 46:28"> So Jacob left in a hurry with everything he had, crossed the Euphrates River, and headed towards the hill country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:12–13; Genesis 30:36; Exodus 14:5–31"> Three days later Laban found out that Jacob had run away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 24:27; Exodus 2:11"> Taking some of his relatives with him, he chased after Jacob and caught up with him seven days later in the hill country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:50; Genesis 31:29; Genesis 20:3"> But during the night God came to Laban in a dream and told him, “Watch what you say to Jacob. Don't try to persuade him to come back, and don't threaten him either.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Don't try to get him to come back, and don't threaten him either”: literally, “from good to bad.” This idiomatic expression covered the range of possible approaches Laban might have been tempted to take, from trying to induce Jacob to return by offering some reward, to threatening him with force or some kind of penalty.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 33:18; Hebrews 11:9"> Jacob had set up his tents in the hill country of Gilead when Laban caught up with him, so Laban and his relatives did the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:2; Genesis 12:18; Genesis 20:9–10"> “Why did you deceive me like this?” Laban asked Jacob. “You carried off my daughters as if they were some prisoners captured by the sword!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Proverbs 26:23–26; Genesis 31:3–5"> Why did you run away in secret, trying to trick me? Why didn't you come and tell me? I would have given you a good send-off, a celebration with singing and the music of tambourines and lyres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Acts 20:37"> You didn't even let me kiss my grandchildren and daughters goodbye! You've really acted stupidly!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Genesis 31:42; Genesis 31:24"> I could really punish you badly, but the God of your father spoke to me last night and told me, ‘Watch what you say to Jacob. Don't try to persuade him to come back, and don't threaten him either.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:24"> Clearly you wanted to leave and go back to your family home, but why did you have to steal my idols?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 31:26–27; Genesis 20:11"> “I ran away because I was afraid,” Jacob explained to Laban. “I was worried that you would take your daughters from me by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3–5; Genesis 31:30; Genesis 31:19"> As for your idols, anyone you find who has them will die. You can search everything in the presence of our relatives, and if you find I have anything that belongs to you, you can take it.” (Jacob didn't know that Rachel had stolen the household idols.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:28"> Laban searched the tents of Jacob, Leah, and the two personal maids, but didn't find anything. He left Leah's tent and went into Rachel's tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Genesis 31:17"> Rachel had put the household idols in a camel's saddlebag and was sitting on it. Laban carefully searched the whole tent but couldn't find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Exodus 20:12; 1 Peter 2:18"> She said to her father, “Sir, please don't get upset with me for not standing up in your presence, but I have my period.” He looked everywhere but didn't find the idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Genesis 49:7; Numbers 16:15"> Jacob got angry with Laban and confronted him, saying, “What crime am I guilty of? What wrong have I done to you that you've come hunting me down?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Corinthians 6:4–5; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> You've searched through all my possessions. Did you find anything belonging to you? If you did, bring it out here before my relatives and yours so they can decide who's right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Deuteronomy 28:4; Ezekiel 34:2–4"> I've worked for you for these past twenty years. During that time none of your sheep and goats miscarried, and I haven't eaten a single ram from your flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Exodus 22:10–13; Leviticus 22:8"> If any of them were killed by wild animals, I never even brought you the carcass to prove the loss—I bore the loss myself. But you on the other hand always made me compensate you for any animals that were stolen, whether at night or in broad daylight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:8; Exodus 2:19–22; 1 Peter 5:2–4"> Whether it was sweating in the heat of the day, or freezing in the cold of the night when I couldn't sleep, I went on working for you for twenty years in your home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> I worked fourteen years for your two daughters, and six more years with your flocks. You reduced my wages ten times!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 31:7; Genesis 31:53; Isaiah 8:13"> If it weren't for the God of my father, the God of Abraham, the awesome God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“The awesome God”: literally “the Fear.”">c</a></sup>of Isaac, who took care of me, you would have dismissed me with nothing. But God saw my suffering, how hard I worked, and he condemned you last night.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Laban replied, “These are my daughters and these are my children and these are my flocks! In fact, everything you see here is mine! However, what can I do now about my daughters and their children?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:25–27; Joshua 22:27"> So let's make a solemn agreement between you and I, and it will be a witness to our mutual commitment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–22"> Jacob took a stone and set it upright as a pillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:5; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:54"> Then he told his relatives, “Go and collect some stones.” They all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“They all”: including both groups.">d</a></sup>made a pile of stones and then sat beside it to eat a meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1"> Laban called it Jegar-sahadutha, while Jacob called it Galeed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Both names mean “pile of stones,” the first in Aramaic, the second in Hebrew.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Joshua 13:8–9; Genesis 31:23"> Laban announced, “This pile of stone serves as a witness between me and you.” This is why it was called Galeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> It was also called Mizpah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Mizpah”: meaning “watchtower.”">f</a></sup>for as Laban said, “May the Lord keep a close eye on both of us when we're not together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Jeremiah 29:23; Micah 1:2"> If you treat my daughters badly or marry more wives in addition to them, God will see what you do even if no one else finds out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Then Laban told Jacob, “Look at this pile of stones and this pillar that I have set up as a memorial of the agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“A memorial of the agreement”: supplied for clarity.">g</a></sup>between you and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 31:48; Genesis 31:44–45"> They also act as a witness to our solemn promises to each other: I will not come past them to attack you; and you will not come past them to attack me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Genesis 16:5; Joshua 24:2"> May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor—the God of our forefathers—be the one to judge between us in any dispute.” Jacob in turn made his solemn promise in the name of the awesome God of his father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 26:30"> Then he offered a sacrifice on the mountain and invited all his relatives to eat a meal there. They spent the night on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 31:28; Deuteronomy 23:5; Genesis 18:33"> Laban got up early in the morning and kissed his grandchildren and his daughters goodbye. He blessed them, and then left to go back home.</span>
