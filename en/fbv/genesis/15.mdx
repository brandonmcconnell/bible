<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; PSALMS 119:114; PSALMS 84:11"> After all this had happened, God spoke to Abram in a vision, telling him, “Don't be afraid, Abram! I am your protector, and your truly great reward!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 25:21; Acts 7:5; Genesis 12:1–3"> But Abram replied, “Lord God, what good is whatever you give me? I don't have any children, and the heir to all that I have is Eliezer of Damascus.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It was the practice of the time for childless couples to appoint their most trusted servant as their heir.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Proverbs 29:21; Ecclesiastes 2:7"> Abram went on to complain, “Look! You haven't given me any children, so a servant from my household has to be my heir!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:16; Galatians 4:28; 2 Samuel 7:12"> But then the Lord told him, “This man won't be your heir. Your heir will be your very own son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:12; Romans 4:18; Deuteronomy 1:10"> The Lord took Abram outside and said to him, “Look up at the sky. See if you can count the stars! That's how many descendants you will have!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–14; Romans 4:9"> Abram trusted what the Lord said, and so the Lord counted Abram as being in a right relationship with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Acts 7:2–4; Romans 4:13"> The Lord also told him, “I am the Lord, who led you from Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land for you to own.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:18; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 7:11"> “But Lord God, how can I be certain that I will own it?” Abram asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:24; Leviticus 14:30; Leviticus 14:22"> The Lord told him, “Bring me a cow, a goat, and a ram, all of them three years old, together with a dove and a young pigeon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Leviticus 1:17; Genesis 15:17"> So Abram took and killed the three animals. Then he cut them in half, and placed each half opposite the other. However, he didn't cut the birds in half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:4; PSALMS 119:13; Ezekiel 17:3"> When vultures flew down on the carcasses, Abram frightened them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Job 33:15; Daniel 10:8–9"> As the sun went down, a deep sleep came over Abram, and at the same time a dense and terrifying darkness fell on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:6–7; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:40–41"> The Lord explained to Abram, “You can be absolutely sure that your descendants will be strangers in a foreign land, where they will be slaves and mistreated for 400 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:9–11; Exodus 6:5–6; Exodus 12:32–38"> However, I will punish the nation that makes them slaves, and later on they will leave, taking many valuable possessions with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 5:26; Genesis 25:7–9; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> But as for you, you will die in peace and be buried, having lived a good long life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 21:26; Daniel 8:23; Exodus 12:40"> Four generations later your descendants will come back here to live, because right now the sins of the Amorites have not reached their full extent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Judges 6:21; Jeremiah 11:4"> After the sun set and it grew dark, suddenly a smoking furnace and a flaming torch appeared and passed between the halves of the animal carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 1:3–4; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 24:7"> This is how the Lord made an agreement with Abram that day and promised him, “I'm giving this land to your descendants. It extends from the Wadi of Egypt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Wadi of Egypt”: Not the Nile, but what is known as the Wadi Arish today. See Numbers 34:5; Joshua 15:4, Joshua 15:47.">b</a></sup>to the great Euphrates River,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 24:21–22"> and includes the territory of the Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 17:5; Genesis 14:5"> Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Exodus 23:23–28; Deuteronomy 7:1"> Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites.”</span>
