<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; 2 Samuel 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> From the time he put Joseph in charge and trusted him with all he had, the Lord blessed Potiphar's household because of Joseph. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> So Potiphar left Joseph to care for everything he owned. He didn't bother with anything except to decide what food he was going to eat.<br />Now Joseph was handsome, having a good physique,</span>

## Footnotes

<ol className="fnl">
<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">39:8</span> “Trusts me so much”: supplied for clarity.</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">39:12</span> “Clothing”: the Hebrew word is a general word for clothing and is not specific. However, ancient Egyptian art depicts servants as wearing only a loincloth, and it is likely this what was Joseph was wearing. It also fits the story in that this piece of cloth could easily have been torn off. However, since nothing specific is mentioned, the general term is used here.</li>
