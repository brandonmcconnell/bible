<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 26:1; Genesis 26:6; Genesis 18:1"> Abraham traveled towards the Negev, staying between Kadesh and Shur. After that he moved on to live in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:15; Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:11–13"> During his time there, when Abraham told people about his wife Sarah, he said, “She's my sister.” So Abimelech,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Abimelech” means “my father is the king,” or “my father is Molech,” a Canaanite god. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Matthew 27:19; PSALMS 105:14"> But God appeared to Abimelech in a dream and told him, “Listen! You're going to die because the woman you've taken is already married—she has a husband.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–25; 1 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Samuel 4:11"> Abimelech hadn't touched Sarah, and he asked, “Lord, do you kill good people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 7:8; 1 Kings 9:4; Daniel 6:22"> Didn't Abraham tell me himself, ‘She's my sister,’ and didn't Sarah also say, ‘He's my brother’? I did this in all innocence—my conscience is clear!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 21:1; 1 Samuel 25:26"> God told him in the dream, “Yes, I know you did this in all innocence, so I prevented you from sinning against me. That's why I didn't let you touch her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:8; 1 Samuel 7:5; 2 Samuel 24:17"> Send the man's wife back to him. He's a prophet. He will pray for you, and you will live. But if you don't send her back to him, you should know for sure that you and all your family will die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Abimelech got up early in the morning and called all his servants together. He explained everything that had happened, and they were all terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:18; Genesis 26:10; Genesis 39:9"> Then Abimelech summoned Abraham and asked him “What have you done to us? How have I wronged you that you should treat me like this, bringing this terrible sin on me and my kingdom? You've done things to me that no one should ever do!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Abimelech asked Abraham “What were you thinking when you did this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:12; Job 28:28"> “Well, I thought to myself, ‘Nobody respects God in this place. They'll kill me just to get my wife,’” Abraham replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> “Anyway, she really is my sister, the daughter of my father but not my mother, and I married her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Genesis 12:9; Acts 7:3–5"> Since my God made me move far away from my family home, I told her, ‘If you really love me, then wherever you go with me you must tell people: He's my brother.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:16; Genesis 20:2; Genesis 20:7"> Then Abimelech gave Abraham gifts of sheep, cattle, and male and female slaves, and returned his wife Sarah to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 13:9; Genesis 47:6"> Abimelech told him, “Look over my land. You can choose to live anywhere you like.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5; Genesis 20:5; Genesis 24:65"> Abimelech also told Sarah, “Notice that I'm giving your brother a thousand pieces of silver. This is to compensate you for the wrong done to you in the eyes of everyone with you, and to make sure that your name is publicly cleared.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:16; Matthew 21:22; Job 42:9–10"> Then Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female slaves, so that they could have children again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 30:2; Genesis 16:2"> For the Lord had made all the women unable to have children because Abraham's wife, Sarah, had been taken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Had been taken”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
