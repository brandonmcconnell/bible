<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 45:10; Genesis 46:31; Genesis 46:28"> Joseph went to report to Pharaoh and told him, “My father and brothers, along with their flocks and herds and all their possessions, have arrived from the land of Canaan and now they're here in Goshen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Colossians 1:28; Jude 1:24"> Joseph took five of his brothers to go with him and introduced them to Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:33–34; 2 Thessalonians 3:10; Genesis 4:2"> Pharaoh asked the brothers, “What work do you do?”<br />“Your servants are shepherds, just like our fathers before us,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Genesis 15:13; Deuteronomy 26:5"> “We have come to live in Egypt because there's no grass left in Canaan for our flocks to eat,” they explained. “The famine is really bad in Canaan. So we would like to please ask permission to live in Goshen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Now that your father and brothers have arrived to join you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 47:11"> you can choose wherever you like in Egypt, the best place, for them to live. Let them live in Goshen. If you know any of them who are good at what they do, put them in charge of my livestock as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; 2 Samuel 8:10; 1 Samuel 2:20"> Then Joseph went with his father Jacob and introduced him to Pharaoh. After Jacob blessed Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Pharaoh asked him, “So how long have you lived?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 39:12; PSALMS 39:5; Job 14:1"> “I have been wandering for 130 years,” Jacob replied. “My life has been short and difficult—I have not lived as long as my forefathers who also wandered from place to place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:7; PSALMS 129:8; Hebrews 7:7"> Then Jacob blessed Pharaoh again before leaving him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:37"> So Joseph arranged for his father and brothers to live in Egypt and gave them land in the best part near Rameses, as Pharaoh had ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; 1 Timothy 5:8; Genesis 47:1"> He also provided food for all of them—his father, his brothers, and his father's whole household—depending on family size.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:11; 1 Kings 18:5; Lamentations 4:9"> No food was left in the whole country because the famine had become so bad. Throughout Egypt and Canaan people were starving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:56; Luke 16:10–12; Luke 16:1–2"> By selling grain Joseph collected all the money in Egypt and Canaan, and placed it in Pharaoh's treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:8; PSALMS 37:3; Matthew 6:11"> Once the money from Egypt and Canaan had run out, the Egyptians all came to Joseph and demanded, “Give us food! Do you want us to die right in front of you? All our money is gone!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Daniel 6:5–7; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> “Bring me your livestock,” Joseph told them. “I'll give you grain in exchange for your livestock if you've run out of money.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:4; Exodus 9:3; Isaiah 31:1"> So they brought Joseph their livestock, and he provided them with grain in exchange for their horses, sheep, goats, cattle, and donkeys. He gave them grain in return for their livestock during that year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:26; Jeremiah 38:9"> But when that year was over, they came to him the next year and said, “My lord, we can't hide from you the fact that our money is gone and that you now own our livestock. All we have left to give you are our bodies and our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:2–3; Job 2:4; Lamentations 1:11"> Do you want us to die right in front of you? So buy us and our land in return for food. Then our land will belong to Pharaoh, and we'll be his slaves. Just give us grain so we can live and won't die, and so the land won't be abandoned.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Joseph bought all the land in Egypt for Pharaoh. Each and every Egyptian sold their fields, because the famine was hurting them so badly. The land ended up being owned by Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:48"> and all the people became his slaves,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The people became his slaves”: Septuagint and other ancient translations. The Hebrew has “he moved them to the cities.”">a</a></sup>from one end of Egypt to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Nehemiah 13:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> The only land he didn't buy belonged to the priests because they had a food allowance provided to them by Pharaoh, so they didn't have to sell their land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 112:5; PSALMS 41:1; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> Then Joseph told the people, “Listen to me! Now that I have bought you and your land for Pharaoh, I'm giving you some seed for you to sow the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 41:34; PSALMS 41:1; PSALMS 112:5"> However, when it's harvested, you have to give one-fifth of it to Pharaoh. The other four-fifths you can keep as seed for the fields and as food for you, your households, and your children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Proverbs 11:26–27; Genesis 18:3"> “You've saved our lives,” they declared. “May you continue to treat us well, my lord, and we'll be Pharaoh's slaves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 47:22; Ezekiel 7:24"> So Joseph made it a law for Egypt which is still is in effect today that one-fifth of all produce from the land belongs to Pharaoh. Only the priests' land was exempt since it did not belong to Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7; Acts 7:17"> The Israelites settled in Goshen in Egypt where they became prosperous landowners and rapidly increased in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 90:10; Genesis 47:8–9; Genesis 37:2"> Jacob lived in Egypt for seventeen years, so he lived in total 147 years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:14; Genesis 24:49; Genesis 24:2"> When the time came for Israel to die, he called for his son Joseph and said to him, “If you think well of me, place your hand under my thigh and promise to treat me with trustworthy love and faithfulness. Don't bury me here in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–32; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 25:9"> When I die, bury me with my forefathers. You must take my body from Egypt to the family tomb and bury me with them.”<br />“I will do as you say,” Joseph promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 1:47; Hebrews 11:21; Genesis 24:3"> “Swear to me that you will,” he said. Joseph swore that he would. Israel bowed in worship at the head of his bed.</span>
</div>
