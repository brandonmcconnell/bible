<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–6"> The Lord told Abram, “Leave your country, your relatives, your family home,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Your family home”: literally, “your father's house.”">a</a></sup>and travel to the land I'm going to show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 18:18; Galatians 3:14"> I will make you the ancestor of a great nation and I will bless you. I will make sure you have a great reputation and that you are a blessing to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 27:29"> I will bless those who bless you; I will curse those who curse you. Everyone on earth will be blessed through you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 11:27"> So Abram left following the Lord's instructions, and Lot went with him. Abram was 75 when he left Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 14:14; Genesis 11:31; Hebrews 11:8–9"> With him went his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, together with all the possessions they had collected and the people that had joined them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“The people that had joined them”: this would include servants, but the term used is not specific and applies to anyone who had joined Abram's group for whatever reason.">b</a></sup>in Haran. They left for the land of Canaan. When they arrived there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Deuteronomy 11:30; Genesis 35:4"> Abram traveled on through the land as far as a place called Shechem, stopping at the oak tree of Moreh. At that time the land was occupied by Canaanites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 13:15"> The Lord appeared to Abram and said, “I'm going to give this land to your descendants.” So Abram built an altar to the Lord there because that was where the Lord appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; Genesis 4:26; Genesis 21:33"> Then he moved on to the hill country east of Bethel and set up camp there. Bethel was to the west and Ai to the east. He built an altar to the Lord there and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:3; Genesis 24:62; Genesis 13:1"> After that he went on his way, heading towards the Negev.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“The Negev”: the desert area to the south.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 43:1; Genesis 26:1–3; 1 Kings 17:1–18"> But the land had been hit by famine. So Abram continued on to Egypt, planning to live there because the famine was so bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 29:17; Genesis 12:14"> As he approached Egypt and was about to cross the border, he said to his wife Sarai, “Look, I know what a beautiful woman you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 20:11; Genesis 26:7; 1 John 1:8–10"> When the Egyptians see you, they'll say, ‘She's his wife,’ and they'll kill me but not you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 20:12–13; Genesis 20:2"> Tell them you're my sister so I'll be treated well because of you, and my life will be spared for your sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Matthew 5:28; Genesis 39:7"> When Abram arrived in Egypt, the people there saw how beautiful Sarai was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 20:2; Genesis 41:1; 2 Kings 18:21"> Pharaoh's officials also noticed and spoke positively about her to Pharaoh. So Sarai was taken to his palace to become one of his wives.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“To become one of his wives”: supplied for clarity.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 20:14; Genesis 13:2; Genesis 24:35"> Pharaoh treated Abram well because of her, giving him sheep and cattle, male donkeys, male servants, female servants, female donkeys, and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:21; Genesis 20:18; PSALMS 105:14–15"> But the Lord caused Pharaoh and the people in his palace to suffer from terrible diseases because of Sarai, Abram's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 26:9–11; 1 Samuel 14:43"> So Pharaoh ordered Abram brought to him and said, “What have you done to me? Why didn't you tell me that she was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why did you say, ‘She's my sister,’ and let me take her to become one of my wives? So here's your wife! Take her and leave!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 105:14–15; Proverbs 21:1; Exodus 18:27"> Pharaoh ordered his guards to expel him and his wife from the country, along with everyone with them and all their possessions.</span>
