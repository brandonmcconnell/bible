<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 23:4; Genesis 36:7"> Jacob settled down and lived in Canaan as his father had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:25–26; Genesis 35:22; 1 Samuel 2:22–24"> This is the story of Jacob and his family. Joseph was seventeen, and helped look after the flock with his brothers, the sons of Bilhah and Zilpah, his father's wives. Joseph told his father about some of the bad things his brothers were doing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Genesis 37:32; 2 Samuel 13:18"> Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Israel,” that is, Jacob.">a</a></sup>loved Joseph more than any of his other sons, because Joseph had been born to him when he was already old. He made a colorful robe with long sleeves for Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 27:41; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 4:20"> When his brothers noticed that their father loved him more than any of them, they hated him and had nothing good to say about him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 42:9; John 17:14"> Joseph had a dream, and when he told his brothers about it, they hated him even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 44:18; Judges 9:7"> “Listen to this dream I had,” he told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 44:14; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 43:26"> “We were tying up bundles of grain out in the fields when all of a sudden my bundle stood up, and your bundles came over and bowed down to it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Acts 7:35; Hebrews 10:29"> “Do you really think you're going to be our king?” they asked. “Do you honestly believe you're going to rule over us?” They hated him even more because of his dream and how he described it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:9–14; Genesis 37:7; Daniel 8:10"> Then he had another dream and told his brothers about it. “Listen, I had another dream,” he explained. “The sun and moon and eleven stars were bowing down before me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Philippians 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> He also told his father as well as his brothers, and his father told him off, saying, “What's this dream that you've had? Are we—I and your mother and brothers—really going to come and bow down to the ground before you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> Joseph's brothers became jealous of him, but his father puzzled over the meaning of the dream.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 37:1; Genesis 33:18; Genesis 34:25–31"> One day Joseph's brothers took their father's flocks to graze near Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; 1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:16"> Israel told Joseph, “Your brothers are looking after the sheep near Shechem. Get ready because I want you to go and see them.”<br />“I'll do it,” Joseph replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:27; Luke 19:42; PSALMS 125:5"> So he told him, “Off you go and see how your brothers and the flocks are doing, and come back and let me know.” So he sent him off.<br />Joseph set out from the Hebron Valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:27; Judges 4:22; Genesis 21:14"> and arrived in Shechem. A man there found him wandering about in the field, so he asked him, “What are you looking for?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Song of Songs 1:7"> “I'm looking for my brothers,” Joseph replied. “Can you please tell me where they're looking after the flock?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13"> “They've already left,” the man replied. “I heard them say, ‘Let's go to Dothan.’” So Joseph followed his brothers and caught up with them at Dothan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 23:12; PSALMS 37:12; PSALMS 37:32"> But they saw him coming way off in the distance, and before he got to them, they made plans to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Genesis 37:11; Genesis 37:5"> “Look, here comes the Lord of Dreams!” they said to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Proverbs 27:4; Matthew 2:2–16"> “Come on, let's kill him and throw him into one of the pits. We'll say that some wild animal has eaten him. Then we'll see what happens to his dreams!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Genesis 35:22; Joshua 10:28"> When Reuben heard all this, he tried to save Joseph from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 22:12; Deuteronomy 13:9; Genesis 42:22"> “Let's not attack and kill him,” he suggested. “Don't murder him, just throw him into this pit here in the desert. You don't need to be guilty of violence.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“You don't need to be guilty of violence”: literally “you must not send a hand against him.” Reuben is suggesting that they don't have to actively kill Joseph, but if they throw him into a pit he will die without them being guilty of committing murder.">b</a></sup>Reuben said this so that he could come back later and rescue Joseph from them and take him home to his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Matthew 27:28; Genesis 42:21"> So when Joseph arrived, his brothers ripped off his robe—the colorful long-sleeved robe he was wearing—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; PSALMS 130:1–2; Zechariah 9:11"> grabbed him and threw him into a pit. (The pit was empty—it didn't have any water in it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Genesis 43:11; Jeremiah 8:22"> They were just sitting down to have a meal when they saw a caravan of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead. Their camels were carrying aromatic spices, balm, and myrrh to take to Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 4:10; Genesis 25:32"> “What's the point of killing our brother?” Judah asked his brothers. “Then we'd have to cover up his death!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Genesis 42:21; 1 Samuel 18:17"> Instead, why don't we sell him to these Ishmaelites? We don't have to kill him. After all he's our brother, our own flesh and blood.” His brothers agreed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 7:9; PSALMS 105:17; Genesis 45:4–5"> So when the Ishmaelites (who were traders from Midian)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The text sometimes refers to them as Ishmaelites and sometimes as Midianites but are clearly one and the same group. Also verse 36.">c</a></sup>came by, they pulled Joseph out of the pit and sold him to them for twenty pieces of silver. The Ishmaelites took him to Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Genesis 37:34; Genesis 44:13"> When Reuben came back later and looked into the pit, Joseph was gone. He tore his clothes in grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Jeremiah 31:15; Genesis 42:32"> He returned to his brothers. “The boy's gone!” he moaned. “What am I going to do now?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:23; Proverbs 28:13; Genesis 37:3"> They slaughtered a goat and dipped Joseph's robe in the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Genesis 37:3; Genesis 44:20–23"> Then they sent the colorful robe to their father with the message, “We found this. Please examine it and see if it's your son's robe or not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Genesis 44:28; John 13:7"> His father recognized it right away and said, “This is my son's robe! Some wild animal must have eaten him. Poor Joseph has been ripped to pieces, no doubt about it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31; PSALMS 69:11"> Jacob tore his clothes in grief and dressed in sackcloth. He mourned the death of his son for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Job 2:11; 2 Samuel 12:17"> All his sons and daughters tried to console him, but he rejected their attempts. “No,” he said, “I will go down into my grave mourning for my son.” So Joseph's father went on weeping for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Genesis 37:28; 2 Kings 25:8"> In the meantime the Ishmaelites had arrived in Egypt and had sold Joseph to Potiphar. Potiphar was one of Pharaoh's officers, the captain of the guard.</span>
</div>
</div>
