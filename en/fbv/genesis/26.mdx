<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> There was a famine in the country—not the one that happened before in Abraham's time, but a later one. So Isaac moved to Gerar in the territory of Abimelech, king of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; PSALMS 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> The Lord appeared to Isaac and told him, “Don't go to Egypt—live in the country that I tell you to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; PSALMS 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> Stay here in this country. I will be with you and I will bless you, because I'm going to give you and your descendants all these lands. I will keep the solemn promise that I swore to Abraham your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> I will make your descendants as numerous as the stars of heaven and I will give them all these lands. All the nations of the earth will be blessed by your descendants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because Abraham did what I told him, and kept my requirements, my commands, my regulations, and my laws.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> So Isaac stayed in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> When the men there asked him about his wife, he told them, “She's my sister,” because he was afraid. He said to himself, “If I say she's my wife, the men here will kill me to get Rebekah, because she's so beautiful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> But later on, after he'd been there a while, Abimelech, king of the Philistines, happened to look out the window and saw Isaac lovingly fondling his wife Rebekah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Abimelech sent for Isaac and complained. “From what I saw she's clearly your wife!” he said. “Why on earth did you say, ‘She's my sister’?”<br />“Because I thought I'd be killed because of her,” Isaac replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> “Why would you do this to us?” Abimelech asked. “One of the men here might have slept with your wife, and you would have made us all guilty!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Abimelech issued orders to all the people, warning them, “Anyone who touches this man or his wife will be executed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> Isaac sowed grain that year, and the Lord blessed him with a harvest that was a hundred times what he planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> He became a rich man, and his wealth steadily increased until he was very rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> He owned many flocks of sheep and herds of cattle, as well as many slaves. He had so much that the Philistines became jealous of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> So the Philistines used dirt to block up all the wells his father Abraham's servants had dug.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> Then Abimelech told Isaac, “You have to leave our country, because you've become much too powerful for us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Isaac moved away and set up his tents in the Gerar Valley where he settled down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> He unblocked the wells that had been dug in his father Abraham's time—the ones the Philistines had blocked after the death of Abraham. He gave them the same names his father had.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Songs 4:15"> Isaac's servants also dug a new well in the valley and found spring water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> But the herdsmen from Gerar argued with Isaac's herdsmen, claiming, “That's our water!” So Isaac named the well, “Argument,” because they argued with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> He had another well dug, and they argued over that one too. He named the well, “Opposition.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Opposition”: The word is in fact the female form of the word, “satan,” meaning opponent or adversary.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; PSALMS 118:5"> So they moved on from there and he had another well dug. This time there was no argument so he named the well, “Freedom,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Freedom”: literally, “wide/open space,” which is often used in Hebrew as a synonym for freedom, since people are then given room to move around. See for example Job 36:16; Psalms 118:5.">b</a></sup>saying, “Now the Lord has given us freedom to expand and be successful in this land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> From there he moved on to Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> That night the Lord appeared to him and said, “I am the God of Abraham your father. Don't be afraid, for I am with you. I will bless you and give you many descendants for the sake of my servant Abraham.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; PSALMS 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> Isaac built an altar and worshiped the Lord. He also set up his tent, and his servants dug a well there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> Sometime later Abimelech came from Gerar to see Isaac, along with Ahuzzath his advisor, and Phicol the commander of his army.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 21:22. In view of the length of time between these events it is unlikely that they are the same individuals. These were probably official titles rather than personal names.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> “Why have you come to see me?” Isaac asked them. “Previously you hated me and told me to leave!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> “Now we realize that the Lord is with you,” they replied. “So we agreed that we should make a sworn agreement with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> You'll promise not to harm us in the same way we've never hurt you. You'll agree that we've always treated you well, and when we asked you to leave we did so kindly. Now look at how the Lord is blessing you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> So Isaac had a special meal prepared to celebrate the agreement. They ate and drank,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> and got up early in the morning and they each swore oaths to one other. Then Isaac sent them on their way, and they left in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> It was that very day when Isaac's servants who'd been digging a well came and told him, “We've found water!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> So Isaac named the well, “Oath,” and that's why the name of the town is “Well of the Oath” (Beersheba) to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> When Esau was 40, he married Judith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, as well as Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> They caused Isaac and Rebekah a great deal of grief.</span>
</div>
