<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> Abraham circumcised him when Isaac was eight days old, following God's command.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> She also said, “Would anyone have announced to Abraham that Sarah was going to have children to nurse? But now I have had a son for Abraham even when he was old!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> The baby grew up, and on the day Isaac was weaned Abraham held a large feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> But Sarah noticed that Ishmael, Hagar the Egyptian's son she'd had for Abraham, was making fun of Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> So she went to Abraham and told him, “You have to get rid of this slave woman and this son of hers! A slave woman's son is not going to be one of your heirs and inherit together with my son Isaac!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> Abraham felt very bad about it because Ishmael was his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> But God told Abraham, “Don't feel bad about the boy and the slave woman. Do whatever Sarah tells you, because it's through Isaac that your descendants will be counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> Don't worry—I will also make the son of the slave woman into a nation because he's your son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; PSALMS 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> Abraham got up early the next morning. He packed up some food and a skin of water which he gave to Hagar, putting them on her shoulder. Then he sent her and the boy away. She left and wandered through the Desert of Beersheba.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; PSALMS 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> When the water ran out, she left the boy under one of the bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> She went and sat down some way off, a few hundred yards away,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“A few hundred yards away”: literally, “a bowshot.”">b</a></sup>saying, “I can't bear to watch my son die!” As she sat down she burst into tears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; PSALMS 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> God heard the boy's cries, and the angel of God called to Hagar from heaven and asked her, “What's the matter, Hagar? Don't be afraid! God has heard the boy crying from where he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Get up, go over and help the boy up, and encourage him, for I will make him into a great nation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> God opened her eyes and she saw a well nearby. She went and filled the skin with water and gave the boy a drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> God blessed Ishmael and he grew up, living in the desert. He became a skilled archer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> He lived in the Desert of Paran. His mother chose a wife for him from the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> Around this time Abimelech came with Phicol, the commander of his army, to see Abraham. “God blesses you in everything you do,” Abimelech said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> “So swear to me right here and now that you won't betray me, my children, or my descendants. In the same way I've proved I'm trustworthy to you, do the same to me and my country where you're currently living.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> “I so swear,” Abraham replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> Then Abraham raised an issue with Abimelech of a well that Abimelech's servants had taken by force.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> “I don't know who did this, and you didn't mention it before. I haven't heard anything about it until today,” Abimelech responded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> Then Abraham gave Abimelech some of his sheep and cattle, and the two of them made an agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Abraham also separated out seven female lambs from the flock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> “What are these seven female lambs for that you've separated from the flock?” Abimelech asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> “I'm giving you these seven female lambs in return for your admission that I dug this well,” Abraham replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> That's why he called the place Beersheba,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Beersheba means both “well of swearing” and “well of seven.”">c</a></sup>because the two of them swore an oath to each other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> After making the agreement at Beersheba, Abimelech and Phicol the commander of his army left and went home to the country of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; PSALMS 90:2"> Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba and there he worshiped the Lord, the Eternal God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> Abraham lived in the country of the Philistines for a long time.</span>
</div>
