<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:14; Exodus 19:10; Ezekiel 18:31"> So Jacob told his family and everyone who was with him, “Get rid of the pagan idols you have with you. Purify yourselves and change your clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 32:7; Genesis 31:3"> We have to get ready and go to Bethel so I can build an altar to God who answered me in my time of trouble. He has been with me wherever I went.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Deuteronomy 7:25; Exodus 32:20"> They handed over to Jacob all the pagan idols they had, as well as their earrings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Earrings”: some commentators believe that these earrings also had some religious associations.">b</a></sup>and he buried them under the oak tree at Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; 2 Chronicles 17:10; Joshua 5:1"> As they left on their journey, the terror of God spread over all the surrounding towns, so nobody tried to retaliate against Jacob's sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 28:22; Genesis 12:8"> Jacob and everyone with him arrived at Luz (also known as Bethel) in the country of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:13"> He built an altar there and called the place El-Bethel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“El-Bethel”: meaning “the God of Bethel.” Bethel in turn means “the house of God” (see 28:19).">c</a></sup>because that was where God had appeared to him when he was running away from his brother Esau.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:59; 1 Samuel 31:13; Judges 2:5"> Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died and was buried under the oak near Bethel. So it was named “the oak of weeping.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 35:1; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 26:2"> God appeared to Jacob again and blessed him after his return from Paddan-aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:5; Genesis 32:27–28; Genesis 17:15"> God told him, “Jacob will not be your name any longer. Instead of Jacob your name will be Israel.” So God called him Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 22:17"> Then God said, “I am God Almighty! Reproduce, increase, and you will become a nation—in fact a group of nations—and kings will be among your descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:3–4"> I will give to you and to your descendants the land I also gave to Abraham and Isaac.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Genesis 18:33; Judges 6:21"> Then God left the place where he had been speaking to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–19; 1 Samuel 7:12; Exodus 17:15"> Afterwards Jacob set up a stone pillar where God had spoken with him. He poured out a drink offering on it, and also olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 28:19"> Jacob called the place Bethel, because he had spoken with God there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; 2 Kings 5:19"> Then they moved on from Bethel. While they were still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel went into labor and had great difficulty giving birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:24; 1 Samuel 4:19–21"> When she was in the worst birth-pains, the midwife told her, “Don't give up—you have another son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Acts 7:59; Luke 23:46"> But she was dying, and with her last breath she named him Benoni.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Benoni” means “son of my suffering.”">d</a></sup>But his father named him Benjamin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Benjamin” means “son of my right hand.” The right hand was considered more favorable.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Ruth 4:11"> Rachel died and was buried on the way to Ephrath (also known as Bethlehem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; Genesis 35:14; 2 Samuel 18:17–18"> Jacob set up a stone memorial over Rachel's grave, and it's still there to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 4:8; Luke 2:8"> Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Israel”: referring of course to Jacob after his name change.">f</a></sup>moved on and camped beyond the watch tower at Eder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:1; Leviticus 18:8; 2 Samuel 20:3"> During the time he was living there, Reuben went and slept with Bilhah, his father's concubine, and Israel found out about it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Septuagint adds, “and it was very distressing to him.”">g</a></sup><br />These were the twelve sons of Jacob:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 30:18–20; Genesis 46:8–15; Genesis 29:32–35"> The sons of Leah: Reuben (Jacob's firstborn), Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 35:16–18"> The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 46:23–25; Genesis 37:2; Genesis 30:4–8"> The sons of Rachel's personal maid Bilhah: Dan and Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 30:9–13; Genesis 46:16–18; Genesis 25:20"> The sons of Leah's personal maid Zilpah: Gad and Asher.<br />These were the sons of Jacob, who were born to him while in Paddan-aram.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 18:1"> Jacob returned home to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath-arba (also known as Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 25:7; Genesis 50:26; Genesis 47:28"> Isaac lived to be 180</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 49:33; Genesis 49:31"> when he breathed his last and died at an old age. He had lived a full life, and now he joined his forefathers in death. His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.</span>
</div>
