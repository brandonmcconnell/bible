<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Genesis 48:10; Genesis 25:23–25"> Isaac was old and going blind. He called for Esau, his oldest son, and said, “My son.” “I'm here,” Esau replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Proverbs 27:1"> “I'm old now,” said Isaac, “I may die soon, who knows?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–28; Genesis 10:9; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> So please take your bow and arrows and go hunting in the countryside for some meat for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:20; Genesis 48:9; Genesis 49:28"> Make me that tasty food that I love and bring it to me to eat, so I can bless you before I die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Rebekah heard what Isaac told his son Esau. So when Esau left to go hunting in the countryside for wild game,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 25:28"> Rebekah told her son Jacob, “Listen! I heard your father tell your brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; 1 Samuel 24:19; Deuteronomy 33:1"> ‘Get me some wild game and make me some tasty food so I can eat it and then bless you in the presence of the Lord before I die.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Genesis 27:13; Acts 5:29"> Now then, my son, listen to me and do exactly what I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:15; 1 Samuel 16:20"> Go to the flock and bring me two nice young goats. I'll cook them and make the tasty food your father loves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then you take it to your father to eat, so he can bless you in the presence of the Lord before he dies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:25"> “But listen,” Jacob replied to his mother Rebekah, “my brother Esau is a hairy man, and I'm a smooth man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachi 1:14"> Maybe my father will notice when he touches me. Then it will look like I'm deceiving him and I'll bring a curse down on myself instead of a blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Samuel 25:24; 2 Samuel 14:9"> “Let the curse fall on me, my son,” his mother replied. “Just do what I tell you. Go and get the young goats for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> So Jacob went and got them and took them to his mother, and she made some tasty food, the way his father loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> Then Rebekah went and got her older son Esau's best clothes that she had at home and put them on Jacob her younger son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> She put the goatskins on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then she handed her son Jacob the tasty food and the bread she'd made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He went in to see his father, and called out, “My father, I'm here.” “Which son are you?” Isaac asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> “It's me Esau, your firstborn son,” Jacob told his father. “I did what you told me. So please sit up and eat some of my wild game meat so you can bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> “How did you find an animal so fast, my son?” Isaac asked. “Because the Lord your God sent it my way,” Jacob replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> “Come over here so I can touch you, my son,” Isaac told Jacob, “so I can tell if you're really my son Esau or not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Jacob went over to his father Isaac, who touched him and said, “It's Jacob's voice but Esau's hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> Isaac didn't realize it was really Jacob because his hands were hairy like his brother Esau's, so Isaac got ready to bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> “It's really you, my son Esau?” he asked again. “Yes, it's me,” Jacob replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> Then he said, “My son, bring me some of your wild game to eat, so that I may give you my blessing.” Jacob brought some for him to eat, as well as some wine for him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Afterwards he said to Isaac, “Come here and kiss me, my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; PSALMS 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> So Jacob went over and kissed him, and Isaac could smell the clothes Jacob was wearing. So he went ahead with the blessing, saying to himself, “See—the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that the Lord has blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; PSALMS 133:3"> “May God use the dew of heaven and fertile land to give you rich harvests of grain and new wine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> May the people of different nations serve you and bow down to you. May you rule over your relatives, and may they bow down to you. May everyone who curses you be cursed, and may everyone who blesses you be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> After Isaac finished blessing Jacob—in fact Jacob had just left his father—Esau returned from his hunting trip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> He had also made some tasty food, and took it to his father. Esau said to Isaac, “Sit up, my father, and eat some of my wild game so you can bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> “Who are you?” Isaac asked him. “I'm your son, your firstborn son, Esau,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> Isaac started to shake all over and asked, “So who was it who went hunting game and then brought it to me? I ate it all before you came back and I blessed him. His blessing will remain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Samuel 30:4"> When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out in great anger and bitterness, and pleaded with his father, “Please bless me too, my father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachi 2:10"> But Isaac replied, “Your brother came and deceived me—he stole your blessing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> “Isn't he well named—Jacob the deceiver!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Deceiver.” See 25:26.">a</a></sup>said Esau. “He's deceived me twice. First he took my birthright, and now he's stolen my blessing! Haven't you kept a blessing for me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> Isaac replied to Esau. “I have made him ruler over you, and have said that all his relatives will be his servants. I have declared that he will be well supplied with grain and new wine. So what is left that I can do for you, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> “Do you only have one blessing, my father?” Esau asked. “Please bless me too!” Then Esau began to cry very loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> Then his father Isaac declared, “Listen! You will live far away from fertile land, far from the dew of heaven that falls from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> You will make a living by using your sword, and you will be your brother's servant. But when you rebel, you will throw off his yoke from your neck.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> From then on Esau hated Jacob because of his father's blessing. Esau said to himself, “Soon the time will come when I'll mourn my father's death. Then I'll kill my brother Jacob!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; 1 Samuel 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> However, Rebekah found out what Esau was saying, so she sent for Jacob. “Look,” she told him, “your brother Esau is making himself feel better by making plans to kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> So, my son, listen carefully to what I tell you. Leave immediately and go to my brother Laban in Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> Stay with him for a while until your brother's anger cools down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> Once he's cooled down and forgets what you did to him, I'll send for you to come back. Why should I lose both of you in a single day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> Then Rebekah went and told Isaac, “I'm so sick of these Hittite women—they're ruining my life! If Jacob also marries a Hittite woman like them, one of the local people, I'd rather die!”</span>
