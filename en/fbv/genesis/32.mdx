<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 91:11; PSALMS 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> Jacob went on his way and some angels of God came to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> When he saw them he said, “This must be God's camp!” He named the place “Two Camps.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> He sent messengers on ahead to meet his brother Esau who was living in the region of Seir in the country of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> He told them, “This is what you are to say to my lord Esau. Your servant Jacob sends you this message. I've been staying with Laban up till now,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> and I have cattle and donkeys and sheep and goats, and male and female slaves. I've sent these messengers to explain this to you my lord, hoping you'll be pleased to see me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> The messengers returned to Jacob and told him, “Your brother Esau is coming to meet you with 400 armed men!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> When Jacob heard this, he was absolutely terrified. He split all the people with him, along with the sheep, goats, cattle, and camels, into two groups,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> saying to himself, “If Esau comes and destroys one group, the other one can get away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; PSALMS 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> Jacob prayed, “God of my grandfather Abraham, God of my father Isaac! Lord, you were the one who told me, ‘Return to your own country and your family home, and I will treat you well.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I don't deserve all the trustworthy love and faithfulness you have shown your servant. I crossed the Jordan years ago<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Years ago”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>with just my walking stick, and now I have two large camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> Please save me from my brother; defend me from Esau! I'm terrified that he's coming to attack me, my wives, and my children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> You yourself told me, ‘I will definitely treat you well. I will make your descendants as numerous as the sand of the seashore—too many to count.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> Jacob stayed the night there. Then he picked out animals as a gift to his brother Esau:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> 200 female goats, 20 male goats; 200 ewes, 20 rams;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> 30 female camels with their young, 40 cows, 10 bulls; 20 female donkeys, 10 male donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> He put his servants in charge of each of the separate herds and told them, “Go on ahead of me, and keep a good distance between the herds.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> He gave these instructions to those with the first herd: “When my brother Esau meets you and asks, ‘Who is your master, and where are you going, and whose are these animals with you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> you are to say to him, ‘Your servant Jacob sends these as a gift to my lord Esau, and he's following us.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He gave the same instructions to those with the second and third and all the subsequent herds, telling them, “This is what you are to say to Esau when he meets you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> You must also tell him, ‘Your servant Jacob is right behind us.’” Jacob said to himself, “Maybe by sending these gifts on ahead Esau won't be angry with me and when I meet him he'll be kind to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the gifts went on ahead while Jacob spent the night at the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> He got up during the night and took his two wives and the two personal maids and his eleven sons and crossed the Jabbok River at the ford.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> After helping them cross he also sent over everything that belonged to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> But Jacob stayed there alone. A man came and wrestled with him until dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> When the man realized he couldn't beat Jacob, he hit Jacob's hip socket and put it out of joint as he wrestled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; PSALMS 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> Then the man said, “Let me go because it's almost dawn.” “I won't let you go unless you bless me,” Jacob replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> “What's your name?” the man asked. “Jacob,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> “Jacob will no longer be your name,” said the man. “Instead you will be called Israel, because you fought with God and with men and you won.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> “Please tell me your name,” Jacob asked. “Why do you ask me my name?” the man replied. Then he blessed Jacob there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> Jacob named the place Peniel, saying, “I saw God face to face and I'm still alive!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> The sun came up as Jacob left Peniel, limping along because of his damaged hip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> (That's why, even today, Israelites don't eat the thigh tendon attached to the hip socket, because that's where the man hit Jacob's hip socket.)</span>
