<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 43:16; Genesis 24:2"> Joseph ordered his household supervisor, “Fill the men's sacks with as much grain as they can hold and put each man's money at the top of his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; Genesis 42:20; Deuteronomy 8:16"> Then put my special silver cup at the top of the sack of the youngest, along with the money for his grain.” He did as Joseph told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> At sunrise they were sent on their way with their donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:32; PSALMS 35:12; Proverbs 17:13"> They had hardly left the city when Joseph told his household supervisor, “Go after those men, and when you catch up with them, ask them, ‘Why have you paid back good with evil by stealing my master's silver cup?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“By stealing my master's silver cup”: Septuagint addition, used for clarity.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Leviticus 19:26; 2 Kings 21:6"> This is the cup he personally drinks from, and which he uses for divination.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Divination”: a way of discovering secrets or hidden knowledge. Sometimes this is close to magic, but, in this case, it may be that Joseph is using a common superstition to cover up his plan.">b</a></sup>What you've done is really evil!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> When he caught up with them, he told them what Joseph had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 38:16–18; Genesis 37:18–32; 2 Kings 8:13"> “My lord, what are you saying?” they replied. “We your servants wouldn't do anything like that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:35; Romans 13:9"> Remember that we brought back the money we found at the top of our sacks when we returned from Canaan. Why would we steal silver or gold from your master's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:32; PSALMS 7:3–5; Job 31:38–40"> If any one of us is found with it, he shall die, and all of us will become your slaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Genesis 44:17; Genesis 44:33"> “Whatever you say,” the man replied, “but only the one found with it will become my slave since the rest of you will be free of any blame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They all unloaded their sacks and put them on the ground. They each opened their own sacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 44:26–32; Genesis 42:36–38; Genesis 43:14"> The household supervisor searched the sacks, beginning with the oldest and working his way down to the youngest. The cup was found in Benjamin's sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; Genesis 37:29–34; 2 Samuel 1:2"> The brothers tore their clothes in grief. Then they loaded their sacks back on their donkeys and headed back to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:18; Genesis 43:25; Genesis 43:16"> Joseph was still at home when Judah and his brothers arrived, and they fell to the ground before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:10; Genesis 21:26; Genesis 3:13"> “Why did you do this?” Joseph asked. “Don't you know a man like me can find out things through divination?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:9; Daniel 9:7; Numbers 32:23"> “My lord, what can we say?” Judah replied. “How can we explain this to you? In what way can we prove our innocence? God has exposed the guilt of your servants. My lord, we are your slaves—all of us, including the one who was found with the cup.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Samuel 23:3; Genesis 18:25"> “I wouldn't do anything like that!” Joseph replied. “Only the man who was found with the cup will become my slave. The rest of you are free to return to your father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 32:22; Genesis 41:40; Genesis 18:30"> Judah came closer and said to him, “If you please, my lord, let your servant just say a word. Please don't become angry with your servant, even though you are as powerful as Pharaoh himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 43:7; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 42:7–10"> My lord, previously you asked us, ‘Do you have a father or a brother?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Genesis 42:38; Luke 7:12"> We told you, my lord, ‘We have an elderly father, and a younger brother, born when our father was already old. The boy's brother is dead. He is the only one of his mother's children left, and his father loves him dearly.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:15; Genesis 42:20; Amos 9:4"> Then you ordered us, ‘Bring him here to me so I can see him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 44:30; Genesis 42:38"> We told you, ‘The boy can't leave his father; if he did, his father would die.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 43:5; Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:15–20"> But you told us, ‘If your youngest brother doesn't come with you, you won't see me again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 42:29–34"> So when we went back to your servant, our father, we explained to him everything you had told us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:2; Genesis 43:5"> However, later on, our father told us, ‘Go back and buy some more food.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Genesis 43:4–5"> But we said, ‘There's no way we can go back unless Benjamin our youngest brother goes with us, because we won't be able to see the man if Benjamin isn't with us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:19; Genesis 35:16–18; Genesis 30:22–25"> Then my father said to us, ‘You realize that my wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“My wife”: referring to Rachel. Evidently Jacob considered her as his one true wife.">c</a></sup>had two sons for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:33; Genesis 37:13–14; Genesis 42:36"> One is no more, ripped to pieces no doubt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 37:33.">d</a></sup>for I've never seen him since.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:14; Deuteronomy 31:17"> If you take this one away from me too, and something bad happens to him, you'll send this old man to his grave in grief.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 2 Samuel 18:33; Genesis 44:31"> So if the boy isn't with us when I go back to my father, whose life depends on the life of the boy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 44:29; Genesis 37:35; 2 Corinthians 7:10"> as soon as he sees the boy isn't there he'll die, and we will really send this old man, our father, to his grave in grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 43:8–9; Genesis 43:16"> In fact I gave myself as the guarantee for the boy to my father. I told him, ‘If I don't bring him back to you, then I will always carry the blame!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 John 3:16; Exodus 32:32; Hebrews 7:22"> So please let me stay here as my lord's slave instead of the boy. Let him go back home with his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esther 8:6; PSALMS 116:3; PSALMS 119:143"> For how could I ever go back to my father if the boy wasn't with me? I couldn't stand seeing the anguish that would cause my father.”</span>
