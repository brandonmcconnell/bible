<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:18; Revelation 14:7; PSALMS 69:22"> If you will not listen and if you will not take it to heart to honor me, says the Lord Almighty, then I will place a curse on you and I will curse your blessings—in fact I have already cursed them because you haven't taken what I said to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:6; Malachi 2:9; Job 20:7"> Watch out! I will spread manure on your faces from the animals you sacrifice, the manure from your religious festivals, and you will be thrown out with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Ezekiel 38:23; Matthew 3:12"> Then you will know that I sent you this command so that my agreement with Levi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Levi”: this refers not only to Levi, but to his descendants as priests.">c</a></sup>can continue, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 37:26"> My agreement with him was one of life and peace, which I gave to him, and respect—he respected me. He stood in awe of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> He taught the people the truth; nothing in his teaching was false. He walked with me in peace and did what was right, and he helped many to turn away from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> A priest should explain the truth about God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“A priest should explain the truth about God”: literally, “A priest should guard knowledge.”">d</a></sup>they should go to him to be taught, for he is the Lord Almighty's messenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremiah 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> But you have turned from my way. You have made many people to fall into sin. By your teaching you have broken the agreement with Levi, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:30; Deuteronomy 1:17; Matthew 19:17–18"> So I have destroyed any respect you had, and humiliated you before all the people. For you have not kept my ways, and have shown favoritism in what you teach.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “You have not brought blessings to people through your teaching.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; Malachi 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> Don't we all have one Father? Didn't one God create us? So why are we unfaithful to each other, violating the agreement made by our forefathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> The people of Judah have been unfaithful and have committed a disgusting sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“A disgusting sin”: or “an abominable thing.”">f</a></sup>in Israel and Jerusalem. For the men of Judah have defiled the Lord's Temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Temple”: literally, “holiness.”">g</a></sup>(which he loves) by marrying women who worship idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Ezekiel 24:21"> May the Lord expel the family of any man who does this from the nation of Israel! May there be no one left to bring an offering to the Lord Almighty!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="There is much debate over the meaning of the Hebrew text here.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; Jeremiah 14:12; PSALMS 78:34–37"> Something else you do is to pour out your tears on the Lord's altar weeping and moaning because he no longer pays attention to your offerings or doesn't want to accept them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> “Why not?” you ask. Because the Lord witnessed the vows you and your wife exchanged when you were young.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some believe that these priests not only divorced their wives but then married foreign women.">i</a></sup>You were unfaithful to her, your wife and partner joined to you by marriage contract.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> Did he not make you one, and give some of his Spirit to you? And what does he want? Children of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This is one of the most obscure verses in the Old Testament, and consequently there are many very different interpretations. The response then would be that Abraham was protecting the “children of God” through Isaac. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> For I hate divorce, says the Lord God of Israel, for it's a violent attack on the wife,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“A violent attack on the wife”: literally, “covers his garment with violence.”">k</a></sup>says the Lord Almighty. So watch what you do, and don't be unfaithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> You have worn the Lord out with your words.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="This may be a reference to mindless repetitions of prayers.">l</a></sup>“How have we worn him out?” you ask. By saying everyone who does evil is good in the sight of the Lord and he is happy with them, or by asking, where is the Lord's justice?</span>

Others see the “not one” as the subject of the sentence and so another possible translation would be something like: “No one would have acted like that if he had a remnant of the spirit.” Some have seen the “one” as referring to Abraham as the father of Israel, and the fact that he divorced Hagar may have cited as a precedent by some for their divorces. The response then would be that Abraham was protecting the “children of God” through Isaac. Whatever the case, it seems best to leave some of the ambiguity present, though in the context of discussing marriage relationships it would seem that the original design for marriage in Eden would be a relevant aspect for Malachi to mention.</li>
