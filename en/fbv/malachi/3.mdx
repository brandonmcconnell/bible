The messenger of the agreement you say is so happy with you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“You say is so happy with you”: referring back to 2:17.">c</a></sup>is coming, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Isaiah 4:4; 1 Corinthians 3:13–15"> Who can survive the day when he comes? Who can stand before him when he appears? For he will be like a blazing furnace that refines metal, or like the strong alkali that cleanses stains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9"> He will sit down like a refiner who purifies silver; he will purify Levi's descendants and refine them like gold and silver so they can present pure offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> Then the offerings of Judah and Jerusalem will please the Lord as they did in the old days, in the former years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:11"> I will come and put you on trial. I am ready to be a witness against those who:<br />practice witchcraft<br />commit adultery<br />tell lies<br />give false evidence<br />cheat employees<br />oppress widows and orphans<br />abuse foreigners<br />and do not respect me, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17"> For I am the Lord, I don't change, and you haven't stopped being descendants of Jacob.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="There is debate over the meaning of this second part of the verse. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:3; Romans 10:21; Romans 10:3"> From the time of your forefathers onward, you have turned away from my laws and not kept them. Return to me, and I will return to you, says the Lord Almighty.<br />But you ask, “How should we return?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The sense seems to be that the people do not see any necessity to return, not recognizing they have done anything wrong.">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Bring the full tithe into the storehouse so that there will be food in my Temple. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Joel 2:22; Zechariah 8:12"> I will prevent locusts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “the devourer.”">g</a></sup>from destroying your crops, and your vineyards will not fail to bear fruit, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Isaiah 62:4; Zechariah 8:23"> All nations will call you blessed because you live in such a wonderful land, says the Lord Almighty.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Zephaniah 1:12; PSALMS 73:8–13"> You have said, “What's the point of serving God? What benefit is there in keeping his commandments or going before the Lord Almighty with long faces?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Long faces”: literally, “as mourners”—however it does not appear that the people were sincerely repentant.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Malachi 2:17"> From now on we'll say that the proud are blessed. Evil people do well, and nothing happens when they dare God to punish them.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 7:6; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> They shall be mine, says the Lord Almighty, my prized possession on the day when I take action. I will treat them kindly, as a father treats an obedient son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 25:46; PSALMS 58:10–11; Genesis 18:25"> Then you will once again be able to distinguish those who do right from those who do wrong, between those who serve him and those who don't.</span>
