<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:4; Leviticus 23:37; Numbers 29:39"> “Tell the Israelites that these are my religious festivals, the festivals of the Lord that you are to call as the holy times when we meet together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 23:12; Exodus 35:2–3"> You have six days to work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, a holy day of meeting together. Don't do any work. It is the Lord's Sabbath everywhere you live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Exodus 23:14; Leviticus 23:37"> These are the Lord's religious festivals, the holy meetings that you are to announce at their specific times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 28:16–17"> The Lord's Passover starts on the evening of the fourteenth day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Deuteronomy 16:8; Exodus 12:15–16"> The Lord's Festival of Unleavened Bread begins on the fifteenth day of the first month. For seven days the bread you eat must be made without yeast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 28:18–25; Leviticus 23:8; Leviticus 23:21"> On the first day you are to hold a holy meeting. You must not do any of your usual work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For seven days you shall present food offerings to the Lord. There is to be a holy meeting on the seventh day. You must not do any of your usual work.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 16:9"> “Tell the Israelites that when you enter the land that I'm giving you and you harvest your crops, take a stack of grain from the firstfruits of your harvest to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 10:14; Leviticus 9:21"> He will wave the stack of grain before the Lord so that it may be accepted on your behalf. The priest is to do this the day following the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10–12; 1 Peter 1:19; Leviticus 1:10"> When you wave the stack of grain, you are to present a one-year-old lamb without defects as a burnt offering to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14–16; Joel 1:9; Numbers 28:10"> together with its grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of the best flour mixed with olive oil (a food offering to the Lord to be accepted by him) and its drink offering of a quarter of a hin of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Genesis 4:4–5; Leviticus 3:17"> Don't eat any bread, roasted grain, or new grain until the time you bring this offering to your God. This regulation is for all time and for future generations everywhere you live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:8; Deuteronomy 16:9–10"> Count seven full weeks from the day after the Sabbath—the day you brought the stack of grain as a wave offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Numbers 28:26"> Count fifty days until the day after the seventh Sabbath, and on that day present an offering of new grain to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Exodus 34:26; Exodus 34:22"> Bring two loaves of bread from your homes as a wave offering. Make them from two-tenths of an ephah of the best flour, baked with yeast, as the firstfruits to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 28:27–31; Leviticus 23:12–13; Malachi 1:13–14"> As well as the bread, present seven one-year-old male lambs without defects, one young bull, and two rams. They will be a burnt offering to the Lord, as well as their grain offerings and drink offerings, a food offering to the Lord to be accepted by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 28:30; Leviticus 4:23–28; Leviticus 7:11–18"> Present one male goat as a sin offering and two one-year-old male lambs as a peace offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Ephesians 2:14; Numbers 18:8–12"> The priest will wave the lambs as a wave offering before the Lord, along with the bread of the firstfruits. The bread and the two lambs are holy to the Lord and belong to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Numbers 18:23"> That same day you are to announce a holy meeting, and you must not do any of your usual work. This regulation is for all time and for future generations everywhere you live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:19–21; Leviticus 19:9–10; Isaiah 58:10"> When you harvest the crops grown on your land, don't harvest right up to the edges of your field, or collect what has been missed. Leave them for the poor people and foreigners. I am the Lord your God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Leviticus 25:9"> “Tell the Israelites that on the first day of the seventh month you are to have a special Sabbath of complete rest, a holy meeting that is announced by the sound of trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:21"> Don't do any of your usual work; instead you are to present a food offering to the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 16:29–31; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 16:24"> “The Day of Atonement is on the tenth day of this seventh month. You are to have a holy meeting, deny yourselves,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Deny yourselves”: this is usually taken to mean fasting and the avoidance of pleasures.">a</a></sup>and present a food offering to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 3:9; Leviticus 16:34; Hebrews 10:10"> On this day you must not do any of your usual work because it is the Day of Atonement, when things are made right for you before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 5:2; Leviticus 23:27"> Anyone who does not practice self-denial on this day must be expelled from their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:3; Zephaniah 2:5; Jeremiah 15:7"> I will destroy anyone of you who does any work on this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Don't do any kind of work at all. This regulation is for all time and for future generations everywhere you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 16:31; Matthew 11:28–30; PSALMS 69:10–11"> It is to be a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you shall practice self-denial. You are to observe your Sabbath from the evening of the ninth day of the month until evening the next day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 7:2; Nehemiah 8:14; Ezra 3:4"> “Tell the Israelites that the Feast of Tabernacles to honor the Lord begins on the fifteenth day of the seventh month and lasts for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24–25; Leviticus 23:7–8"> On the first day have a holy meeting. You must not do any of your usual work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:18; John 7:37; 2 Chronicles 7:8–11"> For seven days you shall present food offerings to the Lord. On the eighth day you are to have another holy meeting and present a food offering to the Lord. It is a meeting for worship. You must not do any of your usual work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Ecclesiastes 3:1"> These are the Lord's holy festivals, which you are to announce as holy meetings for presenting food offerings to the Lord. These include burnt offerings, grain offerings, sacrifices and drink offerings, each according to the specific day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Leviticus 19:3; Ezra 2:68–69"> All of these offerings are in addition to those for the Lord's Sabbaths. They are also in addition to your gifts, to all your offerings to fulfill promises, and to all the freewill offerings you present to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:13; Leviticus 23:34"> You shall celebrate a feast to honor the Lord for seven days on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, once you have harvested your crops. The first day and the eighth day are Sabbaths of complete rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Revelation 7:9; Deuteronomy 16:14–15"> On the first day you are to gather branches from large trees, from palm trees, from leafy trees and of river willows, and celebrate before the Lord your God for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 29:12; Nehemiah 8:18"> You are to hold this festival to honor the Lord for seven days every year. This regulation is for all time and for all future generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Genesis 33:17; Numbers 24:5"> You are to live in temporary shelters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Made from the tree branches mentioned in verse 40.">b</a></sup>for seven days. Every Israelite born in the country must live in shelters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10–13; PSALMS 78:5–6; Exodus 13:14"> so that your descendants will remember that I made the Israelites live in shelters when I led them out of Egypt. I am the Lord your God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 23:1–2; Matthew 18:20; Leviticus 21:24"> So Moses told the Israelites all about the Lord's festivals.</span>
</div>
</div>
