<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> The Lord told Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> “Tell the Israelites, When any man has a discharge from his genitals, the discharge is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> The uncleanness comes from his discharge, whether his body allows it to flow out or blocks it. It makes him unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> Any bed the man with the discharge lies on will be unclean, and anything he sits on will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> Anyone who touches his bed has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> Anyone who sits on whatever the man was sitting on has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Anyone who touches the man's body has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> If the man with the discharge spits on anyone who is clean, they have to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> Whatever the man sits on when he's riding will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; PSALMS 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> Anyone who touches whatever was under him will be unclean until the evening. Anyone who picks these things up has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If the man with the discharge touches anyone without washing his hands with water first, the person who was touched has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Any clay item touched by the man must be broken, and any wooden item must be washed with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> Once the discharge has healed, the man must allocate seven days for his cleaning process, wash his clothes, and wash himself in fresh water, and he will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> On the eighth day he must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the Lord at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew 3:17"> The priest will sacrifice them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. This is how the priest will make the man right before the Lord because of his discharge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> When a man has a release of semen, he must wash his whole body with water, and he will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Any clothing or leather that the release of semen falls on must be washed with water, and it will remain unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> If a man sleeps with a woman and there is a release of semen, both of them must wash with water, and they will remain unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew 15:19; Mark 5:25"> When a woman has a discharge of blood from her body, she will be unclean because of her period for seven days, and anyone who touches her will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> Anything she lies or sits on during her period will be unclean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> and anyone who touches her bed must wash their clothes and wash themselves with water, and will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Anyone who touches what she was sitting on has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Whether it's a bed or something she was sitting on, anyone who touches it will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> If a man sleeps with her and the blood from her period touches him, he will be unclean for seven days, and any bed he lies on will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 5:25; Matthew 9:20; Luke 8:43"> When a woman has a discharge of blood for several days when it's not the time for her period, or if it continues past her period, she will be unclean for the whole time she is bleeding, not just for the days of her period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Any bed she lies on or anything she sits on while she has her discharge will be unclean, just as her bed is during her period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> Anyone who touches them has to wash their clothes, and wash themselves with water, and they will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> Once the woman's discharge has healed, she must allocate seven days for her cleaning process, wash her clothes, and wash herself in fresh water, and she shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> On the eighth day she must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the Lord at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The priest will sacrifice them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. This is how the priest will make her right before the Lord because of her discharge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> This is how you must stop the Israelites from becoming ceremonially unclean, so that they don't die by making my Tabernacle unclean, the place where I live with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> These are the regulations regarding a man who has a discharge, a man who has a release of semen that makes him unclean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> a woman during her period, any male or female who has a discharge, and a man who sleeps with an unclean woman.”</span>
