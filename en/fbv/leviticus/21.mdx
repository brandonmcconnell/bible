<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> The Lord told Moses, “Tell Aaron's sons, the priests: A priest is not to make himself unclean by touching the dead body of any of his relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> The only exceptions are for his immediate family. This includes his mother, father, son, daughter, or brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> or his unmarried sister since she is a close relative because she doesn't have a husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> He must not make himself unclean for those only related to him by marriage—he is not to make himself ceremonially impure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> Priests are not to shave bald spots on their heads, trim the sides of their beards, or cut their bodies.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 19:27.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They must be holy to their God and not disgrace their God's reputation. They are the ones who present the food offerings to the Lord, the food of their God. Consequently they must be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> A priest is not to marry a woman made unclean through prostitution<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This may include cultic prostitution at pagan temples.">b</a></sup>or who is divorced by her husband, for the priest must be holy to his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> You shall consider him holy because he presents the food offerings to your God. He shall be holy to you, because I am holy. I am the Lord, and I chose you as my special people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Chose you as my special people”: literally, “set you apart.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> A priest's daughter who makes herself unclean through prostitution makes her father unclean. She must be executed by burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> The high priest has the highest place among the other priests. He has been anointed with olive oil poured on his head and has been ordained to wear the priestly clothing. He must not leave his hair uncombed or tear his clothes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="These were signs of distress or mourning.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> He is not to go near any dead body. He must not make himself unclean, even if it is for his own father or mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> He must not leave to deal with someone who has died<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“To deal with someone who has died”: supplied for clarity. It doesn't mean the high priest could never leave the sanctuary.">e</a></sup>or make the sanctuary of his God unclean because he has been dedicated by the anointing oil of his God. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> He can only marry a virgin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> He must not marry a widow, a divorced woman, or one made unclean through prostitution. He has to marry a virgin from his own people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> so that he doesn't make his children unclean among his people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Having a foreign wife would mean that any children of the marriage would be considered unclean. They also could not follow their father in the priesthood, and indeed he could not continue as a priest either.">f</a></sup>for I am the Lord who makes him holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> “Tell Aaron: These rules apply for all future generations. If any of your descendants has a physical defect, he is not allowed to come and present the food offerings of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> No man is allowed to do this if he has any defects, including anyone who is blind, crippled, facially disfigured, or has deformities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> anyone who has a broken foot or arm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> anyone who is a hunchback or dwarf, or who has cataracts, skin sores or scabs, or a damaged testicle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> No descendant of Aaron the priest who has a defect is allowed to come and present the food offerings to the Lord. Because he has a defect, he must not come and offer the food of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> He is still allowed to eat the food from the Most Holy Place of his God and also from the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> but because he has a defect, he is not allowed to go near the curtain or the altar, so that my sanctuary and everything in it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“My sanctuary and everything in it”: literally, “my sanctuaries.”">g</a></sup>are not made unclean, because I am the Lord who makes them holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> Moses repeated this to Aaron and his sons, and to all the Israelites.</span>
</div>
