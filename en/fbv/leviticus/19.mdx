<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> “Tell all the Israelites: You must be holy because I am holy; I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> Show respect for your mother and father, and keep my Sabbaths. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; PSALMS 115:4–7"> Don't turn to idols for help or make metal images of gods. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> When you sacrifice a peace offering to the Lord, make sure you do it correctly so God will accept you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> It needs to be eaten the day you sacrifice it, or the next day. Whatever is left over on the third day must be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> If you do eat some of it on the third day, the sacrifice becomes repulsive and won't be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> Anyone who eats it will bear responsibility for their guilt, for they have made unclean what is holy to the Lord. They must be expelled from their people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> When you harvest the crops grown on your land, don't harvest right up to the edges of your field, or collect what has been missed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> Don't take every last grape from your vineyard or pick up the ones that have fallen. Leave them for the poor people and foreigners. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> Don't steal. Don't lie. Don't deceive each other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> Don't swear oaths in my name that are not true, otherwise you defame the character<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “name.” Throughout Scripture “name” is related to aspects of character.">a</a></sup>of your God. I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Don't cheat others or rob them. Don't refuse to pay wages due to workers until the morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> Don't speak badly about deaf people. Don't put objects in the way of blind people to trip them up. Instead show respect to your God. I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> Don't be a corrupt judge. Don't show favoritism to the poor or to the rich. Judge others fairly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> Don't go around spreading false rumors about people. Don't keep quiet when the lives of others are at risk.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “don't stand still on the blood of your neighbor.”">b</a></sup>I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> Don't hold onto hateful feelings towards others. Talk honestly with your neighbors, so that you don't sin because of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> Don't look for revenge or hold a grudge against anybody, but love your neighbor as yourself. I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> Do what I tell you! Don't make different kinds of livestock breed together. Don't sow your fields with two different kinds of seed. Don't wear clothes made of two different kinds of material.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> If a man has sex with a servant girl promised to become another man's wife, but who hasn't yet been bought or set free, then compensation has to be paid. However, they are not to be killed, because she hadn't been set free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> But the man must bring a ram as his guilt offering to the Lord at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> The priest will set things right for him before the Lord using the ram of the guilt offering for the sin he has committed, and his sin will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> When you enter the land and plant any kind of fruit tree, treat the fruit at first as unclean.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Unclean”: literally, “uncircumcised.”">c</a></sup>For three years you are forbidden to eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> The fourth year all the fruit must be dedicated to the Lord as a praise offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> However, the fifth year you may eat the fruit and in this way you will have an even greater harvest. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> Don't eat meat with blood in it. Don't use fortune-telling or witchcraft.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> Don't cut your hair on the sides of your head or trim the sides of your beard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="It is thought that this prohibition is associated with the next regarding some pagan ceremony.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> don't cut your bodies in some pagan ritual for the dead, and don't get yourselves tattoos. I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> Don't bring shame on your daughter by making her a prostitute, otherwise the land will become morally and spiritually depraved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> Keep my Sabbaths and show respect for my sanctuary. I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> Don't try and find help from mediums or spiritists—don't even go looking for them, otherwise they will corrupt you. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> Stand up and be respectful of older people. Show reverence for your God. I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> Don't mistreat foreigners who live in your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> Treat them in the same way as a fellow citizen, and love them as you love yourself, because you were once foreigners living in Egypt. I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> Don't use dishonest weights and measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> Make sure your scales and weights are accurate, that your measures of ephah and hin are correct. I am the Lord your God who led you out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> Keep all my rules and regulations, and make sure you follow them. I am the Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>
