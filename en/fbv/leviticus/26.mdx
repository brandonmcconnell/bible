<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Deuteronomy 27:15; Numbers 33:52"> “Don't make yourselves idols anywhere in your land and bow down to worship them, whether they are images or sacred pillars or stone sculptures. For I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:30"> Keep my Sabbaths and show respect for my sanctuary. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 11:13–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14"> If you follow my rules and keep my regulations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; PSALMS 67:6; Leviticus 25:21"> I will make sure it rains at the right time so that the ground will grow good crops and the fruit trees will be productive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:13; Joel 2:26; Joel 2:19"> Your time of threshing will last right up to the grape harvest, and the grape harvest until the time you have to sow again. You will have more than enough to eat and you will live in safety in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9; Zephaniah 3:13; PSALMS 147:14"> I will make sure your land is at peace. You will be able to sleep without being afraid of anything. I will get rid of dangerous animals from the land, and you will not suffer from any violent enemy attacks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“You will not suffer from any violent attacks”: literally, “a sword will not pass through your land.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> You will chase your enemies, and kill them with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:30"> Five of you will kill one hundred, and one hundred of you will kill ten thousand. You will destroy your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; Ezekiel 16:62"> I will come to help you, so you will reproduce and increase in numbers, and I will confirm my agreement with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:22; 2 Kings 19:29; Luke 12:17"> You'll still be eating your old stock of grain when you need to get rid of it so you can store the new grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45"> I will come and live with you—I certainly won't reject you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 7:23"> I shall always be right there with you. I will be your God, and you will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Leviticus 25:38; PSALMS 116:16"> I am the Lord your God, who led you out of Egypt so you wouldn't have to be slaves to the Egyptians any longer. I smashed the yoke that kept you bent down and helped you to stand up straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:18; Jeremiah 17:27"> But if you don't pay attention to me and do what I tell you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Deuteronomy 31:16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> if you reject my laws, hate my regulations, and refuse to follow my commandments, and consequently break my agreement,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:8; Jeremiah 5:17; Micah 6:15"> then this is what I am going to do to you: I will make you panic, and suffer from diseases like tuberculosis and fever that make you blind and drain your life away. It will be pointless for you to sow seed in your fields because your enemies will eat the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Proverbs 28:1; PSALMS 53:5"> I will turn against you, and you'll be defeated by your enemies. People who hate you will rule over you, and you'll run away even when there's no one chasing you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> If after all this you still refuse to obey me, I will move to punishing you seven times over for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; 1 Kings 17:1; Daniel 4:37"> I will break your self-reliant strength you're so proud of, and make your sky hard as iron and your land hard as bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 127:1; Deuteronomy 11:17; Isaiah 49:4"> Your strength will be completely wasted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words attempts to cultivate the land will be pointless.">b</a></sup>because your land won't produce crops, and your trees won't produce fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:18; Leviticus 26:27"> If you go on opposing me and refusing to do what I tell you, I will make your punishments seven times worse, based on your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:15; Deuteronomy 32:24; Judges 5:6"> I will send wild animals to kill your children, wipe out your livestock, and make you so few in number that there won't be anyone on your roads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 2:30; Amos 4:6–12"> However, if in spite of all this correction you still don't change but go on opposing me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 18:26; 2 Samuel 22:27; Job 9:4"> then I will take action against you. I will punish you seven times over for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:12; Ezekiel 5:17; Jeremiah 29:17–18"> I will send enemies with swords to attack you in retaliation for breaking the agreement. Even though you retreat into your towns for defense, I will plague you with disease, and you will be handed over to your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Isaiah 3:1; PSALMS 105:16"> I will send a famine so bread is in short supply. One oven will serve the needs of ten women baking bread. It will be distributed by weight so that you'll eat but won't have enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Leviticus 26:21"> However, if in spite of all this you don't obey me, but go on opposing me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 66:15"> then I will take action against you in a rage of fury, and I will punish you myself seven times over for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Lamentations 4:10; Matthew 24:19"> You will eat the bodies of your own sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 23:20; Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 6:13"> I will destroy your high places<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“High places”: often associated with idol worship.">c</a></sup>of worship, smash your altars of incense, and pile up your dead bodies on what remains of your idols, which also have no life at all. I will really despise you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; Lamentations 1:10; Hebrews 10:26"> I will demolish your towns and destroy your pagan sanctuaries, and I will refuse to accept your sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 19:8; Jeremiah 18:16"> I will devastate your land myself, so that your enemies who come and live in it will be horrified at what has happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 20:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–66"> I'm going to scatter you among the nations. You will be chased out by armies with swords as your land is ruined and your towns are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:43; Leviticus 25:2–4"> At least then the land will be able to enjoy its Sabbaths all the time it's abandoned while you are in exile in the land of your enemies. The land will finally be able to rest and enjoy its Sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5–6; Romans 8:22"> The whole time it's abandoned the land will observe the Sabbaths of rest that it wasn't able to do while you were living there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Another rebuke since clearly the rule about leaving the ground uncultivated one year in seven was not properly observed.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Isaiah 30:17; Leviticus 26:17"> I will make those of you who survive so discouraged that as you live in the lands of your enemies even the sound of a leaf blowing in the wind will scare you into running away! You'll run away like you're being chased by someone with a sword, and fall down even though no one is after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 7:12–13; Judges 2:14; Jeremiah 37:10"> You'll trip over each other as you run away from the attack, even though no one's there. You'll have no power to resist your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 27:13; Jeremiah 42:17–18"> You'll die in exile and you'll be buried in a foreign country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:17; Ezekiel 24:23; Ezekiel 33:10"> Those of you who do manage to survive in the countries of your enemies will wither away because of their guilt, decaying as they share the sins of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 John 1:8–10"> They need to confess their sins and those of their fathers, acting in such an unfaithful way towards me, opposing me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:7; 2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7"> Because of this I took action against them and exiled them in the countries of their enemies. However, if they humbly give up their stubborn attitude and accept their punishment for their sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 106:45; Exodus 6:5; Ezekiel 16:60"> then I will fulfill the agreement I made with Jacob, Isaac, and Abraham, and I will not forget my promise about the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Leviticus 26:15; John 7:7"> For the land will be left empty by them, and it will enjoy its Sabbaths being abandoned. They will pay for their sins, because they rejected my rules and regulations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> But despite all this, even while they are living in land of their enemies, I will not reject or hate them so much as to destroy them and break my agreement with them, because I am the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 25:38; Ezekiel 20:22; Ezekiel 20:9"> Because of them I will renew the agreement I made with their fathers, those I led out of Egypt as the other nations watched, in order that I might be their God. I am the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 6:1"> These are the rules, regulations, and laws that the Lord set up between himself and the Israelites through Moses on Mount Sinai.</span>
