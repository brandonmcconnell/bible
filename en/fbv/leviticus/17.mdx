<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “Tell Aaron, his sons, and all the Israelites that this is what the Lord commands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 17:8; Leviticus 17:15; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Any Israelite who kills a bull, a lamb, or a goat, whether inside the camp or outside of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:7; Deuteronomy 12:5–21; Isaiah 66:3"> instead of bringing it to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting to present it as an offering to the Lord there, that person shall be held guilty of illegal killing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Illegal killing”: literally simply “blood,” the idea of bloodguilt being implied.">a</a></sup>They have shed blood and must be expelled from their people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Kings 14:23; 2 Chronicles 28:4"> This is why the Israelites have to bring to the Lord the sacrifices they currently offer out in the fields. They must bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and offer them as peace offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 18:17; Leviticus 3:2; Exodus 29:18"> The priest will sprinkle the blood on the altar of the Lord at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and burn the fat to be accepted by the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 34:15; Deuteronomy 32:17"> They must not continue to offer their sacrifices to the goat devils<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Clearly some form of pagan worship.">b</a></sup>with whom they have acted like prostitutes. This is a regulation for all time and for all future generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2–3; Leviticus 17:4; Leviticus 17:10"> Warn them that any Israelite or any foreigner living among them who offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 17:4"> without bringing it to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting to sacrifice it to the Lord must be expelled from their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 3:17; Deuteronomy 12:23"> I will disown any Israelite or any foreigner living among them who eats any blood and I will expel them from their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22; 1 John 1:7; Romans 3:25"> Life is in the body's blood. I have given it to you so that by putting it on the altar your lives can be made right, for it is the lifeblood that makes people's lives right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:49"> That's why I'm warning the Israelites: None of you is allowed to eat blood, and no foreigner living among you is allowed to eat blood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Ezekiel 24:7; Leviticus 7:26"> Any Israelite or foreigner living among them who hunts and kills a wild animal or a bird which are allowed be eaten must drain the blood from its body and cover it with earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Deuteronomy 12:23; Leviticus 17:11–12"> for life is in the body's blood. That's why I have warned the Israelites: You are not allowed to eat the blood of anything living, for life is in the body's blood. Anyone who eats it must be expelled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 22:8"> Anyone, Israelite or foreigner, who eats anything found dead or killed by wild animals must wash their clothes and wash with water, and they will be unclean until the evening. Then they will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 19:19–20; Leviticus 7:18"> But if they don't wash their clothes and wash themselves with water, then they bear responsibility for their guilt.”</span>
</div>
</div>
