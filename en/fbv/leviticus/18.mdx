<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> “Tell the Israelites: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> Don't follow the ways of Egypt where you used to live, and don't follow the ways of Canaan where I'm going to take you. Don't adopt their practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> Do what I tell you and keep my rules. I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> If you keep my rules and do what I tell you, you will live. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> Don't have sex with a close relative.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> Don't shame your father by having sex with your mother. She is your mother; don't have sex with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Don't have sex with any of your father's wives and shame your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> Don't have sex with your sister,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This clearly includes a half-sister or a step-sister.">a</a></sup>whether she's your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, or whether she was born in the same house as you or somewhere else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Don't have sex with your granddaughter, your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter, because that would be a shameful thing for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Don't have sex with the daughter of any of your father's wives or the daughter of your father. She is your sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> Don't have sex with your father's sister. She is a close relative of your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Don't have sex with your mother's sister. She is a close relative of your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> Don't bring shame on your uncle by having sex with his wife. She is your aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> Don't have sex with your daughter-in-law. She is your son's wife. Don't have sex with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> Don't have sex with your brother's wife and bring shame on your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> Don't have sex with both a woman and her daughter. Don't have sex with her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter. They are her close relatives. That is something I loathe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> Don't marry your wife's sister and have sex with her while your wife is still alive. They will be hostile wives to one other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> Don't have sex with a woman during the time she is unclean because of her period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> Don't commit any sex act with any other man's wife. This would pollute you and make you unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> Don't give any of your children as a human sacrifice to Molech,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Molech was a pagan god. The sacrifices were of children placed alive into the metal idols hands which had been heated with fire.">b</a></sup>for you must not disgrace the character of your God. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Don't have sex with a man as with a woman. That is something disgusting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> Don't have sex with any animal. This would pollute you and make you unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> A woman must not give herself to an animal to have sex with it. That is something disgusting. Don't pollute yourselves and make yourselves unclean by doing anything like this. That's the reason I'm expelling these nations from the land—they polluted themselves because of all these practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> Even the land has become polluted, so I am punishing it for the sins committed by the people who live there, and the land will vomit them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> You, however, must do what I tell you and keep my rules. You must not do any of these disgusting acts, whether an Israelite or a foreigner living among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> The people who lived in the land before you practiced all these disgusting things, and the land became polluted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> If you pollute the land, it will vomit you out just like it did the nations before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> Consequently anyone who does any of these disgusting things must be expelled from their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> You must accept my demand that you don't follow any of these disgusting practices done before you arrived. Don't pollute yourselves and make yourselves unclean. I am the Lord your God.”</span>
</div>
