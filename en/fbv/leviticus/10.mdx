<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:22; Leviticus 21:6; Isaiah 49:3"> Moses explained to Aaron, “This is what the Lord was talking about when he said: ‘I will show my holiness to those who approach me; I will reveal my glory so everyone can see.” But Aaron didn't reply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:22; Exodus 6:18; Acts 5:6"> Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, sons of Aaron's uncle Uzziel, and told them, “Come and carry away the bodies of your cousins and take them outside the camp, away from the front of the sanctuary.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13"> They came and picked them up by their clothes, and took them outside the camp, as Moses had ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 22:18; Joshua 7:1; Numbers 16:22"> Then Moses told Aaron and his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, “Don't leave your hair uncombed, and don't tear your clothes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In distress or mourning.">d</a></sup>otherwise you will die, and the Lord will be angry with everyone. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Leviticus 8:30; Acts 10:38"> Don't go outside the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, or you will die, because you have been anointed by the Lord.” They did what Moses said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The Lord told Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 3:3; Ephesians 5:18"> “You and your descendants must not drink wine or any other alcohol when you go in to the Tent of Meeting, otherwise you will die. This regulation is for all time and for all future generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Leviticus 11:47"> You must realize the difference between the holy and the ordinary, between clean and unclean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Malachi 2:7; Nehemiah 8:8"> so you can teach the Israelites all the regulations that the Lord has given to them through Moses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 7:9"> Moses told Aaron and his two sons who were left, Eleazar and Ithamar, “Take the grain offering that is left over from the food offerings given to the Lord and eat it without yeast beside the altar, because it is most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 6:16; Numbers 18:10"> You must eat it in a holy place, because it is the share for you and your descendants from the food offerings given to the Lord. This is what I have been ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:11; Leviticus 7:29–34; Exodus 29:24–28"> You and your male and female descendants can eat the breast of the wave offering and the thigh contribution anywhere that is ceremonially clean, because you and your descendants have been given these as your share from the peace offerings of the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 7:34; Genesis 17:13; Leviticus 7:29–30"> The thigh contribution and the breast of the wave offering, as well as the fat portions of the food offerings made, they are to bring and wave as a wave offering before the Lord. They belong to you and your children forever as the Lord has ordered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:3; Leviticus 9:15; Leviticus 6:26"> Moses checked what had happened to the goat of the sin offering, and found out that it had been burned. He got angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons Aaron had left, and asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6–7; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 6:24–30"> “Why didn't you take the sin offering and eat it in the holy place, because it is most holy and it was given to you to take away the people's guilt by making them right before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:30; Leviticus 6:26"> As its blood was not taken into the holy place, you should have eaten it in the sanctuary area, as I ordered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12; Leviticus 9:8; Jeremiah 14:12"> So Aaron explained to Moses, “Look, it was today that they presented their sin offering and their burnt offering before the Lord. After all that's just happened to me, would the Lord have been pleased if I'd eaten the sin offering today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 7:8–9; Matthew 12:20; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> When Moses heard what Aaron had to say, he accepted the explanation.</span>
