<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="LEV" data-book-slug="leviticus" data-book-name="Leviticus" data-chapter="4" data-verses="35">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 19:12; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 4:27"> “Tell the Israelites that these are the rules to handle cases<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The rules to handle cases”: supplied for clarity.">a</a></sup>of those who sin unintentionally against any of the Lord's commandments and do what is not permitted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:27–28; Leviticus 4:14; Leviticus 9:2"> If it is the high priest who sins and brings guilt on everyone, he must present to the Lord a young bull without defects as a sin offering for his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 1:3–4; Daniel 9:26; Isaiah 53:6"> He must take the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before the Lord, put his hand on its head and kill it before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:14; Numbers 19:4; Leviticus 4:16–17"> Then the high priest shall take some of the bull's blood into the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 16:14; Leviticus 14:18; Leviticus 26:28"> The high priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of it seven times before the Lord in front of the veil of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 16:18"> The priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of aromatic incense that stands before the Lord in the Tent of Meeting. The rest of the bull's blood he is to pour out at the bottom of the altar of burnt offering at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–5; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:31"> Then he shall remove all the fat from the bull of the sin offering: all the fat that covers the insides,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> both kidneys with the fat on them by the loins, and the best part of the liver, which he is to remove together with the kidneys</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> in the same way as the fat is removed from the bull of the peace offering. Then the priest shall burn this on the altar of burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:5; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:8–11"> But the skin of the bull, all its flesh, head, legs, insides and waste—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:11; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 16:27"> all the rest of it—he has to take outside the camp to a place that is ceremonially clean, where the ashes are dumped, and he must burn it on a wood fire there on the ash heap.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Leviticus 5:2–5; Hosea 5:15"> If the whole Israelite community goes astray unintentionally, and even though they are unaware of doing what is not permitted by any of the Lord's commandments, they are all still guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:3; Leviticus 4:28"> When they realize their sin, then they must bring a young bull as a sin offering and present it before the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 16:21; Leviticus 8:14"> The elders of Israel are to put their hands on its head and kill it before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 4:5–12; Hebrews 9:12–14"> Then the high priest shall take some of the bull's blood into the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7"> He shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times before the Lord in front of the veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7"> He shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that stands before the Lord in the Tent of Meeting. Then he is to pour out the rest of the bull's blood at the bottom of the altar of burnt offering at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Leviticus 4:26; Numbers 15:25"> Then he shall remove all the fat from the bull and burn it on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 15:25; Hebrews 10:10–12; Leviticus 12:8"> He shall offer this bull in the same way he did for the sin offering. This is how the priest will make them right, and they will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 16:15; Matthew 20:28; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Then he shall take the bull outside the camp and burn it, just as he burned the bull previously mentioned. It is the sin offering for the whole community.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:13; Numbers 31:13"> If a leader sins unintentionally and does what is not permitted by any of the commandments of the Lord his God, he is guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; Romans 8:3; Numbers 7:22"> When he realizes his sin, he must bring a male goat without defects as his offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 3:13; Leviticus 7:2; Leviticus 4:3–35"> He must put his hand on the head of the goat and kill it at the place where the burnt offering is killed before the Lord. It is a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Leviticus 4:18; Leviticus 4:30"> Then the priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Leviticus 4:31; Leviticus 4:35"> He shall burn all its fat on the altar like the fat of the peace offerings. In this way the priest will make the man's sin right, and he will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Numbers 15:27; Leviticus 4:13"> If any other Israelite sins unintentionally and does what is not permitted by any of the commandments of the Lord his God, he is guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:3; Leviticus 4:32"> When he realizes his sin, he must bring a female goat without defects as his offering for that sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 4:4; Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:4"> He must put his hand on the head of the sin offering and kill it at the place of the burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Leviticus 4:25; Leviticus 4:34"> Then the priest is to take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 1:9"> He shall remove all its fat like the fat of the peace offerings and burn it on the altar and it will be accepted by the Lord. In this way the priest will make the man's sin right, and he will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:22"> If he brings a lamb as a sin offering, he is to bring a female without defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 4:29–31; Leviticus 4:4"> He must put his hand on the head of the sin offering and kill it as a sin offering at the place where the burnt offering is killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:25; Leviticus 4:7"> Then the priest is to take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20; Leviticus 14:18"> He shall remove all its fat like the fat of the lamb is removed from the peace offerings and burn it on the altar to be accepted by the Lord. In this way the priest will make the man's sin right, and he will be forgiven.”</span>
</div>
