<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord told Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> “Give these instructions to the Israelites. These are the animals you are allowed to eat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> any animal that both has a divided hoof and also chews the cud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> However, if it either chews the cud, or has a divided hoof, then you may not eat it. These include: the camel, which though it chews the cud doesn't have a divided hoof, so it is unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> The rock hyrax, which though it chews the cud doesn't have a divided hoof, so it is unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> The hare, which though it chews the cud doesn't have a divided hoof, so it is unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> The pig, which though it has a divided hoof doesn't chew the cud, so it is unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You are not to eat their meat or touch their dead bodies. They are unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> You are allowed to eat any creature with fins and scales that lives in the water, whether in the sea or in fresh water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; PSALMS 139:21–22"> But you are not allowed to eat any of the many creatures that don't have fins and scales that live in the water, whether in the sea or in fresh water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They are repulsive.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Repulsive”: not only in a sensory way, but also in the sense that they are ceremonially unclean.">a</a></sup>You must not eat their meat, and you must treat their dead bodies as repulsive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> All such water creatures that don't have fins and scales are to be repulsive to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> As for the birds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The identification of the specific birds in the list that follows is uncertain. However, it primarily refers to birds of prey and other birds that eat carrion or unclean animals. In addition the word translated “birds” really means “flying creatures,” which accounts for the inclusion of bats at the end of the list.">b</a></sup>these must not be eaten because they are repulsive: eagle, griffon vulture, bearded vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> buzzard, kite and similar birds of prey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> any raven or crow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> tawny owl, long-eared owl, gulls, any kind of hawk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> little owl, fish owl, eagle owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> barn owl, desert owl, Egyptian vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> storks and any kind of heron, hoopoe, and bats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> All flying insects that crawl<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Crawl”: literally, “that go on all fours.” However, this does not mean that insects had only four legs when they do in fact have six, it simply refers to the usual way animals move, most having four legs.">c</a></sup>are repulsive to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But you can eat the following kinds of flying insects that crawl: those that have jointed legs they use to jump.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> So in this category you can eat any kind of locust, bald locust, cricket, or grasshopper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All other flying insects that crawl are repulsive to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> and will make you unclean. If you touch their dead bodies you will be unclean until the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> and if you pick up one of their dead bodies you must wash your clothes, and you will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every animal with hooves that are not divided, or that does not chew the cud, is unclean for you. If you touch any of them you will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> Any four-legged animal that walks on its paws are unclean for you. If you touch their dead bodies you will be unclean until the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> and if you pick up one of their dead bodies you must wash your clothes, and you will be unclean until the evening. They are unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; PSALMS 10:3"> The following animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The identification of the specific animals in the list that follows is uncertain.">d</a></sup>that run along the ground are unclean for you: rats, mice, any kind of large lizards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> geckos, monitor lizards, wall lizards, skinks, and chameleons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These animals that run along the ground are unclean for you. If you touch a dead one of them you will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> Anything that one of them dies and lands on becomes unclean. Whatever it is—something made of wood, clothing, leather, sackcloth, or any work tool—it must be washed with water and will be unclean until the evening. Then it will become clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> If one of them falls into a clay pot, all that's in it becomes unclean. You must smash the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> If water from that pot touches any food, that food becomes unclean, and any drink from a pot like that also becomes unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> Anything that one of their dead bodies falls on becomes unclean. If it's an oven or a stove,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Stove”: the word here is only used once in the Old Testament and so its meaning is uncertain. Clearly it is made of something breakable such a pottery.">e</a></sup>it must be smashed. It is permanently unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> On the other hand, if it's a spring or cistern containing water then it will remain clean, but if you touch one of these dead bodies in it you will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> Similarly, if one of their dead bodies falls on any seed used for sowing, the seed remains clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> but if the seed has been soaked in water and one of their dead bodies falls on it, it is unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> If an animal that you are allowed to eat dies, anyone who touches the dead body will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> If you eat anything from the dead body you must wash your clothes and you will be unclean until the evening. If you pick up the dead body you must wash your clothes and you will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> Every animal that crawls along the ground is repulsive—you must not eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Don't eat any animal that crawls along the ground, whether it moves on its belly or walks on four feet or many feet. All such animals are repulsive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Don't defile yourselves by any such crawling animal. Don't make yourselves unclean or defiled by them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> because I am the Lord your God; so dedicate yourselves and be holy, because I am holy. Don't defile yourselves by any animal that crawls along the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> I am the Lord who led you out of Egypt so that I could be your God. So be holy, because I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> These are the regulations about animals, birds, everything that lives in the water, and all animals that crawl along the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> You must recognize the difference between unclean and clean, between those animals that can be eaten and those that can't.”</span>
</div>
