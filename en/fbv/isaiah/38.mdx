<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Chronicles 32:24; 2 Kings 20:1–11"> About this time Hezekiah fell very sick and was about to die. The prophet Isaiah, son of Amoz, went to him and said, “This is what the Lord says: Put your affairs in order, because you are going to die. You won't recover.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; PSALMS 91:15; 1 Kings 8:30"> When Hezekiah heard this, he went to pray privately<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Privately”: literally, “turned his face to the wall.”">a</a></sup>to the Lord, saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; PSALMS 18:20–27; Hebrews 6:10"> “Please remember Lord how I have followed you faithfully with all my heart. I have done what is good in your sight.” Then Hezekiah cried and cried.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then the Lord sent a message to Isaiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 34:5–6; 1 John 5:14–15; Revelation 7:17"> “Go and tell Hezekiah, This is what the Lord, the God of your forefather David, says: I have heard your prayer, I have seen your tears. I will add fifteen years to your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; Isaiah 31:4–5; Isaiah 12:6"> I will save you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will defend this city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> This is the sign from the Lord to you that the Lord will do what he promised:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:9–11; Joshua 10:12–14; Matthew 16:1"> Look, I will make the shadow made by the sun go back the ten steps that it has gone down on the stairway of Ahaz. So the sun went back the ten steps that it had gone down on the stairway of Ahaz.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 18:1; Exodus 15:1–21; Job 5:18"> This is what Hezekiah, king of Judah, wrote after he recovered from his sickness:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 102:24; 2 Corinthians 1:9; PSALMS 107:18"> I said to myself, “Do I have to go to my death<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Death” literally, “gates of Sheol.”">b</a></sup>just as my life is going well? Why can't I count on the rest of my years?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:13; PSALMS 116:8–9; PSALMS 31:22"> I said, “I will never again see the Lord, the Lord, in the land of the living. I won't see anyone else again, none of the inhabitants of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 73:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 1:12"> Like a shepherd's tent, the place where I live<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“The place where I live”: referring to his body.">c</a></sup>has been pulled up and taken away from me. Like a weaver I have rolled up the cloth of my life and cut it from the loom. Day and night you bring me to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 51:8; Daniel 6:24; PSALMS 50:22"> I lie there patiently until the morning, but I feel like there's a lion breaking every bone in my body. Day and night you bring me to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; PSALMS 69:3; PSALMS 123:1–4"> I scream like a swift or a songbird,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Songbird”: some have suggested “thrush,” but the meaning is uncertain. It was certainly meant to reflect a plaintive, sad cry, and therefore the common translation of “crane” is certainly incorrect.">d</a></sup>I moan like a dove. My eyes grow dim as I look heavenwards. I'm being attacked, Lord, please come and support me!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; Job 7:11; Job 10:1"> Yet what can I say? He told me what was going to happen, and he himself did it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to his illness.">e</a></sup>I will walk quietly for the rest of my life because of the painful experience I went through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; 1 Corinthians 11:32; PSALMS 119:25"> Lord, we live by what you say and do, and I find life in all of this. You have given me back my health and allowed me to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:18–19; Jonah 2:6"> It was definitely for my own good I went through this bitter experience. You in your love saved me from the pit of destruction and you have forgiven all my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; PSALMS 30:9; PSALMS 6:5"> Those in the grave cannot praise you, the dead cannot praise you. Those who go down into the pit can no longer hope in your faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; PSALMS 119:175; PSALMS 78:3–6"> It's only the living who can praise you as I'm doing today. Parents explain to their children how you can be trusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 116:2; PSALMS 150:4; PSALMS 145:2"> The Lord saved me! We will sing my songs with stringed instruments all the days of our lives in the Lord's Temple.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 20:7–8; Mark 7:33; John 9:6"> Isaiah had said, “Prepare a dressing of figs and spread it on the skin sores so he may recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 118:18–19; 2 Kings 20:8; John 5:14"> Hezekiah had asked, “What is the sign to confirm that I will go to the Lord's Temple?”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
