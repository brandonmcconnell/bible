<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> There are shouts and commotion all over the city with people celebrating.<br />Your dead weren't killed by the sword or in battle.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A criticism of the people of Jerusalem who were not fighting the invaders.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All your leaders ran away together; they were captured without resistance. All your people trying to escape were captured together, even though they had run a long way away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; PSALMS 77:2"> That's why I said, “Go away! Let me mourn in peace. Don't insist on comforting me as the daughter of my people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Daughter of my people”: probably a reference to Jerusalem. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For the Lord has a day of defeat, of panic and confusion in the Valley of Vision, a day of tearing down walls and crying for help to the mountains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> The Elamites pick up their quivers full of arrows, and prepare their chariots and horsemen, while the people of Kir uncover their shields ready for battle.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Ready for battle”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> Your most productive valleys are now full of enemy chariots; and their cavalry are at your gates!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> Judah's defenses have been stripped away and so at that time you went looking for weapons in the Palace of the Forest.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“The Palace of the Forest”: “The Palace of the Forest of Lebanon” made by Solomon. See 1 Kings 10:17, 1 Kings 10:21; 14:27-28.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> You examined the breaks in the walls of the City of David and found there were many. You had water collect in the lower pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Your reviewed the number of houses in the city and demolished some to provide stone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“To provide stone”: supplied for clarity.">e</a></sup>to repair the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> You built a reservoir inside the walls for the waters from the old pool, but you did not respect its Maker or think about the One who planned it long ago.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> At that time the Lord, the Lord Almighty, was calling you to weep and mourn, to shave your heads and wear sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> Instead, you go on happily partying! You slaughter cattle and sheep so you can have your feasts, eating meat and drinking wine, saying, “Let's eat and drink, because we're going to die tomorrow!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> The Lord Almighty has made this clear to me: “I will not forgive this sin till your dying day, says the Lord, the Lord Almighty.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> This is what the Lord, the Lord Almighty, told me to do. “Go to Shebna, the palace manager, and give him this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> ‘What are you doing here? Who do you think you are, cutting out a tomb for yourself high up on a hill, carving out for yourself a place to rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Watch out, you great man! The Lord is about to grab you and violently toss you aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> He's going to roll you up into a ball, and throw you far away into a vast country.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Vast country”: literally, “a broad-handed land.”">f</a></sup>You will die there, and that's where the chariots you were so proud of will remain. You're a disgrace to your lord's royal family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; PSALMS 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Referring to the Lord.">g</a></sup>will push you out of office, I will strip you of your position.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> After that I will call for my servant, Eliakim, son of Hilkiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> I will put your robe and place your sash around him, and I will give your authority to him. He will be a father to the people living in Jerusalem and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> I will give him the key to the house of David.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“House of David” this could refer both the palace and to David's descendants.">h</a></sup>What he opens, nobody can shut; what he shuts, nobody can open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> I will drive him like a nail hammered securely into a wall. He will bring honor to his family.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> The heavy burden of his father's family will hang on him—all the descendants and the inlaws—all the little containers, bowls and all kinds of jars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; PSALMS 146:3"> So the time will come, declares the Lord Almighty, when the nail will come out of the wall, even though it was hammered in securely. It will break off and fall down, and everything hanging on it will fall down too. The Lord has spoken.’”</span>
</div>
