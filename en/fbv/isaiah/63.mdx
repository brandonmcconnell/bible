<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> I've been treading the winepress by myself; from the nations around no one came to help me. So in my anger I trod them down as if they were grapes, in my fury I trampled them. It's their blood that has stained my clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For I decided this was the day of my vengeance; the year of my redemption has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; PSALMS 98:1"> I looked around, but no one came to help; I was shocked that there was no one to assist me. So I saved them through my own strength alone, and my own anger kept me going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> I trampled down the nations in my anger; in my fury I made them drunk, and spilled their blood upon the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 86:15"> I want to tell everyone about the Lord's trustworthy love. I will praise the Lord for everything he's done for us—all the good things he has done for the descendants of Israel because of his kindness and his infinite trustworthy love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> He said, “Aren't they my people, children who wouldn't lie to me?” So he became their Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> He suffered with them in all their suffering, and the angel of his presence<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“The angel of his presence”: the exact implications of this phrase have been debated so is left as usually translated. He picked them up and carried them all throughout those years long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; PSALMS 78:56"> But they rebelled against him, and caused his Holy Spirit grief, so to them he became like an enemy and he fought against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; PSALMS 77:20"> Then they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“They”: the Hebrew text reads “he” at this point, and so could refer to the Lord. They shouted out, “Where is the one who took Israel through the sea, along with the shepherds of his flock? Where is the one who placed his Holy Spirit among his people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; PSALMS 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> Where is the one who lifted Moses' right hand, and who used his amazing power to divide the sea in front of them, giving him such a wonderful, enduring reputation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; PSALMS 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> Where is the one who led them through the depths of the sea?” They were like a horse running through the desert, they didn't stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> Like cattle descending into a valley, the Lord's Spirit gave them rest. This is how you led your people, earning a glorious reputation for yourself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; PSALMS 80:14"> Lord, look down on us from heaven! Watch from your high home, in your holiness and glory! Where is your enthusiasm and power? You're not showing me your feelings of sympathy and kindness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> But you are still our Father, even though Abraham wouldn't know us and Israel wouldn't acknowledge us. You, Lord, are our Father; you have always been called our Redeemer from long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Lord, why do you make us wander from your ways, and make us stubborn so we don't respect you? Come back to us for the sake of your servants, the tribes that belong to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> Your Temple belonged to us for a little while, but then our enemies invaded and destroyed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We were yours from ancient times, but we've ended up like people you've never ruled, never identified as yours.</span>
