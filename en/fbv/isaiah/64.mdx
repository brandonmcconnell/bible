<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 99:1; Jeremiah 33:9; Ezekiel 38:22–23"> In the same way that fire burns wood and makes water boil, make your reputation known to your enemies, so that nations will tremble in your presence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; PSALMS 65:5–6; PSALMS 66:3"> In the past you did things we weren't anticipating. You came down and the mountains trembled in your presence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; PSALMS 31:19; James 5:7"> Since the beginning, no one has heard about, no one has paid attention to, and no one has seen any God except you, the one who helps those who place their confidence in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> You join with those who are glad, and with those who do what's right and remember to follow your ways. But when we went on sinning, you became angry. How can we be saved if we go on like this?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew of the end of this verse is unclear.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> We've all become unclean, and all the good things we do are like dirty rags. We wither and die like autumn leaves, and our sins, like the wind, blow us away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> There's no one who calls for you or really wants to hold on to you, because you have hidden your face from us and let us drown in our own sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; PSALMS 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> But you, Lord, are our Father. We're the clay, you're the potter. You made us all with your own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; PSALMS 74:1–2; PSALMS 79:13"> Please limit your anger, Lord, and don't remember our sins forever. Look at us, and see that we are all your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Your holy cities have been turned into a wilderness; Zion has become a desert; yes, even Jerusalem is an abandoned ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; PSALMS 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> Our beautiful, holy Temple where our forefathers praised you has been burned down, and all that we treasured has been destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; PSALMS 74:10–11; PSALMS 83:1"> In view of all this, are you still going to refuse to help us? Are you going to stay quiet and punish us so severely?</span>
