<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> At that time, this will be the song that is sung in the land of Judah: “Ours is a strong city! Its walls and defenses are our salvation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open the gates so the nation that does right may enter, the nation that stays faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> You will keep in complete peace those who keep their minds focused on you, because they trust in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; PSALMS 62:8; PSALMS 55:22"> Trust in the Lord forever, for the Lord God is the eternal Rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> He brings down those who live so high and mighty; he demolishes the proud city with its high walls, bringing it down to the ground, into the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> The poor tread it underfoot; the oppressed walk on top of it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> You God, do what is right, and you straighten out the way for those who live right; you smooth out their path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> Yes, we follow your instructions, Lord, we put our hope in you. What we most want is to remember you and your wonderful character.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; PSALMS 63:1; PSALMS 119:62"> I look for you in the night; deep inside me I long for you. When your laws are shown to the earth, then the people of the world learn what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> Though grace is shown to the wicked, they don't learn to do right. Even in a country where people do what's right, they continue to do evil and they don't think about how great the Lord is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> Lord, you are holding your hand up, but they don't see it. Let them see your passionate commitment for your people, and be embarrassed; may the fire reserved for your enemies destroy them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; PSALMS 57:2; PSALMS 29:11"> Lord, you give us peace and prosperity; everything we've achieved you have done for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> Lord our God, there have been other lords besides you who have ruled us, but you are the only one we worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> They are dead, they will not live again;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Probably referring to those enemies of Judah who at times ruled them. Alternatively some see this as a reference to previous idol worship--the pagan gods who “ruled” over them.">a</a></sup>they will not rise from the grave. Lord, you punished them and destroyed them—even wiping out every memory about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> You have made the nation grow, Lord, you have made the nation grow. You have expanded our borders of the land, and we honored you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 77:1–2"> Lord, when we were suffering we came to you, pouring out our prayers like whispers as you disciplined us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> Just as a pregnant woman giving birth tosses about and cries out in pain, that's what we were like in your presence, Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; PSALMS 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> However, even though we became pregnant and we tossed about in pain, we gave birth to nothing but air. We did not bring about the salvation of the earth, and the people of the world have not become alive.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Become alive”: the word is actually “fall,” but is taken to mean “to be born.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> But those who died in you, Lord, will live! Their bodies will rise again! Wake up, and sing for joy, you people sleeping in the dust, for the dew you receive is like the dew of the morning giving life to those in the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 30:5; PSALMS 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> My people, go inside your homes and close your doors behind you. Hide there for a little while until the fury has passed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> Watch out! The Lord is coming from where he lives to punish the people of the earth for their sins. The earth will reveal the blood that has been shed on it; the earth won't hide those who have been killed any longer.</span>
