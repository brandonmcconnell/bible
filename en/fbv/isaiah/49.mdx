<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="ISA" data-book-slug="isaiah" data-book-name="Isaiah" data-chapter="49" data-verses="26">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Galatians 1:15; Isaiah 44:2"> Listen to me, you people of the islands!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Islands”: or, “coastlands.”">a</a></sup>Pay attention, you who live far away! The Lord called me before I was born; he gave me my name while I was still in my mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 50:4"> The words he gave me to speak are like a sharp sword. He has protected me by covering me with his hand. He put me in his quiver like a sharp arrow, keeping me safe there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 44:23"> He told me, “You are my servant, Israel, and I will reveal my glory through you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Galatians 4:11; Isaiah 53:10–12"> But I replied, “I've worked for nothing! I've exhausted myself, and for what? Even so, I leave it with the Lord to do what's right, and my reward is with my God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Matthew 17:5; John 3:35"> Now my Lord is going to speak, the one who formed me in the womb as his servant to bring Jacob back to him, to gather Israel to himself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“To gather Israel to himself”: Dead Sea Scrolls and some Hebrew manuscripts; the Hebrew text includes the word “not.”">b</a></sup>I am honored in the Lord's sight, and my God has given me strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> He says, “It's nothing much for you to be my servant to bring back the tribes of Jacob, those people of Israel that I've preserved. I'm also going to make you a light for the foreigners so that my salvation may reach everybody.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; 1 Peter 2:4; Isaiah 53:3"> This is what the Lord says, the Redeemer and Holy One of Israel, to the one who was despised and detested by the nation, to the one who is the servant of rulers: Kings will see you and stand up, and princes will bow down to you, because the Lord, who is trustworthy, the Holy One of Israel, has chosen you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 42:6; PSALMS 69:13"> This is what the Lord says: I will answer you at the proper time; I will help you on the day of salvation. I will take care of you, and I will give you to the people as my agreement with them, to restore the land and to reassign the parts that have been abandoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 42:7; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> Tell the prisoners, “Come out!” Tell those living in darkness, “Come into the light!” Like sheep they will feed along the roads and in the pastures on hills that once were barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Matthew 5:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> They won't be hungry or thirsty, and they won't get hot in the sun, for the one who loves them will lead them to springs, and guide them to water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8–10"> I will turn all my mountains into a road; my highways will be really high!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> Look at these people coming from far away! Look at these people coming from the north, and from the west, and from Upper Egypt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Upper Egypt”: following the reading from the Dead Sea Scrolls which appears to indicate Aswan. The Septuagint reads “Persia,” while the Hebrew reads “Sinim,” an unknown place.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 51:3; PSALMS 96:11–13"> Heavens, shout for joy! Earth, celebrate! Mountains, sing out in happiness! The Lord has come to care for his people, and he will treat his suffering people kindly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27; Romans 11:1–5; PSALMS 22:1"> But Zion said, “The Lord has given up on me; the Lord has forgotten about me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:21; PSALMS 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> Really? Can a mother forget her nursing baby? Can she forget to be kind to the child she carried in her womb? Even if she could forget, I will never forget you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 2:23; Isaiah 62:6; Isaiah 60:18"> Look at your names I've written on the palms of my hands! I'm always thinking about your walls.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Presumably the walls of Jerusalem that were knocked down.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 62:5; Ezekiel 28:24"> Soon your children will come running back. Your destroyers, those that devastated your land, will be gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–3; Isaiah 66:12–13; Isaiah 61:10"> Take a look around. See all your children gathering and coming back to you. As I live, declares the Lord, you will wear them all as jewelry, proudly putting them on like a bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10; Isaiah 54:1–2; Jeremiah 30:18–19"> Your ruined towns and abandoned places and devastated lands will be crowded with people, while those who took over your country will be long gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 1:10; Joshua 17:14–16; Galatians 4:26–28"> The children born during your time of mourning in exile will say, “This place is too crowded for me! Make room so I have a place to live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Isaiah 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> Then you will say to yourself, “Who gave birth to all these children for me? My children were killed and I was unable to have more; I was exiled and thrown aside—so who brought these children up? Look, I was abandoned, so where did they come from?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 14:2"> This is what the Lord God says, Watch as I give the signal to the nations, as I raise my flag to let everyone know. They will bring them back, carrying your sons in their arms, and lifting your daughters onto their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 60:3"> Kings will be your childminders; queens will be your nurses. They will bow low before you, and lick the dust from your feet. Then you will know that I am the Lord, and that those who put their trust in me will never be ashamed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; PSALMS 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> Can loot be taken from a warrior? Can prisoners be rescued from a dictator?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Dictator”: Dead Sea Scrolls reading.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 54:15–17; Isaiah 35:4; Isaiah 41:11–12"> But this is what the Lord says: Even the prisoners of warriors will be won back; even loot will be recovered from a dictator. I will fight with your enemies, and I will rescue your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Isaiah 41:14–20; Revelation 16:6"> I will make your oppressors eat their own flesh and drink their own blood like wine. Then everyone will know that I, the Lord, am your Savior and Redeemer, the Mighty One of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
