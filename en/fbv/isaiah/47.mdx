<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; PSALMS 137:8; Zechariah 2:7"> Go down and sit in the dust, virgin daughter of Babylon. Sit on the ground without a throne, daughter of Babylonia!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Babylonia”: literally, “Chaldea,” another name for Babylonia. Also verse 5, and 48:14.">a</a></sup>No longer will people call you gentle and delicate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5"> Go to work grinding flour with millstones. Remove your veil. Strip off your skirt, bare your legs, wade through rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Jeremiah 50:27–28"> You will be seen naked; what should be kept private will be shamefully exposed. I will take vengeance—I won't spare anyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 49:26"> Our Redeemer—his name is the Lord Almighty—is the Holy One of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 18:16–19; Habakkuk 2:20; Isaiah 47:7"> Sit quietly, and go into the darkness, daughter of Babylonia. Never again will you be called queen of all kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:9; Zechariah 1:15; Deuteronomy 28:50"> I was angry with my people, and I abandoned those who belonged to me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“I abandoned those who belonged to me”: literally, “I profaned my inheritance.”">b</a></sup>I handed them over to you. But you didn't show them mercy—you even mistreated old people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Isaiah 47:5; Jeremiah 5:31"> You said, “I will reign forever as the eternal queen.” But you didn't think about what was coming; you didn't remember what would happen to you in the end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:15; Isaiah 32:9; Isaiah 47:10"> Now listen to this, you sensual woman,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Though the image of a woman continues, the subject is the kingdom of Babylon.">c</a></sup>sitting there so sure of yourself, saying to yourself, “I am supreme<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“I am supreme”: the Hebrew simply has the emphatic “I.”">d</a></sup>—there's nobody besides me. I shall never be a widow or experience the loss of my children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 73:19; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 47:12–13"> But both these things will happen to you in quick succession! In just one day you will lose your children and become a widow. You will have this experience in its totality, in spite of all your witchcraft, in spite of all your magic spells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Isaiah 29:15; Romans 1:22"> You put your trust in your evil actions, saying, “No one can see what I'm doing.” Your wisdom and knowledge seduced you, and you told yourself, “I am supreme—there's nobody besides me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 18:9–10; Daniel 5:25–30"> Evil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The same word is used here as in verse 10.">e</a></sup>is going to strike you, and you won't be able to magic it away. Disaster will fall on you that you can't stop by paying a ransom. Destruction will suddenly hit you that you weren't expecting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:8–12; Nahum 3:4; Jeremiah 2:28"> So keep going with your magic spells and all your witchcraft, which you have worked at since you were young. Maybe you'll be successful, maybe you'll terrify people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; Daniel 2:2–10; Ezekiel 24:12"> All the advice you've received has worn you out! Where are your astrologers, those who look to the stars for guidance, who give you their predictions every month? Let them stand up and save you from what's coming down on you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Nahum 1:10; Ezekiel 15:7"> But look at them! They're like stubble that fire burns up completely—they can't even save their own lives from the flames. This is no fire to sit beside and grow warm!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 27:12–25; Jeremiah 51:6–9"> All those people you've worked with, all those you've traded with from when you were young—they will all go their own way, nobody will come and save you.</span>
</div>
</div>
