<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Jeremiah 2:13; PSALMS 22:26"> Why are you spending money on what isn't food, and why work for something that doesn't satisfy you? Listen carefully to me: eat what is good, and you'll enjoy the very best food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Proverbs 4:20; Matthew 11:28"> Come here to me, and pay attention, so you can truly live. I'm going to make an agreement with you that will last forever, based on the trustworthy love I showed to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–24; Isaiah 9:6–7; Daniel 9:25"> See how I made him a witness to the peoples, a leader and commander for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:9; Isaiah 60:5; Zechariah 2:11"> You will call nations you don't even know, and nations who don't know you will come running to you. For the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, I made you glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–14; PSALMS 145:18; Isaiah 45:19"> Look for the Lord while you can still find him; call out to him while he's nearby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 4:8–10; Ezekiel 18:21–23"> Wicked people should change their ways and get rid of even the thought of doing something wrong. They should turn to the Lord so he can be merciful to them. Come back to our God, because he's generous with his forgiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 92:5; PSALMS 40:5; Hosea 14:9"> For your thoughts are not my thoughts; and your ways are not my ways, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:31–36; PSALMS 103:11; Matthew 11:25"> In the same way the heavens are higher than the earth, my ways are higher than your ways, and my thoughts higher than your thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 32:2; Isaiah 30:23"> It's like the rain and snow that fall from heaven. They don't go back there until they've watered the earth, making plants grow and flower, providing seeds for the sower and food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 46:10; Romans 10:17"> In the same way the words I say don't come back to me unfulfilled, they accomplish what I want—they successfully achieve my purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:22; Isaiah 44:23; PSALMS 105:43"> You will live happily, taught the ways of peace. Mountains and hills will celebrate, singing beside you; and all the trees will clap their hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Isaiah 41:19; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> Cypress trees will grow instead of thorn bushes; myrtle trees will grow instead of brambles. This is to confirm the Lord's reputation—an everlasting sign, never to be destroyed.</span>
