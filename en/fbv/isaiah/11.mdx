<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16; Zechariah 6:12"> A shoot will grow from the stump of Jesse, and a branch from his roots will produce fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; John 16:13; Ephesians 1:17–18"> The Spirit of the Lord will rest on him, which is a Spirit of wisdom and understanding, a Spirit of advice and power, a Spirit of knowledge and awe of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; John 8:15–16; Luke 2:52"> His happiness will be in giving reverence to the Lord. He will not judge by what he sees, and he will not make decisions based on what he hears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 19:15; Malachi 4:6"> Instead, he will judge the poor justly, and make decisions fairly on behalf of the destitute people of the earth. He will strike the earth when he pronounces judgment, and he will execute the wicked with just a word from his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; 1 John 1:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> He will wear goodness like a sash and trustworthiness like a belt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 65:25; Hosea 2:18; Ezekiel 34:25"> Wolves will live with lambs; leopards will lie down with young goats, calves and young lions and young livestock will be together, and a small child will lead them along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Cows and bears will graze side by side; young lions will eat straw like cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5; PSALMS 140:3"> Babies will be able to play safely near snake holes, little children will be able to put their hands into a vipers' den.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 52:10; PSALMS 98:2–3"> Nothing will cause any harm or damage anywhere on my holy mountain, for the earth will be full of the knowledge of the Lord in the same way that water fills the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:9–12; Luke 2:32; Isaiah 11:1"> At that time the root of Jesse will stand like a banner for the nations. Foreigners will come to him, and the place of where he lives will be glorious.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:12; Isaiah 10:20; Zechariah 10:8–12"> At that time the Lord will act a second time<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Second time”: The first time was the exodus from Egypt.">a</a></sup>to bring back the remnant of His people from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Ethiopia, Elam, Babylonia, Hamath, and from the Mediterranean islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; Isaiah 43:6; Zephaniah 3:10"> He will raise a banner for the nations and gather the exiled people of Israel; he will bring together the scattered people of Judah from the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Hosea 1:11; Ezekiel 37:16–24"> Ephraim's jealousy will disappear, and Judah's enemies will be destroyed; Ephraim won't be jealous of Judah, and Judah won't treat Ephraim as an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:19; Isaiah 25:10; Jeremiah 49:28"> Together they will fly downhill to attack the Philistines to the west; they will plunder the people of the east. They will defeat Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites will become their subjects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 50:2"> The Lord will divide the Gulf of Suez;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Gulf of Suez”: modern name. Literally, “the tongue of the sea of Egypt.”">b</a></sup>he will wave his hand over the Euphrates River creating a scorching wind. He will split it into seven streams so that people can cross easily on foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23; Isaiah 35:8–10; Isaiah 11:11"> There will be a highway from Assyria for the remnant of his people that are left, just as there was for Israel when they left the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
