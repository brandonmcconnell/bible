<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Jeremiah 3:8; Matthew 18:25"> This is what the Lord says: Where's your mother's divorce certificate I gave her when I sent her away? Which of my creditors did I sell you to? Now look! You were sold because of your sins, and your mother was sent away because of your wrongdoing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 66:4; Numbers 11:23"> When I came, why wasn't anyone there? When I called, why didn't anyone answer? Is it because I don't have the strength to save you, or the power to rescue you? Can't you see that if I order it, the sea will dry up? I can turn rivers into a desert. Their fish stink because they've died of thirst since there's no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:12; PSALMS 18:11–12; Exodus 10:21"> I can make the heavens go dark, covering them with sackcloth like they're in mourning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Jeremiah 1:9; John 7:15–17"> The Lord God has given me the ability to teach others, to know how to encourage those who are exhausted with a word. He wakes me up every morning; he helps me listen as a disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:31; Hebrews 5:8; Isaiah 35:5"> The Lord God has instructed me, and I haven't been rebellious and I haven't turned away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Mark 14:65; Mark 15:19"> I offered my back for people to beat me and my cheeks for people to pull my beard. I didn't hide my face from their mocking and spitting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Ezekiel 3:8–9; John 16:33"> The Lord God helps me, so I haven't been disgraced. That's why I'm so determined, setting my face hard as stone, knowing I won't be humiliated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:32–34; Revelation 12:10; Isaiah 41:1"> The one who vindicates me is close by, so who will bring charges against me? Let's stand to oppose one another! Come on, anyone who wants to accuse me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:28; Isaiah 41:10; Hebrews 1:11–12"> The Lord God defends me. Who is going to declare me guilty? Look! Those who try will fall apart like old clothing, eaten up by moths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 12:46; Micah 7:7–9; Isaiah 26:3–4"> Who among you respects the Lord and obeys what his servant says? Who among you walks in darkness and doesn't have light? Let them trust in the Lord and put their confidence in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–7; Romans 1:21–22; Romans 10:3"> Beware all you who start a fire, who pick up blazing torches! Go ahead, walk in the light of your own fire and of the torches you yourselves have set alight! This is what you will receive from me: You are going to lie down in a place of suffering.</span>
</div>
