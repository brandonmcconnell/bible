<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; PSALMS 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> Listen to this, descendants of Jacob, called by the name of Israel, and who come from the lineage<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Lineage”: literally, “waters.”">a</a></sup>of Judah. Listen, you people who swear by the name of the Lord, or who invoke the God of Israel, but not truly or sincerely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> You say you're from the “Holy City,” and claim you're trusting in the God of Israel, whose name is the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> I predicted long ago what was going to happen—I said it and let people know. Then suddenly I decided to act, and it all came true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> I know how stubborn you are, with necks as unbending as iron and foreheads as hard as bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> I predicted these things to you long ago, before they happened. I explained them to you so that you couldn't say “My idol did this,” or “My image and my metal god ordered this to happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> You've heard all I predicted, and seen it happen. Won't you admit it? Now I'm going to tell you new things, secrets you don't know anything about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> These are brand-new, not something from the past. Before today you won't have heard anything about them, so you can't say, “Oh yes, I know about that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> No, you've never heard that, and you've never known that! Nobody's told you about that before! I know how deviously you operate—you're called “rebels from birth”!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 79:9; PSALMS 106:8; PSALMS 143:11"> But because of my nature<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Nature”: literally, “name.”">b</a></sup>I am delaying my punishment; and because of my reputation, I'm not going to destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> Look how I've refined you, but not like silver—I chose you in the furnace of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> The reason I'm doing this is because of who I am, yes, because of my very nature. I won't let my reputation be damaged—I won't let anyone else have it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Listen to me, Jacob, and Israel, the one I called. Only I am God. I am the first, and I am the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; PSALMS 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> I laid the foundations of the earth with my own hands; I personally spread out the heavens. When I call the stars, they all take their positions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Everyone gather round and listen. Which of your “gods”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Of your ‘gods’”: literally, “among you,” but the reference in context is clearly to idol.">c</a></sup>told you anything like this? The one the Lord loves will carry out the Lord's wishes against Babylon—he will attack the Babylonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; PSALMS 45:4"> I myself have spoken. I have called him to do this, and he will be successful in what he does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Come close to me, and listen to this. From the very beginning I haven't spoken in secret; I am always there right when it happens. Now the Lord God and his Spirit has sent me to tell you this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; PSALMS 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> This is what the Lord says, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, I am the Lord your God. I am the one who teaches you what is good for you, who leads you along the way you should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; PSALMS 119:165"> If you had only listened carefully to what I told you! Then your blessings would have flowed like a river, and goodness would have washed over you like the waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> Your children, your descendants, would have been like all those grains of sand. They wouldn't have had to be destroyed, wiped out before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Leave Babylon! Run away from Babylonia with happy shouts! Let everyone know, telling the whole earth, “The Lord has redeemed his servant Jacob!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; PSALMS 105:41"> They weren't thirsty when he led them through the desert—he made water come out of the rock for them. He split the rock open and water poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> The wicked have no peace, says the Lord.</span>
