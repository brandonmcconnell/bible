<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6–7; Proverbs 4:18; Isaiah 32:15–17"> I can't remain silent because of my love for Zion, I can't keep quiet because of my love for Jerusalem, until her integrity shines like a beam of light, until her salvation blazes like a burning torch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 66:12"> The nations will see the way you live right, and all the kings will see how you are blessed, and you will be called by a new name that the Lord gives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 28:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:19; Zechariah 9:16"> You will be a crown of beauty in the Lord's hand; a royal head-dress in the hand of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Hosea 2:19–20; Isaiah 62:12"> You won't be called Abandoned anymore; your land won't be called Desolate. Instead you will be called She Makes Me Happy, and your land will be called Married, because you make the Lord happy, and your land will be married.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The literal names are Hephzibah and Beulah. Hephzibah was actually the name of Hezekiah's queen, and Beulah became a symbolic name for Jerusalem.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Isaiah 65:19; Hebrews 12:2"> For just like a young man marries a young woman, so your sons will marry you; and like a groom is happy with his bride, so your God will be happy with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Ezekiel 3:17–21; PSALMS 134:1–2"> I have placed watchmen on your walls, Jerusalem; they won't ever be silent, day or night.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It was the duty of watchmen to regularly give a report by calling out. In this case it could be the prophets who are represented as Jerusalem's watchmen.">b</a></sup>You who pray to the Lord, don't ever stop and take a rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Isaiah 60:18"> Don't give the Lord a rest either, until he finishes his work, until he makes Jerusalem the most praiseworthy place on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–23; Judges 6:3–6; Deuteronomy 28:33"> The Lord has made a solemn promise, swearing by his power and might: I won't ever again let your enemies have your grain to eat, or let foreigners drink the new wine you worked hard for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7"> Instead, those who harvest the grain will eat it and praise the Lord, and those who harvest the grapes for the wine will drink it in the courts of my Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Isaiah 40:3; Hebrews 12:13"> Exit! Exit through the gates! Clear the way for the people! Build the highway! Get rid of any rocks on the road! Lift up a flag so the nations can see!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The imagery of this verse is taken to represent the people leaving Babylon.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 21:5; John 12:15"> See! The Lord has announced to everyone on earth, Tell the Daughter of Zion: Look! Your Savior is coming. He's bringing his reward with him, coming to give his gift!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 26:19; Isaiah 62:4"> They will be called the Holy People, the Redeemed of the Lord; and you will be called the Sought-After Place, A City No Longer Abandoned.</span>
