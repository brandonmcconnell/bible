<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:15–16; 2 Kings 15:29; Isaiah 8:22"> Even so, there will be no more darkness for those who suffered. In the past he humiliated the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, but in the future he will bring honor to Galilee of the foreigners that is on the route that leads from Jordan to the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:16; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> People walking in the dark will see a bright light; a light will shine on those living in a land of total darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; PSALMS 119:162; Isaiah 26:15"> You will make the nation grow enormously, and will make it extremely happy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The verbs in this section (9:2-7) are in the past tense, but are translated here in the future tense, since this is an example of the “prophetic perfect” tense in which future actions are considered so definite they are stated as if they had already happened.">a</a></sup>The people will celebrate before you as people celebrate at harvest-time, as soldiers celebrate when dividing up what has been looted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 10:26–27"> For you will smash the yoke that burdens them down, the bar across their shoulders, and the oppressors' rod used to beat them just as you did when you defeated the Midianite army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8; PSALMS 46:9; Isaiah 30:33"> For every army boot that noisily trampled people down, and every uniform soaked in blood—they are going to be burned, fuel for the fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:11; Matthew 28:18; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> For a child will be born for us, a son will be given to us. He will bear the responsibility to rule. He will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Daniel 2:44; Daniel 7:14"> His rule and the peace he brings know no limits and will never come to an end. He will sit on David's throne and rule his kingdom, making it secure. He will operate from principles of justice and goodness, from the time he begins his rule and for all eternity. The Lord Almighty is determined to make this happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–8; Zechariah 1:6; Zechariah 5:1–4"> The Lord has sent a message directed against Jacob, and its consequences will fall upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:12; 1 Peter 5:5; Ezekiel 7:27"> All the people will soon realize it—Ephraim and the people who live in Samaria. Proudly and arrogantly they say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; 1 Kings 10:27; 1 Kings 7:9–12"> “The brick buildings have collapsed, but we will rebuild them with dressed stone; the sycamore fig trees have been cut down, but we will replace them with cedars.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–7; Isaiah 17:1–5; Isaiah 7:8"> But the Lord has strengthened Rezin's enemies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Rezin's enemies would be the Assyrians.">b</a></sup>against Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Israel”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup>and has encouraged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; 2 Chronicles 28:18; PSALMS 79:7"> Arameans from the east and Philistines from the west have greedily swallowed Israel up. In all of this he is still angry and his hand is still raised.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Meaning that in these events God is still displaying his anger and it is not over yet. Also in verses 17 and 21.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 5:3"> But the people didn't return to the one who punished them; they didn't come to worship the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 19:15; Revelation 18:8; Hosea 1:6"> So in just a single day the Lord will chop off Israel's head and tail, palm branch and reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Matthew 24:24; 1 Samuel 9:6"> Elders and honorable people are the head, and prophets who teach lies are the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 3:12; Matthew 23:16–36"> The people's leaders have misled them; those who were guided by them are confused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Isaiah 10:6; Isaiah 5:25"> As a result the Lord isn't pleased with their young men, he has no compassion for their orphans and widows, because all of them are hypocrites and do what's evil—they all talk stupidly. In all of this he is still angry and his hand is still raised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 83:14; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 5:24"> For wickedness blazes like a fire, burning up brambles and thorns, setting the forest on fire, sending a column of smoke swirling upwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Micah 7:6; Joel 2:2"> The anger of the Lord Almighty burns the land. People are like fuel for the fire, and nobody tries to help anyone else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10"> People destroy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Destroy”: literally, “devour.”">e</a></sup>others on the right, yet they're still hungry for more; people destroy others on the left, but they're still not satisfied. In the end they even destroy themselves!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Destroy themselves”: literally, “devour the flesh of their own arm.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Galatians 5:15; Isaiah 9:12"> Manasseh destroys Ephraim, and Ephraim destroys Manasseh. Together they turn to destroy Judah. In all of this he is still angry and his hand is still raised.</span>
</div>
</div>
