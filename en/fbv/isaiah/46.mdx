<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:31; Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1"> Listen to me, descendants of Jacob, all those who are left of the people of Israel. I have looked after you since you were born, carrying you from birth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Isaiah 40:25; PSALMS 89:6"> Can you think of anyone is like me? Can you consider anyone to be my equal? Can you compare anyone with me, as if we were alike?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 40:19–20; Habakkuk 2:18–20"> There are those who extravagantly tip out gold from their bags, and weigh out silver on the scales, and hire a goldsmith to make them a god they can bow down to and worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 46:1"> They lift the idol onto their shoulders, carry it along, and then put it in place. It stays there and doesn't move. Even when people cry out to it for help, it doesn't answer—it can't save them from their troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:18–21; PSALMS 115:8; Ezekiel 18:28"> Remember this, and act like men! Think about it, you rebels!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 111:4; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 45:21–22"> Remember what I've done for you since the beginning, for I am God, and there is no God apart from me. I am God, and there is none like me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; PSALMS 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> I make known the end from the very beginning, declaring from ancient times what the future will bring. Whatever I plan will take place; I will accomplish everything I wish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 14:24–27; Job 23:13"> I'm calling a bird of prey from the east, a man from a distant country who will carry out my plan.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Once again this is taken to refer to Cyrus.">b</a></sup>I have spoken, and I will make sure it happens. I have made my plan, and I will carry it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 46:3; PSALMS 76:5"> Listen to me, you stubborn people, who are such a long way from doing what's right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 61:3"> Very shortly I am going to make things right—it won't be long. I will come with my salvation without delay—I will save Zion to demonstrate my glory to Israel.</span>
