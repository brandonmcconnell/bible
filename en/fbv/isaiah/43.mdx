<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; Isaiah 43:7; Zechariah 13:9"> But now this is what the Lord says to Jacob, the one who created you; to Israel, the one who formed you: “Don't be afraid! I have saved you! I have called you by name; you are mine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:9; PSALMS 66:12"> When you walk through the water, I will be with you; and when you go through the rivers, they won't flood over you. When you walk through fire, you will not be burned; the flames will not set you on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:15; 2 Chronicles 14:9–14"> For I am the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior. I gave Egypt to pay for your freedom; I traded Ethiopia and Seba for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 31:3"> Because you are so valuable to me, because I honor you, and because I love you, I give people in exchange for you, nations in exchange for your lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 49:12; Luke 13:29"> Don't be afraid, for I am with you! I will bring you and your children from the east and the west, and gather you together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18; Jeremiah 3:14; Jeremiah 3:18–19"> I will tell the north, ‘Hand them over!’ and the south, ‘Don't stop them!’ Bring my sons back from far away and my daughters from distant lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:21; Ephesians 1:12"> Bring back everyone who bears my name, those I created for my honor, those I formed and made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:18–20"> Bring back those who have eyes but are blind, those who have ears but are deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:10; Isaiah 44:7–9; Isaiah 41:21–26"> Have all the nations gather together! Have all the peoples assemble! Who among them could have said this, and predict what was going to happen? Have them bring their witnesses to prove that they're right. Then have them listen, and say, ‘It's true!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This refers back to the previous chapter and the inability of the idols to predict the future, as God has just done. Clearly nobody could meet God's challenge.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 46:8–9"> However, you are my witnesses, the Lord declares, and my chosen servant, so that you can think about it, and believe me and understand that I am God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“I am God”: literally, “I am he.” Also in verse 25.">b</a></sup>No god preceded me, and none will come after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:4; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 45:21–22"> I, yes I am the Lord, and there is no Savior apart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:12; PSALMS 81:9–10; Isaiah 43:10"> I predicted what was going to happen, then I saved you, then I announced it—there was no foreign god among you that did this. You are my witnesses that I am God, declares the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:27; Isaiah 46:10; PSALMS 90:2"> I am God from the beginning.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“I am God from the beginning”: literally, “Before the day was I am he.”">c</a></sup>No one can snatch anybody from my hand. No one can reverse what I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 23:13; Jeremiah 51:24"> This is what the Lord, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, says: For your sake I will send attackers against Babylon and bring them down. All the Babylonians will be like fugitives, escaping in the ships they're so proud of.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew of the last part of this verse is obscure.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 43:3; Matthew 25:34; Isaiah 48:17"> I am the Lord, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, and your King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 43:2; PSALMS 77:19"> This is what the Lord says, the one who makes a way through the sea, a path through the mighty waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:4–9; Isaiah 1:31; Ezekiel 38:8–18"> the one who brought out a great army with its horses and chariots and laid them down, never to rise again, snuffed out like a burning wick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> But don't dwell on the past; don't concentrate on what happened back then.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:6; Isaiah 42:9; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Just look at something new I'm going to do now! In fact it's started already. Can't you see it? Yes, I'm making a way through the wilderness, rivers in the desert!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:17; Isaiah 48:21; Isaiah 49:10"> The wild animals will be grateful to me—the jackals and the owls—because I'm providing water in the wilderness, rivers in the desert, so my people, my chosen people, can drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Hebrews 13:15; PSALMS 102:18"> I made this people for myself so that through their praise for me they could make me known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Malachi 3:14; Malachi 1:13"> But you haven't called on me for help, Jacob. You've grown tired of me, Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Zechariah 7:5–6; Malachi 3:8"> You haven't brought me sheep for burnt offerings; you haven't honored me with your sacrifices. I haven't burdened you by asking for grain offerings; I haven't tired you out by demanding incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Isaiah 1:14"> You haven't used your money to buy scented calamus;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Calamus”: a sweet-smelling plant used in perfumes and ancient medicines.">e</a></sup>you have not pleased me with the fat of your sacrifices. Instead you have burdened me with your sins, and tired me out with your guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:12; Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 10:17"> I, yes I am the God who wipes out your sins because of who I am, and who doesn't remember your sins any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 43:9; Romans 8:33"> Remind me of the evidence so we can come to a decision together! Present your case to prove that you're right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 28:7; Jeremiah 5:31; Ezekiel 22:25–28"> Your very first father sinned, and your leaders rebelled against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Isaiah 65:15; Zechariah 8:13"> So I treated the priests of the sanctuary with contempt, and I handed Jacob over to be destroyed, and Israel to be scorned.”</span>
