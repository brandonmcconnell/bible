<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> Watch! A king is coming who does what's right when he rules, and those who rule under him will act fairly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 9:6"> Each one will be like a shelter from the wind, like a protection from the storm, like rivers of water in the dry desert, like the shade of a large rock in a sun-baked<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Sun-baked”: literally, “faint, weary”—in other words an oppressively hot climate that causes fatigue.">a</a></sup>land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> Then everyone with eyes will be able to truly see, and everyone who has ears will be able to truly hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> Those whose minds are impulsive will be sensible and think things through; those who stammer will speak clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; PSALMS 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> No longer will fools be called respectable or crooks highly-regarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For fools only talk foolishness, thinking evil things. They act in opposition to the Lord, misrepresenting him in what they say. They refuse to give food to the hungry and deny water to the thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> Cheats use evil methods; they devise schemes to ruin the poor with lies, even when right is on the side of the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> But noble people act unselfishly; they keep to their principles of generosity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Get up, you women who are lying around relaxing, and pay attention to what I'm saying! You ladies without a care in the world, listen to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> In just over a year, you who think yourselves so safe will tremble with fear. The grape harvest is going to fail and there'll be no harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Shudder, you women relaxing! Tremble, you ladies who feel so safe! Strip off your clothes, bare yourselves and put on sackcloth around your waists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> Beat your breasts in sadness over the loss of your lovely fields, your productive vines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> over the loss of the land of my people. Mourn because it's now overgrown with thorns and brambles, mourn because of the loss of all the happy homes and joyful towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> The palace is abandoned, the crowded town is deserted. Castles and watch towers will forever become homes loved by wild donkeys, and places where sheep graze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> until the Spirit is poured out on us from above, and the desert becomes like a good field full of crops, and a good field will grow like a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> Then people living in the desert will practice justice, and those living among the fields will do what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; PSALMS 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> Living right will bring peace and security that endures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> My people will live in peace, safe and secure in their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> Even though hail can destroy a forest, and a town can be demolished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> you will be happy as you sow your crops beside the waters and let your cattle and donkeys roam freely.</span>
