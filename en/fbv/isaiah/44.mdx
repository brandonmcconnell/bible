<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; Romans 11:5–6; Jeremiah 30:10"> But now listen, Jacob, my servant, Israel, the one I've chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 43:1; Isaiah 46:3–4"> This is what the Lord says, the one who made you, who formed you in the womb, and who will help you: Don't be afraid, Jacob, my servant, Jeshurun,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Jeshurun”: another name for Israel.">a</a></sup>the one I've chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; Isaiah 59:21"> For I'm going to pour out water on the thirsty land, and streams on the dry ground. I'm going to pour out my Spirit on your descendants, and my blessing on your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; PSALMS 1:3; PSALMS 92:13–15"> They will grow up among the grass, like willows beside streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:5; Zechariah 13:9; 1 Peter 2:9"> One of them will say, “I belong to the Lord,” while another will call himself by the name Jacob, and yet another will write on his hand, “Belonging to the Lord,” and will name himself Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> This is what the Lord says, the King and Redeemer of Israel, the Lord Almighty: I am the first and I am the last, and there is no God besides me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:26; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 46:9–10"> Who is like me? Let them announce it, make a declaration, and demonstrate it before me. For I was the one who long ago established a people and predicted its future. Let them predict what's going to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; 1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> Don't tremble, don't be afraid! Didn't I tell you long ago what I was going to do? Didn't I explain it? You are my witnesses. Is there any God besides me? There is no other Rock—I don't know any!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:14–15; PSALMS 97:7"> All those who make idols are stupid; these things they love so much don't bring them any benefit. Those people who believe in idols can't see this, and they don't know anything, making them look foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Habakkuk 2:18; Acts 19:26"> Who makes a god, who molds an idol that's no use to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:17; Isaiah 45:16; PSALMS 97:7"> Everyone who makes idols should be ashamed of themselves. Idol-makers are just human beings! Bring them all together and have them stand up, trembling in shame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:6–7; Isaiah 40:19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> The blacksmith makes an iron tool for woodcarving. Working over hot coals, he hammers it into shape as hard as he can. He gets hungry and loses his strength, and because he doesn't drink he grows weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:4–5; PSALMS 115:5–7; Acts 17:29"> The woodworker measures out a piece of wood, and draws an image on it. He carves it with a chisel and uses a compass to make an outline. He creates an idol that looks like a person, someone beautiful, to be put in a shrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:20; Jeremiah 10:3–8; Hosea 4:12"> He chops down cedars, or takes a cypress or an oak. He lets them grow strong in the forest. He plants a fir tree, and the rain makes it grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; Revelation 9:20; Judges 2:19"> Some wood he burns, to keep him warm and to bake his bread. Then he uses some of the same wood to make a god to worship, an idol he bows down to!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So he burns part of the wood to roast his meat to eat, and to warm him up, and says, “Ah! Look at that fire that keeps me warm!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; 1 Kings 18:26; Daniel 6:27"> Then he uses the rest of the wood to make himself a god, an idol he bows down to in worship and prays to, saying, “Save me, for you are my god!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> How ignorant they are, how dumb! Their eyes have been plastered over so they can't see, their minds closed so they don't have insight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; 1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5"> They can't think things over, they don't have the wisdom or the understanding to say, “Some of the wood I burned in the fire—I used it to bake my bread and roast my meat to eat. The rest of it I used to make a disgusting idol, and I bow down in worship to a block of wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Hosea 12:1"> He is feeding on ashes, seduced by the deceptive thinking of his mind. He can't save himself, and he doesn't even ask, “Isn't this idol I'm holding just a lie?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; Isaiah 49:15–16; Isaiah 43:15"> “Remember all this, Jacob and Israel, for you are my servant. I made you, Israel, you are my servant. I won't forget you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> I have wiped away your acts of rebellion as if they were like a cloud, your sins as if they were like the mist. Come back to me, for I have set you free.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; PSALMS 69:34"> Sing in celebration, you heavens, for the Lord has done this; cry out loud, you depths of the earth. Shout with joy, you mountains, you forests and every tree. For the Lord has set Jacob free, and his glory is shown in Israel!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 54:5"> This is what the Lord says, your Redeemer, who shaped you in the womb: “I am the Lord, the Creator of everything. I alone made the heavens, and I myself formed the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isaiah poetically speaks of the heavens being “stretched out,” and the earth being “spread out.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremiah 50:36"> I am the one who proves the signs of false prophets are wrong. I am the one who makes fools of fortune-tellers. I turn the wisdom of the wise upside down, and make their knowledge ridiculous.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Ridiculous”: or “nonsensical.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–38; Isaiah 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> But I confirm the messages given by my servants, and fulfill what my messengers say. They state, ‘Jerusalem will be inhabited again, the towns of Judah will be rebuilt, your ruins will be repaired.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 42:15; Jeremiah 51:36; Jeremiah 50:38"> When I order deep waters, ‘Dry up!’ I will make sure the rivers dry up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–3; Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 45:13"> When I say to Cyrus, ‘You are my shepherd,’ he will carry out everything I wish. He will give the order, ‘Jerusalem shall be rebuilt,’ and he will say, ‘the Temple shall be restored.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
