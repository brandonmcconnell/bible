<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:1; Luke 20:9; John 15:1"> Let me sing a song for my love, about his vineyard. My love owned a vineyard on a productive hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Mark 11:13"> He dug it over, cleared the ground of stones, and planted it with the very best vines. In the middle of it he built a watchtower, and he also cut out a winepress from the rock. Then he waited for a good harvest of grapes, but it only produced wild, sour grapes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; PSALMS 51:4; Micah 6:2–3"> “Now, you people who live in Jerusalem and Judah, please judge between me and my vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:37; Micah 6:3–4; Jeremiah 2:5"> What more could I have done for my vineyard than I've already done? When I looked for sweet grapes, why did it only produce sour ones?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Luke 21:24; Isaiah 28:18"> So let me tell you what I'm going to do to my vineyard. I'll remove its hedge, and it will be destroyed. I'll tear down its wall, and it will be trampled underfoot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23–25; Jeremiah 25:11; Isaiah 24:1–3"> I'll turn it into a wasteland. It won't be pruned or weeded—it will be overgrown with brambles and thorns. I'll order the clouds not to rain on it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 80:8–11; Jeremiah 12:10; James 5:4"> Israel is the vineyard of the Lord Almighty, and the people of Judah are the plants in his garden that made him happy. Yet while he hoped for justice, he only saw injustice; he hoped people would live right, but he only heard the cries of those who were suffering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 2:2; Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> Tragedy is coming to you who buy house upon house and field upon field, joining them all together until no one else has anywhere to live and you live alone in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 23:38; Isaiah 22:14; Amos 5:11"> I heard the Lord Almighty declare: You can be sure that many houses are destined to become ruins, and beautiful mansions destined to become uninhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 2:16; Haggai 1:6"> Ten acres of vineyard will only produce six gallons of wine, and a measure of seed only a tenth of that in grain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “a homer of seed will only produce an ephah of grain.”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–30; Romans 13:13; Proverbs 20:1"> Tragedy is coming to you who get up early in the morning wanting a drink, and who stay up late drinking wine until you're drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:11–14; PSALMS 28:5; Amos 6:4–6"> At their feasts you have lyres and harps, tambourines and flutes, and wine, but you don't ever consider what the Lord is doing, and you don't recognize his help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 27:11"> As a result my people will be exiled for their lack of understanding.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“For their lack of understanding”: or “unawares.”">b</a></sup>Their honored leaders will starve, and the crowds will be dying of thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 30:16; Luke 21:34; Habakkuk 2:5"> The grave's appetite increases, its mouth opens wide, and Jerusalem's nobility and the masses will go down into it, along with the rowdy, drunken mobs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 2:9; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> Everyone will be brought down, everyone will be humbled; the proud will lower their eyes in humiliation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 46:10; Isaiah 12:4; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> But the Lord Almighty will be vindicated because he does what is right; the holy God will be shown to be holy because of his goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:6; Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 7:25"> Lambs will graze as in their own pasture; fattened livestock and goats will feed among the ruins of the rich.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Septuagint reading.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Ezekiel 13:22; Jeremiah 5:31"> Tragedy is coming to you who drag along your sins behind you with cords made of lies, pulling with ropes your cartful of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:22; Jeremiah 17:15; Jeremiah 23:36"> You are among the people who say, “God should hurry up! Why doesn't God get a move on with what he's doing so we can see it? Why doesn't the Holy One of Israel execute his plan? Let's see it happen so we can understand what it is!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Timothy 3:1–5; Luke 16:15"> Tragedy is coming to you who say evil is good, and good is evil; who turn darkness into light and light into darkness; who make bitter sweet, and sweet bitter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:18–20; Proverbs 3:7; Romans 12:16"> Tragedy is coming to you who are wise in your own eyes and think you're so clever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11; Habakkuk 2:15; Isaiah 28:7"> Tragedy is coming to you who are wine-drinking champions, and experts at mixing alcoholic drinks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; James 5:6; PSALMS 94:21"> you who set the guilty free for a bribe, and yet deny justice to the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 30:12; Job 18:16; Joel 2:5"> In the same way fire burns up stubble and dry grass falls down in the flames, so their roots will decay and their flowers disintegrate into dust. For they have rejected the law of the Lord Almighty, and have treated with contempt what the Holy One of Israel has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:4; Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 9:17"> That's why the Lord burns with anger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="There are many references to the Lord's anger in Isaiah, as in other Old Testament books. This should not be equated to human anger which is often “out of control” and vindictive. The Lord's anger is an expression of his extreme displeasure, couched in human language so we can understand to some extent the impact of human sin on the Lord. Nor is it a question of God taking personal offense, but rather his concern as to what continued sin does to us, and a desire to do all he can to heal the damage sin causes.">d</a></sup>against his people. He has lifted up his hand and hit them, shaking the mountains, and leaving their corpses lying like refuse in the streets. Despite all this, his anger is not finished, and his hand is still lifted up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Zechariah 10:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 7:18"> He will send a signal to the distant nations, and will whistle for those living at the ends of the earth. See how quickly they respond, how speedily they come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:7–8; Job 12:18; Job 12:21"> None of them gets tired or stumbles; none of them rests or sleeps. No belt comes loose, and no sandal strap breaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 45:5; Nahum 2:3–4; Ezekiel 21:9–11"> Their arrows are already sharpened, and all their bows have been strung. The hooves of their horses are hard as flint; their chariot wheels spin like a whirlwind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 42:22; Zechariah 11:3; Micah 5:8"> They roar like lions, like young lions. They growl, and pounce on their prey. They drag it off so it can't be rescued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 8:22; Joel 2:10; Jeremiah 4:23–28"> At that time they will roar over their prey like the roaring of the sea. Anyone who looks out over the land will see only darkness and distress—even the sunlight will be darkened by clouds.</span>
</div>
