<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Zechariah 9:1; Amos 1:3–5"> A message about Damascus. Look, Damascus will cease to exist as a city. Instead it will become a pile of ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:5; Jeremiah 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> The towns of Aroer will be abandoned. Flocks will live in the streets and rest there, because there won't be anyone to chase them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> The fortified city will disappear from Ephraim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, Samaria, capital city of the northern tribes symbolized by Ephraim, will be destroyed.">a</a></sup>Damascus will no longer be a kingdom, and those that are left of the Arameans will be like the lost glory of Israel, declares the Lord Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> At that time the glory of Jacob will fade away; he will lose his strength.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“He will lose his strength”: literally, “the fat of his flesh will become lean.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> It will look as empty as fields after reapers have harvested the grain, gathering up the grain in their arms. It will be like when people pick the heads of grain in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> Yet there will be some left behind, like an olive tree that has been shaken—two or three ripe olives are left at the top of the tree, four or five on its lower branches, declares the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> At that time people will pay attention to their Creator and look to the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> They won't believe in the altars they built and the idols they made; they will not look to the Asherah poles or the altars of incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> At that time their fortified cities will be like places left to be taken over by bushes and trees, just as they were previously abandoned when the Israelites invaded.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The reference is made to the time when the Israelites conquered the land. This is made explicit in the Septuagint which states that the cities will be abandoned just as the Amorites and the Hivites had done when confronted by the Israelites.">c</a></sup>The country will become completely desolate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> You have forgotten the God who saves you; you have not remembered the Rock who protects you. So, even though you plant beautiful plants and grow exotic vines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> even though you make them grow on the day that you plant them, and have them blossom in the morning that you sow them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Clearly an impossibility, and is to be taken as a symbol of the rapid “cultivation” of pagan fertility religions.">d</a></sup>your harvest will heap of trouble on a day of grief and pain that cannot be cured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke 21:25; PSALMS 18:4"> Disaster is coming to the many nations that growl, growling like the raging sea! Disaster is coming to the peoples who roar, roaring like thundering waters!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="While the nation is not named, this prophecy probably applies to Assyria.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 9:5; PSALMS 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> The nations roar like the roaring of crashing waves. But he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“He”: referring to the Lord.">f</a></sup>confronts them, and they run far away, blown by the wind like chaff on the mountains, like tumbleweeds driven by a storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremiah 13:25"> Sudden terror comes in the evening! By morning, they're gone! This is what happens to those who loot us, the fate of those who plunder us.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
