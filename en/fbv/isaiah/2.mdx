<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Habakkuk 1:1"> This is the vision that Isaiah, son of Amoz, saw about Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> In the last days the mountain where the Lord's Temple stands will be recognized as the highest of all mountains, rising above other hills. Many from the other nations will travel to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zechariah 8:20–23"> People will come and say, “Let's go up to the mountain of the Lord, to the Temple of the God of Jacob. There God will teach us his ways and we will follow his directions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “walk in his paths.”">a</a></sup>God's teachings will spread out from Zion, his word from Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:18; Micah 4:3; Isaiah 11:6–9"> The Lord will decide the cases of the nations; he will settle arguments between nations. They will hammer their swords and turn them into plow blades, and their spears into pruning hooks. Nations will not fight against each other anymore; they will no longer learn methods of warfare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Luke 1:79; Isaiah 60:19–20"> Come, you Israelites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “house of Jacob.”">b</a></sup>let's walk in the Lord's light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Kings 1:2; PSALMS 106:35"> For you, Lord, have given up on your people the Israelites, because they have adopted pagan practices from the East, they use spells like the Philistines, and make friends with foreigners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Make friends with foreigners”: Hebrew uncertain, probably “shake hands with children of foreigners.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Isaiah 30:16; Isaiah 31:1"> Their country is full of silver and gold, and endless wealth. Their land is full of horses; and they have an endless amount of chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremiah 2:28"> Their country is full of idols; they bow down and worship what they have made themselves—produced by their own hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> These people will be brought down and humiliated—Lord, don't forgive them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Isaiah 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> Run away into rock caves, hide underground from the terrifying presence of the Lord, from the glory of his majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15–16; PSALMS 18:27; Isaiah 2:17"> Those who look with arrogance will be brought low; those who are proud will be humbled. On that day only the Lord will be lifted high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> The Lord has set aside a day when he will deal with the proud and arrogant. He will end all self-glorification, bringing it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Zechariah 11:1–2; Ezekiel 31:3–12"> He will cut down the cedars of Lebanon, tall and high, and all the great oaks of Bashan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:25; Isaiah 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> He will bring down the tall mountains and the high hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 25:12"> He will tear down every high tower and every defensive wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; Isaiah 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> He will wipe out all the commercial ships of Tarshish as well as the pleasure vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:29–30; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 13:11"> The arrogant will be humbled; the proud will be brought low. On that day only the Lord will be lifted high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 21:9"> Idols will completely vanish away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8"> People will run away into rock caves and holes in the ground to try and hide from the terrifying presence of the Lord, from the glory of his majesty, when he arrives to shake the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 46:6"> On that day the people will take the idols of silver and gold that they made to worship and throw them away to the rats<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally a “dig pit,” so it can refer to any burrowing animal.">d</a></sup>and the bats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Song of Songs 2:14; Isaiah 2:10"> They will run to cracks in the rocks and gaps on the cliffs to try and hide from the terrifying presence of the Lord, from the glory of his majesty, when he arrives to shake the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; PSALMS 146:3; James 4:14"> Don't bother trusting human beings who only live for a while.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Only live for a while”: literally, “who have breath in their nostrils.”">e</a></sup>How much do they count?</span>
</div>
