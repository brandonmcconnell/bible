<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Tragedy is coming to you, you destroyer who has not experienced destruction yourself, you deceiver who has not experienced deception yourself! When you have finished with your destroying, you will be destroyed yourself. When you are finished with your deceiving, you will be deceived yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> Lord, please be kind to us; we put our confidence in you. Be the strength we rely on every morning; be our salvation in times of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> When you roar, the peoples run away; when you prepare for action, the nations scatter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> You plunder defeated enemy armies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Defeated enemy armies”: supplied for clarity. This is usually taken to refer to the defeat of the Assyrians under Sennacherib.">a</a></sup>like caterpillars eating up plants; like an attack of swarming locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> The Lord is praised for he lives in highest heaven; he has filled Zion with justice and right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> He will be your constant support throughout your lives an abundant source of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge. Reverence for the Lord is what makes Zion rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> But look! Even your bravest soldiers are crying loudly in the street; the messengers you sent to ask for peace are weeping bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> Your highways are deserted; nobody's traveling on your roads anymore. He breaks the treaty;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Treaty”: or “covenant.” In the context here it probably refers to a treaty between Israel and Assyria that the Assyrians have broken, not caring what anybody thinks.">b</a></sup>he despises the witnesses;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Witnesses”: Dead Sea Scrolls reading. Hebrew MT reads “cities.”">c</a></sup>he doesn't care about anybody.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Israel”: literally, “The land.”">d</a></sup>is in mourning and fades away; Lebanon withers in shame; the fields of Sharon have become a desert; the forests of Bashan and Carmel have shed their leaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> “But now I'm going to intervene!” says the Lord. “I'm prepared to act! I will show myself to be above all others!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> All you give birth to is only dry grass, all you deliver is just stubble. Your breath is a fire that will burn you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> You people will be burned to ashes like thorns that are cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> Those of you who are far away, recognize what I have accomplished; those of you who are nearby, recognize how powerful I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> The sinners who live in Zion tremble with fear; those who are irreligious are overcome with terror. They ask,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“They ask”: supplied for clarity.">e</a></sup>“Who can live with this fire that consumes everything? Who can live among such everlasting burning?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> Those who live right and speak the truth, those who refuse to profit from extortion and refuse to take bribes, who don't listen to plots to kill people, who close their eyes rather than look at evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> They will live on high; they will be protected by the mountain fortresses; they will always be provided with food and will always have water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> You will see the king in his wonderful appearance, and you will view a land that stretches into the distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; PSALMS 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> In your mind you will think about the terrifying things that were expected, and then ask yourself, “Where are the enemy officials—the scribes who were to record events, the treasurers who were to weigh the looted money, the surveyors who were to count and destroy the towers?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This verse has been expanded to supply clarity to what seems to be the intended meaning—that the Assyrian officials who had been brought in expectation of a victory over Jerusalem have all disappeared.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> You won't see these offensive people anymore with their barbaric language that sounds like someone stammering and is impossible to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> On the contrary, you'll see Zion as a festival city. You will view Jerusalem as a quiet and peaceful place. It will be like a tent that's never taken down, whose tent-pegs are never pulled up, whose guy ropes never snap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> Right here our majestic Lord will be like a place of broad rivers and waters that no enemy ship with oars can cross—no great ship can pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king. He is the one who will save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 7:8; PSALMS 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> The rigging on your ship hangs loose so the mast isn't secure and the sail can't be spread. Then all the looted treasure you're carrying will be divided among the victors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Among the victors”: supplied for clarity.">g</a></sup>—even those who are lame will have their share.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> Nobody in Israel will say, “I'm sick,” and those who live there will have their guilt removed.</span>
