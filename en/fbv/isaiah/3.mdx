<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> Take a look! The Lord, the Lord Almighty, is going to take away from Jerusalem and from Judah everything they rely on—all their stocks of food and all means of supplying water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> their strong fighters and soldiers, leaders and prophets, fortune-tellers and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> army captains, state officials, counselors, craftsmen, masters of magic, and experts in the occult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> I will give them boys as their leaders who will rule over them in childish ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> People will mistreat one another, person against person, neighbor against neighbor; the young will assault the old, and the dregs of society will attack those who are reputable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> A man will grab hold of one of his brothers in their father's house, and tell him, “You've got a coat, you can be our leader! Take over the running of this pile of ruins!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> But immediately his brother will shout back, “No, not me! I can't take care of this family's problems. In my house I don't have food or clothes. Don't put me in charge as your leader!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem has collapsed and Judah has fallen because of what they said and did in opposition to the Lord, rebelling right in front of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> The look on their faces proves what they've done, proclaiming their sin just like Sodom—they don't even bother to try and hide it! Tragedy is coming to them! They have brought disaster down upon themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Tell those who live right that they will be fine, for they will be happy to receive the reward for what they have done.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“They will be happy to receive the reward for what they have done”: literally, “they will eat the fruit of their deeds.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; PSALMS 62:12"> But tragedy comes to the wicked, for what they did will be done to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> Leaders who act in childish ways mistreat my people; women rule over them. My people, your leaders are misleading you, confusing you about which way to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> The Lord stands up to prosecute his case. The Lord stands up to judge the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; PSALMS 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> The Lord comes to give his judgment against the elders and leaders of his people: You are the ones who have destroyed my vineyard; your houses are full with everything you've stolen from the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; PSALMS 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> Why have you crushed my people? Why have you ground the faces of the poor into the dirt? the Lord Almighty demands to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> The Lord says, The women of Zion are so conceited, walking with their heads held high, giving flirty glances, tripping along with their ankle bracelets jingling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> So the Lord will make their heads full of scabs, and the Lord will expose their private parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> At that time the Lord will remove their fine ornaments: ankle bracelets, headbands, crescent necklaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> pendant earrings, bracelets, veils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> headdresses, bangles, sashes, perfume holders, lucky charms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> rings, nose rings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> festival clothes, gowns, cloaks, handbags,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> hand mirrors, fine linen underwear, head wraps, and shawls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> What will happen is that instead of smelling of perfume, she will stink. Instead of a wearing a sash, she will be tied with a rope. Instead of styled hair, she will be bald. Instead of fine clothes, she will wear sackcloth. Instead of looking beautiful, she will be shamed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Shamed”: Dead Sea Scroll reading rather than the often assumed reading of “branded.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> Your men will be killed by the sword; your soldiers will die in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> The gates of Zion will cry and mourn. The city will be like a banished woman sitting on the ground.</span>
