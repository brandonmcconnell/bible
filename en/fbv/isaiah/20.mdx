<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Jeremiah 25:29–30; Amos 1:8"> In the year when Sargon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is the only Biblical mention of Sargon II, king of Assyria. However, his campaign against Ashdod in 711 BC has been confirmed by archaeology.">a</a></sup>king of Assyria, sent his army commander to attack the town of Ashdod and conquered it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Matthew 3:4; 1 Samuel 19:24"> at that time the Lord spoke through Isaiah, son of Amoz. He told him, “Take off the sackcloth clothes from your body and remove your sandals.” Isaiah did so and went around naked and barefoot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:5–6"> Then the Lord said, “In the same way that my servant Isaiah has gone naked and barefoot for three years as a sign and a warning against Egypt and Ethiopia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:4; Jeremiah 13:26; Jeremiah 13:22"> so shall the king of Assyria lead the Egyptian prisoners and the Ethiopian exiles, both young and old, naked and barefoot. Their buttocks will be bare, to Egypt's shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 Kings 18:21"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring to the Philistines in Ashdod.">b</a></sup>will be discouraged and humiliated because they had put their hope in Ethiopia and proudly trusted in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Hebrews 2:3"> At that time the people living in the coastal lands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The area including Ashdod occupied by the Philistines.">c</a></sup>will say, ‘Look what has happened to those we were depending on! We ran to them for help to save us from the king of Assyria. We don't stand a chance!’”</span>
</div>
