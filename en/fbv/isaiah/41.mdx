<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13"> Listen to me in silence, you people who live in lands beyond the sea. Let the nations regain their strength. Then let them come and speak, and let us join together for judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="From what follows, and the context of previous verses, this chapter appears to be a call to decide between the true God and idols.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; 2 Samuel 22:43; Ezra 1:2"> Who has encouraged this one from the east who is called into God's service<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Into God's service”: literally, “to his feet.”">b</a></sup>to do what is right?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The prophecy is usually taken to refer to King Cyrus.">c</a></sup>He gives him nations, and helps him trample kings underfoot. He makes them like dust with his sword, and turns them into chaff with his bow, blown before him on the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He chases them down, and goes on unharmed, not following the usual routes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who has achieved and done this, calling into existence each generation from the beginning of time? I, the Lord, the first and the last, I am the one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; PSALMS 65:8; PSALMS 66:3"> The islands watch in fear, the distant lands tremble. They get together and consult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> They all help each other, encouraging one another to “Be strong!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> The craftsman encourages the goldsmith; the one who beats out the metal with a hammer encourages the one hitting the anvil, saying the soldering is good. They nail the idol down so it won't fall over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> But you, Israel, my servant, Jacob, the ones I have chosen, descendants of my friend Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; PSALMS 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> I brought you back from distant lands, I called you back from faraway places, telling you, “You are my servants.” I have chosen you, and I have not abandoned you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Don't be afraid, for I am with you! Don't be frightened for I, your God, will make you strong, and I will certainly help you. I will support you with my strong hand, acting for what is right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> See—everyone who is angry with you will be ashamed and disgraced. Those who fight against you will end up as nothing and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> Even though you look for your enemies, you won't find them. Those who attack you will become absolutely nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; PSALMS 73:23; PSALMS 63:8"> For I the Lord will grab you by the hand and tell you, “Don't be afraid! I myself will help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; PSALMS 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Don't be frightened, Jacob, you little worm, you people of Israel, for I will help you,” declared the Lord your Savior, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> Look! I will make you into a new and sharp threshing device,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Threshing device”: used for processing grain.">d</a></sup>with many pointed teeth. You will thresh the mountains and crush the hills, turning them into chaff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> You will throw them into the air, the wind will carry them away, and a storm will scatter them. Then you will be happy in the Lord, and boast about the Holy One of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> The poor and needy search for water, but don't find any—their tongues are dry with thirst. I, the Lord, will respond to them; I, the God of Israel, won't abandon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I will open up rivers in the desolate highlands and springs in the valleys. I will make pools in the wilderness and springs in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I will plant cedars, acacias, myrtles, and olive trees in the desert. I will place evergreens in the desert, firs and box trees together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> In this way everyone can see and know, they can think about it and draw the conclusion that it is the Lord who has done this, that the Holy One of Israel made it happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> “Now you submit your case,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Regarding the abilities of idols in contrast to the evidence of the actions of God already mentioned">e</a></sup>says the Lord. “Present your evidence,” says the King of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> “Let them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Referring to the idols, the false gods.">f</a></sup>come and tell us what's about to happen. Let them explain the things of the past, so that we can think about them and discover the final outcome. Or they can tell us about the future.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Predict what's coming so we can know for sure that you are gods. At least do something, good or bad, to impress us when we see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> But look at you! You're nothing, and you can't do anything! Anyone who chooses you is disgusting!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I have encouraged the one who comes from the north. He is from the east,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="If this prophecy is taken to refer to Cyrus (see verse 2), then he comes from the east (literally “the rising sun”), but his entrance into Palestine would be from the north.">g</a></sup>and will respect me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Respect me”: literally, “call on my name,” often associated with the idea of worship.">h</a></sup>He will tread on rulers as if they were mortar, like a potter treading on clay.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Both mortar and clay were mixed using the feet.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who announced this beforehand so that we could know about it? Who let us know in the past, so that now we could say: ‘He was right’? None of you announced it, none of you predicted it, and nobody heard you say a word!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Referring to the inability of idols to predict the future.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> I was the first to announce to Zion: ‘Look, here they are!’ I was the one who brought to Jerusalem the good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> I look at these idols, and find there is no one who can say anything.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Expanded for clarity.">k</a></sup>None of them can give advice; when I ask them something, they can't even answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> Look at them! They're all evil, deceptive things. They can't do anything! They're just idols full of hot air!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="“Hot air”: literally, “wind and emptiness.”">l</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>
