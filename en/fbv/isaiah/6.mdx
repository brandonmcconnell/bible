<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:41; Revelation 15:8; Revelation 7:15–17"> In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord seated in majesty on a high throne, and the train of his robe filled the Temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Ezekiel 1:11; PSALMS 104:4"> Seraphim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “shining ones.”">a</a></sup>stood above him, and each of them had six wings: They used two wings to cover their faces, two to cover their feet, and two to fly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; PSALMS 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> They were calling out to one another: Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Almighty; his glory fills the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; 2 Chronicles 5:13–1"> The sound of their shouts made the doorposts and doorsteps shake, and the Temple was filled with smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremiah 9:3–8"> I cried out, “I'm doomed! I'm going to die because I'm a man of unclean lips,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Unclean lips”: symbolizing imperfection.">b</a></sup>and I live among a people of unclean lips, for I've seen the King, the Lord Almighty!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> Then one of the seraphim flew over to me. He was holding a glowing coal that he had picked up with tongs from the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> He touched me on my mouth with it and told me, “Look! This has touched your lips, so now your guilt is taken away and your sin is forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> Then I heard the Lord asking, “Who shall I send? Who will go and speak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“And speak”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup>for us?” So I said, “I'm here! Please send me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> He replied, “Go and tell this people: ‘Keep on listening, but never understanding. Keep on seeing, but never comprehending.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> Make the minds of this people insensitive; make their ears deaf and shut their eyes. Otherwise they might see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their minds, and repent and be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; PSALMS 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> Then I asked, “How long will this last, Lord?” He replied, “Until towns are ruined and empty, houses are abandoned, and the land is devastated and destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> until the Lord sends the people far away and the country is totally deserted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> Even though a tenth of the population remains in the land, it will be destroyed again. But in the same way as the terebinth and oak trees leave stumps when they're cut down, so the holy seed will remain as a stump.”</span>
