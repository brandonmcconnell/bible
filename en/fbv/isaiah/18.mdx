<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; 2 Kings 19:9; Zephaniah 2:12"> Tragedy is coming to the land of whirring wings that lies along the rivers of Ethiopia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 18:7; Genesis 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 16:8"> They send messengers downriver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Probably referring to the Nile. Literally, “sea.”">a</a></sup>in papyrus boats. Swift messengers, go and take a message to a tall and smooth-skinned people, to a people feared by everyone, to a very powerful nation of conquerors, whose land is washed away by rivers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew of this verse is unclear and has been variously interpreted.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 26:11; Matthew 13:9"> All you people of the world, everyone who lives on earth—you will see when a banner is raised on the mountains, you will hear when a trumpet sounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Isaiah 26:21; Isaiah 18:7"> For this is what the Lord has told me: I will watch quietly from where I live, quiet as heat haze in sunlight, quiet as a mistcloud in the heat of harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Song of Songs 2:13; Ezekiel 17:6–10"> For before the harvest, after the flower is gone and becomes an unripe grape, he prunes the vine with a knife to take out the shoots and branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 32:4–6"> They will all be left as carrion for the birds of prey of the mountains, and for the wild animals. The birds will eat them in summer, and all the wild animals in winter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Acts 8:27–28; Zechariah 14:16–17"> At that time a gift will be brought to the Lord Almighty from a tall and smooth-skinned people, from a people feared by everyone, from a very powerful nation of conquerors, whose land is washed away by rivers. It will be brought to Mount Zion, the place identified with the Lord Almighty.</span>
</div>
