<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Watch out! The Lord is going to destroy the earth, to make it totally devastated. He's going to rip up the surface of the earth and scatter its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> It will happen the same for everybody—whether people or priests, servants or their masters, maids or their mistresses, buyers or sellers, lenders or borrowers, creditors or debtors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The earth will be completely laid waste and looted. This is what the Lord has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The earth dries up and withers away; the world shrivels up and withers away, the high and mighty people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The high and mighty people”: or, “the heavens.”">a</a></sup>shrivel up along with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> The earth is polluted by its people; they have flouted God's laws, violated his regulations, and broken the eternal agreement with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> That is why a curse is destroying the earth. The people suffer because of their guilt. The inhabitants of the earth are burned up and only a few survive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine dries up, and the vine withers. All the people celebrating groan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The happy sound of tambourines is over; the noise of the party-goers has stopped; the delightful harp music has finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> People don't sing any more as they drink wine, and the beer tastes bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> The chaotic city is falling apart; every house is barred shut to keep others out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> Crowds on the street are shouting, demanding to have wine. Joy turns into darkness. There's no happiness left on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> The city is in a horrible state of ruin; its gates have been broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> This is the way it's going to be all throughout the earth among the nations—only a few olives are left after the tree is shaken, only a few grapes are left to be gleaned after the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> These survivors shout aloud and sing for joy. From the west they praise the Lord's majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> From the east they glorify the Lord; from the sea shores they praise the name of the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> We hear songs coming from the ends of the earth, singing, “Glory to the God who does right.” But I'm miserable, miserable. Pity me! Deceitful people go on betraying, again and again.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In the Hebrew this line is literally, “The treacherous deal treacherously, with treachery the treacherous deal treacherously.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Terrors and pit-traps and snares are waiting for you, people of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; PSALMS 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> Those who run away in terror will fall into a pit-trap, and those who escape from the pit-trap will be caught in a snare. Heaven's windows<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Heaven's windows”: usually associated with rainfall/floods.">c</a></sup>are opened; earth's foundations shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is completely broken up; the earth is ripped apart, the earth is violently shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth staggers to and fro like a drunk, and sways this way and that like a shelter. The guilt of its rebelliousness weighs heavily on it, and it collapses—it won't rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> At that time the Lord will punish all the high heavenly beings and the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> They will be brought together, prisoners in a pit. They will be imprisoned, and eventually they will be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> The moon will be embarrassed and the sun will be ashamed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Because their light will seem so dim in comparison to the Lord's glory.">d</a></sup>for the Lord Almighty will reign in glory on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem in the presence of its leaders.</span>
</div>
