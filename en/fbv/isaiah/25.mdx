<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; PSALMS 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> Lord, you are my God. I will honor you and praise who you are, because you have done wonderful things that you planned long ago. You are faithful and trustworthy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> You have turned the city<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Though no specific city is named, it is probable that Babylon is meant. Certainly it is an enemy city (having a foreigner's palace).">a</a></sup>into a pile of rubble; the fortified town is now a ruin; the foreigner palace is gone. It is no longer a city and it will never be rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; PSALMS 46:10–11"> Because of this powerful nations will honor you; cities of brutal nations will be in awe of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> But you have protected the poor and needy when they were in trouble, you shielded them from storms and shaded them from the heat. For the actions of brutal people are like rain beating against a wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; PSALMS 105:39"> like heat in a desert. You end the noisy uprising of foreigners. In the same way that a cloud's shadow cools down the heat of the day, so the song sung by brutal people is silenced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; PSALMS 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> On this mountain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring to Mount Zion.">b</a></sup>the Lord Almighty will prepare a feast for all nations, a lavish feast of aged wines, rich food, and the best meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> On this mountain he will destroy the veil that covers all the nations, the sheet that is over everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He will destroy death forever. The Lord God will wipe away all tears, and everywhere he will take away the humiliation suffered by his people. The Lord has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> At that time his people will say, “Look! This is our God; we have trusted in him and he has saved us! This is the Lord we have been looking for. Now we can be happy and celebrate the salvation he brings!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> The Lord's hand of protection will be on this mountain. But the Moabites will be trampled underfoot like straw into the water of a manure pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> They will reach out their hands to try and save themselves, like swimmers using their hands to swim, but their pride will be humbled however much they thrash about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> Moab,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Moab”: supplied for clarity.">c</a></sup>he will demolish your fortress with the high walls, bringing it down to the ground, into the dust.</span>
