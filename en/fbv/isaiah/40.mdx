<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:12; Isaiah 51:3"> “Comfort, yes comfort my people!” says your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 61:7; Hosea 2:14"> “Speak lovingly to the people of Jerusalem, telling them that their hard times are over, that their sins have been forgiven, and that the Lord has paid them twice over for their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> A voice is heard calling, “Prepare the way for the Lord in the wilderness, make a straight highway for our God through the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Fill in all the valleys; level all the mountains and hills; smooth out the uneven ground; make the rough places flat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> The Lord's glory will be revealed, and everyone will see it together. This is what the Lord has declared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> A voice is heard saying, “Shout it out.” I asked, “What shall I shout?” “All human beings are like grass, and all their trustworthiness is like the flowers of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> The grass wilts and the flower fades when the Lord breathes on them. Yes, the people are grass!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> However, even though the grass wilts and the flower fades, the word of our God will endure forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> Zion, bringer of good news, go up a high mountain. Jerusalem, bringer of good news, raise your voice and shout out loud. Don't be afraid to shout really loud! Tell the towns of Judah, “Here is your God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Look! The Lord God is coming with power! He will rule with a firm hand. Look! He's bringing his reward with him, coming to give his gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; PSALMS 23:1–6"> He looks after his flock like a shepherd. He picks up the lambs in his arms and holds them close to his chest. He leads those that are nursing young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; PSALMS 102:25–26"> Who has measured the waters he holds in the palm of his hand? Who has marked off the heavens with the span of his hand? Who has worked out the amount of dust of the earth? Who has weighed the mountains on a scale and the hills with a balance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who has measured the Spirit of the Lord, or taught him what to do as his counselor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> Who did the Lord go to for advice to help him understand? Who taught him right from wrong? Who gave him knowledge and showed him the way of wisdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> It's obvious that the nations are just a drop in a bucket. They're like dust on a set of scales. He can pick up islands as if they weigh next to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; PSALMS 40:6; PSALMS 50:9–12"> All the wood in Lebanon for a fire and all its animals as a sacrifice wouldn't be enough to provide a burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> To him all the nations are like nothing. He counts them as less than nothing—like they don't exist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> Who do you think is like God? What image do you think he looks like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> Is he a metal idol that a craftsman casts in a mold, and then a goldsmith overlays it with gold and makes silver chains for it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> Those who are too poor to pay for that choose wood that won't rot, then they look for a skilled wood-carver to make an idol that won't fall over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; PSALMS 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Don't you know? Haven't you heard? Hasn't it been explained to you from the very beginning? Haven't you understood from the time the world was created?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“The world was created”: literally, “the foundations of the world.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; PSALMS 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> God sits on his throne above the horizon of the earth; the people that live there are like grasshoppers. He stretches out the heavens like a curtain, spreading them out like a tent to live in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; PSALMS 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> He brings leaders down; he makes the rulers of the world like nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> In fact they are hardly even planted, hardly even sown, hardly even taken root, when he blows on them and they wither, and the wind carries them away like straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> “Who are you going to compare me with? Who is equal to me?” asks the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Look at the heavens. Who created all this? He leads the stars like an army, and calls each one by name. Because he has great power and incredible strength, not a single one of them is missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why do you speak like this, Jacob, and why do you say, Israel, “The Lord doesn't see what's happening to me, and he's ignoring my rights!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; PSALMS 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Don't you know? Haven't you heard? The Lord is the eternal God, the Creator of the whole earth. He's never weak or tired; you can't find out all he knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He gives strength to the weary and power to the powerless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; PSALMS 34:10; PSALMS 33:16"> Even young people grow weak and tired—they fall down when they're exhausted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; PSALMS 27:13–14"> But those who trust in the Lord will have their strength renewed. They will fly high with wings like eagles. They will run and not be tired. They will walk and not be worn out.</span>
