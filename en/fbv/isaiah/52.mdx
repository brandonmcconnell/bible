<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Isaiah 51:17; Zechariah 3:4"> Wake up, wake up, Zion! Be strong! Put on your best clothes, Jerusalem, the holy city. Heathen foreigners won't ever enter you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Jeremiah 51:50; Luke 4:18"> Shake yourself free from the dust and get up. Sit on your throne, Jerusalem. Throw off the chains around your neck, captive daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; Isaiah 45:13; PSALMS 44:12"> This is what the Lord says: You were sold for nothing, and you will be bought back without money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; PSALMS 69:4; Acts 7:14–15"> This is what the Lord God says: First of all, my people went to live in Egypt, then Assyria conquered them for no reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:24; PSALMS 44:16; PSALMS 74:10"> What do I have to do now? asks the Lord. My people have been taken into captivity for no reason. Those who rule them mock them, and I'm treated with contempt that whole time, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10–11; Ezekiel 37:13–14; Ezekiel 20:44"> So I'm going to make sure my people know me; at that time they will know that I am the one who means what he says. Yes, it's me!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:12–15; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 61:1–3"> What a wonderful sight in the mountains is the one running to bring good news, announcing peace and good news, announcing salvation, telling Zion, “Your God reigns!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6; Ezekiel 33:7; Jeremiah 32:39"> The city watchmen shout loudly and sing for joy together; they all see the Lord returning to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; PSALMS 98:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Let Jerusalem's ruins all sing for joy for the Lord has come to care for his people; he has set Jerusalem free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 3:6; Isaiah 51:9; PSALMS 98:1–3"> The Lord has demonstrated his holy power to all the nations; the whole world will see our God's salvation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17; Zechariah 2:6–7; Isaiah 48:20"> Leave, leave, get out of there! Don't bring anything pagan;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Pagan”: literally “unclean,” likely referring to anything associated with idol worship.">a</a></sup>come out and leave it all behind. Those of you who carry the Lord's sacred articles are to purify yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8; Deuteronomy 20:4; Exodus 14:19–20"> But don't leave in a hurry, don't be in a rush as if you're running away, for the Lord will go ahead of you, and he will also protect those at the back.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–11; Matthew 28:18; John 3:31"> Look: my servant will act wisely; he will be praised highly, he will be elevated in position, and seen as someone people look up to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:2–5; Matthew 26:67"> But many were horrified by him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Him”: This is the reading of the Syriac and the Targums. Hebrew has “you.”">b</a></sup>so disfigured in appearance, no longer looking like a man, so unlike anyone human.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20–21; Isaiah 49:7; Isaiah 55:5"> He will surprise many nations, and kings will keep quiet because of him—for they'll see what they haven't been told, and they'll understand what they hadn't heard.</span>
</div>
</div>
