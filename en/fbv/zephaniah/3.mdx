<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Isaiah 30:12; Isaiah 59:13"> What disaster is coming to corrupted, rebellious Jerusalem, you who oppress people!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Jerusalem”: implied by the context.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 22:21; Jeremiah 7:23–28"> You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “she,” but using the second person pronoun makes the warning more vivid.">b</a></sup>don't listen to anybody, you don't accept correction, you don't trust in the Lord, you don't ask for God's help.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “you don't draw near to God.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Ezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremiah 5:6"> Your leaders are as greedy as roaring lions; your judges are ravenous wolves that leave nothing behind by morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Hosea 9:7; Malachi 2:8"> Your prophets are arrogant, deceitful men who defile what is sacred and who openly break the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “use the law to their own advantage.”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; PSALMS 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> But the Lord who does right is still among you, he does no wrong. Every morning he gives his judgment, every day without fail. But those who act unjustly have no shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Zephaniah 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> I have destroyed nations. Their fortresses are deserted; their streets are empty; their cities are destroyed—there are no survivors, not one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> I told myself, “Surely they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Referring to the people of Jerusalem.">e</a></sup>will respect me now and accept my correction. Then their homes would not be destroyed to teach them a lesson.” But instead you're just as eager to go on doing evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Zephaniah 1:18; Micah 7:7"> You just wait, declares the Lord. The day is coming when I will rise to give evidence. For I have decided to gather together all the nations and kingdoms and to pour out on them my anger, my fury and my rage. The whole earth will be burned up by the fire of my jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="On jealousy see note on verse 1:18.">f</a></sup>anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; PSALMS 22:27; Isaiah 19:18"> For then I will give the nations pure speech so they can all pray and worship the Lord together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; PSALMS 68:31; Isaiah 18:1"> From beyond the rivers of Ethiopia my scattered people, my worshipers, will come to bring their offerings to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> On that day you won't be ashamed because of what you did in rebelling against me, for I will remove from among you those who are proud and boastful. Never again will you be conceited on my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> I will leave among you those who are meek and humble, those who trust in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> The people of Israel who remain will not act wickedly nor will they tell lies. They will not deceive one another. They will be able to eat in peace and sleep in safety for there will be nothing to frighten them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> Sing out, Jerusalem! Shout aloud, Israel! Jerusalem, be happy and celebrate with all your heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:22; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 33:22"> For the Lord has turned aside from punishing you, and he has turned back your enemies. The Lord, the king of Israel, is with you, and you will never again have to fear disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> On that day the message to the people of Jerusalem will be, “Don't be afraid; don't be discouraged!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Don't be discouraged”: literally, “don't have limp hands.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 149:4; PSALMS 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> The Lord your God is among you as a mighty warrior who saves. He will be so happy with you. He will renew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Septuagint. Hebrew: “he will be silent in his love” hardly fits the previous or following phrase.">h</a></sup>his love for you. He will celebrate over you with loud singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> I will gather those of you who mourn for the religious festivals—you will no longer have to bear the disgrace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This is one interpretation of the Hebrew which is unclear. The meaning is that when in exile the Israelites were not able to celebrate their religious festivals as they wished and this was a cause of disgrace to them.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> Look at what I am going to do! At that time I am going to deal with all those who have oppressed you. I will save those who are helpless and bring back those who were scattered, and I will turn their shame into praise, and all the world will respect them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> At that time I will bring you home, gathering you together. I will give you a good reputation, and you will be praised by all the peoples of the earth when I restore your status before your very eyes, says the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
