<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 11:4"> Come on, you rich people! You should weep and wail for all the problems that are coming your way!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Job 13:28; Luke 12:33"> Your wealth is ruined, and your clothes have been eaten by moths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; James 5:7–8; Revelation 21:8"> Your gold and your silver are corroded, and this corrosion will be evidence against you, eating away your flesh like fire. You hoarded up your wealth in these last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Look, the wages of your farm workers that you cheated are crying out against you—the cries of the farm workers have reached the ears of the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:6; Luke 16:25; Luke 16:19"> You've enjoyed a life of luxury here on earth, full of self-indulgent pleasure, fattening yourselves up for the day of slaughter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–23; John 19:9–11; Isaiah 53:7"> You have condemned and killed the innocent who didn't even resist you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1–3; James 5:8–9; Joel 2:23"> Friends, be patient as you wait for the Lord's return. Think of the farmer waiting patiently for the earth's precious harvest as it grows with the early and later rains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 5:9; Galatians 5:22; PSALMS 37:7"> You need to be patient too. Stay strong for the Lord's return is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; James 4:11"> My friends, don't complain about each other, so you won't be judged. Look, the judge is standing right at the door!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32–38; Matthew 5:11–12; Hebrews 13:7"> Take the prophets as an example, my friends—see how they spoke in the Lord's name as they suffered and waited patiently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Exodus 34:6; Job 1:21–22"> Notice that we say that those who endure are blessed. You've heard about the patience of Job, and you've seen how the Lord brought this to a positive conclusion—for the Lord is full of compassion and mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:33–37; Matthew 23:16–22; 2 Corinthians 1:17–20"> Above all, my friends, don't swear. Not by heaven, not by earth, and not by any other kind of oath. Just let your yes be yes, and your no be no, so you won't fall under condemnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; Jonah 2:2; Acts 16:24–25"> Are any of you suffering? You should pray. Are some of you cheerful? You should sing songs of praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:13; Acts 28:8; Mark 16:18"> Are any of you sick? You should call for the church elders and have them pray over you, and anoint you with oil in the Lord's name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 5:14–16; James 5:13; James 5:16"> Such a prayer, trusting in God, will heal those who are sick, and the Lord will make them well. If they've committed sins, they will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Proverbs 15:29; Matthew 21:22"> Admit the wrongs you've done to each other, and pray for one another so that you'll be healed. Earnest prayer from those who live right is very effective.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Revelation 11:6"> Elijah was a man who had the same human nature as we do. He prayed earnestly that it wouldn't rain, and it didn't rain on the earth for three and a half years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 18:42–45; Jeremiah 14:22; 1 Kings 18:18"> He prayed again—heaven sent rain and the earth gave its harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15; James 5:20"> My friends, if any one of you strays from the truth and someone brings you back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 10:12; 1 Timothy 4:16"> let them know that whoever rescues a sinner from the error of their ways will save them from death, and will gain forgiveness for many sins.</span>
