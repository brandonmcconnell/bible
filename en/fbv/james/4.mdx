<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> Where do the fights and quarrels you're having come from? Aren't they due to the sensual passions that are in conflict within you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You burn with desire, but don't get what you want. You kill for what you lust after, but don't get what you're looking for. You argue and you fight, but you don't get anything, because you don't pray for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> You pray, but don't receive anything, because you ask with a wrong motive, wanting to spend what you'd receive on your selfish pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> You adulterous people! Don't you realize that friendship with the world is hatred towards God? Those who want to be friends of the world make themselves enemies of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Do you think Scripture doesn't mean what it says: that the spirit that he put in us is very jealous?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “God has placed a spirit in us that is filled with strong desires.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But God gives us even more grace, as Scripture says: “God is against those who are arrogant, but gives grace to those who are humble.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Quoting Proverbs 3:34.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> So place yourself under God's direction. Confront the devil, and he will run away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Come close to God and he will come close to you. Wash your hands, you sinners. Purify your way of thinking, you people with divided loyalties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Show some remorse, cry and weep! Turn your laughter into mourning, and your joy to sadness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Be humble before the Lord and he will lift you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Friends, don't speak badly of one another. Anyone who criticizes a fellow-believer and condemns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “judges.”">c</a></sup>them, criticizes and condemns the law. If you condemn the law you're not someone who keeps the law, because you're sitting in judgment of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> There's only one lawgiver and judge—the one who can either save or destroy you—so who are you to judge your neighbor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come on now!—you people who say, “Either today or tomorrow we'll go to such-and-such a city, spend a year in business there, and make a profit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; PSALMS 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> You have no idea what will happen tomorrow! What's your life like? It's just a mist that appears for a little while, and then is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> What you should say is, “If it's the Lord's will, we'll live like this and plan to do that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; PSALMS 52:7"> But right now you're caught up in your vain ideas. All this boasting is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> For it is a sin if you know to do what's right but don't do it.</span>
