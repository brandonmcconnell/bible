<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> My friends, not many of you should become teachers, because you know that we who teach will have a heavier responsibility in the judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> All of us make mistakes in many ways. Anyone who doesn't make mistakes in what they say is truly a good person who can keep the whole body under control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 32:9; PSALMS 39:1; James 1:26"> We put bridles into the mouths of horses so that they'll obey us, and we can direct them wherever we want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Look at ships as well: even though they're very big and are driven along by strong winds, they're steered by a very small rudder in the direction the pilot wants to go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> In just the same way the tongue is a very small part of the body, but it makes great boasts! Think how a big forest can be set on fire by a very small flame!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire! The tongue is a world of evil among the parts of the body. It disgraces all you are as a person, and can burn down the whole of your life, since it is set on fire by Gehenna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gehenna: the trash dump outside of Jerusalem where fires were used to burn the trash. The word is used symbolically as the endpoint for the wicked. See note on Matthew 5:22.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> People have tamed all kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and sea creatures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; PSALMS 140:3; PSALMS 55:21"> but no one can tame the tongue—it's an evil thing, hard to control, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; PSALMS 62:4"> We use the same tongue to bless our Lord and Father, as well as to curse people who are made in God's image—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> blessings and curses come from the same mouth! Friends, it shouldn't be this way!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Does a spring send both sweet and bitter water out of the same opening?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> My friends, a fig tree can't produce olives, and a vine can't produce figs, any more than a salt water spring can produce fresh water!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who among you has wisdom and understanding? Let their good lives demonstrate what they do—doing what is right with wise kindness and consideration.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your heart, don't boast about it and don't lie against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This kind of “wisdom” doesn't come from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, and demonic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Wherever there is jealousy and selfish ambition you'll also find confusion and all kinds of evil practices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> However, wisdom that comes from above is first of all pure, and it also brings peace. It is kind and open to reason. It is full of mercy and produces good things. It is genuine and not hypocritical.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> Those who sow peace will harvest the peace of what is truly good and right.</span>
</div>
