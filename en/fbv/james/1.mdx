<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:15; 1 Peter 1:1; John 7:35"> This letter comes from James, servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ. It is sent to the twelve tribes scattered abroad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring to the twelve tribes of Israel, of course.">a</a></sup>Best wishes to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> My friends, choose to stay happy even when all kinds of troubles come your way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> because you know that endurance comes from dealing with challenges to your trust in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> Let your endurance become as strong as possible, so that you will be completely mature, without any shortcomings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> If any of you need wisdom, ask God, who gives generously to everyone without holding back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But when you ask, remember to trust in God—don't have any doubts. Someone who doubts is like the crashing waves of the sea, tossed about and driven by the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> Nobody like that should think they'll get anything from the Lord—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> their minds think every which way, and they're unstable in whatever they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> Believers who are born poor should take pride in the high position they've been given,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> while the rich should “boast” in the humble position they now have,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring primarily to the way each group is viewed by God (1:9-10); not necessarily their position in society…">b</a></sup>since they will fade away like flowers in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; PSALMS 37:35–36; PSALMS 49:6–14"> For the sun rises along with the blistering wind and scorches the grass. The flowers fall and their beauty is marred. Everything the rich achieve will fade in just the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Happy is anyone who patiently endures temptation, for when they've proved that they are trustworthy, they will receive the crown of life which God promises to those who love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> Nobody should say when they're tempted, “I'm being tempted by God.” For God is not tempted by evil, nor does he tempt anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> Temptations come from our own evil desires that lead us astray and entrap us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Such desires lead to sin, and sin, when it's fully developed, causes death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> My dear friends, don't be deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> All that's good, every perfect gift, is from above, and comes down from the Father who made heaven's lights. Unlike them he doesn't change—he doesn't vary or cause shadows.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="James seems to be referring to the varying movements of heavenly bodies and eclipses (shadows).">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> He chose to give us new life through the word of truth, so that out of all his creation we would be very special to him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Usually thought to refer to God's call and provision for us to be born again spiritually.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> Remember this, my dear friends: everyone should be quick to listen but slow to speak, and slow to get angry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> because human anger doesn't reflect the good character of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “achieve God's righteousness.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> So get rid of all that's filthy and evil. Humbly accept the word that's been implanted within you—for this is what can save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> But do what the word says—don't just listen to it and delude yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> If you just listen to the word, and don't act on it, it's like staring at your own face in a mirror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> You see yourself, but then you leave, and immediately forget what you looked like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But if you look to the perfect law of freedom and follow it, not as someone who just listens and then forgets, but as someone who acts on it—then you will be blessed in what you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; PSALMS 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If you think you're religious, but don't control what you say, you deceive yourself—your religion is pointless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> In the eyes of our God and Father, religion that's pure and genuine is to visit orphans and widows in their suffering, and to keep yourself from being contaminated by the world.</span>
</div>
