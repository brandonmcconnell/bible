<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="1JN" data-book-slug="1-john" data-book-name="1 John" data-chapter="4" data-verses="21">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Dear friends, don't trust every spirit, but put the spirits to the test to find out whether they're from God, for there are many false prophets out there in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> How can you recognize God's Spirit? Every spirit that accepts Jesus Christ came with a human body is from God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> while every spirit that doesn't accept Jesus, that spirit isn't from God. In fact it is the spirit of the antichrist which you heard was coming, and which is already in the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> But you belong to God, my friends, and you have defeated them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Referring back to the false prophets and the spirit that inspires them.">a</a></sup>for the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They belong to the world, so they speak like people of the world, and the world listens to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We belong to God and whoever knows God listens to us, while whoever doesn't belong to God doesn't listen to us. This is how we can distinguish the spirit of truth from the spirit of deception.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Dear friends, let's go on loving one another, for love comes from God. All those who love are born of God and know God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; PSALMS 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> Those who don't love don't know God, for God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> How was God's love shown to us? God sent his one and only Son into the world so that we could live through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> This is love! It's not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and he sent his Son to be the means by which our sins are forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Friends, if this is the way God loves us, we ought to love one another like this too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> No one has ever seen God. However, if we love one another then God lives in us, and his love is fulfilled in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> How can we know that we live in him, and that he lives in us? He's given us the ability to love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Implied.">b</a></sup>by his Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> For we are witnesses to what we have seen and testify that the Father sent the Son as the Savior of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> God lives in everyone who declares that Jesus is the Son of God, and they live in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; PSALMS 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> We have experienced and trusted in the love that God has for us. God is love, and those who live in love, live in God, and God lives in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> This is how love is made complete in us so that we can be confident on judgment day: by the fact that we live just like him in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> Where there is love there can be no fear. God loves us completely, and this love drives all our fears away. If we do fear, it's because we fear being punished, and this shows that we have not been fully remade by the completeness of God's love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> We love because he loved us first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> Anyone who says, “I love God,” but hates a Christian brother or sister, is a liar. Anyone who doesn't love a brother whom they can see, can't love God whom they can't see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> This is the command he gave to us: those who love God love their brothers and sisters too.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
