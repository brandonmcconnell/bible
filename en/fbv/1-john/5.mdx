<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12–13; John 8:42; 1 John 2:29"> Whoever trusts that Jesus is the Christ is born of God, and whoever loves the father also loves his child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 3:22–24"> How do we know that we love God's children? When we love God and follow his commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; John 14:21–24"> Loving God means that we follow his commands, and his commands are not hard to bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; John 16:33"> Everyone who is born of God defeats the world. The way we gain victory and defeat the world is by trusting God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:15; 1 John 5:1"> Who can defeat the world? Only those who trust in Jesus, believing he is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:26; Hebrews 9:14; John 16:13"> He is the one who came through water and blood, Jesus Christ. He did not only come by water, but by water and blood.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is often interpreted to mean the water of baptism and the blood meaning his death.">a</a></sup>The Spirit gives evidence to confirm this, for the Spirit is the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Acts 5:32"> So there are three that give evidence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 5:6–7; John 15:26; 1 Peter 3:21"> the Spirit, the water, and the blood, and all three agree as one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The authenticity of verses 7 and 8 has been disputed.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:16–17; Matthew 17:5; Acts 17:31"> If we accept the evidence that human witnesses provide, then the evidence that God provides is much more important. The evidence that God provides is his testimony about his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:16; Galatians 4:6; John 3:33"> Those trusting in the Son of God have accepted and hold on to this evidence. Those who don't trust God make God out to be a liar, because they don't believe the evidence God has given about his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 John 2:25; John 3:36"> The evidence is this: God has given eternal life to us, and we have this life through his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 3:36; John 5:24; John 1:12"> Whoever has the Son has life; whoever doesn't have the Son of God doesn't have life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> I'm writing to tell those of you who trust in the name of the Son of God so you can be certain you have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> We can be confident that he will listen to us as long as we ask in accordance with his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> If we know that he hears our requests, we can be sure that we will receive what we've asked him for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If you see your Christian brother or sister committing a sin that is not a deadly sin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Deadly sin. Literally, “a sin to death.”">c</a></sup>you ought to pray and God will grant life to the one who's sinned. (But not for a deadly sin. There's a sin that is deadly, and I'm not saying people should pray about that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> Yes, all that is not right is sin, but there's sin that is not deadly.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We recognize that those who are born of God don't keep on sinning. The Son of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, the One born of God, following the concept at the beginning of the verse. The identification is made clear in the following verse.">d</a></sup>protects them and the devil cannot harm them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we belong to God, and that the world is under the power of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> We also know that the Son of God has come, and has helped us to understand so we can recognize the one who is true. We live in him who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God, and is eternal life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This can be understood to mean he gives eternal life, and also that he lives eternally.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Dear friends, stay away from idol worship.</span>
</div>
</div>
