<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="fbv" data-translation-id="eng_fbv" data-translation-label="FBV" data-book="1JN" data-book-slug="1-john" data-book-name="1 John" data-chapter="3" data-verses="24">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12; Galatians 3:26; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> Look at the amazing kind of love the Father has for us! This is why we can be called God's children—for that's who we are! The reason why the people of this world don't recognize us as God's children is because they don't recognize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> My friends, we are already God's children; however what we shall become hasn't been revealed just yet. But we do know that when he appears, we shall be like him, because we shall see him as he really is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 John 2:6; 2 Peter 3:14"> All those who have this hope in them make sure they are pure, just as he is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> All those who sin are lawless, because sin is lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> But of course you know that Jesus came to take away sins, and that there is no sin in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> All those who live in him don't go on sinning; all those who keep on sinning haven't seen him and haven't known him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Dear friends, don't let anyone fool you: those who do what is right are right, in the same way as Jesus is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> Those who sin are of the devil, for the devil has been sinning from the beginning. That's the reason why the Son of God came—to destroy what the devil has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> All those who are born of God don't keep on sinning, for God's nature<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “his seed.”">a</a></sup>lives in them. They can't keep on sinning because they are born of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> This is how God's children and the devil's children can be distinguished: all those who don't do what's right don't belong to God, nor do those who don't love their fellow Christians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> The message you've heard from the beginning is simply this: we should love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> We cannot be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one, and murdered his brother. Why did he murder him? Because Cain did what was evil, while his brother did what was right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> So don't be surprised, my friends, if this world hates you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> The reason we know that we have changed from death to life is because we love our Christian brothers and sisters. Anyone who doesn't love remains dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> All those who hate their Christian brothers and sisters are murderers, and you know that no murderers have eternal life within them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> This is how we know what love is: Jesus laid down his life for us, and we ought to lay down our lives for our Christian brothers and sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> If one of you is living well in this world and you see your Christian brother or sister in need, but you don't show compassion—how can God's love be living in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> Dear friends, let's not just say we love with words, but show our love in what we do and how we demonstrate the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> This is how we will know that we belong to the truth, and how we will set our minds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally, “hearts.” It was believed that the heart was where you did your thinking.">b</a></sup>at rest before God</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> whenever we think we're in the wrong. God is greater than how we think, and he knows everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Dear friends, if we're reassured we're not in the wrong, we can have confidence before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> We'll receive from him whatever we ask him for, because we follow his commands and do what pleases him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> This is what he commands: we should trust in the name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Name in the sense of character and reputation rather than just a given name.">c</a></sup>of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as he commanded us to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> Those who keep his commands continue to live in him, and he lives in them. We know that he lives in us by the Spirit he has given us.</span>
</div>
</div>
