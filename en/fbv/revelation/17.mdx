<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 17:1–17; Jeremiah 51:13"> One of the seven angels with the seven bowls came and talked with me. “Come here,” he said, “and I will show you the judgment of the infamous prostitute that sits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="To be seated has a similar idea to that in English of being “seated” as a ruler, also in verse 9.">a</a></sup>beside many waters.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This is a reference to the city of Babylon and its rivers and waterways.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:7; Revelation 18:3"> The kings of the earth have committed adultery with her, and those who live on the earth became drunk on the wine of her immorality.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:3; Revelation 13:1–6; Revelation 12:6"> Then he carried me off in the Spirit to a deserted place, and I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet beast that had seven heads and ten horns and was covered in blasphemous names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; Jeremiah 51:7; Revelation 19:2"> The woman was clothed in purple and scarlet, and wore jewelry made of gold and gems and pearls. She held in her hand a golden cup full of obscene things and her disgusting immorality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:7"> A name of mystery<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Mystery”: having a hidden meaning; now revealed.">c</a></sup>was written on her forehead: Babylon the Great, the Mother of Prostitutes and of the World's Obscene Things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Revelation 13:15; Revelation 2:13"> I saw that the woman was drunk on the blood of believers, and on the blood of martyrs who had died for Jesus. When I saw her, I was totally amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 17:1–6; Revelation 17:8"> The angel asked me, “Why were you amazed? I will explain to you the mystery of the woman and the beast she rode which has seven heads and ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 17:11; Revelation 11:7; Revelation 3:10"> The beast you saw once was, but is not,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “that once existed but does not exist now.”">d</a></sup>will soon come up again out of the Abyss, and will then be completely destroyed. Those that live on the earth who don't have their names written in the book of life will be amazed when they see the beast that once was but is not, and yet shall return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:18; Revelation 17:7; Matthew 13:11"> A mind that has understanding is needed here. The seven heads are seven hills where the woman sits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Again the idea here is of being seated as a ruler, see also verse 1.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> they are seven kings. Five have already fallen, one is reigning now, and the last is still to come—and his reign will be short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 17:8"> The beast that was, and is not, is also an eighth king and belongs to the seven. He too will be completely destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:3; Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:24"> The ten horns that you saw are ten kings who have not begun to reign yet. However, they will be given authority to reign as kings together with the beast for one hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Ezekiel 38:10; Isaiah 10:5–7"> They have one agreed purpose: to give their power and authority to the beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:30; 1 Timothy 6:15; Matthew 22:14"> They will make war on the Lamb, and the Lamb will defeat them for he is Lord of lords and King of kings. His followers are called and chosen, and they trust in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 51:13; Jeremiah 51:55"> The angel went on to explain to me, “The waters that you saw where the prostitute was sitting represent peoples, crowds of people, nations, and languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 18:8; Ezekiel 16:37–44; Leviticus 21:9"> The ten horns that you saw and the beast will detest the prostitute, and will take away everything she has and strip her naked, eat her flesh and burn her to ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Proverbs 21:1; Revelation 10:7"> For God put into their minds to do what he wanted, to have one agreed purpose: to give their kingdom to the beast—and so God's words will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:19; Revelation 18:2; Daniel 7:23"> The woman you saw is the great city which rules over the kings of the earth.”</span>
