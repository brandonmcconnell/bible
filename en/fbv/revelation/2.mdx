<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:20; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 1:11–13"> “Write this to the angel of the Ephesus church: This is what the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand says, the one who walks among the seven golden candlesticks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21"> I know what you've accomplished, your hard work and perseverance. I know you can't tolerate evil people, and how you investigated those who claimed to be apostles but are not, and discovered they were frauds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 6:9; James 5:7–11"> I know about your patience, and what you endured for my sake—and that you didn't give up!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 24:12–13; Jeremiah 2:2–5; Hebrews 6:10–11"> But I have something against you: you have neglected your first love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:2–3; Revelation 3:19; Revelation 2:16"> So remember how far you've fallen—repent and go back to what you were doing at first. Otherwise I'll come to you and I will remove your candlestick from where it is—unless you repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 101:3; 2 John 1:9–10; PSALMS 26:5"> However, you do have this to your credit: you hate the actions of the Nicolaitans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Nicolaitans: little is known about this group. It is suggested that they taught the physical had no impact on the spiritual, so the kind of life you led was not important.">a</a></sup>just as I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15"> If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit is telling the churches.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In other words, if you are able to hear what is said, then listen and pay attention.">b</a></sup>I will give to those who are victorious the privilege of eating from the tree of life, which stands in the Paradise of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Revelation 1:8; Revelation 1:11"> Write this to the angel of the Smyrna church: This is what the first and last says, the one who was dead and came back to life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; 2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:5–6"> I know the troubles you are going through, and how poor you are (but you are rich), and the abuse from those who say they are Jews but are not, but belong to Satan's synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Matthew 10:22; Revelation 3:10–11"> Don't be afraid about what you are going to suffer. Yes, the devil will throw some of you into prison to test you, and you will suffer persecution for ten days. Just remain faithful, even if it means death, and I will give you the crown of life!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; Revelation 20:14; Revelation 21:8"> If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit is telling the churches. The second death will not harm those who are victorious.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:16; Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16"> Write this to the angel of the Pergamum church: This is what the one holding the sharp two-edged sword says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:9–10; 1 Timothy 5:8; Revelation 3:8–9"> I know you're living where Satan has his throne, and that you have stayed true to me. You have not denied your trust in me, even when my faithful witness Antipas was killed right among you there where Satan lives!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 31:16; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:20"> But I have a few things against you: There are some among you who hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Referring to the story in Numbers 22and following.">c</a></sup>how to entrap the children of Israel by means of eating food sacrificed to idols and committing sexual sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:6"> Similarly you also have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:5"> So repent, or soon I will come to you and fight against them using the sword of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Isaiah 65:15; Revelation 19:12–13"> If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit is telling the churches. I will give the hidden manna to those who are victorious. I will give them a white stone with a new name written on it that nobody knows except those who receive it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 1:14–15; Revelation 1:11; John 3:35–36"> Write this to the angel of the Thyatira church: This is what the Son of God says, the one who has eyes like flaming fire and feet like polished brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Peter 1:7; John 15:2"> I know what you have accomplished, your love and faithfulness and service and perseverance, and that you are doing more now than when you first believed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Corinthians 10:18–21"> But I have something against you: you are allowing the woman called Jezebel who calls herself a prophetess to teach my followers, leading them astray into sexual sins, and to eat food sacrificed to idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Romans 2:4–5; Revelation 9:20–21"> I gave her time to repent of her sexual sins, but she's not willing to repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 17:2; Luke 13:5; Ezekiel 16:37–41"> So I'm throwing her into a bed together with her adulterous partners and they will suffer terribly unless they repent of what they have done with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Romans 8:27; PSALMS 7:9"> I will put her children to death. Then all the churches will know that I am the one who examines thoughts and motives.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Thoughts and motives”—Literally, “kidneys and hearts.”">d</a></sup>I will repay each of you according to what you've done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Revelation 12:9; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> For the rest of you there in Thyatira who don't follow this teaching, who have not learned Satan's ‘deep depravities’ as they are called, I don't place on you any other burden. I say to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 3:11; Revelation 3:3; Hebrews 10:23"> ‘Just hold on to what you have until I come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 3:21; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 2:7"> I will give authority over the nations to those who are victorious and who do what I say until the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 2:8–9; Revelation 12:5; Revelation 19:15"> He will rule the nations with an iron rod, breaking them into pieces like clay pots.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Psalms 2:8-9.">e</a></sup>In the same way that I received authority from my Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; 2 Peter 1:19; Luke 1:78–79"> I will give them the morning star.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Morning star”—an ancient reference to the planet Venus, associated with victory. It is also a title given to Christ.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> If you have ears, listen to what the Spirit is telling the churches.”</span>
</div>
</div>
