<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> I saw the seven angels that stand before God. They were given seven trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; PSALMS 141:2"> Then another angel came and stood at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “on.”">a</a></sup>the altar. He had a golden censer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="An object that is used to hold burning incense.">b</a></sup>and he was given a large quantity of incense to add to the prayers of all the saints on the golden altar that stands in front of the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> The smoke of the incense rose together with the prayers of the saints before God from the hand of the angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> The angel took the censer and filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it down upon the earth; there was the sound of thunder, with lightning flashes, and a terrible earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> Then the seven angels holding the seven trumpets prepared to blow them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> The first angel blew his trumpet. Hail and fire mixed with blood rained down on the earth. One third of the earth was burned up, one third of the trees were burned up and all the green grass was burned up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> The second angel blew his trumpet. Something looking like a huge mountain of flaming fire was thrown into the sea. One third of the sea turned to blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> and one third of the creatures that lived in the sea died, and one third of all ships were destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> The third angel blew his trumpet. A great star fell from heaven, blazing brightly. It fell on a third of the rivers and springs of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> The name of the star is Wormwood, and one third of the water turned bitter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In Greek, “Wormwood” is synonymous with “bitter.”">c</a></sup>and many people died from drinking the water because it had become poisonous.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A different word is used here, “to make bitter or sour,” but due to its effects it is probably best to translate this as “poisonous.”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> The fourth angel blew his trumpet. One third of the sun, moon, and stars were struck so a third of them would be darkened, and a third part of the day would not give light, similarly the night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> I saw and heard an eagle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or vulture.">e</a></sup>flying in mid-heaven, shouting loudly, “Disaster, disaster, disaster is coming to those who live on the earth because of what is going to happen when the three remaining angels blow their trumpets.”</span>
</div>
