<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:10; Romans 10:7; Revelation 9:2"> The fifth angel blew his trumpet. I watched a star fall from heaven to earth. The star was given the key to the opening of the Abyss.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sometimes translated “bottomless pit.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Genesis 19:28; Joel 2:2"> He opened the entrance to the Abyss, and smoke came up out of the Abyss like the smoke of a huge furnace. The sun and the atmosphere became dark because of the smoke from the Abyss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Revelation 9:5; Joel 2:25"> Locusts came out of the smoke onto the earth, and they were given power like that possessed by scorpions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 7:2–4; Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 14:1"> They were told not to harm the grass or any vegetation or any trees, only those who did not have the seal of God on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> They were not allowed to kill, but they could torture these people for five months. The torture was like that of a scorpion when it stings someone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> During that time people will look for death, but won't find it; they will want to die, but death will run away from them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> The locusts looked like war-horses. They wore what seemed to be golden crowns on their heads, and their faces looked human.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> They had long hair like women and had teeth like lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> Their breastplates looked like they were made of iron, and the noise made by their wings sounded like many horses and chariots racing into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> They had tails like scorpions, complete with stingers. They had the power to hurt people for five months with their tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> Ruling over them as their king was the angel of the Abyss who is called Abaddon in Hebrew and Apollyon in Greek.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Abaddon in Hebrew means “destruction” while Apollyon in Greek means “destroyer.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> The first Disaster is over, but there are still two more to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> The sixth angel blew his trumpet. I heard a voice come from the horns of the golden altar that stands in front of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> speaking to the sixth angel that had the trumpet: “Release the four angels that are tied up beside the great River Euphrates.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> The four angels who had been kept ready for this particular hour, day, month and year were released to kill one third of humanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; PSALMS 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> I was told the number of the army of soldiers on horseback: it was 200 million.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> In my vision I saw the horses and their riders who wore breastplates as red as fire, and dark blue and yellow.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “hyacinth and sulfur.”">c</a></sup>The heads of the horses looked like lions, and fire and smoke and sulfur streamed out of their mouths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> One third of humanity was killed by these three plagues, by the fire and smoke and sulfur streaming out of their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> The horses' power was in their tails as well as their mouths, for their tails were like serpents' heads that they used to hurt people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Deuteronomy 31:29; 1 Timothy 4:1"> But the rest of humanity who were not killed by these plagues did not repent of what they were doing. They did not stop worshiping demons and idols of gold, silver, bronze, and stone—which can't see or hear or walk!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> They did not repent of their murders, their witchcraft, their sexual sins, or their thefts.</span>
</div>
</div>
