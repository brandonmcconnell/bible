<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 21:25; Ephesians 5:32; Luke 21:11"> Then an amazing sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and a crown of twelve stars on her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:21; Galatians 4:19; Revelation 12:4"> She was pregnant, and she cried out because of her labor pains, groaning as she was giving birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Revelation 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> Another sign appeared in heaven: a great red dragon with seven heads and ten horns, with seven small crowns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “diadems” (also 13:1). The Greek word used here is different from that used in 12:1.">a</a></sup>on his heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:9–12; Revelation 12:9; John 8:44"> His tail swept away a third of the stars of heaven and threw them down to the earth. The dragon stood right in front of the woman who was giving birth, so that he could eat her child as soon as it was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:2"> She gave birth to a son, a male child, who will rule all nations with an iron rod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Psalms 2:9.">b</a></sup>Her son was snatched up to God and his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:5; Revelation 11:2–3; Revelation 12:14"> The woman ran away into the desert, where God had prepared a place for her, so that she could be looked after for 1,260 days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> There was war in heaven. Michael and his angels fought with the dragon. The dragon and his angels fought</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> but he was not strong enough, and they could no longer remain in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> The great dragon, the ancient serpent called the devil and Satan who deceives the whole world, was thrown down to the earth, and his angels with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> Then I heard a loud voice in heaven saying, “Now salvation has come, and power, and the kingdom of our God, and the authority of his Christ. The Accuser of the believers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “brothers.”">c</a></sup>has been thrown down—the one who accuses them in God's presence day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> They conquered him through the blood of the Lamb and through their personal testimony—they did not love their lives so much that they were willing to die if necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; PSALMS 96:11–13"> So celebrate, heaven, and everyone who lives there! Mourn, earth and sea, for the devil has come down to you and is very angry, knowing his time is short.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> When the dragon realized he had been thrown down to the earth, he persecuted the woman that had given birth to the male child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> The woman was given the wings of a large eagle so she could fly away to a deserted place where she could be looked after for a time, times, and half a time, protected from the serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; PSALMS 18:4"> The serpent spewed water like a river out of his mouth, trying to sweep her away in the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> The earth came to the woman's aid by opening its mouth and swallowing up the river that the dragon spewed out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> The dragon was furious with the woman, and went off to attack the rest of her offspring, those who keep God's commandments and have the testimony of Jesus.</span>
