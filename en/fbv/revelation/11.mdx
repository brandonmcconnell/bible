<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> I was given a measuring rod and told, “Get up and measure the Temple of God, and the altar, and count those who are worshiping there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> Don't measure the court outside the Temple, leave that, because it has been handed over to the nations. They will trample down the holy city for forty-two months.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> I will give my two witnesses power, and they will prophesy 1,260 days, dressed in sackcloth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> They are the two olive trees and the two candlesticks that stand in front of the Lord of the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A reference to Zechariah 4:14.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> If anyone tries to harm them, fire comes out of their mouths and burns up their enemies. This is how anyone who tries to harm them will be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; PSALMS 105:26–36"> These two witnesses have the power to close the sky so that it will not rain during the time of their prophecy. They also have the power to turn the waters into blood, and to strike the earth with all kinds of plagues, as often as they want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> When they finish their testimony, the beast coming from the Abyss will attack them, defeat them, and kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> Their dead bodies will lie on the open street of the great city, called Sodom and Egypt in spiritual symbolism—also the place where their Lord was crucified.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Clearly referring to Jerusalem.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> For three and a half days those from the peoples and tribes and languages and nations will look at their dead bodies, and will not allow their corpses to be buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> The inhabitants of the earth are delighted they are dead, and celebrate, sending gifts to each other, because these two prophets had been a torment to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> But three and half days later God's life-giving breath entered them and they stood on their feet. Those who saw this were absolutely terrified.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The verb tense in this passage (11:7-11) in Greek changes from future to present to past. This translation has maintained this change.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> The two witnesses heard a loud voice from heaven telling them, “Come up here!” So they ascended to heaven in a cloud while their enemies watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> At the same time there was a huge earthquake, and one tenth of the city collapsed. Seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake, and the rest were terrified, and gave glory to the God of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second Disaster is over; the third Disaster is about to arrive!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> The seventh angel blew his trumpet, and loud voices shouted in heaven, “The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord, and of his Christ, and he shall reign for ever and ever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> The twenty-four elders who sit on their thrones in God's presence fell down on their faces and worshiped God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> They said, “We thank you, Lord God, the All-powerful One, who is and was, because you have taken up your great power and have asserted your rule.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> The nations became furious, but your judgment has come,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In other words, the day of judgment. On the issue of human anger as opposed to divine anger see on 6:16.">d</a></sup>the time when the dead will be judged. This is the time when your servants the prophets and the believers will be given their reward, those who respect your authority, both the weak and the powerful. This is also the time when you will destroy those who destroy the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Revelation 15:5–8; Ezekiel 38:22"> Then the Temple of God in heaven was opened, and the Ark of the Agreement<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “covenant.”">e</a></sup>could be seen inside his Temple. There were lightning flashes, the sound of thunder, an earthquake, and a great hailstorm.</span>
</div>
