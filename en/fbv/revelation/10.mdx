<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Revelation 4:5; Joel 3:16"> He gave a great shout, like a lion roaring. When he shouted, the seven thunders responded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Daniel 12:9"> When the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write down what they said when I heard a voice from heaven that told me, “Keep under seal what the seven thunders said. Don't write them down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> The angel I saw standing on the sea and on the land held up his right hand to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Revelation 4:9; Revelation 16:17"> He made a sacred oath by the one who lives for ever and ever, who created the heavens and everything in them, and the earth and everything in it, and the sea and everything in it. “No more delay!” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:3–9; Romans 16:25; Luke 24:44–47"> But at the time when the seventh angel speaks, when he blows his trumpet, then the mystery<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="As always, this is a “revealed mystery.” In other words, “God's mysterious plan will be accomplished.”">a</a></sup>of God will be completed—the good news he announced through his servants the prophets.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> So I went to the angel, and asked him to give me the little scroll.
