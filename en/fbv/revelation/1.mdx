<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> This is the Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show his servants what will happen soon. He sent his angel to reveal it to his servant John</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> who confirmed everything he saw concerning the word of God and the testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the witness and evidence given by and about Jesus.">a</a></sup>of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> Anyone who reads<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Referring particularly to those who read this book out loud to others.">b</a></sup>this is blessed, as are those who hear these prophetic words and pay attention to what's written, because the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> This letter comes from John and is sent to the seven churches in the province of Asia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In Asia Minor, modern-day Turkey.">c</a></sup>May you have grace and peace from the one who was, who is, and who is to come, and from the seven Spirits before his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> and from Jesus Christ the trustworthy witness, the firstborn from the dead, the ruler of earthly kings. To Jesus who loves us and freed us from our sins by his blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> who made us into his kingdom, priests to his God and Father—to Jesus be glory and authority for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> Look, he is coming surrounded by clouds, and everybody shall see him, even those who killed him. All the peoples of the earth will weep because of him. May it be so! Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> “I am the Beginning and the End,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally, “Alpha and Omega,” the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet.">d</a></sup>says the All-powerful Lord God, who was, who is, and who is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I am John, your brother who shares with you in the suffering and in the kingdom and in the patient waiting that are our experience in Jesus. I was detained on the island of Patmos for sharing the word of God and the truth as revealed by Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I was filled by the Spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard a loud voice behind me that sounded like a trumpet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> It told me, “Write down in a book what you see and send it to the seven churches: Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> I turned round to see who was speaking to me. When I turned I saw seven golden candlesticks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> and standing among the candlesticks was someone who looked like a Son of man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Son of man: the term Jesus often used to describe himself, and also an apocalyptic term used in the book of Daniel: Daniel 7:13.">e</a></sup>He was wearing a robe that reached down to his feet with a golden sash across his chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> His hair looked like white wool, and his eyes like flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> His feet looked like polished brass that had been refined in a furnace. His voice sounded like a thundering waterfall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> He was holding seven stars in his right hand, and a sharp double-edged sword came out of his mouth. His face shone like the sun at its brightest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> When I saw him I fell down at his feet as if I were dead. But he touched me with his right hand and said, “Don't be afraid, I am the first and the last,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> the living one. I was dead, but look! Now I am alive for ever and ever, and I hold the keys of death and the grave.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “Hades.”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> So write down what you've seen—what's happening in the present and what will happen in the future.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> The meaning of the seven stars that you saw me holding in my right hand and the seven golden candlesticks is this: the seven stars are the angels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Angel means “messenger.”">g</a></sup>of the seven churches and the seven candlesticks are the seven churches.”</span>
</div>
