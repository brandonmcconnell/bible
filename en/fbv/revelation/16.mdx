</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:7; Revelation 16:11; Revelation 13:15–18"> So the first angel went and poured out his bowl on the earth, and terrible, painful sores broke out on those who had the mark of the beast and who worshiped his image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–21; Revelation 8:8–9; Revelation 11:6"> The second poured out his bowl on the sea, and it became like the blood of a corpse and everything living in the sea died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–20; Revelation 8:10–11; Hosea 13:15"> The third poured out his bowl on the rivers and springs of water, and the water turned into blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 11:17; Revelation 1:4"> I heard the angel who had power over the waters declare, “You are truly right, you who are and was, the Holy One, as this judgment demonstrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Revelation 11:18; Revelation 6:10–11"> These people shed the blood of believers. Now you've given them blood to drink, as they deserve!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 6:9; Revelation 15:3–4"> I heard a voice from the altar saying, “Yes, Lord God, the All-powerful One, your judgments are right and true!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:20; Revelation 14:18; Jonah 4:8"> The fourth angel poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was given the power to scorch people with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Revelation 16:21; Revelation 11:13"> They were scorched by intense heat, and they cursed the name of God who controlled these plagues. They did not repent and give him glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:2; Matthew 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> The fifth angel poured out his bowl on the beast's throne, and darkness fell over his kingdom. The people bit their tongues because of the pain they felt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:2; Revelation 16:21"> and they cursed the God of heaven because of their pain and their sores, but they did not repent and stop what they were doing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:14; Jeremiah 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> The sixth angel poured out his bowl on the mighty River Euphrates, and its water was dried up, so that a way could be prepared for the kings that come from the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 20:10"> Then I saw three evil spirits that looked like frogs coming out of the mouths of the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 4:1; Revelation 20:8"> These are demonic spirits that perform miracles, going out to gather together all the kings of the whole world for the battle on God the All-powerful One's great day of judgment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Matthew 24:42–43; 2 Peter 3:10"> (Pay attention! I will come like a thief.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, unexpectedly.">a</a></sup>Blessed are those who keep watch, and have their clothes ready so that they don't have to go out naked and be embarrassed.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; Judges 5:19; Joel 3:9–14"> The evil spirits gathered the kings for battle at a place called Armageddon in Hebrew.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:30; Revelation 21:6; Ephesians 6:12"> The seventh angel poured out his bowl on the air, and a loud voice came from the Temple, from the throne, shouting out, “It's over!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> Lightning flashed and thunder boomed, and a massive earthquake shook the earth. It was the worst earthquake that had ever happened since people lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:5; Revelation 17:18; Revelation 18:21"> The great city was split into three. The cities of the nations were destroyed. Babylon the great was remembered in God's presence so that she should be given the cup filled with the wine of his hostility.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Revelation 20:11; Jeremiah 4:23–25"> All the islands vanished, and all the mountains disappeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:9"> Huge hailstones, each one weighing one hundred pounds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Greek says a “weight of a talent.”">b</a></sup>rained down from the sky on people. The people cursed God because the plague of hail was so terrible.</span>
</div>
