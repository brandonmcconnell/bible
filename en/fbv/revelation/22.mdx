<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; PSALMS 36:8–9"> The angel showed me the river of water of life, crystal-clear, flowing out of the throne of God and of the Lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; 1 Peter 2:24; Revelation 2:7"> right in the middle of the main street of the city. On both sides of the river was the tree of life, producing twelve crops of fruit, one every month. The leaves of the tree were used to heal people from all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Revelation 21:3–4; John 12:26"> There will no longer be any curse.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “anything under a curse.”">a</a></sup>The throne of God and of the Lamb will be there in the city, and his servants will worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 3:12"> They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4"> There will be no more night, and they will not need the light of a lamp, or the light of the sun, because the Lord God will give them light. They shall reign for ever and ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:1; Revelation 21:5; Revelation 19:9"> The angel told me, “These words are trustworthy and true. The Lord God, who gave his Spirit to the prophets, sent his angel to show his servants what is about to happen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:3; Revelation 3:11; Revelation 22:20"> “I am coming soon!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="These are understood to be the words of Jesus, see also verses 12 and 20.">b</a></sup>Blessed are those who obey the prophetic words of this book.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 1:1; Revelation 19:19"> I am John who heard and saw all this. When I heard and saw them, I fell down at the feet of the angel who had shown me these things to worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> He told me, “Don't do that! I am one of God's servants just as you are, together with your brothers the prophets, and those who obey the words of this book. Worship God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> Then he told me, “Don't seal up the words of prophecy of this book and keep them secret, for the time is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> Whoever doesn't do what is right, let them stay that way. Whoever is impure, let them stay that way. Whoever does what is right, let them stay that way. Whoever is holy, let them stay that way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> “I am coming soon, and I am bringing my reward to give to everybody based on what they've done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am the Alpha and Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are those who wash their robes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “who keep his commandments.”">c</a></sup>so that they have the right to the tree of life, and can enter into the city through the gates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> Those outside the city are dogs, those who practice witchcraft, those who are sexually immoral, those who murder, those who worship idols, and all those who love and invent lies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> I, Jesus, have sent my angel to give you this testimony to share with the churches. I am both the root and the descendant of David, the bright morning star.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> The Spirit and the bride say, “Come.” Whoever hears this, say, “Come.” Whoever is thirsty, come, and whoever wants to, freely drink of the water of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> I solemnly declare to everyone who hears the prophetic words of this book that if anyone adds to them then God will add to them the plagues described in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> If anyone takes away the prophetic words of this book, God will take away their share of the tree of life and of the holy city that are described in this book.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 22:7; Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 22:12"> He who confirms all this says, “Yes, I am coming soon.” Amen, come, Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with the believers. Amen.</span>
</div>
