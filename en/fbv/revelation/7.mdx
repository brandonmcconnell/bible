<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Daniel 7:2; Jeremiah 49:36"> Then I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In ancient times the earth is seen as having these four corners from where the winds blow.">a</a></sup>holding back the earth's four winds to prevent any wind from blowing on the earth, or over the sea, or against any tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Revelation 7:3–8"> I watched another angel rise up from the east, holding the seal of the living God. He shouted in a loud voice to the four angels who had been given the power to damage the earth and the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 22:4; Revelation 9:4"> “Don't hurt the earth or the sea or the trees until we have placed a seal on the foreheads of God's true followers!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 14:1; Revelation 14:3; Matthew 19:28"> I was told the number of those who were sealed: one hundred and forty-four thousand. Those who were sealed came from every tribe of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:2–4; Numbers 10:14–27; Numbers 13:4–16"> twelve thousand from the tribe of Judah, twelve thousand from the tribe of Reuben, twelve thousand from the tribe of Gad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:36"> twelve thousand from the tribe of Asher, twelve thousand from the tribe of Naphtali, twelve thousand from the tribe of Manasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Numbers 26:14; Numbers 25:14"> twelve thousand from the tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand from the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand from the tribe of Issachar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> twelve thousand from the tribe of Zebulun, twelve thousand from the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; John 12:13; PSALMS 117:1–2"> After this I looked and saw a great crowd that nobody could count, made up of every nation, tribe, people and language. They were standing in front of the throne and the Lamb, dressed in white robes, with palm branches in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:1; PSALMS 3:8; Isaiah 45:21"> They gave a loud shout, “Salvation is from our God who sits on the throne, and from the Lamb.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:11–13; Revelation 19:4–6; Revelation 4:10"> All the angels surrounding the throne, and the elders and the four living creatures, fell down on their faces before the throne, worshiping God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:12–14; Jude 1:25; PSALMS 100:4"> “Amen!” they said. “Blessing, glory, wisdom, gratitude, honor, power, and strength, be to God for ever and ever. Amen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 5:5"> One of the elders spoke to me and asked, “Who are those who are dressed in white robes, and where have they come from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Revelation 22:14; Hebrews 13:12"> I replied, “My Lord, you know the answer.” He told me, “These are the ones who have passed through great persecution. They washed their robes, making them white through the blood of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Revelation 22:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> That's why they can stand in front of God's throne, and they serve him day and night in his Temple. The one who sits on the throne will protect them with his presence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Protect with his presence”: literally, “tents among them,” using the same word for Jesus' coming in John 1:14.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:10; PSALMS 121:6; Matthew 5:6"> They will never be hungry ever again, or thirsty; the sun will not beat down on them and they will not suffer scorching heat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 49:10.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:37–38; PSALMS 36:8–9; John 4:14"> because the Lamb who is at the center of the throne will be their shepherd,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 23:1.">d</a></sup>and will lead them to springs of living water,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 49:10.">e</a></sup>and God will wipe away every tear from their eyes.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 25:8.">f</a></sup></span>
