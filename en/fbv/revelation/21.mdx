<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Isaiah 65:17, Isaiah 66:22.">a</a></sup>The first heaven and the first earth had disappeared, and the sea no longer existed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> I saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, descending from God out of heaven, prepared like a bride made beautiful for her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> I heard a loud voice from the throne say, “Now God's home<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word is literally, “to pitch a tent.”">b</a></sup>is with human beings and he will live with them. They will be his people. God himself will be with them as their God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“As their God” is not found in all ancient manuscripts.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Quoting Isaiah 25:8.">d</a></sup>and death will never happen again. There will be no mourning or crying or pain ever again for the former world no longer exists.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> The one who sits on the throne said, “I am making everything new!” He told me, “Write this down, for these words are trustworthy and true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> Then he said to me, “Everything's done! I am the Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end. To anyone who is thirsty I will give the free gift of water from the spring of the water of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> Those who are victorious will inherit all these things, and I will be their God and they shall be my children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> But anyone who is a coward, who doesn't trust me, who does disgusting things, who is a murderer, who is sexually immoral, who practices witchcraft, who worships idols, who tells lies—their chosen place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Their chosen place”: or “their lot.”">e</a></sup>is in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur. This is the second death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> One of the seven angels who had the seven bowls containing the seven last plagues came and spoke to me. He told me, “Come with me. I will show you the bride, the Lamb's wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> He took me in the Spirit to the top of a very high mountain. There he showed me the holy city Jerusalem descending from God out of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> shining with the glory of God's presence. The light sparkled like that from a gemstone, a jasper, clear and bright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> The city wall was high and thick, with twelve gates guarded by twelve angels. On the gates were inscribed the names of the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> There were three gates on the east, three on the north, three on the south, and three on the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> The city wall had twelve foundations, and inscribed on them were the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> The angel who spoke to me had a golden measuring rod to measure the city, its gates, and its wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> The city was square. The length was the same as the width. He measured the city with the rod, it was 12,000 furlongs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="About 1,500 miles.">f</a></sup>The length, width, and height were all the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> He measured the wall and it was 144 cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Over 200 feet.">g</a></sup>thick in human measurements that the angel was using.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> The wall was built out of jasper. The city was made of pure gold that looked like glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> The foundations of the city wall were decorated with all kinds of gemstones. The first foundation was jasper, the second sapphire, the third chalcedony, the fourth emerald,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth sardonyx, the sixth carnelian, the seventh chrysolite, the eighth beryl, the ninth topaz, the tenth, chrysoprase, the eleventh jacinth, the twelfth amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> The twelve gates were made of pearl, each one made from a single pearl. The main street was made of pure gold, clear like glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> I did not see a temple there, because the Lord God the All-powerful One, and the Lamb, they are its temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> The city does not need the sun or the moon to shine on it because the glory of God provides its light—the Lamb is its lamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> The nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth bring their glory into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> The gates of the city will never be shut during the day (and there will be no night there).</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> The nations' honor and glory will be brought into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> Nothing unclean will ever enter into it, or anyone who worships idols, or who is a liar—only those who are written in the Lamb's book of life.</span>
