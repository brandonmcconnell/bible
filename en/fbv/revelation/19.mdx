<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:10–12; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Revelation 11:15"> After this I heard what sounded like the noise of an enormous crowd in heaven, shouting, “Hallelujah! Salvation, glory, and power describe our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:43; Revelation 6:10; Revelation 18:20"> because his judgments are true and right, for he has condemned the infamous prostitute who corrupted the earth with her immorality, and has brought her to justice for her murders of his servants.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “for the blood of his servants at her hand.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Revelation 18:18; Revelation 14:11"> Again they shouted, “Hallelujah! The smoke from her destruction ascends for ever and ever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 5:14; Nehemiah 8:6; PSALMS 106:48"> The twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and worshiped God who sits on the throne. “Amen! Hallelujah!” they shouted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 134:1; PSALMS 148:11–13; PSALMS 150:6"> A voice spoke from the throne that said, “Praise our God, everyone who serves him and respects him, from the smallest to the greatest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:1; Revelation 14:2; PSALMS 93:1"> Then I heard what sounded like the noise of an enormous crowd, like rushing water and loud thunder, shouting, “Hallelujah! For the Lord our God, the All-powerful One, he reigns!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 21:9; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:9"> Let's celebrate and be glad and give him the glory, because the wedding day of the Lamb has arrived, and his bride has prepared herself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Revelation 7:13–14; PSALMS 132:9"> She was given fine linen to wear, white and clean. (Fine linen represents the good works of believers.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:6; Matthew 22:2–4; Luke 14:15–16"> The angel told me, “Write this down: How happy are those who are invited to the Lamb's wedding feast.” Then he said to me, “These are God's true words.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 10:25–26; John 5:39"> I fell down at his feet to worship him. He told me, “Don't do that! I am one of God's servants just as you are, and those who accept Jesus' testimony. Worship God, for Jesus' testimony is the prophetic spirit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 96:13; Isaiah 11:3–5; PSALMS 98:9"> I saw heaven was opened. A white horse was standing there. Its rider was called Trustworthy and True. He is right when he judges, and he is right when he makes war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> His eyes were like flaming fire. On his head were many crowns. He had a name written on him which no one but he himself knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> He wore a robe covered in blood, and his name is The Word of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> Heaven's armies were following him, riding on white horses and clothed in fine linen, white and clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> From his mouth came a sharp sword to strike the nations. He will rule them with an iron rod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Psalms 2:9.">b</a></sup>and he himself treads the winepress of the All-powerful God's judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, “of the fury of the anger.” See on 14:9, and 6:16.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> Written on his robe and on his thigh was the name, King of kings and Lord of lords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:21; Revelation 8:13; Ezekiel 39:17–20"> I saw an angel standing in the sun, shouting in a loud voice to all the birds that fly in the sky, “Come and gather together for God's great feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:18–20; Revelation 6:15; 1 Samuel 17:44"> Here you can eat the flesh of the dead: kings, leaders, and powerful men, as well as that of horses and their riders, the flesh of all people, free and slave, great and small.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:16; Revelation 16:14; Revelation 17:12–14"> I saw the beast and the kings of the earth gathered together to wage war on the one sitting on the horse and his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:10; Revelation 21:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–11"> The beast was captured, along with the false prophet who performed miracles in his presence (by which he deceived those who had received the mark of the beast and those who worshiped the beast's image). Both of them were thrown alive into the like of fire that burns with sulfur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–15; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 19:17–19"> The rest were killed with the sword of the one sitting on the horse—the sword that came out of his mouth. All the birds gorged themselves on their flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>
