<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Luke 17:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> After this I saw another angel descending from heaven. He had great power<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “authority.”">a</a></sup>and the earth was lit up by his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8; Jeremiah 51:37"> He shouted out in a powerful voice, “Babylon the great has collapsed into ruins!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Isaiah 21:9.">b</a></sup>She has become a place where demons live, the refuge of every unclean spirit, and the roost of every unclean and detestable bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:2; Revelation 18:9"> For all the nations have drunk<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “have fallen because of…”">c</a></sup>the wine of her mad sexual immorality. The kings of the earth have committed adultery with her, and the traders of the earth have grown rich from her excessive sensuality.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Jeremiah 51:6"> Then I heard another voice from heaven calling out, “My people, come out of her,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Jeremiah 51:45.">d</a></sup>so that you don't participate in her sinful ways, and so that you don't experience her plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Ezra 9:6; Revelation 16:19"> Her sins have piled up all the way to heaven, and God is very much aware of her wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; PSALMS 137:8; Jeremiah 51:24"> Give back to her what she gave; repay her double for what she did. In her own cup mix double the trouble she mixed for others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–9; Zephaniah 2:15; Lamentations 1:1"> As much as she boasted about herself and indulged her lusts, give her back just as much anguish and sorrow. She told herself, ‘I reign<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally, “sit.”">e</a></sup>as queen. I am no widow; I will never be in mourning.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:34; Jeremiah 50:31; Revelation 17:16"> Because of this her plagues will come upon her in just one day: death, mourning, and famine. She will be completely destroyed by fire, for the Lord God who condemns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “judges.”">f</a></sup>her has great power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Revelation 18:3; Revelation 17:2"> The kings of the earth who had committed adultery with her and indulged their lusts with her will cry and mourn over her when they see the smoke of the fire that destroys her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Revelation 18:8; Revelation 18:15–17"> Standing off at a distance because they are afraid they will suffer the same agony as her, they say, ‘Disaster, disaster has struck Babylon, the great city! In just one hour your sentence of doom was executed!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Revelation 18:23; Ezekiel 27:27–36"> The traders of the earth cry and grieve over her, because nobody is buying their goods any more—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Ezekiel 27:5–25; Proverbs 8:10–11"> products made of gold, silver, gems and pearls; fine linen, purple cloth, silk, and scarlet material; all kinds of objects made of scented wood, or from ivory, or expensive woods, or bronze, iron, or marble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Amos 2:6; 2 Peter 2:3"> shipments of cinnamon, spice, incense, myrrh, frankincense, wine, olive oil, fine flour and wheat, cattle and sheep, horses and wagons, and slaves and prisoners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “bodies and souls of men.”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; PSALMS 106:14; Luke 16:25"> You've lost the sweet pleasures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “ripe fruit.”">h</a></sup>you loved so much; all your luxurious, glittering possessions are gone—you'll never get any of them back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Acts 19:24–27; Ezekiel 27:31"> The traders who sold these things and became rich from trading with her will stand at a distance because they are afraid they will suffer the same agony as her. They will cry and grieve, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Luke 16:19–31; Revelation 18:10–11"> ‘Disaster, disaster has hit the great city! She was clothed in fine linen and purple robes, and wore jewelry made of gold and gems and pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:27–36; Revelation 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> In just one hour all this wealth was destroyed!’ Every sea captain and everyone who travels by sea and every sailor and everyone who earns their living from the sea stood at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Ezekiel 27:30–32; Revelation 18:9–10"> As they watched the smoke of the fire that destroys her, they shouted out, ‘What city could ever compare to this great city?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:10; Joshua 7:6; Ezekiel 27:30"> They threw dust on their heads, shouting and crying and grieving, ‘Disaster, disaster has struck the great city that made every ship-owner rich because of her extravagance! In just one hour she was destroyed!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Revelation 19:1–3; Luke 18:7–8"> Celebrate what's happened to her, heaven and believers and apostles and prophets, for God has condemned her as she condemned you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 5:2"> A powerful angel picked up a rock the size of a large millstone and threw it into the sea, saying, “With this kind of violence the great city of Babylon will be thrown down, and will never exist again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:13; Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34"> “Never again will anyone hear music in you: the sound of harps, singers, flutes, and trumpets. Never again will craftsmen of any trade work in you. Never again will the sound of a mill be heard in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Isaiah 23:8; Nahum 3:4"> Never again will the light of a lamp shine in you. Never again will the voices of bridegroom and bride be heard in you. Your traders led the world. Through your witchcraft all the nations were deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Jeremiah 51:49"> In her the blood of prophets and believers was found, and of all those who have been killed on the earth.”</span>
