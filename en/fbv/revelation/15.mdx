<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Revelation 15:6–7; Revelation 21:9"> Then I saw another amazing, important sign in heaven. Seven angels held the seven last plagues which complete God's judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 12:11; Revelation 19:1–7"> I saw what looked like a sea of glass mixed with fire. Standing beside the sea of glass were those who had defeated the beast and his image and the number of his name. They had harps given to them by God</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; Exodus 15:1–18; PSALMS 139:14"> and they sang the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb: “What you have done is tremendous and marvelous, Lord God, the All-powerful One. Your ways are right and true, King of the nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “King of the ages.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; Malachi 1:11; Jeremiah 10:7"> Who would not be in awe of you, Lord? Who would not glorify your name? For only you are holy. All nations will come and worship you because you have demonstrated by your actions that you do what is right!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Numbers 1:50; Matthew 27:51"> After this I looked and the Temple of the “Tent of Witness” was opened.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="There is some debate as to what is referred to here.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Revelation 15:1; Ezekiel 44:17–18"> Out of the Temple came the seven angels who held the seven plagues, wearing clean, white linen clothes and golden sashes on their chests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> One of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the judgment of God, who lives for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Exodus 40:34–35"> The Temple was filled with smoke that came from the glory of God and from his power. Nobody was able to enter the Temple until the seven plagues that came from the seven angels were over.</span>
