<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 18:37; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22"> On hearing this report, King Hezekiah tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and entered the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 2:1; Matthew 4:14"> And he sent Eliakim the palace administrator, Shebna the scribe, and the leading priests, all wearing sackcloth, to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> to tell him, “This is what Hezekiah says: Today is a day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace; for children have come to the point of birth, but there is no strength to deliver them.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> “Give this message to Hezekiah king of Judah:<br />‘Do not let your God, in whom you trust, deceive you by saying that Jerusalem will not be delivered into the hand of the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> Surely you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the other countries, devoting them to destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.">b</a></sup>Will you then be spared?</span>

## Hezekiah’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalms 91:1–2; Psalms 123:1–4"> So Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers, read it, and went up to the house of the LORD and spread it out before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> And Hezekiah prayed before the LORD:<br />“O LORD, God of Israel, enthroned between the cherubim, You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Incline Your ear, O LORD, and hear; open Your eyes, O LORD, and see. Listen to the words that Sennacherib has sent to defy the living God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> Truly, O LORD, the kings of Assyria have laid waste these nations and their lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> They have cast their gods into the fire and destroyed them, for they were not gods, but only wood and stone—the work of human hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 67:1–2"> And now, O LORD our God, please save us from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that You alone, O LORD, are God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
