## Jehu Anointed King of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 2 Kings 4:29; 1 Kings 1:39"> Now Elisha the prophet summoned one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, “Tuck your cloak under your belt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Gird up your loins">a</a></sup>take this flask of oil, and go to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:5; 1 Kings 19:16–17; 2 Kings 9:11"> When you arrive, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, the son of Nimshi. Go in, get him away from his companions, and take him to an inner room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:16; Exodus 29:7; 1 Samuel 16:2"> Then take the flask of oil, pour it on his head, and declare, ‘This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.’ Then open the door and run. Do not delay!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:19"> and when he arrived, the army commanders were sitting there. “I have a message for you, commander,” he said.<br />“For which of us?” asked Jehu.<br />“For you, commander,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 19:16; 2 Kings 9:3"> So Jehu got up and went into the house, where the young prophet poured the oil on his head and declared, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘I anoint you king over the LORD’s people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:15; 1 Kings 18:4; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And you are to strike down the house of your master Ahab, so that I may avenge the blood of My servants the prophets and the blood of all the servants of the LORD shed by the hand of Jezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; Deuteronomy 32:36; 2 Kings 14:26"> The whole house of Ahab will perish, and I will cut off from Ahab every male,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally (all) those who urinate against a wall">b</a></sup>both slave and free, in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 21:22; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> I will make the house of Ahab like the houses of Jeroboam son of Nebat and Baasha son of Ahijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; 2 Kings 9:35–36; 2 Kings 9:3"> And on the plot of ground at Jezreel the dogs will devour Jezebel, and there will be no one to bury her.’”<br />Then the young prophet opened the door and ran.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:26; Acts 26:24; Hosea 9:7"> When Jehu went out to the servants of his master, they asked, “Is everything all right? Why did this madman come to you?”<br />“You know his kind and their babble,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “That is a lie!” they said. “Tell us now!”<br />So Jehu answered, “He talked to me about this and that, saying, ‘This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; 1 Kings 1:39; 1 Kings 1:34"> Quickly, each man took his garment and put it under Jehu on the bare steps. Then they blew the ram’s horn and proclaimed, “Jehu is king!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehu Kills Joram and Ahaziah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Kings 16:16"> Thus Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, the son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram.<br />(Now Joram and all Israel had been defending Ramoth-gilead against Hazael king of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 1 Samuel 27:9–11; 2 Chronicles 22:6"> but King Joram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or Jehoram, a variant of Joram; also in verses 17, 21, 22, 23, and 24">c</a></sup>had returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds he had suffered at the hands of the Arameans in the battle against Hazael their king.)<br />So Jehu said, “If you commanders wish to make me king, then do not let anyone escape from the city to go and tell it in Jezreel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6–7; 2 Kings 8:28–29"> Then Jehu got into his chariot and went to Jezreel, because Joram was laid up there. And Ahaziah king of Judah had gone down to see him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:4; Ezekiel 33:2–9; 2 Kings 7:14"> Now the watchman standing on the tower in Jezreel saw Jehu’s troops approaching, and he called out, “I see a company of troops!”<br />“Choose a rider,” Joram commanded. “Send him out to meet them and ask, ‘Have you come in peace?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 9:19; 2 Kings 9:22; Jeremiah 16:5"> So a horseman rode off to meet Jehu and said, “This is what the king asks: ‘Have you come in peace?’”<br />“What do you know about peace?” Jehu replied. “Fall in behind me.”<br />And the watchman reported, “The messenger reached them, but he is not coming back.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So the king sent out a second horseman, who went to them and said, “This is what the king asks: ‘Have you come in peace?’”<br />“What do you know about peace?” Jehu replied. “Fall in behind me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:27; 1 Kings 19:17; Ecclesiastes 9:10"> Again the watchman reported, “He reached them, but he is not coming back. And the charioteer is driving like Jehu son of Nimshi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or grandson of Nimshi; see verse 14.">d</a></sup>—he is driving like a madman!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 21:1–7; 2 Chronicles 22:7; Micah 1:13"> “Harness!” Joram shouted, and they harnessed his chariot.<br />Then Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah set out, each in his own chariot, and met Jehu on the property of Naboth the Jezreelite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:13; Nahum 3:4; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> When Joram saw Jehu, he asked, “Have you come in peace, Jehu?”<br />“How can there be peace,” he replied, “as long as the idolatry<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew adultery or prostitution, here a metaphor for idolatry">e</a></sup>and witchcraft of your mother Jezebel abound?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; 2 Chronicles 23:13"> Joram turned around<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally turned his hands">f</a></sup>and fled, calling out to Ahaziah, “Treachery, Ahaziah!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Then Jehu drew his bow and shot Joram between the shoulders. The arrow pierced his heart, and he slumped down in his chariot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; 1 Kings 21:24–29; Isaiah 13:1"> And Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, “Pick him up and throw him into the field of Naboth the Jezreelite. For remember that when you and I were riding together behind his father Ahab, the LORD lifted up this burden against him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Deuteronomy 5:9; 2 Chronicles 25:4"> ‘As surely as I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons yesterday, declares the LORD, so will I repay you on this plot of ground, declares the LORD.’ Now then, according to the word of the LORD, pick him up and throw him on the plot of ground.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11; 2 Kings 8:29"> When King Ahaziah of Judah saw this, he fled up the road toward Beth-haggan.<br />And Jehu pursued him, shouting, “Shoot him too!”<br />So they shot Ahaziah in his chariot on the Ascent of Gur,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Syriac, Vulgate, and LXX; Hebrew “Shoot him, too, in his chariot!” (They did this) on the Ascent of Gur,">g</a></sup>near Ibleam, and he fled to Megiddo and died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:30; 2 Kings 12:21"> Then his servants carried him by chariot to Jerusalem and buried him with his fathers in his tomb in the City of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; 2 Chronicles 22:1–2; 2 Kings 8:24–25"> (In the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah had become king over Judah.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jezebel’s Violent Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 23:40; Ezekiel 24:17"> Now when Jehu arrived in Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it. So she painted her eyes, adorned her head, and looked down from a window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 16:9–20"> And as Jehu entered the gate, she asked, “Have you come in peace, O Zimri, murderer of your master?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “Is there peace for Zimri, the murderer of his master?” See 1 Kings 16:10.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esther 2:15; Exodus 32:26; 2 Chronicles 11:12"> He looked up at the window and called out, “Who is on my side? Who?”<br />And two or three eunuchs looked down at him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Lamentations 1:15; Isaiah 25:10"> “Throw her down!” yelled Jehu.<br />So they threw her down, and her blood splattered on the wall and on the horses as they trampled her underfoot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 21:25; Isaiah 65:15"> Then Jehu went in and ate and drank. “Take care of this cursed woman,” he said, “and bury her, for she was the daughter of a king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Isaiah 14:18–20; Job 31:3"> But when they went out to bury her, they found nothing but her skull, her feet, and the palms of her hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; Leviticus 8:36; 2 Kings 14:25"> So they went back and told Jehu, who replied, “This is the word of the LORD, which He spoke through His servant Elijah the Tishbite: ‘On the plot of ground at Jezreel the dogs will devour the flesh of Jezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 16:4"> And Jezebel’s body will lie like dung in the field on the plot of ground at Jezreel, so that no one can say: This is Jezebel.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="See verse 10 and 1 Kings 21:23.">i</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>
