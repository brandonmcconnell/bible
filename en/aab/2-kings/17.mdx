## Hoshea the Last King of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:30"> In the twelfth year of the reign of Ahaz over Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king of Israel, and he reigned in Samaria nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 13:11; 2 Chronicles 30:5–11"> And he did evil in the sight of the LORD, but not like the kings of Israel who preceded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 19:36–37"> Shalmaneser king of Assyria attacked him, and Hoshea became his vassal and paid him tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; 2 Kings 24:20; Ezekiel 17:13–19"> But the king of Assyria discovered that Hoshea had conspired to send envoys to King So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So is likely an abbreviation for Osorkon.">a</a></sup>of Egypt, and that he had not paid tribute to the king of Assyria as in previous years. Therefore the king of Assyria arrested Hoshea and put him in prison.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Carried Captive to Assyria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 25:1–3; 2 Kings 18:9"> Then the king of Assyria invaded the whole land, marched up to Samaria, and besieged it for three years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Hosea 13:16; Deuteronomy 28:64"> In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and carried away the Israelites to Assyria, where he settled them in Halah, in Gozan by the Habor River, and in the cities of the Medes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 23:16; Psalms 106:35–41; Nehemiah 9:26"> All this happened because the people of Israel had sinned against the LORD their God, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt. They had worshiped other gods</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:9"> and walked in the customs of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the Israelites, as well as in the practices introduced by the kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Job 31:27; Deuteronomy 13:6"> The Israelites secretly did things against the LORD their God that were not right. From watchtower to fortified city, they built high places in all their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; Micah 5:14; Exodus 34:13"> They set up for themselves sacred pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:25; 2 Kings 21:6; Jeremiah 44:17"> They burned incense on all the high places like the nations that the LORD had driven out before them. They did wicked things, provoking the LORD to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:4; Exodus 20:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:23–25"> They served idols, although the LORD had told them, “You shall not do this thing.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Exodus 20:4–6; Deuteronomy 5:8–10">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; 1 Samuel 9:9; Jeremiah 18:11"> Yet through all His prophets and seers, the LORD warned Israel and Judah, saying, “Turn from your wicked ways and keep My commandments and statutes, according to the entire Law that I commanded your fathers and delivered to you through My servants the prophets.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24"> But they would not listen, and they stiffened their necks like their fathers, who did not believe the LORD their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 1:21–23"> They rejected His statutes and the covenant He had made with their fathers, as well as the decrees He had given them. They pursued worthless idols and became worthless themselves, going after the surrounding nations that the LORD had commanded them not to imitate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 14:15"> They abandoned all the commandments of the LORD their God and made for themselves two cast idols of calves and an Asherah pole. They bowed down to all the host of heaven and served Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:6; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> They sacrificed their sons and daughters in the fire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire">c</a></sup>and practiced divination and soothsaying. They devoted themselves to doing evil in the sight of the LORD, provoking Him to anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:13; Hosea 11:12"> So the LORD was very angry with Israel, and He removed them from His presence. Only the tribe of Judah remained,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:22–23; Ezekiel 16:51–52"> and even Judah did not keep the commandments of the LORD their God, but lived according to the customs Israel had introduced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Jeremiah 6:30; 2 Kings 13:3"> So the LORD rejected all the descendants of Israel. He afflicted them and delivered them into the hands of plunderers, until He had banished them from His presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11; 1 Kings 11:31; 1 Kings 14:16"> When the LORD had torn Israel away from the house of David, they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king, and Jeroboam led Israel away from following the LORD and caused them to commit a great sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 3:3; 2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11"> The Israelites persisted in all the sins that Jeroboam had committed and did not turn away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 17:13; 2 Kings 17:20"> Finally, the LORD removed Israel from His presence, as He had declared through all His servants the prophets. So Israel was exiled from their homeland into Assyria, where they are to this day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samaria Resettled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:30–31; Matthew 10:5"> Then the king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in the towns of Samaria to replace the Israelites. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its towns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 17:32; 2 Kings 17:34; 2 Kings 17:41"> Now when the settlers first lived there, they did not worship the LORD, so He sent lions among them, which killed some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 17:27; 1 Samuel 8:9; Amos 8:14"> So they spoke to the king of Assyria, saying, “The peoples that you have removed and placed in the cities of Samaria do not know the requirements of the God of the land. Because of this, He has sent lions among them, which are indeed killing them off.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:13; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:2"> Then the king of Assyria commanded: “Send back one of the priests you carried off from Samaria, and have him go back to live there and teach the requirements of the God of the land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 12:29–32; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:14"> Thus one of the priests they had carried away came and lived in Bethel, and he began to teach them how they should worship the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; Micah 4:5; 1 Kings 13:32"> Nevertheless, the people of each nation continued to make their own gods in the cities where they had settled, and they set them up in the shrines that the people of Samaria had made on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24"> The men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, the men of Cuth made Nergal, the men of Hamath made Ashima,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 19:37"> the Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech the gods of Sepharvaim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; 2 Kings 17:29; 1 Kings 13:33"> So the new residents worshiped the LORD, but they also appointed for themselves priests of all sorts to serve in the shrines of the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 17:41; Luke 16:13; Matthew 6:24"> They worshiped the LORD, but they also served their own gods according to the customs of the nations from which they had been carried away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 32:28; Genesis 35:10; 2 Kings 17:27–28"> To this day they are still practicing their former customs. None of them worship the LORD or observe the statutes, ordinances, laws, and commandments that the LORD gave the descendants of Jacob, whom He named Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 6:10; Joshua 23:16; Joshua 23:7"> For the LORD had made a covenant with the Israelites and commanded them, “Do not worship other gods or bow down to them; do not serve them or sacrifice to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 6:13; Leviticus 19:32"> Instead, worship the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm. You are to bow down to Him and offer sacrifices to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 19:37; Deuteronomy 5:31–2; Deuteronomy 4:44–45"> And you must always be careful to observe the statutes, ordinances, laws, and commandments He wrote for you. Do not worship other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:23; Deuteronomy 6:12; Deuteronomy 8:14–18"> Do not forget the covenant I have made with you. Do not worship other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:74–75; 1 Samuel 12:24; Matthew 10:28"> but worship the LORD your God, and He will deliver you from the hands of all your enemies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:28; 2 Kings 17:8; Jeremiah 13:23"> But they would not listen, and they persisted in their former customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Zephaniah 1:5; 1 Kings 18:21"> So these nations worshiped the LORD but also served their idols, and to this day their children and grandchildren continue to do as their fathers did.</span>
</div>
</div>
