## The Divisions of the Gatekeepers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; Psalms 44:1"> These were the divisions of the gatekeepers:<br />From the Korahites: Meshelemiah son of Kore, one of the sons of Asaph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, and Eliehoenai the seventh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> And Obed-edom also had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peullethai the eighth. For God had blessed Obed-edom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> Also to his son Shemaiah were born sons who ruled over their families because they were strong, capable men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Shemaiah’s sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad; his brothers were Elihu and Semachiah, also capable men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these were descendants of Obed-edom; they and their sons and brothers were capable men with strength to do the work—62 in all from Obed-edom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> Meshelemiah also had sons and brothers who were capable men—18 in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Hosah the Merarite also had sons: Shimri the first (although he was not the firstborn, his father had appointed him as the first),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. The sons and brothers of Hosah numbered 13 in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> These divisions of the gatekeepers, through their chief men, had duties for ministering in the house of the LORD, just as their relatives did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> They cast lots for each gate, according to their families, young and old alike.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The lot for the East Gate fell to Shelemiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shelemiah is a variant of Meshelemiah; see verse 2.">a</a></sup><br />Then lots were cast for his son Zechariah, a wise counselor, and the lot for the North Gate fell to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> The lot for the South Gate fell to Obed-edom, and the lot for the storehouses to his sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> The lots for the West Gate and the Shallecheth Gate on the ascending highway<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or on the upper road">b</a></sup>fell to Shuppim and Hosah.<br />There were guards stationed at every watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> Each day there were six Levites on the east, four on the north, four on the south, and two pairs at the storehouse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> As for the court<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew parbar; twice in this verse">c</a></sup>on the west, there were four at the highway and two at the court.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These were the divisions of the gatekeepers who were descendants of Korah and Merari.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Treasurers, Officers, and Judges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> Now their fellow Levites were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew As for the Levites, Ahijah was">d</a></sup>in charge of the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the dedicated things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> From the descendants of Ladan, who were Gershonites through Ladan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Ladan is a variant of Libni; see 1 Chronicles 6:17.">e</a></sup>and heads of the families of Ladan the Gershonite, were Jehieli,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jehieli is a variant of Jehiel; also in verse 22; see 1 Chronicles 23:8.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> the sons of Jehieli, Zetham, and his brother Joel. They were in charge of the treasuries of the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> From the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> Shebuel, a descendant of Gershom son of Moses, was the officer in charge of the treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> His relatives through Eliezer included Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jeshaiah is a variant of Isshiah; see 1 Chronicles 24:21.">g</a></sup>his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> This Shelomith and his brothers were in charge of all the treasuries for the things dedicated by King David, by the heads of families who were the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and by the army commanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> They had dedicated some of the plunder from their battles to the repair of the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> Everything that had been dedicated by Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah, along with everything else that was dedicated, was under the care of Shelomith and his brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> From the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons had the outside duties<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or the duties outside (the temple) or the duties outside (Jerusalem)">h</a></sup>as officers and judges over Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> From the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his relatives, 1,700 capable men, had charge of the affairs of Israel west of the Jordan for all the work of the LORD and for the service of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> As for the Hebronites, Jerijah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Jerijah is a variant of Jeriah; see 1 Chronicles 23:19.">i</a></sup>was the chief of the Hebronites, according to the genealogies of his ancestors. In the fortieth year of David’s reign the records were searched, and strong, capable men were found among the Hebronites at Jazer in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> Among Jerijah’s relatives there were 2,700 capable men who were heads of families. King David appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh for every matter pertaining to God and for the affairs of the king.</span>
</div>
</div>
