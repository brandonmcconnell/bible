## Preparations for the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1; 1 Chronicles 21:18–28; Genesis 28:17"> Then David said, “Here shall be the house of the LORD God, as well as the altar of ascension offering for Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:17; 1 Kings 9:20–21; 1 Kings 5:17–18"> So David gave orders to gather the foreigners in the land of Israel, from whom he appointed stonecutters to prepare finished stones for building the house of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Kings 7:47"> David provided a large quantity of iron to make the nails for the doors of the gateways and for the fittings, together with more bronze than could be weighed</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6–10; Ezra 3:7; 2 Chronicles 2:3"> and more cedar logs than could be counted; for the Sidonians and Tyrians had brought a large quantity of cedar logs to David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 3:7; 1 Chronicles 29:1; John 3:30"> And David said, “My son Solomon is young and inexperienced, and the house to be built for the LORD must be exceedingly magnificent—famous and glorious throughout all lands. Therefore I must make preparations for it.” So David made lavish preparations before his death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon Anointed to Build the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; Acts 20:25–31; Acts 1:2"> Then David called for his son Solomon and instructed him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:5; 1 Kings 8:16–20; 2 Chronicles 6:7–9"> “My son,” said David to Solomon, “it was in my heart to build a house for the Name of the LORD my God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 28:3; 1 Chronicles 17:4–10"> but this word of the LORD came to me: ‘You have shed much blood and waged great wars. You are not to build a house for My Name because you have shed so much blood on the ground before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 5:4; 1 Kings 4:20"> But a son will be born to you who will be a man of rest. I will give him rest from all his enemies on every side; for his name will be Solomon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Solomon sounds like and is probably derived from the Hebrew word for peace.">a</a></sup>and I will grant to Israel peace and quiet during his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; 2 Samuel 7:13–14; 1 Chronicles 17:12–14"> He is the one who will build a house for My Name. He will be My son, and I will be his Father. And I will establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel forever.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:16; 1 Chronicles 28:20; 2 Timothy 4:22"> Now, my son, may the LORD be with you, and may you succeed in building the house of the LORD your God, as He said you would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 14:8; 1 Kings 3:9–12"> Above all, may the LORD give you insight and understanding when He puts you in command over Israel, so that you may keep the Law of the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:6–9; 1 Chronicles 28:20; 1 Chronicles 28:7"> Then you will succeed, if you carefully follow the statutes and ordinances that the LORD commanded Moses for Israel. Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or discouraged.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:3; 2 Kings 25:16; 1 Chronicles 29:4–7"> Now behold, I have taken great pains to provide for the house of the LORD—100,000 talents of gold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="100,000 talents is approximately 3,770 tons or 3,420 metric tons of gold.">b</a></sup>1,000,000 talents of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1,000,000 talents is approximately 37,700 tons or 34,200 metric tons of silver.">c</a></sup>and bronze and iron too great to be weighed. I have also provided timber and stone, and you may add to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:6; Exodus 35:32; 1 Kings 7:14"> You also have many workers: stonecutters, masons, carpenters, and men skilled in every kind of work—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:11; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 5:14"> in gold and silver, bronze and iron—craftsmen beyond number. Now begin the work, and may the LORD be with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1–6; 3 John 1:8; 1 Chronicles 29:6"> Then David ordered all the leaders of Israel to help his son Solomon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 23:25; 2 Samuel 7:1"> “Is not the LORD your God with you, and has He not granted you rest on every side? For He has given the inhabitants of the land into my hand, and the land has been subdued before the LORD and His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 4:29; 1 Chronicles 16:11"> Now set your heart and soul to seek the LORD your God. Begin building the sanctuary of the LORD God, so that you may bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD and the holy articles of God into the temple that will be built for the Name of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
</div>
