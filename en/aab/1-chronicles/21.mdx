## David’s Military Census

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:1–25; Zechariah 3:1; Job 2:4–6"> Then Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. satan without the definite article. In Job and Zechariah, hasatan (with article) is rendered 'the Accuser' as a title describing a function. Here the anarthrous form may function as a proper name">a</a></sup>rose up against Israel and incited David to take a census of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:23–24; 2 Samuel 3:10; 2 Samuel 24:15"> So David said to Joab and the commanders of the troops, “Go and count the Israelites from Beersheba to Dan and bring me a report, so that I may know their number.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Isaiah 26:15; 1 Chronicles 19:13"> But Joab replied, “May the LORD multiply His troops a hundred times over. My lord the king, are they not all servants of my lord? Why does my lord want to do this? Why should he bring guilt on Israel?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; Daniel 3:18"> Nevertheless, the king’s word prevailed against Joab. So Joab departed and traveled throughout Israel, and then he returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:9; 1 Chronicles 27:23"> And Joab reported to David the total number of the troops. In all Israel there were 1,100,000 men who drew the sword, including 470,000 in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:24; Numbers 1:47–49; 2 Samuel 20:9–10"> But Joab did not include Levi and Benjamin in the count, because the king’s command was detestable to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment for David’s Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; Joshua 7:5; 2 Samuel 21:14"> This command was also evil in the sight of God; so He struck Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; 1 John 1:9; Genesis 34:7"> Then David said to God, “I have sinned greatly because I have done this thing. Now I beg You to take away the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 24:11; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> And the LORD instructed Gad, David’s seer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 13:21–22; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:29–31"> “Go and tell David that this is what the LORD says: ‘I am offering you three options. Choose one of them, and I will carry it out against you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20"> So Gad went and said to David, “This is what the LORD says: ‘You must choose</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27; Acts 12:23; Ezekiel 14:19–21"> between three years of famine, three months of being swept away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX and Vulgate of fleeing; see 2 Samuel 24:13.">b</a></sup>before your enemies and overtaken by their swords, or three days of the sword of the LORD—days of plague upon the land, with the Angel of the LORD ravaging every part of Israel.’ Now then, decide how I should reply to Him who sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; Isaiah 55:7; Philippians 1:23"> David answered Gad, “I am deeply distressed. Please, let me fall into the hand of the LORD, for His mercies are very great; but do not let me fall into the hands of men.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Plague on Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 2 Kings 19:35"> So the LORD sent a plague upon Israel, and seventy thousand men of Israel fell dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:6; Exodus 32:14; Jeremiah 26:18"> Then God sent an Angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See note on 2 Sam 24:16.">c</a></sup>to destroy Jerusalem, but as the Angel was doing so, the LORD saw it and relented from the calamity, and He said to the Angel who was destroying the people, “Enough! Withdraw Your hand now!”<br />At that time the Angel of the LORD was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Ornan is a variant of Araunah; also in verses 18–28; see 2 Samuel 24:16.">d</a></sup>the Jebusite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; 1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 6:17"> When David lifted up his eyes and saw the Angel of the LORD standing between heaven and earth, with a drawn sword in His hand stretched out over Jerusalem, David and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell facedown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:63; 2 Samuel 24:17; John 10:11–12"> And David said to God, “Was it not I who gave the order to count the people? I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or I, the shepherd, see 2 Samuel 24:17 DSS and LXX.">e</a></sup>am the one who has sinned and acted wickedly. But these sheep, what have they done? O LORD my God, please let Your hand fall upon me and my father’s house, but do not let this plague remain upon Your people.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Builds an Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1; 2 Samuel 24:18; 1 Chronicles 21:11"> Then the Angel of the LORD ordered Gad to tell David to go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:6; John 2:5; 2 Kings 5:10–14"> So David went up at the word that Gad had spoken in the name of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 6:11"> Now Ornan was threshing wheat when he turned and saw the Angel; and his four sons who were with him hid themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; 2 Samuel 24:18–20"> David came to Ornan, and when Ornan looked out and saw David, he left the threshing floor and bowed facedown before David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:21; 1 Kings 21:2; Numbers 25:8"> Then David said to Ornan, “Grant me the site of this threshing floor, that I may build an altar to the LORD. Sell it to me for the full price, so that the plague upon the people may be halted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:22–23; 1 Samuel 6:14; Genesis 23:4–6"> Ornan said to David, “Take it! May my lord the king do whatever seems good to him. Look, I will give the oxen for the ascension offerings, the threshing sledges for the wood, and the wheat for the grain offering—I will give it all.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16–17; Genesis 14:23; Genesis 23:13"> “No,” replied King David, “I insist on paying the full price, for I will not take for the LORD what belongs to you, nor will I offer ascension offerings that cost me nothing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:24–25"> So David paid Ornan six hundred shekels of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="600 shekels is approximately 15.1 pounds or 6.8 kilograms of gold.">f</a></sup>for the site.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Judges 6:21; Proverbs 15:8"> And there he built an altar to the LORD and offered ascension offerings and well-being offerings. He called upon the LORD, who answered him with fire from heaven on the altar of ascension offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:11; Psalms 103:20; 1 Chronicles 21:15–16"> Then the LORD spoke to the Angel, who put His sword back into its sheath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> At that time, when David saw that the LORD had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he offered sacrifices there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:39; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 2 Chronicles 1:13"> For the tabernacle of the LORD that Moses had made in the wilderness and the altar of ascension offering were presently at the high place in Gibeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Hebrews 12:28–29; Revelation 15:4"> but David could not go before it to inquire of God, because he was afraid of the sword of the Angel of the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
