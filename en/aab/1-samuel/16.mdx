## Samuel Anoints David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 1 Samuel 15:23; Psalms 78:68–71"> Now the LORD said to Samuel, “How long are you going to mourn for Saul, since I have rejected him as king over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go. I am sending you to Jesse of Bethlehem, for I have selected from his sons a king for Myself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:12; 1 Samuel 20:29; Matthew 10:16"> “How can I go?” Samuel asked. “Saul will hear of it and kill me!”<br />The LORD answered, “Take a heifer with you and say, ‘I have come to sacrifice to the LORD.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 15:11"> Then invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will show you what you are to do. You are to anoint for Me the one I indicate.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13; 1 Chronicles 12:17–18; 1 Samuel 21:1"> So Samuel did what the LORD had said and went to Bethlehem. When the elders of the town met him, they trembled and asked, “Do you come in peace?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Leviticus 20:7–8; Exodus 19:10"> “In peace,” he replied. “I have come to sacrifice to the LORD. Consecrate yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice.”<br />Then he consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 17:22"> When they arrived, Samuel saw Eliab and said, “Surely here before the LORD is His anointed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:24; Jeremiah 17:10; Luke 16:15"> But the LORD said to Samuel, “Do not consider his appearance or height, for I have rejected him; the LORD does not see as man does. For man sees the outward appearance, but the LORD sees the heart.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13"> Then Jesse called Abinadab and presented him to Samuel, who said, “The LORD has not chosen this one either.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Next Jesse presented Shammah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shammah is a variant of Shimeah, Shimea, and Shimei; see 2 Samuel 13:3, 2 Samuel 21:21, and 1 Chronicles 2:13.">a</a></sup>but Samuel said, “The LORD has not chosen this one either.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–15"> Thus Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel, but Samuel told him, “The LORD has not chosen any of these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Samuel 13:3"> And Samuel asked him, “Are these all the sons you have?”<br />“There is still the youngest,” Jesse replied, “but he is tending the sheep.”<br />“Send for him,” Samuel replied. “For we will not sit down to eat until he arrives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:7; Psalms 89:19–20; Song of Solomon 5:10"> So Jesse sent for his youngest son and brought him in. He was ruddy, with beautiful eyes and a handsome appearance. And the LORD said, “Rise and anoint him, for he is the one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:6; Hebrews 1:9; John 3:34"> So Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the presence of his brothers, and the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon David from that day forward. Then Samuel set out and went to Ramah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Serves Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:11; 1 Samuel 18:12; Judges 9:23"> Now the Spirit of the LORD departed from Saul, and a spirit of distress<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or a harmful spirit; similarly in verses 15, 16, and 23">b</a></sup>from the LORD began to torment him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Saul’s servants said to him, “Surely a spirit of distress from God is tormenting you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Kings 10:8; 1 Samuel 19:9"> Let our lord command your servants here to seek out someone who can skillfully play the harp. Whenever the spirit of distress from God is upon you, he is to play it, and you will be well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Saul commanded his servants, “Find me someone who plays well, and bring him to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 39:23; Genesis 39:2"> One of the servants answered, “I have seen a son of Jesse of Bethlehem who knows how to play the harp. He is a mighty man of valor, a warrior, eloquent and handsome, and the LORD is with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 17:15; Psalms 78:70–72"> So Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, “Send me your son David, who is with the sheep.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Proverbs 18:16; 1 Samuel 17:18"> And Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and one young goat and sent them to Saul with his son David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:46; Proverbs 22:29; Psalms 146:3"> When David came to Saul and entered his service, Saul loved him very much, and David became his armor-bearer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Saul sent word to Jesse, saying, “Let David remain in my service, for I am pleased with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14–16; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; Luke 11:24–26"> And whenever the spirit from God came upon Saul, David would pick up his harp and play. Then Saul would find relief and feel better, and the spirit of distress would depart from him.</span>
</div>
</div>
