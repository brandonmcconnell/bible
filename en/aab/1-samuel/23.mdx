## David Delivers Keilah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; Micah 6:15; Nehemiah 3:17–18"> Now it was reported to David, “Look, the Philistines are fighting against Keilah and looting the threshing floors.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:4; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 5:23"> So David inquired of the LORD, “Should I go and attack these Philistines?”<br />And the LORD said to David, “Go and attack the Philistines and save Keilah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:5; Psalms 11:1; 1 Samuel 23:15"> But David’s men said to him, “Look, we are afraid here in Judah; how much more if we go to Keilah against the armies of the Philistines?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:7; Joshua 8:7; 2 Kings 3:18"> Once again, David inquired of the LORD, and the LORD answered him: “Go at once to Keilah, for I will deliver the Philistines into your hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then David and his men went to Keilah, fought against the Philistines, and carried off their livestock, striking them with a mighty blow. So David saved the people of Keilah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 14:18"> (Now Abiathar son of Ahimelech had brought the ephod with him when he fled to David at Keilah.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul Pursues David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4–6; Job 20:5; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> When Saul was told that David had gone to Keilah, he said, “God has delivered him into my hand, for he has trapped himself by entering a town with gates and bars.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then Saul summoned all his troops to go to war at Keilah and besiege David and his men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Samuel 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> When David learned that Saul was plotting evil against him, he said to Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; 1 Samuel 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> And David said, “O LORD, God of Israel, Your servant has heard that Saul intends to come to Keilah and destroy the city on my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Psalms 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> Will the citizens of Keilah surrender me into his hand?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit this question.">a</a></sup>Will Saul come down, as Your servant has heard? O LORD, God of Israel, please tell Your servant.”<br />“He will,” said the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:20; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 62:1"> So David asked, “Will the citizens of Keilah surrender me and my men into the hand of Saul?”<br />“They will,” said the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:2; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Samuel 30:9–10"> Then David and his men, about six hundred strong, set out and departed from Keilah, moving from place to place. When Saul was told that David had escaped from Keilah, he declined to go forth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 54:3–4; Psalms 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> And David stayed in the wilderness strongholds and in the hill country of the Wilderness of Ziph. Day after day Saul searched for him, but God would not deliver David into his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> While David was in Horesh in the Wilderness of Ziph, he saw that Saul had come out to take his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> And Saul’s son Jonathan came to David in Horesh and strengthened his hand in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; 1 Samuel 24:20; Acts 28:16"> saying, “Do not be afraid, for my father Saul will never lay a hand on you. And you will be king over Israel, and I will be your second-in-command. Even my father Saul knows this is true.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 18:3"> So the two of them made a covenant before the LORD. And David remained in Horesh, while Jonathan went home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3; Psalms 54:1–7"> Then the Ziphites came up to Saul at Gibeah and said, “Is not David hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hachilah south of Jeshimon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> Now, O king, come down whenever your soul desires, and we will be responsible for delivering him into your hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; Micah 3:11"> “May you be blessed by the LORD,” replied Saul, “for you have had compassion on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> Please go and prepare further. Investigate and watch carefully where he goes and who has seen him there, for I am told that he is extremely cunning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> Observe and find out all the places where he hides. Then come back to me with certainty, and I will go with you. If he is in the land, I will search him out among all the clans of Judah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> So they set out and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. Now David and his men were in the Wilderness of Maon in the Arabah south of Jeshimon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; 1 Samuel 23:28"> and Saul and his men went to seek him. When David was told about it, he went down to the rock and stayed in the Wilderness of Maon. And when Saul heard of this, he pursued David there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalms 140:1–9"> Saul was proceeding along one side of the mountain, and David and his men along the other side. Even though David was hurrying to get away, Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to capture them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 19:9; Psalms 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Then a messenger came to Saul, saying, “Come quickly, for the Philistines have raided the land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Saul broke off his pursuit of David and went to meet the Philistines. That is why that place is called Sela-hammahlekoth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Sela-hammahlekoth means Rock of Escape.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Joshua 15:62; Ezekiel 47:10"> And David went up from there and lived in the strongholds of En-gedi.</span>
</div>
</div>
