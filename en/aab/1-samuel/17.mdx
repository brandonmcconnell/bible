## Goliath’s Challenge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 2 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Chronicles 11:13"> Now the Philistines gathered their forces for war at Socoh in Judah, and they camped between Socoh and Azekah in Ephes-dammim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:19"> Saul and the men of Israel assembled and camped in the Valley of Elah, arraying themselves for battle against the Philistines.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The Philistines stood on one hill and the Israelites stood on another, with the valley between them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; 2 Samuel 21:16–22; 1 Chronicles 20:4–8"> Then a champion named Goliath, who was from Gath, came out from the Philistine camp. He was six cubits and a span in height,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Goliath was approximately 9 feet 9 inches or 297 centimeters tall; LXX, DSS, and Josephus four cubits and a span in height (approximately 6 feet 9 inches or 206 centimeters tall).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and he had a bronze helmet on his head. He wore a bronze coat of mail weighing five thousand shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5,000 shekels is approximately 125.6 pounds or 57 kilograms.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:45; 2 Chronicles 9:15; 1 Kings 10:16"> and he had armor of bronze on his legs and a javelin of bronze slung between his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 17:41; 1 Chronicles 11:23"> The shaft of his spear was like a weaver’s beam, and its iron point weighed six hundred shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="600 shekels is approximately 15.1 pounds or 6.8 kilograms.">c</a></sup>In addition, his shield bearer went before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And Goliath stood and shouted to the ranks of Israel, “Why do you come out and array yourselves for battle? Am I not a Philistine, and are you not servants of Saul? Choose one of your men and have him come down against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1"> If he is able to fight me and kill me, then we will be your servants. But if I prevail against him and kill him, then you shall be our servants and work for us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25–26; 1 Samuel 17:45; 1 Samuel 17:36"> Then the Philistine said, “I defy the ranks of Israel this day! Give me a man to fight!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 31:8; Proverbs 28:1"> On hearing the words of the Philistine, Saul and all the Israelites were dismayed and greatly afraid.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Accepts the Challenge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:10–11; Genesis 35:19; 1 Samuel 16:18"> Now David was the son of a man named Jesse, an Ephrathite from Bethlehem of Judah who had eight sons. And in the days of Saul, Jesse was old and well along in years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX and Syriac; Hebrew he had become advanced among men">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 21:21; 1 Samuel 17:28"> The three older sons of Jesse had followed Saul into battle: The firstborn was Eliab, the second was Abinadab, and the third was Shammah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; Genesis 25:23"> And David was the youngest.<br />The three oldest had followed Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 16:19–23"> but David went back and forth from Saul to tend his father’s sheep in Bethlehem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Luke 4:2"> For forty days the Philistine came forward every morning and evening to take his stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 17:28; Ruth 2:14"> One day Jesse said to his son David, “Take this ephah of roasted grain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="An ephah is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters of roasted grain.">e</a></sup>and these ten loaves of bread for your brothers and hurry to their camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:14; 1 Samuel 16:20; 2 Samuel 17:29"> Take also these ten portions of cheese to the commander of their unit. Check on the welfare of your brothers and bring back an assurance from them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or some token from them or some pledge from them">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They are with Saul and all the men of Israel in the Valley of Elah, fighting against the Philistines.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:5; Luke 19:43; Ephesians 6:1–2"> So David got up early in the morning, left the flock with a keeper, loaded up, and set out as Jesse had instructed him. He reached the camp as the army was marching out to its position and shouting the battle cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Israel and the Philistines arrayed in formation against each other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Judges 18:15; Isaiah 10:28"> Then David left his supplies in the care of the quartermaster and ran to the battle line. When he arrived, he asked his brothers how they were doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:8–10"> And as he was speaking with them, suddenly the champion named Goliath, the Philistine from Gath, came forward from the ranks of the Philistines and shouted his usual words, which David also heard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6–7; Leviticus 26:36; Isaiah 7:2"> When all the men of Israel saw Goliath, they fled from him in great fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 15:16; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 3:12"> Now the men of Israel had been saying, “Do you see how this man keeps coming out to defy Israel? To the man who kills him the king will give great riches. And he will give him his daughter in marriage and exempt his father’s house from taxation in Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:26; 1 Samuel 11:2; 1 Samuel 14:6"> David asked the men who were standing with him, “What will be done for the man who kills this Philistine and removes this disgrace from Israel? Just who is this uncircumcised Philistine, that he should defy the armies of the living God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25"> The people told him about the offer, saying, “That is what will be done for the man who kills him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 10:36; Genesis 37:11; Proverbs 18:19"> Now when David’s oldest brother Eliab heard him speaking to the men, his anger burned against David. “Why have you come down here?” he asked. “And with whom did you leave those few sheep in the wilderness? I know your pride and wickedness of heart—you have come down to see the battle!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Proverbs 15:1"> “What have I done now?” said David. “Was it not just a question?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26–27"> Then he turned from him toward another and asked about the offer, and those people answered him just as the first ones had answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29"> Now David’s words were overheard and reported to Saul, who sent for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; Deuteronomy 20:1–3; Psalms 3:6"> And David said to Saul, “Let no man’s heart fail on account of this Philistine. Your servant will go and fight him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 13:31; Deuteronomy 9:2; Revelation 13:4"> But Saul replied, “You cannot go out against this Philistine to fight him. You are just a boy, and he has been a warrior from his youth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> David replied, “Your servant has been tending his father’s sheep, and whenever a lion or a bear came and carried off a lamb from the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Amos 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:17–18; Daniel 6:22"> I went after it, struck it down, and delivered the lamb from its mouth. If it reared up against me, I would grab it by its fur, strike it down, and kill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; Acts 5:38–39; Romans 2:28–29"> Your servant has killed lions and bears; this uncircumcised Philistine will be like one of them, for he has defied the armies of the living God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Psalms 138:7–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> David added, “The LORD, who delivered me from the claws of the lion and the bear, will deliver me from the hand of this Philistine.”<br />“Go,” said Saul, “and may the LORD be with you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Slays Goliath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then Saul clothed David in his own tunic, put a bronze helmet on his head, and dressed him in armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 1:7"> David strapped his sword over the tunic and tried to walk, but he was not accustomed to them.<br />“I cannot walk in these,” David said to Saul. “I am not accustomed to them.” So David took them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Matthew 10:10; Judges 15:15–16"> And David took his staff in his hand, selected five smooth stones from the brook, and put them in the pouch of his shepherd’s bag. And with his sling in hand, he approached the Philistine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Now the Philistine came closer and closer to David, with his shield-bearer before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; 1 Samuel 17:33; Psalms 123:3–4"> When the Philistine looked and saw David, he despised him because he was just a boy, ruddy and handsome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> “Am I a dog,” he said to David, “that you come at me with sticks?” And the Philistine cursed David by his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9–10; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Kings 20:10–11"> “Come here,” he called to David, “and I will give your flesh to the birds of the air and the beasts of the field!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; 2 Chronicles 32:8; 2 Corinthians 3:5"> But David said to the Philistine, “You come against me with sword and spear and javelin, but I come against you in the name of the LORD of Hosts, the God of the armies of Israel, whom you have defied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Joshua 4:24; 2 Kings 19:19"> This day the LORD will deliver you into my hand. This day I will strike you down, cut off your head, and give the carcasses of the Philistine army to the birds of the air and the creatures of the earth. Then the whole world will know that there is a God in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 44:6–7; Zechariah 4:6; Hosea 1:7"> And all those assembled here will know that it is not by sword or spear that the LORD saves; for the battle is the LORD’s, and He will give all of you into our hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 27:1"> As the Philistine started forward to attack him, David ran quickly toward the battle line to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; 2 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 22:34"> Then David reached into his bag, took out a stone, and slung it, striking the Philistine on the forehead. The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell facedown on the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Judges 15:15; 1 Samuel 17:39; 1 Samuel 23:21"> Thus David prevailed over the Philistine with a sling and a stone; without a sword in his hand he struck down the Philistine and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 21:9; 2 Samuel 23:21"> David ran and stood over him. He grabbed the Philistine’s sword and pulled it from its sheath and killed him, and he cut off his head with the sword.<br />When the Philistines saw that their hero was dead, they turned and ran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Joshua 15:11; Joshua 15:33–36; Joshua 15:45–46"> Then the men of Israel and Judah charged forward with a shout and pursued the Philistines to the entrance of Gath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew of Gai; that is, of the valley">g</a></sup>and to the gates of Ekron. And the bodies of the Philistines were strewn along the Shaaraim road to Gath and Ekron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; 2 Kings 7:7–16; Jeremiah 30:16"> When the Israelites returned from their pursuit of the Philistines, they plundered their camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 16:33; Joshua 4:7–8; 1 Samuel 21:9"> David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem, and he put Goliath’s weapons in his own tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:21–22; 1 Samuel 17:58"> As Saul had watched David going out to confront the Philistine, he said to Abner the commander of the army, “Abner, whose son is this young man?”<br />“As surely as you live, O king,” Abner replied, “I do not know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> “Find out whose son this young man is!” said the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:54"> So when David returned from killing the Philistine, still holding his head in his hand, Abner took him and brought him before Saul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 16:18–19"> “Whose son are you, young man?” asked Saul.<br />“I am the son of your servant Jesse of Bethlehem,” David replied.</span>
</div>
</div>
