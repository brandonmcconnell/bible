## The LORD Calls Samuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 8:11–12; Psalms 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:21"> And the boy Samuel ministered to the LORD before Eli.<br />Now in those days the word of the LORD was rare, and visions were scarce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:15; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> And at that time Eli, whose eyesight had grown so dim that he could not see, was lying in his room.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> Before the lamp of God had gone out, Samuel was lying down in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was located.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> Then the LORD called to Samuel, and he answered, “Here I am.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am, for you have called me.”<br />“I did not call,” Eli replied. “Go back and lie down.”<br />So he went and lay down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; 1 Samuel 4:16; 2 Samuel 18:22"> Once again the LORD called, “Samuel!”<br />So Samuel got up, went to Eli, and said, “Here I am, for you have called me.”<br />“My son, I did not call,” Eli replied. “Go back and lie down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, because the word of the LORD had not yet been revealed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> Once again, for the third time, the LORD called to Samuel. He got up, went to Eli, and said, “Here I am, for you have called me.”<br />Then Eli realized that it was the LORD who was calling the boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalms 85:8"> “Go and lie down,” he said to Samuel, “and if He calls you, say, ‘Speak, LORD, for Your servant is listening.’”<br />So Samuel went and lay down in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:4–6"> Then the LORD came and stood there, calling as before, “Samuel! Samuel!”<br />And Samuel answered, “Speak, for Your servant is listening.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; 2 Kings 21:12"> Then the LORD said to Samuel, “I am about to do something in Israel at which the ears of all who hear it will tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> On that day I will carry out against Eli everything I have spoken about his house, from beginning to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; 1 Samuel 2:12"> I told him that I would judge his house forever for the iniquity of which he knows, because his sons blasphemed God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew made themselves contemptible">a</a></sup>and he did not restrain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli, ‘The iniquity of Eli’s house shall never be purged by sacrifice or offering.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samuel Shares the Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 1:9"> Samuel lay down until the morning; then he opened the doors of the house of the LORD. He was afraid to tell Eli the vision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> but Eli called to him and said, “Samuel, my son.”<br />“Here I am,” answered Samuel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalms 141:5"> “What was the message He gave you?” Eli asked. “Do not hide it from me. May God punish you, and ever so severely, if you hide from me anything He said to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> So Samuel told him everything and did not hide a thing from him.<br />“He is the LORD,” replied Eli. “Let Him do what is good in His eyes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; 1 Samuel 9:6; 1 Kings 8:56"> And Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him, and He let none of Samuel’s words fall to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> So all Israel from Dan to Beersheba knew that Samuel was confirmed as a prophet of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Genesis 12:7; 1 Samuel 3:4"> And the LORD continued to appear at Shiloh, because there He revealed Himself to Samuel by His word.</span>
</div>
