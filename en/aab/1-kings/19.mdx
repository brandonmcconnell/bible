## Elijah Flees from Jezebel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; 1 Kings 21:25; 1 Kings 21:5–7"> Now Ahab told Jezebel everything that Elijah had done and how he had killed all the prophets with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 2 Kings 6:31; 1 Kings 20:10"> So Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, saying, “May the gods deal with me, and ever so severely, if by this time tomorrow I have not made your life like the lives of those you killed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Matthew 26:70–74; Matthew 26:56"> And Elijah was afraid and ran for his life. When he came to Beersheba in Judah, he left his servant there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Numbers 11:15; Jonah 4:8"> while he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness. He sat down under a broom tree and prayed that he might die. “I have had enough, LORD,” he said. “Take my life, for I am no better than my fathers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7; Hebrews 13:5"> Then he lay down under the broom tree and fell asleep.<br />Suddenly an angel touched him and said, “Get up and eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Matthew 4:11; Matthew 6:32"> And he looked around, and there by his head was a cake of bread baked over hot coals, and a jar of water. So he ate and drank and lay down again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:13–14; Deuteronomy 33:25"> A second time the angel of the LORD returned and touched him, saying, “Get up and eat, or the journey will be too much for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Exodus 3:1"> So he got up and ate and drank. And strengthened by that food, he walked forty days and forty nights until he reached Horeb,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai">a</a></sup>the mountain of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Speaks to Elijah at Horeb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Exodus 33:21–22; Jeremiah 2:18"> There Elijah entered a cave and spent the night. And the word of the LORD came to him, saying, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Kings 18:22; Exodus 34:14"> “I have been very zealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts,” he replied, “but the Israelites have forsaken Your covenant, torn down Your altars, and killed Your prophets with the sword. I am the only one left, and they are seeking my life as well.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Romans 11:3; here and in verse 14">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Psalms 50:3; Job 38:1"> Then the LORD said, “Go out and stand on the mountain before the LORD. Behold, the LORD is about to pass by.”<br />And a great and mighty wind tore into the mountains and shattered the rocks before the LORD, but the LORD was not in the wind.<br />After the wind there was an earthquake, but the LORD was not in the earthquake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:16; Zechariah 4:6; Deuteronomy 4:33"> After the earthquake there was a fire, but the LORD was not in the fire.<br />And after the fire came a still, small voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Isaiah 6:2; Genesis 16:8"> When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his cloak and went out and stood at the mouth of the cave. Suddenly a voice came to him and said, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9–10; Jeremiah 22:9; Isaiah 62:1"> “I have been very zealous for the LORD, the God of Hosts,” he replied, “but the Israelites have forsaken Your covenant, torn down Your altars, and killed Your prophets with the sword. I am the only one left, and they are seeking my life as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Jeremiah 1:10; 2 Kings 8:7–15"> Then the LORD said to him, “Go back by the way you came, and go to the Desert of Damascus. When you arrive, you are to anoint Hazael as king over Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1–14; 1 Kings 19:19–21; 2 Kings 2:9"> You are also to anoint Jehu son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or grandson; see 2 Kings 9:14.">c</a></sup>of Nimshi as king over Israel and Elisha son of Shaphat from Abel-meholah to succeed you as prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 13:22; 2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 13:3"> Then Jehu will put to death whoever escapes the sword of Hazael, and Elisha will put to death whoever escapes the sword of Jehu.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 10:20–22"> Nevertheless, I have reserved seven thousand in Israel—all whose knees have not bowed to Baal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Romans 11:4">d</a></sup>and whose mouths have not kissed him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Call of Elisha

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8; 2 Kings 2:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:14"> So Elijah departed and found Elisha son of Shaphat. He was plowing with twelve teams of oxen, and he was with the twelfth team. Elijah passed by him and threw his cloak around him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:21–22; Luke 9:61–62; Acts 20:37"> So Elisha left the oxen, ran after Elijah, and said, “Please let me kiss my father and mother goodbye, and then I will follow you.”<br />“Go on back,” Elijah replied, “for what have I done to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:22; 2 Timothy 4:11; Philemon 1:13"> So Elisha turned back from him, took his pair of oxen, and slaughtered them. Using the oxen’s equipment for fuel, he cooked the meat and gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he set out to follow and serve Elijah.</span>
</div>
</div>
