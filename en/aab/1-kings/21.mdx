## Naboth’s Vineyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:33; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Joshua 19:18"> Some time after these events, Naboth the Jezreelite owned a vineyard in Jezreel next to the palace of Ahab king of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:14; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 6:9"> So Ahab said to Naboth, “Give me your vineyard to use as a vegetable garden, since it is next to my palace. I will give you a better vineyard in its place—or if you prefer, I will give you its value in silver.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 36:7; Leviticus 25:23; Ezekiel 46:18"> But Naboth replied, “The LORD forbid that I should give you the inheritance of my fathers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 20:43; James 1:14; Jonah 4:9"> So Ahab went to his palace, sullen and angry because Naboth the Jezreelite had told him, “I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers.” He lay down on his bed, turned his face away, and refused to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; Genesis 3:6; 1 Kings 19:2"> Soon his wife Jezebel came in and asked, “Why are you so sullen that you refuse to eat?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 5:9–14; James 4:2–7; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> Ahab answered, “Because I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and told him, ‘Give me your vineyard for silver, or if you wish, I will give you another vineyard in its place.’ And he replied, ‘I will not give you my vineyard!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:14; Micah 7:3; 2 Samuel 13:4"> But his wife Jezebel said to him, “Do you not reign over Israel? Get up, eat some food, and be cheerful, for I will get you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jezebel’s Plot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:5; 2 Kings 10:11; Ezra 4:7–8"> Then Jezebel wrote letters in Ahab’s name, sealed them with his seal, and sent them to the elders and nobles who lived with Naboth in his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47"> In the letters she wrote:<br />“Proclaim a fast and give Naboth a seat of honor among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Acts 6:11; Leviticus 24:15–16"> But seat two scoundrels opposite him and have them testify, ‘You have cursed both God and the king!’ Then take him out and stone him to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 4:19; Leviticus 19:15; 2 Kings 10:6–7"> So the elders and nobles who lived in Naboth’s city did as Jezebel had instructed in the letters she had written to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; 1 Kings 21:8–10"> They proclaimed a fast and gave Naboth a seat of honor among the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 9:26; Deuteronomy 22:24; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> And the two scoundrels came in and sat opposite Naboth, and these men testified against him before the people, saying, “Naboth has cursed both God and the king!”<br />So they took him outside the city and stoned him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:14; 2 Samuel 11:14–24; Ecclesiastes 5:8"> Then they sent word to Jezebel: “Naboth has been stoned to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–16; Proverbs 4:17; 1 Kings 21:7"> When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned to death, she said to Ahab, “Get up and take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, who refused to give it to you for silver. For Naboth is no longer alive, but dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12–14; Romans 1:32; 2 Samuel 11:25–27"> And when Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he got up and went down to take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah Denounces Ahab and Jezebel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:12; Isaiah 26:21; 2 Kings 5:26"> Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:32; 2 Chronicles 22:9"> “Get up and go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria. See, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone to take possession of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 22:38; 2 Samuel 12:9; Habakkuk 2:9"> Tell him that this is what the LORD says: ‘Have you not murdered a man and seized his land?’<br />Then tell him that this is also what the LORD says: ‘In the place where the dogs licked up the blood of Naboth, there also the dogs will lick up your blood—yes, yours!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1 Kings 22:38">a</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:14; 1 Kings 21:25"> When Elijah arrived, Ahab said to him, “So you have found me out, my enemy.”<br />He replied, “I have found you out because you have sold yourself to do evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 25:34; 2 Kings 10:1–7"> This is what the LORD says:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See LXX; the source of the quotation is clarified in verses 17 and 23; Hebrew Behold.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will bring calamity on you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and consume your descendants;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off from Ahab every male in Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">both slave and free.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 16:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make your house like that of Jeroboam son of Nebat</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and like that of Baasha son of Ahijah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you have provoked My anger</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and caused Israel to sin.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 9:10; 2 Kings 9:30–37; 1 Kings 21:25"> And the LORD also speaks concerning Jezebel:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The dogs will devour Jezebel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by the wall of Jezreel.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Most Hebrew manuscripts; a few Hebrew manuscripts, Vulgate, and Syriac (see also 2 Kings 9:36) ‘The dogs will devour Jezebel at the plot of ground at Jezreel.’">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; 1 Kings 14:11; Jeremiah 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Anyone belonging to Ahab who dies in the city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be eaten by dogs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and anyone who dies in the field</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be eaten by the birds of the air.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Ahab’s Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; 1 Kings 21:20; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> (Surely there was never one like Ahab, who sold himself to do evil in the sight of the LORD, incited by his wife Jezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:11; Genesis 15:16; Leviticus 18:25–30"> He committed the most detestable acts by going after idols, just like the Amorites whom the LORD had driven out before the Israelites.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 6:30; Genesis 37:34; 2 Samuel 3:31"> When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, and fasted. He lay down in sackcloth and walked around meekly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Micah 7:18; Psalms 86:15"> “Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before Me? Because he has humbled himself before Me, I will not bring the calamity during his days, but I will bring it upon his house in the days of his son.”</span>
</div>
</div>
