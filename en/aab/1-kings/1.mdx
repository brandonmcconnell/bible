## Abishag Cares for David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:27–28; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> Now King David was old and well along in years, and though they covered him with blankets, he could not keep warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:11; Genesis 16:5; Micah 7:5"> So his servants said to him, “Let us search for a young virgin for our lord the king, to attend to him and care for him and lie by his side to keep him warm.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Esther 2:4; Esther 2:2"> Then they searched throughout Israel for a beautiful girl, and they found Abishag the Shunammite and brought her to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:25"> The girl was unsurpassed in beauty; she cared for the king and served him, but he had no relations with her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Adonijah Usurps the Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:1; 2 Samuel 3:4; Deuteronomy 17:15–16"> At that time Adonijah, David’s son by Haggith, began to exalt himself, saying, “I will be king!” And he acquired chariots and horsemen and fifty men to run ahead of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 3:13; Hebrews 12:5–6"> (His father had never once reprimanded him by saying, “Why do you act this way?” Adonijah was also very handsome, born next after Absalom.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> So Adonijah conferred with Joab son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, who supported him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 4:18; 2 Samuel 23:8–39; 2 Samuel 20:25"> But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David’s mighty men would not join Adonijah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 13:23–27; 2 Samuel 15:11–12"> And Adonijah sacrificed sheep, oxen, and fattened calves near the stone of Zoheleth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or the Serpent’s Stone">a</a></sup>which is next to En-rogel. He invited all his royal brothers and all the men of Judah who were servants of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 2 Samuel 12:1–15"> But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Nathan and Bathsheba before David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:4; 2 Samuel 7:12–17; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> Then Nathan said to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon, “Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, and our lord David does not know it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Acts 27:31; Genesis 19:17"> Now please, come and let me advise you. Save your own life and the life of your son Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 1:30; 1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 132:11–12"> Go at once to King David and say, ‘My lord the king, did you not swear to your maidservant, “Surely your son Solomon will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne”? Why then has Adonijah become king?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Kings 1:17–27"> Then, while you are still there speaking with the king, I will come in after you and confirm your words.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1"> So Bathsheba went to see the king in his bedroom. Since the king was very old, Abishag the Shunammite was serving him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; 1 Samuel 24:8; 1 Samuel 20:41"> And Bathsheba bowed down in homage to the king, who asked, “What is your desire?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:30; 1 Peter 3:6"> “My lord,” she replied, “you yourself swore to your maidservant by the LORD your God: ‘Surely your son Solomon will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:24; 1 Kings 1:11; Acts 3:17"> But now, behold, Adonijah has become king, and you, my lord the king, do not know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:7–10"> And he has sacrificed an abundance of oxen, fattened calves, and sheep, and has invited all the other sons of the king, as well as Abiathar the priest and Joab the commander of the army. But he has not invited your servant Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:1; Psalms 123:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8–10"> And as for you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are upon you to tell them who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Deuteronomy 31:16; Genesis 15:15"> Otherwise, when my lord the king rests with his fathers, I and my son Solomon will be counted as criminals.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 1:16–18; Genesis 24:15; Daniel 9:20"> And just then, while Bathsheba was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 1 Peter 2:17; Romans 13:7"> So the king was told, “Nathan the prophet is here.” And Nathan went in and bowed facedown before the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17–18; 1 Kings 1:5; 1 Kings 1:13–14"> “My lord the king,” said Nathan, “did you say, ‘Adonijah will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 1 Kings 1:9"> For today he has gone down and sacrificed an abundance of oxen, fattened calves, and sheep, and has invited all the sons of the king, the commanders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX Joab the commander">b</a></sup>of the army, and Abiathar the priest. And behold, they are eating and drinking before him, saying, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:10; 2 Samuel 7:2"> But me your servant he has not invited, nor Zadok the priest, nor Benaiah son of Jehoiada, nor your servant Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 1:24; 2 Kings 4:27"> Has my lord the king let this happen without informing your servant who should sit on the throne after my lord the king?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Renews His Oath to Bathsheba

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then King David said, “Call in Bathsheba for me.” So she came into the king’s presence and stood before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:9; Psalms 136:24; 1 Samuel 14:45"> And the king swore an oath, saying, “As surely as the LORD lives, who has redeemed my life from all distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:17"> I will carry out this very day exactly what I swore to you by the LORD, the God of Israel: Surely your son Solomon will reign after me, and he will sit on my throne in my place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 5:10; Daniel 3:9"> Bathsheba bowed facedown in homage to the king and said, “May my lord King David live forever!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon Anointed King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:38; 1 Kings 1:26"> Then King David said, “Call in for me Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada.” So they came before the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Chronicles 33:14"> “Take my servants with you,” said the king. “Set my son Solomon on my own mule and take him down to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 2 Samuel 15:10; 1 Samuel 10:1"> There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet are to anoint him king over Israel. You are to blow the ram’s horn and declare, ‘Long live King Solomon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 72:1–2; 1 Kings 1:13"> Then you shall go up with him, and he is to come and sit on my throne and reign in my place. For I have appointed him ruler over Israel and Judah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:6; 1 Corinthians 14:16; Psalms 72:19"> “Amen,” replied Benaiah son of Jehoiada. “May the LORD, the God of my lord the king, so declare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Kings 1:47; Joshua 1:17"> Just as the LORD was with my lord the king, so may He be with Solomon and make his throne even greater than that of my lord King David.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:33"> Then Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, along with the Cherethites and Pelethites, went down and set Solomon on King David’s mule, and they escorted him to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 89:20; 1 Kings 1:34; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tabernacle and anointed Solomon. Then they blew the ram’s horn, and all the people proclaimed, “Long live King Solomon!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; Zechariah 9:9; 2 Kings 11:20"> All the people followed him, playing flutes and rejoicing with such a great joy that the earth was split by the sound.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Adonijah Learns of Solomon’s Kingship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Job 15:21–22; Luke 17:26–29; Acts 21:31"> Now Adonijah and all his guests were finishing their feast when they heard the sound of the ram’s horn. “Why is the city in such a loud uproar?” asked Joab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 18:27"> As he was speaking, suddenly Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest arrived. “Come in,” said Adonijah, “for you are a man of valor. You must be bringing good news.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> “Not at all,” Jonathan replied. “Our lord King David has made Solomon king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And with Solomon, the king has sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada, along with the Cherethites and Pelethites, and they have set him on the king’s mule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; 1 Samuel 4:5"> Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king at Gihon, and they have gone up from there with rejoicing that rings out in the city. That is the noise you hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:23; Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:13"> Moreover, Solomon has taken his seat on the royal throne.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 47:31; 1 Kings 1:37; Ezra 6:10"> The king’s servants have also gone to congratulate our lord King David, saying, ‘May your God make the name of Solomon more famous than your own name, and may He make his throne greater than your throne.’<br />And the king has bowed in worship on his bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 3:6; Psalms 132:11–12; Psalms 145:2"> saying, ‘Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel! Today He has provided one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="LXX one of my offspring">c</a></sup>to sit on my throne, and my eyes have seen it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> At this, all the guests of Adonijah arose in terror and scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28; Exodus 21:14; Exodus 27:2"> But Adonijah, in fear of Solomon, got up and went to take hold of the horns of the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> It was reported to Solomon: “Behold, Adonijah fears King Solomon, and he has taken hold of the horns of the altar, saying, ‘Let King Solomon first swear to me not to put his servant to the sword.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Samuel 14:11; Proverbs 13:6"> And Solomon replied, “If he is a man of character, not a single hair of his will fall to the ground. But if evil is found in him, he will die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 2 Samuel 14:24; 2 Samuel 1:2"> So King Solomon summoned Adonijah down from the altar, and he came and bowed down before King Solomon, who said to him, “Go to your home.”</span>
</div>
</div>
