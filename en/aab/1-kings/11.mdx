## Solomon’s Foreign Wives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Proverbs 6:24; Nehemiah 13:23–27"> King Solomon, however, loved many foreign women along with the daughter of Pharaoh—women of Moab, Ammon, Edom, and Sidon, as well as Hittite women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:16; Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Joshua 23:12–13"> These women were from the nations about which the LORD had told the Israelites, “You must not intermarry with them, for surely they will turn your hearts after their gods.” Yet Solomon clung to these women in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Samuel 5:13–16; 2 Chronicles 11:21"> He had seven hundred wives of royal birth and three hundred concubines—and his wives turned his heart away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 11:6"> For when Solomon grew old, his wives turned his heart after other gods, and he was not wholeheartedly devoted to the LORD his God, as his father David had been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 2:13; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> Solomon followed Ashtoreth the goddess of the Sidonians and Milcom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Milcom is a variant of Molech; also in verse 33; see verse 7 and Leviticus 18:21.">a</a></sup>the abomination of the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:8; Joshua 14:14"> So Solomon did evil in the sight of the LORD; unlike his father David, he did not follow the LORD completely.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Judges 11:24; Acts 7:43"> At that time on a hill east of Jerusalem, Solomon built a high place for Chemosh the abomination of Moab and for Molech the abomination of the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11–12; 1 Kings 11:1; Ezekiel 16:22–29"> He did the same for all his foreign wives, who burned incense and sacrificed to their gods.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Anger against Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:2; 1 Kings 3:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> Now the LORD grew angry with Solomon, because his heart had turned away from the LORD, the God of Israel, who had appeared to him twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 7:17–22; 1 Kings 9:4–7"> Although He had warned Solomon explicitly not to follow other gods, Solomon did not keep the LORD’s command.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15–16; 1 Kings 11:31; 2 Kings 17:21"> Then the LORD said to Solomon, “Because you have done this and have not kept My covenant and My statutes, which I have commanded you, I will tear the kingdom away from you and give it to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 20:19; 1 Samuel 9:4–5; 2 Kings 22:19–20"> Nevertheless, for the sake of your father David, I will not do it during your lifetime; I will tear it out of the hand of your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; Deuteronomy 12:11; 1 Kings 11:32"> Yet I will not tear the whole kingdom away from him. I will give one tribe to your son for the sake of My servant David and for the sake of Jerusalem, which I have chosen.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hadad’s Return

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Psalms 89:30–34; Isaiah 13:17"> Then the LORD raised up against Solomon an adversary, Hadad the Edomite, from the royal line of Edom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Chronicles 18:12–13; Deuteronomy 20:13"> Earlier, when David was in Edom, Joab the commander of the army had gone to bury the dead and had struck down every male in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Joab and all Israel had stayed there six months, until he had killed every male in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 2:13–14; Exodus 2:1–10; 2 Kings 11:2"> But Hadad, still just a young boy, had fled to Egypt, along with some Edomites who were servants of his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:12; Deuteronomy 33:2; Deuteronomy 1:1"> Hadad and his men set out from Midian and went to Paran. They took men from Paran with them and went to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt, who gave Hadad a house and land and provided him with food.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:4; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Genesis 39:21"> There Hadad found such great favor in the sight of Pharaoh that he gave to him in marriage the sister of Queen Tahpenes, his own wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And the sister of Tahpenes bore Hadad a son named Genubath. Tahpenes herself weaned him in Pharaoh’s palace, and Genubath lived there among the sons of Pharaoh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Exodus 4:19; Matthew 2:20"> When Hadad heard in Egypt that David had rested with his fathers and that Joab, the commander of the army, was dead, he said to Pharaoh, “Let me go, that I may return to my own country.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22–23; Luke 22:35; Mark 14:31"> But Pharaoh asked him, “What have you lacked here with me that you suddenly want to go back to your own country?”<br />“Nothing,” Hadad replied, “but please let me go.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rezon’s Hostility

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 11:14; 2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 10:15–18"> And God raised up against Solomon another adversary, Rezon the son of Eliada, who had fled from his master, Hadadezer king of Zobah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:8; 2 Samuel 10:18; 1 Kings 20:34"> and had gathered men to himself. When David killed the Zobaites, Rezon captained a band of raiders and went to Damascus, where they settled and gained control.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 106:40; 1 Kings 5:4"> Rezon was Israel’s enemy throughout the days of Solomon, adding to the trouble caused by Hadad. So Rezon ruled over Aram with hostility toward Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeroboam’s Rebellion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:6; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> Now Jeroboam son of Nebat was an Ephraimite from Zeredah whose mother was a widow named Zeruah. Jeroboam was a servant of Solomon, but he rebelled against the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 9:15; Proverbs 30:32"> and this is the account of his rebellion against the king.<br />Solomon had built the supporting terraces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew the Millo">b</a></sup>and repaired the gap in the wall of the city of his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; 2 Samuel 19:20; Judges 1:22–23"> Now Jeroboam was a mighty man of valor. So when Solomon noticed that the young man was industrious, he put him in charge of the whole labor force of the house of Joseph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 1 Kings 14:2; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> During that time, the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite met Jeroboam on the road as he was going out of Jerusalem. Now Ahijah had wrapped himself in a new cloak, and the two of them were alone in the open field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–28; 1 Samuel 24:4–5"> And Ahijah took hold of the new cloak he was wearing, tore it into twelve pieces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11–12"> and said to Jeroboam, “Take ten pieces for yourself, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Behold, I will tear the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and I will give you ten tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 1 Kings 14:21"> But one tribe will remain for the sake of My servant David and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 11:9; 1 Kings 11:5–7; 1 Kings 9:5–7"> For they have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX, Syriac, and Vulgate he has; twice in this verse">c</a></sup>forsaken Me to worship Ashtoreth the goddess of the Sidonians, Chemosh the god of the Moabites, and Milcom the god of the Ammonites. They have not walked in My ways, nor done what is right in My eyes, nor kept My statutes and judgments, as Solomon’s father David did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 1 Kings 11:31; Job 11:6"> Nevertheless, I will not take the whole kingdom out of Solomon’s hand, because I have made him ruler all the days of his life for the sake of David My servant, whom I chose because he kept My commandments and statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:12; Exodus 20:5–6"> But I will take ten tribes of the kingdom from the hand of his son and give them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 8:19; 1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 15:4"> I will give one tribe to his son, so that My servant David will always have a lamp before Me in Jerusalem, the city where I chose to put My Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:26; 1 Kings 11:26; 2 Samuel 3:21"> But as for you, I will take you, and you shall reign over all that your heart desires, and you will be king over Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:8"> If you listen to all that I command you, walk in My ways, and do what is right in My sight in order to keep My statutes and commandments as My servant David did, then I will be with you. I will build you a lasting dynasty just as I built for David, and I will give Israel to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Luke 2:11; Isaiah 11:1–10"> Because of this, I will humble David’s descendants—but not forever.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:2–9; Isaiah 14:24–27; Lamentations 3:37"> Solomon therefore sought to kill Jeroboam. But Jeroboam arose and fled to Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, where he remained until the death of Solomon.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29–31"> As for the rest of the acts of Solomon—all that he did, as well as his wisdom—are they not written in the Book of the Acts of Solomon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11"> Thus the time that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel was forty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; 1 Kings 2:10; Matthew 1:7"> And Solomon rested with his fathers and was buried in the city of his father David. And his son Rehoboam reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
