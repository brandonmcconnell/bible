## Elijah’s Message to Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25; James 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:12"> After a long time, in the third year of the drought,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally And the days were many, and in the third year,">a</a></sup>the word of the LORD came to Elijah: “Go and present yourself to Ahab, and I will send rain upon the face of the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25; Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:26"> So Elijah went to present himself to Ahab. The famine was severe in Samaria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Kings 16:9; Matthew 10:28"> and Ahab summoned Obadiah, who was in charge of the palace.<br />(Now Obadiah greatly feared the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; Matthew 10:40–42; Revelation 17:4–6"> for when Jezebel had slaughtered the prophets of the LORD, Obadiah had taken a hundred prophets and hidden them, fifty men per cave, providing them with food and water.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:5–6; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:22"> Then Ahab said to Obadiah, “Go throughout the land to every spring and every valley. Perhaps we will find grass to keep the horses and mules alive so that we will not have to destroy any livestock.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3"> So they divided the land to explore. Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went the other way by himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6–8; Genesis 44:20; Isaiah 60:14"> Now as Obadiah went on his way, Elijah suddenly met him. When Obadiah recognized him, he fell facedown and said, “Is it you, my lord Elijah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Kings 18:3; 1 Peter 2:17–18"> “It is I,” he answered. “Go tell your master, ‘Elijah is here!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; 1 Kings 18:12; 1 Kings 17:18"> But Obadiah replied, “How have I sinned, that you are handing your servant over to Ahab to put me to death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 1:29; 1 Kings 18:15"> As surely as the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent someone to search for you. When they said, ‘He is not here,’ he made that kingdom or nation swear that they had not found you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 18:8; 1 Kings 18:14"> And now you say, ‘Go tell your master that Elijah is here!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 2:16; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 8:3"> I do not know where the Spirit of the LORD may carry you off when I leave you. Then when I go and tell Ahab and he does not find you, he will kill me. But I, your servant, have feared the LORD from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; Matthew 25:35; Psalms 18:21–24"> Was it not reported to my lord what I did when Jezebel slaughtered the prophets of the LORD? I hid a hundred prophets of the LORD, fifty men per cave, and I provided them with food and water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28"> And now you say, ‘Go tell your lord that Elijah is here!’ He will kill me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 18:10; Luke 2:13–14"> Then Elijah said, “As surely as the LORD of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, I will present myself to Ahab today.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah on Mount Carmel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Obadiah went to inform Ahab, who went to meet Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; 1 Kings 21:20; Acts 16:20"> When Ahab saw Elijah, he said to him, “Is that you, O troubler of Israel?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 9:9; Jeremiah 2:19"> “I have not troubled Israel,” Elijah replied, “but you and your father’s house have, for you have forsaken the commandments of the LORD and have followed the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 19:26; 1 Kings 16:33; 2 Peter 2:1"> Now summon all Israel to meet me on Mount Carmel, along with the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of Asherah who eat at Jezebel’s table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9"> So Ahab summoned all the Israelites and assembled the prophets on Mount Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15; 1 Corinthians 10:21–22"> Then Elijah approached all the people and said, “How long will you waver between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow Him. But if Baal is God, follow him.”<br />But the people did not answer a word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; 1 Kings 19:14; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Then Elijah said to the people, “I am the only remaining prophet of the LORD, but Baal has four hundred and fifty prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Get two bulls for us. Let the prophets of Baal choose one bull for themselves, cut it into pieces, and place it on the wood but not light the fire. And I will prepare the other bull and place it on the wood but not light the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then you may call on the name of your god, and I will call on the name of the LORD. The God who answers by fire, He is God.”<br />And all the people answered, “What you say is good.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, “Since you are so numerous, choose for yourselves one bull and prepare it first. Then call on the name of your god, but do not light the fire.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Matthew 6:7"> And they took the bull that was given them, prepared it, and called on the name of Baal from morning until noon, shouting, “O Baal, answer us!”<br />But there was no sound, and no one answered as they leaped around the altar they had made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:65–66; Isaiah 41:23; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> At noon Elijah began to taunt them, saying, “Shout louder, for he is a god! Perhaps he is deep in thought, or occupied, or on a journey. Perhaps he is sleeping and must be awakened!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1; Mark 5:5"> So they shouted louder and cut themselves with knives and lances, as was their custom, until the blood gushed over them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:26; 1 Samuel 18:10; Exodus 29:39"> Midday passed, and they kept on raving until the time of the evening sacrifice. But there was no response; no one answered, no one paid attention.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:16; 1 Kings 19:14; 1 Kings 19:10"> Then Elijah said to all the people, “Come near to me.” So all the people approached him, and he repaired the altar of the LORD that had been torn down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 35:10; Genesis 32:28"> And Elijah took twelve stones, one for each tribe of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of the LORD had come and said, “Israel shall be your name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Exodus 20:24–25; Judges 21:4"> And with the stones, Elijah built an altar in the name of the LORD. Then he dug a trench around the altar large enough to hold two seahs of seed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2 seahs is approximately 13.2 dry quarts or 14.6 liters (probably about 25.4 pounds or 11.5 kilograms of seed).">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 22:9; Judges 6:20; Leviticus 1:6–8"> Next, he arranged the wood, cut up the bull, placed it on the wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> and said, “Fill four waterpots and pour the water on the offering and on the wood.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some texts continue verse 33 to this point.">c</a></sup><br />“Do it a second time,” he said, and they did it a second time.<br />“Do it a third time,” he said, and they did it a third time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 18:32; 1 Kings 18:38"> So the water ran down around the altar and even filled the trench.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 18:29; 2 Kings 19:19; Exodus 3:6"> At the time of the evening sacrifice, Elijah the prophet approached the altar and said, “O LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known this day that You are God in Israel and that I am Your servant and have done all these things at Your command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Luke 1:16–17; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> Answer me, O LORD! Answer me, so that this people will know that You, the LORD, are God, and that You have turned their hearts back again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:24; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then the fire of the LORD fell and consumed the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, and the dust, and it licked up the water in the trench.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:24; 2 Chronicles 7:3; 1 Kings 18:21"> When all the people saw this, they fell facedown and said, “The LORD, He is God! The LORD, He is God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Judges 4:7; Deuteronomy 13:5"> Then Elijah ordered them, “Seize the prophets of Baal! Do not let a single one escape.” So they seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the Kishon Valley and slaughtered them there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Sends Rain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 18:1; Ecclesiastes 9:7; 1 Kings 17:1"> And Elijah said to Ahab, “Go up, eat and drink, for there is the sound of a heavy rain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Daniel 9:7; Joshua 7:6"> So Ahab went up to eat and drink. But Elijah climbed to the summit of Carmel, bent down on the ground, and put his face between his knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 18:1"> “Go and look toward the sea,” he said to his servant.<br />So the servant went and looked, and he said, “There is nothing there.”<br />Seven times Elijah said, “Go back.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 12:54; Job 8:7; Zechariah 4:10"> On the seventh time the servant reported, “There is a cloud as small as a man’s hand rising from the sea.”<br />And Elijah replied, “Go and tell Ahab, ‘Prepare your chariot and go down before the rain stops you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 18:39–40; Joshua 17:16; 1 Kings 21:1"> Meanwhile, the sky grew dark with clouds and wind, and a heavy rain began to fall. So Ahab rode away and went to Jezreel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 1:3; 2 Kings 9:1"> And the hand of the LORD came upon Elijah, and he tucked his cloak into his belt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew he girded up his loins">d</a></sup>and ran ahead of Ahab all the way to Jezreel.</span>
</div>
