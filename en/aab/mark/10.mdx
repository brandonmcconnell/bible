## Teachings about Divorce

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Mark 4:2; John 11:7"> Then Jesus left that place and went into the region of Judea, beyond the Jordan. Again the crowds came to Him and He taught them, as was His custom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 11:53–54; John 8:6"> Some Pharisees came to test Him. “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife?” they inquired.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 4:21; Luke 10:25"> “What did Moses command you?” He replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31–32; Isaiah 50:1"> They answered, “Moses permitted a man to write his wife a certificate of divorce and send her away.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 24:1.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 19:8; Nehemiah 9:26; Acts 7:51"> But Jesus told them, “Moses wrote this commandment for you because of your hardness of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2; Genesis 1:1"> However, from the beginning of creation, ‘God made them male and female.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5–6; Ephesians 5:31"> ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NE and WH do not include and be united to his wife.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Genesis 2:24; 1 Corinthians 6:16"> and the two will become one flesh.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Genesis 2:24 (see also LXX)">d</a></sup>So they are no longer two, but one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 7:1–3; 1 Chronicles 7:10–13"> Therefore what God has joined together, let man not separate.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 9:33; Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> When they were back inside the house, the disciples asked Jesus about this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:31–32; Matthew 19:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11"> So He told them, “Whoever divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:11; 1 Corinthians 7:13"> And if a woman divorces her husband and marries another man, she commits adultery.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–15; Luke 18:15–17; Exodus 10:9–11"> Now people were bringing the little children to Jesus for Him to place His hands on them, and the disciples rebuked those who brought them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 19:14; Matthew 18:10; Matthew 18:4"> But when Jesus saw this, He was indignant and told them, “Let the little children come to Me, and do not hinder them! For the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:17; John 3:3–6"> Truly I tell you, anyone who does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.”</span>

## The Rich Young Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 19:16–30; Luke 18:18–30; Romans 2:7"> As Jesus started on His way, a man ran up and knelt before Him. “Good Teacher,” he asked, “what must I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:19; Matthew 19:17; Romans 3:12"> “Why do you call Me good?” Jesus replied. “No one is good except God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Romans 13:9; James 2:11"> You know the commandments: ‘Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not bear false witness, do not cheat others, honor your father and mother.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Exodus 20:12–16; Deuteronomy 5:16–20">e</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; 2 Timothy 3:5; Philippians 3:6"> “Teacher,” he replied, “all these I have kept from my youth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; Revelation 2:4; Luke 18:22"> Jesus looked at him, loved him, and said to him, “There is one thing you lack: Go, sell everything you own and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow Me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR Then come, take up the cross, and follow Me.">f</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15"> But the man was saddened by these words and went away in sorrow, because he had great wealth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26; Luke 18:24; Mark 3:5"> Then Jesus looked around and said to His disciples, “How hard it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:1–3; Proverbs 11:28; Psalms 52:7"> And the disciples were amazed at His words.<br />But Jesus said to them again, “Children, how hard it is to enter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="BYZ and TR how hard it is for those who trust in riches to enter">g</a></sup>the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 19:24–25; Luke 18:25; Matthew 23:24"> It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 16:31; 2 Corinthians 11:23; Romans 10:9–13"> They were even more astonished and said to one another, “Who then can be saved?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Job 42:2"> Jesus looked at them and said, “With man this is impossible, but not with God. For all things are possible with God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 18:28–30; Luke 14:33"> Peter began to say to Him, “Look, we have left everything and followed You.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:23; Matthew 5:10–11; Mark 8:35"> “Truly I tell you,” Jesus replied, “no one who has left home or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or fields for My sake and for the gospel</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 18:30; Philippians 3:8; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> will fail to receive a hundredfold in the present age—houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and fields, along with persecutions—and in the age to come, eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Luke 13:30; Matthew 20:16"> But many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Luke 18:31–34; Luke 9:51"> As they were going up the road to Jerusalem, Jesus was walking ahead of them. <span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Matthew 20:17–19; Mark 8:31"> “Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be delivered over to the chief priests and scribes. They will condemn Him to death and will deliver Him over to the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Matthew 16:21; Mark 14:65"> who will mock Him and spit on Him and flog Him and kill Him. And after three days He will rise again.”</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:20–28; 2 Samuel 14:4–11; 1 Kings 2:20"> Then James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came to Jesus and declared, “Teacher, we want You to do for us whatever we ask.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 15:7; 1 Kings 3:5–15; Mark 10:51"> “What do you want Me to do for you?” He inquired.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:26"> They answered, “Grant that one of us may sit at Your right hand and the other at Your left in Your glory.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:50; Matthew 20:21–22; Psalms 75:8"> “You do not know what you are asking,” Jesus replied. “Can you drink the cup I will drink, or be baptized with the baptism I will undergo?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 12:2; Revelation 1:9; John 17:14"> “We can,” the brothers answered.<br />“You will drink the cup that I drink,” Jesus said, “and you will be baptized with the baptism that I undergo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:23; Matthew 25:34; John 17:24"> But to sit at My right or left is not Mine to grant. These seats belong to those for whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally Mine to grant, but for whom">h</a></sup>they have been prepared.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 20:24; Proverbs 13:10; Romans 12:10"> When the ten heard about this, they became indignant with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; Luke 22:25; Matthew 20:25"> So Jesus called them together and said, “You know that those regarded as rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and their superiors exercise authority over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 23:8–12; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:35"> But it shall not be this way among you. Instead, whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and whoever wants to be first must be the slave of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Philippians 2:5–8; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many.”</span>
</div>

## Jesus Heals Bartimaeus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 18:35–1; Matthew 20:29–34; John 9:8"> Next, they came to Jericho. And as Jesus and His disciples were leaving Jericho with a large crowd, a blind beggar named Bartimaeus, the son of Timaeus, was sitting beside the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Matthew 9:27; Revelation 22:16"> When he heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began to cry out, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; Mark 7:26–29; Hebrews 5:7"> Many people admonished him to be silent, but he cried out all the louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 18:40; Matthew 20:32–34; John 11:28"> Jesus stopped and said, “Call him.”<br />So they called the blind man. “Take courage!” they said. “Get up! He is calling for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Hebrews 12:1"> Throwing off his cloak, Bartimaeus jumped up and came to Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 18:41–43; Mark 10:36; Matthew 7:7"> “What do you want Me to do for you?” Jesus asked.<br />“Rabboni,” said the blind man, “let me see again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Acts 26:18; Mark 8:25"> “Go,” said Jesus, “your faith has healed you.” And immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus along the road.</span>
