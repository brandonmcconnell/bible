## The Feeding of the Four Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:32–39; Mark 6:34–44"> In those days the crowd once again became very large, and they had nothing to eat. Jesus called the disciples to Him and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:13"> “I have compassion for this crowd, because they have already been with Me three days and have nothing to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Judges 8:4–6; 1 Samuel 30:10–12"> If I send them home hungry, they will faint along the way. For some of them have come a great distance.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Kings 7:2; Mark 6:52"> His disciples replied, “Where in this desolate place could anyone find enough bread to feed all these people?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 14:15–17; Matthew 15:34; Luke 9:13"> “How many loaves do you have?” Jesus asked.<br />“Seven,” they replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Matthew 15:35–36; John 6:10–11"> And He instructed the crowd to sit down on the ground. Then He took the seven loaves, gave thanks and broke them, and gave them to His disciples to set before the people. And they distributed them to the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; John 21:5; Luke 24:41–42"> They also had a few small fish, and Jesus blessed them and ordered that these be set before them as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Psalms 145:16; Matthew 16:10"> The people ate and were satisfied, and the disciples picked up seven basketfuls of broken pieces that were left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And about four thousand men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Men is implied here, that is, in addition to women and children; see Matthew 15:38.">a</a></sup>were present.<br />And when Jesus had dismissed the crowd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:39"> He immediately got into the boat with His disciples and went to the district of Dalmanutha.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Demand for a Sign

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Matthew 12:38; Deuteronomy 6:16"> Then the Pharisees came and began to argue with Jesus, testing Him by demanding from Him a sign from heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:39–40; Mark 7:34; Matthew 16:4"> Jesus sighed deeply in His spirit and said, “Why does this generation demand a sign? Truly I tell you, no sign will be given to this generation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 12:36; Acts 13:45–46; Psalms 81:12"> And He left them, got back into the boat, and crossed to the other side.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Leaven of the Pharisees and of Herod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:5"> Now the disciples had forgotten to take bread, except for one loaf they had with them in the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:6–8; Matthew 16:6; Matthew 16:11–12"> “Watch out!” He cautioned them. “Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of Herod.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Luke 20:5; Matthew 16:7–8"> So they began to discuss with one another the fact that they had no bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Isaiah 63:17; Mark 2:8"> Aware of their conversation, Jesus asked them, “Why are you debating about having no bread? Do you still not see or understand? Do you have such hard hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 28:26–27; Matthew 13:14–15; Jeremiah 5:21"> ‘Having eyes, do you not see? And having ears, do you not hear?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 29:4, Isaiah 42:20, Jeremiah 5:21, and Ezekiel 12:2.">b</a></sup>And do you not remember?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:5–13"> When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many basketfuls of broken pieces did you collect?”<br />“Twelve,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:1–9"> “And when I broke the seven loaves for the four thousand, how many basketfuls of broken pieces did you collect?”<br />“Seven,” they said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Matthew 16:11–12; Mark 8:12"> Then He asked them, “Do you still not understand?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Blind Man at Bethsaida

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Mark 6:45; Luke 10:13"> When they arrived at Bethsaida, some people brought a blind man and begged Jesus to touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:33; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:23"> So He took the blind man by the hand and led him out of the village. Then He spit on the man’s eyes and placed His hands on him. “Can you see anything?” He asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 32:3; Isaiah 29:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–12"> The man looked up and said, “I can see the people, but they look like trees walking around.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Peter 3:18; Proverbs 4:18"> Once again Jesus placed His hands on the man’s eyes, and when he opened them his sight was restored, and he could see everything clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:23; Mark 5:43"> Jesus sent him home and said, “Do not go back into the village.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR “Do not go and tell anyone in the village.”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Confession of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:13–20; Luke 9:18–20"> Then Jesus and His disciples went on to the villages around Caesarea Philippi. On the way, He questioned His disciples: “Who do people say I am?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:21; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> They replied, “Some say John the Baptist; others say Elijah; and still others, one of the prophets.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Matthew 16:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> “But what about you?” Jesus asked. “Who do you say I am?”<br />Peter answered, “You are the Christ.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:21; Matthew 16:20; Mark 9:9"> And Jesus warned them not to tell anyone about Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ’s Passion Foretold

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 10:33–34; John 2:19; Luke 24:26"> Then He began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes, and that He must be killed and after three days rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 16:22; John 16:25; John 16:29"> He spoke this message quite frankly, and Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 4:10; Philippians 3:19; 1 John 2:15"> But Jesus, turning and looking at His disciples, rebuked Peter and said, “Get behind Me, Satan! For you do not have in mind the things of God, but the things of men.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Take Up Your Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:26–27; John 10:27"> Then Jesus called the crowd to Him along with His disciples, and He told them, “If anyone wants to come after Me, he must deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:11–13; 1 Corinthians 9:23; Luke 17:33"> For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake and for the gospel will save it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Luke 9:25; Psalms 49:17"> What does it profit a man to gain the whole world, yet forfeit his life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Or what can a man give in exchange for his life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:8–9; Romans 1:16; 1 John 2:23"> If anyone is ashamed of Me and My words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man will also be ashamed of him when He comes in His Father’s glory with the holy angels.”</span>
</div>
