## The Transfiguration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Hebrews 2:9; Luke 22:30"> Then Jesus said to them, “Truly I tell you, there are some standing here who will not taste death before they see the kingdom of God arrive with power.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; Matthew 17:1–8"> After six days Jesus took with Him Peter, James, and John, and led them up a high mountain by themselves. There He was transfigured before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 28:3; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 7:14"> His clothes became radiantly white, brighter than any launderer on earth could bleach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 9:30–31; Matthew 17:3–4; Acts 3:21–24"> And Elijah and Moses appeared before them, talking with Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; John 14:8–9; Revelation 22:3–4"> Peter said to Jesus, “Rabbi, it is good for us to be here. Let us put up three shelters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or three tabernacles">a</a></sup>—one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:15–19; Mark 16:5–8"> For they were all so terrified that Peter did not know what else to say.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Matthew 3:17; Mark 1:11"> Then a cloud appeared and enveloped them, and a voice came from the cloud: “This is My beloved Son. Listen to Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 10:16; Acts 8:39–40; Luke 24:31"> Suddenly, when they looked around, they saw no one with them except Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Matthew 17:9–13; Mark 5:43"> As they were coming down the mountain, Jesus admonished them not to tell anyone what they had seen until the Son of Man had risen from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Luke 24:7–8; Luke 18:33–34"> So they kept this matter to themselves, discussing what it meant to rise from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:14"> And they asked Jesus, “Why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> He replied, “Elijah does indeed come first, and he restores all things. Why then is it written that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 14:3–11; Matthew 17:12–13; Matthew 11:14"> But I tell you that Elijah has indeed come, and they have done to him whatever they wished, just as it is written about him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Boy with an Evil Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> When they returned to the other disciples, they saw a large crowd around them, and scribes arguing with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> As soon as all the people saw Jesus, they were filled with awe and ran to greet Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> “What are you disputing with them?” He asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> Someone in the crowd replied, “Teacher, I brought You my son, who has a spirit that makes him mute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> Whenever it seizes him, it throws him to the ground. He foams at the mouth, gnashes his teeth, and becomes rigid.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or and is withering away">b</a></sup>I asked Your disciples to drive it out, but they were unable.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> “O unbelieving generation!” Jesus replied. “How long must I remain with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring the boy to Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> So they brought him, and seeing Jesus, the spirit immediately threw the boy into a convulsion. He fell to the ground and rolled around, foaming at the mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> Jesus asked the boy’s father, “How long has this been with him?”<br />“From childhood,” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> “It often throws him into the fire or into the water, trying to kill him. But if You can do anything, have compassion on us and help us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> “If You can?” echoed Jesus. “All things are possible to him who believes!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> Immediately the boy’s father cried out,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR cried out with tears">c</a></sup>“I do believe; help my unbelief!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> When Jesus saw that a crowd had come running, He rebuked the unclean spirit. “You deaf and mute spirit,” He said, “I command you to come out and never enter him again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> After shrieking and convulsing him violently, the spirit came out. The boy became like a corpse, so that many said, “He is dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> But Jesus took him by the hand and helped him to his feet, and he stood up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> After Jesus had gone into the house, His disciples asked Him privately, “Why couldn’t we drive it out?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> Jesus answered, “This kind cannot come out, except by prayer.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="BYZ and TR prayer and fasting">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Second Prediction of the Passion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:43–45; Mark 6:31–32"> Going on from there, they passed through Galilee. But Jesus did not want anyone to know,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 9:12; Luke 24:44–46"> because He was teaching His disciples. He told them, “The Son of Man will be delivered into the hands of men. They will kill Him, and after three days He will rise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 2:50; Luke 9:45; Luke 18:34"> But they did not understand this statement, and they were afraid to ask Him about it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Greatest in the Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Hebrews 4:13; Luke 9:46–48"> Then they came to Capernaum. While Jesus was in the house, He asked them, “What were you discussing on the way?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Matthew 20:21–24; Matthew 18:1–5"> But they were silent, for on the way they had been arguing about which of them was the greatest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:42–45; Luke 22:26"> Sitting down, Jesus called the Twelve and said, “If anyone wants to be first, he must be the last of all and the servant of all.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 10:16; Matthew 19:14–15; Matthew 18:2"> Then He had a little child stand among them. Taking the child in His arms, He said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–5; Luke 10:16; Matthew 10:40–42"> “Whoever welcomes one of these little children in My name welcomes Me, and whoever welcomes Me welcomes not only Me, but the One who sent Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 9:49–50; Numbers 11:26–29; Luke 11:19"> John said to Him, “Teacher, we saw someone else driving out demons in Your name, and we tried to stop him, because he does not accompany us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Matthew 13:28–29"> “Do not stop him,” Jesus replied. “For no one who performs a miracle in My name can turn around and speak evil of Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23"> For whoever is not against us is for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Matthew 25:40; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> Indeed, if anyone gives you even a cup of water because you bear the name of Christ, truly I tell you, he will never lose his reward.</span>
</div>
</div>
