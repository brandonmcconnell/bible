## The Rejection at Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:16–30; Matthew 13:54–58; Matthew 2:23"> Jesus went on from there and came to His hometown, accompanied by His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:15; John 6:42; Acts 4:13–14"> When the Sabbath came, He began to teach in the synagogue, and many who heard Him were astonished. “Where did this man get these ideas?” they asked. “What is this wisdom He has been given? And how can He perform such miracles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–57; John 6:42"> Isn’t this the carpenter, the son of Mary and the brother of James, Joses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Joses is a variant of Joseph; see Matthew 13:55.">a</a></sup>Judas, and Simon? Aren’t His sisters here with us as well?” And they took offense at Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:44; Luke 4:24; Matthew 13:57"> Then Jesus said to them, “Only in his hometown, among his relatives, and in his own household is a prophet without honor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:58; Mark 9:23; Mark 5:23"> So He could not perform any miracles there, except to lay His hands on a few of the sick and heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Matthew 8:10; Luke 13:22"> And He was amazed at their unbelief.<br />And He went around from village to village, teaching the people.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:3; Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4"> He instructed them to take nothing but a staff for the journey—no bread, no bag, no money<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or copper coins">b</a></sup>in their belts—</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Luke 9:4; Matthew 10:11–13"> And He told them, “When you enter a house, stay there until you leave that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:14–15; Acts 18:6; Luke 10:10–15"> If anyone will not welcome you or listen to you, shake the dust off your feet when you leave that place, as a testimony against them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include Truly I tell you, it will be more tolerable for Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that town; see Matthew 10:15.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The Beheading of John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Mark 6:14–29; Luke 23:7–12"> Now King Herod heard about this, for Jesus’ name had become well known, and people were saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally they were saying; some manuscripts he was saying">d</a></sup>“John the Baptist has risen from the dead! That is why miraculous powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; Malachi 4:5; Matthew 21:11"> Others were saying, “He is Elijah,” and still others, “He is a prophet, like one of the prophets of old.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; Matthew 27:4; Luke 9:9"> But when Herod heard this, he said, “John, whom I beheaded, has risen from the dead!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 11:2; Luke 3:1"> For Herod himself had ordered that John be arrested and bound and imprisoned, on account of his brother Philip’s wife Herodias, whom Herod had married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; 1 Kings 22:14"> For John had been telling Herod, “It is not lawful for you to have your brother’s wife!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Ephesians 4:26–27; Genesis 39:17–20"> So Herodias held a grudge against John and wanted to kill him. But she had been unable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5; 2 Chronicles 24:15–22"> because Herod feared John and protected him, knowing that he was a righteous and holy man. When he heard John’s words, he was greatly perplexed; yet he listened to him gladly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 2:18; Genesis 40:20; 1 Peter 4:3"> On Herod’s birthday, her opportunity arose. Herod held a banquet for his nobles and military commanders and the leading men of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 1:10–12; Matthew 14:6; Daniel 5:2"> When the daughter of Herodias<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some early manuscripts When his daughter Herodias">e</a></sup>came and danced, she pleased Herod and his guests, and the king said to the girl, “Ask me for whatever you wish, and I will give it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6"> And he swore to her, “Whatever you ask of me, I will give you, up to half my kingdom!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 31:31; Ezekiel 19:2–3; Genesis 27:8–11"> Then she went out and asked her mother, “What should I request?”<br />And her mother answered, “The head of John the Baptist.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Numbers 7:19–89; Numbers 7:13"> At once the girl hurried back to the king with her request: “I want you to give me the head of John the Baptist on a platter immediately.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:24–25; Matthew 14:9; Matthew 27:3–5"> The king was consumed with sorrow, but because of his oaths and his guests, he did not want to refuse her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 14:10–11"> So without delay, the king commanded that John’s head be brought in. He sent an executioner, who went and beheaded him in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The man brought John’s head on a platter and presented it to the girl, who gave it to her mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 14:12; 2 Chronicles 24:16; Matthew 27:57–60"> When John’s disciples heard about this, they came and took his body and placed it in a tomb.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feeding of the Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:10; Luke 10:17; Matthew 10:2"> Meanwhile, the apostles gathered around Jesus and brought Him news of all they had done and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 3:20; John 6:1; Mark 1:45"> And He said to them, “Come with Me privately to a solitary place, and let us rest for a while.” For many people were coming and going, and they did not even have time to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 8:2–9; Mark 6:45; Mark 4:36"> So they went away in a boat by themselves to a solitary place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 6:2; Matthew 15:29–31; Mark 6:54–55"> But many people saw them leaving and recognized them. They ran together on foot from all the towns and arrived before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Luke 9:11; Numbers 27:17"> When Jesus stepped ashore and saw a large crowd, He had compassion on them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd. And He began to teach them many things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:5–15; Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:15–21"> By now the hour was already late. So the disciples came to Jesus and said, “This is a desolate place, and the hour is already late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 3:21; Mark 5:31"> Dismiss the crowd so they can go to the surrounding countryside and villages and buy themselves something to eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 15:32–33; Mark 8:2–3"> But Jesus told them, “You give them something to eat.”<br />They asked Him, “Should we go out and spend two hundred denarii<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.">f</a></sup>to give all of them bread to eat?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:34; Mark 8:5; John 6:9"> “Go and see how many loaves you have,” He told them.<br />And after checking, they said, “Five—and two fish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 14:33; Matthew 15:35"> Then Jesus directed them to have the people sit in groups on the green grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:14–15"> So they sat down in groups of hundreds and fifties.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Mark 7:34; Luke 9:16"> Taking the five loaves and the two fish and looking up to heaven, Jesus spoke a blessing and broke the loaves. Then He gave them to His disciples to set before the people. And He divided the two fish among them all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 9:17; Mark 8:8–9; Matthew 15:37–38"> They all ate and were satisfied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 8:19–20"> and the disciples picked up twelve basketfuls of broken pieces of bread and fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And there were five thousand men who had eaten the loaves.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Walks on Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 14:22–33; John 6:15–21; Mark 6:32"> Immediately Jesus made His disciples get into the boat and go on ahead of Him to Bethsaida, while He dismissed the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 1:35; Matthew 6:6; Luke 6:12"> After bidding them farewell, He went up on the mountain to pray.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 14:23; John 6:16–17"> When evening came, the boat was in the middle of the sea, and Jesus was alone on land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 24:28; Luke 12:38; Job 9:8"> He could see that the disciples were straining to row, because the wind was against them. About the fourth watch of the night,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, between three and six in the morning">g</a></sup>Jesus went out to them, walking on the sea. He intended to pass by them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 14:25–26; Luke 24:37; Job 9:8"> but when they saw Him walking on the sea, they cried out, thinking He was a ghost—</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 14:27; John 6:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> for they all saw Him and were terrified.<br />But Jesus spoke up at once: “Take courage! It is I. Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Mark 4:39; John 6:21"> Then He climbed into the boat with them, and the wind died down. And the disciples were utterly astounded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 8:17–21; Luke 24:25; Isaiah 63:17"> for they had not understood about the loaves, but their hearts had been hardened.</span>
</div>

## Jesus Heals at Gennesaret

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 14:34–36; John 6:24–25; Luke 5:1"> When they had crossed over, they landed at Gennesaret and moored the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 9:10; Philippians 3:10"> As soon as they got out of the boat, the people recognized Jesus</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 2:1–3; Mark 3:7–11"> and ran through that whole region, carrying the sick on mats to wherever they heard He was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 9:20; Luke 6:19; Numbers 15:38–39"> And wherever He went—villages and towns and countrysides—they laid the sick in the marketplaces and begged Him just to let them touch the fringe of His cloak. And all who touched Him were healed.</span>
</div>
