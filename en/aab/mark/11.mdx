## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> As they approached Jerusalem and came to Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent out two of His disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> and said to them, “Go into the village ahead of you, and as soon as you enter it, you will find a colt tied there, on which no one has ever sat. Untie it and bring it here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; Psalms 110:3"> If anyone asks, ‘Why are you doing this?’ tell him, ‘The Lord needs it and will return it shortly.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> So they went and found the colt outside in the street, tied at a doorway. They untied it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and some who were standing there asked, “Why are you untying the colt?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The disciples answered as Jesus had instructed them, and the people gave them permission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> Then they led the colt to Jesus and threw their cloaks over it, and He sat on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> Many in the crowd spread their cloaks on the road, while others spread branches they had cut from the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> The ones who went ahead and those who followed were shouting:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hosanna is a transliteration of the Hebrew Hosia-na, meaning Save, we pray or Save now, which became a shout of praise; see Psalms 118:25.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 118:26">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna in the highest!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Hosanna in the highest heaven!” See Psalms 118:25 and Psalms 148:1.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> Then Jesus entered Jerusalem and went into the temple courts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally the temple; also in verses 15, 16, and 27">d</a></sup>He looked around at everything, but since it was already late, He went out to Bethany with the Twelve.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Curses the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> The next day, when they had left Bethany, Jesus was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> Seeing in the distance a fig tree in leaf, He went to see if there was any fruit on it. But when He reached it, He found nothing on it except leaves, since it was not the season for figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> Then He said to the tree, “May no one ever eat of your fruit again.” And His disciples heard this statement.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> When they arrived in Jerusalem, Jesus entered the temple courts and began to drive out those who were buying and selling there. He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And He would not allow anyone to carry merchandise through the temple courts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> Then Jesus began to teach them, and He declared, “Is it not written: ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isaiah 56:7">e</a></sup>? But you have made it ‘a den of robbers.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jeremiah 7:11">f</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> When the chief priests and scribes heard this, they looked for a way to kill Him. For they were afraid of Him, because the whole crowd was astonished at His teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> And when evening came, Jesus and His disciples went<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally they went; BYZ and TR He went">g</a></sup>out of the city.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Withered Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> As they were walking back in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered from its roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> Peter remembered it and said, “Look, Rabbi! The fig tree You cursed has withered.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> “Have faith in God,” Jesus said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> “Truly I tell you that if anyone says to this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,’ and has no doubt in his heart but believes that it will happen, it will be done for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> Therefore I tell you, whatever you ask for in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> And when you stand to pray, if you hold anything against another, forgive it, so that your Father in heaven will forgive your trespasses as well.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="BYZ and TR include 26 But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father in heaven forgive your trespasses; see Matthew 6:15.">h</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Authority Challenged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> After their return to Jerusalem, Jesus was walking in the temple courts, and the chief priests, scribes, and elders came up to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> “By what authority are You doing these things?” they asked. “And who gave You the authority to do them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> “I will ask you one question,” Jesus replied, “and if you answer Me, I will tell you by what authority I am doing these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> John’s baptism—was it from heaven or from men? Answer Me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> They deliberated among themselves what they should answer: “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ He will ask, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> But if we say, ‘From men’...” they were afraid of the people, for they all held that John truly was a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> So they answered, “We do not know.”<br />And Jesus replied, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I am doing these things.”</span>
</div>
</div>
