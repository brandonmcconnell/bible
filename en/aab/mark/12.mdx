## The Parable of the Wicked Tenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> Then Jesus began to speak to them in parables: “A man planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it, dug a wine vat, and built a watchtower. Then he rented it out to some tenants and went away on a journey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> At harvest time, he sent a servant to the tenants to collect his share of the fruit of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they seized the servant, beat him, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then he sent them another servant, and they struck him over the head and treated him shamefully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> He sent still another, and this one they killed.<br />He sent many others; some they beat and others they killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Finally, having one beloved son, he sent him to them. ‘They will respect my son,’ he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But the tenants said to one another, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> So they seized the son, killed him, and threw him out of the vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What then will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and kill those tenants and give the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have you never read this Scripture:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has become the cornerstone.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is from the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it is marvelous in our eyes’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 118:22–23">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> At this, the leaders sought<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally they sought">b</a></sup>to arrest Jesus, for they knew that He had spoken this parable against them. But fearing the crowd, they left Him and went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paying Taxes to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Later, they sent some of the Pharisees and Herodians to catch Jesus in His words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> “Teacher,” they said, “we know that You are honest and seek favor from no one. Indeed, You are impartial and teach the way of God in accordance with the truth. Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not? Should we pay them or not?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> But Jesus saw through their hypocrisy and said, “Why are you testing Me? Bring Me a denarius<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.">c</a></sup>to inspect.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they brought it, and He asked them, “Whose image is this? And whose inscription?”<br />“Caesar’s,” they answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Then Jesus told them, “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.”<br />And they marveled at Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sadducees and the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, came to Jesus and questioned Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote for us that if a man’s brother dies and leaves a wife but no children, the man is to marry his brother’s widow and raise up offspring for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Deuteronomy 25:5">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> Now there were seven brothers. The first one married and died, leaving no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then the second one married the widow, but he also died and left no children. And the third did likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In this way, none of the seven left any children. And last of all, the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, then,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally In the resurrection, when they rise,">e</a></sup>whose wife will she be? For all seven were married to her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus said to them, “Aren’t you mistaken, because you do not know the Scriptures or the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> When the dead rise, they will neither marry nor be given in marriage. Instead, they will be like the angels in heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> But concerning the dead rising, have you not read about the burning bush in the Book of Moses, how God told him, ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Exodus 3:6">f</a></sup>?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of the dead, but of the living. You are badly mistaken!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Greatest Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> Now one of the scribes had come up and heard their debate. Noticing how well Jesus had answered them, he asked Him, “Which commandment is the most important of all?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus replied, “This is the most important: ‘Hear O Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Deuteronomy 6:4–5">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> The second is this: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Leviticus 19:18">h</a></sup>No other commandment is greater than these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> “Right, Teacher,” the scribe replied. “You have stated correctly that God is One and there is no other but Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> and to love Him with all your heart and with all your understanding and with all your strength, and to love your neighbor as yourself. This is more important than all ascension offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gk. holokautoma, 'wholly burnt,' the Greek OT's rendering of Heb. olah, 'ascension offering.' See note on Genesis 8:20.">i</a></sup>and sacrifices.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> When Jesus saw that the man had answered wisely, He said, “You are not far from the kingdom of God.”<br />And no one dared to question Him any further.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Whose Son Is the Christ?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> While Jesus was teaching in the temple courts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally the temple">j</a></sup>He asked, “How can the scribes say that the Christ is the Son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> Speaking by the Holy Spirit, David himself declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Sit at My right hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until I put Your enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">under Your feet.”’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Psalms 110:1">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> David himself calls Him ‘Lord.’ So how can He be David’s son?”<br />And the large crowd listened to Him with delight.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware of the Scribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> In His teaching Jesus also said, “Watch out for the scribes. They like to walk around in long robes, to receive greetings in the marketplaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and to have the chief seats in the synagogues and the places of honor at banquets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> They defraud widows of their houses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally They devour widows’ houses">l</a></sup>and for a show make lengthy prayers. These men will receive greater condemnation.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Widow’s Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> As Jesus was sitting opposite the treasury, He watched the crowd putting money into it. And many rich people put in large amounts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then one poor widow came and put in two small copper coins, which amounted to a small fraction of a denarius.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Greek put in two lepta, which is a kodrantēs; a lepton was a Jewish coin of bronze or copper worth about 1/128 of a denarius.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> Jesus called His disciples to Him and said, “Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put more than all the others into the treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For they all contributed out of their surplus, but she out of her poverty has put in all she had to live on.”</span>
</div>
</div>
