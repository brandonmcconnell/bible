## The Mission of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–6; John 1:6–8; Luke 1:76"> In those days John the Baptist came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Mark 1:15; Daniel 2:44"> and saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1; John 1:23"> This is he who was spoken of through the prophet Isaiah:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“A voice of one calling in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Prepare the way for the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">make straight paths for Him.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Isaiah 40:3 (see also LXX)">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> John wore a garment of camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Luke 3:7; John 3:23"> People went out to him from Jerusalem and all Judea and the whole region around the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:13–16; Acts 2:38–41"> Confessing their sins, they were baptized by him in the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 5:9"> But when John saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his place of baptism, he said to them, “You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Acts 26:20; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Produce fruit, then, in keeping with repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:27–29"> And do not presume to say to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as our father.’ For I tell you that out of these stones God can raise up children for Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:6; Luke 3:9; Matthew 7:19"> The axe lies ready at the root of the trees, and every tree that does not produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> I baptize you with water<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or in water">b</a></sup>for repentance, but after me will come One more powerful than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or in the Holy Spirit and in fire">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 13:30; Luke 3:17"> His winnowing fork is in His hand to clear His threshing floor and to gather His wheat into the barn; but He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.”</span>
</div>

## The Baptism of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> At that time Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to be baptized by John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 3:3–7; Acts 1:5–8; John 13:6–8"> But John tried to prevent Him, saying, “I need to be baptized by You, and do You come to me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:34; John 15:10; Psalms 40:7–8"> “Let it be so now,” Jesus replied. “It is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness in this way.” Then John permitted Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Mark 1:10; Luke 3:21–22"> As soon as Jesus was baptized, He went up out of the water. Suddenly the heavens were opened,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="NA, BYZ, and TR the heavens were opened to Him">d</a></sup>and He saw<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or and he saw; see John 1:32–33.">e</a></sup>the Spirit of God descending like a dove and resting on Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 2:7; Matthew 17:5"> And a voice from heaven said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased!”</span>
</div>
</div>
