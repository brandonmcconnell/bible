## The Genealogy of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> This is the record of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Abraham was the father of Isaac,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Isaac the father of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Jacob the father of Judah and his brothers.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Judah was the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Perez the father of Hezron,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Hezron the father of Ram.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek Aram, a variant of Ram; also in verse 4; see 1 Chronicles 2:9–10.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ram was the father of Amminadab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Amminadab the father of Nahshon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Nahshon the father of Salmon.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Salmon was the father of Boaz by Rahab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Boaz the father of Obed by Ruth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Obed the father of Jesse,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">and Jesse the father of David the king.</span><br />Next:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">David was the father of Solomon by Uriah’s wife,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Solomon the father of Rehoboam,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rehoboam the father of Abijah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Abijah the father of Asa.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek Asaph, a variant of Asa; also in verse 8; see 1 Chronicles 3:10.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Asa was the father of Jehoshaphat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jehoshaphat the father of Joram,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Joram the father of Uzziah.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Uzziah was the father of Jotham,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jotham the father of Ahaz,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Ahaz the father of Hezekiah.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hezekiah was the father of Manasseh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Manasseh the father of Amon,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek Amōs, a variant spelling of Amon; twice in this verse; see 1 Chronicles 3:14.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Amon the father of Josiah,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">and Josiah the father of Jeconiah and his brothers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the time of the exile to Babylon.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> After the exile to Babylon:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jeconiah was the father of Shealtiel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shealtiel the father of Zerubbabel,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Zerubbabel the father of Abiud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Abiud the father of Eliakim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Eliakim the father of Azor.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Azor was the father of Zadok,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Zadok the father of Achim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Achim the father of Eliud.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Eliud was the father of Eleazar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Eleazar the father of Matthan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Matthan the father of Jacob,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">and Jacob the father of Joseph, the husband of Mary,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> In all, then, there were fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen from David to the exile to Babylon, and fourteen from the exile to the Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Birth of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> This is how the birth of Jesus Christ came about: His mother Mary was pledged in marriage to Joseph, but before they came together, she was found to be with child through the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> Because Joseph her husband was a righteous man and was unwilling to disgrace her publicly, he resolved to divorce her quietly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But after he had pondered these things, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to embrace Mary as your wife, for the One conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> She will give birth to a Son, and you are to give Him the name Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus means The LORD saves.">d</a></sup>because He will save His people from their sins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> All this took place to fulfill what the Lord had said through the prophet:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the virgin will be with child</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will give birth to a son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will call Him Immanuel”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally they will call His name Immanuel; Isaiah 7:14 (see also DSS)">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">(which means, “God with us”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Isaiah 7:14, Isaiah 8:8, and Isaiah 8:10.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">).</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> When Joseph woke up, he did as the angel of the Lord had commanded him and embraced Mary as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> But he had no union with her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally he did not know her">g</a></sup>until she gave birth to a Son. And he gave Him the name Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
