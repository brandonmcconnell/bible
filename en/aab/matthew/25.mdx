## The Parable of the Ten Virgins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> “At that time the kingdom of heaven will be like ten virgins who took their lamps and went out to meet the bridegroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> Five of them were foolish, and five were wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> The foolish ones took their lamps but did not take along any extra oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> But the wise ones took oil in flasks along with their lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> When the bridegroom was delayed, they all became drowsy and fell asleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> At midnight the cry rang out: ‘Here is the bridegroom! Come out to meet him!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> Then all the virgins woke up and trimmed their lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> The foolish ones said to the wise, ‘Give us some of your oil; our lamps are going out.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> ‘No,’ said the wise ones, ‘or there may not be enough for both us and you. Instead, go to those who sell oil and buy some for yourselves.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> But while they were on their way to buy it, the bridegroom arrived. Those who were ready went in with him to the wedding banquet, and the door was shut.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> Later the other virgins arrived and said, ‘Lord, lord, open the door for us!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> But he replied, ‘Truly I tell you, I do not know you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Therefore keep watch, because you do not know the day or the hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR include when the Son of Man comes.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Talents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> For it is just like a man going on a journey, who called his servants and entrusted them with his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> To one he gave five talents,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent was worth about twenty years’ wages for a laborer.">b</a></sup>to another two talents, and to another one talent—each according to his own ability. And he went on his journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> The servant who had received the five talents went at once and put them to work<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or 15... And he went on his journey at once. 16... went and put them to work. Translators vary as to the placement of the Greek adverb eutheōs (at once) at the end of verse 15 or at the beginning of verse 16.">c</a></sup>and gained five more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> Likewise, the one with the two talents gained two more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But the servant who had received the one talent went off, dug a hole in the ground, and hid his master’s money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> After a long time the master of those servants returned and settled accounts with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> The servant who had received the five talents came and presented five more. ‘Master,’ he said, ‘you entrusted me with five talents. See, I have gained five more.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His master replied, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Enter into the joy of your master!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> The servant who had received the two talents also came and said, ‘Master, you entrusted me with two talents. See, I have gained two more.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His master replied, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Enter into the joy of your master!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> Finally, the servant who had received the one talent came and said, ‘Master, I knew that you are a hard man, reaping where you have not sown and gathering where you have not scattered seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> So I was afraid and went out and hid your talent in the ground. See, you have what belongs to you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> ‘You wicked, lazy servant!’ replied his master. ‘You knew that I reap where I have not sown and gather where I have not scattered seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> Then you should have deposited my money with the bankers, and on my return I would have received it back with interest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Therefore take the talent from him and give it to the one who has ten talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> For everyone who has will be given more, and he will have an abundance. But the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> And throw that worthless servant into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sheep and the Goats

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> When the Son of Man comes in His glory, and all the angels with Him, He will sit on His glorious throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> All the nations will be gathered before Him, and He will separate the people one from another, as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> He will place the sheep on His right and the goats on His left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then the King will say to those on His right, ‘Come, you who are blessed by My Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For I was hungry and you gave Me something to eat, I was thirsty and you gave Me something to drink, I was a stranger and you took Me in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> I was naked and you clothed Me, I was sick and you looked after Me, I was in prison and you visited Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then the righteous will answer Him, ‘Lord, when did we see You hungry and feed You, or thirsty and give You something to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When did we see You a stranger and take You in, or naked and clothe You?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> When did we see You sick or in prison and visit You?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the King will reply, ‘Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did for Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Then He will say to those on His left, ‘Depart from Me, you who are cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> For I was hungry and you gave Me nothing to eat, I was thirsty and you gave Me nothing to drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger and you did not take Me in, I was naked and you did not clothe Me, I was sick and in prison and you did not look after Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> And they too will reply, ‘Lord, when did we see You hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and did not minister to You?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then the King will answer, ‘Truly I tell you, whatever you did not do for one of the least of these, you did not do for Me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> And they will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.”</span>
</div>
</div>
