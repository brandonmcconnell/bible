## The Tradition of the Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then some Pharisees and scribes came to Jesus from Jerusalem and asked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> “Why do Your disciples break the tradition of the elders? They do not wash their hands before they eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> Jesus replied, “And why do you break the command of God for the sake of your tradition?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> For God said, ‘Honor your father and mother’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16">a</a></sup>and ‘Anyone who curses his father or mother must be put to death.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Exodus 21:17; Leviticus 20:9">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But you say that if anyone says to his father or mother, ‘Whatever you would have received from me is a gift devoted to God,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> he need not honor his father or mother with it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NE and TR (see also Mark 7:12); literally he need not honor his father or mother; SBL, NA, WH, and BYZ do not include or mother.">c</a></sup>Thus you nullify the word of God for the sake of your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> You hypocrites! Isaiah prophesied correctly about you:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘These people honor Me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but their hearts are far from Me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They worship Me in vain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they teach as doctrine the precepts of men.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isaiah 29:13 (see also LXX)">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## What Defiles a Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> Jesus called the crowd to Him and said, “Listen and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> A man is not defiled by what enters his mouth, but by what comes out of it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then the disciples came to Him and said, “Are You aware that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> But Jesus replied, “Every plant that My heavenly Father has not planted will be pulled up by its roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Disregard them! They are blind guides.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or blind guides of the blind">e</a></sup>If a blind man leads a blind man, both will fall into a pit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> Peter said to Him, “Explain this parable to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> “Do you still not understand?” Jesus asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> “Do you not yet realize that whatever enters the mouth goes into the stomach and then is eliminated?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But the things that come out of the mouth come from the heart, and these things defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false testimony, and slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are what defile a man, but eating with unwashed hands does not defile him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of the Canaanite Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> Leaving that place, Jesus withdrew to the district of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And a Canaanite woman from that region came to Him, crying out, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on me! My daughter is miserably possessed by a demon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But Jesus did not answer a word. So His disciples came and urged Him, “Send her away, for she keeps crying out after us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> He answered, “I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> The woman came and knelt before Him. “Lord, help me!” she said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> But Jesus replied, “It is not right to take the children’s bread and toss it to the dogs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> “Yes, Lord,” she said, “even the dogs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or puppies">f</a></sup>eat the crumbs that fall from their master’s table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> “O woman,” Jesus answered, “your faith is great! Let it be done for you as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that very hour.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feeding of the Four Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> Moving on from there, Jesus went along the Sea of Galilee. Then He went up on a mountain and sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> Large crowds came to Him, bringing the lame, the blind, the crippled, the mute, and many others, and laid them at His feet, and He healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> The crowd was amazed when they saw the mute speaking, the crippled restored, the lame walking, and the blind seeing. And they glorified the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Then Jesus called His disciples to Him and said, “I have compassion for this crowd, because they have already been with Me three days and have nothing to eat. I do not want to send them away hungry, or they may faint along the way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> The disciples replied, “Where in this desolate place could we find enough bread to feed such a large crowd?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> “How many loaves do you have?” Jesus asked.<br />“Seven,” they replied, “and a few small fish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> And He instructed the crowd to sit down on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> Taking the seven loaves and the fish, He gave thanks and broke them. Then He gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> They all ate and were satisfied, and the disciples picked up seven basketfuls of broken pieces that were left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> A total of four thousand men were fed, besides women and children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> After Jesus had dismissed the crowds, He got into the boat and went to the region of Magadan.</span>
</div>
</div>
