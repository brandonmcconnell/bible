## Judging Others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> “Do not judge, or you will be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For with the same judgment you pronounce, you will be judged, and with the measure you use, it will be measured to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> Why do you look at the speck in your brother’s eye but fail to notice the beam in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> How can you say to your brother, ‘Let me take the speck out of your eye,’ while there is still a beam in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> You hypocrite! First take the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to remove the speck from your brother’s eye.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Do not give dogs what is holy; do not throw your pearls before swine. If you do, they may trample them under their feet, and then turn and tear you to pieces.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ask, Seek, Knock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> For everyone who asks receives; he who seeks finds; and to him who knocks, the door will be opened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Which of you, if his son asks for bread, will give him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or if he asks for a fish, will give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> So if you who are evil know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give good things to those who ask Him!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> In everything, then, do to others as you would have them do to you. For this is the essence of the Law and the Prophets.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Narrow Gate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the way that leads to destruction, and many enter through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> But small is the gate and narrow the way that leads to life, and only a few find it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Tree and Its Fruit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Beware of false prophets. They come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are ravenous wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> By their fruit you will recognize them. Are grapes gathered from thornbushes, or figs from thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> Likewise, every good tree bears good fruit, but a bad tree bears bad fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, and a bad tree cannot bear good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> So then, by their fruit you will recognize them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not everyone who says to Me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter the kingdom of heaven, but only he who does the will of My Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many will say to Me on that day, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in Your name, and in Your name drive out demons and perform many miracles?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> Then I will tell them plainly, ‘I never knew you; depart from Me, you workers of lawlessness!’</span>
</div>
</div>

## The House on the Rock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Therefore everyone who hears these words of Mine and acts on them is like a wise man who built his house on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> The rain fell, the torrents raged, and the winds blew and beat against that house; yet it did not fall, because its foundation was on the rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> But everyone who hears these words of Mine and does not act on them is like a foolish man who built his house on sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> The rain fell, the torrents raged, and the winds blew and beat against that house, and it fell—and great was its collapse!”</span>
</div>
</div>
