## The Transfiguration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 26:37; Mark 5:37"> After six days Jesus took with Him Peter, James, and John the brother of James, and led them up a high mountain by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 104:2; Matthew 28:3; Luke 9:29"> There He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun, and His clothes became as white as the light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> Suddenly Moses and Elijah appeared before them, talking with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> Peter said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good for us to be here. If You wish, I will put up three shelters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or three tabernacles">a</a></sup>—one for You, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While Peter was still speaking, a bright cloud enveloped them, and a voice from the cloud said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in 2 Peter 1:17">b</a></sup>Listen to Him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> When the disciples heard this, they fell facedown in terror.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> Then Jesus came over and touched them. “Get up,” He said. “Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> And when they looked up, they saw no one except Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> As they were coming down the mountain, Jesus commanded them, “Do not tell anyone about this vision until the Son of Man has been raised from the dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> The disciples asked Him, “Why then do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> Jesus replied, “Elijah does indeed come, and he will restore all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> But I tell you that Elijah has already come, and they did not recognize him, but have done to him whatever they wished. In the same way, the Son of Man will suffer at their hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples understood that He was speaking to them about John the Baptist.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Boy with a Demon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> When they came to the crowd, a man came up to Jesus and knelt before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> “Lord, have mercy on my son,” he said. “He has seizures and is suffering terribly. He often falls into the fire or into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> I brought him to Your disciples, but they could not heal him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> “O unbelieving and perverse generation!” Jesus replied. “How long must I remain with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring the boy here to Me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> Then Jesus rebuked the demon, and it came out of the boy, and he was healed from that moment.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Power of Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> Afterward the disciples came to Jesus privately and asked, “Why couldn’t we drive it out?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:6; Mark 11:23; Luke 1:37"> “Because you have so little faith,” He answered. “For truly I tell you, if you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include 21 But this kind does not come out except by prayer and fasting; see Mark 9:29.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Second Prediction of the Passion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> When they gathered together in Galilee, Jesus told them, “The Son of Man is about to be delivered into the hands of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> They will kill Him, and on the third day He will be raised to life.” And the disciples were deeply grieved.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Temple Tax

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> After they had arrived in Capernaum, the collectors of the two-drachma tax<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Greek the didrachma; twice in this verse">d</a></sup>came to Peter and asked, “Does your Teacher pay the two drachmas?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> “Yes,” he answered.<br />When Peter entered the house, Jesus preempted him. “What do you think, Simon?” He asked. “From whom do the kings of the earth collect customs and taxes: from their own sons, or from others?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “From others,” Peter answered.<br />“Then the sons are exempt,” Jesus said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> “But so that we may not offend them, go to the sea, cast a hook, and take the first fish you catch. When you open its mouth, you will find a four-drachma coin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Greek a stater; that is, a silver coin worth approximately one shekel">e</a></sup>Take it and give it to them for My tax and yours.”</span>
</div>
