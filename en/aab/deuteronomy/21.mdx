## Purgation for an Unsolved Murder

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> If one is found slain, lying in a field in the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess, and it is not known who killed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> your elders and judges must come out and measure the distance from the victim to the neighboring cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremiah 31:18"> Then the elders of the city nearest the victim shall take a heifer that has never been yoked or used for work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> bring the heifer to a valley with running water that has not been plowed or sown, and break its neck there by the stream.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> And the priests, the sons of Levi, shall come forward, for the LORD your God has chosen them to serve Him and pronounce blessings in His name and to give a ruling in every dispute and case of assault.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Psalms 26:6; Psalms 51:7"> Then all the elders of the city nearest the victim shall wash their hands by the stream over the heifer whose neck has been broken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; 2 Samuel 16:8"> and they shall declare, “Our hands did not shed this blood, nor did our eyes see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Ezekiel 23:24–25"> Accept this purgation, O LORD, for Your people Israel whom You have redeemed, and do not hold the shedding of innocent blood against them.”<br />And the bloodshed will be purged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; 2 Kings 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> So you shall purge from among you the guilt of shedding innocent blood, since you have done what is right in the eyes of the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Marrying a Captive Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> When you go to war against your enemies and the LORD your God delivers them into your hand and you take them captive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> if you see a beautiful woman among them, and you desire her and want to take her as your wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> then you shall bring her into your house. She must shave her head, trim her nails,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> and put aside the clothing of her captivity.<br />After she has lived in your house a full month and mourned her father and mother, you may have relations with her and be her husband, and she shall be your wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> And if you are not pleased with her, you are to let her go wherever she wishes. But you must not sell her for money or treat her as a slave, since you have dishonored her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Inheritance Rights of the Firstborn

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> If a man has two wives, one beloved and the other unloved, and both bear him sons, but the unloved wife has the firstborn son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 2 Chronicles 21:3"> when that man assigns his inheritance to his sons he must not appoint the son of the beloved wife as the firstborn over the son of the unloved wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalms 105:36"> Instead, he must acknowledge the firstborn, the son of his unloved wife, by giving him a double portion of all that he has. For that son is the firstfruits of his father’s strength; the right of the firstborn belongs to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Rebellious Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who does not obey his father and mother and does not listen to them when disciplined,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> his father and mother are to lay hold of him and bring him to the elders of his city, to the gate of his hometown,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> and say to the elders, “This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious; he does not obey us. He is a glutton and a drunkard.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> Then all the men of his city will stone him to death. So you must purge the evil from among you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in 1 Corinthians 5:13">a</a></sup>and all Israel will hear and be afraid.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Cursed Is Anyone Hung on a Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> If a man has committed a sin worthy of death, and he is executed, and you hang his body on a tree,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or impale his body on a pole; similarly in verse 23">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> you must not leave the body on the tree overnight, but you must be sure to bury him that day, because anyone who is hung on a tree is under God’s curse.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew anyone who is hanged is under God’s curse; cited in Galatians 3:13">c</a></sup>You must not defile the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.</span>
</div>
</div>
