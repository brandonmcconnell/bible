## Various Laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 23:4–5; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> If you see your brother’s ox or sheep straying, you must not ignore it;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or you must not hide yourself">a</a></sup>be sure to return it to your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> If your brother does not live near you, or if you do not know who he is, you are to take the animal home to remain with you until your brother comes seeking it; then you can return it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And you shall do the same for his donkey, his cloak, or anything your brother has lost and you have found. You must not ignore it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:1–2; Hebrews 12:12–13; Matthew 5:44"> If you see your brother’s donkey or ox fallen on the road, you must not ignore it; you must help him lift it up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:4–15; Deuteronomy 18:12"> A woman must not wear men’s clothing, and a man must not wear women’s clothing, for whoever does these things is detestable to the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 22:28; Proverbs 12:10; Genesis 8:17"> If you come across a bird’s nest with chicks or eggs, either in a tree or on the ground along the road, and the mother is sitting on the chicks or eggs, you must not take the mother along with the young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> You may take the young, but be sure to let the mother go, so that it may be well with you and that you may prolong your days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; Exodus 21:28–36"> If you build a new house, you are to construct a railing around your roof, so that you do not bring bloodguilt on your house if someone falls from it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> Do not plant your vineyard with two types of seed; if you do, the entire harvest will be defiled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or will be forfeited to the sanctuary">b</a></sup>—both the crop you plant and the fruit of your vineyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16"> Do not plow with an ox and a donkey yoked together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19"> Do not wear clothes of wool and linen woven together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Numbers 15:37–41"> You are to make tassels on the four corners of the cloak you wear.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Marriage Violations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> Suppose a man marries a woman, has relations with her, and comes to hate her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> and he then accuses her of shameful conduct and gives her a bad name, saying, “I married this woman and had relations with her, but I discovered she was not a virgin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the young woman’s father and mother shall bring the proof of her virginity to the city elders at the gate</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and say to the elders, “I gave my daughter in marriage to this man, but he has come to hate her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And now he has accused her of shameful conduct, saying, ‘I discovered that your daughter was not a virgin.’ But here is the proof of her virginity.” And they shall spread out the cloth before the city elders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> Then the elders of that city shall take the man and punish him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> They are also to fine him a hundred shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="100 shekels is approximately 2.5 pounds or 1.1 kilograms of silver.">c</a></sup>and give them to the young woman’s father, because this man has given a virgin of Israel a bad name. And she shall remain his wife; he must not divorce her as long as he lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If, however, this accusation is true, and no proof of the young woman’s virginity can be found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> she shall be brought to the door of her father’s house, and there the men of her city will stone her to death. For she has committed an outrage in Israel by being promiscuous in her father’s house. So you must purge the evil from among you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Here and in verse 24; cited in 1 Corinthians 5:13">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> If a man is found lying with another man’s wife, both the man who slept with her and the woman must die. You must purge the evil from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If there is a virgin pledged in marriage to a man, and another man encounters her in the city and sleeps with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> you must take both of them out to the gate of that city and stone them to death—the young woman because she did not cry out in the city, and the man because he has violated his neighbor’s wife. So you must purge the evil from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> But if the man encounters a betrothed woman in the open country, and he overpowers her and lies with her, only the man who has done this must die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> Do nothing to the young woman, because she has committed no sin worthy of death. This case is just like one in which a man attacks his neighbor and murders him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> When he found her in the field, the betrothed woman cried out, but there was no one to save her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> If a man encounters a virgin who is not pledged in marriage, and he seizes her and lies with her, and they are discovered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> then the man who lay with her must pay the young woman’s father fifty shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="50 shekels is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of silver.">e</a></sup>and she must become his wife because he has violated her. He must not divorce her as long as he lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> A man is not to marry his father’s wife, so that he will not dishonor his father’s marriage bed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or uncover his father’s skirt">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
