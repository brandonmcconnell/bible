## The Death of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Deuteronomy 32:49; Joshua 19:47"> Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, which faces Jericho. And the LORD showed him the whole land—from Gilead as far as Dan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Numbers 34:6; Exodus 23:31"> all of Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah as far as the Western Sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:15; Judges 1:16; Judges 3:13"> the Negev, and the region from the Valley of Jericho (the City of Palms) all the way to Zoar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:52; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13"> And the LORD said to him, “This is the land that I swore to give Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob when I said, ‘I will give it to your descendants.’ I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you will not cross into it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:50; Joshua 1:1–2; Hebrews 3:3–6"> So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, as the LORD had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Deuteronomy 3:29"> And He buried him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SP and some LXX manuscripts they buried him">b</a></sup>in a valley in the land of Moab facing Beth-peor, and no one to this day knows the location of his grave.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:2; Joshua 14:10–11; Genesis 27:1"> Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died, yet his eyes were not weak, and his vitality had not diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:3; Numbers 20:29; Genesis 50:10"> The Israelites grieved for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days, until the time of weeping and mourning for Moses came to an end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Daniel 6:3; Acts 6:6"> Now Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses had laid his hands on him. So the Israelites obeyed him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 12:6–8; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> Since that time, no prophet has risen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 105:26–38"> no prophet who did all the signs and wonders that the LORD sent Moses to do in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh and to all his officials and all his land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and no prophet who performed all the mighty acts of power and awesome deeds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or terrifying deeds">c</a></sup>that Moses did in the sight of all Israel.</span>
</div>
