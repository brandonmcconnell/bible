## Widowhood and Marriage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> When brothers dwell together and one of them dies without a son, the widow must not marry outside the family. Her husband’s brother is to take her as his wife and fulfill the duty of a brother-in-law for her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 22:24, Mark 12:19, and Luke 20:28">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> The first son she bears will carry on the name of the dead brother, so that his name will not be blotted out from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> But if the man does not want to marry his brother’s widow, she is to go to the elders at the city gate and say, “My husband’s brother refuses to preserve his brother’s name in Israel. He is not willing to perform the duty of a brother-in-law for me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then the elders of his city shall summon him and speak with him. If he persists and says, “I do not want to marry her,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Isaiah 50:6"> his brother’s widow shall go up to him in the presence of the elders, remove his sandal, spit in his face, and declare, “This is what is done to the man who will not maintain his brother’s line.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his family name in Israel will be called “The House of the Unsandaled.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> If two men are fighting, and the wife of one comes to rescue her husband from the one striking him, and she reaches out her hand and grabs his genitals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> you are to cut off her hand. You must show her no pity.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Standard Weights and Measures

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> You shall not have two differing weights in your bag, one heavy and one light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You shall not have two differing measures in your house, one large and one small.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> You must maintain accurate and honest weights and measures, so that you may live long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For everyone who behaves dishonestly in regard to these things is detestable to the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Revenge on the Amalekites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what the Amalekites did to you along your way from Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> how they met you on your journey when you were tired and weary, and they attacked all your stragglers; they had no fear of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> When the LORD your God gives you rest from the enemies around you in the land that He is giving you to possess as an inheritance, you are to blot out the memory of Amalek from under heaven. Do not forget!</span>
</div>
