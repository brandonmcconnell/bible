## A Greeting from the Elder

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> The elder,<br />To the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in the truth—and not I alone, but also all who know the truth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, will be with us in truth and love.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Walking in the Truth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> I was overjoyed to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as the Father has commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> And now I urge you, dear lady—not as a new commandment to you, but one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> And this is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the very commandment you have heard from the beginning, that you must walk in love.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware of Deceivers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> For many deceivers have gone out into the world, refusing to confess the coming of Jesus Christ in the flesh. Any such person is the deceiver and the antichrist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what we have worked for,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NE and WH what you have worked for">a</a></sup>but that you may be fully rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> Anyone who runs ahead without remaining in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever remains in His teaching has both the Father and the Son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> If anyone comes to you but does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your home or even greet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> Whoever greets such a person shares in his evil deeds.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> I have many things to write to you, but I would prefer not to do so with paper and ink. Instead, I hope to come and speak with you face to face,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally mouth to mouth">b</a></sup>so that our joy may be complete.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> The children of your elect sister send you greetings.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include Amen.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
