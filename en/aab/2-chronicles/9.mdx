## The Queen of Sheba

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Now when the queen of Sheba heard about the fame of Solomon, she came to test him with difficult questions. She arrived in Jerusalem with a very large caravan—with camels bearing spices, gold in abundance, and precious stones.<br />And she came to Solomon and spoke with him about all that was on her mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 3:12; Mark 4:34"> And Solomon answered all her questions; nothing was too difficult for him to explain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–4; 1 Kings 10:3; 1 Kings 6:1–7"> When the queen of Sheba saw the wisdom of Solomon, the palace he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 12:37; 1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 143:7"> the food at his table, the seating of his servants, the service and attire of his attendants, the attire of his cupbearers, and the ascension offerings he presented<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX and Syriac (see also 1 Kings 10:5); Hebrew and his stairway by which he went up">a</a></sup>at the house of the LORD, it took her breath away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 10:6"> She said to the king, “The report I heard in my own country about your words<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or your achievements">b</a></sup>and wisdom is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 10:7; 2 Chronicles 9:5; 1 Kings 4:34"> But I did not believe the reports until I came and saw with my own eyes. Indeed, not half of the greatness of your wisdom was told to me. You have far exceeded the report I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:21"> How blessed are your men! How blessed are these servants of yours who stand continually before you and hear your wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11; 1 Chronicles 29:23; Isaiah 32:1–2"> Blessed be the LORD your God, who has delighted in you to set you on His throne to be king for the LORD your God. Because your God loved Israel enough to establish them forever, He has made you king over them to carry out justice and righteousness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> Then she gave the king 120 talents of gold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="120 talents is approximately 4.52 tons or 4.1 metric tons of gold.">c</a></sup>a great quantity of spices, and precious stones. There had never been such spices as those the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:18; 1 Kings 10:11; 1 Kings 10:22"> (The servants of Hiram and of Solomon who brought gold from Ophir also brought algum<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Algum is probably a variant of almug; also in verse 11; see 1 Kings 10:11.">d</a></sup>wood and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 10:12; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Psalms 92:1–3"> The king made the algum wood into steps<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or gateways">e</a></sup>for the house of the LORD and for the king’s palace, and into lyres and harps for the singers. Never before had anything like them been seen in the land of Judah.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:13; Ephesians 3:20; Psalms 20:4"> King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba all she desired—whatever she asked—far more than she had brought the king. Then she left and returned to her own country, along with her servants.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Wealth and Splendor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:15; Psalms 68:29; 1 Kings 10:14–28"> The weight of gold that came to Solomon each year was 666 talents,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="666 talents is approximately 25.1 tons or 22.8 metric tons of gold.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:29; Psalms 72:10"> not including the revenue from the merchants and traders. And all the Arabian kings and governors of the land also brought gold and silver to Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> King Solomon made two hundred large shields of hammered gold; six hundred shekels of hammered gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="600 shekels is approximately 15.1 pounds or 6.8 kilograms of gold.">g</a></sup>went into each shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2"> He also made three hundred small shields of hammered gold; three hundred shekels of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="300 shekels is approximately 7.5 pounds or 3.4 kilograms of gold.">h</a></sup>went into each shield. And the king put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:18–20; Psalms 45:8; Revelation 20:11"> Additionally, the king made a great throne of ivory and overlaid it with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Revelation 5:5; Numbers 23:24"> The throne had six steps, and a footstool of gold was attached to it. There were armrests on both sides of the seat, with a lion standing beside each armrest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 21:12"> Twelve lions stood on the six steps, one at either end of each step. Nothing like this had ever been made for any kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; Esther 1:7; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> All King Solomon’s drinking cups were gold, and all the utensils of the House of the Forest of Lebanon were pure gold. There was no silver, because it was accounted as nothing in the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Job 39:13; 1 Kings 10:22"> For the king had the ships of Tarshish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or a fleet of trading ships; twice in this verse">i</a></sup>that went with Hiram’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew Huram’s, a variant of Hiram’s">j</a></sup>servants, and once every three years the ships of Tarshish would arrive bearing gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or baboons">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; Psalms 89:27; 1 Kings 4:30–31"> So King Solomon surpassed all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:5; James 3:17; Proverbs 2:6"> All the kings of the earth sought an audience with Solomon to hear the wisdom that God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 10:25; Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10"> Year after year, each visitor would bring his tribute: articles of silver and gold, clothing, weapons, spices, horses, and mules.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> Solomon had 4,000 stalls for horses and chariots, and 12,000 horses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or horsemen or charioteers">l</a></sup>which he stationed in the chariot cities and also with him in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 4:21; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:24"> He reigned over all the kings from the Euphrates<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew the River">m</a></sup>to the land of the Philistines, as far as the border of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> The king made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar as abundant as sycamore in the foothills.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:16; 1 Kings 10:28; Isaiah 31:1"> Solomon’s horses were imported from Egypt and from all the lands.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 11:41–43; 1 Kings 11:29"> As for the rest of the acts of Solomon, from beginning to end, are they not written in the Records of Nathan the Prophet, in the Prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the Visions of Iddo the Seer concerning Jeroboam son of Nebat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 11:42–43"> Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Solomon rested with his fathers and was buried in the city of his father David. And his son Rehoboam reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
