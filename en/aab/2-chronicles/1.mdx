## Solomon’s Prayer for Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Kings 2:12; Genesis 39:2"> Now Solomon son of David established himself securely over his kingdom, and the LORD his God was with him and highly exalted him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; 1 Chronicles 27:1; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> Then Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, to the judges, and to every leader in all Israel—the heads of the families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:29; 1 Chronicles 16:39; Leviticus 1:1"> And Solomon and the whole assembly went to the high place at Gibeon because it was the location of God’s Tent of Meeting, which Moses the servant of the LORD had made in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Chronicles 15:25–28; 2 Samuel 6:2"> Now David had brought the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim to the place he had prepared for it, because he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 38:1–7; 1 Chronicles 2:19–20"> But the bronze altar made by Bezalel son of Uri, the son of Hur, was in Gibeon before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX, Vulgate, and some Hebrew manuscripts was there before; MT he placed before">a</a></sup>the tabernacle of the LORD. So Solomon and the assembly inquired of Him there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Kings 8:63; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> Solomon offered sacrifices there before the LORD on the bronze altar at the Tent of Meeting, where he offered a thousand ascension offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; 1 John 5:14–15; John 16:23"> That night God appeared to Solomon and said, “Ask, and I will give it to you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5; Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 22:51–1"> Solomon replied to God: “You have shown much loving devotion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">b</a></sup>to my father David, and You have made me king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 132:11–12"> Now, O LORD God, let Your promise to my father David be fulfilled. For You have made me king over a people as numerous as the dust of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 4:7; Proverbs 2:2–6"> Now grant me wisdom and knowledge, so that I may lead this people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally so that I may go out before this people and come in">c</a></sup>For who is able to govern this great people of Yours?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 23:7; 1 Samuel 16:7"> God said to Solomon, “Since this was in your heart instead of requesting riches or wealth or honor for yourself or death for your enemies—and since you have not even requested long life but have asked for wisdom and knowledge to govern My people over whom I have made you king—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; 2 Chronicles 9:22; Matthew 6:33"> therefore wisdom and knowledge have been granted to you. And I will also give you riches and wealth and honor unlike anything given to the kings before you or after you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 4:24–25"> So Solomon went to Jerusalem from the high place in Gibeon, from before the Tent of Meeting, and he reigned over Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Riches

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26–29; 2 Chronicles 9:25"> Solomon accumulated<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally Solomon accumulated chariots and horses; he had">d</a></sup>1,400 chariots and 12,000 horses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or horsemen or charioteers">e</a></sup>which he stationed in the chariot cities and also with him in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:27; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> The king made silver and gold as common in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar as abundant as sycamore in the foothills.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:28; 1 Kings 10:28–29"> Solomon’s horses were imported from Egypt and Kue;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Probably an area in Cilicia, a province in the southeast of Asia Minor">g</a></sup>the royal merchants purchased them from Kue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29"> A chariot could be imported from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="600 shekels is approximately 15.1 pounds or 6.8 kilograms of silver.">h</a></sup>and a horse for a hundred and fifty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="150 shekels is approximately 3.8 pounds or 1.7 kilograms of silver.">i</a></sup>Likewise, they exported them to all the kings of the Hittites and to the kings of Aram.</span>
</div>
</div>
