## Solomon’s Additional Achievements

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:10–28"> Now at the end of the twenty years during which Solomon had built the house of the LORD and his own palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–18"> Solomon rebuilt the cities Hiram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Huram, a variant of Hiram; also in verse 18">a</a></sup>had given him and settled Israelites there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Samuel 8:3"> Then Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:17–19"> He built Tadmor in the wilderness, in addition to all the store cities that he had built in Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:24; Joshua 16:5; 2 Chronicles 14:7"> He rebuilt Upper and Lower Beth-horon as fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> as well as Baalath, all the store cities that belonged to Solomon, and all the cities for his chariots and horses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or horsemen or charioteers">b</a></sup>—whatever he desired to build in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout the land of his dominion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 7:1; 1 Kings 9:20–22"> As for all the people who remained of the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites (these people were not Israelites)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:21; 1 Kings 4:6"> their descendants who remained in the land, those whom the Israelites had not destroyed—Solomon conscripted these people to be forced laborers, as they are to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–12; Exodus 19:5–6; Galatians 4:31"> But Solomon did not consign any of the Israelites to slave labor, because they were his men of war, the leaders of his captains, and the commanders of his chariots and cavalry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 1 Kings 5:16; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> They were also the chief officers for King Solomon: 250 supervisors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 7:8; 1 Kings 9:24"> Solomon brought the daughter of Pharaoh up from the City of David to the palace he had built for her. For he said, “My wife must not live in the house of David king of Israel, because the places the ark of the LORD has entered are holy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 15:8; John 10:23"> At that time Solomon offered ascension offerings to the LORD on the altar of the LORD he had built in front of the portico.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 29:38–42; Ezekiel 46:3–15"> He observed the daily requirement for offerings according to the commandment of Moses for Sabbaths, New Moons, and the three annual appointed feasts—the Feast of Unleavened Bread,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.">c</a></sup>the Feast of Weeks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).">d</a></sup>and the Feast of Tabernacles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as the Feast of Booths or the Feast of Shelters and originally called the Feast of Ingathering (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:17; Ezra 6:18; Nehemiah 12:36"> In keeping with the ordinances of his father David, Solomon appointed the divisions of the priests over their service, and the Levites for their duties to offer praise and to minister before the priests according to the daily requirement. He also appointed gatekeepers by their divisions at each gate, for this had been the command of David, the man of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Chronicles 9:29; 1 Kings 7:51"> They did not turn aside from the king’s command regarding the priests or the Levites in any matter, including that of the treasuries.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Thus all the work of Solomon was carried out, from the day the foundation was laid for the house of the LORD until it was finished. So the house of the LORD was completed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:22; Deuteronomy 2:8; Numbers 33:35"> Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Eloth is a variant of Elath; see LXX, 2 Kings 14:22, and 2 Kings 16:6.">f</a></sup>on the coast of Edom.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, along the shore of the Red Sea in the land of Edom">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:10; 2 Chronicles 9:13; Ecclesiastes 2:8"> So Hiram sent him ships captained by his servants, along with crews of experienced sailors. They went with Solomon’s servants to Ophir and acquired from there 450 talents of gold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="450 talents is approximately 17 tons or 15.4 metric tons of gold.">h</a></sup>which they delivered to King Solomon.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
