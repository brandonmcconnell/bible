## Jehoram Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 22:50; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And Jehoshaphat rested with his fathers and was buried with them in the City of David. And his son Jehoram reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:6; 2 Chronicles 23:2; 2 Chronicles 24:5"> Jehoram’s brothers, the sons of Jehoshaphat, were Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah; these were all sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Judah; in 2 Chronicles, Judah is occasionally called Israel, as representative of the true Israel.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:23; Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 2 Chronicles 11:5"> Their father had given them many gifts of silver and gold and precious things, as well as the fortified cities in Judah; but he gave the kingdom to Jehoram because he was the firstborn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Chronicles 21:17; Genesis 4:8"> When Jehoram had established himself over his father’s kingdom, he strengthened himself by putting to the sword all his brothers along with some of the princes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:17–22"> Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eight years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; 1 Kings 12:28–30; 2 Kings 8:18"> And Jehoram walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, just as the house of Ahab had done. For he married a daughter of Ahab and did evil in the sight of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; Psalms 132:17–18; 2 Samuel 7:12–17"> Yet the LORD was unwilling to destroy the house of David, because of the covenant He had made with David, and since He had promised to maintain a lamp for David and his descendants forever.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Edom and Libnah Rebel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:10; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23; Genesis 27:40"> In the days of Jehoram, Edom rebelled against the hand of Judah and appointed their own king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Jehoram crossed into Edom with his officers and all his chariots. When the Edomites surrounded him and his chariot commanders, he rose up and attacked<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or he went out and escaped">b</a></sup>by night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Jeremiah 2:13; 2 Chronicles 13:10"> So to this day Edom has been in rebellion against the hand of Judah. Likewise, Libnah rebelled against his hand at the same time, because Jehoram had forsaken the LORD, the God of his fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:5; 1 Kings 11:7; Leviticus 17:7"> Jehoram had also built high places on the hills of Judah; he had caused the people of Jerusalem to prostitute themselves and had led Judah astray.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah’s Letter to Jehoram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 2 Chronicles 17:3–4; 2 Kings 2:1"> Then a letter came to Jehoram from Elijah the prophet, which stated:<br />“This is what the LORD, the God of your father David, says:<br />‘You have not walked in the ways of your father Jehoshaphat or of Asa king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:4; 2 Chronicles 21:11; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> but you have walked in the ways of the kings of Israel and have caused Judah and the people of Jerusalem to prostitute themselves, just as the house of Ahab prostituted itself. You have also killed your brothers, your father’s family, who were better than you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Exodus 20:5; Hosea 5:11"> So behold, the LORD is about to strike your people, your sons, your wives, and all your possessions with a serious blow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 1:18; Deuteronomy 28:67"> And day after day you yourself will suffer from a severe illness, a disease of your bowels, until it causes your bowels to come out.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoram’s Disease and Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:11; 2 Chronicles 33:11; 1 Kings 11:14"> Then the LORD stirred against Jehoram the spirit of the Philistines and Arabs who lived near the Cushites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; 2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> So they went to war against Judah, invaded it, and carried off all the possessions found in the king’s palace, along with his sons and wives; not a son was left to him except Jehoahaz,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jehoahaz is a variant of Ahaziah; see 2 Chronicles 22:1.">c</a></sup>his youngest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:15; Acts 12:23; 2 Kings 9:29"> After all this, the LORD afflicted Jehoram with an incurable disease of the bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5"> This continued day after day until two full years had passed. Finally, his intestines came out because of his disease, and he died in severe pain. And his people did not make a fire in his honor as they had done for his fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 28:27; Jeremiah 22:28"> Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem eight years. He died, to no one’s regret, and was buried in the City of David, but not in the tombs of the kings.</span>
</div>
</div>
