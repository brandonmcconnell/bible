## Shemaiah’s Prophecy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 33:16"> When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he mobilized the house of Judah and Benjamin—180,000 chosen warriors—to fight against Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> But the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah the man of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> “Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon king of Judah and all the Israelites in Judah and Benjamin</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> that this is what the LORD says: ‘You are not to go up and fight against your brothers. Each of you must return home, for this is My doing.’”<br />So they listened to the words of the LORD and turned back from going against Jeroboam.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam Fortifies Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and he built up cities for defense in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> He built up Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, the fortified cities in Judah and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> He strengthened their fortifications and put officers in them, with supplies of food, oil, and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> He also put shields and spears in all the cities and strengthened them greatly. So Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeroboam Forsakes the Priests and Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Moreover, the priests and Levites from all their districts throughout Israel stood with Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> For the Levites left their pasturelands and their possessions and went to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them as priests of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> And Jeroboam appointed his own priests for the high places and for the goat demons and calf idols he had made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalms 84:5–7"> Those from every tribe of Israel who had set their hearts to seek the LORD their God followed the Levites to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years, because they walked for three years in the way of David and Solomon.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam’s Family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> And Rehoboam married Mahalath, who was the daughter of David’s son Jerimoth and of Abihail, the daughter of Jesse’s son Eliab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> She bore sons to him: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> After her, he married Maacah daughter of Absalom, and she bore to him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines. In all, he had eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he was the father of twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah as chief prince among his brothers, intending to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> Rehoboam also acted wisely by dispersing some of his sons throughout the districts of Judah and Benjamin, and to all the fortified cities. He gave them abundant provisions and sought many wives for them.</span>
</div>
</div>
