## The Ark Enters the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 22:14; 2 Samuel 8:11"> So all the work that Solomon had performed for the house of the LORD was completed.<br />Then Solomon brought in the items his father David had dedicated—the silver, the gold, and all the furnishings—and he placed them in the treasuries of the house of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12; 2 Chronicles 1:4; Numbers 10:33"> At that time Solomon assembled in Jerusalem the elders of Israel—all the tribal heads and family leaders of the Israelites—to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from Zion, the City of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:2; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10; Leviticus 23:34–36"> So all the men of Israel came together to the king at the feast in the seventh month.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Feast of Tabernacles (or Booths or Shelters); see Leviticus 23:33–36.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:3; Joshua 3:6; Joshua 6:6"> When all the elders of Israel had arrived, the Levites took up the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 8:4"> and they brought up the ark and the Tent of Meeting with all its sacred furnishings. The Levitical priests carried them up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:21; 2 Samuel 6:13; 1 Chronicles 16:1–2"> There, before the ark, King Solomon and the whole congregation of Israel who had assembled with him sacrificed so many sheep and oxen that they could not be counted or numbered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Kings 6:23–28; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place in the inner sanctuary of the temple, the Most Holy Place,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the Holy of Holies">b</a></sup>beneath the wings of the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:12–15; Numbers 4:6; Exodus 37:3–5"> For the cherubim spread their wings over the place of the ark and overshadowed the ark and its poles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8–9"> The poles of the ark extended far enough that their ends were visible from in front of the inner sanctuary,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts and LXX The poles extended far enough that their ends were visible from the Holy Place in front of the inner sanctuary; see 1 Kings 8:8.">c</a></sup>but not from outside the Holy Place;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally not from outside">d</a></sup>and they are there to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; 2 Chronicles 6:11; Deuteronomy 10:2–5"> There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets that Moses had placed in it at Horeb,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai">e</a></sup>where the LORD had made a covenant with the Israelites after they had come out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:4; 2 Chronicles 29:5; Job 1:5"> Now all the priests who were present had consecrated themselves regardless of their divisions. And when the priests came out of the Holy Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:24; Psalms 68:25; 1 Chronicles 6:39"> all the Levitical singers—Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and relatives—stood on the east side of the altar, dressed in fine linen and playing cymbals, harps, and lyres, accompanied by 120 priests sounding trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 3:11; Jeremiah 33:11; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> The trumpeters and singers joined together to praise and thank the LORD with one voice. They lifted up their voices, accompanied by trumpets, cymbals, and musical instruments, in praise to the LORD:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For He is good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His loving devotion endures forever.”</span><br />And the temple, the house of the LORD, was filled with a cloud</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 40:35; 1 Kings 8:11; Isaiah 6:1–4"> so that the priests could not stand there to minister because of the cloud. For the glory of the LORD filled the house of God.</span>
</div>
</div>
