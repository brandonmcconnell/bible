## The Destruction of Idols

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Chronicles 32:12; Genesis 19:15"> When all this had ended, the Israelites in attendance went out to the cities of Judah and broke up the sacred pillars, chopped down the Asherah poles, and tore down the high places and altars throughout Judah and Benjamin, as well as in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had utterly destroyed them all. Then all the Israelites returned to their cities, each to his own property.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:1; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6; 2 Chronicles 29:24–26"> Hezekiah reestablished the divisions of the priests and Levites—each of them according to their duties as priests or Levites—for the ascension offerings and well-being offerings, for ministry, for giving thanks, and for singing praises at the gates of the LORD’s dwelling.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Contributions for Worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7; Colossians 2:16–17"> The king contributed from his own possessions for the regular morning and evening ascension offerings and for the ascension offerings on the Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts, as written in the Law of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; Numbers 18:8–21; Nehemiah 13:10–13"> Moreover, he commanded the people living in Jerusalem to make a contribution for the priests and Levites so that they could devote themselves to the Law of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12; 2 Chronicles 24:10–11; Nehemiah 10:35–39"> As soon as the order went out, the Israelites generously provided the firstfruits of the grain, new wine, oil, and honey, and of all the produce of the field, and they brought in an abundance—a tithe of everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 14:28"> And the Israelites and Judahites who lived in the cities of Judah also brought a tithe of their herds and flocks and a tithe of the holy things consecrated to the LORD their God, and they laid them in large heaps.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 23:16–24"> In the third month they began building up the heaps, and they finished in the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 14:19–20; Philippians 4:10"> When Hezekiah and his officials came and viewed the heaps, they blessed the LORD and His people Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Hezekiah questioned the priests and Levites about the heaps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11"> and Azariah, the chief priest of the household of Zadok, answered him, “Since the people began to bring their contributions into the house of the LORD, we have had enough to eat, and there is plenty left over, because the LORD has blessed His people; this great abundance is what is left over.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hezekiah Organizes the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38–39; Nehemiah 13:5; Nehemiah 13:12–13"> Then Hezekiah commanded them to prepare storerooms in the house of the LORD, and they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:9; 2 Kings 12:15; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> And they faithfully brought in the contributions, tithes, and dedicated gifts. Conaniah the Levite was the officer in charge of them, and his brother Shimei was second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5; 2 Chronicles 31:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:11"> Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were overseers under the authority of Conaniah and his brother Shimei, by appointment of King Hezekiah and of Azariah the chief official of the house of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:38; 1 Chronicles 26:12; Leviticus 27:28"> Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the keeper of the East Gate, was in charge of the freewill offerings given to God, distributing the contributions to the LORD and the consecrated gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 21:9–19; 1 Chronicles 9:22; 2 Chronicles 29:12"> Under his authority, Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah faithfully distributed portions to their fellow priests in their cities, according to their divisions, old and young alike.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or whether large or small">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 3:4; Leviticus 21:22–23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> In addition, they distributed portions to the males registered by genealogy who were three years of age or older—to all who would enter the house of the LORD for their daily duties for service in the responsibilities of their divisions—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; 1 Chronicles 23:27; Numbers 3:15"> and to the priests enrolled according to their families in the genealogy, as well as to the Levites twenty years of age or older, according to their responsibilities and divisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:16; Isaiah 5:16; 2 Chronicles 31:15"> The genealogy included all the little ones, wives, sons, and daughters of the whole assembly. For they had faithfully consecrated themselves as holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12–15; Leviticus 25:34; Numbers 35:2–5"> As for the priests, the descendants of Aaron, who lived on the farmlands around each of their cities or in any other city, men were designated by name to distribute a portion to every male among the priests and to every Levite listed by the genealogies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 2 Kings 22:2; Acts 24:16"> So this is what Hezekiah did throughout Judah. He did what was good and upright and true before the LORD his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Jeremiah 29:13; Joshua 1:7–8"> He acted with all his heart in every work that he began in the service of the house of God, and in the law and the commandments, in order to seek his God. And so he prospered.</span>
</div>
</div>
