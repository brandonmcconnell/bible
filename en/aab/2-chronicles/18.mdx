## Jehoshaphat Allies with Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; 2 Chronicles 21:6; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> Now Jehoshaphat had an abundance of riches and honor, and he allied himself with Ahab by marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 1:9"> And some years later he went down to visit Ahab in Samaria, where Ahab sacrificed many sheep and cattle for him and the people with him and urged him to march up to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> Ahab king of Israel asked Jehoshaphat king of Judah, “Will you go with me against Ramoth-gilead?”<br />And Jehoshaphat replied, “I am as you are, and my people are your people; we will join you in the war.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 2:1"> But Jehoshaphat also said to the king of Israel, “Please inquire first for the word of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> So the king of Israel assembled the prophets, four hundred men, and asked them, “Should we go to war against Ramoth-gilead, or should we refrain?”<br />“Go up,” they replied, “and God will deliver it into the hand of the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7–9; 2 Kings 3:11–13"> But Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there not still a prophet of the LORD here of whom we can inquire?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Micah 2:7; 1 Kings 21:20"> The king of Israel answered, “There is still one man through whom we can inquire of the LORD, but I hate him because he never prophesies anything good for me, but only bad. He is Micaiah son of Imlah.”<br />“The king should not say that!” Jehoshaphat replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; 2 Chronicles 18:25–26"> So the king of Israel called one of his officials and said, “Bring Micaiah son of Imlah at once.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> Dressed in royal attire, the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were sitting on their thrones at the threshing floor by the entrance of the gate of Samaria, with all the prophets prophesying before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; Zechariah 1:18–21; Jeremiah 28:2–3"> Now Zedekiah son of Chenaanah had made for himself iron horns and declared, “This is what the LORD says: ‘With these you shall gore the Arameans until they are finished off.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the prophets were prophesying the same, saying, “Go up to Ramoth-gilead and triumph, for the LORD will deliver it into the hand of the king.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Micaiah Prophesies against Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:6; Psalms 10:11; Micah 2:11"> Then the messenger who had gone to call Micaiah instructed him, “Behold, with one accord the words of the prophets are favorable to the king. So please let your words be like theirs, and speak favorably.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> But Micaiah said, “As surely as the LORD lives, I will speak whatever my God tells me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> When Micaiah arrived, the king asked him, “Micaiah, should we go to war against Ramoth-gilead, or should we refrain?”<br />“Go up and triumph,” Micaiah replied, “for they will be delivered into your hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; 1 Samuel 14:24; 1 Kings 22:16"> But the king said to him, “How many times must I make you swear not to tell me anything but the truth in the name of the LORD?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> So Micaiah declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I saw all Israel scattered on the hills</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like sheep without a shepherd.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the LORD said, ‘These people have no master;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let each one return home in peace.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:7; 1 Kings 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “Did I not tell you that he never prophesies good for me, but only bad?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; 1 Kings 22:19–23"> Micaiah continued, “Therefore hear the word of the LORD: I saw the LORD sitting on His throne, and all the host of heaven standing on His right and on His left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And the LORD said, ‘Who will entice Ahab king of Israel to march up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?’<br />And one suggested this, and another that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Then a spirit came forward, stood before the LORD, and said, ‘I will entice him.’<br />‘By what means?’ asked the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; 1 Kings 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> And he replied, ‘I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouths of all his prophets.’<br />‘You will surely entice him and prevail,’ said the LORD. ‘Go and do it.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> So you see, the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouths of these prophets of yours, and the LORD has pronounced disaster against you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremiah 20:2; 2 Chronicles 18:10"> Then Zedekiah son of Chenaanah went up, struck Micaiah in the face, and demanded, “Which way did the Spirit of the LORD go when He departed from me to speak with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> Micaiah replied, “You will soon see, on that day when you go and hide in an inner room.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:8; 2 Chronicles 34:8; Jeremiah 38:6–7"> And the king of Israel declared, “Take Micaiah and return him to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king’s son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; Psalms 80:5; Luke 23:2"> and tell them that this is what the king says: ‘Put this man in prison and feed him only bread and water until I return safely.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Micah 1:2"> But Micaiah replied, “If you ever return safely, the LORD has not spoken through me.” Then he added, “Take heed, all you people!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ahab’s Defeat and Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 22:29–33"> So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself and go into battle, but you wear your royal robes.” So the king of Israel disguised himself and went into battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; 1 Kings 20:42"> Now the king of Aram had ordered his chariot commanders, “Do not fight with anyone, small or great, except the king of Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:14; Psalms 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, “This is the king of Israel!” So they turned to fight against him, but Jehoshaphat cried out, and the LORD helped him. God drew them away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:33"> And when the chariot commanders saw that he was not the king of Israel, they turned back from pursuing him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; 2 Samuel 15:11; 1 Kings 22:34–35"> However, a certain man drew his bow without taking special aim, and he struck the king of Israel between the joints of his armor. So the king said to his charioteer, “Turn around<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Turn your hand">a</a></sup>and take me out of the battle, for I am badly wounded!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; 2 Chronicles 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> The battle raged throughout that day, and the king of Israel propped himself up in his chariot facing the Arameans until evening. And at sunset he died.</span>
</div>
