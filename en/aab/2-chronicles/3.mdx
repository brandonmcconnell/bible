## Temple Construction Begins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:18; Genesis 22:14; 1 Chronicles 22:1"> Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See LXX; Hebrew where He had appeared">a</a></sup>to his father David. This was the place that David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Ornan is a variant of Araunah; see 2 Samuel 24:16.">b</a></sup>the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1"> Solomon began construction on the second day of the second month in the fourth year of his reign.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Chronicles 28:11–19"> The foundation that Solomon laid for the house of God was sixty cubits long and twenty cubits wide,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The foundation was approximately 90 feet long and 30 feet wide (27.4 meters long and 9.1 meters wide).">c</a></sup>according to the old standard.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The old standard of measurement was a cubit equal to 18 inches or 45.7 centimeters. The new standard, a cubit of approximately 21 inches or 53.3 centimeters (the long cubit) is the basic unit of length throughout Ezekiel 40 to 48.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:23; Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> The portico at the front, extending across the width of the temple, was twenty cubits long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="20 cubits is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters; also in verses 8, 11, and 13.">e</a></sup>and twenty cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some LXX and Syriac manuscripts; Hebrew 120 cubits high (approximately 180 feet or 54.9 meters)">f</a></sup>He overlaid the inside with pure gold.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Temple’s Interior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:15–17; 1 Kings 6:21–22"> He paneled the main room with cypress,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or pine or juniper or fir">g</a></sup>which he overlaid with fine gold and decorated with palm trees and chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:8; Revelation 21:18–21"> He adorned the temple with precious stones for beauty, and its gold was from Parvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20–22; Exodus 26:29; Ezekiel 7:20"> He overlaid its beams, thresholds, walls, and doors with gold, and he carved cherubim on the walls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; 1 Kings 6:19–20; 1 Kings 6:16"> Then he made the Most Holy Place;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or the Holy of Holies; also in verse 10">h</a></sup>its length corresponded to the width of the temple—twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. And he overlaid the inside with six hundred talents of fine gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="600 talents is approximately 22.6 tons or 20.5 metric tons of gold.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11"> The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="50 shekels is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of gold.">j</a></sup>He also overlaid the upper rooms with gold.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cherubim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23–28; Exodus 25:18"> In the Most Holy Place he made two cherubim of sculptured work, and he overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The total wingspan of the cherubim was twenty cubits. One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="5 cubits is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters; also in verse 15.">k</a></sup>and touched the wall of the temple, and its other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The wing of the second cherub also measured five cubits and touched the wall of the temple, while its other wing measured five cubits and touched the wing of the first cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:20"> So the total wingspan of these cherubim was twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, facing the main room.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Veil and Pillars

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:3; Hebrews 10:20; Matthew 27:51"> He made the veil of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen, with cherubim woven into it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–24; Jeremiah 52:20–23"> In front of the temple he made two pillars, which together were thirty-five cubits high,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="35 cubits is approximately 52.5 feet or 16 meters.">l</a></sup>each with a capital on top measuring five cubits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 6:21"> He made interwoven chains<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or He made chains in the inner sanctuary">m</a></sup>and put them on top of the pillars. He made a hundred pomegranates and fastened them into the chainwork.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:21"> Then he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the south and one on the north. The pillar on the south he named Jachin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Jachin probably means He establishes.">n</a></sup>and the pillar on the north he named Boaz.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Boaz probably means in Him is strength.">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
