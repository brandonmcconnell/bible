## God’s Judgment on the Ungodly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; Philippians 1:27; Jude 1:20"> Beloved, although I made every effort to write to you about the salvation we share, I felt it necessary to write and urge you to contend earnestly for the faith entrusted once for all to the holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; Galatians 2:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> For certain men have crept in among you unnoticed—ungodly ones who were designated long ago for condemnation. They turn the grace of our God into a license for immorality, and they deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Hebrews 3:16–2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–12"> Although you are fully aware of this, I want to remind you that after Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NE, WH, BYZ, and TR the Lord">a</a></sup>had delivered His people out of the land of Egypt, He destroyed those who did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 25:41; Ephesians 6:12"> And the angels who did not stay within their own domain but abandoned their proper dwelling—these He has kept in eternal chains under darkness, bound for judgment on that great day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; 2 Peter 2:6; Romans 1:26–27"> In like manner, Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, who indulged in sexual immorality and pursued strange flesh, are on display as an example of those who sustain the punishment of eternal fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Peter 2:10–12"> Yet in the same way these dreamers defile their bodies, reject authority, and slander glorious beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; 2 Peter 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But even the archangel Michael, when he disputed with the devil over the body of Moses, did not presume to bring a slanderous charge against him, but said, “The Lord rebuke you!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This account is attributed by Origen to the Testament of Moses, also called the Assumption of Moses.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Romans 1:21–22"> These men, however, slander what they do not understand, and like irrational animals, they will be destroyed by the things they do instinctively.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:15; 1 John 3:12; Revelation 2:14"> Woe to them! They have traveled the path of Cain; they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam; they have perished in Korah’s rebellion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17–20; Matthew 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These men are hidden reefs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or are blemishes">c</a></sup>in your love feasts, shamelessly feasting with you but shepherding only themselves. They are clouds without water, carried along by the wind; fruitless trees in autumn, twice dead after being uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17; Philippians 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> They are wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shame; wandering stars, for whom blackest darkness has been reserved forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 50:3–5; Hebrews 11:5–6"> Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also prophesied about them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the Lord is coming</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with myriads of His holy ones</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> <span className="pl pl-2">to execute judgment on everyone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to convict all the ungodly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of every ungodly act of wickedness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every harsh word spoken against Him by ungodly sinners.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jude quotes 1 Enoch 1:9, from a Jewish book whose oldest parts date from about 300 to 200 BC; copies were found among the Dead Sea Scrolls.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18; 2 Peter 2:10; Philippians 2:14"> These men are discontented grumblers, following after their own lusts; their mouths spew arrogance; they flatter others for their own advantage.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Call to Persevere

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:2; 1 John 4:6; Ephesians 4:11"> But you, beloved, remember what was foretold by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:1–5"> when they said to you, “In the last times there will be scoffers who will follow after their own ungodly desires.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 2 Peter 3:3.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Proverbs 18:1; Romans 8:9"> These are the ones who cause divisions, who are worldly and devoid of the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> But you, beloved, by building yourselves up in your most holy faith and praying in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Lamentations 3:25–26; Hebrews 9:28"> keep yourselves in the love of God as you await the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ to bring you eternal life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Galatians 6:1; 1 John 5:16–18"> And indeed, have mercy on those who doubt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Revelation 3:4"> save others by snatching them from the fire; and to still others show mercy tempered with fear, hating even the clothing stained by the flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Doxology

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 1:22"> Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you unblemished in His glorious presence, with great joy—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Revelation 5:13–14; 1 Peter 5:10–11"> to the only God our Savior be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord before all time, and now, and for all eternity.<br />Amen.</span>
</div>
