## Walking by the Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:18–19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 3:13"> Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR in Christ Jesus, who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 6:14; 2 Corinthians 3:17; Romans 6:18"> For in Christ Jesus the law of the Spirit of life set you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR me">b</a></sup>free from the law of Sin and Death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 7:18–19"> For what the law was powerless to do in that it was weakened by the flesh, God did by sending His own Son in the likeness of sinful man, as an offering for Sin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin. The Greek peri hamartias is the Greek OT's standard phrase for the purification offering (chattat); Paul may be invoking that category.">c</a></sup>He thus condemned Sin in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Galatians 5:22–25; Colossians 1:22"> so that the righteous standard of the law might be fulfilled in us, who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; John 3:6"> Those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh; but those who live according to the Spirit set their minds on the things of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> The mind of the flesh is death, but the mind of the Spirit is life and peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 John 2:15–16; James 4:4"> because the mind of the flesh is hostile to God: It does not submit to God’s law, nor can it do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> Those controlled by the flesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally Those being in the flesh; similarly in verse 9">d</a></sup>cannot please God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> You, however, are controlled not by the flesh, but by the Spirit, if the Spirit of God lives in you. And if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he does not belong to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> But if Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, yet your spirit is alive<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or yet the Spirit is life">e</a></sup>because of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> And if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead is living in you, He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="NA, BYZ, and TR raised Christ from the dead">f</a></sup>will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit, who lives in you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Heirs with Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:2–15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> Therefore, brothers, we have an obligation, but it is not to the flesh, to live according to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> For if you live according to the flesh, you will die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; John 1:12; Galatians 3:26"> For all who are led by the Spirit of God are sons of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:5–7; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Corinthians 2:12"> For you did not receive a spirit of slavery that returns you to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption to sonship, by whom we cry, “Abba! Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5"> The Spirit Himself testifies with our spirit that we are God’s children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 4:7; Galatians 3:29; Ephesians 3:6"> And if we are children, then we are heirs: heirs of God and co-heirs with Christ—if indeed we suffer with Him, so that we may also be glorified with Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Future Glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> I consider that our present sufferings are not comparable to the glory that will be revealed in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 John 3:2"> The creation waits in eager expectation for the revelation of the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> For the creation was subjected to futility, not by its own will, but because of the One who subjected it, in hope</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> that the creation itself will be set free from its bondage to decay and brought into the glorious freedom of the children of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; John 16:21"> We know that the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until the present time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> Not only that, but we ourselves, who have the firstfruits of the Spirit, groan inwardly as we wait eagerly for our adoption as sons, the redemption of our bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For in this hope we were saved; but hope that is seen is no hope at all. Who hopes for what he can already see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 27:14"> But if we hope for what we do not yet see, we wait for it patiently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> In the same way, the Spirit helps us in our weakness. For we do not know how we ought to pray, but the Spirit Himself intercedes for us with groans too deep for words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 John 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> And He who searches our hearts knows the mind of the Spirit, because the Spirit intercedes for the holy ones according to the will of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Works in All Things

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; Genesis 50:20"> And we know that God works all things together for the good of those who love Him, who are called according to His purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4–5; Jeremiah 1:5; Ephesians 1:11"> For those God foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, so that He would be the firstborn among many brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Romans 8:28; Ephesians 1:11"> And those He predestined, He also called; those He called, He also justified; those He justified, He also glorified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; Jeremiah 1:19; Psalms 56:11"> What then shall we say in response to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:28; Psalms 84:11"> He who did not spare His own Son but gave Him up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gk. paredoken, “gave Him up / handed Him over,” the same verb as Rom 4:25 and Isa 53:6, 12 LXX">g</a></sup>for us all, how will He not also, along with Him, freely give us all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 12:10–11; Isaiah 50:8–9; Isaiah 54:17"> Who will bring any charge against God’s elect? It is God who justifies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Romans 8:27; Hebrews 9:24"> Who is there to condemn us? For Christ Jesus, who died, and more than that was raised to life, is at the right hand of God—and He is interceding for us.</span>
</div>
</div>

## More than Conquerors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 10:28; Psalms 103:17; John 16:33"> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall trouble or distress or persecution or famine or nakedness or danger or sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Isaiah 53:7; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> As it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For Your sake we face death all day long;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we are considered as sheep to be slaughtered.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Psalms 44:22">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> No, in all these things we are more than conquerors through Him who loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 10:28; Romans 14:8; 2 Timothy 1:12"> For I am convinced that neither death nor life, neither angels nor principalities, neither the present nor the future, nor any powers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:28–30; Romans 8:35; Romans 5:8"> neither height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
