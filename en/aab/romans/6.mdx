## Dead to Sin, Alive to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:15"> What then shall we say? Shall we continue in sin so that grace may increase?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:9; Colossians 3:3; Romans 7:4"> Certainly not! How can we who died to Sin live in it any longer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:27; Galatians 2:20–21; Romans 6:8"> Or aren’t you aware that all of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into His death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:22–24"> We were therefore buried with Him through baptism into death, in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too may walk in newness of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:10–11; 2 Corinthians 4:10; Colossians 2:12"> For if we have been united with Him like this in His death, we will certainly also be united with Him in His resurrection.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gk. symphytoi, grown together. Believers share in Christ’s death: they are joined to Him in baptism (verse 3) and put on the cross with Him (Gal 2:20), so that they die in Him, for “one died for all, therefore all died” (2 Cor 5:14; 1 Cor 15:22). They pass out of Satan’s power into God’s by going through that death with Christ and rising with Him (Acts 26:18).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:24"> We know that our old man was crucified with Him so that the body of Sin might be rendered powerless, that we should no longer be slaves to Sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:1; Colossians 3:1–3; Romans 7:4"> For anyone who has died has been justified from Sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:11–12; Colossians 3:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:10–14"> Now if we died with Christ, we believe that we will also live with Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Revelation 1:18; Acts 2:24–28"> For we know that since Christ was raised from the dead, He cannot die again; Death no longer has dominion over Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 4:6; 1 Peter 3:18"> The death He died, He died to Sin once for all; but the life He lives, He lives to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Romans 6:2"> So you too must count yourselves dead to Sin, but alive to God in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 6:16; Romans 8:13"> Therefore do not let Sin reign in your mortal body so that you obey its desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 6:16; Colossians 3:5"> Do not present the parts of your body to Sin as instruments of wickedness, but present yourselves to God as those who have been brought from death to life; and present the parts of your body to Him as instruments of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Romans 8:2; Romans 7:4–11"> For Sin shall not be your master, because you are not under law, but under grace.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Wages of Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; Ephesians 2:8–10; Romans 6:1–2"> What then? Shall we sin because we are not under law, but under grace? Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 8:34; Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15"> Do you not know that when you offer yourselves as obedient slaves, you are slaves to the one you obey, whether you are slaves to Sin leading to Death, or to obedience leading to righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:13; Romans 1:8"> But thanks be to God that, though you once were slaves to Sin, you wholeheartedly obeyed the form of teaching to which you were committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:22; Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:1"> You have been set free from Sin and have become slaves to righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 6:13; 1 Peter 4:2–4; Colossians 3:5–7"> I am speaking in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. Just as you used to offer the parts of your body in slavery to impurity and to escalating wickedness, so now offer them in slavery to righteousness leading to holiness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:16–17; John 8:34"> For when you were slaves to Sin, you were free of obligation to righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 7:5; Romans 6:23"> What fruit did you reap at that time from the things of which you are now ashamed? The outcome of those things is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Romans 6:18; Romans 8:2"> But now that you have been set free from Sin and have become slaves to God, the fruit you reap leads to holiness, and the outcome is eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:36; Romans 5:12; James 1:15"> For the wages of Sin is Death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
