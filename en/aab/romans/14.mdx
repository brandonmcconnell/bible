## The Law of Liberty

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Accept him whose faith is weak, without passing judgment on his opinions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or without quarreling over disputable matters">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> For one person has faith to eat all things, while another, who is weak, eats only vegetables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> The one who eats everything must not belittle the one who does not, and the one who does not eat everything must not judge the one who does, for God has accepted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who are you to judge someone else’s servant? To his own master he stands or falls. And he will stand, for the Lord is able to make him stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One person regards a certain day above the others, while someone else considers every day alike. Each one should be fully convinced in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He who observes a special day does so to the Lord;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR include he who does not regard the day, to the Lord he does not regard it;">b</a></sup>he who eats does so to the Lord, for he gives thanks to God; and he who abstains does so to the Lord and gives thanks to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us lives to himself alone, and none of us dies to himself alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> If we live, we live to the Lord, and if we die, we die to the Lord. So whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For this reason Christ died and returned to life, that He might be the Lord of both the dead and the living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> Why, then, do you judge your brother? Or why do you belittle your brother? For we will all stand before God’s judgment seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> It is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As surely as I live,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">every knee will bow before Me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every tongue will confess to God.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or will give praise to God or will acknowledge God; Isaiah 45:23 (see also LXX)">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So then, each of us will give an account of himself to God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Law of Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Therefore let us stop judging one another. Instead, make up your mind not to put any stumbling block or obstacle in your brother’s way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I am convinced and fully persuaded in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself. But if anyone regards something as unclean, then for him it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> If your brother is distressed by what you eat, you are no longer acting in love. Do not by your eating destroy your brother, for whom Christ died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Do not allow what you consider good, then, to be spoken of as evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> For the kingdom of God is not a matter of eating and drinking, but of righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> For whoever serves Christ in this way is pleasing to God and approved by men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So then, let us pursue what leads to peace and to mutual edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Do not destroy the work of God for the sake of food. All food is clean, but it is wrong for a man to let his eating be a stumbling block.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It is better not to eat meat or drink wine or to do anything to cause your brother to stumble.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="SBL, BYZ, and TR include or to be hindered or weakened.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> Keep your belief about such matters between yourself and God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally Keep the faith that you have to yourself before God">e</a></sup>Blessed is the one who does not condemn himself by what he approves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But the one who has doubts is condemned if he eats, because his eating is not from faith; and everything that is not from faith is sin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts place the text of Romans 16:25–27 here.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
