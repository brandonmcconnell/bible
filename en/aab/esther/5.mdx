## Esther Approaches the King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Esther 4:16; Revelation 3:21"> On the third day, Esther put on her royal robes and stood in the inner court of the palace across from the king’s quarters. The king was sitting on his royal throne in the royal courtroom, facing the entrance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 8:4; Esther 4:11; Proverbs 21:1"> As soon as the king saw Queen Esther standing in the court, she found favor in his sight. The king extended the gold scepter in his hand toward Esther, and she approached and touched the tip of the scepter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:23; Esther 7:2; Esther 5:6"> “What is it, Queen Esther?” the king inquired. “What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom, it will be given to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Proverbs 29:11; Esther 3:15"> “If it pleases the king,” Esther replied, “may the king and Haman come today to the banquet I have prepared for the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 6:14"> “Hurry,” commanded the king, “and bring Haman, so we can do as Esther has requested.”<br />So the king and Haman went to the banquet that Esther had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 9:12"> And as they drank their wine, the king said to Esther, “What is your petition? It will be given to you. What is your request? Even up to half the kingdom, it will be fulfilled.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Esther replied, “This is my petition and my request:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 7:3; Proverbs 16:9; Esther 8:5"> If I have found favor in the sight of the king, and if it pleases the king to grant my petition and fulfill my request, may the king and Haman come tomorrow to the banquet I will prepare for them. Then I will answer the king’s question.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Haman’s Plot against Mordecai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 3:5; Matthew 2:16; Psalms 27:3"> That day Haman went out full of joy and glad of heart. At the king’s gate, however, he saw Mordecai, who did not rise or tremble in fear at his presence. And Haman was filled with rage toward Mordecai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 6:13; Ecclesiastes 7:9; 2 Samuel 13:22–23"> Nevertheless, Haman restrained himself and went home. And calling for his friends and his wife Zeresh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 9:7–10; Esther 3:1; Luke 12:19–20"> Haman recounted to them his glorious wealth, his many sons, and all the ways the king had honored and promoted him over the other officials and servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:5–8"> “What is more,” Haman added, “Queen Esther invited no one but me to join the king at the banquet she prepared, and I am invited back tomorrow along with the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:20; Job 18:4; Philippians 4:11–12"> Yet none of this satisfies me as long as I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king’s gate.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Psalms 37:14; 1 Kings 21:7"> His wife Zeresh and all his friends told him, “Have them build a gallows fifty cubits high,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="50 cubits is approximately 75 feet or 22.9 meters high.">a</a></sup>and ask the king in the morning to have Mordecai hanged on it. Then go to the banquet with the king and enjoy yourself.”<br />The advice pleased Haman, and he had the gallows constructed.</span>
</div>
</div>
