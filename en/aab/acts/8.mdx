<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Those who had been scattered preached the word wherever they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Philip went down to a city in Samaria and proclaimed the Christ to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> The crowds all paid close attention to Philip’s message and to the signs they saw him perform.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> With loud shrieks, unclean spirits came out of many who were possessed, and many of the paralyzed and lame were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> So there was great joy in that city.</span>

## Simon the Sorcerer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> Prior to that time, a man named Simon had practiced sorcery in the city and astounded the people of Samaria. He claimed to be someone great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> and all the people, from the least to the greatest, heeded his words and said, “This man is the divine power called the Great Power.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> They paid close attention to him because he had astounded them for a long time with his sorcery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed Philip as he preached the gospel of the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Even Simon himself believed and was baptized. He followed Philip closely and was astounded by the great signs and miracles he observed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> When the apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> On their arrival, they prayed for them to receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> For the Holy Spirit had not yet fallen upon any of them; they had simply been baptized into the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then Peter and John laid their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> When Simon saw that the Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles’ hands, he offered them money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> “Give me this power as well,” he said, “so that everyone on whom I lay my hands may receive the Holy Spirit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter replied, “May your silver perish with you, because you thought you could buy the gift of God with money!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> You have no part or share in our ministry, because your heart is not right before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent, therefore, of your wickedness, and pray to the Lord. Perhaps He will forgive you for the intent of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I see that you are poisoned by bitterness and captive to iniquity.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> Then Simon answered, “Pray to the Lord for me, so that nothing you have said may happen to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> And after Peter and John had testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, preaching the gospel in many of the Samaritan villages.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Philip and the Ethiopian

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Now an angel of the Lord said to Philip, “Get up and go south to the desert road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> So he started out, and on his way he met an Ethiopian eunuch, a court official in charge of the entire treasury of Candace,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek Kandakē">a</a></sup>queen of the Ethiopians. He had gone to Jerusalem to worship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> and on his return was sitting in his chariot reading Isaiah the prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> The Spirit said to Philip, “Go over to that chariot and stay by it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> So Philip ran up and heard the man reading Isaiah the prophet. “Do you understand what you are reading?” Philip asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> “How can I,” he said, “unless someone guides me?” And he invited Philip to come up and sit with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> The eunuch was reading this passage of Scripture:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He was led like a sheep to the slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and as a lamb before the shearer is silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so He did not open His mouth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">In His humiliation He was deprived of justice.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who can recount His descendants?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For His life was removed from the earth.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isaiah 53:7–8 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> “Tell me,” said the eunuch, “who is the prophet talking about, himself or someone else?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> Then Philip began with this very Scripture and told him the good news about Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> As they traveled along the road and came to some water, the eunuch said, “Look, here is water! What is there to prevent me from being baptized?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR includes 37 And Philip said, “If you believe with all your heart, you may be baptized.” The eunuch replied, “I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he gave orders to stop the chariot. Then both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord carried Philip away, and the eunuch saw him no more, but went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philip appeared at Azotus and traveled through that region, preaching the gospel in all the towns until he came to Caesarea.</span>
</div>
</div>
