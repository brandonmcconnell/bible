## Daniel’s Prayer for His People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:1; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 5:31–1"> In the first year of Darius son of Xerxes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Ahasuerus">a</a></sup>a Mede by descent, who was made ruler over the kingdom of the Chaldeans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">b</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> in the first year of his reign, I, Daniel, understood from the sacred books, according to the word of the LORD to Jeremiah the prophet, that the desolation of Jerusalem would last seventy years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Jeremiah 25:11–12 and Jeremiah 29:10.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:10–13; James 5:16–18"> So I turned my attention to the Lord God to seek Him by prayer and petition, with fasting, sackcloth, and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Exodus 20:6; Deuteronomy 5:10"> And I prayed to the LORD my God and confessed, “O, Lord, the great and awesome God, who keeps His covenant of loving devotion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">d</a></sup>to those who love Him and keep His commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:15"> we have sinned and done wrong. We have acted wickedly and rebelled. We have turned away from Your commandments and ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Luke 20:10–12"> We have not listened to Your servants the prophets, who spoke in Your name to our kings, leaders, fathers, and all the people of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:15; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> To You, O Lord, belongs righteousness, but this day we are covered with shame—the men of Judah, the people of Jerusalem, and all Israel near and far, in all the countries to which You have driven us because of our unfaithfulness to You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 9:6–7; Lamentations 1:7–8; Lamentations 1:18"> O LORD, we are covered with shame—our kings, our leaders, and our fathers—because we have sinned against You.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–8; Daniel 9:7; Daniel 9:5"> To the Lord our God belong compassion and forgiveness, even though we have rebelled against Him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:6; 2 Kings 18:12; Nehemiah 9:13–17"> and have not obeyed the voice of the LORD our God to walk in His laws, which He set before us through His servants the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5–10; Isaiah 1:4–6; Deuteronomy 28:15–68"> All Israel has transgressed Your law and turned away, refusing to obey Your voice; so the oath and the curse written in the Law of Moses the servant of God has been poured out on us, because we have sinned against You.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:9; Lamentations 1:12; Job 12:17"> You have carried out the words spoken against us and against our rulers by bringing upon us a great disaster. For under all of heaven, nothing has ever been done like what has been done to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Isaiah 9:13; Daniel 9:11"> Just as it is written in the Law of Moses, all this disaster has come upon us, yet we have not sought the favor of the LORD our God by turning from our iniquities and giving attention to Your truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:7; Nehemiah 9:33; Psalms 51:14"> Therefore the LORD has kept the calamity in store and brought it upon us. For the LORD our God is righteous in all He does; yet we have not obeyed His voice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 32:11; Nehemiah 1:10"> Now, O Lord our God, who brought Your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand, and who made for Yourself a name renowned to this day, we have sinned; we have acted wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Joel 3:17; Psalms 87:1–3"> O Lord, in keeping with all Your righteous acts, I pray that Your anger and wrath may turn away from Your city Jerusalem, Your holy mountain; for because of our sins and the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and Your people are a reproach to all around us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Lamentations 5:18; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> So now, our God, hear the prayers and petitions of Your servant. For Your sake, O Lord, cause Your face to shine upon Your desolate sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 37:17; Jeremiah 36:7; 2 Kings 19:16"> Incline Your ear, O my God, and hear; open Your eyes and see the desolation of the city that bears Your name. For we are not presenting our petitions before You because of our righteous acts, but because of Your great compassion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 9:18; Ephesians 1:12; Amos 7:2"> O Lord, listen! O Lord, forgive! O Lord, hear and act! For Your sake, O my God, do not delay, because Your city and Your people bear Your name.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gabriel’s Prophecy of the Seventy Weeks

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Daniel 9:3–4; Romans 3:23"> While I was speaking, praying, confessing my sin and that of my people Israel, and presenting my petition before the LORD my God concerning His holy mountain—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Daniel 8:16; 1 Kings 18:36"> while I was still praying, Gabriel, the man I had seen in the earlier vision, came to me in swift flight about the time of the evening sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:24–27; Daniel 10:21; Daniel 8:16"> He instructed me and spoke with me, saying: “O Daniel, I have come now to give you insight and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 10:11–12"> At the beginning of your petitions, an answer went out, and I have come to tell you, for you are highly precious. So consider the message and understand the vision:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:11–14; Romans 5:10; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Seventy weeks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or Seventy sevens; also twice in verse 25 and once in verse 26">e</a></sup>are decreed for your people and your holy city to stop their transgression, to put an end to sin, to effect purgation for iniquity, to bring in everlasting righteousness, to seal up vision and prophecy, and to anoint the Most Holy Place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or the Holy of Holies or the Most Holy One; literally the Most Holy">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 4:24; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 55:4"> Know and understand this: From the issuance of the decree to restore and rebuild Jerusalem until the Messiah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or the Anointed One; also in verse 26">g</a></sup>the Prince, there will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks. It will be rebuilt with streets and a trench, but in times of distress.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Nahum 1:8; Mark 13:2"> Then after the sixty-two weeks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew Then after sixty-two sevens">h</a></sup>the Messiah will be cut off and will have nothing.<br />Then the people of the prince who is to come will destroy the city and the sanctuary. The end will come like a flood, and until the end there will be war; desolations have been decreed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:15; Luke 21:20; Daniel 12:11"> And he will confirm a covenant with many for one week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or for one seven; similarly again in this verse">i</a></sup>but in the middle of the week he will put an end to sacrifice and offering. And on the wing of the temple will come the abomination that causes desolation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally And on the wing (will come) the abomination that causes desolation, or And on the wing of abominations (will come) one who causes desolation,">j</a></sup>until the decreed destruction is poured out upon him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally until the decreed consummation is poured out upon (him who) is desolate.">k</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>
