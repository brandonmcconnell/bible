## Daniel’s Vision of the Four Beasts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:17; Amos 3:7; Daniel 5:1"> In the first year of the reign of Belshazzar over Babylon, Daniel had a dream, and visions passed through his mind as he lay on his bed. He wrote down the dream, and this is the summary of his account.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:1; Revelation 17:15"> Daniel declared: “In my vision in the night I looked, and suddenly the four winds of heaven were churning up the great sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perhaps the Great Sea, that is, the Mediterranean Sea">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:17; Daniel 2:32–33"> Then four great beasts came up out of the sea, each one different from the others:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 14:13–17; Ezekiel 17:3"> The first beast was like a lion, and it had the wings of an eagle. I watched until its wings were torn off and it was lifted up from the ground and made to stand on two feet like a man and given the mind of a man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:39; Isaiah 13:17–18; Proverbs 17:12"> Suddenly another beast appeared, which looked like a bear. It was raised up on one of its sides, and it had three ribs in its mouth between its teeth. So it was told, ‘Get up and gorge yourself on flesh!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Daniel 8:20–22; Daniel 8:5–8"> Next, as I watched, suddenly another beast appeared. It was like a leopard, and on its back it had four wings like those of a bird. The beast also had four heads, and it was given authority to rule.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:12"> After this, as I watched in my vision in the night, suddenly a fourth beast appeared, and it was terrifying—dreadful and extremely strong—with large iron teeth. It devoured and crushed; then it trampled underfoot whatever was left. It was different from all the beasts before it, and it had ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; Revelation 13:1; Psalms 12:3"> While I was contemplating the horns, suddenly another horn, a little one, came up among them, and three of the first horns were uprooted before it. This horn had eyes like those of a man and a mouth that spoke words of arrogance.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel’s Vision of the Ancient of Days

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:14; Mark 9:3; Daniel 7:13"> As I continued to watch,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">thrones were set in place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the Ancient of Days took His seat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His clothing was white as snow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hair of His head was like pure wool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His throne was flaming with fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its wheels were all ablaze.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:27; Deuteronomy 33:2; Revelation 20:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">A river of fire was flowing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">coming out from His presence.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thousands upon thousands attended Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and myriads upon myriads stood before Him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The court was convened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the books were opened.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Revelation 20:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> Then I kept watching because of the arrogant words the horn was speaking. As I continued to watch, the beast was slain, and its body was destroyed and thrown into the blazing fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 7:4–6"> As for the rest of the beasts, their dominion was removed, but they were granted an extension of life for a season and a time.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel’s Vision of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Revelation 1:7; Luke 21:27"> In my vision in the night I continued to watch,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I saw One like the Son of Man</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or one like a son of man">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">coming with the clouds of heaven.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Matthew 24:30, Matthew 26:64, Mark 13:26, Mark 14:62, Luke 21:27, Revelation 1:13, and Revelation 14:14.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He approached the Ancient of Days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and was led into His presence.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27; John 3:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">And He was given dominion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">glory, and kingship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that the people of every nation and language</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">should serve Him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His dominion is an everlasting dominion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that will not pass away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and His kingdom is one</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that will never be destroyed.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel’s Visions Interpreted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Jeremiah 17:16; Genesis 40:7–8"> I, Daniel, was grieved in my spirit, and the visions in my mind alarmed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:13–14; Zechariah 2:3; Revelation 5:5"> I approached one of those who were standing there, and I asked him the true meaning of all this.<br />So he told me the interpretation of these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19–22; Daniel 2:37–40; John 18:36"> ‘These four great beasts are four kings who will arise from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Isaiah 60:12–14; Revelation 5:10"> But the holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom and possess it forever—yes, forever and ever.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 2:40–43"> Then I wanted to know the true meaning of the fourth beast, which was different from all the others—extremely terrifying—devouring and crushing with iron teeth and bronze claws, then trampling underfoot whatever was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 7:23; Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:11"> I also wanted to know about the ten horns on its head and the other horn that came up, before which three of them fell—the horn whose appearance was more imposing than the others, with eyes and with a mouth that spoke words of arrogance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 17:14; Daniel 8:24"> As I watched, this horn was waging war against the holy ones and prevailing against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4; Revelation 5:10"> until the Ancient of Days arrived and pronounced judgment in favor of the holy ones of the Most High, and the time came for them to possess the kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:40; Daniel 7:7; Luke 2:1"> This is what he said: ‘The fourth beast is a fourth kingdom that will appear on the earth, different from all the other kingdoms, and it will devour the whole earth, trample it down, and crush it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:20; Daniel 11:36; Revelation 13:1"> And the ten horns are ten kings who will rise from this kingdom. After them another king, different from the earlier ones, will rise and subdue three kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> He will speak out against the Most High and oppress the holy ones of the Most High, intending to change the appointed times and laws; and the holy ones will be given into his hand for a time, and times, and half a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 20:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 11:13"> But the court will convene, and his dominion will be taken away and completely destroyed forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 72:11; Luke 1:33; Revelation 11:15"> Then the sovereignty, dominion, and greatness of the kingdoms under all of heaven will be given to the people of the holy ones of the Most High.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Aram. le’am qaddishe elyonin, ‘to the people of the holy ones of the Most High.’ The holy ones of the Most High belong to God’s heavenly council (4:17; Ps 89:5-7), and with the Son of Man they receive the kingdom (vv. 13-14, 18, 22). Here it is given to the people who belong to them. In the New Testament the name passes to God’s people, who will judge the world and even angels (1 Cor 6:2-3); see note on Matt 27:52.">d</a></sup>His kingdom will be an everlasting kingdom, and all rulers will serve and obey Him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51; Daniel 10:8"> Thus ends the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts troubled me greatly, and my face turned pale. But I kept the matter to myself.”</span>
</div>
</div>
