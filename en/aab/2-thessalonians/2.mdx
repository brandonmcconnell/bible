## The Man of Lawlessness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to Him, we ask you, brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> not to be easily disconcerted or alarmed by any spirit or message or letter seeming to be from us, alleging that the Day of the Lord has already come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 John 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Let no one deceive you in any way, for it will not come until the rebellion occurs and the man of lawlessness—the son of destruction—is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> He will oppose and exalt himself above everything called a god or object of worship. So he will seat himself in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Do you not remember that I told you these things while I was still with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And now you know what now restrains, so that he may be revealed at the proper time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work, but he who now restrains it will continue until he is taken out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will slay with the breath of His mouth and annihilate by the majesty of His arrival.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> The coming of the lawless one will be accompanied by the working of Satan, with every kind of power, sign, and false wonder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> and with every wicked deception directed against those who are perishing, because they refused the love of the truth that would have saved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; John 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> For this reason God will send them a powerful delusion so that they believe the lie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> in order that judgment may come upon all who have disbelieved the truth and delighted in wickedness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Stand Firm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> But we should always thank God for you, brothers who are loved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or God has chosen you as the firstfruits">a</a></sup>to be saved by the sanctification of the Spirit and by faith in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> To this He called you through our gospel, so that you may share in the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> Therefore, brothers, stand firm and cling to the traditions we taught you, whether by speech or by letter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; John 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> Now may our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father, who by grace has loved us and given us eternal comfort and good hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good word and deed.</span>
</div>
</div>
