## Prophecy and Tongues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Earnestly pursue love and eagerly desire spiritual gifts, especially the gift of prophecy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> For he who speaks in a tongue does not speak to men, but to God. Indeed, no one understands him; he utters mysteries in the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> But he who prophesies speaks to men for their edification, encouragement, and comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> The one who speaks in a tongue edifies himself, but the one who prophesies edifies the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> I wish that all of you could speak in tongues, but I would rather have you prophesy. He who prophesies is greater than one who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets so that the church may be edified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> Now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I benefit you, unless I bring you some revelation or knowledge or prophecy or teaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> Even in the case of lifeless instruments, such as the flute or harp, how will anyone recognize the tune they are playing unless the notes are distinct?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> Again, if the trumpet sounds a muffled call, who will prepare for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> So it is with you. Unless you speak intelligible words with your tongue, how will anyone know what you are saying? You will just be speaking into the air.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Assuredly, there are many different languages in the world, yet none of them is without meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> If, then, I do not know the meaning of someone’s language, I am a foreigner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally a barbarian; twice in this verse">a</a></sup>to the speaker, and he is a foreigner to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> It is the same with you. Since you are eager to have spiritual gifts, strive to excel in gifts that build up the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> Therefore, the one who speaks in a tongue should pray that he may interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> What then shall I do? I will pray with my spirit, but I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit, but I will also sing with my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> Otherwise, if you speak a blessing in spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or in the Spirit">b</a></sup>how can someone who is uninstructed say “Amen” to your thanksgiving, since he does not know what you are saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> You may be giving thanks well enough, but the other one is not edified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I thank God that I speak in tongues more than all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> But in the church, I would rather speak five coherent words to instruct others than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brothers, stop thinking like children. In regard to evil be infants, but in your thinking be mature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> It is written in the Law:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“By strange tongues and foreign lips</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will speak to this people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but even then they will not listen to Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the Lord.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaiah 28:11–12">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> Tongues, then, are a sign, not for believers, but for unbelievers. Prophecy, however, is for believers, not for unbelievers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> So if the whole church comes together and everyone speaks in tongues, and some who are uninstructed or some unbelievers come in, will they not say that you are out of your minds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But if an unbeliever or uninstructed person comes in while everyone is prophesying, he will be convicted and called to account by all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> and the secrets of his heart will be made known. So he will fall facedown and worship God, proclaiming, “God is truly among you!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Orderly Worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> What then shall we say, brothers? When you come together, everyone has a psalm or a teaching, a revelation, a tongue, or an interpretation. All of these must be done to build up the church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> If anyone speaks in a tongue, two, or at most three, should speak in turn, and someone must interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there is no interpreter, he should remain silent in the church and speak only to himself and God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> Two or three prophets should speak, and the others should weigh carefully what is said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> And if a revelation comes to someone who is seated, the first speaker should stop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> For you can all prophesy in turn so that everyone may be instructed and encouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> The spirits of prophets are subject to prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> For God is not a God of disorder, but of peace—as in all the churches of the holy ones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or of peace. As in all the churches of the holy ones: Thus, some translators begin the new paragraph after peace.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> Women are to be silent in the churches. They are not permitted to speak, but must be in submission, as the law says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> If they wish to inquire about something, they are to ask their own husbands at home; for it is dishonorable for a woman to speak in the church.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts place verses 34–35 after verse 40.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Did the word of God originate with you? Or are you the only ones it has reached?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If anyone considers himself a prophet or spiritual person, let him acknowledge that what I am writing you is the Lord’s command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> But if anyone ignores this, he himself will be ignored.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR let him be ignorant">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> So, my brothers, be eager to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> But everything must be done in a proper and orderly manner.</span>
</div>
</div>
