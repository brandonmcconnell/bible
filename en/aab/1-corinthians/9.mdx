## The Rights of an Apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Are you yourselves not my workmanship in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> Even if I am not an apostle to others, surely I am to you. For you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> This is my defense to those who scrutinize me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Have we no right to food and to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Have we no right to take along a believing wife,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally take along a sister—a wife—">a</a></sup>as do the other apostles and the Lord’s brothers and Cephas?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Peter">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or are Barnabas and I the only apostles who must work for a living?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally Or is it only Barnabas and I who do not have authority not to work?">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who serves as a soldier at his own expense? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat of its fruit? Who tends a flock and does not drink of its milk?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> Do I say this from a human perspective? Doesn’t the Law say the same thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For it is written in the Law of Moses: “Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Deuteronomy 25:4">d</a></sup>Is it about oxen that God is concerned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> Isn’t He actually speaking on our behalf? Indeed, this was written for us, because when the plowman plows and the thresher threshes, they should also expect to share in the harvest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we have sown spiritual seed among you, is it too much for us to reap a material harvest from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others have this right to your support, shouldn’t we have it all the more? But we did not exercise this right. Instead, we put up with anything rather than hinder the gospel of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do you not know that those who work in the temple eat of its food, and those who serve at the altar partake of its offerings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> In the same way, the Lord has prescribed that those who preach the gospel should receive their living from the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have not used any of these rights. And I am not writing this to suggest that something be done for me. Indeed, I would rather die than let anyone nullify my boast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> Yet when I preach the gospel, I have no reason to boast, because I am obligated to preach. Woe to me if I do not preach the gospel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> If my preaching is voluntary, I have a reward. But if it is not voluntary, I am still entrusted with a responsibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> What then is my reward? That in preaching the gospel I may offer it free of charge, and so not use up my rights in preaching it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul the Servant to All

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> Though I am free of obligation to anyone, I make myself a slave to everyone, to win as many as possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> To the Jews I became like a Jew, to win the Jews. To those under the law I became like one under the law (though I myself am not under the law), to win those under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> To those without the law I became like one without the law (though I am not outside the law of God but am under the law of Christ), to win those without the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> To the weak I became weak, to win the weak. I have become all things to all people so that by all possible means I might save some.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> I do all this for the sake of the gospel, so that I may share in its blessings.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Run Your Race to Win

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Do you not know that in a race all the runners run, but only one receives the prize? Run in such a way as to take the prize.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Everyone who competes in the games trains with strict discipline. They do it for a crown that is perishable, but we do it for a crown that is imperishable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> Therefore I do not run aimlessly; I do not fight like I am beating the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> No, I discipline my body and make it my slave, so that after I have preached to others, I myself will not be disqualified.</span>
</div>
</div>
