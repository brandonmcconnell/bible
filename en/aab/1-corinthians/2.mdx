## Paul’s Message by the Spirit’s Power

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> When I came to you, brothers, I did not come with eloquence or wisdom as I proclaimed to you the testimony about God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> For I resolved to know nothing while I was with you except Jesus Christ and Him crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> I came to you in weakness and fear, and with much trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> My message and my preaching were not with persuasive words of wisdom, but with a demonstration of the Spirit’s power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> so that your faith would not rest on men’s wisdom, but on God’s power.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> Among the mature, however, we speak a message of wisdom—but not the wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are coming to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> No, we speak of the mysterious and hidden wisdom of God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or we speak God’s wisdom in a mystery">a</a></sup>which He destined for our glory before time began.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> None of the rulers of this age understood it. For if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; James 1:12"> Rather, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“No eye has seen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no ear has heard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no heart has imagined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">what God has prepared for those who love Him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isaiah 64:4">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> But God has revealed it to us by the Spirit.<br />The Spirit searches all things, even the deep things of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> For who among men knows the thoughts of man except his own spirit within him? So too, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> We have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, that we may understand what God has freely given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> And this is what we speak, not in words taught us by human wisdom, but in words taught by the Spirit, expressing spiritual truths in spiritual words.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or to spiritual people">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> The natural man does not accept the things that come from the Spirit of God. For they are foolishness to him, and he cannot understand them, because they are spiritually discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> The spiritual man judges all things, but he himself is not subject to anyone’s judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> “For who has known the mind of the Lord, so as to instruct Him?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Isaiah 40:13 (see also LXX)">d</a></sup>But we have the mind of Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>
