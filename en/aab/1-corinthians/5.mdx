## Immorality Rebuked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Revelation 2:21; Colossians 3:5"> It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and of a kind that is intolerable even among pagans: A man has his father’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 7:7–11; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> And you are proud! Shouldn’t you rather have been stricken with grief and have removed from your fellowship the man who did this?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 13:2"> Although I am absent from you in body, I am present with you in spirit, and I have already pronounced judgment on the one who did this, just as if I were present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:23; Colossians 3:17; Matthew 16:19"> When you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or In the name of our Lord Jesus, when you are assembled">a</a></sup>and I am with you in spirit, along with the power of the Lord Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15; Galatians 6:1–2"> hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved on the Day of the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR the Lord Jesus">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 15:33; James 4:16"> Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little leaven works through the whole batch of dough?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 4:22; Colossians 3:5–9"> Get rid of the old leaven, that you may be a new unleavened batch, as you really are. For Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="At the first Passover each household slew a lamb, put its blood on the doorposts, and ate the lamb together that night (Exod 12:7-8). Where the LORD saw the blood, He passed over that house, and the Destroyer did not enter to strike its firstborn (12:13, 23). The Destroyer is the LORD Himself, the Son before His incarnation (see notes on Exod 12:23 and 1 Cor 10:10). So Christ is both the Lamb whose blood marks His household and the LORD who passes over it. Before the feast every trace of leaven was cleared from the house (12:15), and Paul reads the church the same way: God’s household is to clear out the old leaven of malice and wickedness and keep the feast with sincerity and truth (v. 8).">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 12:15"> Therefore let us keep the feast, not with the old bread, leavened with malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and of truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Expel the Immoral Brother

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> I wrote you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; John 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> I was not including the sexually immoral of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters. In that case you would have to leave this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But now I am writing you not to associate with anyone who claims to be a brother but is sexually immoral or greedy, an idolater or a verbal abuser, a drunkard or a swindler. With such a man do not even eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> What business of mine is it to judge those outside the church? Are you not to judge those inside?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 21:21"> God will judge those outside. “Expel the wicked man from among you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally Expel the evil from among you; Deuteronomy 13:5, 17:7, 19:19, 21:21, 22:21, 22:24, and 24:7">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
