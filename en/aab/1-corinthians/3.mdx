## God’s Fellow Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> Brothers, I could not address you as spiritual, but as worldly—as infants in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> I gave you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet ready for solid food. In fact, you are still not ready,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> for you are still worldly. For since there is jealousy and dissension among you, are you not worldly? Are you not walking in the way of man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> For when one of you says, “I follow Paul,” and another, “I follow Apollos,” are you not mere men?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? They are servants through whom you believed, as the Lord has assigned to each his role.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I planted the seed and Apollos watered it, but God made it grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> So neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only God, who makes things grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> He who plants and he who waters are one in purpose,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally are one">a</a></sup>and each will be rewarded according to his own labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> For we are God’s fellow workers; you are God’s field, God’s building.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ Our Foundation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> By the grace God has given me, I laid a foundation as an expert builder, and someone else is building on it. But each one must be careful how he builds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> For no one can lay a foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> If anyone builds on this foundation using gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> his workmanship will be evident, because the Day will bring it to light. It will be revealed with fire, and the fire will prove the quality of each man’s work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> If what he has built survives, he will receive a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> If it is burned up, he will suffer loss. He himself will be saved, but only as if through the flames.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Temple and God’s Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Do you not know that you yourselves are God’s temple, and that God’s Spirit dwells in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or among">b</a></sup>you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him; for God’s temple is holy, and you are that temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Let no one deceive himself. If any of you thinks he is wise in this age, he should become a fool, so that he may become wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> For the wisdom of this world is foolishness in God’s sight. As it is written: “He catches the wise in their craftiness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Job 5:13">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> And again, “The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise are futile.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Psalms 94:11">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> Therefore, stop boasting in men. All things are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, Peter">e</a></sup>or the world or life or death or the present or the future. All of them belong to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.</span>
</div>
</div>
