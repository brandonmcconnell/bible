## Judgment on Israel’s Neighbors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 1 Kings 19:19"> These are the words of Amos, who was among the sheepherders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or sheep breeders">a</a></sup>of Tekoa—what he saw concerning Israel two years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or during the two years">b</a></sup>before the earthquake, in the days when Uzziah was king of Judah and Jeroboam son of Jehoash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew Joash, a variant of Jehoash">c</a></sup>was king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 3:16; Jeremiah 25:30; Amos 9:3"> He said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The LORD roars from Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and raises His voice from Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the pastures of the shepherds mourn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the summit of Carmel withers.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; Amos 1:9; Isaiah 8:4"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For three transgressions of Damascus, even four,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not revoke My judgment,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or I will not revoke the punishment; Hebrew I will not revoke it; also in verses 6, 9, 11, and 13">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they threshed Gilead</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with sledges of iron.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:27; 2 Kings 6:24; 2 Kings 13:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will send fire upon the house of Hazael</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to consume the citadels of Ben-hadad.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Amos 9:7; Jeremiah 51:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will break down the gates of Damascus;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will cut off the ruler</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or the inhabitants">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the Valley of Aven</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Aven means wickedness.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one who wields the scepter in Beth-eden.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The people of Aram will be exiled to Kir,”</span>says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:11; 2 Chronicles 28:18; Obadiah 1:11"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For three transgressions of Gaza, even four,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not revoke My judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they exiled a whole population,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">delivering them up to Edom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Jeremiah 47:1; Zephaniah 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will send fire upon the walls of Gaza,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to consume its citadels.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Isaiah 14:29–31; Jeremiah 47:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off the ruler of Ashdod</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the one who wields the scepter in Ashkelon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will turn My hand against Ekron,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the remnant of the Philistines will perish,”</span>says the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–14; Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For three transgressions of Tyre, even four,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not revoke My judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they delivered up a whole congregation of exiles to Edom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and broke a covenant of brotherhood.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 9:4; Amos 1:7; Amos 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will send fire upon the walls of Tyre</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to consume its citadels.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–14; 2 Chronicles 28:17; Malachi 1:4"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For three transgressions of Edom, even four,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not revoke My judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because he pursued his brother with the sword</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and stifled all compassion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his anger raged continually,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his fury flamed incessantly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7; Obadiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 49:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will send fire upon Teman</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to consume the citadels of Bozrah.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Ezekiel 25:2–7; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For three transgressions of the Ammonites, even four,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not revoke My judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to enlarge their territory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Amos 2:2; Deuteronomy 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will kindle a fire in the walls of Rabbah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to consume its citadels</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">amid war cries on the day of battle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a violent wind on the day of tempest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their king will go into exile</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Possibly Milcom will go into exile; Milcom, also called Molech, was god of the Ammonites; see Leviticus 18:21 and 1 Kings 11:7.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he and his princes together,”</span>says the LORD.</span>
</div>
