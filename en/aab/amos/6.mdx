<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; James 5:5; Luke 12:17–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those at ease in Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those secure on Mount Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the distinguished ones of the foremost nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to whom the house of Israel comes.</span></span>

## The Pride of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself—the LORD, the God of Hosts, has declared:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I abhor Jacob’s pride</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and detest his citadels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so I will deliver up the city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and everything in it.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalms 109:13"> And if there are ten men left in one house, they too will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And when the relative who is to burn the bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or to make a funeral fire to honor the dead">a</a></sup>picks them up to remove them from the house, he will call to one inside, “Is anyone else with you?”<br />“None,” that person will answer.<br />“Silence,” the relative will retort, “for the name of the LORD must not be invoked.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> For the LORD gives a command:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The great house will be smashed to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the small house to rubble.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Do horses gallop on the cliffs?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Does one plow the sea</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally Does one plow (there)">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with oxen?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you have turned justice into poison</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">you who rejoice in Lo-debar</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lo-debar means nothing.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Did we not take Karnaim</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Karnaim means horn, a symbol of strength.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by our own strength?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For behold, I will raise up a nation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against you, O house of Israel,”</span>declares the LORD, the God of Hosts,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“and they will oppress you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of the Arabah.”</span></span>
