## The Destruction of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 68:21; Revelation 1:17; Isaiah 6:1"> I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and He said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Strike the tops of the pillars</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that the thresholds shake.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Topple them on the heads of all the people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will kill the rest with the sword.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">None of those who flee will get away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">none of the fugitives will escape.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:53; Psalms 139:7–10; Obadiah 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though they dig down to Sheol,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from there My hand will take them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and though they climb up to heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from there I will pull them down.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Isaiah 27:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though they hide themselves atop Carmel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there I will track them and seize them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and though they hide from Me at the bottom of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there I will command the serpent</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew nachash; translated in most cases as snake">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to bite them.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:8; Psalms 46:6; Isaiah 64:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord GOD of Hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He who touches the earth and it melts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all its dwellers mourn—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the land rises like the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then sinks like the river of Egypt—</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely the eyes of the Lord GOD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are on the sinful kingdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will destroy it from the face of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yet I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Deuteronomy 28:64; Isaiah 30:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For surely I will give the command,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will shake the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">among all the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as grain is sifted in a sieve;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but not a pebble will reach the ground.</span></span>

## A Promise of Restoration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 15:15–17; Jeremiah 33:14–16; Isaiah 16:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“In that day I will restore</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fallen tent of David.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will repair its gaps, restore its ruins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rebuild it as in the days of old,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:7; Isaiah 65:1; Acts 15:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">that they may possess the remnant of Edom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the nations that bear My name,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX that the remnant of men, and all the nations that bear My name, may earnestly seek Me; cited in Acts 15:16–18">d</a></sup>declares the LORD, who will do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 3:18; John 4:35; Leviticus 26:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the days are coming,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“when the plowman will overtake the reaper</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the treader of grapes, the sower of seed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The mountains will drip with sweet wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with which all the hills will flow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Jeremiah 30:3; Jeremiah 30:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will restore My people Israel from captivity;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or restore the fortunes of My people Israel">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will rebuild and inhabit the ruined cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will plant vineyards and drink their wine;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will make gardens and eat their fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Ezekiel 37:25; Isaiah 60:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will firmly plant them in their own land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">never again to be uprooted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the land that I have given them,”</span>says the LORD your God.</span>
