## Jesus the True Vine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 27:2–3; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> “I am the true vine, and My Father is the keeper of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 12:10–11; Matthew 15:13"> He cuts off every branch in Me that bears no fruit, and every branch that does bear fruit, He prunes to make it even more fruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 17:17; Ephesians 5:26; 1 Peter 1:22"> You are already pruned because of the word I have spoken to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Galatians 2:20; John 8:31"> Remain in Me, and I will remain in you. Just as no branch can bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the vine, neither can you bear fruit unless you remain in Me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> I am the vine and you are the branches. The one who remains in Me, and I in him, will bear much fruit. For apart from Me you can do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> If anyone does not remain in Me, he is like a branch that is thrown away and withers. Such branches are gathered up, thrown into the fire, and burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> If you remain in Me and My words remain in you, ask whatever you wish, and it will be done for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:31; Matthew 5:16; Philippians 1:11"> This is to My Father’s glory, that you bear much fruit, proving yourselves to be My disciples.</span>
</div>
</div>

## No Greater Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:23–24; John 17:26; 1 John 2:28"> As the Father has loved Me, so have I loved you. Remain in My love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 3:21–24"> If you keep My commandments, you will remain in My love, just as I have kept My Father’s commandments and remain in His love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> I have told you these things so that My joy may be in you and your joy may be complete.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> This is My commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 10:11; Ephesians 5:2; Romans 5:6–8"> Greater love has no one than this, that he lay down his life for his friends.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> You are My friends if you do what I command you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 2:9–12; Matthew 13:11"> No longer do I call you servants, for a servant does not understand what his master is doing. But I have called you friends, because everything I have learned from My Father I have made known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> You did not choose Me, but I chose you. And I appointed you to go and bear fruit—fruit that will remain—so that whatever you ask the Father in My name, He will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> This is My command to you: Love one another.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Hatred of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> If the world hates you, understand that it hated Me first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> If you were of the world, it would love you as its own. Instead, the world hates you, because you are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Remember the word that I spoke to you: ‘No servant is greater than his master.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="John 13:16">a</a></sup>If they persecuted Me, they will persecute you as well; if they kept My word, they will keep yours as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> But they will treat you like this because of My name, since they do not know the One who sent Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not be guilty of sin. Now, however, they have no excuse for their sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> Whoever hates Me hates My Father as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:36; John 14:9; John 7:31"> If I had not done among them the works that no one else did, they would not be guilty of sin; but now they have seen and hated both Me and My Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 109:3; Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:19"> But this is to fulfill what is written in their Law: ‘They hated Me without reason.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Psalms 35:19, Psalms 38:19, and Psalms 69:4.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 16:13–15"> When the Advocate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or Comforter or Helper or Counselor; Greek Paraclete">c</a></sup>comes, whom I will send to you from the Father—the Spirit of truth who proceeds from the Father—He will testify about Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> And you also must testify, because you have been with Me from the beginning.</span>
</div>
</div>
