## The Wedding at Cana

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> On the third day a wedding took place at Cana in Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> and Jesus and His disciples had also been invited to the wedding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> When the wine ran out, Jesus’ mother said to Him, “They have no more wine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> “Woman, what is that to you and to Me?” Jesus replied. “My hour has not yet come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother said to the servants, “Do whatever He tells you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Now six stone water jars had been set there for the Jewish rites of purification. Each could hold from twenty to thirty gallons.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek two or three metretae; that is, approximately 20.8 to 31.2 gallons (78.8 to 118.1 liters)">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus told the servants, “Fill the jars with water.”<br />So they filled them to the brim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> “Now draw some out,” He said, “and take it to the master of the banquet.”<br />They did so,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> and the master of the banquet tasted the water that had been turned into wine. He did not know where it was from, but the servants who had drawn the water knew. Then he called the bridegroom aside</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and said, “Everyone serves the fine wine first, and then the cheap wine after the guests are drunk. But you have saved the fine wine until now!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> Jesus performed this, the first of His signs, at Cana in Galilee. He thus revealed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this, He went down to Capernaum with His mother and brothers and His disciples, and they stayed there a few days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> When the Jewish Passover was near, Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> In the temple courts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally the temple; also in verse 15">b</a></sup>He found men selling cattle, sheep, and doves, and money changers seated at their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> So He made a whip out of cords and drove all from the temple courts, both sheep and cattle. He poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> To those selling doves He said, “Get these out of here! How dare you turn My Father’s house into a marketplace!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> His disciples remembered that it is written: “Zeal for Your house will consume Me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 69:9">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> On account of this, the Jews demanded, “What sign can You show us to prove Your authority to do these things?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> “This temple took forty-six years to build,” the Jews replied, “and You are going to raise it up in three days?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But Jesus was speaking about the temple of His body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> After He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had said this. Then they believed the Scripture and the word that Jesus had spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> While He was in Jerusalem at the Passover Feast, many people saw the signs He was doing and believed in His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus did not entrust Himself to them, for He knew them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> He did not need any testimony about man, for He knew what was in a man.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
