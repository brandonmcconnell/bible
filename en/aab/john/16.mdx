<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Matthew 13:21; Matthew 26:31–33"> “I have told you these things so that you will not fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:22; Acts 26:9–11; Romans 10:2–3"> They will put you out of the synagogues. In fact, a time is coming when anyone who kills you will think he is offering a service to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:21; John 17:25; John 8:55"> They will do these things because they have not known the Father or Me.</span>

</div>

## The Promise of the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> Now, however, I am going to Him who sent Me; yet none of you asks Me, ‘Where are You going?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> Instead, your hearts are filled with sorrow because I have told you these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> But I tell you the truth, it is for your benefit that I am going away. Unless I go away, the Advocate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Comforter or Helper or Counselor; Greek Paraclete">a</a></sup>will not come to you; but if I go, I will send Him to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> And when He comes, He will convict the world in regard to sin and righteousness and judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> in regard to sin, because they do not believe in Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> in regard to righteousness, because I am going to the Father and you will no longer see Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> and in regard to judgment, because the prince of this world has been condemned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> I still have much to tell you, but you cannot yet bear to hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> However, when the Spirit of truth comes, He will guide you into all truth. For He will not speak on His own, but He will speak what He hears, and He will declare to you what is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He will glorify Me by taking from what is Mine and disclosing it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> Everything that belongs to the Father is Mine. That is why I said that the Spirit will take from what is Mine and disclose it to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> In a little while you will see Me no more, and then after a little while you will see Me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="BYZ and TR include because I go away to the Father.">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Grief Will Turn to Joy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> Then some of His disciples asked one another, “Why is He telling us, ‘In a little while you will not see Me, and then after a little while you will see Me’ and ‘Because I am going to the Father’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> They kept asking, “Why is He saying, ‘a little while’? We do not understand what He is saying.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> Aware that they wanted to question Him, Jesus said to them, “Are you asking one another why I said, ‘In a little while you will not see Me, and then after a little while you will see Me’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 126:5–6"> Truly, truly, I tell you, you will weep and wail while the world rejoices. You will grieve, but your grief will turn to joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> A woman has pain in childbirth because her time has come; but when she brings forth her child, she forgets her anguish because of her joy that a child has been born into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> So also you have sorrow now, but I will see you again and your hearts will rejoice, and no one will take away your joy.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ask in My Name

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> In that day you will no longer ask Me anything. Truly, truly, I tell you, whatever you ask the Father in My name, He will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> Until now you have not asked for anything in My name. Ask and you will receive, so that your joy may be complete.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:12; Mark 4:13; Matthew 13:34–35"> I have spoken these things to you in figures of speech. An hour is coming when I will no longer speak to you this way, but will tell you plainly about the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> In that day you will ask in My name. I am not saying that I will ask the Father on your behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:23; John 17:23"> For the Father Himself loves you, because you have loved Me and have believed that I came from God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="WH from the Father">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:1; John 13:3; John 8:14"> I came from the Father and entered the world. In turn, I will leave the world and go to the Father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 16:25"> His disciples said, “See, now You are speaking plainly and without figures of speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:17; John 17:8; John 16:17"> Now we understand that You know all things and that You have no need for anyone to question You. Because of this, we believe that You came from God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> “Do you finally believe?” Jesus replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> “Look, an hour is coming and has already come when you will be scattered, each to his own home, and you will leave Me all alone. Yet I am not alone, because the Father is with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> I have told you these things so that in Me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take courage; I have overcome the world!”</span>
</div>
</div>
