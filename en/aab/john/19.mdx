## The Soldiers Mock Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then Pilate took Jesus and had Him flogged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> The soldiers twisted together a crown of thorns, set it on His head, and dressed Him in a purple robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> And they went up to Him again and again, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!” and slapping Him in the face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Once again Pilate came out and said to the Jews, “Look, I am bringing Him out to you to let you know that I find no basis for a charge against Him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> When Jesus came out wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe, Pilate said to them, “Here is the man!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> As soon as the chief priests and officers saw Him, they shouted, “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!”<br />“You take Him and crucify Him,” Pilate replied, “for I find no basis for a charge against Him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> “We have a law,” answered the Jews, “and according to that law He must die, because He declared Himself to be the Son of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When Pilate heard this statement, he was even more afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and he went back into the Praetorium. “Where are You from?” he asked.<br />But Jesus gave no answer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> So Pilate said to Him, “Do You refuse to speak to me? Do You not know that I have authority to release You and authority to crucify You?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus answered, “You would have no authority over Me if it were not given to you from above. Therefore the one who handed Me over to you is guilty of greater sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> From then on, Pilate tried to release Him, but the Jews kept shouting, “If you release this man, you are no friend of Caesar. Anyone who declares himself a king is defying Caesar.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> When Pilate heard these words, he brought Jesus out and sat on the judgment seat at a place called the Stone Pavement, which in Hebrew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in Aramaic; also in verses 17 and 20">a</a></sup>is Gabbatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> It was the day of Preparation for the Passover, about the sixth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, about noon">b</a></sup>And Pilate said to the Jews, “Here is your King!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> At this, they shouted, “Away with Him! Away with Him! Crucify Him!”<br />“Shall I crucify your King?” Pilate asked.<br />“We have no king but Caesar,” replied the chief priests.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> Then Pilate handed Jesus over to be crucified, and the soldiers took Him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> Carrying His own cross, He went out to The Place of the Skull, which in Hebrew is called Golgotha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> There they crucified Him, and with Him two others, one on each side, with Jesus in the middle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> Pilate also had a notice posted on the cross. It read:<br />JESUS OF NAZARETH,<br />THE KING OF THE JEWS.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> Many of the Jews read this sign, because the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city, and it was written in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> So the chief priests of the Jews said to Pilate, “Do not write, ‘The King of the Jews,’ but only that He said, ‘I am the King of the Jews.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> When the soldiers had crucified Jesus, they divided His garments into four parts, one for each soldier, with the tunic remaining. It was seamless, woven in one piece from top to bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> So they said to one another, “Let us not tear it. Instead, let us cast lots to see who will get it.” This was to fulfill the Scripture:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They divided My garments among them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cast lots for My clothing.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 22:18">c</a></sup><br />So that is what the soldiers did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> Near the cross of Jesus stood His mother and her sister, as well as Mary the wife of Clopas and Mary Magdalene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> When Jesus saw His mother and the disciple whom He loved standing nearby, He said to His mother, “Woman, here is your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then He said to the disciple, “Here is your mother.” So from that hour, this disciple took her into his home.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this, knowing that everything had now been accomplished, and to fulfill the Scripture, Jesus said, “I am thirsty.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Psalms 22:15.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> A jar of sour wine<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or A jar of wine vinegar; similarly in verse 30">e</a></sup>was sitting there. So they soaked a sponge in the wine, put it on a stalk of hyssop, and lifted it to His mouth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Psalms 69:21.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> When Jesus had received the sour wine, He said, “It is finished.” And bowing His head, He yielded up His spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Side Is Pierced

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> It was the day of Preparation, and the next day was a High Sabbath. In order that the bodies would not remain on the cross during the Sabbath, the Jews asked Pilate to have the legs broken and the bodies removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> So the soldiers came and broke the legs of the first man who had been crucified with Jesus, and those of the other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But when they came to Jesus and saw that He was already dead, they did not break His legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> Instead, one of the soldiers pierced His side with a spear, and immediately blood and water flowed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> The one who saw it has testified to this, and his testimony is true. He knows that he is telling the truth, so that you also may believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> Now these things happened so that the Scripture would be fulfilled: “Not one of His bones will be broken.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Psalms 34:20; see also Exodus 12:46 and Numbers 9:12.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> And, as another Scripture says: “They will look on the One they have pierced.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Zechariah 12:10">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Burial of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> Afterward, Joseph of Arimathea, who was a disciple of Jesus (but secretly for fear of the Jews), asked Pilate to let him remove the body of Jesus. Pilate gave him permission, so he came and removed His body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> Nicodemus, who had previously come to Jesus at night, also brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about seventy-five pounds.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Greek about a hundred litras; that is, approximately 34 kilograms">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> So they took the body of Jesus and wrapped it in linen cloths with the spices, according to the Jewish burial custom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> Now there was a garden in the place where Jesus was crucified, and in the garden a new tomb in which no one had yet been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> And because it was the Jewish day of Preparation and the tomb was nearby, they placed Jesus there.</span>
</div>
</div>
