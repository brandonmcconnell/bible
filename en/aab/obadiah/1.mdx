## The Destruction of Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Joel 3:19; Ezekiel 35:3–15"> This is the vision of Obadiah:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">This is what the Lord GOD says about Edom—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We have heard a message from the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an envoy has been sent among the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to say, “Rise up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let us go to battle against her!”—</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:18; Isaiah 23:9; Micah 7:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I will make you small among the nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be deeply despised.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Proverbs 29:23; Jeremiah 49:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The pride of your heart has deceived you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O dwellers in the clefts of the rocks</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in the retreats of Sela">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose habitation is the heights,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who say in your heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Who can bring me down to the ground?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:2; Job 20:6–7; Habakkuk 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though you soar like the eagle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and make your nest among the stars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even from there I will bring you down,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Deuteronomy 24:21; Isaiah 17:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“If thieves came to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if robbers by night—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">oh, how you will be ruined—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">would they not steal only what they wanted?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If grape gatherers came to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">would they not leave some gleanings?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:10; Psalms 139:1; Isaiah 45:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">But how Esau will be pillaged,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his hidden treasures sought out!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Jeremiah 49:7; Jeremiah 30:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the men allied with you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will drive you to the border;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the men at peace with you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will deceive and overpower you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who eat your bread</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew does not include Those who eat.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will set a trap for you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">without your awareness of it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Job 5:12–14; Psalms 33:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">In that day, declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will I not destroy the wise men of Edom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the men of understanding</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the mountains of Esau?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Amos 1:12; Isaiah 63:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then your mighty men, O Teman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be terrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that everyone in the mountains of Esau</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be cut down in the slaughter.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Malachi 1:3–4; Genesis 27:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of the violence against your brother Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be covered with shame</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cut off forever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Nahum 3:10; Joel 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">On the day you stood aloof</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while strangers carried off his wealth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and foreigners entered his gate</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cast lots for Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you were just like one of them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Micah 4:11; Proverbs 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you should not gloat in that day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your brother’s day of misfortune,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor rejoice over the people of Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their destruction,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor boast proudly</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew nor enlarge your mouth">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their distress.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 35:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You should not enter the gate of My people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their disaster,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor gloat over their affliction</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their disaster,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor loot their wealth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their disaster.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:6; Psalms 31:8; Obadiah 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nor should you stand at the crossroads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to cut off their fugitives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor deliver up their survivors</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their distress.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Deliverance of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 2:13; Habakkuk 2:8; Ezekiel 30:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Day of the LORD is near</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As you have done, it will be done to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your recompense will return upon your own head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jeremiah 49:12; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For as you drank on My holy mountain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so all the nations will drink continually.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will drink and gulp it down;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be as if they had never existed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But on Mount Zion there will be deliverance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it will be holy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the house of Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will reclaim their possession.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Isaiah 10:17; Obadiah 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the house of Jacob will be a blazing fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the house of Joseph a burning flame;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the house of Esau will be stubble—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Jacob will set it ablaze and consume it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore no survivor will remain</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the house of Esau.”</span>For the LORD has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Amos 9:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those from the Negev will possess the mountains of Esau;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those from the foothills</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will possess the land of the Philistines.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will occupy the fields of Ephraim and Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Benjamin will possess Gilead.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–10; Luke 4:26; Jeremiah 32:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the exiles of this host of the Israelites</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the exiles from Jerusalem who are in Sepharad</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will possess the cities of the Negev.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; James 5:20; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The deliverers will ascend</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or The deliverers will go up from; LXX Those being delivered will go up">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Mount Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to rule over the mountains of Esau.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the kingdom will belong to the LORD.</span></span>
</div>
