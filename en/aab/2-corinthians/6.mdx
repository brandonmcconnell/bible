## Paul’s Hardships and God’s Grace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; Hebrews 12:15; 1 Peter 4:10"> As God’s fellow workers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally Now working together">a</a></sup>then, we urge you not to receive God’s grace in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:8; Hebrews 4:7; Hebrews 3:13"> For He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“In the time of favor I heard you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in the day of salvation I helped you.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Isaiah 49:8 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup><br />Behold, now is the time of favor; now is the day of salvation!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> We put no obstacle in anyone’s way, so that no one can discredit our ministry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> Rather, as servants of God we commend ourselves in every way: in great endurance; in troubles, hardships, and calamities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> in beatings, imprisonments, and riots; in labor, sleepless nights, and hunger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> in purity, knowledge, patience, and kindness; in the Holy Spirit and in sincere love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> in truthful speech and in the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness in the right hand and in the left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> through glory and dishonor, slander and praise; viewed as imposters, yet genuine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> unknown, yet well-known; dying, and yet we live on; punished, yet not killed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; poor, yet making many rich; having nothing, and yet possessing everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> We have spoken freely to you, Corinthians. Our hearts are open wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> It is not our affection, but yours, that is restrained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> As a fair exchange—I speak as to my children—open wide your hearts also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Be Unequally Yoked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership can righteousness have with wickedness? Or what fellowship does light have with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> What harmony is there between Christ and Belial?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Scrivener’s TR and GOC; many Greek sources Beliar">c</a></sup>Or what does a believer have in common with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> What agreement can exist between the temple of God and idols? For we are the temple of the living God. As God has said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will dwell with them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and walk among them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be My people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Therefore come out from among them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be separate, says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Touch no unclean thing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will receive you.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Isaiah 52:11; see also Ezekiel 20:34, including LXX.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will be a Father to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will be My sons and daughters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the Lord Almighty.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 2 Samuel 7:14.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
