## Generosity Commended

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> Now, brothers, we want you to know about the grace that God has given the churches of Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> In the terrible ordeal they suffered, their abundant joy and deep poverty overflowed into rich generosity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For I testify that they gave according to their ability and even beyond it. Of their own accord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> they earnestly pleaded with us for the privilege of sharing in this service to the holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> And not only did they do as we expected, but they gave themselves first to the Lord and then to us, through the will of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> So we urged Titus to help complete your act of grace, just as he had started it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> But just as you excel in everything—in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in complete earnestness, and in the love we inspired in you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in your love for us">a</a></sup>—see that you also excel in this grace of giving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> I am not giving a command, but I am testing the sincerity of your love through the earnestness of others.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sake He became poor, so that you through His poverty might become rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> And this is my opinion about what is helpful for you in this matter: Last year you were the first not only to give, but even to have such a desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> Now finish the work, so that you may complete it with the same eager desire, according to your means.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> For if the eagerness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what he does not have.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> It is not our intention that others may be relieved while you are burdened, but that there may be equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> At the present time, your surplus will meet their need, so that in turn their surplus will meet your need. This way there will be equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> As it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He who gathered much had no excess,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he who gathered little had no shortfall.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Exodus 16:18">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Titus Commended

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> But thanks be to God, who put into the heart of Titus the same devotion I have for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> For not only did he welcome our appeal, but he is eagerly coming to you of his own volition.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> Along with Titus we are sending the brother who is praised by all the churches for his work in the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> More than that, this brother was chosen by the churches to accompany us with the gracious offering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1 Corinthians 16:3–4.">c</a></sup>we administer to honor the Lord Himself and to show our eagerness to help.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> We want to avoid any criticism of the way we administer this generous gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> For we are taking great care to do what is right, not only in the eyes of the Lord, but also in the eyes of men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> And we are sending along with them our brother who has proven his earnestness to us many times and in many ways, and now even more so by his great confidence in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> As for Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker among you. As for our brothers, they are messengers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or apostles">d</a></sup>of the churches, the glory of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> In full view of the churches, then, show these men the proof of your love and the reason for our boasting about you.</span>
</div>
</div>
