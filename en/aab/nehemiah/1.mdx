## Nehemiah’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Zechariah 7:1; Esther 1:2"> These are the words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah:<br />In the month of Chislev,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Chislev is the ninth month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of November and December.">a</a></sup>in the twentieth year, while I was in the citadel of Susa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Ezekiel 6:9; Jeremiah 44:14"> Hanani, one of my brothers, arrived with men from Judah. So I questioned them about the remnant of the Jews who had survived the exile, and also about Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 Kings 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> And they told me, “The remnant who survived the exile are there in the province, in great trouble and disgrace. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Psalms 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> When I heard these words, I sat down and wept. I mourned for days, fasting and praying before the God of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; Daniel 9:4–19; Deuteronomy 7:21"> Then I said:<br />“O LORD, God of heaven, the great and awesome God who keeps His covenant of loving devotion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">b</a></sup>with those who love Him and keep His commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Daniel 9:4; Psalms 34:15"> let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to hear the prayer that I, Your servant, now pray before You day and night for Your servants, the Israelites.<br />I confess the sins that we Israelites have committed against You. Both I and my father’s house have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 9:5–6; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:13"> We have behaved corruptly against You and have not kept the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that You gave Your servant Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Deuteronomy 4:25–27; Leviticus 26:33–46"> Remember, I pray, the word that You commanded Your servant Moses when You said, ‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11–14; Deuteronomy 30:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> but if you return to Me and keep and practice My commandments, then even if your exiles have been banished to the farthest horizon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or to the extremity of the heavens">c</a></sup>I will gather them from there and bring them to the place I have chosen as a dwelling for My Name.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Deuteronomy 30:1–4">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:29; Daniel 9:15–27; Exodus 32:11"> They are Your servants and Your people. You redeemed them by Your great power and mighty hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Proverbs 21:1"> O Lord, may Your ear be attentive to my prayer and to the prayers of Your servants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally to the prayer of Your servant and to the prayer of Your servants">e</a></sup>who delight to revere Your name. Give Your servant success this day, I pray, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man.”<br />(At that time I was the cupbearer to the king.)</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
