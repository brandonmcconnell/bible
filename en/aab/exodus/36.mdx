## The People Bring More than Enough

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> “So Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person are to carry out everything commanded by the LORD, who has given them skill and ability to know how to perform all the work of constructing the sanctuary.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> Then Moses summoned Bezalel, Oholiab, and every skilled person whom the LORD had gifted—everyone whose heart stirred him to come and do the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> They received from Moses all the contributions that the Israelites had brought to carry out the service of constructing the sanctuary.<br />Meanwhile, the people continued to bring freewill offerings morning after morning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> so that all the skilled craftsmen who were doing all the work on the sanctuary left their work</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> and said to Moses, “The people are bringing more than enough for doing the work the LORD has commanded us to do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> After Moses had given an order, they sent a proclamation throughout the camp: “No man or woman should make anything else as an offering for the sanctuary.” So the people were restrained from bringing more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> since what they already had was more than enough to perform all the work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ten Curtains for the Tabernacle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> All the skilled craftsmen among the workmen made the ten curtains for the tabernacle. They were made of finely spun linen, as well as blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, with cherubim skillfully worked into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Each curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Each of the ten curtains was approximately 42 feet long and 6 feet wide (12.8 meters long and 1.8 meters wide).">a</a></sup>all the curtains were the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he joined five of the curtains together, and the other five he joined as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> He made loops of blue material on the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and also on the end curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> He made fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the end curtain of the second set, so that the loops lined up opposite one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> He also made fifty gold clasps to join the curtains together, so that the tabernacle was a unit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Eleven Curtains of Goat Hair

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> He then made curtains of goat hair for the tent over the tabernacle—eleven curtains in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Each of the eleven curtains was the same size—thirty cubits long and four cubits wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Each of the eleven curtains was approximately 45 feet long and 6 feet wide (13.7 meters long and 1.8 meters wide).">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He joined five of the curtains into one set and the other six into another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He made fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and fifty loops along the edge of the corresponding curtain in the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He also made fifty bronze clasps to join the tent together as a unit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> Additionally, he made for the tent a covering of ram skins dyed red, and over that a covering of fine leather.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Possibly the hides of large aquatic mammals">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Frames and Bases

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> Next, he constructed upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Each frame was ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Each frame was approximately 15 feet long and 2.25 feet wide (4.6 meters long and 68.6 centimeters wide).">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Two tenons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, projecting pieces of wood made for insertion into another piece; similarly in verse 24.">e</a></sup>were connected to each other for each frame. He made all the frames of the tabernacle in this way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He constructed twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> with forty silver bases to put under the twenty frames—two bases for each frame, one under each tenon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, he made twenty frames</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and forty silver bases—two bases under each frame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> He made six frames for the rear of the tabernacle, the west side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and two frames for the two back corners of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> coupled together from bottom to top and fitted into a single ring. He made both corners in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> So there were eight frames and sixteen silver bases—two under each frame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> He also made five crossbars of acacia wood for the frames on one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> five for those on the other side, and five for those on the rear side of the tabernacle, to the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He made the central crossbar to run through the center of the frames, from one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he overlaid the frames with gold and made gold rings to hold the crossbars. He also overlaid the crossbars with gold.</span>
</div>
</div>
