## Garments for the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:1–5; Numbers 17:2–9"> “Next, have your brother Aaron brought to you from among the Israelites, along with his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar, to serve Me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 31:10; Revelation 19:8"> Make holy garments for your brother Aaron, to give him glory and splendor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11; Isaiah 11:2"> You are to instruct all the skilled craftsmen, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, to make garments for Aaron’s consecration, so that he may serve Me as priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Revelation 9:17; Isaiah 59:17"> These are the garments that they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a woven tunic, a turban, and a sash. They are to make these holy garments for your brother Aaron and his sons, so that they may serve Me as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:3–4; Exodus 39:2–3"> They shall use gold, along with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine linen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ephod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:2–7; Exodus 26:1"> They are to make the ephod of finely spun linen embroidered with gold, and with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 39:4"> It shall have two shoulder pieces attached at two of its corners, so it can be fastened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:27–28; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:5"> And the skillfully woven waistband of the ephod must be of one piece, of the same workmanship—with gold, with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and with finely spun linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Chronicles 2:7; Isaiah 49:16"> Take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–4; Genesis 43:33"> six of their names on one stone and the remaining six on the other, in the order of their birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; Revelation 7:2; Jeremiah 22:24"> Engrave the names of the sons of Israel on the two stones the way a gem cutter engraves a seal. Then mount the stones in gold filigree settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:29; Joshua 4:7; Exodus 39:6–7"> Fasten both stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as memorial stones for the sons of Israel. Aaron is to bear their names on his two shoulders as a memorial before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Fashion gold filigree settings</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:17–18; 1 Kings 7:17"> and two chains of pure gold, made of braided cord work; and attach these chains to the settings.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Breastpiece

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:30; Exodus 26:1; Exodus 28:4"> You are also to make a breastpiece of judgment with the same workmanship as the ephod. Construct it with gold, with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and with finely spun linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It must be square when folded over double, a span long and a span wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The breastpiece, when folded over, was approximately 9 inches or 22.9 centimeters in both length and width.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Exodus 39:10–21; Proverbs 8:11"> And mount on it a setting of gemstones, four rows of stones:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The precise identification of some of these gemstones is uncertain.">b</a></sup><br />In the first row there shall be a ruby, a topaz, and an emerald;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Job 28:6; Song of Solomon 5:14"> in the second row a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:12; Exodus 39:12"> in the third row a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Revelation 4:3; Daniel 10:6"> and in the fourth row a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper.<br />Mount these stones in gold filigree settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:12; Revelation 7:4–8; 1 Kings 18:31"> The twelve stones are to correspond to the names of the sons of Israel, each engraved like a seal with the name of one of the twelve tribes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For the breastpiece, make braided chains like cords of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–15"> You are also to make two gold rings and fasten them to the two corners of the breastpiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then fasten the two gold chains to the two gold rings at the corners of the breastpiece,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:4; Exodus 28:14"> and fasten the other ends of the two chains to the two filigree settings, attaching them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Make two more gold rings and attach them to the other two corners of the breastpiece, on the inside edge next to the ephod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Make two additional gold rings and attach them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, on its front, near its seam just above its woven waistband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:38; Exodus 28:31; Exodus 39:30–31"> The rings of the breastpiece shall be tied to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue yarn, so that the breastpiece is above the waistband of the ephod and does not swing out from the ephod.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:30; Jeremiah 30:21"> Whenever Aaron enters the Holy Place, he shall bear the names of the sons of Israel over his heart on the breastpiece of judgment, as a continual reminder before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Ezra 2:63; Numbers 27:21"> And place the Urim and Thummim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally Lights and Perfections">c</a></sup>in the breastpiece of judgment, so that they will also be over Aaron’s heart whenever he comes before the LORD. Aaron will continually carry the judgment of the sons of Israel over his heart before the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Additional Priestly Garments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 39:22–26"> You are to make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:16; John 19:23–24; Exodus 39:28"> with an opening at its top in the center. Around the opening shall be a woven collar with an opening like that of a garment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The meaning of the Hebrew word is uncertain; possibly a coat of mail.">d</a></sup>so that it will not tear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 39:24–26; Zechariah 14:20; 1 Kings 7:18"> Make pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn all the way around the lower hem, with gold bells between them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; John 15:4–8; Colossians 1:10"> alternating the gold bells and pomegranates around the lower hem of the robe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Leviticus 16:2"> Aaron must wear the robe whenever he ministers, and its sound will be heard when he enters or exits the sanctuary before the LORD, so that he will not die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 14:20; Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 39:30"> You are to make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it as on a seal:<br />HOLY TO THE LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the LORD">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:6; Exodus 28:4; Zechariah 3:5"> Fasten to it a blue cord to mount it on the turban; it shall be on the front of the turban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 10:17"> And it will be worn on Aaron’s forehead, so that he may bear the iniquity<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Heb. nasa’ ‘awon: Aaron carries responsibility for the consecrated gifts as the steward of the sanctuary; cf. Lev 10:17; Num 18:1">f</a></sup>of the holy things that the sons of Israel consecrate with regard to all their holy gifts. It shall always be on his forehead, so that they may be acceptable before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 45:14"> You are to weave the tunic with fine linen, make the turban of fine linen, and fashion an embroidered sash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 39:41; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:2"> Make tunics, sashes, and headbands for Aaron’s sons, to give them glory and splendor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:1–36; Exodus 40:15"> After you put these garments on your brother Aaron and his sons, anoint them, ordain them, and consecrate them so that they may serve Me as priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; Leviticus 6:10; Leviticus 16:4"> Make linen undergarments to cover their bare flesh, extending from waist to thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 20:26; Leviticus 5:17"> Aaron and his sons must wear them whenever they enter the Tent of Meeting or approach the altar to minister in the Holy Place, so that they will not incur guilt and die. This is to be a permanent statute for Aaron and his descendants.</span>
</div>
</div>
