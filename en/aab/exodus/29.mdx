## Consecration of the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Malachi 1:13–14; Exodus 12:5"> “Now this is what you are to do to consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests: Take a young bull and two rams without blemish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:15; Leviticus 2:15; Leviticus 7:12"> along with unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil. Make them out of fine wheat flour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 6:17; Leviticus 8:26; Leviticus 8:2"> put them in a basket, and present them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Hebrews 10:22; Exodus 26:36"> Then present Aaron and his sons at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:2–8; Leviticus 8:7–8"> Take the garments and clothe Aaron with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod itself, and the breastplate. Fasten the ephod on him with its woven waistband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 28:36–39"> Put the turban on his head and attach the holy diadem to the turban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Leviticus 21:10; John 3:34"> Then take the anointing oil and anoint him by pouring it on his head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13; Exodus 28:40"> Present his sons as well and clothe them with tunics.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Leviticus 8:22–28; Deuteronomy 18:5"> Wrap the sashes around Aaron and his sons and tie headbands on them. The priesthood shall be theirs by a permanent statute. In this way you are to ordain Aaron and his sons.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Order of the Sacrifices

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 8:14; Exodus 29:15"> You are to present the bull at the front of the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 9:8; Leviticus 9:12; Exodus 29:4"> And you shall slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 8:15"> Take some of the blood of the bull and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger; then pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:16; Leviticus 9:10"> Take all the fat that covers the entrails and the lobe of the liver, and both kidneys with the fat on them, and burn them on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 4:21; Exodus 30:10"> But burn the flesh of the bull and its hide and dung outside the camp; it is a purification offering.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or sin offering; also in verse 36">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 29:19; Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:18–21"> Take one of the rams, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 29:11–12"> You are to slaughter the ram, take its blood, and splatter it on all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 1:9"> Cut the ram into pieces, wash the entrails and legs, and place them with its head and other pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 22:7"> Then burn the entire ram on the altar; it is an ascension offering to the LORD, a soothing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:3; Leviticus 8:22–29"> Take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 14:16"> Slaughter the ram, take some of its blood, and put it on the right earlobes of Aaron and his sons, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Splatter the remaining blood on all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22; Exodus 29:7; Exodus 29:1"> And take some of the blood on the altar and some of the anointing oil and sprinkle it on Aaron and his garments, as well as on his sons and their garments. Then he and his garments will be consecrated, as well as his sons and their garments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 9:21; Leviticus 9:19"> Take the fat from the ram, the fat tail, the fat covering the entrails, the lobe of the liver, both kidneys with the fat on them, and the right thigh (since this is a ram for ordination),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:2–3; Leviticus 8:26"> along with one loaf of bread, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:27; Leviticus 9:21"> Put all these in the hands of Aaron and his sons and wave them before the LORD as a wave offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:28; Exodus 29:41; Leviticus 3:16"> Then take them from their hands and burn them on the altar atop the ascension offering as a soothing aroma before the LORD; it is a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 8:29; Leviticus 7:31; Leviticus 7:34"> Take the breast of the ram of Aaron’s ordination and wave it before the LORD as a wave offering, and it will be your portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 10:15; Numbers 18:11; Deuteronomy 18:3"> Consecrate for Aaron and his sons the breast of the wave offering that is waved and the thigh of the heave offering that is lifted up from the ram of ordination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:19–20; Leviticus 7:11–38; Leviticus 10:14–15"> This will belong to Aaron and his sons as a regular portion from the Israelites, for it is the heave offering the Israelites will make to the LORD from their well-being offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Exodus 28:3–4; Exodus 30:30"> The holy garments that belong to Aaron will belong to his sons after him, so they can be anointed and ordained in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 20:28; Leviticus 13:5; Acts 20:6–7"> The son who succeeds him as priest and enters the Tent of Meeting to minister in the Holy Place must wear them for seven days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Food for the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; 1 Samuel 2:13; Ezekiel 46:20–24"> You are to take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; Exodus 29:23; Exodus 29:2–3"> At the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, Aaron and his sons are to eat the meat of the ram and the bread that is in the basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:4; Leviticus 10:13–18; Psalms 22:26"> They must eat those things by which purgation was effected for their ordination and consecration. But no outsider may eat them, because these things are sacred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Leviticus 8:32; Exodus 29:22"> And if any of the meat of ordination or any bread is left until the morning, you are to burn up the remainder. It must not be eaten, because it is sacred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 29:30; Leviticus 14:8–11; Leviticus 8:4–36"> This is what you are to do for Aaron and his sons based on all that I have commanded you, taking seven days to ordain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 9:22–23; Exodus 30:28–29"> Sacrifice a bull as a purification offering each day for purgation. Purify the altar by effecting purgation for it, and anoint it to consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 40:10; Matthew 23:19; Exodus 30:28–29"> For seven days you shall effect purgation for the altar and consecrate it. Then the altar will become most holy; whatever touches the altar will be holy.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Daily Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; 1 Chronicles 16:40; Numbers 28:3–8"> This is what you are to offer regularly on the altar, each day: two lambs that are a year old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 2 Kings 16:15; Psalms 5:3"> Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew between the two evenings; also in verse 41">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:17; Ezekiel 46:14; Joel 2:14"> With the first lamb offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A tenth of an ephah is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour).">c</a></sup>mixed with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew a quarter hin of pressed oil; that is, approximately 0.97 quarts or 0.92 liters">d</a></sup>and a drink offering of a quarter hin of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:13–15; 1 Kings 18:29; Psalms 141:2"> And offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, as a soothing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 25:22"> For the generations to come, this ascension offering shall be made regularly at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting before the LORD, where I will meet you to speak with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 8:11; 2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Malachi 3:1"> I will also meet with the Israelites there, and that place will be consecrated by My glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 21:15; Leviticus 22:9; Leviticus 22:16"> So I will consecrate the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and I will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve Me as priests.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Will Dwell among the People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Exodus 25:8"> Then I will dwell among the Israelites and be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 20:5"> And they will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt so that I might dwell among them.<br />I am the LORD their God.</span>
