## Manna and Quail from Heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> On the fifteenth day of the second month after they had left the land of Egypt, the whole congregation of Israel set out from Elim and came to the Desert of Sin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The geographical name Sin is related to Sinai and should not be mistaken for the English word sin.">a</a></sup>which is between Elim and Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> And there in the desert the whole congregation of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> “If only we had died by the LORD’s hand in the land of Egypt!” they said. “There we sat by pots of meat and ate our fill of bread, but you have brought us into this desert to starve this whole assembly to death!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Behold, I will rain down bread from heaven for you. Each day the people are to go out and gather enough for that day. In this way I will test whether or not they will follow My instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> Then on the sixth day, when they prepare what they bring in, it will be twice as much as they gather on the other days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> So Moses and Aaron said to all the Israelites, “This evening you will know that it was the LORD who brought you out of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> and in the morning you will see the LORD’s glory, because He has heard your grumbling against Him. For who are we, that you should grumble against us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Moses added, “The LORD will give you meat to eat this evening and bread to fill you in the morning, for He has heard your grumbling against Him. Who are we? Your grumblings are not against us but against the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> Then Moses said to Aaron, “Tell the whole congregation of Israel, ‘Come before the LORD, for He has heard your grumbling.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> And as Aaron was speaking to the whole congregation of Israel, they looked toward the desert, and there in a cloud the glory of the LORD appeared.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> “I have heard the grumbling of the Israelites. Tell them, ‘At twilight<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Between the two evenings">b</a></sup>you will eat meat, and in the morning you will be filled with bread. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> That evening quail came and covered the camp, and in the morning there was a layer of dew around the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> When the layer of dew had evaporated, there were thin flakes on the desert floor, as fine as frost on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> When the Israelites saw it, they asked one another, “What is it?” For they did not know what it was.<br />So Moses told them, “It is the bread that the LORD has given you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is what the LORD has commanded: ‘Each one is to gather as much as he needs. You may take an omer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="An omer is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters; also in verses 18, 32, 33, and 36.">c</a></sup>for each person in your tent.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So the Israelites did this. Some gathered more, and some less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> When they measured it by the omer, he who gathered much had no excess, and he who gathered little had no shortfall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in 2 Corinthians 8:15">d</a></sup>Each one gathered as much as he needed to eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> Then Moses said to them, “No one may keep any of it until morning.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> But they did not listen to Moses; some people left part of it until morning, and it became infested with maggots and began to smell. So Moses was angry with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> Every morning each one gathered as much as was needed, and when the sun grew hot, it melted away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sabbath Observed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> On the sixth day, they gathered twice as much food—two omers per person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2 omers is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters per person.">e</a></sup>—and all the leaders of the congregation came and reported this to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> He told them, “This is what the LORD has said: ‘Tomorrow is to be a day of complete rest, a holy Sabbath to the LORD. So bake what you want to bake, and boil what you want to boil. Then set aside whatever remains and keep it until morning.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> So they set it aside until morning as Moses had commanded, and it did not smell or contain any maggots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> “Eat it today,” Moses said, “because today is a Sabbath to the LORD. Today you will not find anything in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> For six days you may gather, but on the seventh day, the Sabbath, it will not be there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> Yet on the seventh day some of the people went out to gather, but they did not find anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “How long will you refuse to keep My commandments and instructions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> Understand that the LORD has given you the Sabbath; that is why on the sixth day He will give you bread for two days. On the seventh day, everyone must stay where he is; no one may leave his place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> So the people rested on the seventh day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Jar of Manna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> Now the house of Israel called the bread manna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Manna sounds like the Hebrew for What is it? (see verse 15).">f</a></sup>It was white like coriander seed and tasted like wafers made with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> Moses said, “This is what the LORD has commanded: ‘Keep an omer of manna for the generations to come, so that they may see the bread I fed you in the wilderness when I brought you out of the land of Egypt.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> So Moses told Aaron, “Take a jar and fill it with an omer of manna. Then place it before the LORD to be preserved for the generations to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> And Aaron placed it in front of the Testimony,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Testimony refers to the stone tablets in the ark of the covenant inscribed with the Ten Commandments.">g</a></sup>to be preserved just as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> The Israelites ate manna forty years, until they came to a land where they could settle; they ate manna until they reached the border of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> (Now an omer is a tenth of an ephah.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="An ephah was a dry measure having the capacity of about 20 dry quarts or 22 liters.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
