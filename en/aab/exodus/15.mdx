<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> Then Moses led Israel from the Red Sea, and they went out into the Desert of Shur. For three days they walked in the desert without finding water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> And when they came to Marah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Marah means bitter.">c</a></sup>they could not drink the water there because it was bitter. (That is why it was named Marah.)</span>
