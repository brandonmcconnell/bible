## Moses at the Burning Bush

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalms 78:70–72"> Meanwhile, Moses was shepherding the flock of his father-in-law Jethro,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Moses’ father-in-law Jethro was also called Reuel; see Exodus 2:18.">a</a></sup>the priest of Midian. He led the flock to the far side of the wilderness and came to Horeb,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai">b</a></sup>the mountain of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Isaiah 43:2; Mark 12:26"> There the angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or Angel">c</a></sup>of the LORD appeared to him in a blazing fire from within a bush. Moses saw the bush ablaze with fire, but it was not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalms 107:8; Job 37:14"> So Moses thought, “I must go over and see this marvelous sight. Why is the bush not burning up?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; 1 Samuel 3:6; Acts 9:4"> When the LORD saw that he had gone over to look, God called out to him from within the bush, “Moses, Moses!”<br />“Here I am,” he answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> “Do not come any closer,” God said. “Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy ground.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Acts 7:33">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> Then He said, “I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 22:32, Mark 12:26, Luke 20:37, and Acts 7:32">e</a></sup><br />At this, Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to look at God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> The LORD said, “I have indeed seen the affliction of My people in Egypt. I have heard them crying out because of their oppressors, and I am aware of their sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> I have come down to rescue them from the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up out of that land to a good and spacious land, a land flowing with milk and honey—the home of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> And now the cry of the Israelites has reached Me, and I have seen how severely the Egyptians are oppressing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:4; 1 Samuel 12:6; Psalms 105:26"> Therefore, go! I am sending you to Pharaoh to bring My people the Israelites out of Egypt.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Cited in Acts 7:34">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> But Moses asked God, “Who am I, that I should go to Pharaoh and bring the Israelites out of Egypt?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 41:10"> “I will surely be with you,” God said, “and this will be the sign to you that I have sent you: When you have brought the people out of Egypt, all of you will worship God on this mountain.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Cited in Acts 7:7">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Isaiah 9:6; Jeremiah 23:6"> Then Moses asked God, “Suppose I go to the Israelites and say to them, ‘The God of your fathers has sent me to you,’ and they ask me, ‘What is His name?’ What should I tell them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> God said to Moses, “I AM WHO I AM.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or I WILL BE WHAT I WILL BE. The Hebrew ehyeh echoes God’s pledge in verse 12, ‘I will be (ehyeh) with you’: the Name declares His active presence with His people, not an abstract statement of being.">h</a></sup>This is what you are to say to the Israelites: ‘I AM has sent me to you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachi 3:6"> God also told Moses, “Say to the Israelites, ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob—has sent me to you.’ This is My name forever, and this is how I am to be remembered in every generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> Go, assemble the elders of Israel and say to them, ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—has appeared to me and said: I have surely attended to you and have seen what has been done to you in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> And I have promised to bring you up out of your affliction in Egypt, into the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—a land flowing with milk and honey.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> The elders of Israel will listen to what you say, and you must go with them to the king of Egypt and tell him, ‘The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us. Now please let us take a three-day journey into the wilderness, so that we may sacrifice to the LORD our God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> But I know that the king of Egypt will not allow you to go unless a mighty hand compels him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally except by a mighty hand">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> So I will stretch out My hand and strike the Egyptians with all the wonders I will perform among them. And after that, he will release you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalms 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> And I will grant this people such favor in the sight of the Egyptians that when you leave, you will not go away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> Every woman shall ask her neighbor and any woman staying in her house for silver and gold jewelry and clothing, and you will put them on your sons and daughters. So you will plunder the Egyptians.”</span>
</div>
</div>
