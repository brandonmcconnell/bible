## Bezalel and Oholiab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> “See, I have called by name Bezalel son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 Kings 7:14"> And I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with skill, ability, and knowledge in all kinds of craftsmanship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14"> to design artistic works in gold, silver, and bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> to cut gemstones for settings, and to carve wood, so that he may be a master of every craft.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> Moreover, I have selected Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, as his assistant.<br />I have also given skill to all the craftsmen, that they may fashion all that I have commanded you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the ark of the covenant">a</a></sup>and the throne seat upon it, and all the other furnishings of the tent—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> the table with its utensils, the pure gold lampstand with all its utensils, the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> the altar of ascension offering with all its utensils, and the basin with its stand—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> as well as the woven garments, both the holy garments for Aaron the priest and the garments for his sons to serve as priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> in addition to the anointing oil and fragrant incense for the Holy Place. They are to make them according to all that I have commanded you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sign of the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> “Tell the Israelites, ‘Surely you must keep My Sabbaths, for this will be a sign between Me and you for the generations to come, so that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> Keep the Sabbath, for it is holy to you. Anyone who profanes it must surely be put to death. Whoever does any work on that day must be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> For six days work may be done, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, holy to the LORD. Whoever does any work on the Sabbath day must surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> The Israelites must keep the Sabbath, celebrating it as a permanent covenant for the generations to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> It is a sign between Me and the Israelites forever; for in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, but on the seventh day He rested and was refreshed.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
