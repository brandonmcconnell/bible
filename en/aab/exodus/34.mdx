## New Stone Tablets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Exodus 31:18; Psalms 119:89"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Chisel out two stone tablets like the originals, and I will write on them the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:20; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 19:11"> Be ready in the morning, and come up on Mount Sinai to present yourself before Me on the mountaintop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:21; 1 Timothy 2:5"> No one may go up with you; in fact, no one may be seen anywhere on the mountain—not even the flocks or herds may graze in front of the mountain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Moses chiseled out two stone tablets like the originals. He rose early in the morning, and taking the two stone tablets in his hands, he went up Mount Sinai as the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Numbers 11:25; Luke 9:34–35"> And the LORD descended in a cloud, stood with him there, and proclaimed His name, the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Psalms 103:8–13"> Then the LORD passed in front of Moses and called out:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The LORD, the LORD God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is compassionate and gracious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">slow to anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">abounding in loving devotion and faithfulness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 5:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">maintaining loving devotion to a thousand generations,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew to thousands">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">bearing iniquity, transgression, and sin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet He will by no means leave the guilty unpunished;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will visit the iniquity of the fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on their children and grandchildren</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the third and fourth generations.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 20:18; Genesis 17:3"> Moses immediately bowed down to the ground and worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Deuteronomy 32:9; Psalms 94:14"> “O Lord,” he said, “if I have indeed found favor in Your sight, my Lord, please go with us. Although this is a stiff-necked people, forgive our iniquity and sin, and take us as Your inheritance.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The LORD Renews the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 77:14; Isaiah 64:3"> And the LORD said, “Behold, I am making a covenant. Before all your people I will perform wonders that have never been done in any nation in all the world. All the people among whom you live will see the LORD’s work, for it is an awesome thing that I am doing with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8"> Observe what I command you this day. I will drive out before you the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:32–33; Joshua 23:12–13; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Be careful not to make a treaty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew berit are translated in most passages as covenant.">b</a></sup>with the inhabitants of the land you are entering, lest they become a snare in your midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> Rather, you must tear down their altars, smash their sacred stones, and chop down their Asherah poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 32:21; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> For you must not worship any other god, for the LORD, whose name is Jealous,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Heb. qanna, ‘jealous,’ here made a name: the LORD whose name is Jealous. See note on Exod 20:5.">c</a></sup>is a jealous God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 2:17; Psalms 106:28; Numbers 25:2"> Do not make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, for when they prostitute themselves to their gods and sacrifice to them, they will invite you, and you will eat their sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; 1 Kings 11:2–4; Ezra 9:2"> And when you take some of their daughters as brides for your sons, their daughters will prostitute themselves to their gods and cause your sons to do the same.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Exodus 32:8; Isaiah 46:6–7"> You shall make no molten gods for yourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 13:6–7"> You are to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.">d</a></sup>For seven days at the appointed time in the month of Abib,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Abib was the first month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April; twice in this verse.">e</a></sup>you are to eat unleavened bread as I commanded you. For in the month of Abib you came out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Exodus 13:12"> The first offspring of every womb belongs to Me, including all the firstborn males among your livestock, whether cattle or sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:16"> You must redeem the firstborn of a donkey with a lamb; but if you do not redeem it, you are to break its neck. You must redeem all the firstborn of your sons. No one shall appear before Me empty-handed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 21:4; Luke 13:14"> Six days you shall labor, but on the seventh day you shall rest; even in the seasons of plowing and harvesting, you must rest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:10–15; John 7:2"> And you are to celebrate the Feast of Weeks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).">f</a></sup>with the firstfruits of the wheat harvest, and the Feast of Ingathering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is later called the Feast of Tabernacles (or Booths or Shelters).">g</a></sup>at the turn of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:14; Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:17"> Three times a year all your males are to appear before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 33:2; Proverbs 16:7"> For I will drive out the nations before you and enlarge your borders, and no one will covet your land when you go up three times a year to appear before the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 12:10; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8"> Do not offer the blood of a sacrifice to Me along with anything leavened, and do not let any of the sacrifice from the Passover Feast remain until morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:2"> Bring the best of the firstfruits of your soil to the house of the LORD your God.<br />You must not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 24:4; Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 4:13"> The LORD also said to Moses, “Write down these words, for in accordance with these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 34:1; Exodus 24:18; Deuteronomy 9:18"> So Moses was there with the LORD forty days and forty nights without eating bread or drinking water. He wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant—the Ten Commandments.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew the Ten Words">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Luke 9:29; Exodus 32:15"> And when Moses came down from Mount Sinai with the two tablets of the Testimony in his hands, he was unaware that his face had become radiant from speaking with the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Luke 5:8; Numbers 12:8"> Aaron and all the Israelites looked at Moses, and behold, his face was radiant. And they were afraid to approach him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Exodus 24:1–3; Genesis 45:3"> But Moses called out to them; so Aaron and all the leaders of the congregation returned to him, and Moses spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:14; Exodus 24:3; Matthew 28:20"> And after this all the Israelites came near, and Moses commanded them to do everything that the LORD had told him on Mount Sinai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4–6; 2 Corinthians 3:13–18; Romans 10:4"> When Moses had finished speaking with them, he put a veil over his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 4:16"> But whenever Moses went in before the LORD to speak with Him, he would remove the veil until he came out. And when he came out, he would tell the Israelites what he had been commanded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 34:29–30; John 5:35; Daniel 12:3"> and the Israelites would see that the face of Moses was radiant. So Moses would put the veil back over his face until he went in to speak with the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
