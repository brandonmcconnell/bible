## The Israelites Multiply in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:1–2; Genesis 46:8–26; Genesis 35:23–26"> These are the names of the sons of Israel who went to Egypt with Jacob, each with his family:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:22"> Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Genesis 35:23"> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Dan and Naphtali;<br />Gad and Asher.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:26–27; Judges 8:30"> The descendants of Jacob numbered seventy-five<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX, DSS (see also Gen 46:27), and Acts 7:14 read seventy-five. The MT reads seventy. The AAB follows the reading attested by the NT (Acts 7:14).">a</a></sup>in all, including Joseph, who was already in Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> Now Joseph and all his brothers and all that generation died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> but the Israelites were fruitful and increased rapidly; they multiplied and became exceedingly numerous, so that the land was filled with them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Oppression by a New King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> Then a new king, who did not know Joseph, came to power in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> “Look,” he said to his people, “the Israelites have become too numerous and too powerful for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalms 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, or they will increase even more; and if a war breaks out, they may join our enemies, fight against us, and leave the country.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or and take the country">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> So the Egyptians appointed taskmasters over the Israelites to oppress them with forced labor. As a result, they built Pithom and Rameses as store cities for Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> But the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied and flourished; so the Egyptians came to dread the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> They worked the Israelites ruthlessly</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> and made their lives bitter with hard labor in brick and mortar, and with all kinds of work in the fields. Every service they imposed was harsh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the king of Egypt said to the Hebrew midwives, whose names were Shiphrah and Puah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> “When you help the Hebrew women give birth, observe them on the birthstools. If the child is a son, kill him; but if it is a daughter, let her live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> The midwives, however, feared God and did not do as the king of Egypt had instructed; they let the boys live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> So the king of Egypt summoned the midwives and asked them, “Why have you done this? Why have you let the boys live?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> The midwives answered Pharaoh, “The Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women, for they are vigorous and give birth before a midwife arrives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> So God was good to the midwives, and the people multiplied and became even more numerous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalms 37:3"> And because the midwives feared God, He gave them families of their own.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> Then Pharaoh commanded all his people: “Every son born to the Hebrews<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="SP, LXX, and Targum Yonaton; Hebrew does not include to the Hebrews.">c</a></sup>you must throw into the Nile, but every daughter you may allow to live.”</span>
</div>
</div>
