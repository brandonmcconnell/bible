## The Supremacy of the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> On many past occasions and in many different ways, God spoke to our fathers through the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> But in these last days He has spoken to us by a Son,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or in His Son">a</a></sup>whom He appointed heir of all things, and through whom He made the world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="literally the ages">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> The Son is the radiance of God’s glory and the exact representation of His nature, upholding all things by His powerful word. After He had provided purification for sins, He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> So He became as far superior to the angels as the name He has inherited is excellent beyond theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels did God ever say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are My Son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">today I have become Your Father”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 2:7; literally today I have begotten You">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span><br />Or again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will be His Father,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He will be My Son”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 17:13">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And again, when God brings His firstborn into the world, He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let all God’s angels worship Him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Deuteronomy 32:43 (see DSS and LXX)">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> Now about the angels He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He makes His angels winds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His servants flames of fire.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Psalms 104:4 (see also LXX)">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> But about the Son He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved righteousness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hated wickedness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">therefore God, Your God, has anointed You</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">above Your companions with the oil of joy.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Psalms 45:6–7">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“In the beginning, O Lord, You laid the foundations of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the heavens are the work of Your hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will perish, but You remain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will all wear out like a garment.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will roll them up like a robe;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a garment</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="BYZ and TR do not include like a garment.">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be changed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but You remain the same,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your years will never end.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Psalms 102:25–27">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> Yet to which of the angels did God ever say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at My right hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Psalms 110:1">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are not the angels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally Are they not all">k</a></sup>ministering spirits sent to serve those who will inherit salvation?</span>
</div>
