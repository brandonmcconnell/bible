## Christ’s Perfect Sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law is only a shadow of the good things to come, not the realities themselves. It can never, by the same sacrifices offered year after year, make perfect those who draw near to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> If it could, would not the offerings have ceased? For the worshipers would have been cleansed once for all, and would no longer have felt the guilt of their sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> Instead, those sacrifices are an annual reminder of sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> because it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> Therefore, when Christ came into the world, He said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sacrifice and offering You did not desire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a body You prepared for Me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In ascension offerings</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gk. holokautoma; see note on Mark 12:33.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and purification offerings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You took no delight.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, ‘Here I am, it is written about Me in the scroll:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have come to do Your will, O God.’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 40:6–8 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> In the passage above He says, “Sacrifices and offerings, ascension offerings and purification offerings You did not desire, nor did You delight in them” (although they are offered according to the law).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> Then He adds, “Here I am, I have come to do Your will.” He takes away the first to establish the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> And by that will, we have been sanctified through the sacrifice of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> Day after day every priest stands to minister and to offer again and again the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But when this Priest had offered for all time one sacrifice for sins, He sat down at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> Since that time, He waits for His enemies to be made a footstool for His feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> because by a single offering He has made perfect for all time those who are being sanctified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> The Holy Spirit also testifies to us about this. First He says:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“This is the covenant I will make with them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days, declares the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put My laws in their hearts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and inscribe them on their minds.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jeremiah 31:33">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> Then He adds:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their sins and lawless acts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will remember no more.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jeremiah 31:34">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> And where these have been forgiven, an offering for sin is no longer needed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Call to Persevere

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Therefore, brothers, since we have confidence to enter the Most Holy Place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or the Holy Place">e</a></sup>by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> by the new and living way opened for us through the curtain of His body,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally through the veil that is His flesh">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and since we have a great priest over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us draw near with a sincere heart in full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled to cleanse us from a guilty conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Let us hold resolutely to the hope we profess, for He who promised is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> And let us consider how to spur one another on to love and good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> Let us not neglect meeting together, as some have made a habit, but let us encourage one another, and all the more as you see the Day approaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> If we deliberately go on sinning after we have received the knowledge of the truth, no further sacrifice for sins remains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but only a fearful expectation of judgment and of raging fire that will consume all adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Anyone who rejected the law of Moses died without mercy on the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> How much more severely do you think one deserves to be punished who has trampled on the Son of God, profaned the blood of the covenant that sanctified him, and insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know Him who said, “Vengeance is Mine; I will repay,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Deuteronomy 32:35 (see also LXX)">g</a></sup>and again, “The Lord will judge His people.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Deuteronomy 32:36; Psalms 135:14">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> Remember the early days that you were in the light.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or Remember when you were first enlightened.">i</a></sup>In those days, you endured a great conflict in the face of suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> Sometimes you were publicly exposed to ridicule and persecution; at other times you were partners with those who were so treated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> You sympathized with those in prison and joyfully accepted the confiscation of your property, knowing that you yourselves had a better and permanent possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> So do not throw away your confidence; it holds a great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> You need to persevere, so that after you have done the will of God, you will receive what He has promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> For,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“In just a little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He who is coming will come and will not delay.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But My righteous one will live by faith;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="BYZ and TR But the righteous will live by faith">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if he shrinks back,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will take no pleasure in him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Habakkuk 2:3–4 (see also LXX)">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not of those who shrink back and are destroyed, but of those who have faith and preserve their lives.</span>
</div>
</div>
