## The Perfect High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> Every high priest is appointed from among men to represent them in matters relating to God, to offer gifts and sacrifices for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He is able to deal gently with those who are ignorant and misguided, since he himself is subject to weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> That is why he is obligated to offer sacrifices for his own sins, as well as for the sins of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> No one takes this honor upon himself; he must be called by God, just as Aaron was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> So also Christ did not take upon Himself the glory of becoming a high priest, but He was called by the One who said to Him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are My Son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">today I have become Your Father.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 2:7; literally today I have begotten You">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> And in another passage God says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the order of Melchizedek.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Psalms 110:4">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> During the days of Jesus’ earthly life, He offered up prayers and petitions with loud cries and tears to the One who could save Him from death, and He was heard because of His reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> Although He was a Son, He learned obedience from what He suffered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> And having been made perfect, He became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey Him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> and was designated by God as high priest in the order of Melchizedek.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Milk and Solid Food

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> We have much to say about this, but it is hard to explain, because you are dull of hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> Although by this time you ought to be teachers, you need someone to reteach you the basic principles of God’s word.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or of the oracles of God">c</a></sup>You need milk, not solid food!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For everyone who lives on milk is still an infant, inexperienced in the message of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> But solid food is for the mature, who by constant use have trained their senses to distinguish good from evil.</span>
</div>
</div>
