## Faith and Assurance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> Now faith is the assurance of what we hope for and the certainty of what we do not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Hebrews 11:4"> This is why the ancients were commended.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 2 Peter 3:5; John 1:3"> By faith we understand that the universe was formed at God’s command, so that what is seen was not made out of what was visible.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Abel, Enoch, Noah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12"> By faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain did. By faith he was commended as righteous when God gave approval to his gifts. And by faith he still speaks, even though he is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; Jude 1:14; Hebrews 11:6"> By faith Enoch was taken up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally transferred or translated">a</a></sup>so that he did not see death: “He could not be found, because God had taken him away.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Genesis 5:24 (see also LXX)">b</a></sup>For before he was taken, he was commended as one who pleased God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; Matthew 6:33"> And without faith it is impossible to please God. For anyone who approaches Him must believe that He exists and that He rewards those who earnestly seek Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; 1 Peter 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> By faith Noah, when warned about things not yet seen, in godly fear built an ark to save his family. By faith he condemned the world and became heir of the righteousness that comes by faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Abraham and Sarah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4; Matthew 7:24–25"> By faith Abraham, when called to go to a place he would later receive as his inheritance, obeyed and went, without knowing where he was going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 13:18; Genesis 13:3"> By faith he dwelt in the promised land as a stranger in a foreign country. He lived in tents, as did Isaac and Jacob, who were heirs with him of the same promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 12:22"> For he was looking forward to the city with foundations, whose architect and builder is God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Hebrews 10:23; Genesis 18:11–14"> By faith Sarah, even though she was barren and beyond the proper age, was enabled to conceive a child, because she considered Him faithful who had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> And so from one man, and he as good as dead, came descendants as numerous as the stars in the sky and as countless as the sand on the seashore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; Psalms 39:12; Hebrews 11:27"> All these people died in faith, without having received the things they were promised. However, they saw them and welcomed them from afar. And they acknowledged that they were strangers and exiles on the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18–7; Hebrews 11:16; Hebrews 13:14"> Now those who say such things show that they are seeking a country of their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> If they had been thinking of the country they had left, they would have had opportunity to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Hebrews 11:10; Philippians 3:20"> Instead, they were longing for a better country, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed to be called their God, for He has prepared a city for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; James 2:21–24"> By faith Abraham, when he was tested, offered up Isaac on the altar. He who had received the promises was ready to offer his one and only son,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or only begotten son or unique son">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Romans 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> even though God had said to him, “Through Isaac your seed will be reckoned.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Genesis 21:12. Gk. sperma, ‘seed’; see note on Acts 3:25.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 4:17–21; Matthew 9:28; Ephesians 3:20"> Abraham reasoned that God could raise the dead, and in a sense, he did receive Isaac back from death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning the future.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> By faith Jacob, when he was dying, blessed each of Joseph’s sons and worshiped as he leaned on the top of his staff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> By faith Joseph, when his end was near, spoke about the exodus of the Israelites and gave instructions about his bones.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; Hebrews 13:6"> By faith Moses’ parents hid him for three months after his birth, because they saw that he was a beautiful child, and they were unafraid of the king’s edict.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; Acts 7:21–24"> By faith Moses, when he was grown, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Timothy 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> He chose to suffer oppression with God’s people rather than to experience the fleeting enjoyment of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Hebrews 11:6; 1 Peter 4:14"> He valued disgrace for Christ above the treasures of Egypt, for he was looking ahead to his reward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:1"> By faith Moses left Egypt, not fearing the king’s anger; he persevered because he saw Him who is invisible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; Hebrews 9:19; Hebrews 12:24"> By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of blood, so that the Destroyer of the firstborn would not touch Israel’s own firstborn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; Psalms 106:9–11; Psalms 78:13"> By faith the people passed through the Red Sea as on dry land; but when the Egyptians tried to follow,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally made an attempt">e</a></sup>they were drowned.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Faith of Many


## (Joshua–Malachi)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> By faith the walls of Jericho fell, after the people had marched around them for seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; Matthew 1:5"> By faith the prostitute Rahab, because she welcomed the spies in peace, did not perish with those who were disobedient.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> And what more shall I say? Time will not allow me to tell of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 144:1–2; Hebrews 6:12–15; Hebrews 10:36"> who through faith conquered kingdoms, administered justice, and gained what was promised; who shut the mouths of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> quenched the raging fire, and escaped the edge of the sword; who gained strength from weakness, became mighty in battle, and put foreign armies to flight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 4:27–37; 1 Kings 17:22–24; Philippians 3:11"> Women received back their dead, raised to life again. Others were tortured and refused their release, so that they might gain a better resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:2; Genesis 39:20; Jeremiah 37:15–21"> Still others endured mocking and flogging, and even chains and imprisonment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21; Jeremiah 26:23; 1 Kings 19:10"> They were stoned, they were sawed in two,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="NE, WH, BYZ, and TR include they were put to the test,">f</a></sup>they were put to death by the sword. They went around in sheepskins and goatskins, destitute, oppressed, and mistreated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Samuel 22:1; 1 Kings 19:9"> The world was not worthy of them. They wandered in deserts and mountains, and hid in caves and holes in the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:2"> These were all commended for their faith, yet they did not receive what was promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 6:11; Hebrews 8:6; Hebrews 10:11–14"> God had planned something better for us, so that together with us they would be made perfect.</span>
</div>
</div>
