<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> We must pay closer attention, therefore, to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the message spoken by angels was binding, and every transgression and disobedience received its just punishment,</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> and was affirmed by God through signs, wonders, various miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit distributed according to His will.</span>

## Jesus like His Brothers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For it is not to angels that He has subjected the world to come, about which we are speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But somewhere it is testified in these words:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is man that You are mindful of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or the son of man that You care for him?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made him a little lower</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or a little while lower; also in verse 9">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than the angels;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You crowned him with glory and honor</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="WH and TR include and set him over the works of Your hands.">b</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-2">and placed everything under his feet.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Psalms 8:4–6 (see also LXX)">c</a></sup><br />When God subjected all things to him, He left nothing outside of his control. Yet at present we do not see everything subject to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels, now crowned with glory and honor because He suffered death, so that by the grace of God He might taste death for everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> In bringing many sons to glory, it was fitting for God, for whom and through whom all things exist, to make the author<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or pioneer or founder">d</a></sup>of their salvation perfect through suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both the One who sanctifies and those who are sanctified are of the same family. So Jesus is not ashamed to call them brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will proclaim Your name to My brothers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will sing Your praises in the assembly.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Psalms 22:22 (see also LXX)">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will put My trust in Him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isaiah 8:17">f</a></sup><br />And once again:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Here am I, and the children God has given Me.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Isaiah 8:18">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Now since the children have flesh and blood, He too shared in their humanity, so that by His death He might destroy him who holds the power of death, that is, the devil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Satan holds the power of death because the sin he tempts people into brings death (Rom 5:12; 6:23), and all who sin share the fate of the one who was ‘a murderer from the beginning’ (John 8:44; Rev 20:10, 15). <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and free those who all their lives were held in slavery by their fear of death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For surely it is not the angels He helps, but the descendants of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> For this reason He had to be made like His brothers in every way, so that He might become a merciful and faithful high priest in service to God, in order to make purification<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gk. hilaskesthai. Commonly rendered 'to make propitiation'; the word names cleansing, so the AAB renders it 'to make purification.'">i</a></sup>for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> Because He Himself suffered when He was tempted, He is able to help those who are being tempted.</span>
</div>
