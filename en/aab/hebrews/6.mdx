## A Call to Maturity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Therefore let us leave the elementary teachings about Christ and go on to maturity, not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or from acts that lead to death">a</a></sup>and of faith in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> instruction about baptisms,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or cleansing rites">b</a></sup>the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> And this we will do, if God permits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> It is ultimately impossible for those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift, who have shared in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> who have tasted the goodness of the word of God and the powers of the coming age—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and then have fallen away—to be restored to repentance, because they themselves are crucifying the Son of God all over again and subjecting Him to open shame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For land that drinks in the rain often falling on it and that produces a crop useful to those for whom it is tended receives the blessing of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> But land that produces thorns and thistles is worthless, and its curse is imminent. In the end it will be burned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> Even though we speak like this, beloved, we are convinced of better things in your case—things that accompany salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For God is not unjust. He will not forget your work and the love you have shown for His name as you have ministered to the holy ones and continue to do so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> We want each of you to show this same diligence to the very end, in order to make your hope sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> Then you will not be sluggish, but will imitate those who through faith and patience inherit what has been promised.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Unchangeable Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> When God made His promise to Abraham, since He had no one greater to swear by, He swore by Himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “I will surely bless you and multiply you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Genesis 22:17, where God says ‘I will multiply your seed.’ Hebrews quotes it in a shorter form, ‘multiply you,’ for the promise to Abraham’s seed is a promise to Abraham himself; see note on Acts 3:25.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so Abraham, after waiting patiently, obtained the promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> Men swear by someone greater than themselves, and their oath serves as a confirmation to end all argument.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> So when God wanted to make the unchanging nature of His purpose very clear to the heirs of the promise, He guaranteed it with an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> Thus by two unchangeable things in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled to take hold of the hope set before us may be strongly encouraged.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> where Jesus our forerunner has entered on our behalf. He has become a high priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.</span>
</div>
</div>
