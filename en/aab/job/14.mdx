<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like a flower, he comes forth, then withers away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a fleeting shadow, he does not endure.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do You open Your eyes to one like this?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will You bring him into judgment before You?</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as a mountain erodes and crumbles</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a rock is dislodged from its place,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">as water wears away the stones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and torrents wash away the soil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so You destroy a man’s hope.</span></span>
