<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet by His power, God drags away the mighty;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">though rising up, they have no assurance of life.</span></span>
