## Eliphaz: The Innocent Prosper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 6:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“If one ventures a word with you, will you be wearied?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yet who can keep from speaking?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely you have instructed many,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have strengthened their feeble hands.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your words have steadied those who stumbled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have braced the knees that were buckling.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now trouble has come upon you, and you are weary.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It strikes you, and you are dismayed.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Consider now, I plead:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who, being innocent, has ever perished?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or where have the upright been destroyed?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the breath of God they perish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and by the blast of His anger they are consumed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The lion may roar, and the fierce lion may growl,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet the teeth of the young lions are broken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">The old lion perishes for lack of prey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now a word came to me secretly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my ears caught a whisper of it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">In disquieting visions in the night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when deep sleep falls on men,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">fear and trembling came over me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and made all my bones shudder.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then a spirit</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or a wind">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">glided past my face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hair on my body bristled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It stood still,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I could not discern its appearance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a form loomed before my eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I heard a whispering voice:</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or a man more pure than his Maker?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">how much more those who dwell in houses of clay,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose foundations are in the dust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who can be crushed like a moth!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are smashed to pieces from dawn to dusk;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">unnoticed, they perish forever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Are not their tent cords pulled up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that they die without wisdom?’</span></span>
