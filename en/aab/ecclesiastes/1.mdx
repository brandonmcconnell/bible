## With Wisdom Comes Sorrow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen all the things that are done under the sun, and have found them all to be vapor, a pursuit of the wind.</span>
