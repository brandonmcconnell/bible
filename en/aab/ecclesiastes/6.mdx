## The Futility of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> There is another evil I have seen under the sun, and it weighs heavily upon mankind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalms 17:14; Psalms 73:7"> God gives a man riches, wealth, and honor, so that he lacks nothing his heart desires; but God does not allow him to enjoy them. Instead, a stranger will enjoy them. This is vapor and a grievous affliction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremiah 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> A man may father a hundred children and live for many years; yet no matter how long he lives, if he is unsatisfied with his prosperity and does not even receive a proper burial, I say that a stillborn child is better off than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:13"> For a stillborn child enters in vapor and departs in darkness, and his name is shrouded in obscurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> The child, though neither seeing the sun nor knowing anything, has more rest than that man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> even if he lives a thousand years twice over but fails to enjoy his prosperity. Do not all go to the same place?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All a man’s labor is for his mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet his appetite is never satisfied.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew filled">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalms 101:2"> What advantage, then, has the wise man over the fool? What gain comes to the poor man who knows how to conduct himself before others?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> Better what the eye can see than the wandering of desire. This too is vapor and a pursuit of the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> Whatever exists was named long ago, and it is known what man is; but he cannot contend with one stronger than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> For the more words, the more vapor—and how does that profit anyone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> For who knows what is good for a man during the few days in which he passes through his vapor of a life like a shadow? Who can tell a man what will come after him under the sun?</span>
</div>
