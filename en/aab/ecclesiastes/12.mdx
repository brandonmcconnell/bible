<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:27; Ecclesiastes 11:8; Psalms 71:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember your Creator in the days of your youth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before the days of adversity come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the years approach of which you will say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I find no pleasure in them,”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">on the day the keepers of the house tremble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the strong men stoop,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when those grinding cease because they are few</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those watching through windows see dimly,</span></span>

## The Whole Duty of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:32; Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 10:8"> Not only was the Teacher wise, but he also taught the people knowledge; he pondered, searched out, and arranged many proverbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:5; 1 Timothy 1:15; Proverbs 16:21–24"> The Teacher searched to find delightful sayings and to record accurate words of truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or and sought to write what was upright and true">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Hebrews 4:12; Ezekiel 34:23"> The words of the wise are like goads, and the anthologies of the masters are like firmly embedded nails driven by a single Shepherd.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or shepherd">d</a></sup></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:2"> When all has been heard, the conclusion of the matter is this: Fear God and keep His commandments, because this is the whole duty of man.</span>
