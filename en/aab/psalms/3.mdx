## Deliver Me, O LORD!

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 17:7; 2 Samuel 17:11–13; 2 Samuel 16:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, how my foes have increased!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">How many rise up against me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:11; Psalms 42:3; Psalms 42:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many say of me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“God will not deliver him.”</span>Selah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Selah or Interlude is probably a musical or literary term; here and throughout the Psalms.">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 91:15; Isaiah 65:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">To the LORD I cry aloud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He answers me from His holy mountain.</span>Selah</span>
