## As the Deer Pants for the Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In many Hebrew manuscripts Psalms 42 and 43 constitute one psalm.
