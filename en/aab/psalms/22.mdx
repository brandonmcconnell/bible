## The Psalm of the Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Doe of the Dawn.” A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet You are holy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">enthroned on the praises of Israel.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am a worm and not a man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">scorned by men and despised by the people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">All who see me mock me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they sneer and shake their heads:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He trusts in the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let the LORD deliver him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let the LORD rescue him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">since He delights in him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 27:43">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet You brought me forth from the womb;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You made me secure at my mother’s breast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">From birth I was cast upon You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from my mother’s womb You have been my God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be not far from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for trouble is near</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there is no one to help.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many bulls surround me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">strong bulls of Bashan encircle me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">They open their jaws against me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like lions that roar and maul.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am poured out like water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all my bones are disjointed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My heart is like wax;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it melts away within me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">My strength</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="MT; or My mouth, a possible reading of the original Hebrew text">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is dried up like a potsherd,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You lay me in the dust of death.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">For dogs surround me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a band of evil men encircles me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have pierced my hands and feet.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="DSS, LXX, Vulgate, and Syriac; most MT like a lion at my hands and feet">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I can count all my bones;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they stare and gloat over me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They divide my garments among them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cast lots for my clothing.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Cited in John 19:24; see also Matthew 27:35, Mark 15:24, and Luke 23:34">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But You, O LORD, be not far off;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O my Strength, come quickly to help me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver my soul from the sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my precious life from the power of wild dogs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save me from the mouth of the lion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the horns of the wild oxen You have answered me!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will proclaim Your name to my brothers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will praise You in the assembly.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX I will sing Your praises in the assembly; cited in Hebrews 2:12.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who fear the LORD, praise Him!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All descendants of Jacob, honor Him!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All offspring of Israel, revere Him!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For He has not despised or detested</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the torment of the afflicted.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has not hidden His face from him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but has attended to his cry for help.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My praise for You resounds in the great assembly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will fulfill my vows before those who fear You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The poor will eat and be satisfied;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who seek the LORD will praise Him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">May your hearts live forever!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the ends of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will remember and turn to the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All the families of the nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will bow down before Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For dominion belongs to the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He rules over the nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the rich of the earth will feast and worship;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who go down to the dust will kneel before Him—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even those unable to preserve their lives.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Posterity will serve Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will declare the Lord to a new generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will come and proclaim His righteousness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to a people yet unborn—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all that He has done.</span></span>
