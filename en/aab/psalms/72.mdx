## Endow the King with Your Justice

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">Of Solomon.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Endow the king with Your justice, O God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the son of the king with Your righteousness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he judge Your people with righteousness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your afflicted with justice.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the mountains bring peace to the people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hills bring righteousness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he vindicate the afflicted among the people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may he save the children of the needy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and crush the oppressor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">May they fear him</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX He shall endure">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as long as the sun shines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as long as the moon remains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">through all generations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he be like rain that falls on freshly cut grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like spring showers that water the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the righteous flourish in his days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and prosperity abound</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until the moon is no more.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">May he rule from sea to sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from the Euphrates</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew the River">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the ends of the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the nomads bow before him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his enemies lick the dust.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">May the kings of Tarshish and distant shores bring tribute;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">May all kings bow down to him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all nations serve him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he will deliver the needy who cry out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the afflicted who have no helper.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will take pity on the poor and needy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and save the lives of the oppressed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will redeem them from oppression and violence,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for their blood is precious in his sight.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Long may he live!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">May gold from Sheba be given him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May people ever pray for him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may they bless him all day long.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">May there be an abundance of grain in the land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may it sway atop the hills.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May its fruit trees flourish like the forests of Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the people of its cities like the grass of the field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">May his name endure forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may his name continue</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or increase">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as long as the sun shines.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In him may all nations be blessed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may they call him blessed.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who alone does marvelous deeds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And blessed be His glorious name forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may all the earth be filled with His glory.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Amen and amen.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> Thus conclude the prayers of David son of Jesse.</span>
</div>
