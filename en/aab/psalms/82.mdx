## God Presides in the Divine Assembly

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:35; 2 Chronicles 19:6–7; Psalms 82:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God presides in the divine assembly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He renders judgment among the gods:</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The divine assembly is God’s council of heavenly beings, the elohim He judges here (Ps 89:5-7). </div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 18:5; Psalms 58:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">“How long will you judge unjustly</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or How long will you defend the unjust">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and show partiality to the wicked?</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:27; Psalms 10:18; Jeremiah 22:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Defend the cause of the weak and fatherless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">uphold the rights of the afflicted and oppressed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:11–12; Job 5:15–16; Job 29:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue the weak and needy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">save them from the hand of the wicked.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:3; Micah 3:1; Romans 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">They do not know or understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they wander in the darkness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the foundations of the earth are shaken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:34–36; Psalms 82:1; Exodus 22:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have said, ‘You are gods;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you are all sons of the Most High.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Cited in John 10:34">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:14; Psalms 49:12; Job 21:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">But like mortals you will die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and like rulers you will fall.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="God sentences these heavenly beings to death: they will die as mortals die and fall as rulers fall. See note on John 10:34.">d</a></sup></span>

## Footnotes

<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">82:7</span> God sentences these heavenly beings to death: they will die as mortals die and fall as rulers fall. Human judges already die, so the sentence falls on beings who are not mortal. Isaiah sees the host of heaven punished together with the kings of the earth, gathered like prisoners in a pit and punished after many days (Isa 24:21-22). The sentence is carried out at the last judgment, in the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels, the lake of fire that is the second death (Matt 25:41; Rev 20:10, 14). See note on John 10:34.</li>
