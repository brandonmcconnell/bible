## I Will Exalt You, My God and King

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm of praise. Of David.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This psalm is an acrostic poem, each verse beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.">a</a></sup></div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 47:6–8; Psalms 45:6; Psalms 34:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will exalt You, my God and King;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will bless Your name forever and ever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:15; Psalms 71:6; Psalms 119:164"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every day I will bless You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will praise Your name forever and ever.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Romans 11:33; Job 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His greatness is unsearchable.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–7; Psalms 71:18; Deuteronomy 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">One generation will commend Your works to the next,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will proclaim Your mighty acts—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 119:27; Psalms 72:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">the glorious splendor of Your majesty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And I will meditate on Your wondrous works.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Daniel 3:28–29; Psalms 107:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will proclaim the power of Your awesome deeds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will declare Your greatness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 36:10; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will extol the fame of Your abundant goodness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sing joyfully of Your righteousness.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:18; Psalms 103:8; Psalms 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is gracious and compassionate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">slow to anger and abounding in loving devotion.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:7; Psalms 100:5; Matthew 5:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is good to all;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His compassion rests on all He has made.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Psalms 103:22; Revelation 7:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All You have made will give You thanks, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your godly ones will bless You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalms 93:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will tell of the glory of Your kingdom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and speak of Your might,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:10; Psalms 105:1; Matthew 28:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">to make known to men Your mighty acts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the glorious splendor of Your kingdom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Daniel 7:14; Psalms 10:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Your dominion endures through all generations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is faithful in all His words</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and kind in all His actions.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The final two lines are supplied by one MT manuscript, LXX, Syriac, and DSS.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; Psalms 37:24; Psalms 94:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD upholds all who fall</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lifts up all who are bowed down.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 104:27; Acts 17:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The eyes of all look to You,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and You give them their food in season.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 104:28; Psalms 107:9; Psalms 132:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You open Your hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and satisfy the desire of every living thing.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 45:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is righteous in all His ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and kind in all His deeds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 34:18; Deuteronomy 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD is near to all who call on Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to all who call out to Him in truth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Ephesians 3:16–20; John 15:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He fulfills the desires of those who fear Him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He hears their cry and saves them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; John 10:27–29; Romans 8:28–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD preserves all who love Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but all the wicked He will destroy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 71:8; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 150:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">My mouth will declare the praise of the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let every creature bless His holy name</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">forever and ever.</span></span>
</div>
