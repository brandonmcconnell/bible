## Surely God Is Good to Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They mock and speak with malice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with arrogance they threaten oppression.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They set their mouths against the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their tongues strut across the earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So their people</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or His people">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">return to this place</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and drink up waters in abundance.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely You set them on slick ground;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You cast them down into ruin.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">When my heart was grieved</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I was pierced within,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was senseless and ignorant;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was a brute beast before You.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet I am always with You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You hold my right hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You guide me with Your counsel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and later receive me in glory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have I in heaven but You?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And on earth I desire no one besides You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My flesh and my heart may fail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but God is the strength</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew rock">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of my heart</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my portion forever.</span></span>
