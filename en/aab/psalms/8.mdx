## How Majestic Is Your Name!

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. According to Gittith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gittith is probably a musical or liturgical term; here and in Psalms 81 and 84.">a</a></sup>A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 8:9; Psalms 113:2–4; Psalms 148:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, our Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how majestic is Your name in all the earth!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have set Your glory</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">above the heavens.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the mouths of children and infants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have ordained praise</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally You have ordained strength; LXX You have prepared praise; cited in Matthew 21:16">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on account of Your adversaries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to silence the enemy and avenger.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Psalms 33:6; Psalms 111:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I behold Your heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the work of Your fingers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the moon and the stars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which You have set in place—</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:17; Psalms 144:3; Hebrews 2:6–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">what is man that You are mindful of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or the son of man that You care for him?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:7; Hebrews 2:9; Genesis 1:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made him a little lower than the heavenly beings;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Heb. elohim, the heavenly beings; the Greek OT and Heb 2:7 render ‘angels.’ Or than God.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You crowned him with glory and honor.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 1:26; Matthew 28:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made him ruler of the works of Your hands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have placed everything under his feet:</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in 1 Corinthians 15:27 and Hebrews 2:6–8">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">all sheep and oxen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and even the beasts of the field,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–25; Psalms 148:10; Job 38:39–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">the birds of the air and the fish of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all that swim the paths of the seas.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 8:1; Job 11:7; Psalms 104:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, our Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how majestic is Your name in all the earth!</span></span>
</div>
