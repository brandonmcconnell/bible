## The Mighty One Calls

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A Psalm of Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Mighty One, God the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">speaks and summons the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from where the sun rises to where it sets.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">From Zion, perfect in beauty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God shines forth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God approaches and will not be silent!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Consuming fire precedes Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a tempest rages around Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He summons the heavens above,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the earth, that He may judge His people:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Gather to Me My holy ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the heavens proclaim His righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for God Himself is Judge.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or He is a God of justice">a</a></sup>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Hear, O My people, and I will speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Israel, and I will testify against you:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am God, your God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your ascension offerings are ever before Me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have no need for a bull from your stall</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or goats from your pens,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">for every beast of the forest is Mine—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the cattle on a thousand hills.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know every bird in the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the creatures of the field are Mine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I were hungry, I would not tell you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the world is Mine, and the fullness thereof.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do I eat the flesh of bulls,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or drink the blood of goats?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sacrifice a thank offering to God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fulfill your vows to the Most High.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Call upon Me in the day of trouble;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will deliver you, and you will honor Me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">To the wicked, however, God says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“What right have you to recite My statutes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to bear My covenant on your lips?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you hate My instruction</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cast My words behind you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you see a thief, you befriend him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and throw in your lot with adulterers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You unleash your mouth for evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and harness your tongue to deceit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sit and malign your brother;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you slander your own mother’s son.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have done these things, and I kept silent;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you thought I was</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or you thought the ‘I AM’ was">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just like you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But now I rebuke you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and accuse you to your face.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally and I set it in order before your eyes">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now consider this, you who forget God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest I tear you to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with no one to rescue you:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to him who rightly orders his way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will show the salvation of God.”</span></span>
</div>
