## Why Do You Boast of Evil?

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. <span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Psalms 94:4; Psalms 107:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The loving devotion of God endures all day long.</span></span>
