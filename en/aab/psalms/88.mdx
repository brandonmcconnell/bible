## I Cry Out before You

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. For the choirmaster. According to Mahalath Leannoth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mahalath Leannoth is probably a musical or liturgical term; see also Psalms 53:1.">a</a></sup>A Maskil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Maskil is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.">b</a></sup>of Heman the Ezrahite.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD, the God of my salvation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">day and night I cry out before You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">May my prayer come before You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">incline Your ear to my cry.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my soul is full of troubles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my life draws near to Sheol.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am counted among those descending to the Pit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am like a man without strength.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am forsaken among the dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like the slain who lie in the grave,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whom You remember no more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who are cut off from Your care.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have laid me in the lowest Pit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the darkest of the depths.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your wrath weighs heavily upon me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all Your waves have submerged me.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have removed my friends from me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have made me repulsive to them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am confined and cannot escape.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My eyes grow dim with grief.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I call to You daily, O LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I spread out my hands to You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do You work wonders for the Rephaim?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do the departed rise up to praise You?</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can Your loving devotion be proclaimed in the grave,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your faithfulness in Abaddon</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Abaddon means Destruction.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will Your wonders be known in the darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or Your righteousness in the land of oblivion?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">But to You, O LORD, I cry for help;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the morning my prayer comes before You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why, O LORD, do You reject me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why do You hide Your face from me?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">From my youth I was afflicted and near death.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have borne Your terrors; I am in despair.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your wrath has swept over me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your terrors have destroyed me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">All day long they engulf me like water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they enclose me on every side.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have removed my beloved and my friend;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">darkness is my closest companion.</span></span>
</div>
