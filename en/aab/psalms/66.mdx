## Make a Joyful Noise

<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">For the choirmaster. A song. A Psalm.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 98:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make a joyful noise to God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the earth!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing the glory of His name;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">make His praise glorious.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say to God, “How awesome are Your deeds!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So great is Your power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that Your enemies cower before You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the earth bows down to You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they sing praise to You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they sing praise to Your name.”</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and see the works of God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how awesome are His deeds toward mankind.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He turned the sea into dry land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they passed through the waters on foot;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there we rejoiced in Him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rules forever by His power;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His eyes watch the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not let the rebellious exalt themselves.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bless our God, O peoples;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let the sound of His praise be heard.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He preserves our lives</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and keeps our feet from slipping.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For You, O God, have tested us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have refined us like silver.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> <span className="pl pl-1">You led us into the net;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You laid burdens on our backs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You let men ride over our heads;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we went through fire and water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but You brought us into abundance.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will enter Your house with ascension offerings;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will fulfill my vows to You—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">the vows that my lips promised</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my mouth spoke in my distress.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will offer You fatlings as ascension offerings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the fragrant smoke of rams;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will offer bulls and goats.</span>Selah</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come and listen, all you who fear God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will declare what He has done for me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cried out to Him with my mouth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and praised Him with my tongue.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or and His praise was on my tongue">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I had cherished iniquity in my heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Lord would not have listened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God has surely heard;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has attended to the sound of my prayer.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be God, who has not rejected my prayer</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or withheld from me His loving devotion!</span></span>
</div>
