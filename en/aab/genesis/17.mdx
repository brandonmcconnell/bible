## Abraham to Father Many Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 3:20; Luke 1:6"> When Abram was ninety-nine years old, the LORD appeared to him and said, “I am God Almighty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew El-Shaddai">a</a></sup>Walk before Me and be blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; Psalms 105:8–11"> I will establish My covenant between Me and you, and I will multiply you exceedingly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> Then Abram fell facedown, and God said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> “As for Me, this is My covenant with you: You will be the father of many nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> No longer will you be called Abram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Abram means exalted father.">b</a></sup>but your name will be Abraham,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Abraham means father of many.">c</a></sup>for I have made you a father of many nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Romans 4:17">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> I will make you exceedingly fruitful; I will make nations of you, and kings will descend from you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> I will establish My covenant as an everlasting covenant between Me and you and your seed after you, to be your God and the God of your seed after you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Psalms 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> And to you and your seed after you I will give the land where you are residing—all the land of Canaan—as an eternal possession; and I will be their God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Covenant of Circumcision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; Psalms 25:10; Psalms 103:18"> God also said to Abraham, “You must keep My covenant—you and your seed in the generations after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> This is My covenant with you and your seed after you, which you are to keep: Every male among you must be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> You are to circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, and this will be a sign of the covenant between Me and you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> Generation after generation, every male must be circumcised when he is eight days old, including those born in your household and those purchased from a foreigner—even those who are not your offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> Whether they are born in your household or purchased, they must be circumcised. My covenant in your flesh will be an everlasting covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> But if any male is not circumcised, he will be cut off from his people; he has broken My covenant.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> Then God said to Abraham, “As for Sarai your wife, do not call her Sarai, for her name is to be Sarah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Both Sarai and Sarah mean princess; the change in spelling may reflect the difference in dialect between Ur and Canaan.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> And I will bless her and will surely give you a son by her. I will bless her, and she will be the mother of nations; kings of peoples will descend from her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> Abraham fell facedown. Then he laughed and said to himself, “Can a child be born to a man who is a hundred years old? Can Sarah give birth at the age of ninety?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Psalms 41:12; Psalms 4:6"> And Abraham said to God, “O that Ishmael might live under Your blessing!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> But God replied, “Your wife Sarah will indeed bear you a son, and you are to name him Isaac.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Isaac means he laughs.">f</a></sup>I will establish My covenant with him as an everlasting covenant for his seed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> As for Ishmael, I have heard you, and I will surely bless him; I will make him fruitful and multiply him greatly. He will become the father of twelve rulers, and I will make him into a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> But I will establish My covenant with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this time next year.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> When He had finished speaking with Abraham, God went up from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> On that very day Abraham took his son Ishmael and all those born in his household or purchased with his money—every male among the members of Abraham’s household—and he circumcised them, just as God had told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> So Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and his son Ishmael was thirteen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> And all the men of Abraham’s household—both servants born in his household and those purchased from foreigners—were circumcised with him.</span>
</div>
</div>
