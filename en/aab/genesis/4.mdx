## Cain and Abel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> And Adam had relations with his wife Eve, and she conceived and gave birth to Cain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cain sounds like the Hebrew for acquired or brought forth.">a</a></sup><br />“With the help of the LORD I have brought forth a man,” she said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> Later she gave birth to Cain’s brother Abel.<br />Now Abel was a keeper of sheep, while Cain was a tiller of the soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> So in the course of time, Cain brought some of the fruit of the soil as an offering to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> while Abel brought the best portions of the firstborn of his flock.<br />And the LORD looked with favor on Abel and his offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> but He had no regard for Cain and his offering. So Cain became very angry, and his countenance fell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> “Why are you angry,” said the LORD to Cain, “and why has your countenance fallen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> If you do what is right, will you not be accepted? But if you refuse to do what is right, sin is crouching at your door;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. rovets, ‘crouching,’ echoes rabitsu, a demon that lurks at the door in the Akkadian texts of Babylon and Assyria (about 2000 to 500 BC); sin is pictured as a predator crouching at Cain’s threshold, desiring him, which he must master.">b</a></sup>it desires you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or it desires to control you">c</a></sup>but you must master it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> Then Cain said to his brother Abel, “Let us go out to the field.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="SP, LXX, Syriac, and Vulgate; Hebrew Then Cain spoke to his brother Abel.">d</a></sup>And while they were in the field, Cain rose up against his brother Abel and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> And the LORD said to Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?”<br />“I do not know!” he answered. “Am I my brother’s keeper?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> “What have you done?” replied the LORD. “The voice of your brother’s blood cries out to Me from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> Now you are cursed and banished from the ground, which has opened its mouth to receive your brother’s blood from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> When you till the ground, it will no longer yield its produce to you. You will be a fugitive and a wanderer on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> But Cain said to the LORD, “My punishment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or guilt or sin">e</a></sup>is greater than I can bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> Behold, this day You have driven me from the face of the earth, and from Your face I will be hidden; I will be a fugitive and a wanderer on the earth, and whoever finds me will kill me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> “Not so!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="LXX, Vulgate, and Syriac; Hebrew “Very well!” or “Therefore:”">f</a></sup>replied the LORD. “If anyone slays Cain, then Cain will be avenged sevenfold.” And the LORD placed a mark on Cain, so that no one who found him would kill him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> So Cain went out from the presence of the LORD and settled in the land of Nod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Nod means wandering.">g</a></sup>east of Eden.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Descendants of Cain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> And Cain had relations with his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Enoch. Then Cain built a city and named it after his son Enoch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> Now to Enoch was born Irad, and Irad was the father of Mehujael, and Mehujael was the father of Methusael, and Methusael was the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> And Lamech married two women, one named Adah and the other Zillah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremiah 35:9–10; John 8:44"> Adah gave birth to Jabal; he was the father of those who dwell in tents and raise livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> And his brother’s name was Jubal; he was the father of all who play the harp and flute.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And Zillah gave birth to Tubal-cain, a forger of every implement of bronze and iron. And the sister of Tubal-cain was Naamah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> Then Lamech said to his wives:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Adah and Zillah, hear my voice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">wives of Lamech, listen to my speech.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I have slain a man for wounding me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a young man for striking me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:22; Genesis 4:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">If Cain is avenged sevenfold,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then Lamech seventy-sevenfold.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX can be translated as either seventy times seven or seventy-sevenfold; see also Matthew 18:22.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> And to Seth also a son was born, and he called him Enosh.<br />At that time men began to call upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or to proclaim; some translators to invoke or to call themselves by or to profane">j</a></sup>the name of the LORD.</span>
