## The Great Flood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; 2 Peter 2:5–9; Genesis 6:9"> Then the LORD said to Noah, “Go into the ark, you and all your family, because I have found you righteous in this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 8:20; Leviticus 10:10; Leviticus 11:1–47"> You are to take with you seven pairs of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or by sevens; also in verse 3">a</a></sup>every kind of clean animal, a male and its mate; a pair of every kind of unclean animal, a male and its mate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and seven pairs of every kind of bird of the air, male and female, to preserve their offspring on the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> For seven days from now I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe from the face of the earth every living thing I have made.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> And Noah did all that the LORD had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> Now Noah was 600 years old when the floodwaters came upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And Noah and his wife, with his sons and their wives, entered the ark to escape the waters of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The clean and unclean animals, the birds, and everything that crawls along the ground</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> came to Noah to enter the ark, two by two, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And after seven days the floodwaters came upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, on the seventeenth day of the second month, all the fountains of the great deep burst forth, and the floodgates of the heavens were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the rain fell upon the earth for forty days and forty nights.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> On that very day Noah entered the ark, along with his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth, and his wife, and the three wives of his sons—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> they and every kind of wild animal, livestock, crawling creature, bird, and winged creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> They came to Noah to enter the ark, two by two of every creature<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally of all flesh; similarly in verses 16 and 21">b</a></sup>with the breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> And they entered, the male and female of every living thing, as God had commanded Noah. Then the LORD shut him in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> For forty days the flood kept coming on the earth, and the waters rose and lifted the ark high above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> So the waters continued to surge and rise greatly on the earth, and the ark floated on the surface of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> Finally, the waters completely prevailed upon the earth, so that all the high mountains under all the heavens were covered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> The waters rose and covered the mountaintops to a depth of fifteen cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15 cubits is approximately 22.5 feet or 6.9 meters.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> And every living thing that moved upon the earth perished—birds, livestock, animals, every creature that swarms upon the earth, and all mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> Of all that was on dry land, everything that had the breath of life in its nostrils died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And every living thing on the face of the earth was destroyed—man and livestock, crawling creatures and birds of the air; they were blotted out from the earth, and only Noah and those with him in the ark remained.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the waters prevailed upon the earth for 150 days.</span>
</div>
</div>
