## Jacob’s Departure

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 27:46"> So Isaac called for Jacob and blessed him. “Do not take a wife from the Canaanite women,” he commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 25:20; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 24:50"> “Go at once to Paddan-aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, northwest Mesopotamia; also in verses 5, 6, and 7">a</a></sup>to the house of your mother’s father Bethuel, and take a wife from among the daughters of Laban, your mother’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 48:3; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 1:28"> May God Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew El-Shaddai">b</a></sup>bless you and make you fruitful and multiply you, so that you may become a company of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:14; Genesis 12:7; Ephesians 1:3"> And may He give the blessing of Abraham to you and your seed, so that you may possess the land where you dwell as a foreigner, the land God gave to Abraham.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So Isaac sent Jacob to Paddan-aram, to Laban son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, who was the mother of Jacob and Esau.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Esau Marries Mahalath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 27:33; Genesis 28:1"> Now Esau learned that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him to Paddan-aram to take a wife there, commanding him, “Do not marry a Canaanite woman,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Ephesians 6:3; Colossians 3:20"> and that Jacob had obeyed his father and mother and gone to Paddan-aram.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; 1 Samuel 8:6"> And seeing that his father Isaac disapproved of the Canaanite women,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 36:3; Genesis 36:18; Genesis 36:13"> Esau went to Ishmael and married Mahalath, the sister of Nebaioth and daughter of Abraham’s son Ishmael, in addition to the wives he already had.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob’s Ladder

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 32:10"> Meanwhile Jacob left Beersheba and set out for Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Genesis 28:18; Genesis 31:46"> On reaching a certain place, he spent the night there because the sun had set. And taking one of the stones from that place, he put it under his head and lay down to sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:51; Numbers 12:6; Genesis 32:1–2"> And Jacob had a dream about a stairway that rested on the earth with its top reaching up to heaven, and God’s angels were going up and down the stairway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 35:12"> And there at the top<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or there beside him">c</a></sup>the LORD was standing and saying, “I am the LORD, the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. I will give you and your seed the land on which you now lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Acts 3:25; Genesis 18:18"> Your seed will be like the dust of the earth, and you will spread out to the west and east and north and south. All the families of the earth will be blessed through you and your seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 41:10; Jeremiah 1:19"> Look, I am with you, and I will watch over you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land. For I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5; Exodus 15:11"> When Jacob woke up, he said, “Surely the LORD is in this place, and I was unaware of it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:15; Genesis 35:1–13; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> And he was afraid and said, “How awesome is this place! This is none other than the house of God; this is the gate of heaven!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Stone of Bethel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; Genesis 31:45; Genesis 31:13"> Early the next morning, Jacob took the stone that he had placed under his head, and he set it up as a pillar. He poured oil on top of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hosea 12:4–5; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 48:3"> and he called that place Bethel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Bethel means house of God.">d</a></sup>though previously the city had been named Luz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:8; 2 Samuel 15:8; Genesis 31:13"> Then Jacob made a vow, saying, “If God will be with me and watch over me on this journey, and if He will provide me with food to eat and clothes to wear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:31; Deuteronomy 26:17; 2 Samuel 19:24"> so that I may return safely to my father’s house, then the LORD will be my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 14:20; Deuteronomy 14:22–23"> And this stone I have set up as a pillar will be God’s house, and of all that You give me I will surely give You a tenth.”</span>
</div>
</div>
