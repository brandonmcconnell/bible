## Jacob Prepares to Meet Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:4"> Jacob also went on his way, and the angels of God met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 5:14; Joshua 21:38"> When Jacob saw them, he said, “This is the camp of God.” So he named that place Mahanaim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Mahanaim means two camps.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 25:30; Joshua 24:4; Deuteronomy 2:5"> Jacob sent messengers ahead of him to his brother Esau in the land of Seir, the country of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 33:8"> He instructed them, “You are to say to my master Esau, ‘Your servant Jacob says: I have been staying with Laban and have remained there until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:8; Genesis 33:15; Genesis 33:11"> I have oxen, donkeys, flocks, menservants, and maidservants. I have sent this message to inform my master, so that I may find favor in your sight.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 33:1; Genesis 32:8; Genesis 32:11"> When the messengers returned to Jacob, they said, “We went to your brother Esau, and now he is coming to meet you—he and four hundred men with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> In great fear and distress, Jacob divided his people into two camps, as well as the flocks and herds and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Genesis 33:1–3"> He thought, “If Esau comes and attacks one camp, then the other camp can escape.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> Then Jacob declared, “O God of my father Abraham, God of my father Isaac, the LORD who told me, ‘Go back to your country and to your kindred, and I will make you prosper,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I am unworthy of all the kindness and faithfulness You have shown Your servant. Indeed, with only my staff I came across the Jordan, but now I have become two camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 59:1–2; Proverbs 18:19; Matthew 6:13"> Please deliver me from the hand of my brother Esau, for I am afraid that he may come and attack me and the mothers and children with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> But You have said, ‘I will surely make you prosper, and I will make your seed like the sand of the sea, too numerous to count.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> Jacob spent the night there, and from what he had brought with him, he selected a gift for his brother Esau:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> 200 female goats, 20 male goats, 200 ewes, 20 rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> 30 milk camels with their young, 40 cows, 10 bulls, 20 female donkeys, and 10 male donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:8–9; Genesis 32:20; Proverbs 2:11"> He entrusted them to his servants in separate herds and told them, “Go on ahead of me, and keep some distance between the herds.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> He instructed the one in the lead, “When my brother Esau meets you and asks, ‘To whom do you belong, where are you going, and whose animals are these before you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> then you are to say, ‘They belong to your servant Jacob. They are a gift, sent to my lord Esau. And behold, Jacob is behind us.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He also instructed the second, the third, and all those following behind the herds: “When you meet Esau, you are to say the same thing to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> You are also to say, ‘Look, your servant Jacob is right behind us.’” For he thought, “I will appease Esau<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or I will appease his face">b</a></sup>with the gift that is going before me. After that I can face him, and perhaps he will accept me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally perhaps he will lift up my face">c</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So Jacob’s gifts went on before him, while he spent the night in the camp.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob Wrestles with God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> During the night Jacob got up and took his two wives, his two maidservants, and his eleven sons, and crossed the ford of the Jabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He took them and sent them across the stream, along with all his possessions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> So Jacob was left all alone, and there a man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. ish, ‘a man.’ The one who wrestles Jacob is more than a man: Jacob says he has seen God face to face (v. 30), and Hosea calls Him both God and the Angel (Hos 12:3-4). He is the Angel of the LORD, the Son appearing in human form; see note on Gen 16:7.">d</a></sup>wrestled with him until daybreak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> When the man saw that He could not overpower Jacob, He struck the socket of Jacob’s hip and dislocated it as they wrestled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> Then the man said, “Let Me go, for it is daybreak.”<br />But Jacob replied, “I will not let You go unless You bless me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> “What is your name?” the man asked.<br />“Jacob,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> Then the man said, “Your name will no longer be Jacob,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jacob means he grasps the heel or he deceives.">e</a></sup>but Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Israel means he struggles with God.">f</a></sup>because you have struggled with God and with men, and you have prevailed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> And Jacob requested, “Please tell me Your name.”<br />But He replied, “Why do you ask My name?” Then He blessed Jacob there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> So Jacob named the place Peniel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Peniel means the face of God.">g</a></sup>saying, “Indeed, I have seen God face to face, and yet my life was spared.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> The sun rose above him as he passed by Penuel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Penuel is a variant of Peniel; see verse 30.">h</a></sup>and he was limping because of his hip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> Therefore to this day the Israelites do not eat the tendon attached to the socket of the hip, because the socket of Jacob’s hip was struck near that tendon.</span>
</div>
</div>
