## The Defiling of Dinah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Now Dinah, the daughter Leah had borne to Jacob, went out to visit the daughters of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> When Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, the prince of the region, saw her, he took her and lay with her by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> And his soul was drawn to Dinah, the daughter of Jacob. He loved the young girl and spoke to her tenderly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> So Shechem told his father Hamor, “Get me this girl as a wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> Jacob heard that Shechem had defiled his daughter Dinah, but since his sons were with his livestock in the field, he remained silent about it until they returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Meanwhile, Shechem’s father Hamor came to speak with Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> When Jacob’s sons heard what had happened, they returned from the field. They were filled with grief and fury, because Shechem had committed an outrage in Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or against Israel">a</a></sup>by lying with Jacob’s daughter—a thing that should not be done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> But Hamor said to them, “My son Shechem longs for your daughter. Please give her to him as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> Intermarry with us; give us your daughters and take our daughters for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> You may settle among us, and the land will be open to you. Live here, move about freely, and acquire your own property.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Then Shechem said to Dinah’s father and brothers, “Grant me this favor, and I will give you whatever you ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> Demand a high dowry and an expensive gift, and I will give you whatever you ask. Only give me the girl as my wife!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Revenge of Dinah’s Brothers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> But because Shechem had defiled their sister Dinah, Jacob’s sons answered him and his father Hamor deceitfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> “We cannot do such a thing,” they said. “To give our sister to an uncircumcised man would be a disgrace to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> We will consent to this on one condition, that you become circumcised like us—every one of your males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then we will give you our daughters and take your daughters for ourselves. We will dwell among you and become one people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not agree to be circumcised, then we will take our sister and go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Their offer seemed good to Hamor and his son Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> The young man, who was the most respected of all his father’s household, did not hesitate to fulfill this request, because he was delighted with Jacob’s daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> So Hamor and his son Shechem went to the gate of their city and addressed the men of their city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are at peace with us. Let them live and trade in our land; indeed, it is large enough for them. Let us take their daughters in marriage and give our daughters to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But only on this condition will the men agree to dwell with us and be one people: if all our men are circumcised as they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Will not their livestock, their possessions, and all their animals become ours? Only let us consent to them, and they will dwell among us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> All the men who went out of the city gate listened to Hamor and his son Shechem, and every male of the city was circumcised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> Three days later, while they were still in pain, two of Jacob’s sons (Dinah’s brothers Simeon and Levi) took their swords, went into the unsuspecting city, and slaughtered every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with their swords, took Dinah out of Shechem’s house, and went away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> Jacob’s other sons came upon the slaughter and looted the city, because their sister had been defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks and herds and donkeys, and everything else in the city or in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They carried off all their possessions and women and children, and they plundered everything in their houses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble upon me by making me a stench to the Canaanites and Perizzites, the people of this land. We are few in number; if they unite against me and attack me, I and my household will be destroyed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But they replied, “Should he have treated our sister like a prostitute?”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
