## The First Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; John 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> And God said, “Let there be light,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in 2 Corinthians 4:6">b</a></sup>and there was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:13; Genesis 1:18; Ecclesiastes 11:7"> And God saw that the light was good, and He separated the light from the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:5"> God called the light “day,” and the darkness He called “night.”<br />And there was evening, and there was morning—the first day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally day one">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Second Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 33:6; Job 37:18"> And God said, “Let there be an firmament<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or a canopy or a vault; also in verses 7, 8, 14, 15, 17, and 20">d</a></sup>between the waters, to separate the waters from the waters.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 8:28–29; Psalms 148:4; Matthew 8:27"> So God made the firmament and separated the waters beneath it from the waters above. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:5; Genesis 1:19; Genesis 1:10"> God called the firmament “sky.”<br />And there was evening, and there was morning—the second day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Third Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 3:5; Jonah 1:9; Jeremiah 5:22"> And God said, “Let the waters under the sky be gathered into one place, so that the dry land may appear.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 104:31; Genesis 1:4"> God called the dry land “earth,” and the gathering of waters He called “seas.” And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:14–17; Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 147:8"> Then God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants and fruit trees, each bearing fruit with seed according to its kind.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; Matthew 13:24–26"> The earth produced vegetation: seed-bearing plants according to their kinds and trees bearing fruit with seed according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Fourth Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19–20; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jeremiah 31:35"> And God said, “Let there be lights in the firmament of the sky to distinguish between the day and the night, and let them be signs to mark the seasons and days and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And let them serve as lights in the firmament of the sky to shine upon the earth.” And it was so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Deuteronomy 4:19; Psalms 8:3"> God made two great lights: the greater light to rule the day and the lesser light to rule the night. And He made the stars as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Psalms 8:1; Job 38:12"> God set these lights in the firmament of the sky to shine upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 19:6"> to preside over the day and the night, and to separate the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Fifth Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:19; Psalms 104:24–25; 1 Kings 4:33"> And God said, “Let the waters teem with living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth in the open firmament of the sky.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–26; Genesis 1:25; Genesis 8:19"> So God created the great sea creatures and every living thing that moves, with which the waters teemed according to their kinds, and every winged bird after its kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 40:15; Genesis 8:17; Psalms 107:38"> Then God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the waters of the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sixth Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 50:9–10; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 8:19"> And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kinds: livestock, land crawlers, and beasts of the earth according to their kinds.” And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Job 12:8–10; Genesis 2:19–20"> God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that crawls upon the earth according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:24; Psalms 8:4–8"> Then God said, “Let us make man in our image, after our likeness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="God speaks to His heavenly council, the sons of God who were present at creation and shouted for joy (Job 38:7; cf. 1 Kgs 22:19-22). Many read ‘us’ here as the Trinity; the AAB reads it with the council, as at 3:22.">e</a></sup>to rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air, over the livestock, and over all the earth itself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="MT; Syriac and over all the beasts of the earth">f</a></sup>and every creature that crawls upon it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Ephesians 2:10; Genesis 5:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So God created man in His own image;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the image of God He created him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">male and female He created them.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 19:4 and Mark 10:6">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:7; Leviticus 26:9"> God blessed them and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it; rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 9:3; Psalms 104:14–15; Psalms 145:15–16"> Then God said, “Behold, I have given you every seed-bearing plant on the face of all the earth, and every tree whose fruit contains seed. They will be yours for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> And to every beast of the earth and every bird of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth—everything that has the breath of life in it—I have given every green plant for food.” And it was so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Psalms 104:24; Psalms 19:1–2"> And God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good.<br />And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.</span>
</div>
</div>
