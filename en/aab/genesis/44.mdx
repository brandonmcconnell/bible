## Benjamin and the Silver Cup

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 43:16; Genesis 24:2"> Then Joseph instructed his steward: “Fill the men’s sacks with as much food as they can carry, and put each one’s silver in the mouth of his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; Genesis 42:20; Deuteronomy 8:16"> Put my cup, the silver one, in the mouth of the youngest one’s sack, along with the silver for his grain.”<br />So the steward did as Joseph had instructed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> At daybreak, the men were sent on their way with their donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:32; Psalms 35:12; Proverbs 17:13"> They had not gone far from the city when Joseph told his steward, “Pursue the men at once, and when you overtake them, ask, ‘Why have you repaid good with evil?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX includes Why have you stolen my silver cup?">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Leviticus 19:26; 2 Kings 21:6"> Is this not the cup<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Is it not this which">b</a></sup>my master drinks from and uses for divination? What you have done is wicked!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> When the steward overtook them, he relayed these words to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 38:16–18; Genesis 37:18–32; 2 Kings 8:13"> “Why does my lord say these things?” they asked. “Your servants could not possibly do such a thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:35; Romans 13:9"> We even brought back to you from the land of Canaan the silver we found in the mouths of our sacks. Why would we steal silver or gold from your master’s house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:32; Psalms 7:3–5; Job 31:38–40"> If any of your servants is found to have it, he must die, and the rest will become slaves of my lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 22:3; Genesis 44:17; Genesis 44:33"> “As you say,” replied the steward. “But only the one who is found with the cup will be my slave, and the rest of you shall be free of blame.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So each one quickly lowered his sack to the ground and opened it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 44:26–32; Genesis 42:36–38; Genesis 43:14"> The steward searched, beginning with the oldest and ending with the youngest—and the cup was found in Benjamin’s sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; Genesis 37:29–34; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then they all tore their clothes, loaded their donkeys, and returned to the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:18; Genesis 43:25; Genesis 43:16"> When Judah and his brothers arrived at Joseph’s house, he was still there, and they fell to the ground before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:10; Genesis 21:26; Genesis 3:13"> “What is this deed you have done?” Joseph declared. “Do you not know that a man like me can surely divine the truth?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:9; Daniel 9:7; Numbers 32:23"> “What can we say to my lord?” Judah replied. “How can we plead? How can we justify ourselves? God has exposed the iniquity of your servants. We are now my lord’s slaves—both we and the one who was found with the cup.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Samuel 23:3; Genesis 18:25"> But Joseph replied, “Far be it from me to do this. The man who was found with the cup will be my slave. The rest of you may return to your father in peace.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judah Pleads for Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 32:22; Genesis 41:40; Genesis 18:30"> Then Judah approached Joseph and said, “Sir, please let your servant speak personally to my lord. Do not be angry with your servant, for you are equal to Pharaoh himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 43:7; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 42:7–10"> My lord asked his servants, ‘Do you have a father or a brother?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 37:3; Genesis 42:38; Luke 7:12"> And we answered, ‘We have an elderly father and a younger brother, the child of his old age. The boy’s brother is dead. He is the only one of his mother’s sons left, and his father loves him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:15; Genesis 42:20; Amos 9:4"> Then you told your servants, ‘Bring him down to me so that I can see him for myself.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 44:30; Genesis 42:38"> So we said to my lord, ‘The boy cannot leave his father. If he were to leave, his father would die.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 43:5; Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:15–20"> But you said to your servants, ‘Unless your younger brother comes down with you, you will not see my face again.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 42:29–34"> Now when we returned to your servant my father, we relayed your words to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:2; Genesis 43:5"> Then our father said, ‘Go back and buy us some food.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Genesis 43:4–5"> But we answered, ‘We cannot go down there unless our younger brother goes with us. So if our younger brother is not with us, we cannot see the man.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 46:19; Genesis 35:16–18; Genesis 30:22–25"> And your servant my father said to us, ‘You know that my wife bore me two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:33; Genesis 37:13–14; Genesis 42:36"> When one of them was gone, I said: “Surely he has been torn to pieces.” And I have not seen him since.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:14; Deuteronomy 31:17"> Now if you also take this one from me and harm comes to him, you will bring my gray hair down to Sheol in sorrow.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 2 Samuel 18:33; Genesis 44:31"> So if the boy is not with us when I return to your servant, and if my father, whose life is wrapped up in the boy’s life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 44:29; Genesis 37:35; 2 Corinthians 7:10"> sees that the boy is not with us, he will die. Then your servants will have brought the gray hair of your servant our father down to Sheol in sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 43:8–9; Genesis 43:16"> Indeed, your servant guaranteed the boy’s safety to my father, saying, ‘If I do not return him to you, I will bear the guilt before you, my father, all my life.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 John 3:16; Exodus 32:32; Hebrews 7:22"> Now please let your servant stay here as my lord’s slave in place of the boy. Let him return with his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esther 8:6; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 119:143"> For how can I go back to my father without the boy? I could not bear to see the misery that would overwhelm him.”</span>
</div>
</div>
