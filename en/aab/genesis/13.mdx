## Abram and Lot Part Ways

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:9–20; Joshua 10:40; 2 Samuel 24:7"> So Abram went up out of Egypt into the Negev—he and his wife and all his possessions—and Lot was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Psalms 112:1–3; Genesis 26:12–13"> And Abram had become extremely wealthy in livestock and silver and gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:8–9; Genesis 12:6"> From the Negev he journeyed from place to place toward Bethel, until he came to the place between Bethel and Ai where his tent had formerly been pitched,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Genesis 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> to the site where he had built the altar. And there Abram called on the name of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Genesis 25:27; Genesis 4:20"> Now Lot, who was traveling with Abram, also had flocks and herds and tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 36:6–7; Ecclesiastes 5:10–11; 1 Timothy 6:9"> But the land was unable to support both of them while they stayed together, for they had so many possessions that they were unable to coexist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 26:20; Genesis 12:6; James 3:16"> And there was discord between the herdsmen of Abram and the herdsmen of Lot. At that time the Canaanites and the Perizzites were also living in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:6–7; Acts 7:26; Psalms 133:1"> So Abram said to Lot, “Please let there be no contention between you and me, or between your herdsmen and my herdsmen. After all, we are kinsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 34:10; 1 Peter 3:8–12"> Is not the whole land before you? Now separate yourself from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; if you go to the right, I will go to the left.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lot Proceeds toward Sodom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> And Lot looked out and saw that the whole plain of the Jordan, all the way to Zoar, was well watered like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt. (This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> So Lot chose the whole plain of the Jordan for himself and set out toward the east. And Abram and Lot parted company.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram lived in the land of Canaan, but Lot settled in the cities of the plain and pitched his tent toward Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:10; 2 Peter 2:6–8; Jude 1:7"> But the men of Sodom were wicked, sinning greatly against the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Renews the Promise to Abram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> After Lot had departed, the LORD said to Abram, “Now lift up your eyes from the place where you are, and look to the north and south and east and west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> for all the land that you see, I will give to you and your seed forever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Galatians 3:16">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 28:14; Numbers 23:10; Genesis 32:12"> I will make your seed like the dust of the earth, so that if one could count the dust of the earth, then your seed could be counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Numbers 13:17–24"> Get up and walk around the land, through its length and breadth, for I will give it to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:1; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 35:27"> So Abram moved his tent and went to live near the Oaks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Terebinths or Great Trees">b</a></sup>of Mamre at Hebron, where he built an altar to the LORD.</span>
</div>
