## Jacob Flees from Laban

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; Psalms 49:16–17; Job 31:31"> Now Jacob heard that Laban’s sons were saying, “Jacob has taken away all that belonged to our father and built all this wealth at our father’s expense.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 Samuel 18:9–11; Genesis 4:5"> And Jacob saw from the countenance of Laban that his attitude toward him had changed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 32:9; Hebrews 13:5"> Then the LORD said to Jacob, “Go back to the land of your fathers and to your kindred, and I will be with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Jacob sent word and called Rachel and Leah to the field where his flocks were,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:2–3; Genesis 31:53; Genesis 48:15"> and he told them, “I can see from your father’s countenance that his attitude toward me has changed; but the God of my father has been with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 30:29; Ephesians 6:5–8; Titus 2:9–10"> You know that I have served your father with all my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; Genesis 31:29; Numbers 14:22"> And although he has cheated me and changed my wages ten times, God has not allowed him to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 30:32"> If he said, ‘The speckled will be your wages,’ then the whole flock bore speckled offspring. If he said, ‘The streaked will be your wages,’ then the whole flock bore streaked offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:1; Genesis 31:16; Proverbs 13:22"> Thus God has taken away your father’s livestock and given them to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 30:39; Genesis 20:6"> When the flocks were breeding, I saw in a dream that the streaked, spotted, and speckled males were mating with the females.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Genesis 31:13; 1 Samuel 3:6"> In that dream the angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Angel">a</a></sup>of God said to me, ‘Jacob!’<br />And I replied, ‘Here I am.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Deuteronomy 24:15; Genesis 31:42"> ‘Look up,’ he said, ‘and see that all the males that are mating with the flock are streaked, spotted, or speckled; for I have seen all that Laban has done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:3; Genesis 32:9; Genesis 28:12–22"> I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed the pillar and made a solemn vow to Me. Now get up, leave this land at once, and return to your native land.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 2:24; Genesis 29:24"> And Rachel and Leah replied, “Do we have any portion or inheritance left in our father’s house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:15–20; Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:27–30"> Are we not regarded by him as outsiders? Not only has he sold us, but he has certainly squandered what was paid for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:35–1; Genesis 31:9; Psalms 45:10"> Surely all the wealth that God has taken away from our father belongs to us and to our children. So do whatever God has told you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; Genesis 24:61; Genesis 24:10"> Then Jacob got up and put his children and his wives on camels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:1–2; Genesis 27:41; Genesis 35:27–29"> and he drove all his livestock before him, along with all the possessions he had acquired in Paddan-aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, northwest Mesopotamia">b</a></sup>to go to his father Isaac in the land in Canaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 31:30; Hosea 3:4"> Now while Laban was out shearing his sheep, Rachel stole her father’s household idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Moreover, Jacob deceived<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or stole the heart of; also in verses 26 and 27">c</a></sup>Laban the Aramean by not telling him that he was running away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 46:28"> So he fled with all his possessions, crossed the Euphrates,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew the River">d</a></sup>and headed for the hill country of Gilead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Laban Pursues Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:12–13; Genesis 30:36; Exodus 14:5–31"> On the third day Laban was informed that Jacob had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 24:27; Exodus 2:11"> So he took his relatives with him, pursued Jacob for seven days, and overtook him in the hill country of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:50; Genesis 31:29; Genesis 20:3"> But that night God came to Laban the Aramean in a dream and warned him, “Be careful not to say anything to Jacob, either good or bad.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 33:18; Hebrews 11:9"> Now Jacob had pitched his tent in the hill country of Gilead when Laban overtook him, and Laban and his relatives camped there as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:2; Genesis 12:18; Genesis 20:9–10"> Then Laban said to Jacob, “What have you done? You have deceived me and carried off my daughters like captives of war!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Proverbs 26:23–26; Genesis 31:3–5"> Why did you run away secretly and deceive me, without even telling me? I would have sent you away with joy and singing, with tambourines and harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Acts 20:37"> But you did not even let me kiss my grandchildren and my daughters goodbye. Now you have done a foolish thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Genesis 31:42; Genesis 31:24"> I have power to do you great harm, but last night the God of your father said to me, ‘Be careful not to say anything to Jacob, either good or bad.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:24"> Now you have gone off because you long for your father’s house. But why have you stolen my gods?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 31:26–27; Genesis 20:11"> “I was afraid,” Jacob answered, “for I thought you would take your daughters from me by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3–5; Genesis 31:30; Genesis 31:19"> If you find your gods with anyone here, he shall not live! In the presence of our relatives, see for yourself if anything is yours, and take it back.” For Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen the idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:28"> So Laban went into Jacob’s tent, then Leah’s tent, and then the tents of the two maidservants, but he found nothing. Then he left Leah’s tent and entered Rachel’s tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Genesis 31:17"> Now Rachel had taken Laban’s household idols, put them in the saddlebag of her camel, and was sitting on them. And Laban searched everything in the tent but found nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Exodus 20:12; 1 Peter 2:18"> Rachel said to her father, “Sir, do not be angry that I cannot stand up before you; for I am having my period.” So Laban searched but could not find the household idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Genesis 49:7; Numbers 16:15"> Then Jacob became incensed and challenged Laban. “What is my crime?” he said. “For what sin of mine have you so hotly pursued me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Corinthians 6:4–5; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> You have searched all my goods! Have you found anything that belongs to you? Put it here before my brothers and yours, that they may judge between the two of us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Deuteronomy 28:4; Ezekiel 34:2–4"> I have been with you for twenty years now. Your sheep and goats have not miscarried, nor have I eaten the rams of your flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Exodus 22:10–13; Leviticus 22:8"> I did not bring you anything torn by wild beasts; I bore the loss myself. And you demanded payment from me for what was stolen by day or night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:8; Exodus 2:19–22; 1 Peter 5:2–4"> As it was, the heat consumed me by day and the frost by night, and sleep fled from my eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> Thus for twenty years I have served in your household—fourteen years for your two daughters and six years for your flocks—and you have changed my wages ten times!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 31:7; Genesis 31:53; Isaiah 8:13"> If the God of my father, the God of Abraham and the Fear of Isaac, had not been with me, surely by now you would have sent me away empty-handed. But God has seen my affliction and the toil of my hands, and last night He rendered judgment.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob’s Covenant with Laban

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> But Laban answered Jacob, “These daughters are my daughters, these sons are my sons, and these flocks are my flocks! Everything you see is mine! Yet what can I do today about these daughters of mine or the children they have borne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:25–27; Joshua 22:27"> Come now, let us make a covenant, you and I, and let it serve as a witness between you and me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–22"> So Jacob picked out a stone and set it up as a pillar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:5; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:54"> and he said to his relatives, “Gather some stones.” So they took stones and made a mound, and there by the mound they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1"> Laban called it Jegar-sahadutha, and Jacob called it Galeed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Aramaic Jegar-sahadutha and the Hebrew Galeed both mean heap of witnesses.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Joshua 13:8–9; Genesis 31:23"> Then Laban declared, “This mound is a witness between you and me this day.”<br />Therefore the place was called Galeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> It was also called Mizpah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Mizpah means watchtower.">f</a></sup>because Laban said, “May the LORD keep watch between you and me when we are absent from each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Jeremiah 29:23; Micah 1:2"> If you mistreat my daughters or take other wives, although no one is with us, remember that God is a witness between you and me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Laban also said to Jacob, “Here is the mound, and here is the pillar I have set up between you and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 31:48; Genesis 31:44–45"> This mound is a witness, and this pillar is a witness, that I will not go past this mound to harm you, and you will not go past this mound and pillar to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Genesis 16:5; Joshua 24:2"> May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor, the God of their father, judge between us.”<br />So Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 26:30"> Then Jacob offered a sacrifice on the mountain and invited his relatives to eat a meal. And after they had eaten, they spent the night on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 31:28; Deuteronomy 23:5; Genesis 18:33"> Early the next morning, Laban got up and kissed his grandchildren and daughters and blessed them. Then he left to return home.</span>
</div>
</div>
