## Corruption on the Earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> Now when men began to multiply on the face of the earth and daughters were born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> the sons of God saw that the daughters of men were beautiful, and they took as wives whomever they chose.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> So the LORD said, “My Spirit will not contend with man forever,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX and Syriac My Spirit will not remain in man forever">a</a></sup>for he is mortal; his days shall be 120 years.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God went in to the daughters of man, who bore children to them. These were the mighty men who were of old, the men of renown.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The reading of “sons of God” as supernatural beings is the unbroken position of every documented interpreter through c. 221 AD, including 1 Enoch, Jubilees, Philo, Josephus, Justin Martyr, Irenaeus, Clement of Alexandria, Tertullian, and Origen. No alternative was entertained until Julius Africanus (c. 221 AD), who proposed the Sethite reading but did not commit to it. Augustine popularized the Sethite reading in City of God (c. 413–426 AD), after which it became dominant in the Western church. The discovery of the Dead Sea Scrolls and advances in Second Temple Jewish literature have brought the original supernatural reading back into serious academic discussion as the correct reading, given that it is what the biblical authors themselves held.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> Then the LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great upon the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of his heart was altogether evil all the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> And the LORD regretted that He had made man on the earth, and He was grieved in His heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> So the LORD said, “I will blot out man, whom I have created, from the face of the earth—every man and beast and crawling creature and bird of the air—for I am grieved that I have made them.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> This is the account of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generation; Noah walked with God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And God looked upon the earth and saw that it was corrupt; for all living creatures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally all flesh; similarly in verses 13, 17, and 19">c</a></sup>on the earth had corrupted their ways.</span>
</div>

## Preparing the Ark

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> Then God said to Noah, “The end of all living creatures has come before Me, because through them the earth is full of violence. Now behold, I will destroy both them and the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gopher is an unknown kind of tree; possibly cypress or cedar.">d</a></sup>make rooms in the ark and coat it with pitch inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And this is how you are to build it: The ark is to be 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The ark was approximately 450 feet long, 75 feet wide, and 45 feet high (137.2 meters long, 22.9 meters wide, and 13.7 meters high).">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> You are to make a roof<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or skylight or window">f</a></sup>for the ark, finish its walls a cubit from the top,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A cubit is approximately 18 inches or 45.7 centimeters.">g</a></sup>place a door in the side of the ark, and build lower, middle, and upper decks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> And behold, I will bring floodwaters upon the earth to destroy every creature under the heavens that has the breath of life. Everything on the earth will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> But I will establish My covenant with you, and you will enter the ark—you and your sons and your wife and your sons’ wives with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And you are to bring two of every living creature into the ark—male and female—to keep them alive with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Two of every kind of bird and animal and crawling creature will come to you to be kept alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> You are also to take for yourself every kind of food that is eaten and gather it as food for yourselves and for the animals.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> So Noah did everything precisely as God had commanded him.</span>
</div>
</div>
