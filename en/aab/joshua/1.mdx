## God Instructs Joshua

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Deuteronomy 1:38; Deuteronomy 31:23"> Now after the death of His servant Moses, the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ assistant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7; Numbers 27:16–21"> “Moses My servant is dead. Now therefore arise, you and all these people, and cross over the Jordan into the land that I am giving to the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 14:9; Titus 1:2"> I have given you every place where the sole of your foot will tread, just as I promised to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 3:25"> Your territory shall extend from the wilderness and Lebanon to the great River Euphrates—all the land of the Hittites—and west as far as the Great Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Matthew 28:20"> No one shall stand against you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so will I be with you; I will never leave you nor forsake you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 13:5">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9"> Be strong and courageous, for you shall give these people the inheritance of the land that I swore to their fathers I would give them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:8"> Above all, be strong and very courageous. Be careful to observe all the law that My servant Moses commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you may prosper wherever you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 19:14; Colossians 3:16"> This Book of the Law must not depart from your mouth; meditate on it day and night, so that you may be careful to do everything written in it. For then you will prosper and succeed in all you do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Jeremiah 1:7–8; Joshua 1:6–7"> Have I not commanded you to be strong and courageous? Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joshua Takes Charge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1; Hosea 6:2; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Go through the camp and tell the people, ‘Prepare your provisions, for within three days you will cross the Jordan to go in and take possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–22"> But to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–28; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 22:1–4"> “Remember what Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you when he said, ‘The LORD your God will give you rest, and He will give you this land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Exodus 13:18; Revelation 17:4"> Your wives, your young children, and your livestock may remain in the land that Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan. But all your mighty men of valor must be armed for battle to cross over ahead of your brothers and help them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:4; Numbers 32:17–22; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> until the LORD gives them rest as He has done for you, and your brothers also possess the land that the LORD your God is giving them. Then you may return to the land of your inheritance and take possession of that which Moses the servant of the LORD gave you on the east side of the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Deuteronomy 5:27; 1 Peter 2:13–15"> So they answered Joshua, “Everything you have commanded us we will do, and everywhere you send us we will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; 1 Kings 1:37; Joshua 1:9"> Just as we obeyed Moses in all things, so we will obey you. And may the LORD your God be with you, as He was with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9; Ezra 10:4"> Anyone who rebels against your order and does not obey your words, all that you command him, will be put to death. Above all, be strong and courageous!”</span>
</div>
</div>
