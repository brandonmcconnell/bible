## The Circumcision and Passover at Gilgal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Numbers 13:29; 1 Kings 10:5"> Now when all the Amorite kings west of the Jordan and all the Canaanite kings along the coast<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, along the Mediterranean coast; literally along the sea">a</a></sup>heard how the LORD had dried up the waters of the Jordan before the Israelites until they had crossed over,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or until we had crossed over">b</a></sup>their hearts melted and their spirits failed for fear of the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Deuteronomy 10:16; Exodus 4:25"> At that time the LORD said to Joshua, “Make flint knives and circumcise the sons of Israel once again.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally again the second time">c</a></sup>”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Genesis 17:23–27"> So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the sons of Israel at Gibeath-haaraloth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gibeath-haaraloth means the hill of the foreskins.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Deuteronomy 2:16"> Now this is why Joshua circumcised them: All those who came out of Egypt—all the men of war—had died on the journey in the wilderness after they had left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8–9; Hosea 6:6–7; Matthew 12:7"> Though all who had come out were circumcised, none of those born in the wilderness on the journey from Egypt had been circumcised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:23; Deuteronomy 2:14; Hebrews 3:11"> For the Israelites had wandered in the wilderness forty years, until all the nation’s men of war who had come out of Egypt had died, since they did not obey the LORD. So the LORD vowed never to let them see the land He had sworn to their fathers to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 14:31; Deuteronomy 1:39"> And He raised up their sons in their place, and these were the ones Joshua circumcised. Until this time they were still uncircumcised, since they had not been circumcised along the way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 34:25"> And after all the nation had been circumcised, they stayed there in the camp until they were healed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14"> Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Today I have rolled away the reproach of Egypt from you.” So that place has been called Gilgal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gilgal sounds like the Hebrew for roll.">e</a></sup>to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:1–5; Ezekiel 12:3"> On the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, while the Israelites were camped at Gilgal on the plains of Jericho, they kept the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 23:14; Exodus 13:6–7; Leviticus 23:6"> The day after the Passover, on that very day, they ate unleavened bread and roasted grain from the produce of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 16:35; Isaiah 65:13–14; Proverbs 13:22"> And the day after they had eaten from the produce of the land, the manna ceased. There was no more manna for the Israelites, so that year they began to eat the crops of the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Commander of the LORD’s Army

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Numbers 22:23; Numbers 22:31"> Now when Joshua was near Jericho, he looked up and saw a man standing in front of him with a drawn sword in His hand. Joshua approached Him and asked, “Are You for us or for our enemies?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 55:4; Genesis 17:3; Revelation 19:10–14"> “Neither,” He replied. “I have now come as Commander of the LORD’s army.”<br />Then Joshua fell facedown in reverence<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or and paid homage or and worshiped">f</a></sup>and asked Him, “What does my Lord have to say to His servant?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Acts 7:32–33; 2 Peter 1:18"> The Commander of the LORD’s army replied, “Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy.”<br />And Joshua did so.</span>
</div>
</div>
