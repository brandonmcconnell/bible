## Ephraim’s Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 8:15; 2 Kings 2:19–21"> The allotment for the descendants of Joseph extended from the Jordan at Jericho to the waters of Jericho on the east, through the wilderness that goes up from Jericho into the hill country of Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:13; Genesis 28:19; 2 Samuel 16:16"> It went on from Bethel (that is, Luz)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX (See also Joshua 18:13); Hebrew from Bethel to Luz">a</a></sup>and proceeded to the border of the Archites in Ataroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:33; 2 Chronicles 8:5; Joshua 18:13"> Then it descended westward to the border of the Japhletites as far as the border of Lower Beth-horon and on to Gezer, and it ended at the Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea; also in verses 6 and 8">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:14"> So Ephraim and Manasseh, the sons of Joseph, received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 18:13; Joshua 16:2"> This was the territory of the descendants of Ephraim by their clans:<br />The border of their inheritance went from Ataroth-addar in the east to Upper Beth-horon</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:7; Joshua 18:1"> and out toward the Sea. From Michmethath on the north it turned eastward toward Taanath-shiloh and passed by it to Janoah on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:28; Joshua 6:26; Joshua 3:16"> From Janoah it went down to Ataroth and Naarah, and then reached Jericho and came out at the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 17:8–9; Joshua 16:3–6; Joshua 12:17"> From Tappuah the border went westward to the Brook of Kanah and ended at the Sea.<br />This was the inheritance of the clans of the tribe of Ephraim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 17:9"> along with all the cities and villages set apart for the descendants of Ephraim within the inheritance of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:29; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; 1 Kings 9:16"> But they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer. So the Canaanites dwell among the Ephraimites to this day, but they are forced laborers.</span>
</div>
