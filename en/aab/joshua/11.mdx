## Conquest of the Northern Cities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 11:10; Psalms 2:1–4; Judges 4:2"> Now when Jabin king of Hazor heard about these things, he sent word to Jobab king of Madon; to the kings of Shimron and Achshaph;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11"> to the kings of the north in the mountains, in the Arabah south of Chinnereth, in the foothills,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea; also in verse 16">a</a></sup>and in Naphoth-dor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or in the heights of Dor; Naphoth-dor is a variant of Naphath-dor; see Joshua 12:23.">b</a></sup>to the west;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:3; Joshua 18:26; Genesis 31:49"> to the Canaanites in the east and west; to the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites in the hill country; and to the Hivites at the foot of Hermon in the land of Mizpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:12; 1 Samuel 13:5; Genesis 32:12"> So these kings came out with all their armies, a multitude as numerous as the sand on the seashore, along with a great number of horses and chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; Revelation 16:14; Isaiah 8:9"> All these kings joined forces and encamped at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; 2 Samuel 8:4; Judges 20:28"> Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid of them, for by this time tomorrow I will deliver all of them slain before Israel. You are to hamstring their horses and burn up their chariots.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> So by the waters of Merom, Joshua and his whole army came upon them suddenly and attacked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:6; Joshua 19:28; Zechariah 9:2"> and the LORD delivered them into the hand of Israel, who struck them down and pursued them all the way to Greater Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, and eastward as far as the Valley of Mizpeh. They struck them down, leaving no survivors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> Joshua treated them as the LORD had told him; he hamstrung their horses and burned up their chariots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:2"> At that time Joshua turned back and captured Hazor and put its king to the sword, because Hazor was formerly the head of all these kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 10:40; Deuteronomy 20:16"> The Israelites put everyone in Hazor to the sword, devoting them to destruction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering; also in verses 12, 20, and 21.">c</a></sup>Nothing that breathed remained, and Joshua burned down Hazor itself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Deuteronomy 7:2; Numbers 33:50–53"> Joshua captured all these kings and their cities and put them to the sword. He devoted them to destruction, as Moses the LORD’s servant had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18"> Yet Israel did not burn any of the cities built on their mounds, except Hazor, which Joshua burned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9; Joshua 8:27"> The Israelites took for themselves all the plunder and livestock of these cities, but they put all the people to the sword until they had completely destroyed them, not sparing anyone who breathed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Exodus 34:11–13; Acts 20:20"> As the LORD had commanded His servant Moses, so Moses commanded Joshua. That is what Joshua did, leaving nothing undone of all that the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joshua Takes the Whole Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 10:41; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 12:8"> So Joshua took this entire region: the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the western foothills, the Arabah, and the mountains of Israel and their foothills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 11:3; Joshua 13:5"> from Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir, as far as Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon at the foot of Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings and struck them down, putting them to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 14:7–10; Joshua 11:23"> Joshua waged war against all these kings for a long period of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–27"> No city made peace with the Israelites except the Hivites living in Gibeon; all others were taken in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 4:21"> For it was of the LORD to harden<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or stiffen or strengthen">d</a></sup>their hearts to engage Israel in battle, so that they would be set apart for destruction and would receive no mercy, being annihilated as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:2; Numbers 13:22–23; Joshua 15:13–14"> At that time Joshua proceeded to eliminate the Anakim from the hill country of Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and of Israel. Joshua devoted them to destruction, along with their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:4; 1 Chronicles 18:1; Joshua 15:46"> No Anakim were left in the land of the Israelites; only in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod did any survive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 21:44–45; Numbers 26:52–55; Joshua 14:1–15"> So Joshua took the entire land, in keeping with all that the LORD had spoken to Moses. And Joshua gave it as an inheritance to Israel according to the allotments to their tribes. Then the land had rest from war.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
