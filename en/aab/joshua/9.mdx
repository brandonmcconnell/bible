## The Deceit of the Gibeonites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 23:23"> Now when news of this reached all the kings west of the Jordan—those in the hill country, the foothills,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew Shephelah or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea">a</a></sup>and all along the coast of the Great Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea">b</a></sup>toward Lebanon (the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; Psalms 2:1–2"> they came together to wage war against Joshua and Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> But the people of Gibeon, having heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> acted deceptively and set out as envoys,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or set out with provisions">c</a></sup>carrying on their donkeys worn-out sacks and old wineskins, cracked and mended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> They put worn, patched sandals on their feet and threadbare clothing on their bodies, and their whole supply of bread was dry and moldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> They went to Joshua in the camp at Gilgal and said to him and the men of Israel, “We have come from a distant land; please make a treaty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew berit are translated in most passages as covenant; also in verses 7, 11, 15, and 16.">d</a></sup>with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> But the men of Israel said to the Hivites, “Perhaps you dwell near us. How can we make a treaty with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> “We are your servants,” they said to Joshua.<br />Then Joshua asked them, “Who are you and where have you come from?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> “Your servants have come from a very distant land,” they replied, “because of the fame of the LORD your God. For we have heard the reports about Him: all that He did in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> and all that He did to the two kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan—Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> So the elders and inhabitants of our land told us, ‘Take provisions for your journey; go to meet them and say to them: We are your servants. Please make a treaty with us.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> This bread of ours was warm when we packed it at home on the day we left to come to you. But look, it is now dry and moldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> These wineskins were new when we filled them, but look, they are cracked. And these clothes and sandals are worn out from our very long journey.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> Then the men of Israel sampled their provisions but did not seek the counsel of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> And Joshua made a treaty of peace with them to let them live, and the leaders of the congregation swore an oath to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> Three days after they had made the treaty with the Gibeonites, the Israelites learned that they were neighbors, living among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> So the Israelites set out and on the third day arrived at their cities—Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> But the Israelites did not attack them, because the leaders of the congregation had sworn an oath to them by the LORD, the God of Israel. And the whole congregation grumbled against the leaders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> All the leaders answered, “We have sworn an oath to them by the LORD, the God of Israel, and now we cannot touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> This is how we will treat them: We will let them live, so that no wrath will fall on us because of the oath we swore to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> They continued, “Let them live, but let them be woodcutters and water carriers for the whole congregation.” So the leaders kept their promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> Then Joshua summoned the Gibeonites and said, “Why did you deceive us by telling us you live far away from us, when in fact you live among us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> Now therefore you are under a curse and will perpetually serve as woodcutters and water carriers for the house of my God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> The Gibeonites answered, “Your servants were told clearly that the LORD your God had commanded His servant Moses to give you all the land and wipe out all its inhabitants before you. So we greatly feared for our lives because of you, and that is why we have done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> Now we are in your hands. Do to us whatever seems good and right to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So Joshua did this and delivered them from the hands of the Israelites, and they did not kill the Gibeonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> On that day he made them woodcutters and water carriers, as they are to this day for the congregation of the LORD and for the altar at the place He would choose.</span>
</div>
</div>
