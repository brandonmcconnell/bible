## The Prince’s Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 66:23; Ezekiel 44:1–2; Exodus 20:9"> “This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘The gate of the inner court that faces east must be kept shut during the six days of work, but on the Sabbath day and on the day of the New Moon it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:3; Ezekiel 46:8; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> The prince is to enter from the outside through the portico of the gateway and stand by the gatepost, while the priests sacrifice his ascension offerings and well-being offerings. He is to bow in worship at the threshold of the gate and then depart, but the gate must not be shut until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:10; John 10:9; Hebrews 10:19–22"> On the Sabbaths and New Moons the people of the land are also to bow in worship before the LORD at the entrance to that gateway.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; Numbers 28:9–10"> The ascension offering that the prince presents to the LORD on the Sabbath day shall be six unblemished male lambs and an unblemished ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:24; Ezekiel 46:7; Deuteronomy 16:17"> The grain offering with the ram shall be one ephah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="An ephah is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters (probably about 38.3 pounds or 17.4 kilograms of grain); also in verses 7 and 11.">a</a></sup>and the grain offering with the lambs shall be as much as he is able, along with a hin of oil per ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1"> On the day of the New Moon he shall offer a young, unblemished bull, six lambs, and a ram without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5"> He is to provide a grain offering of an ephah with the bull, an ephah with the ram, and as much as he is able with the lambs, along with a hin of oil per ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 44:1–3; Colossians 1:18"> When the prince enters, he shall go in through the portico of the gateway, and he shall go out the same way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; 2 Peter 2:20–21"> When the people of the land come before the LORD at the appointed feasts, whoever enters by the north gate to worship must go out by the south gate, and whoever enters by the south gate must go out by the north gate. No one is to return through the gate by which he entered, but each must go out by the opposite gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> When the people enter, the prince shall go in with them, and when they leave, he shall leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5; Ezekiel 46:7; Numbers 15:1–41"> At the festivals and appointed feasts, the grain offering shall be an ephah with a bull, an ephah with a ram, and as much as one is able to give with the lambs, along with a hin of oil per ephah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 45:17; Ezekiel 44:3"> When the prince makes a freewill offering to the LORD, whether an ascension offering or a well-being offering, the gate facing east must be opened for him. He is to offer his ascension offering or well-being offering just as he does on the Sabbath day. Then he shall go out, and the gate must be closed after he goes out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; John 1:29; Exodus 12:5"> And you shall provide an unblemished year-old lamb as a daily ascension offering to the LORD; you are to offer it every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:5"> You are also to provide with it every morning a grain offering of a sixth of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A sixth of an ephah is approximately 3.3 dry quarts or 3.7 liters (probably about 4.2 pounds or 1.9 kilograms of flour).">b</a></sup>with a third of a hin of oil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or a third of a hin of olive oil; that is, approximately 1.3 quarts or 1.2 liters">c</a></sup>to moisten the fine flour—a grain offering to the LORD. This is a permanent statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1–10; Numbers 28:6; Exodus 29:42"> Thus they shall provide the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil every morning as a regular ascension offering.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Galatians 4:7; Genesis 25:5–6"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons as an inheritance, it will belong to his descendants. It will become their property by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Luke 19:25–26; Galatians 4:30–31"> But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it will belong to that servant until the year of freedom; then it will revert to the prince. His inheritance belongs only to his sons; it shall be theirs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:8; 1 Kings 21:19; Ezekiel 22:27"> The prince must not take any of the inheritance of the people by evicting them from their property. He is to provide an inheritance for his sons from his own property, so that none of My people will be displaced from his property.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Courts for Boiling and Baking

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:9; Ezekiel 44:4–5; Ezekiel 40:44–46"> Then the man brought me through the entrance at the side of the gate into the holy chambers facing north, which belonged to the priests, and he showed me a place there at the far western end</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Ezekiel 44:29; Ezekiel 44:19"> and said to me, “This is the place where the priests shall boil the reparation offering and the purification offering, and where they shall bake the grain offering, so that they do not bring them into the outer court and transmit holiness to the people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he brought me into the outer court and led me around to its four corners, and I saw a separate court in each of its corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the outer court there were enclosed courts, each forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The enclosed courts were approximately 70 feet long and 52.5 feet wide (21.3 meters long and 16 meters wide).">d</a></sup>Each of the four corner areas had the same dimensions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Around the inside of each of the four courts was a row of masonry with ovens built at the base of the walls on all sides.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2"> And he said to me, “These are the kitchens where those who minister at the temple will cook the sacrifices offered by the people.”</span>
</div>
</div>
