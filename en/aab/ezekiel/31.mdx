## Egypt Will Fall like Assyria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Jeremiah 52:5–6; Ezekiel 1:2"> In the eleventh year, on the first day of the third month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 31:18"> “Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his multitude:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Who can be compared</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to your greatness?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; Daniel 4:10; Daniel 4:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look at Assyria, a cedar in Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with beautiful branches that shaded the forest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It towered on high;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its top was among the clouds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 17:15; Proverbs 14:28; Ezekiel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters made it grow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the deep springs made it tall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">directing their streams all around its base</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sending their channels to all the trees of the field.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:11; Ezekiel 17:5; Psalms 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore it towered higher</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than all the trees of the field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its branches multiplied,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its boughs grew long</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as it spread them out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the abundant waters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:23; Matthew 13:32; Daniel 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the birds of the air</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nested in its branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the beasts of the field</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">gave birth beneath its boughs;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the great nations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lived in its shade.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was beautiful in its greatness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the length of its limbs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for its roots extended</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to abundant waters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cedars in the garden of God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">could not rival it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the cypresses</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or pines or junipers or firs">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">could not compare with its branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor the plane trees</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Possibly chestnut or juniper">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">match its boughs.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No tree in the garden of God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">could compare with its beauty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Daniel 2:37–38; Genesis 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I made it beautiful with its many branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the envy of all the trees of Eden,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which were in the garden of God.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:17; Daniel 5:20; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Since it became great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew you became great">c</a></sup>in height and set its top among the clouds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or through the thick boughs">d</a></sup>and it grew proud on account of its height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11–12; Nahum 3:18; Daniel 5:18–19"> I delivered it into the hand of the ruler of the nations, for him to deal with it according to its wickedness. I have banished it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 35:8; Ezekiel 30:11"> Foreigners, the most ruthless of the nations, cut it down and left it. Its branches have fallen on the mountains and in every valley; its boughs lay broken in all the earth’s ravines. And all the peoples of the earth left its shade and abandoned it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:4; Ezekiel 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> All the birds of the air nested on its fallen trunk, and all the beasts of the field lived among its boughs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 Peter 2:6"> This happened so that no other trees by the waters would become great in height and set their tops among the clouds, and no other well-watered trees would reach them in height. For they have all been consigned to death, to the depths of the earth, among the mortals who descend to the Pit.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 3:4; Nahum 2:8–10; Revelation 18:9–11"> This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘On the day it was brought down to Sheol, I caused mourning. I covered the deep because of it; I held back its rivers; its abundant waters were restrained. I made Lebanon mourn for it, and all the trees of the field fainted because of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; Ezekiel 26:15"> I made the nations quake at the sound of its downfall, when I cast it down to Sheol with those who descend to the Pit.<br />Then all the trees of Eden, the choicest and best of Lebanon, all the well-watered trees, were filled with regret in the earth below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Ezekiel 31:6; Ezekiel 31:3"> They too descended with it to Sheol, to those slain by the sword. As its allies they had lived in its shade among the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:19; Ezekiel 32:21; Ezekiel 28:10"> Who then is like you in glory and greatness among the trees of Eden? You also will be brought down to the depths of the earth to be with the trees of Eden. You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those slain by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude, declares the Lord GOD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
