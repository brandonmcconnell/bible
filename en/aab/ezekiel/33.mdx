## Ezekiel the Watchman for Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:30; Ezekiel 33:17; Ezekiel 3:11"> “Son of man, speak to your people and tell them: ‘Suppose I bring the sword against a land, and the people of that land choose a man from among them, appointing him as their watchman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Nehemiah 4:20; Nehemiah 4:18"> and he sees the sword coming against that land and blows the ram’s horn to warn the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:13; Acts 18:6; Jeremiah 6:17"> Then if anyone hears the sound of the horn but fails to heed the warning, and the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 9:19–21; Hebrews 11:7; John 8:39"> Since he heard the sound of the horn but failed to heed the warning, his blood will be on his own head. If he had heeded the warning, he would have saved his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18–20; Ezekiel 33:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> But if the watchman sees the sword coming and fails to blow the horn to warn the people, and the sword comes and takes away a life, then that one will be taken away in his iniquity, but I will hold the watchman accountable for his blood.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:17–21; Jeremiah 26:2; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> As for you, O son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel; so hear the word from My mouth and give them the warning from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 20:26–27; Ezekiel 33:6; Ezekiel 33:14"> If I say to the wicked, ‘O wicked man, you will surely die,’ but you do not speak out to dissuade him from his way, then that wicked man will die in his iniquity, yet I will hold you accountable for his blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:19; Acts 20:26; Proverbs 15:10"> But if you warn the wicked man to turn from his way, and he does not turn from it, he will die in his iniquity, but you will have saved your life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Acts 20:25–26">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Message of the Watchman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:14; Ezekiel 37:11; Ezekiel 24:23"> Now as for you, son of man, tell the house of Israel that this is what they have said: ‘Our transgressions and our sins are heavy upon us, and we are wasting away because of them! How can we live?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Ezekiel 18:23; 2 Peter 3:9"> Say to them: ‘As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather that the wicked should turn from their ways and live. Turn! Turn from your evil ways! For why should you die, O house of Israel?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Ezekiel 33:18–19; Romans 3:25"> Therefore, son of man, say to your people: ‘The righteousness of the righteous man will not deliver him in the day of his transgression; neither will the wickedness of the wicked man cause him to stumble on the day he turns from his wickedness. Nor will the righteous man be able to survive by his righteousness on the day he sins.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 10:38; Ezekiel 18:4"> If I tell the righteous man that he will surely live, but he then trusts in his righteousness and commits iniquity, then none of his righteous works will be remembered; he will die because of the iniquity he has committed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27; Jeremiah 18:7–8; Hosea 14:1"> But if I tell the wicked man, ‘You will surely die,’ and he turns from his sin and does what is just and right—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Luke 19:8; Exodus 22:1–4"> if he restores a pledge, makes restitution for what he has stolen, and walks in the statutes of life without practicing iniquity—then he will surely live; he will not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Ezekiel 18:22; Micah 7:18–19"> None of the sins he has committed will be held against him. He has done what is just and right; he will surely live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–26; Ezekiel 18:25; Ezekiel 18:29"> Yet your people say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ But it is their way that is not just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:12–13; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:38"> If a righteous man turns from his righteousness and commits iniquity, he will die for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Ezekiel 33:14"> But if a wicked man turns from his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will live because of this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Ezekiel 18:25; Ezekiel 33:17"> Yet you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ But I will judge each of you according to his ways, O house of Israel.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Word of Jerusalem’s Fall

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:1–8; 2 Kings 25:4"> In the twelfth year of our exile, on the fifth day of the tenth month, a fugitive from Jerusalem came to me and reported, “The city has been taken!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:3; Ezekiel 3:26–27; Ezekiel 24:26–27"> Now the evening before the fugitive arrived, the hand of the LORD was upon me, and He opened my mouth before the man came to me in the morning. So my mouth was opened and I was no longer mute.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Isaiah 51:2; Acts 7:5"> “Son of man, those living in the ruins in the land of Israel are saying, ‘Abraham was only one man, yet he possessed the land. But we are many; surely the land has been given to us as a possession.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Jeremiah 7:9–10; Genesis 9:4"> Therefore tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘You eat meat with the blood in it, lift up your eyes to your idols, and shed blood. Should you then possess the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:40; Micah 2:1–2; Zephaniah 3:3"> You have relied on your swords, you have committed detestable acts, and each of you has defiled his neighbor’s wife. Should you then possess the land?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; Jeremiah 42:22; Ezekiel 39:4"> Tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘As surely as I live, those in the ruins will fall by the sword, those in the open field I will give to be devoured by wild animals, and those in the strongholds and caves will die by plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:24; Jeremiah 44:2; Jeremiah 44:6"> I will make the land a desolate waste, and the pride of her strength will come to an end. The mountains of Israel will become desolate, so that no one will pass through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 36:17–18; Ezekiel 22:2–15"> Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I have made the land a desolate waste because of all the abominations they have committed.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 22:16–17; Jeremiah 23:35; Isaiah 29:13"> As for you, son of man, your people are talking about you near the city walls and in the doorways of their houses. One speaks to another, each saying to his brother, ‘Come and hear the message that has come from the LORD!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 13:22; Psalms 78:36–37; James 1:22–24"> So My people come to you as usual, sit before you, and hear your words; but they do not put them into practice. Although they express love with their mouths, their hearts pursue dishonest gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Mark 4:16–17; John 5:35"> Indeed, you are to them like a singer of love songs with a beautiful voice, who skillfully plays an instrument. They hear your words but do not put them into practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:9; 1 Samuel 3:19–20; Ezekiel 2:5"> So when it comes to pass—and surely it will come—then they will know that a prophet has been among them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
