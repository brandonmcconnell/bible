## A Prophecy against Tyre

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:2"> In the eleventh month of the twelfth year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Likely reading of the original Hebrew text; MT In the eleventh year">a</a></sup>on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Ezekiel 36:2; Joel 3:4"> “Son of man, because Tyre has said of Jerusalem, ‘Aha! The gate to the nations is broken; it has swung open to me; now that she lies in ruins I will be filled,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Isaiah 5:30; Micah 4:11"> therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, O Tyre, I am against you, and I will raise up many nations against you, as the sea brings up its waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 23:11; Amos 1:10; Ezekiel 26:9"> They will destroy the walls of Tyre and demolish her towers. I will scrape the soil from her and make her a bare rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 27:32; Ezekiel 25:7"> She will become a place to spread nets in the sea, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD. She will become plunder for the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Ezekiel 26:8; Jeremiah 49:2"> and the villages on her mainland will be slain by the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 7:12; Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 23:24"> For this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, I will bring against Tyre from the north Nebuchadnezzar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew Nebuchadrezzar, a variant of Nebuchadnezzar (king of Babylon). The latter spelling is used throughout Ezekiel for consistency.">b</a></sup>king of Babylon, king of kings, with horses and chariots, with cavalry and a great company of troops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 52:4; Jeremiah 32:24"> He will slaughter the villages of your mainland with the sword; he will set up siege works against you, build a ramp to your walls, and raise his shields against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:15"> He will direct the blows of his battering rams against your walls and tear down your towers with his axes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Ezekiel 27:28; Jeremiah 47:3"> His multitude of horses will cover you in their dust.<br />When he enters your gates as an army entering a breached city, your walls will shake from the noise of cavalry, wagons, and chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Isaiah 26:5; Habakkuk 1:8"> The hooves of his horses will trample all your streets. He will slaughter your people with the sword, and your mighty pillars will fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 23:8; 2 Chronicles 32:27; Zechariah 9:3–4"> They will plunder your wealth and pillage your merchandise. They will demolish your walls, tear down your beautiful homes, and throw your stones and timber and soil into the water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 23:16; Isaiah 14:11"> So I will silence the sound of your songs, and the music of your lyres will no longer be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; Job 12:14; Numbers 23:19"> I will make you a bare rock, and you will become a place to spread the fishing nets. You will never be rebuilt, for I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:35; Jeremiah 49:21; Ezekiel 26:18"> This is what the Lord GOD says to Tyre: ‘Will not the coastlands quake at the sound of your downfall, when the wounded groan at the slaughter in your midst?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Jonah 3:6; Job 8:22"> All the princes of the sea will descend from their thrones, remove their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments. Clothed with terror, they will sit on the ground, trembling every moment, appalled over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12; Ezekiel 19:1; 2 Samuel 1:25–27"> Then they will lament for you, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“How you have perished, O city of renown</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">inhabited by seafaring men—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">she who was powerful on the sea, along with her people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who imposed terror on all peoples!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or on all her inhabitants">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Isaiah 23:5–7; Ezekiel 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now the coastlands tremble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the day of your downfall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the islands in the sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are dismayed by your demise.”’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 26:3; Daniel 11:40"> For this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘When I make you a desolate city like other deserted cities, and when I raise up the deep against you so that the mighty waters cover you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Luke 10:15; Zechariah 2:8"> then I will bring you down with those who descend to the Pit, to the people of antiquity. I will make you dwell in the earth below like the ancient ruins, with those who descend to the Pit, so that you will no longer be inhabited or set in splendor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX or take your place">d</a></sup>in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:36; Ezekiel 28:19; Revelation 18:21"> I will make you an object of horror, and you will be no more. You will be sought, but will never be found,’ declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
</div>
