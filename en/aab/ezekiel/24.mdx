## The Parable of the Cooking Pot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 29:17"> In the ninth year, on the tenth day of the tenth month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1; Jeremiah 52:4; 2 Kings 25:1"> “Son of man, write down today’s date, for on this very day the king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 17:2; Ezekiel 20:49"> Now speak a parable to this rebellious house and tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Put the pot on the fire;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">put it on and pour in the water.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Ezekiel 22:18–22; Matthew 7:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Put in the pieces of meat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every good piece—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">thigh and shoulder—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">fill it with choice bones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 39:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take the choicest of the flock</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pile the fuel beneath it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Bring it to a boil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cook the bones in it.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Nahum 3:1; Obadiah 1:11"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Woe to the city of bloodshed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the pot now rusted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose rust will not come off!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Empty it piece by piece;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cast no lots for its contents.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or let no lot fall upon it">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 17:13; Deuteronomy 12:16; Job 16:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the blood she shed is still within her;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she poured it out on the bare rock;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">she did not pour it on the ground</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to cover it with dust.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21–22; Revelation 18:5–10; Ezekiel 22:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">In order to stir up wrath</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take vengeance,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have placed her blood on the bare rock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that it would not be covered.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6; Habakkuk 2:12; Isaiah 31:9"> Yes, this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Woe to the city of bloodshed!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I, too, will pile the kindling high.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Jeremiah 17:3; Lamentations 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pile on the logs and kindle the fire;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cook the meat well</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and mix in the spices;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let the bones be burned.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Set the empty pot on its coals</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until it becomes hot and its copper glows.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then its impurity will melt within;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its rust will be consumed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:5; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It has frustrated every effort;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its thick rust has not been removed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even by the fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:24; Ezekiel 5:13; Zephaniah 3:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of the indecency of your uncleanness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I tried to cleanse you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you would not be purified</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from your filthiness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will not be pure again</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until My wrath against you has subsided.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, the LORD, have spoken;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the time is coming, and I will act.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not refrain or show pity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor will I relent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will judge you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">according to your ways and deeds,’</span>declares the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>

## Ezekiel’s Wife Dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10; Jeremiah 13:17; Song of Solomon 7:10"> “Son of man, behold, I am about to take away the desire of your eyes with a fatal blow. But you must not mourn or weep or let your tears flow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:4; 2 Samuel 15:30; Leviticus 10:6"> Groan quietly; do not mourn for the dead. Put on your turban and strap your sandals on your feet; do not cover your lips or eat the bread of mourners.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:29–30"> So I spoke to the people in the morning, and in the evening my wife died. And the next morning I did as I had been commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:9; Ezekiel 37:18; Malachi 3:7–8"> Then the people asked me, “Won’t you tell us what these things you are doing mean to us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So I answered them, “The word of the LORD came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 23:25; Psalms 27:4"> Tell the house of Israel that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘I am about to desecrate My sanctuary, the pride of your power, the desire of your eyes, and the delight of your soul. And the sons and daughters you left behind will fall by the sword.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 16:4–7; Ezekiel 24:16–17"> Then you will do as I have done: You will not cover your lips or eat the bread of mourners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:10; Ezekiel 4:17; Leviticus 26:39"> Your turbans will remain on your heads and your sandals on your feet. You will not mourn or weep, but you will waste away because of your sins, and you will groan among yourselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:3; Isaiah 20:3; Luke 11:29–30"> ‘Thus Ezekiel will be a sign for you; you will do everything that he has done. When this happens, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Psalms 48:2; Jeremiah 11:22"> And you, son of man, know that on the day I take away their stronghold, their pride and joy—the desire of their eyes which uplifted their souls—and their sons and daughters as well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; Ezekiel 33:21–22; 1 Samuel 4:12–18"> on that day a fugitive will come and tell you the news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:22; Ezekiel 29:21; Luke 21:15"> On that day your mouth will be opened to him who has escaped; you will speak and no longer be mute. So you will be a sign to them, and they will know that I am the LORD.”</span>
</div>
</div>
