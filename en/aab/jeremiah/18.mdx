## The Potter and the Clay

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> “Go down at once to the potter’s house, and there I will give you My message.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> So I went down to the potter’s house and saw him working at the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> But the vessel that he was shaping from the clay became flawed in his hand; so he formed it into another vessel, as it seemed best for him to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> “O house of Israel, declares the LORD, can I not treat you as this potter treats his clay? Just like clay in the potter’s hand, so are you in My hand, O house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> At any time I might announce that a nation or kingdom will be uprooted, torn down, and destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> But if that nation I warned turns from its evil, then I will relent of the disaster I had planned to bring.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> And if at another time I announce that I will build up and establish a nation or kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> and if it does evil in My sight and does not listen to My voice, then I will relent of the good I had intended for it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> Now therefore, tell the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem that this is what the LORD says: ‘Behold, I am planning a disaster for you and devising a plan against you. Turn now, each of you, from your evil ways, and correct your ways and deeds.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> But they will reply, ‘It is hopeless. We will follow our own plans, and each of us will act according to the stubbornness of his evil heart.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> Therefore this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Inquire among the nations:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who has ever heard things like these?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Virgin Israel has done a most terrible thing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does the snow of Lebanon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">ever leave its rocky slopes?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Or do its cool waters flowing from a distance</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">ever run dry?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet My people have forgotten Me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They burn incense to worthless idols</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that make them stumble in their ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">leaving the ancient roads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to walk on rutted bypaths</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead of on the highway.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have made their land a desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a perpetual object of scorn;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all who pass by will be appalled</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shake their heads.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will scatter them before the enemy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like the east wind.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will show them My back and not My face</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their calamity.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Another Plot against Jeremiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> Then some said, “Come, let us make plans against Jeremiah, for the law will never be lost to the priest, nor counsel to the wise, nor an oracle to the prophet. Come, let us denounce him and pay no heed to any of his words.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Attend to me, O LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Hear what my accusers are saying!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should good be repaid with evil?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yet they have dug a pit for me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Remember how I stood before You</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to speak good on their behalf,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to turn Your wrath from them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, hand their children over to famine;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">pour out the power of the sword upon them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let their wives become childless and widowed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let their husbands be slain by disease,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their young men struck down by the sword in battle.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let a cry be heard from their houses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when You suddenly bring raiders against them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they have dug a pit to capture me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have hidden snares for my feet.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But You, O LORD, know all their deadly plots against me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not wipe out their guilt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or blot out their sin from Your sight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them be overthrown before You;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">deal with them in the time of Your anger.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
