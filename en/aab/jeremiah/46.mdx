## Judgment on Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> This is the word of the LORD about the nations—the word that came to Jeremiah the prophet</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> concerning Egypt and the army of Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt, which was defeated at Carchemish on the Euphrates River by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Deploy your shields, small and large;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">advance for battle!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Harness the horses; mount the steeds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">take your positions with helmets on!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Polish your spears;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">put on armor!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why am I seeing this?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are terrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are retreating;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their warriors are defeated,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they flee in haste without looking back;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">terror is on every side!”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The swift cannot flee,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the warrior cannot escape!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the north by the River Euphrates</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they stumble and fall.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who is this, rising like the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like rivers whose waters churn?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt rises like the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its waters churn like rivers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">boasting, ‘I will rise and cover the earth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will destroy the cities and their people.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Advance, O horses! Race furiously, O chariots!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let the warriors come forth—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cush</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the upper Nile region">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Put carrying their shields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">men of Lydia drawing the bow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For that day belongs to the Lord GOD of Hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of vengeance against His foes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sword will devour until it is satisfied,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until it is quenched with their blood.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord GOD of Hosts will hold a sacrifice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the land of the north by the River Euphrates.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up to Gilead for balm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O Virgin Daughter of Egypt!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In vain you try many remedies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but for you there is no healing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations have heard of your shame,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your outcry fills the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because warrior stumbles over warrior</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and both of them have fallen together.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> This is the word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet about the coming of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to strike the land of Egypt:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Announce it in Egypt, and proclaim it in Migdol;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">proclaim it in Memphis</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew Noph; also in verse 19">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Tahpanhes:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Take your positions and prepare yourself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the sword devours those around you.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why have your warriors been laid low?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They cannot stand, for the LORD has thrust them down.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They continue to stumble;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">indeed, they have fallen over one another.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They say, ‘Get up! Let us return to our people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to the land of our birth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">away from the sword of the oppressor.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">There they will cry out:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Pharaoh king of Egypt was all noise;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he has let the appointed time pass him by.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">As surely as I live, declares the King,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose name is the LORD of Hosts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there will come one who is like Tabor among the mountains</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and like Carmel by the sea.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pack your bags for exile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O daughter dwelling in Egypt!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Memphis will be laid waste,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">destroyed and uninhabited.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt is a beautiful heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a gadfly from the north is coming against her.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even the mercenaries among her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are like fattened calves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They too will turn back;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">together they will flee, they will not stand their ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the day of calamity is coming upon them—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the time of their punishment.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt will hiss like a fleeing serpent,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew nachash; translated in most cases as snake">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the enemy will advance in force;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with axes they will come against her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like woodsmen cutting down trees.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will chop down her forest, declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">dense though it may be,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they are more numerous than locusts;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they cannot be counted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Daughter of Egypt will be put to shame;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she will be delivered into the hands of the people of the north.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Behold, I am about to punish Amon god of Thebes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew Amon of No">d</a></sup>along with Pharaoh, Egypt with her gods and kings, and those who trust in Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> I will deliver them into the hands of those who seek their lives—of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his officers. But after this, Egypt will be inhabited as in days of old, declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, O Jacob My servant, do not be afraid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not be dismayed, O Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will surely save you out of a distant place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your descendants from the land of their captivity!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jacob will return to quiet and ease,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with no one to make him afraid.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">And you, My servant Jacob, do not be afraid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD, for I am with you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Though I will completely destroy all the nations to which I have banished you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not completely destroy you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet I will discipline you justly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will by no means leave you unpunished.”</span></span>
</div>
