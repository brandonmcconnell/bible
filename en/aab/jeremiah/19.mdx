## The Broken Jar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:11–12; Isaiah 30:14; Lamentations 4:2"> This is what the LORD says: “Go and buy a clay jar from a potter. Take some of the elders of the people and leaders of the priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; 2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 7:31–32"> and go out to the Valley of Ben-hinnom near the entrance of the Potsherd Gate.<br />Proclaim there the words I speak to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; Jeremiah 17:20; Isaiah 28:19"> saying, ‘Hear the word of the LORD, O kings of Judah and residents of Jerusalem. This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am going to bring such disaster on this place that the ears of all who hear of it will ring,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Deuteronomy 28:20; 2 Kings 21:16"> because they have abandoned Me and made this a foreign place. They have burned incense in this place to other gods that neither they nor their fathers nor the kings of Judah have ever known. They have filled this place with the blood of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:35; Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 18:21"> They have built high places to Baal on which to burn their children in the fire as offerings to Baal—something I never commanded or mentioned, nor did it even enter My mind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; Isaiah 30:33; Jeremiah 19:2"> So behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when this place will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Jeremiah 16:4; Psalms 33:10–11"> And in this place I will ruin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew term for ruin sounds like the Hebrew for jar; see verses 1 and 10.">a</a></sup>the plans of Judah and Jerusalem. I will make them fall by the sword before their enemies, by the hands of those who seek their lives, and I will give their carcasses as food to the birds of the air and the beasts of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:13; 1 Kings 9:8"> I will make this city a desolation and an object of scorn.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally and a hissing">b</a></sup>All who pass by will be appalled and will scoff at all her wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Leviticus 26:29; Lamentations 4:10"> I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and daughters, and they will eat one another’s flesh in the siege and distress inflicted on them by their enemies who seek their lives.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Jeremiah 48:12; Jeremiah 19:1"> Then you are to shatter the jar in the presence of the men who accompany you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Isaiah 30:14; Lamentations 4:2"> and you are to proclaim to them that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: I will shatter this nation and this city, like one shatters a potter’s jar that can never again be repaired. They will bury the dead in Topheth until there is no more room to bury them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 11:5"> This is what I will do to this place and to its residents, declares the LORD. I will make this city like Topheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:29; 2 Kings 23:12; Zephaniah 1:5"> The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be defiled like that place, Topheth—all the houses on whose rooftops they burned incense to all the host of heaven and poured out drink offerings to other gods.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:2; 2 Chronicles 20:5; Jeremiah 19:2–3"> Then Jeremiah returned from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the courtyard of the house of the LORD and proclaimed to all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 17:23; Nehemiah 9:29"> “This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Behold, I am about to bring on this city and on all the villages around it every disaster I have pronounced against them, because they have stiffened their necks so as not to heed My words.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
