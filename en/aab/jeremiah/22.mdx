## A Warning to Judah’s Kings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; 2 Samuel 24:11–12"> This is what the LORD says: “Go down to the palace of the king of Judah and proclaim this message there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; Jeremiah 22:4"> saying, ‘Hear the word of the LORD, O king of Judah, who sits on the throne of David—you and your officials and your people who enter these gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:17"> This is what the LORD says: Administer justice and righteousness. Rescue the victim of robbery from the hand of his oppressor. Do no wrong or violence to the foreigner, the fatherless, or the widow. Do not shed innocent blood in this place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25"> For if you will indeed carry out these commands, then kings who sit on David’s throne will enter through the gates of this palace riding on chariots and horses—they and their officials and their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> But if you do not obey these words, then I swear by Myself, declares the LORD, that this house will become a pile of rubble.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning about the Palace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 27:10"> For this is what the LORD says concerning the house of the king of Judah:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are like Gilead to Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like the summit of Lebanon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but I will surely turn you into a desert,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like cities that are uninhabited.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Isaiah 10:33–34; Jeremiah 4:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will appoint destroyers against you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each man with his weapons,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will cut down the choicest of your cedars</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and throw them into the fire.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; 2 Chronicles 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> And many nations will pass by this city and ask one another, ‘Why has the LORD done such a thing to this great city?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 2 Chronicles 34:25; Jeremiah 50:7"> Then people will reply, ‘Because they have forsaken the covenant of the LORD their God and have worshiped and served other gods.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning about Shallum

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremiah 22:11; Isaiah 57:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not weep for him who is dead;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not mourn his loss.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Weep bitterly for him who is exiled,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for he will never return</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to see his native land.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Chronicles 36:1–4; 2 Kings 23:34"> For this is what the LORD says concerning Shallum<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Shallum was also called Jehoahaz.">a</a></sup>son of Josiah, king of Judah, who succeeded his father Josiah but has gone forth from this place: “He will never return,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> but he will die in the place to which he was exiled; he will never see this land again.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning about Jehoiakim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Micah 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Woe to him who builds his palace by unrighteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his upper rooms without justice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who makes his countrymen serve without pay,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fails to pay their wages,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">who says, ‘I will build myself a great palace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with spacious upper rooms.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So he cuts windows in it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">panels it with cedar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and paints it with vermilion.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does it make you a king to excel</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or because you enclose yourself">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in cedar?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Did not your father have food and drink?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He administered justice and righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and so it went well with him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Isaiah 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He took up the cause of the poor and needy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and so it went well with him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is this not what it means to know Me?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Jeremiah 22:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“But your eyes and heart are set on nothing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">except your own dishonest gain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on shedding innocent blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on practicing extortion and oppression.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:30; Jeremiah 16:6; Jeremiah 16:4"> Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They will not mourn for him:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Alas, my brother! Alas, my sister!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not mourn for him:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Alas, my master! Alas, his splendor!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; 1 Kings 21:23–24; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will be buried like a donkey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">dragged away and thrown outside the gates of Jerusalem.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Ezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up to Lebanon and cry out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">raise your voice in Bashan;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">cry out from Abarim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all your lovers have been crushed.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I warned you when you were secure.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You said, ‘I will not listen.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This has been your way from youth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you have not obeyed My voice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30–31; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wind will drive away all your shepherds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your lovers will go into captivity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then you will be ashamed and humiliated</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of all your wickedness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:24; Jeremiah 4:30–31; Jeremiah 22:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">O inhabitant of Lebanon,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the palace in Jerusalem; see 1 Kings 7:2.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nestled in the cedars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">how you will groan when pangs of anguish come upon you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">agony like a woman in labor.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## A Warning to Coniah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Solomon 8:6; 1 Chronicles 3:16"> “As surely as I live,” declares the LORD, “even if you, Coniah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Coniah is a variant of Jehoiachin; also in verse 28.">d</a></sup>son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, were a signet ring on My right hand, I would pull you off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> In fact, I will hand you over to those you dread, who want to take your life—to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to the Chaldeans.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> I will hurl you and the mother who gave you birth into another land, where neither of you were born—and there you both will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> You will never return to the land for which you long.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is this man Coniah a despised and shattered pot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a jar that no one wants?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why are he and his descendants hurled out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cast into a land they do not know?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">O land, land, land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hear the word of the LORD!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Enroll this man as childless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a man who will not prosper in his lifetime.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">None of his descendants will prosper</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to sit on the throne of David</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or to rule again in Judah.”</span></span>
</div>
