## The Linen Loincloth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> This is what the LORD said to me: “Go and buy yourself a linen loincloth and put it around your waist, but do not let it touch water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I bought a loincloth in accordance with the word of the LORD, and I put it around my waist.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> Then the word of the LORD came to me a second time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take the loincloth that you bought and are wearing, and go at once to Perath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or possibly to the Euphrates; similarly in verses 5–7">a</a></sup>and hide it there in a crevice of the rocks.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went and hid it at Perath, as the LORD had commanded me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many days later the LORD said to me, “Arise, go to Perath, and get the loincloth that I commanded you to hide there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> So I went to Perath and dug up the loincloth, and I took it from the place where I had hidden it. But now it was ruined—of no use at all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the word of the LORD came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> “This is what the LORD says: In the same way I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> These evil people, who refuse to listen to My words, who follow the stubbornness of their own hearts, and who go after other gods to serve and worship them, they will be like this loincloth—of no use at all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For just as a loincloth clings to a man’s waist, so I have made the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah cling to Me, declares the LORD, so that they might be My people for My renown and praise and glory. But they did not listen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Wineskins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> Therefore you are to tell them that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Every wineskin shall be filled with wine.’<br />And when they reply, ‘Don’t we surely know that every wineskin should be filled with wine?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> then you are to tell them that this is what the LORD says: ‘I am going to fill with drunkenness all who live in this land—the kings who sit on David’s throne, the priests, the prophets, and all the people of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> I will smash them against one another, fathers and sons alike, declares the LORD. I will allow no mercy or pity or compassion to keep Me from destroying them.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
