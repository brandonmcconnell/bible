## The Broken Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> “Listen to the words of this covenant and tell them to the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> You must tell them that this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Cursed is the man who does not obey the words of this covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> which I commanded your forefathers when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, saying, ‘Obey Me, and do everything I command you, and you will be My people, and I will be your God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalms 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> This was in order to establish the oath I swore to your forefathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is to this day.”<br />“Amen, LORD,” I answered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> Then the LORD said to me, “Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, saying: Hear the words of this covenant and carry them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> For from the time I brought your fathers out of the land of Egypt until today, I strongly warned them again and again,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally I earnestly warned them, rising up early and warning (them),">a</a></sup>saying, ‘Obey My voice.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> Yet they would not obey or incline their ears, but each one followed the stubbornness of his evil heart. So I brought on them all the curses of this covenant I had commanded them to follow but they did not keep.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> And the LORD told me, “There is a conspiracy among the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalms 78:8–10"> They have returned to the sins of their forefathers who refused to obey My words. They have followed other gods to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken the covenant I made with their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> Therefore this is what the LORD says: ‘I am about to bring upon them a disaster that they cannot escape. They will cry out to Me, but I will not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> Then the cities of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods to which they have been burning incense, but these gods certainly will not save them in their time of disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> Your gods are indeed as numerous as your cities, O Judah; the altars of shame you have set up—the altars to burn incense to Baal—are as many as the streets of Jerusalem.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 John 5:16"> As for you, do not pray for these people. Do not raise up a cry or a prayer on their behalf, for I will not be listening when they call out to Me in their time of disaster.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">What right has My beloved in My house,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">having carried out so many evil schemes?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can consecrated meat avert your doom?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When you are wicked, then you rejoice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 52:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD once called you a flourishing olive tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">beautiful with well-formed fruit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But with a mighty roar He will set it on fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its branches will be consumed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> The LORD of Hosts, who planted you, has decreed disaster against you on account of the evil that the house of Israel and the house of Judah have brought upon themselves, provoking Me to anger by burning incense to Baal.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Plot against Jeremiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the LORD informed me, so I knew.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then You showed me their deeds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 83:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I was like a gentle lamb led to slaughter;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I did not know that they had plotted against me:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let us destroy the tree with its fruit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let us cut him off from the land of the living,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that his name may be remembered no more.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">O LORD of Hosts, who judges righteously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who examines the heart</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew the kidneys">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and mind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let me see Your vengeance upon them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for to You I have committed my cause.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning the people of Anathoth who are seeking your life and saying, “You must not prophesy in the name of the LORD, or you will die by our hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> So this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “I will punish them. Their young men will die by the sword, their sons and daughters by famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> There will be no remnant, for I will bring disaster on the people of Anathoth in the year of their punishment.”</span>
</div>
</div>
