## Seventy Years of Captivity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; 2 Kings 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> This is the word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, which was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> So the prophet Jeremiah spoke to all the people of Judah and all the residents of Jerusalem as follows:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 1:2"> “From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah until this very day—twenty-three years—the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you again and again,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally I have spoken to you, rising up early and speaking,">a</a></sup>but you have not listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 13:10–11"> And the LORD has sent all His servants the prophets to you again and again,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally to you, rising up early and sending (them),">b</a></sup>but you have not listened or inclined your ear to hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:7; Genesis 17:8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> The prophets told you, ‘Turn now, each of you, from your evil ways and deeds, and you can dwell in the land that the LORD has given to you and your fathers forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremiah 7:6; Jeremiah 35:15"> Do not follow other gods to serve and worship them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the works of your hands. Then I will do you no harm.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> ‘But to your own harm, you have not listened to Me,’ declares the LORD, ‘so you have provoked Me to anger with the works of your hands.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Because you have not obeyed My words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 1:15"> behold, I will summon all the families of the north, declares the LORD, and I will send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, whom I will bring against this land, against its residents, and against all the surrounding nations. So I will devote them to destruction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew cherem refer to the giving over of things or persons, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.">c</a></sup>and make them an object of horror and contempt, an everlasting desolation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23; Ezekiel 26:13"> Moreover, I will banish from them the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of the bride and bridegroom, the sound of the millstones, and the light of the lamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12; 2 Chronicles 36:21–22"> And this whole land will become a desolate wasteland, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 29:10"> But when seventy years are complete, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the land of the Chaldeans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the Babylonians">d</a></sup>for their guilt, declares the LORD, and I will make it an everlasting desolation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> I will bring upon that land all the words I have pronounced against it, all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Jeremiah 27:7; Jeremiah 50:9"> For many nations and great kings will enslave them, and I will repay them according to their deeds and according to the work of their hands.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cup of God’s Wrath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 14:10"> This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, said to me: “Take from My hand this cup of the wine of wrath, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:11; Jeremiah 51:7; Jeremiah 51:39"> And they will drink and stagger and go out of their minds, because of the sword that I will send among them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 25:28; Ezekiel 43:3"> So I took the cup from the LORD’s hand and made all the nations drink from it, each one to whom the LORD had sent me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Psalms 60:3; Jeremiah 44:22"> to make them a ruin, an object of horror and contempt and cursing, as they are to this day—Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and officials;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:13–26; Nahum 3:8–10; Jeremiah 46:2"> Pharaoh king of Egypt, his officials, his leaders, and all his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:1; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 20:1"> all the mixed tribes; all the kings of Uz; all the kings of the Philistines: Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1–22; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 9:26"> Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 1:9–10; Ezekiel 28:22–23"> all the kings of Tyre and Sidon; the kings of the coastlands across the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 49:32; Jeremiah 49:8"> Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who cut the corners of their hair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:14; Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 30:5"> all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the mixed tribes who dwell in the desert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 13:17"> all the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:41; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 50:1–46"> all the kings of the north, both near and far, one after another—all the kingdoms on the face of the earth. And after all of them, the king of Sheshach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Sheshach is a code name for Babylon.">e</a></sup>will drink it too.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:16; Habakkuk 2:16; Ezekiel 21:4–5"> “Then you are to tell them that this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Drink, get drunk, and vomit. Fall down and never get up again, because of the sword I will send among you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:33; Jeremiah 51:29; Isaiah 14:24–27"> If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink it, you are to tell them that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘You most certainly must drink it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Peter 4:17"> For behold, I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that bears My Name, so how could you possibly go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am calling down a sword upon all the inhabitants of the earth, declares the LORD of Hosts.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 3:16; Isaiah 42:13"> So you are to prophesy all these words against them and say to them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The LORD will roar from on high;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will raise His voice from His holy habitation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will roar loudly over His pasture;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like those who tread the grapes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will call out with a shout</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against all the inhabitants of the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Joel 3:2; Isaiah 66:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tumult will resound to the ends of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the LORD brings a charge against the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He brings judgment on all mankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and puts the wicked to the sword,’”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19; Isaiah 34:2; 2 Chronicles 15:6"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold! Disaster is spreading</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from nation to nation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a mighty storm is rising</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the ends of the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 79:3"> Those slain by the LORD on that day will be spread from one end of the earth to the other. They will not be mourned, gathered, or buried. They will be like dung lying on the ground.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cry of the Shepherds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 50:27; Psalms 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, you shepherds, and cry out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the days of your slaughter have come;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will fall and be shattered like fine pottery.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew; LXX you will fall like the best of the rams">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 11:20; Amos 2:14; Jeremiah 52:8–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Flight will evade the shepherds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and escape will elude the leaders of the flock.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Jeremiah 25:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear the cry of the shepherds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the wailing of the leaders of the flock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the LORD is destroying their pasture.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 27:10–11; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The peaceful meadows have been silenced</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the LORD’s burning anger.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Hosea 13:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has left His den like a lion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for their land has been made a desolation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the sword</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some Hebrew manuscripts and LXX (see also Jeremiah 46:16 and Jeremiah 50:16); most Hebrew manuscripts anger">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of the oppressor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and because of the fierce anger of the LORD.</span></span>
</div>
