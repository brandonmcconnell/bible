## Drought, Famine, Sword, and Plague

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8"> This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the drought:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Jeremiah 8:21; 1 Samuel 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Judah mourns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her gates languish.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Her people wail for the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a cry goes up from Jerusalem.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Psalms 40:14; 2 Kings 18:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nobles send their servants for water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they go to the cisterns, but find no water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their jars return empty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are ashamed and humiliated;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they cover their heads.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 1:19–20; Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ground is cracked</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because no rain has fallen on the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The farmers are ashamed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they cover their heads.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even the doe in the field deserts her newborn fawn</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is no grass.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wild donkeys stand on barren heights;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they pant for air like jackals;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or serpents or dragons">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their eyes fail for lack of pasture.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Although our iniquities testify against us, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">act for the sake of Your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, our rebellions are many;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we have sinned against You.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Jeremiah 50:7; Isaiah 43:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Hope of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its Savior in times of distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">why are You like a stranger in the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a traveler who stays but a night?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; Jeremiah 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why are You like a man taken by surprise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a warrior powerless to save?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet You are among us, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we are called by Your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not forsake us!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 8:13; Psalms 119:101"> This is what the LORD says about this people:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Truly they love to wander;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have not restrained their feet.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So the LORD does not accept them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will now remember their iniquity</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and punish them for their sins.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:14; Exodus 32:32–34"> Then the LORD said to me, “Do not pray for the well-being of this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28"> Although they may fast, I will not listen to their cry; although they may offer ascension offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead, I will finish them off by sword and famine and plague.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> “Ah, Lord GOD!” I replied, “Look, the prophets are telling them, ‘You will not see the sword or suffer famine, but I will give you lasting peace in this place.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> “The prophets are prophesying lies in My name,” replied the LORD. “I did not send them or appoint them or speak to them. They are prophesying to you a false vision, a worthless divination, the futility and delusion of their own minds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> Therefore this is what the LORD says about the prophets who prophesy in My name: I did not send them, yet they say, ‘No sword or famine will touch this land.’<br />By sword and famine these very prophets will meet their end!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> And the people to whom they prophesy will be thrown into the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword. There will be no one to bury them or their wives, their sons or their daughters. I will pour out their own evil upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:21; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 13:17"> You are to speak this word to them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘My eyes overflow with tears;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">day and night they do not cease,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the virgin daughter of my people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has been shattered by a crushing blow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a severely grievous wound.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I go out to the country,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I see those slain by the sword;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">if I enter the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I see those ravaged by famine!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For both prophet and priest</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">travel to a land they do not know.’”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
