## A Lament over Zion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:17; Isaiah 22:4; Lamentations 2:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Oh, that my head were a spring of water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my eyes a fountain of tears!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I would weep day and night</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">over the slain daughter of my people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Jeremiah 5:7–8; Hosea 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If only I had a traveler’s lodge in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I would abandon my people and depart from them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they are all adulterers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a crowd of faithless people.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:8; Jeremiah 4:22; Judges 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“They bend their tongues like bows;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lies prevail over truth in the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they proceed from evil to evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they do not take Me into account,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Proverbs 26:24–25; Psalms 12:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Let everyone guard against his neighbor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not trust any brother,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for every brother deals craftily,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every friend spreads slander.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 6:12; Habakkuk 2:13; Job 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Each one betrays his friend;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no one tells the truth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have taught their tongues to lie;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they wear themselves out committing iniquity.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Jeremiah 13:10; Jeremiah 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You dwell</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Jeremiah dwells (the Hebrew is singular)">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of deception;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in their deceit they refuse to know Me,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Jeremiah 6:27; Malachi 3:3"> Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I will refine them and test them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for what else can I do</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX because of the wickedness of">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the daughter of My people?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 26:24–26; Jeremiah 9:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their tongues are deadly arrows;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they speak deception.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With his mouth a man speaks peace to his neighbor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but in his heart he sets a trap for him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:29; Isaiah 1:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should I not punish them for these things?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">declares the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Should I not avenge Myself</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on such a nation as this?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 12:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will take up a weeping and wailing for the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a dirge over the wilderness pasture,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they have been scorched so no one passes through,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the lowing of cattle is not heard.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Both the birds of the air and the beasts have fled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have gone away.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 10:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And I will make Jerusalem a heap of rubble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a haunt for jackals;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or serpents or dragons">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">without inhabitant.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Psalms 107:43; Psalms 107:34"> Who is the man wise enough to understand this? To whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, that he may explain it? Why is the land destroyed and scorched like a desert, so no one can pass through it?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:19; Jeremiah 22:9; Ezra 9:10"> And the LORD answered, “It is because they have forsaken My law, which I set before them; they have not walked in it or obeyed My voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Jeremiah 7:24; 1 Peter 1:18"> Instead, they have followed the stubbornness of their hearts and gone after the Baals, as their fathers taught them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 80:5"> Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Behold, I will feed this people wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:2; Leviticus 26:33"> I will scatter them among the nations that neither they nor their fathers have known, and I will send a sword after them until I have finished them off.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 5:16–17; Ecclesiastes 12:5; 2 Chronicles 35:25"> This is what the LORD of Hosts says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Take note, and summon the wailing women;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">send for the most skillful among them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Isaiah 22:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them come quickly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take up a lament over us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that our eyes may overflow with tears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and our eyelids may gush with water.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 7:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the sound of wailing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is heard from Zion:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘How devastated we are!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">How great is our shame!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For we have abandoned the land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because our dwellings have been torn down.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 23:27–30; Isaiah 32:9–13; Job 22:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now, O women, hear the word of the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Open your ears to the word of His mouth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Teach your daughters to wail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and one another to lament.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:11; Ezekiel 9:5–6; Jeremiah 15:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For death has climbed in through our windows;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it has entered our fortresses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to cut off the children from the streets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the young men from the town squares.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Jeremiah 25:33; Psalms 83:10"> Declare that this is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The corpses of men will fall like dung</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">upon the open field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like newly cut grain behind the reaper,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with no one to gather it.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Isaiah 5:21; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> This is what the LORD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor the strong man in his strength,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor the wealthy man in his riches.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:17; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">But let him who boasts boast in this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that he understands and knows Me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I am the LORD,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in 1 Corinthians 1:31 and 2 Corinthians 10:17">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who exercises loving devotion,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Forms of the Hebrew chesed are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as loving devotion; the range of meaning includes love, goodness, kindness, faithfulness, and mercy, as well as loyalty to a covenant.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">justice and righteousness on the earth—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for I delight in these things,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 5:2–6; Romans 2:25–26"> “Behold, the days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when I will punish all who are circumcised only in the flesh—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Leviticus 26:41; Ezekiel 44:7"> Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, Moab, and all the inhabitants of the desert who clip the hair of their temples. For all these nations are uncircumcised, and the whole house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart.”</span>
</div>
