## The Restitution offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 5:14–7; Leviticus 6:25"> “Now this is the law of the reparation offering, which is most holy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 1:11"> The reparation offering must be slaughtered in the place where the ascension offering is slaughtered, and the priest shall splatter its blood on all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:15–16; Psalms 51:6"> And all the fat from it shall be offered: the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is to be removed with the kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> The priest shall burn them on the altar as a food offering to the LORD; it is a reparation offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 2:3"> Every male among the priests may eat of it. It must be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25–26; Leviticus 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> The reparation offering is like the purification offering; the same law applies to both. It belongs to the priest who effects purgation with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:11; Exodus 29:14; Romans 13:14"> As for the priest who presents an ascension offering for anyone, the hide of that offering belongs to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:9; Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:10"> Likewise, every grain offering that is baked in an oven or cooked in a pan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, a deep pan or stew pan">a</a></sup>or on a griddle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, a shallow pan for baking or frying">b</a></sup>belongs to the priest who presents it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14; Exodus 16:18"> and every grain offering, whether dry or mixed with oil, belongs equally to all the sons of Aaron.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Well-Being Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Ezekiel 45:15; Leviticus 3:1–17"> Now this is the law of the well-being offering that one may present to the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Numbers 6:15; 1 Peter 2:5"> If he offers it in thanksgiving, then along with the sacrifice of thanksgiving he shall offer unleavened cakes mixed with olive oil, unleavened wafers coated with oil, and well-kneaded cakes of fine flour mixed with oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 4:5; Leviticus 23:17; Matthew 13:33"> Along with his well-being offering of thanksgiving he is to present an offering with cakes of leavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 18:24–32"> From the cakes he must present one portion of each offering as a contribution to the LORD. It belongs to the priest who sprinkles the blood of the well-being offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 22:29–30"> The meat of the sacrifice of his well-being offering of thanksgiving must be eaten on the day he offers it; none of it may be left until morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:5–8; Deuteronomy 12:6; Psalms 116:14"> If, however, the sacrifice he offers is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he presents his sacrifice, but the remainder may be eaten on the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Hosea 6:2; 1 Corinthians 15:4"> But any meat of the sacrifice remaining until the third day must be burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:7–8; Leviticus 11:41; Numbers 18:27"> If any of the meat from his well-being offering is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted. It will not be credited to the one who presented it; it shall be an abomination, and the one who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:41; Titus 1:15; Acts 10:15–16"> Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten; it is to be burned up. As for any other meat, anyone who is ceremonially clean may eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:2–33; Genesis 17:14; Leviticus 22:3–7"> But if anyone who is unclean eats meat from the well-being offering that belongs to the LORD, that person must be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–59; Leviticus 11:20; Ezekiel 4:14"> If one touches anything unclean, whether human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean, detestable thing, and then eats any of the meat of the well-being offering that belongs to the LORD, that person must be cut off from his people.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Fat and Blood Forbidden

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16–17; Leviticus 17:6; 1 Samuel 2:15–17"> “Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘You are not to eat any of the fat of an ox, a sheep, or a goat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 17:15; Leviticus 22:8"> The fat of an animal found dead or mauled by wild beasts may be used for any other purpose, but you must not eat it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> If anyone eats the fat of an animal from which a food offering may be presented to the LORD, the one who eats it must be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 3:17; Acts 15:20"> You must not eat the blood of any bird or animal in any of your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Leviticus 7:20–21; Leviticus 7:25"> If anyone eats blood, that person must be cut off from his people.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Priests’ Portion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; 1 John 1:7; Leviticus 3:1–17"> “Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘Anyone who presents a well-being offering to the LORD must bring it as his sacrifice to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 3:9; Leviticus 3:14"> With his own hands he is to bring the food offerings to the LORD; he shall bring the fat, together with the breast, and wave the breast as a wave offering before the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 3:5; Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 3:16"> The priest is to burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 6:20; Leviticus 7:34; Leviticus 9:21"> And you are to give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your well-being offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1–46; Leviticus 6:1–30; Leviticus 7:3"> The son of Aaron who presents the blood and fat of the well-being offering shall have the right thigh as a portion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 18:18–19; Leviticus 10:14–15; Exodus 29:9"> I have taken from the sons of Israel the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution of their well-being offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as a permanent portion from the sons of Israel.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27; Numbers 18:7–19"> This is the portion of the food offerings to the LORD for Aaron and his sons since the day they were presented to serve the LORD as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 40:13–15"> On the day they were anointed, the LORD commanded that this be given them by the sons of Israel. It is a permanent portion for the generations to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 1:1–6; Leviticus 6:9–18"> This is the law of the ascension offering, the grain offering, the purification offering, the reparation offering, the ordination offering, and the well-being offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 1:1–2"> which the LORD gave Moses on Mount Sinai on the day He commanded the Israelites to present their offerings to the LORD in the Wilderness of Sinai.</span>
</div>
</div>
