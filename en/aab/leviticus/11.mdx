## Clean and Unclean Animals

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke again to Moses and Aaron, telling them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> “Say to the Israelites, ‘Of all the beasts of the earth, these ones you may eat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> You may eat any animal that has a split hoof completely divided and that chews the cud.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But of those that only chew the cud or only have a divided hoof, you are not to eat the following:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The precise identification of some of the birds and animals in this chapter is uncertain.">a</a></sup><br />The camel, though it chews the cud, does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> The rock badger,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or The coney or The hyrax">b</a></sup>though it chews the cud, does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> The rabbit, though it chews the cud, does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> And the pig, though it has a split hoof completely divided, does not chew the cud; it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You must not eat their meat or touch their carcasses; they are unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> Of all the creatures that live in the water, whether in the seas or in the streams, you may eat anything with fins and scales.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> But the following among all the teeming life and creatures in the water are detestable to you: everything in the seas or streams that does not have fins and scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They shall be an abomination to you; you must not eat their meat, and you must detest their carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Everything in the water that does not have fins and scales shall be detestable to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> Additionally, you are to detest the following birds, and they must not be eaten because they are detestable:<br />the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> the kite, any kind of falcon,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> any kind of raven,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> the ostrich,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally the daughter of the ostrich or the daughter of the owl">c</a></sup>the screech owl, the gull, any kind of hawk,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the white owl, the desert owl, the osprey,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the stork, any kind of heron,<br />the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> All flying insects that walk on all fours are detestable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> However, you may eat the following kinds of flying insects that walk on all fours: those having jointed legs above their feet for hopping on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> Of these you may eat any kind of locust, katydid, cricket, or grasshopper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All other flying insects that have four legs are detestable to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> These creatures will make you unclean. Whoever touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> and whoever picks up one of their carcasses must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every animal with hooves not completely divided<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally a split hoof not completely divided">d</a></sup>or that does not chew the cud is unclean for you. Whoever touches any of them will be unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> All the four-footed animals that walk on their paws are unclean for you; whoever touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> and anyone who picks up a carcass must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening. They are unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> The following creatures that move along the ground are unclean for you: the mole, the mouse, any kind of great lizard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> the gecko, the monitor lizard, the common lizard, the skink, and the chameleon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These animals are unclean for you among all the crawling creatures. Whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> When one of them dies and falls on something, that article becomes unclean; any article of wood, clothing, leather, sackcloth, or any implement used for work must be rinsed with water and will remain unclean until evening; then it will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> If any of them falls into a clay pot, everything in it will be unclean; you must break the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Any food coming into contact with water from that pot will be unclean, and any drink in such a container will be unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> Anything upon which one of their carcasses falls will be unclean. If it is an oven or cooking pot, it must be smashed; it is unclean and will remain unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Nevertheless, a spring or cistern containing water will remain clean, but one who touches a carcass in it will be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> If a carcass falls on any seed for sowing, the seed is clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> but if water has been put on the seed and a carcass falls on it, it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> If an animal that you may eat dies, anyone who touches the carcass will be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> Whoever eats from the carcass must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening, and anyone who picks up the carcass must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> Every creature that moves along the ground is detestable; it must not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Do not eat any creature that moves along the ground, whether it crawls on its belly or walks on four or more feet; for such creatures are detestable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Do not defile yourselves by any crawling creature; do not become unclean or defiled by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am the LORD your God; consecrate yourselves, therefore, and be holy, because I am holy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Here and in verse 45; cited in 1 Peter 1:16">e</a></sup>You must not defile yourselves by any creature that crawls along the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt so that I would be your God; therefore be holy, because I am holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This is the law regarding animals, birds, all living creatures that move in the water, and all creatures that crawl along the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> You must distinguish between the unclean and the clean, between animals that may be eaten and those that may not.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
