## Sins Requiring a Purification Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Proverbs 29:24; Leviticus 19:8"> “If someone sins by failing to testify when he hears a public charge about something he has witnessed, whether he has seen it or learned of it, he shall bear the iniquity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Deuteronomy 14:8; Haggai 2:13"> Or if a person touches anything unclean—whether the carcass of any unclean wild animal or livestock or crawling creature—even if he is unaware of it, he is unclean and guilty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–16; Leviticus 15:1–33; Leviticus 12:1–8"> Or if he touches human uncleanness—anything by which one becomes unclean—even if he is unaware of it, when he realizes it, he is guilty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–6; Acts 23:12; Judges 11:31"> Or if someone swears thoughtlessly with his lips to do anything good or evil—in whatever matter a man may rashly pronounce an oath—even if he is unaware of it, when he realizes it, he is guilty in the matter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 26:40"> If someone incurs guilt in one of these ways, he must confess the sin he has committed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:20"> and he must bring his reparation offering to the LORD for the sin he has committed: a female lamb or goat from the flock as a purification offering. And the priest will effect purgation for him concerning his sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 14:21–22; Matthew 3:16"> If, however, he cannot afford a lamb, he may bring to the LORD as reparation for his sin two turtledoves or two young pigeons—one as a purification offering and the other as an ascension offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:15; Leviticus 1:17; Romans 4:25"> He is to bring them to the priest, who shall first present the one for the purification offering. He is to twist its head at the front of its neck without severing it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:34; Leviticus 4:18"> then he is to sprinkle some of the blood of the purification offering on the side of the altar, while the rest of the blood is drained out at the base of the altar. It is a purification offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:14–17; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> And the priest must prepare the second bird as an ascension offering according to the ordinance. In this way the priest will effect purgation for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Leviticus 2:1–2; Exodus 16:36"> But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he may bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A tenth of an ephah is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour).">a</a></sup>as a purification offering. He must not put olive oil or frankincense on it, because it is a purification offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 2:2"> He is to bring it to the priest, who shall take a handful from it as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar atop the food offerings to the LORD; it is a purification offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 4:26; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> In this way the priest will effect purgation for him for any of these sins he has committed, and he will be forgiven. The remainder will belong to the priest, like the grain offering.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Laws for Reparation Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 6:6"> “If someone acts unfaithfully and sins unintentionally against any of the LORD’s holy things, he must bring his reparation offering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. asham. The offering makes amends for a breach against God or neighbor, including trespass against the LORD's holy things where no property can simply be returned (vv. 14-16); restitution, where possible, is paid separately plus one-fifth (Lev 6:5). Traditionally 'guilt offering,' but the offering remedies the breach, not the feeling of guilt. Rendered 'reparation offering' throughout.">b</a></sup>to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, of proper value<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or flock or its equivalence">c</a></sup>in silver shekels according to the sanctuary shekel;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A shekel is approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams of silver.">d</a></sup>it is a reparation offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:14; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 27:27"> Regarding any holy thing he has harmed, he must make restitution by adding a fifth of its value to it and giving it to the priest, who will effect purgation on his behalf with the ram as a reparation offering, and he will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 4:13; Leviticus 4:2–4"> If someone sins and violates any of the LORD’s commandments even though he was unaware, he is guilty and shall bear his punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15–16; 1 Timothy 2:5–6; Leviticus 1:4"> He is to bring to the priest an unblemished ram of proper value from the flock as a reparation offering. Then the priest will effect purgation on his behalf for the wrong he has committed in ignorance, and he will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Malachi 3:8; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21"> It is a reparation offering; he was certainly guilty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or he has paid full compensation">e</a></sup>before the LORD.”</span>
</div>
</div>
