## The Oil for the Lamps

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> “Command the Israelites to bring you pure oil of pressed olives for the light, to keep the lamps burning continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Outside the veil of the Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Testimony refers to the stone tablets in the ark of the covenant inscribed with the Ten Commandments.">a</a></sup>in the Tent of Meeting, Aaron is to tend the lamps continually before the LORD from evening until morning. This is to be a permanent statute for the generations to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> He shall tend the lamps on the pure gold lampstand before the LORD continually.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Showbread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> You are also to take fine flour and bake twelve loaves, using two-tenths of an ephah for each loaf,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Two-tenths of an ephah is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour).">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; 2 Chronicles 4:19"> and set them in two rows—six per row—on the table of pure gold before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> And you are to place pure frankincense near each row, so that it may serve as a memorial portion for the bread, a food offering to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Numbers 4:7; 1 Chronicles 9:32"> Every Sabbath day the bread is to be set out before the LORD on behalf of the Israelites as a permanent covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> It belongs to Aaron and his sons, who are to eat it in a holy place; for it is to him a most holy part of the food offerings to the LORD—his portion forever.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Punishment for Blasphemy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> Now the son of an Israelite mother and an Egyptian father went out among the Israelites, and a fight broke out in the camp between him and an Israelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name with a curse. So they brought him to Moses. (His mother’s name was Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> They placed him in custody until the will of the LORD should be made clear to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> “Take the blasphemer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally the one who cursed; also in verse 23">c</a></sup>outside the camp, and have all who heard him lay their hands on his head; then have the whole assembly stone him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And you are to tell the Israelites, ‘If anyone curses his God, he shall bear the consequences of his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> Whoever blasphemes the name of the LORD must surely be put to death; the whole assembly must surely stone him, whether he is a foreign resident or native; if he blasphemes the Name, he must be put to death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Eye for an Eye

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> And if a man takes the life of anyone else, he must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> Whoever kills an animal must make restitution—life for life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> If anyone injures his neighbor, whatever he has done must be done to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 5:38">d</a></sup>Just as he injured the other person, the same must be inflicted on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> Whoever kills an animal must make restitution, but whoever kills a man must be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> You are to have the same standard of law for the foreign resident and the native; for I am the LORD your God.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> Then Moses spoke to the Israelites, and they took the blasphemer outside the camp and stoned him. So the Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>
