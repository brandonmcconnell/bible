## Holiness Required of Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> Then the LORD said to Moses, “Speak to Aaron’s sons, the priests, and tell them that a priest is not to defile himself for a dead person among his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> except for his immediate family—his mother, father, son, daughter, or brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> or his unmarried sister who is near to him, since she has no husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> He is not to defile himself for those related to him by marriage, and so profane himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> Priests must not make bald spots on their heads, shave off the edges of their beards, or make cuts in their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They must be holy to their God and not profane the name of their God. Because they present to the LORD the food offerings, the food of their God, they must be holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> A priest must not marry a woman defiled by prostitution or divorced by her husband, for the priest is holy to his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> You are to regard him as holy, since he presents the food of your God. He shall be holy to you, because I the LORD am holy—I who set you apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> If a priest’s daughter defiles herself by prostituting herself, she profanes her father; she must be burned in the fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> The priest who is highest among his brothers, who has had the anointing oil poured on his head and has been ordained to wear the priestly garments, must not let his hair hang loose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or must not uncover his head">a</a></sup>or tear his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> He must not go near any dead body; he must not defile himself, even for his father or mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> He must not leave or desecrate the sanctuary of his God, for the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is on him. I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> The woman he marries must be a virgin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> He is not to marry a widow, a divorced woman, or one defiled by prostitution. He is to marry a virgin from his own people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> so that he does not defile his offspring among his people, for I am the LORD who sanctifies him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Restrictions against Those with Blemishes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> “Say to Aaron, ‘For the generations to come, none of your descendants who has a physical defect may approach to offer the food of his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> No man who has any defect may approach—no man who is blind, lame, disfigured, or deformed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> no man who has a broken foot or hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> or who is a hunchback or dwarf, or who has an eye defect, a festering rash, scabs, or a crushed testicle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> No descendant of Aaron the priest who has a defect shall approach to present the food offerings to the LORD. Since he has a defect, he is not to come near to offer the food of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> He may eat the most holy food of his God as well as the holy food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> but because he has a defect, he must not go near the veil or approach the altar, so as not to desecrate My sanctuaries. For I am the LORD who sanctifies them.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> Moses told this to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
