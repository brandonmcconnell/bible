## Laws for Ascension Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 19:3; Exodus 25:22; Exodus 29:42"> Then the LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–19; Romans 12:6; 1 Chronicles 16:29"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When any of you brings an offering to the LORD, you may bring as your offering an animal from the herd or the flock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Hebrews 9:14; Leviticus 22:19–24"> If his offering is an ascension offering from the herd, he is to present an unblemished male. He must bring it to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting for its acceptance before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Exodus 29:15; Leviticus 4:20"> He is to lay his hand on the head of the ascension offering, so it can be accepted on his behalf to effect purgation for him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. kipper, 'to purge, wipe clean' (cognate to Akkadian kuppuru, 'to wipe'). Blood functions as a ritual cleansing agent applied to sacred space and persons; the offering purges, and forgiveness is God's response to the completed purgation (Lev 4:31). Traditionally rendered 'make atonement,' which has come to suggest appeasement or payment; the Hebrew names an act of cleansing. Rendered 'effect purgation' throughout. 'Purgation' names the act, the wiping away of contamination from the sanctuary; 'purification' names the clean state it produces. The AAB keeps 'purgation' for the act (kipper) and uses 'purification' for the offering and the annual day, which are named by their result.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 3:8; Leviticus 3:2"> And he shall slaughter the young bull before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons the priests are to present the blood and splatter it on all sides of the altar at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:8; Genesis 3:21"> Next, he is to skin the ascension offering and cut it into pieces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Genesis 22:9; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> The sons of Aaron the priest shall put a fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 9:13–14; Leviticus 1:12; 1 Kings 18:33"> Then Aaron’s sons the priests are to arrange the pieces, including the head and the fat, atop the burning wood on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Leviticus 1:13; Ephesians 5:2"> The entrails and legs must be washed with water, and the priest shall burn all of it on the altar as an ascension offering, a food offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; Leviticus 1:2–3; Genesis 8:20"> If, however, one’s offering is an ascension offering from the flock—from the sheep or goats—he is to present an unblemished male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Exodus 40:22; Leviticus 7:2"> He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons the priests are to splatter its blood against the altar on all sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He is to cut the animal into pieces, and the priest shall arrange them, including the head and fat, atop the burning wood that is on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:9"> The entrails and legs must be washed with water, and the priest shall present all of it and burn it on the altar; it is an ascension offering, a food offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Luke 2:24; Leviticus 12:8"> If, instead, one’s offering to the LORD is an ascension offering of birds, he is to present a turtledove or a young pigeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:8–9; Psalms 22:21; 1 John 2:27"> Then the priest shall bring it to the altar, twist off its head, and burn it on the altar; its blood should be drained out on the side of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Luke 1:35; Leviticus 16:27"> And he is to remove the crop with its contents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the crop and feathers">b</a></sup>and throw it to the east side of the altar, in the place for ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; John 19:30; Leviticus 1:9–10"> He shall tear it open by its wings, without dividing the bird completely. And the priest is to burn it on the altar atop the burning wood. It is an ascension offering, a food offering, a soothing aroma to the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>
