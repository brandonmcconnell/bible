<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Do not rebuke an older man, but appeal to him as to a father.<br />Treat younger men as brothers,</span>

## Honoring True Widows

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honor the widows who are truly widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> But if a widow has children or grandchildren, they must first learn to show godliness to their own family and repay their parents, for this is pleasing in the sight of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> The widow who is truly in need and left all alone puts her hope in God and continues night and day in her petitions and prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> But she who lives for pleasure is dead even while she is still alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Give these instructions to the believers, so that they will be above reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> If anyone does not provide for his own, and especially his own household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> A widow should be enrolled if she is at least sixty years old, faithful to her husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> and well known for good deeds such as bringing up children, entertaining strangers, washing the feet of the holy ones, imparting relief to the afflicted, and devoting herself to every good work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But refuse to enroll younger widows. For when their passions draw them away from Christ, they will want to marry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> and thus will incur judgment because they are setting aside their first faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> At the same time they will also learn to be idle, going from house to house and being not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, discussing things they should not mention.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> So I advise the younger widows to marry, have children, and manage their households, denying the adversary occasion for slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For some have already turned aside to follow Satan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If any believing woman has dependent widows, she must assist them and not allow the church to be burdened, so that it can help the widows who are truly in need.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Honoring Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Elders who lead effectively are worthy of double honor, especially those who work hard at preaching and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> For the Scripture says, “Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Deuteronomy 25:4">a</a></sup>and, “The worker is worthy of his wages.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Luke 10:7; see also Leviticus 19:13 and Deuteronomy 24:14–15.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Do not entertain an accusation against an elder, except on the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> But those who persist in sin should be rebuked in front of everyone, so that the others will stand in fear of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Charge to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels to maintain these principles without bias, and to do nothing out of partiality.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Do not be too quick in the laying on of hands and thereby share in the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> Stop drinking only water and use a little wine instead, because of your stomach and your frequent ailments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> The sins of some men are obvious, going ahead of them to judgment; but the sins of others do not surface until later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> In the same way, good deeds are obvious, and even the ones that are inconspicuous cannot remain hidden.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
