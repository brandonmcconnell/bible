## Judgment on Israel and Judah

<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When Ephraim saw his sickness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Judah his wound,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then Ephraim turned to Assyria</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sent to the great king.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or to King Jareb">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he cannot cure you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or heal your wound.</span></span>
