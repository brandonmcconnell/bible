## Hosea Redeems His Wife

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:12–14; Micah 7:18–20; Isaiah 45:22"> Then the LORD said to me, “Go show love to your wife again, though she is loved by another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Go show love to a woman who is loved by another">a</a></sup>and is an adulteress. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites, though they turn to other gods and love to offer raisin cakes to idols.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally and love raisin cakes">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 27:16; Genesis 34:12; Ezekiel 45:11"> So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15 shekels is approximately 6 ounces or 171 grams of silver.">c</a></sup>and a homer and a lethech of barley.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or a homer and a half of barley; that is, a total of approximately 9.36 bushels or 330 liters (probably about 436 pounds or 198 kilograms of barley); LXX a homer of barley and a wineskin full of wine">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:13"> Then I said to her, “You must live with me for many days; you must not be promiscuous or belong to another, and I will do the same for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:5; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Genesis 31:19"> For the Israelites must live many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred pillar, and without ephod or idol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:9; Jeremiah 50:4–5; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> Afterward, the people of Israel will return and seek the LORD their God and David their king. They will come trembling to the LORD and to His goodness in the last days.</span>
</div>
</div>
