## Zephaniah Prophesies Judgment on Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; 2 Peter 1:19"> This is the word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:13; 2 Kings 22:16–17; Ezekiel 33:27–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will completely sweep away</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everything from the face of the earth,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Ezekiel 7:19; Jeremiah 12:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will sweep away man and beast;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will sweep away the birds of the air,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the fish of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the idols with their wicked worshipers.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or and the idols that cause the wicked to stumble">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off mankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the face of the earth,”</span>declares the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will stretch out My hand against Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against all who dwell in Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cut off from this place every remnant of Baal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the names of the idolatrous and pagan priests—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; 1 Kings 11:33; 2 Kings 17:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">those who bow on the rooftops</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to worship the host of heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who bow down and swear by the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but also swear by Milcom,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or by their king; Milcom is a variant of Molech; see Leviticus 18:21 and 1 Kings 11:7.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">and those who turn back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from following the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">neither seeking the LORD</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor inquiring of Him.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Day of the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be silent in the presence of the Lord GOD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the Day of the LORD is near.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, the LORD has prepared a sacrifice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has consecrated His guests.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; Jeremiah 22:24–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">“On the Day of the LORD’s sacrifice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will punish the princes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the sons of the king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all who are dressed in foreign apparel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day I will punish</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who leap over the threshold,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1 Samuel 5:5.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who fill the house of their master</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or the temple of their gods">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with violence and deceit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Amos 8:3; 2 Kings 22:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">On that day,” declares the LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“a cry will go up from the Fish Gate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a wail from the Second District,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or the Second Quarter, a newer section of Jerusalem; Hebrew the Mishneh">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a loud crashing from the hills.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:1; Jeremiah 25:34; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, O dwellers of the Hollow,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or the market district or the Mortar">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for all your merchants</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or all the people of Canaan">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be silenced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who weigh out silver will be cut off.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:1; Jeremiah 48:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And at that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and punish the men settled in complacency,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or thickening on the dregs">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who say to themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘The LORD will do nothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">either good or bad.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their wealth will be plundered</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their houses laid waste.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will build houses but not inhabit them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and plant vineyards but never drink their wine.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The great Day of the LORD is near—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">near and coming quickly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen, the Day of the LORD!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then the cry of the mighty will be bitter.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day will be</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of trouble and distress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of destruction and desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a day of darkness and gloom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a day of clouds and blackness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Amos 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">a day of horn blast and battle cry</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against the fortified cities,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against the high corner towers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will bring such distress on mankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that they will walk like the blind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they have sinned against the LORD.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their blood will be poured out like dust</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their flesh like dung.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Neither their silver nor their gold</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be able to deliver them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the Day of the LORD’s wrath.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The whole earth will be consumed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by the fire of His jealousy.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For indeed, He will make a sudden end</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of all who dwell on the earth.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or of all the people living in the land">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
