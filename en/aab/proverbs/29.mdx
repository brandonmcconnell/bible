<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">An angry man stirs up dissension,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a hot-tempered man abounds in transgression.</span></span>
