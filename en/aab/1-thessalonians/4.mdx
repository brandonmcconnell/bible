## Living to Please God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Peter 3:18"> Finally, brothers, we ask and encourage you in the Lord Jesus to live in a way that is pleasing to God, just as you have received from us. This is how you already live, so you should do so all the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> For you know the instructions we gave you by the authority of the Lord Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> For it is God’s will that you should be holy: You must abstain from sexual immorality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> each of you must know how to control his own body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally know how to possess his own vessel">a</a></sup>in holiness and honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> not in lustful passion like the Gentiles who do not know God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> and no one should ever violate or exploit his brother in this regard,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or no one should ever harm or cheat his brother in this matter">b</a></sup>because the Lord will avenge all such acts, as we have already told you and solemnly warned you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> For God has not called us to impurity, but to holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> Anyone, then, who rejects this command does not reject man but God, the very One who gives you His Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> Now about brotherly love, you do not need anyone to write to you, because you yourselves have been taught by God to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> And you are indeed showing this love to all the brothers throughout Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers, to excel more and more</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> and to aspire to live quietly, to attend to your own matters, and to work with your own hands, as we instructed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> Then you will behave properly toward outsiders, without being dependent on anyone.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Return of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> Brothers, we do not want you to be uninformed about those who sleep in death,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally those who have fallen asleep, as in verses 14 and 15">c</a></sup>so that you will not grieve like the rest, who are without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> For since we believe that Jesus died and rose again, we also believe that God will bring with Jesus those who have fallen asleep in Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> By the word of the Lord, we declare to you that we who are alive and remain until the coming of the Lord will by no means precede those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a loud command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will be the first to rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> After that, we who are alive and remain will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will always be with the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Luke 21:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore encourage one another with these words.</span>
</div>
</div>
