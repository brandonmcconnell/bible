## Timothy’s Visit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:15; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17"> So when we could bear it no longer, we were willing to be left on our own in Athens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:22–23; Ephesians 6:21–22; Philippians 2:19–25"> We sent Timothy, our brother and fellow worker for God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or our brother and God’s fellow worker; NE, WH, BYZ, and TR our brother and minister of God">a</a></sup>in the gospel of Christ, to strengthen and encourage you in your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Romans 5:3; Acts 9:16"> so that none of you would be shaken by these trials. For you know that we are destined for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; 2 Corinthians 8:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> Indeed, when we were with you, we kept warning you that we would suffer persecution; and as you know, it has come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:13–14; Matthew 4:3; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> For this reason, when I could bear it no longer, I sent to find out about your faith, for fear that the tempter had somehow tempted you and that our labor might have been in vain.</span>
</div>

## Timothy’s Encouraging Report

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But just now, Timothy has returned from his visit with the good news about your faith, your love, and the fond memories you have preserved, longing to see us just as we long to see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> For this reason, brothers, in all our distress and persecution, we have been reassured about you, because of your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> For now we can go on living, as long as you are standing firm in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:14–15; Psalms 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> How can we adequately thank God for you in return for our great joy over you in His presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> Night and day we pray most earnestly that we may see you face to face and supply what is lacking in your faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now may our God and Father Himself, and our Lord Jesus, direct our way to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> And may the Lord cause you to increase and overflow with love for one another and for everyone else, just as our love for you overflows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> so that He may establish your hearts in blamelessness and holiness before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all His holy ones. Amen.</span>
</div>
