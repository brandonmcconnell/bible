## The Doomed Flock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:6–7; Jeremiah 22:23; Luke 21:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Open your doors, O Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the fire may consume your cedars!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 31:17; Isaiah 10:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, O cypress,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or pine or juniper or fir">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the cedar has fallen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the majestic trees are ruined!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wail, O oaks of Bashan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the dense forest has been cut down!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:44; Jeremiah 25:34–36; Zechariah 11:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the wailing of the shepherds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for their glory is in ruins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the roaring of the young lions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the thickets of the Jordan are destroyed.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> This is what the LORD my God says: “Pasture the flock marked for slaughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:7; Hosea 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> whose buyers slaughter them without remorse. Those who sell them say, ‘Blessed be the LORD, for I am rich!’ Even their own shepherds have no compassion on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> For I will no longer have compassion on the people of the land, declares the LORD, but behold, I will cause each man to fall into the hands of his neighbor and his king, who will devastate the land, and I will not deliver it from their hands.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Zechariah 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> So I pastured the flock marked for slaughter, especially the afflicted of the flock. Then I took for myself two staffs, calling one Favor and the other Union, and I pastured the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> And in one month I dismissed three shepherds.<br />My soul grew impatient with the flock, and their souls also detested me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> Then I said, “I will no longer shepherd you. Let the dying die, and the perishing perish; and let those who remain devour one another’s flesh.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Thirty Pieces of Silver

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Psalms 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> Next I took my staff called Favor and cut it in two,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or broke it; similarly in verse 14">b</a></sup>revoking the covenant I had made with all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> It was revoked on that day, and so the afflicted of the flock who were watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> Then I told them, “If it seems right to you, give me my wages; but if not, keep them.” So they weighed out my wages, thirty pieces of silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And the LORD said to me, “Throw it to the potter”—this magnificent price at which they valued me. So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them to the potter in the house of the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> Then I cut in two my second staff called Union, breaking the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> And the LORD said to me: “Take up once more the equipment of a foolish shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremiah 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> For behold, I will raise up a shepherd in the land who will neither care for the lost, nor seek the young, nor heal the broken, nor sustain the healthy, but he will devour the flesh of the choice sheep and tear off their hooves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Ezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to the worthless shepherd,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who deserts the flock!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May a sword strike his arm</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his right eye!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May his arm be completely withered</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his right eye utterly blinded!”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
