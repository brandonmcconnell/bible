## The Destroyers of Jerusalem Destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 13:9; Isaiah 2:12; Isaiah 13:6"> Behold, a day of the LORD is coming when your plunder will be divided in your presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 13:16; Romans 9:27–29"> For I will gather all the nations for battle against Jerusalem, and the city will be captured, the houses looted, and the women ravished. Half of the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be removed from the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> Then the LORD will go out to fight against those nations, as He fights in the day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Ezekiel 11:23; Acts 1:11–12"> On that day His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem, and the Mount of Olives will be split in two from east to west, forming a great valley, with half the mountain moving to the north and half to the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> You will flee by My mountain valley, for it will extend to Azal. You will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew with You">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:10–11; Psalms 112:4; John 12:46"> On that day there will be no light, no cold or frost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> It will be a unique day known only to the LORD, without day or night; but when evening comes, there will be light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> And on that day living water will flow out from Jerusalem, half of it toward the Eastern Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">b</a></sup>and the other half toward the Western Sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea">c</a></sup>in summer and winter alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> On that day the LORD will become King over all the earth—the LORD alone, and His name alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; 1 Kings 15:22"> All the land from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem will be turned into a plain, but Jerusalem will be raised up and will remain in her place, from the Benjamin Gate to the site of the First Gate to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the royal winepresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:3; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Amos 9:15"> People will live there, and never again will there be an utter destruction. So Jerusalem will dwell securely.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> And this will be the plague with which the LORD strikes all the peoples who have warred against Jerusalem: Their flesh will rot while they stand on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> On that day a great panic from the LORD will come upon them, so that each will seize the hand of another, and the hand of one will rise against the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 20:25–27"> Judah will also fight at Jerusalem, and the wealth of all the surrounding nations will be collected—gold, silver, and apparel in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> And a similar plague will strike the horses and mules, camels and donkeys, and all the animals in those camps.</span>
</div>
</div>

## All Nations Will Worship the King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> Then all the survivors from the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, and to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as the Feast of Booths or the Feast of Shelters and originally called the Feast of Ingathering (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> And should any of the families of the earth not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, then the rain will not fall on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> And if the people of Egypt will not go up and enter in, then the rain will not fall on them; this will be the plague with which the LORD strikes the nations who do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This will be the punishment of Egypt and of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> On that day, HOLY TO THE LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the LORD">e</a></sup>will be inscribed on the bells of the horses, and the cooking pots in the house of the LORD will be like the sprinkling bowls before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Indeed, every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Hosts, and all who sacrifice will come and take some pots and cook in them. And on that day there will no longer be a Canaanite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or merchant">f</a></sup>in the house of the LORD of Hosts.</span>
</div>
</div>
