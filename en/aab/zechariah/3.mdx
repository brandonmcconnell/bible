## The Vision of Joshua the High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 22:31; Psalms 109:6"> Then the angel showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Angel; also in verses 3, 4, 5, and 6; corresponding pronouns may also be capitalized.">a</a></sup>of the LORD, with the Accuser<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. hasatan, &quot;accuser&quot; or &quot;adversary&quot; (lexical range). See Job 1:6; also in verse 2">b</a></sup>standing at his right hand to accuse him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 4:11; Jude 1:23; Jude 1:9"> And the LORD said to the Accuser: “The LORD rebukes you, Accuser! Indeed, the LORD, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebukes you! Is not this man a firebrand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, a burning stick">c</a></sup>snatched from the fire?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Revelation 19:8; Ezra 9:15"> Now Joshua was dressed in filthy garments as he stood before the angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Isaiah 61:10; Ezekiel 36:25"> So the angel said to those standing before him, “Take off his filthy clothes!”<br />Then he said to Joshua, “See, I have removed your iniquity, and I will clothe you with splendid robes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:11; Exodus 29:6; Job 29:14"> Then I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.” So a clean turban was placed on his head, and they clothed him, as the angel of the LORD stood by.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–17; Genesis 22:15–16; Zechariah 3:1"> Then the angel of the LORD gave this charge to Joshua:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Genesis 26:5; 2 Timothy 4:1–2"> “This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘If you walk in My ways and keep My instructions, then you will govern My house and will also have charge of My courts; and I will give you a place among these who are standing here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Isaiah 11:1; Zechariah 6:12"> Hear now, O high priest Joshua, you and your companions seated before you, who are indeed a sign. For behold, I am going to bring My servant, the Branch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> See the stone I have set before Joshua; on that one stone are seven eyes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or facets">d</a></sup>Behold, I will engrave on it an inscription, declares the LORD of Hosts, and I will remove the iniquity of this land in a single day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4; Zechariah 2:11"> On that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, you will each invite your neighbor to sit under your own vine and fig tree.’”</span>
</div>
