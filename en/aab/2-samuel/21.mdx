## David Avenges the Gibeonites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 12:10"> During the reign of David there was a famine for three successive years, and David sought the face of the LORD.<br />And the LORD said, “It is because of the blood shed by Saul and his family, because he killed the Gibeonites.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–21; 2 Kings 10:31; 1 Samuel 14:44"> At this, David summoned the Gibeonites and spoke to them. (Now the Gibeonites were not Israelites, but a remnant of the Amorites. The Israelites had taken an oath concerning them, but in his zeal for Israel and Judah, Saul had sought to kill them.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:19; Hebrews 9:22; 1 Samuel 26:19"> So David asked the Gibeonites, “What shall I do for you? How can I make amends so that you may bless the inheritance of the LORD?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> The Gibeonites said to him, “We need no silver or gold from Saul or his house, nor should you put to death anyone in Israel for us.”<br />“Whatever you ask, I will do for you,” he replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1; Daniel 9:26; Matthew 7:2"> And they answered the king, “As for the man who consumed us and plotted against us to exterminate us from existing within any border of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; Numbers 25:4–5; 1 Samuel 11:4"> let seven of his male descendants be delivered to us so that we may hang them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or expose them or execute them; similarly in verse 9">a</a></sup>before the LORD at Gibeah of Saul, the chosen of the LORD.”<br />“I will give them to you,” said the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:15; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> Now the king spared Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, because of the oath before the LORD between David and Jonathan son of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 3:7"> But the king took Armoni and Mephibosheth, the two sons whom Rizpah daughter of Aiah had borne to Saul, as well as the five sons whom Merab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Two Hebrew manuscripts, some LXX manuscripts, and Syriac (see also 1 Samuel 18:19); most Hebrew and LXX manuscripts Michal">b</a></sup>daughter of Saul had borne to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Deuteronomy 21:1–9; 2 Samuel 6:17"> And he delivered them into the hands of the Gibeonites, and they hanged them on the hill before the LORD. So all seven of them fell together; they were put to death in the first days of the harvest, at the beginning of the barley harvest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 1 Kings 21:27; Joel 2:23"> And Rizpah the daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and spread it out for herself on a rock. From the beginning of the harvest until the rain from heaven poured down on the bodies, she did not allow the birds of the air to rest on them by day, nor the beasts of the field by night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:4; Ruth 2:11–12"> When David was told what Saul’s concubine Rizpah, daughter of Aiah, had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 31:10–13; 2 Samuel 2:5–7"> he went and took the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan from the men of Jabesh-gilead, who had stolen them from the public square of Beth-shan where the Philistines had hung the bodies after they had struck down Saul at Gilboa.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> So David had the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan brought from there, and they also gathered the bones of those who had been hanged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 18:28; 2 Samuel 24:25; Joshua 7:26"> And they buried the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan in Zela in the land of Benjamin, in the tomb of Saul’s father Kish.<br />After they had done everything the king had commanded, God answered their prayers for the land.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Four Battles against the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 14:10–11; 2 Samuel 5:22; Ecclesiastes 12:3"> Once again the Philistines waged war against Israel, and David and his servants went down and fought against the Philistines. But David became exhausted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:20; 1 Samuel 17:4–5"> Then Ishbi-benob, a descendant of Rapha,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or the giant; also in verses 18, 20, and 22">c</a></sup>whose bronze spear weighed three hundred shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="300 shekels is approximately 7.5 pounds or 3.4 kilograms.">d</a></sup>and who was bearing a new sword, resolved to kill David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 18:3; Psalms 132:17"> But Abishai son of Zeruiah came to his aid, struck the Philistine, and killed him.<br />Then David’s men swore to him, “You must never again go out with us to battle, so that the lamp of Israel may not be extinguished.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:29; 2 Samuel 21:20; 2 Samuel 21:16"> Some time later at Gob, there was another battle with the Philistines. At that time Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Saph is a variant of Sippai; see 1 Chronicles 20:4.">e</a></sup>a descendant of Rapha.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–11; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Once again there was a battle with the Philistines at Gob, and Elhanan son of Jair<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See 1 Chronicles 20:5; Hebrew Jaare-oregim">f</a></sup>the Bethlehemite killed the brother of Goliath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See 1 Chronicles 20:5; Hebrew does not include the brother of.">g</a></sup>the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:16; 1 Chronicles 20:6"> And there was also a battle at Gath, where there was a man of great stature with six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He too was descended from Rapha,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 16:9"> and when he taunted Israel, Jonathan the son of David’s brother Shimei<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Shimei is a variant of Shammah, Shimeah, and Shimea; see 1 Samuel 16:9, 2 Samuel 13:3, and 1 Chronicles 2:13.">h</a></sup>killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:8; Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 9:23"> So these four descendants of Rapha in Gath fell at the hands of David and his servants.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
