## Absalom Killed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 8:10; 1 Samuel 8:12; Exodus 17:9"> Then David reviewed his troops and appointed over them commanders of thousands and of hundreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:16; 2 Samuel 15:19–22; 2 Samuel 10:7–10"> He sent out the troops, a third under Joab, a third under Joab’s brother Abishai son of Zeruiah, and a third under Ittai the Gittite. And the king said to the troops, “I will surely march out with you as well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:17; Exodus 17:10–12; 1 Kings 22:31"> But the people pleaded, “You must not go out! For if we have to flee, they will not care about us. Even if half of us die, they will not care. But you are worth ten thousand of us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Two Hebrew manuscripts, some LXX manuscripts, and Vulgate; most Hebrew manuscripts care; for now there are ten thousand like us">a</a></sup>It is better now if you support us from the city.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:24; 2 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 29:2"> “I will do whatever seems best to you,” the king replied. So he stood beside the gate, while all the troops marched out by hundreds and by thousands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; 2 Samuel 16:11; Psalms 103:13"> Now the king had commanded Joab, Abishai, and Ittai, “Treat the young man Absalom gently for my sake.” And all the people heard the king’s orders to each of the commanders regarding Absalom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:18; Joshua 17:15; Judges 12:4–6"> So David’s army marched into the field to engage Israel, and the battle took place in the forest of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 2 Samuel 2:31; 2 Samuel 2:26"> There the people of Israel were defeated by David’s servants, and the slaughter was great that day—twenty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:10; Joshua 10:11; Psalms 43:1"> The battle spread over the whole countryside, and that day the forest devoured more people than the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; Proverbs 30:17; Job 31:3"> Now Absalom was riding on his mule when he met the servants of David, and as the mule went under the thick branches of a large oak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or large terebinth or very great tree; similarly in verses 10 and 14">b</a></sup>Absalom’s head was caught fast in the tree, and he was left hanging between heaven and earth while the mule under him kept going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> When one of the men saw this, he told Joab, “I just saw Absalom hanging in an oak tree!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “You just saw him!” Joab exclaimed. “Why did you not strike him to the ground right there? I would have given you ten shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10 shekels is approximately 4 ounces or 114 grams of silver.">c</a></sup>and a warrior’s belt!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5"> The man replied, “Even if a thousand shekels of silver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1,000 shekels is approximately 25.1 pounds or 11.4 kilograms of silver.">d</a></sup>were weighed out into my hands, I would not raise my hand against the son of the king. For we heard the king command you and Abishai and Ittai, ‘Protect the young man Absalom for my sake.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A few Hebrew manuscripts, LXX, Vulgate, and Syriac; most Hebrew manuscripts Whoever you may be, protect the young man Absalom. or Let no one touch the young man Absalom.">e</a></sup>’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19–20; Hebrews 4:13; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> If I had jeopardized my own life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or If I had dealt treacherously against his life">f</a></sup>—and nothing is hidden from the king—you would have abandoned me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Matthew 12:40; 2 Samuel 14:30"> But Joab declared, “I am not going to wait like this with you!” And he took three spears in his hand and thrust them through the heart of Absalom while he was still alive in the oak tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And ten young men who carried Joab’s armor surrounded Absalom, struck him, and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:28; 2 Samuel 20:22; 1 Corinthians 14:8"> Then Joab blew the ram’s horn, and the troops broke off their pursuit of Israel because Joab had restrained them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 19:8"> They took Absalom, cast him into a large pit in the forest, and piled a huge mound of stones over him. Meanwhile, all the Israelites fled, each to his home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 15:12"> During his lifetime, Absalom had set up for himself a pillar in the King’s Valley, for he had said, “I have no son to preserve the memory of my name.” So he gave the pillar his name, and to this day it is called Absalom’s Monument.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Mourns for Absalom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 18:31; Psalms 9:4"> Then Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, “Please let me run and tell the king the good news that the LORD has avenged him of his enemies.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 17:16–21"> But Joab replied, “You are not the man to take good news today. You may do it another day, but you must not do so today, because the king’s son is dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So Joab said to a Cushite, “Go, tell the king what you have seen.” The Cushite bowed to Joab and took off running.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Ephesians 5:4"> Ahimaaz son of Zadok, however, persisted and said to Joab, “No matter what, please let me also run behind the Cushite!”<br />“My son,” Joab replied, “why do you want to run, since you will not receive a reward?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:4"> “No matter what, I want to run!” he replied.<br />“Then run!” Joab told him.<br />So Ahimaaz ran by way of the plain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, the plain of the Jordan">g</a></sup>and outran the Cushite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:8; 2 Samuel 13:34; Ezekiel 33:2–7"> Now David was sitting between the two gates when the watchman went up to the roof of the gateway by the wall, looked out, and saw a man running alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So he called out and told the king.<br />“If he is alone,” the king replied, “he bears good news.”<br />As the first runner drew near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> the watchman saw another man running, and he called out to the gatekeeper, “Look! Another man is running alone!”<br />“This one also brings good news,” said the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 9:20; 1 Kings 1:42; Romans 10:15"> The watchman said, “The first man appears to me to be running like Ahimaaz son of Zadok.”<br />“This is a good man,” said the king. “He comes with good news.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Genesis 14:20; Genesis 24:27"> Then Ahimaaz called out to the king, “All is well!” And he bowed facedown before the king.<br />He continued, “Blessed be the LORD your God! He has delivered up the men who raised their hands against my lord the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22; 2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 18:19–20"> The king asked, “Is the young man Absalom all right?”<br />And Ahimaaz replied, “When Joab sent the king’s servant and your servant, I saw a great tumult, but I do not know what it was.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> “Move aside,” said the king, “and stand here.”<br />So he stepped aside.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 18:7–8"> Just then the Cushite came and said, “May my lord the king hear the good news: Today the LORD has avenged you of all who rose up against you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 5:31; 1 Samuel 25:26; Daniel 4:19"> The king asked the Cushite, “Is the young man Absalom all right?”<br />And the Cushite replied, “May the enemies of my lord the king and all who rise up against you to harm you be like that young man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:4; 2 Samuel 12:10–23; Proverbs 17:25"> The king was shaken and went up to the chamber over the gate and wept. And as he walked, he cried out, “O my son Absalom! My son, my son Absalom! If only I had died instead of you, O Absalom, my son, my son!”</span>
</div>
</div>
