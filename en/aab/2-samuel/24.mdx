## David’s Military Census

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1; 1 Chronicles 27:23–24; James 1:13–14"> Again the anger of the LORD burned against Israel, and He stirred up David against them, saying, “Go and take a census of Israel and Judah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:10; Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 20:23"> So the king said to Joab the commander of his army, who was with him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX to Joab and the army commanders with him">a</a></sup>“Go now throughout the tribes of Israel from Dan to Beersheba and register the troops, so that I may know their number.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Psalms 115:14; Proverbs 14:28"> But Joab replied to the king, “May the LORD your God multiply the troops a hundred times over, and may the eyes of my lord the king see it. But why does my lord the king want to do such a thing?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Ecclesiastes 8:4; 1 Chronicles 21:4"> Nevertheless, the king’s word prevailed against Joab and against the commanders of the army. So Joab and the commanders of the army departed from the presence of the king to register the troops of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:16"> They crossed the Jordan and camped near Aroer, south of the town in the middle of the valley, and proceeded toward Gad and Jazer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 19:28; Genesis 31:47–48; Judges 18:28–29"> Then they went to Gilead and the land of Tahtim-hodshi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew; some LXX manuscripts to Gilead and to the land of the Hittites">b</a></sup>and on to Dan-jaan and around to Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:29; Joshua 11:3; Genesis 21:22–33"> They went toward the fortress of Tyre and all the cities of the Hivites and Canaanites. Finally, they went on to the Negev of Judah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or to the south of Judah">c</a></sup>to Beersheba.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> At the end of nine months and twenty days, having gone through the whole land, they returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:23–24; 1 Chronicles 21:5–6; Numbers 1:44–46"> And Joab reported to the king the total number of the troops. In Israel there were 800,000 men of valor who drew the sword, and in Judah there were 500,000.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment for David’s Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 13:13; Proverbs 28:13"> After David had numbered the troops, his conscience was stricken and he said to the LORD, “I have sinned greatly in what I have done. Now, O LORD, I beg You to take away the iniquity of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:5; 1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> When David got up in the morning, the word of the LORD had come to Gad the prophet, David’s seer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6–10; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12"> “Go and tell David that this is what the LORD says: ‘I am offering you three options. Choose one of them, and I will carry it out against you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:12; Leviticus 26:25; 2 Samuel 21:1"> So Gad went and said to David, “Do you choose to endure three<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="LXX (see also 1 Chronicles 21:12); Hebrew seven">d</a></sup>years of famine in your land, three months of fleeing the pursuit of your enemies, or three days of plague upon your land? Now then, think it over and decide how I should reply to Him who sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:1; Psalms 119:156; 1 Chronicles 21:13"> David answered Gad, “I am deeply distressed. Please, let us fall into the hand of the LORD, for His mercies are great; but do not let me fall into the hands of men.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Plague on Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:14; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Numbers 25:9"> So the LORD sent a plague upon Israel from that morning until the appointed time, and seventy thousand of the people from Dan to Beersheba died.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally and of the people from Dan to Beersheba, seventy thousand men died">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:13–14; Genesis 6:6; Exodus 12:23"> But when the Angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The destroying Angel is the Angel of the LORD (vv. 16-17; 1 Chr 21:15-16). See note on Gen 16:7.">f</a></sup>stretched out His hand to destroy Jerusalem, the LORD relented from the calamity and said to the Angel who was destroying the people, “Enough! Withdraw Your hand now!” At that time the Angel of the LORD was by the threshing floor of Araunah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Araunah is a variant of Ornan; see 1 Chronicles 21:15 and 2 Chronicles 3:1.">g</a></sup>the Jebusite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; 1 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Samuel 24:10"> When David saw the Angel striking down the people, he said to the LORD, “Surely I, the shepherd,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="DSS and LXX; MT does not include the shepherd.">h</a></sup>have sinned and acted wickedly. But these sheep, what have they done? Please, let Your hand fall upon me and my father’s house.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David Builds an Altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:18–30; 2 Samuel 24:11"> And that day Gad came to David and said to him, “Go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> So David went up at the word of Gad, just as the LORD had commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Ruth 2:10; 1 Chronicles 21:20–21"> When Araunah looked out and saw the king and his servants coming toward him, he went out and bowed facedown before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:22; Numbers 25:8; 2 Samuel 21:3–14"> “Why has my lord the king come to his servant?” Araunah said.<br />“To buy your threshing floor,” David replied, “that I may build an altar to the LORD, so that the plague upon the people may be halted.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:21; 1 Samuel 6:14; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> Araunah said to David, “May my lord the king take whatever seems good to him and offer it up. Here are the oxen for an ascension offering and the threshing sledges and ox yokes for the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:40–41; Isaiah 32:8; Psalms 20:3–4"> O king, Araunah gives all these to the king.” He also said to the king, “May the LORD your God accept you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:24–25; Malachi 1:12–14; Genesis 23:13"> “No,” replied the king, “I insist on paying a price, for I will not offer to the LORD my God ascension offerings that cost me nothing.”<br />So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="50 shekels is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of silver.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Lamentations 3:32–33; Genesis 8:20"> And there he built an altar to the LORD and offered ascension offerings and well-being offerings.<br />Then the LORD answered the prayers on behalf of the land, and the plague upon Israel was halted.</span>
</div>
</div>
