## David’s Mighty Men

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> but Eleazar stood his ground and struck the Philistines until his hand grew weary and stuck to his sword. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> And after him was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. When the Philistines had banded together near a field full of lentils, Israel’s troops fled from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalms 3:8"> But Shammah took his stand in the middle of the field, defended it, and struck down the Philistines. So the LORD brought about a great victory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> At harvest time, three of the thirty chief men went down to David at the cave of Adullam, while a company of Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> At that time David was in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was at Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> David longed for water and said, “Oh, that someone would get me a drink of water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> So the three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well near the gate of Bethlehem, and brought it back to David. But he refused to drink it; instead, he poured it out to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> saying, “Far be it from me, O LORD, to do this! Is this not the blood of the men who risked their lives?” So he refused to drink it.<br />Such were the exploits of the three mighty men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> Now Abishai, the brother of Joab and son of Zeruiah, was chief of the Three,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Most Hebrew manuscripts (see also 1 Chronicles 11:20); two Hebrew manuscripts and Syriac the Thirty">f</a></sup>and he wielded his spear against three hundred men, killed them, and won a name along with the Three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> Was he not more honored than the Three?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew; Syriac the Thirty">g</a></sup>And he became their commander, even though he was not included among the Three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These were the exploits of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who won a name along with the three mighty men.</span>
