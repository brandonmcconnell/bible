## The Murder of Ish-bosheth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 4:4; Isaiah 13:7; Jeremiah 6:24"> Now when Ish-bosheth son of Saul heard that Abner had died in Hebron, he lost courage, and all Israel was dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 9:17; 2 Kings 6:23"> Saul’s son had two men who were leaders of raiding parties. One was named Baanah and the other Rechab; they were sons of Rimmon the Beerothite of the tribe of Benjamin—Beeroth is considered part of Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:33; 1 Samuel 31:7"> because the Beerothites fled to Gittaim and have lived there as foreigners to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Chronicles 9:40"> And Jonathan son of Saul had a son who was lame in his feet. He was five years old when the report about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse picked him up and fled, but as she was hurrying to escape, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; 2 Chronicles 25:27; 1 Kings 16:9"> Now Rechab and Baanah, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, set out and arrived at the house of Ish-bosheth in the heat of the day, while the king was taking his midday nap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 20:10; 2 Samuel 3:27"> They entered the interior of the house as if to get some wheat, and they stabbed him in the stomach. Then Rechab and his brother Baanah slipped away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:11; Mark 6:28–29; 1 Samuel 17:54"> They had entered the house while Ish-bosheth was lying on his bed, and having stabbed and killed him, they beheaded him, took his head, and traveled all night by way of the Arabah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; 2 Samuel 22:48; 1 Samuel 19:2–11"> They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, “Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy who sought your life. Today the LORD has granted vengeance to my lord the king against Saul and his offspring.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Execution of Rechab and Baanah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 1:29; Genesis 48:16; Psalms 107:2"> But David answered Rechab and his brother Baanah, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, “As surely as the LORD lives, who has redeemed my life from all distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2–16"> when someone told me, ‘Look, Saul is dead,’ and thought he was a bearer of good news, I seized him and put him to death at Ziklag. That was his reward for his news!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:12; Numbers 35:31–34; Genesis 9:5–6"> How much more, when wicked men kill a righteous man in his own house and on his own bed, shall I not now require his blood from your hands and remove you from the earth!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:15; 2 Samuel 3:32; Matthew 7:2"> So David commanded his young men, and they killed Rechab and Baanah. They cut off their hands and feet and hung their bodies by the pool in Hebron, but they took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in Abner’s tomb in Hebron.</span>
</div>
</div>
