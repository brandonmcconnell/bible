## The Two Silver Trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13; Joel 1:14; Psalms 81:3"> “Make two trumpets of hammered silver to be used for calling the congregation and for having the camps set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Joel 2:15–16"> When both are sounded, the whole congregation is to assemble before you at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 1:4–16"> But if only one is sounded, then the leaders, the heads of the clans of Israel, are to gather before you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 10:14; Isaiah 58:1"> When you sound short blasts, the camps that lie on the east side are to set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:10–16"> When you sound the short blasts a second time, the camps that lie on the south side are to set out. The blasts are to signal them to set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Numbers 10:3–4"> To convene the assembly, you are to sound long blasts, not short ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:4–16; Numbers 31:6; 1 Chronicles 15:24"> The sons of Aaron, the priests, are to sound the trumpets. This shall be a permanent statute for you and the generations to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:18; Judges 2:18; Genesis 8:1"> When you enter into battle in your land against an adversary who attacks you, sound short blasts on the trumpets, and you will be remembered before the LORD your God and saved from your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 81:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26"> And on your joyous occasions, your appointed feasts, and the beginning of each month, you are to blow the trumpets over your ascension offerings and well-being offerings to serve as a reminder for you before your God. I am the LORD your God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## From Sinai to Paran

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:17; Numbers 1:1; Numbers 9:11"> On the twentieth day of the second month of the second year, the cloud was lifted from above the tabernacle of the Testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 21:21; Numbers 12:16; Numbers 9:5"> and the Israelites set out from the Wilderness of Sinai, traveling from place to place until the cloud settled in the Wilderness of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Numbers 9:23"> They set out this first time according to the LORD’s command through Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 7:12"> First, the divisions of the camp of Judah set out under their standard, with Nahshon son of Amminadab in command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 1:8"> Nethanel son of Zuar was over the division of the tribe of Issachar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 1:9"> and Eliab son of Helon was over the division of the tribe of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:6–8; Numbers 1:51; Hebrews 12:28"> Then the tabernacle was taken down, and the Gershonites and the Merarites set out, transporting it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 2:10–16; Numbers 1:5; Numbers 26:5–18"> Then the divisions of the camp of Reuben set out under their standard, with Elizur son of Shedeur in command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 7:36"> Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai was over the division of the tribe of Simeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42"> and Eliasaph son of Deuel was over the division of the tribe of Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:4–16"> Then the Kohathites set out, transporting the holy objects; the tabernacle was to be set up before their arrival.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 2:18–24; Numbers 1:10; Numbers 26:23–41"> Next, the divisions of the camp of Ephraim set out under their standard, with Elishama son of Ammihud in command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54"> Gamaliel son of Pedahzur was over the division of the tribe of Manasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60"> and Abidan son of Gideoni was over the division of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:9; Numbers 1:12; Genesis 49:16–17"> Finally, the divisions of the camp of Dan set out under their standard, serving as the rear guard for all units, with Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai in command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:72"> Pagiel son of Ocran was over the division of the tribe of Asher,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 7:78"> and Ahira son of Enan was over the division of the tribe of Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 2:34; Numbers 24:4–5; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> This was the order of march for the Israelite divisions as they set out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Judges 4:11; Exodus 3:1"> Then Moses said to Hobab, the son of Moses’ father-in-law Reuel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Reuel was also called Jethro; see Exodus 3:1.">a</a></sup>the Midianite, “We are setting out for the place of which the LORD said: ‘I will give it to you.’ Come with us, and we will treat you well, for the LORD has promised good things to Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:15–17; Psalms 45:10; Matthew 21:29"> “I will not go,” Hobab replied. “Instead, I am going back to my own land and my own people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 29:15; Galatians 6:2; 1 Corinthians 12:14–21"> “Please do not leave us,” Moses said, “since you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you can serve as our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 10:18; 1 John 1:3"> If you come with us, we will share with you whatever good things the LORD gives us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:33; Exodus 3:1; Psalms 95:11"> So they set out on a three-day journey from the mountain of the LORD, with the ark of the covenant of the LORD traveling ahead of them for those three days to seek a resting place for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 105:39"> And the cloud of the LORD was over them by day when they set out from the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 68:1–2; Isaiah 17:12–14; Psalms 132:8"> Whenever the ark set out, Moses would say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rise up, O LORD!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">May Your enemies be scattered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">may those who hate You flee before You.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Psalms 90:13–17; Isaiah 63:17"> And when it came to rest, he would say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Return, O LORD,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the countless thousands of Israel.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
