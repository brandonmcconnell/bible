## The Spies Explore Canaan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> “Send out for yourself men to spy out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites. From each of their fathers’ tribes send one man who is a leader among them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> So at the command<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally according to the mouth">a</a></sup>of the LORD, Moses sent them out from the Wilderness of Paran. All the men were leaders of the Israelites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and these were their names:<br />From the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> from the tribe of Manasseh (a tribe of Joseph), Gaddi son of Susi;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> These were the names of the men Moses sent to spy out the land; and Moses gave to Hoshea son of Nun the name Joshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> When Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, he told them, “Go up through the Negev and into the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> See what the land is like and whether its people are strong or weak, few or many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Is the land where they live good or bad? Are the cities where they dwell open camps or fortifications?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> Is the soil fertile or unproductive? Are there trees in it or not? Be courageous and bring back some of the fruit of the land.” (It was the season for the first ripe grapes.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> So they went up and spied out the land from the Wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> They went up through the Negev and came to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, dwelled. It had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> When they came to the Valley of Eshcol,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Eshcol means cluster; also in verse 24.">b</a></sup>they cut down a branch with a single cluster of grapes, which they carried on a pole between two men. They also took some pomegranates and figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Because of the cluster of grapes the Israelites cut there, that place was called the Valley of Eshcol.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Reports of the Spies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> After forty days the men returned from spying out the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> and they went back to Moses, Aaron, and the whole congregation of Israel in the Wilderness of Paran at Kadesh. They brought back a report for the whole congregation and showed them the fruit of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And they gave this account to Moses: “We went into the land to which you sent us, and indeed, it is flowing with milk and honey. Here is some of its fruit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> Nevertheless, the people living in the land are strong, and the cities are large and fortified. We even saw the descendants of Anak there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> The Amalekites live in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites live in the hill country; and the Canaanites live by the sea and along the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> Then Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, “We must go up and take possession of the land, for we can certainly conquer it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> But the men who had gone up with him replied, “We cannot go up against the people, for they are stronger than we are!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> So they gave the Israelites a bad report about the land that they had spied out: “The land we explored devours its inhabitants, and all the people we saw there are great in stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> We even saw the Nephilim there—the descendants of Anak that come from the Nephilim! We seemed like grasshoppers in our own sight, and we must have seemed the same to them!”</span>
</div>
</div>
