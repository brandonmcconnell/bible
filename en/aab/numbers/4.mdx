## The Duties of the Kohathites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> “Take a census of the Kohathites among the Levites by their clans and families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3–5; 2 Kings 11:4–12"> men from thirty to fifty years old—everyone who is qualified to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Mark 13:34; Numbers 4:30"> This service of the Kohathites at the Tent of Meeting regards the most holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3; Exodus 40:3"> Whenever the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons are to go in, take down the veil of the curtain, and cover the ark of the Testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the ark of the covenant">a</a></sup>with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:13–15; Exodus 35:19; Numbers 4:7–8"> They are to place over this a covering of fine leather,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Possibly the hides of large aquatic mammals; also in verses 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, and 25; see Exodus 25:5.">b</a></sup>spread a solid blue cloth over it, and insert its poles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 37:10–16; Exodus 25:23–30"> Over the table of the Presence they are to spread a blue cloth and place the plates and cups on it, along with the bowls and pitchers for the drink offering. The regular bread offering is to remain on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:6–7; Numbers 4:11–13; Numbers 4:9"> And they shall spread a scarlet cloth over them, cover them with fine leather, and insert the poles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Exodus 37:17–24; Revelation 1:20–1"> They are to take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand used for light, together with its lamps, wick trimmers, and trays, as well as the jars of oil with which to supply it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:12; Numbers 4:6"> Then they shall wrap it and all its utensils inside a covering of fine leather and put it on the carrying frame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Exodus 30:1–19; Exodus 40:26–27"> Over the gold altar they are to spread a blue cloth, cover it with fine leather, and insert the poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:29; Exodus 25:9; 2 Chronicles 4:22"> They are to take all the utensils for serving in the sanctuary, place them in a blue cloth, cover them with fine leather, and put them on the carrying frame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Exodus 39:1; Exodus 39:41"> Then they shall remove the ashes from the bronze altar, spread a purple cloth over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:19; Exodus 38:1–7"> and place on it all the vessels used to serve there: the firepans, meat forks, shovels, and sprinkling bowls—all the equipment of the altar. They are to spread over it a covering of fine leather and insert the poles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 7:9; Deuteronomy 31:9"> When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the holy objects and all their equipment, as soon as the camp is ready to move, the Kohathites shall come and do the carrying. But they must not touch the holy objects, or they will die. These are the transportation duties of the Kohathites regarding the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:6; Leviticus 24:2; Exodus 30:23–38"> Eleazar son of Aaron the priest shall oversee the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the daily grain offering, and the anointing oil. He has oversight of the entire tabernacle and everything in it, including the holy objects and their utensils.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 17:10; Numbers 18:5"> “Do not allow the Kohathite tribal clans to be cut off from among the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 4:15"> In order that they may live and not die when they come near the most holy things, do this for them: Aaron and his sons are to go in and assign each man his task and what he is to carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 19:21; Numbers 4:15"> But the Kohathites are not to go in and look at the holy objects, even for a moment, or they will die.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Duties of the Gershonites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 3:18; Numbers 3:21; Numbers 3:24"> “Take a census of the Gershonites as well, by their families and clans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:7"> from thirty to fifty years old, counting everyone who comes to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Numbers 4:31–32; Numbers 4:47"> This is the service of the Gershonite clans regarding work and transport:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 3:25–26; Exodus 26:14; Numbers 7:5–7"> They are to carry the curtains of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting with the covering of fine leather over it, the curtains for the entrance to the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 27:9"> the curtains of the courtyard, and the curtains for the entrance at the gate of the courtyard that surrounds the tabernacle and altar, along with their ropes and all the equipment for their service. The Gershonites will do all that needs to be done with these items.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:70; 1 Corinthians 11:2"> All the service of the Gershonites—all their transport duties and other work—is to be done at the direction of Aaron and his sons; you are to assign to them all that they are responsible to carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; 1 Corinthians 12:5–6"> This is the service of the Gershonite clans at the Tent of Meeting, and their duties shall be under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Duties of the Merarites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 3:33–35"> As for the sons of Merari, you are to number them by their clans and families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 2:4"> from thirty to fifty years old, counting everyone who comes to serve in the work of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 3:36–37; Exodus 26:15; Numbers 7:8–9"> This is the duty for all their service at the Tent of Meeting: to carry the frames of the tabernacle with its crossbars, posts, and bases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; 1 Chronicles 9:29; Numbers 3:8"> and the posts of the surrounding courtyard with their bases, tent pegs, and ropes, including all their equipment and everything related to their use. You shall assign by name the items that they are responsible to carry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 4:28; Joshua 3:6; Isaiah 3:6"> This is the service of the Merarite clans according to all their work at the Tent of Meeting, under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Numbering of the Levite Clans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of the congregation numbered the Kohathites by their clans and families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> everyone from thirty to fifty years old who came to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And those numbered by their clans totaled 2,750.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> These were counted from the Kohathite clans, everyone who could serve at the Tent of Meeting. Moses and Aaron numbered them according to the command of the LORD through Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then the Gershonites were numbered by their clans and families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> everyone from thirty to fifty years old who came to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:32"> And those numbered by their clans and families totaled 2,630.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> These were counted from the Gershonite clans who served at the Tent of Meeting, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the LORD’s command.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And the Merarites were numbered by their clans and families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> everyone from thirty to fifty years old who came to serve in the work at the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Corinthians 12:8–12"> The men registered by their clans numbered 3,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 4:29"> These were counted from the Merarite clans, whom Moses and Aaron numbered at the LORD’s command through Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> So Moses, Aaron, and the leaders of Israel numbered by their clans and families all the Levites</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–31; Romans 12:6–8; Numbers 4:3"> from thirty to fifty years old who came to do the work of serving and carrying the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Matthew 20:16; Matthew 22:15"> And the number of men was 8,580.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 1:54; Numbers 4:31; Numbers 4:15"> At the LORD’s command through Moses they were numbered, and each one was assigned his work and burden, as the LORD had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
