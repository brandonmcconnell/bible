## The Boundaries of Canaan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Command the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land of Canaan, it will be allotted to you as an inheritance with these boundaries:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> Your southern border will extend from the Wilderness of Zin along the border of Edom. On the east, your southern border will run from the end of the Salt Sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea; also in verse 12">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> cross south of the Ascent of Akrabbim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or the Ascent of Scorpions or Scorpion Pass">b</a></sup>continue to Zin, and go south of Kadesh-barnea. Then it will go on to Hazar-addar and proceed to Azmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> where it will turn from Azmon, join the Brook of Egypt, and end at the Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea; also in verses 6 and 7">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> Your western border will be the coastline of the Great Sea; this will be your boundary on the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> Your northern border will run from the Great Sea directly to Mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> and from Mount Hor to Lebo-hamath, then extend to Zedad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> continue to Ziphron, and end at Hazar-enan. This will be your boundary on the north.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And your eastern border will run straight from Hazar-enan to Shepham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> then go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain and continue along the slopes east of the Sea of Chinnereth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> Then the border will go down along the Jordan and end at the Salt Sea.<br />This will be your land, defined by its borders on all sides.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> So Moses commanded the Israelites, “Apportion this land by lot as an inheritance. The LORD has commanded that it be given to the nine and a half tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> For the tribes of the Reubenites and Gadites, along with the half-tribe of Manasseh, have already received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> These two and a half tribes have received their inheritance across the Jordan from Jericho, toward the sunrise.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Leaders to Divide the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These are the names of the men who are to assign the land as an inheritance for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> Appoint one leader from each tribe to distribute the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> These are their names:<br />Caleb son of Jephunneh from the tribe of Judah;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> Shemuel son of Ammihud from the tribe of Simeon;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> Elidad son of Chislon from the tribe of Benjamin;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Bukki son of Jogli, a leader from the tribe of Dan;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Hanniel son of Ephod, a leader from the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Kemuel son of Shiphtan, a leader from the tribe of Ephraim;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Eli-zaphan son of Parnach, a leader from the tribe of Zebulun;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Paltiel son of Azzan, a leader from the tribe of Issachar;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Ahihud son of Shelomi, a leader from the tribe of Asher;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and Pedahel son of Ammihud, a leader from the tribe of Naphtali.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are the ones whom the LORD commanded to apportion the inheritance to the Israelites in the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
</div>
