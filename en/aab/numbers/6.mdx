## The Nazirite Vow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Amos 2:11–12; Romans 1:1"> “Speak to the Israelites and tell them that if a man or woman makes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Nazirite means one separated or one consecrated.">a</a></sup>to separate himself to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 5:23; Amos 2:12"> he is to abstain from wine and strong drink. He must not drink vinegar made from wine or strong drink, and he must not drink any grape juice or eat fresh grapes or raisins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; Numbers 6:21; Numbers 6:8–9"> All the days of his separation, he is not to eat anything that comes from the grapevine, not even the seeds or skins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 16:17; Judges 13:5"> For the entire period of his vow of separation, no razor shall touch his head. He must be holy until the time of his separation to the LORD is complete; he must let the hair of his head grow long.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 19:28; Leviticus 21:1–3"> Throughout the days of his separation to the LORD, he must not go near a dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 9:6; Leviticus 21:1–2; Leviticus 21:10–12"> Even if his father or mother or brother or sister should die, he is not to defile himself, because the symbol of consecration to his God is upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18"> Throughout the time of his separation, he is holy to the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:18; Acts 18:18; Leviticus 14:9"> If someone suddenly dies in his presence and defiles his consecrated head of hair, he must shave his head on the day of his cleansing—the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:29; Leviticus 1:14; Leviticus 15:14"> On the eighth day he must bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 5:8–10"> And the priest is to offer one as a purification offering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or purification offering; here and throughout Numbers">b</a></sup>and the other as an ascension offering to effect purgation for him, because he has sinned by being in the presence of the dead body. On that day he must consecrate his head again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Matthew 24:13; Leviticus 14:24"> He must rededicate his time of separation to the LORD and bring a year-old male lamb as a reparation offering. But the preceding days shall not be counted, because his separation was defiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 21:26"> Now this is the law of the Nazirite when his time of separation is complete: He must be brought to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 3:6; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:2–3"> and he is to present an offering to the LORD of an unblemished year-old male lamb as an ascension offering, an unblemished year-old female lamb as a purification offering, and an unblemished ram as a well-being offering—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Exodus 29:2; Numbers 15:10"> together with their grain offerings and drink offerings—and a basket of unleavened cakes made from fine flour mixed with oil and unleavened wafers coated with oil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The priest is to present all these before the LORD and make the purification offering and the ascension offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He shall also offer the ram as a well-being offering to the LORD, along with the basket of unleavened bread. And the priest is to offer the accompanying grain offering and drink offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:9; Acts 18:18"> Then at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the Nazirite is to shave his consecrated head, take the hair, and put it on the fire under the well-being offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:15; Leviticus 7:30; Exodus 29:23–28"> And the priest is to take the boiled shoulder from the ram, one unleavened cake from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and put them into the hands of the Nazirite who has just shaved the hair of his consecration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 5:25"> The priest shall then wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. This is a holy portion for the priest, in addition to the breast of the wave offering and the thigh that was presented. After that, the Nazirite may drink wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Ezra 2:69; Numbers 5:29"> This is the law of the Nazirite who vows his offering to the LORD for his separation, in addition to whatever else he can afford; he must fulfill whatever vow he makes, according to the law of his separation.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Aaron’s Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then the LORD said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Genesis 48:20"> “Tell Aaron and his sons: This is how you are to bless the Israelites. Say to them:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; Psalms 121:4–7; Philippians 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘May the LORD bless you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and keep you;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Psalms 119:135; Psalms 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">may the LORD cause His face to shine upon you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be gracious to you;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 29:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">may the LORD lift up His countenance toward you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and give you peace.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Daniel 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 28:10"> So they shall put My name on the Israelites, and I will bless them.”</span>
</div>
