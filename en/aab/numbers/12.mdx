## The Complaint of Miriam and Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:37"> Then Miriam and Aaron criticized Moses because of the Cushite woman he had married, for he had taken a Cushite wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:12–13; Numbers 16:3; Micah 6:4"> “Does the LORD speak only through Moses?” they said. “Does He not also speak through us?” And the LORD heard this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; James 3:13; 2 Corinthians 11:5"> Now Moses was a very humble man, more so than any man on the face of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:9; Numbers 16:16–21"> And suddenly the LORD said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, “You three, come out to the Tent of Meeting.” So the three went out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalms 99:7"> and the LORD came down in a pillar of cloud, stood at the entrance to the Tent, and summoned Aaron and Miriam. When both of them had stepped forward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> He said, “Hear now My words:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">If there is a prophet among you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I, the LORD, will reveal Myself to him in a vision;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will speak to him in a dream.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalms 105:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But this is not so with My servant Moses;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he is faithful in all My house.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Hebrews 3:5">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I speak with him face to face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">clearly and not in riddles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he sees the form of the LORD.</span><br />Why then were you unafraid to speak against My servant Moses?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Hosea 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> So the anger of the LORD burned against them, and He departed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; 2 Kings 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> As the cloud lifted from above the Tent, suddenly Miriam became leprous,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew word traditionally translated as leprous was used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.">b</a></sup>white as snow. Aaron turned toward her, saw that she was leprous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> and said to Moses, “My lord, please do not hold against us this sin we have so foolishly committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> Please do not let her be like a stillborn infant whose flesh is half consumed when he comes out of his mother’s womb.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> So Moses cried out to the LORD, “O God, please heal her!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> But the LORD answered Moses, “If her father had but spit in her face, would she not have been in disgrace for seven days? Let her be confined outside the camp for seven days; after that she may be brought back in.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Micah 7:8–9; Micah 6:4"> So Miriam was confined outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not move on until she was brought in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:35; Numbers 33:18; Numbers 10:12"> After that, the people set out from Hazeroth and camped in the Wilderness of Paran.</span>
</div>
</div>
