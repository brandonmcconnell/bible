## Laws about Vows

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; Exodus 18:25"> Then Moses said to the heads of the tribes of Israel, “This is what the LORD has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 116:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> If a man makes a vow to the LORD or swears an oath to obligate himself by a pledge, he must not break his word; he must do everything he has promised.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Matthew 5:33">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And if a woman in her father’s house during her youth makes a vow to the LORD or obligates herself by a pledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and her father hears about her vow or pledge but says nothing to her, then all the vows or pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Matthew 15:4–6; Ephesians 6:1"> But if her father prohibits her on the day he hears about it, then none of the vows or pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand. The LORD will absolve her because her father has prohibited her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:12"> If a woman marries while under a vow or rash promise by which she has bound herself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and her husband hears of it but says nothing to her on that day, then the vows or pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 14:34"> But if her husband prohibits her when he hears of it, he nullifies the vow that binds her or the rash promise she has made, and the LORD will absolve her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:37; Romans 7:2; Leviticus 21:7"> Every vow a widow or divorced woman pledges to fulfill is binding on her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If a woman in her husband’s house has made a vow or put herself under an obligation with an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and her husband hears of it but says nothing to her and does not prohibit her, then all the vows or pledges by which she has bound herself shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 30:8; 1 Corinthians 11:3; Numbers 15:25"> But if her husband nullifies them on the day he hears of them, then nothing that came from her lips, whether her vows or pledges, shall stand. Her husband has nullified them, and the LORD will absolve her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Isaiah 58:5"> Her husband may confirm or nullify any vow or any sworn pledge to deny herself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or to fast">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But if her husband says nothing to her from day to day, then he confirms all the vows and pledges that bind her. He has confirmed them, because he said nothing to her on the day he heard about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> But if he nullifies them after he hears of them, then he will bear her iniquity.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54–57; Leviticus 11:46–47; Numbers 5:29–30"> These are the statutes that the LORD commanded Moses concerning the relationship between a man and his wife, and between a father and a young daughter still in his home.</span>
</div>
</div>
