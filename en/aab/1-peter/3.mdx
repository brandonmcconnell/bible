## Wives and Husbands

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> Wives, in the same way, submit yourselves to your husbands, so that even if they refuse to believe the word, they will be won over without words by the behavior of their wives</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> when they see your pure and reverent demeanor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> Your beauty should not come from outward adornment, such as braided hair or gold jewelry or fine clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> but from the inner disposition of your heart, the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is precious in God’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For this is how the holy women of the past adorned themselves. They put their hope in God and were submissive to their husbands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> just as Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him lord. And you are her children if you do what is right and refuse to give way to fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> Husbands, in the same way, treat your wives with consideration as a delicate vessel, and with honor as fellow heirs of the gracious gift of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Turning from Evil

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, all of you, be like-minded and sympathetic, love as brothers, be tenderhearted and humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult, but with blessing, because to this you were called so that you may inherit a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Whoever would love life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and see good days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">must keep his tongue from evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his lips from deceitful speech.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He must turn from evil and do good;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he must seek peace and pursue it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and His ears are inclined to their prayer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the face of the Lord is against</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who do evil.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Psalms 34:12–16 (see also LXX)">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Who can harm you if you are zealous for what is good?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Suffering for Righteousness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But even if you should suffer for what is right, you are blessed. “Do not fear what they fear; do not be shaken.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Do not fear their threats; do not be shaken.” Isaiah 8:12">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> But in your hearts sanctify Christ as Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts. This sentence may also be included with the quotation from the previous verse; see Isaiah 8:13.">c</a></sup>Always be prepared to give a defense to everyone who asks you the reason for the hope that is in you. But respond with gentleness and respect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> keeping a clear conscience, so that those who slander you may be put to shame by your good behavior in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For it is better, if it is God’s will, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For Christ also suffered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="NE and WH died">d</a></sup>for sins once for all, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring you to God. He was put to death in the body but made alive in the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> in whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or in the spirit, 19 in which">e</a></sup>He also went and preached to the spirits in prison</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> who disobeyed long ago when God waited patiently in the days of Noah while the ark was being built.<br />In the ark a few people, only eight souls, were saved through water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> And this water symbolizes the baptism that now saves you also—not the removal of dirt from the body, but the pledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or appeal for">f</a></sup>a clear conscience toward God—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> who has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of God, with angels, authorities, and powers subject to Him.</span>
</div>
