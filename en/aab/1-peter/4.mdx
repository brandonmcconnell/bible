## Living for God’s Glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 3:18; Colossians 3:3–5"> Therefore, since Christ suffered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="BYZ and TR include for us.">a</a></sup>in His body, arm yourselves with the same resolve, because anyone who has suffered in his body is done with sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22–24; 1 Peter 1:14; Titus 3:3–8"> Consequently, he does not live out his remaining time on earth for human passions, but for the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:5"> For you have spent enough time in the past carrying out the same desires as the Gentiles: living in debauchery, lust, drunkenness, orgies, carousing, and detestable idolatry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; 1 Peter 2:12; Jude 1:10"> Because of this, they consider it strange of you not to plunge with them into the same flood of reckless indiscretion, and they heap abuse on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:9; Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:1"> But they will have to give an account to Him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:2; 1 Peter 3:19; Romans 8:9–11"> That is why the gospel was preached even to those who are now dead,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or to those who are dead">b</a></sup>so that they might be judged as men in the flesh, but live according to God in the spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:8–9; 1 Peter 1:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> The end of all things is near. Therefore be clear-minded and sober, so that you can pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Colossians 3:14; Proverbs 17:9"> Above all, love one another deeply, because love covers over a multitude of sins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Proverbs 10:12">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Romans 12:13; Philippians 2:14"> Show hospitality to one another without complaining.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> As good stewards of the manifold grace of God, each of you should use whatever gift he has received to serve one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Ephesians 6:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> If anyone speaks, he should speak as one conveying the words of God. If anyone serves, he should serve with the strength God provides, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Suffering as Christians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; 1 Peter 1:6–7; 1 Peter 5:9"> Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery trial that has come upon you, as though something strange were happening to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:17; James 1:2–3; Romans 5:3"> But rejoice that you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may be overjoyed at the revelation of His glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; Luke 6:22; Matthew 5:11"> If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests on you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="BYZ and TR include On their part He is spoken of as evil, but on your part He is glorified.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:13"> Indeed, none of you should suffer as a murderer or thief or wrongdoer, or even as a meddler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Philippians 1:29; 1 Peter 4:19"> But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed, but glorify God that you bear that name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally glorify God in that name; NA glorify God in this matter">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:29; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 2:9"> For it is time for judgment to begin with the family of God; and if it begins with us, what will the outcome be for those who disobey the gospel of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:31; Ezekiel 18:24; Luke 23:31"> And,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If it is hard for the righteous to be saved,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Proverbs 11:31 (see also LXX)">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 3:17"> So then, those who suffer according to God’s will should entrust their souls to their faithful Creator and continue to do good.</span>
</div>
</div>
