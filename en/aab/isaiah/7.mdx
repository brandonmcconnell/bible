## A Message to Ahaz

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> Now in the days that Ahaz son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, was king of Judah, Rezin king of Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Syria">a</a></sup>marched up to wage war against Jerusalem. He was accompanied by Pekah son of Remaliah the king of Israel, but he could not overpower the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> When it was reported to the house of David that Aram was in league with Ephraim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or had set up camp in Ephraim, that is, the northern kingdom of Israel">b</a></sup>the hearts of Ahaz and his people trembled like trees in the forest shaken by the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> Then the LORD said to Isaiah, “Go out with your son Shear-jashub<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Shear-jashub means a remnant shall return.">c</a></sup>to meet Ahaz at the end of the aqueduct that feeds the upper pool, on the road to the Launderer’s Field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> and say to him: Calm down and be quiet. Do not be afraid or disheartened over these two smoldering stubs of firewood—over the fierce anger of Rezin and Aram and of the son of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> For Aram, along with Ephraim and the son of Remaliah, has plotted your ruin, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> ‘Let us invade Judah, terrorize it, and divide it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew split it open">d</a></sup>among ourselves. Then we can install the son of Tabeal over it as king.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> But this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘It will not arise;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will not happen.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the head of Aram is Damascus,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the head of Damascus is Rezin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Within sixty-five years</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Ephraim will be shattered as a people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The head of Ephraim is Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the head of Samaria is the son of Remaliah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you do not stand firm in your faith,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then you will not stand at all.’”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sign of Immanuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> Again the LORD spoke to Ahaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> “Ask for a sign from the LORD your God, whether from the depths of Sheol or the heights of heaven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> But Ahaz replied, “I will not ask; I will not test the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> Then Isaiah said, “Hear now, O house of David! Is it not enough to try the patience of men? Will you try the patience of my God as well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore the Lord Himself will give you a sign: Behold, the virgin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or young woman">e</a></sup>will be with child and give birth to a son, and will call Him Immanuel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Immanuel means God with us; literally and she will call His name Immanuel; DSS and His name will be called Immanuel or and He will call His name Immanuel; LXX and you will call His name Immanuel; cited in Matthew 1:23.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> By the time He knows enough to reject evil and choose good, He will be eating curds and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> For before the boy knows enough to reject evil and choose good, the land of the two kings you dread will be laid waste.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment to Come

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> The LORD will bring on you and on your people and on the house of your father a time unlike any since the day Ephraim separated from Judah—He will bring the king of Assyria.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> On that day the LORD will whistle to the flies at the farthest streams of the Nile and to the bees in the land of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they will all come and settle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the steep ravines and clefts of the rocks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in all the thornbushes and watering holes.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> On that day the Lord will use a razor hired from beyond the Euphrates<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew the River">g</a></sup>—the king of Assyria—to shave your head and the hair of your legs, and to remove your beard as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> On that day a man will raise a young cow and two sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> and from the abundance of milk they give, he will eat curds; for all who remain in the land will eat curds and honey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> And on that day, in every place that had a thousand vines worth a thousand shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew a thousand of silver; that is, approximately 25.1 pounds or 11.4 kilograms of silver">h</a></sup>only briers and thorns will be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> Men will go there with bow and arrow, for the land will be covered with briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> For fear of the briers and thorns, you will no longer traverse the hills once tilled by the hoe; they will become places for oxen to graze and sheep to trample.</span>
</div>
</div>
