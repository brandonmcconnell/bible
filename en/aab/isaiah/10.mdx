## Woe to Tyrants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Micah 3:1–4; Matthew 23:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to those who enact unjust statutes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and issue oppressive decrees,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Isaiah 3:14; Isaiah 29:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">to deprive the poor of fair treatment</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and withhold justice from the oppressed of My people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to make widows their prey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and orphans their plunder.</span></span>

## Judgment on Assyria

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 30:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to Assyria, the rod of My anger;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the staff in their hands is My wrath.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Micah 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">But this is not his intention;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this is not his plan.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For it is in his heart to destroy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cut off many nations.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Kings 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> <span className="pl pl-2">“Is not Calno like Carchemish?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Is not Hamath like Arpad?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Is not Samaria like Damascus?</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 65:7; Jeremiah 50:18"> So when the Lord has completed all His work against Mount Zion and Jerusalem, He will say, “I will punish the king of Assyria for the fruit of his arrogant heart and the proud look in his eyes.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 45:9; Isaiah 10:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does an axe raise itself above the one who swings it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Does a saw boast over him who saws with it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It would be like a rod waving the one who lifts it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or a staff lifting him who is not wood!</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the Lord GOD of Hosts will send a wasting disease</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">among Assyria’s stout warriors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and under his pomp will be kindled</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a fire like a burning flame.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Isaiah 10:33–34; Isaiah 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The splendor of its forests and orchards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">both soul and body,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will completely destroy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a sickness consumes a man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:17; Isaiah 37:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">The remaining trees of its forests will be so few</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that a child could count them.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
