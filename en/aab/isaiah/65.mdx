## Judgments and Promises

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was found by those who did not seek Me.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Cited in Romans 10:20">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To a nation that did not call My name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I said, ‘Here I am! </div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My servants will eat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will go hungry;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My servants will drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will go thirsty;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My servants will rejoice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will be put to shame.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will leave behind your name</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a curse for My chosen ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the Lord GOD will slay you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but to His servants He will give another name.</span></span>
