## The Servant and Light to the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will turn all My mountains into roads,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and My highways will be raised up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, they will come from far away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the north and from the west,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or from the sea">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from the land of Aswan.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, a region in southern Egypt (from DSS); MT the land of Sinim">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 96:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shout for joy, O heavens; rejoice, O earth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">break forth in song, O mountains!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the LORD has comforted His people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He will have compassion on His afflicted ones.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:21; Psalms 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Can a woman forget her nursing child,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or lack compassion for the son of her womb?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even if she could forget,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not forget you!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 62:5; Ezekiel 28:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your builders</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="DSS; MT Your sons">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">hasten back;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your destroyers and wreckers depart from you.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Isaiah 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you will say in your heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Who has begotten these for me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I was bereaved and barren;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was exiled and rejected.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So who has reared them?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look, I was left all alone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so where did they come from?’”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 14:2"> This is what the Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I will lift up My hand to the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and raise My banner to the peoples.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will bring your sons in their arms</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew in their bosom">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and carry your daughters on their shoulders.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 60:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Kings will be your foster fathers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their queens your nursing mothers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will bow to you facedown</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lick the dust at your feet.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then you will know that I am the LORD;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who hope in Me will never be put to shame.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; Psalms 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Can the plunder be snatched from the mighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or the captives of a tyrant</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="DSS, Syriac, and Vulgate (see also verse 25); MT of the righteous">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be delivered?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
