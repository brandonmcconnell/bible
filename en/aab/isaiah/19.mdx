## The Burden against Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> This is the burden against Egypt:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the LORD rides on a swift cloud;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is coming to Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The idols of Egypt will tremble before Him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hearts of the Egyptians will melt within them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">“So I will incite Egyptian against Egyptian;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">brother will fight against brother, neighbor against neighbor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the spirit of the Egyptians will be emptied out from among them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will frustrate their plans,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that they will resort to idols and spirits of the dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to mediums and spiritists.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will deliver the Egyptians into the hands of a harsh master,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a fierce king will rule over them,”</span>declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters of the Nile will dry up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the riverbed will be parched and empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The canals will stink;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the streams of Egypt will trickle and dry up;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the reeds and rushes will wither.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The bulrushes by the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by the mouth of the river,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the fields sown along the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will wither, blow away, and be no more.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the fishermen will mourn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all who cast a hook into the Nile will lament,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who spread nets on the waters will pine away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The workers in flax will be dismayed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the weavers of fine linen will turn pale.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The workers in cloth</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or Its pillars">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be dejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the hired workers will be sick at heart.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The princes of Zoan are mere fools;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Pharaoh’s wise counselors give senseless advice.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How can you say to Pharaoh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I am one of the wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a son of eastern kings”?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where are your wise men now?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let them tell you and reveal</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The princes of Zoan have become fools;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the princes of Memphis</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX; Hebrew Noph">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are deceived.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The cornerstones of her tribes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">have led Egypt astray.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The LORD has poured into her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a spirit of confusion.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Egypt has been led astray in all she does,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a drunkard staggers through his own vomit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is nothing Egypt can do—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">head or tail, palm or reed.</span></span>
</div>

## A Blessing upon the Earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> In that day the Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble with fear beneath the uplifted hand of the LORD of Hosts, when He brandishes it against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> The land of Judah will bring terror to Egypt; whenever Judah is mentioned, Egypt will tremble over what the LORD of Hosts has planned against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> In that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts. One of them will be called the City of the Sun.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some MT manuscripts, DSS, and Vulgate; most MT manuscripts City of Destruction">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the center of the land of Egypt, and a pillar to the LORD near her border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, He will send them a savior and defender to rescue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John 17:3"> The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and on that day Egypt will acknowledge the LORD. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings; they will make vows to the LORD and fulfill them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> And the LORD will strike Egypt with a plague; He will strike them but heal them. They will turn to the LORD, and He will hear their prayers and heal them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians will go to Egypt, and the Egyptians to Assyria. The Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day Israel will join a three-party alliance with Egypt and Assyria—a blessing upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> The LORD of Hosts will bless them, saying, “Blessed be Egypt My people, Assyria My handiwork, and Israel My inheritance.”</span>
</div>
</div>
