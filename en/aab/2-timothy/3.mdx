## Evil in the Last Days

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> But understand this: In the last days terrible times will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:21; Romans 1:29–31; Jude 1:16"> For men will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; Romans 1:31; Matthew 10:21"> unloving, unforgiving, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, without love of good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; 1 Timothy 3:6; Jude 1:4"> traitorous, reckless, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 23:27–28"> having a form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from such as these!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 3:17; Titus 3:3"> They are the kind who worm their way into households and captivate vulnerable women who are weighed down with sins and led astray by various passions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 2:4"> who are always learning but never able to come to a knowledge of the truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:8–11; Revelation 2:20; 1 Timothy 6:5"> Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Jasher 79:27. The Book of Jashar or the Book of the Upright One is often cited as Jasher.">a</a></sup>so also these men oppose the truth. They are depraved in mind and disqualified from the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they will not advance much further. For just like Jannes and Jambres, their folly will be plain to everyone.</span>
</div>
</div>

## All Scripture Is God-Breathed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Titus 2:7; 2 Peter 1:5–7"> You, however, have observed my teaching, my conduct, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my perseverance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28"> my persecutions, and the sufferings that came upon me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. What persecutions I endured! Yet the Lord rescued me from all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:12–16; 1 Peter 2:20–21"> Indeed, all who desire to live godly lives in Christ Jesus will be persecuted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1; 2 Peter 2:20; Titus 3:3"> while evil men and imposters go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 2 Timothy 2:2; 2 Timothy 1:13"> But as for you, continue in the things you have learned and firmly believed, since you know from whom you have learned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:98–99; Luke 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> From infancy you have known the Holy Scriptures, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Hebrews 4:12; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for instruction, for conviction, for correction, and for training in righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:21; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> so that the man of God may be complete, fully equipped for every good work.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
