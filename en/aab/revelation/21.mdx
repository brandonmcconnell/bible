## A New Heaven and a New Earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Isaiah 65:17 and Isaiah 66:22 (see also LXX).">a</a></sup>for the first heaven and earth had passed away, and the sea was no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He will dwell with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be His people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and God Himself will be with them as their God.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="SBL, NE, WH, and BYZ God Himself will be with them.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Isaiah 25:8.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will be no more death</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or mourning or crying or pain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the former things have passed away.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> And the One seated on the throne said, “Behold, I make all things new.” Then He said, “Write this down, for these words are faithful and true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> And He told me, “It is done! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. To the thirsty I will give freely from the spring of the water of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> The one who overcomes will inherit all things, and I will be his God, and he will be My son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> But to the cowardly and unbelieving and abominable and murderers and sexually immoral and sorcerers and idolaters and all liars, their place will be in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur. This is the second death.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The New Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> Then one of the seven angels with the seven bowls full of the seven final plagues came and said to me, “Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> And he carried me away in the Spirit to a mountain great and high, and showed me the holy city of Jerusalem coming down out of heaven from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> shining with the glory of God. Its radiance was like a most precious jewel, like a jasper, as clear as crystal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> The city had a great and high wall with twelve gates inscribed with the names of the twelve tribes of Israel, and twelve angels at the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> There were three gates on the east, three on the north, three on the south, and three on the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> The wall of the city had twelve foundations bearing the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> The angel who spoke with me had a golden measuring rod to measure the city and its gates and walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> The city lies foursquare, with its width the same as its length. And he measured the city with the rod, and all its dimensions were equal—12,000 stadia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12,000 stadia is approximately 1,380 miles or 2,220 kilometers.">d</a></sup>in length and width and height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> And he measured its wall to be 144 cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="144 cubits is approximately 216 feet or 65.8 meters. The measure could indicate either height or thickness.">e</a></sup>by the human measure the angel was using.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> The wall was made of jasper, and the city itself of pure gold, as pure as glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> The foundations of the city walls were adorned with every kind of precious stone:<br />The first foundation was jasper,<br />the second sapphire,<br />the third chalcedony,<br />the fourth emerald,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth sardonyx,<br />the sixth carnelian,<br />the seventh chrysolite,<br />the eighth beryl,<br />the ninth topaz,<br />the tenth chrysoprase,<br />the eleventh jacinth,<br />and the twelfth amethyst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> And the twelve gates were twelve pearls, with each gate consisting of a single pearl. The main street of the city was pure gold, as clear as glass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> But I saw no temple in the city, because the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> And the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, because the glory of God illuminates the city, and the Lamb is its lamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> By its light the nations will walk, and into it the kings of the earth will bring their glory.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="BYZ and TR include and honor.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> Its gates will never be shut at the end of the day, because there will be no night there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> And into the city will be brought the glory and honor of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> But nothing unclean will ever enter it, nor anyone who practices an abomination or a lie, but only those whose names are written in the Lamb’s Book of Life.</span>
</div>
</div>
