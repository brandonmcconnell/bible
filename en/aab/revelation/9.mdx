## The Fifth Trumpet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:10; Romans 10:7; Revelation 9:2"> Then the fifth angel sounded his trumpet, and I saw a star that had fallen from heaven to earth, and it was given the key to the Abyss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Genesis 19:28; Joel 2:2"> The star opened the Abyss, and smoke rose out of it like the smoke of a great furnace, and the sun and the air were darkened by the smoke from the Abyss.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Revelation 9:5; Joel 2:25"> And out of the smoke, locusts descended on the earth, and they were given power like that of the scorpions of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 7:2–4; Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 14:1"> They were told not to harm the grass of the earth or any plant or tree, but only those who did not have the seal of God on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> The locusts were not given power to kill them, but only to torment them for five months, and their torment was like the stinging of a scorpion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> In those days men will seek death and will not find it; they will long to die, but death will escape them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> And the locusts looked like horses prepared for battle, with something like crowns of gold on their heads; and their faces were like the faces of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> They had hair like that of women, and teeth like those of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> They also had breastplates like breastplates of iron, and the sound of their wings was like the roar of many horses and chariots rushing into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> They had tails with stingers like scorpions, which had the power to injure people for five months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> They were ruled by a king, the angel of the Abyss. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Abaddon means Destruction.">a</a></sup>and in Greek it is Apollyon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Apollyon means Destroyer.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> The first woe has passed. Behold, two woes are still to follow.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sixth Trumpet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> Then the sixth angel sounded his trumpet, and I heard a voice from the four horns of the golden altar before God</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> saying to the sixth angel with the trumpet, “Release the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> So the four angels who had been prepared for this hour and day and month and year were released to kill a third of mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Psalms 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> And the number of mounted troops was two hundred million; I heard their number.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> Now the horses and riders in my vision looked like this: The riders had breastplates the colors of fire, sapphire, and sulfur. The heads of the horses were like the heads of lions, and out of their mouths proceeded fire, smoke, and sulfur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> A third of mankind was killed by the three plagues of fire, smoke, and sulfur that proceeded from their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> For the power of the horses was in their mouths and in their tails; indeed, their tails were like snakes, having heads with which to inflict harm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Deuteronomy 31:29; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Now the rest of mankind who were not killed by these plagues still did not repent of the works of their hands. They did not stop worshiping demons and idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood, which cannot see or hear or walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> Furthermore, they did not repent of their murder, sorcery, sexual immorality, and theft.</span>
</div>
</div>
