## The Decree of the Assembly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 11:11; 1 Samuel 3:20; 1 Samuel 10:17"> Then all the Israelites from Dan to Beersheba and from the land of Gilead came out, and the congregation assembled as one man before the LORD at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 20:17; Judges 20:15"> The leaders of all the people and all the tribes of Israel presented themselves in the assembly of God’s people: 400,000 men on foot, armed with swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 19:22–27; Luke 14:31–32; Luke 12:58–59"> (Meanwhile the Benjamites heard that the Israelites had gone up to Mizpah.) And the Israelites asked, “Tell us, how did this wicked thing happen?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 19:15–28"> So the Levite, the husband of the murdered woman, answered: “I and my concubine came to Gibeah in Benjamin to spend the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Judges 19:25–26; Genesis 19:4–8"> And during the night, the men of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded the house. They intended to kill me, but they abused my concubine, and she died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Joshua 7:15; Judges 19:23"> Then I took my concubine, cut her into pieces, and sent her throughout the land of Israel’s inheritance, because they had committed a lewd and outrageous act in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:30; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Corinthians 5:10–12"> Behold, all you Israelites, give your advice and verdict here and now.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Judges 20:1; Proverbs 21:3"> Then all the people stood as one man and said, “Not one of us will return to his tent or to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 1:7; Nehemiah 11:1; Joshua 14:2"> Now this is what we will do to Gibeah: We will go against it as the lot dictates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> We will take ten men out of every hundred from all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of every thousand, and a thousand out of every ten thousand, to supply provisions for the army when they go to Gibeah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="One Hebrew manuscript; most Hebrew manuscripts Geba, a variant of Gibeah">a</a></sup>in Benjamin to punish them for the outrage they have committed in Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So all the men of Israel gathered as one man, united against the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 20:10; Romans 12:18"> And the tribes of Israel sent men throughout the tribe of Benjamin, saying, “What is this wickedness that has occurred among you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; Judges 19:22"> Hand over the wicked men of Gibeah so we can put them to death and purge Israel of this evil.”<br />But the Benjamites refused to heed the voice of their fellow Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 20:20; Job 15:25–26; Numbers 21:23"> And from their cities they came together at Gibeah to go out and fight against the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:25; Numbers 1:37"> On that day the Benjamites mobilized 26,000 swordsmen from their cities, in addition to the 700 select men of Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:2; 1 Samuel 17:49–50"> Among all these soldiers there were 700 select left-handers, each of whom could sling a stone at a hair without missing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; 2 Chronicles 17:14–18; Numbers 26:51"> The Israelites, apart from Benjamin, mobilized 400,000 swordsmen, each one an experienced warrior.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Civil War against Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Judges 20:26–27; Numbers 27:21"> The Israelites set out, went up to Bethel, and inquired of God, “Who of us shall go up first to fight against the Benjamites?”<br />“Judah will be first,” the LORD replied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:16; Joshua 3:1; Joshua 6:12"> The next morning the Israelites set out and camped near Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the men of Israel went out to fight against Benjamin and took up their battle positions at Gibeah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Psalms 73:18–19; 2 Chronicles 28:10"> And the Benjamites came out of Gibeah and cut down 22,000 Israelites on the battlefield that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 64:5; Judges 20:17; 1 Samuel 30:6"> But the Israelite army took courage and again took their battle positions in the same place where they had arrayed themselves on the first day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:26–27; Psalms 78:34–36; Joshua 7:6"> They went up and wept before the LORD until evening, inquiring of Him, “Should I again draw near for battle against the sons of Benjamin my brother?”<br />And the LORD answered, “Go up against him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> On the second day the Israelites advanced against the Benjamites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 20:21; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:5"> That same day the Benjamites came out against them from Gibeah and cut down another 18,000 Israelites, all of them armed with swords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Joel 2:12–18; Judges 20:18"> Then the Israelites, all the people, went up to Bethel, where they sat weeping before the LORD. That day they fasted until evening and presented ascension offerings and well-being offerings to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> And the Israelites inquired of the LORD. (In those days the ark of the covenant of God was there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 7:9; Joshua 24:33; Deuteronomy 18:5"> and Phinehas son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron, served before it.) The Israelites asked, “Should I again go out to battle against the sons of Benjamin my brother, or should I stop?”<br />The LORD answered, “Go up, for tomorrow I will deliver him into your hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; 2 Samuel 5:23; Judges 20:34"> So Israel set up an ambush around Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> On the third day the Israelites went up against the Benjamites and arrayed themselves against Gibeah as they had done before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 19:13–14; Joshua 7:5; Isaiah 10:29"> The Benjamites came out against them and were drawn away from the city. They began to attack the people as before, killing about thirty men of Israel in the fields and on the roads, one of which led up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–16"> “We are defeating them as before,” said the Benjamites.<br />But the Israelites said, “Let us retreat and draw them away from the city onto the roads.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 8:18–22"> So all the men of Israel got up from their places and arrayed themselves at Baal-tamar, and the Israelites in ambush charged from their positions west of Gibeah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some LXX manuscripts and Vulgate; Hebrew charged from their positions in Maareh-geba or charged from their positions into the open space of Geba">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:14; Isaiah 47:11; Job 21:13"> Then 10,000 select men from all Israel made a frontal assault against Gibeah, and the battle was fierce. But the Benjamites did not realize that disaster was upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 20:5; Judges 20:15; Judges 20:44–46"> The LORD defeated Benjamin in the presence of Israel, and on that day the Israelites slaughtered 25,100 Benjamites, all armed with swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–29"> Then the Benjamites realized they had been defeated.<br />Now the men of Israel had retreated before Benjamin because they were relying on the ambush they had set against Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 8:19; Joshua 6:5; Exodus 19:13"> The men in ambush rushed suddenly against Gibeah; they advanced and put the whole city to the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Genesis 17:21; 2 Kings 4:16"> The men of Israel had arranged a signal with the men in ambush: When they sent up a great cloud of smoke from the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Judges 20:32"> the men of Israel would turn in the battle.<br />When the Benjamites had begun to strike them down, killing about thirty men of Israel, they said, “They are defeated before us as in the first battle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Revelation 19:3; Genesis 19:28"> But when the column of smoke began to go up from the city, the Benjamites looked behind them and saw the whole city going up in smoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Luke 21:26; Revelation 6:15–17"> Then the men of Israel turned back on them, and the men of Benjamin were terrified when they realized that disaster had come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 8:15; Hosea 9:9; Lamentations 1:3"> So they fled before the men of Israel toward the wilderness, but the battle overtook them, and the men coming out of the cities struck them down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 8:20–22"> They surrounded the Benjamites, pursued them, and easily overtook them in the vicinity of Gibeah on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And 18,000 Benjamites fell, all men of valor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Joshua 15:32; Judges 21:13; Zechariah 14:10"> Then the Benjamites turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and Israel cut down 5,000 men on the roads. And they overtook them at Gidom and struck down 2,000 more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 20:35; Judges 20:15"> That day 25,000 Benjamite swordsmen fell, all men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Psalms 103:9–10; Habakkuk 3:2"> But 600 men turned and fled into the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, where they stayed four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:15–17; 2 Chronicles 25:13; 2 Chronicles 28:6–9"> And the men of Israel turned back against the other Benjamites and put to the sword all the cities, including the animals and everything else they found. And they burned down all the cities in their path.</span>
</div>
