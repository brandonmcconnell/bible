## Jonah Flees from the LORD

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; 2 Kings 14:25"> Now the word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> “Get up! Go to the great city of Nineveh and preach against it, because its wickedness has come up before Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> Jonah, however, got up to flee to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD. He went down to Joppa and found a ship bound for Tarshish. So he paid the fare and went aboard to sail for Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Great Storm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> Then the LORD hurled a great wind upon the sea, and such a violent storm arose that the ship was in danger of breaking apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> The sailors were afraid, and each cried out to his own god. And they threw the ship’s cargo into the sea to lighten the load. But Jonah had gone down to the lowest part of the vessel, where he lay down and fell into a deep sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> The captain approached him and said, “How can you sleep? Get up and call upon your god. Perhaps this god will consider us, so that we may not perish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> “Come!” said the sailors to one another. “Let us cast lots to find out who is responsible for this calamity that is upon us.”<br />So they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> “Tell us now,” they demanded, “who is to blame for this calamity that is upon us? What is your occupation, and where have you come from? What is your country, and who are your people?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> “I am a Hebrew,” replied Jonah. “I worship the LORD, the God of the heavens, who made the sea and the dry land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> Then the men were even more afraid and said to him, “What have you done?” The men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jonah Cast into the Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> Now the sea was growing worse and worse, so they said to Jonah, “What must we do to you to calm this sea for us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> “Pick me up,” he answered, “and cast me into the sea, so it may quiet down for you. For I know that I am to blame for this violent storm that has come upon you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> Nevertheless, the men rowed hard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew the men dug in">a</a></sup>to get back to dry land, but they could not, for the sea was raging against them more and more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> So they cried out to the LORD: “Please, O LORD, do not let us perish on account of this man’s life! Do not charge us with innocent blood! For You, O LORD, have done as You pleased.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> Then they picked up Jonah and cast him into the sea, and the raging sea grew calm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> Then the men feared the LORD greatly, and they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows to Him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Luke 11:30; Matthew 16:4"> Now the LORD had appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah spent three days and three nights in the belly of the fish.</span>
</div>
</div>
