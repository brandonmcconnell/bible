## Gabriel Foretells John’s Birth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> In the time of Herod king of Judea there was a priest named Zechariah, who belonged to the priestly division of Abijah, and whose wife Elizabeth was a descendant of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> Both of them were righteous in the sight of God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and decrees of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> But they had no children, because Elizabeth was barren, and they were both well along in years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> One day while Zechariah’s division was on duty and he was serving as priest before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> he was chosen by lot, according to the custom of the priesthood, to enter the temple of the Lord and burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> And at the hour of the incense offering, the whole congregation was praying outside.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> Just then an angel of the Lord appeared to Zechariah, standing at the right side of the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> When Zechariah saw him, he was startled and gripped with fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, because your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you are to give him the name John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> He will be a joy and delight to you, and many will rejoice at his birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> for he will be great in the sight of the Lord. He shall never take wine or strong drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother’s womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> Many of the sons of Israel he will turn back to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> And he will go on before the Lord in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of the fathers to their children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Malachi 4:5–6">a</a></sup>and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous—to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> “How can I be sure of this?” Zechariah asked the angel. “I am an old man, and my wife is well along in years.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> “I am Gabriel,” replied the angel. “I stand in the presence of God, and I have been sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> And now you will be silent and unable to speak until the day this comes to pass, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their proper time.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah and wondering why he took so long in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> When he came out and was unable to speak to them, they realized he had seen a vision in the temple. He kept making signs to them but remained unable to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And when the days of his service were complete, he returned home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After these days, his wife Elizabeth became pregnant and for five months remained in seclusion. She declared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> “The Lord has done this for me. In these days He has shown me favor and taken away my disgrace among the people.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gabriel Foretells Jesus’ Birth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> to a virgin pledged in marriage to a man named Joseph, who was of the house of David. And the virgin’s name was Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> The angel appeared to her and said, “Greetings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or Rejoice">b</a></sup>you who are highly favored! The Lord is with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="BYZ and TR include Blessed are you among women!">c</a></sup>”</span>

</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

## The Birth of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> When the time came for Elizabeth to have her child, she gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had shown her great mercy, and they rejoiced with her.</span>

<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> On the eighth day, when they came to circumcise the child, they were going to name him after his father Zechariah.</span>
