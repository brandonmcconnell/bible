## The Leaven of the Pharisees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; James 3:17"> In the meantime, a crowd of many thousands had gathered, so that they were trampling one another. Jesus began to speak first to His disciples: “Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:22; Luke 8:17; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> There is nothing concealed that will not be disclosed, and nothing hidden that will not be made known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:27; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 10:20"> What you have spoken in the dark will be heard in the daylight, and what you have whispered in the inner rooms will be proclaimed from the housetops.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Jeremiah 1:8; Revelation 2:10"> I tell you, My friends, do not be afraid of those who kill the body and after that can do no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 10:28; Revelation 14:7"> But I will show you whom you should fear: Fear the One who, after you have been killed, has authority to throw you into Gehenna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gk. geenna; see note on Matt 5:22.">a</a></sup>Yes, I tell you, fear Him!</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> Are not five sparrows sold for two pennies?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek two assaria; an assarion was a Roman copper coin worth about 1/16 of a denarius.">b</a></sup>Yet not one of them is forgotten by God.</span>

## The Parable of the Rich Fool

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Timothy 6:5; Ezekiel 33:31"> Someone in the crowd said to Him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:3; Romans 2:1; Romans 9:20"> But Jesus replied, “Man, who appointed Me judge or executor between you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:6–10; Psalms 119:36–37"> And He said to them, “Watch out! Guard yourselves against every form of greed, for one’s life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:12–14; Hosea 2:8; Genesis 41:47–49"> Then He told them a parable: “The ground of a certain rich man produced an abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Isaiah 58:7; Romans 12:13"> So he thought to himself, ‘What shall I do, since I have nowhere to store my crops?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:15; James 4:15; Luke 12:21"> Then he said, ‘This is what I will do: I will tear down my barns and will build bigger ones, and there I will store up all my grain and my goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Then I will say to myself, “You have plenty of good things laid up for many years. Take it easy. Eat, drink, and be merry!”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> But God said to him, ‘You fool! This very night your life will be required of you. Then who will own what you have accumulated?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Luke 12:33; James 2:5"> This is how it will be for anyone who stores up treasure for himself but is not rich toward God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Worry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> Then Jesus said to His disciples, “Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will wear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> For life is more than food, and the body more than clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:7"> Consider the ravens: They do not sow or reap, they have no storehouse or barn; yet God feeds them. How much more valuable you are than the birds!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:27; Matthew 5:36; Luke 19:3"> Who of you by worrying can add a single hour to his life?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or a single cubit to his height; a cubit was approximately 18 inches or 45 centimeters.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 39:6; Ecclesiastes 7:13"> So if you cannot do such a small thing, why do you worry about the rest?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:28–30; Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Consider how the lilies grow: They do not labor or spin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="NE and Tischendorf Consider the lilies: They do not spin or weave.">d</a></sup>Yet I tell you, not even Solomon in all his glory was adorned like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Isaiah 40:6; Matthew 6:30"> If that is how God clothes the grass of the field, which is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the furnace, how much more will He clothe you, O you of little faith!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 6:31; Luke 12:22; Luke 22:35"> And do not be concerned about what you will eat or drink. Do not worry about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Matthew 6:8; Ephesians 4:17"> For the Gentiles of the world strive after all these things, and your Father knows that you need them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 34:9"> But seek His<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="BYZ and TR God’s; see Matthew 6:33.">e</a></sup>kingdom, and these things will be added unto you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Readiness at Any Hour

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Ephesians 6:14; Matthew 25:4–10"> Be dressed for service and keep your lamps burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jude 1:20–21; James 5:7–8; Revelation 3:20"> Then you will be like servants waiting for their master to return from the wedding banquet, so that when he comes and knocks, they can open the door for him at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; Matthew 24:42; John 13:4–5"> Blessed are those servants whom the master finds on watch when he returns. Truly I tell you, he will dress himself to serve and will have them recline at the table, and he himself will come and wait on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:4–5; Matthew 25:6"> Even if he comes in the second or third watch of the night<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, between nine at night and three in the morning">f</a></sup>and finds them alert, those servants will be blessed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> But understand this: If the homeowner had known at what hour the thief was coming,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="BYZ and TR include he would have stayed awake, and">g</a></sup>he would not have let his house be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13; Romans 13:11"> You also must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour you do not expect.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 13:37; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8"> “Lord,” said Peter, “are You addressing this parable to us, or to everyone else as well?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–46; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Hebrews 13:17"> And the Lord answered, “Who then is the faithful and wise manager, whom the master puts in charge of his servants to give them their portion at the proper time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Blessed is that servant whose master finds him doing so when he returns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> Truly I tell you, he will put him in charge of all his possessions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 24:48–50; Revelation 18:7–8; Philippians 3:18–19"> But suppose that servant says in his heart, ‘My master will be a long time in coming,’ and he begins to beat the menservants and maidservants, and to eat and drink and get drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 12:40; Matthew 24:51; Psalms 11:5"> The master of that servant will come on a day he does not expect and at an hour he does not anticipate. Then he will cut him to pieces and assign him a place with the unbelievers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 4:17; John 9:41; John 12:48"> That servant who knows his master’s will but does not get ready or follow his instructions will be beaten with many blows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 3:1; Leviticus 5:17; Luke 16:10–12"> But the one who unknowingly does things worthy of punishment will be beaten with few blows. From everyone who has been given much, much will be required; and from him who has been entrusted with much, even more will be demanded.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> Do you think that I have come to bring peace to the earth? No, I tell you, but division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Acts 14:1–4; John 15:18–21; Micah 7:5–6"> From now on, five in one household will be divided, three against two and two against three.</span>

## Interpreting the Present Time

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> Then Jesus said to the crowds, “As soon as you see a cloud rising in the west, you say, ‘A shower is coming,’ and that is what happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Job 37:17; Matthew 20:12"> And when the south wind blows, you say, ‘It will be hot,’ and it is.</span>
