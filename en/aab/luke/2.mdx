<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> Now in those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that a census should be taken of the whole empire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or of the whole land or of the whole world">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This was the first census to take place while<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or This was the census before">b</a></sup>Quirinius was governor of Syria.</span>

## The Shepherds and the Angels

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And there were shepherds residing in the fields nearby, keeping watch over their flocks by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> Just then an angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> But the angel said to them, “Do not be afraid! For behold, I bring you good news of great joy that will be for all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> Today in the city of David a Savior has been born to you. He is Christ the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this will be a sign to you: You will find a baby wrapped in swaddling cloths and lying in a manger.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> And suddenly there appeared with the angel a great multitude of the heavenly host, praising God and saying:</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Glory to God in the highest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and on earth peace to men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on whom His favor rests!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> When the angels had left them and gone into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, “Let us go to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all they had heard and seen, which was just as the angel had told them.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my eyes have seen Your salvation,</span></span>

## The Boy Jesus at the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> Every year His parents went to Jerusalem for the Feast of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when He was twelve years old, they went up according to the custom of the Feast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> When those days were over and they were returning home, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, but His parents were unaware He had stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> Assuming He was in their company, they traveled on for a day before they began to look for Him among their relatives and friends.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When they could not find Him, they returned to Jerusalem to search for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> Finally, after three days they found Him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> And all who heard Him were astounded at His understanding and His answers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> When His parents saw Him, they were astonished. “Child, why have You done this to us?” His mother asked. “Your father and I have been anxiously searching for You.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> “Why were you looking for Me?” He asked. “Did you not know that I had to be in My Father’s house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or I had to be about My Father’s business">h</a></sup>?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> But they did not understand the statement He was making to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> Then He went down to Nazareth with them and was obedient to them. But His mother treasured up all these things in her heart.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man.</span>
