## The Parable of the Persistent Widow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> Then Jesus told them a parable about their need to pray at all times and not lose heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> “In a certain town there was a judge who neither feared God nor respected men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> And there was a widow in that town who kept appealing to him, ‘Give me justice against my adversary.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> For a while he refused, but later he said to himself, ‘Though I neither fear God nor respect men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet because this widow keeps pestering me, I will give her justice. Otherwise, she will wear me out with her perpetual requests.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> And the Lord said, “Listen to the words of the unjust judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> Will not God bring about justice for His elect who cry out to Him day and night? Will He delay in helping them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I tell you, He will promptly carry out justice on their behalf. Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will He find faith on earth?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Pharisee and the Tax Collector

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> To some who trusted in their own righteousness and viewed others with contempt, He also told this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went up to the temple to pray. One was a Pharisee and the other a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood by himself and prayed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or stood and prayed to himself">a</a></sup>‘God, I thank You that I am not like other men—swindlers, evildoers, adulterers—or even like this tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice a week and pay tithes of all that I acquire.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> But the tax collector stood at a distance, unwilling even to lift up his eyes to heaven. Instead, he beat his breast and said, ‘God, have mercy on me, a sinner!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I tell you, this man, rather than the Pharisee, went home justified. For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but the one who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Blesses the Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> Now people were even bringing their babies to Jesus for Him to place His hands on them. And when the disciples saw this, they rebuked those who brought them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus called the children to Him and said, “Let the little children come to Me, and do not hinder them! For the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> Truly I tell you, anyone who does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Rich Young Ruler

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> Then a certain ruler asked Him, “Good Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> “Why do you call Me good?” Jesus replied. “No one is good except God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> You know the commandments: ‘Do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not bear false witness, honor your father and mother.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Exodus 20:12–16; Deuteronomy 5:16–20">b</a></sup>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> “All these I have kept from my youth,” he said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> On hearing this, Jesus told him, “You still lack one thing: Sell everything you own and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow Me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when the ruler heard this, he became very sad, because he was extremely wealthy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> Seeing the man’s sadness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally Seeing that he had become sorrowful; SBL, NE, and WH Seeing him">c</a></sup>Jesus said, “How hard it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> Indeed, it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> Those who heard this asked, “Who then can be saved?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> But Jesus said, “What is impossible with man is possible with God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> “Look,” said Peter, “we have left all we had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally left our own; BYZ and TR left all">d</a></sup>to follow You.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> “Truly I tell you,” Jesus replied, “no one who has left home or wife or brothers or parents or children for the sake of the kingdom of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> will fail to receive many times more in this age—and in the age to come, eternal life.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Third Prediction of the Passion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> Then Jesus took the Twelve aside and said to them, “Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and everything the prophets have written about the Son of Man will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> He will be delivered over to the Gentiles and will be mocked and insulted and spit upon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> They will flog Him and kill Him, and on the third day He will rise again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> But the disciples did not understand any of these things. The meaning was hidden from them, and they did not comprehend what He was saying.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals a Blind Beggar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> As Jesus drew near to Jericho, a blind man was sitting beside the road, begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> When he heard the crowd going by, he asked what was happening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> “Jesus of Nazareth is passing by,” they told him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> So he called out, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> Those who led the way admonished him to be silent, but he cried out all the louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> Jesus stopped and directed that the man be brought to Him. When he had come near, Jesus asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> “What do you want Me to do for you?”<br />“Lord,” he said, “let me see again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> “Receive your sight!” Jesus replied. “Your faith has healed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus, glorifying God. And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God.</span>
</div>
</div>
