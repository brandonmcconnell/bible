<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> Command the sons of Israel, and say to them: When you have entered into the land of Kenaan, that land which shall be divided to you for a possession, the land of Kenaan by its coasts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> Then your south border shall be from the wilderness of Zin on the frontier of Edom, and your south border shall be from the extremities of the Sea of Salt eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> And your border shall turn from the south to the going up of Akrabbim, and shall pass over to Zin; and the going out of it shall be from the south unto Rekem Giah, and go on to Hazar-Adar, and pass over to Azemon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> And the border shall turn from Azemon to the stream of Mizraim, and its goings out shall be toward the west (Heb. the sea).</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> And for the western border you shall have the Great Sea; this shall be your western border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> And this shall be your northern border: from the Great Sea you shall appoint for you Mount Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> From Mount Hor you shall appoint to the entrance of Hamath, and the goings forth of the border shall be unto Zedad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> And the border shall be unto Zaphron, and its goings forth to Ezarenan; this shall be your north border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And you shall appoint your eastern border from Ezarenan to Shepham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> And the border shall go down from Shepham unto Riblah, eastward of the fountain, and the border shall go down and come to the bank of the Sea of Genesar on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> And the border shall go down to the Jordan, and its going out be at the Sea of Salt. This shall be your land with its confines round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> And Mosheh commanded the sons of Israel, saying: This shall be the land which you are to possess by lot, which the Lord hath commanded to give to the nine tribes and to the half tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> For the tribe of the Beni Reuben by the house of their fathers, and the tribe of the Beni Gad by the house of their fathers, and the half tribe of Menasheh, have received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> The two tribes and the half tribe have received their inheritance across the Jordan by Jericho towards the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land to you: Elazar the priest, and Jehoshua bar Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> And you shall take of each tribe one prince to apportion the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> and these are the names of the men: of the tribe of Jehudah, Kaleb bar Jephuneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> of the tribe of the Beni Shemeon, Shemuel bar Ammihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad bar Kiselon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> of the tribe of the Beni Dan, Rabba Bokki bar Jagli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> of the Beni-Joseph, of the tribe of the Beni Menasheh, Rabba Haniel bar Ephod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> of the tribe of the Beni-Ephraim, Rabba Kemuel bar Shiphtan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> of the tribe of the Beni Zebulon, Rabba Elizaphan bar Parnak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> of the tribe of the Beni Issakar, Rabba Peltiel bar Ezar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And of that of the Beni Asher, Rabba Ahihud bar Shelomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and of that of the Beni Naphtali, Rabba Phadael bar Ammihud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are they whom the Lord commanded to divide the inheritance of the children of Israel in the land of Kenaan.</span>
