<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they removed from Rimmon-pharez, and encamped in Libnah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they removed from Mitheka, and encamped in Hashmona.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 21:4"> And they removed from Mount Hor, and encamped in Zalmona.</span>

<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Deuteronomy 34:1; Numbers 27:12"> And they removed from Elmon Diblathaimah, and pitched at the mountains of Abaraee, which are before Nebo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> And they removed from the mountains of Abaraee, and encamped in the plains of Moab by Jordan (over against) Jericho.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1"> Speak with the sons of Israel, and say to them: When you have passed over Jordan to the land of Kenaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> You shall cast out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, demolish all the houses of their worship, destroy all their molten images, and lay waste all their high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Psalms 115:16; Deuteronomy 32:8"> And you shall cast out the inhabitants of the land, and dwell in it; for I have given you the land to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Joshua 18:11–48; Joshua 15:1–12"> And you shall possess the land by lot, according to your families; to the many you shall make their possession large, and to the few diminish the possession: where the lot falleth to anyone, there shall he be; according to the tribes of your fathers you shall possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Judges 2:3; Psalms 106:34–36; Deuteronomy 7:16"> But if you will not drive out the inhabitants of the country from before you, it will be that those who remain of them, combining, will take arms against you, and surrounding you with camps they will distress you in the land wherein you dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 21:23–24; Deuteronomy 28:63; Leviticus 20:23"> And it shall be that what I had thought to do unto them, I will do unto you.</span>
