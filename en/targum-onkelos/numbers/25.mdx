<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> And Mosheh said to the judges of Israel: Slay every man his men who have joined themselves to Baala Pheor.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> And, behold, a man of the sons of Israel came and brought to his brethren a Midianitha, in the eyes of Mosheh, and in the eyes of all the congregation of the children of Israel, who were weeping at the door of the tabernacle of ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> And Phinehas bar Elazar bar Aharon the priest saw, and arose from the midst of the congregation, and took a lance in his hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalms 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> And he went in after the man, the son of Israel, into the tent, and thrust both of them through, the man, the son of Israel, and the woman, through her belly; and the pestilence was stayed from the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> But they who had died by the pestilence were twenty and four thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> Phinehas the son of Elazar, the son of Aharon, the priest, hath turned away My anger from the children of Israel, in that he was zealous with My zeal among them; and I have not consumed the children of Israel in My jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> Say therefore to him (that), behold, I decree unto him My covenant of peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> And he shall have it and his sons after him; a covenant of priesthood forever, because he hath been zealous before his God, and hath propitiated for the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> And the name of the man of Israel who was slain with the Midianitess was Zimri bar Salu, a chief of the house of his fathers of the house of Shemeon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> And the name of the woman, the Midianitess, was Kosbe, the daughter of Zur, who was chief of the people of his father’s house in Midian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> Punish the Midianites and slay them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> For they afflict you with their snares, with which they ensnare you; as by the occasion of Pheor, and by that of Kosbe, daughter of a prince of Midian, their sister, who was slain on the day of the pestilence in the matter of Pheor.</span>
</div>
