<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> And when the Kenaanah, king Arad, who dwelt in the south, heard that Israel was coming by the way of the explorers, he set battle in array against Israel, and took some of them captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> And Israel vowed a vow before the Lord, and said: If Thou wilt surely deliver this people into my hand, then I will destroy their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalms 10:17"> And the Lord accepted Israel’s prayer, and gave up the Kenaanaah; and he destroyed them and their cities, and he called the name of the place Charma.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> And they journeyed from Mount Hor by the way of the Sea of Suph, to go round from the land of Edom: and the soul of the people was wearied in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> And the people murmured (or, growled) against the Word of the Lord, and contended with Mosheh, (saying) Why have you brought us up out of Mizraim to die in the desert? For there is neither bread nor water, and our soul is weary of manna, this light food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And the Lord sent forth burning serpents among the people, and much people of Israel died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalms 78:34"> And the people came to Mosheh, and said: We have sinned, for we have murmured before the Lord, and have contended with thee. Intercede before the Lord, that He may remove the serpents from among us. And Mosheh prayed for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalms 106:43–45; Psalms 145:8"> And the Lord said to Mosheh: Make thee a burning (serpent), and uplift it on an ensign; and everyone who hath been bitten, and beholdeth it, shall live (or, be saved).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> And Mosheh made a serpent of brass, and set it upon an ensign; and when a serpent had bitten a man, if he looked up to the serpent of brass, he lived (or, was saved).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> And the children of Israel journeyed and encamped in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they journeyed from Oboth, and encamped at the Ford of the Passengers, which is in the wilderness over against Moab, toward the sunrise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> And they removed from thence, and encamped by the brook Zared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> Thence they journeyed, and pitched on the other side of Arnon, that is in the wilderness that stretcheth out from the coasts of the Amoraah; for Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amoraah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> Wherefore it is said in the Book of the Wars, That which the Lord did by the Sea of Suph, and the great deeds which (He wrought) by the torrents of Arnon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And at the flowing of the streams which lead towards Lechayath, and are joined at the confine of Moab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> And from thence was given to them the well, which is the well whereof the Lord spake to Mosheh, Gather the people together, and I will give them water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalms 106:12"> Therefore sang Israel this song: Spring up, O well; sing ye unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> The well which the princes digged, the chiefs of the people cut it, the scribes with their staves; it was given to them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And from (the time) that it was given to them it descended with them to the rivers, and from the rivers it went up with them to the height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> And from the height to the vale which is in the fields of Moab, at the head of Ramatha, which looketh towards Beth Jeshimon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> And Israel sent messengers to Sihon, king of the Amoraah, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> I would pass through thy country: I will not turn aside into field or vineyard; we will not drink of the waters of the cistern; by the king’s highway will we go until we have crossed thy border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> But Sihon would not permit Israel to pass through his coast; and Sihon gathered all his people, and came out to prevent Israel in the wilderness, and came to Jahaz, and arrayed battle against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> And Israel smote him with the edge of the sword, and took possession of his land from Arnon unto Jabbok, unto the Beni Ammon; for the border of the Beni Ammon was strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> And Israel subdued all those cities, and Israel dwelt in all the cities of the Amoraah, in Heshbon, and in all the villages thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For Heshbon was the city of Sihon, king of the Amoraah; he had made war with the former king of Moab, and had taken all his territory from his hand unto Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> Therefore, say the proverbs: Come to Heshbon; let the city of Sihon be builded and finished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> For an east wind strong as fire hath gone out from Heshbon, and the servants of war (as) with a flame from the city of Sihon: they have slain the people who inhabited Lechayath of Moab, the priests who ministered in the idol-temple in the height of Arnona.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> Woe to you, Moabaee, you are undone, ye people who have worshipped Kemosh; he hath delivered up his besieged sons and his daughters into captivity, unto Sihon, king of the Amoraah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> And the kingdom hath ceased from Heshbon, the dominion hath passed away from Dibon, they have laid waste unto Nophak which joineth Medeba.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> And Israel dwelt in the land of the Amoraah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> And Mosheh sent to explore Jaazer; and they subdued the towns, and drave out the Amoraah who were therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> And they turned and went up by the way of Mathnan; and Og, king of Mathnan, came out to meet them, he and all his people, to give battle at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> But the Lord said to Mosheh, Fear him not: for I have delivered him into thy hand with all his people and his country; and thou shalt do to him as thou hast done to Sihon, the king of the Amoraah who dwelt in Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> And they smote him, and his sons, and all his people, until none remained of him who were spared; and they took possession of his land.</span>
</div>
