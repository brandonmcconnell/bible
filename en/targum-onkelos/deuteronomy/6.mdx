<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> And thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thy hand, and they shall be for tephillin between thine eyes;</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> And houses full of goods that thou didst not fill, and cisterns hewn which thou hewedst not, vineyards and olive trees which thou didst not plant, and thou have eaten and be full;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Beware, lest thou forget the fear of the Lord who brought thee out from the land of Mizraim, from the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and serve before Him, and swear by His Name.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> (for the Lord thy God is a jealous God, His Shekinah dwelleth in the midst of thee,) lest the anger of the Lord thy God be kindled against thee, and He destroy thee from off the face of the earth.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> to break all thy enemies before thee, as He hath said.</span>
