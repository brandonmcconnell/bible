<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> Every mandate that I command thee this day, you shall observe to do, that you may live and multiply, and go in and possess the land which the Lord did covenant unto your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:3; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:16"> And thou shalt remember all the way that the Lord thy God hath led thee these forty years in the wilderness, that He might humble thee, to prove thee, to know what was in thy heart, whether thou wouldst keep His commandments or not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:4; Luke 4:4; Hebrews 13:5–6"> And He humbled (or afflicted) thee, and let thee hunger, and fed thee with the manna which thou knewest not, nor did thy fathers know; that He might make thee to know, That not by bread only is man sustained, but by every forth-coming word from before the Lord shall man live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 29:5; Matthew 26:25–30"> Thy raiment faded not away from thee, and thy shoes wore not out these forty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And know thou with thy heart, that as a man instructeth his son, (so) the Lord thy God instructeth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:31; Deuteronomy 5:33; 1 Samuel 12:24"> Keep then the commandments of the Lord thy God, to walk in the ways which are right before Him, to fear Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:9–12; Exodus 3:8; Ezekiel 20:6"> For the Lord thy God bringeth thee into the good land; a land streaming with brooks of water, with fountain springs, and depths (of water) gushing from valleys and hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 6:13; Psalms 147:14; Deuteronomy 32:14"> A land of wheat and barley, vines, figs, and pomegranates; a land of olives yielding oil, and which produceth honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; Deuteronomy 33:25; Joshua 22:8"> A land where, without poverty, thou mayest eat bread, and have want of nothing; a land whose stones are iron, and out of whose hills thou mayest cast brass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 103:2; Romans 14:6; Proverbs 3:9"> And thou shalt eat and be satisfied, and shalt bless the Lord thy God in the good land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Hosea 2:8–9; Proverbs 1:32"> Beware, lest thou forget the fear of the Lord thy God, in not keeping His commandments, His judgments, and His statutes, which I command thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:15"> Lest, when thou hast eaten and art full, and bast built goodly houses and inhabited (them),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:3; Genesis 13:1–5; Luke 12:13–21"> And when thy oxen and sheep have multiplied to thee, and silver and gold have increased, and whatever thou hast is increased with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; 1 Corinthians 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:11"> thy heart should be lifted up, and thou shouldest forget the fear of the Lord thy God, who brought thee up from the land of Mizraim, from the house of bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:6; Numbers 20:11; Jeremiah 2:6"> Who led thee through the great and terrible desert, the place of burning serpents and scorpions, the place of thirsting, where there is no water, who brought out water for thee from the hard rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; James 1:12; Exodus 16:15"> Who fed thee in the wilderness with manna which thy fathers knew not, that he might humble thee, and prove the, to do thee good in thy latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:4; 1 Corinthians 4:7; Daniel 4:30"> And (lest) thou say in thy heart, My power and the might of my hand have gathered to me these possessions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Hosea 2:8; Deuteronomy 7:8"> But remember the Lord thy God, for He it is who giveth thee power to acquire riches, that He may confirm his covenant which He sware to thy fathers, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26; 1 Samuel 12:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> But it will be that if thou forget the fear of the Lord thy God, and walk after the idols of the Gentiles to serve and worship them, I testify against you this day that perishing you will perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:16–17"> As the nations which the Lord destroyeth from before you, so shall you perish, because you would not obey the Word of the Lord your God.</span>
