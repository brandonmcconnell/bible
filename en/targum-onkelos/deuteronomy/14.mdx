<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> Children are you before the Lord your God. You shall not lacerate yourselves, nor make baldness between your eyes for the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For thou art (to be) a holy people before the Lord thy God, chosen to be unto Him a people more beloved than all the nations upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> Thou shalt not eat anything that is abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the cattle that you may eat: oxen, lambs of the flock, and kids of the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The hart, and the antelope, the forest deer, and the wild goat, the pygarg, the buffalo, and the chamois;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> And any animal that hath the hoof cloven, and that divideth the hoof into two parts, and ruminateth the cud, among cattle, that you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Nevertheless, you shall not eat of those that (only) chew the cud, or of them that (only) separate the hoof into divided parts: the camel, the hare, and the coney, for they bring up the cud, but divide not the hoof, they are unclean to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> And the swine, because it divideth the hoof, but doth not ruminate, shall be unclean to you; of their flesh you may not eat, nor touch their carcases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> Of all that are in the waters these you may eat: all that have fins and scales you may eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But any (fish) that hath not fins and scales you may not eat; it is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You may eat any clean bird;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> But of these you shall not eat: the eagle, ossifrage, osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Gleed, vulture, and kite after his kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and every raven after his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> The owl, nightbird, gull, and hawk after his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The heron, swan, and stork,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the cormorant, pelican, and owl;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The bittern and ibis after his kind; the lapwing and the bat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> and no reptile may you eat whose flesh is unclean to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> but you may eat any fowl (or winged thing) that is clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> You shall not eat of anything that dieth of itself: thou mayest give it to the uncircumcised stranger who is in thy city, and he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it to the outward people; for thou art to be a holy people to the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not eat flesh with the milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Thou shalt tithe all the produce of thy seed, and all that thy field may bring forth from year to year;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And before the Lord thy God in the place which He will choose to make His Shekinah dwell there, thou shalt eat the tithe of thy corn, wine, and oil, and the firstlings of thy herd and flock, that thou mayest learn to fear before the Lord thy God all days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> But if the way be too great for thee, so that thou art not able to bring it, if the way be too distant from the place which the Lord thy God may choose to make His Shekinah to dwell there, when the Lord thy God shall have blessed thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then thou shalt put (change) it into silver, and bind the silver in thy hand, and go to the place which the Lord thy God will choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> and shalt give the silver for whatever thy soul may please, for oxen, sheep, wine, new or old, or anything thy soul may desire, and shalt eat there before the Lord thy God, and rejoice, thou, the men of thy house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> and the Levite who is in thy cities; thou shalt not forsake him, for he hath no portion or inheritance with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of three years thou shalt bring forth all the tithes of thy produce in that year, and lay it up in thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> And the Levite, because he hath no part or inheritance with thee, and the stranger, the orphan, and the widow who are in thy cities, shall come and eat and be satisfied, that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thy hand which thou shalt do.</span>
