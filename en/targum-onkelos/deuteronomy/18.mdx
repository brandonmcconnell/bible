<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> For the priests and Levites, (even) all the tribe of Levi, there shall be no part or inheritance with Israel; they shall eat the oblations of the Lord and his inheritance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> but have no heritage among their brethren, the gifts presented unto the Lord they are his heritage, as the Lord hath said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> And this is what shall appertain to the priests from the people, from them who offer a sacrifice, whether bullock or lamb; they shall give to the priest the shoulder, the cheeks, and the maw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; 2 Chronicles 31:4–10"> The first of thy corn, wine, and oil, and the first of the fleece of thy sheep, thou shalt set apart for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> For the Lord thy God hath chosen him out of all thy tribes to stand to minister in the Name of the Lord, he, and his sons, all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> And if a Levite come from one of thy cities out of all Israel, where he hath dwelt, and come with all the desire of his soul, to the place which the Lord will choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2–4"> then he shall minister in the Name of the Lord his God with his brethren the Levites who minister there before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 Chronicles 31:4"> Portion for portion shall they eat, beside the accustomed allotment which cometh on the Sabbath, as the fathers have appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> When thou hast entered into the land which the Lord thy God will give thee, thou shalt not learn to do after the abominations of these peoples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> No one shall be found among thee who maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire, using enchantments, observing times or augury or witchcraft,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> Nor an incantator, or a consulter at a heathen oracle, or a wizard, or an inquirer from the dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> For all who do these things are an abomination to the Lord; and because of these abominations the Lord thy God driveth them out from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> Thou shalt be perfect in the fear of the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> For these nations which thou shalt possess hearkened to diviners and enchanters; not so hath the Lord given thee to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> A PROPHET from among thee, of thy brethren, like me, will the Lord thy God raise up unto thee, to him shall ye hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> According to all that thou didst ask before the Lord thy God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying: Let me not again hear the voice of the Word of the Lord my God, and let me not see the great fire any more, lest I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> And the Lord said to me, That which they have spoken is right:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> I will raise up to them a Prophet from among them like unto thee, and I will put My words of prophecy upon his lips, and he will speak to them all that I shall command him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> And the man who will not hearken to My words which he will speak in My Name, My Word will require it of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> But a prophet who shall do wickedly in speaking in My Name a word which I had not commanded him to speak, or who shall speak in the name of the gods of the Gentiles, that prophet shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> And if thou say in thy heart, How shall we know the word which the Lord hath not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> When a prophet speaketh in the Name of the Lord, if the word come not to pass, neither be confirmed, that is a word which the Lord hath not spoken: of the prophet who hath spoken in wickedness thou shalt not be afraid.</span>
