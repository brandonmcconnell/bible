<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10; Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:2–4"> At the end of seven years thou shalt make the Release,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> and this is the sentence (word) of the Release: That every man who is a creditor shall give release to him who oweth to his neighbour; he shall not exact it from his neighbour or his brother, because the release is proclaimed before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> From a son of the Gentiles thou mayest demand; but that which is with thy brother, thou shalt release by thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> Save when there shall not be the poor among thee, for the Lord in blessing will bless thee in the land which the Lord thy God will give thee for a possession to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Isaiah 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> Only if thou wilt indeed be obedient to the Word of the Lord thy God, and observe to do all these precepts which I command thee this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> For the Lord thy God doth bless thee, as He hath said to thee: and thou shalt lend to many peoples, but thou shalt not take a loan; and thou shalt have rule over many peoples, but they shall not have rule over thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> If there be with thee a poor man of thy brethren, in one of the cities in the land which the Lord thy God will give thee, thou shalt not harden thy heart, nor shut up thy hand from thy poor brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> But thou shalt open thy hand to him, and lend, according to the measure of his want, of that which he may need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremiah 17:10"> Beware, lest there be a word with thy wicked heart, saying, The year of release draweth near, and thine eye be evil toward thy poor brother, and thou give not to him, and he cry against thee before the Lord, and there be guiltiness in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:10"> Giving thou shalt give to him, and thy heart shall not be evil when thou givest to him: because for this thing the Lord thy God will bless thee in all thy works, and in all thou put test thy hand unto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> For the poor will not cease (to be) in the midst of thy land; therefore I command thee, saying, Thou shalt verily open thy hand to thy brother, to thy afflicted, and to thy poor in thy land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> If thy brother, a son of Israel, or a daughter of Israel, be sold to thee, and shall have served thee six years, in the seventh year let him go, a son of liberty, from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when thou let test him go free from thee, thou shalt not send him away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalms 68:10"> But thou shalt separate to him from thy flock, and thy floor, and thy wine-press, and give to him of that which the Lord thy God hath blessed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a servant in the land of Mizraim, and the Lord thy God set thee free: therefore I command thee this thing today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 40:6"> But if he say to thee, I will not go out from being with thee, because he loveth thee, and the men of thy house, (and) because it is good for me to be with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> then thou shalt take an awl, put it through his ear, and into the door, and he shall be a ministering servant to thee evermore. And also likewise shalt thou do to thy handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16"> It shall not be a hardship in thy eyes when thou dost send him from thee to be a son of freedom, for he hath been doubly worth a hireling, serving thee six years; and the Lord thy God will bless thee in all that thou shalt do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> Every firstling male which cometh of thy cattle or thy sheep, thou shalt consecrate before the Lord thy God: thou shalt not work with the firstling of thy herd, nor shear the firstling of thy flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Thou shalt eat it before the Lord thy God from year to year in the place which the Lord will choose, thou and the men of thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> And if there be any blemish (spot) in it, (if it be) lame, or blind, or have any evil spot, thou shalt not sacrifice it before the Lord thy God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> Thou mayest eat it in thy cities, unclean (persons) and clean alike (may eat it), as the antelope and the hart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> Only thou shalt not eat the blood, but pour it out upon the ground like water.</span>
</div>
