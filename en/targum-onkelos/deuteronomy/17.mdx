<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 23:18"> Thou shalt not sacrifice before the Lord thy God a bullock or ram which hath blemish in it, not anything that is evil; for that is abomination before the Lord thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 2:20; Hosea 8:1; Joshua 7:11"> If, in anyone of the cities which the Lord thy God will give thee, there be found a man or woman who hath done evil before the Lord thy God in transgressing his covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Deuteronomy 4:19; Job 31:26–27"> And going to do service to the idols of the Gentiles, or to the sun, moon, or all the host of the heavens, in worshipping them, which I have not commanded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:51; Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Deuteronomy 19:18"> And it be told thee, and thou hast heard; thou shalt inquire fairly, and if such word be true that this abomination hath been wrought in Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:14; Joshua 7:25; Leviticus 24:16"> thou shalt bring forth that man or woman, and stone them with stones that they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Hebrews 10:28; 1 Timothy 5:19"> On the word of two witnesses or, of three shall he die who is guilty of death: he shall not die on the word of one witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:9; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Deuteronomy 17:12"> The hands of the witnesses shall be upon him first to kill him, and the hands of all the people afterward; and thou shalt put down the doer of evil from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Haggai 2:11; Deuteronomy 1:17"> If a matter for judgment be extraordinary to thee, between blood and blood, between cause and cause, or between plague and plague, of leprosy, they being matters of divided judgment in thy cities, then thou shalt arise and go up to the place which the Lord thy God shall choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:24; Haggai 2:11; Malachi 2:7"> and come to the priests, the Levites, and to the judge who may be in those days, and inquire; and they will show thee the sentence of decision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–3"> And thou shalt do according to the word of the sentence which they will show thee from the place which the Lord will choose, and thou shalt observe to do according to all that they will teach thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Deuteronomy 5:32; Romans 13:1–6"> Upon the word of the law which they teach thee, and upon the judgment they tell thee, thou shalt act; thou shalt not swerve from the word they will have shown thee, to the right or the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:30; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 18:5"> And the man who doeth wickedly in not receiving from the priest who standeth there to minister before the Lord thy God, or from the judge, that man shall die; and thou shalt put down the evil doer from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:11; Deuteronomy 19:20; Numbers 15:30–31"> And all the people will hear and be afraid, and do wickedly no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:19–20; 1 Samuel 12:19; Joshua 1:13"> When thou art come into the land which the Lord thy God will give thee, and dost possess and dwell in it, and thou mayest say, I will appoint a king over me, like the nations who are about me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:21; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 1 Chronicles 12:23"> Thou mayest verily appoint over thee a king whom the Lord thy God will choose, from among thy brethren thou shalt appoint the king over thee. Thou shalt not have power to set over thee a foreign man, who is not thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Ezekiel 17:15; Deuteronomy 28:68"> Only he shall not multiply to him horses, nor cause the people to return to Mizraim for the purpose of multiplying horses; for the Lord hath said to you, Ye shall no more return by that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Matthew 19:5; Proverbs 30:8–9"> Neither shall he multiply wives to him, that his heart be not turned away; nor shall he increase silver and gold for himself greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; 2 Kings 22:8; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> And it shall be, when he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom, he shall write for himself a copy of this Law in a book, out (of that which is) before the priests, the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Psalms 119:97–100; Psalms 1:2"> And he shall have it with him, to read in it all the days of his life, that he may learn to fear before the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law, and these statutes, to perform them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalms 131:1–2"> That his heart may not be lifted up from his brethren, nor swerve from the precepts of the Lord to the right or to the left, and may prolong (his days) in his kingdom, he and his sons in the midst of Israel.</span>
