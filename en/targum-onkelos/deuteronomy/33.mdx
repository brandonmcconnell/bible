<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; Psalms 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> And this is the benediction wherewith Mosheh, the prophet of the Lord, blessed the children of Israel before his death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Habakkuk 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> And he said: The Lord was revealed from Sinai, and the brightness of His glory appeared to us from Seir. He was revealed in His power upon the mountain of Pharan, and with Him were ten thousand saints; He gave us, written with His own right hand, the law from the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> He loved the tribes, all the holy ones of the house of Israel; with power He led them out of Mizraim, and they were conducted under Thy Cloud, they journeyed by Thy Word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; Psalms 119:111; John 7:19"> Mosheh gave to us the law and delivered it an inheritance to the congregation of Jakob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> And he was the king in Israel, when the heads of the people were gathered with the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> Let Reuben live in life eternal, and not die the second death; and let his children receive their inheritance according to their numbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalms 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> And of Jehudah he said this: Hear, O Lord, the prayer of Jehudah when he goeth forth to battle, and let him return to his people in peace; let his hands do vengeance upon his enemies, and be Thou to him a help against his foes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> And of Levi he said: With perfections and lights, clothe Thou the man who is found holy before Thee whom Thou didst try in the temptation, and he was upright, and whom Thou didst prove at the Waters of Contention, and he was faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> Who, when they had sinned, had no mercy in the judgment upon his father or his mother, and no respect to his brother or his children; for they have kept the charge of Thy word, and Thy covenant have they not altered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> These are worthy to teach Thy judgments unto Jakob, and Thy law unto Israel. They shall set the sweet incense before Thee, and the full free-will offerings upon Thy altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Bless, Lord, his substance, and the oblation of his hands receive Thou with favour; break the loins of his enemies and of his adversaries, that they may not arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> Of Benjamin he said: The beloved of the Lord shall dwell in safety by Him: the shield will be over him all the days, and the Shekinah will dwell in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> And of Joseph he said: Blessed be his land from before the Lord; let it make fruit by the dew of the heavens from above, and from the fountain springs, and the depths which flow from the abysses of the earth beneath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> Let it make fruit and produce which the sun causeth to grow; let it make fruit from the beginning of month after month;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> And firstfruits from the mountain tops, and goodly things from the unfailing hills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> With the good of the earth and its fulness, and the favour of Him Those glory is in the heavens, and who was revealed unto Mosheh at the Bush: let all these come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the man who was separated from his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> The greatness of his children will be his beauty, and the mighty works wrought for him from before the Omnipotent and the Most High, by whose strength he will slay nations together unto the ends of the earth; and these are the myriads of the house of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of the house of Menasheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> And unto Zebulon he said: Rejoice, Zebulon, when thou goest out to war against thy adversaries, and thou, Issakar, when thou goest to compute the time of the festivals in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> They shall assemble the tribes of Israel at the mountain of the sanctuary, to offer there the holy sacrifices with free-will; for they will eat the victims of the Gentiles, and the treasures hidden in the sand shall be disclosed for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> And of Gad he said: Blessed is He who enlargeth Gad. He dwelleth as a lioness; he will kill sultans with kings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> And he shall receive his own at the beginning: for there, in his inheritance, Mosheh the scribe-prince of Israel is (to be) buried; he who came out and went in at the head of the people, who hath wrought righteousness before the Lord and His judgments with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> And of Dan he said: Dan is strong as a lion’s whelp; his land is watered by the streams that flow from Mathnan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> And of Naphtali he said: Naphtali, satisfied with favour and full of blessings from the Lord, shall inherit from the western sea, Genesar, unto the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And of Asher he said: Blessed shall be Asher with the blessings of children: let him be acceptable to his brethren, and be nourished with the dainties of kings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> Strong shalt thou be as iron and brass, and as the days of thy youth shall be thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> There is no God like the God of Israel, whose Shekinah in the skies is thy help, and whose power is in the heaven of heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> The habitation of Eloha is from eternity, and the world was made by His Word; and He will drive out thy enemies from before thee, and will say, Destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> And Israel shall dwell securely by themselves, according to the benediction with which Jakob their father blessed them in the land producing corn and wine; the heavens also above them shall drop down with dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> Happy art thou, Israel; none is like unto thee, a people whose salvation is from the Lord, who is the strength of thy help, and from whom is the might of thy victory: thy enemies shall be liars unto thee, and thou shalt tread upon the necks of their kings.</span>
