<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Acts 5:4; Leviticus 5:15"> Instruct Aharon and his sons, saying: This is the law of the Burnt Offering. It is burnt offering, because burned upon the altar all night until morning, and the fire of the altar shall be burning in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:4; Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 22:9–11"> And the priest shall dress himself with the vestments of linen, and wear drawers of linen upon his flesh; and he shall separate the ashes which the fire hath consumed with the burnt offering upon the altar, and set them beside the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Amos 3:10; Leviticus 5:3–4"> And he shall take off his vestments, and dress himself with other garments, and carry forth the ashes without the camp unto a clean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 5:16; Luke 19:8; Numbers 5:7–8"> But the fire upon the altar shall be burning on it, and never be extinguished; and the priest shall burn wood on it from morning to morning, and lay the burnt offering in order upon it, and burn upon it the fat of the sanctified oblations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15; Leviticus 5:18; Isaiah 53:10–11"> The fire shall be ever burning on the altar, it shall not be extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; 1 John 1:9; 1 John 2:1–2"> And this is the law of the Mincha which the sons of Aharon shall offer in the presence of the Lord before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he shall separate therefrom his handful lof the flour of the mincha and of its oil, and all the frankincense that is upon the mincha, and burn it at the altar as its memorial to be accepted with favour before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Numbers 28:3; Exodus 29:38–42"> And the remainder of it may Aharon and his sons eat, unleavened shall it be eaten in the holy place, in the court of the tabernacle of ordinance shall they eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Leviticus 16:4; Leviticus 1:13"> It shall not be baked with leaven. I have given it as their portion of my oblations; it is most sacred, as the sin offering and as the trespass offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Ezekiel 44:19; Hebrews 13:11–13"> All the males of the children of Aharon may eat it. (This) is an everlasting statute for your generations concerning the oblations of the Lord: everyone who toucheth them shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Exodus 29:38–42; Nehemiah 13:31"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> This is the oblation of Aharon and his sons which they shall present before the Lord on the day when they anoint him. The tenth of three seahs of flour for a mincha perpetually, a half in the morning, and a half at eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 15:4; Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:32"> It shall be made in a pan with oil; while soft it shall be brought a baken mincha offered in pieces to be accepted with favour before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9"> And of his sons, the priest who shall be anointed in his stead shall perform it. (This is) an everlasting statute before the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 10:12–13"> It shall be burned entirely, and every mincha of the priest shall be entirely (burned); it is not to be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 2:11; Numbers 18:9–10"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:10; Haggai 2:12–14"> Speak with Aharon and with his sons, saying, This is the law of the sin offering: In the place where the burnt offering is killed, there shall the sin offering be killed before the Lord; it is most sacred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The priest who maketh atonement with its blood shall eat it; in the holy place it shall be eaten, in the court of the tabernacle of ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; Exodus 16:36; Leviticus 2:1–16"> Everyone who toucheth the flesh thereof must be holy. And if he drop some of its blood upon a vestment, that which is bedropped shall be purified in the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 2:5; Leviticus 7:9; 1 Chronicles 9:31"> But the earthen vessel in which it was sodden shall be broken; and if it be sodden in a vessel of brass, (that) shall be scoured and washed in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:22–25; Isaiah 53:10; Leviticus 8:21"> Any man of the priests may eat thereof: it is most sacred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 2:10; Leviticus 6:16–17"> But no sin offering whose blood is brought into the tabernacle of ordinance to make atonement in the sanctuary may be eaten, but shall be burned with fire.</span>
