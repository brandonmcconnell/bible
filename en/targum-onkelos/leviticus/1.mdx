<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 19:3; Exodus 25:22; Exodus 29:42"> And the Lord called unto Mosheh, and the Lord spake with him from the tabernacle of ordinance, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–19; Romans 12:6; 1 Chronicles 16:29"> Speak with the sons of Israel, and say to them: When one of you will bring an offering before the Lord of the cattle, of oxen or of sheep, you shall offer your oblations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Hebrews 9:14; Leviticus 22:19–24"> If his oblation be a burnt offering of the oxen, (it shall be) a male, unblemished; he shall offer him at the door of the tabernacle of ordinance; he shall offer him for acceptance before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Exodus 29:15; Leviticus 4:20"> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the burnt offering, that it may be acceptable for him to propitiate on his behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 3:8; Leviticus 3:2"> And he shall sacrifice the young bullock before the Lord; and the sons of Aharon the priest shall bring the blood, and sprinkle the blood round about upon the altar which is at the door of the tabernacle of ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:8; Genesis 3:21"> And he shall take away (the skin) of the burnt offering, and divide it, by its members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Genesis 22:9; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> And the sons of Aharon the priest shall put fire upon the altar, and lay the wood in order upon the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 9:13–14; Leviticus 1:12; 1 Kings 18:33"> And the priests, the sons of Aharon, shall arrange the limbs and the head and the fat on the wood upon the fire which is on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Leviticus 1:13; Ephesians 5:2"> But his inwards and his legs he shall wash with water; and the priest shall burn the whole upon the altar an entire burnt offering, an oblation to be received with acceptance before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; Leviticus 1:2–3; Genesis 8:20"> And if his oblation be from the flock, of the sheep or the young of the goats for a burnt offering, he shall bring a perfect male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Exodus 40:22; Leviticus 7:2"> And he shall sacrifice him at the north side of the altar before the Lord; and the priests the sons of Aharon shall sprinkle his blood round about upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he shall divide him by his members, and his head and his feet; and the priest shall lay them upon the wood which is on the fire upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:9"> But the inwards and the legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall bring the whole and burn (it) upon the altar: it is a whole burnt offering, an oblation to be received with acceptance before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Luke 2:24; Leviticus 12:8"> But if the burnt offering of his oblation before the Lord be from fowl, he shall bring his oblation from the turtles or the young of a pigeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:8–9; Psalms 22:21; 1 John 2:27"> And the priest shall offer it upon the altar, and pour its blood by the side of the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Luke 1:35; Leviticus 16:27"> And he shall remove its crop with its food, and throw it on the east side of the altar, at the place where they empty the ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; John 19:30; Leviticus 1:9–10"> And he shall cleave it through its wings, (but) divide (it) not; and the priest shall burn it at the altar upon the wood that is on the fire: it is a burnt offering, an oblation that shall be received with acceptance before the Lord.</span>
</div>
