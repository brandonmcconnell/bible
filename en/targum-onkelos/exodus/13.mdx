<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Numbers 3:13; Deuteronomy 15:19"> Sanctify unto Me all the firstborn. Everyone which openeth the womb among the children of Israel, of man and of beast, that is Mine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 20:2"> And Mosheh said to the people, Remember this day, in which you went forth from Mizraim from the house of servitude; for with a mighty hand hath the Lord brought you forth from thence; and you shall not eat what is leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 12:2"> This day have you come out, in the month of Abiba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 12:25–26"> And it shall be when the Lord hath led thee into the land of the Kenaanaee and Hittaee and Emoraee and Hivaee and Jebusaee, which He covenanted to thy fathers to give thee, a land producing milk and honey, that thou shalt perform this service in this month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; Exodus 34:18; Leviticus 23:8"> Seven days thou shalt eat the (phatira) unleavened cake, and in the seventh day solemnize a feast before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:19; Matthew 16:6"> The unleavened cake thou shalt eat seven days; that which is leavened shall not be seen with thee; the leavened thing shall not be seen with thee in all thy limits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26–27; Psalms 44:1"> And thou shalt teach thy son on that day, saying, (It is) on account of that which the Lord did for me in bringing me out of Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:8; Deuteronomy 11:18–19; Numbers 15:39"> And it shall be a sign to thee on thy hand, and for a memorial between thine eyes, that the Law of the Lord may be in thy mouth, for that with a mighty hand did the Lord bring thee forth from Mizraim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Exodus 12:24–25; Exodus 23:15"> And thou shalt keep this ordinance in its season from time to time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 13:5"> And it shall be, when the Lord hath brought thee into the land of the Kenaanaee, as He sware to thee and to thy fathers, and hath given it to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 18:15; Leviticus 27:26; Exodus 34:19"> that thou shalt make over whatsoever openeth the womb before the Lord; among the cattle which thou hast the male shall be consecrate before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Numbers 18:15–17; Revelation 14:4"> And every firstling of an ass thou shalt ransom with a lamb; but if thou wilt not ransom it, thou shalt destroy it; and every firstborn of man among thy children thou shalt ransom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 13:3; Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4"> And it shall be, when thy son shall ask thee tomorrow, saying, Why is this? Thou shalt say to him, By a mighty hand the Lord brought us out of Mizraim from the house of servitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:29"> And it was when Pharoh was obdurate about letting us go away that the Lord slew all the firstborn in the land of Mizraim, from the firstborn of man to the firstborn of cattle: therefore I sacrifice before the Lord of all that openeth the womb, the males, and all the firstborn of my children I ransom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 13:9; Deuteronomy 11:18; Deuteronomy 6:7–9"> And it shall be for a sign upon thy hand and for Tephillin between thine eyes, because with a mighty hand hath the Lord brought thee out of Mizraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; Numbers 14:1–4"> And it was when Pharoh had sent the people away, that the Lord led them not by the way of the land of the Philistaee because it was the nearest: for the Lord said, Lest the people be terrified at the seeing of war, and return to Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:10; Numbers 33:6–8"> But the Lord led the people round by the way of the desert to the sea of Suph; and harnessed (or girded) went the sons of Israel up out of the land of Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32; Acts 7:16"> And Mosheh brought up the bones of Joseph with him; for he had adjured the sons of Israel with an oath, saying, Remembering, the Lord will remember you, and you shall carry up my bones from hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 33:5–6; Exodus 12:37"> And they journeyed from Sukkoth, and encamped in Etham, which is beside the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; Deuteronomy 1:33"> And the Lord went before them by day in the column of the cloud to lead them in the way, and by night in the column of fire to enlighten them, that they might go in the day and in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 121:5–8; Revelation 10:1"> The column of the cloud by day, nor the column of the fire by night, departed not before the people.</span>
</div>
