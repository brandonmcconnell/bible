<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> Speak with the sons of Israel that they set apart before Me a separated portion: from every man who is willing in his heart thou shalt receive that which is set apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And this is the separation that thou shalt take of them: gold, and silver and brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> And hyacinth, and purple and vermilion, (bright colour,) and fine linen, (butz,) and hair of goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> And skins of rams made red, and skins of purple, and sittin woods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> Oil for the illuminators, aromatics for the anointing oil, and aromatics for the incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> Burilla stones, and stones that may complete the insetting of the ephod and the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And they shall make before me A SANCTUARY in which I will dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> Altogether as I show thee the pattern of the sanctuary, and the pattern of all its vessels, even so shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And they shall make an ARK of sitta wood; two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> And thou shalt cover it with pure gold within and without, and thou shalt make a crown of gold upon it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And thou shalt cast for it four rings of gold, and set them upon its four corners, two rings upon one side of it, and two rings upon its other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> And thou shalt make staves of sittin woods, and cover them with gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and shalt insert the staves in the rings upon the sides of the ark, that the ark may be carried upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> In the rings of the ark shall be the staves, they shall not be removed therefrom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And thou shalt place in the ark the Testimony that I will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And thou shalt make A PROPITIATORY Of pure gold; two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its breadth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> And thou shalt make two KERUBIN of gold, beaten (ductile) shalt thou make them, on the two sides of the propitiatory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And thou shalt make one keruba on this side and one keruba on that side of the propitiatory; thou shalt make the kerubin on its two sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> And the kerubin shall have their wings outspreading above, overshadowing the propitiatory with their wings; and their faces shall be opposite one to another, towards the propitiatory shall be the faces of the kerubin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> And thou shalt set the propitiatory upon the Ark over above, and within the ark shalt thou put the Testimony that I will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> And I will appoint My Word (Memra) with thee there; and I will speak with thee from above the Propitiatory, from between the two kerubin that are upon the Ark of the Testimony, all that I may command thee for the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> And thou shalt Make A TABLE of sittin woods, two cubits the length of it, and a cubit its breadth, and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, and make for it a crown of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And thou shalt make to it a border, a handbreadth high round about, and make a crown of gold to the border of it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou shalt make for it four golden rings, and put the rings upon the four corners of its four feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> Opposite to the border shall be the rings for the place of the staves for carrying the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> And thou shalt make the staves of sittin woods, and cover them with gold, that they may carry the table upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> And thou shalt make its dishes and its vases, its vials and its chalices with which the libations are outpoured, of pure gold shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And thou shalt set upon the table the PRESENCE BREAD continually before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> And thou shalt make A CANDELABRUM; of pure ductile gold shalt thou make the candelabrum; its base and its shaft, its cups, apples, and lilies, of the same shall they be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And six branches shall come out from its sides, three branches of the candelabrum from one side, and three branches of the candelabrum on the second side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> Three cups (calyxes) figured on one branch with apple and lily, and three cups figured on one branch with apple and lily, so for the six branches that come out from the candelabrum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And upon the candelabrum shall be four cups, figurated with apples and lilies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> An apple under the two branches of this, and an apple under the two branches of that, according to the six branches that come forth from the candelabrum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> Their apples and their branches shall be of it, all beaten of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> And thou shalt make its seven lights, and kindle its lights that they may shine towards its face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> And its snuffers and shovels (shall be made) of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Of a talent of pure gold shall he make it and all these vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> And look thou, and make, according to their resemblance which was displayed in the mount.</span>
