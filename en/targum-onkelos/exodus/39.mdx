<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> And of the hyacinth, and purple, and crimson, they made the vestments of ministration to minister in the sanctuary, and they made the holy vestments of Aharon, as the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> And he made the ephoda of gold, hyacinth, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> And they beat out the plates of gold, and cut (them) into threads, to insert into the hyacinth, and the purple, and the crimson, and the fine linen, the work of the artificer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Shoulder-pieces made they for it, conjoined; at the two sides were they conjoined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> And the band of its fastening which is upon it was of the same according to its work, of gold, hyacinth, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen twined, as the Lord had commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> And they wrought the onyx stones, inset in sockets of gold, engraven in distinct writing, with the names of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> And he set them upon the shoulders of the ephoda, stones of memorial of the sons of Israel, as the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> And he made the BREASTPLATE (chushena), the work of the artificer, according to the work of the ephoda, of gold, hyacinth, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Foursquare was it, doubled made they the breastplate; a span its length, and a span its breadth, doubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And they filled it with four rows of precious stones: the first row carnelian, topaz, and carbuncle, row one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> The second row, smaragud, sapphire, and emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the third row, jacinth, agate, and amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the fourth row, chrysolite, beryl, and jasper: they were set in sockets of gold, in their infillings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> And the stones were according to the names of the sons of Israel, twelve, according to their names, written distinctly, as the engraving of a ring, each according to his name for the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> And they made upon the breastplate chains enwreathed, a work of braiding, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> And they made two sockets of gold, and two golden rings, and put the two rings upon the two sides of the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And they put the two golden wreaths upon the two rings upon the sides of the breastplate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> and the two wreaths which were upon the two sides they put upon the two sockets, and set them upon the shoulders of the ephoda towards its face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they made two rings of gold, and set them on the two sides of the breastplate, upon its edge which was on the side of the ephoda inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> And they made two golden rings, and set them on the two shoulders of the ephoda underneath towards its front, toward the place of its conjoinment, above the band of the ephoda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> And they adunited the breastplate by its rings to the rings of the ephoda, by a ribbon of hyacinth, to be upon the band of the ephoda, that the breastplate might not be loosened from the ephoda, as the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> And he made the ROBE of the ephoda, the work of the embroiderer, altogether of hyacinth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the opening (mouth) of the robe in the middle of it like the opening of a corslet, with a binding going about its border, that it might not be torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> And they made upon the hem of the robe pomegranates of hyacinth and purple and crimson inwoven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> And they made bells of pure gold, and set the bells among the pomegranates upon the hem of the robe round about among the pomegranates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> A bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, upon the hem of the robe round about, to minister, as the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And they made the tunics of fine linen, the work of the weaver, for Aharon and for his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> And the tiara of fine linen, and the mitres of beauty of fine linen; and the drawers of linen, of fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> And the girdle of fine linen twined, and hyacinth and purple and crimson, the work of the embroiderer, as the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> And they made the plate of the diadem of holiness of pure gold, and wrote upon it as the engraving of a ring in distinct writing, HOLINESS UNTO THE LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And they put upon it a ribbon of hyacinth, that it might be upon the tiara above, as the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> Arid completed was all the work of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of ordinance; and the sons of Israel did according to all that the Lord had commanded Mosheh, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> And they brought the tabernacle to Mosheh, the tabernacle and all its vessels, its taches, its boards, its bars, and its pillars, and its bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the covering of rams’ skins reddened, and the covering of purple skins, and the veil for the hanging;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> And the ark of the testimony and its staves, and the mercy seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> The table and all its vessels, and the bread of the Presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> The pure candelabrum and its lamps, the lamps of order, and all its vessels; and the oil for the light</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> And the golden altar, and the oil of consecration, and the sweet incense; and the hanging for the door of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> And the brasen altar and the brasen grate for it; its staves and all its vessels; and the laver and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> the hangings of the court, its pillars, and its sockets, and the veil for the gate of the court, its cords, and its pins, and all the vessels of the service of the tabernacle, for the tabernacle of ordinance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> The vestments of ministration to minister in the sanctuary, and the holy vestments of Aharon the priest, and the vestments of his sons to minister:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> According to all that the Lord commanded Mosheh, so made the sons of Israel all the service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> And Mosheh surveyed all the work, and, behold, they had done it as the Lord had commanded, so had they done; and Mosheh blessed them.</span>
