<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:5; Proverbs 19:5; Deuteronomy 19:16–21"> Thou shalt not take up a false report, nor set thine hand with the wicked to be a false witness for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–11; Proverbs 1:15; Luke 23:23–24"> Thou shalt not follow the many to wickedness, neither shalt thou fail to teach that which in thine eyes is judgment; after the many (majority?) thou shalt fulfil judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; Leviticus 19:15; Psalms 82:2–3"> And upon the poor thou shalt not be pitiful in judging him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:1–4; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:44"> If thou meet the ox of thy enemy, or his ass, wandering away, thou shalt surely bring it back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> When thou seest thine enemy’s ass prostrate beneath his burden, thou shalt forbear from forsaking him; thou shalt surely abandon what is in thy heart against him, and shalt deliver it unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> Thou shalt not warp the judgment of the poor man in his cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Exodus 34:7; Exodus 23:1"> From a false matter keep distant; and him who has been acquitted and has come forth from judgment uncondemned thou shalt not kill; for I will not justify the guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> And thou art not to receive a bribe; for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise, and perverts the words of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> And thou shalt not distress the sojourner; for you know the mind of a sojourner; for you were dwellers in the land of Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> Six years thou shalt sow the land and gather in its produce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> but the seventh year thou shalt let it alone and suffer it to rest, that the poor of thy people may eat, and what they leave the beast of the field may eat. So also shalt thou do with thy vineyard and with thy olive ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> Six days shalt thou do thy work, and in the seventh day have rest, that thy ox and thy ass may rest, and the son of thine handmaid and thy sojourner may be quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> And of all that I have spoken to you be mindful, and the name of the idols of the Gentiles remember not; let it not be heard upon thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Three times thou shalt solemnize festival before Me in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> Thou shalt keep the festival of unleavened bread. Seven days shalt thou eat unleavened cake, as I have commanded thee, in the time of the moon of Abiba, for therein you went forth from Mizraim; and you shall not appear before Me empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> And the festival of harvest, the firstfruits of thy labours which thou hast sowed in thy fields; and the festival of gathering, at the end of the year when thou gatherest in thy labours from the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Three times in the year shall all thy males appear before the Lord, the Ruler of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> Thou shalt not offer with unleavened bread the blood of My pascha, neither shall the fat of the sacrifice of the feast remain without on the altar until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> The beginning of the firstfruits of thy land thou shalt bring into the sanctuary of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not eat flesh with milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Psalms 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> Behold, I send My Angel before thee, to protect thee in the way, and to bring thee into the place which I have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:56; Psalms 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Take heed before Him, and hearken to His words; rebel not against Him, for He will not forgive your sins; for in My Name are His words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> For if thou wilt truly hearken to His words, and do all that I shall speak, I will be a foe to thy enemies, and will afflict them that afflict thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> For My Angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in, to the Amoraee and Hittaee, and Perizaee and Kenaanaee, Hivaee and Jebusaee; and I will destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> Thou shalt not worship their idols, nor serve them, nor do according to their doings, but shalt utterly demolish them, and break their images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> And you shall serve before the Lord your God, and He will bless thy food and thy drink, and will take away grievous evils from among thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> There shall be none abortive or barren in thy land; the number of thy days will I complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> I will send My terror before thee, and will perturb all the people among whom thou shalt come to fight against them, and I will make all thy adversaries turn their back before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> I will send the hornet before thee, and it shall drive out the Hivaee and Kenaanaee and the Hittaee from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not expel them before thee in one year, lest the land be made desolate, and the beasts of the field multiply against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> By little and little I will drive them out before thee, until thou shalt be increased and inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> And I will appoint thy boundary from the sea of Suph unto the sea of the Philistaee, and from the desert to the river; for I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hands, and thou shalt drive them out before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> Thou shalt strike no covenant with them nor with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they cause thee to commit sin before Me; for if thou serve their idols, it will be a stumbling-block to thee.</span>
