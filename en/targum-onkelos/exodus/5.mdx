<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> And afterward Mosheh and Aharon went in, and said to Pharoh: Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, Let My people go, to solemnize a feast (yechagun) before Me in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> And Pharoh said, The name of the Lord is not know to me, that I should hearken to His word to send Israel away. The name of the Lord is not revealed to me, and Israel I shall not release.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they said, The God of the Jehudaee hath revealed Himself to us; let us now go three days’ journey into the desert to sacrifice before the Lord our God, lest He come upon us with death or with slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> And the king of Mizraim said to them, Why, Mosheh and Aharon, do you hinder the people from their works? Go to your employment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> And Pharoh said, Behold now, the people of the land are many, and you make them relax from their employment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Pharoh that day commanded the masters of the people and the overseers, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> You shall not continue (add) to give straw to the people to cast bricks, as heretofore; let them go and collect straw for themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> Yet the number of bricks which they have made heretofore you shall still lay upon them and not diminish; for they are idle, and therefore cry, saying, We will go and sacrifice before our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Make labour heavy upon the men: let them be occupied with it, and not with vain words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the masters of the people and the overseers went forth and spake to the people, saying, Thus saith Pharoh, I will not give you straw;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> You shall go and gather up straw for yourselves wheresoever you can find it; though from you work there shall be mother diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> And the people were scattered abroad over all the country of Mizraim to gather stubble for straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the masters were urgent, saying, Fulfil your work, the matter of a day in a day, as you did when straw was given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And the masters whom Pharoh set over the sons of Israel smote them, saying, Why do you not fulfil your requirement to cast bricks as heretofore, as yesterday, so also today?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the overseers of the sons of Israel came and complained before Pharoh, saying, Why hast thou done thus with thy servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Thou hast not given thy servants straw, yet they say to us, Make bricks; and, behold, thy servants are beaten, and thy people sin against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> But he said, You are idle: therefore you say, We will go and sacrifice before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> And now go, work: but straw I will not give you; yet the number of bricks you shall render.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And the overseers of the sons of Israel perceived that they were in evil: for they said to them, You shall not diminish form your bricks the matter of a day, in a day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they met Mosheh and Aharon standing before them in their coming out from being with Pharoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> And they said to them, May the Lord manifest Himself to you and adjudge; because you have made our savour evil in the eyes of Pharoh, and in the eyes of his servants, and have put a sword into their hands to kill us!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Mosheh returned before the Lord and said, O Lord, why hast Thou done evil unto this people, and wherefore didst Thou send me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> And from the time that I went in to Pharoh to speak in Thy name he hath done evil to this people, but liberating Thou hast not liberated Thy people.</span>
</div>
