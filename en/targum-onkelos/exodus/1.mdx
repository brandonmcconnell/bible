<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; Genesis 50:24; Acts 7:14–16"> And Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Genesis 1:28; Deuteronomy 26:5"> But the sons of Israel increased and propagated, and became great and very mighty, and the land was filled with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 7:18; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Ecclesiastes 9:15"> But a new king arose over Mizraim who did not hold valid (or confirm) the decree of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 105:24–25; Ecclesiastes 4:4; James 3:14–16"> And he said to his people, Behold, the people of the sons of Israel are more numerous and stronger than we:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Psalms 83:3–4; Proverbs 1:11"> Come, let us deal wisely by them, lest they multiply, and it be that should war happen to us they join themselves with our enemies, and break forth in the war against us, and go up from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Genesis 47:11; Exodus 3:7"> And they appointed over them evildoing governors (shiltonin) to afflict them in their labours. And they builded cities of treasure houses for Pharoh, Pithom, and Raamsas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 105:24; Exodus 1:9; Romans 8:28"> But by as much as they afflicted them, so they increased and waxed strong, and the Mizraee had vexation on account of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:20"> And the Mizraee made the sons of Israel serve with rigour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Numbers 20:15; Exodus 2:23"> and embittered their lives with hard labour, in clay and in brick, and in all labour of the field, all the work which they wrought, they made them do with hardship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the king of Mizraim spake to the midwives of Jewesses, (Yehuditha,) of whom the name of the one was Shiphra, and the name of the second Puvah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Matthew 21:38; Revelation 12:4"> And he said, When you do the office of the midwife among the Jewish women, and you look upon the childbirth, if it be a son, you shall kill him; but if a daughter, let her live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:21; Matthew 10:28"> But the midwives feared before the Lord, and did not act as the king of Mizraim had bidden them, but preserved the sons alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:4; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And the king of Mizraim called</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 2:4–24; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 1 Samuel 21:2"> The midwives said to Pharoh, It is because the Jewesses are unlike the Mizraite women; they are cunning, and give birth before the midwives come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12; Proverbs 11:18"> And the Lord did good to the midwives; and the people multiplied and became strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; 1 Samuel 2:35; Psalms 37:3"> And because the midwives feared before the Lord He made for them houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Proverbs 1:16; Exodus 1:16"> But Pharoh commanded all his people, saying, Every son who is born to the Jews you shall throw into the river, and every daughter you shall keep alive.</span>
