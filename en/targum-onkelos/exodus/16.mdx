<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> And they journeyed from Elim, and came, the whole assembly of the sons of Israel, to the desert of Sir, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month from their outgoing from the land of Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> And all the congregation of the children of Israel were troublous against Mosheh and against Aharon in the desert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> And the children of Israel said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v0" data-v="0"> that we had died before the Lord in the land of Mizraim, when we sat by the caldrons of flesh, and could eat bread and be satisfied! Why have you brought us out into this wilderness to kill this whole assembly with famine?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And the Lord said to Mosheh, Behold, I will cause bread to come down to you from heaven; and the people shall go out and collect the matter of the day for the day; that I may prove them whether they will walk in My law, or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> And in the sixth day, when they prepare that which they bring in, it shall be two for one upon what they collect from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> And Mosheh and Aharon said to all the children of Israel, In the evening you shall know that the Lord brought you out of the land of Mizraim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> And in the morning shall you see the Glory of the Lord; for your tumults are heard before the Lord: and we what, that you are restive against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Mosheh said, When the Lord will give you at evening flesh to eat, and bread in the morning to satisfy, while are heard before the Lord your tumults against Him! For what are we? Your tumults are not against us, but against the Word of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> And Mosheh said to Aharon, Bid all the congregation of the sons of Israel to come together before the Lord; for your tumult is heard before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> And it was, while Aharon was speaking with all the congregation of the sons of Israel, that they turned towards the desert, and, behold, the glory of the Lord was revealed in the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spake with Mosheh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> The tumult of the children of Israel is heard before Me. Speak with them to say, Between the evenings you shall eat flesh, and in the morning be satisfied with bread, and you shall know that I am the Lord your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> And it was in the evening that the quails came up and covered the camp; and in the morning a dew descended round about the camp;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> And when the dew which had fallen had gone up, behold, upon the face of the desert, a small (substance) without covering, small like hoar frost, heaped on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> And the sons of Israel saw, and said, a man to his brother, Mana-hu! For they knew not what it was. And Mosheh said to them, This is the bread which the Lord will give you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the word that the Lord hath commanded: Let every man gather of it according to his eating an omera for every head according to the number of your souls, a man for those of his tent shall you take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the sons of Israel did so, and gathered, some more, (others) less;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And they measured with an omera, and he who had (gathered) much had not more, and he who had (gathered) little had not less; every man according to his eating they gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> And Mosheh said to them, No man must leave of it for the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> But they hearkened not to Mosheh, but some left for the morning, and it swarmed worms and corrupted. And Mosheh was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> And they gathered it from morning to morning a man according to his eating; and when the sun grew hot on that which remained upon the face of the field, it melted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> And it was that on the sixth day they gathered a double (quantity) of bread, two omers for one: and all the chiefs of the congregation came and showed Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> And be said to them, Tomorrow is the rest of the holy Shabbath before the Lord. That which you prepare by baking, bake, and that which you prepare by boiling, boil, and all the remainder lay up to you, a store for the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> And they laid it up till the morning, as Mosheh had instructed; and it did not corrupt, neither were there worms in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> And Mosheh said, Eat that today, for this day is Shabbath before the Lord; this day you would not find it in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> Six days you shall collect it; but on the seventh day, the Shabbath, there will be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> And it was on the seventh day that some of the people went out to gather, but they found it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> And the Lord said to Mosheh, How long will you be unwilling to keep My commandments and My laws?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> See, because the Lord hath given you the Shabbath He hath therefore given you on the sixth day the bread for two days; let every man dwell in his resting, and not go out from his place on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> And the people reposed on the seventh day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> And the house of Israel called the name of it Manna; and it was as the seed of Gida white, and its taste was like cake broiled with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> And Mosheh said, This is the word which the Lord hath commanded. Fill an omera of it to be kept for your generations, that they may see the bread which I made you eat in the desert when I brought you forth from the land of Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> And Mosheh said to Aharon, Take one vase, and put therein an omera full of manna, and lay it up before the Lord to be preserved for your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> As the Lord commanded, so did Mosheh; and Aharon laid it up before the Testimony, to keep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> And the children of Israel ate the manna forty years, until they came to the land inhabited; they did eat the manna till they came to the confines of the land of Kenaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> And one omera is the tenth of three seahs.</span>
</div>
