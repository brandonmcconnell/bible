<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalms 78:70–72"> And Mosheh tended the flock of Jethro his father-in-law, the rabba of Midian, and he led the flock to the place of the best pastures of the wilderness, and came to the mountain on which was revealed the glory of the Lord, unto Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Isaiah 43:2; Mark 12:26"> And the Angel of the Lord appeared to him in a flame of fire in the midst of a bush. And he gazed, and, behold, the bush burned with fire, but the bush was not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalms 107:8; Job 37:14"> And Mosheh said, I will now turn and see this great sight, why the bush is not burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; 1 Samuel 3:6; Acts 9:4"> And the Lord saw that he turned to see, and the Lord called to him from the midst of the bush, and said, Mosheh, Mosheh! And he said, Behold me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> And He said, Approach not hither; loose the sandal from thy foot, for the place where thou standest is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> And He said, I am the God of thy fathers; the God of Abraham, the God of Izhak, and the God of Jakob. And Mosheh bowed with his face; for he was afraid to look up to the glory of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> And the Lord said, The bondage of My people who is in Mizraim is verily disclosed before Me, and before Me is heard their cry on account of their toils; for their afflictions are disclosed before me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> And I have appeared to deliver them from the hand of the Mizraee, and to bring them up from that land, unto a land good and large, a land producing milk and honey, unto the place of the Kenaanaee, an the Hittaee, and the Amoraee, and the Perizaee, and the Hivaee, and the Yevusaee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> And now, behold, the cry of the sons of Israel ascendeth before Me, and the affliction is also revealed before Me wherewith the Mizraee afflict them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:4; 1 Samuel 12:6; Psalms 105:26"> And now, come, I will send thee to Pharoh, and will bring forth the sons of Israel form Mizraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> And Mosheh said before the Lord, Who am I, that I should go unto Pharoh to bring forth the sons of Israel from Mizraim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 41:10"> And He said, Because My Word shall be thy helper: and this shall be the sign that I have sent thee: In thy leading forth of the people from Mizraim you shall do service before the Lord upon this mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Isaiah 9:6; Jeremiah 23:6"> And Mosheh said before the Lord, Behold, when I am come to the sons of Israel, and say to them, The God of your fathers hath sent me to you, and they say to me, What is His name? What shall I say to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> And the Lord said unto Mosheh, EHEYEH ASHER EHEYEH. And he said, Thus shalt thou speak to the sons of Israel, EHEYEH hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachi 3:6"> The Lord said moreover to Mosheh, Thus shalt thou speak to the sons of Israel, The Lord, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Izhak, and the God of Jakob, hath sent me unto you. This is My Name forever, and this is My Memorial in every generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> Go and assemble the elders of Israel and say to them, The Lord, the God of your fathers, hath revealed Himself to me, the God of Abraham, Izhak, and Jakob, saying, Remembering I have remembered you, and that which hath been done to you in Mizraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> And I have said that I would bring you up from the bondage of Mizraim to the land of the Kenaanaee, and Hittaee, and Emoraee, and the Pherizaee, and Hivaee, and Jebusaee, to a land producing milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> And they will be obedient to thee, and thou shalt go, thou and the elders of Israel, to the king of Mizraim, and say to him, The Lord, the God of the Jehudaee, hath called us; and now let us go, as (for) a journey of three days into the desert, that we may sacrifice before the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> But it is manifest before Me that the king of Mizraim will not release you, that you may go, not even on account of Him whose power is mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> But I will send forth the stroke of My power, and will smite the Mizraee with all My miracles which I will perform among them, and afterward they will send you away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalms 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> And I will give this people to become favourites in the eyes of the Mizraee, and it shall be that when you go you shall not go empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> But you shall demand, a woman of her neighbour and the inmates of her house, articles of silver and of gold and vestments, and put them upon you sons and upon your daughters, and shall make the Mizraee empty.</span>
