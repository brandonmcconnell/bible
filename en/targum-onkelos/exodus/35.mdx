<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> You may not kindle a fire in all your dwellings on the day of the Sabbath.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Mosheh spake to all the congregation of the sons of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Take from you a separation (aphrashutha) before the Lord of everyone whose heart may be willing; let him bring the separation before the Lord: gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> And hyacinth, and crimson, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goat’s hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and ram’s skin dyed red, and purple skins, and sittin woods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> And oil for the illuminator, and aromatics for the anointing oil, and for the sweet perfumes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> And onyx stones and complete stones for insetting in the ephod and in the breastplate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> And all the wise-hearted among you shall come and make all that the Lord hath commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> The tabernacle, its tent and its coverings, its hasps, its boards, its bars, its pillars, and its bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> The ark, and its staves, the mercy seat, and the veil that shall be spread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> The table, and its staves, and all its vessels; and the bread of the Presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> and the candelabrum for light, and its vessels, and its lamps, and the oil for illumination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> And the altar of sweet incense, and its staves, and the oil of anointing, and the incense of perfumes, and the curtain of the door of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> The altar of burnt offering, and its brasen grate, its staves, and all its vessels; the laver and its base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> The curtains of the court, and its pillars, and its bases, and the hanging of the gate of the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> The nails of the tabernacle, and the nails of the court and their cordings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> The vestments of ministration for ministering in the sanctuary, the holy vestments of Aharon the priest, and the vestments of his sons for ministration.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the congregation of the sons of Israel went forth from before Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> And they came, every man who was led by his heart, and everyone whose spirit was ample, and brought their separation before the Lord, for the work of the tabernacle of ordinance, and for all its service, and for the holy vestments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> And they came, the men with the women, everyone who was willing of heart, and brought chains, and bracelets, and rings, and bands, all of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> And every man who uplifted an offering of gold before the Lord, and every man with whom was found hyacinth, or purple, or crimson, or fine linen, or goats’ skins, or rams’ skins dyed red, or purpled skins, brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> Everyone who would offer silver or brass, brought the separation before the Lord; and everyone with whom was found woods of sittin for any work of the service, brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And every woman wise in heart spun with her hands, and brought what was spun, the hyacinth, the purple, the crimson, and the fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And all the women with whom was willingness of heart with wisdom spun goat’s hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the princes brought onyx stones and complete stones for the insetting of the ephod and the breastplate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> And the perfume, and oil for the light and for the oil of anointing, and incense of perfumes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> Every man and woman whose heart led them to bring for all the work which the Lord had commanded to make by Mosheh, did the children of Israel bring willingly before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Mosheh said to the sons of Israel, See, the Lord hath ordained by mane Bezalel bar Uri bar Hur, of the tribe of Jehudah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And hath filled him with the spirit of prophecy from before the Lord, with wisdom, with intelligence, and with knowledge, for all handicraft,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And to teach the arts of working in gold, and in silver, and in brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the skilful work of precious stones for enchasing, and the workmanship of wood to work in all the work of the artificer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> And be hath ingiven in his heart to teach also Ahaliab bar Achisamak, of the tribe of Dan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> And hath filled them with wisdom of heart to make all the work of the carpenter, and artificer, and embroiderer, in hyacinth, and in purple, and in scarlet, and in fine linen, and the weaver, of (such as) do any work, and who teach the arts.</span>
</div>
