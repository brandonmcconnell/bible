<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> And he made its horns upon its four corners, of the same were its horns, and he coated it with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> And he made all the vessels of the altar, the caldrons, and the cleaners, and the basins, and the fleshhooks, and the receivers; all its vessels made he of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he made for the altar a brasen grate of network, under its border beneath, (reaching) unto its middle.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of brass; the hooks, pillars, and their uniting rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And for the northern side (hangings) of a hundred cubits, their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their uniting rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And for the western side, curtains, fifty cubits; their pillars ten, and their sockets ten; the hooks of the pillars and their uniting rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And on the eastern side, eastward, fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> The hangings fifteen cubits on (one) side (of the gate), their pillars three, and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And on the second side of the gate of the court, here and there, hangings, fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the hangings of the court round about were of fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the bases of the pillars were of brass, the hooks, pillars, and their uniting rods of silver, and the overlaying of their heads was of silver, and there were uniting rods of silver for all the pillars of the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> And the veil for the gate of the court was the work of the embroiderer, hyacinth, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen; and twenty cubits was its length, and the height, in the breadth five cubits, according (to the height of) the curtains of the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And their pillars four, and their bases four, of brass, and their hooks silver, and the overlaying of their head and their uniting rods, silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> And all the pins of the tabernacle, and of the court round about, were of brass.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> And Bezalel bar Uri bar Hur of the tribe of Jehudah made all that the Lord commanded Mosheh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> And with him Ahaliab bar Achisamak, of the tribe of Dan, a woodworker, and artificer, and embroiderer in hyacinth, and in purple, and in crimson, and in fine linen.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> And the silver of the numbered ones of the congregation was a hundred talents, and a thousand and seven hundred and seventy and five shekels, in the shekels of the sanctuary.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> And the hundred talents of silver were for casting the bases of the sanctuary, and the bases of the veil (or tent), a hundred bases with a hundred talents, a talent for a base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> And with the thousand and seven hundred and seventy and five (shekels) he made the hooks for the pillars, and covered their capitals, and made their uniting rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the brass of the oblation was seventy talents, and two thousand and four hundred shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> And with it he made the bases of the door of the Tabernacle of Ordinance, and the altar of brass, and the brasen grate which pertained to it, and all the vessels of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the bases of the court round about, and the bases of the door of the court, and all the pins of the tabernacle, and all the pins of the court round about.</span>
