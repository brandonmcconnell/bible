<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> And thou shalt make THE ALTAR of sittin woods; five cubits the length and five cubits the breadth; square shall be the altar, and three cubits its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And thou shalt make its horns upon its four corners; of the same shall be its horns, and overlay it with brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And make its pots, to collect the ashes, and its fireshovels, and its basins, and its flesh-hooks, and its thuribles, all its vessels make thou of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And make for it a grate, a work of netting of brass, and make upon the network four rings of brass upon its four sides,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> And place it under the surrounding of the altar beneath, that the net may extend to the middle of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And make thou staves for the altar, staves of sittin wood, and overlay them with brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And he shall put the staves into the rings, and the staves shall be at the two sides of the altar to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Hollow (with) boards make thou it, according to what was showed thee in the mount, so do thou.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> And thou shalt make THE COURT of the tabernacle on the side toward the south: curtains (shall there be) for the court of fine twined linen a hundred cubits in length on one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> And its pillars twenty and their bases twenty of brass, the hooks of the pillars and their rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And so for the north side in length (there shall be) curtains of a hundred cubits long, and their columns twenty and their bases twenty of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their rods shall be of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And on the side of the court toward the west curtains of fifty cubits, their columns ten and their bases ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the breadth of the court toward the east side eastward, fifty cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> And fifteen cubits the curtains on a side, their columns three and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And on the second side fifteen curtains, their pillars three, and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> And for the door of the court shall be an hanging of twenty cubits, of hyacinth, and purple, and vermilion, and fine linen twined, the work of the embroiderer; their pillars four and their bases four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars round about the court shall be (united) with rods of silver, their hooks shall be of silver and their bases of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the court, one hundred cubits, and the breadth fifty, and the height five cubits, of fine linen twined, and their bases of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the vessels of the tabernacle, and all its service, and all its utensils, and all the pins of the court shall be of brass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And thou shalt instruct the sons of Israel to bring to thee the pure oil of olives, beaten, to illuminate, that the lamps may burn continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the tabernacle of ordinance, without the veil that is before the testimony, Aharon and his sons shall set it in order from evening to morning before the Lord; a perpetual statute for the generations of the sons of Israel.</span>
</div>
