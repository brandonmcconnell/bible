<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> And Shekem bar Hamor the Hivite, the prince of the land, saw her, and took her, and lay with her, and humbled her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> And his soul took pleasure in Dinah the daughter of Jakob, and he loved the damsel, and spake to the heart of the damsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> And Shekem spake to Hamor his father, saying, Take to me this damsel for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> And Jakob heard that he had polluted Dinah his daughter. And his sons were with his flocks in the field; and Jakob was silent until they were come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Hamor the father of Shekem came forth to Jakob to speak with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> And the sons of Jakob came up from the field when they heard. And the men were indignant and very angry, because shame had been wrought in Israel by his lying with the daughter of Jacob; for so it should not have been done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> Hamor spake with them, saying, The soul of Shekem my son hath pleasure in your daughter; give her now to him to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> And marry with us, and take our daughters to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> And the land shall be before you; inhabit and do business in it, and possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> And Shekem said to her father and to her brethren, Let me find favour in your eyes, and what you shall tell me I will give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> Multiply upon me dowry and present, and I will give as you may tell me; but give me the damsel to wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> And the sons of Jakob answered Shekem and Hamor his father, and with subtlety they spake, because he had defiled Dinah their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> And they said to them, We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to a man uncircumcised; for that (would be) a reproach to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> Nevertheless in this we will agree with you, if you will be as we are, by circumcising every male among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and we will give our daughters to you, and your daughters will we take to us, and will dwell with you and be one people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not consent to us to be circumcised, we will take our daughter and go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And their words were pleasing in the eyes of Hamor, and in the eyes of Shekem the son of Hamor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> And the young man delayed not to do the thing, because he delighted in the daughter of Jakob, and he was more honourable than all his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> And Hamor came, and Shekem his son, to the gate of the city, and spake to the men of the city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> These men are friendly with us, and they may dwell in the land, and do business in it, and the land, behold, it is wide (on both) hands before them; their daughters we will take to us for wives, and our daughters we will give to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only in this will the men agree with us to dwell with us, to be one people, in every male of us being circumcised, as they are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> Their flocks, and their possessions, and all their cattle, will they not be ours? Only let us consent to them, and they will dwell with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> And everyone who went out of the gate of his city received of Hamor and of Shekem his son, and they were circumcised, every male, everyone who went out of the gate of his city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> And it was on the third day, when their pains were strong upon them, that the two sons of Jakob, Shemeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, took each man his sword, and came upon the city, which dwelt in security, and slew every male,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> and Hamor and Shekem his son they killed with the edge of the sword. And they brought Dinah out of the house of Shekem, and went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> And the sons of Jakob came up to the spoil of the slain, and sacked the town, because they had defiled their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> Their sheep, and their oxen, and their asses, and all that was in the city and the field, they took.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And all their substance, and all their little ones, and their wives, they captured and spoiled, and all which was in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> And Jakob said to Shemeon and Levi, You have troubled me, in putting strife between me and the inhabitants of the land, the Kenaanites and the Perrezites, and I a people (few) for number; and they will gather against me, and smite me, and I shall perish, I and the men of my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> And they said, As with an outcast should he deal with our sister?</span>
