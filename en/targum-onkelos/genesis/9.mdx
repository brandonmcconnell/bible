<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> And the Lord blessed Noach and his sons, and said to them, Spread abroad, and multiply, and fill the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth, and upon every fowl of the heavens, in all which creepeth upon the earth, and in all fish of the sea; into your hands shall they be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Every moving thing that liveth, for you it shall be to eat; as the green herb I have given you the whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> But the flesh with its life-blood you shall not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 21:28–29; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 4:9–10"> And also your blood of your lives will I require, from every beast will I require it, and from the hand of man. From the hand of the man who sheddeth the blood of his brother will I require the life of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> Whoso sheddeth the blood of man, by witnesses, upon the sentence of the judges, his blood shall be shed; because in the image of the Lord made He man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> And you, spread abroad and multiply; bring forth in the earth and multiply therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the Lord spake to Noach, and to his sons with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> and I, behold, I establish my covenant with you and with your children after you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> and with every living thing which is with you, of fowl, of cattle, and of every beast of the field that is with you, of all going forth from the ark of every animal of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I will establish My covenant with you, and all flesh shall not again be consumed by the waters of a deluge, nor shall there be again a deluge to destroy the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> And the Lord said, This is the sign of the covenant which I appoint (give) between My Word, and between you, and between every living soul that is with you unto perpetual generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> I have set My bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between My Word and between the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And it shall be that when clouding I becloud the earth, the bow shall be seen in the cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> and I will remember the covenant which is between My Word, and between You, and between every living soul of all flesh; and there shall not be again the waters of a deluge to destroy all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> And the bow shall be in the cloud, and I will look upon it, to remember the everlasting covenant between the Word of the Lord and between every living soul of all flesh that is upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord said, This is the sign of the covenant which I have established between My Word and between all flesh which is upon the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> And the sons of Noach who went forth from the ark were Shem and Cham and Yapheth: and Cham is the father of Kenaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three are the sons of Noach and from them was the whole earth Overspread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> And Noach began to be a man working on the earth; and he planted a vineyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> and he drank of the wine, and was drunk; and he was uncovered in the midst of his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> And Cham the father of Kenaan saw the nakedness of his father, and he showed to his two brethren without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And Shem and Yapheth took a mantle, and laid it upon the shoulders of both, and went backward, and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were averted, and the nakedness of their father they saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Noach awoke from his wine, and knew that which his youngest son had done unto him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> and he said; Accursed be Kenaan, A working servant shall he be to his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> And he said: Blessed be the Lord the God of Shem, And Kenaan shall be servant unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> The Lord shall enlarge Yapheth, And He shall make his Shekinah to dwell in the tabernacles of Shem: And Kenaan shall be servant unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Noach lived after the deluge three hundred and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 5:27; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 5:32"> And all the days of Noach were nine hundred and fifty years; and he died.</span>
