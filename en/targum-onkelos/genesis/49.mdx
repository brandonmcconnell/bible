<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:14; Jeremiah 23:20; Daniel 10:14"> And Jacob called his sons, and said, Gather together, and I will show you what will befall you in the end of the days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 7:1; Proverbs 23:22"> Assemble, and hearken, O sons of Jakob, and receive instruction from Israel your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Reuben, thou art my first-born, my strength, and the beginning of my power. Thine should it have been taking to take the three portions, the birthright, the priesthood, and the kingdom: but because thou hast proceeded perversely,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; 1 Chronicles 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Behold, as water outpoured thou wilt not prosper, neither wilt thou receive the excellent portion; because thou wentest up to thy father’s place of sleep: then, my son, didst thou become profane, when thou wentest up to my bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Shimeon and Levi are brothers; mighty men in the land of their sojourning, they did mightily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> My soul was not in their secret; into their company to come I would not have descended from mine honour. For in their anger they slew a slaughter, and in their wilfulness they razed the wall of their enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; 1 Chronicles 4:24–31; 1 Chronicles 6:65"> Accursed was their anger, for it was strong, and their wrath, for it was relentless. I will divide them in Jakob, and I will scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Jehuda, thou art praise and not shame; thy brethren shall praise thee; thy hand shall prevail against thine adversaries, thine enemies shall be dispersed; they will be turned backward before thee, and the sons of thy father will come before thee with salutations. The dominion shall be (thine) in the beginning, and in the end the kingdom shall be increased from the house of Jehuda, because from the judgment of death, my son, hast thou withdrawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> He shall repose, and abide in strength as a lion, and as a lioness, there shall be no king that may cut him off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 9:6"> He who exerciseth dominion shall not pass away from the house of Jehuda, nor the saphra from his children’s children forever, until the Meshiha come, whose is the kingdom, and unto whom shall be the obedience of the nations (or, whom the peoples shall obey).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> Israel shall pass round about in his cities; the people shall build his temple, they will be righteous round about him, and be doers of the law through his doctrine. Of goodly purple will be his raiment, and his vesture of crimson wool with colours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> His mountains shall be red with his vineyards, and his hills be dropping with wine; his valleys shall be white with corn, and with flocks of sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zebulon will dwell nigh the haven of the sea; he will subdue provinces with ships, and will eat the good of the sea, and his boundary shall come unto Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Issakar, rich in substance, will have his heritage between the boundaries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> And he, seeing his portion that it is good, and the land that it is fruit-bearing, will subdue the provinces of the people, and disperse their inhabitants, and they who remain of them will become servants to him and bringers of tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> From the house of Dan will be chosen and will arise a man in whose days his people shall be delivered, and in whose years the tribes of Israel have rest together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> A chosen man will arise from the house of Dan, the terror of whom shall fall upon the peoples; (a man) who will smite the Philistines with strength, as the serpent, the deadly serpent, lurking by the way, be will slay the mighty of the Philistines host, the horsemen with the foot; he will weaken (loosen) the horses and chariots, and throw their riders backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> For thy salvation have I waited, O Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> From the house of Gad will armed hosts go over the Jardena before their brethren to the battle; and with much substance will they return unto their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> The land of Asher will be good; and he shall be nourished with the dainties of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> In a good land will the lot of Naphtali be cast, and his inheritance be fruitful; over them will they give praise and benediction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph is my son who shall increase, my son who shall be blessed, as a vine planted by a fountain of waters. Two tribes will come forth from his sons, and they shall receive a portion and inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> The mighty men, the men of division, were bitter against him; they afflicted him and sorely grieved him</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> And his prophecy shall be fulfilled in them, because he was faithful to the law in secret, and set his confidence firmly. Therefore was gold laid upon his arm, and the kingdom was strengthened and confirmed. This was to him from the mighty God of Jacob, who by His Word pastureth the fathers and the children of the seed of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> The Word of the God of thy father shall be thy Helper, and the All-Sufficient shall bless thee, with the blessings of the dew that descends from the heavens above, with the blessings that spring from the depths of the earth beneath, with the blessings of thy father and of thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> The blessing of thy father shall be added upon the blessing with which my fathers blessed me; which the princes who are of the world have desired: all of them shall be upon the head of Joseph, and upon the man who was separated from his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Benjamin: in his land will dwell the Shekina, and in his possession will the sanctuary be builded. In the morning and evening will the priests offer the oblation, and at eventime divide the remaining portions of the residue of the sacred things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> All these the tribes of Israel are twelve; and this it is which their father spake to them, and blessed them; according to the blessing of each man, blessed he them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> And he commanded them, and said to them, I am to be gathered unto my people; bury me with my fathers in the cavern which is in the field of Ephron the Hitaah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> In the cavern which is in the double field over against Mamre, in the land of Kenaan, which field Abraham bought of Ephron the Hitaah for an inheritancesepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> There buried they Abraham and Sarah his wife; there buried they Izhak and Revekah his wife; and there buried I Leah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> The field, and the cavern that is in it, purchased of the sons of Hitaah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> And Jakob ceased to instruct his sons, and drew his feet together in the bed, and died, and was gathered unto his people.</span>
