<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But the dove found no rest for the dividing of her foot, and she returned unto him into the ark, because the waters were upon all the earth; and he stretched forth his hand, and took her, and brought her to be with him in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he prolonged yet seven days, and afterward added to send forth the dove from the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And the dove came to him at the time of evening, and, behold, a leaf of olive broken off was in her mouth; and Noach knew that the waters were lightened from upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he prolonged yet other seven days, and sent forth the dove, and she added not to return to be with him again.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, in the seventeenth day of the month, the earth was fully dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the Lord spake with Noach, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> Go forth from the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and the wives of thy sons with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Every living thing which is with thee of all flesh, of fowl, and of cattle, and every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, bring forth with thee, and they shall generate in the earth, and spread abroad and multiply upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noach went forth, and his sons, and his wife, and the wives of his sons with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every animal, every reptile and bird, everything which moveth upon the earth after their kind, went forth from the ark.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And the Lord received with approval his oblation; and the Lord said in His Word, I will not add to curse again the earth on account of the guilt of man, because the creations of the heart of man are evil from his infancy; and I will not add again to smite every living thing as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> Yet all the days of the earth, seed-time and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night, shall not cease.</span>
