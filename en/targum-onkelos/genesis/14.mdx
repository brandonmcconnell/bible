<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; Numbers 34:12"> All these were assembled at the plain of the field, which is (now) the place of the sea of salt.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Deuteronomy 2:12; Genesis 21:21"> and the Horaee who were in the mountain of Seir, unto the plain of Paran which lieth upon the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 20:1"> And they turned, and came to the plain of the division of judgment, which is Rekam, and smote all the fields of the Amalkaah, and also the Amoraah who dwelt in Ein-gadey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:22; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:20"> And the king of Sedom, and the king of Amoraah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboim, and tile king of Bela, which is Zoar, went forth and set the battle in array against them in the plain of the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> with Kedar-laomer king of Elam, and Thidal, king of peoples, and Amraphel, king of Babel, and Ariok, king of Elasar; four kings against five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:17; Genesis 19:30; Isaiah 24:18"> And the plain of the field had many pits, from which, they raised asphalta; and the kings of Sedom and Amorah fled, and they fell there; and they who remained fled to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:21; Genesis 14:16; Deuteronomy 28:31"> And they took all the possession of Sedom and Amorah, and, all their food, and went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:5; Genesis 11:27; Revelation 3:19"> And they captured Lot and his substance, son of the brother of Abram, and went. And he had dwelt in Sedom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Genesis 15:3; Genesis 17:27"> And Abram heard that his brother had been captured; and he armed the young men born in his house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued unto Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Psalms 112:5; 1 Kings 15:18"> And he divided against them by night, he and his servants, and smote them, and pursued them unto Hoba, which was northward of Damasek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18–19; 1 Samuel 30:8; Genesis 12:2"> And he recovered all the substance, and Lot also, the son of his brother; and his substance he recovered, the women also, and the people.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> And the king of Sedom came out to meet him after returning from the smiting of Kedar-laomer and the kings who were with him, at the plain of Mephana, which was the king’s place of racing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> And Malki-zedek, king of Yerushelem, brought forth bread and wine (chemar), and he was minister (meshamesh) before EL ILLAAH,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalms 24:1"> and he blessed him, and said: “Blessed be Abram before El Illah, Whose possession is heaven and earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:22; Hebrews 7:4–9; Malachi 3:10"> And blessed be El Illah (Most High), Who hath delivered thine enemies into thine hand.” And he gave him one in ten of the whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the king of Sedom said to Abram, Give me the souls, and the substance take thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> And Abram said to the king of Sedom, I have uplifted my hands in prayer before the Lord God most high, whose possession is heaven and earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 2 Corinthians 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> If from a thread unto the sole of a sandal I take of all that is thine, (and not that thou shouldst say, I have enriched Abram,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Proverbs 3:27; Matthew 7:12"> except of the food for the young men, and the share of the men who went with me, Amer, Eshkol, and Mamre, these will receive their shares.</span>
