<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> And there was a famine in the land, besides the former famine that was in the days of Abraham; and Izhak went to Abimelek, king of the Philistaee, to Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And the Lord appeared to him and said, Go not down into Mizraim, remain in the land as I have told thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> Dwell in this land, and My Word shall be to thee for a helper; and I will bless thee. For to thee and to thy son will I give all these lands; and I will confirm the oath that I have sworn to Abraham thy father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> And I will multiply thy son as the stars of heaven, and will give thy son all these lands, and through thy son shall all the peoples of the earth be blessed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because Abraham obeyed My word, and kept the keeping of My word, My commandments, My covenant, and My law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> And Izhak dwelt at Gerar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> and the men of the place asked respecting his wife. And he said, She is my sister; for he feared to say, My wife, lest the men of the place should kill him on account of Revekah; for she was of beautiful aspect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And it was when be had been there many days that Abimelek the king of the Philistaee looked from a window, and, behold, Izhak was sporting with Revekah his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Abimelek called Izhak and said, Nevertheless, behold, she is thy wife: why then hast thou said, She is my sister? And Izhak said to him, Because I said, Lest I be killed on account of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abimelek said, What is this that thou hast done to us? It might have been that some one of the people had lain with thy wife, and thou wouldst have brought upon us the guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> And Abimelek commanded all the people saying, Whoever injureth this man shall surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> And Izhak sowed in the land, and found in that year a hundred fold on that which he had expended, (or estimated,) and the Lord blessed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> And the man increased, and went on multiplying and increasing, until he had increased greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> And he had flocks of sheep, and herds of cattle, and many servants; and the Philistaee were envious of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> And all the wells that the servants of his father had digged in the days of Abraham the Philistaee stopped up, and filled with dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> And Abimelek said to Izhak, Go from us; for thou art much stronger than we.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Izhak went thence, and sojourned in the vale of Gerar, and dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> And Izhak returned, and digged the well of water which they had digged in the days of Abraham his father, and which the Philistaee had stopped after the death of Abraham; and he called their names after the names by which his father had called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Solomon 4:15"> And the servants of Izhak digged in the valley, and found there a well of flowing waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> And the shepherds of Gerar strove with the shepherds of Izhak, saying, The water is ours; and he called the name of the well Contention, because they had contended with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> And they digged another well, and they strove also on account of it, and he called the name of it Hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> And he went up from thence, and digged another well; and for that they strove not: and he called the name of it Spaciousness. For he said, Because now hath the Lord enlarged us, and we shall spread abroad in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> And he went up from thence to Beer Shava;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> and the Lord appeared to him in the night, and said, I am the God of Abraham thy father; fear not: for in thy help is My Word; and I will bless thee, and will multiply thy sons for the sake of Abraham My servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> And he builded there an altar, and prayed in the name of the Lord. And he spread there his tabernacle; and the servants of Izhak digged there a well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> And Abimelek came to him from Gerar, and a company of his friends, and Phikol the chief of his host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> And Izhak said to him, Why have you come to me, when you have hated me, and sent me from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> And they said, Seeing we have seen that the Word of the Lord is for thy help; and we have said, Let the oath which was between our fathers be now confirmed between us and thee, and let us enter into a covenant with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that thou do us no evil, as we have not injured thee, and as we have done thee only good, and we will leave thee in peace; thou art now blessed of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And he made them a feast, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> And they arose in the morning and covenanted, each man with his brother; and Izhak dismissed them, and they went from him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> And it was in that day that the servants of Izhak came and showed him concerning the well that they had digged. And they said to him, We have found water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> And he called it Sheva, (the swearing). Wherefore the name of the city is Beer-sheva unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> And Esau was the son of forty years, and he took to wife Jehudith, daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, daughter of Elon the Hittite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> And they were rebels and irritators against the word of Izhak and Revekah.</span>
</div>
