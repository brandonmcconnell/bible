<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> And Lot chose to him all the plain of Yardena; and Lot went forth before, and they were separated each man from his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram dwelt in the land of Kenaan, and Lot dwelt in the cities of the plain, and extended unto Sedom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> For all this land that thou seest, to thee will I give it, and to thy children forever.</span>
