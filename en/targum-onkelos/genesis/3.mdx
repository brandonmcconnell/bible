<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 2 Corinthians 11:14; Revelation 12:9"> And the serpent (chivja) was more crafty than all the animals of the field which the Lord God bad made. And he said to the woman, Is it in truth (that) the Lord said, You shall not eat of every tree of the garden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 58:4"> And the woman said to the serpent, Of the fruit of the trees of the garden we may eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Exodus 19:12–13"> but of the fruit of the tree which is in the midst of the garden, the Lord hath said, You shall not eat of it, nor approach to it, lest you die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14"> And the serpent said to the woman. Not to die will you die:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> for it is manifest before the Lord, that in the day in which you eat of it your eyes will be opened, and you will be (ki-rabrebin) as the Great-ones, knowing good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 2:14"> And the woman saw that the tree was good to eat, and that it was salutary to the eyes, and a tree desirable to contemplate; and she took of its fruitage (aiba) and ate; and she gave to her husband with her, and he ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:6; Genesis 2:25; Genesis 3:5"> And the eyes of both of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked; and they sewed for themselves the leaves of the fig tree, and made for themselves cinctures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 15:3; Hebrews 4:13"> And they heard the voice of the Word of the Lord God walking in the garden in the evening of the day; and Adam and his wife hid themselves from before the Lord God among the trees of the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 4:9; Genesis 16:8; Genesis 18:20–21"> And the Lord God called to Adam and said to him, Where art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 2:25; Psalms 119:120; Isaiah 47:3"> And he said, The voice of Thy Word heard I in the garden, and I was afraid, because I (was) naked, and I would hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Genesis 4:10; Psalms 50:21"> And He said, Who showed thee that thou wast naked? Of the tree of which I commanded that eating of it thou shouldst not eat, hast thou eaten?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 1:13–15; Job 31:33"> And Adam said, The woman whom Thou gavest (to be) with me, she gave to me of the tree, and I did eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14; Genesis 4:10–12"> And the Lord God said to the woman, What is this that thou hast done? And the woman said, The serpent led me astray, and I did eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 7:17; Isaiah 65:25; Psalms 72:9"> And the Lord God said to the serpent, Because thou hast done this, more accursed art thou than all cattle, and than all the beasts of the held; upon thy belly shalt thou go, and the dust shalt thou eat all the days of thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 1 John 3:8; Revelation 12:17"> And I will put enmity between thee and between the woman, and between thy son and her son. He will remember thee, what thou didst to him (at) from the beginning, and thou shalt be observant unto him at the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Colossians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> And to the woman He said, Multiplying, I will multiply thy sorrows and thy pains. In sorrow shalt thou bring forth children; and unto thy husband shall be thy desire, and lie. He shall rule over thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:20–22; Genesis 5:29; Isaiah 24:5–6"> And unto Adam He said, Because thou hast obeyed the word of thy wife, and hast eaten of the tree (about) which I commanded thee, saying, Thou shalt not eat of it, accursed is the ground on thy account. With labour shalt thou eat from it all the days of thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:7; Job 5:5; Isaiah 7:23"> Thorns and thistles it shall put forth for thee, and thou shalt eat the herb of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 34:15; Psalms 104:29"> In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, until that thou return to the earth, because from it thou wast created; for dust thou art, and to dust thou wilt return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Matthew 1:23; Genesis 5:29"> And Adam called the name of his wife Hava, because she was the mother of all the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–3"> And the Lord God made for Adam and for his wife vestments of honor upon the skin of their flesh, and clothed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14; Proverbs 3:18"> And the Lord God said, Behold, man is become singular (or alone, yechid) in the world by himself, knowing good and evil; and now, lest he stretch forth his hand and take also of the Tree of Life, and eat, and live forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Genesis 3:19; Genesis 4:12"> And the Lord God sent him forth from the Garden of Eden to till the ground from whence he had been created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:4; Exodus 25:18–22"> And He drove out the man, and before the garden of Eden he caused to dwell the kerubaya, and the sharp sword which revolved to keep the way of the Tree of Life.</span>
