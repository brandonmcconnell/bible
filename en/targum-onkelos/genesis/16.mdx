<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> And he went in unto Hagar, and she conceived, and she saw that she had conceived, and her mistress was despised in her eyes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> And he said, Hagar handmaid of Sara, whence comest thou, and whither art thou going? And she said, From before Sara my mistress do I escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> And the Angel of the Lord said to her, Return to thy mistress and be subject under her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> And the Angel of the Lord said to her: Multiplying I will multiply thy sons, and they shall not be numbered for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> And the Angel of the Lord said to her, Behold, thou hast conceived, and shalt give birth to a son, and thou shalt call his name Yishmael, because the Lord hath heard thy prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> And he will be untameable by man, and he will be needy to everyone, and also all men will be needy to him, and before the face of all his brethren will he dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> And she prayed in the Name of the Lord who had spoken with her; and she said: Thou art Eloha, seeing all: for she said, I also have begun to see after that He hath been revealed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Therefore she called the name of the well, The well at which appeared the Angel of the Covenant: behold, it is between Rekam and Hagra.</span>
