## JESUS MADE FOOD FOR FOUR THOUSAND (8:1-10)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:32–39; Mark 6:34–44"> Again one day many people gathered together in the desert. They were listening to Jesus. After that their food ran out (lit. 'became nothing for them'). Jesus called his men, he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:13"> “These people have been with me a long time, three days. Now their food has run out. I feel sorry for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Judges 8:4–6; 1 Samuel 30:10–12"> I will not send them away to their countries hungry. When they go they would fall down with hunger. They have come from a long way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Kings 7:2; Mark 6:52"> His men said to him, “This countryside has no food. Who will search here and get food for all those people? They are truly very many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 14:15–17; Matthew 15:34; Luke 9:13"> Jesus asked them, “How many dampers do you have?” They replied, “7 small dampers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Matthew 15:35–36; John 6:10–11"> They also had a few small fish. Jesus said to them, “Tell them to sit on the ground.” After that he picked up the dampers, the fish also, he said to God, “You are the one who gave us this food. Thank you! You have helped us! You are good!” Then he broke the dampers into pieces, he gave them to his men, he gave them the fish also, the said to them, “Give this food and meat to them.” That's what they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Psalms 145:16; Matthew 16:10"> At that time very many people ate, 4,000 men ate till they were full. Then Jesus told them, “Yes, off you go to your own countries.” After that Jesus' men picked up the leftovers, they filled 7 big carrying dishes with leftovers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:39"> After that Jesus got into the dinghy, his men also, they went to that country Dalmanutha.</span>
</div>
</div>

## JESUS HEALED A BLIND MAN (8:22-26)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Mark 6:45; Luke 10:13"> Jesus went to that town Bethsaida. Some people brought to him a man on a lead, he was truly blind, he could not see. They begged Jesus, “Touch him to make him well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:33; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:23"> Jesus took the blind man by the hand to outside the town. He spat on his own hand, he touched the spit, he put it on the man's eyes, he asked him, “Do you see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 32:3; Isaiah 29:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–12"> The man looked around a bit, he said to him, “I see people blurrily. I think they look like trees. My eyes deceive me. They are not trees. They have been walking around.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Peter 3:18; Proverbs 4:18"> Again Jesus put his hands on the man's eyes. The man was looking carefully, he saw well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:23; Mark 5:43"> Jesus sent him to his camp. He told him, “Do not go to that town, do not tell them I did this for you.”</span>
</div>
