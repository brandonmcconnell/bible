## JESUS SENT HIS TWELVE MEN TO OTHER TOWNS (6:6b-13)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:16–30; Matthew 13:54–58; Matthew 2:23"> After that Jesus went to his own country, his men followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Matthew 8:10; Luke 13:22"> Jesus was going to towns, he was teaching them about God's word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–10; Luke 10:17–20; Matthew 10:1–5"> After that he called his men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–10; Luke 10:17–20; Matthew 10:1–5"> he told them, I will send you to people, you will tell them about God. I told you this word. From now on you send evil spirits from people. You go, do not take money or food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 12:8; Ephesians 6:15"> Put on shoes, take only one set of clothes. Leave other clothes behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Luke 9:4; Matthew 10:11–13"> Perhaps in some towns a person will say to you, 'Come here, stay a little while with us.' At that time you will stay in his camp in that town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:14–15; Acts 18:6; Luke 10:10–15"> Perhaps you will come to other towns, they will ignore you, they will not ask you to stay in their town, they will not listen to God's word. If they do that do not stay in that town. Brush the dust from your shoes. When you do that you will show them God will be angry with them after ignoring his word. Then leave that town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Luke 13:3; Acts 20:21"> Yes, that is all. Go by twos.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Luke 13:3; Acts 20:21"> After that they went. As they were going they were telling people, “You have been ignoring God. Leave doing wrong and believe in God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:14–15; Mark 6:7; Luke 10:17"> They were sending away many evil spirits, they put medicine on many sick people, they were asking God, “Will you make this person well.” After that the sick person became well. God made them well.</span>
</div>
</div>

## JESUS MADE FOOD FOR 5,000 (6:30-44)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:10; Luke 10:17; Matthew 10:2"> Later Jesus' mob who had been sent out returned to him. They told him they were making people well, they were sending out evil spirits, they were teaching people about God, in that way, in response to Jesus' word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 3:20; John 6:1; Mark 1:45"> At that time very many people came to Jesus. They were crowding him from all sides. They did not let Jesus and his men eat. After that Jesus said to his men, “Just we will go to countryside where there are no people. We will rest for a while.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 8:2–9; Mark 6:45; Mark 4:36"> They climbed into a dinghy, they went to where there were no people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 6:2; Matthew 15:29–31; Mark 6:54–55"> Many were watching them, they knew about them, and they knew where Jesus was going. They went quickly around the end of the waterhole to the far side on foot, they were ready waiting for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Luke 9:11; Numbers 27:17"> When Jesus disembarked he saw many ready waiting for him. When he saw them he felt sorry for them. He said to himself, “These people are truly in a bad way, without a boss. They are lost, like sheep without a carer.” For a long he was teaching them about God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:5–15; Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:15–21"> It became late afternoon. They were still listening to his word. His men said to him, “It is late afternoon. The towns are far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 3:21; Mark 5:31"> Send these people to the towns so they can get food for themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 15:32–33; Mark 8:2–3"> Jesus replied, “You give them food.” His men said to him, “We cannot give them food. We could only get food for those many people with a lot of money. We have only a little money.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:34; Mark 8:5; John 6:9"> In reply he said to them, “How much damper do you have? Look.” After looking they said to him, “Five dampers and two small fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 14:33; Matthew 15:35"> He told his men, “Tell the people to sit down.” They told them to sit down on the grass in groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:14–15"> They sat down at his word, here 50 were in a group, over there 100 were in a group. All the others were like that, they were in groups everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Mark 7:34; Luke 9:16"> Jesus picked up their dampers, he looked upwards, he said to God, “You are the one who gave us this food. Thank you! You have helped us! You are good.” When he had said that, he broke the dampers into pieces, he gave them to his men, he said to them, “Give them to these people.” He did the same sort of thing with the two fish also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Many people ate. Five thousand men ate that food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 9:17; Mark 8:8–9; Matthew 15:37–38"> They all ate till they were full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 8:19–20"> After that his men picked up the leftovers, they put them in carrying dishes, they filled 12 big carrying dishes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## JESUS WALKED ON TOP OF THE WATER (6:45-52)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 14:22–33; John 6:15–21; Mark 6:32"> After that Jesus told his men, “Get in the dinghy, go to the other side, to Bethsaida. Later I will come to you.” They went ahead in the dinghy. Then Jesus sent all the others to their camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 1:35; Matthew 6:6; Luke 6:12"> After that Jesus climbed up a hill to talk to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 14:23; John 6:16–17"> It became dark. He was staying there alone. His men were in the dinghy in the middle of the water, they were in a bad way. A big wind was blowing for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 24:28; Luke 12:38; Job 9:8"> They were still trying in vain to make the dinghy go, in vain they were trying to make it go with oars (lit. 'with wood') because of the big wind. Jesus saw them from a long way off. He came to them before it was day. He was going on top of the water. He kept coming close to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 14:25–26; Luke 24:37; Job 9:8"> His men saw him, he was following them from behind on top of the water. They did not recognise him, they were thinking something like an evil spirit was coming. They were afraid of him, they really called out with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 14:27; John 6:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> Jesus told them, “Don't be afraid. It is me coming. Quieten down, stay well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Mark 4:39; John 6:21"> He climbed in the dinghy with them. Right then the wind abated. His men wondered at him. They were saying to each other, “Yes, look! He was walking on top of the water. As soon as he came right then the wind abated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 8:17–21; Luke 24:25; Isaiah 63:17"> They did not understand. Earlier they saw him give food to 5,000 men from a little fish and damper. They didn't keep it in their minds. They were still thinking to themselves, “He is just a person like us.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## JESUS WAS HEALING PEOPLE AT GENNESARET (6:53-56)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 14:34–36; John 6:24–25; Luke 5:1"> After that they arrived in that country Gennesaret. They tied the dinghy with a rope at the edge of the waterhole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 9:10; Philippians 3:10"> Jesus got out of the dinghy, those inhabitants of the place saw him, straight away they knew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 2:1–3; Mark 3:7–11"> They ran everywhere in that country, they were saying to them, “Jesus has come.” After that they were bringing sick ones on blankets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 9:20; Luke 6:19; Numbers 15:38–39"> That is what they were doing in town after town, in camp after camp. They were bringing sick people to him. In the middle of the towns on flat places some people used to sell food and all sorts of things. There on the flat places they were putting the sick ones on blankets on the ground. They were saying to Jesus, “These sick people want to touch your clothes. They will touch your clothes, they will become well.” Truly those who touched him all became well.</span>
</div>
