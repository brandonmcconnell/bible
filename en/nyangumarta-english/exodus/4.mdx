<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:16; Deuteronomy 18:18; Luke 21:15"> You will speak to him, you will tell him what to say. I will help you two to speak, and I will tell you two what to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:1–2; Psalms 82:6; John 10:34–35"> He will speak to the people for you. You will tell him my words and he will speak for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Exodus 7:9–20"> Take your stick with you, with it you will do wonderful (things).”</span>
