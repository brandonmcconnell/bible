## JACOB AND LABAN (29:1-14)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:3; Judges 6:33; Hosea 12:12"> After that he went from Bethel on foot. At last he came to them in another country, in the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:6"> After that he was looking around, truly he saw a well, a big stone covered it. There he saw a few groups of sheep, they were lying close to the water, they were waiting for water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the distance other people also were bringing their sheep, they were bringing them to the well. As soon as all had arrived at the water the people in charge of the sheep used to move that stone back. After giving water to the sheep they used to put that stone back to cover the well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:10; Genesis 27:43; Acts 7:2"> Jacob said to the people in charge (of the sheep), “Where is your camp?” They answered him, “We are from the town, from what's-its-name, from Haran.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:24; Genesis 24:29; Genesis 31:53"> He said to them, “Do you know Laban, Nahor's grandson?” “Yes, we know him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; Genesis 37:14; Exodus 18:7"> “Is he well?” “Yes, he is well. Look, that is his daughter, she is coming now with sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16; Galatians 6:9–10"> He said to them, “Listen. The sun is still above. Why are you bringing the sheep when the sun is above? Quickly give them water, then quickly take them to the grass.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 16:3; Luke 24:2; Genesis 34:14"> They replied, “We shouldn't give water yet, we will wait for the rest of the sheep. Truly after that we will move back this stone, when we have done that we will give them water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Exodus 2:15–16"> While they were still talking, Rachel arrived with her father's sheep. She was the one who used to look after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> As soon as he saw Rachel and his uncle's sheep Jacob went to the well, he moved back that big stone from on top, he gave water to his uncle's sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14–15; Genesis 45:2"> Then he hugged Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> He said to her, “Your father is older brother for my mother. My mother is Rebekah.” Rachel ran to tell that word to her father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> After hearing that word Laban went quickly to his nephew. He hugged him, he took him to his camp. Jacob told him how he came from far away, from another country, his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> Laban said to him, “You are my own nephew. Stay here with me.” Jacob stayed there, he worked for his uncle for one month.</span>
</div>
</div>

## JACOB WAS WORKING FOR HIS UNCLE SO THAT HIS UNCLE WILL GIVE HIM A WIFE (29:15-30)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> After that Laban said to him, “You are my own nephew. You were just working for me. Now after working what shall I give you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 49:31; Genesis 31:4"> Laban had two daughters. One was Leah, the other Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> Leah's eyes were a bit bad. Rachel was very attractive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> Jacob was liking Rachel, he was wanting her for his wife. Therefore he said to Laban, “I will work for you for 7 years. After that you will give me Rachel as a wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalms 12:2"> Laban said to him, “Yes. I will give this Rachel to you, not to others. Truly you will stay right here with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> After that he was working for his uncle for a long time, for 7 years. Truly he liked that girl. Therefore he was working, he was waiting 7 years, he was thinking it was like a few days he was working for that girl.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> After that he said to his uncle, “I worked for you for 7 years. Quickly give me my promised one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Ruth 4:10–13; Judges 14:10–18"> Laban sent word to the people nearby to come to his camp. “We will all eat food for these two when they are joined, they will be man and wife.” Then his workers cooked a lot of food for them to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Genesis 38:14–15; Genesis 24:65"> After it became dark Laban brought Leah, Rachel's older sister, to Jacob, he said to him, “This one now is your promised one.” (Laban gave one working woman to Leah, her name was Zilpah.) That night Jacob slept with Leah. As soon as he woke up, he saw it was another, Leah. Then he became angry, he said to his uncle, “Why did you deceive me? I worked for you for another woman, for Rachel. Why did you give me Leah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Laban said to him, “This little one I will keep for you. The big one will be first. That is the way we do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> Wait for just a little while. Let us complete 7 days for this one, after that I will give you her younger sister for a wife. After that you will work for me for 7 years for that little one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Jacob listened to Laban. “Yes, I will again work for you.” Then he was staying with Leah for 7 days, after that Laban gave him another wife, Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> (Laban gave one working woman to Rachel, her name was Bilhah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> Jacob slept with Rachel also. He loved Rachel a lot, he didn't care about Leah. For that wife Rachel he was again working for his uncle for 7 years.</span>
</div>
</div>

## JACOB'S CHILDREN (29:31—30:24)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> God knew about Jacob, he did not love Leah. God was sorry for Leah, he gave her children. Her younger sister was staying without children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> Leah became pregnant. Truly she got a male child. She thought about God, “God felt sorry for me. He gave me this boy. Truly my husband will love me, I got this child for him.” So she named that eldest child Reuben.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> After that she got another child, a male. “God knows about my husband, he does not love me, therefore God gave me another child.” So she named that child Simeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> After that she got another child, a male. Then she said, “I gave my husband three male children. Now truly he will love me.” So she named that one Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> After that she got another child, a male. She said to God, “Thank you, God. I will praise you.” So she named that child Judah. After that she did not have any more children for a long time.</span>
</div>
</div>
