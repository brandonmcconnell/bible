## SARAH DIED (23:1-20)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:17"> After that Abraham's wife, that Sarah, died. She was very old, having 127 years. She passed away there, at Hebron, in the country Canaan. Abraham cried for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 10:15; Genesis 23:7; 1 Samuel 26:6"> After crying for her he went to the bosses of the town. (Those men were Hittites &#91;lit. 'Yiti's-plural'&#93;.) Abraham talked to those people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 17:8"> “You know me. I came from far away. This is not my country. I am like a stranger/foreigner. My wife has died. Please give me land, there I will put the dead one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> “Yes, you choose a cave. You are an important boss. Choose a good cave, there you will put your wife. We will not withhold land from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> Abraham bowed down to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> He said to them, “Please speak to the man, Zohar's son, Ephron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> You might ask him to give me a cave in what's-that-place, in Machpelah. That cave is on his land. I will give whatever money he wants for that. I will give the money here, you all will see. After that that cave will become mine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> That man Ephron was sitting there among them. He told Abraham, while other men were listening, “Old man, I will give you that cave, that land close to the cave also. You will put your wife there. All the men here heard what I said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> Again Abraham bowed down to the men of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> He said to Ephron, “No. I will give you money for that land. Take this money from me. After that that land will be mine, I will put the dead one there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> That Ephron said to him, “Old man, please listen to me. I might ask you to give me 400 (pieces of) money, but no. I will give the land to you, just take it for no money. You should put your dead one in the cave.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> “No, I will truly give you the money.” Right then Abraham gave Ephron 400 (pieces of) money, in accordance with Ephron's word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 23:20"> Therefore that cave in Machpelah became Abraham's, that land near the cave and all the trees on that land also. Those important bosses were watching. They all knew, Abraham got that land with money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> After that Abraham put his dead wife in that cave, in the cave at Machpelah, east of Mamre, in the country Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> In that way that cave and land near the cave became Abraham's forever, for a grave.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
