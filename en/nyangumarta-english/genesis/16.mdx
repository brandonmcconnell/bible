## SARAH TOLD ABRAM, “SLEEP WITH MY WORKING WOMAN.” (16:1-6)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:24; Genesis 12:16; Genesis 15:2–3"> Abram was staying in that country, in Canaan, 10 years. He was still childless. That wife of his, Sarai, had not had a child. Sarai was keeping another woman from another country, from Egypt, a working woman for Sarai, her name was Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:4; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 3:17"> At that time Sarai said to Abram, “Husband, I am childless. God has withheld children from me. Take my working woman, take her for a wife, you two sleep together. Maybe she will have a child for me.” He replied, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> After that Sarai gave that woman Hagar to Abram for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> They slept together, Hagar and Abram. After that Hagar became pregnant. Because of that she became proud towards her boss, Sarai. “She is just nobody”, said Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> After that Sarai became angry with Abram. “I gave you my working woman to keep in the camp. Now after becoming pregnant she has beome proud towards me. You are to blame. God will tell you, I am right.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> Abram said to her, “She is your worker, you are boss for her. Whatever you want with her, do that to her.” After that Sarai was keeping Hagar in a bad way. Because of that Hagar ran away towards the desert.</span>
</div>
</div>

## GOD APPEARED TO HAGAR (16:7-16)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> In that desert in dry country Hagar sat down near a spring. God appeared to her like an angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> There he said to her, “Hagar, you are Sarai's working woman. Where have you come from? Where are you going?” Hagar replied, “I am running from my boss afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> He said to her, “Return to your mistress, obey her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> I will give you very many grandchildren's grandchildren and those who come after them, you won't be able to count &#91;lit 'name'&#93; them, they will be very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> You are pregnant, you will have a child, a boy. Yes, I heard you crying, for that reason you will name your son Ishmael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> When he grows up he will be a fighter against all people, they all will be fighters against him. He will be a solitary dweller.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> After that Hagar thought, “Truly it was God I saw, I am really alive, he did not kill me.” She named God, 'The One Who Habitually Sees Me'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Because of that she named that well, “The Living One Sees Me”.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> After that Hagar returned to Sarai. After that she had a child for Abram, a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Abram had 86 years. At that same time Hagar had the child. Abram named him Ishmael.</span>
</div>
</div>
