## CAIN KILLED ABEL (4:1-17, 25)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> Adam and Eve went from that place Eden. After that Eve had a son. She said, “God has given me a son.” His name was Cain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> After that she had another son. His name was Abel.<br />The two became big. They became men. Abel used to care for sheep. Cain used to dig the ground. He used to put foods in the ground, they used to come up, he used to gather them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; 1 Kings 17:7"> Once Cain took food, he gave it to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> His younger brother took a sheep, he killed it, he gave good parts to God. Abel pleased God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> Cain did not please him. Because of that Cain was (habitually) angry. His face changed (became different).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> God said to him, “Why are you angry with me? Why did your face change towards me? You should not be sullen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> If you did right you would have been happy. You were doing badly. You should straighten yourself up to do good. If you do that you will make me happy. If you don't you will keep doing evil.” &#91;Missed concept of need to resist temptation.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> Cain didn't listen to God. He kept on being sullen. Because of that he said to his younger brother, “Let's go somewhere a long way off.” They went off. Then Cain killed his younger brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> Because of that God asked him, “Where is your younger brother?” The eldest one lied, “I don't know. I don't take care of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> God said to him, “You lied. You killed him. His blood is on the ground. You truly did badly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> In response I will do evil to you because of him/it. The ground will stay bad for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> And foods will not come up for you. I will send you far off. With fear you will keep going to other places.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> Cain said to him, “You have made it extremely hard for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> I will go about to other places. Perhaps someone will kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> God said to him, “Whoever hits/kills you I will hit/kill him truly because of you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> Because of that Cain went far off. He stayed eastwards a long way from Eden. They call that far country, 'He just keeps wandering around'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> His wife had a child named Enoch. Cain built a town. He named that town for his child, he named that town Enoch also. &#91;4:18-24 omitted.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; 1 Chronicles 1:1"> After Abel died Eve had another child. She said, “Cain killed Abel. God in response has given me another child.” She named him Seth.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
