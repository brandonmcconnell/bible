<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> Leah said, “Very good.” So she named that child Gad.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> After that Zilpah got another child, a male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> Leah said, “I am happy. Women will call me 'Happy One'.” So she named that child Asher.</span>

Rachel asked her older sister, “Give me some of those mandrakes belonging to your child.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> Then Leah became angry with her. “You took my husband, now you are begging for what my child got, that medicine to make (a woman) pregnant.” Rachel replied, “Give me some of the mandrakes. In exchange you will sleep with our husband tonight.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> Later on she got another child, a female. She named that one Dinah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> Truly she became pregnant, she got a child, a male. She said, “For years and years I was ashamed from being without children. Truly God gave me a child. They will not make me ashamed any more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> She said, “Perhaps God will give me another son.” So she named that child Joseph.</span>

## JACOB WAS WORKING FOR HIS UNCLE SO THAT HE WILL GIVE HIM ANIMALS (30:25-43)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> As soon as Joseph was born Jacob said to his uncle, “I will return to my own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Truly I worked for you for my wives (dual) and children. You know that. Now I will take them. Do not make me stay. I will go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> Laban said to him, “Listen to me. I want you to stay here. As soon as you came from over there earlier God did good to me, he gave me many tame animals. I know about that by magic from above. Resulting from that I want you just to stay with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> Name for yourself whatever you want, I will give you whatever, after that you will stay with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> Jacob said to him, “I have worked comepletely for you. I took good care of your animals. You know that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> You had a small number of animals. As soon as I came here they became many. I worked for you. Therefore God gave you lots of belongings. From now on I must take good care of my children.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> Laban asked him, “What shall I give you? Tell me.” Jacob replied, “Do not give me money. Wait, I will tell you. Today I will look at your animals, I will separate them. I will take the black sheep and the spotted and striped goats. Those particular ones. They will be mine, all the others will be yours. If you say yes, I will stay with you, I will take care of your animals for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> Later you will see I do the right thing to you, I am not a deceiver for you. You will see those animals. Some will be black sheep, some will be spotted and striped goats. If you see other kinds you will know I stole them from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> Laban replied, “Yes, good. You will do like that.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> Laban deceived Jacob. That very day he took all the spotted and striped goats and all the black sheep, he told his sons, “Take all these goats and sheep over there far away, take care of them over there.” As a result of that word they took them 3 days' journey.<br />Then Jacob was taking care of all the rest of Laban's animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> He broke different kinds of green trees/sticks. He trimmed those various small sticks finely to peel (them) down to the white parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> He put those whittled sticks near the water. As soon as those animals came to the water they saw those sticks. When they came close to the water they used to mate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> When they mated they looked at those sticks. Then the sheep gave birth to black young, the goats gave birth to spotted and striped young.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> After that he separated those young ones. He kept them for himself, he did not put them with Laban's animals. He put the oldish &#91;= mature, matable&#93; animals facing towards Laban's animals. &#91;Laban's animals: The plain ones in Jacob's care, not the non-plain ones that Laban's sons had taken far away. 'Facing' here is taken to be a euphemism for 'mating them with'.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Whenever the strong animals were mating Jacob put the sticks in front of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Whenever the weakish animals were mating Jacob did not put those sticks there. The young ones from the weak animals became Laban's. The young ones from the strong animals became Jacob's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> Because of doing that Jacob got for himself many sheep and goats. He was owning many camels and donkeys, and working men and women.</span>
</div>
</div>
