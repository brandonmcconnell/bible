## SARAH GAVE BIRTH TO A CHILD (21:1-7)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> After that God's word for Sarah came true for her. Earlier he said to Abraham, “Your wife Sarah will give birth to a child.” In accordance with God's word she had a child, a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> Abraham named his child Isaac. That name says, 'He Laughs'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> After 8 days Abraham cut him in accordance with God's word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> At that time he was very old, he had 100 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> Sarah was very happy, she laughed, “God made me happy. Others when they hear will be happy, they also will laugh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> Everyone was saying I would remain forever without a child for Abraham. Now I have given birth to a child for him after he is old.”</span>
</div>

## ABRAHAM SENT HAGAR AND ISHMAEL AWAY (21:8-21)

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> The child became big, he left the breast. After that Abraham told his workers to cook a lot of food for many people. After that they all came to Abraham's camp, they were eating that food, they were happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> Ishmael, Hagar's son, in play was mocking Isaac. Sarah saw those two, she became upset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> She said to Abraham, “After you die my son, Isaac, he only will get your belongings. That other child, the working woman's child, he will not get your belongings. Send that working woman and her child right away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> Sarah's word made Abraham upset. He did not want to send away his own child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> God spoke to him, “Don't be upset about Ishmael and his mother. Listen to Sarah, do in accordance with her word. Earlier I said to you that I will give you many grandchildren's grandchildren and those who come after them. From Isaac they will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> Ishmael's grandchildren's grandchildren and those who come after them will be very many. He also is your child. Therefore I will do good to him also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> In the morning at daybreak Abraham gave Hagar food and water. He put a water container on Hagar's shoulder. After that he sent the two of them away. They were going about in the desert near what's-that-place, near Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> Their water ran out. After that Hagar left her son near a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> She went a little way off, she sat down. She said to herself, “Truly my son will soon die. I don't want to see that.” She was really crying for the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> The lad was crying, God heard him. From the sky he called Hagar, “Hagar, do not be upset, do not be afraid. I heard the lad cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> I will look after him. I will give him many grandchildren's grandchildren and those who come after them. Now go to him, take him by the hand, make him stand up.” Like that God spoke to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> After that Hagar looked, God showed her water. She went there, she filled the water container, she took it to the lad, she gave him water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> Ishmael became big, God was caring for him. He became a hunter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> He was staying in the desert, in the country named Paran. His mother got a wife for him from Egypt. &#91;21:22-34 omitted at this stage.&#93;</span>
</div>
