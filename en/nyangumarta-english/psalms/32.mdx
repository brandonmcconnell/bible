<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:47; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21; Revelation 14:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people have done badly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but they told God they were sorry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they did not try to deceive him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he did not growl at them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now they are very happy.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Psalms 38:2–8; Psalms 39:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Day by day, night by night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you made it hard for me, Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I was weakened completely.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the summer the sun pierces the water and dries it up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In that same way you growled at me, Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I became very weak and thin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I nearly died.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 38:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I told you true,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like that I had done badly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not try to hide from you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I decided I would tell you the whole story,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I told you I was ashamed of myself.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because of that you let me off completely.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Isaiah 43:2; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore all your people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the ones who believe in you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">should tell you about their problems.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They should ask you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God, what should I do about this?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With doing that they will go well.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When really big trouble comes for them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a big flood rushing along in a creek,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will care for them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that trouble will not get high enough</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to carry them away.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 119:114; Psalms 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am safe when I am with you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will keep me well when trouble might hurt me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You take my part.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I sing loudly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Ngarrka has saved me!”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 33:18; Psalms 25:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Boss says, “Listen to me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Don't be stupid like a horse or donkey.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You must put a bridle on them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you don't, they won't go where you want them to go.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Don't be stupid like them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Should I have to put a bridle on you?
