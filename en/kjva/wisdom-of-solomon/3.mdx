<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and there shall no torment touch them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure is taken for misery,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And their going from us to be utter destruction: but they are in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For though they be punished in the sight of men, yet is their hope full of immortality.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly rewarded: for God proved them, and found them worthy for himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As gold in the furnace hath he tried them, and received them as a burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And in the time of their visitation they shall shine, and run to and fro like sparks among the stubble.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They shall judge the nations, and have dominion over the people, and their Lord shall reign for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They that put their trust in him shall understand the truth: and such as be faithful in love shall abide with him: for grace and mercy is to his saints, and he hath care for his elect.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But the ungodly shall be punished according to their own imaginations, which have neglected the righteous, and forsaken the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For whoso despiseth wisdom and nurture, he is miserable, and their hope is vain, their labours unfruitful, and their works unprofitable:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Their wives are foolish, and their children wicked:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their offspring is cursed. Wherefore blessed is the barren that is undefiled, which hath not known the sinful bed: she shall have fruit in the visitation of souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And blessed is the eunuch, which with his hands hath wrought no iniquity, nor imagined wicked things against God: for unto him shall be given the special gift of faith, and an inheritance in the temple of the Lord more acceptable to his mind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For glorious is the fruit of good labours: and the root of wisdom shall never fall away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> As for the children of adulterers, they shall not come to their perfection, and the seed of an unrighteous bed shall be rooted out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For though they live long, yet shall they be nothing regarded: and their last age shall be without honour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or, if they die quickly, they have no hope, neither comfort in the day of trial.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For horrible is the end of the unrighteous generation.</span>
</div>
</div>
