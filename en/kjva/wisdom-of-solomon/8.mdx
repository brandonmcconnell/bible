<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Wisdom reacheth from one end to another mightily: and sweetly doth she order all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I loved her, and sought her out from my youth, I desired to make her my spouse, and I was a lover of her beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In that she is conversant with God, she magnifieth her nobility: yea, the Lord of all things himself loved her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For she is privy to the mysteries of the knowledge of God, and a lover of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If riches be a possession to be desired in this life; what is richer than wisdom, that worketh all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And if prudence work; who of all that are is a more cunning workman than she?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if a man love righteousness her labours are virtues: for she teacheth temperance and prudence, justice and fortitude: which are such things, as men can have nothing more profitable in their life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> If a man desire much experience, she knoweth things of old, and conjectureth aright what is to come: she knoweth the subtilties of speeches, and can expound dark sentences: she foreseeth signs and wonders, and the events of seasons and times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore I purposed to take her to me to live with me, knowing that she would be a counsellor of good things, and a comfort in cares and grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For her sake I shall have estimation among the multitude, and honour with the elders, though I be young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgment, and shall be admired in the sight of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> When I hold my tongue, they shall bide my leisure, and when I speak, they shall give good ear unto me: if I talk much, they shall lay their hands upon their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Moreover by the means of her I shall obtain immortality, and leave behind me an everlasting memorial to them that come after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I shall set the people in order, and the nations shall be subject unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Horrible tyrants shall be afraid, when they do but hear of me; I shall be found good among the multitude, and valiant in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> After I am come into mine house, I will repose myself with her: for her conversation hath no bitterness; and to live with her hath no sorrow, but mirth and joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now when I considered these things in myself, and pondered them in my heart, how that to be allied unto wisdom is immortality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And great pleasure it is to have her friendship; and in the works of her hands are infinite riches; and in the exercise of conference with her, prudence; and in talking with her, a good report; I went about seeking how to take her to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For I was a witty child, and had a good spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yea rather, being good, I came into a body undefiled.</span>
