<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="kjva" data-translation-id="eng_kja" data-translation-label="KJVA" data-book="WIS" data-book-slug="wisdom-of-solomon" data-book-name="Wisdom" data-chapter="14" data-verses="31">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again, one preparing himself to sail, and about to pass through the raging waves, calleth upon a piece of wood more rotten than the vessel that carrieth him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For verily desire of gain devised that, and the workman built it by his skill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But thy providence, O Father, governeth it: for thou hast made a way in the sea, and a safe path in the waves;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Shewing that thou canst save from all danger: yea, though a man went to sea without art.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Nevertheless thou wouldest not that the works of thy wisdom should be idle, and therefore do men commit their lives to a small piece of wood, and passing the rough sea in a weak vessel are saved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For in the old time also, when the proud giants perished, the hope of the world governed by thy hand escaped in a weak vessel, and left to all ages a seed of generation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For blessed is the wood whereby righteousness cometh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But that which is made with hands is cursed, as well it, as he that made it: he, because he made it; and it, because, being corruptible, it was called god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For the ungodly and his ungodliness are both alike hateful unto God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For that which is made shall be punished together with him that made it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore even upon the idols of the Gentiles shall there be a visitation: because in the creature of God they are become an abomination, and stumblingblocks to the souls of men, and a snare to the feet of the unwise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the devising of idols was the beginning of spiritual fornication, and the invention of them the corruption of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For neither were they from the beginning, neither shall they be for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For by the vain glory of men they entered into the world, and therefore shall they come shortly to an end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For a father afflicted with untimely mourning, when he hath made an image of his child soon taken away, now honoured him as a god, which was then a dead man, and delivered to those that were under him ceremonies and sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Thus in process of time an ungodly custom grown strong was kept as a law, and graven images were worshipped by the commandments of kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Whom men could not honour in presence, because they dwelt far off, they took the counterfeit of his visage from far, and made an express image of a king whom they honoured, to the end that by this their forwardness they might flatter him that was absent, as if he were present.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Also the singular diligence of the artificer did help to set forward the ignorant to more superstition.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For he, peradventure willing to please one in authority, forced all his skill to make the resemblance of the best fashion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And so the multitude, allured by the grace of the work, took him now for a god, which a little before was but honoured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And this was an occasion to deceive the world: for men, serving either calamity or tyranny, did ascribe unto stones and stocks the incommunicable name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moreover this was not enough for them, that they erred in the knowledge of God; but whereas they lived in the great war of ignorance, those so great plagues called they peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For whilst they slew their children in sacrifices, or used secret ceremonies, or made revellings of strange rites;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They kept neither lives nor marriages any longer undefiled: but either one slew another traiterously, or grieved him by adultery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So that there reigned in all men without exception blood, manslaughter, theft, and dissimulation, corruption, unfaithfulness, tumults, perjury,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Disquieting of good men, forgetfulness of good turns, defiling of souls, changing of kind, disorder in marriages, adultery, and shameless uncleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For the worshipping of idols not to be named is the beginning, the cause, and the end, of all evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For either they are mad when they be merry, or prophesy lies, or live unjustly, or else lightly forswear themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For insomuch as their trust is in idols, which have no life; though they swear falsely, yet they look not to be hurt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Howbeit for both causes shall they be justly punished: both because they thought not well of God, giving heed unto idols, and also unjustly swore in deceit, despising holiness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For it is not the power of them by whom they swear: but it is the just vengeance of sinners, that punisheth always the offence of the ungodly.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
