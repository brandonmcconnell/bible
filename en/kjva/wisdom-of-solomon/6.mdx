<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Because, being ministers of his kingdom, ye have not judged aright, nor kept the law, nor walked after the counsel of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Horribly and speedily shall he come upon you: for a sharp judgment shall be to them that be in high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For mercy will soon pardon the meanest: but mighty men shall be mightily tormented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For he which is Lord over all shall fear no man’s person, neither shall he stand in awe of any man’s greatness: for he hath made the small and great, and careth for all alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But a sore trial shall come upon the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Unto you therefore, O kings, do I speak, that ye may learn wisdom, and not fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For they that keep holiness holily shall be judged holy: and they that have learned such things shall find what to answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Wherefore set your affection upon my words; desire them, and ye shall be instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Wisdom is glorious, and never fadeth away: yea, she is easily seen of them that love her, and found of such as seek her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> She preventeth them that desire her, in making herself first known unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whoso seeketh her early shall have no great travail: for he shall find her sitting at his doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> To think therefore upon her is perfection of wisdom: and whoso watcheth for her shall quickly be without care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For she goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her, sheweth herself favourably unto them in the ways, and meeteth them in every thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And love is the keeping of her laws; and the giving heed unto her laws is the assurance of incorruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And incorruption maketh us near unto God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore the desire of wisdom bringeth to a kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If your delight be then in thrones and sceptres, O ye kings of the people, honour wisdom, that ye may reign for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> As for wisdom, what she is, and how she came up, I will tell you, and will not hide mysteries from you: but will seek her out from the beginning of her nativity, and bring the knowledge of her into light, and will not pass over the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Neither will I go with consuming envy; for such a man shall have no fellowship with wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But the multitude of the wise is the welfare of the world: and a wise king is the upholding of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Receive therefore instruction through my words, and it shall do you good.</span>
