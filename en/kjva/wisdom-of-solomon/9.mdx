<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O God of my fathers, and Lord of mercy, who hast made all things with thy word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And ordained man through thy wisdom, that he should have dominion over the creatures which thou hast made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And order the world according to equity and righteousness, and execute judgment with an upright heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Give me wisdom, that sitteth by thy throne; and reject me not from among thy children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For I thy servant and son of thine handmaid am a feeble person, and of a short time, and too young for the understanding of judgment and laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For though a man be never so perfect among the children of men, yet if thy wisdom be not with him, he shall be nothing regarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Thou hast chosen me to be a king of thy people, and a judge of thy sons and daughters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thou hast commanded me to build a temple upon thy holy mount, and an altar in the city wherein thou dwellest, a resemblance of the holy tabernacle, which thou hast prepared from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And wisdom was with thee: which knoweth thy works, and was present when thou madest the world, and knew what was acceptable in thy sight, and right in thy commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> O send her out of thy holy heavens, and from the throne of thy glory, that being present she may labour with me, that I may know what is pleasing unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For she knoweth and understandeth all things, and she shall lead me soberly in my doings, and preserve me in her power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So shall my works be acceptable, and then shall I judge thy people righteously, and be worthy to sit in my father’s seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For what man is he that can know the counsel of God? or who can think what the will of the Lord is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the thoughts of mortal men are miserable, and our devices are but uncertain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth, and with labour do we find the things that are before us: but the things that are in heaven who hath searched out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And thy counsel who hath known, except thou give wisdom, and send thy Holy Spirit from above?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed, and men were taught the things that are pleasing unto thee, and were saved through wisdom.</span>
