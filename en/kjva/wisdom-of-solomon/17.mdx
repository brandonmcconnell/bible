<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> For great are thy judgments, and cannot be expressed: therefore unnurtured souls have erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For when unrighteous men thought to oppress the holy nation; they being shut up in their houses, the prisoners of darkness, and fettered with the bonds of a long night, lay there exiled from the eternal providence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For while they supposed to lie hid in their secret sins, they were scattered under a dark veil of forgetfulness, being horribly astonished, and troubled with strange apparitions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For neither might the corner that held them keep them from fear: but noises as of waters falling down sounded about them, and sad visions appeared unto them with heavy countenances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> No power of the fire might give them light: neither could the bright flames of the stars endure to lighten that horrible night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Only there appeared unto them a fire kindled of itself, very dreadful: for being much terrified, they thought the things which they saw to be worse than the sight they saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> As for the illusions of art magick, they were put down, and their vaunting in wisdom was reproved with disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For they, that promised to drive away terrors and troubles from a sick soul, were sick themselves of fear, worthy to be laughed at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For though no terrible thing did fear them; yet being scared with beasts that passed by, and hissing of serpents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> They died for fear, denying that they saw the air, which could of no side be avoided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For wickedness, condemned by her own witness, is very timorous, and being pressed with conscience, always forecasteth grievous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For fear is nothing else but a betraying of the succours which reason offereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the expectation from within, being less, counteth the ignorance more than the cause which bringeth the torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But they sleeping the same sleep that night, which was indeed intolerable, and which came upon them out of the bottoms of inevitable hell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Were partly vexed with monstrous apparitions, and partly fainted, their heart failing them: for a sudden fear, and not looked for, came upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So then whosoever there fell down was straitly kept, shut up in a prison without iron bars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For whether he were husbandman, or shepherd, or a labourer in the field, he was overtaken, and endured that necessity, which could not be avoided: for they were all bound with one chain of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Whether it were a whistling wind, or a melodious noise of birds among the spreading branches, or a pleasing fall of water running violently,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Or a terrible sound of stones cast down, or a running that could not be seen of skipping beasts, or a roaring voice of most savage wild beasts, or a rebounding echo from the hollow mountains; these things made them to swoon for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the whole world shined with clear light, and none were hindered in their labour:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Over them only was spread an heavy night, an image of that darkness which should afterward receive them: but yet were they unto themselves more grievous than the darkness.</span>
