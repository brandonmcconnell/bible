<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In those days arose Mattathias the son of John, the son of Simeon, a priest of the sons of Joarib, from Jerusalem, and dwelt in Modin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he had five sons, Joannan, called Caddis:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Simon; called Thassi:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Judas, who was called Maccabeus:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Eleazar, called Avaran: and Jonathan, whose surname was Apphus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And when he saw the blasphemies that were committed in Juda and Jerusalem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He said, Woe is me! wherefore was I born to see this misery of my people, and of the holy city, and to dwell there, when it was delivered into the hand of the enemy, and the sanctuary into the hand of strangers?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Her temple is become as a man without glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Her glorious vessels are carried away into captivity, her infants are slain in the streets, her young men with the sword of the enemy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> What nation hath not had a part in her kingdom and gotten of her spoils?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All her ornaments are taken away; of a free woman she is become a bondslave.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And, behold, our sanctuary, even our beauty and our glory, is laid waste, and the Gentiles have profaned it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> To what end therefore shall we live any longer?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then Mattathias and his sons rent their clothes, and put on sackcloth, and mourned very sore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> In the mean while the king’s officers, such as compelled the people to revolt, came into the city Modin, to make them sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when many of Israel came unto them, Mattathias also and his sons came together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then answered the king’s officers, and said to Mattathias on this wise, Thou art a ruler, and an honourable and great man in this city, and strengthened with sons and brethren:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Now therefore come thou first, and fulfil the king’s commandment, like as all the heathen have done, yea, and the men of Juda also, and such as remain at Jerusalem: so shalt thou and thy house be in the number of the king’s friends, and thou and thy children shall be honoured with silver and gold, and many rewards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then Mattathias answered and spake with a loud voice, Though all the nations that are under the king’s dominion obey him, and fall away every one from the religion of their fathers, and give consent to his commandments:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yet will I and my sons and my brethren walk in the covenant of our fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> God forbid that we should forsake the law and the ordinances.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> We will not hearken to the king’s words, to go from our religion, either on the right hand, or the left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Now when he had left speaking these words, there came one of the Jews in the sight of all to sacrifice on the altar which was at Modin, according to the king’s commandment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Which thing when Mattathias saw, he was inflamed with zeal, and his reins trembled, neither could he forbear to shew his anger according to judgment: wherefore he ran, and slew him upon the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Also the king’s commissioner, who compelled men to sacrifice, he killed at that time, and the altar he pulled down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Thus dealt he zealously for the law of God like as Phinees did unto Zambri the son of Salom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Mattathias cried throughout the city with a loud voice, saying, Whosoever is zealous of the law, and maintaineth the covenant, let him follow me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So he and his sons fled into the mountains, and left all that ever they had in the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then many that sought after justice and judgment went down into the wilderness, to dwell there:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Both they, and their children, and their wives; and their cattle; because afflictions increased sore upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Now when it was told the king’s servants, and the host that was at Jerusalem, in the city of David, that certain men, who had broken the king’s commandment, were gone down into the secret places in the wilderness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> They pursued after them a great number, and having overtaken them, they camped against them, and made war against them on the sabbath day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they said unto them, Let that which ye have done hitherto suffice; come forth, and do according to the commandment of the king, and ye shall live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But they said, We will not come forth, neither will we do the king’s commandment, to profane the sabbath day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> So then they gave them the battle with all speed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Howbeit they answered them not, neither cast they a stone at them, nor stopped the places where they lay hid;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But said, Let us die all in our innocency: heaven and earth will testify for us, that ye put us to death wrongfully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> So they rose up against them in battle on the sabbath, and they slew them, with their wives and children and their cattle, to the number of a thousand people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Now when Mattathias and his friends understood hereof, they mourned for them right sore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And one of them said to another, If we all do as our brethren have done, and fight not for our lives and laws against the heathen, they will now quickly root us out of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> At that time therefore they decreed, saying, Whosoever shall come to make battle with us on the sabbath day, we will fight against him; neither will we die all, as our brethren that were murdered in the secret places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then came there unto him a company of Assideans who were mighty men of Israel, even all such as were voluntarily devoted unto the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Also all they that fled for persecution joined themselves unto them, and were a stay unto them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So they joined their forces, and smote sinful men in their anger, and wicked men in their wrath: but the rest fled to the heathen for succour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Then Mattathias and his friends went round about, and pulled down the altars:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And what children soever they found within the coast of Israel uncircumcised, those they circumcised valiantly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> They pursued also after the proud men, and the work prospered in their hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> So they recovered the law out of the hand of the Gentiles, and out of the hand of kings, neither suffered they the sinner to triumph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Now when the time drew near that Mattathias should die, he said unto his sons, Now hath pride and rebuke gotten strength, and the time of destruction, and the wrath of indignation:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Now therefore, my sons, be ye zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of your fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Call to remembrance what acts our fathers did in their time; so shall ye receive great honour and an everlasting name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Was not Abraham found faithful in temptation, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> Joseph in the time of his distress kept the commandment and was made lord of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Phinees our father in being zealous and fervent obtained the covenant of an everlasting priesthood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> Jesus for fulfilling the word was made a judge in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Caleb for bearing witness before the congregation received the heritage of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> David for being merciful possessed the throne of an everlasting kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Elias for being zealous and fervent for the law was taken up into heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, by believing were saved out of the flame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> Daniel for his innocency was delivered from the mouth of lions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And thus consider ye throughout all ages, that none that put their trust in him shall be overcome.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> Fear not then the words of a sinful man: for his glory shall be dung and worms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> To day he shall be lifted up and to morrow he shall not be found, because he is returned into his dust, and his thought is come to nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> Wherefore, ye my sons, be valiant and shew yourselves men in the behalf of the law; for by it shall ye obtain glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> And behold, I know that your brother Simon is a man of counsel, give ear unto him alway: he shall be a father unto you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> As for Judas Maccabeus, he hath been mighty and strong, even from his youth up: let him be your captain, and fight the battle of the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> Take also unto you all those that observe the law, and avenge ye the wrong of your people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> Recompense fully the heathen, and take heed to the commandments of the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> So he blessed them, and was gathered to his fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> And he died in the hundred forty and sixth year, and his sons buried him in the sepulchres of his fathers at Modin, and all Israel made great lamentation for him.</span>
</div>
</div>
