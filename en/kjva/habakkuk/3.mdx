<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> Thou didst strike through with his staves the head of his villages: they came out as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="3.14 came…: Heb. were tempestuous">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, through the heap of great waters.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="3.15 heap: or, mud">n</a></sup></span>
