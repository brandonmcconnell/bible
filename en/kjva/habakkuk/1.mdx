<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> The burden which Habakkuk the prophet did see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> O LORD, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! even cry out unto thee of violence, and thou wilt not save!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> Why dost thou shew me iniquity, and cause me to behold grievance? for spoiling and violence are before me: and there are that raise up strife and contention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> Therefore the law is slacked, and judgment doth never go forth: for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore wrong judgment proceedeth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.4 wrong: or, wrested">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> Behold ye among the heathen, and regard, and wonder marvellously: for I will work a work in your days, which ye will not believe, though it be told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> For, lo, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, which shall march through the breadth of the land, to possess the dwellingplaces that are not theirs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.6 breadth: Heb. breadths">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> They are terrible and dreadful: their judgment and their dignity shall proceed of themselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.7 their judgment…: or, from them shall proceed the judgment of these, and the captivity of these">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> Their horses also are swifter than the leopards, and are more fierce than the evening wolves: and their horsemen shall spread themselves, and their horsemen shall come from far; they shall fly as the eagle that hasteth to eat.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.8 fierce: Heb. sharp">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> They shall come all for violence: their faces shall sup up as the east wind, and they shall gather the captivity as the sand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.9 their…: or, the supping up of their faces, etc: or, their faces shall look (Heb. the opposition of their faces) toward the east">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> And they shall scoff at the kings, and the princes shall be a scorn unto them: they shall deride every strong hold; for they shall heap dust, and take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> Then shall his mind change, and he shall pass over, and offend, imputing this his power unto his god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> Art thou not from everlasting, O LORD my God, mine Holy One? we shall not die. O LORD, thou hast ordained them for judgment; and, O mighty God, thou hast established them for correction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.12 mighty…: Heb. rock">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.12 established: Heb. founded">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil, and canst not look on iniquity: wherefore lookest thou upon them that deal treacherously, and holdest thy tongue when the wicked devoureth the man that is more righteous than he?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.13 iniquity: or, grievance">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> And makest men as the fishes of the sea, as the creeping things, that have no ruler over them?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.14 creeping: or, moving">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> They take up all of them with the angle, they catch them in their net, and gather them in their drag: therefore they rejoice and are glad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.15 drag: or, flue net">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore they sacrifice unto their net, and burn incense unto their drag; because by them their portion is fat, and their meat plenteous.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.16 plenteous: or, dainty: Heb. fat">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> Shall they therefore empty their net, and not spare continually to slay the nations?</span>
</div>
