<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Therefore we ought to give the more earnest heed to the things which we have heard, lest at any time we should let them slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the word spoken by angels was stedfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompence of reward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> How shall we escape, if we neglect so great salvation; which at the first began to be spoken by the Lord, and was confirmed unto us by them that heard him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> God also bearing them witness, both with signs and wonders, and with divers miracles, and gifts of the Holy Ghost, according to his own will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For unto the angels hath he not put in subjection the world to come, whereof we speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But one in a certain place testified, saying, What is man, that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man, that thou visitest him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> Thou madest him a little lower than the angels; thou crownedst him with glory and honour, and didst set him over the works of thy hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> Thou hast put all things in subjection under his feet. For in that he put all in subjection under him, he left nothing that is not put under him. But now we see not yet all things put under him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour; that he by the grace of God should taste death for every man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it became him, for whom are all things, and by whom are all things, in bringing many sons unto glory, to make the captain of their salvation perfect through sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one: for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> Saying, I will declare thy name unto my brethren, in the midst of the church will I sing praise unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again, I will put my trust in him. And again, Behold I and the children which God hath given me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Forasmuch then as the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same; that through death he might destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> And deliver them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For verily he took not on him the nature of angels; but he took on him the seed of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Wherefore in all things it behoved him to be made like unto his brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest in things pertaining to God, to make reconciliation for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For in that he himself hath suffered being tempted, he is able to succour them that are tempted.</span>
