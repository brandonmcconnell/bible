<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Therefore leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ, let us go on unto perfection; not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works, and of faith toward God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> Of the doctrine of baptisms, and of laying on of hands, and of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> And this will we do, if God permit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened, and have tasted of the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> And have tasted the good word of God, and the powers of the world to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> If they shall fall away, to renew them again unto repentance; seeing they crucify to themselves the Son of God afresh, and put him to an open shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For the earth which drinketh in the rain that cometh oft upon it, and bringeth forth herbs meet for them by whom it is dressed, receiveth blessing from God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> But that which beareth thorns and briers is rejected, and is nigh unto cursing; whose end is to be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But, beloved, we are persuaded better things of you, and things that accompany salvation, though we thus speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> And we desire that every one of you do shew the same diligence to the full assurance of hope unto the end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For when God made promise to Abraham, because he could swear by no greater, he sware by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> Saying, Surely blessing I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so, after he had patiently endured, he obtained the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> For men verily swear by the greater: and an oath for confirmation is to them an end of all strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> Where in God, willing more abundantly to shew unto the heirs of promise the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> That by two immutable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we might have a strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope set before us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> Which hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and stedfast, and which entereth into that within the veil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> Whither the forerunner is for us entered, even Jesus, made an high priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec.</span>
