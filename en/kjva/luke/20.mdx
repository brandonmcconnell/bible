<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> <span className="woj">And again he sent another servant: and they beat him also, and entreated him shamefully, and sent him away empty.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And he said unto them,<span className="woj">Render therefore unto Cæsar the things which be Cæsar’s, and unto God the things which be God’s.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> Therefore in the resurrection whose wife of them is she? for seven had her to wife.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> <span className="woj">But they which shall be accounted worthy to obtain that world, and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry, nor are given in marriage:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> <span className="woj">Neither can they die any more: for they are equal unto the angels; and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> <span className="woj">For he is not a God of the dead, but of the living: for all live unto him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> And he said unto them,<span className="woj">How say they that Christ is David’s son?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> <span className="woj">Till I make thine enemies thy footstool.</span></span>
