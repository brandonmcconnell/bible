<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> And the people, when they knew it, followed him: and he received them, and spake unto them of the kingdom of God, and healed them that had need of healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:5–15; Hosea 13:5"> And when the day began to wear away, then came the twelve, and said unto him, Send the multitude away, that they may go into the towns and country round about, and lodge, and get victuals: for we are here in a desert place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; John 6:5–9; 2 Kings 4:42–43"> But he said unto them,<span className="woj">Give ye them to eat.</span>And they said, We have no more but five loaves and two fishes; except we should go and buy meat for all this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 8:6; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Mark 6:39–40"> For they were about five thousand men. And he said to his disciples,<span className="woj">Make them sit down by fifties in a company.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they did so, and made them all sit down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; 1 Timothy 4:4–5; Matthew 15:36"> Then he took the five loaves and the two fishes, and looking up to heaven, he blessed them, and brake, and gave to the disciples to set before the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 4:44; Matthew 15:37–38; John 6:11–13"> And they did eat, and were all filled: and there was taken up of fragments that remained to them twelve baskets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 9:7–8; Matthew 14:2"> They answering said, John the Baptist; but some say, Elias; and others say, that one of the old prophets is risen again.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:20; Matthew 17:9; Mark 8:30–31"> And he straitly charged them, and commanded them to tell no man that thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:7; Luke 18:31–34"> Saying,<span className="woj">The Son of man must suffer many things, and be rejected of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be slain, and be raised the third day.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 10:38–39; Luke 14:26–27; Romans 8:13"> And he said to them all,<span className="woj">If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:33; John 12:25; Acts 20:23–24"> <span className="woj">For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: but whosoever will lose his life for my sake, the same shall save it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 8:36; Matthew 16:26; Psalms 49:6–8"> <span className="woj">For what is a man advantaged, if he gain the whole world, and lose himself, or be cast away?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:32–33; 2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 12:8–9"> <span className="woj">For whosoever shall be ashamed of me and of my words, of him shall the Son of man be ashamed, when he shall come in his own glory, and in his Father’s, and of the holy angels.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; John 21:22–23"> <span className="woj">But I tell you of a truth, there be some standing here, which shall not taste of death, till they see the kingdom of God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:1–13; Matthew 26:37–39; Mark 14:33–36"> And it came to pass about an eight days after these sayings, he took Peter and John and James, and went up into a mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> And as he prayed, the fashion of his countenance was altered, and his raiment was white and glistering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11; Luke 1:17"> And, behold, there talked with him two men, which were Moses and Elias:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; 1 Peter 1:11–12; 1 Peter 5:10"> Who appeared in glory, and spake of his decease which he should accomplish at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> But Peter and they that were with him were heavy with sleep: and when they were awake, they saw his glory, and the two men that stood with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Mark 10:38; Psalms 4:6–7"> And it came to pass, as they departed from him, Peter said unto Jesus, Master, it is good for us to be here: and let us make three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias: not knowing what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:34–38; Matthew 17:5–7; Judges 13:22"> While he thus spake, there came a cloud, and overshadowed them: and they feared as they entered into the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 3:22; Acts 3:22–23; Matthew 3:17"> And there came a voice out of the cloud, saying, This is my beloved Son: hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 17:9; Mark 9:9–10; Mark 9:6"> And when the voice was past, Jesus was found alone. And they kept it close, and told no man in those days any of those things which they had seen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 17:14–20; Mark 9:14–29"> And it came to pass, that on the next day, when they were come down from the hill, much people met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; John 4:47; Luke 7:12"> And, behold, a man of the company cried out, saying, Master, I beseech thee, look upon my son: for he is mine only child.</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Acts 19:13–16; Luke 10:17–19"> And I besought thy disciples to cast him out; and they could not.</span>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 9:26–27; Luke 7:15; Revelation 12:12"> And as he was yet a coming, the devil threw him down, and tare him. And Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, and healed the child, and delivered him again to his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:43–45; Luke 5:26; Matthew 17:22"> And they were all amazed at the mighty power of God. But while they wondered every one at all things which Jesus did, he said unto his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Acts 2:23; Luke 2:19"> <span className="woj">Let these sayings sink down into your ears: for the Son of man shall be delivered into the hands of men.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 2:50; John 12:16"> But they understood not this saying, and it was hid from them, that they perceived it not: and they feared to ask him of that saying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Mark 9:33–37; Matthew 20:20–22"> Then there arose a reasoning among them, which of them should be greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 139:23; John 16:30; Luke 5:22"> And Jesus, perceiving the thought of their heart, took a child, and set him by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:37"> And said unto them,<span className="woj">Whosoever shall receive this child in my name receiveth me: and whosoever shall receive me receiveth him that sent me: for he that is least among you all, the same shall be great.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Numbers 11:27–29; Mark 10:13–14"> And John answered and said, Master, we saw one casting out devils in thy name; and we forbad him, because he followeth not with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; 1 Corinthians 12:3"> And Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">Forbid him not: for he that is not against us is for us.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5–9; Mark 16:19; Luke 13:22"> And it came to pass, when the time was come that he should be received up, he stedfastly set his face to go to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 10:5; Luke 17:16; Luke 10:1"> And sent messengers before his face: and they went, and entered into a village of the Samaritans, to make ready for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:9; John 4:40–42; Luke 9:48"> And they did not receive him, because his face was as though he would go to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 Kings 1:10–14; James 3:14–18; James 1:19–20"> And when his disciples James and John saw this, they said, Lord, wilt thou that we command fire to come down from heaven, and consume them, even as Elias did?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Peter 3:9; Jeremiah 17:9"> But he turned, and rebuked them, and said,<span className="woj">Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 12:21; John 3:17"> <span className="woj">For the Son of man is not come to destroy men’s lives, but to save them.</span>And they went to another village.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 8:19–22; John 13:37; Luke 9:57–60"> And it came to pass, that, as they went in the way, a certain man said unto him, Lord, I will follow thee whithersoever thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Matthew 8:20; Luke 14:26–33; James 2:5"> And Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">Foxes have holes, and birds of the air have nests; but the Son of man hath not where to lay his head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Matthew 6:33; 1 Kings 19:20"> And he said unto another,<span className="woj">Follow me.</span>But he said, Lord, suffer me first to go and bury my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:6; John 21:15–17"> Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">Let the dead bury their dead: but go thou and preach the kingdom of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Luke 14:18–20; 1 Kings 19:20"> And another also said, Lord, I will follow thee; but let me first go bid them farewell, which are at home at my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Philippians 3:13; Hebrews 10:38; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> And Jesus said unto him,<span className="woj">No man, having put his hand to the plough, and looking back, is fit for the kingdom of God.</span></span>
