<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the children of Heth answered Abraham, saying unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 14:14; Exodus 32:22"> Hear us, my lord: thou art a mighty prince among us: in the choice of our sepulchres bury thy dead; none of us shall withhold from thee his sepulchre, but that thou mayest bury thy dead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.6 a mighty…: Heb. a prince of God">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> And Abraham stood up, and bowed himself to the people of the land, even to the children of Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> And he communed with them, saying, If it be your mind that I should bury my dead out of my sight; hear me, and intreat for me to Ephron the son of Zohar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> That he may give me the cave of Machpelah, which he hath, which is in the end of his field; for as much money as it is worth he shall give it me for a possession of a buryingplace amongst you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.9 as much…: Heb. full money">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> And Ephron dwelt among the children of Heth: and Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham in the audience of the children of Heth, even of all that went in at the gate of his city, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.10 audience: Heb. ears">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; Deuteronomy 19:15"> Nay, my lord, hear me: the field give I thee, and the cave that is therein, I give it thee; in the presence of the sons of my people give I it thee: bury thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> And Abraham bowed down himself before the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> And he spake unto Ephron in the audience of the people of the land, saying, But if thou wilt give it, I pray thee, hear me: I will give thee money for the field; take it of me, and I will bury my dead there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Ephron answered Abraham, saying unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:12; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 30:15"> My lord, hearken unto me: the land is worth four hundred shekels of silver; what is that betwixt me and thee? bury therefore thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> And Abraham hearkened unto Ephron; and Abraham weighed to Ephron the silver, which he had named in the audience of the sons of Heth, four hundred shekels of silver, current money with the merchant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Jeremiah 32:12; Genesis 34:20"> Unto Abraham for a possession in the presence of the children of Heth, before all that went in at the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> And after this, Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the cave of the field of Machpelah before Mamre: the same is Hebron in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> And the field, and the cave that is therein, were made sure unto Abraham for a possession of a buryingplace by the sons of Heth.</span>
