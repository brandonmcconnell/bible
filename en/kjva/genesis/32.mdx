<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10; Exodus 14:10"> Then Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed: and he divided the people that was with him, and the flocks, and herds, and the camels, into two bands;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Psalms 34:4–6; Genesis 31:3"> And Jacob said, O God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Isaac, the LORD which saidst unto me, Return unto thy country, and to thy kindred, and I will deal well with thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:35; Genesis 24:27; 2 Samuel 7:18"> I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies, and of all the truth, which thou hast shewed unto thy servant; for with my staff I passed over this Jordan; and now I am become two bands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="32.10 I am not…: Heb. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–15; Hebrews 6:17; Titus 1:2"> And thou saidst, I will surely do thee good, and make thy seed as the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered for multitude.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 19:6"> And he lodged there that same night; and took of that which came to his hand a present for Esau his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 1:3; Deuteronomy 8:18; Job 42:12"> Two hundred she goats, and twenty he goats, two hundred ewes, and twenty rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thirty milch camels with their colts, forty kine, and ten bulls, twenty she asses, and ten foals.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 33:3"> And he commanded the foremost, saying, When Esau my brother meeteth thee, and asketh thee, saying, Whose art thou? and whither goest thou? and whose are these before thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:4–5"> Then thou shalt say, They be thy servant Jacob’s; it is a present sent unto my lord Esau: and, behold, also he is behind us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And so commanded he the second, and the third, and all that followed the droves, saying, On this manner shall ye speak unto Esau, when ye find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 42:8–9; Proverbs 21:14; Genesis 43:11"> And say ye moreover, Behold, thy servant Jacob is behind us. For he said, I will appease him with the present that goeth before me, and afterward I will see his face; peradventure he will accept of me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="32.20 of me: Heb. my face">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So went the present over before him: and himself lodged that night in the company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:16; Deuteronomy 2:37; 1 Timothy 5:8"> And he rose up that night, and took his two wives, and his two womenservants, and his eleven sons, and passed over the ford Jabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he took them, and sent them over the brook, and sent over that he had.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="32.23 sent them: Heb. caused to pass">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 12:3–5; Ephesians 6:12; Luke 13:24"> And Jacob was left alone; and there wrestled a man with him until the breaking of the day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="32.24 breaking…: Heb. ascending of the morning">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Hosea 12:3–4; Matthew 26:41"> And when he saw that he prevailed not against him, he touched the hollow of his thigh; and the hollow of Jacob’s thigh was out of joint, as he wrestled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Psalms 115:12–13; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And he said, Let me go, for the day breaketh. And he said, I will not let thee go, except thou bless me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he said unto him, What is thy name? And he said, Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Genesis 35:10; Isaiah 62:2–4"> And he said, Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with God and with men, and hast prevailed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="32.28 Israel: that is, A prince of God">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:16–18; Job 11:7"> And Jacob asked him, and said, Tell me, I pray thee, thy name. And he said, Wherefore is it that thou dost ask after my name? And he blessed him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 24:10–11; Numbers 12:8; Isaiah 6:5"> And Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="32.30 Peniel: that is, The face of God">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7; Genesis 19:15; Judges 8:8"> And as he passed over Penuel the sun rose upon him, and he halted upon his thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5"> Therefore the children of Israel eat not of the sinew which shrank, which is upon the hollow of the thigh, unto this day: because he touched the hollow of Jacob’s thigh in the sinew that shrank.</span>
