<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 8:26"> In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia a thing was revealed unto Daniel, whose name was called Belteshazzar; and the thing was true, but the time appointed was long: and he understood the thing, and had understanding of the vision.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.1 long: Heb. great">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:4; Daniel 9:24–27; Ezra 9:4–5"> In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.2 full…: Heb. weeks of days">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:17; Daniel 11:8; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.3 pleasant…: Heb. bread of desires">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:14; Daniel 8:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> And in the four and twentieth day of the first month, as I was by the side of the great river, which is Hiddekel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13–15; Daniel 12:6–7; Ezekiel 9:2"> Then I lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a certain man clothed in linen, whose loins were girded with fine gold of Uphaz:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.5 a…: Heb. one man">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 19:12; Revelation 1:13–17; Matthew 17:2"> His body also was like the beryl, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his feet like in colour to polished brass, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Acts 22:9; Genesis 3:10"> And I Daniel alone saw the vision: for the men that were with me saw not the vision; but a great quaking fell upon them, so that they fled to hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:27"> Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no strength in me: for my comeliness was turned in me into corruption, and I retained no strength.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.8 comeliness: or, vigour">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; Job 4:13; Genesis 15:12"> Yet heard I the voice of his words: and when I heard the voice of his words, then was I in a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Jeremiah 1:9; Daniel 8:18"> And, behold, an hand touched me, which set me upon my knees and upon the palms of my hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.10 set: Heb. moved">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 26:16; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 8:16–17"> And he said unto me, O Daniel, a man greatly beloved, understand the words that I speak unto thee, and stand upright: for unto thee am I now sent. And when he had spoken this word unto me, I stood trembling.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.11 greatly…: Heb. of desires">g</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="10.11 upright: Heb. upon thy standing">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:30–31; Daniel 9:20–23; Daniel 10:19"> Then said he unto me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thine heart to understand, and to chasten thyself before thy God, thy words were heard, and I am come for thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Daniel 10:20–21"> But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me; and I remained there with the kings of Persia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="10.13 chief: or, first">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Daniel 2:28; Daniel 12:4"> Now I am come to make thee understand what shall befall thy people in the latter days: for yet the vision is for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Ezekiel 24:27; Daniel 10:9"> And when he had spoken such words unto me, I set my face toward the ground, and I became dumb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 6:7; Daniel 8:15; Jeremiah 1:9"> And, behold, one like the similitude of the sons of men touched my lips: then I opened my mouth, and spake, and said unto him that stood before me, O my lord, by the vision my sorrows are turned upon me, and I have retained no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Exodus 24:10–11; Mark 12:36"> For how can the servant of this my lord talk with this my lord? for as for me, straightway there remained no strength in me, neither is there breath left in me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="10.17 the…: or, this servant of my lord">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Luke 22:43; Daniel 8:18"> Then there came again and touched me one like the appearance of a man, and he strengthened me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Joshua 1:9; Judges 6:23"> And said, O man greatly beloved, fear not: peace be unto thee, be strong, yea, be strong. And when he had spoken unto me, I was strengthened, and said, Let my lord speak; for thou hast strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:2–4; Daniel 8:5–8"> Then said he, Knowest thou wherefore I come unto thee? and now will I return to fight with the prince of Persia: and when I am gone forth, lo, the prince of Grecia shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:13; Daniel 12:1; Revelation 12:7"> But I will shew thee that which is noted in the scripture of truth: and there is none that holdeth with me in these things, but Michael your prince.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="10.21 holdeth: Heb. strengtheneth himself">k</a></sup></span>
