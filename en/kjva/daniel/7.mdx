<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:17; Amos 3:7; Daniel 5:1"> In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon Daniel had a dream and visions of his head upon his bed: then he wrote the dream, and told the sum of the matters.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.1 had: Chaldee, saw">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.1 matters: or, words">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:1; Revelation 17:15"> Daniel spake and said, I saw in my vision by night, and, behold, the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:17; Daniel 2:32–33"> And four great beasts came up from the sea, diverse one from another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 14:13–17; Ezekiel 17:3"> The first was like a lion, and had eagle’s wings: I beheld till the wings thereof were plucked, and it was lifted up from the earth, and made stand upon the feet as a man, and a man’s heart was given to it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.4 and it: or, wherewith it">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:39; Isaiah 13:17–18; Proverbs 17:12"> And behold another beast, a second, like to a bear, and it raised up itself on one side, and it had three ribs in the mouth of it between the teeth of it: and they said thus unto it, Arise, devour much flesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.5 it raised…: or, it raised up one dominion">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Daniel 8:20–22; Daniel 8:5–8"> After this I beheld, and lo another, like a leopard, which had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl; the beast had also four heads; and dominion was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:12"> After this I saw in the night visions, and behold a fourth beast, dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly; and it had great iron teeth: it devoured and brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with the feet of it: and it was diverse from all the beasts that were before it; and it had ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; Revelation 13:1; Psalms 12:3"> I considered the horns, and, behold, there came up among them another little horn, before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots: and, behold, in this horn were eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:14; Mark 9:3; Daniel 7:13"> I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne was like the fiery flame, and his wheels as burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:27; Deuteronomy 33:2; Revelation 20:11–15"> A fiery stream issued and came forth from before him: thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him: the judgment was set, and the books were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Revelation 20:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> I beheld then because of the voice of the great words which the horn spake: I beheld even till the beast was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the burning flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 7:4–6"> As concerning the rest of the beasts, they had their dominion taken away: yet their lives were prolonged for a season and time.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.12 their lives…: Chaldee, a prolonging in life was given them">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Revelation 1:7; Luke 21:27"> I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27; John 3:35"> And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Jeremiah 17:16; Genesis 40:7–8"> I Daniel was grieved in my spirit in the midst of my body, and the visions of my head troubled me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.15 body: Chaldee, sheath">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:13–14; Zechariah 2:3; Revelation 5:5"> I came near unto one of them that stood by, and asked him the truth of all this. So he told me, and made me know the interpretation of the things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19–22; Daniel 2:37–40; John 18:36"> These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Isaiah 60:12–14; Revelation 5:10"> But the saints of the most High shall take the kingdom, and possess the kingdom for ever, even for ever and ever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="7.18 most…: Chaldee, high ones, that is, things, or, places">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 2:40–43"> Then I would know the truth of the fourth beast, which was diverse from all the others, exceeding dreadful, whose teeth were of iron, and his nails of brass; which devoured, brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with his feet;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="7.19 from…: Chaldee, from all those">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 7:23; Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:11"> And of the ten horns that were in his head, and of the other which came up, and before whom three fell; even of that horn that had eyes, and a mouth that spake very great things, whose look was more stout than his fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 17:14; Daniel 8:24"> I beheld, and the same horn made war with the saints, and prevailed against them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4; Revelation 5:10"> Until the Ancient of days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:40; Daniel 7:7; Luke 2:1"> Thus he said, The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth, which shall be diverse from all kingdoms, and shall devour the whole earth, and shall tread it down, and break it in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:20; Daniel 11:36; Revelation 13:1"> And the ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings that shall arise: and another shall rise after them; and he shall be diverse from the first, and he shall subdue three kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 20:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 11:13"> But the judgment shall sit, and they shall take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it unto the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 72:11; Luke 1:33; Revelation 11:15"> And the kingdom and dominion, and the greatness of the kingdom under the whole heaven, shall be given to the people of the saints of the most High, whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="7.27 dominions: or, rulers">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51; Daniel 10:8"> Hitherto is the end of the matter. As for me Daniel, my cogitations much troubled me, and my countenance changed in me: but I kept the matter in my heart.</span>
</div>
