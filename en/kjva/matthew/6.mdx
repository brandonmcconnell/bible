<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> <span className="woj">But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Luke 12:30; Philippians 4:6"> <span className="woj">Be not ye therefore like unto them: for your Father knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him.</span></span>

Are ye not much better than they?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; Psalms 39:5–6"> <span className="woj">Which of you by taking thought can add one cubit unto his stature?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> <span className="woj">And yet I say unto you, That even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.</span></span>
