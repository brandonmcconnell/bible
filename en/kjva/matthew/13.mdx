<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> The same day went Jesus out of the house, and sat by the sea side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> And great multitudes were gathered together unto him, so that he went into a ship, and sat; and the whole multitude stood on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> And he spake many things unto them in parables, saying,<span className="woj">Behold, a sower went forth to sow;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> <span className="woj">And when he sowed, some seeds fell by the way side, and the fowls came and devoured them up:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> <span className="woj">Some fell upon stony places, where they had not much earth: and forthwith they sprung up, because they had no deepness of earth:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> <span className="woj">And when the sun was up, they were scorched; and because they had no root, they withered away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> <span className="woj">And some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprung up, and choked them:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> <span className="woj">But other fell into good ground, and brought forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> <span className="woj">Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> And the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> He answered and said unto them,<span className="woj">Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> <span className="woj">For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> <span className="woj">Therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> <span className="woj">And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias, which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="woj">For this people’s heart is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes they have closed; lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and should understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> <span className="woj">But blessed are your eyes, for they see: and your ears, for they hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> <span className="woj">For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> <span className="woj">Hear ye therefore the parable of the sower.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> <span className="woj">When any one heareth the word of the kingdom, and understandeth it not, then cometh the wicked one, and catcheth away that which was sown in his heart. This is he which received seed by the way side.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> <span className="woj">But he that received the seed into stony places, the same is he that heareth the word, and anon with joy receiveth it;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> <span className="woj">Yet hath he not root in himself, but dureth for a while: for when tribulation or persecution ariseth because of the word, by and by he is offended.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> <span className="woj">He also that received seed among the thorns is he that heareth the word; and the care of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, choke the word, and he becometh unfruitful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> <span className="woj">But he that received seed into the good ground is he that heareth the word, and understandeth it; which also beareth fruit, and bringeth forth, some an hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Another parable put he forth unto them, saying,<span className="woj">The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a man which sowed good seed in his field:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> <span className="woj">But while men slept, his enemy came and sowed tares among the wheat, and went his way.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> <span className="woj">But when the blade was sprung up, and brought forth fruit, then appeared the tares also.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> <span className="woj">So the servants of the householder came and said unto him, Sir, didst not thou sow good seed in thy field? from whence then hath it tares?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> <span className="woj">He said unto them, An enemy hath done this. The servants said unto him, Wilt thou then that we go and gather them up?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="woj">But he said, Nay; lest while ye gather up the tares, ye root up also the wheat with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> <span className="woj">Let both grow together until the harvest: and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather ye together first the tares, and bind them in bundles to burn them: but gather the wheat into my barn.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> Another parable put he forth unto them, saying,<span className="woj">The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took, and sowed in his field:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> <span className="woj">Which indeed is the least of all seeds: but when it is grown, it is the greatest among herbs, and becometh a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches thereof.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> Another parable spake he unto them;<span className="woj">The kingdom of heaven is like unto leaven, which a woman took, and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> All these things spake Jesus unto the multitude in parables; and without a parable spake he not unto them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying, I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then Jesus sent the multitude away, and went into the house: and his disciples came unto him, saying, Declare unto us the parable of the tares of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> He answered and said unto them,<span className="woj">He that soweth the good seed is the Son of man;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> <span className="woj">The field is the world; the good seed are the children of the kingdom; but the tares are the children of the wicked one;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> <span className="woj">The enemy that sowed them is the devil; the harvest is the end of the world; and the reapers are the angels.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> <span className="woj">As therefore the tares are gathered and burned in the fire; so shall it be in the end of this world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> <span className="woj">The Son of man shall send forth his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom all things that offend, and them which do iniquity;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> <span className="woj">And shall cast them into a furnace of fire: there shall be wailing and gnashing of teeth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> <span className="woj">Then shall the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father. Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> <span className="woj">Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field; the which when a man hath found, he hideth, and for joy thereof goeth and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> <span className="woj">Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a merchant man, seeking goodly pearls:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> <span className="woj">Who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> <span className="woj">Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a net, that was cast into the sea, and gathered of every kind:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> <span className="woj">Which, when it was full, they drew to shore, and sat down, and gathered the good into vessels, but cast the bad away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> <span className="woj">So shall it be at the end of the world: the angels shall come forth, and sever the wicked from among the just,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> <span className="woj">And shall cast them into the furnace of fire: there shall be wailing and gnashing of teeth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Jesus saith unto them,<span className="woj">Have ye understood all these things?</span>They say unto him, Yea, Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> Then said he unto them,<span className="woj">Therefore every scribe which is instructed unto the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> And it came to pass, that when Jesus had finished these parables, he departed thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> And when he was come into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, insomuch that they were astonished, and said, Whence hath this man this wisdom, and these mighty works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Is not this the carpenter’s son? is not his mother called Mary? and his brethren, James, and Joses, and Simon, and Judas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And his sisters, are they not all with us? Whence then hath this man all these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> And they were offended in him. But Jesus said unto them,<span className="woj">A prophet is not without honour, save in his own country, and in his own house.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> And he did not many mighty works there because of their unbelief.</span>
</div>
