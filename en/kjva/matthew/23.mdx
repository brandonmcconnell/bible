<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> <span className="woj">All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> <span className="woj">For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> <span className="woj">But all their works they do for to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> <span className="woj">And love the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> <span className="woj">And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> <span className="woj">But be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your Master, even Christ; and all ye are brethren.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> <span className="woj">And call no man your father upon the earth: for one is your Father, which is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="woj">Neither be ye called masters: for one is your Master, even Christ.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> <span className="woj">But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> <span className="woj">And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> <span className="woj">But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye devour widows’ houses, and for a pretence make long prayer: therefore ye shall receive the greater damnation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of hell than yourselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, ye blind guides, which say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> <span className="woj">Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="woj">And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever sweareth by the gift that is upon it, he is guilty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> <span className="woj">Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="woj">Whoso therefore shall swear by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all things thereon.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> <span className="woj">And whoso shall swear by the temple, sweareth by it, and by him that dwelleth therein.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> <span className="woj">And he that shall swear by heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him that sitteth thereon.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> <span className="woj">Ye blind guides, which strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> <span className="woj">Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> <span className="woj">Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> <span className="woj">Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! because ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> <span className="woj">And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> <span className="woj">Wherefore ye be witnesses unto yourselves, that ye are the children of them which killed the prophets.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> <span className="woj">Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> <span className="woj">Ye serpents, ye generation of vipers, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> <span className="woj">Wherefore, behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes: and some of them ye shall kill and crucify; and some of them shall ye scourge in your synagogues, and persecute them from city to city:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> <span className="woj">That upon you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel unto the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> <span className="woj">Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> <span className="woj">O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, thou that killest the prophets, and stonest them which are sent unto thee, how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> <span className="woj">Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> <span className="woj">For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span></span>
