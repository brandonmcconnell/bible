<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:6–7; Matthew 3:4; Revelation 11:3"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the prophets shall be ashamed every one of his vision, when he hath prophesied; neither shall they wear a rough garment to deceive:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.4 a rough…: Heb. a garment of hair">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.4 deceive: Heb. lie">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> And it shall come to pass, that in all the land, saith the LORD, two parts therein shall be cut off and die; but the third shall be left therein.</span>
