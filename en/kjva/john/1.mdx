<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; John 17:5; Revelation 19:13"> In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The same was in the beginning with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; John 5:26"> In him was life; and the life was the light of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; John 3:28; Isaiah 40:3–5"> There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> The same came for a witness, to bear witness of the Light, that all men through him might believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> He was not that Light, but was sent to bear witness of that Light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> That was the true Light, which lighteth every man that cometh into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, and the world was made by him, and the world knew him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Isaiah 53:2–3; John 3:32"> He came unto his own, and his own received him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John bare witness of him, and cried, saying, This was he of whom I spake, He that cometh after me is preferred before me: for he was before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> And this is the record of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, Who art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> And he confessed, and denied not; but confessed, I am not the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> And they asked him, What then? Art thou Elias? And he saith, I am not. Art thou that prophet? And he answered, No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> Then said they unto him, Who art thou? that we may give an answer to them that sent us. What sayest thou of thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> He said, I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as said the prophet Esaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> And they which were sent were of the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> And they asked him, and said unto him, Why baptizest thou then, if thou be not that Christ, nor Elias, neither that prophet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John answered them, saying, I baptize with water: but there standeth one among you, whom ye know not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> He it is, who coming after me is preferred before me, whose shoe’s latchet I am not worthy to unloose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> These things were done in Bethabara beyond Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> The next day John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This is he of whom I said, After me cometh a man which is preferred before me: for he was before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> And I knew him not: but that he should be made manifest to Israel, therefore am I come baptizing with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> And John bare record, saying, I saw the Spirit descending from heaven like a dove, and it abode upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> And I knew him not: but he that sent me to baptize with water, the same said unto me, Upon whom thou shalt see the Spirit descending, and remaining on him, the same is he which baptizeth with the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 1:49; Psalms 89:26–27"> And I saw, and bare record that this is the Son of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> Again the next day after John stood, and two of his disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> And looking upon Jesus as he walked, he saith, Behold the Lamb of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> And the two disciples heard him speak, and they followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> Then Jesus turned, and saw them following, and saith unto them,<span className="woj">What seek ye?</span>They said unto him, Rabbi, (which is to say, being interpreted, Master,) where dwellest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> He saith unto them,<span className="woj">Come and see.</span>They came and saw where he dwelt, and abode with him that day: for it was about the tenth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> One of the two which heard John speak, and followed him, was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalms 89:20"> He first findeth his own brother Simon, and saith unto him, We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> And he brought him to Jesus. And when Jesus beheld him, he said,<span className="woj">Thou art Simon the son of Jona: thou shalt be called Cephas,</span>which is by interpretation, A stone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> The day following Jesus would go forth into Galilee, and findeth Philip, and saith unto him,<span className="woj">Follow me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> Now Philip was of Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip findeth Nathanael, and saith unto him, We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> And Nathanael said unto him, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth? Philip saith unto him, Come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and saith of him,<span className="woj">Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> Nathanael saith unto him, Whence knowest thou me? Jesus answered and said unto him,<span className="woj">Before that Philip called thee, when thou wast under the fig tree, I saw thee.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael answered and saith unto him, Rabbi, thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus answered and said unto him,<span className="woj">Because I said unto thee, I saw thee under the fig tree, believest thou? thou shalt see greater things than these.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> And he saith unto him,<span className="woj">Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man.</span></span>
</div>
