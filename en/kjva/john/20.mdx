<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Peter therefore went forth, and that other disciple, and came to the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> So they ran both together: and the other disciple did outrun Peter, and came first to the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> And he stooping down, and looking in, saw the linen clothes lying; yet went he not in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Then cometh Simon Peter following him, and went into the sepulchre, and seeth the linen clothes lie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> And the napkin, that was about his head, not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then went in also that other disciple, which came first to the sepulchre, and he saw, and believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For as yet they knew not the scripture, that he must rise again from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> Then the disciples went away again unto their own home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary stood without at the sepulchre weeping: and as she wept, she stooped down, and looked into the sepulchre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> And seeth two angels in white sitting, the one at the head, and the other at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And they say unto her, Woman, why weepest thou? She saith unto them, Because they have taken away my Lord, and I know not where they have laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> And when she had thus said, she turned herself back, and saw Jesus standing, and knew not that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus saith unto her,<span className="woj">Woman, why weepest thou? whom seekest thou?</span>She, supposing him to be the gardener, saith unto him, Sir, if thou have borne him hence, tell me where thou hast laid him, and I will take him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus saith unto her,<span className="woj">Mary.</span>She turned herself, and saith unto him, Rabboni; which is to say, Master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus saith unto her,<span className="woj">Touch me not; for I am not yet ascended to my Father: but go to my brethren, and say unto them, I ascend unto my Father, and your Father; and to my God, and your God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that he had spoken these things unto her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> Then the same day at evening, being the first day of the week, when the doors were shut where the disciples were assembled for fear of the Jews, came Jesus and stood in the midst, and saith unto them,<span className="woj">Peace be unto you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> And when he had so said, he shewed unto them his hands and his side. Then were the disciples glad, when they saw the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Then said Jesus to them again,<span className="woj">Peace be unto you: as my Father hath sent me, even so send I you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> And when he had said this, he breathed on them, and saith unto them,<span className="woj">Receive ye the Holy Ghost:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> <span className="woj">Whose soever sins ye remit, they are remitted unto them; and whose soever sins ye retain, they are retained.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> But Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples therefore said unto him, We have seen the Lord. But he said unto them, Except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the print of the nails, and thrust my hand into his side, I will not believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> And after eight days again his disciples were within, and Thomas with them: then came Jesus, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst, and said,<span className="woj">Peace be unto you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then saith he to Thomas,<span className="woj">Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side: and be not faithless, but believing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> And Thomas answered and said unto him, My Lord and my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus saith unto him,<span className="woj">Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed.</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name.</span>
