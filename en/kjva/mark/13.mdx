<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> <span className="woj">For in those days shall be affliction, such as was not from the beginning of the creation which God created unto this time, neither shall be.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> <span className="woj">But take ye heed: behold, I have foretold you all things.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> <span className="woj">And then shall they see the Son of man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> <span className="woj">So ye in like manner, when ye shall see these things come to pass, know that it is nigh, even at the doors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> <span className="woj">Verily I say unto you, that this generation shall not pass, till all these things be done.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> <span className="woj">Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> <span className="woj">Take ye heed, watch and pray: for ye know not when the time is.</span></span>
