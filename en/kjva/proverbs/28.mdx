<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> For the transgression of a land many are the princes thereof: but by a man of understanding and knowledge the state thereof shall be prolonged.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="28.2 by…: or, by men of understanding and wisdom shall they likewise be prolonged">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> Whoso keepeth the law is a wise son: but he that is a companion of riotous men shameth his father.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="28.7 is a companion…: or, feedeth gluttons">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> Whoso causeth the righteous to go astray in an evil way, he shall fall himself into his own pit: but the upright shall have good things in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> The rich man is wise in his own conceit; but the poor that hath understanding searcheth him out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.11 own…: Heb. eyes">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When righteous men do rejoice, there is great glory: but when the wicked rise, a man is hidden.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="28.12 hidden: or, sought for">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Happy is the man that feareth alway: but he that hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> As a roaring lion, and a ranging bear; so is a wicked ruler over the poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> The prince that wanteth understanding is also a great oppressor: but he that hateth covetousness shall prolong his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man that doeth violence to the blood of any person shall flee to the pit; let no man stay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> Whoso walketh uprightly shall be saved: but he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He that tilleth his land shall have plenty of bread: but he that followeth after vain persons shall have poverty enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A faithful man shall abound with blessings: but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="28.20 innocent: or, unpunished">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> To have respect of persons is not good: for for a piece of bread that man will transgress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> He that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye, and considereth not that poverty shall come upon him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="28.22 hasteth…: or, hath and evil eye hasteth to be rich">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He that rebuketh a man afterwards shall find more favour than he that flattereth with the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Whoso robbeth his father or his mother, and saith, It is no transgression; the same is the companion of a destroyer.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="28.24 a destroyer: Heb. a man destroying">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He that is of a proud heart stirreth up strife: but he that putteth his trust in the LORD shall be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> He that trusteth in his own heart is a fool: but whoso walketh wisely, he shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack: but he that hideth his eyes shall have many a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When the wicked rise, men hide themselves: but when they perish, the righteous increase.</span>
