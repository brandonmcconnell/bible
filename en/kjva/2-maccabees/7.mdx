<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> It came to pass also, that seven brethren with their mother were taken, and compelled by the king against the law to taste swine’s flesh, and were tormented with scourges and whips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But one of them that spake first said thus, What wouldest thou ask or learn of us? we are ready to die, rather than to transgress the laws of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then the king, being in a rage, commanded pans and caldrons to be made hot:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Which forthwith being heated, he commanded to cut out the tongue of him that spake first, and to cut off the utmost parts of his body, the rest of his brethren and his mother looking on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now when he was thus maimed in all his members, he commanded him being yet alive to be brought to the fire, and to be fried in the pan: and as the vapour of the pan was for a good space dispersed, they exhorted one another with the mother to die manfully, saying thus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The Lord God looketh upon us, and in truth hath comfort in us, as Moses in his song, which witnessed to their faces, declared, saying, And he shall be comforted in his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So when the first was dead after this manner, they brought the second to make him a mocking stock: and when they had pulled off the skin of his head with the hair, they asked him, Wilt thou eat, before thou be punished throughout every member of thy body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But he answered in his own language, and said, No. Wherefore he also received the next torment in order, as the former did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when he was at the last gasp, he said, Thou like a fury takest us out of this present life, but the King of the world shall raise us up, who have died for his laws, unto everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After him was the third made a mocking stock: and when he was required, he put out his tongue, and that right soon, holding forth his hands manfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And said courageously, These I had from heaven; and for his laws I despise them; and from him I hope to receive them again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Insomuch that the king, and they that were with him, marvelled at the young man’s courage, for that he nothing regarded the pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now when this man was dead also, they tormented and mangled the fourth in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So when he was ready to die he said thus, It is good, being put to death by men, to look for hope from God to be raised up again by him: as for thee, thou shalt have no resurrection to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Afterward they brought the fifth also, and mangled him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then looked he unto the king, and said, Thou hast power over men, thou art corruptible, thou doest what thou wilt; yet think not that our nation is forsaken of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But abide a while, and behold his great power, how he will torment thee and thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After him also they brought the sixth, who being ready to die said, Be not deceived without cause: for we suffer these things for ourselves, having sinned against our God: therefore marvellous things are done unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But think not thou, that takest in hand to strive against God, that thou shalt escape unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But the mother was marvellous above all, and worthy of honourable memory: for when she saw her seven sons slain within the space of one day, she bare it with a good courage, because of the hope that she had in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yea, she exhorted every one of them in her own language, filled with courageous spirits; and stirring up her womanish thoughts with a manly stomach, she said unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> I cannot tell how ye came into my womb: for I neither gave you breath nor life, neither was it I that formed the members of every one of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But doubtless the Creator of the world, who formed the generation of man, and found out the beginning of all things, will also of his own mercy give you breath and life again, as ye now regard not your own selves for his laws’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now Antiochus, thinking himself despised, and suspecting it to be a reproachful speech, whilst the youngest was yet alive, did not only exhort him by words, but also assured him with oaths, that he would make him both a rich and a happy man, if he would turn from the laws of his fathers; and that also he would take him for his friend, and trust him with affairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when the young man would in no case hearken unto him, the king called his mother, and exhorted her that she would counsel the young man to save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And when he had exhorted her with many words, she promised him that she would counsel her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But she bowing herself toward him, laughing the cruel tyrant to scorn, spake in her country language on this manner; O my son, have pity upon me that bare thee nine months in my womb, and gave thee suck three years, and nourished thee, and brought thee up unto this age, and endured the troubles of education.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> I beseech thee, my son, look upon the heaven and the earth, and all that is therein, and consider that God made them of things that were not; and so was mankind made likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Fear not this tormentor, but, being worthy of thy brethren, take thy death that I may receive thee again in mercy with thy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Whiles she was yet speaking these words, the young man said, Whom wait ye for? I will not obey the king’s commandment: but I will obey the commandment of the law that was given unto our fathers by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And thou, that hast been the author of all mischief against the Hebrews, shalt not escape the hands of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For we suffer because of our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And though the living Lord be angry with us a little while for our chastening and correction, yet shall he be at one again with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But thou, O godless man, and of all other most wicked, be not lifted up without a cause, nor puffed up with uncertain hopes, lifting up thy hand against the servants of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For thou hast not yet escaped the judgment of Almighty God, who seeth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For our brethren, who now have suffered a short pain, are dead under God’s covenant of everlasting life: but thou, through the judgment of God, shalt receive just punishment for thy pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But I, as my brethren, offer up my body and life for the laws of our fathers, beseeching God that he would speedily be merciful unto our nation; and that thou by torments and plagues mayest confess, that he alone is God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And that in me and my brethren the wrath of the Almighty, which is justly brought upon our nation, may cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then the king being in a rage, handed him worse than all the rest, and took it grievously that he was mocked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So this man died undefiled, and put his whole trust in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Last of all after the sons the mother died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Let this be enough now to have spoken concerning the idolatrous feasts, and the extreme tortures.</span>
