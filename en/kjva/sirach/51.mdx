<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> I will thank thee, O Lord and King, and praise thee, O God my Saviour: I do give praise unto thy name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For thou art my defender and helper, and has preserved my body from destruction, and from the snare of the slanderous tongue, and from the lips that forge lies, and has been mine helper against mine adversaries:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And hast delivered me, according to the multitude of thy mercies and greatness of thy name, from the teeth of them that were ready to devour me, and out of the hands of such as sought after my life, and from the manifold afflictions which I had;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> From the choking of fire on every side, and from the midst of the fire which I kindled not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> From the depth of the belly of hell, from an unclean tongue, and from lying words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> By an accusation to the king from an unrighteous tongue my soul drew near even unto death, my life was near to the hell beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> They compassed me on every side, and there was no man to help me: I looked for the succour of men, but there was none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then thought I upon thy mercy, O Lord, and upon thy acts of old, how thou deliverest such as wait for thee, and savest them out of the hands of the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then lifted I up my supplications from the earth, and prayed for deliverance from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> I called upon the Lord, the Father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the days of my trouble, and in the time of the proud, when there was no help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I will praise thy name continually, and will sing praises with thanksgiving; and so my prayer was heard:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For thou savedst me from destruction, and deliveredst me from the evil time: therefore will I give thanks, and praise thee, and bless they name, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When I was yet young, or ever I went abroad, I desired wisdom openly in my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I prayed for her before the temple, and will seek her out even to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Even from the flower till the grape was ripe hath my heart delighted in her: my foot went the right way, from my youth up sought I after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I bowed down mine ear a little, and received her, and gat much learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> I profited therein, therefore will I ascribe glory unto him that giveth me wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For I purposed to do after her, and earnestly I followed that which is good; so shall I not be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> My soul hath wrestled with her, and in my doings I was exact: I stretched forth my hands to the heaven above, and bewailed my ignorances of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I directed my soul unto her, and I found her in pureness: I have had my heart joined with her from the beginning, therefore shall I not be foresaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> My heart was troubled in seeking her: therefore have I gotten a good possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The Lord hath given me a tongue for my reward, and I will praise him therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Draw near unto me, ye unlearned, and dwell in the house of learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Wherefore are ye slow, and what say ye to these things, seeing your souls are very thirsty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> I opened my mouth, and said, Buy her for yourselves without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Put your neck under the yoke, and let your soul receive instruction: she is hard at hand to find.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Behold with your eyes, how that I have but little labour, and have gotten unto me much rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Get learning with a great sum of money, and get much gold by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Let your soul rejoice in his mercy, and be not ashamed of his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Work your work betimes, and in his time he will give you your reward.</span>
