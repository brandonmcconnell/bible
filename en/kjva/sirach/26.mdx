<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Blessed is the man that hath a virtuous wife, for the number of his days shall be double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband, and he shall fulfil the years of his life in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A good wife is a good portion, which shall be given in the portion of them that fear the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Whether a man be rich or poor, if he have a good heart toward the Lord, he shall at all times rejoice with a cheerful countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There be three things that mine heart feareth; and for the fourth I was sore afraid: the slander of a city, the gathering together of an unruly multitude, and a false accusation: all these are worse than death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But a grief of heart and sorrow is a woman that is jealous over another woman, and a scourge of the tongue which communicateth with all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> An evil wife is a yoke shaken to and fro: he that hath hold of her is as though he held a scorpion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A drunken woman and a gadder abroad causeth great anger, and she will not cover her own shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The whoredom of a woman may be known in her haughty looks and eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Watch over an impudent eye: and marvel not if she trespass against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> She will open her mouth, as a thirsty traveller when he hath found a fountain, and drink of every water near her: by every hedge will she sit down, and open her quiver against every arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The grace of a wife delighteth her husband, and her discretion will fatten his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> A silent and loving woman is a gift of the Lord; and there is nothing so much worth as a mind well instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> A shamefaced and faithful woman is a double grace, and her continent mind cannot be valued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> As the sun when it ariseth in the high heaven; so is the beauty of a good wife in the ordering of her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> As the clear light is upon the holy candlestick; so is the beauty of the face in ripe age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> As the golden pillars are upon the sockets of silver; so are the fair feet with a constant heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> My son, keep the flower of thine age sound; and give not thy strength to strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When thou hast gotten a fruitful possession through all the field, sow it with thine own seed, trusting in the goodness of thy stock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So thy race which thou leavest shall be magnified, having the confidence of their good descent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> An harlot shall be accounted as spittle; but a married woman is a tower against death to her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A wicked woman is given as a portion to a wicked man: but a godly woman is given to him that feareth the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> A dishonest woman contemneth shame: but an honest woman will reverence her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> A shameless woman shall be counted as a dog; but she that is shamefaced will fear the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A woman that honoureth her husband shall be judged wise of all; but she that dishonoureth him in her pride shall be counted ungodly of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A loud crying woman and a scold shall be sought out to drive away the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> There be two things that grieve my heart; and the third maketh me angry: a man of war that suffereth poverty; and men of understanding that are not set by; and one that returneth from righteousness to sin; the Lord prepareth such an one for the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A merchant shall hardly keep himself from doing wrong; and an huckster shall not be freed from sin.</span>
