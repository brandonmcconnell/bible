<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> O how comely is the wisdom of old men, and understanding and counsel to men of honour.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Much experience is the crown of old men, and the fear of God is their glory.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> There be nine things which I have judged in mine heart to be happy, and the tenth I will utter with my tongue: A man that hath joy of his children; and he that liveth to see the fall of his enemy:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
