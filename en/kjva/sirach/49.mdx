<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The remembrance of Josias is like the composition of the perfume that is made by the art of the apothecary: it is sweet as honey in all mouths, and as musick at a banquet of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He behaved himself uprightly in the conversion of the people, and took away the abominations of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He directed his heart unto the Lord, and in the time of the ungodly he established the worship of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> All, except David and Ezekias and Josias, were defective: for they forsook the law of the most High, even the kings of Juda failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Therefore he gave their power unto others, and their glory to a strange nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They burnt the chosen city of the sanctuary, and made the streets desolate, according to the prophecy of Jeremias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For they entreated him evil, who nevertheless was a prophet, sanctified in his mother’s womb, that he might root out, and afflict, and destroy; and that he might build up also, and plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> It was Ezekiel who saw the glorious vision, which was shewed him upon the chariot of the cherubims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For he made mention of the enemies under the figure of the rain, and directed them that went right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And of the twelve prophets let the memorial be blessed, and let their bones flourish again out of their place: for they comforted Jacob, and delivered them by assured hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> How shall we magnify Zorobabel? even he was as a signet on the right hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So was Jesus the son of Josedec: who in their time builded the house, and set up an holy temple to the Lord, which was prepared for everlasting glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And among the elect was Neemias, whose renown is great, who raised up for us the walls that were fallen, and set up the gates and the bars, and raised up our ruins again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But upon the earth was no man created like Enoch; for he was taken from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Neither was there a young man born like Joseph, a governor of his brethren, a stay of the people, whose bones were regarded of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Sem and Seth were in great honour among men, and so was Adam above every living thing in creation.</span>
