<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The bee is little among such as fly; but her fruit is the chief of sweet things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Boast not of thy clothing and raiment, and exalt not thyself in the day of honour: for the works of the Lord are wonderful, and his works among men are hidden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Many kings have sat down upon the ground; and one that was never thought of hath worn the crown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many mighty men have been greatly disgraced; and the honourable delivered into other men’s hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Blame not before thou hast examined the truth: understand first, and then rebuke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Answer not before thou hast heard the cause: neither interrupt men in the midst of their talk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Strive not in a matter that concerneth thee not; and sit not in judgment with sinners.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> My son, meddle not with many matters: for if thou meddle much, thou shalt not be innocent; and if thou follow after, thou shalt not obtain, neither shalt thou escape by fleeing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> There is one that laboureth, and taketh pains, and maketh haste, and is so much the more behind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Again, there is another that is slow, and hath need of help, wanting ability, and full of poverty; yet the eye of the Lord looked upon him for good, and set him up from his low estate,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And lifted up his head from misery; so that many that saw it marvelled at him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Prosperity and adversity, life and death, poverty and riches, come of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Wisdom, knowledge, and understanding of the law, are of the Lord: love, and the way of good works, are from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Error and darkness had their beginning together with sinners: and evil shall wax old with them that glory therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The gift of the Lord remaineth with the ungodly, and his favour bringeth prosperity for ever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> There is that waxeth rich by his wariness and pinching, and this is the portion of his reward:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Whereas he saith, I have found rest, and now will eat continually of my goods; and yet he knoweth not what time shall come upon him, and that he must leave those things to others, and die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Be stedfast in thy covenant, and be conversant therein, and wax old in thy work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Marvel not at the works of sinners; but trust in the Lord, and abide in thy labour: for it is an easy thing in the sight of the Lord on the sudden to make a poor man rich.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The blessing of the Lord is in the reward of the godly, and suddenly he maketh his blessing flourish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Say not, What profit is there of my service? and what good things shall I have hereafter?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Again, say not, I have enough, and possess many things, and what evil shall I have hereafter?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In the day of prosperity there is a forgetfulness of affliction: and in the day of affliction there is no more remembrance of prosperity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For it is an easy thing unto the Lord in the day of death to reward a man according to his ways.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Bring not every man into thine house: for the deceitful man hath many trains.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Like as a partridge taken and kept in a cage, so is the heart of the proud; and like as a spy, watcheth he for thy fall:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For he lieth in wait, and turneth good into evil, and in things worthy praise will lay blame upon thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Take heed of a mischievous man, for he worketh wickedness; lest he bring upon thee a perpetual blot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Receive a stranger into thine house, and he will disturb thee, and turn thee out of thine own.</span>
</div>
