<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Every friend saith, I am his friend also: but there is a friend, which is only a friend in name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Is it not a grief unto death, when a companion and friend is turned to an enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> O wicked imagination, whence camest thou in to cover the earth with deceit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> There is a companion, which rejoiceth in the prosperity of a friend, but in the time of trouble will be against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There is a companion, which helpeth his friend for the belly, and taketh up the buckler against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Forget not thy friend in thy mind, and be not unmindful of him in thy riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Every counsellor extolleth counsel; but there is some that counselleth for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Beware of a counsellor, and know before what need he hath; for he will counsel for himself; lest he cast the lot upon thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And say unto thee, Thy way is good: and afterward he stand on the other side, to see what shall befall thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Consult not with one that suspecteth thee: and hide thy counsel from such as envy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Neither consult with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous; neither with a coward in matters of war; nor with a merchant concerning exchange; nor with a buyer of selling; nor with an envious man of thankfulness; nor with an unmerciful man touching kindness; nor with the slothful for any work; nor with an hireling for a year of finishing work; nor with an idle servant of much business: hearken not unto these in any matter of counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But be continually with a godly man, whom thou knowest to keep the commandments of the Lord, whose, mind is according to thy mind, and will sorrow with thee, if thou shalt miscarry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And let the counsel of thine own heart stand: for there is no man more faithful unto thee than it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For a man’s mind is sometime wont to tell him more than seven watchmen, that sit above in an high tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And above all this pray to the most High, that he will direct thy way in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Let reason go before every enterprize, and counsel before every action.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The countenance is a sign of changing of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Four manner of things appear: good and evil, life and death: but the tongue ruleth over them continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> There is one that is wise and teacheth many, and yet is unprofitable to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> There is one that sheweth wisdom in words, and is hated: he shall be destitute of all food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For grace is not given, him from the Lord, because he is deprived of all wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Another is wise to himself; and the fruits of understanding are commendable in his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A wise man instructeth his people; and the fruits of his understanding fail not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> A wise man shall be filled with blessing; and all they that see him shall count him happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The days of the life of man may be numbered: but the days of Israel are innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A wise man shall inherit glory among his people, and his name shall be perpetual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> My son, prove thy soul in thy life, and see what is evil for it, and give not that unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For all things are not profitable for all men, neither hath every soul pleasure in every thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be not unsatiable in any dainty thing, nor too greedy upon meats:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For excess of meats bringeth sickness, and surfeiting will turn into choler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> By surfeiting have many perished; but he that taketh heed prolongeth his life.</span>
