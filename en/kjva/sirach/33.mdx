<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> There shall no evil happen unto him that feareth the Lord; but in temptation even again he will deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A wise man hateth not the law; but he that is an hypocrite therein is as a ship in a storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A man of understanding trusteth in the law; and the law is faithful unto him, as an oracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Prepare what to say, and so thou shalt be heard: and bind up instruction, and then make answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The heart of the foolish is like a cartwheel; and his thoughts are like a rolling axletree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A stallion horse is as a mocking friend, he neigheth under every one that sitteth upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Why doth one day excel another, when as all the light of every day in the year is of the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> By the knowledge of the Lord they were distinguished: and he altered seasons and feasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some of them hath he made high days, and hallowed them, and some of them hath he made ordinary days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And all men are from the ground, and Adam was created of earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In much knowledge the Lord hath divided them, and made their ways diverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Some of them hath he blessed and exalted and some of them he sanctified, and set near himself: but some of them hath he cursed and brought low, and turned out of their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As the clay is in the potter’s hand, to fashion it at his pleasure: so man is in the hand of him that made him, to render to them as liketh him best.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Good is set against evil, and life against death: so is the godly against the sinner, and the sinner against the godly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So look upon all the works of the most High; and there are two and two, one against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I awaked up last of all, as one that gathereth after the grapegatherers: by the blessing of the Lord I profited, and tred my winepress like a gatherer of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Hear me, O ye great men of the people, and hearken with your ears, ye rulers of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Give not thy son and wife, thy brother and friend, power over thee while thou livest, and give not thy goods to another: lest it repent thee, and thou intreat for the same again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> As long as thou livest and hast breath in thee, give not thyself over to any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For better it is that thy children should seek to thee, than that thou shouldest stand to their courtesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In all thy works keep to thyself the preeminence; leave not a stain in thine honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> At the time when thou shalt end thy days, and finish thy life, distribute thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Fodder, a wand, and burdens, are for the ass; and bread, correction, and work, for a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> If thou set thy servant to labour, thou shalt find rest: but if thou let him go idle, he shall seek liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A yoke and a collar do bow the neck: so are tortures and torments for an evil servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Send him to labour, that he be not idle; for idleness teacheth much evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Set him to work, as is fit for him: if he be not obedient, put on more heavy fetters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But be not excessive toward any; and without discretion do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> If thou have a servant, let him be unto thee as thyself, because thou hast bought him with a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If thou have a servant, entreat him as a brother: for thou hast need of him, as of thine own soul: if thou entreat him evil, and he run from thee, which way wilt thou go to seek him?</span>
