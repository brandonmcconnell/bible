<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> If thou be made the master of a feast, lift not thyself up, but be among them as one of the rest; take diligent care for them, and so sit down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And when thou hast done all thy office, take thy place, that thou mayest be merry with them, and receive a crown for thy well ordering of the feast.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Speak, thou that art the elder, for it becometh thee, but with sound judgment; and hinder not musick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Pour not out words where there is a musician, and shew not forth wisdom out of time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> A concert of musick in a banquet of wine is as a signet of carbuncle set in gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As a signet of an emerald set in a work of gold, so is the melody of musick with pleasant wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Speak, young man, if there be need of thee: and yet scarcely when thou art twice asked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Let thy speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one that knoweth and yet holdeth his tongue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Before the thunder goeth lightning; and before a shamefaced man shall go favour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Rise up betimes, and be not the last; but get thee home without delay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There take thy pastime, and do what thou wilt: but sin not by proud speech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for these things bless him that made thee, and hath replenished thee with his good things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He that seeketh the law shall be filled therewith: but the hypocrite will be offended thereat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man of counsel will be considerate; but a strange and proud man is not daunted with fear, even when of himself he hath done without counsel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Do nothing without advice; and when thou hast once done, repent not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Go not in a way wherein thou mayest fall, and stumble not among the stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Be not confident in a plain way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And beware of thine own children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In every good work trust thy own soul; for this is the keeping of the commandments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse.</span>
