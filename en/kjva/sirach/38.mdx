<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Honour a physician with the honour due unto him for the uses which ye may have of him: for the Lord hath created him.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For of the most High cometh healing, and he shall receive honour of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The skill of the physician shall lift up his head: and in the sight of great men he shall be in admiration.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The Lord hath created medicines out of the earth; and he that is wise will not abhor them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Was not the water made sweet with wood, that the virtue thereof might be known?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he hath given men skill, that he might be honoured in his marvellous works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> With such doth he heal men, and taketh away their pains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of such doth the apothecary make a confection; and of his works there is no end; and from him is peace over all the earth,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> My son, in thy sickness be not negligent: but pray unto the Lord, and he will make thee whole.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Leave off from sin, and order thine hands aright, and cleanse thy heart from all wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Give a sweet savour, and a memorial of fine flour; and make a fat offering, as not being.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then give place to the physician, for the Lord hath created him: let him not go from thee, for thou hast need of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> There is a time when in their hands there is good success.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For they shall also pray unto the Lord, that he would prosper that, which they give for ease and remedy to prolong life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He that sinneth before his Maker, let him fall into the hand of the physician.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> My son, let tears fall down over the dead, and begin to lament, as if thou hadst suffered great harm thyself; and then cover his body according to the custom, and neglect not his burial.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Weep bitterly, and make great moan, and use lamentation, as he is worthy, and that a day or two, lest thou be evil spoken of: and then comfort thyself for thy heaviness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For of heaviness cometh death, and the heaviness of the heart breaketh strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In affliction also sorrow remaineth: and the life of the poor is the curse of the heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Take no heaviness to heart: drive it away, and remember the last end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Forget it not, for there is no turning again: thou shalt not do him good, but hurt thyself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Remember my judgment: for thine also shall be so; yesterday for me, and to day for thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When the dead is at rest, let his remembrance rest; and be comforted for him, when his Spirit is departed from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The wisdom of a learned man cometh by opportunity of leisure: and he that hath little business shall become wise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough, and that glorieth in the goad, that driveth oxen, and is occupied in their labours, and whose talk is of bullocks?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He giveth his mind to make furrows; and is diligent to give the kine fodder.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So every carpenter and workmaster, that laboureth night and day: and they that cut and grave seals, and are diligent to make great variety, and give themselves to counterfeit imagery, and watch to finish a work:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The smith also sitting by the anvil, and considering the iron work, the vapour of the fire wasteth his flesh, and he fighteth with the heat of the furnace: the noise of the hammer and the anvil is ever in his ears, and his eyes look still upon the pattern of the thing that he maketh; he setteth his mind to finish his work, and watcheth to polish it perfectly:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So doth the potter sitting at his work, and turning the wheel about with his feet, who is alway carefully set at his work, and maketh all his work by number;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He fashioneth the clay with his arm, and boweth down his strength before his feet; he applieth himself to lead it over; and he is diligent to make clean the furnace:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> All these trust to their hands: and every one is wise in his work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Without these cannot a city be inhabited: and they shall not dwell where they will, nor go up and down:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> They shall not be sought for in publick counsel, nor sit high in the congregation: they shall not sit on the judges’ seat, nor understand the sentence of judgment: they cannot declare justice and judgment; and they shall not be found where parables are spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But they will maintain the state of the world, and all their desire is in the work of their craft.</span>
</div>
