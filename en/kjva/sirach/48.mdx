<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then stood up Elias the prophet as fire, and his word burned like a lamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He brought a sore famine upon them, and by his zeal he diminished their number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> By the word of the Lord he shut up the heaven, and also three times brought down fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Elias, how wast thou honoured in thy wondrous deeds! and who may glory like unto thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Who didst raise up a dead man from death, and his soul from the place of the dead, by the word of the most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Who broughtest kings to destruction, and honorable men from their bed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Who heardest the rebuke of the Lord in Sinai, and in Horeb the judgment of vengeance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Who annointedst kings to take revenge, and prophets to succeed after him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Who was taken up in a whirlwind of fire, and in a chariot of fiery horses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who wast ordained for reproofs in their times, to pacify the wrath of the Lord’s judgment, before it brake forth into fury, and to turn the heart of the father unto the son, and to restore the tribes of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Blessed are they that saw thee, and slept in love; for we shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Elias it was, who was covered with a whirlwind: and Eliseus was filled with his spirit: whilst he lived, he was not moved with the presence of any prince, neither could any bring him into subjection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> No word could overcome him; and after his death his body prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He did wonders in his life, and at his death were his works marvellous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For all this the people repented not, neither departed they from their sins, till they were spoiled and carried out of their land, and were scattered through all the earth: yet there remained a small people, and a ruler in the house of David:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Of whom some did that which was pleasing to God, and some multiplied sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Ezekias fortified his city, and brought in water into the midst thereof: he digged the hard rock with iron, and made wells for waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> In his time Sennacherib came up, and sent Rabsaces, and lifted up his hand against Sion, and boasted proudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then trembled their hearts and hands, and they were in pain, as women in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But they called upon the Lord which is merciful, and stretched out their hands toward him: and immediately the Holy One heard them out of heaven, and delivered them by the ministry of Esay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He smote the host of the Assyrians, and his angel destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For Ezekias had done the thing that pleased the Lord, and was strong in the ways of David his father, as Esay the prophet, who was great and faithful in his vision, had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In his time the sun went backward, and he lengthened the king’s life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He saw by an excellent spirit what should come to pass at the last, and he comforted them that mourned in Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He shewed what should come to pass for ever, and secret things or ever they came.</span>
