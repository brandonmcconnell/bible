<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; James 2:9; Leviticus 19:15"> My brethren, have not the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Lord of glory, with respect of persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Esther 3:10; Esther 8:2"> For if there come unto your assembly a man with a gold ring, in goodly apparel, and there come in also a poor man in vile raiment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:6; Luke 7:44–46"> And ye have respect to him that weareth the gay clothing, and say unto him, Sit thou here in a good place; and say to the poor, Stand thou there, or sit here under my footstool:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:24; Job 34:19; Matthew 7:1–5"> Are ye not then partial in yourselves, and are become judges of evil thoughts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20; Revelation 2:9"> Hearken, my beloved brethren, Hath not God chosen the poor of this world rich in faith, and heirs of the kingdom which he hath promised to them that love him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 17:5; 1 Corinthians 11:22; Proverbs 14:31"> But ye have despised the poor. Do not rich men oppress you, and draw you before the judgment seats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Revelation 13:5–6; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Do not they blaspheme that worthy name by the which ye are called?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> If ye fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, ye do well:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; James 2:1–4; 1 John 3:4"> But if ye have respect to persons, ye commit sin, and are convinced of the law as transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Matthew 5:18–19; Deuteronomy 27:26"> For whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:13–14; Matthew 5:21–28; Deuteronomy 5:17–18"> For he that said, Do not commit adultery, said also, Do not kill. Now if thou commit no adultery, yet if thou kill, thou art become a transgressor of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:25; James 2:8; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> So speak ye, and so do, as they that shall be judged by the law of liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 6:15; Proverbs 21:13"> For he shall have judgment without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy; and mercy rejoiceth against judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What doth it profit, my brethren, though a man say he hath faith, and have not works? can faith save him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> And one of you say unto them, Depart in peace, be ye warmed and filled; notwithstanding ye give them not those things which are needful to the body; what doth it profit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> Yea, a man may say, Thou hast faith, and I have works: shew me thy faith without thy works, and I will shew thee my faith by my works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> Thou believest that there is one God; thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not Abraham our father justified by works, when he had offered Isaac his son upon the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> Seest thou how faith wrought with his works, and by works was faith made perfect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> And the scripture was fulfilled which saith, Abraham believed God, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness: and he was called the Friend of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalms 60:12"> Ye see then how that by works a man is justified, and not by faith only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> Likewise also was not Rahab the harlot justified by works, when she had received the messengers, and had sent them out another way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.</span>
