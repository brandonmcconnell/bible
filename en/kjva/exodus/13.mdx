<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; Exodus 34:18; Leviticus 23:8"> Seven days thou shalt eat unleavened bread, and in the seventh day shall be a feast to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:19; Matthew 16:6"> Unleavened bread shall be eaten seven days; and there shall no leavened bread be seen with thee, neither shall there be leaven seen with thee in all thy quarters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:8; Deuteronomy 11:18–19; Numbers 15:39"> And it shall be for a sign unto thee upon thine hand, and for a memorial between thine eyes, that the LORD’s law may be in thy mouth: for with a strong hand hath the LORD brought thee out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Exodus 12:24–25; Exodus 23:15"> Thou shalt therefore keep this ordinance in his season from year to year.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 18:15; Leviticus 27:26; Exodus 34:19"> That thou shalt set apart unto the LORD all that openeth the matrix, and every firstling that cometh of a beast which thou hast; the males shall be the LORD’s.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.12 set…: Heb. cause to pass over">b</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 13:3; Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4"> And it shall be when thy son asketh thee in time to come, saying, What is this? that thou shalt say unto him, By strength of hand the LORD brought us out from Egypt, from the house of bondage:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.14 in…: Heb. to morrow">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 13:9; Deuteronomy 11:18; Deuteronomy 6:7–9"> And it shall be for a token upon thine hand, and for frontlets between thine eyes: for by strength of hand the LORD brought us forth out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; Numbers 14:1–4"> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh had let the people go, that God led them not through the way of the land of the Philistines, although that was near; for God said, Lest peradventure the people repent when they see war, and they return to Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:10; Numbers 33:6–8"> But God led the people about, through the way of the wilderness of the Red sea: and the children of Israel went up harnessed out of the land of Egypt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.18 harnessed: or, by five in a rank">e</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; Deuteronomy 1:33"> And the LORD went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them the way; and by night in a pillar of fire, to give them light; to go by day and night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 121:5–8; Revelation 10:1"> He took not away the pillar of the cloud by day, nor the pillar of fire by night, from before the people.</span>
