<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he take him another wife; her food, her raiment, and her duty of marriage, shall he not diminish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And if he do not these three unto her, then shall she go out free without money.</span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> If men strive, and hurt a woman with child, so that her fruit depart from her, and yet no mischief follow: he shall be surely punished, according as the woman’s husband will lay upon him; and he shall pay as the judges determine.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if he smite out his manservant’s tooth, or his maidservant’s tooth; he shall let him go free for his tooth’s sake.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the ox were wont to push with his horn in time past, and it hath been testified to his owner, and he hath not kept him in, but that he hath killed a man or a woman; the ox shall be stoned, and his owner also shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If there be laid on him a sum of money, then he shall give for the ransom of his life whatsoever is laid upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Whether he have gored a son, or have gored a daughter, according to this judgment shall it be done unto him.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> The owner of the pit shall make it good, and give money unto the owner of them; and the dead beast shall be his.</span>
