<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 2:21–22"> And it was found written, that Mordecai had told of Bigthana and Teresh, two of the king’s chamberlains, the keepers of the door, who sought to lay hand on the king Ahasuerus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.2 Bigthana: also called, Bigthan">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.2 door: Heb. threshold">c</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:14; Esther 4:11; Esther 5:1"> And the king said, Who is in the court? Now Haman was come into the outward court of the king’s house, to speak unto the king to hang Mordecai on the gallows that he had prepared for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the king’s servants said unto him, Behold, Haman standeth in the court. And the king said, Let him come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Obadiah 1:3; Isaiah 62:4–5"> So Haman came in. And the king said unto him, What shall be done unto the man whom the king delighteth to honour? Now Haman thought in his heart, To whom would the king delight to do honour more than to myself?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.6 whom the king…: Heb. in whose honour the king delighteth">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 6:11; Esther 6:9"> And Haman answered the king, For the man whom the king delighteth to honour,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.7 whom the king…: Heb. in whose honour the king delighteth">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Luke 15:22; Esther 1:11"> Let the royal apparel be brought which the king useth to wear, and the horse that the king rideth upon, and the crown royal which is set upon his head:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.8 Let…: Heb. Let them bring the royal apparel">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.8 which the king…: Heb. wherewith the king clotheth himself">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 41:43; 1 Kings 1:33–34; Zechariah 9:9"> And let this apparel and horse be delivered to the hand of one of the king’s most noble princes, that they may array the man withal whom the king delighteth to honour, and bring him on horseback through the street of the city, and proclaim before him, Thus shall it be done to the man whom the king delighteth to honour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="6.9 bring…: Heb. cause him to ride">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; Daniel 4:37; 2 Kings 10:10"> Then the king said to Haman, Make haste, and take the apparel and the horse, as thou hast said, and do even so to Mordecai the Jew, that sitteth at the king’s gate: let nothing fail of all that thou hast spoken.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="6.10 let…: Heb. suffer not a whit to fall">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Esther 9:3; Revelation 3:9"> Then took Haman the apparel and the horse, and arrayed Mordecai, and brought him on horseback through the street of the city, and proclaimed before him, Thus shall it be done unto the man whom the king delighteth to honour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; Jeremiah 14:3–4; Job 20:5"> And Mordecai came again to the king’s gate. But Haman hasted to his house mourning, and having his head covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Hosea 14:9; Genesis 40:19"> And Haman told Zeresh his wife and all his friends every thing that had befallen him. Then said his wise men and Zeresh his wife unto him, If Mordecai be of the seed of the Jews, before whom thou hast begun to fall, thou shalt not prevail against him, but shalt surely fall before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:35–36"> And while they were yet talking with him, came the king’s chamberlains, and hasted to bring Haman unto the banquet that Esther had prepared.</span>
