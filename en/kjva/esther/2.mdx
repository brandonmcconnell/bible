<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:14; 1 Samuel 17:18; Acts 15:36"> And Mordecai walked every day before the court of the women’s house, to know how Esther did, and what should become of her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.11 to know…: Heb. to know the peace">h</a></sup></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esther 8:9; Esther 2:3; Esther 2:1"> So Esther was taken unto king Ahasuerus into his house royal in the tenth month, which is the month Tebeth, in the seventh year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Luke 1:48–52; Ezekiel 17:24"> And the king loved Esther above all the women, and she obtained grace and favour in his sight more than all the virgins; so that he set the royal crown upon her head, and made her queen instead of Vashti.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.17 favour: or, kindness">i</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2.17 in his…: Heb. before him">j</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Esther 2:10; Esther 2:7"> Esther had not yet shewed her kindred nor her people; as Mordecai had charged her: for Esther did the commandment of Mordecai, like as when she was brought up with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 6:2; 1 Kings 16:9; 2 Kings 21:23"> In those days, while Mordecai sat in the king’s gate, two of the king’s chamberlains, Bigthan and Teresh, of those which kept the door, were wroth, and sought to lay hand on the king Ahasuerus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="2.21 Bigthan: also called, Bigthana">l</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="2.21 the door: Heb. the threshold">m</a></sup></span>
