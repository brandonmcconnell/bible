<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; John 16:13; Ephesians 1:17–18"> And the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; John 8:15–16; Luke 2:52"> And shall make him of quick understanding in the fear of the LORD: and he shall not judge after the sight of his eyes, neither reprove after the hearing of his ears:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.3 quick…: Heb. scent or, smell">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 19:15; Malachi 4:6"> But with righteousness shall he judge the poor, and reprove with equity for the meek of the earth: and he shall smite the earth with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.4 reprove: or, argue">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; 1 John 1:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> And righteousness shall be the girdle of his loins, and faithfulness the girdle of his reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 65:25; Hosea 2:18; Ezekiel 34:25"> The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid; and the calf and the young lion and the fatling together; and a little child shall lead them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the cow and the bear shall feed; their young ones shall lie down together: and the lion shall eat straw like the ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5; Psalms 140:3"> And the sucking child shall play on the hole of the asp, and the weaned child shall put his hand on the cockatrice’ den.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.8 cockatrice’: or, adder’s">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 52:10; Psalms 98:2–3"> They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain: for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:9–12; Luke 2:32; Isaiah 11:1"> And in that day there shall be a root of Jesse, which shall stand for an ensign of the people; to it shall the Gentiles seek: and his rest shall be glorious.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.10 glorious: Heb. glory">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:12; Isaiah 10:20; Zechariah 10:8–12"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria, and from Egypt, and from Pathros, and from Cush, and from Elam, and from Shinar, and from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; Isaiah 43:6; Zephaniah 3:10"> And he shall set up an ensign for the nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.12 corners: Heb. wings">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Hosea 1:11; Ezekiel 37:16–24"> The envy also of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off: Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:19; Isaiah 25:10; Jeremiah 49:28"> But they shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines toward the west; they shall spoil them of the east together: they shall lay their hand upon Edom and Moab; and the children of Ammon shall obey them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.14 them of: Heb. the children of">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.14 they shall lay…: Heb. Edom and Moab shall be the laying on of their hand">g</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="11.14 shall obey…: Heb. their obedience">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 50:2"> And the LORD shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea; and with his mighty wind shall he shake his hand over the river, and shall smite it in the seven streams, and make men go over dryshod.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="11.15 dryshod: Heb. in shoes">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23; Isaiah 35:8–10; Isaiah 11:11"> And there shall be an highway for the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria; like as it was to Israel in the day that he came up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
