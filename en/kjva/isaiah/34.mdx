<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremiah 50:27"> And the unicorns shall come down with them, and the bullocks with the bulls; and their land shall be soaked with blood, and their dust made fat with fatness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="34.7 unicorns: or, rhinocerots">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="34.7 soaked: or, drunken">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> And the streams thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the dust thereof into brimstone, and the land thereof shall become burning pitch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 13:20; Malachi 1:3–4"> It shall not be quenched night nor day; the smoke thereof shall go up for ever: from generation to generation it shall lie waste; none shall pass through it for ever and ever.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 10:22; Malachi 1:3"> And thorns shall come up in her palaces, nettles and brambles in the fortresses thereof: and it shall be an habitation of dragons, and a court for owls.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="34.13 owls: or, ostriches: Heb. daughters of the owl">e</a></sup></span>
