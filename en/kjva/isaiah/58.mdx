</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Job 22:26; Isaiah 33:16"> Then shalt thou delight thyself in the LORD; and I will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth, and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: for the mouth of the LORD hath spoken it.</span>
