<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Nahum 2:10; Nahum 1:6"> Therefore shall all hands be faint, and every man’s heart shall melt:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.7 be faint: or, fall down">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> And they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces shall be as flames.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.8 be amazed: Heb. wonder">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.8 one…: Heb. every man at his neighbour">d</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.8 flames: Heb. faces of the flames">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> Behold, the day of the LORD cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> For the stars of heaven and the constellations thereof shall not give their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not cause her light to shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> And I will punish the world for their evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; Isaiah 24:6; Job 28:16"> I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth shall remove out of her place, in the wrath of the LORD of hosts, and in the day of his fierce anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Jeremiah 50:16; 1 Kings 22:36"> And it shall be as the chased roe, and as a sheep that no man taketh up: they shall every man turn to his own people, and flee every one into his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> Every one that is found shall be thrust through; and every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> Their children also shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses shall be spoiled, and their wives ravished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> Behold, I will stir up the Medes against them, which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces; and they shall have no pity on the fruit of the womb; their eye shall not spare children.</span>
</div>
