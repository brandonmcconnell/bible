<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Behold, ye are of nothing, and your work of nought: an abomination is he that chooseth you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="41.24 of nothing: or, worse than nothing">n</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="41.24 of nought: or, worse than of a viper">o</a></sup></span>
