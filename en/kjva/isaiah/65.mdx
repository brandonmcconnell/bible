<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> I am sought of them that asked not for me; I am found of them that sought me not: I said, Behold me, behold me, unto a nation that was not called by my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremiah 4:14"> I have spread out my hands all the day unto a rebellious people, which walketh in a way that was not good, after their own thoughts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Isaiah 66:17"> A people that provoketh me to anger continually to my face; that sacrificeth in gardens, and burneth incense upon altars of brick;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="65.3 upon…: Heb. upon bricks">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Isaiah 66:3"> Which remain among the graves, and lodge in the monuments, which eat swine’s flesh, and broth of abominable things is in their vessels;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="65.4 broth: or, pieces">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> Which say, Stand by thyself, come not near to me; for I am holier than thou. These are a smoke in my nose, a fire that burneth all the day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="65.5 nose: or, anger">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:3; Jeremiah 16:18; Psalms 79:12"> Behold, it is written before me: I will not keep silence, but will recompense, even recompense into their bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:27–28; Isaiah 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> Your iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together, saith the LORD, which have burned incense upon the mountains, and blasphemed me upon the hills: therefore will I measure their former work into their bosom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> Thus saith the LORD, As the new wine is found in the cluster, and one saith, Destroy it not; for a blessing is in it: so will I do for my servants’ sakes, that I may not destroy them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I will bring forth a seed out of Jacob, and out of Judah an inheritor of my mountains: and mine elect shall inherit it, and my servants shall dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 33:9; Hosea 2:15"> And Sharon shall be a fold of flocks, and the valley of Achor a place for the herds to lie down in, for my people that have sought me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2; Isaiah 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> But ye are they that forsake the LORD, that forget my holy mountain, that prepare a table for that troop, and that furnish the drink offering unto that number.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="65.11 troop: or, Gad">d</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="65.11 number: or, Meni, a pagan god">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> Therefore will I number you to the sword, and ye shall all bow down to the slaughter: because when I called, ye did not answer; when I spake, ye did not hear; but did evil before mine eyes, and did choose that wherein I delighted not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, my servants shall eat, but ye shall be hungry: behold, my servants shall drink, but ye shall be thirsty: behold, my servants shall rejoice, but ye shall be ashamed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalms 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> Behold, my servants shall sing for joy of heart, but ye shall cry for sorrow of heart, and shall howl for vexation of spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="65.14 vexation: Heb. breaking">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> And ye shall leave your name for a curse unto my chosen: for the Lord GOD shall slay thee, and call his servants by another name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18; Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 31:5"> That he who blesseth himself in the earth shall bless himself in the God of truth; and he that sweareth in the earth shall swear by the God of truth; because the former troubles are forgotten, and because they are hid from mine eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Isaiah 66:22"> For, behold, I create new heavens and a new earth: and the former shall not be remembered, nor come into mind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="65.17 come…: Heb. come upon the heart">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Isaiah 66:10–14"> But be ye glad and rejoice for ever in that which I create: for, behold, I create Jerusalem a rejoicing, and her people a joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Isaiah 25:8"> And I will rejoice in Jerusalem, and joy in my people: and the voice of weeping shall be no more heard in her, nor the voice of crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> There shall be no more thence an infant of days, nor an old man that hath not filled his days: for the child shall die an hundred years old; but the sinner being an hundred years old shall be accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Isaiah 62:8–9; Isaiah 32:18"> And they shall build houses, and inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 91:16; Psalms 21:4"> They shall not build, and another inhabit; they shall not plant, and another eat: for as the days of a tree are the days of my people, and mine elect shall long enjoy the work of their hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="65.22 shall long…: Heb. shall make them continue long, or, shall wear out">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Jeremiah 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> They shall not labour in vain, nor bring forth for trouble; for they are the seed of the blessed of the LORD, and their offspring with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Isaiah 58:9"> And it shall come to pass, that before they call, I will answer; and while they are yet speaking, I will hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Micah 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> The wolf and the lamb shall feed together, and the lion shall eat straw like the bullock: and dust shall be the serpent’s meat. They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain, saith the LORD.</span>
</div>
