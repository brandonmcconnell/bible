<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And the eyes of them that see shall not be dim, and the ears of them that hear shall hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> The heart also of the rash shall understand knowledge, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be ready to speak plainly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="32.4 rash: Heb. hasty">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="32.4 plainly: or, elegantly">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; Psalms 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> The vile person shall be no more called liberal, nor the churl said to be bountiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For the vile person will speak villany, and his heart will work iniquity, to practise hypocrisy, and to utter error against the LORD, to make empty the soul of the hungry, and he will cause the drink of the thirsty to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> The instruments also of the churl are evil: he deviseth wicked devices to destroy the poor with lying words, even when the needy speaketh right.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="32.7 the needy…: or, he speaketh against the poor in judgment">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> But the liberal deviseth liberal things; and by liberal things shall he stand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="32.8 stand: or, be established">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Rise up, ye women that are at ease; hear my voice, ye careless daughters; give ear unto my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> Many days and years shall ye be troubled, ye careless women: for the vintage shall fail, the gathering shall not come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="32.10 Many…: Heb. Days above a year">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Tremble, ye women that are at ease; be troubled, ye careless ones: strip you, and make you bare, and gird sackcloth upon your loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> They shall lament for the teats, for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="32.12 pleasant…: Heb. fields of desire">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> Upon the land of my people shall come up thorns and briers; yea, upon all the houses of joy in the joyous city:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="32.13 yea…: or, burning upon">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> Because the palaces shall be forsaken; the multitude of the city shall be left; the forts and towers shall be for dens for ever, a joy of wild asses, a pasture of flocks;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="32.14 forts…: or, clifts and watchtowers">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> Until the spirit be poured upon us from on high, and the wilderness be a fruitful field, and the fruitful field be counted for a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> Then judgment shall dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness remain in the fruitful field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; Psalms 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> And the work of righteousness shall be peace; and the effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in sure dwellings, and in quiet resting places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> When it shall hail, coming down on the forest; and the city shall be low in a low place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="32.19 low in…: or, utterly abased">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> Blessed are ye that sow beside all waters, that send forth thither the feet of the ox and the ass.</span>
