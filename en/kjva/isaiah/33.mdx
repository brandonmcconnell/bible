<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> The earth mourneth and languisheth: Lebanon is ashamed and hewn down: Sharon is like a wilderness; and Bashan and Carmel shake off their fruits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="33.9 hewn…: or, withered away">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> Ye shall conceive chaff, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath, as fire, shall devour you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> But there the glorious LORD will be unto us a place of broad rivers and streams; wherein shall go no galley with oars, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="33.21 of…: Heb. broad of spaces, or, hands">l</a></sup></span>
