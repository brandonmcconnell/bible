<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not an apostle? am I not free? have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord? are not ye my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> If I be not an apostle unto others, yet doubtless I am to you: for the seal of mine apostleship are ye in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> Mine answer to them that do examine me is this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Have we not power to eat and to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Have we not power to lead about a sister, a wife, as well as other apostles, and as the brethren of the Lord, and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or I only and Barnabas, have not we power to forbear working?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who goeth a warfare any time at his own charges? who planteth a vineyard, and eateth not of the fruit thereof? or who feedeth a flock, and eateth not of the milk of the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> Say I these things as a man? or saith not the law the same also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For it is written in the law of Moses, Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn. Doth God take care for oxen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> Or saith he it altogether for our sakes? For our sakes, no doubt, this is written: that he that ploweth should plow in hope; and that he that thresheth in hope should be partaker of his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we have sown unto you spiritual things, is it a great thing if we shall reap your carnal things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others be partakers of this power over you, are not we rather? Nevertheless we have not used this power; but suffer all things, lest we should hinder the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do ye not know that they which minister about holy things live of the things of the temple? and they which wait at the altar are partakers with the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> Even so hath the Lord ordained that they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have used none of these things: neither have I written these things, that it should be so done unto me: for it were better for me to die, than that any man should make my glorying void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of: for necessity is laid upon me; yea, woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward: but if against my will, a dispensation of the gospel is committed unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> What is my reward then? Verily that, when I preach the gospel, I may make the gospel of Christ without charge, that I abuse not my power in the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> For though I be free from all men, yet have I made myself servant unto all, that I might gain the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> And unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews; to them that are under the law, as under the law, that I might gain them that are under the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> To them that are without law, as without law, (being not without law to God, but under the law to Christ,) that I might gain them that are without law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> To the weak became I as weak, that I might gain the weak: I am made all things to all men, that I might by all means save some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> And this I do for the gospel’s sake, that I might be partaker thereof with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Know ye not that they which run in a race run all, but one receiveth the prize? So run, that ye may obtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> I therefore so run, not as uncertainly; so fight I, not as one that beateth the air:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway.</span>
