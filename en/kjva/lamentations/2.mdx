<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalms 132:7; Matthew 11:23"> How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger, and cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel, and remembered not his footstool in the day of his anger!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Psalms 89:39–40; Lamentations 3:43"> The Lord hath swallowed up all the habitations of Jacob, and hath not pitied: he hath thrown down in his wrath the strong holds of the daughter of Judah; he hath brought them down to the ground: he hath polluted the kingdom and the princes thereof.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.2 brought…: Heb. made to touch">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:11; Psalms 75:10; Psalms 75:5"> He hath cut off in his fierce anger all the horn of Israel: he hath drawn back his right hand from before the enemy, and he burned against Jacob like a flaming fire, which devoureth round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:5; Lamentations 3:12–13"> He hath bent his bow like an enemy: he stood with his right hand as an adversary, and slew all that were pleasant to the eye in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion: he poured out his fury like fire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.4 all…: Heb. all the desirable of the eye">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:2; Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 52:13"> The Lord was as an enemy: he hath swallowed up Israel, he hath swallowed up all her palaces: he hath destroyed his strong holds, and hath increased in the daughter of Judah mourning and lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 1:4; Zephaniah 3:18; Lamentations 4:16"> And he hath violently taken away his tabernacle, as if it were of a garden: he hath destroyed his places of the assembly: the LORD hath caused the solemn feasts and sabbaths to be forgotten in Zion, and hath despised in the indignation of his anger the king and the priest.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.6 tabernacle: or, hedge">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–8"> The Lord hath cast off his altar, he hath abhorred his sanctuary, he hath given up into the hand of the enemy the walls of her palaces; they have made a noise in the house of the LORD, as in the day of a solemn feast.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.7 given up: Heb. shut up">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Isaiah 34:11; Isaiah 3:26"> The LORD hath purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion: he hath stretched out a line, he hath not withdrawn his hand from destroying: therefore he made the rampart and the wall to lament; they languished together.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.8 destroying: Heb. swallowing up">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:26; Hosea 3:4; 2 Chronicles 15:3"> Her gates are sunk into the ground; he hath destroyed and broken her bars: her king and her princes are among the Gentiles: the law is no more; her prophets also find no vision from the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 15:3; Job 2:12–13"> The elders of the daughter of Zion sit upon the ground, and keep silence: they have cast up dust upon their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloth: the virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:20; Job 16:13; Lamentations 1:16"> Mine eyes do fail with tears, my bowels are troubled, my liver is poured upon the earth, for the destruction of the daughter of my people; because the children and the sucklings swoon in the streets of the city.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.11 swoon: or, faint">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Ezekiel 30:24"> They say to their mothers, Where is corn and wine? when they swooned as the wounded in the streets of the city, when their soul was poured out into their mothers’ bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Jeremiah 8:22; 2 Samuel 5:20"> What thing shall I take to witness for thee? what thing shall I liken to thee, O daughter of Jerusalem? what shall I equal to thee, that I may comfort thee, O virgin daughter of Zion? for thy breach is great like the sea: who can heal thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Isaiah 58:1; Jeremiah 5:31"> Thy prophets have seen vain and foolish things for thee: and they have not discovered thine iniquity, to turn away thy captivity; but have seen for thee false burdens and causes of banishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 48:2; Psalms 50:2"> All that pass by clap their hands at thee; they hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, Is this the city that men call The perfection of beauty, The joy of the whole earth?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.15 by: Heb. by the way">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46; Job 16:9–10"> All thine enemies have opened their mouth against thee: they hiss and gnash the teeth: they say, We have swallowed her up: certainly this is the day that we looked for; we have found, we have seen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 89:42; Ezekiel 5:11; Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> The LORD hath done that which he had devised; he hath fulfilled his word that he had commanded in the days of old: he hath thrown down, and hath not pitied: and he hath caused thine enemy to rejoice over thee, he hath set up the horn of thine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:145; Jeremiah 9:1; Lamentations 2:8"> Their heart cried unto the Lord, O wall of the daughter of Zion, let tears run down like a river day and night: give thyself no rest; let not the apple of thine eye cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Isaiah 26:9"> Arise, cry out in the night: in the beginning of the watches pour out thine heart like water before the face of the Lord: lift up thy hands toward him for the life of thy young children, that faint for hunger in the top of every street.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10; Psalms 78:64"> Behold, O LORD, and consider to whom thou hast done this. Shall the women eat their fruit, and children of a span long? shall the priest and the prophet be slain in the sanctuary of the Lord?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.20 of a span…: or, swaddled with their hands?">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Lamentations 3:43; Zechariah 11:6"> The young and the old lie on the ground in the streets: my virgins and my young men are fallen by the sword; thou hast slain them in the day of thine anger; thou hast killed, and not pitied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:25; Psalms 31:13; Jeremiah 16:2–4"> Thou hast called as in a solemn day my terrors round about, so that in the day of the LORD’s anger none escaped nor remained: those that I have swaddled and brought up hath mine enemy consumed.</span>
</div>
