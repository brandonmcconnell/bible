<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Afterward saw I the same man come down from the mountain, and call unto him another peaceable Multitude.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And there came much people unto him, whereof some were glad, some were sorry, and some of them were bound, and other some brought of them that were offered: then was I sick through great fear, and I awaked, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou hast shewed thy servant these wonders from the beginning, and hast counted me worthy that thou shouldest receive my prayer:</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shew me now yet the interpretation of this dream.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For they that were not left were in heaviness.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore are they come into great perils and many necessities, like as these dreams declare.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And when all the people hear his voice, every man shall in their own land leave the battle they have one against another.</span>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And shall lay before them their evil thoughts, and the torments wherewith they shall begin to be tormented, which are like unto a flame: and he shall destroy them without labour by the law which is like unto fire.</span>
