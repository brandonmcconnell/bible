<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; 1 Timothy 6:3; 2 Timothy 2:25"> Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God’s elect, and the acknowledging of the truth which is after godliness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 John 2:25; 2 Timothy 1:9"> In hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:11; Titus 3:4–6; 1 Timothy 1:1"> But hath in due times manifested his word through preaching, which is committed unto me according to the commandment of God our Saviour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Peter 1:1"> To Titus, mine own son after the common faith: Grace, mercy, and peace, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ our Saviour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–7; 1 Timothy 3:12; Titus 1:6–8"> If any be blameless, the husband of one wife, having faithful children not accused of riot or unruly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; 2 Peter 2:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> For a bishop must be blameless, as the steward of God; not selfwilled, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> But a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3"> Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> For there are many unruly and vain talkers and deceivers, specially they of the circumcision:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> Whose mouths must be stopped, who subvert whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> One of themselves, even a prophet of their own, said, The Cretians are alway liars, evil beasts, slow bellies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> This witness is true. Wherefore rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> Not giving heed to Jewish fables, and commandments of men, that turn from the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> Unto the pure all things are pure: but unto them that are defiled and unbelieving is nothing pure; but even their mind and conscience is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> They profess that they know God; but in works they deny him, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.</span>
