<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 10:10–14; Leviticus 8:14"> And he said unto Aaron, Take thee a young calf for a sin offering, and a ram for a burnt offering, without blemish, and offer them before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; Revelation 5:9; Titus 2:14"> And unto the children of Israel thou shalt speak, saying, Take ye a kid of the goats for a sin offering; and a calf and a lamb, both of the first year, without blemish, for a burnt offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:43; Ezekiel 43:2; Leviticus 9:23"> Also a bullock and a ram for peace offerings, to sacrifice before the LORD; and a meat offering mingled with oil: for to day the LORD will appear unto you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2–3; Exodus 19:17; Nehemiah 8:1"> And they brought that which Moses commanded before the tabernacle of the congregation: and all the congregation drew near and stood before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:23; Ezekiel 43:2; Exodus 24:16"> And Moses said, This is the thing which the LORD commanded that ye should do: and the glory of the LORD shall appear unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:3; Hebrews 5:1; Leviticus 4:3"> And Moses said unto Aaron, Go unto the altar, and offer thy sin offering, and thy burnt offering, and make an atonement for thyself, and for the people: and offer the offering of the people, and make an atonement for them; as the LORD commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:1–12; Leviticus 1:4–5; Leviticus 4:29"> Aaron therefore went unto the altar, and slew the calf of the sin offering, which was for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7; Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 4:25"> And the sons of Aaron brought the blood unto him: and he dipped his finger in the blood, and put it upon the horns of the altar, and poured out the blood at the bottom of the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 8:16–17; Proverbs 23:26; Leviticus 3:9–11"> But the fat, and the kidneys, and the caul above the liver of the sin offering, he burnt upon the altar; as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 8:17; Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 16:27–28"> And the flesh and the hide he burnt with fire without the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Ephesians 5:25–27; Leviticus 1:1–17"> And he slew the burnt offering; and Aaron’s sons presented unto him the blood, which he sprinkled round about upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they presented the burnt offering unto him, with the pieces thereof, and the head: and he burnt them upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 8:21"> And he did wash the inwards and the legs, and burnt them upon the burnt offering on the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–31; Hebrews 5:3; Leviticus 9:3"> And he brought the people’s offering, and took the goat, which was the sin offering for the people, and slew it, and offered it for sin, as the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12–14; Leviticus 8:18–21; Hebrews 10:1–22"> And he brought the burnt offering, and offered it according to the manner.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.16 manner: or, ordinance">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:53; Leviticus 9:1"> And he brought the meat offering, and took an handful thereof, and burnt it upon the altar, beside the burnt sacrifice of the morning.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.17 took…: Heb. filled his hand out of it">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Romans 5:1; Ephesians 2:14–17"> He slew also the bullock and the ram for a sacrifice of peace offerings, which was for the people: and Aaron’s sons presented unto him the blood, which he sprinkled upon the altar round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 9:10; Leviticus 3:9"> And the fat of the bullock and of the ram, the rump, and that which covereth the inwards, and the kidneys, and the caul above the liver:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 3:14–17; Leviticus 7:29–34"> And they put the fat upon the breasts, and he burnt the fat upon the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Leviticus 7:24; Exodus 29:24"> And the breasts and the right shoulder Aaron waved for a wave offering before the LORD; as Moses commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Luke 24:50; Numbers 6:23–27"> And Aaron lifted up his hand toward the people, and blessed them, and came down from offering of the sin offering, and the burnt offering, and peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 9:6; Numbers 16:19; Luke 1:21–22"> And Moses and Aaron went into the tabernacle of the congregation, and came out, and blessed the people: and the glory of the LORD appeared unto all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 6:21; 1 Kings 18:38–39; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> And there came a fire out from before the LORD, and consumed upon the altar the burnt offering and the fat: which when all the people saw, they shouted, and fell on their faces.</span>
