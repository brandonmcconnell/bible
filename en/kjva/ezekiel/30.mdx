<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6; Ezekiel 21:12; Joel 1:11"> Son of man, prophesy and say, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Howl ye, Woe worth the day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 1:15"> For the day is near, even the day of the LORD is near, a cloudy day; it shall be the time of the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 29:8; Isaiah 16:7"> And the sword shall come upon Egypt, and great pain shall be in Ethiopia, when the slain shall fall in Egypt, and they shall take away her multitude, and her foundations shall be broken down.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.4 pain: or, fear">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 27:10; Jeremiah 25:24"> Ethiopia, and Libya, and Lydia, and all the mingled people, and Chub, and the men of the land that is in league, shall fall with them by the sword.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.5 Libya: Heb. Phut">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30.5 men: Heb. children">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:10; Isaiah 20:3–6; Isaiah 31:3"> Thus saith the LORD; They also that uphold Egypt shall fall; and the pride of her power shall come down: from the tower of Syene shall they fall in it by the sword, saith the Lord GOD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="30.6 from…: or, from Migdol to Syene">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:12; Jeremiah 25:18–26; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> And they shall be desolate in the midst of the countries that are desolate, and her cities shall be in the midst of the cities that are wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:6; Ezekiel 29:16; Amos 1:7"> And they shall know that I am the LORD, when I have set a fire in Egypt, and when all her helpers shall be destroyed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="30.8 destroyed: Heb. broken">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1–2; Isaiah 23:5; Ezekiel 32:9–10"> In that day shall messengers go forth from me in ships to make the careless Ethiopians afraid, and great pain shall come upon them, as in the day of Egypt: for, lo, it cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 32:11–16; Ezekiel 29:4–5"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; I will also make the multitude of Egypt to cease by the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 39:11–20"> He and his people with him, the terrible of the nations, shall be brought to destroy the land: and they shall draw their swords against Egypt, and fill the land with the slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 1:4; Revelation 16:12"> And I will make the rivers dry, and sell the land into the hand of the wicked: and I will make the land waste, and all that is therein, by the hand of strangers: I the LORD have spoken it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="30.12 dry: Heb. drought">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="30.12 all…: Heb. the fulness thereof">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 2:16"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; I will also destroy the idols, and I will cause their images to cease out of Noph; and there shall be no more a prince of the land of Egypt: and I will put a fear in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 46:25; Psalms 78:43"> And I will make Pathros desolate, and will set fire in Zoan, and will execute judgments in No.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30.14 Zoan: or, Tanis">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 16:1; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 11:6"> And I will pour my fury upon Sin, the strength of Egypt; and I will cut off the multitude of No.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="30.15 Sin: or, Pelusium">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:8–9; Ezekiel 28:18"> And I will set fire in Egypt: Sin shall have great pain, and No shall be rent asunder, and Noph shall have distresses daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 41:45"> The young men of Aven and of Pi-beseth shall fall by the sword: and these cities shall go into captivity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="30.17 Aven: or, Heliopolis">j</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="30.17 Pi-beseth: or, Pubastum">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:3; Ezekiel 29:15; Isaiah 10:27"> At Tehaphnehes also the day shall be darkened, when I shall break there the yokes of Egypt: and the pomp of her strength shall cease in her: as for her, a cloud shall cover her, and her daughters shall go into captivity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="30.18 darkened: or, restrained">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Revelation 17:1; Ezekiel 5:8"> Thus will I execute judgments in Egypt: and they shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 29:1"> And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first month, in the seventh day of the month, that the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:13; Jeremiah 46:11; Psalms 37:17"> Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and, lo, it shall not be bound up to be healed, to put a roller to bind it, to make it strong to hold the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 37:17; Jeremiah 37:7; 2 Kings 24:7"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and will break his arms, the strong, and that which was broken; and I will cause the sword to fall out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:26; Ezekiel 30:17–18; Ezekiel 29:12–13"> And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:25; Zephaniah 2:12; Isaiah 45:5"> And I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and put my sword in his hand: but I will break Pharaoh’s arms, and he shall groan before him with the groanings of a deadly wounded man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:21; Ezekiel 32:15; Ezekiel 29:16"> But I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and the arms of Pharaoh shall fall down; and they shall know that I am the LORD, when I shall put my sword into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall stretch it out upon the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:42; Ezekiel 29:12; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and disperse them among the countries; and they shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
