<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because thou hast had a perpetual hatred, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time that their iniquity had an end:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="35.5 perpetual…: or, hatred of old">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="35.5 shed…: Heb. poured out the children">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="35.5 force: Heb. hands">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> Thus will I make mount Seir most desolate, and cut off from it him that passeth out and him that returneth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="35.7 most…: Heb. desolation and desolation">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> And I will fill his mountains with his slain men: in thy hills, and in thy valleys, and in all thy rivers, shall they fall that are slain with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> I will make thee perpetual desolations, and thy cities shall not return: and ye shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalms 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because thou hast said, These two nations and these two countries shall be mine, and we will possess it; whereas the LORD was there:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="35.10 whereas…: or, though the LORD was there">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalms 73:17–18"> Therefore, as I live, saith the Lord GOD, I will even do according to thine anger, and according to thine envy which thou hast used out of thy hatred against them; and I will make myself known among them, when I have judged thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> And thou shalt know that I am the LORD, and that I have heard all thy blasphemies which thou hast spoken against the mountains of Israel, saying, They are laid desolate, they are given us to consume.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="35.12 to consume: Heb. to devour">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; When the whole earth rejoiceth, I will make thee desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> As thou didst rejoice at the inheritance of the house of Israel, because it was desolate, so will I do unto thee: thou shalt be desolate, O mount Seir, and all Idumea, even all of it: and they shall know that I am the LORD.</span>
