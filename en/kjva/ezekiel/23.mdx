<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:44–46; Jeremiah 3:7–10"> Son of man, there were two women, the daughters of one mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 17:7; Ezekiel 16:22; Joshua 24:14"> And they committed whoredoms in Egypt; they committed whoredoms in their youth: there were their breasts pressed, and there they bruised the teats of their virginity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Psalms 76:2; Jeremiah 2:2–3"> And the names of them were Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister: and they were mine, and they bare sons and daughters. Thus were their names; Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.4 Aholah: that is, His tent, or, tabernacle">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.4 Aholibah: that is, My tabernacle in her">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; 2 Kings 17:3; 2 Kings 16:7"> And Aholah played the harlot when she was mine; and she doted on her lovers, on the Assyrians her neighbours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23"> Which were clothed with blue, captains and rulers, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding upon horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Hosea 6:10; Ezekiel 22:3–4"> Thus she committed her whoredoms with them, with all them that were the chosen men of Assyria, and with all on whom she doted: with all their idols she defiled herself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.7 committed…: Heb. bestowed her whoredoms upon them">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.7 the chosen…: Heb. the choice of the children of Asshur">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19; Ezekiel 23:3; Exodus 32:4"> Neither left she her whoredoms brought from Egypt: for in her youth they lay with her, and they bruised the breasts of her virginity, and poured their whoredom upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 11:5; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:23"> Wherefore I have delivered her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the Assyrians, upon whom she doted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:29; Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 22:8–9"> These discovered her nakedness: they took her sons and her daughters, and slew her with the sword: and she became famous among women; for they had executed judgment upon her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.10 famous: Heb. a name">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Ezekiel 16:47–51; Ezekiel 23:4"> And when her sister Aholibah saw this, she was more corrupt in her inordinate love than she, and in her whoredoms more than her sister in her whoredoms.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="23.11 she was…: Heb. she corrupted her inordinate love more than, etc">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="23.11 more than…: Heb. more than the whoredoms of her sister">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:7–15; Ezekiel 23:23; 2 Chronicles 28:16–23"> She doted upon the Assyrians her neighbours, captains and rulers clothed most gorgeously, horsemen riding upon horses, all of them desirable young men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 12:1–2; 2 Kings 17:18–19; Ezekiel 23:31"> Then I saw that she was defiled, that they took both one way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Jeremiah 22:14; Jeremiah 50:2"> And that she increased her whoredoms: for when she saw men pourtrayed upon the wall, the images of the Chaldeans pourtrayed with vermilion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:18; 1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 22:21"> Girded with girdles upon their loins, exceeding in dyed attire upon their heads, all of them princes to look to, after the manner of the Babylonians of Chaldea, the land of their nativity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:29; Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1"> And as soon as she saw them with her eyes, she doted upon them, and sent messengers unto them into Chaldea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="23.16 as soon…: Heb. at the sight of her eyes">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:28; Ezekiel 23:22; Genesis 10:10"> And the Babylonians came to her into the bed of love, and they defiled her with their whoredom, and she was polluted with them, and her mind was alienated from them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="23.17 Babylonians: Heb. children of Babel">i</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="23.17 alienated: Heb. loosed, or, disjointed">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 78:59; Psalms 106:40; Jeremiah 12:8"> So she discovered her whoredoms, and discovered her nakedness: then my mind was alienated from her, like as my mind was alienated from her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 20:7; Ezekiel 16:51"> Yet she multiplied her whoredoms, in calling to remembrance the days of her youth, wherein she had played the harlot in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Ezekiel 16:26; Jeremiah 5:8"> For she doted upon their paramours, whose flesh is as the flesh of asses, and whose issue is like the issue of horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Thus thou calledst to remembrance the lewdness of thy youth, in bruising thy teats by the Egyptians for the paps of thy youth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Revelation 17:16; Jeremiah 6:22–23"> Therefore, O Aholibah, thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I will raise up thy lovers against thee, from whom thy mind is alienated, and I will bring them against thee on every side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:21; 2 Kings 24:2; 2 Kings 20:14–17"> The Babylonians, and all the Chaldeans, Pekod, and Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them: all of them desirable young men, captains and rulers, great lords and renowned, all of them riding upon horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:14; Ezekiel 26:10; Ezekiel 23:45"> And they shall come against thee with chariots, wagons, and wheels, and with an assembly of people, which shall set against thee buckler and shield and helmet round about: and I will set judgment before them, and they shall judge thee according to their judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 8:1–18; Ezekiel 15:6–7"> And I will set my jealousy against thee, and they shall deal furiously with thee: they shall take away thy nose and thine ears; and thy remnant shall fall by the sword: they shall take thy sons and thy daughters; and thy residue shall be devoured by the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Jeremiah 13:22; Ezekiel 23:29"> They shall also strip thee out of thy clothes, and take away thy fair jewels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="23.26 fair…: Heb. instruments of thy decking">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:41; Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 23:19"> Thus will I make thy lewdness to cease from thee, and thy whoredom brought from the land of Egypt: so that thou shalt not lift up thine eyes unto them, nor remember Egypt any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:22; Ezekiel 23:17; Jeremiah 34:20"> For thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I will deliver thee into the hand of them whom thou hatest, into the hand of them from whom thy mind is alienated:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 23:25–26; Ezekiel 23:45–47"> And they shall deal with thee hatefully, and shall take away all thy labour, and shall leave thee naked and bare: and the nakedness of thy whoredoms shall be discovered, both thy lewdness and thy whoredoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Psalms 106:35–38; Jeremiah 2:18–20"> I will do these things unto thee, because thou hast gone a whoring after the heathen, and because thou art polluted with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Jeremiah 7:14–15; Ezekiel 16:47–51"> Thou hast walked in the way of thy sister; therefore will I give her cup into thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 60:3; Ezekiel 22:4–5"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Thou shalt drink of thy sister’s cup deep and large: thou shalt be laughed to scorn and had in derision; it containeth much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> Thou shalt be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, with the cup of astonishment and desolation, with the cup of thy sister Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 18:7"> Thou shalt even drink it and suck it out, and thou shalt break the sherds thereof, and pluck off thine own breasts: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 32:33"> Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thou hast forgotten me, and cast me behind thy back, therefore bear thou also thy lewdness and thy whoredoms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 16:2"> The LORD said moreover unto me; Son of man, wilt thou judge Aholah and Aholibah? yea, declare unto them their abominations;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="23.36 judge: or, plead for">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:36; Jeremiah 32:35; Jeremiah 7:31"> That they have committed adultery, and blood is in their hands, and with their idols have they committed adultery, and have also caused their sons, whom they bare unto me, to pass for them through the fire, to devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; 2 Kings 21:7; Ezekiel 7:20"> Moreover this they have done unto me: they have defiled my sanctuary in the same day, and have profaned my sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Kings 21:4; Ezekiel 44:7; Jeremiah 23:11"> For when they had slain their children to their idols, then they came the same day into my sanctuary to profane it; and, lo, thus have they done in the midst of mine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 16:13–16"> And furthermore, that ye have sent for men to come from far, unto whom a messenger was sent; and, lo, they came: for whom thou didst wash thyself, paintedst thy eyes, and deckedst thyself with ornaments,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="23.40 to come: Heb. coming">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Jeremiah 44:17; Isaiah 65:11"> And satest upon a stately bed, and a table prepared before it, whereupon thou hast set mine incense and mine oil.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="23.41 stately: Heb. honourable">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Revelation 12:3; Exodus 32:18–19"> And a voice of a multitude being at ease was with her: and with the men of the common sort were brought Sabeans from the wilderness, which put bracelets upon their hands, and beautiful crowns upon their heads.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="23.42 of the…: Heb. of the multitude of men">o</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="23.42 Sabeans: or, drunkards">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Jeremiah 13:23; Ezra 9:7"> Then said I unto her that was old in adulteries, Will they now commit whoredoms with her, and she with them?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="23.43 whoredoms…: Heb. her whoredoms">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:9–13; Ezekiel 23:3"> Yet they went in unto her, as they go in unto a woman that playeth the harlot: so went they in unto Aholah and unto Aholibah, the lewd women.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hosea 6:5; Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:36–39"> And the righteous men, they shall judge them after the manner of adulteresses, and after the manner of women that shed blood; because they are adulteresses, and blood is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40; Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 24:9"> For thus saith the Lord GOD; I will bring up a company upon them, and will give them to be removed and spoiled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="23.46 to be…: Heb. for a removing and spoil">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:40–41; Jeremiah 39:8; Ezekiel 24:21"> And the company shall stone them with stones, and dispatch them with their swords; they shall slay their sons and their daughters, and burn up their houses with fire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="23.47 dispatch…: or, single them out">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:6; Ezekiel 23:27; Ezekiel 16:41"> Thus will I cause lewdness to cease out of the land, that all women may be taught not to do after your lewdness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 6:7"> And they shall recompense your lewdness upon you, and ye shall bear the sins of your idols: and ye shall know that I am the Lord GOD.</span>
