<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Kings 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; An evil, an only evil, behold, is come.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> And mine eye shall not spare, neither will I have pity: I will recompense thee according to thy ways and thine abominations that are in the midst of thee; and ye shall know that I am the LORD that smiteth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.9 thee according: Heb. upon thee, etc">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> Violence is risen up into a rod of wickedness: none of them shall remain, nor of their multitude, nor of any of theirs: neither shall there be wailing for them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.11 theirs: or, their tumultuous persons: Heb. tumult">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> And I will give it into the hands of the strangers for a prey, and to the wicked of the earth for a spoil; and they shall pollute it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> My face will I turn also from them, and they shall pollute my secret place: for the robbers shall enter into it, and defile it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="7.22 robbers: or, burglers">m</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> Wherefore I will bring the worst of the heathen, and they shall possess their houses: I will also make the pomp of the strong to cease; and their holy places shall be defiled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="7.24 their holy…: or, they shall inherit their holy places">n</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The king shall mourn, and the prince shall be clothed with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land shall be troubled: I will do unto them after their way, and according to their deserts will I judge them; and they shall know that I am the LORD.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="7.27 according…: Heb. with their judgments">p</a></sup></span>
