<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Isaiah 53:6"> For I have laid upon thee the years of their iniquity, according to the number of the days, three hundred and ninety days: so shalt thou bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> And when thou hast accomplished them, lie again on thy right side, and thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.6 each…: Heb. a day for a year, a day for a year">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:25"> And, behold, I will lay bands upon thee, and thou shalt not turn thee from one side to another, till thou hast ended the days of thy siege.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.8 from one…: Heb. from thy side to thy side">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; John 3:34; Ezekiel 4:16"> Thou shalt drink also water by measure, the sixth part of an hin: from time to time shalt thou drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> And thou shalt eat it as barley cakes, and thou shalt bake it with dung that cometh out of man, in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Hosea 9:3–4"> And the LORD said, Even thus shall the children of Israel eat their defiled bread among the Gentiles, whither I will drive them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then he said unto me, Lo, I have given thee cow’s dung for man’s dung, and thou shalt prepare thy bread therewith.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Ezekiel 33:10"> That they may want bread and water, and be astonied one with another, and consume away for their iniquity.</span>
