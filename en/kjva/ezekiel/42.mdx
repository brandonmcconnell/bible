<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:4"> And the way before them was like the appearance of the chambers which were toward the north, as long as they, and as broad as they: and all their goings out were both according to their fashions, and according to their doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And according to the doors of the chambers that were toward the south was a door in the head of the way, even the way directly before the wall toward the east, as one entereth into them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> When the priests enter therein, then shall they not go out of the holy place into the utter court, but there they shall lay their garments wherein they minister; for they are holy; and shall put on other garments, and shall approach to those things which are for the people.</span>
