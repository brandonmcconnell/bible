<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pluck up that which is planted;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.2 to be…: Heb. to bear">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> A time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to break down, and a time to build up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> A time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> A time to cast away stones, and a time to gather stones together; a time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.5 to refrain from: Heb. to be far from">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> A time to get, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to cast away;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.6 get: or, seek">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> A time to rend, and a time to sew; a time to keep silence, and a time to speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> A time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What profit hath he that worketh in that wherein he laboureth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen the travail, which God hath given to the sons of men to be exercised in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> He hath made every thing beautiful in his time: also he hath set the world in their heart, so that no man can find out the work that God maketh from the beginning to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I know that there is no good in them, but for a man to rejoice, and to do good in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> And also that every man should eat and drink, and enjoy the good of all his labour, it is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I know that, whatsoever God doeth, it shall be for ever: nothing can be put to it, nor any thing taken from it: and God doeth it, that men should fear before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> That which hath been is now; and that which is to be hath already been; and God requireth that which is past.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.15 that which is past: Heb. that which is driven away">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> And moreover I saw under the sun the place of judgment, that wickedness was there; and the place of righteousness, that iniquity was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> I said in mine heart, God shall judge the righteous and the wicked: for there is a time there for every purpose and for every work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said in mine heart concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.18 that God…: or, that they might clear God, and see, etc">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other; yea, they have all one breath; so that a man hath no preeminence above a beast: for all is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> All go unto one place; all are of the dust, and all turn to dust again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Who knoweth the spirit of man that goeth upward, and the spirit of the beast that goeth downward to the earth?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.21 of man: Heb. of the sons of man">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.21 goeth upward: Heb. is ascending, etc">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Wherefore I perceive that there is nothing better, than that a man should rejoice in his own works; for that is his portion: for who shall bring him to see what shall be after him?</span>
