<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 2 Peter 1:12–15; 1 Peter 1:22"> This second epistle, beloved, I now write unto you; in both which I stir up your pure minds by way of remembrance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:17; 2 Peter 2:21; Luke 1:70"> That ye may be mindful of the words which were spoken before by the holy prophets, and of the commandment of us the apostles of the Lord and Saviour:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; 2 Peter 2:10"> Knowing this first, that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:15; Malachi 2:17; Jeremiah 5:12–13"> And saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 1:9; Psalms 24:2"> For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:38–39; 2 Peter 2:5; Luke 17:27"> Whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 51:6; 2 Peter 3:12"> But the heavens and the earth, which are now, by the same word are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:4; Romans 11:25; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> But, beloved, be not ignorant of this one thing, that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Romans 2:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:7"> But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night; in the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat, the earth also and the works that are therein shall be burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27; 1 Peter 2:12"> Seeing then that all these things shall be dissolved, what manner of persons ought ye to be in all holy conversation and godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 34:4; Micah 1:4"> Looking for and hasting unto the coming of the day of God, wherein the heavens being on fire shall be dissolved, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Revelation 21:1; Isaiah 66:22"> Nevertheless we, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 1:10"> Wherefore, beloved, seeing that ye look for such things, be diligent that ye may be found of him in peace, without spot, and blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 1:16"> And account that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation; even as our beloved brother Paul also according to the wisdom given unto him hath written unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:6; Hebrews 5:11; James 1:8"> As also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things; in which are some things hard to be understood, which they that are unlearned and unstable wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Colossians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Ye therefore, beloved, seeing ye know these things before, beware lest ye also, being led away with the error of the wicked, fall from your own stedfastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Ephesians 4:15; 1 Peter 2:2"> But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and for ever. Amen.</span>
