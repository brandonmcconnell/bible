<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> O foolish Galatians, who hath bewitched you, that ye should not obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth, crucified among you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> This only would I learn of you, Received ye the Spirit by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Are ye so foolish? having begun in the Spirit, are ye now made perfect by the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have ye suffered so many things in vain? if it be yet in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> He therefore that ministereth to you the Spirit, and worketh miracles among you, doeth he it by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> Even as Abraham believed God, and it was accounted to him for righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> Know ye therefore that they which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> So then they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> For as many as are of the works of the law are under the curse: for it is written, Cursed is every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> But that no man is justified by the law in the sight of God, it is evident: for, The just shall live by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> And the law is not of faith: but, The man that doeth them shall live in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brethren, I speak after the manner of men; Though it be but a man’s covenant, yet if it be confirmed, no man disannulleth, or addeth thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> And this I say, that the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, which was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul, that it should make the promise of none effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance be of the law, it is no more of promise: but God gave it to Abraham by promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> Wherefore then serveth the law? It was added because of transgressions, till the seed should come to whom the promise was made; and it was ordained by angels in the hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> Now a mediator is not a mediator of one, but God is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> Is the law then against the promises of God? God forbid: for if there had been a law given which could have given life, verily righteousness should have been by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> But the scripture hath concluded all under sin, that the promise by faith of Jesus Christ might be given to them that believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> But before faith came, we were kept under the law, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> Wherefore the law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> But after that faith is come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> And if ye be Christ’s, then are ye Abraham’s seed, and heirs according to the promise.</span>
</div>
