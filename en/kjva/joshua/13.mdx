<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 19:30; 1 Samuel 4:1; Judges 1:34–36"> From the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah that is beside the Sidonians, unto Aphek, to the borders of the Amorites:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.4 Mearah: or, the cave">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 11:8; Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:21–23"> All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon unto Misrephoth-maim, and all the Sidonians, them will I drive out from before the children of Israel: only divide thou it by lot unto the Israelites for an inheritance, as I have commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Numbers 33:54; Ezekiel 48:23–29"> Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe of Manasseh,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Numbers 21:30; Joshua 12:2"> From Aroer, that is upon the bank of the river Arnon, and the city that is in the midst of the river, and all the plain of Medeba unto Dibon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–26"> And all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, which reigned in Heshbon, unto the border of the children of Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23; Joshua 12:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:47–48"> And Gilead, and the border of the Geshurites and Maachathites, and all mount Hermon, and all Bashan unto Salcah;</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 22:41; Numbers 32:38; Numbers 21:19"> Heshbon, and all her cities that are in the plain; Dibon, and Bamoth-baal, and Beth-baal-meon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.17 Bamoth-baal…: or, the high places of Baal, and house of Baal-meon">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Deuteronomy 4:46; Ezekiel 25:9"> And Beth-peor, and Ashdoth-pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.20 Ashdoth-pisgah: or, springs of Pisgah, or, the hill">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26; 2 Samuel 11:1; Numbers 32:35"> And their coast was Jazer, and all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the children of Ammon, unto Aroer that is before Rabbah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27; 2 Samuel 2:8; Judges 10:17"> And from Heshbon unto Ramath-mizpeh, and Betonim; and from Mahanaim unto the border of Debir;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 32:36; Numbers 34:11; Genesis 33:17"> And in the valley, Beth-aram, and Beth-nimrah, and Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, Jordan and his border, even unto the edge of the sea of Chinnereth on the other side Jordan eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> This is the inheritance of the children of Gad after their families, the cities, and their villages.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; Deuteronomy 3:13–15; Numbers 32:39–41"> And their coast was from Mahanaim, all Bashan, all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the towns of Jair, which are in Bashan, threescore cities:</span>
