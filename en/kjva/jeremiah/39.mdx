<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; 2 Kings 25:7; Jeremiah 52:11"> Moreover he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, and bound him with chains, to carry him to Babylon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="39.7 with…: Heb. with two brasen chains, or, fetters">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:2; Psalms 34:22; Jeremiah 21:9"> For I will surely deliver thee, and thou shalt not fall by the sword, but thy life shall be for a prey unto thee: because thou hast put thy trust in me, saith the LORD.</span>
