<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremiah 14:11"> Then said the LORD unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people: cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Zechariah 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> And it shall come to pass, if they say unto thee, Whither shall we go forth? then thou shalt tell them, Thus saith the LORD; Such as are for death, to death; and such as are for the sword, to the sword; and such as are for the famine, to the famine; and such as are for the captivity, to the captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> And I will appoint over them four kinds, saith the LORD: the sword to slay, and the dogs to tear, and the fowls of the heaven, and the beasts of the earth, to devour and destroy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.3 kinds: Heb. families">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 23:26–27; 2 Kings 24:3–4"> And I will cause them to be removed into all kingdoms of the earth, because of Manasseh the son of Hezekiah king of Judah, for that which he did in Jerusalem.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.4 cause…: Heb. give them for a removing">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Nahum 3:7; Jeremiah 21:7"> For who shall have pity upon thee, O Jerusalem? or who shall bemoan thee? or who shall go aside to ask how thou doest?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.5 how…: Heb. of thy peace?">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Zephaniah 1:4; Isaiah 1:4"> Thou hast forsaken me, saith the LORD, thou art gone backward: therefore will I stretch out my hand against thee, and destroy thee; I am weary with repenting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 41:16"> And I will fan them with a fan in the gates of the land; I will bereave them of children, I will destroy my people, since they return not from their ways.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.7 children: or, whatsoever is dear">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 3:25–1; Jeremiah 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> Their widows are increased to me above the sand of the seas: I have brought upon them against the mother of the young men a spoiler at noonday: I have caused him to fall upon it suddenly, and terrors upon the city.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.8 the mother…: or, the mother city a young man spoiling, etc, or, the mother and the young men">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:5; Isaiah 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> She that hath borne seven languisheth: she hath given up the ghost; her sun is gone down while it was yet day: she hath been ashamed and confounded: and the residue of them will I deliver to the sword before their enemies, saith the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> Woe is me, my mother, that thou hast borne me a man of strife and a man of contention to the whole earth! I have neither lent on usury, nor men have lent to me on usury; yet every one of them doth curse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 29:11–14; Psalms 106:46"> The LORD said, Verily it shall be well with thy remnant; verily I will cause the enemy to entreat thee well in the time of evil and in the time of affliction.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.11 cause…: or, intreat the enemy for thee">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremiah 21:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Shall iron break the northern iron and the steel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:3; Psalms 44:12; Isaiah 52:3"> Thy substance and thy treasures will I give to the spoil without price, and that for all thy sins, even in all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremiah 17:4"> And I will make thee to pass with thine enemies into a land which thou knowest not: for a fire is kindled in mine anger, which shall burn upon you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:3; Psalms 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> O LORD, thou knowest: remember me, and visit me, and revenge me of my persecutors; take me not away in thy longsuffering: know that for thy sake I have suffered rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:1–3; Psalms 119:101–103"> Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart: for I am called by thy name, O LORD God of hosts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.16 I am…: Heb. thy name is called upon me">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; Psalms 26:4–5"> I sat not in the assembly of the mockers, nor rejoiced; I sat alone because of thy hand: for thou hast filled me with indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:15; Job 34:6; Micah 1:9"> Why is my pain perpetual, and my wound incurable, which refuseth to be healed? wilt thou be altogether unto me as a liar, and as waters that fail?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.18 fail: Heb. be not sure?">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> Therefore thus saith the LORD, If thou return, then will I bring thee again, and thou shalt stand before me: and if thou take forth the precious from the vile, thou shalt be as my mouth: let them return unto thee; but return not thou unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Ezekiel 3:9"> And I will make thee unto this people a fenced brasen wall: and they shall fight against thee, but they shall not prevail against thee: for I am with thee to save thee and to deliver thee, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Isaiah 49:24–25; Jeremiah 50:34"> And I will deliver thee out of the hand of the wicked, and I will redeem thee out of the hand of the terrible.</span>
