<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4–5; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Isaiah 3:1"> For through the anger of the LORD it came to pass in Jerusalem and Judah, till he had cast them out from his presence, that Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1; Zechariah 8:19; Ezekiel 24:1–2"> And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, that Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came, he and all his army, against Jerusalem, and pitched against it, and built forts against it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the city was besieged unto the eleventh year of king Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; Isaiah 3:1; 2 Kings 25:3"> And in the fourth month, in the ninth day of the month, the famine was sore in the city, so that there was no bread for the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4–7; Jeremiah 51:32; 2 Kings 25:4"> Then the city was broken up, and all the men of war fled, and went forth out of the city by night by the way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the king’s garden; (now the Chaldeans were by the city round about:) and they went by the way of the plain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:21; Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 38:23"> But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king, and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho; and all his army was scattered from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5; Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 25:6"> Then they took the king, and carried him up unto the king of Babylon to Riblah in the land of Hamath; where he gave judgment upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; 2 Kings 25:7; Genesis 21:16"> And the king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes: he slew also all the princes of Judah in Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 34:3–5"> Then he put out the eyes of Zedekiah; and the king of Babylon bound him in chains, and carried him to Babylon, and put him in prison till the day of his death.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="52.11 put out: Heb. blinded">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="52.11 chains: or, fetters">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="52.11 prison: Heb. house of the wards">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 25:8; Jeremiah 39:9; Jeremiah 52:29"> Now in the fifth month, in the tenth day of the month, which was the nineteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, came Nebuzar-adan, captain of the guard, which served the king of Babylon, into Jerusalem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="52.12 captain…: or, chief marshal: Heb. chief of the executioners, or, slaughtermen">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="52.12 served: Heb. stood before">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19"> And burned the house of the LORD, and the king’s house; and all the houses of Jerusalem, and all the houses of the great men, burned he with fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; 2 Kings 25:10"> And all the army of the Chaldeans, that were with the captain of the guard, brake down all the walls of Jerusalem round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1–2; Zechariah 14:2"> Then Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard carried away captive certain of the poor of the people, and the residue of the people that remained in the city, and those that fell away, that fell to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 25:12; Jeremiah 39:9–10; Jeremiah 40:5–7"> But Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard left certain of the poor of the land for vinedressers and for husbandmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:19–22; Jeremiah 52:21–23; 2 Chronicles 4:12–15"> Also the pillars of brass that were in the house of the LORD, and the bases, and the brasen sea that was in the house of the LORD, the Chaldeans brake, and carried all the brass of them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Numbers 4:14; 1 Kings 7:45"> The caldrons also, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the bowls, and the spoons, and all the vessels of brass wherewith they ministered, took they away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="52.18 shovels: or, instruments to remove the ashes">g</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="52.18 bowls: or, basons">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49–50; 2 Chronicles 4:6–22; Leviticus 26:12"> And the basons, and the firepans, and the bowls, and the caldrons, and the candlesticks, and the spoons, and the cups; that which was of gold in gold, and that which was of silver in silver, took the captain of the guard away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="52.19 firepans: or, censers">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; 2 Kings 25:16; 2 Chronicles 4:18"> The two pillars, one sea, and twelve brasen bulls that were under the bases, which king Solomon had made in the house of the LORD: the brass of all these vessels was without weight.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="52.20 the brass: Heb. their brass">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:17; 1 Kings 7:15–21; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> And concerning the pillars, the height of one pillar was eighteen cubits; and a fillet of twelve cubits did compass it; and the thickness thereof was four fingers: it was hollow.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="52.21 fillet: Heb. thread">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:25; 2 Kings 25:17; 2 Chronicles 3:15"> And a chapiter of brass was upon it; and the height of one chapiter was five cubits, with network and pomegranates upon the chapiters round about, all of brass. The second pillar also and the pomegranates were like unto these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20"> And there were ninety and six pomegranates on a side; and all the pomegranates upon the network were an hundred round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 25:18; Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 35:4"> And the captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, and Zephaniah the second priest, and the three keepers of the door:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="52.24 door: Heb. threshold">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 25:19; Esther 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> He took also out of the city an eunuch, which had the charge of the men of war; and seven men of them that were near the king’s person, which were found in the city; and the principal scribe of the host, who mustered the people of the land; and threescore men of the people of the land, that were found in the midst of the city.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="52.25 were near…: Heb. saw the face of the king">m</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="52.25 principal…: or, scribe of the captain of the host">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard took them, and brought them to the king of Babylon to Riblah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:28; Isaiah 32:13–14; Isaiah 6:11–12"> And the king of Babylon smote them, and put them to death in Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus Judah was carried away captive out of his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–16; 2 Kings 24:2–3; Daniel 1:1–3"> This is the people whom Nebuchadrezzar carried away captive: in the seventh year three thousand Jews and three and twenty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:12; Genesis 12:5; 2 Kings 25:11"> In the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar he carried away captive from Jerusalem eight hundred thirty and two persons:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="52.29 persons: Heb. souls">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:15; Jeremiah 6:9"> In the three and twentieth year of Nebuchadrezzar Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard carried away captive of the Jews seven hundred forty and five persons: all the persons were four thousand and six hundred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalms 3:3; Genesis 40:13"> And it came to pass in the seven and thirtieth year of the captivity of Jehoiachin king of Judah, in the twelfth month, in the five and twentieth day of the month, that Evil-merodach king of Babylon in the first year of his reign lifted up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah, and brought him forth out of prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 12:25; Daniel 5:18–19; Daniel 2:37"> And spake kindly unto him, and set his throne above the throne of the kings that were with him in Babylon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="52.32 kindly…: Heb. good things with him">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 1 Kings 2:7; Genesis 41:14"> And changed his prison garments: and he did continually eat bread before him all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:10; Luke 11:3; Matthew 6:11"> And for his diet, there was a continual diet given him of the king of Babylon, every day a portion until the day of his death, all the days of his life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="52.34 every…: Heb. the matter of the day in his day">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
