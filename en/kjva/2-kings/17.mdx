<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:30"> In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah began Hoshea the son of Elah to reign in Samaria over Israel nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 13:11; 2 Chronicles 30:5–11"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD, but not as the kings of Israel that were before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 19:36–37"> Against him came up Shalmaneser king of Assyria; and Hoshea became his servant, and gave him presents.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.3 gave: Heb. rendered">a</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="17.3 presents: or, tribute">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; 2 Kings 24:20; Ezekiel 17:13–19"> And the king of Assyria found conspiracy in Hoshea: for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and brought no present to the king of Assyria, as he had done year by year: therefore the king of Assyria shut him up, and bound him in prison.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="17.4 present: or, tribute">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 25:1–3; 2 Kings 18:9"> Then the king of Assyria came up throughout all the land, and went up to Samaria, and besieged it three years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Hosea 13:16; Deuteronomy 28:64"> In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria, and carried Israel away into Assyria, and placed them in Halah and in Habor by the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 23:16; Psalms 106:35–41; Nehemiah 9:26"> For so it was, that the children of Israel had sinned against the LORD their God, which had brought them up out of the land of Egypt, from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and had feared other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:9"> And walked in the statutes of the heathen, whom the LORD cast out from before the children of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, which they had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Job 31:27; Deuteronomy 13:6"> And the children of Israel did secretly those things that were not right against the LORD their God, and they built them high places in all their cities, from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; Micah 5:14; Exodus 34:13"> And they set them up images and groves in every high hill, and under every green tree:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="17.10 images: Heb. statues">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:25; 2 Kings 21:6; Jeremiah 44:17"> And there they burnt incense in all the high places, as did the heathen whom the LORD carried away before them; and wrought wicked things to provoke the LORD to anger:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:4; Exodus 20:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:23–25"> For they served idols, whereof the LORD had said unto them, Ye shall not do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; 1 Samuel 9:9; Jeremiah 18:11"> Yet the LORD testified against Israel, and against Judah, by all the prophets, and by all the seers, saying, Turn ye from your evil ways, and keep my commandments and my statutes, according to all the law which I commanded your fathers, and which I sent to you by my servants the prophets.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="17.13 by: Heb. by the hand of">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24"> Notwithstanding they would not hear, but hardened their necks, like to the neck of their fathers, that did not believe in the LORD their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 1:21–23"> And they rejected his statutes, and his covenant that he made with their fathers, and his testimonies which he testified against them; and they followed vanity, and became vain, and went after the heathen that were round about them, concerning whom the LORD had charged them, that they should not do like them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 14:15"> And they left all the commandments of the LORD their God, and made them molten images, even two calves, and made a grove, and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:6; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> And they caused their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire, and used divination and enchantments, and sold themselves to do evil in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:13; Hosea 11:12"> Therefore the LORD was very angry with Israel, and removed them out of his sight: there was none left but the tribe of Judah only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:22–23; Ezekiel 16:51–52"> Also Judah kept not the commandments of the LORD their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel which they made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Jeremiah 6:30; 2 Kings 13:3"> And the LORD rejected all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of spoilers, until he had cast them out of his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11; 1 Kings 11:31; 1 Kings 14:16"> For he rent Israel from the house of David; and they made Jeroboam the son of Nebat king: and Jeroboam drave Israel from following the LORD, and made them sin a great sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 3:3; 2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11"> For the children of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam which he did; they departed not from them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 17:13; 2 Kings 17:20"> Until the LORD removed Israel out of his sight, as he had said by all his servants the prophets. So was Israel carried away out of their own land to Assyria unto this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:30–31; Matthew 10:5"> And the king of Assyria brought men from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Ava, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and placed them in the cities of Samaria instead of the children of Israel: and they possessed Samaria, and dwelt in the cities thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 17:32; 2 Kings 17:34; 2 Kings 17:41"> And so it was at the beginning of their dwelling there, that they feared not the LORD: therefore the LORD sent lions among them, which slew some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 17:27; 1 Samuel 8:9; Amos 8:14"> Wherefore they spake to the king of Assyria, saying, The nations which thou hast removed, and placed in the cities of Samaria, know not the manner of the God of the land: therefore he hath sent lions among them, and, behold, they slay them, because they know not the manner of the God of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:13; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:2"> Then the king of Assyria commanded, saying, Carry thither one of the priests whom ye brought from thence; and let them go and dwell there, and let him teach them the manner of the God of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 12:29–32; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:14"> Then one of the priests whom they had carried away from Samaria came and dwelt in Beth-el, and taught them how they should fear the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; Micah 4:5; 1 Kings 13:32"> Howbeit every nation made gods of their own, and put them in the houses of the high places which the Samaritans had made, every nation in their cities wherein they dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24"> And the men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, and the men of Cuth made Nergal, and the men of Hamath made Ashima,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 19:37"> And the Avites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burnt their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; 2 Kings 17:29; 1 Kings 13:33"> So they feared the LORD, and made unto themselves of the lowest of them priests of the high places, which sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 17:41; Luke 16:13; Matthew 6:24"> They feared the LORD, and served their own gods, after the manner of the nations whom they carried away from thence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="17.33 whom…: or, who carried them away from thence">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 32:28; Genesis 35:10; 2 Kings 17:27–28"> Unto this day they do after the former manners: they fear not the LORD, neither do they after their statutes, or after their ordinances, or after the law and commandment which the LORD commanded the children of Jacob, whom he named Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 6:10; Joshua 23:16; Joshua 23:7"> With whom the LORD had made a covenant, and charged them, saying, Ye shall not fear other gods, nor bow yourselves to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 6:13; Leviticus 19:32"> But the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great power and a stretched out arm, him shall ye fear, and him shall ye worship, and to him shall ye do sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 19:37; Deuteronomy 5:31–2; Deuteronomy 4:44–45"> And the statutes, and the ordinances, and the law, and the commandment, which he wrote for you, ye shall observe to do for evermore; and ye shall not fear other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:23; Deuteronomy 6:12; Deuteronomy 8:14–18"> And the covenant that I have made with you ye shall not forget; neither shall ye fear other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:74–75; 1 Samuel 12:24; Matthew 10:28"> But the LORD your God ye shall fear; and he shall deliver you out of the hand of all your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:28; 2 Kings 17:8; Jeremiah 13:23"> Howbeit they did not hearken, but they did after their former manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Zephaniah 1:5; 1 Kings 18:21"> So these nations feared the LORD, and served their graven images, both their children, and their children’s children: as did their fathers, so do they unto this day.</span>
</div>
