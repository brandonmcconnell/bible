<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 7:1; 2 Kings 15:38"> In the seventeenth year of Pekah the son of Remaliah Ahaz the son of Jotham king of Judah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:1–4; 2 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 11:4–8"> Twenty years old was Ahaz when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and did not that which was right in the sight of the LORD his God, like David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 18:21; 2 Kings 21:2"> But he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, yea, and made his son to pass through the fire, according to the abominations of the heathen, whom the LORD cast out from before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; 1 Kings 14:23; Jeremiah 17:2"> And he sacrificed and burnt incense in the high places, and on the hills, and under every green tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 8:9–10"> Then Rezin king of Syria and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel came up to Jerusalem to war: and they besieged Ahaz, but could not overcome him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 14:22; Deuteronomy 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26"> At that time Rezin king of Syria recovered Elath to Syria, and drave the Jews from Elath: and the Syrians came to Elath, and dwelt there unto this day.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.6 from Elath: Heb. from Eloth">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Lamentations 4:17; Hosea 14:3"> So Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, saying, I am thy servant and thy son: come up, and save me out of the hand of the king of Syria, and out of the hand of the king of Israel, which rise up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17–18; 2 Chronicles 28:20–21; 2 Chronicles 16:2"> And Ahaz took the silver and gold that was found in the house of the LORD, and in the treasures of the king’s house, and sent it for a present to the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:7; Isaiah 22:6; Amos 1:3–5"> And the king of Assyria hearkened unto him: for the king of Assyria went up against Damascus, and took it, and carried the people of it captive to Kir, and slew Rezin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.9 Damascus: Heb. Dammesek">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Ezekiel 23:16–17; Isaiah 8:2"> And king Ahaz went to Damascus to meet Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and saw an altar that was at Damascus: and king Ahaz sent to Urijah the priest the fashion of the altar, and the pattern of it, according to all the workmanship thereof.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.10 Damascus: Heb. Dammesek">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 2 Chronicles 26:17–18; Hosea 5:11"> And Urijah the priest built an altar according to all that king Ahaz had sent from Damascus: so Urijah the priest made it against king Ahaz came from Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16–19; 2 Chronicles 28:25; 1 Kings 13:1"> And when the king was come from Damascus, the king saw the altar: and the king approached to the altar, and offered thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:1–3"> And he burnt his burnt offering and his meat offering, and poured his drink offering, and sprinkled the blood of his peace offerings, upon the altar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="16.13 his peace offerings: Heb. the peace offerings which were his">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; Exodus 40:6; Exodus 40:29"> And he brought also the brasen altar, which was before the LORD, from the forefront of the house, from between the altar and the house of the LORD, and put it on the north side of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 29:39–41; 1 Kings 8:64; 2 Chronicles 29:21–24"> And king Ahaz commanded Urijah the priest, saying, Upon the great altar burn the morning burnt offering, and the evening meat offering, and the king’s burnt sacrifice, and his meat offering, with the burnt offering of all the people of the land, and their meat offering, and their drink offerings; and sprinkle upon it all the blood of the burnt offering, and all the blood of the sacrifice: and the brasen altar shall be for me to enquire by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 4:19; Acts 5:29; Jude 1:11"> Thus did Urijah the priest, according to all that king Ahaz commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:14–15; Jeremiah 52:20; 2 Chronicles 28:24"> And king Ahaz cut off the borders of the bases, and removed the laver from off them; and took down the sea from off the brasen oxen that were under it, and put it upon a pavement of stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 11:5; 1 Kings 10:5; Ezekiel 46:2"> And the covert for the sabbath that they had built in the house, and the king’s entry without, turned he from the house of the LORD for the king of Assyria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 15:36; 2 Chronicles 28:26; 2 Kings 20:20–21"> Now the rest of the acts of Ahaz which he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 1:9; Isaiah 1:1; 1 Chronicles 3:13"> And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David: and Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
