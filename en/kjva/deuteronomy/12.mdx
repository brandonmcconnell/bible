<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:19–20; Malachi 3:10; Leviticus 27:32–33"> And thither ye shall bring your burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, and your tithes, and heave offerings of your hand, and your vows, and your freewill offerings, and the firstlings of your herds and of your flocks:</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 21:25; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 21:2"> Ye shall not do after all the things that we do here this day, every man whatsoever is right in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Psalms 95:11; Deuteronomy 25:19"> For ye are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance, which the LORD your God giveth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:11; Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 3:27"> But when ye go over Jordan, and dwell in the land which the LORD your God giveth you to inherit, and when he giveth you rest from all your enemies round about, so that ye dwell in safety;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 15:20; Deuteronomy 17:8"> Then there shall be a place which the LORD your God shall choose to cause his name to dwell there; thither shall ye bring all that I command you; your burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, your tithes, and the heave offering of your hand, and all your choice vows which ye vow unto the LORD:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.11 your choice…: Heb. the choice of your vows">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 10:9; Deuteronomy 26:12"> And ye shall rejoice before the LORD your God, ye, and your sons, and your daughters, and your menservants, and your maidservants, and the Levite that is within your gates; forasmuch as he hath no part nor inheritance with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–32; 1 Kings 15:34; 2 Chronicles 15:17"> Take heed to thyself that thou offer not thy burnt offerings in every place that thou seest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; 2 Corinthians 5:19; Deuteronomy 12:5"> But in the place which the LORD shall choose in one of thy tribes, there thou shalt offer thy burnt offerings, and there thou shalt do all that I command thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:5; Deuteronomy 15:22–23; Deuteronomy 14:26"> Notwithstanding thou mayest kill and eat flesh in all thy gates, whatsoever thy soul lusteth after, according to the blessing of the LORD thy God which he hath given thee: the unclean and the clean may eat thereof, as of the roebuck, and as of the hart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:23–24"> Only ye shall not eat the blood; ye shall pour it upon the earth as water.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5"> But thou must eat them before the LORD thy God in the place which the LORD thy God shall choose, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy manservant, and thy maidservant, and the Levite that is within thy gates: and thou shalt rejoice before the LORD thy God in all that thou puttest thine hands unto.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 1 Kings 14:21; Deuteronomy 16:11"> If the place which the LORD thy God hath chosen to put his name there be too far from thee, then thou shalt kill of thy herd and of thy flock, which the LORD hath given thee, as I have commanded thee, and thou shalt eat in thy gates whatsoever thy soul lusteth after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> Even as the roebuck and the hart is eaten, so thou shalt eat them: the unclean and the clean shall eat of them alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 17:13–14"> Only be sure that thou eat not the blood: for the blood is the life; and thou mayest not eat the life with the flesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.23 be…: Heb. be strong">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Numbers 5:9–10; Deuteronomy 12:11"> Only thy holy things which thou hast, and thy vows, thou shalt take, and go unto the place which the LORD shall choose:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 1:9"> And thou shalt offer thy burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood, upon the altar of the LORD thy God: and the blood of thy sacrifices shall be poured out upon the altar of the LORD thy God, and thou shalt eat the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; John 15:14; John 15:10"> Observe and hear all these words which I command thee, that it may go well with thee, and with thy children after thee for ever, when thou doest that which is good and right in the sight of the LORD thy God.</span>
</div>
