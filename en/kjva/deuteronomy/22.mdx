<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In like manner shalt thou do with his ass; and so shalt thou do with his raiment; and with all lost thing of thy brother’s, which he hath lost, and thou hast found, shalt thou do likewise: thou mayest not hide thyself.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> But thou shalt in any wise let the dam go, and take the young to thee; that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> Thou shalt not sow thy vineyard with divers seeds: lest the fruit of thy seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of thy vineyard, be defiled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.9 fruit of thy seed: Heb. fulness of the seed">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> And give occasions of speech against her, and bring up an evil name upon her, and say, I took this woman, and when I came to her, I found her not a maid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then shall the father of the damsel, and her mother, take and bring forth the tokens of the damsel’s virginity unto the elders of the city in the gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the damsel’s father shall say unto the elders, I gave my daughter unto this man to wife, and he hateth her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And, lo, he hath given occasions of speech against her, saying, I found not thy daughter a maid; and yet these are the tokens of my daughter’s virginity. And they shall spread the cloth before the elders of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> And the elders of that city shall take that man and chastise him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> And they shall amerce him in an hundred shekels of silver, and give them unto the father of the damsel, because he hath brought up an evil name upon a virgin of Israel: and she shall be his wife; he may not put her away all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if this thing be true, and the tokens of virginity be not found for the damsel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> Then they shall bring out the damsel to the door of her father’s house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones that she die: because she hath wrought folly in Israel, to play the whore in her father’s house: so shalt thou put evil away from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If a damsel that is a virgin be betrothed unto an husband, and a man find her in the city, and lie with her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> Then ye shall bring them both out unto the gate of that city, and ye shall stone them with stones that they die; the damsel, because she cried not, being in the city; and the man, because he hath humbled his neighbour’s wife: so thou shalt put away evil from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> But if a man find a betrothed damsel in the field, and the man force her, and lie with her: then the man only that lay with her shall die:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.25 force: or, take strong hold of">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> But unto the damsel thou shalt do nothing; there is in the damsel no sin worthy of death: for as when a man riseth against his neighbour, and slayeth him, even so is this matter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> For he found her in the field, and the betrothed damsel cried, and there was none to save her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> Then the man that lay with her shall give unto the damsel’s father fifty shekels of silver, and she shall be his wife; because he hath humbled her, he may not put her away all his days.</span>
</div>
