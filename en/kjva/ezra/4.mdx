<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Proverbs 25:2; Deuteronomy 13:14"> And I commanded, and search hath been made, and it is found that this city of old time hath made insurrection against kings, and that rebellion and sedition have been made therein.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="4.19 I commanded: Chaldee, by me a decree is set">p</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="4.19 made insurrection: Chaldee, lifted up itself">q</a></sup></span>
