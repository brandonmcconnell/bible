<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> I know it is so of a truth: but how should man be just with God?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.2 with God: or, before God?">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he will contend with him, he cannot answer him one of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength: who hath hardened himself against him, and hath prospered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> Which removeth the mountains, and they know not: which overturneth them in his anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Which shaketh the earth out of her place, and the pillars thereof tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Which commandeth the sun, and it riseth not; and sealeth up the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Which alone spreadeth out the heavens, and treadeth upon the waves of the sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.8 waves: Heb. heights">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Which maketh Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades, and the chambers of the south.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.9 Arcturus…: Heb. Ash, Cesil, and Cimah">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Which doeth great things past finding out; yea, and wonders without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Lo, he goeth by me, and I see him not: he passeth on also, but I perceive him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Behold, he taketh away, who can hinder him? who will say unto him, What doest thou?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.12 hinder…: Heb. turn him away?">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> If God will not withdraw his anger, the proud helpers do stoop under him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.13 proud…: Heb. helpers of pride, or, strength">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less shall I answer him, and choose out my words to reason with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Whom, though I were righteous, yet would I not answer, but I would make supplication to my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> If I had called, and he had answered me; yet would I not believe that he had hearkened unto my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> For he breaketh me with a tempest, and multiplieth my wounds without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He will not suffer me to take my breath, but filleth me with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If I speak of strength, lo, he is strong: and if of judgment, who shall set me a time to plead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I justify myself, mine own mouth shall condemn me: if I say, I am perfect, it shall also prove me perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Though I were perfect, yet would I not know my soul: I would despise my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> This is one thing, therefore I said it, He destroyeth the perfect and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If the scourge slay suddenly, he will laugh at the trial of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> The earth is given into the hand of the wicked: he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, and who is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> Now my days are swifter than a post: they flee away, they see no good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They are passed away as the swift ships: as the eagle that hasteth to the prey.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.26 swift…: or, ships of Ebeh: Heb. ships of desire">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say, I will forget my complaint, I will leave off my heaviness, and comfort myself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I am afraid of all my sorrows, I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> If I be wicked, why then labour I in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I wash myself with snow water, and make my hands never so clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Yet shalt thou plunge me in the ditch, and mine own clothes shall abhor me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.31 abhor…: or, make me to be abhorred">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him, and we should come together in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> Neither is there any daysman betwixt us, that might lay his hand upon us both.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.33 any…: Heb. one that should argue">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="9.33 daysman: or, umpire">i</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then would I speak, and not fear him; but it is not so with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="9.35 it is…: Heb. I am not so with myself">j</a></sup></span>
