<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in the days of my youth, when the secret of God was upon my tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When the Almighty was yet with me, when my children were about me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When I washed my steps with butter, and the rock poured me out rivers of oil;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="29.6 me: Heb. with me">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and the aged arose, and stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> The princes refrained talking, and laid their hand on their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The nobles held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="29.10 The nobles…: Heb. The voice of the nobles was hid">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> When the ear heard me, then it blessed me; and when the eye saw me, it gave witness to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> Because I delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, and him that had none to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me: and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I put on righteousness, and it clothed me: my judgment was as a robe and a diadem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was a father to the poor: and the cause which I knew not I searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> And I brake the jaws of the wicked, and plucked the spoil out of his teeth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="29.17 the jaws: Heb. the jawteeth, or, the grinders">e</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="29.17 plucked: Heb. cast">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> Then I said, I shall die in my nest, and I shall multiply my days as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root was spread out by the waters, and the dew lay all night upon my branch.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="29.19 spread…: Heb. opened">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory was fresh in me, and my bow was renewed in my hand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="29.20 fresh: Heb. new">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="29.20 renewed: Heb. changed">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> Unto me men gave ear, and waited, and kept silence at my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After my words they spake not again; and my speech dropped upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> And they waited for me as for the rain; and they opened their mouth wide as for the latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> If I laughed on them, they believed it not; and the light of my countenance they cast not down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I chose out their way, and sat chief, and dwelt as a king in the army, as one that comforteth the mourners.</span>
