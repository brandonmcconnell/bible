<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> That the wicked is reserved to the day of destruction? they shall be brought forth to the day of wrath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="21.30 wrath: Heb. wraths">k</a></sup></span>
