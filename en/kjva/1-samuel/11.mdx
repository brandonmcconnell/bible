<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> And, behold, Saul came after the herd out of the field; and Saul said, What aileth the people that they weep? And they told him the tidings of the men of Jabesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> And the Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard those tidings, and his anger was kindled greatly.</span>

And the fear of the LORD fell on the people, and they came out with one consent.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.7 with…: Heb. as one man">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> And when he numbered them in Bezek, the children of Israel were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:17"> And they said unto the messengers that came, Thus shall ye say unto the men of Jabesh-gilead, To morrow, by that time the sun be hot, ye shall have help. And the messengers came and shewed it to the men of Jabesh; and they were glad.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.9 help: or, deliverance">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> And it was so on the morrow, that Saul put the people in three companies; and they came into the midst of the host in the morning watch, and slew the Ammonites until the heat of the day: and it came to pass, that they which remained were scattered, so that two of them were not left together.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> And Saul said, There shall not a man be put to death this day: for to day the LORD hath wrought salvation in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> Then said Samuel to the people, Come, and let us go to Gilgal, and renew the kingdom there.</span>
