But his armourbearer would not; for he was sore afraid. Therefore Saul took a sword, and fell upon it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="31.4 abuse…: or, mock me">d</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Leviticus 26:32; Leviticus 26:36"> And when the men of Israel that were on the other side of the valley, and they that were on the other side Jordan, saw that the men of Israel fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, they forsook the cities, and fled; and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:25; 1 Chronicles 10:8"> And it came to pass on the morrow, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, that they found Saul and his three sons fallen in mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Judges 16:23–24; 1 Samuel 17:54"> And they cut off his head, and stripped off his armour, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to publish it in the house of their idols, and among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 21:9"> And they put his armour in the house of Ashtaroth: and they fastened his body to the wall of Beth-shan.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4"> And when the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard of that which the Philistines had done to Saul;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="31.11 of that: or, concerning him that">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:12–14; Genesis 50:10; 1 Samuel 22:6"> And they took their bones, and buried them under a tree at Jabesh, and fasted seven days.</span>
