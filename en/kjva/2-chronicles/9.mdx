<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 3:12; Mark 4:34"> And Solomon told her all her questions: and there was nothing hid from Solomon which he told her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–4; 1 Kings 10:3; 1 Kings 6:1–7"> And when the queen of Sheba had seen the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 12:37; 1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 143:7"> And the meat of his table, and the sitting of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers, and their apparel; his cupbearers also, and their apparel; and his ascent by which he went up into the house of the LORD; there was no more spirit in her.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.4 cupbearers: or, butlers">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 10:6"> And she said to the king, It was a true report which I heard in mine own land of thine acts, and of thy wisdom:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.5 report: Heb. word">b</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.5 acts: or, sayings">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 10:7; 2 Chronicles 9:5; 1 Kings 4:34"> Howbeit I believed not their words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it: and, behold, the one half of the greatness of thy wisdom was not told me: for thou exceedest the fame that I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:21"> Happy are thy men, and happy are these thy servants, which stand continually before thee, and hear thy wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11; 1 Chronicles 29:23; Isaiah 32:1–2"> Blessed be the LORD thy God, which delighted in thee to set thee on his throne, to be king for the LORD thy God: because thy God loved Israel, to establish them for ever, therefore made he thee king over them, to do judgment and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> And she gave the king an hundred and twenty talents of gold, and of spices great abundance, and precious stones: neither was there any such spice as the queen of Sheba gave king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:18; 1 Kings 10:11; 1 Kings 10:22"> And the servants also of Huram, and the servants of Solomon, which brought gold from Ophir, brought algum trees and precious stones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.10 algum…: also called, almug trees">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 10:12; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Psalms 92:1–3"> And the king made of the algum trees terraces to the house of the LORD, and to the king’s palace, and harps and psalteries for singers: and there were none such seen before in the land of Judah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.11 terraces: or, stairs: Heb. highways">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:13; Ephesians 3:20; Psalms 20:4"> And king Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desire, whatsoever she asked, beside that which she had brought unto the king. So she turned, and went away to her own land, she and her servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:15; Psalms 68:29; 1 Kings 10:14–28"> Now the weight of gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and threescore and six talents of gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:29; Psalms 72:10"> Beside that which chapmen and merchants brought. And all the kings of Arabia and governors of the country brought gold and silver to Solomon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.14 governors: or, captains">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And king Solomon made two hundred targets of beaten gold: six hundred shekels of beaten gold went to one target.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2"> And three hundred shields made he of beaten gold: three hundred shekels of gold went to one shield. And the king put them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:18–20; Psalms 45:8; Revelation 20:11"> Moreover the king made a great throne of ivory, and overlaid it with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Revelation 5:5; Numbers 23:24"> And there were six steps to the throne, with a footstool of gold, which were fastened to the throne, and stays on each side of the sitting place, and two lions standing by the stays:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.18 stays: Heb. hands">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 21:12"> And twelve lions stood there on the one side and on the other upon the six steps. There was not the like made in any kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; Esther 1:7; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And all the drinking vessels of king Solomon were of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were of pure gold: none were of silver; it was not any thing accounted of in the days of Solomon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.20 pure: Heb. shut up">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="9.20 none were of silver: or, there was no silver in them">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Job 39:13; 1 Kings 10:22"> For the king’s ships went to Tarshish with the servants of Huram: every three years once came the ships of Tarshish bringing gold, and silver, ivory, and apes, and peacocks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="9.21 ivory: or, elephants’ teeth">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; Psalms 89:27; 1 Kings 4:30–31"> And king Solomon passed all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:5; James 3:17; Proverbs 2:6"> And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom, that God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 10:25; Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10"> And they brought every man his present, vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and raiment, harness, and spices, horses, and mules, a rate year by year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> And Solomon had four thousand stalls for horses and chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen; whom he bestowed in the chariot cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 4:21; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:24"> And he reigned over all the kings from the river even unto the land of the Philistines, and to the border of Egypt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="9.26 river: that is, Euphrates">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the king made silver in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar trees made he as the sycomore trees that are in the low plains in abundance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="9.27 made silver: Heb. gave silver">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:16; 1 Kings 10:28; Isaiah 31:1"> And they brought unto Solomon horses out of Egypt, and out of all lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 11:41–43; 1 Kings 11:29"> Now the rest of the acts of Solomon, first and last, are they not written in the book of Nathan the prophet, and in the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the seer against Jeroboam the son of Nebat?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="9.29 book: Heb. words">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 11:42–43"> And Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Solomon slept with his fathers, and he was buried in the city of David his father: and Rehoboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
