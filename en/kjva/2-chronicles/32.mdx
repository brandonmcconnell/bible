<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> He took counsel with his princes and his mighty men to stop the waters of the fountains which were without the city: and they did help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 2 Kings 18:9; 1 Kings 19:21"> So there was gathered much people together, who stopped all the fountains, and the brook that ran through the midst of the land, saying, Why should the kings of Assyria come, and find much water?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="32.4 ran: Heb. overflowed">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22; Nehemiah 8:16"> And he set captains of war over the people, and gathered them together to him in the street of the gate of the city, and spake comfortably to them, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="32.6 spake…: Heb. he spoke to their heart">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Isaiah 41:10"> With him is an arm of flesh; but with us is the LORD our God to help us, and to fight our battles. </div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Joshua 10:31; Isaiah 36:2"> After this did Sennacherib king of Assyria send his servants to Jerusalem, (but he himself laid siege against Lachish, and all his power with him,) unto Hezekiah king of Judah, and unto all Judah that were at Jerusalem, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="32.9 power: Heb. dominion">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:19; Isaiah 36:4"> Thus saith Sennacherib king of Assyria, Whereon do ye trust, that ye abide in the siege in Jerusalem?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="32.10 siege: or, strong hold">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:43; Psalms 11:1–3; Isaiah 36:18"> Doth not Hezekiah persuade you to give over yourselves to die by famine and by thirst, saying, The LORD our God shall deliver us out of the hand of the king of Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Deuteronomy 12:26–27"> Hath not the same Hezekiah taken away his high places and his altars, and commanded Judah and Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall worship before one altar, and burn incense upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 44:8–10; Daniel 4:30"> Know ye not what I and my fathers have done unto all the people of other lands? were the gods of the nations of those lands any ways able to deliver their lands out of mine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:11–12; Exodus 15:9–11; Isaiah 42:8"> Who was there among all the gods of those nations that my fathers utterly destroyed, that could deliver his people out of mine hand, that your God should be able to deliver you out of mine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 36:18"> Now therefore let not Hezekiah deceive you, nor persuade you on this manner, neither yet believe him: for no god of any nation or kingdom was able to deliver his people out of mine hand, and out of the hand of my fathers: how much less shall your God deliver you out of mine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 73:9; John 15:21; Job 15:25–26"> And his servants spake yet more against the LORD God, and against his servant Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; 2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:28–29"> He wrote also letters to rail on the LORD God of Israel, and to speak against him, saying, As the gods of the nations of other lands have not delivered their people out of mine hand, so shall not the God of Hezekiah deliver his people out of mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 2 Kings 18:26–28; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Then they cried with a loud voice in the Jews’ speech unto the people of Jerusalem that were on the wall, to affright them, and to trouble them; that they might take the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 2:8; Jeremiah 32:30"> And they spake against the God of Jerusalem, as against the gods of the people of the earth, which were the work of the hands of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–15; Isaiah 37:14–20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And for this cause Hezekiah the king, and the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz, prayed and cried to heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Psalms 76:5; Psalms 18:50"> And the LORD sent an angel, which cut off all the mighty men of valour, and the leaders and captains in the camp of the king of Assyria. So he returned with shame of face to his own land. And when he was come into the house of his god, they that came forth of his own bowels slew him there with the sword.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="32.21 slew…: Heb. made him fall">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4–5; Psalms 18:48–50; 2 Thessalonians 3:5"> Thus the LORD saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib the king of Assyria, and from the hand of all other, and guided them on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; Matthew 2:11; 2 Chronicles 1:1"> And many brought gifts unto the LORD to Jerusalem, and presents to Hezekiah king of Judah: so that he was magnified in the sight of all nations from thenceforth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="32.23 presents: Heb. precious things">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Kings 20:1–11; Isaiah 38:21–22"> In those days Hezekiah was sick to the death, and prayed unto the LORD: and he spake unto him, and he gave him a sign.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="32.24 gave…: or, wrought a miracle for him">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:18; 2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> But Hezekiah rendered not again according to the benefit done unto him; for his heart was lifted up: therefore there was wrath upon him, and upon Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18–19; 2 Chronicles 34:27–28; 2 Chronicles 33:12"> Notwithstanding Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, both he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of the LORD came not upon them in the days of Hezekiah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="32.26 pride: Heb. lifting up">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 1:12; Proverbs 10:22"> And Hezekiah had exceeding much riches and honour: and he made himself treasuries for silver, and for gold, and for precious stones, and for spices, and for shields, and for all manner of pleasant jewels;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="32.27 pleasant…: Heb. instruments of desire">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Kings 4:26"> Storehouses also for the increase of corn, and wine, and oil; and stalls for all manner of beasts, and cotes for flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:12; Proverbs 10:22; 1 Samuel 2:7"> Moreover he provided him cities, and possessions of flocks and herds in abundance: for God had given him substance very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Isaiah 22:9–11; Psalms 1:1–3"> This same Hezekiah also stopped the upper watercourse of Gihon, and brought it straight down to the west side of the city of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Proverbs 17:3; Genesis 22:1"> Howbeit in the business of the ambassadors of the princes of Babylon, who sent unto him to enquire of the wonder that was done in the land, God left him, to try him, that he might know all that was in his heart.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="32.31 ambassadors: Heb. interpreters">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
