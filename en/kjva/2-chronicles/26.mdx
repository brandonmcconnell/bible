<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> Then all the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in the room of his father Amaziah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="26.1 Uzziah: or, Azariah">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 2 Chronicles 25:28; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> He built Eloth, and restored it to Judah, after that the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Sixteen years old was Uzziah when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty and two years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the LORD, according to all that his father Amaziah did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 24:2; 1 Chronicles 22:13"> And he sought God in the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the visions of God: and as long as he sought the LORD, God made him to prosper.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="26.5 in the visions…: Heb. in the seeing of God">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 5:1"> And he went forth and warred against the Philistines, and brake down the wall of Gath, and the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod, and built cities about Ashdod, and among the Philistines.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="26.6 about…: or, in the country of Ashdod">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16; 1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And God helped him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians that dwelt in Gur-baal, and the Mehunims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; 2 Chronicles 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> And the Ammonites gave gifts to Uzziah: and his name spread abroad even to the entering in of Egypt; for he strengthened himself exceedingly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="26.8 spread…: Heb. went">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Kings 14:13"> Moreover Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the corner gate, and at the valley gate, and at the turning of the wall, and fortified them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="26.9 fortified: or, repaired">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; 2 Kings 3:4"> Also he built towers in the desert, and digged many wells: for he had much cattle, both in the low country, and in the plains: husbandmen also, and vine dressers in the mountains, and in Carmel: for he loved husbandry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="26.10 digged…: or, cut out many cisterns">f</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="26.10 Carmel: or, fruitful fields">g</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="26.10 husbandry: Heb. ground">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2"> Moreover Uzziah had an host of fighting men, that went out to war by bands, according to the number of their account by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the ruler, under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king’s captains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The whole number of the chief of the fathers of the mighty men of valour were two thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And under their hand was an army, three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred, that made war with mighty power, to help the king against the enemy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="26.13 an army: Heb. the power of an army">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> And Uzziah prepared for them throughout all the host shields, and spears, and helmets, and habergeons, and bows, and slings to cast stones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="26.14 slings…: Heb. stones of slings">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And he made in Jerusalem engines, invented by cunning men, to be on the towers and upon the bulwarks, to shoot arrows and great stones withal. And his name spread far abroad; for he was marvellously helped, till he was strong.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="26.15 spread: Heb. went forth">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed against the LORD his God, and went into the temple of the LORD to burn incense upon the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:10; 1 Chronicles 26:6; 1 Chronicles 12:28"> And Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him fourscore priests of the LORD, that were valiant men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> And they withstood Uzziah the king, and said unto him, It appertaineth not unto thee, Uzziah, to burn incense unto the LORD, but to the priests the sons of Aaron, that are consecrated to burn incense: go out of the sanctuary; for thou hast trespassed; neither shall it be for thine honour from the LORD God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> Then Uzziah was wroth, and had a censer in his hand to burn incense: and while he was wroth with the priests, the leprosy even rose up in his forehead before the priests in the house of the LORD, from beside the incense altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> And Azariah the chief priest, and all the priests, looked upon him, and, behold, he was leprous in his forehead, and they thrust him out from thence; yea, himself hasted also to go out, because the LORD had smitten him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; 2 Kings 7:3"> And Uzziah the king was a leper unto the day of his death, and dwelt in a several house, being a leper; for he was cut off from the house of the LORD: and Jotham his son was over the king’s house, judging the people of the land.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="26.21 several: Heb. free">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> Now the rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, did Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 26:18"> So Uzziah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial which belonged to the kings; for they said, He is a leper: and Jotham his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
