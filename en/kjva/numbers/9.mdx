<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Exodus 40:2"> And the LORD spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 35:1"> Let the children of Israel also keep the passover at his appointed season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–11; Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> In the fourteenth day of this month, at even, ye shall keep it in his appointed season: according to all the rites of it, and according to all the ceremonies thereof, shall ye keep it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.3 at even: Heb. between the two evenings">a</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:2; Exodus 18:15; Numbers 5:2"> And there were certain men, who were defiled by the dead body of a man, that they could not keep the passover on that day: and they came before Moses and before Aaron on that day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Exodus 12:27"> And those men said unto him, We are defiled by the dead body of a man: wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer an offering of the LORD in his appointed season among the children of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 85:8; Numbers 27:5; 1 Corinthians 4:4"> And Moses said unto them, Stand still, and I will hear what the LORD will command concerning you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If any man of you or of your posterity shall be unclean by reason of a dead body, or be in a journey afar off, yet he shall keep the passover unto the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2–15; Exodus 12:43–49; John 19:36"> The fourteenth day of the second month at even they shall keep it, and eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 19:36; Exodus 12:46; Exodus 12:10"> They shall leave none of it unto the morning, nor break any bone of it: according to all the ordinances of the passover they shall keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15; Numbers 9:7"> But the man that is clean, and is not in a journey, and forbeareth to keep the passover, even the same soul shall be cut off from among his people: because he brought not the offering of the LORD in his appointed season, that man shall bear his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:48–49; Leviticus 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> And if a stranger shall sojourn among you, and will keep the passover unto the LORD; according to the ordinance of the passover, and according to the manner thereof, so shall he do: ye shall have one ordinance, both for the stranger, and for him that was born in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Exodus 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> And on the day that the tabernacle was reared up the cloud covered the tabernacle, namely, the tent of the testimony: and at even there was upon the tabernacle as it were the appearance of fire, until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Exodus 40:38; 1 Corinthians 10:1"> So it was alway: the cloud covered it by day, and the appearance of fire by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 40:36–38; Psalms 32:8; John 10:9"> And when the cloud was taken up from the tabernacle, then after that the children of Israel journeyed: and in the place where the cloud abode, there the children of Israel pitched their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1; 2 John 1:6; Exodus 17:1"> At the commandment of the LORD the children of Israel journeyed, and at the commandment of the LORD they pitched: as long as the cloud abode upon the tabernacle they rested in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Numbers 1:52–53; Numbers 3:8"> And when the cloud tarried long upon the tabernacle many days, then the children of Israel kept the charge of the LORD, and journeyed not.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.19 tarried…: Heb. prolonged">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And so it was, when the cloud was a few days upon the tabernacle; according to the commandment of the LORD they abode in their tents, and according to the commandment of the LORD they journeyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> And so it was, when the cloud abode from even unto the morning, and that the cloud was taken up in the morning, then they journeyed: whether it was by day or by night that the cloud was taken up, they journeyed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.21 abode: Heb. was">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Exodus 40:36–37; Exodus 40:16"> Or whether it were two days, or a month, or a year, that the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle, remaining thereon, the children of Israel abode in their tents, and journeyed not: but when it was taken up, they journeyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 22:3; Numbers 9:19; Genesis 26:5"> At the commandment of the LORD they rested in the tents, and at the commandment of the LORD they journeyed: they kept the charge of the LORD, at the commandment of the LORD by the hand of Moses.</span>
