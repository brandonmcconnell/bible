<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> And the LORD’s anger was kindled against Israel, and he made them wander in the wilderness forty years, until all the generation, that had done evil in the sight of the LORD, was consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalms 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> And, behold, ye are risen up in your fathers’ stead, an increase of sinful men, to augment yet the fierce anger of the LORD toward Israel.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> But thy servants will pass over, every man armed for war, before the LORD to battle, as my lord saith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> So concerning them Moses commanded Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the chief fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Moses said unto them, If the children of Gad and the children of Reuben will pass with you over Jordan, every man armed to battle, before the LORD, and the land shall be subdued before you; then ye shall give them the land of Gilead for a possession:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they will not pass over with you armed, they shall have possessions among you in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the children of Gad and the children of Reuben answered, saying, As the LORD hath said unto thy servants, so will we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will pass over armed before the LORD into the land of Canaan, that the possession of our inheritance on this side Jordan may be ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> And Moses gave unto them, even to the children of Gad, and to the children of Reuben, and unto half the tribe of Manasseh the son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land, with the cities thereof in the coasts, even the cities of the country round about.</span>
</div>
