<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:15"> What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:9; Colossians 3:3; Romans 7:4"> God forbid. How shall we, that are dead to sin, live any longer therein?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:27; Galatians 2:20–21; Romans 6:8"> Know ye not, that so many of us as were baptized into Jesus Christ were baptized into his death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:22–24"> Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:10–11; 2 Corinthians 4:10; Colossians 2:12"> For if we have been planted together in the likeness of his death, we shall be also in the likeness of his resurrection:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:24"> Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:1; Colossians 3:1–3; Romans 7:4"> For he that is dead is freed from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:11–12; Colossians 3:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:10–14"> Now if we be dead with Christ, we believe that we shall also live with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Revelation 1:18; Acts 2:24–28"> Knowing that Christ being raised from the dead dieth no more; death hath no more dominion over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 4:6; 1 Peter 3:18"> For in that he died, he died unto sin once: but in that he liveth, he liveth unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Romans 6:2"> Likewise reckon ye also yourselves to be dead indeed unto sin, but alive unto God through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 6:16; Romans 8:13"> Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body, that ye should obey it in the lusts thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 6:16; Colossians 3:5"> Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members as instruments of righteousness unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Romans 8:2; Romans 7:4–11"> For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; Ephesians 2:8–10; Romans 6:1–2"> What then? shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace? God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 8:34; Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15"> Know ye not, that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey; whether of sin unto death, or of obedience unto righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:13; Romans 1:8"> But God be thanked, that ye were the servants of sin, but ye have obeyed from the heart that form of doctrine which was delivered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:22; Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:1"> Being then made free from sin, ye became the servants of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 6:13; 1 Peter 4:2–4; Colossians 3:5–7"> I speak after the manner of men because of the infirmity of your flesh: for as ye have yielded your members servants to uncleanness and to iniquity unto iniquity; even so now yield your members servants to righteousness unto holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:16–17; John 8:34"> For when ye were the servants of sin, ye were free from righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 7:5; Romans 6:23"> What fruit had ye then in those things whereof ye are now ashamed? for the end of those things is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Romans 6:18; Romans 8:2"> But now being made free from sin, and become servants to God, ye have your fruit unto holiness, and the end everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:36; Romans 5:12; James 1:15"> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
