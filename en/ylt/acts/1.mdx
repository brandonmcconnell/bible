<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> The former account, indeed, I made concerning all things, O Theophilus, that Jesus began both to do and to teach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> till the day in which, having given command, through the Holy Spirit, to the apostles whom he did choose out, he was taken up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> to whom also he did present himself alive after his suffering, in many certain proofs, through forty days being seen by them, and speaking the things concerning the reign of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> And being assembled together with them, he commanded them not to depart from Jerusalem, but to wait for the promise of the Father, which, &#91;saith he,&#93; 'Ye did hear of me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> because John, indeed, baptized with water, and ye shall be baptized with the Holy Spirit — after not many days.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> They, therefore, indeed, having come together, were questioning him, saying, 'Lord, dost thou at this time restore the reign to Israel?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> and he said unto them, 'It is not yours to know times or seasons that the Father did appoint in His own authority;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> but ye shall receive power at the coming of the Holy Spirit upon you, and ye shall be witnesses to me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judea, and Samaria, and unto the end of the earth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> And these things having said — they beholding — he was taken up, and a cloud did receive him up from their sight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> and as they were looking stedfastly to the heaven in his going on, then, lo, two men stood by them in white apparel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> who also said, 'Men, Galileans, why do ye stand gazing into the heaven? this Jesus who was received up from you into the heaven, shall so come in what manner ye saw him going on to the heaven.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then did they return to Jerusalem from the mount that is called of Olives, that is near Jerusalem, a sabbath's journey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> and when they came in, they went up to the upper room, where were abiding both Peter, and James, and John, and Andrew, Philip, and Thomas, Bartholomew, and Matthew, James, of Alphaeus, and Simon the Zelotes, and Judas, of James;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> these all were continuing with one accord in prayer and supplication, with women, and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> And in these days, Peter having risen up in the midst of the disciples, said, (the multitude also of the names at the same place was, as it were, an hundred and twenty,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–15"> 'Men, brethren, it behoved this Writing that it be fulfilled that beforehand the Holy Spirit spake through the mouth of David, concerning Judas, who became guide to those who took Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> because he was numbered among us, and did receive the share in this ministration,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalms 55:23"> this one, indeed, then, purchased a field out of the reward of unrighteousness, and falling headlong, burst asunder in the midst, and all his bowels gushed forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> and it became known to all those dwelling in Jerusalem, insomuch that that place is called, in their proper dialect, Aceldama, that is, field of blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 69:25; Psalms 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> for it hath been written in the book of Psalms: Let his lodging-place become desolate, and let no one be dwelling in it, and his oversight let another take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> 'It behoveth, therefore, of the men who did go with us during all the time in which the Lord Jesus went in and went out among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> beginning from the baptism of John, unto the day in which he was received up from us, one of these to become with us a witness of his rising again.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> And they set two, Joseph called Barsabas, who was surnamed Justus, and Matthias,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> and having prayed, they said, 'Thou, Lord, who art knowing the heart of all, shew which one thou didst choose of these two</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to receive the share of this ministration and apostleship, from which Judas, by transgression, did fall, to go on to his proper place;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> and they gave their lots, and the lot fell upon Matthias, and he was numbered with the eleven apostles.</span>
