<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> And as they are speaking unto the people, there came to them the priests, and the magistrate of the temple, and the Sadducees —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> being grieved because of their teaching the people, and preaching in Jesus the rising again out of the dead —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> and they laid hands upon them, and did put them in custody unto the morrow, for it was evening already;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> and many of those hearing the word did believe, and the number of the men became, as it were, five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> And it came to pass upon the morrow, there were gathered together of them the rulers, and elders, and scribes, to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> and Annas the chief priest, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and as many as were of the kindred of the chief priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> and having set them in the midst, they were inquiring, 'In what power, or in what name did ye do this?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, having been filled with the Holy Spirit, said unto them: 'Rulers of the people, and elders of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> if we to-day are examined concerning the good deed to the ailing man, by whom he hath been saved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> be it known to all of you, and to all the people of Israel, that in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom ye did crucify, whom God did raise out of the dead, in him hath this one stood by before you whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> 'This is the stone that was set at nought by you — the builders, that became head of a corner;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> and there is not salvation in any other, for there is no other name under the heaven that hath been given among men, in which it behoveth us to be saved.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> And beholding the openness of Peter and John, and having perceived that they are men unlettered and plebeian, they were wondering — they were taking knowledge also of them that with Jesus they had been —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> and seeing the man standing with them who hath been healed, they had nothing to say against &#91;it&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> and having commanded them to go away out of the sanhedrim, they took counsel with one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> saying, 'What shall we do to these men? because that, indeed, a notable sign hath been done through them, to all those dwelling in Jerusalem &#91;is&#93; manifest, and we are not able to deny &#91;it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> but that it may spread no further toward the people, let us strictly threaten them no more to speak in this name to any man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> And having called them, they charged them not to speak at all, nor to teach, in the name of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> and Peter and John answering unto them said, 'Whether it is righteous before God to hearken to you rather than to God, judge ye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> for we cannot but speak what we did see and hear.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> And they having further threatened &#91;them&#93;, let them go, finding nothing how they may punish them, because of the people, because all were glorifying God for that which hath been done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> for above forty years of age was the man upon whom had been done this sign of the healing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalms 119:63"> And being let go, they went unto their own friends, and declared whatever the chief priests and the elders said unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> and they having heard, with one accord did lift up the voice unto God, and said, 'Lord, thou &#91;art&#93; God, who didst make the heaven, and the earth, and the sea, and all that &#91;are&#93; in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> who, through the mouth of David thy servant, did say, Why did nations rage, and peoples meditate vain things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> the kings of the earth stood up, and the rulers were gathered together against the Lord and against His Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> for gathered together of a truth against Thy holy child Jesus, whom Thou didst anoint, were both Herod and Pontius Pilate, with nations and peoples of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> to do whatever Thy hand and Thy counsel did determine before to come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> 'And now, Lord, look upon their threatenings, and grant to Thy servants with all freedom to speak Thy word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> in the stretching forth of Thy hand, for healing, and signs, and wonders, to come to pass through the name of Thy holy child Jesus.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> And they having prayed, the place was shaken in which they were gathered together, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and were speaking the word of God with freedom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> and of the multitude of those who did believe the heart and the soul was one, and not one was saying that anything of the things he had was his own, but all things were to them in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> And with great power were the apostles giving the testimony to the rising again of the Lord Jesus, great grace also was on them all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> for there was not any one among them who did lack, for as many as were possessors of fields, or houses, selling &#91;them&#93;, were bringing the prices of the thing sold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and were laying them at the feet of the apostles, and distribution was being made to each according as any one had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> And Joses, who was surnamed by the apostles Barnabas — which is, having been interpreted, Son of Comfort — a Levite, of Cyprus by birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> a field being his, having sold &#91;it&#93;, brought the money and laid &#91;it&#93; at the feet of the apostles.</span>
