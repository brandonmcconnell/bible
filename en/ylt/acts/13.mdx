<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:35; Acts 11:22–27"> And there were certain in Antioch, in the assembly there, prophets and teachers; both Barnabas, and Simeon who is called Niger, and Lucius the Cyrenian, Manaen also — Herod the tetrarch's foster-brother — and Saul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> and in their ministering to the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, 'Separate ye to me both Barnabas and Saul to the work to which I have called them,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 2:2; Acts 13:2"> then having fasted, and having prayed, and having laid the hands on them, they sent &#91;them&#93; away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 13:2; Acts 11:19"> These, indeed, then, having been sent forth by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia, thence also they sailed to Cyprus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Acts 13:46; Acts 14:1"> and having come unto Salamis, they declared the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews, and they had also John &#91;as&#93; a ministrant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 1 John 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> and having gone through the island unto Paphos, they found a certain magian, a false prophet, a Jew, whose name &#91;is&#93; Bar-Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:38; Acts 13:12; Acts 18:12"> who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man; this one having called for Barnabas and Saul, did desire to hear the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Acts 13:6–7; 2 Timothy 4:14–15"> and there withstood them Elymas the magian — for so is his name interpreted — seeking to pervert the proconsul from the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 3:8; Acts 4:8; Acts 2:4"> And Saul — who also &#91;is&#93; Paul — having been filled with the Holy Spirit, and having looked stedfastly on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 13:38"> said, 'O full of all guile, and all profligacy, son of a devil, enemy of all righteousness, wilt thou not cease perverting the right ways of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 9:3; Psalms 32:4; Hebrews 10:31"> and now, lo, a hand of the Lord &#91;is&#93; upon thee, and thou shalt be blind, not seeing the sun for a season;' and presently there fell upon him a mist and darkness, and he, going about, was seeking some to lead &#91;him&#93; by the hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Acts 13:7; Luke 4:22"> then the proconsul having seen what hath come to pass, did believe, being astonished at the teaching of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 15:38; Acts 2:10; Acts 14:24–25"> And those about Paul having set sail from Paphos, came to Perga of Pamphylia, and John having departed from them, did turn back to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:13; Acts 14:19; Acts 13:5"> and they having gone through from Perga, came to Antioch of Pisidia, and having gone into the synagogue on the sabbath-day, they sat down,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 15:21; Mark 5:22"> and after the reading of the law and of the prophets, the chief men of the synagogue sent unto them, saying, 'Men, brethren, if there be a word in you of exhortation unto the people — say on.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 13:26; Acts 10:2"> And Paul having risen, and having beckoned with the hand, said, 'Men, Israelites, and those fearing God, hearken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalms 105:23–24"> the God of this people Israel did choose our fathers, and the people He did exalt in their sojourning in the land of Egypt, and with an high arm did He bring them out of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Psalms 95:8–11; Psalms 106:13–29"> and about a period of forty years He did suffer their manners in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Psalms 78:55; Acts 7:45"> and having destroyed seven nations in the land of Canaan, He did divide by lot to them their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:16; 1 Samuel 3:20; 2 Kings 23:22"> 'And after these things, about four hundred and fifty years, He gave judges — till Samuel the prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 1 Samuel 15:1; 1 Samuel 12:12–19"> and thereafter they asked for a king, and God did give to them Saul, son of Kish, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalms 78:70–72"> and having removed him, He did raise up to them David for king, to whom also having testified, he said, I found David, the &#91;son&#93; of Jesse, a man according to My heart, who shall do all My will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Psalms 132:11; Revelation 22:16"> 'Of this one's seed God, according to promise, did raise to Israel a Saviour — Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 1:22; John 1:6–8; Luke 3:2–20"> John having first preached, before his coming, a baptism of reformation to all the people of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Mark 1:7; John 1:26–27"> and as John was fulfilling the course, he said, Whom me do ye suppose to be? I am not &#91;he&#93;, but, lo, he doth come after me, of whom I am not worthy to loose the sandal of &#91;his&#93; feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Romans 1:16; Luke 1:69"> 'Men, brethren, sons of the race of Abraham, and those among you fearing God, to you was the word of this salvation sent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 3:17; Luke 24:24–27; Luke 24:20"> for those dwelling in Jerusalem, and their chiefs, this one not having known, also the voices of the prophets, which every sabbath are being read — having judged &#91;him&#93; — did fulfil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 23:21–25; Luke 23:14–16; John 19:4"> and no cause of death having found, they did ask of Pilate that he should be slain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 23:53; John 19:28; John 19:30"> and when they did complete all the things written about him, having taken &#91;him&#93; down from the tree, they laid him in a tomb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Acts 17:31; Matthew 28:6"> and God did raise him out of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:3; Acts 1:11"> and he was seen for many days of those who did come up with him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are his witnesses unto the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Acts 26:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> 'And we to you do proclaim good news — that the promise made unto the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Hebrews 5:5; Hebrews 1:5–6"> God hath in full completed this to us their children, having raised up Jesus, as also in the second Psalm it hath been written, My Son thou art — I to-day have begotten thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 89:2–4; Romans 6:9"> 'And that He did raise him up out of the dead, no more to return to corruption, he hath said thus — I will give to you the faithful kindnesses of David;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:27–31; Psalms 49:9"> wherefore also in another &#91;place&#93; he saith, Thou shalt not give Thy kind One to see corruption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:29; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 2:10"> for David, indeed, his own generation having served by the will of God, did fall asleep, and was added unto his fathers, and saw corruption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 13:30; Acts 2:24"> but he whom God did raise up, did not see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 24:47; 1 John 2:12; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> 'Let it therefore be known to you, men, brethren, that through this one to you is the forgiveness of sins declared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Galatians 2:16; Romans 8:3"> and from all things from which ye were not able in the law of Moses to be declared righteous, in this one every one who is believing is declared righteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Hebrews 3:12; Malachi 3:2"> see, therefore, it may not come upon you that hath been spoken in the prophets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Peter 4:17; Romans 11:7–14"> See, ye despisers, and wonder, and perish, because a work I — I do work in your days, a work in which ye may not believe, though any one may declare &#91;it&#93; to you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Acts 10:33; Matthew 19:30"> And having gone forth out of the synagogue of the Jews, the nations were calling upon &#91;them&#93; that on the next sabbath these sayings may be spoken to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 2:8"> and the synagogue having been dismissed, many of the Jews and of the devout proselytes did follow Paul and Barnabas, who, speaking to them, were persuading them to remain in the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:3; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 11:10"> And on the coming sabbath, almost all the city was gathered together to hear the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Acts 18:6; Acts 5:17"> and the Jews having seen the multitudes, were filled with zeal, and did contradict the things spoken by Paul — contradicting and speaking evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Matthew 21:43; Acts 18:5–6"> And speaking boldly, Paul and Barnabas said, 'To you it was necessary that first the word of God be spoken, and seeing ye do thrust it away, and do not judge yourselves worthy of the life age-during, lo, we do turn to the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> for so hath the Lord commanded us: I have set thee for a light of nations — for thy being for salvation unto the end of the earth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 11:7"> And the nations hearing were glad, and were glorifying the word of the Lord, and did believe — as many as were appointed to life age-during;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 19:10; Acts 19:26"> and the word of the Lord was spread abroad through all the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 13:45; Acts 14:2"> And the Jews stirred up the devout and honourable women, and the first men of the city, and did raise persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and did put them out from their borders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:14; Acts 18:6; Luke 9:5"> and they having shaken off the dust of their feet against them, came to Iconium,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Romans 15:13; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; Acts 4:31"> and the disciples were filled with joy and the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
