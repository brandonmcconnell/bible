<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Acts 11:19; Acts 15:3"> and having found a ship passing over to Phenicia, having gone on board, we sailed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 21:16; Acts 4:36; Matthew 4:24"> and having discovered Cyprus, and having left it on the left, we were sailing to Syria, and did land at Tyre, for there was the ship discharging the lading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 20:22–23; Acts 21:10–12; Acts 20:6–7"> And having found out the disciples, we tarried there seven days, and they said to Paul, through the Spirit, not to go up to Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 20:36; Acts 9:40; Psalms 95:6"> but when it came that we completed the days, having gone forth, we went on, all bringing us on the way, with women and children, unto the outside of the city, and having bowed the knees upon the shore, we prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:27; John 1:11; 2 Corinthians 2:13"> and having embraced one another, we embarked in the ship, and they returned to their own friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:24; 1 Samuel 10:4; Acts 28:12"> And we, having finished the course, from Tyre came down to Ptolemais, and having saluted the brethren, we remained one day with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 8:26–40; Ephesians 4:11"> and on the morrow Paul and his company having gone forth, we came to Caesarea, and having entered into the house of Philip the evangelist — who is of the seven — we remained with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Revelation 2:20; Joel 2:28"> and this one had four daughters, virgins, prophesying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 11:28; Acts 21:4; Acts 20:16"> And we remaining many more days, there came down a certain one from Judea, a prophet, by name Agabus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Jeremiah 13:1–11; 1 Samuel 15:27–28"> and he having come unto us, and having taken up the girdle of Paul, having bound also his own hands and feet, said, 'Thus saith the Holy Spirit, The man whose is this girdle — so shall the Jews in Jerusalem bind, and they shall deliver &#91;him&#93; up to the hands of nations.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:21–23; Acts 20:22; Acts 21:4"> And when we heard these things, we called upon &#91;him&#93; — both we, and those of that place — not to go up to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Philippians 2:17; Philippians 1:20–21"> and Paul answered, 'What do ye — weeping, and crushing mine heart? for I, not only to be bound, but also to die at Jerusalem, am ready, for the name of the Lord Jesus;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 26:39"> and he not being persuaded, we were silent, saying, 'The will of the Lord be done.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 25:1; Acts 25:6; Acts 25:9"> And after these days, having taken &#91;our&#93; vessels, we were going up to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Acts 21:8; Psalms 92:14"> and there went also of the disciples from Caesarea with us, bringing with them him with whom we may lodge, a certain Mnason of Cyprus, an aged disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 15:4; Romans 15:7; Hebrews 13:1–2"> And we having come to Jerusalem, the brethren did gladly receive us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 1:19; Galatians 2:9; Acts 15:13"> and on the morrow Paul was going in with us unto James, all the elders also came,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 1:17; Romans 15:18–19"> and having saluted them, he was declaring, one by one, each of the things God did among the nations through his ministration,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2; Galatians 1:14; Acts 22:3"> and they having heard, were glorifying the Lord. They said also to him, 'Thou seest, brother, how many myriads there are of Jews who have believed, and all are zealous of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:12–15; 1 Corinthians 9:19–21; Galatians 5:1–6"> and they are instructed concerning thee, that apostasy from Moses thou dost teach to all Jews among the nations, saying — Not to circumcise the children, nor after the customs to walk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Acts 15:22; Acts 19:32"> what then is it? certainly the multitude it behoveth to come together, for they will hear that thou hast come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 18:18; Numbers 6:2–7"> 'This, therefore, do that we say to thee: We have four men having a vow on themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:26; Acts 24:18; Acts 18:18"> these having taken, be purified with them, and be at expence with them, that they may shave the head, and all may know that the things of which they have been instructed concerning thee are nothing, but thou dost walk — thyself also — the law keeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Acts 15:19–20"> 'And concerning those of the nations who have believed, we have written, having given judgment, that they observe no such thing, except to keep themselves both from idol-sacrifices, and blood, and a strangled thing, and whoredom.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 24:18; Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:13–20"> Then Paul, having taken the men, on the following day, with them having purified himself, was entering into the temple, announcing the fulfilment of the days of the purification, till the offering was offered for each one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 24:18; Acts 13:50"> And, as the seven days were about to be fully ended, the Jews from Asia having beheld him in the temple, were stirring up all the multitude, and they laid hands upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 24:5–6; Acts 21:21; Acts 26:20–21"> crying out, 'Men, Israelites, help! this is the man who, against the people, and the law, and this place, all everywhere is teaching; and further, also, Greeks he brought into the temple, and hath defiled this holy place;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 18:19"> for they had seen before Trophimus, the Ephesian, in the city with him, whom they were supposing that Paul brought into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:21; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 16:19–22"> All the city also was moved and there was a running together of the people, and having laid hold on Paul, they were drawing him out of the temple, and immediately were the doors shut,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–33; Acts 17:5; Acts 26:9–10"> and they seeking to kill him, a rumour came to the chief captain of the band that all Jerusalem hath been thrown into confusion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Isaiah 3:15; Acts 5:40"> who, at once, having taken soldiers and centurions, ran down upon them, and they having seen the chief captain and the soldiers, did leave off beating Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Acts 21:11; Acts 20:23"> Then the chief captain, having come nigh, took him, and commanded &#91;him&#93; to be bound with two chains, and was inquiring who he may be, and what it is he hath been doing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:32; Acts 23:10; Acts 22:24"> and some were crying out one thing, and some another, among the multitude, and not being able to know the certainty because of the tumult, he commanded him to be carried to the castle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Psalms 55:9; Psalms 58:2"> and when he came upon the steps, it happened he was borne by the soldiers, because of the violence of the multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 19:15; Acts 22:22; Luke 23:18"> for the crowd of the people was following after, crying, 'Away with him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Luke 21:15; Acts 19:30"> And Paul being about to be led into the castle, saith to the chief captain, 'Is it permitted to me to say anything unto thee?' and he said, 'Greek dost thou know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 24:26; Acts 5:36–37; Matthew 5:11"> art not thou, then, the Egyptian who before these days made an uprising, and did lead into the desert the four thousand men of the assassins?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Acts 9:11; Acts 23:27"> And Paul said, 'I, indeed, am a man, a Jew, of Tarsus of Cilicia, of no mean city a citizen; and I beseech thee, suffer me to speak unto the people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 22:2; John 5:2"> And he having given him leave, Paul having stood upon the stairs, did beckon with the hand to the people, and there having been a great silence, he spake unto them in the Hebrew dialect, saying:</span>
