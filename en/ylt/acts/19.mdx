<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:23–28; 1 Corinthians 16:12; 1 Corinthians 1:12"> And it came to pass, in Apollos' being in Corinth, Paul having gone through the upper parts, came to Ephesus, and having found certain disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:39; Acts 11:15–17; Galatians 3:5"> he said unto them, 'The Holy Spirit did ye receive — having believed?' and they said unto him, 'But we did not even hear whether there is any Holy Spirit;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 8:16"> and he said unto them, 'To what, then, were ye baptized?' and they said, 'To John's baptism.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:29–34; Acts 11:16; Acts 1:5"> And Paul said, 'John, indeed, did baptize with a baptism of reformation, saying to the people that in him who is coming after him they should believe — that is, in the Christ — Jesus;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:12; Acts 8:16; Romans 6:3–4"> and they, having heard, were baptized — to the name of the Lord Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> and Paul having laid on them &#91;his&#93; hands, the Holy Spirit came upon them, they were speaking also with tongues, and prophesying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and all the men were, as it were, twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> And having gone into the synagogue, he was speaking boldly for three months, reasoning and persuading the things concerning the reign of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> and when certain were hardened and were disbelieving, speaking evil of the way before the multitude, having departed from them, he did separate the disciples, every day reasoning in the school of a certain Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> And this happened for two years so that all those dwelling in Asia did hear the word of the Lord Jesus, both Jews and Greeks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> mighty works also — not common — was God working through the hands of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> so that even unto the ailing were brought from his body handkerchiefs or aprons, and the sicknesses departed from them; the evil spirits also went forth from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> And certain of the wandering exorcist Jews, took upon &#91;them&#93; to name over those having the evil spirits the name of the Lord Jesus, saying, 'We adjure you by Jesus, whom Paul doth preach;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and there were certain — seven sons of Sceva, a Jew, a chief priest — who are doing this thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> and the evil spirit, answering, said, 'Jesus I know, and Paul I am acquainted with; and ye — who are ye?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> And the man, in whom was the evil spirit, leaping upon them, and having overcome them, prevailed against them, so that naked and wounded they did flee out of that house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> and this became known to all, both Jews and Greeks, who are dwelling at Ephesus, and fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was being magnified,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalms 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> many also of those who did believe were coming, confessing and declaring their acts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> and many of those who had practised the curious arts, having brought the books together, were burning &#91;them&#93; before all; and they reckoned together the prices of them, and found &#91;it&#93; five myriads of silverlings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> so powerfully was the word of God increasing and prevailing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> And when these things were fulfilled, Paul purposed in the Spirit, having gone through Macedonia and Achaia, to go on to Jerusalem, saying — 'After my being there, it behoveth me also to see Rome;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> and having sent to Macedonia two of those ministering to him — Timotheus and Erastus — he himself stayed a time in Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And there came, at that time, not a little stir about the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> for a certain one, Demetrius by name, a worker in silver, making silver sanctuaries of Artemis, was bringing to the artificers gain not a little,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> whom, having brought in a crowd together, and those who did work about such things, he said, 'Men, ye know that by this work we have our wealth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> and ye see and hear, that not only at Ephesus, but almost in all Asia, this Paul, having persuaded, did turn away a great multitude, saying, that they are not gods who are made by hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> and not only is this department in danger for us of coming into disregard, but also, that of the great goddess Artemis the temple is to be reckoned for nothing, and also her greatness is about to be brought down, whom all Asia and the world doth worship.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> And they having heard, and having become full of wrath, were crying out, saying, 'Great &#91;is&#93; the Artemis of the Ephesians!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> and the whole city was filled with confusion, they rushed also with one accord into the theatre, having caught Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians, Paul's fellow-travellers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> And on Paul's purposing to enter in unto the populace, the disciples were not suffering him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> and certain also of the chief men of Asia, being his friends, having sent unto him, were entreating him not to venture himself into the theatre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> Some indeed, therefore, were calling out one thing, and some another, for the assembly was confused, and the greater part did not know for what they were come together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> and out of the multitude they put forward Alexander — the Jews thrusting him forward — and Alexander having beckoned with the hand, wished to make defence to the populace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> and having known that he is a Jew, one voice came out of all, for about two hours, crying, 'Great &#91;is&#93; the Artemis of the Ephesians!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> And the public clerk having quieted the multitude, saith, 'Men, Ephesians, why, who is the man that doth not know that the city of the Ephesians is a devotee of the great goddess Artemis, and of that which fell down from Zeus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> these things, then, not being to be gainsaid, it is necessary for you to be quiet, and to do nothing rashly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> 'For ye brought these men, who are neither temple-robbers nor speaking evil of your goddess;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> if indeed, therefore, Demetrius and the artificers with him with any one have a matter, court &#91;days&#93; are held, and there are proconsuls; let them accuse one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> 'And if ye seek after anything concerning other matters, in the legal assembly it shall be determined;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> for we are also in peril of being accused of insurrection in regard to this day, there being no occasion by which we shall be able to give an account of this concourse;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> and these things having said, he dismissed the assembly.</span>
