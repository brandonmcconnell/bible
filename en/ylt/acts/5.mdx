<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; 2 Timothy 2:20; Matthew 13:47–48"> And a certain man, Ananias by name, with Sapphira his wife, sold a possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:37; 2 Peter 2:14–15; Matthew 23:5"> and did keep back of the price — his wife also knowing — and having brought a certain part, at the feet of the apostles he laid &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Isaiah 29:15; John 13:2"> And Peter said, 'Ananias, wherefore did the Adversary fill thy heart, for thee to lie to the Holy Spirit, and to keep back of the price of the place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Joshua 7:25–26; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> while it remained, did it not remain thine? and having been sold, in thy authority was it not? why &#91;is&#93; it that thou didst put in thy heart this thing? thou didst not lie to men, but to God;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:10–11; Acts 2:43; Acts 5:13"> and Ananias hearing these words, having fallen down, did expire, and great fear came upon all who heard these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:40; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:23"> and having risen, the younger men wound him up, and having carried forth, they buried &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it came to pass, about three hours after, that his wife, not knowing what hath happened, came in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 5:2"> and Peter answered her, 'Tell me if for so much ye sold the place;' and she said, 'Yes, for so much.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Acts 5:3–4; Psalms 95:8–11"> And Peter said unto her, 'How was it agreed by you, to tempt the Spirit of the Lord? lo, the feet of those who did bury thy husband &#91;are&#93; at the door, and they shall carry thee forth;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:5"> and she fell down presently at his feet, and expired, and the young men having come in, found her dead, and having carried forth, they buried &#91;her&#93; by her husband;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11–12; Acts 19:17"> and great fear came upon all the assembly, and upon all who heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:11; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4"> And through the hands of the apostles came many signs and wonders among the people, and they were with one accord all in the porch of Solomon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:21; John 12:42"> and of the rest no one was daring to join himself to them, but the people were magnifying them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:4; Isaiah 55:11–13"> (and the more were believers added to the Lord, multitudes both of men and women,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Acts 19:11–12; John 14:12"> so as into the broad places to bring forth the ailing, and to lay &#91;them&#93; upon couches and mats, that at the coming of Peter, even &#91;his&#93; shadow might overshadow some one of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 16:17–18; Mark 2:3–4; Matthew 15:30–31"> and there were coming together also the people of the cities round about to Jerusalem, bearing ailing persons, and those harassed by unclean spirits — who were all healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:45; Acts 7:9; Acts 17:5"> And having risen, the chief priest, and all those with him — being the sect of the Sadducees — were filled with zeal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 4:3; Luke 21:12; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> and laid their hands upon the apostles, and did put them in a public prison;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:26; Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7–11"> and a messenger of the Lord through the night opened the doors of the prison, having also brought them forth, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 6:63; John 6:68; 1 John 5:11–12"> 'Go on, and standing, speak in the temple to the people all the sayings of this life;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:2; Acts 22:15; Acts 12:18–19"> and having heard, they did enter at the dawn into the temple, and were teaching. And the chief priest having come, and those with him, they called together the sanhedrim and all the senate of the sons of Israel, and they sent to the prison to have them brought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and the officers having come, did not find them in the prison, and having turned back, they told,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Psalms 33:10; Daniel 6:22–24"> saying — 'The prison indeed we found shut in all safety, and the keepers standing without before the doors, and having opened — within we found no one.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:26; John 12:19"> And as the priest, and the magistrate of the temple, and the chief priests, heard these words, they were doubting concerning them to what this would come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and coming near, a certain one told them, saying — 'Lo, the men whom ye did put in the prison are in the temple standing and teaching the people;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:13; Acts 4:21; Matthew 26:5"> then the magistrate having gone away with officers, brought them without violence, for they were fearing the people, lest they should be stoned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:22; Acts 4:7; Luke 22:66"> and having brought them, they set &#91;them&#93; in the sanhedrim, and the chief priest questioned them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Acts 7:52; Acts 4:10–11"> saying, 'Did not we strictly command you not to teach in this name? and lo, ye have filled Jerusalem with your teaching, and ye intend to bring upon us the blood of this man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:19; 1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 7:7–9"> And Peter and the apostles answering, said, 'To obey God it behoveth, rather than men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 10:39"> and the God of our fathers did raise up Jesus, whom ye slew, having hanged upon a tree;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:47; Acts 2:33; Hebrews 2:10"> this one God, a Prince and a Saviour, hath exalted with His right hand, to give reformation to Israel, and forgiveness of sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:26–27; Hebrews 2:3–4; Acts 1:8"> and we are His witnesses of these sayings, and the Holy Spirit also, whom God gave to those obeying him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 7:54; Acts 2:37; Luke 4:28–29"> And they having heard, were cut &#91;to the heart&#93;, and were taking counsel to slay them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Luke 2:46; Luke 5:17"> but a certain one, having risen up in the sanhedrim — a Pharisee, by name Gamaliel, a teacher of law honoured by all the people — commanded to put the apostles forth a little,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 22:26; Jeremiah 26:19; Matthew 27:19"> and said unto them, 'Men, Israelites, take heed to yourselves about these men, what ye are about to do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 8:9; 2 Peter 2:2; Galatians 6:3"> for before these days rose up Theudas, saying, that himself was some one, to whom a number of men did join themselves, as it were four hundred, who was slain, and all, as many as were obeying him, were scattered, and came to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 2:1–2; Psalms 7:14–15; Matthew 26:52"> 'After this one rose up, Judas the Galilean, in the days of the enrolment, and drew away much people after him, and that one perished, and all, as many as were obeying him, were scattered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 8:9–10"> and now I say to you, Refrain from these men, and let them alone, because if this counsel or this work may be of men, it will be overthrown,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Isaiah 46:10; Matthew 16:18"> and if it be of God, ye are not able to overthrow it, lest perhaps also ye be found fighting against God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:28; Acts 4:17–21"> And to him they agreed, and having called near the apostles, having beaten &#91;them&#93;, they commanded &#91;them&#93; not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13–16; Luke 6:22"> they, indeed, then, departed from the presence of the sanhedrim, rejoicing that for his name they were counted worthy to suffer dishonour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 2:46; Acts 20:20; Luke 21:37"> every day also in the temple, and in every house, they were not ceasing teaching and proclaiming good news — Jesus the Christ.</span>
