<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:29; Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2"> Hear, I pray you, that which Jehovah is saying: 'Rise — strive thou with the mountains, And cause thou the hills to hear thy voice.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Hosea 12:2; Isaiah 1:18"> Hear, O mountains, the strife of Jehovah, Ye strong ones — foundations of earth! For a strife &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah, with His people, And with Israel He doth reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 43:22–23; Psalms 81:13"> O My people, what have I done to thee? And what — have I wearied thee? Testify against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Deuteronomy 7:8; Amos 2:10"> For I brought thee up from the land of Egypt, And from the house of servants I have ransomed thee, And I send before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:9–10; Numbers 25:1; Judges 5:11"> O My people, remember, I pray you, What counsel did Balak king of Moab, What answer him did Balaam son of Beor, (From Shittim unto Gilgal,) In order to know the righteous acts of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalms 40:6–8"> With what do I come before Jehovah? Do I bow to God Most High? Do I come before Him with burnt-offerings? With calves — sons of a year?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Samuel 15:22; Psalms 51:16"> Is Jehovah pleased with thousands of rams? With myriads of streams of oil? Do I give my first-born &#91;for&#93; my transgression? The fruit of my body &#91;for&#93; the sin of my soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12–13; Proverbs 21:3; Colossians 3:12"> He hath declared to thee, O man, what &#91;is&#93; good; Yea, what is Jehovah requiring of thee, Except — to do judgment, and love kindness, And lowly to walk with thy God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 30:27"> A voice of Jehovah to the city calleth, And wisdom doth fear Thy name, Hear ye the rod, and Him who appointed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 3:10; Amos 8:5–6; Jeremiah 5:26–27"> Are there yet &#91;in&#93; the house of the wicked Treasures of wickedness, And the abhorred scanty ephah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Leviticus 19:36; Proverbs 16:11"> Do I reckon &#91;it&#93; pure with balances of wickedness? And with a bag of deceitful stones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; Jeremiah 9:8; Hosea 7:13"> Whose rich ones have been full of violence, And its inhabitants have spoken falsehood, And their tongue &#91;is&#93; deceitful in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Hosea 13:16"> And I also, I have begun to smite thee, To make desolate, because of thy sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 4:10; Leviticus 26:26; Isaiah 65:13"> Thou — thou eatest, and thou art not satisfied, And thy pit &#91;is&#93; in thy midst, And thou removest, and dost not deliver, And that which thou deliverest, to a sword I give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:13; Amos 5:11; Jeremiah 12:13"> Thou — thou sowest, and thou dost not reap, Thou — thou treadest the olive, And thou pourest not out oil, And new wine — and thou drinkest not wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:51; 1 Kings 16:25–33; Jeremiah 7:24"> And kept habitually are the statutes of Omri, And all the work of the house of Ahab, And ye do walk in their counsels, For My giving thee for a desolation, And its inhabitants for a hissing, And the reproach of My people ye do bear!</span>
