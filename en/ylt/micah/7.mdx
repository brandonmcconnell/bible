<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 28:4; Isaiah 24:13; Hosea 9:10"> My woe &#91;is&#93; to me, for I have been As gatherings of summer-fruit, As gleanings of harvest, There is no cluster to eat, The first-ripe fruit desired hath my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 59:7"> Perished hath the kind out of the land, And upright among men — there are none, All of them for blood lie in wait, Each his brother they hunt &#91;with&#93; a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Amos 5:12"> On the evil &#91;are&#93; both hands to do &#91;it&#93; well, The prince is asking — also the judge — for recompence, And the great — he is speaking the mischief of his soul, And they wrap it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 22:5; Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 10:3"> Their best one &#91;is&#93; as a brier, The upright one — than a thorn-hedge, The day of thy watchmen — Thy visitation — hath come. Now is their perplexity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:4; Psalms 118:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> Believe not in a friend, trust not in a leader, From her who is lying in thy bosom keep the openings of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 10:35–36; Luke 12:53; Matthew 10:21"> For a son is dishonouring a father, A daughter hath stood against her mother, A daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law, The enemies of each &#91;are&#93; the men of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; Psalms 37:7; 1 John 5:14–15"> And I — in Jehovah I do watch, I do wait for the God of my salvation, Hear me doth my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 27:1"> Thou dost not rejoice over me, O mine enemy, When I have fallen, I have risen, When I sit in darkness Jehovah is a light to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Samuel 24:15"> The indignation of Jehovah I do bear, For I have sinned against Him, Till that He doth plead my cause, And hath executed my judgment, He doth bring me forth to the light, I look on His righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 50:33–34; Psalms 42:10"> And see doth mine enemy, And cover her doth shame, Who saith unto me, 'Where &#91;is&#93; Jehovah thy God?' Mine eyes do look on her, Now she is for a treading-place, As mire of the out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:17"> The day to build thy walls! That day — removed is the limit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23–25; Isaiah 11:16; Hosea 11:11"> That day — even unto thee it doth come in, From Asshur and the cities of the fortress, And from the fortress even unto the river, And from sea to sea, and mount to mount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Jeremiah 25:11; Micah 6:13"> And the land hath been for a desolation, Because of its inhabitants, Because of the fruit of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:1–4; Micah 5:4; Psalms 100:3"> Rule Thou Thy people with Thy rod, The flock of Thine inheritance, Dwelling alone &#91;in&#93; a forest in the midst of Carmel, They enjoy Bashan and Gilead as in days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:12–72; Exodus 3:20; Psalms 68:22"> According to the days of thy coming forth out of the land of Egypt, I do shew it wonderful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Isaiah 52:15; Job 29:9–10"> See do nations, and they are ashamed of all their might, They lay a hand on the mouth, their ears are deaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 18:45"> They lick dust as a serpent, as fearful things of earth, They tremble from their enclosures, Of Jehovah our God they are afraid, Yea, they are afraid of Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 103:8–9"> Who &#91;is&#93; a God like Thee? taking away iniquity, And passing by the transgression of the remnant of His inheritance, He hath not retained for ever His anger, Because He — He delighteth &#91;in&#93; kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 38:17; Romans 6:14; Isaiah 43:25"> He doth turn back, He pitieth us, He doth subdue our iniquities, And Thou castest into the depths of the sea all their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–14; Luke 1:72–74; Deuteronomy 7:8"> Thou givest truth to Jacob, kindness to Abraham, That thou hast sworn to our fathers, from the days of antiquity!</span>
