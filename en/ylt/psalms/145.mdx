<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 47:6–8; Psalms 45:6; Psalms 34:1"> Praise by David. I exalt Thee, my God, O king, And bless Thy name to the age and for ever.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 147:5; Romans 11:33; Job 5:9"> Great &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, and praised greatly, And of His greatness there is no searching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–7; Psalms 71:18; Deuteronomy 6:7"> Generation to generation praiseth Thy works, And Thy mighty acts they declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 119:27; Psalms 72:18"> The honour — the glory of Thy majesty, And the matters of Thy wonders I declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Daniel 3:28–29; Psalms 107:31–32"> And the strength of Thy fearful acts they tell, And Thy greatness I recount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 36:10; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> The memorial of the abundance of Thy goodness they send forth. And Thy righteousness they sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:18; Psalms 103:8; Psalms 86:5"> Gracious and merciful &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, Slow to anger, and great in kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:7; Psalms 100:5; Matthew 5:45"> Good &#91;is&#93; Jehovah to all, And His mercies &#91;are&#93; over all His works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Psalms 103:22; Revelation 7:9–12"> Confess Thee O Jehovah, do all Thy works, And Thy saints do bless Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalms 93:1–2"> The honour of Thy kingdom they tell, And &#91;of&#93; Thy might they speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:10; Psalms 105:1; Matthew 28:18"> To make known to sons of men His mighty acts, The honour of the majesty of His kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Daniel 7:14; Psalms 10:16"> Thy kingdom &#91;is&#93; a kingdom of all ages, And Thy dominion &#91;is&#93; in all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; Psalms 37:24; Psalms 94:18"> Jehovah is supporting all who are falling, And raising up all who are bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 104:27; Acts 17:25"> The eyes of all unto Thee do look, And Thou art giving to them their food in its season,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 104:28; Psalms 107:9; Psalms 132:15"> Opening Thy hand, and satisfying The desire of every living thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 45:21"> Righteous &#91;is&#93; Jehovah in all His ways, And kind in all His works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 34:18; Deuteronomy 4:7"> Near &#91;is&#93; Jehovah to all those calling Him, To all who call Him in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Ephesians 3:16–20; John 15:7"> The desire of those fearing Him He doth, And their cry He heareth, and saveth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; John 10:27–29; Romans 8:28–30"> Jehovah preserveth all those loving Him, And all the wicked He destroyeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 71:8; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 150:6"> The praise of Jehovah my mouth speaketh, And all flesh doth bless His holy name, To the age and for ever!</span>
