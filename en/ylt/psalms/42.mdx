<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 79:10; Psalms 115:2; Joel 2:17"> My tear hath been to me bread day and night, In their saying unto me all the day, 'Where &#91;is&#93; thy God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 30:29; Psalms 122:1; 1 Samuel 1:15–16"> These I remember, and pour out my soul in me, For I pass over into the booth, I go softly with them unto the house of God, With the voice of singing and confession, The multitude keeping feast!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Hebrews 10:36–37; Psalms 61:2"> What! bowest thou thyself, O my soul? Yea, art thou troubled within me? Wait for God, for still I confess Him: The salvation of my countenance — My God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Jonah 2:7; Deuteronomy 3:8–9"> In me doth my soul bow itself, Therefore I remember Thee from the land of Jordan, And of the Hermons, from the hill Mizar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Psalms 88:7"> Deep unto deep is calling At the noise of Thy water-spouts, All Thy breakers and Thy billows passed over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 63:6; Psalms 16:7; Psalms 149:5"> By day Jehovah commandeth His kindness, And by night a song &#91;is&#93; with me, A prayer to the God of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2; Psalms 18:2"> I say to God my rock, 'Why hast Thou forgotten me? Why go I mourning in the oppression of an enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Joel 2:17; Luke 2:35"> With a sword in my bones Have mine adversaries reproached me, In their saying unto me all the day, 'Where &#91;is&#93; thy God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 42:5; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 43:5"> What! bowest thou thyself, O my soul? And what! art thou troubled within me? Wait for God, for still I confess Him, The salvation of my countenance, and my God!</span>
