<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:7; Psalms 18:2; Proverbs 1:12"> By David. Unto Thee, O Jehovah, I call, My rock, be not silent to me! Lest Thou be silent to me, And I have been compared With those going down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 138:2; Psalms 141:2; Psalms 140:6"> Hear the voice of my supplications, In my crying unto Thee, In my lifting up my hands toward thy holy oracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 12:2; Jeremiah 9:8–9"> Draw me not with the wicked, And with workers of iniquity, Speaking peace with their neighbours, And evil in their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; 2 Timothy 4:14; Revelation 18:6"> Give to them according to their acting, And according to the evil of their doings. According to the work of their hands give to them. Return their deed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Romans 1:28; John 12:37"> For they attend not to the doing of Jehovah, And unto the work of His hands. He throweth them down, And doth not build them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 31:21–22; Psalms 107:19–22; Psalms 118:5"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, For He hath heard the voice of my supplications.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 56:3–4; Isaiah 61:10"> Jehovah &#91;is&#93; my strength, and my shield, In Him my heart trusted, and I have been helped. And my heart exulteth, And with my song I thank Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 20:6; Psalms 140:7; Isaiah 61:1"> Jehovah &#91;is&#93; strength to him, Yea, the strength of the salvation of His anointed &#91;is&#93; He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Psalms 78:71; Deuteronomy 9:29"> Save Thy people, and bless Thine inheritance, And feed them, and carry them to the age!</span>
