<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> A Prayer of Moses, the man of God. Lord, a habitation Thou — Thou hast been, To us — in generation and generation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 102:24–27"> Before mountains were brought forth, And Thou dost form the earth and the world, Even from age unto age Thou &#91;art&#93; God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou turnest man unto a bruised thing, And sayest, Turn back, ye sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> For a thousand years in Thine eyes &#91;are&#93; as yesterday, For it passeth on, yea, a watch by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; Psalms 73:20; Job 22:16"> Thou hast inundated them, they are asleep, In the morning as grass he changeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 92:7; James 1:11"> In the morning it flourisheth, and hath changed, At evening it is cut down, and hath withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> For we were consumed in Thine anger, And in Thy fury we have been troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Thou hast set our iniquities before Thee, Our hidden things at the light of Thy face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 78:33; Psalms 39:5; Psalms 90:4"> For all our days pined away in Thy wrath, We consumed our years as a meditation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> Days of our years, in them &#91;are&#93; seventy years, And if, by reason of might, eighty years, Yet &#91;is&#93; their enlargement labour and vanity, For it hath been cut off hastily, and we fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> Who knoweth the power of Thine anger? And according to Thy fear — Thy wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalms 39:4; John 9:4"> To number our days aright let &#91;us&#93; know, And we bring the heart to wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Psalms 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Turn back, O Jehovah, till when? And repent concerning Thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 31:7"> Satisfy us at morn &#91;with&#93; Thy kindness, And we sing and rejoice all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 30:5; John 16:20"> Cause us to rejoice according to the days Wherein Thou hast afflicted us, The years we have seen evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Let Thy work appear unto Thy servants, And Thine honour on their sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> And let the pleasantness of Jehovah our God be upon us, And the work of our hands establish on us, Yea, the work of our hands establish it!</span>
