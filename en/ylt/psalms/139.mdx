<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 12:3; Jeremiah 17:9–10"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm by David. Jehovah, Thou hast searched me, and knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:4; 2 Kings 19:27; Psalms 94:11"> Thou — Thou hast known my sitting down, And my rising up, Thou hast attended to my thoughts from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> My path and my couch Thou hast fanned, And &#91;with&#93; all my ways hast been acquainted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> For there is not a word in my tongue, Lo, O Jehovah, Thou hast known it all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> Behind and before Thou hast besieged me, And Thou dost place on me Thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Knowledge too wonderful for me, It hath been set on high, I am not able for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> Whither do I go from Thy Spirit? And whither from Thy face do I flee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I ascend the heavens — there Thou &#91;art&#93;, And spread out a couch in Sheol, lo, Thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> I take the wings of morning, I dwell in the uttermost part of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> Also there Thy hand doth lead me, And Thy right hand doth hold me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> And I say, 'Surely darkness bruiseth me, Then night &#91;is&#93; light to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> Also darkness hideth not from Thee, And night as day shineth, as &#91;is&#93; darkness so &#91;is&#93; light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For Thou — Thou hast possessed my reins, Thou dost cover me in my mother's belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 111:2; Psalms 104:24"> I confess Thee, because that &#91;with&#93; wonders I have been distinguished. Wonderful &#91;are&#93; Thy works, And my soul is knowing &#91;it&#93; well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My substance was not hid from Thee, When I was made in secret, Curiously wrought in the lower part of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> Mine unformed substance Thine eyes saw, And on Thy book all of them are written, The days they were formed — And not one among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> And to me how precious have been Thy thoughts, O God, how great hath been their sum!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 139:3; Psalms 40:5"> I recount them! than the sand they are more, I have waked, and I am still with Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Psalms 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> Dost Thou slay, O God, the wicked? Then, men of blood, turn aside from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalms 74:18"> Who exchange Thee for wickedness, Lifted up to vanity &#91;are&#93; Thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Psalms 31:6; Psalms 26:5"> Do not I hate, Jehovah, those hating Thee? And with Thy withstanders grieve myself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> &#91;With&#93; perfect hatred I have hated them, Enemies they have become to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> Search me, O God, and know my heart, Try me, and know my thoughts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalms 25:8–9"> And see if a grievous way be in me, And lead me in a way age-during!</span>
