O Jehovah, God of my salvation, Daily I have cried, nightly before Thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> My prayer cometh in before Thee, Incline Thine ear to my loud cry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> For my soul hath been full of evils, And my life hath come to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> I have been reckoned with those going down &#91;to&#93; the pit, I have been as a man without strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> Among the dead — free, As pierced ones lying in the grave, Whom Thou hast not remembered any more, Yea, they by Thy hand have been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> Thou hast put me in the lowest pit, In dark places, in depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> Upon me hath Thy fury lain, And &#91;with&#93; all Thy breakers Thou hast afflicted. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> Thou hast put mine acquaintance far from me, Thou hast made me an abomination to them, Shut up — I go not forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> Mine eye hath grieved because of affliction, I called Thee, O Jehovah, all the day, I have spread out unto Thee my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> To the dead dost Thou do wonders? Do Rephaim rise? do they thank Thee? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> Is Thy kindness recounted in the grave? Thy faithfulness in destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> Are Thy wonders known in the darkness? And Thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> And I, unto Thee, O Jehovah, I have cried, And in the morning doth my prayer come before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> Why, O Jehovah, castest Thou off my soul? Thou hidest Thy face from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> I &#91;am&#93; afflicted, and expiring from youth, I have borne Thy terrors — I pine away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> Over me hath Thy wrath passed, Thy terrors have cut me off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> They have surrounded me as waters all the day, They have gone round against me together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> Thou hast put far from me lover and friend, Mine acquaintance &#91;is&#93; the place of darkness!</span>
