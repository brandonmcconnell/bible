<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> A Psalm. — A song of the dedication of the house of David. I exalt Thee, O Jehovah, For Thou hast drawn me up, and hast not let mine enemies rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> Jehovah my God, I have cried to Thee, And Thou dost heal me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> Jehovah, Thou hast brought up from Sheol my soul, Thou hast kept me alive, From going down &#91;to&#93; the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> Sing praise to Jehovah, ye His saints, And give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> For — a moment &#91;is&#93; in His anger, Life &#91;is&#93; in His good-will, At even remaineth weeping, and at morn singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> And I — I have said in mine ease, 'I am not moved — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> O Jehovah, in Thy good pleasure, Thou hast caused strength to remain for my mountain,' Thou hast hidden Thy face — I have been troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> Unto Thee, O Jehovah, I call, And unto Jehovah I make supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> 'What gain &#91;is&#93; in my blood? In my going down unto corruption? Doth dust thank Thee? doth it declare Thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> Hear, O Jehovah, and favour me, O Jehovah, be a helper to me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> Thou hast turned my mourning to dancing for me, Thou hast loosed my sackcloth, And girdest me &#91;with&#93; joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> So that honour doth praise Thee, and is not silent, O Jehovah, my God, to the age I thank Thee!</span>
