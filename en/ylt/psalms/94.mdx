<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Nahum 1:2; Romans 12:19"> God of vengeance — Jehovah! God of vengeance, shine forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 7:6; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalms 31:23"> Be lifted up, O Judge of the earth, Send back a recompence on the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 20:5; Revelation 6:10; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Till when &#91;do&#93; the wicked, O Jehovah? Till when do the wicked exult?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:18; Psalms 52:1; Revelation 13:5–6"> They utter — they speak an old saw, All working iniquity do boast themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 3:15; Psalms 79:2–3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Thy people, O Jehovah, they bruise, And Thine inheritance they afflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 7:6"> Widow and sojourner they slay, And fatherless ones they murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 10:11–13; Luke 18:3–4; Zephaniah 1:12"> And they say, 'Jehovah doth not see, And the God of Jacob doth not consider.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; Titus 3:3; Psalms 49:10"> Consider, ye brutish among the people, And ye foolish, when do ye act wisely?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 11:4"> He who planteth the ear doth He not hear? He who formeth the eye doth He not see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 35:11; Isaiah 28:26; Psalms 44:2"> He who is instructing nations, Doth He not reprove? He who is teaching man knowledge &#91;is&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:20; 1 Corinthians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 1:21"> He knoweth the thoughts of man, that they &#91;are&#93; vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17; Hebrews 12:5–11"> O the happiness of the man Whom Thou instructest, O Jah, And out of Thy law teachest him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; 2 Peter 2:9; Psalms 9:15"> To give rest to him from days of evil, While a pit is digged for the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 37:28; Romans 11:1–2"> For Jehovah leaveth not His people, And His inheritance forsaketh not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 37:34"> For to righteousness judgment turneth back, And after it all the upright of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Numbers 25:6–13; John 7:50–51"> Who riseth up for me with evil doers? Who stationeth himself for me with workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 124:1–2; Psalms 31:17; Psalms 118:13"> Unless Jehovah &#91;were&#93; a help to me, My soul had almost inhabited silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 119:116–117"> If I have said, 'My foot hath slipped,' Thy kindness, O Jehovah, supporteth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; 2 Corinthians 1:4–5; Psalms 77:2–10"> In the abundance of my thoughts within me, Thy comforts delight my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 58:2; Isaiah 10:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> Is a throne of mischief joined &#91;with&#93; Thee? A framer of perverseness by statute?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; Matthew 27:1; Proverbs 17:15"> They decree against the soul of the righteous, And innocent blood declare wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 27:1–3; Psalms 59:16–17"> And Jehovah is for a high place to me, And my God &#91;is&#93; for a rock — my refuge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 14:32; Proverbs 2:22"> And turneth back on them their iniquity, And in their wickedness cutteth them off; Jehovah our God doth cut them off!</span>
