O God, Thou hadst cast us off, Thou hadst broken us — hadst been angry! — Thou dost turn back to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> Thou hast caused the land to tremble, Thou hast broken it, Heal its breaches, for it hath moved.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:6–13; Psalms 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> That Thy beloved ones may be drawn out, Save &#91;with&#93; Thy right hand, and answer us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; Psalms 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> God hath spoken in His holiness: I exult — I apportion Shechem, And the valley of Succoth I measure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> Mine &#91;is&#93; Gilead, and mine &#91;is&#93; Manasseh, And Ephraim &#91;is&#93; the strength of my head, Judah &#91;is&#93; my lawgiver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; Psalms 108:9–10"> Moab &#91;is&#93; my pot for washing, over Edom I cast my shoe, Shout, concerning me, O Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> Who doth bring me &#91;to&#93; a city of bulwarks? Who hath led me unto Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalms 60:1"> Is it not Thou, O God? hast Thou cast us off? And dost Thou not go forth, O God, with our hosts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 108:12; Psalms 130:8"> Give to us help from adversity, And vain &#91;is&#93; the deliverance of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 63:3"> In God we do mightily, And He treadeth down our adversaries!</span>
