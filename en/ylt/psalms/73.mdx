<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> A Psalm of Asaph. Only — good to Israel &#91;is&#93; God, to the clean of heart. And I — as a little thing, My feet have been turned aside,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> As nothing, have my steps slipped, For I have been envious of the boastful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> The peace of the wicked I see, That there are no bands at their death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> And their might &#91;is&#93; firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> In the misery of mortals they are not, And with common men they are not plagued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore hath pride encircled them, Violence covereth them as a dress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> Their eye hath come out from fat. The imaginations of the heart transgressed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> They do corruptly, And they speak in the wickedness of oppression, From on high they speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> They have set in the heavens their mouth, And their tongue walketh in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> Therefore do His people return hither, And waters of fulness are wrung out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> And they have said, 'How hath God known? And is there knowledge in the Most High?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> Lo, these &#91;are&#93; the wicked and easy ones of the age, They have increased strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> Only — a vain thing! I have purified my heart, And I wash in innocency my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> And I am plagued all the day, And my reproof &#91;is&#93; every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> If I have said, 'I recount thus,' Lo, a generation of Thy sons I have deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> And I think to know this, Perverseness it &#91;is&#93; in mine eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> Till I come in to the sanctuaries of God, I attend to their latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> Only, in slippery places Thou dost set them, Thou hast caused them to fall to desolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> How have they become a desolation as in a moment, They have been ended — consumed from terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> As a dream from awakening, O Lord, In awaking, their image Thou despisest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> For my heart doth show itself violent, And my reins prick themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> And I am brutish, and do not know. A beast I have been with Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> And I &#91;am&#93; continually with Thee, Thou hast laid hold on my right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> With Thy counsel Thou dost lead me, And after honour dost receive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> Whom have I in the heavens? And with Thee none I have desired in earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> Consumed hath been my flesh and my heart, The rock of my heart and my portion &#91;is&#93; God to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> For, lo, those far from Thee do perish, Thou hast cut off every one, Who is going a whoring from Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> And I — nearness of God to me &#91;is&#93; good, I have placed in the Lord Jehovah my refuge, To recount all Thy works!</span>
