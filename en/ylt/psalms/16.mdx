<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 142:5; Lamentations 3:24"> Jehovah &#91;is&#93; the portion of my share, and of my cup, Thou — Thou dost uphold my lot.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 62:6; Acts 2:25–28; Psalms 121:5"> I did place Jehovah before me continually, Because — at my right hand I am not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:26; Psalms 30:12; Psalms 57:8"> Therefore hath my heart been glad, And my honour doth rejoice, Also my flesh dwelleth confidently:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:27–31; Acts 13:35–38; Psalms 49:15"> For Thou dost not leave my soul to Sheol, Nor givest thy saintly one to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Jude 1:24; Psalms 36:7–8"> Thou causest me to know the path of life; Fulness of joys &#91;is&#93; with Thy presence, Pleasant things by Thy right hand for ever!</span>
