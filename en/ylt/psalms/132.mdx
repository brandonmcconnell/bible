<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Matthew 6:33"> 'If I enter into the tent of my house, If I go up on the couch of my bed,</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Acts 2:30; Isaiah 59:21"> If thy sons keep My covenant, And My testimonies that I teach them, Their sons also for ever and ever, Do sit on the throne for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:68–69; Hebrews 12:22; Psalms 87:2"> For Jehovah hath fixed on Zion, He hath desired &#91;it&#93; for a seat to Himself,</span>
