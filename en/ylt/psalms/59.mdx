<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalms 18:48; Psalms 57:1"> To the Overseer. — 'Destroy not,' by David. — A secret treasure, in Saul's sending, and they watch the house to put him to death. Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God, From my withstanders set me on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:19; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 55:23"> Deliver me from workers of iniquity, And from men of blood save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> For, lo, they laid wait for my soul, Assembled against me are strong ones, Not my transgression nor my sin, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> Without punishment they run and prepare themselves, Stir up to meet me, and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 84:8; Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:15"> And Thou, Jehovah, God of Hosts, God of Israel, Awake to inspect all the nations. Favour not any treacherous dealers of iniquity. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> They turn back at evening, They make a noise like a dog, And go round about the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> Lo, they belch out with their mouths, Swords &#91;are&#93; in their lips, for 'Who heareth?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> And Thou, O Jehovah dost laugh at them, Thou dost mock at all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> O my Strength, unto Thee I take heed, For God &#91;is&#93; my tower — the God of my kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> God doth go before me, He causeth me to look on mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> Slay them not, lest my people forget, Shake them by Thy strength, And bring them down, O Lord our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> The sin of their mouth &#91;is&#93; a word of their lips, And they are captured in their pride, And from the curse and lying they recount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> Consume in fury, consume and they are not, And they know that God is ruling in Jacob, To the ends of the earth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 59:6"> And they turn back at evening, They make a noise like a dog, And they go round about the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalms 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> They — they wander for food, If they are not satisfied — then they murmur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> And I — I sing &#91;of&#93; Thy strength, And I sing at morn &#91;of&#93; Thy kindness, For thou hast been a tower to me, And a refuge for me in a day of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 59:9–10; Psalms 46:1; Psalms 18:1"> O my Strength, unto Thee I sing praise, For God &#91;is&#93; my tower, the God of my kindness!</span>
