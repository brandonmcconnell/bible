<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 98:4"> To the Overseer. — A Song, a Psalm. Shout ye to God, all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> Praise ye the honour of His name, Make ye honourable His praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> Say to God, 'How fearful &#91;are&#93; Thy works, By the abundance of Thy strength, Thine enemies feign obedience to Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> All the earth do bow to Thee, They sing praise to Thee, they praise Thy name.' Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> Come ye, and see the works of God, Fearful acts toward the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> He hath turned a sea to dry land, Through a river they pass over on foot, There do we rejoice in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> Ruling by His might to the age, His eyes among the nations do watch, The refractory exalt not themselves. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> Bless, ye peoples, our God, And sound the voice of His praise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> Who hath placed our soul in life, And suffered not our feet to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> For Thou hast tried us, O God, Thou hast refined us as the refining of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> Thou hast brought us into a net, Thou hast placed pressure on our loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> Thou hast caused man to ride at our head. We have entered into fire and into water, And Thou bringest us out to a watered place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> I enter Thy house with burnt-offerings, I complete to Thee my vows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> For opened were my lips, And my mouth spake in my distress:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> 'Burnt-offerings of fatlings I offer to Thee, With perfume of rams, I prepare a bullock with he-goats.' Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> Come, hear, all ye who fear God, And I recount what he did for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> Unto Him &#91;with&#93; my mouth I have called, And exaltation &#91;is&#93; under my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> Iniquity, if I have seen in my heart, The Lord doth not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> But God hath heard, He hath attended to the voice of my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; God, Who hath not turned aside my prayer, And His loving-kindness, from me!</span>
