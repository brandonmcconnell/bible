<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:19–20; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 50:6"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. The heavens &#91;are&#93; recounting the honour of God, And the work of His hands The expanse &#91;is&#93; declaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 134:1–3; Genesis 8:22"> Day to day uttereth speech, And night to night sheweth knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is no speech, and there are no words. Their voice hath not been heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 98:3"> Into all the earth hath their line gone forth, And to the end of the world their sayings, For the sun He placed a tent in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> And he, as a bridegroom, goeth out from his covering, He rejoiceth as a mighty one To run the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> From the end of the heavens &#91;is&#93; his going out, And his revolution &#91;is&#93; unto their ends, And nothing is hid from his heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:130; Psalms 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> The law of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; perfect, refreshing the soul, The testimonies of Jehovah &#91;are&#93; stedfast, Making wise the simple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; Psalms 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> The precepts of Jehovah &#91;are&#93; upright, Rejoicing the heart, The command of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; pure, enlightening the eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalms 119:142; Psalms 119:137–138"> The fear of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; clean, standing to the age, The judgments of Jehovah &#91;are&#93; true, They have been righteous — together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalms 119:72"> They are more desirable than gold, Yea, than much fine gold; and sweeter than honey, Even liquid honey of the comb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> Also — Thy servant is warned by them, 'In keeping them &#91;is&#93; a great reward.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalms 139:23–24"> Errors! who doth understand? From hidden ones declare me innocent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> Also — from presumptuous ones keep back Thy servant, Let them not rule over me, Then am I perfect, And declared innocent of much transgression,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:34; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 119:108"> Let the sayings of my mouth, And the meditation of my heart, Be for a pleasing thing before Thee, O Jehovah, my rock, and my redeemer!</span>
