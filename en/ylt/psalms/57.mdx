<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3; Psalms 72:19"> Be Thou exalted above the heavens, O God, Above all the earth Thine honour.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11; Psalms 36:5"> For great unto the heavens &#91;is&#93; Thy kindness, And unto the clouds Thy truth.</span>
