<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 83:1"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. O God of my praise, be not silent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 52:4; Proverbs 6:17; Acts 6:13"> For the mouth of wickedness, and the mouth of deceit, Against me they have opened, They have spoken with me — A tongue of falsehood, and words of hatred!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> They have compassed me about, And they fight me without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> For my love they oppose me, and I — prayer!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> And they set against me evil for good, And hatred for my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> Appoint Thou over him the wicked, And an adversary standeth at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> In his being judged, he goeth forth wicked, And his prayer is for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> His days are few, his oversight another taketh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> His sons are fatherless, and his wife a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> And wander continually do his sons, Yea, they have begged, And have sought out of their dry places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> An exactor layeth a snare for all that he hath, And strangers spoil his labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> He hath none to extend kindness, Nor is there one showing favour to his orphans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> His posterity is for cutting off, In another generation is their name blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> The iniquity of his fathers Is remembered unto Jehovah, And the sin of his mother is not blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> They are before Jehovah continually, And He cutteth off from earth their memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> Because that he hath not remembered to do kindness, And pursueth the poor man and needy, And the smitten of heart — to slay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> And he loveth reviling, and it meeteth him, And he hath not delighted in blessing, And it is far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> And he putteth on reviling as his robe, And it cometh in as water into his midst, And as oil into his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> It is to him as apparel — he covereth himself, And for a continual girdle he girdeth it on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> This &#91;is&#93; the wage of mine accusers from Jehovah, And of those speaking evil against my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> And Thou, O Jehovah Lord, Deal with me for Thy name's sake, Because Thy kindness &#91;is&#93; good, deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> For I &#91;am&#93; poor and needy, And my heart hath been pierced in my midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> As a shadow when it is stretched out I have gone, I have been driven away as a locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> My knees have been feeble from fasting, And my flesh hath failed of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> And I — I have been a reproach to them, They see me, they shake their head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> Help me, O Jehovah my God, Save me, according to Thy kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> And they know that this &#91;is&#93; Thy hand, Thou, O Jehovah, Thou hast done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> They revile, and Thou dost bless, They have risen, and are ashamed, And Thy servant doth rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> Mine accusers put on blushing, and are covered, As an upper robe &#91;is&#93; their shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> I thank Jehovah greatly with my mouth, And in the midst of many I praise Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> For He standeth at the right hand of the needy, To save from those judging his soul.</span>
