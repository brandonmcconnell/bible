<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> To the Overseer, for Jeduthun. — A Psalm of Asaph. My voice &#91;is&#93; to God, and I cry, my voice &#91;is&#93; to God, And He hath given ear unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> In a day of my distress the Lord I sought, My hand by night hath been spread out, And it doth not cease, My soul hath refused to be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> I remember God, and make a noise, I meditate, and feeble is my spirit. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> Thou hast taken hold of the watches of mine eyes, I have been moved, and I speak not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> I have reckoned the days of old, The years of the ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> I remember my music in the night, With my heart I meditate, and my spirit doth search diligently:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> To the ages doth the Lord cast off? Doth He add to be pleased no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> Hath His kindness ceased for ever? The saying failed to all generations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> Hath God forgotten &#91;His&#93; favours? Hath He shut up in anger His mercies? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> And I say: 'My weakness is, The changes of the right hand of the Most High.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> I mention the doings of Jah, For I remember of old Thy wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> And I have meditated on all Thy working, And I talk concerning Thy doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> O God, in holiness &#91;is&#93; Thy way, Who &#91;is&#93; a great god like God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> Thou &#91;art&#93; the God doing wonders. Thou hast made known among the peoples Thy strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> Thou hast redeemed with strength Thy people, The sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> The waters have seen Thee, O God, The waters have seen Thee, They are afraid — also depths are troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> Poured out waters have thick clouds, The skies have given forth a noise, Also — Thine arrows go up and down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> The voice of Thy thunder &#91;is&#93; in the spheres, Lightnings have lightened the world, The earth hath trembled, yea, it shaketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> In the sea &#91;is&#93; Thy way, And Thy paths &#91;are&#93; in many waters, And Thy tracks have not been known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> Thou hast led as a flock Thy people, By the hand of Moses and Aaron!</span>
