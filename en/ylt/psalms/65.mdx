<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. A Song. To Thee, silence — praise, O God, &#91;is&#93; in Zion, And to Thee is a vow completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> Hearer of prayer, to Thee all flesh cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> Matters of iniquities were mightier than I, Our transgressions — Thou dost cover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> O the happiness of &#91;him whom&#93; Thou choosest, And drawest near, he inhabiteth Thy courts, We are satisfied with the goodness of Thy house, Thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> By fearful things in righteousness Thou answerest us, O God of our salvation, The confidence of all far off ends of earth and sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Micah 6:2; Habakkuk 3:6"> Establishing mountains by His power, He hath been girded with might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Isaiah 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> Restraining the noise of seas, the noise of their billows, And the multitude of the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> And the inhabitants of the uttermost parts From Thy signs are afraid, The outgoings of morning and evening Thou causest to sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> Thou hast inspected the earth, and waterest it, Thou makest it very rich, the rivulet of God &#91;is&#93; full of water, Thou preparest their corn, When thus Thou dost prepare it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> Its ridges have been filled, Deepened hath been its furrow, With showers Thou dost soften it, Its springing up Thou blessest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> Thou hast crowned the year of Thy goodness, And Thy paths drop fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> Drop do the pastures of a wilderness, And joy of the heights Thou girdest on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Acts 14:17; Isaiah 35:1–2"> Clothed have lambs the flock, And valleys are covered with corn, They shout — yea, they sing!</span>
