<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> By Solomon. O God, Thy judgments to the king give, And Thy righteousness to the king's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> He judgeth Thy people with righteousness, And Thy poor with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> The mountains bear peace to the people, And the heights by righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> He judgeth the poor of the people, Giveth deliverance to the sons of the needy, And bruiseth the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They fear Thee with the sun, and before the moon, Generation — generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> He cometh down as rain on mown grass, As showers — sprinkling the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> Flourish in his days doth the righteous, And abundance of peace till the moon is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> And he ruleth from sea unto sea, And from the river unto the ends of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> Before him bow do the inhabitants of the dry places, And his enemies lick the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> Kings of Tarshish and of the isles send back a present. Kings of Sheba and Seba a reward bring near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> And all kings do bow themselves to him, All nations do serve him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> For he delivereth the needy who crieth, And the poor when he hath no helper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He hath pity on the poor and needy, And the souls of the needy he saveth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> From fraud and from violence he redeemeth their soul, And precious is their blood in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> And he liveth, and giveth to him of the gold of Sheba, And prayeth for him continually, All the day he doth bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> There is a handful of corn in the earth, On the top of mountains, Shake like Lebanon doth its fruit, And they flourish out of the city as the herb of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> His name is to the age, Before the sun is his name continued, And they bless themselves in him, All nations do pronounce him happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed is Jehovah God, God of Israel, He alone is doing wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> And blessed &#91;is&#93; the Name of His honour to the age, And the whole earth is filled &#91;with&#93; His honour. Amen, and amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> The prayers of David son of Jesse have been ended.</span>
