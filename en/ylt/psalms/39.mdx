<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> To the Overseer, to Jeduthun. — A Psalm of David. I have said, 'I observe my ways, Against sinning with my tongue, I keep for my mouth a curb, while the wicked &#91;is&#93; before me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> I was dumb &#91;with&#93; silence, I kept silent from good, and my pain is excited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> Hot &#91;is&#93; my heart within me, In my meditating doth the fire burn, I have spoken with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> 'Cause me to know, O Jehovah, mine end, And the measure of my days — what it &#91;is&#93;,' I know how frail I &#91;am&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> Lo, handbreadths Thou hast made my days, And mine age &#91;is&#93; as nothing before Thee, Only, all vanity &#91;is&#93; every man set up. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Only, in an image doth each walk habitually, Only, &#91;in&#93; vain, they are disquieted, He heapeth up and knoweth not who gathereth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> And, now, what have I expected? O Lord, my hope — it &#91;is&#93; of Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> From all my transgressions deliver me, A reproach of the fool make me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> I have been dumb, I open not my mouth, Because Thou — Thou hast done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> Turn aside from off me Thy stroke, From the striving of Thy hand I have been consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> With reproofs against iniquity, Thou hast corrected man, And dost waste as a moth his desirableness, Only, vanity &#91;is&#93; every man. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> Hear my prayer, O Jehovah, And &#91;to&#93; my cry give ear, Unto my tear be not silent, For a sojourner I &#91;am&#93; with Thee, A settler like all my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> Look from me, and I brighten up before I go and am not!</span>
