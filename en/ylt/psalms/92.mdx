<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 147:1; Psalms 107:1; Ephesians 5:19"> A Psalm. — A Song for the sabbath-day. Good to give thanks to Jehovah, And to sing praises to Thy name, O Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22–23; Psalms 89:1–2; John 1:17"> To declare in the morning Thy kindness, And Thy faithfulness in the nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:2; 1 Chronicles 13:8; Nehemiah 12:27"> On ten strings and on psaltery, On higgaion, with harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Psalms 8:6; Luke 1:47"> For Thou hast caused me to rejoice, O Jehovah, in Thy work, Concerning the works of Thy hands I sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Isaiah 55:8–9; Psalms 40:5"> How great have been Thy works, O Jehovah, Very deep have been Thy thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Psalms 94:8"> A brutish man doth not know, And a fool understandeth not this; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 37:35–36"> When the wicked flourish as a herb, And blossom do all workers of iniquity — For their being destroyed for ever and ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Acts 12:22–24; Acts 12:1"> And Thou &#91;art&#93; high to the age, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 37:20; Psalms 89:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–9"> For, lo, Thine enemies, O Jehovah, For, lo, Thine enemies, do perish, Separate themselves do all workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 89:17; Psalms 45:7"> And Thou exaltest as a reem my horn, I have been anointed with fresh oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:8; Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:10"> And mine eye looketh on mine enemies, Of those rising up against me, The evil doers, do mine ears hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 52:8"> The righteous as a palm-tree flourisheth, As a cedar in Lebanon he groweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; Isaiah 61:3; Ephesians 3:17"> Those planted in the house of Jehovah, In the courts of our God do flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 71:18"> Still they bring forth in old age, Fat and flourishing are they,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Romans 9:14; Psalms 62:6"> To declare that upright &#91;is&#93; Jehovah my rock, And there is no perverseness in Him!</span>
