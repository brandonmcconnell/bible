<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> To the Overseer. — By sons of Korah. An Instruction. O God, with our ears we have heard, Our fathers have recounted to us, The work Thou didst work in their days, In the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> Thou, &#91;with&#93; Thy hand, nations hast dispossessed. And Thou dost plant them. Thou afflictest peoples, and sendest them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> For, not by their sword Possessed they the land, And their arm gave not salvation to them, But Thy right hand, and Thine arm, And the light of Thy countenance, Because Thou hadst accepted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 74:12; Psalms 42:8; Psalms 149:2"> Thou &#91;art&#93; He, my king, O God, Command the deliverances of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> By Thee our adversaries we do push, By Thy name tread down our withstanders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Hosea 1:7; 1 Samuel 17:47"> For, not in my bow do I trust, And my sword doth not save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> For Thou hast saved us from our adversaries, And those hating us Thou hast put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremiah 9:24"> In God we have boasted all the day, And Thy name to the age we thank. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> In anger Thou hast cast off and causest us to blush, And goest not forth with our hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> Thou causest us to turn backward from an adversary, And those hating us, Have spoiled for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> Thou makest us food like sheep, And among nations Thou hast scattered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> Thou sellest Thy people — without wealth, And hast not become great by their price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbours, A scorn and a reproach to our surrounders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 19:21; Job 16:4"> Thou makest us a simile among nations, A shaking of the head among peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> All the day my confusion &#91;is&#93; before me, And the shame of my face hath covered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> Because of the voice of a reproacher and reviler, Because of an enemy and a self-avenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> All this met us, and we did not forget Thee, Nor have we dealt falsely in Thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:51; Psalms 119:157; Psalms 78:57"> We turn not backward our heart, Nor turn aside doth our step from Thy path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 3:5; Psalms 23:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> But Thou hast smitten us in a place of dragons, And dost cover us over with death-shade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 81:9; Job 11:13; Deuteronomy 6:14"> If we have forgotten the name of our God, And spread our hands to a strange God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 23:24; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Doth not God search out this? For He knoweth the secrets of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 44:11"> Surely, for Thy sake we have been slain all the day, Reckoned as sheep of the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:65; Psalms 7:6; Psalms 35:23"> Stir up — why dost Thou sleep, O Lord? Awake, cast us not off for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:24; Deuteronomy 32:20; Psalms 42:9"> Why Thy face hidest Thou? Thou forgettest our afflictions and our oppression,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:25; Lamentations 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> For bowed to the dust hath our soul, Cleaved to the earth hath our belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 130:7–8; Psalms 25:22; Psalms 26:11"> Arise, a help to us, And ransom us for thy kindness' sake.</span>
