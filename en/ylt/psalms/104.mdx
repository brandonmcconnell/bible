<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> Bless, O my soul, Jehovah! Jehovah, my God, Thou hast been very great, Honour and majesty Thou hast put on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> Covering himself &#91;with&#93; light as a garment, Stretching out the heavens as a curtain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> Who is laying the beam of His upper chambers in the waters, Who is making thick clouds His chariot, Who is walking on wings of wind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> Making His messengers — the winds, His ministers — the flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> He hath founded earth on its bases, It is not moved to the age and for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> The abyss! as with clothing Thou hast covered it, Above hills do waters stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> From Thy rebuke they flee, From the voice of Thy thunder haste away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> They go up hills — they go down valleys, Unto a place Thou hast founded for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> A border Thou hast set, they pass not over, They turn not back to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> Who is sending forth fountains in valleys, Between hills they go on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> They water every beast of the field, Wild asses break their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> By them the fowl of the heavens doth dwell, From between the branches They give forth the voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> Watering hills from His upper chambers, From the fruit of Thy works is the earth satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> Causing grass to spring up for cattle, And herb for the service of man, To bring forth bread from the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And wine — it rejoiceth the heart of man, To cause the face to shine from oil, And bread — the heart of man it supporteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> Satisfied &#91;are&#93; the trees of Jehovah, Cedars of Lebanon that He hath planted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> Where birds do make nests, The stork — the firs &#91;are&#93; her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high hills &#91;are&#93; for wild goats, Rocks &#91;are&#93; a refuge for conies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> He made the moon for seasons, The sun hath known his place of entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> Thou settest darkness, and it is night, In it doth every beast of the forest creep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> The young lions are roaring for prey, And to seek from God their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> The sun riseth, they are gathered, And in their dens they crouch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Man goeth forth to his work, And to his service — till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> How many have been Thy works, O Jehovah, All of them in wisdom Thou hast made, Full is the earth of thy possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> This, the sea, great and broad of sides, There &#91;are&#93; moving things — innumerable, Living creatures — small with great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> There do ships go: leviathan, That Thou hast formed to play in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> All of them unto Thee do look, To give their food in its season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> Thou dost give to them — they gather, Thou dost open Thy hand — they &#91;are&#93; satisfied &#91;with&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou hidest Thy face — they are troubled, Thou gatherest their spirit — they expire, And unto their dust they turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Thou sendest out Thy Spirit, they are created, And Thou renewest the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> The honour of Jehovah is to the age, Jehovah rejoiceth in His works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> Who is looking to earth, and it trembleth, He cometh against hills, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> I sing to Jehovah during my life, I sing praise to my God while I exist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> Sweet is my meditation on Him, I — I do rejoice in Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> Consumed are sinners from the earth, And the wicked are no more. Bless, O my soul, Jehovah. Praise ye Jehovah!</span>
