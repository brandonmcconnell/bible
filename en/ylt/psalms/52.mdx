<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Psalms 94:4; Psalms 107:1"> To the Overseer. — An instruction, by David, in the coming in of Doeg the Edomite, and he declareth to Saul, and saith to him, 'David came in unto the house of Ahimelech.'
