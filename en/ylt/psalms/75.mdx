<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Psalms 138:2; Jeremiah 10:6"> To the Overseer. — 'Destroy not.' — A Psalm of Asaph. — A Song. We have given thanks to Thee, O God, We have given thanks, and near &#91;is&#93; Thy name, They have recounted Thy wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Acts 17:31; Acts 1:7"> When I receive an appointment, I — I do judge uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Isaiah 24:19; Hebrews 1:3"> Melted is the earth and all its inhabitants, I — I have pondered its pillars. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 8:5"> I have said to the boastful, 'Be not boastful,' And to the wicked, 'Raise not up a horn.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ezekiel 2:4; Isaiah 48:4"> Raise not up on high your horn, (Ye speak with a stiff neck.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3"> For not from the east, or from the west, Nor from the wilderness — &#91;is&#93; elevation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Daniel 2:21–22; Psalms 147:6"> But God &#91;is&#93; judge, This He maketh low — and this He lifteth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 21:20; Jeremiah 25:15; Psalms 11:6"> For a cup &#91;is&#93; in the hand of Jehovah, And the wine hath foamed, It is full of mixture, and He poureth out of it, Only its dregs wring out, and drink, Do all the wicked of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:14; Psalms 145:1–2; Psalms 40:10"> And I — I declare &#91;it&#93; to the age, I sing praise to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 92:10; Psalms 89:17; Jeremiah 48:25"> And all horns of the wicked I cut off, Exalted are the horns of the righteous!</span>
