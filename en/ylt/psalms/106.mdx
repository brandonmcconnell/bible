<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalms 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> Praise ye Jah, give thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age, &#91;is&#93; His kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalms 145:3–12"> Who doth utter the mighty acts of Jehovah? Soundeth all His praise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> O the happiness of those keeping judgment, Doing righteousness at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O Jehovah, With the favour of Thy people, Look after me in Thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalms 105:43; Psalms 1:3"> To look on the good of Thy chosen ones, To rejoice in the joy of Thy nation, To boast myself with Thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalms 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> We have sinned with our fathers, We have done perversely, we have done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalms 78:42; Psalms 78:11"> Our fathers in Egypt, Have not considered wisely Thy wonders, They have not remembered The abundance of Thy kind acts, And provoke by the sea, at the sea of Suph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> And He saveth them for His name's sake, To make known His might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> And rebuketh the sea of Suph, and it is dried up, And causeth them to go Through depths as a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalms 107:2; Micah 6:4"> And He saveth them from the hand Of him who is hating, And redeemeth them from the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalms 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> And waters cover their adversaries, One of them hath not been left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> And they believe in His words, they sing His praise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> They have hasted — forgotten His works, They have not waited for His counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> And they lust greatly in a wilderness, And try God in a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; Psalms 78:29–31"> And He giveth to them their request, And sendeth leanness into their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> And they are envious of Moses in the camp, Of Aaron, Jehovah's holy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> Earth openeth, and swalloweth up Dathan, And covereth over the company of Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> And fire burneth among their company, A flame setteth on fire the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> They make a calf in Horeb, And bow themselves to a molten image,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> And change their Honour Into the form of an ox eating herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 78:42–51; Psalms 106:13"> They have forgotten God their saviour, The doer of great things in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> Of wonderful things in the land of Ham, Of fearful things by the sea of Suph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> And He saith to destroy them, Unless Moses, His chosen one, Had stood in the breach before Him, To turn back His wrath from destroying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> And they kick against the desirable land, They have not given credence to His word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> And they murmur in their tents, They have not hearkened to the voice of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> And He lifteth up His hand to them, To cause them to fall in a wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> And to cause their seed to fall among nations, And to scatter them through lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> And they are coupled to Baal-Peor, And eat the sacrifices of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> And they provoke to anger by their actions, And a plague breaketh forth upon them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> And Phinehas standeth, and executeth judgment, And the plague is restrained,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> And it is reckoned to him to righteousness, To all generations — unto the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> And they cause wrath by the waters of Meribah, And it is evil to Moses for their sakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalms 107:11; James 3:2"> For they have provoked his spirit, And he speaketh wrongfully with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> They have not destroyed the peoples, As Jehovah had said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> And mix themselves among nations, and learn their works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> And serve their idols, And they are to them for a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And they sacrifice their sons And their daughters to destroyers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> And they shed innocent blood — Blood of their sons and of their daughters, Whom they have sacrificed to idols of Canaan, And the land is profaned with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> And they are defiled with their works, And commit whoredom in their habitual doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> And the anger of Jehovah Is kindled against His people, And He doth abominate His inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> And giveth them into the hand of nations, And those hating them rule over them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> And their enemies oppress them, And they are humbled under their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalms 1:1"> Many times He doth deliver them, And they rebel in their counsel, And they are brought low in their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> And He looketh on their distress When He heareth their cry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> And remembereth for them His covenant, And is comforted, According to the abundance of His kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> And He appointeth them for mercies Before all their captors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Save us, O Jehovah our God, and gather us from the nations, To give thanks to Thy holy name, To glory in Thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 89:52; Psalms 105:45–1"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, God of Israel, From the age even unto the age. And all the people said, 'Amen, praise Jah!'</span>
