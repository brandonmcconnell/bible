<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:48; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 71:4"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. Deliver me, O Jehovah, from an evil man, From one of violence Thou keepest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 36:4; Psalms 64:5–6"> Who have devised evils in the heart, All the day they assemble &#91;for&#93; wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Romans 3:13–14"> They sharpened their tongue as a serpent, Poison of an adder &#91;is&#93; under their lips. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:11; Psalms 71:4; Psalms 17:5"> Preserve me, Jehovah, from the hands of the wicked, From one of violence Thou keepest me, Who have devised to overthrow my steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 18:22"> The proud hid a snare for me — and cords, They spread a net by the side of the path, Snares they have set for me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> I have said to Jehovah, 'My God &#91;art&#93; Thou, Hear, Jehovah, the voice of my supplications.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 62:7; Psalms 89:26; Psalms 18:1–2"> O Jehovah, my Lord, strength of my salvation, Thou hast covered my head in the day of armour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:2–3; Deuteronomy 32:27; Psalms 27:12"> Grant not, O Jehovah, the desires of the wicked, His wicked device bring not forth, They are high. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> The chief of my surrounders, The perverseness of their lips covereth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> They cause to fall on themselves burning coals, Into fire He doth cast them, Into deep pits — they arise not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> A talkative man is not established in the earth, One of violence — evil hunteth to overflowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 9:4; 1 Kings 8:49; Proverbs 23:10–11"> I have known that Jehovah doth execute The judgment of the afflicted, The judgment of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 11:7; Isaiah 3:10"> Only — the righteous give thanks to Thy name, The upright do dwell with Thy presence!</span>
