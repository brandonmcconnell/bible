<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:1–3; Psalms 25:2–3; 1 Peter 2:6"> In Thee, O Jehovah, I have trusted, Let me not be ashamed to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 116:1–2"> In Thy righteousness Thou dost deliver me, And dost cause me to escape, Incline unto me Thine ear, and save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Be to me for a rock — a habitation, To go in continually, Thou hast given command to save me, For my rock and my bulwark &#91;art&#93; Thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:1–4; Psalms 59:1–2; 2 Samuel 17:1–2"> O my God, cause me to escape From the hand of the wicked, From the hand of the perverse and violent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7; Psalms 39:7; Psalms 119:81"> For Thou &#91;art&#93; my hope, O Lord Jehovah, My trust from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 22:9–10; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 139:15–16"> By Thee I have been supported from the womb, From my mother's bowels Thou dost cut me out, In Thee &#91;is&#93; my praise continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> As a wonder I have been to many, And Thou &#91;art&#93; my strong refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 104:1"> Filled is my mouth &#91;with&#93; Thy praise, All the day &#91;with&#93; Thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalms 71:18"> Cast me not off at the time of old age, According to the consumption of my power forsake me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For mine enemies have spoken against me, And those watching my soul have taken counsel together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Psalms 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> Saying, 'God hath forsaken him, Pursue and catch him, for there is no deliverer.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:22; Psalms 38:21–22; Psalms 70:5"> O God, be not far from me, O my God, for my help make haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 35:26"> They are ashamed, they are consumed, Who are opposing my soul, They are covered &#91;with&#93; reproach and blushing, Who are seeking my evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> And I continually do wait with hope, And have added unto all Thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Psalms 35:28; Psalms 71:8"> My mouth recounteth Thy righteousness, All the day Thy salvation, For I have not known the numbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalms 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> I come in the might of the Lord Jehovah, I mention Thy righteousness — Thine only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 71:5; Psalms 119:9; Psalms 119:102"> God, Thou hast taught me from my youth, And hitherto I declare Thy wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Psalms 71:9; Psalms 78:4"> And also unto old age and grey hairs, O God, forsake me not, Till I declare Thy strength to a generation, To every one that cometh Thy might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> And Thy righteousness, O God, &#91;is&#93; unto the heights, Because Thou hast done great things, O God, who &#91;is&#93; like Thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 138:7; Psalms 60:3; Psalms 86:13"> Because Thou hast showed me many and sad distresses, Thou turnest back — Thou revivest me, And from the depths of the earth, Thou turnest back — Thou bringest me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; 2 Samuel 3:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Thou dost increase my greatness, And Thou surroundest — Thou comfortest me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; 2 Kings 19:22; Psalms 33:2"> I also thank Thee with a vessel of psaltery, Thy truth, O my God, I sing to Thee with a harp, O Holy One of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 103:4; Luke 1:46–47"> My lips cry aloud when I sing praise to Thee, And my soul that Thou hast redeemed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 71:13; Psalms 18:37–43; 1 Corinthians 15:25"> My tongue also all the day uttereth Thy righteousness, Because ashamed — because confounded, Have been those seeking my evil!</span>
