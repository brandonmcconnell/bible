<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 71:2; Psalms 31:1; Psalms 140:6"> A Psalm of David. O Jehovah, hear my prayer, Give ear unto my supplications, In Thy faithfulness answer me — in Thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> And enter not into judgment with Thy servant, For no one living is justified before Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For an enemy hath pursued my soul, He hath bruised to the earth my life, He hath caused me to dwell in dark places, As the dead of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> And my spirit in me is become feeble, Within me is my heart become desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> I have remembered days of old, I have meditated on all Thine acts, On the work of Thy hand I muse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I have spread forth my hands unto Thee, My soul &#91;is&#93; as a weary land for Thee. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> Haste, answer me, O Jehovah, My spirit hath been consumed, Hide not Thou Thy face from me, Or I have been compared with those going down &#91;to&#93; the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> Cause me to hear in the morning Thy kindness, For in Thee I have trusted, Cause me to know the way that I go, For unto Thee I have lifted up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliver me from mine enemies, O Jehovah, Near Thee I am covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do Thy good pleasure, For Thou &#91;art&#93; my God — Thy Spirit &#91;is&#93; good, Lead me into a land of uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> For Thy name's sake O Jehovah, Thou dost quicken me, In Thy righteousness, Thou bringest out from distress my soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> And in Thy kindness cuttest off mine enemies, And hast destroyed all the adversaries of my soul, For I &#91;am&#93; Thy servant!</span>
