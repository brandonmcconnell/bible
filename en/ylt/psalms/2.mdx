<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:25–26; Revelation 17:14; Psalms 46:6"> Why have nations tumultuously assembled? And do peoples meditate vanity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:59; Matthew 27:1; Revelation 17:12–14"> Station themselves do kings of the earth, And princes have been united together, Against Jehovah, and against His Messiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:5; Luke 19:14; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> 'Let us draw off Their cords, And cast from us Their thick bands.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Psalms 59:8; Proverbs 1:26"> He who is sitting in the heavens doth laugh, The Lord doth mock at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Revelation 19:15; Psalms 110:5–6"> Then doth He speak unto them in His anger, And in His wrath He doth trouble them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Revelation 14:1; Ephesians 1:22"> 'And I — I have anointed My King, Upon Zion — My holy hill.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 1:5–6"> I declare concerning a statute: Jehovah said unto me, 'My Son Thou &#91;art&#93;, I to-day have brought thee forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 89:27; Psalms 72:8"> Ask of Me and I give nations — thy inheritance, And thy possession — the ends of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:5; Psalms 89:23"> Thou dost rule them with a sceptre of iron, As a vessel of a potter Thou dost crush them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Isaiah 60:10–11; Isaiah 60:3"> And now, O kings, act wisely, Be instructed, O judges of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 12:28–29"> Serve ye Jehovah with fear, And rejoice with trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 40:4; John 5:23; Psalms 84:12"> Kiss the Chosen One, lest He be angry, And ye lose the way, When His anger burneth but a little, O the happiness of all trusting in Him!</span>
