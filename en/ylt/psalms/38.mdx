<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> A Psalm of David, 'To cause to remember.' Jehovah, in Thy wrath reprove me not, Nor in Thy fury chastise me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> For Thine arrows have come down on me, And Thou lettest down upon me Thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> Soundness is not in my flesh, Because of Thine indignation, Peace is not in my bones because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> For mine iniquities have passed over my head, As a heavy burden — too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> Stunk — become corrupt have my wounds, Because of my folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> I have been bent down, I have been bowed down — unto excess, All the day I have gone mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> For my flanks have been full of drought, And soundness is not in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> I have been feeble and smitten — unto excess, I have roared from disquietude of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> Lord, before Thee &#91;is&#93; all my desire, And my sighing from Thee hath not been hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> My heart &#91;is&#93; panting, my power hath forsaken me, And the light of mine eyes, Even they are not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> My lovers and my friends over-against my plague stand. And my neighbours afar off have stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> And those seeking my soul lay a snare, And those seeking my evil Have spoken mischievous things, And they do deceits meditate all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> And I, as deaf, hear not. And as a dumb one who openeth not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> Yea, I am as a man who heareth not, And in his mouth are no reproofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> Because for Thee, O Jehovah, I have waited, Thou dost answer, O Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> When I said, 'Lest they rejoice over me, In the slipping of my foot against me they magnified themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> For I am ready to halt, And my pain &#91;is&#93; before me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> For mine iniquity I declare, I am sorry for my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> And mine enemies &#91;are&#93; lively, They have been strong, and those hating me without cause, Have been multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> And those paying evil for good accuse me, Because of my pursuing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> Do not forsake me, O Jehovah, My God, be not far from me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> Haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation!</span>
