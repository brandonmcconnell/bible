<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> To the Overseer. — By a servant of Jehovah, by David, who hath spoken to Jehovah the words of this song in the day Jehovah delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul, and he saith: — I love Thee, O Jehovah, my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 19:14"> Jehovah &#91;is&#93; my rock, and my bulwark, And my deliverer, My God &#91;is&#93; my rock, I trust in Him: My shield, and a horn of my salvation, My high tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 91:15; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 62:8"> The 'Praised One' I call Jehovah, And from my enemies I am saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> Compassed me have cords of death, And streams of the worthless make me afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> Cords of Sheol have surrounded me, Before me have been snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; Psalms 34:15"> In mine adversity I call Jehovah, And unto my God I cry. He heareth from His temple my voice, And My cry before Him cometh into His ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalms 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> And shake and tremble doth the earth, And foundations of hills are troubled, And they shake — because He hath wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> Gone up hath smoke by His nostrils, And fire from His mouth consumeth, Coals have been kindled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> And He inclineth the heavens, and cometh down, And thick darkness &#91;is&#93; under His feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; Psalms 99:1"> And He rideth on a cherub, and doth fly, And He flieth on wings of wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalms 91:1"> He maketh darkness His secret place, Round about Him His tabernacle, Darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> From the brightness over-against Him His thick clouds have passed on, Hail and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 140:10"> And thunder in the heavens doth Jehovah, And the Most High giveth forth His voice, Hail and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> And He sendeth His arrows and scattereth them, And much lightning, and crusheth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> And seen are the streams of waters, And revealed are foundations of the earth. From Thy rebuke, O Jehovah, From the breath of the spirit of Thine anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 57:3"> He sendeth from above — He taketh me, He draweth me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 142:6"> He delivereth me from my strong enemy, And from those hating me, For they have been stronger than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 46:11"> They go before me in a day of my calamity And Jehovah is for a support to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 118:5; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 18:36"> And He bringeth me forth to a large place, He draweth me out, because He delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Matthew 6:4; Psalms 58:11"> Jehovah doth recompense me According to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands, He doth return to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:102; Psalms 17:4; Psalms 26:1"> For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not done wickedly against my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:13; Psalms 119:112; Psalms 119:30"> For all His judgments &#91;are&#93; before me, And His statutes I turn not from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; Psalms 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> And I am perfect with him, And I keep myself from mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And Jehovah doth return to me, According to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands, Over-against his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Psalms 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> With the kind Thou showest Thyself kind, With a perfect man showest Thyself perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> With the pure Thou showest Thyself pure, And with the perverse showest Thyself a wrestler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> For Thou a poor people savest, And the eyes of the high causest to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> For Thou — Thou lightest my lamp, Jehovah my God enlighteneth my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> For by Thee I run — a troop! And by my God I leap a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> God! perfect &#91;is&#93; His way, The saying of Jehovah is tried, A shield &#91;is&#93; He to all those trusting in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; Psalms 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> For who &#91;is&#93; God besides Jehovah? And who &#91;is&#93; a rock save our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> God! who is girding me &#91;with&#93; strength, And He maketh perfect my way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> Making my feet like hinds, And on my high places causeth me to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> Teaching my hands for battle, And a bow of brass was brought down by my arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; Psalms 28:7"> And Thou givest to me the shield of Thy salvation, And Thy right hand doth support me, And Thy lowliness maketh me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> Thou enlargest my step under me, And mine ankles have not slidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; Psalms 44:5; Psalms 35:2"> I pursue mine enemies, and overtake them, And turn back not till they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> I smite them, and they are not able to rise, They fall under my feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; Psalms 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> And Thou girdest me &#91;with&#93; strength for battle, Causest my withstanders to bow under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Psalms 94:23"> As to mine enemies — Thou hast given to me the neck, As to those hating me — I cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> They cry, and there is no saviour, On Jehovah, and He doth not answer them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> And I beat them as dust before wind, As mire of the streets I empty them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> Thou dost deliver me From the strivings of the people, Thou placest me for a head of nations, A people I have not known do serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Psalms 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> At the hearing of the ear they hearken to me, Sons of a stranger feign obedience to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> Sons of a stranger fade away, And are slain out of their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 57:5; Psalms 18:2"> Jehovah liveth — and blessed &#91;is&#93; my rock, And exalted is the God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> God — who is giving vengeance to me, And He subdueth peoples under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 86:14"> My deliverer from mine enemies, Above my withstanders Thou raisest me, From a man of violence dost deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; Psalms 72:18–19"> Therefore I confess Thee among nations, O Jehovah, And to Thy name I sing praise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; Psalms 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Magnifying the salvation of His king, And doing kindness to His anointed, To David, and to his seed — unto the age!</span>
</div>
