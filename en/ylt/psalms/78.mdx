<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:4; Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> An Instruction of Asaph. Give ear, O my people, to my law, Incline your ear to sayings of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> I open with a simile my mouth, I bring forth hidden things of old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> That we have heard and do know, And our fathers have recounted to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> We do not hide from their sons, To a later generation recounting praises of Jehovah, And His strength, and His wonders that He hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> And He raiseth up a testimony in Jacob, And a law hath placed in Israel, That He commanded our fathers, To make them known to their sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> So that a later generation doth know, Sons who are born, do rise and recount to their sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> And place in God their confidence, And forget not the doings of God, But keep His commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; 2 Kings 17:14"> And they are not like their fathers, A generation apostate and rebellious, A generation! it hath not prepared its heart, Nor stedfast with God &#91;is&#93; its spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; 1 Samuel 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> Sons of Ephraim — armed bearers of bow, Have turned in a day of conflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> They have not kept the covenant of God, And in His law they have refused to walk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> And they forget His doings, And His wonders that He shewed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 19:11"> Before their fathers He hath done wonders, In the land of Egypt — the field of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> He cleft a sea, and causeth them to pass over, Yea, He causeth waters to stand as a heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:39; Exodus 14:24; Exodus 13:21–22"> And leadeth them with a cloud by day, And all the night with a light of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:41; Numbers 20:11; Exodus 17:6"> He cleaveth rocks in a wilderness, And giveth drink — as the great deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Numbers 20:10–11; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And bringeth out streams from a rock, And causeth waters to come down as rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Hebrews 3:16–19; Deuteronomy 9:12–22"> And they add still to sin against Him, To provoke the Most High in the dry place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And they try God in their heart, To ask food for their lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 11:4; Exodus 16:3"> And they speak against God — they said: 'Is God able to array a table in a wilderness?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Numbers 11:21–23; Psalms 78:41"> Lo, He hath smitten a rock, And waters flow, yea, streams overflow. 'Also — bread &#91;is&#93; He able to give? Doth He prepare flesh for His people?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Numbers 11:1–3"> Therefore hath Jehovah heard, And He sheweth Himself wroth, And fire hath been kindled against Jacob, And anger also hath gone up against Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:18–19; Hebrews 3:12; Jude 1:5"> For they have not believed in God, Nor have they trusted in His salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10; Psalms 33:9"> And He commandeth clouds from above, Yea, doors of the heavens He hath opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; Psalms 105:40; John 6:31–71"> And He raineth on them manna to eat, Yea, corn of heaven He hath given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Matthew 15:37; Psalms 103:20"> Food of the mighty hath each eaten, Venison He sent to them to satiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 11:31; Psalms 135:7"> He causeth an east wind to journey in the heavens, And leadeth by His strength a south wind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 11:18–19; Exodus 16:12–13; Numbers 11:32"> And He raineth on them flesh as dust, And as sand of the seas — winged fowl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And causeth &#91;it&#93; to fall in the midst of His camp, Round about His tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 11:19–20"> And they eat, and are greatly satisfied, And their desire He bringeth to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 11:33–34; Luke 16:19–23; Numbers 22:20–22"> They have not been estranged from their desire, Yet &#91;is&#93; their food in their mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> And the anger of God hath gone up against them, And He slayeth among their fat ones, And youths of Israel He caused to bend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 78:22; Psalms 78:11"> With all this they have sinned again, And have not believed in His wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 14:35; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65"> And He consumeth in vanity their days, And their years in trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Numbers 21:7; Judges 3:12–15"> If He slew them, then they sought Him, And turned back, and sought God earnestly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 41:14; Exodus 15:13"> And they remember that God &#91;is&#93; their rock, And God Most High their redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 29:13; Hosea 11:12"> And — they deceive Him with their mouth, And with their tongue do lie to Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Psalms 78:8; Psalms 51:10"> And their heart hath not been right with Him, And they have not been stedfast in His covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Numbers 14:18–20; Ezekiel 20:17"> And He — the Merciful One, Pardoneth iniquity, and destroyeth not, And hath often turned back His anger, And waketh not up all His fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 6:3; James 4:14; Job 7:7"> And He remembereth that they &#91;are&#93; flesh, A wind going on — and it returneth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 95:8–10; Hebrews 3:15–17"> How often do they provoke Him in the wilderness, Grieve Him in the desolate place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Hebrews 3:8–11; Numbers 14:22; Acts 7:39"> Yea, they turn back, and try God, And the Holy One of Israel have limited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 8:34; Psalms 78:11; Jeremiah 32:21"> They have not remembered His hand The day He ransomed them from the adversary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:22; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 3:19–20"> When He set His signs in Egypt, And His wonders in the field of Zoan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:29; Revelation 16:3–6; Exodus 7:17–21"> And He turneth to blood their streams, And their floods they drink not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 8:21–24; Psalms 105:30–31; Exodus 8:2–15"> He sendeth among them the beetle, and it consumeth them, And the frog, and it destroyeth them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 105:34–35; Amos 7:1–2"> And giveth to the caterpillar their increase, And their labour to the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 105:32–33; Exodus 9:18–34"> He destroyeth with hail their vine, And their sycamores with frost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 9:19; Exodus 9:28"> And delivereth up to the hail their beasts, And their cattle to the burning flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Exodus 12:13; 1 Kings 22:21–22; Lamentations 4:11"> He sendeth on them the fury of His anger, Wrath, and indignation, and distress — A discharge of evil messengers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; 2 Peter 2:4–5; Romans 8:32"> He pondereth a path for His anger, He kept not back their soul from death, Yea, their life to the pestilence He delivered up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 135:8; Psalms 106:22"> And He smiteth every first-born in Egypt, The first-fruit of the strong in tents of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 77:20; John 10:11–42"> And causeth His people to journey as a flock, And guideth them as a drove in a wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 14:19–20; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:27–28"> And He leadeth them confidently, And they have not been afraid, And their enemies hath the sea covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Exodus 15:17; Isaiah 11:9"> And He bringeth them in unto the border of His sanctuary, This mountain His right hand had got,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Joshua 13:7; Psalms 44:2; Nehemiah 9:22–25"> And casteth out nations from before them, And causeth them to fall in the line of inheritance, And causeth the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Judges 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 32:15–21; Psalms 78:40–41"> And they tempt and provoke God Most High, And His testimonies have not kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Ezekiel 20:27–28; Psalms 78:8"> And they turn back, And deal treacherously like their fathers, They have been turned like a deceitful bow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Leviticus 26:30; 1 Kings 12:31"> And make Him angry with their high places, And with their graven images make Him zealous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Lamentations 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:19"> God hath heard, and sheweth Himself wroth. And kicketh exceedingly against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:4–11; Jeremiah 26:6–9"> And He leaveth the tabernacle of Shiloh, The tent He had placed among men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Samuel 4:21–2; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And He giveth His strength to captivity, And His beauty into the hand of an adversary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Psalms 89:38; Isaiah 64:9; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> And delivereth up to the sword His people, And with His inheritance shewed Himself angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Jeremiah 16:9; Deuteronomy 32:22"> His young men hath fire consumed, And His virgins have not been praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:17; Job 27:15; Ezekiel 24:23"> His priests by the sword have fallen, And their widows weep not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> And the Lord waketh as a sleeper, As a mighty one crying aloud from wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 23:40; Job 40:12"> And He smiteth His adversaries backward, A reproach age-during He hath put on them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 2 Samuel 6:2; 2 Samuel 6:17"> And He kicketh against the tent of Joseph, And on the tribe of Ephraim hath not fixed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 87:2; Psalms 132:12–14; Ruth 4:17–22"> And He chooseth the tribe of Judah, With mount Zion that He loved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Job 26:7; 1 Chronicles 29:19; 1 Samuel 2:8"> And buildeth His sanctuary as a high place, Like the earth, He founded it to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11–12; Acts 13:22; Exodus 3:1"> And He fixeth on David His servant, And taketh him from the folds of a flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 11:2; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> From behind suckling ones He hath brought him in, To rule over Jacob His people, And over Israel His inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 2 Timothy 2:15; Isaiah 11:2–4"> And he ruleth them according to the integrity of his heart, And by the skilfulness of his hands leadeth them!</span>
