<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> To the Overseer. — 'On the Lilies,' by David. Save me, O God, for come have waters unto the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> I have sunk in deep mire, And there is no standing, I have come into the depths of the waters, And a flood hath overflown me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> I have been wearied with my calling, Burnt hath been my throat, Consumed have been mine eyes, waiting for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> Those hating me without cause Have been more than the hairs of my head, Mighty have been my destroyers, My lying enemies, That which I took not away — I bring back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> O God, Thou — Thou hast known Concerning my overturn, And my desolations from Thee have not been hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> Let not those waiting on Thee be ashamed because of me, O Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, Let not those seeking Thee Blush because of me, O God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> For because of Thee I have borne reproach, Shame hath covered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> A stranger I have been to my brother, And a foreigner to sons of my mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> For zeal for Thy house hath consumed me, And the reproaches of Thy reproachers Have fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> And I weep in the fasting of my soul, And it is for a reproach to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> And I make my clothing sackcloth, And I am to them for a simile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> Those sitting at the gate meditate concerning me, And those drinking strong drink, Play on instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> And I — my prayer &#91;is&#93; to Thee, O Jehovah, A time of good pleasure, O God, In the abundance of Thy kindness, Answer me in the truth of Thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> Deliver me from the mire, and let me not sink, Let me be delivered from those hating me, And from deep places of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> Let not a flood of waters overflow me, Nor let the deep swallow me up, Nor let the pit shut her mouth upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> Answer me, O Jehovah, for good &#91;is&#93; Thy kindness, According to the abundance Of Thy mercies turn Thou unto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> And hide not Thy face from Thy servant, For I am in distress — haste, answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> Be near unto my soul — redeem it, Because of mine enemies ransom me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> Thou — Thou hast known my reproach, And my shame, and my blushing, Before Thee &#91;are&#93; all mine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am sick, And I look for a bemoaner, and there is none, And for comforters, and I have found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> And they give for my food gall, And for my thirst cause me to drink vinegar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> Their table before them is for a snare, And for a recompence — for a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Darkened are their eyes from seeing, And their loins continually shake Thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> Pour upon them Thine indignation, And the fierceness of Thine anger doth seize them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> Their tower is desolated, In their tents there is no dweller.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> For they have pursued him Thou hast smitten, And recount of the pain of Thy pierced ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Give punishment for their iniquity, And they enter not into Thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> They are blotted out of the book of life, And with the righteous are not written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> And I &#91;am&#93; afflicted and pained, Thy salvation, O God, doth set me on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> I praise the name of God with a song, And I magnify Him with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> And it is better to Jehovah than an ox, A bullock — horned — hoofed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> The humble have seen — they rejoice, Ye who seek God — and your heart liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> For Jehovah hearkeneth unto the needy, And His bound ones He hath not despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> The heavens and earth do praise Him, Seas, and every moving thing in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> For God doth save Zion, And doth build the cities of Judah, And they have dwelt there, and possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> And the seed of His servants inherit it, And those loving His name dwell in it!</span>
