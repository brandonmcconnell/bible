<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm, a song of David. Rise doth God — scattered are His enemies! And those hating Him flee from His face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Psalms 37:20; Isaiah 9:18"> As the driving away of smoke Thou drivest away, As the melting of wax before fire, The wicked perish at the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Psalms 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> And the righteous rejoice, they exult before God, And they joy with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> Sing ye to God — praise His name, Raise up a highway for Him who is riding in deserts, In Jah &#91;is&#93; His name, and exult before Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; Psalms 82:3–4"> Father of the fatherless, and judge of the widows, &#91;Is&#93; God in His holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:33; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 107:10"> God — causing the lonely to dwell at home, Bringing out bound ones into prosperity, Only — the refractory have inhabited a dry place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> O God, in Thy going forth before Thy people, In Thy stepping through the wilderness, Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> The earth hath shaken, Yea, the heavens have dropped before God, This Sinai — before God, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Psalms 65:9–13; Psalms 78:24–27"> A shower of free-will gifts thou shakest out, O God. Thine inheritance, when it hath been weary, Thou hast established it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> Thy company have dwelt in it, Thou preparest in Thy goodness for the poor, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> The Lord doth give the saying, The female proclaimers &#91;are&#93; a numerous host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Kings of hosts flee utterly away, And a female inhabitant of the house apportioneth spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 105:37; Genesis 49:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Though ye do lie between two boundaries, Wings of a dove covered with silver, And her pinions with yellow gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> When the Mighty spreadeth kings in it, It doth snow in Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> A hill of God &#91;is&#93; the hill of Bashan, A hill of heights &#91;is&#93; the hill of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> Why do ye envy, O high hills, The hill God hath desired for His seat? Jehovah also doth tabernacle for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> The chariots of God &#91;are&#93; myriads, thousands of changes, The Lord &#91;is&#93; among them, in Sinai, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> Thou hast ascended on high, Thou hast taken captive captivity, Thou hast taken gifts for men, That even the refractory may rest, O Jah God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; the Lord, day by day He layeth on us. God Himself &#91;is&#93; our salvation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> God Himself &#91;is&#93; to us a God for deliverances, And Jehovah Lord hath the outgoings of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> Only — God doth smite The head of His enemies, The hairy crown of a habitual walker in his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> The Lord said: 'From Bashan I bring back, I bring back from the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> So that thou dashest thy foot in blood, &#91;In the blood of&#93; enemies — the tongue of Thy dogs.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> They have seen Thy goings, O God, Goings of my God, my king, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> Singers have been before, Behind &#91;are&#93; players on instruments, In the midst virgins playing with timbrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> In assemblies bless ye God, The Lord — from the fountain of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> There &#91;is&#93; little Benjamin their ruler, Heads of Judah their defence, Heads of Zebulun — heads of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> Thy God hath commanded thy strength, Be strong, O God, this Thou hast wrought for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> Because of Thy temple at Jerusalem, To Thee do kings bring a present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> Rebuke a beast of the reeds, a company of bulls, With calves of the peoples, Each humbling himself with pieces of silver, Scatter Thou peoples delighting in conflicts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> Come do fat ones out of Egypt, Cush causeth her hands to run to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> Kingdoms of the earth, sing ye to God, Praise ye the Lord. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> To him who is riding on the heavens of the heavens of old, Lo, He giveth with His voice a strong voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> Ascribe ye strength to God, Over Israel &#91;is&#93; His excellency, and His strength in the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13"> Fearful, O God, out of Thy sanctuaries, The God of Israel Himself, Giving strength and might to the people. Blessed &#91;is&#93; God!</span>
</div>
