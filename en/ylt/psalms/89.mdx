<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> An instruction, by Ethan the Ezrahite. Of the kind acts of Jehovah, to the age I sing, To all generations I make known Thy faithfulness with my mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> For I said, 'To the age is kindness built, The heavens! Thou dost establish Thy faithfulness in them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> I have made a covenant for My chosen, I have sworn to David My servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> 'Even to the age do I establish thy seed, And have built to generation and generation thy throne. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> and the heavens confess Thy wonders, O Jehovah, Thy faithfulness also &#91;is&#93; in an assembly of holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> For who in the sky, Compareth himself to Jehovah? Is like to Jehovah among sons of the mighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> God is very terrible, In the secret counsel of His holy ones, And fearful over all surrounding Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> O Jehovah, God of Hosts, Who &#91;is&#93; like Thee — a strong Jah? And Thy faithfulness &#91;is&#93; round about Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> Thou &#91;art&#93; ruler over the pride of the sea, In the lifting up of its billows Thou dost restrain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> Thou hast bruised Rahab, as one wounded. With the arm of Thy strength Thou hast scattered Thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> Thine &#91;are&#93; the heavens — the earth also &#91;is&#93; Thine, The habitable world and its fulness, Thou hast founded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> North and south Thou hast appointed them, Tabor and Hermon in Thy name do sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> Thou hast an arm with might, Strong is Thy hand — high Thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> Righteousness and judgment &#91;Are&#93; the fixed place of Thy throne, Kindness and truth go before Thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> O the happiness of the people knowing the shout, O Jehovah, in the light of Thy face they walk habitually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> In Thy name they rejoice all the day, And in Thy righteousness they are exalted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> For the beauty of their strength &#91;art&#93; Thou, And in Thy good will is our horn exalted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 47:9; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> For of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; our shield, And of the Holy One of Israel our king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; Psalms 89:3"> Then Thou hast spoken in vision, To Thy saint, yea, Thou sayest, I have placed help upon a mighty one, Exalted a chosen one out of the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> I have found David My servant, With My holy oil I have anointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 18:32–39; Psalms 89:13"> With whom My hand is established, My arm also doth strengthen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> An enemy exacteth not upon him, And a son of perverseness afflicteth him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; Psalms 18:40"> And I have beaten down before him his adversaries, And those hating him I plague,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 91:14"> And My faithfulness and kindness &#91;are&#93; with him, And in My name is his horn exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 72:8–11"> And I have set on the sea his hand, And on the rivers his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 95:1"> He proclaimeth me: 'Thou &#91;art&#93; my Father, My God, and the rock of my salvation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; Psalms 2:7"> I also first-born do appoint him, Highest of the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> To the age I keep for him My kindness, And My covenant &#91;is&#93; stedfast with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 89:36"> And I have set his seed for ever, And his throne as the days of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 119:53"> If his sons forsake My law, And in My judgments do not walk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 55:20"> If My statutes they pollute, And My commands do not keep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> I have looked after with a rod their transgression, And with strokes their iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> And My kindness I break not from him, Nor do I deal falsely in My faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> I profane not My covenant, And that which is going forth from My lips I change not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> Once I have sworn by My holiness, I lie not to David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 72:5; Psalms 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> His seed is to the age, And his throne &#91;is&#93; as the sun before Me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 104:19"> As the moon it is established — to the age, And the witness in the sky is stedfast. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; Psalms 106:40"> And Thou, Thou hast cast off, and dost reject, Thou hast shown Thyself wroth With Thine anointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; Psalms 74:7; Psalms 89:34–36"> Hast rejected the covenant of Thy servant, Thou hast polluted to the earth his crown,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> Thou hast broken down all his hedges, Thou hast made his fenced places a ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> Spoiled him have all passing by the way, He hath been a reproach to his neighbours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> Thou hast exalted the right hand of his adversaries, Thou hast caused all his enemies to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; Psalms 44:10"> Also — Thou turnest back the sharpness of his sword, And hast not established him in battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; Psalms 89:39"> Hast caused &#91;him&#93; to cease from his brightness, And his throne to the earth hast cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 44:15; Micah 7:10"> Thou hast shortened the days of his youth, Hast covered him over &#91;with&#93; shame. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 78:63; Psalms 88:14"> Till when, O Jehovah, art Thou hidden? For ever doth Thy fury burn as fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> Remember, I pray Thee, what &#91;is&#93; life-time? Wherefore in vain hast Thou created All the sons of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> Who &#91;is&#93; the man that liveth, and doth not see death? He delivereth his soul from the hand of Sheol. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> Where &#91;are&#93; Thy former kindnesses, O Lord. Thou hast sworn to David in Thy faithfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 74:22; Psalms 74:18; Psalms 69:9"> Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Thy servants, I have borne in my bosom all the strivings of the peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; Psalms 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> Wherewith Thine enemies reproached, O Jehovah, Wherewith they have reproached The steps of Thine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Psalms 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah to the age. Amen, and amen!</span>
</div>
