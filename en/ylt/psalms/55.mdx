<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 86:6; Psalms 61:1"> To the Overseer with stringed instruments. — An instruction, by David. Give ear, O God, &#91;to&#93; my prayer, And hide not from my supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Psalms 66:19; Psalms 77:3"> Attend to me, and answer me, I mourn in my meditation, and make a noise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:7–8; Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11"> Because of the voice of an enemy, Because of the oppression of the wicked, For they cause sorrow to move against me, And in anger they hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Mark 14:33–34; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> My heart is pained within me, And terrors of death have fallen on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Job 21:6; 2 Samuel 15:14"> Fear and trembling come in to me, And horror doth cover me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalms 139:9"> And I say, 'Who doth give to me a pinion as a dove? I fly away and rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; 2 Samuel 17:21–22; Jeremiah 37:12"> Lo, I move far off, I lodge in a wilderness. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> I hasten escape for myself, From a rushing wind, from a whirlwind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> Swallow up, O Lord, divide their tongue, For I saw violence and strife in a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> By day and by night they go round it, on its walls. Both iniquity and perverseness &#91;are&#93; in its midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalms 10:7; Psalms 5:9"> Mischiefs &#91;are&#93; in its midst. Fraud and deceit depart not from its street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Matthew 26:21–23"> For an enemy reproacheth me not, or I bear &#91;it&#93;, He who is hating me Hath not magnified himself against me, Or I hide from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> But thou, a man — as mine equal, My familiar friend, and mine acquaintance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> When together we sweeten counsel, Into the house of God we walk in company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 59:13; Psalms 64:7; Psalms 9:17"> Desolations &#91;are&#93; upon them, They go down &#91;to&#93; Sheol — alive, For wickedness &#91;is&#93; in their dwelling, in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 109:4; Psalms 73:28"> I — to God I call, and Jehovah saveth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalms 141:2"> Evening, and morning, and noon, I meditate, and make a noise, and He heareth my voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:3; Psalms 3:6–7; 2 Samuel 22:1"> He hath ransomed in peace my soul From him who is near to me, For with the multitude they were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Revelation 6:10–11; Jeremiah 48:11"> God doth hear and afflict them, And He sitteth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, and fear not God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalms 89:34; Psalms 7:4"> He hath sent forth his hands against his well-wishers, He hath polluted his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 5:3–4"> Sweeter than honey hath been his mouth, And his heart &#91;is&#93; war! Softer have been his words than oil, And they &#91;are&#93; drawn &#91;swords&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 37:5; Philippians 4:6–7"> Cast on Jehovah that which He hath given thee, And He doth sustain thee, He doth not suffer for ever the moving of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 5:6; Job 15:32"> And Thou, O God, dost bring them down To a pit of destruction, Men of blood and deceit reach not to half their days, And I — I do trust in Thee!</span>
