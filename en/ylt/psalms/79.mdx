<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> A Psalm of Asaph. O God, nations have come into Thy inheritance, They have defiled Thy holy temple, They made Jerusalem become heaps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> They gave the dead bodies of Thy servants Food for the fowls of the heavens, The flesh of Thy saints For the wild beast of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> They have shed their blood As water round about Jerusalem, And there is none burying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> We have been a reproach to our neighbours, A scorn and a derision to our surrounders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> Till when, O Jehovah? art Thou angry for ever? Thy jealousy doth burn as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> Pour Thy fury on the nations who have not known Thee, And on kingdoms that have not called in Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> For &#91;one&#93; hath devoured Jacob, And his habitation they have made desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> Remember not for us the iniquities of forefathers, Haste, let Thy mercies go before us, For we have been very weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> Help us, O God of our salvation, Because of the honour of Thy name, And deliver us, and cover over our sins, For Thy name's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> Why do the nations say, 'Where &#91;is&#93; their God?' Let be known among the nations before our eyes, The vengeance of the blood of Thy servants that is shed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> Let the groaning of the prisoner come in before Thee, According to the greatness of Thine arm, Leave Thou the sons of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> And turn Thou back to our neighbours, Sevenfold unto their bosom, their reproach, Wherewith they reproached Thee, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> And we, Thy people, and the flock of Thy pasture, We give thanks to Thee to the age, To all generations we recount Thy praise!</span>
