<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 135:6; Daniel 4:35; Isaiah 46:10"> And our God &#91;is&#93; in the heavens, All that He hath pleased He hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:28; Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:3–5"> Their idols &#91;are&#93; silver and gold, work of man's hands,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Ears they have, and they hear not, A nose they have, and they smell not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Their hands, but they handle not, Their feet, and they walk not;</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:26; Proverbs 30:5; Acts 10:35"> Ye fearing Jehovah, trust in Jehovah, 'Their help and their shield &#91;is&#93; He.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 136:23; Ephesians 1:3; Isaiah 44:21"> Jehovah hath remembered us, He blesseth, He blesseth the house of Israel, He blesseth the house of Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:1; Luke 1:50; Psalms 128:1"> He blesseth those fearing Jehovah, The small with the great.</span>
