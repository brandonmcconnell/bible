<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:15; Habakkuk 3:1; 1 Peter 4:19"> 'The Erring One,' by David, that he sung to Jehovah concerning the words of Cush a Benjamite. O Jehovah, my God, in Thee I have trusted, Save me from all my pursuers, and deliver me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Isaiah 38:13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Lest he tear as a lion my soul, Rending, and there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; 1 Samuel 24:11; 1 Samuel 26:18–19"> O Jehovah, my God, if I have done this, If there is iniquity in my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:20; 1 Samuel 22:14; 1 Samuel 24:17–19"> If I have done my well-wisher evil, And draw mine adversary without cause,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 17:13"> An enemy pursueth my soul, and overtaketh, And treadeth down to the earth my life, And my honour placeth in the dust. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 35:23; Psalms 3:7"> Rise, O Jehovah, in Thine anger, Be lifted up at the wrath of mine adversaries, And awake Thou for me: Judgment Thou hast commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> And a company of peoples compass Thee, And over it on high turn Thou back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 43:1"> Jehovah doth judge the peoples; Judge me, O Jehovah, According to my righteousness, And according to mine integrity on me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:20; Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 20:12"> Let, I pray Thee be ended the evil of the wicked, And establish Thou the righteous, And a trier of hearts and reins is the righteous God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 125:4; Psalms 84:11; Proverbs 28:18"> My shield &#91;is&#93; on God, Saviour of the upright in heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:2; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 94:15"> God &#91;is&#93; a righteous judge, And He is not angry at all times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:10; Deuteronomy 32:41"> If &#91;one&#93; turn not, His sword he sharpeneth, His bow he hath trodden — He prepareth it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 45:5; Psalms 64:7"> Yea, for him He hath prepared Instruments of death, His arrows for burning pursuers He maketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Isaiah 33:11"> Lo, he travaileth &#91;with&#93; iniquity, And he hath conceived perverseness, And hath brought forth falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 4:8; Psalms 141:10; Proverbs 26:27"> A pit he hath prepared, and he diggeth it, And he falleth into a ditch he maketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esther 9:25; Psalms 37:12–13; 1 Kings 2:32"> Return doth his perverseness on his head, And on his crown his violence cometh down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:2; Psalms 98:2; Psalms 71:15–16"> I thank Jehovah, According to His righteousness, And praise the name of Jehovah Most High!</span>
