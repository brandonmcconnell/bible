<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:7; Psalms 66:1; Psalms 100:1–2"> To the Overseer. — 'On the Gittith.' By Asaph. Cry aloud to God our strength, Shout to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 149:1–3; Psalms 92:3; Colossians 3:16"> Lift up a song, and give out a timbrel, A pleasant harp with psaltery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 10:1–10; Numbers 15:3; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> Blow in the month a trumpet, In the new moon, at the day of our festival,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For a statute to Israel it &#91;is&#93;, An ordinance of the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 114:1; Deuteronomy 28:49; 1 Corinthians 14:21–22"> A testimony on Joseph He hath placed it, In his going forth over the land of Egypt. A lip, I have not known — I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 10:27; Exodus 6:6"> From the burden his shoulder I turned aside, His hands from the basket pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 19:19; Numbers 20:13; Exodus 2:23"> In distress thou hast called and I deliver thee, I answer thee in the secret place of thunder, I try thee by the waters of Meribah. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:7; John 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> Hear, O My people, and I testify to thee, O Israel, if thou dost hearken to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; Deuteronomy 32:12; Deuteronomy 6:14"> There is not in thee a strange god, And thou bowest not thyself to a strange god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah thy God, Who bringeth thee up out of the land of Egypt. Enlarge thy mouth, and I fill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 2:11–13"> But, My people hearkened not to My voice, And Israel hath not consented to Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> And I send them away in the enmity of their heart, They walk in their own counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 5:29; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> O that My people were hearkening to Me, Israel in My ways would walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 2:20–23; Numbers 14:9"> As a little thing their enemies I cause to bow, And against their adversaries I turn back My hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 1:30; Exodus 20:5"> Those hating Jehovah feign obedience to Him, But their time is — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 147:14; 1 Samuel 14:25–26"> He causeth him to eat of the fat of wheat, And &#91;with&#93; honey from a rock I satisfy thee!</span>
