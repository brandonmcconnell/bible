<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6; Exodus 15:2"> By David. Jehovah &#91;is&#93; my light and my salvation, Whom do I fear? Jehovah &#91;is&#93; the strength of my life, Of whom am I afraid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Psalms 14:4; Psalms 9:3"> When evil doers come near to me to eat my flesh, My adversaries and mine enemies to me, They have stumbled and fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 3:6; 1 Peter 3:14; Romans 8:35–37"> Though a host doth encamp against me, My heart doth not fear, Though war riseth up against me, In this I &#91;am&#93; confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Psalms 23:6; Psalms 65:4"> One &#91;thing&#93; I asked of Jehovah — it I seek. My dwelling in the house of Jehovah, All the days of my life, To look on the pleasantness of Jehovah, And to inquire in His temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> For He hideth me in a tabernacle in the day of evil, He hideth me in a secret place of His tent, On a rock he raiseth me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 107:22"> And now, lifted up is my head, Above my enemies — my surrounders, And I sacrifice in His tent sacrifices of shouting, I sing, yea, I sing praise to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 130:2–4; Psalms 13:3; Psalms 5:2"> Hear, O Jehovah, my voice — I call, And favour me, and answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> To Thee said my heart 'They sought my face, Thy face, O Jehovah, I seek.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> Hide not Thy face from me, Turn not aside in anger Thy servant, My help Thou hast been. Leave me not, nor forsake me, O God of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Isaiah 40:11; John 16:32"> When my father and my mother Have forsaken me, then doth Jehovah gather me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8; Psalms 86:11"> Shew me, O Jehovah, Thy way, And lead me in a path of uprightness, For the sake of my beholders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 41:11; Matthew 26:59–60"> Give me not to the will of my adversaries, For risen against me have false witnesses, And they breathe out violence to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 142:5; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 2 Corinthians 4:8–14"> I had not believed to look on the goodness of Jehovah In the land of the living!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 31:24; Isaiah 30:18"> Look unto Jehovah — be strong, And He doth strengthen thy heart, Yea, look unto Jehovah!</span>
