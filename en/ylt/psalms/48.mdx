<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 4:1; Zechariah 8:3; Psalms 96:4"> A Song, a Psalm, by sons of Korah. Great &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, and praised greatly, In the city of our God — His holy hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:2; Lamentations 2:15; Matthew 5:35"> Beautiful &#91;for&#93; elevation, A joy of all the land, &#91;is&#93; Mount Zion, The sides of the north, the city of a great king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 46:7"> God in her high places is known for a tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6–19; Revelation 17:12–14; Revelation 20:8–9"> For, lo, the kings met, they passed by together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 2 Kings 7:6–7; 2 Kings 19:35–37"> They have seen — so they have marvelled, They have been troubled, they were hastened away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 13:6–8; Isaiah 21:3"> Trembling hath seized them there, Pain, as of a travailing woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Ezekiel 27:25–26; 1 Kings 22:48"> By an east wind Thou shiverest ships of Tarshish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 87:5; Psalms 78:3–6; Psalms 46:5"> As we have heard, so we have seen, In the city of Jehovah of hosts, In the city of our God, God doth establish her — to the age. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> We have thought, O God, of Thy kindness, In the midst of Thy temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; Psalms 113:3"> As &#91;is&#93; Thy name, O God, so &#91;is&#93; Thy praise, Over the ends of the earth, Righteousness hath filled Thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> Rejoice doth Mount Zion, The daughters of Judah are joyful, For the sake of Thy judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; Matthew 24:1–2"> Compass Zion, and go round her, count her towers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 122:7; Deuteronomy 11:19"> Set your heart to her bulwark, Consider her high places, So that ye recount to a later generation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 23:3–4; Psalms 73:24"> That this God &#91;is&#93; our God — To the age and for ever, He — he doth lead us over death!</span>
