<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 10:4; Psalms 53:1; Romans 3:10–12"> To the Overseer. — By David. A fool hath said in his heart, 'God is not;' They have done corruptly, They have done abominable actions, There is not a doer of good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 3:11; Psalms 33:13–14; Hebrews 11:6"> Jehovah from the heavens Hath looked on the sons of men, To see if there is a wise one — seeking God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 3:10–12; Isaiah 53:6; Isaiah 64:6"> The whole have turned aside, Together they have been filthy: There is not a doer of good, not even one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Jeremiah 10:25; Psalms 82:5"> Have all working iniquity not known? Those consuming my people have eaten bread, Jehovah they have not called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 73:15; Psalms 24:6"> There they have feared a fear, For God &#91;is&#93; in the generation of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 42:10; Psalms 4:2"> The counsel of the poor ye cause to stink, Because Jehovah &#91;is&#93; his refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 53:6; Psalms 85:1"> 'Who doth give from Zion the salvation of Israel? When Jehovah doth turn back &#91;To&#93; a captivity of His people, Jacob doth rejoice — Israel is glad!</span>
