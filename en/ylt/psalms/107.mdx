<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> 'Give ye thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age &#91;is&#93; His kindness:'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 1:74; Galatians 3:13"> Let the redeemed of Jehovah say, Whom He redeemed from the hand of an adversary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:47; Isaiah 43:5–6; Revelation 5:9"> And from the lands hath gathered them, From east and from west, From north, and from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Ezekiel 34:6"> They wandered in a wilderness, in a desert by the way, A city of habitation they have not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:2–3; 1 Samuel 30:11–12; Lamentations 2:19"> Hungry — yea — thirsty, Their soul in them becometh feeble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> And they cry unto Jehovah in their adversity, From their distress He delivereth them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 77:20; Hebrews 11:9–10"> And causeth them to tread in a right way, To go unto a city of habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:31; Psalms 147:1; Daniel 4:2–3"> They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 34:10"> For He hath satisfied a longing soul, And a hungry soul hath filled &#91;with&#93; goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Micah 7:8; Romans 6:20–21"> Inhabitants of dark places and death-shade, Prisoners of affliction and of iron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Psalms 78:40; Romans 1:28"> Because they changed the saying of God, And the counsel of the Most High despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Exodus 5:18–19; Exodus 2:23"> And He humbleth with labour their heart, They have been feeble, and there is no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:6; Judges 4:3"> And they cry unto Jehovah in their adversity, From their distresses He saveth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 42:16; Ephesians 5:8; Psalms 116:16"> He bringeth them out from the dark place, And death-shade, And their bands He draweth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21; Psalms 107:31; Psalms 107:8"> They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; Judges 16:3; Micah 2:13"> For He hath broken doors of brass, And bars of iron He hath cut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:39; Proverbs 1:22; Psalms 38:1–8"> Fools, by means of their transgression, And by their iniquities, afflict themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 88:3; Job 33:19–22"> All food doth their soul abominate, And they come nigh unto the gates of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–35; Psalms 34:4–6; Psalms 107:13"> And cry unto Jehovah in their adversity, From their distresses He saveth them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:8; Psalms 103:3–4; Psalms 147:15"> He sendeth His word and healeth them, And delivereth from their destructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 107:15; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 118:17; Psalms 9:11"> And they sacrifice sacrifices of thanksgiving, And recount His works with singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:17; Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 27:26"> Those going down &#91;to&#93; the sea in ships, Doing business in many waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–27; Psalms 95:5; Job 38:8–11"> They have seen the works of Jehovah, And His wonders in the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Matthew 8:24; Psalms 148:8"> And He saith, and appointeth a tempest, And it lifteth up its billows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Psalms 119:28; Isaiah 13:7"> They go up &#91;to&#93; the heavens, they go down &#91;to&#93; the depths, Their soul in evil is melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 19:3"> They reel to and fro, and move as a drunkard, And all their wisdom is swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:25; Psalms 107:13; Psalms 107:19"> And they cry to Jehovah in their adversity, And from their distresses He bringeth them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Psalms 65:7; Luke 8:23–25"> He establisheth a whirlwind to a calm, And hushed are their billows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:21"> And they rejoice because they are quiet, And He leadeth them to the haven of their desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 103:2; Psalms 107:21"> They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 99:5"> And they exalt Him in the assembly of the people, And in the seat of the elders praise Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Isaiah 42:15; Amos 4:7–8"> He maketh rivers become a wilderness, And fountains of waters become dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10; Ezekiel 47:11"> A fruitful land becometh a barren place, For the wickedness of its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 114:8; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 41:17–19"> He maketh a wilderness become a pool of water, And a dry land become fountains of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 107:7; Luke 1:53; Acts 17:26"> And He causeth the hungry to dwell there, And they prepare a city of habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 2 Corinthians 9:10; Jeremiah 29:5"> And they sow fields, and plant vineyards, And they make fruits of increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 1:7; Genesis 12:2; Genesis 17:20"> And He blesseth them, and they multiply exceedingly, And their cattle He doth not diminish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; 2 Kings 10:32; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> And they are diminished, and bow down, By restraint, evil, and sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 12:24; Job 12:21; Daniel 4:33"> He is pouring contempt upon nobles, And causeth them to wander in vacancy — no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Job 21:11; Esther 8:15–17"> And setteth on high the needy from affliction, And placeth families as a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 63:11; Job 22:19; Romans 3:19"> The upright do see and rejoice, And all perversity hath shut her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Jeremiah 9:12; Psalms 64:9"> Who &#91;is&#93; wise, and observeth these? They understand the kind acts of Jehovah!</span>
