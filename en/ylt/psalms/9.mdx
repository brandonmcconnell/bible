<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Deuteronomy 9:14; Proverbs 13:9"> Thou hast rebuked nations, Thou hast destroyed the wicked, Their name Thou hast blotted out to the age and for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:22–23; Exodus 14:13; Micah 7:8"> O thou Enemy, Finished have been destructions for ever, As to cities thou hast plucked up, Perished hath their memorial with them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 91:1–2; Nahum 1:7"> And Jehovah is a tower for the bruised, A tower for times of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:14; Proverbs 18:10; Isaiah 26:3–4"> They trust in Thee who do know Thy name, For Thou hast not forsaken Those seeking Thee, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:22; Psalms 96:1–2; Psalms 148:1–5"> Sing ye praise to Jehovah, inhabiting Zion, Declare ye among the peoples His acts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Exodus 3:7; Revelation 16:6"> For He who is seeking for blood Them hath remembered, He hath not forgotten the cry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 30:3; Psalms 107:18"> Favour me, O Jehovah, See mine affliction by those hating me, Thou who liftest me up from the gates of death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 51:12; Psalms 106:2"> So that I recount all Thy praise, In the gates of the daughter of Zion. I rejoice on Thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 35:8"> Sunk have nations in a pit they made, In a net that they hid hath their foot been captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Judges 1:7; Proverbs 12:13"> Jehovah hath been known, Judgment He hath done, By a work of his hands Hath the wicked been snared. Meditation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Job 8:13; Romans 2:8–9"> The wicked do turn back to Sheol, All nations forgetting God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Proverbs 23:18; Proverbs 24:14"> For not for ever is the needy forgotten, The hope of the humble lost to the age.</span>
