<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> To the Overseer. — By sons of Korah. A Psalm. Hear this, all ye peoples, Give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> Both low and high, together rich and needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> My mouth speaketh wise things, And the meditations of my heart &#91;are&#93; things of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> I incline to a simile mine ear, I open with a harp my riddle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> Why do I fear in days of evil? The iniquity of my supplanters doth compass me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> Those trusting on their wealth, And in the multitude of their riches, Do shew themselves foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> A brother doth no one at all ransom, He doth not give to God his atonement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> And precious &#91;is&#93; the redemption of their soul, And it hath ceased — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> And still he liveth for ever, He seeth not the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> For he seeth wise men die, Together the foolish and brutish perish, And have left to others their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> Their heart &#91;is&#93;: Their houses &#91;are&#93; to the age, Their tabernacles to all generations. They proclaimed their names over the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> And man in honour doth not remain, He hath been like the beasts, they have been cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> This their way &#91;is&#93; folly for them, And their posterity with their sayings are pleased. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> As sheep for Sheol they have set themselves, Death doth afflict them, And the upright rule over them in the morning, And their form &#91;is&#93; for consumption. Sheol &#91;is&#93; a dwelling for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> Only, God doth ransom my soul from the hand of Sheol, For He doth receive me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> Fear not, when one maketh wealth, When the honour of his house is abundant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> For at his death he receiveth nothing, His honour goeth not down after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> For his soul in his life he blesseth, (And they praise thee when thou dost well for thyself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> It cometh to the generation of his fathers, For ever they see not the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> Man in honour, who understandest not, Hath been like the beasts, they have been cut off!</span>
