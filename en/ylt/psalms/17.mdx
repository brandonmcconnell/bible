<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; Ezekiel 18:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9"> From before thee my judgment doth go out; Thine eyes do see uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Job 23:10; Psalms 26:2"> Thou hast proved my heart, Thou hast inspected by night, Thou hast tried me, Thou findest nothing; My thoughts pass not over my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 119:9–11; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> As to doings of man, Through a word of Thy lips I have observed The paths of a destroyer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; Psalms 119:133; 1 Samuel 2:9"> To uphold my goings in Thy paths, My steps have not slidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 86:7; Psalms 88:2"> I — I called Thee, for Thou dost answer me, O God, incline Thine ear to me, hear my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 60:5; Psalms 5:11–12; Psalms 31:21"> Separate wonderfully Thy kindness, O Saviour of the confiding, By Thy right hand, from withstanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 91:1"> Keep me as the apple, the daughter of the eye; In shadow of Thy wings thou dost hide me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 7:5"> From the face of the wicked who spoiled me. Mine enemies in soul go round against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:3; Psalms 123:4; Psalms 31:18"> Their fat they have closed up, Their mouths have spoken with pride:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:14; Proverbs 6:13–14; Psalms 10:8–10"> 'Our steps now have compassed &#91;him&#93;;' Their eyes they set to turn aside in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; 1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 10:9"> His likeness as a lion desirous to tear, As a young lion dwelling in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 44:26; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> Arise, O Jehovah, go before his face, Cause him to bend. Deliver my soul from the wicked, Thy sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:18; John 17:14; Luke 16:25"> From men, Thy hand, O Jehovah, From men of the world, their portion &#91;is&#93; in life, And &#91;with&#93; Thy hidden things Thou fillest their belly, They are satisfied &#91;with&#93; sons; And have left their abundance to their sucklings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; Matthew 5:6; Psalms 11:7"> I — in righteousness, I see Thy face; I am satisfied, in awaking, &#91;with&#93; Thy form!</span>
