<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:12; 1 Chronicles 12:38; 1 Chronicles 11:1–3"> Their judges have been released by the sides of a rock, And they have heard my sayings, For they have been pleasant.</span>
