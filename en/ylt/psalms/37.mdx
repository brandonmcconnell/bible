<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 24:19"> By David. Do not fret because of evil doers, Be not envious against doers of iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 92:7; Psalms 90:5–6; James 1:10–11"> For as grass speedily they are cut off, And as the greenness of the tender grass do fade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Matthew 6:31–33; Psalms 62:8"> Trust in Jehovah, and do good, Dwell &#91;in&#93; the land, and enjoy faithfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 145:19; John 15:16"> And delight thyself on Jehovah, And He giveth to thee the petitions of thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:6–7"> Roll on Jehovah thy way, And trust upon Him, and He worketh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Job 11:17; Isaiah 58:8"> And hath brought out as light thy righteousness, And thy judgment as noon-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:7–11; Psalms 27:14; Psalms 62:5"> Be silent for Jehovah, and stay thyself for Him, Do not fret because of him Who is making prosperous his way, Because of a man doing wicked devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Proverbs 16:32; James 1:19–20"> Desist from anger, and forsake fury, Fret not thyself only to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:21; Psalms 25:13; Psalms 37:29"> For evil doers are cut off, As to those waiting on Jehovah, they possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 103:16; Psalms 37:35–36"> And yet a little, and the wicked is not, And thou hast considered his place, and it is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 26:3"> And the humble do possess the land, And they have delighted themselves In the abundance of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Psalms 35:16; Psalms 31:13"> The wicked is devising against the righteous, And gnashing against him his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 1:26; 1 Samuel 26:10"> The Lord doth laugh at him, For He hath seen that his day cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Psalms 11:2; 1 John 3:12"> A sword have the wicked opened, And they have trodden their bow, To cause to fall the poor and needy, To slaughter the upright of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14–15; Psalms 35:8; Jeremiah 51:56"> Their sword doth enter into their own heart, And their bows are shivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; 1 Timothy 6:6"> Better &#91;is&#93; the little of the righteous, Than the store of many wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 63:8; Psalms 145:14"> For the arms of the wicked are shivered, And Jehovah is sustaining the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:6; Romans 6:23; Psalms 103:17"> Jehovah knoweth the days of the perfect, And their inheritance is — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:20–22"> They are not ashamed in a time of evil, And in days of famine they are satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 68:2; Psalms 102:3; Luke 13:3"> But the wicked perish, and the enemies of Jehovah, As the preciousness of lambs, Have been consumed, In smoke they have been consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Psalms 112:5; Proverbs 22:7"> The wicked is borrowing and repayeth not, And the righteous is gracious and giving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Galatians 3:13"> For His blessed ones do possess the land, And His reviled ones are cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Psalms 119:133; Proverbs 4:26"> From Jehovah &#91;are&#93; the steps of a man, They have been prepared, And his way he desireth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 145:14; Psalms 94:18"> When he falleth, he is not cast down, For Jehovah is sustaining his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 37:28; Psalms 112:2"> Young I have been, I have also become old, And I have not seen the righteous forsaken, And his seed seeking bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Matthew 5:7; Psalms 37:21"> All the day he is gracious and lending, And his seed &#91;is&#93; for a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; Proverbs 16:6; 1 John 2:16–17"> Turn aside from evil, and do good, and dwell to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 21:10"> For Jehovah is loving judgment, And He doth not forsake His saintly ones, To the age they have been kept, And the seed of the wicked is cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:21; Psalms 37:9; 2 Peter 3:13"> The righteous possess the land, And they dwell for ever on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 25:11–13; Ephesians 4:29"> The mouth of the righteous uttereth wisdom, And his tongue speaketh judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 40:8; Deuteronomy 6:6; Isaiah 51:7"> The law of his God &#91;is&#93; his heart, His steps do not slide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Psalms 10:8–10; Psalms 37:12"> The wicked is watching for the righteous, And is seeking to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 109:31; 2 Peter 2:9; Romans 8:1"> Jehovah doth not leave him in his hand, Nor condemn him in his being judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 37:9; Proverbs 20:22"> Look unto Jehovah, and keep His way, And He doth exalt thee to possess the land, In the wicked being cut off — thou seest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 5:3; Ezekiel 31:18; Isaiah 14:14–19"> I have seen the wicked terrible, And spreading as a green native plant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Acts 12:22–23; Isaiah 10:16–19"> And he passeth away, and lo, he is not, And I seek him, and he is not found!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 57:1–2; 2 Timothy 4:6–8"> Observe the perfect, and see the upright, For the latter end of each &#91;is&#93; peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Proverbs 14:32; Psalms 52:5; Psalms 1:4–6"> And transgressors were destroyed together, The latter end of the wicked was cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 91:15; Isaiah 12:2"> And the salvation of the righteous &#91;is&#93; from Jehovah, Their strong place in a time of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 31:5; 1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 6:23"> And Jehovah doth help them and deliver them, He delivereth them from the wicked, And saveth them, Because they trusted in Him!</span>
