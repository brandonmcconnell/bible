<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 28:7"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. Jehovah, in Thy strength is the king joyful, In Thy salvation how greatly he rejoiceth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Psalms 20:4–5; Psalms 2:8–9"> The desire of his heart Thou gavest to him, And the request of his lips Thou hast not withheld. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> For Thou puttest before him blessings of goodness, Thou settest on his head a crown of fine gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalms 119:77"> Life he hath asked from Thee, Thou hast given to him — length of days, Age-during — and for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> Great &#91;is&#93; his honour in Thy salvation, Honour and majesty Thou placest on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> For Thou makest him blessings for ever, Thou dost cause him to rejoice with joy, By Thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 91:2; Psalms 20:7–8"> For the king is trusting in Jehovah, And in the kindness of the Most High He is not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 110:1–2"> Thy hand cometh to all Thine enemies, Thy right hand doth find Thy haters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> Thou makest them as a furnace of fire, At the time of Thy presence. Jehovah in His anger doth swallow them, And fire doth devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> Their fruit from earth Thou destroyest, And their seed from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:2; Psalms 2:1; Psalms 31:13"> For they stretched out against Thee evil, They devised a wicked device, they prevail not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> For Thou makest them a butt, When Thy strings Thou preparest against their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 19:1–6; Revelation 15:3–4"> Be Thou exalted, O Jehovah in, Thy strength, We sing and we praise Thy might!</span>
