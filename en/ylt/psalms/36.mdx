<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> To the Overseer. — By a servant of Jehovah, by David. The transgression of the wicked Is affirming within my heart, 'Fear of God is not before his eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> For he made &#91;it&#93; smooth to himself in his eyes, To find his iniquity to be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremiah 4:22"> The words of his mouth &#91;are&#93; iniquity and deceit, He ceased to act prudently — to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> Iniquity he deviseth on his bed, He stationeth himself on a way not good, Evil he doth not refuse.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> O Jehovah, in the heavens &#91;is&#93; Thy kindness, Thy faithfulness &#91;is&#93; unto the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> Thy righteousness &#91;is&#93; as mountains of God, Thy judgments &#91;are&#93; a great deep. Man and beast Thou savest, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> How precious &#91;is&#93; Thy kindness, O God, And the sons of men In the shadow of Thy wings do trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> They are filled from the fatness of Thy house, And the stream of Thy delights Thou dost cause them to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> For with Thee &#91;is&#93; a fountain of life, In Thy light we see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> Draw out Thy kindness to those knowing Thee, And Thy righteousness to the upright of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> Let not a foot of pride meet me, And a hand of the wicked let not move me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> There have workers of iniquity fallen, They have been overthrown, And have not been able to arise!</span>
