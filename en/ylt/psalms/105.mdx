<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 145:11–12; Romans 10:13"> Give ye thanks to Jehovah — call ye in His name, Make known among the peoples His acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:27; Psalms 98:5; Isaiah 12:5–6"> Sing ye to Him — sing praise to Him, Meditate ye on all His wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:29"> Boast yourselves in His Holy Name, The heart of those seeking Jehovah rejoiceth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Zephaniah 2:2–3; 2 Chronicles 6:41"> Seek ye Jehovah and His strength, Seek ye His face continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:11; Psalms 119:13; Deuteronomy 32:7"> Remember His wonders that He did, His signs and the judgments of His mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; John 15:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> O seed of Abraham, His servant, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 26:17–18; Exodus 20:2"> He &#91;is&#93; Jehovah our God, In all the earth &#91;are&#93; His judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 106:45; Psalms 111:5"> He hath remembered to the age His covenant, The word He commanded to a thousand generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:2; Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 26:3"> That He hath made with Abraham, And His oath to Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; 2 Samuel 23:5; Genesis 28:13–15"> And doth establish it to Jacob for a statute, To Israel — a covenant age-during,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 15:18; Psalms 78:55"> Saying, 'To thee I give the land of Canaan, The portion of your inheritance,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Hebrews 11:9"> In their being few in number, But a few, and sojourners in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they go up and down, from nation unto nation, From a kingdom unto another people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:5; Exodus 7:16–17; Genesis 26:14–33"> He hath not suffered any to oppress them And He reproveth for their sakes kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 26:11; Genesis 20:7"> 'Strike not against Mine anointed, And to My prophets do not evil.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Genesis 41:54; Isaiah 3:1"> And He calleth a famine on the land, The whole staff of bread He hath broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 45:4–5"> He hath sent before them a man, For a servant hath Joseph been sold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 16:24; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:15"> They have afflicted with fetters his feet, Iron hath entered his soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> Till the time of the coming of His word The saying of Jehovah hath tried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> The king hath sent, and looseth him, The ruler of the peoples, and draweth him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:40–44; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 41:55"> He hath made him lord of his house, And ruler over all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> To bind his chiefs at his pleasure, And his elders he maketh wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 7:11–15; Acts 13:17; Psalms 78:51"> And Israel cometh in to Egypt, And Jacob hath sojourned in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7–9; Acts 7:17"> And He maketh His people very fruitful, And maketh it mightier than its adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 9:16"> He turned their heart to hate His people, To conspire against His servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 29:5–46; Exodus 28:12"> He hath sent Moses His servant, Aaron whom He had fixed on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:43–51; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Psalms 105:23"> They have set among them the matters of His signs, And wonders in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:2"> He hath sent darkness, and it is dark, And they have not provoked His word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 7:20–21; Psalms 78:44; Isaiah 50:2"> He hath turned their waters to blood, And putteth to death their fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:13–14; Exodus 8:3–14; Psalms 78:45"> Teemed hath their land &#91;with&#93; frogs, In the inner chambers of their kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 8:16–18; Exodus 8:21–24; Isaiah 7:18"> He hath said, and the beetle cometh, Lice into all their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> He hath made their showers hail, A flaming fire &#91;is&#93; in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> And He smiteth their vine and their fig, And shivereth the trees of their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> He hath said, and the locust cometh, And the cankerworm — innumerable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And it consumeth every herb in their land, And it consumeth the fruit of their ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalms 78:51"> And He smiteth every first-born in their land, The first-fruit of all their strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> And bringeth them out with silver and gold, And there is not in its tribes a feeble one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> Rejoiced hath Egypt in their going forth, For their fear had fallen upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 78:14; Isaiah 4:5"> He hath spread a cloud for a covering, And fire to enlighten the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> They have asked, and He bringeth quails, And &#91;with&#93; bread of heaven satisfieth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> He hath opened a rock, and waters issue, They have gone on in dry places — a river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> For He hath remembered His holy word, With Abraham His servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremiah 31:11–12"> And He bringeth forth His people with joy, With singing His chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalms 78:55"> And He giveth to them the lands of nations, And the labour of peoples they possess,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Titus 2:14; Ephesians 2:8–10"> That they may observe His statutes, And His laws may keep. Praise ye Jehovah!</span>
