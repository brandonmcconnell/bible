<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> To the Overseer. — 'On the Lilies.' — By sons of Korah. — An Instruction. — A song of loves. My heart hath indited a good thing, I am telling my works to a king, My tongue &#91;is&#93; the pen of a speedy writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> Thou hast been beautified above the sons of men, Grace hath been poured into thy lips, Therefore hath God blessed thee to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> Gird Thy sword upon the thigh, O mighty, Thy glory and Thy majesty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> As to Thy majesty — prosper! — ride! Because of truth, and meekness — righteousness, And Thy right hand showeth Thee fearful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Numbers 24:8; Psalms 38:2"> Thine arrows &#91;are&#93; sharp, — Peoples fall under Thee — In the heart of the enemies of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalms 93:2; John 1:1"> Thy throne, O God, &#91;is&#93; age-during, and for ever, A sceptre of uprightness &#91;Is&#93; the sceptre of Thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalms 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> Thou hast loved righteousness and hatest wickedness, Therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee, Oil of joy above thy companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> Myrrh and aloes, cassia! all thy garments, Out of palaces of ivory Stringed instruments have made thee glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> Daughters of kings &#91;are&#93; among thy precious ones, A queen hath stood at thy right hand, In pure gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> Hearken, O daughter, and see, incline thine ear, And forget thy people, and thy father's house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Solomon 4:1–5"> And the king doth desire thy beauty, Because he &#91;is&#93; thy lord — bow thyself to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> And the daughter of Tyre with a present, The rich of the people do appease thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> All glory &#91;is&#93; the daughter of the king within, Of gold-embroidered work &#91;is&#93; her clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Solomon 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> In divers colours she is brought to the king, Virgins — after her — her companions, Are brought to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> They are brought with joy and gladness, They come into the palace of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> Instead of thy fathers are thy sons, Thou dost appoint them for princes in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; Psalms 72:17–19"> I make mention of Thy name in all generations, Therefore do peoples praise Thee, To the age, and for ever!</span>
