<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 84:2; Psalms 42:1–2"> A Psalm of David, in his being in the wilderness of Judah. O God, Thou &#91;art&#93; my God, earnestly do I seek Thee, Thirsted for Thee hath my soul, Longed for Thee hath my flesh, In a land dry and weary, without waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 84:2–11"> So in the sanctuary I have seen Thee, To behold Thy strength and Thine honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:16; Psalms 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> Because better &#91;is&#93; Thy kindness than life, My lips do praise Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 134:2; Psalms 28:2"> So I bless Thee in my life, in Thy name I lift up my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 36:7–9; Isaiah 25:6; Ezra 3:11–13"> As &#91;with&#93; milk and fatness is my soul satisfied, And &#91;with&#93; singing lips doth my mouth praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Psalms 149:5"> If I have remembered Thee on my couch, In the watches — I meditate on Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalms 61:4; Psalms 27:9"> For Thou hast been a help to me, And in the shadow of Thy wings I sing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 18:35; Psalms 73:23"> Cleaved hath my soul after Thee, On me hath Thy right hand taken hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:17; Psalms 40:14"> And they who for desolation seek my soul, Go in to the lower parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 39:4"> They cause him to run on the edge of the sword, A portion for foxes they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalms 21:1; Isaiah 65:16"> And the king doth rejoice in God, Boast himself doth every one swearing by Him, But stopped is the mouth of those speaking lies!</span>
