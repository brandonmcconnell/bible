<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> A Psalm of Asaph. The God of gods — Jehovah — hath spoken, And He calleth to the earth From the rising of the sun unto its going in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> From Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God cometh, and is not silent, Fire before Him doth devour, And round about him it hath been very tempestuous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> He doth call unto the heavens from above, And unto the earth, to judge His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> Gather ye to Me My saints, Making covenant with Me over a sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> And the heavens declare His righteousness, For God Himself &#91;is&#93; judge. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> Hear, O My people, and I speak, O Israel, and I testify against thee, God, thy God &#91;am&#93; I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> Not for thy sacrifices do I reprove thee, Yea, thy burnt-offerings &#91;Are&#93; before Me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> I take not from thy house a bullock, From thy folds he goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> For Mine &#91;is&#93; every beast of the forest, The cattle on the hills of oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I have known every fowl of the mountains, And the wild beast of the field &#91;is&#93; with Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> If I am hungry I tell not to thee, For Mine &#91;is&#93; the world and its fulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do I eat the flesh of bulls, And drink the blood of he-goats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Sacrifice to God confession, And complete to the Most High thy vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> And call Me in a day of adversity, I deliver thee, and thou honourest Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> And to the wicked hath God said: What to thee — to recount My statutes? That thou liftest up My covenant on thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Yea, thou hast hated instruction, And dost cast My words behind thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> If thou hast seen a thief, Then thou art pleased with him, And with adulterers &#91;is&#93; thy portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> Thy mouth thou hast sent forth with evil, And thy tongue joineth deceit together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> Thou sittest, against thy brother thou speakest, Against a son of thy mother givest slander.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> These thou didst, and I kept silent, Thou hast thought that I am like thee, I reprove thee, and set in array before thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Understand this, I pray you, Ye who are forgetting God, Lest I tear, and there is no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> He who is sacrificing praise honoureth Me, As to him who maketh a way, I cause him to look on the salvation of God!</span>
