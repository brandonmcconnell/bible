<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David, in the coming in unto him of Nathan the prophet, when he hath gone in unto Bath-Sheba. Favour me, O God, according to Thy kindness, According to the abundance of Thy mercies, Blot out my transgressions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 1:7–9; Psalms 51:7; Hebrews 10:21–22"> Thoroughly wash me from mine iniquity, And from my sin cleanse me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 59:12; Psalms 38:18"> For my transgressions I do know, And my sin &#91;is&#93; before me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13–14; Luke 15:21; Genesis 20:6"> Against Thee, Thee only, I have sinned, And done the evil thing in Thine eyes, So that Thou art righteous in Thy words, Thou art pure in Thy judging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Job 14:4; Psalms 58:3"> Lo, in iniquity I have been brought forth, And in sin doth my mother conceive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Peter 3:4"> Lo, truth Thou hast desired in the inward parts, And in the hidden part Wisdom Thou causest me to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Isaiah 1:18; Hebrews 9:13–14"> Thou cleansest me with hyssop and I am clean, Washest me, and than snow I am whiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:17–18; Hosea 6:1–2; Matthew 5:4"> Thou causest me to hear joy and gladness, Thou makest joyful bones Thou hast bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:1"> Hide Thy face from my sin. And all mine iniquities blot out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 4:22–24"> A clean heart prepare for me, O God, And a right spirit renew within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Kings 13:23; Isaiah 63:10–11"> Cast me not forth from Thy presence, And Thy Holy Spirit take not from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 61:10"> Restore to me the joy of Thy salvation, And a willing spirit doth sustain me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:32; Acts 26:18–20; Acts 3:19"> I teach transgressors Thy ways, And sinners unto Thee do return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; 2 Samuel 12:9; Psalms 26:9"> Deliver me from blood, O God, God of my salvation, My tongue singeth of Thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 63:3–5; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 9:14"> O Lord, my lips thou dost open, And my mouth declareth Thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 40:6; Hosea 6:6; Hebrews 10:5–6"> For Thou desirest not sacrifice, or I give &#91;it&#93;, Burnt-offering Thou acceptest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:2; Psalms 147:3"> The sacrifices of God &#91;are&#93; a broken spirit, A heart broken and bruised, O God, Thou dost not despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 122:6–9; Luke 12:32"> Do good in Thy good pleasure with Zion, Thou dost build the walls of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 4:5; Ephesians 5:2; Malachi 3:3"> Then Thou desirest sacrifices of righteousness, Burnt-offering, and whole burnt-offering, Then they offer bullocks on thine altar!</span>
