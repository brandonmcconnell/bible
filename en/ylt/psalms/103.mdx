<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 57:7–11; John 4:24"> By David. Bless, O my soul, Jehovah, And all my inward parts — His Holy Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 116:12; Psalms 105:5"> Bless, O my soul, Jehovah, And forget not all His benefits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Isaiah 53:5; Jeremiah 17:14"> Who is forgiving all thine iniquities, Who is healing all thy diseases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> Who is redeeming from destruction thy life, Who is crowning thee — kindness and mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Who is satisfying with good thy desire, Renew itself as an eagle doth thy youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> Jehovah is doing righteousness and judgments For all the oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> He maketh known His ways to Moses, To the sons of Israel His acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> Merciful and gracious &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, Slow to anger, and abundant in mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 57:16; Psalms 30:5; Micah 7:18–19"> Not for ever doth He strive, Nor to the age doth He watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> Not according to our sins hath He done to us, Nor according to our iniquities Hath He conferred benefits upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> For, as the height of the heavens &#91;is&#93; above the earth, His kindness hath been mighty over those fearing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Micah 7:18"> As the distance of east from west He hath put far from us our transgressions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> As a father hath mercy on sons, Jehovah hath mercy on those fearing Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> For He hath known our frame, Remembering that we &#91;are&#93; dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Isaiah 51:12"> Mortal man! as grass &#91;are&#93; his days, As a flower of the field so he flourisheth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> For a wind hath passed over it, and it is not, And its place doth not discern it any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> And the kindness of Jehovah &#91;Is&#93; from age even unto age on those fearing Him, And His righteousness to sons' sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> To those keeping His covenant, And to those remembering His precepts to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> Jehovah in the heavens Hath established His throne, And His kingdom over all hath ruled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 148:2; Luke 1:19"> Bless Jehovah, ye His messengers, Mighty in power — doing His word, To hearken to the voice of His Word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> Bless Jehovah, all ye His hosts, His ministers — doing His pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 145:10; Isaiah 42:10–12"> Bless Jehovah, all ye His works, In all places of His dominion. Bless, O my soul, Jehovah!</span>
