<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:21; 2 Corinthians 9:8–14"> To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. O the happiness of him Who is acting wisely unto the poor, In a day of evil doth Jehovah deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:4–5; Psalms 27:12; 1 Timothy 4:8"> Jehovah doth preserve him and revive him, He is happy in the land, And Thou givest him not into the will of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:5–6; Psalms 73:26; Philippians 2:26–27"> Jehovah supporteth on a couch of sickness, All his bed Thou hast turned in his weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; James 5:15–16; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> I — I said, 'O Jehovah, favour me, Heal my soul, for I did sin against Thee,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 102:8; Psalms 38:12"> Mine enemies say evil of me: When he dieth — his name hath perished!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Luke 11:53–54; Proverbs 26:24–26"> And if he came to see — vanity he speaketh, His heart gathereth iniquity to itself, He goeth out — at the street he speaketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 56:5–6; Psalms 31:13"> All hating me whisper together against me, Against me they devise evil to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:11; Job 2:7–8; Matthew 27:41–46"> A thing of Belial is poured out on him, And because he lay down he riseth not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–14; Matthew 26:23"> Even mine ally, in whom I trusted, One eating my bread, made great the heel against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–10; Luke 19:27; Psalms 18:37–42"> And Thou, Jehovah, favour me, And cause me to rise, And I give recompence to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:15; Psalms 124:6; Psalms 147:11"> By this I have known, That Thou hast delighted in me, Because my enemy shouteth not over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 36:7; Psalms 73:23–24; Psalms 37:17"> As to me, in mine integrity, Thou hast taken hold upon me, And causest me to stand before Thee to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Ephesians 1:3; Revelation 4:8"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, God of Israel, From the age — and unto the age. Amen and Amen.</span>
