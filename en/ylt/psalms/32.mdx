<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 4:6–8; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 85:2"> By David. — An Instruction. O the happiness of him whose transgression &#91;is&#93; forgiven, Whose sin is covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:47; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21; Revelation 14:5"> O the happiness of a man, To whom Jehovah imputeth not iniquity, And in whose spirit there is no deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Psalms 38:8; Psalms 38:3"> When I have kept silence, become old have my bones, Through my roaring all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Psalms 38:2–8; Psalms 39:10–11"> When by day and by night Thy hand is heavy upon me, My moisture hath been changed Into the droughts of summer. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 38:18"> My sin I cause Thee to know, And mine iniquity I have not covered. I have said, 'I confess concerning My transgressions to Jehovah,' And Thou — Thou hast taken away, The iniquity of my sin. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Isaiah 43:2; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> For this doth every saintly one pray to Thee, As the time to find. Surely at an overflowing of many waters, Unto him they come not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 119:114; Psalms 9:9"> Thou &#91;art&#93; a hiding-place for me, From distress Thou dost keep me, &#91;With&#93; songs of deliverance dost compass me. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 33:18; Psalms 25:8–10"> I cause thee to act wisely, And direct thee in the way that thou goest, I cause mine eye to take counsel concerning thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:3"> Be ye not as a horse — as a mule, Without understanding, With bridle and bit, its ornaments, to curb, Not to come near unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–3; Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 13:21"> Many &#91;are&#93; the pains of the wicked; As to him who is trusting in Jehovah, Kindness doth compass him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 97:12"> Be glad in Jehovah, and rejoice, ye righteous, And sing, all ye upright of heart!</span>
