<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Psalms 9:9; Proverbs 18:10"> To the Overseer. — By sons of Korah. 'For the Virgins.' — A song. God &#91;is&#93; to us a refuge and strength, A help in adversities found most surely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 27:3"> Therefore we fear not in the changing of earth, And in the slipping of mountains Into the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Jeremiah 5:22; Job 9:5–6"> Roar — troubled are its waters, Mountains they shake in its pride. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–3; Ezekiel 47:1–12; Psalms 36:8–9"> A river — its rivulets rejoice the city of God, Thy holy place of the tabernacles of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Zephaniah 3:15; Joel 2:27"> God &#91;is&#93; in her midst — she is not moved, God doth help her at the turn of the morn!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Psalms 18:13; Habakkuk 3:5–6"> Troubled have been nations, Moved have been kingdoms, He hath given forth with His voice, earth melteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:9"> Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; with us, A tower for us &#91;is&#93; the God of Jacob. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 66:5; Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 61:4"> Come ye, see the works of Jehovah, Who hath done astonishing things in the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Micah 4:3–4; Micah 5:10"> Causing wars to cease, Unto the end of the earth, the bow he shivereth, And the spear He hath cut asunder, Chariots he doth burn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Habakkuk 2:20; Ezekiel 38:23"> Desist, and know that I &#91;am&#93; God, I am exalted among nations, I am exalted in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Jeremiah 16:19; Psalms 46:7"> Jehovah of hosts &#91;is&#93; with us, A tower for us &#91;is&#93; the God of Jacob! Selah.</span>
