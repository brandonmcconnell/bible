<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Jesus, therefore, six days before the passover, came to Bethany, where was Lazarus, who had died, whom he raised out of the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> they made, therefore, to him a supper there, and Martha was ministering, and Lazarus was one of those reclining together (at meat) with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Mary, therefore, having taken a pound of ointment of spikenard, of great price, anointed the feet of Jesus and did wipe with her hair his feet, and the house was filled from the fragrance of the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> Therefore saith one of his disciples — Judas Iscariot, of Simon, who is about to deliver him up —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> 'Wherefore was not this ointment sold for three hundred denaries, and given to the poor?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> and he said this, not because he was caring for the poor, but because he was a thief, and had the bag, and what things were put in he was carrying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Jesus, therefore, said, 'Suffer her; for the day of my embalming she hath kept it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> for the poor ye have always with yourselves, and me ye have not always.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> A great multitude, therefore, of the Jews knew that he is there, and they came, not because of Jesus only, but that Lazarus also they may see, whom he raised out of the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> and the chief priests took counsel, that also Lazarus they may kill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> because on account of him many of the Jews were going away, and were believing in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the morrow, a great multitude that came to the feast, having heard that Jesus doth come to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> took the branches of the palms, and went forth to meet him, and were crying, 'Hosanna, blessed &#91;is&#93; he who is coming in the name of the Lord — the king of Israel;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> and Jesus having found a young ass did sit upon it, according as it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> 'Fear not, daughter of Sion, lo, thy king doth come, sitting on an ass' colt.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> And these things his disciples did not know at the first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were having been written about him, and these things they did to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> The multitude, therefore, who are with him, were testifying that he called Lazarus out of the tomb, and did raise him out of the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> because of this also did the multitude meet him, because they heard of his having done this sign,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> the Pharisees, therefore, said among themselves, 'Ye see that ye do not gain anything, lo, the world did go after him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> And there were certain Greeks out of those coming up that they may worship in the feast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> these then came near to Philip, who &#91;is&#93; from Bethsaida of Galilee, and were asking him, saying, 'Sir, we wish to see Jesus;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip cometh and telleth Andrew, and again Andrew and Philip tell Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> And Jesus responded to them, saying, 'The hour hath come that the Son of Man may be glorified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> verily, verily, I say to you, if the grain of the wheat, having fallen to the earth, may not die, itself remaineth alone; and if it may die, it doth bear much fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> he who is loving his life shall lose it, and he who is hating his life in this world — to life age-during shall keep it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> if any one may minister to me, let him follow me, and where I am, there also my ministrant shall be; and if any one may minister to me — honour him will the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> 'Now hath my soul been troubled, and what? shall I say — Father, save me from this hour? — but because of this I came to this hour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorify Thy name.' There came, therefore, a voice out of the heaven, 'I both glorified, and again I will glorify &#91;it&#93;;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> the multitude, therefore, having stood and heard, were saying that there hath been thunder; others said, 'A messenger hath spoken to him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered and said, 'Not because of me hath this voice come, but because of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> now is a judgment of this world, now shall the ruler of this world be cast forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> and I, if I may be lifted up from the earth, will draw all men unto myself.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> And this he said signifying by what death he was about to die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> the multitude answered him, 'We heard out of the law that the Christ doth remain — to the age; and how dost thou say, That it behoveth the Son of Man to be lifted up? who is this — the Son of Man?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, 'Yet a little time is the light with you; walk while ye have the light, that darkness may not overtake you; and he who is walking in the darkness hath not known where he goeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> while ye have the light, believe in the light, that sons of light ye may become.' These things spake Jesus, and having gone away, he was hid from them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> yet he having done so many signs before them, they were not believing in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> that the word of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he said, 'Lord, who gave credence to our report? and the arm of the Lord — to whom was it revealed?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> Because of this they were not able to believe, that again Isaiah said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> 'He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart, that they might not see with the eyes, and understand with the heart, and turn back, and I might heal them;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> these things said Isaiah, when he saw his glory, and spake of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Still, however, also out of the rulers did many believe in him, but because of the Pharisees they were not confessing, that they might not be put out of the synagogue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> for they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> And Jesus cried and said, 'He who is believing in me, doth not believe in me, but in Him who sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> and he who is beholding me, doth behold Him who sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> I a light to the world have come, that every one who is believing in me — in the darkness may not remain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> and if any one may hear my sayings, and not believe, I — I do not judge him, for I came not that I might judge the world, but that I might save the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> 'He who is rejecting me, and not receiving my sayings, hath one who is judging him, the word that I spake, that will judge him in the last day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> because I spake not from myself, but the Father who sent me, He did give me a command, what I may say, and what I may speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> and I have known that His command is life age-during; what, therefore, I speak, according as the Father hath said to me, so I speak.'</span>
</div>
