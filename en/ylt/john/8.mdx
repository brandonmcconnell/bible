<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> And at dawn he came again to the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> and all the people were coming unto him, and having sat down, he was teaching them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and the scribes and the Pharisees bring unto him a woman having been taken in adultery, and having set her in the midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> they say to him, 'Teacher, this woman was taken in the very crime — committing adultery,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> and in the law, Moses did command us that such be stoned; thou, therefore, what dost thou say?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> and this they said, trying him, that they might have to accuse him. And Jesus, having stooped down, with the finger he was writing on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> and when they continued asking him, having bent himself back, he said unto them, 'The sinless of you — let him first cast the stone at her;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and again having stooped down, he was writing on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> and they having heard, and by the conscience being convicted, were going forth one by one, having begun from the elders — unto the last; and Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> And Jesus having bent himself back, and having seen no one but the woman, said to her, 'Woman, where are those — thine accusers? did no one pass sentence upon thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> and she said, 'No one, Sir;' and Jesus said to her, 'Neither do I pass sentence on thee; be going on, and no more sin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Again, therefore, Jesus spake to them, saying, 'I am the light of the world; he who is following me shall not walk in the darkness, but he shall have the light of the life.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> The Pharisees, therefore, said to him, 'Thou of thyself dost testify, thy testimony is not true;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered and said to them, 'And if I testify of myself — my testimony is true, because I have known whence I came, and whither I go, and ye — ye have not known whence I come, or whither I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> 'Ye according to the flesh do judge; I do not judge any one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> and even if I do judge my judgment is true, because I am not alone, but I and the Father who sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> and also in your law it hath been written, that the testimony of two men are true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am &#91;one&#93; who is testifying of myself, and the Father who sent me doth testify of me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> They said, therefore, to him, 'Where is thy father?' Jesus answered, 'Ye have neither known me nor my Father: if me ye had known, my Father also ye had known.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> These sayings spake Jesus in the treasury, teaching in the temple, and no one seized him, because his hour had not yet come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> therefore said Jesus again to them, 'I go away, and ye will seek me, and in your sin ye shall die; whither I go away, ye are not able to come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> The Jews, therefore, said, 'Will he kill himself, because he saith, Whither I go away, ye are not able to come?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> and he said to them, 'Ye are from beneath, I am from above; ye are of this world, I am not of this world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> I said, therefore, to you, that ye shall die in your sins, for if ye may not believe that I am &#91;he&#93;, ye shall die in your sins.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> They said, therefore, to him, 'Thou — who art thou?' and Jesus said to them, 'Even what I did speak of to you at the beginning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> many things I have to speak concerning you and to judge, but He who sent me is true, and I — what things I heard from Him — these I say to the world.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They knew not that of the Father he spake to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, 'When ye may lift up the Son of Man then ye will know that I am &#91;he&#93;; and of myself I do nothing, but according as my Father did teach me, these things I speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> and He who sent me is with me; the Father did not leave me alone, because I, the things pleasing to Him, do always.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> As he is speaking these things, many believed in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Jesus, therefore, said unto the Jews who believed in him, 'If ye may remain in my word, truly my disciples ye are, and ye shall know the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and the truth shall make you free.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They answered him, 'Seed of Abraham we are; and to no one have we been servants at any time; how dost thou say — Ye shall become free?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them, 'Verily, verily, I say to you — Every one who is committing sin, is a servant of the sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> and the servant doth not remain in the house — to the age, the son doth remain — to the age;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> if then the son may make you free, in reality ye shall be free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> 'I have known that ye are seed of Abraham, but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I — that which I have seen with my Father do speak, and ye, therefore, that which ye have seen with your father — ye do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered and said to him, 'Our father is Abraham;' Jesus saith to them, 'If children of Abraham ye were, the works of Abraham ye were doing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> and now, ye seek to kill me — a man who hath spoken to you the truth I heard from God; this Abraham did not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> ye do the works of your father.' They said, therefore, to him, 'We of whoredom have not been born; one Father we have — God;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus then said to them, 'If God were your father, ye were loving me, for I came forth from God, and am come; for neither have I come of myself, but He sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> wherefore do ye not know my speech? because ye are not able to hear my word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> 'Ye are of a father — the devil, and the desires of your father ye will to do; he was a man-slayer from the beginning, and in the truth he hath not stood, because there is no truth in him; when one may speak the falsehood, of his own he speaketh, because he is a liar — also his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> 'And because I say the truth, ye do not believe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Who of you doth convict me of sin? and if I speak truth, wherefore do ye not believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> he who is of God, the sayings of God he doth hear; because of this ye do not hear, because of God ye are not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Jews, therefore, answered and said to him, 'Do we not say well, that thou art a Samaritan, and hast a demon?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered, 'I have not a demon, but I honour my Father, and ye dishonour me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> and I do not seek my own glory; there is who is seeking and is judging;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> verily, verily, I say to you, If any one may keep my word, death he may not see — to the age.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> The Jews, therefore, said to him, 'Now we have known that thou hast a demon; Abraham did die, and the prophets, and thou dost say, If any one may keep my word, he shall not taste of death — to the age!</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Art thou greater than our father Abraham, who died? and the prophets died; whom dost thou make thyself?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, 'If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing; it is my Father who is glorifying me, of whom ye say that He is your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> and ye have not known Him, and I have known Him, and if I say that I have not known Him, I shall be like you — speaking falsely; but I have known Him, and His word I keep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Abraham, your father, was glad that he might see my day; and he saw, and did rejoice.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Jews, therefore, said unto him, 'Thou art not yet fifty years old, and Abraham hast thou seen?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, 'Verily, verily, I say to you, Before Abraham's coming — I am;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> they took up, therefore, stones that they may cast at him, but Jesus hid himself, and went forth out of the temple, going through the midst of them, and so passed by.</span>
