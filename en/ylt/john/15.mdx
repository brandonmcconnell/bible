<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 27:2–3; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> 'I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 12:10–11; Matthew 15:13"> every branch in me not bearing fruit, He doth take it away, and every one bearing fruit, He doth cleanse by pruning it, that it may bear more fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 17:17; Ephesians 5:26; 1 Peter 1:22"> already ye are clean, because of the word that I have spoken to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Galatians 2:20; John 8:31"> remain in me, and I in you, as the branch is not able to bear fruit of itself, if it may not remain in the vine, so neither ye, if ye may not remain in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> 'I am the vine, ye the branches; he who is remaining in me, and I in him, this one doth bear much fruit, because apart from me ye are not able to do anything;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> if any one may not remain in me, he was cast forth without as the branch, and was withered, and they gather them, and cast to fire, and they are burned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> if ye may remain in me, and my sayings in you may remain, whatever ye may wish ye shall ask, and it shall be done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:31; Matthew 5:16; Philippians 1:11"> 'In this was my Father glorified, that ye may bear much fruit, and ye shall become my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:23–24; John 17:26; 1 John 2:28"> According as the Father did love me, I also loved you, remain in my love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 3:21–24"> if my commandments ye may keep, ye shall remain in my love, according as I the commands of my Father have kept, and do remain in His love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> these things I have spoken to you, that my joy in you may remain, and your joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> 'This is my command, that ye love one another, according as I did love you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 10:11; Ephesians 5:2; Romans 5:6–8"> greater love than this hath no one, that any one his life may lay down for his friends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> ye are my friends, if ye may do whatever I command you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 2:9–12; Matthew 13:11"> no more do I call you servants, because the servant hath not known what his lord doth, and you I have called friends, because all things that I heard from my Father, I did make known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> 'Ye did not choose out me, but I chose out you, and did appoint you, that ye might go away, and might bear fruit, and your fruit might remain, that whatever ye may ask of the Father in my name, He may give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> 'These things I command you, that ye love one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> if the world doth hate you, ye know that it hath hated me before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> if of the world ye were, the world its own would have been loving, and because of the world ye are not — but I chose out of the world — because of this the world hateth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> 'Remember the word that I said to you, A servant is not greater than his lord; if me they did persecute, you also they will persecute; if my word they did keep, yours also they will keep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> but all these things will they do to you, because of my name, because they have not known Him who sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> if I had not come and spoken to them, they were not having sin; but now pretext they have not for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> 'He who is hating me, doth hate also my Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:36; John 14:9; John 7:31"> if I did not do among them the works that no other hath done, they were not having sin, and now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 109:3; Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:19"> but — that the word may be fulfilled that was written in their law — They hated me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 16:13–15"> 'And when the Comforter may come, whom I will send to you from the Father — the Spirit of truth, who from the Father doth come forth, he will testify of me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> and ye also do testify, because from the beginning ye are with me.</span>
