<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> When therefore the Lord knew that the Pharisees heard that Jesus more disciples doth make and baptize than John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (though indeed Jesus himself was not baptizing, but his disciples,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> he left Judea and went away again to Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> and it was behoving him to go through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> He cometh, therefore, to a city of Samaria, called Sychar, near to the place that Jacob gave to Joseph his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> and there was there a well of Jacob. Jesus therefore having been weary from the journeying, was sitting thus on the well; it was as it were the sixth hour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> there cometh a woman out of Samaria to draw water. Jesus saith to her, 'Give me to drink;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> for his disciples were gone away to the city, that they may buy victuals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> the Samaritan woman therefore saith to him, 'How dost thou, being a Jew, ask drink from me, being a Samaritan woman?' for Jews have no dealing with Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered and said to her, 'If thou hadst known the gift of God, and who it is who is saying to thee, Give me to drink, thou wouldest have asked him, and he would have given thee living water.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman saith to him, 'Sir, thou hast not even a vessel to draw with, and the well is deep; whence, then, hast thou the living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who did give us the well, and himself out of it did drink, and his sons, and his cattle?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered and said to her, 'Every one who is drinking of this water shall thirst again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> but whoever may drink of the water that I will give him, may not thirst — to the age; and the water that I will give him shall become in him a well of water, springing up to life age-during.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman saith unto him, 'Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come hither to draw.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus saith to her, 'Go, call thy husband, and come hither;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the woman answered and said, 'I have not a husband.' Jesus saith to her, 'Well didst thou say — A husband I have not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> for five husbands thou hast had, and, now, he whom thou hast is not thy husband; this hast thou said truly.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman saith to him, 'Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> our fathers in this mountain did worship, and ye — ye say that in Jerusalem is the place where it behoveth to worship.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus saith to her, 'Woman, believe me, that there doth come an hour, when neither in this mountain, nor in Jerusalem, shall ye worship the Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> ye worship what ye have not known; we worship what we have known, because the salvation is of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> but, there cometh an hour, and it now is, when the true worshippers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father also doth seek such to worship him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God &#91;is&#93; a Spirit, and those worshipping Him, in spirit and truth it doth behove to worship.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman saith to him, 'I have known that Messiah doth come, who is called Christ, when that one may come, he will tell us all things;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus saith to her, 'I am &#91;he&#93;, who am speaking to thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And upon this came his disciples, and were wondering that with a woman he was speaking, no one, however, said, 'What seekest thou?' or 'Why speakest thou with her?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> The woman then left her water-jug, and went away to the city, and saith to the men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> 'Come, see a man, who told me all things — as many as I did; is this the Christ?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> They went forth therefore out of the city, and were coming unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> And in the meanwhile his disciples were asking him, saying, 'Rabbi, eat;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> and he said to them, 'I have food to eat that ye have not known.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> The disciples then said one to another, 'Did any one bring him anything to eat?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus saith to them, 'My food is, that I may do the will of Him who sent me, and may finish His work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> do not say that it is yet four months, and the harvest cometh; lo, I say to you, Lift up your eyes, and see the fields, that they are white unto harvest already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> 'And he who is reaping doth receive a reward, and doth gather fruit to life age-during, that both he who is sowing and he who is reaping may rejoice together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> for in this the saying is the true one, that one is the sower and another the reaper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap that on which ye have not laboured; others laboured, and ye into their labour have entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> And from that city many believed in him, of the Samaritans, because of the word of the woman testifying, — 'He told me all things — as many as I did.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> When, then, the Samaritans came unto him, they were asking him to remain with them, and he remained there two days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> and many more did believe because of his word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> and said to the woman — 'No more because of thy speaking do we believe; for we ourselves have heard and known that this is truly the Saviour of the world — the Christ.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> And after the two days he went forth thence, and went away to Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> for Jesus himself testified that a prophet in his own country shall not have honour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> when then, he came to Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all things that he did in Jerusalem in the feast — for they also went to the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> Jesus came, therefore, again to Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine, and there was a certain courtier, whose son was ailing in Capernaum,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> he, having heard that Jesus is come out of Judea to Galilee, went away unto him, and was asking him that he may come down and may heal his son, for he was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Jesus then said unto him, 'If signs and wonders ye may not see, ye will not believe.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The courtier saith unto him, 'Sir, come down before my child die;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Jesus saith to him, 'Be going on; thy son doth live.' And the man believed the word that Jesus said to him, and was going on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> and he now going down, his servants met him, and told, saying — 'Thy child doth live;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> he inquired then of them the hour in which he became better, and they said to him — 'Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> then the father knew that &#91;it was&#93; in that hour in which Jesus said to him — 'Thy son doth live,' and he himself believed, and his whole house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> this again a second sign did Jesus, having come out of Judea to Galilee.</span>
</div>
