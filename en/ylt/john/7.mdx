<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> And Jesus was walking after these things in Galilee, for he did not wish to walk in Judea, because the Jews were seeking to kill him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> and the feast of the Jews was nigh — that of tabernacles —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> his brethren, therefore, said unto him, 'Remove hence, and go away to Judea, that thy disciples also may behold thy works that thou dost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> for no one in secret doth anything, and himself seeketh to be in public; if thou dost these things — manifest thyself to the world;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> for not even were his brethren believing in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Jesus, therefore, saith to them, 'My time is not yet present, but your time is always ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> the world is not able to hate you, but me it doth hate, because I testify concerning it that its works are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Ye — go ye up to this feast; I do not yet go up to this feast, because my time hath not yet been fulfilled;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and saying these things to them, he remained in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> And when his brethren went up, then also he himself went up to the feast, not manifestly, but as in secret;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> the Jews, therefore, were seeking him, in the feast, and said, 'Where is that one?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> and there was much murmuring about him among the multitudes, some indeed said — 'He is good;' and others said, 'No, but he leadeth astray the multitude;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> no one, however, was speaking freely about him, through fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> And it being now the middle of the feast, Jesus went up to the temple, and he was teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> and the Jews were wondering, saying, 'How hath this one known letters — not having learned?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus answered them and said, 'My teaching is not mine, but His who sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> if any one may will to do His will, he shall know concerning the teaching, whether it is of God, or — I do speak from myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> 'He who is speaking from himself his own glory doth seek, but he who is seeking the glory of him who sent him, this one is true, and unrighteousness is not in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> hath not Moses given you the law? and none of you doth the law; why me do ye seek to kill?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The multitude answered and said, 'Thou hast a demon, who doth seek to kill thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered and said to them, 'One work I did, and ye all wonder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> because of this, Moses hath given you the circumcision — not that it is of Moses, but of the fathers — and on a sabbath ye circumcise a man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> if a man doth receive circumcision on a sabbath that the law of Moses may not be broken, are ye wroth with me that I made a man all whole on a sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> judge not according to appearance, but the righteous judgment judge.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Certain, therefore, of the Jerusalemites said, 'Is not this he whom they are seeking to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> and, lo, he doth speak freely, and they say nothing to him; did the rulers at all know truly that this is truly the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> but this one — we have known whence he is; and the Christ, when he doth come, no one doth know whence he is.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Jesus cried, therefore, in the temple, teaching and saying, 'Ye have both known me, and ye have known whence I am; and I have not come of myself, but He who sent me is true, whom ye have not known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> and I have known Him, because I am from Him, and He did send me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> They were seeking, therefore, to seize him, and no one laid the hand on him, because his hour had not yet come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> and many out of the multitude did believe in him, and said — 'The Christ — when he may come — will he do more signs than these that this one did?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the multitude murmuring these things concerning him, and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers that they may take him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Jesus, therefore, said to them, 'Yet a little time I am with you, and I go away unto Him who sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> ye will seek me, and ye shall not find; and where I am, ye are not able to come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> The Jews, therefore, said among themselves, 'Whither is this one about to go that we shall not find him? — to the dispersion of the Greeks is he about to go? and to teach the Greeks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> what is this word that he said, Ye will seek me, and ye shall not find? and, Where I am, ye are not able to come?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> And in the last, the great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried, saying, 'If any one doth thirst, let him come unto me and drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> he who is believing in me, according as the Writing said, Rivers out of his belly shall flow of living water;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> and this he said of the Spirit, which those believing in him were about to receive; for not yet was the Holy Spirit, because Jesus was not yet glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> Many, therefore out of the multitude, having heard the word, said, 'This is truly the Prophet;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> others said, 'This is the Christ;' and others said, 'Why, out of Galilee doth the Christ come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Did not the Writing say, that out of the seed of David, and from Bethlehem — the village where David was — the Christ doth come?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> A division, therefore, arose among the multitude because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> And certain of them were willing to seize him, but no one laid hands on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> the officers came, therefore, unto the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, 'Wherefore did ye not bring him?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The officers answered, 'Never so spake man — as this man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> The Pharisees, therefore, answered them, 'Have ye also been led astray?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> did any one out of the rulers believe in him? or out of the Pharisees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> but this multitude, that is not knowing the law, is accursed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus saith unto them — he who came by night unto him — being one of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> 'Doth our law judge the man, if it may not hear from him first, and know what he doth?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered and said to him, 'Art thou also out of Galilee? search and see, that a prophet out of Galilee hath not risen;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> and each one went on to his house, but Jesus went on to the mount of the Olives.</span>
