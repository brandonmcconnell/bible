<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> And the third day a marriage happened in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> and also Jesus was called, and his disciples, to the marriage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> and wine having failed, the mother of Jesus saith unto him, 'Wine they have not;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus saith to her, 'What — to me and to thee, woman? not yet is mine hour come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother saith to the ministrants, 'Whatever he may say to you — do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> And there were there six water-jugs of stone, placed according to the purifying of the Jews, holding each two or three measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus saith to them, 'Fill the water-jugs with water;' and they filled them — unto the brim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> and he saith to them, 'Draw out, now, and bear to the director of the apartment;' and they bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> And as the director of the apartment tasted the water become wine, and knew not whence it is, (but the ministrants knew, who have drawn the water,) the director of the feast doth call the bridegroom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and saith to him, 'Every man, at first, the good wine doth set forth; and when they may have drunk freely, then the inferior; thou didst keep the good wine till now.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This beginning of the signs did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested his glory, and his disciples believed in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> after this he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, and his brethren, and his disciples; and there they remained not many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> And the passover of the Jews was nigh, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> and he found in the temple those selling oxen, and sheep, and doves, and the money-changers sitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> and having made a whip of small cords, he put all forth out of the temple, also the sheep, and the oxen; and of the money-changers he poured out the coins, and the tables he overthrew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> and to those selling the doves he said, 'Take these things hence; make not the house of my Father a house of merchandise.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> And his disciples remembered that it is written, 'The zeal of Thy house did eat me up;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> the Jews then answered and said to him, 'What sign dost thou shew to us — that thou dost these things?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered and said to them, 'Destroy this sanctuary, and in three days I will raise it up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jews, therefore, said, 'Forty and six years was this sanctuary building, and wilt thou in three days raise it up?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> but he spake concerning the sanctuary of his body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> when, then, he was raised out of the dead, his disciples remembered that he said this to them, and they believed the Writing, and the word that Jesus said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> And as he was in Jerusalem, in the passover, in the feast, many believed in his name, beholding his signs that he was doing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> and Jesus himself was not trusting himself to them, because of his knowing all &#91;men&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and because he had no need that any should testify concerning man, for he himself was knowing what was in man.</span>
