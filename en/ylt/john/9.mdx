<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And passing by, he saw a man blind from birth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> and his disciples asked him, saying, 'Rabbi, who did sin, this one or his parents, that he should be born blind?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, 'Neither did this one sin nor his parents, but that the works of God may be manifested in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> it behoveth me to be working the works of Him who sent me while it is day; night doth come, when no one is able to work: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> when I am in the world, I am a light of the world.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> These things saying, he spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and rubbed the clay on the eyes of the blind man, and said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> 'Go away, wash at the pool of Siloam,' which is, interpreted, Sent. He went away, therefore, and did wash, and came seeing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> the neighbours, therefore, and those seeing him before, that he was blind, said, 'Is not this he who is sitting and begging?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> others said — 'This is he;' and others — 'He is like to him;' he himself said, — 'I am &#91;he&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They said, therefore, to him, 'How were thine eyes opened?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> he answered and said, 'A man called Jesus made clay, and rubbed my eyes, and said to me, Go away to the pool of Siloam, and wash; and having gone away and having washed, I received sight;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> they said, therefore, to him, 'Where is that one?' he saith, 'I have not known.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They bring him to the Pharisees who once &#91;was&#93; blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> and it was a sabbath when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Again, therefore, the Pharisees also were asking him how he received sight, and he said to them, 'Clay he did put upon my eyes, and I did wash — and I see.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Of the Pharisees, therefore, certain said, 'This man is not from God, because the sabbath he doth not keep;' others said, 'How is a man — a sinful one — able to do such signs?' and there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They said to the blind man again, 'Thou — what dost thou say of him — that he opened thine eyes?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> and he said — 'He is a prophet.' The Jews, therefore, did not believe concerning him that he was blind and did receive sight, till that they called the parents of him who received sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> and they asked them, saying, 'Is your son, of whom ye say that he was born blind? how then now doth he see?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered them and said, 'We have known that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and how he now seeth, we have not known; or who opened his eyes, we have not known; himself is of age, ask him; he himself shall speak concerning himself.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> These things said his parents, because they were afraid of the Jews, for already had the Jews agreed together, that if any one may confess him — Christ, he may be put out of the synagogue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> because of this his parents said — 'He is of age, ask him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> They called, therefore, a second time the man who was blind, and they said to him, 'Give glory to God, we have known that this man is a sinner;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> he answered, therefore, and said, 'If he be a sinner — I have not known, one thing I have known, that, being blind, now I see.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they said to him again, 'What did he to thee? how did he open thine eyes?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, 'I told you already, and ye did not hear; why again do ye wish to hear? do ye also wish to become his disciples?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They reviled him, therefore, and said, 'Thou art his disciple, and we are Moses' disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> we have known that God hath spoken to Moses, but this one — we have not known whence he is.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, 'Why, in this is a wonderful thing, that ye have not known whence he is, and he opened my eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> and we have known that God doth not hear sinners, but, if any one may be a worshipper of God, and may do His will, him He doth hear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> from the age it was not heard, that any one did open eyes of one who hath been born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> if this one were not from God, he were not able to do anything.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, 'In sins thou wast born altogether, and thou dost teach us!' and they cast him forth without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they cast him forth without, and having found him, he said to him, 'Dost thou believe in the Son of God?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> he answered and said, 'Who is he, sir, that I may believe in him?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> And Jesus said to him, 'Thou hast both seen him, and he who is speaking with thee is he;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> and he said, 'I believe, sir,' and bowed before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said, 'For judgment I to this world did come, that those not seeing may see, and those seeing may become blind.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> And those of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and they said to him, 'Are we also blind?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, 'If ye were blind, ye were not having had sin, but now ye say — We see, therefore doth your sin remain.</span>
