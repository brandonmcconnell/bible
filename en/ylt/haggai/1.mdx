<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:2; Ezra 3:8; Ezra 3:2"> In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, in the first day of the month, hath a word of Jehovah been by the hand of Haggai the prophet, unto Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and unto Joshua son of Josedech, the high priest, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:23–2; Nehemiah 4:10; Ecclesiastes 11:4"> Thus spake Jehovah of Hosts, saying: This people! — they have said, 'The time hath not come, The time the house of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; to be built.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 5:1; Zechariah 1:1"> And there is a word of Jehovah by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Psalms 132:3–5; Philippians 2:21"> Is it time for you — you! To dwell in your covered houses, And this house to lie waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Haggai 1:7"> And now, thus said Jehovah of Hosts, Set your heart to your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 1:9; Deuteronomy 28:38–40; Hosea 4:10"> Ye have sown much, and brought in little, To eat, and not to satiety, To drink, and not to drunkenness, To clothe, and none hath heat, And he who is hiring himself out, Is hiring himself for a bag pierced through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:59–60; Haggai 1:5; Philippians 3:1"> Thus said Jehovah of Hosts: Set your heart to your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 2 Chronicles 7:16; Haggai 2:7"> Go up the mountain, and ye have brought in wood, And build the house, and I am pleased with it. And I am honoured, said Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Haggai 2:16–17; Haggai 1:6"> Looking for much, and lo, little, And ye brought &#91;it&#93; home, and I blew on it, Wherefore? — an affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts, Because of My house that is waste, And ye are running — each to his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Leviticus 26:19; Deuteronomy 28:23–24"> Therefore, over you refrained have the heavens from dew, And the land hath refrained its increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:22; 2 Kings 8:1"> And I proclaim draught on the land, And on the mountains, and on the corn, And on the new wine, and on the oil, And on what the ground doth bring forth, And on man, and on beast, And on all labour of the hands.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 50:10; Ezra 5:2; Haggai 1:14"> And Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Joshua son of Josedech, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people, do hearken to the voice of Jehovah their God, and unto the words of Haggai the prophet, as Jehovah their God had sent him, and the people are afraid of the face of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Matthew 28:20; Malachi 3:1"> And Haggai, messenger of Jehovah, in messages of Jehovah, speaketh to the people, saying: 'I &#91;am&#93; with you, an affirmation of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22; Hebrews 13:21; Haggai 2:21"> And Jehovah doth stir up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Josedech, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people, and they come in, and do work in the house of Jehovah of Hosts their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:1"> in the twenty and fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.</span>
