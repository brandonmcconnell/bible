<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1–2; 2 Kings 25:1–2; Jeremiah 25:1"> The word that hath been unto Jeremiah from Jehovah, in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah — it &#91;is&#93; the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:21; Nehemiah 3:25; Jeremiah 33:1"> And then the forces of the king of Babylon are laying siege against Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet hath been shut up in the court of the prison that &#91;is&#93; in the house of the king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2–3; Jeremiah 21:4–7; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> Where Zedekiah king of Judah hath shut him up, saying, 'Wherefore art thou prophesying, saying, Thus said Jehovah, Lo, I am giving this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he hath captured it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4–7; 2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 37:17"> And Zedekiah king of Judah doth not escape out of the hand of the Chaldeans, but is certainly given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and his mouth hath spoken with his mouth, and his eyes see his eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Ezekiel 17:15"> And &#91;to&#93; Babylon he leadeth Zedekiah, and there he is till My inspecting him, — an affirmation of Jehovah — because ye fight with the Chaldeans, ye do not prosper.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Jeremiah saith, 'A word of Jehovah hath been unto me saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Jeremiah 11:21; Leviticus 25:25"> Lo, Hanameel son of Shallum, thine uncle, is coming unto thee, saying, Buy for thee my field that &#91;is&#93; in Anathoth, for thine &#91;is&#93; the right of redemption — to buy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:7; Jeremiah 32:2; Jeremiah 33:1"> And Hanameel, my uncle's son, cometh in unto me, according to the word of Jehovah, unto the court of the prison, and saith unto me, 'Buy, I pray thee, my field that &#91;is&#93; in Anathoth, that &#91;is&#93; in the land of Benjamin, for thine &#91;is&#93; the right of possession, and thine of redemption — buy for thee.' And I know that it &#91;is&#93; the word of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Esther 3:9; 1 Kings 20:39"> And I buy the field, that &#91;is&#93; in Anathoth, from Hanameel, my uncle's son, and I weigh to him the money — seventeen shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 32:25"> And I write in a book, and seal, and cause witnesses to testify, and weigh the silver in balances;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 2:27; 1 Corinthians 11:16; Acts 26:3"> And I take the purchase-book, the sealed one, according to law and custom, and the open one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:16; Jeremiah 51:59; Jeremiah 36:4–5"> And I give the purchase-book unto Baruch son of Neriah, son of Maaseiah, before the eyes of Hanameel, my uncle's son, and before the eyes of the witnesses, those writing in the purchase-book, before the eyes of all the Jews who are sitting in the court of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And I charge Baruch before their eyes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> 'Thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel, Take these books, this purchase-book, both the sealed one and the open one, and thou hast put them in an earthen vessel, that they may remain many days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 3:10; Amos 9:14–15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel, Again are houses and fields and vineyards bought in this land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:1; Genesis 32:9–12; Philippians 4:6–7"> And I pray unto Jehovah — after my giving the purchase-book unto Baruch son of Neriah — saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> 'Ah, Lord Jehovah, lo, Thou hast made the heavens and the earth by Thy great power, and by Thy stretched-out arm; there is nothing too wonderful for Thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 5:9–10; Exodus 34:7"> Doing kindness to thousands, and recompensing iniquity of fathers into the bosom of their sons after them; God, the great, the mighty, Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 16:27; Psalms 62:12"> Great in counsel, and mighty in act, in that Thine eyes are open on all the ways of the sons of Adam, to give to each according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his doings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; 2 Samuel 7:23; Exodus 10:2"> In that thou hast done signs and wonders in the land of Egypt unto this day, and in Israel, and among men, and Thou dost make for Thee a name as &#91;at&#93; this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 26:8; Psalms 105:43"> 'And Thou bringest forth Thy people Israel from the land of Egypt, with signs and with wonders, and by a strong hand, and by a stretched-out arm, and by great fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 13:5; Jeremiah 11:5"> And thou givest to them this land that thou didst swear to their fathers to give to them, a land flowing with milk and honey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Psalms 78:54–55; Daniel 9:10–14"> And they come in, and possess it, and they have not hearkened to Thy voice, and in Thy law have not walked, all that which Thou didst lay a charge on them to do they have not done, and Thou dost proclaim &#91;to&#93; them all this evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:36; Deuteronomy 4:26; Joshua 23:15–16"> 'Lo, the mounts — they have come in to the city to capture it, and the city hath been given into the hand of the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword, and the famine, and the pestilence; and that which Thou hast spoken hath come to pass, and lo, Thou art seeing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 77:19"> Yet Thou hast said unto me, O Lord Jehovah, Buy for thee the field with money, and cause witnesses to testify — and the city hath been given into the hand of the Chaldeans!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the word of Jehovah is unto Jeremiah, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 64:8"> 'Lo, I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, God of all flesh: For Me is anything too wonderful?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 19:7–12"> 'Therefore, thus said Jehovah, Lo, I am giving this city into the hand of the Chaldeans, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and he hath captured it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 19:13; Jeremiah 52:13"> And come in have the Chaldeans who are fighting against this city, and they have set this city on fire, and have burned it, and the houses on whose roofs they made perfume to Baal, and poured out libations to other gods, so as to provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:22–26; Deuteronomy 9:7–12; Jeremiah 25:7"> For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have been only doing evil in Mine eyes, from their youth; for the sons of Israel are only provoking Me with the work of their hands — an affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:27; Jeremiah 27:10; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> 'For a cause of Mine anger, and a cause of My fury, hath this city been to Me, even from the day that they built it, and unto this day — to turn it aside from before My face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Jeremiah 2:26; Isaiah 1:4–6"> Because of all the evil of the sons of Israel, and of the sons of Judah that they have done, so as to provoke Me — they, their kings, their heads, their priests, and their prophets, and the men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 2:27; Ezekiel 8:16"> And they turn unto Me the neck, and not the face, and teaching them, rising early and teaching, and they are not hearkening to accept instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:5–16; 2 Kings 21:4–7"> 'And they set their abominations in the house over which My name is called, so as to defile it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; 2 Chronicles 33:6; Leviticus 18:21"> And they build the high places of Baal, that &#91;are&#93; in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to cause their sons and their daughters to pass through to Molech, which I did not command them, nor did it come up on my heart to do this abomination, so as to cause Judah to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Jeremiah 32:3; Jeremiah 32:28"> 'And now, therefore, thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, concerning this city, of which ye are saying, It hath been given into the hand of the king of Babylon by sword, and by famine, and by pestilence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:3; Jeremiah 23:6; Ezekiel 34:12–14"> Lo, I am gathering them out of all the lands whither I have driven them in Mine anger, and in My fury, and in great wrath, and I have brought them back unto this place, and have caused them to dwell confidently;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 11:19–20; Hebrews 8:10"> and they have been to Me for a people, and I am to them for God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Acts 4:32; John 17:21"> and I have given to them one heart, and one way, to fear Me all the days, for good to them, and to their sons after them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–33; Ezekiel 36:26; Jeremiah 24:7"> and I have made for them a covenant age-during, in that I turn not back from after them for My doing them good, and My fear I put in their heart, so as not to turn aside from me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 62:5"> and I have rejoiced over them to do them good, and have planted them in this land in truth, with all my heart, and with all My soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Joshua 23:14–15; Jeremiah 33:10–11"> 'For thus said Jehovah: As I brought in unto this people all this great evil, so I am bringing in on them all the good that I am speaking concerning them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:11–14; Jeremiah 32:15; Jeremiah 32:36"> and bought hath been the field in this land of which ye are saying, A desolation it &#91;is&#93;, without man and beast, it hath been given into the hand of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 33:11"> Fields with money they buy, so as to write in a book, and to seal, and to cause witnesses to testify, in the land of Benjamin, and in suburbs of Jerusalem, and in cities of Judah, and in cities of the hill-country, and in cities of the low country, and in cities of the south, for I turn back their captivity — an affirmation of Jehovah.'</span>
