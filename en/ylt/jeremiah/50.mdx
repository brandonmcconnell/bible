<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26–27; Jeremiah 51:1–14; Isaiah 13:1–3"> The word that Jehovah hath spoken concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:44"> 'Declare ye among nations, and sound, And lift up an ensign, sound, do not hide, Say ye: Captured hath been Babylon, Put to shame hath been Bel, Broken hath been Merodach, Put to shame have been her grievous things, Broken have been her idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:62"> For come up against her hath a nation from the north, It maketh her land become a desolation, And there is not an inhabitant in it. From man even unto beast, They have moved, they have gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31; Hosea 1:11; Hosea 3:5"> In those days, and at that time, An affirmation of Jehovah, Come in do sons of Israel, They and sons of Judah together, Going on and weeping they go, And Jehovah their God they seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Hebrews 8:6–10; Isaiah 55:3"> &#91;To&#93; Zion they ask the way, Thitherward &#91;are&#93; their faces: Come in, and we are joined unto Jehovah, A covenant age-during — not forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:14"> A perishing flock hath My people been, Their shepherds have caused them to err, &#91;To&#93; the mountains causing them to go back, From mountain unto hill they have gone, They have forgotten their crouching-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:8; Jeremiah 2:3; Zechariah 11:5"> All finding them have devoured them, And their adversaries have said: We are not guilty, Because that they sinned against Jehovah, The habitation of righteousness, And the hope of their fathers — Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4"> Move ye from the midst of Babylon, And from the land of the Chaldeans go out. And be as he-goats before a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 15:14; 2 Samuel 1:22"> For, lo, I am stirring up, And am causing to come up against Babylon, An assembly of great nations from a land of the north, And they have set in array against her, From thence she is captured, Its arrow — as a skilful hero — returneth not empty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Isaiah 33:23; Isaiah 33:4"> And Chaldea hath been for a spoil, All her spoilers are satisfied, An affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Ezekiel 25:15–17; Ezekiel 26:2–3"> Because thou rejoicest, because thou exultest, O spoilers of Mine inheritance, Because thou increasest as a heifer &#91;at&#93; the tender grass, And dost cry aloud as bulls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 49:2; Galatians 4:26"> Ashamed hath been your mother greatly, Confounded hath she been that bare you, Lo, the hindermost of nations &#91;is&#93; a wilderness, A dry land, and a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 49:17; Lamentations 2:15–16"> Because of the wrath of Jehovah it is not inhabited, And it hath been a desolation — all of it. Every passer by at Babylon is astonished, And doth hiss because of all her plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Habakkuk 2:17; 1 Samuel 17:20"> Set yourselves in array against Babylon round about, All ye treading a bow, Shoot at her, have no pity on the arrow, For against Jehovah she hath sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Revelation 18:6; Jeremiah 46:10"> Shout against her round about, She hath given forth her hand, Fallen have her foundations, Thrown down have been her walls, For it &#91;is&#93; the vengeance of Jehovah, Be avenged of her, as she did — do ye to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:16; Isaiah 13:14"> Cut off the sower from Babylon, And him handling the sickle in the time of harvest, Because of the oppressing sword, Each unto his people — they turn, And each to his land — they flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; John 10:10–12; 2 Kings 18:9–13"> A scattered sheep is Israel, lions have driven away, At first, devour him did the king of Asshur, And now, at last, broken his bone Hath Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; Zephaniah 2:13–15; Nahum 1:1–3"> Therefore thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel: Lo, I am seeing after the king of Babylon, And after his land, As I have seen after the king of Asshur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Micah 7:14; Jeremiah 30:18"> And I have brought back Israel unto his habitation, And he hath fed on Carmel, and on Bashan. And in mount Ephraim, and on Gilead is his soul satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 1:9"> In those days, and at that time, An affirmation of Jehovah, Sought is the iniquity of Israel, and it is not, And the sin of Judah, and it is not found, For I am propitious to those whom I leave!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:23; 1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 50:3"> Against the land of Merathaim: Go up against it, and unto the inhabitants of Pekod, Waste and devote their posterity, An affirmation of Jehovah, And do according to all that I have commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:19–21; Jeremiah 51:54–56; Isaiah 21:2–4"> A noise of battle &#91;is&#93; in the land, and of great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:4–6; Revelation 18:16"> How hath it been cut and broken, The hammer of the whole earth! How hath Babylon been for a desolation among nations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 9:4; Daniel 5:30–31; Job 40:2"> I have laid a snare for thee, And also — thou art captured, O Babylon, And thou — thou hast known, Thou hast been found, and also art caught, For against Jehovah thou hast stirred thyself up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:55; Jeremiah 51:25; Amos 3:6"> Jehovah hath opened His treasury, And He bringeth out the weapons of His indignation, For a work &#91;is&#93; to the Lord Jehovah of Hosts, In the land of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:10; Isaiah 45:3"> Come ye in to her from the extremity, Open ye her storehouses, Raise her up as heaps, and devote her, Let her have no remnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:21; Isaiah 34:7; Jeremiah 48:44"> Slay all her kine, they go down to slaughter, Woe &#91;is&#93; on them, for come hath their day, The time of their inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Jeremiah 50:15; Lamentations 1:10"> A voice of fugitives and escaped ones &#91;Is&#93; from the land of Babylon, To declare in Zion the vengeance of Jehovah our God, The vengeance of His temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:56; Isaiah 47:10; Daniel 4:37"> Summon unto Babylon archers, all treading the bow, Encamp against her round about, Let &#91;her&#93; have no escape; Recompense to her according to her work, According to all that she did — do to her, For unto Jehovah she hath been proud, Unto the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 18:21; Jeremiah 9:21"> Therefore fall do her young men in her broad places, And all her men of war are cut off in that day, An affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Jeremiah 50:29; Ezekiel 39:1"> Lo, I &#91;am&#93; against thee, O pride, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah of Hosts, For thy day hath come, the time of thy inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Jeremiah 49:27; Isaiah 10:12–15"> And stumbled hath pride, And he hath fallen, and hath no raiser up, And I have kindled a fire in his cities, And it hath devoured all round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 14:17; Exodus 9:2–3; Isaiah 58:6"> Thus said Jehovah of Hosts: Oppressed are the sons of Israel, And the sons of Judah together, And all their captors have kept hold on them, They have refused to send them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 54:5; Jeremiah 15:21; Isaiah 41:14"> Their Redeemer &#91;is&#93; strong, Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His name, He doth thoroughly plead their cause, So as to cause the land to rest, And He hath given trouble to the inhabitants of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Daniel 5:30; Daniel 5:7–8"> A sword &#91;is&#93; for the Chaldeans, An affirmation of Jehovah, And it &#91;is&#93; on the inhabitants of Babylon, And on her heads, and on her wise men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:22; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 44:25"> A sword &#91;is&#93; on the princes, And they have become foolish; A sword &#91;is&#93; on her mighty ones, And they have been broken down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 51:21"> A sword &#91;is&#93; on his horses and on his chariot, And on all the rabble who &#91;are&#93; in her midst, And they have become women; A sword &#91;is&#93; on her treasuries, And they have been spoiled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 50:2"> A sword &#91;is&#93; on her waters, and they have been dried up, For it &#91;is&#93; a land of graven images, And in idols they do boast themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20–22; Revelation 18:2; Jeremiah 25:12"> Therefore dwell do Ziim with Iim, Yea, dwelt in her have daughters of the ostrich, And it is not inhabited any more for ever, Nor dwelt in unto all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 19:24–25; Jude 1:7; Isaiah 13:19–20"> As overthrown by God with Sodom, And with Gomorrah, and with its neighbours, An affirmation of Jehovah, none doth dwell there, Nor sojourn in her doth a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Isaiah 13:2–5"> Lo, a people hath come from the north, Even a great nation, And many kings are stirred up from the sides of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Isaiah 13:17–18; Isaiah 47:6"> Bow and halbert they seize, Cruel &#91;are&#93; they, and they have no mercy, Their voice as a sea soundeth, and on horses they ride, Set in array as a man for battle, Against thee, O daughter of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Jeremiah 49:24; Isaiah 21:3–4"> Heard hath the king of Babylon their report, And feeble have been his hands, Distress hath seized him; pain as a travailing woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:19–21; Isaiah 46:11; Psalms 89:6"> Lo, as a lion he cometh up, Because of the rising of the Jordan, Unto the enduring habitation, But I cause to rest, I cause them to run from off her. And who is chosen? on her I lay a charge, For who &#91;is&#93; like Me? And who doth convene Me? And who &#91;is&#93; this shepherd who standeth before Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:20; Jeremiah 51:10–11; Revelation 17:16–17"> Therefore, hear ye the counsel of Jehovah, That He counselled concerning Babylon, And His devices that He hath devised Concerning the land of the Chaldeans; Drag them out do not little ones of the flock, Doth He not make desolate over them the habitation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:21; Revelation 18:9–19; Ezekiel 31:16"> From the voice: Captured was Babylon, Hath the earth been shaken, And a cry among nations hath been heard!</span>
