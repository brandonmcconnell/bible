<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 3:15; Psalms 92:7; Psalms 37:1"> Righteous &#91;art&#93; Thou, O Jehovah, When I plead towards thee, Only, judgments do I speak with Thee, Wherefore did the way of the wicked prosper? At rest have been all treacherous dealers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:8"> Thou hast planted them, yea, they have taken root, They go on, yea, they have made fruit, Near &#91;art&#93; Thou in their mouth, And far off from their reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:18; 2 Kings 20:3; Psalms 17:3"> And Thou, O Jehovah, Thou hast known me, Thou seest me, and hast tried my heart with Thee, Draw them away as sheep to slaughter, And separate them for a day of slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:10; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 4:25"> Till when doth the earth mourn, And the herb of the whole field wither? For the wickedness of those dwelling in it, Consumed have been beast and fowl, Because they said, 'He doth not see our latter end.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 24:10; Hebrews 12:3–4"> For — with footmen thou hast run, And they weary thee, And how dost thou fret thyself with horses! Even in the land of peace, &#91;In which&#93; thou art confident — And how dost thou in the rising of Jordan!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:25; Jeremiah 9:4; Micah 7:5–6"> For even thy brethren and the house of thy father, Even they dealt treacherously against thee, Even they — they called after thee fully, Trust not in them, when they speak to thee good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:6; Jeremiah 11:15; Psalms 78:59–60"> I have forsaken My house, I have left Mine inheritance, I have given the beloved of My soul Into the hand of her enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hosea 9:15; Jeremiah 2:15"> Mine inheritance hath been to Me as a lion in a forest, She gave forth against Me with her voice, Therefore I have hated her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:9; Jeremiah 7:33; 2 Kings 24:2"> A speckled fowl &#91;is&#93; Mine inheritance to Me? Is the fowl round about against her? Come, assemble, every beast of the field, Come ye for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–7; Jeremiah 23:1; Isaiah 63:18"> Many shepherds did destroy My vineyard, They have trodden down My portion, They have made My desirable portion Become a wilderness — a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:10; Isaiah 42:25; Jeremiah 14:2"> He hath made it become a desolation, The desolation hath mourned unto Me, Desolated hath been all the land, But there is no one laying it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:6; Amos 9:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> On all high places in the plain have spoilers come in, For the sword of Jehovah is consuming, From the end of the land even unto the end of the land, There is no peace to any flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:38; Haggai 1:6"> They sowed wheat, and have thorns reaped, They have become sick — they profit not, And they have been ashamed of your increases, Because of the fierceness of the anger of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Zechariah 2:8; Zechariah 10:6–12"> Thus said Jehovah concerning all my evil neighbours, who are striking against the inheritance that I caused my people — Israel — to inherit: 'Lo, I am plucking them from off their ground, And the house of Judah I pluck out of their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:39"> And it hath been, after My plucking them out, I turn back, and have pitied them, And I have brought them back, Each to his inheritance, and each to his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Joshua 23:7; Zephaniah 1:5"> And it hath come to pass, If they learn well the ways of My people, To swear by My name, 'Jehovah liveth,' As they taught My people to swear by Baal, Then they have been built up in the midst of My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 60:12; Psalms 2:8–12; Daniel 11:4"> And if they do not hearken, Then I have plucked up that nation, Plucking up and destroying, An affirmation of Jehovah!'</span>
