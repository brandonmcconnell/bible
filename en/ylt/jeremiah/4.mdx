<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:12; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 3:12"> If thou dost turn back, O Israel, An affirmation of Jehovah, unto Me turn back, And if thou dost turn aside Thine abominations from My face, Then thou dost not bemoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Jeremiah 9:24; Genesis 22:18"> And thou hast sworn — Jehovah liveth, In truth, in judgment, and in righteousness, And blessed themselves in Him have nations, And in Him they boast themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Luke 8:7; Luke 8:14"> For thus said Jehovah, To the man of Judah, and to Jerusalem: Till for yourselves tillage, And do not sow unto the thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 2:28–29"> Be circumcised to Jehovah, And turn aside the foreskins of your heart, O man of Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem, Lest My fury go out as fire, and hath burned, And there is none quenching, Because of the evil of your doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 6:1; Joshua 10:20"> Declare in Judah, and in Jerusalem sound, And say ye, 'Blow a trumpet in the land,' Call ye fully, and say ye: 'Be gathered, and we go in to the fenced city.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Jeremiah 1:13–15; Jeremiah 51:27"> Lift up an ensign Zionward, Strengthen yourselves, stand not still, For evil I am bringing in from the north, And a great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Isaiah 6:11"> Gone up hath a lion from his thicket, And a destroyer of nations hath journeyed, He hath come forth from his place To make thy land become a desolation, Thy cities are laid waste, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Jeremiah 6:26; Isaiah 10:4"> For this, gird on sackcloth, lament and howl, For the fierce anger of Jehovah hath not turned back from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9–16; Isaiah 29:9–10; Isaiah 22:3–5"> And it hath come to pass, in that day, An affirmation of Jehovah: 'Perish doth the heart of the king, And the heart of the princes, And astonished have been the priests, And the prophets do wonder.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:35"> And I say, 'Ah, Lord Jehovah, Surely thou hast entirely forgotten this people and Jerusalem, saying, Peace is for you, And struck hath a sword unto the soul!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 23:19"> At that time it is said of this people, And of Jerusalem: 'A dry wind of high places in the wilderness,' The way of the daughter of My people, (Not for winnowing, nor for cleansing,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:16; Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 6:11–13"> A full wind from these doth come for Me, Now, also, I speak judgments with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 66:15; Habakkuk 1:8"> Lo, as clouds he cometh up, And as a hurricane his chariots, Lighter than eagles have been his horses, Woe to us, for we have been spoiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 55:7; Acts 8:22"> Wash from evil thy heart, O Jerusalem, That thou mayest be saved, Till when dost thou lodge in thy heart Thoughts of thy strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Joshua 17:15; Judges 18:29"> For a voice is declaring from Dan, And sounding sorrow from mount Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:15; Ezekiel 21:22; Isaiah 39:3"> Make ye mention to the nations, Lo, sound ye to Jerusalem: 'Besiegers are coming from the land afar off, And they give forth against cities of Judah their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:23; 2 Kings 25:1–4; Nehemiah 9:30"> As the keepers of a field They have been against her round about, For with Me she hath been rebellious, An affirmation of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Psalms 107:17; Proverbs 1:31"> Thy way and thy doings have done these to thee, This &#91;is&#93; thy vexation, for &#91;it is&#93; bitter, For it hath struck unto thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 22:4"> My bowels, my bowels! I am pained &#91;at&#93; the walls of my heart, Make a noise for me doth My heart, I am not silent, For the voice of a trumpet I have heard, O my soul — a shout of battle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Matthew 10:28; Joel 1:15"> Destruction on destruction is proclaimed, For spoiled hath been all the land, Suddenly spoiled have been my tents, In a moment — my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Chronicles 36:3; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> Till when do I see an ensign? Do I hear the voice of a trumpet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Romans 16:19; Jeremiah 5:21"> For my people &#91;are&#93; foolish, me they have not known, Foolish sons &#91;are&#93; they, yea, they &#91;are&#93; not intelligent, Wise &#91;are&#93; they to do evil, And to do good they have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24–25; Isaiah 13:10"> I looked &#91;to&#93; the land, and lo, waste and void, And unto the heavens, and their light is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:20; Isaiah 5:25; Nahum 1:5–6"> I have looked &#91;to&#93; the mountains, And lo, they are trembling. And all the hills moved themselves lightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:2–3; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 9:10"> I have looked, and lo, man is not, And all fowls of the heavens have fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 14:2–6"> I have looked, and lo, The fruitful place &#91;is&#93; a wilderness, And all its cities have been broken down, Because of Jehovah, Because of the fierceness of His anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:28; Jeremiah 5:10; Jeremiah 5:18"> For thus said Jehovah: All the land is a desolation, but a completion I make not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 50:3; Hosea 4:3"> For this doth the land mourn, And black have been the heavens above, because I have spoken — I have purposed, And I have not repented, Nor do I turn back from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19–21; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 52:7"> From the voice of the horseman, And of him shooting with the bow, all the city is fleeing, They have come into thickets, And on cliffs they have gone up, All the city is forsaken, And there is no one dwelling in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 9:30; Lamentations 1:2; Lamentations 1:19"> And thou, O spoiled one, what dost thou? For thou puttest on scarlet, For thou adornest thyself &#91;with&#93; ornaments of gold. For thou rendest with pain thine eyes, In vain thou dost make thyself fair, Kicked against thee have doting ones, Thy life they do seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 1:17; Isaiah 1:15; Jeremiah 13:21"> For a voice as of a sick woman I have heard, Distress, as of one bringing forth a first-born, The voice of the daughter of Zion, She bewaileth herself, she spreadeth out her hands, 'Woe to me now, for weary is my soul of slayers!'</span>
