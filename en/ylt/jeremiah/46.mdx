<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> That which hath been the word of Jehovah unto Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> For Egypt, concerning the force of Pharaoh-Necho king of Egypt, that hath been by the river Phrat, in Carchemish, that Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon hath smitten, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> 'Set ye in array shield and buckler, And draw nigh to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> Gird the horses, and go up, ye horsemen, And station yourselves with helmets, Polish the javelins, put on the coats of mail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> Wherefore have I seen them dismayed — They are turned backward, And their mighty ones are beaten down, And &#91;to&#93; a refuge they have fled, and not turned the face? Fear &#91;is&#93; round about — an affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> The swift do not flee, nor do the mighty escape, Northward, by the side of the river Phrat, They have stumbled and fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> Who is this? as a flood he cometh up, As rivers do his waters shake themselves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> Egypt, as a flood cometh up, And as rivers the waters shake themselves. And he saith, I go up; I cover the land, I destroy the city and the inhabitants in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> Go up, ye horses; and boast yourselves, ye chariots, And go forth, ye mighty, Cush and Phut handling the shield, And Lud handling — treading the bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> And that day &#91;is&#93; to the Lord Jehovah of Hosts A day of vengeance, To be avenged of His adversaries, And the sword hath devoured, and been satisfied, And it hath been watered from their blood, For a sacrifice &#91;is&#93; to the Lord Jehovah of Hosts, In the land of the north, by the river Phrat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> Go up to Gilead, and take balm, O virgin daughter of Egypt, In vain thou hast multiplied medicines, Healing there is none for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> Nations have heard of thy shame, And thy cry hath filled the land, For the mighty on the mighty did stumble, Together they have fallen — both of them!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> The word that Jehovah hath spoken unto Jeremiah the prophet concerning the coming in of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, to smite the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> 'Declare ye in Egypt, and sound in Migdol, Yea, sound in Noph, and in Tahpanhes say: Station thyself, yea, prepare for thee, For a sword hath devoured around thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> Wherefore hath thy bull been swept away? He hath not stood, because Jehovah thrust him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> He hath multiplied the stumbling, Yea one hath fallen upon his neighbour, And they say: Rise, and we turn back to our people, And unto the land of our birth, Because of the oppressing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> They have cried there: Pharaoh king of Egypt &#91;is&#93; a desolation, Passed by hath the appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> I live — an affirmation of the King, Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His name, Surely as Tabor &#91;is&#93; among mountains, And as Carmel by the sea — he cometh in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> Goods for removal make for thee, O inhabitant, daughter of Egypt, For Noph becometh a desolation, And hath been burnt up, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> A heifer very fair &#91;is&#93; Egypt, Rending from the north doth come into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> Even her hired ones in her midst &#91;are&#93; as calves of the stall, For even they have turned, They have fled together, they have not stood, For the day of their calamity hath come on them, The time of their inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> Its voice as a serpent goeth on, For with a force they go, And with axes they have come in to her, As hewers of trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> They have cut down her forest, An affirmation of Jehovah — for it is not searched, For they have been more than the grasshopper, And they have no numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> Ashamed hath been the daughter of Egypt, She hath been given into the hand of the people of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> Said hath Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel: Lo, I am seeing after Amon of No, And after Pharaoh, and after Egypt, And after her gods, and after her kings, And after Pharaoh, and after those trusting in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> And I have given them into the hand of those seeking their life, And into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, And into the hand of his servants, And afterwards it is inhabited, As &#91;in&#93; days of old — an affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> And thou, thou dost not fear, my servant Jacob, Nor &#91;art&#93; thou dismayed, O Israel, For lo, I am saving thee from afar, And thy seed from the land of their captivity, And Jacob hath turned back, And hath been at rest, and been at ease, And there is none disturbing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> Thou, thou dost not fear, My servant Jacob, An affirmation of Jehovah — for with thee I &#91;am&#93;, For I make an end of all the nations Whither I have driven thee, And of thee I do not make an end, And I have reproved thee in judgment, And do not entirely acquit thee!'</span>
