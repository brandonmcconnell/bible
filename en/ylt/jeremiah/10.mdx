<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Jeremiah 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Hear ye the word, O house of Israel, That Jehovah hath spoken for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> Thus said Jehovah: Unto the way of the nations accustom not yourselves, And by the signs of the heavens be not affrighted, For the nations are affrighted by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> For the statutes of the peoples are vanity, For a tree from a forest hath one cut, Work of the hands of an artificer, with an axe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; Psalms 115:4"> With silver and with gold they beautify it, With nails and with hammers they fix it, And it doth not stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> As a palm they &#91;are&#93; stiff, and they speak not, They are surely borne, for they step not, Be not afraid of them, for they do no evil, Yea, also to do good is not in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 96:4; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 48:1"> Because there is none like Thee, O Jehovah, Great &#91;art&#93; Thou, and great Thy name in might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Psalms 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Who doth not fear Thee, king of the nations? For to Thee it is becoming, For among all the wise of the nations, And in all their kingdom there is none like Thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; Psalms 115:8"> And in one they are brutish and foolish, An instruction of vanities &#91;is&#93; the tree itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> Spread-out silver from Tarshish is brought, And gold from Uphaz, Work of an artisan, and of the hands of a refiner, Blue and purple &#91;is&#93; their clothing, Work of the skilful — all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> And Jehovah &#91;is&#93; a God of truth, He &#91;is&#93; a living God, and a king age-during, From His wrath shake doth the earth, And nations endure not His indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> Thus do ye say to them, The gods Who the heavens and earth have not made, They do perish from the earth, And from under these heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> The maker of the earth by His power, The establisher of the world by His wisdom, Who, by His understanding, stretched forth the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> At the voice He giveth forth, A multitude of waters &#91;is&#93; in the heavens, And He causeth vapours to come up from the end of the earth, Lightnings for rain He hath made, And bringeth out wind from His treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 135:16–18; Psalms 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> Brutish is every man by knowledge, Put to shame is every refiner by a graven image, For false &#91;is&#93; his molten image. And there is no breath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> Vanity &#91;are&#93; they, work of erring ones, In the time of their inspection they perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> Not like these &#91;is&#93; the Portion of Jacob, For framer of all things &#91;is&#93; He, And Israel &#91;is&#93; the rod of His inheritance, Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> Gather from the land thy merchandise, O dweller in the bulwark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> For thus said Jehovah: Lo, I am slinging out the inhabitants of the land at this time, And have been an adversary to them, So that they are found out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> Woe to me for my breaking, Grievious hath been my smiting, And I said, Only, this &#91;is&#93; my sickness, and I bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> My tent hath been spoiled, And all my cords have been broken, My sons have gone out from me, and they are not, There is none stretching out any more my tent, And raising up my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> For the shepherds have become brutish, And Jehovah they have not sought, Therefore they have not acted wisely, And all their flock is scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> A voice of a report, lo, it hath come, Even a great shaking from the north country, To make the cities of Judah a desolation, A habitation of dragons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalms 37:23"> I have known, O Jehovah, that not of man &#91;is&#93; his way, Not of man the going and establishing of his step.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:1; Psalms 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> Chastise me, O Jehovah, only in judgment, Not in Thine anger, lest Thou make me small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out Thy fury on the nations that have not known Thee, And on the families that have not called in Thy name, For they have eaten up Jacob, Yea, they have eaten him up, yea, they consume him, And his habitation they have made desolate!</span>
