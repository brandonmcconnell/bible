<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:11; Jeremiah 40:13; Jeremiah 6:13"> And they come nigh — all the heads of the forces, and Johanan son of Kareah, and Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and all the people from the least even unto the greatest —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Leviticus 26:22; Acts 8:24"> and they say unto Jeremiah the prophet, 'Let, we pray thee, our supplication fall before thee, and pray for us unto Jehovah thy God, for all this remnant; for we have been left a few out of many, as thine eyes do see us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Proverbs 3:6; Psalms 25:4–5"> and Jehovah thy God doth declare to us the way in which we walk, and the thing that we do.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:23; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Exodus 8:29"> And Jeremiah the prophet saith unto them, 'I have heard: lo, I am praying unto Jehovah your God according to your words, and it hath come to pass, the whole word that Jehovah answereth you, I declare to you — I do not withhold from you a word.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:50; Judges 11:10; Malachi 3:5"> And they have said to Jeremiah, 'Jehovah is against us for a witness true and faithful, if — according to all the word with which Jehovah thy God doth send thee unto us — we do not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Deuteronomy 5:29; Romans 8:7"> Whether good or evil, to the voice of Jehovah our God, to whom we are sending thee, we do hearken; because it is good for us when we hearken to the voice of Jehovah our God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Isaiah 28:16; Habakkuk 2:3"> And it cometh to pass, at the end of ten days, that there is a word of Jehovah unto Jeremiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 41:11–16; Jeremiah 43:2–5"> and he calleth unto Johanan son of Kareah, and unto all the heads of the forces that &#91;are&#93; with him, and to all the people, from the least even unto the greatest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Jeremiah 42:2; 2 Kings 19:6"> and he saith unto them, 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, unto whom ye sent me, to cause your supplication to fall before Him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 36:36; Jeremiah 24:6"> 'If ye do certainly dwell in this land, then I have builded you up, and I throw not down; and I have planted you, and I pluck not up; for I have repented concerning the evil that I have done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 1:19; Jeremiah 1:8"> Be not afraid of the king of Babylon, whom ye are afraid of; be not afraid of him — an affirmation of Jehovah — for with you &#91;am&#93; I, to save you, and to deliver you from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Psalms 106:45–46; Proverbs 16:7"> And I give to you mercies, and he hath pitied you, and caused you to turn back unto your own ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Exodus 5:2; Jeremiah 42:10"> 'And if ye are saying, We do not dwell in this land — not to hearken to the voice of Jehovah your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:17; Jeremiah 4:19; Isaiah 31:1"> saying, No; but the land of Egypt we enter, that we see no war, and the sound of a trumpet do not hear, and for bread be not hungry; and there do we dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Jeremiah 44:12–14; Jeremiah 42:17"> And now, therefore, hear ye a word of Jehovah, O remnant of Judah: Thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel: If ye really set your faces to enter Egypt, and have gone in to sojourn there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:27; Ezekiel 11:8; Amos 9:1–4"> then it hath come to pass, the sword that ye are afraid of, doth there overtake you, in the land of Egypt; and the hunger, because of which ye are sorrowful, doth there cleave after you in Egypt, and there ye die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Jeremiah 44:13–14"> 'Thus are all the men who have set their faces to enter Egypt to sojourn there; they die — by sword, by hunger, and by pestilence, and there is not to them a remnant and an escaped one, because of the evil that I am bringing in upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 29:18"> for thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel: As poured out hath been Mine anger and My fury on the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so poured out is My fury upon you in your entering Egypt, and ye have been for an execration, and for an astonishment, and for a reviling, and for a reproach, and ye do not see any more this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 30:1–7; Nehemiah 9:26"> 'Jehovah hath spoken against you, O remnant of Judah, do not enter Egypt: know certainly that I have testified against you to-day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:3–4; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 22:15–18"> for ye have shewed yourselves perverse in your souls, for ye sent me unto Jehovah your God, saying, Pray for us unto Jehovah our God, and according to all that Jehovah our God saith, so declare to us, and we have done &#91;it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Zechariah 7:11–12; Acts 20:26–27"> and I declare to you to-day, and ye have not hearkened to the voice of Jehovah your God, and to anything with which He hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:17; Ezekiel 6:11; Hosea 9:6"> And now, know ye certainly that by sword, by famine, and by pestilence ye die, in the place that ye have desired to go in to sojourn there.'</span>
