<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that hath been unto Jeremiah from Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremiah 19:1–2"> Rise, and thou hast gone down &#91;to&#93; the potter's house, and there I cause thee to hear My words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonah 1:3; Acts 26:19"> and I go down &#91;to&#93; the potter's house, and lo, he is doing a work on the stones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremiah 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> and marred is the vessel that he is making, as clay in the hand of the potter, and he hath turned and he maketh it another vessel, as it was right in the eyes of the potter to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there is a word of Jehovah to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> As this potter am I not able to do to you? O house of Israel, an affirmation of Jehovah. Lo, as clay in the hand of the potter, So &#91;are&#93; ye in My hand, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> The moment I speak concerning a nation, And concerning a kingdom, To pluck up and to break down, and to destroy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> And that nation hath turned from its evil, Because I have spoken against it, Then I have repented of the evil that I thought to do to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> And the moment I speak concerning a nation, And concerning a kingdom, to build, and to plant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> And it hath done the evil thing in Mine eyes, So as not to hearken to My voice, Then I have repented of the good That I have spoken of doing to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> And now, speak, I pray thee, unto men of Judah, And against inhabitants of Jerusalem, Saying: Thus said Jehovah: Lo, I am framing against you evil, And devising against you a device, Turn back, I pray you, each from his evil way And amen your ways and your doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> And they have said, It is incurable, For after our own devices we do go, And each the stubbornness of his evil heart we do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> Therefore, thus said Jehovah: Ask, I pray you, among the nations, Who hath heard like these? A very horrible thing hath the virgin of Israel done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> Doth snow of Lebanon Cease from the rock of the field? Failed are the cold strange waters that flow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> But My people have forgotten Me, to a vain thing they make perfume, And they cause them to stumble in their ways — paths of old, To walk in paths — a way not raised up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> To make their land become a desolation, A hissing age-during, Every passer by it is astonished, And bemoaneth with his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> As an east wind I scatter them before an enemy, The neck, and not the face, I shew them, In the day of their calamity.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> And they say, Come, And we devise against Jeremiah devices, For law doth not perish from the priest, Nor counsel from the wise, Nor the word from the prophet, Come, and we smite him with the tongue, And we do not attend to any of his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> Give attention, O Jehovah, unto me, And hearken to the voice of those contending with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> Is evil recompensed instead of good, That they have dug a pit for my soul? Remember my standing before Thee to speak good of them, To turn back Thy wrath from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> Therefore, give up their sons to famine, And cause them to run on the sides of the sword, And their wives are bereaved and widows, And their men are slain by death, Their young men smitten by sword in battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> A cry is heard from their houses, For Thou bringest against them suddenly a troop, For they dug a pit to capture me, And snares they have hidden for my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> And Thou, O Jehovah, Thou hast known, All their counsel against me &#91;is&#93; for death, Thou dost not cover over their iniquity, Nor their sin from before Thee blottest out, And they are made to stumble before Thee, In the time of Thine anger work against them!</span>
