<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Kings 25:1–9"> The word that hath been unto Jeremiah from Jehovah — and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and all his force, and all kingdoms of the land of the dominion of his hand, and all the peoples are fighting against Jerusalem, and against all its cities — saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 34:22; Jeremiah 37:8–10"> 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel: Go, and thou hast spoken unto Zedekiah king of Judah, and hast said unto him, Thus said Jehovah: Lo, I am giving this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he hath burned it with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> and thou, thou dost not escape out of his hand, for thou art certainly caught, and into his hand thou art given, and thine eyes see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and his mouth with thy mouth speaketh, and Babylon thou enterest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> 'Only, hear a word of Jehovah, O Zedekiah king of Judah, Thus said Jehovah unto thee: Thou dost not die by sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Kings 22:20"> in peace thou diest, and with the burnings of thy fathers, the former kings who have been before thee, so they make a burning for thee; and Ah, lord, they lament for thee, for the word I have spoken — an affirmation of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:16–24; 1 Samuel 3:18; 1 Kings 21:19"> And Jeremiah the prophet speaketh unto Zedekiah king of Judah all these words in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Jeremiah 4:5; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> and the forces of the king of Babylon are fighting against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that are left — against Lachish, and against Azekah, for these have been left among the cities of Judah, cities of fortresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; 2 Kings 23:2–3; 2 Kings 11:17"> The word that hath been unto Jeremiah from Jehovah, after the making by the king Zedekiah of a covenant with all the people who &#91;are&#93; in Jerusalem, to proclaim to them liberty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Leviticus 25:39–46; Exodus 3:18"> to send out each his man-servant, and each his maid-servant — the Hebrew and the Hebrewess — free, so as not to lay service on them, any on a Jew his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:16; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 38:4"> and hearken do all the heads, and all the people who have come in to the covenant to send forth each his man-servant and each his maid-servant free, so as not to lay service on them any more, yea, they hearken, and send them away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Proverbs 26:11; Matthew 12:43–45"> and they turn afterwards, and cause the men-servants and the maid-servants to return, whom they had sent forth free, and they subdue them for men-servants and for maid-servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto Jeremiah from Jehovah, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 6:8; Joshua 24:17; Deuteronomy 5:27"> 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, I — I made a covenant with your fathers in the day of My bringing them forth from the land of Egypt, from a house of servants, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> At the end of seven years ye do send forth each his brother, the Hebrew, who is sold to thee, and hath served thee six years, yea, thou hast sent him forth free from thee: and your fathers hearkened not unto Me, nor inclined their ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; 2 Kings 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> 'And ye turn back, ye to-day, and ye do that which is right in Mine eyes, to proclaim liberty each to his neighbour, and ye make a covenant before Me in the house over which My name is called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 20:7; Jeremiah 34:11"> And — ye turn back, and pollute My name, and ye cause each his man-servant and each his maid-servant, whom he had sent forth free, (at their pleasure,) to return, and ye subdue them to be to you for men-servants and for maid-servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 28:25; Galatians 6:7"> 'Therefore, thus said Jehovah: Ye have not hearkened unto Me to proclaim freedom, each to his brother, and each to his neighbour; lo, I am proclaiming to you liberty — an affirmation of Jehovah — unto the sword, unto the pestilence, and unto the famine, and I have given you for a trembling to all kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; Genesis 15:17–18; Hosea 6:7"> 'And I have given the men who are transgressing My covenant, who have not established the words of the covenant that they have made before Me, by the calf, that they have cut in two, and pass through between its pieces —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:10; Zephaniah 3:3–4; Daniel 9:6"> heads of Judah, and heads of Jerusalem, the officers, and the priests, and all the people of the land those passing through between the pieces of the calf —</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 19:7"> yea, I have given them into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those seeking their soul, and their carcase hath been for food to the fowl of the heavens, and to the beast of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 52:10"> 'And Zedekiah king of Judah, and his heads, I give into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those seeking their soul, and into the hand of the forces of the king of Babylon, that are going up from off you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 39:1–2"> Lo, I am commanding — an affirmation of Jehovah — and have brought them back unto this city, and they have fought against it, and captured it, and burned it with fire, and the cities of Judah I do make a desolation — without inhabitant.'</span>
