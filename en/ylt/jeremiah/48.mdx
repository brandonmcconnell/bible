<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:3; Numbers 32:37–38; Jeremiah 48:22–23"> Concerning Moab: 'Thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel: Woe unto Nebo, for it is spoiled, Put to shame, captured hath been Kiriathaim, Put to shame hath been the high tower, Yea, it hath been broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:37; Jeremiah 31:36; Isaiah 16:14"> There is no more praise of Moab, In Heshbon they devised against it evil: Come, and we cut it off from &#91;being&#93; a nation, Also, O Madmen, thou art cut off, After thee goeth a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; Jeremiah 48:34; Jeremiah 48:5"> A voice of a cry &#91;is&#93; from Horonaim, Spoiling and great destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 137:9; Numbers 21:27–30; Esther 8:11"> Destroyed hath been Moab, Caused a cry to be heard have her little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5"> For the ascent of Luhith with weeping, Go up doth weeping, For in the descent of Horonaim Adversaries a cry of desolation have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Jeremiah 51:6; Job 30:3–7"> Flee ye, deliver yourselves, Ye are as a naked thing in a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 52:7"> For, because of thy trusting in thy works, And in thy treasures, even thou art captured, And gone out hath Chemosh in a removal, His priests and his heads together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 51:56; Jeremiah 25:9"> And come in doth a spoiler unto every city, And no city doth escape, And perished hath the valley, And destroyed been the plain, as Jehovah said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Jeremiah 48:28; Isaiah 16:2"> Give wings to Moab, for she utterly goeth out, And her cities are for a desolation, Without an inhabitant in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; 1 Kings 20:42; Judges 5:23"> Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is doing the work of Jehovah slothfully, And cursed &#91;is&#93; he Who is withholding his sword from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Zechariah 1:15; Jeremiah 48:29"> Secure is Moab from his youth, And at rest &#91;is&#93; he for his preserved things, And he hath not been emptied out from vessel unto vessel, And into captivity he hath not gone, Therefore hath his taste remained in him, And his fragrance hath not been changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 2:2; Isaiah 30:14; Ezekiel 25:9–10"> Therefore, lo, days are coming, An affirmation of Jehovah, And I have sent to him wanderers, And they have caused him to wander, And his vessels they empty out, And his bottles they dash in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 45:16; Judges 11:24; Jeremiah 48:39"> And ashamed hath been Moab because of Chemosh, As the house of Israel have been ashamed Because of Beth-El their confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 33:16; Isaiah 10:13; Isaiah 10:16"> How do ye say, We &#91;are&#93; mighty, And men of strength for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 51:57"> Spoiled is Moab, and her cities hath one gone up, And the choice of its young men Have gone down to slaughter, An affirmation of the King, Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 1:12; Ezekiel 12:28"> Near is the calamity of Moab to come, And his affliction hath hasted exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 14:4–5; Ezekiel 19:11–14"> Bemoan for him, all ye round about him, And all knowing his name, say ye: How hath it been broken, the staff of strength, The rod of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Isaiah 47:1; Isaiah 15:2"> Come down from honour, sit in thirst, O inhabitant, daughter of Dibon, For a spoiler of Moab hath come up to thee, He hath destroyed thy fenced places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; 1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 4:13–14"> On the way stand, and watch, O inhabitant of Aroer, Ask the fugitive and escaped, Say, What hath happened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Numbers 21:26–28; Deuteronomy 2:36"> Put to shame hath been Moab, For it hath been broken down, Howl and cry, declare ye in Arnon, For spoiled is Moab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 13:18; Isaiah 15:4; Jeremiah 48:8"> And judgment hath come in unto the land of the plain — unto Holon, And unto Jahazah, and on Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Numbers 33:46; Numbers 32:34"> And on Dibon, and on Nebo, And on Beth-Diblathaim, and on Kirathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 13:17; Jeremiah 48:1; Numbers 32:38"> And on Beth-Gamul, and on Beth-Meon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:2; Jeremiah 48:41; Joshua 21:36"> And on Kerioth, and on Bozrah, And on all cities of the land of Moab, The far off and the near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 10:15; Zechariah 1:19–21"> Cut down hath been the horn of Moab, And his arm hath been broken, An affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:42; Isaiah 29:9; Jeremiah 51:39"> Declare ye him drunk, For against Jehovah he made himself great And Moab hath stricken in his vomit, And he hath been for a derision — even he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:26; Micah 7:8–10; Zephaniah 2:8"> And was not Israel the derision to thee? Among thieves was he found? For since thy words concerning him, Thou dost bemoan thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 55:6–7; Judges 6:2; Song of Solomon 2:14"> Forsake cities, and dwell in a rock, Ye inhabitants of Moab, And be as a dove making a nest in the passages of a pit's mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Psalms 138:6; Isaiah 2:11–12"> We have heard of the arrogance of Moab, Exceeding proud! His haughtiness, and his arrogance, And his pride, and the height of his heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:36; Isaiah 16:6; Isaiah 37:28–29"> I — I have known, an affirmation of Jehovah, His wrath, and &#91;it is&#93; not right, His devices — not right they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 15:5; 2 Kings 3:25; Jeremiah 48:36"> Therefore for Moab I howl, even for Moab — all of it, I cry for men of Kir-Heres, it doth mourn,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 21:32; Joshua 13:19"> With the weeping of Jazer, I weep for thee, O vine of Sibmah, Thy branches have passed over a sea, Unto the sea of Jazer they have come, On thy summer fruits, and on thy harvest, A spoiler hath fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10; Haggai 2:16; Jeremiah 25:9–10"> And removed hath been joy and gladness From the fruitful field, Even from the land of Moab, And wine from wine-presses I have caused to cease, Shouting doth not proceed, The shouting &#91;is&#93; no shouting!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 15:4–6; Numbers 32:3; Deuteronomy 34:3"> Because of the cry of Heshbon unto Elealeh, Unto Jahaz they have given their voice, From Zoar unto Horonaim, A heifer of the third &#91;year&#93;, For even waters of Nimrim become desolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 15:2; Isaiah 16:12; Numbers 28:14"> And I have caused to cease to Moab, An affirmation of Jehovah, Him who is offering in a high place, And him who is making perfume to his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 15:7; Isaiah 16:11; Isaiah 15:5"> Therefore my heart for Moab as pipes doth sound, And my heart for men of Kir-Heres As pipes doth sound, Therefore the abundance he made did perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:5; Genesis 37:34; Jeremiah 47:5"> For every head &#91;is&#93; bald, and every beard diminished, On all hands cuttings, and on the loins — sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 25:34; Isaiah 22:1"> On all roofs of Moab, and in her broad-places, All of it — &#91;is&#93; lamentation, For I have broken Moab as a vessel in which there is no pleasure, An affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4–6; Lamentations 4:1; Lamentations 2:1"> How hath it been broken down! they have howled, How hath Moab turned the neck ashamed, And Moab hath been for a derision. And for a terror to all round about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 8:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremiah 49:22"> For thus said Jehovah: Lo, as an eagle he doth flee, And hath spread his wings unto Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; Micah 4:9–10"> Captured have been the cities, And the strongholds are caught, And the heart of the mighty of Moab Hath been in that day as the heart of a distressed woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Isaiah 7:8; Isaiah 37:23"> And Moab hath been destroyed from &#91;being&#93; a people, For against Jehovah he exerted himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 3:47; Isaiah 24:17–18; Deuteronomy 32:23–25"> Fear, and a snare, and a gin, &#91;are&#93; for thee, O inhabitant of Moab — an affirmation of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:23; 1 Kings 19:17; Jeremiah 46:21"> Whoso is fleeing because of the fear falleth into the snare, And whoso is coming up from the snare is captured by the gin, For I bring in unto her — unto Moab — The year of their inspection, An affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Numbers 21:28; Zechariah 10:4"> In the shadow of Heshbon stood powerless have fugitives, For fire hath gone forth from Heshbon, And a flame from within Sihon, And it consumeth the corner of Moab, And the crown of the sons of Shaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Jeremiah 48:7; Judges 11:24"> Woe to thee, O Moab, Perished hath the people of Chemosh, For thy sons were taken with the captives, And thy daughters with the captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:39; Jeremiah 49:6; Jeremiah 23:20"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of Moab, In the latter end of the days, An affirmation of Jehovah! Hitherto &#91;is&#93; the judgment of Moab.</span>
