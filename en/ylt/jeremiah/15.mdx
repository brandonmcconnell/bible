<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremiah 14:11"> And Jehovah saith unto me: Though Moses and Samuel should stand before Me, My soul is not toward this people, Send from before My face, and they go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Zechariah 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> And it hath come to pass, when they say to thee, Whither do we go out? that thou hast said unto them, Thus said Jehovah: Those who &#91;are&#93; for death — to death, And those who are for the sword, to the sword, And those who are for famine, to famine, And those who are for captivity, to captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> And I have appointed over them four kinds, an affirmation of Jehovah, The sword to slay, and the dogs to drag, And the fowl of the heavens, And the beast of the earth, to consume and to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 23:26–27; 2 Kings 24:3–4"> And I have given them for a trembling To all kingdoms of the earth, Because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah king of Judah, For that which he did in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Nahum 3:7; Jeremiah 21:7"> For who hath pity on thee, O Jerusalem? And who doth bemoan for thee? And who turneth aside to ask of thy welfare?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Zephaniah 1:4; Isaiah 1:4"> Thou — thou hast left Me — an affirmation of Jehovah, Backward thou goest, And I stretch out My hand against thee, And I destroy thee, I have been weary of repenting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 41:16"> And I scatter them with a fan, in the gates the land, I bereaved, I have destroyed My people, From their ways they turned not back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 3:25–1; Jeremiah 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> Its widows have been more to Me than the sand of the seas, I brought in to them — against the mother — A young man — a spoiler — at noon. I caused to fall upon her suddenly, wrath and trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:5; Isaiah 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> Languished hath the bearer of seven, She hath breathed out her spirit, Gone in hath her sun while yet day, It hath been ashamed and confounded, And their residue to the sword I give up before their enemies, An affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> Woe to me, my mother, For thou hast borne me a man of strife, And a man of contention to all the land, I have not lent on usury, Nor have they lent on usury to me — All of them are reviling me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremiah 29:11–14; Psalms 106:46"> Jehovah said, Did I not direct thee for good? Did not I intercede for thee in a time of evil, And in a time of adversity, with the enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremiah 21:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Doth one break iron — northern iron, and brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:3; Psalms 44:12; Isaiah 52:3"> Thy strength and thy treasures For a prey I do give — not for price, Even for all thy sins, and in all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremiah 17:4"> And I have caused thine enemies To pass over into the land — Thou hast not known, For a fire hath been kindled in Mine anger, Against you it doth burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:3; Psalms 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> Thou, Thou hast known, O Jehovah, Remember me, and inspect me, And take vengeance for me of my pursuers, In Thy long-suffering take me not away, Know — I have borne for Thee reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:1–3; Psalms 119:101–103"> Thy words have been found, and I eat them, And Thy word is to me for a joy, And for the rejoicing of my heart, For Thy name is called on me, O Jehovah, God of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; Psalms 26:4–5"> I have not sat in an assembly of deriders, Nor do I exult, because of thy hand, — Alone I have sat, For &#91;with&#93; indignation Thou hast filled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:15; Job 34:6; Micah 1:9"> Why hath my pain been perpetual? And my wound incurable? It hath refused to be healed, Thou art surely to me as a failing stream, Waters not stedfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> Therefore, thus said Jehovah: If thou turnest back, then I bring thee back, Before Me thou dost stand, And if thou bringest out the precious from the vile, As My mouth thou art! They — they turn back unto thee, And thou dost not turn back unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Ezekiel 3:9"> And I have made thee to this people For a wall — brazen — fenced, And they have fought against thee, And they do not prevail against thee, For with thee &#91;am&#93; I to save thee, And to deliver thee — an affirmation of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Isaiah 49:24–25; Jeremiah 50:34"> And I have delivered thee from the hand of evil doers, And I have ransomed thee From the hand of the terrible!</span>
