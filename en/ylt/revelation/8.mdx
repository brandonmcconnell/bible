<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Habakkuk 2:20; Revelation 6:1"> And when he openeth the seventh seal, there came silence in the heaven about half-an-hour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> and I saw the seven messengers who before God have stood, and there were given to them seven trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; Psalms 141:2"> and another messenger did come, and he stood at the altar, having a golden censer, and there was given to him much perfume, that he may give &#91;it&#93; to the prayers of all the saints upon the golden altar that &#91;is&#93; before the throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> and go up did the smoke of the perfumes to the prayers of the saints out of the hand of the messenger, before God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> and the messenger took the censer, and did fill it out of the fire of the altar, and did cast &#91;it&#93; to the earth, and there came voices, and thunders, and lightnings, and an earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> And the seven messengers who are having the seven trumpets did prepare themselves that they may sound;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> and the first messenger did sound, and there came hail and fire, mingled with blood, and it was cast to the land, and the third of the trees was burnt up, and all the green grass was burnt up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> And the second messenger did sound, and as it were a great mountain with fire burning was cast into the sea, and the third of the sea became blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> and die did the third of the creatures that &#91;are&#93; in the sea, those having life, and the third of the ships were destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> And the third messenger did sound, and there fell out of the heaven a great star, burning as a lamp, and it did fall upon the third of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> and the name of the star is called Wormwood, and the third of the waters doth become wormwood, and many of the men did die of the waters, because they were made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> And the fourth messenger did sound, and smitten was the third of the sun, and the third of the moon, and the third of the stars, that darkened may be the third of them, and that the day may not shine — the third of it, and the night in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> And I saw, and I heard one messenger, flying in the mid-heaven, saying with a great voice, 'Woe, woe, woe, to those dwelling upon the land from the rest of the voices of the trumpet of the three messengers who are about to sound.'</span>
