<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Luke 17:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And after these things I saw another messenger coming down out of the heaven, having great authority, and the earth was lightened from his glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8; Jeremiah 51:37"> and he did cry in might — a great voice, saying, 'Fall, fall did Babylon the great, and she became a habitation of demons, and a hold of every unclean spirit, and a hold of every unclean and hateful bird,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:2; Revelation 18:9"> because of the wine of the wrath of her whoredom have all the nations drunk, and the kings of the earth with her did commit whoredom, and merchants of the earth from the power of her revel were made rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Jeremiah 51:6"> And I heard another voice out of the heaven, saying, 'Come forth out of her, My people, that ye may not partake with her sins, and that ye may not receive of her plagues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Ezra 9:6; Revelation 16:19"> because her sins did follow — unto the heaven, and God did remember her unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Psalms 137:8; Jeremiah 51:24"> Render to her as also she did render to you, and double to her doubles according to her works; in the cup that she did mingle mingle to her double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–9; Zephaniah 2:15; Lamentations 1:1"> 'As much as she did glorify herself and did revel, so much torment and sorrow give to her, because in her heart she saith, I sit a queen, and a widow I am not, and sorrow I shall not see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:34; Jeremiah 50:31; Revelation 17:16"> because of this, in one day, shall come her plagues, death, and sorrow, and famine; and in fire she shall be utterly burned, because strong &#91;is&#93; the Lord God who is judging her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Revelation 18:3; Revelation 17:2"> and weep over her, and smite themselves for her, shall the kings of the earth, who with her did commit whoredom and did revel, when they may see the smoke of her burning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Revelation 18:8; Revelation 18:15–17"> from afar having stood because of the fear of her torment, saying, Woe, woe, the great city! Babylon, the strong city! because in one hour did come thy judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Revelation 18:23; Ezekiel 27:27–36"> 'And the merchants of the earth shall weep and sorrow over her, because their lading no one doth buy any more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Ezekiel 27:5–25; Proverbs 8:10–11"> lading of gold, and silver, and precious stone, and pearl, and fine linen, and purple, and silk, and scarlet, and all thyine wood, and every vessel of ivory, and every vessel of most precious wood, and brass, and iron, and marble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Amos 2:6; 2 Peter 2:3"> and cinnamon, and odours, and ointment, and frankincense, and wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat, and cattle, and sheep, and of horses, and of chariots, and of bodies and souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Psalms 106:14; Luke 16:25"> 'And the fruits of the desire of thy soul did go away from thee, and all things — the dainty and the bright — did go away from thee, and no more at all mayest thou find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Acts 19:24–27; Ezekiel 27:31"> The merchants of these things, who were made rich by her, far off shall stand because of the fear of her torment, weeping, and sorrowing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Luke 16:19–31; Revelation 18:10–11"> and saying, Woe, woe, the great city, that was arrayed with fine linen, and purple, and scarlet, and gilded in gold, and precious stone, and pearls — because in one hour so much riches were made waste!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:27–36; Revelation 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> 'And every shipmaster, and all the company upon the ships, and sailors, and as many as work the sea, far off stood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Ezekiel 27:30–32; Revelation 18:9–10"> and were crying, seeing the smoke of her burning, saying, What &#91;city is&#93; like to the great city?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:10; Joshua 7:6; Ezekiel 27:30"> and they did cast dust upon their heads, and were crying out, weeping and sorrowing, saying, Woe, woe, the great city! in which were made rich all having ships in the sea, out of her costliness — for in one hour was she made waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Revelation 19:1–3; Luke 18:7–8"> 'Be glad over her, O heaven, and ye holy apostles and prophets, because God did judge your judgment of her!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:63–64; Ezekiel 26:21; Revelation 5:2"> And one strong messenger did take up a stone as a great millstone, and did cast &#91;it&#93; to the sea, saying, 'Thus with violence shall Babylon be cast, the great city, and may not be found any more at all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:13; Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34"> and voice of harpers, and musicians, and pipers, and trumpeters, may not be heard at all in thee any more; and any artisan of any art may not be found at all in thee any more; and noise of a millstone may not be heard at all in thee any more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Isaiah 23:8; Nahum 3:4"> and light of a lamp may not shine at all in thee any more; and voice of bridegroom and of bride may not be heard at all in thee any more; because thy merchants were the great ones of the earth, because in thy sorcery were all the nations led astray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Jeremiah 51:49"> and in her blood of prophets and of saints was found, and of all those who have been slain on the earth.'</span>
