<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:10–12; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Revelation 11:15"> And after these things I heard a great voice of a great multitude in the heaven, saying, 'Alleluia! the salvation, and the glory, and the honour, and the power, &#91;is&#93; to the Lord our God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:43; Revelation 6:10; Revelation 18:20"> because true and righteous &#91;are&#93; His judgments, because He did judge the great whore who did corrupt the earth in her whoredom, and He did avenge the blood of His servants at her hand;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Revelation 18:18; Revelation 14:11"> and a second time they said, 'Alleluia;' and her smoke doth come up — to the ages of the ages!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 5:14; Nehemiah 8:6; Psalms 106:48"> And fall down did the elders — the twenty and four — and the four living creatures, and they did bow before God who is sitting upon the throne, saying, 'Amen, Alleluia.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 134:1; Psalms 148:11–13; Psalms 150:6"> And a voice out of the throne did come forth, saying, 'Praise our God, all ye His servants, and those fearing Him, both the small and the great;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:1; Revelation 14:2; Psalms 93:1"> and I heard as the voice of a great multitude, and as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of mighty thunderings, saying, 'Alleluia! because reign did the Lord God — the Almighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 21:9; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:9"> may we rejoice and exult, and give the glory to Him, because come did the marriage of the Lamb, and his wife did make herself ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Revelation 7:13–14; Psalms 132:9"> and there was given to her that she may be arrayed with fine linen, pure and shining, for the fine linen is the righteous acts of the saints.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:6; Matthew 22:2–4; Luke 14:15–16"> And he saith to me, 'Write: Happy &#91;are&#93; they who to the supper of the marriage of the Lamb have been called;' and he saith to me, 'These &#91;are&#93; the true words of God;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 10:25–26; John 5:39"> and I fell before his feet, to bow before him, and he saith to me, 'See — not! fellow servant of thee am I, and of thy brethren, those having the testimony of Jesus; bow before God, for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of the prophecy.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Isaiah 11:3–5; Psalms 98:9"> And I saw the heaven having been opened, and lo, a white horse, and he who is sitting upon it is called Faithful and True, and in righteousness doth he judge and war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> and his eyes &#91;are&#93; as a flame of fire, and upon his head &#91;are&#93; many diadems — having a name written that no one hath known, except himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> and he is arrayed with a garment covered with blood, and his name is called, The Word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> And the armies in the heaven were following him upon white horses, clothed in fine linen — white and pure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> and out of his mouth doth proceed a sharp sword, that with it he may smite the nations, and he shall rule them with a rod of iron, and he doth tread the press of the wine of the wrath and the anger of God the Almighty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> and he hath upon the garment and upon his thigh the name written, 'King of kings, and Lord of lords.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:21; Revelation 8:13; Ezekiel 39:17–20"> And I saw one messenger standing in the sun, and he cried, a great voice, saying to all the birds that are flying in mid-heaven, 'Come and be gathered together to the supper of the great God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:18–20; Revelation 6:15; 1 Samuel 17:44"> that ye may eat flesh of kings, and flesh of chiefs of thousands, and flesh of strong men, and flesh of horses, and of those sitting on them, and the flesh of all — freemen and servants — both small and great.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:16; Revelation 16:14; Revelation 17:12–14"> And I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, having been gathered together to make war with him who is sitting upon the horse, and with his army;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:10; Revelation 21:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–11"> and the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet who did the signs before him, in which he led astray those who did receive the mark of the beast, and those who did bow before his image; living they were cast — the two — to the lake of the fire, that is burning with brimstone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–15; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 19:17–19"> and the rest were killed with the sword of him who is sitting on the horse, which &#91;sword&#93; is proceeding out of his mouth, and all the birds were filled out of their flesh.</span>
