<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Isaiah 29:11; Revelation 5:7"> And I saw upon the right hand of Him who is sitting upon the throne a scroll, written within and on the back, sealed with seven seals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> and I saw a strong messenger crying with a great voice, 'Who is worthy to open the scroll and to loose the seals of it?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> and no one was able in the heaven, nor upon the earth, nor under the earth, to open the scroll, nor to behold it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> And I was weeping much, because no one was found worthy to open and to read the scroll, nor to behold it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> and one of the elders saith to me, 'Weep not; lo, overcome did the Lion, who is of the tribe of Judah, the root of David, to open the scroll, and to loose the seven seals of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> and I saw, and lo, in the midst of the throne, and of the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb hath stood as it had been slain, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the Seven Spirits of God, which are sent to all the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> and he came and took the scroll out of the right hand of Him who is sitting upon the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> And when he took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell before the Lamb, having each one harps and golden vials full of perfumes, which are the prayers of the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> and they sing a new song, saying, 'Worthy art thou to take the scroll, and to open the seals of it, because thou wast slain, and didst redeem us to God in thy blood, out of every tribe, and tongue, and people, and nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> and didst make us to our God kings and priests, and we shall reign upon the earth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> And I saw, and I heard the voice of many messengers round the throne, and the living creatures, and the elders — and the number of them was myriads of myriads, and thousands of thousands —</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> saying with a great voice, 'Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive the power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalms 148:2–13"> and every creature that is in the heaven, and in the earth, and under the earth, and the things that are upon the sea, and the all things in them, heard I saying, 'To Him who is sitting upon the throne, and to the Lamb, &#91;is&#93; the blessing, and the honour, and the glory, and the might — to the ages of the ages!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> and the four living creatures said, 'Amen!' and the twenty-four elders fell down and they bow before Him who is living to the ages of the ages.</span>
