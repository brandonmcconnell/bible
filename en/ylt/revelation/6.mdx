<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 6:5; Revelation 5:1; Revelation 6:7"> And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying, as it were a voice of thunder, 'Come and behold!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 6:3–8; Zechariah 1:8; Psalms 45:3–5"> and I saw, and lo, a white horse, and he who is sitting upon it is having a bow, and there was given to him a crown, and he went forth overcoming, and that he may overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:1"> And when he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, 'Come and behold!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Matthew 10:34; Revelation 13:10"> and there went forth another horse — red, and to him who is sitting upon it, there was given to him to take the peace from the land, and that one another they may slay, and there was given to him a great sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:2; Ezekiel 4:16; Zechariah 6:6"> And when he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, 'Come and behold!' and I saw, and lo, a black horse, and he who is sitting upon it is having a balance in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 7:3"> and I heard a voice in the midst of the four living creatures saying, 'A measure of wheat for a denary, and three measures of barley for a denary,' and 'The oil and the wine thou mayest not injure.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Revelation 6:3; Revelation 6:1"> And when he opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature saying, 'Come and behold!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Revelation 1:18"> and I saw, and lo, a pale horse, and he who is sitting upon him — his name is Death, and Hades doth follow with him, and there was given to them authority to kill, (over the fourth part of the land,) with sword, and with hunger, and with death, and by the beasts of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; John 16:2; Revelation 1:9"> And when he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those slain because of the word of God, and because of the testimony that they held,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Psalms 9:12; Revelation 16:5–7"> and they were crying with a great voice, saying, 'Till when, O Master, the Holy and the True, dost Thou not judge and take vengeance of our blood from those dwelling upon the land?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 3:4–5"> and there was given to each one white robes, and it was said to them that they may rest themselves yet a little time, till may be fulfilled also their fellow-servants and their brethren, who are about to be killed — even as they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:10; Acts 2:19–20"> And I saw when he opened the sixth seal, and lo, a great earthquake came, and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Matthew 24:29; Revelation 9:1"> and the stars of the heaven fell to the earth — as a fig-tree doth cast her winter figs, by a great wind being shaken —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 16:20; 2 Peter 3:10"> and heaven departed as a scroll rolled up, and every mountain and island — out of their places they were moved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 2:21; Isaiah 24:21–22"> and the kings of the earth, and the great men, and the rich, and the chiefs of thousands, and the mighty, and every servant, and every freeman, hid themselves in the dens, and in the rocks of the mountains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8; Revelation 4:2"> and they say to the mountains and to the rocks, 'Fall upon us, and hide us from the face of Him who is sitting upon the throne, and from the anger of the Lamb,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Joel 2:11; Psalms 76:7"> because come did the great day of His anger, and who is able to stand?</span>
