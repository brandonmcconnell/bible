<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> And I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth did pass away, and the sea is not any more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> and I, John, saw the holy city — new Jerusalem — coming down from God out of the heaven, made ready as a bride adorned for her husband;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> and I heard a great voice out of the heaven, saying, 'Lo, the tabernacle of God &#91;is&#93; with men, and He will tabernacle with them, and they shall be His peoples, and God Himself shall be with them — their God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> and God shall wipe away every tear from their eyes, and the death shall not be any more, nor sorrow, nor crying, nor shall there be any more pain, because the first things did go away.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> And He who is sitting upon the throne said, 'Lo, new I make all things; and He saith to me, 'Write, because these words are true and stedfast;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> and He said to me, 'It hath been done! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End; I, to him who is thirsting, will give of the fountain of the water of the life freely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> he who is overcoming shall inherit all things, and I will be to him — a God, and he shall be to me — the son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> and to fearful, and unstedfast, and abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all the liars, their part &#91;is&#93; in the lake that is burning with fire and brimstone, which is a second death.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> And there came unto me one of the seven messengers, who have the seven vials that are full of the seven last plagues, and he spake with me, saying, 'Come, I will shew thee the bride of the Lamb — the wife,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> and he carried me away in the Spirit to a mountain great and high, and did shew to me the great city, the holy Jerusalem, coming down out of the heaven from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> having the glory of God, and her light &#91;is&#93; like a stone most precious, as a jasper stone clear as crystal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> having also a wall great and high, having twelve gates, and at the gates twelve messengers, and names written thereon, which are &#91;those&#93; of the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> at the east three gates, at the north three gates, at the south three gates, at the west three gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> and the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> And he who is speaking with me had a golden reed, that he may measure the city, and its gates, and its wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> and the city lieth square, and the length of it is as great as the breadth; and he did measure the city with the reed — furlongs twelve thousand; the length, and the breadth, and the height, of it are equal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> and he measured its wall, an hundred forty-four cubits, the measure of a man, that is, of the messenger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> and the building of its wall was jasper, and the city &#91;is&#93; pure gold — like to pure glass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> and the foundations of the wall of the city with every precious stone have been adorned; the first foundation jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprasus; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> And the twelve gates &#91;are&#93; twelve pearls, each several one of the gates was of one pearl; and the broad-place of the city &#91;is&#93; pure gold — as transparent glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> And a sanctuary I did not see in it, for the Lord God, the Almighty, is its sanctuary, and the Lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> and the city hath no need of the sun, nor of the moon, that they may shine in it; for the glory of God did lighten it, and the lamp of it &#91;is&#93; the Lamb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> and the nations of the saved in its light shall walk, and the kings of the earth do bring their glory and honour into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> and its gates shall not at all be shut by day, for night shall not be there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> and they shall bring the glory and the honour of the nations into it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> and there may not at all enter into it any thing defiling and doing abomination, and a lie, but — those written in the scroll of the life of the Lamb.</span>
