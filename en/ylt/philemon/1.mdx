<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timotheus the brother, to Philemon our beloved and fellow-worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> and Apphia the beloved, and Archippus our fellow-soldier, and the assembly in thy house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace to you, and peace, from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I give thanks to my God, always making mention of thee in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> hearing of thy love and faith that thou hast unto the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> that the fellowship of thy faith may become working in the full knowledge of every good thing that &#91;is&#93; in you toward Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> for we have much joy and comfort in thy love, because the bowels of the saints have been refreshed through thee, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Wherefore, having in Christ much boldness to command thee that which is fit —</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> because of the love I rather entreat, being such an one as Paul the aged, and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I entreat thee concerning my child — whom I did beget in my bonds — Onesimus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> who once was to thee unprofitable, and now is profitable to me and to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> whom I did send again, and thou him (that is, my own bowels) receive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> whom I did wish to retain to myself, that in thy behalf he might minister to me in the bonds of the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> and apart from thy mind I willed to do nothing, that as of necessity thy good deed may not be, but of willingness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> for perhaps because of this he did depart for an hour, that age-duringly thou mayest have him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no more as a servant, but above a servant — a brother beloved, especially to me, and how much more to thee, both in the flesh and in the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If, then, with me thou hast fellowship, receive him as me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> and if he did hurt to thee, or doth owe anything, this to me be reckoning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul did write with my hand, I — I will repay; that I may not say that also thyself, besides, to me thou dost owe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, may I have profit of thee in the Lord; refresh my bowels in the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> having been confident in thy obedience I did write to thee, having known that also above what I may say thou wilt do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> and at the same time also prepare for me a lodging, for I hope that through your prayers I shall be granted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Salute thee doth Epaphras, (my fellow-captive in Christ Jesus,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Markus, Aristarchus, Demas, Lukas, my fellow-workmen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with your spirit! Amen.</span>
