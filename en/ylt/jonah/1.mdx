<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; 2 Kings 14:25"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto Jonah son of Amittai, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> 'Rise, go unto Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim against it that their wickedness hath come up before Me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> And Jonah riseth to flee to Tarshish from the face of Jehovah, and goeth down &#91;to&#93; Joppa, and findeth a ship going &#91;to&#93; Tarshish, and he giveth its fare, and goeth down into it, to go with them to Tarshish from the face of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> And Jehovah hath cast a great wind on the sea, and there is a great tempest in the sea, and the ship hath reckoned to be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> and the mariners are afraid, and cry each unto his god, and cast the goods that &#91;are&#93; in the ship into the sea, to make &#91;it&#93; light of them; and Jonah hath gone down unto the sides of the vessel, and he lieth down, and is fast asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> And the chief of the company draweth near to him, and saith to him, 'What — to thee, O sleeper? rise, call unto thy God, it may be God doth bethink himself of us, and we do not perish.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And they say each unto his neighbour, 'Come, and we cast lots, and we know on whose account this evil &#91;is&#93; on us.' And they cast lots, and the lot falleth on Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> And they say unto him, 'Declare to us, we pray thee, on what account this evil &#91;is&#93; on us? what &#91;is&#93; thine occupation, and whence comest thou? what &#91;is&#93; thy country, seeing thou art not of this people?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> And he saith unto them, 'A Hebrew I &#91;am&#93;, and Jehovah, God of the heavens, I am reverencing, who made the sea and the dry land.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> And the men fear a great fear, and say unto him, 'What &#91;is&#93; this thou hast done!' for the men have known that from the face of Jehovah he is fleeing, for he hath told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> And they say unto him, 'What do we do to thee that the sea may cease from us, for the sea is more and more tempestuous?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> And he saith unto them, 'Lift me up, and cast me into the sea, and the sea doth cease from you; for I know that on my account this great tempest &#91;is&#93; upon you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> And the men row to turn back unto the dry land, and are not able, for the sea is more and more tempestuous against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> And they cry unto Jehovah, and say, 'We pray Thee, O Jehovah, let us not, we pray Thee, perish for this man's life, and do not lay on us innocent blood, for Thou, Jehovah, as Thou hast pleased, Thou hast done.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> And they lift up Jonah, and cast him into the sea, and the sea ceaseth from its raging;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> and the men fear Jehovah — a great fear, and sacrifice a sacrifice to Jehovah, and vow vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Luke 11:30; Matthew 16:4"> And Jehovah appointeth a great fish to swallow up Jonah, and Jonah is in the bowels of the fish three days and three nights.</span>
