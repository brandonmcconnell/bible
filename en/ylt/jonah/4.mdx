<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 15:28; Jonah 4:9; Jonah 4:4"> And it is grievous unto Jonah — a great evil — and he is displeased at it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Exodus 34:6–7; Numbers 14:18–19"> and he prayeth unto Jehovah, and he saith, 'I pray Thee, O Jehovah, is not this my word while I was in mine own land — therefore I was beforehand to flee to Tarshish — that I have known that Thou &#91;art&#93; a God, gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and abundant in kindness, and repenting of evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> And now, O Jehovah, take, I pray Thee, my soul from me, for better &#91;is&#93; my death than my life.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonah 4:9; Micah 6:3"> And Jehovah saith, 'Is doing good displeasing to thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; 1 Kings 19:13; Jeremiah 17:15–16"> And Jonah goeth forth from the city, and sitteth on the east of the city, and maketh to himself there a booth, and sitteth under it in the shade, till that he seeth what is in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> And Jehovah God appointeth a gourd, and causeth it to come up over Jonah, to be a shade over his head, to give deliverance to him from his affliction, and Jonah rejoiceth because of the gourd &#91;with&#93; great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalms 102:10"> And God appointeth a worm at the going up of the dawn on the morrow, and it smiteth the gourd, and it drieth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Psalms 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> And it cometh to pass, about the rising of the sun, that God appointeth a cutting east wind, and the sun smiteth on the head of Jonah, and he wrappeth himself up, and asketh his soul to die, and saith, 'Better &#91;is&#93; my death than my life.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> And God saith unto Jonah: 'Is doing good displeasing to thee, because of the gourd?' and he saith, 'To do good is displeasing to me — unto death.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> And Jehovah saith, 'Thou hast had pity on the gourd, for which thou didst not labour, neither didst thou nourish it, which a son of a night was, and a son of a night perished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> and I — have not I pity on Nineveh, the great city, in which there are more than twelve myriads of human beings, who have not known between their right hand and their left — and much cattle!'</span>
