<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 22:31; Psalms 109:6"> And he sheweth me Joshua the high priest standing before the messenger of Jehovah, and the Adversary standing at his right hand, to be an adversary to him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Revelation 19:8; Ezra 9:15"> And Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and is standing before the messenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Isaiah 61:10; Ezekiel 36:25"> And he answereth and speaketh unto those standing before him, saying: 'Turn aside the filthy garments from off him.' And he saith unto him, 'See, I have caused thine iniquity to pass away from off thee, so as to clothe thee with costly apparel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 6:11; Exodus 29:6; Job 29:14"> He also said, 'Let them set a pure diadem on his head. And they set the pure diadem on his head, and clothe him with garments. And the messenger of Jehovah is standing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:13–17; Genesis 22:15–16; Zechariah 3:1"> and the messenger of Jehovah doth protest to Joshua, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Genesis 26:5; 2 Timothy 4:1–2"> 'Thus said Jehovah of Hosts: If in My ways thou dost walk, And if My charge thou dost keep, Then also thou dost judge My house, And also thou dost keep My courts, And I have given to thee conductors among these standing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Isaiah 11:1; Zechariah 6:12"> Hear, I pray thee, Joshua, the high priest, Thou and thy companions sitting before thee, (For men of type &#91;are&#93; they,) For lo, I am bringing in My servant — a Shoot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; Jeremiah 50:20; Isaiah 28:16"> For lo, the stone that I put before Joshua, On one stone &#91;are&#93; seven eyes, Lo, I am graving its graving, An affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts, And I have removed the iniquity of that land in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4; Zechariah 2:11"> In that day — an affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts, Ye do call, each unto his neighbour, Unto the place of the vine, And unto the place of the fig-tree!'</span>
