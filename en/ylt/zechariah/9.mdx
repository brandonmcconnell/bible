<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremiah 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> The burden of a word of Jehovah against the land of Hadrach, and Demmeseh — his place of rest: (When to Jehovah &#91;is&#93; the eye of man, And of all the tribes of Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> And also Hamath doth border thereon, Tyre and Zidon, for — very wise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> And Tyre doth build a bulwark to herself, And doth heap silver as dust, And gold as mire of out-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Lo, the Lord doth dispossess her, And He hath smitten in the sea her force, And she with fire is consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> See doth Ashkelon and fear, Also Gaza, and she is exceedingly pained, Also Ekron — for her expectation dried up, And perished hath a king from Gaza, And Ashkelon doth not remain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> And dwelt hath a foreigner in Ashdod, And I have cut off the excellency of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> And turned aside his blood from his mouth, His abominations from between his teeth, And he hath remained, even he, to our God, And he hath been as a leader in Judah, And Ekron as a Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> And I have pitched for My house a camp, Because of the passer through, and of the returner, And pass not through against them again doth an exactor, For, now, I have seen with My eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Rejoice exceedingly, O daughter of Zion, Shout, O daughter of Jerusalem, Lo, thy King doth come to thee, Righteous — and saved is He, Afflicted — and riding on an ass, And on a colt — a son of she-asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> And I have cut off the chariot from Ephraim, And the horse from Jerusalem, Yea, cut off hath been the bow of battle, And he hath spoken peace to nations, And his rule &#91;is&#93; from sea unto sea, And from the river unto the ends of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> Also thou — by the blood of thy covenant, I have sent thy prisoners out of the pit, There is no water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Turn back to a fenced place, Ye prisoners of the hope, Even to-day a second announcer I restore to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> For I have trodden for Me Judah, A bow I have filled &#91;with&#93; Ephraim, And I have stirred up thy sons, O Zion, Against thy sons, O Javan, And I have set thee as the sword of a hero.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> And Jehovah doth appear for them, And gone forth as lightning hath His arrow, And the Lord Jehovah with a trumpet bloweth, And He hath gone with whirlwinds of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> Jehovah of Hosts doth cover them over, And they consumed, and subdued sling-stones, Yea, they have drunk, They have made a noise as wine, And they have been full as a bowl, As corners of an altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> And saved them hath Jehovah their God In that day, as a flock of His people, For stones of a crown are displaying themselves over His ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> For what His goodness! and what His beauty! Corn the young men, And new wine the virgins — make fruitful!</span>
