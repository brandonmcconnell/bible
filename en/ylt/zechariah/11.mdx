<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:6–7; Jeremiah 22:23; Luke 21:23–24"> Open, O Lebanon, thy doors, And fire doth devour among thy cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 2:12–17; Ezekiel 31:17; Isaiah 10:33–34"> Howl, O fir, for fallen hath the cedar, For their honourable ones were destroyed, Howl, ye oaks of Bashan, For come down hath the fenced forest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:44; Jeremiah 25:34–36; Zechariah 11:8"> A voice of the howling of the shepherds! For destroyed was their robe of honour, A voice of the roaring of young lions! For destroyed was the excellency of Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> Thus said Jehovah my God: 'Feed the flock of the slaughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:7; Hosea 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> Whose buyers slay them, and are not guilty, And their sellers say, Blessed &#91;is&#93; Jehovah, And I am rich, And their shepherds have no pity on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> For I have pity no more on inhabitants of the land, An affirmation of Jehovah, And lo, I am causing man to come forth, Each into the hand of his neighbour, And into the hand of his king, And they have beaten down the land, And I do not deliver out of their hand.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Zechariah 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> And I feed the flock of slaughter, even you, ye afflicted of the flock; and I take to me two staves, the one I have called Pleasantness, and the other I have called Bands, and I feed the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> And I cut off the three shepherds in one month, and my soul is grieved with them, and also their soul hath abhorred me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> And I say, 'I do not feed you, the dying, let die; and the cut off, let be cut off; and the remaining ones, let each eat the flesh of its neighbour.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:7; Psalms 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> And I take My staff Pleasantness, and cut it asunder, to make void My covenant that I had made with all the peoples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> and it is broken in that day, and know well do the afflicted of the flock who are observing me, that it &#91;is&#93; a word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> And I say unto them: 'If good in your eyes, give my hire, and if not, forbear;' and they weigh out my hire — thirty silverlings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And Jehovah saith unto me, 'Cast it unto the potter;' the goodly price that I have been prized at by them, and I take the thirty silverlings, and cast them &#91;to&#93; the house of Jehovah, unto the potter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> And I cut asunder my second staff, Bands, to break the unity between Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> And Jehovah saith unto me, 'Again take to thee the instrument of a foolish shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremiah 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> For lo, I am raising up a shepherd in the land, The cut off he doth not inspect, The shaken off he doth not seek, And the broken he doth not heal, The standing he doth not sustain, And the flesh of the fat he doth eat, And their hoofs he doth break off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Ezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> Woe &#91;to&#93; the worthless shepherd, forsaking the flock, A sword &#91;is&#93; on his arm, and on his right eye, His arm is utterly dried up, And his right eye is very dim!'</span>
