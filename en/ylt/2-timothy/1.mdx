<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; John 17:3; John 10:28"> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, through the will of God, according to a promise of life that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:1; 1 Timothy 1:2; Acts 16:1"> to Timotheus, beloved child: Grace, kindness, peace, from God the Father, and Christ Jesus our Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Hebrews 13:8; Acts 23:1"> I am thankful to God, whom I serve from progenitors in a pure conscience, that unceasingly I have remembrance concerning thee in my supplications night and day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–1"> desiring greatly to see thee, being mindful of thy tears, that with joy I may be filled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Acts 16:1; 2 Timothy 3:15"> taking remembrance of the unfeigned faith that is in thee, that dwelt first in thy grandmother Lois, and thy mother Eunice, and I am persuaded that also in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8"> For which cause I remind thee to stir up the gift of God that is in thee through the putting on of my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:18; Galatians 5:22"> for God did not give us a spirit of fear, but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Mark 8:38; 1 Peter 4:13–15"> therefore thou mayest not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner, but do thou suffer evil along with the good news according to the power of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; Titus 3:4–5; Ephesians 1:3–4"> who did save us, and did call with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, that was given to us in Christ Jesus, before the times of the ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:2; Romans 16:26; 2 Peter 3:18"> and was made manifest now through the manifestation of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who indeed did abolish death, and did enlighten life and immortality through the good news,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:7–8; Acts 9:15"> to which I was placed a preacher and an apostle, and a teacher of nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; Jude 1:24; 1 Peter 4:19"> for which cause also these things I suffer, but I am not ashamed, for I have known in whom I have believed, and have been persuaded that he is able that which I have committed to him to guard — to that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 1:9; 2 Timothy 3:14; Philippians 4:9"> The pattern hold thou of sound words, which from me thou didst hear, in faith and love that &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:17; Romans 8:13; 1 Timothy 6:20"> the good thing committed guard thou through the Holy Spirit that is dwelling in us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:16; Philippians 2:21; Acts 19:10"> thou hast known this, that they did turn from me — all those in Asia, of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:19; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> may the Lord give kindness to the house of Onesiphorus, because many times he did refresh me, and of my chain was not ashamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 28:30–31"> but being in Rome, very diligently he sought me, and found;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Timothy 1:3"> may the Lord give to him to find kindness from the Lord in that day; and how many things in Ephesus he did minister thou dost very well know.</span>
