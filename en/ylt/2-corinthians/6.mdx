<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; Hebrews 12:15; 1 Peter 4:10"> And working together also we call upon &#91;you&#93; that ye receive not in vain the grace of God —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:8; Hebrews 4:7; Hebrews 3:13"> for He saith, 'In an acceptable time I did hear thee, and in a day of salvation I did help thee, lo, now &#91;is&#93; a well-accepted time; lo, now, a day of salvation,' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> in nothing giving any cause of offence, that the ministration may be not blamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> but in everything recommending ourselves as God's ministrants; in much patience, in tribulations, in necessities, in distresses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> in stripes, in imprisonments, in insurrections, in labours, in watchings, in fastings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> in pureness, in knowledge, in long-suffering, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love unfeigned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> in the word of truth, in the power of God, through the armour of the righteousness, on the right and on the left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> through glory and dishonour, through evil report and good report, as leading astray, and true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> as unknown, and recognized; as dying, and lo, we live; as chastened, and not put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> as sorrowful, and always rejoicing; as poor, and making many rich; as having nothing, and possessing all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> Our mouth hath been open unto you, O Corinthians, our heart hath been enlarged!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> ye are not straitened in us, and ye are straitened in your &#91;own&#93; bowels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> and &#91;as&#93; a recompense of the same kind, (as to children I say &#91;it&#93;,) be ye enlarged — also ye!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Become not yoked with others — unbelievers, for what partaking &#91;is there&#93; to righteousness and lawlessness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> and what fellowship to light with darkness? and what concord to Christ with Belial? or what part to a believer with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> and what agreement to the sanctuary of God with idols? for ye are a sanctuary of the living God, according as God said — 'I will dwell in them, and will walk among &#91;them&#93;, and I will be their God, and they shall be My people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> wherefore, come ye forth out of the midst of them, and be separated, saith the Lord, and an unclean thing do not touch, and I — I will receive you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> and I will be to you for a Father, and ye — ye shall be to Me for sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.'</span>
