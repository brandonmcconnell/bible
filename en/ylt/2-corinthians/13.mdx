<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6"> This third time do I come unto you; on the mouth of two witnesses or three shall every saying be established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> I have said before, and I say &#91;it&#93; before, as being present, the second time, and being absent, now, do I write to those having sinned before, and to all the rest, that if I come again, I will not spare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Luke 21:15"> since a proof ye seek of the Christ speaking in me, who to you is not infirm, but is powerful in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Romans 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:3–4"> for even if he was crucified from infirmity, yet he doth live from the power of God; for we also are weak in him, but we shall live with him from the power of God toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Corinthians 11:28; Psalms 139:23–24"> Your own selves try ye, if ye are in the faith; your own selves prove ye; do ye not know your own selves, that Jesus Christ is in you, if ye be not in some respect disapproved of?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:3–4"> and I hope that ye shall know that we — we are not disapproved of;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Timothy 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:8–9"> and I pray before God that ye do no evil, not that we may appear approved, but that ye may do that which is right, and we may be as disapproved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Mark 9:39; Acts 4:28–30"> for we are not able to do anything against the truth, but for the truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:10"> for we rejoice when we may be infirm, and ye may be powerful; and this also we pray for — your perfection!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Titus 1:13"> because of this, these things — being absent — I write, that being present, I may not treat &#91;any&#93; sharply, according to the authority that the Lord did give me for building up, and not for casting down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Romans 12:16; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Henceforth, brethren, rejoice; be made perfect, be comforted, be of the same mind, be at peace, and the God of the love and peace shall be with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Peter 5:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> salute one another in an holy kiss;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Romans 16:21–23; Hebrews 13:24"> salute you do all the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Romans 5:5; Romans 16:20"> the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit, &#91;is&#93; with you all! Amen.</span>
