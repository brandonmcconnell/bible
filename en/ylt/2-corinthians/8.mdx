<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 16:9; 2 Corinthians 9:2; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> And we make known to you, brethren, the grace of God, that hath been given in the assemblies of Macedonia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> because in much trial of tribulation the abundance of their joy, and their deep poverty, did abound to the riches of their liberality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> because, according to &#91;their&#93; power, I testify, and above &#91;their&#93; power, they were willing of themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> with much entreaty calling on us to receive the favour and the fellowship of the ministration to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> and not according as we expected, but themselves they did give first to the Lord, and to us, through the will of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 2 Corinthians 8:4"> so that we exhorted Titus, that, according as he did begin before, so also he may finish to you also this favour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; 2 Peter 1:5–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> but even as in every thing ye do abound, in faith, and word, and knowledge, and all diligence, and in your love to us, that also in this grace ye may abound;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 John 3:17–19; James 2:14–16"> not according to command do I speak, but because of the diligence of others, and of your love proving the genuineness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 2:6–8; Matthew 20:28"> for ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that because of you he became poor — being rich, that ye by that poverty may become rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18–19; Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> and an opinion in this do I give: for this to you &#91;is&#93; expedient, who not only to do, but also to will, did begin before — a year ago,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:2"> and now also finish doing &#91;it&#93;, that even as &#91;there is&#93; the readiness of the will, so also the finishing, out of that which ye have,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 12:42–44; 2 Corinthians 9:7; Luke 21:1–4"> for if the willing mind is present, according to that which any one may have it is well-accepted, not according to that which he hath not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 4:34; Romans 15:26–27"> for not that for others release, and ye pressured, &#91;do I speak,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:12; Acts 4:34"> but by equality, at the present time your abundance — for their want, that also their abundance may be for your want, that there may be equality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; Luke 22:35"> according as it hath been written, 'He who &#91;did gather&#93; much, had nothing over; and he who &#91;did gather&#93; little, had no lack.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Corinthians 2:13–14"> And thanks to God, who is putting the same diligence for you in the heart of Titus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 13:22; 2 Corinthians 8:10"> because indeed the exhortation he accepted, and being more diligent, of his own accord he went forth unto you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:18; 2 Corinthians 8:19; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> and we sent with him the brother, whose praise in the good news &#91;is&#93; through all the assemblies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:15; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> and not only so, but who was also appointed by vote by the assemblies, our fellow-traveller, with this favour that is ministered by us, unto the glory of the same Lord, and your willing mind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22; Romans 14:16; Ephesians 5:15"> avoiding this, lest any one may blame us in this abundance that is ministered by us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Peter 2:12; Philippians 4:8"> providing right things, not only before the Lord, but also before men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:20–22"> and we sent with them our brother, whom we proved in many things many times being diligent, and now much more diligent, by the great confidence that is toward you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Philemon 1:17; 2 Corinthians 8:6"> whether — about Titus — my partner and towards you fellow-worker, whether — our brethren, apostles of assemblies — glory of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Corinthians 9:2–4"> the shewing therefore of your love, and of our boasting on your behalf, to them shew ye, even in the face of the assemblies.</span>
