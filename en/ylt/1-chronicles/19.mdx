<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–2; 2 Samuel 10:1–19; 1 Samuel 12:12"> And it cometh to pass after this, that Nahash king of the sons of Ammon dieth, and his son reigneth in his stead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–6; 1 Samuel 30:26; Genesis 19:37–38"> and David saith, 'I do kindness with Hanun son of Nahash, for his father did with me kindness;' and David sendeth messengers to comfort him concerning his father. And the servants of David come in unto the land of the sons of Ammon, unto Hanun, to comfort him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:23–24; 1 Corinthians 13:5–7; 1 Kings 12:8–11"> and the heads of the sons of Ammon say to Hanun, 'Is David honouring thy father, in thine eyes, because he hath sent to thee comforters? in order to search, and to overthrow, and to spy out, the land, have not his servants come in unto thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:4–5; Isaiah 15:2; Mark 12:4"> And Hanun taketh the servants of David, and shaveth them, and cutteth their long robes in the midst, unto the buttocks, and sendeth them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:24–26; Matthew 18:31; 1 Kings 16:34"> And &#91;some&#93; go, and declare to David concerning the men, and he sendeth to meet them — for the men have been greatly ashamed — and the king saith, 'Dwell in Jericho till that your beard is grown, then ye have returned.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:5; Genesis 34:30; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> And the sons of Ammon see that they have made themselves abhorred by David, and Hanun and the sons of Ammon send a thousand talents of silver, to hire to them, from Aram-Naharaim, and from Aram-Maachah, and from Zobah, chariots and horsemen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Joshua 13:9; Joshua 13:16"> and they hire to them two and thirty thousand chariots, and the king of Maachah and his people, and they come in and encamp before Medeba, and the sons of Ammon have been gathered out of their cities, and come in to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Chronicles 11:10–47; 2 Samuel 23:8–39"> And David heareth, and sendeth Joab, and all the host of the mighty men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 20:24; Isaiah 28:6"> and the sons of Ammon come out and set battle in array at the opening of the city, and the kings who have come &#91;are&#93; by themselves in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:9–14; Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:22"> And Joab seeth that the front of the battle hath been unto him, before and behind, and he chooseth out of all the choice in Israel, and setteth in array to meet Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:12; 1 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> and the remnant of the people he hath given into the hand of Abishai his brother, and they set in array to meet the sons of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:27–28; Nehemiah 4:20; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> And he saith, 'If Aram be stronger than I, then thou hast been to me for salvation; and if the sons of Ammon be stronger than thou, then I have saved thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:14; Psalms 27:14; 2 Samuel 10:12"> be strong, and we strengthen ourselves, for our people, and for the cities of our God, and Jehovah doth that which is good in His eyes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 20:19–21; 2 Chronicles 13:5–16; Jeremiah 46:15–16"> And Joab draweth nigh, and the people who &#91;are&#93; with him, before Aram to battle, and they flee from his face;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Leviticus 26:7"> and the sons of Ammon have seen that Aram hath fled, and they flee — they also — from the face of Abishai his brother, and go in to the city. And Joab cometh in to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:16; Zechariah 14:1–3; Micah 4:11–12"> And Aram seeth that they have been smitten before Israel, and send messengers, and bring out Aram that &#91;is&#93; beyond the River, and Shophach head of the host of Hadarezer &#91;is&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6–7; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> And it is declared to David, and he gathereth all Israel, and passeth over the Jordan, and cometh in unto them, and setteth in array against them; yea, David setteth in array the battle to meet Aram, and they fight with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:18; Psalms 33:16; Psalms 18:32"> and Aram fleeth from the face of Israel, and David slayeth of Aram seven thousand charioteers, and forty thousand footmen, and Shophach head of the host he hath put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:19; Joshua 9:9–11; 1 Kings 20:1"> And the servants of Hadarezer see that they have been smitten before Israel, and they make peace with David and serve him, and Aram hath not been willing to help the sons of Ammon any more.</span>
