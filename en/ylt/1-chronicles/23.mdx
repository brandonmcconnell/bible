<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> And David is old, and satisfied with days, and causeth his son Solomon to reign over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> and gathereth all the heads of Israel, and the priests, and the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> and the Levites are numbered from a son of thirty years and upward, and their number, by their polls, is of mighty men thirty and eight thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> Of these to preside over the work of the house of Jehovah &#91;are&#93; twenty and four thousand, and officers and judges six thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalms 87:7"> and four thousand gatekeepers, and four thousand giving praise to Jehovah, 'with instruments that I made for praising,' &#91;saith David.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> And David distributeth them into courses: Of the sons of Levi: of Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> Of the Gershonite: Laadan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> Sons of Laadan: the head &#91;is&#93; Jehiel, and Zetham, and Joel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Sons of Shimei &#91;are&#93; Shelomith, and Haziel, and Haran, three; these &#91;are&#93; heads of the fathers of Laadan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And sons of Shimei &#91;are&#93; Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah; these &#91;are&#93; sons of Shimei, four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jahath is the head, and Zizah the second, and Jeush and Beriah have not multiplied sons, and they become the house of a father by one numbering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> Sons of Kohath &#91;are&#93; Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> Sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses; and Aaron is separated for his sanctifying the holy of holies, he and his sons — unto the age, to make perfume before Jehovah, to serve Him, and to bless in His name — unto the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalms 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> As to Moses, the man of God, his sons are called after the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> Sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> Sons of Gershom: Shebuel the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> And sons of Eliezer are Rehabiah the head, and Eliezer had no other sons, and the sons of Rehabiah have multiplied exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> Sons of Izhar: Shelomith the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Sons of Hebron: Jeriah the head, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Sons of Uzziel: Micah the head, and Ishshiah, the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> Sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi; sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And Eleazar dieth, and he had no sons, but daughters, and sons of Kish their brethren take them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> Sons of Mushi: Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These &#91;are&#93; sons of Levi, by the house of their fathers, heads of the fathers, by their appointments, in the number of names, by their polls, doing the work for the service of the house of Jehovah, from a son of twenty years and upward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> for David said, 'Jehovah, God of Israel, hath given rest to His people, and He doth tabernacle in Jerusalem unto the age;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> and also of the Levites, 'None &#91;are&#93; to bear the tabernacle and all its vessels for its service;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalms 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> for by the last words of David they &#91;took&#93; the number of the sons of Levi from a son of twenty years and upward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> for their station &#91;is&#93; at the side of the sons of Aaron, for the service of the house of Jehovah, over the courts, and over the chambers, and over the cleansing of every holy thing, and the work of the service of the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> and for the bread of the arrangement, and for fine flour for present, and for the thin unleavened cakes, and for &#91;the work of&#93; the pan, and for that which is fried, and for all &#91;liquid&#93; measure and &#91;solid&#93; measure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> and to stand, morning by morning, to give thanks, and to give praise to Jehovah, and so at evening;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> and for all the burnt-offerings — burnt-offerings to Jehovah for sabbaths, for new moons, and for appointed seasons, by number, according to the ordinance upon them continually, before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> And they have kept the charge of the tent of meeting, and the charge of the sanctuary, and the charge of the sons of Aaron, their brethren, for the service of the house of Jehovah.</span>
