<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; Psalms 132:5"> And he maketh for himself houses in the city of David, and prepareth a place for the ark of God, and stretcheth out for it a tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then said David, 'None &#91;are&#93; to carry the ark of God, except the Levites, for on them hath Jehovah fixed to carry the ark of God, and to serve Him — unto the age.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> And David assembleth all Israel unto Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of Jehovah unto its place that he had prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> And David gathereth the sons of Aaron, and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> Of sons of Kohath: Uriel the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> Of sons of Merari: Asaiah the chief, and his brethren, two hundred and twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> Of sons of Gershom: Joel the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> Of sons of Elizaphan: Shemaiah the chief, and his brethren, two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> Of sons of Hebron: Eliel the chief, and his brethren, eighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the chief, and his brethren, a hundred and twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And David calleth to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, and to the Levites, to Uriel, Asaiah, and Joel, Shemaiah, and Eliel, and Amminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> and saith to them, 'Ye &#91;are&#93; heads of the fathers of the Levites; sanctify yourselves, ye and your brethren, and ye have brought up the ark of Jehovah, God of Israel, unto &#91;the place&#93; I have prepared for it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> because at the first &#91;it was&#93; not ye, Jehovah our God made a breach upon us, because we sought Him not according to the ordinance.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> And the priests and the Levites sanctify themselves, to bring up the ark of Jehovah, God of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> and sons of the Levites bear the ark of God, as Moses commanded, according to the word of Jehovah, on their shoulder, with staves, above them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> And David saith to the heads of the Levites to appoint their brethren the singers, with instruments of song, psalteries, and harps, and cymbals, sounding, to lift up with the voice for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> And the Levites appoint Heman son of Joel, and of his brethren, Asaph son of Berechiah, and of the sons of Merari their brethren, Ethan son of Kushaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> and with them their brethren, the seconds &#91;in rank&#93;, Zechariah, Ben, and Jaaziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Unni, Eliab, and Benaiah, and Maaseiah, and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah; and Obed-Edom and Jeiel the gatekeepers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> and the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, with cymbals of brass to sound,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> and Zechariah, and Aziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jeheil, and Unni, and Eliab, and Maaseiah, and Benaiah, with psalteries besides virgins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah, and Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, and Azaziah, with harps, on the octave, to oversee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> And Chenaniah, head of the Levites, &#91;is&#93; over the burden; he instructeth about the burden, for he &#91;is&#93; intelligent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> And Berechiah and Elkanah &#91;are&#93; gatekeepers for the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> And Shebaniah, and Joshaphat, and Nethaneel, and Amasai, and Zechariah, and Benaiah, and Eliezer the priests, are blowing with trumpets before the ark of God; and Obed-Edom and Jehiah &#91;are&#93; gatekeepers for the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> And it is David, and the elders of Israel, and the heads of the thousands, who are going to bring up the ark of the covenant of Jehovah from the house of Obed-Edom with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> and it cometh to pass, in God's helping the Levites bearing the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, that they sacrifice seven bullocks and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> And David is wrapped in an upper robe of fine linen, and all the Levites who are bearing the ark, and the singers, and Chenaniah head of the burden of the singers; and on David &#91;is&#93; an Ephod of linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> And all Israel are bringing up the ark of the covenant of Jehovah with shouting, and with the sound of a cornet, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, sounding with psalteries and harps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> and it cometh to pass, the ark of the covenant of Jehovah is entering in unto the city of David, and Michal daughter of Saul is looking through the window, and seeth king David dancing and playing, and despiseth him in her heart.</span>
