<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 21:6; 1 Chronicles 11:33"> The head &#91;is&#93; Ahiezer, and Joash, sons of Shemaab the Gibeathite, and Jeziel, and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth, and Berachah, and Jehu the Antothite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 15:36; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> and Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty one among the thirty, and over the thirty, and Jeremiah, and Jahaziel, and Johanan, and Josabad the Gederathite.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 17:10; 2 Samuel 23:20"> And of the Gadite there have been separated unto David, to the fortress, to the wilderness, mighty of valour, men of the host for battle, setting in array target and buckler, and their faces the face of the lion, and as roes on the mountains for speed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Ezer the head, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Mishmannah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Attai the sixth, Eliel the seventh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Johanan the eighth, Elzabad the ninth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Jeremiah the tenth, Machbannai the eleventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8"> These &#91;are&#93; of the sons of Gad, heads of the host, one of a hundred &#91;is&#93; the least, and the greatest, of a thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Joshua 4:18; Jeremiah 49:19"> these &#91;are&#93; they who have passed over the Jordan in the first month, — and it is full over all its banks — and cause all &#91;they of&#93; the valley to flee to the east and to the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And there come of the sons of Benjamin and Judah unto the stronghold to David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Jude 1:9; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> and David goeth out before them, and answereth and saith to them, 'If for peace ye have come in unto me, to help me, I have a heart to unite with you; and if to betray me to mine adversaries — without violence in my hands — the God of our fathers doth see and reprove.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Judges 3:10; 2 Samuel 17:25"> And the Spirit hath clothed Amasai, head of the captains: 'To thee, O David, and with thee, O son of Jesse — peace! peace to thee, and peace to thy helper, for thy God hath helped thee;' and David receiveth them, and putteth them among the heads of the troop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:2–9"> And of Manasseh there have fallen unto David in his coming with the Philistines against Israel to battle — and they helped them not, for by counsel the princes of the Philistines sent him away, saying, 'With our heads he doth fall unto his master Saul.' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; 1 Samuel 29:11"> In his going unto Ziglag there have fallen unto him of Manasseh, Adnah, and Jozabad, and Jediael, and Michael, and Jozabad, and Elihu, and Zillthai, heads of the thousands that &#91;are&#93; of Manasseh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1–17; 1 Chronicles 12:20; 1 Chronicles 5:24"> and they have helped with David over the troop, for mighty of valour &#91;are&#93; all of them, and they are captains in the host,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> for at that time, day by day, they come in unto David to help him, till it is a great camp, like a camp of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And these &#91;are&#93; the numbers of the head, of the armed men of the host; they have come in unto David to Hebron to turn round the kingdom of Saul unto him, according to the mouth of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The sons of Judah, bearing target and spear, &#91;are&#93; six thousand and eight hundred, armed ones of the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the sons of Simeon, mighty ones of valour for the host, &#91;are&#93; seven thousand and a hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the sons of Levi &#91;are&#93; four thousand and six hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> and Jehoiada &#91;is&#93; the leader of the Aaronite, and with him &#91;are&#93; three thousand and seven hundred,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> and Zadok, a young man, mighty of valour, and of the house of his father &#91;are&#93; twenty and two heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> And of the sons of Benjamin, brethren of Saul, &#91;are&#93; three thousand, and hitherto their greater part are keeping the charge of the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> And of the sons of Ephraim &#91;are&#93; twenty thousand and eight hundred, mighty of valour, men of name, according to the house of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> And of the half of the tribe of Manasseh &#91;are&#93; eighteen thousand, who have been defined by name, to come in to cause David to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> And of the sons of Issachar, having understanding for the times, to know what Israel should do; their heads &#91;are&#93; two hundred, and all their brethren &#91;are&#93; at their command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; John 1:47"> Of Zebulun, going forth to the host, arranging battle with all instruments of battle, &#91;are&#93; fifty thousand, and keeping rank without a double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And of Naphtali, a thousand heads, and with them, with target and spear, &#91;are&#93; thirty and seven thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And of the Danite, arranging battle, &#91;are&#93; twenty and eight thousand and six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> And of Asher, going forth to the host, to arrange battle, &#91;are&#93; forty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> And from beyond the Jordan, of the Reubenite, and of the Gadite, and of the half of the tribe of Manasseh, with all instruments of the host for battle, &#91;are&#93; a hundred and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; Psalms 101:2"> All these &#91;are&#93; men of war, keeping rank — with a perfect heart they have come to Hebron, to cause David to reign over all Israel, and also all the rest of Israel &#91;are&#93; of one heart, to cause David to reign,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> and they are there, with David, three days, eating and drinking, for their brethren have prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And also those near unto them, unto Issachar, and Zebulun, and Naphtali, are bringing in bread on asses, and on camels, and on mules, and on oxen — food of fine flour, fig-cakes and grape-cakes, and wine, and oil, and oxen, and sheep, in abundance, for joy &#91;is&#93; in Israel.</span>
