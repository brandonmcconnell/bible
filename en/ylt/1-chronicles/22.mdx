<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1; 1 Chronicles 21:18–28; Genesis 28:17"> And David saith, 'This is the house of Jehovah God, and this the altar for burnt-offering for Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:17; 1 Kings 9:20–21; 1 Kings 5:17–18"> And David saith to gather the sojourners who &#91;are&#93; in the land of Israel, and appointeth hewers to hew hewn-stones to build a house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Kings 7:47"> And iron in abundance for nails for leaves of the gates, and for couplings, hath David prepared, and brass in abundance — there is no weighing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6–10; Ezra 3:7; 2 Chronicles 2:3"> And cedar-trees even without number, for the Zidonians and the Tyrians brought in cedar-trees in abundance to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 3:7; 1 Chronicles 29:1; John 3:30"> And David saith, 'Solomon my son &#91;is&#93; a youth and tender, and the house to be built to Jehovah &#91;is&#93; to be made exceedingly great, for name and for beauty to all the lands; let me prepare, I pray Thee, for it;' and David prepareth in abundance before his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; Acts 20:25–31; Acts 1:2"> And he calleth for Solomon his son, and chargeth him to build a house to Jehovah, God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:5; 1 Kings 8:16–20; 2 Chronicles 6:7–9"> and David saith to Solomon his son, 'As for me, it hath been with my heart to build a house to the name of Jehovah my God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 28:3; 1 Chronicles 17:4–10"> and the word of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; against me, saying, Blood in abundance thou hast shed, and great wars thou hast made: thou dost not build a house to My name, for much blood thou hast shed to the earth before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 5:4; 1 Kings 4:20"> 'Lo, a son is born to thee; he is a man of rest, and I have given rest to him from all his enemies round about, for Solomon is his name, and peace and quietness I give unto Israel in his days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; 2 Samuel 7:13–14; 1 Chronicles 17:12–14"> he doth build a house to My name, and he is to Me for a son, and I &#91;am&#93; to him for a father, and I have established the throne of his kingdom over Israel unto the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:16; 1 Chronicles 28:20; 2 Timothy 4:22"> 'Now, my son, Jehovah is with thee, and thou hast prospered, and hast built the house of Jehovah thy God, as He spake concerning thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 14:8; 1 Kings 3:9–12"> Only, Jehovah give to thee wisdom and understanding, and charge thee concerning Israel, even to keep the law of Jehovah thy God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:6–9; 1 Chronicles 28:20; 1 Chronicles 28:7"> then thou dost prosper, if thou dost observe to do the statutes and the judgments that Jehovah charged Moses with concerning Israel; be strong and courageous; do not fear, nor be cast down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:3; 2 Kings 25:16; 1 Chronicles 29:4–7"> 'And lo, in mine affliction, I have prepared for the house of Jehovah of gold talents a hundred thousand, and of silver a thousand thousand talents; and of brass and of iron there is no weighing, for in abundance it hath been, and wood and stones I have prepared, and to them thou dost add.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:6; Exodus 35:32; 1 Kings 7:14"> 'And with thee in abundance &#91;are&#93; workmen, hewers and artificers of stone and of wood, and every skilful man for every work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:11; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 5:14"> To the gold, to the silver, and to the brass, and to the iron, there is no number; arise and do, and Jehovah is with thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1–6; 3 John 1:8; 1 Chronicles 29:6"> And David giveth charge to all heads of Israel to give help to Solomon his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 23:25; 2 Samuel 7:1"> 'Is not Jehovah your God with you? yea, He hath given rest to you round about, for He hath given into my hand the inhabitants of the land, and subdued hath been the land before His people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 4:29; 1 Chronicles 16:11"> 'Now, give your heart and your soul to seek to Jehovah your God, and rise and build the sanctuary of Jehovah God, to bring in the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, and the holy vessels of God, to the house that is built to the name of Jehovah.'</span>
