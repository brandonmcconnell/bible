<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; Psalms 44:1"> For the courses of the gatekeepers: of the Korhites &#91;is&#93; Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> and to Meshelemiah &#91;are&#93; sons, Zechariah the first-born, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Elioenai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> And to Obed-Edom &#91;are&#93; sons, Shemaiah the first-born, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, and Sacar the fourth, and Nethaneel the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth, for God hath blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> And to Shemaiah his son have sons been born, who are ruling throughout the house of their father, for they &#91;are&#93; mighty of valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Sons of Shemaiah &#91;are&#93; Othni, and Rephael, and Obed, Elzabad; his brethren &#91;are&#93; sons of valour, Elihu and Semachiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> all these &#91;are&#93; of the sons of Obed-Edom; they, and their sons, and their brethren, men of valour with might for service, &#91;are&#93; sixty and two of Obed-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> And to Meshelemiah &#91;are&#93; sons and brethren, sons of valour, eighteen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> and to Hosah, of the sons of Merari, &#91;are&#93; sons: Shimri the head (though he was not first-born, yet his father setteth him for head),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth; all the sons and brethren of Hosah &#91;are&#93; thirteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> According to these &#91;are&#93; the courses of the gatekeepers; to the heads of the mighty ones &#91;are&#93; charges over-against their brethren, to minister in the house of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> and they cause to fall lots, as well the small as the great, according to the house of their fathers, for gate and gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the lot falleth eastward to Shelemiah; and &#91;for&#93; Zechariah his son — a counsellor with understanding — they cause to fall lots, and his lot goeth out northward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> to Obed-Edom southward, and to his sons, the house of the gatherings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> to Shuppim and to Hosah to the west, with the gate Shallecheth, in the highway, the ascent, charge over-against charge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> to the east the Levites &#91;are&#93; six; to the north daily four, to the south daily four, and to the gatherings two by two,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> at Parbar, to the west, &#91;are&#93; four at the highway, two at Parbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These are the courses of the gatekeepers, of the sons of the Korhite, and of the sons of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> And of the Levites, Ahijah &#91;is&#93; over the treasures of the house of God, even for the treasures of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> Sons of Laadan: sons of the Gershonite, of Laadan, heads of the fathers of Laadan the Gershonite: Jehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> Sons of Jehieli: Zetham, and Joel his brother, over the treasures of the house of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> for the Amramite, for the Izharite, for the Hebronite, for the Uzzielite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> And Shebuel son of Gershom, son of Moses, &#91;is&#93; president over the treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> And his brethren, of Eliezer, &#91;are&#93; Rehabiah his son, and Jeshaiah his son, and Joram his son, and Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> This Shelomith and his brethren &#91;are&#93; over all the treasures of the holy things, that David the king, and heads of the fathers, even heads of thousands, and of hundreds, and heads of the host, sanctified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> from the battles, even from the spoil they sanctified to strengthen the house of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> and all that Samuel the seer, and Saul son of Kish, and Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah sanctified, every one sanctifying &#91;any thing — it is&#93; by the side of Shelomith and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> Of the Izharite, Chenaniah and his sons &#91;are&#93; for the outward work over Israel, for officers and for judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the Hebronite, Hashabiah and his brethren, sons of valour, a thousand and seven hundred, &#91;are&#93; over the inspection of Israel, beyond the Jordan westward, for all the work of Jehovah, and for the service of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> Of the Hebronite, Jerijah &#91;is&#93; the head, of the Hebronite, according to his generations, for the fathers — in the fortieth year of the reign of David they have been sought out, and there are found among them mighty ones of valour, in Jazer of Gilead —</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> and his brethren, sons of valour, two thousand and seven hundred, &#91;are&#93; heads of the fathers, and king David appointeth them over the Reubenite, and the Gadite, and the half of the tribe of Manasseh, for every matter of God and matter of the king.</span>
