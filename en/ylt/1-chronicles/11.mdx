<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 1 Samuel 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:3"> And all the elders of Israel come in unto the king to Hebron, and David maketh with them a covenant in Hebron before Jehovah, and they anoint David for king over Israel, according to the word of Jehovah by the hand of Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 1:21; Genesis 15:21; Joshua 15:8"> And David goeth, and all Israel, to Jerusalem — it &#91;is&#93; Jebus — and there the Jebusite, the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2; 1 Kings 8:1; Psalms 87:2"> And the inhabitants of Jebus say to David, 'Thou dost not come in hither;' and David captureth the fortress of Zion — it &#91;is&#93; the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 2 Samuel 20:23; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And David saith, 'Whoever smiteth the Jebusite first doth become head and prince;' and go up first doth Joab son of Zeruiah and becometh head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Samuel 5:7; Psalms 2:6"> And David dwelleth in the fortress, therefore they have called it, 'City of David;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 9:6; 1 Kings 11:27; 2 Kings 12:20"> and he buildeth the city round about, from Millo, and unto the circumference, and Joab restoreth the rest of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 41:10; 1 Chronicles 9:20"> And David goeth, going on and becoming great, and Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:3; 1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Chronicles 12:38"> And these &#91;are&#93; heads of the mighty ones whom David hath, who are strengthening themselves with him in his kingdom, with all Israel, to cause him to reign, according to the word of Jehovah, over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8; 1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 12:18"> And this &#91;is&#93; an account of the mighty ones whom David hath: Jashobeam son of a Hachmonite &#91;is&#93; head of the thirty; he is lifting up his spear against three hundred — wounded, at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:17–19; 2 Samuel 23:23"> And after him &#91;is&#93; Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, he &#91;is&#93; among the three mighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:11–12; 1 Samuel 17:1"> he hath been with David in Pas-Dammim, and the Philistines have been gathered there to battle, and a portion of the field is full of barley, and the people have fled from the face of the Philistines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; 2 Kings 5:1; 1 Samuel 14:23"> and they station themselves in the midst of the portion, and deliver it, and smite the Philistines, and Jehovah saveth — a great salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 2 Samuel 5:18; Isaiah 17:5"> And three of the thirty heads go down on the rock unto David, unto the cave of Adullam, and the host of the Philistines is encamping in the valley of Rephaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:23"> and David &#91;is&#93; then in the fortress, and the station of the Philistines &#91;is&#93; then in Beth-Lehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 12:3; 2 Samuel 23:15–16"> and David longeth, and saith, 'Who doth give me to drink water from the well of Beth-Lehem, that &#91;is&#93; at the gate!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:13; 1 Samuel 19:5; Acts 20:24"> And the three break through the camp of the Philistines, and draw water from the well of Beth-Lehem, that &#91;is&#93; at the gate, and bear and bring in unto David, and David hath not been willing to drink it, and poureth it out to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:17; Psalms 72:14; Judges 9:17"> and saith, 'Far be it from me, by my God, to do this; the blood of these men do I drink with their lives? for with their lives they have brought it;' and he was not willing to drink it; these &#91;things&#93; did the three mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:6; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And Abishai brother of Joab, he hath been head of the three: and he is lifting up his spear against three hundred — wounded, and hath a name among three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:41; Matthew 13:8"> Of the three by the two he is honoured, and becometh their head; and unto the &#91;first&#93; three he hath not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21"> Benaiah son of Jehoiada, son of a man of valour, of great deeds, from Kabzeel: he hath smitten the two lion-like Moabites, and he hath gone down and smitten the lion in the midst of the pit, in the day of snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:7; 1 Samuel 17:4; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And he hath smitten the man, the Egyptian — a man of measure, five by the cubit — and in the hand of the Egyptian &#91;is&#93; a spear like a beam of weavers, and he goeth down unto him with a rod, and taketh violently away the spear out of the hand of the Egyptian, and slayeth him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> These &#91;things&#93; hath Benaiah son of Jehoiada done, and hath a name among the three mighty ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23"> Of the thirty, lo, he &#91;is&#93; honoured, and unto the &#91;first&#93; three he hath not come, and David setteth him over his guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:7; 2 Samuel 21:19; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> And the mighty ones of the forces &#91;are&#93; Asahel brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo of Beth-Lehem,</span>
