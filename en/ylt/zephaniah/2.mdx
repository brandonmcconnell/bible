<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:14; 2 Chronicles 20:4; Esther 4:16"> Bend yourselves, yea, bend ye, O nation not desired,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Hosea 13:3; Nahum 1:6"> Before the bringing forth of a statute, As chaff hath the day passed on, While yet not come in upon you doth the heat of the anger of Jehovah, While yet not come in upon you doth a day of the anger of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:14–15; Isaiah 26:20–21; Psalms 76:9"> Seek Jehovah, all ye humble of the land, Who His judgment have done, Seek ye righteousness, seek humility, It may be ye are hidden in a day of the anger of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:5–7; Amos 1:6–8; Jeremiah 47:1–7"> For Gaza is forsaken, And Ashkelon &#91;is&#93; for a desolation, Ashdod! at noon they do cast her forth, And Ekron is rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Jeremiah 47:7; Joshua 13:3"> Ho! O inhabitants of the sea-coast, Nation of the Cherethites, A word of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; against you, Canaan, land of the Philistines, And I have destroyed thee without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Ezekiel 25:5; Isaiah 5:17"> And the sea-coast hath been habitations, Cottages &#91;for&#93; shepherds, and folds &#91;for&#93; a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Ezekiel 39:25; Zephaniah 3:20"> And the coast hath been for the remnant of the house of Judah, By them they have pleasure, In houses of Ashkelon at even they lie down, For inspect them doth Jehovah their God, And He hath turned back &#91;to&#93; their captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 1:13; Psalms 83:4–7; Jeremiah 48:27–29"> I have heard the reproach of Moab, And the revilings of the sons of Ammon, Wherewith they reproached My people, And magnify &#91;themselves&#93; against their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Isaiah 11:14; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Therefore, I live, An affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel, Surely, Moab is as Sodom, And the sons of Ammon as Gomorrah, An overrunning of nettles and salt-pits, And a desolation — unto the age. A residue of My people do seize them, And a remnant of My nation inherit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Jeremiah 48:29; Zephaniah 2:8"> This &#91;is&#93; to them for their arrogancy, Because they have reproached, And they magnify &#91;themselves&#93; against the people of Jehovah of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Zechariah 13:2; Psalms 138:4"> Fearful &#91;is&#93; Jehovah against them, For He made bare all gods of the land, And bow themselves to Him, each from his place, Do all islanders of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:4–9; Isaiah 43:3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Also ye, O Cushim, pierced of My sword &#91;are&#93; they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 3:7; Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 10:12"> And He stretcheth His hand against the north, And doth destroy Asshur, And he setteth Nineveh for a desolation, A dry land like a wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 34:11–17; Amos 9:1"> And crouched in her midst have droves, Every beast of the nation, Both pelican and hedge-hog in her knobs lodge, A voice doth sing at the window, 'Destruction &#91;is&#93; at the threshold, For the cedar-work is exposed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 47:7–8"> This &#91;is&#93; the exulting city that is dwelling confidently, That is saying in her heart, 'I &#91;am&#93;, and beside me there is none,' How hath she been for a desolation, A crouching-place for beasts, Every one passing by her doth hiss, He doth shake his hand!</span>
