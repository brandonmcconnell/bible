<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When a man maketh a wonderful vow, by thy valuation the persons &#91;are&#93; Jehovah's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> When thy valuation hath been of the male from a son of twenty years even unto a son of sixty years, then hath been thy valuation fifty shekels of silver by the shekel of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> And if it &#91;is&#93; a female — then hath thy valuation been thirty shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and if from a son of five years even unto a son of twenty years — then hath thy valuation been of the male twenty shekels, and for the female, ten shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> and if from a son of a month even unto a son of five years — then hath thy valuation been of the male five shekels of silver, and for the female thy valuation &#91;is&#93; three shekels of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:10"> and if from a son of sixty years and above — if a male, then hath thy valuation been fifteen shekels, and for a female, ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> 'And if he is poorer than thy valuation, then he hath presented himself before the priest, and the priest hath valued him; according to that which the hand of him who is vowing doth reach doth the priest value him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> 'And if &#91;it is&#93; a beast of which they bring near an offering to Jehovah, all that &#91;one&#93; giveth of it to Jehovah is holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> he doth not change it nor exchange it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good; and if he really change beast for beast, — then it hath been — it and its exchange is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> 'And if &#91;it is&#93; any unclean beast of which they do not bring near an offering to Jehovah, then he hath presented the beast before the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the priest hath valued it; whether good or bad, according to thy valuation, O priest, so it is;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> and if he really redeem it, then he hath added its fifth to thy valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> 'And when a man sanctifieth his house, a holy thing to Jehovah, then hath the priest valued it, whether good or bad; as the priest doth value it so it standeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> and if he who is sanctifying doth redeem his house, then he hath added a fifth of the money of thy valuation to it, and it hath become his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> 'And if of the field of his possession a man sanctify to Jehovah, then hath thy valuation been according to its seed; a homer of barley-seed at fifty shekels of silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> if from the year of the jubilee he sanctify his field, according to thy valuation it standeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> and if after the jubilee he sanctify his field, then hath the priest reckoned to him the money according to the years which are left, unto the year of the jubilee, and it hath been abated from thy valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> 'And if he really redeem the field — he who is sanctifying it — then he hath added a fifth of the money of thy valuation to it, and it hath been established to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and if he do not redeem the field, or if he hath sold the field to another man, it is not redeemed any more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> and the field hath been, in its going out in the jubilee, holy to Jehovah as a field which is devoted; to the priest is its possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> 'And if the field of his purchase (which &#91;is&#93; not of the fields of his possession) &#91;one&#93; sanctify to Jehovah —</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> then hath the priest reckoned to him the amount of thy valuation unto the year of jubilee, and he hath given thy valuation in that day — a holy thing to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> in the year of the jubilee the field returneth to him from whom he bought it, to him whose &#91;is&#93; the possession of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> And all thy valuation is by the shekel of the sanctuary: twenty gerahs is the shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> 'Only, a firstling which is Jehovah's firstling among beasts — no man doth sanctify it, whether ox or sheep; it &#91;is&#93; Jehovah's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> And if among the unclean beasts, then he hath ransomed &#91;it&#93; at thy valuation, and he hath added its fifth to it; and if it is not redeemed, then it hath been sold at thy valuation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> 'Only, no devoted thing which a man devoteth to Jehovah, of all that he hath, of man, and beast, and of the field of his possession, is sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> 'No devoted thing, which is devoted of man, is ransomed, it is surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> And all tithe of the land, of the seed of the land, of the fruit of the tree, is Jehovah's — holy to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> 'And if a man really redeem &#91;any&#93; of his tithe, its fifth he addeth to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> 'And all the tithe of the herd and of the flock — all that passeth by under the rod — the tenth is holy to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> he enquireth not between good and bad, nor doth he change it; and if he really change it — then it hath been — it and its exchange is holy; it is not redeemed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These &#91;are&#93; the commands which Jehovah hath commanded Moses for the sons of Israel, in mount Sinai.</span>
