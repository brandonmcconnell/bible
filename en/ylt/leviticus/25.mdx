<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Galatians 4:24–25; Exodus 19:1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, in mount Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:34–35; Exodus 23:10"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When ye come in unto the land which I am giving to you, then hath the land kept a sabbath to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> 'Six years thou dost sow thy field, and six years thou dost prune thy vineyard, and hast gathered its increase,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 25:20–23; Exodus 23:10–11"> and in the seventh year a sabbath of rest is to the land, a sabbath to Jehovah; thy field thou dost not sow, and thy vineyard thou dost not prune;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Isaiah 37:30"> the spontaneous growth of thy harvest thou dost not reap, and the grapes of thy separated thing thou dost not gather, a year of rest it is to the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:44; Exodus 23:11; Acts 4:34–35"> 'And the sabbath of the land hath been to you for food, to thee, and to thy man-servant, and to thy handmaid, and to thy hireling, and to thy settler, who are sojourning with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and to thy cattle, and to the beast which &#91;is&#93; in thy land, is all thine increase for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:15; Genesis 2:2"> 'And thou hast numbered to thee seven sabbaths of years, seven years seven times, and the days of the seven sabbaths of years have been to thee nine and forty years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Leviticus 23:27; 1 Thessalonians 1:8"> and thou hast caused a trumpet of shouting to pass over in the seventh month, in the tenth of the month; in the day of the atonements ye do cause a trumpet to pass over through all your land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; Isaiah 63:4; 1 Peter 2:16"> and ye have hallowed the year, the fiftieth year; and ye have proclaimed liberty in the land to all its inhabitants; a jubilee it is to you; and ye have turned back each unto his possession; yea, each unto his family ye do turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 27:17; Leviticus 25:4–5"> 'A jubilee it &#91;is&#93;, the fiftieth year, a year it is to you; ye sow not, nor reap its spontaneous growth, nor gather its separated things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 25:6–7"> for a jubilee it &#91;is&#93;, holy it is to you; out of the field ye eat its increase;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 27:17–24; Numbers 36:4"> in the year of this jubilee ye turn back each unto his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Leviticus 25:17; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> 'And when thou sellest anything to thy fellow, or buyest from the hand of thy fellow, ye do not oppress one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18–23; Philippians 4:5"> by the number of years after the jubilee thou dost buy from thy fellow; by the number of the years of increase he doth sell to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 25:27; Leviticus 25:51"> according to the multitude of the years thou dost multiply its price, and according to the fewness of the years thou dost diminish its price; for a number of increases he is selling to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:43"> and ye do not oppress one another, and thou hast been afraid of thy God; for I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; Deuteronomy 12:10; Proverbs 1:33"> 'And ye have done My statutes, and My judgments ye keep, and have done them, and ye have dwelt on the land confidently,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:26; Psalms 85:12; Leviticus 26:5"> and the land hath given its fruit, and ye have eaten to satiety, and have dwelt confidently on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 25:4; Luke 12:29; Numbers 11:4"> 'And when ye say, What do we eat in the seventh year, lo, we do not sow, nor gather our increase?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Corinthians 9:10"> then I have commanded My blessing on you in the sixth year, and it hath made the increase for three years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 26:10; Isaiah 37:30"> and ye have sown the eighth year, and have eaten of the old increase; until the ninth year, until the coming in of its increase, ye do eat the old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Psalms 39:12; Ezekiel 48:14"> 'And the land is not sold — to extinction, for the land &#91;is&#93; Mine, for sojourners and settlers &#91;are&#93; ye with Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Leviticus 25:51–53; Leviticus 25:27"> and in all the land of your possession a redemption ye do give to the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Jeremiah 32:7–8; Ruth 3:12"> 'When thy brother becometh poor, and hath sold his possession, then hath his redeemer who is near unto him come, and he hath redeemed the sold thing of his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7"> and when a man hath no redeemer, and his own hand hath attained, and he hath found as sufficient &#91;for&#93; its redemption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> then he hath reckoned the years of its sale, and hath given back that which is over to the man to whom he sold &#91;it&#93;, and he hath returned to his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9–10; Leviticus 25:10; 1 Peter 1:4–5"> 'And if his hand hath not found sufficiency to give back to him, then hath his sold thing been in the hand of him who buyeth it till the year of jubilee; and it hath gone out in the jubilee, and he hath returned to his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> 'And when a man selleth a dwelling-house &#91;in&#93; a walled city, then hath his right of redemption been until the completion of a year from its selling; days — is his right of redemption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and if it is not redeemed until the fulness to him of a perfect year, then hath the house which &#91;is&#93; in a walled city been established to extinction to the buyer of it, to his generations; it goeth not out in the jubilee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8"> and a house of the villages which have no wall round about, on the field of the country is reckoned; redemption is to it, and in the jubilee it goeth out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 21:1–45; Numbers 35:1–8"> 'As to cities of the Levites — houses of the cities of their possession — redemption age-during is to the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> as to him who redeemeth from the Levites, both the sale of a house and the city of his possession have gone out in the jubilee, for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession in the midst of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 25:23; Acts 4:36–37; Numbers 35:2–5"> And a field, a suburb of their cities, is not sold; for a possession age-during it &#91;is&#93; to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–8; Acts 11:29; Leviticus 25:25"> 'And when thy brother is become poor, and his hand hath failed with thee, then thou hast kept hold on him, sojourner and settler, and he hath lived with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Ezekiel 18:8"> thou takest no usury from him, or increase; and thou hast been afraid of thy God; and thy brother hath lived with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> thy money thou givest not to him in usury, and for increase thou givest not thy food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Numbers 15:41; Exodus 20:2"> I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah your God, who hath brought you out of the land of Egypt, to give to you the land of Canaan, to become your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 21:2; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 4:1"> 'And when thy brother becometh poor with thee, and he hath been sold to thee, thou dost not lay on him servile service;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3"> as an hireling, as a settler, he is with thee, till the year of the jubilee he doth serve with thee, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Exodus 21:3; John 8:32"> then he hath gone out from thee, he and his sons with him, and hath turned back unto his family; even unto the possession of his fathers he doth turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Leviticus 25:55; Romans 6:22; 1 Corinthians 7:21–23"> 'For they &#91;are&#93; My servants, whom I have brought out from the land of Egypt: they are not sold &#91;with&#93; the sale of a servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Leviticus 25:46; Exodus 1:13–14"> thou rulest not over him with rigour, and thou hast been afraid of thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Psalms 2:8–9; Isaiah 14:1–2"> 'And thy man-servant and thy handmaid whom thou hast &#91;are&#93; of the nations who &#91;are&#93; round about you; of them ye buy man-servant and handmaid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–6"> and also of the sons of the settlers who are sojourning with you, of them ye buy, and of their families who &#91;are&#93; with you, which they have begotten in your land, and they have been to you for a possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 14:2; Leviticus 25:43"> and ye have taken them for inheritance to your sons after you, to occupy &#91;for&#93; a possession; to the age ye lay service upon them, but upon your brethren, the sons of Israel, one with another, thou dost not rule over him with rigour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 2:5; Leviticus 25:26; 1 Samuel 2:7–8"> 'And when the hand of a sojourner or settler with thee attaineth &#91;riches&#93;, and thy brother with him hath become poor, and he hath been sold to a sojourner, a settler with thee, or to the root of the family of a sojourner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Hebrews 2:11–13; Leviticus 25:35"> after he hath been sold, there is a right of redemption to him; one of his brethren doth redeem him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 25:26"> or his uncle, or a son of his uncle, doth redeem him, or any of the relations of his flesh, of his family, doth redeem him, or — his own hand hath attained — then he hath been redeemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16; Leviticus 25:53"> 'And he hath reckoned with his buyer from the year of his being sold to him till the year of jubilee, and the money of his sale hath been by the number of years; as the days of an hireling it is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> 'If yet many years, according to them he giveth back his redemption &#91;money&#93;, from the money of his purchase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> 'And if few are left of the years till the year of jubilee, then he hath reckoned with him, according to his years he doth give back his redemption &#91;money&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Leviticus 25:46; Leviticus 25:43"> as an hireling, year by year, he is with him, and he doth not rule him with rigour before thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 21:2–3; Leviticus 25:40–41; Isaiah 49:9"> 'And if he is not redeemed in these &#91;years&#93;, then he hath gone out in the year of jubilee, he and his sons with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Leviticus 25:42; Isaiah 43:3; Exodus 13:3"> For to Me &#91;are&#93; the sons of Israel servants; My servants they &#91;are&#93;, whom I have brought out of the land of Egypt; I, Jehovah, &#91;am&#93; your God.</span>
