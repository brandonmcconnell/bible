<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Speak unto the priests, sons of Aaron, and thou hast said unto them, For &#91;any&#93; person &#91;a priest&#93; is not defiled among his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> except for his relation who &#91;is&#93; near unto him — for his mother, and for his father, and for his son, and for his daughter, and for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and for his sister, the virgin, who is near unto him, who hath not been to a man; for her he is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> 'A master &#91;priest&#93; doth not defile himself among his people — to pollute himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> they do not make baldness on their head, and the corner of their beard they do not shave, and in their flesh they do not make a cutting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> they are holy to their God, and they pollute not the name of their God, for the fire-offerings of Jehovah, bread of their God, they are bringing near, and have been holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> 'A woman, a harlot, or polluted, they do not take, and a woman cast out from her husband they do not take, for he &#91;is&#93; holy to his God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> and thou hast sanctified him, for the bread of thy God he is bringing near; he is holy to thee; for holy &#91;am&#93; I, Jehovah, sanctifying you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> 'And a daughter of any priest when she polluteth herself by going a-whoring — her father she is polluting; with fire she is burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> 'And the high priest of his brethren, on whose head is poured the anointing oil, and hath consecrated his hand to put on the garments, his head doth not uncover, nor rend his garments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> nor beside any dead person doth he come; for his father and for his mother he doth not defile himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> nor from the sanctuary doth he go out, nor doth he pollute the sanctuary of his God, for the separation of the anointing oil of his God &#91;is&#93; on him; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> 'And he taketh a wife in her virginity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> widow, or cast out, or polluted one — a harlot — these he doth not take, but a virgin of his own people he doth take &#91;for&#93; a wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> and he doth not pollute his seed among his people; for I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, sanctifying him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> 'Speak unto Aaron, saying, No man of thy seed to their generations in whom there is blemish doth draw near to bring near the bread of his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> for no man in whom &#91;is&#93; blemish doth draw near — a man blind, or lame or dwarfed, or enlarged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> or a man in whom there is a breach in the foot, or a breach in the hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> or hump-backed, or a dwarf, or with a mixture in his eye, or a scurvy person, or scabbed, or broken-testicled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> 'No man in whom is blemish (of the seed of Aaron the priest) doth come nigh to bring near the fire-offerings of Jehovah; blemish &#91;is&#93; in him; the bread of his God he doth not come nigh to bring near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> 'Bread of his God — of the most holy things, and of the holy things — he doth eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> only, unto the vail he doth not enter, and unto the altar he doth not draw nigh; for blemish &#91;is&#93; in him; and he doth not pollute My sanctuaries; for I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, sanctifying them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> And Moses speaketh unto Aaron, and unto his sons, and unto all the sons of Israel.</span>
