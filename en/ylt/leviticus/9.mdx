<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Ezekiel 43:26–27; Leviticus 15:14"> And it cometh to pass on the eighth day, Moses hath called for Aaron and for his sons, and for the elders of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 10:10–14; Leviticus 8:14"> and he saith unto Aaron, 'Take to thyself a calf, a son of the herd, for a sin-offering, and a ram for a burnt-offering, perfect ones, and bring near before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; Revelation 5:9; Titus 2:14"> 'And unto the sons of Israel thou dost speak, saying, Take ye a kid of the goats for a sin-offering, and a calf, and a lamb, sons of a year, perfect ones, for a burnt-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:43; Ezekiel 43:2; Leviticus 9:23"> and a bullock and a ram for peace-offerings, to sacrifice before Jehovah, and a present mixed with oil; for to-day Jehovah hath appeared unto you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2–3; Exodus 19:17; Nehemiah 8:1"> And they take that which Moses hath commanded unto the front of the tent of meeting, and all the company draw near and stand before Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:23; Ezekiel 43:2; Exodus 24:16"> and Moses saith, 'This &#91;is&#93; the thing which Jehovah hath commanded; do &#91;it&#93;, and the honour of Jehovah doth appear unto you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:3; Hebrews 5:1; Leviticus 4:3"> And Moses saith unto Aaron, 'Draw near unto the altar, and make thy sin-offering, and thy burnt-offering, and make atonement for thyself, and for the people, and make the offering of the people, and make atonement for them, as Jehovah hath commanded.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:1–12; Leviticus 1:4–5; Leviticus 4:29"> And Aaron draweth near unto the altar, and slaughtereth the calf of the sin-offering, which &#91;is&#93; for himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7; Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 4:25"> and the sons of Aaron bring the blood near unto him, and he dippeth his finger in the blood, and putteth &#91;it&#93; on the horns of the altar, and the blood he hath poured out at the foundation of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 8:16–17; Proverbs 23:26; Leviticus 3:9–11"> and the fat, and the kidneys, and the redundance of the liver, of the sin-offering, he hath made a perfume on the altar, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 8:17; Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 16:27–28"> and the flesh and the skin he hath burnt with fire, at the outside of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Ephesians 5:25–27; Leviticus 1:1–17"> And he slaughtereth the burnt-offering, and the sons of Aaron have presented unto him the blood, and he sprinkleth it on the altar round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the burnt-offering they have presented unto him, by its pieces, and the head, and he maketh perfume on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 8:21"> and he washeth the inwards and the legs, and maketh perfume for the burnt-offering on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–31; Hebrews 5:3; Leviticus 9:3"> And he bringeth near the offering of the people, and taketh the goat of the sin-offering which &#91;is&#93; for the people, and slaughtered it, and maketh it a sin-offering, like the first;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12–14; Leviticus 8:18–21; Hebrews 10:1–22"> and he bringeth near the burnt-offering, and maketh it, according to the ordinance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:53; Leviticus 9:1"> and he bringeth near the present, and filleth his palm with it, and maketh perfume on the altar, apart from the burnt-offering of the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Romans 5:1; Ephesians 2:14–17"> And he slaughtereth the bullock and the ram, a sacrifice of the peace-offerings, which &#91;are&#93; for the people, and sons of Aaron present the blood unto him (and he sprinkleth it on the altar round about),</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 9:10; Leviticus 3:9"> and the fat of the bullock, and of the ram, the fat tail, and the covering &#91;of the inwards&#93;, and the kidneys, and the redundance above the liver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 3:14–17; Leviticus 7:29–34"> and they set the fat on the breasts, and he maketh perfume with the fat on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Leviticus 7:24; Exodus 29:24"> and the breasts, and the right leg hath Aaron waved — a wave-offering before Jehovah, as He hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Luke 24:50; Numbers 6:23–27"> And Aaron lifteth up his hand towards the people, and blesseth them, and cometh down from making the sin-offering, and the burnt-offering, and the peace-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 9:6; Numbers 16:19; Luke 1:21–22"> And Moses goeth in — Aaron also — unto the tent of meeting, and they come out, and bless the people, and the honour of Jehovah appeareth unto all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 6:21; 1 Kings 18:38–39; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> and fire cometh out from before Jehovah, and consumeth on the altar the burnt-offering, and the fat; and all the people see, and cry aloud, and fall on their faces.</span>
