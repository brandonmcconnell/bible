<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 19:3; Exodus 25:22; Exodus 29:42"> And Jehovah calleth unto Moses, and speaketh unto him out of the tent of meeting, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–19; Romans 12:6; 1 Chronicles 16:29"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, Any man of you when he doth bring near an offering to Jehovah, out of the cattle — out of the herd, or out of the flock — ye do bring near your offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Hebrews 9:14; Leviticus 22:19–24"> 'If his offering &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering out of the herd — a male, a perfect one, he doth bring near, unto the opening of the tent of meeting he doth bring it near, at his pleasure, before Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Exodus 29:15; Leviticus 4:20"> and he hath laid his hand on the head of the burnt-offering, and it hath been accepted for him to make atonement for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 3:8; Leviticus 3:2"> and he hath slaughtered the son of the herd before Jehovah; and sons of Aaron, the priests, have brought the blood near, and sprinkled the blood on the altar round about, which &#91;is&#93; at the opening of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:8; Genesis 3:21"> 'And he hath stripped the burnt-offering, and hath cut it into its pieces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Genesis 22:9; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> and the sons of Aaron the priest have put fire on the altar, and arranged wood on the fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 9:13–14; Leviticus 1:12; 1 Kings 18:33"> and sons of Aaron, the priests, have arranged the pieces, with the head and the fat, on the wood, which &#91;is&#93; on the fire, which &#91;is&#93; on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Leviticus 1:13; Ephesians 5:2"> and its inwards and its legs he doth wash with water; and the priest hath made perfume with the whole on the altar, a burnt-offering, a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; Leviticus 1:2–3; Genesis 8:20"> 'And if his offering &#91;is&#93; out of the flock — out of the sheep or out of the goats — for a burnt-offering, a male, a perfect one, he doth bring near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Exodus 40:22; Leviticus 7:2"> and he hath slaughtered it by the side of the altar northward, before Jehovah; and sons of Aaron, the priests, have sprinkled its blood on the altar round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and he hath cut it into its pieces, and its head and its fat, and the priest hath arranged them on the wood, which &#91;is&#93; on the fire, which &#91;is&#93; on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:9"> and the inwards and the legs he doth wash with water, and the priest hath brought the whole near, and hath made perfume on the altar; it &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering, a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Luke 2:24; Leviticus 12:8"> 'And if his offering &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering out of the fowl to Jehovah, than he hath brought near his offering out of the turtle-doves or out of the young pigeons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:8–9; Psalms 22:21; 1 John 2:27"> and the priest hath brought it near unto the altar, and hath wrung off its head, and hath made perfume on the altar, and its blood hath been wrung out by the side of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Luke 1:35; Leviticus 16:27"> and he hath turned aside its crop with its feathers, and hath cast it near the altar, eastward, unto the place of ashes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; John 19:30; Leviticus 1:9–10"> and he hath cleaved it with its wings (he doth not separate &#91;it&#93;), and the priest hath made it a perfume on the altar, on the wood, which &#91;is&#93; on the fire; it &#91;is&#93; a burnt-offering, a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah.</span>
