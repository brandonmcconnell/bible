<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:22; Leviticus 15:19; Job 25:4"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, saying, A woman when she giveth seed, and hath born a male, then she hath been unclean seven days, according to the days of separation for her sickness she is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Luke 1:59; John 7:22–23"> and in the eighth day is the flesh of his foreskin circumcised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:22–23; Leviticus 15:25–28; Haggai 2:13"> and thirty and three days she doth abide in the blood of her cleansing; against any holy thing she doth not come, and unto the sanctuary she doth not go in, till the fulness of the days of her cleansing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:14–15; Genesis 3:13; Leviticus 12:2"> 'And if a female she bear, then she hath been unclean two weeks, as in her separation; and sixty and six days she doth abide for the blood of her cleansing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:22; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> 'And in the fulness of the days of her cleansing for son or for daughter she doth bring in a lamb, a son of a year, for a burnt-offering, and a young pigeon or a turtle-dove for a sin-offering, unto the opening of the tent of meeting, unto the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 15:28–30; Hebrews 9:12–14; Leviticus 4:35"> and he hath brought it near before Jehovah, and hath made atonement for her, and she hath been cleansed from the fountain of her blood; this &#91;is&#93; the law of her who is bearing, in regard to a male or to a female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 1:14"> 'And if her hand find not the sufficiency of a sheep, then she hath taken two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, one for a burnt-offering, and one for a sin-offering, and the priest hath made atonement for her, and she hath been cleansed.'</span>
