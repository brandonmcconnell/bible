<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:4; Leviticus 23:37; Numbers 29:39"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, Appointed seasons of Jehovah, which ye proclaim, holy convocations, &#91;are&#93; these: they &#91;are&#93; My appointed seasons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 23:12; Exodus 35:2–3"> six days is work done, and in the seventh day &#91;is&#93; a sabbath of rest, a holy convocation; ye do no work; it &#91;is&#93; a sabbath to Jehovah in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Exodus 23:14; Leviticus 23:37"> 'These &#91;are&#93; appointed seasons of Jehovah, holy convocations, which ye proclaim in their appointed seasons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 28:16–17"> in the first month, on the fourteenth of the month, between the evenings, &#91;is&#93; the passover to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Deuteronomy 16:8; Exodus 12:15–16"> and on the fifteenth day of this month &#91;is&#93; the feast of unleavened things to Jehovah; seven days unleavened things ye do eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 28:18–25; Leviticus 23:8; Leviticus 23:21"> on the first day ye have a holy convocation, ye do no servile work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and ye have brought near a fire-offering to Jehovah seven days; in the seventh day &#91;is&#93; a holy convocation; ye do no servile work.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 16:9"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When ye come in unto the land which I am giving to you, and have reaped its harvest, and have brought in the sheaf, the beginning of your harvest unto the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 10:14; Leviticus 9:21"> then he hath waved the sheaf before Jehovah for your acceptance; on the morrow of the sabbath doth the priest wave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10–12; 1 Peter 1:19; Leviticus 1:10"> 'And ye have prepared in the day of your waving the sheaf a lamb, a perfect one, a son of a year, for a burnt-offering to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14–16; Joel 1:9; Numbers 28:10"> and its present two tenth deals of flour mixed with oil, a fire-offering to Jehovah, a sweet fragrance, and its drink-offering, wine, a fourth of the hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Genesis 4:4–5; Leviticus 3:17"> 'And bread and roasted corn and full ears ye do not eat until this self-same day, until your bringing in the offering of your God — a statute age-during to your generations, in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:8; Deuteronomy 16:9–10"> 'And ye have numbered to you from the morrow of the sabbath, from the day of your bringing in the sheaf of the wave-offering: they are seven perfect sabbaths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Numbers 28:26"> unto the morrow of the seventh sabbath ye do number fifty days, and ye have brought near a new present to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Exodus 34:26; Exodus 34:22"> out of your dwellings ye bring in bread of a wave-offering, two &#91;loaves&#93;, of two tenth deals of flour they are, &#91;with&#93; yeast they are baken, first -&#91;fruits&#93; to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 28:27–31; Leviticus 23:12–13; Malachi 1:13–14"> 'And ye have brought near, besides the bread, seven lambs, perfect ones, sons of a year, and one bullock, a son of the herd, and two rams; they are a burnt-offering to Jehovah, with their present and their libations, a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 28:30; Leviticus 4:23–28; Leviticus 7:11–18"> 'And ye have prepared one kid of the goats for a sin-offering, and two lambs, sons of a year, for a sacrifice of peace-offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Ephesians 2:14; Numbers 18:8–12"> and the priest hath waved them, besides the bread of the first -&#91;fruits&#93; — a wave-offering before Jehovah, besides the two lambs; they are holy to Jehovah for the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Numbers 18:23"> and ye have proclaimed on this self-same day: a holy convocation is to you, ye do no servile work — a statute age-during in all your dwellings, to your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:19–21; Leviticus 19:9–10; Isaiah 58:10"> 'And in your reaping the harvest of your land thou dost not complete the corner of thy field in thy reaping, and the gleaning of thy harvest thou dost not gather, to the poor and to the sojourner thou dost leave them; I Jehovah &#91;am&#93; your God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Leviticus 25:9"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, saying, In the seventh month, on the first of the month, ye have a sabbath, a memorial of shouting, a holy convocation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:21"> ye do no servile work, and ye have brought near a fire-offering to Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 16:29–31; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 16:24"> 'Only — on the tenth of this seventh month is a day of atonements; ye have a holy convocation, and ye have humbled yourselves, and have brought near a fire-offering to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 3:9; Leviticus 16:34; Hebrews 10:10"> and ye do no work in this self-same day, for it is a day of atonements, to make atonement for you, before Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 5:2; Leviticus 23:27"> 'For any person who is not humbled in this self-same day hath even been cut off from his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:3; Zephaniah 2:5; Jeremiah 15:7"> and any person who doth any work in this self-same day I have even destroyed that person from the midst of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> ye do no work — a statute age-during to your generations in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 16:31; Matthew 11:28–30; Psalms 69:10–11"> It &#91;is&#93; a sabbath of rest to you, and ye have humbled yourselves in the ninth of the month at even; from evening till evening ye do keep your sabbath.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 7:2; Nehemiah 8:14; Ezra 3:4"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, saying, In the fifteenth day of this seventh month &#91;is&#93; a feast of booths seven days to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24–25; Leviticus 23:7–8"> on the first day &#91;is&#93; a holy convocation, ye do no servile work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:18; John 7:37; 2 Chronicles 7:8–11"> seven days ye bring near a fire-offering to Jehovah, on the eighth day ye have a holy convocation, and ye have brought near a fire-offering to Jehovah; it &#91;is&#93; a restraint, ye do no servile work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Ecclesiastes 3:1"> 'These &#91;are&#93; appointed seasons of Jehovah, which ye proclaim holy convocations, to bring near a fire-offering to Jehovah, a burnt-offering, and a present, a sacrifice, and libations, a thing of a day in its day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Leviticus 19:3; Ezra 2:68–69"> apart from the sabbaths of Jehovah, and apart from your gifts, and apart from all your vows, and apart from all your willing-offerings, which ye give to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:13; Leviticus 23:34"> 'Only — in the fifteenth day of the seventh month, in your gathering the increase of the land, ye do keep the feast of Jehovah seven days; on the first day &#91;is&#93; a sabbath, and on the eighth day a sabbath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Revelation 7:9; Deuteronomy 16:14–15"> and ye have taken to yourselves on the first day the fruit of beautiful trees, branches of palms, and boughs of thick trees, and willows of a brook, and have rejoiced before Jehovah your God seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 29:12; Nehemiah 8:18"> 'And ye have kept it a feast to Jehovah, seven days in a year — a statute age-during to your generations; in the seventh month ye keep it a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Genesis 33:17; Numbers 24:5"> 'In booths ye dwell seven days; all who are natives in Israel dwell in booths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10–13; Psalms 78:5–6; Exodus 13:14"> so that your generations do know that in booths I caused the sons of Israel to dwell; in my bringing them out of the land of Egypt; I, Jehovah, &#91;am&#93; your God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 23:1–2; Matthew 18:20; Leviticus 21:24"> And Moses speaketh &#91;concerning&#93; the appointed seasons of Jehovah unto the sons of Israel.</span>
