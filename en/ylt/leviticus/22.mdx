<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 15:19; Leviticus 22:32"> 'Speak unto Aaron, and unto his sons, and they are separated from the holy things of the sons of Israel, and they pollute not My holy name in what they are hallowing to Me; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20–21; Numbers 19:13; Psalms 16:11"> 'Say unto them, To your generations, any man who draweth near, out of all your seed, unto the holy things which the sons of Israel do sanctify to Jehovah, and his uncleanness on him — even that person hath been cut off from before Me; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:39; Leviticus 15:2–3; Leviticus 15:13–16"> 'Any man of the seed of Aaron, and is leprous or hath an issue — of the holy things he doth not eat till that he is clean; and he who is coming against any uncleanness of a person, or a man whose seed of copulation goeth out from him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:43–44; Leviticus 15:7; Leviticus 15:19"> or a man who cometh against any teeming thing which is unclean to him, or against a man who is unclean to him, even any of his uncleanness —</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Hebrews 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> the person who cometh against it — hath even been unclean till the evening, and doth not eat of the holy things, but hath bathed his flesh with water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Deuteronomy 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> and the sun hath gone in, and he hath been clean, and afterwards he doth eat of the holy things, for it &#91;is&#93; his food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 17:15; Exodus 22:31; Ezekiel 44:31"> a carcase or torn thing he doth not eat, for uncleanness thereby; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Numbers 18:22; Numbers 18:32"> 'And they have kept My charge, and bear no sin for it, that they have died for it when they pollute it; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah sanctifying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; 1 Samuel 21:6; Exodus 29:33"> 'And no stranger doth eat of the holy thing; a settler of a priest and an hireling doth not eat of the holy thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:13; Exodus 12:44; Numbers 18:11–13"> and when a priest buyeth a person, the purchase of his money, he doth eat of it, also one born in his house; they do eat of his bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; Leviticus 21:3"> 'And a priest's daughter, when she is a strange man's, — she, of the heave-offering of the holy things doth not eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:14; Genesis 38:11; Numbers 18:11–19"> and a priest's daughter, when she is a widow, or cast out, and hath no seed, and hath turned back unto the house of her father, as &#91;in&#93; her youth, of her father's bread she doth eat; but no stranger doth eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 27:13; Leviticus 5:15–19"> 'And when a man doth eat of a holy thing through ignorance, then he hath added its fifth part to it, and hath given &#91;it&#93; to the priest, with the holy thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 18:32; Leviticus 19:8; Ezekiel 22:26"> and they do not pollute the holy things of the sons of Israel — that which they lift up to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> nor have caused them to bear the iniquity of the guilt-offering in their eating their holy things; for I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, sanctifying them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; Deuteronomy 12:6; Numbers 15:3"> 'Speak unto Aaron, and unto his sons, and unto all the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, Any man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourners in Israel, who bringeth near his offering, of all his vows, or of all his willing offerings which they bring near to Jehovah for a burnt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Leviticus 1:3; Ephesians 5:27"> at your pleasure a perfect one, a male of the herd, of the sheep or of the goats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 15:21"> nothing in which &#91;is&#93; blemish do ye bring near, for it is not for a pleasing thing for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 15:8; Numbers 15:3; Leviticus 3:6"> 'And when a man bringeth near a sacrifice of peace-offerings to Jehovah, to complete a vow, or for a willing-offering, of the herd or of the flock, it is perfect for a pleasing thing: no blemish is in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Malachi 1:8; Leviticus 1:9"> blind, or broken, or maimed, or having a wen, or scurvy, or scabbed — ye do not bring these near to Jehovah, and a fire-offering ye do not make of them on the altar to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:18"> 'As to an ox or a sheep enlarged or dwarfed — a willing-offering ye do make it, but for a vow it is not pleasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Deuteronomy 23:1; Leviticus 21:20"> As to a bruised, or beaten, or enlarged, or cut thing — ye do not bring &#91;it&#93; near to Jehovah; even in your land ye do not do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; Numbers 15:14–16"> And from the hand of a son of a stranger ye do not bring near the bread of your God, of any of these, for their corruption &#91;is&#93; in them; blemish &#91;is&#93; in them; they are not pleasing for you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:30; Leviticus 19:23–24; Leviticus 22:25"> 'When ox or lamb or goat is born, and it hath been seven days under its dam, then from the eighth day and henceforth, it is pleasing for an offering, a fire-offering to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:6–7; Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 34:26"> but an ox or sheep — it and its young one, ye do not slaughter in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; Psalms 107:22; Amos 4:5"> 'And when ye sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to Jehovah, at your pleasure ye do sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 7:15–18; Exodus 16:19–20; Leviticus 19:7"> on that day it is eaten, ye do not leave of it till morning; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Leviticus 19:37; Numbers 15:40"> and ye have kept my commands, and have done them; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Luke 11:2; Matthew 6:9"> and ye do not pollute My holy name, and I have been hallowed in the midst of the sons of Israel; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, sanctifying you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 25:38"> who am bringing you up out of the land of Egypt, to become your God; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.'</span>
