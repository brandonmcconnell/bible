<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> according to the work of the land of Egypt in which ye have dwelt ye do not, and according to the work of the land of Canaan whither I am bringing you in, ye do not, and in their statutes ye walk not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> 'My judgments ye do, and My statutes ye keep, to walk in them; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> and ye have kept My statutes and My judgments which man doth and liveth in them; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> 'None of you unto any relation of his flesh doth draw near to uncover nakedness; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> 'The nakedness of thy father and the nakedness of thy mother thou dost not uncover, she &#91;is&#93; thy mother; thou dost not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> 'The nakedness of the wife of thy father thou dost not uncover; it &#91;is&#93; the nakedness of thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> 'The nakedness of thy sister, daughter of thy father, or daughter of thy mother, born at home or born without; thou dost not uncover their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> 'The nakedness of thy son's daughter, or of thy daughter's daughter: thou dost not uncover their nakedness; for theirs &#91;is&#93; thy nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> 'The nakedness of a daughter of thy father's wife, begotten of thy father, she &#91;is&#93; thy sister; thou dost not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> 'The nakedness of a sister of thy father thou dost not uncover; she &#91;is&#93; a relation of thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> 'The nakedness of thy mother's sister thou dost not uncover; for she &#91;is&#93; thy mother's relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> 'The nakedness of thy father's brother thou dost not uncover; unto his wife thou dost not draw near; she &#91;is&#93; thine aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> 'The nakedness of thy daughter-in-law thou dost not uncover; she &#91;is&#93; thy son's wife; thou dost not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> 'The nakedness of thy brother's wife thou dost not uncover; it &#91;is&#93; thy brother's nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> 'The nakedness of a woman and her daughter thou dost not uncover; her son's daughter, and her daughter's daughter thou dost not take to uncover her nakedness; they &#91;are&#93; her relations; it &#91;is&#93; wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> 'And a woman unto another thou dost not take, to be an adversary, to uncover her nakedness beside her, in her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> 'And unto a woman in the separation of her uncleanness thou dost not draw near to uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> 'And unto the wife of thy fellow thou dost not give thy seed of copulation, for uncleanness with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> 'And of thy seed thou dost not give to pass over to the Molech; nor dost thou pollute the name of thy God; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> 'And with a male thou dost not lie as one lieth with a woman; abomination it &#91;is&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> 'And with any beast thou dost not give thy copulation, for uncleanness with it; and a woman doth not stand before a beast to lie down with it; confusion it &#91;is&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> 'Ye are not defiled with all these, for with all these have the nations been defiled which I am sending away from before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> and the land is defiled, and I charge its iniquity upon it, and the land vomiteth out its inhabitants:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> and ye — ye have kept My statutes and My judgments, and do not &#91;any&#93; of all these abominations, the native and the sojourner who is sojourning in your midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (for all these abominations have the men of the land done who &#91;are&#93; before you, and the land is defiled),</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> and the land doth not vomit you out in your defiling it, as it hath vomited out the nation which &#91;is&#93; before you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> for any one who doth &#91;any&#93; of all these abominations — even the persons who are doing &#91;so&#93;, have been cut off from the midst of their people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> and ye have kept My charge, so as not to do &#91;any&#93; of the abominable statutes which have been done before you, and ye do not defile yourselves with them; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah your God.'</span>
