<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:12; Leviticus 6:15; Acts 10:4"> and he hath brought it in unto the sons of Aaron, the priests, and he hath taken from thence the fulness of his hand of its flour and of its oil, besides all its frankincense, and the priest hath made perfume with its memorial on the altar, a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 10:12–13; Numbers 18:9; Leviticus 21:22"> and the remnant of the present &#91;is&#93; for Aaron and for his sons, most holy, of the fire-offerings of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Psalms 22:14"> 'And when thou bringest near an offering, a present baked in an oven, &#91;it is of&#93; unleavened cakes of flour mixed with oil, or thin unleavened cakes anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:21; Leviticus 7:9"> 'And if thine offering &#91;is&#93; a present &#91;made&#93; on the girdel, it is of flour, mixed with oil, unleavened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:1–19; Psalms 22:1–21; Mark 14:1–15"> divide thou it into parts, and thou hast poured on it oil; it &#91;is&#93; a present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; Leviticus 7:9"> 'And if thine offering &#91;is&#93; a present &#91;made&#93; on the frying-pan, of flour with oil it is made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and thou hast brought in the present which is made of these to Jehovah, and &#91;one&#93; hath brought it near unto the priest, and he hath brought it nigh unto the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Philippians 4:18; Leviticus 6:15"> and the priest hath lifted up from the present its memorial, and hath made perfume on the altar, a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3"> and the remnant of the present &#91;is&#93; for Aaron and for his sons, most holy, of the fire-offerings of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; Luke 12:1; Exodus 23:18"> No present which ye bring near to Jehovah is made fermented, for with any leaven or any honey ye perfume no fire-offering to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Genesis 23:10–11; Exodus 22:29"> 'An offering of first -&#91;fruits&#93; — ye bring them near to Jehovah, but on the altar they go not up, for sweet fragrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:24; Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6"> And every offering — thy present — with salt thou dost season, and thou dost not let the salt of the covenant of thy God cease from thy present; with all thine offerings thou dost bring near salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Malachi 1:11; Numbers 28:2"> 'And if thou bring near a present of first-ripe &#91;fruits&#93; to Jehovah, — of green ears, roasted with fire, beaten out &#91;corn&#93; of a fruitful field thou dost bring near the present of thy first-ripe &#91;fruits&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and thou hast put on it oil, and laid on it frankincense, it &#91;is&#93; a present;</span>
