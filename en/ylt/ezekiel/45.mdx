<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:29; Zechariah 14:20–21; Numbers 34:13"> And in your causing the land to fall in inheritance, ye lift up a heave-offering to Jehovah, a holy &#91;portion&#93; of the land: the length — five and twenty thousand &#91;is&#93; the length, and the breadth ten thousand; it &#91;is&#93; holy in all its border round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:16–20; Ezekiel 27:28"> There is of this for the sanctuary five hundred by five hundred, square, round about; and fifty cubits of suburb &#91;is&#93; to it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:10"> And by this measure thou dost measure: the length &#91;is&#93; five and twenty thousand, and the breadth ten thousand: and in it is the sanctuary, the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 40:45"> The holy &#91;portion&#93; of the land it &#91;is&#93;; for priests, ministrants of the sanctuary, it is, who are drawing near to serve Jehovah; and it hath been to them a place for houses, and a holy place for a sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:13; Nehemiah 10:38–39; Ezekiel 48:20"> 'And of the five and twenty thousand of length, and of the ten thousand of breadth, there is to the Levites, ministrants of the house, for them — for a possession — twenty chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:15–18; Ezekiel 48:30–35"> 'And of the possession of the city ye give five thousand of breadth, and of length five and twenty thousand, over-against the heave-offering of the holy &#91;portion&#93;: to all the house of Israel it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:21; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 46:16–18"> As to the prince, on this side, and on that side, of the heave-offering of the holy place, and of the possession of the city, at the front of the heave-offering of the holy place, and at the front of the possession of the city, from the west corner westward, and from the east corner eastward — and the length &#91;is&#93; over-against one of the portions from the west border unto the east border —</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–5; Ezekiel 46:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> of the land there is to him for a possession in Israel, and My princes do not oppress any more My people, and the land they give to the house of Israel according to their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 22:3; Ezekiel 44:6"> 'Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Enough to you — princes of Israel; violence and spoil turn aside, and judgment and righteousness do; lift up your exactions from off My people — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:4–6; Proverbs 16:11"> Just balances, and a just ephah, and a just bath — ye have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10"> The ephah and the bath is of one measure, for the bath to bear a tenth of the homer, and the ephah a tenth of the homer: according to the homer is its measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> And, the shekel &#91;is&#93; twenty gerah: twenty shekels, five and twenty shekels, fifteen shekels — is your maneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> 'This &#91;is&#93; the heave-offering that ye lift up; a sixth part of the ephah of a homer of wheat, also ye have given a sixth part of the ephah of a homer of barley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the portion of oil, the bath of oil, a tenth part of the bath out of the cor, a homer of ten baths — for ten baths &#91;are&#93; a homer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 6:30; Colossians 1:21"> and one lamb out of the flock, out of two hundred, out of the watered country of Israel, for a present, and for a burnt-offering, and for peace-offerings, to make atonement by them — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:14–15; Isaiah 16:1"> All the people of the land are at this heave-offering for the prince in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–12; 2 Chronicles 31:3; Isaiah 66:23"> And on the prince are the burnt-offerings, and the present, and the libation, in feasts, and in new moons, and in sabbaths, in all appointed times of the house of Israel: he doth make the sin-offering, and the present, and the burnt-offering, and the peace-offerings, to make atonement for the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Exodus 12:2; Ezekiel 43:22"> 'Thus said the Lord Jehovah: In the first &#91;month&#93;, in the first of the month, thou dost take a bullock, a son of the herd, a perfect one, and hast cleansed the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:20; Ezekiel 43:14; Leviticus 16:18–20"> and the priest hath taken of the blood of the sin offering, and hath put on the door-post of the house, and on the four corners of the border of the altar, and on the post of the gate of the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 19:12; Leviticus 4:27–35; Ezekiel 45:15"> And so thou dost do on the seventh of the month, because of each erring one, and because of the simple one — and ye have purified the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Numbers 9:2–14"> 'In the first &#91;month&#93;, in the fourteenth day of the month, ye have the passover, a feast of seven days, unleavened food is eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Matthew 20:28"> And the prince hath prepared on that day, for himself, and for all the people of the land, a bullock, a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 42:8; Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 23:1–2"> And the seven days of the feast he prepareth a burnt-offering to Jehovah, seven bullocks, and seven rams, perfect ones, daily seven days, and a sin-offering, a kid of the goats, daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 28:12–15"> And a present of an ephah for a bullock, and an ephah for a ram, he doth prepare, and of oil a hin for an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> In the seventh &#91;month&#93;, in the fifteenth day of the month, in the feast, he doth according to these things seven days; as the sin-offering so the burnt-offering, and as the present so also the oil.</span>
