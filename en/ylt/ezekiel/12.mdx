<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 4:12; Jeremiah 5:21; Psalms 78:40"> 'Son of man, in the midst of the rebellious house thou art dwelling, that have eyes to see, and they have not seen; ears they have to hear, and they have not heard; for a rebellious house &#91;are&#93; they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; 2 Timothy 2:25; Jeremiah 26:3"> And thou, son of man, make to thee vessels of removal, and remove by day before their eyes, and thou hast removed from thy place unto another place before their eyes, it may be they consider, for a rebellious house they &#91;are&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:4; Ezekiel 12:12; 2 Kings 25:4"> And thou hast brought forth thy vessels as vessels of removal by day before their eyes, and thou, thou dost go forth at even before their eyes, as the goings forth of a removal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:2–4"> Before their eyes dig for thee through the wall, and thou hast brought forth by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:3; Isaiah 8:18; Ezekiel 24:24"> Before their eyes on the shoulder thou dost bear, in the darkness thou dost bring forth, thy face thou dost cover, and thou dost not see the earth, for a type I have given thee to the house of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:18; Ezekiel 37:10; Ezekiel 37:7"> And I do so, as I have been commanded; my vessels I have brought forth as vessels of removal by day, and at even I have dug for me through the wall with the hand; in the darkness I have brought forth, on the shoulder I have borne away, before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, in the morning, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:12; Ezekiel 20:49"> 'Son of man, have they not said unto thee — the house of Israel — the rebellious house — What art thou doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 9:25; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 24:8"> say unto them, Thus said the Lord Jehovah: 'The prince &#91;is&#93; this burden in Jerusalem, and all the house of Israel who are in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:15; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 52:28–30"> Say: I &#91;am&#93; your type; as I have done so it is done to them, into a removal, into a captivity, they do go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4; Ezekiel 12:6"> As to the prince who &#91;is&#93; in their midst, on the shoulder he beareth in the darkness, and he goeth forth, through the wall they dig to bring forth by it, his face he covereth, that he may not look on the very surface of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:7; Hosea 7:12; Ezekiel 17:20"> And I have spread My net for him, and he hath been caught in My snare, and I have brought him in to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, and it he doth not see — and there doth he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; 2 Kings 25:4–5; Ezekiel 17:21"> 'And all who are round about him to help him, and all his bands, I do scatter to every wind, and a sword I draw out after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 12:20"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, in My scattering them among nations, and I have spread them through lands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Ezekiel 14:22–23; Ezekiel 6:8–10"> and I have left of them, a few in number, from the sword, from the famine, and from the pestilence, so that they recount all their abominations among the nations whither they have come, and they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 5:9; Psalms 80:5; Job 3:24"> 'Son of man, thy bread in haste thou dost eat, and thy water with trembling and with fear thou dost drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 6:6–7; Ezekiel 6:14"> and thou hast said unto the people of the land, Thus said the Lord Jehovah concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem, concerning the land of Israel: Their bread with fear they do eat, and their water with astonishment drink, because its land is desolate, because of its fulness, because of the violence of all who are dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 25:9"> And the cities that are inhabited are laid waste, and the land is a desolation, and ye have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:2–3; Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 12:27"> 'Son of man, what &#91;is&#93; this simile to you, concerning the land of Israel, saying, Prolonged are the days, and perished hath every vision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Ezekiel 7:5–7; Zephaniah 1:14"> therefore say unto them: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: I have caused this simile to cease, And they use it not as a simile again in Israel, But speak to them: Drawn near have the days, And spoken hath every vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:13–16; Ezekiel 13:23; Zechariah 13:2–4"> For there is no more any vain vision, and flattering divination, In the midst of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Ezekiel 12:28; Isaiah 55:11"> For I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, I speak, The word that I speak — it is done, It is not prolonged any more, For, in your days, O rebellious house, I speak a word, and I have done it, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 10:14; Ezekiel 12:22; 2 Peter 3:4"> 'Son of man, lo, the house of Israel are saying, The vision that he is seeing &#91;is&#93; for many days, and of times far off he is prophesying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:23–25; Mark 13:32–37; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> therefore say unto them: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: None of my words are prolonged any more, When I speak a word — it is done, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah!'</span>
