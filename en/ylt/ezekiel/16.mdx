<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 20:4; Isaiah 58:1"> 'Son of man, cause Jerusalem to know her abominations, and thou hast said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:45; Isaiah 51:1–2; Deuteronomy 20:17"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah to Jerusalem: Thy birth and thy nativity &#91;Are&#93; of the land of the Canaanite, Thy father the Amorite, and thy mother a Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 5:16–21"> As to thy nativity, in the day thou wast born, Thou — thy navel hath not been cut, And in water thou wast not washed for ease, And thou hast not been salted at all, And thou hast not been swaddled at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Genesis 21:10; Exodus 1:22"> No eye hath had pity on thee, to do to thee any of these, To have compassion on thee, And thou art cast on the face of the field, With loathing of thy person. In the day thou hast been born — thou!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; John 5:25; Titus 3:3–7"> And I do pass over by thee, And I see thee trodden down in thy blood, And I say to thee in thy blood, Live, And I say to thee in thy blood, Live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Exodus 1:7; Isaiah 62:3"> A myriad — as the shoot of the field I have made thee, And thou art multiplied, and art great, And comest in with an excellent adornment, Breasts have been formed, and thy hair hath grown — And thou, naked and bare!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 3:9; Jeremiah 2:2–3; Romans 5:8"> And I pass over by thee, and I see thee, And lo, thy time &#91;is&#93; a time of loves, And I spread My skirt over thee, And I cover thy nakedness, And I swear to thee, and come in to a covenant with thee, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, And thou dost become Mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ruth 3:3; 1 John 2:27"> And I do wash thee with water, And I wash away thy blood from off thee, And I anoint thee with perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18; Ezekiel 16:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And I clothe thee with embroidery, And I shoe thee with badger's skin, And I gird thee with fine linen, And I cover thee with figured silk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Isaiah 3:19; Genesis 41:42"> And I adorn thee with adornments, And I give bracelets for thy hands, And a chain for thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:21; Isaiah 28:5; Jeremiah 13:18"> And I give a ring for thy nose, And rings for thine ears, And a crown of beauty on thy head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 50:2; 1 Kings 4:21"> And thou dost put on gold and silver, And thy clothing &#91;is&#93; fine linen, And figured silk and embroidery, Fine flour, and honey, and oil thou hast eaten, And thou art very very beautiful, And dost go prosperously to the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 10:24; Lamentations 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> And go forth doth thy name among nations, Because of thy beauty — for it &#91;is&#93; complete, In My honour that I have set upon thee, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 57:8; Jeremiah 2:20; Ezekiel 16:25"> And thou dost trust in thy beauty, And goest a-whoring because of thy renown, And dost pour out thy whoredoms On every passer by — to him it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; Ezekiel 7:20; 2 Chronicles 28:24"> And thou dost take of thy garments, And dost make to thee spotted high-places, And dost go a-whoring upon them, They are not coming in — nor shall it be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–21; Jeremiah 3:9; Ezekiel 16:11"> And thou dost take thy beauteous vessels Of My gold and My silver that I gave to thee, And dost make to thee images of a male, And dost go a-whoring with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And dost take the garments of thy embroidery, And thou dost cover them, And My oil and My perfume thou hast set before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:13; Hosea 2:8–13; Genesis 8:21"> And My bread, that I gave to thee, Fine flour, and oil, and honey, that I caused thee to eat. Thou hast even set it before them, For a sweet fragrance — thus it is, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 20:31; Jeremiah 7:31"> And thou dost take thy sons and thy daughters Whom thou hast born to Me, And dost sacrifice them to them for food. Is it a little thing because of thy whoredoms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 18:10"> That thou dost slaughter My sons, And dost give them up in causing them to pass over to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 11:1; Ezekiel 16:43"> And with all thine abominations and thy whoredoms, Thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, When thou wast naked and bare, Trodden down in thy blood thou wast!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 12:12; Ezekiel 2:10; Ezekiel 24:6"> And it cometh to pass, after all thy wickedness, (Woe, woe, to thee — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 16:31"> That thou dost build to thee an arch, And dost make to thee a high place in every broad place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 3:2; Genesis 38:21"> At every head of the way thou hast built thy high place, And thou dost make thy beauty abominable, And dost open wide thy feet to every passer by, And dost multiply thy whoredoms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19–21; Ezekiel 20:7–8; Ezekiel 8:10"> And dost go a-whoring unto sons of Egypt, Thy neighbours — great of appetite! And thou dost multiply thy whoredoms, To provoke Me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:12; Ezekiel 16:57; Ezekiel 16:37"> And lo, I have stretched out My hand against thee, And I diminish thy portion, And give thee to the desire of those hating thee, The daughters of the Philistines, Who are ashamed of thy wicked way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:7; Hosea 10:6; 2 Kings 16:10–18"> And thou goest a-whoring unto sons of Asshur, Without thy being satisfied, And thou dost go a-whoring with them, And also — thou hast not been satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 21:9; Judges 2:12–19; Ezekiel 13:14–23"> And thou dost multiply thy whoredoms On the land of Canaan — toward Chaldea, And even with this thou hast not been satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 3:9"> How weak &#91;is&#93; thy heart, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, In thy doing all these, The work of a domineering whorish woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33–34; Isaiah 52:3; Ezekiel 16:39"> In thy building thine arch at the head of every way, Thy high place thou hast made in every broad place, And — hast not been as a whore deriding a gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Ezekiel 23:37; Jeremiah 2:25"> The wife who committeth adultery — Under her husband — doth receive strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:9; Hosea 8:9–10; Ezekiel 16:41"> To all whores they give a gift, And — thou hast given thy gifts to all thy lovers, And dost bribe them to come in unto thee, From round about — in thy whoredoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the contrary is in thee from women in thy whoredoms, That after thee none doth go a-whoring; And in thy giving a gift, And a gift hath not been given to thee; And thou art become contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 4:18; 1 Kings 22:19; John 4:10"> Therefore, O whore, hear a word of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:10; Ezekiel 24:13; Zephaniah 3:1"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Because of thy brass being poured forth, And thy nakedness is revealed in thy whoredoms near thy lovers, And near all the idols of thy abominations, And according to the blood of thy sons, Whom thou hast given to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 13:22; Hosea 2:3"> Therefore, lo, I am assembling all thy lovers, To whom thou hast been sweet, And all whom thou hast loved, Besides all whom thou hast hated; And I have assembled them by thee round about, And have revealed thy nakedness to them, And they have seen all thy nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Genesis 9:6; Zephaniah 1:17"> And I have judged thee — judgments of adulteresses, And of women shedding blood, And have given thee blood, fury, and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:26; Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 16:24–25"> And I have given thee into their hand, And they have thrown down thine arch, And they have broken down thy high places, And they have stript thee of thy garments, And they have taken thy beauteous vessels, And they have left thee naked and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Habakkuk 1:6–10; John 8:5–7"> And have caused an assembly to come up against thee, And stoned thee with stones, And thrust thee through with their swords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:48; Jeremiah 52:13; Jeremiah 39:8"> And burnt thy houses with fire, And done in thee judgments before the eyes of many women, And I have caused thee to cease from going a-whoring, And also a gift thou givest no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 40:1–2"> And I have caused My fury against thee to rest, And My jealousy hath turned aside from thee, And I have been quiet, and I am not angry any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Ezekiel 11:21; Psalms 78:42"> Because thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, And dost give trouble to Me in all these, Lo, even I also thy way at first gave up, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, And I did not this thought for all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:2–3; Psalms 106:35–38; 1 Samuel 24:13"> Lo, every one using a simile, Doth use a simile concerning thee, saying: As the mother — her daughter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Isaiah 1:4; Ezekiel 23:37–39"> Thy mother's daughter thou &#91;art&#93;, Loathing her husband and her sons, And thy sisters' sister thou &#91;art&#93;, Who loathed their husbands and their sons, Your mother &#91;is&#93; a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:4; Ezekiel 16:48–49; Ezekiel 16:53–56"> And thine elder sister &#91;is&#93; Samaria, she and her daughters, Who is dwelling at thy left hand, And thy younger sister, who is dwelling on thy right hand, &#91;is&#93; Sodom and her daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Kings 21:9; Ezekiel 16:51; Ezekiel 5:6–7"> And — in their ways thou hast not walked, And according to their abominations done, As a little thing it hath been loathed, And thou dost more corruptly than they in all thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Luke 10:12; Acts 7:52"> I live — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, Sodom thy sister hath not done — she and her daughters — As thou hast done — thou and thy daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Ezekiel 28:2; Genesis 13:10"> Lo, this hath been the iniquity of Sodom thy sister, Arrogancy, fulness of bread, and quiet ease, Have been to her and to her daughters, And the hand of the afflicted and needy She hath not strengthened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 19:24; Genesis 18:20; Genesis 19:5"> And they are haughty and do abomination before Me, And I turn them aside when I have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Luke 12:47–48; Romans 3:9–20"> As to Samaria, as the half of thy sins — she hath not sinned, And thou dost multiply thine abominations more than they, And dost justify thy sisters by all thy abominations that thou hast done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Ezekiel 36:15; Ezekiel 16:56"> Thou also — bear thy shame, That thou hast adjudged to thy sisters, Because of thy sins that thou hast done more abominably than they, They are more righteous than thou, And thou, also, be ashamed and bear thy shame, In thy justifying thy sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 12:16; Ezekiel 39:25"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; their captivity, The captivity of Sodom and her daughters, And the captivity of Samaria and her daughters, And the captivity of thy captives in their midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22–23; Jeremiah 2:26; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> So that thou dost bear thy shame, And hast been ashamed of all that thou hast done, In thy comforting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:11; Malachi 3:4; Ezekiel 16:53"> And thy sisters, Sodom and her daughters, Do turn back to their former state, And Samaria and her daughters Do turn back to their former state, And thou and thy daughters do turn back to your former state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 65:5; Luke 15:28–30; Zephaniah 3:11"> And thy sister Sodom hath not been for a report in thy mouth, In the day of thine arrogancy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Kings 16:5–7; Ezekiel 16:36–37; 2 Chronicles 28:5–6"> Before thy wickedness is revealed, As &#91;at&#93; the time of the reproach of the daughters of Aram, And of all her neighbours, the daughters of the Philistines, Who are despising thee round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Lamentations 5:7; Genesis 4:13"> Thy devices and thine abominations, Thou hast borne them, an affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5; Ezekiel 17:19; Deuteronomy 29:25"> For thus said the Lord Jehovah: I have dealt with thee as thou hast done, In that thou hast despised an oath — to break covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Hebrews 12:24; Ezekiel 16:8"> And I — I have remembered My covenant with thee, In the days of thy youth, And I have established for thee a covenant age-during.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Hebrews 8:13; Galatians 4:26–31; Isaiah 66:7–12"> And thou hast remembered thy ways, And thou hast been ashamed, In thy receiving thy sisters — Thine elder with thy younger, And I have given them to thee for daughters, And not by thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 39:22"> And I — I have established My covenant with thee, And thou hast known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 39:9; Romans 3:19; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> So that thou dost remember, And thou hast been ashamed, And there is not to thee any more an opening of the mouth because of thy shame, In My receiving atonement for thee, For all that thou hast done, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah!'</span>
