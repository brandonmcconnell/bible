<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2–3; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 41:23"> And he bringeth me in unto the temple, and he measureth the posts, six cubits the breadth on this side, and six cubits the breadth on that side — the breadth of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:17; 2 Chronicles 3:3; 1 Kings 6:2"> And the breadth of the opening &#91;is&#93; ten cubits; and the sides of the opening &#91;are&#93; five cubits on this side, and five cubits on that side; and he measureth its length forty cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16"> And he hath gone inward, and measureth the post of the opening two cubits, and the opening six cubits, and the breadth of the opening seven cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20; Hebrews 9:3–8; Exodus 26:33–34"> And he measureth its length twenty cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits, unto the front of the temple, and he saith unto me, 'This &#91;is&#93; the holy of holies.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 42:3–14; Ezekiel 41:6–9"> And he measureth the wall of the house six cubits, and the breadth of the side-chamber four cubits, all round the house round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:10; 1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 40:17"> And the side-chambers &#91;are&#93; side-chamber by side-chamber, three and thirty times; and they are entering into the wall — which the house hath for the side-chambers all round about — to be taken hold of, and they are not taken hold of by the wall of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:8; Hebrews 6:1; Matthew 13:32"> And a broad place and a turning place still upwards &#91;are&#93; to the side-chambers, for the turning round of the house &#91;is&#93; still upwards all round about the house: therefore the breadth of the house &#91;is&#93; upwards, and so the lower one goeth up unto the higher by the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Revelation 21:16"> And I have looked at the house, the height all round about: the foundations of the side-chambers &#91;are&#93; the fulness of the reed, six cubits by the joining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:11; Ezekiel 42:4; Ezekiel 42:1"> The breadth of the wall that &#91;is&#93; to the side-chamber at the outside &#91;is&#93; five cubits; and that which is left &#91;is&#93; the place of the side-chambers that &#91;are&#93; to the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:17"> And between the chambers &#91;is&#93; a breadth of twenty cubits round about the house, all round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:9; Ezekiel 42:4"> And the opening of the side-chamber &#91;is&#93; to the place left, one opening northward, and one opening southward, and the breadth of the place that is left &#91;is&#93; five cubits all round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> As to the building that &#91;is&#93; at the front of the separate place &#91;at&#93; the corner westward, the breadth &#91;is&#93; seventy cubits, and the wall of the building five cubits broad all round about, and its length ninety cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> And he hath measured the house, the length &#91;is&#93; a hundred cubits; and the separate place, and the building, and its walls, the length &#91;is&#93; a hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the breadth of the front of the house, and of the separate place eastward, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:3; Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 41:12"> And he hath measured the length of the building unto the front of the separate place that &#91;is&#93; at its hinder part, and its galleries on this side and on that side, a hundred cubits, and the inner temple and the porches of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16"> the thresholds, and the narrow windows, and the galleries round about them three, over-against the threshold, a ceiling of wood all round about, and the ground unto the windows and the covered windows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:15"> over above the opening, and unto the inner-house, and at the outside, and by all the wall all round about within and without &#91;by&#93; measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> And it is made &#91;with&#93; cherubs and palm-trees, and a palm-tree &#91;is&#93; between cherub and cherub, and two faces &#91;are&#93; to the cherub;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10"> and the face of man &#91;is&#93; unto the palm-tree on this side, and the face of a young lion unto the palm-tree on that side; it is made unto all the house all round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> from the earth unto above the opening &#91;are&#93; the cherubs and the palm-trees made, and &#91;on&#93; the wall of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> Of the temple the side post &#91;is&#93; square, and of the front of the sanctuary, the appearance &#91;is&#93; as the appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; Ezekiel 23:41; Malachi 1:7"> Of the altar, the wood &#91;is&#93; three cubits in height, and its length two cubits; and its corners &#91;are&#93; to it, and its length, and its walls &#91;are&#93; of wood, and he speaketh unto me, 'This &#91;is&#93; the table that &#91;is&#93; before Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 6:31–35; 2 Chronicles 4:22; Ezekiel 41:1"> And two doors &#91;are&#93; to the temple and to the sanctuary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; Ezekiel 40:48"> and two leaves &#91;are&#93; to the doors, two turning leaves &#91;are&#93; to the doors, two to the one door, and two leaves to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> And made on them, on the doors of the temple, &#91;are&#93; cherubs and palm-trees as are made on the walls, and a thickness of wood &#91;is&#93; at the front of the porch on the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:16; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:5"> And narrow windows and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; on this side, and on that side, at the sides of the porch, and the side-chambers of the house, and the thick places.</span>
