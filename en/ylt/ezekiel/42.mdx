<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13"> At the front of the length &#91;is&#93; a hundred cubits &#91;at&#93; the north opening, and the breadth fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:15–16; Ezekiel 41:10; Song of Solomon 7:5"> Over-against the twenty &#91;cubits&#93; that are to the inner court, and over-against the pavement that &#91;is&#93; to the outer court, &#91;is&#93; gallery over-against gallery, in the three &#91;storeys&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Luke 13:24; Matthew 7:14"> And at the front of the chambers &#91;is&#93; a walk of ten cubits in breadth unto the inner part, a way of one cubit, and their openings &#91;are&#93; at the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:7"> And the upper chambers &#91;are&#93; short, for the galleries contain more than these, than the lower, and than the middle one, of the building;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6; 1 Kings 6:8"> for they &#91;are&#93; threefold, and they have no pillars as the pillars of the court, therefore it hath been kept back — more than the lower and than the middle one — from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:12; Ezekiel 42:10"> As to the wall that &#91;is&#93; at the outside, over-against the chambers, the way of the outer-court at the front of the chambers, its length &#91;is&#93; fifty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13–14"> for the length of the chambers that &#91;are&#93; to the outer court &#91;is&#93; fifty cubits, and of those on the front of the temple a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Ezekiel 44:5"> And under these chambers &#91;is&#93; the entrance from the east, in one's going into them from the outer court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:12–15"> In the breadth of the wall of the court eastward, unto the front of the separate place, and unto the front of the building, &#91;are&#93; chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:4"> And the way before them &#91;is&#93; as the appearance of the chambers that &#91;are&#93; northward, according to their length so &#91;is&#93; their breadth, and all their outlets, and according to their fashions, and according to their openings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And according to the openings of the chambers that &#91;are&#93; southward &#91;is&#93; an opening at the head of the way, the way directly in the front of the wall eastward in entering them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17; Leviticus 7:6; Ezekiel 40:46"> And he saith unto me, 'The north chambers, the south chambers, that &#91;are&#93; at the front of the separate place, they &#91;are&#93; holy chambers, where the priests (who &#91;are&#93; near to Jehovah) eat the most holy things, there they place the most holy things, and the present, and the sin-offering, and the guilt-offering, for the place &#91;is&#93; holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> In the priests' going in, they come not out from the sanctuary unto the outer court, and there they place their garments with which they minister, for they &#91;are&#93; holy, and have put on other garments, and have drawn near unto that which &#91;is&#93; for the people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 41:15; Ezekiel 40:6–16"> And he hath finished the measurements of the inner house, and hath brought me forth the way of the gate whose front &#91;is&#93; eastward, and he hath measured it all round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Revelation 11:1–2; Zechariah 2:1"> He hath measured the east side with the measuring-reed, five hundred reeds, with the measuring-reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He hath measured the north side, five hundred reeds, with the measuring reed round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The south side he hath measured, five hundred reeds, with the measuring-reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He hath turned round unto the west side, he hath measured five hundred reeds with the measuring-reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:2; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 40:5"> At the four sides he hath measured it, a wall &#91;is&#93; to it all round about, the length five hundred, and the breadth five hundred, to separate between the holy and the profane place.</span>
