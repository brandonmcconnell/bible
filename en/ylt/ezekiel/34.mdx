<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Jeremiah 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> 'Son of man, prophesy concerning shepherds of Israel, prophesy, and thou hast said unto them: To the shepherds, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Woe &#91;to&#93; the shepherds of Israel, Who have been feeding themselves! The flock do not the shepherds feed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 11:16; Jeremiah 2:30; Ezekiel 33:25–26"> The fat ye do eat, and the wool ye put on, The fed one ye slaughter, the flock ye feed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 18:12–13; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:16"> The weak ye have not strengthened, And the sick one ye have not healed, And the broken ye have not bound up, And the driven away have not brought back, And the lost ye have not sought, And with might ye have ruled them and with rigour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; 2 Chronicles 18:16; Jeremiah 23:2"> And they are scattered from want of a shepherd, And are for food to every beast of the field, Yea, they are scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 142:4; 1 Peter 2:25; John 10:16"> Go astray do My flock on all the mountains, And on every high hill, And on all the face of the land have My flock been scattered, And there is none inquiring, and none seeking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:2–3; Micah 3:8–9; Psalms 82:1–7"> Therefore, shepherds, hear a word of Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Acts 20:33; Ezekiel 34:5–6"> I live — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, If not, because of My flock being for a prey, Yea, My flock is for food to every beast of the field, Because there is no shepherd, And My shepherds have not sought My flock, And the shepherds do feed themselves, And My flock they have not fed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore, O shepherds, hear a word of Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Zechariah 10:3; Ezekiel 13:8"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I &#91;am&#93; against the shepherds, And have required My flock from their hand, And caused them to cease from feeding the flock, And no more do the shepherds feed themselves, And I have delivered My flock from their mouth, And they are not to them for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:16; Luke 19:10; Jeremiah 23:3"> For thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I — even I, have required My flock, And I have sought it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; John 10:11–12; Luke 19:10"> As a shepherd's searching of his drove, In the day of his being in the midst of his scattered flock, so I do seek My flock, And have delivered them out of all places, Whither they have been scattered, In a day of cloud and thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25–26; Ezekiel 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> And brought them out from the peoples, And have gathered them from the lands, And brought them unto their own ground, And have fed them on mountains of Israel, By streams, and by all dwellings of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:9; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 31:8–10"> With good pasture I do feed them, And on mountains of the high place of Israel is their habitation, There do they lie down in a good habitation, And fat pastures they enjoy on mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Psalms 23:1–2; John 21:15"> I feed My flock, and cause them to lie down, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> The lost I seek, and the driven away bring back, And the broken I bind up, and the sick I strengthen, And the fat and the strong I destroy, I feed it with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20–22; Matthew 25:32–33; Zechariah 10:3"> And you, My flock, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I am judging between sheep and sheep, Between rams and he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 11:52; Numbers 16:13"> Is it a little thing for you — the good pasture ye enjoy, And the remnant of your pasture ye tread down with your feet, And a depth of waters ye do drink, And the remainder with your feet ye trample,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And My flock the trodden thing of your feet consumeth, And the trampled thing of your feet drinketh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; Ezekiel 34:10; Psalms 22:12–16"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah to them: Lo, I — even I, have judged between fat sheep and lean sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 11:5; Deuteronomy 33:17; Luke 13:14–16"> Because with side and with shoulder ye thrust away, And with your horns push all the diseased, Till ye have scattered them to the out-place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:2–3; Psalms 72:12–14; Ezekiel 34:10"> And I have given safety to My flock, And they are not any more for prey, And I have judged between sheep and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> And have raised up over them one shepherd, And he hath fed them — my servant David, He doth feed them, and he is their shepherd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And I, Jehovah, I am their God, And My servant David prince in their midst, I, Jehovah, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Ezekiel 37:26; Leviticus 26:6"> And I have made for them a covenant of peace, And caused evil beasts to cease out of the land, And they have dwelt in a wilderness confidently, And they have slept in forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; Leviticus 26:4"> And I have given them, and the suburbs of my hill, a blessing, And caused the shower to come down in its season, Showers of blessing they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Leviticus 26:4; Leviticus 26:13"> And given hath the tree of the field its fruit, And the land doth give her increase, And they have been on their land confident, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, In My breaking the bands of their yoke, And I have delivered them from the hand of those laying service on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27; Jeremiah 30:10; Ezekiel 39:26"> And they are no more a prey to nations, And the beast of the earth devoureth them not, And they have dwelt confidently, And there is none troubling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; Ezekiel 34:26–27"> And I have raised for them a plant for renown, And they are no more consumed by hunger in the land, And they bear no more the shame of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; Ezekiel 34:24; Psalms 46:11"> And they have known that I, Jehovah, their God, &#91;am&#93; with them, And they — the house of Israel — My people, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:16; Psalms 78:52"> And ye, My flock, the flock of My pasture, Men ye &#91;are&#93; — I &#91;am&#93; your God, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah!'</span>
