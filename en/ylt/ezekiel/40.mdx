<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 3:14"> In the twenty and fifth year of our removal, in the beginning of the year, in the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after that the city was smitten, in this self-same day hath a hand of Jehovah been upon me, and He bringeth me in thither;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> in visions of God He hath brought me in unto the land of Israel, and causeth me to rest on a very high mountain, and upon it &#91;is&#93; as the frame of a city on the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> And He bringeth me in thither, and lo, a man, his appearance as the appearance of brass, and a thread of flax in his hand, and a measuring-reed, and he is standing at the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> and the man speaketh unto me: 'Son of man, see with thine eyes, And with thine ears hear, And set thy heart to all that I am shewing thee, For, in order to shew &#91;it&#93; thee, Thou hast been brought in hither, Declare all that thou art seeing to the house of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And lo, a wall on the outside of the house all round about, and in the hand of the man a measuring-reed, six cubits by a cubit and a handbreadth, and he measureth the breadth of the building one reed, and the height one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 11:1"> And he cometh in unto the gate whose front &#91;is&#93; eastward, and he goeth up by its steps, and he measureth the threshold of the gate one reed broad, even the one threshold one reed broad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:33; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> and the little chamber one reed long and one reed broad, and between the little chambers five cubits, and the threshold of the gate, from the side of the porch of the gate from within, one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he measureth the porch of the gate from within one reed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:19"> and he measureth the porch of the gate eight cubits, and its posts two cubits, and the porch of the gates from within,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and the little chambers of the gate eastward, three on this side, and three on that side; one measure &#91;is&#93; to them three, and one measure &#91;is&#93; to the posts, on this side and on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measureth the breadth of the opening of the gate ten cubits, the length of the gate thirteen cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and a border before the little chambers, one cubit, and one cubit &#91;is&#93; the border on this side, and the little chamber &#91;is&#93; six cubits on this side, and six cubits on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he measureth the gate from the roof of the &#91;one&#93; little chamber to the roof of another; the breadth twenty and five cubits, opening over-against opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; Ezekiel 42:1"> And he maketh the posts of sixty cubits, even unto the post of the court, the gate all round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and by the front of the gate of the entrance, by the front of the porch of the inner gate, fifty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:5; 1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 41:26"> and narrow windows &#91;are&#93; unto the little chambers, and unto their posts at the inside of the gate all round about — and so to the arches — and windows all round about &#91;are&#93; at the inside, and at the post &#91;are&#93; palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Ezekiel 42:1; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And he bringeth me in unto the outer court, and lo, chambers and a pavement made for the court all round about — thirty chambers on the pavement —</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the pavement unto the side of the gates over-against the length of the gates &#91;is&#93; the lower pavement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27; Ezekiel 46:1–2"> and he measureth the breadth from before the lower gate, to the front of the inner court, on the outside, a hundred cubits, eastward and northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:6"> As to the gate of the outer court whose front &#91;is&#93; northward, he hath measured its length and its breadth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29–30; Ezekiel 40:25–26; Ezekiel 40:10–16"> and its little chambers, three on this side, and three on that side, and its posts and its arches have been according to the measure of the first gate, fifty cubits its length, and the breadth five and twenty by the cubit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37; Ezekiel 40:6"> and its windows, and its arches, and its palm-trees &#91;are&#93; according to the measure of the gate whose face &#91;is&#93; eastward, and by seven steps they go up on it, and its arches &#91;are&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:27–28; Exodus 38:9–12"> And the gate of the inner court &#91;is&#93; over-against the gate at the north and at the east; and he measureth from gate unto gate, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 40:6; Ezekiel 40:20–21"> And he causeth me to go southward, and lo, a gate southward, and he hath measured its posts and its arches according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:33; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:21–22"> and windows &#91;are&#93; to it and to its arches all round about, like these windows, fifty cubits the length, and the breadth five and twenty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:6"> and seven steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent, and its arches &#91;are&#93; before them, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; to it, one on this side, and one on that side, at its posts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 40:19"> and the gate of the inner court &#91;is&#93; southward, and he measureth from gate unto gate southward, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:32"> And he bringeth me in unto the inner court by the south gate, and he measureth the south gate according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:7"> and its little chambers, and its posts, and its arches &#91;are&#93; according to these measures, and windows &#91;are&#93; to it and to its arches all round about; fifty cubits the length, and the breadth twenty and five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> As to the arches all round about, the length &#91;is&#93; five and twenty cubits, and the breadth five cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:26; Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:22"> and its arches &#91;are&#93; unto the outer court, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; unto its posts, and eight steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:28–31"> And he bringeth me in unto the inner court eastward, and he measureth the gate according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> and its little chambers, and its posts, and its arches &#91;are&#93; according to these measures: and windows &#91;are&#93; to it and to its arches all round about, the length fifty cubits, and the breadth five and twenty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37"> and its arches &#91;are&#93; toward the outer court, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; toward its posts, on this side and on that side, and eight steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:2; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 44:4"> And he bringeth me in unto the north gate, and hath measured according to these measures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29; Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:16"> its little chambers, its posts, and its arches; and windows &#91;are&#93; to it all round about: the length fifty cubits, and the breadth five and twenty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:31"> and its posts &#91;are&#93; to the outer court, and palm-trees &#91;are&#93; unto its posts, on this side and on that side, and eight steps &#91;are&#93; its ascent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:6; Ezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> And the chamber and its opening &#91;is&#93; by the posts of the gates, there they purge the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> And in the porch of the gate &#91;are&#93; two tables on this side, and two tables on that side, to slaughter on them the burnt-offering, and the sin-offering, and the guilt-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and at the side without, at the going up to the opening of the north gate, &#91;are&#93; two tables; and at the other side that &#91;is&#93; at the porch of the gate, &#91;are&#93; two tables;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39–40"> four tables &#91;are&#93; on this side, and four tables on that side, at the side of the gate, eight tables on which they slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> And the four tables for burnt-offering &#91;are&#93; of hewn stone: the length one cubit and a half, and the breadth one cubit and a half, and the height one cubit: on them they place the instruments with which they slaughter the burnt-offering and the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> And the boundaries &#91;are&#93; one handbreadth, prepared within all round about: and on the tables &#91;is&#93; the flesh of the offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–32; 1 Chronicles 16:41–43; Ezekiel 40:27"> And on the outside of the inner gate &#91;are&#93; chambers of the singers, in the inner court, that &#91;are&#93; at the side of the north gate, and their fronts &#91;are&#93; southward, one at the side of the east gate &#91;hath&#93; the front northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:23; Psalms 134:1; Leviticus 8:35"> And he speaketh unto me: 'This chamber, whose front &#91;is&#93; southward, &#91;is&#93; for priests keeping charge of the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:15; Ezekiel 43:19; 1 Kings 2:35"> and the chamber, whose front &#91;is&#93; northward, &#91;is&#93; for priests keeping charge of the altar: they &#91;are&#93; sons of Zadok, who are drawing near of the sons of Levi unto Jehovah, to serve Him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27"> And he measureth the court: the length a hundred cubits, and the breadth a hundred cubits, square, and the altar &#91;is&#93; before the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> And he bringeth me in unto the porch of the house, and he measureth the post of the porch, five cubits on this side, and five cubits on that side, and the breadth of the gate, three cubits on this side, and three cubits on that side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–21; Ezekiel 40:34; 2 Chronicles 3:17"> the length of the porch twenty cubits, and the breadth eleven cubits; and by the steps whereby they go up unto it: and pillars &#91;are&#93; at the posts, one on this side, and one on that side.</span>
