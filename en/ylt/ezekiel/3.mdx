<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Ezekiel 2:8–9"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, that which thou findest eat, eat this roll, and go, speak unto the house of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:17; Acts 26:19"> And I open my mouth, and He causeth me to eat this roll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Revelation 10:9–10; Psalms 119:103"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, thy belly thou dost feed, and thy bowels thou dost fill with this roll that I am giving unto thee;' and I eat it, and it is in my mouth as honey for sweetness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Acts 1:8; Ezekiel 2:3"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, go, enter in unto the house of Israel, and thou hast spoken with My words unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Isaiah 33:19; Jonah 3:2–4"> For, not unto a people deep of lip and heavy of tongue &#91;art&#93; thou sent — unto the house of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 11:20–24; Romans 9:30–33; Jonah 3:5–10"> not unto many peoples, deep of lip and heavy of tongue, whose words thou dost not understand. If I had not sent thee unto them — they, they do hearken unto thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Ezekiel 2:4; 1 Samuel 8:7"> but the house of Israel are not willing to hearken unto thee, for they are not willing to hearken unto Me, for all the house of Israel are brazen-faced and strong-hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Acts 7:51–56; Micah 3:8"> 'Lo, I have made thy face strong against their face, and thy forehead strong against their forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 50:7"> As an adamant harder than a rock I have made thy forehead; thou dost not fear them, nor art thou affrighted before them, for a rebellious house &#91;are&#93; they.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 22:22; Ezekiel 3:1–3"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, all My words, that I speak unto thee, receive with thy heart, and with thine ears hear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 33:30"> and go, enter in unto the Removed, unto the sons of thy people, and thou hast spoken unto them, and hast said unto them: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: whether they hear, or whether they forbear.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 11:1"> And lift me up doth a spirit, and I hear behind me a noise, a great rushing — 'Blessed &#91;is&#93; the honour of Jehovah from His place!' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Ezekiel 10:16–17; Ezekiel 10:5"> even a noise of the wings of the living creatures touching one another, and a noise of the wheels over-against them, even a noise of a great rushing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1; Jeremiah 20:7–9; 2 Kings 3:15"> And a spirit hath lifted me up, and doth take me away, and I go bitterly, in the heat of my spirit, and the hand of Jehovah on me &#91;is&#93; strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 137:1; Job 2:13; Genesis 50:10"> And I come in unto the Removed, at Tel-Ahib, who are dwelling at the river Chebar, and where they are dwelling I also dwell seven days, causing astonishment in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:7"> And it cometh to pass, at the end of seven days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Hebrews 13:17; 2 Chronicles 19:10"> that there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying, 'Son of man, a watchman I have given thee to the house of Israel, and thou hast heard from My mouth a word, and hast warned them from Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:6; James 5:19–20; Ezekiel 3:20"> In My saying to the wicked: Thou dost surely die; and thou hast not warned him, nor hast spoken to warn the wicked from his wicked way, so that he doth live; he — the wicked — in his iniquity dieth, and his blood from thy hand I require.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:9; Ezekiel 14:20; 1 Timothy 4:16"> And thou, because thou hast warned the wicked, and he hath not turned back from his wickedness, and from his wicked way, he in his iniquity dieth, and thou thy soul hast delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 33:12–13"> 'And in the turning back of the righteous from his righteousness, and he hath done perversity, and I have put a stumbling-block before him, he dieth; because thou hast not warned him, in his sin he dieth, and not remembered is his righteousness that he hath done, and his blood from thy hand I require.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; Proverbs 9:9; Titus 2:15"> And thou, because thou hast warned him — the righteous — that the righteous sin not, and he hath not sinned, he surely liveth, because he hath been warned; and thou thy soul hast delivered.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Ezekiel 37:1; Ezekiel 8:4"> And there is on me there a hand of Jehovah, and He saith to me, 'Rise, go forth to the valley, and there I do speak with thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Acts 7:55; Revelation 1:17"> And I rise and go forth unto the valley, and lo, there the honour of Jehovah is standing as the honour that I had seen by the river Chebar, and I fall on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:19; Daniel 10:8–10"> And come into me doth a spirit, and causeth me to stand on my feet, and He speaketh with me, and saith unto me, 'Go in, be shut up in the midst of thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:8; Acts 9:16; Mark 3:21"> 'And thou, son of man, lo, they have put on thee thick bands, and have bound thee with them, and thou goest not forth in their midst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:27; Micah 3:6–7; Amos 8:11–12"> and thy tongue I cause to cleave unto thy palate, and thou hast been dumb, and art not to them for a reprover, for a rebellious house &#91;are&#93; they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2–3; Ezekiel 24:27; Ezekiel 3:11"> And in My speaking with thee, I do open thy mouth, and thou hast said unto them: Thus said the Lord Jehovah; the hearer doth hear, and the forbearer doth forbear; for a rebellious house &#91;are&#93; they.</span>
