<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 37:22"> And thou, son of man, prophesy unto mountains of Israel, and thou hast said, O mountains of Israel, hear a word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Ezekiel 25:3; Ezekiel 35:10"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Because the enemy said against you, Aha, and the high places of old for a possession have been to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremiah 18:16"> therefore, prophesy, and thou hast said: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Because, even because, of desolating, And of swallowing you up from round about, For your being a possession to the remnant of the nations, And ye are taken up on the tip of the tongue, And &#91;are&#93; an evil report of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Ezekiel 36:6"> Therefore, O mountains of Israel, Hear a word of the Lord Jehovah: Thus said the Lord Jehovah, to mountains, and to hills, To streams, and to valleys, And to wastes that &#91;are&#93; desolate, And to cities that are forsaken, That have been for a prey, And for a scorn, to the remnant of the nations who &#91;are&#93; round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:8; Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 137:7"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Have I not, in the fire of My jealousy, Spoken against the remnant of the nations, And against Edom — all of it, Who gave My land to themselves for a possession, With the joy of the whole heart — with despite of soul, For the sake of casting it out for a prey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 123:3–4; Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 74:23"> Therefore, prophesy concerning the ground of Israel, And thou hast said to mountains, and to hills, To streams, and to valleys, Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I, in My jealousy, and in My fury, I have spoken, Because the shame of nations ye have borne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Ezekiel 20:15"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah: I — I have lifted up My hand, Do not — the nations who &#91;are&#93; with you from round about — they their own shame bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 34:26–29"> And ye, O mountains of Israel, Your branch ye give out, and your fruits ye bear for My people Israel, For they have drawn near to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:11; Hosea 2:21–23; Ezekiel 36:34"> For, lo, I &#91;am&#93; for you, and have turned to you, And ye have been tilled and sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:33; Jeremiah 33:12; Ezekiel 36:37"> And I have multiplied on you men, All the house of Israel — all of it, And the cities have been inhabited, And the wastes are built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:12; Jeremiah 23:5–8; Jeremiah 31:27"> And I have multiplied on you man and beast, And they have multiplied and been fruitful, And I have caused you to dwell according to your former states, And I have done better than at your beginnings, And ye have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Ezekiel 36:13; Jeremiah 32:44"> And I have caused man to walk over you, — My people Israel, And they possess thee, and thou hast been to them for an inheritance, And thou dost add no more to bereave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Because they are saying to you: A devourer of men &#91;art&#93; thou, And a bereaver of thy nations thou hast been,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> Therefore, man thou devourest no more, And thy nations thou causest not to stumble any more, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 89:50; Ezekiel 36:6"> And I proclaim not unto thee any more the shame of the nations, And the reproach of peoples thou bearest no more, And thy nations stumble not any more, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Micah 2:10"> 'Son of man, The house of Israel are dwelling on their land, And they defile it by their way and by their doings, As the uncleanness of a separated one hath their way been before Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Ezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> And I do pour out My fury upon them For the blood that they shed on the land, And with their idols they have defiled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:12"> And I scatter them among nations, And they are spread through lands, According to their way, and according to their doings, I have judged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Jeremiah 33:24"> And one goeth in unto the nations whither they have gone, And they pollute My holy name by saying to them, The people of Jehovah &#91;are&#93; these, And from His land they have gone forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Psalms 74:18; Ezekiel 20:9"> And I have pity on My holy name, That the house of Israel have polluted among nations whither they have gone in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Ezekiel 36:32; Psalms 115:1–2"> Therefore, say to the house of Israel, Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Not for your sake am I working, O house of Israel, But — for My holy name, That ye have polluted among nations whither ye have gone in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; Ezekiel 39:7"> And I have sanctified My great name, That is profaned among nations, That ye have polluted in your midst, And known have the nations that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, In My being sanctified in you before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:21; Ezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> And I have taken you out of the nations, And have gathered you out of all the lands, And I have brought you in unto your land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremiah 33:8"> And I have sprinkled over you clean water, And ye have been clean; From all your uncleannesses, and from all your idols, I do cleanse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Psalms 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> And I have given to you a new heart, And a new spirit I give in your midst, And I have turned aside the heart of stone out of your flesh, And I have given to you a heart of flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> And My Spirit I give in your midst, And I have done this, so that in My statutes ye walk, And My judgments ye keep, and have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27"> And ye have dwelt in the land that I have given to your fathers, And ye have been to Me for a people, And I — I am to you for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Hosea 2:21–23"> And I have saved you from all your uncleannesses, And I have called unto the corn, and multiplied it, And I have put no famine upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> And I have multiplied the fruit of the tree, And the increase of the field, So that ye receive not any more a reproach of famine among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> And ye have remembered your ways that &#91;are&#93; evil, And your doings that &#91;are&#93; not good, And have been loathsome in your own faces, For your iniquities, and for your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Ezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> Not for your sake am I working, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, Be it known to you, Be ashamed and confounded, because of your ways, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:10; Jeremiah 33:10"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: In the day of My cleansing you from all your iniquities, I have caused the cities to be inhabited, And the wastes have been built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Jeremiah 25:9–11; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> And the desolate land is tilled, Instead of which it was a desolation before the eyes of every passer by,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremiah 33:9"> And they have said: This land, that was desolated, Hath been as the garden of Eden, And the cities — the wasted, And the desolated, and the broken down, Fenced places have remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Ezekiel 17:24"> And known have the nations who are left round about you, That I Jehovah have built the thrown down, I have planted the desolated: I Jehovah have spoken, and I have done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Ezekiel 14:3"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Yet this I am required, By the house of Israel to do to them, I multiply them as a flock of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> As a flock of holy ones, as a flock of Jerusalem, In her appointed seasons, So are the waste cities full of flocks of men, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah!'</span>
