<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Ezekiel 28:12"> 'And thou, son of man, lift up concerning Tyre a lamentation, and thou hast said to Tyre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:4; Isaiah 23:11; Isaiah 23:2–3"> O dweller on the entrances of the sea, Merchant of the peoples unto many isles, Thus said the Lord Jehovah: O Tyre, thou — thou hast said: I &#91;am&#93; the perfection of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:5"> In the heart of the seas &#91;are&#93; thy borders, Thy builders have perfected thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; Song of Solomon 4:8"> Of firs of Senir they have built to thee all thy double-boarded ships, Of cedars of Lebanon they have taken to make a mast for thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; Zechariah 11:2"> Of oaks of Bashan they made thine oars, Thy bench they have made of ivory, A branch of Ashurim from isles of Chittim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> Of fine linen with embroidery from Egypt hath been thy sail, To be to thee for an ensign, Of blue and purple from isles of Elishah hath been thy covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; 1 Kings 9:27; Ezekiel 27:11"> Inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad have been rowers to thee, Thy wise men, O Tyre, have been in thee, They &#91;are&#93; thy pilots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> Elders of Gebal and its wise men have been in thee, Strengthening thy breach; All ships of the sea and their mariners, Have been in thee, to trade &#91;with&#93; thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:5; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> Persian and Lud and Phut Have been in thy forces — thy men of war. Shield and helmet they hung up in thee, They — they have given out thine honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:3–4"> The sons of Arvad, and thy force, &#91;Are&#93; on thy walls round about, And short swordsmen in thy towers have been, Their shields they have hung up on thy walls round about, They — they have perfected thy beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; Ezekiel 38:13"> Tarshish &#91;is&#93; thy merchant, Because of the abundance of all wealth, For silver, iron, tin, and lead, They have given out thy remnants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> Javan, Tubal, and Meshech — they &#91;are&#93; thy merchants, For persons of men, and vessels of brass, They have given out thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 38:6; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> They of the house of Togarmah, &#91;For&#93; horses, and riding steeds, and mules, They have given out thy remnants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; 1 Kings 10:22"> Sons of Dedan &#91;are&#93; thy merchants, Many isles &#91;are&#93; the mart of thy hand, Horns of ivory and ebony they sent back thy reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Aram &#91;is&#93; thy merchant, Because of the abundance of thy works, For emerald, purple, and embroidery, And fine linen, and coral, and agate, They have given out thy remnants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> Judah and the land of Israel — they &#91;are&#93; thy merchants, For wheat of Minnith, and Pannag, And honey, and oil, and balm, They have given out thy merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> Damascus &#91;is&#93; thy merchant, For the abundance of thy works, Because of the abundance of all wealth, For wine of Helbon, and white wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> Vedan and Javan go about with thy remnants, They have given shining iron, cassia, and cane, In thy merchandise it hath been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Ezekiel 27:15"> Dedan &#91;is&#93; thy merchant, For clothes of freedom for riding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; Genesis 25:13; 1 Kings 10:15"> Arabia, and all princes of Kedar, They &#91;are&#93; the traders of thy hand, For lambs, and rams, and he-goats, In these thy merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> Merchants of Sheba and Raamah — they &#91;are&#93; thy merchants, For the chief of all spices, And for every precious stone, and gold, They have given out thy remnants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Haran, and Canneh, and Eden, merchants of Sheba, Asshur — Chilmad — &#91;are&#93; thy merchants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8"> They &#91;are&#93; thy merchants for perfect things, For wrappings of blue, and embroidery, And for treasuries of rich apparel, With cords bound and girded, for thy merchandise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> Ships of Tarshish &#91;are&#93; thy double walls of thy merchandise, And thou art filled and honoured greatly, In the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> Into great waters have they brought thee, Those rowing thee, The east wind hath broken thee in the heart of the seas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Ezekiel 27:24; Ezekiel 27:12"> Thy wealth and thy remnants, Thy merchandise, thy mariners, And thy pilots, strengtheners of thy breach, And the traders of thy merchandise, And all thy men of war, who &#91;are&#93; in thee, And in all thine assembly that &#91;is&#93; in thy midst, Fall into the heart of the seas in the day of thy fall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Ezekiel 31:16"> At the voice of the cry of thy pilots shake do the suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> And come down from their ships have all handling an oar, Mariners, all the pilots of the sea, on the land they stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> And have sounded for thee with their voice, And cry bitterly, and cause dust to go up on their heads, In ashes they do roll themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> And they have made for thee baldness, And they have girded on sackcloth, And they have wept for thee, In bitterness of soul — a bitter mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> And lifted up for thee have their sons a lamentation, And they have lamented over thee, who &#91;is&#93; as Tyre? As the cut-off one in the midst of the sea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 28:16"> With the outgoing of thy remnants from the seas, Thou hast filled many peoples, With the abundance of thy riches, and thy merchandise, Thou hast made rich things of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> The time of &#91;thy&#93; being broken by the seas in the depths of the waters, Thy merchandise and all thy assembly in thy midst have fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 26:15–18"> All inhabitants of the isles have been astonished at thee, And their kings have been sore afraid, They have been troubled in countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Zephaniah 2:15"> Merchants among the peoples have shrieked for thee, Wastes thou hast been, and thou art not — to the age!'</span>
</div>
