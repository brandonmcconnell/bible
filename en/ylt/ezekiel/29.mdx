<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth &#91;month&#93;, in the twelfth of the month, hath a word of Jehovah been unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> 'Son of man, set thy face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy concerning him, and concerning Egypt — all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak, and thou hast said: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I &#91;am&#93; against thee, Pharaoh king of Egypt! The great dragon that is crouching in the midst of his floods, Who hath said, My flood &#91;is&#93; my own, And I — I have made it &#91;for&#93; myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> And I have put hooks in thy jaws, And I have caused the fish of thy floods to cleave to thy scales, And I have caused thee to come up from the midst of thy floods, And every fish of thy floods to thy scales doth cleave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> And I have left thee in the wilderness, Thou and every fish of thy floods, On the face of the field thou dost fall, Thou art not gathered nor assembled, To the beast of the earth and to the fowl of the heavens I have given thee for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> And known have all inhabitants of Egypt That I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, Because of their being a staff of reed to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> In their taking hold of thee by thy hand, — thou art crushed, And hast rent to them all the shoulder, And in their leaning on thee thou art broken, And hast caused all their thighs to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I am bringing in against thee a sword, And have cut off from thee man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> And the land of Egypt hath been for a desolation and a waste, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah. Because he said: The flood &#91;is&#93; mine, and I made &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> Therefore, lo, I &#91;am&#93; against thee, and against thy floods, And have given the land of Egypt for wastes, A waste, a desolation, from Migdol to Syene, And unto the border of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> Not pass over into it doth a foot of man, Yea, the foot of beast doth not pass into it, Nor is it inhabited forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I have made the land of Egypt a desolation, In the midst of desolate lands, And its cities, in the midst of waste cities, Are a desolation forty years, And I have scattered the Egyptians among nations, And I have dispersed them through lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> But thus said the Lord Jehovah: At the end of forty years I gather the Egyptians Out of the peoples whither they have been scattered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> And I have turned back &#91;to&#93; the captivity of Egypt, And I have brought them back &#91;To&#93; the land of Pathros, to the land of their birth, And they have been there a low kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> Of the kingdoms it is lowest, And it lifteth not up itself any more above the nations, And I have made them few, So as not to rule among nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And it is no more to the house of Israel for a confidence, Bringing iniquity to remembrance, By their turning after them, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; the Lord Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it cometh to pass, in the twenty and seventh year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, in the first of the month, hath a word of Jehovah been unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> 'Son of man, Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, Hath caused his force to serve a great service against Tyre, Every head &#91;is&#93; bald — every shoulder peeled, And reward he had none, nor his force, out of Tyre, For the service that he served against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah, Lo, I am giving to Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon the land of Egypt, And he hath taken away its store, And hath taken its spoil, and taken its prey, And it hath been a reward to his force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> His wage for which he laboured I have given to him, The land of Egypt — in that they wrought for Me, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> In that day I cause to shoot up a horn to the house of Israel, And to thee I give an opening of the mouth in their midst, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah!'</span>
