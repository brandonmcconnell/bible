<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:22; Ezekiel 1:3; Acts 8:39"> There hath been upon me a hand of Jehovah, and He taketh me forth in the Spirit of Jehovah, and doth place me in the midst of the valley, and it is full of bones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 141:7; Ezekiel 37:11; Deuteronomy 11:30"> and He causeth me to pass over by them, all round about, and lo, very many &#91;are&#93; on the face of the valley, and lo, very dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:21; Deuteronomy 32:39"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, do these bones live?' And I say, 'O Lord Jehovah, Thou — Thou hast known.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 5:25; John 5:28–29; Matthew 21:21"> And He saith unto me, 'Prophesy concerning these bones, and thou hast said unto them: O dry bones, hear a word of Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:14; Psalms 104:29–30; Genesis 2:7"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah to these bones: Lo, I am bringing into you a spirit, and ye have lived,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Isaiah 49:23; Joel 3:17"> and I have given on you sinews, and cause flesh to come up upon you, and covered you over with skin, and given in you a spirit, and ye have lived, and ye have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:2; 1 Kings 19:11–13; Jeremiah 13:5–7"> And I have prophesied as I have been commanded, and there is a noise, as I am prophesying, and lo, a rushing, and draw near do the bones, bone unto its bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I beheld, and lo, on them &#91;are&#93; sinews, and flesh hath come up, and cover them doth skin over above — and spirit there is none in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:8; Song of Solomon 4:16; Psalms 104:30"> And He saith unto me: 'Prophesy unto the Spirit, prophesy, son of man, and thou hast said unto the Spirit: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: From the four winds come in, O Spirit, and breathe on these slain, and they do live.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:11; Psalms 104:30; Revelation 20:4–5"> And I have prophesied as He commanded me, and the Spirit cometh into them, and they live, and stand on their feet — a very very great force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1; Psalms 77:7–9; Ezekiel 39:25"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel; lo, they are saying: Dried up have our bones, And perished hath our hope, We have been cut off by ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Hosea 13:14; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> Therefore, prophesy, and thou hast said unto them, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I am opening your graves, And have brought you up out of your graves, O My people, And brought you in unto the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:6; Ezekiel 16:62; Psalms 126:2–3"> And ye have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, In My opening your graves, And in My bringing you up out of your graves, O My people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Joel 2:28–29; Ezekiel 36:36"> And I have given My Spirit in you, and ye have lived, And I have caused you to rest on your land, And ye have known that I Jehovah, I have spoken, and I have done &#91;it&#93;, An affirmation of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 10:19; 2 Chronicles 10:17"> 'And thou, son of man, take to thee one stick, and write on it, For Judah, and for the sons of Israel, his companions; and take another stick, and write on it, For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and all the house of Israel, his companions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 1:11; Jeremiah 50:4; Isaiah 11:13"> and bring them near one unto another, to thee, for one stick, and they have become one in thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 12:9; Ezekiel 17:12"> 'And when sons of thy people speak unto thee, saying, Dost thou not declare to us what these &#91;are&#93; to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Ezekiel 37:16–17; Ephesians 2:13–14"> Speak unto them, Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I am taking the stick of Joseph, that &#91;is&#93; in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel his companions, and have given them unto him, with the stick of Judah, and have made them become one stick, and they have been one in My hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3; Numbers 17:6–9; Hosea 12:10"> And the sticks on which thou writest have been in thy hand before thine eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:24; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:8"> and speak thou unto them: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I am taking the sons of Israel, From among the nations whither they have gone, And have gathered them from round about, And I have brought them in unto their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Jeremiah 3:18"> And I have made them become one nation in the land, on mountains of Israel, And one king is to them all for king, And they are no more as two nations, Nor are they divided any more into two kingdoms again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Ezekiel 36:28–29; 1 John 1:9"> Nor are they defiled any more with their idols, And with their abominations, And with any of their transgressions, And I have saved them out of all their dwellings, In which they have sinned, And I have cleansed them, And they have been to Me for a people, And I — I am to them for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–24; Hosea 3:5; Ezekiel 37:25"> And My servant David &#91;is&#93; king over them, And one shepherd have they all, And in My judgments they go, And My statutes they keep, and have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Amos 9:15; Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 28:25"> And they have dwelt on the land that I gave to My servant, to Jacob, In which your fathers have dwelt, And they have dwelt on it, they and their sons, And their son's sons — unto the age, And David My servant &#91;is&#93; their prince — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 30:19; Ezekiel 34:25"> And I have made to them a covenant of peace, A covenant age-during it is with them, And I have placed them, and multiplied them, And placed My sanctuary in their midst — to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; John 1:14; Ezekiel 37:23"> And My tabernacle hath been over them, And I have been to them for God, And they have been to Me for a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17–19; Ezekiel 20:12; Exodus 31:13"> And known have the nations that I Jehovah am sanctifying Israel, In My sanctuary being in their midst — to the age!'</span>
