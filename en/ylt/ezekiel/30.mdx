<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6; Ezekiel 21:12; Joel 1:11"> 'Son of man, prophesy, and thou hast said: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Howl ye, ha! for the day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 1:15"> For near &#91;is&#93; a day, near &#91;is&#93; a day to Jehovah! A day of clouds, the time of nations it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 29:8; Isaiah 16:7"> And come in hath a sword to Egypt, And there hath been great pain in Cush, In the falling of the wounded in Egypt, And they have taken its store, And broken down have been its foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 27:10; Jeremiah 25:24"> Cush, and Phut, and Lud, and all the mixture, and Chub, And the sons of the land of the covenant with them by sword do fall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:10; Isaiah 20:3–6; Isaiah 31:3"> Thus said Jehovah: And — fallen have supporters of Egypt, And come down hath the arrogance of her strength, From Migdol to Syene, by sword they fall in her, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:12; Jeremiah 25:18–26; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> And they have been desolated in the midst of desolate lands, And its cities are in the midst of wasted cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:6; Ezekiel 29:16; Amos 1:7"> And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, In My giving fire against Egypt, And broken have been all her helpers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1–2; Isaiah 23:5; Ezekiel 32:9–10"> In that day go forth do messengers from before Me in ships, To trouble confident Cush, And there hath been great pain among them, As the day of Egypt, for lo, it hath come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 32:11–16; Ezekiel 29:4–5"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: I have caused the multitude of Egypt to cease, By the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 32:12; Ezekiel 39:11–20"> He and his people with him — the terrible of nations, Are brought in to destroy the land, And they have drawn their swords against Egypt, And have filled the land &#91;with&#93; the wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Nahum 1:4; Revelation 16:12"> And I have made floods a dry place, And I have sold the land into the hand of evil doers, And I have made desolate the land, And its fulness, by the hand of strangers, I, Jehovah, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 2:16"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: And — I have destroyed idols, And caused vain things to cease from Noph, And a prince of the land of Egypt there is no more, And I give fear in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 46:25; Psalms 78:43"> And I have made Pathros desolate, And I have given fire against Zoan, And I have done judgments in No,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 16:1; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 11:6"> And I have poured out My fury on Sin, the stronghold of Egypt, And I have cut off the multitude of No.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:8–9; Ezekiel 28:18"> And I have given fire against Egypt, Greatly pained is Sin, and No is to be rent, And Noph hath daily distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 41:45"> The youths of Aven and Pi-Beseth by sword do fall, And these into captivity do go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:3; Ezekiel 29:15; Isaiah 10:27"> And in Tehaphnehes hath the day been dark, In My breaking there the yokes of Egypt, And ceased in her hath the excellency of her strength, She — a cloud doth cover her, And her daughters into captivity do go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Revelation 17:1; Ezekiel 5:8"> And I have done judgments in Egypt, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 29:1"> And it cometh to pass, in the eleventh year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, in the seventh of the month, hath a word of Jehovah been unto me, saying: 'Son of man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:13; Jeremiah 46:11; Psalms 37:17"> The arm of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, I have broken, And lo, it hath not been bound up to give healing, To put a bandage to bind it, To strengthen it — to lay hold on the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 37:17; Jeremiah 37:7; 2 Kings 24:7"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I &#91;am&#93; against Pharaoh, king of Egypt, And I have broken his arms, The strong one and the broken one, And have caused the sword to fall out of his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:26; Ezekiel 30:17–18; Ezekiel 29:12–13"> And scattered the Egyptians among nations, And I have spread them through lands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:25; Zephaniah 2:12; Isaiah 45:5"> And strengthened the arms of the king of Babylon, And I have given My sword into his hand, And I have broken the arms of Pharaoh, And he hath groaned the groans of a pierced one — before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:21; Ezekiel 32:15; Ezekiel 29:16"> And I have strengthened the arms of the king of Babylon, And the arms of Pharaoh do fall down, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, In My giving My sword into the hand of the king of Babylon, And he hath stretched it out toward the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:42; Ezekiel 29:12; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I have scattered the Egyptians among nations, And I have spread them through lands, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah!'</span>
