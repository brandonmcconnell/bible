<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:24; Numbers 11:25–26; Ezekiel 3:12"> And there doth come into me a spirit, when He hath spoken unto me, and it causeth me to stand on my feet, and I hear Him who is speaking unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; 1 Samuel 8:7–8; Deuteronomy 9:24"> And He saith unto Me, 'Son of man, I am sending thee unto the sons of Israel, unto nations who are rebels, who have rebelled against Me; they and their fathers have transgressed against Me, unto this self-same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:7; Isaiah 48:4; Jeremiah 5:3"> And the sons &#91;are&#93; brazen-faced and hard-hearted to whom I am sending thee, and thou hast said unto them: Thus said the Lord Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:33; Ezekiel 3:27; Matthew 10:12–15"> and they — whether they hear, or whether they forbear, for a rebellious house they &#91;are&#93; — have known that a prophet hath been in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 51:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> 'And thou, son of man, thou art not afraid of them, yea, of their words thou art not afraid, for briers and thorns are with thee, and near scorpions thou art dwelling, of their words thou art not afraid, and of their faces thou art not affrighted, for they &#91;are&#93; a rebellious house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Ezekiel 3:17; Jeremiah 1:7"> And thou hast spoken My words unto them, whether they hear or whether they forbear, for they &#91;are&#93; rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:9; Jeremiah 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> 'And thou, son of man, hear that which I am speaking unto thee: Thou art not rebellious like the rebellious house, open thy mouth, and eat that which I am giving unto thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Revelation 5:1–5; Daniel 10:10"> And I look, and lo, a hand &#91;is&#93; sent forth unto me, and lo, in it a roll of a book,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Isaiah 3:11; Revelation 9:12"> and He spreadeth it before me, and it is written in front and behind, and written on it &#91;are&#93; lamentations, and mourning, and woe!</span>
