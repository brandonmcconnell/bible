<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:1; Ezekiel 42:15; Ezekiel 46:1"> And he causeth me to go to the gate, the gate that is looking eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 18:1; Ezekiel 11:23; Isaiah 6:3"> And lo, the honour of the God of Israel hath come from the way of the east, and His voice &#91;is&#93; as the noise of many waters, and the earth hath shone from His honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:5; Ezekiel 9:1; Jeremiah 1:10"> And according to the appearance &#91;is&#93; the appearance that I saw, as the appearance that I saw in my coming in to destroy the city, and the appearances &#91;are&#93; as the appearance that I saw at the river Chebar, and I fall on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:18–19; Ezekiel 44:2; Ezekiel 43:2"> And the honour of Jehovah hath come in unto the house, the way of the gate whose face &#91;is&#93; eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:2–4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Ezekiel 8:3"> And take me up doth the Spirit, and bringeth me in unto the inner court, and lo, the honour of Jehovah hath filled the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Isaiah 66:6; Revelation 16:1"> And I hear one speaking unto me from the house, and a man hath been standing near me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:9; Psalms 47:8; Leviticus 26:30"> and He saith unto me: 'Son of man, the place of My throne, And the place of the soles of My feet, Where I dwell in the midst of the sons of Israel to the age, Defile no more do the house of Israel My holy name, They, and their kings, by their whoredom, And by the carcases of their kings — their high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:3–16; 2 Chronicles 33:7"> In their putting their threshold with My threshold, And their door-post near My door-post, And the wall between Me and them, And they have defiled My holy name, By their abominations that they have done, And I consume them in Mine anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Ezekiel 37:26–28; Ezekiel 18:30–31"> Now do they put far off their whoredom, And the carcases of their kings — from Me, And I have dwelt in their midst to the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 16:61; Ezekiel 16:63"> Thou, son of man, Shew the house of Israel the house, And they are ashamed of their iniquities, And they have measured the measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:20; Matthew 28:20"> And since they have been ashamed of all that they have done, The form of the house, and its measurement, And its outlets, and its inlets, and all its forms, And all its statutes, even all its forms, And all its laws cause them to know, And write &#91;it&#93; before their eyes, And they observe all its forms, And all its statutes, and have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2; Revelation 21:27; Psalms 93:5"> This &#91;is&#93; a law of the house: on the top of the mountain, all its border all round about &#91;is&#93; most holy; lo, this &#91;is&#93; a law of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 41:8"> 'And these &#91;are&#93; measures of the altar by cubits: The cubit &#91;is&#93; a cubit and a handbreadth, and the centre &#91;is&#93; a cubit, and a cubit the breadth; and its border on its edge round about &#91;is&#93; one span, and this &#91;is&#93; the upper part of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And from the centre of the ground unto the lower border &#91;is&#93; two cubits, and the breadth one cubit, and from the lesser border unto the greater border four cubits, and the breadth a cubit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Psalms 118:27; Leviticus 9:9"> 'And the altar &#91;is&#93; four cubits, and from the altar and upward &#91;are&#93; four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1; 2 Chronicles 4:1; Exodus 38:1–2"> And the altar &#91;is&#93; twelve long by twelve broad, square in its four squares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 20:26; Ezekiel 40:6; Exodus 25:25"> And the border &#91;is&#93; fourteen long by fourteen broad, at its four squares, and the border round about it &#91;is&#93; half a cubit, and the centre to it &#91;is&#93; a cubit round about, and its steps are looking eastward.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; Hebrews 10:4–12; Ezekiel 2:1"> And He saith unto me, 'Son of man, Thus said the Lord Jehovah: These &#91;are&#93; statutes of the altar in the day of its being made to cause burnt-offering to go up on it, and to sprinkle on it blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:15; Numbers 16:40"> And thou hast given unto the priests, the Levites, who &#91;are&#93; of the seed of Zadok — who are near unto Me, an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, to serve Me — a calf from the herd, for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 16:19; Leviticus 8:15; Exodus 29:12"> And thou hast taken of its blood, and hast put it on its four horns, and on the four corners of its border, and on the border round about, and hast cleansed it, and purified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 8:17"> And thou hast taken the bullock of the sin-offering, and hast burnt it in the appointed place of the house at the outside of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:25–26; Ezekiel 43:20; 1 Peter 1:19"> And on the second day thou dost bring near a kid of the goats, a perfect one, for a sin-offering, and they have cleansed the altar, as they cleansed &#91;it&#93; for the bullock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 29:1"> In thy finishing cleansing, thou dost bring near a calf, a son of the herd, a perfect one, and a ram out of the flock, a perfect one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50; Numbers 18:19"> And thou hast brought them near before Jehovah, and the priests have cast upon them salt, and have caused them to go up, a burnt-offering to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:33; Exodus 29:35–37; Leviticus 8:35"> Seven days thou dost prepare a goat for a sin-offering daily, and a bullock, a son of the herd, and a ram out of the flock, perfect ones, do they prepare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 8:34; Exodus 32:29"> Seven days they purify the altar, and have cleansed it, and filled their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 9:1; Ezekiel 20:40–41; 1 Peter 2:5"> And the days are completed, and it hath come to pass on the eighth day, and henceforth, the priests prepare on the altar your burnt-offerings and your peace-offerings, and I have accepted you — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah.'</span>
