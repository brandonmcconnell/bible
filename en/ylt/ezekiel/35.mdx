<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> 'Son of man, set thy face against mount Seir, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> and thou hast said to it: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, I &#91;am&#93; against thee, O mount Seir, And have stretched out My hand against thee, And made thee a desolation and an astonishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> Thy cities a waste I make, and thou art a desolation, And thou hast known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because of thy having an enmity age-during, And thou dost saw the sons of Israel, By the hands of the sword, In the time of their calamity, In the time of the iniquity of the end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> Therefore, I live — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, Surely for blood I do appoint thee, And blood doth pursue thee, If blood thou hast not hated, Blood also doth pursue thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> And I have given mount Seir for a desolation and an astonishment, And have cut off from it him who is passing over and him who is returning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> And filled his mountains with his wounded, Thy hills, and thy valleys, and all thy streams, The pierced of the sword fall into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> Desolations age-during I make thee, And thy cities do not return, And ye have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalms 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> Because of thy saying: The two nations and the two lands are mine, and we have possessed it, And Jehovah hath been there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalms 73:17–18"> Therefore, I live — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, And I have done according to thine anger, And according to thine envy, With which thou hast wrought, Because of thy hatred against them, And I have been known among them when I judge thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalms 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> And thou hast known that I — Jehovah, I have heard all thy despisings that thou hast spoken Against mountains of Israel, saying: A desolation, to us they were given for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> And ye magnify yourselves against Me with your mouth, And have made abundant against Me your words, I — I have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: According to the rejoicing of the whole land, A desolation I make of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> According to thy joy at the inheritance of the house of Israel because of desolation, So I do to thee — a desolation thou art, O mount Seir, and all Edom — all of it, And they have known that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah!</span>
