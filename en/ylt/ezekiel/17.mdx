<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> 'Son of man, put forth a riddle, and use a simile unto the house of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> and thou hast said: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: The great eagle, great-winged, long-pinioned, Full of feathers, that hath diverse colours, Hath come in unto Lebanon, And it taketh the foliage of the cedar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> The top of its tender twigs it hath cropped, And it bringeth it in to the land of Canaan. In a city of merchants it hath placed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> And it taketh of the seed of the land, And doth put it in a field of seed, To take by many waters, In a conspicuous place it hath set it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> And it springeth up, and becometh a spreading vine, humble of stature, To turn its thin shoots toward itself, And its roots are under it, And it becometh a vine, and maketh boughs, And sendeth forth beauteous branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> And there is another great eagle, Great-winged, and abounding with feathers, And lo, this vine hath bent its roots toward him, And its thin shoots it hath sent out toward him, To water it from the furrows of its planting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> On a good field, by many waters, it is planted, To make branches, and to bear fruit, To be for an goodly vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> Say: Thus said the Lord Jehovah: It prospereth — its roots doth he not draw out, And its fruit cut off, and it is withered? &#91;In&#93; all the leaves of its springing it withereth, And not by great strength, and by a numerous people, To lift it up by its roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> And lo, the planted thing — doth it prosper? When come against it doth the east wind, Doth it not utterly wither? On the furrows of its springing it withereth.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And there is a word of Jehovah unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> 'Say, I pray thee, to the rebellious house, Have ye not known what these &#91;are&#93;? Say, Lo, come hath the king of Babylon to Jerusalem, And he taketh its king, and its princes, And bringeth them to himself to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> And he taketh of the seed of the kingdom, And maketh with him a covenant, And bringeth him in to an oath, And the mighty of the land he hath taken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> That the kingdom may be humble, That it may not lift itself up, To keep his covenant — that it may stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> And he rebelleth against him, To send his messengers to Egypt, To give to him horses, and much people, Doth he prosper? doth he escape who is doing these things? And hath he broken covenant and escaped?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> I live — an affirmation of the Lord Jehovah, Doth he not — in the place of the king who is causing him to reign, Whose oath he hath despised, And whose covenant he hath broken, With him — in the midst of Babylon — die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And not with a great force, and with a numerous assembly, Doth Pharaoh maintain him in battle, By pouring out a mount, and in building a fortification, To cut off many souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> And he despised the oath — to break covenant, And lo, he hath given his hand, And all these he hath done, he escapeth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah: I live — Mine oath that he hath despised, And My covenant that he hath broken, Have I not put it on his head?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> And I have spread out for him My snare, And he hath been caught in My net, And I have brought him in to Babylon, And pleaded with him there his trespass, That he hath trespassed against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> And all his fugitives, with all his bands, By sword do fall, and those remaining, To every wind they are spread out, And ye have known that I, Jehovah, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> Thus said the Lord Jehovah: I have taken of the foliage of the high cedar, And I have set &#91;it&#93;, From the top of its tender shoots a tender one I crop, And I — I have planted &#91;it&#93; on a mountain high and lofty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> In a mountain — the high place of Israel, I plant it, And it hath borne boughs, and yielded fruit, And become a goodly cedar, And dwelt under it have all birds of every wing, In the shade of its thin shoots they dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> And known have all trees of the field That I, Jehovah, have made low the high tree, I have set on high the low tree, I have dried up the moist tree, And I have caused the dry tree to flourish, I, Jehovah, have spoken, and have done &#91;it&#93;!'</span>
