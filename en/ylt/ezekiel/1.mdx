<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 3:16; Ezekiel 11:24; Acts 7:56"> And it cometh to pass, in the thirtieth year, in the fourth &#91;month&#93;, in the fifth of the month, and I &#91;am&#93; in the midst of the Removed by the river Chebar, the heavens have been opened, and I see visions of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–15; Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the fifth of the month — it is the fifth year of the removal of the king Jehoiachin —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 3:22; 2 Kings 3:15"> hath the word of Jehovah certainly been unto Ezekiel son of Buzi the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans, by the river Chebar, and there is on him there a hand of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:27; Ezekiel 8:2; Jeremiah 23:19"> And I look, and lo, a tempestuous wind is coming from the north, a great cloud, and fire catching itself, and brightness to it round about, and out of its midst as the colour of copper, out of the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:6–8; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 6:6"> And out of its midst &#91;is&#93; a likeness of four living creatures, and this &#91;is&#93; their appearance; a likeness of man &#91;is&#93; to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7–8; Ezekiel 1:15"> and four faces &#91;are&#93; to each, and four wings &#91;are&#93; to each of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:15; Daniel 10:6; Psalms 104:4"> and their feet &#91;are&#93; straight feet, and the sole of their feet &#91;is&#93; as a sole of a calf's foot, and they are sparkling as the colour of bright brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:21; Ezekiel 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> and hands of man under their wings — on their four sides, and their faces and their wings — &#91;are&#93; to them four;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:11–12"> joining one unto another &#91;are&#93; their wings, they turn not round in their going, each straight forward they go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> As to the likeness of their faces, the face of a man, and the face of a lion, toward the right &#91;are&#93; to them four, and the face of an ox on the left &#91;are&#93; to them four, and the face of an eagle &#91;are&#93; to them four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; Ezekiel 1:23; Ezekiel 10:19"> And their faces and their wings are separate from above, to each &#91;are&#93; two joining together, and two are covering their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 10:22; Hebrews 1:14"> And each straight forward they go, whither the spirit is to go, they go, they turn not round in their going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Psalms 104:4; Matthew 28:3"> As to the likeness of the living creatures, their appearances &#91;are&#93; as coals of fire — burning as the appearance of lamps; it is going up and down between the living creatures, and brightness &#91;is&#93; to the fire, and out of the fire is going forth lightning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Zechariah 4:10; Mark 13:27"> And the living creatures are running, and turning back, as the appearance of the flash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 10:9; Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And I see the living creatures, and lo, one wheel &#91;is&#93; in the earth, near the living creatures, at its four faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Ezekiel 10:9–11; Exodus 39:13"> The appearance of the wheels and their works &#91;is&#93; as the colour of beryl, and one likeness &#91;is&#93; to them four, and their appearances and their works &#91;are&#93; as it were the wheel in the midst of the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> On their four sides, in their going they go, they turn not round in their going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:12; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 4:8"> As to their rings, they are both high and fearful, and their rings &#91;are&#93; full of eyes round about them four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 10:19; Psalms 103:20"> And in the going of the living creatures, the wheels go beside them, and in the living creatures being lifted up from off the earth, lifted up are the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:12; Ezekiel 10:17; 1 Corinthians 14:32"> Whither the spirit is to go, they go, thither the spirit &#91;is&#93; to go, and the wheels are lifted up over-against them, for a living spirit &#91;is&#93; in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:17; Ezekiel 1:19–20; Romans 8:2"> In their going, they go; and in their standing, they stand; and in their being lifted up from off the earth, lifted up are the wheels over-against them; for a living spirit &#91;is&#93; in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 4:6"> And a likeness &#91;is&#93; over the heads of the living creatures of an expanse, as the colour of the fearful ice, stretched out over their heads from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 4:18; Ezekiel 1:11–12; Psalms 89:7"> And under the expanse their wings &#91;are&#93; straight, one toward the other, to each are two covering on this side, and to each are two covering on that side — their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 1:15"> And I hear the noise of their wings, as the noise of many waters, as the noise of the Mighty One, in their going — the noise of tumult, as the noise of a camp, in their standing they let fall their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:22"> And there is a voice from above the expanse, that &#91;is&#93; above their head: in their standing they let fall their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Exodus 24:10; Revelation 1:13"> And above the expanse that &#91;is&#93; over their head, as an appearance of a sapphire stone, &#91;is&#93; the likeness of a throne, and on the likeness of the throne a likeness, as the appearance of man upon it from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 8:2; Hebrews 12:29"> And I see as the colour of copper, as the appearance of fire within it round about, from the appearance of his loins and upward, and from the appearance of his loins and downward, I have seen as the appearance of fire, and brightness &#91;is&#93; to it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 10:1; Revelation 4:3; Genesis 17:3"> As the appearance of the bow that is in a cloud in a day of rain, so &#91;is&#93; the appearance of the brightness round about.</span>
