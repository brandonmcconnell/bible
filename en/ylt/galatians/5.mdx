<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:13; 1 Peter 2:16"> In the freedom, then, with which Christ did make you free — stand ye, and be not held fast again by a yoke of servitude;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> lo, I Paul do say to you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> and I testify again to every man circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> ye were freed from the Christ, ye who in law are declared righteous; from the grace ye fell away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> for we by the Spirit, by faith, a hope of righteousness do wait for,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> for in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth anything, nor uncircumcision, but faith through love working.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> Ye were running well; who did hinder you — not to obey the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> the obedience &#91;is&#93; not of him who is calling you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> a little leaven the whole lump doth leaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have confidence in regard to you in the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded; and he who is troubling you shall bear the judgment, whoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> And I, brethren, if uncircumcision I yet preach, why yet am I persecuted? then hath the stumbling-block of the cross been done away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> O that even they would cut themselves off who are unsettling you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> For ye — to freedom ye were called, brethren, only not the freedom for an occasion to the flesh, but through the love serve ye one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> for all the law in one word is fulfilled — in this: 'Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> and if one another ye do bite and devour, see — that ye may not by one another be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> And I say: In the Spirit walk ye, and the desire of the flesh ye may not complete;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> for the flesh doth desire contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit contrary to the flesh, and these are opposed one to another, that the things that ye may will — these ye may not do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> and if by the Spirit ye are led, ye are not under law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> And manifest also are the works of the flesh, which are: Adultery, whoredom, uncleanness, lasciviousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, strifes, emulations, wraths, rivalries, dissensions, sects,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> envyings, murders, drunkennesses, revellings, and such like, of which I tell you before, as I also said before, that those doing such things the reign of God shall not inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> And the fruit of the Spirit is: Love, joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness, goodness, faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> meekness, temperance: against such there is no law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> and those who are Christ's, the flesh did crucify with the affections, and the desires;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> if we may live in the Spirit, in the Spirit also we may walk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> let us not become vain-glorious — one another provoking, one another envying!</span>
