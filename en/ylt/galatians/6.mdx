<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; John 13:34"> of one another the burdens bear ye, and so fill up the law of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> for if any one doth think &#91;himself&#93; to be something — being nothing — himself he doth deceive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 26:2; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> and his own work let each one prove, and then in regard to himself alone the glorying he shall have, and not in regard to the other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Romans 14:10–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10–11"> for each one his own burden shall bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Romans 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> And let him who is instructed in the word share with him who is instructing — in all good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Be not led astray; God is not mocked; for what a man may sow — that also he shall reap,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:13; James 3:18; 2 Peter 2:19"> because he who is sowing to his own flesh, of the flesh shall reap corruption; and he who is sowing to the Spirit, of the Spirit shall reap life age-during;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> and in the doing good we may not be faint-hearted, for at the proper time we shall reap — not desponding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> therefore, then, as we have opportunity, may we work the good to all, and especially unto those of the household of the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> Ye see in how large letters I have written to you with my own hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> as many as are willing to make a good appearance in the flesh, these constrain you to be circumcised — only that for the cross of the Christ they may not be persecuted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> for neither do those circumcised themselves keep the law, but they wish you to be circumcised, that in your flesh they may glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> And for me, let it not be — to glory, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which to me the world hath been crucified, and I to the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> for in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> and as many as by this rule do walk — peace upon them, and kindness, and on the Israel of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> Henceforth, let no one give me trouble, for I the scars of the Lord Jesus in my body do bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Timothy 4:22; Philemon 1:25"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; with your spirit, brethren! Amen.</span>
