<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Galatians 4:29; Genesis 24:2–3"> And I say, so long time as the heir is a babe, he differeth nothing from a servant — being lord of all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> but is under tutors and stewards till the time appointed of the father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> so also we, when we were babes, under the elements of the world were in servitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> and when the fulness of time did come, God sent forth His Son, come of a woman, come under law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> that those under law he may redeem, that the adoption of sons we may receive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> and because ye are sons, God did send forth the spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying, 'Abba, Father!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> so that thou art no more a servant, but a son, and if a son, also an heir of God through Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> But then, indeed, not having known God, ye were in servitude to those not by nature gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> and now, having known God — and rather being known by God — how turn ye again unto the weak and poor elements to which anew ye desire to be in servitude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> days ye observe, and months, and times, and years!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I am afraid of you, lest in vain I did labour toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> Become as I &#91;am&#93; — because I also &#91;am&#93; as ye brethren, I beseech you; to me ye did no hurt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> and ye have known that through infirmity of the flesh I did proclaim good news to you at the first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> and my trial that &#91;is&#93; in my flesh ye did not despise nor reject, but as a messenger of God ye did receive me — as Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> what then was your happiness? for I testify to you, that if possible, your eyes having plucked out, ye would have given to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> so that your enemy have I become, being true to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> they are zealous for you — &#91;yet&#93; not well, but they wish to shut us out, that for them ye may be zealous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> and &#91;it is&#93; good to be zealously regarded, in what is good, at all times, and not only in my being present with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> my little children, of whom again I travail in birth, till Christ may be formed in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> and I was wishing to be present with you now, and to change my voice, because I am in doubt about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, ye who are willing to be under law, the law do ye not hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> for it hath been written, that Abraham had two sons, one by the maid-servant, and one by the free-woman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> but he who &#91;is&#93; of the maid-servant, according to flesh hath been, and he who &#91;is&#93; of the free-woman, through the promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> which things are allegorized, for these are the two covenants: one, indeed, from mount Sinai, to servitude bringing forth, which is Hagar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> for this Hagar is mount Sinai in Arabia, and doth correspond to the Jerusalem that now &#91;is&#93;, and is in servitude with her children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> and the Jerusalem above is the free-woman, which is mother of us all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> for it hath been written, 'Rejoice, O barren, who art not bearing; break forth and cry, thou who art not travailing, because many &#91;are&#93; the children of the desolate — more than of her having the husband.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> And we, brethren, as Isaac, are children of promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> but as then he who was born according to the flesh did persecute him according to the spirit, so also now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> but what saith the Writing? 'Cast forth the maid-servant and her son, for the son of the maid-servant may not be heir with the son of the free-woman;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> then, brethren, we are not a maid-servant's children, but the free-woman's.</span>
