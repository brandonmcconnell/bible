<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:29; Genesis 41:51; Genesis 50:23"> And the lot is for the tribe of Manasseh (for he &#91;is&#93; first-born of Joseph), for Machir first-born of Manasseh, father of Gilead, for he hath been a man of war, and his are Gilead and Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:18; Numbers 26:29–32; Judges 6:11"> And there is for the sons of Manasseh who are left, for their families; for the sons of Abiezer, and for the sons of Helek, and for the sons of Asriel, and for the sons of Shechem, and for the sons of Hepher, and for the sons of Shemida; these &#91;are&#93; the children of Manasseh son of Joseph — the males — by their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33; Numbers 36:2–11"> As to Zelophehad, son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, he hath no children except daughters, and these &#91;are&#93; the names of his daughters: Mahlah, and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Numbers 27:5–7; Galatians 3:28"> and they draw near before Eleazar the priest, and before Joshua son of Nun, and before the princes, saying, 'Jehovah commanded Moses to give to us an inheritance in the midst of our brethren;' and he giveth to them, at the command of Jehovah, an inheritance in the midst of the brethren of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:2–3; Numbers 32:30–42; Joshua 13:29–31"> And ten portions fall &#91;to&#93; Manasseh, apart from the land of Gilead and Bashan, which &#91;are&#93; beyond the Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> for the daughters of Manasseh have inherited an inheritance in the midst of his sons, and the land of Gilead hath been to the sons of Manasseh who are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:21; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 24:32"> And the border of Manasseh is from Asher to Michmethah, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Shechem, and the border hath gone on unto the right, unto the inhabitants of En-Tappuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:8; Joshua 15:34; Joshua 12:17"> To Manasseh hath been the land of Tappuah, and Tappuah unto the border of Manasseh is to the sons of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 16:3; Joshua 19:29; Joshua 16:8–9"> And the border hath come down &#91;to&#93; the brook of Kanah, southward of the brook; these cities of Ephraim &#91;are&#93; in the midst of the cities of Manasseh, and the border of Manasseh &#91;is&#93; on the north of the brook, and its outgoings are at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Southward &#91;is&#93; to Ephraim and northward to Manasseh, and the sea is his border, and in Asher they meet on the north, and in Issachar on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:29; 2 Kings 9:27; Judges 5:19"> And Manasseh hath in Issachar and in Asher, Beth-Shean and its towns, and Ibleam and its towns, and the inhabitants of Dor and its towns, and the inhabitants of En-Dor and its towns, and the inhabitants of Taanach and its towns, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its towns, three counties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 1:27–28; Exodus 23:29–33; Romans 6:12–14"> And the sons of Manasseh have not been able to occupy these cities, and the Canaanite is desirous to dwell in this land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Philippians 4:13; Judges 1:35"> and it cometh to pass when the sons of Israel have been strong, that they put the Canaanite to tribute, and have not utterly dispossessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:34–37; Genesis 48:19; Genesis 48:22"> And the sons of Joseph speak with Joshua, saying, 'Wherefore hast thou given to me an inheritance — one lot and one portion, and I a numerous people? hitherto hath Jehovah blessed me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; Genesis 14:5; Luke 12:48"> And Joshua saith unto them, 'If thou &#91;art&#93; a numerous people, go up for thee to the forest, then thou hast prepared for thee there, in the land of the Perizzite, and of the Rephaim, when mount Ephraim hath been narrow for thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Judges 4:3; Judges 1:19"> And the sons of Joseph say, 'The hill is not found to us, and a chariot of iron &#91;is&#93; with every Canaanite who is dwelling in the land of the valley — to him who &#91;is&#93; in Beth-Shean and its towns, and to him who &#91;is&#93; in the valley of Jezreel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Joshua speaketh unto the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and to Manasseh, saying, 'Thou &#91;art&#93; a numerous people, and hast great power; thou hast not one lot &#91;only&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:6–9"> because the mountain is thine; because it &#91;is&#93; a forest — thou hast prepared it, and its outgoings have been thine; because thou dost dispossess the Canaanite, though it hath chariots of iron — though it &#91;is&#93; strong.'</span>
