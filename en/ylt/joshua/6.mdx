<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:24; Joshua 2:9–14; Psalms 127:1"> (And Jericho shutteth itself up, and is shut up, because of the presence of the sons of Israel — none going out, and none coming in;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 8:1; Joshua 2:24"> And Jehovah saith unto Joshua, 'See, I have given into thy hand Jericho and its king — mighty ones of valour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; Numbers 14:9; Joshua 6:7"> and ye have compassed the city — all the men of battle — going round the city once; thus thou dost six days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Revelation 16:1; 1 Kings 18:43"> and seven priests do bear seven trumpets of the jubilee before the ark, and on the seventh day ye compass the city seven times, and the priests blow with the trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hebrews 11:30; Exodus 19:19"> and it hath been, in the prolongation of the horn of the jubilee, in your hearing the voice of the trumpet, all the people shout — a great shout, and the wall of the city hath fallen under it, and the people have gone up, each over-against him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:1; Deuteronomy 20:2–4; Joshua 3:3"> And Joshua son of Nun calleth unto the priests, and saith unto them, 'Bear ye the ark of the covenant, and seven priests do bear seven trumpets of the jubilee before the ark of Jehovah;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:13; Joshua 6:3; Joshua 1:14"> and He said unto the people, 'Pass over, and compass the city, and he who is armed doth pass over before the ark of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:20; Joshua 6:3–4"> And it cometh to pass, when Joshua speaketh unto the people, that the seven priests bearing seven trumpets of the jubilee before Jehovah have passed over and blown with the trumpets, and the ark of the covenant of Jehovah is going after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:13; Numbers 10:25; Isaiah 58:8"> and he who is armed is going before the priests blowing the trumpets, and he who is gathering up is going after the ark, going on and blowing with the trumpets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:49; Matthew 12:19; Isaiah 42:2"> and the people hath Joshua commanded, saying, 'Ye do not shout, nor cause your voice to be heard, nor doth there go out from your mouth a word, till the day of my saying unto you, Shout ye — then ye have shouted.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the ark of Jehovah doth compass the city, going round once, and they come into the camp, and lodge in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 3:1; Hebrews 11:7–8; Genesis 22:3"> And Joshua riseth early in the morning, and the priests bear the ark of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Matthew 24:13; 1 Chronicles 15:26"> and seven priests bearing seven trumpets of the jubilee before the ark of Jehovah are walking, going on, and they have blown with the trumpets — and he who is armed is going before them, and he who is gathering up is going behind the ark of Jehovah — going on and blowing with the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 6:15; Joshua 6:11; Joshua 6:3"> And they compass the city on the second day once, and turn back to the camp; thus they have done six days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:147; 2 Peter 1:19; Matthew 28:1"> And it cometh to pass, on the seventh day, that they rise early, at the ascending of the dawn, and compass the city, according to this manner, seven times; (only, on that day they have compassed the city seven times);</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Joshua 6:5; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> and it cometh to pass, at the seventh time, the priests have blown with the trumpets, and Joshua saith unto the people, 'Shout ye, for Jehovah hath given to you the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:17; James 2:25; Micah 4:13"> and the city hath been devoted, it and all that &#91;is&#93; in it, to Jehovah; only Rahab the harlot doth live, she and all who &#91;are&#93; with her in the house, for she hid the messengers whom we sent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 5:21; Ephesians 5:11; Joshua 7:1"> and surely ye have kept from the devoted thing, lest ye devote &#91;yourselves&#93;, and have taken from the devoted thing, and have made the camp of Israel become a devoted thing, and have troubled it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:20; Matthew 27:6; Mark 12:41"> and all the silver and gold, and vessels of brass and iron, holy they &#91;are&#93; to Jehovah; into the treasury of Jehovah they come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:30; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Joshua 6:5"> And the people shout, and blow with the trumpets, and it cometh to pass when the people hear the voice of the trumpet, that the people shout — a great shout, and the wall falleth under it, and the people goeth up into the city, each over-against him, and they capture the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 137:8–9; Jeremiah 48:18; Joshua 11:14"> and they devote all that &#91;is&#93; in the city, from man even unto woman, from young even unto aged, even unto ox, and sheep, and ass, by the mouth of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:17; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> And to the two men who are spying the land Joshua said, 'Go into the house of the woman, the harlot, and bring out thence the woman, and all whom she hath, as ye have sworn to her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 18:24; Acts 27:24"> And the young man, the spies, go in and bring out Rahab, and her father, and her mother, and her brethren, and all whom she hath; yea, all her families they have brought out, and place them at the outside of the camp of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 8:28; Revelation 17:16; 2 Kings 25:9"> And the city they have burnt with fire, and all that &#91;is&#93; in it; only, the silver and the gold, and the vessels of brass, and of iron, they have given &#91;to&#93; the treasury of the house of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Matthew 1:5; Judges 1:24–25"> and Rahab the harlot, and the house of her father, and all whom she hath, hath Joshua kept alive; and she dwelleth in the midst of Israel unto this day, for she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 16:34; Acts 19:13; Numbers 5:19–21"> And Joshua adjureth &#91;them&#93; at that time, saying, 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man before Jehovah who raiseth up and hath built this city, &#91;even&#93; Jericho; in his first-born he doth lay its foundation, and in his youngest he doth set up its doors;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 1:5; Matthew 28:20"> and Jehovah is with Joshua, and his fame is in all the land.</span>
