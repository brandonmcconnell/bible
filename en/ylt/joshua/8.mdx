<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> And Jehovah saith unto Joshua, 'Fear not, nor be affrighted, take with thee all the people of war, and rise, go up to Ai; see, I have given into thy hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; 2 Chronicles 20:22"> and thou hast done to Ai and to her king as thou hast done to Jericho and to her king; only, its spoil and its cattle ye spoil for yourselves; set for thee an ambush for the city at its rear.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> And Joshua riseth, and all the people of war, to go up to Ai, and Joshua chooseth thirty thousand men, mighty ones of valour, and sendeth them away by night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> and commandeth them, saying, 'See, ye are liers in wait against the city, at the rear of the city, ye go not very far off from the city, and all of you have been prepared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> and I and all the people who &#91;are&#93; with me draw near unto the city, and it hath come to pass when they come out to meet us as at the first, and we have fled before them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> and they have come out after us till we have drawn them out of the city, for they say, They are fleeing before us as at the first, and we have fled before them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; 2 Kings 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> and ye rise from the ambush, and have occupied the city, and Jehovah your God hath given it into your hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> and it hath been, when ye capture the city, ye burn the city with fire, according to the word of Jehovah ye do, see, I have commanded you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> And Joshua sendeth them away, and they go unto the ambush, and abide between Bethel and Ai, on the west of Ai; and Joshua lodgeth on that night in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> And Joshua riseth early in the morning, and inspecteth the people, and goeth up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and all the people of war who &#91;are&#93; with him have gone up, and draw nigh and come in over-against the city, and encamp on the north of Ai; and the valley &#91;is&#93; between him and Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And he taketh about five thousand men, and setteth them an ambush between Bethel and Ai, on the west of the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:12; Joshua 8:8"> and they set the people, all the camp which &#91;is&#93; on the north of the city, and its rear on the west of the city, and Joshua goeth on that night into the midst of the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And it cometh to pass, when the king of Ai seeth &#91;it&#93;, that hasten, and rise early, and go out do the men of the city to meet Israel for battle, he and all his people, at the appointed season, at the front of the plain, and he hath not known that an ambush &#91;is&#93; against him, on the rear of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> And Joshua and all Israel &#91;seem&#93; stricken before them, and flee the way of the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> and all the people who &#91;are&#93; in the city are called to pursue after them, and they pursue after Joshua, and are drawn away out of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> and there hath not been left a man in Ai and Bethel who hath not gone out after Israel, and they leave the city open, and pursue after Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; 1 Samuel 17:41"> And Jehovah saith unto Joshua, 'Stretch out with the javelin which &#91;is&#93; in thy hand towards Ai, for into thy hand I give it;' and Joshua stretcheth out with the javelin which &#91;is&#93; in his hand toward the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the ambush hath risen &#91;with&#93; haste, out of its place, and they run at the stretching out of his hand, and go into the city, and capture it, and hasten, and burn the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> And the men of Ai look behind them, and see, and lo, the smoke of the city hath gone up unto the heavens, and there hath not been in them power to flee hither and thither — and the people who are fleeing to the wilderness have turned against the pursuer, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and Joshua and all Israel have seen that the ambush hath captured the city, and that the smoke of the city hath gone up, and they turn back and smite the men of Ai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> and these have come out from the city to meet them, and they are in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that, and they smite them till he hath not left to them a remnant and escaped one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> and the king of Ai they caught alive, and bring him near unto Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> And it cometh to pass, at Israel's finishing to slay all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness in which they pursued them (and they fall all of them by the mouth of the sword till their consumption), that all Israel turn back to Ai, and smite it by the mouth of the sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> and all who fall during the day, of men and of women, are twelve thousand — all men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:11–12; Joshua 8:18"> And Joshua hath not brought back his hand which he stretched out with the javelin till that he hath devoted all the inhabitants of Ai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalms 50:10"> only, the cattle and the spoil of that city have Israel spoiled for themselves, according to the word of Jehovah which He commanded Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Kings 19:25"> And Joshua burneth Ai, and maketh it a heap age-during — a desolation unto this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> and the king of Ai he hath hanged on the tree till even-time, and at the going in of the sun hath Joshua commanded, and they take down his carcase from the tree, and cast it unto the opening of the gate of the city, and raise over it a great heap of stones till this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then doth Joshua build an altar to Jehovah, God of Israel, in mount Ebal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> as Moses, servant of Jehovah, commanded the sons of Israel, as it is written in the book of the law of Moses — an altar of whole stones, over which he hath not waved iron — and they cause to go up upon it burnt-offerings to Jehovah, and sacrifice peace-offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> and he writeth there on the stones the copy of the law of Moses, which he hath written in the presence of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And all Israel, and its elders, and authorities, and its judges, are standing on this side and on that of the ark, over-against the priests, the Levites, bearing the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, as well the sojourner as the native, half of them over-against mount Gerizim, and the half of them over-against mount Ebal, as Moses servant of Jehovah commanded to bless the people of Israel at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> And afterwards he hath proclaimed all the words of the law, the blessing and the reviling, according to all that is written in the book of the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremiah 26:2"> there hath not been a thing of all that Moses commanded which Joshua hath not proclaimed before all the assembly of Israel, and the women, and the infants, and the sojourner who is going in their midst.</span>
