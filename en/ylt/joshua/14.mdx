<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:17–29"> And these &#91;are&#93; they &#91;of&#93; the sons of Israel who inherited in the land of Canaan, whom Eleazar the priest, and Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the sons of Israel, caused to inherit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 34:13; Numbers 26:55–56"> by lot &#91;is&#93; their inheritance, as Jehovah commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine of the tribes, and the half of the tribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:14; Joshua 13:8; Joshua 13:32–33"> for Moses hath given the inheritance of two of the tribes, and of half of the tribe, beyond the Jordan, and to the Levites he hath not given an inheritance in their midst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 48:5; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2; Numbers 35:2–8"> for the sons of Joseph hath been two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, and they have not given a portion to the Levites in the land, except cities to dwell in, and their suburbs for their cattle, and for their possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:2; Numbers 35:1–2"> as Jehovah commanded Moses, so have the sons of Israel done, and they apportion the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:24"> And the sons of Judah come nigh unto Joshua in Gilgal, and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenezzite saith unto him, 'Thou hast known the word that Jehovah hath spoken unto Moses the man of God, concerning me and concerning thee in Kadesh-Barnea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 13:26–33; Numbers 14:6–10"> a son of forty years &#91;am&#93; I in Moses, servant of Jehovah, sending me from Kadesh-Barnea, to spy the land, and I bring him back word as with my heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 13:31"> and my brethren who have gone up with me have caused the heart of the people to melt, and I have been fully after Jehovah my God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:3; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:22–24"> and Moses sweareth in that day, saying, If not — the land on which thy foot hath trodden, to thee it is for inheritance, and to thy sons — to the age, for thou hast been fully after Jehovah my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:33–34; Numbers 14:30; Joshua 11:18"> 'And, now, lo, Jehovah hath kept me alive, as He hath spoken, these forty and five years, since Jehovah spake this word unto Moses, when Israel went in the wilderness; and now, lo, I &#91;am&#93; to-day a son of five and eighty years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Psalms 103:5; Deuteronomy 31:2"> yet &#91;am&#93; I to-day strong as in the day of Moses' sending me; as my power then, so &#91;is&#93; my power now, for battle, and to go out, and to come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:33; Philippians 4:13; Numbers 13:33"> 'And now, give to me this hill-country, of which Jehovah spake in that day, for thou didst hear in that day, for Anakim &#91;are&#93; there, and cities, great, fenced; if so be Jehovah &#91;is&#93; with me, then I have dispossessed them, as Jehovah hath spoken.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:20; Joshua 22:6; 1 Chronicles 6:55–56"> And Joshua blesseth him, and giveth Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh for an inheritance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 14:8–9; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> therefore hath Hebron been to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenezzite for an inheritance unto this day, because that he was fully after Jehovah, God of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Genesis 23:2; Joshua 15:13"> and the name of Hebron formerly &#91;is&#93; Kirjath-Arba (he &#91;is&#93; the great man among the Anakim); and the land hath rest from war.</span>
