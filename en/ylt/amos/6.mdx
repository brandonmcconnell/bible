<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; James 5:5; Luke 12:17–20"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those secure in Zion, And those confident in the mount of Samaria, The marked of the chief of the nations, And come to them have the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nahum 3:8; Genesis 10:10; 2 Kings 18:34"> Pass ye over &#91;to&#93; Calneh and see, And go thence &#91;to&#93; Hamath the great, And go down &#91;to&#93; Gath of the Philistines, Are &#91;they&#93; better than these kingdoms? Greater &#91;is&#93; their border than your border?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:10; Isaiah 56:12; Ezekiel 12:27"> Who are putting away the day of evil, And ye bring nigh the seat of violence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Luke 16:19; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> Who are lying down on beds of ivory, And are spread out on their couches, And are eating lambs from the flock, And calves from the midst of the stall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; Amos 5:23; Amos 8:3"> Who are taking part according to the psaltery, Like David they invented for themselves instruments of music;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Matthew 26:7–9"> Who are drinking with bowls of wine, And &#91;with&#93; chief perfumes anoint &#91;themselves&#93;, And have not been pained for the breach of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 7:11; Daniel 5:4–6; Amos 5:5"> Therefore now they remove at the head of the captives, And turned aside is the mourning-feast of stretched-out ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:14; Amos 4:2; Amos 8:7"> Sworn hath the Lord Jehovah by Himself, An affirmation of Jehovah, God of Hosts: I am abominating the excellency of Jacob, And his high places I have hated, And I have delivered up the city and its fulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:3; Job 1:2; Psalms 109:13"> And if there are left ten persons in one house, It hath come to pass — that they have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 8:3; 1 Samuel 31:12; Amos 5:13"> And lifted him up hath his loved one, even his burner, To bring forth the bones from the house, And he said to him who &#91;is&#93; in the sides of the house, 'Is there yet with thee?' And he said, 'None,' then he said, 'Hush! Save to make mention of the name of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 Kings 25:9"> For lo, Jehovah is commanding, And He hath smitten the great house &#91;with&#93; breaches, And the little house &#91;with&#93; clefts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Hosea 10:4; Amos 5:11–12"> Do horses run on a rock? Doth one plough &#91;it&#93; with oxen? For ye have turned to gall judgment, And the fruit of righteousness to wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Kings 14:25"> O ye who are rejoicing at nothing, Who are saying, 'Have we not by our strength taken to ourselves horns?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 5:15–17"> Surely, lo, I am raising against you a nation, O house of Israel, An affirmation of Jehovah, God of Hosts, And they have oppressed you from the coming in to Hamath, Unto the stream of the desert.</span>
