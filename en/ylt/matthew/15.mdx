<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then come unto Jesus do they from Jerusalem — scribes and Pharisees — saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> 'Wherefore do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they do not wash their hands when they may eat bread.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> And he answering said to them, 'Wherefore also do ye transgress the command of God because of your tradition?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> for God did command, saying, Honour thy father and mother; and, He who is speaking evil of father or mother — let him die the death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> but ye say, Whoever may say to father or mother, An offering &#91;is&#93; whatever thou mayest be profited by me; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> and he may not honour his father or his mother, and ye did set aside the command of God because of your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> 'Hypocrites, well did Isaiah prophesy of you, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> This people doth draw nigh to Me with their mouth, and with the lips it doth honour Me, but their heart is far off from Me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> and in vain do they worship Me, teaching teachings — commands of men.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> And having called near the multitude, he said to them, 'Hear and understand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> not that which is coming into the mouth doth defile the man, but that which is coming forth from the mouth, this defileth the man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then his disciples having come near, said to him, 'Hast thou known that the Pharisees, having heard the word, were stumbled?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> And he answering said, 'Every plant that my heavenly Father did not plant shall be rooted up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> let them alone, guides they are — blind of blind; and if blind may guide blind, both into a ditch shall fall.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> And Peter answering said to him, 'Explain to us this simile.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> And Jesus said, 'Are ye also yet without understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> do ye not understand that all that is going into the mouth doth pass into the belly, and into the drain is cast forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> but the things coming forth from the mouth from the heart do come forth, and these defile the man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> for out of the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, whoredoms, thefts, false witnessings, evil speakings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> these are the things defiling the man; but to eat with unwashen hands doth not defile the man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> And Jesus having come forth thence, withdrew to the parts of Tyre and Sidon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> and lo, a woman, a Canaanitess, from those borders having come forth, did call to him, saying, 'Deal kindly with me, Sir — Son of David; my daughter is miserably demonized.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> And he did not answer her a word; and his disciples having come to him, were asking him, saying — 'Let her away, because she crieth after us;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> and he answering said, 'I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> And having come, she was bowing to him, saying, 'Sir, help me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> and he answering said, 'It is not good to take the children's bread, and to cast to the little dogs.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> And she said, 'Yes, sir, for even the little dogs do eat of the crumbs that are falling from their lords' table;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> then answering, Jesus said to her, 'O woman, great &#91;is&#93; thy faith, let it be to thee as thou wilt;' and her daughter was healed from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> And Jesus having passed thence, came nigh unto the sea of Galilee, and having gone up to the mountain, he was sitting there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> and there came to him great multitudes, having with them lame, blind, dumb, maimed, and many others, and they did cast them at the feet of Jesus, and he healed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> so that the multitudes did wonder, seeing dumb ones speaking, maimed whole, lame walking, and blind seeing; and they glorified the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> And Jesus having called near his disciples, said, 'I have compassion upon the multitude, because now three days they continue with me, and they have not what they may eat; and to let them away fasting I will not, lest they faint in the way.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> And his disciples say to him, 'Whence to us, in a wilderness, so many loaves, as to fill so great a multitude?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> And Jesus saith to them, 'How many loaves have ye?' and they said, 'Seven, and a few little fishes.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> And he commanded the multitudes to sit down upon the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> and having taken the seven loaves and the fishes, having given thanks, he did break, and gave to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> And they did all eat, and were filled, and they took up what was over of the broken pieces seven baskets full,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and those eating were four thousand men, apart from women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> And having let away the multitudes, he went into the boat, and did come to the borders of Magdala.</span>
