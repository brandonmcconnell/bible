<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; Luke 2:4–7"> And Jesus having been born in Beth-Lehem of Judea, in the days of Herod the king, lo, mages from the east came to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16"> saying, 'Where is he who was born king of the Jews? for we saw his star in the east, and we came to bow to him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 11:47–48; Matthew 23:37"> And Herod the king having heard, was stirred, and all Jerusalem with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Matthew 7:29; Acts 6:12"> and having gathered all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he was inquiring from them where the Christ is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 7:42; Ruth 1:1; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And they said to him, 'In Beth-Lehem of Judea, for thus it hath been written through the prophet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–25; Micah 5:2; Isaiah 9:6–7"> And thou, Beth-Lehem, the land of Judah, thou art by no means the least among the leaders of Judah, for out of thee shall come one leading, who shall feed My people Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:21; Psalms 55:21"> Then Herod, privately having called the mages, did inquire exactly from them the time of the appearing star,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19–20; Jeremiah 41:5–7; Luke 20:20–21"> and having sent them to Beth-Lehem, he said, 'Having gone — inquire ye exactly for the child, and whenever ye may have found, bring me back word, that I also having come may bow to him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 25:12"> And they, having heard the king, departed, and lo, the star, that they did see in the east, did go before them, till, having come, it stood over where the child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:3; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:10"> And having seen the star, they rejoiced with exceeding great joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10; Luke 2:16"> and having come to the house, they found the child with Mary his mother, and having fallen down they bowed to him, and having opened their treasures, they presented to him gifts, gold, and frankincense, and myrrh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 2:22; Matthew 2:19; Matthew 2:13"> and having been divinely warned in a dream not to turn back unto Herod, through another way they withdrew to their own region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:7; Acts 5:19; Matthew 1:20"> And on their having withdrawn, lo, a messenger of the Lord doth appear in a dream to Joseph, saying, 'Having risen, take the child and his mother, and flee to Egypt, and be thou there till I may speak to thee, for Herod is about to seek the child to destroy him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 2:20–21; Acts 26:21; Matthew 1:24"> And he, having risen, took the child and his mother by night, and withdrew to Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22; Numbers 24:8"> and he was there till the death of Herod, that it might be fulfilled that was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying, 'Out of Egypt I did call My Son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 28:15; Judges 16:10"> Then Herod, having seen that he was deceived by the mages, was very wroth, and having sent forth, he slew all the male children in Beth-Lehem, and in all its borders, from two years and under, according to the time that he inquired exactly from the mages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Jeremiah 9:17–21; Jeremiah 4:31"> 'A voice in Ramah was heard — lamentation and weeping and much mourning — Rachel weeping &#91;for&#93; her children, and she would not be comforted because they are not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Daniel 8:25; Matthew 2:12–13"> And Herod having died, lo, a messenger of the Lord in a dream doth appear to Joseph in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:19; 1 Kings 12:1–3; Matthew 2:13"> saying, 'Having risen, take the child and his mother, and be going to the land of Israel, for they have died — those seeking the life of the child.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 6:22"> And he, having risen, took the child and his mother, and came to the land of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 48:17–18; Luke 2:39; John 7:52"> and having heard that Archelaus doth reign over Judea instead of Herod his father, he was afraid to go thither, and having been divinely warned in a dream, he withdrew to the parts of Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 13:5; John 1:45–46; Mark 1:24"> and coming, he dwelt in a city named Nazareth, that it might be fulfilled that was spoken through the prophets, that 'A Nazarene he shall be called.'</span>
