<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> A roll of the birth of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> Abraham begat Isaac, and Isaac begat Jacob, and Jacob begat Judah and his brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> and Judah begat Pharez and Zarah of Tamar, and Pharez begat Hezron, and Hezron begat Ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> and Ram begat Amminadab, and Amminadab begat Nahshon, and Nahshon begat Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> and Salmon begat Boaz of Rahab, and Boaz begat Obed of Ruth, and Obed begat Jesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> and Jesse begat David the king. And David the king begat Solomon, of her &#91;who had been&#93; Uriah's,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> and Solomon begat Rehoboam, and Rehoboam begat Abijah, and Abijah begat Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> and Asa begat Jehoshaphat, and Jehoshaphat begat Joram, and Joram begat Uzziah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> and Uzziah begat Jotham, and Jotham begat Ahaz, and Ahaz begat Hezekiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> and Hezekiah begat Manasseh, and Manasseh begat Amon, and Amon begat Josiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> and Josiah begat Jeconiah and his brethren, at the Babylonian removal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> And after the Babylonian removal, Jeconiah begat Shealtiel, and Shealtiel begat Zerubbabel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and Zerubbabel begat Abiud, and Abiud begat Eliakim, and Eliakim begat Azor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and Azor begat Sadok, and Sadok begat Achim, and Achim begat Eliud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and Eliud begat Eleazar, and Eleazar begat Matthan, and Matthan begat Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> and Jacob begat Joseph, the husband of Mary, of whom was begotten Jesus, who is named Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> All the generations, therefore, from Abraham unto David &#91;are&#93; fourteen generations, and from David unto the Babylonian removal fourteen generations, and from the Babylonian removal unto the Christ, fourteen generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> And of Jesus Christ, the birth was thus: For his mother Mary having been betrothed to Joseph, before their coming together she was found to have conceived from the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> and Joseph her husband being righteous, and not willing to make her an example, did wish privately to send her away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> And on his thinking of these things, lo, a messenger of the Lord in a dream appeared to him, saying, 'Joseph, son of David, thou mayest not fear to receive Mary thy wife, for that which in her was begotten &#91;is&#93; of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> and she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name Jesus, for he shall save his people from their sins.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> And all this hath come to pass, that it may be fulfilled that was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> 'Lo, the virgin shall conceive, and she shall bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel,' which is, being interpreted 'With us &#91;he is&#93; God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> And Joseph, having risen from the sleep, did as the messenger of the Lord directed him, and received his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> and did not know her till she brought forth her son — the first-born, and he called his name Jesus.</span>
