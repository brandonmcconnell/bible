<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Matthew 19:1"> And it came to pass, when Jesus finished all these words, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Mark 14:1–2; John 13:1–2"> 'Ye have known that after two days the passover cometh, and the Son of Man is delivered up to be crucified.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then were gathered together the chief priests, and the scribes, and the elders of the people, to the court of the chief priest who was called Caiaphas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:14; Psalms 2:2; Acts 13:10"> and they consulted together that they might take Jesus by guile, and kill &#91;him&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:24; Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5"> and they said, 'Not in the feast, that there may not be a tumult among the people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> And Jesus having been in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> there came to him a woman having an alabaster box of ointment, very precious, and she poured on his head as he is reclining (at meat).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> And having seen &#91;it&#93;, his disciples were much displeased, saying, 'To what purpose &#91;is&#93; this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> for this ointment could have been sold for much, and given to the poor.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> And Jesus having known, said to them, 'Why do ye give trouble to the woman? for a good work she wrought for me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> for the poor always ye have with you, and me ye have not always;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> for she having put this ointment on my body — for my burial she did &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Verily I say to you, Wherever this good news may be proclaimed in the whole world, what this &#91;one&#93; did shall also be spoken of — for a memorial of her.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then one of the twelve, who is called Judas Iscariot, having gone unto the chief priests, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> 'What are ye willing to give me, and I will deliver him up to you?' and they weighed out to him thirty silverlings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> and from that time he was seeking a convenient season to deliver him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> And on the first &#91;day&#93; of the unleavened food came the disciples near to Jesus, saying to him, 'Where wilt thou &#91;that&#93; we may prepare for thee to eat the passover?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> and he said, 'Go away to the city, unto such a one, and say to him, The Teacher saith, My time is nigh; near thee I keep the passover, with my disciples;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> and the disciples did as Jesus appointed them, and prepared the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> And evening having come, he was reclining (at meat) with the twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> and while they are eating, he said, 'Verily I say to you, that one of you shall deliver me up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> And being grieved exceedingly, they began to say to him, each of them, 'Is it I, Sir?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 41:9; John 13:26–28"> And he answering said, 'He who did dip with me the hand in the dish, he will deliver me up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> the Son of Man doth indeed go, as it hath been written concerning him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is delivered up! good it were for him if that man had not been born.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> And Judas — he who delivered him up — answering said, 'Is it I, Rabbi?' He saith to him, 'Thou hast said.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And while they were eating, Jesus having taken the bread, and having blessed, did brake, and was giving to the disciples, and said, 'Take, eat, this is my body;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> and having taken the cup, and having given thanks, he gave to them, saying, 'Drink ye of it — all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> for this is my blood of the new covenant, that for many is being poured out — to remission of sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> and I say to you, that I may not drink henceforth on this produce of the vine, till that day when I may drink it with you new in the reign of my Father.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> And having sung a hymn, they went forth to the mount of the Olives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> then saith Jesus to them, 'All ye shall be stumbled at me this night; for it hath been written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be scattered abroad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> but, after my having risen, I will go before you to Galilee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> And Peter answering said to him, 'Even if all shall be stumbled at thee, I will never be stumbled.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Jesus said to him, 'Verily I say to thee, that, this night, before cock-crowing, thrice thou wilt deny me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> Peter saith to him, 'Even if it may be necessary for me to die with thee, I will not deny thee;' in like manner also said all the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then come with them doth Jesus to a place called Gethsemane, and he saith to the disciples, 'Sit ye here, till having gone away, I shall pray yonder.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> And having taken Peter, and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to be sorrowful, and to be very heavy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> then saith he to them, 'Exceedingly sorrowful is my soul — unto death; abide ye here, and watch with me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> And having gone forward a little, he fell on his face, praying, and saying, 'My Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me; nevertheless, not as I will, but as Thou.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them sleeping, and he saith to Peter, 'So! ye were not able one hour to watch with me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> watch, and pray, that ye may not enter into temptation: the spirit indeed is forward, but the flesh weak.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> Again, a second time, having gone away, he prayed, saying, 'My Father, if this cup cannot pass away from me except I drink it, Thy will be done;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> and having come, he findeth them again sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> And having left them, having gone away again, he prayed a third time, saying the same word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> then cometh he unto his disciples, and saith to them, 'Sleep on henceforth, and rest! lo, the hour hath come nigh, and the Son of Man is delivered up to the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Rise, let us go; lo, he hath come nigh who is delivering me up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> And while he is yet speaking, lo, Judas, one of the twelve did come, and with him a great multitude, with swords and sticks, from the chief priests and elders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> And he who did deliver him up did give them a sign, saying, 'Whomsoever I will kiss, it is he: lay hold on him;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> and immediately, having come to Jesus, he said, 'Hail, Rabbi,' and kissed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> and Jesus said to him, 'Comrade, for what art thou present?' Then having come near, they laid hands on Jesus, and took hold on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And lo, one of those with Jesus, having stretched forth the hand, drew his sword, and having struck the servant of the chief priest, he took off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> Then saith Jesus to him, 'Turn back thy sword to its place; for all who did take the sword, by the sword shall perish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> dost thou think that I am not able now to call upon my Father, and He will place beside me more than twelve legions of messengers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> how then may the Writings be fulfilled, that thus it behoveth to happen?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> In that hour said Jesus to the multitudes, 'As against a robber ye did come forth, with swords and sticks, to take me! daily with you I was sitting teaching in the temple, and ye did not lay hold on me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> but all this hath come to pass, that the Writings of the prophets may be fulfilled;' then all the disciples, having left him, fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> And those laying hold on Jesus led &#91;him&#93; away unto Caiaphas the chief priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> and Peter was following him afar off, unto the court of the chief priest, and having gone in within, he was sitting with the officers, to see the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> And the chief priests, and the elders, and all the council, were seeking false witness against Jesus, that they might put him to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> and they did not find; and many false witnesses having come near, they did not find; and at last two false witnesses having come near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> said, 'This one said, I am able to throw down the sanctuary of God, and after three days to build it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> And the chief priest having stood up, said to him, 'Nothing thou dost answer! what do these witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> and Jesus was silent. And the chief priest answering said to him, 'I adjure thee, by the living God, that thou mayest say to us, if thou art the Christ — the Son of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Jesus saith to him, 'Thou hast said; nevertheless I say to you, hereafter ye shall see the Son of Man sitting on the right hand of the power, and coming upon the clouds, of the heaven.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the chief priest rent his garments, saying, — 'He hath spoken evil; what need have we yet of witnesses? lo, now ye heard his evil speaking;</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> what think ye?' and they answering said, 'He is worthy of death.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then did they spit in his face and buffet him, and others did slap,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> saying, 'Declare to us, O Christ, who he is that struck thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> And Peter without was sitting in the court, and there came near to him a certain maid, saying, 'And thou wast with Jesus of Galilee!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> And he denied before all, saying, 'I have not known what thou sayest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> And he having gone forth to the porch, another female saw him, and saith to those there, 'And this one was with Jesus of Nazareth;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> and again did he deny with an oath — 'I have not known the man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> And after a little those standing near having come, said to Peter, 'Truly thou also art of them, for even thy speech doth make thee manifest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then began he to anathematise, and to swear — 'I have not known the man;' and immediately did a cock crow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> and Peter remembered the saying of Jesus, he having said to him — 'Before cock-crowing, thrice thou wilt deny me;' and having gone without, he did weep bitterly.</span>
