<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> And morning having come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus, so as to put him to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> and having bound him, they did lead away, and delivered him up to Pontius Pilate, the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas — he who delivered him up — having seen that he was condemned, having repented, brought back the thirty silverlings to the chief priests, and to the elders, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> 'I did sin, having delivered up innocent blood;' and they said, 'What — to us? thou shalt see!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> and having cast down the silverlings in the sanctuary, he departed, and having gone away, he did strangle himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> And the chief priests having taken the silverlings, said, 'It is not lawful to put them to the treasury, seeing it is the price of blood;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and having taken counsel, they bought with them the field of the potter, for the burial of strangers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> therefore was that field called, 'Field of blood,' unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then was fulfilled that spoken through Jeremiah the prophet, saying, 'And I took the thirty silverlings, the price of him who hath been priced, whom they of the sons of Israel did price,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and gave them for the field of the potter, as the Lord did appoint to me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor did question him, saying, 'Art thou the king of the Jews!' And Jesus said to him, 'Thou sayest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And in his being accused by the chief priests and the elders, he did not answer any thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> then saith Pilate to him, 'Dost thou not hear how many things they witness against thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> And he did not answer him, not even to one word, so that the governor did wonder greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> And at the feast the governor had been accustomed to release one to the multitude, a prisoner, whom they willed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> and they had then a noted prisoner, called Barabbas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> they therefore having been gathered together, Pilate said to them, 'Whom will ye I shall release to you? Barabbas or Jesus who is called Christ?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> for he had known that because of envy they had delivered him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> And as he is sitting on the tribunal, his wife sent unto him, saying, 'Nothing — to thee and to that righteous one, for many things did I suffer to-day in a dream because of him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> And the chief priests and the elders did persuade the multitudes that they might ask for themselves Barabbas, and might destroy Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the governor answering said to them, 'Which of the two will ye &#91;that&#93; I shall release to you?' And they said, 'Barabbas.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate saith to them, 'What then shall I do with Jesus who is called Christ?' They all say to him, 'Let be crucified!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> And the governor said, 'Why, what evil did he?' and they were crying out the more, saying, 'Let be crucified.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> And Pilate having seen that it profiteth nothing, but rather a tumult is made, having taken water, he did wash the hands before the multitude, saying, 'I am innocent from the blood of this righteous one; ye — ye shall see;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> and all the people answering said, 'His blood &#91;is&#93; upon us, and upon our children!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then did he release to them Barabbas, and having scourged Jesus, he delivered &#91;him&#93; up that he may be crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> then the soldiers of the governor having taken Jesus to the Praetorium, did gather to him all the band;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> and having unclothed him, they put around him a crimson cloak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> and having plaited him a crown out of thorns they put &#91;it&#93; on his head, and a reed in his right hand, and having kneeled before him, they were mocking him, saying, 'Hail, the king of the Jews.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And having spit on him, they took the reed, and were smiting on his head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> and when they had mocked him, they took off from him the cloak, and put on him his own garments, and led him away to crucify &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And coming forth, they found a man, a Cyrenian, by name Simon: him they impressed that he might bear his cross;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> and having come to a place called Golgotha, that is called Place of a Skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they gave him to drink vinegar mixed with gall, and having tasted, he would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And having crucified him, they divided his garments, casting a lot, that it might be fulfilled that was spoken by the prophet, 'They divided my garments to themselves, and over my vesture they cast a lot;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> and sitting down, they were watching him there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> and they put up over his head, his accusation written, 'This is Jesus, the king of the Jews.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then crucified with him are two robbers, one on the right hand, and one on the left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> and those passing by were speaking evil of him, wagging their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, 'Thou that art throwing down the sanctuary, and in three days building &#91;it&#93;, save thyself; if Son thou art of God, come down from the cross.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> And in like manner also the chief priests mocking, with the scribes and elders, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> 'Others he saved; himself he is not able to save! If he be King of Israel, let him come down now from the cross, and we will believe him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> he hath trusted on God, let Him now deliver him, if He wish him, because he said — Son of God I am;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> with the same also the robbers, who were crucified with him, were reproaching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> And from the sixth hour darkness came over all the land unto the ninth hour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> and about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a great voice, saying, 'Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?' that is, 'My God, my God, why didst Thou forsake me?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> And certain of those standing there having heard, said — 'Elijah he doth call;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> and immediately, one of them having run, and having taken a spunge, having filled &#91;it&#93; with vinegar, and having put &#91;it&#93; on a reed, was giving him to drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> but the rest said, 'Let alone, let us see if Elijah doth come — about to save him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> And Jesus having again cried with a great voice, yielded the spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> and lo, the vail of the sanctuary was rent in two from top unto bottom, and the earth did quake, and the rocks were rent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who have fallen asleep, arose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> and having come forth out of the tombs after his rising, they went into the holy city, and appeared to many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> And the centurion, and those with him watching Jesus, having seen the earthquake, and the things that were done, were exceedingly afraid, saying, 'Truly this was God's Son.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And there were there many women beholding from afar, who did follow Jesus from Galilee, ministering to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> among whom was Mary the Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and of Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> And evening having come, there came a rich man, from Arimathea, named Joseph, who also himself was discipled to Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> he having gone near to Pilate, asked for himself the body of Jesus; then Pilate commanded the body to be given back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And having taken the body, Joseph wrapped it in clean linen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and laid it in his new tomb, that he hewed in the rock, and having rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, he went away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> and there were there Mary the Magdalene, and the other Mary, sitting over-against the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> And on the morrow that is after the preparation, were gathered together the chief priests, and the Pharisees, unto Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> saying, 'Sir, we have remembered that that deceiver said while yet living, After three days I do rise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> command, then, the sepulchre to be made secure till the third day, lest his disciples, having come by night, may steal him away, and may say to the people, He rose from the dead, and the last deceit shall be worse than the first.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> And Pilate said to them, 'Ye have a watch, go away, make secure — as ye have known;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> and they, having gone, did make the sepulchre secure, having sealed the stone, together with the watch.</span>
