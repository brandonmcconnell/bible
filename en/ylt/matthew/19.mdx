<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Matthew 19:1–9; Matthew 7:28"> And it came to pass, when Jesus finished these words, he removed from Galilee, and did come to the borders of Judea, beyond the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:15; Mark 6:55–56; Matthew 15:30–31"> and great multitudes followed him, and he healed them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:2; Hebrews 3:9; Matthew 16:1"> And the Pharisees came near to him, tempting him, and saying to him, 'Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2"> And he answering said to them, 'Did ye not read, that He who made &#91;them&#93;, from the beginning a male and a female made them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 2:21–24; 1 Corinthians 7:4; 1 Corinthians 7:2"> and said, For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and cleave to his wife, and they shall be — the two — for one flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Mark 10:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14"> so that they are no more two, but one flesh; what therefore God did join together, let no man put asunder.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31; Mark 10:4"> They say to him, 'Why then did Moses command to give a roll of divorce, and to put her away?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 2:13–14; Genesis 2:24; Mark 10:5"> He saith to them — 'Moses for your stiffness of heart did suffer you to put away your wives, but from the beginning it hath not been so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> 'And I say to you, that, whoever may put away his wife, if not for whoredom, and may marry another, doth commit adultery; and he who did marry her that hath been put away, doth commit adultery.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–35; Proverbs 19:13–14; Proverbs 21:19"> His disciples say to him, 'If the case of the man with the woman is so, it is not good to marry.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 7:35; 1 Corinthians 7:7–9"> And he said to them, 'All do not receive this word, but those to whom it hath been given;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–38; 1 Corinthians 9:5; Isaiah 56:3–4"> for there are eunuchs who from the mother's womb were so born; and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by men; and there are eunuchs who kept themselves eunuchs because of the reign of the heavens: he who is able to receive &#91;it&#93; — let him receive.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Luke 18:15–17; Matthew 18:2–5"> Then were brought near to him children that he might put hands on them and pray, and the disciples rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:16–17; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> But Jesus said, 'Suffer the children, and forbid them not, to come unto me, for of such is the reign of the heavens;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:16; 2 Timothy 3:15; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> and having laid on them &#91;his&#93; hands, he departed thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 10:17–30; John 3:15; Jude 1:21"> And lo, one having come near, said to him, 'Good teacher, what good thing shall I do, that I may have life age-during?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Luke 10:26–28; Galatians 3:11–13"> And he said to him, 'Why me dost thou call good? no one &#91;is&#93; good except One — God; but if thou dost will to enter into the life, keep the commands.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Exodus 20:12–17; Mark 10:19"> He saith to him, 'What kind?' And Jesus said, 'Thou shalt not kill, thou shalt not commit adultery, thou shalt not steal, thou shalt not bear false witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 20:12; Matthew 22:39"> honour thy father and mother, and, thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Mark 10:20–21; Philippians 3:6"> The young man saith to him, 'All these did I keep from my youth; what yet do I lack?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 10:21; Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33"> Jesus said to him, 'If thou dost will to be perfect, go away, sell what thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven, and come, follow me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 13:22"> And the young man, having heard the word, went away sorrowful, for he had many possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Matthew 13:22; Mark 10:23"> and Jesus said to his disciples, 'Verily I say to you, that hardly shall a rich man enter into the reign of the heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:44; Luke 18:25; Mark 10:24–25"> and again I say to you, it is easier for a camel through the eye of a needle to go, than for a rich man to enter into the reign of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 10:13; Matthew 24:22; Romans 11:5–7"> And his disciples having heard, were amazed exceedingly, saying, 'Who, then, is able to be saved?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Job 42:2"> And Jesus having earnestly beheld, said to them, 'With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Luke 18:28; Luke 5:11"> Then Peter answering said to him, 'Lo, we did leave all, and follow thee, what then shall we have?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:28–30; 1 Corinthians 6:2–3; Matthew 25:31"> And Jesus said to them, 'Verily I say to you, that ye who did follow me, in the regeneration, when the Son of Man may sit upon a throne of his glory, shall sit — ye also — upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:29–30; Mark 10:29–30; Philippians 3:8"> and every one who left houses, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or fields, for my name's sake, an hundredfold shall receive, and life age-during shall inherit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 20:16; Luke 13:30; Mark 10:31"> and many first shall be last, and last first.</span>
