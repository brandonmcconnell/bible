<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Luke 6:1–5"> At that time did Jesus go on the sabbaths through the corn, and his disciples were hungry, and they began to pluck ears, and to eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; John 7:21–24; Luke 14:3"> and the Pharisees having seen, said to him, 'Lo, thy disciples do that which it is not lawful to do on a sabbath.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 6:3; Mark 2:25–26"> And he said to them, 'Did ye not read what David did, when he was hungry, himself and those with him —</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 8:31"> how he went into the house of God, and the loaves of the presentation did eat, which it is not lawful to him to eat, nor to those with him, except to the priests alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:9–10; John 7:22–23; Nehemiah 13:17"> 'Or did ye not read in the Law, that on the sabbaths the priests in the temple do profane the sabbath, and are blameless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Peter 2:4–5"> and I say to you, that a greater than the temple is here;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Matthew 9:13; Micah 6:6–8"> and if ye had known what is: Kindness I will, and not sacrifice — ye had not condemned the blameless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 2:28; Luke 6:5; John 5:17–23"> for the son of man is lord even of the sabbath.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Luke 6:6–11"> And having departed thence, he went to their synagogue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:14; John 9:16; John 8:6"> and lo, there was a man having the hand withered, and they questioned him, saying, 'Is it lawful to heal on the sabbaths?' that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4; Luke 14:5; Luke 13:15–17"> And he said to them, 'What man shall be of you, who shall have one sheep, and if this may fall on the sabbaths into a ditch, will not lay hold on it and raise &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:26; Luke 6:9; Mark 3:4"> How much better, therefore, is a man than a sheep? — so that it is lawful on the sabbaths to do good.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Acts 3:7–8"> Then saith he to the man, 'Stretch forth thy hand,' and he stretched &#91;it&#93; forth, and it was restored whole as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:53; John 10:39; Mark 3:6"> And the Pharisees having gone forth, held a consultation against him, how they might destroy him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 3:7–12; Matthew 10:23; Matthew 19:2"> and Jesus having known, withdrew thence, and there followed him great multitudes, and he healed them all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Matthew 17:9"> and did charge them that they might not make him manifest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1–4; Isaiah 42:9; Matthew 13:35"> that it might be fulfilled that was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Luke 4:18; Acts 10:38"> 'Lo, My servant, whom I did choose, My beloved, in whom My soul did delight, I will put My Spirit upon him, and judgment to the nations he shall declare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 18:36–38; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> he shall not strive nor cry, nor shall any hear in the broad places his voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 42:3–4; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 61:1–3"> a bruised reed he shall not break, and smoking flax he shall not quench, till he may put forth judgment to victory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Romans 15:12–13; Isaiah 42:4"> and in his name shall nations hope.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:14–15; Mark 7:35–37; Isaiah 29:18"> Then was brought to him a demoniac, blind and dumb, and he healed him, so that the blind and dumb both spake and saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; John 4:29; Matthew 22:42–43"> And all the multitudes were amazed, and said, 'Is this the Son of David?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 9:34; Luke 11:15"> but the Pharisees having heard, said, 'This one doth not cast out demons, except by Beelzeboul, ruler of the demons.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 9:4"> And Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said to them, 'Every kingdom having been divided against itself is desolated, and no city or house having been divided against itself, doth stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; Revelation 20:2–3; 1 John 5:19"> and if the Adversary doth cast out the Adversary, against himself he was divided, how then doth his kingdom stand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:13–16; Mark 9:38–39; Luke 9:49–50"> 'And if I, by Beelzeboul, do cast out the demons, your sons — by whom do they cast out? because of this they — they shall be your judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 11:20; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 6:33"> 'But if I, by the Spirit of God, do cast out the demons, then come already unto you did the reign of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24; Mark 3:27; Luke 11:21–22"> 'Or how is one able to go into the house of the strong man, and to plunder his goods, if first he may not bind the strong man? and then his house he will plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:40; Luke 11:23; Joshua 24:15"> 'He who is not with me is against me, and he who is not gathering with me, doth scatter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Mark 3:28–30; Hebrews 10:29"> Because of this I say to you, all sin and evil speaking shall be forgiven to men, but the evil speaking of the Spirit shall not be forgiven to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 3:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Hebrews 6:4–6"> And whoever may speak a word against the Son of Man it shall be forgiven to him, but whoever may speak against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this age, nor in that which is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 7:16–20; James 4:8"> 'Either make the tree good, and its fruit good, or make the tree bad, and its fruit bad, for from the fruit is the tree known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 15:18; Ephesians 4:29"> 'Brood of vipers! how are ye able to speak good things — being evil? for out of the abundance of the heart doth the mouth speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 15:4; Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 15:28"> The good man out of the good treasure of the heart doth put forth the good things, and the evil man out of the evil treasure doth put forth evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 2:16; Revelation 20:12"> 'And I say to you, that every idle word that men may speak, they shall give for it a reckoning in a day of judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; James 2:21–25"> for from thy words thou shalt be declared righteous, and from thy words thou shalt be declared unrighteous.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12; Luke 11:16"> Then answered certain of the scribes and Pharisees, saying, 'Teacher, we will to see a sign from thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 16:4; Luke 11:29–32; Mark 8:38"> And he answering said to them, 'A generation, evil and adulterous, doth seek a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it, except the sign of Jonah the prophet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jonah 1:17; Matthew 27:63–64; Matthew 16:21"> for, as Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights, so shall the Son of Man be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 12:6"> 'Men of Nineveh shall stand up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it, for they reformed at the proclamation of Jonah, and lo, a greater than Jonah here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1–13; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12; Hebrews 1:2–4"> 'A queen of the south shall rise up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it, for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and lo, a greater than Solomon here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 11:24–26; Luke 8:28–32; Matthew 8:29"> 'And, when the unclean spirit may go forth from the man, it doth walk through dry places seeking rest, and doth not find;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Revelation 13:8–9; Jude 1:4–5"> then it saith, I will turn back to my house whence I came forth; and having come, it findeth &#91;it&#93; unoccupied, swept, and adorned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 11:26; Mark 5:9; Hebrews 6:4–8"> then doth it go, and take with itself seven other spirits more evil than itself, and having gone in they dwell there, and the last of that man doth become worse than the first; so shall it be also to this evil generation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; John 2:12; John 7:3"> And while he was yet speaking to the multitudes, lo, his mother and brethren had stood without, seeking to speak to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> and one said to him, 'Lo, thy mother and thy brethren do stand without, seeking to speak to thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 3:32–33; Matthew 10:37; Luke 2:52"> And he answering said to him who spake to him, 'Who is my mother? and who are my brethren?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 3:34; John 17:8–9; John 20:17–20"> And having stretched forth his hand toward his disciples, he said, 'Lo, my mother and my brethren!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 8:21; Mark 3:35; John 15:14"> for whoever may do the will of my Father who is in the heavens, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.'</span>
