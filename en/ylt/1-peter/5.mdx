<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> be humbled, then, under the powerful hand of God, that you He may exalt in good time,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> whom resist, stedfast in the faith, having known the same sufferings to your brotherhood in the world to be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> And the God of all grace, who did call you to His age-during glory in Christ Jesus, having suffered a little, Himself make you perfect, establish, strengthen, settle &#91;you&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> to Him &#91;is&#93; the glory, and the power — to the ages and the ages! Amen.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> Salute you doth the &#91;assembly&#93; in Babylon jointly elected, and Markus my son.</span>
