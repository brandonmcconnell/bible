<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; James 1:21; James 4:11"> Having put aside, then, all evil, and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envyings, and all evil speakings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–13; Ephesians 4:15; 2 Peter 3:18"> as new-born babes the word's pure milk desire ye, that in it ye may grow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:8; Hebrews 6:5–6; Psalms 63:5"> if so be ye did taste that the Lord &#91;is&#93; gracious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> to whom coming — a living stone — by men, indeed, having been disapproved of, but with God choice, precious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> and ye yourselves, as living stones, are built up, a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> Wherefore, also, it is contained in the Writing: 'Lo, I lay in Zion a chief corner-stone, choice, precious, and he who is believing on him may not be put to shame;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> to you, then, who are believing &#91;is&#93; the preciousness; and to the unbelieving, a stone that the builders disapproved of, this one did become for the head of a corner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> and a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence — who are stumbling at the word, being unbelieving, — to which also they were set;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> and ye &#91;are&#93; a choice race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people acquired, that the excellences ye may shew forth of Him who out of darkness did call you to His wondrous light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> who &#91;were&#93; once not a people, and &#91;are&#93; now the people of God; who had not found kindness, and now have found kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Beloved, I call upon &#91;you&#93;, as strangers and sojourners, to keep from the fleshly desires, that war against the soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> having your behaviour among the nations right, that in that which they speak against you as evil-doers, of the good works having beheld, they may glorify God in a day of inspection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Be subject, then, to every human creation, because of the Lord, whether to a king, as the highest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> whether to governors, as to those sent through him, for punishment, indeed, of evil-doers, and a praise of those doing good;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> because, so is the will of God, doing good, to put to silence the ignorance of the foolish men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> as free, and not having the freedom as the cloak of the evil, but as servants of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> to all give ye honour; the brotherhood love ye; God fear ye; the king honour ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> The domestics! be subjecting yourselves in all fear to the masters, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the cross;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> for this &#91;is&#93; gracious, if because of conscience toward God any one doth endure sorrows, suffering unrighteously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> for what renown &#91;is it&#93;, if sinning and being buffeted, ye do endure &#91;it&#93;? but if, doing good and suffering &#91;for it&#93;, ye do endure, this &#91;is&#93; gracious with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> for to this ye were called, because Christ also did suffer for you, leaving to you an example, that ye may follow his steps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> who did not commit sin, nor was guile found in his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> who being reviled — was not reviling again, suffering — was not threatening, and was committing himself to Him who is judging righteously,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> who our sins himself did bear in his body, upon the tree, that to the sins having died, to the righteousness we may live; by whose stripes ye were healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> for ye were as sheep going astray, but ye turned back now to the shepherd and overseer of your souls.</span>
