<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 1 Peter 4:8; Galatians 5:22"> If with the tongues of men and of messengers I speak, and have not love, I have become brass sounding, or a cymbal tinkling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:3; 1 John 4:20–21"> and if I have prophecy, and know all the secrets, and all the knowledge, and if I have all the faith, so as to remove mountains, and have not love, I am nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:14–17; John 15:13; Matthew 23:5"> and if I give away to feed others all my goods, and if I give up my body that I may be burned, and have not love, I am profited nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 5:21–22"> The love is long-suffering, it is kind, the love doth not envy, the love doth not vaunt itself, is not puffed up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:3–5; 1 John 3:16–17"> doth not act unseemly, doth not seek its own things, is not provoked, doth not impute evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 John 1:4"> rejoiceth not over the unrighteousness, and rejoiceth with the truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4; 2 Timothy 2:24"> all things it beareth, all it believeth, all it hopeth, all it endureth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Galatians 5:6"> The love doth never fail; and whether &#91;there be&#93; prophecies, they shall become useless; whether tongues, they shall cease; whether knowledge, it shall become useless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 2:2–3; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 2:9"> for in part we know, and in part we prophecy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; 2 Corinthians 5:7–8"> and when that which is perfect may come, then that which &#91;is&#93; in part shall become useless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2; Galatians 4:1"> When I was a babe, as a babe I was speaking, as a babe I was thinking, as a babe I was reasoning, and when I have become a man, I have made useless the things of the babe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 3:2; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–10"> for we see now through a mirror obscurely, and then face to face; now I know in part, and then I shall fully know, as also I was known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:14; Colossians 3:14; 1 John 4:7–18"> and now there doth remain faith, hope, love — these three; and the greatest of these &#91;is&#93; love.</span>
