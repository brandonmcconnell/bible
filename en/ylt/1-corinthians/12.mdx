<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> And concerning the spiritual things, brethren, I do not wish you to be ignorant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; Psalms 115:5"> ye have known that ye were nations, unto the dumb idols — as ye were led — being carried away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> wherefore, I give you to understand that no one, in the Spirit of God speaking, saith Jesus &#91;is&#93; anathema, and no one is able to say Jesus &#91;is&#93; Lord, except in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> And there are diversities of gifts, and the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> and there are diversities of ministrations, and the same Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> and there are diversities of workings, and it is the same God — who is working the all in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> And to each hath been given the manifestation of the Spirit for profit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; Psalms 143:10"> for to one through the Spirit hath been given a word of wisdom, and to another a word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> and to another faith in the same Spirit, and to another gifts of healings in the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> and to another in-workings of mighty deeds; and to another prophecy; and to another discernings of spirits; and to another &#91;divers&#93; kinds of tongues; and to another interpretation of tongues:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> and all these doth work the one and the same Spirit, dividing to each severally as he intendeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For, even as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of the one body, being many, are one body, so also &#91;is&#93; the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> for also in one Spirit we all to one body were baptized, whether Jews or Greeks, whether servants or freemen, and all into one Spirit were made to drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> for also the body is not one member, but many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> if the foot may say, 'Because I am not a hand, I am not of the body;' it is not, because of this, not of the body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> and if the ear may say, 'Because I am not an eye, I am not of the body;' it is not, because of this, not of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body &#91;were&#93; an eye, where the hearing? if the whole hearing, where the smelling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> and now, God did set the members each one of them in the body, according as He willed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and if all were one member, where the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> and now, indeed, &#91;are&#93; many members, and one body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> and an eye is not able to say to the hand, 'I have no need of thee;' nor again the head to the feet, 'I have no need of you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> But much more the members of the body which seem to be more infirm are necessary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> and those that we think to be less honourable of the body, around these we put more abundant honour, and our unseemly things have seemliness more abundant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> and our seemly things have no need; but God did temper the body together, to the lacking part having given more abundant honour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same anxiety for one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> and whether one member doth suffer, suffer with &#91;it&#93; do all the members, or one member is glorified, rejoice with &#91;it&#93; do all the members;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> and ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> And some, indeed, did God set in the assembly, first apostles, secondly prophets, thirdly teachers, afterwards powers, afterwards gifts of healings, helpings, governings, divers kinds of tongues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> &#91;are&#93; all apostles? &#91;are&#93; all prophets? &#91;are&#93; all teachers? &#91;are&#93; all powers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> have all gifts of healings? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> and desire earnestly the better gifts; and yet a far excelling way do I shew to you:</span>
