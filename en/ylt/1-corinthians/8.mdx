<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> And concerning the things sacrificed to idols, we have known that we all have knowledge: knowledge puffeth up, but love buildeth up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> and if any one doth think to know anything, he hath not yet known anything according as it behoveth &#91;him&#93; to know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> and if any one doth love God, this one hath been known by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; 1 Corinthians 10:19–20"> Concerning the eating then of the things sacrificed to idols, we have known that an idol &#91;is&#93; nothing in the world, and that there is no other God except one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> for even if there are those called gods, whether in heaven, whether upon earth — as there are gods many and lords many —</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> yet to us &#91;is&#93; one God, the Father, of whom &#91;are&#93; the all things, and we to Him; and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom &#91;are&#93; the all things, and we through Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> but not in all men &#91;is&#93; the knowledge, and certain with conscience of the idol, till now, as a thing sacrificed to an idol do eat &#91;it&#93;, and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> But victuals do not commend us to God, for neither if we may eat are we in advance; nor if we may not eat, are we behind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> but see, lest this privilege of yours may become a stumbling-block to the infirm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> for if any one may see thee that hast knowledge in an idol's temple reclining at meat — shall not his conscience — he being infirm — be emboldened to eat the things sacrificed to idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> and the brother who is infirm shall perish by thy knowledge, because of whom Christ died?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> and thus sinning in regard to the brethren, and smiting their weak conscience — in regard to Christ ye sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 14:21; 2 Corinthians 6:3; 2 Corinthians 11:29"> wherefore, if victuals cause my brother to stumble, I may eat no flesh — to the age — that my brother I may not cause to stumble.</span>
