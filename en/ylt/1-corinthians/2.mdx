<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And I, having come unto you, brethren, came — not in superiority of discourse or wisdom — declaring to you the testimony of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> for I decided not to know any thing among you, except Jesus Christ, and him crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> and I, in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling, was with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> and my word and my preaching was not in persuasive words of human wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> that your faith may not be in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> And wisdom we speak among the perfect, and wisdom not of this age, nor of the rulers of this age — of those becoming useless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> but we speak the hidden wisdom of God in a secret, that God foreordained before the ages to our glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> which no one of the rulers of this age did know, for if they had known, the Lord of the glory they would not have crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; James 1:12"> but, according as it hath been written, 'What eye did not see, and ear did not hear, and upon the heart of man came not up, what God did prepare for those loving Him — '</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> but to us did God reveal &#91;them&#93; through His Spirit, for the Spirit all things doth search, even the depths of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> for who of men hath known the things of the man, except the spirit of the man that &#91;is&#93; in him? so also the things of God no one hath known, except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> And we the spirit of the world did not receive, but the Spirit that &#91;is&#93; of God, that we may know the things conferred by God on us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> which things also we speak, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in those taught by the Holy Spirit, with spiritual things spiritual things comparing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> and the natural man doth not receive the things of the Spirit of God, for to him they are foolishness, and he is not able to know &#91;them&#93;, because spiritually they are discerned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> and he who is spiritual, doth discern indeed all things, and he himself is by no one discerned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> for who did know the mind of the Lord that he shall instruct Him? and we — we have the mind of Christ.</span>
