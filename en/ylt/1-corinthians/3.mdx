<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> And I, brethren, was not able to speak to you as to spiritual, but as to fleshly — as to babes in Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> with milk I fed you, and not with meat, for ye were not yet able, but not even yet are ye now able,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> for yet ye are fleshly, for where &#91;there is&#93; among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not fleshly, and in the manner of men do walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> for when one may say, 'I, indeed, am of Paul;' and another, 'I — of Apollos;' are ye not fleshly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> Who, then, is Paul, and who Apollos, but ministrants through whom ye did believe, and to each as the Lord gave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I planted, Apollos watered, but God was giving growth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> so that neither is he who is planting anything, nor he who is watering, but He who is giving growth — God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> and he who is planting and he who is watering are one, and each his own reward shall receive, according to his own labour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> for of God we are fellow-workmen; God's tillage, God's building ye are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> According to the grace of God that was given to me, as a wise master-builder, a foundation I have laid, and another doth build on &#91;it&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> for other foundation no one is able to lay except that which is laid, which is Jesus the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> and if any one doth build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw —</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> of each the work shall become manifest, for the day shall declare &#91;it&#93;, because in fire it is revealed, and the work of each, what kind it is, the fire shall prove;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> if of any one the work doth remain that he built on &#91;it&#93;, a wage he shall receive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> if of any the work is burned up, he shall suffer loss; and himself shall be saved, but so as through fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> have ye not known that ye are a sanctuary of God, and the Spirit of God doth dwell in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> if any one the sanctuary of God doth waste, him shall God waste; for the sanctuary of God is holy, the which ye are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Let no one deceive himself; if any one doth seem to be wise among you in this age — let him become a fool, that he may become wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> for the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God, for it hath been written, 'Who is taking the wise in their craftiness;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> and again, 'The Lord doth know the reasonings of the wise, that they are vain.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> So then, let no one glory in men, for all things are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things about to be — all are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> and ye &#91;are&#93; Christ's, and Christ &#91;is&#93; God's.</span>
