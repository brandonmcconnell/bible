<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> And I make known to you, brethren, the good news that I proclaimed to you, which also ye did receive, in which also ye have stood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> through which also ye are being saved, in what words I proclaimed good news to you, if ye hold fast, except ye did believe in vain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> for I delivered to you first, what also I did receive, that Christ died for our sins, according to the Writings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalms 16:10–11"> and that he was buried, and that he hath risen on the third day, according to the Writings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> afterwards he appeared to above five hundred brethren at once, of whom the greater part remain till now, and certain also did fall asleep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> afterwards he appeared to James, then to all the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> And last of all — as to the untimely birth — he appeared also to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> for I am the least of the apostles, who am not worthy to be called an apostle, because I did persecute the assembly of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> and by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace that &#91;is&#93; towards me came not in vain, but more abundantly than they all did I labour, yet not I, but the grace of God that &#91;is&#93; with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> whether, then, I or they, so we preach, and so ye did believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> And if Christ is preached, that out of the dead he hath risen, how say certain among you, that there is no rising again of dead persons?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> and if there be no rising again of dead persons, neither hath Christ risen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> and if Christ hath not risen, then void &#91;is&#93; our preaching, and void also your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> and we also are found false witnesses of God, because we did testify of God that He raised up the Christ, whom He did not raise if then dead persons do not rise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> for if dead persons do not rise, neither hath Christ risen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> and if Christ hath not risen, vain is your faith, ye are yet in your sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> then, also, those having fallen asleep in Christ did perish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> if in this life we have hope in Christ only, of all men we are most to be pitied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> And now, Christ hath risen out of the dead — the first-fruits of those sleeping he became,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> for since through man &#91;is&#93; the death, also through man &#91;is&#93; a rising again of the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> for even as in Adam all die, so also in the Christ all shall be made alive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> and each in his proper order, a first-fruit Christ, afterwards those who are the Christ's, in his presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> then — the end, when he may deliver up the reign to God, even the Father, when he may have made useless all rule, and all authority and power —</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> for it behoveth him to reign till he may have put all the enemies under his feet —</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> the last enemy is done away — death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> for all things He did put under his feet, and, when one may say that all things have been subjected, &#91;it is&#93; evident that He is excepted who did subject the all things to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> and when the all things may be subjected to him, then the Son also himself shall be subject to Him, who did subject to him the all things, that God may be the all in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Seeing what shall they do who are baptized for the dead, if the dead do not rise at all? why also are they baptized for the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> why also do we stand in peril every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> Every day do I die, by the glorying of you that I have in Christ Jesus our Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> if after the manner of a man with wild beasts I fought in Ephesus, what the advantage to me if the dead do not rise? let us eat and drink, for to-morrow we die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Be not led astray; evil communications corrupt good manners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> awake up, as is right, and sin not; for certain have an ignorance of God; for shame to you I say &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But some one will say, 'How do the dead rise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> unwise! thou — what thou dost sow is not quickened except it may die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and that which thou dost sow, not the body that shall be dost thou sow, but bare grain, it may be of wheat, or of some one of the others,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> and God doth give to it a body according as He willed, and to each of the seeds its proper body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> All flesh &#91;is&#93; not the same flesh, but there is one flesh of men, and another flesh of beasts, and another of fishes, and another of birds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and &#91;there are&#93; heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies; but one &#91;is&#93; the glory of the heavenly, and another that of the earthly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalms 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> one glory of sun, and another glory of moon, and another glory of stars, for star from star doth differ in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> So also &#91;is&#93; the rising again of the dead: it is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> it is sown in dishonour, it is raised in glory; it is sown in weakness, it is raised in power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> it is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body; there is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> so also it hath been written, 'The first man Adam became a living creature,' the last Adam &#91;is&#93; for a life-giving spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> but that which is spiritual &#91;is&#93; not first, but that which &#91;was&#93; natural, afterwards that which &#91;is&#93; spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man &#91;is&#93; out of the earth, earthy; the second man &#91;is&#93; the Lord out of heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> as &#91;is&#93; the earthy, such &#91;are&#93; also the earthy; and as &#91;is&#93; the heavenly, such &#91;are&#93; also the heavenly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> and, according as we did bear the image of the earthy, we shall bear also the image of the heavenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> And this I say, brethren, that flesh and blood the reign of God is not able to inherit, nor doth the corruption inherit the incorruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> lo, I tell you a secret; we indeed shall not all sleep, and we all shall be changed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, in the last trumpet, for it shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we — we shall be changed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> for it behoveth this corruptible to put on incorruption, and this mortal to put on immortality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> and when this corruptible may have put on incorruption, and this mortal may have put on immortality, then shall be brought to pass the word that hath been written, 'The Death was swallowed up — to victory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalms 89:48; Acts 2:27"> where, O Death, thy sting? where, O Hades, thy victory?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> and the sting of the death &#91;is&#93; the sin, and the power of the sin the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> and to God — thanks, to Him who is giving us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> so that, my brethren beloved, become ye stedfast, unmovable, abounding in the work of the Lord at all times, knowing that your labour is not vain in the Lord.</span>
