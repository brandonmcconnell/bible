<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 102:13; Zechariah 1:17; Zechariah 8:22–23"> Because Jehovah loveth Jacob, And hath fixed again on Israel, And given them rest on their own land, And joined hath been the sojourner to them, And they have been admitted to the house of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> And peoples have taken them, And have brought them in unto their place, And the house of Israel have inherited them, On the land of Jehovah, For men-servants and for maid-servants, And they have been captors of their captors, And have ruled over their exactors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:10; Isaiah 32:18; Ezra 9:8–9"> And it hath come to pass, In the day of Jehovah's giving rest to thee, From thy grief, and from thy trouble, And from the sharp bondage, That hath been served upon thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Habakkuk 1:2–10; Jeremiah 51:34–35"> That thou hast taken up this simile Concerning the king of Babylon, and said, How hath the exactor ceased,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Isaiah 9:4; Jeremiah 48:15–17"> Ceased hath the golden one. Broken hath Jehovah the staff of the wicked, The sceptre of rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–21; James 2:13; Revelation 17:16–17"> He who is smiting peoples in wrath, A smiting without intermission, He who is ruling in anger nations, Pursuing without restraint!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Psalms 98:7–9; Psalms 96:11–13"> At rest — quiet hath been all the earth, They have broken forth &#91;into&#93; singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Isaiah 55:12–13; Zechariah 11:2"> Even firs have rejoiced over thee, Cedars of Lebanon — &#91;saying&#93;: Since thou hast lain down, The hewer cometh not up against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:21–32; Jeremiah 50:8; Proverbs 15:24"> Sheol beneath hath been troubled at thee, To meet thy coming in, It is waking up for thee Rephaim, All chiefs ones of earth, It hath raised up from their thrones All kings of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–14; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 82:6–7"> All of them answer and say unto thee, Even thou hast become weak like us! Unto us thou hast become like!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:25–30; Job 21:11–15; Isaiah 66:24"> Brought down to Sheol hath been thine excellency, The noise of thy psaltery, Under thee spread out hath been the worm, Yea, covering thee is the worm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13–17; Revelation 12:7–10; Luke 10:18"> How hast thou fallen from the heavens, O shining one, son of the dawn! Thou hast been cut down to earth, O weakener of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–12; Ezekiel 28:2; Ezekiel 28:12–16"> And thou saidst in thy heart: the heavens I go up, Above stars of God I raise my throne, And I sit in the mount of meeting in the sides of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 47:8; Genesis 3:5; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> I go up above the heights of a thick cloud, I am like to the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 11:23; Ezekiel 32:23; Acts 12:22–23"> Only — unto Sheol thou art brought down, Unto the sides of the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:23; Psalms 58:10–11; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> Thy beholders look to thee, to thee they attend, Is this the man causing the earth to tremble, Shaking kingdoms?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:3; Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 45:13"> He hath made the world as a wilderness, And his cities he hath broken down, Of his bound ones he opened not the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Isaiah 22:16; Ezekiel 32:18–32"> All kings of nations — all of them, Have lain down in honour, each in his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:9; Jeremiah 41:7; 1 Kings 21:24"> And — thou hast been cast out of thy grave, As an abominable branch, raiment of the slain, Thrust through ones of the sword, Going down unto the sons of the pit, As a carcase trodden down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 21:10; Job 18:19; Psalms 37:28"> Thou art not united with them in burial, For thy land thou hast destroyed, Thy people thou hast slain, Not named to the age is the seed of evil doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; Exodus 20:5; Habakkuk 2:8–12"> Prepare ye for his sons slaughter; Because of the iniquity of their fathers, They rise not, nor have possessed the land, Nor filled the face of the world &#91;with&#93; cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29–35; Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 14:10"> And I have risen up against them, (The affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts,) And have cut off, in reference to Babylon, Name and remnant, and continuator and successor, The affirmation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 13:21–22; 1 Kings 14:10"> And have made it for a possession of a bittern, And ponds of waters, And daubed it with the mire of destruction, The affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Job 23:13; Isaiah 46:10–11"> Sworn hath Jehovah of Hosts, saying, 'As I thought — so hath it not been? And as I counselled — it standeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Nahum 1:13; Isaiah 37:36–38"> To break Asshur in My land, And on My mountains I tread him down, And turned from off them hath his yoke, Yea, his burden from off their shoulder turneth aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:9; Zephaniah 3:6–8; Exodus 15:12"> This &#91;is&#93; the counsel that is counselled for all the earth, And this &#91;is&#93; the hand that is stretched out for all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Job 23:13; 2 Chronicles 20:6"> For Jehovah of Hosts hath purposed, And who doth make void? And His hand that is stretched out, Who doth turn it back?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:20; Isaiah 13:1; 2 Chronicles 28:27"> In the year of the death of king Ahaz was this burden:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 30:6; Isaiah 11:8; 2 Chronicles 26:6"> Rejoice not thou, Philistia, all of thee, That broken hath been the rod of thy smiter, For from the root of a serpent cometh out a viper, And its fruit &#91;is&#93; a flying saraph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Isaiah 5:17"> And delighted have the first-born of the poor, And the needy in confidence lie down, And I have put to death with famine thy root, And thy remnant it slayeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:14; Isaiah 20:1; Isaiah 14:29"> Howl, O gate; cry, O city, Melted art thou, Philistia, all of thee, For from the north smoke hath come, And there is none alone in his set places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 87:5; James 2:5"> And what doth one answer the messengers of a nation? 'That Jehovah hath founded Zion, And in it do the poor of His people trust!'</span>
