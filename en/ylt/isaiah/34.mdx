<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Isaiah 41:1; Isaiah 1:2"> Come near, ye nations, to hear, And ye peoples, give attention, Hear doth the earth and its fulness, The world, and all its productions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:15–29; Zephaniah 3:8; Zechariah 14:12–16"> For wrath &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah against all the nations, And fury against all their host, He hath devoted them to destruction, He hath given them to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:7"> And their wounded are cast out, And their carcases cause their stench to ascend, And melted have been mountains from their blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 6:13–14; Matthew 24:29; Ezekiel 32:7–8"> And consumed have been all the host of the heavens, And rolled together as a book have been the heavens, And all their hosts do fade, As the fading of a leaf of a vine, And as the fading one of a fig-tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Malachi 1:4; Isaiah 63:1"> For soaked in the heavens was My sword, Lo, on Edom it cometh down, On the people of My curse for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1; Jeremiah 49:13; Isaiah 34:5"> A sword &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah — it hath been full of blood, It hath been made fat with fatness, With blood of lambs and he-goats. With fat of kidneys of rams, For a sacrifice &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah in Bozrah, And a great slaughter in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremiah 50:27"> And come down have reems with them, And bullocks with bulls, And soaked hath been their land from blood, And their dust from fatness is made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:4; Isaiah 61:2; Jeremiah 46:10"> (For a day of vengeance &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah, A year of recompences for Zion's strife,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> And turned have been her streams to pitch, And her dust to brimstone, And her land hath become burning pitch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 13:20; Malachi 1:3–4"> By night and by day she is not quenched, To the age go up doth her smoke, From generation to generation she is waste, For ever and ever, none is passing into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:8; 2 Kings 21:13; Zephaniah 2:14"> And possess her do pelican and hedge-hog, And owl and raven dwell in her, And He hath stretched out over her A line of vacancy, and stones of emptiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6–8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; Jeremiah 39:6"> &#91;To&#93; the kingdom her freemen they call, But there are none there, And all her princes are at an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 10:22; Malachi 1:3"> And gone up her palaces have thorns, Nettle and bramble &#91;are&#93; in her fortresses, And it hath been a habitation of dragons, A court for daughters of an ostrich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:21–22"> And met have Ziim with Aiim, And the goat for its companion calleth, Only there rested hath the night-owl, And hath found for herself a place of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:13"> There made her nest hath the bittern, Yea, she layeth, and hath hatched, And hath gathered under her shadow, Only there gathered have been vultures, Each with its companion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Joshua 1:8; Matthew 5:18"> Seek out of the book of Jehovah, and read, One of these hath not been lacking, None hath missed its companion, For My mouth — it hath commanded, And His spirit — He hath gathered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Acts 17:26; Isaiah 17:14"> And He hath cast for them a lot, And His hand hath apportioned &#91;it&#93; to them by line, Unto the age they possess it, To all generations they dwell in it!</span>
