<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Habakkuk 1:1"> The thing that Isaiah son of Amoz hath seen concerning Judah and Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> And it hath come to pass, In the latter end of the days, Established is the mount of Jehovah's house, Above the top of the mounts, And it hath been lifted up above the heights, And flowed unto it have all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zechariah 8:20–23"> And gone have many peoples and said, 'Come, and we go up unto the mount of Jehovah, Unto the house of the God of Jacob, And He doth teach us of His ways, And we walk in His paths, For from Zion goeth forth a law, And a word of Jehovah from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:18; Micah 4:3; Isaiah 11:6–9"> And He hath judged between the nations, And hath given a decision to many peoples, And they have beat their swords to ploughshares, And their spears to pruning-hooks, Nation doth not lift up sword unto nation, Nor do they learn any more — war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Luke 1:79; Isaiah 60:19–20"> O house of Jacob, come, And we walk in the light of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Kings 1:2; Psalms 106:35"> For Thou hast left Thy people, the house of Jacob. For they have been filled from the east, And &#91;are&#93; sorcerers like the Philistines, And with the children of strangers strike hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Isaiah 30:16; Isaiah 31:1"> And its land is full of silver and gold, And there is no end to its treasures, And its land is full of horses, And there is no end to its chariots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremiah 2:28"> And its land is full of idols, To the work of its hands it boweth itself, To that which its fingers have made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> And the low boweth down, and the high is humbled, And Thou acceptest them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Isaiah 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> Enter into a rock, and be hidden in dust, Because of the fear of Jehovah, And because of the honour of His excellency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15–16; Psalms 18:27; Isaiah 2:17"> The haughty eyes of man have been humbled, And bowed down hath been the loftiness of men, And set on high hath Jehovah alone been in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> For a day &#91;is&#93; to Jehovah of Hosts, For every proud and high one, And for every lifted up and low one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Zechariah 11:1–2; Ezekiel 31:3–12"> And for all cedars of Lebanon, The high and the exalted ones, And for all oaks of Bashan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:25; Isaiah 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> And for all the high mountains, And for all the exalted heights,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 25:12"> And for every high tower, And for every fenced wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; Isaiah 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> And for all ships of Tarshish, And for all desirable pictures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:29–30; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 13:11"> And bowed down hath been the haughtiness of man, And humbled the loftiness of men, And set on high hath Jehovah alone been in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 21:9"> And the idols — they completely pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8"> And &#91;men&#93; have entered into caverns of rocks, And into caves of dust, Because of the fear of Jehovah, And because of the honour of His excellency, In His rising to terrify the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 46:6"> In that day doth man cast his idols of silver, And his idols of gold, That they have made for him to worship, To moles, and to bats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Song of Solomon 2:14; Isaiah 2:10"> To enter into cavities of the rocks, And into clefts of the high places, Because of the fear of Jehovah, And because of the honour of His excellency, In His rising to terrify the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalms 146:3; James 4:14"> Cease for you from man, Whose breath &#91;is&#93; in his nostrils, For — in what is he esteemed?</span>
