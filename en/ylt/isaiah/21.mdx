<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 51:42"> The burden of the wilderness of the sea. 'Like hurricanes in the south for passing through, From the wilderness it hath come, From a fearful land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Jeremiah 49:34; Psalms 60:3"> A hard vision hath been declared to me, The treacherous dealer is dealing treacherously, And the destroyer is destroying. Go up, O Elam, besiege, O Media, All its sighing I have caused to cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6; Isaiah 26:17"> Therefore filled have been my loins &#91;with&#93; great pain, Pangs have seized me as pangs of a travailing woman, I have been bent down by hearing, I have been troubled by seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; 1 Samuel 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> Wandered hath my heart, trembling hath terrified me, The twilight of my desire He hath made a fear to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> Arrange the table, watch in the watch-tower, Eat, drink, rise, ye heads, anoint the shield,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:2–7; Ezekiel 3:17; Habakkuk 2:1–2"> For thus said the Lord unto me: 'Go, station the watchman, That which he seeth let him declare.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Isaiah 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> And he hath seen a chariot — a couple of horsemen, The rider of an ass, the rider of a camel, And he hath given attention — He hath increased attention!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1–2; Isaiah 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> And he crieth — a lion, 'On a watch-tower my lord, I am standing continually by day, And on my ward I am stationed whole nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8"> And lo, this, the chariot of a man is coming, A couple of horsemen.' And he answereth and saith: 'Fallen, fallen hath Babylon, And all the graven images of her gods He hath broken to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:13; 2 Kings 13:7"> O my threshing, and the son of my floor, That which I heard from Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel, I have declared to you!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obadiah 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> The burden of Dumah. Unto me is &#91;one&#93; calling from Seir 'Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> The watchman hath said, 'Come hath morning, and also night, If ye inquire, inquire ye, turn back, come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23–24; 1 Chronicles 1:32; Ezekiel 27:15"> The burden on Arabia. In a forest in Arabia ye lodge, O travellers of Dedanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> To meet the thirsty brought water have Inhabitants of the land of Tema, With his bread they came before a fugitive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Isaiah 13:14"> For from the face of destructions they fled, From the face of a stretched-out sword, And from the face of a trodden bow, And from the face of the grievousness of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 16:14"> For thus said the Lord unto me: 'Within a year, as years of a hireling, Consumed hath been all the honour of Kedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> And the remnant of the number of bow-men, The mighty of the sons of Kedar are few, For Jehovah, God of Israel, hath spoken!'</span>
