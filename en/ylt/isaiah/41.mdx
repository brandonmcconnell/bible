<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13"> Keep silent towards Me, O isles, And the peoples pass on &#91;to&#93; power, They come nigh, then they speak, 'Together — to judgment we draw near.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; 2 Samuel 22:43; Ezra 1:2"> Who stirred up from the east a righteous one? He calleth him to His foot, He giveth before him nations, And kings He causeth him to rule, He giveth &#91;them&#93; as dust &#91;to&#93; his sword, As driven stubble &#91;to&#93; his bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 5:24; Isaiah 57:2"> He pursueth them, he passeth over in safety A path with his feet he entereth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 48:12; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:17"> Who hath wrought and done, Calling the generations from the first? I, Jehovah, the first, and with the last I &#91;am&#93; He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15–16; Psalms 65:8; Psalms 66:3"> Seen have isles and fear, ends of the earth tremble, They have drawn near, yea, they come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Isaiah 40:19; Daniel 3:1–7"> Each his neighbour they help, And to his brother he saith, 'Be strong.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:19–20; Daniel 3:1–7; Judges 18:17–18"> And strengthen doth an artisan the refiner, A smoother &#91;with&#93; a hammer, Him who is beating &#91;on&#93; an anvil, Saying, 'For joining it &#91;is&#93; good,' And he strengtheneth it with nails, it is not moved!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 2:23; 2 Chronicles 20:7; Deuteronomy 14:2"> — And thou, O Israel, My servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen, Seed of Abraham, My lover,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 94:14; Deuteronomy 7:6–7"> Whom I have taken hold of, from the ends of the earth, And from its near places I have called thee, And I say to thee, My servant Thou &#91;art&#93;, I have chosen thee, and not rejected thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 43:1–2; Romans 8:31"> Be not afraid, for with thee I &#91;am&#93;, Look not around, for I &#91;am&#93; thy God, I have strengthened thee, Yea, I have helped thee, yea, I upheld thee, With the right hand of My righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 23:22; Isaiah 45:24; Revelation 3:9"> Lo, all those displeased with thee, They are ashamed and blush, They are as nothing, yea, perish Do the men who strive with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 20:7–9; Isaiah 17:14"> Thou seekest them, and findest them not, The men who debate with thee, They are as nothing, yea, as nothing, The men who war with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 73:23; Psalms 63:8"> For I, Jehovah thy God, Am strengthening thy right hand, He who is saying to thee, 'Fear not, I have helped thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Psalms 22:6; Isaiah 54:5"> Fear not, O worm Jacob, ye men of Israel, I helped thee, an affirmation of Jehovah, Even thy redeemer, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 4:13; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Zechariah 4:7"> Lo, I have set thee for a new sharp threshing instrument, Possessing teeth, thou threshest mountains, And beatest small, and hills as chaff thou makest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:2; Isaiah 61:10–11; Isaiah 45:24–25"> Thou winnowest them, and a wind lifteth them up, And a whirlwind scattereth them, And thou — thou rejoicest in Jehovah, In the Holy One of Israel dost boast thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; Isaiah 30:19; Isaiah 44:3"> The poor and the needy are seeking water, And there is none, Their tongue with thirst hath failed, I, Jehovah do answer them, The God of Israel — I forsake them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 58:11"> I open on high places rivers, And in midst of valleys fountains, I make a wilderness become a pond of water, And a dry land become springs of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:13; Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 35:1"> I give in a wilderness the cedar, Shittah, and myrtle, and oil-tree, I set in a desert the fir-pine and box-wood together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 12:9; Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 43:21"> So that they see, and know, And regard, and act wisely together, For the hand of Jehovah hath done this, And the Holy One of Israel hath prepared it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 23:3–4; Job 38:3; Micah 6:1–2"> Bring near your cause, saith Jehovah, Bring nigh your mighty ones, saith the king of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21; Isaiah 48:14; Isaiah 44:7"> They bring nigh, and declare to us that which doth happen, The first things — what they &#91;are&#93; declare ye, And we set our heart, and know their latter end, Or the coming things cause us to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:19; Isaiah 42:9; Jeremiah 10:5"> Declare the things that are coming hereafter, And we know that ye &#91;are&#93; gods, Yea, ye may do good or do evil, And we look around and see &#91;it&#93; together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 115:8; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 41:29"> Lo, ye &#91;are&#93; of nothing, and your work of nought, An abomination — it fixeth on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; 2 Samuel 22:43; Isaiah 10:6"> I have stirred up &#91;one&#93; from the north, And he cometh, From the rising of the sun he calleth in My name, And he cometh in &#91;on&#93; prefects as &#91;on&#93; clay, And as a potter treadeth down mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 44:7; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 45:21"> Who hath declared from the first, and we know? And beforetime, and we say, 'Righteous?' yea, there is none declaring, Yea, there is none proclaiming, Yea, there is none hearing your sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 52:7; Isaiah 44:28"> First to Zion, Behold, behold them, And to Jerusalem one proclaiming tidings I give,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Daniel 2:10–11; Isaiah 40:13–14"> And I see that there is no man, Yea, of these that there is no counsellor, And I ask them, and they return word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:13; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:2–16"> 'Lo, all of them &#91;are&#93; vanity, Nought &#91;are&#93; their works, Wind and emptiness their molten images!'</span>
