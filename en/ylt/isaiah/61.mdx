<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:18–19; Luke 7:22; Isaiah 42:7"> The Spirit of the Lord Jehovah &#91;is&#93; on me, Because Jehovah did anoint me To proclaim tidings to the humble, He sent me to bind the broken of heart, To proclaim to captives liberty, And to bound ones an opening of bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Isaiah 34:8; Luke 4:19"> To proclaim the year of the good pleasure of Jehovah, And the day of vengeance of our God, To comfort all mourners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Jeremiah 17:7–8; John 16:20"> To appoint to mourners in Zion, To give to them beauty instead of ashes, The oil of joy instead of mourning, A covering of praise for a spirit of weakness, And He is calling to them, 'Trees of righteousness, The planting of Jehovah — to be beautified.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:33–36; Amos 9:14–15"> And they have built the wastes of old, The desolations of the ancients they raise up, And they have renewed waste cities, The desolations of generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10–14; Isaiah 14:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–20"> And strangers have stood and fed your flock, Sons of a foreigner &#91;are&#93; your husbandmen, And your vine-dressers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 19:6; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> And ye are called 'Priests of Jehovah,' 'Ministers of our God,' is said of you, The strength of nations ye consume, And in their honour ye do boast yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:10; Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 40:2"> Instead of your shame and confusion, A second time they sing of their portion, Therefore in their land A second time do they take possession, Joy age-during &#91;is&#93; for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Jeremiah 9:24; Zechariah 8:16–17"> For I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, loving judgment, Hating plunder for a burnt-offering, And I have given their wage in truth, And a covenant age-during I make for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:18"> And known among nations hath been their seed, And their offspring in the midst of the peoples, All their beholders acknowledge them, For they &#91;are&#93; a seed Jehovah hath blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Psalms 132:9; Psalms 132:16"> I greatly rejoice in Jehovah, Joy doth my soul in my God, For He clothed me with garments of salvation, With a robe of righteousness covereth Me, As a bridegroom prepareth ornaments, And as a bride putteth on her jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 4:26–32; Isaiah 58:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> For, as the earth bringeth forth her shoots, And as a garden causeth its sown things to shoot up, So the Lord Jehovah causeth righteousness and praise To shoot up before all the nations!</span>
