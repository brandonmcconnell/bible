<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Jeremiah 3:8; Matthew 18:25"> Thus said Jehovah: 'Where &#91;is&#93; this — the bill of your mother's divorce, Whom I sent away? Or to which of My creditors have I sold you? Lo, for your iniquities ye have been sold, And for your transgressions Hath your mother been sent away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 66:4; Numbers 11:23"> Wherefore have I come, and there is no one? I called, and there is none answering, Hath My hand been at all short of redemption? And is there not in me power to deliver? Lo, by My rebuke I dry up a sea, I make rivers a wilderness, Their fish stinketh, for there is no water, And dieth with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:12; Psalms 18:11–12; Exodus 10:21"> I clothe the heavens &#91;with&#93; blackness, And sackcloth I make their covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Jeremiah 1:9; John 7:15–17"> The Lord Jehovah hath given to me The tongue of taught ones, To know to aid the weary &#91;by&#93; a word, He waketh morning by morning, He waketh for me an ear to hear as taught ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:31; Hebrews 5:8; Isaiah 35:5"> The Lord Jehovah opened for me the ear, And I rebelled not — backward I moved not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Mark 14:65; Mark 15:19"> My back I have given to those smiting, And my cheeks to those plucking out, My face I hid not from shame and spitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Ezekiel 3:8–9; John 16:33"> And the Lord Jehovah giveth help to me, Therefore I have not been ashamed, Therefore I have set my face as a flint, And I know that I am not ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:32–34; Revelation 12:10; Isaiah 41:1"> Near &#91;is&#93; He who is justifying me, Who doth contend with me? We stand together, who &#91;is&#93; mine opponent? Let him come nigh unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:28; Isaiah 41:10; Hebrews 1:11–12"> Lo, the Lord Jehovah giveth help to me, Who &#91;is&#93; he that declareth me wicked? Lo, all of them as a garment wear out, A moth doth eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 12:46; Micah 7:7–9; Isaiah 26:3–4"> Who &#91;is&#93; among you, fearing Jehovah, Hearkening to the voice of His servant, That hath walked in dark places, And there is no brightness for him? Let him trust in the name of Jehovah, And lean upon his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–7; Romans 1:21–22; Romans 10:3"> Lo, all ye kindling a fire, girding on sparks, Walk ye in the light of your fire, And in the sparks ye have caused to burn, From my hand hath this been to you, In grief ye lie down!</span>
