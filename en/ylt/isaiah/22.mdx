<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 125:2; Joel 3:12"> The burden of the Valley of Vision. What — to thee, now, that thou hast gone up, All of thee — to the roofs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:20"> Full of stirs — a noisy city — an exulting city, Thy pierced are not pierced of the sword, Nor dead in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; Jeremiah 39:4–7"> All thy rulers fled together from the bow, Bound have been all found of thee, They have been kept bound together, Afar off they have fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Matthew 2:18; Psalms 77:2"> Therefore I said, 'Look ye from me, I am bitter in my weeping, Haste not to comfort me, For the destruction of the daughter of my people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; Luke 23:30"> For a day of noise, and of treading down, And of perplexity, &#91;is&#93; to the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, In the valley of vision, digging down a wall, And crying unto the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; Amos 1:5"> And Elam hath borne a quiver, In a chariot of men — horsemen, And Kir hath exposed a shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> And it cometh to pass, The choice of thy valleys have been full of chariots, And the horsemen place themselves diligently at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> And one removeth the covering of Judah, And thou lookest in that day Unto the armour of the house of the forest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:1–6"> And the breaches of the city of David ye have seen, For they have become many, And ye gather the waters of the lower pool,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the houses of Jerusalem ye did number, And ye break down the houses to fence the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Jeremiah 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> And a ditch ye made between the two walls, For the waters of the old pool, And ye have not looked unto its Maker, And its Framer of old ye have not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Micah 1:16; Joel 1:13"> And call doth the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, In that day, to weeping and to lamentation, And to baldness and to girding on of sackcloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; Luke 17:26–29"> And lo, joy and gladness, slaying of oxen, And slaughtering of sheep, Eating of flesh, and drinking of wine, Eat and drink, for to-morrow we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:13; 1 Samuel 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> And revealed it hath been in mine ears, &#91;By&#93; Jehovah of Hosts: Not pardoned is this iniquity to you, Till ye die, said the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 19:2"> Thus said the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts: 'Go, enter in unto this steward, Unto Shebna, who &#91;is&#93; over the house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:60; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Samuel 18:18"> What — to thee here? And who — to thee here? That thou hast hewn out to thee here — a sepulchre? Hewing on high his sepulchre, Graving in a rock a dwelling for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3; Job 9:24; Esther 7:8"> Lo, Jehovah is casting thee up and down, A casting up and down, O mighty one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Amos 7:17"> And thy coverer covering, wrapping round, Wrappeth thee round, O babbler, On a land broad of sides — there thou diest, And there the chariots of thine honour &#91;Are&#93; the shame of the house of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:6–7; Job 40:11–12"> And I have thrust thee from thy station, And from thine office he throweth thee down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 Kings 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> And it hath come to pass, in that day, That I have called to my servant, To Eliakim son of Hilkiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; Genesis 41:42–43"> And I have clothed him with thy coat, And with thy girdle I strengthen him, And thy garment I give into his hand, And he hath been for a father to the inhabitant of Jerusalem, And to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:18–19; Revelation 3:7; Job 12:14"> And I have placed the key Of the house of David on his shoulder, And he hath opened, and none is shutting, And hath shut, and none is opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; Job 36:7; Zechariah 10:4"> And I have fixed him a nail in a stedfast place, And he hath been for a throne of honour To the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:1–3; John 20:21–23; Matthew 28:18"> And they have hanged on him All the honour of the house of his father, The offspring and the issue, All vessels of small quality, From vessels of basins to all vessels of flagons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; Psalms 146:3"> In that day — an affirmation of Jehovah of Hosts, Moved is the nail that is fixed In a stedfast place, Yea, it hath been cut down, and hath fallen, And cut off hath been the burden that &#91;is&#93; on it, For Jehovah hath spoken!'</span>
