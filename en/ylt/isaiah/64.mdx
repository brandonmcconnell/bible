<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Mark 1:10; Revelation 20:11"> Didst Thou not rend the heavens? Thou didst come down, From thy presence did mountains flow,</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalms 65:5–6; Psalms 66:3"> In Thy doing fearful things — we expect not, Thou didst come down, From Thy presence did mountains flow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalms 31:19; James 5:7"> Even from antiquity &#91;men&#93; have not heard, They have not given ear, Eye hath not seen a God save Thee, He doth work for those waiting for Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> Thou hast met with the rejoicer And the doer of righteousness, In Thy ways they remember Thee, Lo, Thou hast been wroth when we sin, By them &#91;is&#93; continuance, and we are saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> And we are as unclean — all of us, And as a garment passing away, all our righteous acts; And we fade as a leaf — all of us. And our iniquities as wind do take us away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> And there is none calling in Thy name, Stirring up himself to lay hold on Thee, For Thou hast hid Thy face from us, And thou meltest us away by our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> And now, O Jehovah, thou &#91;art&#93; our Father, We &#91;are&#93; the clay, and Thou our Framer, And the work of Thy hand — all of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalms 74:1–2; Psalms 79:13"> Be not wroth, O Jehovah, very sore, Nor for ever remember iniquity, Lo, look attentively, we beseech Thee, Thy people &#91;are&#93; we all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Thy holy cities have been a wilderness, Zion a wilderness hath been, Jerusalem a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> Our holy and our beautiful house, Where praise Thee did our fathers, Hath become burnt with fire, And all our desirable things have become a waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalms 74:10–11; Psalms 83:1"> For these dost Thou refrain Thyself, Jehovah? Thou art silent, and dost afflict us very sore!'</span>
