<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Isaiah 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> 'Who &#91;is&#93; this coming from Edom? With dyed garments from Bozrah? This that is honourable in his clothing, Travelling in the abundance of his power?' — 'I, speaking in righteousness, mighty to save.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> 'Wherefore &#91;is&#93; thy clothing red? And thy garments as treading in a wine fat?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> — 'A wine-press I have trodden by myself, And of the peoples there is no one with me, And I tread them in mine anger, And I trample them in my fury, Sprinkled is their strength on my garments, And all my clothing I have polluted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of vengeance &#91;is&#93; in my heart, And the year of my redeemed hath come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I look attentively, and there is none helping, And I am astonished that there is none supporting, And give salvation to me doth mine own arm. And my wrath — it hath supported me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> And I tread down peoples in mine anger, And I make them drunk in my fury, And I bring down to earth their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> The kind acts of Jehovah I make mention of, The praises of Jehovah, According to all that Jehovah hath done for us, And the abundance of the goodness to the house of Israel, That He hath done for them, According to His mercies, And according to the abundance of His kind acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> And He saith, Only My people they &#91;are&#93;, Sons — they lie not, and He is to them for a saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> In all their distress &#91;He is&#93; no adversary, And the messenger of His presence saved them, In His love and in His pity He redeemed them, And He doth lift them up, And beareth them all the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> And they have rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit, And He turneth to them for an enemy, He Himself hath fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> And He remembereth the days of old, Moses — his people. Where &#91;is&#93; He who is bringing them up from the sea, The shepherd of his flock? Where &#91;is&#93; He who is putting in its midst His Holy Spirit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> Leading by the right hand of Moses, the arm of His glory, Cleaving waters from before them, To make to Himself a name age-during.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> Leading them through the depths, As a horse in a plain they stumble not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> As a beast into a valley goeth down, The Spirit of Jehovah causeth him to rest, So hast Thou led Thy people, To make to Thyself a glorious name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Look attentively from the heavens, And see from Thy holy and beauteous habitation, Where &#91;is&#93; Thy zeal and Thy might? The multitude of Thy bowels and Thy mercies Towards me have refrained themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> For Thou &#91;art&#93; our Father, For Abraham hath not known us, And Israel doth not acknowledge us, Thou, O Jehovah, &#91;art&#93; our Father, Our redeemer from the age, &#91;is&#93; Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Why causest Thou us to wander, O Jehovah, from Thy ways? Thou hardenest our heart from Thy fear, Turn back for Thy servants' sake, The tribes of Thine inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> For a little while did Thy holy people possess, Our adversaries have trodden down Thy sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> We have been from of old, Thou hast not ruled over them, Not called is Thy name upon them!</span>
