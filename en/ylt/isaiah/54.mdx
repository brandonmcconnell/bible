<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:27; Isaiah 62:4; 1 Samuel 2:5"> Sing, O barren, she hath not borne! Break forth with singing, and cry aloud, She hath not brought forth! For more &#91;are&#93; the sons of the desolate, Than the sons of the married one, said Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:20; Jeremiah 10:20; Isaiah 49:19–20"> Enlarge the place of thy tent, And the curtains of thy tabernacles they stretch out, Restrain not — lengthen thy cords, And thy pins make strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35–36; Genesis 28:14; Isaiah 43:5–6"> For right and left thou breakest forth, And thy seed doth nations possess,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 1 Peter 2:6; Jeremiah 31:19"> And desolate cities they cause to be inhabited. Fear not, for thou art not ashamed, Nor blush, for thou art not confounded, For the shame of thy youth thou forgettest, And the reproach of thy widowhood Thou dost not remember any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25–27; Hosea 2:19–20; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> For thy Maker &#91;is&#93; thy husband, Jehovah of Hosts &#91;is&#93; His name, And thy Redeemer &#91;is&#93; the Holy One of Israel, 'God of all the earth,' He is called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Hosea 2:14–15"> For, as a woman forsaken and grieved in spirit, Called thee hath Jehovah, Even a youthful wife when she is refused, said thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; 2 Peter 3:8; Micah 4:6"> In a small moment I have forsaken thee, And in great mercies I do gather thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:3; Isaiah 60:10; Ezekiel 39:23–24"> In overflowing wrath I hid my face &#91;for&#93; a moment from thee, And in kindness age-during I have loved thee, Said thy Redeemer — Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Genesis 8:21; Hebrews 6:16–18"> For, the waters of Noah &#91;is&#93; this to Me, In that I have sworn — the waters of Noah Do not pass again over the earth — So I have sworn, Wrath is not upon thee, Nor rebuke against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:33–34; Psalms 46:2; Ephesians 2:4–5"> For the mountains depart, and the hills remove, And My kindness from thee departeth not, And the covenant of My peace removeth not, Said hath thy loving one — Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–6; Acts 14:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> O afflicted, storm-tossed, not comforted, Lo, I am laying with cement thy stones, And have founded thee with sapphires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And have made of agate thy pinnacles, And thy gates of carbuncle stones, And all thy border of stones of delight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:45; Philippians 4:7; Psalms 119:165"> And all thy sons are taught of Jehovah, And abundant &#91;is&#93; the peace of thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Jeremiah 30:10; Zechariah 9:8"> In righteousness thou establishest thyself, Be far from oppression, for thou fearest not, And from ruin, for it cometh not near unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–16; Psalms 37:12–13; Zechariah 12:9"> Lo, he doth diligently assemble without My desire, Who hath assembled near thee? By thee he falleth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Isaiah 46:11"> Lo, I — I have prepared an artisan, Blowing on a fire of coals, And bringing out an instrument for his work, And I have prepared a destroyer to destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; Isaiah 54:15; John 10:28–30"> No weapon formed against thee prospereth, And every tongue rising against thee, In judgment thou condemnest. This &#91;is&#93; the inheritance of the servants of Jehovah, And their righteousness from me, an affirmation of Jehovah!</span>
