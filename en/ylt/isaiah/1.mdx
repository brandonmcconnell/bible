<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:1–21; 2 Chronicles 26:1–33; Isaiah 6:1"> The Visions of Isaiah son of Amoz, that he hath seen concerning Judah and Jerusalem, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah, kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 30:19; Micah 1:2"> Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth, For Jehovah hath spoken: Sons I have nourished and brought up, And they — they transgressed against Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 44:18"> An ox hath known its owner, And an ass the crib of its master, Israel hath not known, My people hath not understood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:13"> Ah, sinning nation, a people heavy &#91;with&#93; iniquity, A seed of evil doers, sons — corrupters! They have forsaken Jehovah, They have despised the Holy One of Israel, They have gone away backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:8–11; Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> Wherefore are ye stricken any more? Ye do add apostasy! Every head is become diseased, and every heart &#91;is&#93; sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 33:6; Matthew 9:12"> From the sole of the foot — unto the head, There is no soundness in it, Wound, and bruise, and fresh smiting! They have not been closed nor bound, Nor have they softened with ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:43; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Your land &#91;is&#93; a desolation, your cities burnt with fire, Your ground, before you strangers are consuming it, And a desolation as overthrown by strangers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Zechariah 2:10"> And left hath been the daughter of Zion, As a booth in a vineyard, As a lodge in a place of cucumbers — as a city besieged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 9:29; Isaiah 37:31–32; Genesis 19:24"> Unless Jehovah of Hosts had left to us a remnant, Shortly — as Sodom we had been, To Gomorrah we had been like!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:8; Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 9:7"> Hear the word of Jehovah, ye rulers of Sodom, Give ear to the law of our God, ye people of Gomorrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 6:20"> 'Why to Me the abundance of your sacrifices? saith Jehovah, I have been satiated &#91;with&#93; burnt-offerings of rams, And fat of fatlings; And blood of bullocks, and lambs, And he-goats I have not desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:17; Micah 6:8"> When ye come in to appear before Me, Who hath required this of your hand, To trample My courts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Malachi 1:10; Matthew 15:9"> Add not to bring in a vain present, Incense — an abomination it &#91;is&#93; to Me, New moon, and sabbath, calling of convocation! Rendure not iniquity — and a restraint!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:21; Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 7:13"> Your new moons and your set seasons hath My soul hated, They have been upon me for a burden, I have been weary of bearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 59:2–3; Proverbs 1:28"> And in your spreading forth your hands, I hide mine eyes from you, Also when ye increase prayer, I do not hear, Your hands of blood have been full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Isaiah 55:6–7; 1 Peter 2:1"> Wash ye, make ye pure, Turn aside the evil of your doings, from before Mine eyes, Cease to do evil, learn to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Psalms 82:3–4"> Seek judgment, make happy the oppressed, Judge the fatherless, strive &#91;for&#93; the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Revelation 7:14; Psalms 51:7"> Come, I pray you, and we reason, saith Jehovah, If your sins are as scarlet, as snow they shall be white, If they are red as crimson, as wool they shall be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15–16; Isaiah 3:10; Joel 2:26"> If ye are willing, and have hearkened, The good of the land ye consume,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Isaiah 58:14; Numbers 23:19"> And if ye refuse, and have rebelled, &#91;By&#93; the sword ye are consumed, For the mouth of Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1–23; Zechariah 8:3; Zephaniah 3:1–3"> How hath a faithful city become a harlot? I have filled it &#91;with&#93; judgment, Righteousness lodgeth in it — now murderers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Lamentations 4:1–2; Hosea 4:18"> Thy silver hath become dross, Thy drink polluted with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Exodus 23:8; Zechariah 7:10"> Thy princes &#91;are&#93; apostates, and companions of thieves, Every one loving a bribe, and pursuing rewards, The fatherless they judge not, And the plea of the widow cometh not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 35:4; Deuteronomy 28:63"> Therefore — the affirmation of the Lord — Jehovah of Hosts, the Mighty One of Israel: Ah, I am eased of Mine adversaries, And I am avenged of Mine enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 9:7; Isaiah 4:4"> And I turn back My hand upon thee, And I refine as purity thy dross, And I turn aside all thy tin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 62:1–2"> And I give back thy judges as at the first, And thy counsellors as in the beginning, After this thou art called, 'A city of righteousness — a faithful city.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Isaiah 45:21–25; Isaiah 5:16"> Zion in judgment is redeemed, And her captivity in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8–9; Psalms 1:6; Isaiah 30:13"> And the destruction of transgressors and sinners &#91;is&#93; together, And those forsaking Jehovah are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3; Isaiah 57:5; Isaiah 66:17"> For &#91;men&#93; are ashamed because of the oaks That ye have desired, And ye are confounded because of the gardens That ye have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Matthew 21:19"> For ye are as an oak whose leaf is fading, And as a garden that hath no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Ezekiel 32:21; Malachi 4:1"> And the strong hath been for tow, And his work for a spark, And burned have both of them together, And there is none quenching!</span>
