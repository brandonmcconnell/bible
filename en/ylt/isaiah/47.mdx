<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Psalms 137:8; Zechariah 2:7"> Come down, and sit on the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon, Sit on the earth, there is no throne, O daughter of the Chaldeans, For no more do they cry to thee, 'O tender and delicate one.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5"> Take millstones, and grind flour, Remove thy veil, draw up the skirt, Uncover the leg, pass over the floods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Jeremiah 50:27–28"> Revealed is thy nakedness, yea, seen is thy reproach, Vengeance I take, and I meet not a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 49:26"> Our redeemer &#91;is&#93; Jehovah of Hosts, His name &#91;is&#93; the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 18:16–19; Habakkuk 2:20; Isaiah 47:7"> Sit silent, and go into darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans, For no more do they cry to thee, 'Mistress of kingdoms.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:9; Zechariah 1:15; Deuteronomy 28:50"> I have been wroth against My people, I have polluted Mine inheritance And I give them into thy hand, Thou hast not appointed for them mercies, On the aged thou hast made thy yoke very heavy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Isaiah 47:5; Jeremiah 5:31"> And thou sayest, 'To the age I am mistress,' While thou hast not laid these things to thy heart, Thou hast not remembered the latter end of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:15; Isaiah 32:9; Isaiah 47:10"> And now, hear this, O luxurious one, Who is sitting confidently — Who is saying in her heart, 'I &#91;am&#93;, and none else, I sit not a widow, nor know bereavement.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 47:12–13"> And come in to thee do these two things, In a moment, in one day, childlessness and widowhood, According to their perfection they have come upon thee, In the multitude of thy sorceries, In the exceeding might of thy charms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Isaiah 29:15; Romans 1:22"> And thou art confident in thy wickedness, Thou hast said, 'There is none seeing me,' Thy wisdom and thy knowledge, It is turning thee back, And thou sayest in thy heart, 'I &#91;am&#93;, and none else.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 18:9–10; Daniel 5:25–30"> And come in on thee hath evil, Thou knowest not its rising, And fall on thee doth mischief, Thou art not able to pacify it, And come on thee suddenly doth desolation, Thou knowest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:8–12; Nahum 3:4; Jeremiah 2:28"> Stand, I pray thee, in thy charms, And in the multitude of thy sorceries, In which thou hast laboured from thy youth, It may be thou art able to profit, It may be thou dost terrify!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; Daniel 2:2–10; Ezekiel 24:12"> Thou hast been wearied in the multitude of thy counsels, Stand up, I pray thee, and save thee, Let the charmers of the heavens, Those looking on the stars, Those teaching concerning the months, From those things that come on thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Nahum 1:10; Ezekiel 15:7"> Lo, they have been as stubble! Fire hath burned them, They deliver not themselves from the power of the flame, There is not a coal to warm them, a light to sit before it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 27:12–25; Jeremiah 51:6–9"> So have they been to thee with whom thou hast laboured, Thy merchants from thy youth, Each to his passage they have wandered, Thy saviour is not!</span>
