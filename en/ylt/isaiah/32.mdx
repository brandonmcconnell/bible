<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> Lo, for righteousness doth a king reign, As to princes, for judgment they rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 9:6"> And each hath been as a hiding-place &#91;from&#93; wind, And as a secret hiding-place &#91;from&#93; inundation, As rivulets of waters in a dry place, As a shadow of a heavy rock in a weary land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> And not dazzled are the eyes of beholders, And the ears of hearers do attend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> And the heart of those hastened Understandeth to know, And the tongue of stammerers hasteth to speak clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; Psalms 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> A fool is no more called 'noble,' And to a miser it is not said, 'rich;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For a fool speaketh folly, And his heart doth iniquity, to do profanity, And to speak concerning Jehovah error, To empty the soul of the hungry, Yea, drink of the thirsty he causeth to lack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> And the miser — his instruments &#91;are&#93; evil, He hath counselled wicked devices, To corrupt the poor with lying sayings, Even when the needy speaketh justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> And the noble counselled noble things, And he for noble things riseth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Women, easy ones, rise, hear my voice, Daughters, confident ones, give ear &#91;to&#93; my saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> Days and a year ye are troubled, O confident ones, For consumed hath been harvest, The gathering cometh not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Tremble ye women, ye easy ones, Be troubled, ye confident ones, Strip and make bare, with a girdle on the loins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> For breasts they are lamenting, For fields of desire, for the fruitful vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> Over the ground of my people thorn — brier goeth up, Surely over all houses of joy of the exulting city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> Surely the palace hath been left, The multitude of the city forsaken, Fort and watch-tower hath been for dens unto the age, A joy of wild asses — a pasture of herds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> Till emptied out on us is the Spirit from on high, And a wilderness hath become a fruitful field, And the fruitful field for a forest is reckoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> And dwelt in the wilderness hath judgment, And righteousness in the fruitful field remaineth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; Psalms 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> And a work of the righteousness hath been peace, And a service of the righteousness — Keeping quiet and confidence unto the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> And dwelt hath My people in a peaceful habitation, And in stedfast tabernacles, And in quiet resting-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> And it hath hailed in the going down of the forest, And in the valley is the city low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> Happy &#91;are&#93; ye sowing by all waters, Sending forth the foot of the ox and the ass!</span>
