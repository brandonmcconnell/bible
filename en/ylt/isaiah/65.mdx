<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> I have been inquired of by those who asked not, I have been found by those who sought Me not, I have said, 'Behold Me, behold Me,' Unto a nation not calling in My name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremiah 4:14"> I have spread out My hands all the day Unto an apostate people, Who are going in the way not good after their own thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Isaiah 66:17"> The people who are provoking Me to anger, To My face continually, Sacrificing in gardens, and making perfume on the bricks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Isaiah 66:3"> Who are dwelling among sepulchres, And lodge in reserved places, Who are eating flesh of the sow, And a piece of abominable things — their vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> Who are saying, 'Keep to thyself, come not nigh to me, For I have declared thee unholy.' These &#91;are&#93; a smoke in Mine anger, A fire burning all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:3; Jeremiah 16:18; Psalms 79:12"> Lo, it is written before Me: 'I am not silent, but have recompensed; And I have recompensed into their bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:27–28; Isaiah 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> Your iniquities, and the iniquities of your fathers together, said Jehovah, Who have made perfume on the mountains, And on the heights have reproached Me, And I have measured their former work into their bosom.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> Thus said Jehovah: As the new wine is found in the cluster, And one hath said, 'Destroy it not for a blessing &#91;is&#93; in it,' So I do for My servants' sake, not to destroy the whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I have brought out from Jacob a seed, And from Judah a possessor of My mount, And possess it do My chosen ones, And My servants do dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 33:9; Hosea 2:15"> And Sharon hath been for the habitation of a flock, And the valley of Achor for the lying down of a herd, For My people who have sought Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2; Isaiah 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And ye &#91;are&#93; those forsaking Jehovah, Who are forgetting My holy mountain, Who are setting in array for Gad a table, And who are filling for Meni a mixture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:13; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> And I have numbered you for the sword, And all of you for slaughter bow down, Because I called, and ye have not answered, I have spoken, and ye have not hearkened, And ye do the evil thing in Mine eyes, And on that which I desired not — fixed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 61:7"> Therefore, thus said the Lord Jehovah: Lo, My servants do eat, and ye do hunger, Lo, My servants do drink, and ye do thirst, Lo, My servants rejoice, and ye are ashamed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalms 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> Lo, My servants sing from joy of heart, And ye cry from pain of heart, And from breaking of spirit ye do howl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> And ye have left your name For an oath for My chosen ones, And the Lord Jehovah hath put thee to death, And to His servants He giveth another name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18; Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 31:5"> So that he who is blessing himself in the earth, Doth bless himself In the God of faithfulness, And he who is swearing in the earth, Doth swear by the God of faithfulness, Because the former distresses have been forgotten, And because they have been hid from Mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Isaiah 66:22"> For, lo, I am creating new heavens, and a new earth, And the former things are not remembered, Nor do they ascend on the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Isaiah 66:10–14"> But joy ye, and rejoice for ever, that I &#91;am&#93; Creator, For, lo, I am creating Jerusalem a rejoicing, And her people a joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Isaiah 25:8"> And I have rejoiced in Jerusalem, And have joyed in My people, And not heard in her any more Is the voice of weeping, and the voice of crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> There is not thence any more a suckling of days, And an aged man who doth not complete his days, For the youth a hundred years old dieth, And the sinner, a hundred years old, is lightly esteemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Isaiah 62:8–9; Isaiah 32:18"> And they have built houses, and inhabited, And planted vineyards, and eaten their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 91:16; Psalms 21:4"> They do not build, and another inhabit, They do not plant, and another eat, For as the days of a tree &#91;are&#93; the days of My people, And the work of their hands wear out do My chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Jeremiah 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> They labour not for a vain thing, Nor do they bring forth for trouble, For the seed of the blessed of Jehovah &#91;are&#93; they, And their offspring with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Isaiah 58:9"> And it hath come to pass, They do not yet call, and I answer, They are yet speaking, and I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Micah 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> Wolf and lamb do feed as one, And a lion as an ox eateth straw, As to the serpent — dust &#91;is&#93; its food, They do no evil, nor destroy, In all My holy mountain, said Jehovah!</span>
