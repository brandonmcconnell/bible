<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; Romans 11:5–6; Jeremiah 30:10"> And now, hear, O Jacob, My servant, And Israel, whom I have fixed on:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 43:1; Isaiah 46:3–4"> Thus said Jehovah, thy Maker, and thy Former, From the womb He doth help thee; Fear not, my servant Jacob, And Jeshurun, whom I have fixed on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17; Isaiah 59:21"> For I pour waters on a thirsty one, And floods on a dry land, I pour My Spirit on thy seed, And My blessing on thine offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 92:13–15"> And they have sprung up as among grass, As willows by streams of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:5; Zechariah 13:9; 1 Peter 2:9"> This &#91;one&#93; saith, For Jehovah I &#91;am&#93;, And this calleth &#91;himself&#93; by the name of Jacob, And this &#91;one&#93; writeth &#91;with&#93; his hand, 'For Jehovah,' and by the name of Israel surnameth himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 43:10–11; Revelation 1:8"> Thus said Jehovah, king of Israel, And his Redeemer, Jehovah of Hosts: 'I &#91;am&#93; the first, and I the last, And besides Me there is no God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:26; Isaiah 41:22; Isaiah 46:9–10"> And who as I, doth call and declare it, And arrange it for Me, Since My placing the people of antiquity, And things that are coming, And those that do come, declare they to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; 1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:35"> Fear not, nor be afraid, Have I not from that time caused thee to hear, and declared? And ye &#91;are&#93; My witnesses, Is there a God besides Me? yea, there is none, A Rock I have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 10:14–15; Psalms 97:7"> Framers of a graven image &#91;are&#93; all of them emptiness, And their desirable things do not profit, And their own witnesses they &#91;are&#93;, They see not, nor know, that they may be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Habakkuk 2:18; Acts 19:26"> Who hath formed a god, And a molten image poured out — not profitable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:17; Isaiah 45:16; Psalms 97:7"> Lo, all his companions are ashamed, As to artisans — they &#91;are&#93; of men, All of them gather together, they stand up, They fear, they are ashamed together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:6–7; Isaiah 40:19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> He hath wrought iron &#91;with&#93; an axe, And hath wrought with coals, And with hammers doth form it, And doth work it by his powerful arm, Yea, he is hungry, and there is no power, He doth not drink water, and he is wearied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:4–5; Psalms 115:5–7; Acts 17:29"> He hath wrought &#91;with&#93; wood, He hath stretched out a rule, He doth mark it out with a line, He maketh it with carving tools, And with a compass he marketh it out, And maketh it according to the form of a man, According to the beauty of a man, To remain in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:20; Jeremiah 10:3–8; Hosea 4:12"> Cutting down to himself cedars, He taketh also a cypress, and an oak, And he strengtheneth &#91;it&#93; for himself Among the trees of a forest, He hath planted an ash, and the shower doth nourish &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; Revelation 9:20; Judges 2:19"> And it hath been for man to burn, And he taketh of them, and becometh warm, Yea, he kindleth &#91;it&#93;, and hath baked bread, Yea, he maketh a god, and boweth himself, He hath made it a graven image, And he falleth down to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Half of it he hath burnt in the fire, By &#91;this&#93; half of it he eateth flesh, He roasteth a roasting, and is satisfied, Yea, he is warm, and saith: 'Aha, I have become warm, I have enjoyed the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; 1 Kings 18:26; Daniel 6:27"> And its remnant for a god he hath made — For his graven image, He falleth down to it, and worshippeth, And prayeth unto it, and he saith, 'Deliver me, for my god thou &#91;art&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:39–40"> They have not known, nor do they understand, For He hath daubed their eyes from seeing, Their heart from acting wisely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:15; 1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5"> And none doth turn &#91;it&#93; back unto his heart, Nor hath knowledge nor understanding to say, 'Half of it I have burned in the fire, Yea, also, I have baked bread over its coals, I roast flesh and I eat, And its remnant for an abomination I make, To the stock of a tree I fall down.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Job 15:31; Hosea 12:1"> Feeding on ashes, the heart is deceived, It hath turned him aside, And he delivereth not his soul, nor saith: 'Is there not a lie in my right hand?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; Isaiah 49:15–16; Isaiah 43:15"> Remember these, O Jacob, and Israel, For My servant thou &#91;art&#93;, I formed thee, a servant to Me thou &#91;art&#93;, O Israel, thou dost not forget Me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Acts 3:18–19"> I have blotted out, as &#91;by&#93; a thick cloud, Thy transgressions, And as &#91;by&#93; a cloud thy sins, Return unto Me, for I have redeemed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–7; Revelation 5:8–14; Psalms 69:34"> Sing, O heavens, for Jehovah hath wrought, Shout, O lower parts of earth, Break forth, O mountains, with singing, Forest, and every tree in it, For Jehovah hath redeemed Jacob, And in Israel He doth beautify Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12; Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 54:5"> Thus said Jehovah, thy redeemer, And thy framer from the womb: 'I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, doing all things, Stretching out the heavens by Myself, Spreading out the earth — who &#91;is&#93; with Me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–14; Jeremiah 50:36"> Making void the tokens of devisers, And diviners it maketh mad, Turning the wise backward, And their knowledge it maketh foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 18:36–38; Isaiah 42:9; Nehemiah 1:3"> Confirming the word of His servant, The counsel of His messengers it perfecteth, Who is saying of Jerusalem, She is inhabited, And of cities of Judah, They shall be built, and her wastes I raise up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 42:15; Jeremiah 51:36; Jeremiah 50:38"> Who is saying to the deep, Be dry, and thy rivers I cause to dry up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–3; Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 45:13"> Who is saying of Cyrus, My shepherd, And all my delight He doth perfect, So as to say of Jerusalem, Thou art built, And of the temple, Thou art founded.</span>
