<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6–7; Proverbs 4:18; Isaiah 32:15–17"> For Zion's sake I am not silent, And for Jerusalem's sake I do not rest, Till her righteousness go out as brightness, And her salvation, as a torch that burneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 66:12"> And nations have seen thy righteousness, And all kings thine honour, And He is giving to thee a new name, That the mouth of Jehovah doth define.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 28:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:19; Zechariah 9:16"> And thou hast been a crown of beauty in the hand of Jehovah, And a diadem of royalty in the hand of thy God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Hosea 2:19–20; Isaiah 62:12"> It is not said of thee any more, 'Forsaken!' And of thy land it is not said any more, 'Desolate,' For to thee is cried, 'My delight &#91;is&#93; in her,' And to thy land, 'Married,' For Jehovah hath delighted in thee, And thy land is married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Isaiah 65:19; Hebrews 12:2"> For a young man doth marry a virgin, Thy Builders do marry thee, With the joy of a bridegroom over a bride, Rejoice over thee doth thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Psalms 134:1–2"> 'On thy walls, O Jerusalem, I have appointed watchmen, All the day, and all the night, Continually, they are not silent.' O ye remembrancers of Jehovah, Keep not silence for yourselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Isaiah 60:18"> And give not silence to Him, Till He establish, and till He make Jerusalem A praise in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–23; Judges 6:3–6; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Sworn hath Jehovah by His right hand, Even by the arm of His strength: 'I give not thy corn any more &#91;as&#93; food for thine enemies, Nor do sons of a stranger drink thy new wine, For which thou hast laboured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7"> For, those gathering it do eat it, and have praised Jehovah, And those collecting it do drink it in My holy courts.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Isaiah 40:3; Hebrews 12:13"> Pass ye on, pass on through the gates, Prepare ye the way of the people, Raise up, raise up the highway, clear it from stones, Lift up an ensign over the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 21:5; John 12:15"> Lo, Jehovah hath proclaimed unto the end of the earth: 'Say ye to the daughter of Zion, Lo, thy salvation hath come,' Lo, his hire &#91;is&#93; with him, and his wage before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 26:19; Isaiah 62:4"> And they have cried to them, 'People of the Holy One, Redeemed of Jehovah,' Yea, to thee is called, 'Sought out one, a city not forsaken!'</span>
