<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 62:11; Ephesians 5:19–20"> In that day sung is this song in the land of Judah: 'We have a strong city, Salvation He doth make walls and bulwark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Isaiah 60:11; 1 Peter 2:9"> Open ye the gates, that enter may a righteous nation, Preserving stedfastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; John 14:27"> An imagination supported Thou fortifiest peace — peace! For in Thee it is confident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 62:8; Psalms 55:22"> Trust ye in Jehovah for ever, For in Jah Jehovah &#91;is&#93; a rock of ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:37; Revelation 18:2; Job 40:11–13"> For He bowed down the dwellers on high, A city set on high He maketh low, He maketh it low unto the earth, He causeth it to come unto the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Luke 10:19; Revelation 2:26"> Tread it down doth a foot, Feet of the poor — steps of the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 42:16; 1 John 3:7"> The path for the righteous &#91;is&#93; uprightness, O upright One, The path of the righteous Thou dost ponder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–7; James 5:7–11; Isaiah 33:2"> Also, &#91;in&#93; the path of Thy judgments, O Jehovah, we have waited &#91;for&#93; Thee, To Thy name and to Thy remembrance &#91;Is&#93; the desire of the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Psalms 63:1; Psalms 119:62"> &#91;With&#93; my soul I desired Thee in the night, Also, &#91;with&#93; my spirit within me I seek Thee earnestly, For when Thy judgments &#91;are&#93; on the earth, The inhabitants of the world have learned righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:7; John 5:37–38; Romans 2:4–5"> The wicked findeth favour, He hath not learned righteousness, In a land of straightforwardness he dealeth perversely, And seeth not the excellency of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:27; Micah 5:9; Isaiah 5:24"> O Jehovah, high &#91;is&#93; Thy hand — they see not, They see the zeal of the people, and are ashamed, Also, the fire — Thine adversaries, consumeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 57:2; Psalms 29:11"> O Jehovah, Thou appointest peace to us, For, all our works also Thou hast wrought for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 10:11"> O Jehovah our God, lords have ruled us besides Thee, Only, by Thee we make mention of Thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 18:2–3; Proverbs 10:7; Isaiah 51:12–13"> Dead — they live not, Rephaim, they rise not, Therefore Thou hast inspected and dost destroy them, Yea, thou destroyest all their memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 33:17; Nehemiah 9:23"> Thou hast added to the nation, O Jehovah, Thou hast added to the nation, Thou hast been honoured, Thou hast put far off all the ends of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Psalms 50:15; Psalms 77:1–2"> O Jehovah, in distress they missed Thee, They have poured out a whisper, Thy chastisement &#91;is&#93; on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 21:3; Isaiah 13:8; John 16:21"> When a pregnant woman cometh near to the birth, She is pained — she crieth in her pangs, So we have been from Thy face, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Psalms 17:14; 2 Kings 19:3"> We have conceived, we have been pained. We have brought forth as it were wind, Salvation we do not work in the earth, Nor do the inhabitants of the world fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ezekiel 37:1–14; Matthew 27:52"> 'Thy dead live — My dead body they rise. Awake and sing, ye dwellers in the dust, For the dew of herbs &#91;is&#93; thy dew, And the land of Rephaim thou causest to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Psalms 27:5; Isaiah 54:7–8"> Come, My people, enter into thy inner chambers, And shut thy doors behind thee, Hide thyself shortly a moment till the indignation pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 6:9–11; Isaiah 13:11; Jude 1:14–15"> For, lo, Jehovah is coming out of His place, To charge the iniquity of the inhabitant of the earth upon him, And revealed hath the earth her blood, Nor doth she cover any more her slain!'</span>
