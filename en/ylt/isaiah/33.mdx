<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Woe, spoiler! and thou not spoiled, And treacherous! and they dealt not treacherously with thee, When thou dost finish, O spoiler, thou art spoiled, When thou dost finish dealing treacherously, They deal treacherously with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> O Jehovah, favour us, for thee we have waited, Be their arm, in the mornings, Yea, our salvation in time of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> From the voice of a multitude fled have peoples, From thine exaltation scattered have been nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And gathered hath been your spoil, A gathering of the caterpillar, As a running to and fro of locusts is he running on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> Set on high is Jehovah, for He is dwelling on high, He filled Zion &#91;with&#93; judgment and righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> And hath been the stedfastness of thy times, The strength of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge, Fear of Jehovah — it &#91;is&#93; His treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> Lo, 'Their Ariel,' they have cried without, Messengers of peace do weep bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> Desolated have been highways, Ceased hath he who passeth along the path, He hath broken covenant, He hath despised enemies, He hath not esteemed a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> Mourned, languished hath the land, Confounded hath been Lebanon, Withered hath been Sharon as a wilderness, And shaking are Bashan and Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> Now, do I arise, saith Jehovah, Now I am exalted, now I am lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> Ye conceive chaff, ye bear stubble, Your spirit! — fire devoureth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> And peoples have been &#91;as&#93; burnings of lime, Thorns, as sweepings, with fire they burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> Hear, ye far off, that which I have done, And know, ye near ones, My might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> Afraid in Zion have been sinners, Seized hath trembling the profane: Who doth dwell for us — consuming fire, Who doth dwell for us — burnings of the age?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> Whoso is walking righteously, And is speaking uprightly, Kicking against gain of oppressions, Shaking his hands from taking hold on a bribe, Stopping his ear from hearing of blood, And shutting his eyes from looking on evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> He high places doth inhabit, Strongholds of rock &#91;are&#93; his high tower, His bread hath been given, his waters stedfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> A king in his beauty, see do thine eyes, They see a land afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> Thy heart doth meditate terror, Where &#91;is&#93; he who is counting? Where &#91;is&#93; he who is weighing? Where &#91;is&#93; he who is counting the towers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> The strong people thou seest not, A people deeper of lip than to be understood, Of a scorned tongue, there is no understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> See Zion, the city of our meetings, Thine eyes see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, A tent not taken down, Not removed are its pins for ever, And none of its cords are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> But there mighty &#91;is&#93; Jehovah for us, A place of rivers — streams broad of sides, No ship with oars doth go into it, And a mighty ship doth not pass over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For Jehovah our judge, Jehovah our lawgiver, Jehovah our king — He doth save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 7:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Left have been thy ropes, They strengthen not rightly their mast, They have not spread out a sail, Then apportioned hath been a prey of much spoil, The lame have taken spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> Nor doth an inhabitant say, 'I was sick,' The people that is dwelling in it, is forgiven of iniquity!</span>
