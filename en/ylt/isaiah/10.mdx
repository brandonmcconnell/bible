<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Micah 3:1–4; Matthew 23:23"> Woe &#91;to&#93; those decreeing decrees of iniquity, And writers who have prescribed perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Isaiah 3:14; Isaiah 29:21"> To turn aside from judgment the poor, And to take violently away the judgment Of the afflicted of My people, That widows may be their prey, That the fatherless they may spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Luke 19:44; Job 31:14"> And what do ye at a day of inspection? And at desolation? — from afar it cometh. Near whom do ye flee for help? And where do ye leave your honour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Isaiah 9:12; Jeremiah 37:10"> Without Me it hath bowed down In the place of a bound one, And in the place of the slain they fall. With all this not turned back hath His anger, And still His hand is stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 30:30"> Woe &#91;to&#93; Asshur, a rod of Mine anger, And a staff in their hand &#91;is&#93; Mine indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Jeremiah 25:9; Zechariah 10:5"> Against a profane nation I send him, And concerning a people of My wrath I charge him, To spoil spoil, and to seize prey, And to make it a treading-place as the clay of out places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Micah 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> And he — he thinketh not so, And his heart reckoneth not so, For — to destroy &#91;is&#93; in his heart, And to cut off nations not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:24; 2 Kings 19:10; Ezekiel 26:7"> For he saith, 'Are not my princes altogether kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Kings 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> Is not Calno as Carchemish? Is not Hamath as Arpad? Is not Samaria as Damascus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:14; 2 Kings 19:17–19; 2 Kings 18:33–35"> As my hand hath got to the kingdoms of a worthless thing, and their graven images, &#91;Greater&#93; than Jerusalem and than Samaria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:10–13; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 36:19–20"> Do I not — as I have done to Samaria, And to her worthless things, So do to Jerusalem and to her grievous things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 65:7; Jeremiah 50:18"> And it hath come to pass, When the Lord doth fulfil all His work In mount Zion and in Jerusalem, I see concerning the fruit of the greatness Of the heart of the king of Asshur. And concerning the glory of the height of his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30; Ezekiel 26:2"> For he hath said, 'By the power of my hand I have wrought, And by my wisdom, for I have been intelligent, And I remove borders of the peoples, And their chief ones I have spoiled, And I put down as a mighty one the inhabitants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 31:25; Proverbs 21:6–7; Nahum 2:9–1"> And my hand as to a nest Getteth to the wealth of the peoples, And as a gathering of forsaken eggs All the earth I — I have gathered, And there hath not been one moving wing, Or opening mouth, or whispering.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 45:9; Isaiah 10:5"> — Doth the axe glorify itself Against him who is hewing with it? Doth the saw magnify itself Against him who is shaking it? As a rod waving those lifting it up! As a staff lifting up that which is not wood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> Therefore doth the Lord, the Lord of Hosts, Send among his fat ones leanness, And under his honour He kindleth a burning As the burning of a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; Numbers 11:1–3; Isaiah 37:23"> And the light of Israel hath been for a fire, And his Holy One for a flame, And it hath burned, and devoured his thorn And his brier in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Isaiah 10:33–34; Isaiah 9:18"> And the honour of his forest, and his fruitful field, From soul even unto flesh He doth consume, And it hath been as the fainting of a standard-bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:17; Isaiah 37:36"> And the rest of the trees of his forest &#91;are&#93; few, And a youth doth write them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 50:10; Isaiah 1:9"> And it hath come to pass, in that day, The remnant of Israel, And the escaped of the house of Jacob, Do not add any more to lean on its smiter, And have leant on Jehovah, The Holy One of Israel, in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Hosea 7:10"> A remnant returneth — a remnant of Jacob, Unto the Mighty God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Isaiah 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> For though thy people Israel be as the sand of the sea, A remnant doth return of it, A consumption determined, Overflowing &#91;with&#93; righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 24:1–23; Romans 9:28"> For a consumption that is determined, The Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, Is making in the midst of all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22–24; Isaiah 37:6; Isaiah 61:3"> Therefore, thus said the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, 'Be not afraid, my people, inhabiting Zion, because of Asshur, With a rod he doth smite thee, And his staff lifteth up against thee, in the way of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; 2 Kings 19:35; Hebrews 10:37"> For yet a very little, And the indignation hath been completed, And Mine anger by their wearing out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Judges 7:25; 2 Kings 19:35"> And awaking for him is Jehovah of Hosts, A scourge like the smiting of Midian at the rock Oreb, And his rod &#91;is&#93; over the sea, And he hath lifted it in the way of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Luke 4:18; Isaiah 14:25"> And it hath come to pass, in that day, Turned is his burden from off thy shoulder, And his yoke from off thy neck, And destroyed hath been the yoke, because of prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:5"> He hath come in against Aiath, He hath passed over into Migron, At Michmash he looketh after his vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> They have gone over the passage, Geba they have made a lodging place, Trembled hath Rama, Gibeah of Saul fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44; Jeremiah 1:1; Joshua 21:18"> Cry aloud &#91;with&#93; thy voice, daughter of Gallim, Give attention, Laish! answer her, Anathoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 15:31"> Fled away hath Madmenah, The inhabitants of the high places have hardened themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1; Isaiah 37:22; Isaiah 19:16"> Yet to-day in Nob to remain, Wave its hand doth the mount of the daughter of Zion, The hill of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 37:38"> Lo, the Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, Is lopping a branch with violence, And the high of stature are cut down, And the lofty are become low,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Daniel 4:23; Jeremiah 48:2"> And He hath gone round the thickets of the forest with iron, And Lebanon by a mighty one falleth!</span>
