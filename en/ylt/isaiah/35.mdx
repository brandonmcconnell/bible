<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 55:12–13"> They joy from the wilderness and dry place, And rejoice doth the desert, and flourish as the rose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 60:13; Amos 9:13–15"> Flourishing it doth flourish, and rejoice, Yea, &#91;with&#93; joy and singing, The honour of Lebanon hath been given to it, The beauty of Carmel and Sharon, They — they see the honour of Jehovah, The majesty of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Job 4:3–4; Luke 22:32"> Strengthen ye the feeble hands, Yea, the stumbling knees strengthen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Isaiah 41:10–14; Haggai 2:4"> Say to the hastened of heart, 'Be strong, Fear not, lo, your God; vengeance cometh, The recompence of God, He Himself doth come and save you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6–7; Luke 7:20–23; Isaiah 29:18"> Then opened are eyes of the blind, And ears of the deaf are unstopped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:8–9; John 7:37–39; Luke 11:14"> Then leap as a hart doth the lame, And sing doth the tongue of the dumb, For broken up in a wilderness have been waters, And streams in a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:14"> And the mirage hath become a pond, And the thirsty land fountains of waters, In the habitation of dragons, Its place of couching down, a court for reed and rush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And a highway hath been there, and a way, And the 'way of holiness' is called to it, Not pass over it doth the unclean, And He Himself &#91;is&#93; by them, Whoso is going in the way — even fools err not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> No lion is there, yea, a destructive beast Ascendeth it not, it is not found there, And walked have the redeemed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 25:8"> And the ransomed of Jehovah return, And have entered Zion with singing. And joy age-during on their head, Joy and gladness they attain, And fled away have sorrow and sighing!</span>
