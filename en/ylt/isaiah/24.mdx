<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Lo, Jehovah is emptying the land, And is making it waste, And hath overturned &#91;it on&#93; its face, And hath scattered its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> And it hath been — as a people so a priest, As the servant so his master, As the maid-servant so her mistress, As the buyer so the seller, As the lender so the borrower, As the usurer so he who is lifting &#91;it&#93; on himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Utterly emptied is the land, and utterly spoiled, For Jehovah hath spoken this word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> Mourned, faded hath the land, Languished, faded hath the world, Languished have they — the high place of the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> And the land hath been defiled under its inhabitants, Because they have transgressed laws, They have changed a statute, They have made void a covenant age-during.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore a curse hath consumed the land, And the inhabitants in it are become desolate, Therefore consumed have been inhabitants of the land, And few men have been left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> Mourned hath the new wine, languished the vine, Sighed have all the joyful of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> Ceased hath the joy of tabrets, Ceased hath the noise of exulting ones, Ceased hath the joy of a harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> With a song they drink not wine, Bitter is strong drink to those drinking it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> It was broken down — a city of emptiness, Shut hath been every house from entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> A cry over the wine &#91;is&#93; in out-places, Darkened hath been all joy, Removed hath been the joy of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> Left in the city &#91;is&#93; desolation, And &#91;with&#93; wasting is the gate smitten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> When thus it is in the heart of the land, In the midst of the peoples, As the compassing of the olive, As gleanings when harvest hath been finished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> They — they lift up their voice, They sing of the excellency of Jehovah, They have cried aloud from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Therefore in prosperity honour ye Jehovah, In isles of the sea, the name of Jehovah, God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the skirt of the earth we heard songs, The desire of the righteous. And I say, 'Leanness &#91;is&#93; to me, Leanness &#91;is&#93; to me, woe &#91;is&#93; to me.' Treacherous dealers dealt treacherously, Yea, treachery, treacherous dealers dealt treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Fear, and a snare, and a gin, &#91;Are&#93; on thee, O inhabitant of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> And it hath come to pass, He who is fleeing from the noise of the fear Doth fall into the snare, And he who is coming up from the midst of the snare, Is captured by the gin, For windows on high have been opened, And shaken are foundations of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> Utterly broken down hath been the land, Utterly broken hath been the land, Utterly moved hath been the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> Stagger greatly doth the land as a drunkard, And it hath been moved as a lodge, And heavy on it hath been its transgression, And it hath fallen, and addeth not to rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> And it hath come to pass, in that day, Jehovah layeth a charge on the host of the high place in the high place, And on the kings of the land on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> And they have been gathered — A gathering of bound ones in a pit, And shut up they have been in a prison, And after a multitude of days are inspected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> And confounded hath been the moon, And ashamed hath been the sun, For reigned hath Jehovah of Hosts In mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, And over-against His elders — honour!</span>
