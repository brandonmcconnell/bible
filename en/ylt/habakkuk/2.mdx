<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 21:8; Isaiah 62:6; 2 Samuel 18:24"> On my charge I stand, and I station myself on a bulwark, and I watch to see what He doth speak against me, and what I do reply to my reproof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Revelation 14:13; Revelation 1:18–19"> And Jehovah answereth me and saith: 'Write a vision, and explain on the tables, That he may run who is reading it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Hebrews 10:36–37; Ezekiel 12:25"> For yet the vision &#91;is&#93; for a season, And it breatheth for the end, and doth not lie, If it tarry, wait for it, For surely it cometh, it is not late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> Lo, a presumptuous one! Not upright is his soul within him, And the righteous by his stedfastness liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11–12; Proverbs 27:20; Proverbs 20:1"> And also, because the wine &#91;is&#93; treacherous, A man is haughty, and remaineth not at home, Who hath enlarged as sheol his soul, And is as death that is not satisfied, And doth gather unto itself all the nations, And doth assemble unto itself all the peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> Do not these — all of them — against him a simile taken up, And a moral of acute sayings for him, And say, Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is multiplying &#91;what is&#93; not his? Till when also is he multiplying to himself heavy pledges?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> Do not thy usurers instantly rise up, And those shaking thee awake up, And thou hast been for a spoil to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> Because thou hast spoiled many nations, Spoil thee do all the remnant of the peoples, Because of man's blood, and of violence &#91;to&#93; the land, &#91;To&#93; the city, and &#91;to&#93; all dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is gaining evil gain for his house, To set on high his nest, To be delivered from the hand of evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> Thou hast counselled a shameful thing to thy house, To cut off many peoples, and sinful &#91;is&#93; thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> For a stone from the wall doth cry out, And a holdfast from the wood answereth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is building a city by blood, And establishing a city by iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Lo, is it not from Jehovah of Hosts And peoples are fatigued for fire, And nations for vanity are weary?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalms 22:27; Psalms 72:19"> For full is the earth of the knowledge of the honour of Jehovah, As waters cover &#91;the bottom of&#93; a sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is giving drink to his neighbour, Pouring out thy bottle, and also making drunk, In order to look on their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> Thou hast been filled — shame without honour, Drink thou also, and be uncircumcised, Turn round unto thee doth the cup of the right hand of Jehovah, And shameful spewing &#91;is&#93; on thine honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalms 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> For violence &#91;to&#93; Lebanon doth cover thee, And spoil of beasts doth affright them, Because of man's blood, and of violence &#91;to&#93; the land, &#91;To&#93; the city, and &#91;to&#93; all dwelling in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> What profit hath a graven image given That its former hath graven it? A molten image and teacher of falsehood, That trusted hath the former on his own formation — to make dumb idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> Woe &#91;to&#93; him who is saying to wood, 'Awake,' 'Stir up,' to a dumb stone, It a teacher! lo, it is overlaid — gold and silver, And there is no spirit in its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; Psalms 46:10"> And Jehovah &#91;is&#93; in His holy temple, Be silent before Him, all the earth!</span>
