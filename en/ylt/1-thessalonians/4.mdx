<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Peter 3:18"> As to the rest, then, brethren, we request you, and call upon you in the Lord Jesus, as ye did receive from us how it behoveth you to walk and to please God, that ye may abound the more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> for ye have known what commands we gave you through the Lord Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> for this is the will of God — your sanctification; that ye abstain from the whoredom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> that each of you know his own vessel to possess in sanctification and honour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> not in the affection of desire, as also the nations that were not knowing God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> that no one go beyond and defraud in the matter his brother, because an avenger &#91;is&#93; the Lord of all these, as also we spake before to you and testified,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> for God did not call us on uncleanness, but in sanctification;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> he, therefore, who is despising — doth not despise man, but God, who also did give His Holy Spirit to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> And concerning the brotherly love, ye have no need of &#91;my&#93; writing to you, for ye yourselves are God-taught to love one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> for ye do it also to all the brethren who &#91;are&#93; in all Macedonia; and we call upon you, brethren, to abound still more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> and to study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands, as we did command you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> that ye may walk becomingly unto those without, and may have lack of nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> And I do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, concerning those who have fallen asleep, that ye may not sorrow, as also the rest who have not hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> for if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, so also God those asleep through Jesus he will bring with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> for this to you we say in the word of the Lord, that we who are living — who do remain over to the presence of the Lord — may not precede those asleep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> because the Lord himself, in a shout, in the voice of a chief-messenger, and in the trump of God, shall come down from heaven, and the dead in Christ shall rise first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> then we who are living, who are remaining over, together with them shall be caught away in clouds to meet the Lord in air, and so always with the Lord we shall be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Luke 21:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> so, then, comfort ye one another in these words.</span>
