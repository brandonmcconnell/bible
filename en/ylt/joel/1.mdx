<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:16; Jeremiah 1:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> A word of Jehovah that hath been unto Joel, son of Pethuel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Hosea 5:1; Job 8:8"> Hear this, ye aged ones, And give ear, all ye inhabitants of the land, Hath this been in your days? Or in the days of your fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Exodus 13:14; Psalms 145:4"> Concerning it to your sons talk ye, And your sons to their sons, And their sons to another generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Amos 4:9; Isaiah 33:4"> What is left of the palmer-worm, eaten hath the locust, And what is left of the locust, Eaten hath the cankerworm, And what is left of the cankerworm, Eaten hath the caterpillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:1; Isaiah 32:10–12; Luke 21:34–36"> Awake, ye drunkards, and weep, And howl all drinking wine, because of the juice, For it hath been cut off from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Revelation 9:7–10; Proverbs 30:14"> For a nation hath come up on my land, Strong, and there is no number, Its teeth &#91;are&#93; the teeth of a lion, And it hath the jaw-teeth of a lioness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Amos 4:9; Joel 1:12"> It hath made my vine become a desolation, And my fig-tree become a chip, It hath made it thoroughly bare, and hath cast down, Made white have been its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Malachi 2:15; Jeremiah 3:4"> Wail, as a virgin girdeth with sackcloth, For the husband of her youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Joel 2:14; Hosea 9:4"> Cut off hath been present and libation from the house of Jehovah, Mourned have the priests, ministrants of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Hosea 9:2; Jeremiah 12:11"> Spoiled is the field, mourned hath the ground, For spoiled is the corn, Dried up hath been new wine, languish doth oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Isaiah 17:11; Jeremiah 9:12"> Be ashamed, ye husbandmen, Howl, vine-dressers, for wheat and for barley, For perished hath the harvest of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Isaiah 24:11; Song of Solomon 2:3"> The vine hath been dried up, And the fig-tree doth languish, Pomegranate, also palm, and apple-tree, All trees of the field have withered, For dried up hath been joy from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 1:8–9; Jeremiah 4:8; Joel 2:17"> Gird, and lament, ye priests, Howl, ye ministrants of the altar, Come in, lodge in sackcloth, ministrants of my God, For withheld from the house of your God hath been present and libation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 29:10–11; 2 Chronicles 20:13"> Sanctify a fast, proclaim a restraint, Gather the elders — all the inhabitants of the land, &#91;Into&#93; the house of Jehovah your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6–9; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Jeremiah 30:7"> And cry unto Jehovah, 'Alas for the day! For near &#91;is&#93; a day of Jehovah, And as destruction from the mighty it cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:6–7; Psalms 43:4; Isaiah 62:8–9"> Is not before our eyes food cut off? From the house of our God joy and rejoicing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Genesis 23:16"> Rotted have scattered things under their clods, Desolated have been storehouses, Broken down have been granaries, For withered hath the corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 14:5–6"> How have cattle sighed! Perplexed have been droves of oxen, For there is no pasture for them, Also droves of sheep have been desolated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 9:10; Amos 7:4"> Unto Thee, O Jehovah, I do call, For fire hath consumed comely places of a wilderness, And a flame hath set on fire all trees of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; 1 Kings 17:7; Job 38:41"> Also the cattle of the field long for Thee, For dried up have been streams of water, And fire hath consumed comely places of a wilderness!'</span>
