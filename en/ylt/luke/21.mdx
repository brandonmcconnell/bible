<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> And having looked up, he saw those who did cast their gifts to the treasury — rich men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> and he saw also a certain poor widow casting there two mites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> and he said, 'Truly I say to you, that this poor widow did cast in more than all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> for all these out of their superabundance did cast into the gifts to God, but this one out of her want, all the living that she had, did cast in.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> And certain saying about the temple, that with goodly stones and devoted things it hath been adorned, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> 'These things that ye behold — days will come, in which there shall not be left a stone upon a stone, that shall not be thrown down.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> And they questioned him, saying, 'Teacher, when, then, shall these things be? and what &#91;is&#93; the sign when these things may be about to happen?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> And he said, 'See — ye may not be led astray, for many shall come in my name, saying — I am &#91;he&#93;, and the time hath come nigh; go not on then after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> and when ye may hear of wars and uprisings, be not terrified, for it behoveth these things to happen first, but the end &#91;is&#93; not immediately.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then said he to them, 'Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> great shakings also in every place, and famines, and pestilences, there shall be; fearful things also, and great signs from heaven there shall be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> and before all these, they shall lay on you their hands, and persecute, delivering up to synagogues and prisons, being brought before kings and governors for my name's sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> and it shall become to you for a testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> 'Settle, then, to your hearts, not to meditate beforehand to reply,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> for I will give to you a mouth and wisdom that all your opposers shall not be able to refute or resist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> 'And ye shall be delivered up also by parents, and brothers, and kindred, and friends, and they shall put of you to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> and ye shall be hated by all because of my name —</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> and a hair out of your head shall not perish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> in your patience possess ye your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> 'And when ye may see Jerusalem surrounded by encampments, then know that come nigh did her desolation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> then those in Judea, let them flee to the mountains; and those in her midst, let them depart out; and those in the countries, let them not come in to her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> because these are days of vengeance, to fulfil all things that have been written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> 'And woe to those with child, and to those giving suck, in those days; for there shall be great distress on the land, and wrath on this people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> and they shall fall by the mouth of the sword, and shall be led captive to all the nations, and Jerusalem shall be trodden down by nations, till the times of nations be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> 'And there shall be signs in sun, and moon, and stars, and on the land &#91;is&#93; distress of nations with perplexity, sea and billow roaring;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> men fainting at heart from fear, and expectation of the things coming on the world, for the powers of the heavens shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> 'And then they shall see the Son of Man, coming in a cloud, with power and much glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> and these things beginning to happen bend yourselves back, and lift up your heads, because your redemption doth draw nigh.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> And he spake a simile to them: 'See the fig-tree, and all the trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> when they may now cast forth, having seen, of yourselves ye know that now is the summer nigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> so also ye, when ye may see these things happening, ye know that near is the reign of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> verily I say to you — This generation may not pass away till all may have come to pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> the heaven and the earth shall pass away, but my words may not pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> 'And take heed to yourselves, lest your hearts may be weighed down with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and anxieties of life, and suddenly that day may come on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> for as a snare it shall come on all those dwelling on the face of all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> watch ye, then, in every season, praying that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that are about to come to pass, and to stand before the Son of Man.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> And he was during the days in the temple teaching, and during the nights, going forth, he was lodging at the mount called of Olives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> and all the people were coming early unto him in the temple to hear him.</span>
