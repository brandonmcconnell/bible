<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> And he spake also a simile to them, that it behoveth &#91;us&#93; always to pray, and not to faint,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying, 'A certain judge was in a certain city — God he is not fearing, and man he is not regarding —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> and a widow was in that city, and she was coming unto him, saying, Do me justice on my opponent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> and he would not for a time, but after these things he said in himself, Even if God I do not fear, and man do not regard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet because this widow doth give me trouble, I will do her justice, lest, perpetually coming, she may plague me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> And the Lord said, 'Hear ye what the unrighteous judge saith:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> and shall not God execute the justice to His choice ones, who are crying unto Him day and night — bearing long in regard to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I say to you, that He will execute the justice to them quickly; but the Son of Man having come, shall he find the faith upon the earth?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> And he spake also unto certain who have been trusting in themselves that they were righteous, and have been despising the rest, this simile:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> 'Two men went up to the temple to pray, the one a Pharisee, and the other a tax-gatherer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> the Pharisee having stood by himself, thus prayed: God, I thank Thee that I am not as the rest of men, rapacious, unrighteous, adulterers, or even as this tax-gatherer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all things — as many as I possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> 'And the tax-gatherer, having stood afar off, would not even the eyes lift up to the heaven, but was smiting on his breast, saying, God be propitious to me — the sinner!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I say to you, this one went down declared righteous, to his house, rather than that one: for every one who is exalting himself shall be humbled, and he who is humbling himself shall be exalted.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And they were bringing near also the babes, that he may touch them, and the disciples having seen did rebuke them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> and Jesus having called them near, said, 'Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not, for of such is the reign of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> verily I say to you, Whoever may not receive the reign of God as a little child, may not enter into it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> And a certain ruler questioned him, saying, 'Good teacher, what having done — shall I inherit life age-during?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> And Jesus said to him, 'Why me dost thou call good? no one &#91;is&#93; good, except One — God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> the commands thou hast known: Thou mayest not commit adultery, Thou mayest do no murder, Thou mayest not steal, Thou mayest not bear false witness, Honour thy father and thy mother.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> And he said, 'All these I did keep from my youth;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> and having heard these things, Jesus said to him, 'Yet one thing to thee is lacking; all things — as many as thou hast — sell, and distribute to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven, and come, be following me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> and he, having heard these things, became very sorrowful, for he was exceeding rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> And Jesus having seen him become very sorrowful, said, 'How hardly shall those having riches enter into the reign of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> for it is easier for a camel through the eye of a needle to enter, than for a rich man into the reign of God to enter.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> And those who heard, said, 'And who is able to be saved?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> and he said, 'The things impossible with men are possible with God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> And Peter said, 'Lo, we left all, and did follow thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> and he said to them, 'Verily I say to you, that there is not one who left house, or parents, or brothers, or wife, or children, for the sake of the reign of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who may not receive back manifold more in this time, and in the coming age, life age-during.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> And having taken the twelve aside, he said unto them, 'Lo, we go up to Jerusalem, and all things shall be completed — that have been written through the prophets — to the Son of Man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> for he shall be delivered up to the nations, and shall be mocked, and insulted, and spit upon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> and having scourged they shall put him to death, and on the third day he shall rise again.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> And they none of these things understood, and this saying was hid from them, and they were not knowing the things said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> And it came to pass, in his coming nigh to Jericho, a certain blind man was sitting beside the way begging,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> and having heard a multitude going by, he was inquiring what this may be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> and they brought him word that Jesus the Nazarene doth pass by,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> and he cried out, saying, 'Jesus, Son of David, deal kindly with me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> and those going before were rebuking him, that he might be silent, but he was much more crying out, 'Son of David, deal kindly with me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> And Jesus having stood, commanded him to be brought unto him, and he having come nigh, he questioned him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> saying, 'What wilt thou I shall do to thee?' and he said, 'Sir, that I may receive sight.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> And Jesus said to him, 'Receive thy sight; thy faith hath saved thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> and presently he did receive sight, and was following him, glorifying God; and all the people, having seen, did give praise to God.</span>
