<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 11:47–53; Matthew 12:14; Luke 20:19"> and the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they may take him up, for they were afraid of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 13:2; Mark 14:10–11; John 13:26–27"> And the Adversary entered into Judas, who is surnamed Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:24; Luke 22:52"> and he, having gone away, spake with the chief priests and the magistrates, how he might deliver him up to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:15–16; Acts 1:18; Matthew 27:3–5"> and they rejoiced, and covenanted to give him money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:2; Matthew 26:5"> and he agreed, and was seeking a favourable season to deliver him up to them without tumult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> And the day of the unleavened food came, in which it was behoving the passover to be sacrificed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> and he sent Peter and John, saying, 'Having gone on, prepare to us the passover, that we may eat;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and they said to him, 'Where wilt thou that we might prepare?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> And he said to them, 'Lo, in your entering into the city, there shall meet you a man, bearing a pitcher of water, follow him to the house where he doth go in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> and ye shall say to the master of the house, The Teacher saith to thee, Where is the guest-chamber where the passover with my disciples I may eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> and he shall show you a large upper room furnished, there make ready;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> and they, having gone away, found as he hath said to them, and they made ready the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> And when the hour come, he reclined (at meat), and the twelve apostles with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:50; John 4:34; John 17:1"> and he said unto them, 'With desire I did desire to eat this passover with you before my suffering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 14:15; Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30"> for I say to you, that no more may I eat of it till it may be fulfilled in the reign of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Psalms 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> And having taken a cup, having given thanks, he said, 'Take this and divide to yourselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> for I say to you that I may not drink of the produce of the vine till the reign of God may come.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> And having taken bread, having given thanks, he brake and gave to them, saying, 'This is my body, that for you is being given, this do ye — to remembrance of me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> In like manner, also, the cup after the supping, saying, 'This cup &#91;is&#93; the new covenant in my blood, that for you is being poured forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> 'But, lo, the hand of him delivering me up &#91;is&#93; with me on the table,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalms 109:6–15; Psalms 55:12–15"> and indeed the Son of Man doth go according to what hath been determined; but woe to that man through whom he is being delivered up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> And they began to reason among themselves, who then of them it may be, who is about to do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> And there happened also a strife among them — who of them is accounted to be greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> And he said to them, 'The kings of the nations do exercise lordship over them, and those exercising authority upon them are called benefactors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> but ye &#91;are&#93; not so, but he who is greater among you — let him be as the younger; and he who is leading, as he who is ministering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> for who is greater? he who is reclining (at meat), or he who is ministering? is it not he who is reclining (at meat)? and I — I am in your midst as he who is ministering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> 'And ye — ye are those who have remained with me in my temptations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> and I appoint to you, as my Father did appoint to me, a kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> that ye may eat and may drink at my table, in my kingdom, and may sit on thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> And the Lord said, 'Simon, Simon, lo, the Adversary did ask you for himself to sift as the wheat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> and I besought for thee, that thy faith may not fail; and thou, when thou didst turn, strengthen thy brethren.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> And he said to him, 'Sir, with thee I am ready both to prison and to death to go;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> and he said, 'I say to thee, Peter, a cock shall not crow to-day, before thrice thou mayest disown knowing me.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> And he said to them, 'When I sent you without bag, and scrip, and sandals, did ye lack anything?' and they said, 'Nothing.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> Then said he to them, 'But, now, he who is having a bag, let him take &#91;it&#93; up, and in like manner also a scrip; and he who is not having, let him sell his garment, and buy a sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> for I say to you, that yet this that hath been written it behoveth to be fulfilled in me: And with lawless ones he was reckoned, for also the things concerning me have an end.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> And they said, 'Sir, lo, here &#91;are&#93; two swords;' and he said to them, 'It is sufficient.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> And having gone forth, he went on, according to custom, to the mount of the Olives, and his disciples also followed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> and having come to the place, he said to them, 'Pray ye not to enter into temptation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> And he was withdrawn from them, as it were a stone's cast, and having fallen on the knees he was praying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> saying, 'Father, if Thou be counselling to make this cup pass from me — ;but, not my will, but Thine be done.' —</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalms 91:11–12"> And there appeared to him a messenger from heaven strengthening him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 22:12–21"> and having been in agony, he was more earnestly praying, and his sweat became, as it were, great drops of blood falling upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> And having risen up from the prayer, having come unto the disciples, he found them sleeping from the sorrow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> and he said to them, 'Why do ye sleep? having risen, pray that ye may not enter into temptation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> And while he is speaking, lo, a multitude, and he who is called Judas, one of the twelve, was coming before them, and he came nigh to Jesus to kiss him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> and Jesus said to him, 'Judas, with a kiss the Son of Man dost thou deliver up?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> And those about him, having seen what was about to be, said to him, 'Sir, shall we smite with a sword?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> And a certain one of them smote the servant of the chief priest, and took off his right ear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> and Jesus answering said, 'Suffer ye thus far,' and having touched his ear, he healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> And Jesus said to those having come upon him — chief priests, and magistrates of the temple, and elders — 'As upon a robber have ye come forth, with swords and sticks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> while daily I was with you in the temple, ye did stretch forth no hands against me; but this is your hour and the power of the darkness.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> And having taken him, they led and brought him to the house of the chief priest. And Peter was following afar off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> and they having kindled a fire in the midst of the court, and having sat down together, Peter was sitting in the midst of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> and a certain maid having seen him sitting at the light, and having earnestly looked at him, she said, 'And this one was with him!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> and he disowned him, saying, 'Woman, I have not known him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> And after a little, another having seen him, said, 'And thou art of them!' and Peter said, 'Man, I am not.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> And one hour, as it were, having intervened, a certain other was confidently affirming, saying, 'Of a truth this one also was with him, for he is also a Galilean;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> and Peter said, 'Man, I have not known what thou sayest;' and presently, while he is speaking, a cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> And the Lord having turned did look on Peter, and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he said to him — 'Before a cock shall crow, thou mayest disown me thrice;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> and Peter having gone without, wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> And the men who were holding Jesus were mocking him, beating &#91;him&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> and having blindfolded him, they were striking him on the face, and were questioning him, saying, 'Prophesy who he is who smote thee?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> and many other things, speaking evilly, they spake in regard to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> And when it became day there was gathered together the eldership of the people, chief priests also, and scribes, and they led him up to their own sanhedrim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> saying, 'If thou be the Christ, tell us.' And he said to them, 'If I may tell you, ye will not believe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> and if I also question &#91;you&#93;, ye will not answer me or send me away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Mark 16:19"> henceforth, there shall be the Son of Man sitting on the right hand of the power of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> And they all said, 'Thou, then, art the Son of God?' and he said unto them, 'Ye say &#91;it&#93;, because I am;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> and they said, 'What need yet have we of testimony? for we ourselves did hear &#91;it&#93; from his mouth.'</span>
