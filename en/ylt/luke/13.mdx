<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Lamentations 2:20; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> And there were present certain at that time, telling him about the Galileans, whose blood Pilate did mingle with their sacrifices;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:2; Acts 28:4; Job 22:5–16"> and Jesus answering said to them, 'Think ye that these Galileans became sinners beyond all the Galileans, because they have suffered such things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38–40"> No — I say to you, but, if ye may not reform, all ye even so shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:15; Job 1:19; John 9:11"> 'Or those eighteen, on whom the tower in Siloam fell, and killed them; think ye that these became debtors beyond all men who are dwelling in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Luke 13:3; Isaiah 28:10–13"> No — I say to you, but, if ye may not reform, all ye in like manner shall perish.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> And he spake this simile: 'A certain one had a fig-tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit in it, and he did not find;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> and he said unto the vine-dresser, Lo, three years I come seeking fruit in this fig-tree, and do not find, cut it off, why also the ground doth it render useless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Joel 2:17; Romans 10:1"> 'And he answering saith to him, Sir, suffer it also this year, till that I may dig about it, and cast in dung;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> and if indeed it may bear fruit — ;and if not so, thereafter thou shalt cut it off.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> And he was teaching in one of the synagogues on the sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> and lo, there was a woman having a spirit of infirmity eighteen years, and she was bowed together, and not able to bend back at all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> and Jesus having seen her, did call &#91;her&#93; near, and said to her, 'Woman, thou hast been loosed from thy infirmity;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> and he laid on her &#91;his&#93; hands, and presently she was set upright, and was glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> And the chief of the synagogue answering — much displeased that on the sabbath Jesus healed — said to the multitude, 'Six days there are in which it behoveth &#91;us&#93; to be working; in these, then, coming, be healed, and not on the sabbath-day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> Then the Lord answered him and said, 'Hypocrite, doth not each of you on the sabbath loose his ox or ass from the stall, and having led away, doth water &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> and this one, being a daughter of Abraham, whom the Adversary bound, lo, eighteen years, did it not behove to be loosed from this bond on the sabbath-day?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Psalms 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> And he saying these things, all who were opposed to him were being ashamed, and all the multitude were rejoicing over all the glorious things that are being done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:30–34; Luke 17:21; Matthew 13:31–32"> And he said, 'To what is the reign of God like? and to what shall I liken it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:12; Daniel 4:21; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like to a grain of mustard, which a man having taken, did cast into his garden, and it increased, and came to a great tree, and the fowls of the heavens did rest in its branches.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And again he said, 'To what shall I liken the reign of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:23–24"> It is like leaven, which a woman, having taken, did hide in three measures of meal, till that all was leavened.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> And he was going through cities and villages, teaching, and making progress toward Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> and a certain one said to him, 'Sir, are those saved few?' and he said unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> 'Be striving to go in through the straight gate, because many, I say to you, will seek to go in, and shall not be able;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> from the time the master of the house may have risen up, and may have shut the door, and ye may begin without to stand, and to knock at the door, saying, Lord, lord, open to us, and he answering shall say to you, I have not known you whence ye are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> then ye may begin to say, We did eat before thee, and did drink, and in our broad places thou didst teach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> and he shall say, I say to you, I have not known you whence ye are; depart from me, all ye workers of the unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> 'There shall be there the weeping and the gnashing of the teeth, when ye may see Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, and all the prophets, in the reign of God, and yourselves being cast out without;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> and they shall come from east and west, and from north and south, and shall recline in the reign of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> and lo, there are last who shall be first, and there are first who shall be last.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9–11; Psalms 11:1–2; Matthew 14:1"> On that day there came near certain Pharisees, saying to him, 'Go forth, and be going on hence, for Herod doth wish to kill thee;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 5:9; John 17:4–5"> and he said to them, 'Having gone, say to this fox, Lo, I cast forth demons, and perfect cures to-day and to-morrow, and the third &#91;day&#93; I am being perfected;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Matthew 21:11; Acts 13:27"> but it behoveth me to-day, and to-morrow, and the &#91;day&#93; following, to go on, because it is not possible for a prophet to perish out of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 23:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Acts 7:52"> 'Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that is killing the prophets, and stoning those sent unto her, how often did I will to gather together thy children, as a hen her brood under the wings, and ye did not will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Mark 11:9–10; Matthew 21:9"> 'Lo, your house is being left to you desolate, and verily I say to you — ye may not see me, till it may come, when ye may say, Blessed &#91;is&#93; he who is coming in the name of the Lord.'</span>
