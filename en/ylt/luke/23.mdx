<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> And having risen, the whole multitude of them did lead him to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> and began to accuse him, saying, 'This one we found perverting the nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying himself to be Christ a king.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> And Pilate questioned him, saying, 'Thou art the king of the Jews?' and he answering him, said, 'Thou dost say &#91;it&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> And Pilate said unto the chief priests, and the multitude, 'I find no fault in this man;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> and they were the more urgent, saying — 'He doth stir up the people, teaching throughout the whole of Judea — having begun from Galilee — unto this place.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> And Pilate having heard of Galilee, questioned if the man is a Galilean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> and having known that he is from the jurisdiction of Herod, he sent him back unto Herod, he being also in Jerusalem in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> And Herod having seen Jesus did rejoice exceedingly, for he was wishing for a long &#91;time&#93; to see him, because of hearing many things about him, and he was hoping some sign to see done by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> and was questioning him in many words, and he answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and the scribes stood vehemently accusing him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> and Herod with his soldiers having set him at nought, and having mocked, having put around him gorgeous apparel, did send him back to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> and both Pilate and Herod became friends on that day with one another, for they were before at enmity between themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> And Pilate having called together the chief priests, and the rulers, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> said unto them, 'Ye brought to me this man as perverting the people, and lo, I before you having examined, found in this man no fault in those things ye bring forward against him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> no, nor yet Herod, for I sent you back unto him, and lo, nothing worthy of death is having been done by him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> having chastised, therefore, I will release him,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> for it was necessary for him to release to them one at every feast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> and they cried out — the whole multitude — saying, 'Away with this one, and release to us Barabbas,'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> who had been, because of a certain sedition made in the city, and murder, cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate again then — wishing to release Jesus — called to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> but they were calling out, saying, 'Crucify, crucify him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> And he a third time said unto them, 'Why, what evil did he? no cause of death did I find in him; having chastised him, then, I will release &#91;him&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> And they were pressing with loud voices asking him to be crucified, and their voices, and those of the chief priests, were prevailing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> and Pilate gave judgment for their request being done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> and he released him who because of sedition and murder hath been cast into the prison, whom they were asking, and Jesus he gave up to their will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And as they led him away, having taken hold on Simon, a certain Cyrenian, coming from the field, they put on him the cross, to bear &#91;it&#93; behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And there was following him a great multitude of the people, and of women, who also were beating themselves and lamenting him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> and Jesus having turned unto them, said, 'Daughters of Jerusalem, weep not for me, but for yourselves weep ye, and for your children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> for, lo, days do come, in which they shall say, Happy the barren, and wombs that did not bare, and paps that did not give suck;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> then they shall begin to say to the mountains, Fall on us, and to the hills, Cover us; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> for, if in the green tree they do these things — in the dry what may happen?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> And there were also others — two evil-doers — with him, to be put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> and when they came to the place that is called Skull, there they crucified him and the evil-doers, one on the right hand and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> And Jesus said, 'Father, forgive them, for they have not known what they do;' and parting his garments they cast a lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people were standing, looking on, and the rulers also were sneering with them, saying, 'Others he saved, let him save himself, if this be the Christ, the choice one of God.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> And mocking him also were the soldiers, coming near and offering vinegar to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, 'If thou be the king of the Jews, save thyself.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And there was also a superscription written over him, in letters of Greek, and Roman, and Hebrew, 'This is the King of the Jews.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> And one of the evil-doers who were hanged, was speaking evil of him, saying, 'If thou be the Christ, save thyself and us.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> And the other answering, was rebuking him, saying, 'Dost thou not even fear God, that thou art in the same judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> and we indeed righteously, for things worthy of what we did we receive back, but this one did nothing out of place;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> and he said to Jesus, 'Remember me, lord, when thou mayest come in thy reign;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> and Jesus said to him, 'Verily I say to thee, To-day with me thou shalt be in the paradise.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> And it was, as it were, the sixth hour, and darkness came over all the land till the ninth hour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> and the sun was darkened, and the vail of the sanctuary was rent in the midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> and having cried with a loud voice, Jesus said, 'Father, to Thy hands I commit my spirit;' and these things having said, he breathed forth the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> And the centurion having seen what was done, did glorify God, saying, 'Really this man was righteous;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> and all the multitudes who were come together to this sight, beholding the things that came to pass, smiting their breasts did turn back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> and all his acquaintances stood afar off, and women who did follow him from Galilee, beholding these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> And lo, a man, by name Joseph, being a counsellor, a man good and righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> — he was not consenting to their counsel and deed — from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who also himself was expecting the reign of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> he, having gone near to Pilate, asked the body of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> and having taken it down, he wrapped it in fine linen, and placed it in a tomb hewn out, where no one was yet laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> And the day was a preparation, and sabbath was approaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> and the women also who have come with him out of Galilee having followed after, beheld the tomb, and how his body was placed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> and having turned back, they made ready spices and ointments, and on the sabbath, indeed, they rested, according to the command.</span>
