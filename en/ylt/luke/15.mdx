<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> And all the tax-gatherers and the sinners were coming nigh to him, to hear him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> and the Pharisees and the scribes were murmuring, saying — This one doth receive sinners, and doth eat with them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he spake unto them this simile, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> 'What man of you having a hundred sheep, and having lost one out of them, doth not leave behind the ninety-nine in the wilderness, and go on after the lost one, till he may find it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> and having found, he doth lay &#91;it&#93; on his shoulders rejoicing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> and having come to the house, he doth call together the friends and the neighbours, saying to them, Rejoice with me, because I found my sheep — the lost one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> 'I say to you, that so joy shall be in the heaven over one sinner reforming, rather than over ninety-nine righteous men, who have no need of reformation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> 'Or what woman having ten drachms, if she may lose one drachm, doth not light a lamp, and sweep the house, and seek carefully till that she may find?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> and having found, she doth call together the female friends and the neighbours, saying, Rejoice with me, for I found the drachm that I lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> 'So I say to you, joy doth come before the messengers of God over one sinner reforming.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> And he said, 'A certain man had two sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> and the younger of them said to the father, Father, give me the portion of the substance falling to &#91;me&#93;, and he divided to them the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> 'And not many days after, having gathered all together, the younger son went abroad to a far country, and there he scattered his substance, living riotously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> and he having spent all, there came a mighty famine on that country, and himself began to be in want;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> and having gone on, he joined himself to one of the citizens of that country, and he sent him to the fields to feed swine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> and he was desirous to fill his belly from the husks that the swine were eating, and no one was giving to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> 'And having come to himself, he said, How many hirelings of my father have a superabundance of bread, and I here with hunger am perishing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> having risen, I will go on unto my father, and will say to him, Father, I did sin — to the heaven, and before thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> and no more am I worthy to be called thy son; make me as one of thy hirelings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> 'And having risen, he went unto his own father, and he being yet far distant, his father saw him, and was moved with compassion, and having ran he fell upon his neck and kissed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> and the son said to him, Father, I did sin — to the heaven, and before thee, and no more am I worthy to be called thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> 'And the father said unto his servants, Bring forth the first robe, and clothe him, and give a ring for his hand, and sandals for the feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> and having brought the fatted calf, kill &#91;it&#93;, and having eaten, we may be merry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> because this my son was dead, and did live again, and he was lost, and was found; and they began to be merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> 'And his elder son was in a field, and as, coming, he drew nigh to the house, he heard music and dancing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and having called near one of the young men, he was inquiring what these things might be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> and he said to him — Thy brother is arrived, and thy father did kill the fatted calf, because in health he did receive him back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> 'And he was angry, and would not go in, therefore his father, having come forth, was entreating him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> and he answering said to the father, Lo, so many years I do serve thee, and never thy command did I transgress, and to me thou didst never give a kid, that with my friends I might make merry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> but when thy son — this one who did devour thy living with harlots — came, thou didst kill to him the fatted calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> 'And he said to him, Child, thou art always with me, and all my things are thine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> but to be merry, and to be glad, it was needful, because this thy brother was dead, and did live again, he was lost, and was found.'</span>
