<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> And it came to pass, in the multitude pressing on him to hear the word of God, that he was standing beside the lake of Gennesaret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> and he saw two boats standing beside the lake, and the fishers, having gone away from them, were washing the nets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> and having entered into one of the boats, that was Simon's, he asked him to put back a little from the land, and having sat down, was teaching the multitudes out of the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> And when he left off speaking, he said unto Simon, 'Put back to the deep, and let down your nets for a draught;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> and Simon answering said to him, 'Master, through the whole night, having laboured, we have taken nothing, but at thy saying I will let down the net.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> And having done this, they enclosed a great multitude of fishes, and their net was breaking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> and they beckoned to the partners, who &#91;are&#93; in the other boat, having come, to help them; and they came, and filled both the boats, so that they were sinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> And Simon Peter having seen, fell down at the knees of Jesus, saying, 'Depart from me, because I am a sinful man, O lord;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Psalms 8:8; Mark 9:6"> for astonishment seized him, and all those with him, at the draught of the fishes that they took,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> and in like manner also James and John, sons of Zebedee, who were partners with Simon; and Jesus said unto Simon, 'Fear not, henceforth thou shalt be catching men;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> and they, having brought the boats upon the land, having left all, did follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> And it came to pass, in his being in one of the cities, that lo, a man full of leprosy, and having seen Jesus, having fallen on &#91;his&#93; face, he besought him, saying, 'Sir, if thou mayest will, thou art able to cleanse me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> and having stretched forth &#91;his&#93; hand, he touched him, having said, 'I will; be thou cleansed;' and immediately the leprosy went away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> And he charged him to tell no one, 'But, having gone away, shew thyself to the priest, and bring near for thy cleansing according as Moses directed, for a testimony to them;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> but the more was the report going abroad concerning him, and great multitudes were coming together to hear, and to be healed by him of their infirmities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> and he was withdrawing himself in the desert places and was praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> And it came to pass, on one of the days, that he was teaching, and there were sitting by Pharisees and teachers of the Law, who were come out of every village of Galilee, and Judea, and Jerusalem, and the power of the Lord was — to heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> And lo, men bearing upon a couch a man, who hath been struck with palsy, and they were seeking to bring him in, and to place before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> and not having found by what way they may bring him in because of the multitude, having gone up on the house-top, through the tiles they let him down, with the little couch, into the midst before Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> and he having seen their faith, said to him, 'Man, thy sins have been forgiven thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> And the scribes and the Pharisees began to reason, saying, 'Who is this that doth speak evil words? who is able to forgive sins, except God only?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> And Jesus having known their reasonings, answering, said unto them, 'What reason ye in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> which is easier — to say, Thy sins have been forgiven thee? or to say, Arise, and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> 'And that ye may know that the Son of Man hath authority upon the earth to forgive sins — (he said to the one struck with palsy) — I say to thee, Arise, and having taken up thy little couch, be going on to thy house.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalms 107:20–22"> And presently having risen before them, having taken up &#91;that&#93; on which he was lying, he went away to his house, glorifying God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> and astonishment took all, and they were glorifying God, and were filled with fear, saying — 'We saw strange things to-day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> And after these things he went forth, and beheld a tax-gatherer, by name Levi, sitting at the tax-office, and said to him, 'Be following me;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> and he, having left all, having arisen, did follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> And Levi made a great entertainment to him in his house, and there was a great multitude of tax-gatherers and others who were with them reclining (at meat),</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> and the scribes and the Pharisees among them were murmuring at his disciples, saying, 'Wherefore with tax-gatherers and sinners do ye eat and drink?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> And Jesus answering said unto them, 'They who are well have no need of a physician, but they that are ill:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> I came not to call righteous men, but sinners, to reformation.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> And they said unto him, 'Wherefore do the disciples of John fast often, and make supplications — in like manner also those of the Pharisees — but thine do eat and drink?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> And he said unto them, 'Are ye able to make the sons of the bride-chamber — in the bridegroom being with them — to fast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> but days will come, and, when the bridegroom may be taken away from them, then they shall fast in those days.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> And he spake also a simile unto them — 'No one a patch of new clothing doth put on old clothing, and if otherwise, the new also doth make a rent, and with the old the patch doth not agree, that &#91;is&#93; from the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalms 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> 'And no one doth put new wine into old skins, and if otherwise, the new wine will burst the skins, and itself will be poured out, and the skins will be destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> but new wine into new skins is to be put, and both are preserved together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> and no one having drunk old &#91;wine&#93;, doth immediately wish new, for he saith, The old is better.'</span>
