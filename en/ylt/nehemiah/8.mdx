<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Nehemiah 3:26; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> And all the people are gathered as one man unto the broad place that &#91;is&#93; before the water-gate, and they say to Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the law of Moses, that Jehovah commanded Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Malachi 2:7; Numbers 29:1"> And Ezra the priest bringeth the law before the assembly, both of men and women, and every one intelligent to hear, on the first day of the seventh month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:22; Revelation 2:29; Hebrews 2:1–3"> and he readeth in it before the broad place that &#91;is&#93; before the water-gate, from the light till the middle of the day, over-against the men, and the women, and those intelligent, and the ears of all the people &#91;are&#93; unto the book of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 10:29; Nehemiah 12:13; Nehemiah 10:20"> And Ezra the scribe standeth on a tower of wood that they made for the purpose, and Mattithiah standeth near him, and Shema, and Anaiah, and Urijah, and Hilkiah, and Maaseiah, on his right; and on his left Pedaiah, and Mishael, and Malchijah, and Hashum, and Hashbaddana, Zechariah, Meshullam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:20; 1 Kings 8:14; Luke 4:16–17"> And Ezra openeth the book before the eyes of all the people — for above all the people he hath been — and at his opening &#91;it&#93; all the people have stood up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Lamentations 3:41; Exodus 4:31"> and Ezra blesseth Jehovah, the great God, and all the people answer, 'Amen, Amen,' with lifting up of their hands, and they bow and do obeisance to Jehovah — faces to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:4; Leviticus 10:11; Nehemiah 3:23"> And Jeshua, and Bani, and Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodijah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the Levites, giving the people understanding in the law, and the people, &#91;are&#93; on their station,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Acts 28:23; Luke 24:32"> and they read in the book, in the law of God, explaining — so as to give the meaning, and they give understanding to the convocation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Nehemiah 7:70; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And Nehemiah — he &#91;is&#93; the Tirshatha — saith (and Ezra the priest, the scribe, and the Levites who are instructing the people) to all the people, 'To-day is holy to Jehovah your God, do not mourn, nor weep:' for all the people are weeping at their hearing the words of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 28:7–8; Proverbs 17:22; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And he saith to them, 'Go, eat fat things, and drink sweet things, and sent portions to him for whom nothing is prepared, for to-day &#91;is&#93; holy to our Lord, and be not grieved, for the joy of Jehovah is your strength.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:30"> And the Levites are keeping all the people silent, saying, 'Be silent, for to-day &#91;is&#93; holy, and be not grieved.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:111; Psalms 119:130"> And all the people go to eat, and to drink, and to send portions, and to make great joy, because they have understood concerning the words that they made known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–6; Proverbs 8:33–34; Proverbs 12:1"> And on the second day have been gathered heads of the fathers of all the people, the priests, and the Levites, unto Ezra the scribe, even to act wisely concerning the words of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34; Deuteronomy 16:13–15; Leviticus 23:40–43"> And they find written in the law that Jehovah commanded by the hand of Moses, that the sons of Israel dwell in booths in the feast, in the seventh month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 16:16; Leviticus 23:4"> and that they proclaim and cause to pass over all their cities, (and in Jerusalem,) saying, 'Go ye out to the mount, and bring leaves of the olive, and leaves of the oil tree, and leaves of the myrtle, and leaves of the palms, and leaves of thick trees, to make booths as it is written.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; 2 Kings 14:13; Nehemiah 12:37"> And the people go out, and bring in, and make for themselves booths, each on his roof, and in their courts, and in the courts of the house of God, and in the broad place of the water-gate, and in the broad place of the gate of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:13; Ezra 3:4; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> And they make — all the assembly of the captives of the captivity — booths, and they sit in booths; for the sons of Israel had not done, from the days of Jeshua son of Nun, so unto that day, and there is very great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 29:35; Leviticus 23:36; Deuteronomy 31:10–13"> And he readeth in the book of the law of God day by day, from the first day till the last day, and they make a feast seven days, and on the eighth day a restraint, according to the ordinance.</span>
