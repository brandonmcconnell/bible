<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Zechariah 7:1; Esther 1:2"> Words of Nehemiah son of Hachaliah. And it cometh to pass, in the month of Chisleu, the twentieth year, and I have been in Shushan the palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Ezekiel 6:9; Jeremiah 44:14"> and come in doth Hanani, one of my brethren, he and men of Judah, and I ask them concerning the Jews, the escaped part that have been left of the captivity, and concerning Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 Kings 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> and they say to me, 'Those left, who have been left of the captivity there in the province, &#91;are&#93; in great evil, and in reproach, and the wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burnt with fire.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Psalms 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> And it cometh to pass, at my hearing these words, I have sat down, and I weep and mourn &#91;for&#93; days, and I am fasting and praying before the God of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; Daniel 9:4–19; Deuteronomy 7:21"> And I say, 'I beseech thee, O Jehovah, God of the heavens, God, the great and the fearful, keeping the covenant and kindness for those loving Him, and for those keeping His commands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Daniel 9:4; Psalms 34:15"> let Thine ear be, I pray Thee, attentive, and Thine eyes open, to hearken unto the prayer of Thy servant, that I am praying before Thee to-day, by day and by night, concerning the sons of Israel Thy servants, and confessing concerning the sins of the sons of Israel, that we have sinned against Thee; yea, I and the house of my father have sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 9:5–6; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:13"> we have acted very corruptly against Thee, and have not kept the commands, and the statutes, and the judgments, that Thou didst command Moses Thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Deuteronomy 4:25–27; Leviticus 26:33–46"> 'Remember, I pray Thee, the word that Thou didst command Moses Thy servant, saying, Ye — ye trespass — I scatter you among peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11–14; Deuteronomy 30:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> and ye have turned back unto Me, and kept My commands, and done them — if your outcast is in the end of the heavens, thence I gather them, and have brought them in unto the place that I have chosen to cause My name to tabernacle there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:29; Daniel 9:15–27; Exodus 32:11"> And they &#91;are&#93; Thy servants, and Thy people, whom Thou hast ransomed by Thy great power, and by Thy strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Proverbs 21:1"> 'I beseech Thee, O Lord, let, I pray Thee, Thine ear be attentive unto the prayer of Thy servant, and unto the prayer of Thy servants, those delighting to fear Thy Name; and give prosperity, I pray Thee, to Thy servant to-day, and give him for mercies before this man;' and I have been butler to the king.</span>
