<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Many teachers become not, my brethren, having known that greater judgment we shall receive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> for we all make many stumbles; if any one in word doth not stumble, this one &#91;is&#93; a perfect man, able to bridle also the whole body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> lo, the bits we put into the mouths of the horses for their obeying us, and their whole body we turn about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> lo, also the ships, being so great, and by fierce winds being driven, are led about by a very small helm, whithersoever the impulse of the helmsman doth counsel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> so also the tongue is a little member, and doth boast greatly; lo, a little fire how much wood it doth kindle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> and the tongue &#91;is&#93; a fire, the world of the unrighteousness, so the tongue is set in our members, which is spotting our whole body, and is setting on fire the course of nature, and is set on fire by the gehenna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every nature, both of beasts and of fowls, both of creeping things and things of the sea, is subdued, and hath been subdued, by the human nature,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> and the tongue no one of men is able to subdue, &#91;it is&#93; an unruly evil, full of deadly poison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> with it we do bless the God and Father, and with it we do curse the men made according to the similitude of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> out of the same mouth doth come forth blessing and cursing; it doth not need, my brethren, these things so to happen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> doth the fountain out of the same opening pour forth the sweet and the bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> is a fig-tree able, my brethren, olives to make? or a vine figs? so no fountain salt and sweet water &#91;is able&#93; to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who &#91;is&#93; wise and intelligent among you? let him shew out of the good behaviour his works in meekness of wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> and if bitter zeal ye have, and rivalry in your heart, glory not, nor lie against the truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> this wisdom is not descending from above, but earthly, physical, demon-like,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> for where zeal and rivalry &#91;are&#93;, there is insurrection and every evil matter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> and the wisdom from above, first, indeed, is pure, then peaceable, gentle, easily entreated, full of kindness and good fruits, uncontentious, and unhypocritical: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> and the fruit of the righteousness in peace is sown to those making peace.</span>
