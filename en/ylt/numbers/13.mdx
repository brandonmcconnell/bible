<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> 'Send for thee men, and they spy the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the sons of Israel; one man, one man for the tribe of his fathers ye do send, every one a prince among them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And Moses sendeth them from the wilderness of Paran by the command of Jehovah; all of them &#91;are&#93; men, heads of the sons of Israel they are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and these their names: For the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> For the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> For the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea, son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the tribe of Joseph, (for the tribe of Manasseh,) Gaddi son of Susi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vopshi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> These &#91;are&#93; the names of the men whom Moses hath sent to spy the land; and Moses calleth Hoshea son of Nun, Jehoshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> And Moses sendeth them to spy the land of Canaan, and saith unto them, 'Go ye up this &#91;way&#93; into the south, and ye have gone up the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> and have seen the land what it &#91;is&#93;, and the people which is dwelling on it, whether it &#91;is&#93; strong or feeble; whether it &#91;is&#93; few or many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and what the land &#91;is&#93; in which it is dwelling, whether it &#91;is&#93; good or bad; and what &#91;are&#93; the cities in which it is dwelling, whether in camps or in fortresses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Ezekiel 34:14"> And what the land &#91;is&#93;, whether it &#91;is&#93; fat or lean; whether there is wood in it or not; and ye have strengthened yourselves, and have taken of the fruit of the land;' and the days &#91;are&#93; days of the first-fruits of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> And they go up and spy the land, from the wilderness of Zin unto Rehob at the going in to Hamath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Isaiah 19:11"> and they go up by the south, and come in unto Hebron, and there &#91;are&#93; Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, children of Anak (and Hebron was built seven years before Zoan in Egypt),</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> and they come in unto the brook of Eshcol, and cut down thence a branch and one cluster of grapes, and they bear it on a staff by two, also &#91;some&#93; of the pomegranates, and of the figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> That place hath &#91;one&#93; called Brook of Eshcol, because of the cluster which the sons of Israel cut from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> And they turn back from spying the land at the end of forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And they go and come in unto Moses, and unto Aaron, and unto all the company of the sons of Israel, unto the wilderness of Paran, to Kadesh; and they bring them and all the company back word, and shew them the fruit of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And they recount to him, and say, 'We came in unto the land whither thou hast sent us, and also it &#91;is&#93; flowing with milk and honey — and this &#91;is&#93; its fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> only, surely the people which is dwelling in the land &#91;is&#93; strong; and the cities are fenced, very great; and also children of Anak we have seen there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> Amalek is dwelling in the land of the south, and the Hittite, and the Jebusite, and the Amorite is dwelling in the hill country, and the Canaanite is dwelling by the sea, and by the side of the Jordan.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> And Caleb stilleth the people concerning Moses, and saith, 'Let us certainly go up — and we have possessed it; for we are thoroughly able for it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> And the men who have gone up with him said, 'We are not able to go up against the people, for it &#91;is&#93; stronger than we;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Ezekiel 36:13–14"> and they bring out an evil account of the land which they have spied unto the sons of Israel, saying, 'The land into which we passed over to spy it, is a land eating up its inhabitants; and all the people whom we saw in its midst &#91;are&#93; men of stature;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Isaiah 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> and there we saw the Nephilim, sons of Anak, of the Nephilim; and we are in our own eyes as grasshoppers; and so we were in their eyes.'</span>
