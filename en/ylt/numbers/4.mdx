<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, and unto Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> 'Take up the sum of the sons of Kohath from the midst of the sons of Levi, by their families, by the house of their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3–5; 2 Kings 11:4–12"> from a son of thirty years and upward, even till a son of fifty years, every one going in to the host, to do work in the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Mark 13:34; Numbers 4:30"> 'This &#91;is&#93; the service of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting, the holy of holies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3; Exodus 40:3"> that Aaron and his sons have come in, in the journeying of the camp, and have taken down the vail of the hanging, and have covered with it the ark of the testimony;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:13–15; Exodus 35:19; Numbers 4:7–8"> and have put on it a covering of badger skin, and have spread a garment completely of blue above, and have placed its staves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 37:10–16; Exodus 25:23–30"> 'And on the table of the presence they spread a garment of blue, and have put on it the dishes, and the spoons, and the bowls, and the cups of the libation, and the bread of continuity is on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:6–7; Numbers 4:11–13; Numbers 4:9"> and they have spread over them a garment of scarlet, and have covered it with a covering of badger skin, and have placed its staves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Exodus 37:17–24; Revelation 1:20–1"> and have taken a garment of blue, and have covered the candlestick of the lamp, and its lights, and its snuffers, and its snuff-dishes, and all its oil vessels wherewith they minister to it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:12; Numbers 4:6"> and they have put it and all its vessels unto a covering of badger skin, and have put &#91;it&#93; on the bar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Exodus 30:1–19; Exodus 40:26–27"> 'And on the golden altar they spread a garment of blue, and have covered it with a covering of badger skin, and have placed its staves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:29; Exodus 25:9; 2 Chronicles 4:22"> and have taken all the vessels of ministry wherewith they minister in the sanctuary, and have put &#91;them&#93; unto a garment of blue, and have covered them with a covering of badger skin, and have put &#91;them&#93; on the bar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Exodus 39:1; Exodus 39:41"> and have removed the ashes of the altar, and have spread over it a garment of purple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:19; Exodus 38:1–7"> and have put on it all its vessels wherewith they minister about it, the censers, the hooks, and the shovels, and the bowls, all the vessels of the altar, and have spread on it a covering of badger skin, and have placed its staves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 7:9; Deuteronomy 31:9"> 'And Aaron hath finished — his sons also — covering the sanctuary, and all the vessels of the sanctuary, in the journeying of the camp, and afterwards do the sons of Kohath come in to bear &#91;it&#93;, and they do not come unto the holy thing, that they have died; these &#91;things are&#93; the burden of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:6; Leviticus 24:2; Exodus 30:23–38"> 'And the oversight of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, &#91;is&#93; the oil of the lamp, and the spice-perfume, and the present of continuity, and the anointing oil, the oversight of all the tabernacle, and of all that &#91;is&#93; in it, in the sanctuary, and in its vessels.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 17:10; Numbers 18:5"> 'Ye do not cut off the tribe of the families of the Kohathite from the midst of the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 4:15"> but this do to them, and they have lived, and do not die in their drawing nigh the holy of holies: — Aaron and his sons go in, and have set them, each man to his service, and unto his burden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 19:21; Numbers 4:15"> and they go not in to see when the holy thing is swallowed, that they have died.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 3:18; Numbers 3:21; Numbers 3:24"> 'Take up the sum of the sons of Gershon also by the house of their fathers, by their families;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:7"> from a son of thirty years and upward, till a son of fifty years thou dost number them, every one who is going in to serve the host, to do the service in the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Numbers 4:31–32; Numbers 4:47"> 'This &#91;is&#93; the service of the families of the Gershonite, to serve — and for burden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 3:25–26; Exodus 26:14; Numbers 7:5–7"> and they have borne the curtains of the tabernacle, and the tent of meeting, its covering, and the covering of the badger &#91;skin&#93; which &#91;is&#93; on it above, and the vail at the opening of the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 27:9"> and the hangings of the court, and the vail at the opening of the gate of the court which &#91;is&#93; by the tabernacle, and by the altar round about, and their cords, and all the vessels of their service, and all that is made for them — and they have served.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:70; 1 Corinthians 11:2"> 'By the command of Aaron and his sons is all the service of the sons of the Gershonite in all their burden, and in all their service; and ye have laid a charge on them concerning the charge of all their burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; 1 Corinthians 12:5–6"> This &#91;is&#93; the service of the families of the sons of the Gershonite in the tent of meeting; and their charge &#91;is&#93; under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 3:33–35"> 'The sons of Merari, by their families, by the house of their fathers, thou dost number them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 2:4"> from a son of thirty years and upward even unto a son of fifty years thou dost number them, every one who is going in to the host, to do the service of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 3:36–37; Exodus 26:15; Numbers 7:8–9"> 'And this &#91;is&#93; the charge of their burden, of all their service in the tent of meeting; the boards of the tabernacle, and its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; 1 Chronicles 9:29; Numbers 3:8"> and the pillars of the court round about, and their sockets, and their pins, and their cords, of all their vessels, and of all their service; and by name ye do number the vessels of the charge of their burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 4:28; Joshua 3:6; Isaiah 3:6"> 'This &#91;is&#93; the service of the families of the sons of Merari, for all their service, in the tent of meeting, by the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Moses numbereth — Aaron also, and the princes of the company — the sons of the Kohathite, by their families, and by the house of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> from a son of thirty years and upward even unto a son of fifty years, every one who is going in to the host, for service in the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and their numbered ones, by their families, are two thousand seven hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> These &#91;are&#93; those numbered of the families of the Kohathite, every one who is serving in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron numbered, by the command of Jehovah, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And those numbered of the sons of Gershon, by their families, and by the house of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> from a son of thirty years and upward even unto a son of fifty years, every one who is going in to the host, for service in the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:32"> even their numbered ones, by their families, by the house of their fathers, are two thousand and six hundred and thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> These &#91;are&#93; those numbered of the families of the sons of Gershon, every one who is serving in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the command of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And those numbered of the families of the sons of Merari, by their families, by the house of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> from a son of thirty years and upward even unto a son of fifty years, every one who is going in to the host, for service in the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Corinthians 12:8–12"> even their numbered ones, by their families, are three thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 4:29"> These &#91;are&#93; those numbered of the families of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron numbered, by the command of Jehovah, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> All those numbered, whom Moses numbered — Aaron also, and the princes of Israel — of the Levites, by their families, and by the house of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–31; Romans 12:6–8; Numbers 4:3"> from a son of thirty years and upward even unto a son of fifty years, every one who is going in to do the work of the service, even the service of burden in the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Matthew 20:16; Matthew 22:15"> even their numbered ones are eight thousand and five hundred and eighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 1:54; Numbers 4:31; Numbers 4:15"> by the command of Jehovah hath &#91;one&#93; numbered them, by the hand of Moses, each man by his service, and by his burden, with his numbered ones, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
