<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, in the plains of Moab, by Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> 'Command the sons of Israel, and they have given to the Levites of the inheritance of their possession cities to inhabit; also a suburb for the cities round about them ye do give to the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> And the cities have been to them to inhabit, and their suburbs are for their cattle, and for their goods, and for all their beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> 'And the suburbs of the cities which ye give to the Levites &#91;are&#93;, from the wall of the city and without, a thousand cubits round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And ye have measured from the outside of the city, the east quarter, two thousand by the cubit, and the south quarter, two thousand by the cubit, and the west quarter, two thousand by the cubit, and the north quarter, two thousand by the cubit; and the city &#91;is&#93; in the midst; this is to them the suburbs of the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalms 9:9"> 'And the cities which ye give to the Levites &#91;are&#93; the six cities of refuge, which ye give for the fleeing thither of the man-slayer, and besides them ye give forty and two cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> all the cities which ye give to the Levites &#91;are&#93; forty and eight cities, them and their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> And the cities which ye give &#91;are&#93; of the possession of the sons of Israel, from the many ye multiply, and from the few ye diminish; each, according to his inheritance which they inherit, doth give of his cities to the Levites.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When ye are passing over the Jordan to the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> and have prepared to yourselves cities — cities of refuge they are to you — then fled thither hath a man-slayer, smiting a person unawares,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> and the cities have been to you for a refuge from the redeemer, and the man-slayer doth not die till his standing before the company for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> 'As to the cities which ye give — six &#91;are&#93; cities of refuge to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> the three of the cities ye give beyond the Jordan, and the three of the cities ye give in the land of Canaan; cities of refuge they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> To sons of Israel, and to a sojourner, and to a settler in their midst, are these six cities for a refuge, for the fleeing thither of any one smiting a person unawares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> 'And if with an instrument of iron he hath smitten him, and he dieth, he &#91;is&#93; a murderer: the murderer is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> 'And if with a stone &#91;in&#93; the hand, wherewith he dieth, he hath smitten him, and he dieth, he &#91;is&#93; a murderer: the murderer is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> 'Or with a wooden instrument &#91;in&#93; the hand, wherewith he dieth, he hath smitten him, and he dieth, he &#91;is&#93; a murderer: the murderer is certainly put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> 'The redeemer of blood himself doth put the murderer to death; in his coming against him he doth put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> 'And if in hatred he thrust him through, or hath cast &#91;anything&#93; at him by lying in wait, and he dieth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or in enmity he hath smitten him with his hand, and he dieth; the smiter is certainly put to death; he &#91;is&#93; a murderer; the redeemer of blood doth put the murderer to death in his coming against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> 'And if, in an instant, without enmity, he hath thrust him through, or hath cast at him any instrument, without lying in wait;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or with any stone wherewith he dieth, without seeing, and causeth &#91;it&#93; to fall upon him, and he dieth, and he &#91;is&#93; not his enemy, nor seeking his evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> then have the company judged between the smiter and the redeemer of blood, by these judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> 'And the company have delivered the man-slayer out of the hand of the redeemer of blood, and the company have caused him to turn back unto the city of his refuge, whither he hath fled, and he hath dwelt in it till the death of the chief priest, who hath been anointed with the holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> 'And if the man-slayer at all go out &#91;from&#93; the border of the city of his refuge whither he fleeth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and the redeemer of blood hath found him at the outside of the border of the city of his refuge, and the redeemer of blood hath slain the man-slayer, blood is not for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> for in the city of his refuge he doth dwell till the death of the chief priest; and after the death of the chief priest doth the man-slayer turn back unto the city of his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> 'And these things have been to you for a statute of judgment to your generations, in all your dwellings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> whoso smiteth a person, by the mouth of witnesses doth &#91;one&#93; slay the murderer; and one witness doth not testify against a person — to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalms 51:14"> 'And ye take no atonement for the life of a murderer who &#91;is&#93; condemned — to die, for he is certainly put to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> and ye take no atonement for him to flee unto the city of his refuge, to turn back to dwell in the land, until the death of the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalms 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> 'And ye profane not the land which ye &#91;are&#93; in, for blood profaneth the land; as to the land, it is not pardoned for blood which is shed in it except by the blood of him who sheddeth it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalms 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> and ye defile not the land in which ye are dwelling, in the midst of which I do tabernacle, for I Jehovah do tabernacle in the midst of the sons of Israel.'</span>
