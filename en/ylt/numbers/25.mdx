<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Numbers 33:49; Joshua 2:1"> And Israel dwelleth in Shittim, and the people begin to go a-whoring unto daughters of Moab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> and they call for the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people eat, and bow themselves to their gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalms 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> and Israel is joined to Baal-Peor, and the anger of Jehovah burneth against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Take all the chiefs of the people, and hang them before Jehovah — over-against the sun; and the fierceness of the anger of Jehovah doth turn back from Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> And Moses saith unto the judges of Israel, 'Slay ye each his men who are joined to Baal-Peor.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> And lo, a man of the sons of Israel hath come, and bringeth in unto his brethren the Midianitess, before the eyes of Moses, and before the eyes of all the company of the sons of Israel, who are weeping at the opening of the tent of meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> and Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, the priest, seeth, and riseth from the midst of the company, and taketh a javelin in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalms 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> and goeth in after the man of Israel unto the hollow place, and pierceth them both, the man of Israel and the woman — unto her belly, and the plague is restrained from the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> and the dead by the plague are four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> 'Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, hath turned back My fury from the sons of Israel, by his being zealous with My zeal in their midst, and I have not consumed the sons of Israel in My zeal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> 'Therefore say, Lo, I am giving to him My covenant of peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> and it hath been to him and to his seed after him a covenant of a priesthood age-during, because that he hath been zealous for his God, and doth make atonement for the sons of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> And the name of the man of Israel who is smitten, who hath been smitten with the Midianitess, &#91;is&#93; Zimri son of Salu, prince of the house of a father of the Simeonite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> and the name of the woman who is smitten, the Midianitess, &#91;is&#93; Cozbi daughter of Zur, head of a people — of the house of a father in Midian &#91;is&#93; he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> 'Distress the Midianites, and ye have smitten them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> for they are adversaries to you with their frauds, &#91;with&#93; which they have acted fraudulently to you, concerning the matter of Peor, and concerning the matter of Cozbi, daughter of a prince of Midian, their sister, who is smitten in the day of the plague for the matter of Peor.'</span>
