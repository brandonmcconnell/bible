<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> 'Command the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When ye are coming in unto the land of Canaan — this &#91;is&#93; the land which falleth to you by inheritance, the land of Canaan, by its borders —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> then hath the south quarter been to you from the wilderness of Zin, by the sides of Edom, yea, the south border hath been to you from the extremity of the Salt Sea, eastward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> and the border hath turned round to you from the south to the ascent of Akrabbim, and hath passed on to Zin, and its outgoings have been from the south to Kadesh-Barnea, and it hath gone out at Hazar-Addar, and hath passed on to Azmon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> and the border hath turned round from Azmon to the brook of Egypt, and its outgoings have been at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> 'As to the west border, even the great sea hath been to you a border; this is to you the west border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> 'And this is to you the north border: from the great sea ye mark out for yourselves mount Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> from mount Hor ye mark out to go in to Hamath, and the outgoings of the border have been to Zedad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> and the border hath gone out to Ziphron, and its outgoings have been at Hazar-Enan; this is to you the north border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> 'And ye have marked out for yourselves for the border eastward, from Hazar-Enan to Shepham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> and the border hath gone down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east of Ain, and the border hath gone down, and hath smitten against the shoulder of the sea of Chinnereth eastward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> and the border hath gone down to the Jordan, and its outgoings have been at the Salt Sea; this is for you the land by its borders round about.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> And Moses commandeth the sons of Israel, saying, 'This &#91;is&#93; the land which ye inherit by lot, which Jehovah hath commanded to give to the nine tribes and the half of the tribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> for the tribe of the sons of Reuben have received, by the house of their fathers; and the tribe of the children of Gad, by the house of their fathers; and the half of the tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> the two tribes and the half of the tribe have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho, eastward, at the &#91;sun&#93; -rising.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> 'These &#91;are&#93; the names of the men who give to you the inheritance of the land: Eleazar the priest, and Joshua son of Nun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> and one prince — one prince — for a tribe ye do take to give the land by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> 'And these &#91;are&#93; the names of the men: of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> and of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, Shemuel son of Aminihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and of the tribe of the sons of Dan, the prince Bukki son of Jogli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> of the sons of Joseph, of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh, the prince Hanniel son of Ephod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and of the tribe of the sons of Ephraim, the prince Kemuel son of Shiphtan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun, the prince Elizaphan son of Parnach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, the prince Paltiel son of Azzan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and of the tribe of the sons of Asher, the prince Ahihud son of Shelomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, the prince Pedahel son of Ammihud.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These &#91;are&#93; those whom Jehovah hath commanded to give the sons of Israel inheritance in the land of Canaan.</span>
