<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> 'Execute the vengeance of the sons of Israel against the Midianites — afterwards thou art gathered unto thy people.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> And Moses speaketh unto the people, saying, 'Be ye armed some of you for the host, and they are against Midian, to put the vengeance of Jehovah on Midian;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> a thousand for a tribe — a thousand for a tribe, to all the tribes of Israel — ye do send to the host.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there are given out of the thousands of Israel a thousand for a tribe, twelve thousand armed ones of the host;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> and Moses sendeth them, a thousand for a tribe, to the host, them and Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest, to the host; and the holy vessels, and the trumpets of the shouting, in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And they war against Midian, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses, and slay every male;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> and the kings of Midian they have slain, besides their pierced ones, Evi, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, five kings of Midian; and Balaam son of Beor, they have slain with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> And the sons of Israel take captive the women of Midian, and their infants; and all their cattle, and all their substance, and all their wealth they have plundered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> and all their cities, with their habitations, and all their towers, they have burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they take all the spoil, and all the prey, among man and among beast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> and they bring in, unto Moses, and unto Eleazar the priest, and unto the company of the sons of Israel, the captives, and the prey, and the spoil, unto the camp, unto the plains of Moab, which &#91;are&#93; by Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and all the princes of the company, go out to meet them, unto the outside of the camp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> and Moses is wroth against the inspectors of the force, chiefs of the thousands, and chiefs of the hundreds, who are coming in from the host of the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And Moses saith unto them, 'Have ye kept alive every female?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> lo, they — they have been to the sons of Israel, through the word of Balaam, to cause a trespass against Jehovah in the matter of Peor, and the plague is in the company of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> 'And now, slay ye every male among the infants, yea, every woman known of man by the lying of a male ye have slain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> and all the infants among the women, who have not known the lying of a male, ye have kept alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> 'And ye, encamp ye at the outside of the camp seven days — any who hath slain a person, and any who hath come against a pierced one, ye cleanse yourselves on the third day, and on the seventh day — ye and your captives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> and every garment, and every skin vessel, and every work of goats' &#91;hair&#93;, and every wooden vessel, ye yourselves cleanse.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> And Eleazar the priest saith unto the men of the host who go in to battle, 'This &#91;is&#93; the statute of the law which Jehovah hath commanded Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> only, the gold, and the silver, the brass, the iron, the tin, and the lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> every thing which may go into fire, ye cause to pass over through fire, and it hath been clean; only, with the water of separation it is cleansed, and all that may not go into fire, ye cause to pass over through water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> and ye have washed your garments on the seventh day, and have been clean, and afterwards ye come in unto the camp.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> 'Take up the sum of the prey of the captives, among man and among beast, thou, and Eleazar the priest, and the heads of the fathers of the company;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> and thou hast halved the prey between those handling the battle who go out to the host and all the company;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> and thou hast raised a tribute to Jehovah from the men of war, who go out to the host, one body out of five hundred, of man, and of the herd, and of the asses, and of the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> from their half ye do take, and thou hast given to Eleazar the priest — the heave-offering of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> 'And from the sons of Israel's half thou dost take one possession out of fifty, of man, of the herd, of the asses, and of the flock, of all the cattle, and thou hast given them to the Levites keeping the charge of the tabernacle of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moses doth — Eleazar the priest also — as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the prey, the residue of the spoil which the people of the host have spoiled, is of the flock six hundred thousand, and seventy thousand, and five thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and of the herd two and seventy thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and of asses one and sixty thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and of human beings — of the women who have not known the lying of a male — all the persons &#91;are&#93; two and thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the half — the portion of those who go out into the host — the number of the flock is three hundred thousand, and thirty thousand, and seven thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And the tribute to Jehovah of the sheep is six hundred five and seventy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and the herd &#91;is&#93; six and thirty thousand, and their tribute to Jehovah &#91;is&#93; two and seventy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and the asses &#91;are&#93; thirty thousand and five hundred, and their tribute to Jehovah &#91;is&#93; one and sixty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and the human beings &#91;are&#93; sixteen thousand, and their tribute to Jehovah &#91;is&#93; two and thirty persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses giveth the tribute — Jehovah's heave-offering — to Eleazar the priest, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And of the sons of Israel's half, which Moses halved from the men who war —</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and the company's half is, of the flock three hundred thousand, and thirty thousand, seven thousand and five hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and of the herd six and thirty thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and of asses thirty thousand and five hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and of human beings sixteen thousand —</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> Moses taketh from the sons of Israel's half the one possession from the fifty, of man and of beast, and giveth them to the Levites keeping the charge of the tabernacle of Jehovah, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And the inspectors whom the thousands of the host hath, (heads of the thousands and heads of the hundreds), draw near unto Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> and they say unto Moses, 'Thy servants have taken up the sum of the men of war who &#91;are&#93; with us, and not a man of us hath been missed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> and we bring near Jehovah's offering, each that which he hath found, vessels of gold — chain, and bracelet, seal-ring, &#91;ear&#93; -ring, and bead — to make atonement for ourselves before Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moses receiveth — Eleazar the priest also — the gold from them, every made vessel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> and all the gold of the heave-offering which they have lifted up to Jehovah is sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels, from heads of the thousands, and from heads of the hundreds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> (the men of the host have spoiled each for himself);</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> and Moses taketh — Eleazar the priest also — the gold from the heads of the thousands and of the hundreds, and they bring it in unto the tent of meeting — a memorial for the sons of Israel before Jehovah.</span>
</div>
