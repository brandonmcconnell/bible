<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When ye come in unto the land of your dwellings, which I am giving to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> then ye have prepared a fire-offering to Jehovah, a burnt-offering, or a sacrifice, at separating a vow or free-will-offering, or in your appointed things, to make a sweet fragrance to Jehovah, out of the herd, or out of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> 'And he who is bringing near his offering to Jehovah hath brought near a present of flour, a tenth deal, mixed with a fourth of the hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> and wine for a libation, a fourth of the hin thou dost prepare for the burnt-offering or for a sacrifice, for the one lamb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> or for a ram thou dost prepare a present of flour, two-tenth deals, mixed with oil, a third of the hin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and wine for a libation, a third part of the hin, thou dost bring near — a sweet fragrance to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> 'And when thou makest a son of the herd a burnt-offering or a sacrifice, at separating a vow or peace-offerings to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> then he hath brought near for the son of the herd a present of flour, three-tenth deals, mixed with oil, a half of the hin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> and wine thou bringest near for a libation, a half of the hin — a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> thus it is done for the one ox, or for the one ram, or for a lamb of the sheep or of the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> 'According to the number that ye prepare, so ye do to each, according to their number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> every native doth thus with these, at bringing near a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and when a sojourner sojourneth with you, or whoso &#91;is&#93; in your midst to your generations, and he hath made a fire-offering of sweet fragrance to Jehovah, as ye do so he doth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> 'One statute is for you of the congregation and for the sojourner who is sojourning, a statute age-during to your generations: as ye &#91;are&#93; so is the sojourner before Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> one law and one ordinance is to you and to the sojourner who is sojourning with you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, In your coming in unto the land whither I am bringing you in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> then it hath been, in your eating of the bread of the land, ye heave up a heave-offering to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> the beginning of your dough a cake ye heave up — a heave-offering; as the heave-offering of a threshing-floor, so ye do heave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> Of the beginning of your dough ye do give to Jehovah a heave-offering — to your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> 'And when ye err, and do not all these commands which Jehovah hath spoken unto Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> the whole that Jehovah hath charged upon you by the hand of Moses, from the day that Jehovah hath commanded, and henceforth, to your generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> then it hath been, if from the eyes of the company it hath been done in ignorance, that all the company have prepared one bullock, a son of the herd, for a burnt-offering, for sweet fragrance to Jehovah, and its present, and its libation, according to the ordinance, and one kid of the goats for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> 'And the priest hath made atonement for all the company of the sons of Israel, and it hath been forgiven them, for it &#91;is&#93; ignorance, and they — they have brought in their offering, a fire-offering to Jehovah, even their sin-offering before Jehovah for their ignorance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> and it hath been forgiven to all the company of the sons of Israel, and to the sojourner who is sojourning in their midst; for to all the company &#91;it is done&#93; in ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> 'And if one person sin in ignorance, then he hath brought near a she-goat, daughter of a year, for a sin-offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> and the priest hath made atonement for the person who is erring, in his sinning in ignorance before Jehovah, by making atonement for him, and it hath been forgiven him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> for the native among the sons of Israel, and for the sojourner who is sojourning in their midst — one law is to you, for him who is doing &#91;anything&#93; through ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> 'And the person who doth &#91;aught&#93; with a high hand — of the native or of the sojourner — Jehovah he is reviling, and that person hath been cut off from the midst of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> because the word of Jehovah he despised, and His command hath broken — that person is certainly cut off; his iniquity &#91;is&#93; on him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> And the sons of Israel are in the wilderness, and they find a man gathering wood on the sabbath-day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> and those finding him gathering wood bring him near unto Moses, and unto Aaron, and unto all the company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> and they place him in ward, for it &#91;is&#93; not explained what is &#91;to be&#93; done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'The man is certainly put to death, all the company stoning him with stones, at the outside of the camp.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> And all the company bring him out unto the outside of the camp, and stone him with stones, and he dieth, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, and they have made for themselves fringes on the skirts of their garments, to their generations, and they have put on the fringe of the skirt a ribbon of blue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> and it hath been to you for a fringe, and ye have seen it, and have remembered all the commands of Jehovah, and have done them, and ye search not after your heart, and after your eyes, after which ye are going a-whoring;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> so that ye remember and have done all My commands, and ye have been holy to your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah your God, who hath brought you out from the land of Egypt to become your God; I, Jehovah, &#91;am&#93; your God.'</span>
