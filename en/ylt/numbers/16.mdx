<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 11:6; Exodus 6:21"> And Korah, son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, taketh both Dathan and Abiram sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth, sons of Reuben,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:9; Ezekiel 23:10; Genesis 6:4"> and they rise up before Moses, with men of the sons of Israel, two hundred and fifty, princes of the company, called of the convention, men of name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:16; Numbers 14:14; Exodus 19:6"> and they are assembled against Moses and against Aaron, and say unto them, 'Enough of you! for all the company — all of them &#91;are&#93; holy, and in their midst &#91;is&#93; Jehovah; and wherefore do ye lift yourselves up above the assembly of Jehovah?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 20:6; Joshua 7:6"> And Moses heareth, and falleth on his face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 17:5; 2 Timothy 2:19"> and he speaketh unto Korah, and unto all his company, saying, 'Morning! — and Jehovah is knowing those who are his, and him who is holy, and hath brought near unto Him; even him whom He doth fix on He bringeth near unto Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Numbers 16:46–48; 1 Kings 18:21–23"> This do: take to yourselves censers, Korah, and all his company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 16:3; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Kings 18:17–18"> and put in them fire, and put on them perfume, before Jehovah to-morrow, and it hath been, the man whom Jehovah chooseth, he &#91;is&#93; the holy one; — enough of you, sons of Levi.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Moses saith unto Korah, 'Hear ye, I pray you, sons of Levi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> is it little to you that the God of Israel hath separated you from the company of Israel to bring you near unto Himself, to do the service of the tabernacle of Jehovah, and to stand before the company to serve them? —</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; 3 John 1:9; Numbers 18:7"> yea, He doth bring thee near, and all thy brethren the sons of Levi with thee — and ye have sought also the priesthood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:7–8; Exodus 17:2; Numbers 16:3"> Therefore, thou and all thy company who are met &#91;are&#93; against Jehovah; and Aaron, what &#91;is&#93; he, that ye murmur against him?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13–14; Jude 1:8; Proverbs 29:9"> And Moses sendeth to call for Dathan and for Abiram sons of Eliab, and they say, 'We do not come up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Exodus 2:14; Numbers 11:5"> is it little that thou hast brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey to put us to death in a wilderness that thou also certainly makest thyself prince over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:24; Numbers 36:8–10; Numbers 20:5"> Yea, unto a land flowing with milk and honey thou hast not brought us in, nor dost thou give to us an inheritance of field and vineyard; the eyes of these men dost thou pick out? we do not come up.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:4–5; 2 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:10"> And it is very displeasing to Moses, and he saith unto Jehovah, 'Turn not Thou unto their present; not one ass from them have I taken, nor have I afflicted one of them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3; 1 Samuel 12:7; 2 Timothy 2:14"> And Moses saith unto Korah, 'Thou and all thy company, be ye before Jehovah, thou, and they, and Aaron, to-morrow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:7"> and take ye each his censer, and ye have put on them perfume, and brought near before Jehovah, each his censer, two hundred and fifty censers; and thou and Aaron, each his censer.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they take each his censer, and put on them fire, and lay on them perfume, and they stand at the opening of the tent of meeting, with Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:10; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 16:7"> And Korah assembleth against them all the company unto the opening of the tent of meeting, and the honour of Jehovah is seen by all the company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:45; Exodus 32:10; Psalms 73:19"> 'Be ye separated from the midst of this company, and I consume them in a moment;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:16; Zechariah 12:1; Job 12:10"> and they fall on their faces, and say, 'God, God of the spirits of all flesh — the one man sinneth, and against all the company Thou art wroth!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 16:21"> 'Speak unto the company, saying, Go ye up from round about the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:16–17; Numbers 11:25; Numbers 11:30"> And Moses riseth, and goeth unto Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel go after him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Revelation 18:4"> and he speaketh unto the company, saying, 'Turn aside, I pray you, from the tents of these wicked men, and come not against anything that they have, lest ye be consumed in all their sins.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 9:4; Isaiah 28:14; Job 40:10–11"> And they go up from the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan and Abiram, from round about, and Dathan, and Abiram have come out, standing at the opening of their tents, and their wives, and their sons, and their infants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:38; Exodus 3:12; John 14:11"> And Moses saith, 'By this ye do know that Jehovah hath sent me to do all these works, that &#91;they are&#93; not from my own heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 4:22; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 32:34"> if according to the death of all men these die — or the charge of all men is charged upon them — Jehovah hath not sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Numbers 16:33; Job 31:3"> and if a strange thing Jehovah do, and the ground hath opened her mouth and swallowed them, and all that they have, and they have gone down alive to Sheol — then ye have known that these men have despised Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 106:17–18; Numbers 27:3; Deuteronomy 11:6"> And it cometh to pass at his finishing speaking all these words, that the ground which &#91;is&#93; under them cleaveth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:17; Numbers 26:11; Revelation 12:16"> and the earth openeth her mouth, and swalloweth them, and their houses, and all the men who &#91;are&#93; for Korah, and all the goods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Isaiah 14:9; Psalms 9:15"> and they go down, they, and all that they have, alive to Sheol, and the earth closeth over them, and they perish from the midst of the assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; Isaiah 33:3; Revelation 6:15–17"> and all Israel who &#91;are&#93; round about them have fled at their voice, for they said, 'Lest the earth swallow us;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Leviticus 10:2; Psalms 106:18"> and fire hath come out from Jehovah, and consumeth the two hundred and fifty men bringing near the perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Numbers 16:18; Numbers 16:7"> 'Say unto Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, and he lifteth up the censers from the midst of the burning, and the fire scatter thou yonder, for they have been hallowed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Ezekiel 14:8; 2 Peter 2:6"> &#91;even&#93; the censers of these sinners against their own souls; and they have made them spread-out plates, a covering for the altar, for they have brought them near before Jehovah, and they are hallowed; and they are become a sign to the sons of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Eleazar the priest taketh the brazen censers which they who are burnt had brought near, and they spread them out, a covering for the altar —</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 18:4–7; 2 Chronicles 26:16–21"> a memorial to the sons of Israel, so that a stranger who is not of the seed of Aaron doth not draw near to make a perfume before Jehovah, and is not as Korah, and as his company, — as Jehovah hath spoken by the hand of Moses to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–7; Psalms 106:23; Numbers 14:2"> And all the company of the sons of Israel murmur, on the morrow, against Moses and against Aaron, saying, 'Ye — ye have put to death the people of Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Leviticus 9:23; Numbers 20:6"> And it cometh to pass, in the company being assembled against Moses and against Aaron, that they turn towards the tent of meeting, and lo, the cloud hath covered it, and the honour of Jehovah is seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and Moses cometh — Aaron also — unto the front of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 16:21–22; Numbers 16:24; Numbers 20:6"> 'Get you up from the midst of this company, and I consume them in a moment;' and they fall on their faces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Leviticus 10:6"> and Moses saith unto Aaron, 'Take the censer, and put on it fire from off the altar, and place perfume, and go, hasten unto the company, and make atonement for them, for the wrath hath gone out from the presence of Jehovah — the plague hath begun.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Deuteronomy 33:10–11; Psalms 106:29"> And Aaron taketh as Moses hath spoken, and runneth unto the midst of the assembly, and lo, the plague hath begun among the people; and he giveth the perfume, and maketh atonement for the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; Hebrews 7:24–25; Numbers 16:18"> and standeth between the dead and the living, and the plague is restrained;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 25:9; Numbers 16:32–35; Hebrews 2:1–3"> and those who die by the plague are fourteen thousand and seven hundred, apart from those who die for the matter of Korah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26–30; Numbers 16:43"> and Aaron turneth back unto Moses, unto the opening of the tent of meeting, and the plague hath been restrained.</span>
