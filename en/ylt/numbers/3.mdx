<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> And these &#91;are&#93; births of Aaron and Moses, in the day of Jehovah's speaking with Moses in mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> And these &#91;are&#93; the names of the sons of Aaron: the first-born Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> these &#91;are&#93; the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whose hand he hath consecrated for acting as priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> And Nadab dieth — Abihu also — before Jehovah, in their bringing near strange fire before Jehovah, in the wilderness of Sinai, and sons they had not; and Eleazar — Ithamar also — acteth as priest in the presence of Aaron their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> 'Bring near the tribe of Levi, and thou hast caused it to stand before Aaron the priest, and they have served him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> and kept his charge, and the charge of all the company before the tent of meeting, to do the service of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> and they have kept all the vessels of the tent of meeting, and the charge of the sons of Israel, to do the service of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> and thou hast given the Levites to Aaron and to his sons; they are surely given to him out of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> 'And Aaron and his sons thou dost appoint, and they have kept their priesthood, and the stranger who cometh near is put to death.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> 'And I, lo, I have taken the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel instead of every first-born opening a womb among the sons of Israel, and the Levites have been Mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> for Mine &#91;is&#93; every first-born, in the day of My smiting every first-born in the land of Egypt I have sanctified to Myself every first-born in Israel, from man unto beast; Mine they are; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> 'Number the sons of Levi by the house of their fathers, by their families; every male from a son of a month and upward thou dost number them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> And Moses numbereth them according to the command of Jehovah, as he hath been commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> And these are sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> And these &#91;are&#93; the names of the sons of Gershon by their families: Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> And the sons of Kohath, by their families, &#91;are&#93; Amram and Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> And the sons of Merari by their families &#91;are&#93; Mahli and Mushi; these are the families of the Levites, by the house of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Of Gershon &#91;is&#93; the family of the Libnite, and the family of the Shimite; these are the families of the Gershonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> Their numbered ones, in number, every male from a son of a month and upward, their numbered ones &#91;are&#93; seven thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The families of the Gershonite, behind the tabernacle, do encamp westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the prince of a father's house for the Gershonite &#91;is&#93; Eliasaph son of Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> And the charge of the sons of Gershon in the tent of meeting &#91;is&#93; the tabernacle, and the tent, its covering, and the vail at the opening of the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> and the hangings of the court, and the vail at the opening of the court, which &#91;is&#93; by the tabernacle and by the altar round about, and its cords, to all its service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> And of Kohath &#91;is&#93; the family of the Amramite, and the family of the Izharite, and the family of the Hebronite, and the family of the Uzzielite; these are families of the Kohathite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> In number, all the males, from a son of a month and upward, &#91;are&#93; eight thousand and six hundred, keeping the charge of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The families of the sons of Kohath encamp by the side of the tabernacle southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the prince of a father's house for the families of the Kohathite &#91;is&#93; Elizaphan son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> And their charge &#91;is&#93; the ark, and the table, and the candlestick, and the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary with which they serve, and the vail, and all its service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> And &#91;to&#93; the prince of the princes of the Levites, Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, &#91;is&#93; the oversight of the keepers of the charge of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> Of Merari &#91;is&#93; the family of the Mahlite, and the family of the Mushite; these &#91;are&#93; the families of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> And their numbered ones, in number, all the males from a son of a month and upward, &#91;are&#93; six thousand and two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> And the prince of a father's house for the families of Merari &#91;is&#93; Zuriel son of Abihail; by the side of the tabernacle they encamp northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> And the oversight — the charge of the sons of Merari — &#91;is&#93; the boards of the tabernacle, and its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets, and all its vessels, and all its service,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the pillars of the court round about, and their sockets, and their pins, and their cords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> And those encamping before the tabernacle eastward, before the tent of meeting, at the east, &#91;are&#93; Moses and Aaron, and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the sons of Israel, and the stranger who cometh near is put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> All those numbered of the Levites whom Moses numbered — Aaron also — by the command of Jehovah, by their families, every male from a son of a month and upward, &#91;are&#93; two and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Number every first-born male of the sons of Israel from a son of a month and upward, and take up the number of their names;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> and thou hast taken the Levites for Me (I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah), instead of every first-born among the sons of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites instead of every firstling among the cattle of the sons of Israel.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Moses numbereth, as Jehovah hath commanded him, all the first-born among the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> And all the first-born — male — by the number of names, from a son of a month and upward, of their numbered ones, are two and twenty thousand two hundred and seventy and three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> 'Take the Levites instead of every first-born among the sons of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites instead of their cattle; and the Levites have been Mine; I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> 'And &#91;from&#93; those ransomed of the two hundred and seventy and three (who are more than the Levites) of the first-born of the sons of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> thou hast even taken five shekels a-piece by the poll — by the shekel of the sanctuary thou takest; twenty gerahs the shekel &#91;is&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> and thou hast given the money to Aaron, and to his sons, whereby those over and above are ransomed.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And Moses taketh the ransom money from those over and above those ransomed by the Levites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> from the first-born of the sons of Israel he hath taken the money, a thousand and three hundred and sixty and five — by the shekel of the sanctuary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> and Moses giveth the money of those ransomed to Aaron, and to his sons, according to the command of Jehovah, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
