<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 14:8; Exodus 12:37; Exodus 13:4"> And they journey from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month, on the morrow of the passover have the sons of Israel gone out with a high hand, before the eyes of all the Egyptians —</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Isaiah 19:1; Exodus 18:11"> and the Egyptians are burying those whom Jehovah hath smitten among them, every first-born, and on their gods hath Jehovah done judgments —</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:22–26; Exodus 14:21–31"> And they journey from Pi-Hahiroth, and pass over through the midst of the sea, into the wilderness, and go a journey of three days in the wilderness of Etham, and encamp in Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 15:27"> And they journey from Marah, and come in to Elim, and in Elim &#91;are&#93; twelve fountains of waters, and seventy palm trees, and they encamp there;</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:1"> And they journey from the Red Sea, and encamp in the wilderness of Sin;</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they journey from Dophkah, and encamp in Alush;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:1–8; Exodus 19:2"> and they journey from Alush, and encamp in Rephidim; and there was there no water for the people to drink.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:34; Numbers 10:11–13; Numbers 10:33"> and they journey from the wilderness of Sinai, and encamp in Kibroth-Hattaavah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and they journey from Rimmon-Parez, and encamp in Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:1"> And they journey from Libnah, and encamp in Rissah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and they journey from Rissah, and encamp in Kehelathah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they journey from Kehelathah, and encamp in mount Shapher;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and they journey from mount Shapher, and encamp in Haradah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they journey from Haradah, and encamp in Makheloth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and they journey from Makheloth, and encamp in Tahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And they journey from Tahath, and encamp in Tarah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and they journey from Tarah, and encamp in Mithcah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they journey from Mithcah, and encamp in Hashmonah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:5–6"> and they journey from Hashmonah, and encamp in Moseroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Genesis 36:27; 1 Chronicles 1:43"> And they journey from Moseroth, and encamp in Bene-Jaakan;</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:7"> And they journey from Hor-Hagidgad, and encamp in Jotbathah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and they journey from Jotbathah, and encamp in Ebronah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26; 1 Kings 22:48"> And they journey from Ebronah, and encamp in Ezion-Gaber;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 27:14; Deuteronomy 32:51"> and they journey from Ezion-Gaber, and encamp in the wilderness of Zin, which &#91;is&#93; Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Numbers 20:22–23; Numbers 20:16"> And they journey from Kadesh, and encamp in mount Hor, in the extremity of the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 20:24–28"> And Aaron the priest goeth up unto mount Hor, by the command of Jehovah, and dieth there, in the fortieth year of the going out of the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first of the month;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and Aaron &#91;is&#93; a son of a hundred and twenty and three years in his dying in mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 21:1–9"> And the Canaanite — king Arad — who is dwelling in the south, in the land of Canaan, heareth of the coming of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 21:4"> And they journey from mount Hor, and encamp in Zalmonah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and they journey from Zalmonah, and encamp in Punon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 21:10"> And they journey from Punon, and encamp in Oboth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 21:11"> and they journey from Oboth, and encamp in Ije-Abarim, in the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they journey from Iim, and encamp in Dibon-Gad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 6:14; Isaiah 15:2"> and they journey from Dibon-Gad, and encamp in Almon-Diblathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Deuteronomy 34:1; Numbers 27:12"> And they journey from Almon-Diblathaim, and encamp in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> and they journey from the mountains of Abarim, and encamp in the plains of Moab, by Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Joshua 2:1; Joshua 13:20; Numbers 25:1–9"> And they encamp by the Jordan from Beth-Jeshimoth, unto Abel-Shittim, in the plains of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 33:48–49"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, in the plains of Moab, by Jordan, &#91;near&#93; Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and thou hast said unto them, When ye are passing over the Jordan unto the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> then ye have dispossessed all the inhabitants of the land from before you, and have destroyed all their imagery, yea, all their molten images ye destroy, and all their high places ye lay waste,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Psalms 115:16; Deuteronomy 32:8"> and ye have possessed the land, and dwelt in it, for to you I have given the land — to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Joshua 18:11–48; Joshua 15:1–12"> 'And ye have inherited the land by lot, by your families; to the many ye increase their inheritance, and to the few ye diminish their inheritance; whither the lot goeth out to him, it is his; by the tribes of your fathers ye inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Judges 2:3; Psalms 106:34–36; Deuteronomy 7:16"> 'And if ye do not dispossess the inhabitants of the land from before you, then it hath been, those whom ye let remain of them, &#91;are&#93; for pricks in your eyes, and for thorns in your sides, and they have distressed you on the land in which ye are dwelling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 21:23–24; Deuteronomy 28:63; Leviticus 20:23"> and it hath come to pass, as I thought to do to them — I do to you.'</span>
