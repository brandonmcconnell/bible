<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Ezekiel 19:14; Genesis 49:10"> 'Speak unto the sons of Israel, and take from them each a rod, for a father's house, from all their princes, for the house of their fathers, twelve rods; the name of each thou dost write on his rod,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Numbers 18:1; Numbers 3:2–3"> and Aaron's name thou dost write on the tribe of Levi; for one rod &#91;is&#93; for the head of their fathers' house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:36; Exodus 29:42–43; Exodus 25:16–22"> and thou hast placed them in the tent of meeting, before the testimony, where I meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Numbers 17:8; Numbers 16:11"> 'And it hath come to pass, the man's rod on whom I fix doth flourish, and I have caused to cease from off me the murmurings of the sons of Israel, which they are murmuring against you.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses speaketh unto the sons of Israel, and all their princes give unto him one rod for a prince, one rod for a prince, for their fathers' house, twelve rods, and the rod of Aaron &#91;is&#93; in the midst of their rods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> and Moses placeth the rods before Jehovah, in the tent of the testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; John 15:1–6; Psalms 132:17–18"> And it cometh to pass, on the morrow, that Moses goeth in unto the tent of the testimony, and lo, the rod of Aaron hath flourished for the house of Levi, and is bringing out flourishing, and doth blossom blossoms, and doth produce almonds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and Moses bringeth out all the rods from before Jehovah, unto all the sons of Israel, and they look, and take each his rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Ephesians 5:6; Isaiah 1:2"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Put back the rod of Aaron, before the testimony, for a charge, for a sign to the sons of rebellion, and thou dost remove their murmurings from off me, and they do not die;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and Moses doth as Jehovah hath commanded him; so he hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 26:11; Isaiah 57:16; Isaiah 6:5"> And the sons of Israel speak unto Moses, saying, 'Lo, we have expired; we have perished; we have all of us perished;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; 2 Samuel 6:6–12; Genesis 3:3"> any who is at all drawing near unto the tabernacle of Jehovah dieth; have we not been consumed — to expire?'</span>
