<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:33; Galatians 3:28; 1 Chronicles 7:15"> And daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the families of Manasseh son of Joseph, draw near — and these &#91;are&#93; the names of his daughters, Mahlah, Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 15:33–34; Deuteronomy 17:8–10; Exodus 18:19–26"> and stand before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the princes, and all the company, at the opening of the tent of meeting, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Ezekiel 18:4; Numbers 14:35"> 'Our father died in the wilderness, and he — he was not in the midst of the company who were met together against Jehovah in the company of Korah, but for his own sin he died, and had no sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:4; Psalms 109:13; Exodus 32:11"> why is the name of our father withdrawn from the midst of his family because he hath no son? give to us a possession in the midst of the brethren of our father;'</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12–13; Job 23:4; Proverbs 3:5–6"> and Moses bringeth near their cause before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Psalms 68:5–6"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Jeremiah 49:11; Joshua 17:4"> 'Rightly are the daughters of Zelophehad speaking; thou dost certainly give to them a possession of an inheritance in the midst of their father's brethren, and hast caused to pass over the inheritance of their father to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> 'And unto the sons of Israel thou dost speak, saying, When a man dieth, and hath no son, then ye have caused his inheritance to pass over to his daughter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and if he have no daughter, then ye have given his inheritance to his brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and if he have no brethren, then ye have given his inheritance to his father's brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:29; Ruth 4:3–6; Jeremiah 32:8"> and if his father have no brethren, then ye have given his inheritance to his relation who is near unto him of his family, and he hath possessed it;' and it hath been to the sons of Israel for a statute of judgment, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:47–48; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Go up unto this mount Abarim, and see the land which I have given to the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Numbers 31:2; Numbers 20:24–28"> and thou hast seen it, and thou hast been gathered unto thy people, also thou, as Aaron thy brother hath been gathered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:8–13; Exodus 17:7"> because ye provoked My mouth in the wilderness of Zin, in the strife of the company — to sanctify Me at the waters before their eyes;' they &#91;are&#93; waters of Meribah, in Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Moses speaketh unto Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Jeremiah 3:15; Hebrews 12:9"> 'Jehovah — God of the spirits of all flesh — appoint a man over the company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:5; Mark 6:34; 1 Kings 22:17"> who goeth out before them, and who cometh in before them, and who taketh them out, and who bringeth them in, and the company of Jehovah is not as sheep which have no shepherd.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Numbers 27:23; Genesis 41:38"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Take to thee Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom &#91;is&#93; the Spirit, and thou hast laid thine hand upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:23"> and hast caused him to stand before Eleazar the priest, and before all the company, and hast charged him before their eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 1:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:9; 2 Kings 2:9–10"> and hast put of thine honour upon him, so that all the company of the sons of Israel do hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 9:14; 1 Samuel 28:6; Exodus 28:30"> 'And before Eleazar the priest he standeth, and he hath asked for him by the judgment of the Lights before Jehovah; at His word they go out, and at His word they come in; he, and all the sons of Israel with him, even all the company.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Moses doth as Jehovah hath commanded him, and taketh Joshua, and causeth him to stand before Eleazar the priest, and before all the company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 27:19; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> and layeth his hands upon him, and chargeth him, as Jehovah hath spoken by the hand of Moses.</span>
