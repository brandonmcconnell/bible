<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> And the sons of Israel come in, — all the company — to the wilderness of Zin, in the first month, and the people abide in Kadesh, and Miriam dieth there, and is buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> And there hath been no water for the company, and they are assembled against Moses, and against Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> and the people strive with Moses, and speak, saying, 'And oh that we had expired when our brethren expired before Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> and why have ye brought in the assembly of Jehovah unto this wilderness to die there, we and our beasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> and why hast thou brought us up out of Egypt to bring us in unto this evil place? no place of seed, and fig, and vine, and pomegranate; and water there is none to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> And Moses and Aaron go in from the presence of the assembly unto the opening of the tent of meeting, and fall on their faces, and the honour of Jehovah is seen by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> 'Take the rod, and assemble the company, thou and Aaron thy brother; and ye have spoken unto the rock before their eyes, and it hath given its water, and thou hast brought out to them water from the rock, and hast watered the company, and their beasts.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> And Moses taketh the rod from before Jehovah, as He hath commanded him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> and Moses and Aaron assemble the assembly unto the front of the rock, and he saith to them, 'Hear, I pray you, O rebels, from this rock do we bring out to you water?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> and Moses lifteth up his hand, and smiteth the rock with his rod twice; and much water cometh out, and the company drink, also their beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, and unto Aaron, 'Because ye have not believed in Me to sanctify Me before the eyes of the sons of Israel, therefore ye do not bring in this assembly unto the land which I have given to them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> These &#91;are&#93; waters of Meribah, because the sons of Israel have 'striven' with Jehovah, and He is sanctified upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> And Moses sendeth messengers from Kadesh unto the king of Edom, 'Thus said thy brother Israel, Thou — thou hast known all the travail which hath found us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> that our fathers go down to Egypt, and we dwell in Egypt many days, and the Egyptians do evil to us and to our fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> and we cry unto Jehovah, and He heareth our voice, and sendeth a messenger, and is bringing us out of Egypt; and lo, we &#91;are&#93; in Kadesh, a city &#91;in&#93; the extremity of thy border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> Let us pass over, we pray thee, through thy land; we pass not over through a field, or through a vineyard, nor do we drink waters of a well; the way of the king we go, we turn not aside — right or left — till that we pass over thy border.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Edom saith unto him, 'Thou dost not pass over through me, lest with sword I come out to meet thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> And the sons of Israel say unto him, 'In the highway we go, and if of thy waters we drink — I and my cattle — then I have given their price; only (it is nothing) on my feet I pass over.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> And he saith, 'Thou dost not pass over;' and Edom cometh out to meet him with much people, and with a strong hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> and Edom refuseth to suffer Israel to pass over through his border, and Israel turneth aside from off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And the sons of Israel, the whole company, journey from Kadesh, and come in unto mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> and Jehovah speaketh unto Moses and unto Aaron in mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> 'Aaron is gathered unto his people, for he doth not go in unto the land which I have given to the sons of Israel, because that ye provoked My mouth at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> 'Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and cause them to go up mount Hor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> and strip Aaron of his garments, and thou hast clothed &#91;with&#93; them Eleazar his son, and Aaron is gathered, and doth die there.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Moses doth as Jehovah hath commanded, and they go up unto mount Hor before the eyes of all the company,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> and Moses strippeth Aaron of his garments, and clotheth with them Eleazar his son, and Aaron dieth there on the top of the mount; and Moses cometh down — Eleazar also — from the mount,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> and all the company see that Aaron hath expired, and they bewail Aaron thirty days — all the house of Israel.</span>
