<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Moses goeth and speaketh these words unto all Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> and he saith unto them, 'A son of a hundred and twenty years &#91;am&#93; I to-day; I am not able any more to go out and to come in, and Jehovah hath said unto me, Thou dost not pass over this Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 3:28"> 'Jehovah thy God He is passing over before thee, He doth destroy these nations from before thee, and thou hast possessed them; Joshua — he is passing over before thee as Jehovah hath spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:28–31; Deuteronomy 2:33; Deuteronomy 3:3–11"> and Jehovah hath done to them as he hath done to Sihon and to Og, kings of the Amorite, and to their land, whom He destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 23:32–33"> And Jehovah hath given them before your face, and ye have done to them according to all the command which I have commanded you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10; Psalms 27:14"> be strong and courageous, fear not, nor be terrified because of them, for Jehovah thy God &#91;is&#93; He who is going with thee; He doth not fail thee nor forsake thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:38"> And Moses calleth for Joshua, and saith unto him before the eyes of all Israel, 'Be strong and courageous, for thou — thou dost go in with this people unto the land which Jehovah hath sworn to their fathers to give to them, and thou — thou dost cause them to inherit it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Deuteronomy 31:6; Joshua 1:9"> and Jehovah &#91;is&#93; He who is going before thee, He himself is with thee; He doth not fail thee nor forsake thee; fear not, nor be affrighted.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Joshua 3:3; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And Moses writeth this law, and giveth it unto the priests (sons of Levi, those bearing the ark of the covenant of Jehovah), and unto all the elders of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34–43; Deuteronomy 15:1–2"> and Moses commandeth them, saying, 'At the end of seven years, in the appointed time, the year of release, in the feast of booths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:2; Joshua 8:34–35; Deuteronomy 16:16–17"> in the coming in of all Israel to see the face of Jehovah in the place which He chooseth, thou dost proclaim this law before all Israel, in their ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Psalms 34:11–14; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> 'Assemble the people, the men, and the women, and the infants, and thy sojourner who &#91;is&#93; within thy gates, so that they hear, and so that they learn, and have feared Jehovah your God, and observed to do all the words of this law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:2; Deuteronomy 6:7; Proverbs 22:6"> and their sons, who have not known, do hear, and have learned to fear Jehovah your God all the days which ye are living on the ground whither ye are passing over the Jordan to possess it.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Deuteronomy 31:23; Job 2:1"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Lo, thy days have drawn near to die; call Joshua, and station yourselves in the tent of meeting, and I charge him;' and Moses goeth — Joshua also — and they station themselves in the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 40:38; Psalms 99:7; Exodus 33:9–10"> and Jehovah is seen in the tent, in a pillar of a cloud; and the pillar of the cloud standeth at the opening of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Judges 10:13; Judges 2:12"> And Jehovah saith unto Moses, 'Lo, thou art lying down with thy fathers, and this people hath risen, and gone a-whoring after the gods of the stranger of the land into the midst of which it hath entered, and forsaken Me, and broken My covenant which I made with it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:42; Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 104:29"> and Mine anger hath burned against it in that day, and I have forsaken them, and hidden My face from them, and it hath been for consumption, and many evils and distresses have found it, and it hath said in that day, Is it not because that my God is not in my midst — these evils have found me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> and I certainly hide My face in that day for all the evil which it hath done, for it hath turned unto other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 31:21–22; Deuteronomy 31:26"> 'And now, write for you this song, and teach it the sons of Israel; put it in their mouths, so that this song is to Me for a witness against the sons of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Nehemiah 9:25–26"> and I bring them in unto the ground which I have sworn to their fathers — flowing with milk and honey, and they have eaten, and been satisfied, and been fat, and have turned unto other gods, and they have served them, and despised Me, and broken My covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Psalms 139:2; John 2:24–25"> 'And it hath been, when many evils and distresses do meet it, that this song hath testified to its face for a witness; for it is not forgotten out of the mouth of its seed, for I have known its imagining which it is doing to-day, before I bring them in unto the land of which I have sworn.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And Moses writeth this song on that day, and doth teach it the sons of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:14; Acts 7:45"> and He commandeth Joshua son of Nun, and saith, 'Be strong and courageous, for thou dost bring in the sons of Israel unto the land which I have sworn to them, and I — I am with thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And it cometh to pass, when Moses finisheth to write the words of this law on a book till their completion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9"> that Moses commandeth the Levites bearing the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; 2 Kings 22:8–11; Galatians 2:19"> 'Take this Book of the Law, and thou hast set it on the side of the ark of the covenant of Jehovah your God, and it hath been there against thee for a witness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:20; Deuteronomy 9:24; Acts 7:51"> for I — I have known thy rebellion, and thy stiff neck; lo, in my being yet alive with you to-day, rebellious ye have been with Jehovah, and also surely after my death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 4:26; Deuteronomy 30:19"> 'Assemble unto me all the elders of your tribes, and your authorities, and I speak in their ears these words, and cause to testify against them the heavens and the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:19; Deuteronomy 32:5; Luke 21:24"> for I have known that after my death ye do very corruptly, and have turned aside out of the way which I commanded you, and evil hath met you in the latter end of the days, because ye do the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah, to make Him angry with the work of your hands.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2; Deuteronomy 4:5; John 12:49"> And Moses speaketh in the ears of all the assembly of Israel the words of this song, till their completion: —</span>
