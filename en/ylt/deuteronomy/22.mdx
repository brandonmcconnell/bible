<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 23:4–5; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> 'Thou dost not see the ox of thy brother or his sheep driven away, and hast hidden thyself from them, thou dost certainly turn them back to thy brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> and if thy brother &#91;is&#93; not near unto thee, and thou hast not known him, then thou hast removed it unto the midst of thy house, and it hath been with thee till thy brother seek it, and thou hast given it back to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and so thou dost to his ass, and so thou dost to his garment, and so thou dost to any lost thing of thy brother's, which is lost by him, and thou hast found it; thou art not able to hide thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:1–2; Hebrews 12:12–13; Matthew 5:44"> 'Thou dost not see the ass of thy brother, or his ox, falling in the way, and hast hid thyself from them; thou dost certainly raise &#91;them&#93; up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:4–15; Deuteronomy 18:12"> 'The habiliments of a man are not on a woman, nor doth a man put on the garment of a woman, for the abomination of Jehovah thy God &#91;is&#93; any one doing these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 22:28; Proverbs 12:10; Genesis 8:17"> 'When a bird's nest cometh before thee in the way, in any tree, or on the earth, brood or eggs, and the mother sitting on the brood or on the eggs, thou dost not take the mother with the young ones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> thou dost certainly send away the mother, and the young ones dost take to thyself, so that it is well with thee, and thou hast prolonged days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; Exodus 21:28–36"> 'When thou buildest a new house, then thou hast made a parapet to thy roof, and thou dost not put blood on thy house when one falleth from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> 'Thou dost not sow thy vineyard &#91;with&#93; divers things, lest the fulness of the seed which thou dost sow, and the increase of the vineyard, be separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16"> 'Thou dost not plow with an ox and with an ass together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19"> 'Thou dost not put on a mixed cloth, wool and linen together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Numbers 15:37–41"> 'Fringes thou dost make to thee on the four skirts of thy covering with which thou dost cover &#91;thyself&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> 'When a man taketh a wife, and hath gone in unto her, and hated her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> and laid against her actions of words, and brought out against her an evil name, and said, This woman I have taken, and I draw near unto her, and I have not found in her tokens of virginity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> 'Then hath the father of the damsel — and her mother — taken and brought out the tokens of virginity of the damsel unto the elders of the city in the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and the father of the damsel hath said unto the elders, My daughter I have given to this man for a wife, and he doth hate her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and lo, he hath laid actions of words, saying, I have not found to thy daughter tokens of virginity — and these &#91;are&#93; the tokens of the virginity of my daughter! and they have spread out the garment before the elders of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> 'And the elders of that city have taken the man, and chastise him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> and fined him a hundred silverlings, and given to the father of the damsel, because he hath brought out an evil name on a virgin of Israel, and she is to him for a wife, he is not able to send her away all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> 'And if this thing hath been truth — tokens of virginity have not been found for the damsel —</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> then they have brought out the damsel unto the opening of her father's house, and stoned her have the men of her city with stones, and she hath died, for she hath done folly in Israel, to go a-whoring &#91;in&#93; her father's house; and thou hast put away the evil thing out of thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> 'When a man is found lying with a woman, married to a husband, then they have died even both of them, the man who is lying with the woman, also the woman; and thou hast put away the evil thing out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> 'When there is a damsel, a virgin, betrothed to a man, and a man hath found her in a city, and lain with her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> then ye have brought them both out unto the gate of that city, and stoned them with stones, and they have died: — the damsel, because that she hath not cried, &#91;being&#93; in a city; and the man, because that he hath humbled his neighbour's wife; and thou hast put away the evil thing out of thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> 'And if in a field the man find the damsel who is betrothed, and the man hath laid hold on her, and lain with her, then hath the man who hath lain with her died alone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> and to the damsel thou dost not do anything, the damsel hath no deadly sin; for as a man riseth against his neighbour and hath murdered him — the life, so &#91;is&#93; this thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> for in a field he found her, she hath cried — the damsel who is betrothed — and she hath no saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> 'When a man findeth a damsel, a virgin who is not betrothed, and hath caught her, and lain with her, and they have been found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> then hath the man who is lying with her given to the father of the damsel fifty silverlings, and to him she is for a wife; because that he hath humbled her, he is not able to send her away all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> 'A man doth not take his father's wife, nor uncover his father's skirt.</span>
