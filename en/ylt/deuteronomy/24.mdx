<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 19:7–9; Mark 10:4–12; Isaiah 50:1"> 'When a man doth take a wife, and hath married her, and it hath been, if she doth not find grace in his eyes (for he hath found in her nakedness of anything), and he hath written for her a writing of divorce, and given &#91;it&#93; into her hand, and sent her out of his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 10:11; 1 Corinthians 7:15; Ezekiel 44:22"> and she hath gone out of his house, and hath gone and been another man's,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and the latter man hath hated her, and written for her a writing of divorce, and given &#91;it&#93; into her hand, and sent her out of his house, or when the latter man dieth, who hath taken her to himself for a wife:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Joshua 22:17–18; Leviticus 18:24–28"> 'Her former husband who sent her away is not able to turn back to take her to be to him for a wife, after that she hath become defiled; for an abomination it &#91;is&#93; before Jehovah, and thou dost not cause the land to sin which Jehovah thy God is giving to thee — an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18; Deuteronomy 20:7; Genesis 2:24"> 'When a man taketh a new wife, he doth not go out into the host, and &#91;one&#93; doth not pass over unto him for anything; free he is at his own house one year, and hath rejoiced his wife whom he hath taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19; Luke 12:15; Revelation 18:22"> 'None doth take in pledge millstones, and rider, for life it &#91;is&#93; he is taking in pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 21:16; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:1–4"> 'When a man is found stealing a person, of his brethren, of the sons of Israel, and hath tyrannized over him, and sold him, then hath that thief died, and thou hast put away the evil thing out of thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2; Mark 1:44; Leviticus 13:1–46"> 'Take heed, in the plague of leprosy, to watch greatly, and to do according to all that the priests, the Levites, teach you; as I have commanded them ye observe to do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 12:10–15; 1 Corinthians 10:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> remember that which Jehovah thy God hath done to Miriam in the way, in your coming out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:8; Exodus 22:27"> 'When thou liftest up on thy brother a debt of anything, thou dost not go in unto his house to obtain his pledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> at the outside thou dost stand, and the man on whom thou art lifting &#91;it&#93; up is bringing out unto thee the pledge at the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Job 22:6; Job 24:3"> 'And if he is a poor man, thou dost not lie down with his pledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:25; Daniel 4:27; Exodus 22:26–27"> thou dost certainly give back to him the pledge at the going in of the sun, and he hath lain down in his own raiment, and hath blessed thee; and to thee it is righteousness before Jehovah thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 14:31; Proverbs 22:16"> 'Thou dost not oppress a hireling, poor and needy, of thy brethren or of thy sojourner who is in thy land within thy gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Proverbs 3:27–28; James 5:4"> in his day thou dost give his hire, and the sun doth not go in upon it, for he &#91;is&#93; poor, and unto it he is lifting up his soul, and he doth not cry against thee unto Jehovah, and it hath been in thee — sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Chronicles 25:4; Jeremiah 31:29–30"> 'Fathers are not put to death for sons, and sons are not put to death for fathers — each for his own sin, they are put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 23:6"> 'Thou dost not turn aside the judgment of a fatherless sojourner, nor take in pledge the garment of a widow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:15; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> and thou hast remembered that a servant thou hast been in Egypt, and Jehovah thy God doth ransom thee from thence; therefore I am commanding thee to do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 14:29"> 'When thou reapest thy harvest in thy field, and hast forgotten a sheaf in a field, thou dost not turn back to take it; to the sojourner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, it is; so that Jehovah thy God doth bless thee in all the work of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 19:10"> 'When thou beatest thine olive, thou dost not examine the branch behind thee; to the sojourner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 24:19"> 'When thou cuttest thy vineyard, thou dost not glean behind thee; to the sojourner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, it is;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; 1 John 4:10–11; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> and thou hast remembered that a servant thou hast been in the land of Egypt; therefore I am commanding thee to do this thing.</span>
