<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; Psalms 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> And this &#91;is&#93; the blessing &#91;with&#93; which Moses the man of God blessed the sons of Israel before his death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Habakkuk 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> and he saith: — 'Jehovah from Sinai hath come, And hath risen from Seir for them; He hath shone from mount Paran, And hath come &#91;with&#93; myriads of holy ones; At His right hand &#91;are&#93; springs for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> Also He &#91;is&#93; loving the peoples; All His holy ones &#91;are&#93; in thy hand, And they — they sat down at thy foot, &#91;Each&#93; He lifteth up at thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; Psalms 119:111; John 7:19"> A law hath Moses commanded us, A possession of the assembly of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> And he is in Jeshurun king, In the heads of the people gathering together, The tribes of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> Let Reuben live, and not die, And let his men be a number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalms 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> And this &#91;is&#93; for Judah; and he saith: — Hear, O Jehovah, the voice of Judah, And unto his people do Thou bring him in; His hand hath striven for him, And an help from his adversaries art Thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> And of Levi he said: — Thy Thummim and thy Urim &#91;are&#93; for thy pious one, Whom Thou hast tried in Massah, Thou dost strive with Him at the waters of Meribah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> Who is saying of his father and his mother, I have not seen him; And his brethren he hath not discerned, And his sons he hath not known; For they have observed Thy saying, And Thy covenant they keep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> They teach Thy judgments to Jacob, And Thy law to Israel; They put perfume in Thy nose, And whole burnt-offering on Thine altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Bless, O Jehovah, his strength, And the work of his hands Thou acceptest, Smite the loins of his withstanders, And of those hating him — that they rise not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> Of Benjamin he said: — The beloved of Jehovah doth tabernacle confidently by him, Covering him over all the day; Yea, between his shoulders He doth tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> And of Joseph he said: — Blessed of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; his land, By precious things of the heavens, By dew, and by the deep crouching beneath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> And by precious things — fruits of the sun, And by precious things — cast forth by the moons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> And by chief things — of the ancient mountains, And by precious things — of the age-during heights,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> And by precious things — of earth and its fulness, And the good pleasure Of Him who is dwelling in the bush, — Let it come for the head of Joseph, And for the crown of him Who is separate from his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> His honour &#91;is&#93; a firstling of his ox, And his horns &#91;are&#93; horns of a reem; By them peoples he doth push together To the ends of earth; And they &#91;are&#93; the myriads of Ephraim, And they &#91;are&#93; the thousands of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> And of Zebulun he said: — Rejoice, O Zebulun, in thy going out, And, O Issachar, in thy tents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> Peoples &#91;to&#93; the mountain they call, There they sacrifice righteous sacrifices; For the abundance of the seas they suck, And hidden things hidden in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> And of Gad he said: — Blessed of the Enlarger &#91;is&#93; Gad, As a lioness he doth tabernacle, And hath torn the arm — also the crown!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> And he provideth the first part for himself, For there the portion of the lawgiver is covered, And he cometh &#91;with&#93; the heads of the people; The righteousness of Jehovah he hath done, And His judgments with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> And of Dan he said: — Dan &#91;is&#93; a lion's whelp; he doth leap from Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> And of Naphtali he said: — O Naphtali, satisfied with pleasure, And full of the blessing of Jehovah, West and south possess thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And of Asher he said: — Blessed with sons &#91;is&#93; Asher, Let him be accepted by his brethren, And dipping in oil his foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> Iron and brass &#91;are&#93; thy shoes, And as thy days — thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> There is none like the God of Jeshurun, Riding the heavens in thy help, And in His excellency the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> A habitation &#91;is&#93; the eternal God, And beneath &#91;are&#93; arms age-during. And He casteth out from thy presence the enemy, and saith, 'Destroy!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> And Israel doth tabernacle &#91;in&#93; confidence alone; The eye of Jacob &#91;is&#93; unto a land of corn and wine; Also His heavens drop down dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> O thy happiness, O Israel! who is like thee? A people saved by Jehovah, The shield of thy help, And He who &#91;is&#93; the sword of thine excellency: And thine enemies are subdued for thee, And thou on their high places dost tread.'</span>
