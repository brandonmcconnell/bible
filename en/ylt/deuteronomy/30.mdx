<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; 1 Kings 8:47–48; Deuteronomy 30:19"> 'And it hath been, when all these things come upon thee, the blessing and the reviling, which I have set before thee, and thou hast brought &#91;them&#93; back unto thy heart, among all the nations whither Jehovah thy God hath driven thee away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Nehemiah 1:9; Deuteronomy 4:28–31"> and hast turned back unto Jehovah thy God, and hearkened to His voice, according to all that I am commanding thee to-day, thou and thy sons, with all thy heart, and with all thy soul —</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Ezekiel 36:24; Psalms 147:2"> then hath Jehovah thy God turned back &#91;to&#93; thy captivity, and pitied thee, yea, He hath turned back and gathered thee out of all the peoples whither Jehovah thy God hath scattered thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:9; Ezekiel 39:25–29; Deuteronomy 28:64"> 'If thine outcast is in the extremity of the heavens, thence doth Jehovah thy God gather thee, and thence He doth take thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14"> and Jehovah thy God hath brought thee in unto the land which thy fathers have possessed, and thou hast inherited it, and He hath done thee good, and multiplied thee above thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 32:39"> 'And Jehovah thy God hath circumcised thy heart, and the heart of thy seed, to love Jehovah thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, for the sake of thy life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Lamentations 4:21–22; Jeremiah 51:24–26"> and Jehovah thy God hath put all this oath on thine enemies, and on those hating thee, who have pursued thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:16; Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> 'And thou dost turn back, and hast hearkened to the voice of Jehovah, and hast done all His commands which I am commanding thee to-day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:11–14; Jeremiah 32:41; Jeremiah 33:9"> and Jehovah thy God hath made thee abundant in every work of thy hand, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy ground, for good; for Jehovah turneth back to rejoice over thee for good, as He rejoiced over thy fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Deuteronomy 4:29; Nehemiah 1:9"> for thou dost hearken to the voice of Jehovah thy God, to keep His commands, and His statutes, which are written in the book of this law, for thou turnest back unto Jehovah thy God, with all thy heart, and with all thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:19; Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 16:25–26"> 'For this command which I am commanding thee to-day, it is not too wonderful for thee, nor &#91;is&#93; it far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 10:6–8; Proverbs 30:4; John 3:13"> It is not in the heavens, — saying, Who doth go up for us into the heavens, and doth take it for us, and doth cause us to hear it — that we may do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:33; Matthew 12:42; Proverbs 2:1–5"> And it &#91;is&#93; not beyond the sea, — saying, Who doth pass over for us beyond the sea, and doth take it for us, and doth cause us to hear it — that we may do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 10:8–10; Jeremiah 12:2; Matthew 7:21"> For very near unto thee is the word, in thy mouth, and in thy heart — to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:26; Deuteronomy 30:19; 1 John 5:11–12"> 'See, I have set before thee to-day life and good, and death and evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Matthew 22:37–38; 1 John 5:2–3"> in that I am commanding thee to-day to love Jehovah thy God, to walk in His ways, and to keep His commands, and His statutes, and His judgments; and thou hast lived and multiplied, and Jehovah thy God hath blessed thee in the land whither thou art going in to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Hebrews 3:12; Proverbs 1:32"> 'And if thy heart doth turn, and thou dost not hearken, and hast been driven away, and hast bowed thyself to other gods, and served them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Isaiah 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I have declared to you this day, that ye do certainly perish, ye do not prolong days on the ground which thou art passing over the Jordan to go in thither to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26; Psalms 119:30"> 'I have caused to testify against you to-day the heavens and the earth; life and death I have set before thee, the blessing and the reviling; and thou hast fixed on life, so that thou dost live, thou and thy seed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 17:3"> to love Jehovah thy God, to hearken to His voice, and to cleave to Him (for He &#91;is&#93; thy life, and the length of thy days), to dwell on the ground which Jehovah hath sworn to thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them.'</span>
