<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> 'Sons ye &#91;are&#93; to Jehovah your God; ye do not cut yourselves, nor make baldness between your eyes for the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> for a holy people &#91;art&#93; thou to Jehovah thy God, and on thee hath Jehovah fixed to be to Him for a people, a peculiar treasure, out of all the peoples who &#91;are&#93; on the face of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> 'Thou dost not eat any abominable thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> 'this &#91;is&#93; the beast which ye do eat: ox, lamb of the sheep, or kid of the goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> hart, and roe, and fallow deer, and wild goat, and pygarg, and wild ox, and chamois;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> and every beast dividing the hoof, and cleaving the cleft into two hoofs, bringing up the cud, among the beasts — it ye do eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> 'Only, this ye do not eat, of those bringing up the cud, and of those dividing the cloven hoof: the camel, and the hare, and the rabbit, for they are bringing up the cud but the hoof have not divided; unclean they &#91;are&#93; to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> and the sow, for it is dividing the hoof, and not &#91;bringing&#93; up the cud, unclean it &#91;is&#93; to you; of their flesh ye do not eat, and against their carcase ye do not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> 'This ye do eat of all that &#91;are&#93; in the waters; all that hath fins and scales ye do eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and anything which hath not fins and scales ye do not eat; unclean it &#91;is&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> 'Any clean bird ye do eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> and these &#91;are&#93; they of which ye do not eat: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the glede, and the kite, and the vulture after its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and every raven after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and the owl, and the night-hawk, and the cuckoo, and the hawk after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the &#91;little&#93; owl, and the &#91;great&#93; owl, and the swan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the pelican, and the gier-eagle, and the cormorant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the stork, and the heron after its kind, and the lapwing, and the bat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> and every teeming thing which is flying, unclean it &#91;is&#93; to you; they are not eaten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> any clean fowl ye do eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> 'Ye do not eat of any carcase; to the sojourner who &#91;is&#93; within thy gates thou dost give it, and he hath eaten it; or sell &#91;it&#93; to a stranger; for a holy people thou &#91;art&#93; to Jehovah thy God; thou dost not boil a kid in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> 'Thou dost certainly tithe all the increase of thy seed which the field is bringing forth year by year;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> and thou hast eaten before Jehovah thy God, in the place where He doth choose to cause His name to tabernacle, the tithe of thy corn, of thy new wine, and of thine oil, and the firstlings of thy herd, and of thy flock, so that thou dost learn to fear Jehovah thy God all the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> 'And when the way is too much for thee, that thou art not able to carry it — when the place is too far off from thee which Jehovah thy God doth choose to put His name there, when Jehovah thy God doth bless thee; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then thou hast given &#91;it&#93; in money, and hast bound up the money in thy hand, and gone unto the place on which Jehovah thy God doth fix;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> and thou hast given the money for any thing which thy soul desireth, for oxen, and for sheep, and for wine, and for strong drink, and for any thing which thy soul asketh, and thou hast eaten there before Jehovah thy God, and thou hast rejoiced, thou and thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> As to the Levite who &#91;is&#93; within thy gates, thou dost not forsake him, for he hath no portion and inheritance with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> 'At the end of three years thou dost bring out all the tithe of thine increase in that year, and hast placed &#91;it&#93; within thy gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> and come in hath the Levite (for he hath no part and inheritance with thee), and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow, who &#91;are&#93; within thy gates, and they have eaten, and been satisfied, so that Jehovah thy God doth bless thee in all the work of thy hand which thou dost.</span>
