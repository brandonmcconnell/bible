<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> 'And Moses — the elders of Israel also — commandeth the people, saying, Keep all the command which I am commanding you to-day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> and it hath been, in the day that ye pass over the Jordan unto the land which Jehovah thy God is giving to thee, that thou hast raised up for thee great stones, and plaistered them with plaister,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> and written on them all the words of this law in thy passing over, so that thou goest in unto the land which Jehovah thy God is giving to thee — a land flowing with milk and honey, as Jehovah, God of thy fathers, hath spoken to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> 'And it hath been, in your passing over the Jordan, ye raise up these stones which I am commanding you to-day, in mount Ebal, and thou hast plaistered them with plaister,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> and built there an altar to Jehovah thy God, an altar of stones, thou dost not wave over them iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> Of complete stones thou buildest the altar of Jehovah thy God, and hast caused to ascend on it burnt-offerings to Jehovah thy God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> and sacrificed peace-offerings, and eaten there, and rejoiced before Jehovah thy God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> and written on the stones all the words of this law, well engraved.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10–11; Deuteronomy 26:16–18; Ephesians 5:8–9"> And Moses speaketh — the priests, the Levites, also — unto all Israel, saying, 'Keep silent, and hear, O Israel, this day thou hast become a people to Jehovah thy God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> and thou hast hearkened to the voice of Jehovah thy God, and done His commands, and His statutes, which I am commanding thee to-day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moses commandeth the people on that day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> 'These do stand, to bless the people, on mount Gerizzim, in your passing over the Jordan: Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Joseph, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And these do stand, for the reviling, on mount Ebal: Reuben, Gad, and Asher, and Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> 'And the Levites have answered and said unto every man of Israel — a loud voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; the man who maketh a graven and molten image, the abomination of Jehovah, work of the hands of an artificer, and hath put &#91;it&#93; in a secret place, — and all the people have answered and said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; He who is making light of his father and his mother, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is removing his neighbour's border, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is causing the blind to err in the way, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is turning aside the judgment of fatherless, sojourner, and widow, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is lying with his father's wife, for he hath uncovered his father's skirt, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is lying with any beast, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is lying with his sister, daughter of his father, or daughter of his mother, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is lying with his mother-in-law, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is smiting his neighbour in secret, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is taking a bribe to smite a person, innocent blood, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> 'Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who doth not establish the words of this law, to do them, — and all the people have said, Amen.</span>
