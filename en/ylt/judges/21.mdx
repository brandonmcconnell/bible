<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Mark 6:23; Acts 23:12"> And the men of Israel have sworn in Mizpeh, saying, 'None of us doth give his daughter to Benjamin for a wife.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 20:26; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 30:4"> And the people come in to Beth-El, and sit there till the evening before God, and lift up their voice, and weep — a great weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Joshua 7:7–9; Isaiah 63:17"> and say, 'Why, O Jehovah, God of Israel, hath this been in Israel — to be lacking to-day, from Israel, one tribe?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; 1 Kings 8:64; Psalms 78:34–35"> And it cometh to pass on the morrow, that the people rise early, and build there an altar, and cause to ascend burnt-offerings and peace-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:1"> And the sons of Israel say, 'Who &#91;is&#93; he that hath not come up in the assembly out of all the tribes of Israel unto Jehovah?' for the great oath hath been concerning him who hath not come up unto Jehovah to Mizpeh, saying, 'He is surely put to death.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> And the sons of Israel repent concerning Benjamin their brother, and say, 'There hath been to-day cut off one tribe from Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> what do we do for them — for those who are left — for wives, and we — we have sworn by Jehovah not to give to them of our daughters for wives?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 2:5–6"> And they say, 'Who is &#91;that&#93; one out of the tribes of Israel who hath not come up unto Jehovah to Mizpeh?' and lo, none hath come in unto the camp from Jabesh-Gilead — unto the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the people numbered themselves, and lo, there is not there a man of the inhabitants of Jabesh-Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; 1 Samuel 11:7"> And the company send there twelve thousand men of the sons of valour, and command them, saying, 'Go — and ye have smitten the inhabitants of Jabesh-Gilead by the mouth of the sword, even the women and the infants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And this &#91;is&#93; the thing which ye do; every male, and every woman knowing the lying of a male, ye devote.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12; Psalms 78:60"> And they find out of the inhabitants of Jabesh-Gilead four hundred young women, virgins, who have not known man by the lying of a male, and they bring them in unto the camp at Shiloh, which &#91;is&#93; in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> And all the company send, and speak, unto the sons of Benjamin who &#91;are&#93; in the rock Rimmon, and proclaim to them peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> and Benjamin turneth back at that time, and they give to them the women whom they have kept alive of the women of Jabesh-Gilead, and they have not found for &#91;all of&#93; them so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Isaiah 58:12"> And the people repented concerning Benjamin, for Jehovah had made a breach among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the elders of the company say, 'What do we do to the remnant for wives — for the women have been destroyed out of Benjamin?'</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> And they say, 'A possession of an escaped party &#91;is&#93; to Benjamin, and a tribe is not blotted out from Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> and we — we are not able to give to them wives out of our daughters, for the sons of Israel have sworn, saying, Cursed &#91;is&#93; he who is giving a wife to Benjamin.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> And they say, 'Lo, a festival of Jehovah &#91;is&#93; in Shiloh, from time to time, which &#91;is&#93; on the north of Beth-El, at the rising of the sun, by the highway which is going up from Beth-El to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they command the sons of Benjamin, saying, 'Go — and ye have laid wait in the vineyards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> and have seen, and lo, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in dances — then ye have gone out from the vineyards, and caught for you each his wife out of the daughters of Shiloh, and gone to the land of Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> and it hath been, when their fathers or their brethren come in to plead unto us, that we have said unto them, Favour us &#91;by&#93; them, for we have not taken &#91;to&#93; each his wife in battle, for ye — ye have not given to them at this time &#91;that&#93; ye are guilty.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> And the sons of Benjamin do so, and take women according to their number, out of the dancers whom they have taken violently away; and they go, and turn back unto their inheritance, and build the cities, and dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the sons of Israel go up and down thence at that time, each to his tribe, and to his family; and they go out thence each to his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> In those days there is no king in Israel; each doth that which is right in his own eyes.</span>
