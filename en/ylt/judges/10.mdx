<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> And there riseth after Abimelech, to save Israel, Tola son of Puah, son of Dodo, a man of Issachar, and he is dwelling in Shamir, in the hill-country of Ephraim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and he judgeth Israel twenty and three years, and he dieth, and is buried in Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> And there riseth after him Jair the Gileadite, and he judgeth Israel twenty and two years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> and he hath thirty sons riding on thirty ass-colts, and they have thirty cities, (they call them Havoth-Jair unto this day), which &#91;are&#93; in the land of Gilead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and Jair dieth, and is buried in Kamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And the sons of Israel add to do the evil thing in the eyes of Jehovah, and serve the Baalim, and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Aram, and the gods of Zidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the Bene-Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines, and forsake Jehovah, and have not served Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> and the anger of Jehovah burneth against Israel, and He selleth them into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the Bene-Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> and they crush and oppress the sons of Israel in that year — eighteen years all the sons of Israel &#91;who&#93; are beyond the Jordan, in the land of the Amorite, which &#91;is&#93; in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> And the Bene-Ammon pass over the Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim, and Israel hath great distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> And the sons of Israel cry unto Jehovah, saying, 'We have sinned against Thee, even because we have forsaken our God, and serve the Baalim.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And Jehovah saith unto the sons of Israel, '&#91;Have I&#93; not &#91;saved you&#93; from the Egyptians, and from the Amorite, from the Bene-Ammon, and from the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> And the Zidonians, and Amalek, and Maon have oppressed you, and ye cry unto Me, and I save you out of their hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> and ye — ye have forsaken Me, and serve other gods, therefore I add not to save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and cry unto the gods on which ye have fixed; they — they save you in the time of your adversity.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the sons of Israel say unto Jehovah, 'We have sinned, do Thou to us according to all that is good in Thine eyes; only deliver us, we pray Thee, this day.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> And they turn aside the gods of the stranger out of their midst, and serve Jehovah, and His soul is grieved with the misery of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> And the Bene-Ammon are called together, and encamp in Gilead, and the sons of Israel are gathered together, and encamp in Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the people — heads of Gilead — say one unto another, 'Who &#91;is&#93; the man that doth begin to fight against the Bene-Ammon? he is for head to all inhabitants of Gilead.'</span>
