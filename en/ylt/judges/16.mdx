<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:47; Ezra 9:1–2; Genesis 38:16–18"> And Samson goeth to Gaza, and seeth there a woman, a harlot, and goeth in unto her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:10–12; 1 Samuel 23:26; Acts 9:24"> &#91;it is told&#93; to the Gazathites, saying, 'Samson hath come in hither;' and they go round and lay wait for him all the night at the gate of the city, and keep themselves silent all the night, saying, 'Till the light of the morning — then we have slain him.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Psalms 107:16; Isaiah 63:1–5"> And Samson lieth down till the middle of the night, and riseth in the middle of the night, and layeth hold on the doors of the gate of the city, and on the two side posts, and removeth them with the bar, and putteth on his shoulders, and taketh them up unto the top of the hill, which &#91;is&#93; on the front of Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Proverbs 22:14; Nehemiah 13:26"> And it cometh to pass afterwards that he loveth a woman in the valley of Sorek, and her name &#91;is&#93; Delilah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Judges 14:15; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> and the princes of the Philistines come up unto her, and say to her, 'Entice him, and see wherein his great power &#91;is&#93;, and wherein we are able for him — and we have bound him to afflict him, and we — we give to thee, each one, eleven hundred silverlings.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21; Proverbs 6:26; Proverbs 22:14"> And Delilah saith unto Samson, 'Declare, I pray thee, to me, wherein thy great power &#91;is&#93;, and wherewith thou art bound, to afflict thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:2–3; Romans 3:8; 1 Samuel 27:10"> And Samson saith unto her, 'If they bind me with seven green withs which have not been dried, then I have been weak, and have been as one of the human race.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And the princes of the Philistines bring up to her seven green withs which have not been dried, and she bindeth him with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 58:9"> And the ambush is abiding with her in an inner chamber, and she saith unto him, 'Philistines &#91;are&#93; upon thee, Samson;' and he breaketh the withs as a thread of tow is broken in its smelling fire, and his power hath not been known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:13; Luke 22:48; Judges 16:7"> And Delilah saith unto Samson, 'Lo, thou hast played upon me, and speakest unto me lies; now, declare, I pray thee, to me, wherewith thou art bound.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:5; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 13:3"> And he saith unto her, 'If they certainly bind me with thick bands, new ones, by which work hath not been done, then I have been weak, and have been as one of the human race.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Delilah taketh thick bands, new ones, and bindeth him with them, and saith unto him, 'Philistines &#91;are&#93; upon thee, Samson;' and the ambush is abiding in an inner chamber, and he breaketh them from off his arms as a thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Delilah saith unto Samson, 'Hitherto thou hast played upon me, and dost speak unto me lies; declare to me wherewith thou art bound.' And he saith unto her, 'If thou weavest the seven locks of my head with the web.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Ezra 9:13–14"> And she fixeth &#91;it&#93; with the pin, and saith unto him, 'Philistines &#91;are&#93; upon thee, Samson;' and he awaketh out of his sleep, and journeyeth with the pin of the weaving machine, and with the web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 14:16; 1 John 2:15–16; John 14:21–24"> And she saith unto him, 'How dost thou say, I have loved thee, and thy heart is not with me? these three times thou hast played upon me, and hast not declared to me wherein thy great power &#91;is&#93;.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21–23; Luke 18:5; Mark 14:24"> And it cometh to pass, because she distressed him with her words all the days, and doth urge him, and his soul is grieved to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Judges 13:5; Proverbs 12:23"> that he declareth to her all his heart, and saith to her, 'A razor hath not gone up on my head, for a Nazarite to God I &#91;am&#93; from the womb of my mother; if I have been shaven, then hath my power turned aside from me, and I have been weak, and have been as any of the human race.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Judges 16:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And Delilah seeth that he hath declared to her all his heart, and she sendeth and calleth for the princes of the Philistines, saying, 'Come up this time, for he hath declared to me all his heart;' and the princes of the Philistines have come up unto her, and bring up the money in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 7:26–27; Proverbs 7:21–23; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> and she maketh him sleep on her knees, and calleth for a man, and shaveth the seven locks of his head, and beginneth to afflict him, and his power turneth aside from off him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:42–43; 1 Samuel 16:14; Joshua 7:12"> and she saith, 'Philistines &#91;are&#93; upon thee, Samson;' and he awaketh out of his sleep, and saith, 'I go out as time by time, and shake myself;' and he hath not known that Jehovah hath turned aside from off him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 2:19; Proverbs 14:14; Isaiah 47:2"> And the Philistines seize him, and pick out his eyes, and bring him down to Gaza, and bind him with two brazen fetters; and he is grinding in the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:13–14; Psalms 106:44–45; Leviticus 26:44"> And the hair of his head beginneth to shoot up, when he hath been shaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–5; Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:15–16"> and the princes of the Philistines have been gathered together to sacrifice a great sacrifice to Dagon their god, and to rejoice; and they say, 'Our god hath given into our hand Samson our enemy.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23; Judges 15:16"> And the people see him, and praise their god, for they said, 'Our god hath given in our hand our enemy, and he who is laying waste our land, and who multiplied our wounded.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 19:6; Judges 9:27"> And it cometh to pass, when their heart &#91;is&#93; glad, that they say, 'Call for Samson, and he doth play before us;' and they call for Samson out of the prison-house, and he playeth before them, and they cause him to stand between the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Samson saith unto the young man who is keeping hold on his hand, 'Let me alone, and let me feel the pillars on which the house is established, and I lean upon them.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2; Joshua 2:8"> And the house hath been full of men and of women, and thither &#91;are&#93; all the princes of the Philistines, and on the roof &#91;are&#93; about three thousand men and women, who are looking on the playing of Samson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:15; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 143:12"> And Samson calleth unto Jehovah, and saith, 'Lord Jehovah, remember me, I pray Thee, and strengthen me, I pray Thee, only this time, O God; and I am avenged — vengeance at once — because of my two eyes, on the Philistines.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Samson turneth aside &#91;to&#93; the two middle pillars, on which the house is established, and on which it is supported, &#91;to&#93; the one with his right hand, and one with his left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Matthew 16:25; Matthew 24:38–39"> and Samson saith, 'Let me die with the Philistines,' and he inclineth himself powerfully, and the house falleth on the princes, and on all the people who &#91;are&#93; in it, and the dead whom he hath put to death in his death are more than those whom he put to death in his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 15:20; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> And his brethren come down, and all the house of his father, and lift him up, and bring him up, and bury him between Zorah and Eshtaol, in the burying-place of Manoah his father; and he hath judged Israel twenty years.</span>
